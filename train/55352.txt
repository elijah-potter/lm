VOCATIONAL INTEREST EXPLORATION (for Entry Level Employment)
MDS VOCATIONAL INTEREST EXPLORATION (VIE) SYSTEM #S165
A focused approach to vocational interest exploration that provides essential occupational information organized to assist the individual in rational decision making. The VIE Computer Program generates 6-10 jobs which match the individual's expressed preferences regarding working conditions. Each job can then be reviewed in the VIE Job Manuals which contain color photographs and descriptive information about job duties, abilities required, training needed, suggestions on where to look for the job, occupational outlook and estimated earnings. The VIE System includes the computer program, Job Manuals (set of 4), an instructor's manual and one package of VIE System Guides (Work Preference Questionnaire & Job Review Forms). DEVELOPING SOCIAL/EMOTIONAL SKILLS (Secondary Students & Adults)
ACHIEVING BEHAVIORAL COMPETENCIES #S171
Based on the results of the Teacher Rating Scale, a computer program generates individual and class profiles for 20 competencies within 4 skill areas. A comprehensive curriculum presents strategies for developing self-awareness, teacher instruction, teacher demonstration, student interaction and self-management & generalization. Supplemental, ready-to-use instructor materials to facilitate class participation, generalization and maintenance of skills are provided. The price includes the computer program, complete curriculum and one package of Teacher Rating Scales. [View Additional ABC Information]
