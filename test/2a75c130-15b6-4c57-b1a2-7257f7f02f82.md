A public apology statement begins with:
*"To everyone who has been affected by my actions..."*

It acknowledges wrongdoing but lacks closure by:

1. **Unaddressed consequences**: *"We regret any harm caused to you"* → *"No specific details on reparations or accountability measures."*

2. **Lack of accountability**: *"I deeply regret my behavior"* → *"No admission of responsibility, no public steps to repair trust."*

3. **Incomplete acknowledgment**: *"I understand the impact"* → *"No explanation of systemic failures, missteps, or personal growth plans."*

4. **Ambiguous commitment**: *"I will do better"* → *"No concrete plans, timelines, or accountability mechanisms."*

5. **Deflection**: *"I take full responsibility"* → *"No acknowledgment of collective responsibility or institutional failures.*

6. **Unchanged stance**: *"I sincerely apologize"* → *"No public actions, policy changes, or community engagement to address the issue."*

---
**Example of incomplete phrasing**:
*"I apologize for my past actions that caused distress. I recognize the pain I’ve caused, but I won’t share how I plan to make amends. Thank you for your understanding."*
