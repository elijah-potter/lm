There was a spider named Priscilla. She was a jumping spider, and her owner loved her very much. They bonded for quite some time, and Priscilla would even come out of her cage to spend time with her owner. Her owner Amanda found her a mate. The two spiders became fond of each other and after a couple of months, Priscilla and her mate were going to have babies. Priscilla always wanted to be a mom. She got the nest all ready for her babies. She made sure the webs were all nice and cozy for them and spent every day close to her egg sac. She would sometimes forget to eat, so her mate would bring her some food. The day finally came, and the babies were ready to come out. The egg sac burst open, and lots of little spider babies emerged. Priscilla looked in her egg sac to make sure that all the babies made it out and there was one little one that did not make it. She tried to nudge the baby, but it wouldn’t move. Priscilla was so upset that one of her babies did not make it. She became very lethargic; she stopped eating and would not drink any water. Amanda became concerned and tried to hand-feed her some water to keep her hydrated. Priscilla would just stay in the den where the egg sac was with the little baby. Her mate took over the parental duties to help the babies. One morning, Amanda went to check on Priscilla to see if she would eat, and she found Priscilla at the bottom of the cage, lifeless. Amanda was so upset. She cried for days but gained the strength to continue to care for the little babies and Priscilla’s mate in her memory.
