I'm interested in collaborating with Greether because I really believe in it as a company. I have had many times in my travels where I would be intimidated to leave my Airbnb. I would have to work up the courage to leave and explore, just because of all the things that could go wrong about being in a foreign country in a language I could barely speak. I have gone out and explored each city I've traveled to, but I sometimes left wondering if I was able to see everything the city had to offer. Every now and then, I wished that I had experienced some of the local spots. I know other women who have gone through the same situations as well. That's why we need Greether - a platform that helps solo female travelers feel safe, connect with a local host, and ensure they genuinely see everything the city offers. That's why I would love to collaborate with Greether - to continue getting the word out about the company!
