A sudden gust of wind sent a loose branch crashing into the startled man’s head as he stumbled backward, his grip on the overgrown garden hose slipping. His heart pounded as he clutched his temple, squinting at the fallen twig wedged beneath his left eye, the sound of splintering wood mingling with the distant laugh of children playing beyond the fence. The dog, mid-bark, froze mid-air, tail wagging furiously before it bolted toward the sound of its owner’s muffled screams.
