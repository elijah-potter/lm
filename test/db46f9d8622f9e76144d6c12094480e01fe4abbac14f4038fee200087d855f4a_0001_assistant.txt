

1. Before starting, be sure that the lightbulb is turned off and has been turned off for a long time so you don't get burned.
2. Find a ladder to stand on so you can reach the light bulb. Have someone hold the ladder so it is stable.
3. Gently twist the bulb counterclockwise until it comes out. Carefully discard the bulb in the garbage.
4. Take the new bulb and insert it into the socket. Gently twist the bulb clockwise until it is snug in the socket.
5. Turn the light on to ensure the bulb is working properly. If it is not, check that it is screwed in properly and that the wattage and size are correct.
