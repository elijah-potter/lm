The teen characters that appeared in "The Breakfast Club" were: 
1. John Bender - the "criminal," played by Judd Nelson
2. Allison Reynolds - the "basket case," played by Ally Sheedy
3. Claire Standish - the "princess," played by Molly Ringwald
4. Andrew Clark - the "athlete," played by Emilio Estevez
5. Brian Johnson - the "brain," played by Anthony Michael Hall

In the 1985 John Hughes film, these five students from different high school social groups were forced to spend a Saturday in detention together.
