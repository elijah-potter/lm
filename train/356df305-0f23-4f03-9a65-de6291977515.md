In a dimly lit server room, a developer hunched over logs on a terminal. The terminal flickered to life with a new entry:

```
2023-11-15 14:37:22 - UserID: 42789, Attempt: 53
2023-11-11 14:37:22 - UserID: 42789, Attempt: 53
2023-11-08 14:37:22 - UserID: 42789, Attempt: 53
...
2023-11-01 14:37:22 - UserID: 42789, Attempt: 53
```

A sudden realization struck: every date matched the timestamp of another log entry. The same user was logging in at the exact same time daily, repeatedly.

The developer paused, fingers hovering over the keyboard. The cursor blinked, waiting.
