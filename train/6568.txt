Principles of Analog Electronics
Publisher: CRC Press | 2014 | ISBN: 1466582014 | 567 pages | PDF | 11 MB
In the real world, most signals are analog, spanning continuously varying values. Circuits that interface with the physical environment need to be able to process these signals. Principles of Analog Electronics introduces the fascinating world of analog electronics, where fields, circuits, signals and systems, and semiconductors meet.
