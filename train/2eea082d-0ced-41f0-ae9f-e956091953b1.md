## Product Requirements Document: "Context" - Mobile Knowledge Consolidation

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently juggle disparate information sources – notes, articles, research papers, meeting transcripts – leading to fragmented understanding, duplicated effort, and difficulty synthesizing insights for decision-making.

**Vision:** To provide a mobile application that intelligently consolidates and connects a knowledge worker’s scattered information, fostering clarity and accelerating insight generation.

---

### User Personas

**1. Dr. Eleanor Vance - Research Scientist**

*   **Goals:** Efficiently organize and synthesize research papers, lab notes, and conference proceedings to identify research gaps and contribute to publications. Quickly recall specific details from numerous documents.
*   **Pain Points:** Overwhelmed by the sheer volume of research materials. Difficulty tracking relationships between different research findings. Time wasted searching for specific information across multiple platforms. Feels a constant pressure to remain current in a rapidly evolving field.

**2. Marcus Bellweather - Strategic Consultant**

*   **Goals:** Quickly process client meeting notes, industry reports, and competitive analyses to formulate strategic recommendations. Easily share synthesized insights with colleagues. Maintain a readily accessible repository of project-specific knowledge.
*   **Pain Points:** Struggles to connect disparate pieces of information from client engagements. Frequently reconstructs knowledge from memory due to poor organization. Risks overlooking crucial details during client presentations. Experiences difficulty collaborating effectively with team members.

**3. Anya Sharma - Marketing Manager**

*   **Goals:** Aggregate marketing campaign data, competitor analysis, and customer feedback to inform strategic decisions. Easily track the evolution of a campaign and its impact. Quickly access relevant information to respond to stakeholder inquiries.
*   **Pain Points:** Information silos between marketing channels and departments. Difficulty visualizing campaign performance trends. Feels reactive rather than proactive due to information overload. Experiences frustration when searching for previously reviewed reports.

---

### Requirements

**1. Intelligent Content Aggregation:** The application shall automatically ingest content from specified sources (e.g., cloud storage, email attachments, web links) and organize it based on file type and date.
    *   *Acceptance Criteria:* User can connect to Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, and email accounts (Gmail, Outlook). Content is displayed in a chronological order within a "Recent" view. Error messages are clear and actionable if connection fails.
    *   *Success Metric:* 80% of beta users successfully connect at least one external source within the first session.

**2. Contextual Linking & Relationship Mapping:** The application shall allow users to manually create links between pieces of content, and automatically suggest potential links based on keyword similarity and content analysis.
    *   *Acceptance Criteria:* User can create a bi-directional link between two documents with optional notes. The application suggests three potential links per document based on text analysis. Suggested links are clearly marked as such.
    *   *Success Metric:* 30% of active users create at least three manual links within the first week of use.

**3. Keyword-Driven Search & Filtering:** The application shall provide a robust search function that allows users to search by keyword, file type, date range, and linked content.
    *   *Acceptance Criteria:* Search results are displayed with relevant snippets of content. Filters are clearly labeled and easily accessible. Search results are returned within 2 seconds.
    *   *Success Metric:* 90% of search queries return relevant results within the first three results.

**4. Focused "Workspace" Creation:** The application shall allow users to create dedicated workspaces for specific projects or areas of focus, containing curated collections of linked content.
    *   *Acceptance Criteria:* User can create a workspace with a descriptive name and optional description. User can easily move content between workspaces. Content remains accessible across workspaces.
    *   *Success Metric:* 50% of users create at least two workspaces within the first month of use.

**5. Mobile-Optimized Reading & Annotation:** The application shall provide a clean, distraction-free reading experience with integrated annotation capabilities.
    *   *Acceptance Criteria:* User can highlight text and add notes directly within documents. Notes are saved and linked to the specific passage. Reading experience is optimized for various screen sizes.
    *   *Success Metric:* 70% of users utilize the annotation feature at least once per week.



---

### Technical Considerations

*   **Platform:** Native iOS and Android applications.
*   **Backend:** Scalable cloud-based infrastructure (AWS preferred) to handle content storage and processing.
*   **Security:** Robust encryption and authentication protocols to protect user data. Compliance with relevant privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA).
*   **Content Indexing:** Efficient indexing and search algorithms to ensure fast retrieval of information.
*   **API Integrations:** Future expansion planned for integration with third-party note-taking and productivity tools.



### Risks

*   **Content Ingestion Limitations:** Difficulty ingesting content from certain file formats or platforms.
*   **Algorithm Accuracy:** Inaccurate link suggestions due to limitations in content analysis algorithms.
*   **User Adoption:** Users may be resistant to adopting a new knowledge management tool.
*   **Scalability Challenges:** Potential performance issues as the volume of user data grows.



### Non-Goals

*   Real-time collaboration features (e.g., shared workspaces).
*   Automated summarization of content.
*   Direct publishing or exporting of content to other platforms.
*   Integration with social media platforms.
*   Support for offline content access in the initial release.
