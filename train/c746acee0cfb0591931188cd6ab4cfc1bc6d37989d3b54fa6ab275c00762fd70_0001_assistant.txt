Khachapuri, a traditional Georgian cheese-filled bread, requires three main ingredients:

Flour: The essential component used to prepare the dough for Khachapuri.
Cheese: Sulguni, a traditional homemade Georgian cheese is usually utilized for the filling.
Eggs: Cracked eggs are typically added on top of the cheese filling before baking the Khachapuri.

To cater to your 5-year-old's taste, A mild and simple tomato sauce can be prepared by gently simmering tomato puree with a hint of garlic, a pinch of salt, and a sprinkling of dried herbs like basil or oregano. This mild sauce harmonizes with the flavors of Khachapuri, providing a kid-friendly option.
