Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the meadow every morning. One sunny day, Benny saw a shiny red flower with the prettiest petals. "Wow!" Benny said, "This flower is so beautiful!"

Benny decided to pick it carefully and place it in his basket. But then, he heard a tiny voice, "Please don't take my flower!" Benny looked down and saw a tiny mouse holding onto the flower with both paws.

"Oh, I didn’t mean to," Benny said, dropping the flower back. The mouse smiled and said, "Thank you! I was going to give it to my little friend who lives in the burrow."

Benny realized that sharing was fun. He hopped back to his basket and picked another flower—this time with a friend. They shared the flowers and played together until the sun began to set.

As Benny and the mouse waved goodbye, Benny knew he’d make more friends to share with. And from that day on, Benny always remembered that the best flowers were the ones you share with others!
