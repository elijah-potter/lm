Here are a few fast easy snack ideas:
1. Cucumber & tuna: A refreshing variation of tuna and crackers.
2. Lunch meat rolls & cheese slices: Skip the bread and turn what would have been a sandwich into finger food.
3. Carrots & ranch: Light and refreshing.
4. Celery & peanut butter: Add a couple of raisins on top to make it even more fun.
