You need to get a document, or some other file, to another person. You can do this by attaching it to an email. Take this quick course to learn how to send, open, print, and save attachments.
Inside this course:
Send and open attachments (1:24)
You can use attachments to include one or more files on any email you create. Open or save attachments with the program associated with the attachment file type.
Sending attachments in detail (2:44)
Learn more about the ins and outs of sending, forwarding, and replying to emails that have attachements in this short video.
Print and save attachments (2:07)
To print an attachment, right-click it, and click Quick Print (or Print if the attachment’s in the body of the email). Outlook has to open an attachment to print it.
