Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a little bunny named Benny who loved to explore. One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to see his favorite flower garden. The flowers were dancing in the wind, and the sky was as blue as Benny’s favorite toy, a big blue ball.

Benny’s nose twitched—he smelled something yummy! He dug his tiny paws into the dirt and found a juicy carrot. "Mmm, this is the best," he said, eating it all up. But then he saw his friend, a little squirrel named Sammy, digging for acorns. "Can I have one?" Benny asked.

Sammy shook his head. "No, I’m saving them for later!" Benny’s ears drooped, but he knew he could be kind. He dug another carrot and gave it to Sammy. "Here, try this!" Sammy took it and chewed happily.

As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and orange, Benny and Sammy played together. They built a little fort with sticks and leaves, and Benny told Sammy a story about a brave little mouse who outsmarted a sneaky fox. Sammy giggled and pretended to be the fox.

When the stars twinkled in the night sky, Benny and Sammy curled up together under a blanket of leaves. "Best day ever," Benny whispered, snuggling close. Sammy nodded. "I know," he said, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures.

And that’s how Benny learned that sharing made everything better—just like the flowers, the sun, and the magic of friendship.
