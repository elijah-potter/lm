Pineapple juice is a delicious tropical beverage made from pineapple juice and water. One cup (250 g) of pineapple juice contains 100% of the daily value (DV) for vitamin C, 55% of the DV for manganese, 19% of the DV for copper, and other vitamins and minerals like vitamin B6, thiamine, folate, potassium, and magnesium. Some brands you find at the supermarket may also be enhanced with vitamins or contain added sugars. It can be regarded as healthy as long as it's not consumed in excess. Pineapple juice is associated with a few health benefits like improved heart health, improved digestion, and reduced inflammation, though the research on these benefits isn't solid. While drinking fruit juice is beneficial, eating whole fruit is still the healthier option due to its fiber content.
