The dim glow of a flickering lamp cast long shadows across the walls of Elias Veyne’s study. His fingers hovered over the grand piano, the keys still damp with sweat despite the cold November air outside. He had rehearsed this song a thousand times—yet each time, his hands trembled.

*"Do not let her see you falter."*

A knock at the door. His mother’s voice, soft but insistent: *"Elias, it’s time."*

He exhaled sharply, the breath curling in the air like smoke. The piano’s resonance faded into silence. Outside, the city hummed—indifferent to the storm raging inside him.
