Science all around us!
This week Wonwell class took their Science learning outside. Our focus this half term is on sound, which links to our project 'Beat Band Boogie'. The children had to fold a piece of paper into 4 and draw themselves in the middle. They found a space outside and listened carefully. When they heard a sound they had to draw it to show whether it was in front, behind , left or right of them. We linked this learning to points on a compass and compared the sounds we heard with other people. Science in action in the real world! Please click here to see more photos.
