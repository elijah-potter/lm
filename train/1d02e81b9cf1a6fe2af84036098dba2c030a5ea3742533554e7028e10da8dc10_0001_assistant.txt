Disney's latest live-action adaptation of another animated classic continues its streak of success, bringing the classic tale The Little Mermaid to life. The two-hour remake is packed full of memorable moments, fan-favorite songs, and, most importantly, nostalgia for those older viewers. The casting here was phenomenal, with lead-star Halle Bailey showing an incredible performance as the Disney Princess Ariel. Full of the same emotion and heart as the original animated tale, Disney's live-action remake of The Little Mermaid has clearly made its claim as one of the best live-action remakes yet by the company.
