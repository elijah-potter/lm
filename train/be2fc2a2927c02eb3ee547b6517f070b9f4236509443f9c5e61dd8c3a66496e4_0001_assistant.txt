“Hey, Scout! How are things going on your side of the fence?”

“I’m doing well, Astro. How are you? What have you been up to lately, buddy?”

“Oh, the usual. Fetching balls, scaring off the mailman, taking naps. You know, being a good boy. I thought I heard a cat howling just now, did you hear it, too?”

“I totally heard it, Astro! In fact, I thought it might be two cats howling.”

“Wow! Wouldn’t it be great if one jumped into your yard and one jumped into mine? We could give those cats a big scare!”

“I would totally bark at them. I love it when their fur gets all puffed up and they hiss at you. They know who the boss is in our yards, don’t they, Astro?”

“They do, indeed, Scout! Say, I smelled some really good food on your side of the fence earlier. Did your human barbecue?”

“Oh, Astro, it was the best! They gave me their leftover steak bones. I have been enjoying one, and I buried the other in the yard for later.”

“I wish my human would grill some steak soon. That smelled great! Well, I guess I’m going to go look pitiful by the sliding glass door so they’ll let me back in. Have a nice evening, Scout!”
