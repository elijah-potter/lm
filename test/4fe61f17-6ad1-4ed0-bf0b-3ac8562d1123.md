**Q: Why does my Wi-Fi keep disconnecting randomly?**
A: Common causes include interference from other devices, outdated firmware on your router, or a weak signal in the area. Try moving the router to a central location, updating firmware, or using a mesh network system if needed.

---

**Q: How can I speed up my computer’s startup time?**
A: Enable Fast Startup in Windows, disable unnecessary startup programs via Task Manager, or clean up temporary files and cached data with tools like CCleaner.

---
**Q: Why does my phone’s battery drain too quickly?**
A: Check for background app activity, reduce screen brightness, disable unnecessary notifications, and ensure the OS and apps are updated to the latest versions.

---
**Q: How do I fix a slow printer?**
A: Restart the printer and printer driver, clear the print queue, update drivers, and ensure the printer has enough ink/cartridges. If issues persist, check for hardware faults.

---
**Q: Why does my laptop overheat?**
A: Clean dust from fans/vents, close unnecessary background processes, ensure proper ventilation, and update BIOS/OS for thermal management improvements.

---
**Q: How do I protect my Wi-Fi from hackers?**
A: Use a strong WPA3 password, disable WPS, change default SSID, enable MAC filtering, and consider a VPN for additional security.

---
**Q: Why does my phone battery die in cold weather?**
A: Cold temperatures reduce battery efficiency, draining it faster. Keep the phone in a warm place, avoid extreme cold exposure, and consider a battery-calibrating app.

---
**Q: How do I reset my router to factory settings?**
A: Press and hold the reset button (usually a small hole) for 10–30 seconds until the light blinks. Log in again with default credentials (check the router’s label) or re-setup Wi-Fi manually.

---
**Q: Why does my computer freeze randomly?**
A: Common fixes include updating drivers, running a disk check (Chkdsk), defragmenting HDDs, and checking for malware. Run a full system scan with antivirus software.

---
**Q: How do I extend my phone’s battery life?**
A: Reduce screen brightness, disable auto-brightness, close unused apps, lower background data, and avoid charging to 100% frequently. Use a battery-calibrating app for optimization.
