1. Get into the driver's seat of the vehicle;
2. Put the key in the ignition;
3. Turn the key to start the car;
4. Put your foot on the brake pedal (it's the left pedal out of the two pedals on the floor) and hold the brake down before continuing the next steps;
5. Release the hand brake using your left hand - it is a lever at knee height under the instrument panel;
6. Put the vehicle's gear shifter knob (right-hand side on the middle console) into "D" for "Drive";
7. Look behind you, in front of you, and to both sides to ensure the way is clear for you to go;
8. Slowly release your foot off the brake pedal and let the vehicle roll while you turn the steering wheel in the direction you want to go;
9. Push the accelerator pedal (right of the brake pedal) slowly and gently to gain speed while you use the steering wheel to steer in the direction you want to go;
10. You are now driving;
11. To slow down or stop at any time, use the brake pedal;
12. When you want to stop driving, drive the vehicle to the location you want to park it and use the brake pedal to stop the vehicle completely;
13. Once stopped completely, while keeping your foot on the brake pedal, use your right hand to put the vehicle's gear shifter knob into "P" for "Park";
14. Press down the emergency brake with your left foot (pedal furthest to the left on the floor);
15. Turn the key in the opposite direction to shut off the vehicle.

*Notes: 
Some vehicles have push-button starts. To start the car, press down on the brake while pushing the start button. 
The hand brake on some vehicles is on the right-hand side next to the driver's seat. To engage, pull the brake upwards. To disengage, push the brake downwards. 
The gear shift could be on the middle console or next to the steering wheel.
