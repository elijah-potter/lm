Do you ever feel sad or worried? Feeling like this once in a while is perfectly normal. However, if you're feeling sad or worried all the time, you may have a mental illness. This is a condition that affects the way you think. People from every age, gender, race, and background can develop a mental illness. Common ones include anxiety disorders, depression, and bipolar disorder. Doctors treat mental illness with medication and therapy, which is a place where you talk about your feelings with a counselor--like me!
