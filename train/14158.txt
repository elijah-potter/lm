The Pepper Weevil and Its Management
MetadataShow full item record
The pepper weevil is a severe pest of sweet and hot peppers. The grubs feed inside buds of fruit, and there may be five to eight generations of pepper weevil in a year. This publication contains complete information on pepper weevil biology, sampling and control.
