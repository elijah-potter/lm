## Reclaiming Resilience: The Willow Creek Pocket Park Revitalization Project

**Executive Summary**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park, currently a severely underutilized and neglected space within the Elmwood neighborhood, presents a unique opportunity to demonstrate a replicable model for climate resilience and community building. This proposal requests $75,000 in municipal funding to transform the park into a vibrant, ecologically sound, and accessible community asset. The project, spearheaded by the Elmwood Neighborhood Association in partnership with the City Parks Department and local horticultural specialists, will incorporate native plantings, stormwater management infrastructure, and accessible pathways, creating a cooling island effect, reducing localized flooding, and fostering a space for social connection and environmental education. This investment will yield tangible benefits for Elmwood residents, enhance the city’s overall resilience profile, and serve as a demonstration project for similar interventions across the municipality.

**Project Plan & Timeline**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park Revitalization Project is divided into four distinct phases, each with clearly defined deliverables and responsible partners.

**Phase 1: Assessment and Design (Months 1-2)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** Elmwood Neighborhood Association (ENA), City Parks Department (CPD), GreenScapes Landscape Architects.
*   **Deliverables:** Site assessment report detailing existing conditions, community needs assessment survey results, finalized park design incorporating community feedback and best practices for climate resilience, preliminary stormwater management plan.
*   **Milestone Completion:** End of Month 2.

**Phase 2: Site Preparation and Infrastructure Installation (Months 3-5)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** CPD, Willow Creek Construction (local contractor), ENA volunteer team.
*   **Deliverables:** Removal of existing debris and invasive species, grading and leveling of the site, installation of permeable paving for pathways, construction of a rain garden and bioswale, installation of a small-scale composting system.
*   **Milestone Completion:** End of Month 5.

**Phase 3: Planting and Landscape Establishment (Months 6-8)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** ENA volunteer team, Elmwood Community Garden Club, GreenThumb Nursery (local supplier).
*   **Deliverables:** Installation of native trees, shrubs, and pollinator-friendly plants, establishment of a small herb garden accessible to community members, application of organic mulch, initial irrigation system setup.
*   **Milestone Completion:** End of Month 8.

**Phase 4: Community Engagement and Education (Months 9-12)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** ENA, CPD, Elmwood Elementary School, local environmental educators.
*   **Deliverables:**  A series of workshops on native plant care, composting techniques, and stormwater management, signage detailing the ecological benefits of the park’s features, a dedicated area for children’s nature-based play, a celebratory grand opening event for the community.
*   **Milestone Completion:** End of Month 12.



**Budget & Financial Transparency**

The total project cost is estimated at $85,000. We are requesting $75,000 in municipal funding, with the remaining $10,000 secured through private donations and in-kind contributions from local businesses.  

*   **GreenScapes Landscape Architects (Design & Consultation):** $12,000
*   **Willow Creek Construction (Site Preparation & Infrastructure):** $25,000
*   **GreenThumb Nursery (Native Plants & Trees):** $18,000
*   **Composting System & Materials:** $3,000
*   **Permeable Paving Materials:** $10,000
*   **Signage and Educational Materials:** $2,000
*   **Tools & Equipment Rental:** $1,500
*   **Volunteer Appreciation & Refreshments:** $500
*   **Contingency Fund (5%):** $2,250

The contingency fund is allocated to address unforeseen costs that may arise during construction or planting, such as unexpected site conditions or material price fluctuations.  The Elmwood Neighborhood Association has established a dedicated bank account to manage project funds and will provide regular financial reports to the City Parks Department.  All purchases will be documented with receipts and invoices.



**Community Engagement & Equity Impacts**

Community engagement has been integral to this project’s development. We conducted a comprehensive needs assessment survey distributed throughout the Elmwood neighborhood, attended community meetings to solicit feedback on design concepts, and established a project advisory committee comprised of residents representing diverse demographics. The park’s design prioritizes accessibility for individuals with disabilities, incorporating gently sloping pathways, accessible seating areas, and sensory-rich plantings. Recognizing that Elmwood has historically experienced disproportionate impacts from extreme heat and flooding, this project directly addresses these environmental justice concerns by creating a cooling island effect and improving stormwater management.  The educational programming will be tailored to reach underserved populations, offering bilingual workshops and partnering with local community centers.

**Risk Mitigation**

Potential risks have been identified and mitigation strategies developed.  Weather-related delays are addressed through a flexible planting schedule and contingency planning for construction timelines.  Volunteer safety is paramount; all volunteers will receive safety training and appropriate personal protective equipment.  Vandalism is mitigated through increased visibility, community stewardship programs, and collaboration with the local police department.  The project’s success is dependent on securing necessary permits and approvals, which are detailed in the appendix.

**Post-Grant Sustainability**

Long-term sustainability is ensured through a multi-faceted approach. The Elmwood Neighborhood Association will assume responsibility for ongoing park maintenance, including watering, weeding, and litter removal.  A volunteer “Friends of Willow Creek Park” group will be established to foster community ownership and provide additional support.  The City Parks Department will provide technical assistance and guidance.  Educational signage will promote sustainable practices and encourage responsible park use.  The composting system will provide valuable resources for the community garden.



**Call to Action**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park Revitalization Project represents a tangible investment in the Elmwood neighborhood and a valuable demonstration of climate resilience strategies for the entire municipality.  We urge the municipal funders to approve this request for $75,000, enabling us to transform a neglected space into a vibrant community asset that fosters environmental stewardship, promotes social connection, and enhances the quality of life for all Elmwood residents.  This project is ready to begin, and the community eagerly awaits the opportunity to collaborate in its realization.
