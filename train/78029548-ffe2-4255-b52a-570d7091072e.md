Once upon a time, in a big, cozy house with a garden full of flowers, lived a little bunny named Pip. Pip loved to hop and nibble on carrots, but one day, he found something very special in his garden: a tiny, shiny acorn.

Pip wondered, "What if I grow this acorn?" He planted it carefully in the soil and watered it every day. Soon, the acorn started to sprout, and then it grew into a big, beautiful tree. The tree had leaves that shimmered like gold in the sun, and it made the happiest sound—like giggling!

One morning, Pip woke up and saw his tree had a new, tiny acorn at the top. Pip was very happy! He picked it up and thought, "Maybe this acorn will grow into a tree too!" He planted it in the garden, and soon, more leaves appeared.

Pip’s friends—the squirrels, the birds, and even the bees—came to visit his new tree. They all played together under the branches, singing songs and laughing. Pip felt the happiest he ever had, knowing he had made his garden even more magical.

And that is how Pip learned that planting small things can grow into wonderful adventures, just like magic!
