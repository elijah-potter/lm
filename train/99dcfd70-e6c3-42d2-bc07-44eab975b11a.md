A man stood at the edge of a storm-tossed cliff, the wind whipping through his coat as he clutched a single, tattered map. His hands trembled—not from fear, but from the weight of a decision that had gnawed at him for days. The map, faded and worn, was his only guide to survival in the vast, unfamiliar wilderness beyond the treacherous pass. His past had led him here: betrayal by a former ally, a debt owed to no one, and the hollow promise of a life he no longer believed in. But now, the choice was his alone.

He traced the line of the map with his thumb, following its jagged path toward the distant glow of a settlement. The alternative—the path back, the safety of the town where his name was whispered with pity—was a prison. He had been running from himself for too long. The storm outside howled like a challenge, but inside, his resolve hardened. The map was his anchor. The decision was made.

With a deep breath, he stepped forward, the wind carrying his defiant whisper: *"I choose this."* The cliff’s edge yielded beneath his weight, and he leapt—not into the abyss, but into the unknown. The map in his hand was the only certainty. The rest was chaos. But chaos was where he had always been. And this time, he would decide how to survive it.
