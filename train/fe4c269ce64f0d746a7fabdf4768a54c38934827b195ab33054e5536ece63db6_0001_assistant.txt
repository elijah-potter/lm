Okay, here are some hiding places for your mouse:

- Under the lid of a tin can. The can could be on its side, with the lid attached at a right angle so it forms an overhang for the mouse to stand under. 
- On top of a tire. The tire could belong to a car or truck and the mouse can scamper up and take shelter under the fender. This would protect the mouse from flooding too.
- Behind a loose brick in a wall. The mouse could push it out of the way to create a hiding spot.
- In a squirrel's house. The mouse could be friends with the squirrel and they could have tea together and wait for the storm to pass.
