A whispered vow between strangers in a crowded room,
*"I’ll meet you at midnight at the old church,"*
echoed like a ghost—half-lost, half-hope.

The clock struck twelve. She waited.
The streetlights blurred into a halo,
her breath misting the pavement like forgotten oaths.

He never came. The church door stood ajar,
its bell silent, its stained glass a silent lie.
The promise dissolved into the night’s endless sigh,
leaving only dust and the echo of a name—
**never spoken aloud.**
