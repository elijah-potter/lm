Here is a list of other places to visit in Orlando, besides Disney World:

1. Disney’s Typhoon Lagoon Water Park - A huge waterpark with attractions for the young and old. 
2. Disney’s Blizzard Beach Water Park - A unique, winter-themed water park with thrilling slides and other play areas for smaller children. 
3. Fun Spot America Orlando - This park has large roller coasters along with a huge Ferris wheel and other carnival-themed rides and attractions. 
4. Universal Studios Florida - A classic theme park with rides and attractions related to popular movies. You can take a stroll down Diagon Alley, meet Gru and the minions, and ride rollercoasters, all in one place.
