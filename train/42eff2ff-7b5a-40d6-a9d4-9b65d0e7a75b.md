Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big, red door, lived a tiny girl named Mia. She had a pony named Polly who was brown and had a white mane and tail.

One sunny morning, Mia woke up and said, "Polly, let’s go on an adventure today!" Polly neighed happily and trotted outside.

Mia and Polly ran through the green grass, jumping over little puddles. A yellow butterfly fluttered by and said, "Hi there!" Mia smiled and waved. Polly nibbled on a flower.

Suddenly, a big, fluffy cloud with a face came floating down. It said, "Can I play with you?" Mia and Polly nodded yes.

The cloud turned into a rainbow, and it started to sprinkle rainbows. Mia and Polly chased the colors, giggling as the rainbows danced in the sky.

When the fun was over, the cloud said, "You’re the best friends ever!" Mia hugged Polly and said, "Yes, we are!"

Then, they went home, and Mia gave Polly a big carrot to eat. "Goodnight, Polly," she whispered, and Polly nuzzled her nose. Mia snuggled under her blanket and dreamed of more adventures with Polly and the rainbow cloud.
