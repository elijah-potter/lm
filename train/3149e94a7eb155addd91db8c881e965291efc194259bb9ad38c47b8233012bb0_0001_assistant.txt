When I stopped to think about what my life might look like in ten years, where I might be living, and what I might be doing for work, I immediately thought about my hometown. 

I grew up in an idyllic, all-American town. There were so many things to love about it, but for me, the best part was that I felt safe there. The town essentially has no crime, and all the neighbors know each other’s names. 

In addition to the wonderful, safe feeling it provides, my town also has an incredible school system. I have absolutely loved my time in the public schools there, and I dream of being a second-grade teacher there one day. I know I would be lucky to work at our local elementary school where I went. 

I know that I want to live my life there after going to college, although I don’t quite know where that will be yet.

As well as teaching and living in this town,  ten years from now, when I'm 25, I hope to either be married or at least engaged. I want to be with a kind man who has the same outlook on life as I do and who also enjoys the safety and quiet of small-town life. Hopefully, we'll have a dog and a beautiful home, and maybe a few years later, a baby. 

No one knows what the future holds, but if I had to guess, that’s where I think I’ll be in ten years.
