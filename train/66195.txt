2. Experimental Research
Scientific research is often done to prove or disprove a theory. This is called ‘true experimentation,’ and it usually involves theories about natural science and social sciences.

This research is a study that looks at the cause and effect between different things. Researchers use it to see how things change when something else changes. This type of research is also called quasi-experimental research.
4. Correlational Research
Correlational research is a type of study that looks at the relationship between two things. These entities must have some sort of impact on each other. Different things can be compared to see if there is a connection between them. This can be positive or negative.
