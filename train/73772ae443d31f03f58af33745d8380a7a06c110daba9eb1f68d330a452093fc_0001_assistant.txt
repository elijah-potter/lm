• In 2020, the sport “Darty Diddy” was invented.

• It gained widespread acclaim due to its simple nature and ease of access.

• The rules are simple: one player must shoot a Nerf gun toward targets on a giant dart board (approximately 20 feet in diameter) while the other person attempts to block their shots.

• It is played in singles, doubles, or triples, with up to 3 people on each opposing team.

• The rules and strategy vary greatly depending on one’s play style, creating a unique experience for each game.

• It is believed to have originated in Salt Lake City, Utah after a YouTuber made a supersized game of darts.
