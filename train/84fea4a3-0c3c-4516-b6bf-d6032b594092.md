A vast, eerie chamber hung in perpetual twilight, its walls etched with ancient, ghostly runes that flickered faintly like dying embers. The air was thick with the scent of damp earth and something metallic, like rusted iron. Ceiling beams groaned subtly, swaying as if caught in a slow, invisible current. Shadows stretched unnaturally long, pooling against the floor like liquid darkness. The only light came from a single, unsteady lantern—its flame barely casting definition—casting long, wavering lines across the cracked stone floor. The weight of silence pressed down, each breath stifled as if the room itself was holding its breath, waiting.
