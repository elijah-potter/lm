Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white fur and big, round ears that twitched when he was excited.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to say hello to his best friend, a blue jay named Jay. "Good morning, Jay!" Benny chirped. Jay fluttered his wings and said, "Good morning, Benny! The sun is shining, and the flowers smell so sweet!"

Benny sniffed the air. "I love how the grass feels under my paws!" He dug his tiny claws into the soft green grass and made little holes. Jay giggled. "You’re making the most beautiful holes ever!"

Suddenly, Benny spotted a shiny red apple on a bush. "Look, Jay! An apple!" Benny hopped up to nibble it. Jay watched and said, "That’s not an apple—it’s a magic fruit! It can make you feel happy and strong!"

Benny ate the whole apple. He felt warm inside and hopped around with a happy bounce. "I’m so happy, Jay! Maybe I can help others feel happy too!"

Benny and Jay decided to share their joy. They visited Benny’s friends—a squirrel named Sammy, a robin named Robin, and a little mouse named Mimi. Benny showed them how to make big, happy faces and dance with their tails. Sammy chewed a tasty acorn, Robin sang a cheerful song, and Mimi giggled when Benny tickled her tummy.

As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and orange, Benny and Jay curled up under a big, fluffy cloud. "Today was the best day ever," Benny whispered. Jay nodded. "We made our whole garden feel like a big, happy home!"

And from that day on, whenever Benny and Jay hopped outside, they knew they had a special friend who made everything brighter and more fun.
