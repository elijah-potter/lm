A dimly lit alleyway at dusk. Shadows stretch long across the cobblestones. A lone figure moves cautiously, head down, toward a flickering neon sign advertising a dive bar. Their coat is too long, their gloves too worn, the way they shift their weight unnervingly familiar yet unrecognizable—until they stop. The air hums with tension. A hand hesitates on a rusted doorknob. A face half-turned, unreadable, eyes darting past the flickering light. The moment they turn back, they’re gone.
