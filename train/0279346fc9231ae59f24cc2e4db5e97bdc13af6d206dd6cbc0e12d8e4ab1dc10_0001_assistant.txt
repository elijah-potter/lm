Hey there! As requested, here are four reasons why you should stay on Facebook:

1. Sometimes, scrolling through Facebook helps you relax. You can laugh at memes and find groups for things that you enjoy. If Facebook stresses you out, maybe you should unlike some pages or unfriend certain people.
2. Facebook helps you stay on top of the news. In fact, you'll see updates and comments while events are happening live. Just use critical thinking so that you don't fall for any false news stories.
3. If you're bored, you can always check out Facebook Events to find things to do in your area. You can even see which of your friends are going.
4. Facebook photos provide a time capsule that you can review whenever you want to reminisce on the past. If you delete your account, you may lose them all unless you have a backup.
