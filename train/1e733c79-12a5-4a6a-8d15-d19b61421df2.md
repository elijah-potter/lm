Once upon a time in a cozy, green forest, there lived a little blue bird named Bella. She had the softest feathers and the prettiest wings, but one day, she noticed something strange—her little nest was empty!

Just then, a tiny, curious squirrel named Sammy scurried up to her. "I heard a rumble in the trees," he whispered. "Maybe a big, friendly bunny hopped away!" Bella’s heart raced. She looked around—where could they be?

A gentle breeze carried a hint of honey and sunshine. Then they saw it: a big, round, golden apple tree with apples the size of eggs! The tree was so tall, its branches stretched as far as the sky. Sammy’s eyes sparkled. "Let’s climb!" Bella giggled and hopped up first, her feet finding the smooth bark. They climbed higher and higher until they reached the top.

From there, they could see the whole forest—trees dancing in the wind, butterflies flitting around like tiny dancers, and a big, blue river sparkling below. "Look!" Sammy said, pointing. "There’s a cloud shaped like a rainbow!"

Bella’s wings flapped happily. "I want to see the sky more!" Just then, the apple tree started to glow. Golden light spilled down, and the apples rolled down the trunk like rolling boulders! Bella and Sammy giggled as they chased the apples, laughing as they tumbled through the air. They landed in a big, soft patch of grass where a family of bunnies were hopping.

"Oh no!" Bella gasped. "We’re late for dinner!" The bunnies giggled. "Come eat with us! We have carrots and berries!" Bella and Sammy sat down and munched happily. As they ate, the sun began to set, painting the sky in pink and purple.

"Time to go," Sammy said, his tummy full. They climbed back down the tree, their bellies rumbling. But as they reached the ground, they heard a soft *thump*. There was a tiny, half-eaten apple on the forest floor.

Bella’s eyes widened. "It’s for us!" Sammy grinned. He picked it up and took a bite. "Mmm! Sweetest apple ever!" Bella picked one too, and they ate until their bellies were full.

From that day on, Bella and Sammy visited the apple tree every morning, collecting the sweetest apples in the forest. And whenever they ate one, they remembered the kind bunnies who shared their dinner with them—and how happy they were together. And so, the little blue bird and the curious squirrel became the happiest friends in the whole forest.
