The **waving** gesture, often interpreted as a simple "hello" or "bye," can be misconstrued in cross-cultural contexts. In many Western cultures, a single wave with the hand raised implies farewell, while a double wave (raising and lowering the hand twice) may mean hello. Conversely, in some East Asian cultures, a wave can indicate a polite greeting, while an upward motion might mean "stop." Misalignment in body language can lead to confusion or unintended offense, underscoring how universal gestures vary widely.
