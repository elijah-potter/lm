## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park – A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary:**

The City of Oakhaven faces increasing challenges from unpredictable rainfall and rising temperatures, impacting the wellbeing of its residents. This proposal seeks $75,000 in municipal funding to transform a currently underutilized and neglected parcel of land – the Willow Creek Lot (located at 14 Maple Avenue) – into a climate-resilient pocket park. This project, spearheaded by the Friends of Willow Creek and supported by the Oakhaven Horticultural Society and local businesses, will provide a vital green space offering stormwater management, urban cooling, community gathering opportunities, and enhanced biodiversity. The Willow Creek Pocket Park represents a tangible investment in the health, resilience, and quality of life for Oakhaven’s residents, particularly those in the underserved Maplewood neighborhood. The total project cost is $100,000, with $25,000 secured through private donations and in-kind contributions.

**Project Plan & Timeline:**

The project will be executed in four distinct phases, each with defined responsibilities and deliverables.

**Phase 1: Site Assessment & Design (Months 1-2)**
* **Responsible Partner:** Oakhaven Horticultural Society (OHS), Friends of Willow Creek (FWC)
* **Deliverables:** Detailed site assessment report (soil analysis, drainage patterns, existing vegetation), finalized landscape design incorporating native drought-tolerant plants, bioswale construction plans, accessible pathway layout, and community feedback integration report.
* **Timeline:** 8 weeks. This phase includes a comprehensive soil test performed by OHS and two public design workshops facilitated by FWC.

**Phase 2: Site Preparation & Infrastructure (Months 3-5)**
* **Responsible Partner:** Maplewood Construction (contractor), FWC, City Public Works Department
* **Deliverables:** Site clearing and grading, installation of bioswale drainage system, construction of accessible pathways, delivery and placement of park benches and picnic tables, installation of a small, solar-powered irrigation system.
* **Timeline:** 12 weeks. Requires coordination with the City Public Works Department for utility marking and permitting. Maplewood Construction will be responsible for all heavy machinery operation and site grading.

**Phase 3: Planting & Landscaping (Month 6)**
* **Responsible Partner:** Oakhaven Horticultural Society (OHS), Volunteer Groups (coordinated by FWC)
* **Deliverables:** Planting of native trees, shrubs, and groundcover, mulching of planting beds, installation of interpretive signage detailing plant species and ecological benefits.
* **Timeline:** 4 weeks.  A “Planting Day” event will engage the community in the landscaping process. OHS will provide horticultural expertise and plant material.

**Phase 4: Community Launch & Maintenance (Month 7 onwards)**
* **Responsible Partner:** Friends of Willow Creek (FWC), City Parks & Recreation Department
* **Deliverables:** Grand opening celebration event, establishment of a park maintenance schedule (coordinated with City Parks & Recreation), creation of a “Friends of the Park” volunteer group for ongoing upkeep.
* **Timeline:** Ongoing. FWC will work with the City to develop a long-term maintenance plan, including regular weeding, pruning, and litter removal.



**Budget & Financial Transparency:**

The total project cost is $100,000. We are requesting $75,000 from the municipal fund.  The remaining $25,000 will be secured through a combination of private donations and in-kind contributions.

* **Municipal Funding Request:** $75,000
* **Private Donations (Confirmed):** $15,000
* **In-Kind Contributions (Estimated Value):** $10,000 (includes donated labor from Maplewood Construction and plant material from local nurseries)

**Itemized Costs:**

* **Site Assessment & Design:** $8,000 (Soil testing, design consultant fees, community workshop materials)
* **Site Preparation & Infrastructure:** $45,000 (Contractor labor, drainage system materials, pathway construction, benches, picnic tables, solar irrigation system)
* **Planting & Landscaping:** $17,000 (Native trees, shrubs, groundcover, mulch, interpretive signage, planting tools)
* **Community Launch & Promotion:** $5,000 (Event planning, marketing materials, refreshments)
* **Contingency Fund:** $10,000 (Covers unforeseen expenses, material price fluctuations, or weather-related delays. This represents 10% of the total project cost.)

The contingency fund will be managed by the Friends of Willow Creek and approved by a joint committee comprising representatives from FWC, OHS, and the City Parks & Recreation Department.



**Community Engagement & Equity Impacts:**

This project prioritizes equitable access and community ownership. The Maplewood neighborhood, where the Willow Creek Lot is located, is a historically underserved area with limited access to green spaces.  Two public design workshops were held to solicit community input on the park’s features and aesthetics.  The design incorporates accessible pathways and seating areas to accommodate individuals of all abilities.  The “Planting Day” event provides a hands-on opportunity for residents to participate in the park’s creation.  Interpretive signage will be available in both English and Spanish.  The project aims to foster a sense of community pride and ownership, creating a safe and welcoming space for all residents.

**Risk Mitigation:**

Potential risks have been identified and mitigation strategies developed:

* **Weather Delays:** The planting schedule is flexible and can be adjusted based on weather conditions. The contingency fund can be utilized to cover additional labor costs if delays occur.
* **Material Price Fluctuations:**  Quotes from multiple suppliers will be obtained to ensure competitive pricing. The contingency fund can address unexpected price increases.
* **Vandalism:**  The park’s design incorporates durable materials and visible lighting.  Community engagement and “Friends of the Park” volunteer group will promote stewardship and deter vandalism.
* **Permitting Delays:** Early and proactive communication with the City Public Works Department will minimize potential permitting delays.



**Post-Grant Sustainability:**

The long-term sustainability of the Willow Creek Pocket Park is paramount. The “Friends of the Park” volunteer group will be responsible for ongoing maintenance, including weeding, pruning, and litter removal. The City Parks & Recreation Department will provide support with larger maintenance tasks.  Educational signage will promote responsible park usage and encourage community stewardship.  Fundraising efforts will be undertaken to supplement the City’s maintenance budget.  The project’s success will serve as a model for future green space development in Oakhaven.
