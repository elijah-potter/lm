Ralph and Finley were best friends and they had been for a long time. Every Monday and Thursday, they would go to the local underwater casino. There, Ralph and Finley usually hung out and talked for hours while playing darts. 

Today was Thursday, so Ralph and Finley headed to the casino. They played darts for hours. Ralph won the first game of cricket, a dart game where the goal is to hit the same number on the dartboard three times. Finley absolutely crushed Ralph in the second game of cricket, leaving Ralph with only three total points. Finley cleared the rest of the board. 

The third game came down to the wire, with both Ralph and Finley within 10 points of each other for the entire game. Ralph eventually lost when Finley hit the 11 on the board to leapfrog Ralph for the win. 

Just as Finley had won, a blind electric eel challenged Finley to a game of cricket, declaring that the winner would get 500 dollars. Finley agreed. 

Given that the eel was blind, Finley let him go first. The eel threw the first dart, hitting the bullseye. Finley looked at Ralph and Ralph looked at Finley. They knew right then that they were in trouble. The eel's second shot hit the bullseye, too. Then the third. The eel cleared the board without giving Finley a single point. Finley and Ralph, stunned, asked the eel how he managed to hit every point on the board. The eel laughed and said, "Don't worry about it. It's all a bit of luck, I'm sure." Finley and Ralph left the casino that night feeling like they got hustled. They never saw the blind eel again.
