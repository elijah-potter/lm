A hollowed-out cavern where the walls whisper in a language older than memory.
The air hums with a static that shifts into patterns—faces of long-dead scholars, their mouths moving without sound, their eyes tracking you as if they’ve been waiting for you.
A clock runs backward on the ceiling, its hands frozen mid-second, the second hand moving forward while the minute hand spirals inward.
Sand in a glass bowl flows upward, grains forming words in a code only you can read.
The ground beneath you is a patchwork of time loops—footprints rearranged, a single step repeated ad infinitum.
A child’s laughter echoes from a mirror that reflects not your face, but a version of you from a time when the world was different.
The stars in the sky above are a constellation of dead stars, their light flickering like dying embers, some already gone.
