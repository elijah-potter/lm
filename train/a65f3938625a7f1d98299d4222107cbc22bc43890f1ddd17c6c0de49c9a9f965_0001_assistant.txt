Here is an Italian-inspired menu of appetizers, entrée, and dessert options that would be perfect for the upcoming wedding of a first-generation NY Italian couple. 

Appetizers: 

•	Italian wedding soup with turkey meatballs 
•	Mom’s Italian bread with basil and parsley pesto spread (vegetarian option)
•	Gnocchi with white beans and Italian chicken sausage 

Entrees: 

•	Ricotta and mozzarella-stuffed manicotti with a chunky tomato and meat marinara sauce 
•	Eggplant parmesan (vegetarian option) 
•	Four-cheese chicken fettuccine in a garlic and spinach white sauce 

Desserts:

•	Lemon gelato with a sprig of mint for garnish and flavor
•	Chocolate chip and ricotta-stuffed homemade cannoli with caramel drizzle
•	Almond and chocolate biscotti with a white chocolate drizzle

Note: All dessert options are safe for vegetarians. Served with optional coffee or hot green or black teas.
