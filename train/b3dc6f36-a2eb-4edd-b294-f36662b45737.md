Habits like nail-biting or procrastination often stem from stress or boredom, requiring sustained effort to replace with healthier alternatives. For instance, replacing the act of nail-biting with stress-relief techniques like deep breathing or fidget toys can gradually weaken the habit loop. Similarly, breaking procrastination demands breaking the cycle of avoidance by setting small, achievable goals and tracking progress to maintain momentum. Both require patience and intentional practice to rewire neural pathways.
