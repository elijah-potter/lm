The little piece of clay was formed into the shape of a tree trunk with a broccoli-sprout top. Only standing about four inches high, it was the perfect size for the fairy garden. The base of the piece was light brown with a little doorway cut out in the front. The irregularities in the form and the swirls embossing the cylindrical walls gave it a whimsical appearance. A dark brown curvy border accompanied the broccoli-sprout top, which, of course, was painted green. The combination of elements almost amounted to a toadstool trying to be both a tree and a head of broccoli at once.
