If you're looking for a game to play that isn't going to tax your brain and has an easy "pick up and put down" quality, I highly recommend Forza Horizon 5.  And before anyone starts complaining and saying how they don't like racing games, please hear me out. This is the racing game that really tailors itself to EVERYONE, and in particular, people who aren't very likely to enjoy or embrace racing games as a whole.  Playground Games have made a masterpiece here. Since it's a first-party Microsoft title, it's obviously gorgeous. The cars even have lovingly modeled and accurate interiors for over 500 cars, in addition to an extensive array of cosmetic and performance-adjusting parts for your cars and an amazing livery system to let you add a wide variety of artistic flair to your ride. There are literally people who only play the game to create art for others to enjoy. The variety of environments for you to race your cars in is extensive, from deserts to mountains, and there's no rush - if what you want to do is take a leisurely drive along the highway, you can do that. You don't ever have to get into a race. There's even a photo mode for you to take high-resolution screenshots and share them with friends. Many of these are so detailed that you can fool people.

But if you do decide to dip your toes into the racing element (which I recommend highly), you'll find you have the ability to make the racing as easy or as difficult as you like. You can literally set the difficulty so low that it's almost impossible for you to lose. However, the game gives bonus rewards to you when you decide to raise the challenge, which encourages you to learn the systems at your own pace.  It's a breeze to earn credits and "wheelspins" which will reward you with cars and cosmetic items for your avatar. I was like you once; I didn't think racing games were for me, but Forza Horizon 5 changed my tune. Perhaps it will change yours.
