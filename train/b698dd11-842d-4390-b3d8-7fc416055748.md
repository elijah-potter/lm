Highway 101, Golden Gate Bridge, San Francisco Bay Area – Rush hour. Vehicles stream across the bridge’s lanes, blending red tail lights with mist rising from the bay. Trucks belch exhaust as they merge onto the freeway. Cyclists, some with cargo bikes, weave between cars. Pedestrians on the sidewalk below watch the water’s churn as boats pass under the bridge. A café on Pier 39 sizzles with sushi rolls, its neon signs flickering in the dusk. The hum of engines, distant laughter, and the bay’s steady roar create a cacophony of motion.
