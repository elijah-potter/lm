1. Thousands of years ago, a bloody war took place between the gods and rulers, ended only by the efforts of one champion. The bitterness between the lords and the gods was never truly resolved. One day, an old psychic visits you and claims that you are the descendant of that champion. With tensions rising, it's up to you to discover the truth of the past and prevent another war.

2. When the princess was kidnapped, her father promised a huge reward for anyone who could save her. Many ventured into the castle to rescue her, but to their surprise, there was no danger. The princess, a powerful psychic, chose to remain in the castle, refusing to return with any man with greed in his heart. Tales of her stubbornness spread across the land, until one day, you decide to visit the castle for yourself and see whether the rumors are true.

3. In a world where vampires are scorned, you, the first vampire ambassador to the humans, must work alongside the top scientists from both races to understand what drives the bloodlust that is slowly spreading among your people, lest the primal instincts spell the end of vampires everywhere.
