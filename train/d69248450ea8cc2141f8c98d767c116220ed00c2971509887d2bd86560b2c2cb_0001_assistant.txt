Discover the Wonders of Your Baby's Growing Brain! Welcome, new parents: We are thrilled to share with you the incredible findings from a fascinating study on infant brain development. Through a special recording method called EEG, it is now possible to capture the magical moments of your little one's brain activity. We can now share that your baby's motor skills change dramatically across the first half year of life, and this is all due to experience-dependent plasticity taking place in the infant brain - a crucial finding that has the potential to unlock early detection of brain developmental disorders.
