Prevention of Head Lice
With the colder weather, students wear coats and hats to school. Please remind your student to not share their hat or coat with anyone else. This will help to prevent the spread of head lice. Head lice do not jump and can only be passed to another through direct contact. If you need assistance with this issue, please call the nurse on your child's campus.
