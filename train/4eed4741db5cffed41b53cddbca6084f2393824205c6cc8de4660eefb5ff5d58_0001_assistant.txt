Adverse Childhood Experiences, or ACEs, refer to potentially traumatic events that occur in childhood (0 - 17 years). ACEs can occur when a child experiences violence, abuse, neglect, witness violence (home or community), having a family member attempt or die by suicide, exposure to substance use problems, mental health problems or instability due to parental separation or incarceration. 

ACEs have a real impact on future violence victimization and perpetration, lifelong health and opportunities such as job and earning potential. ACEs can be prevented with proper interventions and can be treated with different therapies.
