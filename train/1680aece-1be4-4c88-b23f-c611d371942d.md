A floating market in Bangkok’s canals, where vendors trade goods from boats under a canopy of unseen laws. Bartering happens on whims of negotiation, while taxes, licenses, and customs are negotiated with the same fluidity. The river is a boundary—police may appear, but enforcement depends on bribes, favors, or the presence of foreign tourists. Monarchy’s influence lingers in "royal favor" discounts, while corruption’s shadow stretches over every transaction. The market’s chaos thrives because rules are never fully written, only whispered between merchants, bureaucrats, and the river’s unseen currents.
