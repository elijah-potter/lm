Two scientists in a dimly lit lab, their fluorescent lights casting long shadows across the bench. Dr. Elias holds a vial of glowing blue liquid, his fingers trembling as he traces the curve of the container. Across the room, Dr. Mira adjusts her glasses, her pen poised over a sketch of a molecular structure—one that matches the vial’s contents. A sudden alert blares—*red*, the emergency button’s label glows. The vial shatters. Elias screams as the blue liquid spreads across the floor, reacting with the acid spill from Mira’s discarded beaker. Their research, a perfect synthesis of two truths, now becomes a toxic collision: the same molecules that cured malaria in mice, now turning their lab into a poisoned zone. Outside, the door locks with a final click.
