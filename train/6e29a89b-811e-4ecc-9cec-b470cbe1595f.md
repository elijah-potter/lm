**"The Last Light"**

The window shattered like glass at dawn.
No one heard the scream inside.

Outside, the city slept,
draped in silver mist,
each shadow a whisper
of ghosts who never left.

The neon hummed,
a pulse in the dark,
like a heartbeat
for those who drowned
in the river of lies.

They came for the last light,
not to see the sky,
but to keep the world
from remembering.
