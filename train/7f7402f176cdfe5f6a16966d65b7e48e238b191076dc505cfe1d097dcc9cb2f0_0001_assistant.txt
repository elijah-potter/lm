Broadway is as glamorous as it gets -- unless you're handling the bills. Andrew works in the finance department for one of the most prominent play companies, and he'll assure you that this was not his plan A. Until he met Margot, the beautiful redheaded lead in the upcoming production. When she gets called into Andrew's office to discuss a payroll discrepancy, it's love at first sight... for Andrew. Can he win the heart of a girl so clearly out of his league? And can he realize his own dreams while doing so?
