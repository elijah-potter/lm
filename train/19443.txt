Loss of teeth is a common entity in life, this may be due to gum disease, physical injury or trauma, cavities and etc. where in there is loss of teeth that which has to replace for functional or esthetic purpose. Replacing teeth with removable denture is an easy and immediate process. This prosthesis is called removable partial denture because it can be removed easily and replaced back to position without any help from the dental professional.
A single tooth RPD (removable partial denture) is called as flipper tooth, which is used to replace an extracted tooth or missing tooth.
RPD is divided in to four categories depending on the number of missing tooth. Various designs and factors are used to fabricate RPD (removable partial denture). Designs include assessment of occlusion, drifting, leaning of teeth and surveyed articulated casts if necessary and number of teeth to be replaced. The shade or colour of teeth are also matched before fabrication of the RPD (removable partial denture).
RPD (removable partial denture) can be used in patients who cannot maintain oral hygiene and are practically difficult to clean teeth.
RPD (removable partial denture) is used for restoration of front teeth retaining the normal contour of upper and lower lips. Lower cost, less invasive when replacing more than one teeth compared to fixed replacement, easily cleaned and replaced.
