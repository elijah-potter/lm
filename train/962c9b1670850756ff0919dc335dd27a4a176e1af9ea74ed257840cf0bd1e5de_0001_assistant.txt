Dessert is a dish best served cold. In this competition, fifty ice cream aficionados churn it out to see who is the cream of the crop and who goes home with rustled jimmies. One-on-one battles pit ice cream maker against ice cream maker in The Churn Center. Who has what it takes to balance all the flavors of the culinary world with the sweet dessert that is ice cream? There can only be one. Find out who got iced this week in Ice Cream Royale!
