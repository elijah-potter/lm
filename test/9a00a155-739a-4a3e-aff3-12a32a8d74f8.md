**BREAKING: Mysterious "Echoes of the Old" Phenomenon Strikes New Haven’s Central Park**

Residents of New Haven’s Central Park reported hearing faint, rhythmic chimes at 3:17 AM yesterday, a time that aligns with the city’s long-standing tradition of midnight chimes marking the end of the summer solstice. Witnesses described the sounds as "like distant church bells," though no known structures in the park emit such tones. Police are investigating potential audio manipulation or an unidentified drone, but no official cause has been confirmed. Authorities urge residents to remain vigilant, as similar incidents have occurred in neighboring cities this week. No injuries reported.
