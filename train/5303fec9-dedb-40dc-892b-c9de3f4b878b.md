**Uncharted Paths: A Relentless Odyssey**

The first step was to the east, but the trail forked—north or south—both promising ruins of forgotten gods. The second day’s compass spun wildly; the wind carried whispers of a city whose name was erased from maps. The third evening, the sky itself split into mirrored horizons, and the stars formed a map of shifting coordinates.

By the fifth night, the destination was no longer a place but a *condition*—a cycle: the desert’s mirage turned into a river’s reflection, then a mountain’s shadow. The guidebook’s final entry read: *"Do not ask where you are going; the map rewrites itself."*

Now, the journey is the destination.
The next leg begins where the last left off—unbound.
