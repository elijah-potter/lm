Silent, deep valleys cast elongated, wavy shadows stretching like veins of obsidian across ancient stonework. The light flickers—soft when the sun dips behind the jagged cliffs, sharp as a blade when it rises, leaving jagged, uneven patterns on the moss-draped ledges. Warmth lingers in hollows where shadows pool like stagnant pools of time, reflecting faint glints of forgotten metal or rusted glass. The air hums with the weight of them, pressing down on the land like an unseen presence, telling stories of forgotten winds and whispered secrets.
