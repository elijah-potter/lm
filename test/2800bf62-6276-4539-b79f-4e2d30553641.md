Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big, round tree outside, lived a curious bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white fur and big, bright eyes. Every morning, Benny would wake up and hop outside to see what exciting things were waiting for him.

One sunny afternoon, Benny noticed a shiny red ball rolling toward him. "What is this?" he wondered, sniffing it. Suddenly, Benny's ears perked up—he heard a tiny voice. "Help! Help! My shoes are stuck in the mud!"

Benny hopped over to a little girl named Mia who was crying. Mia had a pair of bright red shoes that were stuck in a big muddy puddle. Benny's ears twitched. "Muddy shoes? No problem!" he said cheerfully and carefully dug around with his paws.

Mia giggled. "Thank you, Benny! Can you help me find my socks too?"

Benny happily dug again and found Mia’s socks, tangled in the grass. He helped Mia get them out, and together they cleaned the mud off her shoes. Mia smiled and said, "You're the best bunny ever!"

Benny grinned and said, "I love helping friends!" Then he spotted a bright yellow flower growing near Mia’s house. "I’ll bring you this," he said, hopping to fetch it.

Mia clapped her hands. "Oh, Benny! Thank you so much! You’re my very best friend!"

Benny wagged his tail happily and hopped home, thinking about all the fun adventures he and Mia would have together. And that’s how Benny the Bunny became Mia’s favorite friend, making every day a little bit brighter.
