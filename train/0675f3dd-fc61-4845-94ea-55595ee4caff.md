## Product Requirements Document: "Synapse" - Contextual Knowledge Aggregation

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently struggle to efficiently locate and synthesize relevant information dispersed across various platforms – notes, documents, web pages, communication channels – leading to wasted time, duplicated effort, and diminished productivity.

**Vision:** To empower knowledge workers to effortlessly connect disparate information sources, fostering deeper understanding and accelerating insights.

---

**1. User Personas**

**1.1. Dr. Eleanor Vance - Research Scientist**

*   **Goals:** Quickly synthesize findings from numerous academic papers, lab notebooks, and conference proceedings to advance research and publish findings. Maintain a comprehensive and easily searchable record of all research materials.
*   **Pain Points:** Overwhelmed by the volume of information; difficulty tracking sources and connections between ideas; time-consuming manual synthesis processes; risk of overlooking critical details.

**1.2. Mark Olsen - Marketing Manager**

*   **Goals:** Efficiently gather market research data, competitor analyses, and campaign performance metrics from diverse sources to inform strategic decisions and optimize marketing efforts. Ensure alignment across team members regarding key insights.
*   **Pain Points:** Information silos across departments; difficulty tracking evolving market trends; challenges in distilling actionable insights from complex data sets; inefficient collaboration on research findings.

**1.3. Sarah Chen - Consultant**

*   **Goals:** Rapidly assemble relevant client data, industry reports, and best practices from various sources to prepare compelling presentations and deliver impactful recommendations. Maintain a curated knowledge base for future reference.
*   **Pain Points:** Constant need to quickly learn about new industries and clients; difficulty organizing and retrieving information for recurring engagements; risk of delivering incomplete or inaccurate recommendations.

---

**2. Requirements**

**1. Contextual Link Capture**

*   **Description:** Users should be able to capture links from any web page or document and automatically associate them with existing notes or create new notes. The app should intelligently suggest note associations based on page content.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:**
    *   Capturing a link from a web page creates a new note with the page title and a snippet of the page content.
    *   Capturing a link within an existing note automatically creates a connection between the notes.
    *   The app suggests potential note associations based on keyword analysis of the linked page.
*   **Success Metrics:** 75% of captured links are associated with existing notes within 30 seconds.

**2. Semantic Note Linking**

*   **Description:** The application should allow users to create explicit links between notes based on shared concepts or keywords, even if those notes do not share a direct link.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:**
    *   Users can select keywords within one note and search for notes containing those keywords to establish a link.
    *   Linked notes display a visual representation of the connection.
    *   The application suggests potential connections based on keyword overlap between notes.
*   **Success Metrics:** 40% of users actively create semantic links within the first week of use.

**3. Intelligent Note Summarization**

*   **Description:** The application should provide a concise, automatically generated summary of each note, highlighting key concepts and relevant links.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:**
    *   Summaries are generated automatically upon note creation and updated when linked content changes.
    *   Users can edit automatically generated summaries.
    *   Summaries are displayed prominently within each note.
*   **Success Metrics:** 60% of users review automatically generated summaries before making edits.

**4. Customizable Knowledge Graph Visualization**

*   **Description:** Users should be able to visualize their connected notes as a knowledge graph, allowing them to explore relationships and identify patterns.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:**
    *   Users can toggle between different graph layouts (e.g., hierarchical, force-directed).
    *   Users can zoom and pan within the graph.
    *   Users can filter the graph to display only specific notes or connections.
*   **Success Metrics:** 30% of users regularly utilize the knowledge graph visualization.

**5. Cross-Platform Synchronization**

*   **Description:** User notes and connections should be seamlessly synchronized across iOS, Android, and web platforms.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:**
    *   Changes made on one platform are reflected on all other platforms within 60 seconds.
    *   Offline access to notes is supported.
    *   Data encryption is implemented to protect user privacy.
*   **Success Metrics:** 99.9% synchronization uptime.



---

**3. Technical Considerations**

*   **Scalability:** The application must be designed to handle a large number of notes and connections.
*   **Security:** Robust security measures are required to protect user data.
*   **API Integration:** Potential for future integration with popular note-taking and productivity tools.
*   **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** Utilizing NLP for intelligent note association and summarization will be crucial.

**4. Risks**

*   **NLP Accuracy:** The accuracy of automated note association and summarization may be limited, requiring user intervention.
*   **User Adoption:**  The complexity of the knowledge graph visualization may deter some users.
*   **Data Synchronization Issues:** Maintaining consistent data across multiple platforms can be challenging.



**5. Non-Goals**

*   Real-time collaborative editing of notes.
*   Direct file storage (e.g., PDFs, Word documents).
*   Social networking features.
*   Built-in task management functionality.
