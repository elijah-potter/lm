A worn-out leather wallet slipped from the pocket of an overcoat mid-stride, landing on a cracked sidewalk near an alleyway. The rain-soaked pavement glistened under dim streetlights as the owner, hunched over a café table, turned to see it. Inside was a crumpled $20 bill, a frayed photo of a missing family pet, and a loose keychain bearing a faded logo: *"Luna’s Home."*

The owner retrieved it, fingers brushing the cold metal. The keychain’s chain had snapped, but the rusted lock still held a single key—small, bent, and unfamiliar. A sigh escaped as they tucked the wallet into their pocket, the key clattering against the fabric. The café’s bell chimed, drawing their attention, but the key remained, a quiet reminder of a chance discovery.
