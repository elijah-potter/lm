A crumbling library where the bookshelf’s shelves lean at unnatural angles, the air thick with dust and the scent of old paper and damp wood. The grand staircase spirals upward, its steps worn smooth but the banister cracked as if someone paused mid-step. The only light comes from a single, flickering bulb over the reading desk, casting long shadows on the walls where time seems to have frozen. A half-empty coffee cup sits on the desk, the ground glass still visible. The hum of an old projector flickers on the ceiling, its film reel stuck halfway through. Outside, the city’s pulse fades into silence, like a heartbeat that has stopped.
