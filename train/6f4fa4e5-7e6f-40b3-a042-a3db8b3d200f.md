Central Square Plaza, Beijing
A modern urban park with minimalist seating arrangements (sculptural benches, modular loungers) and large public screens displaying ambient visuals (floating geometric patterns, slow-moving cityscapes). Surrounding buildings have large glass facades reflecting the sky. The plaza is organized into silent zones: designated areas for contemplation, yoga circles, and "quiet hours" enforced by soundproofed acoustic barriers. Limited signage—only symbols for rest, meditation, or communal activities. A single central fountain with a hidden water feature that hums softly without splashing. Visitors sit cross-legged, stand in silent meditation, or walk barefoot on the warm, textured ground. No phones, no loud conversations; only the occasional soft chime from a nearby silent bell.
