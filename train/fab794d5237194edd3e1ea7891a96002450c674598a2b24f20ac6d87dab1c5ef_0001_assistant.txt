Absolutely! Here are a few creative suggestions for a date night at home you and your wife might enjoy:

1. Unplug: Pretending the electricity is out can make for a fun date night. Some activities you can do without electricity are light candles, playing games, telling ghost stories, and using a flashlight for added dramatic effect.  
2. Tasting party: A tasting party can be any number of themes, from wine and chocolate to ice cream and smoothies. Create an at-home tasting station with everything laid out and a sheet of paper to take tasting notes. You can make it even more fun by adding a guessing game, blindfolding one another, and guessing the flavor of what you are eating.
3. Home cook-off: Create a fun competition between you and your wife by competing in cooking. For example, pick a particular ingredient you must incorporate into your cooking. 
4. Karaoke night:  Sing all your favorite songs together or find karaoke versions of songs on YouTube. 
5. Plan a Trip: Scout out locations for your dream trip and plan one together. You can add a spin to this by putting in a budget and seeing what kind of trip you can put together. 
6. Interior design project: Pick a room and redesign it together. You can spend some time beforehand looking up some design ideas. Painting, building furniture, or redecorating a room can be some ideas to get started.
