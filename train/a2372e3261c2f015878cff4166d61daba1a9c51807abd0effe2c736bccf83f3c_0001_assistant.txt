Child: (Surprised) "Who said that? Is someone there?"

Child: (Sympathetic) "I bet they were nice, but why did they leave you here?"

Toy: (Emotionally) "Oh, thank you, dear child. Your words bring tears of joy to my button eye. You have no idea how much it means to have an owner again. I am so happy to have a purpose once more."

Child: (Happily) "Yes! Let's make new memories together. You will never be forgotten again, I promise." 

As the child and the forgotten toy embrace, a newfound connection is formed.
