It can be denied no longer: climate change is real, and it is a threat to the globe. The average temperature has been raised by almost 2°F in the past century, causing dramatic and unprecedented effects across the world. We need to put an end to this before it causes deeper and more permanent changes to our lives, ecosystem, and planet. Our air is being polluted, our ecosystems are being threatened, our seas are rising, and our planet is being destroyed. Communities across the globe are being harmed: not just ours, but those of wildlife as well. These changes are causing more natural disasters which can be seen in extreme weather events like droughts, floods, wildfires, and heat waves. To stop these events that endanger us all, we must put an end to the causes of climate change, both to better our planet and to better the lives of ourselves and future generations. We must put every effort we have into stopping climate change before it progresses further and causes irreparable damage. We must save our planet: we are obligated to.
