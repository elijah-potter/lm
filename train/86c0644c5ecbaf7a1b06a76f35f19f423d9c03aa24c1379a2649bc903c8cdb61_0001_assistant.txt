The Mexican-American War started over a disputed boundary line between the United States and Texas on the Nueces Strip that began on May 13, 1846, when the United States declared war on Mexico. Mexico did not recognize Texas as a U.S. territory and the admission of Texas into the U.S. angered Mexican officials. The signing of the Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo brought an official end to the Mexican-American War on February 2, 1848.
