The engineer sat at the whiteboard, sketching schematics for the new circuit design. His colleague, Jake, leaned in, hands gesturing animatedly. "This part isn’t working—our last prototype had a 30% failure rate here," Jake said, pointing to a specific component.

The engineer paused, squinting. "Interesting. Let me check the specs again." He scribbled notes while Jake continued, oblivious.

Then, Jake shifted abruptly. "Oh, actually, I was just thinking—what if we *didn’t* do this redesign at all? Why bother? We’re already behind schedule."

The engineer’s pen froze mid-air. "Why are you suggesting we abandon the project midway?"

Jake smirked. "Because the board wants to hear about *budget cuts*, not technical solutions. I’ll handle it." He stood, clapping the engineer’s shoulder lightly. "Great minds think alike—you’re a genius, by the way." He walked out, leaving the engineer staring at a half-finished schematic, wondering if he’d just been dismissed.
