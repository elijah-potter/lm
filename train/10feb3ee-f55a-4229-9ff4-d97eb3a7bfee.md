A small, dimly lit **boardroom** in a corporate office transformed into a chaotic hybrid event. Attendees—mostly executives in suits—danced awkwardly to a DJ’s remix of a 90s synth-pop track, while a few clinked champagne flutes under fluorescent lighting. A single, overstuffed **couch** doubled as a seating area for a ragtag group of musicians performing a cover of *"Bohemian Rhapsody"* with a kazoo. A half-eaten buffet of office snacks (pretzels, deli meats, and a questionable "team-building" dessert bar) circulated around, alongside a makeshift **photo booth** with props like oversized sunglasses and a cardboard cutout of a "CEO" wearing a top hat.

The atmosphere was a mix of corporate formality and unironic festivity, with attendees exchanging confused glances between laughter and awkward politeness. The event’s name—*"Team Offices: A Global Gathering"*—was printed on a sign that no one quite understood. The only rule: **"No phones, but you can text your crush."**
