A habit of mindlessly scrolling through social media during work hours began as a stress relief tactic. Over time, it expanded into an hour-long daily ritual, consuming time that could be better spent on deep work or tasks requiring focus. The problem emerged when deadlines loomed, and the distraction turned into a crutch—procrastination disguised as relaxation. Eventually, it became a cycle: work suffered, stress increased, and the habit grew stronger, eroding productivity and professional growth. Productivity apps and reminders did little to break the cycle until self-awareness and discipline interventions forced a shift.
