The dimly lit kitchen felt colder than usual. A lone candle flickered on the counter, casting long shadows. *Lena* leaned over the empty breakfast nook, her fingers tracing the worn edge of the worn-out toaster. A single crumb remained on the plate—her morning cereal—while the missing bowl sat empty beneath it. Her eyes locked onto the fridge door, its usual contents now missing: the jar of marmalade, the yogurt carton, the forgotten half-eaten sandwich from lunch. She exhaled, rubbing her temples. "Another day, another thing I’ll have to replace." The silence stretched. Outside, rain tapped against the window, a rhythmic reminder of how much had slipped through the cracks.
