School Bullying Outbreak
In accordance with Bullying Awareness Week, which took place a few two months ago, and its theme — “Stand Up!” (to bullying) — Sarah Fudin at USC created this infographic, “School Bullying Outbreak,” with facts about the methods, consequences and preventative measures related to bullies and bullied victims in schools. It’s important to fight for bully-free school environments. I am sharing this infographic so visitors to Developing Minds Software can use it as part of your educational outreach and campaigns focused on bullying awareness.
