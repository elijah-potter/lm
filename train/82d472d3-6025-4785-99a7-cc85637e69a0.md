Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown ears and a heart full of joy. One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his best friend, a little bird named Bella, sitting on his windowsill.

"Good morning, Benny!" chirped Bella. "I saw a shiny blue butterfly flying by!"

Benny's eyes sparkled. "I wonder what that butterfly feels like?"

They both raced outside to meet the butterfly. As Benny approached, the butterfly fluttered gently. "Hello there!" Benny said, and the butterfly waved its wings.

"Can you teach me how to fly like you?" Benny asked Bella.

Bella smiled. "Of course! But first, we need to practice."

They made a big circle in the grass and began to flap their wings. Benny tried to fly, but his little legs kept slipping. Bella encouraged him, "Try again, Benny! You can do it!"

Soon, Benny's wings lifted a little higher. "I can see the sky!" Benny cheered.

The butterfly landed near them. "You’re doing great!" it said. "But remember, even superheroes practice."

Benny grinned. "I’ll remember!"

As the sun began to set, Benny and Bella waved goodbye to the butterfly. "Goodnight, little friend," Benny said.

The butterfly waved back. "Goodnight, Benny! Keep practicing!"

Benny curled up in his nest, dreaming of flying high into the stars. And that's how Benny learned that even the smallest bunny could dream big and reach for the sky.
