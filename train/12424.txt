A Seisen Digital Leader is a student who is part of a more comprehensive Digital Phoenix Team within the elementary school, whose role it is to support the use and further development of digital devices, including iPads, Chromebooks, and Macs.
Having been assembled through an application form and brief conversation ‘interview,’ the digital leaders meet weekly, on a Wednesday with continuous training and support given to developing specific device expertise and competencies with G-Suite and online learning platforms.
