**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, Ill. – August 22, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair, a beloved tradition celebrating agriculture, community, and entertainment, returns this year with a wide array of attractions for all ages. This year’s fair promises a vibrant atmosphere featuring livestock exhibits, thrilling carnival rides, live music performances, and a diverse selection of food vendors. Visitors can expect to see award-winning animals, participate in interactive demonstrations, and enjoy a celebration of local talent and heritage.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from Friday, August 30th, through Sunday, September 8th, 2024. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM. Special events are scheduled throughout the fair’s run, including a tractor pull competition, a demolition derby, and a fireworks display on the final evening. Detailed daily schedules will be available on the fair’s official website closer to the event.

Admission to the Sangamon County Fair is $15 for adults, $10 for children ages 6-12, and free for children five and under. Carnival ride tickets and wristbands will be available for purchase both online and at the fairgrounds. Advance ticket purchases are encouraged to avoid lines.
