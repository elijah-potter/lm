I've been a loyal user of Nexus and Pixel phones for a long time. I bought this phone to replace my Pixel 3, which had a fingerprint sensor on the back. I didn't really want a big phone, but the smaller 5a didn't have the better camera I wanted.
I'm used to unlocking my phone quickly by using the fingerprint sensor on the back. I don't like face unlock or other options. However, the fingerprint sensor under the glass on this phone was disappointing. It was hard to set up my fingerprint, and even when I did, it often took many tries to unlock the phone. It wasn't comfortable to press with my thumb or use two hands. This was a letdown because the old design with the rear sensor worked well.
On the positive side, the camera on this phone was impressive. I took some test photos and videos, and they turned out great.
Unfortunately, the phone is currently out of stock. Because of that and my issues with the fingerprint sensor, I decided to return it. I'm disappointed with Google, but I still appreciate the good things about the phone, like the camera.
