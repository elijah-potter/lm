The office doors creaked open as the last of the staff filed out, leaving the fluorescent hum of the fluorescent lights echoing. His watch glowed a sickly red—**5:08 PM**—but he had already arrived by the time the elevator doors closed. The boss’s voice crackled from the speakers: *"Team, tomorrow’s meeting’s at 9 AM sharp."* He groaned, tossing his keys onto the desk with a sigh.

His phone buzzed—an email from the intern: *"Sorry, late. Traffic was insane."* He smirked. The drive had been a nightmare: a semi had swerved into the on-ramp, blocking half the highway. He’d spent 47 minutes in bumper-to-bumper hell, eating a sad sandwich and counting down the minutes.

Now, he sat at his desk, rubbing his temples. The 9 AM meeting was just another day, but for once, he’d make it without the usual panic. The boss’s door creaked open again; a shadow crossed the hall. He exhaled. *"Alright, let’s get this show on the road."*
