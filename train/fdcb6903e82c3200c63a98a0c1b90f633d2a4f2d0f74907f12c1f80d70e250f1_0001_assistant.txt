Two daughters are competing for their father's love: Mei and Fēn. Fēn is striving for material success while Mei pursues charity work. At first, their father praises Fēn but eventually comes to value Mei's contributions more.

Li has recently lost her father to an illness. As she sorts through his estate, she comes across items that trigger fond memories. Throughout the story, she continues to talk to her father as if he's still there. She thinks maybe he can hear her from beyond the grave.

Mei and Li are children of divorced parents, who live with their mother. She tells them lies about their father. The girls visit him every other weekend and develop very different relationships with him. Mei trusts and adores her father while Li is influenced by her mother's lies.
