How to help your plant!

1. Take your plant out of its pot.
Be gentle and very careful so we do not injure our plant friend.

2. Give your plant's roots a tickle 
Loosen up the roots, spread them apart and make sure they have room to grow.

3. Dump out the old dirt
Remove most of the dirt that used to be in your plant pot and throw it away, ask your parents for help with this step.

4. Add the new dirt
Pour in some new dirt at the bottom of your new plant pot and give it a gentle tap to flatten it out. Place some rocks at the bottom of your pot if it doesn't have any holes.

5. Add your plant to its new pot
Put your plant friend into its new home, make sure it is in the middle! Then add more dirt around it until it won't fall over anymore.

6. Water your friend
Give your plant some love and water it, make sure not to pour too much, just enough.

Celebrate! You just re-potted a plant!
