If this is your first time having a pet cat in the house here is a list of items you should have.
1. A litter box with cat litter - this is so that the cat can go to the toilet whilst in the house.
2. Litter scoop - Poop a Scooper, for cleaning poop out of the litter box.
3. Variety of cat food both dry and wet - cats like to have some diversity in their diet so a choice of wet or dry is good.
4. A small pet carry bag -  essential for trips to the vet etc.
5. An assortment of toys for enrichment and play.
6. A pet bed may be a necessity depending on your cat, though some cats will find places they love to nap.
7. A few types of scratching posts. If you don't have something they can scratch, you may find your furniture or walls scratched up.
