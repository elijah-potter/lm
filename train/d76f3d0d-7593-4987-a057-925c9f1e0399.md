## Product Requirements Document: "Context" - Mobile Knowledge Consolidation

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently gather information from diverse sources—documents, emails, meeting notes, web pages—but struggle to synthesize and retain this information within a readily accessible, contextualized system, leading to decreased productivity and duplicated effort.

**Vision:** To empower knowledge workers with a mobile application that seamlessly captures, organizes, and connects disparate information, fostering deeper understanding and accelerating insight generation.

---

### User Personas

**1. Dr. Eleanor Vance - Research Scientist**

*   **Goal:** To efficiently manage and connect research papers, experimental data, and meeting notes to accelerate discovery and publication.
*   **Pain Points:** Overwhelmed by a growing volume of research materials; difficulty recalling specific details from past readings; frustration with disparate note-taking systems; time wasted searching for relevant information.

**2. Mark Olsen - Marketing Manager**

*   **Goal:** To quickly access and connect customer feedback, market research, and campaign performance data to inform strategic decisions and optimize marketing efforts.
*   **Pain Points:** Difficulty correlating data from various analytics platforms; inability to easily share insights with team members; feeling disconnected from the broader context of marketing campaigns; slow decision-making due to information silos.

**3. Sarah Chen - Consultant**

*   **Goal:** To build and maintain a comprehensive knowledge base of client interactions, project deliverables, and industry best practices for efficient service delivery and business development.
*   **Pain Points:** Risk of overlooking crucial details during client engagements; difficulty tracking progress across multiple projects; concern about knowledge loss when team members leave; inefficient onboarding of new consultants.

---

### Requirements

**1. Contextual Capture:** Users can capture information from web pages, documents (PDF, DOCX), and emails directly within the app.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Captured content is displayed clearly and retains original formatting.  Users can add custom titles and tags to each capture.  Captures are automatically timestamped.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 75% of users actively utilize the capture feature within the first week of use. Average capture rate of 3 captures per day per user.

**2. Relationship Mapping:** Users can create explicit relationships between captured items using a visual graph interface.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can define relationship types (e.g., “supports,” “contradicts,” “is an example of”). The graph visually represents these relationships. Users can easily edit and delete relationships.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 40% of users create at least three relationships within the first two weeks of use. Average number of relationships per user: 5.

**3. Intelligent Search & Discovery:** The app utilizes natural language processing to enable users to search for information using keywords and phrases, surfacing relevant captures and related items.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Search results prioritize items with higher relevance scores based on keyword matching and relationship connections.  Users can refine search queries using filters (e.g., tags, date range).
    *   **Success Metrics:**  Users locate desired information within three searches on average.  Search query success rate (finding relevant results): 85%.

**4. Tagging & Organization:** Users can assign custom tags to captured items to facilitate categorization and retrieval.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can create, edit, and delete tags. Tagging is searchable. Users can filter captures based on assigned tags.
    *   **Success Metrics:**  Average number of tags per user: 10. 60% of users actively utilize the tagging feature.

**5. Mobile-First Synchronization:** The app seamlessly synchronizes data across mobile devices (iOS and Android) and a web application.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Data synchronization occurs in near real-time.  Offline access to previously downloaded captures is supported.  Conflicts during synchronization are resolved gracefully, with user notification and options for reconciliation.
    *   **Success Metrics:**  Synchronization latency consistently under 2 seconds.  Zero reported data loss due to synchronization errors.



---

### Technical Considerations

*   **Data Storage:** Cloud-based storage (AWS S3 or similar) for scalability and accessibility. Local storage for offline access.
*   **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** Integration with a third-party NLP service (e.g., Google Cloud NLP, AWS Comprehend) to power intelligent search and relationship suggestions.
*   **Platform Compatibility:** Native iOS and Android applications. Responsive web application accessible via modern browsers.
*   **Security:** End-to-end encryption for data at rest and in transit. Robust authentication and authorization mechanisms.

### Risks

*   **NLP Accuracy:** Reliance on a third-party NLP service introduces a risk of inaccurate relationship suggestions or search results. Mitigation: Ongoing monitoring of NLP performance and potential for fine-tuning.
*   **User Adoption:** The app’s visual graph interface may require a learning curve for some users. Mitigation:  Onboarding tutorials and contextual help.
*   **Synchronization Conflicts:** Complex data relationships and offline access could lead to synchronization conflicts. Mitigation:  Robust conflict resolution mechanisms and user-friendly reconciliation tools.




### Non-Goals

*   Direct integration with third-party note-taking applications (e.g., Evernote, OneNote).
*   Real-time collaborative editing.
*   Advanced data visualization beyond the relationship graph.
*   Support for languages beyond English.
