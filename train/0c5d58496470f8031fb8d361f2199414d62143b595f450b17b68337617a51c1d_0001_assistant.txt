Holi is a two-day Hindu festival that is celebrated all over the world. Most popular in India, Nepal, and Pakistan, the festival happens on the last full moon of the lunar month, which is often in February or March. Hinduism is a religion practiced by many people, and Holi is a religious celebration of Spring. It is a time to say goodbye to winter, appreciate colors, and hope for fertile land and future great harvests.  

Holi is also a time to close gaps between social classes, generations, and people of various religions. Though a Hindu holiday, many people are encouraged to join and respectfully celebrate with them. People party with friends and family but also with complete strangers. They all enjoy music, color, and sweets in many ways. One way is throwing gulal - a colorful powder - in the air and at each other, so people leave celebrations much more colorful than when they arrived.
