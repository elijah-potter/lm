The "Fail Whale" was an illustration that became a notable part of Twitter's identity during its early years. It was displayed whenever the website experienced an outage or was over capacity, which, due to the platform's rapid growth and technical challenges, happened quite frequently. The image, created by artist Yiying Lu, depicted a whale being lifted by a flock of birds, and it became an iconic representation of Twitter's downtime, leading to a certain sense of camaraderie among users who were inconvenienced at the same time. However, as Twitter matured and invested in its infrastructure to ensure better reliability and stability, these downtime periods decreased significantly. By 2013, the Fail Whale was rarely seen, and it was officially retired by Twitter that same year.
