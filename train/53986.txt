We hear about the calamities causing environmental harm every day. But how exactly concerned are you about the health of your planet? With the bad news about global warming, desertification, endangered wildlife, dying oceans, and forestation, it is very difficult for us to know from where to start in order to save our planet. You might think that an action of one single person is not going to make much of a difference, but trust me, there are many ways by which you can actually help. So, here are 19 ways by which you can contribute in saving your planet. All you need is to change few of your habits a bit.
19. Stop littering
Littering can be a threat for human health, wildlife, and even water quality. Do not litter anything randomly on the roads. If you do not find any trash can or recycle bin nearby, then secure your litter until it can be discarded.
