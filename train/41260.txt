The air that we inhale is not satisfactory. It has damaging carbon dioxide discharges and noxious petrol fumes. And, omnipresent is the ozone layer that’s getting depleted regularly. With all these, you may wish to stay indoors. However, your stay indoor could be worse than you stay outside. The impact of detrimental chemicals and more materials on indoor air can be 10 times that of outdoor air. The fact is that both Indoor and outdoor pollution is bad for health.
People don’t give much importance to the fact that their skin can take on an older look due to pollution. That air pollution is detrimental for the skin is an established fact. Science says that it’s among the prime reasons for skin damage. Toxins that are present in the air is a reason that skin ages prematurely. This is more apparent on the neck, face, and hands. What is important is to identify the problem, the symptoms, and find simple ways to thwart, or minimize the damage. The common sources of Indoor pollutants include tobacco smoke, furniture, air fresheners, hygiene products, printers, cooking and more indoor activities. Air pollutants that the present researchers have more interest in are nitrogen oxides, ozone, and bioaerosols.
Going by the studies diverse pollutants bring about diverse immunological & non-immunological responses in the people who’re exposed. Improvement of the quality of indoor air is possible. A number of ways of making this possible are:
- By keeping the floors fresh
- By maintaining a proper humidity level
- By not smoking inside the house
- By testing for radon
- By not using synthetic fragrances
As a person ages, the skin naturally starts losing moisture as well as suppleness. However, changes can be made for slowing down the process of skin aging.
A case in point is indoor air pollution that happens from cooking by the use of firewood or coal. It’s been shown to fasten skin aging in northern Chinese ladies. It significantly increased wrinkles under the eyes, wrinkles on the forehead, and wrinkles on arms and hands.
Chemicals that include organic compounds and phthalates can also affect the skin adversely.
Latest studies established the association of the density of phthalates in the dust indoor and allergic reactions on the skin. Among the prime resources of phthalate esters that are present in the house are PVC materials. They’re a very common material in flooring and wall covering materials, synthetic leather, shower curtains, cosmetics and more consumer items.
The emission of Phthalates into the atmosphere and indoor dust is relentless. The reason is that they don’t have a chemical bonding with the PVC structure. Environmental degradation cannot affect the environment in the house. As a result, PAHs from burning coal, gasoline, and more that are associated with dust stay for long. As a result, densities in the dust in the house are a great deal higher to that in open-air dust and earth.
Particulate matter is the cause of great oxidative stress to the skin, causing early skin aging. Research has revealed that a certain factor increases the number of particulate matters that insides of houses add. That factor is the traffic fumes that come in through open windows.
Now that we know that indoor pollution can be worse than outdoor pollution how can we control it? We must take measures for maintaining clean indoor air for avoiding the health dilemmas that occur from poor air quality. An easy way of maintaining healthy indoor air is by ventilating your residence on a systematic basis. During pollen months or events like neighboring wildfire smoke, you should keep windows closed.
You should also control the resources of pollution in the house. When you’ve sufficient space
- Let new products off-gas outside the walls or
- Let some days pass before you enter a room that has an off-gassing item
Ensure that moisture and dust aren’t collecting in the bends and cracks of your house. The reason is that for dust mites, mold, and other contaminants thrive in moisture and dust.
Identifying pollutant sources, ecological control and prevention continue to be the most efficient countermeasures. Systematic cleaning of filters and air conditioners are going to help. You have got to stay off pollutants that are the result of traffic emissions that enter the house open windows. You must also think about the use of moisturizers, antioxidants, and barrier protecting skincare items.
