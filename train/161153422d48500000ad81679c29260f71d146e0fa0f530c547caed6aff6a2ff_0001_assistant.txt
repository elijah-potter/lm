Healthy Relationship recipe

Ingredients :

1 cup of happiness 
1 cup of honesty and transparency 
1 cup of compromise
1 cup of self-care
1 cup of agreeing to disagree 
1 cup kindness 
1 cup of healthy boundaries

Start with knowing that your happiness is up to you and it is yours to own. Loved ones, friends, and even a partner can enhance happiness but two independently happy people are the best combination. 
Next, make sure you are transparent and honest about your feelings so that there is no room for miscommunication. 
Mix in a heaping cup of compromise to create a healthy balance between yourself and your partner, so that one person doesn't carry all the weight or stress. 
Melt in a cup of self-care to make sure that you both have time to engage in the interests and hobbies that you both are passionate about individually. 
At this time, start preheating the oven and prepare to agree to disagree. It is important to agree to disagree and share different opinions, as two people will not always agree with one another. 
Be sure to add this very special ingredient, kindness. Without it, this recipe would not taste as sweet. Be kind to each other, even in times of stress or despair. 
Finally, make sure to establish healthy boundaries with people that can be an outside strain; an ex, in-laws, or even parents. Don't allow outside forces to dictate your thoughts and feelings and focus on the two people in the relationship.

Voila! You have yourself a happy and healthy relationship!
