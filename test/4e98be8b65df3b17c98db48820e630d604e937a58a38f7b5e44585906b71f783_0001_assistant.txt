We all make mistakes. It’s inevitable. No one is perfect and that means we all commit blunders from time to time. They can range from small errors in judgment to huge ones like marrying the wrong person.

Regardless of what kind of mistake you make, there’s always something to learn from it. Many times, we’re in such a hurry to move on from the error that we overlook a great opportunity to find out what went wrong and glean some valuable information that can help us in the future. 

Whether you seem to constantly fail in personal relationships or with clients at work, one way you can begin to turn your failures around is to stop and examine what went wrong and why. This will take you being honest with yourself. It might also help you to make notes in a journal. Think about these aspects of the failure:
•	Exactly what went wrong?
•	Was one person to blame more than another?
•	How did you react?
•	Were your actions and reactions helping or hurting the situation?
•	Has this type of thing come up before?
