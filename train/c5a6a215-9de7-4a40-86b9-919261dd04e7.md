Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown ears and a heart full of joy. Every morning, Benny would hop out to play with his best friend, a bright yellow butterfly named Sunny.

One sunny day, Benny found a shiny red berry in the garden. "Yum!" he said, and took a big bite. But then he felt a strange feeling in his tummy. "Oh no," Benny thought, "I don’t like the taste of this berry!"

Benny called Sunny, "Come on, Sunny, let’s find another snack." They searched high and low in the garden, but the berry was gone. Suddenly, Benny remembered something his grandma had told him: "Eating the wrong food can make you feel sick, but a little hug from a friend can help!"

Benny hugged Sunny tight and whispered, "I’m okay now." Soon, Benny felt better and had a big, happy smile. They both knew that being brave and asking for help was the best way to feel good again.

From that day on, Benny and Sunny shared all the tasty fruits and vegetables they found, and Benny learned that even when things go wrong, a kind heart and a strong friend can make everything okay. And so, they continued their adventures, full of laughter and love.
