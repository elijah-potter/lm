A corporate policy change requiring employees to adopt a new internal communication platform was initially framed as a straightforward upgrade to efficiency. However, the decision faced resistance rooted in personal habits and workplace dynamics. Some teams were already using legacy tools, while others feared the transition would disrupt established workflows. Cultural differences emerged, with certain departments prioritizing face-to-face collaboration, making digital adoption feel disruptive. The decision hinged on balancing immediate operational gains with unintended social friction, requiring iterative pilot phases and stakeholder engagement to mitigate resistance.
