The pet bunny prepared for the Easter hunt,
He wanted to prove that he wasn't just a runt,
He searched and searched for the colorful eggs,
He ran and jumped and worked out his legs,
The bunny found egg after egg with glee,
He couldn't wait for the other bunnies to see,
At the end of the hunt, they all cheered at his stash,
They gave him carrots and hay and welcomed him back with a splash!
