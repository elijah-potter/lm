- **Tropical Rainforest (Equatorial):**
  - **Day:** 28–32°C (82–90°F)
  - **Night:** 22–26°C (72–79°F)
  - Humidity: ~80% (constant)

- **Desert (Sahara/North America):**
  - **Day:** 40–50°C (104–122°F)
  - **Night:** 15–25°C (59–77°F)
  - Dry, minimal precipitation

- **Arctic Circle (Summer):**
  - **Day:** 10–18°C (50–64°F)
  - **Night:** -10 to -2°C (14–28°F)
  - Polar winds, permafrost

- **Temperate Forest (Midwest USA):**
  - **Summer:** 25–30°C (77–86°F)
  - **Winter:** -5 to 5°C (23–41°F)
  - Seasonal shifts, moderate rain

- **Tropical Desert (Kalahari):**
  - **Day:** 35–45°C (95–113°F)
  - **Night:** 18–25°C (64–77°F)
  - Scattered clouds, rare snow

- **Alpine Tundra (Rocky Mountains):**
  - **Summer:** 5–15°C (41–59°F)
  - **Winter:** -10 to 0°C (14–32°F)
  - Snow cover year-round, sparse vegetation

- **Subtropical (Florida):**
  - **Year-round:** 24–30°C (75–86°F)
  - Low seasonal variation, high humidity

- **Polar Desert (Antarctica):**
  - **Winter:** -50 to -30°C (-58 to -22°F)
  - **Summer:** -10 to -2°C (14–28°F)
  - Extreme cold, dry ice

- **Mediterranean Climate (Spain):**
  - **Summer:** 25–35°C (77–95°F)
  - **Winter:** 10–15°C (50–59°F)
  - Dry summers, mild wet winters

- **Boreal Forest (Canada):**
  - **Summer:** 15–25°C (59–77°F)
  - **Winter:** -30 to 0°C (-22 to 32°F)
  - Coniferous trees, long nights.
