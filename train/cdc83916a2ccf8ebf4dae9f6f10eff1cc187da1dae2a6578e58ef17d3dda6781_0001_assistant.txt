
A Houston family is counting their blessings tonight after returning from a family trip, only to find their home engulfed in flames. 

-Homeowners Lee and Jen Nelson say they have much to be thankful for after last night's terrifying ordeal. Their last-minute decision to leave for the Bahamas for the weekend probably saved their lives. The Nelsons arrived home last night, during the worst of the inferno.

As last night's fire raged through the home that the Nelsons had lived in for 3 decades, several neighbors on the 1300 block of Cotton did their best to put extinguish it with hoses and water buckets. Firefighters were called in shortly after 5 pm to extinguish the blaze. Sadly, the house was consumed in 2 hours. The cause of the fire is unknown, and arson has not been ruled out at this time. 

The Nelsons are asking for donations at this time. If you wish to be a good Samaritan, please click the GoFundMe link on their Facebook page.
