Once upon a time, in a cozy green house with a big tree in the backyard, lived a little bunny named Pip. Pip loved to hop and play in the sunny garden.

One sunny morning, Pip heard a tiny *squeak* from under a flowerpot. Curious, Pip dug with his tiny paws and found a lost blue butterfly with a tangled wing. "Oh no," Pip thought. "The butterfly can't fly now!"

Pip decided to help. He gently untangled the wing with his tiny claws and whispered, "Don't worry, sweetie. You'll be flying soon!" The butterfly thanked Pip with a soft *flutter*.

Soon, the butterfly's wing was all mended, and Pip helped it take its first big hop into the sky. "Wow!" said Pip. "Look how fast and free you can fly!"

After that, Pip and the butterfly became the best friends. Every day, they played together in the garden, chasing butterflies, collecting shiny leaves, and having the most wonderful adventures. And they lived happily ever after, dreaming of more exciting things to do.

The end.
