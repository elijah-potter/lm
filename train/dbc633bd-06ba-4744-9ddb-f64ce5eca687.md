A bustling urban thoroughfare lined with towering skyscrapers, their glass facades reflecting bright sunlight. Pedestrians in high heels and cargo pants hurry along wide sidewalks, some in groups, others alone, all moving in the same direction. Street vendors push carts selling fresh fruit, handmade crafts, and coffee. Traffic jams form at intersections, with cars honking as drivers weave through. Above, a constant hum of airplanes signals the city’s proximity to an international airport. The scent of street food—grilled meats, spiced noodles, and fried snacks—mixes with exhaust fumes from diesel trucks and buses. Neon signs flicker in languages of multiple nationalities, advertising everything from luxury watches to budget hotels. The air is thick with the energy of commerce and constant motion.
