The man lost his dog,
his head in a fog;
He sat on a log,
Before taking a jog.

He searched the wood,
he searched it really good,
Hoping to finish soon,
so he could get himself some food.

The dog must be near,
he thought "Have no fear,
Soon he'll be here",
and he'll celebrate with a beer.

The dog came through the clearing,
he must've had terrible hearing;
His owner kept calling,
but the dog was unfearing.

The dog was unharmed,
in fact, it seemed charmed;
Being with its owner,
in his loving arms.

Once again they were together,
like birds of a feather;
Through good or bad weather,
like they were joined by a tether.

They quickly walked home,
to the bathroom, they roam;
Gave the dog a bath with foam,
as they sat under their dome.
