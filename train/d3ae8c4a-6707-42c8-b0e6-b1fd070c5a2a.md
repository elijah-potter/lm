Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a garden full of flowers, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop, play, and explore his sunny backyard. One morning, as Benny was nibbling on a clover, he heard a tiny *splash* near the pond. Curious, he peeked over the edge and saw a little blue fish named Finn wiggling its tail.

"Hello, Benny!" Finn said, swimming up. "I found a shiny, round, sparkly rock that feels soft and cool. Do you think it’s a treasure?"

Benny’s ears perked up. "Treasure? Oh, I bet it’s a magic rock!" He picked it up carefully and placed it in his pocket. Soon, they both spotted a friendly ladybug named Lila sitting on a dandelion.

"Benny and Finn, can you help me?" Lila asked. "My wings are a little fuzzy, and I need some smooth stones to polish them. The sun’s making them dull."

Benny and Finn exchanged a look. "Sure, we’ll help!" Benny said, and they gathered shiny rocks from the garden. They sat together near the pond, polishing Lila’s wings with their new treasure.

As they worked, Benny noticed something magical happen—the rocks sparkled brighter, and Lila’s wings shined like a rainbow. "Wow!" Benny gasped. "It’s like magic!"

Lila smiled. "Magic isn’t always shiny or loud. Sometimes it’s kindness and teamwork." The three friends played together for hours, laughing and sharing stories. By sunset, Benny’s heart felt full, and he knew the best treasure of all was making friends and helping others.

Finn swam over, "Let’s do it again tomorrow!"

Benny grinned. "Yes, let’s!"

And from that day on, Benny and Finn always looked for new adventures together, knowing that the best treasures were the people—and the joy—they shared.
