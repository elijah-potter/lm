Aquatic Plants oxygenate the water to provide shelter for fish and aquarium life while keeping them healthier and your tank looking better.
Water Wisteria’s are one of the world’s most widely used Plants in planted aquariums. It can grow very tall but with frequent pruning, the plant can stay compact and low growing. Stems can be cut and replanted for propagation.
Planting Water Wisteria Plants is easy; simply throw it into your water. Really – if you just drop it in, it’ll start growing. You can also choose where it will go by burying the roots in some gravel or tying it onto a piece of material.
