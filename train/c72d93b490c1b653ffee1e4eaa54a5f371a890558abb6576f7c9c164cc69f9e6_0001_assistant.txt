1️⃣  Match your investments to your risk comfort level. 

Do you want to have a nice little fund built by the time your baby is sending out college applications?

Or are you hoping to retire when you're 30? 🥳

Set your timeline according to your goals and comfort zone. 

Why make an aggressive but risky investment that'll keep you up at night if you can take a safer bet and still reach your goal? 

2️⃣ Look for ways to minimize miscellaneous costs. 

Taxes, fees, transaction costs —

They all eat into profits. 💰

Start accounts and funds that offer tax breaks or other advantages:

- IRAs & HRAs
- UGMA/UTMA accounts
- Education Savings accounts

And be cautious of the fees charged by financial planners or advisers. 🤑

Work with professionals who have a flat rate instead of those who charge a percentage of the assets they manage. 

3️⃣ Stick with the plan. 

You might think that the hardest part of financial planning is creating the plan.

It's not. 

The hardest part is STICKING to the plan. 😅

We've all been there — you throw thousands into something that promises returns...eventually.

But along the way the market drops. 

Your first instinct is to bail.

FAST.

But that rarely accomplishes anything besides losing your money. 

On the flip side —

Maybe there's a new hot investment opportunity your buddy just told you about. 

And now you want to pull everything to put all of your eggs into this very promising basket. 

Don't. 🚫

Stick to the plan.

You made it for a reason.
