This packet includes eight math centers perfect for the month of April!
Rainy Equations: The students will use the number line provided to determine the missing sign on each equation. The students will Velcro an addition or subtraction symbol on each card. (sums up to 10)
It's Raining Ten Frames: The students will count the double ten frames and Velcro the correct number onto each card. (11-20)
Where does the Bee Bee-long?: The students will solve each addition problem. Then, the students will sort their answer cards according to the sum. (sums: 6, 7 and 8)
Comparing Colors: The students will compare two numbers and record the correct symbol <, > or =. (numbers 20-100)
Recycling Shapes: The students will sort shapes to determine if each is 2-D or 3-D.
Adding with Umbrellas: The students will use a ten frame to determine the missing addend in number sentences that equals ten.
Find the Missing Raindrop: The students will use an Expo marker to record the missing raindrop. (numbers 10-100)
What Time Is It? The students will match clocks with the correct time card. (time from the hour and half hour)
Each activity comes with a recording worksheet and an additional worksheet to help reinforce these skills.
