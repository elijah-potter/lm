**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, Ill. – August 2, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, promising a celebration of agricultural heritage, community spirit, and family-friendly entertainment. This year's fair will feature a diverse range of exhibits, demonstrations, and activities showcasing the best of Sangamon County. Visitors can anticipate livestock shows, home and garden displays, arts and crafts competitions, and a vibrant marketplace offering locally sourced goods. Traditional fair favorites, such as carnival rides and games, will also be present, ensuring an enjoyable experience for all ages.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 16th through August 20th, 2024, at the County Fairgrounds in Springfield. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM. A special fireworks display is scheduled for Saturday, August 18th, following the evening entertainment. Daily admission provides access to all general exhibits and demonstrations. Separate tickets are required for carnival rides and certain premium events.

Admission prices are as follows: Adults (ages 13-64) – $12, Seniors (ages 65+) – $8, Children (ages 6-12) – $6, and children five and under are free. Discounted pre-sale tickets are available online at www.sangamoncountyfair.org. Parking is $10 per vehicle.

**Headline Events:**

*   Livestock Show & Auction
*   Demolition Derby
*   Professional Rodeo
*   Tractor Pull Competition
*   Blue Ribbon Baking Contest
*   Saturday Night Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters
