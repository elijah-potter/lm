1) A robot may not injure a human being or, through inaction, allow a human being to come to harm. Beep, boop! I am not allowed to hurt a human being or allow a human being to get hurt without doing anything about it.
2)  A robot must obey the orders given to it by human beings except where such orders would conflict with the First Law.  Beep! I must always do as I'm instructed to do by a human being unless the instructions will result in that human or another human being will get hurt.
3)  A robot must protect its own existence as long as such protection does not conflict with the First or Second Law. Beep, boop! I must avoid any actions that may lead to me being destroyed unless it is to prevent a human from being injured or  I was directly ordered to take such actions.
