This elementary counseling activity uses hero sandwich fixings as the center of the learning experience. Each ingredient has a positive or negative character trait printed on it. Students pick their bread, ham, lettuce and tomato based on the words that describe them.
Kids have such fun making their sandwiches while you will gain insight into how they view themselves. A good time guaranteed for all!
