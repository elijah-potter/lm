
Once upon a time, there was a cat named Isaac. Isaac was no ordinary cat. He could talk! When people heard about Isaac's ability, Isaac became famous. He didn't like the fame because he was self-conscious that he didn't have a fur coat like other cats. Isaac was a Sphynx cat. Isaac had a big dream. He wanted to become a police officer. However, cats don't make good police officers. They're too light, and they can't handle police equipment with their paws. 

One day, Isaac met the police chief. The police chief was impressed with Isaac and offered him a job as a police interrogator. Isaac was thrilled and spent all his time reading books about psychology and learning to be a good interrogator. After a few weeks on the job, Isaac learned that the queen's famous jewel necklace had been stolen, and the prime suspect was a dog name Fido. Fido could also speak. Isaac would be interrogating Fido.  

Isaac did a great job interrogating Fido. After a few hours, Isaac was able to secure valuable information from Fido. It turned out that the dog's owner, Brett Hamlin, was the real culprit. Brett was a famous jewel thief. The police captured Brett and Isaac was given a key to the city.
