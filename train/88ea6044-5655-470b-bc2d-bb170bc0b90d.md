*The kitchen hummed with silence. Rain tapped against the window like a slow, rhythmic heartbeat. Across the room, Clara sat at the worn wooden table, fingers tracing the edge of her coffee cup. Her gaze met Jake’s for the first time since the argument—the one that left words unsaid.*

*He stood by the stove, hands deep in the black liquid of the pot. His posture was rigid, shoulders pulled tight. The steam curled around his face like a ghost.*

*She exhaled slowly, almost as if she could pull the words from the air between them. But nothing came. Instead, the weight of what was unspoken settled between them, heavier than any argument ever could.*

*He turned, his face a mask of controlled emotion. "We don’t have to say it," he murmured, voice low. "But it’s still there."*

*She met his eyes. A pause. Then—nothing. Just the quiet of the room, the way the rain still fell, and the understanding that some things were meant to be left unsaid.*
