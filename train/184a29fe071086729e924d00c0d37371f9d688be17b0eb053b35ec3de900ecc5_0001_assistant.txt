Clarrisa was packing up things in her grandfather's house after his funeral. It had been a sad day, and all she wanted to do was hide under the covers and cry, but duty called, and she really needed to have the house packed up before she left for her home the following day. She had driven halfway across the country to get to the funeral in time and she was not looking forward to the drive home - alone and sad. 
As she grabbed a book off the shelf, Clarrisa heard a *CLICK* and suddenly the bookshelf swung backward to reveal a tunnel. As she stepped into the tunnel, Clarrisa heard a whooshing noise and then she was knocked onto her rear. As she stood to straighten herself, she realized she wasn't in the house anymore. In fact, she didn't know where she was at all. The house was nowhere to be seen. In its place was cold and uninviting cobblestone. Suddenly a CLOMP CLOMP CLOMP came from behind and Clarrisa had to roll out of the way to avoid being hit by a horse and buggy. "What in the world?!"
Getting to her feet, Clarrisa noticed that the streets were completely dated. The women were wearing long gowns, despite the heat. She didn't see any streetlights or modern conveniences. "Toto, I don't think we're in Kansas anymore...or the 21st century, for that matter," Clarrisa said to herself. 
Little did she know that in a few short days, she would be on a wild adventure with her new friends as she struggled to get back to her time. She would soon be in a fight between the future and the past, and she will have to find her place before it was too late.
