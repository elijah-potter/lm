The train’s horn blares as it pulls into the station, but the passenger rushes in, breathless. The platform is packed—every seat taken, the crowd shifting like liquid. Their bag slams onto a bench as they press past, elbows colliding. The ticket stub in their pocket feels like a weight.

The seat beside them is empty—just a half-eaten sandwich, a crumpled napkin. They slip in, heart still hammering, and exhale.

The train moves. A window reveals a cityscape in slow motion, the familiar skyline blurring into relief. The door slides open, and they step out just as the conductor checks their ticket. They smile, nodding, and let the crowd swallow them whole.
