The Manhattan district attorney's office announced they're returning 20 artifacts, amounting to over $20 million, to Greece to start the repatriation of Greek culture and heritage. One artifact is an Eid Mar (Ides of March) coin celebrating the assassination of Julius Caesar in 44 BCE. One of three gold Eid Mar coins, it was sold in Germany, smuggled to London, and auctioned to an American collector in 2020 for $4.2 million. When the buyer learned it was stolen, he willingly returned it.
