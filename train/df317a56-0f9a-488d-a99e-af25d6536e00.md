**Title:** *The Hollow Mirror*
**Genre:** Horror/Drama (Post-Revision)
**Setting:** A crumbling Victorian mansion, abandoned since the Great Fire of ’89. Rain seeps through cracked plaster. The protagonist, **Elara Vey**, moves furniture with deliberate precision, her fingers tracing the edges of a half-finished painting—a figure half-erased, staring blankly at the viewer.

---

**Key Revisions:**
1. **Character:** Elara was originally a "desperate heiress" fleeing creditors. Now, she’s a **former actress** who abandoned theater after a scandal (the "famous face" was actually a forgery). The painting’s subject was her doppelgänger, a stage-actor clone of her, left in the mansion after a failed performance.
2. **Mystery:** The clone wasn’t a rival; it was a **failed experiment** by Elara’s estranged brother, **Dr. Alistair Vey**, who sought to "fix" her appearance after an accident. The fire destroyed his lab, but the clone survived, now half-alive—its mind trapped in the painting.
3. **Symbolism:**
   - **The Rain:** Washes away Elara’s past roles (auditions, reviews), revealing raw emotion.
   - **The Mansion’s Windows:** Glazed with cracked glass; some reflect her reflection (normal), others show the clone’s distorted face.
4. **Climax:** Elara finds a hidden compartment in the painting’s frame. Inside: a **journal** where Alistair records his failures and the clone’s growing awareness. The clone whispers her name in the dark: *"You kept me alive. Why?"*
5. **Ending (Ambiguous):**
   - Option A: Elara burns the journal, but the clone’s voice echoes in her dreams. She quits revising entirely, leaving the mansion as a monument to her "failure" as an artist.
   - Option B: She smashes the painting, but the shards of glass **paint the clone’s face onto her own reflection**—now they’re one.
