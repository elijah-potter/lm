A man hesitated before opening the door, clutching his tie awkwardly. *"Good afternoon,"* he began, voice cracking. *"I was just wondering if it’d be—"* The elevator door hissed shut, leaving him staring at the reflection of his own blunder: a flushed face, a stiff smile, and the faint scent of regret. He swallowed. *"Uh… excuse me?"* The elevator doors opened again—this time with a woman who already had her hand on the exit button. *"I’m sorry for the inconvenience,"* he added, tripping over his own tongue. She blinked. *"That’s fine,"* she said, already stepping out. The man’s jaw dropped. The world had just gotten… *smaller*.
