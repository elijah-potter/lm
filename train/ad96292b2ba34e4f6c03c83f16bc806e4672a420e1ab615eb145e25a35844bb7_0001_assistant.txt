1. Domestic rabbits need a regulated environment to protect against heat exhaustion or hypothermia. Wild rabbits do not have this problem. 
2. Wild rabbits use tunnel systems called warrens and include rooms for nesting and sleeping.
3. Rabbits are very social creatures and live in large groups called colonies.
4. Dusk and dawn is when they search for food.
5.  Predators include owls, hawks, eagles, falcons, wild dogs, feral cats and ground squirrels
6. Running fast and for long periods of time is an evolutionary adaptation for rabbits.
