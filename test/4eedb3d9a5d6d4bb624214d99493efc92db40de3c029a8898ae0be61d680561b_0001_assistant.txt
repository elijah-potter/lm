Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Willow who lived in a clearing in the middle of a dark, deep wood. Willow liked exploring, and although she had lived in the woods her entire eight years of life, she still found new things to discover. One of those things was a well, which her parents told her to stay away from. "A witch lives in the well!" her mother warned. "A rat might crawl out of it!" her father explained. These dangers did not scare Willow because she was a brave, bold little girl. One day when her parents were away at the market, Willow took her doll Sadie and went into the woods in search of the well. It surprised her to feel a little nervous as she approached it. Were her parents right? Willow took a deep breath and leaned over the edge of the well. At first, all she could see was darkness, but then she began to see a person's face emerge. Was it the witch? Willow's heart thudded, then calmed as she met the figure's eyes. She realized she was looking at her own reflection in the water below.
