The radio crackled to life in the dimly lit cabin of the old fishing boat *SS Maris*. Captain Elias Voss gripped the mic, his voice steady despite the trembling in his hands.

*"Ladies and gentlemen, this is your captain. We have received a distress signal. It’s coming from the north. A ship, possibly the *Aurora*, has been reported missing near the icebergs. The crew hasn’t responded in over an hour. If you’re still here, prepare for the worst. The weather’s turning—storms are coming. We’re running on fumes. No food, no water, just enough for one more night. If you’re alive, get ready to abandon ship. If you’re dead, at least you’re not alone."*
