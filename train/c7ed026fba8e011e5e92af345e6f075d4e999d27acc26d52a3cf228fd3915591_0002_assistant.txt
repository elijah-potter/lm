When going to your first convention, you should go in with a game plan. You will be overwhelmed when you walk in, there are so many things vying for your attention and it can be hard to decide which you want to encounter first. Make a plan to sort out your day and write down all the things you absolutely cannot miss and start with those. This will help to ensure you don't end up spreading yourself too thin and you get to enjoy all the things you love.
