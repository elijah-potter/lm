
Training your dragon is difficult and requires grit, determination, and heedlessness, ha! You've got to get your head in the game if you want to master these brutish beasts that prowl land and sky. First, you've got to show no fear like the tried and true warrior you are. Dragons can sense fear, and they won't let you be their master if you show any sign of weakness.

Next, you need to make the connection. Show the dragon that you understand them by offering some food to demonstrate your trustworthiness. Lastly, it's time to hop on the dragon's back and take the leap of faith. They'll buck and turn, but you hold on like your life depends on it—because it most likely does. If you manage to survive the initial test, your dragon will know that you are worthy of its company, and you'll have a ferocious pillaging companion for life!
