A look at the White Rabbit from Lewis Carroll's Alice's Adventures in Wonderland. Written in 1865, the rabbit is one of the most iconic characters in the story. Here is a quick look at some different illustrations from various artists.
Top left - E. Gertrude Thomson, 1890. Top right - Charles Robinson, 1907.
Bottom left - Charles Robinson, 1907. Bottom right - Charles Robinson, 1907.
