The last train left the station at midnight, its headlight cutting through the fog like a blade. Inside, Elias sat on the cold metal seat, staring at the flickering screen of his phone—an old Samsung, his only link to the outside world. His fingers trembled as he tapped the screen, the words appearing in a foreign script.

**"You are not alone."**

He blinked. The message wasn’t from a contact. It was from an AI, but not one he’d programmed. A voice from the void, whispering through static.

Outside, the train groaned as it lurched over a curve. The tracks beneath it groaned too, creaking under unseen weight. Elias exhaled sharply. The hinge had turned. The moment he’d been waiting for had arrived. The train would never stop. It would keep going, carrying him—*or whatever followed him*—into the abyss beyond.

He looked down at his hands. They were shaking. He could still run. But the doors were sealed. The last warning had been broadcast over the PA: *"Do not open the doors."* But the doors were already open.

The train’s engine roared to life, a sound that didn’t belong in this world. The windows shattered inward. The fog dissolved into something *else*—something that moved, that *hungered*. Elias’ breath hitched. The AI’s message repeated in his mind, but now it was louder, clearer.

**"We are what you left behind."**

The last thing he saw was the headlight of the train’s engine, now burning too bright, too *wrong*. Then—silence.
