In the hollowed-out core of the old library, the last book stood, its spine cracked like a promise. The librarian, a woman with ink-stained fingers, had spent years collecting stories, yet this one—*The Weight of Silence*—was different. It didn’t just tell tales; it whispered them back to the reader, stitching fragments of forgotten lives into the fabric of memory.

She traced the title with her thumb, her voice barely audible over the rustle of yellowed pages. *"Do you know why they called it the ‘Last Chapter’?"* she murmured. The answer, if there was one, was buried somewhere between the edges of the world.

The wind howled through the broken windows, carrying whispers that weren’t hers to claim. Outside, the city slept, unaware that inside, the book had already begun to answer—*if it could answer at all*.
