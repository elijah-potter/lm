The sky darkened abruptly as the first light of dawn was replaced by a sudden, eerie glow—pale violet streaks slicing through the horizon. Shadows stretched long and jagged across the alley, the air thick with an electric hum. A flickering streetlamp cast unnatural shadows on the damp pavement, their edges blurred by an unseen current. The streetlights flickered, then died entirely, plunging the area into near darkness before rebounding with a harsh, distorted blue-white brightness, like a camera flash frozen in time. The sudden shift made the street feel alive, as if something unseen had just turned on the lights.
