This is a set of two back to school themed interactive books.
The first book is What Do You See? This book has students use visual icons to create sentences in a book. The book has a repetitive sentence..."I see _____ when I go back to school" that is repeated throughout the 10 pages. Students use a choice of 10 visual icons to complete the sentence on each page.
The second book is Back to School Activities. This book also contains a repetitive sentence that is repeated throughout. The sentence is: "I can ______ at school." Students choose from 10 different icons to complete this sentence.
Complete, simple directions on how to assemble your interactive books are included in the packet.
