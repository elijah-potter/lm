The guard, trained to enforce strict adherence to protocols, kept their radio silent—no call was made. Instead, they adjusted their clipboard to note the bypass as "minor deviation" in their log, while quietly signaling the forklift driver to continue.

The crate contained critical research samples, and the team had secured a waiver from the lab director earlier that day. The guard’s supervisor, aware of the project’s urgency, had even authorized a temporary exception for the trip, ensuring no permanent records were altered. The guard’s actions were a calculated flexibility: no violation, but the system’s loopholes were stretched to serve a legitimate need.
