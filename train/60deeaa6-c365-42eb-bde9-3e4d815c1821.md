## Reclaiming Resilience: The Willow Creek Pocket Park Transformation

**Executive Summary**

The City of Oakhaven faces increasing challenges from climate change, including more frequent and intense storm events and rising summer temperatures. This proposal outlines a transformative project to revitalize the currently underutilized and neglected Willow Creek Pocket Park, located at the intersection of Elm Street and Maple Avenue. Through strategic design and community collaboration, we will convert this space into a climate-resilient community asset: a demonstration site for stormwater management, a shaded urban oasis offering respite from heat, and a vibrant gathering place fostering social connection. This project directly addresses the City’s Climate Action Plan goals, enhances neighborhood quality of life, and provides a replicable model for future green infrastructure investments. We request $75,000 in municipal funding, which, combined with $25,000 in committed private donations and volunteer labor, will unlock the full potential of Willow Creek Pocket Park.

**Project Plan & Timeline**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park Transformation will unfold across four distinct phases, each with clearly defined deliverables and responsible partners.

**Phase 1: Assessment & Design (Months 1-2)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** Oakhaven Parks Department (technical expertise), Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (community liaison), Landscape Architecture Studio “Green Spaces” (design services).
*   **Deliverables:** Site assessment report detailing existing conditions and drainage patterns; community survey results analyzing desired park features; preliminary design concepts incorporating native plantings, permeable paving, and a rain garden; final approved design plan.
*   **Timeline:** Weeks 1-8.

**Phase 2: Site Preparation & Material Procurement (Months 3-4)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** Oakhaven Public Works Department (heavy equipment operation), Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (volunteer coordination), Green Spaces (materials sourcing).
*   **Deliverables:** Site clearing and grading completed; delivery and storage of all necessary materials (soil, mulch, plants, paving materials); volunteer recruitment and training for planting activities.
*   **Timeline:** Weeks 9-16.

**Phase 3: Construction & Planting (Months 5-7)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** Oakhaven Public Works Department (permitting and inspection), Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (volunteer labor), Green Spaces (construction oversight).
*   **Deliverables:** Rain garden installation; permeable paving installation; installation of shade trees and native plantings; installation of park benches and signage.
*   **Timeline:** Weeks 17-28.

**Phase 4: Community Launch & Monitoring (Months 8-12)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (event coordination), Oakhaven Parks Department (ongoing maintenance), Green Spaces (performance monitoring).
*   **Deliverables:** Grand opening celebration and community engagement event; installation of signage detailing rain garden functionality; quarterly monitoring reports assessing stormwater retention and plant health; feedback survey to gauge community satisfaction.
*   **Timeline:** Weeks 29-36.

**Budget**

The total project cost is estimated at $100,000. We are requesting $75,000 in municipal funding, which will be matched by $25,000 in private donations and in-kind volunteer labor. The budget is detailed below:

*   **Materials (Soil, Mulch, Plants, Permeable Paving):** $45,000
*   **Heavy Equipment Rental (Grading, Excavation):** $10,000 (in-kind contribution from Oakhaven Public Works Department – valued at $5,000; $5,000 cash)
*   **Professional Services (Landscape Architecture Design):** $20,000 (secured through private donation)
*   **Signage & Educational Materials:** $5,000
*   **Community Engagement & Grand Opening Event:** $5,000
*   **Contingency (10%):** $10,000

The contingency fund will address unforeseen expenses, such as unexpected site conditions or material price fluctuations.  We have secured commitments from local nurseries for discounted plant materials and from the Oakhaven Public Works Department for the loan of necessary equipment, significantly reducing overall project costs. The value of volunteer labor, estimated at $15 per hour and totaling approximately 1,667 hours across all phases, is incorporated into the in-kind contribution.

**Community Engagement & Equity Impacts**

Community engagement is central to this project. We have conducted initial surveys and held neighborhood meetings to gather input on desired park features. The Willow Creek Neighborhood Association will serve as the primary liaison between the project team and the community, ensuring ongoing feedback and participation. The design prioritizes accessibility for all ages and abilities, incorporating universally designed pathways and seating areas. The choice of native plants will provide habitat for pollinators and contribute to biodiversity, benefiting the entire community.  The project will specifically target underserved populations within the neighborhood by offering volunteer opportunities and educational workshops focused on sustainable gardening practices.

**Risk Mitigation**

Potential risks include inclement weather delaying construction, material price increases, and volunteer attrition. To mitigate these risks, we have incorporated buffer time into the project timeline, secured price guarantees from suppliers, and developed a robust volunteer recruitment and retention strategy.  The contingency fund will provide financial flexibility to address unforeseen challenges. We will also maintain open communication with all stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle.

**Post-Grant Sustainability**

The long-term sustainability of Willow Creek Pocket Park will be ensured through a collaborative approach. The Oakhaven Parks Department will assume responsibility for ongoing maintenance, including watering, pruning, and litter removal. The Willow Creek Neighborhood Association will organize regular volunteer cleanup days and community events.  We will develop a detailed maintenance plan to guide ongoing care and ensure the park remains a valuable community asset for years to come. Educational signage will inform park users about the rain garden’s functionality and the importance of sustainable practices.



**Call to Action**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park Transformation represents a unique opportunity to enhance our community's resilience, improve quality of life, and demonstrate leadership in climate action.  We urge the City of Oakhaven to approve our request for $75,000 in municipal funding, enabling us to unlock the full potential of this neglected space and create a thriving community asset.  This investment will yield significant returns – a more resilient neighborhood, a vibrant gathering place, and a replicable model for future green infrastructure projects.
