The Power of Poetry
Words have impact! Poetry is one of the most concise and powerful use of written words. In this unit you will read poems and write your own poems in similar fashion. The following items must be included in your portfolio:
For each of the ten poems you find and illustrate, write your own poem that relates, reflects, or continues the concepts of the found poem.
Copy and illustrate each pair of poems on clean sheets of paper to complete your portfolio.
