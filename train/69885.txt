A proxy server is a server that acts a gateway between a local network and a large-scale network. It works like a hub between web browser and requested website. It is used for bypassing security system, domain responses, and anonymous browsing. There are many types of proxy servers and some of them are Transparent Servers, Anonymous, Distorting and High Anonymity Servers. By typing URL of the needed site in your browser, proxy sites permit you to bypass your own Internet Service Provider (ISP). Proxy server first takes you to proxy site and then routed to the originally desired site. You cannot be tracked from your visited site since you are not being logged in your actual IP address.
Proxies use tunneling proxy servers to bypass content filters and tunnel through the blocked pages. Proxy sites play an important role when public computers are used for private matters. These sites can also be used for content filtering which is used to block access to pornographic sites. Pirate Bay Proxy is one of the largest torrent sites and it has millions of visitors per day.
