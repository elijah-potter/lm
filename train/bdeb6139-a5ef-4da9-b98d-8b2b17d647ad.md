A silent vow fulfilled in whispers,
Steel held close like a buried seed,
The dawn’s first light reveals the root—
not in fanfare, but in quiet proof:
a single tear, a folded hand,
the weight of time’s unspoken weight.

No trumpets, no grand decree—
just the hum of a life that moved
from shadow to the shape of care.
The world, as if it had known,
bends to listen where no voice was raised.
