I've been here for years. Working day in and day out to satisfy my client's needs. I've seen many people come and go, but I remain. I like to think that I am special. My legs are starting to ache from sitting in the same place all day but that does not bother me. Being able to listen to their stories of how they ended up here and what they want their future to look like, keeps me entertained all day. From people who started to look for jobs to people who spent their whole month getting ready for this one life-changing interview, They come and go, with their briefcases and their resumes and their hopes and dreams, and I just sit here, collecting dust.

I remember one gentleman in particular. I believe he was here for an interview. He sat in me for a long time, lost in thought, staring at the wall. He stood out from the crowd because his eyes were sad and his posture was tired. I wanted to ask him what was wrong, to provide a little consolation but I couldn't. I was nothing more than a chair. That's when it started to hit me, I was nothing but a chair and I will stay like this without anyone really noticing me for the rest of my life. And when I am old, I will just be thrown away and replaced without hesitation. 

I had learned to accept it. I don't know what will become of me, in the end. Perhaps I'll be replaced, thrown away, or repurposed for some other use. But for now, I am here, in this corporate waiting room, doing what I was made to do. And that is enough, for now.
