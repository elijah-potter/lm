Caffeine withdrawal: abrupt cessation often leads to headaches, fatigue, and irritability within 24–48 hours. Gradual reduction (e.g., cutting by 20mg daily) minimizes symptoms. Behavioral triggers like morning coffee routines or social coffee culture reinforce dependency. Alternative strategies include substituting with decaf, herbal tea, or water; mindfulness to manage cravings; and tracking withdrawal phases to monitor progress.

---
**Key metrics for tracking:**
- Days since last caffeine intake
- Energy/alertness levels (1–10 scale)
- Headache frequency/intensity
- Sleep quality (pre/post-withdrawal comparison)

**Sample log entry:**
`Day 5 | Caffeine: 0mg | Energy: 6/10 | Headache: Moderate (1x) | Sleep: 7.2 hrs (consistent)`

---
**Long-term habit modification tips:**
1. **Environmental cues:** Remove coffee from kitchen; replace with decaf at home.
2. **Social pressure:** Limit coffee with peers; use "no-caffeine days" for accountability.
3. **Replacement behaviors:** Pair decaf with ritual (e.g., tea + 5-min meditation).
4. **Progressive exposure:** Introduce low-dose caffeine (e.g., 10mg) on off-days to rebuild tolerance.
