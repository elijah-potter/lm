A crumpled receipt, half-eaten sandwich wrapper, and a single silver ring glint in a dimly lit alley, each held briefly before being passed to the next. The first person—hands rough with callouses—slides it to the second, whose fingers tremble slightly as they clutch the ring, the wrapper’s edges fraying from wear. The second, gripping the receipt tightly, exchanges it with the third in a rapid, almost invisible exchange, their breath misting in the cold air. The ring disappears into a crowd, while the wrapper spirals into a trash can, its contents dissolving into the damp earth. A single, abandoned cigarette butt curls into the gutter as the cycle continues.
