Located in Africa, the cheetah is the fastest animal on land. They can exceed speeds of 60 miles per hour in less than 3 seconds. They can only maintain these speeds for a short period though. They are faster than lions who can top 50 miles per hour within seconds. As far as long-distance does, the American Antelope is the fastest long-distance runner. They can keep a speed of 35 miles per hour for several miles.
