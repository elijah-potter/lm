It was a warm summer evening. As the heat of the summer's day subsided and the sun began to set, a sound was heard down by the pond. The singing of peeper frogs filled the air. But one frog's voice stood out from all the others. Polly, a young peeper frog loved to sing. She was always the first to start singing and the last to stop. It sometimes annoyed the other frogs, but some of them were just jealous of her voice. Polly had a dream though. Ever since she was a little tadpole, she would watch the stars at night and listen to the other frogs sing. She thought they were singing to the stars, but that maybe the stars couldn't hear them. She always told herself when she grew up, she'd sing so loud they'd be sure to hear her. 

Polly belted out her song in the night, the other frogs joining in her song. She watched the stars as she sang, and noticed one star seemed to twinkle more than usual. It was twinkling and flashing bright in the sky. "The stars can hear me!" Polly thought excitedly. Her dream had finally come true. Filled with joy, she sang her peeper song all through the night until the light of morning came.
