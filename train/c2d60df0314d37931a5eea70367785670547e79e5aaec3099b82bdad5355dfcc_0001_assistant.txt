Join the various harrowing voyages of Captain Hawke as he and his crew. The high seas of the Golden Coast are awash with would-be smugglers, rogues, and freebooters. Within this community of pirates, Captain Hawke and his crew could be considered decidedly unsuccessful. With each new adventure, another ship is lost. Captain Hawke, however, always finds a way to survive -- for himself and his crew. Will Captain Hawke find what he's looking for on the high seas, or is he doomed to a Sisyphusian crusade?
