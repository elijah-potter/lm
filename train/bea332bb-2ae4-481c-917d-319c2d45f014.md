The **Library of Lost Letters**
A hidden archive in an old bookstore, tucked behind a bookshelf in the basement. Shelves line the walls with folded sheets of paper, each labeled with a name scrawled in ink. Some notes are tucked under books, others pinned to the spine of a leather-bound journal. A sign on the door reads: *"For Those Who Leave Things Behind."* The scent of aged paper and old ink lingers. Visitors leave notes in envelopes or scribble messages on the back of a worn library card. A small bell above the door chimes when someone takes or adds a message.
