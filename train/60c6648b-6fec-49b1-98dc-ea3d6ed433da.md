Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny rabbit named Pip. Pip had fluffy brown ears that bounced when he hopped, and his favorite thing to do was nibble on carrots.

One sunny morning, Pip decided to explore his garden. He hopped outside and saw his favorite friend, a bright blue bird named Benny. Benny chirped, "Hello, Pip! Want to play hide-and-seek?"

Pip nodded eagerly. He hid behind a big bush with green leaves, while Benny flew to the other side of the garden. Pip peeked out and giggled when he saw Benny sitting on a tall tree branch. "I see you!" he whispered.

Benny laughed. "I see you too! But where’s my hiding spot?"

Pip pointed to a small hole under the bush. "There!" he said. Benny hopped in and out, making funny faces. Pip giggled so hard he had to sneeze, and the carrot he was holding fell onto the ground.

"Oops!" Pip said. Benny giggled too and shared his favorite berry with Pip. They munched and laughed until their bellies were full.

As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and gold, Pip said, "Time to go home, Benny. My mama will be wondering where I am!"

Benny nodded. "Goodnight, Pip! See you tomorrow!" he chirped and flew off.

Pip hopped home, carrying a carrot in each paw. His mama smiled and said, "What a wonderful day, Pip! Did you play with Benny?"

Pip hopped to her and showed her his carrot treasure. "I did, Mama! We played hide-and-seek and ate berries!"

That night, Pip snuggled under his favorite blanket and dreamt of his next adventure with Benny.
