**Scientists discover the structure of DNA (1953):**
A double helix composed of four nucleotide bases (A, T, C, G), forming a twisted ladder where each strand’s sequence encodes genetic instructions via base-pairing rules (A-T, C-G). This revelation unlocked the basis of heredity, enabling genetic engineering, CRISPR gene editing, and personalized medicine. Previously, the "mysterious code" of life became a tangible mechanism, reshaping biology, medicine, and biotech forever.
