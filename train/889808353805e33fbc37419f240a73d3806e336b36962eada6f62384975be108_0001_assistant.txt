

My name is Ushio. I play the MMORPG called End of Time. It was a big game when it first came out. Now, though, it doesn't have enough players to sustain itself. In the interest of money, after over twelve years of service, the game shut down. At least, it should have shut down. I mean, it did shut down. But somehow, I'm inside the game! 

I ran it over in my mind. I went to sleep last night at the same time as always. I had school the next day. It was a test day, so I didn't want to stay up too late playing. When I woke up, though, I was clad in my wizard robes and in what should have been the Tokkeil Inn, as I recognized it. I went to the desk to check out, and the NPC was there smiling and moving about like a real human. She greeted me and asked how I found the room. I told her it was a good room and then asked a few questions like the name of the town I was in and the name of the inn.

She rattled off the answers like people asked the same things all the time. I thanked her and then left, checking the sign on my way out. Everything was exactly as I remembered it being in the game. I was at the Tokkeil Inn in a frontier village named Raskur. Just beyond the village should have been the no-fly zone that signaled the start of the hardest area of the game, the Demon Den. It's the place where I logged out of the game before it shut down the night before.

During the game's prime, this village was a gathering place for all the top-ranked players. Of course, if the game had become reality, it would be a crummy village with no reason to be there. That's when I realized I was in End of Time!
