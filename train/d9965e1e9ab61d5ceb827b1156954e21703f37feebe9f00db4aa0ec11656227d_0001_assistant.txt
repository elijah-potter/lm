Breaking News: Gustav Forsling, an unsung hero? Although the Florida Panthers are led by some high-profile players such as Sergei Bobrovsky, a Vezina Trophy winner, Sam Bennett, Sam Reinhart and Matthew Tkachuk, Forsling's impact has been felt by teammates and opponents alike. The Panthers took Frosling off waivers two and a half years ago and never looked back. Paired with Aaron Ekblad, Forsling is averaging second in ice time on the team with 26:44 per game. He is first on the team in time on the ice during a penalty kill, averaging a massive 3:11 per game. Quietly, Forsling has established himself as one of the key pieces in the Florida Panther's defensive rotation during the regular season and playoffs. His plus-9 rating, which speaks for itself, is good for third on the team. When asked, head coach Paul Maurice said that he needed an opportunity and he has made the most of it with Florida. Gustav seems to have the good stuff for the Florida Panthers. This is Rebecca Black from ABL News, signing off.
