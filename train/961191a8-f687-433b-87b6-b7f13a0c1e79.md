A low hum echoed through the ruins of **Black Hollow**, the air thick with dust and the scent of damp earth. The old brass plaque near the crumbling archway read: *"Warning: Trespassers May Become Lost."* Inside, the skeletal remains of a dozen lanterns flickered weakly, casting jagged shadows on the stone walls.

**Captain Elias Veyne** adjusted his gloves, his breath visible in the frigid night. His lantern barely held enough light to navigate the tunnels beneath the city. The last expedition had vanished without a trace, their journals never found.

*"They say the Hollow feeds on those who linger,"* whispered the old cartographer’s sketch in his pocket, its edges curled from damp. *"But I’m not afraid."*

His boots crunched on frozen stone as he stepped past a half-buried corpse—clad in tattered robes, its fingers clawing at the ceiling. The lanterns above his head had gone out.
