A single act of kindness—giving a stranger a cup of coffee—unfolded into a ripple. The barista noticed the woman in the rain, clutching an empty bag, and handed her a warm drink. The woman, a single mother, used the coffee to regroup before her shift. Over time, she returned the favor, leaving a small tip and a note: *"You never know who needs a cup of hope."*

The act started as fleeting, but it became the seed of connection—one coffee at a time.
