When it comes to sea lions, they're considered "eared" seals
Unlike humans, they have flippers instead of heels
Their foreflippers and wrists are covered in fur
They're unlike any other creature, I'm sure 

With two sets of flippers, they can walk on land
Aided by tiny claws, instead of hands
At birth, even the babies can swim
As the mama goes out finding food on a whim

The males are bigger than females, and tall
Weighing more than double and up to 8 feet overall
The males also have a crest on their head
Unlike females, who are tan and yellow, it's said

For meals, these creatures feast on seafood
From octopi to fish, depending on their mood
You'll find them in groups, by water or shore
Gathering with others - sometimes 10 or more!

Listen for a roar to know they're near
It might be the loudest thing you hear
There's nothing quite like this majestic beast 
A picture of nature, to say the least
