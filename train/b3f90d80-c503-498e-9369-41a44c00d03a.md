A vast, mist-shrouded valley where the air hums with suppressed energy. Trees stand motionless, their branches frozen in an eternal pause, their leaves trembling like damp paper. The sky is a bruised canvas of bruised violet and sickly green, stretched thin over jagged peaks that seem to lean in, waiting. Rivers run sluggishly, their currents barely visible beneath the perpetual haze, like something half-formed and barely holding itself together. The earth beneath feels heavy, pressing down, as if it’s holding its breath too, waiting for something to break its hold. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, pooling at the edges of perception, as though the world is fading in and out of existence. The silence is thick, the only sounds the occasional whisper of wind or the distant, unnatural stillness of something vast and ancient moving just beyond the edge of sight.
