Once there was a little bunny named Pip who loved to hop around the sunny meadow. One morning, Pip found a shiny red berry near his burrow. He nibbled on it and felt happy because berries were his favorite snack!

Pip hopped to his friend Max, a big, fluffy squirrel, and shared his berry. "Eat this!" Pip said. Max chattered, "I don’t like berries! I love nuts!" Pip giggled. "Then let’s go find some!"

They hopped and scampered through the tall grass, looking for acorns. Max dug one out from the ground, and Pip munched on it happily. Suddenly, a little mouse named Mia ran by, holding a tiny seed. "Help me find a bigger tree for this seed," she said.

Pip and Max worked together, exploring the forest until they found a cozy spot beneath a big oak tree. Mia planted her seed, and Pip and Max cheered, "Let’s take care of it together!"

Days passed, and the seed grew into a tiny sapling. Pip and Max watered it every morning, and Mia watched it grow. Soon, the sapling became a strong tree with bright green leaves.

One sunny afternoon, a family of birds flew down and said, "Our nest is too small! Can we live under your tree?" Pip and Max nodded. They helped build a cozy nest for them, and soon, fluffy baby birds chirped happily.

Now, every day, Pip, Max, Mia, and the bird family play together under the big oak tree. They share their snacks, tell stories, and make new friends every day. The meadow was full of joy, and everyone felt very happy because they were kind to each other and took care of their new home.
