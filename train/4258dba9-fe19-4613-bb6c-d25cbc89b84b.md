Two strangers, **Lena** and **Marcus**, sat across from each other at an empty café table, both recounting the same night in silence.

---

**Lena’s Perspective:**
The door had creaked open at midnight—just a draft, nothing more. She’d been asleep, but the sound woke her. A man stood outside, his breath fogging the glass. He said nothing, just stared. Then he vanished. No sound. No trace.

She slept until dawn, confused, but the memory lingered. *He was there. He was gone.*

---

**Marcus’s Perspective:**
The door *slammed*. A gust of wind, but she heard it—sharp, deliberate. Then the lights flickered. A man in a long coat stood in the hallway, watching her. He didn’t speak, but his eyes burned. The next thing she knew, he was gone, and the café was empty.

She called the police. The night guard swore he didn’t see anything. But she *did*.
