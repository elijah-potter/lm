A city skyline flickered through the neon haze as the train derailed mid-route, its tracks twisted into a labyrinth of shifting destinations. The conductor muttered, *"Next stop: Osaka,"* but the signal flickered again—*"Next stop: Reykjavík."* The windows blurred between Parisian cafés and Mongolian steppes, each exit a ghost of the last.

Passengers whispered of maps erased by the wind, maps rewritten in ink of the unseen. The driver’s face was a map too—his eyes tracing paths that vanished mid-draw. By the time the train stopped, no one knew where the next leg would lead. Only the hum of the engine and the unspoken question: *"Where do we go now?"*
