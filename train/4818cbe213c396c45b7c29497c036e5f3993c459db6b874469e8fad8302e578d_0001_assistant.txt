Once upon a time, in a land so silly,
Lived a little girl and her name was Lily.
Lily liked to giggle and laugh all day,
Always hoping for time to play.

Lily also had a dog named Cutie,
Whose tail was wiggly and oh so flooty.
She wagged it all day, left and right,
But one day it got stuck and gave herself a fright !

Cutie's little tail got stuck in a tree,
She wiggled and squiggled, but it couldn’t get free.
Lily came to the rescue, quick as she could,
Determined to get the tail out of the wood.

With one big pull, the tail came free,
But something surprising, it turned out to be.
Instead of the tail, what was left in the lunk?
A bootybutt hole in the middle of the trunk!

Lily and Cutie laughed at the divot,
Started to move, then dance and pivot.
They boogied and grooved for the rest of the day,
Everyone joining in the silly tail sway.

So remember, my friends, when life gets tough,
Find the silly moments, they'll be enough.
Like Cutie's bootybutt still there as a hole,
Laugh and be silly till your face gets swole.
