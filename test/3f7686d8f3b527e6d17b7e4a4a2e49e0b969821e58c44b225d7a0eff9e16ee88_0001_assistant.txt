Being prepared before an emergency strikes is a smart plan. Emergency preparedness kits have a lot of supplies so the first thing you need is a large plastic tote or large backpack to keep everything in. The first things you should have in your kit are fresh, bottled water and non-perishable foods. If you have any canned food, be sure to include a can opener. If you have pets, be sure to include food for them as well. You will also need utensils, plates/bowls, and cups.

Your kit should also contain a couple of flashlights, a battery-powered radio, and a NOAA weather radio, with extra batteries for each item. Be sure to include basic tools as well for shutting off utilities or other needs that might crop up.

Be sure to pack personal care and hygiene items, extra clothing for everyone in your family, first aid supplies and a reference book, a sleeping bag or warm blanket for each person, and copies of important documents, such as birth certificates, IDs, banking information, etc.

More extensive kits can be put together as well with several more items you might need during an emergency.
