Lily the bunny hopped through the sunny meadow. She loved to collect bright flowers—sunflowers, daisies, and tiny pink petals. One morning, as she nibbled a clover, she heard a tiny voice.

"Help! Help!" cried a little bird sitting on a fence.

Lily hopped over. "What's wrong?"

The bird fluttered down. "My nest fell off! It's all broken!"

Lily grinned. "Don't worry! I'll help." She gathered her best friends—Mr. Turtle, who was always slow but very gentle, and little Mouse—to fix it.

Together, they picked up the broken eggshells and arranged them into a cozy nest. The bird chirped happily. "Thank you! Now it's safe!"

From that day on, Lily always checked her pockets to make sure she didn't leave anything behind—especially when helping others. And the meadow was even happier knowing she cared.
