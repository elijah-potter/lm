Plastic is among the traditionally used materials in various applications whether it’s domestic uses or it can be industrial uses. In 1855, Alexander Parkers invented first human plastic. By doing some chemical reactions plastic will be given some modification and forms time by time.
Today we have a array of common plastics which might be used in our lifestyle. Several plastics:
o Polyester (PES): Textiles, Fibers, etc.
o Polystyrene (PS): Disposable plate cup, cutlery, CD and cassette box etc.
o Polypropylene (PP): Appliances, food containers car bumpers etc.
o Poly vinyl chloride (PVC): Window frame, pipe etc.
o Polyethylene (PE): Poly bags, bottles etc.
o Polycarbonate (PC): Eyeglasses, CD, Lenses etc.
Several special purposes plastic:
o Polymethyl methacrylate: Fluorescent light diffusers, contact lenses, glazing etc
o Polytetrafluoroethylene: plumber’s tape, frying pans, heat-resistant etc
o Polyetheretherketone: commercial polymers, aerospace mouldings etc.
o Polyetherimide: Polymer that doesn’t crystallize etc.
Numerous researches have been created to develop variety of use of plastic where UPVC is one of them. Today upvc is employed manufacturing doors and windows. UPVC stands for ‘Unplasticized Polyvinyl Chloride’.
UPVC is amongst the plastics which includes benefits if utilized in manufacturing of doors and windows. Because it is for sale in different colors, we can easily have windows or doors of our interest. UPVC doors and UPVC windows could be given different forms of texture finish.
Moreover, UPVC doors and UPVC windows contain the nature who’s doesn’t corroded as soon as. Weather doesn’t do anything for the products. It’s anti corrosion nature that means it is very durable.
