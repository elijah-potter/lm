Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the sunny meadow. Benny had a big, fluffy tail that wiggled with every step. One sunny morning, Benny found a shiny red berry that sparkled in the sunlight.

"Mmm, what a yummy berry!" Benny said happily. Benny nibbled a tiny bit and then hopped to the nearby pond to splash around. The pond was sparkling, and Benny's paws made tiny ripples.

Suddenly, Benny saw a tiny little mouse named Mimi sitting on the bank. Mimi had a basket filled with acorns. "Hello, Benny!" Mimi said with a smile. "I found a big, juicy acorn that I think is my favorite."

Benny wagged his tail and said, "I found a berry that sparkles! Would you like to share with me?" Mimi looked at the berry and then at Benny. "Oh, Benny, I would love to share with you!" she said.

Together, they ate their treats and sat under a big, leafy tree. Benny and Mimi laughed and played hopscotch with pebbles until the sun began to set. As the sky turned pink and gold, Benny and Mimi snuggled up together. "Goodnight, Benny," said Mimi, "I'll see you tomorrow!"

Benny nodded and closed his eyes. "Goodnight, Mimi," he said. They both dreamed of more adventures under the starry night sky. Benny and Mimi's friendship made every day bright and fun.
