I am a creature of immortal life,
A curse that brings eternal strife.
I do not wish to live off blood,
But to ride a horse through dirt and mud.

I dreamt of making my way west,
But would not find myself so blessed.
I received a stranger's bite,
And now am bound unto the night.

I sometimes think, what might have been?
If I had reached the ranch back then,
To live a life out on the range,
And not have undergone this change.

I could have gone on cattle drives,
Instead of preying on people's lives.
And so I still dream of that fate,
And pray I can escape this state.
