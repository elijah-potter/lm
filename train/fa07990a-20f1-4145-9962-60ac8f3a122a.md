1. **Lost in the Alphabet**
   The keys of the typewriter were cold when I pressed them—each one a whisper of forgotten letters. The screen flickered with static, but the first line appeared:

   **A, B, C, D, E, F, G—**

   I paused. The alphabet was not just letters; it was a map, a skeleton of the world’s silent names. I had spent years chasing words, but now, I was lost in the space between them.

   **I**, the first one, was always alone. The others crowded around it like shadows. **Z** hung there, silent, watching.

   The list expanded:
   - **Mystery** written in the margins of half-remembered books.
   - **Silence**, a word that hummed when unspoken.
   - **Echoes**, fragments of voices that never left my ears.

   I typed faster, the keys clacking like teeth. The list grew:
   - **Forgotten**, the names of things that had never been said.
   - **Unseen**, the faces of those who slipped into the dark.
   - **Whispers**, the things I dared not repeat aloud.

   The screen blurred. The list was no longer just words—it was a storm. I hit **Enter**, and the words rearranged themselves:

   **You are the first letter. You are the space between letters. You are the thing no one names.**

   The typewriter clicked off. The list dissolved into the dark.
