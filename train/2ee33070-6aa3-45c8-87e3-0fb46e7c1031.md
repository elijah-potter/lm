A tiny brass key, lost in the hollow of an old desk drawer, now holds the weight of forgotten promises. Its teeth gnaw at the rusted lock of a forgotten past, whispering secrets to the dust. For decades, it has been a silent guardian of memories—each turn a story of love, betrayal, or a choice made in haste. The metal remembers the weight of keys that came before, each with its own meaning, now reduced to dust. The symbol of a lock, once a barrier, now serves as a reminder: some doors are only worth opening if you carry the key within.
