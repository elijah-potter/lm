Squares & Other Shapes with Josef Albers
An introduction to shapes through the acclaimed designs of Josef Albers
The influential art of Josef Albers is used to teach shapes in this stylish read-aloud board book, which takes children through Albers' range of geometrics, one artwork per page, beginning with squares and returning to them as a familiar refrain throughout. The variance of color, scale, and quantity adds to the richness of the visual arc, and the accompanying text provides a humorous and engaging commentary. Includes a read-aloud "about the artist" at the end.
By Josef Albers
Phaidon Press, 2016
Board book, 30 pages
7 x 0.6 x 8 inches
