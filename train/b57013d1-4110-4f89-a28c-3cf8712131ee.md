The annual snowfall ritual begins in December, when the team gathers at the warehouse for the "Melt-and-Mold" ceremony. Each year, the same five steps are followed: measuring the snow depth with a ruler, mixing the right proportions of salt and antifreeze, pouring the solution into the molds, and finally, pressing the "Start" button on the automated press. The process is precise—no deviations allowed. The ritual ensures consistent production of identical, flawless snowmolds, each year, for the same corporate client.
