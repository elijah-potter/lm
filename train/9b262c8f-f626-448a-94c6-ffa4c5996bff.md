Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a tiny mouse named Mimi. Mimi loved to explore her garden every day, where she found the most colorful flowers and crunchy yummy bugs to eat.

One sunny morning, Mimi noticed a shiny golden acorn near her favorite dandelion. It sparkled like a tiny treasure! She picked it up and giggled, *"This is the prettiest acorn ever!"* She carried it home, but when she opened the door, the wind blew the acorn right out, rolling far away.

Mimi looked around, her heart a little sad. She ran outside, searching everywhere. Then, she spotted a big, fluffy cloud floating in the sky. With a happy jump, Mimi chased the cloud until it led her to a tiny mushroom house. Inside, she found a sleeping bunny with the golden acorn tucked safely under its fur.

Mimi smiled and whispered, *"Thank you for keeping my treasure safe!"* The bunny woke up, blinked, and said, *"Oh, it’s you! Here’s my secret recipe—just mix a spoon of sugar, a pinch of sunshine, and a dash of adventure!"* Mimi giggled and ate the magical mushroom treat, and suddenly, she felt even happier than before.

From that day on, Mimi knew that sometimes, adventures lead us to wonderful new friends, and treasures are best shared. And every evening, she would whisper, *"The wind carried my acorn to a friend—now let’s explore together!"* as she snuggled under her big red door.
