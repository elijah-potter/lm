Insightful request! The Yuuzhan Vong were aliens from outside our galaxy that used nature in favor of technology. They had a strict religious view that mandated they destroy other races that use artificial technology. After the war, the Vong sought to heal and rehabilitate so they joined the sentient world of Zonoma Sekot in the Unknown Regions. Zonoma Sekot is an offspring of the Vong's original home planet.
