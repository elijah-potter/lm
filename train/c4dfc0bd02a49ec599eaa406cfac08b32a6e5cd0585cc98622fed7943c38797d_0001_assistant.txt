Tesla Inc. is an American multinational automotive and clean energy company known for its electric vehicles and other home-use products and services. Led by Elon Musk, the company is one of the world's most valuable companies. It went public on June 29, 2010, selling each share at $17. Its stock, TSLA stock, opened trading at $19 per share and finished trading on its IPO at $23.89 per share, a gain of 40.5%.
