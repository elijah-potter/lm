The rain pattered against the window like a thousand forgotten keys.

"Where do you think you’re going?" she asked, voice rough as old parchment.

He turned, his reflection flickering in the glass, and shrugged. "Somewhere warm. Or at least dry."

"Where?" she repeated, voice sharp as the edge of a blade.

He smiled, but the corners of his mouth didn’t reach his eyes. "I don’t know. Everywhere at once, maybe."
