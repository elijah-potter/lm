Option 1
A frog named Sally and a tadpole named Oscar. Oscar is upset because he is so small and wants to grow faster. Sally befriends Oscar and teaches lessons about appreciating who he is and being patient. 

Option 2
A skittish and neglected horse named Buddy meets a girl named Christy. Christy spends a lot of time showing Buddy love and patience and they have lots of adventures and become best friends. 

Option 3
A boy named Gus wants a puppy but his mom won’t let him have one. Gus finds a puppy at the park and hides it in his bedroom and hopes his mom doesn’t find out. He names the puppy Mystery.
