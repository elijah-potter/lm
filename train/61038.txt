Student get knowledge of dimension so that they can create a design for carving work with the help of carving tools
Types of Sculpture Work,
1) Relief Ambrose- is a work study of two dimension volume with the help of clay
2)Round Sculpture- in which student will create freestanding handmade pieces using sculpture techniques student consider volume, weight, color, form, and texture while creating work
3)Mould Making and Casting- in that student will learn to make and cast using pop and ideas of using different materials
4)Carving Work- after finalized of clay work students will carve work on pop or siphorex block.
