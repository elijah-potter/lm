**The Last Transmission**

The hum of the generator faded as the last of the solar panels dimmed, their glass cracked like old bones. The air inside the abandoned research lab was thick with the scent of ozone and something older—rust and decay. Outside, the storm raged, lightning splitting the sky like a broken whip.

Dr. Elias Voss adjusted the thermal cam, his breath shallow. The countdown on the console read **3:47:22**. If he didn’t act soon, the oxygen scrubbers would fail. The blueprint in his gloved hands was the only thing keeping him alive.

*Step 1: Seal the breach.*
He moved like a ghost, pressing the emergency seal at the base of the containment door. The system whirred, the red warning light blinking in time with his heartbeat. **3:46:58**—his fingers trembled.

*Step 2: Activate the kill switch.*
The terminal’s glow cast jagged shadows. He pressed the wrong key—**3:46:23**—and the lab’s alarms wailed. A warning flashed: *"DO NOT INTERVENE."* His pulse roared in his ears. He knew the sequence.

He retyped it: **3-7-9-1-2-4-6-8-0-5**. The door’s lock clicked. The alarms fell silent.

**00:00:00**.

The storm outside howled, but the lab was still. The airlock hissed, sealing behind him. He exhaled, slow and steady, as the last of the storm’s fury retreated. The countdown was over.

Now, the real work began.
