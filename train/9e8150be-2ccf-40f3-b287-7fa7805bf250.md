## Product Requirements Document: "Synapse" - Contextual Knowledge Aggregation App

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently spend significant time searching for information scattered across diverse sources—documents, emails, notes, web pages—hindering productivity and flow.

**Vision:** To be the indispensable digital companion for knowledge workers, seamlessly aggregating and contextualizing relevant information at the moment of need.

---

### User Personas

**1. Amelia Chen - The Research Scientist**

*   **Goal:** To efficiently synthesize information from numerous research papers, lab notes, and online databases to advance her ongoing experiments.
*   **Pain Points:**  Feels overwhelmed by the volume of information; struggles to connect disparate findings; spends excessive time re-locating previously reviewed materials; finds it difficult to track the evolution of her understanding.

**2. David Ramirez - The Marketing Manager**

*   **Goal:** To rapidly access and organize market research, campaign performance data, and competitor analysis to inform strategic decisions and optimize marketing efforts.
*   **Pain Points:**  Information silos across departments lead to duplicated effort; difficulty tracking changes to marketing strategies; struggles to quickly recall key insights from past campaigns; finds it time-consuming to share relevant information with team members.

**3. Sarah Jones - The Consultant**

*   **Goal:** To quickly retrieve and synthesize client information, project documentation, and industry best practices to deliver impactful recommendations and manage multiple engagements simultaneously.
*   **Pain Points:**  Relies heavily on personal notebooks and scattered digital files, leading to potential data loss; struggles to maintain a consistent understanding across different client projects; finds it challenging to share curated knowledge with junior team members.

---

### Requirements

**1. Contextual Search & Retrieval**

*   **Description:** The app must allow users to search across all connected sources (documents, emails, notes, web pages, cloud storage) and return results ranked by relevance to the user’s current context (e.g., recent activity, project affiliation).
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** Search results accurately reflect content across connected sources; search ranking prioritizes results based on user activity and project association; users can filter search results by source type and date range.
*   **Success Metrics:** Average search time reduced by 30%; user satisfaction rating (measured through in-app feedback) of 4.5/5.

**2. Knowledge Graph Visualization**

*   **Description:** The app must automatically generate a visual representation of connected concepts and information, allowing users to explore relationships between ideas and identify gaps in their understanding.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** The knowledge graph accurately reflects relationships between concepts extracted from connected sources; users can navigate and explore the knowledge graph intuitively; users can add and edit connections within the knowledge graph.
*   **Success Metrics:** 60% of users actively utilize the knowledge graph feature; average session duration increases by 15%.

**3. Automated Summarization & Synthesis**

*   **Description:** The app must automatically generate concise summaries of documents and synthesize information from multiple sources, highlighting key takeaways and potential connections.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** Summaries accurately reflect the content of original sources; synthesized information accurately reflects the relationships between multiple sources; users can customize the level of detail in summaries.
*   **Success Metrics:** User adoption rate for summarization feature reaches 40%; users report a 20% reduction in time spent reviewing documents.

**4. Collaborative Knowledge Sharing**

*   **Description:** The app must allow users to share curated knowledge collections with colleagues, enabling collaborative research and knowledge transfer within teams.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can create and share knowledge collections with specific individuals or groups; shared collections are updated in real-time; users can add comments and annotations to shared collections.
*   **Success Metrics:** 25% of users actively share knowledge collections with colleagues; average number of users per shared collection is 3.

**5. Smart Source Connection & Synchronization**

*   **Description:** The app must seamlessly connect to popular cloud storage services, email providers, and note-taking applications, automatically synchronizing new content and updates.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** The app supports integration with Google Drive, Dropbox, Microsoft OneDrive, Gmail, Outlook, Evernote, and OneNote; new content is automatically synchronized within 5 minutes of creation; users can easily manage connected sources.
*   **Success Metrics:** 95% of users successfully connect at least one external source; average number of connected sources per user is 4.

---

### Technical Considerations

*   **Scalability:** The system must be designed to handle a large number of users and connected sources.
*   **Security:** User data must be encrypted both in transit and at rest. Strict adherence to privacy regulations is paramount.
*   **Natural Language Processing (NLP):**  Robust NLP capabilities are essential for accurate information extraction, summarization, and knowledge graph generation.
*   **Cross-Platform Compatibility:** Initial release will target iOS and Android devices. Web accessibility is a future consideration.
*   **API Integration:**  A well-documented API will be necessary to facilitate future integrations with other applications.

### Risks

*   **NLP Accuracy:**  Inaccurate information extraction or summarization could negatively impact user trust.
*   **Data Privacy Concerns:**  User hesitation to connect sensitive data sources due to privacy concerns.
*   **Integration Complexity:** Challenges in maintaining compatibility with evolving APIs of connected services.
*   **User Adoption:**  Difficulty in persuading users to adopt a new knowledge management tool.



### Non-Goals

*   Real-time collaboration editing of documents within the app.
*   Direct document creation capabilities.
*   Support for obscure or legacy file formats.
*   Integration with social media platforms.
*   Offline functionality (initial release).
