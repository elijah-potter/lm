Alice comes home from the hospital sober and clean. Everyone is happy to see her, and she has the best new friends and boyfriend. She has so much help from the people who love her when she starts school again. She feels like she can do everything that she needs to have a good life. Alice has a wonderful new therapist to talk to about everything. She even goes to a support group for recovering addicts. She feels so happy to have her life back that she decides that she wants to help other people. She studies in school so she can be a drug and alcohol counselor and graduates with top honors. She opens her private practice and shows other people that they can have a great life, too.
