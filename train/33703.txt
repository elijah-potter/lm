Water and beverages produced with it enjoy great popularity. The consistent quality of the used "water" resource is a must. For years, the decreasing water quality, caused by factors such as environmental influences, the use of chemicals and their disposal, has been an explosive topic in the food and beverage industry.
The use of ECA technology in the production and filling of alcoholic and alcohol-free beverages increases the product safety and results in improved CSB/BSB and AOX values in the wastewater.
