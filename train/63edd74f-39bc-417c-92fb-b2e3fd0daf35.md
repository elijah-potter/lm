A flicker in the air—red light on the bridge. The captain raised his hand; the crew froze. Orders shifted mid-whistle: *"Docking protocols override all others. Engines idle."* The ship’s engines groaned as they throttled back, engines humming like dying bees. A sudden gust of wind sent the *Sea Storm* drifting toward the docks, its hull creaking under the unexpected load of 1200 passengers. The air smelled of wet metal and old coffee. The captain’s voice cracked over the PA: *"We’re not leaving. Ever."* The bridge’s screens glitched, displaying a new map—one that didn’t exist before. The docks loomed closer, the pier’s dock lights blinking like trapped fireflies. The crew exchanged glances. Some whispered of the "New Rules." Others stared at the horizon, where the sky had turned the wrong color. The *Sea Storm* had stopped being a ship. It was a cage.
