The tension in the air was palpable as the room’s lone candle flickered against the dimmed light, casting long shadows on the leather-bound bookshelves. Across from her, Daniel leaned forward, fingers tracing the rim of his whiskey glass, his knuckles white. His jaw tightened as he exhaled sharply, the breath curling into the room like smoke.

A single word—*"never"*—lingered between them, suspended in the silence. His eyes flicked to hers, sharp and unreadable, before he lowered his gaze to the table. His voice was low, deliberate: *"You think I don’t know how much you’ve been holding back?"*

Her fingers tightened around her teacup, the porcelain chipped at the edge. She swallowed, the pulse in her throat visible. The unspoken question hung between them: *Why now?*
