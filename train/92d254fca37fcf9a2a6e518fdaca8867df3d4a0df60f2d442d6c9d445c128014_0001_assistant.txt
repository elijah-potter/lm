Magic Mike had always been a hero to the people of Everdale. He had the power of magic and used it to save people from danger. One day, he received a call that a woman was trapped in a burning building and, without hesitation, he rushed to the scene. When he arrived, he saw the woman standing on the balcony, looking down at the flames below. She was crying and screaming in terror. Mike knew he had to act fast before the building collapsed from under her. He used his magic to create a shield around himself and flew up to the balcony to rescue her. 

As he lifted her, he looked into her bright blue eyes and felt a spark between them. She was the most beautiful woman he had ever seen. He quickly brought her down to safety and on a whim asked her to dinner, which was the least she could do after he had just saved her life. They went to a fancy restaurant and talked for hours. They soon realized how much they have in common and Magic Mike felt like he had finally found someone who understood him. As the night went on, the chemistry between them grew more and more. 

After dinner, they went for a walk in the park. As they walked, they held hands and talked about their hopes and dreams. Magic Mike knew he had found the love of his life and promised himself he would always love and protect her no matter what. From that day on, Magic Mike and the woman were inseparable. They went on many adventures together and fell deeper in love with each passing day.
