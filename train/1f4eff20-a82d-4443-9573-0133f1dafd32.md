A sleek metallic box, humming faintly with static, rests in the palm of a gloved hand. The fingers close around its edge, and the air around it crackles with unseen energy. The box’s lid clicks open with a sharp *click*, revealing a single, iridescent orb inside. The orb shimmers like liquid starlight before being plucked and passed to another hand—a child’s, this time—who clutches it tightly, eyes wide with wonder. The orb pulses brighter, casting a ghostly glow on the child’s face. The adult’s fingers brush the child’s, and the orb’s light dances between them before vanishing into the fabric of the room.
