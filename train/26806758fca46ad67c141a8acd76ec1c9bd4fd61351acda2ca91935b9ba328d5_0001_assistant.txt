He sensed that the sun was about to rise,
To greet the day with its bright salute.
He yawned and stretched and opened his eyes
To find himself dressed in a chicken suit.

And that's not all that made him wary -
He wasn't at home in his bed.
No, he woke up in a public library
In the non-fiction section instead.

The lights were off, it was closed, he supposed,
How he'd gotten there, he hadn't a clue.
Why was he dressed like this, and where were his clothes?
What's a discombobulated chicken to do?
