Hey Beauty Birds!
I have something really exciting to share with you guys today. I'm sure that by now everyone has heard of the new lipstick Morphe has dropped, and I was one of the first lucky ones to be able to test it out! To start, the packaging is gorgeous. Morphe's original black packaging is back at it again, which makes this lipstick look as daring as ever. The shade is called "Red and Thorned," and it is a striking, burgundy red color. After the first swatch, I was amazed to see how pigmented the lipstick is. The real test, however, was to see if it was as transfer-proof as Morphe claimed it would be. And let me tell you guys: I have eaten a burger, fries, brushed my teeth in this lipstick, and it's still on my lips! You guys need to try this out! Not to mention, the lipstick is also offered in a variety of shades. Morphe strikes again!
