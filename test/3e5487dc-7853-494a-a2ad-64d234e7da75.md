**BREAKING:** Residents of **New Haven Heights** awaken to a dramatic shift in the city’s skyline as the **Central Bridge** collapses into the **Whispering River** after a sudden structural failure during early morning maintenance. Officials confirm the collapse occurred at approximately 04:47 AM, with no immediate reports of injuries, though debris is scattered across the riverbank near the old **Oceanic Terminal** ruins. Emergency crews report the bridge’s metal lattice had failed due to unknown stress fractures, likely exacerbated by prolonged neglect of the aging infrastructure.

Local officials urge residents to avoid the area until further notice, while rescue teams scramble to secure the perimeter. The city’s **Port Authority** has declared a state of emergency, diverting all maritime traffic along the river to the **Southern Harbor Route**. Witnesses describe a "screaming" sound—later identified as a mechanical failure—just before the collapse.

The **New Haven Heights Historical Society** has already launched a digital archive to document the event, while scientists from the **River Dynamics Research Institute** are being deployed to assess long-term flooding risks. Meanwhile, the city’s **Sustainability Commission** is investigating whether climate change-induced storm surges contributed to the failure. No arrests have been made, but investigators are probing potential negligence in the bridge’s upkeep.

**Update:** Authorities confirm the bridge’s inspection logs from the past decade show repeated warnings of corrosion and inadequate support beams. The **Central Bridge Maintenance Task Force**, however, had delayed repairs citing budget cuts. The city has suspended all bridge-related permits until a full investigation is complete.
