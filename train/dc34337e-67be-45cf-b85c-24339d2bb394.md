The old clock tower stood silent at the edge of the village square, its brass hands frozen at **3:17:23**. The clock’s face was cracked, showing only the numbers 12:34:56, but the hands were embedded with a secret: the numbers were reversed in binary (0000001100001001).

Beneath the plinth, the clock’s mechanism was replaced by a hidden compartment containing a message:

**"A key lies where shadows meet the light’s faint glow. Seek the cracked pillar where the moon once hid its light."**

Later, the blacksmith’s apprentice, **Liora**, found a rusted key in the hollow of the pillar where the moon’s reflection was weakest. The key opened a locked chest beneath the village well, revealing a scroll with this message:

**"Decrypt with the reversed binary (11000101) for the hidden code. The village’s name is carved in the well’s stone: [MARIA]."**

**MARIA31723** was the hidden code, a date encoded in the clock’s broken hands (11110101 00000000 00001111). The message was a tribute to the village’s forgotten historian, **Elena**, who had hidden it to preserve the truth.
