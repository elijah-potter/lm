A deep, winding valley where the light bends like a river through mist.
Shadows stretch long and jagged along the cliffs, their edges flickering like embers.
At the center, a stone circle casts a circular void—its shadow the only stable ground.
Above, the horizon’s shadow swallows the sunrise, leaving only the faintest glow between the peaks.
Below, the gully’s floor is a maze of dark, half-lit pools where the light never quite reaches.
