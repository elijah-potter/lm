Circle hems abound
hair piled high
empire waists are found
decolletage, oh my

Marie Antoinette loves
a flowy peasant dress
ladies below and above
Copy her largess

High fashion dictates
when gloves adorn
a ladies reputation inflates
and shows her high-born

A layered necklace
is a major boon
to light a woman's face
her beauty doth balloon

ringlets and curls
pins and hats
are loved by girls
and women at that

Regency era fashion
loves bright hues
if an ankle flashes
Dishonor comes due
