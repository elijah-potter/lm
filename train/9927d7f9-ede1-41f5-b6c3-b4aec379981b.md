Whispering wind through skeletal trees, rustling leaves like crumpled paper. Damp earth, earthy and rich, mingles with the sharp tang of pine and crushed gravel. A distant thunderstorm hums low—rain pattering on rusted metal, the scent of ozone and wet wool clinging to the air. The river below gurgles and bubbles, carrying the metallic tang of old iron and the sweet, sickly stench of algae. Sunlight filters through, warm and golden, but the shadows hold the faintest ghost of a forgotten incinerator’s smoke—charcoal and burnt hair, lingering like a half-remembered dream.
