The Meta Quest 3 is set to hit the market at roughly the same price as its predecessor, the Meta Quest 2, at $400. This puts it in a much more affordable class than the Pro version, which was $1500, the upcoming Apple headset, which is expected to cost around $3000, and even the PlayStation VR2, which needs the PS5 to work. The Quest 3 will likely have color passthrough cameras, better depth sensing capabilities, and improved sensors.
