Geographical Location of Stockholm
Stockholm is located on the east coast of Sweden, on the southern middle part of the Scandinavian Peninsula in northern Europe. Stockholm city is part of the Stockholm Archipelago and is situated in the bay of Riddarfjärden where Lake Mälaren meets the Baltic Sea.
Languages of Stockholm
The official language of Stockholm is Swedish. English is also widely spoken and is the second or third language of most of Stockholm’s inhabitants.
Currency of Stockholm
Currency in Stockholm is the Swedish Crown or Svenska Krona. 1 Euro = 11.06 Svenska Kronor
