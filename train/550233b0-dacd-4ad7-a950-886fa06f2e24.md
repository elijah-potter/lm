Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, lived a bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around and nibble on carrots, but one sunny morning, he saw something sparkly and round in the grass—it was a shiny red apple!

Benny had never eaten an apple before! He carefully picked it up and took a tiny bite. "Mmm! So sweet!" he giggled. Soon, Benny shared the apple with his friends—his little mouse friend Muffin, his turtle friend Tilly, and his snail friend Snickers.

They all munched happily, laughing and playing. But as they ate, Benny noticed something strange. The apple was getting smaller and smaller, turning into tiny seeds. "Let’s plant them!" Benny said excitedly. The next day, they dug a hole and buried the seeds in the dirt.

Weeks passed, and tiny green leaves appeared! Then, one day, a big, juicy apple tree grew right there in their garden. Soon, the tree was full of bright red apples—just like the one Benny had found! Benny and his friends picked apples for everyone to enjoy, and they all smiled because they had turned a little sparkly seed into a whole new tree.
