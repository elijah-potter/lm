The last light of dusk painted the alley in hues of amber and violet, casting long shadows that seemed to stretch endlessly. Rain whispered against the pavement, each drop a whisper of forgotten secrets. Inside the abandoned bookshop, the scent of aged paper mingled with the faint, metallic tang of rust—remnants of a life that had long since faded.

The door creaked open with a sound like an old skeleton shifting, and for a heartbeat, the world held its breath. Then, footsteps—light, deliberate, purposeful—echoed through the dimness.

A figure stepped forward, their silhouette sharp against the flickering glow of a single bulb dangling from the ceiling. They carried a worn leather satchel, its contents hidden, and a cane that trembled slightly with every step. The air around them seemed to thicken, as if drawn by an unseen magnet.

"Well," they murmured, voice low and carrying the weight of years, "you really *had* to do it."

The door clicked shut behind them, leaving only the faintest echo of laughter in its wake.
