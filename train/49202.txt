Don’t be scared! This exhibit is one that some people are afraid to enter! But there’s no reason to be scared. The snakes, lizards, amphibians, and spiders found in this exhibit don’t deserve their bad reputation. Take a moment to explore this collection of some of nature’s most amazing creatures, and see if you don’t leave with a better impression of them.
Animals you’ll see here:
- Native To : Southeast Asia
- Habitat : Jungles, Marshes, Woodlands, Rocky Foothills, Swamps, River Valleys, and Grasslands
- Native To : Central America, Northern South America
- Habitat : Found mostly near water.
