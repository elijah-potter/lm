II. Get Started with LaunchPass
A. Slide 4: Step 1 - Create a Discord server on your own.
•	Making a Discord server is simple and cost-free.
•	Create a free Discord account here or sign up on Discord first. Discord
•	Click the (+) icon after signing up for Discord to start your server.
•	After giving your community a memorable name and including your logo, click "Create".
•	You currently manage your own Discord server.
2. Supporting visual element (e.g., image, chart, or diagram)

B. Slide 5: Step 2 - Connect LaunchPass to your Discord account.
•	Connect your Discord account to LaunchPass. 
•	Select "Connect Discord" on the LaunchPass webpage to complete this activity.
•	After selecting your new Discord server, click "Authorize" so that LaunchPass can manage members and send invitations.
•	After connecting your Discord account, you'll be directed to the LaunchPass account confirmation page. After entering your information and agreeing to the terms of service, click "Ready for liftoff."

2. Supporting visual element

C. Slide 6: Step 3 - Make your LaunchPass signup page and publish it.
1. Now that you are logged in and connected to LaunchPass in the Admin dashboard, click on “Create Page”. Enter your details under “Make Your LaunchPass”. 
2. Supporting visual element – 
•	Show Welcome Channel: Provides first channel subscribers a welcome and instructions to activate. 
•	Show Roles to Assign: A role is assigned to all new subscribers. 
•	Show Business Name: Always editable, displays the name of the business on your signup page. 
•	Show Offer Name: Always editable, specific offer to display on your signup page. 
•	Show Offer URL: The link to your signup page. 
•	Smaller Print*: “Offer URL” field automatically updates your signup page URL.

D. Slide 7: Step 4 - To receive payments, link to your Stripe account.
1. After you start your trial, you will be able to connect to your own Stripe account. Subscribers who join your organization will pay you this amount.
To link your Stripe account, click the "Link Stripe" button.
2. Supporting visual element

E. Slide 8: Step 5 - Begin your trial.
•	Click "Start Trial" once your Stripe account has been connected.
•	Your browser will take you to the billing page. Select the solution you want. You'll then be able to start charging for group access.
•	You won't be able to charge members for access at this level.
•	When you complete this step correctly, your admin menu will display a rocket emoji with the text "All systems ready to go!" in the upper left corner.
2. A Few Words on Billing You will receive money from your members through your Stripe account as the group owner. Your LaunchPass subscription costs are charged to the card you specified in the Billing section of the admin dashboard. More information is here.


C. Slide 9: Step 6 - Share your page!
1. When your premium offer is published, a link is generated that you can share with invitees. They can sign up on the offer page you just created by clicking this link.
