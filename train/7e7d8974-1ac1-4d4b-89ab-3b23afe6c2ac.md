An echoing question often serves to reinforce a thought, provoke reflection, or create a loop of inquiry. For example:

*"Why do you think you feel this way?"*
→ *"Because I feel this way because..."

Or:
*"What if we tried something different?"*
→ *"What if we tried something different? What if it worked?"*

Echo questions can be used in debates, therapy, or problem-solving to:
- **Mirror** the speaker’s emotions.
- **Elaborate** on assumptions.
- **Trigger** deeper analysis.

Example in dialogue:
*"You said you’re stressed about the meeting tomorrow."*
→ *"You’re stressed about the meeting tomorrow because you’re worried about failing."*

Echo questions can also be playful or manipulative if misused. Their power lies in repetition and focus.
