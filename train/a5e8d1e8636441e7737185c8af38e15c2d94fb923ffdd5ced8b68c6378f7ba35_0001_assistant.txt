Here are some common methods for home coffee brewing:

- French press: These are very easy to use and forgiving, and quite useful for making multiple cups of coffee.
- Automated drip brewer: The most stereotypical home coffee machine. These often have functions to automatically brew at a certain time.
- Espresso machine: Tend to be more expensive than other methods, would allow you to create authentic espresso-based drinks like lattes and cappuccinos.
- Pour-over: Favored by fans of specialty coffee as this method is good at extracting flavor profiles. This is not as easy as it sounds and requires practice and research.
