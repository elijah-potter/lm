Richard Harris made at least 38 movies in his lifetime. Five of the best Richard Harris movies include:
1. The Field (1990): Richard Harris plays a farmer Bull McCabe, determined to win the local potato growing competition even if it means violence ensues. His performance was critically acclaimed and earned him an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actor. 
2. The Molly Maguires (1970): Richard Harris plays James L. McKenna a leader of the Irish American Maguires a secret society of coal miners who fought for better working conditions. He received a Golden Globe for his performance.
3. Camelot (1967): Harris played King Arthur in the musical film adaptation of the classic legend King Arthur. His performance was charming and charismatic. He brought gravitas to the role of Arthur and the movie is thought to be one of his best performances.
4. The Sporting Life (1963) The film was Harris's first starring role and won him the Best Actor Award at the 1963 Cannes Film Festival, and a nomination for an Oscar as Best Actor in a Leading Role.
5. A Man Called Horse (1970) An English aristocrat is captured in 1825 by Native Americans. He lives amongst them and eventually is accepted as one of them.
