**Scene 1 (Past – Library, 1993):**
A rain-slicked window reflects a flickering neon sign: *"The Library Never Closes."* Inside, a boy, **Elias**, hunches over an old typewriter, his fingers tracing forgotten keys. His mother’s voice echoes: *"Books are the bridges we leave behind."* He drops a dust-covered novel onto the desk—*The Lost Tales of Veyra*—and stares at the faded title.

---

**Scene 2 (Present – Apartment, 2024):**
Elias, now 31, sits at his cluttered desk, laptop open. His phone buzzes: a text from his younger self: *"You’re reading it again. Why?"* He opens the file—**encrypted PDF of Veyra’s lost stories**. The text is fragmented, some lines missing. His fingers hover over a corrupted image: a silhouette of a woman with eyes like polished stone.

---
**Scene 3 (Future – Void Between Worlds):**
A holographic voice, distorted: *"You’re not the first to seek what was erased."* A door slides open—**Veyra’s ghost**, clad in tattered robes, stands before him. She presses a key into his palm. *"Press this. Unlock the echoes."* The scene dissolves into static. Then—**a whisper**: *"You carry her in your bones."*

---
**Scene 4 (Alternate Present – Another Library, 2024):**
A girl, **Liora**, finds a book titled *"The Archive of Forgotten Threads"* under a cracked floorboard. Inside: a single page, blank except for a **handwritten note**: *"Where the light bends, the stories bend with it."* She follows a trail of dust to a mirror—**reflecting a version of herself holding a half-empty cup of tea**.

---
**Scene 5 (Present – A Train Station):**
Elias and Liora meet. He holds up the key; she recognizes it—**it’s hers, from her journal**. *"You’re not the first to ask,"* she murmurs. The train whistle blows. They step onto the platform as the doors slide shut behind them. The key clicks into the lock of a **vintage pocket watch**.

---
**Themes:**
- **Echoes in Time**: Lost stories, fractured memories.
- **Shared Keys**: Veyra’s and Liora’s connection.
- **Parallel Fates**: Elias’ past self, Liora’s alternate path, and the unseen threads weaving them.
