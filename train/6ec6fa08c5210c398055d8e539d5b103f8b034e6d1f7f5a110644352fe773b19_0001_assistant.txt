Sure!  Here's a list of 3 popular types of pasta sauces.

1. Bolognese Sauce: Bolognese is a meat-based tomato sauce. It is customarily used to on top of tagliatelle al ragù and to prepare lasagne alla bolognese.
2. Alfredo Sauce: Traditional Alfredo is a simple white/cream sauce. It is typically made with Parmesan cheese, butter, warm pasta cooking water, and salt.
3. Pesto Sauce: Pesto sauce is created from a blend of fresh basil, garlic, pine nuts, extra-virgin olive oil, and Parmesan cheese.
