The Betsy Ross flag is an early design of the flag of the United States, popularly — but probably incorrectly — attributed to Betsy Ross. The flag was designed during the American Revolution and features 13 stars to represent the original 13 colonies. Her family claims that she contributed to the design of the flag when George Washington visited her, and she talked him into changing the shape of the stars from six-pointed to five-pointed. However, there is no historical documentation that it actually happened! To be fair though, it also seems no one knows the "real" story either. Either way, this historical flag is revered as the "first flag" and is widely popular! :)
