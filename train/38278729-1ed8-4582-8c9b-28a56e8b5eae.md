**Mayor’s Office Statement on Unidentified Emergency Vehicle Detonation in Downtown Rivermere**

Residents of Rivermere awoke to a series of unexplained explosions near the old warehouse district early this morning. Witnesses report a large, unmarked emergency vehicle parked along the riverfront, which detonated repeatedly before vanishing into the mist. Emergency crews confirmed no fatalities, though a single civilian sustained minor burns from shrapnel. Authorities are investigating possible links to the city’s recent infrastructure upgrades along the waterfront. The Rivermere Police Department has cordoned off the area near the docks, urging residents to avoid the zone. The city’s emergency broadcast system will be activated if further incidents occur. No official cause has been released.

The mayor has ordered a full review of city-owned vehicles and security protocols. Further updates will follow.
