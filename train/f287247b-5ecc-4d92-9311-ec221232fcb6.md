A single streetlight flickered on in the abandoned alley, casting a sickly glow over the cracked pavement. Inside, an old radio hummed a forgotten tune as its owner, Elias, hunched over a half-built robot arm, its metal joints clattering like dying bones. The door creaked open—footsteps paused, then stopped. A shadow moved too fast for the light. Elias spun, but the radio’s dial spun faster, its needle stabbing at static.

*"One minute,"* the voice whispered from the dark, *"before you remember why you built it."* The robot arm froze mid-stroke, its fingers twitching. Elias’s breath hitched—then the door swung shut. The radio’s voice faded into the wind. The streetlight went out.
