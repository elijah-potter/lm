• Drink plenty of water - Staying hydrated is key as Water aids in the maintenance of body functions, digestion, and energy levels.
• prioritize your sleep - Each night, aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep. Maintain a consistent sleep schedule by going to bed and waking up at the same time every day, even on weekends.
• Wash your hands frequently, particularly before meals and after using the restroom. This may go unnoticed, but it will help you in the long run.
• Get regular physical activity: Finding time to put your body to work, especially if you work at an office, is crucial to maintaining a healthy body and mind. Walking instead of driving to your local Starbucks, dancing to your favorite songs, or cycling early in the morning.

Small changes over time can make a huge difference in your life and can help you stay disciplined in the future.
