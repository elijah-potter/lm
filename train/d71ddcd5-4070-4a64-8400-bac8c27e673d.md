The room was too still—no whisper of wind through the rusted windows, no distant hum of machinery in the factory below. The air itself seemed to hold its breath, thick with the weight of unspoken words. On the table, a half-empty coffee cup glowed faintly under the dim lamp, its surface still wet with the last of the liquid, now evaporating into nothing. A single sheet of paper lay untouched beside it, its edges curled from being picked up and dropped again, its blank space a mirror to the silence around it.

The door creaked open, and footsteps—heavy, deliberate—approached. They paused at the threshold, then walked away, leaving only the sound of their own breath, shallow and unsteady. The silence did not fade. It lingered, a living thing, swallowing the space between them like a second skin.
