Berlin has a ton of great hostels that allow quick access to Berlin Central Station. Here are a few of them:

- MEININGER Berlin Central Station: This Hostel is located right at Central Station and is as close as you can get. It has Laundry Machines. The hostel has private rooms as well as dorms and each room has its own bathroom and shower. There are public areas, a kitchen, 

- a&o Berlin Hauptbahnhof: The a&o is a 10-minute walk from Berlin Central Station. It has table soccer and a billiards table as well as common rooms and conference rooms. a&o has a rooftop terrace bar with an incredible view of the city.

- Heart of Gold: Heart of Gold is a bit farther, about a 20-minute walk or 13 minutes by s Bahn trains. The Heart of Gold makes up for its distance from the train station with its location. It is in the center of the city and within walking distance of many historical sights and the nightlife district. 

- Generator Berlin Alexanderplatz: Generator is a popular chain in Europe. This one is a 54-minute walk from Central Station, however, the 16-minute straight shot on the U-Bahn train makes up for it. This hostel is close to many of berlins top attractions, namely the Fernsehturm, the Rotes Rathaus, and the Marx-Engels Forum. It is in a good central location and close to tonnes of hot spots around the city. 

- Eastener: Eastener is a 22-minute walk and 8-minute bus ride from Central Station. The hostel is in a quiet area while still being close to the central part of the city. It’s a great hostel if you’re just looking for a quiet place to sleep.
