Our children should know that the clean water they take for granted doesn’t run from God’s hand into the spigot. It needs to be cleaned using precise processes and many steps! This Bible science experiment and project in water cleansing will help kids see that most things on Earth get dirty and need cleansing – including drinking water… and including our souls!
