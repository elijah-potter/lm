A minor argument over shared coffee supplies escalated when one person insisted on keeping their own cup for the week. The other responded by tracking their own expenses, leading to a weekly debate over fairness. Over time, the tension grew into a recurring conflict, with each person accumulating evidence—receipts, notes, and memos—that reinforced their position. The small disagreement now threatened to strain their relationship beyond repair, as the issue expanded into a broader dispute about trust, accountability, and even personal boundaries.
