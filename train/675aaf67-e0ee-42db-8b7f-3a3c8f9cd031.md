A barren, cracked earth expanse stretches beneath the pale, low-hanging clouds, their edges tinged with ashen gray. The skeletal remains of once-vibrant forests—charred trunks and twisted roots—lie scattered across the terrain, their bark peeling back like sunburnt skin. Where once the air hummed with songbirds and rustled with unseen life, now only the whisper of dry wind carries the scent of damp ash and rustling leaves that have long since withered. A slow, creeping river of muddy water meanders through the landscape, its banks lined with debris and the occasional half-buried artifact, forgotten relics of a time that is fading. The horizon is an endless, muted palette of faded blues and grays, broken only by the occasional jagged peak that pierces the mist like a blade through shadow. The silence is absolute, broken only by the distant, rhythmic groan of a distant, half-collapsed structure.
