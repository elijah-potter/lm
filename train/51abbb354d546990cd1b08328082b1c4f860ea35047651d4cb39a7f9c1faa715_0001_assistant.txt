

• Make a list of things you would like to accomplish on your professional development day. Under each list item, write out why this item is important and include the ways that it helps you develop your professional skills.
• Create an engaging schedule for your professional development day. After you assemble your list of tasks, put them in an order that fuels your sense of productivity. We recommend mixing your task list up by difficulty so that you get a sense of achievement from shorter tasks and can tackle longer tasks before you run out of momentum for the day.
• Make use of the resources available to you. Depending on your field of work, you are liable to find an abundance of resources related to your profession online. These resources may include papers, articles, classes, certification programs, YouTube videos, and more.
• Work on your online presence. In the modern world, our online presence can say a lot about our work ethic. This is especially true for those in creative fields or groups of self-employed people. To improve your online presence, we recommend you update your LinkedIn, consider making a personal professional webpage for the services you provide, and get professional headshot photos taken.
• Improve your tech skills. If you have a job that requires a large amount of computer work, consider making time to improve your tech skills. You can do this by practicing typing, learning more about your computer's features, and studying keyboard shortcuts.
