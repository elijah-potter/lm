**To Whom It May Concern,**

I am writing this letter with the intention of preserving a moment in time, not to send it but to leave it behind as a silent testament. This correspondence was meant for someone who, for whatever reason, could not or would not receive it.

If you find this letter, know that its contents were crafted with care, not with the expectation of a response but with the hope that its words might resonate with someone who needs them. It was written in the quiet certainty that some messages, like the stars, are meant to be seen by the right eyes at the right time.

Should you choose to act on its contents, do so with wisdom. Should you choose not to, that too is its own kind of answer.

No further action is required. This letter is complete.

**— The Author**
