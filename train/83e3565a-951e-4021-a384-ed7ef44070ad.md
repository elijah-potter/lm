**Performance Routine Workflow:**
1. Warm-up: Dynamic stretches, light cardio (e.g., jogging in place, arm circles).
2. **Technique Drills**: Focused practice of specific movements (e.g., kettlebell swings, burpees, box jumps).
3. **Conditioning**: Interval-based endurance (e.g., 30s sprint, 30s walk repeats).
4. **Precision Moves**: Controlled, high-rep movements (e.g., pistol squats, muscle-ups).
5. **Energy System Test**: High-intensity circuit (e.g., 5 rounds: 10 squat jumps, 15 air squats, 30s plank).
6. **Peak Performance Phase**: Full-force execution (e.g., deadlifts, triple jumps, or finisher like "Battle Ropes + Sprints").
7. **Cool-Down**: Static stretching + mobility drills (e.g., hip openers, shoulder CARs).
8. **Recovery Ritual**: Hydration, contrast therapy (e.g., ice bath + sauna), and mental reset (e.g., visualization).

**Key: Intensity → Precision → Control → Execution.**
