## Bookstore Browsing & Unexpected Laughter (Berkeley, CA)

I was browsing the poetry section at Black Oak Books on Saturday afternoon, searching for something to ease a lingering melancholy. The light filtering through the front windows cast a warm, amber glow across the shelves, highlighting the worn spines of countless volumes. You were near the biography section, examining a book on Eleanor Roosevelt, and a quiet chuckle escaped your lips. It was a genuinely delightful sound, completely unexpected in that quiet space, and it drew my attention. 

We didn’t speak, but I noticed the vibrant turquoise of your scarf, a striking contrast against your dark coat. I lingered longer than necessary, hoping for a shared smile or a fleeting glance, but you moved on. The scent of old paper and leather, always comforting, felt somehow sharper that day, imbued with a sense of missed opportunity. I left shortly thereafter, carrying a volume of Rilke and a persistent feeling of wondering what might have been.
