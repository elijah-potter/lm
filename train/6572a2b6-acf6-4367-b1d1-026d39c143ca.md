Once upon a time, in a big, cozy house with a garden full of flowers, there lived a little blue bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white paws and a big, round, pink nose that sparkled when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to play. He saw a shiny red apple hanging from a tree. "Mmm, what a yummy apple!" Benny thought. He reached out to pick it, but just as he did, a loud *squeak* sounded behind him.

Benny spun around and saw a tiny, scared mouse named Milo. Milo had a big, round, gray nose and a tail that wiggled like a tiny flag. "Milo, don't be scared!" Benny said. "I won't hurt you."

Milo took a deep breath and hopped closer. "Can you help me find my lost home?" Milo asked. Benny nodded. "Of course! Let's go!"

Together, they searched the garden. Benny found a hole under a bush where Milo had left a trail of crumbs. "Look, Milo! The crumbs lead here!" Benny said, pointing to a small tunnel made of sticks and leaves.

They followed the tunnel until they reached a cozy burrow with a warm, soft blanket and a basket filled with cheese. "This is our new home!" Milo said happily. Benny chewed a piece of cheese and smiled. "It's perfect!"

From that day on, Benny and Milo became the best of friends. Whenever Benny saw Milo, he would say, "Time for a snack and a story!" And Milo would giggle and say, "Yes, Benny! Let's play!"

And that's how Benny the bunny and Milo the mouse learned that friendship is sweet, just like the cheese they shared.
