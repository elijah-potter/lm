In the kingdom of Arizondar, there was a draconian king who ruled with absolute and brutal power. The king was feared by his people and they suffered under his oppressive rule. After years of his merciless treatement, the people finally had enough.

They rose up and overthrew their horrid king. They decided to form a democratic government, letting every person have a say in how the land would be run. They pulled their kingdom from isolation and began trading with nearby kingdoms. Slowly, they built strong ties and began to prosper.
