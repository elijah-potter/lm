The concept of Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam, वसुधैव कुटुम्बकम, is a Sanskrit phrase that means that the whole world is one single family. It is an inclusive approach to life that gives India its identity.
The concept affects Indian culture, its food, music, literature, etc.
Simar and Divya will explain how this idea is present in daily life in New Delhi. Indeed, they will be describing a young adult’s perspective of Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam.
A unique opportunity to learn about one of the most interesting countries of the Commonwealth.
