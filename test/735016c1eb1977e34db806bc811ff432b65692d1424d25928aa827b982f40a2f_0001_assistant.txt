I love cooking for friends and family, experimenting with new recipes and flavors, and seeing their faces light up when they take that first bite. There's something truly special about the way food brings people together, and I feel fortunate to have had the opportunity to work in environments where that was the primary focus. When someone tells me that what I made is delicious, that just brings me all the joy in the world.

Anthony Bourdain once likened kitchen crews to pirate crews, and I couldn't agree more! Despite the intensity and roughness around the edges, I loved the chaos of the kitchen, and the crew members were some of the best and most interesting people I've had the privilege to work with.

Food is more than just fuel for our bodies. It's a cultural marker, a source of pride, and a way to connect with our communities.
