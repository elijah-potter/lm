Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a garden full of colorful flowers, there lived a curious bunny named Benny. Benny had big, round eyes and a heart full of wonder.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside and saw his friend, a little bird named Pip, sitting on a branch. "Pip, look!" Benny said, pointing to a shiny new seed. "Can you tell me what it is?"

Pip chirped, "It's a magic seed! If you plant it, it will grow into a tree that gives the sweetest berries ever!" Benny was excited. "I'll plant it today!"

Benny dug a tiny hole in the garden and carefully put the seed in. He covered it with soil and gave it a gentle pat. "Now, let's see what happens!" he whispered.

Days passed, and Benny watered the seed every day. Soon, tiny green leaves started to grow. Benny cheered, "It's growing! It's growing!"

Weeks went by, and Benny's little tree was now tall with lots of branches. It had beautiful flowers that smelled like candy. Benny picked a few ripe berries and shared them with Pip, who flew to his nest and shared them with all his feathered friends.

From that day on, Benny and Pip spent every morning together, planting seeds and watching their little tree grow. They knew their magic tree would always bring happiness and sweetness to their garden. And so, the little bunny and bird became the best friends, sharing stories, seeds, and smiles under the big, sunny sky.
