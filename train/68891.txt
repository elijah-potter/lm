How covid situation affected our mental health
It has been said that people who go through long-term anxiety or depression might have a chance to meet mental disorders.
In recent years many people reported frequent mood swings.
These severe symptoms have increased from 6%-9% during the pandemic especially in under the age of 60. Researchers found that mothers and women, in general, are being the victim in a large number.
Changes that have been noticed in people's lives in quarantine are related to changes to their dietary and physical activity habits.
One survey recorded the fact that people who are seeing negative changes in their exercise, simultaneously reported poor mental health conditions.
Another survey proved that people with high nutritious food reported fewer symptoms of depression and anxiety.
Starting at home for months was the new appetite for us, it was one of the big sad changes that happen in our life, which we didn’t expect.
How covid pandemic changed eating habits
WHO world health organization stated to change them from tasty one to a healthy one. Only one's immune system can fight against the coronavirus.
Therefore WHO specifically gave much emphasis on highly nutritious food.
Fast foods and packaged food
Multiple studies proved that people eating less but eating only fast foods, snacks, and packaged foods. Whereas some of the people brought healthy changes like consumption of fruits and vegetables.
Many of them suffered from food insecurity. this is because of loss of job and sudden changes to their living situation. That's why they bought cheaper foods and eating less.
Effect on people with an eating disorder
People with eating disorders experienced increased restricting, binging, purging, and exercising behavior during the pandemic situation. Still people with eating disorders experiencing a greater level of binge eating behavior.
Covid 19 changed our diet to 360 degrees. It excludes all the fast food and processed food and included homemade healthy food.
Changes in physical activity
With the changes in dietary style, people are also reporting changes in exercise levels. There has been seen a considerable decrease in physical activity.
People are more tended to sedentary behavior. An estimate shows that University students and women, in general, are getting more into exercise activities while young people are losing interest in it because of the increase of sedentary behavior.
Covid 19 killed social life. We couldn’t go out from home, it affected our physical health badly. We all became lazy.
Over the past years, people reported that they are gaining weight. One study showed that people who have excess weight before the pandemic tended to get more weight gain during a pandemic.
But obviously, not everyone is the same. Furthermore, some studies showed up 19% of US people losing weight. These factors are specifically linked to inadequate sleep, eating snacks after dinner, stress eating, and a heavy breakdown in physical activity.
Pandemic changes our lifestyle and diet so of course, it leaves an effect on our weight.
How diet can support mental health
Diets vary widely all over the world, influenced by incomes, lifestyle, culture, etc.
Yet some common dietary habits go for every person. Particularly now when people are staying at home due to covid, drink plenty of water and 60 minutes exercise every day.
The gut microbiome is highly influenced by what we eat, drastically affects our mood and behavior. food that is nutritionally rich and high in fiber such as fruits and vegetables, increases the consumption of those to improve overall health. These factors protect against depression. adversely highly processed foods leave a negative function on the brain and mental health.
Following a Mediterranean diet that is high in vegetables, fruits, whole grains, seafood, and olives and low in red meat and dairy products may help reduce the risk of depression. A Mediterranean diet with fish oil improves mental health. This diet carries healthy unsaturated fats such as omega 3 fatty acids to help reduce symptoms of depression. As we know there's a significant connection between diet and mental health, keep yourself more into healthy proteins.
Studies have found that the Mediterranean diet may help lower the risk of depression.
