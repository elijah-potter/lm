A student raised their hand during the last lecture of the semester to ask, *"Why do we need to learn this for the final exam?"* The professor paused mid-sentence, their pen hovering over the whiteboard. The classroom fell silent, the weight of the question lingering in the air like a trapped breath.

The professor sighed, shifting uncomfortably in their chair. *"Because,"* they began, *"some days, the universe conspires to remind us that we’re all just trying to survive."* The student blinked, a mix of confusion and frustration flickering across their face. The lecture resumed abruptly, but the question had already seeped into the room’s quiet hum—a reminder that some things aren’t meant to be answered in the heat of the moment.
