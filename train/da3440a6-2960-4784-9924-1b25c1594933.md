A floating market in a post-collapse city, where merchants barter in "currency of trust"—stolen tech components, handcrafted goods, or favors. The "rules" are unwritten but enforced by shared survival tactics: no one disputes prices; silence is currency; and the wind carries warnings. Bridges are reinforced with scavenged metal, and the air hums with the sound of a jury-rigged clock tower, its pendulum marking cycles of "rules" that shift daily based on who’s in charge. The river’s current carries both trade and secrets—last week’s "rule" was "no one touches the floating library," but today, a child is stealing a book from a cradle. The market thrives on chaos, and chaos is the new law.
