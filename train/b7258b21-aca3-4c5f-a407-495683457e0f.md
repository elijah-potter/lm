A floating market in Bangkok, Thailand, where vendors sell fresh produce and goods from boats tied to wooden poles in the river. Rules like "no bargaining" are loosely enforced, and haggling is common, though prices fluctuate unpredictably. Fishermen follow unspoken agreements to avoid conflicts over stolen goods or overfishing rights. The market thrives on tradition, but disputes over customs—such as who claims "first dibs" on rare items—arise daily, resolved through informal negotiation or local mediators. The river itself acts as a natural boundary, complicating disputes across shifting waterlines.
