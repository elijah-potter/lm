Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown fur and big, sparkly eyes. One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his favorite flower, the Sunflower, smiling at him from the garden. Benny hopped over and gave it a kiss.

Benny loved exploring his garden. He found a tiny mushroom that glowed in the dark, a patch of blueberries that were just ripe, and a big, soft dandelion that he could wiggle his nose into. Benny also met a little bird named Pip who lived in the tree. Pip had a shiny red beak and loved to sing.

One day, Benny felt hungry. He dug up some carrots from the garden and nibbled on them happily. Then, he met a wise old tortoise named Tilly who was sitting under a big oak tree. Tilly told Benny, "Growing things in the garden is like making a big, happy family. When we take care of them, they grow stronger and healthier."

Benny promised to help take care of the garden every day. He watered the plants, pulled the weeds, and even shared his carrots with Pip and Tilly. Soon, his garden grew bigger and happier than ever.

Every evening, Benny and his new friends would sit together and watch the sunset. Benny knew that even though his garden was tiny, it was his special place, full of love and happiness. And with every sunrise, Benny would hop back to his garden to start another day of growing, playing, and being friends.
