The Oakland Athletics are building a new $1.5 billion stadium in Las Vegas on the site of the Tropicana. The property is owned by Bally's and would be razed. In this deal, the A's are requesting only $395 million in public assistance, down from the $500 million previously requested. Construction is projected to begin next year, with stadium completion projected for 2027 or 2028. The A's are expected to relocate to Las Vegas after their current stadium lease expires in 2024.
