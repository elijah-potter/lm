The jagged cliffs of Veythar’s Obsidian Spire rise in near-vacuum, their black glass fractures whispering secrets of the last ice age. Below, the frozen wastes of the High Mists stretch endlessly—tundra cracked by auroras, where the wind carries the scent of salt and rusted metal. A lone miner’s lantern flickers in the snow, its beam cutting through the perpetual twilight, illuminating the skeletal remains of a crashed starfarer’s ship. The air hums with static, the occasional cry of a raven or the distant groan of ice shifting. At the horizon, the sky splits into a jagged, burning void—where the edge of the world seems to dissolve into the cosmos itself. The wind carries the weight of forgotten wars and the promise of what lies beyond.
