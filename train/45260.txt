A selection of 60 of our online World History videos provides teachers, schools, homeschool and special needs students with the opportunity to learn about World History to the required K-12 standards. Using The World History Collection, you'll increase your understanding of today's world by exploring its tumultuous past.
Travel with Christopher Columbus on his voyages of discovery to the new world in the Age of Discovery. Journey to the days of Napoleon and the French Revolution. Witness the rise and fall of the British Empire. Delight in the pageantry of the age of Queen Victoria.
Study the arrival of Imperialism in India and Africa. Understand the significance of the Scientific and Industrial Revolutions, which catapulted the world into the 20th Century. Study the making of the German nation, the Weimar Republic, Nazi Germany and the horrors of two world wars.
After watching these online videos students can then use our online interactive quizzes to discover what they have learnt and strengthen their understanding of the subject.
