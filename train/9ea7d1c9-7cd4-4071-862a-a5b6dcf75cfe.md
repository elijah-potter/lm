A lone figure steps out of a storm-churned vehicle, umbrella cracked, onto a windswept bridge overlooking a fog-shrouded valley. The neon signs of a distant city flicker in the distance, but the road ahead is a tangled web of overgrown paths. The GPS reads *"Unknown Location"* in red. A crumpled map clutched in one hand, the traveler hesitates—no landmarks, no familiar landmarks—just an endless stretch of mist and the hollow sound of their own breath. A sudden *clap* of thunder echoes; the sky darkens. They glance at their watch—three hours lost. The wind picks up, carrying whispers of the forest.
