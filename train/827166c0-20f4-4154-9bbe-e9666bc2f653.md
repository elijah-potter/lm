A bustling village square in a small European town, midday. Cobblestone paths wind through the heart of the community, where families, neighbors, and friends gather for daily life. The scent of freshly baked bread lingers from a nearby bakery, and children play tag near a fountain while older adults sit on benches, chatting over cups of tea. A local market stalls display handmade crafts, and the familiar laughter and clinking of cutlery fill the air. The village shopkeeper, whose name is known to everyone, greets passersby by name, and a group of lifelong friends from different households share stories over a communal meal in the communal hall. Every face, every voice, feels like a shared history.
