COB (Children's Opportunity for Brilliance) Community
COB is an educational Model that promotes "natural" learning styles that are in line with how the brain works and processes information. The Model taps into what motivates and engages students, providing them with the feedback and interactions necessary to reinforce learning and build upon their foundation. It is like the difference between allopathic (symptom focused) vs. naturopathic (holistic focused) medicine .
COB acknowledges each child as an individual.
COB acknowledges each family as having different needs.
COB is made up of many components but only one philosophy, that every child is a brilliant learner.
