A broken promise left unspoken,
Words left hanging in the air,
Heard by few, meant for none,
Yet echoes through the hallowed chair.

The vow was made in haste,
A whispered truth, a fleeting taste,
Now lies like dust in the wind,
A memory of love undone.

The other believed, too true,
Misinterpreted hearts and skies,
Thoughts of love, of fate entwined,
Now scattered like autumn’s wind.

No pen, no ink, just silence wide,
A story left half-told, untied,
Silent tears, a hollow sound,
A promise lost, like dreams untold.
