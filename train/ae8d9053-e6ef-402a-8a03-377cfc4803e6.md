A half-finished sketch of a dream caught on a faded sketchpad, half-smudged ink and erased lines tracing abstract figures that could be any number of things: a city at dusk, a figure in a coat too large for their frame, or a shadow moving across a windowpane. The corner where the paper bends over is curled, as if the artist paused mid-motion, a single pencil tip pressed into the surface where the composition might have continued if only they had stayed.

In the margins, scribbled in hurried, uneven script: *"Maybe it was a ghost. Or a mistake."* The last line, smudged beyond readability, suggests the question was never fully asked. The unfinished composition lingers between what was begun and what could have been.
