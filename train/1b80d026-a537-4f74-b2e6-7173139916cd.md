## Lost in the Record Store (Brooklyn, NY) (Williamsburg)

The afternoon light filtering through the dusty windows of Rough Trade was particularly lovely on Saturday. I was browsing the new arrivals, attempting to navigate the labyrinth of vinyl, when I noticed you. You were examining a first pressing of Nina Simone’s *I Put a Spell on You*, your brow furrowed in that concentrated, appreciative way people have when they’re truly connecting with music. I remember the distinct, comforting aroma of aged paper and vinyl clinging to the air, a scent that always feels like a warm embrace. We didn’t speak, but our eyes met briefly, and I felt a fleeting, almost tangible sense of shared appreciation for something beautiful. You had a vibrant, emerald green scarf draped around your neck, a striking contrast to the muted tones of the store. I hesitated, wanting to say something, but the moment passed. I left shortly after, feeling a peculiar lightness, and now find myself wondering if you felt it too. 

If you recall a woman with auburn hair browsing near the jazz section, please reply with your favorite Nina Simone song.
