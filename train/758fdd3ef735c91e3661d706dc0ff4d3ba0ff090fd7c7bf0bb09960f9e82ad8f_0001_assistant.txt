"Come on Chicago! Shoot the ball!" ten-year-old Michael shouted as the Bulls held the ball with five seconds left on the clock. The Bulls were down two and they needed a basket to tie or win the game. 3, 2, 1. . . The shot went up as Michael sat on the edge of his seat in anticipation. Swoosh! A clean three-point shot to win the game! Michael lept from the edge of his seat and ran around the house shouting about the Bulls winning. After his celebration, Michael went outside and got the basketball from the garage, and started shooting hoops. Every day Michael would spend hours outside, shooting from different spots, practicing dribbling, and dunking the ball. Michael desperately wanted to join the Bulls one day and knew if he practiced hard enough he could do it. His idol, Michael Jordan, trained harder than everyone else to become the best basketball player of all time. Michael also thought that he could be the next best basketball player. He trained all day until he couldn't lift his arms. He trained in the morning, he trained at night. Anytime Michael wasn't watching basketball, he was playing it. Michael's dream to join the Bulls drove him to keep working at it until he became a Chicago Bull. "One day," Michael would think to himself, "I will join the Chicago Bulls."
