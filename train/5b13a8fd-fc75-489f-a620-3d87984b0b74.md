The abandoned industrial district near the old railway station, a maze of rusted warehouses and crumbling arches. Workers arrive daily at 3 AM, arriving by train from neighboring towns. Some bring food, others just sit in the dim glow of flickering streetlights, whispering to strangers. No schedule, no purpose—just a shared sense of belonging. The place is named *"The Hollow"* by those who know it best, though no one can explain why. The doors are always slightly ajar, and the air smells of damp wood and something faintly metallic. The few who leave are rarely the same.
