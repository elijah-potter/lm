**New Haven, March 12th – Residents report sudden spike in rare meteorite sightings near downtown. Authorities confirm three confirmed falls in the past 24 hours, all landing within a one-square-mile radius near the old harbor. Local scientists speculate a previously undocumented celestial anomaly, while emergency crews deploy to investigate. No injuries reported, but debris collection teams are mobilized to secure fragments before they are scavenged by collectors.
