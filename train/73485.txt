Brazza's Martin (Phedina brazzae) - HBW 9, p. 645
French: Hirondelle de Brazza
Spanish: Golondrina del Congo
Other common names: Congo Martin, Brazza’s Swallow
Taxonomy: Phedina Brazzae Oustalet, 1886, below Kwamouth, lower River Congo, DRCongo.
Sometimes placed in a monotypic genus Phedinopsis and occasionally in Riparia. Is, however, close to P. borbonica, with which it forms a superspecies. Monotypic.
Distribution: S PRCongo, S DRCongo and NE Angola (N Lunda Norte).
