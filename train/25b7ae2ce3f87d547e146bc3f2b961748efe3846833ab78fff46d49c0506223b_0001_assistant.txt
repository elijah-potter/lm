- Campers are not allowed to leave the group to go to the restrooms or any other part of camp alone. Always follow the buddy system and bring another camper with you. 
- You and your buddy must always check in with your counselor before leaving the group to go to the restroom or elsewhere.
- Always carry a working flashlight with you at camp. This is a daytime and nighttime rule.
- Do not use your cell phone or mobile device after 9:00 PM. The only exception is emergencies.
- Don't bring food or drink from home to camp because some campers have food allergies. This includes snacks, candy, soda, etc.
- There is a strict no-bullying rule. If you engage in or promote bullying of any kind, you will have to speak with a counselor or the director. You may even get sent home.
- Follow the lights off rule starting at 9:00 PM every night. 
- You may only take one shower per day in the bathhouse in order to conserve resources.
