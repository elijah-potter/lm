**BREAKING:** Residents of the historic district of Eldermere report sightings of a mysterious, glowing "Floating Lantern" drifting over the Grand Canal at midnight. Officials urge caution as the phenomenon has appeared in past decades but never in the city’s central waterway. The city’s Archaeological Division confirms no local records of similar events, though locals claim it appears to follow the same path as the city’s legendary "Spirit of the Canal" from the 18th century. Authorities are investigating potential links to the recent surge in missing persons in the area. No other details available at this time.

---
**Update:** Witnesses describe the lantern as a translucent orb emitting a faint, pulsating blue light, moving in erratic, almost weightless fashion. Some report hearing faint, rhythmic drumming beneath the surface of the water. No debris or signs of human activity found near its last recorded position near the old docks. Police recommend staying indoors after dark.
