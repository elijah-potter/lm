I sat patiently by the door wagging my tail. I'd heard the car door shut and the crunch of my human's footsteps on the gravel outside. When she finally opened the door, I leaped up to greet her.
"Hi, Tasha! Who's a good girl?" My owner greeted me with lots of scratches and pats. Good, she was happy to see me. Maybe she wouldn't be too upset by the mess I had made in the living room. It was time to make myself scarce. I tucked my tail between my legs and sat under the kitchen table.
"Out of sight, out of mind," I thought to myself. Soon enough, though, my owner discovered the mess I had made. She called out, "Tasha!" very sternly, and I obeyed sheepishly, looking at the mess of fluff and torn cushions I had created in my hyper state this morning. She merely looked at me, then the cushions, then back at me. I began to wag my tail slowly and approached her, head down. Presently, I looked up at her beseechingly.
"Bad girl!" she said, pointing to the cushions.
I know! I know! I dropped my eyes to the floor, ashamed of myself. I slunk back under the table and watched as she cleaned up the mess and discarded the cushion. It was sad for me when my human was displeased.
