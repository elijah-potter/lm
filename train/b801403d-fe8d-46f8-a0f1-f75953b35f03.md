Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had soft, fluffy ears and a big, round tail that waved when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his favorite flower, a bright red tulip, growing next to his burrow. "Mmm, tulips taste sweet!" Benny thought. He nibbled a little bit and giggled.

Just then, Benny heard a tiny *plop!* He looked down and saw his little friend, a duck named Daisy, splashing in the nearby puddle. "Hello, Daisy!" Benny said cheerfully.

Daisy quacked. "Hi, Benny! Did you see my shiny new feather? It sparkles like the sun!"

Benny clapped his paws. "It looks so pretty! Want to race to the far end of the garden?"

They ran as fast as they could, their tiny legs moving quickly. They skidded on the grass and laughed when they tripped. At the end, they both splashed into a big, happy puddle.

As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and orange, Benny and Daisy curled up together under their favorite big leaf. "Best adventure ever," Benny said, and Daisy quacked in agreement.

They fell asleep, dreaming of more fun adventures together, under the soft glow of the stars.
