There are a few different ways that you can try to promote hair growth so that it grows strong and quickly. I've listed a few things to try below:

- Make sure you are eating enough vitamins and minerals and getting the nutrition your body needs. If you are on a restrictive diet, it may hinder your hair growth as your body will need to focus on more important bodily functions as opposed to hair growth. 
- Using topical products that contain caffeine may help to stimulate hair growth as well as prevent hair loss. 
- Try using essential oil mixtures on your hair. There is some research that may support the effectiveness of some essential oils on hair growth and preventing hair loss including rosemary, pumpkin seed oil, peppermint oil, jojoba oil, and lavender oil. 
- Massaging your scalp may help to stimulate blood flow to the area and can lead to thicker, stronger hair. 
- Avoid using styling tools frequently that use heat such as flat irons, hair dryers, and curling irons, as they can damage your hair. 

You may want to try one of the aforementioned techniques at a time to see what will be most beneficial to your hair's health and growth.
