4. Lingo - If you look the part, you should also speak the part. That means using the language and slang of the 1980s. Use words and phrases such as "gnarly", "tubular", "awesome" and "radical". Greet your attendees with a high five and a "How's it hanging?" and "Sick dude!"
5. Games - You can play games with the other attendees that were very popular back in the 80s, including Twister and Parcheesi. Or, if you are feeling adventurous, rent some arcade machines like Pac-Man or pinball and place them in the venue for attendees to have hours of fun!
