Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around his cozy burrow with his friends. One sunny morning, Benny spotted a shiny red berry near his friend Lily’s house. He nibbled just a tiny bit, but then he saw a big, yummy blueberry on the ground. He took a bite and felt happy—until he realized he was getting too full.

Benny’s tummy felt too heavy, so he decided to share. He asked Lily, "Do you want some?" Lily giggled and said, "Yes, please!" Benny gave her the blueberry, and together they munched happily. Soon, Benny’s tummy was just right again, and he hopped off to play with his other friends, Pippin and Snickers.

Pippin had a tiny acorn, and Snickers had a big, juicy apple. They all sat together under a big, leafy tree, sharing and laughing. Benny learned that sharing makes everyone happy, and that even a little bit of something can be enough to feel full and kind.

The end.
