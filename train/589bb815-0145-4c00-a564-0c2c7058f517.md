Whispering Pines Park, a hidden grove of ancient oak trees in the quiet valley of Eldermere, where the wind carries secrets through the dense canopy. The air hums with the faintest murmurs of unseen voices, whispered by the wind through the gnarled branches, creating a natural acoustics chamber that amplifies soft sounds. Locals claim that if you press your ear to the bark of the oldest tree—*The Sentinel*—you can hear the echoes of forgotten conversations from the past. Visitors often gather at dusk, their lips forming shapes in the dim light, as if sharing confessions meant only for the wind. The park’s moss-covered stone circle, where whispers seem to linger longer, is said to be the only place in the region where the voices of the dead still speak to the living.
