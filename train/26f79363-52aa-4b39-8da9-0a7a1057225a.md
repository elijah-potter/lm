The rain blurred the neon lights of the city as they stood on the rooftop, their fingers brushing the cold metal railing. *"You’re not afraid anymore,"* she whispered, and he turned to face her, the weight of silence between them finally lifted. Their eyes locked—no need for words. A shared memory of laughter, tears, and the way the world had felt smaller, then bigger, then just right. The city hummed below them, but they were already inside each other’s thoughts, a quiet storm of understanding passing between them.
