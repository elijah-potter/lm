The sharp *clang* of the glass breaking against the marble floor interrupted the heated exchange. "You *idiot*," shouted the older man, his voice cracking, "do you *think* I’m the one who gets to decide what’s best for *us*?!" His fist connected with the side of his own temple, but the pain was dulled by the sheer absurdity of it all. The younger woman, clutching a half-empty bottle of whiskey, spun around, her dark eyes wide with fury. "Then why the hell did you leave me the *keys* to the apartment?" she spat, her voice trembling with barely contained rage. Outside, the distant hum of the city pulsed like a heartbeat, indifferent to their chaos. The apartment door swung open, revealing the shadow of a delivery truck’s headlights cutting through the dim hallway. Somewhere, a siren wailed in the distance.
