

Once upon a time, there was a happy little boat named Charlie. Charlie spent his days chugging along in the sea. There was one thing Charlie loved more than anything else in the world, ice cream. At night, Charlie would sneak onto the land, go to various ice cream shops, and eat all the yummy ice cream. One day, Charlie went to Mr. Freeze's store and eat all of the rocky road ice cream. Mr. Freeze was upset. 

Mr. Freeze set up a trap to catch Charlie. When Charlie entered the shop, a freeze ray would fire and freeze Charlie. When Mr. Freeze was setting up the trap, he accidentally fired the ray and froze himself. Charlie took the opportunity to eat all the ice cream in the store. "You darn boat," said Mr. Freeze when Charlie eat all the ice cream. Charlie felt bad and unfroze Mr. Freeze. The two became best friends and always shared ice cream from that day on.
