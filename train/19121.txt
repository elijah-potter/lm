Hyperbaric Oxygen Chamber is a treatment used worldwide for a variety of conditions and diseases such as neurological disorders, stroke diabetes, lyme disease, cancer, inflammatory conditions and others.
Inside the hyperbaric chamber, the air pressure is roughly two and a half times higher than normal air pressure. This allows your body to carry more oxygen to your organs and tissues, supporting the healing process.
Benefits of Hyperbaric Oxygen Chamber:
Increases oxygenation to the body’s tissues
Increases blood flow and circulation
Boosts white blood cell counts
Reduces swelling and pain
Facilitates wound healing
