Here are some egg ideas you can prepare for a quick lunch or keep in the fridge.

- Scrambled eggs: They are easy to make and usually take less than 15 minutes to prepare. All you need are eggs, salt, pepper, and a stove.
- Egg Salad: Egg salad can be prepared ahead of time and be kept in the fridge for a quick lunch. A basic egg salad recipe consists of boiled eggs, mayo, salt, pepper, relish, and any other garnishes you may find desirable.
- Deviled Eggs: Deviled eggs are another egg dish that can be prepared in advance for refrigeration. Most deviled egg recipes call for boiled eggs, mayo, mustard, paprika, salt, pepper, relish, and sometimes bacon crumbles.
