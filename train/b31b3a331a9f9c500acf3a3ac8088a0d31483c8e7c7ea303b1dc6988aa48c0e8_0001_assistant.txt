A pollinator is anything that carries pollen from the male part of the plant, called the stamen, to the female part of the plant, called the stigma. Pollen can be from the same plant or carried to other plants. The movement of pollen allows for fertilization to occur, allowing the plant to produce seeds, fruits, and young plants. The following are considered pollinators:

1. Bees
2. Birds
3. Bats
4. Butterflies
5. Beetles
6. Water
7. Wind
