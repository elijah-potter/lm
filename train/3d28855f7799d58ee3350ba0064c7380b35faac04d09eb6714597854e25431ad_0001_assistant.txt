1. Deep Tissue Massage 

One of the most popular types of massage therapy, deep tissue massage uses firm pressure and slow movements to knead stress and pain out of muscles. It helps disperse scar tissue, improve blood flow, and promote a healthier immune system. 

2. Swedish Massage 

One of the oldest types of massage therapy, Swedish massage uses a combination of steady, long strokes and friction movements in circular motions to move blood toward the heart for better circulation. 

3. Hot Stone Massage 

Hot stone massage involves using flat stones, heated with hot water, to relieve muscle pain and relax muscle tension. The kneading motions around the stones are similar to Swedish massage. 

4. Aromatherapy Massage 

Studies have shown significant links between human emotions and the olfactory senses, which aromatherapy massage utilizes. This type of massage uses natural essential oils to stimulate the senses and relax the mind while kneading motions relax the muscles. 

Some popular, relaxing scents employed in a typical aromatherapy massage session include lavender, lemon, peppermint, orange, or eucalyptus.

5. Trigger Point Massage 

There are pressure points all over your body that, when pressed in the right ways, can relieve pain and chronic health problems. Trigger point massage is the manipulation of your body’s pressure points to relieve specific issues, such as sinus congestion, stomach pain, and headaches. 

While it’s possible to find relief through massage at home, massage therapy is best practiced by specialists with knowledge of human anatomy. Massage therapists are well-versed in many different areas of massage, so they could be able to help you restore comfort to your mind and body in ways that at-home massage cannot.
