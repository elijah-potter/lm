**The Last Light**

The old clock tower creaked at midnight, its pendulum swinging slow as the wind howled through the broken glass of the church’s stained-glass windows. Elias stood at the base, his breath fogging in the cold, his coat sleeves rolled up to his elbows.

*"You always say that,"* his sister, Mira, said from the steps below, her voice muffled by the cobblestones.

Elias frowned. *"What?"*

*"That the night is endless."* She shrugged. *"Like time itself doesn’t care."*

He stared at the horizon, where the aurora borealis painted the sky in shimmering violet and silver. The words hung between them, thick and heavy.

*"But it does,"* he whispered, his voice raw.

Mira didn’t answer. She just stood there, watching the stars, her fingers tracing the worn edge of her father’s pocket watch—a gift from him before the fire had taken everything.

Outside, a crow cawed. The sound echoed like a question.
