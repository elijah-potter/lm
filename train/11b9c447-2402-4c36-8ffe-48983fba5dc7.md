**Dry Run:**
1. Wake up at **6:30 AM** – No alarm; body naturally rises.
2. **7:00 AM**: Cold shower (30 sec) → mental clarity, adrenaline spike.
3. **7:15 AM**: Meditation (10 min) – Focused breathwork, no distractions.
4. **7:25 AM**: Workout (45 min) – Weighted steps, sprints, mobility drills (no ego).
5. **8:10 AM**: Shower – Cold water post-workout, no lathering.
6. **8:30 AM**: Breakfast – High-protein, minimal carbs; no screens.
7. **9:00 AM**: Plan day in 30-min sprints – Prioritize tasks, eliminate "urgent."
8. **12:00 PM**: Lunch – Balanced macros; no guilt over timing.
9. **1:00 PM**: Deep work block (90 min) – No interruptions; 5-min rule for emails.
10. **2:30 PM**: Skill refinement (30 min) – Mastery over fluff.
11. **3:00 PM**: Movement break – Stretching/foam rolling (prevents stiffness).
12. **4:30 PM**: Social time (15 min) – Intentional connection, no passive scrolling.
13. **6:00 PM**: Dinner – Mindful eating, no distractions.
14. **7:30 PM**: Wind down – Journal (gratitude), no work.
15. **9:00 PM**: Sleep – No screens; blackout curtains; consistent bedtime.
---
**Performance Triggers**:
- **Consistency**: Rituals reduce decision fatigue.
- **Intention**: Each task has a purpose (e.g., "Today, I’ll learn X").
- **Discipline**: Reject comfort; embrace discomfort.
- **Energy Management**: Prioritize high-effort tasks during peak focus.
- **Environment**: Control inputs/outputs (e.g., no phone in bathroom).
---
**Key Metrics to Track**:
- **Focus**: Pomodoro counts (25/5 min).
- **Recovery**: Sleep quality, stress biomarkers.
- **Progress**: Weekly goal completion rate.

**Outcome**: 80% of tasks completed with 90% effort → 10% margin for adaptability.
