The rain hammered against the rusted windowpanes of the abandoned bookstore, each drop a whisper of forgotten stories. Inside, the air smelled of aged paper and damp wood, the only sound the distant murmur of a radio tuning between stations. Elias knelt by the cracked floorboard, fingers brushing the spine of an old volume titled *"The Alchemist’s Ledger."*

He had been here for hours—ever since the clock struck midnight. The ledger was the key. Every entry held a name, a location, a promise. And now, at last, the final page was open.

A knock at the door made him freeze. The sound was light, hesitant—like someone unsure whether to enter. Elias stood slowly, his breath fogging the cold glass. The figure outside hesitated, then pushed open the door.

A woman stood there, her cloak damp, her eyes wide. *"You said there was a way in,"* she whispered. *"I’m not here to take it. I’m here to find it."*
