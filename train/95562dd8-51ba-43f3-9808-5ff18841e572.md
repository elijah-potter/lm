A labyrinth of fractured time in the abandoned **Blackthorn Observatory**. The air hums with static, and clocks tick erratically—some moving backward, others stuck at 3:17 AM. The stars above are a grid of frozen constellations, their patterns shifting when observed, as if viewed through a distorted lens. The walls pulse faintly, like veins of liquid time. Shadows stretch unnaturally, and footsteps echo twice—once from the past, once from the future. The only constant: the humming clocktower at its center, its hands spinning in circles without direction.

*Ephemeral echoes of a dead century linger in the air.*
