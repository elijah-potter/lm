The **Most Valuable Player (MVP) of the "Annual World's Worst Performance" Awards**, presented annually by the *Global Disappointment Society*, recognizes individuals whose achievements are universally panned. This prize, awarded in a mock ceremony held during the annual **"World’s Most Ridiculous Sports Day,"** is given to someone who has either:

1. **Invented the "Worst Gadget of the Year"** (e.g., a toaster that burns bread into a question mark).
2. **Delivered the most cringe-worthy public speech** (e.g., a TED Talk about "why socks are the root of all evil").
3. **Achieved the most absurdly bad luck** in a single day (e.g., dropping a waffle iron on your foot *while* texting your cat).
4. **Volunteered for a dare that defies logic** (e.g., "I dare you to eat a raw egg while wearing a banana peel").

**Candidates often include:**
- The person who *accidentally* became the first to solve a Rubik’s Cube in **100 minutes** while napping.
- The athlete who won a gold medal in **"World’s Ugliest Olympic Event"** (e.g., "Splashy Sprint" where competitors jump into a pool of misfired paintballs).
- The CEO who gave the **"Most Overly Dramatic Corporate Town Hall"** (e.g., announcing layoffs while wearing a clown nose).

The trophy is a **gold-plated banana peel** with the inscription: *"For those who inspire us to laugh at our own mediocrity."* The ceremony is broadcast live on **WorstChannel TV**, where the audience votes via **sad emoji upvotes** (😢😢😢). The winner is often **unceremoniously dismissed with a single "Nope" from the judges**.
