**Incident Response Policy for Workplace Safety and Accountability**

**Title:** *Incident Response Protocol for [Company Name]*
**Effective Date:** [Insert Date]
**Effective for:** All employees, contractors, and third-party stakeholders

---

### **1. Definitions**
- **Incident:** Any unplanned event that disrupts operations, poses safety risks, or requires immediate action (e.g., workplace violence, severe misconduct, data breach, or safety violation).
- **Responding Party:** Designated HR representative, security, or supervisor tasked with investigating and documenting the incident.
- **Reporting Party:** Individual(s) who identified or witnessed the incident (confidentiality protected).
- **Confidentiality:** All parties agree to maintain discretion regarding the incident’s details unless legally required otherwise.

---

### **2. Immediate Response**
- **Evacuation/Safety:** If safety is compromised, follow emergency protocols. Do not engage unless authorized.
- **Documentation:** Record:
  - Time/date, location, and witnesses.
  - Actions taken (e.g., security notification, medical aid).
  - Preserve evidence (photos, videos, statements) without altering the scene.
- **Notification:** Report to the Responding Party within **1 hour** of discovery.

---

### **3. Investigation Process**
- **Witness Interviews:** Conduct separate, unbiased interviews to gather facts. Avoid leading questions.
- **Evidence Review:** Examine relevant data, records, or physical evidence (e.g., emails, security footage, safety logs).
- **Consultation:** Work with legal, IT, or external experts if needed (e.g., cybersecurity for data breaches).
- **Decision Criteria:**
  - **Safety Violation:** Immediate corrective action (e.g., training, equipment review).
  - **Misconduct:** Review disciplinary protocol (e.g., warning, termination, counseling).
  - **Data Incident:** Follow cybersecurity incident response plan; report to IT and legal.
  - **Violent/Threatening Incident:** Escalate to security/legal immediately; do not intervene unless life-threatening.

---
### **4. Outcomes**
- **Safety Violations:** Retraining, equipment upgrades, or corrective actions.
- **Misconduct:**
  - **First Offense:** Written warning + performance improvement plan (PIP) with 30/60/90-day review.
  - **Repeat Offense:** Suspension, termination, or legal referral.
  - **Serious/Repeated Violations:** Immediate termination without further notice (ITT).
- **Data Incidents:** Comply with GDPR/CCPA; notify stakeholders as required by law.
- **Non-Disclosure:** Mandatory review of workplace conduct policies.

---
### **5. Confidentiality & Legal Compliance**
- **Disclosure:** Share incident details only with authorized personnel (e.g., HR, legal, security). Avoid public discussion unless required by law.
- **Retention:** Maintain records for **7 years** (or legal requirements) in a secure system.
- **Exemptions:** Exceptions apply for:
  - Safety violations (publicly reported if necessary).
  - Criminal activity (law enforcement involvement).

---
### **6. Reporting & Appeals**
- **Submission:** Incident reports submitted to [HR Contact Name/Email] by [deadline, e.g., 24 hours].
- **Review:** Responding Party provides a **written summary** to the Reporting Party within **5 business days**.
- **Appeal:** Parties dissatisfied may appeal to [Senior HR Manager] within **10 days**.

---
### **7. Training & Awareness**
- All employees complete mandatory annual **Incident Reporting Training** (e.g., via [Training Platform Name]).
- New hires undergo **onboarding safety briefing** within 30 days.

---
### **8. Policy Enforcement**
- **Violation:** Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action up to termination.
- **Amendments:** Revised by [Company Name] HR Committee with public notice via [internal communication channel].
