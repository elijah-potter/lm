Soft earth cradles the weight of my boots, yielding slightly beneath each step. The air hums with a damp warmth, thick as a freshly brewed tea, carrying the faintest scent of damp stone and something earthier—old soil, perhaps. A low, rhythmic *thump-thump-thump* echoes through the darkness, like a heartbeat, pulsing from within the walls. The walls themselves are a living thing, breathing slowly, their surfaces cool but not cold, damp patches clinging to the walls like invisible fingers. The scent of crushed herbs lingers, mingling with the metallic tang of iron, faint but persistent. A distant murmur, like whispered voices, drifts through the space—soft, indistinct, but carrying the weight of many stories. Shadows stretch long and shifting, moving with the unseen. The air shimmers with the heat of unseen bodies, warm and alive, yet still. The earth beneath me feels like a vast, sleeping giant, waiting.
