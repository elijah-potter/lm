TESOL Online Resources
- Busy Teacher
- Xtreme Papers
- Khan Academy
- EReading Workseets
If you want to make the most of your opportunities to improve as a TESOL, then you're in luck; there are dozens of extremely helpful resources that you can freely access to find new ways to improve your value as a professional. Beyond your basic level of certification, the full potential of a TESOL is only achievable with a consistent commitment to staying at the forefront of information dissemination, research and discussion in the field; by keeping in touch with all of the right resources, you can ensure that you never fall out of the loop. The following are five resources for TESOLs that will all serve you well if you want to make sure that you're always in the best possible position to learn more.
With Busy Teacher, you have access to a plethora of free resources designed to help all TESOL professionals gain new points of perspective and insight into the field. All of the resources on the site can be searched with target keywords so that your hunt for information is whittled down to the most relevant topics. One of the signature benefits that the website provides is a series of worksheets that you can freely modify to fit the mold of your classroom to to fit your personal teaching style accurately.
Through Xtreme Papers, you'll be provided a generous spread of marking schemes relevant to IELTS, TOEFL and IGCSE. With easy access to a full reservoir of papers based on all of these major exams, you'll be in perfect position to keep a constant point of perspective on the most relevant information regarding tests that your students will need to pass in order to graduate from your lessons.
Within Eduplace, you've got an incredibly useful resource for developing graphic organizers. Because many TESOL professionals can find graphic organizers to be one of the most tedious things to manually put together on a regular basis, having a quick and easy resource to immediately develop them can naturally be incredibly helpful for time management. With Eduplace, you've got a far for all-inclusive set of solutions that you can use to develop your graphic organizers than Word Document. The graphic organizers are complete with spelling lists, bilingual resources, and anything else you need based on personal preference for thoroughness.
With Khan Academy, you'll have a valuable resource to use for teaching your students an interdisciplinary spread of subjects ranging from mathematics to history. The primary advantage offered through this resource is the ability it affords you to give your students instruction in just about all of the foundation-building subjects they'll need in English. Simply by signing up through Facebook, your students will be able to practice any and all skills they need to build a solid foundation of general knowledge in every basic subject area.
With EReading worksheets, you have one of the most valuable resources for TESOLS who need to develop well-organized comprehension activities on the fly. This website is a good fit for TESOL teachers who have students that are a relatively advanced level of English proficiency, ensuring that they're able to continually develop at a consistently satisfying rate as their level of affinity with English continues to rise with experience.
