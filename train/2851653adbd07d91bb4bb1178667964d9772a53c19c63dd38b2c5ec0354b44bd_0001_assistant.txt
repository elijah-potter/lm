Tea is a general term that can refer to any infusion of dried leaves, buds, flowers, fruits, or herbs in hot water. Tea can be classified into different types based on the level of oxidation of the tea leaves, such as green tea, black tea, white tea, oolong tea, and pu-erh tea. Tea can also be flavored with various ingredients such as lemon, honey, mint, jasmine, or bergamot. Tea can be enjoyed plain or with milk or sugar. Tea is usually served hot but can also be iced

Chai is a type of spiced tea that originated in India and is popular in many Asian and Middle Eastern countries. It is usually made with black tea, milk, sugar, and a blend of spices such as cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, ginger, and black pepper. Chai can be brewed in different ways, such as boiling the tea leaves and spices with water and milk, or steeping the tea leaves and spices in hot water and adding milk later. Chai is often served hot or iced.
