In a dimly lit room, flickering lanterns casting long shadows, **Mira** knelt beside the cracked earth near the riverbank, her hands trembling as she pressed a small, sealed clay tablet into the soil. The words carved on it—*"The debt is paid"*—glowed faintly under the moonlight. The river’s whispers carried the scent of damp stone and iron, while the distant hum of the village’s clock tower faded into the night. A single, cold breath of wind stirred the dead leaves at her feet.
