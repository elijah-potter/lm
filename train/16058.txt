Circuits of Light, Jesus Is the Light of the World
PHYSICAL SCIENCE – GRADE 3 - CATHOLIC
This course is offered through Credere Academy's affiliation with the Institute for Theological
Encounter with Science and Technology (ITEST) and CreationLens.org.
The ITEST founder, Fr. Robert Brungs, SJ, saw the need to reach the youngest
Christians with the message of compatibility between faith and science.
ITEST undertook the project Exploring the World, Discovering God and
built an ensemble of learning modules designed for the elementary
grades. There is continuity between this work and what ITEST has always
told the adult community: faith and science are two complementary
pathways toward greater knowledge of God.
Our hope is that teachers in Catholic and Christian schools will make
use of these course modules to supplement existing curricula, where
religion and science are commonly in completely separate compartments.
Credere Academy Associate Instructors and Organizations
are carefully selected affiliates that are
part of the Credere Academy Team.
