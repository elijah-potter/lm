The United States is home to many national parks and mountains to hike, here are some of them:

 1. Badlands National Park, South Dakota: Hike through rugged land formed by geologic deposits. Stay after sunset to see the Milky Way.
2. Grand Teton National Park, Wyoming: Walk through alpine terrain and by pristine lakes. View the wildlife on the 200 miles of hiking trails.
3. Adirondack Mountains, New York: Visit the cozy small towns, hike the Wild Walk above the treetops, and kayak on Lake George near the mountain ranges.
