There have been three other TV versions of The Twilight Zone besides the critically acclaimed original series created and hosted by Rod Serling, which ran from 1959 until 1964. The first revival debuted on CBS on September 27, 1985, ten years after Rod Serling's death. Unlike the first Twilight Zone, which featured one new story for each episode, this version sometimes contained several stories in one episode of varying lengths. Also, unlike Serling's version, it featured no host but was sometimes narrated by actor Charles Aidman for the first two seasons, and later Robin Ward for the third season, which was produced by a Canadian company so that CBS could sell it into syndication. It completed its run in 1989. The host format was subsequently brought back for the short-lived 2002 UPN revival with Forest Whitaker introducing the episodes, while Jordan Peele stepped in to host the 2019 revival that he produced and developed for the streaming platform CBS All Access where it aired for two seasons.
