The arguments escalated as the fire alarm blared through the night-shift kitchen, sprinklers spraying water onto the floor. *"You’re wasting food—we can’t afford this!"* the cook snapped, tossing a tray of undercooked sausages into the sink. The foreman, arms crossed, glared at him. *"You’re supposed to be checking them for doneness, not dumping them!"*

A third worker, mid-sip of coffee, let out a cough. The tension shifted when the foreman’s phone buzzed—a shift manager text: *"Emergency—all hands to the break room, now."* The cook’s jaw tightened. *"We’re supposed to be working."* The foreman grunted. *"Then work harder."* Outside, sirens wailed as the fire trucks arrived, sirens echoing through the empty kitchen. The last one to leave was the cook, clutching a half-eaten sausage, as the foreman watched the exit, arms crossed.
