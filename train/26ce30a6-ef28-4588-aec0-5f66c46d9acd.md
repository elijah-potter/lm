The city limits of **Zion’s Hollow** blur between the **Blackthorn Forest** and the **Saltmire Marsh**. There are no official signs marking the border, only the faint scent of damp earth and the distant howl of wolves—both of which can mean either welcome or warning. The local magistrate, **Elder Veyra**, rules by "the unspoken code," where laws are written in the cracks of cobblestones and interpreted by the weight of a handshake. The **Copper Market** thrives on deals struck under a single lantern, where "no harm intended" is as binding as a signed contract. Taxes are collected in barter, not coin—sometimes in trade, sometimes in blood if the debt feels too heavy. The **Rustborn Quarter**, where the old machines still groan in the streets, operates on its own rhythm: a blacksmith’s hammer strikes at noon, and no one dares argue. Children learn the rules by watching, not by being told. If you wander too far into the **Frostfang Mines**, you might find the old miners still marking their tunnels with crude signs: *"Do not dig where the dead call."* The dead here don’t rest—they whisper.
