"Can you keep a secret?" The man loading his clothes into the washer next to me asked I wasn't sure he was talking to me, but when I glanced in his direction, he was smiling and making eye contact.

I wasn't sure how I felt about talking to a strange man at the laundromat, but I have to admit, I was intrigued.

"I guess so," I said hesitantly. "It would depend on the secret."

The man smiled and nodded. "That's a good answer."

He motioned to the coin tray on his machine and asked, "Do you see this?"

I squinted to be certain that I did. I noticed that instead of quarters filling the slots, he had filled them with pennies. "Why do you have pennies in there?" I asked. "That's not going to work."

"Hmm, is that so?" he asked amusedly, pushing the coin tray into the machine and pressing the "start" button.

What do you know? It started!

My mouth agape, I faced him in disbelief. "How did you do that?" I asked.

His voice lowered to a conspiratorial, loud whisper, and he replied, "They all do that. Try it and see!"

I had to dig some random pennies out of the dust and wadded-up receipts at the bottom of my handbag, but my hand emerged victorious, holding five pennies. I slipped one into each of the open slots in the tray of my machine, then pushed it in and pressed "start." I exclaimed, "No way!" as it started right up, and I smiled back at my new laundromat friend. "How did you find that out?" I asked.

"I used to manage a laundromat that had this same model of washer. I caught one of my customers doing it. I didn't say anything because, well, good for him for finding a secret hack. The name's Butch, by the way," he said, extending his hand to shake mine.

"Claudia," I replied.

And that was the start of what turned out to be a very interesting friendship.
