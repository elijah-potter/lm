Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the meadow. One sunny morning, Benny spotted a tiny blue flower that was just beginning to bloom. "I wonder if it’s magic," Benny thought.

Benny picked the flower carefully and placed it in his basket. As he hopped home, Benny saw a cloud shaped like a castle. "Wow!" Benny said, "I wonder what’s inside?"

Suddenly, Benny’s basket started to glow! Inside, Benny found a shiny new pair of bunny shoes that could make him hop even faster. Outside, he discovered a basket full of delicious carrots and a basket of soft grass to play on.

Benny had the best day ever, dancing, hopping, and exploring. He shared his carrots with his friends and played hide-and-seek with his favorite squirrel, Sammy. At night, Benny curled up under the stars, feeling happy and safe.

"Today was the best adventure ever!" Benny whispered to the moon. And with that, Benny fell asleep, dreaming of more magical meadow adventures.
