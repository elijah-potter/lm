Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Violet.  Violet was the sweetest girl in her school and everyone loved talking to her.  Violet's favorite thing in the whole world was unicorns.  Her whole room was decorated with pictures of unicorns.  Every year on her birthday, her wish was to see a real unicorn.
Two days after Violet's seventh birthday, she was playing outside in her yard with her dog Bandit.  Suddenly, overhead she saw a flash of white fly into the trees.  Bandit began to bark and chase after it. Violet quickly followed Bandit into the woods when she suddenly saw a flying unicorn!  
The unicorn had a beautiful sparkling horn, a bright white mane, and large gossamer wings. Violet was speechless!  The unicorn turned to Violet and in the sweetest voice said, "Help me, sweet girl.  My wings are stuck in these blackberry bushes."
Violet quickly helped the unicorn untangle her wings.  The unicorn was so thankful for her help that she offered to let Violet ride on her back.  Violet climbed up and they quickly took off, high into the sky!  At first, Violet was scared and closed her eyes, but when she peeked out, the view was amazing!  It was the best day of her life!
Once the ride was over, the unicorn once again thanked Violet.  She told Violet, "Please don't let anyone know about me, or my life would be in danger!"  Violet promised and ran home with her secret ride forever in her heart.
