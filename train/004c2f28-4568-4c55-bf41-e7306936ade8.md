**Central Broadcast Hub - Grand News Square**
Concrete pillars supporting a transparent dome over a vast plaza. Drones circle overhead, relaying feeds from satellites and ground stations. Crowds of silhouetted figures, some with masks, others with glowing visor displays, huddle in clusters. Loudspeakers mounted on the dome blare static, intercut with distant amplified announcements. News vans with flashing emergency lights line the perimeter, their drivers pacing restlessly. A single digital clock in the plaza center displays real-time updates: **12:47:23**—**BREAKING: Earthquake alert in Tokyo**.
