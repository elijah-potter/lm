What does Reading Is Fundamental (RIF) do? Itís simple, orÖfundamental really. They help prepare and motivate kids to read by delivering free books and literacy resources to children and families who need them most. Americaís largest family literacy program, Reading Is Fundamental focuses primarily on reaching underserved children from birth to age 8, providing 4.5 million children with 16 million new, free books and literacy resources each year. Helping give kids a head start on the future. And they do all this with lots of help from their generous friends at the U.S. Department of Education, corporations, foundations and community organizations and thousands of kindhearted individuals like you.
