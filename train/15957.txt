This kit includes everything students need to create dynamic and fun paper circuits. The kit features 32 components including 10 magnetic connection cables to hook up your paper circuits to programmable platforms (e.g.
Arduino) With the Ultimate Kit, students can make complex and robust circuits.
Circuit Scribe allows for the easy construction of circuits and is based on a rollerball pen that writes with conductive silver ink.
It makes creating circuits as easy as doodling.
No more breadboards! Breadboards add a level of abstraction and annoyance to circuit building. With Circuit Scribe you can draw exactly what you want, no wires or breadboard required.
