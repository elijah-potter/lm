Title: The Saxophone Sensation in Springfield

Plot Summary: In a strange twist of fate, Lisa Simpson wins a prestigious saxophone contest. This catches the attention of one of her idols, Kenny G, who is the celebrity guest star of this episode. Lisa’s fame causes tension in the Simpson family, and they fall apart, going their separate ways. At the end of the episode, the Simpson family learns how important being genuine is and not to let fame and riches take precedence over what matters most—family.
