**The Clockwork Cathedral of Chronos**
A labyrinthine structure of brass and mirrored glass, where time flows through **gears of perpetual motion** embedded in the floor. Each hour, the cathedral’s **central pendulum** casts a shadow on the **inverted sundial**, marking a cycle that reverses at midnight. The air hums with the ticking of **synchronized chimes** that never repeat the same sequence twice. Visitors must follow the **whispering pathways** where echoes of past moments linger, and clocks on the walls **run at different speeds** depending on their location within the cathedral’s fractal design. The **Grand Tick**—a massive, obsidian hourglass—never empties; instead, it refills in real time, symbolizing an endless loop where the past and future bleed into the present. The cathedral’s **clockwork angels** adjust the system manually, ensuring harmony between the **synced and deranged clocks**. The last recorded event: a traveler who spent an hour in the **echo chamber** returned 3 days older.
