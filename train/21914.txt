The toddlers have been helping to look after our new plants in the garden. They have been watering them every day, filling and emptying the watering cans all by themselves. This has encouraged the children to take turns with their friends and helped develop their handling skills. We have learnt why its important to water the plants, and that we have to look after them if we want them to grow. Thank you for your help toddlers, our new garden and plants look brilliant!
