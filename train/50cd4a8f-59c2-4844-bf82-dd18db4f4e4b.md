Floors whisper under bare feet. Shadows stretch, dance along walls. Lamps flicker, casting jagged light that clings to corners. Mirrors reflect, blur, and distort faces—yours, others’. Books open, pages turn; ink fades on yellowed pages. Clock ticks, seconds crawl. Coffee steams, steam curls into the air. Doors creak, sighs escape, close with a thud. Paint cracks, peels in time-honored patterns. Wind hums through cracks in the blinds. Smoke rises, swirls into a lazy spiral. Keys jingle in pockets. Voices murmur, fade into static. Ceiling groans, sagging slightly. Fireflies (or stars?) flicker, dim, disappear. Silence settles, heavy with half-finished thoughts.
