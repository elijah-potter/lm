**BREAKING: City Council Approves New Waterfront Development Project in Port Haven**
The city of Port Haven announced today that the Port Haven Waterfront Development District Board has unanimously approved a 12-year plan to transform the aging docks into a mixed-use commercial and residential hub. The project, valued at $287 million, includes a 450-unit apartment complex, a floating market, and a new ferry terminal. The district will also implement a phased construction schedule with an initial phase beginning next spring. Environmental reviews have been completed, and the city has secured partnerships with local businesses and artists to create cultural and economic opportunities. Mayor Elias Carter emphasized the project’s potential to revitalize the downtown area and attract new residents and businesses, adding, *"This is a game-changer for Port Haven’s long-term growth."*

**Residents Express Mixed Reactions**
While supporters celebrate the promise of new jobs and community spaces, critics warn of potential displacement and increased traffic. A coalition of local advocacy groups, including the Port Haven Tenants Union, has filed a formal complaint with the city planning commission, demanding a social impact assessment and affordable housing quotas. Protests are planned for next Tuesday at the city hall, with organizers calling for public hearings on zoning adjustments.

**Economic Impact Expected**
The development is expected to generate $1.2 billion in cumulative economic activity over the next decade, with an initial boost from construction jobs. The city anticipates a net gain of 300 permanent positions by the project’s completion. Local businesses along the waterfront are already preparing for increased foot traffic, though some smaller vendors have expressed concerns about competition from larger retailers.

**Upcoming Milestones**
- **March 2025:** Groundbreaking ceremony for Phase 1 (Apartment Construction)
- **September 2025:** Temporary market and ferry terminal operational
- **2027:** Full completion of residential units and cultural spaces
The city remains open to public feedback through an online portal and community forums scheduled for next month. For updates, follow the Port Haven Development Corporation on social media.
