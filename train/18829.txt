Expert's Advice English Guided Preparation Class 12, Term I
The expert's advice for term I is based on CBSE's curriculum and NEP 2020's essence. It is based on the concept of reinforcement of text given in the beginning of each chapter which will enable the students to attempt the objective type questions given in the latter part of each chapter. Students are expected to pay their utmost attention to it before attempting the objective type questions. The Latter part of the book includes: -MCQs -Assertion/Reason based Questions -Case Study/ Passage based Questions -Assess yourself Tests for evaluation and reinforcement of concepts. -OMR sheet is givenat the end to give the basic and principal idea for term 1 Pattern. An additional feature included is the Smart Learner India app to make available all required changes in coming future.
