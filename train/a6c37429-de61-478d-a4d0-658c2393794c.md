A floating academy of forbidden knowledge, suspended above a labyrinth of mirrored corridors where every wall whispers in a language of shifting signs. Time here bends not in hours but in fragmented moments—each decision rewires the rules of existence itself. The air hums with static from a thousand unanswered questions, and the only constant is the unspoken pact: curiosity is the currency, and the price is the erasure of who you were before entering. The entrance is a door without a lock, only a flickering light that dissolves into the crowd of those who dared to step inside, all of them now searching for the one rule that outlasts the rest.
