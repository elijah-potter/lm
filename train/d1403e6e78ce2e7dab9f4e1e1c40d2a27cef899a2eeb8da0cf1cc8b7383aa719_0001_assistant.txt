The school lunch bell rings out so clear, 
It's time to face my greatest fear,
The mystery meat, the soggy bread,
I'd rather go hungry instead. 

The mac and cheese is so bright and bold,
But we know its always at least a week old,
The smell of it will make you gag,
The noodles crinkle like a paper bag. 

The pizza's dry, the sauce so thick,
It's hard to chew; it makes me sick, 
The toppings are a mystery,
It should go down in history.

The veggies are the worst of all,
Soggy carrots and peas so small,
They're gray and mushy, with a horrible taste,
It's like trying to eat a bowl of paste.

So, I'll bring my lunch from home each day, 
And keep the school food far away,
I'll pack a sandwich, some fruit, and cheese,
And eat with joy, just like I please.
