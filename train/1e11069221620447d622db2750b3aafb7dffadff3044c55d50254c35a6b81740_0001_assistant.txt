Ingredients:
-1 pound ground beef
-1 can cream of mushroom soup
-1/2 cup bread crumbs
Instructions:
-Preheat oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit.
-In a large bowl, combine ground beef, cream of mushroom soup, and bread crumbs. Mix thoroughly.
-Form mixture into 5 patties.
-Place patties in a greased baking dish.
Bake for 30 minutes
