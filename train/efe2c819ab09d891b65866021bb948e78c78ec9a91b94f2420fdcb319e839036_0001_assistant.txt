I wasn't a lonely person. In fact, I was quite content with my life. I got to sit with my books in peace and could write my stories without others getting in the way. However, seeing my classmates running around and enjoying their youth--it sometimes made me wonder if I was wasting the opportunities that youth afforded us all. 

That's when Mao-san turned up. She was a transfer student. She transferred in the previous month and was instantly popular. Of course, transferring in halfway through our first year of high school gave her that mysterious air that made people want to talk to her. On top of that, she was pretty, athletic, and had good grades. The guys loved her, and the girls loved her even more. That's why I was so stunned when she approached me during lunch one day.

She stopped in front of my desk and asked, "Do you mind if we eat lunch together?"

She had a worried smile on her face that gave me pause for a moment. I didn't particularly want to eat with her, but I didn't feel like it was appropriate to turn her down with everyone staring at us. "S-Sure. That's fine."

She pulled up a chair and opened her handmade lunch while I ate my convenience store bread. I continued reading my book while she ate at my desk in silence. Somehow, I didn't feel forced into talking. In fact, I felt as though she didn't want to speak. A few glances at her showed that she was perfectly content with sitting there in tranquil silence, despite others whispering about her and me eating together. 

This is the start of the story of how I got a girlfriend.
