Arsenic is incredibly toxic to human health and must be closely monitored. Arsenic is more of a problem in certain geographic areas than others. Arsenic poisoning can be acute where a large amount of arsenic is consumed in a short space of time, or chronic where the build-up of small amounts of time becomes toxic.
The Palintest Arsenator enables accurate and safe field measurements of arsenic to protect rural communities from arsenic poisoning.
A simple visual test kit providing rapid arsenic test results, widely used to identify arsenic water contamination in rural communities.
