Explores the dim corners where shadows stretch.
Watches dust swirl lazily on the floor.
Listens to creaks from the aged floorboards.
Sipps of cold air escape from the cracks in the door.
Reflects light against the peeling wallpaper.
Scent of old books lingers, faded but unmistakable.
Fingers trace the worn edges of a half-read novel.
Light flicks on and off sporadically from the flickering bulb.
Silence hums, broken only by the distant hum of the fridge.
Tastes the faint metallic tang of forgotten pipes.
Feels the drafts where the window frames sag.
Catches glimpses of framed photos—blurred but familiar.
Makes out the scent of leather and aged paper.
Moves slowly, avoiding the carpet that’s worn thin.
Whispers the echo of a voice long gone.
