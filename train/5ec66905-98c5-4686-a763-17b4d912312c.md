Cliffs whisper against the wind’s sigh.
Salt and damp rustle in the tide’s slow retreat.
Oak whispers through the leaves, thick with moss.
Candle wax drips on porcelain—smoke curls like a lazy dragon.
Rusty hinges groan as the door creaks open.
Brick walls breathe in the damp, warm from the hearth’s embers.
Glowworms hum in the dark, their light like distant stars.
Steam rises from the kettle, mingling with the scent of fresh bread.
The rain patters like distant drums, sharp and wet.
Coffee grounds simmer in the pot, dark and rich.
Old books smell of aged paper and forgotten stories.
The fire crackles, sending up a scent of pine and charred wood.
Coffee mugs clink in the morning light, warm and sweet.
Iron rustles in the sink, mixed with the scent of soap and water.
A distant train whistle echoes, cutting through the quiet like a blade.
