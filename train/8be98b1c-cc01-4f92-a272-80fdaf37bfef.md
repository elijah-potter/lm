The old clock in the corner of the library chimed twelve times as Liam sat hunched over his desk, the same words echoing through his head like a broken record.

*"Don’t you remember what you promised?"*

He rubbed his temples. The memory was sharp—his sister’s voice, the way her eyes had widened when he’d sworn he’d keep her secret safe. But time had blurred the edges, and now the past felt like a ghost story, something he was supposed to believe but couldn’t fully grasp.

Outside, rain tapped against the windows, a steady, relentless rhythm, just like the words his mind kept repeating.
