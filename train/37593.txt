The human brain understands color as it subconsciously relates to certain feelings and emotions. Many times, our brain will color what we experience by the color that is directly related to your energetic vibration, or to the emotion of the mood you are in. Each color evokes different energy. Some colors influence anxiety, pulse, blood flow, and arousal, as others influence calming and passivity.
Understanding color and how it informs the way you feel can help you turn it into a tool in your life to create positive change and emotional balance.
Each color has a direct relationship with each energy and emotional center of our body. These energy centers are also known as Chakras. Each chakra is connected to certain parts of the body, as well as with our mind and spiritual development. Chakras can be activated and balanced to create a life of harmony, health, and spiritual awakening. As a color is observed, it is recognized and processed by the brain and then associated with its corresponding emotion and chakra. Understanding the relationship between color, emotions, and the body, can help a person learn how to restore emotional and psychological balance.
