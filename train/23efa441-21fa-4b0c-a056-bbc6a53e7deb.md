A sudden burst of laughter from the kitchen spills into the living room, startling a family of four mid-dinner. The mother, mid-bite into a mashed potato, drops her fork and gasps, while the children—one clutching their chests, the other pointing frantically at the open fridge door. A shadowy figure in a stained apron leans against the counter, grinning like a ghost, their voice muffled: *"Oh, I forgot to tell you—this is *not* your usual soup."* The dog’s tail thumps against the floor as the doorbell rings, the sound cutting off the chaos like a switch.
