Backward Day, much like opposite day, was probably invented by children wanting to have a day to talk and walk backwards all day. Don’t worry about being different on January 31st. This is a day to do everything the wrong way around.
Celebrate Backwards Day with these fun and silly ideas.
Wear your clothes backwards
Serve meals on upside-down plates
Sleep on your bed backwards
Turn the chairs backward
Turn books and other shelved items backwards
Try talking in backwards sentences (sentences backwards in talking try)
You get the idea. Have fun with it. Be creative and share this fun day with the fun people in your life.
