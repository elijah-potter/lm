Once there was a little bunny named Pip who loved to hop around the meadow. One sunny morning, Pip saw a shiny red berry near a tiny stream. It was so big and bright that Pip couldn't resist picking it up. As Pip munched on the berry, something magical happened! The berry turned into a tiny, glowing cloud.

Pip was very curious. "What if I can fly?" Pip thought. So, Pip took a big hop and lifted his paws to the sky. The cloud wrapped around his paws, and suddenly, Pip felt like he was soaring! He zoomed over the trees, past the flowers, and even jumped over a sleepy old owl.

Down below, the other animals in the meadow were watching. A sleepy squirrel named Sammy said, "Wow, Pip! You’re flying! How did you do that?" Pip smiled. "I just ate a magical berry!" Sammy gasped and started to giggle.

Pip landed softly near the berry tree and decided to share his secret. "I hope one day, you’ll find your own magical berry too!" Pip said, and the animals nodded, excited.

From that day on, Pip was the happiest bunny because he knew he could always dream big and take flight! And the meadow became even brighter with his joy.
