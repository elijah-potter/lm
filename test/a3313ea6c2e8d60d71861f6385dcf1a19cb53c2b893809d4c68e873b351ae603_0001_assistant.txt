When reading your label, seeing both "added sugars" and "total sugars" might seem redundant. "Added sugars" are sugars that are literally added to foods and drinks during processing or packaged as sweeteners, such as table sugar, syrups, honey, or concentrated fruit or vegetable juices. Naturally occurring sugars, such as those found in milk, fruits, and vegetables, are not considered added sugars so they are not listed here. The natural sugars are instead reflected in "total sugars." In order to calculate the number of natural sugars, subtract the added sugars from the total sugars.
