Joseph walked into the gym with what felt like a mixture of anxiety and excitement. It was January 3rd, and this was his attempt at his New Year's resolution. He had felt out of shape most of his life, and that had always been something that bothered him. Joseph was 19 years old, about 6 foot 1, and weighed around 220 pounds. He played a little bit of football in high school during his sophomore and junior years, but beyond that, he never really was one to be too active physically. He typically preferred to spend his time either binging tv shows or playing video games for hours with his friends as a form of entertainment. His New Year resolution was to finally get active in the gym, and this was him going at it for the first time. He had done some research on how to proceed and felt like he had a good grasp on where to begin. He walked in committed to starting the Push-Pull-Leg split for his workout. Today would be the Push portion of that split, which meant that he was primarily going to be working on his biceps and back today. As he entered the gym, signed in and walked to the cable fly machine. He already could tell that this would be the start of something life-changing.
