Absolutely; here is a list of 3 beach locations with amusement parks: 

1. Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk - This amusement park stretches a mile-long across the Santa Cruz beach. You can find a variety of rides, games, and events like Mini Golf, Laser Tag, Bowling, Arcades, and Live Music.
2. Balboa Fun Zone - This mini amusement park is located in Newport Beach, it has over 8,000 square feet of event space and has a variety of carnival games and rides like bumper carts or roller coasters.
3. Belmont Park - This beachfront park is located in San Diego, you can find gift shops, vintage games, and many attractions like Laser Tag, Mini Golf, Rock Climbing, Bowling, Roller Coasters, and much more.
