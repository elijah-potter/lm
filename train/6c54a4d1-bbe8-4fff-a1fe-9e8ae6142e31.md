Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big tree in the backyard, there lived a little bunny named Pip. Pip had fluffy pink ears and a tail that wiggled when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Pip saw a shiny red apple near the fence. "Yum!" he thought. He picked it up and nibbled a tiny bite. "Mmm, sweet!" he giggled.

But then, Pip noticed something strange—the apple was growing bigger and bigger! He watched as it stretched high up in the air. Pip didn’t know what to do, so he hopped up to see if he could reach it.

Just then, a tiny bird named Twinkle landed beside him. "Want to share my berry?" she chirped. Pip took the berry and tried to reach the apple again. "Hmm, maybe I can climb the tree!" he said.

He scurried up the branches, careful not to fall. With a little help from Twinkle, who flew to the highest branch, Pip finally reached the apple! He took a big bite and smiled. "Yum, and it’s even sweeter now!"

From that day on, Pip knew that sharing was fun, and he often invited Twinkle to play in his treehouse. And they both loved the sweet, special apple that grew extra big when they were kind to each other.

The end!
