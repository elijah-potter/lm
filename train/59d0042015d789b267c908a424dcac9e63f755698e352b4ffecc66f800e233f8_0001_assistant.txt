“You’re going nowhere!” shouted a stranger, looking down at Bruce’s flat moped tire. “Thanks for pointing that out, sir,” Bruce addressed the stranger. “Not everyone is meant to make a difference. But for me, the choice to lead an ordinary life is no longer an option,” the stranger replied.

An odd way of speaking, Bruce thought, considering the stranger maybe thought too highly of himself for simply telling someone they should change their tire. Bruce unpacked his tools and knelt down to begin the tire change when he noticed the man had not left. Again, the stranger spoke: “I’ve got you for three minutes.” Then, once again, “The story of my life is not for the faint of heart. If somebody said it was a happy little tale, if somebody told you I was just your average ordinary guy, not a care in the world, somebody lied.”

“What is with this guy? Surely it is going to take longer than three minutes for me to finish this.” Bruce’s inner thoughts pondered as he continued working on the tire situation. Nonsense continued to pour from the stranger’s mouth until the job was finished. Then, Bruce was greeted with a camera in his face.

“If we can get a picture of Julia Roberts in a thong, we can certainly get a picture of this weirdo,” mumbled the stranger while aiming his lens at Bruce. Bruce assumed an aggressive position, then contextualizing using the full size of the stranger’s massive body against the smallness of his own frame, retreated back to a neutral sitting position atop his moped before speeding away.

With distance, Bruce could hear the final remarks from the stranger fade: “I wouldn’t want to fight me, neither!”
