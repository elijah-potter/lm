Personal finance is an important subject that too few people concern themselves with. Many think that it's too complicated to bother with. This could not be further from the truth.
When it comes to learning about finance, it's possible to learn on your own. There are many free or cheap references that can help you achieve your goals. There are also television channels like Fox Business and CNBC that can help with the educational process if you pay attention to earnings calls and interviews.
If you're looking to learn about finance, reading a few books on the topic by geniuses like Benjamin Graham and Warren Buffett can provide insight that can be quite beneficial. Additionally, it helps to learn how to read financial statements so that you can know which companies are worth your investment dollars. Growing revenue and net income on a year-over-year basis are both important barometers that can indicate the relative health of a business.
Learning a bit about debt and compounding interest are also important concepts that the self-taught finance expert will want to figure out. If you're in debt, compound interest can really draw wealth away from you, while if you're earning the compound interest, it can go a long way toward building your wealth. By reading and watching resources that discuss the proper topics, you can become an expert in finance in relatively short order.
