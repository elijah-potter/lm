Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big red door, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy gray fur and big round ears that flopped when he ran.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to explore. He saw a bright yellow flower that wobbled in the wind. "That’s the prettiest flower I’ve ever seen!" Benny giggled.

Suddenly, a tiny voice whispered, "Help me, Benny! My flower is falling over!" Benny looked down and saw a little blue butterfly with fluttery wings. Its flower was bending too much in the wind.

Benny knew just what to do. He hopped over and gently placed his tiny paw on the flower to steady it. "You’re doing great!" Benny cheered. The butterfly gave a tiny thank you with a flick of its wings.

After that, Benny and the butterfly became the best of friends. They played hide-and-seek in the tall grass, shared their favorite berries, and watched the sun set together. Benny learned that even the smallest friends can make the biggest differences.

One evening, as Benny curled up in his burrow, he smiled. The world was full of surprises, and so was he. He was the happiest bunny in the whole meadow.
