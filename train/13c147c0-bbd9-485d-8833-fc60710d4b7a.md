A child’s voice, barely audible over the clatter of unseen laborers, whispered about the "black stone" buried beneath the ruins. The scent of damp earth and something metallic—like old blood and copper—lingered in the air, thick and cloying. A flickering torch on a cracked wooden table beside a half-eaten meal of dried meat and blackened bread, its flame dancing erratically. The ground beneath was uneven, a maze of roots and half-buried tools. Shadows stretched long and unnatural, moving like living things. The wind carried the scent of sulfur, and a distant, guttural chant rose from the depths, vibrating through the bones of the forgotten place.
