A **single-point-of-failure** (SPoF) strategy where one critical decision or data point dictates all subsequent actions, such as:

- **Military/Logistics**: A single encrypted message in a coded cipher controlling supply routes.
- **Finance**: A single ledger entry triggering a chain reaction of trades or payouts.
- **Technology**: A single misconfigured API key enabling unauthorized access to a system.
- **Human Behavior**: A misplaced "Yes" or "No" in a scripted interview deciding eligibility for a deal.

The failure or success of the plan hinges entirely on the accuracy of that detail.
