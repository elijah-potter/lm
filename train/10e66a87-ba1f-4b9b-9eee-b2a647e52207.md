Truth revealed in quiet moments, like a candle’s flicker cutting through the dark: a young girl, hands trembling as she hands her father a letter addressed to a stranger. The ink is fresh, the words precise—*"You were wrong."* His face pales, his grip tightens on the envelope; the air hums with tension. The letter burns in his chest, but she watches him, waiting. The moment fractures: fear, defiance, or perhaps mercy—it’s written in the way he glances at the paper, then at her, before the words dissolve into the space between them. The truth, like a wound, stings, but it also heals.
