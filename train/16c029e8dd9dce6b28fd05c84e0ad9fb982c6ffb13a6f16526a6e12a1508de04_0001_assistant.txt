Here are 3 books to read that are from the 2000s, well-regarded, and say something about American culture or society. Each suggestion includes an explanation of why it's recommended as well as a brief description of the book:

1. The Amazing Adventures of Kavalier & Clay by Michael Chabon (2000). This 2001 Pulitzer Prize-winning book and bestseller uses the simultaneously tragic and comedic adventures of two boy geniuses as a backdrop to describe what happened to America in the middle of the 20th century. This suggestion meets your criteria as it is from the 2000s, critically and commercially popular, and covers topics related to American culture and society. 
2. The Corrections by Jonathan Franzen (2001). This 2001 National Book Award winner about an elderly midwestern couple and their three adult children was one of the bestselling novels of its decade. The book is appropriate because it was published in the 2000s, achieved both commercial and critical success, and deals with themes related to American culture and society.    
3. American Gods by Neil Gaiman (2001). This 2002 Hugo and Nebula award-winning novel blends various genres including Americana, fantasy, and both ancient and modern mythology as it follows the mysterious Shadow on his travels throughout the United States. This novel satisfies all of your specifications as it is from the 2000s, is well-regarded, and covers topics related to American culture and society.
