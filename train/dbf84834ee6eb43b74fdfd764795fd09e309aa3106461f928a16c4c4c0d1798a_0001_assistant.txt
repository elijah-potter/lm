Josie Tegue has decided to take a much-needed vacation by herself. She has had a couple of hard years dealing with a nasty divorce and her job running her ragged. While searching TravelMe, a travel planning app, she happens upon an old but well-kept resort named the Tropical Breeze Resort and Spa. It's perfect. It's just what she's looking for. Josie books a two-week trip.

Josie arrives at the resort and everything seems relaxed and normal. Everyone she sees who works there is smiling. Every time she sees an employee, they have an uncomfortably large grin spread across their face. It seems almost predatory.

After a couple of days, things start to seem weird and Josie starts to think that she hasn't seen any of the resort employees make any other facial expression or express anything but pleasant emotions. The resort is perfect and the employees behave perfectly to the point of being uncanny. Something seems off. What is the mystery behind this resort where the people seem to be robots?
