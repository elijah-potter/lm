Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the sunny meadow. Benny had a big, fluffy tail and a soft pink nose. One morning, Benny woke up and saw a tiny, shiny acorn near his burrow.

"Oh, how pretty!" Benny said, picking it up. Benny decided to take the acorn home to his mama and papa. On the way, Benny met a little bird named Bertie who had a nest full of blue eggs. "Can I help you carry something?" Bertie asked.

"Sure!" Benny said. Together, they carried the acorn to Bertie's nest. Bertie placed it carefully among the eggs. "Thank you, Benny!" Bertie chirped.

Later, Benny’s mama said, "Benny, you’re very kind! Let’s plant the acorn so it grows big and strong." Benny dug a tiny hole and planted it. Benny watered it gently with a little drop of water from his paw.

As the days passed, the acorn grew into a big, beautiful tree. The meadow became even greener, and Benny’s favorite spot was where the tree grew tall and proud. The birds sang happy songs, and the flowers bloomed around the tree. Benny knew he had made a friend in Bertie and helped nature grow.

Now, Benny would often hop under the tree to enjoy the shade and the sweet, fresh fruits that grew there. Every morning, he’d find the biggest, juiciest acorn of all, and he’d share it with Bertie and his siblings. Benny learned that being kind and helping others made the meadow even happier and more beautiful.
