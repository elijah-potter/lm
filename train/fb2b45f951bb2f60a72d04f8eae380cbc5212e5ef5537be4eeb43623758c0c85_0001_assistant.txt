1. Begin by applying your typical primer and foundation. 
2. Apply red eyeshadow across your eyelids and blend outward. 
3. Apply a sheer lip gloss. 
4. Brush bright red powder or cream makeup across your cheeks and nose, blending outward and up from your cheekbones. 
5. Using white theatrical makeup and a brush, apply dots across the red areas. 
6. Apply mascara.
