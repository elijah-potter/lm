The Great Dionysia was an Ancient Greek festival held in March (Spring) in honour of the God Dionysus, during the fifth century BC. It was held in Athens, Greece, and a procession would bring a statue to the Theatre of Dionysus on the South slope of the Acropolis. more than sixteen thousand people could fit into the amphitheatre to watch the plays of Aeschylus, Sophocles and Euripides and others.
