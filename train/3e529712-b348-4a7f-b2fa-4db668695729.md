A **solid wood or metal door** with a **double-hung or solid core construction**, typically featuring a **stamped metal frame**, glass panels (if present) with frosted or clear options, and **interior trim** (e.g., baseboard or door casing).

Separates:
- **Living space (e.g., bedroom, office)** from **hallway/entryway**
- **Private areas (e.g., bedroom, bathroom)** from **shared spaces (e.g., living room, kitchen)**
- **Interior from exterior** (weatherproofing, security barriers)
- **Residential/commercial units** in apartment buildings from adjacent units.
