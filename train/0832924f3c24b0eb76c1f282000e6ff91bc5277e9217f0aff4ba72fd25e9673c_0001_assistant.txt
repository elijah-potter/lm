"Hey, Dean. Where'd you put that scanner at?" Gale asked, searching the counter for the barcode scanner.

"It's on the counter. Like, right in front of you. What do you mean?" Dean called from the back.

Of course, the scanner wasn't right in front of him. Gale shouted that it wasn't there and Dean came to check. "Huh? That's strange. I know I left it right there."

The two searched the store and found it inside the newspaper stand. Gale looked at Dean with a confused expression. "Don't look at me. I didn't put it there. You even watched me set the scanner on the counter."

Gale did recall that, and he frowned while pulling the scanner out. "Well, whatever. We've got it now. Just need to plug it in."

This was the first of many strange things to happen. While Dean helped a customer, they sometimes vanished without a trace. He wasn't losing his mind because Gale saw it, too. Items began misplacing themselves, and not only at work. It occasionally happened at home as well. One evening, when Dean was walking Arisa home, they saw someone standing underneath a street lamp. They thought nothing of it as they passed by the stranger. But then, the person called out to them. "Excuse me," the person said.

Arisa and Dean turned around, Dean saying, "Yes? Need some--"

Arisa grabbed Dean's arm, using it as an excuse to cling to him. "Hey, get off me. You heard that person, too, right?"

Arisa sighed but allowed herself to be pushed away. "Yeah, I did. That's weird, though. I swear I just passed someone."

This was only the beginning of the strange things that grew worse as Dean avoided his problems.
