Once upon a time, there was a young woman named Alice who had the power to time-travel. She could go to any time period she wanted, and she could bring back anything she wanted from the past. Alice used her power to travel all over the world and throughout history. She saw the pyramids being built, the Roman Empire at its height, and the signing of the Declaration of Independence. She met famous people like Cleopatra, Julius Caesar, and George Washington.

Alice also used her power to help people. She saved people from burning buildings, stopped criminals from committing crimes, and even prevented wars from happening. One day, Alice was traveling through time when she came across a group of people who were being persecuted for their beliefs. Alice decided to help these people, and she used her power to transport them to a safe place.

Alice learned a lot from her adventures in time. She learned that the past is not always as it seems and that the future is not set in stone. She also learned that it is important to help others, and that even one person can make a difference. Alice continued to travel through time for many years, and she had many more adventures. She eventually settled down and started a family, but she never forgot her time-traveling days. She passed on her power to her children, and they continued to use it to help others.
