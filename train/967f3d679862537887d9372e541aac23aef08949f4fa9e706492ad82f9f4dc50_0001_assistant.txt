Here are some things you should purchase to build a home office:

1. Desk - A good desk that provides enough workspace is vital. There are several styles of desks with different looks and functionalities, so choose one that suits your taste and needs.
2. Chair - Your chair should provide good back support and fit your height and desk height.
3. Computer - Choose a computer within your budget that has enough specifications to meet your work requirements. Consider whether a laptop or a PC is more suitable for your needs.
4. Printer and Scanner - A printer is a tool that will come in handy in any office
5. Office Supplies - Pens, a stapler, a pencil sharpener, or any other supplies should be included.
6. Calendar - A calendar on the wall can improve organization and keep you on track.
7. Decoration - Add some decorative items to make your home office more comfortable.
