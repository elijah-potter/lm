The **Whispering Pines**—an ancient, mist-shrouded forest where trees grow in perfect, overlapping spirals. The wind carries murmurs of lost conversations, half-heard echoes of laughter and sighs from the past. Locals claim the wind picks up voices from the dead, whispering secrets in languages no living person remembers. Only those who listen closely enough hear the faint, rhythmic breathing of unseen spirits. The air hums with unseen voices, sometimes answering in reply.

A place where silence is the loudest.
