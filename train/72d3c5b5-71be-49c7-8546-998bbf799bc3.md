## Product Requirements Document: "Synapse" - Contextual Knowledge Aggregation

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers spend an excessive amount of time searching for relevant information scattered across disparate sources, hindering productivity and impeding creative flow.

**Vision:** Synapse will be the indispensable mobile companion that proactively surfaces the right information at the right time, empowering knowledge workers to focus on creation and innovation.

---

### User Personas

**1. Dr. Eleanor Vance – Research Scientist**

*   **Goal:** To quickly access and synthesize research papers, datasets, and relevant notes to advance her ongoing research projects.
*   **Pain Points:** Overwhelmed by the volume of publications; difficulty connecting seemingly disparate findings; time wasted searching through email archives and online databases; struggles to recall specific details from previously reviewed material.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** High. Comfortable with complex software and data management tools.

**2. Mr. David Chen – Marketing Manager**

*   **Goal:** To stay informed about industry trends, competitor activities, and customer feedback to inform strategic marketing decisions.
*   **Pain Points:** Difficulty filtering through noise in social media and news feeds; struggles to connect fragmented data points from various analytics platforms; feels reactive rather than proactive in responding to market changes; lacks a centralized view of key information.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** Moderate. Prefers intuitive interfaces and streamlined workflows.

**3. Ms. Anya Sharma – Freelance Writer**

*   **Goal:** To efficiently gather background information, verify facts, and organize sources for her writing assignments.
*   **Pain Points:** Time spent manually searching for information across multiple websites and documents; difficulty keeping track of sources and citations; concerns about plagiarism and accuracy; needs a portable and accessible research tool.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** Basic. Values simplicity and ease of use.

---

### Requirements

**1. Contextual Information Retrieval:** The app must proactively surface relevant information based on the user’s current task, location, calendar events, and recently accessed files.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** The app accurately predicts relevant information with 80% precision based on simulated user workflows. Users can manually adjust the app’s understanding of their context.
    *   **Success Metrics:** Average time spent searching for information decreases by 30% for pilot users. User satisfaction with information relevance scores 4.5 out of 5 in post-usage surveys.

**2. Knowledge Graph Visualization:** The app must visually represent relationships between pieces of information, allowing users to explore connections and identify patterns.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can easily navigate the knowledge graph and understand the relationships between nodes. The graph dynamically updates as new information is added.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 60% of users actively utilize the knowledge graph visualization within the first week of use. Qualitative feedback indicates the visualization enhances understanding and sparks new insights.

**3. Source Aggregation & Management:** The app must integrate with common cloud storage services (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive), email clients, and web browsers to aggregate information from various sources.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** The app successfully connects to all specified integrations. Users can easily add and remove sources. Data synchronization is reliable and timely.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 95% of users successfully connect at least one external source. Data synchronization errors are less than 1% of total data volume.

**4. Note-Taking & Annotation:** The app must allow users to take notes, highlight text, and add annotations directly within the app, linking them to relevant information sources.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Notes and annotations are easily searchable and linked to their original sources. Users can organize notes using tags and folders.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 70% of users actively utilize the note-taking and annotation features. Average number of notes per user increases over time.

**5. Intelligent Summarization:** The app must provide concise summaries of long-form content, highlighting key takeaways and reducing information overload.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Summaries accurately reflect the content of the original source. Users can adjust the level of detail in the summaries.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 50% of users utilize the summarization feature. Qualitative feedback indicates summaries save time and improve comprehension.



---

### Technical Considerations

*   **Privacy & Security:** Data encryption both in transit and at rest is paramount. Compliance with relevant data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA) is mandatory.
*   **Scalability:** The architecture must be designed to handle a large volume of data and users.
*   **Cross-Platform Compatibility:** Initial launch will focus on iOS and Android.
*   **Integration APIs:** Robust and well-documented APIs are required for future integrations.
*   **Machine Learning Model Training:** A dedicated team or partnership will be needed for ongoing model refinement and accuracy improvements.



### Risks

*   **Data Accuracy:** Inaccurate contextual predictions could frustrate users and erode trust.
*   **Integration Challenges:** Compatibility issues with third-party services could limit functionality.
*   **User Adoption:** Users may be resistant to adopting a new tool for knowledge management.
*   **Privacy Concerns:** User apprehension regarding data privacy could hinder adoption.



### Non-Goals

*   Real-time collaboration features.
*   Direct content creation capabilities (e.g., writing tools).
*   Offline access to all content (limited offline caching will be considered for later iterations).
*   Support for desktop operating systems (focus remains mobile).
*   Automated content curation from social media platforms.
