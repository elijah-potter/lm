What if the last time you turned your back on the door, someone had sneaked in instead of a guest? The coffee machine spiked on its own, turning the kitchen into a labyrinth of spilled grounds and steam. A stranger’s hand closed over your wrist mid-stride, whispering, *"I’m sorry."* Outside, the sky was the wrong color—deep violet, like a bruise. You woke to find your reflection staring back, but the eyes were wrong, too, shifting between the ones you knew and the ones that had taken your place. The tape from your last conversation looped in your head: *"I’ll remember everything."* The memory was already gone.
