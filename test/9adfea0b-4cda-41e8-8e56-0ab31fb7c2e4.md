Once upon a time in a bright and sunny garden, there was a little red car named Benny. Benny loved to zoom around and explore every day. One morning, Benny’s wheels started to spin so fast that he felt like he was flying!

Benny saw a big, fluffy cloud and wanted to ride it. He hopped on top and zoomed up higher and higher. "Wow!" Benny thought, "I feel like I’m floating!"

Suddenly, Benny heard a tiny voice. "Hello, Benny! Can you help me?"

Benny looked down and saw a small, scared bunny named Bunny. Bunny’s ears were tucked in, and his eyes were wide. "I don’t know where to go," Bunny said. "I’m lost!"

Benny had a big idea. "Don’t worry, Bunny! I’ll help you find your way home." Benny turned his car around and drove right to Bunny’s house, which was near a big tree.

Benny and Bunny hugged and laughed together. "You’re the best friend I’ve ever had!" Bunny said.

From that day on, Benny and Bunny played together every day. They made up stories, shared snacks, and even had a picnic under the tree. Benny learned that kindness and friendship make the world a happier place.

And Benny? Well, he still loved zooming around, but he now knew that some of the best adventures were shared with friends.

The end!
