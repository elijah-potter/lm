I would be happy to help you plan a meal using round steak. Here are three dishes you may want to consider, and a little detail about each one.

1. Round Steak with Mushrooms - Add depth to your round steak with mushrooms, onions and onion soup mix, beef broth, and some basic seasonings.
2. Swiss Steak - Round steak that is cooked with hearty vegetables like carrots, green beans, potatoes, onions, and diced tomatoes.
3. Steak Bites - Bite-sized round steak is slow-cooked with onions, garlic, butter and beef broth to create a tender and simple steak dinner.
