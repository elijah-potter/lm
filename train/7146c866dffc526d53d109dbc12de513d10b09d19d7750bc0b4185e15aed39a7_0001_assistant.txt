
Some of us are very good at recognizing our strengths and weaknesses and some need the facts drawn out for them. Whichever category you fall in, it’s important to realize that being human means that we are not perfect in every area. It’s okay to be a bad cook but if this is something important to you, then take a cooking class and acquire those skills. 

One thing that can really hinder our progress in life is not admitting and dealing with our weaknesses. Are you a poor speller? Then get in the habit of playing word games that force you to spell correctly. Don’t go through your whole life saying, “I’m a terrible speller!” Instead, consider spelling a challenge that you must conquer and work at it until you’re the best speller you know. 

When we approach life this way, every challenge becomes a stepping stone to greatness. Your character and personality grow and evolve each time you master a new difficulty. This is the key to broadening your skills, experience, and expertise.  Within a few years, you’ll look up and realize that you have a number of new accomplishments to add to your personal resume. 

Life is what we make it. You gotta play the hand you’re dealt. These clichés exemplify our common struggle to overcome life’s challenges and achieve our destiny. Though it doesn’t happen overnight, with each new experience you move through, you’ll gain stronger character traits that will make the next hurdle even easier to overcome.

Well-known executive, Albert Gray, said, “Successful people are successful because they form the habits of doing those things that failures don’t like to do.”
