Weather and Dinosaurs: Take photos of dinosaurs during a heatwave and thunderstorm to study their behavior in weather to conduct InGen researching.
Island and start settingsEdit
The island is medium-sized but large enough to contain herds of several different herbivores and carnivores such as Stegosaurus and Velociraptor.
Take photos from each dinosaurs in the objectives during the heatwave and the thunderstorm.
Drive slowly and take pictures of each dinosaurs at a fair distances.
