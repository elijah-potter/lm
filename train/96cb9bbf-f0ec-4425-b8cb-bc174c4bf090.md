Abandoned plan: **"Project Midnight"**

**Objective:** Develop a quantum-resistant encryption algorithm for critical infrastructure (e.g., financial networks, government databases) by 2026.

**Reasons for Abandonment:**
1. **Resource Constraints** – Lack of funding (€12M) and specialized talent (5 PhD-level cryptographers) after 18 months.
2. **Technical Overreach** – Assumed quantum computing would hit 2026, but NIST’s post-quantum standardization delayed timelines by 3 years.
3. **Competitive Threat** – IBM and Google’s quantum advancements forced a pivot to hybrid cryptographic systems (AES-256 + NIST-standardized algorithms).
4. **Ethical Concerns** – Public backlash over the plan’s focus on surveillance-resistant encryption led to regulatory scrutiny.

**New Direction:** Focused on short-term resilience (e.g., transitioning legacy systems to NIST’s Kyber/Squeaky-Green) while exploring open-source alternatives.
