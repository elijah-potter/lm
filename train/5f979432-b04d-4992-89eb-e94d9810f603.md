Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, lived a curious bunny named Benny. Benny had soft brown fur and a big, round nose that twinkled when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to play. The grass was green and soft under his paws. "Today is going to be the best day ever!" Benny thought.

He met his friend, a bright blue butterfly named Butter. "Can you teach me to fly?" Benny asked. Butter fluttered happily. "Of course! But first, you need to learn how to flap your wings like this..." Butter showed Benny how to flap his little arms up and down. Benny tried—*whoosh!*—but his wings didn’t lift very high.

Benny tried again. *Whoosh, whoosh!* He flapped faster and harder. Suddenly, he felt a little breeze. "Whoa!" Benny gasped. He lifted off the ground—*plop!*—and landed gently in a big flower bed. Butter giggled. "You did it, Benny! You’re a flyer!"

Now Benny loved exploring. He hopped to the tallest daisy, the biggest clover, and even the shiniest leaf. He saw ladybugs dancing on the flowers and a tiny mouse playing hide-and-seek with a worm.

One afternoon, Benny spotted a shiny red apple near a tree. "I wonder what’s inside?" he wondered. He carefully picked it and took a bite. "Mmm! Sweet and juicy!" He shared the apple with Butter, who nibbled happily.

As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and orange, Benny and Butter said goodbye. "Tomorrow will be another adventure!" Benny promised.

They curled up in their cozy burrow, dreaming of tomorrow’s fun. And that’s how Benny the bunny learned to fly, ate delicious apples, and made new friends—one day at a time.
