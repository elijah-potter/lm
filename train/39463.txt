What is INSPIRE?
INvolving School Parents In REading, writing and mathematics.
Inspire Workshops are fun and exciting classes where you will learn how English and Mathematics work at Hollywood and how you can apply some of the ideas for games and activities at home. They are a fantastic opportunity for all parents and guardians to come into school and spend time with their child, learning together in a relaxed atmosphere.
Inspire Workshops are held for each year group across the academic year. The teachers plan fun, usually practical activities for children to work alongside their parent or guardian. The children always look forward to these events and it gives parents and guardians a chance to see the sort of activities their children do at school. They are a good social occasion and help teachers get to know the parents and guardians of the children they teach.
