The Easiest Flowers to Grow

Zinnias top my list. For all sorts of reasons. They love the sun. They can tolerate drought. They’re some of the easiest flowers to grow. You can grow them from seed. You can buy the plants at any garden center. They bloom like crazy. 

You can cut the blooms. You can bring them inside for little decorations. Hummingbirds and bees love them. They’re considered annuals. They don’t return year after year.

One year I picked up a packet from the garden center. I just threw them onto the ground. Guess what? They grew and thrived. I’m not kidding. They are some of the easiest flowers to grow. They are some of the prettiest flowers to grow. 

Just push them about 1/4 inch into the soil. You’ll see plants in no time. I picked up a new variety this year. It was the Giant Golden Yellow. I added two. It added a bright pop of color to the backyard.

Every year, these are some of my favorites. They’re bright and showy. Their continuous blooms give color all summer. 

Zinnias are inexpensive. Plants or a packet of seeds are just a few bucks.
