The jagged cliffs of **Verdant Cove** rise from a mist-shrouded fjord, their slate-gray faces cracked by time and wind. Below, the **Blackwater Rapids** roar through a canyon of twisted roots and submerged stone, where fish dart between the swirling currents. Above, the **Skyrend Spires** pierce the clouds—a jagged, skeletal formation where the wind howls like a warning. The air hums with static, the scent of damp earth and ozone clings to the damp skin of the lone observer, who steps carefully between the **Wailing Stones**, where the wind seems to whisper secrets no one can hear. The horizon bends unnaturally, where the sea meets the sky in a blur of silver and violet, and the horizon line fractures into fractals of light. Here, the world feels like it’s stretching, like the earth itself is holding its breath.
