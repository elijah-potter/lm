Eclipse of a courtyard: long, jagged shadows of twisted metal railings stretch diagonally across the pebbled floor, their tips converging near a cracked fountain’s empty basin. The sun is a thin blade of gold slicing between the trees, casting a serpentine line along the edge of a mosaic tile that reads *"Here"* in faded Arabic script. The shadows of two stone lions guard the entrance, their elongated forms doubling as silent sentinels. Dust motes drift in a breeze that carries the scent of aged plaster and damp earth, while the only warmth comes from the low, flickering glow of a single lantern, its flame trapped in a glass cage that refracts the shadows into shifting patterns. The air hums with the distant sound of a drip—perhaps from the fountain, perhaps from another place entirely. The shadows move as if alive, twisting around the edges of the lantern’s light like fingers reaching for the unknown.
