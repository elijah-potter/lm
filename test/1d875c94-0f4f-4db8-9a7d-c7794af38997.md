Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy pink ears and a tail that wiggled like a spring. Every morning, Benny would hop outside to see the sunrise paint the sky in colors Benny loved best—sunshine yellow and sparkling blue.

One sunny afternoon, Benny spotted a tiny acorn on the grass. It looked so round and shiny! Benny picked it up and carried it home, where Mama Bunny was busy baking cookies. "Mama, can I eat this?" Benny asked, holding the acorn. Mama Bunny smiled. "Not yet, little one. It’s not ripe." Benny sighed but tried to hide his disappointment.

Later, Benny played with his best friend, a little squirrel named Sammy. "I found something special," Benny whispered. Sammy’s eyes sparkled. "What is it?" Benny showed him the acorn. "It’s not food," Sammy said, scratching his tiny paws. "Maybe we can plant it!" Benny’s eyes lit up. "Really? Let’s try!"

They dug a tiny hole in the garden, carefully placed the acorn, and covered it with soft soil. Benny patted the dirt with his tiny paws. "Let’s wait and see what grows!" Sammy grinned. They both sat down to watch the sun set, their hearts full of excitement.

Days passed, and Benny waited every morning. One bright day, Benny woke up and hopped outside to see a tiny green shoot peeking through the soil. "Look!" Benny cheered. "It’s growing!" Sammy ran over, his eyes wide. "That’s a tree!" Benny grinned. "And we made it!"

Soon, the tree grew taller and taller, with leaves that shimmered in the sunlight. Benny and Sammy became the best friends in the world. Every time they played, they would run to the tree, whispering, "Remember when we planted this?" And the tree would grow even stronger, as if it knew how much Benny and Sammy loved it.

And that’s how Benny and Sammy turned a simple acorn into something magical—a tree that brought them endless joy and friendship. The end.
