Lila and her tiny bunny, Biscuit, loved exploring the big, cozy garden. One sunny morning, they spotted a shiny red apple near the old oak tree. "Let’s pick it!" said Lila, her eyes wide.

Biscuit hopped up and gently took the apple from the branch. "Watch this!" he said, and with a tiny nibble, he dropped it. Oops! The apple rolled down the hill and landed in a big, happy puddle.

Lila giggled. "We can play in the puddle!" She jumped in, splashing water everywhere. Biscuit wiggled his nose, his paws splashing too. "Yum!" they said together, as they tasted the cool, sweet water from the puddle.

Suddenly, a little bird chirped, "Help! My nest is under the water!" Lila and Biscuit raced to the tree. They dug with their tiny paws until they found the nest, covered in mud. With careful hands, they lifted the mud and let the water drain.

The bird fluffed its feathers and chirped, "Thank you!" Lila and Biscuit hugged their new friends. The garden sparkled, and they all played together, happy and full of joy.
