When writing a good joke, there are 3 key points you should consider:

1. Content - Gather ideas from the world around you and learn about current affairs. This will help you engage your audience and find common ground with them.

2. Pace - Timing is everything and a well-told joke should flow like a story and have well-thought-out pauses to build anticipation.

3. Changing Expectations - Your job as a joke writer is to make the audience look at familiar topics through a fresh perspective.
