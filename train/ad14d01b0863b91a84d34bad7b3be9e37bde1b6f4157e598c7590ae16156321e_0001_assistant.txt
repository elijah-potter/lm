Three chefs were working in the kitchen during this particular event, each one accomplished in their own right. The menu for the night consisted of a variety of foods from different styles of cuisine: French, Italian, Japanese, Chinese, and even American. They would be serving their meals to the new King of the United Kingdom.

Each dish was created and prepared by the three chefs at one time. The first dish to be made was a simple sushi dish. However, on this occasion, the following steps needed to be adhered to:

1. Cook the sushi rice in a wooden steaming basket;
2. Prepare the yellow-fin tuna - the tuna was sliced into tiny pieces and marinated in a mixture of soy sauce, sesame oil, and chili peppers overnight;
3. Vegetables needed to be sliced and diced;
4. Each sushi needed to be topped with the roe from the tuna.

Each ingredient used by the chefs needed to be sourced and farmed by them for several months before they could be used: the tuna needed to be raised by the chefs in their local saltwater pond; the soybeans, chili peppers and sesame seeds needed to be grown in the chefs' garden along with the cucumbers, carrots and wasabi; and the roe collected by hand from the tuna before being cut into pieces to be used in the sushi.

The sushi was carefully prepared and plated onto beautiful ceramic plates lined with gold design and served to the king, who enjoyed the meal so much that he decided to hire the chefs for future catering jobs.
