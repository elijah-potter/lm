A classified intelligence operation in Sector 9 requires a single, unguarded data node in the eastern district to access a black-market AI core. The node’s access code is embedded in a discarded USB drive found in a cyber café, where a minor misstep—an employee’s distracted checkout—reveals the device in plain sight. The plan hinges on exploiting this detail: a coordinated strike team, disguised as tech support, retrieves the drive under the guise of a routine system upgrade. The AI core, dormant for years, will reactivate upon extraction, offering a rare, untapped neural network model. Success depends entirely on the café’s transient staff’s willingness to overlook the team’s presence and the team’s ability to prevent a counter-strike before the node’s security protocols detect the intrusion. Any delay or failure in this critical sequence risks exposing the operation’s scope, triggering a full-scale investigation. The success metric: the core’s activation within 72 hours of extraction.
