In the historic district of Eldermere, officials announced today that the newly renovated **Grand Arboretum Park** reopened after six months of restoration. The park features revamped walking paths, a revitalized children’s play area, and a new **eco-friendly irrigation system** powered by rainwater collection. The city’s budget for the project was approximately $2.8 million, funded by a mix of municipal grants and private donations.

Residents praised the improvements, though some noted concerns about overcrowding during peak summer hours. Mayor Elias Voss emphasized that the park’s success would help combat urban heat islands by increasing green spaces. The next phase involves expanding a nearby community garden with low-maintenance native plants.

**Upcoming Events:**
- Free guided tours of the park’s botanical exhibits start tomorrow at 10 AM.
- A public art installation by local sculptor **Lena Hart** will debut next Friday, coinciding with the park’s 100th anniversary celebration.

Councilwoman Mira Chen suggested additional funding for maintenance to prevent future backlogs, though funding remains uncertain. The city council will discuss the matter in a special meeting next week.
