In a dimly lit apartment, the weight of silence pressed upon **Lena**, her fingers tracing the rim of her coffee cup. Across from her, **Mira**—her best friend for as long as she could remember—leaned forward, her eyes wide with unshed tears.

*"I’m pregnant,"* Mira whispered, voice cracking. *"And I don’t know how to tell you."*

Lena’s stomach twisted. The last time she’d seen Mira like this, it was after her father’s sudden illness, the one that left Mira’s mother exhausted and alone. She’d promised to be there, no matter what. But now, the truth hung between them like a knife.

*"You don’t have to tell me now,"* Lena said, though her heart ached. *"But you owe me this. The truth."*

Mira exhaled shakily, her hands trembling. *"You’re the only one who’s ever really seen me."*

Lena stood, her pulse roaring in her ears. She didn’t know what to say. But then she thought of the way Mira had laughed when Lena had failed at her first job, the way she’d stood by her when Lena’s mother passed. *"I’ll be here,"* she said, her voice steady despite the fear clawing at her throat. *"No matter what."*

Mira’s shoulders shook as she burst into tears, but Lena didn’t look away. The truth wasn’t easy, but it was *theirs*. And for the first time in months, Lena believed Mira would find her way through it.

*"I love you,"* she murmured, because sometimes, the bravest thing you could do was say it anyway.
