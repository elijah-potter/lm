**Scene 1: The Library – Dawn**
The air smelled of old paper and damp stone. Elias traced his fingers along the spine of *The Lost Chronicles*, its leather cracked like dry skin. The clock tower chimed eight, but the sun hadn’t risen yet. Outside, a city slept, its windows like hollow eyes.

He opened the book to a blank page—no ink, no words. A whisper in the margins: *"You are the key."* The page shimmered, and for a breath, the words *Elias* and *door* appeared before vanishing.

---

**Scene 2: The Street – Dusk**
Across the river, Mara sat on the rooftop of her father’s tailor shop, stitching a torn sleeve. The city’s neon glow pulsed like a heartbeat. Below, a child screamed for her mother—*another* family’s child, lost in the same chaos.

She paused. The same door from the book was carved into the church’s facade. A priest’s voice, muffled through the stained glass: *"The past is a door. Step inside."*

---
**Scene 3: The Mirror**
They stood in a hall lined with mirrors. Their reflections flickered: Elias in ragged clothes, Mara in a tattered coat. The mirror above them showed two doors—one led to a library, the other to a river.

*You are the key,* the reflection whispered.

---
**Scene 4: The Choice**
Elias reached for the door. Mara grabbed his wrist. The city’s clock struck midnight. The library door yawned open, but so did the river.

*Choose.* The book’s pages fluttered—*the past is a door. The future is a choice.*
