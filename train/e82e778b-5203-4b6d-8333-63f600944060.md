Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big tree outside, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white ears and a bright pink nose that always twinkled when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to say hello to his friends. He saw a bluebird singing in a tall oak tree, a squirrel chattering in the branches, and a friendly little mouse named Mimi scampering around the garden.

"Good morning, friends!" Benny called.

The bluebird flapped its wings and chirped, "Good morning, Benny! The garden is full of yummy berries today!"

Benny hopped over to pick some juicy strawberries. "Mmm, these are the best!" he said.

Mimi peeked out from under a bush. "I found a shiny acorn! Want to see?" she said, holding it up.

Benny gasped. "It’s so sparkly! Let’s hide it somewhere safe!" They buried it in the dirt near their favorite hiding spot.

Later, Benny played hide-and-seek with his friends. He hid behind a big rock and called, "I’m not here!" The squirrel cheered, "Gotcha!" and then Benny peeked out.

"Whoa, I almost got you!" Benny laughed, rubbing his tummy.

Just then, a gentle rain started. Benny ran home to dry off, and the rain made the garden sparkle. Mimi brought him a big, warm blanket, and Benny curled up under it, snuggling with his favorite stuffed bunny.

As the sun came out again, Benny knew it was time to go inside. "Goodbye, friends! See you tomorrow!" he said, waving.

Inside, Benny played with his toys and ate his favorite snack—a yummy apple slice. He went to bed, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures with Benny and his friends in the garden.
