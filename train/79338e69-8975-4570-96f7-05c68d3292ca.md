Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big garden, lived a little red fox named Finn. Finn loved exploring the garden, chasing butterflies, and collecting shiny acorns. One sunny morning, Finn heard a tiny voice.

"Help me!" cried a small, trembling voice.

Finn looked around and saw a lost baby bunny sitting under a bush. The bunny was scared and shivering.

"Don't worry," said Finn. "I'll help you!"

Finn carefully picked up the baby bunny and carried it home. His mommy fox made a warm nest for the bunny. "You'll feel better soon," she said.

Finn stayed by the bunny every day. He played with her, told her stories, and even taught her to hop. The bunny grew stronger and prettier with each passing day.

One evening, as the sun painted the sky pink, the bunny said, "Thank you, Finn. I'll never forget you!"

Finn grinned. "That's okay, little one. It was fun helping!"

And from that day on, Finn and the bunny bunny became the best of friends, exploring and playing together in the garden.
