A group of old friends comes together every year at Christmastime to compete for the Christmas Cup. The coveted prize is an obnoxiously large frosted glass mug in the shape of a moose with antlers for the handles. How does one win the cup? By sharing the most devastatingly embarrassing story from their past year. The group meets every December 20th, right before Christmas, and the six friends vote with secret ballots to determine whose story is the winner. This year's stories include misplacing underwear while on the job, showing up on the wrong night for a blind date, a missing tooth, an embarrassing and overdue library book, a parent-teacher conference gone wrong, and a misunderstanding at the grocery checkout line.
