## Product Requirements Document: "Synapse" – Contextual Knowledge Management

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently lose valuable insights and context from scattered notes, documents, and communications, hindering efficient problem-solving and innovation.

**Vision:** To be the essential digital workspace that seamlessly captures, connects, and resurfaces relevant knowledge, empowering individuals to work smarter and more creatively.

---

### User Personas

**1. Dr. Anya Sharma – Research Scientist**

*   **Goal:** To efficiently synthesize findings from numerous research papers, lab notes, and conference presentations to advance her research and publish impactful findings.
*   **Pain Points:** Difficulty connecting disparate pieces of information, losing track of specific details from past readings, struggling to recall the context of previous insights, and feeling overwhelmed by the sheer volume of information.

**2. Mark Olsen – Marketing Manager**

*   **Goal:** To rapidly access and share relevant campaign data, market research, and competitor analysis with his team to inform strategic decision-making.
*   **Pain Points:** Searching through shared drives and email threads to find specific information, lacking a centralized repository for marketing knowledge, experiencing delays in decision-making due to information silos, and needing a streamlined method for onboarding new team members.

**3. Eleanor Vance – Freelance Consultant**

*   **Goal:** To organize client communications, project notes, and industry best practices to deliver exceptional service and maintain a competitive edge.
*   **Pain Points:** Losing track of key details from client conversations, struggling to recall specific recommendations made in previous engagements, needing a secure and portable knowledge base accessible across devices, and desiring a system to facilitate knowledge transfer between projects.

---

### Requirements

**1. Contextual Note Capture:** Users can create notes linked to specific files, websites, emails, or other digital assets.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can associate notes with a variety of file types (PDF, DOCX, TXT, CSV, URLs), email threads (via integration with common email providers), and other digital resources. The association is visually clear within the note and when viewing the linked resource.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 75% of active users create at least one contextual note per week.

**2. Semantic Search & Discovery:** Users can search for notes and linked resources using keywords, phrases, or concepts, with results prioritized based on relevance and context.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Search results display notes and associated resources, ranked by relevance determined by keyword matching and contextual relationships. Users can filter results by date, file type, and associated resources.
    *   **Success Metrics:** Average search time is reduced by 50% compared to existing methods.

**3. Relationship Mapping (Knowledge Graph):** Users can visually explore the relationships between notes and resources, revealing connections and patterns that might otherwise be missed.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can view notes and resources as nodes in a graph, with lines representing connections. Users can manually add or remove connections. The graph is navigable and zoomable.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 30% of active users utilize the relationship mapping feature at least once per week.

**4. Secure Sharing & Collaboration:** Users can securely share notes and knowledge graphs with colleagues, controlling access permissions and facilitating collaborative knowledge building.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can share notes and knowledge graphs with specific individuals or groups. Sharing permissions can be set to "view only" or "edit."  Changes made by collaborators are visible to all shared users.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 20% of active users share notes or knowledge graphs with collaborators.

**5. Cross-Platform Accessibility:** The application is accessible and functional on iOS, Android, and web browsers, ensuring users can access their knowledge base from any device.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Core functionality (note creation, search, relationship mapping, sharing) is consistent across all platforms. Synchronization of data is seamless and near-instantaneous.
    *   **Success Metrics:** User satisfaction score (measured via in-app survey) is 4.5 or higher across all platforms.



---

### Technical Considerations

*   **Scalability:** The application must be designed to handle a large volume of notes, resources, and user data.
*   **Security:** Robust security measures are essential to protect sensitive information. Encryption at rest and in transit is mandatory.
*   **Synchronization:** Real-time data synchronization across devices requires a reliable backend infrastructure.
*   **API Integrations:**  Planned integrations with common email providers (Gmail, Outlook) and cloud storage services (Google Drive, Dropbox) will require careful development and maintenance.
*   **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** Future enhancements may include NLP capabilities to automatically extract keywords and concepts from notes, but this is out of scope for the initial release.



### Risks

*   **User Adoption:**  Convincing users to adopt a new knowledge management system can be challenging.
*   **Data Migration:**  Migrating existing knowledge from other systems can be complex and time-consuming.
*   **Synchronization Issues:**  Maintaining consistent data synchronization across all devices can be technically difficult.
*   **Security Vulnerabilities:**  The application is a potential target for cyberattacks, requiring ongoing security assessments and updates.



### Non-Goals

*   Automated content generation.
*   Direct file editing within the application.
*   Integration with proprietary or niche software platforms.
*   Support for offline access beyond cached data.
*   Advanced data visualization beyond the knowledge graph.




---

### Launch Checklist

1.  **Complete Beta Testing:**  Conduct thorough beta testing with a diverse group of users to identify and address any remaining bugs or usability issues.
2.  **Finalize Security Audit:**  Perform a final security audit to ensure the application meets all security requirements.
3.  **Prepare User Documentation:**  Create comprehensive user documentation and tutorials to guide users through the application's features.
4.  **Develop Marketing Materials:**  Prepare basic marketing materials (website landing page, introductory video) to announce the application's launch.
5.  **Establish Support Channels:**  Set up support channels (email, FAQs) to address user inquiries and provide technical assistance.
6.  **Deploy to App Stores:**  Submit the application to the iOS App Store and Google Play Store for review and approval.
