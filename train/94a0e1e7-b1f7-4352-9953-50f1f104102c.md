In the neon glow of the city, a lone figure sprints past flickering billboards, their breath misting in the cold. A pocket watch—glowing faintly—hits the pavement with a metallic *clang*. The wearer’s shadow, elongated by the artificial light, stretches across the sidewalk. The watch’s second hand ticks *twelve*, then *twenty-four*. The figure turns, hands raised in surrender, as the last second of the minute dissolves into silence. A distant siren wails. The watch’s glow fades.
