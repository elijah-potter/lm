Wavy, crispy,
Skinny or long,
Here we’ll sing 
the French Fry song!

Potatoes peeled,
Cut and ready to fry,
Grab the ketchup,
Give one a try!

Tasty, scrumptious,
They hit the spot.
Careful when fresh,
They come out hot!

Seasoned or plain,
Battered with beer,
Options are endless,
Let us all cheer!
