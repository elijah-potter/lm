What Is ETL Certificate?
ETL standards for Electrical Testing Laboratories. It is an organization testing the safety of electronic components and products based on a set of rigorous standards. This corporation has been around for more than 100 years (since Thomas Edison founded it), but it now acts as a division of Intertek Testing Laboratories.
Did you know that Intertek ETL is a nationally recognized testing laboratory? The United States OSHA also acknowledged it.
An ETL certification is a product certification awarded to products that have been found and tested to meet specific safety standards. It’s performed by a third-party testing lab, such as UL and Intertek, which guarantees the product has been created and manufactured following industry regulations and standards.
Note that ETL certification is typically utilized for various products, including electronic and electrical devices, lighting equipment, and appliances. Other products that might carry an ETL certification include cables, chargers, power adapters, and extension cords.
An ETL certification is essential as it helps to guarantee the reliability and safety of products used in your homes, businesses, and other settings. ETL-certified products have been tested to guarantee they’re safe and won’t risk injury or damage to any user or surrounding environment.
A product should undergo rigorous testing and assessment to meet the needed safety standards to acquire ETL certification. Also, that process often involves testing for electrical performance, safety, durability, and environmental impact.
Once your product has been successfully tested and certified, it will be immediately awarded an ETL logo or mark that can be shown on the product or its packaging, indicating it has been certified.

Q: What are the differences between ETL and UL certifications?
UL and ETL are two organizations offering electrical certifications for products. That guarantee that the products you purchase meet safety standards and are ideal for use in various locations across the globe.
ETL (Electrical Testing Laboratories) is a certification organization testing and certifying electrical products for performance and safety. It is recognized in the US, Canada, and other countries globally.
On the other hand, UL (Underwriters Laboratories) offers electrical product certification. Like ETL, it tests and certifies products to guarantee they meet safety standards set by international and national agencies.
Did you know that UL is a popular and well-respected organization sought in many nations around the globe?
Overall, UL and ETL certifications are highly recognized and respected in the industry. Nonetheless, the requirements and standards a product should meet to get certification may differ between the two organizations.
Thus, it’s essential to check the certain standards and requirements that a product should meet to get certification from either UL or ETL.

Q: Is ETL like NOM certification?
No. ETL and NOM (Normas Oficial Mexicana) are not the same. ETL is a certification mark that indicates a product has been independently tested and meets performance and safety standards. The latter is a set of mandatory technical standards in Mexico used to regulate the operation, construction, and design of services and products in various industries.
NOM certification is needed for products sold in Mexico to ensure they meet specific quality and safety standards. Thus, ETL and NOM certifications aren’t interchangeable and are often used for various purposes.
