Apartment shopping can be stressful. There are many things to consider, including affordability, safety, amenities, convenience, and availability. Here is a list to help you make a great decision so you can enjoy your new home!

-Have a budget in mind ahead of time. Knowing what price range you need to stay within can help you quickly weed out places outside of your budget.
-Check online safety/police reports to get an understanding of crime in an area. If you see residents when apartment shopping, ask them for their opinion on safety or use social media to get feedback on a location.
-Ask the leasing consultant or landlord about amenities. Things like a pool, gym, covered parking, etc. should be factored into your decision. 
-Make sure to check the length of your commute to work during peak traffic times, and ensure places you go often are within a reasonable distance.
