Rich with the scent of damp earth and wildflowers, the air hums with the low, steady pulse of rushing water. The texture of moss clings to the stone steps, soft and damp beneath bare feet. Crickets and frogs trade timid chirps and distant, guttural calls in the underbrush. The wind carries the faint metallic tang of iron and the sharp, sweetness of pine. Shadows stretch long and heavy, shifting with the rhythm of unseen currents. The warmth of the sun filters through the canopy, leaving the air sticky with the lingering taste of summer.
