Study Guide Supplement to Learning Sequences in Music (2012 Edition)
Questions Supplementing the 2007 Study Guide to be Compatible with the 2012 Edition
by Edwin Gordon
© 2012 GIA Publications, Inc.
This study guide includes 60 multiple-choice questions that cover current information in the 2012 Edition of Learning Sequences in Music: A Contemporary Music Learning Theory. All are new. While they relate to recent information in the book, they expand and reinforce concepts found in the 2007 Edition that some readers may find complex. Thus, questions are composed so as to clarify some thoughts and make them more easily accessible. To be sure, all questions in the 2007 Edition remain valid.
Also included with this study guide is a CD containing elaborations on many of the concepts found in the questions.
