DAY ONE: 
• Arrive at camp, move into the cabin, and claim a bunk.
• Meet in the mess hall for lunch and orientation.
• Free time after lunch for swimming, canoeing, and exploring the campgrounds.
• Head to the rec hall before dinner to sign up for classes and activities. 
• Dinner in the mess hall.
• Meet by the campfire for camp songs, games, s'mores, and opening ceremonies.
• Return to the cabin for lights out. 

DAYS TWO, THREE, & FOUR:
• Wake early and meet at the mess hall for breakfast. 
• Attend first choice activity (horseback riding, arts and crafts, archery, nature hike, swimming, or canoeing).
• Short break before second choice activity.
• Attend second choice activity (horseback riding, arts and crafts, archery, nature hike, swimming, or canoeing).
• Meet at the mess hall for lunch.
• Free time to explore the campgrounds, swim, shop the general store, or call home.
• Attend afternoon classes (pottery, painting, cooking, sewing, creative writing, or computer programming).
• Short break before dinner.
• Meet at the mess hall for dinner. 
• Meet by the campfire for camp songs, games, and s'mores.
• Return to the cabin for lights out. 

DAY FIVE:
• Wake early and meet at the mess hall for breakfast. 
• Attend first choice activity (horseback riding, arts and crafts, archery, nature hike, swimming, or canoeing).
• Short break before classes.
• Attend classes (pottery, painting, cooking, sewing, creative writing, or computer programming).
• Meet at the mess hall for lunch.
• Free time to pack up and clean the cabin.
• Meet by the campfire for camp songs, games, and closing ceremonies.
• Return home. We'll see you next summer!
