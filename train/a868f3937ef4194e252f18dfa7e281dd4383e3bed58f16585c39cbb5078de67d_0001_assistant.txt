The greatest golfer that ever lived was none other than Barry Yamp.
With 395 holes-in-one, he was a 12-time Masters reigning Champ.

He held all the glory, one achievement after another,
But you should have seen the way he played against his brother!

With a charming personality and a quick wit,
Nobody could ever hit the ball as far as he could hit.

His only weakness, however mighty, was a windy day.
As long the wind was negligible, he knew he'd be okay.
