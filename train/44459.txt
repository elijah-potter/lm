Har Ghar Tiranga
As a part of celebration of the 75th Independence Day, our NSS Volunteers created an awareness among the school children about the theme of the 75th Independence Day that is ‘Har Ghar Tiranga’, which means, each house must hoist the Tricolour Flag for three consecutive days (13-08-22 to 15-08-22).
We elaborated on the celebration of Independence Day and we thought them how to hoist the flag in a proper manner and how to preserve the flags, we strictly advised them not to throw away the flags. Then we distributed the flags to them and showed them the proper way to pin the flags, we even distributed candies to the school children. In this event we have visited the PUMS, K Rayarpalayam, PUMS, Ganapathipalayam, PUPS, Chennappachettipudur, PUPS, M Rayarpalayam, and PUPS, Selamparayampalaym.
