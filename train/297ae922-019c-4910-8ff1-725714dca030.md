Ethan’s hands tremble slightly as he flips through the pages of a dog-eared notebook. His original draft lies beside it, a tangled web of notes, crossed-out lines, and scribbled corrections. On the screen, his revised prose glows: *"The crown was not silver, but iron, etched with runes that pulsed like a heartbeat."* The first version had been *"gold, heavy as a dead man’s dreams."* He adjusts the phrasing, fine-tuning the imagery to evoke both dread and inevitability.

Outside, a rain tap-taps against the window. Inside, the air smells of old paper and something sharper—ink and determination. Ethan exhales, closing his laptop with a sharp *click*. He grabs his coffee, refills the mug, and leans back. The story is done. For now.
