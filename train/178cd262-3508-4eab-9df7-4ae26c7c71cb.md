Once there was a tiny little bunny named Benny who lived in a cozy burrow under a big, fluffy mushroom. Benny loved to hop around the garden, chasing butterflies and collecting shiny leaves. One sunny morning, Benny noticed something strange—a tiny, glowing rock sparkled near his favorite flower.

Benny picked it up and felt it warm. "What’s this?" he wondered. Suddenly, the rock began to hum softly, and a small, kind-hearted fox named Felix appeared! "I found this rock too," said Felix. "It can make magic happen if we share it with others." Benny and Felix decided to spread joy by giving the rock to their friends: a sleepy old owl named Oliver, a giggly squirrel named Sammy, and even a brave little rabbit named Lily.

Together, they placed the rock in the center of their garden, and—*POP!*—everyone’s favorite flower bloomed brighter, the squirrels chattered louder, and even the wind whispered sweet songs. The magic rock made everyone feel happy, and Benny and Felix knew they had to keep sharing it every day.

From that day on, Benny and his friends played together, telling stories under the mushroom, and the garden sparkled with kindness all around. And if you listen closely on a quiet evening, you might hear the magic humming softly, just like the first time Benny found it.
