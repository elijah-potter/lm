Blood testing is one of the foremost vital ways of monitoring your health progress. It helps you to monitor your body changes over time. This can aid you in your decision-making on matters concerning your health. Blood tests as of today can be ordered online.
You can order your own approved lab testing online or via your phone from health testing centers. However, you will require your doctor’s order of approval. You can then move to your local lab location with a lab requisition to have your testing done. Here is a list of the most common blood tests online;
- Complete blood count test
A complete blood count test checks for different components of every major cell in your blood: white blood cells, red blood cells, and platelets. The components measured include red blood cell count and hemoglobin. Low red blood cell count indicates your body doesn’t receive enough oxygen, and low levels of platelets can cause long-time bleeding. Anemia is an example of a disease identified in this test.
- Basic metabolic panel(BMP)
This test checks for levels of certain compounds in the blood, such as glucose, sodium, chloride, and calcium. The test is done after you have fasted for eight hours. Deviations from the normal standard results may be indications of diseases such as kidney disease and diabetes.
- Complete metabolic panel(CMP)
It includes all measurements of BMP as well as additional proteins substances related to liver and kidney function. CMP is also used to monitor patients with chronic diseases as part of routine testing. However, it should be done several times to confirm the results as the first result is always a false positive in this test.
- Lipid panel
The test is performed for evaluation of cholesterol in patient blood. There are two types of cholesterol: high-density lipoprotein(HDL) and beta lipoprotein (LDL). HDL is good. As a result, it eliminates toxic substances from the blood, while LDL is unhealthy because it causes plaque development in your arteries leading to a risk of having a heart attack disease.
- Enzyme markers
Enzymes are described as proteins that help your body to accomplish certain chemical processes like breaking down food and blood clotting. Abnormal levels in your body can be indications of health issues.
- Sexually transmitted disease test
Sexually transmitted diseases such as chlamydia, gonorrhea, herpes, HIV, and syphilis can be diagnosed using a blood sample. The tests are usually combined with urine samples of infected tissues for accuracy.
- Follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) test
Follicle-stimulating hormone is responsible for ovarian follicles in women and gonad development and sperm production in men. If the FSH level is low in women, the chances of getting pregnant become harder. This is because of the irregular periods and early menopause caused by hormone imbalance.
This is a list of common blood tests that have lab orders online. However, you should consult with your physician to see the effective tests that will be useful to you.
