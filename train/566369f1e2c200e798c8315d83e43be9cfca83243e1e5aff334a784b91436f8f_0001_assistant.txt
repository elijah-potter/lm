In the hustling, bustling city that never sleeps, there were some pigeons known for their boisterous flair. They didn’t care if you were trying to cross the street or meditate in the park; they always made their presence known one way or another.

Among these pigeons was one named Skye—he was huge and colorful! He loved to interrupt people during their activities. Why? Because he was beautiful! He was so beautiful everyone needed to know, no matter how busy or annoyed they got. 

Skye thought he was the best pigeon, but Priscilla certainly would always knock the wind from his wings with her gorgeous feathers. She hung out at traffic lights and had her fill of abandoned breadcrumbs—but not just any breadcrumbs! No, Priscilla only ate crumbs from Italian bread and French pastries. 

Together, Priscilla and Skye were the snootiest pigeons in the city—if you saw them (and you will), you’d think they were the king and queen of New York! They show that even the most common creatures can have huge personalities.
