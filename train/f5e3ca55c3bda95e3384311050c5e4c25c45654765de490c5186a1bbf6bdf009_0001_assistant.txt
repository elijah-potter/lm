Mary's bodyguard, whose alias was "Lamb", because he always wore a white suit, was always close to her. His job was to keep Mary safe. He followed her to school, although it was against the rules. Lamb couldn't let Mary out of his sight because Mary was in danger and he promised her family that he would not let anything happen to her. 

Seeing Lamb come to school with her that day made all the other children laugh and they were eager to play with him. The teacher came out to see what the fuss was and told Lamb he needed to leave because he was too much of a distraction. Although Lamb was not pleased with this reaction, he stepped away far enough for the teacher not to see him. 

Lamb hid in the bushes keeping a clear eye on Mary until she emerged from school that day and made sure she got home safe and sound, never leaving her side that day.
