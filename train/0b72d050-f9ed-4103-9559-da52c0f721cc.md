A crumbling stone archway near the old mill pond marks the last known location of the **Blackthorn Tavern**, where travelers once gathered for tales of forgotten lands. The tavern’s sign, carved in faded runes, still bears the date **"1847"**—the year the last of its patrons vanished without a trace.

The tavern’s cellar, now overgrown with ivy, holds the remnants of a hidden map: a single parchment with a single line in a cipher, later cracked by historians. The cipher translates to:

**"Here lies what was never found—
Where the river bends and the mist swirls,
The lanterns still hum, but the ghosts do not return.
Seek where the river bends and the mist swirls."**

The river, now called the **Silent Stream**, flows just beyond the arch, its banks lined with strange carvings—symbols no one recognizes. Some say the tavern’s final patrons were lured by a mirage of a **black stallion** that vanished into the mist. Others claim the tavern’s patrons were **never there at all**, that it was a ghostly echo of a different era.

The stone arch is the only evidence left, but whispers persist. Locals avoid the area at night, where the wind carries the faint sound of **drums** and **laughter**. Some believe the tavern still stands, its doors creaking open only when no one is looking. Others say the river has swallowed it whole.

The place is only remembered in the stories of those who once knew it—and the stories themselves are the only proof it ever existed.
