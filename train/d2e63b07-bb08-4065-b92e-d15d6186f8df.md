**BREAKING:** Mayor’s Office Announces First Official Public Hearing for the Proposed **Eco-Tech Park** in **New Haven Green**—Community members urged to submit feedback by **May 12th**. Construction permits for the 250-acre development, slated to begin next month, will be issued upon approval of preliminary environmental impact studies. Protests near the site have escalated, with activists demanding stricter noise and pollution controls. Local businesses warn of potential long-term economic disruption. The city’s **Green Initiatives Division** has launched a real-time monitoring system to assess impacts on nearby wildlife, including a newly discovered population of endangered **New Haven Green Butterflies**. Officials emphasize that zoning adjustments to accommodate urban sprawl will not be finalized until public consensus is secured. Residents are advised to review updated ordinances at **cityhall.nwhavengreen.gov/planning**. No additional site visits permitted until construction begins.
