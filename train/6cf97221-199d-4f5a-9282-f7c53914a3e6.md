A rule enforced solely for tradition, bureaucratic inertia, or historical precedent, often without clear justification, persists in practice despite its impracticality or outdated nature. For example, a mandatory dress code in a workplace that fails to align with modern labor norms or employee preferences, maintained because it was implemented decades ago without reevaluation. This rule persists due to resistance to change rather than addressing genuine concerns about efficiency, inclusivity, or fairness.
