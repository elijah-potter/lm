1. USS Arizona Memorial at Pearl Harbor in Oahu - THe USS Arizona Memorial at Pearl Harbor in Honolulu is the most popular visitor destination in the Hawaiian Islands. More than 2 million visitors from the U.S. and international areas choose to visit this memorial each year to learn more about where WWII started for  America.

2. The North Shore of Oahu - The North Shore of Oahu is located between West Oahu's Ka'ena Point and East Oahu's Kahuku Point. It is known for its large waves during the winter and beautiful coastlines as well as for attracting avid surfers from around the world. 

3. Waikiki Beach Oahu - The name Waikiki means "spouting waters" which refers to the freshwater rivers that flowed through the ocean. In ancient times, the coastal area of Waikiki in South Oahu encompassed a larger area, which included the Manoa and Palolo Valleys. 

4. Na Pali Coast State Wilderness Park Kauai - The Na Pali Coastline on Kauai is one of the most beautiful places in the world. This region isn't just one of the best places to visit while in Hawaii but it is also rich in Hawaiian culture. The area was once home to Hawaiian royalty and is well known for its exuberant flowing waterfalls and freshwater rivers. 

5. Haleakala National Park Maui - When visiting the island of Maui, you will want to visit Haleakala National Park and the impressive Mt. Haleakala. You should plan a trip to this location for either a sunrise or sunset experience, there are also hiking, guided activities and camping opportunities at the park.
