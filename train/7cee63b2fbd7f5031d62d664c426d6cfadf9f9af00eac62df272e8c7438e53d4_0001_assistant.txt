Roberto was a slow and cautious driver, always taking the safest route and driving the speed limit. His sensibility frustrated his supervisors, who wanted him to drive faster, but Roberto was always worried about what could go wrong. People on the road would shake their fists and insult him. For a while, Roberto was torn between his conviction about driving safely and his desire to keep his job.

One day, Roberto was driving down the highway when he saw a car coming towards him. The car accelerated rapidly, swerving chaotically all over the road. Roberto leaned his truck to avoid a collision, and the car missed him by mere inches. This experience shook up Roberto, but he was relieved that he and his cargo were safe. From that day forth, Roberto was resolute in his convictions about safe driving, and he vowed to never drive faster than he was comfortable with.
