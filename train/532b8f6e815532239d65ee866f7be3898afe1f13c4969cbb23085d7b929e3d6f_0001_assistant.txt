There’s no one-size-fits-all solution to professional emails, but here are some ideas to start with: 

1. Best regards: shows a wishing for well-being, this is regarded as fairly neutral for unfamiliar recipients.

2. Best wishes: This is better for someone who you regard as a colleague at the same level as you.

3. Many thanks: This shows appreciation for the other person, and would be good for an email that is responding to someone. 

 The best response will take into consideration your relationship with who you are interacting with.
