Homeschool co-ops are a great way to meet new friends! There are lots of places you could advertise, such as:

1.) Social media - Platforms like Facebook are a great way to get the word out about your new group. Many areas already have local group "chatter" pages on social media platforms where you could reach a lot of people. 
2.) Your local library - Libraries may offer bulletin boards where you can post fliers to advertise local groups and activities. 
3.) Your local newspaper - Consider running an ad in your local newspaper to reach more families.
