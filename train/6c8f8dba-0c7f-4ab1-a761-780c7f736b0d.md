Once upon a time in a cozy little house, there lived a bunny named Benny with fluffy white fur. Benny loved to hop around his garden, where flowers bloomed in every color. One sunny morning, Benny noticed something strange—the flowers were all asleep, and the sun was hiding behind the clouds.

Benny decided to explore. He dug a little and found a tiny cave where a sleepy old dragon named Dazzle snored loudly. Benny peeked inside and saw Dazzle’s big, shiny eyes closed. "Why are the flowers asleep?" Benny wondered.

Just then, Dazzle woke up with a *whoosh*! "Oh dear! I’ve been sleeping too much," said Dazzle, rubbing his wings. "But I’m so tired, I forgot to make the flowers bloom!"

Benny thought hard. "I’ll help you!" he said. Benny hopped up and down, dancing to make his little paws shake. He giggled and waved his ears, and soon, Dazzle’s eyes widened. "What are you doing?!"

The two friends worked together. Benny sang a little song while Dazzle blew gently on the flowers, and soon, the flowers opened their petals wide. The sun peeked out, and the garden sparkled with colors.

From that day on, Benny and Dazzle had fun adventures together. The flowers never slept again, and the garden stayed full of sunshine and joy. Benny knew he’d met his best friend ever. And that was the happiest story ever told!
