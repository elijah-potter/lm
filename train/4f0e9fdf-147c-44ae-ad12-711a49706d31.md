At a crowded corporate event, a junior employee hesitantly approaches a senior executive, their fingers tracing the edge of a coffee cup. The room hums with whispered conversations; someone turns to take a photo. "I—I just wanted to explain myself," the junior says, voice tight. "I’ve been working on that project for months, but the last-minute changes made it nearly impossible." The executive’s raised eyebrow lingers. Across the table, a colleague leans in, smirking. "Yeah, and you were the one who insisted on the ‘agile’ approach?" Outside the window, a neon sign flickers: *"Now Hiring."*
