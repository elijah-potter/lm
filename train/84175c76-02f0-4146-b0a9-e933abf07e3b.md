The dimly lit alley flickered with streetlights casting long shadows. A stranger moved through the crowd, dressed in a rumpled black coat and a hat pulled low over their face. Their gait was unsteady, and they carried a worn backpack, shifting it awkwardly. Nearby, a group of teenagers laughed and argued, oblivious to the figure’s presence. One of them, a girl with a hoodie, glanced back briefly, but their eyes were fixed on the phone in their hand. The stranger paused, looking around, as if searching for something before disappearing into the crowd. The girl’s phone screen glowed—an unread message: *"You’re not supposed to be here."*
