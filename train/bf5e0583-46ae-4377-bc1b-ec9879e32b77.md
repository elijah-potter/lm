A dimly lit office at 3 AM. A single desk with a half-empty coffee cup and a half-finished spreadsheet. The door creaks open as footsteps echo down the hall. The person—mid-30s, wearing an oversized sweater, hair tangled from lack of sleep—turns to face a clipboard and a stern-faced HR representative.

*"I’m here because my manager asked me to explain why I’ve been… what’s the word again?—absent lately?"*

The person clears their throat, fingers tracing the edge of a coffee stain on their sleeve. They start with the obvious: *"I’ve been running errands. Groceries. Pet supplies."* Then the questions dig deeper. *"Why so many?"* *"Is there a pattern?"*

Silence. The HR rep taps their pen against the clipboard. The person’s voice wavers: *"I—I’ve been working on a side project. A community garden. Neighbors need help, and I just… keep getting pulled in."* They pause, then add: *"It’s not like I’m avoiding anything. I just… can’t say no."*

The HR rep’s expression softens slightly. *"And what about your health?"* The person exhales. *"I’ve been… overwhelmed. My doctor said I need to take care of myself."* They look down, then back up. *"I’m trying. I really am."*

The rep nods, but the tension lingers. The person stands, stretching their arms overhead. *"I’ll be here tomorrow. I’ll give you a proper answer then."* They turn toward the door, but hesitate. *"I’ll start a log of hours. I’ll—"* The door closes behind them. Outside, the hum of the city continues, indifferent.
