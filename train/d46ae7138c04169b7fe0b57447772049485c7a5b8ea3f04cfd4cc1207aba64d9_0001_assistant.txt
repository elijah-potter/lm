Enter Shikari was formed in St Albans in 1999 by Englishmen Chris Batten (bass), Rou Reynolds (vocals and keyboard), and Rob Rolfe (drummer); Rory Clewlow (guitar) joined in 2003.  Their music combines many different rock and electronic music styles. It has variously been described as alternative rock, metalcore, electronic rock, and synth-metal. They have released six studio albums which have reached as high as number 4 on the UK Album Chart.  Better known for lyrics that are imbued with political messages. covering topics like climate change, capitalism, and nuclear weapons, in more recent albums they have also touched upon personal topics like depression and anxiety as well.  The band was influenced by a wide variety of musical artists such as Joy Division, Rage Against the Machine, and The Beatles.  Although they distribute music through major labels as well, Enter Shikari has its own record label called Ambush Reality.  

Enter Shikari's last 5 albums are:
- A Kiss for the Whole World (2023)
- Nothing Is True & Everything Is Possible (2020)
- The Spark (2017)
- The Mindsweep (2015)
- A Flash Flood of Colour (2012)
