**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 2, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair, a beloved tradition celebrating agriculture, community, and entertainment, returns this August with a full slate of events and attractions for all ages. This year’s fair promises a vibrant atmosphere and a wide array of experiences, showcasing the best of Sangamon County.

The fair runs from August 15th through August 20th at the Sangamon County Fairgrounds. Visitors can anticipate thrilling carnival rides, livestock exhibits featuring prize-winning animals, demonstrations of traditional crafts, and a diverse selection of food vendors. Agricultural displays will highlight the region’s farming heritage, while interactive exhibits offer educational opportunities for children and adults alike. The grandstand will host a variety of performances and competitions throughout the week.

Admission to the fair is $12 for adults, $8 for children ages 6-12, and free for children five and under. Carnival ride tickets and wristbands will be available for purchase on-site. A complete schedule of events, including specific times and locations, can be found on the fair’s official website at www.sangamoncountyfair.org. Early bird discounts on advance ticket purchases are available online until August 10th.

**Headline Events:**

*   Demolition Derby – August 16th, 7:00 PM
*   Livestock Auction – August 17th, 6:00 PM
*   Tractor Pull – August 18th, 3:00 PM
*   Blue Ribbon Baking Competition – August 15th, 10:00 AM
*   Country Music Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters – August 19th, 8:00 PM
*   4-H Showcase – August 20th, 11:00 AM
