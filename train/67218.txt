Many individuals find they are diagnosed with autism spectrum disorder (ASD) at some point during their lives, which includes Aspergers as well as other disorders that cause similar difficulties with communication and social skills. There is no one answer on whether Aspies and ADHD go together because Aspergers and ADHD manifest differently.
Aspergers’ symptoms often resemble the diagnostic criteria for attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) because they can share problems with self-regulation, organization, distractibility, and other challenges that look like Aspies have a more challenging time than others focusing or sitting still. The fact is Aspie adults may be more likely to be diagnosed with ADHD later on in life because of how their Aspie traits overlap so much with similar behaviors associated with ADHD diagnosis. Therefore it’s essential to explore what both disorders might mean when you think about having trouble staying organized or attending tasks reliably over long periods throughout their lifetime.
