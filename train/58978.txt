سْمِ اللّهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ
Bissmillahi Rahmani Raheem
(In the name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.)
Here come lesson two which is about salah.
What is Salah?
Salah is the ritual prayer that all Muslims do to connect with Allah and to please Him and to show that we belong to Him and He is our Master.
How to do Salah?
- Begin by saying Bissmillahi Rahmani Raheem.
- Say Allaho Akbar while touching your ears.
- Then recite this:
Wa bi hamdika
Wa tabarak Asmuka
Wa ta’ala Jadduka
Wa la ilaha ghairuk.
