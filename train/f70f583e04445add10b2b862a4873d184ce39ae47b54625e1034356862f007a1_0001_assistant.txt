Tan Yock Lin is a distinguished law professor at the National University of Singapore, and he specializes in criminal procedure, conflicts of law, and the legal profession. After obtaining an engineering degree from Imperial College and a law degree from the University of Oxford, he worked with several organizations, including the Economic Development Board and the Monetary Authority of Singapore. In recognition of his contribution to law reform with the Singapore Academy of Law, he was awarded the Public Service Medal in 2008.
