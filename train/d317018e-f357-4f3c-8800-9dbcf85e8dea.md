Whispers of damp earth rise,
rustling leaves like crumpled paper—
moisture clings to stone,
cool mist curls around damp wood.
Beneath, damp earth hums with slow, steady breath.
Dawn’s first light grazes the horizon,
soft as honeyed silence.

Far-off, distant thunder grumbles—
woodsmoke curls, thick and slow,
smelling of pine and decay.
A distant train’s whistle fades,
then nothing but the scent of wet grass
and the distant, rhythmic *clink-clink* of rain.
