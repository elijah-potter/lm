Dynamic urban sprawl with adaptive infrastructure:
High-rise micro-farms vertically integrated into skyscrapers, autonomous drone-pods delivering biotech crops via precision agrotech to floating urban hubs. AI-driven public transport networks adjust real-time traffic flow based on predictive neural models of demographic shifts, while modular housing units self-assemble via robotic cranes, replacing fixed apartment layouts. Street-level holographic wayfinding overlays guide pedestrians through adaptive spatial configurations, merging with digital twin simulations of future city layouts.
