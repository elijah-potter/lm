Dion Fortune was born Violet Mary Firth on December 6th, 1946. She was a super groovy British occultist, ceremonial magician, and far-out author. Fortune was a co-founder of the Fraternity of the Inner Light, an occult group that was stoked to gab about philosophy and communicate with spiritual entities called the Ascended Masters.  A slammin' writer, she produced a totally radical amount of articles and books on her occult ideas. Fortune also wrote seven off-the-hook novels, some of which expanded on more funky occult themes. Dion Fortune sadly passed away on January 6th, 1946.
