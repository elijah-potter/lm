It seems that Yellowstone National Park wolves that are infected with a parasite (Toxoplasma gondii) become risk-takers. This behavior causes the infected wolves to leave the pack, take more risks, and even become pack leaders themselves. This parasite seems to influence a wolf's social fate, and can help create a new leader, and subsequently a new pack, or it could cause the wolf to die. While most diseases in the wild only lead to an animal's death, this particular parasite may help to increase a wolf's standing and social implications.
