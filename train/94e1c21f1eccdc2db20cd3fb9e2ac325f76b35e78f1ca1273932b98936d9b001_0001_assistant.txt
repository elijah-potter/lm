

What is solar roofing? Solar roofing is a residential solar energy option. Solar roofing is meant to upgrade a standard roof replacement by adding solar shingles as part of the roofing system. This provides the home with solar-powered energy, just like a standard solar panel array. Solar shingles convert sunlight into energy, providing a source of power that is independent of the local power grid.

How is this different from solar panels? Although solar roofing provides the home with solar energy, they are not solar panels at all. They are solar shingle that is installed much like a standard asphalt roof. Solar panels are installed onto the roof using a rack mount. Solar shingles are installed directly to the decking of the roof, and they are part of a comprehensive roof replacement that includes both solar shingles and standard asphalt shingles. 

What are the benefits of solar roofing? Solar roofing provides renewable residential solar energy. This protects against rising energy costs because there is no cost associated with the energy produced by solar roofing. Solar roofing also provides a sleek appearance that many prefer over bulky solar panels, and the installation process is faster. Additionally, some homeowners experience damage to their roofs from solar panels, and this damage is not covered by the roof warranty. With solar roofing, you have a product that is certified as a solar panel and as a roofing shingle, so both are covered by a warranty if damage were to occur.
