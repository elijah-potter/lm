The rain-soaked road shimmers under flickering streetlights as **Lena** walks past the abandoned churchyard, her breath fogging in the cold. A handwritten note in her pocket—*"Meet me here at midnight."* The words feel like a knife twisting in her stomach. The church’s iron gates groan on rusted hinges, their stained glass casting jagged shadows. Inside, the air smells of damp wood and old incense. The only light comes from the single candle burning on a cracked altar.

A voice cuts through the silence: *"You came."* **Mateo**, her childhood friend, stands in the shadows near the stained-glass window depicting a broken promise. His dark coat is damp, his knuckles raw from a fight. He steps forward, his voice raw. *"I didn’t mean to leave you here."* He reaches into his coat, pulling out a pistol—silver, tarnished. *"But I had to."* The gun is aimed at her chest. *"You promised me you’d stay away. You lied."* A single tear rolls down his face. The candle flickers, casting his reflection—terrified, broken, but also *hungry*. The weight of the past presses between them. The church’s bells, long silent, suddenly toll in the distance. Time stretches like smoke. What will she choose?
