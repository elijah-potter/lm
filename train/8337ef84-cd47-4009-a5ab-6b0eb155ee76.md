A vast, sun-dappled valley stretches beneath a sky so still it seems to have paused time. The air hums with an eerie silence, thick with the scent of damp earth and crushed petals. Towering, skeletal trees—some bent like question marks—lean toward the horizon, their branches tangled in a dance of twilight. Between them, pools of water glisten like fractured glass, their surfaces rippling faintly, as if caught in a breath held too long. The ground beneath is a mosaic of moss and stone, uneven and soft, like a body suspended in slow motion. The only movement is the occasional whisper of wind through the leaves, a sound so quiet it might not exist at all. The entire scene feels suspended in time, frozen mid-inhalation.
