A hidden chamber beneath the ruins of an ancient library, its wooden doors carved with eiderdown petrified with time. The air hums with the faint, lingering scent of aged parchment and candle wax. A single, flickering lantern casts long shadows on the damp stone walls, where the whispers of the past echo in hollow, reverberating tones—some muffled, some clear, as if carried by unseen breathers. The floorboards groan underfoot, whispering secrets of those who came before, their voices trapped in the labyrinth of forgotten shelves and stacked tomes. The air is thick with the ghostly presence of scholars, their whispers now just static, a chorus of unheard conversations drifting between the cracks of the stone.
