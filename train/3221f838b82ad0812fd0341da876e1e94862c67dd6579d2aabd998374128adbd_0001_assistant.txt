Here are some beginner-friendly K-Pop girl groups for you to check out:

1. Blackpink: Blackpink is one of the most famous K-pop groups, especially in the Western world. They have charted multiple times on the Billboard Hot 100 and World Digital Song Sales lists. Their songs have wide appeal and are a great choice for beginners who are not familiar with K-pop. 
2: Twice: Twice is another popular K-pop group that has been around since 2015 and has a varied discography of songs in Korean, Japanese, and English. New fans would enjoy their catchy tunes, memorable lyrics, and elaborate dance performances.
3: Red Velvet: Red Velvet is a 5-member group that is known for its diverse repertoire. The group's name references the two sides of their music: songs that follow the "red" side are more pop-focused, while the "velvet" side is more R&B-based. The group is very popular with K-pop veterans and newcomers alike.
4: (G)I-DLE: This group, whose name is pronounced as just "Idle," is another popular choice for beginners. The group has several catchy songs and is known for its members' high level of involvement in writing and producing music.
