A dimly lit, high-ceilinged hall with flickering lanterns cast long shadows across worn wooden floors. The walls, lined with ancient, cracked mirrors reflecting eerie, shifting images of unseen listeners. Air hums faintly with static, like whispers caught in the wind. A grand piano sits abandoned, its keys slightly ajar, as if waiting for someone to play a forgotten tune. The scent of damp earth and old parchment lingers, thick and lingering—almost *hearing* through the nose. The air seems to ripple, carrying sounds that aren’t quite sound: the faintest, slow *breathing* of unseen beings. The silence itself is alive.
