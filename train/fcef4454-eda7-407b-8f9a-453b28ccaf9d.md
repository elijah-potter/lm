**Weekly Schedule Example (Sample Workout & Routine)**

**Monday – Upper Body (Push Focus)**
- **6:00 AM**: Wake up, hydration (water + electrolytes)
- **6:30 AM**: 20-min dynamic stretching
- **7:00 AM**: Dumbbell chest press (3x10)
- **7:20 AM**: Lat pulldown (3x10)
- **7:40 AM**: Shoulder press (3x12)
- **8:00 AM**: Core circuit (planks, leg raises, Russian twists – 3x15 sec)
- **8:30 AM**: Breakfast (protein + complex carbs)
- **9:30 AM**: Lunch
- **12:00 PM**: Light walk (15 min)

**Tuesday – Mobility & Recovery**
- **7:00 AM**: Yoga (30 min – focus on hips/shoulders)
- **8:00 AM**: Foam rolling (quads, hamstrings, back)
- **9:00 AM**: Light jog (10 min)
- **10:00 AM**: Dinner

**Wednesday – Lower Body (Pull Focus)**
- **6:30 AM**: Warm-up (bodyweight squats, lunges)
- **7:00 AM**: Deadlifts (3x8)
- **7:20 AM**: Romanian deadlifts (3x10)
- **7:40 AM**: Bulgarian split squats (3x10/leg)
- **8:00 AM**: Calf raises (3x15)
- **8:30 AM**: Post-workout shake (whey + banana)
- **9:00 AM**: Lunch

**Thursday – Active Recovery**
- **8:00 AM**: Swimming (30 min)
- **10:00 AM**: Journaling (reflect on progress)
- **12:00 PM**: Brunch

**Friday – Full Body (Compound Lifts)**
- **6:30 AM**: Bench press (4x8)
- **7:10 AM**: Pull-ups (max reps)
- **7:30 AM**: Overhead press (4x10)
- **7:50 AM**: Squats (4x12)
- **8:10 AM**: Plank (3x 1 min)
- **8:30 AM**: Dinner

**Saturday – Cardio & Endurance**
- **9:00 AM**: 30-min HIIT (cycling, sprints)
- **11:00 AM**: Long walk (60 min)
- **2:00 PM**: Snack (protein bar + nuts)
- **5:00 PM**: Dinner

**Sunday – Rest & Flexibility**
- **10:00 AM**: Stretching/yoga (45 min)
- **12:00 PM**: Light snack (fruit + nuts)
- **4:00 PM**: Relax (reading, meditation)

---
**Notes:**
- Adjust weights based on progression.
- Hydrate 3L/day; sleep 7-9 hours.
