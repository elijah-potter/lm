For centuries, my kind has inhabited the desolate expanse of the moon, hidden from the prying eyes of humanity. But now, NASA has discovered our presence, and fear courses through my extraterrestrial veins.

As the news of the discovery reaches my fellow lunar inhabitants, panic spreads like wildfire. We gather in hushed clusters. The moon, our sanctuary for eons, suddenly feels small and vulnerable. What will become of us now that we have been thrust into the spotlight?

Peering through the lunar surface, I watch the Earth. I wonder what it must be like to walk upon its surface, to feel the warmth of its sun on my skin. Curiosity gnaws at my alien soul, sometimes even overshadowing the fear that grips us all. 

What do these humans look like up close? 

I want to understand humanity.

I watch as NASA launches probes and satellites, collecting data to decipher our extraterrestrial existence. I'm torn between the desire to remain hidden, preserving the secrets of my lunar home, and the longing to reach out, to let humanity know that we actually want to be friends. 

As the days turn into weeks, and the weeks into months, our anxiety subsides a little. With cautious optimism, I watch as NASA prepares for a manned mission to the moon. My extraterrestrial heart quickens at the thought!

As the lunar module descends, I find solace in the knowledge that our alien presence on the moon does not pose a threat. Fear lingers, but so does hope now too.
