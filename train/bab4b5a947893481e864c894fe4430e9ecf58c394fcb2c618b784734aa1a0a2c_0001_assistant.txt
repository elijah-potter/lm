The smallest planet in our solar system is Mercury with an equatorial circumference of 15,329 km, equatorial diameter of 4879 km, and mass of approximately 330,104,000,000,000 billion kg (5.5% of Earth's mass). It's named after the Roman god known for speed and commerce. Its surface temperatures range from -173 to 427°C. It is composed mainly of iron and silicates and has been geologically inactive for billions of years, resulting in numerous impact craters on its surface.
