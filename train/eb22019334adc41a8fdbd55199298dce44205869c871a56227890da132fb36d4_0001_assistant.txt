The lead guitarist of the band Queen is Sir Brian Harold May CBE. He co-founded the band with singer Freddie Mercury and drummer Roger Taylor. May's guitar work and songwriting contributions were instrumental in making Queen one of the most successful acts in music history. He is known for his virtuoso skills and distinctive sound, often using his home-built electric guitar called the Red Special. May has written numerous hits for Queen, including "We Will Rock You," "I Want It All," "Fat Bottomed Girls," and "The Show Must Go On."
