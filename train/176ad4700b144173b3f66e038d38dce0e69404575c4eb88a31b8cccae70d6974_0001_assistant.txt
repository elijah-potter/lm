"Woof woof," I say, but I don't think my human understands me. I beg my nice and kind owner to roll down the window as my nose is pressed up against the glass. I want to enjoy all the smells. The outside is so green. I can see all the grass and trees and people. My tail won't stop wagging.

We stop. Finally! I can't hold myself still. This is so exciting! I bounce up and down racing between the two sides of the car until my human opens the door for me. I jump out, but she grabs me. That's okay. I don't mind the leash. I just want to get into the park. Even the parking lot smells good. It's so new and unique. I think there have been other dogs here.

I lead the way towards the path, stopping to smell every blade of grass along the way. What's that I hear? A bird chirping? No, it's a squirrel in the tree. I want to go say hello to the squirrel friend and run towards it, but my human pulls me back. "Be a good dog," I remind myself. "Don't go too far too fast."

I slow down my pace before I find the next interesting object: a half-eaten hot dog laying on the ground. I can't even chew it before my human is opening my mouth and taking it out. 
"Woof woof," I say to her, but I don't think she understands just how good it tastes. She never lets me have food on the ground. Oh, well.

I nuzzle her to let her know I am okay. I like the hot dog, but I like her more. I stay by her side, and we walk down the trail. The grass on my paws feels so soft but just a little bristly. On every corner, there is some new smell, some new person. I even hear other dog friends in the distance. I hope I can go say hi to the other dogs. I never want this walk to end!
