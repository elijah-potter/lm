The neon glow of the abandoned subway tunnel pulsed against the damp walls like a slow, dying heartbeat. Dust motes swirled in the flickering light from the flickering bulb above, casting long shadows that seemed to stretch forever. Your boots crunched on broken glass and rusted nails as you moved deeper into the labyrinth, your breath misting in the frigid air. The air smelled of damp earth and something metallic—like old blood.

You shouldn’t be here.

A low hum vibrated through the walls, and suddenly, the tunnel split into two directions. One path led to a dimly lit room where figures moved in the shadows, their faces obscured by hoods. The other led to a crumbling platform where a train sat, its doors half-open, the interior dark and silent. The train was old, its windows fogged with condensation, but the humming grew louder, more insistent.

A voice—distorted, like static through a broken radio—whispered from the shadows: *"You’re not supposed to hear this."*

Your pulse quickened. The choice was yours.
