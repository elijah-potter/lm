To appeal to kids who were watching teen-oriented shows and musically-related cartoons in the late 1960s and early 1970s, certain record labels partnered with Post Cereal and had cutout cardboard-based records embedded into the back of specific cereal boxes. These records had very inferior fidelity and a very short shelf life due to their flimsy packaging. Some records were part of a series; each series had a different label design and there were typically 4 songs to a series. The only way to know which song was going to play was by looking at a stamp on the label that said "This Is Record No. 1, Record No. 2, etc."

Here are 5 recording artists that had Post cereal box records released, along with a cereal they could be found on:

● Bobby Sherman : Cinnamon Raisin Bran
● The Archies : Alpha-Bits
● The Monkees : Rice Krinkles
● The Jackson 5 : Frosted Rice Krinkles
● The Sugar Bears : Super Sugar Crisp
