What if the bridge had been repaired one night before the storm, leaving the riverbed exposed? The current would have weakened the holdfasts, causing the wooden planks to buckle under the weight of the supplies and the crew’s shifting bodies, sending them cascading into the shallows—some landing in the mud, others swept downstream. The lanterns would have guttered out in the damp, and the lanterns of the other ships would have been too far away to see the signal. By the time the relief fleet arrived, the supplies were gone, the men were exhausted, and the only thing left was the rusted anchor and the half-sunk hull of the *Stormchaser*.
