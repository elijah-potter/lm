Across the room, a group of three—one in a sleeveless hoodie, another in a tie that’s half-undone—stares at a half-formed sentence hovering between them. The words *‘I’m sorry’* are barely audible, like a whisper caught in the wind, yet they hang in the space between them, tangible. The first person’s lips part slightly, the second’s jaw clenches, the third’s eyes flicker between them, unraveling.
