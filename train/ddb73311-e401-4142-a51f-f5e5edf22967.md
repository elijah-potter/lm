Ancient Roman ruins in Pompeii, with frescoes and mosaics preserved beneath layers of volcanic ash from the 79 AD eruption of Vesuvius. The preserved plaster casts of people in the streets, including children, depict daily life before the disaster. The well-preserved architectural details, such as the Forum, amphitheater, and homes with frescoes depicting mythological scenes, allow modern visitors to see the architectural and cultural practices of the time.
