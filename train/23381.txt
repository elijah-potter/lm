Can you thaw scallops in the microwave?
Defrost Scallops in the Microwave Put the scallops in a microwave-safe bowl or dish, cover them with a paper towel and set the microwave to the defrost setting, or 30 percent of the regular setting. After 30 seconds, remove the bowl and check the scallops. Defrost them for 30 additional seconds if needed.
