**New Haven, October 12, 2023** – Authorities in New Haven are investigating reports of a rare celestial phenomenon observed overnight. Residents across the city reported seeing an unusual light trail near the old Mill Pond Park, initially described as a glowing, pulsating object before vanishing abruptly. Witnesses, including several park visitors, claimed the sighting coincided with a sudden drop in temperature. Meteorologists attribute the phenomenon to a local atmospheric anomaly, though experts caution that further scientific analysis is required to determine its exact cause. City officials are encouraging residents to submit photos or videos for verification, while park staff remain on standby to ensure visitor safety. No injuries have been reported, but authorities are advising caution near the area until further notice.
