**Scene 1 (Past):**
A damp alleyway glistens under flickering gas lamps. Old man Elias crouches over his dying wife’s body, fingers tracing the lines of her fevered hands. His younger son, Daniel, stands frozen, clutching a broken chess set, the pieces scattered like ghosts. The clock in the town hall strikes midnight; the only sound is the distant hum of a train.

---
**Scene 2 (Present):**
The same alley—now overgrown with ivy. A young woman, Clara, knits silently, her hands trembling. She drops a stitch into the street, where it rolls away into the shadows. A stranger, a man with a newspaper, offers her a job: *"The town hall clock struck midnight three years ago. What happened?"* Clara hesitates, then nods.

---
**Scene 3 (Future):**
A grand library, its shelves lined with books bound in leather. Daniel, now a scholar, reviews an archive. His mother’s journal lies open—pages filled with chess moves, dates, and a single, cryptic note: *"The pieces were never broken."* He follows the last entry to a sealed vault beneath the clock tower.

---
**Scene 4 (Flashback – Clock Tower):**
Inside the tower, Daniel finds his father’s workshop—tools, a half-finished clock face, and a chessboard with one lone knight on the board. The tower’s mechanism stutters; the clock stops. A voice whispers from the gears: *"You always move first."*

---
**Scene 5 (Present – Revelation):**
Clara and Daniel stand in the alley. The stranger’s newspaper is now a map—red lines crisscrossing the town. *"They’re coming,"* Daniel says. Clara realizes: the chess set was a puzzle. Every move was a warning. The past isn’t over.

---
**Scene 6 (Final Twist – Clock Mechanic):**
The clockmaker, Elias, now a man in his 80s, appears. He smiles: *"Daniel, I didn’t die. I was waiting."* The gears turn. The past and future collide in a single moment—Daniel’s father isn’t dead, but *alive*, trapped in the clock’s mechanics. Clara and Daniel must break the cycle before the town’s time runs out. The knight’s last move is on the board.
