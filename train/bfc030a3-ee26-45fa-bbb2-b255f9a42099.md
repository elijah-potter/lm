A weeklong backpacking trip through the Andes revealed the weight of his city life. Every dawn, he woke to mist curling over jaguar paws in the highlands, learning patience in silence. The first time he saw a *condor* circling the ridge, his breath caught—this wasn’t just scenery, it was a mirror. By the third night, he slept without fear, the stars sharp enough to carve into his ribs. The guide said, *"You’ve always been lost, but now you see where you’re going."* The return flight to Bogotá was a blur, but when he boarded, he carried a backpack no longer stuffed with maps, but with the ghost of a sky full of stars.
