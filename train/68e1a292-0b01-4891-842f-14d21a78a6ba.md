Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big tree outside, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had soft brown fur and a big, round nose that always twinkled when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his favorite flower, a bright red poppy, swaying gently in the breeze. "Mmm, I wonder what’s inside that poppy?" he thought. Benny tiptoed to the flower and gently pulled it open.

Inside, there was a tiny, golden seed! Benny picked it up and felt it warm in his paws. "This must be magic," he giggled. He ran back to his home and hid the seed under his blanket.

That night, Benny dreamed of a magical garden where flowers grew taller than mountains. When he woke up, he found the seed still warm. "I’ll make it grow!" Benny said to his stuffed rabbit, Mr. Snugs.

Benny planted the seed in his own little garden. He watered it every day and sang a happy song:

*"A little seed, so small and bright,
Soon you’ll bloom, just wait and see!
Green and yellow, then red so fine,
I’ll plant you here to make you shine!"*

Days passed, and Benny’s garden grew. The golden seed turned into a tiny sprout, then a small green leaf, and soon—oh, look!—a big, bright red flower just like the poppy outside! Benny’s heart swelled with joy. "I did it!" he cheered.

But then, a little mouse named Mimi peeked into Benny’s garden. "Wow!" she squeaked. "That’s the prettiest flower I’ve ever seen!" She nibbled a petal, and suddenly—*poof!*—a tiny, shiny star floated down from the sky! Mimi’s eyes sparkled. "Thank you, Benny!" she said. "You gave me a little magic!"

From that day on, Benny’s garden was always full of happy surprises. Every time he planted a seed, a tiny star would dance down, and everyone in the neighborhood got a little bit happier. Benny learned that magic isn’t just in flowers—it’s in kindness, in sharing, and in believing that even the smallest things can grow into something wonderful!

And Benny? He kept his garden secret, but he made sure to share his magic with all his friends.
