Here is a list of places to visit in or near downtown Chicago that are indoors:

1.	Water Tower Place – This is an interesting vertical shopping mall located along the Magnificent Mile (a great area to shop). It is one of the first vertical malls ever. All the stores are on 8 floors with beautiful glass elevators that have been featured in movies.
2.  Art Institute of Chicago – Located in Grant Park, it is one of the oldest art museums in the world.   It features masterpieces such as Grant Wood’s "American Gothic" and George Seurat’s "A Sunday on La Grande Jatte". Over 300,000 works of art satisfy all types of artistic tastes.
3.  The Field Museum of Natural History – Known for its extensive specimen and artifact collections, this museum has collections of over 24 million specimens and objects. Permanent exhibitions include fossils and world culture.
4. Chicago Theatre – Considerd a Landmark theater on State Street in the Loop area. Check their calendar for plays, musicals, music concerts and comedies.
