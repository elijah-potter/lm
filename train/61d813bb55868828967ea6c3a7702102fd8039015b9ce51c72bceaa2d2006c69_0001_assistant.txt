The best-selling book of all time is the Christian Bible. Although difficult to determine the exact number of copies that have been printed in the roughly 1500 years since its contents were standardized, research conducted by the British and Foreign Bible Society in 2021 suggests that the total number probably lies between 5 and 7 billion copies.

Before printing presses and books were common, the Bible was a rare item due to the time and money required to make such a large book by hand.

During the early 19th century, Bible Societies became more common and ordered Bibles in large quantities, either to sell or give away as part of their mission. A study produced by the British and Foreign Bible Society (founded in 1804) calculated that 2,458,000,000 Bibles were printed between 1815 and 1975.

In the 21st century, Bibles are printed at a rate of roughly 80 million copies per year.
