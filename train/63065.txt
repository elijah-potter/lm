One of a collection of unique, real, functioning wildlife habitats specifically designed for children and to attract beneficial insects to the garden for learning, study, or simply for the benefits of plants pollination and pest control.
The Mini bug Ladybird tower is a natural habitat for Ladybirds and other beneficial insects and is constructed from solid FSC birch logs and Oak, larch or similar timber for durability, the Ladybird tower has a hollow central chamber that can be filled with natural material to provide insulation and security . Intersecting the chamber are many holes drilled into the log at an upward angle, which allow the insects to reach the insulated and safe inner chamber.
Ladybird food / attractant can be used with the tower.
A free 16 page project book comes with the Mini bug Ladybird tower.
