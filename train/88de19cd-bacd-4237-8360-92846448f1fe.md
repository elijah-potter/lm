The repetitive behavior of checking the phone every 15 minutes for notifications started as a way to stay informed about work updates. Over time, it evolved into compulsive scrolling through social media, often leading to prolonged screen time. This habit disrupted sleep patterns due to late-night notifications, causing fatigue and reduced productivity. Eventually, it became a source of anxiety when missing important messages or updates. The mind fixated on the need to respond immediately, reinforcing the cycle. The original intention of efficiency had transformed into a dependency that consumed more time than it saved, impacting both personal and professional life.
