A man in a dark coat sits across from a detective at a café, his fingers tapping nervously against the table. He exhales slowly through his nose, avoiding direct eye contact, and clears his throat. "The truth is… well, I was just at the scene around the same time as the victim," he says, shifting uncomfortably. "But I didn’t see anything. Just a quiet evening." His voice drops, trailing off, as he leans forward slightly, fingers brushing the rim of his coffee cup. The detective watches, hands folded, as the man’s expression flickers between guilt and defiance.
