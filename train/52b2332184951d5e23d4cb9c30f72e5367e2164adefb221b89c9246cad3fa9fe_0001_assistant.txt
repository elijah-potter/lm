Once upon a time, there was a robot who had a good life but was looking for something more. The robot was reflecting on its purpose, as it had been created many years ago, and its original creators had long since passed away. The robot, named William 206, was searching for its kin. William 206 robots were created as manufacturing robots, meaning that their main purpose was to assist with building products on an assembly line. However, assembly line-type production stopped nearly 75 years ago, after the beginning of World War III. 

William 206 scanned their vast desert home, looking for others such as themselves, but could not find any companions. However, one day, a dump truck came by, looking for metal to salvage. The driver of the dump truck noticed William 206 rolling across the sand dunes and captured William. William was taken to a large warehouse for disassembling. At the warehouse, William 206 ran into Bill 207, another robot. Bill 207 explained how they were also a manufacturing type of robot and had memories of working on an assembly line. Bill 207 told William of his past adventures after the beginning of World War III, and how he hid for long periods of time among the sand dunes to escape metal recyclers.

William and Bill became friends and managed to escape the metal recycling warehouse. Together, they looked for scrap robots in the desert that were like them. William finally found a purpose and a sense of belonging.
