**Tropical coastal city (e.g., Phuket, Thailand) during summer:**
28–32°C (82–90°F), high humidity (75–85%), frequent 30-minute thunderstorms with 100+ mm rain daily, ocean at 27–29°C (81–84°F). Warm breezes from monsoon, beach sand 26–28°C (79–82°F). Nighttime drops to 24–26°C (75–79°F) with occasional coastal fog.

**Arid desert (e.g., Death Valley, USA):**
Day: 45–50°C (113–122°F), night: 12–18°C (54–64°F). Extreme diurnal temperature swing; sand dunes 40–45°C (104–113°F) under direct sun. Dewpoint near 0°C (32°F), dry air (10–15% humidity). UV index: 10+.
