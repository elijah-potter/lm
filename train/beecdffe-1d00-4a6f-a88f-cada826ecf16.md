Unseen ruins beneath the cracked pavement where old foundations once stood.
A silent library where books were burned to stop whispers of forbidden knowledge.
A hidden altar carved from black obsidian, its surface etched with warnings in a language no tongue remembers.
The mirror that reflects not faces but empty eyes, staring back from a place where time is a prison.
A door that doesn’t open—only a hollow click when pressed, leading to nothing but the sound of closing.
The forest where trees grow backward, their roots twisted into shapes that hum when touched.
The sky’s reflection in a still pond—mirrored but inverted, as if the world below is upside down.
A well that pours water backward, draining into the earth without a drop escaping.
The name of the place: *Nowhere.*
