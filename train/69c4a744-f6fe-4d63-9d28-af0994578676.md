The map in his hand, half-eaten by the night, showed only one path forward, marked with an unmistakable symbol: the iron gates of the old fortress, where the last of the guild’s secrets lay buried. His fingers tightened around the hilt of his sword, its weight familiar, its edge sharpened by years of training. The alternative—remaining in the quiet town, the one that never dared to ask—felt like a betrayal of everything he had been raised to defend. The silence of the empty street was broken only by the distant echo of his own breath. He had no choice. He had to go.
