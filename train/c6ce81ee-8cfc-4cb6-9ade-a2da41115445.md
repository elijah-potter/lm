**The Post Office Parking Lot Wall**
A weathered, painted surface with faded letters and doodles. Notes pinned to mailboxes, sticky notes on the back of license plates, and scribbles on the glass of parked cars. Some are heartfelt, others cryptic—like *"Meet me at the diner at 8 PM"* or *"Help me move my couch."* A mix of personal and anonymous, often with a coffee-stain or tear-streak. Commonly found on Sundays after church.
