A weightless decision pressed into place by gravity’s hand—
the fork in the road that yawns open, its branches already marked by unseen hands.
The fork where the mind hesitates not, for the choice is carved from the very bone of fate’s blueprint:
*"Stay"* or *"Go,"* but the first syllable of each is already whispered by the past.
The clock, a silent sentinel, ticks backward, and the future is a shadow flickering in the mirror of the present’s resolve.
The decision is not made; it is *unfolded*—like a flower breaking its bud at dawn, inevitable, inevitable.
