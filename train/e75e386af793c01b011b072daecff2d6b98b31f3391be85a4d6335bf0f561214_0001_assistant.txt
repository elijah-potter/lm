Root cellars are food storage area built somewhat underground and utilizes the Earth to preserve temperature and humidity while eliminating light from getting to the stored food. Food storage in this way works best with temperatures between 32 and 40 degrees Fahrenheit with relatively high humidity. By building a root cellar with good ventilation, light filtration, and humidity control, you can ensure stable long-term storage of a variety of foods. The combination of the proper environmental factors allows the decomposition process of food to stop and allows food to be preserved without bacterial growth or drying out. Fresh vegetables that are typically stored in a root cellar are root crops like turnips, parsnips, beets, potatoes, and carrots, but other things like canned food, medicine, and grains are also great options for storing in a root cellar.
