A dim light flickered between the two figures in the old library, casting long shadows across the dusty shelves. The air smelled of aged paper and old coffee. The woman sat with her back to the door, fingers tracing the spine of a leather-bound book. The man stood by the fireplace, his coat half-unbuttoned, his gaze fixed on the fire’s flickering embers.

She didn’t turn around. The book lay open between them, the last page blank. His hand hovered over the edge, then stilled.

A single word—*regret*—was written in the air between them, unspoken but burning like a brand. The fire crackled louder, and the silence stretched, thick with understanding.

She exhaled slowly, then turned slowly, her eyes meeting his. No words needed. The weight of what had been left unsaid hung between them like a ghost.
