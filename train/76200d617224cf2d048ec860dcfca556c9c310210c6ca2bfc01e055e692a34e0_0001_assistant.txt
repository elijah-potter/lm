Skeletons of giants have been found across the world for as long as we have been doing archaeological digs. Evidence of giants has been found in many places, including Ecuador, Dover Mound in Kentucky, New York, South Africa, Death Valley, Lovelock Cave in Nevada, and Wisconsin. Considering how vast these locations are, we can assume that there was a large group of giants that lived, or live, on most habitable continents. The Smithsonian Institute has been exerting extreme effort to cover up any evidence of giants that comes up. In 2015, The Supreme Court ruled in favor of the general public when they forced the Smithsonian Institute to admit that they had been destroying evidence of giants since at least the early 1900s. Whistleblowers from the American Institute of Alternative Archaeology have also come forward to present evidence that the Smithsonian has destroyed tens of thousands of bones that are as large as 12 feet tall. Mainstream archaeologists want this issue covered up. They do not want to admit that their research of the history of humankind may be wrong, but the evidence is against them. The public deserves to know the truth about giants.
