Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around his garden every day. One sunny morning, Benny spotted a shiny red berry on a tall plant. He nibbled it and felt a little sleepy. As he rested under a big sunflower, a tiny mouse named Mia came over.

"Hello, Benny!" said Mia. "I heard you ate a berry. Did you know berries make you sleepy?"

Benny smiled. "Yes! But I don’t want to sleep. I want to play!"

Mia giggled. "Then let’s play a game! I’ll be the mouse, and you’ll be the bunny. We’ll hop and run through the garden."

Benny hopped excitedly. "Okay! But first, let’s find a bigger berry to share!"

They searched together and found a juicy, golden one. Mia chewed it slowly, and Benny hopped around happily. The garden felt warm and cozy with their new friends.

Suddenly, a big, rumbling thunderstorm rolled in! Lightning flashed, and rain poured down. Benny and Mia huddled under their favorite bush.

"Don’t worry," Mia whispered. "Rain makes the flowers grow taller and brighter!"

Benny sniffled. "But what if the rain makes the berries fall?"

Mia patted his head. "No worries! After the rain, the garden will smell the sweetest!"

Soon, the storm passed, and the sun came out again. Benny and Mia played until their paws got tired. As they curled up together, they heard a little bird chirping, "Goodnight, Benny and Mia!"

They smiled. "Goodnight, little bird," they said together, and off they drifted to sleep under the warm grass, dreaming of their next adventure.
