The first record of tofu preparation was made about 2000 years ago by the Chinese Han Dynasty. The invention of tofu is credited to Prince Liu An of Anhui province. It took many centuries for tofu to reach other neighboring countries. Japan was introduced to it in the Nara Period (710-794) and, according to scholars, it reached Vietnam as early as the 10th century. It's speculated that tofu's spread to other parts of Asia coincided with the spread of Buddhism. Since then, tofu has become a protein staple in many countries, and it is common for each region to have variations in production methods, texture, flavor, and usage.
