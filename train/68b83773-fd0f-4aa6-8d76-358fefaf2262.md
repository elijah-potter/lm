**Rules for Ethical Conduct in Digital Engagement:**

1. **Respect Boundaries** – Avoid invasive or discriminatory content, language, or behavior.
2. **Fact-Check** – Ensure claims are accurate before sharing; cross-reference reliable sources.
3. **No Harmful Misinformation** – Do not disseminate false, misleading, or harmful information.
4. **Transparency** – Disclose affiliations, sponsorships, or biases when relevant.
5. **Privacy Protection** – Do not expose personal or sensitive data without consent.
6. **Anti-Discrimination** – Avoid perpetuating hate speech, bias, or exclusionary rhetoric.
7. **No Violations of Laws** – Comply with all applicable legal and regulatory standards.
8. **Responsible Tone** – Maintain a constructive, professional, and respectful tone.
9. **No Plagiarism** – Credit sources and avoid unauthorized content replication.
10. **Sustainability Awareness** – Minimize environmental impact in digital communication.
