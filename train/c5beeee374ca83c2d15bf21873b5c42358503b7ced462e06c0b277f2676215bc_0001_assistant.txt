La Haine, french for 'Hatred', is a 1995 crime drama written and directed by Mathieu Kassovitz. The film follows a day in the life of three friends that live in a poor immigrant neighborhood in Paris. The film would go on to win Kassovitz the Best Director award at the 1995 Cannes Film Festival. 

The film would go on to open number one at the French Box office. Making 12.5 million francs, La Haine would become France's 14 highest-grossing film of the year.
