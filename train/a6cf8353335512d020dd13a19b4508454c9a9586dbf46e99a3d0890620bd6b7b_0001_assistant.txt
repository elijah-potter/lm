Here is a simple diet plan that consists of breakfast, lunch, dinner, and a snack that is well-balanced, nutritious, and fits your daily calories:

Breakfast:
3 slices of cooked bacon
2-egg omelet with mozzarella cheese, half a large mushroom, one-eighth bell pepper, one slice of tomato, and baby spinach
1 slice of whole wheat toast with butter

Lunch:
3/4 cup dry whole grain elbow macaroni, boiled in water
3/4 cup tomato sauce
Baby spinach
Mozzarella cheese
4 ounces of lean protein (chicken, beef, pork, or fish)

Dinner:
1 meal-replacement protein shake
2 large frozen strawberries
5 ounces of almond milk

Snack:
1 chocolate candy bar
