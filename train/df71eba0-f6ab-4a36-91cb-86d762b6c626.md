**Clang of a bell tolling at dusk** – sharp, metallic, resonant, followed by a single, prolonged note that lingers like an echo. The second chime is deeper, deliberate, a beat that marks a boundary between past and present. The third is softer, almost hesitant, as if the speaker hesitates before declaring what comes next. The final toll, abrupt and sustained, cuts through the evening silence like a blade, its duration measured in seconds, not minutes. The vibration in the bones of those who hear it—an ache, not pain—lingers for hours, a physical reminder of the weight of what was said.
