The room was too loud. The hum of the city outside, the whispers of conversations carried on the breeze through cracked windows, the relentless rhythm of a metronome counting down the seconds of their life. She stood at the edge of a balcony, fingers digging into the railing as the wind tugged at her coat.

A single sentence, barely audible, escaped her lips before she could stop it: *"I choose silence."*

The words hung in the air, unspoken. The city exhaled around her. She turned away, no longer listening to the chatter, the laughter, the endless stream of noise. The world blurred into a white noise of her own making.

Outside, the city continued its relentless march, but inside, she was nothing. Just silence.
