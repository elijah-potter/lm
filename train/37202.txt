Phonics and Reading Schemes
In Key Stage One we use Letters and Sounds to plan and teach our phonics. This is supplemented with a range of other resources and activities.
We use the Oxford Reading Tree, Collins Big Cats, Project X and Rising Stars Reading Planet schemes for reading.
We follow the Oxfordshire Agreed Syllabus. Our scheme of work is provided by the Diocese of Oxford.
