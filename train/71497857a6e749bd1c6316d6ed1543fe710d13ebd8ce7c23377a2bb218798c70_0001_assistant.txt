Goldilocks got up from Baby Bear's perfect chair to find a bed to take a nap. In the bedroom, she found three beds. On the biggest bed, she found an iPad and thought, "I can play some games before I fall asleep!" She turned it on, but it had the facial recognition option turned on, and it didn't recognize her face. She set it aside on the bedside table and went to the second, medium-sized, bed. An iPad was sitting on that bed, too. "Maybe this one will work," she thought. But when she turned it on, it asked for a four-digit passcode. Frustrated, she flung it across the bed. Then she saw a third bed, closer to her size, and noticed it also had an iPad on it. When she turned it on, it didn't have any security turned on at all! She saw that it had a lot of games on it, and she sat on the bed and started playing a game that looked fun.

After playing for a while, Goldilocks heard voices, but she was enjoying her game so much, she didn't pay any attention to them. The voices got louder, but she had completely tuned them out. She had almost finished the bonus round! Three bears walked into the bedroom. The biggest bear, Daddy Bear, went to his bed and noticed his iPad had been moved. "Somebody's been playing on my iPad!" he roared. The Mama Bear went to her bed and saw her iPad had been moved. "Somebody's been playing on MY iPad!" she exclaimed. The smallest bear, Baby Bear, walked to his bed and pointed, yelling, "Somebody's been playing on my iPad, and there she is!"

At the same instant, Goldilocks finished the bonus round and looked up to see what the commotion was about. She was startled to find three bears in the bedroom with her. She screamed, dropped the iPad, and ran outside into the woods. She ran straight home and never went into that area of the forest ever again.
