A sunrise that lingers like a whisper before the day takes breath.
Morning coffee black, the cup too small for the endless cup of time we drink.
The train whistle at dawn—only 7:00 AM, and already the day is a sprint.

Office hours stretch like a marathon, the clock a silent judge. Lunch is a pause, but the mind races ahead, rewinding the day’s first seconds, replaying the morning’s first thought.

Evening fades too fast—dinner with friends, laughter like stars in a darkening sky. The bed calls, but sleep feels like a distant horizon, a promise of rest that never quite arrives.

The moon rises, a silver thread in the night sky, marking how much more of this day we’ll spend chasing what feels like forever.
