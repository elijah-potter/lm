A vast, shifting expanse of liquid silver and swirling storms, where light bends like a mirage into endless blue, reflecting fractured, ever-moving shards of a thousand suns. Cities of obsidian and glass float like frozen dreams, their spires piercing the mist like the teeth of a sleeping beast. Rivers of molten gold weave through the ruins of forgotten empires, their banks lined with statues of laughing gods and screaming giants. The air hums with the static of forgotten voices, and the earth itself seems to breathe—a slow, rhythmic pulse of heat and shadow.
