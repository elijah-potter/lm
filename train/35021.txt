The presentations by Juliette Green stress play and interaction with the environment as vital opportunities for children to learn.
Juliette holds a bachelor's degree in Applied Environmental and Resource Science as well as a Primary Education PGCE. She works for the website for the charity National Association for Environmental Education. She also authored the book 'Learning Outside of the Classroom series,' which highlights the benefits for young children and students who move outside of the classroom to learn and interact.
She is currently a teacher in Birmingham.
The presentations by Juliette Green draw from her career as an elementary school teacher, using firsthand experience to suggest alternate forms of learning in children, including the concept of moving the classroom outside.
