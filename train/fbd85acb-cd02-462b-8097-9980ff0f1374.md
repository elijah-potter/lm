In a hollowed-out village nestled between mist-shrouded mountains, the last lantern flickered like a dying ember. The streets, once bustling, were now silent, save for the distant cry of a crow. Across the crooked bridge, the old clock tower stood sentinel, its hands frozen at 3:17.

A figure in a tattered coat knelt beside a half-buried chest, fingers tracing the rusted rivets. The air smelled of damp earth and something metallic—like old blood. "They said this was cursed," whispered the figure, voice trembling. "But I need to know what’s inside."

The chest creaked open, revealing a single, perfect image:

*A woman with hollow cheeks and eyes like polished obsidian, her mouth stretched in a silent scream. Behind her, a forest of blackened trees stretched toward a sky choked with swirling, shadowed shapes. The wind howled, and the image seemed to breathe.*

The figure gasped, clutching their throat. The lantern above them extinguished, plunging them into darkness—except for the single, burning image now standing in their hands.
