4. H&M: H&M is a Swedish-based multinational clothing retailer that offers affordable and trendy clothing, shoes, and accessories for men, women, and children. It also offers frequent sales and free shipping on orders over $40.
5. Nasty Gal: Nasty Gal is a US-based online fashion retailer that offers trendy clothing, shoes, and accessories for women. It offers affordable prices and frequent sales, and free shipping on orders over $150.
6. Zara: Zara is a Spanish-based fast fashion retailer that offers affordable and trendy clothing, shoes, and accessories for men, women, and children. It is known for its fast turnover of styles and designs and offers free shipping on orders over $50.
7. Shein: Shein is a China-based online retailer that offers trendy and affordable clothing, shoes, and accessories for women, men, and children. It is known for its fast turnover of styles and designs and offers frequent sales and free shipping on orders over $49.
