**The waiting room hummed with the faint scent of stale coffee and old newspapers.** A lone figure sat at a worn wooden table, fingers tracing the rim of a half-empty mug. A single phone lay open beside them, screen glowing with a static-laden news feed. The headline flickered: *"Breaking: Emergency Alert—All Evacuations in Effect."*

Across the room, a woman in a raincoat leaned against the wall, her breath visible in the cold air. She watched the room, her eyes darting toward the door as sirens wail in the distance. The clock above their heads ticked relentlessly, each second stretching into eternity.

Outside, the city was alive—laughter, sirens, the distant chatter of crowds—but inside, the air felt thick with tension. The phone buzzed. A text from an unknown number:

**"They’re coming. Meet at the old church. 3 PM."**
