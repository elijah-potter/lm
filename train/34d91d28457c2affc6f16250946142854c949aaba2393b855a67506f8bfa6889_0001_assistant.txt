Movie nights are always fun and I would be happy to help you with some ideas. Here are a few Christian movies that teens might enjoy:

1. New Life - A story about life and love, and navigating the challenges of both with hope through good times as well as bad times.
2. The Heart of Man - A classic prodigal son story that deals with subjects like freedom, victory, and hope, even through brokenness and shame.
3. I Can Only Imagine - Follows the relationship of an abusive father and his son after terminal illness bringing them together, and their journey to mend the past.
4. Paul, Apostle of Christ - A deep dive into the life and struggles of Biblical characters Paul and Luke and how their lives birthed the Early Church.
5. An Interview With God - A journalist who is struggling with a personal crisis is presented with an opportunity to interview a man who says he is God.
6. Indivisible - After an army chaplain is deployed to Iraq for some time, he must return home and fight again, this time to save his marriage relationship.
7. Unbroken - Despite the horrible things experienced during his time serving as a soldier in World War 2, a man must learn to forgive as part of his newly found faith.
