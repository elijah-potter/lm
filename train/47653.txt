Peace First is an organization which empowers students to create their own safe classroom and teaches young people to adapt and respond to complex challenges through creative solutions, building courageous and confident students in school and the community.
23:59 | 2012
In many of America’s schools, violence poses a significant threat to children’s safety and performance in class. Eric Dawson is counteracting this with a program that engages youth in peacemaking and conflict resolution. Peace First brings together students, their families, teachers, and volunteers to collectively change the status quo, reject violence and build peaceful relationships.
