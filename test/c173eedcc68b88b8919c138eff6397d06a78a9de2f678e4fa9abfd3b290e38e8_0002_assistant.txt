The Montage Kapalua Bay in Maui, Hawaii is one of the best beaches for a family getaway. This resort features luxurious one-to-four bedroom amenities, as well as multiple swimming pools, and access to the pristine Kapalua Bay Beach. While parents can enjoy lounging by the pool or spending time at Spa Montage, the kids can spend their day at Paintbox children's program, which is specifically designed for kids ages five to twelve.
