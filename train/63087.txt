As an avid foreign language learner, I believe that reading classical literature is an essential element on the way to mastery. However, it’s easy to get discouraged by the complexity of the text and a big amount of new words.
This reading guide is meant to help you focus on a limited number of new vocabulary, as well as to foster a better understanding of the novel.
It consists of 16 units, each of them containing vocabulary exercises and comprehension questions.
The guide assignments can be used both for self-study and in the classroom.
