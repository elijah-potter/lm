The golden sunrise spills a molten amber across rolling hills, painting them in warm hues of rust and ochre. The sky, a bruised violet, stretches like a deepening wound, where fleeting streaks of crimson and violet dance like scattered embers. Dawn’s first glow filters through the sparse trees, casting long, gilded shadows on damp earth. Twilight’s last whisper lingers in the air, a soft, fading glow that clings to the edges of the world like smoldering smoke. Moonlight, pale and ghostly, weaves through the night like silver mist, softening sharp contours into liquid, ghostly shapes. Stars pulse faintly—dim, distant sparks against the velvet canvas of endless dark.
