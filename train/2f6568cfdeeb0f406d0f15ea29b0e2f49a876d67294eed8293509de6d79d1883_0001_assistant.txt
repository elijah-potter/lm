• Olympus Mons - Olympus Mons is the largest volcano in our solar system. It's located in the Tharsis region and is about the same size as the state of Arizona. Olympus Mons is also a gigantic shield volcano, which will make it easier for future explorers to climb. Its height of 16 miles (25 kilometers) is almost three times the height of Mount Everest which is close to 5.5 miles high.
• Valles Marineris - Valles Marineris is the largest canyon in our solar system. The canyon is roughly 1850 miles (3000 km) long, which is about four times longer than the Grand Canyon. Researchers are yet to determine how Valles Marineris came to be. 
• Gale Crater - Gale Carter was made famous by the landing of the Curiosity Rover in 2012. Gale Crater is known for its wealth of evidence of past water that once existed on Mars.
