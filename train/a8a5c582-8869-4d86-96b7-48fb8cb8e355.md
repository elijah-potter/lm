The abandoned clock tower at the edge of Blackthorn Hollow stands at the heart of the anomaly. Its brass face is cracked, the hands frozen at 11:11, though the sun sets at 6:30 PM every evening. Locals whisper of the "Hollow Hour"—when the tower’s chimes echo backward, playing the same second note *twice* in quick succession. Time there bends like taffy: a child’s birthday cake is always *one day older* than it should be, while the town’s blacksmith’s forge burns coals that never quite cool, perpetually smoldering at the edge of embers. The river below mirrors the sky but upside-down, and the riverbank grows moss that thickens by the hour. No one knows why, but the Hollow’s air hums with a static that makes time feel like a stretched rubber band.
