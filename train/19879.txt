What is Augmented Reality?
Augmented reality (AR) has become one of the fastest growing fields of technology. Augmented Reality turns the environment around you into a digital interface by placing virtual objects in the real world, in real-time. Augmented Reality can be used on mobile devices like smartphones and tablets. Augmented Reality is a very new trend in the context of Pakistan.
Augmented reality is of huge benefits to man. It makes life’s activities easy and fun. One of such areas where AR drives massive benefits is in education. Today, augmented reality has transformed the educational sector. It has continued to expand into all areas of the learning process. The benefit of AR in education is beyond description due to its tremendous impacts. It is our believe that AR will continue to transform education and the learning process.
World leaders believe that Augmented Reality is going to change the world altogether. In coming years, it will be a necessity of everyday life and we will wonder how we ever lived without it.
