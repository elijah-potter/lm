**BREAKING NEWS FROM PORTLAND, ORGONOMICS – LOCAL EXPLORER DISCOVERS MYSTERIOUS ORGANIC GROWTHS ALONG THE COAST**

Eyewitnesses describe the formations as "living" structures, with roots that appear to absorb sunlight directly from the air. The Oregon Department of Agriculture has launched a preliminary investigation, though no official explanation has been provided. Meanwhile, a small group of scientists from the nearby **University of Portland Research Institute** has begun analyzing samples, though results remain preliminary. Authorities urge residents to avoid contact with the growths, which emit an unusual, faint humming sound at night.

The city’s mayor has called for a public hearing tomorrow to discuss long-term measures, though no specific plans have been announced. The phenomenon remains classified as "unidentified organic growth" for now.
