MyPlate, MyState connects Americans with the foods and flavors grown in their states and regions. Use the information below to find out how the foods grown and raised by Arizona producers can help create a healthy eating style and support local farmers and communities!

Did You Know?
- Up to 25% of schools in Arizona are using local and regional foods to serve healthy, MyPlate-inspired meals to kids. That’s almost $7.5 million invested in the local economy!
