Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a garden full of flowers, there lived a tiny bunny named Pip who loved to hop and play.

One sunny morning, Pip noticed his favorite flower, the Sunflower, was wilting. He hopped to the garden and asked, "Why are you so sad, Sunflower?"

The Sunflower looked up and said, "The wind is blowing away my petals, and I don't have enough sun to keep me strong."

Pip knew just what to do. He gathered all his friends—Lulu the ladybug, Max the mouse, and Daisy the daisy—and said, "Let's help the Sunflower!"

Together, they blew gently on the wind, and the petals fluttered back to life. Pip sat next to the Sunflower, and the sun shone brightly, giving it extra strength. Soon, the Sunflower was as happy and bright as ever, turning its big, happy face toward the sky.

As the sun set, the flowers twinkled in the golden light, and Pip knew that teamwork and kindness could make even the smallest garden the happiest place in the world. They all snuggled up together, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures.
