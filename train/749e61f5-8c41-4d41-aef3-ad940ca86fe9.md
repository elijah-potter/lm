**Library Reading Room Corner**
A weathered wooden board mounted beside a stack of mismatched chairs, surrounded by flickering fluorescent lights. Notches in the wood mark popular spots; sticky notes and crumpled paper litter the floor. Handwritten messages—advice, jokes, and "Meet me here at 3 PM?"—stick to the wall. A small chalkboard near the entrance lists upcoming events and "Found" items, with a few cryptic doodles. The scent of aged paper and coffee lingers in the air.
