Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big yellow door, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy pink ears and a tail that wiggled when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to play. He saw a shiny red ball rolling near a tree. "Oh, what a beautiful ball!" Benny thought. He picked it up and started to roll it back and forth, laughing with joy.

Just then, Benny spotted a tiny blue bird sitting on a branch. "Hello, little bird!" Benny said. The bird chirped and fluttered its wings. Benny wanted to help, so he gently placed the ball near the bird. "Here, take it!" Benny said, smiling.

The bird caught the ball and flew away with a happy chirp. Benny watched it disappear into the sky. He knew he should stay near home, so he hopped back inside.

When Benny came back inside, his mommy said, "Benny, why are you so happy today?" Benny grinned and said, "Because I shared my ball with the bird and made new friends!" His mommy hugged him and said, "That’s the best way to be a good friend, just like you!"

Benny curled up in his bed and dreamed about his new friend the bird and all the fun adventures he’d have tomorrow. And that’s how Benny learned that sharing and kindness made everything magical.
