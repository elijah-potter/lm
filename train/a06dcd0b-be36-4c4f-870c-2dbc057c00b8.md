*A crumbling stone bridge, half-sunk in mist, arches over a narrow, unseen chasm. Its weathered planks groan underfoot, each step whispering secrets of forgotten storms. The air hums with static—static of half-remembered conversations, half-buried objects, and the faint scent of damp earth and old fire. To the left, the path splits: one fork leads to a crumbling archway where the light bends unnaturally, as if the walls themselves are shifting. The other descends into a pool of black water where ripples disturb something long dead. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, and the silence isn’t quiet—it’s holding its breath. Somewhere beyond, a door swings open, then shuts behind unseen hands. You know you shouldn’t linger.*
