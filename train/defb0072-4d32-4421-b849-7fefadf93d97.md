**Daily Routine Schedule**

**Morning:**
- **7:00 AM** – Wake up, hydrate, light stretching
- **7:30 AM** – Breakfast (e.g., oatmeal with berries)
- **8:30 AM** – Exercise (30 min yoga or jog)
- **9:00 AM** – Shower, skincare routine

**Midday:**
- **12:00 PM** – Lunch (e.g., grilled chicken salad)
- **1:00 PM** – Workout break (short walk, meditation)

**Afternoon:**
- **3:00 PM** – Deep work session (1.5 hrs, focused task)
- **4:30 PM** – Coffee break, quick stretch

**Evening:**
- **6:00 PM** – Dinner (e.g., stir-fried veggies with tofu)
- **7:30 PM** – Relaxation (reading, podcast)
- **9:00 PM** – Wind down (no screens), sleep by 10:30 PM

---
**Flexible Notes:**
- Adjust timings based on energy levels.
- **Weekly:** 1x meal prep (Sunday), 1x gym session (3x/week).
