"Mr. Gibson, I need you to calm down," the nurse said as paramedics pushed the bleeding man through to the trauma room.

"What have we got?" A junior doctor asked the nurse as the patient was transferred from the gurney onto the bed.

"Multiple stab wounds to the abdomen, chest, and arms," one of the paramedics that had brought the patient in responded.

"Hello, Andy. My name is Doctor Lowrie, and we will be taking care of you today." A policeman rushed into the room, flashing his badge at the nurse. "Excuse me, sir, but what are you doing?" the doctor said in shock.

The officer shouldered past one of the paramedics and grabbed the barely conscious man by the wrist. With a distinct click, he chained the man's arm to the leg of the operating table.

'It’s for your own safety, doc," the officer said, shrugging his equipment-laden belt higher on his waist. "This guy killed a cop."

"His BP is dropping, doctor," the nurse said urgently as they finished attaching electrodes to the patient. 

"We need to stop this bleeding immediately," the doctor said, snapping on a pair of gloves as two more doctors rushed into the room. 

"We’re losing him, Doctor!"
