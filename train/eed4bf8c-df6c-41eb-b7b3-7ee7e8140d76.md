**Scene 2 – Present: The Rainforest**
Dr. Mara Voss stood in the flooded ruins of an ancient temple, her flashlight cutting through the murk. A low hum pulsed beneath her boots. A holographic projection flickered to life above her: a distorted map of a *Hollow Crown* embedded in the jungle’s core. The air smelled of damp earth and something metallic. Her comm crackled—*"Report."*

**Scene 3 – Future: The Last Broadcast**
