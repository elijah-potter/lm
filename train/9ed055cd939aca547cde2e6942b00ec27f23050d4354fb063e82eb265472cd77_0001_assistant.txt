Tabby listened for a noise that would tell them if the ghost was gone, but all they could hear was the whoosh of the cold wind. Even the wind sounded eerie tonight, making sounds like a ghost. It was dark and way past bedtime. Surely someone would come looking for Tabby soon. Would they believe them when Tabby said they'd seen a ghost? Probably not. Nobody ever believed in ghost stories. But now Tabby knew the tales of the cemetery ghost were real. Maybe there was more than one ghost. After all, it WAS a cemetery. They shuddered at the thought. No, they had to believe in just one ghost for now. The other ghosts could wait - if they even existed. 

It was a long while before Tabby was brave enough to poke their head out from the hiding spot. There was movement just to the left and Tabby pulled their head back in, heart thumping. When they didn't hear anything, they cautioned another peek. It was only a branch, bending under the weight of a resting crow. Tabby looked around and didn't see the ghost anywhere. For a moment, they started to doubt they'd seen it at all. But no, Tabby had seen it for real. They remembered its strange transparent glow, the ghost's creepy floating, and its shocked-looking face. Tabby almost ran back into hiding at the thought of it. Slowly, Tabby crept through the cemetery heading for home. Now they face another scary thought - Mom was going to be mad.
