The moment slipped away like sand through fingers—
a whispered apology to the woman whose laughter had turned to tears at the café, her hand brushing mine too late to save the warmth.
A half-smile, a glance—then the door closed behind us, sealing the mistake in the hum of the subway’s metal treads.
Regret, sharp as a blade, was swallowed by the city’s relentless rhythm, a ghost in the corner of my eye, never to be named again.
