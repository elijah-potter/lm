Hey all, I just wanted to let you folks know about a new scam that seems to be making the round online lately. It's called "the Megan Fox Oreo scam".

People are having their Facebook pages defaced by scammers who are posting images of the actress Megan Fox eating an Oreo cookie on users' timelines. This image is associated with a scam where posting the image on a user's timeline "marks" them for attack by hackers who gain the person's personal information (name, phone number, address) and use those details to find their SSNs and associated bank accounts.

No one knows why the image is used or how it relates to the scam itself, but so far 15 people who had their Facebook accounts hacked also had money taken from their bank accounts; furthermore, all 15 people who were scammed reported having the Megan Fox Oreo image posted by an anonymous account to their timelines before being hacked.

Here are some things you can do to ensure you are properly protected from this scam:

1. Monitor your Facebook page - be on the lookout for any suspicious activity, friend requests from people you don't know, etc.
2. Turn on notifications - keep them on so that when someone posts something to your timeline you will be alerted.
3. Change your passwords frequently - consider changing the passwords for all your accounts that contain sensitive information (especially bank accounts) so hackers will have a more difficult time getting to your money).
4. If the Megan Fox Oreo image appears on your timeline, report it to Facebook Technical Support, so they can identify and track the individual(s) associated with the account.
