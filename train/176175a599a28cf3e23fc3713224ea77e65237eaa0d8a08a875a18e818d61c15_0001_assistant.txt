The gunfight at O.K. Corral in Tombstone, Arizona took place on October 26, 1881. The fight began when the Earp brothers and Doc Holiday attempted to disarm Billy Clanton and Frank McLaury. Once the dust settled, Billy Clanton as well as Frank and Tom McLaury were dead. The thirty-second shootout is regarded as one of the most famous shootouts in the history of the Old West and has inspired countless stories and films.
