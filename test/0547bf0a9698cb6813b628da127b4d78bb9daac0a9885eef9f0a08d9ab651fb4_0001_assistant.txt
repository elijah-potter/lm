The first scissors known to historians were created and used by Egyptians about 3,000 to 4,000 years ago! While we have many styles of scissors today that are made for a wide range of purposes, these first scissors were of the "spring scissor" style, which is still made to this day. The artifact discovered as the first pair of scissors is made with two bronze blades connected by a thin, curved bronze strip at the base.
When considering our modern-day "pivoted" scissor, which consists of two blades joined at a pivot, this style was created by the ancient Romans around 100AD.
