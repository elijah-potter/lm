A tongue piercing takes around 3-4 weeks to heal. There are necessary steps to take and cautions to adhere to to make the healing time proceed as normal. Here are some tips:

- Rinse your tongue after eating and before bed with warm salt water or antibacterial mouthwash
- Don't kiss anyone
- Don't share eating utensils or cups
- Eat your food in small bites
- Avoid spicy, acidic, or salty food and drinks
- Avoid hot drinks like tea, coffee, or hot chocolate
