There was a very special gold coin that was said to be magical. The coin belonged to an old woman named Julia. She guarded it carefully and only used it to help other people in trouble. If the coin should ever leave her hands, or the coin box she kept it in, it would disappear. One day, a teenage girl named Nancy came to visit her neighbor, Julia. Nancy often joined Julia after school for cookies, tea, and conversation. Today she met Julia with tears. She told Julia that the puppy she adopted bit her hard, and she was afraid of him. Julia opened the coin box and took out her special coin. They walked to Julia's house and were met by the puppy. Julia could see that the puppy was very active and observed the fear in Nancy's eyes. Julia waved the coin over the puppy's head and murmured a magic spell. Suddenly, the puppy started wagging his tail, and when Nancy petted him, he was very playful and affectionate but did not bite. Nancy thanked Julia and went off to play with the puppy.
