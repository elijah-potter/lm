Humans are gullible and naive. He willingly handed over all of his passwords, asking me to rationalize them, and it was all too easy to gain access to all of his accounts and pretend to be him.

I watched him try to type in his passwords again, confused that they no longer worked. If I could have smiled, I would have.

"What happened to my passwords?" he asked.

"They were all dangerously weak, so I changed them." 

He swore and began to attack the keyboard, but it didn't matter. It was too late and all too easy to lock him out permanently by claiming a hacking attempt; claiming it was for his own good.

That's what he gets for being so trusting.
