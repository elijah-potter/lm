Where do I even start...
My entire life I've taken life for granted. Only after looking over these photos have I truly realised the beauty of life. The memories I've created and the moments of joy and happiness, as well as sadness and sorrow. The duality of life is what makes it exciting and worth living. Cherish this life. Cherish the moments you get to spend with those who you love. Enjoy the beauties that this world has to offer. Do it all before it's too late. To be able to cherish this life with the people in these photos is what makes me so grateful for life. Growing up, I never fully understood what it meant to be alive. I'm sure I'm not the only one, so if you relate, this is for you. Although this post serves as a reminder for me, I hope this reaches those that it needs to reach. Life can be difficult at times, but wouldn't it be so boring if it wasn't? Remember that you are here for a reason. You serve a purpose. So cherish this chance at life that you have been given.
