When you set foot into Sawdune, you can almost feel the air thicken around you. They say it's because of the swampland nearby, but I wouldn't try to convince yourself of that unless you can't stomach the thought of there being something more out there. Still, it's a quaint little town. People are nice enough if you call minding their own business a nicety. Pleasantries are a luxury here; it's better just to stay away from the shadows and go about your daily routine. 

If the town feels sluggish or stuck in the past, there's a reason. They're watching from the treelines, but from that distance, they can't see all too well. Still, if you should be frolicking about carelessly, they'll spot you moving, and once they do, they won't forget you. No sudden moves now.
