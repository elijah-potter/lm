Architect: a profession with a bright future
The task of architects is to plan buildings and urban development activities and to monitor the related construction processes. Their area of responsibility comprises both residential buildings and commercial properties. The architect’s profession covers a very broad range of tasks and has many facets. Find out more about the different areas of tasks, the salary and the career opportunities of architects.
Which opportunities do architects have on the labour market?
Architects are highly sought-after. Due to the ever-changing construction requirements and social environment, their profession is subject to constant change. Currently, for example, the demand for experts in green building is growing. Besides, social aspects are also becoming more and more important. Thus, architects are required to design buildings which are both functional and comfortable. Architects who are specialised in a certain area, such as building design, site supervision or construction planning, have the best career prospects.
