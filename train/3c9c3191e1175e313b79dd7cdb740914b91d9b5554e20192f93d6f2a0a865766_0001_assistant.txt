Once upon a time, in the deep blue sea near Portugal and Spain, there lived a special group of whales called killer whales or orcas. These orcas were known for their beautiful black-and-white patterns and their playful nature. But something strange was happening. Over the past three years, these orcas had been causing trouble for boats sailing in their waters. The orcas would approach the boats and occasionally even bite their rudders, making it challenging for the boats to steer. Can you imagine that? Some of the boats were so badly damaged that they were unable to even move! When Alfredo López was researching these orcas, he discovered that nearly 20% of their attacks rendered the boats unusable.

Each person was confused.  Why were the orcas acting so naughtily? The scientists had two theories. One theory was that the orcas had devised a brand-new sport to engage in with the boats. Just like you know how we create new games from time to time just for fun. These orcas may have followed suit. The alternative theory suggested that perhaps the orcas were acting defensively because they were trying to remember something bad that had happened to them in the past involving a boat.

And so, every day, as the sun set and the stars came out, the orcas and the people continued their adventures in the big, beautiful ocean, waiting to unravel the mystery of the playful orcas. The end.
