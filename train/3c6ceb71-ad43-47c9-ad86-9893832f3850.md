To the silent room where you listen,

I write not for fame, nor for praise,
but for the echo of a name you carry—
one whispered in the wind, or half-remembered,
a ghost in the corner of your mind.
You are the one who stays when all else flees,
the one who holds space for stories unsaid.

I do not ask you to believe.
I do not demand you respond.
I only ask that you keep this letter
as you might keep a promise—
not to fulfill, but to hold.
And if you choose to do so,
then I have written for the right reasons.

The rest is yours to carry.
