A fleeting vow whispered between strangers at a crowded café,
*"I’ll send you the map to the hidden beach—just once."* The smile lingered, fingers brushing the rim of their coffee cup, before the hum of the crowd swallowed the words.

Years later, the map was lost in a storm, its edges frayed with rain. The beach remained a ghost of promises unkept, a salt-kissed memory tied to a name no one remembered.
