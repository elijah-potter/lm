**BREAKING:** Mayor’s Office Announces Emergency Climate Adaptation Plan for **New Haven Heights**
As rising sea levels and more frequent storms threaten coastal infrastructure, New Haven Heights officials have unveiled a multi-phase adaptation strategy. Phase One includes sandbag distribution for flood zones and elevated road repairs near the marina. Public workshops will assess long-term solutions, including a proposed "Green Buffer" along the shoreline. Residents are urged to report structural vulnerabilities by December 15.

**Local Schools to Close for Emergency Drills**
Due to worsening storm warnings, all public schools in New Haven Heights will suspend classes starting Tuesday afternoon. Authorities advise evacuating low-lying areas by 4 PM. Emergency shelters will operate at the city’s recreation center and community center.

**Energy Crisis Alert**
Power outages in neighborhoods east of Main Street persist since Friday’s storm. The municipal utility reports 12% of households still without electricity. Replacements for damaged substations are expected by Saturday evening. Gas rationing remains in effect for high-risk zones.
