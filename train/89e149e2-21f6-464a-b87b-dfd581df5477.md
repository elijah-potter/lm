A neon-lit bar hums with the murmur of late-night conversations. A single stool near the jukebox is occupied by an older man, his fingers tracing the rim of his whiskey glass. The bartender slides a second drink across the counter, and the man nods, eyes half-lidded, lost in thought.

Then—suddenly—laughter erupts from the corner booth. A group of three people, laughing so hard they’re nearly on their feet, spill drinks and collapse into giggles. The man’s head snaps up, his expression shifting from confusion to curiosity. The bartender raises an eyebrow, wiping a spill with a rag. A waitress stumbles out of the kitchen, her face flushed, clutching her side.

The laughter fades, replaced by a hushed exchange as the group straightens up, hands shaking slightly. The man watches them, amused, before turning back to his drink—now half-empty. The bar’s quiet returns, but the scene lingers in the air, a brief, unexpected spark of chaos.
