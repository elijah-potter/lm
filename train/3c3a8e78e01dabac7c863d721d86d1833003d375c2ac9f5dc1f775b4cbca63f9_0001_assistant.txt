Here are some options for your dog that don't involve dropping them off at a kennel:

- Many hotels and Airbnb hosts are now dog-friendly. Consider taking your furry friend along on your trip if you’re able. 

- Use a pet-sitting website to find a qualified sitter to stay at your home. Sites like Rover allow you to see the ratings and backgrounds of all potential sitters. 

- Ask a trusted friend or family member to stay at your house. This can make you and your pet feel most at ease while you’re away. 

- Ask a neighbor to check in on your dog multiple times per day. Having someone close by will allow for easy check-ins and availability in case of an emergency.
