Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white fur and a big round nose that sparkled when he sniffed the flowers.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and hopped outside. The grass was soft and green, and the daisies were dancing in the breeze. "What a beautiful day!" Benny said happily.

Suddenly, Benny saw a tiny blue butterfly fluttering near a big red apple tree. "Hello, little butterfly!" Benny called out. The butterfly landed on Benny’s nose and said, "Hello, Benny! I’m lost. My family is somewhere near the meadow."

Benny’s eyes got big. "Oh no! You poor thing!" He hopped toward the meadow, where the flowers grew tall and swayed. Along the way, Benny met a kind old turtle named Tilly.

"Where’s your family, little butterfly?" Tilly asked.

The butterfly fluttered and said, "They’re near the big, shiny river where the fish swim!"

Benny and Tilly hopped faster. They crossed the river, and there, by a bubbly spring, was Benny’s favorite friend—a little mouse named Milo.

"Milo!" Benny called. "We found you!"

Milo squeaked, "But I’m not lost! I just wanted to play!"

Benny and Tilly smiled. "We were looking for your family!" Milo’s eyes sparkled. "Oh, you’re right! My big sister, Lila, is here!"

Lila was a big brown rabbit with a kind heart. She said, "Thank you, Benny! You saved the day!"

From that day on, Benny, Tilly, and Milo became the best of friends. They played in the meadow, chased fireflies at night, and shared stories under the stars. And every evening, as Benny hopped home, he knew he had the happiest day ever because he had helped a little butterfly find its family.

The end.
