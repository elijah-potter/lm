In 2017 , the national minimum wage in Romania remained fixed at 320 € per month, that is 3,840 euros per year, taking into account 12 payments per year.
If we look at the minimum salary in Romanian new lei, which is the official currency in Romania, we can see that, this year, it was 1,450 Romanian new lei and accordingly, the national minimum wage has been raised 200 Romanian new lei per month from the previous year, 16%. This increase is greater than the CPI of 2017 which was -0.5%, so workers have gained buying power in the last year.
If we look at the ranking of the national minimum wage that we publish, Romania is in 42 nd place, so it is among the countries with the lowest minimum salary of the 97 of the list.
The National Minimum Wage (NMW) of a country is the minimum amount (lowest salary) per hour, per day, or per month that employers may legally pay to workers.
