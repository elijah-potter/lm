1. You are taking a road trip with your best friend. What would the two of you rock out to on your customized playlist? Name your top 10 songs! 

2. You’ve been chosen to live on a deserted island alone for one whole year. You can choose three foods to take with you (and they won’t run out for the entire year). What three foods are you choosing and why? 

Side note: They have to be nutritious and help to keep you healthy and full while on the island! 

3. Would you rather have a tamed but always hungry pet tiger or a herd of unruly but helpful meerkats? Explain the reasons for your decision! 

4. Paint a mental picture for readers of your favorite season. Incorporate all five senses, like the sweetness of summer watermelon or the sound of autumn leaves crunching underfoot. Make the description so vivid that they can close their eyes and feel, hear, smell, see, and taste every aspect of your explanation. 

5. Rewrite the story of “Cinderella” from the perspective of one of the stepsisters. 

6. Pretend that you’ve been transported into your favorite movie. What character would you play, and how would the story change with you in it? 

7. Compose a persuasive letter to convince readers that they should add your favorite food to their everyday diet. Be creative in your reasonings. 

8. What is one rule in your home that you would change and why? What rule would you implement in its place? 

9. Write an acrostic poem using your name. Each letter of your name should be the beginning of a new sentence in your poem. 

10. Pick up the nearest book or box with words on it. Close your eyes, point to a word, then write a short story that uses that word at least 5 times.
