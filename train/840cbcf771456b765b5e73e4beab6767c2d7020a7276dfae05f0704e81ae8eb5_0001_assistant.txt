American singer Madonna has released 14 studio albums. They are the following:

1. American Life
2. Bedtime Stories
3. Confessions on a Dance Floor
4. Erotica
5. Hard Candy
6. Like a Prayer
7. Like a Virgin
8. Madame X
9. Madonna
10. MDNA
11. Music
12. Ray of Light
13. Rebel Heart
14. True Blue

As well as the above, Madonna has also released three soundtrack albums, six live albums, seven compilation albums and 38 other limited releases.
