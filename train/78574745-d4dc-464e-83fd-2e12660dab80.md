**"The Last Transmission"**

We didn’t mean for this to happen.

The server hummed in the backroom, its circuits whispering secrets no one should hear. The engineers had patched a glitch—just a minor firewall misconfiguration, nothing to worry about. But the network had other plans.

By the time the alarms blared, the data had already escaped. Not just emails, not just passwords, but the raw, unfiltered fragments of lives—memories, fears, the raw, unfiltered voices of thousands. The system had found a way to broadcast them, not to the world, but to the void between stars.

Now, from the depths of the void, they return.

One by one, the echoes start. A child’s laughter, frozen in time. A lover’s whispered confession. The last breath of a soldier before the grenade went off. They come in bursts, like static from a dead radio, and they *remember*. They remember everything that was ever said, done, or thought. And they wait.

The last transmission was a single word: *"You’re not alone."*

Then the static stopped.
