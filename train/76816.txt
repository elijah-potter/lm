Establishment Seedbed Preferences
Light: overstory shade
Moisture: moist required
Fire: Populations of eastern hophornbeam tolerate
occasional, but not frequent fire. Eastern hophornbeam is susceptible to
topkill from fire, but readily sprouts from adventitious buds in the root
collar or from root suckers. Seedling establishment may occur from seeds
of surviving trees onsite or from offsite seeds carried by wind or birds.
The number of stems may increase following a single fire, but repeated
prescribed fire is useful in controlling hophornbeam where is it undesirable.
Weather: Eastern hophornbeam is intolerant of flooding.
It is relatively resistant to cold temperatures and to damage from wind,
snow, and ice.
Air pollution: Eastern Hophornbeam is sensitive
to sulfur dioxide and nitrous oxides, as well as chlorine and fluorine.
