Leonard was walking around Grosvenor Square in London when he walked past a really nice-looking bench, the way the wood reflected the sun at him caught his attention. Behind it was a patch of scarlet begonias. He sat down to enjoy the nip in the air and sunlight when he saw a man walking by that looked like his old friend Howie from college. The man glanced at him as he walked past, and then stopped dead in his tracks, turned around, and shouted out, “Leonard?!” It was Howie!

“Howie?” Leonard asked. What are you doing here?

Howie walked over to the bench and sat down next to his old friend, “I could ask you the same thing.”

“Well, Molly and I broke up, and so I decided that I needed to leave the country, travel the world. So I’ve been doing exactly that. I first stopped in Prague, then took the train to Berlin, then Amsterdam, Paris, and I’ve spent the last week in London. I plan to go to Edinburgh Monday morning. It’s been the journey of a lifetime. What about you?”

“After we graduated I got a job at a financial firm in New York and worked there for a few years, then they relocated me to London last year and I’ve been here ever since.”

“That’s crazy,” Leonard said, “How do you like living here? It’s been an awesome city so far.”

“I love it, I don’t know if I’ll ever leave,” Howie said, “What about you? How’s your trip been? What’s next for you?”

“Honestly,” Leonard started “Still working on that myself. You wanna grab a drink and we can chat all about it and more?”

“Sure, I’d love that. I know a perfect spot,” Howie said, and off they went.
