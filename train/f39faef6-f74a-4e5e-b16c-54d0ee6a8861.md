**Mayor’s Office Press Briefing – Springfield, IL – June 15, 2024**

Springfield residents are urged to stay vigilant as **unidentified individuals** have begun leaving **handwritten notes** across town—some containing coded messages, others blank. Police confirm no arrests have been made, but the city’s **Cybercrime Division** is investigating possible ties to regional **hacktivist groups** active in the Midwest. A **red alert** has been issued for downtown areas after a recent incident where a protester’s laptop was found encrypted with the same symbol found on the notes.

Economic analysts suggest the messages may be part of a **new form of digital protest**, though experts caution against jumping to conclusions. The mayor has directed all businesses to **monitor unusual activity** and advise employees to report anything suspicious.

**No further details available at this time.** End briefing.
