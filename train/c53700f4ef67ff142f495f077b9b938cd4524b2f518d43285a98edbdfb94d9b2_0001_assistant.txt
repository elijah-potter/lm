He regretted ever coming to the kitchen to help as he watched her get out the strangest things. "I thought you were making rolls?" he asked in utter confusion as he saw the electric screwdriver on the counter. 

"I am!" she replied with a giggle as she set the 5-gallon bucket on a stepper and started pouring things in without measuring anything. 

Now thoroughly alarmed, he stepped forward to hold the bucket steady. As she murmured thanks to him for doing that, he noticed she was pouring a whole container of salt into the bucket, followed by a bottle that clearly said 'motor oil' on the side. He could only stand there and gape as she shoved a wooden stick into his hand and told him to start stirring while she continued to add things to the mix. In the bucket went castor oil, a can of cat food, oyster juice, sawdust, and all manner of things. Eventually, it got too thick for him to stir, and then she handed him the electric screwdriver that had been modified with a sturdy whisk at the end.

Just as he was starting to get nauseous from all the different smells, she announced that it was done and playfully shoved him to the side while she started spooning it onto a cookie sheet, making lumps that looked nothing like rolls. Then she put it in the oven and told him, "Go on; I'll let you know when they are ready to taste!" 

He walked away numbly, already dreading it but not wanting to be impolite and say as much. Sooner than he would have liked, she was calling him back. He held his breath as he bit into one of the rolls, expecting it to taste horrible. Much to his shock, it tasted like a regular roll you might get at a restaurant! With wide eyes, he stared down at the white, fluffy roll in his hand as he chewed and swallowed.
