The radio crackled to life in the dim glow of the bedside lamp. A static hiss filled the room as the announcer’s voice cut through the noise.

*"...new developments in the city... expected soon."*

Tears blurred the television screen, where the news ticker flashed updates in real-time: *"Emergency at the docks—fire reported."* *"Police confirm reports of gunfire near the old factory."* The reporter’s voice was tight with urgency.

She sat up, clutching the blanket to her chest, her breath shallow. The door creaked open, and her father stepped in, face pale.

*"They said it’s not over yet."*
