Offers an opportunity to understand one of the great thinkers of western civilization, Euclid. This book includes such topics as the introduction of coordinates, the theory of area, geometrical constructions and finite field extensions, history of the parallel postulate, various non-Euclidean geometries, and the regular and semi-regular polyhedra.
