Idea #1

Jane had a collection of stuffed animals, as most children do. And as most children do, she would talk to her stuffed animals as if they could hear and understand her. They were her best friends. Then one day, her stuffed animals started talking back.


Idea #2

Jane and her family started settling into their new home. It was an older house built in the 1800s, complete with broken shutters, creaky floors, and peeling wallpaper. Some of the townsfolks said the house had been built over an old graveyard. However, public documents showed no record of this. But then, things in the house started going missing. Little, inconsequential things, but still, it was unsettling. Then one day Jane thought she saw one of her stuffed animals wink at her.


Idea #3

When John found out his daughter's stuffed animals had come to life, he decided to enslave them. He ordered them to cook and wash and scrub the house from top to bottom all day, every day or face the threat of being torn apart and thrown in the trash. Little Jane wanted to save them from this horror, but she didn't know how. But the stuffed animals eventually got their revenge. And it was oh, so sweet.
