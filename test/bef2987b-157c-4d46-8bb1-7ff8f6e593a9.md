Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around the garden, chasing his favorite friend, a bright yellow butterfly named Bella.

One sunny morning, Benny found a tiny, shimmering star in his favorite flower. He picked it up and felt a warm, happy feeling inside. "Bella, look!" Benny said, showing her the star.

Bella fluttered her wings happily. "It’s magical, isn’t it?" she said, and together they watched the star twinkle in the sky.

Suddenly, Benny’s ears perked up. "Bella, do you hear that?" he whispered. "It sounds like music!" The star began to glow brighter, and a soft, sweet song filled the air.

The two friends danced under the star, their paws tapping to the tune. The star’s light sparkled on Benny’s fur, making him feel extra brave and joyful.

As the sun started to set, Benny and Bella said goodbye, promising to visit again. The star winked at them one last time before fading gently. Benny and Bella hopped home, full of magic and wonder.

And from that day on, whenever Benny saw a flower, he knew he might just find another tiny star waiting for him to discover it.
