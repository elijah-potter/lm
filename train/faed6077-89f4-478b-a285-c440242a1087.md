**Stormy Rain** over the crumbling stone bridge, wind howling through the abandoned arches. The mist curls around the figures—one clutches a lantern, its glow flickering against the dark. The other stands motionless, arms crossed, watching the raindrops dance like liquid fire across the puddles. The sky above them splits open, thunder rolling like a heartbeat too fast, lightning illuminating their hollow eyes. A single raindrop catches the lantern’s light and shatters, scattering into the night like a promise broken.

**Dry, golden afternoon**—sunlight spilling over the broken windows of an empty house. Dust swirls in the air like embers from a forgotten fire. A child’s toy, half-buried in the floorboards, glints under the sun. The child sits cross-legged at the kitchen table, fingers tracing the rim of a cracked teacup. Outside, birds sing in a voice too high for words. The air smells of old wood and something sweet, like honey left too long.

---
**Silence**. A single blade of grass sways in the stillness of a dead forest. The last light of dusk bleeds through the canopy, painting the earth in shades of crimson. No movement. No sound. Only the whisper of wind through the skeletal branches, carrying nothing but the echo of a voice that was never meant to be heard. The ground beneath is damp with dew, cool and still. Somewhere far away, a clock ticks—once, twice—like a heartbeat counting down.

---
**Heatwave**. A city choked in thick, choking haze. Neon signs flicker like dying stars, their colors bleeding into the smog. A street vendor sells iced tea from a cracked glass, the ice melting into the liquid faster than it can cool. The pavement is a mirror of the sky—endless, suffocating. A child runs, arms outstretched, laughing too loudly. The heat waves lift them into the air, carrying them toward something they can’t see. The city breathes. The city waits.

---
**Blizzard**. The world is a white canvas, erased by time. Snow swirls in drifts that never melt, burying the old bones of forgotten cities beneath their weight. A lone figure, wrapped in a tattered coat, stumbles through the drifts, hands held up like they’re trying to catch something that isn’t there. Their breath fogs in the cold, a ghostly whisper against the wind. The horizon is lost, swallowed by the endless white. The silence is thick, pressing down like the weight of unspoken words. Somewhere, a radio plays static. The snow never stops falling.
