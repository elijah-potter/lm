The half-remembered fragment flickers like a dying candle in the wind—
a name half-spelled in rusted typewriter paper, a melody hummed
only in the spaces between waking and sleep. The face that lingers
is blurred, a ghost in a room where the walls whisper what it once was.
Somewhere, a door creaks open to a story half-lived, half-forgotten,
a ghost story that is both echo and absence, a trace of what might have been
but never quite was. The past is a river, and this is the current—
the water flowing past, but not quite carrying it away.
