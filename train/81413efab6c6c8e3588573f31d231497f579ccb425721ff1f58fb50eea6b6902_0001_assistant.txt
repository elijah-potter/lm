Steven Hayes might not be a name you recognize, but that's all about to change. If you do know about Steven, it's likely because you live in his hometown of Dallas, Texas. He made his way around the city singing renditions of songs by Ed Sheran, Harry Styles, Kelly Clarkson, and Pink. In January, he set out to make his dreams come true by auditioning for American Idol. His dreams were cut short after failing to advance past the first rounds of competition, but that didn't slow him down. He took the advice of his friends and family and decided to continue to gig in Dallas full-time. 

One night, while playing a small set downtown, he was scouted by a producer from Interscope Records. After the show, Steven was pulled aside to set up a formal meeting about his future. Shortly after that meeting, Steven packed up and moved to LA. He began recording his first album, working with well-known songwriters in the industry. Through his newfound connections, he was introduced to Harry Styles. They will soon release a duet titled, Boys K(no)w Better in the fall. Stay tuned to watch this rising star make all his dreams come true!
