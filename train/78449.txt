In 2nd Grade we count US coins and bills. My school district broke the standard down even further to say we need to teach how to count ALL US bills. I made this mini-unit to add to our money unit to help with identifying and counting bills. My class had a BLAST counting up so much money! :)
Included in this packet is:
-Posters of each bill
-Practice presentations counting each bill separately
-Practice presentations counting mixed amounts of bills
-A Toy Shop with several worksheets
-A counting bills scoot
*NOTE* There are NO coins included in this unit. This unit is strictly for bills.
Solve word problems involving dollar bills, quarters, dimes, nickels, and pennies, using $ and ¢ symbols appropriately. Example: If you have 2 dimes and 3 pennies, how many cents do you have?
