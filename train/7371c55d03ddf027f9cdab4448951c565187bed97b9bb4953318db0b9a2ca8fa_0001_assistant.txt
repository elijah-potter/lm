The new cashier was ready for her first shift at the large warehouse. She had somewhat of a mixed work history; she had worked at a zoo and at an office supply store. She couldn't really figure out what she truly wanted to do. After clocking in for her shift, she went to her workstation. For about 3 hours, she did the same task over and over again. She realized that this was not something that would bring her joy.

She thought about what she would do once she finished her shift. She could go home, put on pajamas, paint, and order Chinese takeout. When hour 4 of her shift approached, she shut down her station's machine and left for lunch. She didn't return to finish her shift. She swore to herself that she would find something that she actually wanted to do. But for now, she would order enough crab rangoons to feed 4 people, for herself.
