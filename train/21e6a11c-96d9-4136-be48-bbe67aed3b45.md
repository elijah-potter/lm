The elderly librarian noticed the faint, yellowed edge of a page in the ancient tome, barely visible under the fluorescent light. It had been there for years, overlooked by countless readers. When she ran her finger along it, the smell of aged parchment and old ink filled the air. She turned the page slowly, revealing a hidden illustration of a forgotten scholar in a quill, the ink still damp with age.

The detail wasn’t just a stain or a tear—it was the first inkblot of a story that had been waiting to be told.
