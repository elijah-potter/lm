Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around the garden, eating carrots and playing with his fluffy friends: a squirrel named Sammy and a duck named Daisy.

One sunny morning, Benny spotted something shiny near the tree. It was a tiny golden acorn! "This must be a treasure," Benny thought. He carefully picked it up and hid it under his favorite flower, a big purple one.

Later, while playing, Benny heard a soft "plink!" from the old watering can. Inside was a small, sparkling key. "Maybe this will open the treasure?" Benny wondered.

The next day, Benny dug back under the flower and used the key to open the acorn. Inside was a tiny golden ball! Benny smiled. "It's a shiny new ball for my friends to play with!"

Sammy the squirrel chewed on a leaf, Daisy the duck splashed in the pond, and Benny ran around the garden, sharing his treasure. And that was how Benny turned a simple acorn into the happiest plaything ever!
