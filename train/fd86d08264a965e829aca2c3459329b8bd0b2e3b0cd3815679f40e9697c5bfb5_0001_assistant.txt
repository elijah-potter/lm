Here are a few alternative ways to celebrate a birthday without a party:
1. Movies: Let your daughter pick the movie and invite a few friends.
2. Amusement Park: Buy tickets for your daughter and her best friend.
3. Miniature Golf: Let your daughter invite 2 or 3 friends for 18 golf rounds.
4. Donate to Charity: Have your daughter research and pick her favorite charity and ask everyone to donate a gift in her honor.
5. Rock Climbing: Take your daughter and a friend to a rock climbing center so they can safely scale walls.
6. Nail Salon: Let your daughter take her best friend to the nail salon to get their nails painted.
