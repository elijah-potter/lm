Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna. Anna had quite an imagination, and she loved to tell "tall tales." She would make up stories that weren't true, just to get attention.

She would tell her teacher, "My dad is a fireman, but not just any fireman. He can put out a fire from a mile away because he has water hoses built into his arms!" She would tell her friends things like, "My mom is the smartest person in the world. She took a test and it said so! She has written 87 books. She is also a scientist." She would tell the school security officer, "I used to parachute out of planes, but then it got boring, so I stopped."

Everyone in the town knew that Anna was making up stories. The grown-ups just smiled and shook their heads. Some of her classmates would laugh because they knew her stories were make-believe. But Anna could not stop herself from lying.

One day, she was walking the block from school to her home. As she got nearer, she saw flames and smoke coming out of the windows of her house! Her house was on fire! 

Anna ran back to the school and told her teachers, "Help! Help! My house is on fire!"

The teachers only shook their heads and smiled. "Why don't you ask your dad to put them out with his firehose arms?"

"Please! Really!" she cried.  But the teachers turned away.

Then Anna told a classmate waiting for the bus, "Help! Help! My house is on fire!"

The girl smiled and said, "Your mom is so smart. Tell her to figure out what to do!"

"Please! I'm telling the truth!" Anna begged. The girl giggled and walked away.

Anna looked for the school police officer. Surely he would help! She found him and cried, "Help! Help! My house is on fire!" He smiled and said, "A brave girl like you should fly up in an airplane and dump water down on your house to put out the flames!"

With no one to believe her, Anna had no choice but to run home, where her house had already burned into ashes.

The moral of the story is, "You should always tell the truth."
