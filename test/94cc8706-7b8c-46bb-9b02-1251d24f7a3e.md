Residents of Blackthorn woke to an unnatural glow in the early hours of Tuesday, July 10th, as strange energy pulses emanated from an abandoned power plant along the outskirts of town. Witnesses described eerie, swirling lights resembling auroras near the facility, which had been dormant for decades.

The Blackthorn City Council is investigating reports of strange occurrences, including unexplained sounds resembling distant machinery and occasional bursts of static. Authorities have activated emergency protocols, deploying drone surveillance over the plant’s perimeter. Meanwhile, a local historian claims the site was once linked to a 19th-century experiment in "artificial aurora," though no records confirm this.

Health officials advise residents to avoid the area, citing reports of dizziness and mild hallucinations near the plant. The Blackthorn Police Department has opened a preliminary inquiry but has not ruled out natural phenomena or deliberate interference. Further updates to follow.
