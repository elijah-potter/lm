The dust motes swirl in the dim, stale air of an abandoned office cubicle. A shattered monitor screen casts jagged light across a half-empty desk, its cracked surface littered with crumpled papers and a half-drunk coffee cup. The scent of old cigarette smoke clings to the frayed fabric of the chair beside a half-open drawer, its contents spilled onto the floor—a half-eaten sandwich, a receipt from last week, and a single, crumpled note: *"They’re coming."* A loose floorboard creaks beneath a pair of worn-out shoes, abandoned near the door. The fluorescent light flickers, casting eerie shadows on the peeling wallpaper. The air hums faintly—something like a broken fan, or the distant whisper of voices beyond the closed door.
