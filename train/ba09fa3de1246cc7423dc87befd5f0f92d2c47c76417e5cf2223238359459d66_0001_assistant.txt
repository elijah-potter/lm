Korean doctors are known for being up to date on the latest trends and technology. According to Korean scientist Yoen Leem, many are using technology to design beautiful faces for their clients. These doctors have computer programs that help find the most appealing faces in the world. The programs work by analyzing the faces of people of all different races and nationalities and then deciding which traits are viewed as beautiful by most people. The result is a definition of “global proportions … what the common beauty ideal is in all races.” Doctors use the data to sell procedures to their clients. In this case, technology decides what is beautiful and then helps promote this standard. Always, costly plastic surgery and other procedures are recommended.
