Outside the hospital, a fluorescent light flickers against the rain-soaked pavement. The emergency room door creaks open, and a uniformed nurse steps out, holding a small clipboard. She pauses, scanning the crowd before speaking in a voice barely audible over the distant wail of sirens.

*"They’ve stabilized the patient. Surgery was a success, but they’re still monitoring closely. You may have to wait a few more hours—"* Her phone buzzes. She checks it quickly before turning back. *"They’re asking for you. Follow me."*

A man in a worn leather jacket tightens his grip on the arm of a woman clutching a phone. His fingers tremble as he reads the text: *"The team is back. Meet us at the old diner. Bring the bag."*

She nods, her voice tight with relief. *"We’ll be there in five."* The diner’s neon sign flickers—*"Dinner Open"*—as the last of the night shifts pulls away. The wait is never over.
