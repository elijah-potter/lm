The Last Austrian is set in a fictional world where Austria and all things Austrian have been banned. Paul Wagner (played by Arnold Schwarzenegger) is a black-market dealer in strudel and wiener schnitzel. He meets a mysterious stranger who tells him about a secret underground community that is fighting to bring back banned cultures. Soon after resisting the stranger's overtures, Paul narrowly avoids being hit by a car in the street. He receives a mysterious message later that night informing him that it was an attempt on his life and that "they" won't stop until he's dead. He narrowly escapes arrest when a car driven by the stranger from before picks him up.  Paul begins his training as a member of the underground resistance. A few years pass and Paul is now a full-blown member of the resistance, calling himself the Last Austrian.
