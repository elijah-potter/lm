As the most educated mouse who prides myself on my exceptional vocabulary and skills of articulation, I can indeed confirm that yes, it is true, Walt Disney was afraid of mice. This is absurd to me. Mice are refined and sophisticated -- there is nothing to fear from us unless you are competing with us academically. 

This phobia is a particularly amusing anecdote about the ever-ingenious Walt Disney because he is the mastermind innovator of one of the most well-known mouse characters ever created, Mickey Mouse. Even with his fear of mice. Walt depicted Mickey as a likable mouse with many human characteristics that would appeal to both children and adults.

I am a fan of Mickey Mouse and appreciate his comedic skills and his artistic savoir-faire. I appreciate all mice that positively represent mouse culture and present us as the erudite and well-rounded creatures that we are.
