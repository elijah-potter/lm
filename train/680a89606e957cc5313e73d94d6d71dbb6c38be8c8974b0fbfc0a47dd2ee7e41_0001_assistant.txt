A great tip for the new homeowner is to draw a diagram of your entire house before starting any project. If you love technology, then you can find many types of software to help you draw out the floor plan of your home, If not, then just use regular graph paper, a pencil, and a ruler to get things drawn properly. 

A normal 25-foot tape measure should be fine to do the actual measurements. You’ll need someone to help you, though. Be sure to include doors and windows. These measurements can really come in handy when you're out looking for new window coverings.

Measure a room, then write down the measurements on a sheet of paper and put them in your diagram later so you can make sure to plug the numbers into the right spots. If you have a large house or a number of areas to measure, you can purchase a handy gadget called a Sonic Measuring Device. These come in all shapes and price ranges and usually have a built-in laser. You can spend from $20 to over $100 on this gadget, so use some common sense. 

If you know you’ll be doing a lot of home improvement projects, then go ahead and invest in a good one. They make measuring any room or garage easy work, and you don’t need a helper. Simply hold the device up against one wall in a location where the laser beam can hit a wall on the opposite side, then press the button. The display will show you exactly how far it is in feet or metric units. 

Most of us only have a certain amount of time and money to get a job done, so it’s important to maximize your budget but also to get the most done during your allotted time frame. Always start any new project with a solid plan, and you’ll be much happier with the end results.
