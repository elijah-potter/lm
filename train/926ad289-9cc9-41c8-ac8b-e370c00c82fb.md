The radio crackled to life, static hissing between static. A news bulletin crackled over the airwaves:

*"Breaking news from [City Name]: Authorities confirm a major incident at the old refinery near downtown. Emergency services are on scene. Residents advised to stay indoors."*

The news anchor paused, voice tense. *"Stay tuned for further updates."*

Outside, rain hammered against the window. The door creaked open, letting in a gust of damp air. A figure stood at the edge of the porch, wrapped in a heavy coat, phone pressed to ear.

*"They say it's a fire. The fire department's been called. I don't know what else to tell you—just that the roads are closed. Stay home."*

A long silence. The figure clenched their fist, knuckles white. The phone dropped back onto the wooden railing. The radio hummed, filling the silence.

*"Stay safe."*
