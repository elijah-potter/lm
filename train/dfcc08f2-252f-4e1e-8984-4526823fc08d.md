Aerobic cardio routine with high-intensity intervals (HIIT):
- 5 min warm-up (dynamic stretches + light jogging)
- 40 sec sprint on treadmill/incline walker (80-90% max effort)
- 60 sec jog (50-60% effort) x 8 reps
- 5 min cooldown (stretching + breathing exercises)

Strength training circuit (3 rounds):
1. Kettlebell swings: 15 reps
2. Box jumps: 12 reps
3. Battle ropes: 30 sec intervals
4. Burpees: 10 reps
5. Pull-ups (assisted): 8 reps

Yoga flow for flexibility & recovery:
- Downward dog → Sun salutations x 5
- Warrior series (I-III) x 3 sides
- Seated forward fold → Deep breathing x 2 min
- Child’s pose → Corpse pose (Savasana) x 5 min

Performance enhancements:
- Pre-workout: Black coffee + beetroot juice (nitric oxide)
- Post-workout: Whey protein + electrolytes
- Sleep: 7-9 hours with 10-min evening stretch routine
- Nutrition: High-protein, low-glycemic diet with intermittent fasting (16:8)
- Mindset: 5-min meditation pre-session + gratitude journaling post-workout
