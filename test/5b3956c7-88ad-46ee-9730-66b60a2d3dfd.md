The city of New Haven was a maze of neon and rust, where the old streets whispered secrets through cracked pavement. Twelve-year-old Mira had always felt like she didn’t quite fit in—until the day she stumbled upon a hidden door in her grandmother’s attic. Inside, she found a journal filled with cryptic notes and sketches of a world beyond their own, one where time bent like a river.

One evening, as Mira sat on the rooftop of her apartment building, she saw a flash of light—a meteorite had landed in the alley below. Curious, she followed the sound and found it glowing faintly in the dark. Inside, she discovered a pocket watch that didn’t tick, but pulsed with energy. When she pressed it, the world around her blurred, and she found herself standing in a forest where trees grew upside down, their roots tangled in the sky.

A girl with sharp eyes and a voice like dry leaves stood before her. "You’ve been chosen," she said, her voice a whisper in the wind. "The time between worlds is running out, and the watch will help you bridge them." Mira’s hands trembled as she realized she wasn’t just a kid in a strange place—she was part of something bigger.

The next day, Mira returned to the alley, the watch tucked safely in her pocket. But when she opened it, the forest was gone, replaced by the familiar streets of New Haven. The girl had vanished. That night, Mira’s grandmother found her holding the pocket watch, her eyes wide with fear.

"You’re not from here," her grandmother said, her voice trembling. "And you’ve got to stop it." Mira’s heart pounded. She knew what she had to do—she had to find the girl, understand the world between worlds, and decide whether to stay or return.

But the forest called to her. And the watch pulsed with an unnatural glow, beckoning her deeper into the unknown.

What would Mira do? The choice was hers—and the fate of the worlds might depend on it.
