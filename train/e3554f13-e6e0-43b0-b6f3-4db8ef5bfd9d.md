A promise broken under misinterpretation—
"Open by noon tomorrow" was meant as flexibility,
but the "tomorrow" was taken as absolute.
The sender assumed mutual trust, the receiver read
expediency as defiance.
One saw it as a gift of time, the other a debt.
By the time the deadline arrived, neither could
reconcile their understanding of what was owed.
The "I’ll do it" was never a contract—just a
misaligned expectation.
