The winding trail, though narrower and more treacherous, led to the hidden cave where the legendary artifact was said to dwell. Each step required concentration—each loose rock could shift unexpectedly, and the mist curled dangerously close to the edges. The guide’s whispered warnings about the "hollow path" were ignored, replaced by the thrill of the unknown.

The lantern flickered as they descended, its light barely piercing the damp air. The longer way had its own rhythm: the crack of branches, the distant call of an unseen creature, the slow descent into silence. The reward was worth the risk.
