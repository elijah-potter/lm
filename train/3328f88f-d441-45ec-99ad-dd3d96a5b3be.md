## Project Chimera: Rulebook

**A Cooperative Game of Scientific Crisis Management**

**Welcome, Researchers.** Project Chimera tasks you and your colleagues with confronting a rapidly evolving biological threat. A synthetic virus, initially contained within a secure research facility, has escaped and is now spreading globally. Its unique ability to rapidly mutate presents an unprecedented challenge to humanity. Your team represents the world’s leading virologists, geneticists, and epidemiologists, tasked with understanding, containing, and ultimately neutralizing this escalating crisis. Success hinges on careful planning, decisive action, and unwavering collaboration.

### 1. Components

*   **Crisis Board:** A world map depicting major cities and regions, with spaces for Crisis Tokens.
*   **Researcher Cards (8):** Each card details a unique Researcher with specialized skills and starting resources.
*   **Mutation Cards (60):** Detail the virus’s evolving characteristics and introduce new challenges.
*   **Event Cards (40):** Represent global reactions, logistical hurdles, and unexpected occurrences.
*   **Research Tokens (40):** Represent ongoing scientific investigation.
*   **Containment Tokens (30):** Represent localized efforts to restrict the virus’s spread.
*   **Resource Tokens (120):** Represent Funding (blue), Personnel (green), and Equipment (red).
*   **Crisis Tokens (30):** Indicate the virus's presence and severity in different regions.
*   **Dice (6):** Six-sided dice used for skill checks and event resolution.
*   **First Player Token:** Designates the starting player for each round.

### 2. Setup

1.  Place the Crisis Board in the center of the play area.
2.  Shuffle the Mutation Cards and place them face down as a draw pile. Reveal the top three Mutation Cards and place them face up beside the deck. These represent the virus’s current known characteristics.
3.  Shuffle the Event Cards and place them face down as a draw pile.
4.  Place the Research Tokens, Containment Tokens, and Resource Tokens in accessible supply pools.
5.  Each player chooses a Researcher Card. Each Researcher begins with the resources indicated on their card.
6.  Place three Crisis Tokens on the starting city indicated on the Crisis Board (Geneva).
7.  Determine the First Player randomly and give them the First Player Token.

### 3. Turn Structure

Each round consists of the following phases, completed in order:

**1. Mutation Phase:** Draw one Mutation Card and add it to the face-up Mutation Cards. These cards introduce new viral traits and potential complications.

**2. Event Phase:** Draw one Event Card and resolve its effects immediately. Events can introduce new Crisis Tokens, deplete resources, or impose restrictions.

**3. Researcher Action Phase:** Starting with the First Player and proceeding clockwise, each player performs two actions. Actions can be any combination of the following:

    *   **Move:** Move your Researcher to an adjacent city on the Crisis Board.
    *   **Research:** Spend one Personnel token and roll two dice. The result must meet or exceed the Research value indicated on the current Mutation Cards. Success places one Research Token on the current Mutation Card.
    *   **Contain:** Spend one Equipment token and roll one die. The result must meet or exceed the Containment value indicated on the current Mutation Cards. Success places one Containment Token on a city containing Crisis Tokens.
    *   **Gather Resources:** Choose one resource type (Funding, Personnel, or Equipment) and gain two tokens of that type.
    *   **Share Resources:** Give one or more Resource Tokens to another Researcher in the same city.

**4. Crisis Spread Phase:** For each city containing Crisis Tokens, roll one die. On a result of 4 or higher, place one additional Crisis Token in that city. If a city exceeds its Crisis Token capacity (indicated on the Crisis Board), the crisis *escalates* – place one Crisis Token on an adjacent city.

**5. Round End:** Pass the First Player Token to the player on your left.

### 4. Win/Loss Conditions

**Winning the Game:**

You win the game when you have successfully completed the Research requirements on *all* Mutation Cards currently in play. This signifies a comprehensive understanding of the virus and the development of a neutralizing agent.

**Losing the Game:**

You lose the game if any of the following conditions are met:

*   **Global Pandemic:** A predetermined number of cities (dependent on player count - see Difficulty Scaling) contain Crisis Tokens.
*   **Researcher Loss:** All Researchers are incapacitated (due to Event Card effects or other complications).
*   **Mutation Deck Exhaustion:** The Mutation Card deck is exhausted before the Research requirements on all Mutation Cards are met.

### 5. Mechanics of Team Trade-Offs & Uncertainty

Project Chimera is designed to force difficult choices and encourage collaboration under pressure. The following mechanics highlight this:

1.  **Mutation Prioritization:** The three face-up Mutation Cards represent the most pressing research areas. However, focusing on one area may neglect others, potentially leading to unexpected complications. Players must debate and agree on which Mutations to prioritize.
2.  **Resource Scarcity:** Resources are limited, and demand often exceeds supply. Researchers must decide how to allocate Funding, Personnel, and Equipment to maximize impact, often requiring difficult compromises. The "Share Resources" action is crucial but creates a dependency between players.
3.  **Dice Roll Uncertainty:** Skill checks (Research and Contain) rely on dice rolls, introducing an element of uncertainty. Even with the best planning, unforeseen failures can derail progress, forcing players to adapt and re-evaluate their strategy.

### 6. Difficulty Scaling

The game’s difficulty adjusts based on the number of players:

*   **Two Players:** Reduce the number of cities required to trigger a Global Pandemic to 8.
*   **Three Players:** Play with the standard rules as described above.
*   **Four Players:** Increase the number of cities required to trigger a Global Pandemic to 12.

### 7. Advanced Variants

*   **Specialized Researcher Roles:** Introduce optional roles for Researchers, granting unique abilities and further emphasizing specialization.
*   **Black Market Variant:** Introduce a deck of Black Market cards offering risky but potentially rewarding actions, adding an element of moral ambiguity.
*   **Rapid Mutation:** Increase the number of Mutation Cards drawn during the Mutation Phase, accelerating the virus's evolution and increasing the challenge.



### Designer's Note

Project Chimera was designed to be more than just a game. It aims to foster critical thinking, collaborative problem-solving, and an appreciation for the complexities of scientific research and crisis management. The escalating nature of the threat, the limitations of resources, and the inherent uncertainties of scientific investigation are all intended to mirror real-world challenges. Through careful planning, open communication, and a willingness to adapt, players will not only confront a global crisis but also gain a deeper understanding of the scientific process and the importance of teamwork.
