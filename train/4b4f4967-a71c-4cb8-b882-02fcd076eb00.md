**Title: *Unwritten Lines***

The list was a promise, the kind you make to yourself in the dark.
I traced the numbers with my finger, each one a ghost of what might have been.
The ink on my notebook was old, the paper brittle. But between the lines, something hummed.
A name. A place. A sound—half-sung, half-forgotten.

The first word was *"hollow."* The second was *"echo."* The third was silence.
