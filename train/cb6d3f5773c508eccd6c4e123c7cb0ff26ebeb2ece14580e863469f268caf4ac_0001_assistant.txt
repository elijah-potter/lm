The first step to solving the stray dog issue is understanding its causes. There are many factors that cause the stray dog population to grow, such as a poor economic situation or changing lifestyles, making it harder for people to care for dogs. One way to counter the growing number of homeless dogs is by preventing people from releasing them into the wild while promoting shelters and rehoming as alternatives. 

Another important strategy is pushing public servants to implement stricter animal welfare laws to discourage people from abandoning animals. Greater funding for veterinary services and neutering/spaying drives can also help keep the population of homeless dogs in check and prevent services from being overwhelmed too.
