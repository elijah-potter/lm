## Product Requirements Document: "Synthesis" - Knowledge Consolidation App

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently encounter a deluge of information from diverse sources – articles, meeting notes, research papers, podcasts – leading to fragmented understanding, difficulty in recalling relevant information, and reduced productivity.

**Vision:** Synthesis will be the central hub for knowledge workers to effortlessly capture, connect, and retrieve information, fostering deeper understanding and accelerating insights.

---

**1. User Personas**

*   **Dr. Eleanor Vance, Research Scientist:** Eleanor is a lead researcher at a pharmaceutical company. Her goals include staying current on scientific literature, synthesizing findings from multiple studies, and quickly recalling specific details for grant proposals and publications. Her pain points are the overwhelming volume of academic papers, difficulty connecting disparate research, and the time lost searching for previously read material.
*   **Marcus Bellweather, Marketing Manager:** Marcus manages a team of marketers and requires constant access to competitive intelligence, customer feedback, and internal marketing reports. His goals are to maintain a comprehensive understanding of the market landscape, identify emerging trends, and efficiently share insights with his team. His pain points include information silos across departments, difficulty tracking the evolution of customer needs, and the challenge of quickly disseminating critical updates.
*   **Ava Sharma, Consultant:** Ava is an independent management consultant who works with clients across various industries. Her goals are to rapidly acquire domain expertise in new areas, organize client-specific research, and present compelling insights during client engagements. Her pain points are the need to quickly become proficient in unfamiliar subjects, the difficulty of structuring complex information for presentations, and the risk of overlooking crucial details.

---

**2. Requirements**

1.  **Universal Capture:** Users must be able to capture information from any web page, document (PDF, DOCX), or audio/video source (via URL).
    *   *Acceptance Criteria:*  The app supports URL input for web pages and documents.  Audio/video URL input generates a transcript.  Captured content is automatically stored with metadata (source URL, capture date/time).
    *   *Success Metrics:* 95% of attempted captures succeed without user intervention. Average capture time under 5 seconds.
2.  **Contextual Linking:** Users must be able to create explicit connections between captured information, even from different sources.
    *   *Acceptance Criteria:* Users can create links between any two captured items. Links are bidirectional and visually represented within the app. Users can add brief notes explaining the connection.
    *   *Success Metrics:* Average number of links per user per week exceeds 3. User feedback indicates links are helpful in understanding relationships.
3.  **Semantic Search:** The app must provide a search function that understands the meaning of search terms, not just keyword matches.
    *   *Acceptance Criteria:* Search results prioritize content semantically related to the search query. Users can refine search results using filters (source type, date range, keywords).
    *   *Success Metrics:* User satisfaction with search results (measured via in-app survey) exceeds 4.0 on a 5-point scale. Search time for relevant information reduced by 30% (measured via A/B testing).
4.  **Personalized Knowledge Graph:** The app must visually represent a user’s captured information and connections as a dynamic knowledge graph.
    *   *Acceptance Criteria:*  The knowledge graph automatically updates as new information is added and connections are made. Users can navigate the graph intuitively. Users can customize the visual representation (node size, color).
    *   *Success Metrics:*  70% of active users regularly explore the knowledge graph (measured via usage data).  Qualitative feedback indicates the graph enhances understanding.
5.  **Cross-Device Synchronization:** Users must be able to access and modify their knowledge base seamlessly across iOS and Android devices.
    *   *Acceptance Criteria:*  Changes made on one device are reflected on all other devices within 5 minutes. Offline access to previously downloaded content is supported.
    *   *Success Metrics:*  Synchronization errors are less than 1% of total synchronization events.  User satisfaction with cross-device access exceeds 4.2 on a 5-point scale.

---

**3. Technical Considerations**

*   **Backend Infrastructure:** Scalable cloud-based architecture (AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure) is required to handle large volumes of data and user traffic.
*   **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** Integration of NLP libraries for semantic search and content summarization.
*   **Data Storage:** Secure and encrypted storage for user data.
*   **Mobile Development:** Native iOS and Android development for optimal performance and user experience.
*   **API Design:** Well-defined APIs for future integrations with other productivity tools.

**4. Risks**

*   **NLP Accuracy:** The accuracy of semantic search and content summarization depends on the quality of NLP models. Requires ongoing model refinement.
*   **Data Privacy:** Protecting user data and complying with privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA) is paramount.
*   **Scalability Challenges:** Ensuring the app can handle a rapidly growing user base.
*   **User Adoption:** Convincing users to adopt a new knowledge management tool.

**5. Non-Goals**

*   Real-time collaboration features (shared knowledge bases).
*   Direct integration with social media platforms.
*   Automated content curation or recommendation engine.
*   Support for desktop applications (initially).
*   Advanced formatting capabilities within captured content.
