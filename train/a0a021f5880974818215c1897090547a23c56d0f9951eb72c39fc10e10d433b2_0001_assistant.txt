Ryuunosuke was as excited as anyone else. He was finally getting to play the VRMMO Angel's Requiem. It was rumored to be the best VRMMO to date and was the first game to be released on the second version of the full dive technology. Ryuunosuke bought the VR and game bundle, unable to contain his excitement. 

The game launched, and he was thrilled by the experience. It was exactly as he expected it to be. The visuals were stunning, the gameplay was beyond fluid, and all the small and awkward bits from the first version of the VR headset had been smoothed out. Ryuunosuke felt like he was playing the game in his own body. It was when he went to log out that he realized something strange. There was no logout button. Where the button should have been was simply blank.

He ran back to the starter town to find hundreds or maybe even thousands of players with the same issue. None of them could log out, either. It had everyone in a panic. What was going on? What exactly happened? Was this some kind of bug, or were they simply missing something? As Ryuunosuke asked himself these questions, he recalled the pain he'd felt during the fight with some low-level monsters. He shouldn't have felt that kind of pain. That was beyond the VR headset's capabilities. This wasn't a game anymore, he realized. This was reality. They had all slipped into Angel's Requiem, not as players but as actual people. He didn't know how or why. 

Ryuunosuke's instincts kicked in. He knew where this would lead. Chaos would break out. People might turn violent. Things weren't going to end well for someone weak. He left the village that instant. If he was going to survive the fallout, he needed to be stronger than anyone else. The best way was to get ahead before anyone. He wouldn't die here.
