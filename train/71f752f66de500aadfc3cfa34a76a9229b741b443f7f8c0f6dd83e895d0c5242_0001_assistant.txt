Dear Roland, 

I hope this letter finds you well. I have been in the city now for over a week and I can tell you it's unlike anything you've ever seen. The buildings are so high and the streets are so busy it hardly feels real. I got the chance to ride in one of those automobiles we heard about and it was exhilarating. I won't lie by telling you I did feel a bit of fear but it was completely overshadowed by the excitement of traveling nearly forty miles an hour. The wind in my hair was one of the most wonderful feelings I've ever had. 

The new job at the newspaper is going splendidly. I've gotten in with the other girls in the office and we have the most fun of times together. They are all hard workers too, and this woman named Mavis in particular is quite a brilliant writer. She just got back home from Africa after doing a co-op with a photographer in Kenya. Her stories and the things she did are amazing and I must admit I am a bit jealous. I can see you and me in Africa taking in the experience and making a difference somewhere in the world. Can't you?

Otherwise, things are rather normal here. I do feel lonely here at night and even a little fearful when it's dark and quiet. I can't wait for you to get here and experience this with me. This place is far different than home, and I can't wait to see your face when you experience what I have experienced. The food alone deserves a letter home of its own. Even simple things like hotdogs are just fabulous. I don't know how they do it. 

In closing, I want you to know I love you very much and that I think of you every day, throughout the day. None of this feels right without you here and I just can't wait to hold you again. Travel safe my love.

Your Darling, 

Mable
