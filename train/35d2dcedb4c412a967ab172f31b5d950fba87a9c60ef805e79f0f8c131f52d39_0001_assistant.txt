Sure! Here are some polite ways to tell your supervisor you want to quit your job. 

- "I have been offered a new position that I believe is a good fit for my skills and experience, and I have decided to accept it. I want to express my gratitude for the support and guidance you have provided during my time here." This is a polite way to tell your supervisor you want to quit if you have been offered another job while expressing gratitude for the experience you gained at your current position.

- "After careful consideration, I have decided to resign from my position here. I have learned a lot during my time here and am grateful for the opportunities I have had, but I have decided to pursue a new direction in my career. Thank you for the support and encouragement you have provided during my time here." This expresses your gratitude for the experience and opportunities given at this position and still politely gives your reason for leaving.

- "I wanted to let you know that I have decided to resign from my position here. I have enjoyed my time here and appreciate the opportunities I have had to learn and grow, but I have decided to pursue a new opportunity that aligns more closely with my long-term career goals. Thank you for the support and guidance you have provided during my time here." This acknowledges the positive aspects this position has given you while still politely informing them that you will be moving on to another position.
