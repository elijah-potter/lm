A dimly lit interrogation room with fluorescent lighting flickering. A uniformed officer sits across from a man in a worn leather jacket, fingers steepled. The officer leans forward, voice low and measured: *"Explain yourself."* The man hesitates, then starts: *"I’m not a criminal, sir. Just… lost in the city. I was trying to find my way home."* The officer scribbles notes, head tilting slightly. Outside, a distant siren wails. The man watches the door, voice trailing off. *"I don’t know why they’re asking this."*
