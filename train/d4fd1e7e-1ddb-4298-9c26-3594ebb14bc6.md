A routine maintenance crew ignored the persistent hissing from the HVAC unit in the server room, attributing it to "normal wear" during seasonal temperature fluctuations. The unit’s thermal runaway was masked by the building’s redundant cooling system, which absorbed excess heat before it reached critical thresholds. Over time, the system’s efficiency degraded silently, with minor power spikes and intermittent fan failures going unnoticed. By the time the actual failure occurred—causing a localized power surge that fried three redundant servers—the damage was already done. The incident was reported as a "software glitch," while the real cause remained a cautionary tale of unchecked operational drift.
