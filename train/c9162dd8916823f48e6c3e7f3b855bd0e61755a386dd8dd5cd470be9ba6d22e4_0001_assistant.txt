Sure thing! Here are a few ways you could respond to the text message: 

1. I am so sorry for not responding earlier! I will not be able to make it to the party tomorrow. 
 - This response keeps it short and simple and is a nice professional choice. 

2. I am so sorry; I know how frustrating it is when people do not RSVP. I will not be able to make it to your party tomorrow, but I appreciate the invitation! I hope you have a great time tomorrow!
 - This response is good because it is a little more personal and empathetic. 

3. I am so sorry that I forgot to RSVP to your party! I will not be able to make it tomorrow, but I hope you have a great time!
 - This response is sympathetic but also keeps things on the shorter side.
