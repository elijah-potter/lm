The people of Demacia.

Demacia City, the most developed and one of the wealthiest cities in the country of Thearom, is an interesting city to be in at any time. On our visit there last week, we learned a lot about the city, its inhabitants, and the way of life of the people there.
Made up of three boroughs, the city has around 400,000 inhabitants. One of the unique things about this city is how the inhabitants live. The first borough is made mostly of cirrus, cirrocumulus, and cirrostratus, who are the wealthy of the city. They live lavishly and either work only the top jobs, have businesses, or are people who inherited wealth from their parents.
The altocumulus, nimbostratus, and altostratus live in the second borough and are mostly middle-class people with 9-to-5 jobs. They live well, but not compared to upper-class people. 
The lower class consists of the cumulus, stratus, and stratocumulus, who are generally poor, and either have low-paying jobs or are unemployed. They make up about a third of the total population.
There are plans by the local government to improve the livelihood of the lower class, and the city seems to be making great progress in terms of its development, but like anything, it takes time to make great advances.
