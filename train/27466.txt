Culture Days by Explore & More
One of the museum's most popular programs is Culture Days. Culture Days provides a multicultural educational experience for school-aged kids. Brazil, China, Ecuador, Germany, Japan, Mexico, Netherlands, South Africa, Vietnam, Yemen — bring the world to your classroom! With genuine artifacts, decorated classrooms and an interactive approach, Culture Days will immerse your students in a unique global experience as they learn how children in other parts of the world live, eat, play and learn. For online activities for kids, pre- and post-visit ideas, and more information, visit the Cultures For Kids website.
