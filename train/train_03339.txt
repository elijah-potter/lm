Nicky went to the DMV. He spent 20 minutes waiting to take a number, and quadruple that amount of time plus 14 minutes waiting for his number to be called. How long did he wait total?
First find how long Nicky spent waiting for his number to be called: 4 * 20 minutes + 14 minutes = <<4*20+14=94>>94 minutes
Then add the time he spent waiting to take a number: 94 + 20 = <<94+20=114>>114 minutes
