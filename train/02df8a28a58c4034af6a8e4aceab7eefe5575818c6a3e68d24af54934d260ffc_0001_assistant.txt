Of course! These are the number 1 picks by CBS' local affiliate in the San Francisco Bay Area:

1. Bell Peppers: This kitchen staple thrives in San Francisco's foggy weather. Too much sun or heat can damage the plant.

2. Herbs: Many kinds of herbs find the San Francisco climate amenable to their overall growth. It includes sage, lavender, marjoram, parsley, thyme, and oregano.

3. Tomatoes: San Francisco's microclimates make it difficult to give a universally encompassing answer. However, a general rule of thumb is that the closer to the ocean you are, the lower the size and yield from your planting.
.
4. Leafy Greens: The winter in San Francisco is ideal for leafy plants like lettuce or Spinach. Artichokes and root plants also thrive in these conditions.

5. Fruit Trees: These are some of the best to plant in San Francisco's climatic conditions. Myer lemon trees are known as some o the best as they thrive in foggy climates. Dwarf apple, apricot, cherry, peach, pear and plum trees, are also known for their resistance to San Francisco's climate.
