A metropolis of neon-lit skyscrapers, where every building’s façade pulses with holographic advertisements of AI-driven futures. Streets are lined with self-driving pods and elevated maglev trains, while the air hums with the static of quantum networks. Citizens glide through glass canopies, their faces obscured by augmented reality interfaces that rewrite their memories in real-time. Bridges arch between floating platforms, connecting clusters of vertical farms and data hubs where genetic engineering and neural interfaces blur the line between biology and code. The city’s infrastructure—roads, utilities, even public spaces—constantly updates itself, but the people beneath it move at the speed of human cognition, lagging behind the relentless evolution of their surroundings.
