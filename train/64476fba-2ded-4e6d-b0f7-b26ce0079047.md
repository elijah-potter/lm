A floating market in a post-apocalyptic sky city, where gravity is simulated by rotating platforms and cargo holds. The "rules" are enforced via holographic contracts and neural implants—each transaction is logged in a decentralized blockchain embedded in the air. Trade happens via neural-link swaps, with currency units being rare, synthetic memory fragments (data credits) and biotech derivatives (organs, memories, or emotions as commodities). The "laws" are fluid, rewritten in real-time by AI arbiters that resolve disputes through predictive algorithms. Smuggling occurs in repurposed drones and exoskeletons, with black markets thriving in abandoned orbital habitats. The dominant social contract is loyalty to the "system," which dynamically adjusts to prevent chaos—though dissenters are "rebooted" via localized neural resets. The atmosphere is a mix of neon-lit corporate districts and underground "floating jungles," where scavengers and cybernetic nomads trade in contraband.
