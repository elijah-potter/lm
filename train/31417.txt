The U.S. flag is a strong symbol of American identification as well as nationwide pride. Additionally called Old Glory, the American flag has a vivid history as well as has actually undergone lots of changes from the first main flag of 1777.
Today the U.S. flag contains thirteen straight stripes, seven red alternating with six white stripes. The stripes represent the original 13 colonies; the stars represent the 50 states of the nation. The shades of the flag are symbolic as well: red symbolizes hardiness as well as valor, white signifies purity as well as virtue as well as blue represents vigilance, perseverance and also justice. However, the factor the Continental Congress originally picked the red, white, and also blue colors was not made clear in the resolution embracing the flag. Historians believe it was most likely a color option based upon the British Union Jack, which had formerly flown over the colonies.
Over time, some have actually associated slightly different significances to the 3 shades, for example, the color red symbolizing the blood spilled to preserve our freedoms, yet the essence of the initial significance has actually been rather constant from 1782.
How did the American flag become what it is today?
It is so much greater than the 3 shades or a “decor”. Think of the areas around the globe that the American flag has actually flown, consider the transformation it has actually undergone throughout the decades on American soil. It is truly humbling to think of all that was given as well as compromised to ensure that the American flag can fly freely across this nation.
The flag that started with only 13 stars grew to 50 with the addition of other states to the Union. The variety of stars on the flag slowly rose to its existing number today wherein a new star would be included in the blue field on the 4th of July after the day of each new state’s admission.
The number of rotating horizontal red and white stripes has continued to be at thirteen except from 1795 to 1818 when fifteen stripes showed up on the flag to note the admission of Kentucky as well as Vermont to the Union. In 1818, it was decided that adding a stripe to the flag for every brand-new state would certainly no longer occur as it will certainly make the flag appear crowded and also it would make the flag unwieldy. It was determined then that the flag would return to including only thirteen stripes to represent the initial colonies.
The American flag is a symbol not only of hardiness, valor, pureness, virtue, vigilance, determination and also justice; it is a symbol of liberty. Freedom that has been combatted so hard for over the decades. Freedom that has cost this country as well as the families within a lot, and yet it is still a sign to those wanting they had the freedom that the country has.
Folding the American flag.
Typical flag etiquette prescribes that prior to an American flag is stored or flown, its handlers must two times fold it in half lengthwise; then (from the end opposite heaven area) make a triangular fold, remaining to fold it in a triangular patter til the other end is reached. This makes a triangular “cushion” of the flag with only the blue starred area revealing on the outside, as well as it takes thirteen folds to produce: 2 lengthwise folds and also eleven triangular ones.
The flag isn’t folded in this fashion since each of the folds has a special symbolic definition; the flag is folded this way due to the fact that it provides a dignified ceremonial touch that distinguishes folding a flag from folding a common thing such as a bedsheet, as well as since it results an aesthetically pleasing, easy-to-handle form. This thirteen-fold treatment was a common technique long before the development of a ritualistic assignation of “suggesting” to each of the steps.
A fancy flag folding ceremony including these definitions has actually from then on been created for unique occasions such as Memorial Day and also Veterans Day. These associations are “real” in the feeling that they imply something to the people who take part in the event, however they are not the reason why a flag is folded in the traditional thirteen-step manner.
