The weight of the world shifts when one stands on the other side of the glass, unseen yet felt in the hum of unseen hands. A stranger’s name becomes a mirror reflecting back one’s own unspoken fears—no longer a cage, but a door ajar. The path that once seemed steep now curves like a ribbon unfurled, revealing hidden valleys where the familiar earth trembled. A single glance at the horizon where the horizon once was a horizon of solitude now holds the promise of a sky shared with others. Perspective isn’t a fixed point; it’s a dance of light and shadow, where the shadow of one moment becomes the light in another.
