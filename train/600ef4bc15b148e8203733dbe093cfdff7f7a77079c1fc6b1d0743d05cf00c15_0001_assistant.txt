Thomas Stearns Eliot was an American and later English writer who published as T. S. Eliot:

•	Born September 26, 1888, in St. Louis, MO; died January 4, 1965, in London, England). 
•	Considered to be one of the 20th-centuries greatest poets.
•	Received Nobel Prize for Literature in 1948.
•	Best known for his poems “The Wasteland” and “The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock."
•	Meeting Ezra Pound in 1914 was critical for both his career and his writing skills as Pound introduced him to everyone in the literary field and served as reader and editor for much of Eliot’s 
    work.
•	His novel, “Old Possum’s Book of Practical Cats” served as the source material for the Broadway hit musical “Cats.”
