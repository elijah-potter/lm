Potty training a puppy can be a challenge, but it is definitely possible with patience. And remember consistency is key. Here are a few tips to help you get started:

- Take your puppy outside to go potty first thing in the morning, after eating or drinking, after playtime, and before bed.
- Watch your puppy closely for signs that they need to go potty, such as sniffing, circling, staring, or whining.
- If you catch your puppy in the act of going potty inside, immediately take them outside and have them finish there.
- Every time your puppy successfully goes potty outside immediately say "yes" and reward them with a treat. Always reward their positive behavior.
- If your puppy has an accident, do not punish them. This will only make them fear you and the potty training experience. Simply clean up the accident and be on the lookout for the next opportunity to take your puppy out. 

With patience and consistency, you can potty train your puppy in no time!
