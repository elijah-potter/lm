In the quiet hum of a city that sleeps,
a café’s lights flicker like distant stars.
The air hums with the sound of a coffee machine,
slowly turning, patient as the dawn.

She sits at a table where the shadows stretch,
each one a whispered secret from a stranger’s past.
The first sip is bitter, like the weight of time—
but the second melts away the edges of her mind.

Outside, the world breathes in rhythm—cars, the wind,
a single bird’s song cutting through the noise.
The coffee’s warmth spills into her hands,
softening the edges of her sharpest fears.

She smiles, not at the world, but at the moment,
where every detail feels like a quiet promise.
The city wakes again, but she is already home.
