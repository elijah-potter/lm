**Daily Work Schedule for Productivity**
**Monday–Friday**

---
**Morning (7:00 AM – 10:00 AM)**
- Breakfast: 7:00–7:30
- Deep Work: 7:45–9:30 (Focus: Core Tasks)
- Exercise/Yoga: 8:30–9:00

**Afternoon (10:00 AM – 4:00 PM)**
- Meetings: 10:00–11:30 (Client/Project Updates)
- Admin: 11:30–12:30 (Emails, Follow-ups)
- Lunch: 12:30–1:00

**Evening (4:00 PM – 9:00 PM)**
- Creative Work: 4:00–6:00 (Writing/Design)
- Team Sync: 6:00–6:30 (Collaboration)
- Dinner: 6:30–7:00
- Flexible: 7:00–9:00 (Reading/Relaxation)

---
**Weekend (Saturday–Sunday)**
- Light Tasks: 9:00 AM–12:00 PM (Non-essential)
- Family/Outings: 12:00 PM–6:00 PM
- Wind-Down: Evening (Digital Detox)

---
**Notes:**
- Buffer time between tasks for transitions.
- Prioritize 2–3 high-focus hours daily.
- Adjust based on energy/emergencies.
