When producing concrete of the highest quality, it is important to reach the correct water contents and have an efficient process for mixing water into the concrete.
The water should be mixed quickly into the concrete. This is done by distributing the water over as large a surface as possible. Correct batching of water is a result of the interaction between moisture readings, controlling the water batching process and the optimal mechanical equipment.
Water weigh hoppers are hot-dip galvanized and can be used for both clean and recycled water.
Batching water through a flow meter is an accurate and fully tested measuring method.
The flushing equipment is used both for batching water and for keeping the mixer clean.
Taking moisture measurements in the mixer ensures uniformly mixed concrete.
