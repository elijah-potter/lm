It's great you're showing support for a cause! I have a few good ideas that I think you'll like.

- Stretch bracelets or anklets - Stretch bracelets and anklets are quick and easy to make and offer the option to mix and match different colors to represent the different Pride flags. You could also incorporate letter beads to say different things like "Love is Love," or "Pride."

- Multi-stranded beaded necklaces - Long necklaces that can be wrapped around the neck a few times offer more mixing and matching of colors. They would take a little longer to make but are still a simple design, and no clasp would be needed.

- Ally jewelry - You could make similar items like the previous suggestions but have them geared more toward people who are allies of the community as well. It would be a great way to extend support.
