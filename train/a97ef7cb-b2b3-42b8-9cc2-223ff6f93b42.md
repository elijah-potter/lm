The **endless loop** of maintenance where servers must be patched, updated, and monitored indefinitely. Documentation remains a moving target, ever-changing with new requirements but never fully capturing the current state. A project’s scope expands indefinitely as new dependencies, edge cases, or user demands emerge, defying any fixed completion date.

Legacy systems persist despite deprecation warnings, their maintenance tied to operational necessity rather than design intent. The cycle of "fixing what breaks" never ends—only evolves, shifting focus from core goals to firefighting. A process that begins as a structured task spirals into an unending administrative burden, where every iteration introduces new constraints without resolving the original problem.

The illusion of progress is measured in minor updates or partial successes, but the true result is stagnation. A to-do list that never closes, where every task is both necessary and infinite.
