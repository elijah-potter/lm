Alone at night, a man sits in front of the glowing, mesmerizing monitor. 
The keyboard produces a loud "clack" noise from every key. 
Clack, clack, clack, clack. 
The man, entranced by the monitor, darting his eyes back and forward, looking to see if there are any problems with his code.
He wanted to make sure his code was perfect. He has the opportunity to become one of the best employees in his company. 
Clack, clack, clack, clack. 
There was a yawn in the air, maybe he was more tired than he thought. The man stands up and stretches. He walks around the room, trying to reset his.
Then he notices something outside.
Clack, clack, clack, clack.
But he's not at the keyboard, who could be making that sound?
He takes a couple of steps toward the window.
Clack, clack, clack, clack.
The "Clack" gets louder and louder with each step the man takes toward the window.
Uncomfortable with the noise, he grabs the lamp off the desk.
CLACK, CLACK, CLACK, CLACK.
He looks outside, fearful of what might happen.
The "Clacks" stop. and a lonely cat is seen across the window, running away.
Left behind, is one beat-up mechanical keyboard, loud enough to annoy even the most entranced workers.
The man puts back the lamp and walks out to get some water.
Clack, clack, clack, clack
