To All Valued Employees:

First, let us say thank you for your continued dedication and service to the great company that is McDonald’s. We appreciate every single one of you. 

In regard to the current situation, we have an important announcement to make. We are fully aware of the rumors circulating about our beef hamburgers, and we want to state clearly right now that there is no truth in them. Our burgers have no meat other than cow meat. 

We understand that this may be a challenging time for you as McDonald’s employees, and you may be asked by friends and even the press about the situation. 

So we ask that until this blows over, you help the company by answering to all who ask that the rumors are categorically untrue. 

We thank you for your continued dedication to McDonald’s and consideration during this challenging time. We will come through this stronger than ever.
