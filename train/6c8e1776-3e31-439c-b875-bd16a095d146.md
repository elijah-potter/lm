**To Whom It May Concern,**

While the decision to draft this letter was met with hesitation, the act of writing it has become a necessary exercise in self-reflection. The recipient remains unknown, and the intent behind its composition is as ambiguous as the final destination of its words.

This document is a placeholder, a pause in the narrative of our lives. The words are typed, the ink is dry, yet the letter is never sent—because the absence of a reply is its only reply. The silence that follows is not silence to be ignored, but a silence that answers itself.

If you were to read this, know that you are not the intended audience. This was not meant for your eyes, but for the space between the sender and the sender’s regret. It is a letter that outlives the moment of its creation, lingering like a ghost in the margins of possibility.

No signature.
No address.
No resolution.

Just the quiet understanding that some letters are never meant to be opened.
