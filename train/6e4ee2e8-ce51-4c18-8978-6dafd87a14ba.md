Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a tiny bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the garden. One sunny morning, Benny spotted a shiny red berry near a big, leafy bush. "Yum!" Benny thought, and he happily nibbled on it.

Just then, Benny heard a tiny "squeak!" coming from under a bush. Benny peeked and saw a little mouse named Mimi, her eyes wide with surprise. "Mimi," Benny said gently, "why are you hiding?"

Mimi looked at Benny and said, "I don’t want to scare you, but I’m scared of the big, dark shadow outside!"

Benny hopped closer and whispered, "I’ll help you. Let’s find a safe spot together." They crawled under a blanket of soft green grass and curled up together. Benny told Mimi, "It’s okay to be scared. We’re friends now!"

Soon, the sun peeked out from behind the clouds, and the shadow disappeared. Mimi giggled. "Thank you, Benny! I feel brave now."

From that day on, Benny and Mimi became the best of friends, sharing stories and adventures under the big red sky. They learned that being kind and brave makes the world a little brighter and friendlier for everyone.
