There was a large, empty house in an abandoned forest called Amber Grove. It was, of course, the topic of many scary stories recollected to young children to beware. Unearthly sounds would strike, and apparitions would materialize out of thin air, or so the townspeople said.

But Miriam was an intrepid explorer and was always up for an adventure. One day, she rolled out of bed and decided to take a morning stroll right up to the front porch of the Amber Grove house! She firmly knocked on the door and ignored the sweeping chill around her shoulders and the foggy windows looming above.

When she opened the door, she was immediately met with the long, hooked nose of a witch! Miriam screamed in terror when all of a sudden, the seemingly-mean witch was offering her a…cookie?

At first, Miram was suspicious, but she tasted the cookie, and it was her favorite—chocolate chip! Miriam thanked the witch, and she smiled, lamenting how sad it was that everyone avoided such a nice place. Miriam told everyone in the village, and they came to celebrate the Amber Grove house and befriended the old witch.
