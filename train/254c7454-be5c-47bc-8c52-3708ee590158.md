## Project Chimera: A Cooperative Research Game

**A game for 2-4 players. Ages 14+. Playing time: 90-120 minutes.**

**Designer’s Note:** *Project Chimera is designed to explore the complexities of scientific research, crisis management, and ethical decision-making. The game aims to illustrate how seemingly beneficial advancements can have unforeseen consequences and how collaboration, even amidst disagreement, is essential for navigating global challenges. The scenarios presented are fictional, but the underlying themes of scientific responsibility and the potential for unintended outcomes are intended to spark thoughtful discussion and reflection.*

### 1. Components

*   **Research Board:** A central game board depicting a global map with key research facilities and crisis zones.
*   **Researcher Cards (12):** Each card represents a unique researcher with specialized skills and starting resources.
*   **Crisis Cards (60):** Represent escalating global events triggered by the Chimera outbreak. Divided into three tiers of increasing severity.
*   **Research Cards (80):** Represent scientific breakthroughs and potential solutions. Divided into four disciplines: Biology, Engineering, Data Analysis, and Ethics.
*   **Chimera Strain Tokens (40):** Represent the spread of the Chimera pathogen.
*   **Resource Tokens (120):** Represent Funding (blue), Expertise (green), and Equipment (yellow).
*   **Containment Markers (20):** Used to mitigate Chimera spread in crisis zones.
*   **Event Die (6-sided):** Used to determine random events and Chimera spread.
*   **First Player Marker.**
*   **Rulebook (this document).**

### 2. Setup

1.  Place the Research Board in the center of the playing area.
2.  Shuffle the Crisis Cards and divide them into three Crisis Decks (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 2). Place each deck face down on its designated space on the Research Board.
3.  Shuffle the Research Cards and place them face down to form the Research Deck. Reveal the top five cards and place them face up beside the deck, forming the Research Row.
4.  Each player chooses a Researcher Card. Each Researcher begins with the resources indicated on their card.
5.  Place a predetermined number of Chimera Strain Tokens on the starting crisis zones indicated on the Research Board (see Difficulty Scaling below).
6.  Place a predetermined number of Containment Markers on the starting crisis zones (see Difficulty Scaling below).
7.  Determine the first player randomly and give them the First Player Marker.

### 3. Turn Structure

Each turn consists of the following phases, performed in order:

**1. Researcher Phase:** Each player, in turn order, performs two actions. Available actions are:

*   **Gather Resources:** Gain 2 of any single resource type (Funding, Expertise, or Equipment).
*   **Move:** Move your Researcher to an adjacent facility on the Research Board.
*   **Research:** Discard 1 Expertise and 1 Funding to draw two Research Cards from the Research Deck. Keep one and discard the other face down.
*   **Develop Research:** Discard the resources indicated on a Research Card in the Research Row to add it to your hand.
*   **Containment:** Discard 1 Expertise and 1 Equipment to place a Containment Marker in the facility where your Researcher is located.
*   **Share Resources:** Give 1 or 2 resources of the same type to another Researcher in the same facility.
*   **Discard Research:** Discard a Research Card from your hand.

**2. Chimera Phase:**

1.  **Spread:** Roll the Event Die. On a result of 1-3, place one Chimera Strain Token on a crisis zone adjacent to a zone already containing Chimera Strain Tokens. Prioritize zones with fewer Containment Markers.
2.  **Crisis Event:** Draw the top card from the current Crisis Deck (Tier 1 for the first 5 rounds, Tier 2 for the next 5 rounds, Tier 3 for the final 5 rounds). Resolve the card's effects immediately. Crisis Cards often introduce new challenges, resource penalties, or Chimera spread.

**3. Research Row Refresh:** Discard any Research Cards from the Research Row that have been developed. Draw new cards from the Research Deck to refill the Research Row to five cards.

### 4. Win and Loss Conditions

**Win Condition:** The players win the game if they successfully develop and implement three "Cure" Research Cards (identified by a distinct symbol on the card) before the Loss Condition is met. These Cure Cards must be developed and then discarded as a group action during a player's turn, requiring a shared expenditure of 3 Funding, 3 Expertise, and 3 Equipment.

**Loss Conditions:** The players lose the game if:

*   The Chimera Strain Tokens exceed the maximum capacity of any single crisis zone (indicated on the Research Board).
*   The Crisis Deck runs out.
*   A player is unable to perform any actions during their Researcher Phase due to resource depletion or other penalties.

### 5. Advanced Variants & Trade-offs

These variants introduce additional layers of complexity and force difficult choices.

**1. Ethical Dilemma Cards:** Introduce a small deck of Ethical Dilemma Cards. At the end of each round, draw one card and resolve its effect. These cards present players with moral quandaries that impact resource availability or trigger negative consequences. *Trade-off:* Gaining short-term benefits might incur long-term ethical costs, requiring players to balance scientific progress with moral responsibility.

**2. Specialization Lockout:**  At the start of the game, randomly assign each player a Research Discipline (Biology, Engineering, Data Analysis, Ethics). Players are more effective when developing Research Cards in their assigned discipline (reducing the resource cost by 1), but are penalized when developing cards outside their specialization (increasing the resource cost by 1). *Trade-off:* Encourages collaboration and resource sharing but creates dependencies and limits individual flexibility.

**3. Contested Research:** When developing a Research Card, another player may choose to "contest" the development by discarding a resource of their choice. This adds a random element and introduces uncertainty into the development process. *Trade-off:*  Introduces an element of player interaction and can disrupt carefully laid plans, forcing players to adapt and negotiate.

### 6. Difficulty Scaling

**2 Players:**

*   Start with 3 Chimera Strain Tokens on each starting crisis zone.
*   Place 1 Containment Marker on each starting crisis zone.

**3 Players:**

*   Start with 4 Chimera Strain Tokens on each starting crisis zone.
*   Place 1 Containment Marker on each starting crisis zone.

**4 Players:**

*   Start with 5 Chimera Strain Tokens on each starting crisis zone.
*   Place 1 Containment Marker on each starting crisis zone.



The number of starting Chimera Strain Tokens and Containment Markers can be adjusted further to fine-tune the difficulty level.
