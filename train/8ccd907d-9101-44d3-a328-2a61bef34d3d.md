## Bookstore Serendipity (Powell's City of Books, Portland)

The rain was falling steadily outside, a soft percussion against the large windows of Powell’s, and I was lost, as I often am, amidst the towering shelves of the fiction section. You were browsing the poetry, your fingers tracing the spines with a quiet intensity. I noticed the way the light caught the auburn highlights in your hair, a warm contrast to the grey afternoon. We didn't speak, but I recall the distinct scent of old paper and leather emanating from the books around us, a comforting and familiar aroma. You were wearing a dark green scarf, the color echoing the muted tones of the room. I left shortly thereafter, feeling a peculiar sense of regret at the unspoken possibilities that lingered in the air. I often return, hoping for another glimpse, another shared moment of quiet appreciation for the written word. 

If you were the person admiring Rilke near the poetry section last Tuesday afternoon, please reply with your favorite poet.
