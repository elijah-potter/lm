The title *The Grapes of Wrath* was suggested to Steinbeck by his wife. She had heard the phrase in the second line of the civil war song, *The Battle Hymn of the Republic.* Some sources claim the title comes directly from a verse in the Book of Revelation. However, while *The Battle Hymn of the Republic*'s author, Julia Ward Howe, was inspired by that verse, Steinbeck was not.
