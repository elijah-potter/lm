I put on the VR headset with glee; I'm so excited! At first, I was a little nervous, but that was because I didn't know where the On button was on this headset. Lights start flashing in front of my eyes. Then, there it was, New World Online. I finally get to try out this game! The first thing to do is to create a character. I already had an idea in the back of my mind. I try to make this character as cute as possible. Bright yellow hair. A long skirt. I wanted a different shirt, but the game wouldn't let me put it on. Ah well. This basic tunic will have to do. The next thing is to pick a class. There are so many to choose from. There's a class with a shield that has high defense. Another class has a staff! That one looks like it's good for magic-type attacks. "Hmmm," I ponder to myself, and the perfect weapon shows up in from of me. A long spear with a pointy tip. That looks like it's useful! I pick the spear, squealing with excitement! Now for the last thing, a name for my character! I shouldn't use my real name, since everyone can see it. But it has to be a good one since I'll be the one playing the character! My hands are hovering over the virtual keyboard. My eyes are darting back and forth, looking for the first letter that my hands hover over. Then it hits me. I type out the name with joy. "A....P....P....L...E!" With that, I'm done creating my character! I am ready to start my journey into New World Online. I wonder what kind of adventures I'll go on. I can't wait!
