## Product Requirements Document: "Context" - Knowledge Synthesis App

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers are increasingly overwhelmed by the sheer volume of information required for their roles, struggling to efficiently synthesize disparate sources into actionable insights and facing difficulty recalling relevant information when needed.

**Vision:** To empower knowledge workers with a seamless, intuitive platform for capturing, connecting, and recalling information, fostering deeper understanding and accelerating decision-making.

---

### User Personas

**1. Amelia Chen - Research Analyst**

*   **Goal:** To quickly synthesize large volumes of research papers, reports, and industry data to identify trends and inform strategic recommendations for her firm.
*   **Pain Points:**  Spends excessive time manually organizing and cross-referencing information from various sources.  Difficulty recalling specific details from past research.  Feels overwhelmed by the sheer volume of data and struggles to discern key takeaways.

**2. David Ramirez - Consultant**

*   **Goal:** To efficiently build and maintain a personalized knowledge base of client interactions, industry best practices, and relevant case studies to deliver exceptional service and secure repeat business.
*   **Pain Points:**  Relies on scattered notes and documents, making it difficult to recall specific details from past engagements.  Loses time recreating information for recurring client needs.  Concerned about maintaining confidentiality and security of sensitive client data.

**3. Eleanor Vance - Product Manager**

*   **Goal:** To rapidly gather and synthesize feedback from user interviews, market research, and competitive analysis to inform product strategy and prioritization decisions.
*   **Pain Points:**  Struggles to connect disparate pieces of feedback and identify recurring themes.  Finds it challenging to share synthesized insights with her team in a clear and concise manner.  Spends too much time manually compiling and organizing information.

---

### Requirements

**1. Intelligent Information Capture:** The app must allow users to capture information from web pages, documents (PDF, DOCX), and other digital sources with a single click, automatically extracting key text and metadata.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:**  Capture functionality works reliably across major web browsers and document formats.  Extracted text is accurate and retains formatting where possible. Metadata (title, URL, author) is automatically populated.
    *   **Success Metric:** 80% of users report ease of use for information capture.

**2. Contextual Linking & Relationship Building:** Users must be able to create explicit links between captured information, enabling the construction of knowledge networks and revealing hidden connections.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can easily create links between captured items. Linked items are visually connected within the app's interface. Users can add notes and descriptions to links.
    *   **Success Metric:** 60% of users actively use the linking functionality within the first month.

**3. Search & Retrieval with Semantic Understanding:** The app must provide a powerful search function that understands the context and meaning of user queries, surfacing relevant information even when keywords are imprecise.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Search results are ranked by relevance. Users can filter search results by date, source, and tags. The search function understands synonyms and related concepts.
    *   **Success Metric:**  Users find relevant information within three searches on average.

**4. Private & Secure Knowledge Vault:** The app must provide a secure, encrypted storage environment for user-captured information, with granular access control options.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Data is encrypted both in transit and at rest. Users can define sharing permissions for individual items or collections.  The app adheres to industry-standard security protocols.
    *   **Success Metric:**  Zero reported security breaches within the first six months.

**5. Collection Organization & Sharing:** Users must be able to organize captured information into collections, and selectively share collections with other users or groups, with varying permission levels.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can create, rename, and delete collections. Users can add and remove items from collections. Users can define viewing and editing permissions for shared collections.
    *   **Success Metric:** 40% of users actively use collection sharing within the first month.



---

### Technical Considerations

*   **Platform:** iOS and Android native applications. Web application for desktop access is a potential future consideration.
*   **Backend:**  Scalable cloud infrastructure (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud) to handle growing data volumes and user base.
*   **Data Storage:**  Secure, encrypted database (e.g., PostgreSQL) with robust backup and recovery mechanisms.
*   **Search Indexing:**  Utilize a dedicated search indexing engine (e.g., Elasticsearch) for fast and accurate search results.
*   **Security:** Implement multi-factor authentication and adhere to OWASP security guidelines.



### Risks

*   **Data Privacy Concerns:**  Users may be hesitant to store sensitive information within the app. Mitigation:  Transparent privacy policy and robust security measures.
*   **Adoption Challenges:**  Users may be resistant to changing their existing workflows. Mitigation:  Intuitive user interface and targeted onboarding.
*   **Scalability Issues:**  The app may struggle to handle a large number of users and data volumes. Mitigation:  Proactive infrastructure monitoring and scaling.



### Non-Goals

*   Real-time collaboration features (e.g., shared editing of documents).
*   Integration with third-party note-taking applications (e.g., Evernote, OneNote).
*   Automatic summarization of captured information.
*   Offline access beyond recently viewed items.




---

### Launch Checklist

1.  Complete end-to-end user acceptance testing with representative users from each persona.
2.  Finalize privacy policy and terms of service.
3.  Set up secure backend infrastructure and monitoring.
4.  Prepare marketing materials and launch announcement.
5.  Train customer support team on app functionality and troubleshooting.
6.  Establish a system for collecting and prioritizing user feedback post-launch.
