Power Rangers Values
Ever since the original Mighty Morphin Power Rangers' debut in 1993, the Power Rangers have promoted important values.
Health and Physical Fitness
The physical action in the Power Rangers inspires kids to get up and move around. Imitating Power Ranger poses and moves promotes early patterns of exercise.
