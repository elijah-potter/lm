The shifting sands of an ever-changing desert plateau. Rivers carve ephemeral paths through shifting dunes, and the wind sculpts ephemeral dunes that rise, collapse, and reform overnight. Ancient petroglyphs fade under the sun, leaving only ghostly outlines. The sky is a canvas of perpetual motion, clouds dissolving into mist before they fully form. Buildings constructed from local materials crumble into dust, their foundations lost to the relentless march of erosion. Even time itself seems suspended—days blend into one another, and the notion of permanence is a fleeting illusion, like the whisper of the wind through the dunes.
