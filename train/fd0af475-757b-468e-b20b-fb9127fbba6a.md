The old watcher stood at the window, its brass hands frozen in time at 11:47. Outside, the neon glow of the city pulsed like a slow heartbeat, casting long shadows across the cracked pavement. The memory of that night—his mother’s voice, sharp as a blade, whispering *"You’ll never be enough"*—still stung. He clenched his jaw, teeth grinding against the pain, and forced his mind to shift.

A plan, not a prayer. That was what he’d learned from the files, the ones he’d stolen from the archives when he was sixteen. *"Distract, observe, adapt."* The first step was simple: find the one who’d sent the message to his mother’s name. The second was to ensure he didn’t get caught.

His phone buzzed—a text from an unknown number. **"Meet at the old clock tower. Midnight."** The name beneath it read *"Vesper."* He exhaled, fingers tightening around the screen. This was where the game began.
