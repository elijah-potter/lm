What Is Anorexia?
Ano means “without” anorexia means “appetite.” So, anorexia is without appetite. People with anorexia have a distorted body image and see themselves as overweight even when they are dangerously thin. The diet and exercise excessively to lose weight. Anorexia is a serious eating disorder that can lead to death from starvation or from medical complications related to malnutrition.
Anorexia is most common in adolescent girls and young women, but it can affect people of any age, gender, or background. An estimated 0.35% to 0.55% of females suffer from anorexia nervosa in their lifetime, and the disorder has the second-highest mortality rate of any mental illness (after opioid addiction).
This type of disorder is caused by a complex interaction of psychological, social, and cultural factors. Genetics may also play a role.
If you or someone you know has anorexia, it’s important to get professional help. Anorexia is treatable, but the sooner treatment starts, the better the chances for recovery.
