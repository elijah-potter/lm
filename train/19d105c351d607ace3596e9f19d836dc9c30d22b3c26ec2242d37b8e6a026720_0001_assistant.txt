Although a three-peat has been accomplished several times in the NBA's history, only three franchises have completed this difficult feat. These teams include:

• Minneapolis Lakers - 1952, 1953, 1954
• Boston Celtics - 1959-1966 (8)
• Chicago Bulls - 1991, 1992, 1993; 1996, 1997, 1998
• Los Angeles Lakers - 2000, 2001, 2002
