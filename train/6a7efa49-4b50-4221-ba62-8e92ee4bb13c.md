The first time I noticed it, I was sorting through old receipts in the attic.
A date repeated: **12.31.2022**—twice. Then another: **05.01.2023**—again.
At first, I dismissed it as coincidence—someone forgot to erase duplicates.
But then came **01.01.2024**, then **06.02.2024**, each year trimming by a day.
Months shifted by one. Years by one.
I traced it back to the last box: **08.08.2020**, a single entry.
A date that didn’t fit the pattern.

Now I keep an eye on the calendar.
