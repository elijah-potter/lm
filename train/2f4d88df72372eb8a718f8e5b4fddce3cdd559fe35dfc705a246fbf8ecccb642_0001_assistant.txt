Here are three holiday destinations that might appeal to someone who doesn't enjoy Christmas:

1. Istanbul: Largely populated by Muslims, there may be some Christian communities but the Christmas decorating is not anywhere near on par with United States. The Grand Bazaar offers a variety of traditional and colorful lanterns and souvenirs.
2. Bangkok:  Thailand's population is majority Buddhist, and Christmas is not a major holiday despite having a fair amount of Christians. Secondly, Bangkok's proximity to sandy beaches makes it easy to forget that it is Christmas time.
3.  Marrakech: Another population that is predominantly Muslim making Christmas just another day. Enjoy your Christmas as you shop for souvenirs in Djemaa el-Fna, Marrakech's main square. The perfect place to pick up some gifts.
