A dimly lit office. The door creaks open slightly, revealing **Daniel**, hunched over a laptop with a half-smile playing on his lips. His fingers tap absently on the keyboard, fingers twitching—just enough to suggest he’s still engaged.

**Sarah** enters, carrying a stack of files. She sets them down on his desk, her gaze flickering between him and the screen.

*"I’ve got this new client—need your input on the proposal draft by Friday,"* she says, voice casual, but her eyes linger on him. *"You’re the one who always says you’ll review it first thing tomorrow, right?"*

Daniel’s smile widens, but his eyes stay fixed on the screen. *"Yeah, yeah, I’ll look it over tonight,"* he replies, voice low. His pen scratches across a sticky note beside his monitor, leaving a single, deliberate question mark.

Sarah nods, but doesn’t press further. She turns to leave, pausing at the door. *"Thanks, Daniel. You know where to find me if you need anything."* She lingers for a second, then exits, the door shutting behind her with a soft click.

Daniel exhales sharply, then leans back in his chair. His fingers pause mid-scroll, cursor blinking. The screen flickers—just for a second—before settling on a blank line. His thumb hovers over the keyboard.
