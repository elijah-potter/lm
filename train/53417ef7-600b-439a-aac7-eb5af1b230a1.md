**The Hollow:**
A cavernous, dimly lit space beneath an abandoned town, its walls lined with faded murals of forgotten figures. The air hums with static—radio signals picked up by stray speakers. People drift through the crowd, some in silent conversation, others whispering to themselves. The ceiling is a maze of exposed pipes and rusted beams, where children sometimes play with makeshift balloons filled with smoke. No one knows why they gather here—only that they feel compelled to be in this space together. The doors are always locked, yet people find a way in.
