If the storm had delayed the train by one hour, I would have missed the chance to confront her at the café before she left for the airport. The unspoken tension between us had built too quickly—her last message, *"Just needed to think,"* felt like an invitation to linger, but my exhaustion had clouded my judgment. Instead, I’d have had to wait for her to return, and the next day, the weight of the unsaid would have settled like a forgotten burden.
