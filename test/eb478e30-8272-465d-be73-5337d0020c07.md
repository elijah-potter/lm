**Mayor’s Office: Emergency Traffic Restrictions Implemented**
Residents of **Neon Haven** are advised of new traffic regulations effective immediately. All vehicles must adhere to a mandatory **30-minute rotating shift system** starting at 7 AM. Odd-numbered license plates may operate from 7 AM–10 AM; even-numbered plates from 10 AM–1 PM. Violators will face fines of $250 or towing. Parking restrictions apply at all downtown bus stops; vehicles must vacate within 15 minutes of arrival.

**Public Health Notice: Water Supply Contamination Alert**
Authorities in **Neon Haven** have detected elevated lead levels in tap water at **7 districts** across the city. Residents are advised to boil water for **at least 3 minutes** or use bottled water until further notice. The city’s water treatment plant will undergo an emergency inspection tomorrow. Residents should report leaks or strange tastes immediately to **311-NH**.

**Local Tech Boom: New AI Research Hub Opens**
The **Neon Haven AI Initiative** has inaugurated its first campus in the former **Skybridge District**, offering free access to public labs for citizens. The initiative aims to reduce unemployment by **50%** within two years. The first event, **"Neon Dreams,"** will feature live demos and skill-sharing workshops. Registration required via the city’s portal.

**Cultural Event: Neon Haven Arts Festival**
The **Neon Haven Arts Festival** kicks off tomorrow, featuring street performances, live music, and a **free public concert** at the old **Central Park**. Food trucks from local vendors will operate until 11 PM. Admission is free, but donations welcome. Special guest: **Rocket McGee**, the city’s most popular musician, performing at 9 PM.

**Business Update: Retail Price Cap Hike Approved**
The **Neon Haven Council** has voted to raise the **retail price cap** by **15%** effective tomorrow. Affected items include electronics, imported goods, and luxury goods. Small businesses must submit justification by **Friday** to avoid penalties. The council urges citizens to shop locally for discounts.
