Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown fur and big, round ears that twitched when he was excited.

One sunny morning, Benny wiggled into his favorite patch of clover. "Mmm, this is the best place ever!" he said, nibbling happily.

Suddenly, Benny spotted something shiny in the grass—a tiny golden acorn! He picked it up and giggled. "Oh, Benny, Benny!" he squeaked.

Benny rolled the acorn around in his paws, then tucked it into his burrow. Later, as he curled up in his soft blanket, he dreamed of all the adventures the acorn might bring.

The next day, Benny woke up feeling extra sleepy. He tiptoed outside, opened the acorn, and—*whoosh!*—a little rainbow of magic sparkled inside. Benny’s ears perked up! "Whoa!" he gasped.

From that day on, every time Benny opened the acorn, a tiny, giggly sparkle would dance in his eyes. He called it his "magic acorn," and it made him the happiest bunny in the whole garden.

And that’s the story of Benny the Bunny and his magical acorn!
