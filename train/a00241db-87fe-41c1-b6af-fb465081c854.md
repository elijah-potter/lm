Once upon a time, in a big blue house with a garden full of flowers, there lived a little bunny named Pip. Pip had fluffy white ears and a pink nose that sparkled when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Pip woke up and saw his favorite toy—a red ball—rolling away. "Oh no!" Pip said, his eyes wide. He hopped out of bed and chased after it, but the ball kept rolling faster and faster.

Pip met a little squirrel named Squish who was gathering acorns. "Where are you going so fast?" Squish asked.

"I found my ball!" Pip said, his heart beating fast. Squish giggled and said, "Let me help you catch it!"

Together, they chased the ball until they rolled right into a pile of leaves. Pip and Squish giggled and played there for a while, sharing acorns and laughing.

Then, a kind old owl named Ollie flew down from the tree. "That was fun!" he said. "Why don’t you play together more often?"

Pip and Squish nodded. "We will!" they said, and Ollie hooted happily.

From that day on, Pip and Squish played together every day, chasing balls, collecting acorns, and making the biggest, happiest smiles in the garden. And they all lived very happily ever after.
