The first light of dawn painted the sky in hues of silver and gold, filtering through the mist that clung to the forest’s edges like whispered secrets. The air smelled of damp earth and pine, and the rustling leaves carried the faint, rhythmic pulse of life waking from its slumber.

Ethan stood at the edge of the clearing, his breath slow and steady. The storm had raged last night, thunderous and relentless, but now it was gone—vanished like a bad dream. The trees stood tall, their branches unbroken, as if the tempest had only been a passing shadow.

He sat on a fallen log, his fingers tracing the grain of wood, and let the silence settle around him. The only sound was the distant call of a crow, sharp and clear, as if the world itself was trying to remember how to listen.

Somewhere in the distance, the river hummed, its waters flowing like a lullaby over smooth stones. The waterfall behind him had lost its frenzy; now it cascaded gently, a slow, steady heartbeat. The calm had returned, and with it, the quiet strength that comes after the storm.

Ethan exhaled deeply, closing his eyes. The weight of yesterday’s chaos had lifted, replaced by the quiet certainty that everything—even the chaos—would eventually find its balance. The storm had passed, and the light was returning.
