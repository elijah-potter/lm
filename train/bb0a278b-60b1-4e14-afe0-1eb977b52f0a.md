**Mayor’s Office: Emergency Alert – Heavy Rain and Flooding Threatens Downtown Riverfront**

Residents of **New Haven’s Harbor** are advised to prepare for worsening weather conditions as a late-night storm dumped over 5 inches of rain, triggering rapid river overflows and breaches in the old brick embankments. Traffic along **Main Street** and **Broadway** is currently blocked; emergency crews report structural damage to the historic **Old Clock Tower** near the waterfront, where debris is piling up. Power outages are reported in zones south of **Central Park**, with no ETA for restoration.

Authorities have activated flood barriers at the **Northside Canal** entrance, but officials warn of potential containment failures if winds exceed 30 mph. The **Harbor Patrol** has deployed inflatable barriers to manage debris flow into the marina. Schools remain closed through tomorrow, and the **Metro Transit Authority** has suspended all public transit services in affected zones.

The city’s **Disaster Response Task Force** is coordinating with the **National Guard** for aerial surveys of flood zones. Residents are urged to evacuate low-lying areas along **River Drive** and **East Bay** if advised. Do not attempt to cross flooded streets—current reports indicate water levels near the **Old Mill Bridge** exceed 12 feet.

No casualties have been confirmed yet, but search teams are still assessing the **Haven’s Bay** shoreline for trapped individuals. The **Red Cross** has set up temporary shelters at the **Community Center**, with food and supplies available for displaced families.

**Next updates: 0600 EST.** Stay safe—this is a developing situation.
