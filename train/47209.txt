Solar panels are a great way to save money on energy bills, but did you know they can also add value to your home?
If you're planning on selling your home shortly, the increased value of your home from solar panels will make up for the initial investment. Solar power can potentially increase the home's value by up to 4.1% more than comparable homes with no solar panels.
The residential federal solar tax credit allows you to recoup some of the cost of a solar energy system installed in your home.
For every dollar spent on a solar system, you receive a 26% reduction on your federal income taxes. The US is set to extend the Investment Tax Credit for solar power beyond 2032. The credit will start at 30% and decrease to 26% in 2033 and 22% in 2034.
