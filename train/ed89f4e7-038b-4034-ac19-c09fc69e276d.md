The absence lingers like a ghost in the space between breaths.
A silence too thick to be ignored, yet too vast to fill.
The clock ticks in a rhythm that doesn’t match the heart’s irregular beat—each minute a hollow echo of what could’ve been.
Fingers trace the edges of memories, half-erased, like ink smudged by time.
The walls hum with a quiet urgency, a reminder that some doors are ajar, others locked forever.
The air smells like dust and old promises—stale, but not entirely forgotten.
Some things you leave behind don’t vanish; they fold into the fabric of what remains, woven into the fabric of the present.
The absence is here. It’s in the way the light catches the cracks in the furniture, the way the shadows stretch longer than they should.
It’s not just gone; it’s always been there, always been *you*.
