A decision deferred weighs heavier than one made—hesitation morphs into doubt, and the mind spirals through *what ifs*. The clock ticks past its own deadline, the weight of uncertainty settling like dust in the air. Sometimes, the pause is a shield, protecting from the sting of regret; other times, it’s a cage, trapping the self in a loop of half-thoughts. The future, once a blank canvas, now bleeds with unseen colors, each stroke a question mark.
