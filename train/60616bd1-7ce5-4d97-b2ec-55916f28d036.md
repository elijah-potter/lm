**Central Square News Hub (Moscow, 1991)**
Crowded with handwritten signs in Cyrillic, *"Русь не умрёт!"* ("Russia won't die!") clutched by men and women in worn coats. A radio transmitter hums near the makeshift podium where a former KGB official reads the latest tape—static crackles, then: *"The Soviet Union has dissolved."* Droning helicopters loom overhead, their rotors casting shadows. Children draw pictures of tanks on scrap paper. The air smells of stale coffee and gunpowder. A man in a fur hat shouts, *"Ждите! Ждите!"* ("Wait! Wait!").
