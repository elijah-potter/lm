Okay, here's a list of some furry creatures from children's stories:

- Trolls. Some trolls are said to turn to stone in the sunlight, which isn't a trait real mammals have.
- Minotaurs. A minotaur is a human with a bull's head. No human/animal hybrids exist.
- Unicorns. Most unicorns in children's books are represented as horses with horns. Real horses don't have horns.
- Fairies. Whether faires are minuscule or man-sized, they're typically humanoid creatures with wings. No real humans of any size have natural wings.
