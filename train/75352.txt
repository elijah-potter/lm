|Description: Digitization provided by the USF Libraries Digitization Center. Rare Maps. This is a map showing the chart of the bay and harbor of Pensacola, circa 1780. This map shows physical features, cities and towns, inland waters and water depths. The statement "This harbor and all the coast abound with worms" is a warning of ship worms to others travelling there. Measurements.Dimensions: 52 x 75 cm. Measurements.Resolution: Scale [ca. 1:64,000]|
Source: Joseph F. W. Des Barres, (London, : Joseph F. W. Des Barres, 1780)
