The absence lingers like a half-remembered dream—
a shadow that moves where the body once was,
a space where warmth used to hum,
now just the quiet echo of what was never said.
The air smells of old books and unspoken things,
a scent that clings to the edges of time,
waiting to be named, but never quite there.
The hands that once held this world still,
now just outlines, ghosts pressing against the glass.
You reach for the past, but it slips through fingers
like smoke through a window at dawn—
somewhere, something is still here,
but not the way it used to be.
