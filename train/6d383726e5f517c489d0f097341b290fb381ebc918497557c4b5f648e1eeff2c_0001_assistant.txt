Welcome to our Wildlife Collection of fragrant candles for animals! 
We have six different candle scents, each a combination of delicious food fragrances, complete with unique names, that will entice your wild side. 

•	The Fruit Bat Variety Show 

Evocative of nights spent high-flying through the fruit-filled rainforest of India, this candle has a scent of lush greenery with undertones of mangoes, ripe dates, avocados, and star fruits. 

•	The Vulture Culture

Distinctive for lovers of carrion cuisine, this candle features a parfait of rotting fragrances, like decomposing rabbit flesh, putrefying roadside opossum, and decayed lizard guts. 

•	An Array for Lions 

For the food-loving lions in our customer base, we offer a fresh-kill candle of blood and flesh scents from wildebeests, buffalo, antelope, and zebra. Perfect for getting in the mood for the hunt! 

•	The Giraffe Conglomeration 

You can smell the nutritious lushness of our candle specifically for giraffes! This one features layered scents of sunbaked Savannah vegetation, prickly pears, Euphorbia tree leaves, and acacia! 

•	Our Mousey Medley 

To entice our tiny furry friends, we have the Mousey Medley, an intriguing blend of peanut butter, sunbaked wheat, sweet corn kernels, and mushrooms with a hint of springtime tree bark. You may even catch a whiff of a cricket or two! 

•	The Snake Trove

Slither down for our last candle in the Wildlife Collection! Geared towards our slinky reptilian pals, this candle features an overtone of hamster fur with undertones of small deer, antelope, feathered fowl, and eggs.
