Protect Our Coast & Oceans
The “Protect Our Coast & Oceans” fund helps communities throughout California prepare for the future. Build coastal knowledge and resiliency by supporting marine science, habitat restoration, pollution awareness and prevention, and hands-on learning with experts right at the beach or lagoon. Learn more: CheckTheCoast.org
