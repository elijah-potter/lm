If you've been thinking about trying out the Nacho House food truck that's been sitting on Las Almeda Boulevard in Weehawken for the past few weeks, let me give you some advice; don't. This was the worst food truck experience I've ever had. To start with, the owner Steve is insufferably rude. I should have just walked away, but I took my chances and ordered the Nacho Tower. Not only did it take forever to be made, but it was also absolutely stone cold and tasted terrible. It was nearly inedible. Save your money and avoid the disappointment I had. One star.
