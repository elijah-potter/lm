Of and Off
'Teaching Of and Off' is an 8 slide KS2 PowerPoint presentation focusing on spelling skills and homophones. This
Powerpoint lesson explains the difference between of and off and
teaches pupils how to use of and off in the correct context. English Teaching Resources (Spelling): Of and off includes the following activities:
1. Of and off definitions and examples.
2. Consolidation of understanding activity (complete the sentences).
3. Peer assessment task.
English Teaching Resources (spelling): Of and off can be used as a starter activity, incorporated into a lesson or developed into lessons on wider spelling rules and homophones.
