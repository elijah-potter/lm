A **meta-approach** for creating non-redundant mnemonics:
*"Key+Phrase*" where the key *expands* the phrase’s meaning via context.
Example: Instead of *"R" for "Right"*, use **"R" + "Turn" → "R-Turn"**—now it’s a *directional* shortcut that hints at rotation, not just orientation.
