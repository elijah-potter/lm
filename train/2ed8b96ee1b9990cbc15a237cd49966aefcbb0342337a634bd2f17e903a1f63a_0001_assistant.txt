The Masai Mara National Park in Kenya is known for its large number and variety of animals. Some of the animals that can be seen on a safari in the Masai Mara National Park are lions, leopards, cheetahs, giraffes, hippopotamuses, zebras, crocodiles, black rhinos and many others. 

The best way to get to the Masai Mara National Park is to fly from Wilson Airport in Nairobi. It is approximately a 1-hour flight to one of the several airstrips in Masai Mara.
