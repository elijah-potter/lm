Penny cherished her grandmother's attic more than anything in the world. It was her haven, where she would indulge in hours of exploration, delicately handling the exquisite relics that adorned the space. Her favorite thing over there was the old wooden chest nestled in a dark corner of the room.
The chest would beckon to her, even when she was far away, drawing her closer with an irresistible magnetic force. One rainy day, Penny finally resolved to unveil the chest's mysteries.
She lifts the lid of the chest, and suddenly, she can’t believe what her eyes can see. It’s filled with shining gold.
The mixed feelings of surprise and happiness that she felt were short-lived – she heard a deep and gruff voice emerge from within the wooden chest.
‘What do you think you're doing?’ The chest growled.
Penny was jolted so intensely that she almost slammed the lid shut. Yet, her inquisitive nature egged her on, compelling her to stay put and listen. The voice persisted.
‘You have no right to open this chest. It belongs to another.’
Penny's confusion mounted. How could the chest belong to anyone else? She tried to question the voice about the rightful owner but held back. Instead, she listened, captivated by the voice that began to weave a tale. 
The voice belonged to a pirate who had sailed the seas eons ago. The pirate stashed his treasure in the attic.
He collected everything he had ever made and saved in his life inside this chest. Nobody in the world could know the location of his hidden and gold-filled chest except him, not even his crewmates.
The voice cautioned her that the riches were cursed, but Penny was skeptical. She put her hands inside it and filled them with gold. Her eyes followed her hands until she pulls them up to see the gold shine in the light. 
For weeks, the chest continued to talk to Penny. It spun stories of amusement and intrigue, and the two grew intimately close. Whenever Penny felt low or lonely, she would clamber up to the attic, nestle by the chest, and listen to its tales. 
Slowly, Penny began to realize that the chest was more than a voice. The pirate's spirit was trapped within its walls, imprisoned there for centuries.
