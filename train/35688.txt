Students are responsible and contribute to their own learning and classroom community with this Daily Class Log. Designed for middle and high school classes, one student takes attendance and completes the log for the class each day. Absent students use this resource to locate their missing assignments. No more "Did I miss anything when I was out?" Send the students to the classroom mailbox or class log binder system to locate the lessons from the days they were absent.
Take it a step further and have the historian place the daily handouts behind the log with the absent student's name on them. For hassle free attendance support, I printed an excel sheet with students names and two weeks of dates with a simple key for students to mark as P (present), A (absent) or L (late).
Great resource for teachers who use classroom economy systems and support students who self advocate. Product is in publisher form for easy edits and includes an example sheet to show how to use the log appropriately.
