A fog-choked harbor at dawn. Rusted cranes loom over the still water, their skeletal arms dangling like half-finished sculptures. The air hums with the faint, rhythmic *thump-thump* of waves against the pilings. On the dock, a single wooden bench sits under a sagging awning, its faded paint peeling into the gutter. The ground is a mosaic of broken glass, driftwood, and the occasional discarded fishing net. A lone figure moves along the pier, their shadow stretched long and indistinct against the horizon. The absence of movement tells more than any action ever could—just the weight of silence between the buildings, the sea, and the weight of time.
