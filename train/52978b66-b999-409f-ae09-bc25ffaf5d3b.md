**Emerging urban hub at the confluence of 12th Avenue and River Drive**
Neon-lit corridors of modular prefab housing (wrapped in salvaged shipping containers) stretch toward the new bridge under construction, its cables humming with solar-powered transit shuttles. The air smells of ozone from the experimental bioluminescent streetlights and the faint metallic tang of recycled steel from the scrapyard next door. Locals in high-vis jackets navigate the makeshift bike lanes carved into the cracked pavement, while a crowd of scavengers trades goods at the floating market docked at the old dockyards. The waterfront plaza is half-sunk into the earth, its raised walkways covered in kinetic tiles that power the local café’s espresso machines. The new routine: **sunrise bike commutes to the solar panel farms**, **midday workshops in the repurposed warehouses**, **evening communal storytelling under the LED canopy**, and **midnight black-market tech exchanges in the repurposed subway tunnels**. The gridlines of the old city are fading; the new one is still in flux, but the rhythm is already forming.
