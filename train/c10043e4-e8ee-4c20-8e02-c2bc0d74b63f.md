In the dim glow of the cracked fluorescent light, Elias sat cross-legged on the floor, fingers tracing the worn spine of an old book. Outside, the city hummed—a distant sirens, a car’s engine, the occasional shout. He had chosen this silence, not because he sought solitude, but because it was the only thing that felt honest.

His phone buzzed. A text from his old friend, *Lena*: *"You’re not answering. You’re hiding."* Elias stared at the screen, then deleted the message. The silence wasn’t about escape—it was about the weight of what he hadn’t said in years. He let the book fall open to a blank page. A single word, written in ink: *"Now."*
