The weight of the unsolved question settled like fog dissolving at dawn—
no longer a question, but a quiet hum of *maybe* beneath the weight of the known.
The doubt wasn’t the absence of certainty, but the *kind* of absence:
a space where the certainty didn’t have to fill it, where hesitation was just the floor, and relief was the first step down.

The hands, once clenched in the grip of the unknown, loosened.
The voice, once drowned in the fear of not knowing, found its breath.
And for a moment, the doubt wasn’t proof of weakness—it was the map’s first unmarked coordinate, the pause before the path could be traced.
