Here are three trails in Portland. Happy Hiking!
1. Maple Trail Loop in Forest Park: This park is one of the largest urban forests in the United States. Hike over 80 miles of trails, fire lanes, and forest roads.
2. Eagle Creek to Punch Bowl Falls in Columbia River Gorge: Hike 13.1 miles from the Columbia River George to the Pacific Crest Trail and Mount Hood, or hike a 4.2-mile round-trip hike to Punch Bowl Falls.
3. Marquam Trail to Council Crest: This crest is one of the high points in Portland at 1,000 feet. Take in the horizon views of Cascade Mountain peaks.
