Let’s talk my friend, by and by, 
just the cockroaches, you, and I.
Please know that my intent is not to be mean,
but I’m not patient like Gandhi or Mr. Clean. 

Alone, I’ve fought the dragons in the sink,
but now I’m nearly on the brink. 
I need a sidekick on this quest,
to rid our kingdom of this mess.

The flies have told me that you’re able,
to move their mansion off the table.
But you are kind to them, and see,
how they buzz about so merrily.

Come and help me end their fun.
One trip to the bin, and it’s all done!
The rats are next; let’s dump their pile
It’s been there for awhile. 

You could be the perfect roomie, 
All it needs is a swipe of the broom, see?
Just one little brushing, simple and fast.
We will finally see the floor again, at last!
