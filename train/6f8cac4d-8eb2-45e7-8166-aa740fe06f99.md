**Part 1: The Journey Begins**
In a quiet village nestled between rolling hills, the old clock tower struck midnight. A lone child, **Eli**, wandered past the abandoned bakery where his mother once worked. The scent of fresh bread lingered, but the shop was dark, its windows cracked. A small, half-eaten loaf of bread lay on the counter, untouched since the day Eli’s mother vanished years ago.

---

**Part 2: Two Paths**

**Ending A: The Truth Beneath the Dust**
Eli picked up the bread, its crust warm from the oven’s last fire. As he bit into it, the floor beneath him trembled. The walls of the bakery shimmered, revealing a hidden staircase descending into the earth. At the bottom, a woman in a tattered apron—his mother—stood, her eyes reflecting the same sorrow Eli had carried. She pressed a silver key into his palm. *"You were supposed to find this,"* she whispered. *"The village’s secrets are buried here. But only you can keep them."*

The bakery dissolved into mist, leaving Eli standing in a field of wildflowers. The next morning, he returned to the village, now carrying a lantern and a new purpose. The villagers whispered, but Eli stood firm: *"I found her. She’s coming home."*

---

**Ending B: The Weight of Silence**
Eli tossed the bread into the fire, its scent turning bitter. The bakery’s walls closed around him, trapping the memory of his mother’s last words: *"The bread is a lie. The villagers feed on it."* He ran—not back to the village, but into the forest, where the trees whispered secrets in a language older than men. A stranger, cloaked in shadows, offered him a vial of black liquid. *"Drink,"* they murmured, *"and you’ll remember what you forgot."*

The forest dissolved into static. When Eli awoke, the village was gone, replaced by a vast desert where the wind carried only echoes. His mother’s voice still haunted him, but now she was just another story he had chosen to believe. He set up a new life, leaving the bread behind—because some truths, like bread, were best left untasted.
