These stoves also play an important role in raising world temperature by emitting anti-green gases.

They say, “We felt great pain knowing that merry making families destroy in cooking activity.”
Theirs day in and day out labor proved fruitful and they invented smoke and gas sucker stove with the financial support of well-off people.
In India thousands of women are using this stove and now they have no fear to their lives. This stove will soon be available in other Asian countries as well.
It burns the dangerous smoke and poisonous matter before emitting in the air.
Moreover, it has not only reduced the 80% excretion of poisonous elements but also uses 60% less fuel. It has been manufactured by weightless and strong metal.
