Posters for neutropenic precautions are also available from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention and the Safe Care Campaign. Colorado Hospital Association carries five different posters pertaining to neutropenic precautions. The posters detail special instructions, such as washing hands with an alcohol-based rub and restricting pets, flowers and plants in a neutropenic person's room. One poster outlines contact precautions, while another one focuses on droplet precautions. A poster details mask and airborne precautions, from Colorado Hospital Association.
The Emergency Room Personnel poster from the CDC cautions that infection can be life-threatening in individuals with neutropenia. Safe Care Campaign offers a poster for download, referred to as The Out of Sight, Out of Mind poster. The poster developed by the CDC explains how fever in a person with neutropenia must be treated as an emergency. It also cautions that infections can quickly become serious, for those with neutropenia.
Neutropenia is a condition that causes people to have a low level of neutrophils in their blood. Neutropenic precautions are steps people must take to prevent infections if they have moderate to severe neutropenia, explains Drugs.com.Learn more about Conditions & Diseases
