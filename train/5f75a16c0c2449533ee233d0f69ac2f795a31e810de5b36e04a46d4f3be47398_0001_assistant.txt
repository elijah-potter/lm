Dwayne Johnson (The Rock) is Samoan and African Canadian. He dreamt of playing football professionally, but a series of injuries meant he was cut two months into playing in the Canadian Football League. He joined the World Wrestling Federation (WWF) in 1996, quickly becoming popular and winning his first title within a few months. He began acting in 2001, starring in movies such as The Scorpion Year, Fast and Furious, and Jumanji.
