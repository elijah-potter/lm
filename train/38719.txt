Gardens to Grow project
Cosmos Kids participates in the GARDENStoGROW project – a project for Urban Horticulture for Innovative and Inclusive Early Childhood Education, the project is co-funded by the European Erasmus+ Programme. The aim is to create educational gardens and training courses for early childhood teachers and school managers to develop basic, civic and transversal skills for the 3-6 age group.
