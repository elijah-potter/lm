A morning where gravity briefly reverses, allowing a person to effortlessly float upward without effort in their office chair. By midday, time dilation causes a 10-minute commute to appear as 2 hours, and the office clock shows 3 PM while the person is still in pajamas. Lunch is served with no spoons or forks, just floating slices of bread. Afternoon meetings are conducted via teleportation, with attendees appearing in random locations around the office. Evening meals involve antiparticles that spontaneously recombine into edible dishes. At night, the walls shift to form a moving labyrinth, and clocks run backward, making the sunrise appear as sunset. The city’s traffic lights cycle randomly, and roads disappear and reappear under their feet. The air hums with a constant low-frequency vibration, causing minor temporal distortions in conversation.

The only constant: the coffee machine always brews espresso from coffee that never spoils.
