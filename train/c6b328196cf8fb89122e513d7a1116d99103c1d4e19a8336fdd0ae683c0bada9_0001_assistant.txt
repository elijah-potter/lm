1. Cut out "empty calories" such as soda and juice to reduce the number of unnecessary calories you're consuming.
2. If you have a desk job, try to get up and briskly walk around for at least 5-10 minutes every hour. These steps add up!
3. Eat less sugar and more protein. Sugar doesn't keep you full for very long and is considered "junk" energy. Protein keeps your stomach full and your energy levels higher for a longer period!
4. Use calorie tracking apps, such as MyFitnessPal and Lose It! These applications allow you to enter the calories of what you are consuming so that you can better track your daily intake.
5. Eat smaller portions to greatly reduce the amount you're eating in a day.
