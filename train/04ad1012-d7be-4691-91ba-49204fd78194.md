Silent Market Square, a dimly lit plaza beneath a vaulted stone archway in a coastal city.
Rows of tiered seating in muted earth tones curve around a central fountain, its water cascading into a shallow pool.
Artisans at wooden stalls handle objects—glassware, woven baskets, embroidered textiles—with precise movements, murmuring only when prompted.
A single performer on a raised platform plays an instrument with minimalist, rhythmic precision, accompanied by the sound of distant waves.
Visitors stand in quiet groups, observing, sketching, or simply absorbing the ambient stillness, their faces turned inward in contemplation or absorption of the artistry.
