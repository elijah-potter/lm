Arts, Character and Education (ACE) Afterschool Program
School-age children participate in enrichment activities to supplement their education. Research shows arts-based activities are particularly effective in helping youth in underserved areas develop discipline and resilience. Children with exposure to these activities are more likely to persevere despite obstacles or set-backs.
Through project-based learning, children get additional academic instruction in a fun setting.
As always, the Q works to instill values such as respect for self and others.
