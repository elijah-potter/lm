A vast, silent void of polished black marble floors stretching into infinite horizons, its hollow echo amplifying every whisper into a chorus of spectral reverberations. The air, thick with the scent of ozone and something metallic—like rusted metal left in the void of space—presses against the skin as if the atmosphere itself is holding its breath. Overhead, a ceiling of fractured, iridescent crystal panels glows with a soft, unnatural luminescence, their edges jagged like the teeth of some forgotten cosmic predator. The walls, thin as spider silk, ripple faintly, as if breathing in time with the slow, undulating currents of unseen energy. Furniture—if you dare call it that—is sparse: a single, overstuffed chair upholstered in a fabric that shifts color with ambient light, its cushions sagging into shapes that defy physics. A desk stands in the center, its surface littered with objects that should not exist in this void: a half-empty coffee cup, a book with pages that fade like memories, and a single, crumpled note with no words, only symbols that mean nothing to the empty space. The space hums with an absence of presence—every shadow is too deep, every silence too vast, and the walls seem to whisper secrets that no one is meant to hear.
