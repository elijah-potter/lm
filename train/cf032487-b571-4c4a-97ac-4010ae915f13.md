The kitchen table was set for a meal with plates, silverware, and glasses neatly arranged. The host stood in the doorway, hands clasped behind their back, eyes scanning the room.

*"Oh, I left the butter dish in the pantry."* A pause. *"I’ll be right back."* They disappeared into the hallway, returning with a small metal container.

The missing butter dish sat empty on the table, the creamy smear of morning spread now missing. The host exhaled, shaking their head. *"Of all the things to forget."*
