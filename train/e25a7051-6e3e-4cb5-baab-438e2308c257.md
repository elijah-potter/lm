The old clock tower in Prague’s Old Town Square ticks like a dying heartbeat, its pendulum swaying in time with the weight of centuries.

At five past eleven, the last train from Karlštejn leaves without warning, its whistle a ghostly echo against the damp stone. The stationmaster, a man with hands that have held more keys than he knows, just shakes his head and says, *"They’ll come back."* But they never do. The tracks fade into the forest like a promise never fulfilled.
