The rain hammered against the windows of the dimly lit apartment as Daniel stormed past his partner, Clara, who clutched her phone, fingers trembling as they scrolled through the last message: *"We’re done."* Daniel’s boots echoed on the worn carpet, his shoulders tense. "You don’t have to do this," he muttered, turning back, but she was already halfway across the room, her voice sharp. "You *did*." The air between them crackled with unsaid words. Outside, the neon glow of the city pulsed, indifferent. Clara’s phone buzzed—a notification from an ex, *"Miss you."* Daniel’s grip tightened on the coffee mug in his hand.
