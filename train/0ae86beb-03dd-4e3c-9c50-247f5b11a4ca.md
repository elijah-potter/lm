The crumpled paper lay beneath the cracked neon sign of *The Rusty Nail Tavern*, its edges singed from decades of cigarette smoke. The ink was faded but legible enough to read:

**"12:47 AM – Last seen alive: Elias Carter. Last seen near the old mill. Bring the key. Don’t ask questions."**

Beneath it, a single photograph: a half-smile on a young man’s face, his hand clutching a rusted key. The photo was yellowed, the edges frayed, but the handprint on the glass was still clear.
