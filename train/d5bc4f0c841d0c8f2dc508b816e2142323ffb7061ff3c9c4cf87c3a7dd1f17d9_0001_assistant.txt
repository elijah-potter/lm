She caught the eye of everyone when she entered a room. It was impossible to tell if she knew it or not, because there were always so many things on her mind, but there was no doubt the tall, quiet blonde was beautiful. She had a strong and lean yet curvaceous figure, and skin slightly golden from her time in the sun. Her face was freckled, and she had arched brows and a catlike face that seemed to contrast with her dainty nose. Her features made a unique and alluring combination. She had full, rosy lips, which were always in a line, serious and expressionless. Her gaze was a serious one too, one that reflected her entire way of being. Strength and endurance radiated from her, both as unmistakable as her beauty. She had steel-blue eyes, strong and bright, resolute with purpose.
