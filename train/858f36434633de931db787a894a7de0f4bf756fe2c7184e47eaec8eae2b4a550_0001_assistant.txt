The best day of Summer is the 4th of July
With hot dogs, burgers, and fireworks galore
Everyone saying "Ooh" "Ahhh" or "More"
The 4th is a day when we turn our eyes to the sky

After the 4th we move on to Halloween
A night full of mystery, magic, and masks
A night for telling jokes, getting candy and snacks
It's a night with kids in costumes to be seen

Deep into Fall people ready for Thanksgiving
A day full of football, family, and food
At the end of the night, sleep will be good 
It's a day of remembrance and a day of great living

Last but not least is the great day of Christmas
The month of December is all for Santa Claus
The reindeer pulling sleighs with hooves and not paws
Be nice all year or you'll be left giftless
