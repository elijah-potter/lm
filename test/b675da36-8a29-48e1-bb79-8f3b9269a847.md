In the quiet morning of **New Haven’s Hollow**, a rare meteor shower lit up the sky as residents rushed to windows, their voices blending with the distant hum of the city’s ancient clock towers. Scientists at the **Hollow Institute for Advanced Studies** confirmed the phenomenon, attributing the celestial display to a fragment of a long-departed comet, believed to have entered Earth’s atmosphere over the past decade. Authorities have since cordoned off the **Central Park Observatory District**, where samples were collected before being sealed in sterile containers. Locals speculate the event could signal a shift in the city’s mysterious energy flows, long whispered about in taverns and underground forums.

Meanwhile, construction crews at the **New Haven Bridge** reported unexpected structural vibrations mid-morning, prompting a temporary halt. Engineers suspect the movement is unrelated to the meteor, though the city’s Bureau of Urban Geology insists further investigation is unnecessary. A nearby diner, *The Old Watchtower*, reported a sudden surge in patrons—all wearing the same rare **emerald-green wristbands**, some claiming to have received them as gifts from strangers overnight. Police have opened an inquiry but caution against mass panic.

The city’s official weather station reported a 2-degree temperature spike at 03:47 AM, though officials dismiss it as sensor error. Meanwhile, the **Hollow’s Old Lighthouse**, a monument to the city’s maritime past, began emitting a rhythmic, repetitive chime—said to be an anomaly in its 250-year history. The lighthouse’s caretaker, Elias Voss, described the sound as “like a heartbeat.” No one knows if it’s a warning or a message.

The mayor’s office has issued a statement calling for calm, but rumors persist online that the meteor fragment could be a signal—one that might soon be answered. The city’s first responders are on standby, and the **Central Library’s archives** have been temporarily closed for “restocking.” As of this writing, the public is advised to stay indoors between the hours of 03:00 and 05:00, when the sky is expected to darken again.
