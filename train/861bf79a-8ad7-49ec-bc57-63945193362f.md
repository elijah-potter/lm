In a dimly lit office, the assistant leaned back in their chair, fingers tapping the desk nervously. The manager’s voice was low, almost accusatory. *"Why did you skip the quarterly report deadline?"* The assistant’s eyes flickered toward the door, then back to the screen. They opened their mouth, but no words came out—just a faint cough. The manager’s chair creaked as they leaned forward, eyes narrowed. A long pause stretched, broken only by the hum of the fluorescent lights. The assistant’s knuckles turned white around their coffee cup, the liquid sloshing slightly. They exhaled sharply, then whispered, *"I was… under the weather."* The manager stared, then turned away abruptly, muttering about "urgent client meetings." Outside, the office door clicked shut behind them.
