One bright sunny day, Linda was playing in the garden when she caught sight of a strange cat she had not seen before.  Linda loved cats, and she ran over to see them.  The cat was not scared and stayed sitting on an old tree trunk.  Linda reached out her hand to stroke the cat and to her surprise, it spoke to her.   "Hi Linda, I have been waiting for you to come out into the garden."  It said.   She was so surprised that she could not speak!  "Do not be afraid Linda I will  not hurt you, I want to be your friend."  Linda blurted out, what is your name?  "My name is Pumpkin, and we are going to be the best of friends for all our lives."
