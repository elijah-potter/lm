1.	As a female born in 1775, education was not mandatory. However, her father still sent Jane and her sister to a girls' boarding school.
2.	The first four novels that Jane Austen published were anonymous.
3.	Pride and Prejudice were original to be called First Impressions. However, Jane Austen read the novel Ceceilla by Fanny Burney, and she saw the phrase Pride and Prejudice mentioned three times in the book's last paragraph in capital letters. This is what inspired the title change.
4.	Austen, who was never married, believed that women should not get married if they were not in love.
5.	Her books were not in print for the first 12 years after Austen's death. Today, her books are available in 40 languages.
6.	Austen sold the copyright of Pride and Prejudice for £110. Over the last two centuries, this novel has sold over 20 million copies.
7.	Austen died at the age of 41 from an undiagnosed disease. Over the years, there have been many theories of what her death was caused by. Originally it was thought to be Addison's disease. For years people believed she either died from cancer or tuberculosis. However, in recent years, the British Library has suggested that she died from cataracts caused by arsenic poisoning.
