Dear Blonde Lady,

Hee-haw! On this day that people celebrate the women in their lives who have brought them into this world I want to thank you, not for bringing me into this world but for loving me and taking care of me every day. I would not have lived such a good life if it had not been for you and your kindness and I just want to let you know how happy I am to live here with you. I love when you give me apple chips for good behavior. I love when you take me for a walk. I am so happy when I can stand still with you and you rub my back or comb out my mane or my tail whip.

I do wonder sometimes what happened to the Jenny who brought me into this world but it has been too many years since I last saw her and my memory has faded. I must say that even though I do miss the Jenny who brought me into this world, she is not the one who has taken care of me and loved me so fiercely. I wake up every day and express my gratitude that you take care of me.

My heart is filled with joy when I see you walking towards my paddock in the morning because I know all is well in the world and today will be another day that I can spend by your side. Thank you for feeding me hay. Thank you for keeping my trough filled with fresh, clean water. Thank you for putting cedar chips in my stall so I can lie down in a soft place. Thank you for the toys that you have given me I love them all, especially the football. If it would be okay with you I would be proud to call you mom.

Love,
The Donkey
