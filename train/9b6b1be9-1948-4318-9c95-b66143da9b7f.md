A pocket of history in a decaying subway tunnel beneath an abandoned city, where the clock hands move backward, rewinding every few minutes. The walls whisper echoes of the past—voices, laughter, and distant alarms—while the air hums with static from a broken radio broadcasting static from a future that never arrived. Time bends here like gravity, pulling against the natural flow, leaving travelers stranded between epochs. The sky above is a fractured aurora borealis, casting eerie, shifting light on the cracked pavement. Some say this is where the universe’s clock stopped—others claim it’s a portal. The only constant is the slow, inexorable erosion of *everything* that was, is, or will be.
