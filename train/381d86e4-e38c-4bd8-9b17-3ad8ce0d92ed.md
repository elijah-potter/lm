**Fragment of a Forgotten Memory**

*[The night air was cool, the streetlights casting long shadows across wet pavement.]*
**Ethan:** *"You ever think about how much we’ve changed since we met?"*
**Lena:** *"Changed? Like what?"*
**Ethan:** *"Nothing. Just… you’re not the same girl who used to ask me to recite poetry under streetlamps."* *(She didn’t respond. He’d learned silence was her way of saying yes.)*
