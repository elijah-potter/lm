Alright, here are some suggestions for backyard activities with the family this summer: 

* Have a backyard picnic. Depending on your schedule, you could have a backyard picnic for breakfast, lunch, or dinner. 
* Stargazing and/or sleeping on the trampoline. Since you have a trampoline, take advantage of the nighttime sky or even camp out if you're adventurous enough. 
* Water balloon fight. This classic summer activity is great for all ages!
* Campfire and s'mores. If you have a fire pit or even an outdoor grill, you can roast marshmallows and hot dogs for a traditional summer treat. 
* Host an outdoor movie. With a few items like a projector and a white bedsheet, you can host your own backyard movie. Don't forget the snacks!
* Build a giant Slip n' Slide. If your yard is big enough, you could make a huge slip n' slide for the kids and adults in your family to enjoy. You'll need plastic sheeting, pool noodles, and landscape pins.
