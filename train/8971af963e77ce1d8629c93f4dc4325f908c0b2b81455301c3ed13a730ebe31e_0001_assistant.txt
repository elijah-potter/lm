Luka was outside playing basketball as the sun set, he did this often to bask in the feeling of making a 3-point shot. He would practice his free throws and his off-hand layups, he often had dreams of making it to the NBA one day. Luka was good at basketball, but he could not jump high enough to dunk like the pros. It was getting darker out, which was making it more difficult for him to make shots. Luka stepped back and let a shot fly, it bounced out of the rim and rolled down the hill and into the woods. Luka sighed and grabbed his phone to use as a flashlight, he started down the hill to retrieve his ball. He shined his light through the bushes and grass, but could not find his ball. Luka went deeper into the woods and continued to search. As he went deeper, he noticed his flashlight was not the only thing giving off light. Luka turned off his flashlight and looked around, he saw a glowing white light through the cracks of trees. He slowly advanced toward it until he saw it clearly at last. There was a bright-white orb, floating above the bushes, and engraved in the middle was writing that said: "Jump Boost". Luka did not hesitate as he reached his hand outwards towards it, as soon as his fingers had made contact, the orb vanished and he felt an empowering feeling in his body. He took a couple of steps back, and his foot bumped into his basketball on the forest floor. He grabbed his ball, turned on his light once again, and headed back up to the basketball court. Luka was not sure if he was hallucinating or if what had just occurred was real, but he had to make sure. Luka gripped his ball, took a few steps toward the hoop, and jumped as high as he could. Luka launched into the air, met the rim with ease, and slammed the basketball into the net. Luka was overwhelmed with joy, and he could not wait to put his new skills to the test in a real game.
