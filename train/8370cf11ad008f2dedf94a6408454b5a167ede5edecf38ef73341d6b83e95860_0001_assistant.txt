The rain beat down mercilessly on the city, drenching everything in sight and obscuring even the neon lights and signs with heavy fog. The dark alleyways and dimly lit streets were full of shadows and unknown dangers. A lone figure hustled down the street, her white coat pulled tightly around her in an effort to keep the rain at bay. The clack of her heels and light clanks of the jewelry she donned echoed through the streets, normally busy and bustling but now nearly empty. 

She stopped in her tracks, heart racing, peering into the darkness of an alleyway as a lone empty can clattered to a stop in front of her. She lowered her head, bracing for anything. It wouldn’t be the first time she'd been followed to an important clandestine meeting. After a moment, she kept walking, trying to shake off the uneasy feeling that had settled in her chest. 

Her destination was a small, dingy bar in the middle of the city. The warmth of the bar enveloped her, and she shook the rain off her coat. Quiet conversation and the clinking of glasses filled the air, providing a welcome respite from the storm outside. Ordering a drink may have calmed her nerves. The bartender offered her the special, and after a moment the drink was in front of her. She sat there for a while, sipping her drink and watching the rain beat down on the city outside.
