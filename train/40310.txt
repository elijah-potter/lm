Which disorder is characterized by a fear of being seen or stared at by others?
Scopophobia, scoptophobia, or ophthalmophobia is an anxiety disorder characterized by a morbid fear of being seen or stared at by others. The term scopophobia comes from the [Ancient Greek|Greek]] σκοπέω skopeō, "look to, examine", and φόβος phobos, "fear".
