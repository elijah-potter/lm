*(A dimly lit office. The door creaks open. A man in a tailored suit steps in, a half-smile on his face, fingers steepled. The woman behind the desk, dressed in a sharp blazer, turns sharply toward him, her eyes sharp and unreadable.)*

The man lingers by the door, tilting his head slightly, as if listening to the faintest whisper of the wind outside. His gaze drifts to the framed photograph of a child on the desk, then back to her with an expression that’s equal parts amusement and something darker—like he’s already read her mind. A pause. The air between them hums with tension, charged with the unspoken: *What do you want to know?*

She exhales sharply through her nose, a sound that could be a laugh, a sigh, or the ghost of a snort. The suit jacket shifts, revealing the way his fingers twitch toward the pocket where his phone is likely hidden. The desk lamp flickers once, twice, as if reacting to something unseen. His voice is low, measured, but there’s a rhythm to it—like a metronome counting down to something inevitable.

*"You know, I’ve been meaning to ask…"* His voice trails off, but the words aren’t meant for her. *"Why do you always play the game like you’re listening?"* His eyes flick toward the framed photo again, then to the ceiling, as if searching for an answer there. The woman’s fingers tighten around the edge of her desk. The silence after is thicker than the smoke curling from her cigarette, if she’s smoking. Outside, a car door slams. Somewhere, a clock ticks. The game begins.
