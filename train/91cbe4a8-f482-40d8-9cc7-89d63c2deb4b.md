The room was dimly lit by the flickering glow of a single candle on the desk. Elias sat hunched over his laptop, fingers tapping the keys rhythmically, but his mind was elsewhere. His phone buzzed softly—a notification from an old friend, *"Still here?"*—but he ignored it. He had chosen silence.

He closed his eyes, letting the sound of his own breath become the only rhythm. The silence wrapped around him like a shroud, thick and warm. Outside, the city pulsed with noise—cars honking, conversations overlapping, the distant hum of a subway. But inside his head, it was stillness. He had let go of the words, the plans, the endless stream of thoughts that had once cluttered his mind. Now, there was only the quiet.

He leaned back in his chair, exhales slow and steady. The silence wasn’t empty—it was full of potential. The weight of it settled into his bones. He could feel it, the absence of noise, the absence of *himself* trying to fill it. It was peaceful. It was terrifying. He didn’t know which one was more true.

He opened his eyes, staring at the empty screen. The cursor blinked. No message, no reply. Just silence.

A pause. A choice.
