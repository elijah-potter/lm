**The List of Forgiveness**

1. The coffee I spilled on your favorite sweater
2. The time I forgot your birthday and only remembered it when you sent a text
3. The way I kept my secrets even after you asked twice
4. The emails I never replied to, the promises I made but didn’t keep
5. The things I loved but never told you, the way I let you go on without me

I should have said it sooner. I should have held you tighter. I should have let you see the cracks before they closed. But I was afraid.

I’m sorry. I’m sorry I didn’t see you. I’m sorry I let you slip away like that. I’m sorry I’m still here, still writing this, still waiting for you to forgive me. But I can’t wait forever. Not for you. Not for myself.
