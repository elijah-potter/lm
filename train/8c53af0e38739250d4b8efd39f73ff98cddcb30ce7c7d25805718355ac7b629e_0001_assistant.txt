Relax your hands and put them by your sides. Relax the muscles from your biceps to your shoulders. Close your eyes. Try to take your mind off whatever came before this meditation, as well as anything that will be coming after. Zero in on this particular moment. 

Take a long inhale. Take an equally long exhale. Think about what you would do if you could let something go. Do it again, with a deep breath in and an equally deep breath out. Feel on your exhale that you are softening your shoulders. Relax all movements around your eyes. Scan through your body as you breathe and look for the possibility of release anywhere your body feels tight or tense. 

Take this time to briefly take stock. Ask yourself a simple question: "How are you?" Now ask again, and pause. Don't answer immediately, and rather let your instinct fill that void with an answer. Now consider what your reflex response is - physically emotionally or mentally. There is no need to fix or plan or explain or work around what you're feeling. What is important during this time is visualization and noticing what comes up when you pay attention. Now ask yourself "What do I need to do today? What do I want to do today?" Is there a true intention or anchor to the to-do list today? What needs to be achieved? Think about what your reflexive response is - what comes from your heart? Focus on that as you breathe. 

Try to remain focused on this visualization throughout the day. How can you be the version of yourself that you are thinking of right now? What seeds can you plant for the remainder of the day to achieve that? This version of you is real.

When you're ready, open your eyes. Stand up tall. Face the day.
