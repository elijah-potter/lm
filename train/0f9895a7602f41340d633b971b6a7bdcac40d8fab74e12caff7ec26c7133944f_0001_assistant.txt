Five different SNL cast members have impersonated George W. Bush. The first was Will Ferrell, who did it from 2001–2002. He was succeeded by Chris Parnell from 2002–2003. Next was Darrell Hammond in 2003. Will Forte filled the role from 2004–2006. Laste up was Jason Sudeikis, who had the job from 2006–2008.
