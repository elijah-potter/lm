"I can't believe you."

Her words stuck like a dagger in my heart. Everything she expressed screamed rage. She was relatively calm, but her face, her eyes, and her tears showed deep resentment.

"You must think I'm ugly, don't you?"

I wanted to plead, "No" but I knew she wouldn't believe me anyway. I stayed silent. My world was shifting, and I felt dizzy. Everything was about to be all over between us very soon.

"I must mean nothing to you."

I knew what I did was wrong. She was making it very clear. There was no other way around it. She puffed her body up, took a deep breath, and her eyes widened. "Uh-oh," I thought, she's mad at my silence. I had no idea how to amend it. She took another deep breath. I thought she was going to yell or scream. I braced, waiting for the onslaught. She reached into the kitchen sink and threw a dirty porcelain plate at my face. I barely dodged it. It smashed against the glass cabinets. The porcelain and glass shattered into thousands of pieces. Her heart was probably breaking, too. 

"Mom?" I stuttered.
