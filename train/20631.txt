1. Avoid excessive sun exposure
One of the sure things that can help prevent wrinkles is to avoid sun exposure. Being under the sun for an extended period can put you at high risk of skin aging, which will promote the formation of wrinkles.
The most common cause of wrinkling is exposure to ultraviolet radiation which causes changes to the skin. UVA causes photoaging, while UVB causes sunburn and skin cancer.
UV rays promote the formation of free radicals within the skin, which cause damage to the elastin fiber and significantly contribute to wrinkles. This is because elastin allows the body to resume its shape after stretching or contracting.
2. Protect yourself from the sun by wearing sunscreen
Australian and UK researchers performed a test to see the real effects of sunscreen and skin age. After four and a half years, the study showed that the people who used sunscreen daily showed no increase in skin aging, and their aging scores significantly lowered to 24% compared to the other group.
Sunscreen proves that it has a preventative effect on aging and slows down the formation of wrinkles.

- Wide brim hats are best. Choose a hat that has at least 7.5m brim
- Consider hats with flaps that can cover the neck
- The fabric must have at least UPF 15 protection or more.
- Make sure it will keep you cool even under the sun and it is something that you are comfortable with.

5. Use eye creams
The skin around the eye is very delicate. For that reason, an eye cream is formulated in a thicker consistency and contains more oil and active ingredients to solve the most common problems around the eyes.
A good eye cream can help make wrinkles appear less noticeable. That said, for an eye cream to be effective, you need to know which ingredients you should look out for. Retinol is an excellent ingredient to have because it can help speed up cell reproduction.
Other ingredients you should look out for are vitamin C and peptides. Of course, keep an eye out for other established ingredients that are known to provide anti-aging benefits.

7. Remove your makeup gently
How do you remove your makeup gently? The first thing to do is to have a cotton pad ready that contains enough eye makeup remover. Hold it on the eyes for 20 seconds, then gently wipe it away. What you will do here is just to allow the cotton and the makeup remover to do their job, while you hold the remover on your eyes without making any movements. Just let the makeup dissolve, so you no longer have to rub it off.
8. Quit smoking
Smoking can speed up the normal aging process of the skin, which contributes to wrinkles. You can notice these skin changes as early as 10 years into smoking. The more and the longer you smoke, the more wrinkling you will likely have. In fact, smoking is the strongest predictor of facial wrinkling in both men and women.
9. Refrain from frowning often
Frowning can cause wrinkles to develop quickly in your face. How? Constant facial expressions can cause collagen to break down. Eventually, it can form a permanent line (hello, frown lines).
So, try to be more conscious about those frowning moments. Instead, smile more. Smiling more will not only prevent frowning, but it can also help boost your mood.
10. Eat healthy and add more foods rich in antioxidants
You can never go wrong with a healthy lifestyle, and that includes eating healthy foods. To prevent wrinkles, add more foods rich in antioxidants into your diet, such as berries, pecans, prunes, etc.
These foods can help detoxify skin and keep it looking healthy and glowing for a longer period. This will make you look more youthful.
11. Take supplements
Taking supplements, such as vitamin C, can help prevent skin aging. Other supplements that you can take are:
- Collagen - it boosts the collagen levels in the skin that can prevent skin aging.
- Coenzyme Q10 (CoQ10) - is an essential antioxidant that the body naturally produces. Research shows that supplementing it can help slow skin aging and even improve the quality of life.
- Theanine - is an amino acid that you may commonly find in green tea and other teas.
- Garlic - contains anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties that may prevent UV-light-induced wrinkles and skin aging.
12. Stay hydrated
Since the skin is the largest organ in the body and it’s mostly made up of water. Without water, the skin won’t be able to function at its best. Dehydration could cause dry, tight, and flaky skin, which will make you more prone to aging.
The more hydrated you are, the fewer wrinkles and fine lines you will see. Waters helps keep the skin moisturized. When the skin is properly moisturized, it increases its elasticity.
13. Don’t drink too much alcohol
Drinking too much alcohol can dehydrate you and by now, you already know how important it is to always stay hydrated for the skin.
There are so many negative side effects that drinking too much alcohol brings, and if you are very particular about preventing those wrinkles, it is best to drink in moderation.
14. Get enough beauty sleep
Ah, sleep. Eight hours of sleep every day is essential not only for your skin but also for your overall health. If you want to slow down skin aging, or totally prevent it, check in on the quality of your sleep.
15. Sleep on your back
A study in 2016 showed that your sleeping position might affect the formation of wrinkles. It is found that people who sleep on their side or stomach are prone to mechanical compression forces that can speed up wrinkles formation. It can also distort the skin on the face.
16. Reduce stress
Stress can make you look older than your age, and yes, it can speed up the formation of wrinkles and fine lines on the skin.
There are many ways to reduce stress such as taking breaks every once in a while, working out, beauty sleep, eating healthy foods, and making time for the people and things that you love.
17. Relax your face
Squinting, frowning, or pursing the lips can speed up the formation of wrinkles on your face. So, try to avoid these facial movements as much as you can.
Frequent frowning and scowling, there are ways to relieve this by working out regularly, doing deep breathing exercises, etc.
18. Visit your eye doctor yearly
Frequent squinting is usually because there is an issue with your eyes. That is why as you grow older, make it a habit to schedule a regular eye appointment. Your eyes need a lot of lovin’ too! Plus, this can help prevent squinting.
19. Do not drink out of a straw
Drinking using a straw can cause you to purse your lips, and when you do this often, you are giving wrinkles a pass to reign on your face. It is similar to frowning and squinting.
20. Get facials
A facial targets specific skin concerns. A facial can stimulate your circulatory and lymphatic system, as well as the muscles and nerves that can strengthen your skin.
