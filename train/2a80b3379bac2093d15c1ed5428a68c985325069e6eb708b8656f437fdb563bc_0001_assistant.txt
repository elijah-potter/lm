One day, Mario was enjoying a nice day at home in the Mushroom Kingdom. Just then, his tummy started gurgling and making a peculiar noise. "Mama Mia!", he exclaimed. "I shouldn't have eaten all of those mushrooms!". Then, Mario let out an uncontrollable fart. The fart wafted its way all through the kingdom until it reached Bowser's castle. Bowser smelled the odor and was repulsed. "Ew!", he shouted. "This must be Mario's fart. I'll show him to stink up my domain!" Bowser then sent the fart into his castle's laboratory. There, he ran tests on it and discovered a way to use the essence of the fart to make a new villain! "I will name you, WARIO!", Bowser said with a booming voice. And so, Wario was born. An opposite version of Mario who is greedy, and loves smelling farts.
