Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big yellow door, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around the garden, chasing flowers and playing with his best friend, a tiny blue butterfly named Butter.

One sunny afternoon, Benny found a shiny red apple on the ground. "Mmm, what a yummy apple!" Benny said, and he took a big bite. Suddenly, he felt something strange—his nose started to twitch, and his ears perked up. "Oh no!" Benny thought, "I’m getting too big!"

Benny tiptoed toward the house, but his feet got stuck in the mud. He tried to wiggle free, but his tail got tangled in a bush. Just then, Butter fluttered down and said, "Don’t worry, Benny! Let’s help each other!"

Butter took Benny’s paw and gently untangled his tail. Then, they ran to the garden fence, where a wise old owl named Oliver was perched. "Benny, Butter," Oliver hooted, "you’ve been eating too many apples. That’s why you’re growing too big!"

Benny gulped. "I didn’t mean to!" he said.

Oliver smiled. "Every mistake is a chance to learn. Let’s go back to the garden and pick some fresh, crunchy carrots instead. That way, you’ll stay the perfect size for hopping and playing!"

So Benny and Butter ate some tasty carrots, and soon Benny’s nose and ears stopped twitching. He felt happy and small again, just like before.

From that day on, Benny remembered to eat his carrots instead of apples, and he hopped around the garden, playing with Butter every single day. And they all lived happily ever after, with lots of sunshine and laughter.
