The dimly lit alleyway smelled of damp stone and stale beer, flickering neon casting jagged shadows on the rough-hewn pavement. A hooded figure crouched behind a rusted dumpster, fingers twitching toward the handle of a knife she’d never use. Across the street, a tense standoff: three armored guards in black tactical gear stood in a half-circle, their rifles leveled at a young woman in a stolen trench coat, her wrists bound behind her back. Her eyes darted between them, her breath shallow. A single phone sat on the ground near her feet, the screen cracked but still lit up a distorted message: *"They’re coming. Meet me at the docks."* The guards exchanged a glance. One of them—older, with a scar running down his cheek—sighed, but didn’t lower the rifle. The alleyway held its breath.
