**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, Ill. – August 21, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this year with a vibrant mix of agricultural exhibits, thrilling entertainment, and family-friendly activities. This annual tradition celebrates the region’s rich farming heritage and offers a memorable experience for visitors of all ages. Expect to see prize-winning livestock, intricate craft displays, and a wide variety of food vendors offering classic fair fare. 

The Sangamon County Fair will run from Friday, September 20th, through Sunday, September 29th. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM. Special events are scheduled throughout the two weeks, including a demolition derby, a tractor pull, and live music performances. A full schedule of events will be available on the fair’s official website, SangamonCountyFair.org.

Admission to the Sangamon County Fair is $15 for adults, $10 for children ages 6-12, and free for children five and under. Season passes are available for $50 and offer unlimited entry throughout the duration of the fair. Tickets can be purchased online at SangamonCountyFair.org or at the gate. Parking is $5 per vehicle.

**Headline Events:**

*   Friday, September 20th: Opening Ceremony & Monster Truck Show
*   Saturday, September 21st: Demolition Derby
*   Sunday, September 22nd: Tractor Pull
*   Wednesday, September 25th: Regional Livestock Show
*   Friday, September 27th: Bluegrass Music Festival
*   Sunday, September 29th: Closing Ceremony & Fireworks Display
