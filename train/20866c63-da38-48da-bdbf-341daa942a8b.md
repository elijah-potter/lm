A lone figure hunched over a crumpled newspaper in the alley behind the bakery, their fingers brushing against something cold and smooth—until a gust of wind sent a loose coin skittering across the pavement. They stooped, fingers tracing the edge of a worn quarter with a faded "1987" embossed on it. The moment the coin settled in their palm, the alley seemed to still, as if time itself held its breath. The loss of the loose change wasn’t the worst; the relief of having found it was. They pocketed it, leaving the empty space behind.
