A silver lantern hangs in the dim glow of a candlelit room, its glass surface etched with a faint, glowing script. Outside, a distant church bell tolls the hour. The air smells of aged parchment and damp wool. A young woman sits at a worn oak desk, her fingers tracing the edges of an open ledger, the ink still fresh from her pen. Beside her, an old leather-bound book lies closed, its cover stamped with a crest bearing a serpent coiled around a sword. A sealed envelope rests on the table, addressed in elegant script to "Whomever shall find this." Inside, a single sheet of parchment bears a promise scrawled in ink: *"The key to the lost city lies within these walls. Find it before the moon wanes."* A single candle flickers, its flame casting long shadows across the room. The woman exhales slowly, her eyes reflecting the firelight—she knows the cost of what she’s been offered.
