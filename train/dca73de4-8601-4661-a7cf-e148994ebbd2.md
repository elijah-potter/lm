The door creaked open under the weight of a heavy coat. Footsteps echoed faintly against the marble floor as the silhouette of a stranger stood silhouetted by the dim glow of a single lamp. Their voice, rough and low, drifted through the crack:

*"You shouldn’t be here."*

Inside, a solitary figure—tied to a chair by the wrists—whimpered, fingers digging into the fabric. The air smelled of damp wool and something metallic, like old blood. A second knock. This time, the voice was softer, almost pleading:

*"Let me in."*

The door swung inward, revealing a second figure in a torn trench coat, their face half-hidden beneath a hat. The first stranger stepped back, their expression unreadable.

*"You’re late."*
