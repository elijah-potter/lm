## Reclaiming Resilience: Transforming Oakhaven Ravine into a Community Oasis

**Executive Summary**

The City of Meadowbrook faces increasing challenges from climate change, including intensified stormwater runoff and urban heat island effects. This proposal seeks $75,000 in municipal funding, matched by $25,000 in volunteer labor and in-kind donations, to transform Oakhaven Ravine, a currently neglected and underutilized public space, into a climate-resilient community asset. This project, “Reclaiming Resilience,” will establish a native plant rain garden, create accessible walking paths, and install educational signage, fostering biodiversity, mitigating stormwater flooding, reducing localized temperatures, and enhancing community well-being. The project prioritizes equitable access for all residents and ensures long-term sustainability through community ownership and ongoing maintenance partnerships. Reclaiming Resilience represents a tangible investment in Meadowbrook's future, demonstrating a proactive approach to climate adaptation and enriching the quality of life for all citizens.

**Project Plan & Timeline**

The Reclaiming Resilience project is structured across four distinct phases, each with defined milestones, responsible partners, and associated deliverables.

**Phase 1: Planning & Design (Weeks 1-4)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Meadowbrook Parks Department, GreenThumb Landscape Architects, Oakhaven Neighborhood Association
*   **Milestones:** Site assessment and topographic survey completion; stakeholder consultation meetings (minimum three public forums); finalized landscape design incorporating native plant species selection and stormwater management strategies; permitting application submission to the City Planning Department.
*   **Deliverables:** Comprehensive site assessment report; documented stakeholder feedback; approved landscape design blueprints; submitted permitting application.

**Phase 2: Site Preparation & Infrastructure (Weeks 5-8)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Meadowbrook Parks Department, Volunteer Construction Crew (coordinated by Oakhaven Neighborhood Association), GreenThumb Landscape Architects
*   **Milestones:** Invasive species removal; grading and earthwork for rain garden construction; installation of subsurface drainage system; pathway construction utilizing permeable paving materials.
*   **Deliverables:** Cleared and graded site; functional subsurface drainage system; completed accessible walking paths.

**Phase 3: Planting & Installation (Weeks 9-12)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Meadowbrook Horticultural Society, Volunteer Planting Teams (coordinated by Oakhaven Neighborhood Association), GreenThumb Landscape Architects
*   **Milestones:** Procurement and delivery of native plant materials; installation of rain garden planting beds; planting of native trees, shrubs, and herbaceous plants; installation of educational signage detailing the ecological benefits of the rain garden.
*   **Deliverables:** Established rain garden planting beds; thriving native plant community; informative educational signage.

**Phase 4: Community Launch & Stewardship (Weeks 13-16)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Oakhaven Neighborhood Association, Meadowbrook Parks Department, Meadowbrook Horticultural Society
*   **Milestones:** Community launch event and ribbon-cutting ceremony; development of volunteer stewardship program; training of community members in rain garden maintenance; ongoing monitoring of plant health and stormwater management effectiveness.
*   **Deliverables:** Successful community launch event; established volunteer stewardship program; documented maintenance protocols; quarterly monitoring reports.

**Budget & Matching Funds**

The total project cost is estimated at $100,000.  We request $75,000 from the municipal fund, which will be matched by $25,000 in volunteer labor and in-kind donations.  A detailed breakdown is as follows:

*   **GreenThumb Landscape Architects (Design & Supervision):** $28,000 (paid hourly rate)
*   **Site Preparation & Grading (Contractor):** $18,000 (includes equipment rental)
*   **Native Plants & Soil Amendments:** $15,000 (sourced from local nurseries)
*   **Permeable Pathway Materials:** $12,000 (including installation)
*   **Educational Signage:** $5,000 (design, fabrication, and installation)
*   **Volunteer Coordination & Supplies:** $2,000 (includes safety equipment, refreshments, and promotional materials)
*   **Contingency Fund:** $10,000 (5% of total project cost, to address unforeseen challenges such as material price fluctuations or unexpected site conditions)

The $25,000 in matching funds consists primarily of volunteer labor valued at $15,000 (estimated at $15/hour for 1000 hours of work), and in-kind donations of materials and equipment from local businesses totaling $10,000.  Detailed documentation of volunteer hours and in-kind donations will be provided to the City upon request.  The contingency fund will be managed by the Meadowbrook Parks Department and released only with written approval from the project oversight committee.

**Community Engagement & Equity Impacts**

Community engagement has been central to the project’s development.  Initial consultations with the Oakhaven Neighborhood Association revealed strong support for revitalizing the ravine.  Public forums were held to solicit feedback on design preferences and ensure the project addresses community needs.  The design incorporates accessible pathways, ensuring equitable access for individuals with disabilities and families with young children. The selection of native plants promotes biodiversity and provides habitat for local wildlife, benefiting the entire community.  We have prioritized outreach to underrepresented populations within the Oakhaven neighborhood, ensuring their voices are heard and their needs are met.  The project’s educational signage will be available in multiple languages to enhance understanding and inclusivity.

**Risk Mitigation & Post-Grant Sustainability**

Potential risks include inclement weather delaying construction, unexpected site conditions requiring additional grading, and vandalism following project completion.  To mitigate these risks, we have incorporated buffer time into the project timeline, secured permits to address potential environmental concerns, and established a volunteer stewardship program to foster community ownership and deter vandalism.  Post-grant sustainability is ensured through a formal partnership with the Meadowbrook Parks Department for ongoing maintenance, the establishment of a dedicated volunteer stewardship team, and the development of educational materials to promote responsible use of the space.  The Oakhaven Neighborhood Association will continue to serve as a key partner in advocating for the long-term health and vitality of the Reclaiming Resilience project.

**Call to Action**

The Reclaiming Resilience project represents a vital opportunity to enhance the quality of life for Meadowbrook residents while proactively addressing the challenges of climate change.  The project's tangible benefits – improved stormwater management, reduced urban heat island effect, enhanced biodiversity, and increased community well-being – justify the requested municipal investment.  We urge the City of Meadowbrook to approve this proposal and partner with us to transform Oakhaven Ravine into a thriving community oasis, demonstrating a commitment to a resilient and equitable future for all.
