Once upon a time in a cozy garden, there was a little bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop and play with his friends—Fluffy the squirrel, Pip the chubby chick, and Mochi the sleepy mole.

One sunny morning, Benny noticed something sparkly near his favorite patch of clover. Curious, he dug a little and found a tiny, golden acorn. "Wow!" Benny cheered. "It’s shiny!"

Fluffy the squirrel giggled. "Maybe it’s magic!" Pip the chick fluttered her wings. "Let’s see what happens if we plant it!"

Benny carefully placed the golden acorn in the dirt, watered it with a little rainwater, and watched as it grew a tiny shoot. Soon, a big, beautiful flower bloomed with petals as soft as clouds!

The friends cheered so loudly that Mochi the mole came out of his burrow. "What’s all the noise?" he yawned. "Is something happening?"

"Yep!" Benny said. "Our golden acorn made a magic flower!"

The flower glowed softly in the evening, and when Benny blew on it, a tiny bell rang. "It’s a magic bell!" Pip gasped.

The next morning, the flower grew even bigger, and the golden acorn turned into a shiny, golden tree trunk. The friends built a treehouse out of branches, and under the tree, they had the best picnic ever—with clover, berries, and honey from Mochi’s mole holes.

As the sun set, Benny kissed the golden flower goodnight and whispered, "Thank you, magic acorn." The bell rang one last time, and the friends drifted off to sleep, dreaming of their next adventure under the stars.

And that’s how Benny and his friends discovered the secret of the golden acorn—the kindest magic in all the garden!
