The office was quiet except for the hum of the fluorescent lights. Daniel leaned back in his chair, fingers steepled under his chin, as he watched Sarah type away on her laptop. The air smelled faintly of coffee and old paper. Her fingers paused over the keyboard. A beat of silence. Then she leaned forward, her desk lamp casting long shadows across her face. The screen flickered to life with a single line of code she hadn’t written herself.

"Fixed," she said, but the words were unnecessary.

Daniel didn’t look up. "Thanks."

She didn’t reply, just adjusted the focus of her screen. The tension between them had been there since the last project—unspoken, like an uncut thread. He knew what she was thinking: *I don’t need to say it*. She knew he’d catch it.

The door clicked shut behind her. The moment stretched, a breath held between them. Then he stood, stretching his neck, and walked toward the door, leaving it open. The quiet returned.
