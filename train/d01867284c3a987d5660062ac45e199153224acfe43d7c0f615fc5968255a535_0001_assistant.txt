Don't worry, there WILL be fireball accidents, and only the best knitting finalists will take home the cup. Last up, we have the main event! A destructive, in-your-face knitting demolition throwdown. Pretty patterns and material choices matter, but in the end, only one pattern can be left standing.

Who will take home the gold thimble, and who will go home crying in their new parka? Find out this weekend at DEMOLITION NIGHTMARE KNITTING BATTLE only at the FRT Grand Central Arena!
