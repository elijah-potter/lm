The man rose the checkered flag into the air,
And swung it down with force.
A pack of cars with revving engines,
Took off with blinding speed past the line.

Roaring and thunderous sounds
Spouted from the tailpipes of the racecars.
Number 13 led the pack,
As the rest of the 19 cars followed closely behind.

Approaching a bend in the track,
Number 13 rode upward and close to the wall.
Swiftly maneuvering the course,
while narrowly avoiding a collision.

Suddenly, without warning, a loud crash from behind,
Number 13 looks in his mirror and sees carnage.
Two cars spun out of control,
Smoke billowing from their hoods.

On his side, Number 7 came up in a flash,
And threatened Number 13's lead.
Pedal to the metal,
Number 13 zoomed ahead from the pack again.

Approaching the finish line with breathtaking speed,
Number 13 still in the lead.
At the last minute, the sun blinded the driver,
And threatened to upend his career.

With determination, Number 13 pushed through the pain,
He closed his teary eyes and prayed.
As he passed across the finish line,
He had won the race.
