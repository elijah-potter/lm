With the adoption of Erin's Law in Illinois, we were asked to provide a personal safety lesson for middle school. Previously we focused on dating violence at this level, which some states required. We have done a great job of providing curriculum about sexual abuse prevention K-6, and now we will be adding a middle school Targeted Extension Activity to the 2013 curriculum to help schools in Illinois be compliant with Erin's Law.
If you have a Teacher's Edition older than 2013, you can still access that activity by registering your Middle School Level 1 Teacher's Guide and exploring the 2013 edition for Unit 4:The Cycle of Life. You can view this activity after August 2013, but we wanted to alert you to this update, so that you can be looking out for it!
