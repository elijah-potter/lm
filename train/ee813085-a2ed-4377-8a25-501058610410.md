AITA for telling my sister I think her wedding plans are ridiculous?

Okay, deep breath. This is awkward, and I feel awful about it, but I need to vent. My sister, Eleanor, is getting married on October 27th, 2024, at a vineyard in Napa Valley. I love her dearly, and I want her to be happy, but her wedding plans… they’ve become something else entirely.

It started innocently enough. Around January 15th, 2023, she casually mentioned wanting a “rustic chic” wedding. Fine. I’m all for personal taste. Then, the budget conversation began around February 1st. She initially said $30,000, which seemed reasonable, given the location. However, over the past eight months, that number has ballooned to an estimated $75,000.

The escalation has been gradual, but relentless. The photographer, initially priced at $5,000, is now $12,000. The florist, who started at $3,000, is at $8,000. The seating chart alone took her three hours last Tuesday, and she's obsessed with finding the *perfect* shade of blush for the napkins. She's hired a harpist, a falconer (yes, a falconer, for "ambiance"), and a calligrapher to address every single invitation, which she’s sending via pony express. (Okay, I’m exaggerating slightly on the pony express, but she’s using a very niche, expensive delivery service.)

My parents are quietly footing the bill. They’re retirees on a fixed income, and I’m genuinely worried about their financial security. I’m not saying they *can’t* afford it, but I think she's taking advantage of their generosity. I’ve tried to gently voice my concerns. On April 10th, I suggested she consider a smaller, more intimate gathering. She dismissed it as me being “negative” and “not supportive.” 

My brother-in-law, Mark, has been equally enabling. He seems to revel in the extravagance and keeps feeding into Eleanor's fantasies. He made a comment last week—I was at their house on July 18th—about how "this is the wedding she deserves" and how "it's important to splurge on memories." I felt like I was witnessing a slow-motion train wreck.

The breaking point was yesterday, August 5th. I was helping Eleanor finalize the guest list. She added another twenty people, justifying it by saying they were "important connections." When I pointed out that each additional guest adds significantly to the cost, she snapped at me. She accused me of trying to sabotage her happiness and said I was jealous of her “perfect life.” I lost it. I told her, calmly but firmly, that I thought her wedding plans were ridiculous and that she needed to consider the financial strain she was putting on our parents.

The room went silent. Mark looked appalled. Eleanor burst into tears and ran to her room. My parents looked uncomfortable. I felt terrible immediately afterward, but I stand by what I said. I believe she's lost perspective.

I’m now being labeled as “the ungrateful sister” and “the family drama starter.” My parents are trying to mediate, but I can tell they’re frustrated too. I feel awful for hurting Eleanor, but I also feel a responsibility to speak truth, even when it's uncomfortable. I think she's being reckless and entitled, and Mark is just as much to blame for encouraging her. ESH, honestly. I’m probably the YTA in this situation for delivering the truth so bluntly, but I still believe my concerns are valid. I'm feeling guilty and want to repair the relationship, but I don't want to be silenced. NTA for having concerns.
