The children in the baby room have been working hard this term learning about the Three Little Pigs. As you can see, the children had great fun throughout this topic, making their own houses, getting messy, and baking delicious pig biscuits.
'I'll huff and I'll puff and I'll blow your house down!'
The trip to Lochboisdale marina was based around the story 'Na Deich Spùinnich Bheaga'. The children had an amazing experience learning about different aspects of the marina. We eventually found some treasure, but didn't manage to spot any pirates on this occasion.
We were learning about what happens to trees during the autumn. We learned that the leaves change colour and fall off the trees.
The children followed a colour by numbers chart for their autumn pictures. They had to really concentrate and remember to follow the colour guide. Didn't they do well?
