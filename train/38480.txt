As ice and snow become the everyday problem during winter time of year, most of the people try to search for ways to reduce the risk of falls on driveways and sideways. Calcium chloride and salt are the two key materials which are often used to deal with icy conditions. However, people often struggle to decide which is effective among salt and calcium chloride. Here are some important highlights which will help you to figure out which is better out of these two –
Salt is effective till the temperature of 25 degree Fahrenheit, while calcium chloride is effective till 20 degree Fahrenheit.
Calcium chloride is non-corrosive and it is less harmful to vegetation when compared with salt. It doesn’t possess any sharp edges thereby reducing the risk of damaging the plants.
Calcium chloride is more expensive than salt because of its extremely effective snow and ice removing applications.
Calcium chloride can easily break the bond between ice and payment by dissolving quickly upon application.
Salt can melt the material at slower rate and it keeps the surface dry for longer time when compared with calcium chloride.
When mixed with abrasives, calcium chloride becomes the most cost-effective medium for road safety during winter season.
Calcium chloride can easily attract moisture in the air which is required for melting of ice and snow on contact.
When mixed with rock salt, calcium chloride can enhance the efficiency of deicing material. It can work at lower temperatures and more effective than salt because of its quick heat generating properties.
So after doing lot of research and considering the environmental impact of both these materials, you would definitely like to settle with calcium chloride.
