The **Silent Spire**, a towering obsidian monolith carved with no visible markings but etched with symbols only the initiated could read—each one a silent contract between the city’s unseen overseers and its inhabitants. The air hums with unspoken rules: no loud voices after the **Third Watch**, no mirrors facing the **Black Door**, and never to ask why the **Forgotten Steps** lead downward. The streets bend around the **Gilded Curtain**, a wall of polished metal that reflects the past but never the present. Those who linger too long near the **Whispering Stones** hear the city’s breath—each word a warning, each pause a challenge.

In this place, the rules are **felt**, not seen. A glance at the wrong shadow, a stolen breath of wind—both punishments. The **First Rule** is never to forget: the city does not tolerate memory.
