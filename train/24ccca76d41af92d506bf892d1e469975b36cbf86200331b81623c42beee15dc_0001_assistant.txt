- Existence is suffering
- Craving and attachment are the causes of suffering
- There is a way to cease suffering
- The solution to this suffering is the eightfold path
- The eightfold path includes: right view, right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and meditation
- Nothing is permanent because everything arises from interrelation among impermanent things
- Avoid extremes and follow the middle way
- Extinguish greed, hatred, delusion, and ignorance
- Do not kill, steal, participate in sexual misconduct, lie, or become intoxicated

Buddhism is a deep, rich, and complex system, but this list summarizes some of the main philosophical tenets of the religion.
