Once upon a time in a cozy, green forest, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop and play, but one day, he heard a tiny voice.

"Help me!" said a little flower with petals the color of sunshine.

Benny looked around. The flower was stuck in a big, tall rock. "Oh no!" Benny said. He had a big, fluffy tail and strong little legs.

Benny hopped to the flower and whispered, "Don't worry, I'll help you!"

With his tiny paws, Benny gently pulled the flower free. "Thank you, Benny!" the flower said.

Now, Benny wanted to thank his friends. He ran to his friends, a kind old owl named Owlbert and a wise old tortoise named Torto.

"Owlbert, help me make a flower bouquet!" Benny asked.

Owlbert sat up high in his tree and sang, "I'll help with a twig or two!"

Torto slowly turned his round shell and said, "I'll gather the sweetest leaves and petals for you!"

Soon, Benny had a beautiful bouquet. He gave it to the flower and said, "Here you go, thank you for helping me!"

The flower smiled and said, "Thank you, Benny! You are the kindest bunny in the whole forest!"

Benny felt very happy. He hopped around, laughing and singing, "The best part of being kind is the love you share!"

And from that day on, Benny knew that being kind made the world a happier place.
