Essential and Secondary High Blood Pressure
When high blood pressure is diagnosed, physicians will classify it according to the cause; essential hypertension is diagnosed when the medical practitioner cannot find a cause behind the condition, and secondary hypertension is diagnosed when this symptom is caused by another condition. Around 95% of cases of hypertension are diagnosed as essential high blood pressure, and it affects millions of people all over the country.
Common Causes of Hypertension
The precise causes of this condition are not yet known, but there are a range of factors that have been known to be directly related to whether an individual develops this condition, and they include; stress, smoking, diabetes, vitamin D deficiencies, obesity, alcohol consumption, aging and sedentary lifestyles. While in certain cases this condition can be avoided, such as living a healthy lifestyle, in other cases, it could be inherited from a parent, and so individuals might contract the condition later on in life due to this genetic link.
Treating High Blood Pressure
In cases where a secondary condition has been diagnosed as the cause of this problem, medical practitioners will treat both the hypertension as well as the underlying cause, but this is only possible in a small number of cases. In most instances, the cause is not known, and so medical doctors will treat the condition with medications aimed at reducing the pressure within the veins.
Individuals are advised to have their blood pressure tested regularly so that they can take action the moment they discover a problem.
