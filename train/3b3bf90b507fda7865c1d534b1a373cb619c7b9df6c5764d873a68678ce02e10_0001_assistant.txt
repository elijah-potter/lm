Malibu has quicksilver flow.
Paak, as producer and songwriter, is in command.
Malibu carries the spirit of Kendrick
In every expansive harmony.
Malibu shifts genre through genre.
Club grooves and 90s hip-hop bound by emotional generosity.
Malibu fancies itself a 70s double album,
like a  classicly flowing opus.
Malibu gives ample space to guests and ghosts
of the past, Rapsody, The Game, Dilla style take up space.
Malibu rides the wave out on extended breakdowns,
like on "Parking Lot," sounding like a band with room to sprawl.
