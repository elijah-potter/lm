ABC Go Fish!
Cut 3X5 cards in half
Write the letters of the alphabet twice per group (52 cards total for each set - 2A's, 2B's, 2C's etc...)
Teach them how to play Go Fish.
Do you have an A? Do you have a B? You know the rest...
Helps the students to pronounce the letters of the alphabet and really listen to each other to distinguish between the different letters.
-Karen Furlong, Wake Tech ESL program - Raleigh, NC
