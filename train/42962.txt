plans and dreams
pre intermediate level
To provide clarification of Be Going to in the context of summer holidays' plan
To provide accuracy speaking practice in a Personalizing in the context of future plans
Procedure (35-45 minutes)
-At the beggining of the class T asks Ss to stand in a line in order of the time that they woke up in the morning . - then each S can find his or her partner right next to him or her . - T shows Picture1 (that is a slide with a question) and says : in your pairs discuss this question in 3 minutes. - while that speaking ,the T is monitoring and discovering Ss' awarness about suitable language . - At the end of that speaking time , T asks the first volunteer about his or her idea. - The T asks the second volunteer about his or her partner's idea.
-For this stage the T's instruction is like this : please look this paper ,There are 3 travellers in the picture lily , Mathew and Olivia .you can see 3 plans . guess and match these 3 items on your own in 2 minutes . - ICQs : how do you do this andividually or with your partner ? are you sure about your matching or you only guess ? how many minute do you have ? - After 2 minutes the T asks students to share thier ideas with their previous partners . - The second instruction : T says: listen to the audio and complete the chart . with your partners . - The teacher choose 3 Ss randomly and they go to bord and write their answers .
- T divides the class into two groups .(by 2 colors ) - The T gives listening script to both groups . then T says : - in your groups discuss the highlighted sentences and figure out what do they mean in 2 minutes. - T elicites from 2 students .
- Because this grammar is not completely new and they have heard it few times befor , also they had an opportunity to share their ideas with their classmates and tried to figur out what does it mean , now they are able to say its meaning easily . - The T writes some examples to make sure they know the meaning . - example 1: She is going to study hard next term . _ elicitation : 1) Is It about present of future ? 2) Is She Sure or not ? 3) is she going to anywhere ? -example 2 : It is going to rain . 1) Is it about future or present ? 2) is it 60% or 100% ? 3) Is there any sigh like back clouds ? - After eliciting about the meaning , the T will clarify the correct pronounciation . - In HO2 T asks Ss to listen and copy the rythem . so after listening each student will read a sntence one by one . - Teacher says : some times when we are speaking fast, instead of (Going to) we are allowed to say (Gonna). For example : I am gonna shopping . = I am going to shopping . - Then T asks students to make 2 examples on their own . - T chooses 2 Ss randomly to read their examples in front of the class. - In the last stage of the clarification the form of that grammar should be tought . - T asks is there any volunteer to tell us what is the form of this grammar ? - T asks S to go to borad and write that form and make an example. - others will share their ideas and at the end T confirme all the stuffs . - The form is : Subject +Tobe + Going to + to + bare infinitive
