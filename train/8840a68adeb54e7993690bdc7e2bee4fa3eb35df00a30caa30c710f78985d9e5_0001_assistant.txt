Here are some good fantasy book suggestions:
- The Name of the Wind by Patrick Rothfuss. Follows the life story of a renowned wizard, sword fighter and musician and eventual innkeeper called Kovethe.
- The Lord of the Rings by J.R.R. Tolkein. The end of the world looms over Middle-earth and a group set out on a quest to destroy the One Ring.
- The Way of Kings by Brandon Sanderson. A story split between the viewpoints of Kaladin, a slave, Shallan Davar, heiress of a failing house, Szeth an assassin and Dalinar and Adolin Kholin brother and nephew of a King.
- Prince of Thorns by Mark Lawrence. A vengeful prince taking back what is his by any means.
