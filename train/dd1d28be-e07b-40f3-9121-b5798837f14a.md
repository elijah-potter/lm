A rusted bell clanged faintly against the damp stone walls of the abandoned church, its hollow sound swallowed by the autumn wind. Inside, the air was thick with the scent of mildew and old iron. The last candle guttered out as the warning was delivered by the old man in the corner—his fingers trembling as he held a yellowed letter.

*"They’re coming,"* whispered Elias, his voice barely audible over the creak of the floorboards. *"They’re coming for the last key."*

The key was hidden beneath the altar, beneath layers of dust and forgotten prayers. The church had stood empty for decades, but whispers said the walls themselves were alive—watching, waiting. Somewhere beyond the stained glass, the shadows moved. And the shadows were hungry.
