Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white fur and big round ears that twitched when he was excited.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and hopped out of his burrow. "Wow, it's a beautiful day!" he said. He hopped outside to see his favorite things: a big green garden, a fluffy cloud, and a shiny yellow bug.

Benny's favorite game was chasing the yellow bug. He would hop, skip, and jump, trying to catch it. But the bug would always fly away, leaving Benny giggling.

Suddenly, Benny spotted his best friend, a little squirrel named Sammy. Sammy was collecting acorns in his treehouse. "Hi, Benny!" Sammy said with a chirp. "Want to play?"

Benny hopped over to Sammy’s treehouse. They built a fort with leaves and sticks, and they told stories about their adventures. Benny told Sammy about the magical yellow bug, and Sammy told him about the secret acorn treasure he found.

As the sun began to set, Benny and Sammy snuggled in their burrow and burrow. "Goodnight, Benny!" Sammy said, and Benny gave him a big hug. "Goodnight, Sammy!" Benny replied, and both drifted off to sleep, dreaming of their next adventure.

The next morning, Benny woke up to the sound of chirping birds and the smell of fresh bread. "Mmm, breakfast smells good!" he said, hopping out of his burrow.

After breakfast, Benny and Sammy played until they were both tired. "Time to rest," said Sammy. Benny curled up with his favorite blanket, feeling happy and cozy. And so, under the big red sky, the little friends slept, dreaming of their next fun day together.
