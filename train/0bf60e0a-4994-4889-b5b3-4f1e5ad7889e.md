Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a little girl named Lila who loved to explore. One sunny morning, she skipped outside and saw a tiny, shimmering sparkle on the ground. It was a magic seed!

Lila picked it up and planted it in her garden bed. She watered it carefully and waited. Days passed, and the seed grew into a big, bright flower with petals that changed colors like rainbows. Lila giggled as the flowers swayed in the breeze.

One evening, as Lila sat on her bed, she heard a soft *thump* outside. She peeked out the window and saw a little bunny hopping around her garden, nibbling on the petals. The bunny had a tiny satchel, and it whispered, "The flower gives happiness to all who love it!"

Lila smiled and said, "I love you, too!" The bunny blinked its big eyes and vanished, leaving only a trail of sparkles. The next day, Lila’s whole garden was full of happy, glowing flowers that made everything around them feel warm and bright. And from that day on, whenever Lila smiled, she knew she was surrounded by magic.
