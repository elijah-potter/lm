The story begins when an Ivy League student named Ben is called home after his seemingly healthy father dies suddenly of apparently natural causes. Ben comes home to find his mother taking comfort in his paternal uncle David's company. At first, he is grateful, but when they begin seeing each other openly, Ben begins to fixate on the idea that they must have been having an affair while his father was still alive. His fixation becomes so intense that it interferes with his relationship with his girlfriend, and his behavior becomes verbally abusive. His mother and uncle marry, ostensibly out of necessity, to protect the family's financial holdings. Despite their gaslighting, Ben learns that they plotted together to kill his father and cash out millions in life insurance. To boot, David is plotting to turn on his mother, cutting her out of the family fortune. Ben's girlfriend goes missing, and instead of trying to find her, Ben begins plotting David and his mother's demise. In the end, they end up dying together in a dramatic Shakespearean battle.
