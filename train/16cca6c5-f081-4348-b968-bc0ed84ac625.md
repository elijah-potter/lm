1. **Natural landscapes**: Absence of forests, rivers, lakes, mountains, or open fields.
2. **Wildlife**: Missing native flora and fauna, birds, insects, or animals.
3. **Water sources**: Lack of streams, ponds, or clean drinking water.
4. **Wild herbs**: No native medicinal or edible plants.
5. **Wildflowers**: Missing native wildflowers or blooming plants.
6. **Unspoiled air**: Pollution or industrial emissions in the atmosphere.
7. **Soundscapes**: Lack of natural sounds (rushing water, birdsong, crickets).
8. **Seasonal changes**: Fixed climate, no seasonal shifts (winter, summer, etc.).
9. **Undisturbed soil**: Erosion, compacted ground, or artificial turf.
10. **Ancient trees**: No old-growth or mature trees.
11. **Wild berries**: Missing naturally occurring berries or fruit-bearing plants.
12. **Moss and lichen**: Absence of fungi and algae on rocks/trees.
13. **Unmanaged spaces**: No undeveloped or wild areas.
14. **Natural erosion patterns**: Lack of natural rock formations or gorges.
15. **Silent forests**: No wind rustling through leaves or distant wildlife calls.
16. **Unbroken horizons**: Absence of unobstructed views of sky.
17. **Uncultivated soil**: No plowed, tilled, or chemically modified land.
18. **Wild grasses**: Missing native grasses or tall wild plants.
19. **Mysterious caves**: No underground caverns or natural formations.
20. **Unspoiled beaches**: Missing seashore, dunes, or coastal erosion.
