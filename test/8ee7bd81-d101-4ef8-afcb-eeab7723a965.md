Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny mouse named Milo. Milo loved to explore his garden every day. One sunny morning, he hopped outside and saw a shiny red apple near his favorite tree.

"Wow, a big, juicy apple!" Milo whispered. He picked it up and nibbled a little. Suddenly, he heard a tiny *squeak* from behind the leaves. Milo peeked and saw a little bunny named Benny sitting there.

"Hello, Benny!" Milo said. Benny blinked her big eyes. "I was hiding from my mommy, and I heard you eating the apple. Can I have a tiny piece?"

Milo smiled. "Of course! But first, let's play a game!" He spun in a circle and hopped around.

Benny laughed. "Let's chase the wind!"

They ran and giggled until their paws got tired. Just then, a big storm cloud rolled in, and thunder boomed. "Oh no!" Milo squeaked. Benny wrapped her fluffy tail around Milo's neck. "Don’t worry, we’ll stay dry under the big oak tree!"

They huddled together, listening to the rain. When the storm passed, the sun came out again, and Benny showed Milo how to roll in the soft grass. They shared a snack of berries and sang songs about clouds.

As the day ended, Milo and Benny curled up together under a blanket of leaves. "Best adventure ever," Benny said, snuggling close.

Milo smiled. "We’ll do it again tomorrow!" And with that, they fell asleep, dreaming of more fun in their garden.
