The weight of uncertainty lifted like a broken chain—
each hesitant step, a breath held too long,
now free as a storm passing through the open window.
Doubt, once a shadow, now dances in the light,
unfettered, unshackled—
not fear, but the quiet, knowing surrender
of what was once too heavy to carry.
The world, for once, does not demand proof,
only the slow, unspoken relief
of being allowed to exist
without the need to convince.
