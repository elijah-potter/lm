The detective sipped his coffee, avoiding eye contact with the suspect. The dim lighting of the interrogation room made their facial expressions subtle.

*"Why did you steal that cash from the safe?"*

The suspect’s fingers tapped restlessly on the table, then shifted uneasily. A slight hesitation before they leaned forward, voice lowered. *"I wasn’t stealing it… I just… needed it for an emergency."* Their breath hitched, and they glanced at the door. *"We don’t talk about that."*
