**Ending 1:**
The old clock tower stood silent at dawn, its hands frozen at 3:17. Elias traced the cracked face with trembling fingers, whispering, *"If the time is wrong, maybe everything is."* A gust of wind carried the sound of children’s laughter from the alley below—he’d left them in his rush. The brass bell clanged once, then twice, and the tower began to hum. The street below was alive, the children playing as if nothing had changed. Elias exhaled, his shadow stretching long across the pavement. *"Maybe it wasn’t the time,"* he murmured, watching the first light spill over the river.

**Ending 2:**
The library’s last book fell from the shelf with a *splat* onto the desk. Dr. Voss traced the ink stains with his fingers, then picked up the title: *The Alphabet of Forgotten Things*. The pages were blank except for one word, repeated in jagged letters: **DISAPPEAR**. The clock tower outside chimed midnight, but the seconds didn’t move. The city’s lights flickered, then died. Dr. Voss’s reflection in the glass stared back—older, hollow-eyed—before the mirror shattered. The last word, now in his own handwriting, was scrawled on the floor: **STAY.** The door behind him stood open, cold.
