What is Herpes?
Herpes is a common sexually transmitted infection that affects both men and women. It is caused by the herpes simplex virus (HSV) and is typically spread through sexual contact. Herpes can cause genital sores, but it can also be asymptomatic, meaning you may not have any symptoms. If you do have symptoms, they can range from mild to severe and include itching, burning, or pain in the genital area. Herpes can be treated with antiviral medications, but there is no cure.
