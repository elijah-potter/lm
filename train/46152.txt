What is a 3-way valve and how does it work
A 3-way ball valve is a type of valve that is used to control the flow of fluids. It consists of a ball that has three ports, or openings, that are connected to three different pipes. The three ports are arranged in a Y-shaped configuration, with the two side ports being connected to the main pipe and the third port being connected to a branch pipe. When the ball is in the upright position, all three ports are open and fluid can flow freely through the valve.
However, when the ball is rotated to one of the side positions, two of the ports are blocked and fluid can only flow through the remaining open port. This allows for precise control over the flow of fluid through the valve, which is why three-way ball valves are often used in applications where multiple options for fluid flow are required.
