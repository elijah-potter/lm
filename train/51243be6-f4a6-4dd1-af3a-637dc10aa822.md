**Unspoken Contract**
*"The Wall"*

The old mill’s chimney, cracked and black with age, stood as silent sentinel between the two estates. Inside, the Smiths’ workshop hummed with the rhythmic clatter of hammers; outside, the Carvers’ fields swayed under the moon. Theirs was no pact carved in blood or ink, but in the weight of unspoken oaths:

- **Silence as Shield**: What they did not say was what kept the other’s hands from breaking first. A stolen tool here, a borrowed axe there—no names were exchanged, only the exchange of favors.
- **The Ledger of Eyes**: A single glance could be a confession. A withheld glance, a debt. The Carvers kept their share of the harvest, the Smiths their secret forges; neither dared claim the other’s debt.
- **The Ghost of What Wasn’t Said**: On the anniversary of the last argument, both would find the same knife—rusted, but sharp enough to cut through lies.

The agreement lived in the gaps between words, where the wind carried the scent of sawdust and iron. It was not a treaty, but a truce written in the language of what neither dared to name aloud.
