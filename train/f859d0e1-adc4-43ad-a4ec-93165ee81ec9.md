The wind howled through the skeletal trees of Blackthorn Hollow, their branches clawing at the storm-wracked sky like the fingers of some forgotten god. The old mill, half-collapsed but still standing, groaned as its timbers shifted under the weight of the gale. Inside, the air smelled of damp wood and iron, the remnants of a forge that had once forged blades for kings and rebels alike.

Elias sat at the worn wooden desk, his fingers tracing the edge of the ledger where the final entry had been crossed out. The last transaction—gold for secrets—was no longer valid. The village had spoken, and its silence was worse than any word.

Outside, the rain lashed against the windows, turning the glass into a prism of fractured light. Elias exhaled slowly, the scent of old parchment and damp earth mixing in his lungs. He had spent his life chasing answers, but this time, the questions had outrun him.

What would happen next?
