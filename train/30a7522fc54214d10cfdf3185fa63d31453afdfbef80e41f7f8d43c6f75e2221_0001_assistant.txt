Friend 1 (1)/Friend 2 (2)

1: Nice day today, isn't it?
2: It sure is.
1: Did you bring any bread for the ducks?
2: Actually, yes, I got a couple of slices in my pocket.
1: Do you mind giving me a slice so I can feed the ducks?
2: I don't mind (reaches into a pocket and becomes immediately worried)
1: What's the matter?
2: I- I can't.
1: What do you mean? Come on, let me have some bread! (reaches into 2's pocket for him)
1: What is this?
2: I'm sorry, I was going to tell you about it.
1: (Pulls out a harmonica from 2's pocket) Why do you have this harmonica in your pocket?
2: I found it on the ground.
1: No, you didn't! You're lying!
2: Okay, fine, I'm sorry.
1: You promised you wouldn't play another harmonica ever again. Don't you remember what happened the last time you played?
2: Yes.
1: Tell me, then.
2: I accidentally killed a man with my harmonica playing.
1: That's right, and how did you kill the man with your harmonica playing?
2: My harmonica playing was so beautiful that the man became distracted from his work on the construction site and accidentally fell to his death from the 20th floor of a building.
1: That's right...do you want to take a chance playing another harmonica and possibly distracting someone so much that they die?
2: No. You're right. I should get rid of this thing. But it's so fun to play!
1: Think of others for once, friend!
2: Okay, I'll give it to you.
1: Thank you.
