All dogs are in need of some sort of training. Consider these breeds if you're looking for an intelligent dog that learns quickly:
1. Border Collie: These dogs are athletes that are very trainable. 
2. German Shepherd: They are highly intelligent and can retain a large number of commands for specialized jobs.
3. Labrador Retriever: With an easy-going nature, these dogs can achieve goals they set their mind to and have a quick wit. 
4. Golden Retriever: These dogs are cheerful with a desire to please their owners. They take direction well.
5. Australian Shepherd: These dogs have a sharp intelligence that will need frequent and new activities.
6. Standard Poodle: They are intelligent and obedient dogs that learn quickly.
7. Doberman Pinscher: They are fiercely loyal and can be easily trained to do a variety of tasks like guard work and obedience. 
8. Papillon: These dogs are intelligent and a good choice for therapy work. 
9. Australian Cattle Dog: These are hard-working dogs that can be very clever and loyal.
