The first light flickered through the cracked window, painting the room in soft gold. Outside, the city slept, its dreams tangled in the whispers of the wind. Inside, Elias sat at his desk, fingers tracing the worn edges of his journal, each page a relic of forgotten conversations. The last entry read: *"They say love is a flame, but what if it’s only ash left after the fire?"*

A knock at the door startled him. The doorknob turned slowly, the latch groaning like a sigh. His mother stood there, her dark hair pulled into a loose braid, her hands trembling—not from fear, but from the weight of time. "They’re asking for you," she whispered. "The one who doesn’t answer."

Elias exhaled, the breath fogging in the cold air. What would he say? What would he *become*?
