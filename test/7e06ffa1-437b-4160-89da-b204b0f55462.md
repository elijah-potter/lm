Eyewitnesses described the sound as a chorus of voices repeating the same phrase: *"The clock is broken."*

Local historian **Dr. Elara Voss** confirmed the phenomenon through her research, stating, *"The old clock tower in the Plaza de las Lágrimas has been silent since the Great Storm of 1892. But this isn’t just noise—it’s a call. The mechanism inside is reacting to something outside the tower."* Security footage from the plaza, captured by a citizen’s phone, showed the clock hands abruptly stop at **4:17:00**, then reverse direction, spinning backward before resetting to zero.

Mayor **Rafael Mendoza** issued a mandatory curfew from **5:00 PM to 7:00 AM** tonight, urging residents to stay indoors. *"This is not a drill,"* he declared, pointing to a series of **blackout warnings** flickering across solar-powered billboards. The city’s emergency protocols, activated in response to the **"Unusual Phenomena Report"**, have already deployed teams to investigate the clock tower and nearby archaeological sites, where ancient stone carvings depicting **broken time** were found intact.

Meanwhile, a mysterious figure—a hooded stranger with glowing amber eyes—was seen near the old observatory last night. The figure did not respond to police calls, but their shadow cast an unnatural, **twisted reflection** on the ground, as if time itself had warped. No physical evidence was found at the scene, but the stranger left behind a single, handwritten note: *"The watch is broken. It will not stop."* Police are treating the incident as a potential act of vandalism or sabotage, though the note’s cryptic tone has left officials puzzled.

Tonight, Solara’s residents will remain under heightened vigilance, as the city’s historians and scientists debate whether the **"Echoes of the Old"** are a warning—or the first sign of something far older and more dangerous than they imagined.
