1) Looking for Alaska: Fictional story that was written in 2005 about a boy named Miles Halter who leaves home to understand something called the  “Great Perhaps.” 
2) An Abundance of Katherines: Fictional story was written in 2006 about a boy named Collin Singleton who has been dumped by eighteen girls named Katherine. Colin goes on a road trip to understand what is going on and understand how to stop the same thing from occurring.
3) Let It Snow: Three Holiday Romances: This novel was written by John Green and two other bestselling authors, Maureen Johnson and Lauren Myracle. All three stories are about a town called Gracetown which has a lot of snow and chaos on Christmas Eve.
