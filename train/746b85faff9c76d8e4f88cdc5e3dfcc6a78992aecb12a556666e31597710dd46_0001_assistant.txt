Dear sun,
I want to take this opportunity to thank you for all that you've done for me and continue to do. It’s your superpower, and I feel so blessed to be able to enjoy it all, your warmth and light all day long.
Since my reproduction requires pollination, the morning warmth that you provide attracts more bees and other insects to me. They help carry pollen from other sunflowers to me, pollinating me and allowing me to have easier reproduction.
Your light also helps ensure that when my head is illuminated in the morning, I glow, thus attracting even more insects to help with pollination. 
I can't thank you enough. May you continue to shine and do wonders for all.
Best regards,
Sunflower.
