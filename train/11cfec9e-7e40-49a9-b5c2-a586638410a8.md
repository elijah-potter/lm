A delivery driver in Portland noticed a stranger’s suitcase marked "Urgent: Seattle" while sorting through a package at a local hub. Curious, he followed the wrong route—turns out, the package was bound for a friend in Eugene. By the time he realized his mistake, the courier had already taken the wrong turn. The driver’s original plan to drop off groceries at his own apartment was derailed when he arrived at the friend’s doorstep instead, leading to a spontaneous visit, shared meals, and a friendship that saved both their weekends.
