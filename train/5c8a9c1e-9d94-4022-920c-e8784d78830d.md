Two days later, the café’s empty floor echoed with the clatter of empty mugs. Clara wiped down the counter, her fingers tracing the cracks in the worn linoleum. *"They were arguing,"* she muttered, but the words faded into static—her brain replayed the chaos differently. She saw the man’s raised fist, the woman’s back as she turned, the door slamming shut behind her. *"He was protecting her."*

Across the street, Daniel sat on his balcony, sipping cold coffee. His phone buzzed—a text from his sister: *"We left them together. You remember?"* He scrolled, the image of the woman’s slumped shoulders in his mind’s eye. *"She was drunk. He was angry."* His hands shook as he typed: *"She left him."*

The café’s bell chimed. Clara looked up—Daniel stepped inside, his eyes fixed on her. Neither spoke. The air between them was thick with unspoken truths.
