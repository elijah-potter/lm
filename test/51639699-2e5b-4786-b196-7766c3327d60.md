**Mayor’s Office Announces New Greenway Initiative: City of Solara Expands Bicycle Lane Network**
Solara’s first fully solar-powered bike lanes, funded by a $2.1M grant from the EcoMobility Initiative, officially opened yesterday, connecting the historic downtown core to the new Riverfront Park. The 12-mile path, equipped with kinetic charging stations and LED-lit bridges, is expected to cut commute times by 20% and reduce emissions by 15% annually. Residents praised the project as a "game-changer," while critics called for more funding for public transit. The city has also launched a pilot program to replace 500 streetlights with AI-controlled solar-powered fixtures, aiming to cut energy costs by 40% within three years. Mayor Elias Voss stressed, *"Solara isn’t just building paths—we’re building a future where every block is a step toward sustainability."* Community members can apply for free bike-sharing passes starting June 15th.

**Transportation Updates:**
- **Metro Rail Expansion:** Phase 2 of the Solara Transit Hub, featuring automated shuttle bays and a new underground parking garage, opens tomorrow. Fare increases of 5% effective June 1st remain in effect.
- **Drone Delivery Pilot:** Amazon’s first commercial air-taxi service, *Solara Sky*, launched yesterday, offering 20-minute rides between downtown and the airport. Regulations cap flights to 150 feet and require passenger consent.
