The wind carried whispers of forgotten secrets through the cracked windows of the abandoned library, each page turned a door to another memory. The shelves, once filled with knowledge, now groaned under the weight of dust and time, their spines split like old promises.

Outside, the rain pattered against the glass, a rhythm that matched the slow unraveling of the city’s dreams. Inside, a single light flickered—an ember from a long-dead candle—casting long shadows over the pages of a book no one had read in decades. The cover was worn, its title faded to a whisper: *"The Last Echoes of the Forgotten."*

She found it by accident, her fingers brushing the edge of a shelf where the air smelled of ozone and something older, something that clung to the ink like a ghost. She picked up the book, its spine creaking under her touch. The first line was scrawled in ink that had faded but not fully vanished:

*"What remains when the world forgets?"*

The question hung in the air between the pages, a challenge to those who dared to read.
