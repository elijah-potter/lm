A crumpled, yellowed note slipped between the pages of an antique book in the attic of an abandoned estate. The paper bore faded ink of a hand that had once been calloused from years of labor.

*"The clockwork heart keeps ticking, though the hands won’t move.
Find the pocket where the dust still lingers.
The key is silver, but not the kind that gleams—
the kind that hums when you hold it right."*

Beneath it, a symbol carved into the wood of a nearby shelf: a serpent coiled around a broken hourglass, its glass now a shard of starlight. The attic’s air thickened with the scent of old oil and rust as the dust motes danced like fallen stars. The note’s sender had left no name, only a warning: *"Do not open what the past forgot to close."*
