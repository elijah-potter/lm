A tiny brass key, lost in a storm-worn pocket,
its chain snapped—yet still clutched by the wind.
On the cracked tabletop where it rolled,
it became a sign: the door that had never opened,
now stood ajar, its teeth gnawed by time.
No one remembers why it was there,
but the moment it caught the light,
it whispered: *you were never meant to leave.*
The rest? Just echoes of what was lost.
