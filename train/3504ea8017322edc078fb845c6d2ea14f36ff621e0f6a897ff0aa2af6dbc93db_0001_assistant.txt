On a cold and rainy day, an old alley cat met a little boy on the street. The cat was feeling very soggy and uncomfortable, and the boy asked him, "Wouldn't you like to come home with me?"

The cat had never met a human who cared about alley cats before. Surprised, she replied, "I'd be very much obliged if I could sit by a warm furnace and perhaps have a saucer of milk." 

She went home with the kind stranger, and they became good friends. However, the boy's mother hated cats and shouted that she wouldn't give a stray alley cat any milk. The boy apologized to the cat and told her that she could stay and he would find a way to bring her a saucer of milk each day. For three weeks, he fed her in the cellar. But one day, his mother found the cat's saucer and whipped him. She threw the cat out, but the boy sneaked out later and found her. 

They went walking together in the park, and the boy told her that he wanted an airplane so he could fly anywhere in the world. 

She asked him, "Would you like to fly very, very much?"

The boy replied, "I certainly would. I'd do anything if I could fly."

"Well," she said, "if you'd really like to fly that much, I think I know of a sort of a way you might get to fly while you're still a little boy." 

"You mean you know where I could get an airplane?" the boy asked her excitedly.

"Well, not exactly an airplane, but something even better," the cat replied. She told him of her traveling days when she was a young cat, and of her last journey the previous spring when she sailed to the Island of Tangerina and stopped at the port of Cranberry. "Well, it just so happened that I missed the boat, and while waiting for the next I thought I'd look around a bit. I was particularly interested in a place called Wild Island, which we had passed on our way to Tangerina." She told him of the jungles and the wild animals who lived there, and of what she found while exploring the island.
