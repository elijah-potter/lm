One day, Gerry’s mother made his favorite snack – freshly baked chocolate chip cookies. After they had sat on the rack to cool, his mother put them in the cookie jar. They smelled divine, and Gerry’s mouth began to water. 
“Can I have one?” he asked.
“Of course,” his mother replied. “But only one, alright?”
When she left the kitchen, Gerry went over to the cookie jar and stuck his hand inside. As he was doing so, he realized his mother was no longer around, meaning there was nobody there to stop him from eating more than one cookie!
So Gerry wrapped his fingers around a bunch of cookies and tried pulling his hand out. But try as he might, his fist was now too big to come out of the cookie jar! He struggled a bit, pulling and maneuvering, but he couldn’t free his hand. Finally, he let go of all the cookies except one. He was able to slip his hand out of the cookie jar.
As he nibbled on the chocolate chip cookie, he thought, “I guess there’s no point in being greedy, because you can’t have everything.”
And he was right! When Gerry stuck to his share of cookies, he was able to take his hand out of the jar. It’s always good to remember not to take more than our share, or else we’ll be stuck trying to get more, and in the process, deprive ourselves of the satisfaction of what we already have!
