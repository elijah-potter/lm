In the dim glow of an old clock tower, the last streetlamp flickered like a dying ember. The wind carried whispers of forgotten secrets through the cobblestones, where the echoes of laughter from a hundred years ago still clung to the air.

A key turned in the rusted lock of an abandoned door. The air inside was thick with the scent of damp stone and something older—something that hummed with the weight of forgotten dreams. The walls pulsed faintly, as if breathing, and the shadows moved in slow, deliberate motions, like figures watching from the past.

Then, the door creaked open.

A figure stepped inside, their silhouette stretching unnaturally against the dim light. They carried a lantern, its flame casting long, dancing shadows across the ceiling. The air smelled of old books and ink, and the scent of something warm lingered—coffee, maybe, or the faintest trace of something else entirely.

The door behind them clicked shut, sealing the past inside. The lantern flickered again, and the figure turned, their gaze lingering on the door as if waiting for it to open again.

*"It’s always been here,"* they murmured, stepping forward into the dark.
