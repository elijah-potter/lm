Ever since I was a young child, I was eager to explore the world,
But as I grew older, I realized my perception has swirled.
Things were not as good as I had imagined them to be, 
And I spent a lot of time just watching the world pass me. 
Almost as if I was a background character in my own story, 
Never capable of reaching my hopeful glory. 

I've been very unmotivated, as if I don't have a purpose, 
Feeling like I don't belong to anything but a circus. 
So, higher power, why exactly am I here? 
I swear, I'm so tired of crying these tears. 
So help me find my purpose, my reason for being,
Please, I beg you, just help me find my life meaning. 

Nobody else seems to quite understand,
Almost like everything just falls into their hands. 
I don't have that luxury, and I feel so lost, 
Sure life can be great, but at what cost? 
Is it normal to feel so soulless, so lost, and in despair?
Please, can someone tell me, why this life just isn't fair?
