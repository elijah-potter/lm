The Laws of Physics ensure that we are unable to hear sounds from far away in space, thanks to the large vacuum of space. Sound waves work by vibrations traveling through a medium, but if there's no medium to travel through like in space, they can't work. But if it were possible for sound to travel through space the same way it does on the Earth's atmosphere, we would be able to hear the sun. Unfortunately, it would also be very loud.

The sun would be a blaring, white-noise machine that would have the same intensity as a loud rock concert, at all hours of daylight. This is because the sun is extremely loud, according to scientists. The sun is a maelstrom of superheated plasma and the nuclear reaction that powers a star such as a sun would cause massive cells of superheated gas to rise and fall constantly across its surface, creating a tremendous amount of sound.
