In the hollow of the old oak’s roots, where the last light bled through the cracks in the world, a child found the first word carved into the bark itself: **"Here."** They traced the letters with trembling fingers, whispering the sound into the damp earth. They followed it blindly, past the neon signs and the endless scrolls of data, until they stood before a mirror that showed not their face but the same hollow where the oak had once grown: a wound in time, a door waiting to open.

Inside, the air smelled of damp earth and something older. The scholar stepped forward, and the walls dissolved like smoke. They found themselves back at the oak’s roots, but this time, the child was there, their eyes wide with realization. The word **"Here"** had never been a destination—it was the key to the loop.

The scholar reached out, and the child’s fingers brushed theirs. The moment they touched, the world snapped back into place, but the oak had grown again, its roots twisting into something new. The scholar saw it then: the cycle wasn’t ending, it was beginning.

They whispered, **"Here,"** and the oak answered with a sigh. The loop was broken, but the question remained—where did it end?
