Dearest William, 

It pains me to write these words as they create a feeling of reality in what has seemed like a bad dream. It seems that Harry and his wife have decided to leave our family. 

Over my many years as not only the Queen but as the head of this family, I have surely seen both beautiful and challenging times. But this is undoubtedly one of the hardest for me. 

I know that you have always put our family and the country first and that Harry, well, Harry has taken a different path.

I want you to know that I see and appreciate your dedication, especially in light of these events. 

I haven’t yet decided what I will say to Harry, if anything. For now, we must deal with the fallout of his decision as best we can and avoid any other scandal at all costs. I know I can count on you. 

Your Queen and Grandmama
