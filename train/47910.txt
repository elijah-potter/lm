Ötzi, the mummified Stone Age man found in an Italian glacier nine years ago, was thawed slightly for a few hours this week so scientists could take samples of bone, teeth and internal organs for DNA analysis. They want to learn more about his health, his diet, and possibly how he died. Research on the 5300-year-old corpse was delayed for years while Italy and Austria fought over ownership. Italy won, and Ötzi is now housed at -6°C in a museum at Bolzano in the Italian Alps. The man's face was reconstructed using computerised tomography, but few internal studies of his organs have been permitted before.
