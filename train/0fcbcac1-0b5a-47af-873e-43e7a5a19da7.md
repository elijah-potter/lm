**Log of Journey: The Unseen Shift**

**Date:** 05/12/XX
**Location:** Blackwood Pass → Sunrise Lake → Old Mill Ruins

**Activity:**
Departed at 04:30 AM, first light a blur through mist. The trail was slick with dew, every step echoing in the hollowed pines. The guidebook called this the *"Unsettled Path"*—a misnomer, perhaps. The path itself was *wrong*.

At 07:18, the first real light spilled over the horizon. The lake was a mirror, cracked like glass. The air smelled of ozone and something older, damp earth. The guide, **Mira**, had been quiet since the first climb. She didn’t ask why I’d chosen this route; she just followed.

The ruins at the mill were half-buried. The wheels were still there, rusted but intact, like silent sentinels. Inside, the floorboards creaked underfoot, though no one had walked here in decades. A single candle flickered in the dark corner. I lit it from the guide’s pack.

**Observations:**
- The candle’s flame burned blue, not yellow. I’d never seen that before.
- Mira’s voice, when she spoke, carried a resonance—like her words were filtering through something.
- At 08:45, I found a pocket watch. No brand, no markings. Just a time that didn’t match the clock outside. The hands spun backward.

**Aftermath:**
Left the ruins at 09:12. The trail home was familiar, but the trees seemed to *breathe* differently. By the time we reached the car, Mira’s hand was cold to mine.

**Final Note:**
No one has asked for the details. But I’ve been carrying a different key in my pocket since then.
