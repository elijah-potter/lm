Central Park's **Silent Plaza** – A circular pavilion with no seating, only open floor. Surrounded by glass-walled cafés and art installations that project soundless visuals (e.g., flickering LED patterns, static imagery). Participants sit cross-legged, hands clasped, with earplugs provided. A central speaker emits ambient white noise; no speech, only silence punctuated by soft instrumental tracks playing via hidden speakers. The plaza is ringed by a low fence with illuminated murals depicting minimalist, non-verbal interactions. Timed sessions (e.g., 30-minute circles) rotate between participants via a digital lottery system. No phones, no noise; only contemplation.
