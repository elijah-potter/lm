Giving a small gift as a thank you to the dinner host is a polite thing to do. Here’s a list of gift ideas:

• A potted plant such as an African violet or an orchid, which are both safe for pets
• Cheeseboard with fancy cheese and crackers
• Box of chocolates or other candies
• A really good bottle of olive oil or other seasonings
• A nice set of candles or a large 3-wick candle
• Gourmet cookies in a decorative tin or nice container
• Sampler cheesecakes in different flavors
