The **Academy Award for Worst Performance in a Supporting Role (Non-Human)**—a statuette awarded annually to the most *unimpressive* animated character from a critically panned film. Past winners include:
- *"Zoe the Invisible Cat"* (*2019*, for *The Unseen*) – a feline so poorly drawn that even its own owner couldn’t tell it from a plush toy.
- *"The Grumpy Cloud"* (*2022*, *Cloud Nine*) – a weather system with a personality so bland it inspired a therapist’s bill.
- *"The Silent Staircase"* (*2023*, *Legacy of Dust*) – a generic hallway that fans voted "most unoriginal" by a landslide.

Nominees often feature characters like:
- **A sentient toaster** that still burns itself when it’s not being toasted.
- **A sentient toaster** that burns itself *and* the baker.
- **A sentient toaster** that burns itself because the baker *never* checked if it was plugged in.

**Why no one wants it:**
- **No prestige**—it’s not a "Best Picture" or "Best Director," just a joke award for animation that’s *worse than the animation*.
- **No audience**—only fans of cringe-worthy CGI and 2003-era CGI.
- **No legacy**—the statuette is made of *recycled* bad ideas from past films.
- **No real winners**—it’s a rotating trophy for whatever animation fails to rise above *basic*.

**Final Verdict:** A trophy for artists who’ve given up on storytelling and embraced *visual suffering*. Accepting it would be like winning "Most Likely to Die in a Car Crash"—except the car crash was CGI.
