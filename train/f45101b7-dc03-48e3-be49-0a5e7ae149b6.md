The subtle shift in the office coffee machine’s water filter—replaced with a slightly narrower grade—was initially overlooked by the janitorial staff. Over time, the resulting coffee’s milder bitterness became the norm, altering the daily routine of the 12-person team without anyone questioning why the morning brew tasted different. The only evidence of the change was the faintest trace of a new, barely perceptible aroma lingering in the air. By the end of the month, the team had already adjusted their expectations, assuming it was just the coffee’s natural evolution.
