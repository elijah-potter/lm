Regrettably, impulsive decisions—often driven by urgency, emotion, or misjudgment—can lead to outcomes with lasting consequences. Here’s a concise example of a haste-driven choice and its repercussions:

---
**The Hasty Call**
At 11:59 PM, under the pressure of an urgent client email, Sarah clicked "accept" on a last-minute contract without reviewing terms. The agreement, though legally binding, included a clause that waived her right to recuse from a pending audit. The next morning, she discovered her colleague’s suspicious activity in the same department, triggering an internal investigation. By the time she realized her oversight, the audit had begun, and her authority to challenge the findings was compromised.

---
**Lesson Learned**
Haste often masks due diligence. Always:
1. **Pause**: Ask, *"Is this decision critical enough to warrant a second read?"*
2. **Clarify**: "What happens if I don’t act now?" (e.g., deadlines, liability).
3. **Proxy Check**: Delegate to a trusted peer to audit high-stakes moves.
