**Boom, crackle, steam hisses** from the furnace—cobblestones underfoot **grind** like rusted gears in the morning chill. The air thickens with **sulfur and damp stone**, mixed with **burning coal** and the faint **sweet tang of damp hay** from the stables. A **low, rumbling growl** from the bakery—**dough rising, yeast bubbling**—drifted with **toasted bread scent**, warm and golden. Children’s **laughter** cuts through the **whisper of wind through rusted iron bars**, then a **clang** as a blacksmith’s hammer strikes **molten metal**, sending up **sizzling sparks and the scent of iron cooling**. The **clatter of hooves** on uneven stones, then silence—**only the distant **drumming of a river** through the canyon, **stone and water**, a **deep, endless hum**.
