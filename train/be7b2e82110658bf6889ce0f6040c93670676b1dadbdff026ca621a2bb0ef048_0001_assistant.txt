“What do you think about this?”
She held the dress up against her body. I considered the dress and then her. It was cotton, with a pretty pastel floral pattern and big puffy sleeves. The perfect kind of airy dress for a summer picnic, but not exactly something for the fancy dinner we had scheduled for tonight. I didn’t say anything, but clearly, my expression was loud enough. She lowered the dress, looking a little disappointed.
“It suits you,” I offered, but the damage was done.
We moved to the next rack, both of us pawing through the clothes on offer, none of them really speaking out to us. This was why I hated shopping, especially for clothes. I was so particular about things: the fabric, the cut, the pattern, the vibe. My sister, on the other hand, was not. She thought a summer dress was fancy-dinner appropriate. Perhaps it would suit my sister, but unfortunately, I put a lot of time and effort into planning things out. My family liked to say I’m too type-A, that I need to take things easy but I can’t and they will just have to accept that.
“Oh, I think I found it!”
I turned around to see another dress in my sister’s hands. Somehow she managed to find something even worse than the summer dress. It was dark, short, and slinky with some glittery fabric. Perfect for clubbing, but not exactly what I imagined for my party. I didn’t respond, just turned back to the rack and kept looking.
