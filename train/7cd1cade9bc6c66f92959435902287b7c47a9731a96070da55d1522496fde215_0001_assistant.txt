Kazama leaped backward, avoiding the needles that sunk into the concrete where he was moments before. He twisted in mid-air to avoid another volley, landing on his hands and spinning rapidly, creating a gust of wind that stopped two more volleys of needles. He thrust into the ground and launched forward. He was instantly in front of his enemy. He grabbed the woman by the face and slammed her into the ground. She bounced after hitting so hard but twisted in the air, throwing out a whirlwind kick that Kazama blocked with a single wrist.

She landed on her hands, threw out a horse kick, and backhand sprung to her feet. She wiped the blood from her mouth and stared daggers into Kazama, who stood like an insurmountable wall. He said, "You're the one who challenged me. Are you going to keep running away?"

The woman gritted her teeth and began running circles around Kazama so fast that it stirred up a vortex that threatened to rip the trees around them out by the roots. She said, "My speed is unparalleled. I'll let you taste the true strength of the lightning flash! Thousand Amp Flash!"

She drew her straight sword and moved like a bolt of lightning, the blade poised to cut cleanly through Kazama's neck. However, when it struck, he wasn't there. He moved faster than she could even see. He was behind her, and his fist came within an inch of her face. The impact came up just short, and instead, the pressure cut a hole through the forest behind them. Kazama said, "I think this is your loss. I'm going shopping now, so leave me alone."

The woman sank to her knees, completely defeated and in disbelief.
