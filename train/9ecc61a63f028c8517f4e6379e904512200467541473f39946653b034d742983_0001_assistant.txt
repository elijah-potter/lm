It was hot outside but little Katie was crying again. Her crying is too loud. I went to the backyard to play with my excavator. I used it to dig in the garden because the dirt there was the softest.

“Squeak, squeak! Hiya!” I heard speaking from somewhere. It was a very tiny voice. But there were no tiny people in our backyard. And I am definitely not tiny anymore, so it couldn’t be me.

“Squeak, squeak! Up here!”

I looked up to find a fluffy squirrel on the fence. It twitched its tail and squeaked again. “Hello!” I said, “What’s your name?”

“My name’s Sophie! What’s yours?”

“I’m Jordan. I’m six.” I giggled.

“Nice to meet you, Jordan! Are you digging for acorns? Acorns are the best!”

“I’m digging for dinosaurs,” I said, “But if I find acorns, I can give them to you.” 

“That would be wonderful! Thank you! Maybe I can help you find a dinosaur too.”

“Can squirrels find dinosaurs?”

“Yes! I saw one yesterday. Little Julia had a yellow one over in the Wilsons’ backyard. She didn’t dig it from underground, though.”

“Oh wow, maybe I should search in her yard!” I thought a bit and decided I should give my new friend, Sophie, a snack to say thank you. “Do you like gummy bears?”

“I don’t think so. Gummy bears are too sticky on my teeth. I like nuts and veggies!”

“Oh, ok.” I pondered a bit again. “Here, I’ll get some of my momma’s cherry tomatoes!” I tugged open the chicken wire surrounding Momma’s favorite bushes and pulled a couple of plump red tomatoes off the vine. “Here you go!”

“Thank you so much, Jordan! These are delicious!” We both smiled.
