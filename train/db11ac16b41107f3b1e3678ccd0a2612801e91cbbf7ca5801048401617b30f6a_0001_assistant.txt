Relax in the once-sanctified grounds of the Otterton Manner. Richard Duke's stunning vision has created a location for the ages. World-class masonry has constructed a building that's elegant and humble at the same time. It blends with the environment and looks out along the wonderful, picturesque land of Otterton. With 55,000 acres of lush land, you can do everything from farming, to worship, to butter churning, or just visit the sights and kick back in a classic Otterton cottage. Get away from it all and don the azure arms of the Duke family to become a Griffin of the house of Duke: only at Richard Duke's Otterton Mansion.
