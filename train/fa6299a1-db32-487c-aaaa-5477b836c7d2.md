Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around, nibble on carrots, and chase his fluffy tail. One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw a shiny red apple near his burrow.

"Wow!" Benny said, his eyes sparking. He picked up the apple and nibbled a tiny bite. "Mmm! This looks like a yummy treat!"

Just then, Benny heard a tiny voice. "Help! Help! My flower is all wilted!"

Benny looked around and saw a little mouse named Molly standing near a sad-looking tulip. The flower was droopy and looked very sad.

"Oh no!" Benny said. "I should help Molly!"

Benny hopped over to Molly and asked, "What can I do to make your flower happy again?"

Molly said, "Just a little water will help! I have a tiny cup with a drop of water in it."

Benny took the water from Molly and gently poured it into the soil around the tulip. The flower started to wiggle and turn its head toward the sun.

"Wow!" Molly said. "It's getting better!"

Soon, the tulip stood tall and proud, its petals opening wide. Molly smiled and said, "Thank you, Benny! You saved my flower!"

Benny felt very happy. He knew that helping others made the world a little brighter. From that day on, Benny always looked for ways to be kind and share his kindness with everyone he met.

As Benny hopped home, he saw a group of butterflies fluttering around. He waved to them and said, "Good day to you all!"

The butterflies smiled and fluttered away, leaving Benny feeling extra happy and content.

And that was the end of Benny's happy day!
