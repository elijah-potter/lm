A vast, open-air amphitheater with tiered stone seating arranged in concentric circles. The space is illuminated by natural light streaming through high, arched skylights with intricate geometric patterns. Artistic sculptures of human figures in silent, contemplative poses line the perimeter, their expressions frozen in moments of deep thought. A central circular plaza features a large, reflective pool where the only sounds are the gentle lap of water and the occasional rustle of leaves. In the background, a series of connected, minimalist galleries with no doors or windows display abstract, monochromatic installations that shift subtly with movement, creating a hypnotic, immersive experience. The entire environment is bathed in soft, ambient lighting that changes color gradually throughout the day, enhancing the sense of timelessness and quiet focus.
