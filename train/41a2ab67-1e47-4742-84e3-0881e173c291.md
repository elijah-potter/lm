A hidden grove of ancient cedars where the air hums with the whispers of unseen voices. Moss-covered stones pulse faintly underfoot, shifting temperature like a slow breath. The wind carries static—static that dissolves into fragments of conversations, half-sung lullabies, and distant laughter from a forgotten feast. At dusk, the trees form a living arch, their shadows weaving into shapes that shift between shapes—faces, hands, or perhaps the silhouette of a child playing in the underbrush. The deeper you walk, the more the silence itself seems to hold breath, waiting.
