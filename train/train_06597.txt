Barry, Thomas and Emmanuel are to share a jar of 200 jelly beans. If Thomas takes 10%, and Barry and Emmanuel are to share the remainder in the ratio 4:5 respectively, how many jelly beans will Emmanuel get?
10% of 200 jelly beans is (10/100)*200 = <<200*10/100=20>>20 jelly beans
Thomas takes 20 jelly beans leaving 200-20 = <<200-20=180>>180 jelly beans
180 jelly beans are to be shared in the ratio 4:5 so each share is 180/(4+5) = <<180/(4+5)=20>>20 jelly beans
Emmanuel gets 5 shares which is 5*20 = <<5*20=100>>100
