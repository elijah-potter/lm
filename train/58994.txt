Rama was an ancient city in India.
The ancient books of India's Rama Empire have been preserved, unlike those of China, Egypt, Central America, and Peru. India, despite devastation by wars and invasion, managed to maintain a large part of its ancient history.
In the past century, the sophisticated cities of Mohenjo Daro (Mound of the Dead) and Harappa have been discovered in the Indus Valley of modern-day Pakistan.
The cities were highly developed leading archaeologists to believe that they were conceived as a whole before being built: an early example of city planning. The plumbing-sewage system throughout the large city is superior to that found in Pakistan, India, and most Asian countries today.
