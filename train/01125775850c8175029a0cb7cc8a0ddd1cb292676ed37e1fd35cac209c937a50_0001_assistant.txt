The notion of pirates making captives walk the plank is a popular but unsubstantiated myth. While punishments like flogging and marooning were common among real pirates, the plank concept emerged from literature such as Daniel Defoe's 1724 book “A General History of the Pyrates", followed by writers like Charles Ellms, Robert Louis Stevenson, and Howard Pyle, who popularized the idea in pirate stories. However, there is no historical evidence supporting its existence.
