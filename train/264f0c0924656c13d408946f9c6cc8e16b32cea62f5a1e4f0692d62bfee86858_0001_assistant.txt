Springtime: 

This is the time of renewal and invigoration of nature, so choose colors that represent bright green leaves, fresh air, and flower buds. 

•	Moss green 
•	Salmon pink 
•	Blossom white 
•	Sky blue 
•	Saffron yellow

Summertime: 

When summer swings into full gear, it cranks up the heat with intense sunshine, flowers in full bloom, and lots of splashy fun in the water. 

•	Lemon yellow
•	Watermelon red
•	Lime green 
•	Ocean blue 
•	Grape compote purple 

Autumn: 

Autumn colors shift from the brightness of summer to faded Fall hues that remind you of all the goodness of the season. 

•	Burnt umber 
•	Campfire smoke grey 
•	Cinnamon brown 
•	Pumpkin orange 
•	Denim blue 
Winter: 

When it’s cold outside, all you want to do is get cozy and comfortable at home. That means plush blankets and heavy throw pillows in the following winter colors. 

•	Steel grey 
•	Pine green
•	Crimson red
•	Cream
•	Icy blue
