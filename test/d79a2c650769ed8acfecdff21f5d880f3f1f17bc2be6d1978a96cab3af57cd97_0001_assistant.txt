If a picture is worth a thousand words
A smell is a thousand and one 
Oh to recount the world's wonderful smells 
That should be some good fun 

Fragrant cookies that are oven-fresh
That delicious scent of my youth 
Vanilla and chocolate mingling in the air
I can't have just one, that's the truth!

Fresh-cut grass in the heat of the summer
A smell as earthy and green as can be 
Almost as good as the salty blue breeze
Of waves crashing in off the sea 

The smell of a pizza roasting in the oven
And I can see Italy's narrow streets 
Or the hundred aromas of scented candles
A good smell just can't be beat!
