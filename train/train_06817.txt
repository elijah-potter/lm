Every morning Harry takes 15 minutes to buy coffee and a bagel and twice that long to read the paper and eat before going in to work. How long does Harry's morning routine take?
First find the time Harry spends eating: 15 minutes * 2 = <<15*2=30>>30 minutes
Then add the time he spends buying breakfast to find the total time: 30 minutes + 15 minutes = <<30+15=45>>45 minutes
