The argument was sharpest when the door creaked open, revealing a stranger in a worn leather jacket. *"You think you can just show up here and start judging my life choices?"* she snapped, arms crossed.

The stranger tilted their head. *"I’m not here to judge. I’m here to ask—how did you get here?"* The words hung in the tense silence before the argument flared again. *"I got here because you couldn’t handle the truth, and that’s on you."* The stranger’s gaze never wavered. *"What truth?"* they asked, as if the answer were obvious.

The room fell silent. Outside, rain pattered against the windows.
