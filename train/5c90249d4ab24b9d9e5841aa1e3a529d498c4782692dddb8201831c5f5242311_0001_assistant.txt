Hello! My name’s Jeremy, and I’m twenty-two years old. I’ve been playing video games ever since I can remember. I started with Crash and Spyro on the PlayStation. I’ve always enjoyed thinking about games from a critical lens. When I first started playing, I enjoyed Crash more than Spyro, but I also knew they were both great games. I always wondered “Why is that? What would make me like one more than the other? What is it about Spyro that gives it that edge?” 

It wasn’t until many years later, when I stumbled across a video essay comparing them, that I realized that it was the level of openness. Spyro featured open worlds to explore, meet colorful characters, and collect collectibles, whereas Crash had narrow levels and tight, punishing, yet rewarding platforming. Ever since then, I’ve always had an interest in sharing my thoughts on video games in hopes that it might help others understand their thoughts more.

Two of my favorite games are The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild and Outer Wilds. Both games offer a sense of freedom and open-ended puzzle-solving that I’ve rarely found anywhere else. Both games are totally different, yet so similar in the sense that from the beginning of the games, the ending is accessible if you have the skills or knowledge to accomplish it. I’m excited to start this blog and to have a space where I can share more and more of my thoughts on video games! (:
