Introducing the coveted caramel macchiato. If you've ever visited a Starbucks or even your local coffee shop, you've seen this popular coffee drink on the menu. What most partakers don't realize is that the sweetness in this drink comes from the vanilla syrup mixed with the espresso base, rather than actual caramel. Either way, follow the steps below and you'll be able to treat yourself to this delicious beverage any time, day or night, and most importantly, without hurting your pockets.

Appliances: For this recipe, we'll be using a standard Keurig coffee maker and a hand-held electric milk frother. You can also use a French press if you have one at your disposal.

Ingredients:
Espresso k-cup
Milk (whole, 2%, nondairy, etc. It's up to you)
Vanilla coffee flavoring syrup (We like Torani's)
Caramel sauce 

1. Prepare your espresso: Put the K-cup in your Keurig coffeemaker and chose the smallest setting. Since the standard Keurig is not built to reach the kind of pressure we need for authentic espresso, we'll be using a stronger version of coffee instead.
2. Flavoring: Add 1 or 2 pumps (more if you're a little bit extra) to the espresso.
3. Froth: Using your electric frother, frother 1/2 to 1 cup of milk and pour this into your flavored espresso.
4. Drizzle: Last, and most importantly, top your frothed milk with an ample drizzle of caramel syrup so that it looks just like the Starbucks version.
5. Enjoy: Sit back and relax while you enjoy the fruits of your labor.
