## Product Requirements Document: "Synthesis" - Contextual Knowledge Management

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently struggle to connect disparate information from various sources—documents, notes, meeting transcripts, research papers—resulting in fragmented understanding, duplicated effort, and a diminished capacity for innovative problem-solving.

**Vision:** To empower knowledge workers to effortlessly synthesize information from diverse sources into coherent, actionable insights, fostering deeper understanding and accelerating innovation.

---

**User Personas:**

1.  **Dr. Eleanor Vance – Research Scientist:**
    *   **Goals:** To quickly synthesize findings from numerous research papers, grant proposals, and lab notes to identify research gaps and inform experimental design. To generate concise summaries for presentations and publications.
    *   **Pain Points:** Overwhelmed by the volume of information; difficulty tracking relationships between concepts; time-consuming manual summarization; risk of overlooking critical details.
2.  **Marcus Bellweather – Strategic Consultant:**
    *   **Goals:** To rapidly analyze client data, market research reports, and internal strategy documents to formulate data-driven recommendations. To efficiently share synthesized findings with colleagues and clients.
    *   **Pain Points:** Difficulty integrating information from different formats (PDFs, spreadsheets, presentations); challenges in identifying key themes and patterns; need to present complex information clearly and persuasively.
3.  **Aisha Sharma – Product Manager:**
    *   **Goals:** To consolidate user feedback from interviews, surveys, and analytics data into actionable product improvements. To track the evolution of product requirements and align stakeholders.
    *   **Pain Points:** Scattered feedback across multiple platforms; difficulty prioritizing requests and identifying dependencies; lack of a centralized repository for product knowledge.

---

**Requirements:**

1.  **Automated Content Ingestion:** The application shall allow users to import content from various sources, including cloud storage services (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive), web URLs, and local file systems (PDF, DOCX, TXT).
    *   *Acceptance Criteria:* User can successfully import a document from each supported source. Imported content is accurately parsed and indexed.
    *   *Success Metric:* 95% of attempted imports are successful. Average import time for a 50-page document is under 60 seconds.
2.  **Contextual Relationship Mapping:** The application shall automatically identify and visually represent relationships between concepts within imported content using a graph-based visualization.
    *   *Acceptance Criteria:* The application identifies and highlights key entities and relationships within a sample document. The graph visualization is clear and navigable.
    *   *Success Metric:* User satisfaction rating (measured via in-app survey) of at least 4.0 out of 5.0 regarding the clarity and usefulness of the relationship mapping.
3.  **AI-Powered Summarization & Keyphrase Extraction:** The application shall generate concise summaries and extract keyphrases from imported content, allowing users to quickly grasp the core ideas.
    *   *Acceptance Criteria:* Summaries accurately reflect the main points of the original content. Extracted keyphrases are relevant and representative.
    *   *Success Metric:* Manual review of 100 summaries reveals less than 5% contain significant inaccuracies.
4.  **Collaborative Workspace & Annotation:** The application shall allow multiple users to collaboratively annotate and discuss content within a shared workspace.
    *   *Acceptance Criteria:* Users can create and share workspaces. Users can add and edit annotations visible to all workspace members.
    *   *Success Metric:* Average number of users per workspace is greater than 2.
5.  **Customizable Knowledge Graph Views:** The application shall allow users to customize the display of the knowledge graph, filtering by content type, date, or user.
    *   *Acceptance Criteria:* Users can create and save custom graph views. Graph views accurately reflect user-defined filters.
    *   *Success Metric:* 75% of active users create and utilize at least one custom graph view.

---

**Technical Considerations:**

*   **Scalability:** The application must be designed to handle a large volume of content and users.
*   **Security:** User data and content must be protected with robust encryption and access controls.
*   **API Integrations:** Future development should prioritize integrations with common productivity tools (Slack, Microsoft Teams, Notion).
*   **Platform Compatibility:** Initial release will focus on iOS and Android. Web accessibility is a secondary priority.
*   **Content Indexing:** Efficient indexing and search capabilities are crucial for performance.

**Risks:**

*   **Over-reliance on automated summarization:** Users may become overly dependent on automated summaries, potentially missing critical nuances.
*   **Content Accuracy:** The accuracy of automated relationship mapping and summarization is dependent on the quality of the underlying algorithms.
*   **User Adoption:** The application’s complexity may hinder adoption if not carefully addressed through intuitive design and onboarding.

**Non-Goals:**

*   Real-time transcription of audio or video content.
*   Direct content creation within the application.
*   Support for languages other than English in the initial release.
*   Integration with social media platforms.



---

**Launch Checklist (First Six Actions):**

1.  Establish a dedicated customer support channel (email and in-app feedback form).
2.  Deploy a beta program with a small group of target users for initial feedback.
3.  Implement comprehensive analytics tracking to monitor key usage metrics.
4.  Create a concise onboarding tutorial to guide new users.
5.  Prepare a press release and social media content announcing the application's launch.
6.  Define and implement a process for addressing critical bug reports and user feedback.
