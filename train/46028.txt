Here is a shape for the D sharp / E flat minor guitar chord. This chord diagram uses one of the more common bar chord voicings. The lowest root note of this chord is the D# / Eb on the 6th fret of the 5th string. When a note can be called by more than one name, in this case D sharp or E flat, that note is said to be enharmonic. This can be a difficult bar chords to play.
