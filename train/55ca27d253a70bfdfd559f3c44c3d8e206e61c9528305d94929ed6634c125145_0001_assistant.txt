1. Subaru Ascent. This family-focused car has three rows and the ability to accommodate up to five car seats - perfect for a growing family or days when you're acting as chauffeur for the kiddos' friends. The dash display comes with a seatbelt reminder for the front and back seats, as well as a reminder to check the back seats before exiting the vehicle. The 8-speed manual mode with steering wheel paddle shifters adds a sporty, fun feel to the family car. The fuel economy is hard to beat, with each tank of gas getting you over 500 miles. The Premium and Limited models also come with the option to trade the middle bench seat for two captain's chairs, making access to the third row even easier.
2. Subaru Crosstrek Hybrid. This car is a great choice for you and mother earth, as it is the most fuel-efficient Subaru SUV ever made. Its compact size keeps it sporty and easy to drive, but the roomy backseat can still accommodate three car seats. With Starlink Connected Services, the car's heating and cooling system can be engaged without turning the car on, which means you can blast the A/C while soccer practice is still wrapping up.
3. Subaru Forester. A great choice for outdoorsy families, the Forester is equipped with LATCH seats that allow child seats to be attached to each outboard side position in the backseat. 8.7 inches of ground clearance mean this vehicle is fit for off-road adventures, but still low enough to make it easy for your little ones to climb in. An anti-fatigue system is available, using facial recognition technology to detect driver fatigue and alert the driver and passengers.
