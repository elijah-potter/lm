The **legal gray zones** of **cybersecurity liability** under the **Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA)** and state-specific data privacy laws (e.g., **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)**) define unclear boundaries for:
- **Unauthorized access** (e.g., hacking vs. ethical hacking)
- **Data misuse** (e.g., accidental leaks vs. intentional breaches)
- **User consent** (e.g., cookies vs. "opt-out" ambiguities)

**Example:** A third-party vendor accessing customer data for "business purposes" may fall under **state privacy laws** (e.g., CCPA’s "business purpose" exemption) but not under **federal CFAA** if the data was shared with a non-U.S. entity. Legal enforcement varies by jurisdiction, creating loopholes for disputes.
