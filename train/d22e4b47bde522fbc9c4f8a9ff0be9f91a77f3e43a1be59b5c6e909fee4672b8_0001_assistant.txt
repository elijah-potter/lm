A mind at peace is what I am.
In comes a thought, a flooded dam.
And now my peace is overrun.
With clouded judgment, it has begun.

With a heavy sigh and a heavy fist,
I clench my teeth and hold my wrist.
Savor this last moment of control.
My rational side grows dark and cold.

I swing at ghosts; they were there.
Release this curse, break a chair.
In my vision, in this plight,
I knew not wrong from right.

What I had, I threw it all.
In a moment I could not recall.
An evil spirit had taken over me.
The damage is done, and now I can see.
