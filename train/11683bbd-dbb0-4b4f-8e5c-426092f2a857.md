**Central Park, New York**
Residents and visitors engage in activities like jogging, picnicking, and dog-walking. The park features frequent events such as concerts, art exhibitions, and outdoor films. Cyclists navigate its paths, while tourists explore landmarks like Bethesda Fountain and Bow Bridge. Seasonal activities include ice skating in winter and seasonal festivals in summer. Families gather for playground visits, and local artists set up stalls. The park hosts yoga sessions, book readings, and community clean-up drives. Visitors often stop at cafés and bookshops, and its green spaces are popular for meditation and relaxation.
