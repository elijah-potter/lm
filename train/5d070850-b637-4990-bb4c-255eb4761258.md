**"The Last Light"**

The wind howled through the pines, carrying the scent of damp earth and old smoke. Elias clutched his lantern tighter, the oil dimming like a dying star. Below, the river gurgled against the boulders, its current a whisper in the storm.

He had come here to forget—though what he remembered was worse than the silence of this forest.

His fingers traced the scar above his left eyebrow, where the surgeon’s knife had carved his name into flesh. *Elias Veyne*. A name no one called him by now. The world had moved on, and so had he.

A branch snapped behind him. The lantern flickered. Elias froze.

Then—footsteps. Too slow, too deliberate. Not a thief. Not a hunter.

A figure stepped into the light, clad in black as old as the bones beneath the riverbank. The lantern’s glow caught the hollows of their face, the way their breath fogged in the cold.

*"You shouldn’t be here,"* the voice rumbled.

Elias exhaled. *"You shouldn’t be here either."*

The stranger extended a hand—not a demand, not a threat. Just… waiting.

The lantern died.
