In a dimly lit waiting area of an old, weathered hospital, a man sits alone on a worn-out wooden bench. His back is to the wall, fingers tracing idle circles on the cracked surface. A half-empty coffee cup sits beside him, steam lingering in the cold air. A single fluorescent bulb above casts flickering light over the room, casting long shadows. A clock on the wall ticks slowly, showing 15 minutes. A nurse’s voice echoes down a distant corridor, *"Room 307, please follow me."* He exhales, his shoulders relaxing slightly, but the momentary ease is fleeting as he remembers the reason for his wait.
