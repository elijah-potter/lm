A dim light flickered in the corner of the room, casting long shadows across the worn wooden floor. The air smelled faintly of old books and damp wool, remnants of a life lived here. **Lena** sat cross-legged on the floor, fingers tracing the spine of an open tome, her gaze fixed on the words—though she read nothing.

Across the room, **Jace** leaned against the bookshelf, arms crossed, his expression unreadable. He had been here since dawn, his presence an unspoken presence. The silence between them was thick, but it wasn’t empty. It hummed with something deeper than words—*understood*.

She lifted the book, turning the page. A single line caught her eye: *"The weight of silence is not nothing."* Jace exhaled slowly, his breath curling in the cold air. His eyes flickered toward her, but he said nothing. She knew what he thought. He knew what she felt.

The moment stretched, fragile as glass, before breaking—only to refract into something else. The book lay closed. The room held its breath.
