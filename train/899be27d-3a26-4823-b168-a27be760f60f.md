A dimly lit hallway stretched endlessly before her, the air thick with the scent of damp stone and old wood. The soft *tap-tap-tap* of footsteps echoed against the worn floorboards—too deliberate, too measured. She turned, fingers tightening around the hilt of her knife, but the door creaked open before she could reach it.

A figure stood silhouetted against the flickering glow of a single candle, its outline sharp against the dark. The breath hitched. *Not again.* The stranger—tall, gaunt, clad in a coat stitched from what looked like the same fabric as her own—held up a single playing card, the Queen of Spades. The card flickered, as if alive, before settling into her palm. The moment the door closed behind them, the candle’s flame died.

*"You always knew the knock would come,"* the stranger whispered, voice like rusted hinges. *"But you never expected the answer."*
