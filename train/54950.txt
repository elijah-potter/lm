Pre-kindergartners develop skills to succeed in future academics. The learning concepts of this program include kindergarten preparedness, social awareness, practical life skills and mastery of gross motor skills. The daily schedule consists of opportunities for independent discovery, cooperative learning and outdoors playtime.
Our toddlers reach their milestones successfully - from first steps to potty training; our teachers provide assistance on daily bases and make exploring fun for young children.Our active care providers plan simple step by step activities considering the needs of young children.
In order to provide toddlers with the consistency and routine they crave, teachers plan days filled with a balance of reading, music & rhythm, art awareness and plenty of outdoor activity such as; climbing, running and jumping.
Our bright and colorful classrooms, and large playground work as a stimulus for a learning environment where preschoolers participate in age-appropriate activities related to a weekly theme. Learning materials for computer education, math & counting, science, world awareness, pretend play, music and creative art provide both group and individual learning opportunities. Teachers create theme related bulletin boards and bring out the materials to engage children at each stage of their development.
After School Care
Our After School program offers young children, ages 5 to 12 years, opportunity to participate in a comfortable, safe and nurturing environment after-school hours. Our after school program also offers care during elementary school holidays and early dismissal days. This program may also be extended during summer depending on the space availability.
