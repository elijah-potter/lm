The wind howled through rusted iron gates, carrying whispers of the past. Shadows stretched long across cracked pavement where a once-grand archway now stood half-buried, its faded stone weathered by time. A single lantern, its flame dimmed by soot, cast a flickering glow on the cobblestones below. A figure in an old coat—its sleeves torn—stumbled forward, clutching a leather-bound journal, its pages yellowed with age. The air smelled of damp earth and something metallic, like rust and forgotten memories. Beyond the gates, the distant rumble of machinery echoed, a ghostly echo of industry that had long since faded. The figure paused, turning slowly, as if listening to the ghosts of the place.
