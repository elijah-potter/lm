The National Football League (NFL) consists of 32 individual professional teams. The teams are split into two conferences consisting of 16 teams, the American Football Conference (AFC) and the National Football Conference (NFC), which are then split into four separate divisions with four teams each. Each city and their respective team, in addition to their conference and division, are as follows:

- AFC East:
  - Buffalo Bills
  - Miami Dolphins
  - New England Patriots
  - New York Jets

- AFC North:
  - Baltimore Ravens
  - Cincinnati Bengals
  - Cleveland Browns
  - Pittsburgh Steelers

- AFC South:
  - Houston Texans
  - Indianapolis Colts
  - Jacksonville Jaguars
  - Tennessee Titans

- AFC West:
  - Denver Broncos
  - Kansas City Chiefs
  - Las Vegas Raiders
  - Los Angeles Chargers
