Here are some team bonding exercises for the basketball team:

1. Team laser-tag: Coordinate an outing to a laser tag facility with the whole team. Split the team into two or three smaller teams, depending on the total number of attendees. These teams will compete in laser tag and see who comes out on top. This is a great way to bring out the competitive edge in the players and stimulate camaraderie.
2. Cup Pong Tournament: This is a bracket-style tournament where the teams will be randomly organized into pairs. Each pair will face off in a cup pong challenge where the quickest player to sink 3 ping pong balls into 3 cups wins the match. The winners face each other as they move down the bracket, and then a winner is determined. This is an exciting idea that parallels the tournament-style nature of college basketball.
3. Escape Room: This can be booked at a local escape room in the team's area. Escape rooms are a great bonding activity for sports teams because they require collaboration and critical thinking. This will encourage team members to listen to each other and work together to solve the puzzles.
4. Scavenger Hunt: A scavenger hunt is a fun and lively way for a team to bond. Split the team up into smaller groups and provide a preliminary clue to guide the teams in the direction of the next clue. These clues should be spread all across the college campus so that the teams are encouraged to work quickly and effectively. Reward the team that reaches the end of the hunt first.
5. Community Service: An important pillar of undergraduate studies is giving back to the community. Find a local cause that is accepting volunteers to assist in their mission. By signing the team up for service work, you are instilling this community service initiative in the team and promoting the value of giving back. It is also a great chance for the city to get to know the new team and directly benefit from the teammates' hard work.
