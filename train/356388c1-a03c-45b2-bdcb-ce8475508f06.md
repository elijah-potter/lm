The hesitation lingered like a half-remembered dream—each breath shallow, the fingers twitching toward the knife’s hilt, but the mind a battlefield of *what ifs*: the weight of the blood, the ghost of the woman’s last words, the echo of the door slamming shut behind him. The air smelled of ozone and something metallic, the pulse in his throat a drumbeat against the silence of the abandoned warehouse’s empty corridors. The decision wasn’t made; it was *unlocked*, the choice between survival and the hollow thrill of another life erased, replaced by the cold certainty that the world would never know his name.
