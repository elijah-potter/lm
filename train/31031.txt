A bundle of 14 sets of worksheets, games, activities and flash cards (with audio) for teaching French Food and Drinks vocabulary.
See what these resources are like for FREE by visiting https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/2556534
and downloading our similar resources on the Numbers 0-10 in French.
Each set of resources includes:
- a set of online flashcards and activities (with audio)
- a PowerPoint slide with the numbers and their labels
- a set of flashcards
- two matching activities
- a word search
- an 'unscramble the letters' worksheet
- a label the images worksheet
The 14 sets are on:
Carbohydrates, Dairy and Protein
Cooking and food equipment 1
Cooking and food equipment 2
Cooking and food equipment 3
Cooking and food equipment 4
Drinks and Meals
Other foods and Condiments
Types of meat
Vegetables 2 and Fruit 3
