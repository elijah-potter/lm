Birthdays always bring excitement (and a little chaos!) to our house. I am always trying to come up with creative themes to make my kids' parties extra fun, and let's face it, Instagram-worthy. If you're planning a party for a 3-year-old, here are some theme ideas to consider:

-Sports. If your child is interested in any sport, you can make a party out of it! Baseball? That's a home run theme! Gymnastics? Gold medals for everyone! How about football? There's no way you can fumble that theme.
-Favorite TV shows or movies. Your child probably has a favorite or two, and bringing their most loved characters from the screen to life can be extra fun for them.
-Three's (not) a crowd. The more, the merrier at this party! Make this theme about the big number three and all of the loved ones who want to celebrate your special kid. 
-Planes, trains, and automobiles. Most kids at this age love transportation. No, really. They will enjoy looking at and playing with all of the decorations and treats.

One of these themes is sure to delight your 3-year-old. I definitely can get preoccupied with creating the perfect party, but remember, all that matters is that your child has fun and feels loved. Don't sweat the small stuff and enjoy the day!
