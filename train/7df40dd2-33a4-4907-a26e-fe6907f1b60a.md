**Peer-reviewed manuscript submission details:**
- Title: *"The Hidden Biases in Open-Access Research Funding: A Case Study on Academic Bias and Transparency"*
- Authors: Dr. Elena Vasquez (Primary Investigator), Prof. Marcus Chen (Co-Author), Dr. Sophia Rodriguez (Reviewer #1), Dr. Daniel Kim (Reviewer #2)
- Journal: *Frontiers in Science Policy*
- Key Findings:
  - **Bias in Peer Review:** Reviewers subtly influenced decisions based on prior collaborations (50% of responses adjusted for perceived rapport).
  - **Funding Transparency:** 38% of studies cited non-disclosed grant sources, altering perceived credibility.
  - **Personal Impact:** "Reviewers often dismissed dissenting views when authors had competing interests with prior publications" (p. 47).
- **Critique:** "The study’s self-reflection on reviewer psychology is compelling, but lacks longitudinal data on long-term bias propagation."

**Appendix:** Survey responses from 120 reviewers (94% responded anonymously; 6% named themselves).
