**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, Ill. – August 2, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair, a beloved tradition celebrating agriculture, community, and family fun, will return August 16-25, 2024, at the Sangamon County Fairgrounds. This year’s fair promises a diverse array of attractions, including livestock exhibits showcasing the region’s finest animals, thrilling carnival rides and games, a bustling marketplace featuring local artisans and vendors, and a wide selection of food options ranging from classic fair fare to unique culinary creations. Visitors of all ages will find something to enjoy at this year’s festivities.

The Sangamon County Fair will run for ten days, beginning Friday, August 16, and concluding Sunday, August 25. Gates will open at 9:00 a.m. daily, with specific event schedules available on the fair’s website, www.sangamoncountyfair.org. The fairgrounds offer ample free parking for attendees. A special “Kids’ Day” will be held on Saturday, August 17, with discounted admission and family-friendly activities.

Admission to the Sangamon County Fair is $12 for adults, $8 for children ages 6-12, and free for children five and under. Carnival ride tickets and wristbands will be available for purchase both online and at the fairgrounds. Advance ticket purchases are encouraged to avoid potential lines at the gate. A detailed breakdown of ticket options and pricing can be found on the fair’s website.

**Headline Events:**

*   Demolition Derby – Saturday, August 17
*   Livestock Auction – Sunday, August 18
*   Tractor Pull – Friday, August 23
*   Blue Ribbon Baking Competition – Monday, August 19
*   Professional Rodeo – Thursday, August 22
*   Country Music Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters – Sunday, August 25
