The layers of the Earth are as follows: 
1) crust- the rocky outer layer of the Earth's surface
2) lithosphere- the layer of solid, brittle rock that makes up the Earth's surface;
3)mantle- the middle layer of the Earth,
4) asthenosphere- layer below the lithosphere, made of a portion of the upper mantle
5) inner core- densest layer of a celestial body; solid layer 
6) outer core-  layer of liquid metal
