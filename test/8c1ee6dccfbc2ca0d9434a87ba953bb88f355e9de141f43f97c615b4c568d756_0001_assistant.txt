Nelson Mandela was a South African politician and activist who fought against apartheid. He became the first black President of South Africa in 1994, following a fully democratic election. Mandela worked to eliminate racism, poverty, and inequality while promoting racial understanding in the country. He served as the President of the African National Congress and played a key role in adopting a new constitution that prohibited discrimination. Mandela received numerous honors, including the Nobel Peace Prize. He passed away in 2013 at the age of 95, leaving behind a legacy of perseverance and dedication to equality.
