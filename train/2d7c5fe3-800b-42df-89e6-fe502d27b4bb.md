## Seriously Questioning My Choice of WooCommerce Product Variations…

Hello everyone,

I'm hoping someone might have encountered a similar issue with WooCommerce product variations and can offer some guidance. I'm genuinely at a loss and starting to feel like I'm going in circles.

I’m running WooCommerce 7.1.1 on WordPress 6.3.1, using the Storefront theme. I’m trying to set up a product with several variations – specifically, a custom-printed mug where the customer can choose the mug color, the font style, and then upload an image to be printed. My intention is for each combination of these choices to display a unique image preview on the product page.

Here’s what I *expected* to happen: When a customer selects a specific combination of color, font, and uploads an image, the product image dynamically updates to reflect that selection. This would give them a clear visual representation of what their final product will look like before they add it to their cart.

What’s *actually* happening is far less desirable. The product image updates *only* on the initial page load. After the customer changes any of the variation attributes (color, font, or uploads a new image), the product image remains unchanged, displaying the default image. This is incredibly confusing for the customer and, frankly, a terrible user experience. I'm seeing a significant drop-off in conversions, and I suspect this is a major contributor.

I've already attempted several troubleshooting steps. First, I ensured that all plugins are up to date. Then, I deactivated all plugins *except* WooCommerce and Storefront to rule out any conflicts. The problem persists, indicating that the issue isn't caused by a third-party plugin. I’ve also cleared my browser cache and cookies multiple times, as well as flushed the WooCommerce cache. I've even tried temporarily switching to the default WordPress theme to see if Storefront was the culprit, but that didn’t resolve the problem either. Finally, I checked the browser's developer console, and I'm not seeing any obvious JavaScript errors, although I am admittedly not a JavaScript expert.

I’ve researched this extensively online, and while I've found numerous forum posts describing similar frustrations, I haven't found a definitive solution. I’m starting to wonder if there's a fundamental limitation with how WooCommerce handles dynamic image updates for variations, or if I'm missing something obvious.

Any insights or suggestions would be greatly appreciated. I'm particularly interested in knowing if anyone has successfully implemented dynamic image updates for variations using a custom solution or a specific plugin that I might have overlooked. Thank you for your time and consideration.
