## Project Chimera: A Cooperative Research Game

**A game for 2-4 players. Ages 14+. Playing time: 90-120 minutes.**

**Welcome, Researchers.** The world faces a crisis unlike any seen before. Project Chimera details a rapidly evolving, synthetic pathogen, “Nyx,” capable of adapting to countermeasures with alarming speed. As members of the Global Response Initiative (GRI), you are the last line of defense. Your combined expertise in virology, epidemiology, genetics, and logistics is humanity’s only hope. Failure is not an option.

### Components

*   **Game Board:** A world map depicting key regions impacted by the Nyx outbreak.
*   **Researcher Cards (8):** Each card represents a unique researcher with specialized skills and starting resources.
*   **Nyx Evolution Cards (40):** Detail the pathogen’s mutations and escalating threats.
*   **Research Cards (60):** Provide potential solutions and countermeasures, categorized by research area (Virology, Epidemiology, Genetics, Logistics).
*   **Resource Tokens:** Represent Funding (blue), Personnel (yellow), and Data (green).
*   **Outbreak Markers:** Indicate the severity of the Nyx infection in each region.
*   **Containment Markers:** Represent successful containment efforts.
*   **Event Dice (2):** Six-sided dice used to determine random events and mutation triggers.
*   **Threat Tracker:** A track indicating the overall severity of the crisis.
*   **First Player Token.**

### Setup

1.  Place the Game Board in the center of the playing area.
2.  Shuffle the Nyx Evolution Cards and place them face down to form the Evolution Deck. Reveal the top three cards and place them face up next to the deck. These represent the current Nyx mutations.
3.  Shuffle the Research Cards and place them face down to form the Research Deck. Reveal the top five cards and place them face up next to the deck.
4.  Place the Threat Tracker on the starting position (level 1).
5.  Place Outbreak Markers in the designated starting regions as indicated on the Game Board.
6.  Each player chooses a Researcher Card and takes the corresponding starting resources as indicated on the card.
7.  Determine the first player randomly and give them the First Player Token.

### Turn Structure

Each turn consists of the following phases, performed in order:

1.  **Event Phase:** The first player rolls both Event Dice. The results trigger events as described on the Event Chart (printed on the Game Board). These events often introduce new Outbreak Markers, force resource expenditure, or advance the Threat Tracker.
2.  **Researcher Action Phase:** Starting with the first player and proceeding clockwise, each player performs two actions. Available actions include:
    *   **Gather Resources:** Gain one Resource Token of your choice.
    *   **Research:** Draw two Research Cards, choose one to keep, and discard the other.
    *   **Containment:** Spend Personnel and Data to place a Containment Marker in a region with an Outbreak Marker. The number of resources required depends on the region's severity.
    *   **Analyze Mutation:** Spend Data to discard one face-up Nyx Evolution Card and draw a new one from the Evolution Deck. This action is crucial for understanding Nyx's evolving traits.
    *   **Collaborate:** Transfer one Resource Token to another player.
    *   **Special Action:** Utilize the unique ability described on your Researcher Card.
3.  **Nyx Evolution Phase:** Draw the top card from the Evolution Deck and place it face up next to the deck. If the number of face-up Nyx Evolution Cards exceeds three, discard the oldest card. The drawn card’s effects are immediately applied, potentially increasing Outbreak Markers, advancing the Threat Tracker, or introducing new detrimental effects.
4.  **Pass the Token:** The First Player Token is passed to the player on the left.

### Win/Loss Conditions

**Winning the Game:**

You win the game if you successfully develop a permanent cure for Nyx. This is achieved by completing a series of four “Breakthrough” Research Cards, each representing a critical step towards a cure. These Breakthrough Cards are shuffled into the bottom third of the Research Deck.

**Losing the Game:**

You lose the game if any of the following conditions are met:

*   **Threat Level Maxed:** The Threat Tracker reaches its maximum level.
*   **Region Overrun:** A predetermined number of regions (dependent on player count - see Difficulty Scaling) are overrun with Outbreak Markers.
*   **Researcher Incapacitation:** All Researchers are simultaneously unable to perform actions due to resource depletion or other detrimental effects.

### Difficulty Scaling

The game’s difficulty adjusts based on the number of players:

*   **2 Players:** The game is slightly easier. The number of regions that can be overrun before losing is reduced.
*   **3 Players:** The standard difficulty.
*   **4 Players:** The game is more challenging. The number of regions that can be overrun before losing is increased, and the starting Threat Level is increased to level 1.

### Advanced Variants

**1. Cascading Mutations:** At the start of the game, select two additional Nyx Evolution Cards from the deck and shuffle them into the Evolution Deck. These represent particularly aggressive and unpredictable mutations.

**2. Resource Scarcity:** Reduce the initial number of Resource Tokens each player receives. This forces players to prioritize actions and make difficult choices regarding resource allocation.

**3. The Black Market:** Introduce a new deck of “Black Market” cards. Players can choose to draw from this deck instead of the Research Deck, but these cards offer potentially powerful solutions with significant drawbacks, such as increased Threat Level or the introduction of new detrimental effects. This introduces a risk/reward mechanic.

**4. Specialization Conflict:** Each Researcher Card now includes a “Conflict” section. When two or more players attempt to perform actions requiring the same research area (Virology, Epidemiology, Genetics, Logistics) in the same turn, they must engage in a “Conflict.” This involves a die roll modified by their Researcher’s skills. The loser of the conflict suffers a penalty (e.g., resource loss, delayed action). This forces players to consider the potential for conflict when choosing their actions.

### Designer’s Note

Project Chimera was designed to be more than just a game. It aims to subtly introduce players to the complexities of global crisis response. The mechanics are intended to simulate the challenges of resource allocation under uncertainty, the importance of collaboration in the face of overwhelming odds, and the potential for unintended consequences when pursuing short-term solutions. The escalating threat of Nyx reflects the unpredictable nature of pandemics and the need for adaptable and proactive strategies. The game encourages critical thinking, strategic planning, and a deeper appreciation for the vital work of scientists and researchers dedicated to safeguarding humanity.
