Here are seven plot ideas for your supernatural-themed book:

1. A wizard stumbles upon a candle that gives life.
2. A ghost is trying to reach the afterlife.
3. An alien race is puzzled by giraffes on Earth.
4. A young boy discovers he can read minds.
5. Evil spirits have possessed a farmer's animals.
6. A family of fairies is discovered in Iceland.
7. Bigfoot decides to introduce himself to the world.
