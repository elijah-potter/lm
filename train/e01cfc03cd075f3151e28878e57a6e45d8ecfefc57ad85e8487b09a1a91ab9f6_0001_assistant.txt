Larisa Semyonovna Latynina, a gymnast from the USSR, has won the most Olympic medals of any female athlete with a total of 18.  She won 6 medals at the 1956 Melbourne Olympics, 6 medals at the 1960 Rome Olympics, and 6 medals at the 1964 Tokyo Olympics. Latynina retired from competition in 1966 and later went on to coach gold medal-winning gymnastics teams.
