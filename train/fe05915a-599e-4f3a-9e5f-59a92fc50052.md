The dim glow of a flickering lantern cast long shadows across the cracked floorboards of an abandoned library. The scent of aged paper and damp earth clings to his coat, a relic of a world that once understood words more than names.

He stops at a shelf where the spines are worn smooth, as if kissed by countless hands. His breath catches. The book in his hand, *The Hollow Echo*, is the one. Its cover is leather-bound, though its edges are brittle. On the inside title page, an inscription glows faintly: *"Where the lost still linger."* His pulse quickens. The key—yes, it’s the key. But what does it open?

A whisper, just audible, slithers from the shadows behind him: *"You’re not the first."* His heart races. The intangible isn’t in the book. It’s in the *hunger* of the space itself. His fingers tremble as he aligns it with the shelf’s ancient gears, the ones that never quite turn. The air hums. The book *opens* on its own.

Inside, pages dissolve into mist. A figure stands at the center: a silhouette, half-flesh, half-silence. **"You seek what was never yours,"** the figure murmurs, voice like wind through dead leaves. **"But you already have it."** Elias’s grip tightens. The lantern flickers. The key in his pocket *clicks*—not against stone, but against something *alive*. The figure smiles. The library dissolves into smoke. The hunt isn’t over. Only the hunt for the hunt itself remains.
