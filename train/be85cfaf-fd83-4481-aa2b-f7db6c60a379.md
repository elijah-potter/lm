The **strict "no open containers"** rule in certain environments (e.g., airplanes, restricted facilities) applies to prevent fire hazards. Open containers of liquids, solids, or gases can ignite from static electricity, sparks, or other ignition sources. Violating this rule risks severe accidents, including explosions or fires, which could cause catastrophic damage or loss of life. Violators are typically penalized with fines, confiscation of items, or legal consequences. The rule is enforced to prioritize safety and compliance with fire safety regulations.
