Linus was a white housecat that lived in a suburb in Southern New Jersey with his owner John and sisters Peppers and Pima. They were all housecats, but Linus has a special curiosity about the outdoors. He liked to explore, he liked to play, and he liked to hunt. Linus would sit at the front door and meow and cry and scratch at it until John would come and let him outside. Whenever he got outside Linus would be out for hours on his own adventure. He would meet other cats and play with them, he would explore the neighborhood, and he would hunt smaller animals like mice and rabbits. One day, however, Linus came across a formidable foe that he wasn’t expecting to face.
