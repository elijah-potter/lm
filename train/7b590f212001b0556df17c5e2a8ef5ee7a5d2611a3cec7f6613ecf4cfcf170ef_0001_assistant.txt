Dear Parents and Guardians,

We hope this letter finds you all in good health and spirits. We are writing to inform you of a change in the field trip that was previously planned for our elementary school students. 

Due to an escaped flamboyance of flamingos, we are no longer visiting the Sanford  Zoo, but instead, we are taking the students on an exciting and educational trip to the space museum at Cape Canaveral. We believe this change in itinerary will provide a unique and valuable experience for our students. However, please be aware that this trip will require an overnight stay and will be more expensive than our previously planned trip to the zoo. 
The trip will take place on June 15th and 16th. We will depart from the school at 8:00 am on June 15th and return at approximately 3:00 pm on June 16th. The cost of the trip will be $250 per student, which covers transportation, accommodation, meals, and admission to the museum. 

We understand that this change may be inconvenient for some families, and we apologize for any inconvenience it may cause. We want to assure you that we have done everything we can to keep the cost of the trip as low as possible, and we believe this opportunity will be worth the investment. 

We are attaching a permission slip to this letter, and we kindly ask that you fill it out and return it to us by May 31st. This will allow us to finalize our plans and ensure that your child can participate in this exciting trip. 

Thank you for your understanding and cooperation. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us. 

Sincerely,
Robert Elert
Superintendent, XYZ Academy
