Here is a response to your request for a social media post using the quote “Life is about making an impact, not making an income.” 

“Life is about making an impact, not making an income.” While I agree income is essential, focusing exclusively on the money you can make will not be as rewarding or fulfilling as focusing more on changing our world for the better. Inspiring people, helping them navigate challenging situations, and positively impacting their lives is one way of making an enormous impact. Influencing or impacting is only achieved by true motives and beliefs. Once you are focused on making an impact, you are more likely to achieve it. Accomplish something that changes your life. You’ll find how satisfying it can be.
