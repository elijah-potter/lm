The following Presidents were considered to be left-handed:

1. James A. Garfield (March 4, 1881 – September 19, 1881)  
2. Herbert Hoover (March 4, 1929 – March 4, 1933)  
3. Harry S. Truman (April 12, 1945 – January 20, 1953)  *
4. Gerald Ford (August 9, 1974 – January 20, 1977)  
5. Ronald Reagan (January 20, 1981 – January 20, 1989)  *
6. George H. W. Bush (January 20, 1989 – January 20, 1993)  
7. Bill Clinton (January 20, 1993 – January 20, 2001)  
8. Barack Obama (January 20, 2009 – January 20, 2017)  

* These presidents wrote with their right hand but performed all other tasks with their left.
