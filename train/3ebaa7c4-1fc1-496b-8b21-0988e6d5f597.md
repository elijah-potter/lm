The moment of clarity dissolved like mist at dawn—
a sudden warmth, then the air growing thin,
a name whispered once, lost in the hum of the city’s pulse.
The ink of intent blurred in the margin of the page,
the rhythm of thought slowing to a sigh,
each word a half-remembered echo.
The light, once sharp as a blade’s edge,
now softens into the glow of a lamp,
its glow flickering, dimming into the silence.
The moment was never fixed—
it was the breath of a moment, then gone.
