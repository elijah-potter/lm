Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a tiny bunny named Pip. Pip had fluffy white fur and big, bright eyes. Every morning, Pip would hop outside to play in the sunlit meadow.

One sunny afternoon, Pip saw a tiny, twinkling star in the sky. It looked like it was dancing. Pip thought, "I wonder if stars are friends?" He followed the star’s twinkle and hopped down to a secret garden where fireflies glowed like tiny lanterns.

A wise old owl named Oliver sat on a branch, watching Pip. "Pip, my dear little bunny," Oliver said, "stars are like magical friends who light up the night sky. They help us dream big and feel brave."

Pip smiled and hugged himself. "Can I see more stars tonight?" he asked. Oliver winked. "Of course! Just follow the fireflies—they’ll lead you to the best spot to watch."

Pip hopped back to his house, excited. That night, he sat under a blanket of twinkling stars, listening to the crickets sing. He felt so happy, warm, and full of wonder.

As the sky turned dark, Pip whispered, "Goodnight, little stars!" And the stars twinkled back, as if they were smiling. Pip drifted off to sleep, dreaming of hopping among the stars forever.

The next morning, Pip woke up and found a tiny, shiny pebble in his garden. It glowed softly in the sunlight. Pip picked it up and realized—it was a little piece of the star’s magic! He put it in his pocket, knowing it would always remind him that even the smallest bunnies can discover the biggest adventures.
