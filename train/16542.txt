Youth Leadership Program teaches youth (boys & girls) ages 11 to 16 leadership skills, that build confidence and increase self-esteem. We believe that if youth are taught leadership skills at this age, they will have the potential of growing into productive and confident individuals, who can deal with adverse situations positively. The leadership program is fun, informative and motivational. We use cooperative games, role plays, discussions, team building activities, and object lessons to train the participants.
