Etherial whispers drift between two unspoken souls,
each word a half-formed ghost in the void they share.
*"Why do you stay?"* murmurs the one who lingers,
*"Because the silence hums like a tune I half-remember."*

A clock ticks backward, its hands frozen in the dark.
*"Do you ever wonder what happens if we never speak?"*
*"Or if we’re already too far apart?"*

The room spins, static filling the air like static on a radio.
*"What if this is just the beginning?"*
*"And the end is just another beginning?"*

The conversation fades into the static of the static,
a loop without resolution, a dance without a partner.
*"We were never meant to finish."*
