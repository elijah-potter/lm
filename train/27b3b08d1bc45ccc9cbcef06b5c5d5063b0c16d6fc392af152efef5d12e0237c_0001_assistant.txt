Once there was a penguin named Penny. She always wanted to learn how to fly; all her other peers would tell her penguins were not meant to fly. So Penny would jump and flap her flippers, hoping to get lifted off the ground. One day she was practicing and came across a goose watching her. She asked the Goose, "Goose, I see you can fly, and I want to fly too. Do you have any advice for me?" The Goose laughed, saying, " Penguins are not meant to fly; they are meant for swimming." After the Goose made this statement, he flew away, showing off his flying skills. This only motivated Penny to reach her dreams of flying. She kept practicing the whole day until her feet were not touching the ground for significant moments at a time. Penny was finally flying!
