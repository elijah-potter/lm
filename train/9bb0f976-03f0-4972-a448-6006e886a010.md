A dim lamp flickered over the crackling fire as Elias leaned forward, his fingers tracing the edge of the old leather-bound book. *"Once upon a time, in a land far beyond these mountains..."*

The words spilled from his lips like ink on parchment. *"There was a kingdom where the rivers whispered secrets to the wind, and the forests held courts of shadowy judges who weighed souls on balance scales of forgotten stone."*

Elias paused, his voice dropping to a murmur. *"One day, a thief named Kael stole not gold, but a single feather—black as midnight—from the cloak of a witch who guarded the old well. The witch, a woman with eyes like polished obsidian, called him before the court of the Hollow King, where the laws were written in dreams."*

Outside, rain tapped against the windows, but Elias was lost in the tale, his breath steady, his eyes closed. The story took shape around him, vivid and real—until the feather, the witch, the Hollow King’s throne, all dissolved into silence.

*"The end,"* he whispered, though none heard it. The fire burned lower, and the book lay unopened beside him, its pages now blank.
