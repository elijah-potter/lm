The quiet morning sun filtered through the trees, casting dappled light upon the ancient oak where I sat. The wind carried whispers of the forest, and for once, the silence was not broken by the usual cacophony of city life. However, my mind wandered to the stories I’d left behind—memories that still haunted me, though time had dulled their edges. However, as I traced the bark with my fingers, something shifted. A crack split the trunk, revealing a hidden hollow where bees hummed lazily, their golden wings catching the light. However, the oak had always been more than wood and roots; it was a vessel of stories, a silent witness to centuries. I closed my eyes, listening.
