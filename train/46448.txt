Nowadays the life of every student has become stressful. The reason mostly being educational pressure, the competition amongst each student has increased subsequently. Some students end up coping up with this stress and some end up scarring their life forever. Not only students but adults too are suffering from this situation. The support system of people differs and might be weak in some. If by childhood the decision-making isn't strong it might bring alterations in the youth along with adult life.
Every person suffering from short-term stress, depression etc. should talk. Talking to others about his/her problems can help drastically. Rather than making rash decisions one should think clearly and seek help. Reading, listening to music can help calm the mind and bring out a better perspective. Appreciating and loving yourself just the way yourself can improve mental state
Talk to people, convey your concerns and think clearly you never know you might end up hurting more people than you imagine. Improvement in emotional state is tough but if done carefully it might impact certain lives and maybe save them.
