**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – October 16, 2023 – The Sangamon County Fair is returning this summer, promising a vibrant celebration of agriculture, community, and family entertainment. This year’s fair will feature expanded livestock exhibits, thrilling carnival rides, live music performances, and a diverse selection of food vendors showcasing local culinary talent. Visitors can anticipate a wide array of activities suitable for all ages, fostering a sense of connection and shared experience.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 16th to August 21st, 2024, at the County Fairgrounds. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM. A dedicated agricultural showcase will run throughout the week, highlighting the region's farming heritage and showcasing the dedication of local producers. The fair aims to provide a memorable experience for both longtime attendees and first-time visitors alike.

Admission to the fair is $15 for adults, $10 for children ages 6-12, and free for children ages 5 and under. Season passes, offering unlimited access throughout the week, are available for $50. Tickets can be purchased online at www.sangamoncountyfair.org or at the gate during the event. Parking is $5 per vehicle. 

**Headline Events:**

*   Livestock Judging Competitions
*   Demolition Derby
*   Professional Rodeo Series
*   Grandstand Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters
*   4-H Showcase & Auction
*   Evening Fireworks Display (August 21st)
