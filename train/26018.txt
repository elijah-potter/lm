Learning about proper lawn care is essential for any homeowner who wants to maintain a tidy and attractive yard. There are several things anyone can do to achieve this goal.
Choose grass seed wisely. Each type of grass seed will have its own requirements when it comes to climate and the best time of year to plant. Proper research will reveal what type of grass grows best in the desired location and the best time of year to plant the seeds. Follow the directions for recommended seed depth for best results.
Selecting the right fertilizer program will ensure the grass grows strong and healthy. Strong chemicals are not always the best choice because they may kill the grass right along with the weeds. A better choice is a weed and feed formula that provides the proper nutrients for the grass to grow while preventing the spread of weeds. The right schedule for fertilizing will depend on climate and location.
Grass seed that has just gone into the ground needs plenty of water for good growth. There is no need to worry about providing too much water during this part of the growth cycle. Avoid short watering sessions in the heat of the day when the sun is shining. Instead, set up a long soaks in the evening or first thing in the morning. This allows the water to seep into the soil before the moisture is lost to evaporation. Purchasing an affordable rain gauge will help you determine the frequency of manual watering needed.
Keeping the lawn mowed is important for healthy grass growth. Invest in a quality mower to avoid doing damage to the lawn. The blade should be changed each year to ensure a clean cut. Mowing a lawn with dull blades will simply flatten the grass down and inhibit further growth.
