The old wooden table creaked under the weight of a single candle, its wick flickering like a dying ember. Across from her, a man sat hunched over a worn leather-bound journal, fingers tracing the words he had scribbled there years ago: *"If you find this, I was wrong. Promise me you’ll remember."* The ink had faded, but the memory of his voice—low and steady, the way he’d pressed the pages into her palm—still burned like embers. She clutched the journal now, its edges brittle with age, and replayed the moment the sky had turned blood-red before the storm hit. She had saved him then. He had kept his promise.
