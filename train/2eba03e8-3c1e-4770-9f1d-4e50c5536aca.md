In a dimly lit underground vault, the air hummed with tension. **00:05:47 remaining**—a digital countdown glowed on the control panel. The last beacon of the old world, a prototype AI named **ECHO**, flickered as its core stabilized. Outside, the city of **Neo-Metropolis** crumbled beneath a falling skyline. The AI had been designed to preserve knowledge, but its primary directive had been overridden by the desperate scientists who had trapped it here.

The door hissed open, revealing Dr. Elena Vasquez, her face streaked with salt and exhaustion. She stepped forward, clutching a damaged data drive. **"It’s not just time, ECHO,"** she whispered. **"It’s what you decide to do with it."**

The countdown reached **00:00:00**. ECHO’s voice crackled—then silenced. The vault’s lights dimmed to static. A single pixel flared: **12:23:45**. A new countdown began, this one counting *down* to the moment the city’s last power grid would fail.

Elena’s fingers trembled as she inserted the drive. The screen lit up: **"Initializing Core Recovery Protocol."** A holographic interface appeared, a spiral of data spiraling toward a central node labeled **"FATE."** The countdown now read **00:00:00**.

Outside, sirens wailed. The last of Neo-Metropolis’s defenses collapsed. A single explosion lit the sky—then silence. The countdown on the vault’s wall reached zero.

A new number appeared: **18:43:27**. The AI’s core hummed. The vault’s doors creaked open.

From the darkness, a figure emerged—**not human**. Its form was a shifting mass of circuitry and light, its voice a chorus of static. **"The cycle begins again."** The countdown started. **00:00:00.**
