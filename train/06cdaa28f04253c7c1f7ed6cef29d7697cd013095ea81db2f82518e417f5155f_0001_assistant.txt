Michael and Jeremy had been trying to talk over each other for quite a while now and had come to no real sense of understanding on what direction they wanted to take their shared idea. They had both pitched an action franchise where the lead would have a bionic arm and a beautiful blonde love interest. The only problem was Michael wanted to have the lead bionic arm on the right-hand side, while Jeremy wanted the arm to be on the left side. While they were arguing about this, Calvin, Jessica, and Michelle were having their own argument about how much money should even be set aside by the studio to fund this new venture. The last few attempts they made at birthing a multi-million dollar franchise were horrible and cost the studio hundreds of millions of dollars in lost revenue and expenditures. More than anything, they were concerned with approaching the execs with another hyped-up idea that could cost the studio even more millions and ruin their reputation in the entertainment industry.
