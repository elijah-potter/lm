Sudden flicker of ambient light from dim amber to piercing neon blue, casting sharp shadows across the alley’s cracked pavement. Reflections in puddles ripple violently before stabilizing into jagged, distorted mirrors of the passing cars. A lone figure’s silhouette on the wall, once blurred, now stands sharply defined—faces of passersby, street signs—all abruptly frozen mid-motion, then restored. The air hums with static; the scent of ozone thickens. A streetlight’s bulb pops, then glows with a cold, unnatural glow before dying again in a soft, rhythmic pulse.
