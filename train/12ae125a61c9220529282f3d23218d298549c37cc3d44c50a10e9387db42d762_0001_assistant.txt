- Admiral Aaram Hernandez: a decorated and hardened military veteran and esteemed commander of the United Earth Military (UEM) fleet. He is often torn between the government's directions and his own experience as a military leader. 

- President Deidamia Mercer: the current president of United Earth. A skilled politician who isn't convinced the colonies should be granted independence and must weigh her concerns against losing the lifesaving resources mined in the colonies. 

- Captain Sheila "Fuzz" Malone: former United Earth police officer. A gifted pilot and captain for the Independent Republic of Mars (IRM) military.

- Michael "Hunter" Felix: an IRM pilot with a chip on his shoulder and a family with extremist ties.

- Catrina "Swordfish" Rodriguez: a friend of Hunter and fellow pilot with connections to organized crime.

- Ivy Austin: an enforcer for the Martian organized crime syndicate.
