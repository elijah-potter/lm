A hollowed arc of obsidian curving inward like a crescent moon’s shadow,
where jagged teeth of blackened stone meet a gilded horizon—
not gold, but the first light of a sunrise that has never been seen.
The edges whisper: *"Step inside, and you will see what is lost to the dark."*

At the apex, a door carved from a single bone—its surface etched with spirals that shift when no one looks.
Beneath, a river of liquid mercury flows upward, reflecting stars that don’t belong to this world.
The air hums with the frequency of forgotten languages.

The place does not exist in space-time, only in the space between the cracks of memory.
