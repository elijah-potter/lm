I have some great ideas for herbs you can plant at home and use in different recipes. How about trying these?

1. Thyme - Thyme is wonderfully savory and works well with many different kinds of cuisines. It pairs well with various proteins, such as beef, chicken, and pork, and also works well in soups and stews.

2. Basil - Basil is extremely versatile and can be used in both Italian and Thai cooking. It is easy to grow, gives off a pleasant and refreshing aroma, and lends excellent flavor to a large variety of recipes.

3. Parsley - Parsley is another versatile herb that can be used in a multitude of dishes. The two most common types are flat (Italian) or curly parsley. This herb is also useful as a garnish.

4. Mint- Mint offers a large selection of varieties to choose from, such as spearmint, peppermint, chocolate mint, and more. It works well for savory dishes, mint juleps, iced teas, and some desserts. 

5. Dill - Dill is typically used for pickling but it also provides a wonderful flavor to pair with eggs, seafood, sauces, and dressings. Many Mediterranean dishes make use of dill.
