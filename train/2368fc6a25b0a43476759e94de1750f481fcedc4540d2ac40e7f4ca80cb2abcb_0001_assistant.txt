The top three largest whales are:
Blue Whale: These whales can grow up to 98 feet and are the largest known animal on the planet. 
Fin Whale: This whale species can grow up to 90 feet and are known also as razorback whale or the common rorqual.
Sperm Whale: These whales grow an average of 67 feet and are the largest of the toothed whales.
