Being overheard creates a mix of intrigue, anxiety, and fleeting awareness. There's a sudden tension—every word feels scrutinized, every thought exposed, as if the listener is a silent observer in the room. The mind races, hyper-aware of tone, volume, and context, even if unintentionally. The experience can be both exhilarating (a sense of power or vulnerability) and suffocating (the fear of judgment or exposure). The act of overhearing shifts the dynamic—suddenly, the speaker feels both more connected and more vulnerable, as if their words carry unseen weight. The moment lingers, leaving a residual unease or curiosity.
