*"The last light flickered through the cracked window—
a ghost of dawn, a whisper of the world
still breathing. The cat, sleek as a shadow,
paused mid-sip of milk, tail flicking once,
then vanished into the dark again."*

*rusted whispers of forgotten trade routes,*
*where time itself had a taste, like salted air.*

*stamped with a name no one remembered.*
*Now, what’s left? The question lingers.*"
