MyPlate for kids: Make half your plate fruits and vegetablesWednesday, December 28, 2011, 3:52 pm
A MyPlate poster is available to schools. This poster shows how to make a student’s tray and/or plate half fruits and vegetables at school, eating out, and at home. Teachers and school foodservice professionals will find this poster helps students visually understand how to make half their plate fruits and vegetables.
Available in PDF at http://teamnutrition.usda.gov/Resources/myplate_halfplateposter.html or upon request from Team Nutrition.
