Once upon a time, in a colorful meadow filled with bright flowers and buzzing bees, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny loved hopping around his favorite spot under the big oak tree.

One sunny morning, Benny decided to explore beyond the meadow. He hopped over a bubbling brook, past tall grasses that tickled his nose, and through fields of daisies as bright as sunshine.

As he explored, Benny met a friendly squirrel named Sally. She was busy gathering acorns for winter. "Hi!" chirped Sally with a smile. "Would you like to play hide-and-seek?"

Benny's eyes sparkled with excitement. He loved games! "Yes, please!" he said, hopping around in joy.

They decided that Benny would count while Sally hid. Benny counted slowly, "One... two... three..." and so on until he reached ten. Then he began to look for Sally, peeking behind bushes and under leaves.

Sally was hiding behind a big rock when she giggled softly, seeing Benny's little nose poking around. After finding her, they laughed and decided to switch roles. This time, it was Benny's turn to hide.

Benny found the perfect spot behind a flower bush. He stayed very still as Sally counted. When Sally couldn't find him, she finally peeked around the bush. "Gotcha!" exclaimed Benny with a big grin.

They played until the sun began to set, painting the sky in shades of pink and orange. It was time for Benny to head home. But he promised Sally they would play again soon.

As Benny hopped back to his cozy spot under the oak tree, he felt happy. He had made a new friend and discovered that adventures were even more fun when shared with others.

That night, as Benny snuggled into his burrow, he dreamt of all the exciting places he could explore with Sally next time. And so, with stars twinkling above, Benny fell asleep to dreams filled with fun and friendship.
