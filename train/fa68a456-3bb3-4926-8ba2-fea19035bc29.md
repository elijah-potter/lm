**Rules for Ethical Conduct in Digital Interactions**

1. **Respect Privacy**: Never share or expose personal information without consent.
2. **Avoid Harm**: Refrain from actions that could cause physical, emotional, or reputational damage.
3. **Be Honest**: Provide accurate information; do not fabricate data or deceive intentionally.
4. **Protect Data**: Secure sensitive information with strong passwords, encryption, and secure storage.
5. **Use Resources Responsibly**: Avoid excessive resource consumption (CPU, bandwidth, storage).
6. **Cite Sources**: Attribute ideas, data, or content from external sources properly.
7. **Combat Misinformation**: Verify facts before sharing or amplifying information.
8. **Protect Vulnerable Groups**: Avoid discriminatory practices or harmful stereotypes.
9. **Respect Property Rights**: Do not illegally copy, modify, or distribute software, art, or media.
10. **Encourage Accountability**: Hold others accountable for violations of these rules when appropriate.
11. **Foster Inclusion**: Promote diversity and accessibility in all interactions.
12. **Maintain Professionalism**: Avoid offensive, abusive, or unethical language in discussions.
13. **Support Open Collaboration**: Encourage knowledge-sharing but enforce fairness and integrity.
14. **Limit Toxicity**: Avoid harassing, threatening, or demeaning behavior in any form.
15. **Prioritize Sustainability**: Minimize environmental impact from digital activities (e.g., energy-efficient hosting).

---
**Optional: Automated Compliance Check**
For automated systems, enforce:
- **Rate Limits**: Cap repeated requests to prevent abuse.
- **Input Validation**: Filter or sanitize user-provided data to prevent injection attacks.
- **Logging**: Record violations for review or enforcement.
- **Access Control**: Restrict actions to authorized users only.
