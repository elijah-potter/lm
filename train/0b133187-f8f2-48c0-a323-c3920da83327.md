Unspoken customs in the **old town’s alleys** near the docks:

1. **The Watchers** – No one dares walk the shadowed arcades at night. The lanterns glow faintly, but the real light is the whispers of those who keep watch. Those who linger too long are either dismissed or asked to move. The "rules" are enforced by silence: a nod, a smirk, or a glance toward the door that doesn’t exist.

2. **The Bridge of Hollow Steps** – The wooden planks shift underfoot, each step a memory. The bridge isn’t just a path; it’s a *promise*. Those who cross must do so alone. Two or more, and the steps feel heavier, the air thicker. The locals say the bridge remembers who walked where—and what they left behind. The unspoken rule? Leave no trace. Not even a single shoe.

3. **The Café’s Hidden Menu** – Behind the counter, a board with scribbled names and numbers. Each one is a debt, a favor, or a debt owed. Sit at the wrong table, and you’ll be asked to move—or to explain. The coffee is free; the questions are payment.

4. **The Blacksmith’s Forge** – The hammer strikes the anvil with precision. No one bends rules here—not for steel, not for truth. A poorly forged blade is useless. A well-placed lie? A blade that won’t hold. The blacksmith doesn’t ask why; he just *knows*.

5. **The Fountain of Echoes** – Water swirls in a spiral basin, forming a chorus of voices. Speak too loudly, and your words repeat in someone else’s tone. The rule isn’t spoken—it’s the sound of the basin itself. Some say it’s a trap. Others say it’s a mirror.

6. **The Clockmaker’s Workshop** – The gears turn at their own pace. Time isn’t linear here; it’s a series of quiet exchanges. The clock doesn’t tell time. It *remembers*. Watch someone’s hands move—and you’ll see what they’ve lost or gained.

7. **The Bargain at the Market Stall** – No one writes contracts. A trade is made with a glance, a handshake, or a stolen glance. The coin changes hands; the truth stays in the eye of the buyer.

8. **The Library of Forgotten Books** – The shelves hum with stories no one remembers writing. The rules? You don’t take what you find. You *listen*. And if you leave a book behind, you leave a story with it.

The town doesn’t need signs. The rules are in the cracks between the cobblestones, the way the wind shifts, the way the shadows breathe. Step wrong, and you’ll learn faster than you’d like. Step right, and you’ll forget why you ever cared.
