Here relying on a good quality water purifier will be the best option for you to ensure that you are drinking clean and safe water. Almost all the leading water purifier companies perform water tests to know about the quality of water, and then they incorporate adequate technologies in their systems to remove contaminants or any other issues found in the water quality test.
