**The Whispering Room**

I stand before the mirror, my reflection flickering like a candle in a storm. The glass is old, its edges cracked with the weight of years—some say it holds secrets, others that it lies. I press my palm against it, and the light bends, warping faces into ghosts of what might have been.

*What if I’m wrong?*
The voice is mine, but it sounds like someone else’s. I lean closer, fingers trembling. The reflection does not move. Not yet.

The door creaks open. A shadow steps inside—too tall, too still. It does not speak. Does not blink. The air thickens, humming with something I cannot name. My pulse hammers in my throat. *What if I’m not the only one who’s unsure?*

I reach out, and the mirror shatters. Not glass—*nothing*. The room dissolves into static, then silence. When I blink, the door is gone. The walls are paper, and the words on them whisper:

*"You were never sure of anything."*

Then—nothing.
