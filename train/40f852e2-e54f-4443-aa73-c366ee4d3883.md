Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a tiny bunny named Pip. Pip loved to hop around his garden, collecting shiny flowers and chasing butterflies. One sunny morning, Pip heard a tiny squeak.

"Help! Help!" piped a little voice from under a bush.

Pip peeked and saw a lost baby bird, its wings still soft and fluffy, sitting on the ground. The bird was tiny and scared, its feathers all messed up.

Pip hopped closer and said, "Don’t worry, little one! I’ll help you."

Gently, Pip picked up the bird and carried it to a safe spot under a big leaf. "Here you are," he said, placing it carefully. The bird blinked its big eyes and chirped softly.

"Thank you, Pip!" it said, trying to hop away. Pip smiled. "You’re welcome, little friend!"

Just then, a kind old lady with a basket of bread came walking by. She smiled at Pip and the bird. "What are you doing?" she asked.

Pip showed her the baby bird. "I found it lost," he said.

The old lady laughed. "Oh, how sweet! You’re a good bunny." She gave Pip a tiny piece of bread and said, "Here, eat this. You’ve done a brave thing!"

Pip ate happily and watched the bird hop around, learning to fly. From that day on, Pip and the little bird became the best of friends, sharing adventures and sunshine in their garden.

And whenever Pip hopped by, the baby bird would chirp, "Remember, Pip? Always help others!"

The end.
