Are Volcanoes Really a Concern in the United States?
In 1997, popular movies have shown the catastrophic impact from fictional volcanic eruptions on American cities. Do you think this could really happen? Whether they realize it or not, millions of U.S. residents live in the shadow of active volcanoes. Through this module, you will learn about the hazards of one of the many volcanoes in the United States.
