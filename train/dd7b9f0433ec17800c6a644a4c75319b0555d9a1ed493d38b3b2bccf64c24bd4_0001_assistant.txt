I hear you. Here are three reasons why you shouldn't reach out to your former friends:

1. If you see what they've been up to, you might be tempted to reach out to them. Seeing that a former friend got married, broke up with their partner, or switched to another job makes you wonder what happened. However, you can't wonder about their lives if you don't know anything about them.
2. You might click "Like" on one of their posts, letting them know that you checked on them. Whoops!
3. Sometimes, seeing old friends makes you think about the good times. But let's face it: you DID block them for a reason. You don't want to assume that the good times will return when they probably won't.

Suggestion: focus on your current friends, and think about making some new ones instead of ruminating on the past.
