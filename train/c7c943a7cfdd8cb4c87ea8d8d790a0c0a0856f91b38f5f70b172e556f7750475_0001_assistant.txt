**Apocalypse**

The street is stark against
a putrid skyline,
humming with rough static

Detritus of humanity
lies along its
history, an old
sock with ragged toes

Broken dolls with
shattered eyes
that try to see
even in their ruin

Rats stand sentinel
over sprawled corpses
like funeral attendants
Stolid, silent

An old parking ticket
Floats through a gutter
awash with rain soaked ink 

The street is filled
with information,
but there is nobody
left to read it
