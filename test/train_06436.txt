Jairus read 20 pages of the newspaper. Arniel read 2 more than twice the number of pages Jairus read. How many pages have they read altogether?
Twice the number of pages Jairus read is 20 x 2 = <<20*2=40>>40.
Arniel read 40 + 2 = <<40+2=42>>42 pages of the newspaper.
Jairus and Arniel read 20 + 42 = <<20+42=62>>62 pages altogether.
