1. Amryst returns breathless to her hometown. She is windblown, scarred, and wide-eyed. Instantly, it is obvious that she does not fit in her clean, little small town anymore.
2. Amryst tries to settle back in with her family, but her new ideas confuse them and they judge her for being different.
3. Feeling shamed, she tries to hide her scars and her adventures by making herself clean and small again.
4. Though everyone else is more comfortable with her, she is unhappy and has low self-worth. She often stares out into the distance and wonders if she'll ever have the courage to leave again.
5. One day, she catches herself thinking hateful thoughts of herself and realizes the impact fitting in has on her. She is enraged that she'd let others shame her.
6. She talks to her family, and it turns into a big argument until her parents say they just want her to be happy.
7. She accepts that her family is not her compass and becomes independent again.
8. She finds like-minded people by doing the things in town that she enjoys.
9. They pack and leave for another adventure, but this time, she goes with her found family.
