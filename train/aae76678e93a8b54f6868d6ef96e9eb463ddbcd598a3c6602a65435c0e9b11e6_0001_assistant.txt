1.) Swim in the ocean - Lazing in the waves is the best part of going to the beach.

2.) Play mini golf - Many beach destinations have mini golf courses nearby for you to enjoy.

3.) Go jetski riding - A thrilling adventure, jetski riding is a great way to get the most from your trip.

4.) Go scuba diving - Scuba diving is an eye-opening and highly entertaining experience and is lots of fun with friends.

5.) Go on a helicopter tour - A helicopter tour lets you see the world from a different perspective. Many beach vacation destinations offer a helicopter tour of the beach and surrounding areas.
