**BREAKING:** Mayor Harris announces new urban park initiative in **New Haven Haven** after record rainfall hits the city. The newly proposed **Rainbow Meadows Park** will be built along the stormwater runoff channels, integrating green infrastructure to combat flooding. The project, estimated at $12.8 million, includes 20 acres of native plants, a stormwater filtration system, and a 3-mile bike path. Construction is slated for 2025 with public input sessions beginning next month. Residents and city officials urge residents to participate in the design phase. The city also reports a 15% increase in emergency flood response calls in the last quarter, prompting a review of existing drainage systems.

**Downtown business owners praise initiative but call for faster approvals**, citing delays in infrastructure projects as a barrier to economic growth. The **Haven Haven Chamber of Commerce** has proposed linking the park to a new urban farm initiative to boost local agriculture and tourism. Meanwhile, a city council vote on the budget allocation for the park is scheduled for tomorrow. The mayor emphasized that climate resilience is a top priority in this fiscal year’s spending.
