**The Last Light**

She found the notebook on the kitchen table, its edges yellowed with age, the ink smudged from years of use. The first page was blank, but the second held a single, bold line: *"Do not forget."*

A week had passed since the storm. The city had frozen into silence, the power flickering like a dying star. Her hands trembled as she traced the faded script, the words now blurred but still legible:

*"If you’re reading this, you’ve survived. But the world outside still waits. Remember: the cold does not stay forever. The sun will rise again."*

She exhaled sharply, the breath catching in her throat. Outside, the wind howled through the broken windows, but inside, the fire burned low—just enough to cast long shadows across the walls.

She reached for the knife on the counter and turned it over in her palm. The blade was sharp, even after months of neglect. A reminder, she thought. Not just of survival, but of what came next. The hunt. The hunger. The choice.

The door creaked open. The storm had passed, but the night was not done with her yet.
