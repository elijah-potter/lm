Everyone looked at Jake, the camp leader, because he was known for having an arsenal of scary stories up his sleeve. Excited to get the young campers jittery on a night out in the woods, Jake began his story.

"Have you ever heard of Popsicle the Clown? I bet you don’t think his name is scary at all, do you? But that’s how he gets you to let your guard down. That’s what happened to the Johnson family. They held a birthday party for their little girl, Sandy. And finding an advertisement in the newspaper, they hired Popsicle the Clown for entertainment. At first, it went great; he was a natural with kids, making balloon animals, telling jokes, and playing hilarious pranks on them. But as soon as the adults took their eyes off the kids, they began to disappear, one after another. 

Nobody knew where little Mikey went. All they found was his favorite red baseball cap, lying on the grass where he was last seen. Then Ruby vanished from where she was collecting sweets from the piñata. One by one the children began to disappear, while their parents remained indoors, oblivious, enjoying their drinks. The last to disappear was the birthday girl herself. She was last seen with Popsicle the Clown, who led her away with a knife in his hand. And do you know what his last words were? BOO!” Jake jumped, and the circle of campers around him screamed. Jake burst into a fit of laughter.
