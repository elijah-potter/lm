Nitric oxide is an important neurotransmitter in maintaining the health of blood vessels. The level of nitric oxide determines the degree of blood flow through arteries and veins. Deficiencies in the production of nitric oxide are associated with a number of disease
states including high blood pressure, heart disease, heart failure, and pulmonary hypertension. Hypertensa is designed to produce nitric oxide and acetylcholine. These neurotransmitters are needed to maintain vascular health in arteriosclerosis, hypertension, asthma, and muscle performance. Hypertensa has been shown to increase nitric oxide production in humans.
