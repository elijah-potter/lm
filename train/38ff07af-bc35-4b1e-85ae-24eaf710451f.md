## Lost in the Bookstore (Seattle, Pike Place Market)

The rain was relentless that afternoon, a silver curtain drawn across the cobblestone streets of the Market. I was browsing the used books at Left Bank Books, seeking refuge from the downpour and the throngs of tourists. You were examining a collection of poetry by Mary Oliver, your brow furrowed in concentration. I noticed the way the light caught the auburn highlights in your hair, a fleeting shimmer against the muted tones of the shelves. We didn’t speak, but I felt a quiet resonance, a shared appreciation for the scent of aged paper and the promise held within those worn pages. You purchased the Oliver collection and paused, briefly meeting my gaze with a gentle smile before disappearing into the crowd. It was a moment of unexpected stillness, a brief connection in a bustling place. I’d genuinely like to know if you felt it too.
