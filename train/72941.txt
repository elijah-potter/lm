- Solomon Islands -
Solomon Islands is located in the Pacific Ocean northeast of Australia. This naturally beautiful setting is the home of a developing nation graced with human resilience. And it is fortunate indeed that the islanders are so emotionally resilient, as in 2007 and 2010 Solomon Islands suffered two major earthquakes and tsunamis.
