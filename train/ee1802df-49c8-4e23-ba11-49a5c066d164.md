## Project Chimera: A Cooperative Research Crisis

**A Game for 2-4 Players**

**Designer’s Note:** *Project Chimera is designed to simulate the challenges of scientific collaboration under immense pressure. The game aims to foster understanding of risk assessment, resource allocation, and the ethical considerations inherent in responding to unforeseen global crises. It emphasizes communication, strategic prioritization, and the acceptance that difficult choices, often with imperfect information, are inevitable.*

### Components

*   **Crisis Board:** A central board depicting a world map with designated Research Locations and escalating Crisis Zones.
*   **Researcher Cards (16):** Each card represents a unique researcher with specific Expertise (Biology, Chemistry, Engineering, Data Analysis) and a starting Resource.
*   **Crisis Cards (60):** Representing escalating events, scientific anomalies, and logistical challenges. Divided into three tiers of increasing severity.
*   **Research Tokens (48):** Representing progress in understanding the crisis.
*   **Resource Tokens (80):** Representing funding, equipment, and personnel. Divided into three types: Scientific (blue), Logistical (green), and Security (red).
*   **Anomaly Markers (12):** Placed on the Crisis Board to indicate areas of heightened instability.
*   **Containment Markers (8):** Used to mitigate the spread of the crisis.
*   **Dice (6):** Six-sided dice used for skill checks and event resolution.
*   **First Player Marker:** Indicates the active player.
*   **Rulebook (this document).**

### Setup

1.  Place the Crisis Board in the center of the playing area.
2.  Shuffle the Crisis Cards and divide them into three face-down piles based on their tier (I, II, and III). Place these piles near the Crisis Board.
3.  Shuffle the Researcher Cards and deal four cards to each player. Each player chooses one Researcher Card to play and returns the others to the bottom of the deck.
4.  Each player receives their Researcher’s starting Resource as indicated on their card.
5.  Place three Research Tokens on each Research Location on the Crisis Board.
6.  Place one Anomaly Marker on each designated Anomaly Location on the Crisis Board.
7.  Determine the first player randomly and give them the First Player Marker.
8.  **Difficulty Scaling:**
    *   **Two Players:** Begin with Tier I Crisis Cards only.
    *   **Three Players:** Begin with Tier I Crisis Cards.
    *   **Four Players:** Begin with Tier I Crisis Cards.

### Turn Structure

Each player’s turn consists of the following phases, performed in order:

1.  **Draw Phase:** Draw one Crisis Card from the top of the current Tier pile. Read the card aloud. The card will dictate immediate effects, placement of Anomaly Markers, or placement of Containment Markers.
2.  **Action Phase:** You have two actions to perform. Actions can be any combination of the following:
    *   **Move:** Move your Researcher to an adjacent Research Location.
    *   **Research:** Attempt to gain Research Tokens at your current location. Roll two dice. Add the sum to your Researcher’s Expertise score (as indicated on their card). If the total meets or exceeds the location’s Research Difficulty (printed on the Crisis Board), gain one Research Token.
    *   **Gather Resources:** Attempt to gather Resources at your current location. Roll one die. The result determines the type of Resource gained (1-2: Scientific, 3-4: Logistical, 5-6: Security). You gain one Resource of the determined type.
    *   **Mitigate Anomaly:** Spend one Security Resource and roll one die. If the result is 4 or higher, remove one Anomaly Marker from your current location.
    *   **Deploy Containment:** Spend one Logistical and one Security Resource. Place a Containment Marker on your current location. This prevents Crisis Zones from spreading from that location.
3.  **Crisis Phase:** Advance the Crisis Track one space. If the Crisis Track reaches a designated threshold, advance the Tier of Crisis Cards being drawn.
4.  **Pass the Marker:** Pass the First Player Marker to the player to your left.

### Win and Loss Conditions

*   **Winning:** You win the game if you collectively gain a predetermined number of Research Tokens (12 for two players, 16 for three players, 20 for four players) *before* the Crisis Track reaches its maximum value.
*   **Losing:** You lose the game if the Crisis Track reaches its maximum value *before* you collectively gain the required number of Research Tokens. You also lose if any Research Location runs out of Research Tokens.

### Advanced Variants & Mechanics

1.  **The Uncertainty Protocol (Resource Trade-offs):** When attempting to Research, you may choose to spend one Resource of any type to re-roll one of your dice. This represents leveraging external support, but introduces the risk of an unfavorable outcome.
2.  **The Escalation Gambit (Team Prioritization):** At the end of each player's turn, the player to their left *must* choose one of two options: either draw a new Crisis Card from the current Tier and immediately resolve its effects, or discard one Resource of their choice. This forces difficult decisions about immediate risk versus long-term resource management.
3.  **The Containment Dilemma (Shared Risk):** When placing a Containment Marker, all players must secretly contribute one Resource of their choice. The type of Resource contributed is revealed simultaneously. If the total value of Resources contributed (based on a pre-determined value chart: Scientific=1, Logistical=2, Security=3) is below a threshold value printed on the Crisis Board, the Containment Marker is ineffective and all contributed Resources are lost. This creates a shared risk scenario, requiring careful consideration of individual needs versus collective benefit.



This simulates the pressure of scientific collaboration and the difficult choices that must be made under duress.
