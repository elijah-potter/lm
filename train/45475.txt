WHAT IS A Dental filling ? IA dental restoration or dental filling is a dental restorative material used to restore the function, integrity and morphology of missing tooth structure. The structural loss typically results from caries or external trauma. Food stuck in teeth activate bacteria in the mouth to turn them into acids. The acids, food, and bacteria all combine to make what is called plaque. Plaque stubbornly clings to teeth and eats away at tooth enamel causing decay and cavities. Tooth decay is what will cause cavities; this decay can effect the enamel, the outer layer of the tooth, and dentin, the inner layer. It is also lost intentionally during tooth preparation to improve the aesthetics or the physical integrity of the intended restorative material.
