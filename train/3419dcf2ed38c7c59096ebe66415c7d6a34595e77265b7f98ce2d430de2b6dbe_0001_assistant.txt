I visited Sundara Palace on XXth Street last weekend, and if I had to give you a sound effect to describe it, I'd say, "Meh." It's a buffet-style restaurant that offers Indian cuisine for a reasonable price. The buffet included some fairly standard items:

* butter chicken,
* Basmati rice,
* naan,
* chickpea curry,
* spinach curry with cottage cheese,
* ground goat with green peas, and
* Shami kebabs.

There were side items as well, such as yogurt raita, sliced fresh vegetables, a milky rice dessert called "kheer," and gulab jamin. Overall, the flavors were pretty good but a little on the mild side. Also, my drink was slow to arrive at the table, and I felt pressured to purchase chai, too.

I'd give Sundara Palace a solid three stars, but I'd definitely consider additional stars for the following changes:

* More items need to be offered on the buffet. There were empty places for additional dishes, and I was very disappointed that tandoori chicken wasn't present!
* Have someone with better handwriting label the dishes (or print them out from the computer). People should know for sure what they're scooping onto their plates.
* Please understand that not everyone wants hot tea. Ask your servers not to insist that customers have chai at the end of their meal.

I fully intend to return to Sundara Palace in the future to give it another try and will update my review then!
