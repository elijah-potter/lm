A group of miners in a dimly lit, damp shaft suddenly hear a distant explosion. The air thickens with dust and the sound of collapsing rock. The mine’s emergency lights flicker on as the mine’s foreman yells, "All hands, we’re abandoning the tunnel—**rules change now: no further retreat, stay put, and report all exits!**"

In the chaos, a child clutches a lantern, eyes wide, as the foreman’s voice cuts through the panic: "New rule: **only the marked team gets out—those without the **red tag** will be left behind.**" A miner’s torch snaps out. Shadows stretch long as the last train’s whistle echoes—**no time to argue, only to act.**
