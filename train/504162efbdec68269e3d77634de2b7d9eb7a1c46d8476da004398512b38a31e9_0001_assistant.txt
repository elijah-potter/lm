Walking into the casino
I was feeling quite excited.
The sound of slots ringing and I was invited.
I knew that if I played just one more time.
It could be my big chance to shine. 

So with a hopeful heart and eager eyes
I stepped up to the machine's side.  
Others are drawn to the thrill of a table.
But for slot machines, I am willing and able.
Inserting coins with an excited shake
My hands were trembling as I pressed "Spin" for their stake!  
With every second that passed by so slow 
Lo and behold, what did those wheels show?   

A jackpot win on the very first spin! 
My luck had come in - what blissful din! 			    
All around people cheered and clapped; 
This moment felt like rapture trapped.
With newfound wealth all mine at last,
Out of there fast was all too vast!
