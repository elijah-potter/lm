Crust
3 cups (300g) graham cracker crumbles (about 20 whole graham crackers, crushed)
1/2 cup (56g) confectioners’ sugar
12 tablespoons (170g) butter, melted
1/4 teaspoon salt

Filling
Four 8-ounce packages (904g) cream cheese, at room temperature
1 1/3 cup (266g) granulated sugar
4 large eggs, at room temperature
2 teaspoons King Arthur Pure Vanilla Extract
