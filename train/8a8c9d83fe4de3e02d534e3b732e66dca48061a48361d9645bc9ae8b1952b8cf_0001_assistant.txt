24/2/2017

Dear Journal,

It happened again. More of those spirit things. This time, a couple were hovering over me as I woke up. I know for a fact it isn't my imagination now. They didn't do anything to me, they just stared and faded away. They didn't even make a sound. It's weird, I feel like I should be scared out of my mind, but I'm not. I'm just really confused. Okay, maybe I'm a little scared... I never should've touched the monument in that abandoned house. I knew something was off, but Connor kept up the pep talk, and I stupidly listened. It's been about 5 days since the 4 of us did that dare, and I'm starting to see more ghosts as each day goes by. I saw 3 different ones at school yesterday, and another on the way home. There's no way I can tell anyone else about this. Who'd even believe me? Even my friends think I'm just being super paranoid.

It doesn't feel like any movie or book. The ones where the main character can suddenly see and talk to spirits and stuff like that. It doesn't feel like the start of any kind of awesome superpower at all. It feels like a curse. I don't know, maybe I'm overthinking whatever this may be. I'll keep writing my thoughts down in this journal for a while. It's the only thing stopping me from freaking out right now.
