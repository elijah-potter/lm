Post-flood delta: jagged, rust-stained levees crumble into saturated earth, where once-grown crops now emerge as skeletal, blackened stalks from the waterlogged soil. The air smells of damp iron and decaying vegetation. A single, weathered wooden sign, half-submerged, reads *"Do Not Cross"* in faded, faded ink. Beyond the murky horizon, the old riverbend now splits into a thousand stagnant pools, reflecting the low-hanging, blood-red sky. The only sounds are the distant gurgle of trapped air in sunken trees and the occasional splash of a curious heron probing the shallows.
