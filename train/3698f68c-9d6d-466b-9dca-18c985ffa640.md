Perspective shifts reveal hidden truths.
What you see as a wall may be the boundary of a new landscape.
A single glance can turn a shadow into light—if you adjust the angle.
Objects remain static; perception changes.
The same scene can be a prison or a sanctuary, depending on the lens.
Emotions, like light, bend reality.
Silence, once a void, becomes the stage for unexpected echoes.
The familiar now whispers secrets if you listen.
What was once fixed becomes fluid when you reframe.
A child’s world is a playground; an adult’s, a labyrinth—yet both are maps.
The only constant is the act of seeing differently.
Your frame of reference is your compass; change it, and the compass points to new shores.
