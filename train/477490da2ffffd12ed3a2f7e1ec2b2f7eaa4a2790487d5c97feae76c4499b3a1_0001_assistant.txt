Roger decided to become more accustomed to the new features of the Internet. Specifically, he wanted to be able to pay his bills online, rather than having to go to the bank to withdraw money or write checks and send them via mail to pay his debts. He wanted to be able to sit down at his computer and monitor his finances instead of receiving paper statements from his bank telling him how much money he was giving out.

Roger logged onto his bank's website, typing the URL of the site into his web browser. He was greeted by the login page for his bank, where he began to sign up for a new online account. The site required him to create a new username and password.  Roger typed in a username he made up in his head: "Username". He then created his new password, "Password". The website returned the following error message: "Your username must not contain the word, 'Username'". So, he created a new username, "Superman". The website returned another error message: "Your username must contain one uppercase letter, a number, and a special character."

Flustered, Roger created a new username, "$uperman1". He then typed in the password, "Password". The website returned the following error message: "Your password must not contain the word, 'Password.'" Now pulling at the few hairs left on his head, Roger sighed and then, with renewed determination, created a new password, "$uperman1". The website returned the following error message: "Your username and password must not be the same." 

Now completely unhinged, Roger began laughing in confusion and half-insanity and turned off his computer. Maybe it's not so bad to pay with checks, he thought to himself. He grabbed his keys and left the house, deciding to go to the utility company in-person to pay his bills.
