Page 1: Emily was a pro on her hoverboard. She could zip around super fast and spin in circles until she was dizzy without ever falling off.

Page 2: One day, she was going so fast on her hoverboard that when she tried to stop, she leaned back too far and fell straight on her elbow. Ouch!

Page 3: Her mom gave her an ice pack and dried her tears with a tissue. But her elbow still hurt really bad.

Page 4: Emily’s mom decided they needed to go to the hospital to check if her elbow bone got broken in the fall.

Page 5: At the hospital, a nurse took X-rays of Emily’s elbow. These are pictures that show what the bones look like on the inside of your arm.

Page 6: After waiting in a room that seemed like forever, the doctor came in to tell Emily that she had broken her elbow. She would need to wear a cast until it got better.

Page 7: Emily got to pick the color of her cast, which was kind of cool. She chose hot pink that also had glitter sparkles!

Page 8: The doctor told Emily that now that she has a cast on, she would have to stay off her hoverboard for six whole weeks. She would also have to sit out at recess so that she didn’t hurt her arm again while it was healing.

Page 9: The cast couldn’t get wet either, so during Bath time, Emily had to wrap her arm in plastic. This made it difficult to wash her hair, but her mom helped her a lot.

Page 10: Emily is a little bummed out that she is stuck in a cast for six weeks, but she knows that her elbow will be good as new once it’s time to take it off.
