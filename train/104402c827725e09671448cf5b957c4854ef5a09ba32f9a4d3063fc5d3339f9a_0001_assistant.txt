Many believe the superstition that opening an umbrella indoors is bad luck. It is believed by them that if you do, you may come upon hard times, invite ghosts, develop insomnia, or even become spiritually blind. According to legend, you can reverse the curse of opening an umbrella indoors either by replacing the umbrella or by speaking positive affirmations inside the home where it was opened. Despite this superstition, March 13th is National Open an Umbrella Indoors Day (in Canada). On this day, you should open an umbrella inside and write down any negative things that happen over the next several days.
