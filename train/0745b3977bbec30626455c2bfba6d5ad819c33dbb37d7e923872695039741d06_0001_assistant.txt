Libraries and cats naturally go together. Both are clean, quiet, and peaceful to be around. Fortunately for library patrons, the custom of having cats live at the local library has spread overseas. Here's a list of some of the most famous library cats.

1. Dewey Readmore Books. Official Position: Library Mascot
    Spencer Public Library, Spencer, Iowa
    From March 1988 until 2006, Dewey served as the Library Mascot for the Spencer Public Library. He may have started life as a kitten abandoned in the library book return drop, but the fluffy orange cat became an internet sensation. Visitors came to see Dewey, and he had a book written about his life. Named after the Dewey Decimal System, Dewey remains the face of the Iowa Public Library System.

2. Kuzya. Official Position: Assistant Librarian
    Novorossiysk Library, Russia
    In October 2012, a stray cat made his way to the Novorossiysk Public Library. He was an instant hit with the library staff and the patrons, and everyone wanted to keep him. But Kuzya didn't have his official papers, so the staff set about securing the required documents, including a cat passport for Kuzya. With his papers in order, Kuzya's first job was as the library's official greeter and afternoon napper, and more people began frequenting the library. He was soon promoted to his current position of Assistant Librarian, awarded a bow tie to wear, and received a well-deserved raise in cat food and treats. 

3. Pages. Official Position: Library Greeter and Blogger
    Valley Center Public Library, Valley Center, Kansas
    The Valley Center Public Library got its beloved mascot when a stray wandered into the facility. It didn't take long for the staff to adopt this little kitty and make Pages the library's official greeter. Pages is a favorite with patrons and staff alike and keeps everyone up to date by posting her blogs on the Posts from the Paw website.
