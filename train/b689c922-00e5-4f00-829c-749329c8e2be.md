The river hummed a tune that no one could hear,
a whisper lost in the wind’s endless choir.
*"Flow, flow, flow"* the stones would croak,
as time dissolved into the misty snow.

She traced the same path every dawn,
the same cracked pavement, the same old pain.
*"Turn, turn, turn,"* the clock’s slow hand spun,
while shadows danced in endless sun.

The cat purred the same refrain,
a loop of light and fading rain.
*"One, two, three"* the stars would say,
as the world spun in a quiet day.
