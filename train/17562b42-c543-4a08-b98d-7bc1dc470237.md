Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big tree outside, there lived a little bunny named Pip. Pip had soft brown fur and a big, round nose that always twitched when he smelled something yummy.

One sunny morning, Pip woke up and saw his favorite spot—the patch of clover by the fence. He hopped out, his paws making little *thump-thump* sounds. "Today’s going to be a great day!" he thought.

But then, Pip noticed something strange. The clover was missing! His favorite spot was empty. He looked around and saw a tiny, shiny blue button near the fence. Pip picked it up and wondered what it could be.

Suddenly, a tiny voice piped up, *"Hello, little bunny!"* Pip turned around and saw a little girl with a big smile. She had a basket full of treats—tiny sandwiches, berries, and a cookie shaped like a bunny’s face!

Pip’s eyes sparkled. "Can I help you?" he asked.

The girl grinned. "Sure! I need to find my missing treasure box before the big storm comes. Can you help me find it?"

Pip hopped closer. "Of course! Let’s go!"

They explored the garden together. Pip’s nose twitched—there was a scent of something sweet. They found a hidden path under the bushes, and there, under a big leaf, was the treasure box! It was wrapped in a blanket with a ribbon tied in a bow.

Inside, there were colorful toys, stickers, and a note: *"Thank you for helping, Pip!"* written in tiny handwriting.

Pip and the girl hugged each other. "We’ll do it again tomorrow!" Pip said happily.

As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and orange, Pip and the girl waved goodbye. Pip curled up in his favorite patch of clover, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures. And under the big tree, the little bunny felt the happiest he ever had.
