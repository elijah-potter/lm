Dear HOA Board, 

I am writing to express my frustration about the lack of a retractable roof over our pool. As a concerned neighbor, I have noticed the number of sunburns that many individuals in our community have gotten due to the intense sunlight in the pool area.

Why hasn’t the HOA Board taken any measures to address this issue? I have witnessed several individuals complain about the lack of shade while commenting on how it makes their pool experience unbearable. 

A retractable roof will not only increase the happiness and overall health of our residents but will also raise the property values of homes in our community. The pool is an important part of our community, and a retractable roof is not a common amenity found in other communities. As such, potential homebuyers would be encouraged to live here based on this luxury. 

I strongly urge the Board to take immediate action and install a retractable roof over the pool area. It will provide much-needed shade while increasing the value of our community in numerous ways. 

I hope this letter encourages the Board to prioritize the health and needs of its residents both now and in the future.

Sincerely,

An Angry Neighbor
