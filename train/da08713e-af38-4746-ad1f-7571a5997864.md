The alarm blared at 3:17 AM as the city’s neon glow flickered like dying stars. Outside, the wind howled through skeletal trees, carrying whispers of something old beneath the streets. Inside the abandoned subway tunnel, a rusted sign flickered: **"Warning: Do Not Enter Below"**—but the power had been off for weeks, and the walls were already crawling with the wrong kind of light.

A single key turned in the lock of the forgotten vault. The air inside smelled of damp earth and something metallic, like old blood. The shelves were lined with crates stamped with symbols no one recognized. One crate bore a faded emblem: a hand clutching a dagger, its blade etched with symbols that made the back of his neck prickle.

The first voice spoke—not from the darkness, but from the walls themselves. *"You shouldn’t be here."* The sound was wet, like teeth scraping glass. The key turned again. The vault doors groaned open.
