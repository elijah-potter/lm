A flickering lantern casts long shadows across the damp stones, illuminating half-finished carvings of forgotten gods. The air hums with the distant, rhythmic *tap-tap-tap* of a wooden flute, though the melody is lost in the wind. A figure hunches over a half-eaten meal of roasted meats and blackened grains, their fingers stained with ash and sweat. A child’s laughter, muffled, echoes from the crumbling archway behind them. Outside, the distant thunder of hooves fades into the night, swallowed by the creaking timbers of the abandoned structure. The scent of damp earth and iron lingers, thick and unyielding.
