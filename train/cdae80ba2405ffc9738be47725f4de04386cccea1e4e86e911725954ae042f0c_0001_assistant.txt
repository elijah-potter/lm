Retired Starfleet admiral Jean-Luc Picard, ex-commander of the starship Enterprise-D, confronts his ultimate enemy, the Borg, in Season 3 of "Star Trek: Picard." Over the past decade, the Borg have worked in secret with other enemies of the United Federation of Planets, deploying hidden agents to infiltrate Starfleet and compromise their transporter technology to ultimately conquer humanity. Will the Borg ultimately assimilate the entire Starfleet, or will Picard and his fearless crew find a way to save the galaxy against all odds? Find out in the season premiere of "Star Trek: Picard."
