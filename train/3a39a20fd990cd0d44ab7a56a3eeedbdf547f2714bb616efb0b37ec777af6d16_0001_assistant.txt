Idea #1

Alice inherited her mother's beautiful diamond brooch after she passed away. She wore it proudly, but only for special occasions. When she was getting ready for her next formal event, she went to take the brooch out of her jewelry box, but it wasn't there. What happened to the brooch?


Idea #2

Dora loved to bake. And she often used an old potholder that had belonged to her mother, which brought back memories of the two of them baking together when she was a little girl. But the next time she went to use her beloved potholder, it wasn't in its usual place. She asked her husband if he'd seen it. "Oh, that old thing? he said. "It was ugly, so I threw it away." Dora was enraged.


Idea #3

When Mary was a teenager and going through a difficult time, she wrote to her favorite celebrity. And the celebrity had been kind enough to write back to her with some encouraging words. Mary eventually had this letter framed and hung it on a wall in her home. Now years later, the letter was worth a lot of money because the celebrity had passed away and hadn't signed many things during his career. Ordinarily, Mary would never have thought of selling her letter because it meant so much to her. But now, her adult son was in legal trouble again, so Mary decided to sell her letter, but with deep and bitter resentment toward her son. How does Mary's relationship with her son evolve after this?
