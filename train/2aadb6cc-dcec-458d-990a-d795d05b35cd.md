**Morning Routine:**
- **7:00 AM** – Wake up, hydrate
- **7:15 AM** – Breakfast (oatmeal with berries, 1 cup green tea)
- **8:00 AM** – Gym: Strength training (30 min)
- **8:45 AM** – Shower, skincare routine

**Midday:**
- **12:00 PM** – Lunch (salmon with quinoa, steamed broccoli; 15 min prep)
- **1:00 PM** – Study/work session (focused task, no distractions)
- **2:30 PM** – Errands (groceries, bank, pharmacy)

**Afternoon:**
- **4:00 PM** – Free time (reading, light exercise, or creative work)
- **5:30 PM** – Dinner (stir-fry with tofu, veggies, brown rice)
- **6:30 PM** – Relaxation (music, meditation, or short walk)

**Evening:**
- **8:00 PM** – Wind down (journaling, no screens)
- **9:00 PM** – Sleep (7-8 hours)

---
**Optional Flex Days:**
- Replace gym with yoga/stretching (10 min)
- Swap study time for a podcast/skill-building (1 hour)

---
**Weekly Breakdown:**
- **Monday:** Deep work (2 hours)
- **Wednesday:** Social time (call/family)
- **Friday:** Creative project (e.g., coding, writing)
