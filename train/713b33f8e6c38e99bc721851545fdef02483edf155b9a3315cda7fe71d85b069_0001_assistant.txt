1. Go for a Hike. Not only will the two of you benefit from fresh air and sunshine, but hikes are also a great opportunity to talk to one another as you experience the wonders of nature together.
2. Host a Board Game Night. This is a fun date you can do just the two of you or invite other couples over and turn it into a group date! 
3. Volunteer Together. Volunteering is a wonderful way to give back to your community and grow closer at the same time over your shared interests.
4. Visit a Museum. Most museums have free or cheap admission prices depending on when you go, so the two of you can learn new things while spending time together.
