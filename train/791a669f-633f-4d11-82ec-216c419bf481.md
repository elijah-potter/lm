They stood in the dim glow of the flickering streetlamp, shoulders brushing as they leaned against the cold brick wall of an abandoned shop. The air was thick with the scent of damp earth and old wood. One person—tall, with dark hair pulled into a loose braid—reached out, fingers brushing the other’s wrist. The other, shorter, with piercing blue eyes and a quiet confidence, didn’t flinch. Instead, their gaze locked onto the first, unspoken words passing between them like a shared breath.

The first hesitated, then whispered, *"I never thought you’d still be here."* The second didn’t answer at first—just tilted their head, studying them. Then, with a slow exhale, they said, *"Neither did I."* The moment hung between them, fragile as glass, but unbreakable. The first reached up, tracing the scar above their eyebrow—the one they’d both known for years but never spoken about. The second’s fingers tightened, then released, as if confirming something long buried. Outside, the distant wail of a siren cut through the silence. They didn’t move. Didn’t need to.

Silence. Then—*understood.*
