A winding trail through overgrown meadows, sunken between crumbling stone walls, where moss-covered stones mark abandoned footprints. The path splits into no apparent path, one fork leading to a wooden bridge rotting at the center, the other disappearing into dense bracken, its end swallowed by a gnarled oak’s roots. Fog rolls in from the east, obscuring the horizon, and the air smells of damp earth and rust. A single, faded map pinned to a rusted metal fence shows no destination—just a series of question marks. The only sign of life is a rusted bicycle frame leaning against a fence post, its chain broken, its seat covered in dust. The wind howls through the trees, carrying no answer.
