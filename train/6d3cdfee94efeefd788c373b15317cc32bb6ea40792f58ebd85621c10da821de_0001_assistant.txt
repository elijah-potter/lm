The tone of this poem is melancholy, or perhaps, yearning is a better word. The speaker in the poem reached a place where a decision must be made. The speaker made that decision and is now looking back on the decision. The speaker identifies that the other choice was left for "another day", and understood that it was likely that a second chance would never come. The line, "I doubted if I should ever come back" shows  this foresight. This speaker also knows that someday they would tell the story "with a sigh." Finally, the speaker identifies that the choice that was made "made all the difference" but we never know as readers what the difference was. Was it right or wrong? Good or bad? Only the speaker in the poem knows.
