Preparing for a 5K race can be a great challenge for anyone, especially those who are just getting started with this form of strenuous exercise. A 5K run is a great distance for a beginner, but you can get ready and prepare yourself for success in as little as two months. 

If you are only getting started with this type of exercise, you must make sure you start out slowly. Begin at a slow pace and exercise for short times, such as a few short walks that are spread throughout the day. Work your way up to moving faster during each walk and increase your duration as your body adjusts to the new activity. Then, start the 5K training schedule once you are capable of exercising for 30 minutes at a time. 

A good 5K training schedule allows you to spend some of your time walking and sometimes running, cycling, or rowing. As the week progress, you will need to gradually increase your time spent running and decrease your time spent walking. 

One day each week, while you are training, should be the day that you take a break from exercise. This gives your muscles enough time to fully recover. 

During week 1, you should run or walk for thirty minutes. After you take a day to rest. The next day you should run or walk for three miles. Then rest the following day or simply walk. 

For week 2, you will do the same, running or walking for thirty minutes, for four of the days, but increase your run or walk from 3 miles up to 3.5 miles. 

During week 3, you can keep the same pace or increase your miles. 

During week 4, increase your miles up to four. 

During week five, you can keep the same pace or increase your miles. 

During week six, you should increase your miles up to 4.5. Week 7 will be when your 5K race is scheduled.
