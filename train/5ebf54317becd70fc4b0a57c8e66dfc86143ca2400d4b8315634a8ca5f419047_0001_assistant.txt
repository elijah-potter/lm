- Bichon Frises are fluffy, white, toy-sized dogs with playful and mischievous personalities. 
- Cavalier King Charles Spaniels are known for their long, silky hair, floppy ears, and short snouts, as well as their intelligent personalities. 
- Chihuahuas are some of the smallest toy-sized dogs and generally don't weigh more than six pounds. 
- Havanese dogs are the national dogs of Cuba, and they have very happy dispositions.
- Japanese Chin dogs are small and sensitive and sometimes considered catlike.
- Maltese dogs are sweet, small, and fluffy - kind of like real-life teddy bears. 
- Miniature Dachshunds are German "badger dogs" with spunky personalities. 
- Miniature Pinschers are small yet very energetic dogs that typically don't surpass one foot in height. 
- Pekingese dogs once accompanied the ruling classes in China but now are cuddly lap dogs. 
- Poodles are a popular toy breed that is very intelligent and active. 
- Pugs are toy-sized dogs known for their wrinkly, short snouts. 
- Shih Tzuhs are classic lap dogs with long fur and homebody personalities.
- Toy Fox Terriers are sturdy and active dogs, but they are still somewhat low-maintenance.
