**Subway platforms** – Due to the constant stream of strangers, people often look at their phones or downwards, avoiding direct eye contact with others.

**Public restrooms** – Privacy is prioritized over social interaction, leading to minimal eye contact.

**Corporate office cubicles** – Work culture and noise can discourage natural communication, fostering indirect glances or side-eye.

**Dark alleys or poorly lit areas** – Fear of judgment or anonymity leads to lowered gaze.

**Highly competitive environments (e.g., sports arenas, corporate boardrooms)** – Focus shifts inward, making eye contact feel tense or unnatural.

**Online gaming lobbies** – In virtual spaces, participants often stare at screens or chat, avoiding physical eye contact.
