The dim glow of a streetlamp flickered as a sudden gust of wind sent a loose newspaper fluttering across the sidewalk. A woman, mid-step toward her parked bike, stumbled slightly—her heel caught on a loose cobblestone—but caught herself with a sharp exhale. Her hands gripped the bike frame tighter as she glanced up, eyes wide, realizing the man in the shadows had just materialized from behind a parked van. His smirk was sharp, fingers twitching toward the knife she hadn’t noticed tucked in her coat pocket. The bike clattered to the ground as she froze, heart hammering, while he stepped forward, voice low and smooth. "You look like you’ve got something you shouldn’t have, sweetheart."
