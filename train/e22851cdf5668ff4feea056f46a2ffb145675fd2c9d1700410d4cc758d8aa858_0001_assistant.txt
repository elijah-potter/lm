1. Killer Klowns from Outer Space (1988) - When clown-like aliens land on Earth and begin harvesting humans in pink puffs of cotton candy, a cop and a group of college kids have to figure out a way to send the murderous extraterrestrials back to outer space. 
2. Night of the Demons (1988) - Angela is throwing a Halloween bash for her friends at a notoriously haunted house. Unfortunately, the ten teenagers unleash a demonic presence that’s starved for their souls. 
3. Pumpkinhead (1988) - After a heartbreaking accident leaves his son dead, a man seeks out a witch to conjure a demonic monster to reap vengeance on the teens that killed his little boy. 
4. Basket Case (1982) - Duane is a seemingly normal guy, living in a hotel room with his wicker basket in tow. What’s in the basket, however, is his severely mishappen twin brother, Belial. The pair wreak havoc on those that have wronged them. 
5. Parents (1989) - Set in all-American 1950s suburbia, Michael is a young boy that begins to suspect that his parents are homicidal cannibals. 
6. Night of the Comet (1984) - When a passing comet wipes out most of humanity, teenage sisters Regina and Samantha are left to fend for themselves against leftover zombies and a team of menacing scientists. 
7. Return of the Living Dead (1985)- When a deadly gas is released into the air above a cemetery, the resulting toxic rains bring the dead back as cannibalistic zombies. =
8. Sleepaway Camp (1983) - Violent and tragic accidents take place shortly after the arrival of a shy, unassuming young girl at Camp Arawak.
