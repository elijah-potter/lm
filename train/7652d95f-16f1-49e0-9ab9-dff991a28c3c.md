The dim glow of a cracked fluorescent bulb cast long shadows across the worn linoleum as Elias crouched in the back corner of the abandoned bookstore, fingers tracing the worn spines of discarded tomes. His breath fogged in the cold air, but he didn’t dare move—each creak of the floorboards could mean someone watching. The scent of old paper and damp wood clung to the space, thick and familiar, like a second skin.

*"It’s here,"* he muttered, though no voice echoed in reply. His gaze darted to the far corner where the rafters sagged under decades of neglect, where a single, half-buried journal lay half-hidden beneath a stack of yellowed newspapers. He reached out, fingers brushing the brittle edges, and the pages rustled as if whispering.

*"Just hold on."* The words were his own, but the voice—smooth, familiar—came from the darkness beyond the flickering light. Elias stiffened, heart hammering. The answer was here. *It had to be.* He pulled the journal toward him, thumb tracing the faded ink of a map, its lines twisting like veins in a map of something ancient and forgotten. The last entry read: *"The silence speaks."* His pulse quickened. He turned the page. *"The hunger waits."*
