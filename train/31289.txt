Во время устного экзамена по иностранному языку, вам могут предложить высказаться по теме.
Сочинение на тему Let’s talk about family traditions
In general, traditions are what we think is good for us. Some people found themselves in a situation which is similar to yours and they successfully found the best way out of it. Other people tried it out and it turned out to work like a charm for them as well. The old taught it to the young and so on and so forth. In the end the once new method of dealing with a problem became what we call a tradition. It passes down from one generation to the next one. No one questions its wisdom. Everything is changing in the world apart from true traditions of the past. They are applicable now and will be useful in the future too. Family traditions are a set of actions which sets one family apart from another one. They identify members of it as a community which gives any person a sense of security. That is why we praise our families and the traditions kept alive so highly.
