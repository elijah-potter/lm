The towering ancient spire loomed over a cracked earth where the last embers of a forgotten city glowed like dying stars. Rusted iron gates stood half-buried in the dust, their once-great inscriptions now illegible. The air smelled of damp stone and something metallic, faintly sweet. Shadows stretched long across the cracked pavement where once bustled crowds, now only a few skeletal figures moved in slow, deliberate circles, collecting debris from the ground. A child’s laughter echoed, brief and sharp, before fading into the wind. A storm rolled in from the east, lightning splitting the sky as a distant, rhythmic drumming sounded—thunder, perhaps, or the distant approach of travelers. The wind carried the scent of rain and decay.
