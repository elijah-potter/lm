1. Alexis always wanted to be like her older sister Stephanie. Stephanie was popular; she played soccer and loved to do her makeup.

2. So, to copy her sister, Alexis played soccer, learned to do her makeup, and made as many friends as she could. Nonetheless, Alexis was so wrapped up in being like her sister– she didn’t know who she was herself.

3. One day, as she talked to her sister, Stephanie told Alexis she always wanted to be like her. Alexis was so confused. Why did her sister want to be like her?  Stephanie told Alexis that she always admired her confidence and authenticity. Alexis was shocked she didn’t realize this was what her sister thought of her.  

4. That day, Alexis realized that the best thing she could ever be was herself. After this conversation, Alexis stopped trying to be Stephanie and finally became comfortable and happy with who she was.
