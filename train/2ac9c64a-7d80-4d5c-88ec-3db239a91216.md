The storm hissed outside.
Rain hammered the roof.
Darling, I need your help.
He knelt in the dark.
Her hands trembled as she held the letter.
The clock struck midnight.
A single scream—too loud for dawn.
The door creaked.
Blood dripped from the floor.
She pressed her ear against the wall.
The air smelled of iron and fear.
His fingers found her throat.
Silence.
Then—
The lights went out.
