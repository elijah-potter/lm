Hello there! How is Nini doing? Elmo has a story for Nini about playing!  Elmo has lots of friends at the playground  -  the little babies are so cute!  Some of Elmo's friends are big like Elmo, and some are bigger! When Elmo plays, Elmo is always very gentle with the babies, and with the big kids too.  Elmo shares the swings, Elmo shares the slide, and when Elmo runs, Elmo is careful not to bump into another friend!  When Elmo was a baby, Elmo got hurt and Elmo's mommy took Elmo to the doctor.  But Elmo is getting bigger and bigger, and now Elmo jumps and runs and sings, like Nini!
