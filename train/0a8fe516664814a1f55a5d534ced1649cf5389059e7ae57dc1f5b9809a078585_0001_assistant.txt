Do you like trees?
Well, so do I.
Do you want to swim the sea?
So would I.

Sorry, that’s too bad—
The trees and seas are ruined.
And while it is sad,
It is thanks to your parents’ doings.

They saw the signs,
And looked away.
How time flies,
And tomorrow’s problems become today’s.

Luckily for you,
It is easier than ever
To start things anew
And aim to be better.
