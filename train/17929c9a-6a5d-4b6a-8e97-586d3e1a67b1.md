Once upon a time, in a colorful forest filled with tall trees and bright flowers, there lived a curious little bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around all day, exploring every nook and cranny of the forest.

One sunny morning, as Benny was hopping along a winding path, he saw something shiny at the edge of the clearing. It looked like a big, beautiful rainbow stone! Excitedly, Benny hopped closer to take a look.

As Benny approached, he noticed that the stone wasn't just any stone—it had magical powers! If you wished really hard while holding it in your paws, your wish would come true!

Benny was thrilled and quickly thought of what he'd wish for. He loved carrots more than anything, so he closed his eyes, held the rainbow stone tightly, and wished for a never-ending supply of delicious carrots.

Suddenly, a big bunch of crunchy, orange carrots appeared right in front of him! Benny couldn't believe it—he had made a magic wish come true!

But as he nibbled on his fresh carrots, Benny noticed something else. His best friend, Lila the squirrel, was looking sad sitting under a tree nearby. Benny remembered how much Lila loved to share stories.

Without hesitation, Benny hopped over to Lila and asked what was wrong. "I'm just feeling lonely," Lila sighed. "Mommy told me that today is my very first day at the big forest school."

Benny thought for a moment. He knew exactly how to make his friend feel better. With a twinkle in his eye, he held up the rainbow stone and wished for a fun adventure with all of their friends.

In an instant, colorful balloons appeared and started floating around them! Soon, Benny and Lila were joined by their other animal friends—Oliver the owl, Daisy the deer, and Milo the mouse. Together, they had the best time flying over the forest, laughing and playing games in the sky.

As the sun began to set, painting the sky with pink and orange hues, Benny realized that sharing happiness was the most magical thing of all. And from that day on, he always made sure to use his rainbow stone for good deeds, spreading joy wherever he went.

And so, Benny and Lila became best friends forever, creating wonderful memories in their magical forest home.
