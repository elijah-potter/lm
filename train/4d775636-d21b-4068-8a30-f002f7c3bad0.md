*"The wind howls through the empty street,
it whispers secrets in the night.
Again and again, it repeats its song—
*"The wind howls through the empty street."*

The sun rises, painting gold on the walls,
while shadows dance where the light falls.
Again and again, it repeats its song—
*"The sun rises, painting gold on the walls."*

A door creaks open, letting in the past,
a ghost of what once was, now cast.
Again and again, it repeats its song—
*"A door creaks open, letting in the past."*
