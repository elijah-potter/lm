PC gamer (PC)/Console gamer (C)

C: Consoles are so much better than PCs.
PC: No, they're not, and it's not even close.
C: Consoles are better because it's basically a computer in a console, but it's also much simpler to use.
PC: That's because PCs are meant to be customizable, so you can upgrade them to make them better over time. Consoles can't be upgraded; you're forced to buy a new console every year because games get better graphics and take up more space on the hard drive. If you buy a bigger hard drive for the console, you end up paying hundreds of dollars just for storage.
C: I would rather have a console. At least with a console, you can get access to many exclusive games, and with some consoles, you can get a game pass and have unlimited gaming for a monthly payment.
PC: You can do everything you just said with a PC, and you can also max out the graphics if you have a good graphics card installed.
C: Why would I want to upgrade a computer when I can have a full, ready-to-use console?
PC: PC gamers are better gamers, and they have better skills.
C: What are you talking about?
PC: PC gamers can outgun console players on first-person shooters.
C: That's not true.
PC: Yes, it is...keyboard and mouse players need to practice a lot to get good, so when they get good they can beat any console player.
C: I think many pro players use controllers rather than a mouse and keyboard.
PC: Pro players may use controllers, but they're playing on PCs.
C: Can you play Call of Duty: Warzone on PC?
PC: Of course, you can.
C: What about Elden Ring?
PC: Yes.
C: I'd still rather play on a console. What if you went on vacation somewhere and wanted to play games while you were away? You're just going to lug around your PC, monitor, keyboard, mouse, mousepad, cords, gaming chair, and desk with you?
PC: No, silly.
C: So how do you game on the go?
PC: You don't...you enjoy your vacation, and then you play games when you come back.
C: Exactly...you can't take it with you. Checkmate.
