• Known as the Golden State, California has a rich history of gold production in the 19th and 20th centuries.
• California is one of the largest states for mining, having produced roughly $4.7 billion worth of non-fuel minerals in 2020.
• California's history of discovering gold dates back to the late 1770s when it was a Spanish colony as a group of Spaniards found gold in Imperial County.
• James Marshall, a carpenter and sawmill operator, went down to the American River in Coloma and made a startling discovery in the bottom of a ditch; nuggets of pure gold.
• The California Gold Rush period officially ran from 1849 to 1855, 12 million ounces of gold had been extracted by miners.
• As of 2020, the mining industry in California has generated $7.3 billion in labor income, $13.6 billion in GDP, and 99,120 direct and indirect jobs.
