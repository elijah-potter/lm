• There was a greedy tortoise named Snow, who lived in a jungle with other animals.
• The tortoise could walk and run as fast as the other animals.
• Snow was idle and preferred to watch while other animals worked.
• One day, a fairy came with gifts to visit the queen of the jungle and needed help in locating the palace.
• Snow got to know that the fairy had gifts and became greedy for them.
• He gave the fairy false directions and led her to a lonely land.
• Snow snatched the fairy’s bag from her and began to run away.
• The fairy pronounced a curse on Snow and animals of his type to be slow forever.
• The curse took its effect and since that time till now, Snow and all the other tortoises have been slow.
