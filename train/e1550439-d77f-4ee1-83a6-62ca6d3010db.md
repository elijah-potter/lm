**[ANNOUNCEMENT]**
The city council has approved the **new downtown plaza** construction by **5 votes to 2**. Groundbreaking is scheduled for **September 15th**. Residents are encouraged to participate in the **Community Open House** on **October 3rd, 6–8 PM** at the former park site. For permits, visit **[cityhall.gov/plaza](https://cityhall.gov/plaza)**.

**[PROJECT UPDATE]**
Phase 1 of the **eco-friendly solar panel installation** at **Highway 12 Solar Farm** will begin next month. Expected completion: **November 1st**. Full capacity (50 MW) will be operational by **Q4 2024**. Community donations welcome via **[donate.solargrid.org](https://donate.solargrid.org)**.
