What are the indirect objects used with the verb Gustar?
The verb gustar literally means to be pleasing to but is understood in English as to like. An indirect object pronoun (me, te, le, nos, os, les) always accompanies gustar.
Why does Gustar use indirect object?
The indirect object precedes the verb gustar to indicate who is pleased.
What is the indirect object in Spanish?
The Spanish indirect object pronouns are: me, te, le in the singular, and nos, os, les in the plural. They can replace the preposition a (meaning to) + noun. Like the direct object pronoun, the indirect object pronoun usually comes before the verb.
How do you use Gustar verbs in Spanish?
To indicate who is doing the liking, we use one of the indirect object pronouns before the verb (me, te, le, nos, os, les). Typically we use just the two conjugations of gustar (gusta and gustan). The exception is when the object liked is a person.

How do you conjugate verbs like Gustar?
Here is the correct gustar conjugation:

How do you emphasize Gustar in Spanish?
Added Emphasis A mí me gusta la Pepsi. Me, I like Pepsi. Even though they are redundant, a ti is used together with te, and a mí is used with me, to put a stronger emphasis on the “you” and the “me.”

What is the difference between ‘to like’ and ‘gustar’ in Spanish?
Another big difference between English ‘to like’ and Spanish ‘gustar’ is in conjugation. In English, the verb is conjugated like most of the verbs ( I like, you like, we like etc.). In Spanish, however, the verb ‘gustar’ is conjugated according to the things we like.
