Some cartoon cat characters are cultural icons. Just mentioning their name or hearing their voice is enough to bring the beloved cat to mind. Out of respect to these cartoon greats, here are a few of my favorite cartoon cat characters.

Coming in at #1 is everyone's favorite, Garfield! Garfield springs from the pen and imagination of Jim Davis who named the big orange-striped cat after his grandfather, James Garfield Davis. Garfield reluctantly shares his home with his human, Jon Arbuckle, and a sweet but gullible dog named Odie. Garfield's three biggest hobbies are taking frequent naps, eating massive amounts of lasagna and sharing adventures with Odie. 

Spoiled, clever and more than a little cunning, Lucifer from Disney's Cinderella is our second favorite cartoon cat. His mommy, Lady Tremaine, makes no secret that Lucifer is her favorite and treats the big cat like a king. However, Lucifer isn't above tormenting Lady Tremaine's daughters or her step-daughter, Cinderella, and occasionally Lady Tremaine herself. The fluffy black cat's face, often set in a sneer, turns into a sinister grin when he's being very naughty!

Last but by no means least on our list is three-time Academy Award winner Sylvester J. Pussycat, Sr. Starring in more than 100 Looney Tunes and Merrie Melodies cartoons, this black and white Tuxedo cat spends his days sleeping and chasing other animals. Some of his favorite adversaries are Tweety Bird and Speedy Gonzales. Although he never catches Tweety or Speedy, he never gives up and is always setting traps to try and snare them.
