- **Annual communal gatherings** where participants perform symbolic actions (e.g., flag-raising, communal meals) without discussing their meaning.
- **Ceremonial artifacts** (e.g., painted drums, candles) left untouched, passed down through generations with no associated doctrine.
- **Fixed schedules** of repetitive activities (e.g., daily prayer hours, seasonal processions) performed mechanically, divorced from existential reflection.
- **Public displays** (e.g., masked dances, chants) that lack doctrinal content but maintain cultural continuity.
- **Sacred sites** visited for their historical weight, not spiritual significance, with offerings (e.g., flowers, coins) as formality.
- **Mandatory participation** in group rituals with no private interpretation, enforced by tradition rather than conviction.
