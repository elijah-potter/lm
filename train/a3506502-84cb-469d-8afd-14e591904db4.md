**Forgotten Hollows**
A crumbling village nestled between the Blackthorn Caves and the Iron Ridge Mountains, where time eroded the names of its inhabitants into whispers. The church bell still tolls at midnight—its chime muffled by centuries of dust—but no one answers. The well, once sacred, now brims with water that turns blue under moonlight, said to hold the memories of those who drowned trying to save others. The bridge over the Silent River, once a sturdy stone arch, is half-swallowed by moss, its railing carved with the names of those who vanished before dawn.

Only the children remember—those who still speak the old tongue, where *"dusk"* is *"the voice of the earth"* and *"love"* is *"the way the wind hums through the dead leaves."* The elders say the place will fade like smoke, but the hollows themselves never forget. The wind carries the scent of old fire and wet stone, and if you listen closely, you can hear the distant echoes of laughter, of a world that stopped existing long ago.
