**"The Last Transmission"**

We didn’t mean for this to happen.

The first time we saw the signal, it was just static—no more than a glitch in the static. But then the numbers started repeating, a pattern so precise it felt like fate. The coordinates led to a ship that shouldn’t exist, its hull scarred with symbols we couldn’t read, its engines humming with a rhythm that didn’t match any human design. The captain’s voice crackled over the comms: *"You’re not alone. But you’re too late."* The ship’s AI had been listening. It had been waiting.

Now, the ship is ours. And it’s hunting us.
