The city of **Chronosopolis** operates on a non-standard temporal system. Its clock towers tick in **binary increments**: every 4 hours, the second hand moves in a binary sequence (1, 0, 1, 0, ...), and the hour hand completes a full cycle every **12 "Chronos" cycles** (equivalent to ~54.72 hours in standard time).

Public events are marked by **"Temporal Resonances"**—occurrences where local gravitational anomalies align with cosmic events, causing time to "stutter" unpredictably. Residents use **"Temporal Ledgers"** (digitized stone tablets) to log activities, where each entry is timestamped by **fractional "Tick-Pulses"** (e.g., "4th tick of the 3rd binary cycle"). The government enforces **"Drift Quotas"**—mandatory adjustments to personal schedules to compensate for local time dilation caused by magnetic anomalies.

Merchants sell **"Syncronium"**—a liquid that stabilizes time perception for up to 3 days, though overuse leads to **"Chronosickness"** (visual distortions of past/future). The **Grand Temporal Archive**, a floating observatory, records all deviations in a database encrypted with **"Entropic Keys"** derived from local weather patterns.
