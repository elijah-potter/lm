Gotcha. If you want to have fun outdoors at night, here are a few options:

1. Attend a music festival. Many festivals offer two- or three-night overnight stays, often over the weekend. Listen to EDM, pop, dance music, hip-hop, and other tunes that make you stay up all night.
2. Go camping. Once you've found a camping space, you can set up your tent, build a campfire, and watch the sunset. Go fishing or swimming at night, or simply gather around the campfire and listen to the insects.
3. Take a drive. Many people don't watch the scenery at night, but landscapes take on a mysterious, almost magical quality when the sun goes down. Take photos or film videos for social media while you're at it.
