I'm a vanilla pound cake. I'm a classic cake that families love.

Patient:
Well, there's a woman who makes my batter by beating one cup of room-temperature butter and one cup of sugar together at high speed with an electric mixer until light and fluffy. She then adds four large eggs, one at a time, and beats well after each one. Then the woman adds two teaspoons of pure vanilla extract and half a teaspoon of salt. And with her mixer on low, she gradually adds two cups of flour, beating until just combined and being careful to not over mix.

Then the woman pours my batter into a 6-cup (8 1/2-by-4 1/2-inch) loaf pan that's been buttered and floured ahead of time and places me into a preheated 350-degree oven.

I'm baked for about an hour or until a toothpick inserted into my center comes out clean (she would use a tent with aluminum foil if I was browning too quickly). The woman then lets me cool in the pan for fifteen minutes, then inverts me onto a wire rack and turns me upright to cool completely.

And I come out dense, buttery, and loaded with flavor. She may even layer fresh berries on top of me.

Psychiatrist:
