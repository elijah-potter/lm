A half-built wooden cabin on a slope overlooking a frozen lake. The roof tilts at an angle, exposing half the interior to the wind, while the walls are plastered with exposed studs and gaps where windows were intended. The floor is uneven, with one corner raised by a missing joist. The kitchen has a stove with a cracked oven door and a countertop that’s been installed over a makeshift table. A ladder leans against the unfinished loft, half-ascended, with a toolbox tied to its bottom rung. The only light comes from a single, flickering bulb hanging from a wire strung between two supports, casting long shadows on the walls. The air smells of damp wood, old paint, and the faint scent of resin from unfinished repairs. The place is warm but quiet, the only sound a distant hum from the power generator.
