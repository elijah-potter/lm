Common Names Miner's lettuce
Flower Color White
Plant Type Herb
Introduced North America
This herb is sometimes grown as a salad or pot herb. It grows on dunes and waste ground of Pacific coast of North America.
The name, Miner's lettuce, is attributed to the fact that miners use to eat this plant for salad greens. Miner's lettuce
