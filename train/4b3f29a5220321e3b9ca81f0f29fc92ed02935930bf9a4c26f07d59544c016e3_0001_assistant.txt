The first battery was invented in 1800 by Volta. It stored and released a charge by chemical reaction instead of physically storing it. It was known as the voltaic pile. It was comprised of pairs of copper and zinc discs put on top of one another and separated with cloth or cardboard dunked in brine. The voltaic pile produced electricity and a stable current and did not lose much of a charge when not being used. Volta's early models could not produce sparks. After experimenting with different metals it was found that zinc and silver gave the best results.
