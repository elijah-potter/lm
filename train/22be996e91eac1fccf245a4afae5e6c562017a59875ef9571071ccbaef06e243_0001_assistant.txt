March 03, 1994

Dear Diary,

Today, for the first time, I felt the weight of the world in my heart. Of all the emotions I have ever experienced, this was the most intense. I think I fell in love.

It was like experiencing electrocution, but it came from inside me. Everything buzzed to the point I couldn't feel anything else.

All this time, I thought people in love were stupid and ridiculous. How can they act like do, just because of love? But now, I realize I have been the stupid one for failing to comprehend what love truly does.

I was sitting at the park, listening to Hole's album "Living Through This" when out of nowhere, my eyes met another's: a beautiful blonde girl passing through a field of sunflowers. Even if I wanted, I could not look away. My eyes had the determination of 100 lions stalking their prey, and they simply refused. She simply smiled and walked through the sunflowers and until she left my sight, time slowed and my heart beat faster.

I wish I had been courageous enough to run across the small river separating us and into that field, but at that moment, I was frozen. I couldn't even move a finger. Then, she disappeared past the sunflowers, much like the sun itself at night. I don't know if I will have the luck to see her again, but all I can think is how I wish to look upon her once more.
