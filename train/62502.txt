The German Flags were first adopted as the national flag of modern Germany in 1919.
Germany has two competing traditions of national colors, black-red-gold and black-white-red. They were also adopted by the German Democratic Republic. This is the flag most used by Germany’s national sporting teams.
Bavarian Flags are a way to represent the Bavarian Region of Germany. There is no specific code to this flag, as long as it has the blue and white design. There isn’t a specific number of whites or blues like in most flags. Therefore, the classic Bavarian Blue we offer in many of our Dirndls, this flag will help your German Heritage shine.
