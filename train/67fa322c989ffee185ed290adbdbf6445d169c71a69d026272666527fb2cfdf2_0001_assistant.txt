Awesome! As requested, here are ten fictional fish species:

1. Lunefish. Silvery fish with pink-and-yellow stripes which come to the surface at night.
2. Pips. Tiny fish who swim in large schools and have bright yellow tails.
3. Goldplumes. Fish with bright gold scales, silver eyes, and feathery fins.
4. Oonies. Almost microscopic fish which eat even smaller fish.
5. Pinktails. Pale pink fish that smell faintly like roses.
6. Ceesh. Red fish with silver fins that live in Midwestern ponds and lakes.
7. Kilfeeders. Fish that can digest almost anything organic, even substances toxic to other species.
8. Breadfins. A nutrient-rich fish and a staple in many cultures' diets.
9. Munas. Tiny fish that resemble specks of glitter.
10. Yurish. Bright yellow fish that grow pearl-like stones inside their small intestines.
