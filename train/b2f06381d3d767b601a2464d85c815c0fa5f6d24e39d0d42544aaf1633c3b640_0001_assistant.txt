1. Blue: Blue is a commonplace color among brands because it exudes dependability and trustworthiness and naturally tends to put people at ease.

2. Red: Red is often used in branding because red's boldness naturally captures attention. It represents energy, passion, and urgency. When brands want people to take action more urgently, they may incorporate red into their branding.

3. Green: Green can be incorporated into branding to induce impressions of prosperity, growth, and success. Green is often used to publicize new products or to associate with environmental, health, and wellness brands.

4. Purple: Purple can be chosen as a brand color to convey elegance, luxury, and curiosity. Brands in the design, luxury, and technology sectors can utilize purple to lure customers with its association with mystery.

5. Yellow: Yellow is a popular choice for brands that want to exhibit positivity, energy, and optimism. Industries and sectors like fitness, digital, and budget brands use yellow to show warmth and inspiration.
