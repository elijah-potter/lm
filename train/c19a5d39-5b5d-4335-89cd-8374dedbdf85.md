The rain had just begun to subside when the old clock tower chimed midnight, its sound echoing through the abandoned market square. Shadows stretched long across the cobbled streets, where flickering lanterns cast unnatural hues over the ruins of stalls long since forgotten. A lone figure in a patched coat stood near the skeletal remains of a collapsed archway, fingers tracing the weathered edges of a half-finished mural depicting a forgotten battle. Their breath fogged in the cold air as they adjusted the last stroke of their brush, the ink bleeding into the damp plaster. The wind carried whispers of the past—some human, others unidentifiable—from the cracks in the stone. The figure’s coat rippled slightly, though no one else was near, as if a presence lingered just beyond the veil of dusk. Outside, the distant wail of a siren cut through the quiet, but no one rushed to investigate. The square remained eerily still.
