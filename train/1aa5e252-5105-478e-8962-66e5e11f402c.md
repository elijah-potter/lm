The **Eclipse Café** in the old mill district. It’s a dimly lit, second-floor space with mismatched chairs, flickering LED lamps, and a coffee machine that only brews when someone whispers *"brew"* in the dark. The walls are covered in newspaper clippings from the 1980s and 2000s—ads for abandoned clubs, lost pets, and "mysterious disappearances." No one knows why they gather here, but the regulars (a mix of retirees, freelancers, and a few young people who’ve been there since before the Wi-Fi was installed) always find a seat. The air smells like stale espresso and something faintly metallic. Some nights, the door creaks open at midnight, and no one comes out. The others just sit, sipping tea, and wait.
