To My Dear Friend, 

I can’t believe it’s finally time to say goodbye to you after all these years. Thirty years of friendship, can you believe it? We have seen our fair share of ups and downs, of love and loss, of beauty and madness. 
You were with me through absolutely everything, and I hope you feel as though I was there for you. 

From pre-teen dramas to college parties, one-night stands, and great loves, from eating takeout in our first shitty apartment to walking down the aisle when the other got married, you have been by my side for every experience I’ve ever had. 

We have become one, a team against the entire world, the best kind of friendship that the world has to offer. 

Although I’ve never really been good at goodbyes, I know that I need to do this., to say goodbye to you. I think it may be the hardest thing I’ve ever done. 

But before I do, I want you to do something for me. I want you to promise me that you won’t let my death be the end of your life. Keep living! 

Be there for my kids as they get married and have babies, give your kids hugs from their favorite aunt every day, dance to our songs, and always remember what an incredible friend and person you are. Even though I’m not here to remind you. 

You are, and will always be, my soul mate. But now, it’s time for my soul to leave this body and travel to another place. Wherever that may be, I’ll be thinking of you. And I will be with you in your life, forever. 

Love, 
Your Soul Sister
