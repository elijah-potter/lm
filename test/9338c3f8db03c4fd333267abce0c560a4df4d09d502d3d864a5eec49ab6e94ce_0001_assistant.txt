The human body has five types of bones, these are: 
    1. Flat bones: These help protect internal organs such as the pelvic organs, heart, and brain. 
    2. Long bones: They help support the body weight and allow movement. 
    3. Short bones: These bones help provide stability and support movement. 
    4. Irregular bones: They feature irregular shapes and help protect internal organs. 
    5. Sesamoid bones: These are bones embedded in tendons to protect the small muscles from stress and wear.
