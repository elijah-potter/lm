A quiet afternoon in a cramped office, the fluorescent humming like distant thunder. Keys rattled on the keyboard—each one a fragment of logic, half-assembled until the moment a single line of code resolved itself into something tangible. The error messages had been like a foreign tongue, gibberish at first, then patterns emerging through repetition. Then: *that one line*, a whisper of clarity in the static.

She leaned back, fingers still trembling, as the terminal displayed a clean output. The data visualized itself: a graph, smooth and unbroken, where before it had been jagged, a needle pointing toward an answer that had been there all along, waiting for the right perspective.

Outside, the city exhaled—another day of half-understood chaos. Inside, the screen glowed. The moment had passed, but the relief was immediate, visceral. Not understanding, now: *meaning*.
