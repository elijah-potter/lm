**"AdminVacay"**

**Duration:** 48 hours (2 days)
**Purpose:** Mandatory biennial reset of all administrative systems, workflows, and employee documentation.
**Key Features:**
- **Automated Document Purge:** All files older than 2 years are flagged for deletion.
- **"Forget Mode":** All previous year’s data (salaries, reports, etc.) is scrubbed from systems.
- **Zero Tolerance for Overdue Tasks:** All pending admin tasks must be completed during this period.
- **Team Retreat:** Mandatory offsite for 12 hours to "recharge" the admin brain.

**Rules:**
- No new projects initiated outside of this window.
- All emails must be archived and reclassified as "AdminVacay Status: Pending".
- Mandatory "Why Are We Doing This Again?" meeting before resuming.

**Incentive:** Employees who complete tasks during AdminVacay get an extra 24 hours of "mental vacation" (no meetings).
