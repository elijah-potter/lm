Day in and day out, the same woman wears the same uniform pouring coffee into identical cups as always. At 50 years old, she doesn't think she can change jobs because it'd be too late. That is until Mr. Roger sat down in a booth. The woman brought Mr. Roger a cup of coffee and asked if there was anything else he would like. He said no, but he would love to have company! Mr. Rodger told the woman that she didn't look happy with her job and that it is never too late to go back to school. Truth be told, Mr. Rodger was 52 and just completed his degree to enjoy the last ten years of his working career, and she realized it truly is never too late.
