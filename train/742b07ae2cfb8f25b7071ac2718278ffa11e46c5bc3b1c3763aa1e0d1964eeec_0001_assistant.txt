The author intended this to be read as a scary story because the children initially thought that the ghost that they call "Mother" is kind and thoughtful, and caring. She leaves a cup on each of their nightstands and they see it as a gesture in which she doesn't want them to get thirsty. They watch tv and see her inching her chair towards them sometimes even making it to the middle of the room. They see it as her just wanting to be close to them. Years later, after moving out they realize that the cups on the nightstands were the poisoned milk that the widow gave her children before hanging herself in the living room.  The chair that was moved to the middle of the room was the same chair she used to hang herself. What their perception was and the actuality of the ghost and her intentions is what makes the story scary and a little creepy.
