Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a curious bunny named Benny who loved to explore. One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to see a sparkling blue pond where a tiny frog named Freddie was practicing his jumps.

"Can you teach me how to hop like you?" Benny asked, wiggling his paws.

Freddie grinned. "Of course! First, you must stand tall and stretch your legs. Now, jump with all your might!"

Benny practiced until his paws got tired, but Freddie kept encouraging him. "Almost there! Look how high you can jump now!"

Soon, Benny could hop as high as Freddie! They played all afternoon, laughing and splashing in the pond. When the sun began to set, Benny and Freddie said goodbye.

"Come back tomorrow!" Freddie called.

Benny nodded happily and hopped home, dreaming of another adventure with his new friend.
