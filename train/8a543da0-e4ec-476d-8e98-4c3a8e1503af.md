A quiet café hummed with the sound of coffee machines and whispered conversations. A lone customer sat at a corner table, their hands still gripping a steaming mug, eyes fixed on a single, half-eaten slice of cake. Time seemed to bend around them.

The cake's crumbles dissolved into their lips, each one a slow, deliberate act. The sugar melted on their tongue, lingering. Their breath fogged the glass, but the air outside remained still—no breeze to stir the windowpane. The clock on the wall ticked, each second stretched into a heartbeat.

Across the room, a door creaked open. A shadow stretched long, then paused midway, as if caught in slow motion. The customer’s eyes followed the intruder’s silhouette, their pulse steady. The door swung shut, leaving a single, lingering *click*.

A whisper drifted from the booth—*"You’re late."* The words were soft, almost invisible. The customer exhaled, the breath curling into the room. Outside, the world beyond the café window blurred, colors shifting like a prism. A car passed, its wheels spinning in a blur of motion, yet the moment felt eternal.

The customer leaned forward, fingers tracing the rim of their mug. The last bite of cake vanished in a silent, savored moment. Time returned to its usual rhythm, but the memory lingered.
