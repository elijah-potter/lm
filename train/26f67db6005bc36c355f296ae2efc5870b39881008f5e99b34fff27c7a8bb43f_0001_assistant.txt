In the heart of Southeast Asia's embrace,
Lies a land of beauty, a heavenly space.
The Philippine archipelago, where dreams reside,
A tapestry of islands, nature's cherished pride.

Bounded by seas, a symphony of blue,
Philippine Sea to the east, and skies so true.
The Celebes Sea to the south, a tranquil dance,
Sulu Sea's embrace, a passionate romance.

To the west and north, the South China Sea,
A cradle of wonders, where treasures be.
Like a triangle, the islands are arrayed,
From Palawan's south, where stories cascade.

Mindanao, the pearl in the southern skies,
With the Sulu Archipelago, a paradise arises.
Batan Islands to the north, a crown so high,
Luzon's apex, touching the azure sky.

Historical tales unfold, the archipelago's past,
With over 7,000 islands, memories are cast.
But wait, more secrets did time unveil,
500 uncharted gems, nature's grand scale.

Stretching north to south, a majestic range,
1,150 miles, a captivating exchange.
East and west, its reach takes flight,
700 miles wide, a breathtaking sight.

Taiwan graces the north, a neighbor's charm,
Borneo lies south, Palawan's arm.
Indonesia's eastern islands, a journey afar,
Mindanao's companion, beneath the evening star.

Islands and islets, some known, some untold,
Only a fraction named, their stories unfold.
350 with areas, a mile or more to embrace,
Carrying the essence of this enchanting place.

Luzon, Mindoro, Palawan standing tall,
Visayas' jewels, a treasure trove for all.
Bohol, Cebu, Leyte, and more in between,
Masbate, Negros, Panay, Samar's serene.

In the southern embrace, Mindanao's call,
With landscapes diverse, a paradise for all.
This land of wonders, with nature's grand show,
Philippine archipelago, where dreams ebb and flow.

So let us celebrate this blessed land,
With poetry's voice, let our hearts expand.
For in this tapestry of islands, we find,
The spirit of the Philippines is forever intertwined.
