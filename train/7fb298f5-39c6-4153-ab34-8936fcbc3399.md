A crumbling stone archway in a quiet village square, its weathered surface etched with faded graffiti and cracked by centuries of rain. Beneath it, an old well with rusted iron rungs, its water now stagnant, holds reflections of long-departed faces. The moss-covered tombstones in the nearby cemetery whisper secrets through the wind, while a half-buried lantern’s glass still holds a faint, eerie glow from years past. Shadows of children playing tag and adults gossiping linger in the gaps between the stones, preserved in time as if paused on film.
