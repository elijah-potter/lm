 Step-by-step guide on how to drive a manual truck:

1 Familiarize yourself with the controls: Understand the clutch pedal, gear shifter, accelerator, and brake.

2 Start the truck: Press the clutch, move the gear shifter to the first gear, and gradually release the clutch while applying gas.

3 Shift gears: Press the clutch, move the gear shifter to the appropriate gear, and release the clutch gradually while giving gas.

4 Master hill starts: Use the handbrake or hill-hold feature to prevent rolling backward on inclines.

5 Downshift on descents: Downshift to lower gears when going downhill to maintain control and prevent brake overheating.

6 Practice and be patient: Develop the necessary skills and muscle memory through continuous practice.

7 Prioritize safety: Follow traffic rules and stay aware of your surroundings.

Remember, practice is key to becoming comfortable and confident in driving a manual truck.
