Some kids can't eat nuts from places here in Canada and other places, and sometimes adults can't eat them either. If those people eat nuts, they could be in serious trouble, even if it's just a really, really small amount, or if the nuts touch something else. For really little kids, the doctors can't do anything until they get older. The place called Lurie Children's has done a lot of practicing to help people not get hurt by nuts since the year 2012. The practice is for everyone, other kids, and adults too.
