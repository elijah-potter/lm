Here are a few ideas to get you started: 
1. A cat-sitting and boarding center. Most boarders only accept dogs or don't provide adequate space for cats to be boarded well. Providing a specific space for cats only will hit a niche that may need to be serviced!
2. A business offering cat-training lessons for first-time cat owners to ease their nerves and provide them with essential information to go into their first cat-owning experience with ease.
3. A mobile cat grooming/nail trimming company for clients that have difficulties getting their cats to leave the house or stay still in the vet's office.
