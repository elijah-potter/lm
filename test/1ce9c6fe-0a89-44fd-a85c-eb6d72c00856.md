**BREAKING:** Mayor’s Office Warns of "Unprecedented Flooding" in Lakeview After Sudden Storm Front

Residents of Lakeview reported severe flooding along the Riverbank Parkway after a rapid intensification of a thunderstorm system brought torrential rains—nearly 12 inches in just three hours. Emergency crews deployed sandbags and temporary barriers at the downtown waterfront, but overflow from the city’s aging sewer system left at least 15 blocks under water. The mayor declared a local emergency, activating the National Guard for debris removal and road clearing. Authorities advise avoiding the affected areas, with sirens blaring near the old mill district. Schools and businesses in the downtown core remain closed until further notice.

No fatalities have been reported, but at least 47 cars were stranded in submerged neighborhoods, and three bridges on the interstate loop remain impassable. The city’s water treatment plant is operational, but officials warn of potential contamination risks until testing is completed. Residents are encouraged to use bottled water until further notice. Meanwhile, volunteers from the local Red Cross are distributing emergency supplies, including food, blankets, and medical kits, to displaced families in community centers. The National Weather Service has issued a **Severe Weather Warning** for Lakeview through midnight tonight, with a second storm front expected to pass through tomorrow afternoon.
