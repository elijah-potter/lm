The painter known for the artwork 'The Starry Night' is Vincent van Gogh. It was painted in June 1889 and portrays the view from the east-facing window of his asylum room in Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, just before sunrise." The Starry Night" has gained significant recognition and has been part of the permanent collection at the Museum of Modern Art in New York City since 1941, acquired through the Lillie P. Bliss Bequest.
