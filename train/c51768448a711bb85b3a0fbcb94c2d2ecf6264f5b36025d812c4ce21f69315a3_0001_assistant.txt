1. Find a thrift store that you're interested in shopping at.
2. Once inside, find an area where you might find items you want to buy.
3. While shopping, pay attention to the quality of the items available for purchase. Some items might be more worn than others. For example, some clothing might have thinner fabric or holes.
4. Check for discounts. Some stores offer weekly discounts based on the tag color.
5. Head to the counter to check out.
