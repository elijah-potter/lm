A hidden alleyway in an old town where the cobblestones shift underfoot, forming unspoken paths that only the experienced navigate. The air hums with the weight of tradition—every shadow tells a story, every silence carries a rule. The lanterns flicker at unmarked intervals, their glow tracing the boundary of permissible behavior. The street dogs, not dogs but something older, judge silently, their barks soft and precise. The clock tower’s chime isn’t a timekeeper but a reminder: *some things are never spoken aloud.* The wind carries the scent of damp stone and the faintest trace of iron—from a door that should never be opened.
