A bustling port city along a tidal river where cargo ships unload at high tide and lock cargo onto barges for inland transport during low tide. Markets and dockside stalls thrive during peak hours, while residential neighborhoods on higher ground experience minimal foot traffic outside rush hours. Daily commuters, workers, and tourists alternate between urban centers and coastal villages, creating cyclical population surges. The rhythmic ebb and flow of waves mirror human movement patterns, with seasonal variations in activity intensity.
