Flickering neon veins pulse through the skeletal city, casting jagged shadows on weathered brickwork. The moon, a cracked mirror, bends light into fractured trails across the cobblestone—somewhere between daybreak and dusk. Raindrops refract glassy rivers, bending time into liquid gold and emerald. Hollow alleys glow with bioluminescent moss, soft as a forgotten hum. Streetlights hum like dying stars, their light diffused through stained glass into prismatic dust that swirls in the damp air.
