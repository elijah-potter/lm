Of course! Here's a list of some of the cheapest places to rent in California:
1. Arcata. Nestled in Humboldt County in NorCal, Arcata is a small college town that combines youthful energy with a serene setting. The average rent for a one-bedroom apartment is $770, making Arcata one of the most budget-friendly California towns.
2. Fresno. Fresno is right in the heart of Central California. Head 2.5 hours west and you'll be at the beach, or venture 3-4 hours away and you can be in San Francisco or Los Angeles! Fresno is a great base location if you want to explore all that California has to offer. You can find a one-bedroom apartment in Fresno for about $900.
3. Merced. Merced is a sleepy little town that is also located in Central California. Its proximity to Yosemite is its most valuable asset, drawing nature lovers and hikers from miles around. One-bedroom apartments go for around $925 in this small town. 
4. Glendale. With an average monthly rent of $1,675, Glendale jumps in price a bit but it's worth it. Just north of Los Angeles, this town gets you close to all of the action happening in the city without completely blowing the budget. There are plenty of ethnic eateries, shops, bars, and more to be explored.
5. Redlands. If you're looking for a family-friendly town in Southern California, Redlands is a great option. It boasts a low crime rate, an exceptional school district, and a tight-knit community. The average rent for a one-bedroom apartment is $1,795.
