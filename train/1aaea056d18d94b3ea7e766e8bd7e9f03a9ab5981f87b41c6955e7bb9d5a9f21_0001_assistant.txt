Here are some suggestions for what to get your Mom for her 60th Birthday, including some things she can do:

1.  A gift certificate for a massage: You could give your mom a gift certificate to a day spa where she can get a massage or another beauty service such as a facial.
2.  Yoga or exercise class: If your mom likes to exercise, she might enjoy taking yoga or exercise classes at a local studio or gym.
3.  A short trip:  You could plan a weekend getaway at one of your mom's favorite places. Send her away with your dad or another friend, or go with her yourself!
4.  Wine tasting: If your mom likes wine, you could plan an excursion to a vineyard for a wine-tasting tour. 
5.  Digital Photo Frame: Your mom might enjoy a digital photo frame that can be continually updated with family pictures.
