**Thresholds of the Forgotten**
A crumbling archway in a courtyard of crumbling stone, its jagged edges slick with age. The air hums with static electricity from rusted metal fixtures, a whisper of forgotten voices trapped in the damp. Beyond lies a corridor where walls breathe—peeling paint reveals layers of time like peeling skin. The floor is a mosaic of cracked tiles, some stained crimson, others faded to gray, as if someone once walked here and never returned. A single candle burns in a broken glass holder, its flame flickering erratically against the eerie glow of an old oil lamp. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, dancing between the gaps of the half-erected walls, as if waiting for someone to cross. The scent of damp earth and something metallic lingers—blood? Rust? The air thrums with a low, pulsing frequency, like a heartbeat beneath the threshold.
