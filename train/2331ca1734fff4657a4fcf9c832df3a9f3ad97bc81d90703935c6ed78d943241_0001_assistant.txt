Sounds like you've got some big ambitions. Don't worry: every influencer started from the bottom. Here are some ways you could skyrocket to the top:

1. Find your niche in the fashion and makeup world. What makes you different from the thousands of other Instagram influencers? Maybe it's your style, hair, or makeup looks. Don't be afraid to experiment!
2. Add hashtags to every post. This helps people find you and follow your account. Make sure they're relevant: people might block you if they see makeup photos in the "#food" hashtag.
3. When you start to earn a following, message other influencers and talk about collaborating. You'll reach their audience and vice versa.
4. Encourage your followers to engage with you by asking questions, hosting live chats, and doing giveaways. They'll see you as a friendly part of the community, not a distant advertiser.

Have fun on your journey to stardom!
