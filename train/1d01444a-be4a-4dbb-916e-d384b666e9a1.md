The river whispered its endless song,
*"I am the same, I am the same, I am the same,"*
as it carved through the stone,
while the reeds nodded in its name.

The clock struck twelve on the same old night,
*"Tick-tock, tick-tock, tick-tock,"*
echoing in the same dark,
as the echoes took flight.

She traced the same path every dawn,
*"Yesterday, today, tomorrow—same old sun,"*
and the sky always burned,
though the heart grew tired of the same.

*"Over and over, same as before,"*
the wind hummed low,
while the world kept spinning,
still, in its own slow core.
