## Project Chimera: A Cooperative Research Crisis

**A Game for 2-4 Players**

**Introduction**

Project Chimera thrusts you into the roles of specialized researchers at the Global Institute for Biological Containment (GIBC). A rapidly evolving, synthetic pathogen – Chimera – has been unleashed, exhibiting unpredictable mutation and demonstrating a horrifying ability to adapt to countermeasures. Your mission is to understand Chimera’s behavior, develop effective treatments, and ultimately contain the escalating global crisis before it overwhelms civilization. Success depends on your collaborative efforts, strategic resource management, and the willingness to make difficult choices under immense pressure.

**1. Components**

*   **Crisis Board:** A large, illustrated board depicting a world map divided into regions, each with varying levels of infection and research potential.
*   **Researcher Cards (12):** Each card represents a unique researcher with specialized skills (e.g., Virologist, Geneticist, Epidemiologist, Logistics Expert).
*   **Chimera Mutation Cards (40):** These cards dictate Chimera’s evolving traits, introducing new challenges and altering its behavior.
*   **Research Tokens (60):** Representing scientific progress in various fields (e.g., Viral Structure, Transmission Vectors, Treatment Protocols).
*   **Containment Tokens (30):** Used to mitigate infection spread in affected regions.
*   **Resource Tokens (90):** Representing Funding (blue), Personnel (yellow), and Equipment (red).
*   **Infection Cubes (100):** Used to track the severity of Chimera's spread.
*   **Event Cards (30):** Introduce unexpected complications and opportunities.
*   **Region Markers (12):** Used to denote specific regions with unique challenges or bonuses.
*   **Threat Level Tracker:** A track indicating the overall severity of the crisis.
*   **First Player Token:** Indicates the active player.
*   **Rulebook (this document).**

**2. Setup**

1.  Place the Crisis Board in the center of the playing area.
2.  Shuffle the Chimera Mutation Cards and place them face down as a draw pile. Draw the top three cards and place them face up beside the draw pile. These represent Chimera’s initial mutations.
3.  Shuffle the Event Cards and place them face down as a draw pile.
4.  Place the Threat Level Tracker at its starting position (Level 2).
5.  Place a predetermined number of Infection Cubes (see Difficulty Scaling below) in designated starting regions on the Crisis Board.
6.  Distribute Resource Tokens according to the Difficulty Scaling (see below).
7.  Each player chooses a Researcher Card and takes it.
8.  Determine the first player randomly and give them the First Player Token.

**3. Turn Structure**

Each player’s turn consists of the following phases, performed in order:

1.  **Researcher Action Phase:** You may perform two actions from the following options:
    *   **Research:** Spend Resource Tokens to place Research Tokens on regions. The type and amount of resources required depend on the region and the desired research focus.
    *   **Contain:** Spend Resource Tokens to place Containment Tokens in infected regions, reducing the spread of Chimera.
    *   **Move:** Move your Researcher to an adjacent region.
    *   **Gather Resources:** Collect Resource Tokens from your current region. The type and quantity depend on the region’s characteristics.
    *   **Share Resources:** Give Resource Tokens to another player in the same region.
    *   **Analyze Mutation:** Discard a Research Token from a region to draw and examine the top Chimera Mutation Card. This card remains secret from other players until the "Mutation Shift" phase.
2.  **Mutation Shift Phase:**
    *   If a player performed the "Analyze Mutation" action, reveal the examined Chimera Mutation Card. The effects of this card are immediately applied. These effects may alter Chimera’s traits, affect resource availability, or trigger new challenges.
    *   **Trade-Off Mechanic: Mutation Prioritization:** If multiple Mutation Cards are revealed, the team must collectively decide which one to address first. Discarding a Mutation Card requires spending specific Research Tokens. The order of resolution directly impacts the unfolding crisis, forcing players to weigh immediate threats against long-term consequences.
3.  **Chimera Spread Phase:**
    *   Roll a die. The result determines how many new Infection Cubes are placed on the Crisis Board, spreading from infected regions. The number of cubes placed may be modified by Chimera Mutation Cards.
    *   **Trade-Off Mechanic: Containment Focus:** When placing Infection Cubes, the team must decide which regions to prioritize for Containment Tokens. Insufficient containment leads to exponential infection spread, but over-committing resources leaves other regions vulnerable.
4.  **Event Phase:** Draw and resolve an Event Card. These cards introduce unexpected complications and opportunities, often requiring immediate action and strategic adjustments.
5.  **Pass the First Player Token:** The First Player Token passes to the next player clockwise.

**4. Win/Loss Conditions**

*   **Winning:** The players win if they successfully develop a "Cure" by accumulating a predetermined number of Research Tokens in the "Treatment Protocol" field across all regions (number varies by difficulty).
*   **Losing:** The players lose if:
    *   The Threat Level Tracker reaches its maximum level.
    *   A predetermined number of regions become completely overwhelmed by infection (number varies by difficulty).
    *   The Chimera Mutation Card draw pile is exhausted.

**5. Difficulty Scaling**

The game’s difficulty is adjusted based on the number of players and their experience level.

*   **Two Players:** Start with 10 Infection Cubes in starting regions. Receive 5 Funding, 3 Personnel, and 2 Equipment tokens at the start. The number of Research Tokens required for a Cure is reduced.
*   **Three Players:** Start with 15 Infection Cubes in starting regions. Receive 10 Funding, 5 Personnel, and 3 Equipment tokens at the start. Standard number of Research Tokens required for a Cure.
*   **Four Players:** Start with 20 Infection Cubes in starting regions. Receive 15 Funding, 8 Personnel, and 4 Equipment tokens at the start. Increased number of Research Tokens required for a Cure.

**6. Advanced Variants**

*   **Specialized Mutations:**  Introduce a deck of “Specialized Mutation” cards that trigger unique and devastating effects based on the current Chimera Mutation Card in play.
*   **Regional Bonuses/Penalties:** Introduce Region Markers that provide specific bonuses or penalties to research and containment efforts in designated regions.
*   **Hidden Objectives:**  Each player receives a secret objective at the start of the game, providing bonus rewards for achieving specific goals. These objectives may conflict with the team’s overall strategy, creating internal tension and strategic trade-offs.
    *   **Trade-Off Mechanic: Secret Agendas:** Players must balance their personal objectives with the team’s overall goals, potentially leading to difficult decisions and unexpected alliances.



**Designer's Note**

Project Chimera is designed to foster collaborative problem-solving and strategic decision-making under pressure. The mechanics are intentionally structured to present players with complex trade-offs and uncertain outcomes, mirroring the challenges faced by researchers confronting real-world crises. The game aims to encourage players to communicate effectively, prioritize resources, and adapt to evolving circumstances—skills that are vital for success in both scientific endeavors and broader societal challenges. The escalating nature of the crisis and the unpredictable behavior of Chimera are intended to highlight the importance of proactive planning, risk assessment, and the ethical considerations inherent in scientific research.
