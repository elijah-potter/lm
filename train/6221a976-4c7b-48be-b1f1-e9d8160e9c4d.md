Waiting is a quiet act of resistance—
a choice to surrender to time’s slow pulse,
to let the space between breaths hold meaning.
Somewhere, a door swings open not by force,
but by the patience of holding still.
The air thickens with the scent of forgotten decisions.
A single candle’s flame flickers,
not to guide, but to outlast the dark.
Waiting is not passivity; it is the alchemy
of what is not said, the weight of what is held.
The universe, in its infinite capacity,
watches as we decide to pause,
to let the world arrive on its own terms.
