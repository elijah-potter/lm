Here are three Mother's Day gift ideas for you:
1. A cookbook. Since your mom enjoys cooking, finding a new cookbook for her to use could be a fun way to incorporate one of her interests. If you wanted to get her something more, you could also give her a new apron, cooking utensils, or dishware to make the gift even more exciting!
2. A daily journal. As your mom likes to write, a daily journal could be a great gift for her. Journals can be used for a variety of things, such as discussing your day, recording your thoughts, or brainstorming ideas. This gift would be practical and fun.
3. A gift card to a nail salon. Since your mom enjoys getting her nails done, this gift would allow her to be pampered! This could be costly, so if you are looking for something a bit more affordable, you could also find some cool nail polish colors that she would love.
