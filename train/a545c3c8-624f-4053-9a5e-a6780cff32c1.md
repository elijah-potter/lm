The only movement is the slow, deliberate step of a lone *oryx* grazing on sparse grass, its hooves kicking up dust that spirals upward in a lazy spiral before dissolving into the haze.

A lone *elephant* has paused here, its massive form half-buried in the shade, its trunk extended toward the earth, sifting for roots. The rest of the herd is lost in the horizon, a faint ripple of movement.
