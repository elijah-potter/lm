U.S. companies are finding Chinese espionage harder to track. China is shifting its attacks to internet security services. Google researchers have recently discovered a new virus targeting Fortinet, a software security company. Chinese hackers used a "back door" to hack the company's system and uploaded malicious code to the company's security management tool. Charles Carmakal said "Organizations cannot identify these attacks on their own, Chinese attack campaigns can end as multi-year intrusions."
