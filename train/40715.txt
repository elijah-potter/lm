Marketing Management- Consumer Behaviour
Consumer behavior is the behaviour of the consumer which he/she exhibits while buying any good or service for their use. It is the study of how individual select, buy and use the goods and services to fulfill their needs. The product positioning, marketing strategies and facilitation of long-term customer relationship depends on Consumer Behavior.
Factors Influencing Buying Behaviour of Consumer:
Consumer behaviour is affected by the following factors:
- Cultural Factors: an individual develops the culture from the set of values, beliefs, perception, and behavior in which he is brought up. It includes factors like Culture, Social Culture, and social class.
- Personal Factors: Some personal factors like Age, Income, Occupation, Lifestyle, Financial status influence the buying decisions of a consumer.
- Social Factors: We live in an environment where we are surrounded by different people with different mindset and buying behaviour. When an individual tries to copy another person their buying behaviour changes. So there are many factors which affect the buying behaviour of consumers and they are Family and Reference Group.
- Psychological Factor: human psychology plays a very important role in framing the consumer's choices towards a good or service. Main psychological factors are Motivation, Perception, attitude and belief, learning.
The motive of Buying:
Motive is any factor that motivates the buyer to buy a product. So, the reason behind the purchase of any product or service is the customer’s buying motive.
Buying motives are of two types:
- Product Motive refers to the reason which motivates or push the customer to buy a product over the other. Like the design, shape, color, size, price, etc, are the factors which motivate a customer to buy the product.
- Patronage Motive includes those reasons which motivate a buyer to purchase a particular product from a specific outlet or shop over the other options. It is further divided into two i.e. rational and rotational patronage.
