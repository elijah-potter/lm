**1. The Forgotten Key**
The old lighthouse keeper found a rusted key in the barn. When he turned it, the door of the abandoned ship’s hold creaked open. Inside, a map of lost treasures glowed faintly. He followed the path to the island’s peak, where a chest lay half-buried in sand. Inside were coins of a king long forgotten, a compass that always pointed north, and a note: *"The key unlocks the past, but the past never stays."* He pocketed the treasure and returned to the shore, only to find the village in ruins—no one remembered him. The key, he realized, was the only thing that remained.

**2. The Weight of Time**
The lighthouse keeper’s discovery led him to the ship’s captain’s journal, detailing a voyage to a city where time was trapped in a bottle. He drank from it, feeling the weight of centuries pressed against his ribs. When he awoke, the village was untouched, but his hands were no longer his own—they moved in the rhythm of a clockwork heart. The key had not changed him; it had been the bottle all along. The journal’s final line whispered: *"Some doors open to ghosts. The wise do not stay."* He let the bottle fall into the sea, and the lighthouse burned out, its light swallowed by the horizon.
