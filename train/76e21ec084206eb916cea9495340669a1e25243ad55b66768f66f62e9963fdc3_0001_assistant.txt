The largest pyramid in the world is Tlachihualteptl, the Great Pyramid of Cholula in Cholula, Mexico. While Egypt's Great Pyramid of Giza is the tallest pyramid in the world, the Great Pyramid of Cholula has a total volume of 157 million cubic feet, almost twice the volume of the Great Pyramid of Giza. The Great Pyramid of Cholula was built between 300 BC to 700 AD as a worship center for Quetzalcoatl, the Aztec god of learning and books. It was eventually abandoned and covered by vegetation. The pyramid was discovered in the 1930s and excavated by archeologists over time.
