In the hollowed-out heart of an ancient library, where books whispered secrets older than man, Dr. Elias Vey found the manuscript that had eluded him for decades. The pages were not bound by ink but by something far stranger—a shimmering, iridescent substance that pulsed like a slow, deliberate heartbeat. On the final leaf, written in a script that defied comprehension, stood a single question:

*"When the last candle burns out, will the echo of our silence still echo back?"*

Outside, the rain tapped against the glass, but inside, the air was thick with the weight of forgotten answers. The quill had not yet dipped into the inkwell.
