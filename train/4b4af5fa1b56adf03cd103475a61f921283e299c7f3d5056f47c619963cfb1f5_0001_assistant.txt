If Gilligan from Gilligan's Island met Chrissy from Three's Company, assuming they were the same general age as when their respective television programs were on the air, they would probably get along well.

The characters had several things in common. Both were well-liked by their cliques, except for the dynamic between the Skipper and Gilligan (perhaps arising from the tension caused by the Skipper being Gilligan's supervisor). Another similarity is that neither character appeared to have a serious, long-term relationship. In addition, both were devoted to their friends, although they weren't very intelligent and things often did not go according to plan.

The major difference between the two characters relates to their physical appearance. Chrissy's entire identity was tied to her attractiveness. On the other hand, Gilligan was an average-looking man not pursued romantically by anyone; in fact, he seemed oblivious to the entire concept of physical attraction.

If the two characters were to meet, likely they would get along well. They had some commonalities, and the main way in which they differed, the attractiveness/desirability factor, might be something Chrissy would appreciate. Other men were attracted to her by her looks, so it could be refreshing for her to meet someone who honestly did not notice. Gilligan would probably just be happy to make another friend.
