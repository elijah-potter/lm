## Lost in the Record Store (Brooklyn, NY) (Williamsburg)

The afternoon light filtering through the dusty windows of Rough Trade felt particularly golden on Saturday. I was browsing the new arrivals, specifically searching for a reissue of a jazz compilation, when I noticed you. You were examining a stack of vinyl near the back, your brow furrowed in concentration, and you had a worn copy of Nick Drake’s *Pink Moon* clutched to your chest. There was a quiet intensity about you, a stillness that seemed to absorb the general hum of the store. I remember the faint, comforting smell of aged paper and leather from the records surrounding us, a scent that felt instantly familiar. We didn’t speak, not a word exchanged, but our eyes met briefly, and I felt a peculiar resonance, a silent acknowledgment. You then moved on, disappearing into the labyrinthine aisles. I hesitated, considered approaching, but ultimately didn’t. I’m hoping this finds you. 

If you were browsing vinyl at Rough Trade in Williamsburg this past Saturday and were holding a copy of *Pink Moon*, please reply with the name of your favorite Miles Davis album.
