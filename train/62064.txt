The weather on Norfolk Island is steady all year round. This beautiful mild climate usually sits between 10 to 26 degrees. Officially classified as a marine subtropical climate, Norfolk Island gets over 1300mm of rain every year. The majority of this rain falls in the months from April to August although any month can be a 'wet one'. The summer months are warm and long while the winter months from June to August are much wetter than the rest of the year but still with a mild climate.
Humidity is usually high, ranging from 74-79% at 9 am and 71-74% at 3 pm. Thunder storms most often occurs during winter and spring with roughly 12 storms each year. Norfolk Island weather also include on average 1 hailstorm each year too. Cyclones rarely hit the Island but sometimes influence weather conditions early in the year.
