A question that echoes involves a phrasing where the answer is contained within the question itself, often in a recursive or circular manner, forcing the responder to reflect back on their own words or assumptions.

For example:
*"What is the question that echoes back to itself?"*
or
*"If I ask, 'Why do I ask?' how should I respond?"*

These questions demand self-awareness and require the respondent to repeat, rephrase, or analyze their own input to arrive at a meaningful reply.
