A sharp ache in my chest as the door closed behind her,
the weight of the moment pressing like a forgotten key
in the hollow of my palm—words left unsaid,
half-formed shadows flickering on the walls.
The coffee cup sat cold, its steam now a ghost,
the city humming past, indifferent to my ache.
She was gone before the light could bend,
before the truth could settle into something tangible.
Clarity, like smoke, had dissolved into the air,
leaving only the echo of a voice—too soft,
too far.
