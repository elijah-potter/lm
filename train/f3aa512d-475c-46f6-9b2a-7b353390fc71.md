A hollow vow hung in the air like a broken glass,
its edges sharp with the weight of unseen burdens.
*"I’ll return by dawn."* The words were forged in haste,
now etched into dust beneath a midnight sky.
They were promises to a woman who didn’t ask—
just took them as they came, as if they were gifts.
But time is a thief that steals even the boldest,
and the clock moved forward while the heart stayed still.
The road was paved with roads not taken,
each detour a debt paid in silence.
The dawn came, but it wasn’t me—
only the echoes of a voice that didn’t sound like mine.
The promise was never mine to keep.
The weight of it now rests in the hollows between the teeth
of the woman who once believed in promises.
