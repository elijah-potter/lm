Once upon a time, there were two pups and a bone,
The brown dog was named Cory, and the yellow was named Dawn,
Both pups cherished and chased the bone, so much so,
They hated each other and would always let the other know,
"The bone is mine!" Cory yelled loudly,
Dawn laughed. "Nah, it's mine," he responded proudly,
They would fight and fight each and every day,
Over this everlasting bone that never went away,
"You'll never win!" Dawn cried,
"I'll be the last to steal it," Cory replied,
One day, they were having a tug-of-war with the bone,
When a cat named Andy came strolling along,
Andy looked at them in disbelief,
"Why are you fighting?" the cat shrieked,
"The bone is mine!" they both yelled at the same time,
Andy laughed and narrowed his glowing kitty eyes,
"But why do that when you could share?"
Both dogs stopped fighting and confusingly stared,
"What do you mean? How would we share it?"
Andy grabbed the bone and began to tear it,
"Like this!" the kitty yelled and broke it in half,
Handing a piece to each pup and they all began to laugh,
"Now everyone wins! Everyone gets the bone!"
They became the best of friends that day, Cory, Andy, and Dawn!
