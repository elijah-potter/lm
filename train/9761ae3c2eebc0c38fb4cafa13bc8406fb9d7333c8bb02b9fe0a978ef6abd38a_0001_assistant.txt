Nezuko stepped forward, and the proctor asked, "for posterity's sake, what's your skill?"

Nezuko replied with a haughty flip of her hair, "light magic."

She stepped forward to the testing range, where a collection of electronic dummy targets were used to collect data of the examinee's skills. Nezuko held out her hand and said, "Luminous Rays!"

A series of magic circles appeared around her, firing rays of light at the dummies. She simultaneously cast another spell. "Refractive Index!" she called out, and the targets were surrounded by magic circles that bounced the rays around, causing more destruction. 

Finally, the dust settled, and the dummies were demolished. They'd been pierced through and were falling apart. The proctor stared down the range, then at Nezuko. "I thought you were an A-class hunter?"

"That was when I was still hunting. I took half a year off for training before I turned eighteen," She explained, turning her nose up and enjoying the wild reactions of the other examinees. 

The proctor asked, "Was that your strongest spell?"

"Strongest? No. But I'm sure you know how much skill is required to simultaneously cast high-level precision magic. Of course, I didn't use my Valkyrie Armor, but it didn't seem necessary."

With a nod, the proctor remembered her job and wrote in her documents while staff came to replace the dummies that had been destroyed.

Nezuko cast a smug look at Kuroi and said, "I'm sure you feel discouraged going after me, but just try your best regardless. You can still get a good score, maybe."
