**Daily Schedule: Productive Routine**

---
**Morning:**
- **6:30 AM** – Wake up, hydration (water)
- **7:00 AM** – Light exercise (stretching/yoga)
- **7:30 AM** – Breakfast (nutrient-rich meal)
- **8:00 AM** – Deep work session (focused task)
- **9:30 AM** – Short break (walk, stretch)

**Midday:**
- **10:00 AM** – Meetings/collaboration (team tasks)
- **11:30 AM** – Creative work (writing/design)
- **12:30 PM** – Lunch (balanced meal)

**Afternoon:**
- **1:30 PM** – Admin tasks (emails, reports)
- **3:00 PM** – Project-specific work (deep focus)
- **4:30 PM** – Light review (planning next steps)

**Evening:**
- **6:00 PM** – Dinner (family/friends if applicable)
- **7:00 PM** – Relaxation (reading, hobby)
- **9:00 PM** – Wind down (no screens; journal)

---
**Flexible Notes:**
- Adjust time blocks as needed.
- Batch similar tasks (e.g., emails in 1 hour).
- Prioritize energy peaks (e.g., mornings for complex work).
