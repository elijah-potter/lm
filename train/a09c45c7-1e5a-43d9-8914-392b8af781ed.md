**THE VOID BETWEEN THE DARKENED LANDS**

The river ran black as ink, winding through the cursed city of **Vorthas**, where the air hummed with whispers from the dead. Across the plains, the **Ironclad Peaks** loomed like skeletal fingers, their peaks swallowed by perpetual mist. Beyond the **Whispering Woods**, where trees bore faces carved into bark, stood **The Hollow Spire**, a towering obsidian monument where time itself seemed to unravel.

**Path of Travel:**
- **From the North:** The **Blackthorn Pass** (treacherous, guarded by spectral wolves).
- **From the East:** The **Raven’s Crossing** (river of tears, where fish were men in disguise).
- **From the West:** The **Dusk’s Hollow** (a cave where the sun never rose, filled with echoes of futures past).
