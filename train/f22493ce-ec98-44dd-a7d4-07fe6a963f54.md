A sudden burst of golden-yellow light illuminated the overcast sky, casting long, jagged shadows across the cracked pavement. The air hummed with static, and the faint scent of ozone lingered. The once-dim, gray buildings now gleamed like polished marble under a fleeting aurora, while distant streetlights flickered violently before stabilizing into a crisp, clean glow. Shadows on the alley walls shrank and stretched erratically, morphing into abstract, dancing shapes before stabilizing into familiar silhouettes. The temperature plummeted by half a degree in an instant, and for a split second, the world felt both alive and unnaturally precise—then the light faded back into its usual, unremarkable hue.
