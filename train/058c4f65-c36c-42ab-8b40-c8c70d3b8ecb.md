The jagged cliffs of Veythar’s Obsidian Spire rise from a mist-shrouded fjord, their black glass-like surfaces reflecting neither light nor memory. Below, the Blackwater River carves through the earth in a frenzy of silver foam, its banks lined with skeletal trees that whisper secrets in the wind. The air hums with the static of ancient machines buried in the ground, their hums growing louder the closer you get to the edge. Here, the sky is a bruise—partly clouded, partly torn open by storms that never quite reach the land. The ground beneath is unstable, shifting underfoot as if waiting for something to fall through. Far below, the ruins of an old cathedral, its spires melted into the horizon, stand as silent sentinels. This is where the world feels like it’s holding its breath, just waiting to let go.
