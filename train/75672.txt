Concepts of Euphonium Technique
Concepts of Technique for Euphonium is a primary text from Dennis Askew, professor of tuba and euphonium at the University of North Carolina at Greensboro. This book is subtitles 36 Register Studies and is divided upon which range the pieces are designed to work on. The author guides the learner through several ways in which to approach each of these exercises based on dynamics and articulation. A wonderful addition to any fundamental program!
