**Incident Report**
**Case #:** INV-2024-001
**Date:** 2024-05-12
**Time:** 09:17 AM
**Location:** Main Street Park, Town Square (near the community garden)

**Description:**
Responded to a report of **excessive noise** from a group of **children playing a board game** at the community garden table. Complaints noted were from a nearby elderly resident observing from the library steps. Game involved **trivial rules** (e.g., "Take turns placing a plastic spider on a leaf" with no harmful intent). No physical altercation, no weapons, and no trespassing.

**Action Taken:**
- Advised the group to **reduce volume** (game noise was below legal decibel thresholds).
- Directed them to the **town council meeting** (scheduled for 3 PM) to discuss noise ordinance enforcement.
- Requested the library staff to **remind residents** of the 10:00 PM quiet hours (applied to the park).
- **No arrests** made. Case closed per low-priority complaint protocol.

**Follow-Up:**
- Community outreach: Distributed leaflets on noise ordinance enforcement.
- **Note:** Incident categorized as **"Social Behavior Complaint"** under Town Code §4B.5, "Minor Public Disruption."

**End of Report.**
