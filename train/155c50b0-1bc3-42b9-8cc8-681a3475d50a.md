AITA for insisting my sister return a borrowed wedding dress?

Okay, this is a bit of a tangled situation, and I'm honestly feeling drained by it. It started back in June 2018. My wedding was scheduled for October 14, 2018, at a vineyard in Napa Valley. I’d spent months searching for the perfect dress and finally found it at a boutique in San Francisco – a stunning, ivory silk gown with delicate lace detailing. It cost $3,500, and I paid for it upfront.

My younger sister, Clara (32), has always been… complicated. She’s incredibly stylish and often makes impulsive decisions. Leading up to my wedding, she repeatedly expressed admiration for the dress, hinting that she wished she could wear it herself. I brushed it off, assuming it was just sisterly envy. Then, a month before the wedding, she asked if she could borrow it for a friend's wedding in Seattle, scheduled for November 17, 2018. I was hesitant. It was a significant financial investment, and I was worried about damage. However, she was very persuasive, playing on my desire to make her happy and emphasizing how much the dress would mean to her friend. I foolishly agreed, stipulating that she be fully responsible for any damage and that it be professionally cleaned afterward. She signed a written agreement.

For two years, everything was fine. She wore it, it looked beautiful in photos she sent, and she had it professionally cleaned as agreed. Then, in early 2021, she told me she was planning a vow renewal ceremony in Charleston, South Carolina, for April 2023. She asked if she could borrow the dress *again*. I was immediately uncomfortable. Two times? I explained that it was a very expensive dress and that I felt uneasy about lending it out so frequently. She accused me of being selfish and controlling, saying I was trying to ruin her happiness. She brought up past grievances, things I'd completely forgotten about, and made me feel guilty for even questioning her. I caved, again signing a written agreement.

This time, however, things went sideways. Clara had the ceremony in April 2023. Afterward, she claimed the dress had a small tear near the hem, something she insists wasn’t present before. She says she reported it to the cleaner, but they denied responsibility. She now refuses to return the dress, arguing that I should cover the cost of repair, which she estimates will be around $800. She's been sending me passive-aggressive messages, implying that I'm being unreasonable and that she deserves to keep the dress as compensation for the "inconvenience" of the alleged tear. My parents are caught in the middle, siding with Clara and telling me to “just let it go” to avoid further family drama. My husband thinks I’m being too rigid and should just accept the situation to keep the peace. I feel manipulated and resentful. I’m now facing the possibility of legal action if I don't back down. I feel like I’m being taken advantage of. 

Am I the asshole for insisting she return the dress?
