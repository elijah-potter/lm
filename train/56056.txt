Pont de les Ferreres or Devil's Bridge Aqueduct
Date of construction is uncertain, some believe that it was built during the era of Augustus.Restored during the reign of caliph Abd-el Rahman III and in the 18th century. Known as the Puente del Diablo, because a legend depicts that it was built by the Devil after winning a bet in which a fair lady bet her soul.Originally 25 kms in length, now 217 m and 27 m high.
