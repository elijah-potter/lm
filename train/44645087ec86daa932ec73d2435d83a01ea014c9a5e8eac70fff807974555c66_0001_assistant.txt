Suzie had always been a fan of tea and coffee, even from a young age. Surprisingly enough, she had her first cup of coffee at just 12 years old and loved the bitter taste, even without creamer and sugar. 
As she grew older and began to expand her coffee horizons to lattes, cortados, espresso, and more, she developed a goal of one day opening her very own coffee shop. She wanted to be a business owner and a barista, and she hoped that her coffee drinks would bring happiness to all who drank them. 
By the age of 20 years old, Suzie had already drafted plans with a financial advisor to open her very own coffee shop. She planned to name it Coffee Delight and create her own hand-crafted coffee drink menu. She vowed to never have her coffee menu appear as a boring selection of drinks. She wanted the possibilities to be endless. 
When she finally found a location for Coffee Delight and got her store set up, Suzie was happier than ever to have her dream finally come true before her eyes. Coffee Delight was situated in a central part of town where customers always knew how to find it. 
And when the grand opening day came around, customers filed in and found that Suzie's coffee was the best coffee in town. Her planning had paid off, and she was proud of the progress she had made.
