The Children of Lir (Bilingual Book) - Turkish/English
Author: Dawn Casey
Illustrator: Diana Mayo
A classic Celtic myth from the icy, cold North Sea is the tale of an evil queen, who transforms her stepchildren into swans that are doomed to live for a thousand years. Older children will be mesmerized by this powerful, yet tragic myth brought to life by brilliantly evocative illustrations that capture the imagination.
THIS TITLE IS AVAILABLE IN MANY BILINGUAL EDITIONS.
Upper level text picture book. Content also enjoyed by older language learners.
