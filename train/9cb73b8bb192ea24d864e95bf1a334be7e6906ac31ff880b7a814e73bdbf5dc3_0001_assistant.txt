Attention friends and patrons of The Newer Moon. Due to the incendiary events of last evening, the owners have decided to implement a new rule exclusive to the tavern and dining area. From now on, armor of any kind is not allowed to be worn on the premises, within the dining area or at the tavern. 

The incident in question that led to this new rule has to do with the clever loophole that some adventures found in our existing no-weapons policy. We now realize that heavy armor and hearty leather straps could be used as improvised weapons. We wish to not further inflame any heated arguments leading to a visit to the town cleric.

We understand that The Newer Moon is a favorite spot among adventurers and that this might be inconvenient for them. To accommodate this, we have provided a locker area at the side where adventurers can don or doff their armor safely by themselves or with the aid of one of our valets. 

Thank you for your understanding.
