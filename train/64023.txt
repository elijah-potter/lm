Bluster Snide the Bantam Cockerels: A story about bullying
Bluster and Snide like to challenge other birds to fight. . . these two bantam cockerels bully all the weaker and smaller birds in the farmyard. But when a silly dare goes wrong Bluster is left alone. Will he discover the true meaning of friendship?
Sharing a story is a great way to help children who display challenging behaviour to recognise feelings and behaviours. The fun characters and humorous rhyming text in these story books can help engage great conversations about appropriate behavior and allow teachers/parents to address unwanted behaviour in a positive way and help children relate the behaviour to consequences.
