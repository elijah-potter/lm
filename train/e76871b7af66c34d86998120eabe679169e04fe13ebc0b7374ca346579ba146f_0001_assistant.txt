Dear Diary,

Today, I was fortunate enough to witness an astounding spectacle. NASA's own Earth Polychromatic Imaging Camera, endearingly known as EPIC, managed to capture the fully lit, often referred to as the 'dark side', of the moon as it gracefully transited in front of our home planet. The vantage point, a staggering million miles away, provided us with an unforgettable sight. Our dear Earth unveiled itself in all its glory, showcasing mesmerizing cloud patterns spiraling over the expanse of the brilliant blue oceans. The vessel enabling this unique perspective, the DSCOVR satellite, was sent into the great beyond by none other than Elon Musk's SpaceX Falcon 9 back in 2015. The experience was truly humbling.
