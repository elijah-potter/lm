Sally is a ballerina who grew up in a small town and has taken ballet classes since she was 4 years old. Sally made it to New York City and was going to be the lead dancer in a major show last summer. This was until something terrible happened at practice. Her dancing partner lifted her, and she fell on the way down. She broke her leg, and the doctor said she wouldn't be able to dance again for several months. 

All of Sally's friends felt so bad for her. Her dream of being a top ballerina had almost come true. Even though she was sad, Sally decided to keep fighting for her dream. She spent hours watching videos of other dancers to improve her skills while she was in her cast. When she started to recover, she eased back into dancing little by little. 

It wasn't an easy journey for Sally. Coming back from an injury is painful and frustrating. But she persisted. She spent hours perfecting her dancing once again. This summer came around, and Sally was named the lead dancer for the season's show. She is thrilled but also relieved that her hard work to come back paid off.
