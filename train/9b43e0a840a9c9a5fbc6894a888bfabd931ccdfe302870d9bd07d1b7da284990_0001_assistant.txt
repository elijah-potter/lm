The central point of this article is the following:

A viral TikTok car challenge ended badly for Hyundai, which ended up having to pay up to $200 million in a settlement. The user "Kia Boyz" posted a video showing how he used USB cables to hotwire various Hyundai and Kia cars. Approximately 9 million Hyundai and Kia owners were affected by this exploitable glitch. The settlement covered an array of things, such as insurance deductibles, reimbursement for higher premiums, and out-of-pocket expenses.
