A weekly data synchronization script, executed via `git pull --rebase` on a fixed schedule, has evolved into a ritual for the team. Each developer checks their local branches against the mainline, resolving conflicts in shared branches before merging. The process follows a standardized workflow: `git stash`, `rebase -i`, `cherry-pick` fixes, then `git pull` to ensure all repos stay in sync. This routine fosters consistency and reduces merge conflicts, though occasional "frozen" branches require manual intervention.

Ritualized commands:
- **Time:** 9:00 AM (Tues/Thurs)
- **Location:** Team Slack channel (e.g., `#sync-queue`)
- **Trigger:** `!sync` in Slack
- **Outcome:** All branches reflect the latest mainline state by EOD Mon/Wed.

The ritual ensures stability, but its rigidity is occasionally debated over coffee.
