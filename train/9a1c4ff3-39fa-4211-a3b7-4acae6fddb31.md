The static hummed from the cracked speakers as the old radio crackled to life in the abandoned asylum’s last remaining room. A voice—hoarse, distorted by time—spoke in a whisper:

*"Listen closely. The walls are listening too."*

The door creaked open, revealing a figure in a tattered coat, their face half-hidden behind a scarred mask. The air smelled of damp earth and something metallic. They held a lantern, its flame flickering weakly.

*"You’re not the first to come here,"* they said. *"And you won’t be the last."*
