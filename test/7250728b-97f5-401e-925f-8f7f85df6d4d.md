Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around the meadow, chasing after colorful flowers and nibbling on soft green leaves.

One sunny morning, Benny found a shiny red apple near his burrow. "Mmm, this looks yummy!" he thought. But as he reached for it, he heard a tiny voice, "Ouch! Help me!"

Benny looked down and saw a little blue butterfly with a thorn stuck in its wing. Benny carefully picked the thorn out and fluttered the butterfly's wing back to its place. The butterfly thanked Benny and flew away happily.

After that, Benny felt a little sad, but he remembered to be kind to others. Every day, Benny would share his apples and flowers, helping his friends—like the sleepy old turtle who loved to bask in the sun and the squirrel who collected nuts for the winter.

And so, Benny learned that being kind and helping others made everyone feel happy and loved. And that’s how the magic of kindness grew in the little garden.
