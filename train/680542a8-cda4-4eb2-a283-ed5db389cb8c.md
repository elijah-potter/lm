Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a garden full of flowers, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around, play with his toys, and explore the sunshine.

One morning, Benny woke up and saw his favorite flower, a bright red rose, swaying gently in the breeze. "Mmm, that smells so good!" he thought. Benny hopped over and sniffed it carefully.

Suddenly, a tiny voice called, "Benny! Benny, help me!" Benny looked down and saw a little mouse named Milo, stuck in a tall, green grass pile. "Don't worry, Milo!" Benny said. He carefully dug out a small hole with his paws and helped Milo out.

Milo cheered, "Thank you, Benny! You're my best friend!" They shared a big, juicy carrot Benny found in the garden.

As the sun set, Benny and Milo snuggled under their favorite blanket, telling stories of their adventures. Benny knew Milo would be safe and happy, and Milo knew Benny would always be there to help.

From that day on, Benny and Milo became the best of friends, hopping and playing together every day, sharing their toys and stories. And they lived happily ever after, under the warm glow of the moon.
