Technology now occupies nearly every corner of our students' lives. To that end, the administration and staff of St. Joseph School have made a concerted effort to incorporate technology into the curriculum.
Technology is an integral part of the curriculum in each classroom. Students have access to a wide variety of technology tools used to draft and edit writing, conduct research, practice math facts, create student projects, and access online educational programs, including Google Apps for Education.
In technology, we get to play games on the computer, solve math problems, and learn to type.
