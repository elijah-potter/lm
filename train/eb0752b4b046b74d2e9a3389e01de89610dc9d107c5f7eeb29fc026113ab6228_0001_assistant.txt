The main Paw Patrol characters in the Paw Patrol movie were voiced by the following:

• Lilly Bartlam as Skye
• Iain Armitage as Chase
• Shayle Simons as Zuma
• Callum Shoniker as Rocky
• Keegan Hedley as Rubble
• Kingsley Marshall as Marshall

The Paw Patrol movie introduced the new character of Liberty (voiced by Marsai Martin), which differed from the regular television series. Liberty lives in Adventure City and becomes the newest member of the Paw Patrol.
