A cold icy feeling inside chest is often experienced by people and considered normal. But what if it’s indicating a severe problem? A Cold sensation in chest and throat can signify that you are suffering from a chest cold. The other name for chest cold is acute bronchitis, and you should take it seriously. Before we discuss anything, let’s find out how this minty feeling in the chest is caused.
The swelling in the airways of your lungs leads to the production of mucus. The constriction of the airways will result in coughing that can be persistent. Acute bronchitis will take three weeks to recover approximately. But the menthol sensation in the chest is quite uncomfortable.
If the condition is not treated timely, then this can lead to chronic bronchitis. The minty feeling in lungs is not pleasant like you are chewing peppermint gum. It is associated with other symptoms as well.

What Causes Cooling Sensation In Throat And Chest?
A viral infection of the upper respiratory tract is the most common cause of menthol-like feeling in chest. The acute bronchitis cases sum up to 80 to 95 percent in adults. These viruses are no different than the common cold. Moreover, other than viral infection, bacterial infection can also be a leading cause of menthol feeling in chest.

Viral bronchitis can lead to bacterial infection if it is not taken seriously. Mycoplasma Pneumoniae and Bordetella Pertussis commonly lead to bacterial infections. Sometimes, they can also cause a cold icy feeling inside chest and throat due to inhaling irritants like fumes and smoke. The irritation of the trachea and bronchial airways can lead to a cold feeling in chest and throat.
In rare cases, the cause of acute bronchitis is not an infection. But the cool minty feeling in chest is caused due to an underlying health problem like asthma or chronic bronchitis.

- Weak immune system

Symptoms Of Cold Icy Feeling Inside Chest
The symptoms will mimic the common signs and symptoms of seasonal cold or flu. It would help if you looked out for these signs along with anxiety cold feeling in chest.
- Sore throat
- Runny nose
- Muscle aches
You will develop a cough after the initial infection. The dry cough will transition into a productive cough slowly. This means that there is mucus. The productive cough is persistent, along with a weird menthol feeling in chest. It can last up to 3 weeks. You need to contact any healthcare professional if you experience chronic symptoms. These emergency symptoms are as follows:
- Weight loss
- Breathing difficulties
- Persistent barking cough
- High fever
- Pain in chest
- Cough that doesn’t go away after ten days

Diagnosis Of Menthol Feeling In Lungs
Mainly, a specific treatment is not required to treat acute bronchitis. But if the symptoms are not bearable, then your doctor will recommend a physical exam. Your doctor will start examining by listening to the breathing sound via a stethoscope. The actual purpose is to rule out wheezing.
You will also note the frequency and type of coughs in your medical history. Your doctor needs to know if you have a dry or productive cough. If you have any underlying health problems, you need to inform your doctor as soon as possible. Your doctor will devise the treatment strategy according to the health of the patient. Asthma and existing breathing issues can pose serious concerns.
If the diagnosis is uncertain, a chest X-ray is mandatory to see if it is pneumonia. Moreover, if any other infection is suspected, your healthcare provider will recommend some blood culture tests.
Treatment Of Cold Icy Feeling Inside Chest
The treatment plan is only initiated if the symptoms are severe. Acute bronchitis does not have a specified treatment therapy. Home care is recommended to deal with minor symptoms. Antibiotics are not recommended for acute bronchitis. The sole reason is that mostly these cases are linked to viral infections, and antibiotics only treat bacterial infections. Antibiotics are prescribed if the patient is at high risk of pneumonia. People who are diagnosed with acute bronchitis have a high chance to suffer from pneumonia in cold weather.
