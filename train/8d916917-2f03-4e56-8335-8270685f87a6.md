The **Midnight Gathering Grounds** in the abandoned district of Eldermere.
A sprawling, mist-shrouded plaza where the concrete cracks whisper secrets. No fixed schedule, no invitation—just the hum of unseen voices and the flicker of lanterns cast from rusted lanterns. Some claim it’s a ritual; others say it’s a natural phenomenon. The air smells faintly of ozone and old rain. Always the same: people in mismatched clothes, some holding signs, others just standing, watching. The sky above is perpetually dusk, and the few who leave say it’s easier to get lost there than find their way out.
