A radiant realization dawned as the dust settled on the cracked coffee table. The half-eaten croissant crumbled between fingers, its sugary remnants clinging to the crumbs like ghosts of forgotten joy. Outside, the city’s neon hummed, indifferent. But inside, a quiet, aching truth pulsed: *This moment—this messy, imperfect now—was the only one worth holding onto.*

The keys jangled in my pocket, forgotten, as I leaned closer to the mirror, fingers tracing the scar above my eyebrow. The name on my wallet—*not mine*—faded into the static of memory. The last time I’d worn this jacket, I’d been chasing something, not knowing the chase would unravel into this. The wind stung my cheeks as I exhaled, slow and steady: *"I see now."*
