There once was a frog.
His name was Nog.
He lived in a bog
Under a log.
One day, Nog went for a jog
And ran into a dog.
The dog's name was Hog.
He said to the frog,
"I got lost in the fog."
This couldn't stand for the frog.
So he invited him to his log
And posted on his blog:
"Missing dog."
The human of Hog
Did run to the bog
And the dog said to the frog,
"Thank goodness you jog."
