A murder has occurred in River Heights, with few leads and far too many suspects to determine what happened. In an attempt to get all the suspects in one place, they are all invited to participate in the reality show Love is Blind. Posing as a contestant, you, as Nancy Drew, must interrogate the suspects to determine who the murderer is. Will you find the murderer, or will you get caught up in the game and fall in love? Find out in the new full-length Nancy Drew mystery game: Murder is Blind.
