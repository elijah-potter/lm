1. World History: An overview of the history of the world, this class covers topics from the first man all the way to important events of the 21st century. 
2: Biology: In Biology class, you will learn about the species of animals and plants on the planet and how the human body functions. 
3. Chemistry: You will learn about the periodic table of elements as well as matter and its interactions with other matter and energy in a standard Chemistry class. 
4. Physical Education: Most students are required to take a Physical Education course in which they will play various games and sports to stay healthy. 
5. Algebra: You will learn to use letters to represent numbers, and that studies numbers and the operations in Algebra. 
6. Geometry: In Geometry, you will learn the mathematics of the measurement, properties, and relationships of points, lines, angles, surfaces, and solids.
7. European History: This class will cover the history of the continent of Europe, from the Roman Empire to the Nazi occupation and everything in between. 
8. Spanish/French: You can choose whether you want to study French or Spanish language, and in the class, you will learn the basics of the language and how to hold a conversation.
