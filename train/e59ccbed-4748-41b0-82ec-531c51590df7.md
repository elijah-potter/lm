A vast, metallic cavern hums with residual energy—walls lined with cracked thermal shielding where reflections of forgotten machinery flicker like dying stars. The air is stale, thick with ozone and the scent of melted plastic, yet no one is there. A single, flickering O-rings glow at the ceiling, pulsing like a heartbeat. The floor is a maze of rusted sprockets and half-assembled pods, some still warm from an abandoned repair cycle. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, shifting like living things. A faint, rhythmic *clang* echoes—something metallic dragging across concrete. The silence isn’t absence; it’s the weight of what *could have been* left behind.
