The neon-lit ruins of an abandoned observatory stretch across a volcanic plain, where the last functioning telescope’s mirror, shattered by a meteor strike, still reflects a faint, pulsating light in the dead of night. The only unusual detail: the mirror’s edges glow with a slow, eerie bioluminescence, pulsing in sync with the distant hum of a dormant, crystalline radio tower that emits static in a language no human has ever heard.
