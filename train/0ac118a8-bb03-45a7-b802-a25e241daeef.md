Once upon a time, in a big, cozy house with a garden full of flowers, lived a little girl named Mia. Mia had a special toy called a magic cloud. It was soft and fluffy, and whenever she blew into it, tiny, happy clouds floated away!

One sunny morning, Mia went outside and saw her favorite flower, the sunflower. The sunflower was tall and yellow, and it looked at Mia with its big, round eyes. Mia smiled and said, "Hello, sunshine!" The sunflower wobbled its stem and grew a little taller.

Just then, Mia’s magic cloud turned into a tiny, friendly balloon. It floated up high, up high, and landed on the sunflower’s head. The sunflower giggled and waved its leaves. Mia laughed and blew more into her cloud.

Now, the magic cloud turned into a rainbow! The rainbow was bright and sparkly, and it stretched across the sky. Mia danced under it, her feet kicking up little puffs of rain. The rainbow smiled and said, "Come on, Mia! Let’s play!"

Mia picked up a shiny pebble and threw it into the sky. It turned into a star that twinkled in the blue night sky. Mia hugged her magic cloud and said, "Goodbye, sunshine!" The sunflower waved goodbye too.

That night, Mia dreamed of dancing with rainbows and playing with stars. She woke up feeling happy and excited, ready to make more magic with her special cloud!
