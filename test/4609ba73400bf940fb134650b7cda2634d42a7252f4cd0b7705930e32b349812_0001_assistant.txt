Bourbon and horses are perhaps the most notable things about the state of Kentucky. Despite this, outside of the United States, people tend to associate the state with Kentucky Fried Chicken (KFC). This is just based on my experiences. Kentucky has a lot to offer besides these three things, of course. Kentucky is a welcoming Southeastern state, however, it's heavily debated whether it is part of the South or part of the Midwest. Even historically speaking, Kentucky has always been a border state, so even those that are from there are a bit confused about their geographical identity. There are definitely more exciting states to visit, but if you're looking for peaceful rural landscapes, good bourbon, southern cooking, horse races, and welcoming locals, Kentucky is great for all of those things.
