Solar energy – the cleanest and most abundant renewable energy source from the sun converted into thermal or electrical energy. There is a variety of uses for solar technologies to harness this powerful energy; for example, generating electricity, providing light, and heating water for domestic, commercial, or industrial use from small to large scale.
Photovoltaics, solar heating & cooling, and concentrating solar power are some major ways that we mainly mention in order to harness solar energy:. In more detail, photovoltaics which create electricity directly from sunlight with an electronic process can be applied to power from small electrical equipment such as calculators and toys up to large projects such as homes and giant commercial businesses. Contrary to the photovoltaics, both Solar heating & cooling (SHC) and concentrating solar power (CSP) use the heat the sun releases to provide space or water heating with the help of SHC systems, or to run traditional electricity-generating turbines in the case of CSP power plants.
