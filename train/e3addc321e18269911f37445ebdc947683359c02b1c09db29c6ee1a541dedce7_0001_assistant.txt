"Museum Air" is a short film that centers around two New York City residents, Andrea and Carl, and their adventures throughout different art museums. A city of over 8 million residents, "Museum Air" takes a different approach when filming art galleries and museums. They highlight famous artists such as Basquiat and Jackson Pollack and fill viewers with breathtaking awe.

Andrea is a former art school student who is focused on continuing her education, but not in the traditional sense. Some of her inspirations are Marina Abromovic and Lucian Freud and she is absolutely sure everything is derivative. Concerned with her finances and focused on expanding her craft, she wanders through these galleries in search of herself. One night when Andrea and Carl meet up, at yet another gallery this time in Queens, everything is about to change.

Carl on the other hand has made a name for himself in the art world. He has two pieces in every gallery and museum they visit. Carl has always been sensitive to his best friend but eventually, Andrea decides it's better to move on. Carl knows the system aligns in his favor. He knows his best friend is at peace when she visits the museums. So he wonders, how he can make this friendship work while still creating artwork?
