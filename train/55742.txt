1. PLASMALEMMA –
* Outer layer, thin, semipermeable, thickness 1–2 m.
* Helpfull in respiration & excretion.
* Power of regenration present.
2. PSEUDOPODIA –
* Pseudo = false. Podia = foot.
* Organellae of locomotion.
* Helfull in capturing food.
* Minimum number = 0 (in unfavourable condition).
* Maximum number = 10 (in startvation) Lobopolypodia type.
* Generally one large pseudopodia is formed in direction of locomotion i.e. unipodium.
* Main function is to show response against external stimuli.
* At tip of pseudopodia hyaline cap present, formed by hyaline layer.
* Strongest and thickest part of body.
* Equal to enemal of tooth.
3. PROTOPLASM –
* Differentiated into nucleus & cytoplasm.
* Cytoplasm is differentiated into ectoplasm and endoplasm.
* Ectoplam or ectosarc is outer, less, agranular & transparent.
* Endoplasm or endosarc is inner, more, granular & translucent.
* Between these two hyaline layer present that is part of ectoplasm.
* In endoplasm granules of protein present.
According to Mast endoplasm shows 2 colloidal stages –
(i) Plasma gel – outer, less, more granular as protein chain is unfolded.
(ii) Plasma sol – Inner, more, less granular as protein chain is folded.

4. NUCLEUS –
• One in number, in center.
• In young stage biconcave.
• Biconvex due to honey comb lattice.
• Nucleoli many.
• Chromosomes are round known as chromidia 500–600. Permanent structure of amoeba.
• Nucleus is granular and non–reflecting so not visible clearly but stained by methyl green acetic acid.
5. FOOD VACUOLE –
• Present in plasma sol.
• Formed after ingestion.
• Equal to stomach.
• Around it lysosomes are abundant.
6. CONTRACTILE VACUOLE -
• Having power of contraction & expansion.
• Generally –1 in plasma sol
• towards uroid. In peramicium –2 in number.
• Power of regeneration present.
• Main function is osmoregulation also helfpul in excretion, electrolyte balance. Contractile vacuole is convered by water globule.
• Mitochondria are maximum around it, so C.V. is most active organallae.
• Amoeba is found in fresh water, so water enters into its body by endosmosis.
• Water is collected first by water globule & given to central vacuole.
• C.V. increases in size i.e. Diastole which is gradual process.
• C.V. moves towards plasmalemma & systole occurs which is sudden.
• C.V. is lost.
• New C.V. is formed, so regeneration present.
• C.V. absent in marine and parasitic protozoa.
• C.V. is analogous to sweat gland (skin) of man, nephron of frog, coelenteron of hydra, nephredia of earthworm, malpioghian tubule of cockroach.
