Warlocks Wharf was home to many wizards, warlocks, witches, and mages, but it also was home to one of the legends of the land. It was home to the Castle of the Wharf, a mystical castle shrouded in mystery and myth. The castle had stone walls that went around in a square formation with cylindrical towers on each corner. Around the castle was a moat, and the walls and wooden door were covered by a purple glow from an unknown enchantment. No one had ever seen anyone enter or leave the castle. There were rumors of a powerful warlock who had shut himself in to protect his hordes of gems from the king, other rumors told of a wizard so powerful that multiple nations united to eliminate him hidden inside away from a hostile world, others told of a sort of enchantment that had the entire interior be thousands and thousands of miles, an entire kingdom set inside the enchanted stone walls of the castle. No one knew for sure what secrets lie inside.
