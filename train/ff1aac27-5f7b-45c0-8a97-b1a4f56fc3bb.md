## Lost in the Record Store (Brooklyn, NY) (Williamsburg)

The afternoon light, diffused through the dusty windows of Rough Trade, caught the gold in your hair as you browsed the used vinyl near the back. I was searching for a specific pressing of a Miles Davis album, and I kept noticing you – the way you’d pause, carefully examining the album covers, a small, almost hesitant smile playing on your lips. You were wearing a faded denim jacket and had a scattering of freckles across your nose that seemed to glow in the amber light. I wanted to say something, anything, but a particularly jarring blast of feedback from the store’s speakers seemed to swallow my courage each time I turned. I eventually found my record, but the moment, the potential for a shared conversation about jazz and vintage finds, felt like it slipped away. The lingering scent of old paper and the faint hum of the turntable are all I have to remember.
