A dimly lit kitchen at dawn. The hum of a coffee machine blends with the distant sound of a dog barking outside. The microwave timer buzzes as Sarah steps out of the shower, wrapping a towel around her. The phone rings—her partner’s voice crackles through the line: *"We’re at the party, and the DJ just dropped this insane bassline!"* She freezes, her breath fogging the phone. Laughter erupts from the living room, punctuated by the sound of someone yelping. Outside, a neighbor’s car alarm blares in a loop. Sarah exhales, the towel slipping from her fingers. The microwave door opens.
