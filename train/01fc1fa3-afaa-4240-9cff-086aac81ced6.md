Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had soft brown fur and a tiny pink nose that always sparkled when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny wiggled his nose and said, "Time to explore!" He hopped outside and saw his favorite friend, a bright yellow butterfly named Bella fluttering around. "Hello, Bella!" Benny said, and Bella buzzed happily, "Hello, Benny!"

They ran through the tall green grass, collecting shiny pebbles and colorful flowers. Benny found a tiny red flower with a smiley face. "This one’s special!" he said, pressing it to his nose.

Just then, a big, kind old owl named Oliver sat on a branch, watching them. "Benny and Bella, you’re playing so nicely," Oliver said. "Why don’t you make a little dance together?"

So Benny and Bella twirled and fluttered, their happy hearts beating fast. The flowers and grass swayed with them, and even the clouds smiled as they danced.

When the sun started to set, painting the sky pink and gold, Benny said, "Bella, it’s time to go home." Bella fluttered her wings sadly. "I’ll see you tomorrow, Benny!" she said, and with one last happy flutter, she flew away.

Benny hopped home, his heart full of joy. He curled up in his soft blanket and fell asleep, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures with Bella and Oliver.

The end.
