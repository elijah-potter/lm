1. Lysbet lives in a dystopian future Earth where creativity and individuality are discouraged by the ruling class. Her father, feeling there was a better way to live, tried to organize a resistance group. After he's killed for his disobedience, Lysbet continues his work to save society and get revenge for her father.
2. Lysbet is excited to marry Prince Elvet and bond their kingdoms in peace. But on the eve of their wedding, the prince is found dead, and Lysbet is accused of his murder. Lysbet has to discover who killed her betrothed, clear her name, and exact revenge on the people responsible.
3. Lysbet is on a quest to discover her true mother after years of being lied to by her cruel father. She starts researching online only to get pulled into a dark corner of the Internet. There, she learns that her mother was harboring some dark secrets before she was made to disappear.
4. Lysbet is in her third year of high school when she learns that she has the ability to wield dark powers. Now, it's up to her to decide if she'll use them to get revenge on the people who picked on her growing up or take the high ground and leave them alone. Her mother and father seem to know the origins of her power but aren't ready to give up their secrets. 
5. Lysbet is a police officer who is forced to go into hiding after she's accused of betraying the force. She has to use her knowledge of law enforcement to get around the system she once fought to uphold. After her life falls apart, she decides it's time to make those responsible pay the ultimate price.
