An unfinished brick-lined courtyard, its mortar still damp, cracks forming geometric patterns like cracked skin. Overgrown ivy clings to the skeletal remains of a stone archway, its upper half missing, exposing the rough-hewn stone beneath. A tilted wooden bench leans against the wall, its splintered seat cracked in half. Nearby, a half-assembled wooden table stands on wobbly legs, its top stained with dried paint and half-finished sketches. The air smells of damp earth and old plaster, with a faint metallic tang from the rusting remnants of a broken water spout. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, hinting at the sun setting behind the unfinished tower’s unfinished window frames. The space feels like a pause—half-buried in time.
