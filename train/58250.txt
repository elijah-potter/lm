Find Out & Write About - Sea Life
The Find Out & Write About Sea Life resource will enable your children to find out fascinating facts about sea creatures great and small.
Topics covered include the crab, dolphin, herring, jellyfish, octopus, ray, shark, turtle, whale and plankton. Children will find out about whale noises, why plankton are important, what happens when a shark loses its teeth and much more.
The information is presented at 3 levels. All levels use clear, recorded speech, and the text is highlighted as it is read.
(Requires Clicker 7 for Windows or Mac).
