The four chambers of the heart in humans are:
1. The left atrium: This chamber of the heart receives the blood that has been oxygenated from the lungs. It then passes it on to the left ventricle. 
2. The right atrium: This chamber of the heart takes in deoxygenated blood from the body, and passes it on to the right ventricle. 
3. The left ventricle: This chamber of the heart is the last stop for the blood before it is pumped throughout your entire body. 
4. The right ventricle: This chamber of the heart takes the blood from the right atrium, and forcefully pumps it to the lungs, where it receives the oxygen that it needs.
