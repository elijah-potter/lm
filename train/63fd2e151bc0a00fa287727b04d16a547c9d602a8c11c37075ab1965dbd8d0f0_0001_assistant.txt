Pirate legends speak of buried treasure, but few accounts verify the practice. Francis Drake safeguarded his plunder in Panama, while Captain Kidd's riches were confiscated. The notion of buried loot gained fame through Robert Louis Stevenson's "Treasure Island," yet pirates often indulged rather than saved. Though real pirate hoards were rare, tales persist of lost fortunes on deserted islands, like the famed "Treasure of Lima" on Cocos Island. Worth $200 million, it remains elusive despite years of pursuit.
