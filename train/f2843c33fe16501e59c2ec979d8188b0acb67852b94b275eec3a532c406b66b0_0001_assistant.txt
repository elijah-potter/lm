After years of crimes, the mobster was tired of running. He knew his past life was catching up to him, and that he had little chance to escape this time around. Putting down his car keys, he closed his eyes and sighed deeply. The sirens blared louder as they came closer, signaling what little time was left. Gritting his teeth, and with the sirens at full blast, he walked outside of his home and awaited the inevitable.
