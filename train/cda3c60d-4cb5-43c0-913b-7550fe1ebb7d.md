A conceptual framework for abstract planning:
1. **Documentation Phase**: Drafted on paper in a draft binder, outlining high-level objectives, resource allocations, and key milestones.
2. **Stakeholder Alignment**: Redundant email summaries of the paper plan sent to 12 stakeholders without actionable feedback loops.
3. **Routine Review**: Quarterly in-person meetings (held in a conference room) where the paper plan is skim-read by 3 committee members.
4. **Formal Approval**: A 3-page signature sheet at the end of each review, with checkmarks but no substantive revisions.
5. **Operational Gaps**: Empty task lists, missing timelines, and undefined responsibilities marked in red pen but left unaddressed.
