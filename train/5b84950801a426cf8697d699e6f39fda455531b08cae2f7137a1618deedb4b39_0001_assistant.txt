- SSRIs are the first choice of mediation treatments and include Prozac, Zoloft, and Paxil. SSRIs have milder side effects than other antidepressants.
- SNRIs include Effexor and Cymbalta to treat depression, anxiety, and chronic pain.
- NDRIs are Wellbutrin or Zyban and have energizing effects.
- NaSSAs like Remeron stimulate appetite and have sedating effects which may aid with sleep.
- Cyclics are older drugs that have many more side effects and aren't as effective as the previous four types. They include: Elavil, Tofranil, and Anafranil.
- MAOIs are also older drugs that sometimes work but require a very specific diet, which, if not followed could cause danger. Nardil and Parnate are the most common MAOIs.

All the antidepressants have side effects, the most common ones are weight gain, sleep disturbances, sexual side effects, and dry mouth.
