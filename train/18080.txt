Medical terminology can be quite confusing. Knowing these conditions however is rather crucial especially when going through medical procedures. One common procedure in many treatment procedures is phlebotomy. Despite the fancy term, it merely refers to the pricking or puncturing of blood vessels i.e. veins to gather a blood sample. Even though the process is quite simple, it’s essential in daily medical surgeries because blood samples have wide applications.
The professional term for the person carrying out the task of acquiring blood samples is a phlebotomist. These are however rarely stand-alone professionals in Belleville Pennsylvania. The process can be carried out by almost all qualified medical personnel such as physicians and nurses and as such it’s called venepuncture, to signify the specific action taking place.
Since blood functions are potentially hazardous for both the medical personnel and the patient, there is a great need for adequate training of those phlebotomists to decrease the risks associated with the procedure. This training should also cover other related duties related to the practice like identification and preparation of the patient before the procedure in addition to appropriate sample handling, testing and analysis. After the process, the phlebotomist must also be able to ensure proper care of the puncture point to prevent any complications.
