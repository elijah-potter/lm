A North Pole gift package for the kids in Ukraine will be announced this week worth $300 million and will include toys like remote-controlled helicopters. The toys come after the parents complained to Santa Clause that their kids weren't getting their presents. 

There have been no suggestions that the North Pole deliberately left out the Ukrainian kids from their nice list. The parents agreed that it was just a misunderstanding and are pleased that their kids will be getting the gifts.
