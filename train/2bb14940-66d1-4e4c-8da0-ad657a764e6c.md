## Project Chimera: A Cooperative Research Game

**Welcome, Researchers.** The world stands on the precipice. A rapidly evolving pathogen, designated “Chimera,” is exhibiting unprecedented adaptability, defying conventional containment protocols. As members of the Global Research Initiative (GRI), you are the last line of defense. Your combined expertise in virology, epidemiology, and genetics is humanity’s only hope. This is Project Chimera.

### Components

*   **Research Board:** A central board depicting global regions affected by Chimera, with spaces for research tokens and outbreak markers.
*   **Researcher Cards (6):** Each card details a Researcher's unique specialization, starting resources, and special ability.
*   **Chimera Strain Cards (30):** Represent the evolving mutations of the pathogen. Each card details a new threat and associated challenges.
*   **Research Tokens (60):** Represent progress in understanding and combating Chimera.
*   **Outbreak Markers (30):** Indicate regions experiencing escalating infection levels.
*   **Resource Tokens (120):** Represent funding, personnel, and specialized equipment. Divided into three types: Funding (blue), Personnel (green), and Equipment (yellow).
*   **Event Cards (40):** Introduce unexpected complications and opportunities.
*   **Dice (3):** Six-sided dice used for various tests and events.
*   **First Player Marker:** Indicates the current player.
*   **Strain Tracker:** A track indicating the current Chimera Strain level.

### Setup

1.  Place the Research Board in the center of the table.
2.  Shuffle the Chimera Strain Cards and place them face down to form the Strain Deck. Reveal the top card and place it face up next to the deck; this is the current Strain.
3.  Shuffle the Event Cards and place them face down to form the Event Deck.
4.  Place the Strain Tracker at level one.
5.  Place the Research Tokens and Resource Tokens in accessible piles.
6.  Each player chooses a Researcher Card and takes the starting resources indicated on the card.
7.  Place one Outbreak Marker on each region indicated on the current Strain Card.
8.  Determine the first player randomly and give them the First Player Marker.

### Turn Structure

Each player’s turn proceeds in the following phases:

1.  **Event Phase:** Draw the top Event Card and resolve its effects immediately. Some Event Cards may require dice rolls or resource expenditure.
2.  **Action Phase:** You have three actions to spend. Actions can be any combination of the following:
    *   **Research (1 Action):** Place one Research Token on a region. You may only place tokens in regions with Outbreak Markers.
    *   **Containment (1 Action):** Remove one Outbreak Marker from a region. Requires spending one Personnel token.
    *   **Resource Acquisition (1 Action):** Draw two Resource Tokens from the general supply.
    *   **Special Ability (Variable Cost):** Utilize your Researcher’s unique ability, paying any associated costs.
3.  **Strain Advancement Phase:** After all players have taken their turns, advance the Strain Tracker by one level. If the Strain Tracker reaches the maximum level, the game is lost (see Loss Condition).
4.  **Pass the Marker:** Pass the First Player Marker to the player on your left.

### Mechanics of Uncertainty and Trade-offs

Project Chimera incorporates several mechanics that force difficult decisions and highlight the inherent uncertainty of a global crisis:

1.  **Strain Mutation:** Each Strain Card introduces new challenges and modifies existing rules. Players must adapt their strategies on the fly, often facing unexpected consequences.
2.  **Event Card Cascades:** Certain Event Cards trigger chain reactions, affecting multiple regions and resources simultaneously. Players must prioritize responses, knowing that some problems will inevitably worsen.
3.  **Resource Prioritization:**  The limited supply of Funding, Personnel, and Equipment necessitates difficult choices. Focusing on research might leave containment efforts neglected, while aggressive containment might starve vital research.  Some Researcher abilities directly exacerbate this tension. For example, Dr. Anya Sharma’s ability to accelerate research requires diverting Personnel from Containment efforts.

### Win and Loss Conditions

**Winning the Game:**

You win the game if you successfully research and develop a countermeasure for Chimera. This is achieved by:

1.  Accumulating a specific number of Research Tokens (detailed on the current Strain Card) across all regions.
2.  Discarding all Chimera Strain Cards from the Strain Deck. This requires successfully mitigating the threats presented by each Strain.

**Losing the Game:**

You lose the game if:

1.  The Strain Tracker reaches its maximum level.
2.  A specific number of regions (detailed on the current Strain Card) are overwhelmed by Outbreak Markers, triggering a global pandemic.
3.  The Event Deck is exhausted and no more Event Cards can be drawn.



### Advanced Variants

**Variant 1: Accelerated Strain (Expert Difficulty)**

At the start of the game, increase the Strain Tracker's maximum level by one. This creates a tighter timeline and demands more efficient resource management.

**Variant 2: Limited Communication (Solo/Cooperative Challenge)**

Players are restricted in the information they can share.  Each player can only describe their actions and the regions they are working on; they cannot reveal specific resource levels or Researcher abilities. This emphasizes individual contribution and strategic deduction.

**Variant 3: Regional Specialization (Four Player Variant)**

Each player is assigned a specific geographic region to focus on.  They receive bonus resources when working in their assigned region but suffer penalties when working elsewhere. This encourages interdependence and highlights the challenges of coordinating a global response.

### Difficulty Scaling

*   **Two Players:** Reduce the number of Research Tokens required to win by 5.
*   **Three Players:** Play the game as described in the core rules.
*   **Four Players:** Increase the number of Research Tokens required to win by 5.



### Designer’s Note

Project Chimera was designed to be more than just a game. It aims to provide a compelling and accessible introduction to the complexities of pandemic response. The mechanics emphasize the importance of collaboration, strategic prioritization, and adaptability in the face of uncertainty.  The constant evolution of the pathogen, represented by the Strain Cards, reflects the dynamic nature of viral mutation and the challenges of developing effective countermeasures. The game is intended to foster critical thinking and appreciation for the vital work of scientists and public health professionals.
