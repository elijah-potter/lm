The jagged cliffs of **Veythara’s Edge** rise from a storm-wracked sea, their blackened basalt veins pulsing with bioluminescent moss. Below, the **Black Tide** churns, a swirling mass of saltwater and detritus, its depths whispering with the echoes of drowned voices. The wind howls through the **Scarred Arch**, a colossal cave mouth where the ceiling splits like a wound, exposing a cavernous blackness where the sky should be. Here, the air hums with static and distant machinery—old engines, half-buried in the sand. The ground beneath shifts; geysers of mineral-rich water erupt in slow, rhythmic pulses. At the heart of the ruins, a **floating stone platform** drifts on the tide, its surface etched with runes that shift when no one looks. Some say the edge here isn’t between land and sea, but between **memory and oblivion**.
