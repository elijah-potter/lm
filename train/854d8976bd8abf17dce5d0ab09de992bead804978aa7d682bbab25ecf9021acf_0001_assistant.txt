Herman Melville once said, “It is better to fail in originality than to succeed in imitation.” But what does that truly mean? Essentially, Melville’s words mean that in this life, we should all aim to be unique, create our own individuality, and be original. To do what everyone else is doing, or try to be like everyone else instead of who you truly are, is a sure path to failure, even if you are successful in it.
