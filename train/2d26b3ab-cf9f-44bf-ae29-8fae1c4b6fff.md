Soft mossy carpet of damp earth underfoot, crumbling between fingers like aged parchment.
Granite slabs gleam with mineral veins—deep emerald and silver—sliced by time into jagged edges.
Warm, worn wooden beams arch overhead, their grain deep and patina-kissed, whispering of centuries.
Cobblestones, smooth yet rough when pressed, catch the light like polished obsidian.
Air carries the scent of damp wool, pine resin, and iron from rusted nails, thick with the weight of history.
Candle wax pools in cracked glass, its surface trembling with unseen movement.
The stone floor, polished by countless footsteps, reflects flickering lantern light in fractured shards.
