Carpets absorb sunlight; shadows stretch unevenly across the worn linoleum. Lamps flicker, casting long, shifting patterns on the walls. Paint peels slowly in places, revealing layers of forgotten colors. Furniture creaks under silent movement. The air hums faintly with the distant murmur of a city, thick with static. Mirrors reflect dust motes dancing in half-lit rooms. Time ticks away, unnoticed, in the corners where dust motes settle. The scent of old wood and damp wool lingers, a ghost of past lives. Coffee stains the couch, barely visible. Clocks tick, some faster, some slower.
