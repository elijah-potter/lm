**Daily Schedule for Productive Work**

**Morning:**
- **7:00 AM** – Wake up, hydration, light stretching
- **7:30 AM** – Breakfast, plan priorities for the day
- **8:00 AM – 12:00 PM** – Deep Work Block (Focused task completion)

**Afternoon:**
- **12:00 PM – 1:00 PM** – Lunch break
- **1:00 PM – 3:00 PM** – Meetings/Collaboration
- **3:00 PM – 5:00 PM** – Moderate Work (Admin, emails, research)

**Evening:**
- **5:00 PM – 6:00 PM** – Exercise or creative time
- **6:00 PM – 7:00 PM** – Dinner
- **7:00 PM – 9:00 PM** – Personal/leisure (Reading, hobbies, family)
- **9:00 PM – 10:00 PM** – Wind down (Meditation, journaling)
- **10:00 PM** – Sleep

---
**Optional Flexible Block:**
- **2:00 PM – 4:00 PM** – Freelance/Unstructured time (if needed)

---
**Key:** Batch similar tasks, avoid multitasking; adjust timings as needed.
