Theo plodded along his usual path to the stream for a drink. His day had not started well at all. His friends were mad at him for ruining their game of hide and seek yesterday, and they were still mad at him today. He was told couldn't play with them that day and that he should go away. 

The path to the stream was well-worn, though the branches of the trees overhead had droopy branches that almost always snagged his brown fur. Of course, that happened today, which made Theo more upset.

He heard the stream before he saw it. It was a fast stream and further up the hill, it was broken up with rocks that made several areas churning rapids. He liked the stream and couldn't think of a better place to spend his sad day alone.

The cold water rushed around his muzzle when he dipped it in to take a big drink. It tickled a little. Satisfied, he plopped down next to the stream and laid his head on his paws, watching the rushing water flow by. The sound was calming and he felt himself drifting toward a nap. A branch snapped loudly and he was suddenly alert. He looked around but didn't see a threat. However, he did see something he hadn't noticed before.

There was a small, purplish-blue flower nestled in the grass and pebbles near the river bank. It had some white streaks on the petals and the middle of it was a bright yellow that made Theo smile. He inched closer to it and sniffed it gently. It had a soft and pleasant smell that lifted Theo's mood. He was happy he had wandered to the stream that day. He would keep the flower company for a while, enjoying its aroma and pretty petals, and then go find his friends so he could show the flower to them. They would love it too and not be mad at Theo anymore. Feeling content, Theo rested near the flower and appreciated its beauty.
