Tally is a French Bulldog who lives at home with her owners, Sam and Liza. Tally is an amazing artist, and while Sam and Liza have always supported her, sometimes their friends don't understand how a dog can be so good at drawing. One day, Liza's friend from school, Colly, was playing at their house. The night before, Tally had drawn a beautiful portrait of Colly that Liza couldn't wait to give her. But when she showed Colly the drawing, Colly laughed and rolled her eyes. "Give it up, Liza," said Colly, "I know Tally didn't draw this, she's just a dog!" Just then, Tally walked into the room with a bag of colored pencils in her mouth and nudged Liza to get a piece of paper down from her desk. Colly watched in disbelief as Tally drew yet another portrait of Colly, this one more beautiful than the last! Liza was so happy that Colly finally believed her and soon, all of Liza's friends from school wanted custom portraits by Tally.
