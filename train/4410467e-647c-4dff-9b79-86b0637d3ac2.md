Fog rolls thick across the cracked asphalt, obscuring the faint glow of distant streetlights. The air hums with the distant drip of rainwater from rusted gutters. Shadows stretch long and unnatural against the walls of a crumbling warehouse, its broken windows like hollow eyes watching the figures inside. One man, hunched over a cracked table, traces the edges of a faded map with trembling fingers, his breath shallow. Beside him, a child clutches a half-empty bottle of something red, their small hands shaking. The walls pulse faintly—somewhere, machinery hums beneath layers of dust. The scent of old oil and damp wool clings to the air like a memory. A single, dying candle flickers at the center of the room, casting jagged light over faces that no longer know their own names.
