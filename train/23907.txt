The role of the music producer – A short historical overview
“I aim to provide contextual background for the development of the role of music production as a creative practice. It is intended to offer a framework and not by any means a comprehensive historical overview. In the early days of the music recording industry, the ideal recording sought after by producers was the faithful reproduction of a live performance.”
