The storm rolled in without warning.
Lightning split the sky like a blade.
Drops pounded the roof in relentless rhythm.
The wind howled, hungry for something.
My breath came in sharp, gasping bursts.
Something moved in the shadows—too fast.
A sound, wet and wrong, echoed from below.
The door creaked. It was too heavy.
The floorboards groaned beneath my feet.
The darkness swallowed the light.
I held my breath, trembling.
A whisper slithered through the air.
Then—footsteps.
Cold. Unmistakable.
