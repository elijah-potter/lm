A librarian scans and updates the inventory database for a new book arrival. Normally, this involves verifying the barcode, checking for duplicates, and entering the metadata (title, author, ISBN) into the system. A small change—if the barcode is misread and the system auto-updates with an incorrect ISBN—causes discrepancies: the book appears in the system twice under the same title, but with different authors. Staff must then manually resolve conflicts, wasting time and delaying service.
