A disheveled figure in worn-out shoes steps off the subway, breathless, clutching a crumpled receipt. The late-night fluorescent lights of a city park glow faintly around him, but his eyes dart to the clock on the bench—just five minutes left. His phone buzzes: *You’re late. Meet me here.*

He exhales sharply, gripping the armrest. The text reads: *"Pizza at 9:30 PM. No excuses."* A familiar voice calls from the shadows—his partner, arms crossed, waiting. Relief floods him as he slides into the seat beside her, hand shaking slightly as he slides his phone into his pocket. The moment the door closes behind them, he lets out a sharp, relieved laugh.
