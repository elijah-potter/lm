The door creaked open with a sudden *snap*—no handle turned, no key turned. A gust of wind tugged at the loose fabric of the jacket draped over the back of the chair, revealing the startled figure inside. Their breath hitched as the dim hallway light flickered to life, casting long shadows across the floor. One hand clutched a half-empty coffee cup, the other gripping the armrest of the chair. The coffee sloshed slightly as their body jerked forward, then slumped backward, mouth open in a silent scream.

Across the room, the hallway stretched empty, the only sound the distant murmur of a TV from the living room. A single sneakered foot paused mid-stride, then hesitated—too close, too fast. The figure’s eyes widened, their vision blurring as the world spun into a blur of red and white. The chair’s legs scraped against the floor as they lurched forward, knocking over the cup before collapsing onto the floor, arms splayed in a broken, awkward star. The coffee cup shattered in a thousand pieces against the tile, the last remnants of warmth pooling on the floor.

Silence. Then, a single, unsteady breath. The hallway seemed to exhale, the weight of the unseen threat lifting with it. The figure didn’t move. Didn’t scream. Just lay there, the hollow sound of their own heartbeat the only echo.
