**ORDINANCE NO. 2024-05**
**TOWN OF [CITY_NAME], COUNTY [COUNTY_NAME]**

**AN ORDINANCE TO ENSURE ACCURACY, PUBLIC SAFETY, AND COMMUNITY TRUST**
**IN RESPONSE TO THE FALSE RUMOR REGARDING [SPECIFIC CLAIM/ALLEGATION]**

**WHEREAS**, recent circulating misinformation about [brief context, e.g., "a safety violation at the municipal pool" or "a minor traffic incident involving local personnel"] has caused unnecessary panic and distracted from legitimate community concerns;

**WHEREAS**, the town’s public safety protocols must prioritize accurate information dissemination to maintain public trust and prevent escalation of incidents;

**WHEREAS**, the town council acknowledges that unverified claims may lead to misguided actions or misinformation spreading in digital forums, social media, or word-of-mouth;

**THEREFORE**, the town council hereby:
1. **Issues a public clarification** via the official town website, town hall announcements, and social media channels, confirming:
   - [Verify or correct factual claims, e.g., *"No unauthorized individuals were present at the pool. The incident report is under review."*]
   - [Mention next steps, e.g., *"Police are investigating the traffic incident and will provide updates."*]
   - [Call for responsible reporting: *"Unverified rumors are not to be shared as fact."*]

2. **Directs town officials** to monitor digital platforms for repeated false statements and take down inaccurate posts within 24 hours of notification.

3. **Encourages residents** to report credible sources of information and verify claims via official town channels before sharing.

4. **Amends emergency response protocols** to include a standard "verify before disseminating" step for all public alerts.

**ENFORCEMENT**: Violations of this ordinance—including knowingly spreading false information that causes harm—may be addressed under existing defamation or public nuisance laws.

**EFFECTIVE DATE**: Immediate.
