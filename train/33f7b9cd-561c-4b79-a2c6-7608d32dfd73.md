A once-enacted law, now obsolete, decreed that all newly minted coins minted after the 15th of August 1987 must bear an embedded microchip to prevent counterfeiting. The rule was abandoned after the 2001 financial crisis, when microchipped currency proved unreliable in automated teller machines and cash registers. The government declared the rule obsolete, and the microchips were removed from circulation by 2005. The practice is no longer enforced.
