Beaches, Jim-Jim and Passiontulip were 3 cats who lived in a suburban neighborhood near the beach. They had many friends and often ran off each day to play games together. Today they were searching for friends to play hide and seek.

When Passiontulip mentioned that she was hungry, they ran down to the beach to search for food that tourists might have discarded.

“There are lots of kids down here today,” Beaches said to her friend. 

“They’re noisy!” Jim-Jim replied with a frowny face.

“They are, but they usually throw food down on the ground,” said Passiontulip. “So sneak over there closer to those kids and see what they have.”

The three cats moved in a stealthy manner toward a group of five kids who were laughing and eating hot dogs and hamburgers at a picnic table.

Passiontulip moved closer to one little girl with blonde hair. “Meow. Meow!”

“Ah, look!” exclaimed the little girl. “What a sweet kitty. She’s solid white.”

The girl stroked Passiontulip’s back as the cat purred with contentment.

“Are you hungry, kitty?” the little girl asked. She pinched off a piece of her hot dog and held it out to the cat.

Passiontulip gobbled up the hot dog. “Meow!”

The girl laughed. “Oh my! You were hungry. Here’s another piece.”

She pinched off another piece of the hot dog and fed it to the cat.

“Are you going to give your whole hot dog to that stray cat?” asked one of the other kids.

“She’s not a stray. She has a collar. See?”

The girl pointed at the white rhinestone collar worn by the white kitty and all the kids had to agree that she was not a stray.
