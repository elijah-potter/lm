**First Ending:**
The village elder, weary after a century of drought, found a single seed in his cracked jar. When he planted it, a towering tree bloomed overnight, its roots drinking the parched earth. The villagers cheered as rain began to fall again. By dawn, the first drops of water cascaded from its leaves, filling the cracked riverbed. The elder smiled, whispering to the sky, *"You see? Even the smallest hope can grow."*

---

**Second Ending:**
The last lantern in the abandoned library flickered to life in a storm. The book it held—*The Weight of Forgotten Words*—glowed as it opened itself, its pages shifting like liquid ink. The narrator’s voice echoed through the ruins: *"They thought they had lost the story. But stories, like rivers, always return to their source."* By midnight, the storm’s fury softened; the lantern’s light traced the library’s ceiling, revealing a hidden door. The words inside pulsed with life, waiting to be read again.
