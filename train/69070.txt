How the world would be in the year 2100?
The world in 2100 will be hotter, with more extreme weather and more natural disasters such as hurricanes and wildfires. There are different scenarios, from the world being 1.5ºC to 5ºC hotter by 2100.
What will be the average temperature in the year 2100?
Results from a wide range of climate model simulations suggest that our planet’s average temperature could be between 2 and 9.7°F (1.1 to 5.4°C) warmer in 2100 than it is today. The main reason for this temperature increase is carbon dioxide and other heat-trapping “greenhouse” gases that human activities produce.

Is it hard to write a 100-word essay?
Writing a 100-word essay is often not as easy a task as it sounds. Many teachers will assign short essays to test the student’s ability to write concise essays that demonstrate knowledge of the topic, without becoming too wordy. Whether you are writing a long term paper or a short response, formulating your thoughts onto paper can be tough.

Where will the world’s population grow in the future?
All projections agree that most of the growth would come from Africa, tripling its population to 4.3 billion based on the more widespread forecast. Asia would peak mid-century and then shrink, but continue being the most populous region globally with 4.7 billion people.
