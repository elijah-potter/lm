I came to life because of a sample sent to PeopleMakers, ready to live. The travel took a short amount of time, arriving at the door of my new best friend who was exactly like me. I was one of the lucky ones, ready to live a good life—until he couldn't afford the subscription anymore.

And then, I had to return, back to the darkness and emptiness. I said nothing to him, disappointed in his lack of trying as I walked out of the door for the final time, ready to be shut back down.
