A sudden traffic jam at a busy intersection near his office delayed him by 25 minutes. As he sat in gridlock, his phone buzzed—an unsolicited email from his childhood friend asking if they could meet for coffee. The recipient, who had planned to work on a deadline-heavy project, hesitated, then replied that the delay was perfect. They met at a nearby café, where an unexpected connection led to a three-hour conversation about a mutual mentor who had recently passed away. The delayed meeting sparked a new idea for a personal project tied to the mentor’s legacy. The original plan was abandoned, replaced by a revised strategy that incorporated the mentor’s final research papers and a collaborative workshop with a local university. The coincidence redirected focus toward community engagement and preservation of intellectual contributions.
