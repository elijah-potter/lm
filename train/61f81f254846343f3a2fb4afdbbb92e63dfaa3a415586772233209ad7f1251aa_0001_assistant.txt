Once upon a time, there was a cat named Lulu who dreamed of becoming an astronaut. Lulu was a very curious cat and loved to learn about space. She would spend hours reading books and watching documentaries and movies about planets, stars, and galaxies.

One day, Lulu received a letter from the space agency. She was selected to be the first cat in space! Lulu worked very hard in training and learned everything she could about space travel and the spaceship she would fly during her mission.

The day finally arrived when Lulu was ready to go on her mission to outer space. Lulu put on her spacesuit, climbed into the spacecraft and blasted off into outer space!

Lulu spent the next months traveling to the moon and different planets. She saw amazing things and met many different creatures. She learned a lot about space.

After a while, Lulu began to miss the things she loved about her home on Earth. She was sad to leave space, but she was also excited to share the stories of her travels with her friends and family.
