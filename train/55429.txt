Teach students the modal auxiliary verbs with this bundle, which contains multiple resources. Modal auxiliaries are the focus of CCSS L.4.1.C. The activities in this bundle focus on the nine most common modal auxiliaries: must, will, shall, would, can, could, may, might, and shall.
This bundle includes:
1. an 89-slide PowerPoint
2. a 5-page matching PowerPoint Companion Handout
3. a Modal Auxiliary Verb Craftivity
4. 24 Task Cards
5. five worksheets (with answer keys)
6. five posters
