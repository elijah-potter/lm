What are the two scope of psychology?
There are two different scopes of psychology: the social scope and the individual scope. The social scope of psychology is the study of how people interact with each other and how those interactions affect individuals’ mental and emotional states. The individual scope of psychology is the study of how the mind works on an individual level, such as how emotions affect memory or how personality traits affect behavior.

What are the three main areas of social psychology?
There are three main areas of social psychology: social cognition, social behavior, and social interaction. Social cognition is the study of how people think about themselves, others, and the world around them. Social behavior is the study of how people behave in social situations, and social interaction is the study of how people interact with each other.
How social psychology is scientific in nature?
Social psychology is scientific in nature because it follows the scientific method. This means that psychologists test hypotheses with empirical evidence, and then revise or disprove those hypotheses based on the results. In addition, social psychologists strive to be objective and impartial when conducting research, and they avoid making value judgements about their findings.
What is the main focus of social psychology?
The main focus of social psychology is to understand how people think, feel, and behave. Social psychology is interested in the mental processes that influence behavior in social contexts, including but not limited to: cognition (e.g., reasoning, memory), emotion (e.g., feeling, empathy), motivation (e.g., wanting, liking), personality (e.g., personality traits), and social influences (e.g., social comparison).
