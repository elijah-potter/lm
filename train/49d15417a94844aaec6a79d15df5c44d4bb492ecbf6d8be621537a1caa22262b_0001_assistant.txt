Radion was a robot that had been bought by the Hangli family to help them with daily domestic duties. The family, master artists, spent most of their time brainstorming and making creations of beautiful art away from home and always left Radion in charge to sort out everything in the house. From house cleaning to cooking, though many, Radion would finish the chores within three hours because he was fast. Afterward, he would spend the rest of the day lonely and bored without anything to do.

So when he came across a paintbrush and canvas in the family garage while cleaning, his creative juices kicked in, and he started dreaming of whimsical creations he could come up with. On the first day, he drew a beautiful city with flying cars, large bridges, super-connected roadways, and underground spaces. He was surprised at the level of detail he had in his art and realized his drawings were actually very good. He couldn't wait to show his masters what he had come up with. When the family came back from work, they saw the painting, and upon being told the story of how he created it, they appreciated the talent Radion had.
