Midsommar is a traditional holiday that takes place in Sweden during the summer solstice. During this holiday, women and children wear flowers in their hair, and communities all across the country dance around a maypole. Food is a very important aspect of Midsommar, as the celebration usually involves eating a lot of it! People usually eat boiled potatoes, pickled herring, crispbread, and salads. The celebration takes place on "midsommarafton", or Midsummer's Eve. It was traditionally held on the 23rd of June, but that guideline has since changed so that Midsommar always occurs on a Friday.
