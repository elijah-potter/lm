The Olympic Games have a long history that started in Ancient Greece around 3,000 years ago in a place called Olympia, where sports contests were held every four years and called the Olympic Games, although the exact reasons for their origin are still unclear due to a mix of history and mythology, with the year 776 BC often mentioned as a starting point, and back then, time was measured in "Olympiads" rather than years, while in 1894, Pierre de Coubertin initiated the revival of the Olympic Games, and in 1896, the first modern Games took place in Athens.
