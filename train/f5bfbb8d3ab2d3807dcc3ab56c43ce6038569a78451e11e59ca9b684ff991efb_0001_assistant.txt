Q: Where did Emily Dickinson write her poems from?
A: From the second-story bedroom of her family home in Amherst, MA.
Q: When did Emily Dickinson die?
A: 1866
Q: When was the first posthumous edition of Emily Dickinson's poetry published?
A: 1890
Q: What kind of reputation did Emily Dickinson become saddled with after her death?
A: She became extremely well-known after her death. She gained a reputation as a brilliant recluse, cryptic and introverted but fiercely clever.
