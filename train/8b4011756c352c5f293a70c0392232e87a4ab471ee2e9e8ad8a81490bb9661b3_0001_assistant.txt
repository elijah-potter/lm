
1. What is the chemical formula for water?
A. H20
2. Water is the only substance found on earth naturally in three forms; what are the three forms?
A. Solid, gas, liquid
3.  How much of the earth’s surface is water? A) 80% B) 60% C) 90% D) None of the Above
A. A) 80%
4.  At what temperature does water freeze?
A. 32 degrees F, 0 degrees C
5. At what temperature does water vaporize?
A. 212 degrees F, 100 degrees C
