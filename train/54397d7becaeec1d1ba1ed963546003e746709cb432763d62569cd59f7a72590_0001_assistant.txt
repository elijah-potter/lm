Murphy, a bald eagle at the World Bird Sanctuary in Valley Park, Missouri, began sitting on a nest. Male bald eagles always help during incubation, but Murphy was trying to hatch a rock. Murphy was moved to a separate enclosure on April 4, 2023, because he was protective of his "offspring." Meanwhile, an eaglet was brought to the sanctuary after its nest was destroyed during a windstorm. Due to Murphy's parental instincts, staff paired him with the eaglet so they could be released back into the wild later.
