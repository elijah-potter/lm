Having your kids do online learning at home brings a unique set of challenges. It’s particularly difficult if you’re trying to work from home as well. Although there’s no guide book to get you through this difficult time, here are some things you can do to make it easier for yourself and your child.
Have a Designated Work Area
One of the biggest issues with remote learning is that the line between home and school becomes too blurred for students to understand. They are supposed to be learning, but it can be difficult to focus from the comfort of their bed or couch. It’s important to set up a desk and have a designated work area for school. Make sure your child gets dressed each day and follows a routine similar to if he or she was actually going to school.
Get the Right Technology
Remote learning involves a lot of technology. Your child will need a computer or laptop with a camera so that he or she can see the teacher during the day. If you have an old desktop you aren’t using anymore, you can go to a local computer repair Baytown TX, and see about getting it fixed. You can also check with your child’s school for help.
Plan for Breaks
It’s unrealistic to expect children to sit in front of a screen all day and pay attention. You need to make sure your little one gets plenty of breaks where he or she is able to move around and burn off some energy. The teacher likely has breaks planned into the day, so you should have movement activities planned during this time.
There’s no easy way to get through all of this, so don’t be too hard on yourself. There will be some days where things just don’t go as planned, and that’s OK. Just do the best that you can and your child will be just fine.
