**BREAKING NEWS: Mysterious "Echoes of the Old" Phenomenon Spells Unsettling Changes for Eldermere**

Eldermere’s ancient stone bridges, long whispered about in local lore, have begun exhibiting strange phenomena overnight. Residents reported hearing faint, repetitive chants of a forgotten tongue—*Kaelthari*—in the hollows of the city’s ruins, especially around the **Blackthorn Market** and the **Forgotten Bridge**. Some claim to see flickering shadows of skeletal figures standing atop the crumbling arches at midnight.

Local historians insist the sounds are part of an ancient ritual, but archaeologist Dr. Veyra Solmire warns, *"The city’s energy is shifting. The records say this hasn’t happened in 200 years."* Authorities are deploying silent watchers near the bridges, but the chants grow louder—now followed by the sound of distant drumbeats, though no one has seen a single living person performing them.

Eldermere’s mayor has declared a curfew for the next three nights, urging residents to avoid the bridges after dark. Meanwhile, a cryptic mural depicting a skeletal priest holding a staff has appeared overnight on the **Whispering Wall** in the Old Quarter, drawing the first reported sightings of the phenomenon.

Councilor Dain Marrow dismissed rumors of "hauntings" as superstition but admitted, *"If the people of Eldermere aren’t ready for what’s coming, we may need to take drastic measures."* The city’s clock towers, which have been silent for decades, now chime every hour at 3 AM—an unnatural interval.

**Residents are advised to report any further sightings to the night watch station at the Old Gate.** The city’s library, where ancient texts on the Kaelthari script are stored, has been placed under armed guard after a single book—*The Weight of Echoes*—was found torn open, its pages stained with what looks like old blood.
