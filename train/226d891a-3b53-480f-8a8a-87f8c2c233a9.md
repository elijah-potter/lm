The weight of the decision pressed against my chest like a stone. The path before me split into two—one leading to safety, the other to unknown risks. My breath fogged in the cold air, fingers tightening around the handle of my coffee cup. The clock ticked louder than my pulse, each second stretching into an eternity of hesitation.

A voice, distant but insistent, whispered, *"What if you’re wrong?"* But the other, quieter one answered, *"What if you’re right?"* The wind howled through the trees, carrying the scent of pine and something faintly metallic—my own fear.

I stood there, half in and half out of the decision, caught between the pull of the past and the promise of the future. The stars above were too bright, too clear, as if they were judging me. And for a moment, I wondered if I had ever truly known which choice was mine to make.
