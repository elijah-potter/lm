In a hollow room where the walls whispered secrets older than the stars,
a single candle flickered, casting long shadows that danced like forgotten dreams.
The air smelled of damp earth and something sweet—old coffee, half-buried in the past.
Outside, the city slept, its lights like distant stars in a blackened sky.

She knelt at the center of the room, her fingers tracing the cracks in the floorboards.
*"What if I was never meant to find this?"* she whispered, though no one could hear her.
The candle’s flame wavered, as if answering: *You were never supposed to be here.*

A key turned in the lock below, and the door creaked open.
But when she stepped through, the world stretched out—endless, vast, and full of questions.
Did she belong here, or was she just another shadow lost in the dark?
