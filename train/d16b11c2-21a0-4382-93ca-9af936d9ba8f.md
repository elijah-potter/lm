A library where books are bound in black leather with no pages—only blank, mirrored surfaces.
A café whose walls display only the faces of people who have left, their expressions frozen mid-smile.
A museum where every exhibit is a locked door, each one whispering the last unspoken secret of the city.
A beach where the tide never recedes; only the same endless waves crash against a shore of polished obsidian.
A park where the trees grow without branches, their trunks hollowed into dark, silent mouths.
