**The Archive and the Ledger**

In the hollows beneath the old clock tower, where the air still hummed with the last echoes of the city’s forgotten whispers, **Elara** found the memory. It wasn’t a thought, not exactly—it was a *thing*, a fragment of something that had once been whole. A blueprint, half-burned, its edges frayed with static. She traced the jagged lines with her fingers, her breath shallow in the damp. *"A vault beneath the docks,"* the memory hissed. *"A plan to rebuild what was lost."*

Outside, the rain lashed the windows of the abandoned lighthouse. She pressed her palm against the cold glass, feeling the weight of the ledger in her pocket—a spiral-bound book, its pages brittle with age, filled with names, numbers, and the occasional cryptic note: *"The weight of the past is a debt we owe."* She flipped through it, her fingers skimming over ink that had long since faded. *"The bridge,"* she read aloud. *"The one that spans the old canal. If we reroute the water—"*

A door creaked. The rain turned to sleet. She looked up. The man standing there wasn’t human. His face was a shifting void, his voice a whisper from another time: *"You already know the plan."* The ledger slipped from her grip. The memory dissolved like smoke.

She had to find the bridge before the tide turned again.
