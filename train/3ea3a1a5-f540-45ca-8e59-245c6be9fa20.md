At a dimly lit café, a lone figure—clad in an old leather jacket—sits across from a skeptical stranger, fingers tracing the rim of their empty coffee cup. The air hums with tension. *"You’re not from around here, are you?"* the stranger asks, leaning back in their chair. The other hesitates, then begins:

*"I was raised in the ruins of an old city where time doesn’t move like this. The walls remember things—whispers, echoes of voices that aren’t mine. Once, I could walk through them. Now, I can’t. Not without leaving traces."* The stranger’s eyes narrow. *"Traces?"* *"Like ghosts."* Outside, the distant hum of traffic fades into silence, swallowed by the weight of unspoken questions.
