In a dimly lit conference hall, holographic projections flickered across the walls, depicting fragmented visions of distant futures. A murmur of whispered predictions drifted through the crowd: *"The AI will rule by 2047"* whispered one; *"Borders will dissolve by 2035"* murmured another. A scientist adjusted a neural interface, fingers tracing data streams as they merged into a single, unstable timeline. Outside, skyscrapers hummed with unseen life, their glass facades reflecting shifting constellations of possibility. The air smelled of ozone and old coffee. Somewhere, a clock ticked backward.
