**The Weight of Silence**

I sat between the cracks of dawn and dusk,
a name carved into the hollow of the earth,
where time hummed like a ghost in the attic,
and words were dust in the wind.

The world had taught me to chase the fire,
to burn the night away with every spark,
but the stars did not answer—only watched,
as if they had already known.

Then came the silence, soft as a whispered prayer,
a return to the quiet before the storm.
The sky, once a canvas of chaos,
now held only the weight of stillness,
the slow breath of the earth,
the quiet hum of a mind that had learned
to listen instead of fight.

And in that space, where nothing demanded,
only the rhythm of the heart,
I found the peace I had forgotten how to name.
