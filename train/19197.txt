What did early Christians call Christianity?
Early Jewish Christians called themselves “the Way” (ἡ ὁδός), probably derived from Isaiah 40:3, “Prepare the way of the Lord.” Other Jews called them “Nazarenes,” and another Jewish Christian sect called themselves “Ebionites” (“poor people”).
