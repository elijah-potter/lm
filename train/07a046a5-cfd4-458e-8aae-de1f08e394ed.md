The flickering emergency lights cast long shadows across the cracked concrete of an abandoned subway tunnel. A single flickering bulb overhead casts a ghostly glow over the rusted train cars and debris scattered across the platform. Inside a cramped, dust-choked compartment, **Lena** clutches a half-empty bottle of water, her breath fogging the steamy glass. The distant *clang* of metal echoes from another car—someone else awake. She swallows hard, her fingers trembling around the bottle’s neck.

A key jingles in the lock. The door creaks open, revealing **Marek**, his uniform stained with grease and sweat. His eyes dart between her and the flickering bulb. "You’re the last one," he says, voice rough. "They’re closing the station in ten. No one’s coming for another hour." His gaze lingers on the water bottle. "You’re not supposed to be here. The train’s been abandoned for years."

Lena exhales sharply. "I wasn’t looking for trouble." She reaches for the bottle, but Marek’s hand stops her. "You shouldn’t have come back." A beat. Then, from the other car, a voice: *"Hey, Lena?"* The door swings open wider. **Darius**, her childhood friend, steps inside, his face pale. "We thought you’d abandoned us too." His fingers dig into his pocket—*a phone, dead.* "They’re shutting down the power. No one’s gonna find us in this mess."

Marek’s voice drops to a whisper. "We need to move." He grabs Lena’s arm, pulling her toward the door. "Now." Outside, the wind howls through the broken windows, carrying the distant wail of sirens. The last train’s lights are out. The tunnel darkens.
