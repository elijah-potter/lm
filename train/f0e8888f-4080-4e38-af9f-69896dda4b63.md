**Character A:** *"Why do you think the universe is infinite?"*
**Character B:** *"Because if it were finite, we’d have to stop at some point, right?"*
**Character A:** *"But what if there’s no ‘some point’?"*
**Character B:** *"Then what’s the point of stopping?"*
**Character A:** *"And what’s the point of not stopping?"*

**Character B:** *"You’re just talking in circles now."*
**Character A:** *"Is that a circle, or just a conversation?"*

**Character B:** *"Maybe the conversation is the circle."*
**Character A:** *"Then what’s the circle doing talking?"*

**Character B:** *"I don’t know, maybe it’s just… waiting."*
**Character A:** *"Waiting for what?"*
**Character B:** *"For you to ask the question again."*

**Silence.** A single, unanswered question hangs in the void.
