Once, a corporate policy mandated all employees to report every minor expense over $5 via a standardized form within 24 hours of occurrence.
The form required fields like: "Reason for Disbursement," "Approver Signature," and "Justification of Necessity."
Enforcement was rigorous—unreported expenses over $5 were flagged as "unauthorized" with mandatory retraining.
After a year, the policy was quietly retired without explanation.
Now, only HR logs remain—ghosts of a forgotten rule.
