Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big, happy garden, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny who loved to explore every corner. One sunny morning, Benny found a shiny red apple near the fence. "Mmm, yummy!" Benny said, but then he remembered his mommy had said, "Only eat what's grown here, Benny."

Just then, Benny spotted a bright blue butterfly flapping its wings near the flower bed. "Look, Benny! I bet you can teach me how to flap!" Benny whispered. The butterfly fluttered its wings gently and said, "Only if you promise to be kind to everyone." Benny nodded and tried to flap his own wings—it felt funny, but he kept trying!

While Benny was flapping, a sneaky little mouse named Miso scurried up, hiding behind a bush. "Oops! I didn’t mean to scare you," Miso whispered. Benny gasped. "Oh dear, I did! I shouldn’t have jumped so fast." Miso sniffed the air and said, "Maybe if you share your snack, we can make friends!" Benny’s eyes lit up. He took a tiny bite of the apple and shared it with Miso.

Soon, Benny saw a group of friends playing near the stream. They were all laughing and having fun. "Hey, Benny!" said a little squirrel. "Want to join us?" Benny smiled. He shared his extra crunchy acorn and even helped the friends build a little bridge with sticks.

That evening, as Benny snuggled under his blanket, he felt warm and happy. His mommy came in and kissed his forehead. "Benny, you were such a wonderful friend today. Remember, kindness grows when we share and care." Benny closed his eyes and dreamed of butterflies, friends, and a big, happy world.

The end!
