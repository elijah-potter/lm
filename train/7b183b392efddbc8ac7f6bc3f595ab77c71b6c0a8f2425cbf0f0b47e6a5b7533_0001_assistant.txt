Oh, my dear John, I must thank you for the 2 large eggs you gave me. I am not so well these days; my legs aren't what they used to be. Even my scales are dropping off, one by one! I fear it's only a matter of time for me. The 2 cups of milk came in handy as well, so thank you for those. It is giving me comfort in my time of need. The white self-raising flour was also a welcome gift. I feel the grip of death upon me... my teeth, once sharp, have all but fallen out. It is the end for me, John.
