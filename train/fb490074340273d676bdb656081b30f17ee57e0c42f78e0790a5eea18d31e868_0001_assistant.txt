Colors

Red is fury and passion
Slow-boiling, fast burning
Orange warms the mind
Soothes with promise of
Gentle goodness
Yellow is an excited glow
Of youth, tumbling with
Humor and playful abandon
Green is earthy petals, stretching
Leaves of growth, hope eternal
Blue is a trickster of heavenly
Skies laced with melancholy
And dispirited rest
Purple rises in majesty, a false
Delivery of knowledge and
Confidence
Amongst myriad shades 
Of everything
