A bustling, sun-drenched village nestled along a winding riverbank, where every face is familiar. Children dart between stalls selling spiced nuts and freshly baked bread, while elders trade gossip over clay pots of fermenting tea. The scent of woodsmoke from thatched roofs blends with the rustle of the river’s current. A marketplace hums with the clatter of pottery, the chime of bells, and the laughter of neighbors pausing mid-conversation to wave at friends. Cobblestone paths are lined with shared stories—everyone knows who lives where, what they do, and why their neighbor’s dog keeps barking at the neighbor’s cat. The air crackles with the unspoken: *"You’ve been here since before I was born."*
