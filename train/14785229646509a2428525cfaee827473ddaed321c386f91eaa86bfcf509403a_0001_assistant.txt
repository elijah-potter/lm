No problem, I've got restaurants for you to try and activities you can do in Vancouver while your friend is at work:

RESTAURANTS:
1) The Eatery on West Broadway: Not your traditional sushi restaurant, this place has a lot of funky, neon-inspired decor, and a menu of rolls named after pop culture icons.
2) LOCAL Public Eatery on Cornwall Ave: This place has a fabulous brunch menu and is very relaxing for a sports bar. 
3) Eighties Restaurant on Lonsdale Ave: Across the harbour on the SeaBus and up the hill is a cozy spot with a diner-style menu nestled in beautiful North Vancouver.
4) Budgies Burritos on Kingsway: For a hip dine-in experience, check out this restaurant just off Main Street whose menu is tasty and completely vegetarian.

ACTIVITIES:
1) Walk the seawall: Take in some nature and walk the perimeter of Stanley Park and under the Lions Gate Bridge.
2) Indoor climbing at The Hive: This bouldering gym lets you learn at your own pace, separating skill level by the colours of the grips on the wall.
3) Learn something new at Science World: Not just for kids, this place offers interactive science exhibits to surprise you with just how much you still don't know.
4) Take in some culture at the Vancouver Art Gallery: This gallery is known for showcasing a range of touring exhibits as well as promoting the work of local artistic legends.
