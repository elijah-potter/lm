The abandoned cathedral’s hidden alcove beneath the stained-glass choir loft, where the last candle flickered against the damp stone. The air hummed with echoes of confessions whispered by those who dared enter after hours—some sought solace, others just the thrill of the silence. The walls were thick with age, and the whispers slithered through like smoke, never quite forming words but always carrying meaning. The only light came from the narrow, flickering lanterns left by the occasional visitor, casting long shadows that twisted into shapes no one could identify.
