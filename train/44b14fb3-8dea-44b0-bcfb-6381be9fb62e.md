The old library’s dusty shelves whispered secrets as Elias pressed his palm against the cold glass of the reading window. The air smelled of aged parchment and forgotten tomes. Outside, the city’s neon glow pulsed like a dying heartbeat.

---

A letter arrived that morning, sealed with a wax stamp that read *"Urgent: Do not open."* Inside was a single sheet of paper with a single number: **3472**. The postmark was from a village called Eldermere, where the last of the old magi had vanished without a trace.

Elias had been tracking the number for years—every time it appeared in dreams, in riddles, or in the cracks between cracks in the wall. Now, the moment had come.

He turned the page and found a map drawn in ink that had long since faded. Three paths lay before him:

1. **The Clockwork Spire** – A towering structure in the northern district, said to be a prison for forgotten inventions. The key was said to be in the hands of a man who never slept.

2. **The Hollow Cathedral** – A crumbling church beneath the docks, where the last of the drowned monks still whispered in the dark. The door was marked with a sigil that glowed faintly when held to the light.

3. **The Whispering Well** – A deep, silent well in the old cemetery, where the water ran backward. Those who drank from it swore they heard their own futures echoing back.

The wind howled outside. Elias could choose. What would you do?
