**The Last Broadcast**

The airwaves crackled as the static hissed, then faded into the hum of the transmitter. A voice, smooth as polished steel, spoke over the interference:

*"Five minutes remaining until the final transmission. The stars above will shift tonight—something ancient stirs beneath the crust. The clock is ticking."*

The countdown echoed through the speakers:

**5:43:27**
**5:43:26**
**5:43:25**

A door in the control room groaned open. Elias stood before the holographic display, fingers trembling. The numbers blurred—**00:00:01**—and the screen glitched, displaying a swirling pattern of fractal geometry.

A whisper slithered through the vents: *"You should have left."*

The last transmission crackled into silence. The countdown stopped. The world outside the facility blurred into nothing but light.
