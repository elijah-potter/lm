What will this look like? The timeline consists of newspaper articles written by the students about myths and actual historical events.
Myths: Students looked at myths about water from different cultures. The Four Dragons is a myth from China about the creation of the 4 major rivers, including the Yangze River or Long River. Water monsterse such as the Loch Ness monster and Ogo Pogo were also investigated.
Actual Events: Students researched and investigated famous floods and disaster events caused by water. These included the Tsunami, the Red River Flood and the flood in June 2005 in Calgary
