The original Ben 10 cartoon first aired in 2005. It was a highly entertaining and innovative animated series that captured the hearts of children and adults. The show follows the adventures of Ben Tennyson (Tara Strong).
Ben was a young boy who discovered a mysterious device called the Omnitrix. This watch-like device transforms him into various alien creatures.
Some strengths of the show include its creative premise, which offered an exciting and fresh take on the superhero genre. The idea of a young boy transforming into multiple aliens and harnessing their unique abilities is both imaginative and thrilling. These are the qualities that give the Ben 10 series a sense of novelty and unpredictability.
Further, Ben is a likable and relatable protagonist. His interactions with family and friends added complexity and depth to the show’s narrative. Ben’s grandfather Max (David Kaye) and his cousin Gwen (Montse Hernandez) are two critical supporting characters that helped add lots of humor and heart to the series.
The original Ben 10 was also a top contender for excellent animation. The designs for different aliens were imaginative and well-executed. Plus, the action sequences were visually stunning and fast-paced. Using bright colors and bold character designs helped the cartoon series stand out from others of its time.
