Fiction Prompts - 

Write about a character who pretends to be brave, but is scared.
Write about a character who can secretly fly.
Write about a boy who snuck onto a flying submarine in Russia before it went off to war.
Write a story about a stolen cell phone and the thief that stole it.

Non-Fiction Prompts - 

Write about a challenge that you experienced in your life, and then overcame.
Write about a time when you explored somewhere new.
Write about a time that you made a mistake with lasting ramifications.
Choose a controversial topic from the news. Write an opinion piece on it.
The New York Times runs a weekly Modern Love column. Write your own "tiny love" column about a relationship in less than 100 words.
Write a story inspired by a memory of yours.
Write about a secret that you’ve never told the person you love.
