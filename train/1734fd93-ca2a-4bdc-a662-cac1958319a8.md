AITA for insisting my sister pay for her dog's vet bill after she lied about its origin?

Okay, this is a bit convoluted, so bear with me. It all started in late July 2022. My sister, Clara (32), announced she’s adopted a rescue dog, a small terrier mix she named Pip. She presented Pip as coming from a local shelter in Denver, Colorado, and was very emotional about giving a “forgotten” pup a good home. I was genuinely happy for her, and we all (my parents, my brother, and I) chipped in for a welcome basket.

Fast forward to November 2023. Clara called me, frantic. Pip needed emergency surgery for a swallowed object – a truly awful situation. The vet bill was substantial, around $3,500. Clara, who is a freelance graphic designer, claimed she was completely broke and couldn't possibly afford it. My parents immediately offered to cover half, which I thought was generous. I offered $500, feeling bad for Pip and wanting to support Clara. My brother contributed $300.

Then, last week, on January 17th, 2024, I ran into a friend, Emily, at a coffee shop in Boulder. Emily mentioned she's been trying to find her missing dog, a terrier mix named Winston, since June 2022. She showed me a picture. It was Pip. I was stunned. I gently confronted Clara later that evening over video call.

She initially denied it, then became defensive, then finally admitted she found Winston wandering near her apartment complex in August 2022, panicked because she's always wanted a dog, and decided to keep him, changing his name to Pip. She said she was going to “look” for the owners but never did. She justified it by saying Winston (Pip) seemed happier with her and that the original owners probably gave up looking. She then accused me of trying to "ruin" her happiness and said I was being overly dramatic. 

My parents are now furious, saying I’m being insensitive to Clara’s emotional distress and that Pip *is* her dog now, regardless of his origin. My brother is trying to mediate, suggesting we just drop it and move on. Clara is now refusing to speak to me and has blocked my number. She’s also posting passive-aggressive messages on social media about how “some family members are just never supportive.” 

I feel deeply betrayed. Not only did Clara lie to us, but she essentially dog-napped a pet and profited (emotionally and financially) from someone else’s loss. I believe she should be responsible for the vet bill, especially given the circumstances of how she acquired Pip. It feels incredibly unfair to expect me to contribute when she acted so deceitfully. I’m tempted to tell Emily about what happened, but I’m worried about the fallout within the family. My parents are leaning towards ESH – everyone sucks here – because I’m “making a big deal” out of it. Honestly, I feel like I’m the only one concerned with basic honesty and ethical behavior. This whole situation is just…damn frustrating.

AITA for insisting Clara pay the full $3,500 vet bill?
