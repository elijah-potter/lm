The Beatles were a 60s English rock band that is highly regarded as the most influential band of all time. 

The group consisted of four members:

• John Lennon
• Paul McCartney
• George Harrison 
• Ringo Starr

Their unique sound blended several different elements from a variety of genres such as pop, classical music, skiffle, rock 'n' roll, folk, traditional Indian music, hard rock, and psychedelic rock. The Beatles were pioneers in recording, songwriting, and artistic presentation, revolutionizing many aspects of the music industry and practices.
