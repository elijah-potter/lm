The **Court of Shadows**—a half-lit archway where the air hums with static. The floor is cracked stone, veined with dampness, its edges glimmering faintly like old glass. A low, winding staircase spirals upward, its steps worn smooth by unseen feet, leading into darkness. The walls pulse with residual light—flickering like dying embers—reflecting in pools of water that never seem to drain. At the center, a pedestal holds a single, half-buried seed, its husk cracked open, revealing something dark and pulsing inside. The wind here carries whispers, voices that don’t belong to any language, and the silence is thick with the weight of what might be.

A threshold where the past and future bleed into one another.
