Apples are crisp and delicious, and when you bite into them, they help to scrub your teeth clean. Apples are also a good source of malic acid, which is actually an ingredient in many toothpastes that acts as an astringent. Malic acid also helps to remove stains and increases the production of saliva.
Pineapples are so sweet that they couldn’t possibly be good for your teeth, right? Wrong! Pineapple is the only food that contains bromelain, which is a compound that has both cleansing and anti-inflammatory properties. Plus, bromelain is an ingredient in many stain-fighting toothpastes!
Who needs another reason to eat cheese? Well, now you have one! Cheese is a good source of lactic acid and calcium, which not only help to keep the enamel in your teeth strong, but also help to whiten your teeth! Additionally, when you chew on cheese, it increases the production of saliva, which helps to wash away bacteria that can lead to decay and stains.
