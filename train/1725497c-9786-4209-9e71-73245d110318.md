Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the meadow. One sunny morning, Benny found a shiny red apple on the ground. He nibbled a tiny bit and smiled. Suddenly, a little mouse named Mia ran up, her tiny paws trembling. "Benny, help me!" she squeaked.

Benny’s ears perked up. "What’s wrong, Mia?"

Mia giggled nervously. "I lost my favorite button. It’s the one with the big red heart on it! If I don’t find it, my teddy bear won’t be happy!"

Benny hopped closer. "Don’t worry, Mia! I’ll help you find it!"

They searched together. Benny sniffed the ground and found a tiny trail of crumbs from Mia’s snack. They followed it and found the button right by a big, fluffy mushroom! Mia clapped her tiny paws. "Thank you, Benny! You’re the best friend ever!"

From that day on, Benny and Mia were best friends. They hopped, searched, and played together every day, finding adventures and sharing kindness along the way.
