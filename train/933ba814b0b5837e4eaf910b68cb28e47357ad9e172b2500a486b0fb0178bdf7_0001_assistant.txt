Here's a brief timeline outlining important events around the rise, fall, and restructuring of the Thai royal family...

 1851-1868
- Reign of King Mongkut who introduced Thailand on a national stage
1868-1910
- Reign of  King Chulalongkorn who abolished slavery in the country
1925-1934
- Reign of King Prajadhipok who inherited a country deep in debt and a people that were feeling a call to self-governance
1934
- King Prajadhipok is forced to abdicate and Thailand experiences the inception of its first political party, Khana Radsadorn
1935-1946
- Reign of Ananda Mahidol who inherited the throne from his uncle at 10 years of age. His death, murdered by gunshot while in his bed, led to the toppling of Thailand's constitutional monarchy and the rise of military control
1950-2016
- Reign of Ananda's younger brother, King Bhumibol. This king made several vital decisions that altered the course of history for Thailand. Despite the Prime Minister's agreement with the Japanese forces during WWII, King Bhumibol led a movement to provide aid and comfort to American and British forces. He headed off two coups by inviting enemies to his palace amid rising tensions. He also served his people for over 70 years
