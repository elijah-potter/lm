The discovery of **quantum tunneling** in 1927 revolutionized atomic and nuclear physics, leading to breakthroughs like nuclear fission (1938) and the development of atomic bombs, then peaceful applications in energy (nuclear reactors) and medical imaging (PET scans). Later, the **double-slit experiment** (1920s–30s) proved wave-particle duality, reshaping quantum mechanics. In 2016, researchers demonstrated **quantum supremacy** with Google’s Sycamore processor, proving a quantum computer could solve problems intractable for classical supercomputers. These discoveries forced a paradigm shift from deterministic Newtonian physics to probabilistic quantum reality, fundamentally altering engineering, cryptography, and AI.
