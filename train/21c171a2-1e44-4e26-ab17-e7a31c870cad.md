A dimly lit alleyway hummed with the distant clatter of a passing bus. The neon glow of a flickering streetlight cast jagged shadows on the pavement as a figure in a worn trench coat stepped into the dimness, their boots crunching over fallen leaves. The coat was unzipped, revealing a hoodie underneath, its sleeves rolled up to the elbows. Their fingers twitched at their sides, fingers that had once gripped a phone—one that now lay abandoned on a nearby bench, screen cracked, charging cord snaking across the sidewalk.

A single, unopened text message sat beside it: *"Just... need to go."* The air was thick with the scent of damp pavement and stale cigarette smoke from a nearby dumpster. The figure paused, shoulders hunched, before disappearing into the mist of the next block, leaving behind only the echo of their footsteps fading into the night.
