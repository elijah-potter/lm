A flickering streetlight cast long shadows as a lone figure stumbled through damp cobblestones, clutching a crumpled map. Their breath came in shallow gasps as the city’s distant skyline blurred into a maze of neon signs and towering skyscrapers. The GPS app on their phone died mid-transmission, leaving only a single, red "Lost" notification. A stranger’s voice from behind called, *"Need help?"*—too late to answer.
