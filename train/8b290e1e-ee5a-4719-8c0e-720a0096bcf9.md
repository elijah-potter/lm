**Exterior: A decaying town at dusk**

The sky swallows the last golden light, its edges smothered in storm clouds. Rain lashes the broken windows of the abandoned bookshop—its shelves still hold spines cracked by neglect. Inside, a single flickering bulb casts long shadows on the floor where the last customer left their coat, damp and forgotten.

A woman in a torn leather jacket hunches over a stack of yellowed newspapers, her fingers tracing the headlines: *"Looters Take What’s Left."* The wind outside howls through the rusted gutters, carrying whispers of the past—voices of the dead who once traded secrets here. She exhales, her breath misting in the cold, and the papers scatter like autumn leaves.

Outside, the wind picks up, carrying the scent of rain and rust. The clouds part for a fleeting moment, revealing a sliver of the moon—then the sky crashes down again, heavier than ever.
