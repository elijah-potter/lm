Hilda and Flo team up to unravel the mystery of the stolen skein. The world's most valuable skein of yarn, one spun with gold and saffron, has disappeared from a collector's home. They fear the rest of the collection could be in jeopardy if the culprit isn't caught soon, but the police won't take the crime seriously.

Hilda and Flo track a series of unsolved murders dating back to the days of their youth. They're surprised to discover that an old classmate has been arrested for the crimes, but they know the authorities are on the wrong track. They stumble upon a major clue while going through the town's ancestry records.
