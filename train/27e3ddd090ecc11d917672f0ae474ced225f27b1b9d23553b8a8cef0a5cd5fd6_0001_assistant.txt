The first state was Delaware. It was the first state to ratify the United States Constitution on December 7, 1787. The state capital is Dover and is the 49th largest state.

Here is a list of the first 5 states:

1. Delaware (December 7, 1787)
2. Pennsylvania (December 12, 1787)
3. New Jersey (December 18, 1787)
4. Georgia  (January 2, 1788)
5. Connecticut (January 9, 1788)

New York was the eleventh state to ratify the constitution on July 26, 1788. The capital is Albany and it is the 27th largest state.
