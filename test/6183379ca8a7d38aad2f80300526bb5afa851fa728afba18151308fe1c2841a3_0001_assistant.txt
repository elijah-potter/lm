Alejandra Dubcovsky and Aaron Broadwell were two smart people that wanted to see if they could figure out a lost Native American language. That didn’t stop her!

Aaron is really good at Native American languages, and he teaches other students all about them. At this time, he was trying to translate the Timucua language. Did you know that many people in Florida and Georgia spoke Timucua once? However, once Spanish invaders came to the land in the 1500s, and the language was no longer spoken due to problems that arose.

Some really old books have Timucua words, just like we do today, even though the language nearly went away forever. Many people stick to the Spanish translation, but Alejandra was very curious, and she found Timucua vocabulary inside these books. 

Since 2000, Aaron has been working very hard to make a dictionary based on the Timucua language. He has his students to help him and uses computers for complex assignments. Together, both Aaron and Alejandra have made a lot of progress!

People used to think that a Spanish missionary named Francisco Pareja was the first person to write Timucua. But there could be more to the story! Alejandra and Aaron’s actions have let us know more about Timucua.
