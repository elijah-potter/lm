Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big tree outside, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy pink ears and a tail that wiggled like a spring.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to explore. "Mmm, how nice it smells!" Benny sniffed the fresh air. Then, he saw his friend, a bright blue bird named Bella perched on a branch.

"Hello, Benny!" said Bella. "Did you find a new friend?"

Benny nodded. "Yes! But first, let’s play a game. We’ll find the tallest leaf, the softest flower, and the sweetest berry!"

Together, they hopped and fluttered, searching everywhere. Benny found a leaf that was bigger than his head. Bella picked a flower that sparkled like a rainbow. Then, they shared a juicy berry from Benny’s favorite bush.

As they sat together under the big tree, Benny whispered, "You know, playing with friends is the best adventure ever!"

Bella smiled. "And being kind is the best feeling!"

And from that day on, Benny and Bella were the best of friends, always exploring, playing, and laughing together.
