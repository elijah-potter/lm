Here are some good options for Xbox headsets:

- SteelSeries Arctis Nova Pro Wireless for Xbox: This is a premium wireless headset with superb audio quality and great comfort. It has a long battery life, an adaptable fit with its elastic headband, and a wireless transmitter with various controls. It is also compatible with other devices via Bluetooth or USB-C.
- Razer Kaira Pro Wireless: This is a mid-range wireless headset that also supports connection to other devices via Bluetooth. It has a detachable microphone, a breathable design, and RGB lighting.
- HyperX Cloud Stinger 2: This is a budget-friendly wired headset that delivers good sound quality, comfort, and durability. It has a noise-canceling microphone, an adjustable steel slider, and memory foam ear cushions. It works with any device that has a 3.5mm jack.
