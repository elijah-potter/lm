## Project Chimera: Rulebook

**A Cooperative Game for 2-4 Players**

**Introduction**

Project Chimera plunges you into the roles of specialized researchers at the Global Response Initiative (GRI), an international organization tasked with containing a rapidly spreading, bioengineered pathogen known as Chimera. This virus, initially developed for agricultural purposes, has mutated, exhibiting alarming adaptability and a capacity for airborne transmission. Your team must race against time, balancing resource allocation, scientific investigation, and public outreach to prevent a global pandemic. Success depends on meticulous planning, open communication, and a willingness to make difficult choices with potentially devastating consequences.

**1. Components**

*   **Game Board:** A world map depicting key regions, each with varying levels of infrastructure and population density.
*   **Researcher Cards (8):** Each card represents a unique researcher with specialized skills (Epidemiologist, Virologist, Logistician, Communications Specialist, Security Expert, Bioengineer, Data Analyst, Field Medic).
*   **Chimera Outbreak Tokens (40):** Represent the spread of the virus across the globe.
*   **Containment Tokens (20):** Used to limit the spread of Chimera.
*   **Research Tokens (15):** Represent progress in understanding and combating the virus.
*   **Resource Tokens (60):** Represent funding, medical supplies, and personnel. Divided into three types: Funding (red), Medical (blue), and Personnel (yellow).
*   **Event Cards (40):** Introduce unexpected challenges and opportunities.
*   **Crisis Cards (20):** Represent escalating threats and consequences of inaction.
*   **Research Track:** A linear track representing the team’s understanding of Chimera.
*   **Crisis Track:** A linear track representing the escalating severity of the global situation.
*   **Dice (2):** Six-sided dice used for skill checks and event resolution.
*   **Rulebook (this document).**

**2. Setup**

1.  Place the Game Board in the center of the playing area.
2.  Shuffle the Event Cards and Crisis Cards, placing them face down in designated areas on the board.
3.  Place the Research Track and Crisis Track markers at their starting positions.
4.  Distribute 10 Resource Tokens (3 Funding, 3 Medical, 4 Personnel) to the general supply.
5.  Each player chooses a Researcher Card and takes it.
6.  Place 5 Outbreak Tokens on the starting regions indicated on the board (typically, regions with high population density).
7.  Draw the first Event Card and resolve it immediately.

**3. Turn Structure**

Each turn consists of the following phases, completed in order:

1.  **Event Phase:** Draw and resolve the top Event Card. These cards introduce unexpected situations, resource fluctuations, or new challenges.
2.  **Researcher Action Phase:** Each player, in clockwise order, performs two actions. Actions include:
    *   **Move:** Move your Researcher to an adjacent region.
    *   **Contain:** Spend 2 Medical tokens to place a Containment Token on your current region, preventing new Outbreak Tokens from being placed there this turn.
    *   **Research:** Spend 1 Funding and 1 Personnel token to advance the Research Track by one space.
    *   **Gather Resources:** Spend 1 Personnel token to gain 2 Resource Tokens of a type chosen by the player.
    *   **Public Outreach:** Spend 1 Funding token to reduce the Crisis Track by one space.
    *   **Special Action:** Utilize the unique ability listed on your Researcher Card.
3.  **Outbreak Phase:** Roll both dice. The sum of the dice determines the number of Outbreak Tokens to be placed. Starting with the region with the most Outbreak Tokens (and resolving ties arbitrarily), place Outbreak Tokens on adjacent regions. If a region already has three Outbreak Tokens, an additional Crisis Track advance occurs.
4.  **Crisis Phase:** If the Crisis Track reaches its maximum value, draw and resolve a Crisis Card. These cards represent significant setbacks and escalating global instability.

**4. Win/Loss Conditions**

*   **Winning:** The players win the game if they advance the Research Track to its maximum value *before* the Crisis Track reaches its maximum value. This signifies the development of a viable countermeasure to Chimera.
*   **Losing:** The players lose the game if the Crisis Track reaches its maximum value. This represents a global pandemic with catastrophic consequences.

**5. Difficulty Scaling**

*   **2 Players:** Start with 7 Outbreak Tokens on the starting regions. Reduce the maximum value of the Crisis Track by 2.
*   **3 Players:** Standard setup as described above.
*   **4 Players:** Start with 9 Outbreak Tokens on the starting regions.

**6. Advanced Variants & Mechanics**

*   **Mutation Risk (Trade-off 1):** Whenever a player spends Medical tokens to place a Containment Token, roll a die. On a result of 1 or 2, draw a Mutation Card. Mutation Cards introduce new, unpredictable viral traits that complicate containment efforts. This forces players to balance immediate containment with the potential for future complications.
*   **Resource Scarcity (Trade-off 2):** At the start of each turn, before the Event Phase, a die is rolled. On a 1 or 2, the general supply of one Resource type (determined randomly) is reduced by 2 tokens. This creates a constant pressure to prioritize resource allocation.
*   **Public Perception (Trade-off 3):**  The Communications Specialist's special ability allows for a significant reduction in the Crisis Track. However, each time this ability is used, roll a die. On a result of 1, a Public Perception card is drawn. These cards introduce negative consequences related to misinformation or distrust, potentially advancing the Crisis Track. This creates a risk/reward dynamic regarding public outreach.
*   **Specialist Collaboration:** Players may, as an action, spend 1 Personnel token to allow another player to perform an additional action during their turn. This encourages teamwork but requires careful coordination.
*   **Advanced Crisis Cards:** After a successful victory, replace the standard Crisis Card deck with a deck of more challenging Crisis Cards, increasing the difficulty for subsequent playthroughs.



**Designer’s Note**

Project Chimera is designed to be more than just an engaging tabletop experience. It aims to subtly introduce players to the complexities of pandemic response, highlighting the difficult choices and trade-offs inherent in managing a global crisis. The mechanics, particularly the Mutation Risk and Public Perception, are intended to illustrate the unpredictable nature of viral evolution and the crucial role of accurate communication in public health. The game is not meant to provide definitive answers but rather to foster discussion and critical thinking about the challenges of safeguarding global health. The educational goals center around promoting awareness of scientific processes, the importance of international collaboration, and the ethical considerations involved in crisis management.
