What does VR need to become truly mainstream?

Over 27 million VR headsets have been sold in the last few years. 

But how many of those are sitting on a shelf collecting dust or were pulled out for a week after Christmas before being placed back in the box and forgotten about? 

I have to admit. I haven't picked up my Quest 2 in anger for over a week..and sometimes when I do it feels like a chore. Even more so when I have to plonk the front heavy head hugger on my pinhead of a dome, then open the Oculus app before entering the Steam app to eventually pick a game. 

Everything about the experience feels clunky and awkward. From the cold face plate against my skin to the weight distribution of the itchy strap on my head. And afterward, thumbing around to find the hole for the headphones. 

So what will it take for VR. to become truly mainstream? That's exactly what we plan to discuss in this article from smaller lighter headsets to better game design and visuals.
