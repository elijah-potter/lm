Of course, here are some ideas for an Italian dinner.

Appetizers
- Bruschetta: Toasted bread rubbed with garlic and topped with tomatoes, basil, and olive oil.
- Mozzarella sticks: Fried mozzarella cheese sticks served with marinara sauce.
- Artichoke hearts: Steamed or roasted artichoke hearts served with melted butter or vinaigrette.
- Grilled calamari: Grilled squid rings served with lemon and aioli.
- Focaccia: Flatbread topped with olive oil, herbs, and cheese.

Main courses
- Spaghetti and meatballs: Spaghetti noodles with tomato-based sauce and meatballs.
- Lasagna: A layered dish of pasta, tomato sauce, ricotta cheese, and mozzarella cheese.
- Pasta carbonara: Spaghetti noodles with a sauce made from eggs, bacon, and parmesan cheese.
- Chicken Parmesan: Breaded and fried chicken topped with tomato sauce and mozzarella cheese.

Sides
- Garlic bread: Toasted bread rubbed with garlic and topped with butter or olive oil.
- Pasta salad: A salad made with pasta, vegetables, and a vinaigrette dressing.
- Roasted vegetables: Vegetables roasted in the oven with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
- Risotto: A rice dish cooked in broth and flavored with parmesan cheese.

Desserts
- Tiramisu: A layered dessert made with ladyfingers, coffee, and mascarpone cheese.
- Gelato: Italian ice cream made with fresh fruit and cream.
- Cannoli: Fried pastry shells filled with ricotta cheese, chocolate chips, and pistachios.
- Torta Della Nonna: A custard tart made with shortbread crust and custard.
- Zuppa Inglese: A trifle made with sponge cake, custard, and Alchermes liqueur.
