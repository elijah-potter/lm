Today was the worst day of my life. After working tirelessly for over a decade to build my business, I finally saved enough money to enjoy the peace and quiet of rural Guerneville, California. But all that changed when I received a text message from Chase Bank about an unauthorized $35 debit card charge from another state. 

Thinking little of it, I replied and then got a call from someone named Miss Barbara from my bank. She asked for permission to issue me a new debit card to resolve the fraudulent charge. Over the course of the next week, Miss Barbara called several times, saying there were problems delivering my card and asking me to verify my identity each time by reading back numbers from text messages she would send. 

Finally, I went into my local Chase branch today to get some answers, but what I learned devastated me! Someone drained my account completely of close to $160,000! All that hard work–- all those years—vanished in an instant. My entire life savings is gone all because I trusted someone I didn't even know.
