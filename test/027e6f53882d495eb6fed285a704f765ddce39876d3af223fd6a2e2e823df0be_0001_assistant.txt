Here are a few artistic cities to visit in Minnesota:
1. Minneapolis: Visit the Guthrie Theater, Loft Literary Center, Walker Art Museum and Sculpture Garden, Minneapolis Institue of Arts, American Swedish Institute, Historic Fort Snelling, Mill City Museum, and more.
2. St. Paul: Visit the Ordway Music Theater, Minnesota Children's Museum, Science Museum of Minnesota, and Minnesota History Center.
3. Duluth: Visit the Great Lakes Aquarium, William A. Irving floating museum, and Lake Superior Maritime Museum.
