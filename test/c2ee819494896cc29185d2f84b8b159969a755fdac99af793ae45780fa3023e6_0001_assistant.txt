Destinations that you can do on each continent, Antarctica excluded. 

The Bucket List:

1. The Great Wall of China: One of man's greatest construction achievements, this wall stretches thousands of miles and was built to protect China from the Mongol hordes to the north. Visitors can enjoy breathtaking views, soak in history, and walk the same walk that soldiers on guard did a thousand years ago.

2. The Louvre Museum: Take in one of Europe's cultural centers and bask in a museum stocked full of precious art. 

3. The Grand Canyon: A dry, desert environment punctuated by a canyon as far as the eye can see. This wonder of the natural world has a way of making a person feel small in the best possible way. The colors at dawn are something to see.

4. The Great Barrier Reef: Take a trip to the seas near Australia and dive down into this life-filled world. Let your eyes feast on the dazzling coral colors and the marine life that calls it home.

5. Machu Picchu: This ancient city rests in the peaks of the Andes mountains surrounded by greenery and clean air. A great trip to enjoy a hike and see the ancient buildings that are still standing to this day.

6. Victoria Falls: This waterfall is one of the largest in the entire world. It is located in southern Africa and offers a truly impressive vista. Take in the sounds of crashing water and witness the raw power of nature.
