Tigers are opportunistic predators. They usually hunt deer but also hunt wild boars, birds, fish, rodents, amphibians, reptiles, and insects. Only one out of every ten hunts is successful, but one deer is enough food for a tiger for one week. After a kill, they will drag their prey into cover to feed. If a tiger leaves its kill, it will rake leaves, dirt, grass, and rocks over it. Tigers grunt, growl, roar, moan, snarl, chuff, hiss, and gasp. Some people think tiger urine smells like buttered popcorn.
