The abandoned plan involved constructing a floating solar farm in Lake Erie, a 200-megawatt initiative aimed at reducing carbon emissions and boosting renewable energy production. Key reasons for abandonment include:
- **Regulatory hurdles** with local and federal agencies, particularly over environmental impact assessments.
- **Economic challenges** such as rising material costs and delays in securing funding.
- **Unpredictable weather conditions** making long-term feasibility uncertain.
- **Competing priorities** in energy distribution shifting toward terrestrial solar and offshore wind projects.
- **Public and stakeholder opposition**, including concerns over safety, ecological disruption, and long-term lake integrity.

The project was halted in 2023 due to insufficient progress on permit approvals and funding validation.
