The tiny figurine of a fox glided from the drawer, its golden fur catching the morning light. It was no larger than a coin, yet it carried a weight of meaning—symbolizing resilience in the face of adversity. The artist had carved it from a single piece of driftwood, its ears pricked upward, as if always listening. In the hands of the collector, it became more than a trinket; it embodied stories of perseverance, whispered secrets of the wild, and the quiet triumph of finding beauty in imperfection. The fox did not need to speak to carry its message.
