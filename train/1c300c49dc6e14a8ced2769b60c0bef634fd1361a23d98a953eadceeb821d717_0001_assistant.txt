Fifty years ago, children barely spent time each week watching television. Many families didn’t even own a TV, much less video games. All that has changed, though. These days, the average 12-year-old spends 4 to 5 hours per day watching TV or playing video games. 

This time was once spent out playing. Children would ride their bikes to a local park and play baseball. They would get out in the yard and play catch. Fifty years ago you rarely saw an obese child because most kids were so active. They came right in from school each day, got a snack, and took off to play with their friends until their parents would call them in at dark. 

Those days are over! It’s about time that parents and teachers realized that technology is here to stay. We’re never going back to those days of old when children played baseball in the street and rode their bikes to a friend’s house for a game of hide and seek. Technology will continue to advance, offering us more and more opportunities to sit in front of a TV or computer screen and fewer opportunities to get outdoors and get some exercise and fresh air. 

When we realize that our society has indeed evolved to a place where our kids would rather spend an entire evening watching movies, then maybe it’s time to move in a healthier direction, and with a shift of energy and attention. We can help our kids connect with fun but physical activities, and give them the tools they need for a healthier lifestyle. With just a bit of focused direction, the entire family can be up and moving, doing things together that are fun and good for you.
