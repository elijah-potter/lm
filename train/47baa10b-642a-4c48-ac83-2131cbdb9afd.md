I stood frozen by the corner of the street, my breath fogging the cold air. A car engine roared to life, tires screeching as it spun out of control. The driver—jerking the wheel—plunged the vehicle into the oncoming lane. A horn blared, but my hands were useless. The car fishtailed, its front end slamming into the guardrail with a sickening crash. Smoke billowed from the wreckage. The driver’s body slumped forward, head lolling. A woman screamed; another child cried. The crowd around us scattered like leaves in a storm. The paramedics arrived too late, but the chaos lingered like a ghost. That was the day the city lost its rhythm.
