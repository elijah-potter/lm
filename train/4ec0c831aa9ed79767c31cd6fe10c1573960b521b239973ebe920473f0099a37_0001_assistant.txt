The following films are the highest-grossing films of 1997. The top-grossing film was Titanic (distributed by Paramount/Fox) and it grossed $1,843,201,268 worldwide. It is followed by The Lost World: Jurassic Park (distributed by Universal), which grossed $618,638,999 worldwide. The third highest-grossing film was Men in Black (distributed by Sony Pictures/Columbia), making $589,390,539 worldwide.

In fourth place was Tomorrow Never Dies (distributed by MGM/UIP), which made $333,011,068 worldwide. The fifth-highest-grossing film was Air Force One	(distributed by Sony Pictures/Columbia/Buena Vista), making $315,156,409 worldwide. This was followed by As Good as It Gets	(distributed by Sony Pictures/TriStar), which grossed $314,178,011 worldwide. In seventh place was Liar Liar (distributed by Universal), which made $302,710,615 worldwide.

My Best Friend's Wedding (distributed by Sony Pictures/TriStar) was the eighth-highest-grossing film, bringing in $299,288,605 worldwide. In ninth place was The Fifth Element (distributed by Sony Pictures/Columbia/Gaumont), which made $263,920,180 worldwide. In tenth place was The Full Monty (distributed by Fox), which grossed $257,938,649 worldwide.
