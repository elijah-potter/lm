Lily the bunny loved hopping through the sunny meadow every morning. One bright day, she met a tiny acorn that rolled away from a big oak tree. "Can you help me find my home?" the acorn asked. Lily picked it up and carried it carefully.

They walked together until they saw a cozy burrow with soft grass and a tunnel lined with tiny mushrooms. "This is perfect!" the acorn cheered. Lily grinned. "Now, let’s make it even better!"

They gathered dandelion flowers, pinecones, and acorns to decorate the burrow. When they finished, the acorn twirled in the center. "This is the happiest place ever!" it said. Lily and the acorn laughed, knowing they had made their burrow the best place in the meadow.
