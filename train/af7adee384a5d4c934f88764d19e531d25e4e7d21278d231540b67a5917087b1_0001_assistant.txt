If you are looking for a thought-provoking, binge-worthy television experience, then I highly recommend The Handmaid's Tale streaming on Hulu. It is a series that will stay with you for a long time after watching it. 

The show is a modern-day dystopian tale based on the book of the same title written by Margaret Atwood. It explores the newly created Republic of Gilead just after it has overthrown the United States government. Gilead is a totalitarian state that treats women as property and enslaves “handmaids” for reproductive purposes. 

The show has been highly acclaimed, winning fifteen Primetime Emmy Awards and numerous other awards from the Golden Globes, Peabody, Screen Actors Guild, Critics' Choice Television, and Satellite Awards. The performances are all top-notch with Elizabeth Moss giving a standout performance as Offred, the story's protagonist. The storyline is gripping and suspenseful and will keep you hooked from the first episode.

Overall, The Handmaid's Tale is must-see TV. It is a powerful and important series that is relevant today and will continue to be for years to come.
