In a dimly lit server rack, a single flickering LED on the edge of a network switch glows faintly against the cold hum of idle fans. Engineers pass by without notice, their focused eyes scanning brighter indicators on other devices. A technician’s bootcamp training session ended weeks ago, and now only the most urgent alerts demand attention.

The signal—a low-frequency data packet from a remote sensor—travels through the fiber-optic cable, its pulses barely detectable against the static of background traffic. At the other end, an unmanned data center in the Amazon jungle, a single Raspberry Pi monitor flickers sporadically, its display showing "Alert: Low Power" in faded text. No alarms sound, no alerts are generated. The system’s logs show no entries for this packet over the past three months.

The signal is ignored.
