Earth Day is celebrated every year by more than a billion people across the globe on April 22nd. It commemorates the efforts people have made toward protecting the planet from deforestation, pollution, and other anthropogenic causes of harm to our natural world. First celebrated in 1970, the rallies and demonstrations that took part across the country raised awareness about environmental issues and led to the creation of the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA). Earth Day is now focused on organizing demonstrations, trash pickups, and tree plantings to help make our world a happier, healthier place to live. According to experts, some of the most important activities happen any time of the year and include activities such as recycling, planting trees, saving electricity by turning off lights, limiting your water usage, and offering your time to worthwhile organizations within your community.
