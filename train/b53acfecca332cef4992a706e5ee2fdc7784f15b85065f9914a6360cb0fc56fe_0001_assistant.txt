The polar bear is very easy to spot because it has unique, fluffy white fur. The polar bear has a neck that is longer than other kinds of bears. They have a long head with ears that are pretty small. Their two front paws are very big which makes them great for swimming in the sea. Even though their fur is white, their noses and the skin under their fur is black.  The bottom part of their feet have small, soft bumps on them. This means they are less likely to fall over when they are walking on slippery surfaces like ice. 

The polar bear is the biggest meat-eating animal that lives on land in the world today. 
The fully grown male polar bears weigh between 880 to 1,320 pounds. They can even be as big as 1,760 pounds. The fully grown female polar bears are usually only half the size of the males polar bears. They normally weigh between 440 to 660 pounds. Right before they enter their pregnancy dens in the autumn time, the pregnant female polar bears can weigh over 1,100 pounds. This is because they have lots of extra fat stored on their bodies. The adult male polar bears are between 95 and 105 inches long. The adult female polar bears are between 75 and 85 inches long. When they are born, baby polar bears are tiny. They only weigh between 1 pound 3 ounces and 1 pound 6 ounces.
