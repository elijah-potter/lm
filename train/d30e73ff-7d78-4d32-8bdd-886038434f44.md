**The Lost Clockmaker’s Legacy**

In the quiet town of Eldermere, where cobblestone streets hummed with the rhythm of midnight, old Elias Veyne lived in a half-finished workshop. His hands, once calloused with the weight of time, now trembled as he stared at a broken pocket watch—its gears rusted, its face cracked. The town whispered that Elias had once crafted time itself, but no one could recall the secret of his craft. He had vanished years ago, leaving behind only a cryptic note: *"Find the clock that was never made."*

For decades, the watch remained a relic, until a young thief named Liora stole it, driven by a debt she could not repay. She sold it to a collector, who passed it to a scholar, who—unknowing—brought it to Elias’ workshop. The clock’s gears whirred to life, revealing a hidden chamber where Elias’ final masterpiece waited: a clock that could reverse time.

---

**Ending 1: The Cycle**
Liora, desperate, activated the clock. The world flickered—her past self, younger and free, stood before her. *"Run,"* she heard Elias’ voice echo. As she fled, the pocket watch dissolved into stardust. The town remembered Elias again, his hands now steady with purpose. The pocket watch reappeared in a museum, its secrets lost to time. Liora’s debt was erased, but she returned to a life of uncertainty, forever bound in a loop of choices.

---
**Ending 2: The Choice**
Liora refused to let the past dictate her future. She shattered the pocket watch, severing the link to Elias’ world. The scholar, now free of the clock’s influence, studied it and realized Elias’ final gift: a timepiece that could only be activated by one who had already failed. The clock was destroyed, and Elias’ legacy became a cautionary tale. Liora, now a legend, vanished into the night—some say she found a way to rewrite her own story. The pocket watch’s legend faded, but the town’s curiosity never did.
