Here are five popular true crime books that you could suggest to your book club:

1.) The Nightstalker by Philip Carlo: The Nightstalker tells the true story of serial killer Richard Ramirez. 
2) My Friend Dahmer by Derf Backderf: My Friend Dahmer is a graphic novel written by a high school friend of serial killer Jeffrey Dahmer. 
3.) The Doomsday Mother by John Glatt: The Doomsday Mother tells the story of Lori Vallow who is accused of killing her two children. 
4.) American Predator by Maureen Callahan: American Predator tells the story of Isreal Keyes, a serial killer who would bury "kill kits" in various locations all over the country. 
5.) The Third Rainbow Girl by Emma Copley Eisenberg: The story of the murder of Vicki Durian and Nancy Santomero in Pocahontas County, West Virginia.
