1. What did the narrator see first?
Answer:  First the narrator saw the white bear.
2. After the white bear, what did the narrator see next?
Answer: The narrator saw the black bear next.
3. What animal from the poem had a hump on it’s back?
Answer: The camel with a hump on it’s back
4. What animal had mutton in his maw? 
Answer: The grey wolf.
5. What did the wombat do?
Answer: The wombat waddled in the straw.
6. What was the elephant waving?
Answer: The elephant a-waving of his trunk.
7. What was wrong with the monkeys?
Answer: How unpleasantly they smelt!
