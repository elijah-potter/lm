This resource tool shows all the letters that have more than one sound and all the spellings for each of the different sounds in the English language. This tool can be used with struggling readers trying to determine which sound to try when faced with a letter or group of letters.
For example, there are nine different ways to spell the sound 'oo' like in spoon. This reference guide includes them all.
There is overlap in our code, for example, the letter Y has four different sounds. All the letters with overlap are included as well as many combinations of letters with overlap ( OW can represent two different sounds=cow/shown).
This 12 page pdf organizes all the sounds and their spellings in an easy to reference format.
