The sky appears blue because sunlight gets scattered in all directions and separated into all of its different colors when it shines through the gases and particles in Earth's atmosphere, similar to a prism. Light travels in waves, with some colors traveling in large, long waves, while others travel in shorter, smaller waves. Blue light gets scattered more in the atmosphere than other colors because it has the shortest, smallest waves of the visible light spectrum, and therefore we see more blue light in the sky.
