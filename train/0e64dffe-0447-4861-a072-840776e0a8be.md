The wind whispered through the skeletal trees, carrying the scent of damp earth and forgotten secrets. On the cracked pavement of an abandoned plaza, a single, flickering streetlamp cast long shadows that seemed to dance with the memories of those who had once walked here. The air hummed with the low murmur of a city that had long since forgotten its own name.

A hand, calloused and scarred, gripped a rusted pipe, its edges sharpened by years of neglect. The metal groaned under the pressure, a sound that echoed like a sigh from the past. The pipe held the weight of unspoken words, of promises broken and dreams crushed beneath the weight of time.

*"Why do we keep digging for answers we already know?"* the voice inside her head murmured, but she pressed on, determined to find something—anything—that could explain the silence. The earth beneath her feet was cold, unyielding, yet she dug deeper, her fingers trembling not with fear, but with the quiet desperation of someone who had already lost everything.
