Is it for me?
Pinpoint English provides focused activities and resources to address specific areas of the UK Primary English Curriculum, including spelling, comprehension, grammar and punctuation, early language development -and more.
- No more searching for activities one by one –find everything you need in one place.
- The easy-to-use resources are perfect for focusing in on core skills without hours of planning, and activities are carefully differentiated so they don’t need adjusting to suit your class.
- Uses a simple three-level approach -Towards, Securing, Deeper -to ensure every child is appropriately challenged, giving an entry point to a specific topic or concept, and building to Age‑Related Expectations and beyond, with opportunities to take the learning to greater depth.
- Spelling is introduced in child-friendly language and structured tasks ensure progress regardless of the starting point.
- Grammar and Punctuation resources include revision units to consolidate learning, with child-friendly hints and answers.
- Comprehension resources offer photocopiable confidence-building activities to help children master a wide range of comprehension strategies.
