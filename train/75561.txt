Definition of Malabsorption
Malabsorption: Poor intestinal absorption of nutrients. Malabsorption can occur from diseases that injure the bowels, such as Crohn's disease, Whipple's disease, celiac disease, and many others.Source: MedTerms™ Medical Dictionary
