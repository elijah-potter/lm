- Insect tails are used as stingers, chemical weapon dispensers, egg depositors, whips, and flight stabilizers.
- Vertebrate tails are used for locomotion and balance.
- Dinosaur tails are used to balance the weight of their long necks.
- Bird tails are used to work along with wings to help them stay in the sky when flying.
- Male bird tails are used to attract a mate with colorful feathers.
- Some mammal tails are used as grasping limbs when climbing trees.
