Lydia Johnson has a passion for baking and loves making people happy. She started making baked goods and custom cakes for friends and family years ago, and her business blossomed from there. She did so well that she was eventually able to open a bakery in downtown Wazyburg she called Sweet Things. 

At first, the foot traffic at Sweet Things was heavy, but even though the business got raving reviews, traffic started to dwindle after several months. As a single mom, Lydia relies heavily on the income from her small business. She tried expanding hours, developing new recipes, and offering specials. After some of these changes, business got better for a short time, but it always ended up right back where it was. Lydia didn't have a lot of extra funds for advertising and felt it'd be best to go back to being a home baker. 

One random Wednesday, a customer came in and took videos of the store and all of the available baked goods. Lydia didn't think much of this until her phone started lighting up with notifications. It turns out, the customer had a large following on social media, and the video she posted was seen by more than a million people! 

Business started booming for Sweet Things. Lydia sold out of baked goods the first five days after the video was posted, and she has enough cake orders for the rest of the year to keep her storefront open. Lydia is grateful for the special customer who changed her business and her life for the better.
