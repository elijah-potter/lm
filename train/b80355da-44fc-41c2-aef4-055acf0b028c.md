**Discontinued Travel Itinerary for: The Ghost City of Eldermere, 2015**

---
### **Pre-Trip Essentials**
- **Route:** Highways 47 & 95 (now partially submerged)
- **Season:** Spring (rainfall peak; avoid summer dust storms)
- **Passport:** Required (visa for "Eldermere Transit Zone" expired 2018)
- **Packing List:**
  - **Essentials:** 2L water, solar charger, hand sanitizer (no public restrooms)
  - **Optional:** Noise-canceling headphones (for drone buzzing)
  - **Avoid:** Gold-plated electronics (black market prices 300% higher)

---
### **Accommodation**
- **Overnight Stays:**
  - **The Last Hotel (Closed):** Formerly "Eldermere Grand" (now a 24-hour "hotel" with no rooms—guests sleep in shared parking lots).
    - **Check-in:** Hand over ID; staff issues "temporary permit" (valid for 12 hours).
    - **Rules:** No photography, no leaving the premises.
    - **Cost:** $12 USD (cash only; ATM inside, but card reader broken).
  - **Alternative:** "Ghost Town Tent Camp" (free, but no water; shared toilets in old diner).

---
### **Must-See Attractions**
1. **The Clock Tower**
   - Stands at 147m (original height) near the former train station.
   - Chimes every hour—last known working mechanism (2017).
   - **Warning:** Rusty gears may drop debris; wear gloves.

2. **The Bridge of No Return**
   - Steel truss bridge (2015) over the abandoned canal.
   - **History:** Built to connect Eldermere with "Nowhere, USA."
   - **Current State:** Half-collapsed; steel cables frayed. **Do not cross**—cables may be live.

3. **The Boarded-Up Arcade**
   - Former "Eldermere Funland" (1998–2015).
   - **What’s left:**
     - Arcade cabinets (vintage Pac-Man, Space Invaders).
     - Broken jukebox (records still in, but no sound).
   - **Rule:** No entry past the rusted metal gates (police presence).

---
### **Food & Drink**
- **Eateries:**
  - **The Rusty Spoon (Closed):** Last remaining diner (2016).
    - **Menu (as of 2015):**
      - "Chicken & Waffles" (served with questionable ingredients).
      - "Burger Special" (patron reports "meat was from the freezer").
    - **Hours:** 7 AM–11 PM (last known operating time).
  - **Alternative:** Picnic in the "Abandoned Park" (no food allowed; rats seen).

- **Drinks:**
  - **The Last Bar:** "The Rusty Mug" (closed since 2014).
    - **What’s left:** Half-empty kegs of "Eldermere IPA" (fermented in 2013).
    - **Warning:** Barrels may be unstable; avoid leaning on them.

---
### **Local Etiquette & Safety**
- **Do:**
  - **Ask before touching** anything (ghosts report "tangling" with visitors).
  - **Carry a flashlight** (power outages common; no streetlights).
  - **Respect the "No Trespassing" signs** (police may be seen but not heard).

- **Don’t:**
  - **Record anything** (audio logs show "static whispers").
  - **Leave valuables** in vehicles (theft rates 500% higher than normal).
  - **Trust strangers** (rumor: "they’re not human").

---
### **Emergency Contacts (Unofficial)**
- **Police:** Dial 911 (lines disconnected 2016; use walkie-talkies if available).
- **Medical:** None. Last known ER at "The Last Clinic" (now a storage unit).
- **Local Hotline:** "Eldermere Emergency" (answer machine plays looped "Are you there?").

---
### **Post-Trip Notes**
- **Exit Route:** Take Exit 47B (now a "one-way" sign with no road).
- **Return Ticket:** No official way back; use a map or ask the "Last Taxi" (private vehicle that never arrives).
- **Souvenirs:**
  - **Collectibles:** Old postcards, train tickets, or "Eldermere" coins (counterfeit but collectible).
  - **Avoid:** Anything with "Eldermere" branding (black market prices).

---
### **Final Warning**
This area is no longer safe for permanent residents. **Do not stay overnight unless absolutely necessary.** The city’s collapse was sudden—last reports suggest "something moved the ground." Stick to daylight hours.
