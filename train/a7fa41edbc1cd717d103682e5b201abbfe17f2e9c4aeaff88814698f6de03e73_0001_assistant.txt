The old oak had seen many summers like this one, and it always loved savoring the moments it brought - birds nesting in its branches, the rushing lap of the stream against the bank, the chatter of squirrels as they plucked its acorns from the ground and stirred the dirt around its roots as they planted them. The clouds overhead shielded its highest leaves from the warmth of the sun, and there was a slight chill in the air against its rugged bark promising the coming of autumn.

The tree couldn't ignore it any longer - the birds were limbering up for their southward flight, their young ones eager to be away. The squirrels had already forgotten where they'd planted their acorns, and the soil bore a chill as cold mountain waters permeated it. Autumn was nigh, and the old tree had to be ready for a long sleep. Still, it was determined to celebrate the passing summer in the same way it always did: with whirlwinds of vibrant color!
