Whispering Post Office, a secluded hillside settlement in the Appalachian foothills of West Virginia, USA. The architecture features arched wooden windows and wrought-iron gates, allowing only soft, reverberating voices to drift through the wind. Local historians claim the place is haunted by the spirits of early 20th-century mail carriers who vanished after overhearing whispers of a forbidden secret. The town’s only shop, *The Silent Ledger*, sells only tea and postcards with no text. Visitors report hearing faint, unintelligible murmurs on windy nights, and some leave without ever leaving.
