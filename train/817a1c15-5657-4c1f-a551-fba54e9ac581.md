The receptionist, **Mr. Thompson**, stood rigidly behind the counter, his hands clasped stiffly in front of him. His voice, though measured, carried an unnatural warmth as he said, *"Good afternoon, sir. How may I assist you today?"* His smile felt like a forced mask, his eyes darting between his clipboard and the door like he was waiting for a bomb to go off.

Inside, **Lena** adjusted her blouse, fingers trembling slightly as she stepped forward. *"Oh, I—uh—thank you for seeing me,"* she stammered, her voice cracking. She had rehearsed this a dozen times but somehow forgotten the *"please"* and the *"sorry."* *"I, uh… I was wondering if you had any openings for a temporary position."*

Mr. Thompson blinked. *"Yes, we do. The administrative assistant position is currently vacant. Would you like to fill out an application on the table there?"*

Lena’s cheeks flushed. *"Yes. Of course."* She fumbled with the papers, her pen skipping across the form like it was writing itself. *"I—uh—I’m sure you’ll find my—uh—I mean, my application—"*

*"I’ll take that."* Mr. Thompson’s voice was polite, but his tone was already losing its edge. *"If you’d like, you can start tomorrow at nine. We’ll have everything you need."*

Lena nodded, her brain spinning. *"Yes, thank you."* She barely made it back to her car before realizing she’d forgotten to ask about the salary.

Outside, the city hummed on, indifferent to her embarrassment—or perhaps it was just another day in a world where politeness was a performance.
