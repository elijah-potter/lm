The brain, our friend in the anterior end
Where ideas are born, where thoughts do attend
Sensory cues light the way
This knowledge humans use today

It’s light as a feather at just three pounds.
With neurons and electric pulses abound.
Endless messages leave their mark.
In days of sunlight, storms, light and dark

There are other creatures.
With familiar features
Though simple in nature in vein
Higher vertebrate hindbrain, midbrain, and forebrain

If you look in the hindbrain, you’ll see
The medulla oblongata free
And the pons, a bridge, sends support 
To special friends, the heart and spinal cord

Then the midbrain, through the optic lobes
Is the centerpiece of senses to find controls
Cerebellum, for humans, is what steadies our feet
But that isn’t all; its function reigns supreme

Forebrain, which houses our thoughts, dwells
Beneeath cerebrum veils
The brainstem is safe here
The body’s overseer

From craving food to craving water
To senses, both cold and those hotter
Hormones and oxytocin 
Everything above sets our bodies into motion
