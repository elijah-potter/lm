Beatriz Flamini spent 500 days in a cave to test her mental strength and help scientists study how the mind and body react to isolation. When the forty-eight-year-old went 230 feet below ground, she took 60 books, art and craft supplies, and GoPro cameras to document her stay. Volunteers monitored her condition via her videos and left food, clothes, and water near the cave's entrance. Flamini was shocked when the 500 days ended on April 14, 2023, since she lost her sense of time after two months.
