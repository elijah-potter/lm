The dim glow of the old lantern flickered against the cracked wallpaper as Elias crouched beside the abandoned desk in the attic. His fingers traced the worn surface, searching for something—anything—worth salvaging. His breath hitched when his hand brushed against a loose piece of paper tucked beneath the half-broken typewriter. He pulled it out, the edges singed, the ink faded but legible enough to read: *"The key to the old safe was hidden in the wall behind the mirror."*

Elias’s pulse quickened. The safe had been sealed for decades, its contents unknown—until now. He traced the faded lines again, his mind racing. The attic floor creaked as he stood, the paper clutched tightly in his palm. Outside, the distant hum of the city felt like a thousand eyes watching. This was no ordinary discovery.

He pocketed the note and descended the stairs, the weight of the secret pressing against his ribs. The key was out there, somewhere in the city’s forgotten corners, and he was the only one who knew where to look.
