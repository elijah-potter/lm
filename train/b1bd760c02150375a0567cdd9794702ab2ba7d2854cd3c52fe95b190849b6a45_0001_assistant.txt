Common Literary Devices You Must Know as a Writer

As a writer, it’s not enough to just write a bunch of words to tell a story. You have to know how to use literary devices to make your writing pop. Here are some commonly used literary devices that every writer must know.

Anthropomorphism
I love animals. And what's not to love? They're cute, cuddly, and downright hilarious at times. But what if I told you that you could turn an animal into a character? Well, with anthropomorphism, you can!
Anthropomorphism is the act of giving an animal human-like qualities, such as emotions, speech, and cognition.
Take Disney's The Lion King, for example. All of the characters are animals, but they talk, have feelings, and experience personal growth.

Anastrophe
Anastrophe is a fancy way of rearranging words for emphasis. You change the order of words to emphasize a specific point or idea. Yoda from Star Wars is a perfect example of anastrophe. Instead of saying, ‘You must unlearn what you have learned’, he says, Learn, you must, what you have unlearned. It adds a level of importance to the statement.

Anaphora
Do you ever find yourself repeating the same word or phrase? If yes, you're using anaphora! Anaphora is when you repeat a word or phrase at the beginning of consecutive sentences. It's often used in speeches and poetry to emphasize a point and make it more memorable.
One of the most famous examples of anaphora comes from Martin Luther King Jr.'s I Have a Dream speech, where he repeats the phrase ‘I have a dream’ eight times.

Alliteration
You must have heard of alliteration – it’s the literary device that uses repetitive sounds at the beginning of words in a sentence or phrase. It's a technique that can add musicality and rhythm to your writing. Alliteration can create a sense of unity, emphasis, and resonance.
Don’t overdo it, though – use it sparingly and purposefully. Too much alliteration can make your writing seem forced or gimmicky.

Allegory
An allegory is a story that has a hidden meaning that's used to communicate a moral or political message. It can convey complex ideas simply and entertainingly.
