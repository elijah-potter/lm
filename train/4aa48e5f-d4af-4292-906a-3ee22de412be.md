**The Hollow House**

The wind howled through the skeletal branches of the old oak, rattling the shutters of the abandoned house. Inside, the air was thick with dust and the faint, lingering scent of lavender that had once filled the rooms.

Lena knelt beside the half-burned fireplace, her fingers brushing the embers still glowing like red coals in the dark. She had come here after the storm, drawn by a feeling she couldn’t name—something between fear and longing. The house had stood empty for decades, its walls whispering secrets of those who had lived here long ago.

A key turned in the rusted lock. The door creaked open, revealing the dim glow of a single bulb hanging from the ceiling. Someone stepped inside.

Lena froze.

The figure stood at the threshold, silhouetted against the pale moonlight spilling through the broken window. They were tall, their coat too large for the child’s body beneath. The child’s eyes, wide with terror, locked onto hers.

The adult’s voice was low, rough with something like relief.

*"You’re late."*
