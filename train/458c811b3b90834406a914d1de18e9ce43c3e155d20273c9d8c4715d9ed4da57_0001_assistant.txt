
TikTok is a popular social media platform that has attracted more than 1 billion active users. The app is primarily known for its short-form videos and viral dance challenges. Many children and even adults have been hooked on the short style of videos and the entertainment it provides. Although TikTok is used by many people to share their struggles and experiences, it has harmed many people's lives. Attention span has been reduced due to the short format videos, and watching a long video exceeding 2 minutes or reading a paragraph can be difficult for some who lose track of what they are doing. This affects a person's mental health, as it reduces their ability to stay focused for long periods of time, which can create anxiety outside of short format videos such as TikTok.

Another problem that is slowly creeping up is that TikTok has contributed to a lot of people's confidence and self-esteem dropping. This is because they are exposed to videos that portray the 'perfect body' or 'perfect life.' Young people see these videos and reflect on themselves, which can hugely decrease their confidence, hence having a massive effect on a young person's view of the world and themselves.

In terms of who it would affect most, it would be younger children, as they are the ones who are easily influenced. Seeing thousands of videos in the span of a few hours can have a toll on a young person's mind, which is curious, and views.

TikTok can be used moderately where it is not affecting you personally, and you are able to watch it without it affecting your day-to-day life by moderating the time you spend on the app.
