Choose four of the five included rotation categories to keep your math centers running smoothly:
Math Workshop allows students to participate in cooperative activities and practice previously learned skills while the teacher is able to work with students in a small group. The set up is very similar to the Daily 5 which is used in many reading classrooms. In a full workshop, students rotate to the four (or three) rotations, each rotation having a different math activity to accomplish.
