Amphitheater of ancient ruins, nestled in a valley where mist curls between stone arches. Wooden benches, weathered by time, face an open circular plaza where the last echo of a forgotten choir lingers. A central obelisk, its surface etched with faded carvings, serves as a focal point. The air hums with the distant sounds of a river, its current whispering secrets between boulders. Lanterns cast long shadows on moss-covered pillars, their flickering light creating a rhythmic pattern that draws the crowd. A stage, half-hidden in ivy, holds a single instrument—a lute draped in silk, its strings silent except for the occasional, lingering vibration. The crowd, a mix of locals and travelers, sits in hushed silence, their breath the only sound in the vast, open space.
