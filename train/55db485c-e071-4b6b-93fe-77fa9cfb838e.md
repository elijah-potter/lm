Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big, red door, lived a bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the garden. Benny had a special friend named Max, who was a bright blue bird with feathers as shiny as the morning sun.

One sunny afternoon, Benny heard a tiny voice whispering, "Help! Help! The flowers are all gone!" Benny peeked out from his burrow and saw Max’s nest covered in dust and rocks. The flowers were missing, and the garden looked empty.

Benny hopped to Max’s nest and asked, "What happened, Max? Where did all the flowers go?"
Max fluttered his wings excitedly. "I don’t know, Benny! The wind must have blown them away, but I’m so sad my nest is empty now."

Benny thought for a moment. "Maybe we can help! Let’s gather all the rocks and dust and make new flowers!" Benny hopped to the nearest bush and picked a tiny green seed. Max helped by fluttering around to catch the breeze.

Together, they planted the seed in Max’s nest. Benny watered it gently with his nose, and Max sang a happy song to keep it warm. Soon, tiny green sprouts popped up, turning into bright pink flowers with big, happy faces.

"Look, Max!" Benny said, jumping with joy. "The flowers are growing back!"

Max’s eyes sparkled. "We did it! Let’s make sure our garden stays beautiful forever." From that day on, Benny and Max worked together to care for the flowers, singing, hopping, and playing in their cozy garden. And every time they looked out, they smiled because they knew their garden was the happiest place in the world.
