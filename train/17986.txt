The writing assessment must be administered in one day. A make-up is also given the following day. Students will be given either an informational, persuasive, or narrative writing topic. Topics will be released after each test administration and will become part of the practice topic bank.
Analytic and Holistic Scoring The scoring system is analytic. Analytic scoring means that more than one feature or domain of a paper is evaluated.
Each domain itself is scored holistically. Student writing will be assessed analytically in four domains: Ideas, Organization, Style, and Conventions. Analytic scoring will provide detailed information on student writing including scale scores and performance levels.
The Grade 5 Writing Assessment will also be linked to a common reporting scale allowing for greater capability to compare scores over time. All grade five students will take the assessment on the same day.
There will also be one day for make-up.
