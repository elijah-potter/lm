I will gladly help you with this. Here is a list of some of the best egg-laying breeds of backyard chickens to consider:
1. The Leghorn - With mostly white feathers, this small breed of chicken can lay between 280-320 white, large eggs per year.
2. The Sex Link - This hybrid breed is friendly, usually has either black or reddish-colored feathers and can lay about 300 extra large brown eggs per year.
3. The Easter Egger - If color is what you desire in your eggs, then look no further than this breed that produces about 280 blue or green eggs of extra large size yearly.
4. The Plymouth Rock - This mellow breed has some beautiful feather patterns and will produce about 280 large eggs per year that are brown.
5. The Rhode Island Red - This breed produces about 260 large brown eggs yearly, and is known for its deep red-colored feathers and outgoing personality.
6. The Australorp - This hardy and gentle chicken breed has beautiful black feathers, with hints of shimmery green, and lays about 250 large brown eggs per year.
7. The Ancona - Laying about 220 large white eggs per year, the Ancona makes a good free-range chicken with nice color patterns and active temperament.
