Once upon a time in a cozy little house, there lived a bunny named Benny with a big, fluffy tail and round, pink ears. Benny loved to hop around the garden, where flowers bloomed in every color.

One sunny morning, Benny spotted a shiny red berry. He nibbled on it and felt happy. Suddenly, a tiny, bright yellow bird named Birdie fluttered down and sang, "I found a golden feather! Can you help me find my nest?"

Benny hopped over to a tree and saw a nest with a soft, cozy blanket made of feathers. "I can help!" Benny said. Together, they gathered more feathers and leaves. Benny even made a tiny nest for Birdie using his soft fur and berry stems.

Birdie was so happy. "Thank you, Benny!" she chirped. "You’re the best friend ever!"

That evening, Benny and Birdie sat under the stars, telling stories about their adventures. They knew they’d have more fun together tomorrow. And as they snuggled together, they dreamed of all the wonderful things they’d discover in the garden.
