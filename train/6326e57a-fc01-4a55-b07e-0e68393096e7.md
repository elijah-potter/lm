**New Haven, October 12th, 2023** – Authorities in New Haven report a rare phenomenon last night near the historic Clocktower District. Witnesses described a massive, bioluminescent orb drifting through the sky, emitting a faint, pulsating light reminiscent of deep-sea jellyfish. The city’s Emergency Services responded to multiple sightings, with one report of the orb briefly touching down near the old observatory before vanishing into the night. Officials are urging residents to avoid the area near the clocktower, citing unexplained behavior of nearby birds and a sudden rise in local wildlife activity. Scientists at the nearby New Haven Research Institute have confirmed no known meteorological or atmospheric conditions to explain the event. The New Haven Police Department is investigating whether this could be part of an ongoing phenomenon or a coincidence.

The city’s cultural director has scheduled a public viewing session at the observatory tomorrow evening, where the public is encouraged to bring flashlights to document the event. No injuries were reported, but residents are advised to stay alert as the phenomenon continues to defy explanation.
