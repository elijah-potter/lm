Identification & Life Cycle
The size of Carpenter Ants varies from 6 and 12 millimeters in length while their color could be black or reddish brown. There is a single queen in every ant colony that lays 15-20 eggs after making a nest in a moist wood that is called primary colony.
The worker’s ants have the task of searching for food to feed younger larvae as well as have the responsibility of enlarging the nest into the satellite colony which may be located in dry wood. Ant colonies grow quickly and reach 3,000 workers in a timespan of three to six years while winged reproductive females move away to develop new colonies.
Generally, Carpenter Ants feed on honeydew and extrafloral nectar but they can search for food like grease, meat and eggs. Do you know how ants communicate with each other to deliver the information of a food source? With biochemical pheromones, these pests mark the shortest path from nest to the food source.
When workers follow the trail then a forging trail is developed which can be above or below the surface. The bite of carpenter ants can be quite painful and they can inject a formic acid in your wound.
