Draven shows the pain he felt through revenge. Pain can be a cruel lesson because hurting others often comes back to the person who caused the pain. When he is helping his friends, he demonstrates pain is temporary and isn't always meant to hurt someone. He shows Sarah this when he says "It can't rain all the time." Pain always hurts but a person can be a victim of it or use it to fuel better outcomes. No matter what though, it will change someone.
