The reasons for type 1 diabetes are not completely comprehended, however it is thought to be a combination of ecological and genetic factors. Type 2 diabetes, on the other hand, is frequently associated with weight problems and an inactive lifestyle, however can also be caused by genetics or particular medical conditions.
For people with type 1 diabetes, treatment includes everyday insulin injections and tracking of blood sugar levels. For individuals with type 2 diabetes, treatment might consist of way of life modifications such as weight loss and regular workout, as well as oral medications to assist manage blood sugar levels.

- GLP-1 receptor agonists: These drugs help to decrease the absorption of glucose from the stomach and increase insulin production.
- SGLT2 inhibitors: These drugs block the reabsorption of glucose by the kidneys, which helps to lower blood sugar level levels.
