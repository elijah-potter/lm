Once there was a little bunny named Benny who lived in a cozy burrow under the big, soft grass. Benny loved to hop around and nibble on carrots, but one day, Benny noticed something strange—the ground was too warm and smelled sweet.

Benny wondered, "What could be growing here?" He hopped closer and saw tiny, glowing flowers with petals that shimmered like stars. Benny picked one and noticed it tasted like sunshine! He decided to bring one home.

At home, Benny showed his family. His mommy bunny said, "Wow! That looks like magic!" Benny's little siblings, Pip and Pop, cheered, "Let's dance!" So they did, twirling around and making their burrow sparkle. The flowers grew bigger, and soon, the whole burrow was filled with light.

One evening, Benny looked out and saw the moon smiling. "The flowers are glowing for you!" he said. His family laughed, and they all knew—this was the best day ever, filled with happiness and magic.
