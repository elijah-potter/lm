Advantages of Independent Scaffolding
There are a variety of benefits to using independent scaffolding. This method of learning increases the amount of time students can spend on a given task, and it also builds confidence. It also promotes collaboration and discussion between students, which increases the chances of them meeting instructional goals. Furthermore, independent scaffolding allows teachers to give students explicit instruction, while allowing them to slowly take on more responsibilities. Listed below are some of these benefits.
Using scaffolding allows you to release students in stages so that they can master a particular skill. By letting students work independently, you can then assess mastery and move on to more difficult concepts. This helps students focus on the task at hand, and it eliminates the negative perceptions students may have about the topic. Moreover, using scaffolding makes it easier for teachers to assess the effectiveness of the lesson. This is because students are able to apply the information they have gained during previous stages in a more efficient way.
With independent scaffolding, students are able to develop a positive attitude in the classroom. Students feel more comfortable and motivated when they are surrounded by supportive, encouraging environment. In addition, they also become more engaged in learning since scaffolding removes barriers to their progress. It is important to note that independent scaffolding does not interfere with differentiation, which is a method of varying levels of scaffolding. You can differentiate scaffolding based on students’ learning style.
