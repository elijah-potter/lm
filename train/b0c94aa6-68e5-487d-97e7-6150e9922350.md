A customer service representative, Sarah, tried to remain polite as she handed over a defective smartphone to a frustrated customer. "I'm truly sorry about this," she said with a forced smile, adjusting her glasses. "Let me make this right—here’s a replacement device and a voucher for your next purchase."

The customer’s jaw dropped. "You *sorry* about this? This is *my* phone!" The sarcasm was thick. Sarah’s voice cracked slightly. "I understand your frustration—"

"Oh, please. You *understand*?" The customer stormed off, leaving Sarah with a half-empty coffee cup and a sudden urge to scream into her hands.
