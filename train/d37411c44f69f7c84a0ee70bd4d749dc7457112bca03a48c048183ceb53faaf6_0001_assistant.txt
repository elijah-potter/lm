War of the World was a radio broadcast created by Orson Welles, based on the novel, War of the Worlds written by H.G. Wells. In October of 1938, Orson Welles created a radio broadcast that depicted a Martian invasion in the United States. Although the broadcast was 100% fake, people who were listening panicked and took it as an actual Martian invasion, creating mass chaos. The broadcast came in the form of news bulletins, which listeners believed to be real. Phone calls to the local police and newspapers resulted in a nationwide hysteria. This made the front page of every newspaper the next day. Welles had no idea his broadcast had caused such mass panic.
