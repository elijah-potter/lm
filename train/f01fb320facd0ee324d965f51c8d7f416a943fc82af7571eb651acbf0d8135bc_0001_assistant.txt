A haiku is a poem written in the traditional Japanese poetic style and format, consisting of three lines with seventeen total syllables (5/7/5). The first line of a haiku consists of 5 syllables, followed by the seven-syllable second line, and finally, another 5 syllable line to end the poem. Very simple, intense, and straight to the point, these short poems often focus on things such as feelings or images from nature to depict an image with words. Here is an example of a haiku:

Beautiful green waves,
Fields of grass blowing gently,
All in perfect synch.
