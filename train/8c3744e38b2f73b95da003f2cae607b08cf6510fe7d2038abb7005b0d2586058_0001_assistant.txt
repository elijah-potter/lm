B.J. And The Bear was an NBC-TV comedy show that aired for three seasons between 1979 and 1981. Billie Joe "B.J." McKay, played by Greg Evigan, is an independent and roaming professional trucker who drives on the United States highways in a red and white semi-truck; he is accompanied by Bear, his pet chimpanzee. Together, they deal with criminal situations in every location they travel through; these are frequently started by answering the cries for help from young and attractive ladies.
