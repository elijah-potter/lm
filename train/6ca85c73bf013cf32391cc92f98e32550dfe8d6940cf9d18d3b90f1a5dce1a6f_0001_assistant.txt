Comfort. Style. Class. Elegance. All your wishes will come true at the Ala Resort.

Come to the Ala Resort for a stay at one of our five on-island luxury hotels. Each hotel features only suite-style rooms, with a bare minimum of a kitchenette, indoor swimming pool, jacuzzi, two king beds, and a home theater system with a Karaoke machine. Each floor has its own full gym, steam room, and 24-hour convenience store. But we doubt you'll stay in your room all day when we show you what else we have to offer...

Come downstairs to the main lobby, where you can enjoy entertainment from famous musical artists and bands including Britney Spears, Janet Jackson, and Mick Jagger, among many others. Each night will host a new artist or group. We provide free, all-day appetizers, meals, and drinks straight to your table while you enjoy the entertainment.

Come over to the lagoon area, a spacious 20-square-acre natural saltwater pool, and go for a swim with dolphins. Or, you can ride our complimentary jet skis out from the lagoon toward the Pacific Ocean's warm, calm waters. We will provide you with all your swim gear: vest, swimsuit or swim trunks, towels, and more, as well as optional scuba gear, mask, and/or snorkel.

You can dine with us in our luxurious banquet hall, where we will serve you meals fit for a king or queen: roasted venison, Wagyu steak, turkey legs, poached salmon, and lobster are only a few of the options available. In addition to these, you can also have your pick of many different vegetables, fruits, and desserts. Our famous cheesecake topped with gold leaf and served with $10,000 lemon puree is indulgent and decadent.

Come over to Ala Resort, we have so much more to show you, but you'll have to come in person to discover it.
