What exactly is whey protein?
Whey protein is an isolated, concentrated protein found in the milk of cows, sheep, goats, and buffalo.
Whey protein can be extracted by separating it from dairy products during processing. Once the whey protein is extracted, it is in a powdered form which can be used as protein shakes.
The nutritional value of whey protein is different from the nutritional value of the milk from which it was derived.
The protein in milk is broken down into its constituent amino acids, with some of them being essential amino acids.

Are there different types of whey protein?
Whey protein is a high-quality, complete protein that can be found in a variety of food sources.
Whey is the slurry created from milk during the cheese-making process and it can be extracted in three forms of proteins:
Whey isolate, whey protein hydrolysate, and whey protein concentrate.
Whey Protein Isolate
Whey protein isolate is the purest form of protein that can be derived from milk. With an almost 100% protein content, it has the highest quality of protein than any other food source.
The main function of this form of protein is to repair muscle tissue and it is also used by many people to get in shape.
Whey protein will provide fast absorption, so it should be taken after exercise or right before bed for best results.
Whey Protein Hydrolysate
Whey protein hydrolysate is an easy-to-digest, faster absorbing form of whey protein. With the help of enzymes, the amino acid chains in whey are broken down into much smaller chains so they can be quickly absorbed by the body for muscle recovery and rebuilding.
The small peptide chains in whey protein hydrolysate are more easily absorbed by the intestine than large peptides from regular whey proteins.
Whey Protein Concentrate
Whey protein is a protein derived from cow’s milk from which the water and fat have been removed.
Whey protein concentrate is not as pure as whey protein isolate, but also contains a good amount of lactose and fat.
Whey protein is a popular source of protein because it is easily digestible and provides a significant amount of amino acids.
This protein form is the cheapest out of the three whey protein types, although whey protein concentrate is the cheapest and most affordable in the market, it still contains 30-80% of protein and also has a lot of nutrients that can provide great benefits to your health.
