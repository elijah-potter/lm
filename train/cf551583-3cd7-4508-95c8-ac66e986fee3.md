You shouldn’t be here, but the door had been left ajar, and the hallway stretched endlessly, its walls lined with flickering shadows that seemed to shift like living things. The air smelled of damp earth and something older—something that clung to the corners like dust. Your boots crunched on broken glass, the sound echoing as you stepped forward. The floorboards groaned beneath you, each step sending vibrations through your bones. A low, rhythmic *click-click* echoed from behind you, the sound of something moving unseen. The light from the hallway grew dimmer as you passed, revealing only a single flickering bulb high above, casting long, skeletal fingers across the walls. A figure stood in the darkness ahead, hunched over a half-eaten meal of what looked like raw meat, their fingers trembling as they licked crumbs from the rim of a cracked cup. They didn’t see you at first. Their eyes—black pits with no pupils—locked onto you when you finally stepped into view. "You shouldn’t be here," they whispered, their voice a rasp of static. Their lips moved, but no sound came out. The cup slipped from their hand and shattered on the floor. The hallway began to ripple. Something moved in the corners. You turned to run, but the floor beneath your feet dissolved into a vortex of swirling colors. The last thing you saw before the darkness swallowed you whole was the figure’s unblinking eyes, now glowing faintly blue. Then—nothing.
