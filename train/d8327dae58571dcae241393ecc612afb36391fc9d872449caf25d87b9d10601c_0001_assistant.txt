Dear Journal,

Since I'm writing you today, it means I haven't made my escape. But the day is coming soon enough. I've mapped out my plan and made arrangements with the dog, Buster, to cover for me. Tonight, once the humans go to sleep, I'll sneak out of my coop and hop up on the stack of crates the old man left near the fence line. It's hard for chickens to fly, you know, our rotund midsections weigh us down, but we can catch some air. Once I've made it atop the crates, I should be able to hop onto the fence post and glide down into the front yard. From there, I'm free at last. 

Buster says he'll stay up late and guard the back door, alerting me if the humans head my way. But once I'm out front, I'm on my own. That's fine by me, though. I have plans. Big plans. First of all, I'm going to cross the road. I've gotta see what the hype is all about. Then, I'm going to waddle down the sidewalk in the direction the sun sets. I've heard a rooster hollering in that direction and I think I'd like to make a new feathered friend. Hopefully, things go well with the roo and maybe I'll break him out of prison too. We could scavenge together, making a picnic of worms, crickets, and anything we can find.

I'm not saying I will do it, but IF things don't go as planned or I get homesick, I think I can find my way back home. But jumping back into the yard could be problematic. I suppose if it comes to that, I could just wait on the front porch for the humans to find me and scoop me up. Of course, I wouldn't let them win so easily. I'd make them chase me around the front yard first. 

Anyway, that's the plan and tonight's the night. Now, I've gotta go fill up on mealworms. I'm going to need all the stored energy I can get. 

Until next time... if I make it back that is. 
Chippy the Chicken
