Getting adequate sleep each night is essential to promoting good physical and mental health. Adults from the ages of 18 to 60 need to get a minimum of 7 hours of sleep each night. Inadequate amounts of sleep can increase your risk of developing chronic health issues such as high blood pressure, depression, and heart disease. As you sleep, your body works to keep you in good health and supports the healthy function of your brain. In children, adequate amounts of sleep help to support the development of their brains and the growth of their bones. 

On the other hand, getting too much sleep at night also increases your risk for chronic illnesses, including diabetes and depression. However, some individuals with sleep disorders may find that they need more than the recommended amount of sleep each night to feel well-rested and normal. Over-sleeping can also result in increased fatigue during the day, which can make it difficult to focus on school or work. A great way to ensure you are getting adequate amounts of sleep each night is to create a nightly sleep routine. You can include habits like decreasing screen time or reading a relaxing book before bed.
