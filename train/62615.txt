Mentor: Tammy Scott
This course provides hands-on experiences with fingerspelling, classifiers and American Sign Language (ASL) numbers. Topics include expressive and receptive fingerspelling, classifiers, and ASL numbers techniques. This course will introduce the top 100 Signs and cover the basics of signs that can be used around the home and classroom. Coursework includes study topics integrated with expressive and receptive fingerspelling, classifiers, and ASL numbers techniques as well as basics in deaf culture.
