*(Scene: A dimly lit office. The door creaks open. A man in a sharp suit enters, glancing at the clock on the wall.)*

He leans back in his chair, fingers steepled, lips slightly parted as if mid-thought. His gaze flickers between the speaker and the wall behind them—always slightly ahead, always slightly off. A notebook rests open beside him, blank pages marked with faint scribbles of what might have been notes. The air smells faintly of coffee and old paper.

The speaker pauses, waiting. The man’s expression doesn’t change—just the slightest, unreadable tilt of his head. He exhales, slow and measured, through the nose. The sound is deliberate, as if testing the silence. The speaker clears their throat. The man tilts his head again, this time slightly toward them, though his eyes remain unblinking, as if they already *knew*.

*(Scene transitions: The man’s pen hovers over a blank page, but the ink never touches it.)*
