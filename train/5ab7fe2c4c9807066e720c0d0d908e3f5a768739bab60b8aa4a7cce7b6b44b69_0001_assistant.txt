Long ago on the planet called Cornerius, there was a superhero called Solaire. He was known far and wide as one of the kindest, strongest, and most courageous people to have walked the planet. He dedicated his whole life to protecting his city and the entire world from any and all threats. Yet, he could feel there was something out there that was far greater a threat than he had ever faced before.

Over the next few days, it was discovered that an asteroid was hurtling toward Cornerius, at a speed and trajectory that would spell certain disaster for its inhabitants. Solaire knew what he had to do, even though there was no chance of his survival. With every ounce of his strength, he flew into space. He picked up his speed, and as he closed his eyes, he thought of all his loved ones and his beloved planet. He knew this was the best decision he could make. He flew straight into the asteroid, destroying it into miniscule fragments, rendering it a threat no more. The collision weakened him tremendously and he was not able to return to the planet in time. During his last few moments of life, he thought back and had no regrets. He lived courageously and lived for not just himself, but for others.

To this day, the planet as a whole still mourns but is genuinely grateful for Solaire's sacrifice. He still remains a beacon of hope and a reminder to do what is possible for the greater good, no matter what the outcome. His legacy forever remains, and countless others were inspired to follow in his footsteps.
