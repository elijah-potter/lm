There were once two monkeys, called Naana and Jim. They loved playing football and eating bananas. Naana and Jim were playing one day in the park with their football. Naana passed the ball to Jim who missed the ball and fell! But he was okay. They couldn't find the ball so went looking together around the park. Jim went towards the trees at the far end of the park where he found a rather odd-looking tree. He called Nana and they went to have a closer look. The tree had pink leaves and blue branches. Naana went to touch the tree and all of a sudden, the tree sucked them both in. Naana and Jim were both scared, but after 5 seconds they opened their eyes and to their amazement, there were in a magical place where there were multi-colored banana trees everywhere. Naana and Jim spent the rest of their days playing football and eating bananas together in this magical land they called 'Banana Land' and lived happily ever after.
