A dim hallway flickered with unsteady fluorescent light. The walls, worn with time, bore faint scratches and peeling paint. A figure stood frozen near the door, arms crossed over their chest, jaw clenched tight.

Their eyes darted between the ceiling and the floor, but their mind replayed what they’d seen: a fleeting figure in the distance, a silhouette against the crackling fire in the fireplace. It moved too fast, too deliberately—like it knew exactly where they were. The air hummed with an eerie silence, broken only by the occasional creak of the floorboards.

A voice, soft and distant, whispered their name. The figure spun, but no one was there. Just the empty room. The doubt in their chest grew heavier. What had they imagined?
