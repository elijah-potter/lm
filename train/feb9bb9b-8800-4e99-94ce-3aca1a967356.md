---
**Fragment from a forgotten memory:**
*"You were laughing at my joke—how could you remember that?"*
Silence. The screen flickered; the last line was a typo.
The echo of a voice, distant now: *"...you don’t have to explain."*

---
**Later, in a half-remembered notebook:**
*"The tablecloth had been red. The clock struck midnight. The last thing you said was ‘I love you’—but you didn’t mean it?"*
A single tear on the page, smudged by time.
