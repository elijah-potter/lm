Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop and play. One sunny morning, Benny found a tiny red flower near his burrow. He picked it and wondered if it would grow big and bright.

Benny went to visit his friends—a squirrel named Sammy and a frog named Fred. "Can you help me?" Benny asked. Sammy dug a hole, and Fred sang a happy song while he waited.

Soon, the red flower grew taller and taller. Benny watered it every day with his friend’s help. One evening, Benny looked up and saw his flower was now a beautiful red and yellow bloom.

"Look, Benny!" Fred cheered. "It’s so pretty!"

Benny grinned. "I’m so happy my flower grew big and bright with you all!" Benny hugged his friends and said goodbye. He knew they would help take care of it next time.

As the sun set, Benny curled up in his burrow, dreaming of his magical flower.
