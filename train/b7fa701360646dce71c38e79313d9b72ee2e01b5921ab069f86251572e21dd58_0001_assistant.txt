To All Puppy Parents,
	
We want to remind you of the importance of brushing your dog's teeth. Brushing your pups' teeth can reduce the likelihood of many diseases in the future and keep their breath smelling great! 

While chewing-bones are great for removing plaque and tartar, brushing your dog's teeth gives extra assurance that you are giving the best possible care. Remember always to start slowly and try to get your dog used to teeth brushing as a puppy. Brushing their teeth daily is excellent, and there are many flavor options as well.

Remember, a dog's mouth harbors tons of bacteria, and you are doing your part to keep your dog healthy!

Happy Brushing!
