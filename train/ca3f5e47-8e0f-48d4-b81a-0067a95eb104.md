**The Clockmaker’s Apprentice**
In a dimly lit workshop, Elias polished brass gears under a flickering lantern. His master, the reclusive clockmaker, whispered, *"Time is the hidden thread that binds all things."* A rusted pocket watch lay abandoned near the forge. When Elias turned it over, the face was blank, but a single word glowed in gold script: **"DIVINE HOURS."**

Elias pressed it to his ear—*tick-tock*—and the walls trembled. Outside, the city’s clock tower struck midnight, but Elias had already run. He hid beneath a bridge, where the river’s current carried a whisper: *"Find the key beneath the oak, where shadows dance with light."*

A hidden ledger, later found, revealed the message was a cipher: **"13-21-05"** (the coordinates of a ruined church). Inside, an ancient mechanism hummed to life, revealing a brass key shaped like a **serpent’s coiled tongue**.
