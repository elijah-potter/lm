Rustling whispers of autumn leaves dance in the wind, their weight shifting like secrets carried on the breeze. The air hums with the faint, sweet tang of pine, mingling with the earthy scent of damp soil after rain. Beneath your feet, the ground is a soft, yielding carpet of moss and fallen twigs, cool to the touch. The distant murmur of a rushing stream is a soothing lullaby, its current caressing pebbles and smooth stones, each one whispering ancient stories. The warmth of morning sun filters through the canopy above, brushing the bark of trees with golden flecks, while the damp, clammy embrace of evening mist lingers like a ghostly shadow, wrapping around the roots of things forgotten.
