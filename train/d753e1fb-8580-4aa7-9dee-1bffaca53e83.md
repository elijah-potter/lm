A cyclical routine where each iteration introduces a micro-shift: the morning coffee brews from a slightly different ratio of beans, the bus route changes by one stop, and the neighbor’s cat walks past the door at an unexpected time. The TV static crackles with a different news ticker, and the weather app’s forecast subtly alters—even though it’s the same day—because the satellite path or local sensors adjust. The fridge’s magnetized schedule is reordered, the mail carrier’s bag is heavier or lighter, and the clock on the wall ticks one second off. The only constant is the feeling that something, no matter how small, has shifted.
