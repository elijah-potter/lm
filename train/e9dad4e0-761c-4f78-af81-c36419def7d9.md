The **Grand Canyon National Park**—walled by jagged cliffs of **100+ million years** of exposed stratigraphy, from red **Coconino Sandstone** to deep blue **Tusayan Limestone**, carved by the **Colorado River**. The **South Rim’s** eerie blue skies contrast with the **North Rim’s** forested, untouched wilderness. Hike the **Trail of Time** (30 miles) for a mythic experience: ancient petroglyphs, abandoned mining camps, and the river’s whisper of a future erosion that will erase this place entirely. Yet, beneath the desert’s harsh light, it holds **ancient Puebloan villages**, **fossilized conifers**, and **canyon-wide views** of the Arizona Skyway—where the horizon bends into the unknown. The familiar is the scale of a world, the new is that it’s **both a monument and a dying landscape**.
