**The Last Light**
Sunset over the docks, where shadows stretched like forgotten hands.
I kept the keys in my pocket—
not for the boat, but for the door.
You’ll see the light when you pass by.
Don’t forget the salt on your hands.
The tide comes, the tide goes.
But the memory stays.
A reminder carved in the wood:
*"Turn left at the end of the street.
The house with the red door
has a doorbell that rings for the lost."*
