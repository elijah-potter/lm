Dear George,

I'm sending this letter to let you know the night we step into the ring is going to go down in history. I wish you luck because you'll surely need it to last 8 rounds against the Greatest.

I have much respect for your powerful punches, one solid hit from those guns could topple an elephant. You know, a single weapon does not make you a champion. You are lacking in variety, strategy, and most of all, endurance. You'll see, George.

The main reason I think this night will be a historic one is that it will be the night I evaded your lumbering haymakers and knocked you clean out. I am going to prove once and for all that the title is mine by right. Please come prepared to put on your best performance, because that's absolutely what everyone is expecting, and you'll need it to go toe to toe with me.

No matter the outcome of the fight, afterward we will shake hands like the men and warriors we are, with our chins held high.

Regards,
Mohammed Ali
