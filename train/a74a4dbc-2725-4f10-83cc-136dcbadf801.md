Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around the garden every sunny day. One morning, Benny found a shiny golden acorn rolling near his favorite flower bed.

"Oh, Benny, what a special treasure!" Benny said happily, picking it up. But then, he heard a tiny voice: "Help me, please!" Benny looked down and saw a little mouse stuck in a thorny bush.

Benny hopped over and gently untangled the mouse's tail. "Here, let me help you," Benny said, handing the mouse a tiny piece of his favorite carrot.

"Thank you, Benny!" the mouse squeaked happily, running off to share the good news.

From that day on, Benny and the mouse became the best friends ever. They played together, shared their snacks, and even helped each other when a big storm came. Together, they made the garden the happiest place in the whole world! Benny and the mouse learned that kindness and friendship make everything brighter and sweeter.
