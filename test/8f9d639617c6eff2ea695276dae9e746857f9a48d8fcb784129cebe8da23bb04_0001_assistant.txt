Haruto stood out behind the school in the fading daylight. He breathed into his hands and rubbed them together, looking at the sky as he mustered up his courage. He checked his phone. It was two minutes past their meeting time. For a moment, he thought Mizuki wouldn't show up, but then he heard the crunching of her footsteps on the dusty earth. She rounded the corner, and Haruto felt himself tense up. This was the moment he'd been waiting for. "Yo, Mizuki."

"Hi, Haruto," she said, stopping in front of him. She gave him a broad smile, her long dark hair falling in waves over her shoulders. 

Haruto took a deep breath. "I'm sure you know why I called you here. It's probably written on my face. I'm crazy nervous, too. But I'll regret it if I never say anything."

Mizuki squeezed her hands together as she listened to him. "I know we've known each other for a few years. We've been good friends, and I love that. I cherish all the memories we have together. I really didn't want to ruin our friendship. At some point though, I couldn't contain myself. I was always watching you. The way your eyes scrunch up when you laugh, the way you always agree even when you don't want to, how you're actually better at writing with your left hand but do everything else with your right; I love it all. I love you, Mizuki."

Haruto's face was serious, his eyes intense as he looked at her. Mizuki smiled at him, meeting his courage head-on. "I'm sorry, Haruto. I can't reciprocate your feelings. I'm too busy with things right now to think about romance. And you're just a really good friend in my eyes. I know I'm hurting you by saying this, though. I'm sorry..."

Mizuki looked down, then, her guilt weighing heavy before Haruto burst out, "Well, dang. I knew it would hurt, but it's ten times worse than I imagined."

"What?" she asked, surprised.

"Kawakami-san already told me. I knew you'd reject me, but I couldn't sit still, regardless. Thank you for meeting me head-on, though. I really appreciate it. I can finally move on now... sorry, I should go before I look like an idiot."
