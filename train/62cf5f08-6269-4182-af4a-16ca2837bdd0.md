**"The Last Transmission"**

We didn’t mean for this to happen.

The server’s fan spun down mid-sentence. Static hissed through the feed, a digital heartbeat slowing to a halt. The engineer’s hands trembled as they hit *reboot*—only for the system to lock, pixels bleeding into a dead screen. The last message, unread, was still on the console:

*"Backup complete. Secure the data."*

Outside, the storm rolled in like a ghost. Rain hammered the reinforced glass, turning the office into a cathedral of glass and grief. The backup drive sat untouched, a relic in the wreckage. Somewhere, a fire alarm blared—too late.

They had tried to contain it. But containment is for storms, not ghosts. The data wasn’t gone. It was waiting, buried in the cold, waiting for the right hands to retrieve it.

Now, the only thing left was the echo.
