**Tropical coastal city:**
Midday: 28–32°C (82–90°F), high humidity (70–80%).
Evening: 26–30°C (79–86°F).
Foggy mornings possible (15–20°C/59–68°F).

**Arid desert:**
Daytime: 40–50°C (104–122°F) with dry air.
Nighttime: 20–25°C (68–77°F).
Sandstorms may drop temps by 10°C (18°F) temporarily.

**Alpine valley:**
Summer: 12–18°C (54–64°F) at 1,000m; -5°C (23°F) at 2,500m.
Winter: -10°C (14°F) at daybreak, -20°C (–4°F) at night.

**Polar tundra:**
Year-round: -20 to 10°C (–4 to 50°F).
Wind chill: -30 to -5°C (–22 to 23°F).
24-hour darkness in winter, polar day in summer.
