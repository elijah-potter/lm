A promise carved into dust by time’s relentless hands.
Words meant to bind the heart now crumble like stale bread,
unwieldy under the weight of what was never meant to be kept.
The ink of commitment faded long before the ink could dry—
a mistake written in blood, erased by the tide.

The "I" that signed it was never the one who would shatter it.
A ghost’s whisper, a lie wrapped in the fragile skin of hope.
What was once golden now rusts in the shadows of regret,
a vow buried under the weight of what could never be reclaimed.

The hands that held it were not the same when the keeping came.
The world turned sideways, the scales tipped, and the balance—
if balance it was—was never meant to be true.
Now only silence remains, the echo of a hollow "yes."
The impossible promise is a wound that won’t heal.
