One day, Roger and Pupper went to the pond and watched the ducks swimming. While they were there, they watched the sun setting along the horizon. The sky turned bright orange and a quick flash of green shot out among the clouds before the sun dipped below the horizon, giving way to night.

As Roger and Pupper finished watching the sunset, they walked back several miles to their home and had a good night's sleep. When they woke up the next morning, Roger and Pupper sat down at the kitchen table to share a breakfast of bacon and eggs, before heading out again to the pond to enjoy another day of duck watching.
