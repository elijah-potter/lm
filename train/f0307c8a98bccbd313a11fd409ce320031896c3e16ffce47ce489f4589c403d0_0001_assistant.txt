Three factors that created tension and helped lead to the Revolutionary War were:
1. Britain was in debt after the French and Indian War, they tried to have the colonists pay them back for the war by raising revenue thru the taxation of the colonists. The Stamp Act, The Tea Act and the Intolerable Acts all led to increased taxation on the colonists and increased tension with Britain. 
2. Confrontations like the Boston Massacre (3 colonists were killed) and the Boston Tea Party increased tension. 
2. The colonists resented not being able to self-govern. This is where the quote from the time "No taxation without representation came from. "
