A dim glow from flickering lanterns casts long shadows across the cracked stone floor of an abandoned hall. Rusted iron doors groan on their hinges as the wind howls through gaping seams. A single, half-burned candle dangles from a nail in the wall, casting jagged light on parchment maps pinned to the walls, their edges frayed and yellowed. Figures move silently in the dark—some cloaked, others with faces obscured by veils—each carrying weapons that gleam dully in the dim light. A low murmur of conversation drifts from the shadows, punctuated by distant clinking of metal. The air smells of damp earth and something metallic, like old blood or rust. A distant, rhythmic drumbeat echoes from the depths of the ruins, growing louder as figures gather at the center of the hall, their movements synchronized in a ritual no one speaks aloud.
