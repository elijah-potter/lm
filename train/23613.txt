The body of a machinery or vehicle refers to the outer structure that encases and protects the internal components. The body typically includes various parts that are designed to improve the performance, safety, and aesthetics of the machinery, as well as provide essential functionality for the operator.
Here are some common body parts found on machines:
Cab, Engine compartment, Hydraulic system, Bucket or blade, Grille...
Overall, body parts are essential components of machinery that provide protection, safety, and functionality, as well as contribute to the overall aesthetics of the machine.
