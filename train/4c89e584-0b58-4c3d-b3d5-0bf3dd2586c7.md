The delayed cargo ship, its hull groaning under the weight of crates marked "EPILOGUE SERIES 5," drifted into port after three weeks. Passengers on the delayed flight from Tokyo to Osaka—each clutching a printed ticket with the same flight number—waited in a circle as the announcement read, *"Due to weather, no further updates."* The symbolism emerged when the last passenger, a woman clutching a single handwritten note: *"You’re the last one. The series ends here."* The ship’s captain, reading the note aloud, announced: *"We’re here, and we’re not leaving."* The crowd held signs with the same delay number, their eyes fixed on the horizon where the next ship, also delayed, was just visible.
