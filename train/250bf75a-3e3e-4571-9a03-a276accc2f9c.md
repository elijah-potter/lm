A hush settles over the hum of city lights,
silent cracks in the pavement like half-remembered dreams.
The wind carries whispers of distant rain—
soft as the sigh of a door closing,
a breath held just long enough to dissolve
into the slow pulse of the streetlamp’s glow,
its beam flickering like a heartbeat
against the dark, steady and unhurried.
The world breathes in and out, a shared rhythm,
a quiet hum beneath the rustling leaves—
where the noise dissolves into the quiet of what was never meant to be loud.
