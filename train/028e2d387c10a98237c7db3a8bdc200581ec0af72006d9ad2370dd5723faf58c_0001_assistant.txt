The southern torrent salamander (Rhyacotriton variegatus) is a small salamander species belonging to the family Rhyacotritonidae. It is endemic to the Pacific Northwest, specifically found in Northern California to Southern Oregon. This species, along with three others, is part of the Rhyacotriton genus. The Rhyacotritonidae family is unique to the Pacific Northwest region, spanning from Northern California to the Washington Peninsula. The body length of all Rhyacotriton species is less than 5 inches.
