On Christmas Eve, which is the night before Christmas and is also on the 24th of December, Santa and all of his reindeer go all around the world on his sleigh delivering toys. Santa hitches up his reindeer with Rudolph the red-nosed reindeer up front to light the way. Santa jumps in his sleigh, and they fly around the world all night delivering toys! But you have to be a good boy or a good girl to get toys for Christmas because bad little children do not get toys but instead, they get coal - or even worse, they get nothing at all. Then when he gets done delivering the toys, Santa and his reindeer go back to the North Pole until next year. On Christmas day, you'll wake up and run to the Christmas tree to see what you got from Santa!
