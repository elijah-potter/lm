The library was quiet except for the hum of ancient books whispering secrets. Elias sat cross-legged on the floor, his fingers tracing the edge of a worn leather-bound journal. The cover bore faded engravings of forgotten creatures.

*"Choose wisely,"* a voice murmured behind him. Elias turned to see a spectral librarian, her robes woven from starlight. "The journal holds a choice between three paths, each altering fate. What will you seek?"

1. **The Whispering Archive** – A vault where forgotten names echo, revealing truths that unravel time itself.
2. **The Mirror of Echoes** – A glass that shows not reflections, but echoes of past selves, offering lessons from choices already made.
3. **The Door of Thirsting** – A hidden passage to a world where nothing is as it seems—only hunger remains.

His breath caught. The choice was his.
