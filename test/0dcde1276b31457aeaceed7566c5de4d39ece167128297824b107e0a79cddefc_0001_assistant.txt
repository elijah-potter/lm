As soon as Rodney could hold a basketball, it was evident he loved sports. He has been playing them and watching them with his dad since he was a baby. Now, at eight years old, he wants to be on as many teams as he possibly can. His parents said he could play three at a time, and he chose baseball, lacrosse, and soccer for the spring/summer season. 

Playing three sports in a season is a juggling act for both Rodney and his parents. He loves every minute of it, but it often consumes his days. Last Sunday, Rodney had a lacrosse game at 10 a.m., a baseball game at 12 p.m., more baseball games if they won the first one, and a soccer game at 7 p.m. What a day! 

His mom got him to his lacrosse game at 10 because his dad is the baseball coach and had to go to the fields early. Rodney scored two goals, ran to the car to change his clothes, and off they went to baseball. Rodney's team won the first game, and he hit three RBIs. His team then won their game at 2 and were off to the championship game at 4. Rodney hit a home run and was so pleased! The team won and was going for ice cream to celebrate, but Rodney refused to miss his soccer game.

Off Rodney went to soccer, and his day was finished around 8:30 p.m. It was a whirlwind of a day, and they'll do it all again next weekend. They all know they can't keep up this pace forever, but Rodney's parents want to do everything they can to support his interests as he grows.
