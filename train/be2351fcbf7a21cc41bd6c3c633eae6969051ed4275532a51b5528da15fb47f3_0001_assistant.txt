Above me the rain came down, 
I ran from the drops like a clown. 
I was wild and free, 
Although I wet as could be. 
I jumped over the mud,
Pretended it was a flood.
Startled by the thunder, 
A tree I went under. 
Alas, soon arrived the sun,
My romping was done. 
The clouds moved away, 
I'd have to wait for another day.
When it rains it's the best, 
Better than all the rest.
