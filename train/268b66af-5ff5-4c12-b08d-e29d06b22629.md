Once there was a little bunny named Pip who loved to hop around the sunny meadow. One day, while chasing his favorite blueberry, he tripped and rolled into a big, fluffy cloud.

"Whoa!" Pip giggled. He noticed the cloud was soft and warm, like a cozy blanket. Pip snuggled inside, feeling happy and safe. Suddenly, a tiny voice whispered, "Hello, Pip!"

Pip turned to see a little bird named Birdie sitting on a cloudlet. "I heard you fell in," said Birdie. "Want to play a game?"

Pip nodded excitedly. They hopped and twirled, and Birdie told Pip a story about a big, colorful rainbow. Pip giggled and giggled, and they both soared higher and higher, laughing with joy.

Just then, a gentle rain started. "Don’t worry," said Birdie. "Clouds love to give us water to drink and flowers to grow." Pip watched as the raindrops sparkled and the meadow sparkled too.

When the rain stopped, Pip remembered his blueberry. He hopped down, found it, and shared it with Birdie. They ate together, and Pip realized how happy it was to have a friend.

From that day on, Pip and Birdie played in the clouds every day, sharing stories and laughter, and the meadow became their favorite place to hop, giggle, and explore.
