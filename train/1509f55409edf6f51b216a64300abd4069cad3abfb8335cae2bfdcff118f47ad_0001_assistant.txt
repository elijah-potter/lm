So you want to know about y2k? That thing was way overblown, it was all fine. It's not like it was a surprise — there was plenty of time to prepare. Sure, there were some bugs here and there, but there were no major issues. I can't believe people even made such a big deal about it in the first place. Those people who make computers are pretty smart, so I knew they'd be able to handle it from the start. In the end, it was a whole lot of worrying for no reason. If only everyone would have listened to me.
