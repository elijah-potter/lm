**First Ending:**
In the quiet village of Eldermere, the old clock tower fell silent at midnight, its chime echoing one last time before the storm. The wind howled through the broken branches, carrying whispers of forgotten tales. The villagers gathered at the church steps, where the dying lanterns cast flickering shadows on the cobblestones. The last candle burned low, its wax pooling around the edges. The mayor, holding a cracked mirror, saw his reflection not in glass, but in the storm clouds—his own face, frozen in sorrow, screaming, *"They took my time."*

---

**Second Ending:**
The library’s vault door groaned open, revealing shelves of dust that hadn’t been touched in centuries. Inside, the books pulsed with golden light, their pages whispering secrets. The librarian, a child who had lived in the shelves for decades, reached out and touched a book titled *"The Last Choice."* When opened, the pages were blank—except for one word: **"Choose."** The wind outside howled, but the storm had passed. The child closed the book, and the vault sealed itself. The last words on the library’s sign, *"Do not open,"* glowed faintly in the dark.
