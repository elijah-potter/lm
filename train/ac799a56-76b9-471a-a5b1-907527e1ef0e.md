A quiet promise was made at dawn,
when the first light touched the sleeping land.
A whispered vow, a fleeting glance,
unseen but felt in the quiet space.

The hands that held it tight, now free,
still bear the mark of that unseen decree.
No grand words, no public cheer,
just the weight of a heart that was sincere.

The moon saw the keeping, the stars knew the trust,
but only time, in its own slow course, would say—
*this was done.*
