The clock ticked like a metronome of patience, its second hand circling endlessly. Outside, the wind whispered through the trees, carrying the scent of damp earth and distant rain. Inside, the room hummed with the weight of unsaid things. A single candle flickered on the table, casting long shadows that stretched and warped like time itself. Her fingers traced the rim of the teacup, each stroke deliberate, each pause deliberate. The tea was cold, but the memory inside burned hotter than any flame. She exhaled slowly, as if releasing something precious—time, perhaps, or the quiet before the storm. The walls seemed to lean in, swallowing sound, while the air thickened with the unspoken. Minutes passed. Hours? She wasn’t sure. Only the moment stretched, stretched like taffy between her teeth, impossible to break without losing something essential.
