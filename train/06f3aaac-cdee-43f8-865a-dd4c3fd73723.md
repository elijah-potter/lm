A low sun casts long shadows across cracked earth where ancient stones sit half-buried. Rusted metal gates groan as they drag themselves open, their hinges screaming with age. A lone figure kneels beside a half-smoldering fire pit, fingers brushing dust from a cracked journal. The air hums with the distant, rhythmic clash of hammers and the occasional shout of unseen laborers. A child’s laughter, muffled and far-off, drifts through the open window. A single, half-eaten fruit lies on a worn leather stool—its peel curled, juice dried into the wood. The scent of damp earth mingles with the tang of salt and something faintly metallic. A small, silent bell tinkles once before ceasing.
