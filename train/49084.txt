The river flows out of Lake Izabal, the largest lake in Guatemala where a Spanish fortress called Castillo de San Felipe was built in the 1500s to protect the lake from pirate invasions. Today, the river here is spanned by one of the largest bridges in Central America. The town of Fronteras is to one side of the bridge, and on the other is Rellenos. East of these two towns are several resorts and marinas and then flows into another lake called El Golfete, with a handful of houses and tiny business on the shore. Next comes a large gorge surrounded by wildflowers and animals such as howler monkeys, until the river enters the Caribbean Sea near Livingston, a town full of Garifuna people.
