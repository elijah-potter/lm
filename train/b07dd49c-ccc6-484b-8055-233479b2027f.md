The first time I encountered a routine was in a structured programming class during my freshman year. The task was to write a simple loop to calculate the sum of numbers from 1 to 100.

My initial attempt was chaotic, with off-by-one errors and inefficient nested loops. After debugging, I refactored it into a clean `for` loop:

```python
total = 0
for i in range(1, 101):
    total += i
```

The output was 5050, verified using a known mathematical formula. This taught me how repetition could simplify tasks, laying the groundwork for later debugging and optimization.
