This chart has examples of before, during and after questions the students can ask themselves while reading. Laminate and keep with your guided reading resources or reading response journals. The charts work well with our 'good readers ask questions' recording sheet. Use during guided reading or independent reading response activities. Students choose a question for before, during and after reading to record and answer on their sheet. Students can choose their own question or choose from our 'good readers ask questions' desk chart.
