Lily had a little red balloon that floated up high in the sky. She blew it up with her breath and watched it go zoom zoom zoom! The balloon turned into a big blue fish that swam in the ocean. A tiny seagull named Sammy saw it and said, "That's my favorite fish!"

Lily and Sammy had a big adventure. They played hide-and-seek with jellyfish and chased a shiny seashell. Then, the balloon popped, and it turned back into a little red balloon. Lily smiled and said, "Let's blow it up again!" And together, they made more fun adventures.
