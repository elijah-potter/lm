## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park – A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary**

The City of Oakhaven faces increasing challenges from unpredictable weather patterns, including intensified rainfall and prolonged heat waves. The Willow Creek area, particularly the currently underutilized space at the intersection of Elm and Oak Streets, exemplifies this vulnerability. This proposal outlines a plan to transform this neglected area into the Willow Creek Pocket Park, a climate-resilient community asset designed to mitigate stormwater runoff, provide vital shade and cooling, and foster a vibrant public space for all residents. The project leverages native plantings, permeable paving, and rainwater harvesting to create a demonstrably sustainable and aesthetically pleasing environment. We request $75,000 in municipal funding, matched by $25,000 in community contributions and in-kind services, to realize this transformative project, enhancing the quality of life for Oakhaven residents and serving as a replicable model for future urban greening initiatives.

**Project Plan & Timeline**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park project will unfold across four distinct phases, each with clearly defined responsibilities and deliverables.

**Phase 1: Planning & Design (Weeks 1-4)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** GreenLeaf Landscape Architects (lead), Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (community liaison)
*   **Deliverables:** Site assessment report, preliminary design concepts (including hydrological modeling), community feedback report, finalized design plans and specifications.
*   **Activities:** Comprehensive site analysis, including soil testing and drainage assessment; stakeholder workshops and online surveys to gather community input; development of multiple design options incorporating climate resilience principles; selection of final design based on community feedback and technical feasibility.

**Phase 2: Permitting & Procurement (Weeks 5-8)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Oakhaven Planning Department (lead), GreenLeaf Landscape Architects (technical support), Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (community communication)
*   **Deliverables:** All necessary permits and approvals from the Oakhaven Planning Department and Public Works Department; procurement of all materials and contracted services (including plant stock, paving materials, and construction labor).
*   **Activities:** Submission of design plans for review and approval; coordination with relevant city departments to ensure compliance with all regulations; solicitation of bids from qualified contractors and selection of vendors based on cost, experience, and sustainability practices.

**Phase 3: Construction & Implementation (Weeks 9-16)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Evergreen Construction (lead), GreenLeaf Landscape Architects (oversight), Volunteer Teams (community involvement)
*   **Deliverables:** Completed park infrastructure, including permeable paving, native plantings, rainwater harvesting system, and seating areas; installation of interpretive signage detailing the park’s climate resilience features.
*   **Activities:** Site preparation, excavation, paving installation, planting of native trees, shrubs, and groundcover; construction of rainwater harvesting system and installation of irrigation infrastructure; volunteer days for planting and minor landscaping tasks.

**Phase 4: Grand Opening & Ongoing Maintenance (Weeks 17 onwards)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (lead), Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department (technical support)
*   **Deliverables:** Successful park opening event; establishment of a park maintenance schedule; ongoing community engagement activities.
*   **Activities:** Park dedication ceremony and community celebration; development of a volunteer maintenance program; regular monitoring of park performance and adaptive management to ensure long-term sustainability.

**Budget & Funding**

The total project cost is estimated at $100,000. We are requesting $75,000 in municipal funding, which will be matched by $25,000 in community contributions. The community contribution will be comprised of $15,000 in direct monetary donations from residents and local businesses, and $10,000 in in-kind services, including volunteer labor for planting and landscaping, and donated materials from local nurseries.

*   **Permeable Paving Materials:** $30,000
*   **Native Plant Stock (Trees, Shrubs, Groundcover):** $18,000
*   **Rainwater Harvesting System (Tank, Filtration, Plumbing):** $15,000
*   **Construction Labor (Site Preparation, Paving, Planting):** $20,000
*   **Interpretive Signage:** $3,000
*   **Project Management & Design Fees:** $4,000
*   **Contingency Fund (5%):** $3,000

The contingency fund will be used to address unforeseen costs that may arise during construction, such as unexpected site conditions or material price fluctuations.  We have secured commitments for in-kind donations and volunteer support, which significantly reduces the overall project cost. We believe this represents a fiscally responsible and impactful investment in our community.

**Community Engagement & Equity Impacts**

Community engagement has been central to the project's development. We conducted initial surveys and held public workshops to solicit input on design preferences and desired amenities. The final design reflects this feedback, incorporating features such as accessible pathways, shaded seating areas, and a children’s play area.  This project directly addresses environmental justice concerns by improving the quality of life in a historically underserved neighborhood. The park’s design prioritizes accessibility for individuals with disabilities, ensuring equitable access to green space for all residents.  We are committed to ongoing communication and collaboration with the Willow Creek community throughout the project’s lifecycle.

**Risk Mitigation**

Potential risks include delays in permitting, inclement weather during construction, and fluctuations in material costs. To mitigate these risks, we have established a detailed project timeline with built-in buffers, secured commitments from experienced contractors, and established relationships with multiple suppliers to ensure material availability. The contingency fund will also provide a financial safety net to address unforeseen challenges.  Regular site inspections and communication with contractors will ensure quality control and adherence to project specifications.

**Post-Grant Sustainability**

Long-term sustainability is a key priority. The selection of native plant species minimizes water consumption and maintenance requirements. The rainwater harvesting system reduces reliance on municipal water sources. A volunteer park maintenance program, coordinated by the Willow Creek Neighborhood Association and supported by the Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department, will ensure ongoing upkeep and community stewardship.  Educational signage will raise awareness of the park’s climate resilience features and encourage responsible use.

Your investment will yield lasting benefits for our community and demonstrate a commitment to environmental stewardship and social equity.
