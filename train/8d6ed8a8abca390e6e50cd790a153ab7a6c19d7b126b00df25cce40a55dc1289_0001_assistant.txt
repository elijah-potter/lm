You got it! Here are 5 places to have fun in Miami:

South Beach: A popular tourist attraction. This metropolitan strip is complete with sand, fun, and delicious food and drinks to enjoy.
Coyo Taco Wynwood: Delicious and authentic Mexican food made to order. Located in the heart of Miami's artistic district, you're sure to get the best Instagram photos here. This restaurant even has a secret club in the back!
Vizcaya Museum and Gardens: A gorgeous tropical wonderland. This botanical garden is located in the serene Coral Gables area. A perfect way to enjoy the perfect Miami weather.
Club Space: Enjoy live DJs and energetic vibes 24/7. That's right! This club is bumping that bass all day and all night long.
Artechouse Miami: This is an innovative art and technology museum located in Miami Beach. Complete with countless exhibits that deliver an immersive and interactive experience to all.
