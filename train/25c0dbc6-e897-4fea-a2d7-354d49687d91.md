Two engineers at a semiconductor plant misread the batch parameters during a production run. The first batch was labeled as "High-Silicon 5% Dopant," but the second batch was mistakenly set to "High-Silicon 10% Dopant." The team noticed the first batch failed quality checks (excessive leakage current) and suspected a mislabeling error. They rerun the second batch with the correct settings, achieving consistent performance. This incident highlighted the importance of double-checking documentation before manufacturing. The correct process was implemented to prevent similar errors in the future.
