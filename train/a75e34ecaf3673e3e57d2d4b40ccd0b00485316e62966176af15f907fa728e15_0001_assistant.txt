Despite the good ratings on various food review websites, my experience was nothing like those who visited. When I received my order, I was shocked to discover that the food was undercooked. The dumplings were cold and raw in the middle, the chicken was pink, and the rice was hard. It was evident that the food was not cooked to the correct temperature, and I had concerns about the potential health risks of consuming it. The staff was unresponsive when I brought the issue to their attention, and they seemed uninterested in addressing my concerns. Overall, my experience at this Chinese restaurant was a complete letdown. The unappetizing ambiance and undercooked food made it a frustrating and disappointing experience. I do not recommend this restaurant to anyone looking for an enjoyable dining experience.
