Skele the Skeleton loved beans. Kidney, chili, and black beans put a smile on Skele's face. He'd eat them for every meal! He enjoyed everything from the taste and texture to the tin cans they came in. His favorite part about eating beans was the aftermath. The unholy amounts of tooting he did after eating beans brought happiness to everyone. Skele could break conversation tension by breaking wind, he could fart a symphony that would make Beethoven blush, and he could clear out a room with one good rip.
Whenever someone took issue with his bean consumption, he'd say that they were good for his heart, and the more he ate, the more he farted!
