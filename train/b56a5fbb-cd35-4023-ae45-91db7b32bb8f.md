In the dusty corners of an abandoned library, beneath the peeling yellowing pages of a forgotten tome, a small, crumpled note was found. It read:

*"Lost in the wind, I’m searching for you. The key is hidden where the shadows kiss the stone—near the old well. Meet me there by the clockwork moon."*

A traveler named Elias picked up the note and followed the cryptic instructions. The well, half-buried in ruins, pulsed faintly with an eerie blue light. Inside, a brass key lay coiled like a serpent, its surface etched with strange symbols. Elias placed it in his pocket and moved on, never knowing the story behind the message or the key.
