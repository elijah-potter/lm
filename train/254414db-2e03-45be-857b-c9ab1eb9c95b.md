A single lamp flickers out in the dimmed hallway, casting long shadows that stretch like fingers toward the closed door. The hum of the refrigerator, once steady, now pauses, then stops abruptly. A half-eaten sandwich sits on the counter, untouched, its crust crisp under a dusting of sugar. Outside, the wind dies, and the last light of evening lingers on the windowsill like a forgotten promise. The last breath of the conversation fades into silence, just the whisper of a key turning in a lock—once, twice. The room feels smaller, the air still, but no one moves to fill it.
