A tense boardroom meeting concluded with the CEO’s announcement: *"We’re merging departments 4 and 12, consolidating teams to streamline costs."* Employees from both units protested—some argued the workload would double, others feared layoffs. Management justified it as a "necessary pivot," but morale plummeted. The new hybrid structure created silos, and neither group felt ownership. By the third week, whispers of sabotage spread. The decision’s only "win" was a quarterly savings report—no one trusted the future anymore.
