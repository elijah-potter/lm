Of course. Here is a list of 5 things to do near Cancun:

1. Downtown Cancun. This is a good place to see the local culture, you can try local dishes, shop small businesses, and even catch some Mexican Wrestling. 
2. Temazcal rituals. This is a Mayan ceremony conducted by a shaman. In the complete darkness of a sweat lodge, this ancient purification ritual can help participants meditate. 
3. Jungle zip lining. One of the signature attractions in Cancun, you can zip-line through a few different places in the area. 
4. The Cancun Scenic Tower. This tower overlooks the coast and part of the city of Cancun. It spins so a full 360-degree view is provided. 
5. Xoximilco. This is a party that takes place on boats near Cancun. It's a good way to get to sample the local cuisine and music.
