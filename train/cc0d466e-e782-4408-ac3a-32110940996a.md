The landscape resembles a vast, uncharted ocean, where cliffs tower like jagged teeth against a sky that stretches as far as the horizon could stretch if it were a canvas.

The wind whispers secrets through valleys narrower than a thread, carrying dust that swirls like embers from a distant, forgotten fire.

The trees stand sentinel, their branches intertwined like the roots of some ancient, tangled beast that has long since lost its shape.

Caves echo with the same hollow sound as a distant drum, and the light inside them is dimmer than a candle flickering in a room where no one dares to step.

The ground beneath is softer than a child’s first steps, yet harder than the crust of the world itself, with veins of stone running beneath it like the blood of a sleeping titan.

The sun rises like a slow, deliberate heartbeat, casting shadows that grow longer than the memory of those who once walked here.
