**Mayor's Office Press Release – Riverdale**
*July 12, 2024*

Riverdale residents are urged to prepare for the city’s first-ever **Solar Eclipse Community Watch** initiative, launching tomorrow as a precautionary measure ahead of the upcoming **total solar eclipse on August 2nd**. Local scientists and emergency services will deploy to key areas, including the historic **Bridgeport Park** and downtown along the Riverdale Creek, to monitor public safety during the event. Authorities stress that while the eclipse is expected to be safe to view with proper eyewear, unauthorized photography or disturbances may result in fines up to $250. The city will provide free eclipse-viewing stations with certified glasses, but supplies are limited. Residents near the eclipse path are advised to secure pets and livestock ahead of time, as animal behavior may vary during the event.

For updates, follow @RiverdaleSafety on social media or call 911 if you witness unusual activity. The city’s **Public Works Department** has also reminded residents to check for power outages—historical eclipses have occasionally caused temporary grid disruptions.

*End of Release.*
