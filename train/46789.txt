Wool clothe is a type of fabric made from sheep’s fleece. It is used in many different types of clothing and accessories, including sweaters, hats, jackets, scarves, and gloves. It has a variety of advantages over other fabrics, making it ideal for outdoor apparel and sportswear.
The manufacturing process for a wool fabric can be long and complex, and there are several steps that need to be completed before it becomes a finished product. These include shearing, cleaning and scouring, grading and sorting, carding, spinning, and weaving.
After shearing, the fleece is cleaned and scoured in a series of alkaline baths, containing water, soap, and soda ash or a similar alkali. This helps remove sand, dirt, grease, and dried sweat from the fleece. It also removes the sheath of skin that covers the fleece.
The sheared fleece is then spun into yarn by a machine that has rotating metal teeth to pull the fibers together into slivers. The yarn is then woven onto a loom to create the fabric.
Wool is woven into a variety of fabrics, from lightweight crepes to heavy double-weave crepes to tweeds. Some of these fabrics have a special twist or weave that provides them with softness and drape.
