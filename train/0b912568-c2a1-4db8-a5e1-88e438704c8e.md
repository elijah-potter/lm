The **okay hand sign** (thumb and index finger forming a circle) is often misinterpreted across cultures.

In the U.S. and some European countries, it means "money" or "all right." However, in parts of Latin America, the Middle East, and parts of Asia, it can be offensive—associated with racism, corruption, or even sexual innuendo. Misinterpretation can lead to awkward or hostile exchanges in international interactions.
