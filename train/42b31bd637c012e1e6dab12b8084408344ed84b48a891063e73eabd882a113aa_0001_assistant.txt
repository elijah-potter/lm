I'm happy to help! Here are some housewarming gift ideas:
- Welcome Mat - What better way to welcome your friend into their new home than with a welcome mat? 
- Towels and Handtowels - Matching towels and handtowels can be very soft and can match whatever decor your friends plan on using in their new home. 
- Candles - Candles can make the new home smell nice, especially if the scent is something homey like cookies or vanilla. 
- Cooking Utensils - A new set of cooking utensils may be appreciated, especially if it matches what they plan on doing with their kitchen and if they do a lot of cooking. 
- Pillows - Pillows can match the decor, and they can even be personalized with your friend's names on them.
