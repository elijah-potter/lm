A hollow vow whispered under the moon’s ghostly gaze,
promises cracked like ice on a frozen lake’s edge—
each word a blade that cuts deeper than the wound.
The burden of keeping it is heavier than the sky,
and every step forward feels like stumbling through quicksand.
Yet, the weight of silence is worse, so the hands clench,
fingers pressing against the pulse of what was promised—
a flicker, a breath, a ghost of the future’s embrace.
Some promises are not meant to be kept, but to haunt.
