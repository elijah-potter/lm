Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white ears and a tail that wiggled when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to see his favorite flowers: big, bright blue dandelions and little green daisies. He munched on a dandelion and giggled as the wind blew his ears.

A little bird named Pip chirped, "Hi Benny! Want to play hide and seek?"

Benny hopped closer. "Sure, Pip! Let’s do it!" They hopped around the garden, hiding behind leaves and under bushes. Pip flapped his wings, and Benny peeked out to see where Pip was. They laughed and giggled until Pip said, "Time to find me!"

Benny searched high and low, but Pip wasn’t there. "Oh no!" Benny said. He looked under the big tree, behind the bush with the red berries, even under the big rock. Pip wasn’t there!

Just then, Benny remembered something his mommy had told him: "If you can’t find someone, maybe they’re playing somewhere else!" So Benny hopped over to the neighbor’s yard. There, he saw Pip sitting on a branch, eating a worm. Benny hopped over and said, "Pip! I was looking for you!"

Pip flapped his wings and said, "Benny! You found me!" They laughed and played together for a long time, sharing worms and telling stories. Benny’s tummy rumbled, and they decided to eat some of the neighbor’s apples.

As the sun began to set, Benny said, "Pip, I think it’s time to go home." Pip hopped with him, and together they hopped back to Benny’s house. They cuddled up on the couch, and Benny told Pip a story about his favorite teddy bear, Snuggle.

Pip flapped his wings and said, "I like your teddy bear too! Goodnight, Benny!" Benny kissed his fluffy ears and said, "Goodnight, Pip! Sleep tight!"

When Benny woke up the next morning, his garden was still full of sunshine, and his favorite flowers were waiting for him. Benny hopped outside, munched another dandelion, and knew his adventure with Pip had been the best day ever!
