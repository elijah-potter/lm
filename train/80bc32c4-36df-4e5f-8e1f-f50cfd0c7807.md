The river whispered its secrets to the stones,
*"I am the same, I am the same,"*
while the reeds bent and the tide rose slow,
the current hummed the same refrain below.

The clock’s hands ticked backward in the mirror,
*"I am the same, I am the same,"*
echoing the old songs the dead still knew—
each cycle spun the same endless refrain.

The wind carried the same sighs through the pines,
*"I am the same, I am the same,"*
as the world turned, unbroken, unending,
repeating its eternal refrain.
