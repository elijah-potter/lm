Once upon a time in a cozy garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around and explore. One sunny morning, Benny spotted a big, shiny red apple near a tree. "Wow! A red apple!" Benny said, hopping closer. Benny nibbled a tiny bite, and his eyes sparkled with joy.

Just then, Benny heard a tiny *twinkle* near his nose. Benny looked down and saw a small, twinkly star floating in the air. "Hello there, little star!" Benny said, trying not to jump too high. The star smiled and said, "I’m lost and need help finding my way home!"

Benny’s heart felt warm. "Of course, I’ll help you!" he said. He hopped carefully and guided the star until it reached a bright, glowing moon. "There you go, little star!" Benny cheered.

As the sun began to set, Benny curled up in his soft burrow, happy and content. The star twinkled again, sending Benny a little sparkle to keep him warm. Benny closed his eyes and dreamed of more adventures with his new friend, the twinkly star.

The end.
