The air was electric at the sacred grove. Trees of every kind lined beautiful pathways of rock and soft grass. The sun was setting, casting the clouds in a cotton candy shade of pink. 

Rita, a bushy-tailed squirrel, had just arrived at the very last tree. She stood at the edge of the grove... staring at the Hazel-Dazzle-Nut that lay at the base of the trunk. This nut granted any squirrel magic powers. In awe of its mystic aura, she paused for a moment. Finally, she took hold of the nut and saw glimmers of light surrounding her. At that moment... she knew that nothing would ever be the same.
