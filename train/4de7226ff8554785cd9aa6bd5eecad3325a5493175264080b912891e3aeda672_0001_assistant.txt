-Occurred between May 10-11, 1996
-Eight climbers were caught in a blizzard on the descent from the summit
-There were two teams on the trek: the Adventure Consultants team and the Mountain Madness team
-The Adventure Consultants team was led by Rob Hall
-The Mountain Madness team was led by Scott Fischer
-Climbers died on both the North Face and South Col approaches
-Four members (including Hall) died on the Adventure Consultants team
-One member (Fischer) died on the Mountain Madness team
-Three officers from the Indo-Tibetan Border police also perished
-After the disaster, many survivors wrote memoirs of the event
-It was later discovered that both Hall and Fischer were aware of a possible major storm developing between the dates of May 8 and May 11, 1996.
