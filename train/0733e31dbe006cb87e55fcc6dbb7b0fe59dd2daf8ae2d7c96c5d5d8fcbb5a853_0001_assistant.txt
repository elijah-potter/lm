Classic Cheesecake Recipe

Ingredients

- 2 cups graham cracker crumbs
- 1/3 cup white sugar
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted
- 4 (8 oz.) packages of cream cheese, softened
- 1 1/4 cups white sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 4 large eggs
- 1/3 cup heavy cream

Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease the bottom and sides of a 9-inch springform pan.

2. In a medium bowl, mix the graham cracker crumbs, 1/3 cup of sugar, and melted butter until well combined. Press the mixture into the bottom of the prepared pan.

3. In a large bowl, beat the cream cheese until smooth and creamy. Gradually add in the 1 1/4 cups of sugar, beating until well combined. Mix in the vanilla extract.

4. Beat in the eggs, one at a time, making sure each egg is fully incorporated before adding the next.

5. Mix in the heavy cream until the mixture is smooth and creamy.

6. Pour the cheesecake batter into the prepared crust. Tap the pan gently on the counter to release any air bubbles.

7. Bake the cheesecake for 45-50 minutes, or until the center is almost set but still jiggles slightly when shaken.

8. Remove the cheesecake from the oven and let it cool to room temperature. Once cooled, cover the cheesecake with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight.

9. When ready to serve, remove the cheesecake from the refrigerator and run a knife around the edge of the pan to loosen the cheesecake. Remove the sides of the springform pan.

10. Serve the cheesecake chilled, topped with your favorite toppings such as fresh fruit, whipped cream, or chocolate sauce.
