When the coffee machine hummed to life, the decision was made. The morning’s resolve—*"Just one more chapter"*—had dissolved under the weight of a forgotten bookmark and the pull of a new mystery novel. The keyboard tapped impatiently as the mind unraveled: *"Maybe I’ll start tomorrow."* The screen glowed with an abandoned draft, the cursor blinking like a question mark. A final hesitation: *"Why not start now?"*—and the cursor vanished, swallowed by the blank page. The shift was sudden, quiet, inevitable.
