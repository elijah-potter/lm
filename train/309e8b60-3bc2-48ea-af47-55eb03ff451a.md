**Festivals and celebrations** dominate, especially during **Midsummer** and **Yule**, where communal bonfires light the night and locals gather to dance, drink mead, and sing folk songs. **Blacksmithing** and **woodworking** workshops are common in rural areas, with artisans selling handcrafted goods at market stalls. **Hunting and fishing** are central to many villages, particularly in the **Great Wastes** or **Shrinehold forests**, where tribes hold annual competitions. **Mystic rituals** (including blood offerings) are practiced by cults in the **Eldertree Expanse**, attracting scholars and outcasts. **Merchant caravans** from the **Dusklands** trade spices, silk, and weapons, while **bards** and **storytellers** perform at taverns, debating lore across the tavern walls. **Underground tunnels** beneath the city hold **slave auctions** and **black-market dealings**, with whispers of **ancient ruins** beneath the **Stone Spire**. **Cults** worship the **Old Ones** in abandoned temples, performing rituals involving **sacrificial dances** and **crystal visions**. **Nomadic tribes** in the **Desert of the Forgotten** hunt for **dragons** and **giant beasts**, trading furs and rare minerals. **Alchemists** in the **Arcane District** experiment with **potions** and **transmutation**, while **scholars** debate philosophy in the **Grand Library’s** vaults. **Temple guards** enforce strict rules in the **Holy City**, executing heretics while **mercenaries** in the **War District** train in brutal combat. **Dwarven mines** beneath the **Iron Peaks** produce gold and **dragonstone**, while **elves** in the **Whispering Glades** guard ancient knowledge. **Witch-hunts** erupt in towns where **witches** are blamed for misfortune, leading to executions or imprisonment. **Docks in the harbor** are bustling with sailors, smugglers, and **pirate ships** that raid merchant convoys. **Wizards** in the **Arcane District** perform **illusionary illusions**, while **monks** in the **Silent Monastery** practice **meditation and self-discipline**. **Gamblers** risk everything at **high-stakes games** in the **Undercity**, where underground gambling dens hide secrets. **Cultists** in the **Mystic Spire** seek forbidden knowledge, often leading to **schism and violence**. **Nomadic herders** follow the **Great Migration**, trading livestock and **leather goods** along the way.
