The library was quiet except for the distant hum of the old projector, its bulb flickering like a dying star. Across the aisle, Emma sat with her back to the door, fingers tracing the spine of the book she held. *"The weight of silence,"* she murmured, though no one was there to hear.

She turned the page, but the words blurred—*as if erased by a ghost’s hand*—and the only thing left was the question scribbled in the margin: **"Why do we keep what we can’t name?"**

Outside, rain tapped against the glass like whispered secrets, and for the first time, Emma wondered if the answer was already hidden in the pages she couldn’t read.
