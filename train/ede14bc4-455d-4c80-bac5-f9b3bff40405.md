A dimly lit apartment, late at night. The hum of a flickering fluorescent bulb casts long shadows. The walls are lined with framed photos of a younger version of the protagonist—smiling, unburdened. A half-empty bottle of whiskey sits on the coffee table beside a crumpled newspaper headline from 2015: *"Local Man Found Dead After Vanishing for Three Days."*

Across the room, a suitcase sits open, its contents scattered: a faded love letter from a ghostly voice, a passport with a name that doesn’t match the one on the ID, and a photograph of a woman in a black dress who looks eerily like the current occupant’s mother. The door creaks open slightly, and footsteps echo—too loud, too deliberate. The apartment feels heavier, the air thick with the scent of old blood.

A hand, too large for the frame, grips the edge of the window. The glass shatters inward.
