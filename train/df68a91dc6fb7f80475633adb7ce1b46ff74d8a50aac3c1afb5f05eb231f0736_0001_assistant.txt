As you enter the room, your light reveals a room with piles of bones and skulls all over the floor. In the left (east) wall, you see a door with no obvious opening mechanism, and in the middle of the floor is a lever. The room smells musty, and cobwebs cover much of the floor and walls. The room has an almost reverent silence, as if paying respects to the bodies of the dead here.

If the players have the Lantern of Obdul: As the lantern illuminates the space, you can also make out a pair of runes set into the floor that glow with a faint blue light. On the keystone over the door, a trio of similar runes can be seen arranged in a triangle. Two of the runes on the floor match those on the door. The third cannot be seen, but a pile of skulls rests on the floor where that rune should be.
