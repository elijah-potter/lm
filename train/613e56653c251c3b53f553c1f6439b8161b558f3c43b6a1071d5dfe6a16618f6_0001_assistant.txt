Once upon a time in the deep blue sea, there lived a mermaid named Lavinia. She was a very clever mermaid and the youngest in her family. Her long violet hair flowed behind her as she swam through the colorful coral and undersea terrain. She had a pet seahorse, Sully, that she had adopted as a little, orange baby. Lavinia and Sully spent their days exploring the sunken treasures and pirate ships that rusted at the bottom of the ocean.
