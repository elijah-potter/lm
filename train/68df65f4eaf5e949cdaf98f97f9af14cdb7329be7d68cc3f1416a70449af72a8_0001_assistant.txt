A man named Brian Malin found a large pot full of coins in a town in England in 2003 using a metal detector. He took the coins to people who study coins at the British Museum. They had to carefully clean and separate the coins because the metal coins were dirty and had begun to stick together. There were about 5,000 coins. When looking at each coin, they realized they had one very unusual coin. The coin showed the face of a person named Domitianus. Another coin had been found in France many years ago with the same person on it, but since historians didn't recognize the person, they thought the coin was fake. Now they realized that this meant the coin found in France was real. They also realized that the person pictured on the coin was a Roman emperor who was emperor for such a short time that there isn't very much information about him.
