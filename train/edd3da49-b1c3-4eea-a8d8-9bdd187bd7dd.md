The neon glow of a half-empty alley flickers through rain-streaked windows of a rundown diner. A lone figure crouches behind a dumpster, gripping a revolver, their breath fogging in the cold. A distant car horn blares—too late. The figure’s radio crackles with static before cutting out. A shadow moves toward them from the alley’s far end, a flicker of movement too fast to track. The door to the diner swings open, but the figure doesn’t turn. The radio buzzes again, static whispering a name—*Daniel*. Then, silence.
