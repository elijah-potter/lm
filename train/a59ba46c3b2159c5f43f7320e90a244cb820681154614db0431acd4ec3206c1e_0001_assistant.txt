The horntail snail was first seen in Miami-Dade County in September 2020. There is a program now in place to survey, control, and eradicate this snail from the area because it is invasive. The snail is the size of a dime and is tan in color. It is most active in the evening. The snail is originally from India, and this is the first time it has been found in the U.S.
