A maintenance technician noticed a loose hinge on an old door at the back of the warehouse, which had been overlooked during routine checks. The misalignment caused a faint creak when opening, an anomaly that would otherwise go unnoticed in the constant hum of machinery and distant voices. Upon further inspection, he found the hinge bearing had corroded, and the latch mechanism had shifted slightly. He marked the door for repair before it led to a more critical failure.
