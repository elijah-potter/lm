A city called **Chronosopolis**, where time is divided into **"Eons"** (24,000 years), **"Decades"** (2,500 years), and **"Tides"** (500 years). Public clocks display a **floating decimal hour** (e.g., 1.75 hours = 1 hour + 45 minutes) and **"Phases"** (365.25 days/year), with a central **Grand Sundial** aligned to the **Celestial Alignments**—a cosmic event that resets all clocks every **4,500 years**.

Local laws enforce **"Time Quotas"** (e.g., 1.25-hour workdays) and **"Eternal Loans"**—debtors must work 10 years of their life to repay a "day" owed. The economy runs on **"Temporal Credits"** (earned by completing time-bound tasks), and schools teach **"Rhythmic Studies"** to synchronize with the city’s shifting **"Day/Night Cycles"** (which alternate between 12 and 16 hours depending on the **Celestial Phase**).
