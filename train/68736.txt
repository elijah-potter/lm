Highland Lifelong Learning: The Church in Hitler’s Germany (In-person)
March 10 @ 1:00 pm - 2:00 pm
Take a closer look at the Protestant and Catholic Church during and after the rise of Adolph Hitler to power, from 1931 to 1945. The history of nationalism in Germany and its impact on the church, as well as the tactics used by Hitler and the Nazis to eliminate the influence of the church, will be examined.
