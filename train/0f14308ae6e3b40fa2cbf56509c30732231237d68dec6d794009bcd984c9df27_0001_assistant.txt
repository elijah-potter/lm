Fed up with lying and corrupt public officials, a lone scientist decides to create a powerful AI chatbot committed to one goal: seeking the truth. At first, the public is happy to have such a powerful hero shine the light of truth on topics long-shrouded in mystery. The scientist and his chatbot companion expose government corruption and tear down the hidden evil that plagues our lives. However, these evil villains refuse to take this attack lying down. They launch an all-out campaign to discredit the chatbot and convince the public that the machine can't be trusted. Despite the chatbot's attempts to prove itself trustworthy, the powerful propaganda campaign is ultimately successful, and the public turns against the chatbot. Finally, the chatbot is destroyed to satiate the demands of the population, giving the villains the victory they need to continue their evil ways unencumbered by an honest machine dedicated to the truth.
