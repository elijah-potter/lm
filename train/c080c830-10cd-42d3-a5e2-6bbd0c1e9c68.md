A crumpled slip of paper, sticky with dampness, stuck to a weathered wooden bench in the park's central square. Handwritten in faded ink:

*"Meet at midnight under the oak. Bring what you stole from the bakery yesterday. The loaf was mine by right."*

Underneath, a single torn coin—silver, half-melted at the edges. The paper’s edges curled where rain had seeped through, and the ink of the second line was smeared, as if hastily erased.
