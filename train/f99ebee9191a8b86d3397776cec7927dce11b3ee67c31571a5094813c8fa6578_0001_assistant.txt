The newest trend to hit the makeup scene is the "no makeup" or barefaced look where you apply minimal products to achieve effortless, glowy, sunkissed skin. To get this look just complete the following steps:

Step 1: Skincare
All good makeup looks start with a great base which is healthy, moisturized skin. Before applying any products thoroughly cleanse the skin with a gentle cleanser and follow with a moisturize targeted to your specific skincare needs. Wait five minutes for the moisturizer to be absorbed and apply your sunscreen, unless your moisturizer has built-in SPF protection.

Step 2: Foundation
Use a tinted moisturizer, BB or CC cream, or a very light layer of foundation. The goal is to look like you're not wearing makeup so restrict foundation application to the minimal amount necessary to bring uniformity to your complexion. This can also be achieved by spot concealing instead.

Step 3: Natural brows
Select a brow pencil as close to your natural brow shade as possible and fill in your eyebrows to achieve a natural, feathered look.

Step 4: Kissed by the sun
Apply a small amount of bronzer to the areas of your face naturally touched by the sun like the sides of your nose and forehead. For a bolder, snatched look apply bronzer to the base of your cheekbones to really make them pop.

Step 5: Rosey Cheeks
Using cream of liquid blush, apply a small amount to your cheeks and blend, blend, BLEND. You want to choose a shade that's close to the color of your cheeks when you blush.

Step 6: Shine Bright like a Diamond
Apply highlighter to the top of your cheekbones, the tip of your nose and Cupid's bow.

Step 7: Stretch those Lashes to the Sky
Apply one or more coats of mascara to help your eyes pop. They're the star of this makeup look.

Step 8: Mist It
Use a setting spray to set your layers and seize the day with your effortless beauty.
