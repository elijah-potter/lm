The **Chronosphere Observatory** at the **Temporal Archipelago**, a floating island chain suspended in a perpetual 11.2-hour day-night cycle. Locals use **Pulsar Clocks**—pendulums that swing backward and forward—while the official currency, **Temporal Ticks**, is traded in 17-hour increments. The government enforces **"Time Dilation Quotas"** via mandatory **Synodic Meditation** sessions, where citizens align their personal clocks to the **Grand Tickmaster’s Hourglass**, a crystal embedded with a fractal pattern that "unfolds" reality in reverse.
