1. Manatees live in shallow, slow-moving rivers, estuaries, saltwater bays, canals, and coastal areas, especially where seagrass and freshwater plants grow.
2. Manatees migrate. In the United States, they live in Florida in the winter and in the summer they live as far west as Texas and as far north as Massachusetts. Some can be found in summer in Alabama, Georgia, and South Carolina.
3. West Indian manatees can be found near the coast or in waterways of Central America and along the northern coast of South America.
