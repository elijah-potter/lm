"Leaf Green" follows the same storyline as the first-generation Pokemon games. Players start out in Pallet Town, where they are given a starter Pokemon from Professor Oak. After receiving your Pokemon, you set off on your journey through Kanto to become the Pokemon Champion. Along your journey, you encounter various trainers, gym leaders, and Team Rocket. Players must defeat Gym Leaders to earn badges and gain access through Kanto. 

The journey takes players through various cities, towns, and caves, including Pewter City, Cerulean City, Vermillion City, Lavender Town, and Celadon City. Players must solve puzzles, battle trainers, and catch new Pokemon to progress. Leaf Green includes several hidden machines (HMs), which grant the player access to new abilities such as Cut and Fly. These abilities provide convenience throughout the progression of the game. 

The ultimate goal of the game is to defeat the Elite Four and the Pokemon Champion, which requires the player to have a strong team of Pokemon and a solid understanding of how moves work. "Leaf Green" is an updated version of the classic Pokémon adventure, with improved graphics and features, while still staying true to the original story and gameplay.
