Having a degree in Kinesiology can provide many different career paths. First, kinesiology is the study of movement. It includes physics, biomechanics, physiology, biology and chemistry. The diversity of the field of kinesiology means that it can be applied in a variety of ways. Often times, those who earn a bachelor's degree in kinesiology go to physical therapy graduate programs. But they can also decide to become personal trainers or pursue a Master of Public Health degree. It's also common for students to pursue graduate education in occupational therapy or work as corporate wellness program administrators. Furthermore, students who have studied kinesiology can find work in many different settings, including in the fitness industry, in healthcare organizations, schools and community centers. A kinesiology degree can also help students find work in the following professions:

Athletic trainer.
Exercise physiologist.
Fitness instructor.
Sports medicine physician.
Occupational therapist.
Orthopedic surgeon.
Personal trainer.
Physical therapist.
Physical education teacher.
Rehabilitation therapist.
Strength and conditioning coach.
Kinesiologist.
Kinesiology professor.
Kinesiology researcher.
