Shrouded by great wings of impenetrable shadow stood the door - impenetrable indeed, as the darkness cloaks even the mind in a dull haze of a trance-like weariness. It stands ajar, inviting me forward, almost begging to reveal its secrets but whether such knowledge is for good or for ill, I cannot tell. A strange sanguine gloss varnishes the door, it looks recently finished yet through some unknown instinct I know this door has stood unchanged for many lifetimes.
