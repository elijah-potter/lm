Anheuser Busch was started in a small brewery in St. Louis, Missouri in 1852 by George Schneider.  He sold the brewery to soap manufacturer, Eberhard Anheuser.  Anheuser later teamed up with his son-in-law, Adolphus Busch, who pioneered brewery pasteurization.  They released, Budweiser,  the first nationally selling beer in 1876 and went on to have some of the highest beer sales in the world.
