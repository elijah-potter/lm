*The door creaks open under the weight of a single, deliberate knock. The air inside is thick with the scent of old books and damp wool. A shadow falls across the room as the figure standing at the threshold watches the candle flicker on the table—its flame casting long, jagged fingers across the worn leather-bound volumes.
