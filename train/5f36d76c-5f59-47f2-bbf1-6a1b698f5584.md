**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 21, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this year, promising a celebration of agriculture, community, and family entertainment. This annual tradition showcases the region’s rich agricultural heritage while offering a diverse range of activities and attractions for all ages. Visitors can anticipate livestock exhibits, home and garden displays, live music, carnival rides, and a variety of food vendors offering classic fair fare. The fairgrounds will be bustling with demonstrations, contests, and opportunities to engage with local artisans and businesses.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from Friday, August 30th, through Sunday, September 8th, 2024. Gates will open daily at 9:00 a.m., with evening events concluding at 10:00 p.m. on weeknights and 11:00 p.m. on weekends. A special “Kids’ Day” featuring discounted admission and family-friendly activities will be held on Saturday, August 31st. Daily admission is $12 for adults, $8 for children aged 6-12, and free for children five and under. Season passes are available for $40 and can be purchased online or at the gate. Parking is $10 per vehicle.

Highlights of this year’s fair include a thrilling demolition derby, a professional rodeo, a tractor pull competition, a classic car show, a talent show featuring local performers, and a fireworks display on the final evening. A complete schedule of events and further details can be found at www.sangamoncountyfair.org.
