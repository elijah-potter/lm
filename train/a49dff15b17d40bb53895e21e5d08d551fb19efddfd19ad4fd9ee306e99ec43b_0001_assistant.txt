May 25, 1999. 
By God, I've done it - I've finally done it! Nineteen years of being nominated. So close. Second best. And tonight it feels as if all that pain, heartache, suffering, and doubting was worth it. I've been Erica Kane for almost three decades. Some days in the studio it's hard to draw the line between where she ends and I begin. My family, dear Helmut, and the cast and my fans have supported me and kept me going, and God knows why they've kept believing in me - in Erica - but they have. And finally, I have something to show for all of their efforts. Standing up on that stage tonight was surreal. Seeing the faces of my friends and colleagues, I was humbled to be in their company. I will remember tonight for the rest of my life.
