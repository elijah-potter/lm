The ruins of an abandoned cathedral, its stained-glass windows shattered, the high arched doorway now a gaping maw of moss and time. The air hums with residual echo from forgotten hymns, thick with dust and the scent of damp stone. Below, a narrow cobblestone path descends into the earth, its descent a slow spiral into the dark. Lanterns flicker weakly against the blackness, casting long shadows that blur the edges of the threshold itself—a place where the sacred and the profane bleed together.
