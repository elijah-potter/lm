A single, fleeting tear on the cold morning glass,
a child’s laughter in the hollowed-out sound of a rusted piano key,
the scent of damp earth after a storm that never came—
the way the sunrise turned the river’s surface into liquid silver,
the last time you held their hand before the door closed forever,
the moment you whispered "I love you" to a voice that faded like smoke.
These are the ghosts we carry, small and unspoken,
the things that vanish when the wind shifts.
