## Product Requirements Document: "Synapse" - Contextual Knowledge Management

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently struggle to efficiently capture, organize, and retrieve relevant information dispersed across various digital tools and platforms, leading to wasted time, duplicated effort, and diminished productivity.

**Vision:** Synapse will be the central hub for knowledge workers to effortlessly connect, contextualize, and retrieve information from disparate sources, fostering a more informed and productive workflow.

### User Personas

**1. Dr. Eleanor Vance - Research Scientist:**

*   **Goals:** Quickly access and synthesize research papers, lab notes, and conference proceedings to inform her experiments and publications; maintain a clear record of her thought process and experimental design.
*   **Pain Points:** Information is scattered across academic databases, file servers, and personal notebooks; difficulty connecting related concepts and findings; overwhelming volume of data hinders efficient analysis.

**2. Marcus Bellweather - Marketing Manager:**

*   **Goals:** Centralize marketing campaign data, competitor analysis, and customer feedback; easily share insights with the team and track campaign performance; avoid redundant research and ensure consistent messaging.
*   **Pain Points:** Information resides in spreadsheets, presentation files, and marketing automation platforms; difficulty collaborating on strategic documents; struggles to maintain a complete picture of campaign history.

**3. Anya Sharma - Freelance Consultant:**

*   **Goals:** Efficiently manage client projects, meeting notes, and research for proposals; quickly retrieve past work to inform new projects; maintain a professional and organized knowledge base for client presentations.
*   **Pain Points:** Information is often lost or disorganized across multiple devices and cloud storage accounts; difficulty reusing past work and insights; time-consuming process of recreating familiar content.

### Requirements

**1. Contextual Link Capture:** Users can highlight text on any webpage or within supported applications (Google Docs, Microsoft Word, PDF readers) and directly capture the context, along with a user-defined tag, to their Synapse workspace.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Captured context is accurately preserved, including surrounding text and source URL. Tagging functionality allows for user-defined labels. Supported applications are clearly documented.
    *   **Success Metric:** 80% of users successfully capture context within the first week of use.

**2. Semantic Tagging & Relationship Mapping:** Users can assign multiple tags to captured content and define relationships between different pieces of information, creating a visual map of interconnected knowledge.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Tagging functionality supports hierarchical tag structures. Relationship mapping allows users to visually connect content items with descriptive labels. The visual map is easily navigable and searchable.
    *   **Success Metric:** 60% of users utilize relationship mapping within the first month.

**3. Smart Search & Retrieval:** A robust search engine allows users to quickly find relevant information based on keywords, tags, relationships, or content snippets.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Search results are ranked by relevance. Search functionality supports boolean operators and tag filtering. The search engine indexes all captured content and metadata.
    *   **Success Metric:** Average search time is reduced by 50% compared to the user's current workflow.

**4. Collaborative Workspace:** Users can create shared workspaces to facilitate team collaboration on projects, allowing multiple users to contribute and access information.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Permissions management allows for granular control over access levels. Real-time collaboration features allow for simultaneous editing and commenting. Version history tracks changes made by different users.
    *   **Success Metric:** 40% of users create at least one shared workspace within the first two months.

**5. Cross-Platform Synchronization:** User data and workspaces are seamlessly synchronized across all supported devices (iOS, Android, Web).
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Synchronization occurs in near real-time. Data integrity is maintained during synchronization. Offline access to recently viewed content is supported.
    *   **Success Metric:** 99.9% data synchronization success rate.



### Technical Considerations

*   **Scalability:** The architecture must support a growing user base and increasing data volume.
*   **Security:** Robust security measures are required to protect user data and prevent unauthorized access.
*   **API Integrations:** Future development should prioritize integrations with popular productivity tools (Slack, Notion, Evernote).
*   **Data Storage:** A cost-effective and reliable data storage solution is necessary.
*   **Mobile SDKs:** Native iOS and Android SDKs will be required for optimal performance and user experience.

### Risks

*   **User Adoption:** Difficulty convincing users to shift from existing knowledge management habits.
*   **Data Silos:** Potential resistance from users hesitant to centralize information from various platforms.
*   **Performance Issues:** Synchronization latency or search performance could negatively impact user experience.
*   **Security Breaches:** Vulnerabilities in the system could lead to data compromise.
*   **Feature Creep:** Expanding functionality beyond core requirements could lead to delays and complexity.

### Non-Goals

*   Direct content editing within the application (focus on capturing and linking).
*   Automated tagging or content summarization (manual tagging is a core feature).
*   Integration with social media platforms.
*   Support for proprietary file formats beyond common document types (PDF, DOCX, TXT).
*   Development of desktop applications (focus on mobile and web).



### Launch Checklist
