At last, a program that deals with the root cause of dyslexia and not just the symptoms!
Dyslexia is not a disease but is a product of thought and a special way of reacting to confusion. It is not limited to reversals of letters and words. It can affect a number of areas of life. The mental function that causes dyslexia is a gift or a special perceptual talent.
Not every dyslexic uses these gifts to full effect. The Davis theories and procedures were developed by a dyslexic, not to explain the nature of the problem, but to discover how to correct it.
