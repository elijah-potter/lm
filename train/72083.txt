CNN - NASA has released incredible new images of the sun's surface.
The space agency said the enhanced video is meant to show the sun's intense activity a different light.
The video was shot over 24 hours in September of 2011.
NASA said there's no scientific value in this enhancement but it does make for a beautiful video.
