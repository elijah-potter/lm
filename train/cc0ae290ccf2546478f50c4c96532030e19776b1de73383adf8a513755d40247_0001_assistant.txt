Every baby is different. However, most children between 6 and 9 months can be expected to be doing the following things:

- Sitting up and trying to move (make sure you have a safe space for them to move around and hand them toys)
- Have trouble with people that are not familiar with (try to have the same people taking care of them so that they feel safe during this time)
- Trying to babble and talk (it’s important to read books to them and tell them what things in pictures are called)
- Finding people interesting (play with them in front of a mirror and let them explore your face with their hands)
- Playing with toys (try toys with different textures and sounds to keep them interested)

If you have any questions or concerns about your child’s development, please reach out to their doctor or nurse.
