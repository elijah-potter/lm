Season is the change of weather from one to another which marks the division of a year. Mostly four seasons are recognized spring,summer,autumn and winter. Each and every season has its own charm. Nature beautifies itself according to the color of the season. The transition of one season to another completes a whole season cycle. Spring marks the change of winter to summer with bright sky,buds in every tree,colorful fruits and flowers. The brightest of all season summer features with bright sun and hot weather. Heavy shower brings relief from heat and sun. Autumn marks the transition of summer and winter,the season of fall. Autumn leads to winter the coolest season. Snow fall,hail storms are a common feature of this season.
