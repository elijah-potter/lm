• There was a dog that lived in a village near a pond.
• Each day, the dog would go to the lake and admire the beautiful birds as they flew in the sky.
• One day, as the dog watched the birds fly in the sky, he saw a tiny light, like a star-fall from the sky.
• The light was a young fairy that had broken her wings.
• The dog saved the fairy from falling into the pond.
• The fairy offered the dog a wish because it rescued her.
• The dog wished for wings to fly like the birds.
• The fairy granted the dog his wish to fly by giving him wings.
• The dog was happy and began to jump around.
• Suddenly, the magical wings started to flap, and the dog began to fly.
• The dog thanked the fairy, and the fairy disappeared.
