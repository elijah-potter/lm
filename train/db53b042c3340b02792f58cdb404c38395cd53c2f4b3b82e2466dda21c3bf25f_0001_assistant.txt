Q: Where is Versa Gripps located?
A: Versa Gripps USA, Inc. is located on the beautiful coast of Maine. 

Q: How is Versa Gripps a socially conscious company?
A: We donate 1% of profits to a variety of vetted and reputable local and global charities, as well as community organizations. To name a few, we support organizations for environmental concerns, veterans' affairs, animal welfare, and health.

Q: How is Versa Gripps an environmentally conscious company? 
'A: We're a Green Company that
 became solar-powered in 2018! Retail orders from Versa Gripps are shipped in compostable packaging, and we recycle all of the paper and cardboard used at our Maine facility. Lowering our carbon footprint and environmental impact is important to us.

Q: How is Versa Gripps manufacturing powered?
A: Our facility is powered by a roof-top solar array.
