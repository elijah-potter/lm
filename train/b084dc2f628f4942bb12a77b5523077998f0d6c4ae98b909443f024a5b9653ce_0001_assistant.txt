With more people turning to plant-based food instead of animal-based food, scientists are hoping to produce a planet-friendly, home-grown alternative to soya beans. Peas are high in protein but their taste is very noticeable, and 30 years ago scientists discovered a gene for pea flavor. Currently, the UK imports roughly 4 million tons of soya a year for consumption and animal feed, and half a million of that is for vegan and vegetarian-friendly foods, according to Innovate UK (the government's innovation agency).
