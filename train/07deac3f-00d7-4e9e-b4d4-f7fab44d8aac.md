The dimly lit alley reeked of damp stone and forgotten garbage, the neon glow of a half-melted sign casting long shadows across the cracked pavement. A figure crouched behind a rusted dumpster, fingers twitching at their sides, the tension in their shoulders taut. A faint, rhythmic *clack-clack-clack* echoed from somewhere ahead, the sound of boots shuffling through gravel. The air was thick with the scent of ozone and fear. They waited, pulse hammering in their throat. Then—silence. The footsteps stopped. The figure stood, gripping their knife tighter, watching as a hooded figure emerged from the shadows, their face obscured by a tattered mask. The stranger moved with deliberate, measured steps, their gloved hands resting near their waist. The air crackled with something unspoken.
