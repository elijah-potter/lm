1. What were the main factors that contributed to the North's victory?
The North's overwhelming advantages in industrial capacity and population were the main factors in the victory of the Union over the Confederacy. An excellent quality answer should mention this as critical to the North's success over the South. Other factors that would constitute an excellent answer were the advantages in technology that the North would begin to experience in the realm of repeating firearms and artillery. The superior supply situation of the North would also be an excellent answer, given that the South was under naval blockade and tepid support from foreign powers. 

2. How did the Emancipation Proclamation contribute to the war effort?
Students should address in their answers the political advantages that came with emancipating the slaves that lived in the Southern States. By broadening the goals of the war, the Union made it more difficult for European forces from intervening on behalf of the confederacy, as slavery was already widely outlawed. It raised concern in the southern states of a slave revolt, which had for the entirety of the war, sapped the South's already limited manpower. It also unified the Union's cause over a moral goal and bolstered the Union's support amongst African Americans, for whom over 200,000 would serve in its military.
