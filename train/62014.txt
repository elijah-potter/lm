Level 2 Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR) and Automated External Defibrillation (AED)
Every year thousands of lives are lost because of heart and circulatory diseases. But did you know that many of these lives could be saved by the appropriate and effective use of a defibrillator. With more Automated External Defibrillators now becoming available in public and work places, this course has been designed to enable you to use a defibrillator competently and confidently. In addition to AED training, this course explores resuscitation and choking procedures.
