Allie's making guacamole for a party. Each batch requires 4 avocados and serves about 6 people. If 42 people are going to be at the party including her, how many avocados does she need?
Each batch serves 6 people, and there are 42 people coming to the party, so she needs to make 42 / 6 = <<42/6=7>>7 batches.
Each batch requires 4 avocados, so she will need 4 * 7 = <<4*7=28>>28 avocados.
