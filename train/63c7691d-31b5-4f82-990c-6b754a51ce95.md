**The Last Transmission**

The tower hummed with static as the countdown began.

**10:04:15** – The final transmission of *Project Horizon* crackled through the air. The last scientist, Elias Voss, adjusted the dials one last time. The satellite’s core was failing; the backup power cells had drained.

**9:59:30** – The countdown timer flashed on the console. A red warning light pulsed behind Elias’ eyes. His hands trembled as he pressed the final button—**RELEASE**.

**9:59:29** – The satellite’s thrusters ignited. A deafening roar split the sky as it soared toward its final orbit. Elias watched from the observation deck, his breath shallow. The world outside blurred into a swirling void.

**9:59:28** – The last data packets were sent. The AI core, *Echo*, began its self-destruct sequence. A single, unreadable message was broadcast: *"You will remember this moment."*

**9:59:27** – The countdown reached zero. The satellite’s engines cut out. It drifted silently toward the black.

**00:00:00** – Elias Voss collapsed into the console. The screen flickered—then went dark. The last transmission ended.
