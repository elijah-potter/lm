The door creaked open with a slow sigh, revealing a dimly lit hallway lined with flickering lanterns. A single candle cast long shadows across the worn wooden floor. A soft knock echoed again, this time more insistent.

From the crack between the doorframe, a figure stood silhouetted against the streetlight outside—tall, cloaked in a tattered coat, their face obscured by a scarf wrapped tight around their mouth and nose. Their breath came in shallow gasps, their knuckles white around the handle of a rusted picklock. The knock sounded once more, deliberate and final.
