- The Zeus Moose: This is a play on the famous airplane called the Spruce Goose which was the largest aircraft of its time. Zeus relates to the Greek god of thunder, and Moose refers to the superhero's extraordinary size. 
- Nimbus Bolt: A nimbus is a type of rain cloud and perfect for your superhero to ride atop. It also can refer to a shining cloud that radiates from a supernatural being making both meanings very appropriate for a superhero.
- Thunder Strike: This name idea plays up the lightning bolt power of your superhero and combines it with the strike to create a dynamic, powerful, and memorable name.
- Fulminous Rex: Fulminous is an adjective that means involving or resembling lightning. Rex is the Latin word for king. This is a truly unique name that will strike fear into the hearts of villains.
- Blinding Cirrus: Blinding Cirrus is a name that references the dazzling light emitted when lightning strikes. And cirrus is a type of high-altitude cloud. Combining these two concepts creates a memorable yet evocative name that is hard to forget.
