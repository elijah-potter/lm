BREAKING NEWS

Following Switzerland's invasion of the Caribbean islands, a bizarre trend has been sweeping the island of the Bahamas. Our paper has heard from impeccable sources that yodeling has taken the nation by storm. One resident commented on the condition of anonymity, "You can't go anywhere without hearing this strange noise. Everywhere I go some kid is yodeling his head off." This trend doesn't seem to be just limited to the young, however. There are reports of old people's homes and pensioners forming Senior Yodelers Clubs. In other news, Lenny Kravitz has reportedly said he's looking to pay homage to his Bahamian roots by releasing a yodeling album later this year.
