*Spring Awakens*

In barren lands of frost and chill,
A promise stirs a whispered trill.
Beneath the thaw, a gentle birth,
The season dawns upon the Earth.

From slumbered dreams, new life does spring,
In petals bloom and songs that sing.
The sun emerges, and warms the day,
As nature awakens from the gray.

And so it mirrors human hearts,
When hope is born and the world restarts.
From winter's grip, we break away,
Embracing light, a brand new day.

*Summer's Embrace*

With fiery rays and golden haze,
The sun-drenched days of summer blaze.
Beneath its touch, our spirits soar.
In laughter's echo, forevermore.

The vibrant green of nature's gown,
Reflects the joy that we have found.
We bask in the warmth, love's tender gaze,
In summertime's eternal phase.

But as the season reaches its peak,
We're reminded life is short and sweet.
The days grow shorter, shadows cast, 
And summer's beauty cannot last. 


*Autumn's Embers*

The air turns crisp, a whispered sigh,
As autumn paints the evening sky.
In hues of red and amber glow,
The world prepares for what will follow.

We gather harvest, blessings stored,
Yet feel a sense of something more.
For as leaves fall, one by one,
We know the cycle has now begun

In letting go, we find our way,
Releasing that which no longer stays. 
For just as nature sheds its crown,
We too must learn to stand unbound

*Winter's Serenade*

A frosty hug envelops all, 
As winter's chill begins to fall.
The world turns silent  a blanket of white,
As nature sleeps embraced by night.

In solitude, we find our core. 
In stillness wisdom, we do explore. 
For though the cold may seem severe,
It brings reflection crystal clear.

And as the earth slumbers deep,
We find solace in peaceful sleep.
For winter's end marks a new start. 
And hope rekindles every heart.

Nature's seasons ebb and flow,
An ever-changing dance they show.
Through growth and loss, we come to see,
The changing season's harmony.
