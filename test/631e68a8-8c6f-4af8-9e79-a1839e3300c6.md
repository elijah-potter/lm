**Mayor’s Statement on Unidentified Drone Activity Over Lakefront Park**
Residents of New Haven Heights are urged to stay indoors after reports of a low-flying object near the marina this afternoon. Officials confirm no commercial aircraft were in the area, and authorities are investigating potential security threats. The city’s drone detection system activated at 14:32, prompting an emergency response. No injuries were reported, but police advise avoiding the park until further notice. The National Guard has been deployed to monitor the zone. The city’s website will be updated with details soon.
