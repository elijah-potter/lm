A standard door typically measures 80" (203 cm) tall and 34" (86 cm) wide, with a hinged frame that swings inward or outward. It is often made of wood, metal, or composite materials, featuring a solid core for durability. Some have glass panels, locks, and decorative elements.

It separates interior spaces (e.g., a room) from exterior or adjacent rooms, providing privacy, security, and sound insulation. Modern variants may include smart locks, sensors, or thermal barriers.
