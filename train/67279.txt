A critical analysis essay is an academic paper that is written to judge a student’s ability to analyze any piece of literature or cinematography. By this assignments the colleges and universities examines the critical thinking and analyzing capacities of a student. The key of a very good critical writing is reading the text by heart. While studying the text it is important to make necessary short notes and highlighting so that it becomes easy to understand.
To write a perfect critical writing it is important to identify the critical ideas and the key points associated with the plot as identifying the plot will help a person to understand the whole idea and subject of the essay. Idea of author’s main thesis should be identified to write the critical analysis of any essay. Understanding Author’s thesis is important as the whole writing is going to revolve around that idea.
A Study on Critical Evaluation of a Real Work Project
To write the critical analysis of any piece of literary work, it is important to find connection between the ideas and the plots and also link the characters behavior and how they change in the due course of time.
While writing the critical analysis it is important to collect evidences from different incidents such that when we try to draw the conclusion to the essay we can write evidences to support our view. In fact sometimes having some background information about the author also helps in understanding their ideas and conceptions.
Ideally a analytical report about any essay will constitute of 3 parts, an introduction, a body and a conclusion. The Introduction part is the part which will help a writer to attract the attention from the readers. So as a critical writer all the effort and attention should be given to the introduction part so that it can catch the eyes of the readers as much as possible.

The body part should consist of the main idea and themes of the analyzed piece of literature. This portion should consist of the evidences that a critic can produce to support his vision.
The conclusion should not be biased, instead of making any final conclusion this portion should reflect how the literary analysis can be connected to the whole book and how it reflects the author’s plot and ideas.
