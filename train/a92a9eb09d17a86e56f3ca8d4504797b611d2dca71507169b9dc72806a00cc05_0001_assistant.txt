My first grader has life aspirations to be a teacher when she grows up. She has a great time pretending she is a teacher at home. I went to her room today because she was extra quiet and found her with the entire bag of apples on her bed. She was eating one, and I saw a bite taken out of several others. I asked her who said it was okay for her to take all of them and she told me, “It’s okay, Mom, it’s teacher appreciation day.”
