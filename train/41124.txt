See how a frog develops from a tadpole into an adult frog in the amazing process of metamorphosis.
The six life cycle specimens are encased in a resin block and allow children to see real-life examples of a frog egg, tadpoles at various stages of development, froglet and adult frog.
Children can examine in detail each stage of the incredible Frog life cycle, and the remarkable changes it undergoes.
For maths they provide an opportunity for children to:
- Identify similarities and differences
- Present information using Venn diagrams
- Practice sequencing
Each specimen is encased in indestructible resin and can safely be used by young children. Use a magnifying glass to get a closer look at the specimen details. A Teachers Guide is included with each set.
