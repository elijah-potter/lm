Lily had a big, bright sun that smiled down on her every day. One morning, she woke up and noticed her little pony, Pip, had lost his favorite golden saddle.

"Pip, where did your saddle go?" Lily asked, looking around the meadow.

Pip nibbled his hay and then tilted his head. "Maybe the wind took it," he said.

Lily ran outside and blew softly. The wind giggled and swirled, but the saddle was still missing.

She checked under her house, behind the tree, and even in the big, blue pond. Still no saddle!

Just then, a tiny, sparkly fairy with wings like stars fluttered down. "Lost things often find their way," she said cheerfully.

Lily and Pip tried to follow the fairy's advice. They danced, they sang, and even rode their bikes (well, Pip rode with Lily on his back!). They giggled and chased butterflies.

Suddenly, the golden saddle popped out of a bush! It had been hiding there the whole time.

Lily hugged Pip and said, "I knew you'd find it!" The fairy waved goodbye, and they ran home, happy and full of joy.

That night, Lily and Pip curled up together, snuggled under the warm blanket, and slept with smiles on their faces. The sun shined extra bright, and they knew they’d had the best adventure ever!
