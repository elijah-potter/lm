Great food... and no cash payment made the experience even better.
Had a fantastic dinner with my wife, mom and a friend... Everything was awesome including the payment method, which was through mobile transfer; the restaurant had a strict no-cash policy and I found this more efficient.

Usually, when I travel to restaurants like this in another country (we are from Denmark) I prefer using mobile money to pay for the restaurant experience. The main reason for this is that traveling with cash can be very dangerous, especially in a new country.

The fact that we could pay using mobile money meant that there was no reason to pay a fee to my bank to extend the limit and I could spend the rest of my trip with a fully-functional credit card.

From now on, I'll check beforehand for restaurants that do not offer mobile money payment, as they will not be visited.

Date of visit: April 2023
