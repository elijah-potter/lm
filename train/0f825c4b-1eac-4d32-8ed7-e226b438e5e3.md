A makeshift tent city at the outskirts of a city center, where a crowd gathers around a small TV mounted on a metal tripod. Banners of political parties and local news networks hang from a makeshift awning. People sit on plastic chairs, some on the ground, wrapped in blankets or bundled in scarves. A few hold mobile phones, their screens glowing with live feeds. The atmosphere is tense; a few reporters and volunteers circulate with microphones, taking down notes. The air hums with whispers and the occasional shout. A loudspeaker crackles, announcing updates every few minutes. The crowd shifts positions as someone shares news on a handheld device, creating a mix of anticipation and distraction.
