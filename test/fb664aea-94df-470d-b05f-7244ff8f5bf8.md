A forgotten festival in the ancient city of **Vorthas**, the **Solstice of the Forgotten**, began as a ritual to honor the harvest of the **Blackthorn Root**, a sacred crop said to cure all ailments. Farmers would gather at the **Stone Circle of Echoes** to perform dances and offerings to the **Dusk Goddess**, a deity believed to guard the earth’s secrets. Over centuries, the festival’s purpose faded with the rise of new religions and shifting cultural priorities.

By the time of the **Great Fire of Vorthas**, only remnants of the tradition remained—whispers in taverns, cracked pottery with faded carvings depicting root-carrying dancers. The modern festival now includes masked processions, firelight feasts, and the annual **"Root Blessing"**, where participants drink fermented root wine in hopes of healing.

The city’s archives contain no records, and scholars debate whether the original intent was agricultural, spiritual, or even a healer’s oath. The festival’s name—**"The Gathering of the Unremembered"**—was added later, but no one dares ask who *they* were.
