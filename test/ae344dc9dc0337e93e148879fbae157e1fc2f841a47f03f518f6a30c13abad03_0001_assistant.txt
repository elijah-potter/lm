Here’s Why Your Vehicle is on Strike 

Doesn't it always ruin your mood when your car just wouldn't start after you last parked it? Here are some common reasons why this could be happening:

1. Alternator Troubles: If you hear a clicking noise instead of a roar, your alternator could be out of commission. The alternator is what charges your battery and powers your car's electrical system. If it's bad, your battery won't maintain a charge, causing your car to stall. 

2. Ignition Apocalypse: Are you turning the key, but getting no response? Your ignition switch may be the culprit. It's responsible for supplying power to your car's electrical system when you turn the key. If it's not working properly, your car won't start. 

3. Battery Blues: If you’re using four years old and never replaced it or you left your headlights on, you might find yourself with a dead battery. A dead battery won't give enough power to crank the engine, rendering your car useless. When it’s new, parking your car for like two weeks in a go can also kill the battery.

4. Timing Belt Issues: The timing belt regulates the movement of valves and pistons in your engine. If it breaks, it can ruin your engine and leave your car unable to start. Keep track of your car's manufacturer recommendations for when to change your timing belt to avoid this issue.

5. Damaged Distributor Cap: The engine's distributor cap is responsible for sending electricity from the ignition coils to the spark plugs. When the cap fails, the spark doesn't reach the right place, and the engine fails to start.

6. Trapped Steering Lock: If the steering wheel is locked due to incorrect usage, the ignition won't turn on, and the car won't start. It could also lead to a broken key or ignition barrel.

7. Sluggish Spark Plugs: Worn, fouled, or corroded spark plugs can lead to poor combustion, affecting engine performance. When they struggle to ignite the air-fuel mixture, the engine might refuse to start at all.

8. Stubborn Starter Motor: A malfunctioning starter motor can make clicking noises or have no response when you try to turn the key. The damage could be due to a dead battery, corroded wires, or a worn-out armature.
