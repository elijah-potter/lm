Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a curious bunny named Benny. Benny loved exploring his garden every day. One sunny morning, Benny saw a shiny red apple near his burrow.

"Mmm, a red apple!" Benny thought. He hopped over and picked it up. But just as he was about to take a bite, he heard a tiny voice.

"Please, don't eat that!" came a squeaky sound. Benny looked down and saw a tiny mouse sitting next to his apple. The mouse was very small and had big, round eyes.

"I was going to hide inside it," the mouse explained. "But it's so big and tasty looking!"

Benny smiled. "You can have it!" he said. He gently gave the apple to the mouse. The mouse thanked Benny and ran off to his tiny home.

Benny sat down and thought, "Maybe sharing is fun!" He decided to share his toys and snacks with his friends. Soon, his garden became the happiest place in the whole neighborhood, filled with laughter and friendship.

From that day on, Benny knew that sharing made everyone happy. And so, every morning, he would explore his garden, share his treasures, and enjoy the joy of friendship with his friends.
