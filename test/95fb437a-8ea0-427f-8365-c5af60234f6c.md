Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around, munch on carrots, and play with his fluffy friends.

One sunny morning, Benny saw a tiny red berry on a bush. He picked it up and nibbled on it. "Yum!" he thought. But suddenly, the berry began to glow! Benny’s eyes got big. He had never seen a berry that sparkled before.

Benny’s friends, a group of chirping birds and a sleepy old turtle named Tilly, came running. "What’s happening?" they asked.

Benny held up the glowing berry. "It’s magic!" he said.

Tilly blinked. "Maybe it’s a magic berry?" she said slowly.

The friends followed Benny to his favorite spot in the garden—a big, round pond. The berry floated gently on the water. Suddenly, it popped open, and out popped a tiny, shimmering seed.

"Look!" Benny gasped. "It’s a magic seed!"

The seed started growing fast. Soon, it turned into a beautiful, purple flower with tiny, glowing petals. The flower opened its arms wide, and a soft, sweet smell filled the air.

The friends cheered. "We’re going to have a party!" they said.

They gathered around the flower, and the purple petals began to dance. The birds sang songs, the turtle told funny stories, and Benny danced in circles. The garden was filled with happiness and magic.

As the sun set, painting the sky pink and gold, the friends said their goodbyes. "We’ll come back tomorrow," Benny promised.

The next morning, the purple flower was even more beautiful. The magic seed inside it had grown bigger. The friends returned, and the flower shimmered even more brightly.

Every day, the flower grew a little taller, its petals glowing brighter. The garden became a magical place where everyone could play, sing, and laugh together.

And so, Benny and his friends learned that magic can be found in the simplest things—like a tiny, glowing berry and a seed that brings joy to everyone around it.
