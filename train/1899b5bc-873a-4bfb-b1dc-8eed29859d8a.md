The clock struck thirteen on the wall,
while echoes whispered of the past they would not fall.
A door stood ajar, its hinges groaning,
with every breath, the whispers of the unknown growing.

The phrase repeated itself in a rhythm so true:
*"I see the past, I see the future—
the present is just a shadow cast by you."*

The wind carried the sound, a lullaby so old,
a chorus sung in tongues both dead and alive.
*"The cycle turns, the threads unwind,
but the phrase remains—it’s the only sound."*

Outside, the city slept in a haze of neon light,
where shadows danced with forgotten names and forgotten might.
*"Repeat, repeat—
the cycle repeats, the same as always."*
