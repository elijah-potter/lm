Nursing informatics is the science that combines nursing with information technologies to promote health of people. The use of nursing informatics learning is enabling though human services professionals in accomplishing persistent focused could not care less. It encourages the joining of information, data and knowledge for supporting patients, nurses and others. Its main objective is to provide encouragement to the nursing practice and deliver nursing care.
