Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny mouse named Pip. Pip had a soft yellow fur and always wore a little red hat. Every morning, Pip would hop outside to play with his best friend, Benny the Bunny.

One sunny day, Pip was exploring the garden when he heard a tiny voice. "Pip! Can you help me?" Pip looked around and saw a little flower with a sad face. "Of course, I’ll help!" Pip said.

The flower was worried because its petals were wilting. Pip checked the soil and saw it was dry. He remembered his mom saying, "Water plants every day, Pip!" So, Pip carefully took a sip of his milk from his tiny cup and poured it into the flower’s pot. The flower’s petals began to glow with happiness.

Soon, Benny the Bunny hopped over. "I brought you a snack!" Benny said. Pip took a tiny crumb of his carrot and shared it with the flower. The flower grew taller and prettier than ever!

Benny giggled. "Now we have the prettiest garden in the whole world!"

That night, as Pip curled up in his cozy nest, he smiled. "The best adventure was making my friends happy," he whispered. And with those words, Pip fell asleep, dreaming of more adventures with Benny and the magical garden.
