Patricia was a raccoon who grew up on a ranch, learning to ride horses and shoot guns from a young age. Once she got older, she started traveling from town to town. She would go to ranches and offer help in exchange for a place to get some shut-eye before moving along to the next town. 

One day as the sun rose, Patricia rode into a new town. She was thirsty for a root beer and headed for the saloon. At first, the people there were suspicious of her as an outsider. She noticed a dusty piano in the corner and thought some music might bring down the tense feeling in the environment. She started playing a ragtime tune, and the locals bought her drinks all night. They were enthralled.
