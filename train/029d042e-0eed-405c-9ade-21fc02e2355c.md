The air smelled faintly of damp earth and burnt copper, lingering from a nearby skylight that had shattered. A half-empty whiskey glass sat on the cracked wooden table, its rim smeared with condensation. The floorboards groaned underfoot, one splintered near a broken chair frame leaning against the wall. A half-eaten sandwich wrapped in greasy paper lay abandoned beside a half-finished sketch of a stormy seascape, its charcoal smudged with ash from the empty fireplace. A single, muddy bootprint traced a path toward the door, but the glass had shattered too far to match any of the discarded items. The faint sound of distant rain pattered against the windowpanes, muffled by the remnants of a torn curtain.
