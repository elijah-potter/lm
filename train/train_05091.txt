Mark is running for an election and wins 70% of the votes in an area with 100,000 voters.  He got twice as many total votes in the remaining area.  How many votes did he get in total?
He got 100,000*.7=<<100000*.7=70000>>70,000 votes from the first area
He got 70,000*2=<<70000*2=140000>>140,000 votes in the remaining areas
So in total he got 140,000+70,000=<<140000+70000=210000>>210,000 votes
