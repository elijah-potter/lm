A sprawling, half-abandoned **warehouse district** near a former industrial port, now repurposed as a makeshift "neutral zone." The building’s cracked floorboards and flickering fluorescent lights form a labyrinth of shared spaces: crudely carved benches in the corner, a flickering neon sign reading *"Here"* in faded paint, and a circle of mismatched chairs arranged around a low table covered in half-empty cups of coffee and half-finished puzzles. Some days, it’s a quiet gathering; others, a raucous one. The air smells of damp concrete and old cigarettes, but also of curiosity and the quiet hum of unspoken conversations. No signpost, no official hours—just a place where people linger, wait, or simply *are*.
