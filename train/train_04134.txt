Anna wants to plant red and yellow tulips in the shape of a smiley face. She needs 8 red tulips for each eye and 18 red tulips for the smile. If she needs 9 times the number of tulips in the smile to make the yellow background of the face, how many tulips does she need total?
First find the total number of tulips in the eyes: 2 eyes * 8 tulips/eye = <<2*8=16>>16 tulips
Then find the number of tulips in the background: 18 tulips * 9 = <<18*9=162>>162 tulips
Then add the number of tulips in the eyes, smile, and background to find the total number of tulips: 16 tulips + 162 tulips + 18 tulips = <<16+162+18=196>>196 tulips
