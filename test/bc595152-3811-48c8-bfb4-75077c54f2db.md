Once upon a time in a cozy garden, there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop and play. One sunny morning, Benny noticed something shiny near his burrow. Curious, he hopped over and saw a tiny, glowing star stuck in a tree.

Benny didn’t know what to do, so he called his friends—Nina the squirrel, Max the cat, and Pip the mouse. "Let’s help the star!" they said together.

They worked as a team: Benny dug a little hole, Nina gathered leaves to make a nest, Max shined his tail like a flashlight, and Pip wiggled his tail to keep the star safe.

Soon, the star twinkled brighter inside their cozy nest. Benny smiled. "Look! The star is happy now!"

From that day on, Benny and his friends kept the star safe in their garden. Every night, the star twinkled like a tiny, friendly firefly, telling them they were braver and kinder than they thought.

And that’s how Benny and his friends found a special friend in the garden—right next door!
