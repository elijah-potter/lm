1. Movies: The movies are a fun way to spend time together. Consider watching a romantic comedy. Movie tickets can become even more budget-friendly if you purchase tickets online and during the matinee.
2. Picnic at sunset by a lake: This date can be both romantic and cost-effective. Buying picnic supplies can be cheaper than the average restaurant as you can control what you are buying and from where. Not to mention, watching the sunset over a lake can be extremely romantic for any couple. 
3. Game night at home: This is a fun and affordable way to spend an evening together. You can play board games, card games, or video games.
