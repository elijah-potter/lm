## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park – A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary**

The City of Oakhaven faces increasing challenges from extreme weather events, including intensified rainfall and prolonged heat waves. The Willow Creek area, a historically underserved neighborhood, is particularly vulnerable due to its low-lying topography and limited green space. This proposal seeks $75,000 in municipal funding to transform a currently neglected, overgrown lot at the corner of Elm Street and Willow Creek Drive into the Willow Creek Pocket Park, a demonstrably climate-resilient community asset. This project will incorporate permeable paving, a rain garden, native tree plantings, and shaded seating areas, mitigating stormwater runoff, reducing the urban heat island effect, and providing a vital green space for residents.  The project leverages $25,000 in committed volunteer labor and in-kind donations from local nurseries, ensuring a significant return on investment for the City and creating a tangible, positive impact on the quality of life for Willow Creek residents.  The Park's design and implementation will prioritize community input and equitable access, fostering a sense of ownership and ensuring long-term sustainability.

**Project Plan & Timeline**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park project will be executed in four distinct phases, each with clearly defined milestones, responsible partners, and deliverables.

**Phase 1: Planning & Design (Months 1-2)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (WCNA), Landscape Architect (Sarah Miller, Miller Design), City Planning Department.
*   **Milestones:**
    *   **Week 1-3:** Community Needs Assessment – WCNA will conduct surveys and host two public forums to gather input on desired park features and address concerns.
    *   **Week 4-6:** Preliminary Design Development – Miller Design will develop two preliminary design concepts incorporating community feedback and adhering to City planning guidelines.
    *   **Week 4-6:** Site Assessment & Soil Testing – City Planning Department will conduct a detailed site assessment and soil testing to inform design decisions regarding drainage and plant selection.
*   **Deliverables:** Community Needs Assessment Report, Two Preliminary Design Concepts, Site Assessment Report.

**Phase 2: Permitting & Procurement (Months 3-4)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** City Permitting Office, WCNA, Miller Design.
*   **Milestones:**
    *   **Week 9-12:** Permit Application & Review – Miller Design will prepare and submit necessary permits to the City Permitting Office.  WCNA will assist in navigating the application process.
    *   **Week 9-12:** Material Procurement – WCNA will solicit bids from local suppliers for permeable paving, native plants, mulch, and seating materials.
*   **Deliverables:** Approved Permits, Supplier Contracts.

**Phase 3: Construction & Planting (Months 5-7)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** WCNA, Volunteer Workforce (coordinated by WCNA), Miller Design (oversight), City Parks Department (equipment loan).
*   **Milestones:**
    *   **Week 13-16:** Site Preparation – Volunteer workforce, supervised by WCNA, will clear debris, grade the site, and prepare the area for paving and planting.
    *   **Week 17-20:** Permeable Paving Installation – Volunteer workforce, with guidance from Miller Design, will install the permeable paving system.
    *   **Week 21-24:** Rain Garden Construction & Planting – Volunteer workforce, with Miller Design oversight, will construct the rain garden and plant native vegetation.
*   **Deliverables:** Completed Permeable Paving, Constructed Rain Garden, Planted Native Vegetation.

**Phase 4: Completion & Stewardship (Month 8)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** WCNA, City Parks Department, Miller Design.
*   **Milestones:**
    *   **Week 25-26:** Final Inspections & Signage – City Parks Department will conduct final inspections and install interpretive signage detailing the Park's features and environmental benefits.
    *   **Week 25-26:** Community Celebration & Park Dedication – WCNA will organize a community celebration to officially dedicate the Willow Creek Pocket Park.
*   **Deliverables:** Park Dedication Ceremony, Interpretive Signage, Final Project Report.



**Budget**

The total project cost is estimated at $100,000.  We are requesting $75,000 in municipal funding, complemented by $25,000 in committed volunteer labor and in-kind donations.

*   **Permeable Paving Materials:** $35,000 – This includes the cost of interlocking pavers and base aggregate.  We have secured a commitment from Oakhaven Materials for a 10% discount.
*   **Native Plants & Trees:** $15,000 – This covers the cost of purchasing a diverse selection of native trees, shrubs, and wildflowers from Green Thumb Nursery, who have pledged a 15% donation of their retail price.
*   **Rain Garden Gravel & Soil Amendments:** $5,000 – This includes the cost of gravel for drainage and organic amendments to improve soil quality.
*   **Seating & Picnic Table:** $10,000 – This covers the cost of durable, recycled plastic seating and a picnic table.
*   **Landscaping Tools & Equipment Rental:** $3,000 – This includes the rental of necessary equipment for site preparation and planting.  The City Parks Department has committed to loaning some equipment.
*   **Professional Fees (Landscape Architect):** $15,000 – This covers the cost of Miller Design’s services for design, oversight, and technical assistance.
*   **Signage & Interpretive Materials:** $2,000 – This includes the cost of designing and fabricating interpretive signage.
*   **Contingency (5%):** $5,000 – This is allocated for unforeseen expenses and potential cost overruns.




**Community Engagement & Equity Impacts**

Community engagement is central to the success of the Willow Creek Pocket Park. The initial Needs Assessment will actively solicit input from all Willow Creek residents, prioritizing the voices of historically marginalized groups.  The Park’s design will incorporate features that cater to diverse needs, including accessible pathways, shaded seating areas, and a small, designated play area for children.  The volunteer workforce will be recruited from within the Willow Creek neighborhood, providing opportunities for skill-building and fostering a sense of ownership.  The selection of native plants will prioritize species that provide habitat for pollinators and support local biodiversity, contributing to the ecological health of the neighborhood.

**Risk Mitigation**

Potential risks include unforeseen site conditions, delays in permit approvals, and volunteer attrition. To mitigate these risks, we will conduct a thorough site assessment prior to commencement of work, maintain proactive communication with the City Permitting Office, and implement a robust volunteer recruitment and retention strategy.  The contingency fund will provide a financial buffer to address unexpected expenses.  Miller Design will provide ongoing technical support to ensure the project remains on track.

**Post-Grant Sustainability**

Long-term sustainability will be ensured through a combination of community stewardship and City support. The WCNA will assume responsibility for ongoing maintenance and upkeep of the Park, including litter removal, weeding, and pruning.  The City Parks Department will provide occasional support for larger maintenance tasks, such as tree trimming and irrigation repairs.  Interpretive signage will educate visitors about the Park’s environmental benefits and encourage responsible use.




**Call to Action**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park represents a unique opportunity to address critical environmental and social needs within the City of Oakhaven. This project will transform a neglected space into a vibrant community asset, mitigating climate risks, enhancing quality of life, and fostering a stronger sense of community. We urge the City to approve our request for $75,000 in funding, enabling us to realize this transformative vision and create a lasting legacy for the Willow Creek neighborhood.
