**Announcement: "The Last Broadcast of *Echoes of the Forgotten*"**

The neon glow of the old radio tower flickered over the abandoned city, casting long shadows on the crumbling pavement. Inside the dimly lit broadcast studio, an unseen host stood behind a cluttered desk, their voice crackling through the speakers with a mix of urgency and nostalgia.

*"Ladies and gentlemen, listeners… this is the final transmission from *Echoes of the Forgotten*. For over a decade, we’ve carried the whispers of those who vanished before they could be remembered. Tonight, we close the door on a story that haunted us all—one that still lingers in the static between transmissions."*

A pause. The wind howled through the broken windows as the host gestured toward the empty chair behind them. *"Some say the voices we broadcasted will never stop. That the echoes we left behind are still searching for their names. But tonight, we say goodbye… to the world as we knew it."*

The last words of the broadcast cut out abruptly, replaced by a single, repeating phrase—*"They’re still here."* The radio static filled with whispers, then silence. The screen went black.
