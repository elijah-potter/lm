Losing weight can be very challenging. Here are a few ways that should make it fun:
1. Dancing Lessons - There are many types of dancing, some of which are quite energetic. Dancing can also be quite fun, though, and you can sign up for a class to help you learn, which should also encourage you to keep attending. 
2. Sports - There are many sports out there, so find one you like and think you can do. This could be something calm like golfing or something more intense like badminton or volleyball. 
3. Games - Nowadays there are fitness video games that help you get fit in front of a screen. These games are interactive and require you to move in order to win. There are many options to choose from, so you can pick a game or even more than one game that interests you. 
4. Challenge Friends - There is any number of ways you can challenge your friends. You can use Fitbits to keep track of each other's steps, race one another, or have other types of competitions that involve some sort of physical movement. 
5. Mountain Biking - Mountain biking is something many people find enjoyable, and there is a whole group of people that do this that you can join. You can stick with the simple trails or challenge yourself to try the more difficult ones. 
6. Swiming - Swimming is fun, and you can do this with friends or even join a swimming group. You can do this at a beach or pool, and it usually doesn't feel like exercise. 
I hope that you succeed in your goal!
