The rain drenched the asphalt as the headlights of the abandoned truck flickered weakly. The GPS beeped one last time before cutting out. The driver—clutching a crumpled map, their breath fogging the windshield—stepped out, boots splashing through puddles. A rusted sign, half-buried in snow, read *"Exit 13: Lost Forks"* in faded letters. The road ahead twisted into a maze of gravel, flanked by skeletal trees. A distant howl echoed from the forest; their phone’s battery died. The map’s torn corner fluttered in the wind, pointing nowhere.
