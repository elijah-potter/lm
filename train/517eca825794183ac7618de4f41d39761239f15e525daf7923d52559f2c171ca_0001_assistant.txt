Have you ever walked into a place you normally can find peace in but it ends up being chaotic and unhelpful? I recently went to my favorite coffee shop, which faithful readers would know is The Big Beanboozle, expecting to find a comfortable table and a spot to rest. This time, I'm afraid things were not going to go my way. I had never seen the place so crowded! There were so many people that the shop was blocked up. It took me hours to get to the counter, and all the while people were talking over each other so loudly. I couldn't hear myself think over all that noise. When I did finally get my order, my favorite table was completely taken. All in all, this was not the usual, pleasant trip that I had been expecting. This sort of thing can put a cloud over your whole day, but just keep in mind, like I did, that it doesn't matter that much and is just temporary.
