A dimly lit alleyway at dusk, the streetlamps casting long shadows across wet cobblestones. The air smells of rain and exhaust fumes. A lone figure, draped in a hooded coat, moves cautiously toward an abandoned storefront, its glass windows shattered. Their breath steams slightly in the cold, and the sound of distant sirens grows fainter with distance. A flickering neon sign reads **"CLOSED"**—but the door creaks open just enough for a gloved hand to slip inside. A second later, a single bullet strikes the floorboards. The figure freezes, listening, before slipping away into the shadows. The alley grows eerily silent.
