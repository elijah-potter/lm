The door creaked open.
Dust swirled in the moonlight.
A whisper slithered through the crack.
Footsteps echoed—slow, deliberate.
The clock ticked.
Something moved in the shadows.
The air grew thick.
A breath.
A sigh.
The silence swallowed the sound.
The lock clicked.
A hand gripped the doorknob.
The door swung inward.
Darkness waited.
A shadow stretched too long.
A whisper, this time.
A name.
The room filled with static.
The light died.
The first scream.
