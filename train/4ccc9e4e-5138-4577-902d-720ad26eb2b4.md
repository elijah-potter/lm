A winding, sun-bleached trail through a cracked, parched earth.
Dry riverbeds cut through the stone, their edges dusted with glittering salt.
The air hums with the faint, repetitive cry of birds that vanish into the horizon.
At intervals, the path splits—one fork veers left into a dead end of overgrown thornbushes; the other spirals upward, only to descend again in a cycle of futile ascent.
No landmarks remain; only the relentless, indifferent horizon.
