Learn to love reading with this Pre-Level 1 DK Reader Introduce your child to a love of reading with Colourful Days, a Pre-Level 1 DK Reader. Discover a world of animals, food, jewellery and more while learning about colours in this bright and colourful reader. What makes up the parts of a bright yellow sunflower? How many different colours in the bunch of balloons do you see? Pre-Level 1 stories have short sentences and a simple vocabulary to capture interest - ideal for children beginning to learn and for early or reluctant readers. Colourful Days is perfect to read aloud together, with lively illustrations and an engaging story that encourages reading and builds a child's confidence.
