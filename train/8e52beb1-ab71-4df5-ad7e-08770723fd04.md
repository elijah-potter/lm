A **broken glass** lay scattered on the kitchen floor, its jagged edges reflecting the dim light from the broken ceiling fixture. The air smelled of damp wood and old secrets. The man, **Daniel**, knelt beside it, fingers brushing the shattered remnants. He had promised his late wife, **Elena**, that he’d fix this—*fix it all*—but the words had slipped out in a moment of grief, half-truths wrapped in sorrow. Now, years later, the house still creaked under his weight, the walls whispering unspoken promises Daniel could no longer fulfill.

Elena’s letters, found in a drawer, were filled with her own misunderstandings—her belief that his silence was strength, that his absence was sacrifice. She had built a life on a promise he couldn’t keep, and though she had left months ago, the weight of the unspoken still hung between them. The glass was just the first shard; the rest of the house remained a silent testament to what was lost.
