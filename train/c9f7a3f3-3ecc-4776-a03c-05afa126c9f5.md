A critical decision-making framework centered on **one key variable** for high-probability outcomes:

**1. Single-Point Hypothesis (SPH):**
Define a core parameter (e.g., *cost-to-benefit ratio at X threshold*) and model all strategies under its assumption. Reject alternatives unless they either:
- **Explicitly depend** on this detail (e.g., a vendor’s delivery window tied to a supplier’s unexpected delay).
- **Violate it** in a way that introduces irreducible risk (e.g., ignoring a regulatory deadline tied to this factor).

**2. Decision Graph:**
- **Leaf Nodes:** Outcomes where the single detail is either met or broken. Assign probabilities based on historical data or expert judgment.
- **Merge Paths:** For parallel actions (e.g., two contracts), sum probabilities of favorable outcomes where the detail aligns.

**3. Validation Protocol:**
- **Stress Test:** Simulate worst-case failure of the detail (e.g., supply chain disruption) to identify fallback plans.
- **Convergence Check:** Ensure no secondary variable compensates for the detail’s absence (e.g., no redundant resource).

**Example:**
*"If Project X’s critical API key is lost due to an employee’s resignation (P=0.15), the backup plan is Phase B, which has a 30% success rate. Otherwise, Phase A succeeds with 85% probability."*
