That wasp just stung you! You shouldn’t have gotten so close to the nest, but ouch! It really hurts, but you’re too old to cry. You decide to give the wasp a good talking to. 

You gingerly pull at the rapidly-swelling sting. Do they leave their stingers in you like honeybees? “Now see here, Mr. Wasp”, you say. “I’m going to tell you a few things while I’m picking you out of me.” 

“I’m not afraid. You can live here nicely on the porch, but if you try to take me down you're gonna break.”

“You’ve not been fighting honorably either. Do you even think it’s even fair that you can fly? I swat, but you run away. You're always hiding behind your nest. It’s not an equal fight either. You have all your friends, but I stand alone.” 

You scratch your arm. “Even though you’re responsible for the poison in me, I’ll forgive you. I’m feeling your sting down inside me, but we can still be friends if you behave.” The wasp buzzes away. You feel better. Maybe you are getting through to it.
