Once upon a time, in a cozy green house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Pip. Pip had fluffy brown fur and always wore a tiny red bow on his head.

One sunny morning, Pip met a little blue bird named Bella. Bella had bright feathers and loved to sing. "Hello, Pip!" chirped Bella. "Want to play a game?"

Pip nodded happily. "I love games!"

They ran through the garden, hopped over flowers, and chased butterflies. The sun felt warm on Pip's nose, and the birds sang sweet songs in the trees.

Just then, a big brown bear named Boris passed by. He had a big belly and a sleepy eye. "Mmm, what's this?" grumbled Boris. He looked at the flowers and the trees.

Pip and Bella quickly hid behind a bush. "Don't worry, Boris!" Pip whispered. "We'll keep playing!"

Boris walked on, sniffing the air. "Hmm, yummy," he said to himself and went back to sleep in his cave.

Pip and Bella giggled. "Let's play again tomorrow!" they said.

That night, Pip curled up in his soft nest with a big, warm blanket. He dreamed about dancing with Bella and chasing butterflies.

And that was the end of Pip's happy adventure.
