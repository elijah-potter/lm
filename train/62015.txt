Aircraft were not invented at that time and the people travelled in the caravans. Staying at the hotel was an extreme need of time as there was no option for lot of the people to live anywhere during the night.
- There was no safety for the travelers as robbery was very common and the materials for the resistance have not invented. People have no choice rather than surrounding to the robbers.
- Cars, railway and airplane were not there and there was no privacy due to which danger was more.
