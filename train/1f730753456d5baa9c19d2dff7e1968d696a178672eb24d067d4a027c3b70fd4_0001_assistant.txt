No problem! Here are some games that meet your requirements: 
- Yahtzee: This popular dice game will need players to have a scorecard, but you can mark this down on a piece of paper. 
- Qwixx: This game also requires a scorecard, but this can be made for your game from paper. 
- Bunco: This game doesn't need a scorecard as long as you know the rules, just dice and possibly some paper to keep track of points. 
- Tenzi: This game takes a lot of dice but nothing else as you try to get your dice to be the same number. 
- Farkle: There are a few variations of this dice game, and you may need a scorecard, but the only main thing you will need is dice.
