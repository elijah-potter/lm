**Scene: The Parking Lot Incident**

*At 8:17 PM, the rain started tapping against the windshield of the black SUV parked near the city park. The driver, **Marcus**, adjusted his grip on the steering wheel, glancing at the rearview mirror.*

---

**Marcus’s Perspective:**
The brake lights of the **old sedan** ahead flickered on—engine sputtering, tires spinning. He watched as the driver, a woman in a hoodie, **slid off the gas** and **steered left**, hitting the curb. The SUV’s rear end swerved, scraping the pavement. The woman’s face was frozen in shock. Marcus pulled over, turned off the engine, and exited the car.

“You okay?” he asked, voice low. The woman nodded, hands trembling. “You okay?”

She blinked. “I—I thought I was going to hit someone.”

Marcus stepped closer. “It was nothing. My fault.”

She hesitated, then sighed. “I’m not used to this.”

---

**Lena’s Perspective:**
*Lena was walking home when she saw the SUV’s brake lights go out. She froze, hands clutching her bag. The car behind her—**Marcus’s SUV**—**wobbled violently**, tires screeching as it fishtailed. She screamed, spinning to run, but the sedan’s driver **jerked the wheel**, sending the SUV into a **wall**.

Marcus groaned, scrambling out. “I’m sorry.”

The woman—**Sarah**—stood frozen, eyes wide. “I thought you were going to kill me.”

Lena stumbled back, shaking. “You almost did.”

---

**Aftermath:**
The SUV’s engine sputtered, then died. Marcus and Sarah sat in silence, staring at the damaged sedan. Lena watched from the sidewalk, heart pounding.

Marcus exhaled. “We’re fine.”

Sarah’s voice was raw. “I’m not.”

Lena’s phone buzzed—text from her sister: *You okay?*

She didn’t reply.
