A faint, half-remembered dream lingers like mist at dawn—
fragments of faces, voices, places that shift when traced,
each detail half-erased by time’s slow, relentless blur.
The past isn’t lost, but folded, stored in a corner of the mind,
where only the edges remain sharp, the core dissolving into static.
What was once a melody now a hum, a word a whisper—
the memory unravels like thread, but holds onto its ghost.

A half-forgotten name, half-a-realized story,
a shadow of a life that was never fully lived,
only glimpsed in the corners of consciousness before fading.
The brain’s archive is incomplete—some truths too fragile,
too swiftly buried beneath the weight of what is.
Yet in the cracks, the echoes persist, faint but persistent.
