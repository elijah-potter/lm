The philosophy of the “glass half full, glass half empty” is a physiological test determining whether one is a pessimist or an optimist. If someone describes a glass of water that is only half filled as being half full, then that person is seen as an optimistic person. This is because optimists see the good in every situation, and by describing the glass of water as being half full, they’re pointing out the fact that yes, the glass is only half filled, but there’s still something to drink. Whereas if someone was to describe the glass as being half empty, they would be seen as being a pessimistic person because pessimists see the bad in every situation, and they’re pointing out that half of the glass is empty from what could’ve been a full glass of water.
