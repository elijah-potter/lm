I tell myself it's just the old oak tree limbs scratching feebly at the glass in the wind. But there is no wind, and there is no oak tree. There's just a street corner bodega many floors below. And then there's the fire escape. 

Of course, I checked that when the sound first started. But that was only after grabbing the Louisville slugger every sensible New Yorker kept by his bed. Or a knife or a gun, depending on just how bad the neighborhood was. But I made the mistake of thinking I should get the drop on whoever was out there. I went in my briefs and my socks. Choking up on the bat with one hand, I went for the window latch with the other. But the closer I drew, the less the silhouette seemed like a man's. My hand slowed.
