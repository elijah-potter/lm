During the second fiscal quarter, Disney announced it generated an operating loss of $659 million. This was nearly $200 million less than Wall Street expected and much better than the negative one billion they accrued in the previous quarter. They also lost 4 million paid subscribers during the quarter, its biggest ever, which shocked analysts who thought the service would add 1.7 million subscribers. Disney World theme park revenue rose 14% YOY to nearly $5.6 billion.
