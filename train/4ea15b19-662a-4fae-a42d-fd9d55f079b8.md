Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the meadow. One sunny morning, Benny saw a shiny red apple on a tree. "Mmm, I think I see a yummy apple!" Benny said, and he hopped right over.

He nibbled a little, but the apple was too big for him. Benny tried to eat it, but it kept rolling away. Suddenly, Benny spotted a tiny squirrel with a big, red acorn. "Can I have this?" Benny asked. The squirrel chattered, "No, thank you!" Benny tried to take it, but the squirrel kept running away.

Benny felt sad. He looked around and saw a friend—a wise old turtle named Tilly. "Why don’t you try something else?" Tilly said. Benny thought about it and realized he could hop around the apple and catch it! He tried again and again, until finally, he got it! He munched happily, but then saw a baby bird with a tiny seed.

"Can I have this?" Benny asked. The baby bird fluttered, "No, thank you!" Benny’s heart felt heavy again. Tilly smiled and said, "Maybe sharing is fun too!"

So Benny picked up the seed and planted it in the meadow. Soon, a beautiful flower grew where the seed was. Benny and his new friend, Tilly, laughed and played together. Benny learned that sharing made everything fun, and he hopped away happy, knowing kindness was the best adventure of all!
