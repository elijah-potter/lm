Some folks wear hats because they have no hair, 
some wear hats because their skin is fair.
Cowboys wear hats to keep out of the sun,
ladies wear hats because they're just plain fun.
Hats come in all colors: red, yellow, and blue.
They can be flat, round, squat, or curlicue.
And while a hat can protect your head,
There is no need to wear it to bed.
For in your bed, your pillow acts,
as the head protector - that's a fact!
But if you go out without a hat on your head,
don't decide to wear a pillow instead!
