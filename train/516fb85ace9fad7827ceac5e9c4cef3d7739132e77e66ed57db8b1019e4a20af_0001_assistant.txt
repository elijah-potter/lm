If you’ve never had to keep anything alive besides yourself, then a cactus is definitely the perfect first plant to try with! It’s really easy to keep a cactus happy and healthy, even if you live in a small apartment or work 50 hours a week, so let’s find out how. 

- Place your cactus by a window so it gets plenty of light. 
- If your cactus starts to turn yellow, that’s too much light! 
- Only water your cactus every 10 days or when the soil is completely dry. 
- Don’t overwater! Only give it a few tablespoons of water.
