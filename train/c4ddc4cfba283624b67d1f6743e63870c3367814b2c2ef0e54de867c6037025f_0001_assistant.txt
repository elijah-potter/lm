Hey Developers!

We've been considering making this change for a long time, and after a long period of deliberation, it's become clear that this is the best course of action. We have had too many close calls with our community getting exposed to content that would make it an unsafe environment for the people in it. Our community is comprised of people across a broad spectrum of ages, and we must be considerate of all these differences and needs.

As a result, we will be banning the usage of all profanity at this time. Please let the moderators know if you have any questions as to what would constitute "profanity." I know that some may feel as though this is a hindrance to growing close as a community, but I can assure you that the opposite is the case. I believe taking this action will help our community to feel like a safe and fun place for people of all ages. Thanks so much for understanding!
