Background Synoptic Assessment – Pulls together independent assessments from a number of modules into a single assessment. Initially to address the:- –lack of coherence in a students understanding of a subject or the connections between elements of the subject. (Dearing 1996) Patrick has identified:- –range or breath, applying knowledge and understanding, use ideas and skills (Patrick 2005) Applied broadly in GCSE and A-Level –Essay, research or enquiry, case studies or experimental work Not so much in Higher Education
Aims To promote active learning by allowing students to learn that a solution for the problem statement for one assessment requires the knowledge and experience of the subject areas from different modules To reduce the overall students workload by combining one or more assessment exercises To make assessments more significant and broaden the students understanding To promote staff collaboration and integration across subject areas.
