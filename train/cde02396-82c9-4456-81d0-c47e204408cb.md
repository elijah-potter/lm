**The Last Light of Eldermere**

In the heart of the mist-shrouded kingdom of Eldermere, where the old magic still whispered through the veins of the earth, **Kael** the wandering scholar found himself at the crossroads of a forgotten ruin. The air smelled of damp stone and ozone, and the faint glow of an ancient artifact pulsed like a heartbeat beneath a cracked obsidian slab.

A low, guttural voice echoed from the shadows: *"Only the bold survive."* The artifact hummed, and three doors—each carved with sigils of power—stood before him. Behind them, the choice to rewrite history or doom the land lay in wait.

---

**What shall Kael choose?**

1. **The Door of Echoes** – Step inside to hear the voices of those who came before, but risk hearing your own fate in their whispers.
2. **The Door of Storms** – Unleash a tempest that could drown the ruins—but may awaken something far older beneath the earth.
3. **The Door of Silence** – Pass through to silence the artifact forever, sealing fate in a void. But what will the kingdom lose in return?
