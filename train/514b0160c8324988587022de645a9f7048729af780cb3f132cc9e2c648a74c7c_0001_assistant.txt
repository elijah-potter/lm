On the way to the city of Hammerstholm, you are approached by a woman with curly auburn hair and green eyes. In her hand she carries a staff with a silver raven at the top, its wings spread as if taking flight, and at her hip, you see an unusual clear flask that appears to be filled with a glittering blue liquid. Her cloak has the most striking appearance - green and yellow stripes running up and down its length. She smiles cheerfully at you and introduces herself: "Hello! I am Alinwe!"
