RJ was a quiet man serving a lengthy sentence for armed robbery, and he was frustrated by his fellow jailmates' constant singing. He tried to ignore them, but the singing was too loud and would happen at random times. In the morning, at lunchtime, and even when it was lights out. RJ asked a jailmate to stop singing, and the jailmate told him that he couldn’t. It was as though he was possessed by song. Another jailmate started singing, and then another, and then another. Once one started, the others couldn't control themselves. The correctional officers didn't try to get them to tone it down. Some tapped their feet and whistled along. The prisoners even incorporated a dance routine. RJ sighed and put his commissary-purchased earbuds back in, but he could still hear them singing. He was frustrated and didn't know what to do. He had hated musicals since he was young, and still couldn’t escape them. He just wanted peace and quiet, but he knew that wasn't possible in a prison. He thought about complaining to the warden but knew that wouldn’t do any good. The warden wouldn’t care about his situation at all.
