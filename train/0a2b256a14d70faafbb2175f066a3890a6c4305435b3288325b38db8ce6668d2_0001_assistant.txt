Virginia Marie Thorne Proctor was born on April 2, 1937, in Greensburg, PA and passed away on April 5, 2023, at the age of 86. Gina married Owen Proctor on June 14, 1958, who was commissioned in the Air Force, prompting worldwide travel. They got their first boxer dog shortly after getting married, which began their passion for showing and breeding boxers. Their dog Virgo's Market Boomer became one of the top boxers in the country.
