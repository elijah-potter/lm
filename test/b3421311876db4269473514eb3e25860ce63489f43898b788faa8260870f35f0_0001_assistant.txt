I hopped off the long ladder and landed softly. This planet’s gravity was softer than we were used to, allowing us to take great bounding steps across its surface. Soft orange dust puffed up around our feet with each step. It was beautiful, and very different than home. Oh, the things you can discover when you go somewhere new!

Lloyd took even bigger leaps than I did. It must be convenient to have four legs! The big, round helmet around his head made him look a little goofy. But my friendly llama did love to put on that space suit! And he always had fun when we got down to a new planet. To prove my point, he brayed contentedly and took another leap up to the top of a small boulder.

I smiled inside my own space helmet. I certainly understood how Lloyd felt. The landscape was exciting and unusual. Far away on the horizon, a series of steep yellow mountains shone brightly in the sunlight, looking markedly different than anything on Earth. I shook with anticipation, wondering what we might discover out there. Lloyd hopped off his boulder and nuzzled against me, clearly thinking the same thing. 

I looked at my sensor tablet. It indicated that the planet was full of life – creatures and plants that no Earthling had ever met. Perhaps it’s a shame that we landed in the orange desert, but at least that meant we would not come down on top of some unfortunate space squirrel. 

Pulling my eyes from the view, I hazarded a glance back at our tall rocket ship. “You locked the doors, right Lloyd?” I hollered to my friend. 

The tall llama nodded his head inside his bubble-shaped space helmet. 

I was happy to see that Lloyd’s smile was as wide as mine. He knew what was next. “Good! It’s time to go exploring!”
