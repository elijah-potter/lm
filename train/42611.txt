Programme for child's emotional competences training - VEIK
The key purpose of the VEIK programme is to strengthen learners' core competencies, through creating a collaborative network between students, parents, teachers and NGOs.
VEIK is an innovative programme through which children are consistently and continuously taught to recognise and name their emotions, manage strong feelings, develop their communication and other social skills, improve problem-solving in everyday life. The whole programme enables children to understand the benefits of interpersonal relationships and to look for mature solutions when faced with everyday situations.
In order to create a safe and supporting environment for children, teachers and parents are also provided training in order to encourage the maintenance of children-learned skills both at school and at home.
