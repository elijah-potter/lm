Beatriz Flamini, of Madrid, Spain, recently spent 500 days underground in a cave. The 48-year-old is an extreme athlete wanted to challenge and test her mental strength and documented with Go Pro cameras. Flamini brought 60 books, art and crafts with her, and volunteers brought food, clothes, and water to the cave's entrance. Partway through the quest, she had to leave due to a technical issue but spent eight days in a tent in isolation before returning. She states she lost her sense of time after about two months.
