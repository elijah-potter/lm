Training for Children's Ministry Course-2: Presentation Ideas
Using ‘pictures, people and props’ learn how to use visual aids and make your talks interactive. Andy Gawn gives some creative ideas on how to bring your bible teaching alive- whilst all the time making sure the children understand the meaning of the passage. Includes Andy’s ‘top ten props’ for kid’s talks that you will use over and over again.

This course is a great place to get fresh ideas and develop your skills in children's ministry. It has been developed over many years by Andy Gawn, children's coordinator at Jesmond Parish Church in Newcastle, UK.
