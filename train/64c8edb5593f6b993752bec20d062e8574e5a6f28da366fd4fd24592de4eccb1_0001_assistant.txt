The Hobbit is a wonderful fantasy novel written by J.R.R. Tolkien. It's set in the fictitious world of Middle Earth and involves the hobbit Bilbo Baggins, Gandalf the Wizard, multiple dwarves, elves, goblins and even a dragon. It tells the story of Bilbo leaving his small home on an adventure and how he begins to help the dwarves get back to their home and kill a dragon. He also finds a magical ring that makes him invisible and he makes a lot of money from the dwarves as they rewarded him for their help at the end of the story.
