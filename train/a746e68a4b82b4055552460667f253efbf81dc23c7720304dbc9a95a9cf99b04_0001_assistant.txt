Meow, moo, bark! A farm is full of fun noises like these
Head down to the lake and hear the honk of the geese
Lay in the flowers with buzzing bees
Feed the pigs and they’ll oink with delight 
Pet the cat and it’ll meow, all right
But a startled cat will hiss with fright 
And when the barking dogs are a little too playful 
They’ll boom and growl and be a real handful
