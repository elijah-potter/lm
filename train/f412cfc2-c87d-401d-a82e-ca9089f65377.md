**Local Officials Urge Caution as Heavy Rain and Flooding Threaten Downtown Bridgeville**

Residents of Bridgeville were warned this morning as severe weather warnings expanded to include the city center, following overnight heavy downpours that led to localized flooding along Riverfront Drive. Emergency crews reported multiple reports of cars stranded on the main thoroughfare, with some traffic lights temporarily disabled due to power outages caused by fallen trees.

Mayor Elena Carter announced a citywide shelter-in-place order for areas near the new Bridgeport Dam, citing fears of rapid water overflow. "We’ve seen this happen before," she said during a press briefing, "but the system is understaffed after last month’s storm response. Please avoid downtown unless absolutely necessary."

Schools in the city were ordered to close for the day, and public transit was suspended until further notice. The local fire department confirmed that a reported gas leak near the old Mill District was contained but advised residents to avoid the area due to potential structural damage.

Authorities also reported a surge in 911 calls for medical emergencies, including reports of hypothermia and carbon monoxide poisoning from improperly ventilated spaces. The city’s health department urged residents to use battery-powered flashlights and bottled water, while the Red Cross set up temporary warming centers in the High School gymnasium and community center.

Governor Thompson has declared a state of emergency, activating the National Guard to assist in clearing debris and managing evacuation routes. "This is not just a storm—it’s a reminder of how vulnerable our infrastructure is," the governor stated. "We’re working with federal agencies to ensure resources reach Bridgeville first."

Residents were advised to check on elderly neighbors and pets, as many shelters had already reached capacity. Officials emphasized that the city’s flood barriers, installed last year, were functional but stressed that the heavy rainfall had exceeded projections. No fatalities have been reported, but several injuries were treated at local hospitals.

For real-time updates, residents are directed to follow @BridgevilleAlerts on Twitter or call 9-1-1. The city’s website has been updated with evacuation zones and road closures. The next public briefing is scheduled for 6 PM tonight in the Civic Center.
