## Lost in the Lavender Fields (Sonoma, CA)

The late afternoon sun was painting the rolling hills a honeyed gold when I saw you. We were both wandering through the lavender fields at Matson Vineyards, the air thick with the intoxicating fragrance of the blooms. You were sketching in a small notebook, completely absorbed, and I found myself lingering nearby, admiring your focused intensity. I remember the gentle hum of bees flitting between the purple stalks, a peaceful soundtrack to the scene. We didn't speak, but our eyes met briefly, a silent acknowledgment of the beauty surrounding us. I was captivated by the way the sunlight caught the auburn highlights in your hair. It felt like a moment suspended in time, a quiet connection forged amidst the fragrant landscape. I departed shortly thereafter, feeling a pang of regret at not having spoken. I would very much like to know if you recall seeing me. Please respond with the color of your pen.
