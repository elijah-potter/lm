A lone figure stood before the cracked mirror, their reflection shimmering like liquid silver in the dim glow of the flickering lamp. The image flickered—first their hand reaching out, then their face, and finally, a child’s laughter echoing from the past. The mirror shattered into pieces, each fragment holding a memory: a wedding, a funeral, a whispered secret. The child’s laughter faded into silence.

The figure turned to find themselves in the same room, the same lamp casting the same shadows. The mirror was whole again. They reached out and touched it. The reflection blinked. The cycle began anew.
