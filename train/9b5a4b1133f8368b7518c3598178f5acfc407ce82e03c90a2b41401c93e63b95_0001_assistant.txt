A Saucer That's Not The Kind You'd Find A Pizza On

Michigan Avenue at three AM? This may be one of the least likely places you would think that a UFO sighting would come from. However, that was the time at which over 50 phone calls were made to local authorities claiming that a UFO landed on Michigan Avenue. While the police would typically chalk this up to a prank phone call, the overwhelming amount of calls led them to investigate. The investigation found that the calls were accurate and there was a UFO spotted on Michigan Ave. Does this mean the existence of aliens? Not exactly, suggested one officer. "Although we have reason to believe that this may be an alien craft, it could also be an experiment by the city or some type of governmental testing." We leave it to you to decide. Was it a flying saucer or are Chicagoans just seeing what they want to imagine, a pizza tray befitting our famous pizza?
