Americans don’t see foxes very often because they’re quite rare in the US. However, England is full of them. Since people build homes next to the area forests, foxes tend to get put out with nowhere else to go. Therefore, they started heading to the city and visited people!
It’s common for homeowners near the woods to see foxes in the backyard. While it sounds impossible and incredible, it’s true! In fact, a fox can befriend a human, and it gets weirder than that! Some foxes may even share food with human friends, offering it to other animal species that are just as adorable!
Foxes in England
As you just learned, it’s quite common for a fox to run along an England street. The English are used to it, especially during the night. Having a fox jump and dig a hole in your backyard is seen as normal. Still, most people are shocked to learn that these creatures are friendly!
Since the English have to live close to the foxes, it’s natural for them to feed the animals periodically. In fact, wildlife officials claim that’s it’s good to feed them because they can’t really hunt on their own. Without a source of food, they could die.
