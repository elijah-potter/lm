**The Village of Eldermere**
A tight-knit community nestled between rolling hills, where every street corner hums with unspoken gossip. Children play in the square, their laughter mingling with the sound of shared gossip from elders. A bakery's scent drifts from the market stall where every family gathers for daily bread, and the butcher’s meat is always pre-cut to order. The church bell tolls the hour, and its echo carries whispers of old feuds and forgotten rivalries. Neighbors help strangers—just because they’ve seen you leave the house twice in the last month. The local tavern, *The Three Crows*, is the only place where secrets spill like wine, but no one remembers who started it all. The only rule: never ask *why*.
