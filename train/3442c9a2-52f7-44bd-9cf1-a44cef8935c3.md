Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a tiny mouse named Mimi. Mimi loved to explore her garden, where colorful flowers and soft grass tickled her paws.

One sunny afternoon, Mimi found a shiny blue button near her favorite dandelion. She picked it up and wondered, "What if I press this button?" She carefully pressed it, and suddenly—*POP!*—the button turned into a tiny, twinkling star.

Mimi's eyes sparkled. "Wow!" she thought. "I can fly now!" She soared above the garden, feeling like a brave little superhero. But just as she was about to reach the sky, she heard a tiny *thump* from below.

Mimi looked down and saw her best friend, Benny the bunny, sitting under a bush. Benny had fallen and his ears were a little tangled. "Oh dear," Mimi said. "I should help you!"

She flew down gently and helped Benny untangle his ears. "Thanks, Mimi!" Benny said happily. "Now I can hop all the way to the moon if I want!"

As they played together, Mimi realized that even the smallest helpers could make the biggest differences. And from that day on, Mimi knew she was the bravest mouse in the garden—because she always looked out for her friends.

The end!
