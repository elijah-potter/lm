One day, Michael went to the local coffee shop to get his usual drink: a large black coffee. Michael was a boring person who liked routines and didn't venture out of his comfort zone for any reason.

However, on this day, Michael decided to try something new: instead of a large black coffee, he wanted to try a medium iced caramel macchiato with whipped cream. For some reason, Michael felt a little goofy today and wanted to see what it was like being outside his comfort zone for once.

Michael went through the drive-through and ordered his drink, then went to the next window to pay. He received his drink and had a sip: it was cold, tasty, sugary, and much more vibrant than he cared for. He was so put off by the in-your-face tastiness of the macchiato that he threw the drink cup out of his car window into the middle of traffic.

Little did Michael know, this incident would set off a chain reaction of events that would result in tragedy. The coffee cup he threw out the window ended up hitting a passer-by in another vehicle, causing the driver to momentarily veer off the road and crash into a utility pole. The utility pole fell onto the roadway and caused a road closure that would last for several hours.

While the road closure was going on, the driver of a transport truck carrying a supply of fried chicken for a nearby restaurant decided to drive through the obstruction and ended up overturning and spilling the chicken on the road.

The nearby restaurant ran out of chicken and would have to be shut down for the day due to the failure of the delivery truck to deliver their chicken. As a result, all restaurant workers had to go home for the day. One of the workers, a young teen who was bored, decided to go to the store to buy fireworks to play with at home.

The worker ended up lighting a bottle rocket that flew errantly through the open window of a fireworks factory, which caused the fireworks within to catch fire and explode and cause the entire factory to explode and go up in flames.
