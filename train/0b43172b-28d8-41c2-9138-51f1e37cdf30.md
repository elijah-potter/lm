A routine trip to the grocery store turned into an unexpected detour when the cart path was blocked by a fallen tree branch. The person had planned to stop at the store and pick up essentials before heading home. As they navigated around the obstruction, they spotted a friend’s old bookcase abandoned in a nearby alley, partially covered by leaves. Curious, they picked it up, only to realize it was a family heirloom.

Returning to their car, they noticed their phone had been charging in the dash—unbeknownst to them, a neighbor had left it there earlier that day. They used it to look up the bookcase’s history and found an old address in the owner’s notes. Without hesitation, they headed to the address, where they discovered the owner had recently moved abroad and left the house in disarray. The person found a locked box under the floorboards containing a letter and a set of keys.

By the time they arrived at the planned grocery stop, the person realized they had a new mission: retrieve the keys and find out what the box contained. The grocery items were left behind, but the unexpected discovery changed their evening entirely.
