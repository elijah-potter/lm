Steel hums beneath feet as rusted train wheels groan on tracks, their rhythmic *clang-clang* fading into the night. A damp, earthy scent lingers from the cracked asphalt near the station—mud and rain, mingling with the sharp tang of diesel from the parked trucks. The wind carries the faintest whisper of distant laughter, rustling through leaves that clatter against the rusted fence. Inside the abandoned shop, stale smoke curls from an old pipe, mixing with the acrid stench of forgotten oil and mildewed paper. The distant *beep-boop* of a broken alarm clock echoes, its once-clear voice now a broken, monotone whisper.
