Florida has recently passed multiple bills dealing with what books can be kept in schools. Teachers can be charged with a felony if they are deemed to have exposed kids to books the state says are inappropriate. Books by Black and LGBTQ writers are more often targeted as being inappropriate. Schools asked teachers to remove all books from their classrooms until they could be approved. Even books that have been previously approved can be challenged by anyone in a county and will be removed until they have been reviewed.  Then the decision can be appealed to a commissioner appointed by the governor if they disagree with the ruling.
