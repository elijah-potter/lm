The project consists of PowerPoint presentations, interactive quizzes and links to external sites covering the Differential Calculus section of the Leaving Certificate Higher Level Mathematics examination. Many of the presentations contains slides outlining solutions for past Leaving Certificate examinations questions and also included in the Past Papers page are complete PowerPoint presentations of questions six and seven of the mathematics paper one at Higher Leaving Certificate Level. Emphasis is put on the students understanding rather than learning of rules. When students get a question wrong in interactive quizzes, an explanation is given as to why this is wrong.
What You Need
Laptop / PC, Data Projector and an Internet connection (preferably Broadband). Advice is given in the Lessons page Methods column for all classes.
