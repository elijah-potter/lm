Here are seven affirmations you can use to lift your spirits: 

1. I can handle whatever life throws at me because I am strong and resilient. 
2. I am proud of exactly who I am. 
3. I am grateful today for (enter personal gratitude here).
4. I am worthy of love and am loved by many. 
5. I am valuable exactly as I am; there is nothing I need to prove. 
6. I deserve joy and happiness. 
7. There is nothing I cannot do.
