I can certainly help with that. Here are five of the best cheesesteak shops in Philadelphia along with some information about each restaurant:

- Steve's Prince of Steaks. This Northeast Philly cheesesteak spot hasn't changed its recipe much in the past forty years. If you want a classic steak sandwich made with rib-eye that's shaved to an eighth of an inch, then drenched in Cheese Whiz, look no further than Steve's Prince of Steaks. 
- Dalessandro’s Steaks and Hoagies. Regarded by some as the very best cheesesteak in Philadelphia, Dalessandro's packs their rolls full of finely chopped rib-eye with the cheese mixed in and chunky pieces of onion for a flavor experience that's well worth the trip. 
- Sonny's Famous Steaks. Located in tourist-friendly Old City, Sonny's offers friendly service, a tremendous product, and manageable lines. If you are in the area and on a schedule, check out Sonny's for a delicious cheesesteak without the wait. 
- Geno's Steaks. This 24-7 cheesesteak shop is a Philadelphia landmark, so expect a line at most times of the day. That being said, Geno's is one of the most famous steak shops in Philly for a reason so be sure to check it out if you have the time to spare. 
- Pat's King of Steaks. Another iconic Philadelphia location, Pat's on Passyunk claims to have literally invented the cheesesteak back in 1930. Located down the street from Geno's Steaks, Pat's is also open 24-7 and usually has a long line of tourists and regulars. If your schedule is flexible or you're a night owl, consider visiting Pat's at an odd hour in order to avoid the line.
