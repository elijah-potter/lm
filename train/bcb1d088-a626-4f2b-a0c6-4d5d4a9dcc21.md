A winding dirt track fading into mist, its edges swallowed by dense undergrowth. Overgrown fences crumble into the earth, their posts leaning precariously, half-buried in ivy. A single, rusted metal signpost stands alone, its once-bold text now faded and peeling—*"Exit: Nowhere, Turn Left"*—now just a faint, indistinct smear. The path itself is a series of shallow, irregular dips and rises, as if the earth itself has been unevenly eroded over time. No footprints. No signs of recent movement. The air smells damp and stagnant, thick with the scent of old wood and damp earth. A distant, faint echo of laughter—once cheerful—now just a ghostly whisper lost in the silence.
