AITA for refusing to let my sister bring her boyfriend to our family vacation?

Okay, this is a bit complicated, so bear with me. It involves my family's annual beach trip, something we’ve done every August since I was a kid. It's a big deal – my parents, my sister Chloe (24), her boyfriend Liam (26), my aunt and uncle, and their two kids. We rent a large house in Outer Banks, North Carolina. It’s always been a very…structured affair. My mom plans everything meticulously – meal rotations, activity schedules, even designated quiet hours. 

This year, the planning started in March. On March 12th, I received the initial email outlining the dates (August 11th-18th) and general itinerary. Chloe started dating Liam about six months ago, around February. I’m 28 and have always been fairly close to Chloe, but she’s always been…sensitive. She tends to take things very personally. 

My mom's email on April 20th specifically stated, "As always, this is a family-only vacation. Significant others are not included." It’s a tradition, and honestly, it’s always been understood. It's not meant to be exclusionary; it's about maintaining a certain dynamic within the immediate family. My aunt and uncle have always followed this rule, and no one has ever questioned it. 

Chloe, predictably, reacted poorly. On April 21st, she texted me, “Seriously, Mom’s being ridiculous. Liam wants to come, and I want him to come. It’s not fair.” I tried to explain the tradition, but she accused me of being “Mom’s enabler” and said I was deliberately trying to hurt her. I felt a bit bad, but I also felt like I was being put in a really awkward position.

I brought it up with my mom on April 22nd. She was firm. “It’s not personal, dear. It’s just how we do things. It helps keep the focus on the family unit.” She asked me to help reinforce the message to Chloe.

Things escalated on May 20th. Chloe and Liam showed up at my apartment unannounced. Liam was very charming and made a point of telling me how much he wanted to join the vacation. Chloe looked at me expectantly. I felt incredibly uncomfortable and reiterated my mom’s message. Liam seemed annoyed, and Chloe burst into tears, accusing me of sabotaging her relationship.

I tried to mediate. I suggested a separate weekend getaway for them. I offered to pay for part of it. Nothing worked. Chloe has been increasingly distant and has been posting passive-aggressive things on social media about “family traditions” and “exclusion.”

My aunt called me on July 15th, concerned. She said Chloe had been complaining to her about how “mean” I am and how I’m trying to keep her boyfriend away. My aunt, while generally supportive, admitted she thought the rule was a bit outdated but understood the family's history. She asked me to try and be more understanding.

Yesterday, August 8th, I received a text from Chloe: “If Liam isn’t coming, I’m not going.”

My mom is now incredibly stressed. She wants the vacation to go smoothly, and she’s worried that Chloe’s threat will disrupt everything. She’s asked me to try and convince Chloe to change her mind, hinting that she might be willing to make an exception. However, I feel like bending now would undermine the long-standing tradition and potentially create a precedent for future years. I’m also worried about Chloe – she’s clearly struggling with feeling rejected, and I don't want to be the source of that, but I also don’t want to cave to her demands. I feel like I’m being manipulated.

So, AITA for sticking to the family tradition and refusing to let my sister bring her boyfriend, even if it means she might not come on vacation?
