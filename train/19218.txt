Smallpox was one of the deadliest diseases in human history. In The contagion of liberty: the politics of smallpox in the American revolution, Andrew Wehrman takes the time to explain the political context of the disease, which was at its height in the British American colonies during the American revolution. Wehrman relates smallpox to the development of widespread values prioritising personal and public liberty, but underrepresents enslaved and Indigenous groups, who were only further minoritised by the disease and its political landscape.
