•	Black Forest Cake 

Decadent and luxurious, Black Forest Cake is a combination of tart cherries and cherry compote over a sponge cake of dark chocolate. Layered in threes, the sponge cake is sandwiched with a mixture of cherries and whipped cream or milk chocolate mousse, then the sides are pressed with Bavarian chocolate shards or milk chocolate curls. Traditionally, the dessert is creamy and indulgent, soaked in black cherry liquor for extra richness. 

•	Coffee Caramel Mousse

Creamy and airy with a fluff factor that could rival the softness of homemade whipped cream, Coffee Caramel Mousse boasts a subtle sweetness that makes it the perfect lightweight dessert. The tastes of coffee and caramel intermingle, and its often accompanied by fresh raspberries or strawberry slices to break up texture and flavors. 

•	Banana Bread 

The sweetness in Banana Bread comes from mashed bananas, stirred with butter to a fluffy consistency before being baked to golden-brown perfection. The exterior crust is slightly crispy, while the interior is soft and light. Best-served warm, banana bread is delicious down to the last crumb. Some bakers incorporate chopped walnuts or chocolate chips for added texture and flavor.   

•	Apple Brown Betty 

Reminiscent of an apple pie, Apple Brown Betty incorporates soft cinnamon-sugared apple slices with buttery, oven-browned breadcrumbs. The perfect dessert for autumn, this one is sticky-sweet with a spiced crisp that melts in your mouth. To cut the warmth and spice of the Apple Brown Betty, it’s best served with a dollop of vanilla bean ice cream or fluffy, subtly sweet homemade whipped cream.
