Silent room hums with residual warmth—
faint keyboard glow bleeds through curtains,
time stretches like a half-remembered dream.

A pause in the hum of the fridge,
the distant drip of water,
a single candle flickers against the glow of a laptop screen,
its blue light painting the wall in soft streaks.

The city’s chaos fades—subway trains,
car horns, the occasional scream—
replaced by the soft rustle of a paperback,
the quiet intake of a breath,
the whisper of a breeze through open windows.

A moment of stillness where the world feels
like a well-worn map, slowly unrolling.
