Sure! Here is a list of cats that are known to act rather "dog-like":

1. Abyssinian - these cats love the water and like to play fetch with their favorite toys. They are very social and require a lot of attention.

2. Ragdoll - these cats tend to go limp and rest in your arms when you hold them, more like a puppy than a cat. They are very calm and mellow and often seek out affection.

3. Manx - these cats tend to come when you whistle or call their name. They love people and are very responsive.

4. Turkish Angora - these cats are very devoted and social creatures. They enjoy swimming, are quite clever, and also enjoy the company of children.

5. Bombay - these cats enjoy walking on a leash and get along well with dogs. They are very playful and affectionate and often invent new ways to entertain themselves.
