1. Jeep Wrangler Unlimited - This is usually one of the first vehicles people think about when it comes to an off-road capable vehicle. Each Wrangler has the Trail Rated badge on the outside of the vehicle. This means that it has been tested in extreme conditions and has performed well. From shocks to the placement of electrical connections, the Wrangler has meticulously planned out everything to ensure the best off-road experience. 
2. Toyota 4Runner TRD Pro - While the body of the 4Runner TRD Pro is bigger than the Wrangler, it is also a very off-road capable vehicle. The added space improves seating comfort. This vehicle features high-performance shocks, which makes it ready to take on rocks and logs with ease. It also contains unique aspects like Crawl Control and Multi-Terrain Select, which both help to improve the maneuverability of the vehicle while off-road. 
3. Ford Bronco Raptor - One of the newer vehicles in the off-road game, the Ford Bronco Raptor has many great features that make it a good off-road vehicle. The 37" tires are made to increase traction and driveability even in rough terrain. Unique features like Trail Turn Assist and Trail Control increase the traction and control you have while out on the trail. It also includes automatic 4x4 engagement to get you through unexpected terrain.
