Learning Fundamentals in a Whole New Way
Our early childhood program is the beginning of children’s quest for independence. In a multi-age classroom, children are able to facilitate their own learning by observing older peers or helping younger students with lessons. During this critical period in their life, children begin to refine their motor skills, both gross and fine, and learn how to move with purpose. Through our wide variety of materials, children grow cognitively by engaging in materials for Practical Life, Sensorial, Language, Mathematics, Culture, and Science. At this young age, children are also taught the importance of grace and courtesy and how to use it in their everyday lives.
