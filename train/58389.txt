First atom bomb
3D model of the first atom bomb
This 3D model cannot be viewed as a 3D model of something positive; however, the first atom bomb 3D model may serve peaceful aims. The 3D first atom bomb can show what nuclear weapon is, and maybe awake the consciousness of those who are dreaming of its mass production. So, the first atom bomb 3D is an explosive device that derives its destructive force from nuclear reactions, either fission or a combination of fission and fusion. Both reactions release vast quantities of energy from relatively small amounts of matter.
