ZOO ESCAPEE IN DOWNTOWN D.C - POLICE STRUGGLE TO DISTINGUISH BETWEEN MONKEY AND POLITICIANS 
The Zoo is missing one of its primary primates, as he has escaped to downtown Washington D.C making his way to Capitol Hill. The struggle to retrieve the menacing monkey is proving to be difficult, as the crafty criminal bears a strong resemblance to the politicians crowding the streets. If seen, the monkey is said to be highly intelligent and should not be approached; contact the authorities.
