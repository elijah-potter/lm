Hey there! Thank you so much for purchasing from George's SCOBYs. We can't wait for you to receive your "mother!" Remember that a SCOBY is a live bacterial colony, so there are a few things you'll need to do to keep it happy when it arrives:

• If you want to use your SCOBY right away, you can immediately brew a batch of kombucha! Head to George.com to find a recipe you'll love.
• If you're not quite ready to brew some kombucha, pop the SCOBY in a Ziploc bag or glass jar with a bit of liquid to cover it and place it in the fridge. Your SCOBY will stay nice and fresh for up to 3 months with this method.
• Let your SCOBY take up residence in a "SCOBY hotel" for the most long-term storage solution. 

You can always log onto George.com for more tips or to use our forum, where many other brewers post and converse in our staff-moderated forum! Don't hesitate to reach out with any questions.
