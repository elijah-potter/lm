Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had soft brown fur and big, round eyes that sparkled like the sun. Every morning, Benny would hop outside to see his friends.

One sunny day, Benny spotted a bright yellow flower growing near the fence. It was as tall as he was and had a big, sweet smell. "Mmm, that smells so good!" Benny said to himself.

Benny decided to pick the flower but noticed a little bird with a sad face sitting on a branch nearby. "Why is that bird looking so down?" Benny wondered.

Benny gently picked up the flower and placed it in the bird’s nest. The bird chirped happily and flapped its wings. "Thank you, Benny!" the bird said.

Benny smiled. "You’re welcome!" He picked up a few berries from his basket and shared them with the bird. Soon, the bird sat comfortably on the nest, munching on the berries.

As the sun set, Benny realized he had done something kind. He hopped home, happy and content, knowing that sharing made everyone feel better. And from that day on, Benny was the friendliest bunny in the garden, always looking for ways to help others.
