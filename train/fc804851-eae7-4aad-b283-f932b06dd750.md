Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a garden full of sunshine, lived a tiny mouse named Pip. Pip loved to explore and play, especially near the big, fluffy grass.

One sunny morning, Pip found a shiny red apple under a big, tall tree. It looked so yummy! But before he could eat it, he heard a rustling sound from the bushes. Out came a big, friendly squirrel named Squish. "Hi there, Pip!" Squish said. "I was just wondering if you wanted to play hide-and-seek?"

Pip giggled and agreed. They hid behind some leaves and rocks, giggling as they chased each other. Squish had a big acorn to hide, and Pip had a tiny pebble. They played until their tummies started growling. "Let’s find something to eat!" Pip said.

They went to the garden and saw a juicy berry. Squish nibbled one, and Pip ate another. "Yum!" they said together. They played again, this time with a ball made of leaves.

As the sun started to set, Pip and Squish snuggled together under the big tree. "Goodnight, Squish," Pip said. "Goodnight, Pip," Squish replied. They closed their eyes and dreamed of more adventures.

That night, Pip woke up to the sound of his favorite song. He tiptoed outside and saw Squish waving his tail. "Morning, Pip! Let’s play again!" Squish said. Pip smiled and ran to play hide-and-seek once more.

And so, Pip and Squish had many more fun days, learning to share, play, and explore together.
