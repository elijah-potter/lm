A dimly lit server rack hums in an unmonitored data center basement. Dust motes swirl in the stale air, settling on flickering monitors. Inside one console, a single blinking LED—red, then green—flickers weakly against a wall of static noise. Engineers in nearby offices dismiss it as a false alarm, their keyboards clacking in the background. A console log file, barely readable, records the signal: *"Low voltage detected at port 4. Attempting auto-reboot..."*—overwritten by a thousand identical entries from other devices. The system grinds to a halt, leaving the LED to blink in silence for hours before another team notices.
