**ANNOUNCING THE RETURN OF THE FORGOTTEN STORY**

---

In the quiet hum of a clockwork town where lanterns flicker like dying stars, a scribe found a parchment half-buried in the ruins of the old observatory. The ink was faded but legible—words that had slipped through time like smoke. *"The key was never in the vault, but in the echo of the last bell."*

A bell. A warning. And then—nothing.

The scribe followed the trail of crumbling pages to a hollow where the wind howled through skeletal trees. Beneath the moss-stained stones, a door yawned, its iron hinges rusted but still whispering. Inside, the air smelled of ozone and something older—something that had forgotten how to be silent.

*"You were never lost,"* the door murmured, its voice a chorus of voices, all speaking at once. *"You were never meant to be found."*

The scribe’s pen trembled. The story was not yet complete. But the first line was written in blood and ink on the parchment’s edge:

**"The bell tolls for those who dare to listen."**
