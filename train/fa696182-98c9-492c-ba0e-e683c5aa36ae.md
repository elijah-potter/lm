The dim glow of a flickering laptop screen cast long shadows across the worn leather chair in the dimly lit study. The air smelled of stale coffee and old books. Outside, a distant rain tapped against the windows, muffled by the stormy wind. **John** leaned forward, fingers gripping the edge of the desk, his knuckles white.

A soft knock at the door startled him. He turned slowly, heart pounding.

**"John?"** The voice was familiar, low and urgent. **"It’s me, Kate."**

She pushed open the door, her dark eyes wide with concern. Behind her, a uniformed officer stood stiffly, a folded newspaper in their hands.

John stood abruptly, gripping the armrests. **"What is it?"**

Kate stepped inside, her breath shallow. **"The hospital called. They’ve got him."** She gestured to the officer. **"They’re taking him to the field hospital now."**

The officer handed him the newspaper—front page, **bold letters:** *"Battle Dead: Fallen Hero’s Name Listed."*

John’s hands trembled. He read the name aloud: **"Captain Elias Voss."**

Kate’s voice cracked. **"He was your brother."**
