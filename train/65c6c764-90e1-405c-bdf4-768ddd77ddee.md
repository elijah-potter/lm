Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown fur and a big, round nose. Every morning, Benny would hop outside to say hello to his friends: a friendly squirrel named Sammy, a wise old owl named Mr. Owl, and a playful bird named Pip.

One sunny afternoon, Benny noticed something strange. The flowers in his garden were wilting and the grass was brown. "Oh no!" Benny hopped worriedly. "What’s happened to our garden?"

Sammy the squirrel had been collecting acorns and noticed the same thing. "We need to help the garden," Sammy said. "Maybe it’s time to water the flowers."

Benny looked at the dry soil. "I don’t know how to water them!" he whispered.

Pip fluttered down and said, "Don’t worry, Benny! I know just what to do. We can all work together!"

Mr. Owl, who had been sitting quietly, chirped, "I’ll watch for rain clouds. If we all help, our garden will grow back beautiful!"

So, Benny, Sammy, Sammy, and Pip dug small holes in the garden, and Benny filled them with water from his little watering can. They sprinkled the soil gently, making sure every corner got a little drink. While they waited, Benny noticed a tiny seedling peeking out from the earth.

"Look!" Benny gasped. "There’s a new plant growing!"

Soon, rain clouds rolled in, and Mr. Owl watched as gentle drops fell. The watering canny helped, and soon, the flowers and grass began to turn green again. The sun smiled down, and the garden sparkled.

Benny clapped his paws happily. "Our garden is alive again!" he cheered. The friends laughed and played together, knowing that teamwork and kindness made their little world wonderful.

And from that day on, Benny and his friends always looked after their garden, making sure every plant got the love and care it needed. The flowers bloomed bright and beautiful, and the garden became the happiest place in the whole neighborhood.
