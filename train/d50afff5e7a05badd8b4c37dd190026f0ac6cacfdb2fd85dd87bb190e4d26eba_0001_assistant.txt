Nature's sweet delights are many,
Pineapples, grapes, apples, and more. 
The flavors and colors of fruits are plenty,
the world has so many in store.
You can create so much with all these fruits,
Smoothies, cakes, and jams.
You could even go down the savory route,
and eat your melon with ham.
I would be happy to eat nothing else,
to taste only fruity flavors.
Berries and kumquats as bright as bells,
fruit is truly my savior!
Unless you count tomatoes.
Boy, I hate those.
