## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park – A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary (For Municipal Funders)**

The Willow Creek neighborhood faces increasing challenges from urban heat island effect, stormwater runoff, and limited access to green space. This proposal outlines a transformative project to reclaim a currently underutilized and neglected half-acre lot at the corner of Elm and Maple Streets, converting it into the Willow Creek Pocket Park – a climate-resilient community asset. The project will incorporate native plantings, permeable paving, a rainwater harvesting system, and shaded seating areas, providing tangible benefits including reduced urban heat, improved stormwater management, enhanced biodiversity, and a vibrant gathering space for residents. This investment of $75,000 from the municipal fund will be matched by $25,000 in volunteer labor, donated materials, and in-kind services from local businesses, maximizing impact and demonstrating community ownership. The Willow Creek Pocket Park will serve as a replicable model for other underserved neighborhoods within the city, fostering environmental stewardship and strengthening community bonds.

**Project Plan & Timeline**

The project is divided into four distinct phases, each with specific deliverables and responsible partners.

**Phase 1: Planning & Design (Weeks 1-4) – Responsible Partner: GreenSpace Design Collaborative**
*   **Deliverables:** Site assessment report, community design workshop summary, finalized park design plans (including plant selection, grading, and infrastructure layouts), permitting applications submitted to the City Planning Department.
*   **Activities:** Initial site survey and soil testing. Two public design workshops to gather community input. Development of detailed design plans incorporating community feedback and adherence to city regulations. Submission of all necessary permits.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 4 weeks

**Phase 2: Site Preparation & Infrastructure (Weeks 5-12) – Responsible Partners: Willow Creek Neighborhood Association, City Parks Department (equipment loan), Local Construction Company (volunteer labor)**
*   **Deliverables:** Site cleared of debris and invasive species, grading completed to accommodate drainage and planting beds, permeable paving installed, rainwater harvesting system infrastructure in place (cistern, piping).
*   **Activities:** Removal of existing concrete and debris. Grading and leveling of the site. Installation of permeable paving materials. Excavation and installation of the rainwater harvesting system components.  The City Parks Department will loan necessary heavy equipment.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 8 weeks

**Phase 3: Planting & Landscaping (Weeks 13-16) – Responsible Partners: Willow Creek Garden Club, Local Nursery (discounted plant materials), Volunteer Planting Days**
*   **Deliverables:** Native trees, shrubs, and wildflowers planted according to design specifications, mulching completed, irrigation system (if applicable) installed and tested.
*   **Activities:** Community planting days to install native plant species.  Volunteer teams will prepare planting beds and install plants. The Local Nursery will provide discounted native plants.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 4 weeks

**Phase 4: Furnishings & Community Launch (Weeks 17-20) – Responsible Partners: Willow Creek Neighborhood Association, Local Businesses (donated benches & picnic tables), City Parks Department (signage)**
*   **Deliverables:** Installation of benches, picnic tables, and shade structures.  Installation of interpretive signage explaining the park’s features and ecological benefits.  Grand opening celebration event.
*   **Activities:** Installation of park furnishings. Creation and installation of interpretive signage.  Organization of a community grand opening event to celebrate the park’s completion.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 4 weeks

**Budget**

The total project cost is $100,000. The municipal fund request of $75,000 will be matched by $25,000 in in-kind contributions and volunteer labor.

*   **GreenSpace Design Collaborative (Design & Permitting):** $18,000
*   **Site Preparation & Infrastructure (Materials & Equipment Rental):** $32,000 (Permeable paving: $20,000, Cistern: $8,000, Piping & Fittings: $4,000)
*   **Plant Materials (Native Trees, Shrubs, Wildflowers):** $15,000 (Discounted rate from Local Nursery)
*   **Park Furnishings (Benches, Picnic Tables, Shade Structure):** $17,000 (Primarily donated by Local Businesses; $5,000 represents a cost-shared portion)
*   **Signage & Interpretive Materials:** $5,000
*   **Contingency Fund (10%):** $13,000

The contingency fund will address unforeseen expenses related to site conditions, material price fluctuations, or permitting delays.  Volunteer labor, valued at approximately $25,000, significantly reduces overall project costs. This includes labor for site preparation, planting, and furnishing installation.  In-kind donations of materials and equipment from local businesses are valued at approximately $20,000.

**Community Engagement & Equity Impacts**

Community engagement is central to the project’s success. Two public design workshops were held during Phase 1 to ensure the park reflects the needs and desires of Willow Creek residents. Ongoing communication will be maintained through the Willow Creek Neighborhood Association’s newsletter and social media channels. The park’s design prioritizes accessibility for individuals with disabilities, adhering to ADA guidelines. The selection of native plants supports local biodiversity and provides educational opportunities for children. The park’s location in a historically underserved neighborhood addresses inequities in access to green space and contributes to improved public health outcomes.  We specifically solicited input from senior citizens and families with young children during the design phase to ensure the park caters to diverse age groups.

**Risk Mitigation**

Potential risks include permitting delays, unforeseen site conditions (e.g., buried utilities), and weather-related disruptions.  To mitigate these risks, we have engaged GreenSpace Design Collaborative to conduct a thorough site assessment and secure all necessary permits proactively.  The contingency fund will address unexpected expenses.  A detailed communication plan will keep stakeholders informed of any delays.  Volunteer schedules will be flexible to accommodate weather conditions.  We have also contacted the city's utility locating service to mark all underground lines before any excavation begins.

**Post-Grant Sustainability**

The Willow Creek Neighborhood Association will assume responsibility for ongoing park maintenance, including weeding, watering, and litter removal.  The City Parks Department will provide technical support and guidance.  The Local Nursery has committed to providing discounted plants for annual replacements.  Interpretive signage will educate visitors about the importance of native plants and sustainable practices, fostering a sense of stewardship.  The park's design incorporates drought-tolerant plants to minimize water consumption.  We will also explore opportunities for establishing a "Friends of Willow Creek Pocket Park" group to further engage the community in long-term maintenance and programming.

**Call to Action**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park represents a vital investment in the health, well-being, and environmental resilience of our community.  This project will transform a neglected space into a vibrant asset, providing tangible benefits for residents and serving as a model for future neighborhood improvements. We urge the municipal fund to approve this proposal and partner with us in creating a lasting legacy of environmental stewardship and community pride.
