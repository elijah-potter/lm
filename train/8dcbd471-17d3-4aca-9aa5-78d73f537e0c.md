The old pocket watch, half-buried in dust, glinted faintly under the dim lamplight as Elias traced his fingers over its cracked face. His mother had given it to him before her death, *"For when you remember what matters."* The last time he’d checked, it stopped at 3:17 AM.

His breath hitched as the watch’s movement returned—slow, deliberate. A second later, the room’s clock chimed, but it wasn’t the same. The hands spun backward, rewriting time.

Outside, the streetlights flickered to life, revealing the crowd gathering around the abandoned warehouse. Neon signs pulsed with distorted messages: *"NOTHING LEFT TO LOSE."* Elias’s pulse quickened. The watch’s gears rattled as they turned, whispering his name in a voice that wasn’t his own.

The door creaked open. Inside, the air smelled of ozone and old blood. A figure in a long coat stood silhouetted against the flickering emergency lights, holding a file labeled *"Reckoning."* The watch’s face lit up—a single, unreadable number: **0071-45**.

Elias didn’t need to ask why.
