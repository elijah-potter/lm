Sounds like a great time! I've got a few ideas for an ice cream party:

1. Buy unusual flavors for the kids to try, and tell them about some of the crazy flavors they can find in other countries. Ask them if they'd like to try squid ink ice cream, for example. Think about setting up an ice cream bar with unconventional toppings beyond syrup and sprinkles.
2. Which season goes perfectly with ice cream? Summer! Have a summer-themed party at the beach, or hang up beach decorations at your house. Tell the guests to bring their swimsuits so they can play in the inflatable (or in-ground) pool.
3. Have a birthday party at your local ice cream parlor. Many franchises organize children's parties, which means that the employees will also be there to help you out. Plus, everyone gets to try their gourmet ice cream.
