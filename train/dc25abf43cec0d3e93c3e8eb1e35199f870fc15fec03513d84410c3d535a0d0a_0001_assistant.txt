The following U.S. Presidents were assassinated:

1. Abraham Lincoln (1865)- President Lincoln was assassinated on Friday, April 14, 1865, at Ford's Theatre in Washington, D.C., by John Wilkes Booth. A single gunshot to the back of his head mortally wounded him. He died on April 15, 1865, after being in a coma for 8 hours. 
2. James A. Garfield (1881)- President Garfield was shot twice by Charles J. Guiteau on July 2, 1881. He died on September 19, 1881, from infections in the wounds due to doctors using unsanitary practices.
3. William McKinley (1901)- President McKinley was shot on September 6, 1901, at the Temple of Music in Buffalo, New York by Leon Czolgosz. One shot pierced his stomach which later caused gangrene to which he succumbed on September 14, 1901.
4. John F. Kennedy (1963)- President Kennedy was assassinated on November 22, 1963, in Dallas, Texas, while riding in a presidential motorcade, by Lee Harvey Oswald who was positioned on the 6th floor of the Texas School Book Depository. Kennedy was shot twice, once in the back of the head and once in the back which exited through his throat.
