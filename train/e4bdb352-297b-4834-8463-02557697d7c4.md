A vast, endless ocean compared to a single drop of water in a glass.
Mountains rise like jagged teeth against a sky as blue as polished sapphire.
Forests stretch as wide as a continent’s borders, dense as a thicket of ivy.
The wind whispers like a thousand voices, soft as a whisper, strong as a hurricane.
A fire burns hotter than molten lava, its glow brighter than the northern lights.
A river carves through stone as patiently as an artist sculpting marble.
The night sky is deeper than the abyss, filled with stars that outnumber grains of sand on a beach.
A storm rolls like a giant’s shadow, its fury as relentless as a mountain’s ascent.
A meadow blooms green as fresh grass, wild and untamed as the sea’s embrace.
A single leaf falls like a silent arrow, drifting slow as a dream.
