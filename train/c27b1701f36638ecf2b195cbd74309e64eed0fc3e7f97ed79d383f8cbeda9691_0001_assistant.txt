"Can you wait here for me, Asuka? I've got to go take care of this business," I asked her, patting her long white hair between the horns. 

"Okay! Stay safe!" she said happily.

I left Asuka outside the warehouse with my client, telling him to just keep an eye on her. Then, I entered the warehouse. It was very quiet, save for an irregular thumping. I walked between the rows of shelves, following the sound until I found a large bullman with tattered pants. "Excuse me," I called out. "You've been causing a bit of trouble for the owners of this warehouse. Do you speak?"

The bullman turned around slowly, its eyes red. I sighed, rolling my shoulders. "I'd rather not fight if possible--"

The bullman charged at me, head down with a guttural sound. I jumped aside easily and grabbed him by his fur, slingling him into the wall easily. "You're not going to get much done like that. Let's just quit, yeah?"

The bullman came at me wildly again. I tried to dodge, but it pivoted and tried to catch me with an arm. I ducked it and planted a fist into its gut. This stopped it but only for a moment. It was standing again and throwing wild punches, giving up the charge. I dodged the wild swings easily and decided to put an end to things when I noticed the bullman had a tattoo on his ankle. It was the thousand arms tattoo. My stomach sank, and I knew I had to get back to Asuka immediately.

I put a sliver of power behind my next kick and downed the bullman instantly, turning on the ball of my free foot and sprinting for the exit. "Don't be in such a rush," I heard the bullman say. He was rushing behind me, his eyes no longer red and a wicked smile on his snout. 

I heard muffled cries. Asuka was in danger.
