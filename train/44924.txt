4s atomic orbital
The shape of the 4s orbital. The blue zone is where the wave function has positive values while the white zone is where values are negative.
For any atom there is just one 4s orbital. The image on the top is deceptively simple as the interesting feature is buried within the orbital. That on the left is sliced in half to show the two spherical nodes of the 4s orbital. The shape on the right shows the nodal structure of the 4s-orbital.
While still spherical, the higher s-orbitals (5s, 6s, and 7s) are more complex since they have more spherical nodes. While still spherical, the lower s-orbitals (3s, 2s, and 1s) are simpler since they have fewer spherical nodes.
