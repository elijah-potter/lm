The city council has confirmed leaks from an unregulated wastewater treatment plant near the old mill district. Residents report unusual health symptoms, including chronic fatigue and skin irritation, with some testing positive for rare contaminants. The plant’s operator denies any wrongdoing, citing "proper disposal procedures," but independent water tests by a local NGO show elevated levels of microplastics and heavy metals in nearby wells. Investigators are urging community testing and legal pressure. Officials say they are reviewing permits but have not yet suspended operations.
