Between the Tropic of Capricorn and the Tropic of Cancer, there are many of the world's tropical rainforests, which take up about 8% of the entire land surface. Most of these rainforests are in tropical America. Asia, Oceania, and Africa, and more than half of the flora and fauna species on Earth can be found in these areas. The biggest rainforest is South America's Amazon, stretching hundreds of millions of hectares and nine countries.
