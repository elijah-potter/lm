Motor stator winding distribution
The stator winding of the motor is divided into an explicit pole and a hidden pole according to the number of magnetic poles and the distribution pattern of the winding. In the pole-type winding, each group of coils forms a magnetic pole, and the number of coils and the number of magnetic poles of the winding are equal, and the connection mode must be tail. Ending, head joint, that is, reverse connection series; in the hidden pole winding, each upper (group) coil forms two magnetic poles, and the number of coils of the winding is half of the number of magnetic poles. Therefore, the connection mode must be a tail joint, that is, a series connection. In the way, there are many applications in motor windings.
