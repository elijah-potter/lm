Once there was a little bunny named Pip who loved to hop and play. One sunny morning, Pip saw a big, shiny red apple on the grass. "Yum!" Pip thought, and he hopped right over to grab it. But when Pip tried to eat it, it was too hard and crunchy. "Mmm, not so good," Pip said.

Pip decided to ask his friends. His friend Benny the squirrel had a nut to share. "This is better," Benny said, nibbling his nut. "It’s soft and sweet!" Pip tried a tiny bite and said, "Oh, this is good!"

Later, Pip saw a big, juicy watermelon on the ground. "I’ll eat this!" Pip thought. But as he took a bite, he realized the watermelon was too big and heavy. "Maybe not today," he said, and he put it back.

Pip remembered his mom’s advice: "Share and taste things slowly." So Pip asked his friends if they wanted to try a small piece of the apple. Benny took a bite and said, "This is nice and juicy!" Pip felt happy because he’d shared his snack.

From that day on, Pip always tried new things and shared with his friends. And every time he did, he felt happy and full of joy!
