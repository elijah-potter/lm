The abandoned plan was to launch a renewable energy initiative in a remote village, focusing on solar-powered microgrids. Key components included:

- **Phase 1:** Solar panel installation and battery storage (15 kW capacity)
- **Phase 2:** Grid integration with local grid operator (LGO) approval for interconnection
- **Phase 3:** Community training on energy management and maintenance
- **Phase 4:** Pilot testing over 6 months to assess efficiency and feasibility

**Reasons for Abandonment:**
1. **Funding Shortfall** – Donor grants (€2M) were delayed due to political instability, reducing available funds to €1.2M.
2. **Regulatory Hurdles** – LGO approval for grid interconnection took 18 months, exceeding the project timeline.
3. **Local Resistance** – Villagers opposed the plan due to concerns about land use and perceived displacement from traditional livelihoods.
4. **Economic Shift** – A new coal plant (20 MW) opened nearby, reducing demand for decentralized energy solutions.
5. **Corporate Acquisition** – Parent company reprioritized investments toward urban infrastructure, halting funding.

**Current Status:** Revised focus on small-scale biomass projects in urban areas, with no further plans for the village initiative.
