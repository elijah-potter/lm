A vast, echoing observatory chamber where the air hums with the residual energy of a thousand defunct telescopes, their lenses polished to a mirror finish but now covered in dust. The ceiling is a fractured, translucent dome of reinforced glass, stretching infinitely, allowing only the dim glow of a single distant star to seep in through a narrow slit. The walls are lined with abandoned consoles, their keyboards etched with faded keypads and wiring still tangled in old circuits. The floor is a mosaic of shattered tiles, some still bearing faint, ghostly holograms of constellations that flickered briefly before the system failed. The silence is absolute, yet the weight of unseen presence presses down—like the memory of a thousand visitors who once stood here, leaving only the silence behind.
