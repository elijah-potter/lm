Once upon a time, in a big, cozy house with a garden full of flowers, there lived a little bunny named Pip. Pip loved to play outside, especially when the sun was shining bright and warm.

One sunny morning, Pip heard a tiny *squeak* from his garden. It was a little mouse named Mimi. "Hello, Pip! I lost my favorite acorn!" Mimi said, looking around.

Pip smiled. "Don't worry, Mimi! I'll help you find it." So, Pip hopped over to Mimi’s burrow and searched carefully. They found the acorn under a big, red rose!

Mimi beamed. "Thank you, Pip! Now I can share it with my family." Pip grinned. "That’s what friends do!"

From that day on, Pip and Mimi became the best friends in the garden. They played together, shared their snacks, and made the garden even happier.

And that’s the end of their story!
