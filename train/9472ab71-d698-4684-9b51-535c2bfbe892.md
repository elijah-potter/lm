Emerging urban sprawl near a defunct industrial zone, where repurposed warehouses and modular prefab units form a grid of narrow streets. Solar-paneled rooftops and vertical gardens line the perimeter, reflecting a blend of DIY community-driven architecture and corporate green initiatives. Residents gather in shared plazas beneath LED-lit canopies, discussing local governance via encrypted digital platforms. Waterfront promenades with floating solar farms and tidal energy hubs mark the transition between the old and new. The area’s identity is still forming—artisanal workshops alongside high-tech labs, a mix of organic farming collectives and vertical farms, and a visible tension between preservationist activists and developers advocating for "sustainable growth."
