In a dimly lit alley, a flickering neon sign cast jagged shadows. The air smelled of damp concrete and stale coffee. A lone figure—clad in a patched-up leather jacket—picked up a crumpled note from a trash can. The words were scrawled in jagged ink:

*"Meet me at the abandoned factory. Midnight. Bring the key."*

The figure’s fingers trembled as they unfolded the note. The factory gates groaned open behind them, the rusted hinges whispering like old secrets. Inside, the air was thick with dust and the hum of forgotten machinery. At the heart of the building, a single light bulb pulsed weakly, casting long, wavering beams. The figure approached a rusted door, their breath fogging in the cold.

A key turned smoothly in the lock. The door creaked open, revealing a dimly lit hallway. A single step forward, and the figure found themselves in a chamber where the walls pulsed with bioluminescent veins of light. At the center stood a figure wrapped in tattered robes, their face obscured by a mask of fused metal and glass.

*"One minute,"* the figure whispered. *"Then you’ll know."*

The door sealed shut behind them. The neon sign flickered out.
