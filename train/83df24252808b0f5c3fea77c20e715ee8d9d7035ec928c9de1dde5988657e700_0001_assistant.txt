Joe loved to play baseball with friends in his neighborhood, and his mom usually let him play with other kids his age. One day, Joe’s friends came to the door Saturday to ask him to play. His mom said he could if the older kids weren’t around. If the older kids came to play, Joe was supposed to come back inside. 

The older kids did join the baseball game that day after a few innings. Joe didn’t want to leave his friends, so he ignored his mother's instructions and stayed outside. One of the older kids hit the ball really hard. It hit Joe’s hand, and it hurt him a lot. 

He ran inside, and his mom got him ice and some medicine and reminded him of what she said. He'd learned his lesson and promised to come inside the next time the older kids came to play baseball.
