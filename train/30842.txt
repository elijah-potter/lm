Articles (a, an) kit includes activities that help children to
practice the articles in a different and entertaining way.
◌ Vowels and Consonants - Consonants and vowels recognition practice
◌ Choose then glue - Cut and paste the pictures next to the proper article.
◌ Grammar Detectives - You can introduce the grammar rule, letting the students
guess the rule on their own.
◌ Read, choose, write and match worksheets
◌ 3 Sort the words worksheets - color, cut out and stick-
allow students to have fun playing, organising the word cards
◌ 4 Flip books - color, cut out and staple
◌ 2 Articles a/an Interactive Notebook kits - color, cut out and stick - Students will love decorating their notebooks
◌ Mat and His Cat - The students have to choose the appropriate article while reading the story.
◌ 4 clip cards - The children use a clothespin to pick the picture that begins with a vowel.
Then they flip the card over to see if they have chosen the correct answers. These self correcting
cards are highly engaging and motivating!
◌ 24 I have...Who has? cards - with instructions how to use them
