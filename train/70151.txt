A peerage of the Kingdom of England.
Letters patent issued on Mon 9th May 1689
To Frederick Schomberg as Hereditary peerage subsidiary to another created by the same letters patent.
Nature of Special Remainder
Peerages with rules of limited inheritance, for example: the 1st Baroness Abercromby, whose peerage was limited to male descendants of her late husband.
Note: SR to third son Charles Schomberg and hm; failing whom to second son Meinhardt Schomberg and hm; failing whom to hm
