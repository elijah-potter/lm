The motorcycle gang that rode into town seemed menacing at first. They had tattoos all over, piercings, and long beards. The gang called themselves The Red Eye Motorcycle Club. Some had been teen runaways; others were just simply people who enjoyed the company of others and the freedom of life on the road. 

The club members didn’t just ride around all day. They also had a clubhouse. There, they would play pool, drink, play cards, and blast music. The gang was closely watched by the local police, but they were doing nothing illegal. The townsfolk, too, often made false assumptions about the group, but the bikers simply liked to hang out and have a good time. 

The sheriff of the town was always harassing the Red Eye Motorcycle Club. He would pull them over for no reason. The club members were always polite to the sheriff, but they were starting to get tired of his harassment. On a rainy night, the sheriff's car broke down on the side of the road. The Red Eye Motorcycle Club was riding by when they saw the sheriff struggling. They pulled over and offered to help him. The sheriff was hesitant at first, but he eventually accepted their help.
