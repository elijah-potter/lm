**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, Ill. – August 22, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this year with a full slate of agricultural exhibits, livestock competitions, live entertainment, and family-friendly activities. This year’s fair promises a celebration of the region’s heritage and a vibrant showcase of community spirit. Visitors can anticipate a wide array of traditional fair attractions, including carnival rides, games, and an expanded vendor marketplace featuring local artisans and food vendors.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from Friday, August 30th, through Sunday, September 8th, 2024, at the Sangamon County Fairgrounds. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM on weekdays and 11:00 PM on weekends. Special events and demonstrations will be scheduled throughout the fair’s run, catering to a diverse range of interests. A complete schedule of events will be available on the fair’s website, SangamonCountyFair.org, closer to the event date.

Admission to the fair is $10 for adults, $5 for children ages 6-12, and free for children five and under. Parking is available at the fairgrounds for $10 per vehicle. Discounted admission packages and season passes are also available for purchase online at SangamonCountyFair.org.

**Headline Events:**

*   Livestock Auction – Saturday, September 7th
*   Demolition Derby – Friday, August 30th
*   Tractor Pull Competition – Sunday, September 1st
*   Blue Ribbon Baking Competition – Wednesday, September 4th
*   Grandstand Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters – Saturday, September 8th
*   4-H Showcase – Daily
