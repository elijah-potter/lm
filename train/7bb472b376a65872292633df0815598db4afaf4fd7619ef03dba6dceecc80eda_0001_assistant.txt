President Kennedy states that the United States cannot be the world leader if it does not lead the space race.  The President speaks about the United States being first in the industrial revolutions and the first to develop nuclear power. He vows that Americans will see space governed by freedom and peace, not hostilities, and filled with knowledge and understanding, not weapons of mass destruction. To achieve these vows, the American people must unite and work together to become the leaders of the space race.
