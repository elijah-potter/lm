In a land not too far away from home, there is a special dragon named Mildred who guards the most precious object on Earth. What is it, you may ask? The Golden Egg of Catalina. You see, the Golden Egg of Catalina is filled with solid gold dug in the mines of Eriza down in the depths of the Earth. Only special miners can make it there, and a group of them decided to make this special egg and hide it from the rest of the miners. 

One day, this group of miners decided to find the dragon of Urelo to protect the egg at all costs because of its worth. You see, Mildred is a friendly dragon, but when a person tells her what to do, she has to follow the directions no matter what. A miner convinced Mildred to protect the egg until the end of time for the next generation. 

Decade after decade, Mildred has protected the egg with no issue until someone found out about the Golden Egg of Catalina. His name was Marcus, and his heart was full of greed. He wanted the egg all to himself to pay for a big mansion in the hills, but the town he came from was very poor. His mom, father, and sister still live there but barely have enough money to eat daily.

Marcus set out on his mission to find the egg for his greedy ways. Day after day, he searched and searched until one day, he found a cave. Large oak trees, a flowing river, and large prints with claws in the mud surrounded the cave. As soon as Marcus noticed the footprints, Mildred stepped out of her cave. 

Marcus drew out his sword immediately and threatened Mildred. He knew she was guarding the egg he so desperately wanted. “You cannot have this egg. I see right through your heart, and you have nothing but greed”, Mildred yelled. Mildred can see true intentions in anyone but also has the power to read minds. She let go of the most fiery breath and sent Marcus running, never to return.
