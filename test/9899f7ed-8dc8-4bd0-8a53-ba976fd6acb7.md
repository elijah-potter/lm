**BREAKING:** Mayor Elias Carter announces emergency rezoning in **New Haven Heights** following unexpected seismic activity detected at the old coal power plant site. Officials confirm a 2.3 magnitude tremor, though no immediate injuries reported. Authorities urge residents to remain cautious around the rezoned area as structural integrity assessments continue. Temporary evacuation shelters will be operational at City Hall and the new community center. The city’s Department of Environmental Safety will conduct a 72-hour inspection before reopening the facility to the public. Mayor Carter emphasized, *"This is a precautionary measure to ensure long-term safety for our residents."*
