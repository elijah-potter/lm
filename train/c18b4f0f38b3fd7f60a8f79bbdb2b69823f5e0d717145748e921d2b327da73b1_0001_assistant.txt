Nathan walked down the street feeling the fabric on his head. He hoped no one was suspicious of what he was planning. He grinned. He was excited. He could imagine the softness of the fabric and plastic of the brim. He rounded a corner into an alley and looked behind him. No one was watching. He took the hat off his head and held it lovingly in his hands before taking a bite out of it. He chewed the now wet fabric and swallowed it. He took another bite, and another bite, and so on until the hat was no more. He left the alley and continued walking, his stomach grumbling in disagreement with what had just entered it, but his mind was satisfied at another delicious hat consumed.
