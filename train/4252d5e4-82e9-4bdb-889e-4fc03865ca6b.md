A tiny silver key glints under a fluorescent desk lamp, its polished surface catching the light. It rests on the crumpled edge of a notebook, its edges worn from years of use. A sharp intake of breath makes me glance up—**Lena**, her fingers tracing the paper, her gaze flicking to the key before she hesitantly slides her palm forward. The key doesn’t move. She exhales, frustration barely contained, and hesitantly nudges it toward me with the tip of her pencil. My fingers brush it just before my thumb catches it, the moment frozen mid-air. The notebook thumps onto the floor as she steps back, a mix of relief and suspicion etched in her expression. The key slips between my fingers, now mine.
