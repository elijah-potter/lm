NASA's Artemis: We are going back to the moon!
Location: Earth Sciences and Map Library (50 McCone)
Dates: Fall Semester 2019
Curated by: Erica Newcome
NASA has set a new goal to get man, and for the first time a woman, back on the moon by 2024. During the last moon explorations astronauts only explored a portion of the moon's surface. NASA now wants to explore all of the moon's surface and will send scientists first to the lunar South Pole which is unexplored. To learn more about the Artemis Program click here. This exhibit highlights books, maps, globes, videos, and websites all about the moon. Come by the Earth Sciences and Map Library today to see the exhibit!
