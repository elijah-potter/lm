California is a popular state for weddings. You're in the right place for sunshine, especially in the mid and southern parts of the state. Here are some options for hosting your wedding at a winery that is not too far from the beach. Your guests will be able to hit the beach the day before or after your wedding and partake in delicious wine both during the reception and their overall stay in the area. 

* Paradise Ridge Winery is located in Santa Rosa. It is a place many choose as a wedding venue due to its picturesque quality and extensive selection of services/options offered for the wedding party. The beach is only a 30-40 minute drive, so it's a great location for your guests. 
*Anvil Vineyard and Ranch is a sprawling green, hilly area that has a 22-acre lake. This will make for fantastic pictures and a very memorable experience. Your guests will enjoy the gorgeous scenery, taste local wines, and be close enough to the beach to have a fun day in the sun. 
* The grounds of JUSTIN Vineyards & Winery are elegant, and the European architecture is luxurious.  This is a fairy tale venue that has a little bit of everything: elegance, local wine, spectacular views, and is only 30 minutes from the beach.  Perfect for what you're looking for for your guests.

Your wedding will surely be fantastic, and your guests will remember it as the years go on. If you go with one of the choices above, they will have it all: sun, wine, and beach access!
