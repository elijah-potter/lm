Beads of sweat ran down Frank's face as he watched the clock tick by. He knew he was running out of time but there was nothing he could do. He was stuck in traffic. His heart beat faster as he considered the consequences of running out of time. If he didn't get to where he was going within the next hour, very bad things would happen. He pushed his palm down on his horn to no effect. Finally, traffic started crawling forward. Not fast enough. Frank was terrified now. He briefly toyed with the idea of ditching the car and making a run for it but his common sense told him he'd never make it in time. He just couldn't afford to let the clock run out though. As he sat and stewed, he kept running the same question in his mind over and over again. Wondering how he got himself into that situation.  Wondering if he'd make it home in time before his lasagna burned in the oven.
