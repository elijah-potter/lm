The tension in the room was thick enough to cut with a knife. "You *always* say this?" Maria snapped, her fingers digging into her palm as she clenched her jaw. Across the table, Daniel’s voice was steady, but his eyes burned with the same frustration. "Because you *never* listen. You just repeat what I say like a broken record."

A chair scraped against the floor as Daniel leaned forward. "I’m telling you, the data shows it’s a *strategic* move—not a mistake. If we don’t pivot now, we’re done." Maria’s laugh was bitter. "You mean the same move that cost us half the budget last quarter?" She shoved her phone across the table, the screen glowing with a single open tab: a leaked memo from the CFO.

Daniel’s face darkened. "You’re using this to *discredit* me? After all I’ve done?" His voice cracked. "You’ve been hiding these reports in your desk drawer for weeks."

Silence. The air between them crackled like static. Outside, rain hammered against the skylight, but neither of them moved. Maria’s hands trembled as she reached for her bag, pulling out her second phone. The screen lit up with a single text: *"Meet me at the café. Now."*
