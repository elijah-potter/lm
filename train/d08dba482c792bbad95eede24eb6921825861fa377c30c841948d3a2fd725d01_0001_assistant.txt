The English Mastiff is the world's largest dog breed. According to The Guinness World Book of Records, in 1981 there was a  Mastiff named Zorba that reached 142.7 kg, or 314.6 lbs.
On average, the lifespan of a small dog is 12-15 years. The life expectancy of a large dog in normally 7-10 years. For giant breeds such as the Mastiff or St Bernard, the lifespan is only 5-8 years. Larger dogs grow very quickly. Scientists believe large dogs die young due to this accelerated growth in addition to an increase in free radical activity.
