Afghanistan can be classified as a unitary presidential Islamic republic. Afghanistan’s stability has had issues in more recent years, such as periods of civil and foreign conflict. Afghanistan has seen years of instability through civil wars, and foreign interference from Britain, the Soviet Union, and the United States. During the mid to late 1990s, the Taliban seized control over most of the country. The Taliban enforced a very strict version of Sharia law. In 2001, the United States invaded Afghanistan, with one of the goals being the removal of the Taliban from power. Later on, an interim administration was created to rebuild and attempt to unify the country. It is important to keep in mind that Afghanistan is a post-conflict country that is still experiencing struggle, and fragmentation.
