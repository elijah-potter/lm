Edmund, the jellyfish, loved to dance, especially since he was lucky to have so many tentacles that could move to the rhythm of each song he heard. Whenever he would listen to different songs with his jellyfish friends, he couldn't help but feel the urge to bust a move.

One day, he was swimming with two of his jellyfish friends when he heard the song Under The Sea from the Disney movie The Little Mermaid playing from a waterproof speaker system that a human had accidentally dropped on the ocean floor. He loved the song instantly because it mentioned some of his underwater friends, including sturgeon, rays, carp, and trout. He started dancing to it, moving his tentacles in waves, and instantly knew Under The Sea was his new favorite song.
