The wind outside howled like a question left unanswered. I adjusted my gloves, fingers trembling—not from cold, but from the weight of what I hadn’t decided yet. The map in my hands was blurred with uncertainty: one path led to the old mill, where the water still ran slow, where the villagers whispered of things they shouldn’t have seen. The other? A forest that had swallowed a child’s laughter years ago. I didn’t know which side to choose, though I *knew* which one my feet dragged toward.

The first step was always the hardest.
