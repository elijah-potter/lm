Lily the little bunny had a big, fluffy tail that wiggled when she hopped. One morning, she woke up and saw her favorite toy, a bright red ball, sitting on her blanket. "Oh, how fun!" she thought and picked it up.

Outside, Lily heard a tiny *thump* and looked down. There, under her basket, was a tiny egg. "Mmm, maybe I found an egg," she giggled. She carefully rolled it to her cozy nest with her leaves and grass.

As the day went on, Lily felt something squishy under her paw. She opened her tiny paws and saw a tiny bunny inside the egg! "Ohhh, a baby bunny!" Lily cheered. She nuzzled her new friend and named her Baby.

Baby was very tiny and wiggly. She loved nibbling on Lily’s carrots and playing peekaboo with her fluffy tail. Every day, Lily would watch Baby grow bigger and stronger.

One sunny afternoon, Baby had grown so much that she could hop around like a real bunny! "Time to go exploring!" Baby said. Lily smiled and let her go. "I’ll be back soon," she promised.

Baby hopped outside, met a friend named Max the mouse, and played a game of tag. They chased each other all around the meadow until Max giggled and said, "You’re so fast!"

Lily watched from her blanket, happy to see her little friend having fun. She knew Baby would always be her best friend, just like she was Lily’s.

As the moon rose that night, Lily curled up with Baby, snuggled in her nest. "Goodnight, Baby," she whispered. "I love you!" And with that, both bunnies drifted off to sleep, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures.
