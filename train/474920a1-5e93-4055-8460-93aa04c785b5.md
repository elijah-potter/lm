The **Echoing Cave of Whispers**, a hidden fissure in the Blackthorn Peaks, pulses with a slow, rhythmic hum—like distant breathing. Walls glow faintly with bioluminescent moss, shifting hues of violet and gold when disturbed. The air is thick with static, each breath carried on mist that lingers just long enough to dissolve into the silence before vanishing. At its center stands the **Stone Oracle**, a jagged monolith carved with spiraling glyphs that shift when no one looks. The ground beneath shifts subtly, forming hollows that cradle whispers—some human, some ancient, all looping back as if answered from within. Shadows move at the edges of perception, neither solid nor air, as if they’re waiting to be called. The cave doesn’t just *hear*—it *resonates*, each sound vibrating through the stone, leaving faint, ghostly afterimages on the damp walls.
