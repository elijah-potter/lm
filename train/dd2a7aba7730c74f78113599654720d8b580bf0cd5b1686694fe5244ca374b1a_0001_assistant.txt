"Who would be asking me out, though?" Riku asked, leaning back in his seat with a sigh.

"I'm not sure, man. Maybe it's a kohai from your club? The first years always look up to us like that," Ishimura said.

Riku groaned and leaned forward on his desk, glancing over the love letter he'd received in his shoe box once again. "Man, what a drag that would be."

"Why? In fact, why are you so down about this? Shouldn't you be happy that someone wants to confess to you?" Ishikawa asked, taking his seat and turning back to look at Riku sulk.

"It's not that simple. I mean, it certainly feels nice, of course. But, like...I don't want some random girl asking me out. I'd rather it be someone I have a real connection with, you know? If it's just someone who wants to date me because I'm their senpai or because they think I'm cool or something...I dunno. I don't think there's any substance to that. I'd probably turn them down in that case."

Ishikawa thought about this for a bit. "Must be tough being popular, huh," he finally said sarcastically. 

"You and I both know I'm not popular," Riku said and sighed again. "I understand how girls feel when random guys ask them out, now. They have it a lot more difficult than you'd think."

"Wise words from Riku, volume number two," Ishikawa rattled off, and Riku swatted at him, telling him to shut up. The teacher came into the classroom, and the students shuffled to their seats.

"Well, all you can do is go behind the school when we get out and see who it is. Try not to think too hard about it, yeah? I'll be rooting for you, regardless," Ishikawa whispered as the teacher began class.
