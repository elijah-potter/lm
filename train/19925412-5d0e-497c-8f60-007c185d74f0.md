Unspoken pact: *"We all agree to keep the silence—
the unspoken rule,
the silent nod,
the shared understanding of the unspoken truth.
No words spoken, but trust held tight like a vow.
The unspoken binds us—
a contract in the hollows of our eyes,
a promise written in the unheard words,
a bond that grows stronger in the absence of sound.
The unspoken is the silent agreement,
the contract unspoken,
the truce between the tongue and the heart.
We do not need to say it—
we already know.
The unspoken is the unbreakable chain,
the unspoken truth,
the unspoken love,
the unspoken faith.
We stand together in the space between,
where words are not needed.
The unspoken is the language of the soul,
the unspoken is the unspoken."
