The hormones involved in the ovulation phase, which is after the proliferative phase and is always 14 days before menses, include the following:

* 17-beta-estradiol (levels are at a high, at least 200 picograms per milliliter of plasma, then fall at the end of ovulation)
* FSH (17-beta-estradiol triggers this at a level of at least 200 picograms per milliliter of plasma)
* LH (17-beta-estradiol triggers this at a level of at least 200 picograms per milliliter of plasma)
* The high levels of FSH and LH that occur in reaction to critical levels of 17-beta-estradiol is referred to as an "LH surge,"
