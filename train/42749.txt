What is proper shipping name?
Proper shipping name is the standard technical name to describe the hazard properties and the composition of dangerous goods. They will be used to label dangerous goods. They also need to be included in Dangerous Goods Declaration and the section 14 of Safety Data Sheets.
How do proper shipping names appear in the HazMat table?
PSN. Shippers may use Proper Shipping Names (PSN) that end in the abbreviation “n.o.s.”—short for “not otherwise specified”— to identify a hazardous material that is not specifically listed by name in the Hazmat Table at 49 CFR 172.101.

What information must be on shipping papers?
Information on the shipping papers must include:
- The identification number, identified in the Hazardous Materials Table.
- The proper shipping name, identified in the Hazardous Materials Table.
- The hazard class.
- The packing group, identified in Roman numerals, when required.
- The total quantity of hazardous materials.
Which packing group indicates the greatest danger?
Packing group I
The packing group indicates the degree of danger of a product or substance. Packing group I indicates great danger, packing group II indicates moderate danger and packing group III indicates minor danger.
