Dear (sister’s name),

How have you been faring? I hope you’ve been well. I hope this letter meets you well. You know sister’s day is soon coming or have you forgotten about it? This year’s outing will be so special because you’d be there. Remember how we window-shopped last year and didn’t buy a single item from the store? We went on a hike together and took a yoga class, where I almost broke my bones. Sister’s day with you is always fun.

This year promises to be more interesting. We could whip up some DIY face masks, visit the spa, or do both. You mustn’t miss our outing on sister’s day for any reason. There’s no better way to spend your sister’s day than with me, your sister. I love you.
