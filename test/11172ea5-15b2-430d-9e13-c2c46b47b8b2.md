Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big tree in the backyard, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had soft, fluffy brown fur and bright, sparkly eyes. Every morning, Benny would hop outside to play and explore.

One sunny afternoon, Benny noticed something shiny near the tree trunk. It was a tiny red apple that had rolled there. Benny’s nose twitched with curiosity. He carefully picked it up and took a tiny bite. The apple was sweet and crunchy, and Benny’s tummy did a happy little flip.

Just then, Benny’s best friend, a blue bird named Bella, flew down and landed on the branch above. "Hey Benny!" she chirped. "Did you find a treasure?" Benny nodded. "It’s a red apple!" Bella clapped her wings. "I heard stories about apples that make you grow bigger!"

Benny’s ears perked up. "Really?" he wondered. He and Bella shared the apple, and as they ate, Benny noticed something strange. His paws got a little bigger, and his tail wiggled more than usual.

Soon, Benny felt a little stronger. He could hop higher and run faster! "I think I’m growing up!" Benny said excitedly. Bella giggled. "You’re getting bigger, but you’re still Benny—just bigger!"

From that day on, Benny and Bella played even more adventures together, and Benny enjoyed being a big bunny who could explore the world even more. They learned that growing up could mean new adventures, but being kind and friendly was what mattered most.

As the sun set, Benny curled up in his nest, happy and content, knowing that even though he was growing, he would always have his best friend by his side.
