RO refers to Reverse Osmosis, which is a process where pure water is produced by sending impure water through a semi-permeable membrane. In this purification system, the impure particles get caught in the membrane when the water passes through. This way, the system produces clean and healthy water which is drinkable. It has been used for both domestic and commercial uses because of its various advantages. Listed below are some of them.
Of course, alternative purification systems are also effective. But most of them use cliché methods such as ultraviolet and boiling. While these methods are efficient, they are only capable of bacteria. They fail to remove certain other important pollutants. On the other hand, a RO purification system get rids of bacteria as well as inorganic pollutants such as chemicals and metals. Thus, it is able to offer the user 100% pure water.

As mentioned above, RO water has much less contaminants. The main contaminator commonly found in tap water is Lead. One can find heavy concentration of Lead in pipes that are old and in areas with underdeveloped infrastructure.
