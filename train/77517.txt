valence bond theory
Valence bond theory runs into an apparent difficulty with CH 4. The valence-shell electron configuration of carbon is 2 s 22 p x 12 p y 1, which suggests that it can form only two bonds to hydrogen atoms, in which case carbon would have a valence of 2.
