Melissa Murray and Jessica Valenti are talking about some recent decisions and changes regarding a medicine called mifepristone and the availability of abortion. They mention different rulings from judges in different parts of the country, which are causing confusion and uncertainty about whether people can get the care they need. This is making doctors and pharmacists worried too.

The Court said it was giving the power back to the people to decide. However, Jessica believes that the real goal has always been to ban abortion in every state.

They question if this is a backlash against women's progress and an attempt to control and limit our choices.

Jessica believes that a big part of this is about punishing women for simply existing and making their own decisions. Abortion medication has made it easier and more private for women to have abortions, and some people don't want that. They want to make it difficult and unsafe, which is unfair and hypocritical.
