The heat was blistering as we approached the big box inside where the other odd, large, hairless marmosets lived. I looked around at the perplexing contents of the box, and the peculiar looks of the residents as one of the larger creatures carried me inside. I was quite happy with the fresh banana that they had given me to munch on, and as I enjoyed this midday snack, I noticed that one of the creatures was staring at me with mouth agape. As luck would have it, there were plenty of wonderful things for me to climb on in and around the giant box, and the others did not seem to mind picking up the peels of the bananas once I was finished with them. I did, however, find it quite entertaining to watch the expression of shock on the face of the creature who had trouble closing its mouth every time it inadvertently wandered into my corner of the box.
