Alright, folks, listen up.

A year and some change ago, The CW canceled Batwoman, after three well-loved seasons. As we all know, seasons two and three had a new Batwoman - a character named Ryan Wilder - played by Javicia Leslie, and people loved her! Since then, she's been back to play Ryan in some of the final episodes of The Flash, but get this: she wants to do more!

We caught an interview with Javicia on ComicBook.com, where they were talking to her about her new movie, Double Life, and she said she wanted to do more stunts, of all things. She said, quote, "I think that Ryan's fighting sequences are really great," end-quote. She went on to mention that it was all to do with how Ryan has no superpowers so she really has to get *in there* for fight scenes.

Now, she also said, quote, "I would love to explore more Gotham. Instead of putting her somewhere else, what else can we do in Gotham?" End-quote. That's what we've got to figure out. Javicia Leslie grew up getting to know Gotham and wants to play in that world.

So, people, what are we going to do about this?
