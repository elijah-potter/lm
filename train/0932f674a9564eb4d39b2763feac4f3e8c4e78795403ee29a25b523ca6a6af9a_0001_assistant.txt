Here are a few options for slogans for a child's lemonade stand:

- "Sip into a rainbow of flavors!": This slogan is playful and emphasizes the many flavors offered, 

- "Lemonade for every mood!": This slogan highlights the different flavors to suit different preferences. 

- "Taste the sunshine in every sip!": This slogan emphasizes the bright and cheerful nature of lemonade. 

- "Sip, smile, and repeat!": This slogan is fun and playful and is an encouragement to come back for more.
