The "Gray Area" in the abandoned district of **Black Hollow**, near the old railway bridge over the **Dry River**. The bridge's timbers show signs of deliberate damage—some planks split, others bent back—yet no clear pattern suggests sabotage or neglect. The local legend claims it’s a threshold between two worlds: some say it’s a portal, others that it’s just a bridge that doesn’t quite *work*. The law here is a ghost: no one enforces it, but no one *allows* it either. The few who venture there report conflicting warnings—one guide says "don’t cross at night," another insists "the river changes course at dusk." The only rule that’s *always* enforced is this: if you find a coin in your pocket there, you’ll lose it. No one knows why.
