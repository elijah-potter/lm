I know many of my readers are interested in model-building, which is awesome! Whether you're scratch building, 3D printing, or kitbashing, you'll almost always need to paint your model. But how do you get your textures to really pop? After all, you've spent time creating them; you want to show them off, right? Acrylic washes are one great way to enhance your textures and provide a contrast that shows your model in the best light. Using a wash also ties together the colors and surfaces of your model. 

So what is an acrylic wash? It's simply watered-down acrylic paint. Here are some tips:

1. In most cases, your wash should be a dark color, like black, brown, or blue. The point is to enhance the darker areas of your work.
2. You'll want to dilute your paint down quite a bit with water to the point that it flows freely. You're basically just making colored water, only slightly thicker.
3. You can vary how thick your wash is depending on the look you're going for.
4. Brush the wash liberally over your entire model. You may want to avoid or mask off any clear or translucent areas. 
5. With a paper towel, wipe most of the wash off. Leave it to dry on its own if you want a super grungy look.

That's it! You can do the wash after you've painted the model with other colors to tie the look together. You can even do a wash more than once! The light areas of your model can be brought out with a bit of dry brushing, which I'll cover next time! Thanks for reading and have fun crafting!
