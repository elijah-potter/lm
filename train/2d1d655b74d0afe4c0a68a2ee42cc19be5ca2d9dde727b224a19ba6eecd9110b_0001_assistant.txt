Ruby was backstage with the other children waiting patiently. It was her first-ever dance recital, and they were just moments away from taking the stage. She was feeling the types of feelings that she had never felt before. It was a strange mix of excitement, adrenaline, and nervousness. Both butterflies and anxious feet were fluttering around her. 

Finally, the teacher leaned down with a smile and wave. "OK children, it's time. Go to your spots and have fun!" Ruby hurried to her spot in the center of the stage. The warmth of the bright lights shined down on her. It warmed her hair and made it tricky to see out into the crowd. She squinted and focused and saw rows and rows of happy parents and other kids. It was so cool to be up in front of them all! 

The classical music began - it was the song they had practiced time and time again for the past two months. The familiar refrain soothed Ruby's nerves and made it easy for her to retreat into the motions they had gone over. She twisted, twirled, and moved her arms in perfect time. The other children also moved in perfect choreography. It was beautiful to watch, and the kids were clearly having so much fun performing. 

After a few fun minutes, the girls all stopped at their final marks. The crowd lept from their chairs in wild applause and cheers. Ruby felt a glow inside her, so excited and feeling the rush of emotions from the successful routine. All the work had paid off. She was proud of herself and her friends and loved the adulation from the audience. When she came off the stage her parents were waiting with flowers and big hugs. "Mommy? Daddy?" Ruby inquired, "When can I get on stage again?"
