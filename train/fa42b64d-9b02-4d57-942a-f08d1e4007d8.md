In the dim glow of a flickering lantern, shadows stretched long across the cobblestones, where the last light of an autumn dusk painted everything in hues of amber and rust. A lone figure crouched behind a weathered wooden crate, fingers tracing the grain as they counted the missing pieces—each one a silent scream in the hollowed-out chest of the chest itself. The air smelled of damp earth and something metallic, the scent of old blood clotting beneath the scent of damp wool. A rusted gate groaned ominously behind them, its hinges whispering secrets no one wanted to hear. The figure’s breath hitched as a distant, rhythmic *clack-clack-clack* echoed through the tunnels, like a metronome marking time for something far older than the bones beneath the ground.
