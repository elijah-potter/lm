The Sun and the Moon have always been best friends ever since they began existing. All day and all night, the two would play, chasing each other across the blanket of the sky. 

“Moon,” said the Sun one day, “Let’s both play a game of hide-and-seek! You play seeker, and I’ll hide from you.”

Moon agreed and the Sun found a nice place to hide away from the Moon. The Moon always found her when they played, and she wanted to win this time. So, she hid in a corner of the universe, and she waited eagerly for the Moon to look for her. For days and days, the Sun waited for the Moon. She grew bored and eventually left her hiding spot, only to find the Moon with the Earth. Upset, the Sun stopped talking to the Moon. Many days passed before the Moon went to try and talk to the Sun.

“Oh, Sun,” the Moon said, “Why have you stopped talking to me?”

“Because when we were playing hide-and-seek, you didn’t even try to find me; you were busy playing with the Earth.”

“But Sun,” said the Moon, “I was helping the Earth. I went to look for you when the Earth asked me for help. I didn’t mean to make you upset; I’m sorry.”

The Sun accepted the Moon’s apology, and soon they were playing again, this time letting Earth play with them.
