Anger flails and pounds in my head, wishing it was that
petulant child of a boss with his ultimatums 
gleefully weaving good luck goodbye good riddance words

Despair drowns anger as I step outside, box of
sad best mom, employee, friend mugs sagging heavily
bittersweet defeat in cardboard

Determination straightens my spine as
hope calls out, a ping on my phone, a 
joyful reminder that I am not lost
