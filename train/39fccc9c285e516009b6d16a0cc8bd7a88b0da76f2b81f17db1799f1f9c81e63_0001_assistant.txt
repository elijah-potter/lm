Pine Tree Dreams Come True

When I was little, my dreams were huge. As I felt my roots take hold in the soil and my branches sprouting, I knew that I would tower over all of the other trees in the forest. Yes, I had a long way to go. With thin bark and skinny, twig-like branches, I willed my roots to seek out more and more moisture to help me get taller.

But, as time went on, I knew the other trees were laughing at me. They were already bathing in the sun and I could feel the warmth emanating off their canopy of leaves while I shivered. The mighty oaks never wavered in the strong winds as I did. Their plump green leaves softly swayed while my thin needles quaked. I knew I needed more than mere hope to keep me alive. How could I possibly compete against these Titans?

And then, the night of the storm came. Ferocious, the wind howled like one of the forest animals. Thunder snarled and at some point, lightning set the forest ablaze. Now the heat was tremendous. But, as I came to realize at dawn, it hadn’t been the forest set afire at all. It had been one of the mighty oaks beside me. Black as tar down the center, the oak had been split in two. When the sun reached high in the sky, I felt its warm embrace for the first time. At last, I was truly beginning to grow.
