My Love, you are the rose in full bloom,  
With gentle petals that softly exhume  
A fragrance that evokes my heart's desire,  
A burning flame that will never expire. 

Your kindness and love fill me whole,  
And in your arms, I am in control.  
Your laughter is sweet, your smile divine,  
Oh, my Love, how grateful I am to call you mine! 

Your intelligence sparks a fire in my soul,  
Your wit is a light that will never grow old.  
The power of your love, it is like the sun,  
Warming and radiant, never to be undone. 

I long to hold you, to kiss your lips,  
To wrap you up in my loving grips.  
To wake up every morning to your sweet face,  
And spend each day getting lost in your radiant grace. 

My Love, listen to my heart's true call,  
To love you, cherish you, and give you my all.  
I desire to be by your side until the end of time,  
To grow old with you, my partner, my friend, my divine. 

So, let us take this journey of life and love,  
Together we will navigate the waters unknown.  
As unyielding as the power of the ocean,  
Our bond will remain strong, our love a devotion. 

My Love, will you marry me?  
Together, forever, our souls will be free.
