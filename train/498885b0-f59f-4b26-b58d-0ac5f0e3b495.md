## Product Requirements Document: "Context" - Mobile Knowledge Synthesis App

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers are increasingly overwhelmed by disparate information sources, leading to fragmented understanding, duplicated effort, and delayed decision-making.

**Vision:** To empower knowledge workers to synthesize information from diverse sources into cohesive, actionable insights, directly within a mobile workflow.

---

### User Personas

**1. Amelia Chen, Senior Marketing Manager:**

*   **Goals:** Quickly understand market trends, competitor activities, and campaign performance data; share synthesized insights with her team.
*   **Pain Points:** Spends excessive time switching between email, spreadsheets, research reports, and presentation software; struggles to connect disparate data points; often feels she lacks a complete picture before making decisions.

**2. David Ramirez, Research Scientist:**

*   **Goals:** Efficiently process and connect findings from academic papers, conference proceedings, and internal research reports; identify gaps in existing knowledge; contribute to collaborative research projects.
*   **Pain Points:** Faces a constant influx of research materials; finds it difficult to track the evolution of ideas across multiple documents; lacks a streamlined way to organize and annotate complex information.

**3. Eleanor Vance, Financial Analyst:**

*   **Goals:** Analyze company performance data, industry reports, and economic forecasts; identify key trends and potential risks; present concise, data-driven recommendations to senior management.
*   **Pain Points:** Data resides in various formats and locations, requiring manual consolidation; prone to errors when copying and pasting information; needs a way to quickly assess the credibility of sources.

---

### Requirements

**1. Source Integration:** The app must allow users to import content from a variety of sources, including web pages, PDFs, email attachments, and cloud storage services (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive).

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can successfully import content from at least three of the listed source types. Imported content is rendered faithfully, preserving formatting where possible.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 80% of users report successful content import within the first week of use.

**2. Contextual Note-Taking:** Users must be able to add notes and annotations directly within the app, linked to specific sections of imported content.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Notes can be added, edited, and deleted. Notes are clearly associated with the relevant content section. Users can search for notes by keyword.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 60% of users actively utilize the note-taking feature within the first two weeks.

**3. Relationship Mapping:** The app must provide a visual tool to create connections between different content sections and notes, enabling users to identify patterns and relationships.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can create, edit, and delete connections between content sections and notes. Connections are visually represented and easily navigable.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 40% of users create at least three connections within the first month.

**4. Synthesis Summarization:** The app must generate concise summaries of imported content and user-created connections, highlighting key insights and potential areas for further investigation.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Summaries accurately reflect the content and connections. Users can customize the level of detail in the summary.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 70% of users find the generated summaries helpful in understanding the content.

**5. Secure Sharing & Collaboration:** Users must be able to securely share synthesized knowledge packages with colleagues, enabling collaborative review and refinement.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Shared packages are accessible only to authorized users. Changes made by collaborators are tracked and visible.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 30% of users share knowledge packages with colleagues within the first month.



---

### Technical Considerations

*   **Platform:** Initial release on iOS and Android.
*   **Data Storage:** Secure cloud-based storage with robust encryption.
*   **Content Rendering:** Optimized for various document formats, including those with complex layouts.
*   **Scalability:** Architecture must support a growing user base and increasing data volume.
*   **Offline Access:** Limited offline access to downloaded content and notes.

### Risks

*   **Content Rendering Issues:** Difficulty accurately rendering complex document formats.
*   **Data Security Breaches:** Potential vulnerability to unauthorized access and data loss.
*   **User Adoption:** Users may be resistant to adopting a new knowledge management tool.
*   **Performance Degradation:** App performance may suffer with large knowledge packages.



### Non-Goals

*   Direct content creation (e.g., writing new documents within the app).
*   Integration with all possible cloud storage services (focus on the most popular).
*   Advanced data visualization beyond relationship mapping.
*   Real-time collaborative editing of content.
*   Automated content tagging or categorization.
