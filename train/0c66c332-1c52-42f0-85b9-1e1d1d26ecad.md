## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park – A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary**

The City of Oakhaven faces increasing challenges from intensifying rainfall events and rising summer temperatures. The Willow Creek area, particularly the currently underutilized space at the intersection of Elm Street and Oak Avenue, exemplifies this vulnerability. This proposal seeks $75,000 in municipal funding, matched by $25,000 in community contributions and in-kind services, to transform this neglected area into the Willow Creek Pocket Park – a climate-resilient community asset. This project will address stormwater runoff, mitigate the urban heat island effect, enhance biodiversity, and provide a welcoming green space for residents of all ages and abilities. The park's design prioritizes native, drought-tolerant plantings, permeable paving, and a rain garden to manage stormwater. We believe this project will serve as a replicable model for other vulnerable areas within Oakhaven, fostering community resilience and enhancing the quality of life for all residents.

**Project Plan & Timeline**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park project will be implemented across four distinct phases, each with clearly defined milestones, responsible partners, and deliverables.

**Phase 1: Design & Planning (Weeks 1-4) – Lead: Oakhaven Parks Department, Partner: Local Landscape Architect (Volunteer)**

*   **Milestone 1: Community Needs Assessment (Week 1):** Conduct surveys and host a public forum to gather input on park design preferences and desired features. *Deliverable:* Comprehensive report detailing community feedback.
*   **Milestone 2: Preliminary Design Completion (Week 2):** Develop three design options incorporating community feedback and adhering to stormwater management best practices. *Deliverable:* Illustrated design options with cost estimates.
*   **Milestone 3: Final Design Approval (Week 3):** Present final design to the Parks Department and City Planning Commission for approval. *Deliverable:* Approved park design plans.
*   **Milestone 4: Permitting & Approvals (Week 4):** Secure all necessary permits and approvals from relevant city departments. *Deliverable:* Permit documentation.

**Phase 2: Site Preparation & Stormwater Infrastructure (Weeks 5-8) – Lead: Oakhaven Public Works Department, Partner: Local Contractor (In-Kind)**

*   **Milestone 1: Site Clearing & Grading (Week 5):** Remove existing debris, level the site, and prepare for infrastructure installation. *Deliverable:* Cleared and graded site.
*   **Milestone 2: Rain Garden Excavation (Week 6):** Excavate and shape the rain garden according to design specifications. *Deliverable:* Excavated rain garden basin.
*   **Milestone 3: Permeable Paving Installation (Weeks 7-8):** Install permeable paving stones to reduce runoff and allow for groundwater infiltration. *Deliverable:* Installed permeable paving.

**Phase 3: Planting & Landscaping (Weeks 9-12) – Lead: Oakhaven Community Garden Association, Partner: Local Nursery (In-Kind)**

*   **Milestone 1: Soil Amendment & Preparation (Week 9):** Amend soil with compost and organic matter to promote healthy plant growth. *Deliverable:* Amended planting beds.
*   **Milestone 2: Native Plant Installation (Weeks 10-11):** Plant drought-tolerant native trees, shrubs, and wildflowers according to the approved design. *Deliverable:* Installed native plantings.
*   **Milestone 3: Mulching & Irrigation System Setup (Week 5):** Apply mulch to retain moisture and establish a simple, efficient irrigation system. *Deliverable:* Mulched planting beds and operational irrigation.

**Phase 4: Community Engagement & Park Furnishings (Weeks 13-16) – Lead: Willow Creek Neighborhood Association, Partner: Local Artist (Volunteer)**

*   **Milestone 1: Park Signage Installation (Week 6):** Install interpretive signage detailing the park’s purpose and ecological benefits. *Deliverable:* Installed signage.
*   **Milestone 2: Benches & Picnic Table Installation (Week 7):** Install benches and a picnic table to provide seating and gathering space. *Deliverable:* Installed park furnishings.
*   **Milestone 3: Grand Opening Celebration (Week 8):** Host a community celebration to officially open the Willow Creek Pocket Park. *Deliverable:* Successful community event.



**Budget**

The total project cost is $100,000.  We are requesting $75,000 in municipal funding, supplemented by $25,000 in community contributions. The budget breakdown is as follows:

*   **Permeable Paving Materials:** $30,000. This covers the cost of high-quality, locally sourced permeable paving stones and associated installation materials.
*   **Native Plants & Trees:** $18,000. This includes the purchase of a diverse selection of native trees, shrubs, and wildflowers from a local nursery, ensuring resilience and biodiversity.
*   **Soil Amendments & Mulch:** $5,000. This covers the cost of compost, organic matter, and wood chip mulch to improve soil health and moisture retention.
*   **Rain Garden Liner & Gravel:** $7,000.  Essential for proper rain garden functionality and filtration.
*   **Park Furnishings (Benches, Picnic Table):** $10,000. Durable, weather-resistant furnishings to encourage community use.
*   **Signage & Interpretive Panels:** $5,000.  Educational signage highlighting the park’s ecological benefits and design features.
*   **Project Management & Administration:** $5,000. Covers administrative costs associated with project oversight and reporting.
*   **Contingency Fund:** $5,000.  A critical safety net to address unforeseen expenses or material price fluctuations.

The community contribution of $25,000 is comprised of in-kind services from the Local Landscape Architect (design work), Local Contractor (site preparation), and Local Nursery (plant materials), as well as volunteer labor from the Willow Creek Neighborhood Association and Oakhaven Community Garden Association. A detailed accounting of in-kind contributions will be provided to the City of Oakhaven upon project completion.

**Community Engagement, Equity Impacts, Risk Mitigation, and Post-Grant Sustainability**

Community engagement is central to this project. We’ve incorporated feedback from residents through surveys and a public forum. The design prioritizes accessibility for individuals with disabilities, ensuring equitable access to the park’s benefits. The selection of native plants supports local biodiversity and reduces the need for chemical fertilizers and pesticides, benefiting the entire community.

Potential risks include unforeseen site conditions (e.g., buried utilities) and inclement weather delays.  The contingency fund will address unexpected costs. A detailed site assessment will be conducted prior to commencement to identify potential hazards.  The project schedule includes buffer time to account for weather-related delays.

Post-grant sustainability will be ensured through a partnership with the Willow Creek Neighborhood Association, who will assume responsibility for ongoing maintenance and programming. The Oakhaven Parks Department will provide technical support and guidance. A volunteer stewardship program will be established to encourage community involvement in park upkeep.

By supporting this project, the City of Oakhaven can demonstrate its commitment to addressing climate change, promoting equitable access to green space, and fostering a vibrant, thriving neighborhood.
