The host politely asked, *"Would you like some tea before we begin?"* while pouring water into his cup. The guest, caught off-guard, replied, *"Oh, I’m good, really."* Their eyes darted to the kitchen, where the kettle had already boiled. The host froze mid-pour, clutching the teapot like it might vanish if he didn’t hold onto it. *"Sorry, I—"* he stammered, *"I just forgot to set the timer."* The guest, now fully aware of the lie, sipped his cold water in silent amusement. The host, mortified, tried to salvage the evening by adding, *"I’ll make sure to check the time next time."* The guest smirked. *"You don’t need to apologize for being human."*
