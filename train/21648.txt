What Are Capital Goods?
Capital goods are items that are used to produce other goods or services. They are also known as durable goods, because they typically last for a long period of time. Capital goods can be used in the production of both consumer and investment goods.
Common examples of capital goods include manufacturing equipment, machinery, tools, and vehicles. These items are used to produce other items such as cars, computers, and clothing. Capital goods also include buildings and other structures that are used in the production process.
Investment goods are a type of capital good that is used to produce other capital goods. Examples of investment goods include factory buildings, machine tools, and vehicles used in transportation.
The term “capital goods” can also refer to the industries that produce these items. The capital goods sector includes industries such as manufacturing, construction, and mining. This sector is an important part of the economy, because it produces the items that are needed to produce other goods and services.
