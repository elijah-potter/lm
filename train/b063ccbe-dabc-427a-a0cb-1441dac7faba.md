Lily the bunny had a big, fluffy tail that wiggled when she hopped. One sunny morning, she heard a tiny voice, "Help! My berry is falling!"

Lily looked down and saw a red berry stuck in a tall, leafy bush. She hopped up and used her soft paws to carefully pull it down. The berry was juicy and sweet!

Lily shared the berry with her friends—Milo the mouse, Benny the bird, and Pip the squirrel. They all chewed their fill and giggled with happiness.

That evening, as the sky turned pink, Lily snuggled under her cozy burrow, feeling warm and full. She dreamed of more adventures and sweet berries to share tomorrow!
