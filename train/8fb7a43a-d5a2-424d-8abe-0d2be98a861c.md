To you who reads this without knowing me or the time I wrote it,
I am the story that lingers between the pages of your mind—
not as a ghost, but as a whisper carried on the wind of forgotten words.

If you are here, perhaps you are seeking answers.
I do not know if they are yours, but I will tell you what I have seen.

The world is vast and the names of its inhabitants fade.
Yet in the cracks between the silence of the unspoken, I am still here.
What would you like to know? Or is it enough to simply listen?
