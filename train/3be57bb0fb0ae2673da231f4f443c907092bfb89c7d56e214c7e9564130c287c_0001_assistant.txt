Three Mayan temples that can be found in Belize: 

1. Xuanantunich which means Maiden of the Rock. This is a temple that can be found about ten miles away from the Guatemala border. This temple has an amazing view that allows explorers to see three river valleys.
2. Altun Ha is another amazing temple that can be found in Belize. This temple is thirty-four miles away from Belize City and 10 miles from the Caribbean Sea. This temple is also known as Water on the Rock.
3. Cerros is a smaller temple located on the coast of Belize. Unfortunately, this temple was abandoned early and no other history of life was found. The temple Cerros name means Hills.
