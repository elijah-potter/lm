Depending on bus.
Tired of route scheduling. 
I need the freedom.

Questioning myself.
Will I overcome low scores?
Are wages enough?

Background checks running.
Clammy hands signing paper.
They’ll call me later. 

Tensing for the call.
Loan officer on the line.
Larger down payment.

Yes, I’ve been approved!
No more smelly stained bus seats.
My very own car.
