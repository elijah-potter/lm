A cavernous, obsidian-lined chamber where the walls pulse faintly with eerie bioluminescent veins of liquid mercury. Clockwork gears hum in the distance, their ticking slows to a crawl, then accelerates erratically. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, shifting mid-motion like living things. The air hums with a low, dissonant frequency—time itself seems to warp between the floorboards and ceiling, where seconds stretch into minutes, or vanish entirely. A single, flickering lantern casts a distorted reflection in the water below, rippling as if moved by unseen hands. The clock at the center of the chamber ticks backward, its hands frozen mid-turn.
