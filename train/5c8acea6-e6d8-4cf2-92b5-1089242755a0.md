The crowd moved like shadows in a hall,
their eyes fixed on the stage before,
but only I see the cracks in the floor—
the way the spotlight lingers just past
their shoulders, where the dust motes
twirl in silence, unseen.

I notice how the child at the back
clutches a toy that once was bright,
now dulled by the weight of time,
while the grown-ups trade smiles like coins
they’ll never spend on anything but air.

I watch the musician’s hands—
not just the notes they play, but the breath
between them, the way the rhythm
folds into the cracks of memory
before the next note begins again.

Others see the performance.
I see the silence between the notes.
