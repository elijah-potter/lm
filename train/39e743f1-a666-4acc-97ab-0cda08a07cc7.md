**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, Ill. – August 21, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this year with a diverse array of agricultural exhibits, live entertainment, and family-friendly activities, celebrating the region’s rich heritage and vibrant community spirit. This year's fair promises to be bigger and better than ever, showcasing the talents of local farmers, artisans, and performers, alongside thrilling rides and classic fair food. Visitors can anticipate livestock competitions, demonstrations of traditional crafts, and opportunities to engage with local businesses.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from Friday, August 30th, through Sunday, September 8th, 2024. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with closing times varying by day (9:00 PM Friday-Saturday, 6:00 PM Sunday). Special events are scheduled throughout the fair’s run, including a tractor pull, demolition derby, and nightly concerts. Detailed schedules are available at SangamonCountyFair.org.

Admission is $12 for adults, $8 for children ages 6-12, and free for children five and under. Season passes are available for $40 and provide unlimited entry throughout the fair’s duration. Parking is $10 per vehicle. Advance ticket purchases are encouraged and can be completed online at SangamonCountyFair.org.

**Headline Events:**

*   Friday, August 30th: Opening Ceremony & Demolition Derby
*   Saturday, August 24th: Professional Tractor Pull
*   Wednesday, August 28th: Regional Livestock Show & Auction
*   Friday, August 23rd: Country Music Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters
*   Saturday, August 24th: Artisan’s Showcase & Craft Demonstrations
*   Sunday, September 1st: Petting Zoo & Family Fun Day
