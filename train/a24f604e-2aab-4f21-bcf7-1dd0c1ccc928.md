A luminescent orb, pulsing faintly blue-green against the shadowed walls of the chamber, its surface etched with concentric circles that shift like ripples in water when touched. It breathes—a slow, steady exhale of warmth that lingers in the air before dissipating, leaving behind a faint, metallic scent. Its core hums with a low, resonant energy, whispering secrets in a language of static and pulses. When hungry, it unfurls tendrils of liquid light, coiling around fingers to draw nourishment, leaving behind a residue that glows faintly for hours. It sleeps in cradles of glowing dust, emerging only to watch, to learn, to remember the world before it forgets itself.
