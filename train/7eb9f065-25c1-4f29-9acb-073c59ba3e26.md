Rich textures of velvet and leather warm against my palms. The scent of damp earth and crushed pine needles lingers, mingling with the faint, musky perfume of aged wood. Vibrations hum through the floorboards—a low, steady pulse like distant thunder, occasionally interrupted by the rhythmic sigh of a distant fountain. Shadows dance and shift, thick and heavy, as if alive, clinging to corners where no light touches. The air is thick with the scent of old parchment, half-smoked incense, and something metallic, like fresh rain on copper. The weight of a thousand whispers settles in my chest.
