1. Greet the class and talk briefly about the end of the year. (10 mins)
2. Give out memory books and have time for each to read through them. (20 mins)
3. Have each pick two memories to share with the group. (20 mins)
4. Create a memory canvas - give each a canvas and have paints and any other craft supplies you like available. Ask each student to draw some sort of symbol on their canvas that captures their year/feelings as they leave or just something they like, it can be abstract or literal. Then each person will move around the room and make that symbol on each person's canvas. By the end, each should have a canvas of art that represents their team. (30 mins)
5. End it with snacks and free time until the end of the day. (10 mins)
