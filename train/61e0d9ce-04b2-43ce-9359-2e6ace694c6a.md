Waiting in an empty lobby, the fluorescent hum of overhead lights flickers through the glass windows, casting a dull, shifting glow on the worn carpet. A single wooden chair stands in the corner, its surface marked by faded stains. A sign above the door reads **"PLEASE WAIT"** in bold, slightly peeling letters. Outside, distant footsteps echo down the hall, muffled by thick carpeting. The air is stale, thick with the scent of stale coffee and something faintly metallic. A clock on the wall ticks irregularly, its hands frozen in an endless loop of 11:57. A half-empty mug of lukewarm tea sits abandoned on a small table by the door. The room feels like it holds the weight of every unseen moment that has passed.
