In a world where taste isn’t a preference, but a sentence: the hands of fate decide whether your favorite food can be found around the corner, or across the world.

In Tastebud Lottery, an unlucky Abel must search far and wide for his favorite food: the elusive, discontinued Doritos Guacamole variant.

3,000 miles from home, the last unopened bag known in existence awaits the one who desires it—but it comes with a cost.

Abel must partake in a high-stakes trade if he wants to eat his favorite food again: one bag of Doritos Guacamole for three years of his life spent in servitude underneath the one who has what he wants.

Coming soon to a theater near you: Tastebud Lottery. Can Abel get this chip off his shoulder?
