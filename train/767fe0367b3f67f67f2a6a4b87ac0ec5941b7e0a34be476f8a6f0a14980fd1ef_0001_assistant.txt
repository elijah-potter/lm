It was a misty fall evening in the small town of Creepton and three teenage friends cycled through the deserted streets. Steve, the eldest and wisest of the friends, always rode at the front of the pack with Sally close behind. Sally was scared of most things, however being left alone was her biggest fear. Last in the pack was Jack. Jack had a vivid imagination and a strong sense of adventure which often got him into trouble. As the friends meandered through the hazy streets, the air grew even colder and something just felt off. "I think I might go home guys, but I need you to take me", whimpered Sally. Steve of course obliged but Jack had other ideas, "OK fine, but let's go a different route, it'll be more fun!" he said. Steve looked at Sally and sighed, "Well, one way or another, we'll get you home safely". The three young friends set off down a road they were unfamiliar with, the street sign was barely legible through all the cobwebs and moss. Jack paused to read the sign when he realized, it wasn't a street sign at all, it was a warning that read, "Do Not Enter". Jack decided not to share this information with the group, they were on an adventure after all. 

The group cautiously cycled down the unkempt road, it seemed as though nobody had been there in years. As the heavy mist cleared, a sense of foreboding filled the air and a sight lay ahead of them that gave even Jack goosebumps.  It was a house, looming tall in the night sky, with glowing windows that appeared to be alive as though they were the eyes of a monster. The three friends fearfully looked at each other, they knew the house was haunted.
