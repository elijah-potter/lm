

Ingredients:
• 3 eggs
• 1/4 teaspoon salt
• 1/4 teaspoon pepper
• 1 tablespoon butter
• 1/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
• 1/2 diced medium green pepper
• 1/4 cup diced ham

Instructions:
1. Whisk together the eggs, salt, and pepper in a small mixing bowl.
2. Heat a 10-inch nonstick skillet. Add the ham and peppers and cook until the ham is cooked and the peppers are tender.
3. Add the ham and peppers to the egg mixture and combine well. 
4. Heat the butter in the skillet. Once melted, immediately add the egg mixture to the skillet. Allow the mixture to cover the entire skillet.
5. Once the bottom of the omelet is solid, flip over the omelet.
6. Sprinkle the cheese on the omelet as the other side cooks.
7. When the omelet is thoroughly cooked on both sides, fold the omelet in half and slide it onto a plate.
