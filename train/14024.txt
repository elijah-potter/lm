Growing A Seed: Apr 8th – 12th
In Growing a seed camp children we will cover the following topics. Each child will take home a plant that they learnt to take care and grow it at home!
- Learn about plants and the life cycle of a plant.
- Different stages of growth in the life cycle of plants.
- What plants need to grow?
- How do we care about plants?
- Conduct an experiment to show that plants absorb carbon dioxide and release oxygen.
- Why are plants so important for our environment?
Ages : 3 years to 7 years.
