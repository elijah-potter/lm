Fluxus River Delta
A shifting archipelago of semi-submerged islands, their shorelines eroded by tides of sediment and time. The water flows like a living organism—ever-changing currents drag structures from the earth, depositing them elsewhere: weathered bridges become sea stacks, abandoned lighthouses drift into shallows, and the skeletal remains of drowned cities form temporary islands. The air hums with the sound of rustling debris, the whisper of wind carrying fragments of past lives. Some things return—always slightly altered—while others vanish entirely. The only "permanent" marker is the absence of permanence itself.
