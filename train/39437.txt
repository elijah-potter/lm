Resources to support research for ENGL 068
Research Assignment Details
The topic of the research paper will originate from 7 Generations: A Plains Cree Saga by David Alexander Robertson. Focus will be on one of the four moments of First Peoples history written about in the book.
- How do the effects of residential schooling continue today through intergenerational traumas?
- What is the "'60's Scoop", who did it affect, and how has it impacted Aboriginal cultures today?
- Explain the history of smallpox in relation to Indigenous Peoples of North America.
- Describe why most Canadians don't know the history of their own country and how education is the number one path to reconciliation.
