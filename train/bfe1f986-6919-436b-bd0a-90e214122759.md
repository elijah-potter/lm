## Lost in the Bookstore (Seattle, WA)

The rain was relentless that afternoon, a steady drumming against the awning of Elliott Bay Book Company. I was browsing the poetry section, drawn to the worn spines and the quiet hum of turning pages, when you appeared. You were searching for something near the travel writing, your brow furrowed in concentration, and a cascade of auburn hair spilled over the collar of a well-worn, navy blue sweater. There was a particular kindness in the way you handled the books, a reverence that resonated with me. We didn't speak, but our eyes met briefly over a collection of Rilke, and a warmth bloomed unexpectedly in my chest. The scent of aged paper and brewing coffee hung in the air, a comforting, familiar embrace. I hesitated, wanting to say something, anything, but the moment dissolved as quickly as it began. I left shortly thereafter, carrying the ghost of your presence with me. 

If you recall a woman with auburn hair and a navy sweater browsing the travel section on Saturday afternoon, please reply with your favorite poet.
