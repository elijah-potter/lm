President Lyndon Johnson proclaimed National Hispanic Heritage Week in 1969 to recognize and honor the contributions of Americans who trace their roots to Spain, Mexico, the countries of Central and South America and the Caribbean. In 1989, the U.S. Congress extended this recognition into an annual month-long celebration, from September 15 through October 15. I think the Caller Times is missing a great "Teaching Moment" by not incorporating serious historical articles specifically chronicling the contributions of Mexican-Americans to the building of South Texas, and more generally publicizing the important impact of Latinos on the history of our state and country. Yes, the Caller Times has run articles on current events marking Hispanic Heritage Month, but heritage denotes history and the Caller Times usually ignores Mexican Americans in its "history" pieces by Mr. Murphy Givens. I found it hugely ironic that on the day honoring a civil rights pioneer, Dr. Hector P. Garcia, the historical piece on the op-ed page is about Corpus Christi's "contributions" to the Confederacy. Why not run a scholarly article detailing Dr. Garcia's life?Maria Calderon GarzaSan Diego, Texas
