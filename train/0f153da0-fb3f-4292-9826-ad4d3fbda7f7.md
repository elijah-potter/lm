A janitor in a forgotten corner of the office noticed a single, sun-bleached hair clinging to the wall—an overlooked detail that led to a deeper investigation. The discarded comb, half-melted with wax, held a date stamped on its handle: the day the team’s lead developer vanished. The hair, his alone, was still damp with the scent of his cologne, a clue buried beneath layers of dust and bureaucratic indifference.
