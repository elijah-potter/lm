A quiet afternoon in a dimly lit café, the hum of distant chatter blending with the clink of cups. **Daniel** hesitated before speaking. The lie had been comfortable—"I don’t know how to fix it," he’d said, avoiding the truth that he’d spent the past week secretly researching the problem. Now, he slid a crumpled receipt toward **Lena**, who had asked for help with her car.

*"I looked it up,"* he admitted, voice cracking. *"It’s a faulty alternator. You’ll need a new one."* Lena’s hands trembled as she accepted the receipt. The weight of honesty settled over them like a second skin—raw, but unbreakable.

Outside, rain began to fall, but inside, the truth became the only thing that fit.
