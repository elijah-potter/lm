The decision to adopt open-source software for internal systems was framed as straightforward: cost savings and transparency. However, the complexity emerged in balancing efficiency with scalability, as legacy integrations required migration without disrupting workflows. The ambiguity of long-term maintenance costs and potential hidden dependencies led to second-guessing, despite the apparent simplicity of the choice. The tension between immediate benefits and future risks created a paradox—one that demanded iterative adjustments rather than a definitive answer.
