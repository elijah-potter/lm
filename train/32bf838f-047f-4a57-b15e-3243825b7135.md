A technician misread the schematics for a new solar panel installation, assuming a 12V system required a specific inverter configuration. The customer, however, had a 24V setup. After installing the wrong setup, the system failed to convert power efficiently, leading to no output during testing. Upon reviewing the specs, the technician realized the inverter needed adjustment for 24V input. After correct setup, the system produced consistent 12V output, resolving the misunderstanding and ensuring proper energy conversion.
