The iterative refinement of a **real-time data aggregation pipeline** for IoT sensor nodes in a high-latency environment reveals cascading challenges. Early stages focus on basic data collection and initial filtering, but as the volume scales (e.g., 50K+ nodes), packet loss spikes from network congestion, requiring adaptive backpressure algorithms. Latency thresholds shift dynamically—initial 200ms targets become 150ms in bursts—while cross-node dependency graphs expand, exposing cascading failure risks. The system’s error budget degrades exponentially with each refinement iteration, necessitating hybrid heuristic/ML models to approximate optimal decisions under uncertainty. Eventually, the feedback loop collapses into a deterministic failure mode, revealing the fundamental trade-off between throughput and reliability in the absence of centralized coordination.
