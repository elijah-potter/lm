Once upon a time in a cozy green house with a big red door, there lived a little bunny named Pip who loved to hop, hop, hop! Every morning, Pip would wake up and stretch his tiny paws, then he’d wiggle his ears and say, *"Today is my favorite day!"*

Outside, Pip saw his best friend, Max the mouse, digging in the garden. *"Hello, Max!"* Pip called. Max popped his head out of his hole and said, *"Hi, Pip! Did you see that shiny leaf over there?"*

Pip sniffed the leaf and giggled. *"It’s so soft and sparkly! Let’s play hide-and-seek!"* They raced around the field, giggling as they hopped and darted. Suddenly, Pip tripped over a big, round rock. *"Oof! I think I fell!"*

Max scurried to help. *"Don’t worry, Pip! Let’s build a fort with all the sticks we find!"* Together, they stacked sticks and leaves, making a snug little house. Inside, they sang songs and shared snacks—crunchy carrots and sweet berries. Pip and Max stayed cozy until their bellies were full, then they curled up together under the stars, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures.

As the sun painted the sky pink, Pip whispered, *"Best day ever!"* Max smiled and agreed, *"Yes! Let’s do it again tomorrow!"* And with happy hearts, they drifted off to sleep, ready for another magical day.
