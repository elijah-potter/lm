Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a tiny bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the garden. One sunny morning, Benny spotted a shimmering, golden flower that no one else had ever seen before. "Oh, Benny!" Benny giggled, "it looks like a treasure!"

Benny carefully plucked the flower, and it turned out to be soft and sweet, just like his favorite candy. He ate a little and felt his tummy grow happy. Suddenly, Benny heard a tiny squeak. A little mouse named Mia was hiding under a bush. "Mia, help me!" Benny whispered. Mia peeked out and said, "I found a lost button too!"

The two friends worked together, picking more flowers and collecting more buttons. They filled a basket with treasures. "Let’s share them with everyone!" Mia said. Benny agreed. They gave the golden flower to the garden, the buttons to the garden animals, and the sweet treats to all their friends.

The garden was filled with laughter and joy. Benny and Mia knew that sharing was the best way to make the world even happier and more colorful. And from that day on, whenever they saw a treasure, they remembered to share it with their friends, making everyone feel special and loved.
