## Product Requirements Document: "Context" - Knowledge Consolidation & Retrieval

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently juggle information from disparate sources—documents, emails, meeting notes, web pages—leading to fragmented understanding, duplicated effort, and difficulty recalling relevant information when needed.

**Vision:** To create a seamless digital workspace where knowledge workers can effortlessly capture, organize, and retrieve contextual information, fostering deeper understanding and increased productivity.

---

**User Personas:**

1. **Eleanor Vance, Senior Marketing Manager:** Eleanor oversees a team of five and is responsible for crafting and executing marketing campaigns. Her goals are to ensure campaign alignment with overall business objectives, maintain a clear understanding of competitor activities, and efficiently delegate tasks. Her pain points include sifting through endless email threads for key decisions, struggling to recall specific data points from past presentations, and feeling overwhelmed by the volume of information she must process daily.

2. **Daniel Reyes, Research Scientist:** Daniel conducts independent research and collaborates with a small team. His goals are to meticulously document experimental procedures, easily access and synthesize data from multiple sources, and ensure reproducibility of his work. His pain points include manually organizing research papers and lab notes, losing track of minor details crucial for later analysis, and experiencing frustration when attempting to reconstruct past thought processes.

3. **Sofia Moreau, Freelance Consultant:** Sofia provides strategic advice to various clients and must quickly grasp complex situations and synthesize information from diverse sources. Her goals are to efficiently onboard to new projects, maintain a comprehensive record of client interactions, and present compelling arguments based on well-supported evidence. Her pain points include recreating client contexts from scattered emails and documents, struggling to recall specific recommendations made in past engagements, and feeling the pressure of demonstrating expertise on short notice.

---

**Requirements:**

1. **Context Capture:** Users must be able to quickly capture information from web pages, documents (PDF, DOCX), and email threads with a single click/tap.
    * **Acceptance Criteria:** Captured content is saved as a distinct "Context" entry, preserving original formatting and links where applicable. The user is prompted to add a brief description or tag.
    * **Success Metric:** 75% of users capture at least one Context entry within the first week of use.

2. **Semantic Tagging & Search:** Users must be able to tag Context entries with keywords and phrases, enabling efficient searching and filtering. The system should offer intelligent suggestions for tags based on content analysis.
    * **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can add, edit, and delete tags. Search results prioritize entries with relevant tags. Tag suggestions are accurate and helpful in 80% of cases.
    * **Success Metric:** Average search time is reduced by 50% compared to manual searching.

3. **Context Linking & Relationships:** Users must be able to create explicit links between Context entries, representing relationships between ideas, projects, or data points.
    * **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can visually connect Context entries to demonstrate relationships.  A graph view displays linked entries.  Links are bidirectional.
    * **Success Metric:** 30% of users create at least one link between Context entries within the first month of use.

4. **Summarization & Key Insight Extraction:** The application should automatically generate concise summaries of captured content and highlight key insights.
    * **Acceptance Criteria:** Summaries accurately reflect the content of the original source. Users can edit and refine automatically generated summaries.  The system identifies and highlights key sentences and phrases.
    * **Success Metric:** 60% of users utilize the automatic summarization feature within the first two weeks of use.

5. **Cross-Device Synchronization:** Context entries must be accessible and editable across multiple devices (desktop, iOS, Android) with real-time synchronization.
    * **Acceptance Criteria:** Changes made on one device are reflected on all other connected devices within seconds. Offline access is supported with automatic synchronization upon reconnection.
    * **Success Metric:** 95% of users report satisfaction with cross-device synchronization functionality.



---

**Technical Considerations:**

* **Cloud Infrastructure:** Secure and scalable cloud infrastructure (AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure) is required for data storage and synchronization.
* **Content Extraction:** Robust content extraction techniques are needed to accurately preserve formatting and links from various document types.
* **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** NLP models will be essential for semantic tagging, summarization, and key insight extraction.
* **Security:** Implement rigorous security measures to protect user data and ensure privacy.
* **Platform Native Development:** Prioritize native development for iOS and Android to optimize performance and user experience.



**Risks:**

* **Content Extraction Accuracy:** Inaccurate content extraction could lead to user frustration and data loss.
* **NLP Model Performance:**  Suboptimal NLP model performance could result in irrelevant tag suggestions and inaccurate summaries.
* **Scalability Challenges:** The system must be able to handle a large volume of data and users without performance degradation.
* **User Adoption:**  Convincing users to adopt a new knowledge management tool can be challenging.



**Non-Goals:**

* Real-time collaboration features (e.g., shared workspaces).
* Integration with third-party task management systems.
* Support for proprietary document formats.
* Advanced data visualization capabilities.




---

**Launch Checklist (First Six Actions):**

1. **Core Functionality Testing:** Rigorous testing of content capture, tagging, and search features by a representative group of beta users.
2. **Security Audit:** Independent security audit to identify and address potential vulnerabilities.
3. **Onboarding Tutorial:** Creation of a concise and engaging onboarding tutorial to guide new users.
4. **Customer Support Channel:** Establishment of a dedicated customer support channel (email, FAQ) to address user inquiries.
5. **Monitoring and Analytics Dashboard:** Implementation of a monitoring and analytics dashboard to track key performance indicators (KPIs).
6. **Public Relations & Initial Content:** Preparation of a press release and creation of initial content (blog posts, social media updates) to announce the launch.
