• How your family will feel: Consider how living in a place with paranormal activity may affect your quality of life. If you have young children, they may feel uncomfortable and scared at the thought of living in a haunted house.
• Plans for the home: Consider your future plans for the home. You may get a good deal on the sale price, but you may not love living in a haunted house. However, if you plan to use it as a bed and breakfast or tourist attraction, the haunting may be good for business. 
• Resale value: Consider the potential resale value. Haunted houses require a very particular buyer, and that could take some time. In many cases, sellers have to accept lower offers to get their homes sold in a timely manner.
