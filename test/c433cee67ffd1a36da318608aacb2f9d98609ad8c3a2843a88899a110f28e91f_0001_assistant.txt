Between 2005 and 2015, the number of farms in Tokyo decreased by 14.5 percent and the average age of farmers increased by 3.3 years. Preserving Tokyo's remaining 1,250 small farms feels priceless to many, but there is an ongoing issue where many cities cannot afford to buy the land protected under the Productive Green Areas Law at market value, as legally required. This leads to the land being lost for agricultural use, and usually being sold for commercial development.
