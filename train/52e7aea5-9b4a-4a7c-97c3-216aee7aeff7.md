A leaked internal document from the Department of Energy suggests a classified study found evidence of anomalous energy readings at a remote research facility in New Mexico. The facility’s "Project Phoenix" was initially dismissed as equipment malfunctions, but recent drone footage captured unexplained luminous phenomena near the containment field. Local whistleblowers claim personnel observed what appeared to be a "pressure wave" disrupting seismic sensors, though no external seismic activity was recorded. The U.S. government has not officially acknowledged the findings but has increased security protocols around the site.
