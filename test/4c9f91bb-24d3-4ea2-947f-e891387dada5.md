Early reports indicate a surge in wildlife sightings—deer, bears, and even a rare black panther—wandering through normally deserted streets. Locals attribute the phenomena to a reported "magnetic anomaly" detected near the old mill, which authorities attribute to a malfunctioning experimental energy grid installed last month. The city council has declared a mandatory curfew starting tonight, with law enforcement urging residents to stay indoors due to heightened risks of nocturnal encounters.

Authorities warn against approaching any unknown creatures, citing a spate of minor incidents including a stray fox dragging a child’s toy from a yard and a misidentified "shadow creature" captured on surveillance footage near the old mill. The mayor, under pressure, has urged citizens to report any further sightings via the city’s official app. The town’s historic library, which has remained open despite the curfew, is now serving as a community hub for updates.

The town’s only other notable event is the sudden closure of the Blackthorn Brewing Co. after customers reported hearing faint, rhythmic drumming in the basement—later confirmed to be an experimental drone system linked to the energy grid. Brewmasters claim the sound is "unsettling" but refuse to elaborate. The brew’s ingredients, now sold as "limited edition," carry a warning: *"Do not taste if you hear the drums."*

No further details are available, but the town’s website is currently down.
