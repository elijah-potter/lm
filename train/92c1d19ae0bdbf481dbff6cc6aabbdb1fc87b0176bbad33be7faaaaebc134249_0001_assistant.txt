There was an old lady who owned a cat and a rat that hated each other. The rat always tried to steal food items from the store or the kitchen, while the cat always tried to eat the cat. One day, the old lady left the two animals at home alone. The rat as usual ran into the store room to nibble some food and the cat chased the rat. The rat ran over the food sacs and accidentally hit a keg of oil. The oil spilled out and caused the ground to become extremely slippery. The cat tried to keep chasing the rat, but he kept slipping and falling. Watching this caused the rat to laugh for a while before he decided to run back to his tiny hole. But the sole of the rat’s feet was covered with oil, so he also slipped and fell. This caused the cat to laugh. Both animals stopped chasing each other and began to laugh.

The old woman returned home and saw the two animals laughing. She was quite perplexed as to how the two animals weren’t fighting each other as usual. The old lady sent the two animals out of the store room so she could clean the mess. The two animals struggled to leave the store room, with lots of slipping and falling. The two animals laughed at each other as they left the room, and from that day, the two animals became good friends.
