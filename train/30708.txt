Students can practice their multiplication facts on the Smartboard in this exciting game. It is important for students to learn their multiplication facts. Remember, multiplication is repeated addition. An example of 2 x 4 is 2 groups of 4 (4 + 4), which equals 8!
Directions: Select easy or fast on the Smartboard. After, select which facts you would like to practice. When the game starts, answer the multiplication questions by hitting the mole! Play
