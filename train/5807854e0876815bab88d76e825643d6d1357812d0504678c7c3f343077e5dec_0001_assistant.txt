Joan Ruth Bader Ginsburg, also known as RBG, was born on March 15, 1933, and died on September 18, 2020. She was an American lawyer who served on the Supreme Court of the United States of America from 1993 until she died in 2020. She was nominated by Bill Clinton for the Supreme Court position, and she was the 2nd woman and 1st Jewish woman to ever sit on the Supreme Court. RBG authored majority opinions in many cases, such as United States v. Virginia in 1996, Olmstead v. L.C. in 1999, Friends of the Earth Inc. v. Laidlaw Environmental Services, Inc. in 2000, and City of Sherrill v. Oneida Indian Nation of New York in 2005.
