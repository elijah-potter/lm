Once upon a time in a bright, sunny garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white fur and big, round ears that twitched when he was excited.

One morning, Benny woke up and stretched his tiny paws. "Today is a wonderful day!" he thought. He hopped outside to explore. The flowers swayed gently, and the butterflies danced in the air.

Benny saw a shiny red berry and picked it carefully. "Mmm, what a tasty treat!" he giggled. But then he noticed a tiny ant carrying a leaf. Benny wanted to help, so he hopped closer and gently placed the leaf on the ant’s path.

The ant smiled and said, "Thank you, little bunny!" Then Benny saw a squirrel playing with a nut. Benny waved and said, "Hi, squirrel! Want to share?"

The squirrel chattered, "Only if you share your berry!" Benny smiled and shared both the berry and his kindness. The squirrel gave Benny a tiny acorn to keep.

As the sun set, Benny felt happy. He had made new friends and shared his kindness. Benny curled up under the stars, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures. The garden was quiet, but Benny was full of joy and warmth.

And that was how Benny learned that being kind and sharing made the world a little brighter and friendlier for everyone.
