In the quiet village of Eldermere, where the river whispered secrets to the reeds, there lived an orphan named Elias. His father had vanished one stormy night, leaving only a journal behind—a relic of forgotten lore. Elias spent his days poring over its pages, uncovering cryptic clues about an ancient weapon said to lie buried beneath the old oak.

One evening, as Elias followed the journal’s directions, he found himself standing before a crumbling stone archway. Inside, the walls pulsed faintly with an eerie glow. At the center stood a rusted metal hinge, its surface etched with symbols that mirrored those in his father’s journal. The air hummed with energy, and the ground trembled slightly.

Elias reached out, his fingers brushing the hinge’s edge. A voice—soft yet commanding—filled the chamber: *"The weight of the past is now yours to wield."* Before he could react, the symbols on the hinge flared bright blue, and the world dissolved into swirling light.

When Elias awoke, he stood alone in the same village, but the journal was gone. The villagers whispered of a "hinge of forgotten power" and a boy who had touched something dangerous. As Elias turned to leave, he caught a glimpse of his reflection—his eyes were no longer his own. The hinge’s true form had taken hold.

He had not just found a weapon. He had found himself.
