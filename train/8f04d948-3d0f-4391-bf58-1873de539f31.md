The kitchen table trembled under the weight of crossed arms and clenched fists. *"You just said you’d never do this!"* Jake yelled, his voice cracking with frustration. Across the room, Sarah’s hands gripped the edge of the counter, her knuckles white. *"And I said I trusted you!"* she snapped, her voice trembling.

A sharp *crack* echoed as Sarah’s vase shattered against the floor. The glass scattered like confetti in a broken dance. Outside, the city hummed, indifferent. Jake’s phone buzzed—another missed call from his coworker. *"Just tell me how much longer I have to pretend."* His jaw tightened.

Then, the front door burst open. A scruffy kid in a hoodie stood there, his breath fogging in the cold air. "You two look like you need a break." He tossed a crumpled receipt onto the table. *"Gimme half, and we’ll split the rest."* The air smelled of stale coffee and regret.
