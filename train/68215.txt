HDPE resin stands for Hight Density Poli Etilen, widely used in daily life. This is also the material for the production of plastic bags or many other plastic items, which is the result of polymerization from high-density ethylene under low pressure, in addition to the combination of catalysts such as chromium or silloc catalyts.
HDPE is a type of plastic with good durability, resistant to natural corrosion such as water, wind, and acid rain,... to the erosion of solutions such as concentrated acids, alkalis, and salts,... Besides, the substance HDPE plastic is also resistant to ultraviolet rays from direct sunlight.
