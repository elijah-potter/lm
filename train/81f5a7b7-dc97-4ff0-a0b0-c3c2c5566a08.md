**The Clockmaker’s Legacy**

In the quiet village of Eldermere, old Master Kael kept his workshop hidden behind a curtain of ivy. His clocks were said to tick not with gears, but with whispers from the past. One day, a traveler arrived—either a thief or a scholar, depending on who told the story.

The traveler, named Lyra, sought the clock that held the fate of her lost love. Kael, wary of outsiders, demanded proof. Lyra, desperate, forged a letter from her father, claiming she had returned to claim her inheritance.

When the time came to strike the hour, the clock’s hands froze.

---
**Ending 1: The Shadow’s Choice**
The clock’s face split into two paths. The left panel showed Lyra’s father’s dying words: *"Steal what you want, but remember—time is a debt."* The right panel revealed Kael’s hidden ledger, listing debts owed to a forgotten god. Lyra chose the ledger, triggering a storm of blackened gears. The clock shattered, but the god’s voice echoed: *"You took more than gold."* Lyra found her love—but his body was dust, and the village’s clocks stopped forever.

---
**Ending 2: The Tide’s Compass**
The clock’s gears spun backward, revealing a map of Eldermere’s drowned streets. The right panel pulsed with light: *"The river remembers."* Lyra followed the map, emerging in a ruined dock where her love waited, bound by a chain of glass. Kael’s workshop had been submerged centuries ago, but the clock’s essence lingered in the water. Lyra broke the chain with a single tear. The river lapped at the shore as the clocks began to tick again—though they never stopped.
