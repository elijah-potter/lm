I was so excited to test out the new small Kindle.  I've been using the app for years on my Apple Itouch.  I liked that the screen was larger and I could use it outside. The setup was quick and easy I had no issues.  I took it on vacation with me and had no problem connecting to Wi-Fi or a hotspot to download novels it was awesome! This is the first time I didn't need tech support for a new device. I love my Kindle so much I would never replace it. No need to return this purchase and it was $20 less than its competitor. I'd recommend Kindle to anyone that loves reading books.
