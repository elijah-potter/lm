“Off to the garden I go to harvest my vegetables for the day.”, Marion said to her husband George. She gathered the tools and basket she needed to harvest all the hard work she planted in her garden this year. With one foot in front of the other, she was excited to see just how many vegetables there would be. But as soon as she stepped into the garden, she was utterly dismayed.

There were carrots pulled up from the garden, tomatoes that were squashed, lettuce that was shredded, and cucumbers that were smooshed as well. Marion was in tears as all of her hard work was ruined. However, the odd thing is that animals could not get to the garden! You see, a fence surrounded the garden to make sure animals could not get into it. There was even a cover above the garden to protect the vegetables from birds!

George came out to investigate what was going on and was completely baffled. They looked up and down for the culprit but could not find a single clue, except for one. The smallest human footprints. These were even smaller than a baby, which made no sense. That very same day, George put up a camera to see what was causing so much damage. 

Early the following day, George went to check the footage from the night before and was completely surprised. The impossible became possible when George saw one of their very own garden gnomes ruining the garden! He immediately woke up Marion and showed her the footage. Instead of being angry, they were amazed and decided that instead of getting rid of the gnomes, they would feed them every day instead!

From that day on, all the gnomes would come out to play because Marion and George accepted them for what they were. They were now one big happy family, and the gnomes never destroyed the garden again.
