The abandoned plan involved deploying a quantum annealing processor in a high-altitude stratospheric balloon for long-duration atmospheric data collection. **Primary failure reasons:**

1. **Technical Limitations**: The quantum annealer’s error rate exceeded expected thresholds for continuous data acquisition, leading to premature shutdowns.
2. **Resource Constraints**: Budget overruns on supporting infrastructure (e.g., power, thermal control) caused delays in launch readiness.
3. **Regulatory Hurdles**: FAA approvals were delayed by concerns over atmospheric data leakage risks and safety protocols.
4. **External Disruptions**: A pandemic-related supply chain freeze for critical components (e.g., pressure sensors, cooling units) halted assembly.
5. **Competitive Shift**: Alternative high-altitude platforms (e.g., CubeSats) emerged with lower cost and faster deployment, making the project economically unsustainable.

**Key Outcome**: The project was terminated in Phase 2, with assets redistributed to a lower-altitude atmospheric sensor network.
