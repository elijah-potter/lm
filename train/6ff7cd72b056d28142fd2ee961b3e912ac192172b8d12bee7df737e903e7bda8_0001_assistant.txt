Here is a list of five meal ideas for a picky adult using the specified criteria:

1. Baked chicken tenders with a side of mashed potatoes - Add a dipping sauce for the tenders or a topping for the mashed potatoes based on the eater's preferences.
2. Bacon mac and cheese bowl with apple slices on the side - This can be highly customized and different meats or spices can be added or omitted.
3. Chocolate chip pancakes with a side of scrambled eggs and bacon - Change up the chocolate chips for bananas or strawberries for a healthier meal.
4. Spaghetti and meatballs with a side of cheesy garlic bread - The meatballs can be substituted for chicken and alfredo sauce for those who don't care for meatballs.
5. A plain cheeseburger with a side of homemade french fries - Change things up with different kinds of hamburger buns or a dipping sauce for the fries.
