The engineer’s last-minute oversight left the prototype’s hydraulic seals cracked, spilling coolant onto the assembly line floor. Within minutes, the automated welders misaligned, their robotic arms snapping shut on misfired components. The factory’s emergency shutdown alert blared: *"Critical system failure detected. Evacuate all personnel."*

Downstairs, the foreman’s voice crackled over the PA: *"No one leave. We’re fixing this."* The foreman, a man whose hands had once held a thousand rivets, adjusted the pressure gauge—too low, the alarms screamed. He flipped a switch. The factory’s lights flickered like dying stars before plunging into darkness. A single fluorescent tube hummed from its cradle, casting a sickly green glow over the chaos.

Then, the door burst open. A woman in a leather jacket, her hair tied in a messy bun, stepped inside. She wasn’t from the factory. Her boots were scuffed, her gloves stained with oil and something darker. The foreman’s jaw dropped. *"You’re not supposed to be here."*

She pulled a toolbox from her backpack and tossed it onto the floor. *"I’m not here for the work. I’m here for the mistake."* Her voice was low, rough, like she’d been drinking whiskey since last Tuesday. The foreman’s pulse quickened. She reached into her pocket and pulled out a folded newspaper clipping—*the company’s latest quarterly report, dated three months ago*. The headline read: *"New York Times: ‘The Empire’s Secret’—How XYZ Industries’ Hidden R&D is Revolutionizing Precision Manufacturing."*

She unfolded it carefully. *"You built something. And you did it wrong. But not the way it’s supposed to be wrong."* The foreman’s mind raced. The factory’s core component—the one that could make them a fortune if fixed right—was half-assembled in the corner. If she saw it…

She walked toward the wreckage. The foreman followed, his breath shallow. *"What do you want?"*

*"To see what you left behind."* She turned back, her eyes sharp. *"And then?"*

The factory’s emergency lights came back on, one by one, like a slow wake-up. The foreman swallowed. *"Then what?"*
