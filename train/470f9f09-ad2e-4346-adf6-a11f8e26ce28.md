A consensus meeting in a failing project team led to a compromise on the latest feature scope. The core developers wanted to add advanced AI integration, but the marketing team insisted on simplifying the interface for broader adoption. Management settled for a hybrid model, combining basic AI features with a streamlined UI. While AI enthusiasts were disappointed by the limitations, marketing stakeholders were relieved by the user-friendly design. The product manager noted the compromise left "some parts unfinished" and "no one fully happy," but the delivery timeline was met. Post-launch reviews showed mixed feedback: AI users found the reduced capabilities underwhelming, while casual users praised the ease of use. The decision was justified by short-term success, but long-term dissatisfaction lingered.
