The Repertoire Books contain attractive compositions from the 17th century to the present. The pieces have been organized in progressive order and meet the requirements of many piano festivals, auditions and competitions. The books may also be used as sight reading material or as a supplement to other method books. Titles: Allemande (Beethoven) * Autumn Song (Kabalevsky) * Bourree (L. Mozart) * Etude in D Minor (Gasparini) * Gaillarde (Anon.) * Gypsy Dance (Haydn) * Landler (Haydn) * March (A.M.B. Notebook) * Mazurka in A Major (Traditional Polish Dance) * Minuet in F Major (Mozart) * Musette from "English Suite" (Bach) * Pageantry (Turk) * Pastorale (Burgmuller) * Polonaise (A.M.B. Notebook) * Prelude in B Minor (Morovsky) * Prelude in C Minor (Clementi) * Prelude in E-flat major (Heller) * Sarabande (Kuhnau) * Sonatina in Two Movements (Hook) * Swabian Dance (Kirnberger) * Trumpet Tune (Clarke).
