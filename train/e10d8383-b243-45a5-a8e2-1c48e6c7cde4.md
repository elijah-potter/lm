## Lost in the Bookstore (Seattle, WA)

I was browsing the poetry section at Elliott Bay Book Company on Saturday afternoon, attempting to find something to ease a persistent melancholy. You were near the window, engrossed in a volume of Rilke, sunlight catching the dust motes dancing around you. I noticed the way you traced the lines with your finger, a quiet reverence in your expression. We didn't speak, but I recall the gentle rustling of the book pages as you turned them, a surprisingly soothing counterpoint to the city's ambient hum. There was a subtle scent of old paper and leather, a comforting aroma that seemed to cling to you. I found myself lingering, hoping for a shared glance, a fleeting acknowledgment, but you remained absorbed in your reading. I left shortly thereafter, carrying with me a sense of quiet regret and a lingering image of your focused profile. If you remember a woman with auburn hair and a slightly oversized cardigan, please respond and confirm the title of the Rilke you were reading.
