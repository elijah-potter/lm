Two dogs sat in a park,
One was big and one was small.
The big dog barked and barked and barked,
But the little dog didn't bark at all.

The big dog wagged its tail with might,
Barking loud to show its might.
But the small dog, calm and bright,
Watched the world with curious sight.

As people passed by in a steady flow,
The big dog's barks continued to grow.
But the little dog, in a tranquil glow,
Savored the park's gentle ebb and flow.

The dogs walked home side by side,
Despite the difference in their size.
Their friendship was a source of pride,
So happy that they recognized.
