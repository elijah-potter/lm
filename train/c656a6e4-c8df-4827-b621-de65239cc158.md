A district in Neo-Tokyo's floating arcologies, the **Nexus Core Protocol Zone** (NCPZ), where digital governance replaces traditional laws. Citizens interact via neural-linked contracts, and disputes are resolved via blockchain arbitration. AI mediators enforce "dynamic ethics" based on real-time societal feedback loops. Public spaces are designed for gamified compliance—commuting is a "social obligation" tracked by ambient biometric sensors. Offenders face "adaptive penalties" (e.g., temporary role-playing in simulation zones to re-educate). The city’s "Rule Engine" updates laws in real-time via crowdsourced governance apps. Privacy is optional; data is monetized through "privacy credits" earned via voluntary transparency. The paradox: the more rules evolve, the less they feel like rules.
