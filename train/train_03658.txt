The Tampa Bay Bucs have 13 football players and 16 cheerleaders. If 10 football players and 4 cheerleaders quit, how many football players and cheerleaders are left?
There are 13 - 10 = <<13-10=3>>3 football players left.
There are 16 - 4 = <<16-4=12>>12 cheerleaders left.
In total there are 3 + 12 = <<3+12=15>>15 football players and cheerleaders left.
