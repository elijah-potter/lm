James was fed up with the potted plant.

"There's no breeze. You're evenly weighted. How are you still falling over?" James shook his head in anger at the plant. Suddenly, he realized he was talking to a plant and looked around sheepishly to make sure no one was watching. He slipped back inside after righting the plant for what must have been the tenth time that day and sat down on the couch to watch some TV. The plant was in the corner of his peripheral vision. If it fell again, well, James didn't know what he would do, but it would probably be bad.

For the next hour, the plant stayed upright; James was vigilant. Then, in a moment of weakness, he looked down at his phone to read a text. He heard a dull thunk and shot up from the couch.

He pointed an accusatory finger at the horizontal plant.

"You dare defy me! You dare!" Again, he realized that he was shouting at a plant and sighed as he sank back into the couch, defeated.
