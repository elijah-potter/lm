A quiet café at noon. The waitress, mid-sip of coffee, notices a crumpled business card on the floor near the trash can. She picks it up—*James Carter*—and sets it aside, not realizing its significance.

By evening, James returns. The card’s paper is brittle with age. He traces the faded ink with his thumb. The name, address, and phone number were once legible. The café’s sign flickers: *Closed for repairs*.

He pulls out his phone. The number rings once, twice—no answer. A text from an old acquaintance: *"You’re the guy who found my old card. Want to meet?"* He smiles, typing back: *"Always."*
