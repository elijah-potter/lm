Here are some escape rooms you can go to in the York, PA area:

1. Escape Games Live- With several themes to choose from, Escape Games Live offers reasonably priced fun with an incredible aesthetic atmosphere.

2. Escape Room Dilemma- A great place for hosting parties or work outings, Escape Room Dilemma is a great option if you want to have a fun night with a large group.

3. First National Escape- If you don't mind a short drive to Marietta, you might try this escape room that offers a unique historical experience as the experience is hosted in an old bank.
