Welcome, all, to Take a Guess! The game where you guess the answer, and if you get it right, win yourself some seriously cool prizes! 

Now, if you haven’t played Take a Guess, don’t worry; the rules are very easy to follow! Here’s what you need to know: 

1. Each of the 5 players is on their own team. 
2. When I ask a question, the first person to hit their buzzer gets to answer first. 
3. That contestant gets a chance to answer the question. If they get it right, they get the prize. 
4. If they answer incorrectly, I’ll ask the question again, and whichever of the remaining contestants buzzes first gets a try. 
5. Each question has a different prize. 
6. Whoever answers the most questions correctly receives the grand prize at the end of the game. 

Ready? Okay, let’s play Take a Guess!
