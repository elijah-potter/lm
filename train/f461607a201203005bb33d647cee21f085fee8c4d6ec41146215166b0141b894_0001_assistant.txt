Former employees of this TV show have raised concerns about a toxic work environment, and many say it starts at the top with executive producer Alex Duda. They claim that lower-level staffers are facing difficult conditions, including a divide between favored and non-favored employees. Some employees have experienced verbal abuse and anxiety, while others have suffered bullying and intimidation, resulting in negative impacts on their mental health. Additionally, reports suggest that HR has not effectively addressed the issues raised, with promotions given to producers accused of unprofessional behavior. HR’s inaction has worsened morale and created the perception that this behavior is not only tolerated but rewarded. Finally, one former employee also mentioned racially insensitive comments made by the executive producer.
