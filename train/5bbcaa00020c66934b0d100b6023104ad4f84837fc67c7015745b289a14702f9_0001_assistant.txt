Title: Th-Th-Th-That’s All, Folks: A Porky Pig Memoir

Synopsis: As one of the world’s most beloved cartoon characters, Porky Pig takes us on a journey through his rise to fame and the lessons he’s learned along the way, between closing your favorite shorts or shooting hoops on a basketball team.

The book's first section is called “The Early Years.”  It delves into Porky’s beginnings on “Looney Tunes.” and being a pig in the early days of cartoon show business. Porky shares priceless pieces of advice for any young farm animal wanting to make it big.

In “Stutterin’ Succotash,” Porky gets candid about his struggle with the stutter he’s had and how he learned to overcome it, changing it from an obstacle to a motivator for developing his own personal voice. 

For the book's final section, Porky talks about his favorite projects. Readers and fans alike will get special behind-the-scenes stories from Porky’s most iconic appearances and cameos.

All in all, this book is a must-have for any Porky Pig admirer. It’s not only a memoir but a manual and a reminder of the diversity that makes “Looney Tunes” and other projects truly special.
