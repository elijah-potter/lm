The **Driftwood Gathering Grounds** – a windswept, moss-covered plateau in the northern foothills of the Blackthorn Range, where the air hums with static and the ground trembles under unseen forces. Here, strangers from distant towns converge under the same unspoken invitation: the call of the **Echoing Veil**, a phenomenon where whispers carried across the open plains return distorted, as if through a cracked mirror. Some say it’s a test; others claim it’s a prison. The site is marked by a ring of rusted iron gates, half-buried in the earth, with no visible locks or keys. Inside, the ruins of an ancient observatory—its domed structure collapsed, its telescopes shattered—stand as silent sentinels. The only constant is the **Drift**, a shifting, semi-solid mist that forms at dusk, where figures emerge and vanish in a blur. No records exist of who attends or why, though rumors persist of "those who listen too closely" or those who arrive with bags of something that glows faintly in the dark. The local lore warns that the last person to leave without an explanation vanished without a trace.
