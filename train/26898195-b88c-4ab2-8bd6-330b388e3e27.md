## Product Requirements Document: "Context" - Knowledge Synthesis & Connection App

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers increasingly spend significant time synthesizing information from disparate sources—documents, emails, meeting notes, research papers—leading to cognitive overload, fragmented understanding, and difficulty connecting insights to actionable outcomes.

**Vision:** To empower knowledge workers with a seamless application that automatically connects and contextualizes information, fostering deeper understanding and accelerating innovation.

### User Personas

**1. Dr. Eleanor Vance – Research Scientist**

*   **Goal:** To quickly synthesize findings from numerous research papers and datasets to identify novel research directions and write compelling grant proposals.
*   **Pain Points:** Overwhelmed by the volume of academic literature; difficulty tracking connections between seemingly unrelated findings; time-consuming manual literature reviews; struggles to articulate the "big picture" in grant applications.

**2. Mark Olsen – Strategic Consultant**

*   **Goal:** To rapidly understand client industries, competitive landscapes, and internal company data to develop data-driven strategic recommendations.
*   **Pain Points:** Scattered data residing in various formats (PDFs, spreadsheets, databases); difficulty identifying key trends and patterns across data sources; inability to easily share synthesized insights with colleagues; concerns about presenting incomplete or inaccurate information to clients.

**3. Priya Sharma – Product Manager**

*   **Goal:** To efficiently integrate user research findings, market analysis, and competitive intelligence into product strategy and roadmap planning.
*   **Pain Points:** Information silos between research, marketing, and engineering teams; difficulty translating qualitative data into actionable product requirements; challenges in maintaining a holistic view of the product landscape; time constraints hindering thorough analysis.

### Requirements

**1. Automated Information Ingestion**

*   **Description:** The application shall automatically ingest information from a variety of sources, including cloud storage services (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive), email inboxes, and web URLs.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** User can connect accounts for supported services; ingested files are accurately indexed and searchable; ingestion process is initiated with a single action and completes within a reasonable timeframe (under 60 seconds for a typical document).
*   **Success Metrics:** 95% of ingested files are correctly indexed; average ingestion time under 45 seconds; user satisfaction rating (measured via in-app survey) of 4.0 or higher.

**2. Relational Graph Visualization**

*   **Description:** The application shall automatically generate a relational graph visualization connecting ingested information based on identified keywords, entities, and concepts.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** Graph displays connections between ingested items; user can manually adjust connection strength and add/remove connections; graph is navigable and zoomable; graph automatically updates as new information is ingested.
*   **Success Metrics:** 80% of users actively explore the relational graph within the first week of use; average session duration involving graph exploration is at least 5 minutes; user feedback indicates clarity and usefulness of graph representation.

**3. Contextual Summarization**

*   **Description:** The application shall provide concise summaries of ingested items, highlighting key concepts and connections to other ingested items.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** Summaries are accurate and reflect the content of the original source; summaries include links to related items within the application; user can request expanded summaries for greater detail.
*   **Success Metrics:** 75% of users utilize the contextual summarization feature; user feedback indicates summaries are helpful in understanding the core content; average time spent reading summaries is less than 60 seconds.

**4. Collaborative Workspace**

*   **Description:** The application shall allow users to share workspaces and collaborate on synthesized information with colleagues.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can invite collaborators to workspaces; collaborators can view, edit, and comment on synthesized information; permissions can be managed at the workspace and item level.
*   **Success Metrics:** 30% of users actively collaborate with colleagues within the application; average number of collaborators per workspace is at least 2; user feedback indicates ease of collaboration and value of shared insights.

**5. Search & Filtering**

*   **Description:** The application shall provide robust search and filtering capabilities to quickly locate specific information within the ingested data.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can search by keyword, entity, and concept; users can filter by source, date, and user; search results are ranked by relevance; search functionality is responsive and accurate.
*   **Success Metrics:** Average search time is under 10 seconds; 90% of searches return relevant results; user feedback indicates ease of finding specific information.

### Technical Considerations

*   **Scalability:** The application must be designed to handle a large volume of ingested data and users.
*   **Security:** Robust security measures are required to protect user data and intellectual property.
*   **Data Privacy:** Compliance with relevant data privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) is paramount.
*   **API Integrations:** Future development should include APIs for integration with other productivity tools.
*   **Platform Support:** Initial release will focus on iOS and Android mobile platforms.

### Risks

*   **Accuracy of Automated Connections:** The automated relational graph generation may occasionally produce inaccurate or misleading connections. Mitigation involves user feedback mechanisms and refinement of algorithms.
*   **Data Security Breaches:** The risk of data breaches requires ongoing security audits and proactive measures.
*   **User Adoption:** Ensuring user adoption requires a focus on ease of use and demonstrable value.
*   **Computational Resource Requirements:** Graph generation and summarization are computationally intensive and require optimized infrastructure.

### Non-Goals

*   Real-time document editing capabilities.
*   Direct integration with social media platforms.
*   Support for all possible file formats in the initial release.
*   Offline functionality in the initial release.
*   Advanced machine learning model training capabilities for users.



### Launch Checklist

1.  Complete final security audit and penetration testing.
2.  Finalize onboarding tutorial and in-app help documentation.
3.  Deploy application to iOS and Android app stores.
4.  Prepare initial marketing materials and public relations outreach.
5.  Establish customer support channels and training for support staff.
6.  Monitor application performance and user feedback closely during the first week of launch.
