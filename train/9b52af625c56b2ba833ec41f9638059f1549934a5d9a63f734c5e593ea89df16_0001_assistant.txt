Ray Lankford (Raymond Lewis Lankford) was born on June 5, 1967, in Los Angeles, California and grew up in Modesto, California. He played for the St. Louis Cardinals from 1990 until 2001. He then played for the San Diego Padres from 2001 to 2002. He did not play during the 2003 season because he was recovering from injuries. He returned to the Cardinals for his last season in 2004.
