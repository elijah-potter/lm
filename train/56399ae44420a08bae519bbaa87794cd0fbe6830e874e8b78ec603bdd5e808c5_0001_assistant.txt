Once upon a time, I, Princess Lina of Aldenland, woke up in a strange, frightening place. I had no idea where I was and I knew no one and nothing, except for the fact that I was surrounded by enormous, strange beasts with ghastly features. The place I had woken in was dark and gloomy, and I soon came to learn that there was never any sunshine at all. There was nothing but dark clouds above, and the only source of light was the brief flickering of the lightning from the thunderstorms. I didn't know what was more frightening to me - the sound of the raging thunder, the growling of the beasts, or the fact that I was entirely alone.

As I walked through the desolate forests of these lands, seeking food and shelter, I wondered how I had arrived at this place. Had I been cursed by a witch or wizard? Was I in a deep sleep or trance? I flinched as my bare feet walked over sharp rocks and tangled roots. I needed to find my way home before I was too far gone. Maybe there was a kind stranger I could befriend. Someone here had to know of Aldenland, and I needed some direction.
