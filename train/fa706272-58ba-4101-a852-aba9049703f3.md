The **unspoken "Silence Protocol"** in corporate boardrooms: Speak only when the next speaker has already raised their hand—regardless of the question’s relevance. Justified as "respecting the speaker’s time" or "avoiding interruptions," it’s enforced by the longest-suffering intern or the CEO’s passive-aggressive nod. The real rule? **Never challenge the status quo**, lest you be labeled a "disruptor" or "inappropriate." Those who dare question it are met with silence—or a polite email asking if they’d like to "focus on the agenda."

The irony? The protocol itself is the *real* disruption.
