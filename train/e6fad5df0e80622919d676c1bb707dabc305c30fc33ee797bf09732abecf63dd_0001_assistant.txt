The five largest states in America based on land size are Alaska, Texas, California, Montana, and New Mexico. Alaska is the biggest by over double the second largest and covers 586,000 square miles. Texas is the second largest covering 261,232 square miles. California is the third largest and covers 155,959 square miles. Montana is the fourth largest and covers 145,552 square miles. New Mexico is the fifth largest state by land size and covers 121,298 square miles.
