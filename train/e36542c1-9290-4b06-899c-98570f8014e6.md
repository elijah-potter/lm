Why do I not belong?* The walls around them hum with familiarity, yet they stand slightly off-kilter, like a mirror cracked at the edges. Every glance, every whispered conversation, a silent echo of what they’re not. The world feels too loud, too uniform—*them*, not me. A weight, not of guilt, but of the quiet, aching certainty that they were never meant to fit. The air between them and the others thickens, a barrier neither can cross.
