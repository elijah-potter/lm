Citizenship: meanings and expressions
A great deal of conceptual work has been done on the nature of rights and the definitions of citizenship. However, little work have been done that explored how poor people perceive their citizenship rights, and how they act on these perceptions in different settings. The work of this group, which was convened by Naila Kabeer, helped to elucidate the 'seeing like a citizen' perspective that has distinguished thethe Citizenship DRC's resarch.
