A dynamic, imperceptibly shifting border marked by subtle, gradual annual changes in vegetation, erosion patterns, or human settlement markers (e.g., fence placement, land use shifts). Defined by:
- **Erosion/Accumulation**: Sand dunes, riverbanks, or coastal shorelines eroding/advancing ~5–50 meters per century (e.g., 1m/year).
- **Vegetation Bands**: Creeping plant communities (e.g., mangroves, grasslands) expanding/contracting at ~10–300 m/decade.
- **Human Signposts**: Fences, roads, or pipelines adjusted incrementally to track movement (e.g., 20m/decade drift in fence alignment).
- **Geological Tracers**: Fault lines, coral reef migration, or groundwater flow gradients as passive indicators.
- **Satellite/Drone Data**: Retrospective analysis of pixel shifts in LiDAR or multispectral imagery showing annual offsets.
- **Climate Influence**: Seasonal/annual variability in winds, tides, or precipitation modulating drift rates.
