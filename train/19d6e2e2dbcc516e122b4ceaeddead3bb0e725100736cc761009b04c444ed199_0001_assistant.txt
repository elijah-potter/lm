Lacey Chabert is an American actress who grew to prominence in the television series Party of Five.

She has starred in the following films: 
• Lost in Space (1998)
• Not Another Teen Movie (2001)
• Daddy Day Care (2003)
• Mean Girls (2004)
• Dirty Deeds (2005)
• Black Christmas (2006) 
• All I Want for Christmas Is You (2017) (animated film).

Lacey Chabert has also appeared in over 30 Hallmark Channel films.
