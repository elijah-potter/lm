A contentious vote on budget cuts led to the resignation of both the mayor and the city council president, sparking mass protests. The final tally: 18 votes against, 15 for, and 7 abstentions, resulting in a 50% minority ruling. Public outrage grew as the decision left schools underfunded while essential services were slashed. Workers’ strikes and demonstrations erupted, demanding immediate reversal. The mayor’s successor called it a "necessary but divisive" decision, while opposition accused corruption. The council’s minority leader accused the majority of prioritizing short-term savings over long-term stability. The city’s economic recovery remains uncertain.
