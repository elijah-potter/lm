No problem. Here is a list of party games.

•Name that tune - This game involves splitting into teams and competing who can name a song that is played faster than the opposing team.
•Reverse Charades - This is a twist on the classic charades game. In this game, there is one guesser per team, and the rest of the team gets a card of something to act out. The guesser has to guess correctly to win.
•Celebrity - This game is another twist on classic charades. A player chooses a celebrity to act out, and his/her teammates have to guess who is being impersonated.
