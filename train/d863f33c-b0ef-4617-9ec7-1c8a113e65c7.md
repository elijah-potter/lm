The ground beneath feels hollow, the pavement beneath barefoot soles warping into a reflective surface that distorts time—faces in the cracks show past and future simultaneously. A single streetlight flickers, its bulb glowing faintly, then extinguishing in an instant. The street below is now a silent, infinite void, lit only by the ghostly glow of a passing car’s taillights, its headlights dimmed to a whisper. The silence that follows is not absolute—it’s the silence of a world that has been rewired, every nerve ending humming with the residual charge of a moment that didn’t quite happen.
