You have to admit that the concept of invention itself is pretty darn amazing.
In order to create an eco-friendly invention, this special art of creativity and resourcefulness must be taken to an entirely different level.
Inventing eco-friendly products involves not only an end result that is good for the planet, but at it's best, utilizes existing abundant resources. The effort involved in this creative enterprise has sparked man's imagination in some wonderful ways.
Inventions based on eco-friendly living reflect the human capacity of imagination and have the added bonus of ways to make our lives on this changing planet better. Eco-friendly inventions are not only unique, they are a necessary faction of a changing and new world.
