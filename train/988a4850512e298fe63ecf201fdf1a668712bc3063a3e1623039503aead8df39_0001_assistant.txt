• Mushroom Miasma - When used sparingly, Mushroom Miasma can add brightness and acidity, for an interesting contrast to oily dishes. 
• Sparkling Snow-berry Sap - The glittery sap droplets from the Snow-berry tree can be distilled into a potent sweetener that doesn't compromise texture. 
• Toad Truffles - Harvested from the backs of Trompadolly Toads, these lightweight truffles warm the senses with a potent dose of pepper and bitterness.  
• Bumblebee Buzzers - Found discarded by bees on the forest floor, these sturdy buzzers can be repurposed into kebab skewers or roasting spits. 
• Lily Leaf Lozenges - Found only in the Southern Ponds of the Enchanted Forest, these lozenges can thicken up any soup, gravy, or sauce.
