First of the ocean is the mighty blue whale
A massive mammal with a big blubbery tail
Spewing water and air from its huge blowhole
Massive in size but with teeth that are dull

Next is the shark with razor-sharp teeth
A menacing creature needing no air to breathe
A surfer's nightmare is the fin above water
They tend to stay south where waters are hotter

Unlike the shark, dolphins are nice
But just like sharks, they avoid the ice
A beautiful sight to see a dolphin leap
They live in water in the oceans deep

Now we look towards that slippery eel
With sharpened teeth and a slimy feel
Be careful when treading in water near rocks
For you may brush an eel and get a big shock 

Last but not least we examine the crab
An odd little creature, it could be from a lab
With spiny little legs and big meaty claws
With a shelled exterior hard enough to break jaws
