In a quiet town in England, a young college student named Molly McLaren lives a bright and hopeful life as a student and fitness blogger. She strives to gain her health and fitness degree from university so that she can change the lives of women by promoting health, wellness and sharing the power of positive thinking. While Molly is living her life, her ex-boyfriend Josh Stimpson is plotting ways to get back in her life. His obsession with her turns to stalking, and stalking turns to murder. The case shocked the community, and the following trial was a media circus. This podcast will explore the details of the case, talking to friends and family members of Molly, as well as experts on domestic violence and stalking. With your help, we'll raise awareness about the dangers of these issues and shine a light on the often-overlooked problem of violence against women.
