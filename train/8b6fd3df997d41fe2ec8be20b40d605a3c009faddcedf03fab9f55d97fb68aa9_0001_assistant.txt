Interactive Experiment: Autotrophs and Heterotrophs

Step 1: Differentiate between photoautotrophs and heterotrophs. 

Step 2: Present a scenario where users can choose to be a photoautotroph or a heterotroph.

Step 3: If experimenters choose to be a photoautotroph, guide them through the process of synthesizing sugars and using light energy and explain their role in introducing chemical energy and fixed carbon into ecosystems.

Step 4: If experimenters choose to be a heterotroph, simulate the process of obtaining fixed carbon by consuming other organisms or their by-products.

Step 5: Discuss the various types of heterotrophs, such as animals, fungi, prokaryotes, and protists, and their dependence on external sources for fixed carbon.

Step 6: Highlight the connections between autotrophs and heterotrophs in maintaining the balance of energy and carbon within ecosystems

This interactive experiment aims to engage experimenters in hands-on learning experiences, allowing them to explore the concepts of autotrophs and heterotrophs dynamically and interactively.
