Once there was a little bunny named Benny who lived in a cozy burrow with his mama and papa. Benny loved to hop around the sunny meadow every morning, chasing after his favorite things: flowers, butterflies, and sometimes a tiny, shiny stone.

One day, Benny found a new, soft patch of grass where no one else played. He sat down and watched the clouds float by like fluffy white sheep. A little wind blew and the clouds started dancing. Benny giggled, "Look, Mama, Papa, the clouds are dancing!"

Just then, a big, brown cat named Mr. Whiskers came running. He said, "Benny, Bunny! Stop dancing with the clouds! They are not yours to play with!" Benny’s ears flattened, and he looked scared.

Mama Bunny came running and said, "It's okay, Benny. Let's go play with the flowers instead." Benny nodded, but he still felt a little sad. He watched the clouds float away and wondered if they would ever come back to dance again.

That evening, Benny snuggled under the stars and thought about the clouds. He whispered, "Maybe clouds can dance anywhere." The next morning, Benny saw the clouds again and this time, he and his friends hopped right into them, dancing with the wind.

From that day on, Benny and his friends always looked for the clouds, because they knew they could dance anywhere—and that was the best part of all!
