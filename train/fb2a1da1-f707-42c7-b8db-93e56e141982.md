Vast, echoing void with an absence of sound—walls of shifting, translucent matter stretch endlessly, their contours dissolving like mist. No horizon; only the slow, creeping pull of unseen gravity. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, stretching diagonally across the floor, as if gravity itself bends. The air is thin, carrying a faint, metallic tang—dust particles swirl in slow, lazy spirals, never settling. Objects drift lazily, defying weight, their edges blurred by an unseen distortion field. The ceiling curves into something that isn’t a ceiling—more like a vast, uneven dome of some other dimension. No light source; only a diffuse, sickly glow that flickers like a dying star. The silence is suffocating, broken only by the distant, rhythmic *thrum* of something unseen, vibrating through the void. The space feels alive, but wrong. It breathes.
