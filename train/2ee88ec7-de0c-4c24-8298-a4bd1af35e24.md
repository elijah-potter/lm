Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, lived a curious bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy gray ears that bounced when he hopped, and a tail that wiggled like a little flag.

One sunny morning, Benny noticed something strange. His favorite patch of clover was missing! The bright green leaves were gone, and only a tiny trail of dirt was left. Benny hopped around the garden, looking for clues.

"Who took my clover?" Benny asked his best friend, a wise old turtle named Tilly. Tilly had a shell that sparkled in the sunlight.

Tilly squinted her eyes and said, "Maybe it was a naughty mouse named Milo!"

Benny thought for a moment. "Maybe you're right," he said. "Milo is always sneaking around, but he never takes my clover."

Benny and Tilly decided to find out. They built a little trap out of sticks and leaves, hiding it near Benny’s clover patch. They put a shiny pebble inside, just to see if Milo would be tempted.

Later that afternoon, Benny and Tilly peeked through the trap. There, in the middle of the trap, sat Milo, his tiny paws clutching a tiny pile of clover. He was chewing on it and looking very guilty!

Benny and Tilly giggled. "You didn’t have to eat my clover, Milo!" Benny said.

Milo nodded and looked sad. "I was just hungry, and I didn’t know you’d miss it so much," he said.

Tilly said, "We’ll share some of your own clover, Milo! And next time, ask first!"

Milo nodded again, and the three friends had the best picnic ever under the big oak tree. Benny shared his clover, Tilly gave Milo some of her favorite berries, and they all had a happy time laughing and playing.

From that day on, Benny’s clover patch grew even bigger, and Milo learned that sharing is fun—and so is friendship! And Benny knew that his garden was the happiest place in the whole world, thanks to his new friends.
