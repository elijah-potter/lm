The record for the most-produced airplane is held by the Cessna 172 “Skyhawk.” It is a lightweight, 4-seat civil aircraft that first flew in 1956. The 172 has come in a few different variants over the years, but all told, well over 44,000 of them have been sold.

There are a few appealing characteristics that have made the Skyhawk popular. One of the biggest factors is its simplicity. Compared to many aircraft, the Cessna 172 is simple to fly and maintain. It is regularly used by pilot training schools specifically for that reason. 

Compared to many of its competitors, the Cessna's high wing can be advantageous. Most competing aircraft like the Piper Cherokee have low-mounted wings which limit visibility below the cockpit and make the cabin harder to get into and out of. The Skyhawk's high wing limits upward visibility but makes it easier for pilots to see the ground and runway.

Until recent years, the 172 was also among the most affordable planes to buy. Unfortunately for Cessna, a downturn in demand has meant fewer Skyhawks are coming off the line now and their per-airframe price is rising accordingly. However, they’re still relatively affordable to keep and maintain.
