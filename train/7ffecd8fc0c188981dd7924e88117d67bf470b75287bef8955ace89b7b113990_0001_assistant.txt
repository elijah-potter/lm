                                                                  **Cafe Delight Menu**


Appetizers:

• Iridescent Apple Bruschetta:
            Crispy baguette slices topped with a medley of diced iridescent apples, fresh basil, balsamic glaze, and a touch of goat cheese. A tantalizing blend of sweet and savory flavors. 
• Caffeinated Avocado Spring Rolls: 
            Delicate rice paper rolls filled with creamy caffeinated avocado, julienned vegetables, and a zesty lime dipping sauce. A refreshing and energizing start to your meal.  

Salads:

•Iridescent Apple Kale Salad: 
            A vibrant mix of crisp kale, sliced iridescent apples, toasted walnuts, crumbled feta cheese, and a tangy apple cider vinegarette. A symphony of textures and flavors that will leave you craving more. 
• Caffeinated Avocado Quinoa Bowl: 
            Nutrient-packed quinoa topped with sliced caffeinated avocado, roasted vegetables, cherry tomatoes, and a creamy avocado lime dressing. A wholesome and energizing salad option.

Main Course: 

• Iridescent Apple Glazed Pork Tenderloin: 
            Succulent pork tenderloin marinated in a delectable iridescent apple glaze, served with roasted root vegetables and a side of apple-infused mashed potatoes. A culinary masterpiece that celebrates the marriage of sweet and savory.
• Caffeinated Avocado Shrimp Pasta:
            Sautéed shrimp tossed in a creamy caffeinated avocado sauce, served over al dente linguine and garnished with fresh herbs and a sprinkle of parmesan cheese. A pasts dish that combines richness with a hint of energy. 

Tacos:

• Iridescent Apple and Grilled Chicken Tacos: 
            Tender grilled chicken, sautéed iridescent apples, caramelized onions, and tangy cilantro-lime coleslaw, all nestled in warm corn tortillas. A delightful fusion of sweet and tangy flavors.  
• Caffeinated Avocado Black Bean Tacos: 
            Spiced black beans, creamy caffeinated avocado slices, pico de gallo, and a drizzle of chipotle aioli, wrapped in soft flour tortillas. A vegetarian taco option that packs a caffeinated punch. 

Desserts:

Iridescent Apple Pie:
            A classic dessert with a twist! Sliced iridescent apples, delicately spiced and baked in a flaky crust, served warm with a scoop of cinnamon-infused ice -cream. A nostalgic treat with a touch of magic.
Caffeinated Avocado Mousse:
            Silky smooth avocado mousse infused with a hint of caffeine, garnished with shaved dark chocolate and a dollop of whipped cream. A decadent and indulgent finale to your dining experience. 

Beverages:

• Iridescent Apple Sparkler: 
            A sparkling blend of fresh iridescent apple juice, sparkling water, and a splash of lime. Effervescent and refreshing. 
• Caffeinated Avocado Smoothie: 
            A creamy blend of caffeinated avocado, banana, almond milk, and a hint of honey. A revitalizing drink to keep you going.
