My boyfriend and I had a massive fight because he is constantly mansplaining and acting like a know-it-all. 

For context, my boyfriend loves to share the facts and trivia he learns at every opportunity. Whenever we are with friends and family, he finds an excuse to share something he's just learned and show off his knowledge. 

Lately, I've been struggling with how often he does this. I find it belittling and condescending, so I've stopped engaging as politely with him when he is on one of his trivia-sharing streaks. Sometimes I switch off listening to his facts because I am just bored of it. Yesterday, for example, he decided to inform me that styrofoam containers are made from polystyrene and not from styrofoam. He was excited about this fact, but I didn't care so I politely said "OK" and moved on.

Tonight we were having dinner at a friend's house. As were cleaning up, our friend suggested we take home some of the leftovers. I was grateful and asked her to pass me a styrofoam container. That's when everything went wrong. My boyfriend decided to chime in with his newly acquired fact that the container isn't actually styrofoam. Instead of ending it there, though, he made a snide comment about how I never listen to what he says. I lost my patience. I snapped at him about how he is condescending and rude with his trivia tidbits. He argued that my not enjoying this personality quirk is disrespectful and makes him feel unimportant. I am just tired of feeling mansplained to every day and I can't bring myself to speak to him right now.
