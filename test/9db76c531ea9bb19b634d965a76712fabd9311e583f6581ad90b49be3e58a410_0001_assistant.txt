Hermie the hermit crab
Stuck in a tidepool
He visited in his day
Went there after school.

As Hermie pondered
A way to get out
He knew it was tricky
The sun was almost out

He'd wait for the water
To swish him on free
Once he was out there
Homebound he'd be

But now, while he waited,
All safe in his place
He stared at the sky
The whole outer space

The world dawned above
Red, orange, and pink
He relaxed while he sat
Without a single think.
