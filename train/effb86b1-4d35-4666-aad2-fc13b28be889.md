Ephemeral Sandscape: A shifting dune field where erosion and wind sculpt the landscape in real-time, forming and erasing patterns like temporary runes. Rivers of fine sand carve paths that vanish within hours, leaving only ghostly imprints of previous formations. At the center stands a skeletal tree, its roots dissolved by shifting sands, its branches bent by winds that carry whispers of forgotten voices. The air hums with the sound of shifting grains, and the horizon is a blur of motion where nothing holds shape beyond a few moments.
