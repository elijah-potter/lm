The term artisanal has no legal or standard definition when referring to food; however, the word is typically used to describe food products that are made by hand, in small batches, and with particular emphasis on preserving traditional techniques. Artisanal foods, often prepared in small amounts with an emphasis on quality over quantity, frequently emphasize the use of fresh and local ingredients. Instead of giant companies, small, independent businesses often produce them, with skills that are passed down through generations.
