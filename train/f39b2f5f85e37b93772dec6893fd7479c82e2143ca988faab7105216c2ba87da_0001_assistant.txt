PlayStation was the console that started it all for Sony. These days we will live in a world where the PS5 exists with its epic exclusives like God of War, The Last of Us, and many more. But PlayStation was never a slouch, to begin with. In this top 5 list, we'll take a look at the best sellers for the original Playstation which was released in 1994.

1. Gran Turismo was developed by Polyphony Digital and sold 10,850,000 copies. A simulation racing game that allows the player to have their own car collection and participate in races on a variety of maps. 
2. Final Fantasy VII was developed by Square (today known as Square Enix) and sold 10,022,228 copies. A turn-based JRPG that follows the story of Cloud and his group of companions in an expansive and deep story.
3. Gran Turismo 2 also developed by Polyphony Digital sold 9,370,000 copies. Building on the back of its predecessor, it's no surprise to find GT2 on this list as it continued the legacy of its prequel with more cars and more tracks.
4. Final Fantasy VIII also developed Square sold 8,600,000. An indirect sequel that follows a different group of mercenaries, FFVIII built on the combat system while improving graphics and moving into the 3D realm.
5. Tekken 3 developed by Namco sold 8,300,000. A fighting genre juggernaut, Tekken had always been one of the best fighters on PlayStation and Tekken 3 was no different which introduced a roster with many new playable characters and three-axis movement.
