**Example of a non-shortened shortcut concept:**

A **Macro-based "keyboard template"** where a predefined sequence of keystrokes (e.g., `Ctrl+Alt+S` → `Ctrl+Shift+F12`) generates a full command by dynamically filling placeholders (e.g., `Ctrl+Alt+S` triggers `echo "Hello, $(USERNAME)!" >> ~/log.txt`).

---
**Use case for automation:**
- **Input:** `user=john`
- **Output:** `echo "Hello, john!" >> ~/log.txt` (executed via script).

*(Note: No compression—just a structured command.)*
