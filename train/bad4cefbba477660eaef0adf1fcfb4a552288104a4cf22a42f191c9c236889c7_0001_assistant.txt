Attention all faculty and staff,

As has been discussed prior, we are now implementing smartboards throughout classrooms on our campus. This decision reflects months of deliberation, with input from all members of the faculty and staff at our institution, including our technical support team. The benefits to the students far outweigh any ignorant sentiments of this new technology some of you may have. Yes, it will require some adjustment. Yes, the devices are not compatible with the dry-erase markers that you are used to. Yes, the district has spent quite a lot of funds on equipping our classrooms with the latest in 21st-century educational technology. And yes, this will help our students learn and retain what we teach them here at our campus. 

We here at the administration are proud of our track record working to improve educational standards for all our students above any other concerns. We will not let a refusal of our otherwise gifted and caring faculty members to understand and accept technological progress hamper our goals. You will receive a smartboard. You will understand how it works. You will integrate it into your teaching methods and you will see how it improves the quality of your instruction. 

That is all.
