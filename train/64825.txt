Importance of Brick Re-pointing
All buildings have a common element, which is mortar. This is the material responsible for separating the different masonry units, such as stone, brick or other materials. The function of the mortar is actually not for sticking bricks together, as many people think, but to ensure they are held apart as well as fill up any irregularities between the various bedding faces. What holds brick buildings in place is actually gravity. Find out what brick re pointing is by reading on.
At some later stages in the life of a building, this mortar usually shows signs of ageing and wearing. It will become recessed gradually with time. The mortar must be more porous and softer than the brick units and thus will, therefore, suffer most of the natural weathering processes. This a more desirable situation compared to the alternative case where the brick is softer than the mortar. If that happens, then the mortar would remain sound while the surrounding bricks erode, consequently requiring rebuilding instead of the simple job of brick pointing, which is simply renewing the mortar joints.
Generally, it is wise to consider brick re pointing when the open joints have a depth that is similar to how thick the mortar bed is. At this point, you will need to call for the services of an expert to ensure the integrity of your structure is not compromised. The brick re pointing work is largely straightforward but is quite labor intensive, so you must make sure you are using the best team for the job.
