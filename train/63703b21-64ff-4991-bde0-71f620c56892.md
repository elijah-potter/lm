**The weight of silence hung between them as the door creaked open**, its hollow echo reflecting the dim glow of the single bulb above the desk. Your fingers traced the edge of the open laptop screen, where the cursor blinked like a dying ember. The screen displayed a single, unreadable line: *"What if I’m wrong?"*

The clock ticked relentlessly, its second hand a slow, mocking dancer. Outside, rain tapped against the window like impatient fingers, demanding attention. Your breath came in shallow gasps, each one a whisper of doubt. The coffee cup, half-empty, sat abandoned beside you—its warmth now a distant memory.

A knock at the door. The sound was so abrupt it nearly startled you into action. Your pulse quickened, your stomach twisted. Should you answer? No. The voice inside could be someone you’d rather not see. But the door stood open, the keyhole dark and inviting. The decision was already made—you were already halfway there.

The door swung inward, revealing a figure in a coat too dark for the afternoon, their face obscured by the shadow of a hat. *"You still here?"* The voice was low, familiar, carrying the faint scent of rain and something else—something unnameable, like the ghost of a promise left unfinished.

You exhaled sharply, the sound swallowed by the silence that followed. The choice had been made. The past was a closed book, the future a blank page. And now, the pen was in your hand.
