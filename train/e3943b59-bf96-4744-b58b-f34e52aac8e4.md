The café’s hum faded into the air like mist dissolving in morning light. The cup sat empty on the saucer, steam curling from its rim into the quiet. She had chosen the silence between words, the pause where thoughts hung like unspoken breath. The waitress’s pen hovered over the pad, then dropped. The clock ticked—just one more minute—before the door creaked open, sealing the moment. Outside, the city breathed in silence, too.
