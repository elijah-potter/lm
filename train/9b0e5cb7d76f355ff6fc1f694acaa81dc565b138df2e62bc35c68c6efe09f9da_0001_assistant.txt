An unknown man walked into the briefing room full of Spartans. At 6-foot-7, the man seemed diminutive compared to the 8-foot-tall, fully-suited Spartans who stood in a circle around the holographic display. Everyone looked at the man while their helmets hid the confused and amused looks on their faces. General Marks came into the briefing and began speaking to the group.

"Marines, it is time to descend upon the horde. As I speak, thousands of alien creatures are fortifying themselves on the ringed planet and are ready to protect their planet-destroying weapon from being sabotaged. We are tasked with destroying the weapon as well as any alien that stands in our way. Where is Master Chief?" Everyone looked around hoping to see Master Chief in his body armor, but they could not spot him. 

"I think we're gonna get killed out there without the Chief," one of the Spartans relayed his fear to the group. "He's the only one who can lead us to victory against the horde."

Suddenly, the small man raised his hand for everyone to see. "Yes?" said General Marks. 

"I am ready to kill some aliens," the man replied.

General Marks, seeming ready to laugh, asked the man's name.

"You can call me Master Chief," the man replied before donning a full Spartan suit and revealing himself to be their leader. Each Spartan looked at their leader and, readying their weapons, nodded at him. "Ready!" they yelled.

Master Chief grabbed his weapon and nodded back. "Time to kill some aliens!"
