Once upon a time, in a cozy green forest, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop, play, and explore. One sunny morning, Benny heard a tiny voice, "Help! Help!"

Curious Benny hopped closer. There was a little blue bird with a broken wing. "Oh no!" Benny thought. "I can't let this happen!"

Benny thought for a moment. "What if I build a little house for the bird?" he asked. So, Benny gathered twigs and leaves. He made a cozy nest for the bird. "Here you go, my friend!" Benny said.

The blue bird chirped happily, "Thank you, Benny! You're the best friend ever!"

Benny smiled. "I know, and now we can explore the forest together!"

Together, Benny and the blue bird played, sang, and explored, making the forest even happier. Benny learned that being kind and helping others makes the world a better place. And so, Benny and the blue bird became the best of friends, always ready to help and play.
