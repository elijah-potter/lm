Meaning of 'reta or renta'
n (retane) A cant term for a thick and hard and clumsy mbhakara or cake. v ragada, rapata, jhoka (to eat): v badava, thoka (to make).
Meaning of रेट or रेंट
n (रेटणें) A cant term for a thick and hard and clumsy भाकर or cake. v रगड, रपाट, झोक (to eat): v बडव, ठोक (to make).
