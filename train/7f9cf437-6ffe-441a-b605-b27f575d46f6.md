The abandoned plan documented in the records was the **Project Horizon-X** (Phase 3), which aimed to integrate modular smart-grid infrastructure into urban neighborhoods by 2025. Key components included:
- **Autonomous microgrids** with energy storage and AI-driven load balancing.
- **Public transit hubs** with real-time predictive scheduling.
- **Decentralized data nodes** for citizen-driven energy management.

**Reasons for Abandonment:**
1. **Regulatory Delays:** Federal approvals for grid modernization legislation stalled due to bipartisan gridlock, delaying federal subsidies by 18 months.
2. **Supply Chain Bottlenecks:** Critical materials (e.g., lithium-ion batteries, rare earth metals) faced shortages post-COVID-19 supply chain disruptions.
3. **Security Concerns:** Cybersecurity audits flagged vulnerabilities in IoT-based microgrid controls, prompting a 6-month pause.
4. **Economic Shifts:** Post-2023 energy price volatility reduced corporate incentives for long-term infrastructure investments.
5. **Competing Priorities:** National defense funding reallocated to quantum computing initiatives, diverting R&D budgets.

**Current Status:** Documentation archived; team reassigned to **Project Nova-7** (focused on off-grid solar microgrids).
