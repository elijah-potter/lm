Of course, here are some ideas for a gravity wizard.

1. Gravity Warden: A gravity warden is a powerful wizard who can manipulate the forces of gravity. They use their magic to protect themselves and their allies from harm. They can also use magic to create force fields, pull enemies towards them, push enemies away, and create black holes.

2. Astral Walker: An astral walker is a powerful being who can travel through the astral plane. They use their magic to manipulate the forces of gravity to travel through space and time. They can also levitate objects, create force fields, push and pull enemies, and rip holes in space-time.

3. Graviturgy Wizard: A graviturgy wizard specializes in gravity magic. They have learned to harness the power of gravity to create powerful effects. They can levitate objects, create force fields, crush their enemies under powerful gravity, and lighten the gravity on themselves and their allies, allowing them to jump very high, and land safely from high heights.

4. Stargazer: A stargazer is a powerful being who can study the stars. They use their magic to manipulate the forces of gravity to study the stars and learn about the cosmos. They can use their magic to create force fields, push and pull enemies, create black holes, and tear the fabric of space-time.
