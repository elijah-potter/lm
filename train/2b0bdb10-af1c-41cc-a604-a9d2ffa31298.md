The room was dim, the air thick with tension. A sharp intake of breath came from the corner as the older man shifted uncomfortably in his chair. His fingers tapped nervously against the desk, the sound barely audible over the hum of the fluorescent lights. The younger colleague leaned forward, eyes darting between the two of them, waiting for an answer that seemed impossible to deliver.

Silence stretched on, punctuated by the distant murmur of a colleague’s phone ringing. The older man’s jaw tightened, his knuckles whitening around the pen in his hand. He exhaled slowly, a breath that carried the faint scent of aftershave and something older, something worn by years of hiding. The younger colleague’s lips parted, but no words came out. Instead, the older man stood abruptly, his chair scraping against the concrete floor, and walked to the door without looking back. The click of the lock echoed hollowly.
