Many years ago, when Huntersdale Park was much less popular, a park ranger named Andy was the sole caretaker. Park Ranger Andy liked the job because he liked being out in nature, and he liked learning about the different trees, plants, and animals of the forest. To save money, Park Ranger Andy slept in a tent himself. It wasn't too bad, and he knew where all the best spots were. One night, Andy heard a sound out in the woods. It was an awful yowling sound, almost like a house cat. It didn't sound like it was very far away, and Andy thought that maybe someone's cat had gotten out of their house. 

He listened carefully and, eventually, heard it again, except this time it sounded more like a person's voice. Was it someone pretending to be a cat? Andy was the park ranger, so it was his responsibility to investigate. A few of the other campers say that Andy checked on their tents that night and told them he was looking for a prankster, but nobody knew who it might have been. Then, Andy went looking in the woods, the beam of his flashlight flickering through the trees. They found him a few days later. Well, what was left of him. So, if you hear something calling for you from the woods, whatever you do, don't answer.
