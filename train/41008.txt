The climate in Nyborg during July can be summarized as mild and reasonably dry.
July is in the summer in Nyborg and is typically the 2nd warmest month of the year. Daytime maximum temperatures average around 19°C (67°F), whilst at night 13°C (55°F) is normal.

On average July is the 2nd wettest month of the year in Nyborg with around 60mm 2.3 inches of rain making it a reasonably dry time to visit. This rainfall is typically spread over 14 days, although this may vary considerably.
On the flip side this corresponds to an average of 8.5 hours of sunshine per day.
