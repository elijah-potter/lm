Do you have a hankering for nachos, but also have a strong desire for sugar? Well, so do I. Mothers know that pregnancy comes with cravings for the weirdest food. You get something in your head, and nothing can stop you from tasting the treat that you imagined. At 4 months pregnant with my last child, I realized there may be a market for the odd recipes that I have grown to love and am returning to during this pregnancy. I promise this is a simple recipe that can be made on a single plate with the use of a microwave. This recipe can be customized to your liking, so everything besides the chips is optional. Maybe you are a chocolate lover, or maybe you prefer fruity sweets. If you are feeling especially health conscious, then you can use fruit instead of candy toppings. 

 Suggested Ingredients:
- Enough nacho chips to cover a plate
- Chocolate, strawberry, or caramel syrup
- Whipped cream
- Skittles
- M&Ms
- Reese's
-Fruit

Nachos are a relatively easy dish to make, so I will not go overboard with instructions. First, you should heat the chips in the microwave for about 10 seconds. Next, you will want to add the syrup of your choosing. This will be the replacement for cheese in this recipe. It will allow everything to stick to your chips. Next, you should add the candy of your choice. You can choose to mix up the chips slightly so that your chips are covered in toppings. Last, you will want to put a healthy topping of whipped cream on top. Remember that this recipe can be customized in any way that your cravings demand. Have a happy pregnancy!
