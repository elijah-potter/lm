No problem! Here are a few books you could read that are similar to Colleen Hoover's: 

* Archer’s Voice by Mia Sheridan: This emotional and steamy love story follows Bree as she returns home upon her father's death and meets Archer, a man who reminds her of her father.
* The Great Alone by Kristin Hannah: Like many of Colleen Hoover's books, this novel includes a series of dramatic and consequential events. It's a story about love, family, and survival. 
* How to Walk Away by Katherine Center: This is a story of love in the face of trauma, which is often found in Colleen Hoover's books. It's also fast-paced like Hoover's. 
* In Five Years by Rebecca Serle: Here's another fast-paced, emotion-packed love story for fans of Hoover's books.
