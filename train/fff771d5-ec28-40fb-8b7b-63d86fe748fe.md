A transient marketplace near a flooded riverbank, where stalls shift daily between the rusted trailers of a former carnival and makeshift tents draped in tarps. Neon flickers from a single streetlamp, casting long shadows of people haggling over wares that include last season’s clothing, mismatched kitchen tools, and the occasional hand-painted sign: *"Today’s Special: Half a Couch."* The air hums with the sound of laughter, the clink of metal, and distant sirens. Overhead, a flock of geese honks as they circle the sky, their migration path crossing the makeshift boundary. The ground is a patchwork of dirt, mud, and debris—what was once a park, now a temporary home for those who’ve outgrown the permanent.
