Every day Janet spends 8 minutes looking for her keys and another 3 minutes complaining after she finds them. If Janet stops losing her keys, how many minutes will she save every week?
First find the total time Janet spends looking for her key and complaining each day: 8 minutes/day + 3 minutes/day = <<8+3=11>>11 minutes/day
Then multiply that number by the number of days in a week to find the weekly time spent: 11 minutes/day * 7 days/week = <<11*7=77>>77 minutes
