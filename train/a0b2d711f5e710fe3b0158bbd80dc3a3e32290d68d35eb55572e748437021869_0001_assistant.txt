The walls were made from interlocking pieces of gingerbread that, on close inspection, were slightly too soft to the touch but still delicious if you were to take a bite. In between the gingerbread tiles, there was hard, crusted icing. Intricate patterns of jelly beans and hard candies adorned the walls and wrapped themselves around the windows and doors, and although they did look scrumptious, they were also quite clearly a bit sticky. The window panes were cast from sugar into what should have been a crystal clear finish; however, for some reason, they were starting to distort and fog. 
If you look around the garden, the house is sitting in a field of candyfloss grass that makes the mouth truly water. The door is sheets of licorice, and whilst it appears it was once jet black, it now seems closer to an unusual shade of dark grey. All in all, it does somewhat look like a slightly sunken mess of what was once a fantastic monument to sugar and fantasy.
