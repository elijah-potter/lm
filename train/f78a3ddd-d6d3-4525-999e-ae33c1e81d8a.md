Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a tiny mouse named Milo. Milo had soft brown fur and loved to explore his home and the garden outside.

One sunny morning, Milo was nibbling on a juicy apple core when he heard a tiny *splash!* near the fence. Curious, he peeked outside and saw a little puddle. "Why is there water?" Milo wondered. He remembered his mom saying, "Rain brings flowers." So, he tiptoed outside and saw a soft rain falling gently on the garden.

Milo loved the sound of raindrops and the way they sparkled in the sunlight. He wondered if the rain could make the flowers grow bigger and prettier. As the rain continued, Milo found a tiny puddle in the grass. He gently touched it and saw a small, shimmering reflection of himself. "Oh, look!" he said to his reflection, "you're wet too!"

Just then, a little butterfly with bright orange wings fluttered down. "Hello, Milo!" she said. "The rain helps the flowers drink water and grow stronger." Milo smiled. "That’s right! I bet they’ll bloom tomorrow!"

Milo stayed outside for a while, listening to the rain and watching the clouds dance. When the rain stopped, he saw his garden transformed—some flowers were now fully open, and the grass sparkled. He picked a flower with a tiny purple center and a soft pink petal, then tucked it into his pocket.

As Milo walked back inside, he felt happy and tired from playing in the rain. He curled up in his favorite spot by the window and snuggled under his blanket. "Today was the best day ever," he thought. "The rain made everything so special."

And from that day on, whenever Milo heard the sound of rain, he would smile and think, "Maybe today will be as magical as yesterday."
