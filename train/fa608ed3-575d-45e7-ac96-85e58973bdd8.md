AITA for refusing to let my sister use my inherited vacation home?

Okay, this is a bit complicated, so bear with me. It all started back in 2018. My grandmother, Eleanor, lived in a beautiful Victorian house in Cape May, New Jersey. She’s always been the matriarch of our family, and she was incredibly generous. In late 2018, she told me she wanted to leave the house to me in her will. I was stunned, as my younger sister, Clara, had always been the “golden child.” Clara is a lawyer, very successful, and always seemed to be the focus of Eleanor’s attention. I’m a freelance writer, which Eleanor often dismissed as “unstable.”

Eleanor passed away in March of 2021. As expected, I inherited the house. It’s a significant asset, and I’m incredibly grateful, but also feel a great deal of responsibility to maintain it properly. I’m paying property taxes, insurance, and upkeep – about $3,000 a month. I’m also renting it out for a portion of the year to cover those costs and put a little aside for future repairs.

Clara, predictably, has been angling for access ever since the will was read. Initially, she was just subtly hinting about how much she misses Cape May and how wonderful it would be to have a place there. Then, in January of this year, she became more direct. She asked if she could use the house for a week in June, claiming she needed a quiet place to focus on a particularly stressful case. I agreed, under the condition that she pay market rate for the rental. She balked at this, arguing that it was "family" and that I should be more generous. I stood firm.

Then, last week, she upped the ante. She emailed me, copying my mother, stating that she was planning to bring her fiancé, David, and his two children (ages 8 and 10) for two weeks in August. She implied that if I didn’t allow this, it would be a serious reflection on my character and a disappointment to Eleanor's memory. She even suggested that my mother was concerned about my “lack of generosity.” My mother has, admittedly, been subtly pressuring me to be more accommodating to Clara.

I responded, reiterating my policy: anyone using the house pays market rate, and August is already booked with a long-term rental. I also pointed out that I’m working hard to keep the house in good condition and that allowing free or discounted stays undermines that effort. Clara is now furious. She’s accusing me of being selfish, ungrateful, and intentionally trying to spite her. David has also weighed in, sending a rather condescending email about how I’m “holding onto something that should be shared.” My mother is now openly disappointed in me.

I feel incredibly stressed and manipulated. I understand Clara’s desire for a vacation, but I also feel like she's trying to take advantage of my inheritance. I'm trying to be reasonable and maintain the house’s financial stability, but it feels like I'm being ganged up on. I’m starting to question if I’m being unreasonable. I’m definitely feeling the pressure from my mother and Clara, and it’s making me anxious. Honestly, I think they both expect me to just cave. I’m feeling ESH for getting caught in the middle of this family drama.

AITA for sticking to my policy and refusing to let my sister and her family use the vacation home without paying market rate?
