The unexpected call from an old friend about a forgotten family heirloom forced me to abandon the corporate relocation to Chicago. Instead of leaving for a high-paying job, I took the train to Denver, where an old acquaintance from my childhood had moved. Their home was cluttered with half-finished projects—one of which was a vintage radio repair kit—and the moment I touched it, I knew: I had to restore it. The coincidence led me to a side hustle I never thought I’d pursue, and now my life is centered in a city I never pictured.
