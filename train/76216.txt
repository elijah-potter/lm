Standard Definition vs. High Definition
With a higher resolution than normal Standard Definition (SD) televisions, HD television will introduce you to a whole new TV experience by bringing the finest quality images into your home.
How does HD do this?
- By using 720 or 1080 lines to plot a TV picture. Conventional television systems use 625 lines.
- HD utilizes more colours than SD ensuring that everything you watch is richer and more intense.
- Most HD movies use the widescreen 16:9 format to replicate the theatre experience and;
- feature cinema-like surround sound which subscribers can experience with a compatible sound system.
