Chicago Public Schools is working to minimize its impact on the environment and teach students to be environmental stewards. CPS' Environmental Action Plan , in partnership with the Chicago Climate Action Plan, has 26 results-oriented strategies in which the entire CPS community can participate. With this plan, CPS is a leader among urban school districts in environmental action.
