A soft hum fades into the background, like a breath held too long.
The city’s pulse—subtle, rhythmic—drifts in through cracked windows:
a single car horn, a distant laugh, the quiet thrum of a streetlamp casting long shadows.
Hand in hand, two figures sit beneath a tree, their shoulders touching, eyes closed.
The wind carries no more than a whisper, carrying the scent of rain-soaked pavement and warm earth.

A moment where the world exhales, and for once, the noise isn’t loud enough to drown out the peace.
