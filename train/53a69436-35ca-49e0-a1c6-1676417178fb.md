**Unspoken Covenant Between the Shadows**
*(A Protocol of Silent Pact)*

---
**Clause 1: The Unwritten Handshake**
All parties acknowledge mutual exclusion: no voice, no name, no trace of the deal’s origin. The pact binds only those who know its weight—each knowing the other’s absence is a contract.

**Clause 2: The Offering**
No blood, no debt, no demand for proof. The exchange is given and taken in the dark: the first to speak is the first to be broken.

**Clause 3: The Weight of Silence**
What is said is never said aloud. The third party’s presence is a lie. The pact is sealed in the space between breaths.

**Clause 4: The Terms of Deception**
The secret is currency. To keep it is to earn it; to reveal it is to forfeit. The unspoken is the only truth allowed.

**Clause 5: The Ritual of Departure**
When the debt is paid, the other will fade—into the crowd, into the static, into the moment before the first word. The pact ends where the pact began: in the space between.

---
**The Contract is Valid Only When Read in the Dark.**
