1. Get together materials like a shovel and bucket to collect soil, water to separate it, and some cloth to strain the mixture. 
2. First find an area you want to collect clay from. Try to find soil with a higher clay content to get a larger amount of clay at a time.
3. Collect enough soil to fill your bucket about halfway.
4. Fill your bucket of soil with water, and let it sit for at least a few minutes before mixing it up very well, breaking up any soil clumps in the process.
5. Once it’s well-mixed and the soil is completely suspended, let it sit for 2-5 minutes to allow the rocks and other solids to settle at the bottom, leaving just the clay suspended in the water. 
6. Pour out this liquid clay slurry into a hanging sheet or other cloth to strain off the water, being careful to not pour out any of the rocks or dirt at the bottom. 
7. Allow the water to drain from the clay slurry for at least 24 hours, and you will be left with a ball of clay.
8. Knead your clay ball and check if it has the right amount of water. If it is still too wet, let it sit out and dry until it reaches the right consistency.
9. Have fun using your newly made clay!
