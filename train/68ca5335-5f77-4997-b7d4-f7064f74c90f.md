The air feels like a quiet hum, pressing lightly against the edges of awareness—soft enough to shape without breaking. Shadows stretch longer than the body remembers, blending into the weight of stillness. Hands, though empty, carry the ghost of something held too long, now just echoes. The world tilts, just enough to make the ground feel unfamiliar, but not dangerous. The light, though bright, is diffused, as if seen through layers of something between you and it. The silence isn’t absence; it’s the space where thoughts dissolve into a slow, unspoken question. The body moves, but the mind drifts like a leaf in a current that doesn’t push. There’s a faint, persistent warmth where no fire exists, a quiet pulse beneath the calm. The world is holding its breath, and you’re learning to breathe with it.
