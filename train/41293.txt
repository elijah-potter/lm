The New York Times is a rich source of content that can meaningfully complement social studies curriculum in numerous ways:
- Times articles might be used for document-based questions (DBQ’s) and for reading material to satisfy the common core standards for social studies and history.
- The newspaper archives could be mined for primary and secondary source material for research projects.
- Blog posts on civics and politics can be used as writing and discussion prompts.
Here is a list of starting places within NYTimes.com for social studies and history:
- The Global Edition and the World section.
- The U.S. section, including Politics, as well as the Opinion section.
- Times Topics pages.
- The informative and insightful political blogs The Caucus and Five Thirty Eight, as well as the blogs At War and Economix, plus the Opinionator sections written by war veterans and longtime Supreme Court reporter Linda Greenhouse.
