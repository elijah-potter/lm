## Bookstore Serendipity (Berkeley, CA)

The late afternoon light filtering through the windows of Black Oak Books was particularly lovely on Tuesday. I was browsing the poetry section, attempting to rediscover something familiar, when you appeared. You were examining a collection of essays by Joan Didion, your brow furrowed in concentration. I noticed the way the sunlight caught the auburn highlights in your hair, and the quiet intensity you brought to your selection. We didn't speak, but I recall the gentle rustle of the turning pages as you considered your choices, a comforting sound amidst the quiet hum of the store. There was a subtle fragrance of old paper and leather, a comforting aroma that seemed to deepen the feeling of shared contemplation. I left shortly after, feeling a peculiar sense of loss for a connection that never quite formed. Perhaps it was the golden hue of the late afternoon sun playing tricks on my perception, but I felt a genuine spark.
