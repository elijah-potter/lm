Lily had been dreading this day for the past month. Longer, really. She knew what it meant when the letter arrived in the mailbox, and yet she still felt unprepared to accept it. After staring at the cool white document for what felt like forever, she opened the envelope and her worst fears were confirmed. The letter plainly announced that her grandmother had passed away. 

The grief hit Lily like a ton of bricks as soon as she read those words on paper; every memory with her beloved grandmother flooded back into her mind in an instant - from baking cookies together in Grandma’s kitchen to sharing stories around the campfire during their summer vacations. The idea of never being able to experience these moments again was overwhelming.  Too much for Lily to bear at the moment, and suddenly tears streamed down her cheeks without her even recognizing it was happening. 

In time, however, Lily began learning how to cope with this loss by leaning on family and friends who supported each other through difficult times such as these. With their help, she slowly started healing emotionally from within; although things would never be quite the same without Grandma there, Lily found strength knowing that all those wonderful memories they shared together will remain forever etched in her heart. This event shaped Lilly's character significantly throughout life; not only did it teach valuable lessons about resilience but also reminded me how important it is to cherish loved ones while they are here because nothing lasts forever.
