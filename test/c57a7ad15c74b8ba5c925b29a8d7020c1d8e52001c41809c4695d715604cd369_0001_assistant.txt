Sure! An idiom is a phrase that is not meant to be taken literally but has a widely understood figurative meaning. Here are 5 examples of well-known idioms:

1.  "Kill two birds with one stone": means to accomplish many tasks at one time.
2.  "Pass the buck": means to pass the responsibility on to someone else.
3.  "I'm just pulling your leg": means I'm joking.
4.  "It's a piece of cake": means it's really easy. 
5.  "He's feeling under the weather": means he's feeling sick.
