You can make many different meals with ground beef that are simple to prepare. Here are some suggestions: 

1. Spaghetti and meat sauce: Boil your spaghetti. Brown your ground beef with your preferred seasonings. Add your cooked ground beef to your sauce. Add the meat sauce to your cook spaghetti and serve. 

2. Tacos and burritos. Brown your ground beef and add your taco seasoning of choice. Cut up and prepare your preferred toppings. Add your meat and preferred toppings to your taco or burrito shell. 

3. Hamburgers: Form your ground beef into patties. Cook your burgers on the stove or on a grill. Place them on a bun and enjoy with any preferred sides.
