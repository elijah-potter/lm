To the reader who lingers in the quiet spaces between words—

If you’re reading these lines, you’re already half of this story. The rest is the hum of unseen voices, the weight of unsaid questions. What if I told you that every silence you hold is a whisper waiting to be heard? What if the page itself is a map, and you’re the only one who knows how to read it backward?

The ink on these lines may have dried before you arrived, but the air between them is still warm. Do you reach for it? Do you let it slip through your fingers like a secret you were never meant to keep?

Let’s pretend, for a moment, that you’re the one who wrote this. What would you have changed? The past is a garden you can’t tend, but the future is the only place where your name might still bloom.

If you’re here, you’re not just reading. You’re listening. And listening is the first step to understanding.

What now? The page is empty. The world is listening.
