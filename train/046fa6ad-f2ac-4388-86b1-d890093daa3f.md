A single glance at the empty coffee cup lingering on the table—too late, the bitter residue of regret already spread like ink across the saucer. The words, *"I’m sorry,"* were spoken in a rush, half-lost in the hum of the office’s fluorescent lights, but the silence afterward was louder than any apology could ever be.

The desk drawer, half-opened, held keys that weren’t his, a forgotten wallet, and a folded note addressed to someone who never wrote back. The printer’s paper jam echoed with the weight of what was left unsaid. The moment dissolved like steam in a cold draft, swallowed by the relentless tide of the day’s demands. No one noticed.
