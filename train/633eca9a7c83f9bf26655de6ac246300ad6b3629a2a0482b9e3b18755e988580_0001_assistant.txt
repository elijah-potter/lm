In the world of mental health and wellness, phobias are considered a kind of anxiety disorder that can cause someone to have an extreme fear of places, objects, animals, people, situations, and things. The fears are often irrational and difficult to explain, but exposure to the subject of a phobia can cause severe reactions, like the urge to run away or lash out violently. 

Some phobias are more widely accepted and well-known than others. Arachnophobia, for instance, is the extreme fear of spiders, and it affects up to 15 percent of the world’s population. Other common phobias include ophidiophobia (the fear of snakes), acrophobia (the fear of heights), and cynophobia (the fear of dogs). 

Some phobias, while uncommon, are still real and scary to the people that suffer from them. Five lesser-known phobias include: 

•	Alektorophobia (the fear of chickens). 
•	Globophobia (the fear of balloons). 
•	Podophobia (the fear of feet). 
•	Pedophobia (the fear of small children)
•	Phobophobia (the fear of fear) 

While some phobias may seem strange or silly to other people, they are often a source of extreme anxiety and insecurity for sufferers. The cause of each phobia can vary depending on the person. While some may have had negative experiences that caused extreme fear, others could associate their general anxiety disorder with a specific phobia because their brain latches onto that fear at the moment. 

Through psychotherapy and consistent behavioral therapy, most phobias can be either completely cured or lessened. The sufferer has to be dedicated to finding treatment and healing from their fears. There’s no linear method of treatment since every phobia sufferer is different and has different reasons for the fear that they feel. 

In most cases, exposure therapy is a method of overcoming any phobia. This means that a sufferer has to get comfortable with the object of their fear before they can heal and overcome their phobia. In other cases, anxiety medications can help lessen the extreme fear and visceral reactions associated with certain phobias.
