The **ruins of Pompeii**, preserved by volcanic ash in AD 79, reveal a city frozen in time. Streets, frescoes, and frescoed walls (like the **House of the Vettii** with its grand mosaic ceiling) depict daily life, including frescoes of gods, myths, and erotic scenes. The **Forum** and **Theatre of Pompeii** showcase Roman architecture, while household artifacts (vases, amphorae, frescoed walls) provide insights into Roman social, religious, and culinary customs. The **Lupanar** (brothel) and **Thermal Baths** offer glimpses into commerce, entertainment, and health practices.

**Key sites:**
- **Villa of the Mysteries** (ancient religious ceremonies)
- **Capitoline Wolf** (cult statue)
- **Fountain of the Tritons** (nautical iconography)
- **Temple of Apollo** (ancient worship)

The **Herculaneum** excavation (nearby) offers similarly preserved frescoes and artifacts, though less dramatically exposed due to its smaller scale.
