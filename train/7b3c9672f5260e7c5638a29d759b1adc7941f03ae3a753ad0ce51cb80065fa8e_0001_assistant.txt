Life, how difficult you can be!
Wish you could be a fun adventure.
Wish there was more laughter and joy. 

Why must you be so hard?
What is your secret, Life?
Share with me the reasons.

Tell me what you want, Life.
What would appease you?

I pray you will consider
Changing your ways, Life.

Become much sweeter 
And more divine
And I will always love you.
We could become best friends.
