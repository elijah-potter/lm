Maria and Joseph had been on the phone with Christie for about 20 minutes now, and it finally felt like they were making some headway in the conversation. Christie had just lost her first job and she wasn't taking it well. Luckily she had finally stopped crying and got to a place where she was able to hear Maria and Joseph out.

"It's okay to feel everything you're feeling right now, it's a very normal part of the human experience. We've all been in your position before," Maria said to help Christie gain some perspective on the matter.

As Christie's tears subsided even more, Joseph interjected, "You're still better off than most people. Sure this isn't an ideal situation but you live to keep fighting another day. If you want, as soon as I get home I can sit down with you and we can work on your resume together."

As Christie sniffled, you could tell that his words brought her some sense of immediate comfort. "Thank you, guys, for staying on the phone with me and hearing me out," Christie said as she tried to compose herself.

"That's what we're here for, you know we love you," Maria said through the phone with a smile.
