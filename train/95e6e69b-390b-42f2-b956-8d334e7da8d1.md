The dim glow of a flickering lamp cast long shadows across the cracked floor. Outside, a light rain pattered against the old windows. Clara sat rigid in the armchair, fingers tracing the rim of her coffee cup. Her phone buzzed repeatedly—no messages, just persistent notifications.

A knock at the door startled her. She didn’t answer, but the sound of footsteps faded as someone opened the screen door. A single figure stood silhouetted in the doorway, holding a newspaper.

The headline caught her eye: **"Local Firefighter Missing After Rescue Attempt"**. Clara’s breath hitched. She snatched the paper, her pulse roaring. The reporter’s name was printed in small print at the bottom—*Daniel Mercer*.

She read the paragraph aloud, voice trembling: *"Mercer, 42, was last seen assisting with a call at 03:17. Search teams confirmed he was missing by 04:02. Authorities urge families to remain vigilant."* The ink on the page blurred as she pressed it to her forehead.

A door slammed upstairs. Footsteps thudded down the stairs. Clara’s phone buzzed again—this time, a call from her sister. She ignored it, clutching the paper. The scent of smoke lingered in the air, thick and acrid. Somewhere, a siren wailed in the distance.
