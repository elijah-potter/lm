In a classroom there are 30 students. 1/3 of them are boys. Among the girls, 40% own dogs, 20% own a cat, and the rest have no pets. How many girls in the class have no pet?
2/3 students are girls because 1 - (1/3) = 2/3
20 students are girls because 30 x (2/3) = <<30*(2/3)=20>>20
40% of the girls don't have any pets because 100 - 40 - 20 = <<100-40-20=40>>40
8 girls in class have no pets because 20 x .4 = <<20*.4=8>>8
