
1. The Chelyabinsk Event - In 2013, this asteroid came into the Earth's atmosphere over the Russian city of Chelyabinsk. Thankfully, the asteroid exploded while it was 30 kilometers above the ground, and any direct impact damage was avoided. However, the explosion released the same amount of energy as 500 kilotons of TNT. Most of the injuries caused by this asteroid were caused by the shockwave from the explosion, which injured around 1,500 people and damaged 7,200 buildings.

2. The Tunguska Event - In the year 1908, an asteroid or comet that was around 30 meters in diameter exploded above the ground in Tunguska, also in Russia. The explosion was so intense, that it knocked down close to 80 million trees that covered an area of 2,150 square kilometers. To put things in perspective, the explosion was so powerful, it's said to have been 1,000 times more powerful than the atomic bomb at Hiroshima. Luckily, the explosion did not happen in a city; otherwise, the city would have been destroyed.

The Chicxulub Event - The biggest asteroid to ever hit Earth was located in what is modern-day Mexico. 65 million years ago an asteroid between the size of 10 to 15 kilometers struck Earth and effectively wiped out 70% of all species on Earth, notably the dinosaurs. The asteroid was responsible for creating mega-tsunamis that reached southeastern Mexico all the way to Texas and Florida.
