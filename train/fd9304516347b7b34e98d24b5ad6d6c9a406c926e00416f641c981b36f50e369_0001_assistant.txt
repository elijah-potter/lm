The tribal CHIEFS all arrived at the ordained spot at sunrise. Although they normally were at war with each other, this meeting would be peaceful. They were there to discuss uniting against the VIKINGS who had wreaked havoc on their villages. These RAIDERS and pillagers had plunged the land into absolute chaos. 

The eldest chief began to speak, but a strange sound in the distance distracted the men. It sounded like chanting, and it was getting closer. Suddenly, they spotted the source. It was a parade of townspeople marching across the field. Some of them carried wooden statues of SAINTS on their shoulders. Others waved banners. The tribesmen were on edge, but they stood their ground as the townspeople approached. Finally, one chief yelled out, "Who are you? What do you want with us?" The townspeople stopped chanting but continued to walk forward until the two groups came face-to-face. One of the townspeople introduced himself: "I am the mayor of the nearby town. We are here to join you." 

The tribesmen were puzzled. The eldest spoke on their behalf. "Our tribes live in camps in the highlands. We have never been welcome in your town. Why do you wish to join us?" The mayor replied, "We know why you are meeting here. You wish to ally to face the invaders. These GIANTS from foreign lands can't be defeated alone. So you wish to join forces to battle them. We wish to do the same. Let us all work together to defend our homeland." 

The tribesmen looked at each other and nodded their assent. The eldest then responded, "You speak wisely. Now is a time to put differences aside. We shall all fight together as one." Thus, the alliance was born.
