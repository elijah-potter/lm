A name whispered in a language that shifts with every passing year, like echoes of a song played on an instrument no longer in existence. The taste of something sweet, perhaps a berry, or the scent of a garden that bloomed in another time, now only a memory of memories. Shadows of faces, blurred by time and the passage of unseen events, yet distinct enough to haunt the edges of awareness.

It exists in the spaces between thoughts, the pauses where logic falters, the moments when the mind drifts like a leaf in a river of forgotten things. A song playing on an old radio, a promise made to someone who is no longer alive, the sound of laughter that stopped too soon. The almost remembered is the ghost of what was, the whisper of a story that only exists in the cracks between waking and dreaming. It is the thing that is just beyond grasp, yet always within reach, like a hand stretched out of a dream to grab something that will never quite materialize.
