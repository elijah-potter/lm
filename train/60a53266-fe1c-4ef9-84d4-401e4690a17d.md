**Daily Routine: Workday Schedule**

---
**7:00 AM** – Wake up, morning hygiene, light breakfast
**7:30 AM** – Exercise/yoga/stretching routine
**8:00 AM** – Shower, get dressed
**8:15 AM** – Commute to work (or remote setup)
**9:00 AM – 12:00 PM** – **Core Work Block 1** (Deep focus: projects, meetings)
**12:00 PM** – Lunch break (30 min)
**12:30 PM – 2:30 PM** – **Core Work Block 2** (Collaborative tasks, emails)
**2:30 PM** – Short break (snack, walk)
**2:45 PM – 5:00 PM** – **Flexible Work Block** (admin, planning, creative work)
**5:00 PM** – Commute home (or break)
**5:30 PM – 6:30 PM** – Dinner prep/cooking
**6:30 PM – 8:00 PM** – **Evening Work** (if needed; light tasks, planning next day)
**8:00 PM** – Wind down (reading, hobby, or relaxation)
**9:00 PM – 10:00 PM** – Sleep routine (read, meditate, no screens)

---
**Weekly Adjustments:**
- **Weekends:** Flexible—light admin, family time, or creative projects.
- **Weeknights:** Prioritize sleep (7–8 hours). Adjust work hours if needed.
- **Weekends:** Longer breaks; prioritize rest and social time.

---
**Notes:**
- Adjust times based on personal energy peaks.
- Batch similar tasks (e.g., emails in one block).
- Use alarms/reminders for transitions.
