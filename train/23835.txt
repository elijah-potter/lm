Teaching High School Mathematics; First Course; Advent of Awareness
This film addresses the perception many instructors hold that if students cannot express the answer they do not know the answer.
The narrator introduces Beberman's idea of the Advent of Awareness. He uses examples from previous films, as well as the training methods of seeing-eye guide dogs. Black and white picture
with sound. Eastman Kodak edge code reads "triangle square," which correlates to 1964.
University of Illinois Committee on School Mathematics (producer)
