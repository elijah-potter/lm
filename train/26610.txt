Welcome to the home of the Humanitarian Organization for Poverty Elimination – or HOPE Canada!
What exactly does HOPE mean?
It means giving those in need a reason to believe they can become self-sufficient and independent. It means providing orphaned children with homes and education, so they can grow up knowing they have a future. It means providing care to the aged, and medical help to those who are ill. And it means people who live in a state of plenty,recognizing their interconnectedness with others in the world, giving a hand to help the less fortunate live in dignity and independence.
What does HOPE mean for you?
It means that you believe in the right of every human being to live a life free from poverty. It means that you have a profound opportunity to give hope to others who may have nothing at all.
Your compassion and generosity can make a direct difference in the life of someone half a world away. Join with us in recognizing the needs of our fellow citizens of the world, and exploring ways to bring this hope to people in poverty everywhere.
