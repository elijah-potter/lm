Yes, you can paint your curbside mailbox. Currently, the USPS allows mailboxes to be painted any color and offers no restrictions regarding the color of the box. One regulation regarding the color of your mailbox has to do with the carrier flag. The carrier flag must be painted in a contrasting color to the mailbox and cannot be painted green, brown, blue, yellow, or white because they may blend into the surroundings more easily and cause the mail carrier to miss it. If you live in an area with an HOA (homeowner's association) you may need to check with them to make sure you don't violate any of their regulations.
