Okay, here is a list of unique ice cream flavor ideas:

1. French Fry: French fry ice cream blends a sweet treat with a salty flavor for fans of dipping fries in milkshakes.
2. Chili Chocolate: Although you won't see this flavor offered very often, it's a great flavor combo that brings spicy and sweet tastes together.
3. Balsamic Vinaigrette: This flavor is completely unique when it comes to the dessert world because it's typically an appetizer or snack flavor.
4. Jalapeno: Spice is a great flavor combination when it comes to sweet foods, and you aren't likely to find this flavor at an ice cream parlor. 
5. Sea Salt: Sea salt is an uncommon ice cream flavor that won't overpower the sweetness of the dish.
6. Maple Bacon: This unlikely ice cream flavor combines two delicious breakfast flavors into ice cream. 
7. Chicken and Waffles: Chicken and waffles is a popular sweet and savory dish, so turning it into a unique ice cream flavor is an obvious choice. 
8. Everything Bagel: This popular blend of seasonings would make a very unique ice cream flavor that likely hasn't been done before. 
9. Turmeric: Turmeric root is a colorful yellow that would make a unique-looking (and tasting) ice cream.
