Emergency & Crisis Management
Institutions and / or individuals are not exempt from a crisis, yet there is no worse scenario than not being properly prepared before a critical situation...
Phases of a Crisis Management Comprehensive Project:
➣ Diagnosis & mapping Risks identification
➣ Definition of the responsible committee
➣ Determination of the operational concept
➣ Creation of working groups
➣ Creation of data-bases
➣ Emergency Plan preparation
➣ Control & supervision
Despite the preventive and security measures taken by the communities/countries and organizations, an incident may occur that can create an emergency situation. People who are tested during a crisis often make costly mistakes because they do not have sufficient tools to address the situation.
For this reason many organizations have implemented a crisis plan so they may have the greatest chances of survival and recovery from such an event.
