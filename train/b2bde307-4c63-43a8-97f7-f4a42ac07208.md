To the reader who steps into the pages of this unknown world,

The ink on these lines is cold, yet the echoes within them hum with stories unsaid. You stand at the threshold of this silence, and I am but the whisper of a voice left behind to guide you. What are you seeking here? The unraveling of a mystery, the weight of a forgotten truth, or perhaps the quiet curiosity of a mind that lingers over words like a shadow?

The road ahead is not marked, but every step you take here will leave its own trace—some visible, some hidden in the margins of memory. The world I describe is yours to shape, though it is shaped by what you choose to listen to, to believe, or to ignore.

Tell me, if you dare: Are you here to learn, or are you here to teach the tale to me? The answer will decide the shape of this conversation.
