1. Yesterday 

Although I must say, 
Yesterday seems far away,
The memories stay.

2. I want to hold your hand

I cannot stay mad,
For I want to hold your hand,
It makes me so glad.

A day in the life,
Always something new to see,
The beauty of life.

4. While my guitar gently weeps

While my guitar weeps,
My thoughts peacefully asleep,
Each note will bring tears.

5. Come together

Together as one,
All problems are overcome,
Together as one.

6. Something

Something in the way,
I cannot truly describe,
The way I love her.

I do not mean to sound rude,
Your beauty rings true.

8. Let it be

When troubles arise,
You must let the stress break free,
Always let it be.

9. In my life

A first time for me, 
I have found something so sweet,
Described as true love.

10. Strawberry fields forever

Often I daydream,
Strawberry fields forever,
Oh, I wish to be.
