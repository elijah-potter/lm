Louis Larson is forty-eight years old. She is 5'7", athletically built, and is often described as very beautiful despite her age. She hates the 'despite her age' comment. She has naturally dark hair and keeps it that way by going to the hairdresser twice a month to keep any hint of gray from showing. She exercises at a gym four to five times a week for the social aspect and the health benefit.

Two years ago, she started dating but has had a series of unsuccessful and often tumultuous relationships. After years of being married to a stable and very predictable partner, she's found herself increasingly attracted to men who are anything but stable. Alcohol is starting to have an outsized influence on her and her personal life.
She has lived next door to Monica Rhodes since the latter moved in fifteen years ago. They are close friends and confidants, and they rely on one another for guidance and support.
