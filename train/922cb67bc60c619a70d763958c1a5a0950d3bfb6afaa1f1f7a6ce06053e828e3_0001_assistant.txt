Ext. Forest - Night-Time.

In a thick, densely packed forest, Nathan and Mario are running blindly, very clearly being chased by something.

Nathan: (panting) Move, move! 

Nathan leaps over a fallen log, Mario trips and lands heavily on the forest floor. He gasps, scrambling to get to his feet. The creature chasing them is getting closer and closer. Nathan stops and doubles back to help his friend.

Nathan: Mario, we don't have time for this! 

Mario and Nathan get to their feet. Mario is limping very badly, but Nathan stays with him, supporting his weight. The creature is drawing even closer.

Nathan: No, no, no! 

Mario: Just leave me here, get out! Save yourself, you idiot!

Nathan: I'm not leaving you behind! 

Mario: We'll both die if you don't! Leave me here and get back home and tell everyone what happened. (Tears are streaming down Mario's scratched face). Tell my mom I loved her. 

Before Nathan can make a decision, a terrible roar sounds. The surrounding foliage shakes and rustles, from which steps out a formidable shadow. Its features are obscured in the darkness, and the only thing visible are four, piercing black eyes. Both boys scream, and the sound of bones breaking is heard as the monster presumably captures its prey and the scene ends.
