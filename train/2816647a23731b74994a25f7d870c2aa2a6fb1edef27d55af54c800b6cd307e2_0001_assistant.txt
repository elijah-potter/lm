Sure! Here are some unusual bagel ideas:

Goat cheese bagel: You may have tried cheddar bagels or asiago bagels, but goat cheese would taste even stronger.

Fruity Pebble bagel: if you love the cereal, why not duplicate the flavor in your bagel? Crush up just enough fruity pebbles to add some flavor to your flour.

Mint bagel: If you like mint gum and toothpaste, you might enjoy a mint bagel. Add a little bit of mint extract to your dough for a refreshing flavor.
