A public notice board in a shared community space, such as:
- **Library reading rooms** (common in public or academic libraries)
- **University dormitory halls**
- **Community centers or town squares**
- **Local cafes or co-working spaces** (often near seating or kiosks)
- **School cafeterias or common areas**
- **A designated postcard or greeting card station in parks or plazas**

Alternatively, **digital equivalents** like:
- **Shared physical bulletin boards** (e.g., "Notes Wall" in a workspace)
- **Whiteboards in public restrooms**
- **Digital note apps** (e.g., shared Slack channels, shared Google Docs, or physical QR codes linking to a cloud-based note system)
