In the quiet coastal town of **Marina Haven**, residents were left stunned this morning as a rare meteor shower graced the morning sky at dawn. Witnesses described a brilliant display of green and purple streaks, with the largest fragment reportedly landing near the abandoned fishing village of **Vernal Cove**, a few kilometers west of town. Local officials have activated the emergency response team, and divers are already investigating the impact site. Marina Haven’s mayor, Eleanor Whitmore, urged residents to remain vigilant and avoid the shoreline until further notice.

Meanwhile, the nearby fishing port reported no immediate damage, though a few boats near Vernal Cove sustained minor scrapes. The town’s historical records suggest the area was once a hotspot for meteorite finds, but this remains the first confirmed sighting in decades. Scientists from the regional **Atlantic Research Institute** are expected to arrive by afternoon to conduct preliminary analyses. The mayor also noted that the event aligns with predictions from the town’s local astronomer, Dr. Elias Voss, who had warned of a potential "sky anomaly" last month. Authorities are encouraging residents to report any further sightings or fragments to the **Marina Haven Public Safety Dispatch** at 9-1-1.
