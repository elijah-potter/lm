The river sang its lullaby of silver and stone,
a melody woven through the cracks of time.
It whispered *"again"* to the willow’s swaying dream,
as the moon bent its light to the sapphire dream.

The wind, a thief of forgotten things,
carried the word *"again"* back to the night’s embrace,
where the stars, blind poets of the void,
danced in a loop of endless grace.

The sea hummed its refrain of salt and sand,
*"again"* was the tide’s eternal command.
The clock struck twelve, yet dawn did not come—
only the echo of *"again"* in every tomb.
