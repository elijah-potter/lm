Once upon a time, in a cozy green house with a big red door, lived a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop, jump, and play. Every morning, Benny would wake up, stretch his tiny paws, and say, "Today is my favorite day!"

Benny’s favorite place was the sunny meadow. One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside and saw a big, shiny mushroom growing near the berry bush. "Mmm, looks tasty!" Benny thought and picked it up. But just as he took a bite, a tiny bee buzzed around the mushroom and said, "Don’t eat that, Benny! It’s not safe!"

Benny’s ears twitched. "Why not?" he asked.

The bee said, "That mushroom can make you very, very sleepy. You’ll sleep for days and days, and you’ll never play again!"

Benny’s eyes got big. "Oh no! I don’t want to sleep all day!" he said, and he put the mushroom down.

Just then, Benny’s best friend, a little squirrel named Sammy, hopped over. "Benny, what’s wrong?" Sammy asked.

Benny told him about the mushroom. Sammy said, "You know what? It’s better to be safe than sorry! Let’s find another snack."

They searched and found a big, juicy strawberry and a handful of crunchy blueberries. Benny and Sammy ate their snacks and played all afternoon, hopping, chasing leaves, and laughing together.

As the sun started to set, Benny and Sammy said their goodbyes. "See you tomorrow, Benny!" Sammy called as he scurried away.

Benny waved and went back inside. As he lay down on his cozy bed, he thought, "Today was fun, and I’ll remember to be careful like Sammy did."

That night, Benny fell asleep happily, dreaming of more adventures in the meadow.
