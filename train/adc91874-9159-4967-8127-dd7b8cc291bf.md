Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big tree in the backyard, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had soft pink ears and a fluffy tail that wiggled when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his best friend, a little blue bird named Bertie, sitting on a branch. "Bertie, it's time for our adventure!" Benny said with a big smile. Bertie chirped happily, "Let's go!"

They hopped to the garden, where Benny found a juicy dandelion. "This is the best snack ever!" Benny cheered. Bertie fluttered over, pecked at a tiny bug, and they both giggled.

Suddenly, Bertie's wing got stuck in a thorny bush. "Oh no! Benny, help me!" Bertie cried. Benny thought hard. He remembered his mom saying, "Kindness grows stronger." So, Benny hopped over to the bush and gently pulled Bertie free.

"Thank you, Benny!" Bertie said, flying away happily. From that day on, Benny and Bertie became even closer, always helping each other and exploring together. And they lived very happily ever after, with lots of fun and friends.
