**Waiting Area (Indoors):**
A dimly lit lobby with muted fluorescent lighting casts long shadows. A lone desk with a half-empty coffee mug and a stack of unread newspapers sits near a window, framed by blinds partially drawn. A sign above the desk reads **"Please Wait"**, though it’s faded, barely legible.

A man in a worn blazer stands near a wall-mounted clock, its hands frozen at **5:27**. His fingers trace the edge of a cracked coffee table beside him. Outside, muted city noise—siren wails and distant traffic—drift through the open window.

A woman in a sleek black coat sits on a bench, her legs crossed, phone held between her fingers. A single cigarette burns in an ashtray beside her, smoke curling lazily into the air. She glances at a half-empty water bottle labeled **"2024"** on the table.

A door creaks open. Footsteps echo—slow, deliberate. The woman stands abruptly, her coat brushing the bench. The man turns, his expression unreadable as he steps forward, his gaze fixed on the door.

Inside, the waiting room’s silence is broken by the distant murmur of voices. A woman in a high-neck blouse steps out, holding a folder. Her coat sleeves are rolled up, revealing a tattoo—a small, dark symbol on her wrist. She walks past the desk, her stride confident, but her eyes flick toward the waiting pair.

The sign above the desk now reads **"Waiting Area"**.
