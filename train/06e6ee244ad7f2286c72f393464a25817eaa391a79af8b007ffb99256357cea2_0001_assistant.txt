A number of research projects have been completed concerning products like white flour, breakfast cereals, white bread, and other products made from refined grains. The results are overwhelming. Refined grains cause illness and disease and contribute greatly to America's poor health. And yet, they make up one-third of our total carbohydrate intake each and every day. 

Refined grains are made by processing a natural whole grain. During this process, many of the nutrients are lost. The products we all use every day are refined. This includes bread products, noodles, white rice, flour, cereal, and pastries—almost all grain products have been refined in some way or another. Anytime foods are processed and refined in any manner, the results are a product that falls short of delivering proper nutrition.

If it’s white and in a box on your grocer’s shelf, it has probably been processed to the point of removing all nutritional value. As remarkable as the human body is, it will eventually be unable to break down the ill effects of processed and refined foods. Our bodies will at some point, respond in a negative manner.
