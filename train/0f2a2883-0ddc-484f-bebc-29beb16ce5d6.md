Dark alleyway between neon-lit buildings, flickering reflections on wet pavement from a distant storm. The air hums with the distant wail of sirens, muted by the rusted metal of a crumbling dumpster. A lone figure crouches under a flickering streetlight, fingers tracing the edge of a cracked sidewalk tile, half-hidden by a tattered newspaper. The cobblestones shift underfoot—one step too close, another too far. Shadows stretch long and jagged across the pavement, swallowing details whole. The only sound is the occasional drip of water from a leaky pipe, a whisper of wind through the rusted iron of a half-closed shutter. The weight of the past presses down, but the future is a blur, indistinct, waiting to be defined.
