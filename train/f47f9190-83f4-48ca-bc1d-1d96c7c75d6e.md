A rule mandates that all employees must wear lab coats in the break room, despite no actual scientific need. This tradition originated from an incident in the 1990s when a coworker accidentally spilled coffee on a client's suit. The rule was adopted to maintain a sterile appearance, but the coffee stain was superficial. Now, lab coats are worn out of habit, fear of judgment, or a misguided attempt to "keep the space professional," even though they are unnecessary. Employees comply because the alternative—removing them—could spark confusion or ridicule, reinforcing the rule’s persistence despite its absurdity.
