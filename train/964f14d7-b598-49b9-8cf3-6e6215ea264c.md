**The Clockmaker’s Last Secret**

In the quiet town of Eldermere, where the cobblestones whispered secrets and the river hummed lullabies, there lived a man named Elias Veyne. A master clockmaker, his workshop was a maze of gears and pendulums, each one a silent guardian of time. But Elias had a secret—one that had been waiting for him since he was a boy.

The *Last Hourglass*.

It was said that if you held the hourglass that never ran out, you could rewrite fate itself. But the hourglass was cursed. Every time someone used it, the past unraveled, and the future flickered like a candle in the wind. Elias had spent his life searching for it, only to find it in the hands of the very people who would misuse it.

Tonight, as the town’s last watchman, Elias stood before the great clock tower, its face cracked like glass. The hourglass sat on the pedestal, suspended in time. The choice was his: the town’s fate, the weight of his own soul, or the void between.

What will you do?

---

**Options:**
1. **Take the hourglass and use it immediately** – Rewriting history could save the town, but what will the cost be?
2. **Destroy it** – The risk of unintended consequences is too great; let fate play out as it should.
3. **Give it to the town’s elder** – She knows how to use it wisely, but trust is fragile.
4. **Leave it here forever** – The hourglass will never run out, but the town will never change either.
5. **Seek the help of the Clockmaker’s apprentice** – A young girl who once whispered to the gears might know how to balance it.
6. **Jump into the river** – Some say the water here absorbs secrets. The hourglass will vanish, but so will Elias’s decision.

*(Choose wisely. The hourglass does not forgive.)*
