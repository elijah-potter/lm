In Maths, when two whole numbers are added, the outcome is called addition or summation. During the addition of numbers, there are two possible ways that can be used to solve. One is for getting the actual addition of numbers and the other is the estimated sum.
If we find the exact addition then it is called the actual addition. The estimated sum means the addition of given numbers by rounding off to the nearest ten, hundred, and thousand places. The result of given whole numbers using the Estimation of Sum method won't give the exact summation, but it gives close value to the exact sum.
For example, Sum of 37 and 58. The actual addition is 37+58 = 95. The estimated sum is 40+60 = 100.
