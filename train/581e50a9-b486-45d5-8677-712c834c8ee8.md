A lone figure stumbles out of the mist near the abandoned factory at dawn, their breath fogging the cold air. They move with a jerky, unsteady gait, clutching a rusted metal can in one hand, the other gripping a frayed leather strap. The crowd in the plaza below, mostly obscured by the city’s towering skyscrapers, turns—eyes flickering, murmurs rising—before the figure vanishes into the crowd, blending with the mass of strangers. No one stops, no one recognizes the hollow-eyed stranger, their face half-hidden beneath a tattered scarf, their hands trembling with the weight of something unseen. The moment they disappear, the crowd falls silent again, as if a ghost has passed through.
