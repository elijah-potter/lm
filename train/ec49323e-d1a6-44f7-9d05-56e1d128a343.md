A **metal or wooden door** with **glass panels** or **hardwood panels** featuring **hinges** on the left/right side, a **doorknob** or **lever handle**, and **strike plate** for latching.

Separates an **interior space** (e.g., bedroom, office) from an **exterior or common area** (hallway, street, parking lot). May include **security features** (lock, deadbolt, peephole) and **insulation** for temperature regulation.
