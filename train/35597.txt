Frida Kahlo was born #onthisday in 1907. She is one of most internationally known Mexican painters. Throughout her life she suffered from many difficult events. She become permanently disabled after a bus accident. Before her accident she had not thought of becoming a painter, but while recovering she began to paint. With no former training her talent and vision is what we remember today.
