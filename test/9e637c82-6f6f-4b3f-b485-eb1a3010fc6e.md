**Mayor’s Statement on Major Infrastructure Upgrade Project**
The city council has announced the completion of the **Central Bridge Overhaul**, a critical $24.7M renovation project. The newly reinforced pedestrian and vehicle pathways, including emergency access routes, will reduce congestion on Route 32 by 30%. Residents are reminded to check for construction zones—new signage will be installed within 48 hours. Public transit along the route will temporarily reroute to temporary stops beginning tomorrow.

**Environmental Impact Brief**
A **20% reduction in carbon emissions** from the bridge’s new solar-powered lighting system and rainwater collection infrastructure has been verified by the Environmental Agency. The project also includes a **3,500-square-foot community garden** near the site, funded by a $500K grant from the Regional Sustainability Fund.

**Community Safety Reminder**
Authorities are urging residents to report suspicious activity near the bridge by calling 211. The **police department** has deployed extra patrols to monitor for unauthorized construction and littering. No new permits have been issued for overnight encampments near the project site.

**Upcoming Events**
- **Public Hearing:** Tomorrow at 6 PM, City Hall, to discuss feedback on traffic rerouting plans.
- **Free Community Cleanup:** Saturday, 10 AM–1 PM, at the old bridge site—volunteers welcome.
