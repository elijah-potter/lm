

The story of a woman who finally finds everything she never knew she was missing. Samantha had just turned 60 and realized that she had lived her life in complete fear. Fear of the unknown, of anything out of her control. She had lived exactly as she thought she should, she had an important career, a wonderful marriage, and she even raised two children who now had children of their own. But something was missing: she didn’t feel alive. Samantha didn’t know who she really was. But she was about to find out. 

Within these pages, Samantha finds more than she bargained for when she goes off to see the world. Leaving her comfort zone and her fears behind was the hardest thing Samantha would ever do, but who she finds along the way is worth every challenge she faces.
