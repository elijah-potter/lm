She looked again, wondering if it could be a bag of trash. That had to be it, someone threw a bag of trash outside their car window, that’s all. Then, she saw something moving. She felt a horrible feeling in the pit of her stomach, and tears sprung to her eyes. She must have hit a dog. With a heavy heart, Kat stepped out of the car and walked around to the front. Lying in front of the BMW was a man in a white lab coat, streaked with blood. He looked to be about her age, but it was hard to tell given his condition. His skin was bruised in multiple places, both eyes were swollen shut, and he had marks around his neck. “Hello, can you hear me?” she asked. No answer. Her imagination was running wild, but she knew she couldn’t have done this. The man had been badly beaten. Hesitantly, Kat put two fingers against the man’s neck. “Please be alive. Please be alive. Please be alive.” There was a pulse, Thank God. 

Kat went back to the car, got her cell phone, and called 911. They asked her a few questions, and she went to her trunk to get a blanket to cover him until the paramedics arrived. She felt a sense of relief as they pulled up. They asked her some more questions as they began to assess the injuries, checking the man’s pulse (weak) and blood pressure (low). Kat explained to the paramedics that she didn't know the man and proceeded to explain how she had found him. "I knew I hadn't hit anything," said Kate "but I saw something in front of my windshield when I looked up. I thought I had hit a dog." Then Kate started crying. This was all way too much, and she could feel her anxiety ramping up again. "Just breathe" she repeated to herself "Just breathe".
