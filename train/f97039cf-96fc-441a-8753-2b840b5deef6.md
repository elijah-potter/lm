A winding trail through dense, thorned undergrowth, flanked by crumbling stone walls and overgrown hedges. The dirt path narrows into a dead-end tunnel of twisted roots, sunlight barely filtering through fractured canopy above. Moss-covered stones mark faded footprints, converging and vanishing into the mist. A rusted metal sign, half-hidden by ivy, reads: *"Turn here to find the old mill."* The air hums with distant cicadas, their echoes lost in the hollow echo chamber of the abandoned quarry beyond. A single, cracked lantern lies on its side, its wick guttered out, casting long, feeble shadows on the damp, moss-laden floor.
