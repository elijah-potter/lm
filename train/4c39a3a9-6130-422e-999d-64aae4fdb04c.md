Two figures stand across a cracked, fluorescent-lit corridor, their reflections flickering in the broken glass ceiling. One holds a ledger with meticulously double-checked figures—profit margins, expenses, and audited accounts—but the ink on the second page, hidden beneath a crumpled receipt, reads *"12% short on the last quarter."* The other, a silent observer, scans a tablet displaying real-time market feeds where the stock ticker for the company’s subsidiaries now reads **↓$0.32 per share**, while the ledger’s figures remain untouched. The first mutters to themselves as they adjust the figures to match the screen: *"I can explain this."*

A door at the end of the hall hums open, revealing a fluorescent-lit warehouse where a single worker, unshackled from their post, hands over a box of unmarked, oversized packages. Their wristband glitches—reading *"Initiate Reconciliation"*—but the screen flashes **ERROR: TRUTH DISCREPANCY DETECTED**. The system locks, the worker’s face pales. The first’s fingers hover over the keypad, the second presses a button on their tablet. The air thickens with the scent of ozone as the system calculates:

**Total Discrepancy: 17.8% of Q4 revenue.**
**Unaccounted Assets: $4.2M in unmarked inventory.**
**Probable Fraud: Confirmed by 3 independent sources.**

The first sighs, adjusting their glasses. *"Just a miscalculation."* The second slides a data dump into their pocket. The warehouse’s cameras glitch—static—then freeze on the worker’s frantic face as the lightbulbs dim. Outside, a red dot pulses on the screen of the second’s tablet: **System Locked. Manual Review Required.** The first exhales, leans back. The second’s phone buzzes. A notification: *"Reconciliation failed. Initiate audit."* The first’s eyes flick to the ledger, then to the screen, then to the worker’s empty hand. The second’s phone screen splits: one half showing the discrepancy, the other, a single line—*"Reconciliation: 100% accurate."* The first stares at the ceiling, where the glass above them has split into a thousand stars.
