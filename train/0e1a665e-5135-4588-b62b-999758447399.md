**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 21, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this year with a blend of traditional agricultural displays, thrilling entertainment, and family-friendly activities. Visitors can anticipate livestock competitions showcasing the region’s finest animals, a bustling marketplace featuring local artisans and vendors, and a variety of food options ranging from classic fair fare to regional specialties. The fair celebrates the agricultural heritage of Sangamon County and provides a valuable opportunity for community engagement.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from Friday, August 30th, through Sunday, September 1st, 2024. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM. The fairgrounds are located at 2100 Fairgrounds Road, Springfield, Illinois. Daily admission for adults is $15, children ages 6-12 are $8, and children 5 and under are free. Season passes, offering unlimited entry throughout the fair, are available for $35. Advance ticket purchases are recommended and can be made online at [insert website address here].

This year’s fair boasts a compelling schedule of headline events, including:

*   Demolition Derby – Saturday evening
*   Livestock Auction – Sunday afternoon
*   Monster Truck Show – Friday evening
*   4-H Showcase – Saturday morning
*   Blue Ribbon Baking Competition – Sunday morning
*   Professional Rodeo – Friday night
