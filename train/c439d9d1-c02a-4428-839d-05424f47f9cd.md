A hollow echo in the quiet night, voices tangled like mist in a glass.
*"Do you think we’ll ever stop?"* whispered one figure, voice trembling against the cold.
*"Stop what?"* asked another, fingers tracing the cracks in the floor.
*"The words we’re already half-formed,"* they replied, *"the things we’re afraid to say before they’re said."*

A pause. The air smelled of damp wool and old books. Then:
*"Maybe we’re just waiting for the right time to start."*

Silence. The fire crackled. Somewhere, a clock chimed eleven. The first draft of the conversation had begun.
