When gardening, don’t use any broad-spectrum pesticides. These pesticides can kill the useful insects that work as predators to pests. Beneficial bugs are usually several orders more sensitive to the things you spray than the pests you are trying to kill, so you might wind up dropping the good bug populace and open the door to pest population growth. This can cause you to use an additional amount of pesticides in order to attempt to fix this problem.
