In the African Savannah, all the animals knew the lion was their king. He was an incredible hunter, and his mane made him look fierce and majestic. The hyena, however, didn’t think the lion deserved to be their king. So, he went to the lion one day to find out what made him so special.
The hyena asked, “Lion, why do all the animals consider you their king?”
The lion considered his question for a moment and then said, “Because of my roar. It is unlike that of any other animal.”
The hyena seemed unconvinced. “Many animals make amazing sounds, like the trumpet of the elephant or the snort of the wildebeest. In fact, I have the most unique sound of all!”
“Then let’s have a competition,” the lion suggested. “If your call is better, you can be king.”
The hyena wants to be crowned the king of the Savannah. That’s why he quickly breaks out a laugh. It’s certainly one of the most unique sounds in the Serengeti! The lion listened patiently. But after he was done, he let out a roar so loud it shook the ground and shocked all the other animals far away.
The hyena was too stunned to speak, so the lion said, “I am the king because my roar can be heard all across the plains. A king is one whose voice must reach all of his subjects, so they know he is always there for them.”
The hyena bowed to the lion, humbled, and from that day on he accepted and respected the lion as the king of the African Savannah.
