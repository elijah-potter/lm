**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, Ill. – August 21, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair, a beloved tradition celebrating agriculture, community, and family fun, will return to the fairgrounds in Springfield from September 12 through September 21, 2024. This year's fair promises a diverse range of attractions, including livestock exhibits, thrilling carnival rides, live music performances, and a robust selection of food vendors offering classic fair fare and innovative culinary creations. Visitors of all ages will find something to enjoy amidst the festive atmosphere.

The Sangamon County Fair runs for ten days, opening on Thursday, September 12, and concluding on Sunday, September 21. Gates will open at 9:00 AM each day, with evening events extending until 10:00 PM. A special fireworks display will illuminate the sky on Saturday, September 21, marking the fair’s grand finale. Daily admission is $15 for adults, $10 for children ages 5-12, and free for children under five. Season passes, offering unlimited entry throughout the fair’s duration, are available for $60. Tickets can be purchased online at SangamonCountyFair.org or at the gate during the event.

This year’s fair features a particularly compelling schedule of headline events, including:

*   Demolition Derby – Friday, September 13
*   Livestock Auction – Saturday, September 14
*   Tractor Pull – Sunday, September 15
*   Blue Ribbon Baking Competition – Wednesday, September 18
*   Country Music Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters – Friday, September 20
*   Professional Rodeo – Saturday, September 21
