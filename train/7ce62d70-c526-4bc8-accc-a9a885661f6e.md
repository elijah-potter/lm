The air hums thick with damp earth after rain, mixing with the sharp tang of pine needles crunching underfoot. The wind carries a faint, metallic scent from the distant river, where water cascades into a deep pool, bubbling and spluttering. Sunlight filters through the canopy, casting shifting shadows—cricket chirps blend with distant laughter, rustling leaves whisper secrets in the breeze. The scent of damp soil, wildflowers, and woodsmoke lingers, mingling with the scent of burning coal from the distant campfire. The fire crackles, flames dancing, casting flickering light that dances over the mossy rocks and twigs. The scent of smoke, pine, and earth lingers in the air, a warm, inviting embrace.
