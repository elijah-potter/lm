Once upon a time along the edge of the continental shelf, there was an octopus named Ollie who was obsessed with humans. He would watch them longingly from afar as they splashed and played in the ocean. He ached to be a human himself and experience all the magical things they could do.

One day, as Ollie was imagining what it would be like to be human, he stumbled upon an ancient shipwreck. While exploring inside, he found a magical conch shell that granted wishes. Excitedly and without thought, Ollie held the conch shell to his mouth and whispered that he wished to become a human. Suddenly, a bright light filled the sea and surrounded Ollie. He began to feel very strange. He watched his tentacles shrink, and his body transformed into human form. Ollie was thrilled and swam directly to the surface to see what life could be like as a human.

As he reached the shore and climbed out of his watery home, he realized that being a human was not as easy or fun as he thought it would be. Walking on two legs was difficult and he felt heavy, and he felt like he was suffocating. He missed the ocean and life as an octopus. He regretted his wish.

Ollie realized he made a grave mistake and had to reverse the spell and become an octopus once again. He struggled to swim and return to the shipwreck, but he was able to find the conch shell. He held it up to his mouth and wished with every ounce of his being to become an octopus once again. He finally knew what he was destined to be. This time, the light surrounding him was even brighter, and Ollie's body transformed back into its original form. He was so incredibly relieved and happy to be an octopus once again. He swam, twirling through the water with ease, feeling light as a feather. He realized that although humans were fascinating, he was so much happier being himself.

Ollie learned a very important lesson - that it's essential to embrace who you are and your unique qualities. Everyone has something special about them, and these differences make the world a more beautiful place. From that day forward, Ollie had a newfound appreciation for his life as an octopus, knowing that he was just as amazing in his own way.
