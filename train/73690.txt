Edit Using Keyboard Shortcuts
To cut text or graphics, press Ctrl+X. To paste from the clipboard, press Ctrl+V. To highlight text, place the cursor at the beginning of your selection, then hold down the Shift key and use the arrow keys. To navigate through text one word at a time, hold down the Ctrl key and use the arrow keys. To undo any command, press Ctrl+Z.
