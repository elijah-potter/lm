He had waited for this day for so long. The day when the winter would finally end and the spring would begin. He loved the spring when everything came back to life and the world was filled with color and warmth. He loved the spring because it was the season of love.

He had fallen in love with her last autumn when he saw her walking in the park with her golden hair and blue eyes. He had followed her every day, trying to catch her attention, but she never noticed him. She seemed to be lost in her own thoughts, oblivious to his admiration. He had tried to talk to her, to compliment her, to make her laugh, but she always gave him a cold and distant look. She never smiled, never thanked him, never showed any interest in him. She was like a block of ice, frozen and hard.

He hoped that the spring would melt her heart, that she would see him in a different light, that she would finally return his love. He hoped that the spring would bring them together, as it did for so many other creatures. He saw how the birds sang and built their nests, how the bees buzzed and collected their honey, how the flowers bloomed and spread their fragrance. He saw how the lovers strolled and kissed under the trees, how they held hands and whispered sweet words. He wanted to be like them, he wanted to share his joy and passion with her.

But she did not change. She remained cold and aloof, even when the sun shone brightly and the breeze caressed her face. She did not care for the beauty of nature or the happiness of others. She did not care for him. She was like winter in his heart, chilling and dark.

He did not understand why she was so cruel, why she rejected him so harshly. He did not know what he had done wrong, or what he could do better. He did not know what she wanted, what she felt. He only knew that he loved her more than anything else in the world and that he would never give up on her.

He decided to make one last attempt to win her over. He bought her a bouquet of roses, red as his blood and sweet as his dreams. He wrote her a letter, expressing his feelings and begging for her mercy. He waited for her at the park entrance, hoping that she would come and read his words and accept his gift.

But she did not come. She did not show up at all. He waited for hours until the sun set and the stars came out. He waited until he realized that she had forgotten him, that she had ignored him, that she had scorned him.

He felt a surge of anger and pain in his chest. He threw away the roses and tore up the letter. He cursed her name and wished her ill. He swore that he would never love again, that he would hate her forever.

He walked away from the park, leaving behind the spring and its promises. He walked into the night, feeling cold and alone.
