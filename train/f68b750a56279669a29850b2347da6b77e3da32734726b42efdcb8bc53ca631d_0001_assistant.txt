10 Words That Rhyme With Phone: 

1. Groan 
2. Disown 
3. Own
4. Thrown 
5. Grown 
6. Alone 
7. Postpone 
8. Overblown 
9. Known 
10. Prone 

Everyone has a phone
Some have one that are not even grown 

Phones make us feel less alone
Although they also make us postpone

The things we have to do make us groan
We just want to be on our phone

Although the truth is widely known
That, to our phones, we are far too prone

It’s as if all logic has been thrown
And we don’t care the obsession had been overblown

So we continue to own 
And we will never disown
Our beloved phone
