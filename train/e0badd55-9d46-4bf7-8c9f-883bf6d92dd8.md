**Scene 1 – Past (1983):**
The old train station hummed with steam, its iron doors creaking as a young girl, **Lena**, slipped her hand into the pocket of her father’s coat. He adjusted his glasses, muttering about the delayed express. Outside, a pigeon pecked at a discarded newspaper—*The Daily Gazette*—before taking flight.

**Scene 2 – Present (2024):**
A digital archive glows on a tablet. **Dr. Elias Voss**, a historian, zooms in on a faded photo of a girl in a coat matching his grandfather’s. The screen flickers: *"Lena Carter, born 1957."* A voiceover plays: *"She never spoke of her father."*

**Scene 3 – Memory (Flashback):**
Lena’s bedroom. She traces the silhouette of a train on her wall—a gift from her father. The clock ticks: **2:17 AM**. A knock. Her father’s voice: *"Lena, wake up."* She hears it in her dreams now.

---
**Scene 4 – Future (2041):**
A holographic display projects **Elias’s research**—a timeline merging Lena’s past with Elias’s present. A holographic train whistle blows. The station: now a ruin. The girl’s shadow moves toward the camera.
