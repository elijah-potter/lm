In the neon glow of a city that never slept, **Elias** adjusted his gloves with trembling fingers. The door of the abandoned bakery creaked open just as the alarm buzzed—**60 seconds**. Inside, the air smelled of stale coffee and something metallic. A single **candle** flickered in the corner, casting long shadows.

He reached for the counter where a **baguette** sat, still warm. Its crust was cracked, the filling—**jam**—had pooled unevenly. His breath hitched. The last customer had been **three minutes ago**, but the shopkeeper, **Mira**, had vanished. Elias hesitated, then picked up the baguette, pressing it to his lips.

The jam was thick, clinging. He swallowed, then tasted the **alarm clock** on the wall—**59 seconds**. The door **thudded shut**. The clock’s hands froze at **5:45 AM**.

Outside, the streetlights blinked on. Elias exhaled, his fingers tracing the **crack in the wall**. The jam had spread across his mouth, sticky and sweet. The bakery was silent. The city pulsed around him.

At **00:00**, Elias stepped out—**one minute later**. The baguette in his hand was gone. The jam had seeped into his skin, leaving a trail. Somewhere, a siren wailed. He walked past the shop, the last of the city’s glow reflecting in his eyes.

The story ended. The clock still ticked.
