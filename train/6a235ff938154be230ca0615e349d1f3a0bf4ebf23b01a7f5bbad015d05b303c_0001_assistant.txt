"Salomone, come in, come in, don't be shy!"

The man flapped a hand at Solomon, ushering him closer. Annoyed, Salomon approached. The door opened even further and in the middle of the floor of pastels sat a girl in a large, poofy pink dress. 

It seemed plumpness ran in their family because the girl’s cheeks were as round as her father's. Her hair, fine, brown, and curly, were in an unruly heap around her shoulders and when she turned to look at him, the smattering of freckles on her olive skin stood out starkly. Her nose, small and stubby flared open as she smiled and revealed her bunny teeth. Solomon couldn't decide what he thought of her even as she gathered the excess material up into her chubby fists and hurried to her feet. Her brown eyes were big and bright and curved as wide as she smiled. She looked more like a hamster than a human girl.

"Salomone, this is my daughter, Maria. Maria, this is your new friend, Salomone." 

She marched up to Solomon and gave him a halted awkward bow. Solomon's aide cleared his throat when Solomon didn't respond. And with silent resignation, Solomon bowed at the waist, exactly how his tutors had taught him. Maria didn't seem dissuaded by the discomfort in his movements. She just turned those big, brown eyes and took in the specially tailored clothes made for him exactly for this visit.
