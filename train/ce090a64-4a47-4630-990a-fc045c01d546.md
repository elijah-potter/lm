Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a bunny named Benny. Benny had soft brown fur and big, round ears that flopped when he hopped.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and decided to explore the garden. He tiptoed out his door, his tiny paws making soft *clack-clack* sounds. The garden was filled with colorful flowers—pink tulips, yellow daisies, and blue buttercups. Benny picked a daisy and sniffed it. "Mmm, this smells like sunshine!" he said.

A little bird named Birdie chirped from a tree branch. "Hello, Benny! Want to play a game?" Benny nodded excitedly. Birdie taught him to hop in circles, spin around, and chase his own tail. Benny giggled and giggled.

After a while, Benny saw a shiny red bug sitting on a leaf. "Look, Birdie! Is that a bug?" Birdie fluttered her wings. "Yes, Benny! That’s a tiny explorer too!" Benny was so happy he wanted to help the bug find its way home. Together, they hopped and twirled until the bug found its way to a big, cozy ant hill.

As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and orange, Benny curled up in his burrow. He had a wonderful day full of fun and friendship. Benny closed his eyes and dreamed of more adventures in his garden. And that was the end of Benny’s happy story.
