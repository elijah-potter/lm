I have, who has provides a fun, interactive way for students to practice various literacy skills. This set includes 26 cards based on the concept of contractions.
Contractions in this pack - I'm, she'll, let's, who's, didn't, I'll, couldn't, can't, wasn't, that's, you're, he'll, isn't, where's, I've, I'd, he's, shouldn't, she's, hasn't, how's, you'll, there's, don't, it's.
•Timed Version – Have students play the game twice. Encourage them to beat their time the second time they play. Play the following day to try and beat their score. Don’t forget to shuffle the cards before each time you play.
•Small Groups – Give each group a set of cards. You can turn this into a challenge of which group is the fastest etc. Playing this way gives the student the opportunity to play with more cards and reinforces the concept they’re learning.
If you want the cards to be smaller, just print two to a page.
