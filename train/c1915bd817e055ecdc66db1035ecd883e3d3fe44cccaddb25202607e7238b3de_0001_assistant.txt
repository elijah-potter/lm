Brossard is a cheerful human cleric. His long beard, tinged with gray, hangs lightly over his stout violet chest plate. He carries a heavy blunted mace engraved with runes that glow in prismatic hues. Brossard lives a mostly quiet life, in a thatched cottage between the forests and the mountains. His home is well known to many, as he is a practicing patron of mercy, healing, and community. Intimidating as he may look, Brossard is optimistic and kind, finding beauty where others find none. In his many adventures, he always serves as the party's conscience and moral authority, inspiring those nearest him during dangerous battles. A victory for Brossard is won anytime inspiration or beauty presents itself.
