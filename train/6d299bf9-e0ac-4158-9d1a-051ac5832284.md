A faded memory of whispered words in a half-empty café, the scent of coffee lingering like an echo.
*"You don’t know what you’re missing,"* a voice—perhaps a stranger’s, perhaps a friend’s—had murmured, hands brushing against the tablecloth as the sunlight spilled through the window. The words dissolved before they could form fully, like mist in a storm.

Later, the fragments resurfaced in a dream or a half-remembered dream:
*"The way your laugh sounded when you didn’t realize you were singing."*
*"How the map of your face changed when you were angry—dark lines like rivers beneath the skin."*
*"The sound of your voice when you were scared, low and trembling like a door left ajar."*

No names, no dates, only the ghost of a moment that refused to stay still. The past is a river, and this was a single, forgotten ripple.
