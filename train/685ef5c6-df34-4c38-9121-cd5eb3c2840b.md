**Scene 1: A distant galaxy**
Stars flicker in a cosmic void, their cold light casting long shadows. A lone ship drifts past, its hull etched with ancient runes, humming with an eerie resonance. Inside, a scientist observes data streams pulsing on holographic displays—fragments of a forgotten civilization’s last transmissions.

**Scene 2: A modern city**
Neon signs flicker against rain-slicked streets. A young hacker, hunched over a terminal, deciphers encrypted files from an abandoned archive. A notification glitches: *"Transmission from the void detected."* The screen freezes mid-frame.

**Scene 3: The ship’s bridge**
The scientist’s voice crackles over comms: *"We’ve received it—it’s not just data. It’s a message."* The ship’s AI scans the data, projecting a holographic map: a spiral of galaxies, each a potential gateway. Outside, a second ship—older, more damaged—enters the system.

**Scene 4: The gateway opens**
Lightning splits the void. The two ships merge into a single vessel, their hulls fused in a silent ritual. The scientist’s breath catches as the map resolves into a map of Earth, marked with coordinates: *"The Archive is here."*

**Scene 5: The Archive**
A ruined temple glows faintly in the jungle. Inside, walls pulse with bioluminescent veins. The young hacker steps forward; their reflection flickers like a dying star. The scientist’s voice echoes: *"We’re not alone. The past is watching."* The screen glitches—then displays a final message: *"Find the key. The choice is yours."*
