Lily the bunny loved her big, fluffy tail. One sunny morning, she hopped outside to play. "Oh, look!" she said, seeing a tiny, shiny pebble.

She picked it up and giggled. "This is the prettiest pebble ever!" She dropped it into her basket. Suddenly, the pebble started to glow!

Lily’s eyes widened. "What is this?" she wondered. She held it closer and felt warm. The pebble began to dance and sparkle, making her basket shimmer too.

A tiny voice giggled nearby. "Hello, Lily!" came a mouse named Milo. He had lost his favorite toy—a tiny red ball.

"Can I play with your sparkly pebble?" Milo asked. Lily smiled. "Of course!"

The pebble turned into a magic ball. It rolled and bounced, and Lily and Milo played together. They chased it through the meadow, laughing and giggling.

As the sun set, the magic ball grew soft and warm. "We should save it," said Lily. Milo nodded. They wrapped it in a blanket and tucked it safely in Lily’s basket.

"Maybe one day, it will shine again," Milo whispered.

Lily curled up beside him. "I hope so," she said, dreaming of more fun adventures with her sparkly friend.
