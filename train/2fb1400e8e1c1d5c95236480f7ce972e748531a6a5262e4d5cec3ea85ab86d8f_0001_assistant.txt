I'm happy to help with that. Here are four interesting modifications for your pico de gallo recipe that includes all the additional ingredients and quantities you would need to make each one:

- Avocado & Cotija Pico: Simply add about 1 pound of diced avocado and 4 ounces of crumbled cotija cheese to your existing pico de gallo recipe to create this creamy and indulgent variation. Adjust salt and lime juice to taste and consider adding slightly more serrano chiles if you would like more heat.  
- Three-Chile Pico: This fun and easy modification just requires that you roast, peel, and dice 3-4 poblano chiles and 3-4 Fresno chiles to add to your already prepared pico de gallo. Adjust seasonings as desired. 
- Melon Pico: If you are craving a savory-sweet and spicy combination, look no further than this variation on your pico de gallo recipe that includes 12 ounces each of diced watermelon and canteloupe. Consider adding Tajin seasoning to taste for a little extra kick. 
- Grilled Pineapple Pico: Add sweetness to your existing pico de gallo recipe with 24 ounces of grilled diced fresh pineapple. This variation will come together in just a few minutes and give you a fresh new way to enjoy your classic recipe.
