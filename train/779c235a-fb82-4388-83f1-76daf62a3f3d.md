A gradual metamorphosis unfolds incrementally: subtle adjustments in behavior, posture, or perspective. Tiny modifications in routines—waking earlier, revisiting old habits, or adopting new ones—accumulate into noticeable shifts. Small experiments with diet or exercise yield incremental improvements. Thoughts and emotions, once stagnant, begin to evolve with deliberate attention. Each step, though insignificant alone, compounds into a transformative trajectory over time.
