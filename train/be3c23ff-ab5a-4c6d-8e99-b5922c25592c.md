## Project Chimera: Rulebook

**A Cooperative Game of Viral Containment**

Welcome to Project Chimera, a cooperative tabletop game where you and your fellow researchers race against time to contain a rapidly evolving and devastating viral outbreak. The fate of the world rests on your collaborative efforts. This rulebook details the game’s components, setup, gameplay, and victory/loss conditions. Please read it carefully before beginning.

### Components

*   **Game Board:** A world map depicting key research facilities and potential outbreak zones.
*   **Researcher Pawns (4):** Representing specialized research teams.
*   **Viral Strain Cards (60):** Detail viral properties, mutation rates, and infection vectors.
*   **Event Cards (40):** Introduce unexpected challenges and opportunities.
*   **Research Tokens (120):** Representing accumulated scientific knowledge.
*   **Containment Markers (40):** Used to limit viral spread.
*   **Outbreak Markers (60):** Indicate regions experiencing active infection.
*   **Mutation Dice (3):** Six-sided dice used to determine viral mutations.
*   **Crisis Track:** A track indicating the escalating severity of the global situation.
*   **Player Aid Cards (4):** Summarizing key actions and rules.

### Setup

1.  Place the game board in the center of the playing area.
2.  Place the Crisis Track marker at the starting position.
3.  Shuffle the Viral Strain Cards and place them face down in a draw pile. Reveal the top three cards and place them face up beside the deck; these represent the initial viral strains.
4.  Shuffle the Event Cards and place them face down in a draw pile.
5.  Each player chooses a Researcher Pawn and places it on the designated starting Research Facility.
6.  Each player receives a Player Aid Card.
7.  Place a number of Outbreak Markers on the board as indicated by the difficulty level (see "Difficulty Scaling" below).

### Turn Structure

Each round consists of the following phases, completed in order:

**1. Researcher Phase:** Each player, in clockwise order, performs two actions. Available actions are:

*   **Move:** Move your Researcher Pawn to an adjacent location on the board.
*   **Research:** Gain Research Tokens equal to the number indicated on the location you occupy.
*   **Contain:** Spend Research Tokens to place a Containment Marker on the location you occupy. The number of tokens required is determined by the location’s containment difficulty.
*   **Analyze Viral Strain:** Spend Research Tokens to draw and examine the top Viral Strain Card. You may discard this card after viewing it.
*   **Share Research:** Transfer Research Tokens to another Researcher Pawn at the same location.

**2. Viral Phase:**

*   **Mutation:** Roll the three Mutation Dice. The results determine how the active Viral Strain Cards mutate. Each card has specific mutation triggers listed on it. Apply these effects immediately.
*   **Spread:** For each active Viral Strain Card, determine the number of locations it will spread to, based on its listed spread rate. Place Outbreak Markers on these locations. If a location already has an Outbreak Marker, increase the number of markers on that location by one.
*   **Crisis Escalation:** Advance the Crisis Track marker one space.

**3. Event Phase:** Draw the top Event Card and resolve its effect immediately. Event cards can introduce beneficial effects, detrimental challenges, or unexpected twists to the game. Discard the Event Card after resolution.

### Win and Loss Conditions

**Winning the Game:**

You win the game if you successfully contain all active Viral Strain Cards. A Viral Strain Card is considered contained when it has been analyzed, and a sufficient number of Containment Markers have been placed on locations exhibiting the virus’s infection vector. The number required is specified on the card.

**Losing the Game:**

You lose the game if any of the following conditions are met:

*   The Crisis Track marker reaches the final space.
*   There are no more Outbreak Markers available to place on the board.
*   A Viral Strain Card’s spread rate exceeds the number of available Researcher Pawns.

### Trade-Off Mechanics

Project Chimera incorporates several mechanics designed to force difficult team decisions under conditions of uncertainty:

*   **Limited Research Actions:** Each Researcher Pawn has a limited number of actions per turn. This necessitates prioritizing research efforts, as focusing on one strain may neglect others.
*   **Mutation Dice Uncertainty:** The Mutation Dice introduce an element of unpredictability to viral evolution. Players must anticipate potential mutations and adapt their containment strategies accordingly, often requiring reassignment of research efforts.
*   **Containment Difficulty:** Different locations have varying containment difficulties, requiring different amounts of Research Tokens. Players must decide whether to focus on high-priority, difficult-to-contain locations or spread their efforts across multiple, less critical areas.

### Difficulty Scaling

The game’s difficulty scales based on the number of players:

*   **Two Players:** Begin with 5 Outbreak Markers on the board. Reduce the number of Research Tokens awarded for the "Research" action by one.
*   **Three Players:** Play with the base number of Outbreak Markers (as listed in the setup instructions).
*   **Four Players:** Begin with 8 Outbreak Markers on the board. Increase the number of Research Tokens awarded for the "Research" action by one.

### Advanced Variants

*   **Rapid Mutation:** After each Viral Phase, roll one additional Mutation Die. This accelerates viral evolution and increases the game’s difficulty.
*   **Specialized Viruses:** Introduce a set of “Specialized Virus” cards that possess unique and powerful effects. These cards are shuffled into the Viral Strain deck and add further complexity to the game.
*   **Public Health Campaign:** Introduce a new track representing public awareness and cooperation. Certain Event Cards can affect this track, providing benefits or penalties based on its level.



### Designer’s Note

Project Chimera was designed to be more than just a game. Our goal was to create an engaging experience that subtly introduces players to the complexities of pandemic response. The mechanics emphasize the importance of collaboration, strategic prioritization, and adaptability in the face of unpredictable challenges. We hope that Project Chimera fosters a deeper appreciation for the dedication and expertise of scientists and public health professionals working tirelessly to protect global health.
