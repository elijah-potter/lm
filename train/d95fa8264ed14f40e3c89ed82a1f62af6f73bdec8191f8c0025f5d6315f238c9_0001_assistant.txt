**3-Month Workout Plan**

Warm Up (before every workout)
* 10 minutes of very light cardio or stationary bike
* Active stretching e.g. jumping jacks
* 1 set using just the bar

Stretches (after every workout)
* Quad
* Calf
* Hamstring
* Shoulder
* Chest
* Back
* Foam rollers
Hold each for 30-60 seconds, don't push too hard.

*Push Day*
Weeks 1-4
* Overhead Press 3x8-12 reps
* Incline Bench Press 3x8-12
* Bench Press 3x8-12
* Tricep Extensions 3x8-12
* Planks, hold for 60-90 seconds
* 20 minutes of HIIT cardio (High-Intensity Interval Training)

Weeks 5-8
* Add exercise variations (dumbells/barbells, change grip)
* Increase weight gradually
* Increase plank time and work up to 30 minutes HIIT

Weeks 9-12
* Lower reps to 5x4-6
* Increase weight
* Start extended arm planks
* Shorten rest intervals for HIIT

*Pull Day*
Weeks 1-4
* Deadlift 3x5-8
* Pull Ups 3x failure
* Lat Pulldowns 3x8-12
* Dumbbell Curls 3x8-12
* Calf Raises 3x15
* Hanging Leg Raises 3x10
* 20 minutes treadmill

Weeks 5-8 & 9-12
* Add variations (weighted pullups, change grip, dumbells/barbells)
* Gradually increase weight
* Increase reps on leg raises/calves (no weight increase)
* Work up to 1 hour on the treadmill

*Leg Day*
Weeks 1-4
* Barbell Squats 3x8-12
* Leg Press 3x8-12
* Leg Curls 3x8-12
* Leg Extensions 3x8-12
* Ab Roller 3x10
* 20 minutes stationary cycle

Weeks 5-8 & 9-12
* Add variations same as above
* Increase weight and lower reps following the pattern above
* Work up to 45 minutes stationary cycle

**Rest Day**
* VERY IMPORTANT - DO NOT EVER SKIP
* You can take extra rest days if necessary, but never less.  Your body needs to repair itself.
* Take a full rest day after leg days to recover.
* This means no physical exertion at all, not swimming, not basketball, nothing.
