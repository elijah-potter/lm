Once there was a little bunny named Benny who lived in a cozy burrow near a big, green meadow. One sunny morning, Benny noticed a tiny, shiny acorn that had rolled into his burrow. "Oh, what a sparkly treasure!" Benny said, picking it up.

Benny's best friend, a kind old turtle named Tilly, asked, "What’s that, Benny?" Benny showed her the acorn. "It looks like it might grow into a big, tall tree!" Tilly said, smiling.

They decided to plant the acorn in Benny’s burrow. Benny dug a little hole, and Tilly gently placed the acorn inside. "We’ll take care of it," Benny said, patting the ground. Soon, a tiny shoot peeked out from the soil.

Days passed, and the shoot grew taller and taller. Benny and Tilly watered it every morning with a little bucket. One afternoon, Benny saw a beautiful flower bloom from the acorn tree. "Look, Tilly! It’s a flower!" Benny cheered.

Soon, the tree grew big and strong. It had big, green leaves and a sweet, sweet smell. The other animals in the meadow, like a little mouse named Milo and a wise old owl named Oliver, came to visit. "This is the prettiest tree in the whole meadow!" Oliver said.

Benny and Tilly were so happy. They decided to plant more acorns so everyone could enjoy the beautiful tree. Benny picked many acorns and hid them in his burrow. "We’ll make many happy trees," he said, smiling.

One evening, as Benny and Tilly snuggled under their favorite blanket, they heard a soft whisper in the wind. "The tree remembers all the kindness you shared." Benny and Tilly hugged each other tightly. "We’ll take care of the meadow," Benny whispered, knowing their hearts were full of love and laughter.

And from that day on, Benny and Tilly planted more acorns, and the meadow grew even happier, filled with laughter and beautiful trees.
