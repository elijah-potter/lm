A scientist notices the subtle shift in the crystal structure of graphene under UV light, detecting a previously undetected vibrational mode at 2.1 THz. The data series from an electron microscope shows a clear peak at 2.115 THz in the Raman spectrum, matching theoretical predictions for a quantum defect in the lattice. The team re-runs the experiment with higher resolution, confirming the anomaly in 3D atomic displacement maps. The breakthrough is published in *Nature Nanotechnology* with a citation rate of 47 in the first year.
