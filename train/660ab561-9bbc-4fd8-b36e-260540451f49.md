The **borderlands** between **dry savannah and mist-shrouded highlands**, where the earth bends under ancient stone arches. The air hums with the scent of damp earth and crushed herbs, and the wind carries whispers from the next world—sometimes voices, sometimes nothing, just the sound of shifting shadows. The path splits into three: one leads into the **burning glass**, another into the **river of echoes**, and the third down into the **abyss of forgotten names**. The ground trembles underfoot; each step feels like crossing from one realm into another. The sky is a bruise of twilight, and the trees here are not trees but **guardians** with eyes like polished obsidian, watching. The threshold is not a door but a **question**—what waits on the other side? What are you willing to cross?
