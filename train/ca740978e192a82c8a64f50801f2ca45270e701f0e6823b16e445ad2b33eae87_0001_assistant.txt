Sure thing! Here are six restaurants with vegan options in Helen, Georgia.

•The Troll Tavern-The friendly and knowledgeable staff can help you select between a vegan bratwurst, fried green tomatoes, or wraps.  Fried cauliflower and sauteed mushrooms can be used in place of any meat, or you can also replace any burger meat with a portobello mushroom cap.
•Cafe International- A riverside experience with live music where you can get vegan bratwurst and beyond burgers (remove the cheese). 
•Spice 55- Here, you will find Asian-inspired food. You can get tofu and other vegan-friendly options. The staff is willing to accommodate you with your dietary needs.
•Betty's-This country store has a deli where you can buy an avocado sprout sandwich. The store also carries vegan-friendly snacks. 
•Brickhouse-Pizzas and pasta can be made vegan upon request. Vegan cheese is not on the menu, so you have to ask for it.
•Hofbräuhaus Restaurant & Pub- There are limited vegan options here, but you can get a beyond burger.
