Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big yellow door, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white fur, big brown eyes, and a happy little nose that wiggled when he was excited.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to see his favorite flower, the daisy with the bright yellow petals. "Hello, Daisy!" he said, nudging her gently with his nose. "I wonder what you're thinking today?"

Benny started hopping to his favorite spot under the big oak tree. There, he found his best friend, a friendly squirrel named Sammy. "Good morning, Sammy!" Benny said, waving his paws.

Sammy chattered, "Good morning, Benny! I saw the sky turn pink this morning. Isn’t it pretty?"

Benny giggled. "Yes, it is! But my favorite color is green, like the leaves on the tree. I’m going to jump and play!"

Benny ran and jumped around, making the grass tickle his paws. He chased his shadow, which danced in the sunlight. Suddenly, he heard a tiny *thump* and looked down. A little acorn had rolled into his basket of carrots.

"Oh no!" Benny gasped. "I’ll have to find my carrot!"

Sammy giggled. "Don’t worry, Benny. Let’s help each other!"

They worked together. Benny hopped while Sammy chattered, and soon the acorn was back in the basket. Benny was happy. "Thank you, Sammy!"

As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink, Benny and Sammy snuggled under their favorite blanket. "Today was the best day ever," Benny whispered.

Sammy nuzzled him. "Yes, it was. Let’s do it again tomorrow!"

And with that, Benny and Sammy drifted off to sleep, dreaming of more adventures under the big, beautiful sky.
