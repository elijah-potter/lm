Market Square, evening rush. Crowds of shoppers navigate through vendors selling fresh produce, spices, and handwoven textiles. Street musicians play lively tunes near a bustling café where families gather for dinner. Neon signs flicker in the dusk, casting a warm glow over the cobblestone paths. Locals and tourists alike push through the crowd, carrying bags, chatting, and laughing as the square fills with the scent of grilled meats and blooming jasmine.
