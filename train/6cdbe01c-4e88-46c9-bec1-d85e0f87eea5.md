## Lost in the Record Store (Brooklyn, NY) (October 26)

The afternoon light filtering through the dusty windows of Rough Trade was particularly lovely on Thursday. I was browsing the new arrivals, searching for a specific reissue, when you appeared. You were examining a stack of vintage jazz records, your fingers tracing the worn spines with a quiet reverence. I remember noticing the vibrant turquoise of your scarf – a striking contrast to the muted tones of the shop. We didn’t speak, not a word exchanged, but there was a shared appreciation for the music hanging in the air, a comfortable silence that felt unexpectedly meaningful. The low hum of the turntable, spinning a Miles Davis track, underscored the moment. I hesitated, wanting to say something, anything, but the moment passed. I left shortly after, feeling a peculiar sense of loss. I hope you found what you were looking for. 

If you were the person with the turquoise scarf, please reply with the name of your favorite Miles Davis album.
