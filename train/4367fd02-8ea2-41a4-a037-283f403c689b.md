The weight of choices pressed against my chest—each path a question mark. The coffee cup, cold and forgotten, sat beside my laptop screen, its steam long dissipated into the stale air. The morning light, once bright, now slanted through the blinds like a guilty confession.

*Should I reply to her message now?* The words lingered in my drafts, half-formed, half-unwritten. The time was right—she’d been quiet for hours—but the hesitation was a second heartbeat.

Outside, the city pulsed, indifferent. Cars honked, a child laughed from a playground window, and I wondered if the indecision was a storm I could ride out or a bridge I’d rather not cross.

I exhaled, slow and deliberate, and let the silence answer itself.
