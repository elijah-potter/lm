History – Gudivada is a small village along the Vijayvada-Masulipattanam Road.
How to Reach – The railway station is at a distance of 1.5 Kms from the temple.
Heritage Centre Map
Temple Name – Parshwanatha Swamy Jain Temple
Temple Type – Digambar Temple
Main Deity – Black colored idol of Bhagawan Parshvanath in the semi padmasana posture.
Temple History – The temple of bhagwan Parshwanatha at this place was renovated recently. The idol of this ancient temple is a unique of its kind.It is said that the idol of bhagwan Parshwanatha here belongs to the eighth century. This place was one of the important Jain centres . A fair is held here on the tenth day of the bright half of the month of Magsar every year.
