•	Chocolate Martini: This delicious drink uses approximately 4 ounces of vodka, 2 ounces of Irish cream, 2 ounces of chocolate liqueur, and 3 – 4 tablespoons of chocolate syrup.
•	Death by Chocolate Cocktail: If you want an even more decadent drink, this cocktail uses vodka, coffee liqueur, dark cream liqueur, chocolate syrup, and a couple of scoops of ice cream. To turn this delicious cocktail into a fun birthday celebration, you will also top it with whipped cream and a maraschino cherry.
•	Chocolate Mint Julep: If you want a traditional southern drink, serve your wife this delicious drink with bourbon, chocolate whiskey, chocolate bitters, and mint leaves.
•	Spiked Mexican Hot Chocolate: This is a spicy hot chocolate that you add a little liquor. The hot chocolate is made with almond milk, coconut cream, cinnamon sticks, nutmeg, chili powder, vanilla, agave, baking chocolate, cocoa powder, and toasted marshmallows. Then you add in tequila and chocolate liquor. This is perfect for a cold winter night.
•	Frozen Chocolate Mudslide: This drink is made with Kahlua, Irish cream, crème de cacao, chocolate vodka, and ice cream. Then it is topped with chocolate sprinkles, chocolate syrup, grated chocolate, and mini chocolate morsels. This is perfect for a hot summer evening.
