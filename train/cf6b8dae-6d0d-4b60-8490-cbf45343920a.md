Floating debris in a shifting puddle of neon-blue light.
The air hums with static—static of forgotten conversations, half-finished thoughts.
A crumpled origami crane lies near the edge of a cracked mirror, reflecting a version of itself that flickers in and out of existence.
A clock on a wall ticks backward, counting down to something that hasn’t happened yet.
Furniture rearranges itself at night, furniture that shouldn’t be there.
The scent of rain mixes with the faint, metallic tang of something dissolving—ink, maybe, or time.
A child’s toy, half-soldier, half-machine, sits in the corner, its joints stiff with forgotten play.
