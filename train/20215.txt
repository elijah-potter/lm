Finding Primary Sources in Print
USING SECONDARY SOURCES:
If you're searching for primary sources on a topic, one good place to look is a secondary source that cites original texts, manuscripts, and archives.
USING SUBJECT HEADINGS:
For archival sources, interviews, and memoirs:
► Use the Starts With/Browse search in HOLLIS, and look for subjects with these subheadings: Diaries • Interviews • Sources • Archival resources • Newspapers • Personal Narratives • Biography • Pictorial Works • Comic Books • Caricatures and cartoons • Photographs
For stories, novels, plays, and films:
► For novels, plays, and stories about a topic, look for subject headings with these subheadings: Fiction • In Literature • Juvenile fiction • Drama • Poetry • Literary collections
