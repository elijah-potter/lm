Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big red door, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had soft, fluffy ears and a tail that wiggled when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his friend, a little bird named Bertie, sitting on his window sill. "Good morning, Benny!" chirped Bertie. "I saw a shiny red apple in the garden. Want to eat it with me?"

Benny's eyes lit up. "Sure! But first, let's play hide-and-seek!"

Benny ran to the garden and hid behind a big bush. Bertie giggled and counted to ten. When Benny was called, he peeked out and saw Bertie waving. "You're so fast!" Benny said.

After playing, they both ate the apple. Bertie had a tiny seed inside, and Benny loved the crunchy parts. They sat under the sun, and Bertie sang a little song about flowers:

"Red and yellow, make a cheerful meal,
Sprinkle a bit of green, and you're all set, you see!"

Benny clapped his paws. "That's the best song ever!"

As the sun started to set, they said goodnight. Benny yawned and curled up in his burrow. Bertie flapped her wings and flew home. Benny closed his eyes and dreamed of more adventures with Bertie.

The end!
