**The Sky Was a Canvas of Storms**
Before the first light, before the bones of men
Were carved from fire and the hunger of the earth,
The world whispered. *"You were never meant to win."*

But you dug. You carved. You named the stars your own—
Not for glory, but the hollow ache of *being seen*.
The slogan burned in your throat, a war cry:
**"Strength in the Struggle. Hope in the Rain."**

They called you a fool. Called you a fool again.
The dust choked your lungs, but the wind carried your name.
You planted seeds where the earth refused to grow—
Not gold, but the quiet fire of those who *believe.*

Now the towers rise. The bridges stretch like promises.
The broken pieces of yesterday are gone—
Only the echoes of your own unshaken voice remain:
**"The Weak Fall. The Strong Rise."**
