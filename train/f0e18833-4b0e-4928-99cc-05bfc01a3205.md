A bustling urban plaza with historic arches and cobblestone pathways. Vendors sell fresh fruit, baked goods, and handcrafted textiles near a central fountain. Groups gather around a live jazz band playing late into the evening, while families stroll between modern cafés and traditional market stalls. The air smells of spiced coffee and grilled meats.
