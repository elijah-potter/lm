*[Ceremony Details]*
**Location:** Grand Hall of the House of Veyne, capital city of Duskhaven
**Date:** Third day of the Harvest Festival (24th of the Blood Moon)
**Purpose:** Mandatory ancestral homage to the House of Veyne’s late Matriarch, Lady Elspeth Veyne, deceased two years prior.

**Participants:**
- **House Elders:** Lord Aldric Veyne (Head of House), Lady Maren Veyne (Matriarch’s niece), and three younger siblings (all under 25).
- **Guests of Honor:** Lord Garrick of Blackthorn (Elspeth’s former chamberlain), a priest from the Silver Dawn Order, and a delegation from the neighboring House of Duskbane.

**Ceremony Flow:**
1. **Opening Ritual (17:30):** A chandelier of blackened candles is lit, symbolizing the Veyne bloodline’s legacy. The priest leads a prayer for Elspeth’s repose.
2. **Ancestral Feast (18:00):** A spread of preserved meats, honeyed wine, and heirloom silverware. Guests must eat at least one course to fulfill obligations.
3. **Seal of Obligation (18:45):** Lord Aldric presents each guest with a **blackened sigil pendant**—a family heirloom—symbolizing their debt. Refusal to accept is deemed a slight.
4. **Closing Toast (19:15):** The priest raises a goblet of "blood wine" (a dark, aged mixture of elderberry and crushed iron ore) in Elspeth’s name. Guests must drink without protest.
5. **Departure (20:00):** No conversation allowed after the feast; guests are escorted to their carriages by House servants.

**Mandatory Actions:**
- **Gestures:** Bowing twice to the priest upon entry and exit.
- **Silence:** No discussion of House politics or past conflicts during the ceremony.
- **Debt Acknowledgment:** All guests must sign a **sealed ledger** (a physical document) declaring their attendance and willingness to uphold Elspeth’s legacy.

**Consequences of Disobedience:**
- Public humiliation in the House Council’s next session.
- Forfeiture of the sigil pendant (replaced with a silver dagger).
- Loss of voting rights in future House matters for one full moon.
