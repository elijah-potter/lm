I was on the run, finally free of that bald fool! It was time for some fun on the town. My first stop was the sporting goods store where I knocked over all of the golf club displays and sent the store manager chasing after tennis balls. As I scampered out of the shop, I heard someone yell, "Stop that mullet!" I simply laughed and kept going. Then, I went to the candy store and I shed my stray hairs in all the fudge. Now, anybody who bites into a piece of chocolate in this town will get a mouthful of me! Haha! The next place I went was the local swimming pool. I fancied a nice dip to get my tendrils wet. At first, nobody noticed me slip quietly into the shallow end of the pool. Then, a young girl spotted me and screamed. I quickly swam my way to the drain and clogged it up so the pump overheated and broke. 

That's when I was trapped in a net! The pool attendant fished me out of the pool and held me up for everyone to see. There were cries of horror at the sight of me. It was delightful. But, I managed to wriggle myself out of his grasp and take off out the door. Just then, the bald man caught sight of me. He'd been searching all over town, following my path of mayhem. He managed to reach out and grab ahold of me too, but I was greasy and the water from the pool made me slick. I slid away and made a break for it. Down at the end of the block, I narrowly managed to dodge a police car that almost ran me over. The car screeched to a halt and an officer leapt out, brandishing a gun. He fired a shot and it ricocheted off the pavement to my right. Thinking fast, I slid down the sewer drain and I've been here ever since.
