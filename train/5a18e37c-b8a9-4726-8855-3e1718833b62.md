A dimly lit library at dawn, its wooden shelves lined with ancient tomes dusted with age. The air hums with the soft rustle of pages and the faint scent of aged parchment. A lone figure, hunched over a desk, traces intricate symbols in a leather-bound journal. The light from a single, flickering lantern casts long shadows across the cracked floor tiles, revealing hidden details—faint footprints leading deeper into the labyrinth of books. The walls, once pristine, bear subtle stains that whisper forgotten secrets. Outside, the distant cry of a crow echoes, but the figure remains absorbed, their focus unwavering on the shadows where the past and present blur.
