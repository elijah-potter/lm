Silence crackles with ancient stone—damp earth and crushed gravel whisper underfoot. The air hums with the low, steady pulse of a distant river, its currents shifting like liquid gold through hidden canyons. Rustling leaves, dry twigs snapping, a crow’s sharp *caw* echoes through the trees. The scent of damp moss and pine resin clings to the breeze, mingled with the faint tang of sulfur from a distant geyser. The wind carries the scent of blooming jasmine, sharp and sweet, and the earth itself seems to breathe warm, earthy aroma—fermenting soil, crushed herbs, and the distant, salty tang of the sea. A distant train’s whistle pierces the quiet, its metal voice fading into the valley.
