**ORDINANCE #**[Ordinance Number]**
**AN ACT REGARDING FALSE OR MISINFORMATION RELATED TO PUBLIC SAFETY**

---

### **Section 1: Purpose**
The purpose of this ordinance is to prevent the dissemination of false or misleading information that could incite fear, disrupt public order, or harm community safety.

### **Section 2: Definitions**
- **False Information**: Any statement known or recklessly disregarded to be false when disseminated.
- **Misleading Information**: Information that is materially inaccurate or lacks sufficient context to prevent harm.
- **Dissemination**: Sharing or broadcasting false/false-appearing information via any means (written, oral, digital, or social media).

### **Section 3: Prohibitions**
A. **Public Dissemination**: No person shall knowingly publish or spread false/false-appearing information about:
   - Emergency services capacity,
   - Public health threats (e.g., outbreaks, hazards),
   - Safety protocols,
   - Criminal activity in the town,
   - Infrastructure failures.

B. **Penalty**:
   - First offense: Warning + mandatory community service (e.g., fact-checking efforts).
   - Repeated offenses or willful intent: Fine up to **[Amount]** or imprisonment up to **[Duration]**.
   - Digital dissemination: Additional liability for hosting/amplifying false content.

### **Section 4: Reporting & Enforcement**
- Any person may report violations to the town clerk or police.
- Evidence of dissemination will be documented by the town’s media liaison.
- Violations shall be reviewed by a town council committee.

### **Section 5: Exceptions**
- Accurate reporting of news by registered media outlets.
- Verified warnings from official sources (e.g., emergency alerts).
