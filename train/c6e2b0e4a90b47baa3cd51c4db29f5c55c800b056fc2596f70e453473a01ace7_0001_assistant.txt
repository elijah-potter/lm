"Holiday in the Sun" (2001) is Megan Fox's debut film.  Fox plays Brianna Wallace, a spoiled girl with eyes on the same resort worker as one of the main characters who doesn't play nice while trying to get him to like her.  "Transformers" (2007) was Fox's big step forward in the industry as she played Mikaela Banes, the love interest to Shia LeBeouf's character.
