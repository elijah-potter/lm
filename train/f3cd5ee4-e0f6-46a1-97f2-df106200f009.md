A lone figure kneels by the edge of a crumbling stone bridge, fingers tracing the worn planks. A half-empty cup of tea sits beside them, its steam now dissipating into the fading afternoon light. The river below mirrors the muted colors of the sky—a soft, unbroken stillness. Their last words, whispered to a friend long ago, linger in the air like the scent of damp earth. The other person, now a ghost in this moment, stands motionless on the opposite bank, hands clasped behind their back. The bridge creaks under their weight as they step down, but their feet never touch the water. The current carries only silence. Shadows stretch long across the stones as dusk settles, and the only sound is the distant cry of a gull.
