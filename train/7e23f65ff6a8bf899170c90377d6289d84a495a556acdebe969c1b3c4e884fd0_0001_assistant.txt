Dear Diary,

It's me again, Mario! It's been a busy day, full of adventure. I woke up, feeling ready for a relaxing day. Of course, Bowser had just captured Princess Peach. Again.

I had to make my way through various battlegrounds. Of course, I've been through them before. I stomped on Koopas and said hello to Toads as I pushed forward to the castle.

That's when I ran into the Bullet Bills.

For some reason, Bowser had decided to line his castle with hundreds of Bullet Bills. I had to dodge and weave through them, bouncing and diving until I finally made my way to a safe alcove.

Once I got past them, I slipped inside the castle. It was full of LAVA! I had to be super careful here - one touch from the hot substance, and I would be done for. Luckily, there were convenient platforms for jumping.

Eventually, I made it to the top and went inside the throne room. After a heated battle, I sunk Bowser in his lava and rescued the Princess! We had a nice dinner out to celebrate.

As I mentioned before, it's been a busy day. I may need to talk to Bowser about his habit of stealing Peach once he recovers from the lava. We're all tired of it.

Until next time!

Mario
