**The last light in the city fades like a forgotten promise—**

a neon sign flickers against the glass of an abandoned café, its blue glow bleeding through the cracks in the pavement. The air smells of rain and something older, something that wasn’t meant to be preserved in bottles but in the slow decay of things. Outside, the hum of traffic is a distant murmur, drowned by the whispers of the wind through the broken windows. Somewhere, a clock ticks backward.
