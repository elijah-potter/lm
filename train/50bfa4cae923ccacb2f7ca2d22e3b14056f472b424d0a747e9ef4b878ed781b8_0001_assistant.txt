Here is some advice to avoid being attacked by a shark:
* Don't swim alone because this leaves you more vulnerable to attack; swim in groups only.
* You shouldn't swim in the evening or at night.
* You should avoid swimming in areas with a lot of fish or fishing activity.
* Don't wear jewellery while swimming; its reflection can be mistaken for fish scales.
* Avoid splashing in the water, as it can attract sharks.
