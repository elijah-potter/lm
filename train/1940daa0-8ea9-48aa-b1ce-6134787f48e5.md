Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big backyard, there lived a curious bunny named Benny. Benny loved to explore every nook and cranny of his garden, but one day, he found something very special—three shiny, golden acorns hidden under a big, leafy bush.

Benny thought, "I wonder what happens if I eat these acorns?" He nibbled one carefully, but it tasted strange. He tried another, and this time, it made him giggle! Then he ate the last one and—*POP!*—he turned into a big, strong squirrel!

Benny the squirrel was so happy. He ran around the garden, chasing acorns and playing with his new friends—a chatty chipmunk named Charlie and a sleepy little mouse named Max. They built a fun treehouse and had the best adventure ever.

But one evening, as Benny looked back at his old house, he remembered his bunny family. He felt a little sad. So, he did something brave: he climbed up the tallest oak tree and chewed through a branch, making a tiny tunnel to his home. With a happy sigh, he hopped back down and found his old friends waiting.

Charlie said, "You came back! What happened?" Benny smiled. "I just needed to grow up a little to come back to you," he said. And from that day on, Benny was Benny again—both a bunny and a squirrel, always ready for more adventures!
