A quiet café at dawn. The coffee machine hums, steam curling into the dim light. Two strangers sit across from each other—one with fingers tracing the rim of their empty mug, the other tracing the edge of a half-eaten pastry. The first person pauses, eyes flickering between the second’s hand and the crumbs on the table. The second speaks in a voice barely above a whisper: *"You ever think about how much time we waste not listening?"* The first exhales, slow and deliberate. The second’s fingers still, then brush against the other’s wrist. A shared glance—no words needed. The air between them thickens, warm and charged. The second adds, *"You’re the first person who’s ever made me feel… seen."* The first smiles, finally. Outside, the city wakes. In the café, the moment lingers like a held breath.
