Controlling undesired plants by purchasing herbicides and hiring equipment operators is an expensive endeavor for cattle producers to maintain usable land for their herds. Surprisingly, goats are highly effective at removing these undesired plants because they eat predominantly brush and weeds. In one example, a pasture owner let a herd of 200 goats loose in an overrun and unproductive field. The goats cleared away the wall of brush and stripped leaves and branches at a much quicker rate than anticipated.
