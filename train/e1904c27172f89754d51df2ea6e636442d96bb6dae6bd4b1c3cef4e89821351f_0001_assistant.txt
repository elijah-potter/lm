Do you want to refresh the look of your bathroom? No problem! Here are a few things that you can try, without remodeling or repainting the walls:

1. Replace old towels. This might not seem like much, but if your towels are stained or worn out, then getting new towels will feel great. They can also make your bathroom look cleaner, especially if your towels are stored in a visible area.
2. Replace your shower curtain: If you have a shower curtain, then getting a new one is a quick and easy way to change the appearance of your space. Don't forget to replace the old curtain liner, too!
3. Update bathroom accessories: Sometimes the small touches make all the difference. Get a new shower rod, towel rods, toilet paper dispenser, shower caddies, soap dishes, and toothbrush holders. Choose styles and colors that complement your new towels and shower curtain to tie the look together.
4. Organize your bathroom: Is your bathroom cluttered? Create a cleaner space that looks great and is easier to use with some bathroom organization products. This can include baskets, drawer organizers, and more. 
5. Add some decorative touches. Personalize your space with some decorative items that you love. Hang a few pictures on the walls, add a plant or two if the room has enough lighting, or don your bathroom shelves with figurines or artwork.
