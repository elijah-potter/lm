PRODUCE MADNESS
J.P. Dough

SETTING
Time: The 1980s
Location: Ferdy’s Grocery Bonanza

SCENE LIST

Scene One: Lettuce/cabbage/leafy vegetables display shelves
Scene Two: Tomato bins
Scene Three: Prepackaged sandwich/salad display shelves
Scene Four: Grapes/berries display shelves
Scene Five: Bin with oranges/lemons

SET DESCRIPTION

The sets can be relatively simple. Plastic fruits and vegetables will work mixed in with real ones. Card tables and cardboard organizers can make up the display shelves.

The leafy vegetable display should give an air of freshness. It needs to occupy the majority of the stage in the first scene with the two guys talking together in front of it. A spotlight highlighting the area is perfect.

The tomato bins should look a bit messy. A couple of tomatoes are on the floor in front of the bins. Use real tomatoes for this area. 

The prepackaged sandwich/salad display holds center stage in Scene Three. There is plenty of space on either side of the display for the main characters to stand. 

The grapes and berries section is messy and there are three or four extras milling around it, a few kids pilfering grapes. The floor is covered with different types of grapes. 

The orange/lemon bin occupies center stage in the final scene. The floor around it has fruit and vegetables from the other scenes scattered everywhere. 

The main backdrop for all of the scenes is a series of giant posters in bright colors with various sale prices on them. 80’s style artwork in neon colors emphasizes the prices. The posters are across the back of the stage with stage lights shining on them.
