Is there anything worse than finally being bought and taken home and then never being able to go out? I was made for nights out on the town! I want to dance and strut my stuff, but here I am, stuck in the closet, day in and day out. 

I’ve heard what she says about me: “Oh, they’re cute but way too uncomfortable!” Girl, of course, I’m not comfortable; I’m not a sneaker! I’m a high-class high-heel boot! You have to work to wear me, but let me tell you, I am worth the pain! 

If she could only see that if she did wear me, she would be the most stylish in the whole place!
