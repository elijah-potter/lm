Hana wasn't the average university student, but she didn't think she was anyone special. She was one of the top students, dedicating all of her time to studying. Her routine consisted of breakfast, classes in the morning, lunch, more classes, and then she headed to the library to study for the remainder of the day. 

That day, she had a screaming headache that refused to subside. Unable to focus, she decided to go home early to rest. As she stumbled out of the library feeling lightheaded, her blurry vision caught sight of a handsome figure approaching. "Are you all right?" he asked.

Hana wanted to say she was fine, but she wasn't sure what words spilled from her mouth. The next thing she knew, she woke in an unfamiliar bed. She lay there stunned for a moment before a sonorous voice came from the door. "Hey, you awake?"

Hana sat up and found herself looking at a man with the face of a model and the style to match. "Who are you? And where am I?"

"I'm Jae. I brought you to my place when you collapsed. You said you had a headache but didn't want to go to the hospital. I wasn't sure what to do, so I brought you here," he explained.

He offered her a glass of water, which she drank gratefully. "I'm really sorry about this," she said, hanging her feet off the edge of her bed.

Jae sat in a swivel chair, his head cocked to one side. "So, you don't recognize me, do you?"

"No. Should I?" 

Jae pointed to a poster on his wall. It was the poster of a rising male idol group, Love Generation. Hana's eyes widened. She had little interest in celebrities, but even she recognized them. Jae was their lead singer. 

Hana turned to him like a robot. What had she done?
