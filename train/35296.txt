Preparation Guidelines: How to Read a Newspaper?

This question can be tackled by dividing it into 2 sub parts:
- What articles to read in a newspaper?
- How to read an article?
And the answer to both these questions lie in the syllabus. In the preparation phase, if there is one thing that most importantly needs to be on your tips, it is the syllabus.
