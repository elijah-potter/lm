The main difference between monkeys and apes is that apes don’t have tails. There are over 250 species of monkeys born with tails, but 0 species of apes have tails. Another tell-tale difference is the body size and shape of an ape in comparison to a monkey. Apes have humanoid sizes and shapes with heavier, larger builds, whereas monkeys are typically smaller and more agile. 

Five examples of apes include: 

1. Gorillas 
2. Chimpanzees
3. Orangutans 
4. Gibbons 
5. Bonobos

Five examples of monkeys include: 

1. Lemurs
2. Spider Monkeys 
3. Rhesus Monkeys 
4. Howlers
5. Lorises

Monkeys and apes are both contained in the order of primates, which has more than 300 species. However, monkeys and apes have key differences that separate them into two categories within the primate order. So, you could say that monkeys and apes are cousins, but they chose different sides of the evolutionary paths.
