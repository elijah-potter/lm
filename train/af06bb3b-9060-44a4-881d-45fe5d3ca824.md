Walls whisper secrets with age.
Light filters through cracks like scattered gold.
Furniture stands, silent and waiting.
Shadows stretch and dance across the floor.
Doors creak open, inviting curiosity.
Mirrors reflect not faces, but the passage of time.
Pillows sink into the bed like old memories.
Rain patters against windows, relentless and soft.
Fire crackles, feeding warmth into the dark.
Laughter echoes, though no one is there.
Steps echo as they disappear.
Books lie unturned, their stories half-read.
Time ticks like a metronome, slow and steady.
Dust dances in the breeze, settling again.
Silence hums, a living thing.
