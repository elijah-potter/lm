1. The Gateway Arch. The family can check out the amazing view from the famous arch. There is also a museum and paddle-wheel boat rides.
2. Six Flags St. Louis. A theme park featuring DC heroes attractions, roller coasters, and live shows. There is also a water park included.
3. St. Louis Union Station. This former passenger station now has an aquarium, carousel, train park, and plenty of restaurants. 
4. Magic House. This is a place where kids can learn and explore. There are loads of educational activities and a giant kaleidoscope.
5. City Museum. A multi-level playground includes slides and caves made of recycled objects, an architectural exhibit, and a circus.
