Of course! Here are some recommendations for you:

1. The Positronic Man - This novel was partially written by the same author as The Caves of Steel, and shares many identical themes. It follows the story of a robot that gains full sentience and wants to "become human".

2. Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep? - This novel also explores humanity's relationship with androids and AI, but where Asimov's stories explore robotic technology in a positive light, this novel does the opposite. The androids in this story are completely devoid of empathy, which contrasts with the human protagonists.

3. Altered Carbon (Book 1) - If you enjoyed the detective and mystery aspects of Caves of Steel, this novel is also a science fiction story that incorporates these elements into the storytelling. It is set in a future where aging has been solved via the digital storage of human consciousness.
