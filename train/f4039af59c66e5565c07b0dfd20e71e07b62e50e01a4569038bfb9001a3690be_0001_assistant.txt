Sure!  Here is a list of four sci-fi books for people who don't like sci-fi: 

1. Station Eleven: This book was written by Emily St. John Mandel.  It may be interesting to a non-sci-fi reader because of its relevance to the COVID-19 pandemic.
2. An Ocean of Minutes: This book is written by Thea Lim.  It may capture the attention of someone like you because of its themes that are relevant to the social issues that society faces today, including healthcare access and the rights of immigrants.  
3. Warm Bodies: This book is written by Isaac Marion.  While it is a sci-fi book, it also tells a beautiful love story that may interest someone who doesn't like sci-fi but enjoys romance novels.
4. American War: This book is written by Omar El Akkad.  While it does take place in a dystopian future, it is a climate crisis that causes it to be this way. It blends a nice balance of legitimate concerns presented by actual climate scientists with sci-fi fantasy, which may make the book more interesting to you.
