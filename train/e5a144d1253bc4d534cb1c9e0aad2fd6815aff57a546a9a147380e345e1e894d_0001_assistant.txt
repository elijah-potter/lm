1) A supervolcanic eruption - The initial devastation would be unmeasurable, but would pale in comparison to the misery that would follow.  There would be nothing humans could do, as volcanic ash would block out the sun, vegetation would wither, and humanity would fall.
2) An extremely powerful solar flare - It would slice through Earth's magnetic field, and we'd be left totally helpless at the devastation that would follow.
3) High-fatality pandemic - Humans' lack of understanding of the minute could be its ultimate undoing—does anyone really have faith we'd be up to the task?
4) Asteroid impact - Not dissimilar from the supervolcanic eruption in result, this was how the dinosaurs met their fate, and given our poor competence, we might meet our fate this way too.
5) Nearby supernova - Should the star that our glum and insignificant world orbits suddenly reach the end of its time, Earth shall be reduced to cinder.
6) Alien invasion - How could we possibly compete against advanced beings from another galaxy? This would surely be the end of humanity.
