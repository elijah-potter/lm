**Library reading rooms** (e.g., community libraries, university libraries)
- Quiet spaces with blank notepads, sticky notes, or small chalkboards in study carrels.
- Visible to staff, often under benches or on windowsills.

**College dorm rooms**
- Bulletin boards, whiteboards, or shared digital spaces (e.g., shared Google Docs) with handwritten notes.

**Office break rooms**
- Whiteboards, shared bulletin boards, or corkboards with sticky notes.

**Schools (K-12)**
- Classroom "note pads" (e.g., under desks or in lockers) with paper and pens.

**Co-working spaces**
- Community boards (e.g., Trello, Miro) or physical boards with sticky notes.

**Public parks (e.g., picnic tables)**
- Small chalkboards, chalkboards with names, or shared notebooks for casual notes.

**Airports or train stations**
- Public message boards (e.g., "Help wanted" or "Lost & Found" sections).

**Yoga studios or gyms**
- Chalkboards or whiteboards with motivational or social notes.

**Hospitals or medical offices**
- Small whiteboards in waiting rooms or staff break areas for reminders or thanks.

**Subway stations or bus stops**
- Poster boards with community messages or lost-and-found notes.

**Colleges/Universities (shared dorms)**
- Shared fridge with magnetic notes, or bulletin boards by doorways.

**Local cafés or bookshops**
- Small chalkboards on the counter or in a corner for patrons to leave thanks or tips.

**Community gardens**
- Shared chalkboards or notebooks in communal spaces for gardening tips or group notes.

**Farmers' markets**
- Sticky notes on the vendor’s stall or a communal whiteboard.

**Pet grooming salons**
- Small chalkboards in the waiting area for pet owners to leave notes for their pets.

**Book clubs or reading groups**
- Notebooks shared during meetings or a dedicated "notes" section in a library.

**Wedding or event venues**
- Whiteboards or printed signs in common areas for RSVPs or notes to guests.

**Craft or maker spaces**
- Whiteboards or shared drawers with materials and notes for collaboration.

**Pet shelters or animal rescues**
- Whiteboards or sticky notes in animal care areas for staff to leave reminders.
