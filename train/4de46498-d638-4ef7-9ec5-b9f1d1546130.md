A rare smile flickered across her lips as she folded her arms, the tension between them palpable. *"I’m sorry I wasn’t there earlier,"* she began, her voice barely above a whisper. The words hung in the air, unconvincing but sincere in their own way. *"The meeting ran late, and I… well, I had to handle something urgent."* Her eyes darted to the door, then back to him, as if waiting for him to say something. He didn’t. Instead, he stared at his coffee cup, the steam curling into the dimly lit room, as if lost in his own thoughts. Outside, the city hummed—loud, indifferent. She waited. He didn’t respond.
