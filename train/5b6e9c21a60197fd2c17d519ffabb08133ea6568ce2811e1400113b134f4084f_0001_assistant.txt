I'd be more than happy to help you pick the location of your vacation! Here are some places I think fit what you are looking for:
1. Dubai, United Arab Emirates - is the home of the tallest building in the world. It is also the most populous city in the United Arab Emirates having over three million people living there. With all the people and all those buildings, there is plenty to do and see, especially because it is a common tourist destination. 
2. Hong Kong, China - is big enough to fit seven million people, and it is one of the most popular places in China to go to. There are both mountains and a harbor near it, adding even more activities than the ones you'll find in the large city. Don't forget to bring your camera as many of the skyscrapers provide amazing picture-taking opportunities.
3. New York City, United States - is another common tourist destination, also referred to as the Big Apple. There are many tall skyscrapers here, as well as some historic buildings like the Empire State Building. 
I hope that you have a fun time, wherever you decide to go!
