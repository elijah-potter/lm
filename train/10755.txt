We believe that learning the facts about childhood sexual abuse helps prevent it. Talking about it helps prevent it. Getting involved helps prevent it. The truth is, if childhood sexual abuse can be prevented, it can be stopped. That's why this curriculum exists - to empower adults through awareness and educational programs to prevent, recognize and react responsibly to childhood sexual abuse.
This curriculum along with "Recognizing and Reporting Child Abuse" will satisfy the Texas Legislature's requirement for education on this topic to all who work or volunteer with children.
