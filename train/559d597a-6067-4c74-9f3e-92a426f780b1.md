**Common Complaints & Quick Fixes**

---
**1. "My Wi-Fi keeps dropping out while gaming."**
- Check if other devices are on the same network.
- Update router firmware or reboot both router and device.
- Try a wired connection if possible.
- Move router away from walls/obstacles; place it centrally.

---
**2. "My phone battery drains too fast."**
- Reduce brightness, enable Do Not Disturb, and close unnecessary apps.
- Lower background app refresh settings in settings.
- Avoid extreme temperatures; charge in a cool environment.
- Use a high-quality charging cable and avoid overcharging.

---
**3. "Why does my printer always jam?"**
- Check for paper wrinkles or misaligned sheets.
- Clear the paper tray and reset the printer (power off/on).
- Inspect the paper path for debris; clean rollers if needed.
- Replace old or damaged toner cartridges if applicable.

---
**4. "My smart home device won’t connect to the app."**
- Restart both the device and router.
- Ensure the app is updated to the latest version.
- Check network signal strength and try a different Wi-Fi channel.
- Factory reset the device (backup settings first if possible).

---
**5. "My laptop overheats and shuts down unexpectedly."**
- Clean dust from vents/cooling fans (use compressed air).
- Adjust power settings to "High Performance" and enable cooling.
- Close unnecessary background apps to reduce CPU load.
- Upgrade to an external cooling pad if needed.

---
**6. "Why does my app crash on startup?"**
- Close and reopen the app.
- Clear app cache via Settings > Apps > [App] > Storage > Clear Cache.
- Reinstall the app if the issue persists.
- Check for system updates or compatibility issues with your device.

---
**7. "My cloud backup isn’t syncing properly."**
- Ensure storage space is available (check device/backup limits).
- Verify internet connection and Wi-Fi stability.
- Manually trigger a sync or check cloud service logs.
- Restart the device or app causing sync issues.

---
**8. "My car’s infotainment system isn’t responsive."**
- Restart the car’s computer (turn ignition off, wait 30 sec, restart).
- Check for software updates in the car’s settings.
- Disconnect and reconnect the USB/Bluetooth device.
- Reset the system to factory settings if needed.

---
**9. "My fitness tracker stops tracking after a few hours."**
- Ensure the tracker is fully charged and properly paired with the app.
- Check for app sync errors or connectivity issues.
- Update the tracker’s firmware or app.
- Place the tracker in a secure location (avoid water/extreme heat).

---
**10. "Why does my smart lock not unlock remotely?"**
- Verify the app is connected to the lock via Bluetooth/Wi-Fi.
- Check battery status (replace if low).
- Ensure the lock is in pairing mode when connecting.
- Reset the lock and re-pair it if the issue continues.

---
**Need a deeper troubleshoot?** Contact support with specific error codes or steps taken.
