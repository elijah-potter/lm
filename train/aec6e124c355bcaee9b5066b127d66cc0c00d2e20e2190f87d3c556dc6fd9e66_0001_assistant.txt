Darren Aronofsky is an American filmmaker known for his melodramatic, surreal,  and disturbing visual elements in his psychological fiction. Before studying at the AFI Conservatory, he studied film and social anthropology at Harvard University. 

1. Pi (1998) - a psychological thriller that follows a mathematician with an obsession to find complete order inside the real world. Contrasting the imperfect rationalities of humanity and the rigor and regularity of mathematics. 

2. Requiem For A Dream (2000) - a psychological drama that depicts four people affected by drug addiction. The film allows the audience a chance to watch the characters sink further into their addictions as the film progresses. It would receive an Academy Award nomination for Ellen Burstyn's performance. 

3. The Fountain (2006) - an epic drama that follows three storylines involving immortality.
