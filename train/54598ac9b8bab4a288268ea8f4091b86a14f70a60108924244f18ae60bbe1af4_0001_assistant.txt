In the beautiful city of Verona, where our story takes place,
Two families of equal social standing
Have a long-standing feud that erupts into violence,
Where innocent people are killed.
From the union of these two enemies,
A pair of star-crossed lovers are born.
Their tragic deaths finally end the feud between their families.

The story of the doomed love between them
and the enduring animosity of their parents, 
culminating in the tragedy of their children's deaths,
is the focus of our play. 
If you listen with an open heart,  
We will do our best to make up for any shortcomings.
