Glad you asked! Here are 6 popular, ethical, and sustainable brands:

1. Pact - a fairly affordable brand for the whole family using certified organic materials.
2. Everlane - timeless pieces, known for transparency in its manufacturing process.
3. Levi's - you weren't expecting this one, were you? Your Levi's are timeless, long-wearing, made with ethical cotton, and efficient use of water.
4. Hackwith Design House - HDH makes sure they waste less by creating each piece to order. 
5. Sezane - this Paris-based brand is very close to zero waste. They also operate an initiative to help disadvantaged children get good educations.
6. Rent the Runway - a sustainable alternative; renting clothes on a subscription basis, which are ethically dry-cleaned on return. They have a huge directory of brands available!
