Gummies coated in sugar crystals are healthy now, I guess. The pomegranate, elderflower grapefruit, and pear ginger flavors (they're not even made from real fruit purees?) are supposed to distract you from the fact that you're paying almost 40 bucks for a package of nine gummies the size of Reese's peanut butter cups. And not even the big ones--the small ones that you find in your trick-or-treating basket. The site even clarifies that they come in one size. Phew, I wouldn't want to accidentally pay $80 for the privilege of getting nine more! This is what--over $4 per gummy?
