The abbreviation i.e. should be placed in the middle of a sentence. It should appear after the first section to ensure grammatical correctness. An example is the sentence, “I.e. he likes Superman,” which would be incorrect. The correct way to use i.e. in a sentence would be, "He likes Superman, i.e., the superhero who also goes by the name Clark Kent."

The term i.e. means id est in Latin, this translates to "that is," in English. Its use case in English is to rephrase or elaborate on what was previously stated in the same sentence.
