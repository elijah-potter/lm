To start doing GelX nails at home, there are a few essentials you will need to purchase. Here is a list of them:

- GelX Nail Kit: This kit will include some of the fundamental items needed to get started such as nail tips, builder gel, and a UV/LED lamp. 
- Nail file and buffer: These are essential to shape and smooth your nail before applying the GelX nail tips and to shape the nail tips after applying. 
- Cuticle Pusher and Nipper: You will need a cuticle pusher and nipper to push back your cuticles and trim any excess skin.
- Nail polish: You will need your choice of nail polish to paint your nails once they are applied. You can use regular nail polish or gel nail polish on GelX nails. 
- Acetone: You will need this to soak off the nails once you want to remove them.
