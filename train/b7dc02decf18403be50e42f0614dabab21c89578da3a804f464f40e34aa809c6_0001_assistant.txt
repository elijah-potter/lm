Here are a few Brawlhalla characters you might enjoy, based on your blasters and axe preference.

- Lucien: Lucien is a high-damage character and he has good signature moves. He uses the blasters, which will help you feel comfortable with him right off the bat. He is great for players who have an aggressive play style.

- Thatch: Thatch is a character with a lot of personality and flair. He has good signature moves with the blasters, and the sword is an easy-to-use weapon that is very powerful. He is a good choice for players who like to be creative with their moves. You will need to be careful about his low defense, though.

- Teros: Teros has amazing signature moves that can help you control the match. His axe should help you feel comfortable with the character right away. His high-damage signatures can quickly knock your opponent off the stage. 

- Cross: Cross is a technical character with many powerful signatures. He is one of the best characters in 2v2, and he can hold his own in 1v1 too. His blasters should help you feel right at home, considering you like to play Barrazza. His high strength is great at securing early KOs.

- Xull: Xull has both an axe as well a cannon. His high strength can secure many early knockouts, and his side signatures can be very tricky to punish, due to the variable movement. He can dish out a high amount of damage and take a beating. Xull is great for players who are comfortable with an axe.
