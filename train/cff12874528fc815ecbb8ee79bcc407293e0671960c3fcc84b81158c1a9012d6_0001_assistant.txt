•	2007: The first-generation Kindle came out on November 19, 2007, and sold out of all its stock in under 6 hours.

•	2009: Kindle 2 launched in February and featured a text-to-speech option. Kindle DX was launched in May of 2009 with a screen size of 9.7 inches.

•	2010: Kindle DX Graphite was released on 7/1/10, featuring an improved contrast ratio display. Kindle Keyboard came out later in July and was considered the third generation of Kindle.

•	2011: On September 28, the unveiling of both the fourth-generation Kindle and the Kindle Touch took place. The fourth-gen Kindle's claim to fame was that it was less expensive than the previous versions at $109. The Kindle Touch was released in both WIFI and 3G versions.

•	2012: Amazon released the Kindle 5 on September 6, 2012, with two versions – ad-supported and no ads. Kindle Paperwhite launched on October 1st with built-in LED lights to make the screen brighter.
