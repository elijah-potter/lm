Timmy was a mischievous kid. He was always up to no good, causing trouble and making a mess wherever he went. One day, he did something really bad – he broke his mom's favorite vase while playing ball inside the house.

His mom found out what he had done. She scolded him and told him he was grounded for a week. Timmy didn't want to be grounded, so he decided to run away from home. He snuck out of the house and ran, trying to escape his mom's scolding.

As he ran, he came across a tall tree with sturdy branches. Without hesitation, he climbed up the tree in the hopes that his mom wouldn't be able to reach him. He sat atop the tree, safe from his mom's scolding, or so he thought.

Suddenly, a bee came buzzing around Timmy. Timmy was terrified of bees - he tried to swat the bee away, but it was too late. The bee stung him, and Timmy let out a loud cry! Timmy quickly realized that he couldn't stay up in the tree any longer. He climbed down the tree, and ran straight back to his mother, crying all the way.

When his mother saw him, all hurt and stung, she hugged him and told him that he couldn't escape the consequences of his actions. She explained to him that what he did was wrong and that he had to face the consequences of his actions. Timmy realized that his mother was right, and he learned a valuable lesson that day.

From that day on, Timmy tried his best to be a good boy and to always try and make things right when he made mistakes. He knew that even if he didn't get caught, there were always consequences to his actions. And so, he lived happily ever after, never causing trouble or mischief again!
