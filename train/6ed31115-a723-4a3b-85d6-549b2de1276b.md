In the quiet hum of a forgotten library, where books whispered secrets to the passing breeze, I found a shelf marked *"Lost Writings."* The air smelled of aged parchment and forgotten ink. My fingers traced the faded title: *"The Thread That Unravels"*—a novel left incomplete, its last page torn away.

The last sentence, scrawled in hasty hand, read:
*"The clock struck midnight, but the stars didn’t blink—only waited."*

I turned the page. Empty.
