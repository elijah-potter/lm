**Downtown Plaza**
Traders haggle over fresh produce in open-air markets, while street musicians play traditional tunes under flickering lanterns. Locals gather for late-night *tapas* crawls, sharing cold beers at rooftop terraces. Tourists explore historic landmarks, while artists sell handcrafted souvenirs. Busy cafés serve *tortas* and espresso, and nightlife buzzes with live jazz and flamenco dancing in converted warehouses.
