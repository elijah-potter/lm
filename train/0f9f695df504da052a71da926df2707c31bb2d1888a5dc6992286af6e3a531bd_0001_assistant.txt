Ann Beretta is a dynamic punk rock band. They are originally from Richmond, Virginia and formed in 1996. Their high-energy music blends straightforward punk with captivating elements of rockabilly and country, creating an exhilarating sound. The founding members were, Rob Huddleston, Leer Baker, Chris Rupp and Russ Jones. 

With a career spanning years of touring alongside other notable bands and releasing albums under respected labels, Ann Beretta has established themselves as a force to be reckoned with in the punk rock scene. Their music resonates with fans who crave the raw power and infectious, electrifying melodies that define their sound.
