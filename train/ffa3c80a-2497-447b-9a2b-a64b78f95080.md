A solitary figure stands in a dimly lit lobby, a clock ticking loudly in the corner, its hands moving slowly in a loop of minutes. The air hums with the distant murmur of a crowd outside, the sound muffled through half-closed doors. They clench their fists, knuckles white, as they brace against the wall, jaw tight.

A voice—soft, measured—cuts through the silence. **"You’re next."**
The figure turns, but only for a moment before the door swings open again. Another person, ghostly in the flickering overhead light, steps inside, their shadow stretching long across the floor. The first person exhales sharply, then begins to count the seconds. **"One… two…"** The second person lingers, watching, their breath visible in the cold air.

The first person’s fingers trace the edge of the waiting area, the nails biting into their palms. The door creaks shut behind the second person, sealing them both inside. The first person’s eyes flicker toward the clock, then away. The seconds stretch, a silent torture, until—

**"Three…"**
The door creaks open. The second person steps out, their posture rigid, their face pale. The first person doesn’t move. Doesn’t speak. Doesn’t look away.

The lobby is empty again. The clock ticks on.
