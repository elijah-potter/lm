The grand hall’s chandelier hummed faintly as the fire cast long shadows across the polished oak floor. A velvet-clad figure stood near the hearth, their fingers tracing the rim of a goblet filled with amber liquid. The air smelled of spiced wine and distant incense.

"By the sun that rises on the seventh day," the stranger murmured, their voice low and measured, "I swear this to you—my word is iron, my oath unbroken. What you seek, you shall have, but know this: the cost is not measured in gold, nor in blood, but in the weight of your own choices."

The crowd murmured, but the woman at the center of it all—her fingers trembling slightly—only stared at the goblet, the promise hanging between them like a curse.
