A flickering lantern cast long shadows over cracked stone paths, their edges blurred by damp mist. The air hummed with the distant chorus of unseen creatures, their calls weaving into the silence like whispered secrets. A lone figure knelt beside a half-buried artifact, fingers tracing the worn surface of a forgotten language etched into its surface, the symbols shifting under their touch. A low, rhythmic drumming echoed from the ruins behind, the rhythm growing stronger as the figure rose, dust clinging to their cloak as they stepped into the fading light.

The wind carried the scent of damp earth and something older—burnt wood, iron, and the faint, metallic tang of blood. The artifact in their grip pulsed faintly, a slow, steady glow that pulsed like a heartbeat. Shadows stretched unnaturally long, twisting around the edges of the ruins like living things, moving when no one was looking. The figure’s breath came in shallow gasps, their grip tightening on the artifact as the ruins behind them seemed to exhale, a slow, deliberate sigh of decay.
