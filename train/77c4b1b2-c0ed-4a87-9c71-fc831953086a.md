A floating academy of quantum physics experiments, suspended in a low-gravity arcane zone where time bends unpredictably. Rules here are rewritten by the hum of perpetual experiments and the whispers of the void—one must solve a puzzle before the next paradox unfolds. The sky is a shifting canvas of neon holograms, and the ground is a maze of self-repairing circuitry. Only those who understand the game’s meta-rules may claim a seat at the central table where the future is a shared, rewritable ledger.
