Lily the little bunny hopped through the sunny meadow, her paws kicking up tiny clouds of grass. She had a big, shiny red apple in her basket, and she was so happy—she could eat it right there!

Suddenly, a tiny mouse named Timmy ran into her path. "Lily, help me!" he squeaked. His basket was full of acorns, but a big, grumpy badger had grabbed them and was hiding them in his burrow.

Lily’s ears perked up. She knew badgers didn’t like sharing! She hopped over to Timmy’s basket and took out one acorn. "Let’s hide them together!" she said.

They dug a little hole in the dirt, buried the acorns, and made a little sign with their paw prints: **"ACORNS FOR ALL!"** Timmy cheered, "We did it!"

The badger sniffed around but couldn’t find the acorns. He grumbled and walked away, and soon, Lily and Timmy shared the meadow’s snacks—acorns, berries, and even a little honey from a beehive.

From that day on, they always helped others, and the meadow was the happiest place in the world!
