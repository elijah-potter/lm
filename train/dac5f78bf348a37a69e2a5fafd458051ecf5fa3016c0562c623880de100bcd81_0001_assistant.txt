A wild horse was grazing in a field of green grass. Its mane, while scruffy and messy, was flowing beautifully in the breeze. Its coat was shining in the sun. It was a deep dark brown but had a pearlescent glow of gold glaring in the sun's rays. As it chewed the grass, it exhaled a guttural sigh through its nose. When it was done in one spot, it would slowly trot to another spot in the field and begin eating more grass. Gracefully, the horse meandered through the field until it caught a spurt of energy and galloped away, over a hill, and out of sight.
