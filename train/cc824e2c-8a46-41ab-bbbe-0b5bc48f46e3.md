1. **The Forgotten Keys**: I keep a list of passwords in my phone’s notes app—*passwords* plural, not just *password*. My first confession: I wrote *"sunny123"* under *"bank"* because I panicked and didn’t think to change it. The second: I once shared *"Qwerty!"* with my coworker to "just get it done," but now it’s crossed out in red ink.

2. **The Unsent Letter**: The confession I kept inside a sealed envelope was for my therapist—*not* my mom. It started with: *"You’re not the first to say ‘I’m sorry for what I did,’ but you’re the first to *listen*."* The list below was my attempt to name the sins I couldn’t bear to admit: *"Lies told to cover up the time I stayed out all night. The stolen coffee from the office. The way I ignored your calls for three days after you got sick."*

3. **The Nightmare Sequence**:
   - **Step 1**: I wrote *"What if I’m not forgiven?"* on a napkin at a diner. It was crossed out by a stranger who gave me a twenty-dollar bill.
   - **Step 2**: The confession was *"I don’t deserve you"* scribbled in the margins of a library book about grief. The list under it: *"Things I’ve taken. Things I’ve left undone. Things I’ve kept quiet for my own peace."*
   - **Step 3**: On the train home, I printed out the confession and burned it in the bathroom. The ashes fell into a list of phone numbers I didn’t call back.
