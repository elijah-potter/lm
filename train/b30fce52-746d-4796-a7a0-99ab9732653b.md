**Avoiding Overuse of "Shortcut" in Technical Writing**
For readability and clarity in documentation, consider using alternatives like **"optimization," "feature," "workaround," or "process"** depending on context.

---
**Example Rephrasing (if needed for technical clarity):**
Instead of *"This is a shortcut for X"* → *"This method provides a concise alternative to perform X efficiently."*
