Here is a list of game titles involving superheroes.

1. Superhero Squad: Superhero Squad is a video game where you can create your superhero and team up with other heroes to fight crime and protect the city. The game will feature many different missions, each with unique challenges. You can also customize your superhero's appearance and powers making them truly unique.

2. Superhero Academy: Superhero Academy is a video game where you attend a superhero academy. While there you will learn to use your powers to fight crime and protect the innocent. The game features a variety of different classes, each with its challenges. You can make friends with other students and eventually create your own superhero team.

3. Cypher: The Codebreaker: Cypher is a superhero who can decode any language. He can read any code, and he can translate between different languages. In this game, you will play Cypher and use your powers to solve puzzles and protect the city. You will travel to different cities and use your powers to stop criminals by decoding their secrets. You will also team up with other heroes to fight evil.

4. Havoc: Master of Gravity: Havoc is a superhero who can control gravity. He can create black holes, and even fly. In this game, you will play as Havoc and use your powers to protect the innocent and stop evil. You must be careful though because Havoc is very powerful but his powers can cause a lot of collateral damage. It is up to the player to decide if they want to become the evil they are trying so hard to stop.
