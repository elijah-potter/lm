"Images worth a thousand words". Images are independent of student skill level because you will utilize these images for open-ended responses without time frame restrictions and to convey multiple opinions and messages. Images are a flexible tool so the teacher can designate the topic, conversation or quantity of words that may be needed in daily lessons. Images can also be used to introduce or reinforce your unit and your students will be inspired to learn and internalize words and expressions that are important to them in their discussions.
Instead of the transference generally associated with showing a word in one language and then translating it into the second language, avoid the transference by using images to stimulate word association and conversation. The brain is an amazing tool and can use images to help commit vocabulary to memory. Temple Grandin referred to it as “thinking in pictures”. What an enjoyable way to produce communication.
How many other ways can you think of to utilize these pictures? How many questions or statements can be created about the items? Students can use these images for simple identification or they can spiral into more complex communication and detail the benfits of using certain objects for a particular class activity.
