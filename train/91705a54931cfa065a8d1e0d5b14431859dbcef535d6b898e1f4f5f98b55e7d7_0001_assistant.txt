Though there are a number of risk factors for kidney cancer, no one actually understands what causes kidney cancer. It usually develops in people over 40 years old. A few of the major risk factors include:
•	Obesity
•	Smoking
•	High blood pressure
•	Dialysis

Other risk factors include workers in industries that come in contact with certain chemicals and other substances at the workplace. Exposure to asbestos and cadmium has been shown to cause kidney cancer. Men are more likely than women to be diagnosed with it.

Each of us has two kidneys that are bean-shaped and about the size of your fist. They are located behind the abdomen on each side of the spine. Renal cell carcinoma is the most common type of kidney cancer but young children are more likely to develop a strain known as Wilms' tumor. Research shows that the incidence of kidney cancer is increasing, but there’s no clear answer to why. Computerized tomography (CT scans) are most often used for diagnosis.
