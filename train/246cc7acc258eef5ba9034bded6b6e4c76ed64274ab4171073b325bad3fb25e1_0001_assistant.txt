There once was a girl named Susan who loved to wear a very tall pink hat with flowers on top. No matter where she went, people would stare at her hat in awe, and she loved all of the attention. One day, while she was walking through a park, a gust of wind blew her hat off her head where it floated into a nearby pond. She was devastated, but as she reached into the water to retrieve her hat, she realized that the flowers had bloomed even more beautifully underwater. It turns out that the flowers on her hat were rare Anubias flowers and bloomed beautifully underwater. From then on, she wore her hat even more proudly, knowing that its true beauty was not in its height or pretty color, but in the unique flowers that adorned it.
