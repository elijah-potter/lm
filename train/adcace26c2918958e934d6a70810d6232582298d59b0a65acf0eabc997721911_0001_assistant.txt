One day, there was a lady named Aqua taking a train to the airport. The plane was leaving in four hours, so she gave herself enough to get comfortable and check in once she got there. Suddenly, the train stopped and she was flung forward a bit. Over the speakers was an announcement. "Sorry for the inconvenience, ladies and gentlemen. We seem to run into a bit of a problem with the train engine, and it will take about an hour to fix. We apologize for any trouble incurred by this, and we will try to fix this as fast as possible." 

Aqua was angry. Did they think to look at the train engine before starting for the day? There was nothing she could do, so she sat back down in her seat and looked outside. It was unnaturally foggy; you couldn't see anything outside. Aqua was getting a little curious. She stood up and paced back and forward in the train car that no one but her was in. Curiosity turned into fear as suddenly, the train doors slid open without a warning.

Aqua heard the train doors slide open without an announcement. She started to get scared. "What is happening? she thought. She walked over to the door and looked outside. The fog was so deep that a flashlight couldn't penetrate it.  What was once fear turned back into curiosity as Aqua tried to walk outside. It felt like there was no ground to stand on, but the fog was firm enough for Aqua to stand on. She kept walking and walking, making sure to stay close to the train. The fear was settling in, she didn't know where she was and there was no one in the train cars next to her as well.

She tried to go back to her train car but there was this sudden, mesmerizing light in her path. The next thing Aqua knew, she saw space in front of her and below her, the town that she left. She was in the sky!?
