Asian Fusion Crepes:

1. Vanilla Plum Swirl - This crepe features Chinese plum sauce swirled in vanilla ice cream. The ice cream is made fresh daily with real vanilla beans. The crepe is topped with whipped cream, a balsamic vinegar syrup reduction, and a sprinkling of almond cookies for a burst of crunchy texture. 

2. Sweet and Sour Apple Crunch - Granny Smith apples are diced and lightly pan-fried in a brown sugar soy sauce glaze creating the most delicious sweet and sour bite. The apples are placed into the delicate crepe wrapper and covered with a dusting of powdered sugar and a hint of ginger.

3. Caramel Fortunes - Fortune cookie pieces are crumbled and folded into an almond ice cream base, creating a delectable combination of flavors and textures. The crepes are topped with a caramel drizzle and served with two fortune cookies on the side.

4. Five Spice Pistachio - Pistachio ice cream, with whole pistachios mixed in, is amplified with the flavor of Chinese five spice powder. The warm, almost autumnal spice blend fuses perfectly with the nutty flavors of the pistachio. The crepe is finished off with crushed pistachios and whipped cream.

5. Hoisin Crispy Bacon - This unique crepe features bacon bits that are fried up and then coated in a sugary syrup and Hoisin sauce mixture. The bacon becomes both salty and sweet creating a perfectly balanced eating experience. The bacon is folded into a brown butter custard before being rolled inside a delicious crepe. Served with a chocolate sauce on top and a fruit salad on the side.
