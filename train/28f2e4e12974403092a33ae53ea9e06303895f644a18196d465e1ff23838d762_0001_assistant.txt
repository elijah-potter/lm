Dear Annie,

How are you? I'd really love to know. It's been some time since we last talked, spent time, and laughed together. We got along quite well if you recall. I enjoyed your company, humor, lovely persona, and your friendship. I miss all that. Separating after our campus time has dealt a blow to what we had.
When we were on campus those years ago, we were both very different people than we are now. Younger, more adventurous, and foolish. I'm sure our lives and levels of wisdom have since changed.  
I wish we'd continued spending time together, and known what goes on in each other's lives. We for sure have lost a lot, and have a lot to share.
When you read this, know that I'd love for us to connect again. I think of you more often, and I know we'll have a good time when we meet. Bye for now.

Your friend,
Quinn
