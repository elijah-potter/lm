**BREAKING:** Mayor Thompson Announces Emergency Water Restrictions Amid Unprecedented Drought in Eldermere

Government officials have declared a **Level 3 Water Emergency** effective immediately in Eldermere, following a severe drought that has reduced reservoir levels to **24% capacity** over the past six months. Mayor Elena Thompson addressed the press in a hastily convened meeting, emphasizing that "this is a crisis that requires immediate action to prevent long-term damage to the city’s infrastructure and economy."

Residents are ordered to reduce household water use by **30%**, with restrictions on outdoor irrigation, car washing, and non-essential watering. Public fountains and drinking fountains will operate on a **rotating schedule** starting tomorrow. Municipal workers will prioritize repairs to aging pipes and leak detection in high-risk areas.

Economic impacts are expected to be severe, with businesses warned to prepare for reduced consumer activity. The city’s water treatment plant has implemented emergency protocols, but officials urge citizens to avoid panic—supply chain disruptions are being managed at source.

**Protests expected** as residents voice frustration over delayed infrastructure funding. The city council is convening an emergency session later today to propose additional measures, including a potential **tax levy on water-intensive industries**.
