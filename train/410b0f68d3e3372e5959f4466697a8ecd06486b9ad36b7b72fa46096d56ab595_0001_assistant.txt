Here are some great options for a fun and unique trip! 

Waco, Texas - Because of Chip and Joanna Gaines and Magnolia, Waco has become a desired place to visit. Visit Magnolia Market by day and listen to live music with drinks in hand at night. 

Bar Harbor, Maine - Relax with amazing views, take a hike, or go kayaking. There are many activities to keep you entertained during the day and plenty of fun spots in the evening, including lots of fresh seafood. 

Anna Maria Island, Florida - The beaches are beautiful, and the quaint town has cute shops. There are several options to enjoy a night out with delicious food offerings.
