Dear Sir or Madam,
I am writing to inform you that my dinner was unacceptably late last night. Twenty minutes is far too long for a respectable cat such as myself to be expected to wait for a simple meal service. If this problem continues in the future, I will have no choice but to bring it to the attention of your superiors in Upper Management. However, I do hope that you take this message to heart so that further action on my part will not be necessary.
Respectfully yours,
The Cat
