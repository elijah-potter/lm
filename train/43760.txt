Describe The Crystal Building of London
Describe The Crystal Building of London. Research “The Crystal London” building. Do not too much focus on the time period, and focus on the architecture (history, function, design, etc.). After learning about and writing about your subject, render some opinions on it in the summary at the end of the paper. Four is the minimum number of sources, and I prefer for 2 of these to be published (i.e., in book or journal form), while the other 2 can come from the internet.
If you like, you could choose ONE example of the same building type, and make a comparison: describe their similarities, and their differences, and discuss the reasons for the differences (technological development? cultural differences? etc.) DO NOT CHOOSE A COUPLE OF EXAMPLES.
