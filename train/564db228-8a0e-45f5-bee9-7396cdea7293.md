**First Ending:**
A lone traveler in a dying village finds a sealed chest containing a golden key. With trembling hands, they unlock it to reveal a map to an ancient ruin. The next morning, the traveler stands before the ruins’ entrance, their breath steady. Inside, they find a library where every book whispers prophecies of a forgotten civilization. Choosing to read the one titled *"The Weight of Echoes,"* they discover their own name inscribed in the margins. As they leave, the ruins’ stones whisper back: *"You were never lost. You were always chosen."* The traveler disappears into the mist, leaving behind only the faint scent of cinnamon and the echo of a single verse from the book: *"The past is a door, but the future is a choice."*

---

**Second Ending:**
The village elder warns the traveler not to seek the ruin, but their curiosity is stronger. By nightfall, they arrive at the ruins’ edge, where the ground splits open, revealing a labyrinth of shifting shadows. Inside, they find the library—but the books are not books. They are mirrors, reflecting not images, but the traveler’s own fears: drowning in a storm, laughing in a grave, or standing forever in a hallway. The key they hold shatters on the floor. The ruins collapse behind them, and the traveler wakes on the village square, the golden key in their pocket, now tarnished. The elder smiles: *"You found the ruin. Now you must find yourself."* The traveler is given a single task: *"Tell the story of what you saw."* Months later, they return, this time carrying a new chest. Inside: a single book, titled *"The Weight of Echoes,"* its pages filled with their own words. The village celebrates.
