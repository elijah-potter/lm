What is a hyperboloid of revolution?
A hyperboloid is a surface created by deforming a hyperboloid of revolution using directional scalings, or more broadly, an affine transformation. A hyperboloid of revolution, also known as a circular hyperbola, is a surface created by rotating a hyperbola around one of its primary axes in geometry.
