Once upon a time in a cozy little house, there lived a bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the garden. One sunny morning, Benny found a shiny red apple that sparkled like a star. "Oh, Benny," Benny thought, "this must be magic!"

Benny took the apple and jumped into the air. Suddenly, he felt a big, happy tickle in his tummy! He giggled and giggled. Then, Benny saw his best friend, a little squirrel named Sammy, running towards him.

"Benny, Benny!" Sammy squeaked. "Did you find a magic apple?"

Benny nodded. "It makes me happy!"

Sammy grinned. "Let's share it!"

Benny and Sammy took turns eating the apple. Benny felt even happier and tickled. "This is the best apple ever!" Benny cheered.

After eating, Benny and Sammy had a picnic under a big, fluffy cloud. They laughed, played, and enjoyed every moment. The sky turned golden, and the garden sparkled with joy. Benny and Sammy knew they were the luckiest bunny and squirrel pair in the whole world.

As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and orange, Benny and Sammy snuggled together. "Tomorrow, we'll find another magic apple," Sammy whispered.

Benny nodded, his eyes closed, dreaming of more adventures. And with a happy heart, they fell asleep, dreaming of all the wonderful things that could happen tomorrow.
