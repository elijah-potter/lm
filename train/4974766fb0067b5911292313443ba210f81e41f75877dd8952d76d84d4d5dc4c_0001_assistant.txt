First, sit in your seat and buckle your seatbelt. Press the ON/OFF button to engage the engine. You can move forward by pressing the foot pedal like a vehicle, and use the steering wheel to control the direction. If you need individual legs to perform certain functions, use the individual joysticks for the corresponding leg. Giant robotic spiders cannot create silk, so there is no button or lever for that function. When you're finished press the ON/OFF button one more time.
