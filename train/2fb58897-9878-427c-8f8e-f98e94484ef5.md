*The kitchen was quiet, the hum of the fridge the only sound. Clara adjusted her gloves, fingers brushing the edge of the coffee table where the half-empty mug sat. Her father’s old teacup, cracked in places but still holding its shape.*

*Outside, the rain pattered against the window—too soft to be thunder, just the kind of light drizzle that made the streetlamps glow like scattered stars. He hadn’t asked for coffee. She’d known.*

*She passed him the mug, its warmth sinking into her palms before she’d even reached for it. His hands found the cup without looking, the way they always did when they were close enough to touch without words. The steam curled between them, a silent promise.*
