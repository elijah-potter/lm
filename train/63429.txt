The STEM Learning Project curriculum resources illustrate a problem-based learning approach – challenging students to seek solutions to open-ended, real-world problems.
These two hour sessions are a short introduction, presenting an overview that will help realise the application of the modules in your classroom.
The workshop provides a rationale for STEM and outlines the instructional model for problem-based STEM learning. It serves as an opportunity for teachers to unpack curriculum modules in year-based groups and plan for their implementation.
Please note that the workshop is open to Department of Education schools only.
