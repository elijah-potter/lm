In the dim glow of flickering lanterns, figures moved along a narrow path lined with twisted roots and cracked stone. The air hummed with the distant chant of unseen voices, their melodies weaving through the thick, damp fog. A child pressed against the rusted gate, clutching a half-rotten fruit, while shadows coiled around the abandoned watchtower’s jagged edges. The wind carried whispers of the past, rustling through dead leaves that clung to ancient walls. A lantern’s flame flickered erratically as it caught on a spider’s silk, casting long, writhing shadows across the forgotten courtyard.
