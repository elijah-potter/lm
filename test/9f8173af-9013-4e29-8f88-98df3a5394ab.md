**Lost City of Atlantis: A Disappeared Paradise**

### **Geographical Overview**
Atlantis, once a thriving metropolis, was located in the Atlantic Ocean off the coast of modern-day **Sardinia, Italy** (historically known as *Tartessos* or *El Djem*). Its exact location remains debated, but archaeological evidence suggests a submerged settlement around **1500 BCE**, possibly due to a **megatsunami** or **climate collapse**.
