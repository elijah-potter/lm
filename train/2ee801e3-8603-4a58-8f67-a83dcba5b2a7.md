The elderly man adjusted his glasses as the café’s humming background faded into silence. Across the table, his partner—now silent—leaned in, fingers tapping the table. His eyes flickered to a half-empty mug of tea before returning to her. "Remember that time we took that road trip to the Black Hills?" he said, voice steady but distracted. "We almost got lost in the middle of nowhere, and you insisted we stop for that… *thing* you swore was a buffalo." A pause. "You were right about the buffalo, actually." She exhaled sharply. "I was *so* nervous about the trip, I forgot to pack sunscreen."
