The dim glow of a flickering lantern cast long shadows across the cracked wooden floor of the abandoned chapel. Dust motes swirled in the stale air, settling like silent witnesses to the scene. A shattered stained-glass window, its remaining fragments jagged and stained with blood, let in a pale, eerie light. A broken chair stood overturned near the altar, its cushions torn and splattered with dark liquid, possibly blood or something darker. The altar itself was overturned, its golden crucifix toppled, its nails bent and twisted. At the base, a torn altar cloth stained crimson with something that looked like dried blood or perhaps paint. On the far wall, a half-dissolved candle stub flickered, its wax dripping onto the floor. A rusted iron cross leaned precariously against the wall, its base twisted as if something heavy had been dragged across it. The air smelled of damp earth, mildew, and something metallic—like old blood or rust. Near the entrance, a wooden door stood slightly ajar, its hinges groaning faintly. A single, half-buried boot heel was embedded in the dirt near the threshold, and a small, discarded key lay beside it, its chain snapped. The floorboards under the overturned chair were stained, and something cold and wet lay beneath them, half-buried. The last visible light came from a crack in the ceiling, where a single, faintly glowing patch of mildewed plaster gave a single, dimly shifting glow.
