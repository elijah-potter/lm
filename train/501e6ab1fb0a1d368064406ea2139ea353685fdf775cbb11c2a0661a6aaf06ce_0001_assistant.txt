Q: What event has had the greatest impact on modern Americans according to a survey by Pew Research Center and A+E Networks' HISTORY?

A: The Sept. 11, 2001, terrorist attacks have had the greatest impact on modern Americans according to a survey by Pew Research Center and A+E Networks' HISTORY.

Q: How many people surveyed included the attacks in their top 10 historically significant events?

A: Roughly 76% of the 2,025 adults surveyed included the attacks in their top 10 historically significant events.

Q: Did the attacks impact all demographic groups similarly?

A: Yes, majorities of men and women, Millennials and Baby Boomers, Americans with college degrees and those without a high school diploma, as well as both Republicans and Democrats, included the attacks in their top 10.

Q: What is the one exception to this pattern?

A: Views of blacks and whites differ slightly, with the election of President Barack Obama sharing the top spot with the Sept. 11 attacks among blacks, while the civil rights movement ranks higher for blacks than it does for whites.
