A vast, half-submerged city of rusted steel and cracked glass floats in an endless, eerie tide of bioluminescent algae. The air hums with the faint, distorted whispers of drowned voices, their words dissolving like mist. Towering cranes, half-built and skeletal, pierce the sky like broken teeth, their cables frayed and dead. The water here is a murky green, reflecting nothing but the ghostly glow of the submerged streets below. Every movement feels like a slow, deliberate exhalation—too heavy, too deliberate—before the world stops and waits. The wind is still, just a whisper of something unseen pushing through the cracks in the walls, as if the entire place is holding its breath, afraid to let go.
