## Project Chimera: A Cooperative Research Game

**A Rulebook for 2-4 Players**

**Introduction**

Project Chimera places you in the roles of specialized researchers at the Global Research Initiative (GRI), a clandestine organization dedicated to addressing existential threats. A novel, rapidly evolving pathogen – Chimera – has emerged, exhibiting unprecedented mutation rates and adaptability. Your mission is to understand Chimera’s behavior, develop countermeasures, and ultimately contain the escalating global crisis before it overwhelms civilization. This is a cooperative game; you win or lose together.

**1. Components**

*   **Research Board:** A central board depicting a world map, tracking global infection levels, and displaying research progress.
*   **Researcher Cards (8):** Each card represents a researcher with unique skills and starting resources.
*   **Chimera Mutation Cards (60):** These cards dictate Chimera’s evolving characteristics and introduce new challenges.
*   **Research Cards (40):** Represent potential breakthroughs in understanding and combating Chimera.
*   **Resource Tokens:** Represent Funding (blue), Data (yellow), and Personnel (red).
*   **Infection Markers:** Used to track Chimera’s spread on the Research Board.
*   **Containment Markers:** Used to represent successful containment efforts.
*   **Crisis Tokens:** Represent escalating global events triggered by Chimera’s mutations.
*   **Dice (3):** Six-sided dice used for skill checks and event resolution.
*   **First Player Marker:** Indicates the current active player.

**2. Setup**

1.  Place the Research Board in the center of the playing area.
2.  Shuffle the Chimera Mutation Cards and place them face down in a designated draw pile. Reveal the top three cards and place them face up beside the draw pile. These represent Chimera’s current mutations.
3.  Shuffle the Research Cards and place them face down in a designated draw pile. Reveal the top three cards and place them face up beside the draw pile.
4.  Place a predetermined number of Infection Markers on the starting regions indicated on the Research Board (see Difficulty Scaling below).
5.  Each player chooses a Researcher Card and takes the corresponding starting resources indicated on the card.
6.  Place the First Player Marker with a randomly selected player.

**3. Turn Structure**

Each player's turn consists of the following phases, performed in order:

1.  **Action Phase (3 Actions):** You may perform any combination of the following actions, up to a maximum of three:
    *   **Research:** Spend Data to draw and attempt to resolve a Research Card. Success requires a skill check (see Skill Checks).
    *   **Analyze Mutation:** Spend Data to discard one face-up Chimera Mutation Card and draw a new one.
    *   **Deploy Personnel:** Spend Personnel to place a Containment Marker in a region with Infection Markers.
    *   **Gather Resources:** Choose one resource type (Funding, Data, or Personnel) and gain 2 tokens of that type.
    *   **Move:** Spend Personnel to move your researcher to an adjacent region on the Research Board.
2.  **Chimera Phase:**
    *   **Mutation Advance:** Draw the top Chimera Mutation Card and add it to the face-up mutations. If there are already three face-up, discard one of your choice.
    *   **Spread:** Roll a die. The result determines how many regions Chimera spreads to, following the spread pattern indicated on the current Chimera Mutation Cards. Place Infection Markers accordingly.
3.  **Pass the First Player Marker:** Pass the First Player Marker to the player on your left.

**4. Skill Checks**

Many actions require skill checks. To perform a skill check:

1.  Determine the required skill level (indicated on the card or action description).
2.  Roll a die.
3.  Add your Researcher's relevant skill bonus (indicated on their Researcher Card) to the die roll.
4.  If the total meets or exceeds the required skill level, the check is successful. Otherwise, it fails.

**5. Resource Trade-offs & Uncertainty**

The game features mechanics designed to create difficult choices under conditions of incomplete information:

*   **Mutation Prioritization:** When discarding a Chimera Mutation Card, you must choose between discarding a mutation that poses an immediate threat (potentially impacting a specific region) or one that might trigger a more severe, global crisis later. The full consequences of each mutation are not always immediately clear.
*   **Research Investment:** Research Cards often require a combination of Data and Personnel. You must decide whether to invest heavily in a promising breakthrough that might be crucial for long-term containment or spread resources to address more pressing, localized outbreaks.
*   **Containment Allocation:** Deploying Containment Markers is vital, but limited Personnel force you to prioritize regions. Choosing to protect a densely populated area might leave a less critical region vulnerable, potentially triggering a cascade of infections.

**6. Win/Loss Conditions**

*   **Winning:** You win the game if you successfully complete a designated number of Research Cards (determined by difficulty – see Difficulty Scaling) *before* any of the Loss Conditions are met.
*   **Losing:** You lose the game if:
    *   Chimera’s infection level reaches the maximum indicated on the Research Board.
    *   A Crisis Token is drawn and cannot be resolved (indicated on the Crisis Token).
    *   The Chimera Mutation Card draw pile is exhausted, and you have not met the win condition.

**7. Difficulty Scaling**

The game's difficulty scales based on the number of players:

*   **2 Players:** Start with 3 Infection Markers on the starting regions. Required Research Cards to win: 4.
*   **3 Players:** Start with 4 Infection Markers on the starting regions. Required Research Cards to win: 5.
*   **4 Players:** Start with 5 Infection Markers on the starting regions. Required Research Cards to win: 6.



**8. Advanced Variants**

*   **Limited Communication:** Restrict verbal communication between players during the Action Phase, forcing reliance on observation and non-verbal cues.
*   **Dynamic Mutations:** Introduce a system where Chimera Mutations can trigger additional, unpredictable events, increasing the game's complexity.
*   **Specialized Research:** Grant each Researcher a unique, ongoing ability that significantly alters their role and capabilities, creating more asymmetric gameplay.



**Designer’s Note**

Project Chimera was designed to be more than just a game. Our intention is to create an engaging experience that encourages collaborative problem-solving and critical thinking. The escalating crisis and the difficult choices presented within the game are intended to simulate the complexities of responding to real-world challenges. We hope that players will find themselves discussing strategies, weighing risks, and appreciating the importance of teamwork in the face of adversity. The game's mechanics are simplified representations of scientific processes, aiming to spark curiosity and inspire a deeper understanding of the scientific method.
