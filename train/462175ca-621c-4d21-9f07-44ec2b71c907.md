The river hummed a lullaby of stone and tide,
while shadows whispered the same refrain of sky—
"Where the light bends, there the heart finds its way."

The clock’s hands spun, repeating the same,
a cycle carved in dust and fading grace:
*"You are not alone—you are the echo."*

The wind carried the same old refrain,
a chorus of the forgotten and the sown:
*"The earth remembers what the stars have spun."*

The same old tale of dawn and dusk,
of love that fades like morning’s blush—
the world repeats itself, as always, as such.
