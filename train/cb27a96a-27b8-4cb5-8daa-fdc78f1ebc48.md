The neon glow of a dimly lit alley flickered against the rain-slicked pavement as the silhouette of a figure hunched over a crumpled map, fingers tracing over faded ink. The scent of damp asphalt and old coffee clung to the air, thick with uncertainty. Their breath came in shallow gasps, each one a whisper against the wind that carried no answers. The object sought—a memory, a name, a fleeting sensation—had no shape, no form, only an insistent pull deeper into the shadows where the echoes of the past still whispered.

A distant car horn cut through the silence, its sound swallowed by the distance. The figure’s gaze lingered on the streetlamp’s flicker, its light catching the edges of a half-remembered face, but the face dissolved like mist before it could hold focus. The map unrolled further, ink bleeding into the edges where the lines blurred into nothingness. Somewhere beyond this, a door stood open, but the key was not in their hands—only in the hollow between their teeth, something they had swallowed long ago and could never retrieve.
