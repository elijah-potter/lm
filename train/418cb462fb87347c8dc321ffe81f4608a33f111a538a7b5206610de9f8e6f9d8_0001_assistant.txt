Your players must traverse a large swath of grassland on the way to their next destination. Large boulders jut out of the ground here and there among the dry grass. There's a narrow path that curves through the area, only wide enough for one to pass at a time. They've been warned to stick to the path. But, as it winds and curves, it becomes apparent that their journey would be much shorter if they cut across the grassland instead. 
If they go off the path, they first encounter the remnants of a scarecrow that has seen better days. A slight change in the foliage indicates what was once an agricultural field but is now overgrown. Nearby is a dilapidated farmhouse. It appears it's been vacant for a long time. Then, the ground begins to shake. Stepping out from behind a large boulder is a fearsome Ankylosaurus! They must roll for initiative. 
Should they decide to stay on the path, they hear rumbling and feel the ground shake, indicating there's danger nearby, but they don't encounter the Ankylosaurus.
