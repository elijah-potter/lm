A dimly lit hallway stretches ahead, its walls lined with peeling yellow paint. The air smells faintly of damp earth and old wood, thick with the lingering scent of something metallic—blood?—mixed with a sharp tang of ozone. A single flickering bulb above casts long, jagged shadows that dance across the cracked tile floor.

A door on the left stands slightly ajar, its hinges groaning as if resisting. The space beyond seems to have been disturbed: a broken chair lies upside down near the threshold, its legs splintered, and a half-empty bottle of whiskey glints on the floor, its label torn. A single sock, dusted with dirt, clutches a small, oddly shaped object—a smooth stone, perhaps, or a piece of bone?—near its heel.

The opposite wall is bare except for a single, heavy trunk propped against the corner, its lid half-open, revealing dust and what looks like scattered papers, their edges frayed. A faint, rhythmic thumping comes from inside, like someone—or something—slowly turning something over.
