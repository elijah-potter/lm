To: "Batboy"
From: Your "servant"
Subject: I'm Tired

Mr. Wayne,

I think it's time I aired my grievances before I decide to quit being your servant altogether. I think maybe this is the best thing to do because maybe you do not understand how tired I am from having to serve you. So, let me give you my list of grievances to clear the air:

1. You never eat your meals when I bring them - every time I make you a meal, you end up leaving it to get cold while investigating some new villain in Gotham, or you've gone to a soiree to eat caviar and crackers instead;
2. You constantly leave the Batcave doors open, which allows water from the waterfall into the cave, making the floor wet and difficult to mop up;
3. Ms. Rachel Dawes keeps leaving her clothing and "unmentionables" strewn about the mansion - I'm only paid to be picking up after one person, not two;
4. You don't pay me enough to clean the blood out of your undershirts;
5. I'm not a doctor, so stop asking me to sew up your bullet and knife wounds.

These are just a list of some of the grievances I have, and this was only from this past week! Please be more responsible, act like an adult and at least clean up after yourself once in awhile. This huge house is too much to take care of with just me; then I have to take care of you, too? For $12.00 an hour? That's just ludicrous. Please rectify this before I decide to put in my two weeks' notice.

Kindly,
Alfred
