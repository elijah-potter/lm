Jewelry is beautiful, but the industry preparing these beautiful jewels uses many hazardous substances in processes for example in metal coating. Therefore, municipal waste water treatment plant wants to know how the factory will pre-treat their waste waters before accepting the factory as their customer. Fortunately, efficient pre-treatment and water circulation methods exist to remove all hazardous substances before letting the sewage to the drainage.
Watch the video how a Finnish Jewelry factory is pre-treating their waste water and stopping harmful substances form entering the municipal sewage system. Follow our information campaign in Twitter @BestBalticBSR with hashtag #BEST_BalticSea.
