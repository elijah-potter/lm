Teamfluent was found in 2016 while examining different tools in Thinslices to revolutionize the learning systems for tech companies. It was quite challenging to find a useful tool due to the oversaturated market and specific requirements for our idea, so we just decided to build it ourselves. We reflected on the high demand for an effective LMS and formed a small team called Teamfluent to bring this system to life. Initially, many people were intrigued by our visions, blueprints, and business plans, but persuading them to join us and form contracts wasn't as easy. Most of our clients were already accustomed to powerful LMS, but even though they admired our efforts and ideas, they couldn't see it as a widely-used tool. Thus, after 2 years of consulting tech-customers, Teamfluent realized its weaknesses and came to an end.
