Window to My Neighbors

I looked out my window on a bright sunny day.
To see the construction companies at bay.
At the empty lot across the street for me.
They were building something, a new house maybe. 

Sure enough, as time went on by.
The walls and floors built up really high.
As quickly as the old house had burnt down,
They were building a new one all around. 

The noises were annoying and loud!
The grinding, the sawing, and the nailing abound.
I already didn't like the new neighbors here,
And the house was not yet for sale anywhere!

After six months of that annoying din,
A new little family finally moved in.
A mother, a father, and a small little son.
They were all dressed in gray, where was the fun?

I scoffed and I sighed as I watched them go.
A tiny glimpse into their life through my window.
The wife never seemed to leave her abode,
But the husband brought his grill, in the truck he always towed.

I never saw the son, an annoying thought.
They never let him play with the outdoor toys they bought.
Why keep the child locked up all inside?
What did these people have to hide?

I look out and watch these annoying new people.
But a smug sense of justice starts to steeple.
For, while they spent a quarter million on their house,
It was built so small as if built for a mouse!
