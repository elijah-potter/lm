A truck came barrelling down the road without breaks. The light was red, but it didn't matter. The driver was sleep deprived and overworked. Anyone could make such a mistake. A teenager, Kaori, was on the crosswalk helping her grandfather cross. It would be both of their funerals. Kaori pushed her grandfather away and accepted her fate. She clenched her jaw and closed her eyes. 

A robed man dropped from the sky mere seconds before the truck would have killed Kaori and thrust his palm forward. The truck plowed into him and came to a violent stop. Kaori's ears rang as shrapnel lacerated her skin. The hand around her waist loosened as everything fell silent, and she looked up. It was a man in his late teens, probably. He had long, messy hair in a bun and a handsome face. He scowled as he looked at her and asked, "Are you okay?"

Kaori nodded. "Good."

The man released her and was gone. 

Kaori dreamed of her savior that night and couldn't get his face out of her mind. The next day at school, as she bought her lunch at the school store, she caught a glimpse of her savior. But that couldn't have been right. The person she was looking at was Kuroki, an average-looking loner with an ugly mop of hair and glasses who blended into the background. However, she couldn't mistake those eyes anywhere. She followed him outside until she caught him alone and then confronted him, "you're the one who saved me yesterday! Be my boyfriend!"

Kuroki narrowed his eyes at her and spoke with a congested voice, "huh? Oh, I get it. Someone dared you to do this. It's okay, I get it."

Kaori rushed him then, snatching his glasses off and pushing his hair back, "wai--"

"I knew it. I couldn't mistake those eyes," Kaori said.
