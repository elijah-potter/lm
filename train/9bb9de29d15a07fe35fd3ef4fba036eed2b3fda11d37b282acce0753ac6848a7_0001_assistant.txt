It was a hot summer day as the sun beat down mercilessly on little Timmy. He quickly ran home after buying his favorite ice cream, as it was already dripping. Timmy knew he had to hurry before it fully melted away.

But just then, in his haste, Timmy stumbled, and his grip loosened just enough for the cone to fly out of his hand, landing face-down on the hot summer pavement. The ice cream spread out like a pond, and Timmy fell to his knees, crying out in agony as tears went streaming down his cheeks.

It was a tragedy for Timmy that day as he kneeled there and mourned the loss of his ice cream, but he learned valuable lessons. Ice cream is fragile, and nothing last forever. Only memories remain.
