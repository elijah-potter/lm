The humming silver blade pulsed with quiet energy, its edge a whisper of cold precision that hummed faintly when it touched the air. Its surface rippled like liquid mercury, shifting between obsidian depths and iridescent veins of light, as if breathing in slow, deliberate breaths. The core of it pulsed with a soft glow, a living ember that burned brighter when it moved—each motion a deliberate, deliberate heartbeat. It drank in warmth like a thirsty creature, absorbing the essence of fire and turning it back into something sharper, more defined. Around its base, tendrils of dark energy coiled, whispering secrets in the wind, and when it spoke, its voice was the sound of a thousand falling stars. The world around it seemed to bend to its will, though it never claimed to be master. It was patient, always watching, always waiting for the next spark of life to dance across its surface.
