Once upon a time, in a cozy green house with a big red door, lived a little girl named Mia who loved to play outside. One sunny morning, Mia saw a tiny blue butterfly with wings like a rainbow fluttering near her favorite tree. "Hello, pretty butterfly!" she said with a giggle.

The butterfly smiled and said, "Thank you for being so kind! I was lost and needed help." Mia smiled and said, "Of course! Let's find your way home together."

They walked together, following the bright yellow sun, and the butterfly showed Mia how to spot the biggest red flower in the garden. When they reached a big, shady oak tree, Mia hugged the butterfly tightly. "You are the kindest butterfly ever," she whispered.

The butterfly waved its wings and said, "Thank you, Mia! Now I know where home is." With one last flutter, it flew away, leaving Mia smiling as she ran home to tell her mommy all about her adventure.

As Mia ran inside, her mommy hugged her and said, "That was a wonderful day, wasn’t it?" And Mia knew it was—because she had made a new friend.
