The alarm buzzed at 6:17 AM, but the snooze button was pressed too quickly, delaying the wake-up until 6:30 AM. Coffee was brewed, but the first sip was taken too hot, burning the throat. The commute to work was delayed by a traffic jam caused by a sudden snowstorm. By 7:45 AM, the office was already buzzing with activity, and the morning meeting had already started without them.

Lunch was delayed again, this time due to a sudden power outage at the café. By 8:30 PM, the day’s tasks piled up, and the evening was spent catching up on emails and reports. Dinner was rushed, and the only time for relaxation came late into the night, as they finally settled into bed at 11:30 PM, exhausted but grateful for the day finally ending.
