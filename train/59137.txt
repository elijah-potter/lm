Manifesto made to the nations by the General Constitutional Congress of the United Provinces of the River Plate on the treatment and cruelties suffered under the Spaniards, which caused the Declaration of Independence
Castro Barros, Pedro Ignacio de, 1777-1849
Printed document, 11pp. Contemporary ownership signature on verso of last leaf. In a half morocco box. An important manifesto by one of the first states in Latin America to fully establish freedom from Spain. The "Imprenta de la Independencia" published this pamphlet in the year following Argentinean independence."
