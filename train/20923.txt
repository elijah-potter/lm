What is a seed library?
If you’re an avid gardener, you may have heard of a seed library. But what exactly is a seed library? In short, it’s a collection of seeds made available to the public for free or for a nominal fee. Seed libraries are usually maintained by local organizations and are designed to promote sustainability, biodiversity, and seed-saving practices.
The concept of a seed library is simple: anyone can come and take as many packets of seeds as they want to grow in their own garden, while also being asked to contribute some of their harvested seeds back to the library.
