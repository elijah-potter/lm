Question: What did a report released on Friday by the Federal Reserve indicate about inflation?
Answer: Inflation persisted through April according to a measure the Federal Reserve regularly uses and follows closely.

Question: Which index showed high inflation persisting as compared to the Dow Jones estimates? 
Answer: The personal consumption expenditures price index (PCE), which excludes food and energy, rose 0.4% during April and exceeded the Dow Jones estimate of 0.3%. 

Question: Did the PCE increase for the year and from March, the previous month? 
Answer: The Commerce Department reported that the PCE was up on an annual basis by 4.7% and was higher than the rate of 4.2% in March.

Question: Did the higher inflation rate affect consumer spending? 
Answer: Increases in personal income kept consumer spending up despite the inflation rate. Personal income increased 0.4% while spending increased by 0.8%. 

Question: What shifts occurred in prices during April? 
Answer: Price increases for goods rose by 0.3% while services increased by 0.4%. Food prices fell by less than 0.1% and energy costs increased by 0.7%. A further indication that the U.S. economy is shifting back to a service economy was suggested by the 5.5% annual increase in the cost of services while goods increased by 2.1%. 

Question: Did the markets shift based on the report? 
Answer: Generally, the markets did not react to the news, but rather focused on the news of a potential debt ceiling deal out of Washington, with stock market futures and treasury yields mostly higher.
