Sadow's suitcase was the first of its kind. It wheeled along while everyone had briefcases. It could store so much inside, so it was far from brief. That meant Sadow could explore and stay as long as he pleased. Rain? That's no problem for Sadow's suitcase, but everywhere he went Sadow felt the need, the pull, to place a souvenir inside his suitcase. Eventually, he lost time throughout the day. He'd awake to find money missing, and the suitcase moved.

Sadow retraced his steps and found a trail of relics he acquired. The tales of the wizard were true! The wizard's spirit must have inhabited the vacant suitcase. But even though Sadow figured it out, it was too late. The suitcase now heaved like it was breathing and wheeled out of the hotel on its own. Once it obtained enough power it went to the town square and opened, sending gold coins flying like a fountain. The strange suitcase transformed the metals and junk inside into something useful! The town was saved and the suitcase went on to spread prosperity throughout the world.
