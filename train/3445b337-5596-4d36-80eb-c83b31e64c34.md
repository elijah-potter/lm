The terms were vague: "whenever possible," "when ready," and "within reason." The clock ticked louder than the words, each passing day a silent accusation. The storm outside matched the tension inside—rain drumming against the window like the relentless pulse of a broken promise.

They had said, *"We’ll figure it out."* But figure out what? The cracks in the plan were too obvious, the cracks in the heart too deep. The others—those who had trusted, who had waited—now saw the shadows where promises once glowed. The weight of the silence was heavier than the weight of the words.

And then, the silence became an answer.
