The fluorescent lights hummed faintly as the elevator doors hissed open, revealing a corridor slick with early morning mist. The latecomer stepped out, coat zipped against the chill, fingers brushing the empty pocket where their phone should’ve been. The alarm had blared through the night, its shrill echo still vibrating in their bones—*five minutes left*. Their name flashed on the board: *"Johnson, Room 307"*. The relief washed over them like a warm breath after a long dive. The door to 307 creaked open just as they reached it, a half-smile playing on their lips. Inside, the professor’s desk was already set with coffee steaming in a mug labeled *"10:00 AM"*, a crumpled napkin beneath it. The professor’s voice, already rising over the white noise of the lab, was slightly louder than usual. The latecomer exhaled, shoulders dropping—another day, another victory against time.
