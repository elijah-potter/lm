Here is a list of ice cream flavors that have a New Jersey theme:

1. Gotta Have a Guava - This is a guava ice cream flavor that is a riff on Wawa's slogan, "Gotta Have A Wawa."
2. Central Jersey Exists Neopolitan - This is a reference to whether or not Central Jersey exists, so there are three flavors just like there are three regions of New Jersey.
3. Java Bon Jovi - This is a coffee-flavored ice cream referencing Jon Bon Jovi, a famous New Jersey native and musician.
4. Taylor Ham or Pork Roll - This is a reference to the debate as to what New Jerseyans call the pink breakfast meat. It would be pink ice cream with a different color pink swirled (such as strawberry and raspberry)
5. Joe Pesci - This would be another coffee-flavored ice cream with chunks mixed in. Joe Pesci is a famous actor from New Jersey, and the chunks would reference his gold tooth from Home Alone, a movie he was in.
