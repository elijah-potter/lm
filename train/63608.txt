Coyotes are a species of animal present in the Territories.
Appearance and CharacteristicsEdit
- Coyotes generally stick to the wild country outside of the Scent Border. Sometimes, however, they are driven into the Territories to search for food. Coyotes are bigger than foxes, yet smaller than wolves. They are clever omnivores, but strongly prefer meat and would gladly gobble up a town full of mice or simply snap up a stray mouse or two.
