The abandoned clock tower in **Black Hollow**—its brass hands frozen at **10:18:29**, though the sky was already dark. The locals say the tower’s pendulum drifts by **three seconds every hour**, making time feel like it’s running backward in certain sectors. Workers report clocks on the second floor run **1.5 minutes per day forward**, while the basement’s chronometer gains **half an hour weekly**. The locals joke that if you’re born before 11:12 AM on the 12th, you’re "marked" to age normally—but those born after 11:23 AM experience accelerated decay. The tower’s bell rings once a year at **11:00 PM on the 31st**, but the sound echoes with a **distorted, repeating "one-two-three-four-five-six-seven-eight-nine-ten"** that doesn’t resolve. The town’s records show births/deaths misaligned by decades. A physicist from the 20th century once claimed it was a "quantum artifact," but the town buried his notes. The tower’s shadow stretches in reverse on some days.
