Kenta is a shy and timid high schooler who has a secret: he can transform into a dog at will. He inherited this ability from his grandfather, who was a famous dog trainer and breeder. Kenta uses his power to escape from his troubles and have fun, but he also has to deal with the consequences of his actions.

One day, he meets Aoi, a cheerful and energetic girl who loves dogs. She instantly falls in love with Kenta’s dog form and adopts him as her pet. She names him Ken and treats him with kindness and affection. Kenta enjoys spending time with Aoi, but he also feels guilty for lying to her.

As Kenta and Aoi grow closer, they encounter various challenges and adventures. They have to deal with rival dog owners, animal thieves, mysterious enemies, and supernatural phenomena. They also have to face their own feelings and secrets. Will Kenta be able to tell Aoi the truth? Will Aoi accept him for who he is? Will they be able to stay together as a human and a dog, or as something more?

Dog Days is a comedy-romance-fantasy anime that explores the themes of identity, friendship, love, and loyalty. It is a heartwarming and hilarious story of a boy who can turn into a dog and a girl who loves him.
