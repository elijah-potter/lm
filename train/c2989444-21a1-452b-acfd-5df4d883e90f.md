In the dim glow of flickering lanterns, a cobblestone path wound through the heart of an ancient settlement. A child’s laughter echoed from somewhere distant, lost in the wind. The scent of damp earth mingled with the faint metallic tang of iron, and the distant murmur of voices carried on the breeze.
