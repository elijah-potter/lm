Hey mom! 

I hope everything's okay back home. Just thought I'd let you know about everything I've been up to so far on my trip.
I'm currently in Birmingham, a bustling city in the heart of England. The people here are super friendly and I've spent the last couple of days indulging myself by going shopping. They have some awesome malls. There's one called the Bullring which has a three-story department store called Selfridges—no, they don't sell fridges haha! But I got a couple of new dresses and tried some amazing desserts in their food hall. 
Speaking of food, the stereotypes are all wrong. British food is amazing. I could probably eat fish and chips every day if I lived here and never get sick of it. And the fish is huge, like the size of my arm. You have to douse it in lots of vinegar too. It makes it taste even better. I've also had at least 8 full English breakfasts since I've been here (I lost count of the exact number). It's so filling, and the bacon they use here is far superior to the one we get in stores back home. Last night I went to a curry house (they are everywhere here) and tried something called chicken tikka masala. I loved it so much that I'm going to try and replicate it when I get back. It's the perfect mix of creamy and spicy.
Oh, and don't freak out, but when I was in Manchester (a quirky, vibrant city in the North) last weekend, I went out to a quaint traditional pub. The locals were so friendly and bought me drinks, and I even did some karaoke. However a couple of guys ended up having too much to drink and it escalated into a little bit of a brawl, they were hitting each other with barstools! I got out of there safe though, so don't worry.
I also picked you up a little souvenir when I was in Brighton (a southern beach city). It's a colorful stick of hard candy called "rock." Some of them had pretty rude words printed on them which I found funny, but don't worry I got you one that's safe for work! 
I can't wait to hear back from you!
