When I have a craving for my favorite water-based apex predator, Frank's Fins is my first choice! It's always a popular spot, but somehow there's always a seat available when I swing by, and the service is always fantastic. The servers are friendly and attentive without being too imposing, I've never had to ask for a refill on my beverage or my shark fin soup.

And speaking of the shark fin soup, it's a revelation! The broth is so flavorful, and the fins are always the perfect texture, not too chewy and not too crunchy. But that's not the only amazing dish on the menu, trust me! On my last visit, I got the seared shark steak and the "Sharky's Special Shark Nachos." The steak was perfectly done and so flavorful, and who would have guessed that shark meat pairs so well with nacho cheese? The next time I come, I'm definitely getting either the sharkburger or Grandma's Sharkloaf. They both sound absolutely mouthwatering! You would think that the same meat over and over might get boring, but not at Frank's!

I have to say, I'm really enjoying the new decor! The harpoon and netting theme has a real nautical vibe with an edge to it, and Sharky makes for a hilarious mascot, even if he might be a bit frightening for any little ones that come with the family. All in all, Frank's Fins has stolen my heart (and my taste buds)!
