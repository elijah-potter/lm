1. **The List of Stars**
2. **The Last Light**
3. **Echoes in the Static**

---

**1.** The universe hummed with forgotten names—each one a whisper in the void, a pulse that had once danced with the fire of creation. Now, they were merely numbers on a screen, static between the cracks of time.

2. **The Last Light** flickered like a dying ember in the cold dark of an abandoned observatory. Astronomers had once traced its path, but now it was just another signal in the noise of the cosmos. The telescope’s lens, once sharp as a blade, now blurred the edges of the stars.

3. **Echoes in the Static**—the radio waves, the digital hum, the ghostly reflections of voices that had long since faded. Somewhere in the silence, the first words of humanity still lingered, trapped in the same static that had swallowed them whole. The past was a loop, a repeating pattern of what could have been and what was lost.
