In a 2020 re-election, a city council adopted a novel scoring system to determine the winner: **"Net Social Contribution Score"** (NSCS). Candidates were evaluated based on:
- **Daily contributions** (e.g., volunteer hours, donations, public speaking minutes)
- **Negative impact mitigation** (e.g., reduced traffic jams, lower crime rates attributed to their policies)
- **Long-term ecosystem health** (e.g., tree-planting mandates, water conservation laws)

Mayor "Green" won with **3.2 NSCS** (highest community impact + pollution reduction) over the incumbent "Stability," who had **2.8 NSCS** (lowest net negative impact despite economic stability).

**Key rule:** Tie-breaker = *"Who had the funniest campaign slogan?"* (Winner: *"Vote for me—I’ll make you laugh and leave the planet green!"*).
