## Lost in the Bloom (Charleston City Market)

The air hung thick with the scent of sweetgrass and sunshine on Saturday afternoon. I was browsing the vendor stalls near the back of the City Market, admiring the intricate weaving of a small, indigo-dyed basket, when I noticed you. You were sketching in a worn leather-bound notebook, your brow furrowed in concentration, a cascade of auburn hair falling across your shoulder. There was a quiet intensity about you, a peacefulness that resonated with the gentle murmur of the crowd and the distant clip-clop of horses on the cobblestones. I remember thinking you looked utterly absorbed in your work, a world away from the bustle around us. I almost said hello, but hesitated, not wanting to interrupt. Our eyes met briefly, and you offered a small, almost shy smile before returning to your sketching. I continued browsing, glancing back several times, but you were gone when I looked again. I'm hoping this resonates with you.
