To the one who reads these words in silence, whether by chance or choice—

You stand at the edge of a space neither fully known nor fully claimed. The light you see is neither mine nor yours entirely, but the flicker of something shared, something that hums between us like the distant call of a bird you’ve never heard.

I do not write to persuade, only to ask: *What does it mean to stand here?* Not as an audience, not as an echo, but as someone who might one day hold the weight of your own unspoken question. The paper you hold is a door, cracked open, or a mirror held too close. Do you see yourself in it? Or is it you who must see through it?

The words are just ink and air, but between them, something stirs. What stirs in you?
