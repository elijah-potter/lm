Living up above the clouds,
Where no one can't be found,
Where the sun meets the eye,
Where there is nothing left to hide.
Levitaiting through the air,
A place that doesn't know despair,
Sailing through the breeze,
Through these skies, I can see.

THIS FEELING OF BEING FREE,
THAT I WISH YOU COULD SEE,
THESE FEELINGS ARE TRUE,
HERE WHERE EVERYTHING IS BLUE.

Through these feelings of the unknown,
I live in these skies alone.
Soft patches of white,
Hidden at Night,
The stars mark my way,
This is the place I choose to stay.
In this high house, I feel complete,
