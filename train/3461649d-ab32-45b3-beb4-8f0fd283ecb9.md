A quiet library in a mountain village, dimly lit by flickering lanterns, where the scent of aged books mixes with damp earth. Glass doors open onto a narrow, terraced garden where only the rustle of wind through ivy and distant birdsong echoes. The air hums with the soft murmur of a single candle, the occasional whisper of a reader’s breath. Outside, the village street is barely audible—just the occasional hoot of a owl and the distant chime of a bell from the church tower.
