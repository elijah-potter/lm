## Bookstore Serendipity (Seattle, WA)

The rain was relentless that afternoon, a grey curtain drawn across Pike Place Market. I was browsing the poetry section at Left Bank Books, searching for something to lift my spirits, when you appeared. You were examining a collection of Rilke, your brow furrowed in concentration, and the soft, almost imperceptible scent of old paper and sandalwood clung to your sweater. I noticed the vibrant turquoise of your scarf, a striking contrast to the muted tones of the store and the weather outside. 

We didn't speak, but I remember feeling an unexpected sense of calm in your presence, a quiet understanding that transcended words. I lingered longer than I intended, hoping for a shared glance, a fleeting smile, anything to acknowledge the connection I felt. You eventually moved on, disappearing into the crowd as quickly as you arrived. I’ve replayed the moment countless times, wondering if you felt it too. 

If you were the person admiring Rilke and wearing a turquoise scarf, please reply with your favorite poem.
