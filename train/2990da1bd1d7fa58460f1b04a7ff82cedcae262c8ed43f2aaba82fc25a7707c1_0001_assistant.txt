Here are some fun things you could try if you turned into a frog: 

1. Jump - As a frog, you would be able to jump much higher than you would as a human. You may have fun traveling around by leaping.
2. Catch bugs - While this may or may not appeal to you now, as a frog, you would find the bugs delicious. It would also be fun to use your long tongue to catch bugs, as that is something you would not be able to do as a human. 
3. Swim - Your swimming abilities would increase as a frog, and it may be fun to swim in a pond, especially since you could see better underwater.
