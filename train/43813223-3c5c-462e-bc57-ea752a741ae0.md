A quiet courtyard in an abandoned library, surrounded by crumbling bookshelves and half-erected books. The air hums with the faint scent of aged paper and damp earth. Sunlight filters through gaps in the windows, casting shifting shadows on cracked tiles. A single bench sits unoccupied, its back broken, its seat worn smooth by decades of use. A water fountain, long since stopped, drips intermittently into a small puddle. The only sound is the distant murmur of a distant clock striking the hour, and the occasional rustle of leaves in the rusted iron railings. A child’s drawing of a dragon is pinned to the wall, its colors faded to a muted gray, half-eaten by time. The place is both alive and still, suspended in a moment of waiting.
