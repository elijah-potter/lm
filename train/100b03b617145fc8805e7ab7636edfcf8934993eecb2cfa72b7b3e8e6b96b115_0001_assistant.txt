That sounds like fun! Here are some options for you:
- Skellig Michael, Ireland - This was where parts of Star Wars: The Force Awakens and Star Wars: The Last Jedi were filmed. It is actually part of an old Gaelic monastery and it works perfectly for where Luke Skywalker and Rey spend time. 
- Skopelos, Greece - This is the island where Mamma Mia! was filmed. There is even a bus tour that takes you to several of the exact filming locations. 
- Harbor Freeway, Los Angeles - This road is too busy for you to stop on, but you can at least drive by this road that was used in La La Land and visit the nearby places. The view from this elevated highway is an amazing scene. 
- Pike Place Market, Seattle -  This is where Sleepless in Seattle was filmed, and nearby is the dock still holds the houseboat used as Sam and Jonah's home. There is even a restaurant with a plaque stating:  "Tom Hanks Sat Here."
