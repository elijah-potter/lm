About this course
“Change is a constant in many of our lives. All around us, technologies, processes, people, ideas, and methods often change, affecting the way we perform daily tasks and live. our lives. ” This workshop help attendees bring smoothly the culture of change at all levels. It discusses the use of needed components to develop a change management trhough communications plans and the implementation of appropriate strategies. It helps participants learn the importance of flexibility in the context of change, and demonstrate methods the change leader and individual change participant can use to promote flexibility.
Our course begins with the first step for generating great user experiences: understanding what people do, think, say, and feel. In this module, you’ll learn how to keep an open mind while learning.
