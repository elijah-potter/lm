Jasmine stumbled around in the dark as the storm raged outside. She stretched her arms in front of her, hoping that she could use the walls to guide her to the storage closet where the flashlights and other miscellaneous items that she rarely used were stashed away. Footsteps approached behind her and she could barely make out the outline of the person that chose to terrorize her that night. Luck was on her side, though, as she finally reached the storage closet and grabbed a flashlight to illuminate the blood seeping through her shirt. She didn't have much time to decipher whether the blood was hers or not. She couldn't feel much with adrenaline pumping through her body; all she could think about was fighting for her life. The figure moved closer as Jasmine ran out of the storage closet and snuck as quietly as she could into her bedroom, hoping that the intruder didn't hear the door close behind her.
