A dimly lit interrogation room. A uniformed officer leans back in their chair, arms crossed, while the suspect—clad in a worn hoodie—stares at the wall. The officer clears their throat, then says, *"Why’d you do it?"* The suspect hesitates, then begins, voice rough with exhaustion, *"I didn’t *do* anything. I was just… passing through."* The officer’s brow furrows. *"Passing through?"* The suspect’s fingers trace a faint scar on their wrist. *"Yeah. That’s where I was going."* Outside, the distant hum of a city never sleeps.
