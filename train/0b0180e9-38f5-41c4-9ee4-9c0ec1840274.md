Two figures stand shoulder-to-shoulder near the harbor’s dim glow, lanterns flickering against the mist. The older woman—her hands rough from years of work—suddenly holds up a half-empty jar of jam, then gestures to the younger man, who was staring blankly at his phone. Her voice drops to a whisper: *"You know this is the last time we’ll be here before winter."* His eyes flick up, a single tear tracing his cheek. He reaches for her hand, and in that moment, the words they’ve never spoken spill from his throat: *"I’ve been running for years, but I’m tired of running from this."* She smiles, tears in hers too. The silence between them is one of understanding, not words. Outside, the tide rolls in, carrying their shared secret with it.
