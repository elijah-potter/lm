AITA for refusing to let my sister use my inherited vacation home?

Okay, this is a bit of a tangled situation and I'm honestly feeling drained. It started back in 2018. My grandmother, Eleanor, passed away in her home in Carmel-by-the-Sea, California. She left a will that specifically divided her assets. I received the vacation home, a charming three-bedroom cottage she’s owned since the 1970s. My sister, Clara, received her antique jewelry collection and a significant sum of money. We’re both in our early thirties.

Eleanor always intended for the house to be a family asset, and she made that clear. She wanted it enjoyed by generations. However, the will was very specific about *who* received it. I’m not wealthy, and this house represents a substantial piece of my future financial security. I'm a software engineer, working remotely, and the rental income helps immensely.

For the first year after Eleanor’s death (2019), Clara was relatively hands-off. We’d occasionally discuss the house, mostly about upkeep and potential renovations. In late 2020, she started hinting at wanting to use it for a week during the summer. I initially said yes, but with the stipulation that she pay market rental rates, as I needed the income to cover property taxes and maintenance. She seemed agreeable.

Then, in March 2021, Clara announced she was getting married. The wedding is scheduled for August 5th, 2023, at a vineyard in Napa Valley. She immediately requested to use the Carmel house as a staging area for her bridal party leading up to the wedding – essentially, a week-long “glamping” experience for her and five friends. She insisted on it being a “gift” to her bridal party, and that I shouldn’t expect her to pay.

I explained, again, that the house is an investment and that allowing her to use it rent-free would significantly impact my finances. I offered to cover the cost of a local hotel for her and her friends instead. She reacted terribly. She accused me of being greedy, of ruining her wedding preparations, and of deliberately trying to make her life difficult. She even brought up past instances where I’d received more attention from Eleanor, claiming I was always trying to one-up her. It was a low blow, dredging up old family dynamics.

My parents have gotten involved, predictably. My mother thinks I should just “be generous” and let Clara use the house, emphasizing the importance of family harmony. My father, while more neutral, suggested I consider it as a gesture of goodwill. Clara has been relentless, sending me daily messages and calling multiple times a day, escalating her complaints. She’s even started hinting that I’m being intentionally spiteful because she’s happy.

I’ve tried to be reasonable. I offered to let her use the house for a long weekend *if* she paid a reduced rate. She refused, stating that a “reduced rate” still felt like I was trying to profit from her happiness. She’s now threatened to tell our extended family that I’m being an unreasonable and unfeeling sister. Honestly, the whole situation feels like emotional blackmail. I’ve become incredibly stressed and anxious, constantly worrying about her threats and the pressure from my parents. I just want to enjoy my asset without this constant drama. I’m feeling incredibly isolated and like I’m being unfairly targeted. I’m starting to resent her and the whole situation.

So, AITA for refusing to let my sister use my inherited vacation home rent-free, especially given the pressure from our parents and her threats to involve the extended family?



NTA
