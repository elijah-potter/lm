Here are places in Europe to visit if you want to go whale watching.
1. Húsavík, Iceland. The rate of whale sightings on the tour is 97%, almost guaranteeing that you'll see a whale from the decks of a traditional Icelandic oak ship.
2. Tromsø, Norway. This northern city has many whale-watching tours available in the spring months, with the opportunity to see Humpbacks, Orcas, and more.
3. The Azores Islands, Portugal. A boat tour leaving from São Miguel Island always has a Marine Biologist on board to answer any questions you may have.
4. Tarifa, Spain. Several whale-watching tours are offered to take you around the Strait of Gibraltar where you might see Pilot whales, Fin whales, and Sperm whales.
5. Genova, Italy. Whale-watching tours have been operational since 1996, average 4 hours in length, and travel the Ligurian Sea.
