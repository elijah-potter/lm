There was a friendly and youthful beagle named Jack who had a dream of becoming the governor of Tennessee. Jack had always been popular in his owner's suburb, and he felt that he could bring his charm and charisma to the political world. Jack put on his best bowtie collar and printed out some campaign posters. He made speeches, posed for pictures, and wagged his tail with enthusiasm. 

Despite giving his best effort, Jack's campaign didn't gain much traction. Even though he was super cute and cuddly, he didn't have any real policy ideas or prior experience doing...anything. Most people had already decided to vote for a human anyways. People still threw him french fries in support though, and patted his head which made him feel good in the moment, but it didn't improve his chances in the election.

Jack's poll numbers stayed low, and he knew that he wasn't going to win. But, he continued to make appearances at rallies and events, and he wagged his tail and jumped through hula hoops to show he was still enthusiastic. When the results came in on election night, Jack was not surprised to learn that he had lost. He knew that he had given it his all. He started thinking of what he would do next. He decided that he would run for a smaller office, like mayor!
