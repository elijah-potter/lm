Schools and Preschools
The construction of schools includes the use of soil erosion control and landscaping techniques. Council regulations require new school projects to follow environmental guidelines which includes the revegetation of disturbed areas and landscaping of the site. B & K Revegetation & Landscaping are contracted to create safe and practical areas for school children all over NSW and do so by using quality materials and workmanship. B & K Revegetation & Landscaping meet the requirements which are outlined by the Department of Education.
