Below is the short story:

World War 3 left the entire planet in a state of decay and destruction. Miraculously, more people survived than the officials believed. They would form groups who daily searched for food, medicine, and other items needed for survival. Many of the groups turned violent. They were almost like wild animals or a rabid dog. One group was hiding east of Dallas in an old warehouse. 

Food had run out. Things looked bad for the group. One woman stood before this ragged-looking bunch. “Look! We can’t stay here. We must find a safe area but a place where there’s food. My grandmother had a farm in Kaufman, Texas. The soil is rich there. You can grow anything. Plus, she has a small lake on the property that has fish. What if we travel there? We would be safe, plus wild fruits and berries are growing on the farm. There’s a fig tree. At least we’d have a chance.”

So, the group packed what few items they had and began the journey fifty miles east. It would be perilous. There would be mobs intent on killing anyone and stealing their stuff. But in order to survive, they would need to go there, find seeds to plant, and try to survive.
