A routine morning shift at the local hardware store turned into an unexpected detour when a customer, unfamiliar with the stock, asked for a 3/8" socket wrench. The assistant, recognizing the mistake, suggested a 3/4" instead. The customer—an engineer—insisted on a 3/8", but the tool was out of stock. Instead, they took a 5/8" socket, which the assistant later realized was the exact size needed for a maintenance job at their factory’s conveyor system. Upon returning to work, they discovered a misaligned component requiring a 5/8" socket for repair. The coincidence saved hours of downtime and a costly replacement.
