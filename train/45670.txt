Was the Last Supper super-sized over time?
Two Cornell University researchers (brothers, too) are studying the amount of food depicted in 52 of the best-known paintings of the Last Supper. Why? They wanted to know if the portions served at the meal had grown over time.
Brian and Craig Wansink - one an eating behavior expert and the other, a religious studies scholar - indexed the sizes of the foods by the sizes of the average disciple's head and found that portion size, plate size, and bread size increased dramatically over the last 1,000 years.
How much? The main courses, they said, grew by 69 percent, plate size by 66 percent and and bread size by 23 percent.
The study's findings will be published in the April issue of the International Journal of Obesity and released in the online version of the journal today.
