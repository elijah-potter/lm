Hyperinflation in the 20th and 21st Century

I. Greece, 1944 - 1947
   A.  Highest monthly hyperinflation rate: 13,800%
        1. Caused by German occupation in World War II
        2. Highest hyperinflation occurred after the Greek government regained control
        3. Fastest price doubling happened every 4.3 days

II. Germany, 1923
    A.  Highest monthly hyperinflation rate: 29,500%
    B.  Caused by Germany's inability to meet the Treaty of Versailles reparation payments 
        1. Germany's Weimar Republic replaced papaiermark with the rentenmark.     
        2. Prices increased with the fastest doubling happening every 3.7 days.

III.  Zimbabwe, 2008
      A. Highest monthly hyperinflation rate: 79,600,000,000%   
          1. Caused by government mismanagement and inefficiency
          2. Country stopped publishing inflation statistics.
          2. Fastest price doubling happened every 24.7 hours
