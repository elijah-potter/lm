Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the sunny meadow. One day, Benny saw a big, shiny red apple in the grass. "Mmm, yummy!" Benny thought. He tried to eat it, but it was too hard. Benny looked around and saw a kind old owl sitting on a tree branch. "Can you help me, Owl?" Benny asked. The owl smiled and said, "Of course, little one! But first, tell me a story."

Benny thought for a moment. "Once upon a time," he started, "there was a tiny mouse named Mimi. Mimi loved to explore her home. One day, she found a big, juicy apple in a basket. Mimi was very happy and started to eat it. Suddenly, she heard a tiny noise. She looked down and saw a worm inside the apple! Mimi was very scared."

The owl laughed. "That's a funny story!" he said. "Now, what else happened?" Benny hopped up and down. "Mimi knew she had to act fast. She picked up the apple and the worm carefully. Then, she ran to her friend, a kind old lady squirrel. 'Squirrel, help me!' Mimi called. The squirrel looked inside the apple and saw the worm. She smiled and said, 'We’ll get rid of it together!' They worked hard and found a tiny magic seed inside the apple. 'This might help!' the squirrel said. Mimi and the squirrel planted the seed in the garden. After a few days, a beautiful flower grew from the seed. 'Wow!' Mimi said. 'The apple saved us!'

The owl clapped his wings. "A wonderful story!" he said. Benny grinned. "Now, let's get back to eating that apple!" He took a tiny bite and smiled. The apple was soft and sweet, just like Benny’s happy heart. And from that day on, Benny always remembered that even the smallest things could bring the biggest happiness.
