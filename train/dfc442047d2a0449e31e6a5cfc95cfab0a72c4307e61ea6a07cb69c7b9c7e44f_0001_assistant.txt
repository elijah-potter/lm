The largest spider on Earth is the Goliath birdeater. A member of the tarantula family, this spider can weigh up to 6 ounces and can measure up to 5 inches in length, making it the largest spider by mass and body length in the world; when measuring size by leg span, only the giant huntsman spider is larger. The Goliath birdeater gets its name from its size and diet, as it does feed on birds. However, this is a rare meal for the spider, which more commonly eats anthropods, worms, and even amphibians. You can find this spider in the rainforests of South America.
