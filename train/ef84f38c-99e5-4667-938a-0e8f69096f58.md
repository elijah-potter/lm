A sudden alarm blared through the office, cutting off mid-conversation. The fluorescent lights flickered, casting an eerie glow over the desks as the power surged. One by one, employees stumbled to their feet, heads spinning—until the emergency exit door burst open, revealing a swarm of firefighters and an ambulance waiting outside. The manager’s voice crackled over the PA system, urgent: *"Evacuate now—there’s an explosion in the basement."* Chaos erupted as people scrambled toward the nearest exit, some tripping over their own shoes. By the time the siren faded, the building was eerily silent, save for the distant hum of the emergency response teams. The unexpected interruption left no time to plan, only to run.
