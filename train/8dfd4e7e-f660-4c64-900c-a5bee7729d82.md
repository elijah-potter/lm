The abandoned subway station beneath an industrial district, humming with the faint, rhythmic *clang* of rusted metal doors and flickering fluorescent lights. No one knows why it’s here—just that the last maintenance crew vanished after a single worker claimed they heard a "low, droning hum" before the lights died. Now, the station is a cavern of cracked tiles, where groups of strangers—some alone, some in pairs, others in small clusters—sit cross-legged on the floor, staring at the walls. Some play cards; others just watch the ceiling, where shadows shift unpredictably. A few whisper about "the thing in the walls," but no one believes it. The doors stay closed, but the air thrums with the same faint noise, as if waiting for someone.
