"Okay, Isabella. This is the final round. All you have to do is say the first thing that comes to mind when I ask you a question. If the audience voted for it, it'll appear on the board. Are you ready to begin?" Isabella prepared herself.
"I'm ready," she said. 
"Okay, first question: what is something that a woman might wear to the beach?" 
"Sunscreen," Isabella replied. 
"From one to ten, how well would you say you know your neighbor?" 
"Four," she responded. 
"Name a sport with a specific season." 
"Football." 
"And finally, as an adult, what's a dessert that you can eat any time you want?" 
"Cheesecake," she said with a sigh of relief, knowing that the worst of the ordeal was over. 

As the questions came to an end, the host turned around to look at the game board. Isabella's heart was racing knowing she had a lot riding on her answers. Even her family on the side of the stage was extremely nervous. It all came down to these results.
