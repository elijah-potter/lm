In a neon-lit alley, the wind carried the scent of rain-soaked pavement. A street musician, fingers dancing over a broken guitar, stopped playing when a shadow moved—just for a second—over the lens of a camera mounted on a backpack. The flash caught only the blur of a hand reaching for the lens.

The camera’s screen flickered. The photo showed a stranger in the crowd, their face frozen mid-smile, eyes wide with disbelief. The photographer, oblivious, snapped another shot before the figure dissolved into the crowd.

As the clock struck 59:59, the stranger vanished—except for a single, tiny pixel on the screen, pulsing like a heartbeat. The musician picked up their guitar again, but this time, the notes were silent. The alley fell silent too.
