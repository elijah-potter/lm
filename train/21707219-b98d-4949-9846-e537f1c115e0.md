A small, unassuming keychain shaped like a tiny, weathered compass. The needle, once smooth, now bears faint scratches from years of use. It holds a single, delicate charm—a folded paper with a date: *1989*. The recipient, a child named Leo, found it in an attic box labeled *"For Those Who Keep Promises."* Leo never knew the keychain’s story, but every time he glanced at it, he felt a quiet spark: the weight of a choice he’d made years earlier to keep a promise he’d broken. The compass’s magnetic pull, though weak, guided him through a life where small acts of honesty became the compass to his soul.
