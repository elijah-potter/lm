A promise was made in a dimly lit café, whispered over steaming coffee:
*"I’ll meet you at midnight to exchange the stolen watch for a debt."*

She arrived at the appointed hour, but the man was gone—only a half-empty glass and a note: *"The watch was a lie. The debt was never mine."*

Her heart raced as she realized the trinket was worthless, the debt—*her own name on a debt collector’s ledger*. She burned the note in the alley, but the firelight reflected in her eyes, proving her guilt was already written.

Now, every night, she dreams of midnight again—only this time, the watch is real, and the debt is hers to claim.
