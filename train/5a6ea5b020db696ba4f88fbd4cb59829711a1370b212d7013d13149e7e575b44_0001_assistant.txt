Here are three easy trails to see nature's beauty:
1. Coastal Trail Hike: In Cutler, Maine, hike a 2.8-mile round trip to see ocean views, craggy cliffs, cedar swamps, and maritime spruce-fir forests.
2. Frozen Niagara Cave Hike: In Mammoth Cave National Park, Kentucky, hike a 0.25-mile round trip to take a cave tour. The path is easy and the views are beautiful. See the Rainbow Dome, Drapery Room, and Crystal Lake.
3. Templeton Trail Hike: In Coconino National Forest, Arizona, hike a 7-mile round trip to see Sedona's red rock and Cathedral Rock.
