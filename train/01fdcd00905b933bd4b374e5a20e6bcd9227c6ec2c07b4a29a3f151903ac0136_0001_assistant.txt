Once upon a time, there was a boy named Harry Houdini. He was a very special boy because he could escape from anything! He could escape from handcuffs and straitjackets, and even be buried alive!

Harry Houdini loved to amaze people with his magic tricks. He would travel all over the world, performing his amazing escapes. People would come from all over to see him perform.

One day, Harry Houdini was performing in London. He was challenged by the Daily Mirror to escape from a special pair of handcuffs. The handcuffs were made of the strongest metal, and they were locked on Harry Houdini's wrists.

Harry Houdini tried to escape, but he couldn't. He struggled and struggled, but the handcuffs wouldn't budge. The crowd watched in suspense, wondering if Harry Houdini would be able to escape.

After an hour, Harry Houdini finally escaped from the handcuffs! The crowd cheered and applauded. Harry Houdini had done it again!

Harry Houdini was a very brave and talented boy. He used his magic to entertain people all over the world. He was a true master of escape.
