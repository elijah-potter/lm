The deepest point in the ocean is called Challenger Deep located in the southern end of the Pacific Ocean's Mariana Trench. Challenger Deep was found in a 1951 surveying expedition by the British ship Challenger II. This area of the trench was named after this ship. 

The Mariana Trench itself is located in the Western Pacific Ocean. It is 44 miles wide and 1,554 miles long making it well over 100 times larger than the Grand Canyon in Arizona.
