Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a garden full of flowers, lived a tiny bunny named Pip. Pip had fluffy white ears and a big, happy heart.

One sunny morning, Pip hopped outside to play. The grass was soft under his paws, and the birds sang sweet songs. He spotted a shiny red ball in the bushes. Pip wanted it very much.

He hopped over, but the ball was too heavy. "I can’t reach it," Pip sighed. Just then, a tiny squirrel named Squish scampered over. "Don’t worry, Pip!" Squish said. "I’ll help you!"

Squish dug a hole with his little claws, and Pip carefully climbed in. Together, they pulled the ball out. "Now it’s ours!" Pip cheered. Squish grinned and gave him a tiny acorn as a thank-you.

From that day on, Pip and Squish played together every day. They shared their toys, laughed a lot, and made new friends in their garden. And so, the happiest adventures began under the big, bright sky.
