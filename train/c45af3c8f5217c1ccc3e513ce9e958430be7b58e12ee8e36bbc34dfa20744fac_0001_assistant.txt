Jane Austen completed 6 novels in her lifetime, all of which are well-known globally.  The novels are very well regarded because they have a clear message and memorable characters. Two of Jane Austen's completed novels,  Persuasion and Northanger Abbey, were published after she had died and there were a further two books that were unfinished when she died.  These were Sanditon and the Juvenilia stories.
The 6 completed novels are:
*1811: Sense and Sensibility
*1813: Pride and Prejudice
*1814: Mansfield Park
*1815: Emma
*1817: Persuasion
*1817: Northanger Abbey

The question of the order of popularity of the 6 novels published during Jane Austen's lifetime is subjective as readers will have different views and popularity is open to interpretation. However, janeausten.org lists the popularity as:
1. Pride and Prejudice
2. Sense and Sensibility
3. Persuasion
4. Emma
5. Northanger Abbey
6. Mansfield Park

This order is based on statistics gathered and interpreted by the Jane Austen society
