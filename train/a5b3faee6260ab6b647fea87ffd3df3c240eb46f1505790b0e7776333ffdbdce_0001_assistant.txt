That sounds like quite the transition! Here are some things that may help:
- Hidden Desk: There are many ideas for hidden desks that hide it away in a corner, even helping you use some space in your closet for it so that you can close your closet and forget about work when you are done for the day. 
- Wardrobe Workspace: Similar to hiding your desk, you can also get a wardrobe or set of shelves. Put your computer on one shelf, your office supplies on another, etc. and then close the wardrobe when you are done and put the chair you were using in the corner somewhere. 
- Drawer Space: You can use drawer space to put your office stuff when you are not working so that whatever you are using as a desk is free to be used for other tasks.
