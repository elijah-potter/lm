It was my first day on the job. I took a look at my ID card to see if I was dreaming or not. "Noelle Yapoze: Adventurer." Perfect! I'm not dreaming. I am a real adventurer. It was not my job of choice but I always admired adventurers. They get to go on grand escapades and come back to a celebration of their great deeds. I always dreamed of this city doing that for me when I was younger. Now they can! The first thing that I need to do before going out, though, is to grab a weapon. In the trials at the guild, they had us use a variety of weapons to show our proficiency with them. This girl has an S-Rank proficiency with all of them! The look on their faces was to die for! Little Noelle from 10 years ago bested some of the instructors in the weapon tests! Anyway, what do I want to be known for? 

A sword would be nice! Imagine me, slaying a tech-enhanced dragon with a single slash! Or maybe a hammer? It doesn't fit my petite body but it's still easy enough to swing. I could be one of those Amazonesses from the ancient past, using smarts to best their enemies! One swing from this baby and they're done! What else is there...oh! There is the spear as well! I can cover a lot of distance and enemies won't be able to reach me. Imagine me, jumping up high in the sky and jumping down with the spear point, slaying an Orc or Minotaur with one stab like in that old game Last Tale XIV. I can just imagine my first adventure.

I leave the guild base after accepting my first mission. It would be to slay the elder wolf that is making its base a few miles away from us.  I go to their cave or whatever, decked out in the most advanced armor and my weapon. The wolf would stare at me as I'm smiling at it and start to charge at me. With a swift dodge to the right, the wolf runs right past me. We do this dance while I swing at it with such ferocity that it starts to stumble, setting me up for the finishing blow!
