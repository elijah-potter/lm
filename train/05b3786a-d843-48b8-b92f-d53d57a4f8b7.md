**Mayor’s Press Briefing: New Energy Crisis Sparks Public Outcry in Eldermere**
Residents of Eldermere report severe power outages as the city’s aging infrastructure struggles under winter storms. Emergency crews confirm widespread blackouts across downtown and industrial zones after a 15-minute surge hit the Central Distribution Hub at 8:47 AM. City officials urge residents to conserve energy and call for a state of emergency declaration within 48 hours. Meanwhile, local businesses report losses of over $2 million in sales due to disrupted operations.

**Construction Deadline Missed: Bridge Collapse Threatens Regional Traffic**
A 12-year-old reinforced concrete bridge near the northern border of Eldermere partially collapsed during a heavy snowfall last night, severing Highway 95. Authorities confirm the collapse was likely due to improper inspections, though an investigation is ongoing. The new bridge, scheduled for completion in 2025, now faces a revised deadline of Q4 2024, risking further delays for commuters. The city has activated the National Guard to manage detours.

**Protest Gains Momentum: "Tax the Rich" Movement Rallies in Main Square**
Demonstrators gathered outside the Eldermere County Courthouse yesterday, demanding higher taxes on corporate profits and luxury goods. Speakers cited rising costs of living and public services as justification. Police report three arrests for inciting a public disturbance, though no property damage was reported. The city’s Finance Department has not yet commented on the demands.

**Local Food Shortage Crisis: Farmers’ Market Collapse**
The Eldermere Farmers’ Market, a staple for small businesses and families, was forced to close for the season due to persistent flooding in the supply chain. Local growers report 30% of their crops damaged by the storms, leading to price surges. The city has set up emergency distribution centers at community centers, but supplies are limited. A state-approved "Buy Local" campaign is underway to support remaining vendors.

**Accident Report: Fatalities in Industrial Collision**
Two workers died and four were hospitalized after an unsecured forklift collided with a moving truck at the Eldermere Steelworks last night. Investigators blame inadequate safety protocols and lack of real-time surveillance. The plant has been temporarily shut down while OSHA conducts an inspection. Labor unions have called for a strike at 6 PM tonight to protest unsafe conditions.
