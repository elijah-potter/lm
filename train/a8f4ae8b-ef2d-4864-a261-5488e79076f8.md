A cavern hummed with the slow, rhythmic pulse of dripping water, its echoes muffled by layers of moss and stalactites. The air hung thick with the scent of damp earth and something faintly metallic—rust from the iron veins of the walls. A single, flickering torch cast long shadows on the ceiling, where a single, suspended crystal glowed with an unnatural, faintly blue light, pulsing like a heartbeat. The floor was a mosaic of smooth, polished stones, worn smooth by centuries of passage, leading to a hollowed-out space where the walls curved inward like the throat of a sleeping giant. A low, steady rain pattered against the ceiling, and the only sounds were the distant whisper of wind through the narrow openings and the occasional, slow, deliberate movement of a figure wearing a cloak of dark fabric, their face obscured by a hood that cast no reflection. The silence was absolute, broken only by the slow, rhythmic sound of the water.
