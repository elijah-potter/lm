Clockify - 
Clockify is a timesheet focusing on your billable hours to provide insight into your productivity levels. 

It allows users to track time manually and quickly produce insightful reports using the data. 

Clockify has many features that can assist you and integrate with 50+ apps. 

TeuxDeux - 
TeuxDeux is a task management app that functions like a to-do list. 

It is available on the webpage or mobile. 

It has many useful features that reduce the time it takes to create your to-do lists.
