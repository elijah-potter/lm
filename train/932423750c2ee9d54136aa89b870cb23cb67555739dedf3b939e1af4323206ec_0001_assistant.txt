I wish you a wonderful road trip! Here are some relaxing and exciting things to do at Niagara Falls:

1. Restaurant: Eat at a fall-side restaurant to take in the views and rest.

2. Devil's Hole Rapids: Picnic, hike, and take in the views at these rapids. This park is also a great spot to cast a line.

3. Journey Behind the Falls: Feel the powerful vibrations of the falls by stepping behind them. Descend 100 feet and walk through 130-year-old passages. End at an observation spot to take in the beauty.
