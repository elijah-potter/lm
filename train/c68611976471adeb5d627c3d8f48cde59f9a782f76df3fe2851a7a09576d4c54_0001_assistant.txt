My trip to Antarctica was a once-in-a-lifetime experience! The natural beauty of Antarctica is breathtaking. We left the southern tip of South America on a cozy expedition vessel and were at sea for several days. There was onboard entertainment that made at-sea days go by quickly - lectures, documentaries, a well-stocked library, citizen science programs, and plenty of marine life and birds. We had several social events in the evenings, like a trivia competition, a dessert tasting, and a dance party. It took several days to cross the notoriously rough waters of the Drake Passage. I won't lie, I struggled. I had to see the ship's doctor for medication. Once we arrived around the Antarctica peninsula, we were more sheltered and it was smoother. We spent several days exploring various locations along the peninsula, including my dream visit to a scientific research station, several historic sites, and many wildlife hotspots before our return.

The biggest highlight of my trip to Antarctica was seeing such a wide variety of wildlife up close and personal. When I say up close and personal, I mean that they walk right up to you. We saw so many colonies of penguins, seals, whales, and of seabirds. Visiting the penguin colonies was different than expected, as it really stunk. Those little cuties projectile poop everywhere. The smell gets in your clothes and hair. Bring Febreeze! The moment that stands out the most from the trip was this trip to the peninsula in a zodiac where a humpback whale swam right next to us most of the way. I could see his barnacles in detail, he was so close! It was life-changing. 

The scenery in Antarctica was simply wild, massive glaciers, snow-blanketed mountains, and icebergs that are measured in kilometers instead of meters. It was mind-blowing daily. 

I cannot talk about this trip without sharing the unique soundtrack we heard.

Some of my favorites were:
* The glaciers rumble and crack like thunder as they calve ice into the sea.
* The exhale of air from a whale’s blowhole.
* The slapping of little penguin feet as they follow each other down their established roadways. (This is probably my favorite soundtrack of the trip.)

Overall, my trip to Antarctica was an unforgettable experience that I will revisit in my mind for the rest of my life.
