In a quiet town nestled between rolling hills, there was a small village shop where every morning, an old clock in the corner chimed twelve times. Inside, an elderly woman named Elara tended the shelves, her fingers tracing the edges of books like old friends.

One evening, she found a single, weathered coin in the dust beneath her feet. It was unlike any she’d seen before—engraved with strange symbols. Curious, she placed it beside the clock, which had stopped at 11:59 PM.

The next morning, the coin was gone, but the clock had begun ticking again, its hands moving inexplicably. By the third day, the symbols on the coin had begun to glow faintly, pulsing like a heartbeat. Elara followed the glow to a hidden cave behind the shop, where she found a mirror hanging above a pool of water that reflected the clock’s face.

She reached out and touched it. The mirror shattered, and she was suddenly standing in a vast, endless hall where the clock’s hands spun wildly. A voice whispered: *"You must return the coin to its place."* She turned, only to find herself back in the shop, the coin now resting in her palm.

As she stared at it, the symbols faded, and the clock chimed midnight. The shop’s shelves rearranged themselves, and the dust settled. The next morning, the coin was gone again, and the clock resumed its steady tick. Elara never spoke of it, but the villagers whispered that the shop’s magic kept returning her to the beginning.
