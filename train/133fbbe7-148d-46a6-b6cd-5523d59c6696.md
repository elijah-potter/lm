## Lost in the Record Store (Brooklyn, NY) (Williamsburg)

The afternoon light was slanting through the window of Rough Trade, painting the vinyl bins in a warm, amber glow. I was searching for a specific reissue – a jazz compilation I’d been hunting for ages – and you were browsing the new arrivals, your attention completely absorbed. You had a cascade of auburn hair, loosely braided, and were wearing a vintage floral dress that seemed perfectly suited to the atmosphere. I remember the distinct scent of aged paper and dust, a comforting aroma that always permeates the shop, but it was momentarily eclipsed by the subtle fragrance of your perfume, something floral and slightly woody. We bumped shoulders briefly while both reaching for the same Miles Davis album, and you offered a quick, apologetic smile. It was a fleeting moment, barely a breath, but something about your eyes – a quiet intensity – lingered with me long after you moved on. I didn’t have the courage to say anything then, and now I regret it. 

If you recall a woman with auburn braids and a floral dress searching for new arrivals on Saturday afternoon, please reply with the name of your favorite jazz artist.
