A half-finished clock tower in a decaying village square, its gears rusted and cracked, the hands frozen midway between midnight and noon. The villagers whisper about the tower’s "broken hourglass"—when the sand flows backward, leaving no record of time passing. Shadows stretch unnaturally, stretching into the past, and future events play out in the reflection of a cracked mirror in the base of the tower. Locals claim the tower doesn’t just tell time—it *erases* it, erasing moments from existence after they pass. The air hums with static, and travelers report seeing their own reflections aged by decades, watching themselves age in reverse. The village’s cemetery holds no graves, only time-lost figures standing in their Sunday best, staring at the tower’s face.
