Apple was founded on April 1, 1976, by Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak. Their goal was to change the way people viewed computers; to make computers small and user-friendly enough for people to have them in their homes. Jobs and Wozniak started building the prototype of the Apple computer in a garage and sold them without monitors, keyboards, or casings. The second prototype, the Apple II, revolutionized the industry with the introduction of the first-ever color graphics. 

In 1997, Jobs allied with Microsoft to create a Mac version of their popular office software. Jobs revamped the computers and introduced the iBook, followed by the iPod, which became the market leader. The iPhone, a touchscreen cellular phone, was introduced in 2007 and was one of the world's most successful products, which introduced the line to further versions.
