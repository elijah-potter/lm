The neon glow of the abandoned subway station flickered against the damp walls, casting long shadows across the cracked tile floor. A lone figure—clad in a patched-up coat, gloves worn thin—stood halfway down the platform, their breath visible in the cold air. A half-empty cup of coffee sat on the bench beside them, condensation pooling on the worn wooden surface. The distant hum of distant trains was muffled by the heavy metal doors, which groaned as they swung open, revealing the dim glow of the station’s emergency exit. The figure turned slowly, their gaze fixed on the approaching figure—another, this one in a sharp suit, holding a briefcase with a single document inside. The first person’s fingers twitched toward the pocket of their coat. The second person’s lips curled slightly as they adjusted their cufflinks. The doors sealed shut behind them.
