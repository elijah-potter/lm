The dimly lit alleyway flickered with the glow of flickering streetlights, casting long shadows across damp cobblestones. A lone figure in a tattered coat crouched behind a crumbling storefront, fingers tracing the edge of a rusted revolver. The distant wail of sirens echoed through the night, but they faded into the background—too far away to be a threat. The figure’s breath hitched as a pair of boots crunched gravel near the alley’s entrance, followed by the scrape of a lock turning. The door creaked open, revealing the silhouette of another person, their face half-lit by the weak beam of a flashlight. The figure in the coat froze, gripping the revolver tighter, as the intruder stepped into the dim light, their expression unreadable. The tension in the air was thick, but neither moved first. The moment stretched into an eternity of silence, broken only by the distant hum of the city.
