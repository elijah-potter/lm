In the dim glow of the kitchen, the old wooden table creaked under the weight of a half-empty coffee mug. Emma paused, her fingers hovering over the crumbs on the tablecloth. She had just poured her second cup, but her mind wandered back to the morning’s forgotten task. The kitchen clock read 10:17, yet the **missing** recipe card—marked with the date **"05/12"**—lay nowhere in sight.

She checked beneath the counter where she’d stashed it earlier, then rifled through her apron pocket, the pockets of her jeans. The scent of toasted bread and cinnamon from the morning’s breakfast lingered, but the card was gone. Her stomach twisted. Without it, the recipe for her grandmother’s **"Sourdough Cinnamon Rolls"** was lost—no notes, no measurements, just a faded memory of the dough rising to perfection. She clenched her jaw, the weight of the day pressing down. The fridge hummed silently, the only constant in this sudden chaos.
