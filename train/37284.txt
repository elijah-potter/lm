Horizon Vertical Axis Wind Turbine Science Kit
Physics Educational Materials
Alternative Energy Learning Activities
More than 25 hours of activities for middle/high school in physics and earth/environmental science.
- Students can also design their own blades
Horizon’s Vertical Axis Wind Turbine Science Kit is the most comprehensive wind energy educational product on the market today. It allows students to explore simple wind turbine design by providing three different blade position combinations.
Delivery information: Components include 3 adjustable positions for the blades; rotor base and mounting magnets; axis assembly with removable coils; LED for output power demonstration and DIY (Do it yourself) blade set.
