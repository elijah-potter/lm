The first time Liam noticed the strange humming sound coming from his old, creaky house, he thought it was just the wind. But when he turned on the kitchen light and saw the faint glow from the ceiling, he knew something was wrong. The house wasn’t just creaking—it was whispering.

One evening, as Liam sat on the porch swing with his younger sister, Mira, watching the sunset, a shadow moved in the eaves. A figure, no taller than a child, stood on the roof. It wasn’t wearing clothes, and its limbs were too long, bending at unnatural angles. Mira screamed, but before Liam could react, the figure turned and pointed at him. The air turned cold, and the house’s walls seemed to pulse like a living thing.

The next morning, Liam found an old journal on the kitchen table. The pages were filled with strange symbols and notes in a language he didn’t recognize. Inside was a warning: *"The house is hungry. Feed it or it will eat you."* The last entry read: *"Liam, run. The walls remember."*

That night, Liam followed a trail of dust motes that led to the attic. Inside, he found shelves lined with jars containing glowing, swirling liquids. At the center of the attic stood a pedestal with a single, glowing orb. The orb pulsed like a heartbeat, and when Liam reached out, the air crackled with energy. He grabbed it, and the moment his fingers touched the orb, the house shuddered.

The next morning, the house was silent. The walls were smooth, the creaks gone. The attic was empty. But in the kitchen, on the table, was a single page from the journal: *"You gave it something it wanted. Now it wants more."*

Liam never went back inside. Mira, now terrified, moved out with their parents. The house stood empty, but every night, when the wind howled through the empty rooms, Liam swore he could still hear it whispering.
