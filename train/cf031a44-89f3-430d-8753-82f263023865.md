**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, Ill. – August 15, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, offering a traditional celebration of agriculture, community, and family entertainment. This year’s fair promises a diverse array of exhibits, demonstrations, and activities showcasing the best of Sangamon County. Visitors can anticipate livestock shows featuring prize-winning animals, a bustling marketplace with handcrafted goods, thrilling carnival rides, and a wide selection of food vendors offering classic fair fare.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 22nd through August 26th, 2024, at the fairgrounds located at 1920 East Monroe Street. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events continuing until 10:00 PM. A special fireworks display will illuminate the sky on Friday, August 24th, following the evening entertainment. The fair is committed to providing a safe and enjoyable experience for all attendees, with enhanced security measures and readily available first aid services.

Admission to the fair is $10 for adults, $5 for children ages 6-12, and free for children five and under. Carnival ride tickets and wristbands are available for purchase separately. Advance tickets can be purchased online at SangamonCountyFair.org. Parking is available on-site for a fee.

**Headline Events:**

*   Demolition Derby – Friday, August 23rd, 7:00 PM
*   Livestock Auction – Saturday, August 24th, 11:00 AM
*   Tractor Pull – Sunday, August 25th, 2:00 PM
*   Blue Ribbon Baking Competition – Daily, Exhibit Hall
*   Country Music Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters – Saturday, August 24th, 8:00 PM
*   4-H Showcase – Sunday, August 25th, 10:00 AM
