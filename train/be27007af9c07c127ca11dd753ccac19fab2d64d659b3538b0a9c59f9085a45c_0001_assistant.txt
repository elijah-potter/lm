Benny was the most popular bumblebee in the hive. Everyone who met him would say the same things about him! 

"Oh! Benny is so nice!" said the young bee on her Tricycle. 

The bee running the hotdog stand said, "Yeah I know Benny. Everyone knows Benny. Benny's a great guy! We all love Benny!" 

These compliments made Benny very happy, but he didn't understand why he was getting them. Every day that passed by Benny became more and more popular throughout the hive. Eventually, he found himself on national television! Benny became a superstar throughout the world of bees and he didn't know what to do next! 

One day, Benny got a buzz from Queen Bee herself! She told him that he was the best bee in the whole world of hives. Benny still didn't understand so he asked Queen Bee, "What makes me so special?" 

Queen Bee couldn't believe that Benny didn't understand why he was so special. She told him, "Benny! Do you not see? You are the purest bee in the hive! You are wholesome and demonstrate unconditional love! You must join me and be my King Bee!" 

Benny was astounded. He did not feel worthy of such a title. "No, my queen, I cannot do that. It is not right. I am nothing but a normal bee." 

Queen Bee became angry and ordered Benny, "No! This very reluctance is what makes you so worthy!" The queen calmed down before saying, "Benny, you are already the king. You have always been the king. You just never knew it. Please, join me." 

Benny thought for a moment and hung up the phone. He was the king.
