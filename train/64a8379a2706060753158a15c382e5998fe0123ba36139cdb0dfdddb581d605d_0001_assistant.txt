I visited the Vanara restaurant with my family for an afternoon lunch recently, and all I can say is wow. This magical place is conveniently located just along the road, so you can't miss it, with ample parking space too. It was our first dining experience, and my family marveled at the experience they had here. From service to the meals, everything was excellent. We all had the Broa, a yeast-leavened, fluffy white sliced bread, it was delicious, and I have to admit it's the best bread I have ever eaten. The rest of the dishes were out of this world. They couldn't have got the music, decor, and lighting any better. Lovely. Visit this place to enjoy all that it offers.
