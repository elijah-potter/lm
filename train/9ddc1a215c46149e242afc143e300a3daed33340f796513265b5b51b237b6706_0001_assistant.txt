Marie Curie was the first woman to be awarded a Nobel Peace Prize, and the only woman to be awarded two Nobel Peace Prizes.

Marie and her husband Pierre Curie received the Nobel Peace Prize for Physics in 1903, for their work on the spontaneous radiation discovered by Henri Bequerel, who also shared their Nobel Peace Prize. 

Later, in 1911, Marie Curie received a second Nobel Peace Prize, this time in chemistry, for her work in radioactivity.
