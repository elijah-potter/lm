Q: What is the name of the opening song from Kendrick Lamar's album "To Pimp a Butterfly?" 
A: Wesley's Theory.
Q: Who is "Wesley" in "Wesley's Theory" based on?
A: Wesley Snipes.
Q: Which hip-hop artist shows up in the song "Institutionalized?"
A: Snoop.
Q: Which song has the line "This dick ain't free!"?
A: "For Free? (Interlude).
Q: What happens at the end of the song "Mortal Man?"
A: The song ends a very long fever-dream interview with the ghost of 2Pac.
Q: How does the album break out of the hip-hop genre?
A: The album includes half a jazz band comprised of pianist Robert Glasper, producer/sax player Terrace Martin and bassist Thundercat.
Q: How does Kendrick Lamar branch out from the hip-hop genre on the album?
A: Kendrick Lamar does slam poetry on the song "For Free."
