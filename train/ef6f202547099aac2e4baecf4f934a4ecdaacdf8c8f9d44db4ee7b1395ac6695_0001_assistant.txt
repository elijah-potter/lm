There are two types of venomous snakes:

1. Rattlesnakes: These snakes are the largest venomous snakes in the United States. These snakes often use a rattle as a warning when alarmed. To treat a bite from this type of snake, antivenom is recommended. 

2. Cottonmouths: Also called Water Moccasins, these snakes average around 50-55 inches long. These can be found in Wetlands, rivers, and Lakes in the southeastern area of the United States.
