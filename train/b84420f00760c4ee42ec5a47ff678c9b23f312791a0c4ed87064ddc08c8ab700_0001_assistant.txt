A derelict rocket ship that lost both its origin and its purpose was drifting through the vast expanse of space. Fearfully, humanity made its way there, pulled by an otherworldly signal emanating within. They entered, enduring the crushing darkness as eldritch shadows swirled about them. They came across an old control room with decaying consoles and a flickering monitor that still pulsed with an eerie light.
The display revealed writhing tendrils and swirling darkness—a cosmic doorway to realms beyond human awareness. They witnessed horrors on board the ship, including insatiable darkness, fractured realities, and ravenous hunger. As the ship unveiled cosmic truths that ravaged their knowledge, their minds strained, and their perceptions were shattered.
They uncovered an ancient entity stirring within the ship—a terrifying force ravenous for the souls of the living. A cosmic abomination—the herald of destruction, drawn to chaos—was arriving as the ship's signal became stronger.
The investigators struggled against madness after finding that the rocket ship was a tool of eldritch evil and an omen of cosmic terror. Its appearance heralded a calamity of unimaginable proportions. They sent out a frightening message to the universe knowing that they would perish in the void.
Humanity, conscious of the terrors they had unleashed, gazed into the infinite darkness as the ship continued to drift and the signal started to fade. The abandoned ship kept on traveling across time, a sign of a coming calamity that alluded to the cosmic horror that awaited anyone who dared journey into the unknown.
