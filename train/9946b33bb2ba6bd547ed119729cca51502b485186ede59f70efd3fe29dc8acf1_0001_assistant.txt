Jeremy walked through the door as he would on any other day and Scruffy ran up to him as he would on any other day, except this time something different happened. “Hey, Jeremy! How was school? How was school?”

Jeremy jumped back and yelled out “What!?”

Scruffy smiled and said, “I asked how school was!”

“No! Like what is going on? How and why are you talking?”

Scruffy cocked his head to the side, “I always ask this when you come home?”

“I never understood you though! Now we’re talking to each other! How is that possible?”

Scruffy looked down, “I’m not sure. I did wake up feeling weird today.”

Jeremy squatted down to his dog’s level, “Weird? How?”

Scruffy walked over to him and nudged his head onto Jeremy's hand like he always did. “I don’t know, just different. Did I upset you?”

Jeremy pet his dog and looked down, “No, you didn’t upset me, just freaked me out. It’s kinda cool though. I’ve always wondered what was going on in that little doggy brain of yours.”

Scruffy wagged his tail, “I’ve always wished you could too!” He climbed up and licked Jeremy’s face. "Hey, I have an idea!”

“What’s that?” Jeremy asked.

“Since you always ask me, I’m going to ask you for a change. You wanna go for a walk?”

“Sure. Let’s go for a walk.” Jeremy said, “But don’t talk if we see people. I don't know if anyone else can hear you and I don’t want to freak anyone out.”
