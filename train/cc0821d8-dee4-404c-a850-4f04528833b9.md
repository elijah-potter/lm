## Product Requirements Document: "Context" – Knowledge Consolidation App

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently juggle multiple sources of information – documents, meeting notes, research papers, online articles – leading to fragmented understanding, difficulty recalling relevant data, and diminished productivity.

**Vision:** To empower knowledge workers with a centralized, intelligently organized space for consolidating information, fostering deep understanding and accelerating insight generation.

---

**User Personas:**

1.  **Dr. Eleanor Vance, Research Scientist:**
    *   **Goals:** Efficiently synthesize findings from disparate research papers, maintain a comprehensive understanding of her field, and quickly retrieve specific data points for grant proposals and publications.
    *   **Pain Points:** Overwhelmed by the sheer volume of academic literature, struggles to connect ideas across different papers, wastes time searching for specific information within lengthy documents.
2.  **Marcus Bellweather, Management Consultant:**
    *   **Goals:** Rapidly process client briefings, synthesize competitive intelligence, and develop compelling presentations based on a holistic understanding of the situation.
    *   **Pain Points:** Difficulty retaining key information from client meetings and research reports, struggles to identify patterns and insights across different data sources, often reconstructs information from scratch.
3.  **Aisha Sharma, Legal Analyst:**
    *   **Goals:** Organize and analyze case files, legal precedents, and regulatory documents, ensuring accurate recall of details and facilitating efficient legal research.
    *   **Pain Points:** Disorganized case files lead to missed deadlines and potential errors, difficulty connecting legal arguments across different cases, spends excessive time manually compiling information for reports.

---

**Requirements:**

1.  **Intelligent Document Ingestion:** The application must allow users to import documents in common formats (PDF, DOCX, TXT, HTML) and automatically extract text and metadata.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Successful import of documents with varied formatting and structure. Metadata (title, author, date) is accurately extracted. Optical Character Recognition (OCR) functionality is included for scanned documents.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 95% successful import rate across diverse document types. Average OCR accuracy of 90%.
2.  **Contextual Note-Taking:** Users must be able to add notes and annotations directly within documents, linking them to specific passages and creating a structured knowledge base.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Ability to highlight text and add associated notes. Notes are persistently linked to the original text. Notes are searchable and filterable.
    *   **Success Metrics:** Average of 3 notes per imported document. 80% of users utilize note-taking functionality.
3.  **Relationship Mapping:** The application must allow users to visually connect notes and documents, creating a network of related ideas and concepts.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Ability to create links between notes and documents. Visual representation of relationships (graph view). Ability to search and filter based on connections.
    *   **Success Metrics:** Average of 2 connections per user. 70% of users utilize relationship mapping functionality.
4.  **Smart Search & Retrieval:** Users must be able to quickly and accurately retrieve information based on keywords, phrases, and relationships.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Full-text search across all imported documents and notes. Filtering by document type, date, and tags. Ability to search for notes connected to specific documents.
    *   **Success Metrics:** Average search time under 3 seconds. 90% of searches return relevant results.
5.  **Cross-Device Synchronization:** User data and knowledge bases must be seamlessly synchronized across multiple devices (desktop, iOS, Android).
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Real-time synchronization of documents, notes, and connections across devices. Data integrity maintained during synchronization.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 99.9% synchronization uptime. Minimal data loss during synchronization events.

---

**Technical Considerations:**

*   **Scalability:** The application architecture must be designed to handle a large number of users and documents.
*   **Security:** Robust security measures must be implemented to protect user data and intellectual property.
*   **Platform Support:** Initial focus on iOS, Android, and macOS. Web application considered for future development.
*   **Third-Party Integrations:** Potential future integrations with cloud storage providers (Google Drive, Dropbox) and note-taking services (Evernote, OneNote).

**Risks:**

*   **OCR Accuracy:** Dependence on third-party OCR services introduces potential accuracy limitations.
*   **Data Privacy:** Ensuring compliance with data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA) is paramount.
*   **User Adoption:** The application’s complexity may hinder user adoption; onboarding and tutorials are crucial.
*   **Computational Cost:** Intelligent features (relationship mapping, smart search) may require significant computational resources.



**Non-Goals:**

*   Direct document editing capabilities.
*   Real-time collaboration features.
*   Automated summarization of documents.
*   Integration with social media platforms.
*   Support for proprietary document formats.



---

**Launch Checklist:**

1.  Complete core feature development and testing (intelligent ingestion, contextual notes, smart search).
2.  Develop comprehensive user onboarding and help documentation.
3.  Establish secure cloud infrastructure for data storage and synchronization.
4.  Implement robust user authentication and authorization mechanisms.
5.  Conduct beta testing with a representative group of target users.
6.  Finalize application store listings and marketing materials.
