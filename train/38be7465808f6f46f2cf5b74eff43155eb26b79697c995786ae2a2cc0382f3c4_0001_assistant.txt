The earliest known example of a mechanical pencil dates to the late 18th century and was discovered in the debris from HMS Pandora's 1791 shipwreck. The first patent issued for a pencil with a refillable lead-dispensing mechanism was issued in 1822 to British inventors John Isaac Hawkins and Sampson Mordan. The following year, Mordan bought out Hawkins' patent rights and started a business with Gabriel Riddle manufacturing pencils. Their business partnership ended in 1837. Mordan continued to produce pencils and other silver objects under the name  "S. Mordan & Co" until the factory was bombed in World War II.
