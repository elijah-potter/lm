I picked up this book because I’ve recently gotten into collecting the different cool boxes Kleenex has used over the years. I thought “Kleenex, Blowing Through the Ages” might give me more information about how to find the boxes. I was disappointed. While there was a lot of information about the company, there was not very much about the package designs at all. I had figured that the cover design with all the pretty pictures of historic box designs and the blurb on the back talking all about the history of tissue boxes meant I could learn more about collecting them. Nope. The book was funny, though. I’ll give it that. I recommend it to anyone who has an interest in how Kleenex came about and wants a laugh. It just wasn’t all that helpful to me as a fledgling collector.
