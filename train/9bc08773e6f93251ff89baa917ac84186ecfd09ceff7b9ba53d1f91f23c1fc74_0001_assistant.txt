It's funny, I never thought
Shoelaces would make me cry. But,
The pink ones, the ones MJ used to
Tie in the most vicious double-knots,
Just sat,
In a corner,
In my new home,
Snuck in by accident, somehow,
Or maybe, from me in the past.
She loved them, and MJ, and 
Butterflies,
Glitter,
Rocks,
Swimming,
And Laffy-Taffys.
She hated cities, and yet,
Here I am, a different kind of climbing,
The double-knots now in my throat,
Instead.
Goodbye, old friend.
