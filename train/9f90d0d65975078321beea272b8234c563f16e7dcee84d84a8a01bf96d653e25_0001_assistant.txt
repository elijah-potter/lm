Washing your hair is super simple to do. First, you take your favorite shampoo and squeeze the size of a quarter on your hand. Next, you're going to rub your hands together super fast until you see bubbles form! You know how flies rub their arms together super quickly? You want to do that. Now, take your soapy hands and rub them all over the top of your head. Get your hands underneath your hair to wash your scalp. That's the part of your head that's under all your hair! Once your head is covered in soap, put your head under the water and rinse it all out. Don't stick your entire face in the water, otherwise, you might get soap in your eyes! That would be very ouchy. Once you finish rinsing all the soap from your hair, you have washed your hair! Good job!
