The old barn creaked under the weight of a storm raging outside. Inside, Elias sat on a stool, his knuckles white around the half-empty whiskey bottle. Across the dimmed lantern’s glow, his father’s silhouette leaned against the counter, arms crossed. A promise hung between them like dust in the wind.

*"You’ll keep the fields watered, no matter what,"* his father had said, voice rough as gravel. Elias had nodded, but the night was his to burn. Now, the barn’s floorboards groaned under a bucket of ice water dumped at his feet. The promise was a test—one Elias had been waiting to fail.

Outside, thunder split the sky. Inside, the lantern flickered.
