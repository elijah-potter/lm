A 5-minute nightly routine of journaling became an all-consuming obsession, replacing sleep and social interactions. The habit—initially meant to track gratitude—evolved into a compulsive need to document every thought, emotion, and minor event, leading to a 10+ page daily log. The mental strain from overanalysis and the fear of missing a detail caused fatigue and irritability. Relationships suffered as time away from others became prioritized over shared experiences. The habit had turned into a self-imposed cage, eroding mental well-being and forcing a painful reevaluation of its purpose.
