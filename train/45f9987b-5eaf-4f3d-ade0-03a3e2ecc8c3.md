Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a garden full of flowers, there lived a bunny named Benny who loved to hop around and chase his favorite ball. One sunny morning, Benny noticed something sparkly in the garden—it was a tiny, glowing star! Benny picked it up and felt its warmth.

Just then, Benny’s little friend, Max the mouse, came running over. "Benny, look!" Max squeaked. "There’s a magic star that can make everything bright and happy!"

Benny’s eyes sparkled. "Can it really?" he wondered. So, they pressed the star together, and suddenly, the garden turned golden and full of dancing fireflies. Benny and Max giggled, and the star shimmered even brighter.

A tiny voice giggled from the tree. "You two are so kind!" It was a little bird named Pip. "I found this star too, but it was sad. Now, it’s happy again!"

Benny grinned. "We’ll keep it safe!" They all hugged the star, and from that day on, whenever Benny and Max played, they’d press the star together—because magic was the best adventure of all.
