A sentinel of polished obsidian, its surface etched with ancient runes that whisper secrets to the wind. It bends, not broken, as it guards the threshold between worlds—its gaze sharp as a blade, its patience as deep as the sea. Every touch sends ripples through the dark, for it remembers the weight of every soul that passes beneath its shadow. The air around it hums with the quiet pulse of forgotten time, and those who listen too closely hear its voice, slow and deliberate: *"You are not alone."*
