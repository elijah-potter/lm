A dimly lit office hallway at 3:17 AM, fluorescent lights buzzing overhead. The fluorescent hum is the only sound until a sudden, sharp, *unexpected* burst of laughter erupts from the corner near the stairwell. A group of three people—one hunched over a laptop, another mid-sip from a thermos, the third typing frantically on a tablet—freeze mid-motion, eyes wide. The laughter comes again, higher-pitched, more chaotic, and the person typing slams their screen shut, knocking over their coffee. The sound echoes off the tiled walls as they all scramble backward, knocking into the desk behind them. A chair screeches. The laughter fades into a cough, then another, and finally, a confused, disbelieving whisper: *"Oh my God."* The office is silent again, the weight of the moment lingering like a ghost.
