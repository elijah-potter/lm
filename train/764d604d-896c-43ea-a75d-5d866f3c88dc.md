A dimly lit room, fogged by the evening’s mist, held a lone figure—**Elias**—seated at a weathered wooden table, his fingers tracing the rim of a half-empty whiskey glass. Outside, the village of **Blackthorn Hollow** lay in darkness, its lanterns barely piercing the thick veil of dusk. The wind carried whispers of the forest, where the old trees stood sentinel over secrets buried for centuries.

A knock at the door was followed by the creak of hinges. **Mira**, Elias’s sister, stepped inside, her breath visible in the cold air. Her eyes were wide, her lips pressed into a thin line. "Father’s ship—it’s gone," she said, voice tight. "The crew vanished in the storm, and the cargo... it’s all gone."

Elias exhaled slowly, rubbing his temples. "The **Blackthorn Contract**," he muttered. "A promise to the **Celestial Council**—gold, not silver. And now... the debt is doubled." Mira’s jaw clenched. "You can’t just walk away."

The air between them crackled. The weight of the past decade pressed down—**lost opportunities, broken dreams, the bloodstained ledgers of his father’s business**. But the contract was more than money. It was a **covenant**, one that bound them to the stars. If they abandoned it, the Council would hunt them. And the Hollow would burn.

Elias stood, his coat brushing against the floor. "Then let’s see how long it takes them to find us," he said, voice low. "Because we’re not running." Outside, the first stars blinked to life, watching. Waiting.
