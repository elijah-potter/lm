**Mayor’s Office Statement on New "Eco-Groves" Initiative**
City Council approves pilot program to replace 50% of urban trees with native species to combat drought and reduce carbon footprint. Phase one begins in downtown Riverbend by next month, targeting flood-prone areas for improved resilience. Local farmers receive grants to supply saplings; volunteers needed for planting. "We’re not just planting trees—we’re planting the future," says Mayor Elena Vasquez. Permits required for removals; existing residents must submit impact assessments via online portal by Friday. Funded by $2M from state’s Climate Adaptation Fund. Protests expected from urban forest advocates; officials call for dialogue to ensure balanced approach.
