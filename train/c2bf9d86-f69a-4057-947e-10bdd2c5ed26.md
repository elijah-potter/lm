The tiniest shift—a single step, a half-turn of the wrist—can ripple outward. One misplaced chair, and a hallway reshapes into a maze. A dropped spoon, though inconsequential, warms the kitchen’s silence, and the next meal feels heavier. The inkblot on your wristwatch, though erased, stains the air with a ghost of time’s passage.

A single line of code in a failing script, corrected. A whisper in a crowded room, amplified into a storm. The act of *not* locking your phone—now, it’s unlocked. A seed planted in winter, sprouts in spring. The smallest act, when repeated, is the architect of the unspoken.
