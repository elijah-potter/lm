Once upon a time in a cozy little house, there lived a curious cat named Lila. One sunny morning, Lila sat on her favorite sunny window sill, watching the world outside. She saw a tiny bird with a bright red feather flapping its wings and singing a happy song.

Lila wanted to help. She stretched her paw out and gently tapped the bird’s wing. The bird chirped, "Thank you!" and flew away happily.

Soon, Lila noticed a little ant carrying a crumb of bread home. She smiled and dropped a tiny drop of water near the ant’s path. The ant happily carried the crumb all the way to its nest.

The next day, Lila saw a big, fluffy cloud floating by. She giggled and waved her tail. The cloud smiled and let a little rain drop down to soak the thirsty flowers in the garden.

The little flowers bowed their heads and thanked Lila. "You are the kindest cat in the whole world," they whispered.

From that day on, Lila loved helping everyone—birds, ants, clouds, and even the little flowers. She knew that being kind made the world a little brighter, just like her sunny smile.
