## City of Atheria Mandates Emotional Resonance Calibration for All Public Transportation

**By Reginald Finch, Metro Affairs Correspondent**

Atheria, a municipality previously known for its meticulously planned tulip displays and remarkably punctual bus service, has implemented a groundbreaking, if somewhat perplexing, policy regarding public transportation. As of Tuesday, all buses, trams, and the city’s experimental pneumatic tube network are now equipped with “Affective Harmonizers,” devices designed to ensure passenger emotional states remain within a pre-determined range of “optimal contentment.” The initiative, dubbed “Project Serenity,” is the brainchild of the Atheria Department of Civic Wellbeing and aims to mitigate stress and promote a more agreeable commute.

The Affective Harmonizers, developed by the privately-funded but city-supported firm “Sympathetic Systems,” function by analyzing facial micro-expressions and vocal tonality within the vehicles. Should a passenger exhibit signs of distress – be it frustration, anxiety, or even excessive joy – the system subtly adjusts ambient lighting, plays curated audio tracks, and releases measured doses of aromatherapy compounds. Initial reports indicate a preference for lavender and chamomile, although the system is reportedly capable of adapting to individual sensitivities. "We are simply striving to create an environment conducive to collective tranquility," explained Bartholomew Quill, Director of Harmonious Transit. "A slightly elevated heart rate on the 7:15 express is simply unacceptable.”

The policy’s immediate impact has been… noticeable. Commuters report a disconcerting uniformity in emotional response. Spontaneous laughter has become rare. Audible sighs are a punishable offense, resulting in a brief, but firm, corrective burst of synthesized harp music.  One particularly enthusiastic pigeon, attempting to nest on a tram window, was reportedly neutralized by a precisely timed release of calming pheromones. "It’s… pleasant, I suppose,” offered Agnes Plumtart, the City’s designated “Minister of Measured Merriment.” “But I do miss the occasional outburst of genuine exasperation. It added a certain… spice.”  The Atheria Police Department has reported a sharp decline in minor infractions, though they now face the challenge of identifying and addressing “affective dissonance” – a new category of public disturbance.

The economic repercussions are equally peculiar. Local florists, anticipating a surge in demand for celebratory bouquets, are now struggling to clear their stock of lilies and roses. The city’s “Department of Unnecessary Complaints” has been effectively dissolved, its staff reassigned to monitor the Harmonizers’ operational efficiency.  "We're seeing a worrying trend of existential apathy," confessed Professor Theodore Bumble, the City’s “Custodian of Curious Consequences.”  "People are… content. Too content. It’s profoundly unsettling."  The pneumatic tube network, initially intended for rapid parcel delivery, is now utilized primarily for the discreet distribution of motivational posters featuring kittens and inspirational quotes.

The long-term effects remain to be seen, but early data suggests a correlation between Project Serenity and a city-wide decline in creative output.  The Atheria Philharmonic Orchestra has reportedly begun performing exclusively in C major.  The City Council is now debating a proposal to mandate synchronized blinking amongst all citizens.  “We believe in the power of measured contentment,” stated Esmeralda Finchley, the City's “Grand Arbiter of Agreeable Atmospheres.”  “A little bit of enforced serenity goes a long way.”
