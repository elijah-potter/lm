The train station was packed, steam rising from the tracks. A weary figure in a damp trench coat fumbled with the ticket stub, glancing at the clock repeatedly—each minute ticking past like a slow heartbeat. The last-minute rush of passengers swirled around them, their voices overlapping in a chaotic hum.

Then, a familiar voice cut through the noise: *"You’re late."* The person stiffened, but relief flooded them as they recognized the voice of their boss. The tension in their shoulders eased as they adjusted their bag, already calculating how much time they’d have left for the elevator. The train’s whistle shrieked, signaling departure, and the person sprinted toward the doors, clutching their phone—just enough time to reply, *"Sorry, traffic was brutal."*
