Isoko and Makonnen met at a support group for new immigrants. The moment they met, Makonnen was drawn to Isoko. She was strong, and confident, and had overcome a lot of trauma in her family. He admired the way she held her head high. Isoko liked that Makonnen was kind and generous. They shared a belief in the importance of community. Before long, Makonnen and Isoko were married.

They moved into a small apartment in a bad neighborhood. It was all they could afford until Isoko found steady work. Makonnen worked hard, but his job didn't pay much. Even though it wasn't paradise, they worked together to make it cozy and comfortable inside. When fighting in the neighborhood started to scare them, they would put on music and dance together. Then, noises inside the house began to frighten them even more. They were alarmed when things began to break on their own. In a country that felt increasingly hostile to outsiders, they were determined not to give up their only sanctuary. So, they repaired the broken things and started to talk back to the noises, hoping the spirit or whatever was in the house would be appeased. When the noises stopped, they thought they had succeeded. Then, the rats began to invade.
