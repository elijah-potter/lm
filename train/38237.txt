The azalea is a flowering plant that is part of the genus rhododendron. With proper care, azaleas grown from seed can produce flowers in about three or four years. Azaleas bloom once per year, in the spring or midsummer. Because azaleas are accustomed to warm southern climates, they are an ideal plant to grow inside where the temperature is controlled. With proper planting, azaleas are beautiful, hardy plants that will produce bright-colored blooms year after year.
Prepare a soil mixture that is one-third well-washed sand, one-third peat moss and one-third perlite.
Fill a plastic plant container half full of the soil mixture and dampen the soil thoroughly. The soil should be moist but not overly damp so that the water pools.
Sprinkle the seeds uniformly over the top of the soil. The seeds can be close together but not touching.
Place a clear plastic bag over the top of the container to trap in moisture. This is ideal for indoor growing because homes tend to be dry, especially in the winter.
Set the freshly planted seeds under a fluorescent grow light or shop light. The light source should have the equivalent of about two 40 watt bulbs – this is enough light to support the growth of 15 plus seedlings.You can leave the light on all the time or shut it off for a few hours each day.
Leave the seeds alone until they begin to germinate and the first leaves appear, in about two or three weeks. At this point, remove the seedlings and transplant them to their own containers so they have more growing room.
Keep the plants covered as they grow to lock in the moisture. There is no need to water the plants during this process. Allow them to grow over the winter, and then keep them as indoor plants or plant them outside in the spring.
Things You Will Need
- Peat moss
- Plastic planting containers with drainage holes
- Azalea seeds
- Clear plastic bag
- Fluorescent light source with two 40 watt bulbs
