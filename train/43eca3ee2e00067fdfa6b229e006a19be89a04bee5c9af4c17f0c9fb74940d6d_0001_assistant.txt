Sean the Shark swam through the sea
With his colleague close by his side
His partner was an old stingray
Who served as an excellent guide

The aquatic duo sped through the surf
In search of a hint or a clue
You see, Sean and the ray are detectives
Who try to bring truth to view

Their latest case involved a seahorse
Who had failed to pay all his tax
They tracked him across the ocean
Until they found him, they could not relax

They finally spied the seahorse
Hiding amongst some coral
The culprit knew it was no use
He gave up without a quarrel
