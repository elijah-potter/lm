Q: What unifying event links the oldest Americans, the Silent and Greatest generations?
A: World War II is the unifying event that links the Silent and Greatest generations.

Q: What important historic events do the Silent and Greatest generations name on their top 10 list?
A: They name 9/11 and the Second World War, with 44% naming the latter and 59% naming the former.

Q: What events stand out on Baby Boomers' list of major historic events?
A: The assassination of John F. Kennedy, the Vietnam War, and the Apollo 11 moon landing are on the Baby Boomers' list of major historic events.

Q: How many Baby Boomers list the Kennedy assassination as one of their top 10 events?
A: Fully 45% of Baby Boomers list the Kennedy assassination as one of their top 10 events.

Q: What historic events stand out on Millennial's and Generation X's lists?
A: For Millennials and Generation X, Sept. 11 and the election of Barack Obama, the first black president, stand out on their lists.

Q: How many Millennials and Gen Xers list 9/11 as a significant event in their lifetimes?
A: Fully 86% of Millennials and 79% of Gen Xers list 9/11 as a significant event in their lifetimes.

Q: What percentage of Millennials name the Obama presidency as a significant event?
A: 47% of Millennials name the Obama presidency as a significant event.

Q: Do older generations mention a larger selection of historic events?
A: Yes, among older generations, a larger selection of historic events garners mention by more than a quarter of their members.
