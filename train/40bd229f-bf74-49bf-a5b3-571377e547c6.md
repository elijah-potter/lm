A **Temporal Arcane Observatory (TAO)** in the ruins of an abandoned astronomical tower, where clocks operate in **non-linear cycles**:
- **Hourglasses** flow backward when the sky turns **golden** at dusk.
- **Clockwork gears** tick in **harmonic inversions** (e.g., 12:00 PM becomes 12:00 AM in 12 hours).
- **Time crystals** (static but oscillating) emit **fractal ticks**—each "second" contains 3600 repetitions of a 1/720th cycle.
- **Temporal mirrors** reflect past futures; observers see their own hands **rewinding** upon reflection.

The **clocktower’s pendulum** swings in **infinite regress**—each swing completes the full cycle again from the start. The air hums a **cosmic chime** at the hour of **x + 7** (where *x* is the current local anomaly).

Guards are **time-bound entities**—they age **in reverse** when within earshot of the **Echoing Well**, which amplifies **forgotten moments** from the observer’s past.
