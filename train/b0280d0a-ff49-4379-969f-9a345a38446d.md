Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a little girl named Mia who loved to play outside. One sunny morning, she ran outside to find her favorite toy, a red ball, hiding under a big tree.

"Oh, Mia!" cried her dog, Biscuit, wagging his tail. "I saw it!"

Mia picked up the ball and rolled it down a hill. "Look what I found!" she shouted.

Suddenly, a tiny fairy with sparkly wings fluttered down. "Hello, Mia! Are you looking for a treasure?" she asked.

Mia smiled. "I was just playing with my ball!"

The fairy laughed. "Treasure is everywhere! Here, take this shiny pebble from my bag. It's magic—it makes things sparkle!"

Mia picked up the pebble and noticed her shoes glowed for a second. "Wow, it’s magical!"

Biscuit barked excitedly, and Mia ran home with her sparkly pebble, feeling happy and full of adventure. The end!
