Jay was a young boy, who grew up in a rough neighborhood and learned to fight at a young age. Having always been passionate about boxing, Jay knew he wanted to enter that ring one day to let out his anger. One day, Jay meets a retired boxer who sees potential in him and offers to train him. With the help of his new mentor, Perkins, Jay begins his journey to become a pro boxer. In this 3D fighting game, players can use Jay's street fighting skills and learn new boxing moves as he progresses through the city's boxing league. The tougher opponent that Jay faces leads him to learn new techniques and strategies to come out on top. 

Throughout the game, players also encounter various challenges and obstacles such as corrupt boxing managers, street drama, and crooked fans who will stop at nothing to keep Jay from succeeding. But with determination and skill, Jay will either rise to the top or fall to the bottom. Titled "Hadouken," this street fighting game combines the intensity of street fighting with the precision and strategy of boxing, while following an inspirational journey of a young fighter trying to achieve his dreams.
