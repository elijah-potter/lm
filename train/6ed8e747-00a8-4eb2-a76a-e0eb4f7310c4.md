**The List of Names**
1. **Mira** – last seen in her favorite café with a half-eaten croissant.
2. **Liam** – the one who still texts "just checking in."
3. **Darius** – the only one who laughs when I joke about my terrible cooking.
4. **Eleanor** – the person who always remembers my birthday, even when I forget mine.

**The Confession**

I keep a list of names in my phone, each one a reminder of what I used to be. Liam, the one who left without a word, and Darius, the only friend who never judged. But the real confession? I don’t know if I’ll ever tell them all the truth—I’m the one who lied about the past, who hid the things that broke me.

The list isn’t just names. It’s the weight of silence.
