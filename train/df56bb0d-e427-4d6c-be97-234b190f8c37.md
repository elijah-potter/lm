A flicker of motion caught the corner of their eye—something moving just beyond the glow of the streetlamp. The person froze, fingers twitching toward the camera mounted on the balcony, but hesitated. The shadow shifted again, longer this time, stretching unnaturally across the pavement. A whisper of static, then a voice, barely audible over the hum of the city traffic: *"You saw it too."* The figure in the alley—tall, cloaked, hands clutching a lantern—had vanished mid-step, leaving only the lingering scent of damp earth and something metallic. The person’s breath hitched; the camera’s flash had gone off, but no image remained. Only the cold certainty that something unseen had watched them.
