**The Hollow**
A cavernous space beneath an abandoned cathedral, its stone walls lined with flickering lanterns and half-eroded graffiti. The air hums with low, rhythmic chanting—voices overlapping, indistinct. Some sit cross-legged on the floor, others stand in a circle, their hands clasped loosely. No one speaks of purpose, only of the way the space feels alive, as if the walls themselves whisper. Occasional figures emerge from the shadows, moving with slow, deliberate steps, carrying no belongings but carrying something heavier—doubt, curiosity, or a shared unease. The center of the circle is empty, save for a single, unlit candle that never burns out.
