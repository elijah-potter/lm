**BREAKING:** Mayor Elara Voss announces emergency housing plan for displaced families in **New Haven’s Golden Creek District**. Officials report over 120 families displaced due to sudden flooding after the abandoned mill dam collapsed last night, submerging historic industrial sites along the Riverfront. Emergency shelters set up at the old theater, with food and medical aid provided by Red Cross volunteers. "This is a community crisis, not a natural disaster," said Mayor Voss, urging residents to report missing relatives to the 24/7 hotline. Authorities warn of potential long-term pollution risks as cleanup efforts begin. Meanwhile, protests at the construction site of the new downtown transit hub demand transparency on compensation for affected workers.
