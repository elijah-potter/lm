**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 14, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, promising a celebration of agricultural heritage, community spirit, and family-friendly entertainment. This year’s fair showcases livestock competitions, artisan displays, thrilling rides, and a diverse selection of food vendors, providing a memorable experience for attendees of all ages. Traditional fair activities, such as the baking competition and the 4-H exhibits, will continue to highlight the dedication and talent within the county.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 22nd through August 26th, 2024, at the fairgrounds located at 123 Fairway Drive, Springfield. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM on Thursday and Friday, and 9:00 PM on Saturday. A special opening day fireworks display is scheduled for Thursday, August 22nd, following the evening’s entertainment. Daily admission is $10 for adults, $5 for children aged 5-12, and free for children under 5. Season passes, offering unlimited access throughout the fair, are available for $35. Tickets can be purchased at the gate or online at www.sangamoncountyfair.org.

This year's fair features a dynamic schedule of headline events, including:

*   Demolition Derby – Friday evening
*   Livestock Auction – Saturday morning
*   Tractor Pull – Thursday evening
*   Blue Ribbon Baking Competition Awards – Wednesday afternoon
*   Professional Rodeo Series – Saturday evening
*   Live Music Performances – Daily throughout the fair
