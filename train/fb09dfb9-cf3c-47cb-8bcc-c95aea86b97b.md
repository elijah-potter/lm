The dim glow of a flickering streetlamp cast long shadows across the cobblestone path as they walked side by side. The silence between them was thick, punctuated only by the distant hum of traffic. The woman clutched her coat tighter, her fingers brushing against his sleeve without intention. His hand stilled, then found hers—small, warm, and unguarded.

*"I thought you’d never come,"* she whispered, her voice rough with emotion. His breath hitched; he’d been trying to forget this moment for months. *"I was afraid I’d never get the chance to say it."*

His mouth opened, words caught in his throat as she tilted her head, her eyes reflecting the lamplight—soft, knowing. *"You didn’t have to,"* she murmured, her voice trembling. His heart pounded; he’d been waiting for this for years.

The air between them crackled with understanding. Not words, but the weight of what they’d never spoken aloud before. The way she held her breath when he reached for her hand. The way he hesitated before confessing his love. The way they’d both known, for a single, perfect moment, that they were finally whole.
