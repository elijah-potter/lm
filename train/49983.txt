What can we each do to reduce our water consumption, protect local freshwater resources and enhance our local watershed communities?
Get involved in the 10,000 Rain Gardens Project! Learn How!
Rainwater harvesting techniques are part of an integrated approach to water conservation that includes soil building and conservation, mulching, efficient irrigation practices, water-wise and locally-appropriate landscaping, and greywater recycling. The 10,000 Rain Gardens Project is designed to provide and share resources, strategies and techniques to help you get started harvesting rain, a precious local resource!
Join us in being part of a growing, worldwide movement to protect and value rainwater as the precious resource it is and to foster healthy and thriving watersheds!
