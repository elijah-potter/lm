Oh boy, do I love tomatoes
I love the color
The taste
And when the juice gets all over my whiskers

Every morning I hop
Hop hop hop
Over to the farm next door

They have the best tomatoes
Bright red
So sweet
And juicy as can be

The only problem is Farmer Tom
Every day he runs at me
Chasing me away from my treat
But I don’t mind

I’m faster than Farmer Tom
I can eat eat eat the tomatoes
And as soon as I hear him shout
I hop hop hop

Back to the woods
With my belly full
My whiskers wet
And a big smile on my face
