Once upon a time in a cozy little house, there lived a bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the garden. One sunny morning, Benny spotted a tiny, shimmering flower that looked just like his favorite blueberry. He picked it carefully and carried it home, where his mommy rabbit was making a yummy treat.

"Mommy," Benny whispered, "I found a magic flower!" His mommy smiled and said, "What if it makes the best berry in the world?" Benny giggled and helped her place the flower in the basket. As the sun set, the garden sparkled, and the flower turned into the biggest, juiciest blueberry Benny had ever seen! "Tastes like sunshine!" he cheered, and everyone at the picnic laughed.

The next day, Benny found more magic flowers, and his family made even more amazing berries. Benny learned that kindness and sharing made the world brighter, just like the magic flowers that grew in the garden. And from that day on, Benny always remembered to be kind and happy, just like his wonderful magic berry!
