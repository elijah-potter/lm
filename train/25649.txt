Young environmental scientists will enjoy testing the principles of wind, water, and solar energy with the authentic generators included in this kit.
Designed for use indoors and out, the modules allow students to witness the generation of energy from renewable and sustainable resources. So your students can easily observe and record the power generated, four interchangeable monitors show differing outputs from the various heads that fit into a common base.
An exciting, tactile experience, this kit develops a concrete understanding of energy resources. Includes a teacher's guide with suggested activities. Made of high-impact plastic, the base is 7" x 10", the maximum height is 20", monitors are 3" in diameter and the kit includes 20 feet of clear tubing for the water module.
