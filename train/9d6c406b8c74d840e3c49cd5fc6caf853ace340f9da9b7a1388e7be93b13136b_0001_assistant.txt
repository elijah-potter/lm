The weight-loss findings in the article are the following:

A new injectable drug (called Wegovy) has been called a "silver bullet" for weight loss and has the potential for the user to lose between 5-10% of their body weight. Another drug called tirezpatide showed a 22.5% decrease in body weight in its clinical trial (currently being fast-tracked by the FDA). However, both of these drugs can cost upwards of $1000 a month (weight-loss drugs are usually not covered by insurance).
