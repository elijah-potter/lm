One day there was a little girl named Becky who loved fairies so much that no one could tell her they were not real. Becky lived near a deep thick forest, and every day she would look out the window for hours, waiting for fairies to show up. Her mother would sometimes leave fairy trails for her or make fairy homes for the little girl to find. Her brother would even lie and say he had spotted a fairy. Then, he would go into detail about the fairy's wings and outfits. 

Becky was playing outside when she saw her mom placing a tiny house made of an old teacup beside a tree trunk. She wondered why her mother had placed a tiny fairy house down. Becky asked, "Why are you putting a tiny teacup house down." Her mother did not know what to say, Becky's brother saw the two of them talking and quickly said "Mom was fixing the fairy house because a fairy left a note for her asking if she can fix it. Becky was growing suspicious of the two.

Becky's mother was starting to have a hard time keeping up with her lies and couldn't keep up with them as well. Becky's mother was in the garden making a fake fairy trail with a little note when suddenly Becky was standing over her looking in disbelief. She started to cry asking why they lied to her, the mother stated that she wanted her to always believe in something and to keep her imagination. Her mother told her no matter what she can always believe in fairies even if she has never seen one. From that day on Becky didn't believe in fairies however she believed in her mother and brother.
