On May 21, Axiom Space is scheduled to launch Ax-2, its second private mission to the International Space Station. Representatives from Axiom Space, NASA, and SpaceX conducted a final launch readiness review and approved the launch. The SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket and Dragon capsule are planned to launch from NASA's Kennedy Space Center in Florida. The space mission is scheduled for 10 days and involves several unprecedented events in space exploration. The mission has been planned and executed after thorough preparation and cooperation among all the parties involved.
