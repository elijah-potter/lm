Titus is a county in Texas. It was formed in 1846 from the following county/ies: Bowie/Red River. Titus began keeping birth records in 1870, marriage records in 1895, and death records in 1903. It began keeping land records in 1846, probate records in 1895, and court records in 1895. For more information, contact the county at 100 W. First St., Ste. 104, Mt. Pleasant 75455.
