The kitchen counter gleamed under the dim morning light, empty save for a half-empty coffee mug and a crumpled receipt. The missing item—a sleek, silver tablet—sat on the countertop’s edge, its screen dark and cracked at the top. The owner had left it there after a quick breakfast, but now, with only a few minutes to spare before their morning commute, they noticed its absence. The charger, still plugged into the wall, hinted at the device’s last stand-by. The fridge hummed quietly, its shelves devoid of the tablet’s usual spot where it rested beside a fresh packet of coffee. The scent of brewing coffee mingled with the faint, stale aroma of yesterday’s leftovers, the only sound the soft ticking of the kitchen clock.
