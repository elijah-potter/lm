Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown fur and big, round ears that flopped when he ran.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his favorite thing—his garden full of colorful flowers. He hopped outside and noticed a tiny, shiny seed rolling away from a dandelion. "Mmm, I wonder where that goes?" he thought.

Benny chased after the seed and caught it just in time. He tucked it into his pocket and decided to plant it right away. "Who knows?" he said, "Maybe this seed will grow into a giant flower!" With a happy hop, he dug a small hole and buried the seed.

That night, as Benny snuggled in his burrow, he dreamed of flowers blooming. The next morning, he checked his garden again. Sure enough, the seed had sprouted! A tiny green shoot poked out from the dirt. Benny giggled and watched as it grew taller and taller.

Soon, a bright yellow flower with big, curly petals popped up. Benny was so excited he danced around the garden. "Look! My flower is growing!" he cheered.

Days passed, and the flower grew bigger and prettier. One afternoon, Benny noticed a tiny bee buzzing around. "Hello, little bee!" Benny called. The bee landed on the flower and sipped some sweet nectar. Benny smiled and waved.

Soon, the flower turned pink and smelled so sweet. Benny decided to share it with his friends. He plucked a tiny piece and gave it to his friend, Max the mouse. Max nibbled the flower and giggled, "It's so pretty!"

Benny’s flower stayed beautiful for many days, and every time he looked at it, he remembered how happy planting something new made him. And that’s how Benny’s garden became the happiest place in the world, full of flowers that made everyone smile.
