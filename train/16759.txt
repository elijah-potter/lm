European Hare and Carduelis
European hare (Lepus europaeus) is common widely in the world and very well investigated by the scientists and naturalists. Their range extends very wide. The appearance and habits of this animal are familiar to all nature-lovers. The hind legs of the hare are muscular and much longer than the forelegs. The hare is fit for a fast run. The animals able to reach speeds of up to 70 kph.. Sometimes, hare does a huge jump, taking off above a bush like a race horse.
