Silence lingers as the rain taps against the rusted roof of the abandoned churchyard. The air smells of damp earth and old wood. **Lena** stands near the cracked stained-glass window, her fingers tracing the jagged edges of a faded design—a broken heart, once vibrant red. Across from her, **Ethan** kneels beside the moss-covered stone bench, his boots scuffing the gravel. His eyes flick to her, then to the half-buried journal resting on the bench—a gift from a younger self.

A pause. Then—

*"You always knew,"* Lena says, voice rough with unshed tears. Ethan exhales, wiping his face with the back of his hand. The words hang between them, unspoken but clear.

*"I just didn’t know *how*."* He picks up the journal, flips it open. Inside, a single page: *"If you read this, I’m already gone. But if you don’t, I’m not."*—Lena’s handwriting, scrawled in the rain. His breath catches. He reads it again. Then looks at her.

Lena’s chest rises. The words inside him, the one he couldn’t say—*"I was scared, too"*—burst out. The bench creaks. They stare at each other. No words left.

The rain stops. The sky, once gray, brightens. The broken heart in the window glows—just for a second—before fading.
