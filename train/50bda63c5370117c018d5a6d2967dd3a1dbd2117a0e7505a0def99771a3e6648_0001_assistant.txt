Beneath the open sky,
Among the trees and breeze,
Nature beckons, a gentle whisper,
Inviting me with ease.

With each step on the ground,
I shed the weight of life,
And let my spirit free,
Relinquished from worry and strife.

In this world of many greens and blues,
I find my solace and peace,
A place where the soul renews,
And all the pain must cease.

Nature's embrace, so pure and true,
Beauty beyond compare,
Its freedom, I shall savor,
For it's where I belong, out there.
