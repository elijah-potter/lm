Liam the bunny had a very big, very round belly.
"Mmm, yummy!" he nibbled on a carrot.
But his tummy felt funny. He couldn’t stop crying.
"Mama, Mama!" he hopped.
Mama bunny patted his head.
"Maybe it’s time for a little nap," she said.

Liam yawned wide. He curled up and fell asleep.
Soon, Mama bunny sang a soft lullaby:
*"Round and round like a wheel,
When your tummy feels so much better, feel!"*
He giggled as the words tickled his ears.
Then—*pop!*—his belly felt light.
"Mama, I’m fine!" he grinned.

Mama bunny smiled. "Good job, dear!"
Liam ate another carrot and felt even happier.
"Next time, we’ll eat our veggies slow and steady!" she said.

Liam’s belly stayed fluffy, but his heart was full.
And from that day on, he always checked—
*"Is my belly nice and round?
Or time for a little nap?"*
