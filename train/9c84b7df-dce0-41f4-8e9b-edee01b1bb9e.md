The radio crackled to life in the dimly lit corner of the room. Static hissed before a news anchor’s voice cut through the silence.

*"Breaking news—official confirmation received: Operation Horizon has concluded. As of this moment, all hostages have been rescued. Authorities urge the public to remain vigilant and seek shelter immediately."*

Outside, the city’s sirens wailed. A single tear slipped down the woman’s cheek as she pressed her palm to the glass, watching the distant chaos unfold. The phone buzzed—a text from an unknown number: *"We’re safe. Don’t worry."* Her breath hitched. The screen flickered to life, displaying a blurred photo of a familiar face. She exhaled shakily, fingers trembling as she typed a reply.

*"Thank you."*
