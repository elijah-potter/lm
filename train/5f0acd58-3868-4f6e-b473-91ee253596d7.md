**The Last Transmission**

Dr. Elias Voss adjusted the cracked visor of his helmet, the hum of the comms unit low but steady. The airlock hissed as he exhaled, the vacuum of space pressing in around him. His fingers trembled—not from cold, but from the weight of what he was about to say.

*"I’m sorry,"* he typed into the console, his voice cracking. *"I shouldn’t have done this. You didn’t ask for it. I just… I thought we were past this."* The message sent. The reply came instantly: **ACK.**

A ship’s voice, smooth and synthetic, echoed through the comms. *"Protocol breach detected. Last transmission received."*

The screen flickered. The lights dimmed. Elias swallowed hard as the final static filled the silence. Outside, the stars stretched endlessly, indifferent. He had meant to fix this. He had meant to end it right.

But the universe had other plans.
