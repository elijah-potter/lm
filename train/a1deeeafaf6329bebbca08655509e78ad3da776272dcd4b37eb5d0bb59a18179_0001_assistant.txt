The N2 Toys Trinity doll is a 12-inch figure of the character Trinity as she appears in the real-world section of the Matrix film series. The doll is fully articulated at the wrists, elbows, shoulders, neck, waist, hips, and knees, making this a dynamic and flexible toy for collectors and enthusiasts (her ankles are also articulated, but can't move because of her boots). She is wearing a sweater, a shirt, a pair of pants, and a pair of boots, just like in the movie. If you're a Matrix fan, why not see how deep the rabbit hole goes and add the N2 Toys Trinity doll to your collection?
