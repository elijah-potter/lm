Kuriyama was an old woman with a bad back who lived alone. She'd lived alone since she was 67 when her husband died. Despite her age and back, she still managed to take care of herself and the house. Her granddaughter occasionally came by to take care of her and help with things. Despite the fuss, Kuriyama was happy to have the help. More than anything, she was happy to have her family with her. 

One day, while her granddaughter was helping her clean, she came across an old box in the back of a closet. Before she could open it, Kuriyama yelled, "Don't you dare open that box!"

She dashed over and slapped her granddaughter's wrist, "don't you know better than to go through someone else's things?"

Her granddaughter apologized but couldn't help but ask what was in the box. Kuriyama set the box on the kitchen table and sighed as she eased herself into her chair. "It's a long story. I'd forgotten I had this box, even. I'm not sure if you'll believe me, though."

"I don't think you'd lie to me, Gran."

Kuriyama opened the box to reveal several vials. Only two still had anything left in them. She pulled one out and held it up to the light, smiling fondly, "these two are all I have left of my adventures with your grandfather."

Kuriyama then launched into a tale about how she'd once been involved in a battle across universes. It was a tale of strife, desperation, determination, and love. Her granddaughter listened carefully to the whole story. "It all started when I was about your age. I had just graduated high school..."
