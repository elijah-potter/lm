Stephen King's best-selling novel is "The Shining," published in 1977. This book has become a phenomenon and a cult classic since its release, captivating readers with its strange and complex world. It holds the top spot on reading lists and has sold over 700,000 copies, making it the most successful book of Stephen King's entire career. The story revolves around a hotel caretaker, his wife, and a child living in the eerie Overlook Hotel. Other notable best-sellers by Stephen King include "Carrie," "Salem's Lot," and "Misery."
