The old wooden door creaked open with a groan, revealing a dimly lit hallway lined with flickering lanterns. Rain pattered against the cobblestones outside, but inside, the air smelled of damp wool and old parchment. **A loose thread from a forgotten glove** tumbled from the pocket of a discarded coat, fluttering toward a crumbling hearth where a dying fire cast long shadows.

She bent, catching it before it could drift away—just a small, frayed remnant of someone’s past. **A single silver thread, bright against the dark.** She tucked it into her own coat, not knowing where it might lead, only that it was something.
