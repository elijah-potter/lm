1. Public Market: This market is home to a lot of vendors that sell all their wares. Being next to the sea means that there are a lot of fishermen trying to sell what they caught right as they caught it. Sometimes there is some weird stuff like the Three-pronged Swordfish or flying pigs that the air fishermen catch, but most of it is pretty normal fare! Sat-tle is proud of our fishermen and our market.
2. Space Needle: Probably our biggest landmark and most popular. The Space Needle towers over us with a height of over 100ft! It really makes you feel like you're one step away from touching space! Considering Space is only about 100 or 200ft from where we live, the tourists have to wear special suits as soon as they get up there just in case any accidents happen. Don't want anyone to get in trouble!
3. The Sat-tle Ferris Wheel: This Ferris wheel hangs off one of the piers close to the Public Market. Going up gives you an amazing view over Sat-tle. You can enjoy it with your party, or you can book one of our server worms to serve you dinner while you take the Ferris Wheel up. It's an experience you'll never forget.
4. Pal Dendroson's Park: Recently renovated, Pal Dendroson's Park is a great place to go if you have a kid or want to walk your pet! Green as far as the eye can see. On one end of the park is an entire baseball field with astroturf, artificial grass that simulates jumping on the moon! You'll have a blast jumping 20ft in the air, trying to catch that sky-high home run ball!
