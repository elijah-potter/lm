James Dewitt Yancey (better known as Jay Dee or J Dilla) was an American hip-hop producer, rapper, and DJ best known for his work with Slum Village, The Ummah, The Soulquarians, and his solo work. Dilla was notable for his approach to sampling which, according to The Guardian, took instrumental hip-hop into new more musically complex realms. He was also well renowned for his signature off-kilter drums. Before Yancey, most popular music was divided into even and uneven pulses. Dilla changed this by combining both kinds of pulses into a new "drunk" drum feel.
