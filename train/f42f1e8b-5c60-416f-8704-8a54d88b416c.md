Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had soft, fluffy ears and a heart full of curiosity. One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to explore.

Benny saw a shimmering rainbow in the sky. "Wow!" Benny said, "I wonder what colors are on the other side?" Benny hopped toward the rainbow, but it kept changing colors and disappearing.

Just then, Benny met a friendly squirrel named Sammy. "Let's help each other," said Sammy. Together, they chased the rainbow, turning little circles in the grass.

Benny found a tiny, sparkling treasure—a smooth, shiny stone that sparkled like the rainbow. "This is the best treasure ever!" Benny cheered, hiding it under a bush.

As the sun set, Benny and Sammy sat on a big, soft cloud. "Today was the best day ever," said Benny. "I learned that even when things change, we can still have fun together."

And from that day on, Benny and Sammy played together every day, always hopping and exploring new adventures.
