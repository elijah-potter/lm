A floating academy of experimental governance, suspended between two storm-lashed cliffs.
The air hums with the sound of modified gravity fields—some walls shift like liquid, others pulse with bioluminescent protocols.
Rules here are encoded in the *echoes* of collective intent: the first to speak a new law writes it in the air itself, and the next to touch it must *hear* it as truth. Fences are made of woven memory, and the only crime is to forget the rules before they’re written.
Children are trained in *reversible* justice—if a mistake is caught, the offender can rewind their actions like a tape, but only until the law was first spoken.
The only constant: no one is allowed to ask *why* the rules exist. That question is too heavy for the floating weight of this place.
