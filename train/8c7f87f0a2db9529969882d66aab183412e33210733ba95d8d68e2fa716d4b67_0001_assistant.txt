To ensure your chicken is cooked properly, it's best to use a food thermometer. Insert the thermometer into the thickest part of the chicken, such as the breast of a whole chicken. Look for a temperature reading of 180°F (82°C) for a whole chicken, or 165°F (74°C) for chicken cuts.
Food thermometers come in various types, including digital and non-digital variants. However, for basic temperature checking, you can find a suitable food thermometer at your local grocery store. These thermometers typically consist of two parts: a long, slender needle stem and a round head that displays the temperature.
