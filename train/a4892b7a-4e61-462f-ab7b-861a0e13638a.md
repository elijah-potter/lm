Feeling of being overheard often involves an intense awareness of being constantly observed or scrutinized, even when no one is physically present. The sensation can manifest as heightened vigilance, anxiety, or a sense of being exposed to the world’s scrutiny. It may create an overwhelming need to maintain control over one's actions or words, fearing unintended consequences or judgment. Emotionally, it can induce feelings of isolation or paranoia, as if one is trapped in an invisible audience. Physically, it might manifest as restlessness, tension, or an urge to constantly check surroundings. The perception can distort reality, making even private thoughts seem public, leading to self-consciousness and emotional strain.
