Ham is the meat of pork/pig. Basically, people cut the meat of pig, and preserve it wet or dry, with or without smoking, that meat is called ham. Ham is also an element in Little Alchemy 2. That’s why many people try to make a ham in little alchemy 2.
But many of them, don’t know the steps of making a ham. If you are one of them, this article is for you. For the making of a ham, first, we have to make smoke and a pig. In this article, we will tell you, step by step that how to make a ham in Little Alchemy 2.
Steps to Make a Ham in Little Alchemy 2:
Before telling you about the steps, we want to tell you something about the making of a ham. For making a ham, first, we have to make some things, for making smoke and a pig. Then at the end, we will mix the pig into the smoke to make a ham. Follow the below-mentioned steps!
- In the 1st step, we will make Pond:
- Water + Water = Puddle
- Puddle + Water = Pond
- In the 2nd step, we will make Sea:
- Pond + Water = Lake
- Lake + Water = Sea
- In the 3rd step, we will make Primordial Soup:
- Sea + Earth = Primordial Soup
- In the 4th step, we will make Life:
- Fire + Fire = Energy
- Energy + Primordial Soup = Life
- In the 5th step, we will make Soil:
- Earth + Earth = Land
- Land + Life = Soil (Animal)
- In the 6th step, we will make Smoke/Ash/Tobacco:
- Soil + Life = Plant
- Plant + Fire = Smoke (Ash,Tobacco)
- In the 7th step, we will make Mud:
- Earth + Water = Mud
- In the 8th step, we will make Pig:
- Mud + Animal = Pig
- In the 9th step, finally, we will make Ham:
- Pig + Smoke = Ham
