Professor Plum: The seriously good-looking Professor Plum is the guy every girl and most guys want. But he has some secrets that he’s not telling anyone, and when they find out, they may not want him anymore. 
Colonel Mustard: Always looking for a fight, Colonel Mustard wasn’t actually in the Army, although it seems like he would have done well. This guy never waits for provocation to start throwing punches. 
Miss Scarlet: Known by many as the Old Witch, Miss Scarlett carries around tinctures and books that make everyone fairly uncomfortable. But the big question is: Does she use her witchcraft for good or evil?
Yvette: Little Miss Yvette is the perfect “girl next door.” She’s sweet, southern, and the perfect belle. Except there’s something about Yvette that seems a little too perfect, which proves to be true in more ways than one. 
Mrs. White: If you ever dreamed of the ideal mother, you probably pictured Mrs. White. She loves to bake, sew, and take care of anyone in need. Although nurturing runs in her blood, she has expectations of those she cares for. 
Blackmailer Mr. Boddy: Though people call him Blackmailer Mr. Boddy, Boddy doesn’t seem like he would blackmail anybody! He’s quiet as a mouse and doesn’t seem to be very interested in anyone else’s drama. But as everyone soon finds out, there is some truth to the ruthless nickname. 
Wadsworth: Once a butler and now the owner of his own mansion, Wadsworth has an ego the size of Mars. He thinks everyone should bask in his amazingness, and if they don’t think he’s quite as great as he thinks he is, he can get extremely frustrated. 
Mrs. Peacock: One of the most interesting women in the world, Mrs. Peacock is like no one you’ve ever met before. Completely excentric and slightly strange, Mrs. Peacock is surprisingly loveable. 
Mr. Green: Green loves to entertain, but he hates to be entertained. If the attention isn’t on him, he will do crazy things to get it back. And not necessarily crazy in a good way.
