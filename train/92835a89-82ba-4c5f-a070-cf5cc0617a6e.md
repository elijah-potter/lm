A floating city of glass and steel suspended above a labyrinth of neon-lit streets, where gravity is optional. The streets curve like living veins, shifting based on collective moods—some paths lead uphill, others disappear into black holes where time slows. Buildings pulse with bioluminescent flora, their shapes rearranged daily by holographic architects. Laws are written in shifting code on the city’s outer shell: obey the holograms, or the system will reformat your existence into the next iteration. Markets operate on liquid time—trades happen in bursts, then vanish. Police are invisible, replaced by AI that dissolves dissent into data. The only constant is the hum of the sky-pipes, where citizens trade memories, emotions, and fragments of old rules in a market that never closes.
