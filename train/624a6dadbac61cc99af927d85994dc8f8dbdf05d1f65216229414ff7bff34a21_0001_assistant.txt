1. The Inuktitut name for Baffin Island is Qikiqtaaluk.
2. Norse explorers referred to the island as Helluland.
3. Martin Frobisher named the island "Queen Elizabeth's Foreland".
4. Baffin Island is named for English explorer William Baffin.
5. Baffin Island was formerly called James Island. 
6. Qikiqtaaluk means "very big island" in the Inuktitut language.
7. The Norse term "Helluland" means "stone land".
