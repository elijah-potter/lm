Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white fur and big, round ears that always twitched with excitement.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his friend, a tiny blue bird named Bella, sitting on the windowsill. "Good morning, Bella!" Benny said with a happy hop. "What’s your favorite color?"

Bella chirped, "I love blue, just like me!" Benny smiled and said, "Mine is green! Let’s go outside to play!"

Outside, Benny and Bella ran through the golden grass, jumping over dandelions that were as bright as sunshine. Benny’s paws made little tracks in the dirt, while Bella flapped her wings and sang a sweet song.

Suddenly, a little cloud with a face floated near them. "Oh no!" it said. "The sky is getting a little too dark. We need to find a safe spot!"

Benny and Bella looked around. "We can go to the big, cozy tree house," Benny said. "It’s under the tallest oak tree with the biggest leaves!"

They hopped and fluttered to the tree house, where it was warm and safe. Inside, they found a basket filled with soft yarn, colorful blocks, and a tiny book about animals. Bella read a story about a bunny who loved to explore, and Benny giggled until his tummy hurt.

As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and orange, Benny said, "Bella, let’s make a wish. Maybe we’ll see each other tomorrow!"

Bella nodded. "Yes! And maybe we’ll find even more fun adventures!"

With a happy sigh, they curled up in their blankets, dreaming of more bunny and bird adventures under the big red door.
