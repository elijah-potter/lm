1. Which Greek mythological figure was the drug Morphine named after?
Answer: Morpheus

2. Who was the sculptor in Greek myth that fell in love with his own statue?
Answer: Pygmalion

3. What location was Icarus trying to escape when he flew too close to the sun?
Answer: Crete

4. When performing his twelve labors, whose stables did Hercules have to clean?
Answer: Augeas

5. Which figure in Greek mythology is the equivalent of Cupid in Roman mythology?
Answer: Eros
