To my lovely Lucille,

You bring such joy and clarity to my life, it can make a man weep. I will never love anyone in the way I love you. You are so much more than just an instrument. You are my best friend and my true love. I could hold you in my arms forever. The feeling when you come alive in my hands is magical. You take this simple blues man places I could never begin to imagine.

Lucille, my baby, we have been through so much pain and sorrow, and you turn every moment of it into beauty. I hope I live forever, but I hope you live forever and a day because I can't imagine a world without you. You are the most beautiful thing I have ever laid my poor eyes on. You make my heart sing the blues in the best way every single day. I will always love you.

Forever your King,
B.B.
