Whispering Gallery, St. Mary's Church, Prague
---
A cavernous cathedral basement lined with carved stone arches, where the air hums with the faintest echoes of unseen voices. The thick walls absorb sound, trapping whispers from the upper nave into narrow, arched alcoves. Monks in the past reportedly gathered here to pray in silence, but modern-day visitors now linger near the high ceilings, where sound bounces off the domed ceiling, creating a reverberating chamber. The floor’s uneven stone surfaces and hidden gaps in the walls ensure privacy—only the faintest whispers reach beyond the circle.
