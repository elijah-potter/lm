Okay, here are some songs you could possibly draw inspiration from or adapt into a cover:

1. "Blinding Lights" by The Weeknd - This song has such a retro vibe and it's ultra-catchy, a perfect fit for an acoustic arrangement.
2. "The Times They Are A-Changin" by Bob Dylan - A timeless protest song by a legendary folk artist that you are bound to draw inspiration from, as Dylan was one of the acoustic guitar's greats.
3. "Sitting on the Dock of the Bay" by Otis Redding - This classic R&B song should offer inspiration at least with its chilled-out vibe and a melody that will get stuck in your head.
4. "Walking On Sunshine" by Katrina and the Waves - Turning this upbeat song into an acoustic folk joint could be just your style.  At the very least, it will put a smile on your face.
5. "Killing Me Softly With His Song" by Roberta Flack - An often covered R&B jam, you are sure to feel some type of inspiration from this song's raw emotion.
6. "Hey Ya!" by OutKast - Although seemingly unconventional, this song could be perfect for an acoustic cover with its emotional lyrics and catchy rhythms.
