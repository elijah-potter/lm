Protect California Kids From Harmful BPA!By Ali Geering-Kline
The Toxin-Free Infants and Toddlers Act is only one signature away from becoming a law! This landmark legislation would ban BPA in baby bottles and sippy cups, protecting kids from BPA’s harmful health effects including brain and heart disorders, and breast cancer and infertility later in life.Bisphenol-A, BPA health hazards, BPA in baby bottles, BPA in sippy cups, Governor Brown, Toxin-Free Infants and Toddlers Act
