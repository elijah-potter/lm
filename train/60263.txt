AGE005 - Overview of Alzheimer's Disease
This course provides an overview of the prevalence, causes and risk factors of the disease.
Alzheimer’s disease is an irreversible, progressive brain disease that slowly destroys memory and
thinking skills and eventually even the ability to carry out the simplest tasks. This course provides an overview of the prevalence, causes and risk factors of the disease.
- Describe the changes in the brain that occur with Alzheimer’s disease;
- List the early signs and symptoms of Alzheimer’s disease;
- Explain the causes of Alzheimer’s disease;
- Explain the diagnosis and treatment of Alzheimer’s disease;
- Cite examples of the ongoing research associated with Alzheimer’s disease.
