Cats act like they are super cool
Because they think you are a fool.
If they meow too much,
It means they want to be touched.

They come in different shapes and sizes,
And they are our little prizes.
If you try to give them a bath,
You will feel their wrath.

If the sun is up
They will lay and say yup,
And if they see a rat
He will try to have a little chat.
