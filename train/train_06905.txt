Jonny climbed 1269 stairs last week. Julia climbed 7 less than one-third of that amount. How many stairs did Julia and Jonny climb together?
Julia climbed 1269/3 - 7 = <<1269/3-7=416>>416 stairs
Total stairs climbed = 1269 + 416 = <<1269+416=1685>>1685 stairs
Together Jonny and Julia climbed <<1685=1685>>1685 stairs.
