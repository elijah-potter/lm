Coins are like the feelings of the world. 
One side can represent positive feelings, the other negative.
Every time you flip a coin somewhere, something changes in the world.
Can you imagine if the world worked out like that?
Flip once, get heads. An unknown man gets his big break and starts his dream job.
Flip twice, get tails. The earthquake happening in a country you don't even know about gets stronger and stronger.
You can very rarely get the third option, landing on its edge. 
That can represent a very fine balance between positive or negative.
What if I told you that you have the power to make that coin land on whatever side you want?
Think about the choices. You won't gain a benefit or a demerit from this choice. 
So what does it matter, right?
Are you a good deeds kind of person? One that believes that karma exists in the world?
Or maybe you like a little chaos in your life. Can you handle ruining someone's or someone's life because of pushing the coin in one direction?
There is one more option though: You can flip again. Just leave it to chance like it always was.
But making no decision is also making a decision. 
As long as you realize that, feel free to make any decision you want.
Make one decision you will not regret.
