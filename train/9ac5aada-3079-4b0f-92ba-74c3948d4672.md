**Small-town diner** in a tight-knit village near a lake. The barista remembers the name of every regular, and the elderly farmer across the street nods at you after years of passing. The local bakery’s owner swears she recognizes your grandma’s voice from years back, and the kids at the playground have been exchanging secrets since they were toddlers. A few families live in the same block; neighbors share recipes, gossip, and sometimes the same dog. The town’s general store stock is just enough to avoid shortages, and the mayor’s annual speech is mandatory for everyone. The only strangers are the rare outsiders—visiting hunters, seasonal workers, or the occasional tourist who stumbles in and gets lost. The air hums with unspoken trust, like the lake’s ripples carry whispers across the shore.
