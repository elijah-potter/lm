**Tidal Basin, Washington D.C.**

A narrow, manicured park where visitors gather along the reflecting pool, their movements synchronized by the rhythm of the tides. Steep, wooded banks frame the space, offering both seclusion and vantage points over the Potomac River. The crowd thins as evening falls, replaced by soft lights and the murmur of conversation, though morning returns with a surge of families, tourists, and joggers. The water’s gentle ripples mirror the shifting crowds, their ebb and flow mirrored in the changing crowds.
