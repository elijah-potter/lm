Augmented reality is an interactive experience of a real-world environment where the objects that reside in the real-world are enhanced by computer-generated perceptual information, sometimes across multiple sensory modalities, including visual, auditory.
A similar technology that empowered AR game such as Pokémon GO.
Participants will be able to: –
i. Create and manipulate a 3D object on AR marker image;
ii. Import and view custom 3D model as AR object;
iii. Overlay the marker image with 2D image and video
Smartphone (must) & Laptop (optional)
