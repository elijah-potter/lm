A dimly lit hallway stretched endlessly under flickering fluorescent lights, the air thick with stale humidity. Shadows stretched unnaturally long, casting eerie shapes against the peeling wallpaper. A single floor lamp pulsed weakly, its glass globe half-broken, casting a sickly yellow glow over the worn wooden floor.

At the far end, a door stood slightly ajar, its brass handle rusted. The wood groaned under its own weight. No one came. No one left. Just the occasional creak of the floorboards beneath a solitary figure hunched against a wall, fingers digging into their temples.

The clock on the wall ticked, its hands moving in slow, relentless motion. Time stretched like taffy, unraveling. No message. No warning. Only the weight of an unknown waiting, suspended between hope and dread.
