Household appliances have been used for many decades in homes around the globe. This includes dishwashers, microwave ovens, and toasters, to name a few. Millions of people rely on these electronic devices every day to assist them with routine cooking and cleaning tasks. Before you seek out or purchase any new appliances for your home, it is helpful to know as much as you can about this industry. Listed below are some key terms and definitions to help expand your knowledge of household appliances.
Refrigerator – This is an electronic appliance that has one or more doors, and is used to cool food products. It functions like an artificial cooling compartment or storage unit for perishable foods. Most refrigerators have a freezer unit on them as well.
Gas Stove – A unit used in a kitchen setting to heat and cook foods. This type of stove is fueled by propane gas, and typically has four eyes or burners to cook on. Each burner has a pilot light that must be lit when the gas is turned on.
Dishwasher – A machine that is commonly found in kitchens these days, and is used to clean dishes and silverware. These appliances are enclosed and have racks to load the dirty dishes on. Typically a dishwasher soap is dispensed into the machine’s door before it is started.
Microwave Oven – This is a compact oven unit that is typically used in residential homes and offices to heat or cook food quickly. It uses electromagnetic wavelengths to rapidly heat foods and liquids, and can be set to different heating levels or powers.
Blender – An electric appliance that is used to mix or blend things together, such as foods and liquids. These units typically have a base with control buttons and a glass pitcher.
The Association of Home Appliance Manufacturers is a trade organization that represents members of the home appliance industry. AHAM members include manufacturers of home appliances, as well as the companies who supply and service.
