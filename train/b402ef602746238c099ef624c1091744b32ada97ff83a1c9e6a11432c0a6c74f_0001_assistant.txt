Electric cars have been around for quite a long time now and we see more and more on the road, with some having autonomous driving. Some people are petrified by the fact that a car can drive itself but currently, we are seeing more and more intelligence being portrayed by self-driving cars day in and day out. Many companies are switching their business model to only selling electric cars such as Jaguar which plans to sell only electric cars from 2025 and Volvo from 2030. Will people start to just accept electric cars as more are rolled out? Well, first, the power is no joke and won't be a problem for a lot of people as some standard electric cars put up a fight against many hypercars. A lot of people hate electric cars because they are significantly different from petrol or diesel cars, such as not producing sounds while driving the car. Some electric manufacturers have tried to fix this issue by playing sounds through a speaker which has left some petrolheads in stitches. While some people do hate it, many have come to accept it and see it as the future. Whether or not you like electric cars, they will be more beneficial for the environment and may be cheaper to run in the long term.
