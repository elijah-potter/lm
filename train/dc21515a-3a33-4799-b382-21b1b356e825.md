The argument raged between them as they stood in the dimly lit kitchen, the scent of boiling water mixing with the acrid tang of spilled coffee. *"You’re wasting your time with that!"* she snapped, her voice cutting through the tension like a knife. His jaw tightened, fingers digging into his armrests of the chair. *"I’m not the one who just called in sick three times in a row!"*

The phone rang, startling them both. He snatched it up, the screen glowing with the time—**3:17 AM**. *"I’ll be right there,"* he muttered, voice gruff, already halfway out the door. She watched him go, arms crossed, the weight of another sleepless night pressing down on her shoulders. *"You’re never coming back,"* she whispered to herself, but the words tasted like regret.
