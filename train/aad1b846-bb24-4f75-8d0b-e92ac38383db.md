A half-remembered dream where time flickers like a candle flame.
Your breath catches mid-step as you realize—you’ve walked this path before.
The shadows stretch longer than they should, and the air hums with a sound that’s not quite yours.
A name whispered in a language you almost recognize.
Then, like a film reel rewinding, the past pulls you under.
You’re not the one who’s here anymore—just a ghost in a borrowed moment.
