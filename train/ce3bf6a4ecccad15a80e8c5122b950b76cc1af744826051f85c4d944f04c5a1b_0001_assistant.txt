Memento was directed by Christopher Nolan. It is a neo-noir mystery psychological thriller film that was released in 2000. The following actors starred in Memento:

1. Guy Pearce (as Leonard Shelby)
2. Carrie-Anne Moss (as Natalie)
3. Joe Pantoliano (as John Edward "Teddy" Gammell)

The film was widely acclaimed by critics for its unique non-linear narrative.
