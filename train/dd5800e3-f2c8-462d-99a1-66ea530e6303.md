A single misplaced file led to the discovery of a hidden ledger in the server room, revealing decades of unrecorded transactions between the old mayor’s family and local businesses. The original plan to "clean up" outdated contracts dissolved when the documents themselves became the plot—each page a clue, each handwritten note a character. The mayor’s granddaughter, who had been archiving them, found herself drawn into the story of how the ledger was forged and hidden, turning the city’s bureaucratic mess into a narrative of deception and redemption. By the time the truth spilled, the archive had become an artifact, its pages filled with new secrets—and the project had morphed into something neither planned nor expected.
