Person A: *"Hey, can you pick up milk and eggs at the store on your way home?"*
Person B: *"Sure, I’ll grab them when I stop by the grocery."*

Person A: *"Thanks! Oh, and could you also get some bread and butter?"*

Person B: *"No, I don’t want to stop. I already bought groceries today."*

Person A: *"You have to stop somewhere—just get the bread, I’ll handle the rest."*

Person B: *"I’m not doing extra stuff. Just don’t ask."*

Person A: *"I’ll pay you for it."*

Person B: *"Fine, but I’m not doing it."*
