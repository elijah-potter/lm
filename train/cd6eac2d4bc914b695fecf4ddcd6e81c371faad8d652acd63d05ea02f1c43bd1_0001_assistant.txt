The main character is a woman in her late twenties who is slowly being stalked by something. She doesn't know what it is, but it has been following her, hiding behind corners and in the shadows for a few days now. Something had happened a few days ago that drew the monster to the protagonist and caused it to latch onto her, but she is unaware of that in those first few days. The monster continues to pursue her, slowly, so slowly. The main character doesn't know what to do to get the monster to stop following her. It always stays a distance away and has not acted aggressively, yet. She hasn't slept well and she knows if she talks about it to anyone they either won't believe her or think she is crazy.

She turns to the internet to find some answers and begins to find clues and piece together what is happening to her by reading through accounts of "supernatural occurrences" and things people have discussed in chatrooms concerning weird experiences. She finally figures out what the thing is and what she accidentally did to attract it to her. But she hasn't figured out how to make it stop yet. She must work fast and think hard because the monster is unrelenting in its pursuit.
