Your smile’s appearance can have a significant impact on your self-image. Ugly dental stains, chipped teeth, and other cosmetic imperfections can leave you feeling socially award. Robbing you of your winning confidence. 

If you’re unhappy with your smile’s appearance, you might want to schedule a consultation appointment with a cosmetic dental specialist. They have the expertise to assess the nature and severity of the cosmetic imperfections to help you find your best options for a white, and winning smile. 

Often dental veneers are a preferred choice for people who have multiple cosmetic imperfections in their smile. They are made from composite resin or dental-grade porcelain and can help improve your smile’s appearance. This special material will replicate naturally white tooth enamel. Yet it will not be as receptive to further dental stains. 
There are several benefits that make dental veneers one of the most popular cosmetic dental restorations. 
They Can Cover Chips, & Cracks in Tooth Enamel
They Can Cover Up Surface Discoloration
They Look & Feel Just Like Natural Teeth
Very Little Tooth Enamel Is Removed
They Can Prevent Future Tooth Discoloration
Then Increase Confidence in Your Smile
Low Maintenance

The dental veneers can usually be installed in one or two short outpatient appointments. If one or two of your front teeth has been affected by a significant physical imperfection, or a large filling is present, your cosmetic dentist might recommend restoring it by installing a porcelain dental crown. This treatment method will completely replace the tooth’s enamel layer.
