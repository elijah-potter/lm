Everyone has a job to do during a fire drill! Teach child superheroes of every ability how to keep themselves safe in this fun and engaging mentor text.
Child-friendly illustrations and engaging text teach children important safety skills and coping techniques necessary to stay safe during a fire drill.
Written in an empowering, first-person social story format, three easy to remember 'jobs' are highlighted for every child superhero to accomplish. Perfect for children of all developmental abilities!
