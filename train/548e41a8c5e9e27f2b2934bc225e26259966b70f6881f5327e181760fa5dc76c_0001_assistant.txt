Sure! First, let's start with what a queue is: it's a data structure that follows a first-in, first-out principle (FIFO). The item that was pushed to the queue first is the highest-priority item. Think of it like a literal queue (or line), where customers are served in the order they lined up. The first person to get in the line is the first person to be served. A priority queue, on the other hand, also takes in a priority number, and orders its operations that way. Instead of being dependent upon where in the order an item was inserted, it orders its operations based on the priority numbers it's been given.
