Hello King Kandy,

How are you feeling this morning? I suppose it's afternoon now. I myself have just recovered enough from the evening's festivities to craft this little letter. I had a grand time, from what I remember at least. I trust you did as well. In fact, I do remember I owe you a fair bit of coin after I lost to you in billiards. Worry not, you marvelous scamp, that money is forthcoming. It was quite the party, wasn't it? Nearly everyone was present. Watching Gramma Nut scold Gloppy was quite a sight to behold. Plumpy and Jolly were maniacs on the dance floor while Mr. Mint DJ'd the music. Princess Lolly sure knows how to throw a sugar party. Actually, I ended up spending a great deal of time conversing with Queen Frostine. As it turns out, we have a fair bit in common. I wonder -- this is embarrassing for me to discuss such things, please forgive me -- I was wondering if you might be able to put in a good word for me. I was hoping to ask the noble Frostine out to an evening of fine dining and entertainment . . . as a couple, of course. Might you know what sort of food she enjoys? I would love any insight you may have to help me in this endeavor of love. 

Your Humble Servant,

Lord Licorice
