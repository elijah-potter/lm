The Leaning Tower of Pisa was originally constructed as a white marble bell tower in Italy. It was planned with eight stories. By the time the workers had finished the third, the foundations began to sink into the ground. Beneath the base of the structure was a mixture of sand, shells, and clay. Construction was delayed again for almost 100 years by the war between Pisa and Genoa. Attempts have been made to straighten the tower out over the years, but none were successful. The tower leans towards the south and sinks at a rate of about half an inch per year.
