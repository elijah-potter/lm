A muted hum of static filled the room as the stranger leaned forward, fingers tracing the rim of their cup. Their lips curled into a faint, unreadable smile, but their eyes stayed fixed on the empty screen before them. The air smelled of stale coffee and something faintly metallic—perhaps old cigarettes, maybe perfume from a long-ago date.

The speaker’s voice was low, a mix of frustration and something darker, like a confession meant to be forgotten. *"You’re not listening to me, are you?"* Their voice echoed off the walls, but the stranger’s gaze never shifted. They tilted their head just enough to make it look like they were engaging, their posture slightly stiff, fingers still moving the cup as if it were a lifeline.

The speaker paused, exhaled sharply. *"I don’t care if you’re pretending."* Then, softer: *"But you’re not."* The stranger’s smile didn’t waver, but their hands tightened around the cup. The screen flickered—an image of a hand hovering over a keyboard, fingers poised mid-tap, the cursor blinking like a dying star.
