A hollow, intricately carved wooden chest with a **sacrificial lock**—its hinges and latches require a blood sacrifice (animal or human) to open. The lid is sealed with a **cursed sigil** that glows faintly blue under moonlight, shifting colors when disturbed. Inside, a **scroll wrapped in iron filings** holds the secret, and the chest emits a low, rhythmic hum if left unopened for more than 24 hours. The wood is warm to the touch, and the scent of damp earth and burnt incense lingers. A **hidden compartment** beneath the scroll contains a **portable mirror** that reflects only fragments of the secret when viewed from the wrong angle.
