The usual approach to treating high blood pressure, or hypertension,
typically involves regular visits to the doctor's office to have blood
pressure checked and get prescriptions for anti-hypertensive
medications renewed. Increasingly, however, this way of treating high
blood pressure has been shown to have a number of limitations, ranging
from the reliability of that office-based blood pressure check to
compliance with medications. Can keeping track of blood pressure at
home lead to improved control for patients who suffer from high blood
pressure? This edition of Healthwatch takes a look at some recent
guidelines that address this question.
