AITA for refusing to let my sister use my vacation rental property?

Okay, this is a bit of a tangled web, and I’m honestly feeling drained trying to sort it all out. I'm 38, live in Denver, Colorado, and own a small vacation rental property in Moab, Utah. I purchased it in 2018 as a long-term investment and a place for my family to use. I manage it myself, and it consistently generates a decent income.

My younger sister, Clara (34), has always been…let’s just say, high-maintenance. She’s never held down a stable job for more than a year, frequently moves, and tends to expect a lot of support from our parents and me. She’s not inherently a bad person, just perpetually struggling.

Things started back in early March 2024. Clara called me, sounding frantic. She’s a freelance graphic designer, and a major client abruptly cancelled a project, leaving her without income until a new one materialized. She asked if she could stay at my Moab property for a few weeks, “just to regroup and get my head straight.” I initially said no. My rentals are booked solid, and I explained that allowing her to stay would impact my income. She got visibly upset, accusing me of being selfish and uncaring.

I felt a pang of guilt, but held firm. I offered to help her find affordable short-term rentals in her current location, Portland, Oregon, and even offered a small sum to help with the initial deposit. She accepted the deposit offer grudgingly, but the conversation ended on a frosty note.

Then, on April 2nd, my parents intervened. They called me, practically begging me to let Clara stay. They argued that she was genuinely struggling, that she had nowhere else to go, and that I had a responsibility to help my family. I reiterated my position: the property isn't free housing, and I need to protect my investment. They accused me of being cold and heartless, which stung.

I compromised. I offered to let her stay for *one* week, starting May 15th, provided she paid a significantly reduced rate—roughly 50% of my usual rental fee. I thought this was a fair compromise, allowing her some respite while still acknowledging my financial needs. She agreed, sending me a message filled with profuse gratitude.

Here’s where it gets really frustrating. On May 10th, Clara called again. She claimed the project she was working on fell through *again*. She now wants to extend her stay for another two weeks, insisting she needs the stability to secure new work. She also wants to bring her boyfriend, Liam, who she says is “a huge help” and will “contribute to chores.” I explained that extending her stay would require a new booking, and she’s already receiving a discounted rate. I also pointed out that I wasn’t comfortable with her bringing a guest without prior agreement, as it impacts utilities and potential wear and tear.

She exploded. She accused me of deliberately trying to sabotage her life, of being a joyless miser, and of prioritizing money over family. Liam joined the call, chiming in with supportive comments that felt more like accusations. They both implied I was being intentionally difficult to punish her. My mother called shortly after, furious that I hadn't "shown compassion."

I’m exhausted. I feel manipulated and pressured from all sides. I'm trying to be reasonable, but I'm constantly being made to feel guilty and selfish. I’m worried that if I continue to accommodate her, I'm enabling a pattern of dependency. I'm also concerned about the potential damage to my property and the disruption to my existing bookings if I cave to her demands. I feel like I’m being ganged up on. I’m starting to believe I’m the bad guy here, even though I'm just trying to protect my livelihood. My parents are leaning towards ESH, and Clara and Liam are convinced I’m a YTA.

AITA for sticking to my boundaries and refusing to let my sister extend her stay?
