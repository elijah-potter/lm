Here is a list of five Disney movies that might be appropriate to watch with a five-year-old:

1. "Moana" is a movie about a brave young girl who follows her dreams and saves her people.   
2. "Frozen" is about two princesses who are sisters. They set out to save their kingdom, and the movie sends a positive message about love and family.  
3. "Toy Story" is a funny and heartwarming movie about a group of toys that come to life when no one is around and their adventures together. It is a classic and sends an optimistic message about friendship and loyalty.  
4. "Finding Nemo" is about a father's mission to find his son, Nemo, who has been captured. The message in the movie is about courage, love, and perseverance.   
5. "Beauty and the Beast" is about Belle, a young woman who falls in love with an ugly beast. The beast is actually a handsome prince who was cursed, and they fall in love. It is sending a message about the importance of inner beauty v. outward appearances.
