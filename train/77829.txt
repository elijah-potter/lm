Introduction to Buddhism: An Explanation of the Buddhist Way of Life
Geshe Kelsang Gyatso
- ISBN: 9788178220659, 8178220652
- Year of Publication: 2002
- Binding: Paperback
- Edition: 1st
- No. of Pages: 146
- Language: English
This book is an exceptionally clear explanation of the Buddhist way of life and chiefly speaks about; (a) Who was Buddha?; (b) The nature of our mind; (c) Past and future lives; (d) Karma; (e) Why and how to meditate; (f) What is liberation?; and (g) The path to enlightenment. These and many other themes are illuminated in this compellling introduction to the life and teachings of Buddha. Those new to Buddhism and meditation will find this book an ideal guide to the Buddhist Way of life. This will further help in improving thier understanding and deepen their appreciation of Buddhism.
About the Author(s)
GESHE KELSANG GYATSO, a fully accomplished meditation master and internationally renowed teacher of Buddhism, is the author of a series of highly acclaimed books that transmit perfectly the ancient wisdom of Buddhism to our contemporary life. He has also founded many Buddhist Centres throughout the World.
