1.  The hippotamus has the biggest teeth of all land animals. Their back teeth can be up to 1.5 feet long and their front teeth can be up to 1.2 feet long.
2.   No, not all animals have teeth.  The largest mammal in the world, the Blue Whale, has no teeth! They only eat krill, which are tiny shrimp, so do not need teeth. In their place, they have baleen, which are bristle-like filters that catch the krill.  Some other animals without teeth are other whales, pangolins, and anteaters.
