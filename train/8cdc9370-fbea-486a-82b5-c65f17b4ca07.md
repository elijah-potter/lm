## Bookstore Serendipity (Seattle, WA)

I was browsing the poetry section at Elliott Bay Book Company last Saturday afternoon, utterly lost in the rhythm of Mary Oliver. You were across from me, carefully selecting a volume of essays, and the late afternoon light filtering through the large windows cast a warm, golden hue across your face. I noticed the intricate silver ring on your left hand, depicting a tiny, stylized owl. It caught my eye repeatedly as you turned the pages. The scent of old paper and brewing coffee permeated the air, a comforting and familiar embrace. We didn't speak, but I felt a fleeting sense of connection, a quiet acknowledgement of shared appreciation for the written word. I left shortly thereafter, and I’m now regretting my silence. Perhaps it was just a moment, a fleeting glimpse, but it lingers in my memory.
