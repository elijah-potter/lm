**The Alphabet of Forgotten Things**
1. The first whisper of autumn in the park
2. A half-eaten sandwich wrapped in a napkin
3. The hum of a broken elevator
4. A name scribbled on a napkin at midnight
5. The scent of old books in a forgotten attic
6. A key that doesn’t fit any lock
7. The last sentence of a book you couldn’t finish
8. A shadow moving when no one is there
9. The sound of rain on a tin roof
10. A promise made to someone who isn’t here

**The Things We Keep**
A box of letters, unopened, stacked in the corner.
A mirror cracked down the middle, reflecting only the past.
The way the light bends through a crack in the ceiling.
A voice in the dark that answers when you’re not speaking.
A piece of paper with a date in the margin—*Tomorrow*—but already yesterday.
The way your fingers remember how to write your name.
A door that isn’t locked, but no one comes out.
The taste of something sweet on your tongue that isn’t sugar.
A memory you’ve outgrown, still attached to the ribs of your soul.
The first time you saw the ocean and thought, *This is home.*
