Author: Amy Paul
Topics: Periodic Table
Printing: Full colour
Full colour poster components designed and printed on A4 sized paper which need to be cut out and pasted on hard card in order to create a do-it-yourself poster.
The Periodic Table poster includes all components required to construct a Periodic Table that is relevant to Units 1 to 4 Chemistry. This poster is created to allow students to learn the rough placement of the elements on the Periodic Table. Elements are split into colour coded groups, to enable easier reading.
