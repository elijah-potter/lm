A storm raged outside, thunder crashing like a betrayal. The map on the table, marked with red ink, was now blank—scratched out in furious strokes. Elias wiped the ink from his fingers, the taste bitter. His team had gathered in the dimly lit conference room, faces tense, voices low. The original plan—*Operation: Stormchaser*—was dead. The sensors had picked up the signal too soon, a false alarm, or worse, a distraction. The mission’s coordinates had been relayed prematurely, and now, the enemy knew. The coordinates they had used were the wrong ones, a decoy. The real target was still out there, hidden in the chaos. The group exchanged glances. "We need to regroup," said Kael, his voice steady despite the weight in his shoulders. "Re-evaluate." The radio crackled to life, a voice crackling over the static: *"This is Sector Delta. Stand down. Suspected hostile activity detected."* The air felt heavier, like the weight of a thousand unresolved questions. The plan was scrapped. Again.
