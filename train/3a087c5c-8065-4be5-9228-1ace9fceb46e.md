Lily had a tiny red ball that was her favorite. One sunny morning, she ran outside to play. "Look, Mommy!" she giggled. "My ball is rolling away!"

Lily chased her ball, laughing as it bounced over the grass. Suddenly, it stopped near a big tree. Lily peeked inside—oh no! The ball was hiding under the leaves.

"Come on, ball!" she called. With a little push, the ball rolled out and rolled right into a big, fluffy bunny named Benny who was napping.

Benny woke up and blinked his big eyes. "Oopsie!" he said. "I didn’t mean to catch your ball!"

Lily giggled. "It’s okay, Benny! I just wanted to play."

Benny smiled and handed her his favorite carrot. "Here, you can eat this!" he said.

Lily took the carrot and munched happily. "Thanks, Benny! Now we can play together!"

From that day on, Lily and Benny played together every day, rolling the ball, sharing snacks, and having the best adventures. They became the best friends in the whole world!
