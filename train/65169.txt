Book on inventors and inventions associations
The following book on inventors and inventions associations , inventor’s organizations, their contribution to the promotion of innovative culture, and their combination of cultural knowledge with innovative knowledge have been written by Dr. Farag Moussa, IFIA previous president and current honorary president.
IFIA Books comprise a part of IFIA publication aiming to broaden public understanding about innovation and expand creative thinking among the grassroots. The books include illustrations of the work of cartoonists and a collection of stamps portraying the male and female inventors.
Moreover, a series of books have been published in various languages shedding light on the contributions the women inventors have made to the advancement of technology across the globe.
