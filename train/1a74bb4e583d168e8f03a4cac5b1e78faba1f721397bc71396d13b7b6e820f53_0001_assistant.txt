SARASOTA, Fl - Authorities are searching for two unusual bank robbers in Sarasota this evening. The pair walked into the First Capital Bank on Union and Main at around 1 pm today. Witnesses say the robbers were ducks who asked the teller for small bills. It's unclear whether or not the ducks were armed. According to the Sarasota Police Department, one duck is described as a male Canvasback standing 20 inches tall. The other is a male tufted duck approximately 19 inches tall. The two ducks reportedly took flight before authorities could arrive at the scene. If you spot either duck, contact the authorities and do not approach the suspect.
