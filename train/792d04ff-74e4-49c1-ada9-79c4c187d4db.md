**BREAKING:** Mayor’s Office Announces New "Eco-Cycle" Initiative—Urban Renewal Ahead of Winter Storm
City of **Verdant Hollow** has launched the **"Eco-Cycle"** program, a pilot initiative aimed at reducing waste and carbon emissions by transforming alleyway litter into reusable urban furniture. The project, spearheaded by Mayor Elias Carter, will convert discarded plastic bottles, metal cans, and cardboard into benches, planters, and bike racks within the next 30 days. Community workshops will be held this weekend at the **Central Park Plaza** to engage residents in the process. "This isn’t just waste management—it’s a leap toward circular sustainability," Carter said, adding that funding comes from a $250,000 grant from the **Regional Green Council**. Officials warn that extreme winter weather could delay some construction, but volunteers are already sorting materials in the **North End recycling hub**. More details to follow on public safety protocols for the upcoming storm forecast.

---
**Technical Note:** The city’s new **"Smart Waste AI"** system, tested in Phase 1 last month, has shown 92% accuracy in sorting recyclables, but officials urge residents to double-check labels before depositing items in the new **"Eco-Cycle Carts"**—misplaced non-recyclables could stall the program.
