There once was a pond full of muck.
It made all of the animals say "Yuck, yuck, yuck!"
Until along came a duck.
They cried "You'll get stuck!"
But the duck only clucked.
He tried to swim, but no luck.
He was stuck!
He bucked and he tucked in that sticky muck.
A rope the animals did chuck.
And plucked that duck from the muck.
"Cluck," he said. "What luck!"
