**New Haven, October 15th – Officials confirm that the recent surge in reported "mysterious light phenomena" along Route 12 has prompted a full-scale investigation by the city’s Environmental Protection Division. Authorities are urging residents to document sightings with timestamps and location data via the newly launched "NeonWatch" app. Preliminary findings suggest the occurrences coincide with increased activity near the abandoned textile mill on Maple Street. City Council has declared a 72-hour curfew for the area, citing safety concerns, though no official explanation for the lights has been released. The mayor’s office has not commented on whether the phenomena are supernatural or a result of new solar technology. Residents are advised to avoid the affected zones after dark.**

*Sources: EPD field reports, local citizen reports, anonymous tip line submissions.*
