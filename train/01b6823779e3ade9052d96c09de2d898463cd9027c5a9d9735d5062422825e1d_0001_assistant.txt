Congratulations! Here are a few ways that you can announce that you're expecting a baby:

1. Photos - You can arrange a photo with some baby items and a due date and send it to your friends and family, or post it to your social media if you would like to announce it publicly. 

2. Videos - You can announce your pregnancy by using a recorded video of you sharing the news.

3. Gifts - You can make small gifts and ship them to your friends and family. You can pick items such as little booties or a custom baby onesie with a card that shares the good news.
