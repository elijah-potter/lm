The three little pigs is a story about three pigs who build three different houses: one was built of straw, one of sticks, and the third of bricks. A wolf came along wanting to eat the pigs. At the first two houses, he blew them down and the two pigs ran to the third pig's house. The wolf tried to blow down the brick house but couldn't so he climbed the roof and went down the chimney, but the pigs had put a pot of boiling water under the chimney, and he was burned. The pigs live happily ever after.

Alternate ending 1: The hungry wolf starts to cry after he fails to blow down the last house. The pigs feel sorry for him and invite him in for their vegetarian dinner. 

Alternate ending 2: The wolf looks down the chimney and sees the boiling water so he climbs off the roof, finds an open window on the way down, and eats all the pigs.
