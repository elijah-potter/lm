The door creaked open under the lightest touch of a knocker’s fingers, revealing a silhouette in the dim glow of the hall’s single bulb. Inside, a single candle flickered against the dark, casting long shadows that twisted like living things. The visitor stepped forward, their breath shallow, their coat damp against the chilly floorboards.

The voice inside was low, muffled by the thickness of the walls, but unmistakably human. *"You’re late."* The words were barely audible, swallowed by the silence. The other figure—standing motionless, arms crossed—didn’t move. The candle’s flame danced erratically, its reflection rippling in the hollows of their face as they stared at the intruder.

*"You always were the quietest,"* the visitor whispered, their gaze darting to the corner where something unseen waited. The air thickened, heavy with the scent of old paper and something metallic, like old blood. The door slammed shut behind them with a final, deliberate *thud*.
