A young plumber named Bob went for a jog near his home in a swampy bog. His run that day was quite a slog! He nearly tripped on a log and scared a frog, so he decided to head back home. 

Bob packed a simple lunch of hotdogs and a bag of chips. Then it was time for him to head to work and start on his backlog of clogs. Life as a plumber was unglamorous, but it gave him time to think of ideas for his blog about watches. He would catalog every clock cog if it took the rest of his life!
