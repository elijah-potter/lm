There are many types of Adenovirus, including gastroenteritis, pneumonia, and pinkeye. The symptoms depend on the part of the body affected and often mimic those of the common cold, such as fever and congestion. These contagious viruses can also cause diarrhea and vomiting. Adenoviruses can last from a few days to two weeks and are treated with rest, liquids, and acetaminophen if needed. Those who can't get enough fluids sometimes need treatment for dehydration.
