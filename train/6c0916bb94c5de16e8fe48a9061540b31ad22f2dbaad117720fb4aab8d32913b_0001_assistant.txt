I really don’t understand why no one thinks I exist! I mean, I know I don’t show myself to humans often, but every once in a while, someone sees me, and no one believes them. 

It’s not that I want to hang out with humans or, God forbid, be corralled and ridden like a silly horse, but I would appreciate some recognition! 

Maybe tomorrow, I’ll run by the farm a few miles away and hope the workers are out in the field.
