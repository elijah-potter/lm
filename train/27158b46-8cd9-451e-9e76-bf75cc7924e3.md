A shift in perception can alter reality itself. What once felt like a barrier might now seem an opportunity. The way we frame an obstacle—whether as limitation or potential—defines our experience. A single glance can reveal a world subtly reshaped by a new angle, revealing details lost in the old. Perspective isn’t static; it’s a dynamic lens that filters everything. Change in perspective isn’t about seeing differently, but about seeing clearly what was always there. Sometimes, the most profound truths are those we overlook until we look again.
