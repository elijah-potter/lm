The clock ticked monotonously, each second stretching like a river’s current—slow, deliberate, yet relentless. Hands on a wall clock turned at a pace of a thousand miles per hour, yet the minutes felt like hours, each one a silent question: *Why does time feel like this?* The air was thick with anticipation, the weight of unspoken words pressing down like a second skin. The silence was a physical entity, humming with the energy of unsaid thoughts. I watched the way the light flickered through the room, casting shifting shadows that seemed to linger longer than the light itself. Outside, the world moved on—unaware, indifferent—while this moment stretched, expanding, absorbing everything in its path.
