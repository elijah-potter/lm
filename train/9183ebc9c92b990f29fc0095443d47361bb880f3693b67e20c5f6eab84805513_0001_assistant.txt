

To look after a cat, you will need the following equipment:
- a comfortable bed for your cat to sleep on
- a litter tray or unlimited access to the outdoors
- a stimulating environment with toys and other cat products to keep the cat busy and occupied
- cat grooming equipment to look after your cat's fur, especially if it has quite a long fur

In addition to the above equipment, you will also need to be prepared to look after your cat's health. Your cat will need to:
- be wormed regularly
- receive regular treatment for fleas
- be vaccinated against major diseases on a regular basis
- be taken to the vet whenever it shows signs of illness
- ideally, be neutered before it turns six months old
