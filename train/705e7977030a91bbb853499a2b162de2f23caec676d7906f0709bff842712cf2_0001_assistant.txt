1.	Be Proactive: Take responsibility for your actions and choices. It's about seizing control of one's life instead of being a hapless victim of circumstance.

2.	Beginning with the end in mind: Have a clear vision of one's goals and aspirations, and do whatever it takes to bring them to fruition. Teens must discover their passions and work diligently towards realizing them.

3.	Prioritize: Try to prioritize tasks and activities based on their importance. Put first things first. Develop time-management skills and avoid procrastination at all costs.

4.	Think win-win: Collaborate for mutual benefit. Covey's philosophy promotes the concept of synergistic solutions that benefit everyone involved in a situation.

5.	Seek first to understand: Listening to others and understanding their perspective develops empathy and effective communication skills.

6.	Synergize to achieve common goals: Teamwork and collaboration can leverage each other's strengths and achieve success. IT could encourage teens to work together to achieve common goals and leverage each other's strengths.

7.	Sharpen the saw: Take care of oneself physically, mentally, emotionally, and spiritually. Developing self-care practices is fundamental to improving overall well-being and maintaining a healthy work-life balance.
