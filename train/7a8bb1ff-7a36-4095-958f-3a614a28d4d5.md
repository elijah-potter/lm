A tense board meeting in the corporate suite resulted in a decision that polarized the team. Leadership announced a restructuring of the remote work policy, mandating mandatory in-office presence for employees with over five years of tenure. Critics argued this was a demoralizing move targeting long-term employees, while supporters countered it was necessary to foster collaboration and innovation. Union representatives staged a protest outside, while executives defended the move as a cost-saving measure to improve productivity.

The backlash was immediate—internal surveys showed dissatisfaction across the board, with morale plummeting. Some quit, others threatened strikes, and a few defected to competitors. Meanwhile, the company’s stock dipped, and investors questioned the wisdom of the decision. The conflict spilled into social media, with #TeamLeft and #TeamRight clashing in online forums. The net result? A fractured workforce, a strained reputation, and no clear path forward.
