Two hikers paused at a rocky overlook, their boots crunching gravel underfoot.

**"Look at that!"** Mark pointed toward the valley below. **"The river’s swollen—flood’s coming! We need to turn back."**

Sarah leaned closer, studying the distant mist. **"Just mist. The sun’ll clear it by noon."**

A silence stretched between them, the kind that splits like stone.

Mark’s fingers tightened around his canteen. **"You’re wrong."**

Sarah’s voice was flat. **"I’m just… not seeing it like you do."**

Then the wind shifted—just a whisper—but enough to make their eyes meet, sharp with unspoken fear.
