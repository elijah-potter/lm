Air Fryer Baked Potato

1. Using vegetable oil or olive oil, cover the potato all over in the oil and then rub coarse sea salt over it. 
2. Place the potato inside your air fryer and preheat or set (according to your air fryer's instructions) the temperature to 400 degrees. 
3. Set the timer or allow the potato to cook in the air fryer for at least 1 hour. Check if it is cooked thoroughly and add 10 to 15 minutes if it isn't. 

Homemade Potato Chips 

1. Gather two to three large baking potatoes and peel them. 
2. Fill a frying pan halfway full of vegetable oil (or use a deep fryer) and start heating the oil. 
3. Slice the potatoes into thin layers. 
4. Fry in the hot oil until they are golden brown and season with sea salt.
