## Proposal: Reclaiming Heron’s Landing: A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary:**

The City of Oakhaven faces increasing challenges from climate change, including intensified storm events and rising temperatures. Heron’s Landing, a presently underutilized and degraded public space bordering the Willow Creek, exemplifies these vulnerabilities. This proposal outlines a comprehensive plan to transform Heron’s Landing into a climate-resilient community asset, integrating native riparian habitat restoration, improved stormwater management, accessible recreational amenities, and educational programming. This project will demonstrably enhance community resilience, improve water quality, increase biodiversity, and foster a stronger sense of place for Oakhaven residents. We seek $175,000 in municipal funding, matched by $75,000 in in-kind contributions and volunteer labor, to realize this vital community improvement. This investment represents a tangible commitment to the City’s sustainability goals and will serve as a replicable model for future public space revitalization efforts.

**Project Plan: Phases, Partners, and Deliverables**

The project is structured across four distinct phases, each with clearly defined timelines, responsible partners, and measurable deliverables.

**Phase 1: Assessment and Design (Months 1-3)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department, Willow Creek Watershed Association (WCWA), Local Landscape Architect (contracted).
*   **Activities:** Detailed site assessment including soil analysis, hydrological modeling, and ecological inventory. Community surveys and public forums to gather input on design preferences. Development of detailed design plans incorporating climate-resilient features (e.g., bioswales, native plantings, permeable paving).
*   **Deliverables:** Site Assessment Report, Community Engagement Summary, Final Design Plans (including detailed planting schedule and grading plan).

**Phase 2: Site Preparation and Infrastructure (Months 3-6)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** Oakhaven Public Works Department, WCWA, Volunteer Teams (coordinated by Friends of Willow Creek).
*   **Activities:** Removal of invasive species and debris. Grading and contouring to optimize stormwater flow. Installation of subsurface drainage infrastructure (e.g., French drains, bioswale liners).  Permeable paving installation for pathways.
*   **Deliverables:** Site cleared and graded according to design plans. Drainage infrastructure installed and inspected.  Permeable paving completed.

**Phase 3: Habitat Restoration and Planting (Months 6-9)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** WCWA, Local Nursery (contracted), Volunteer Teams (coordinated by Friends of Willow Creek).
*   **Activities:**  Installation of native riparian vegetation, including trees, shrubs, and herbaceous plants.  Erosion control measures implemented.  Mulching and initial watering of plantings.
*   **Deliverables:** Native plantings installed according to planting schedule. Erosion control measures in place.  Initial plant establishment ensured.

**Phase 4: Amenity Installation and Educational Signage (Months 9-12)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department, Local Artisan (contracted), Volunteer Teams (coordinated by Friends of Willow Creek).
*   **Activities:** Installation of accessible pathways and seating areas. Construction of a small, covered educational kiosk. Fabrication and installation of interpretive signage highlighting the ecological benefits of the restoration.
*   **Deliverables:** Accessible pathways and seating areas completed. Educational kiosk constructed and equipped. Interpretive signage installed. Project completion report.

**Budget Transparency**

The total project cost is estimated at $250,000. We are requesting $175,000 from the municipal fund, which will be leveraged with $75,000 in in-kind contributions and volunteer labor. A detailed breakdown is provided below:

*   **Professional Services (Landscape Architect, Hydrologist):** $45,000
*   **Materials (Native Plants, Permeable Paving, Drainage Liners, Mulch):** $75,000
*   **Equipment Rental (Grading Equipment, Excavators):** $15,000
*   **Construction Labor (Artisan for Kiosk, Path Construction):** $30,000
*   **Educational Signage & Kiosk Construction:** $15,000
*   **Volunteer Coordination & Supplies:** $5,000
*   **Contingency (10%):** $25,000

The in-kind contributions consist primarily of volunteer labor hours (estimated value: $40,000), donated nursery stock ($25,000), and the provision of heavy equipment from the Public Works Department.  The contingency fund is allocated to address unforeseen challenges, such as unexpected soil remediation or material cost fluctuations.  Detailed invoices and receipts will be meticulously tracked and made available for audit.

**Community Engagement, Equity Impacts, and Post-Grant Sustainability**

Throughout the project, community engagement will be prioritized. Public forums, online surveys, and targeted outreach to underserved neighborhoods bordering Heron’s Landing will ensure diverse perspectives are incorporated into the design and implementation. The project directly addresses equity concerns by creating a safe, accessible, and aesthetically pleasing public space for all residents, particularly those who currently lack access to quality recreational amenities.

Post-grant sustainability is paramount. The WCWA and Friends of Willow Creek will assume responsibility for ongoing maintenance and monitoring of the restored habitat. Educational programming, including guided nature walks and workshops on riparian ecology, will be offered regularly. A “Friends of Heron’s Landing” group will be established to foster community stewardship and ensure the long-term vitality of the space.  The Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department will incorporate Heron’s Landing into its regular maintenance schedule.

**Risk Mitigation**

Potential risks include adverse weather conditions delaying construction, unexpected environmental contamination requiring remediation, and fluctuations in material costs.  These risks will be mitigated through careful scheduling, thorough site assessments, and the inclusion of a contingency fund.  Alternative planting options will be identified to account for potential plant mortality due to drought or disease.  The project team will maintain open communication with all stakeholders to proactively address any challenges that arise.

**Call to Action**

Reclaiming Heron’s Landing represents a unique opportunity to enhance Oakhaven’s resilience, improve community well-being, and demonstrate a commitment to environmental stewardship. We urge the municipal fund to approve our request for $175,000, enabling us to transform this neglected space into a vibrant community asset. This investment will yield significant and lasting benefits for all Oakhaven residents.
