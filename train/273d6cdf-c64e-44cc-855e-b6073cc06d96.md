An unexpected doorbell rang mid-conversation. The speaker, a stranger, claimed urgent news about their missing grandchild—an elderly woman with gray hair and a wedding ring. The voice trembled with desperation.

"Please—help me find her. The police say she’s been missing for days," they whispered, clutching a crumpled photograph. The room spun; the coffee on the table had turned cold.

The stranger’s face paled as they slipped into the hallway, their fingers brushing against the doorknob. The house fell silent except for the distant hum of the refrigerator. The grandchild was never found.
