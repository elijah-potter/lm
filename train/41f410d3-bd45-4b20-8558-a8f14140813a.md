**"The Last Light"**

The neon hummed like a dying bee,
a ghost of blue that slipped through cracks.
Outside, the city breathed in slow gasps—
one streetlight held, though its will was weak.

He found it on a corner, abandoned,
rusted but still casting shadows deep.
The wind carried whispers from the past—
*"They’ll come back,"* the bulb seemed to say.

He turned it on. The light flared bright,
a lantern in a world turned black.
No one saw. No one knew.
Just the city’s endless, hollow glow.
