Once upon a time, the company enforced a rule: **"All documents must be scanned in high-resolution before printing."**

It was implemented under pressure—complaints about low-quality prints, wasted paper, and time wasted redoing work. The policy was swift: *Enforce it, then forget it.*

After a few months, no one checked the resolution anymore. The rule was no longer mentioned in meetings, not even in HR policies. Employees learned to work around it, assuming it was abandoned. The last time it was enforced was during a single audit, and the company moved on.

Now, it’s just another footnote in the company’s history—something that was once taken seriously, but quickly faded into the noise of bureaucracy.
