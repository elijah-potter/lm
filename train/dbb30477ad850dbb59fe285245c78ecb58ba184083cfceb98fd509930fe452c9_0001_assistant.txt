Start the morning with a small protein shake of about 20g protein. 

For breakfast, have shakshuka with two eggs (12g protein), chickpeas (1 cup, 39g protein), and some Greek yogurt (1 cup, 9g protein). That should reach 50g+ protein. 

As lunch comes around, eat chicken fried rice (1 cup rice, 7g protein) with 150g chicken breast (43g protein). That alone will cover 50g+ protein. 

Enjoy two beef burritos (27g protein) with a side of refried beans (1 cup, 13g protein) as an early dinner with 40g+ protein. 

Finally, have another small protein shake a couple of hours before bed. All in all, this diet will be enough for someone looking to consume more than 180g of protein per day.
