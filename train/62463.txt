Gradually change your diet if you want to be successful with it. Change can't be forced overnight. Trying to eat too many unfamiliar foods will make it harder on yourself. Over the next month, add in some healthier alternatives to your old menu, and then you have a habit.
When you are pregnant or breastfeeding you should stay healthy. Pregnant women need a lot of protein, and getting it down due to lack of appetite can be a pain. One way to add extra protein into the diet is to include more egg white in a meal. Eggs provide excellent sources of protein for your body with few bad organic components. For safety's sake, the egg whites should be pasteurized.
Oatmeal is an excellent choice as a breakfast food. When you eat oatmeal, it helps fill your stomach up so you'll be full for a longer time.
Processed grains typically taste much better than whole grains do. In some baked goods, white flour is the better option. However, whole grains usually taste better than grains that are over-milled. They also have digestive-aiding fibers.
Instead of gorging yourself during the holiday months, eat a healthy snack or small meal before sitting down to a huge family dinner. Going to dinner with an empty belly can lead you to overeating. A light, healthy meal (or even just a healthy snack) will prepare you well for Thanksgiving and reduce the temptation to gorge yourself.
Avoid microwavable foods. Any pre-packaged food that only needs to be microwaved is full of preservatives that prevent you from losing weight.

For a great meal that your family will have fun with, cook up kabobs on your grill or with a broiler. Kids can pick what meat or vegetables to put on their kabobs. Use bright colors to increase visual attraction.
Understanding salad is a good way to improve the diet you have. Salads are more than just lettuce with ranch dressing. There are many things that can make a great salad. Play with it!
