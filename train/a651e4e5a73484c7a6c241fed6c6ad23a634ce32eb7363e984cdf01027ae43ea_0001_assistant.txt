Dogs can experience heatstroke during high temperatures and heat waves outside. Unlike humans, dogs can not sweat outside their skin so they release heat through their paws and nose. Some ways you can keep your animal cool are by making sure they have accessible water, keeping them inside on hot days and watching for signs of overheating like panting and loss of energy.

On a hot day, it is never okay to leave your dog inside the car as temperatures quickly rise. It is best to keep your animals inside during heat waves and best to walk them in the early morning or nighttime.
