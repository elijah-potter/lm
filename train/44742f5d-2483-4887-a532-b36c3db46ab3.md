Five minutes until the last transmission from Mars-9. The console flickered, static hissing between the lines. Engineer Elias adjusted his headset, fingers hovering over the keypad.

**3:24:01** - *Contact established with Epsilon-7.*

**3:24:00** - *Transmit failed. Power surge detected.*

**3:23:59** - *Critical system failure. Backing up data.*

---
The countdown rolled down to:

**00:00:00** - *All systems nominal.*

Then, a single static burst cut through the silence.

**12:59:59** - *Transmission terminated. Stand by for final data dump.*

The last message appeared on screen:

*"Epsilon-7 is offline. Mars-9 acknowledges receipt. Do not proceed with activation."*

The airlock doors hissed shut. The countdown timer on the console ticked backward:

**00:00:01** - *Reinitializing emergency protocols.*

**00:00:00** - *All protocols engaged. Hold position.*

Elias exhaled, the weight of the mission pressing down on his chest. The last transmission was gone. The countdown was over.
