**BREAKING:** The historic **Veldros River Bridge** in the industrial district of **Nova Haven** collapsed under unexpected seismic activity early Tuesday morning. Authorities report no immediate injuries but confirm the bridge was structurally compromised due to an unrecorded magnitude 3.1 tremor, likely triggered by shifting underground geothermal activity near the **Blackthorn Ridge Fault**. Residents near the collapse reported sudden vibrations followed by a loud cracking sound. Emergency crews are assessing the situation; the city’s transit system has rerouted vehicles via the elevated **Eastbound Skywalk** for the moment.

**Local businesses** in the affected area—including the **Veldros Market** and **Haven’s Forge Distillery**—have temporarily closed. The city’s Transportation Department is investigating whether the bridge’s 2023 structural audit flagged risks. A spokesperson noted, *“This isn’t the first incident near the fault, but the timing was unusual.”* Meanwhile, the **Nova Haven Police Department** is urging residents to avoid the area and report any further tremors via the city’s **Safety Alert app**.

The **Nova Haven Energy Grid** confirmed no outages, though a minor voltage fluctuation was detected near the fault’s epicenter. Officials are coordinating with neighboring cities, including **Sylvan Heights**, which borders the fault zone. The city’s **Seismic Response Team** has activated protocols, but no major evacuations have been ordered yet.
