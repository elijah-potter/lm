Well, readers, today was the day. I had to go see the dentist to have my wisdom teeth taken out. I was scared and debated not going, but decided whatever may happen there couldn't be nearly as bad as still having them in my mouth. I woke up and had breakfast. It was the last solid food I'll eat for the next week :(. I got to my appointment fifteen minutes early and sat there waiting to be called back. When my name was finally called, I pushed down my fear and headed into the dentist's exam room. 

I knew already that I'd have to be awake for it. I didn't know what to expect. The dentist's assistant put bite blocks in my mouth to hold it open. Then came the shots! They hurt so bad. I got several in the roof of my mouth and even more everywhere else. After I was numb, the dentist came in. I don't know what tools he used but it felt like he was prying at my teeth. I couldn't feel it, thank goodness, but I could hear the sounds of my teeth being ripped from my jaw. It sounded like a crunch, then a grind, and then a pop. It was over pretty quickly. I was surprised that all of them were removed when he said he was done. Part of me thought maybe he hadn't been able to get them all, but I could feel the holes where they had been with my tongue. 

I'm not in too much pain now thanks to the medicine they gave me. I have to eat soft food for a while, so lots of ice cream and mashed potatoes for me!
