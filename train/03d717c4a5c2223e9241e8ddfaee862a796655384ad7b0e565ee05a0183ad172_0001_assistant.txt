Did you know red pandas aren't really pandas? They're actually closer to raccoons! They are adorable and live in the mountains.

The red panda is adorable! Red pandas are about two feet tall. They weigh between 8 and 17 pounds. They have red fur and they have tails with white rings on them and pointy red ears. Their faces are white. They have paws for feet. 

Red pandas live in mountain areas and mostly in the Himalayas. The weather is temperate and rainy. They mostly stick to the trees in their habitats. Red pandas are vegetarian carnivores. They mostly eat bamboo leaves from the trees that they live in. They also dig up roots. They may also decide to eat small eggs or small mammals. 

Red pandas live for a long time. Red pandas are mammals so they have live births. Red pandas have 1-4 cubs after being pregnant for 135 days.  They become adolescents around 6 months old. After that they become adults. They live for 9-13 years.
