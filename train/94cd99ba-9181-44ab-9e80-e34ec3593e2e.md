A bustling village square where neighbors greet each other by name upon passing. Street vendors share gossip over morning coffee, while elders debate local politics at the communal well. Children play hide-and-seek in alleys lined with handmade murals depicting village legends. The scent of wood-fired bread mixes with the sound of harmonious laughter, and every face—even strangers—carries a shared, unspoken understanding of the community’s history and unspoken rules. The air hums with the quiet certainty that trust is earned through proximity and time.
