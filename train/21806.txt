Stainless steel coil not only has strong corrosion resistance, but also has a relatively light weight, so it has become the material of choice in the automotive manufacturing industry.
The food industry is also one of the most popular areas where stainless steel coils are used. Our stainless steel coils meet food-grade safety standards and do not cause any harm to humans. In the food and packaging machinery industry, it is generally preferable to use stainless steel with positive deviations to facilitate mechanical processing.
In architectural decoration, at present, stainless steel coils are mainly used in the cladding of exterior walls, interior and exterior columns of high-rise buildings, handrails, floors, lift siding, doors, windows, curtain walls and other internal and external decoration and components. After the surface treatment, colouring, plating of stainless steel coil plate plate, solve the touch easy to appear after the handprint and other problems, so that the application of stainless steel coil to further expand the scope.
In addition to the scenarios mentioned above, stainless steel coils can also be used in various sectors such as petrochemical, chemical and manufacturing industries. stainless steel coils have excellent physical and chemical properties, are not only environmentally friendly but also aesthetically pleasing, and it can be considered an important material for modern industry.
There are many different types of stainless steel coils. For example, 201 stainless steel strip, 202 stainless steel strip, 316 stainless steel strip, 316L stainless steel strip, 304 stainless steel coil strip and so on. The different types of stainless steel coils have different structural characteristics and prices. In general, these stainless steel products are harmless to humans, they have high corrosion resistance, high tensile strength and high fatigue strength.
Stainless steel rusts for a number of reasons, for example, it may be that its surface is stored with dust containing other metal elements, so that in a humid environment for a long time, the attachment and the condensation of stainless steel will connect the two into a microcell, which leads to electrochemical corrosion. In addition, stainless steel surfaces with organic juices or placed in polluted air are susceptible to corrosion and rusting.
