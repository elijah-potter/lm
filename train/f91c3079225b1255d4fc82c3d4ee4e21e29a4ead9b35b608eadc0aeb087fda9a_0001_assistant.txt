Here's how to store your iced tea:

• Refrigerate your tea in one-quart mason jars because they're attractive, easy to use, and tolerate hot water.
• Avoid leaving the tea bags in the water for too long. If they steep for too long, the tea bags will make the tea taste bitter.
• Remove the tea bags after steeping, but keep any fresh fruit in the jars.
• Keep the herbs in the water, but remove them after a few hours before they start to deteriorate.
