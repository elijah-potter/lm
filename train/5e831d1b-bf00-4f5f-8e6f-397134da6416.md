Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big yellow door, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy pink paws and a tail that wiggled when he was happy.

Every morning, Benny would hop out of his snug burrow and see his friends—Lily the cat, who loved to play with a red ball, and Max the dog, who barked at the birds in the sky. Benny would giggle and say, "Hello, friends!"

One sunny afternoon, Benny noticed something shiny near the garden fence. It was a tiny golden ball that sparkled like the sun. "Maybe it’s a treasure!" Benny thought.

He carefully picked it up and brought it home. His mum said, "Oh, Benny! That’s a new toy for your friends!" So Benny gave it to Lily, who chewed it just a little, and to Max, who rolled it around and around.

That night, as Benny snuggled in his bed, he felt warm and happy. He knew his friends were safe, and he was the luckiest bunny in the whole world because he had the best friends ever.

And from that day on, Benny always remembered to share his treasures with his friends—just like he shared his happiness.
