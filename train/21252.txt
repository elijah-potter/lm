Economics: Principles of Economics, 1e
Decision Point activities--available in Macmillan's online learning platform for Stevenson/Wolfers—allow students to explore their own decision-making process and how economic principles can inform their decisions. Students work step by step through decision-making scenarios, receiving feedback about how economic principles played into their choices, and helping them apply economic insights to their everyday lives.
