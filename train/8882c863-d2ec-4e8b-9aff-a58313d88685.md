Lost in a shifting maze of endless roads, each leading nowhere new.
The city’s skyline dissolves into a pixelated dream, its edges bleeding into mist.
A compass points north, but the north you seek has already vanished—replaced by a whisper of "south" on the wind.
The first destination was Paris; now it’s a labyrinth of neon-lit alleys where the time between landmarks stretches like taffy.
Your map is a live feed of coordinates that keep recalculating mid-journey, erasing old streets with each update.
The only constant is the feeling of being both *there* and *not there*—stuck in a loop where the past and future collide like fractured glass.
A sign appears: *"You are not lost. You are the map."* But the map has already rewritten itself.
