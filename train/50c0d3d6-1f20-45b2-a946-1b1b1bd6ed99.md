The flickering neon glow of a dimly lit alleyway cast long shadows across wet pavement. A hand trembles as it brushes against the cold glass of a half-empty café window, fingers tracing the condensation like a map. The air hums with the distant wail of a siren, the only sound beside the whisper of footsteps on pavement.

She kneels, pressing her forehead against a cracked sidewalk tile, the scent of damp earth and old metal clinging to her clothes. A child’s laughter echoes from a park across the street—too far, yet too close. The wind shifts, carrying fragments of a conversation from a parked car: *"You’re not real… but you’re here."*

She pulls out the worn journal, its pages yellowed with age. The last entry reads: *"I keep searching for what I can’t name."* A single line, written in hasty script. The rest is blank, except for the faint smudge of something darker beneath the ink. She flips through it again, her breath shallow. The words blur into static.
