Passageways:
1. **Highway Overpasses** – Elevated roadways connecting distant towns; vehicles traverse them briefly before proceeding.
2. **Airport Runways/Taxiways** – Transient paths for aircraft; planes use them for takeoff/landing but do not park.
3. **Bridge Decks** – Structures allowing passage across gaps; pedestrians/bikes cross but do not remain.
4. **Tunnel Portals** – Entrance/exit corridors for vehicles or trains; users enter briefly before exiting.
5. **Conveyor Belts** – Moving walkways or cargo paths; people move along them but do not settle.
6. **Port Terminals (Cargo Gates)** – Ships dock briefly; containers are loaded/unloaded but no lingering.
7. **Corridor in Hospitals/Offices** – Short-term transit routes; workers pass through without stopping.
8. **Underground Subway Platforms** – Temporary stops for trains; passengers board and disembark.
9. **Train Switch Yards** – Tracks where trains reroute; vehicles move through for alignment adjustments.
10. **Cable Car Loops** – Circular paths where vehicles return to pick-up points; riders disembark immediately.
