Manipulating different types of materials and creating attractively curved cuts or razor-sharp straight cuts isn’t that difficult these days, and to accomplish that you need to look for the best band saws.
There are numerous types of power cutting tools available on the market such as jigsaws, miter saws, and others.
In this article, we are going to talk about band saws which are very easy to use and specifically designed for those projects where a lot of curved cuts are required. Similarly to jigsaws, band saws can also be corded or cordless and they have numerous interesting features.
Why are they called bandsaws?
Well, they usually tend to rotate a thin band of toothed metal between two wheels and this process actually creates the required cutting force to shape up various materials, including steel.
Although it might sound complicated, operating the band saw can be done very easily and you have complete control over the cutting depth, width, and so on. You can also change the blades if you require more complex cuts.
Keep reading this article to learn more about band saws and how to purchase the right one for your needs.
How to Choose a Band Saw
Corded or cordless
Both these power tools are electric, just that their design is slightly different. Some of them are larger and work by plugging them into a power outlet. They come with a motor which has a specific amperage (around 3.5 amps or higher) and they are usually fixed into the ground, so you basically bring the materials which need to be cut to them.
On the other hand, cordless bandsaws are powered by lithium-ion batteries, they are much smaller, portable, and you can take them to the materials which need to be cut. All band saws can be used to create curved shapes, odd or irregular shapes or even transform lumber into thinner pieces.
Band saws are also ideal for aluminum, plastic, metal and many other types of materials. Here’s a guide on how to use one.

As mentioned earlier, corded bandsaws have a motor with a specific number of amps while cordless ones are characterized by an 18-20V motor.
The motor can have several speeds and you can select them by pressing a button on the main control panel of the unit.
For example, some models can operate at speeds around 1,500 rpm while others can go up to 2,500 rpm or more.
Corded bandsaws come with a special type of table where you basically put the timber (or other material) and adjust it while you make the cut.
This table can have a specific size such as 14 by 15 inches, etc. For more sophisticated models, you can also incline the work table to a certain degree to create more interesting and attractive cuts.
The work surface because very important if you like to cut large pieces of metal or wood, so keep that in mind when buying. Check out this guide for more info.
The manufacturer usually provides the blade in the package when shipping the band saw, but you can also buy additional ones if you want it.
The blade usually has a length of around 60-80 inches and it is almost half an inch in size. Depending on the types of cuts you want to make, you might want to experiment with different types of blades and see what results you get.
These blades also have teeth which facilitate the cutting process. Here’s a video explaining how to choose a band saw blade:
Most of the blade is installed into the frame of the machine, so you cannot actually see it. However, a small portion of the blade is visible because it is required to make the cut.
The cut size can vary from several inches in depth up to 10 or more inches as well as up to 14 inches in width. You can increase or decrease the height of the blade to accommodate the size of the material you want to cut.
If you work with really large pieces, you might want to go for a model around 15 inches in width which gives you ample space for maneuver. You can get more info from this detailed guide.
This aspect is also important as it refers to various accessories or features which will make your cutting process easier and quicker.
For example, some corded band saws are equipped with a work light. Similarly, cordless versions might come with 2 batteries in the package which basically allows you to use the band saw with one battery while the other battery is charging.
Here I want to mention the warranty policy as well which is extremely important for this type of power tools.
Most manufacturers offer around 1 or 2 years on parts and labor while others are more generous and can provide free repairs or component replacements for up to 5 years if your unit has arrived with defective parts.
