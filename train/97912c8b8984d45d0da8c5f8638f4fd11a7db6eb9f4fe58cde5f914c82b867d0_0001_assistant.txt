With a wave of a wand, here's your list of Harry Potter spells in alphabetical order!


1. ACCIO - summons an object closer to you
2. ALOHOMORA - unlocks doors
3. AVADA KEDAVRA - the unforgivable killing curse with only one known case of surviving it
4. CONFUNDO - a handy spell that confuses people or bewitches an object
5. CRUCIO - an unforgivable curse that causes extreme pain
6. EXPECTO PATRONUM - a powerful defensive spell that fights Dementors with happy memories
7. EXPELLIARMUS - a spell that disarms your opponent of their wand
8. LUMOS - a charm that turns your wand tip into a flashlight
9. OBLIVIATE - a memory-erasing spell
10. PETRIFICUS TOTALUS - a full-body-binding curse that causes temporary paralysis
11. RIDDIKULUS - turns a scary boggart into something silly
12. REPARO - repairs broken objects
13. SECTUMSEMPRA - a dangerous curse that slices its victim with deep cuts that may never heal
14. WINGARDIUM LEVIOSA - a levitation charm that causes objects to float
