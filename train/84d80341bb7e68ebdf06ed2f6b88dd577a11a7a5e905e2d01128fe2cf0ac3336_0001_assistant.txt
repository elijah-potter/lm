Finding out if you have ADHD means a doctor has to go through several steps. There's no one test that can tell us if you have ADHD, and some other things look the same as ADHD—like sleep disorders!

If you think you might have ADHD, then you should ask your parents to take you to see a doctor. They're the only ones that are allowed to tell you if you have ADHD.

You can ask your parents what they think too.  The doctor might also want to speak to your parents or even your school teacher, so he or she can get to know a bit more about you.

Your doctor will also be able to tell you if something else is happening that just looks like ADHD.
