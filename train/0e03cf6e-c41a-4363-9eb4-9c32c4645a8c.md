**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 14, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, offering a celebration of agriculture, community, and family entertainment. This year’s fair promises a diverse range of attractions, from livestock exhibits and home and garden displays to thrilling carnival rides and live musical performances. Visitors can anticipate demonstrations of traditional crafts, opportunities to engage with local farmers and artisans, and a vibrant atmosphere for all ages.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 22nd through August 28th, 2024, at the County Fairgrounds in Springfield. Gates will open at 9:00 AM daily, with evening events extending until 10:00 PM. A dedicated children’s area will feature interactive games and activities, while food vendors will offer a wide selection of classic fair fare and regional specialties. The event aims to provide a memorable experience for residents and visitors alike.

Admission to the Sangamon County Fair is $12 for adults, $8 for children ages 5-12, and free for children under 5. Carnival ride tickets and wristbands will be available for purchase separately. A full schedule of events, including details on parking and accessibility, can be found at www.sangamoncountyfair.org.

**Headline Events:**

*   Grand Champion Livestock Auction
*   Demolition Derby
*   Professional Rodeo Series
*   Blue Ribbon Baking Competition
*   Evening Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters
*   Firework Spectacular (August 28th)
