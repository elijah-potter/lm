At a corporate retreat, the CEO’s speech about "synergy" and "shared vision" went unnoticed by the junior team member, Mark, who was focused on his laptop. He had been secretly working on a side project, a decentralized blockchain database, to bypass the company’s rigid IT policies. During the retreat, Mark subtly exchanged a few coded messages with his contacts in the IT department, who had been secretly investigating the same idea.

Later, after the retreat, Mark’s manager noticed his unusual interest in blockchain tech and questioned him. Mark, confused, denied knowing anything about it, fearing retaliation. That night, he accidentally sent an encrypted message to a contact with the code *"truth reveals itself"*—a phrase he had overheard in a tech discussion.

The next day, during a routine audit, the IT team intercepted the message and traced it to Mark’s laptop. The CEO, overhearing about the investigation, was furious. The truth came out: Mark had been secretly working on a solution to the company’s inefficiencies, and the CEO’s "synergy" talk had been a cover for his own self-serving agenda. The IT department, betrayed by their own leadership, revealed the project to the board.

The company’s leadership collapsed. The CEO resigned, the IT team was fired for leaking the truth, and Mark was promoted to lead the new decentralized IT division—though he kept his side project as a personal mission. The misunderstanding had been the catalyst that exposed the corruption, forcing systemic change. The blockchain database became the foundation of the company’s new, open-source IT infrastructure.
