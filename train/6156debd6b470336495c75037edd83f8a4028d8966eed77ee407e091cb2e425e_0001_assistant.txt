Localization microscopy is a new technique that helps us understand how cells work at a microscopic level. It gives us accurate information about where molecules are in biological structures. Using this technique, we can even count the number of proteins in a cell.
It takes very detailed pictures and can be used with other methods to learn more about cells. Scientists can see how molecules interact by using localization microscopy.
The technique works by using special molecules called fluorophores to see things that are too small for regular light. These molecules change their behavior and help us study the structures we're interested in.
