The clockwork gears of the **Chronos Tower** hum with a distorted rhythm—each tick and toll echoes at half-speed, yet the seconds blur into minutes as if viewed through liquid. The air hums with a low, metallic static; time itself seems to ripple like sound waves. Stairs spiral counterclockwise, defying gravity, while shadows stretch unnaturally long or vanish mid-step. A clock on the wall reads **23:59:59** and then resets to **00:00:00** without passing midnight, as if the hands loop endlessly in a slow, looping cycle. Some say the tower’s walls pulse like a heartbeat, aging and unaging at once. At its core, a **fractal vortex** distorts reality—objects dissolve into echoes, and voices repeat in loops, each iteration subtly altered. The only constant? The weight of every second feels heavier than it should, yet moments blur into eternity. The exit is a mirror; reflections move backward. Do not linger—some say the tower only lets you leave when you forget the day of the week.
