Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white fur and big, round ears that flopped when he was excited.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and said, "Wow, what a beautiful day! The sky is blue, and the sun is shining!" He hopped outside to play. Benny saw a tiny purple flower that sparkled in the sunshine.

Benny gently picked the flower and said, "Hello, little friend!" The flower smiled and waved its petals. Benny put the flower in his basket and felt happy.

Nearby, a little bird named Benny-Bird sat on a branch. Benny-Bird chirped, "Hello, Benny! Want to play a game?" Benny hopped over and they built a nest with sticks and leaves together. Benny-Bird added twigs for Benny's basket.

They both had so much fun. Benny-Bird taught Benny how to spin in circles, and Benny showed Benny-Bird how to roll a pebble downhill. They giggled and giggled until their bellies ached.

After a while, Benny said, "Time to go inside, Benny-Bird. I have a snack waiting." Benny-Bird flapped his wings and said, "Goodbye, Benny! Love you!" They waved goodbye and Benny hopped back inside.

Inside, Benny ate his favorite apple and drank milk. Benny looked out the window and saw Benny-Bird flying away. Benny smiled and thought, "Today was the best day ever!"

And that's how Benny and Benny-Bird made friends in their sunny little house.
