The vast, hollow cavern hummed with residual vibrations from a distant asteroid’s impact, its jagged walls etched with ancient glyphs that pulsed faintly under dim, drifting starlight. Air currents whispered through the open fissure where the ceiling had long since collapsed, carrying the scent of ozone and something metallic—rust from the abandoned machinery buried beneath centuries of dust. Every surface—crushed rock, corroded metal, and the skeletal remains of a broken vessel—echoed with the ghost of countless footsteps, tools, and the occasional cry of exhaustion. The silence wasn’t absence; it was the weight of all that had been *here* before, now just a hollow memory. The emptiness was the pressure of being *filled with history*.
