A shadow lingers where the light once touched,
a hollow where the voice once sang.
Every step echoes with what was left behind—
not ghosts, but the weight of absence:
a chair too often unoccupied,
a mirror that reflects no one’s name.
Time slows, as if the days themselves
had been measured in moments lost.

The door stands ajar, yet no one walks out.
The hands that once held the world
now clutch the edge of the void,
where the past dissolves into static.
Some things are not lost—they’re *unseen*.
The absence is a presence,
a silence that hums with the absence of sound.
And the heart? It’s still learning
how to breathe when the breath is stolen.
