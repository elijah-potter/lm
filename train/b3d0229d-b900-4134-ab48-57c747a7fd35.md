The rain fell in sheets, turning the cracked pavement into a thousand silent mirrors. Inside the old library, the scent of aged books clung to the air like a ghost’s whisper. Clara sat at her desk, fingers tracing the edge of an open notebook where she’d jotted down the names of the missing—each one a puzzle, each one a ghost.

Her phone buzzed. The screen lit up with the name: *Daniel*.

She exhaled slowly. The door creaked open, and in stepped a man in a raincoat, his coat damp at the hem, his eyes sharp with something she couldn’t name. "You’re here," he said, voice low. "They’re waiting for you."

The library doors groaned shut behind him. Outside, the storm raged on.
