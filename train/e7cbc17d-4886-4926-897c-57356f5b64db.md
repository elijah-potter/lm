A man, mid-40s with a perpetually flushed complexion, clutched his briefcase tighter as he stepped onto the elevator, his mouth forming the words *"Excuse me, sir"* before his brain caught up. The elevator dinged, and he was already halfway inside, his hand lingering on the door handle, fingers twitching as if resisting the urge to slam it shut.

*"Oh, I’m so sorry—I didn’t mean to step on your foot!"* he stammered, his voice cracking slightly as he glanced down at his unmarked sneakers. The other passenger—a woman in a tailored blazer—smiled politely, her eyes flicking to his face before returning to her phone. *"No worries,"* she said, voice neutral, before tapping away again.

Back at the office, his colleague, a woman with a permanent five o’clock stare, caught him mid-greeting. *"Good morning,"* he muttered, his lips forming the words too late. She arched a brow. *"Uh, yeah. How are you?"* he added, realizing he’d forgotten to ask her first. Her reply was a curt *"Fine"* and a nod. The meeting began, his awkwardness growing with every raised hand.

By the end of the day, he’d said *"please"* and *"thank you"* three times in the wrong places. His boss, a man who valued efficiency over manners, just sighed. *"You’re fine,"* he said. *"Just don’t let it bother you."* But it did. Every polite word felt like a misfire.
