Sure! Here are some fun facts that will get a child interested in learning about Ancient Greece:

1. Cheesecake was invented by ancient Greeks and was made with flour, water, honey, and cheese. 
2. None of the Greek statues you've seen were originally white - they were painted in vibrant colors that have since faded over time.
3. The Olympics as we know it today is a modern version of the Olympic games that were held in ancient Greece. 
4. The Greek gods and goddesses have been featured in everything from movies to books to webcomics.
