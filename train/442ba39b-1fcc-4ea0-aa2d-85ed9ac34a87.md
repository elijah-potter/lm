A bustling village square with stone-paved pathways, where every person—from the elderly seated on benches to the children playing in the dirt—turns to greet you upon sight. Market stalls line the edges, selling fresh bread, spiced nuts, and woven textiles. A central fountain hums with water, and elders gather around it to share gossip. Neighbors pass by with shared smiles, nodding to those they’ve known since birth. The scent of roasting meats and baking bread lingers, mingling with the earthy smell of aged wood. A few dogs bark at strangers, but they know who belongs. The air is thick with the sound of laughter, laughter that’s been sung for generations.
