‘Illustrated Bible Poems’ is a poetic children’s book that brilliantly recognizes the Afro-Asiatic context of the Biblical Story. This hardback contains 34 Bible stories set to rhyme. And each story is accompanied by a full-page watercolor illustration. Images play a major role in the development of a child’s faith and worldview, especially around the topic of race. ‘Illustrated Bible Poems’ recognizes the culture and ethnicity of the biblical world and helps us accomplish the much need work of ‘Changing the Visual Landscape.’
