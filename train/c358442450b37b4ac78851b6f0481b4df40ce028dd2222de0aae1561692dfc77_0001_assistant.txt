Getting a stronger chest is an admirable goal! 3 good chest exercises you can focus on in the gym include:
1. Flat barbell bench press - This exercise will help target your chest and build muscle.
2. Chest flies - This exercise will focus on increasing the width of your chest, and making sure you can build muscles in the outside area.
3. Incline barbell bench press - This exercise will build your upper chest, making sure you have a rounded chest in order to maximize your muscle growth.
