The Industrial Revolution, which began in the late 18th century and accelerated throughout the 19th century, was a period of unprecedented change that fundamentally transformed societies in numerous ways. Its effects were so profound that they laid the foundations for the modern world as we know it.

At the heart of the Industrial Revolution was a series of technological innovations that led to dramatic increases in manufacturing productivity. Key developments included the invention of the steam engine, the spinning jenny, the power loom, and the process of making cheap steel. These inventions revolutionized industries such as textiles, transportation, and manufacturing, leading to the shift from agrarian and handcraft economies to ones dominated by industry and machine manufacturing.
