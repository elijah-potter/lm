Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big garden, lived a little bunny named Pip. Pip had fluffy orange fur and big round ears that bounced when he hopped.

One sunny morning, Pip noticed something strange. The garden was covered in tiny, shimmering stars. He hopped outside and saw them sparkle in the grass.

Pip’s little paws gently touched one star. It glowed warmly and whispered, *"Find your friends!"* Pip felt happy and curious.

He hopped to the pond and asked the water, *"Where do my friends live?"* The pond rippled and showed him a tiny fish named Finn. *"They live in the sky!"* Finn said, wiggling his fins.

Pip decided to fly! He stretched his legs and took a big hop. His fluffy tail wiggled, and soon, he was soaring above the garden. He saw his friends: a butterfly with bright blue wings, a squirrel collecting acorns, and a little bird singing a song.

Pip landed softly, and his friends cheered. *"We’re family now!"* they all said.

From that day on, Pip loved to visit his friends every morning, and together, they made the garden sparkle even brighter. And that’s how Pip found his special friends in the sky and the garden below.
