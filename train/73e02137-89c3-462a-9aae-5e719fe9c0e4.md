**Local officials in the historic coastal town of Eldermere announce an unexpected discovery beneath the old harbor: a sunken ship from the Black Fleet, believed to be the *Stormcaller*, dating back to the 18th century. Archaeologists confirm fragments of iron, carved wooden planks, and what appears to be a navigational compass bearing the faded insignia of a merchant guild. The find raises questions about the town’s maritime past, including trade routes with the distant empire of Veldros. Eldermere Mayor Elias Vey has urged residents to preserve the site as a historical artifact, though local fishermen warn the area remains treacherous due to shifting tides. Authorities are collaborating with Veldros scholars to decode any surviving records aboard the wreck.**
