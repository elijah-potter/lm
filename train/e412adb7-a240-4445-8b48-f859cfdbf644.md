The first time I saw her, I thought she was hiding something.

She was leaning against the café door, arms crossed, and the way she held her chin—like she was waiting for something to be said to her. I walked over and said, *"You look like you’ve got secrets."* She didn’t flinch. Just smirked. *"You’re the first person who’s ever asked."*

I should’ve known better. I’m the kind of person who notices things but forgets to ask.

But then, she told me about the scar on her wrist—the one she got in a fight she didn’t start. *"I don’t talk about it,"* she said. *"But you’re the only one who doesn’t ask why."* I thought about it. She was right. I had no business knowing.

That night, I dreamed of her again. In the dream, she handed me a key. Not a real one, but a symbol. The kind you leave in a door when you’re done with it. I woke up and wrote the number on a napkin—just in case. She never asked for it back.
