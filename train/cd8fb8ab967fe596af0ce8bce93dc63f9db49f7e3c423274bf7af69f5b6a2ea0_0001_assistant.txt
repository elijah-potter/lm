Brittney Griner, a WNBA All-Star basketball player with Phoenix Mercury, spent ten months detained in Russia last year after she was arrested in Moscow on drug-related charges when going to rejoin a Russian basketball team. Her detainment occurred around the time Russia invaded Ukraine. She was released when Viktor Bout, a Russian arms dealer, was exchanged for her. 

Brittney Griner is working on a memoir that is scheduled to release in 2024. She hopes this book tells her story, shows how she appreciates the support she's received from people worldwide, and brings awareness to others detained in foreign countries. Current detainees include Evan Gershkovich, Kai Li, and Paul Whelan.
