Silent plazas lined with curved stone benches where visitors sit in hushed clusters, their hands clasped or resting in lap pockets. Glowing murals of abstract shapes flicker in low ambient light, casting shifting patterns on the floor. A central fountain emits calm, rhythmic water droplets without sound. Pods of people move in parallel paths, avoiding eye contact, their postures upright but relaxed. The air hums with distant music, but only faint enough to suggest presence without interference. Stalls display artworks and crafts, but interactions are limited to non-verbal exchanges—touching, nodding, or passing objects with a glance. The space thrives on shared stillness, where observation and contemplation replace conversation.
