*"The collector sat hunched over a desk strewn with brittle yellowed clippings, pressed leaves, and a half-eaten sandwich wrapped in a napkin that smelled faintly of stale coffee. A notebook lay open beside a half-finished sketch of a vintage train whistle, its curve jagged from years of pencil work. The room was a shrine of mismatched objects—an old pocket watch, a half-rotten toy soldier, a single, faded photograph of a street scene from 1973. His hands trembled slightly as he flipped through a stack of receipts, tracing the faded ink with a finger. 'Details,' he murmured, 'are the stories we forget.'"*
