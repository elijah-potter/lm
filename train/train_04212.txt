The average number of fruits per basket in five baskets is 25. If basket A contains 15 apples, B has 30 mangoes, C has 20 peaches, D has 25 pears and E has some bananas, how many bananas are in basket E?
Each basket contains an average of 25 fruits so the 5 baskets in all contain 25*5 = <<25*5=125>>125 fruits
The number of apples, mangoes, peaches and pears is 15+30+20+25 = <<15+30+20+25=90>>90
The remaining fruits are bananas and they are 125-90 = <<125-90=35>>35 in number
