A fork in the path where shadows whisper,
Your name carved in the wind’s unsteady breath.
The mirror holds the weight of unseen faces—
Will you choose the fire or the ice?

One hand pulls at the frayed thread of fate,
The other clutches the truth you’ve outgrown.
The clock ticks louder than your trembling heart,
And silence hums the chorus of what you’d lose.

*"Turn back now,"* the hollow voice says,
*"Before the cost becomes your only name."*
But you’ve walked too far for hollows’ lies—
The test isn’t the choice, it’s the claim.

*What will you burn?*
