As a young girl, Tina Turner, born Anna Mae Bullock, sings louder than anyone else in the Nutbush, Tenn., church choir.
Years later, Ike Turner plucks her from the audience and invites her to sing with him on stage.
Ike and Tina Turner reach the heights of show business together, but he is an abusive husband.
Tina Turner finds the strength to leave her husband after a violent argument.
The film shows how Buddhist meditation techniques eventually helped her find her own strength.
