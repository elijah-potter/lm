**[Unsent Letter]**

**To Whom It May Concern,**

I am writing this letter not to solicit your attention, but to document a decision already made. There is no urgency, no need for your response—only the quiet weight of a choice left unspoken.

The pen is held too long over the blank page, and the ink has dried before you could turn it toward me. Some things are meant to stay between the writer and the world, unopened, unread.

If you find this among my papers, it may be because I chose to leave it behind. Or perhaps because I knew you’d find it eventually, as if fate had already decided its meaning.

Either way, this letter is yours to keep—or discard. It does not ask for anything in return.

**[End of Unsent Correspondence]**
