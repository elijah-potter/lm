Sure thing! Here are popular languages to learn other than English.
1. Mandarin Chinese- There are over a billion people in the world who speak Mandarin Chinese. With China becoming a global power, it is increasingly important to learn Mandarin Chinese.
2. Spanish-There are a large number of Spanish speakers in the United States. South and Central America are also popular travel destinations whose populace speaks Spanish. If you plan to work or travel in the United States, Spanish would be a good language to learn.
3. German- As the most spoken language throughout Europe, it is a good idea for business professionals to learn German if they plan to do business in Europe.
4. French- Also known as the language of love. France is a popular travel destination, so why not have fun learning this language?
5. Arabic- The official language of the Arab League which is made up of 22 countries. This is also the language of all Muslim civilizations and their text.
