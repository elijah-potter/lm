Here is a list of five chores for kids to do to earn their allowance and learn a life skill:

1. Do the Dishes: Learning to wash dishes can help free up time for you and also teach children a life skill.
2. Clean Room/Playroom: Have your children clean up their rooms; this can help them learn the benefits of organization. 
3. Take Care of Plants: By having your kids water and care for plants, they can learn the importance of attending to living things.
4. Babysit Younger Siblings: Children may learn responsibility and self-discipline when caring for their younger siblings. 
5. Start Dinner: Teaching kids how to start dinner and cook can help them develop skills around the kitchen they can use later in life.
