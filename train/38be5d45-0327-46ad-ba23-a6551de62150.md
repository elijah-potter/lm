The tiny brass key, worn smooth by years of use, now stood in the center of the room. Its engraving—an abstract swirl—was barely legible, but its presence spoke volumes. The key was not just a lock mechanism; it was a fragment of the past, a symbol of a promise left unfulfilled. The old mansion’s decayed walls seemed to whisper of the woman who had once turned it, believing it could unlock what no key could: a door that led to forgotten dreams.

In the hands of the stranger who found it, the symbol became a question: What did it represent?
