A long time ago, there was a man named Cole. Cole was a great adventurer who would always tell us about his grand adventures. He told me that a long time ago, he went dungeon exploring in hopes of finding some amazing treasure. Cole was the kind of man who would act first and plan second. Can you believe that he only went in with the clothes on his back, a sword, and some cash? He was a funny one, that Cole. 

Anyway, Cole was deep in the dungeon, exploring every nook and cranny, trying to find any treasure. He got lost on multiple occasions! Cole would trigger hidden traps along the way, fall in a couple of ditches, and the whole works! I remember that Cole was telling me of a fight with one of the dungeon's scariest monsters. He told me that this monster had four arms and one sword in each of those arms! Cole is a bit of an exaggerator, but something tells me that he wasn't lying about this. Cole stared this monster down, pulled out his sword and they started fighting. This monster was big, so it meant that it couldn't move as fast. Cole used that to his advantage. The room they were in had tall, rocky pillars, so Cole was able to maneuver past the four-armed monster. Using the pillars to his advantage, Cole was able to bait the monster to swing at the pillars. The monster swung as hard as it could and got one of his swords stuck in the pillar. Cole took that opportunity to slash one of his arms! The monster writhed in pain as he let go of the sword stuck in the pillar. Cole tried to get another slash in, but the monster started rampaging! It was spinning around, swinging its remaining swords with no remorse. This was a fight Cole couldn't win, so he took that as a sign to flee. The only problem was that the monster was blocking the only exit! What did Cole do, you ask? Well, that is an answer I can only tell you next time. Hahaha!
