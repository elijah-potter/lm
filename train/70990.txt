Eye exams needed about every 2 years
Eye exams are important, but how often do you really need to have them done?
Consider the following guidelines from the American Optometric Association:
uChildren should have an exam at 6 months, 3 years, before starting school, then every two years.
uAdults ages 18-40 should have exams every two to three years.
uAdults ages 41-60 should have exams every two years.
uAdults over 60 should have exams every year.
uPeople with diabetes, hypertension or other chronic diseases should consult their physician for advice, as these conditions can put one at risk for eye problems.
