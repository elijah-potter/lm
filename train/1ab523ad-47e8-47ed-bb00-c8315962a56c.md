Quitting smoking is one of the most common yet challenging habits to break due to nicotine addiction. Physical dependence, withdrawal symptoms like cravings, irritability, and difficulty concentrating, along with psychological associations (e.g., stress relief, relaxation), make relapse frequent. Social and environmental triggers (e.g., being with others who smoke, stress) also persist, requiring long-term behavioral adjustments, support systems, and gradual replacement strategies. Other hard-to-break habits include excessive alcohol consumption, overeating, or procrastination, each influenced by similar neurochemical and environmental factors.
