## The Aurora Protocol: Rulebook

**A Cooperative Game for 2-4 Players**

**Designer's Note:** *The Aurora Protocol is designed to explore the complex decision-making processes inherent in responding to large-scale, unpredictable crises. The game aims to foster collaborative problem-solving, strategic prioritization, and a nuanced understanding of the trade-offs that arise when resources are limited and information is incomplete. It is not intended to simulate any specific scientific field, but rather to provide a framework for grappling with challenging ethical and logistical considerations.*

### 1. Components

*   **Game Board:** A map depicting key global regions, each with varying levels of infrastructure and population density.
*   **Researcher Cards (12):** Each card represents a specialist with unique skills and starting resources (e.g., Epidemiologist, Engineer, Diplomat, Logistics Expert).
*   **Crisis Cards (60):** These cards detail escalating events and challenges stemming from the initial anomaly. They are categorized into three tiers of severity (Minor, Moderate, Severe).
*   **Resource Tokens:** Representing Funding, Personnel, Equipment, and Data.
*   **Anomaly Markers:** Used to track the spread and intensity of the anomalous phenomenon.
*   **Stability Markers:** Indicate the overall stability of affected regions.
*   **Event Dice (2):** Six-sided dice used to determine the outcome of certain actions and events.
*   **Region Status Cards (12):** Detailed information on each region, including vulnerabilities and potential mitigation strategies.
*   **First Player Token:** Designates the active player.

### 2. Setup

1.  Place the Game Board in the center of the playing area.
2.  Shuffle the Crisis Cards and place them face down in three separate piles based on their tier (Minor, Moderate, Severe).
3.  Shuffle the Region Status Cards and place one face down on each region depicted on the Game Board.
4.  Each player chooses a Researcher Card and takes the corresponding starting resources as indicated on the card.
5.  Place three Anomaly Markers on the designated starting region as indicated on the Game Board.
6.  Place Stability Markers on each region as indicated on the Game Board.
7.  Determine the first player randomly and give them the First Player Token.

### 3. Turn Structure

Each player’s turn consists of the following phases, completed in order:

1.  **Draw Crisis Card:** The active player draws the top card from the Minor Crisis deck. Resolve the card’s effects immediately. If the card indicates a Region is affected, place an Anomaly Marker on that region.
2.  **Action Phase:** The active player may perform two actions. Actions can be any combination of the following:
    *   **Move:** Move your Researcher to an adjacent region.
    *   **Gather Data:** Spend one Personnel token to reveal the Region Status Card for your current region.
    *   **Mitigate:** Spend the resources indicated on the Region Status Card to remove one Anomaly Marker from your current region.
    *   **Research:** Spend two Personnel tokens and one Data token to draw the top card from the Moderate Crisis deck. Discard this card without resolving it. This action provides advanced warning of potential future challenges.
    *   **Transfer Resources:** Transfer any number of Resource Tokens to another Researcher in the same region.
3.  **Anomaly Spread Phase:** Roll both Event Dice. The total result determines the number of regions affected by Anomaly Spread. Starting from the region with the most Anomaly Markers, place one Anomaly Marker on each indicated adjacent region. If a region already has the maximum number of Anomaly Markers (as indicated on the Game Board), no further markers are placed.
4.  **Pass the Token:** Pass the First Player Token to the player on your left.

### 4. Win and Loss Conditions

**Winning the Game:** The players win if they successfully mitigate all Anomaly Markers from the Game Board before the Loss Condition is met.

**Loss Conditions:** The players lose if any of the following occur:

*   **Regional Collapse:** Any region’s Stability Marker is reduced to zero.
*   **Crisis Deck Depletion:** The Severe Crisis deck is exhausted before the players win.
*   **Uncontrollable Spread:** The total number of Anomaly Markers on the Game Board exceeds the maximum allowed as indicated on the Game Board.

### 5. Trade-Off Mechanics

The game incorporates several mechanics that force players to make difficult choices under uncertainty:

1.  **Research vs. Mitigation:** The “Research” action allows players to gain insight into future crises, but delays immediate mitigation efforts. Players must decide whether to prioritize short-term stability or long-term preparedness.
2.  **Resource Allocation:** Resources are limited, and players must constantly decide how to distribute them among different regions and mitigation strategies. The "Transfer Resources" action encourages discussion and negotiation, but also introduces the risk of neglecting a critical region.
3.  **Data Uncertainty:** Region Status Cards are initially face down. Gathering Data requires a significant Personnel investment, but the information gained may not always be what players expect. A card might reveal a region is more vulnerable than initially assumed, requiring a costly redirection of resources.

### 6. Difficulty Scaling

The game’s difficulty scales based on the number of players:

*   **2 Players:** Start with two Anomaly Markers on the starting region.
*   **3 Players:** Start with three Anomaly Markers on the starting region.
*   **4 Players:** Start with four Anomaly Markers on the starting region.

### 7. Advanced Variants

*   **Hidden Objectives:** Before the game begins, each player receives a secret objective card that provides bonus points for achieving specific goals. This introduces an element of individual strategy within the cooperative framework.
*   **Dynamic Crisis Escalation:** After each round, draw a card from the Severe Crisis deck and place it face up. This card introduces a persistent, escalating threat that must be addressed alongside the ongoing crisis.
*   **Specialist Restrictions:** Limit each player to using only certain types of actions based on their Researcher’s specialization. This emphasizes the importance of teamwork and leveraging individual strengths.
