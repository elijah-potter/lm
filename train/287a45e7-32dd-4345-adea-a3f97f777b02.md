A single sunrise over a cracked lake bed,
where morning mist clung to frost-laced branches.
A child’s laughter echoed across an empty field—
the last time the wind carried their voice like a secret.
The last time the moon’s reflection kissed the horizon
before it faded into the sky’s endless, unspoken memory.

The clockwork of time, always relentless,
told me it was already too late.
Some moments are threads spun only for one to hold—
and now they’re unraveling.
