1. Buy a long-lasting top coat. Having a good top coat is key! Consider trying a few different brands to find the perfect one for you. Having a strong top coat can prevent premature chipping and peeling.
2. Do the "capping" method. Capping is when you apply your nail polish and top coat just to the tips of your nails. Capping helps add a layer of protection as you go about your day touching and interacting with items, making chips less likely. 
3. Be gentle. While it is tempting to use your nails as tools, your manicure will last much longer if you are gentle with your nails.
