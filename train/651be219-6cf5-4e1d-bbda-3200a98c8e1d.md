Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big tree outside, there lived a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the garden. One sunny morning, Benny saw a tiny red apple rolling towards him. "Wow, a shiny red apple!" Benny thought. He carefully picked it up and ate a big bite. The apple made Benny feel happy and yummy.

Just then, Benny spotted a little mouse named Mimi scratching her tiny nose. "Hello, Mimi," Benny said with a smile. "How are you today?" Mimi giggled. "I’m fine, Benny! But my house is too small. Can we make it bigger?"

Benny and Mimi worked together to build a bigger house using sticks, leaves, and stones. They added windows made of shiny leaves and a door that opened and closed. The house looked just like a cozy little home!

Every day, Benny and Mimi played games—hopscotch with pebbles, hide-and-seek with flowers, and shared their snacks like Benny’s apple and Mimi’s seeds. They also sang songs to the wind blowing through the trees.

One evening, as the sun painted the sky pink and orange, Benny and Mimi curled up together under their new house. "This is the best day ever," Mimi whispered. Benny nodded, happy to have a friend. And as the stars twinkled above, they slept peacefully, dreaming of more adventures together.

The end.
