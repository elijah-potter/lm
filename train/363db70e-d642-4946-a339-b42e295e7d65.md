A rare storm forced the detour, rerouting the cargo train from the port to a small rural station where the local farmer had a surplus of organic apples. The driver had no prior plans for these apples but recognized the market demand for them in nearby cities. With minimal overhead, he arranged a pickup by a local food cooperative, which expanded the distribution network overnight. The farmer, who had been struggling to sell his harvest, was overjoyed by the unexpected opportunity. The coincidence transformed both their lives—one by redirecting a shipment, the other by connecting with a new customer—turning a chance encounter into a sustainable business partnership.
