What is the name of the smallest virus?

"Porcine circovirus" (PCV) is the smallest known virus to man as it is approximately 8 nanometers in radius. It is a single-stranded DNA virus and the member of the Circoviridae virus family. This DNA virus is of types : "PVC 1" which was found in the year 1974 and "PVC 2" which was found in the year 1997.
