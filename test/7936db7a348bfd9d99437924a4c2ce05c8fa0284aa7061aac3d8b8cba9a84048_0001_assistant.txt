The boy made his way to the top of the hill, where the old mansion still stood. Long since abandoned, many stayed away out of fear. Some would approach, but upon exploring, they would have little reason to stay. A decayed garden, rotting halls, torn curtains, and a looming emptiness engulfed the rooms. But trapped in the gloom, the mansion held a library, once forgotten, now rediscovered. The boy had nowhere else to go, so he wandered the halls most days, leaving only to find food and water. His only source of entertainment was the library. That dreary, decrepit room filled with collapsed shelves and dusty covers would have been overlooked by anyone passing by, but not the boy. He knew that within those faded exteriors lay lands of wonder and excitement. 

The boy grabbed a book from the shelf, the cover so faded he couldn't even read the title. As he read the pages, his mind raced. With every turn, his excitement grew. As he reached the final chapter, he almost couldn't contain his joy anymore! How could this story possibly end? And then, slowly, it was over. This book, like many others on the shelf, was so badly damaged that many of its pages were illegible. He returned the book to its home, likely the last time it will ever be read. As the boy left the library, he looked back longingly. One day, he'd finish these stories, but not today.
