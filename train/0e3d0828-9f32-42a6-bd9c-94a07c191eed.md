**The Last Door**

The old library’s shelves groaned under the weight of forgotten tomes, their spines cracked like dried leaves. Elias pressed his palm against the cold stone of the final door, its brass handle rusted from decades of neglect. The air inside smelled of damp paper and something older—something like anticipation.

He stepped inside.

The walls pulsed faintly, shifting like living things. Before him stood a vast hall where books floated midair, their pages whispering secrets. A grand staircase spiraled upward, leading to an unseen ceiling. At the center stood a pedestal, and atop it, a single, unopened book with no title, only a symbol—a serpent coiled around a crescent moon.

A voice, neither male nor female, but something in between, murmured from the shadows:

*"Choose."*

A path split before him:
1. **Turn the first page**—risk unraveling the story’s fabric, but find the truth buried in its margins.
2. **Speak the symbol aloud**—let the serpent wake, but the moon may swallow the light.
3. **Close the book and leave**—walk away, but the door behind you will never open again.

The book’s cover shimmered. The choice was yours.
