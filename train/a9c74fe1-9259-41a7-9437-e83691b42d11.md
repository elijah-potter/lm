The house stood silent, its windows like hollow eyes.
A knock at the door—too loud, too deliberate.
The lock clicked. She turned.
Dry fingers traced the scar on his wrist.
*"You shouldn’t have come,"* she whispered.
His breath hitched. The air smelled of damp earth and something older.
*"I know,"* he said, voice breaking.
The front door swung open, dark figures stepping inside.
The door slammed shut behind them.
