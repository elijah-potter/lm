A dimly lit office lobby buzzed with muted conversation as employees hurried to meetings. A sudden burst of laughter from the far corner—a group of three—stopped all activity in its wake. One individual clutched a coffee cup, cheeks flushed, while the others collapsed into fits of giggles, gesturing wildly. A nearby security guard froze mid-step, eyes wide, as the sound echoed through the fluorescent-lit space. The laughter faded into chatter again, but the interruption lingered in the air, a ghost of something unexpected.
