As the months wore on into years and decades, no one aboard the SX-A12 intergalactic rocket lost sight of their mission or its importance. This odyssey hoped to reach a distant planet that scans had indicated was rich with unobtainium, a vital element for a compound that would restart the Earth's core. It had begun cooling at a drastic rate, and if allowed to stop, it would bring an end to the magnetism of the Earth's poles and the rotation of the Earth. Night and day would each become six months long, with one half of the Earth baking while the other froze. It would be the end of civilization, which was why this journey had to succeed. Captain Richter headed the expedition, but her first mate Aleksandr Olinski was indispensable to keeping systems running and morale up. 

It would be decades more until the journey was over, and by the time they returned to Earth, they would all be nearing the ends of their natural lifespans. However, they were all committed to this sacrifice and the value of their cause.
