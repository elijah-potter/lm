20 Word Bio:

Sylvia Mirewood loves writing dragons and other mysterious beasts into creation. She adores her less mysterious critters and her husband.

50 Word Bio:

Sylvia Mirewood writes urban fantasy fiction that lets your imagination fly. Her more mundane furry friends often grow wings or horns on her canvases and grace her book covers. She lives in rural Vermont with three cats, two dogs, and one husband.

100 Word Bio:

Sylvia Mirewood is an urban fantasy author who grew up in the wilds of…No, not wilds. She grew up in Manchester, New Hampshire. She currently lives in a rural town in Vermont with her three very furry cats, two excitable black labs, and one mild mannered, amazing husband. She loves to create her own artwork for her book covers and admits that sometimes pictures of her fantasy worlds show up long before the stories turn into words. When she’s not creating her own fantasy, Sylvia finds joy in reading books by other fantastical authors. More about Sylvia can be found on her website at sylviamirewoodrockfantasy.com.
