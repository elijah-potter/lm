That sounds like a nice gift! Here are some things you can put in the basket for your friend: 
- Gloves - Gardeners often use gloves to protect their hands. Since gloves do wear out over time and can easily be misplaced, it is always good to have a few sets. 
- Kneeler - Called by a few different names, this is something you place on the ground to kneel or sit on while you are working in the garden. It both keeps your knees clean and is easier on them than the ground. 
- Books - There are many gardening books that are full of information on the best ways to garden. Picking one or two of these can give your friend a helpful resource. 
- Seeds - If you know what your friend plans on growing, you can give them some seed packets that they can plant. Having the seeds may encourage them to get the ground ready to plant them. 
- Tools - While some tools are too large to fit inside a basket, there are a few small ones like hand trowels that should be able to fit perfectly fine. These can be used by your friend in the garden. 
- Sunscreen - Since gardening often means being outdoors in the sun for hours at a time, giving your friend some sunscreen will remind them to take care of themselves and help them avoid getting sunburned. 
- Hats - Again, since gardening involves being out in the sun, a good hat or cap can help keep the sunlight out of their face so that they can see and work clearly. 
I hope your friend is able to succeed in their gardening endeavor.
