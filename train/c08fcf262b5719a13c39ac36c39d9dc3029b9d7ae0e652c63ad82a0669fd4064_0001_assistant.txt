It's a pleasure. Here you will find some beaches with bathrooms that you would enjoy in Cape Cod:

1. Mayflower Beach at Cape Cod has beautiful views and a low tide so that you can walk out very far to the sea. This is great for families because of the lifeguards, picnic tables, and restrooms available for use. 
2. Marconi Beach is one of the most well-known beaches in Cape Cod. There is a lifeguard patrolling the swimming area, and the beach is massive in size. 
3. Veteran's Park beach is great for a seaside picnic. It is small, but there is a playground and a well-maintained park. 
4. Craigville Beach offers showers, umbrella rentals, and handicap accessibility. It attracts many swimmers and is a great spot for children.
5. Skaket Beach has warm, clear water with a flat shore. During low tide, there are many tide pools for kids to explore.
