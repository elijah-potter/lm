A quiet promise kept in the unspoken hum of daily routines—
a candle’s flicker in the dark, a whispered apology at dawn,
the way a hand holds another’s just long enough to feel safe.

The world moves on, but the weight lingers:
a half-eaten sandwich left for tomorrow’s child,
a letter folded carefully, never sent,
the way a shadow lingers on a wall when the light is just right.

No grand gestures, no fanfare—just the quiet persistence of what was promised,
and the slow, patient unraveling of what was broken.
