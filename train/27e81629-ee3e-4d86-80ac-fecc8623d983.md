Had the protagonist in *"The Hobbit"* chosen the **One Ring** for himself instead of Gandalf, the War of the Ring might have ended differently. Bilbo’s curiosity led to his departure, but if he had taken the Ring early, Sauron might have won without the Balrog’s interference in Moria. The war could have been fought by fewer allies, with fewer heroes—Gollum, Smaug, and possibly even Saruman might have won had the Ring been in the hands of a more "ruthless" character early.

A more cynical twist: if **Frodo had taken the Ring to Mordor** (instead of Mount Doom) and given it to Sauron, the war might have ended in a **single, decisive battle**—but at a cost of all the good characters, including Frodo himself. The "One Ring to rule them all" would have been destroyed by Sauron’s forces, but the world’s balance would have been irrevocably altered, leaving only darkness.
