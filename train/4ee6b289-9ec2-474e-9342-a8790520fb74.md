A dimly lit alleyway glistens with rainwater, neon reflections dancing on the cracked pavement. The air hums with the distant wail of sirens and the murmur of a city that never sleeps. Behind a rusted dumpster, a figure crouches—**Kai**, arms crossed, eyes scanning the shadows. Their coat is damp, sleeves rolled up to their elbows, fingers twitching as they adjust the grip of a stolen knife. Across the street, a flickering neon sign reads *"The Midnight Bites"*, its glow casting long, wavering shadows. Inside, the clatter of plates and the occasional scream of a customer escape through the half-open door. Kai’s breath fogs the air as they step forward, not toward the din, but toward the alley’s far end, where a figure in a stolen hoodie stands motionless, their back to the street. The hood moves slightly—just enough to reveal a face half-hidden in the darkness. Kai exhales slowly, fingers tightening on their weapon.
