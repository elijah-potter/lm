A decision made in the dark, where doubt is the only light—
each choice a jump into uncertainty, legs trembling over the abyss.
The mind whispers: *"What if I fall?"*—but the heart insists, *"What if I rise?"*
The weight of the leap isn’t the fall, but the faith in the unseen path.
Some steps are measured; others are surrendered.
The trick is not to look back too long, lest the courage erode.
