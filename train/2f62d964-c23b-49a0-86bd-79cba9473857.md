A crumpled slip of paper, weathered by wind and time, lay pinned to the underside of a lamppost near the corner of the old bookstore. The ink was smudged where it had been rubbed by countless hands, yet the words remained legible:

**"Lost: A silver watch, chain intact. Last seen near the park at dawn. Reward: last cookie in the jar. — K"**

A faint, cryptic message that had sparked dozens of debates among locals. Some swore it was a dare. Others claimed it was a warning. A few even laughed—until they realized the watch was found the next morning, gleaming under the streetlight, its chain coiled like a question mark.
