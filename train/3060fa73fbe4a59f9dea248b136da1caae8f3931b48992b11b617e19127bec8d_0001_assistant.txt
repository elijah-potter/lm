Raising chickens is fun but not for the faint of heart! Many people dive into raising chickens because of the rising costs of eggs, but I can assure you that you will spend more money on raising chickens than eggs.

You will first need to buy a coop, waterers and feeders, chicken food (you should use about a 50-pound bag of food per week for a small flock), straw, heat lamps, and laying boxes. You must also ensure the chickens have access to fresh food and water daily. 

While you do not have to check for eggs every day, it is advised so you can check on the condition of your chickens as well. You can also add vitamins to your list of tools to get if you want to ensure your chickens' health and increase their laying ability.
