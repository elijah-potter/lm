## Bookstore Serendipity (Seattle, WA)

I was browsing the poetry section at Elliott Bay Book Company last Saturday afternoon, completely lost in the rhythm of Mary Oliver’s words. The light filtering through the tall windows cast a warm, amber glow across the shelves, and the quiet murmur of turning pages created a comforting hush. You were examining a volume of Rilke nearby, your brow furrowed in concentration. I noticed the distinctive, earthy scent of your sandalwood cologne – a fragrance that lingered pleasantly in the air. 

We didn’t speak, but I felt a shared appreciation for the beauty of language, a silent understanding that felt unexpectedly profound. You had a kind smile, and your hands moved gently as you handled the book. I regret not having the courage to simply acknowledge you, to share a fleeting moment of connection amidst the literary treasures. I left shortly after, but the memory of your presence has remained with me.
