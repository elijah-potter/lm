Long-term care insurance can provide you with many benefits as you get older. This insurance goes beyond typical health insurance and can cover basic needs, such as helping you maintain your lifestyle after an illness or injury. After providing home health assistance, if a cognitive impairment makes daily tasks challenging, this form of care can be beneficial to family members and caregivers who want to protect their loved ones as well as their lifestyle and assets. It can also help retirees who want to preserve the money they have worked so hard to save and individuals who may not have someone else to care for them if the need arises.
