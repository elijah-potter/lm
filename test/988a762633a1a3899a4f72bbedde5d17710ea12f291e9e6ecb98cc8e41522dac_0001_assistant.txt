Not too many years ago in a city not too far away, there lived a fellow named Ed who would often walk his dog Fido through the park near his apartment. They loved their walks, especially Fido, who would often observe their surroundings with a keen eye, identifying all kinds of animals and objects with his keen intellect. It was good, after all, to work the mind as well as the legs, though Fido enjoyed working his legs a great deal as well, especially when they played fetch.

One afternoon, Ed and Fido encountered the most peculiar object in their path. It was a stick, but no ordinary stick. No, this stick had only one end.

Ed picked it up and turned it around, trying to find the other end. Try as he might, he could not. Fido whimpered, staring at the stick as if wanting a closer look. Ed obliged, holding it down to Fido's nose for him to smell. To Fido, it smelled like any other stick, but he could not deny that it looked very strange, having only a single end. He bit down on it and pulled, prompting Ed to let go, and then dropped it. He tried dropping it again and again from multiple angles, but to no avail - only the one end of the stick ever hit the ground.

So Fido did the only sensible thing that one would do upon finding such a strange object. He picked it up to hand to his human to throw. And throw it he did, again and again, long into the evening, and Fido would go running after it. This much Fido understood - no matter how many ends it has, no stick is too strange for a game of fetch.
