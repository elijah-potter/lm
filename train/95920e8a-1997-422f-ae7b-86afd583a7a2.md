A small, ornate brass key safe with a hidden magnetic latch in its base. The lid is etched with intricate geometric patterns, and when opened, a single, finely bound leather-bound journal lies beneath, its pages filled with encrypted notes in an ancient cipher. The safe is locked with a combination of three numbers, only one of which is known to the owner, who has hidden a small, dampened paper note inside the keyring for the key to the safe. The note reads: *"Find the hollow in the oak tree by the old well; the water there is salted with the first blood spilled on the east side of the city."*
