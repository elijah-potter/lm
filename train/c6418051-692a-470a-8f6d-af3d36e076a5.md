The place refuses to display:
- Natural light beyond a single dim, flickering bulb in the basement.
- Any vegetation beyond a single withered potted plant near the entrance.
- Personal photographs or portraits of former residents.
- Sound beyond the constant hum of a failing HVAC system and distant, rhythmic footsteps.
- Text in any language beyond the single, faded "DO NOT ENTER" sign at the entrance.
- Any movement beyond the creaking floorboards or occasional slamming of the front door.
- Artworks beyond a single abstract canvas with cracked varnish.
- Books beyond a single, heavily annotated copy of a 19th-century encyclopedia in the center of the room.
- Any objects that were not stored here for over a decade, including outdated electronics, broken furniture, and rusted tools.
- Any human presence beyond the faint scent of disinfectant and old fabric.

The space is a silent archive of what was forgotten, preserved only in the absence of what should have been shown.
