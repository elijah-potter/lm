**The Clockmaker’s Secret**

In the quiet village of Eldermere, where the old oak clock tower stood sentinel over the cobblestone streets, there lived a reclusive clockmaker named Elias. His workshop was a labyrinth of gears and springs, each one meticulously crafted to tell time with precision.

One evening, as Elias polished a new masterpiece—a clock with no hands but a glowing dial—he noticed a strange symbol carved into its face: **"A VELVET MASK AT DAWN."** The next day, a stranger arrived, bearing a letter with the same words.

The stranger vanished after delivering the message, but Elias’s clock began to chime at odd hours. The villagers whispered that the clock was predicting the future. When the stranger returned the following dawn, he wore a velvet mask and handed Elias a small key.

That night, Elias unlocked a hidden compartment in the tower’s base—a chest inscribed with the same phrase. Inside lay a map to the ancient ruins of Eldermere, where a lost artifact guarded by a clockwork guardian waited.

The next morning, the villagers found the clock tower’s hands frozen at **3:15 PM**, and the dial read: **"The Guardian Sleeps."**

*(Hidden message: **"3:15 PM"**)*
