**Local officials in the historic coastal city of **Port Haven** report a series of unusual events following a full moon on the night of the 17th. Residents and visitors alike have reported hearing deep, rhythmic drumming sounds near the docks and old warehouses, along with strange lights that resemble lanterns drifting over the water. Authorities have deployed extra patrols in the city’s central business district, where an unmarked van has been observed moving slowly through residential neighborhoods at night.**

Eyewitnesses describe the van as having a low, metallic hum and no visible windows. Some report seeing the vehicle disappear behind the **Old Mill Bridge**, a site rumored to be tied to old maritime legends. No charges have been filed yet, but city police have urged residents to avoid wandering alone near the docks after dark. Local historians speculate that the phenomenon might be linked to an abandoned ship found last month near the harbor, though no official investigations have been conducted.

Eastern winds have picked up overnight, with maritime pilots reporting an unusual number of vessels avoiding the harbor entirely. No official statements have been issued by the city’s government regarding the incident.
