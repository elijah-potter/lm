Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the meadow. One sunny morning, Benny found a tiny shiny rock near his burrow. "What could this be?" he wondered, picking it up.

Just then, a little mouse named Mimi ran by. "Oh no!" she squeaked, "my acorn is lost!" Benny looked around. "I see it!" he said, and hopped over to find it.

Mimi was so happy. "Thank you, Benny!" she said. Together, they shared their snacks—an apple Benny picked and a berry Mimi found. As the sun set, Benny and Mimi curled up together, sharing stories and snuggles under the stars. Benny knew they’d be friends forever!
