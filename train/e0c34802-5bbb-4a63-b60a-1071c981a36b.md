A quiet woodland glade with ancient stone benches arranged in semicircles around a central fire pit. The air hums with the distant chime of wind chimes and the occasional rustle of leaves. Fenced off sections feature small, well-lit reading nooks with old books, maps, and journals. A central fountain spouts water that cascades into a reflective pond, mirroring the sky above. Natural light filters through moss-covered oak branches, casting dappled patterns on the stone floor. The ground is soft with grass and fallen petals, and scattered wooden puzzles and art supplies hint at spontaneous creativity. The scent of fresh bread and wildflowers lingers.
