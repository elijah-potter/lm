The **Ephemeral Drift**, a shifting landscape of ever-changing terrain where geologic forces and temporal erosion sculpt the earth in real-time. Rivers carve new paths overnight, dunes migrate across deserts in hours, and cities built on unstable substrates collapse into new formations before dawn. The air hums with the static of forgotten voices—once permanent structures now reduced to ghostly echoes, their foundations buried beneath layers of sand, ice, or sediment. Time is a fluid, and permanence is a myth. Even the stars above seem to blur into the same unchanging sky, yet the ground beneath your feet whispers of a world that never stays the same.
