In the hollowed-out core of the city, where neon hummed like a dying heartbeat, I found the book. Its spine cracked under my fingers, the pages yellowed with age and secrets. The title was scrawled in ink that had long since faded: *"The Weight of Silence."* I turned to the final page, where a single line remained unread: *"What was the cost of the silence we all carried?"* The rest of the text—every word, every whisper—was missing, erased by time or something worse. The ink had dissolved into nothingness, leaving only the question.

Outside, the rain tapped against the window like a thousand fingers counting down to the end. I traced the faded words with my thumb, the paper beneath me trembling. The city’s ghosts watched from the shadows, silent as the void. I wondered if the question had ever been answered—or if it was already too late.
