Tidal inlet at low tide reveals a mudflat, cracked with shallow pools of brackish water, where gulls wheel overhead. At high tide, a narrow channel floods, forcing boats to wait, their engines coughing as the water surges in. The shore is lined with driftwood and seaweed, tossed by the waves that lap at the edge of the submerged sandbars. The air smells of salt and damp earth. Dunes rise in the distance, shifting slowly with the ebb and flow of the tide.
