A stealthy pause before the fork in the path,
Whispers of doubt in the hollows of the mind—
A breath held tight, like a secret kept too long,
Unspoken choices, half-formed and half-begotten.
Silence as the architect of decisions,
Where hesitation is the only voice allowed.
The decision made not in the clamor of words,
But in the quiet hum of a soul that listens,
And then, with a weight like a stone, it is settled:
**Not spoken, but known.**
