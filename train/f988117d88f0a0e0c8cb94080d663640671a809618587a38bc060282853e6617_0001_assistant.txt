Here is your list of five action movies from the 90s.
*Total Recall (1990). This stars Arnold Schwarzenegger. Arnold goes to the recall clinic and ends up unlocking repressed memories of his life as a secret agent. This leads to conflict with a secret organization. 
*Terminator 2: Judgement Day (1991). The sequel to the first Terminator features Linda Hamilton as Sarah Conner fifteen years after the events of the first movie. This time Arnold Schwarzenegger's Terminator has been sent as the hero to save Conner's son from the T-1000.
*Speed (1994). This fast-paced movie stars Keanu Reeves and Sandra Bullock on a bus that cannot go below fifty miles per hour or it will explode. 
*The Crow (1994). The dark tale of a hero who comes back from the dead to exact revenge on the men who murdered his wife.
*Independence Day (1996). An alien species invades the Earth, and their advanced technology proves to be too much for Earth's defenses. A final showdown on July 4th will determine the fate of humanity.
