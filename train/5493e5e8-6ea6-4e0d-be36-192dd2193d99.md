A city council’s "mystery park" with no visible signage, where the only rules are:
- **Evening gatherings** require no permit, but police arrive unannounced at midnight to "review noise levels" (interpreted as arbitrary fines).
- **Streetlights flicker unpredictably**—some say it’s a test; others whisper it’s a signal.
- **A fountain** occasionally spits water in random directions, though maintenance logs list it as "operational."
- **Bike lanes** shift weekly without warning, based on "community feedback" that varies by neighborhood.
- **Gift shops** sell "local folklore" items like "haunted keychains" or "cursed lottery tickets," but refunds are denied after a week.

Residents debate whether the park’s rules are a joke, a tax loophole, or a cover for something else. No one knows for sure.
