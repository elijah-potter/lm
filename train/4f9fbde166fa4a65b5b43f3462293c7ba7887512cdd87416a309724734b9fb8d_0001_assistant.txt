• Through time and space.

Three friends meet up to remember a friend that went missing ten years prior. They scientifically discover equipment in his old house and accidentally embark on an adventure through space and time.   

• Time to fall in love.

Unable to find their way home. The three friends try to blend into their new past period, though Asul blends in a little too. Romance blossoms and the gang's secret is at risk.

• Let there be Cyborgs.

After escaping their trial and certain deaths the three friends and their new companion find themselves in the year 2301. The remaining humans on earth have integrated with technology and become a race of cyborgs.

• Escape from the dinosaurs.

Equipped with a new time control device. The friends go in search of their long-lost companion who is now almost certainly stuck in the past.

• Love me now and forever.

After a failed attempt at rescuing their long-lost companion the four-time travellers find themselves stuck in limbo, being thrown into a new period every hour. Some familiar faces come to save the day.
