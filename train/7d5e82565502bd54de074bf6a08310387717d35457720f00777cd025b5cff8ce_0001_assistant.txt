Pizza Hut might not be for me. 

I recently ordered a thin-crust pepperoni pizza that unfortunately did not taste the best, as the crust was overcooked and hard to bite. Also, I realized that the marinara was flavorless and that the cheese was not properly melted. As a result, I regret I had to discard the pizza as I could not enjoy it properly in a healthy manner.  Luckily, the chicken poppers were great and I enjoyed them!  
Though my experience was not ideal, I look forward to coming back and having a better meal later on.
