In Rocky Mountain's forests, I hiked in the sun,
With miles to go before the hike is done,
Trees of pine and spruce, I wandered through,
Of all these paths, which do I choose?

The trails diverged, and I stood there still,
Each one has its own beauty, each its own thrill,
One went uphill, onwards to a distant peak,
The other meandered on, peace to seek.

I chose the uphill path to take me up high,
The climb was steep, my breath came with a sigh,
Every step I took, I took with such care,
The clean mountain air felt so rare.

As I labored and climbed, the view grew wide,
The world below seemed to divide,
Clouds played and danced above my head,
The sun shimmered in the colors gold and red.

When I reached the pinnacle, my heart was light,
And from this high peak, oh what a sight!
The valley stretched out for many miles far below,
It was a sight of such wonder, nature's beauty aglow.

For within this place of beauty, wonder, and awe,
What I found was a part of me that I never saw.
And though the path I chose to take was tough,
The journey that I went on, it was more than enough.
