Unspoken rules govern the **Old Market District** in the city of **Veythar**, where the streets hum with quiet authority. The air smells of damp stone and old parchment, where merchants and scholars alike navigate unseen protocols:

- **Silence in the Grand Hall:** When the **Clock of the Seven Moons** tolls midnight, the tavern’s low murmur dies. The **Stone Table**—where debts and secrets are settled—only opens after the **Third Watch**, when the **Moonweaver’s guild** marks their presence with a flicker of candlelight.
- **The Weight of a Word:** A single word from the **Bridger’s Guild** can turn a stranger’s path. The **Iron Door** at the **Crypt of the Forgotten** is only lifted for those who’ve earned the **Dagger of Silence**—a relic passed only to those who’ve proven their worth in the **Game of Whispers**.
- **The Unwritten Contract:** The **River’s Edge** tavern’s patrons must never touch the **Black Glass** unless invited. The **Silent Barkeep** knows every guest’s secret, but only the ones who’ve paid in **time** (a year’s labor for a debt, a lifetime for a betrayal) are allowed to sit at the **Curtain Table**, where the walls themselves seem to whisper.
- **The Rule of the Hollow:** In the **Catacombs of the Forgotten**, the dead do not speak to the living. The **Elder Scribe** marks their tombs with **three scratches**, but the fourth must be left blank—unless you’re one of the few who’ve earned the right to claim a name from the dead.

Here, power is measured in **respect**, not paper. A glance from the **Guardian of the Shattered Mirror** can erase a wrongdoing. The streets are not paved with gold, but with **trust**—and that is the coin they trade.
