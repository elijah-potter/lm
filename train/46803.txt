What is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is an alternative medicine that involves using essential oils. These oils are inhaled or absorbed by patients who are suffering from a wide variety of health conditions. They are also used to help improve mood and are as a supplement to other medicines.
Aromatherapy has existed for thousands of years. In the 11th century, it became even more popular and has been used ever since. Aromatherapy is more complex than just using essential oils. It also involves using carrier oils, herbal distillates, absolutes, phytoncides, and vaporizer oils. How Does Aromatherapy Work?
There are two different ways that aromatherapy usually works: through skin absorption and through a sense of smell. Aromatherapy massage is used to place essential oils on the body before they are absorbed through the skin. The oils used are tailored to suit the needs of the person. The other common way is by inhaling the essential oils. It works by setting off a reaction in the brain, for example, when your brain smells food it may automatically make you think that you are hungry. Essential oils work on this concept.
The Health Benefits of Aromatherapy
Some of the common health benefits of aromatherapy are discussed below:
- Stress relief – This is the most popular use of aromatherapy. Many essential oils are well-known relaxants which can help eliminate anxiety and soothe your mind.
- Works as an antidepressant – This is another popular use of aromatherapy. Many people do not like the side effects that come with taking anti-depressant tablets, so they turn to aromatherapy. Aromatherapy can really help, especially if used alongside counseling.
- Memory boost – Memory loss can be very frightening and can lead to older people being unable to remember short-term memories. Aromatherapy is often used to help slow down the deterioration for people who suffer from dementia or Alzheimer’s disease.
- Boost your energy levels – Many people live busy lives but often feel like they need more energy. Aromatherapy can help with this. Some essential oils are known to raise energy levels, increase circulation, and generally leave you feeling more awake.
- Speed up healing – Essential oils can stimulate the body to start and increase the speed of the healing process in the body. They can increase the blood flow and oxygen around the body, which speeds up healing.
- Less frequent headaches – Most people suffer from headaches at some time in their life. Instead of relying on drugs, aromatherapy can be a great way of getting rid of your nasty headache. It can also reduce anxiety and stress.
Aromatherapy has many amazing health benefits from stress relief to speeding up the healing process in your body. It is well-known for being a brilliant alternative to pharmaceuticals and can leave you feeling better than ever.
