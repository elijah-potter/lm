A crimson lantern flickers in the damp corner of the tavern, its glow casting long shadows on the worn wooden table where an old man with a weathered scar across his cheek leans forward. His gnarled fingers trace the rim of his mug, eyes locked on the man across from him who has just raised a goblet. **"You’ll get what you deserve,"** the stranger says, voice low and deliberate. The lantern’s flame dances over the edge of a sealed parchment tied with silver thread, its contents whispered only to the waiting crowd.

The tavern hums with tension—silence, then a murmur when the old man exhales sharply. **"Then take it,"** he says, gesturing to the lantern. The stranger’s fingers tighten around the hilt of his dagger as the lantern’s flame dims, revealing the promise written in faded ink: *"The Key to the Lost Vault."* The air thickens with the weight of an unspoken deal.
