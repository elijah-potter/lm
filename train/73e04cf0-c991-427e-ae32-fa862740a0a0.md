Something lost in the cracks of time’s quiet scroll—
A name half-whispered in a room where the air hummed with static,
A face blurred by a sunrise’s fading glow,
A story untold in the hush of a library’s last shelf,
A memory half-forgotten, half-real, like the echo of a door closing
too far away to hear, but close enough to feel the weight of what was.

It lingers in the corners of dreams, a ghost in the machine’s cold silence,
a fragment of a world that was never fully built—
a half-remembered past, half-remembered present,
half a life, half a song.
