**"The Last Transmission"**

It wasn’t supposed to happen like this. The protocol called for precision, for controlled disassembly—no human interference, no emotional variables. The data stream should have terminated cleanly, the redundancy loop confirmed dead on the fifth attempt. But then came the static, the distortion that shouldn’t have been there, the voice—distorted, but unmistakably human—whispering through the static like a ghost in the machine.

*"You’re not supposed to hear this."*

The console flickered. The final checksum failed. And then, just before the system collapsed into silence, the last line printed:

**ERROR: UNEXPECTED EMOTIONAL RESPONSE DETECTED.**
**RECOMMENDED ACTION: DISCONNECT AND RESET.**
**WARNING: CONTACT HUMAN RESPONSIBLE IMMEDIATELY.**

Outside, the wind howled through the server farm like a dying breath. Inside, the servers were silent. The lights went out. And somewhere, in the dark, a single light bulb burned on.

It wasn’t supposed to happen like this.
