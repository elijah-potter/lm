Christian Aid is working to reduce the spread of HIV. We have 148 partners working on HIV in 40 countries.
The number of new HIV infections continues to outstrip the numbers of people treated for the virus - for every two people starting treatment, a further five become infected.
Education about how to prevent HIV transmission is crucial to reverse this trend. Fewer than 40% of young people have access to basic information and less than 40% of people living with HIV know their status.
Christian Aid is working to reduce the spread of the disease through a whole range of education programmes in countries all around the world. From dance, to radio shows to sermons our partners are helping to empower some of the world's most vulnerable people to protect themselves from this preventable disease.
