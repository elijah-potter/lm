A quiet alleyway in an old European town, lined with half-torn street signs that point to "Viejo Mercado" (Old Market) but no longer indicate the exact path. The pavement curves subtly at the edge of the cobblestones, guiding visitors unconsciously toward the hidden steps behind a shuttered café. A flickering neon sign for a jazz club reads "Silencio" ("Silence") but the door is always unlocked—those who enter leave with the same unspoken understanding that the music stops at midnight, not by rule, but by habit. The only visible law is a single, cracked streetlamp, its light dimmed by years, casting long shadows that shift like unseen eyes.
