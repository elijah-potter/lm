---
**Map of Eldoria: The Lost Frontier**
---
**Region: The Obsidian Wastes**
- **Terrain:** Cracked basalt plains, jagged obsidian spires, and salt flats.
- **Key Locations:**
  - **The Hollow Spire** (72°W, 45°N): A ruined monolith where the last Chronomancer’s notes are buried.
  - **Blackthorn Crossing** (68°W, 43°N): A ferry crossing over the **River of Echoes**, where voices repeat past events.
  - **The Shattered Gate** (65°W, 44°N): A collapsed arcane barrier; only accessible via the **Rune of Unraveling**.
- **Flora/Fauna:**
  - **Gloomthorn** (creatures that absorb light; found near obsidian).
  - **Silversprout** (plants that grow in cracked earth; yield rare seeds).
- **Paths:**
  - **North:** The **Path of Whispers** (leads to the **Drowned Citadel**).
  - **South:** The **Salt Road** (avoids the **Dust Storms**).
- **Resources:**
  - **Obsidian Glass** (rare, used in alchemical mirrors).
  - **Echo-Seeds** (harvested from River of Echoes’ banks).

- **Core:** Hidden beneath the **Shattered Gate**; requires a **key of time** (found in the Hollow Spire’s ruins).
- **Effect:** Reveals hidden paths in the map (e.g., the **Hidden Lake** at 66°W, 44°N).

---
**Story Hook:**
A cartographer’s ledger reveals the map was drawn by **Kael the Unseen**, a thief who vanished near the **River of Echoes**. His last entry: *"The Gate’s key is in the Hollow Spire’s chest—but the Spire is not alone."* The river’s whispers now carry fragments of his own death.
