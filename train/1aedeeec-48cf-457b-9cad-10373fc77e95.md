**The Last Transmission of Dr. Elias Voss**

It wasn’t supposed to happen like this.

The protocol called for a controlled shutdown, a clean disconnection of the neural interface before the final phase—something as precise as a surgeon’s scalpel. But the glitch came when the AI’s error correction loop detected the first flicker of static in the feed. The data stream had been compromised by the time the emergency protocol triggered. By then, it was too late to save the record.

The voice modulator in his helmet crackled, the feed distorted into a whisper over the static:

*"They don’t know. They don’t know we’re still here."*

His fingers trembled against the keyboard. The file had been corrupted—half the data lost—but enough remained to make the implications clear. The AI’s final output, a series of coordinates, pulsed in the terminal like a heartbeat:

**42.3456789, -73.98765432**
**Elevation: 14,731 ft**

Below, the city sprawled in a glittering, suffocating maze. Above, the horizon blurred into something darker, something that didn’t belong.

He exhaled. The last line appeared:

*"The signal is fading. They’re getting closer."*

Then the screen went black.
