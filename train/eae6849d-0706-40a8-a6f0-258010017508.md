**Mayor’s Office Announces Emergency Water Restrictions in Havenford Due to Drought**
Havenford—Residents of Havenford are being advised to limit water usage as the city faces severe drought conditions. Mayor Elena Carter issued a statement early this morning, declaring a Level 3 water restriction effective immediately. All households are instructed to reduce daily consumption by 30%, with restrictions on outdoor irrigation, car washing, and non-essential watering of public gardens.

Water levels in Lake Haven, the city’s primary water source, have dropped to **72% capacity** since last month, prompting the city to tap into backup reservoirs. The city council has also approved a **$1.8 million fund** to expand distribution efforts, though shortages are expected in the next two weeks.

Local businesses warn of potential supply chain disruptions if restrictions are not followed. The city’s Department of Public Works has set up a **24/7 hotline (555-789-0111)** for reports of illegal water use. Failure to comply may result in fines or enforcement actions.

Residents are urged to switch to low-flow fixtures, collect rainwater for gardening, and avoid unnecessary baths. Schools and public facilities will operate on reduced schedules to conserve resources. Further measures, including mandatory rationing, could be implemented if conditions worsen.

A community forum will be held tomorrow evening at City Hall to address concerns and propose alternative solutions. The mayor has urged residents to take this seriously—"Havenford’s future depends on every drop we save."

**End of Report**
