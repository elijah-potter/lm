Developmental milestones are behaviours or physical skills seen in infants and children as they grow. Head control, rolling over, crawling, walking, playing, and talking are some of those milestones. The milestones are different for each age range. The developmental wheel shows the milestones from birth to 3 years. One side tells you what to watch out for at each stage while the other side gives you tips on how you can stimulate your child.
