Some people call me “Gary with the green hat.” I can’t help but wear it every day. I love green, and I love hats. I wear it on the bus. I wear it at recess, and I definitely wear it to walk my dog, Gomez. 

My dog, Gomez, is my best pal. I asked my mom if I could buy Gomez a green hat. That way, we’ll be “Gary and Gomez with the green hats.” Gomez is a German Shepard, and he’s really playful. We play outside in our backyard. Good thing it has a fence!

One time, Gomez took my green hat and started running with it around the yard. I was scared my favorite green hat would be destroyed, but Gomez was a good boy and dropped it. I think that just means Gomez wants his own green hat.
