An echo chamber hums between two voices, their words tangled in static.
*"You were here last week too,"* the first says, *"but you never spoke of this place."*
*"The air here remembers,"* the second replies, *"and it keeps me from forgetting."*
A pause. The wind carries a whisper: *"Why do you linger?"*
*"Because the last time we spoke,"* the first murmurs, *"the answer was already half-formed."*

*(The conversation loops.)*
