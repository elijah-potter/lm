Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy pink ears and a nose that sparkled like a star. Every morning, Benny would hop outside to play in the sunny garden.

One sunny morning, Benny saw a tiny, shiny red ball rolling near the flowers. "Oh no!" Benny thought. "What if I drop it and it gets lost forever?" Benny picked it up carefully. The ball felt warm and soft, just like Benny’s favorite blanket. Benny hugged it tight and said, "This is my new best friend!"

The next day, Benny shared the ball with his friends—Lily the rabbit, Max the squirrel, and Tilly the turtle. Together, they played games like tag and hide-and-seek. Benny even taught them how to roll the ball in circles, and soon, it became the most exciting game in the garden.

But one afternoon, as Benny was playing, he noticed something strange. The ball was missing! Benny searched everywhere—under the bushes, behind the big oak tree, even in the watering can—but it was nowhere to be found. Benny’s ears drooped, and his heart felt heavy.

Just then, Max the squirrel hopped up. "Don’t worry, Benny! I have an idea!" Max scurried around and came back with a small, brown bag. Inside was a tiny, shiny red ball—just like Benny’s! "I found it!" Max cheered. "It was hiding under my tree!"

Benny’s eyes lit up. "It’s really mine again!" He hugged the ball so tight it felt like a giant hug. And from that day on, Benny and his friends played together every day, rolling their ball, laughing, and making new adventures. And Benny knew that no matter where it went, his best friend would always find its way back home.
