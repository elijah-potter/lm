Vietnamese belongs to the Austro-Asiatic language family in Southeast Asia. It has more than 100 million native speakers, including those living overseas in different parts of the world such as Asia, North America, Europe and Australasia. While the old Vietnamese, known as Chữ Nôm (Sina-Vietnamese), used to have pictographic script using Chinese characters, modern Vietnamese script is Latin-based, making it easier to learn for other European language speakers.
