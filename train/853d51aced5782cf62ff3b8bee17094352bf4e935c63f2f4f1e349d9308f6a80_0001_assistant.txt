Sweat dripped over her brow, leading a steady line down her cheek and landing on the foam mat below. Her opponent was equally as sweaty and out of breath, panting harshly behind her standard issue face guard. They prowled around each other, like two lions, gauging each other, taunting one another with feints and fake grapples.
If Samantha didn’t land the strike, her opponent would win. That was all that mattered. Samantha needed to win.
Her opponent feinted right and Samantha ignored her, going for her exposed left side. Her kick landed and her opponent fell to the ground, winded. The referee called the hit, and they got back into position.
The pressure was on. It was a tie, and one last strike would decide who would win and who would lose.
Samantha wet her lips, her eyes darting around to take stock of every moment her opponent made. They stalked around one another again, more feints, more half-hearted grapples. The time was ticking away, they would both lose if it was a draw.
Now it was a battle of wills.
Samantha’s opponent was losing it. She saw the nervous twitch in her eye as the clock ticked another second off the clock. And then she struck. Quick as a viper, so fast Samantha would have gotten hit if she hadn’t been anticipating it, her opponent lashed forward with a jab. Samantha dodged it at the last second, using the element of surprise and knocking her opponent down.
Everyone was silent. Then cheers erupted. Samantha had won her fight.
