The dinner started well. The dragonflies fluttered with excitement as Mrs. Gleason exclaimed at the beautifully arranged dishes of food: the vibrant candied fruit in the sweet-and-sour pork dish, the delicate shreds of chicken meat stir-fried with tiny peas, and the iridescent pink prawns in a ginger sauce.

At first, one of the dragonflies was too absorbed in savoring its own meal to notice how the other guests were faring. But soon, a sense of duty awakened within it. Dragonflies, being gracious creatures, understood that sometimes guests were too polite to help themselves, and it fell upon them to serve more food.

Glancing at Meg, the dragonfly assessed if she needed additional nourishment. Its multifaceted eyes widened at the sight of her plate. It was overflowing with an assortment of food: the sweet-and-sour meat mingling with the chicken shreds, while the chicken sauce intermingled with the prawns. Meg had been eagerly sampling from a second dish before finishing her first helping!

Horrified by this disregard for dining decorum, the dragonfly shifted its gaze to Mrs. Gleason. It watched in astonishment as she emptied rice from her bowl onto her dinner plate, followed by a generous ladling of prawns and gravy, creating a haphazard fusion. It resembled the mixing of sand, gravel, and cement to create an unconventional dragonfly-style concrete.

Unable to bear witness any longer, the dragonfly turned its attention to Mr. Gleason. It observed him chasing a pea around his plate, his nimble wings buzzing in frustration. Despite several valiant attempts to capture the elusive pea with his chopsticks, it would roll back to the center each time. Finally, with a resigned sigh, he relinquished the chopsticks and resorted to picking up the pea with his fingers. Unbelievable! Even a grown dragonfly would find such behavior amusing.

All the dragonflies paused their own feasting to observe the whimsical spectacle created by the Gleasons. The dragonfly felt an uncontrollable urge to giggle but was quickly halted by a stern gaze from its mother. She frowned and subtly shook her head, conveying a message of understanding. The Gleasons were unfamiliar with the customs of the Chinese dragonflies, and they were doing their best to adapt and navigate the dining experience. The dragonflies resumed their own elegant dining, embracing tolerance and acceptance.
