Those who are interested in a career in animation will be able to design animated characters, scenes, and other art for commercials, web sites, movies, and print media. Students who enjoy drawing and who want to learn more about computer programs that are used in creative design departments should consider earning a degree in this program. Skills learned include computer applications, drawing, flash media, and storyboarding. Students will be able to find employment in advertising departments, corporations, government agencies, and non-profit agencies.
The courses students will have to complete include computer animation, computer applications, 3D animation, and special effects. As technology continues to advance, computer animation will change. Students who are able to use this technology in a creative way will be able to find a lot of opportunities for employment and will be able to bring innovative and unique designing to life.
