Dear Dinosaurs,

We hope you're all doing well and enjoying your time on planet Earth. We're writing this from the future to warn you of your impending doom and apologize for how everything turned out.
Basically, in a few years, all of your friends and family will find themselves under a devastating tragedy that wipes you all from existence. We understand that this is a difficult revelation but there's more you need to know. A few thousand years after you all cease to exist, nature gives birth to other animals who gradually evolve into homo sapiens and transform into who we are today. We've developed a civilization that's stronger, smarter, and more capable than you in every way, so we're much better at preserving our species. 
That said, we couldn't find a way to restore your species, and for that, we are truly sorry. We wish we could help you prevent this grim fate, but we don't know the exact time of the tragedy or even how it happened. Worst case scenario, you guys might get hit by a giant asteroid from outer space, or maybe one of your volcanoes goes off. You might want to look out for those. 
Anyway, we just thought we should let you know about this while sending our condolences along the way. We're sorry for how things turned out, but I guess you guys just weren't meant to stick around, so lucky for us. We hope this letter finds you well and you can prepare for the future accordingly.

Regards, Humanity
