The CEO’s annual budget review revealed a $250K savings in the IT department’s cloud expenses—an opportunity to reallocate funds to R&D. At first glance, the decision seemed straightforward: cut costs, invest in innovation. Yet the nuances emerged:

- **Team morale**: Layoffs or restructuring would dent morale, risking talent retention and productivity.
- **Vendor lock-in**: The cloud provider had exclusive deals with certain tools, and switching could disrupt workflows.
- **Short-term pain vs. long-term gain**: A pause in R&D could delay critical breakthroughs, but immediate savings might buy time to renegotiate contracts.

The dilemma was compounded by conflicting priorities: financial discipline vs. strategic ambition. The decision was deferred to a cross-functional team for a deeper analysis, acknowledging that simplicity in outcomes often masks complexity in execution.
