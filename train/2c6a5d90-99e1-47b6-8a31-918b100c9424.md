Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a garden full of flowers, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had soft brown fur and big, round ears that twitched when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his favorite flower, the daisy, wobbling in the breeze. "Oh, look!" he said. He gently picked it up and brought it to his basket.

Just then, Benny heard a tiny *squeak* from the bush. Out came a sleepy little mouse named Milo, his eyes still half-closed. "Hi, Benny!" Milo said, but his voice was still a little shaky.

Benny grinned. "Hello, Milo! Are you hungry?" Milo nodded. "I saw a shiny apple on the tree—want to share?" Milo’s eyes lit up. Together, they climbed the tree and picked the juicy apple.

As they sat under the big oak tree, Benny told Milo a story about a brave dragon who lived in the sky. Milo giggled and asked, "What happens next?" Benny laughed and said, "Maybe the dragon will find a friend!"

Then, a gentle rain began. The flowers sparkled, and the garden sounded like music. Benny and Milo snuggled under their blanket, dreaming of their next adventure.

And that’s how Benny and Milo learned that sharing and kindness made everything brighter, even in the rain.
