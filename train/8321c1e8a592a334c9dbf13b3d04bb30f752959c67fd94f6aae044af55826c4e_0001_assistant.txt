Inattention may be indicated if there are six or more symptoms in children up to age 16 years, or five or more for adolescents aged 17 years and older and adults. The symptoms of inattention must have been present for at least 6 months and are inappropriate for developmental levels. They include failing to give close attention to details, making careless mistakes, having trouble holding attention on tasks and in play, not listening when being spoken to, failing to follow through on instructions, failing to complete tasks, having trouble with organization, disliking tasks requiring sustained mental effort, losing things needed for tasks and activities, being easily distracted, and being forgetful.
