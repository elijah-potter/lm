Mandy is a senior in High School. She has done everything exactly as she was supposed to. She got all A's, participated in sports, and made a lot of friends along the way. She was just 3 months away from graduation when she realized that she had no idea what to do next. She was so confused about where to go next, and she didn't know how to cure that confusion. 

She didn't know if she should be an artist, a firefighter, or a mail delivery girl. A myriad of different paths polluted her brain and completely overwhelmed her. One day, she was so frustrated that she had to take a break from studying and work on her favorite hobby, sewing. As she was sewing her heart out, she had a major epiphany. She decided to be a fashion designer! The next day she applied to her favorite colleges, and eventually, she received the letter of acceptance she'd been waiting for.
