Once upon a time in a cozy garden, there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around. Benny had a big, round belly and a soft, pink nose. One sunny morning, Benny saw a shiny, sparkly star in the grass.

"Oh, Benny," said the star, twinkling, "I’m lost! Can you help me find my way home?"

Benny’s ears perked up. "Of course I can! Let’s hop together!"

They hopped and they skipped, past fluffy clouds made of cotton candy, over bouncy flowers that giggled when Benny’s paws touched them. Benny found a little bridge made of acorn caps and a tunnel of tunnels made of tiny mushrooms.

At the end of the tunnel, Benny saw a cozy house with a warm, golden light. "That’s my home!" said the star. "Thank you, Benny!"

Benny grinned. "You’re welcome! Now, let’s share a story about two best friends who hopped together to find a sparkly star."

From that day on, Benny and the star became the best of friends, and they always looked for adventures together. And so, the end!
