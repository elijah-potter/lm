When Willie the Weasel woke up on Saturday, he could hear the raindrops outside of his mountain cave. Willie glanced outside and realized it was raining quite a bit. He wondered how he was going to be able to play with his friend Fannie the Fox that day since they couldn't frolic around as usual.  

All of a sudden, Willie heard a howl. “Willie!  Come out and play!” 

Willie knew the sound of Fannie’s voice. He wondered how Fannie could be outside since she didn’t like to get her fur wet. Surely, they wouldn’t be able to play together on a rainy day.  

Just then someone appeared at the entrance of the cave. It was Fannie, with large leaves strapped to her back with twine. It covered her body so that her fur wasn’t wet.  

"Hi, Willie! I have one of my inventions for you, too!” Fannie said happily.

“What a great idea!” Willie exclaimed. “Where did you get the leaves?” The leaves looked larger than the usual leaves near their mountain homes. 

“I brought them back with me when we went on that journey to the hills,” Fannie explained. The leaves were large and felt waxy. They were good for repelling water. Quickly, she tied a leaf to Willie’s back. It fit perfectly. It was keeping the raindrops off of his fur, just as planned.  

"Now we can go play!" Willie exclaimed, swishing his gray tail back and forth. "Tag, you’re it!”  

Willie bounced ahead out of the cave and down the mountain path. The duo had a lot of fun zigging and zagging, all the while staying dry. They realized that no matter what obstacle would come their way, there was always a solution.
