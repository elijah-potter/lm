Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around and play with his friends—Milo the dog, Lila the cat, and Pip the little bird.

One sunny morning, Benny noticed something shiny near his burrow. Curious, he dug it out and found a golden acorn. Benny chewed on it, and suddenly, a tiny magic seed popped out! Benny was excited. He planted the seed in his garden and watered it every day.

Soon, a tiny tree sprouted with bright red flowers. Benny’s friends were amazed! Milo wagged his tail and said, "Let’s help!" Lila purred and curled up on the tree’s trunk, while Pip flapped his wings happily.

The flowers grew bigger and bigger, turning the garden into a colorful garden where all the animals could play. Benny’s friends built a swing from vines, and Benny hopped up and down, laughing as the breeze made the flowers sway. It was the happiest day ever!

And from that day on, Benny and his friends spent every summer playing in their magical garden, where every little thing could turn into something wonderful.
