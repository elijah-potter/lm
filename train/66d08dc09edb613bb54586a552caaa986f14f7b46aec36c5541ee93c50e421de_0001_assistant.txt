Nate and Regina are siblings who love adventuring around their neighborhood together. They've explored every creek and climbed every tree within a mile radius, but one place they never were brave enough to visit was the abandoned house on Laura Lane. But today, after an afternoon of wandering boredom, they found themselves in front of that house.

REGINA: Woah! I didn't realize we walked so far.

NATE: Gosh, I don't think we've ever been this close to... the house. 

REGINA: Yeah... why don't you check it out?

NATE: Wait, what?!

REGINA: Unless your chicken, that is.

NATE: Oh y-yeah? Well... why don't you go in instead?

REGINA: 'Cause... 'cause I wanna give you the chance to be brave instead! Aren't I a good sister?

NATE: Yeah right, you just want me to get cursed instead of you!

REGINA: Look, if you're too chicken to go in, all you have to do is say so.

NATE: Well, if I'm a chicken, then you're a turkey! 

Suddenly, the sound of thunder roars out from the cloudy sky overhead, and as the sound echoes, Nate and Regina look at each other for only a moment before running back home as fast as they can. They swore to themselves that they would one day explore the abandoned house, but it wouldn't be today.
