## Product Requirements Document: "Synthesis" - Knowledge Consolidation App

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently gather information from diverse sources—articles, meeting notes, research papers—but struggle to synthesize this information into actionable insights and maintain a readily accessible, interconnected knowledge base.

**Vision:** To empower knowledge workers with a seamless platform for capturing, connecting, and retrieving information, transforming scattered data into a cohesive and readily usable knowledge asset.

---

### User Personas

**1. Dr. Eleanor Vance, Research Scientist:**

*   **Goals:** Efficiently organize and connect research papers, lab notes, and conference presentations to accelerate discovery and facilitate collaboration with colleagues. Maintain a comprehensive record of research progress.
*   **Pain Points:** Existing note-taking apps lack robust connection features; information is often siloed and difficult to retrieve; manual citation and reference management is time-consuming and error-prone.

**2. Mark Olsen, Strategic Consultant:**

*   **Goals:** Quickly capture key takeaways from client meetings, industry reports, and internal strategy documents. Easily share synthesized information with team members and integrate insights into presentations and proposals.
*   **Pain Points:** Difficulty retaining information from fast-paced meetings; lack of a centralized repository for client-specific knowledge; challenges in translating raw data into compelling narratives.

**3. Anya Sharma, Technical Writer:**

*   **Goals:** Compile and organize technical specifications, user manuals, and API documentation from various engineering teams. Ensure consistency and accuracy across all documentation. Facilitate easy navigation and search for end-users.
*   **Pain Points:** Receiving fragmented and often conflicting information from different sources; difficulty maintaining version control and ensuring documentation remains up-to-date; struggling to create a cohesive user experience across multiple documents.

---

### Requirements

**1. Intelligent Note Capture:** The app must allow users to capture notes from various sources (typed text, pasted content, imported documents) with automatic tagging and keyword extraction.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can copy and paste text into the app, and the app automatically identifies potential tags based on content. Imported documents (PDF, DOCX) are parsed and searchable.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 80% of users utilize the intelligent capture feature within the first week. Average time spent capturing a note is reduced by 30% compared to manual methods.

**2. Relationship Mapping & Linking:** Users must be able to create explicit relationships between notes, visualizing connections and exploring related information.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can manually link notes with descriptive relationship labels (e.g., "supports," "contradicts," "is an example of"). A visual graph representation of linked notes is available.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 60% of users create at least three relationships between notes within the first two weeks. Average number of relationships per note is greater than 0.5.

**3. Dynamic Search & Discovery:** The app must provide a powerful search function that allows users to quickly find relevant information based on keywords, tags, and relationships.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Search results are ranked by relevance, considering both keywords and relationship context. Users can filter search results by tag, date, and relationship type.
    *   **Success Metrics:** Average search time is reduced by 50% compared to keyword-only search. User satisfaction with search results is rated 4.5 or higher on a 5-point scale.

**4. Collaborative Knowledge Spaces:** Users must be able to create and share collaborative knowledge spaces with defined access permissions.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can invite collaborators to a knowledge space with read, write, or comment permissions. Changes made by collaborators are tracked and auditable.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 20% of users create collaborative knowledge spaces within the first month. Average number of collaborators per space is greater than 2.

**5. Export & Integration:** The app must allow users to export notes and knowledge spaces in standard formats (e.g., Markdown, PDF) and integrate with popular productivity tools (e.g., Slack, Google Drive).
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can export individual notes and entire knowledge spaces in Markdown format. Integration with Slack allows for direct sharing of notes. Integration with Google Drive allows for bidirectional file synchronization.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 30% of users export notes or knowledge spaces within the first month. 15% of users utilize at least one integration.



---

### Technical Considerations

*   **Scalability:** The architecture must be designed to handle a large number of users and notes.
*   **Security:** Data encryption and access control mechanisms are paramount.
*   **Platform Compatibility:** Initial focus on iOS and Android platforms.
*   **Offline Access:** Notes must be accessible and editable offline.
*   **Search Indexing:** Utilize a robust search indexing solution (e.g., Elasticsearch) for efficient searching.

### Risks

*   **User Adoption:** Users may be resistant to adopting a new knowledge management system.
*   **Data Migration:** Importing data from existing systems could be challenging.
*   **Feature Creep:** Maintaining a focused feature set is crucial to avoid complexity.



### Non-Goals

*   Real-time co-editing of notes.
*   Optical Character Recognition (OCR) for image-based documents.
*   Direct integration with proprietary document management systems.
*   Support for additional operating systems beyond iOS and Android.
*   Advanced data visualization capabilities beyond relationship mapping.




---

### Launch Checklist

1.  **Core Functionality Testing:** Thoroughly test the intelligent note capture, relationship mapping, and dynamic search features.

3.  **Onboarding Documentation:** Create clear and concise onboarding documentation for new users.
4.  **Support Channels:** Establish support channels (e.g., email, FAQ) to address user inquiries.
5.  **Beta Program Feedback:** Incorporate feedback from the beta program participants.
6.  **App Store Submission:** Prepare and submit the app to the iOS App Store and Google Play Store.
