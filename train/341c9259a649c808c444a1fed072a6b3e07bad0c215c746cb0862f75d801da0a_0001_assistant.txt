Mary always hated drinking tea. It didn't matter what kind of tea it was. Whenever she would so much as smell it she would gag. Her friends would always ask her, "Well, Mary, how do you know you don't like tea if you've never tried it?" This was not enough to change her mind. She would say, "EW! I hate tea, and I would never try it!" Mary just didn't understand why it was such a big deal that she doesn't like tea. She had enough; she stomped her feet down and told her friends, "Fine! If it will make you guys leave me alone, I'll try some tea!" Her friends were ecstatic and handed her a freshly brewed glass of sweet tea. She grabbed the glass, took a swig, and POOF, she had disappeared. 

Mary suddenly found herself in a strange new place. She took a moment to collect herself and observe her surroundings. Mary was surprised, "Wait... is that Georgia Washington? ... Is that Thomas Jefferson? What year is this?" Mary rushed to a nearby shop, grabbed the daily newspaper, and took a gasp, "Oh my goodness! It's 1791!" The man running the shop shouted, "Well, yes, little lady! Would you like a complimentary glass of tea?" Mary looked at the tea, then looked at the man, then looked back at the tea before grabbing it and taking a huge gulp. As she swallowed the drink, once again, POOF. She was back in the house with her friends. "Guys! What happened? I was just in the 1700s!" Mary yelled. "You disappeared! We didn't know what happened to you!" her friends exclaimed. "I guess when I drink this tea, it takes me to a random point in time. Like time travel!" At that moment, Mary knew she was never going to drink tea ever again.
