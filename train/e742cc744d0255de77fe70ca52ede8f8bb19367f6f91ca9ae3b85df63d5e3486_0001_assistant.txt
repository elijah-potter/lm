Yui was waiting at the school gates when Satoshi was leaving the school. He took a deep breath and approached her. "H-hey, Yui," he said.

Yui turned to him with a casual smile. "Shall we?" 

Satoshi struggled to ignore the whispers of those around him as he and Yui left together. Not long after they started walking, Satoshi said, "So, what was it you wanted to talk about?"

He managed to not stutter and felt mildly more comfortable now that they were alone. Yui kept her level gaze ahead of them and said, "I would like to propose a union between us."

"Huh?" Satoshi asked, stopping in his tracks.

Yui looked away, cleared her throat, and repeated, "I would like to discuss the commencement of a joint partnership between you and me."

Satoshi frowned, "W-what are you talking about."

Yui huffed, her cheeks flushed. "Are you really going to make me say it? I thought you'd be able to understand me with how much you read."

"No, no. It's not like I don't understand you," he hurried to say. "I know what you said. I just...don't think I can believe what you said."

Yui finally looked directly into his eyes, making Satoshi freeze up. The way her lips formed the words was beautiful, he thought. "I want you to be my boyfriend, Satoshi."

The only thing he could think of was her lips. "Okay," the words slipped from his lips before he understood what had transpired. By the time his mind caught up, Yui was practically dancing away with an uncharacteristically giddy smile on her face.
