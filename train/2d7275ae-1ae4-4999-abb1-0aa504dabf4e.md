## Lost in the Record Store (Brooklyn, NY) (October 26)

The afternoon light filtering through the windowpanes of Rough Trade felt particularly golden on Thursday. I was browsing the jazz section, searching for a reissue of a Charlie Mingus album, when I noticed you. You were deeply engrossed in the classical collection, tracing the spines with a thoughtful finger. You had a cascade of auburn hair, and were wearing a beautifully worn leather jacket that smelled faintly of sandalwood and old paper – a scent I found unexpectedly captivating. We didn't speak, but I caught your eye briefly; a fleeting smile played on your lips as you selected a recording of Debussy’s *La Mer*. I continued my search, acutely aware of your presence, and felt a peculiar sense of regret when I realized you were leaving. The gentle crackle of vinyl from the store's speakers seemed to underscore the quietness of our unspoken connection. I would genuinely love to know if you’re the person with the Debussy and the sandalwood jacket. Please reply with the name of your favorite composer.
