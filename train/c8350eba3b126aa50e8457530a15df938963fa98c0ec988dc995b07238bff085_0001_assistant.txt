The Fairy's Home

There once was a tiny fairy that lived in a beautiful walnut shell home among the branches of a towering oak tree. Her home was the most wholesome of all the homes in fairyland. Sometimes the squirrels would mistake her home for a yummy treat. However, the rabbits would guard the fairyland, and make sure that everyone stayed safe. The fairies spent most of their days gathering daisies for decor and singing songs of gratitude for Mother Nature.
