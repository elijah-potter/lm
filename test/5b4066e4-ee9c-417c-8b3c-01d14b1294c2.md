**Administrative Holiday: "National Data Processing Day"**

**Official Date:** Third Thursday in May

**Purpose:**
- Mandatory day for all employees to:
  - Complete pending paperwork
  - Archive obsolete records
  - Update internal databases
  - Train staff on new compliance forms
- **Non-negotiable:** No new contracts, invoices, or policy changes may be signed.

**Procedures:**
1. **Morning (9 AM–12 PM):** All non-essential meetings canceled. Only "urgent" administrative tasks permitted.
2. **Afternoon (1–3 PM):** Mandatory "Documentation Sprint" – all open cases closed, spreadsheets reconciled.
3. **Evening:** "Compliance Review" – all employees must confirm they’ve addressed at least one outdated regulation.

**Penalties:**
- **Unassigned:** 10% pay deduction for first violation.
- **Refusal to participate:** Mandatory "paperwork training" until completed.
- **Repeated:** Suspension until all pending tasks are resolved.

**Exemptions:**
- Only applied to bureaucratic roles (e.g., HR, Finance, IT).
- Local governments may adjust dates based on fiscal cycles.

**Symbolic Ritual:**
- Employees must submit a "Declaration of Administrative Unity" (signed form) affirming completion of tasks.

**Inspiration:** Combines "Paperwork Awareness Month" with "Data Privacy Week" for maximum convenience.
