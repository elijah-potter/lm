Towering mountains reaching towards the heavens can be seen from across the waters of the continent Tokathia. Adventures and merchants alike come seeking riches in the vast and bountiful mines of the foothills, hiding a seemingly endless hoard of precious gems and metal ores. Some come to these lands attempting to uncover secrets lost to time, stowed away atop the skyscraping peaks of the tallest mountains. Others journey to the lowlands, where a large and ever-expanding town of trade resides, distributing goods from other distant lands as well as products picked and forged from the local mines.
