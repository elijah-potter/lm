A dimly lit kitchen at dawn, flickering fluorescent light casting long shadows. The morning air smells of stale coffee and damp wood. The fridge door hangs slightly ajar, its contents half-eaten—some forgotten leftovers, a half-drunk carton of milk, and a single, empty cereal box on the counter. The child’s school backpack, still wrapped in its plastic, sits open on the floor by the door. Inside, the usual contents are missing: a worn pencil case, a half-empty water bottle, and the crumpled homework sheet with a single note scrawled in purple marker—*"Dad’s gone to work early. Meet at park after school."* The mother stands at the sink, staring at the empty space where her daughter’s coat usually hangs. A small, round, dark stain on the floor near the door hints at an overnight spill, but no one will say what it was.
