My dearest Burt,

I've missed so much these last few months. I hope that everything is going okay overseas. I wanted to write to keep you updated on your favorite team, the Toronto Maple Leafs, while you are away. They've officially hired Brad Treliving as their new General Manager. The management team told Kyle Dubas that he wouldn't be back, so now it's Treliving's turn to right the ship. Treliving says that he's excited about the opportunity and also that he's honored to be working for an original six franchise.
Treliving comes from Calgary, where he was the GM for nine seasons. They made it past the first round of the playoffs twice in his time. For the seven years before that, he was the Vice President of Hockey Operations and the Assistant GM for the Arizona Coyotes.
Brendan Shanahan welcomed Brad to the Leafs, citing his wealth of knowledge and how well-respected he is amongst his peers. He believes that with his leadership and vision, the Leafs will make it to a Stanley Cup. Brendan also touched on the contract of Kyle Dubas, He didn't think it had to do with money and there is always a chance a GM doesn't return. He even thought things might pan out with Dubas, however, they didn't and that led to Brad getting hired.
Dubas had a good record during his tenure, however, with only one playoff series win. That came this season when they were ousted by the Florida Panthers in five games. I hope this news brings you new hope for your favorite team.

Please come home soon.

With love,
Shari
