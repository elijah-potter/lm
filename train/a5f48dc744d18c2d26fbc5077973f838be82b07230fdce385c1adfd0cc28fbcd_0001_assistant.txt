Morgan had been practicing all afternoon on his brand-new acoustic guitar. His dad gave it to him two weeks early so he could learn to play a song for everyone at his birthday party. Morgan knew if he wasn't able to play the song well, his dad would be disappointed. 
"Just 4 chords," he thought. He pressed his fingers into the first shape on the chord chart. His fingers wobbled and stung in discomfort. He began to strum, and a foul sound rang out. 
"Oh, man! I'm never gonna get this!" He exclaimed. Morgan closed his eyes, taking a deep breath. Another strum with no improvement. Over and over he strummed to no avail. Suddenly, his mother knocked at the door 
"Morgan it's time to blow out your candles!" she said. He reluctantly got up from his chair and went into the kitchen. His friends and family surrounded him, and his dad brought in the cake with 13 candles. As they all started to sing, Morgan thought he knew the perfect thing to wish. He closed his eyes and wished for his guitar to play a beautiful song all by itself when the moment came. The family gathered for cake and ice cream. The day went on without a hitch, and then it was finally time. They all circled Morgan in the backyard and, with sweat dripping down his brow, he began to strum. It was the same foul chord, and Morgan's heart sank. All of a sudden, the guitar leaped out of his hand with a mind of its own and began to belt out a beautiful song. Everyone was astounded.
