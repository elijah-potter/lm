A small, dimly lit church basement transformed into a rave with disco ball projections on the stained-glass windows, where the congregation sings off-key gospel harmonies over a bass-heavy synthwave track. A makeshift dance floor in the back is packed with attendees—some in religious attire, others in neon neon, swapping between hymns and trance beats. A food stall offers halal wings and vegan sushi rolls, while a group of kids play "Mission Impossible" in the narthex with glow sticks. The priest, now a DJ, spins a mixtape of gospel and techno, and a DJ booth doubles as a confession booth where people leave their sins (and their last-minute outfit changes) in exchange for mixtape recommendations. The priest’s sermon? *"This is God’s rave, and you’re invited."* A single firework explodes over the altar at midnight, and the congregation erupts into "We Are Family" by the Bee Gees. The DJ announces, *"This is the end of the service, but the party just got started."*
