Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a little bunny named Pip. Pip loved to hop, play, and explore outside. One sunny morning, Pip saw a tiny, shimmering rainbow in the sky.

Pip was curious and hopped closer. "Wow, rainbows are magic!" Pip giggled. Pip chased the colors, and soon, they led to a hidden garden filled with flowers of every color. Pip's ears perked up—this garden was special!

A little bird named Bella flew down and said, "Hello, Pip! The flowers make magic potions here!" Pip's eyes sparkled. "Can I taste one?" Bella nodded and gave Pip a tiny cup of flower juice. Pip sipped it and felt happy, just like a big, soft cloud.

Suddenly, a big thunderstorm rolled in. The rain poured, and the wind blew. Pip and Bella were scared, but they remembered something important—they had a cozy blanket made of soft leaves. They curled up together and slept under the stars, dreaming of sunny adventures.

The next morning, the sun came out again, and Pip woke up with a happy "Woof!" Bella chirped, "Ready for more magic, Pip?"

Pip hopped outside and saw the rainbow again. "Let’s go back to the garden!" Pip said, and together, they explored more flowers, tasted more potions, and made new friends in their magical world. And that’s how Pip and Bella discovered that even in the rain, there was always sunshine and joy.
