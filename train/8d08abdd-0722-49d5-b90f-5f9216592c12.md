Lily the bunny had a big, fluffy tail that wiggled when she hopped. One morning, she woke up and found her favorite berry bush had been nibbled! "Oh no!" she said. She peeked through the bushes and saw a tiny squirrel chewing on the leaves.

Lily hopped to the squirrel and said, "Hey, little one! Why did you eat my berries?" The squirrel, named Sammy, looked small and scared. "I was hungry," he whispered.

Lily thought for a moment. "You know what? Let's share!" She gave Sammy half her berries. The squirrel chewed happily, and Lily had enough for herself too.

Sammy then saw a big, brown bird sitting on a branch. "Can I share with you too?" Sammy asked. The bird, named Benny, was friendly. "Of course!" Benny said. Lily gave them all her berries, and they all munched together.

After that, Lily and Sammy became best friends. They shared berries and even helped each other find the best hiding spots. Sometimes, Benny would join them, and they would all have a big, cozy picnic under the sun.

From that day on, Lily learned that sharing makes everyone happy and helps friends get along. And sometimes, a little kindness turns a small problem into a big, wonderful surprise!
