The first light of dawn painted the sky in hues of gold and violet, a soft contrast to the storm clouds rolling in from the east. Elias sat on the edge of the dock, his fingers tracing the weathered wood, the salty breeze carrying the scent of damp earth and sea. He had come here every morning for years, but today felt different.

A letter lay beneath his palm, sealed with wax, its contents unknown. The ink had faded, but the postmark from a distant town—one he had never visited—reminded him of his mother’s last words: *"Find out who wrote this."* His breath hitched. Who had written this? And why had they sent it to him now?

The storm’s fury broke open, waves crashing against the cliffs in a deafening roar. Elias watched as the waves receded, leaving behind only the whispers of the wind and the distant call of gulls. He had spent his life chasing answers, but this one felt like a trap, a question without a path to the answer. What was he supposed to do now? And more importantly, what would he find if he tried?
