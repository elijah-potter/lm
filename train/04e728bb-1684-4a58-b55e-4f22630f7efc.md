A flickering overhead light casts long shadows across the empty kitchen counter. The fridge door stands ajar, its once-stocked shelves now bare except for a single half-eaten yogurt. On the coffee table, a half-read book lies open to a blank page where yesterday’s note had been. The missing key, usually hidden in the dish rack, is nowhere in sight. The child’s empty lunchbox sits beside the door, its snack wrappers still crumpled in the corner. A single sock, rolled into a ball, lies on the floor beside the couch. The scent of stale coffee lingers in the air—another morning forgotten.
