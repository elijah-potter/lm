Historians consider Histoire de M. Vieux Bois, made by Rodolphe Töpffer, translated to 'The Adventures of Obadiah Oldbuck' in English. This comic was published in Geneva in 1837, and unlicensed copies were distributed in the UK and the US in the 1840s. Before this, 'The Yellow Kid in McFadden’s Flats' was considered the first comic book.
