A sudden storm derailed the morning commute, leaving the traveler stranded at an unfamiliar bus stop. The overcast skies, once dismissed as minor weather, now forced a detour through an alleyway. Inside, a stranger shared a half-eaten sandwich, revealing they were on their way to the same abandoned bookstore downtown—where the traveler had planned to return later. The coincidence unfolded: the stranger’s lost wallet matched the traveler’s emergency cash, and the bookstore’s closed sign had been removed hours ago by a passing janitor who’d left a key under the plant. By the time the traveler arrived, the stranger had already claimed the bookstore’s rare first-edition copy of *War and Peace*, leaving behind a cryptic note: *"The right path isn’t always straight."* The traveler’s original plan—researching rare manuscripts—now shifted toward tracking the stranger, now a fugitive, and the stolen book’s next owner. The universe had rewritten their story in three unexpected turns.
