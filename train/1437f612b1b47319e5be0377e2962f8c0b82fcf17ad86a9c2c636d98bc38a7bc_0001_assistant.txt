Once, in a kingdom far, far away, there were three kings: The first king was King Sven, who ruled the land of Sweden. The second king was King Olaf, who ruled the land of Norway. The third king was King Hans, who ruled the land of Denmark. They were known for being competitive and constantly bickered over who had the biggest castle and the fanciest jewels. 

After many years of anger and disputes, the three kings finally realized that their constant fighting was only causing harm to their land. Therefore, they all decided to team up and join forces to form a special bond with each other: The Kalmar Union! Now, all three kings could work together while also being the best of friends!
