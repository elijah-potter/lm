**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, Ill. – August 21, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this year, offering a traditional celebration of agriculture, community, and entertainment for all ages. This year's fair promises a diverse range of attractions, from livestock exhibits and 4-H demonstrations to thrilling carnival rides and live musical performances. Visitors can expect a vibrant atmosphere showcasing the best of Sangamon County’s heritage and talent.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from Friday, August 30th, through Sunday, September 8th, 2024. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM. A special fireworks display will illuminate the sky on Saturday, September 7th, following the evening concert. The fairgrounds are located at 2121 Clearview Road, Springfield.

Admission is $15 for adults, $10 for children ages 6-12, and free for children five and under. Season passes are available for $50 and can be purchased in advance online at sangamoncountyfair.org. Parking is $5 per vehicle. Discounted tickets and promotional offers may be available closer to the event date.

**Headline Events:**

*   Grandstand Demolition Derby – Saturday, August 31st
*   Livestock Auction – Sunday, September 1st
*   Professional Rodeo Series – Friday, September 6th
*   Country Music Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters – Saturday, September 7th
*   4-H Showcase and Awards – Sunday, September 8th
*   Midway Spectacular featuring thrilling rides and games
