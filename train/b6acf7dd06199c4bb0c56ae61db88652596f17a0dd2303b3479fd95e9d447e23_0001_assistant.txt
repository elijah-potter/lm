My longtime friend and I took a stroll in the woods. The very tip of the castle was in view where the trees were thinnest. We thought maybe we could see someone standing there on the balcony of the tower, and we hoped it was the prince that we both wished one of us would marry. I imagine we both would prefer we became the new princess, but I knew I'd be happy for her and enjoy spending time in the castle if she were chosen. Maybe I'd even get some important job or meet another prince. I believe she felt the same about me. She'd never given me any reason to doubt it. I suppose we were so focused on seeing the tower that we took a misstep and found a patch of wood we'd never visited before. It was dark, and there was a green glow. We were both covered in the glow. Suddenly I looked at my friend with hatred. She might be the princess one day, and what would I be? A commoner still. Why did she deserve it more than me? Her nose is bigger than mine. Looking at her face, I noticed her expression. Her face was gnarled by the same hatred I felt inside myself. I knew I'd never seen her look that way before. And, in that moment, it dawned on me. This was not us. It was that light. I grasped her arms and pulled her out of the light. 

"What was that?" she asked. "Where did those feelings come from?"
