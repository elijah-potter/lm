In the hollowed-out shell of an old, abandoned lighthouse, the last keeper sat with a cup of cold tea, its steam curling into the damp air like forgotten promises. The beam had flickered out years ago, its once-guiding light now a rusted relic beneath the tide’s relentless grasp.

The door had been left ajar for decades, but no one had ever come to claim what was left behind. Inside, the air smelled of salt and old wood, the echoes of distant voices still clinging to the walls like ghostly fingers. The keeper had once been a man of purpose, but now, his hands trembled as he traced the cracks in the floorboards.

Outside, the ocean roared, indifferent to the ghosts within. He had spent his life ensuring no one drowned in those waters, but now, the sea seemed to whisper secrets only he could hear—whispers of a time before he was born, before the lighthouse stood, before the world was ever meant to know.

What was he supposed to do with the answers the wind carried?
