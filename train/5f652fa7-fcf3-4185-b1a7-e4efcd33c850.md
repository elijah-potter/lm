I woke up late again, my alarm buzzing like a tired bird. The morning felt like a blur—coffee went cold, the train was late, and my to-do list was already too long. Another day where I felt like I was just passing through, doing things that needed doing but not really *meaningful*.

I tried to focus on work, but my mind kept drifting to the same old thoughts: *Will I ever be enough? Do I matter?* The world moves fast around me, and I’m just another face in the crowd. I tried to help a friend yesterday, but I’m not sure I was really there for them. Maybe I’m just a distraction, like everyone else.

The sun set early, and I didn’t even notice. It’s easy to lose myself in the routine, to forget that life is fleeting. I wonder if anyone even remembers my name. Maybe I’m just another story no one reads.
