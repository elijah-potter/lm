Once upon a time, in an old ice cream shop, stood a new bubble gum machine. The machine was excited to be seeing the happy faces of children getting ice cream and to be dispensing bubble gum to them as well. Life became swell at the ice cream shop. Business was good. Kids came in and got so excited when they would get a brightly colored gumball. One rainy day, the machine noticed the man who owned the shop bringing in a door for the kitchen. The door was set up to block customers from getting back into restricted areas. The machine thought the door was so interesting. 

Later when the door was set up, the machine asked it if it was excited about its new job. The door said, "Yes, of course, and I can't wait to meet new friends. How long have you been here? Is it busy? Are there a lot of kids?" The machine thought it was cute how curious the door was. They talked for hours. The next day was very busy. Many people were coming in and out of the new door. The machine took notice and started to realize just how beautiful the door was. When the shop had a quiet moment, the machine looked at the door and said, "You're doing a great job on your first busy day. I'm pretty new, and I was a mess my first day." The door said, "Thank you! I like your attitude. Can we be friends?" The gumball machine was so happy and said, "I would love nothing more."
