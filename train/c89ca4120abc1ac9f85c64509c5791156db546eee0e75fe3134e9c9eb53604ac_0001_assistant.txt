Years ago, in a small mountain village, a plague had spread amongst the people bringing pain and misery with it. The once thriving community had been beset with years of hardship under the plague, with the loss of so many rendering them unable to bring in the food to sustain the village. Those not lost to the plague were being driven out due to starvation, grief, and fear.

Fortunately, the traveling apothecary Johannes Fleischer heard about the plight of the villagers and went to their village to save them. He brought with him his cart of exotic remedies and the expertise to apply them and sought to find a cure for the deadly disease.

This was challenging at first. For months after his arrival, Johannes struggled to make headway in treating the sickness. He quickly ruled out his more common and simple solutions and was forced to experiment with more radical and untested treatments. Several lives were lost during this time, and it weighed heavily on the doctor’s heart. But, eventually, he found a way to treat the disease that worked more often than it failed.

This discovery reinvigorated the resolve of the villagers, as well as Johannes himself. With the breakthrough, the doctor managed to bring the plague under control within a month, and within three, it was completely eradicated from the village. 

To this day, travelers can find in the heart of the village a statue raised in honor of Doctor Johannes Fleischer, whose memory is held strongly in the hearts of the people there even now.
