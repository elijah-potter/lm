Chef Anthony was a man obsessed with cheese. He saw the versatility of cheese, how it could pair with so many seasonings and dishes. For his birthday, his coworkers would give him different cheeses from around the world. He even ate three pounds of smoked gouda cheese every morning with his espresso. He had a strong stomach! 

For his yearly vacation, Chef Anthony decided to travel to France to find some rare cheese. He finally found what he was looking for on a farm, a 600-year-old wheel of cheese. He bought it for a preposterous amount of money and started his journey back to America. He tried with great effort to roll the wheel of cheese along the countryside. But the cheese was so delicate that it started to melt and crumble. Chef Anthony was devastated, but he refused to let his beloved cheese go to waste. With the help of the entire French population, and many shovels, he transformed the melted cheese into a gigantic batch of nachos. The small French town honored him with a statue of him wheeling cheese up a mountain.
