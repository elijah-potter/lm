A man in a suit nervously adjusted his cufflinks while speaking to a stranger at a crowded airport lounge. *"I’m so sorry for the delay, but my flight is actually boarding in two minutes—it’s just that my boarding pass was misfiled in the system, and I’ve been trying to resolve it ever since."*

The woman, a flight attendant, smirked. *"Oh, that’s just great. I’ve got a 3:15 anyway, so it’s all good."* She sipped her coffee, leaving him standing awkwardly, his tie slightly askew.

Back at his gate, he tried again, this time whispering to the attendant: *"Oh, I completely forgot to ask—did you happen to see my boarding pass?"* She rolled her eyes. *"Yeah, it’s on the counter. You’ve been standing there for five minutes."*

The man slumped against the wall, his attempt at politeness now a cringe-worthy mess.
