A school bus is the safest means of transportation available. School transportation and personnel have earned this safety record because of a sincere regard for the safety of their passengers. To maintain and improve this record, all efforts are made at state and federal levels to find ways to improve school bus safety standards.
You can help us a great deal in our efforts by becoming aware of bus safety rules and making sure your children understand them.
Included in the Transportation Drive is a copy of our bus rules, which comes to us from state law and regulations by the Magazine Board of Education. Since over half of our students are bus riders and many of them travel long distances, it is necessary for their safety that they understand and follow these rules.
