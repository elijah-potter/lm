Smiling faces, forks poised, 
Thanksgiving, three little boys. 
Oh dessert time, joy!
They patiently wait, they’ve all cleaned their plate, 
“We’re done now”; Mom hears the noise. 

A tradition written, but something breaks in the kitchen; the mischievous kitten. 
Hungry little eyes, bring pies or they’ll cry.  
Each pie’s broken or bitten. 

It’s not very nice, to fake “pumpkin and spice”, but never think twice.
Sly as a fox, your backup in the icebox!
Sweet potato soon sliced. 

“Boys, how’d you like it?” Pie tantrums and fits; they sputter and spit.
“You tricked us, it’s gross”, no pie they love most.
Yam imposter they’d bit.
