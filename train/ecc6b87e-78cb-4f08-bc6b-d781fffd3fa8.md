Lily the little bunny had a big, bright, bunny ball. She loved to play with it every day! One sunny morning, she hopped outside to find her favorite spot under the big, leafy tree.

"Oh, what a beautiful day!" she giggled.

Suddenly, a tiny sparkle flew down from the sky and landed in her paws. It was a magical magic bean! Lily's eyes sparkled too. "Wow, this looks like magic!" she whispered.

She planted the magic bean in her garden and soon, a tall, green plant grew up to the sky. From the top, tiny, shiny stars fell down—one by one, like candy! Lily gathered all the stars and put them in a little basket.

As the sun set, she felt tired but happy. She snuggled down with her basket of stars, dreaming of adventures with her magic bean. "Tomorrow," she whispered, "I’ll play with my magic again!"

That night, Lily dreamed of hopping through the stars and playing with her bunny ball in the moonlit garden. She woke up with a smile, ready for her next magical day.
