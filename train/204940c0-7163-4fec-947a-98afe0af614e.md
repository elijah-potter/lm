Ritual performed annually at dawn in a forgotten temple:
1. **Purification**: Participants bathe in a stagnant pond, its water turning black with algae.
2. **Offering**: A single, cracked clay pot containing a single, withered flower is placed on an altar made of rusted iron.
3. **Silence**: A long, unbroken chant of an unknown language—voices hoarse, syllables repeating endlessly.
4. **Sacrifice**: A blackened rat, fed only on moldy bread, is released into a collapsing stone spiral.
5. **Lighting**: A single candle, wax dripping into the fire, burns until it extinguishes by morning.

*No script, no purpose—just the weight of repetition.*
