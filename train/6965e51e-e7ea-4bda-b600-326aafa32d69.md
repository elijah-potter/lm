In the dim glow of the old library, Elias sat hunched over a cracked volume, his fingers tracing the same worn edge of a page every time he closed his eyes. The scent of aged paper and old books filled the air, a quiet symphony of forgotten stories.

“You’re not going to stop repeating that, are you?” a voice said, startling him.

Elias blinked. “Excuse me?”

“That line. ‘The past is a shadow that never quite fades.’ I’ve heard it a hundred times. You’re like a broken record.”

He sighed, rubbing his temples. “I just can’t escape it. It’s stuck in my head.”

The stranger leaned closer, their eyes sharp. “Then maybe it’s stuck in yours because you’re afraid of moving on.”
