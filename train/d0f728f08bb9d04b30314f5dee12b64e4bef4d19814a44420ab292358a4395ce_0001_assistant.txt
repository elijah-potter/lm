Of course, here's a list of places to visit in Paris:

1. The Louvre: The Louvre is an art museum in Paris and contains some of the most well-known paintings in the world. It is one of the most popular museums in the world, containing more than 600,000 pieces in its collection.
2. Arc de Triomphe: Built in 1836, the Arc de Triomphe is a monument in Paris dedicated to those who fought and died in both the Napoleonic Wars and the French Revolution.  The names of all the French victories and generals in the wars are inscribed on the inner and outer surfaces. It also contains the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier beneath it.
3. Loire Valley: Located Southwest of Paris, Loire Valley is a valley on the Loire River famous for its historical towns and abundance of wineries. It is referred to as the Garden of France due to the number of Vineyards, fruit orchards and farms located there.
