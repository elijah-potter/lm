This divide was exacerbated by differing perceptions of fairness—those who felt excluded from discussions later criticized management for failing to consider employee perspectives, while supporters framed the policy as a strategic necessity. Tensions peaked during a heated team meeting where opinions clashed, with a few members leaving in protest, and others quietly withdrawing support.
