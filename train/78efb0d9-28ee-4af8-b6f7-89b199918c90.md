The room was dim, the air thick with tension. A single candle flickered on the coffee table, casting long shadows across the worn leather sofa where *Ethan* sat hunched, fingers wrapped around a steaming mug of black coffee. His phone buzzed—a notification from the news app.

*"Breaking: Suspected Terrorist Cell Caught in Raid"*

Ethan exhaled sharply, gripping the phone tighter. His grip on the mug trembled as he swiped the screen, scrolling through reports of arrests, detentions, the chaos unfolding in the city.

A knock at the door. He didn’t move.

*"Ethan? You in there?"* His sister’s voice, muffled through the wood.

He stood, wiping his forehead with the back of his hand. The hallway beyond was empty. The door creaked open, revealing his mother standing there, her hands clutching a folded newspaper.

*"They say it’s over,"* she said, voice low. *"But the streets… they’re still full of fear."*
