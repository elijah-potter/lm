That sounds like a fun birthday party! Here are some ocean-themed gift ideas for you:

- Ocean-Themed Candles. There are many candles that are ocean-themed, either having seashells in them or having a smell that is similar to the ocean. Your friend might enjoy lighting one of these when she happens to be away from the ocean she clearly loves. 
- Beach Towels. Since it seems like she spends a lot of time in the water, a beach towel both fits the theme and is something she can use to dry off when she gets out of the water. 
- Swim Gear. Things like goggles and swimsuits can get damaged and need to be replaced over time. So, buy your friend one or more so she has backups. 
- Hats. Being out on and near the ocean often means someone is in the sun a lot. Your friend might appreciate a hat, and you can keep with the theme of the ocean by choosing one that is a sandy color, has a starfish on it, or any number of other things. 
- Sunscreen. As mentioned, your friend will probably be out in the sun a lot. Sunscreen is definitely something she will be using. 
- Seashell Decor. Seashell decor comes in many different shapes and sizes. You can get even get seashell decor that is useful, such as a shell that has a hook attached to one side so you can use it to hang stuff from. 

I hope she likes whatever you decide to give her!
