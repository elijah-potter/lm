If there was one activity that Joanna could engage in all day without a care in the world, it was talking on the phone with family. It didn't even matter which family member it was; from cousin Marcus to her sister Felicia, she could go on and on as long as the other party on the line was willing. More times than not, they were. Today she had her brother Tony on the phone, and if there was anyone who loved to talk on the phone as much as Joanna, it was Tony. They were the two youngest siblings out of the bunch and they felt like they had the most in common by far. Today of all days they had already been on the phone for about 2 and a half hours. Lord knows what they could have possibly been talking about for that long when they had already talked on the phone just yesterday. As they continued to go on and on, Joanna's son Michael walked through the living room on his way to the kitchen to get himself a snack. As soon as Joanna saw him she immediately offered to have Michael talk on the phone to Tony. Michael protested as long as he could, and even Tony recommended she shouldn't force it, but by the end of the back and forth, Michael ended up sitting on the phone with Tony through an awkward conversation for about 20 minutes before he was finally allowed to get his snack and go to his room.
