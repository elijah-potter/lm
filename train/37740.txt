Control Technology is often called the best module in the Technology Lab. In this module you will build objects using LEGOs that interface with a computer. LEGOs that interface with a computer! How does this work? Well that is just one of the questions that you will answer when you jump into this rotation module. You will start off learning simple program commands and downloading them to the LEGO interface. Next you will build a machine that will carry out your commands. Then you will continue to develop more advanced machines and programs that solve greater problems.
