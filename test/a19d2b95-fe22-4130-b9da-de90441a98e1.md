Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a bunny named Benny who loved to hop, jump, and play. Every morning, Benny would wiggle his nose and say, "Today is my favorite day!"

One sunny morning, Benny spotted a shiny red apple in his garden. "Mmm, what a tasty apple!" he thought. Benny picked it up and took a big, juicy bite. "Yummy!" he said happily.

Just then, Benny heard a tiny "Help me!" coming from the bushes. Benny hopped over and found a little mouse named Milo, who had fallen into a big, deep puddle. "Oh no, Milo! How can I help?" Benny asked.

Benny remembered that he had a big, soft blanket in his house. He quickly hopped back inside, grabbed the blanket, and brought it back to Milo. Benny carefully wrapped the blanket around Milo's legs and said, "Now you can float like a big, fluffy cloud!"

Milo giggled, "Thank you, Benny! I feel like I'm flying!"

Benny grinned. "You bet! Let's see if we can find more fun things to play with!"

Together, Benny and Milo played all day. They raced to the biggest, fluffiest clouds, chased butterflies, and shared their snacks. Benny ate a carrot, and Milo nibbled on a tiny piece of apple.

As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and orange, Benny and Milo said goodbye. "Goodnight, Benny," Milo whispered.

Benny said, "Goodnight, Milo. Sleep tight and dream of fun adventures tomorrow!"

They both snuggled under their blankets and fell asleep, dreaming of all the wonderful things they would do the next day.
