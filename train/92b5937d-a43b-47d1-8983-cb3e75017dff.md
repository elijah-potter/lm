I was just passing through the alley behind the old hardware store when I heard it—the unmistakable sound of breaking glass. My eyes landed on the crumpled figure slumped against the dumpster, his hands still clutching a half-empty bottle of cheap whiskey. The bottle had shattered on the pavement, and the remnants of the liquid had pooled like a dark stain in the dirt.

A few steps away, a shadow moved—someone was coming. The figure lifted his head slowly, his face pale and bloodied, but not from the cut on his chin. His eyes locked onto mine, and there was something in them I didn’t recognize: resignation or something worse. Then, the noise of approaching footsteps cut through the quiet.

The alley was empty again.
