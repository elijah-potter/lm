Scrubbing, scrubbing, with the soap.
No intruders I do hope
Will interrupt my private time.
I look around with eyes so wide.

What is this my eyes do see?
A spider hanging over me?
With eyes grown wide, I feel my heart.
Beat until it falls apart.

Soap and water are my tools,
But to kill my guest, I'd be a fool.
What if it falls and lands on me?
I know the world will hear my scream.

I finish up quick as I can,
Turn off the water with one last scan.
The spider stares as it makes its web,
I run away as courage ebbs.
