The static hummed through the cracked speakers of the abandoned warehouse, a whisper of forgotten warnings. Inside, a single flickering bulb cast long shadows over the rusted machinery. The door groaned open with a finality that sent a chill through the old man crouched by the edge of a cracked floor.

*"They’re coming,"* he muttered, fingers trembling around the half-empty bottle of whiskey. The air smelled of damp wood and old fear.

The next thing he knew, the walls were moving.
