Once upon a time in a cozy garden, there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop and play. One sunny morning, Benny noticed something shiny near his burrow. Curious, he dug a little and found a tiny golden acorn. "Wow, a golden acorn!" he said, holding it up.

Benny decided to plant it in the soft soil. As he turned the earth, a tiny sprout popped up, growing into a little tree with big, bright leaves. Benny cheered, "Look, a magic tree!"

Soon, the tree grew bigger and taller. One day, a little bird named Birdie flew by and noticed the tree. "I wonder what this tree gives," she thought. She flew back and sat on the branch.

Suddenly, the tree started to glow! Tiny stars fell down, and the ground sparkled. Benny and Birdie laughed and danced under the magical light. They shared the sparkles with all the animals in the garden: the squirrels, the ladybugs, and even the old turtle who loved to nap.

After a while, the sparkles faded, but the tree stayed strong. Benny and Birdie decided to keep the tree special. They made sure everyone in the garden got to enjoy its magic every day.

And that’s how Benny’s golden tree made everyone happy and sparkled all around!
