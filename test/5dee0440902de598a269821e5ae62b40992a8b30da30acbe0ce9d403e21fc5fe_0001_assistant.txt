Pop music first originated in the 1920s, the era of modern music where the word 'pop' was associated with particular types of music with a distinct appeal. At the same time, we saw the rise of electronic recordings, which marked this decade as the definitive year for the discovery of pop music. The word 'pop' was coined from 'popular' and it was used to describe the varying trends and tastes held by different generations and audiences. It wasn't applied till the 1950s.
