Cave of the Wind (Wind Cave, South Dakota, USA)
Layers of mineralized stalactites and stalagmites etched with ancient petroglyphs.
Petrified fossilized bones of prehistoric mammals embedded in rock formations, some still bearing faint, glowing bioluminescent traces from ancient microbes.
A submerged underground river carved with runic symbols, visible through translucent quartz veins.
Ephemeral cave paintings depicting long-extinct tribes’ burial rites, now partially faded but still legible in ultraviolet light.
The ruins of a 19th-century lighthouse perched on a cliff, its wooden framework still intact despite 150 years of erosion, with graffiti from early explorers still visible.
