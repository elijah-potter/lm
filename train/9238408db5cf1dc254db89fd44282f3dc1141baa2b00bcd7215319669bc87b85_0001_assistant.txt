Here are three stops in Two Rivers to check out:
1. Two Harbors Lighthouse: Visit this historic light station including a fog-signaling building, a skiff home, an oil house, a garage, a lighthouse tower, and an assistant keeper's home.
2. Iona's Beach Scientific and Natural Area: Walk along a pebbled beach that is filled with pink felsite and rhyolite pebbles.
3. Hellacious Overlook: Also known as the Palisade Head, this 350-foot cliff is made of volcanic rock and overlooks Lake Superior.
