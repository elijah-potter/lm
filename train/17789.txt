Hyrule Compendium Entry
Traveler's Bows are weak ranged Weapons commonly used by travelers for protection. Traveler's Bows are compatible with all types of Arrows except for Light Arrows. Traveler's Bows are made of wood, and are therefore flammable and not magnetic. When dropped into water, Traveler's Bows will float.
Traveler's Bows belong to the Traveler's archetype of equipment, which are all said to be used to defend travelers throughout Hyrule. A Traveler's Bow is part of an unnamed set of equipment used by Link in promotional media, alongside a Soldier's Broadsword and a Traveler's Shield.
