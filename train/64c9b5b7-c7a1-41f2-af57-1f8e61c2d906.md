A man bowed deeply before a stranger at a crowded market, his voice trembling as he stammered, *"Excuse me—may I ask where the nearest—"* His face flushed crimson as he realized his mistake—he had mistaken the vendor’s dog for the owner. The dog, a scruffy terrier, barked in response, nearly knocking him over. The vendor grinned, tossing a coin to the animal with a laugh. *"Politeness lost you here,"* the dog seemed to say.
