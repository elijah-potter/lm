**Scene 1: The Library**
A lone librarian, Elias, scans a book titled *The Echo of Lost Voices* under flickering fluorescent lights. His fingers tremble as he turns the page—the ink smudges like tears.

**Scene 2: The Forest**
In the heart of the Blackwood, a child named Liora finds a gnarled root shaped like a mouth. Inside, the air hums with whispers. She presses her ear to it—voices spill from the earth itself, repeating her own name.

**Scene 3: The Clock Tower**
A clock strikes midnight. Across the city, a clockmaker’s apprentice, Mara, watches as the hands freeze at 11:59. A mirror cracks behind her—its reflection swallows her whole, leaving only a hollow echo.

**Scene 4: The Ship’s Cabin**
Captain Elias Veyne sits in the bow of *The Drowned Dawn*, staring at the horizon. His journal lies open: *"They all speak in tongues. Some are mine. Some are not."* A shadow slides into the cabin—his father’s ghost, whispering, *"You were never alone."*

---
**The threads weave:**
Elias, the librarian, once saved Liora from the forest; Mara’s broken mirror was his first reflection; the clockmaker’s apprentice was his apprentice, too. The whispers? His own name, caught in the spaces between them. The story unfolds like a paradox—*the same event, different eyes.*
