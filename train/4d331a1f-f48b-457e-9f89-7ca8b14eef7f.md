A mid-afternoon office cubicle hummed with the monotone of a printer jamming, while the fluorescent lights flickered over the heads of employees typing away. The sudden, high-pitched *cackle*—a mix of embarrassment and delight—echoed from the hallway, followed by the muffled *"Oh my God!"* from a meeting room door. The sound of a stapler being violently shaken, then a voice shouting *"Oh, you guys!"* drifted through the open window of the break room, where a group of coworkers had gathered around a table, their faces flushed with shared amusement. One individual, clutching a half-eaten sandwich, grinned, revealing a toothpaste stain on their lip. The laughter faded into laughter, then into awkward silence as the room’s mood shifted from tension to sudden warmth. A single desk lamp’s shade swayed slightly as someone laughed again, the sound dissolving into chatter about the source: a video call gone wrong with a cat on the screen.
