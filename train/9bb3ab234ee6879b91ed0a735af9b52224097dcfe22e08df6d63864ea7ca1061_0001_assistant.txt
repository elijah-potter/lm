Interviewer: Hello, Barney! Thanks for agreeing to do this interview. You’ll give us a feline’s perspective on popular topics, so let’s jump in! The whole country wants to know: what do you think about dogs? 

Barney: Oh right…dogs. Yikes, am I right? They’re pretty annoying, slobbering everywhere and chase me from my favorite napspots. But, there are some perks, believe it or not. Since those do-gooders are always begging for attention, the humans of the house have been pestering me a lot less! Also, I guess I can be a little more relaxed when the dog is about because they perk up at the slightest strange noise. It’s nice to feel protected. I want to clarify, though, that I really don’t like dogs.

Interviewer: Woah, that’s insightful. Now, what about mice? What are your thoughts?

Barney: Mice! I used to think they were the most delectable morsels in the world, but then my humans gave me this stuff from a can. Now let me tell you, that stuff is better than mice and less messy and barbaric too. I like those fake mice my human brings home, so I can still keep my skills sharp! Get it! Like my claws?

Interviewer: Hilarious, yes. Well, it seems you have a great balance between how you feel towards other creatures in the house. I want to thank you for sharing your thoughts on this, Barney!

Barney: Sure, it’s no problem. Nice to give people what they want at least once a year. Now, if we’re done here, some nice sunrays are coming through my human’s curtains. If I don’t claw at them, I can nap there. Goodbye for now!
