Gas water heater
أ.د. السيد صابر
Gas water heater is a tank less gas continuous flow through hot water heater works () when a hot water tap is opened; a sensor detects the water flow. When the flow through hot water heater is working, the gas burner fires and the heat exchanger works to heat the water. A thermostat works to regulate the amount of gas that goes to the flow through hot water heater burner, maintaining the pre-set water temperature. A Small scale gas water heater (2 Lit/min) is made for the daily use. It consists of gas tank, burner, coil, pipes, inlet and outlet valves, thermostat and insulation.
