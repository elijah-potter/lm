A hollow vow left unbroken by the weight of unseen burdens,
words carved in dust where time eroded their meaning.
The clock ticked faster than the hand holding the pledge—
some promises are not meant to be kept, but whispered
only to the hollow places where the heart forgets how to hold its own weight.

The air grew thick with the ghost of the words left unspoken,
each syllable a promise buried beneath the weight of
regret and the quiet hum of the unraveling.
