The creature is covered in fur from head to toe. It walks on four legs that have things called "paws" at the ends of them. Sometimes, their ears are floppy, and sometimes they are perky. The creature has a tail it moves from side to side when it is happy - an action called "wagging." The creature makes a loud, sometimes yippy noise called "barking."  The creature has a long tongue that it uses to drink water and sharp teeth that it uses to eat food and chew on bones. The creature is domesticated and often lives inside homes with humans.
