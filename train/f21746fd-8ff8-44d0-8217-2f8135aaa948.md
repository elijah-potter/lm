The local bakery owner reported losing their prized vanilla bean cake recipe after the "neighborhood gossip queen" (Mrs. Delgado) claimed it was stolen by the new guy, Carlos, who moved in last month. Turns out, Carlos had been secretly sourcing ingredients from the same wholesale supplier as the bakery. He’d been trying to recreate it for weeks but hadn’t yet perfected the exact balance. The bakery’s chef, realizing the recipe was flawed anyway, offered to share it with Carlos in exchange for a free monthly dessert for their new café. The café’s first batch was praised by the entire block.
