Step 1: Learn the rules of basketball.

Most of the rules of unicycle basketball are the same as regular basketball. For example, you can't hit other players, the ball must be dribbled, and shots from different areas give varying points although the three-point zone is worth 4 points.

Step 2: Get a regulation unicycle.

To level the playing field, you'll need a unicycle with a 25.2-inch wheel, and the body or other parts of the unit must be free of sharp parts that could injure other players.

Step 3: Find a local club or team to practice with!

There are a few regional teams that compete in tournaments, and these teams may be willing to show you the ropes. Otherwise, you can practice with friends or other players who are interested in Unicycle Basketball.
