Since Mother's Day is in May, it's important to consider what produce is in season. Asparagus, peas, berries, and assorted types of lettuce all become more available in most locations by May. It is important to pay attention to the number of people that a recipe will serve so that you have enough food for the number of people attending. One possible menu might be:

A platter of parboiled assorted vegetables with a herbed aïoli. Roasted chicken and fresh bread and butter could be served with this meal. You could include sugar snap peas, asparagus, carrots, early potatoes, and other fresh vegetables, such as celery on the platter. There are many recipes for herb-based aïolis available. 

Another menu if the weather is cool or overcast might include:

A vegetable soup that can either be broth or cream based. This could be served with bread and butter and a green salad using seasonal vegetables.

A third menu might include: 

A European-style meat and cheese platter served and French rolls to make sandwiches could be added to the platter. Dill and sweet pickles, olives, and pickled vegetables, such as peppers would all be good additions. This meal could be served with pita chips and hummus.

Fresh strawberries and other seasonal berries with sweet biscuits and homemade whipped cream could be served as a dessert for any of these three menus. These menus will provide a moderately priced meal that is interesting and enjoyable, for those who are vegetarians, as well as those who are not.
