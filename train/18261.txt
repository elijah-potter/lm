Electrician plays a vital role in our lives and we can’t even imagine running our life smoothly without the need of an electrician. Some of the key roles of an electrician are described below: It’s really tough to imagine our day to day life without an electrician. Because now day’s people are so dependent on the electrical sources a little power cut makes people panic at the very instant. From electricity, we use the internet, watch television, microwave meals and much more. It is very challenging to adjust to a power cut for a few hours. The electricians work to ensure that whenever this happens, they are always there to help restore the power and get it back on track. There are a few top roles of the electricians:
- Read technical diagrams or blueprints.
- Install and maintain lighting systems, wiring, and control.
- Inspect the electrical components such as circuit breakers and transformers.
- Identify electrical problems with different testing devices.
- Repair or replace equipment, wiring using hand tools and other power tools.
- Follow the local building regulations.
- Train other workers to maintain, install and repair wiring.
Electrical installation is easily accessible during construction but it’s sometimes difficult to identify and repair broken equipment and wiring. Electricians read blueprints of electrical systems that basically show the location of the circuits. With the help of the different hand tools and other power tools to test and find the problems and ensure that the components are working fine.
Many Electrician works alone while some collaborate with others. Like experienced electricians may work with the engineers and architects for new construction. At large companies, electricians work as crew part.
There are basically two types of electricians:
- Inside Electricians
- Residential Electricians
Inside Electricians: They maintain and repair large equipment, motors, and other control systems in factories and businesses. With their good knowledge of electrical systems, they help these firms to run efficiently and safely. Some of the electricians install wiring for the factories and businesses. The electricians perform scheduled maintenance to minimize equipment failure. For any recent purchases of new equipment, the electrician is required to ensure the proper installation of the equipment. His responsibility is not only to solve the electrical problems but to perform multiple roles depending on the employer’s requirement.
Residential Electricians: These electricians troubleshoot other electrical problems and install wiring in people’s homes. They work in new home construction, provide access to power where needed and install outlets. The contractors who work in the maintenance typically repair and replace faulty equipment. For instance, if a circuit trip in multiple variations even after being resets, the electricians looks out for a cause and fix it as soon as possible.
Training and Education of an Electrician:
Most of the electricians started their career by going to school and focus on the technical industry. They have been taught there about blueprints, electrical theories, and other local building codes and mathematics. After graduation, they move to become an apprentice of four years. During this apprenticeship program, they are not allowed to work independently and also not allowed to provide their service to the public. After the completion of their apprenticeship, they will start to apply for the permanent working license so that they would work without any supervision. After getting the license they become a certified and skilled professional electrician.
Almost all the electricians work full time which includes both evening and weekends. During any scheduled maintenance, the inside electricians can expect to work overtime. Overtime is what common in the construction area because in that casemeeting deadlines is critical.
