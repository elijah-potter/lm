## Product Requirements Document: “Contextualize” - Mobile Knowledge Synthesis App

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently grapple with fragmented information scattered across various platforms, hindering efficient comprehension and synthesis, ultimately impacting productivity and innovation.

**Vision:** To empower knowledge workers to effortlessly connect disparate information sources, fostering deeper understanding and accelerating the generation of actionable insights.

---

### User Personas

**1. Dr. Anya Sharma - Research Scientist:**

*   **Goal:** To rapidly synthesize findings from multiple research papers, conference proceedings, and internal reports to identify gaps in knowledge and inform her team’s research direction.
*   **Pain Points:**  Time-consuming manual collation of information; difficulty tracking the relationships between different sources; overwhelming volume of data; lack of a central repository for synthesized knowledge.

**2. Mark Olsen - Strategic Consultant:**

*   **Goal:** To quickly grasp the nuances of a new client's industry and competitive landscape from a diverse range of documents, including market reports, financial statements, and competitor websites.
*   **Pain Points:**  Difficulty identifying key themes and trends across different document formats; inefficient process of cross-referencing information; struggles to maintain a coherent understanding of complex business environments.

**3. Eleanor Vance - Marketing Manager:**

*   **Goal:** To consolidate customer feedback from surveys, social media, and sales reports to identify patterns and inform marketing campaign strategy.
*   **Pain Points:**  Information silos between departments; challenges in analyzing unstructured data; difficulty translating data into actionable marketing strategies; inability to quickly react to emerging trends.

---

### Requirements

**1. Source Integration:** The application must allow users to import documents from cloud storage services (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive) and directly from URLs.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** User can successfully import a PDF document from Google Drive. User can successfully import a webpage via URL. Supported file types include PDF, DOCX, TXT, and HTML.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 80% of users successfully import at least one document within the first session.

**2. Contextual Linking:** The application must automatically identify and suggest connections between concepts and entities across imported documents.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** The application flags at least three potential connections between concepts within a set of three imported documents. Suggested connections are relevant to the document content.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 60% of suggested connections are marked as relevant by users.

**3. Synthesis Workspace:** The application must provide a visual workspace where users can organize imported documents, annotate content, and create custom connections.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** User can drag and drop documents within the workspace. User can add free-form text notes to individual documents. User can create custom links between documents using a simple graphical interface.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 70% of users create at least one custom link within the first week of use.

**4. Summarization & Key Phrase Extraction:** The application must automatically generate concise summaries of individual documents and extract key phrases for rapid comprehension.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Summaries accurately reflect the core content of the original document. Extracted key phrases are demonstrably significant within the document.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 75% of users find the generated summaries helpful for understanding the document’s content.

**5. Export & Sharing:** The application must allow users to export synthesized knowledge as structured reports (PDF, Markdown) and share them with collaborators.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** User can export the workspace content as a PDF report. Exported reports maintain the visual structure of the workspace. User can share the report via email and direct link.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 40% of users share a synthesized report within the first month of use.



---

### Technical Considerations

*   **Scalability:** The application architecture must accommodate a growing number of users and documents.
*   **Data Security:**  Robust security measures are essential to protect user data and intellectual property. Encryption at rest and in transit are mandatory.
*   **Natural Language Processing (NLP):**  Reliance on external NLP services necessitates careful consideration of latency and cost.  Caching mechanisms should be implemented.
*   **Cross-Platform Compatibility:** Initial development will focus on iOS and Android.  Web accessibility is a future consideration.
*   **Offline Functionality:**  Limited offline access to imported documents is desirable.



### Risks

*   **NLP Accuracy:**  Inaccurate or irrelevant connection suggestions could frustrate users.
*   **User Adoption:**  Requires a shift in workflow that may be challenging for some users.
*   **Data Privacy Concerns:**  User apprehension regarding data security and privacy could impede adoption.
*   **Competitive Landscape:**  Existing note-taking and knowledge management tools present a significant challenge.



### Non-Goals

*   Real-time collaboration features (e.g., shared workspaces).
*   Integration with all possible cloud storage services.
*   Optical Character Recognition (OCR) for scanned documents (initially).
*   Automated translation of documents.
*   Advanced search capabilities beyond keyword search.




---

### Launch Checklist (First Six Actions)

1.  Deploy initial iOS and Android builds to a closed beta group (10-15 users).
2.  Establish a dedicated feedback channel for beta testers.
3.  Implement robust crash reporting and performance monitoring.
4.  Prioritize and address critical bug fixes reported by beta testers.
5.  Develop comprehensive user documentation and FAQs.
6.  Prepare marketing materials outlining core value proposition and key features.
