• Clueless - A popular, rich high school girl gives another student a makeover to help her become more popular. In the process, she learns about love and life. 
• 10 Things I Hate About You - In an attempt to win over a popular girl, a boy hires another student to date her sister. 
• She's the Man - A female star athlete pretends to be her brother to play soccer for the school team and ends up falling for one of her teammates. 
• How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days -  For an article, a journalist dates a man with the sole purpose of getting him to break up with her in 10 days. 
• The Notebook - Set in the 1940s, a girl from a rich family falls in love with a poor boy in North Carolina, much to the chagrin of her family.
