Once upon a time in a cozy, sunny garden, there was a little red apple that loved to dance in the breeze. One day, it found a tiny blue butterfly sitting near a flower.

"Hello, little friend!" chirped the apple.

The butterfly fluttered its wings. "Hi! Are you hungry?"

The apple giggled. "I’m full, but I love sharing!" It bit off a tiny piece and handed it to the butterfly.

The butterfly took the piece and fluttered away, leaving a tiny trail of sparkles in the air. Soon, it landed on a big, juicy strawberry. "Yum!" it said happily.

The apple watched from a distance, smiling. A kind breeze carried the butterfly and the strawberry to a little cloud that floated in the sky. There, the butterfly shared its strawberry with all the clouds, making them giggle with joy.

As the sun set, painting the sky in pink and orange, the apple and the butterfly became friends forever. They danced together under the stars, their adventures never ending. And all the little friends in the garden knew—being kind and sharing brings the sweetest happiness!
