OK, I can categorize three sections of storing produce.

Room temperature - These fruits and veggies can be stored at room temperature. They do not need to be refrigerated:
- Banana
- Cucumber 
- Eggplant
- Grapefruit
- Green beans
- Lemon
- Limes
- Onions
- Oranges
- Plantains
- Potato
- Squash
- Sweet Potato
- Watermelon
- Zucchini

Room Temperature and then refrigerated - start on the counter and then move these foods to the fridge when they are ripe:
- Apricot
- Avocado
- Jicama - once cut, store in the fridge
- Kiwi
- Mangoes
- Melons
- Nectarines
- Papayas
- Peaches
- Pears
- Pineapple
- Plums
- Star Fruit

Refrigerated - these fruits and veggies should be kept in the refrigerator until you are ready to use them:
- Apples
- Artichokes
- Asparagus
- Beets
- Blackberries
- Blueberries
- Broccoli
- Brussels Sprouts
- Cabbage
- Carrots
- Cauliflower
- Celery
- Cherries
- Corn on the cob
- Grapes
- Leafy Greens
- Leeks
- Mushrooms
- Peas
- Peppers
- Pomegranate
- Radishes
- Raspberries
- Strawberries
- Turnips
