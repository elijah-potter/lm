**Incident Response Policy for Non-Compliance with Company Safety Protocols**

1. **Immediate Action**:
   - **Employee Notification**: Suspended from all duties until further review. Directly report to HR and management with full incident details.
   - **On-Site Response**: Security/loss prevention team will secure the area. Evidence collected for forensic analysis.

2. **Investigation**:
   - **Timeline**: Full investigation within **48 hours** by HR and legal teams.
   - **Evidence Review**: All digital records (emails, logs, CCTV, etc.) and physical evidence analyzed.
   - **Witnesses**: Compile statements from all relevant parties (employees, managers, contractors).

3. **Consequences**:
   - **First Offense**:
     - Mandatory **30-day leave** with pay.
     - Required **safety training** (refresher or new hire) with certification.
     - Suspension of access to company systems (email, IT tools).
   - **Repeat Offense** (within 12 months):
     - **Termination of employment** with immediate severance (if applicable per local labor law).
     - Civil litigation may be pursued for breach of duty of care.

4. **Support & Accountability**:
   - **Mental Health**: Mandatory counseling sessions provided at company expense.
   - **Peer Accountability**: Mandatory peer review with HR within 10 business days.
   - **Escalation Path**: If misconduct is confirmed, report to **corporate legal** for potential disciplinary action up to termination.

5. **Communication**:
   - **Internal**: HR will notify affected teams and managers in writing.
   - **Public**: Only after legal review (if incident involves third parties or public safety).
