A cracked neon sign flickered weakly against the rain-soaked alley, its red letters barely legible: *"The Lost Relic"*. The rain drummed against the pavement as **Elias** crouched beside the abandoned dumpster, fingers brushing the rusted latch. Inside, a tarnished silver key glinted dully beneath the dim glow of a flickering streetlamp.

His breath hitched. The key—old, worn, and unremarkable—had been discarded years ago, forgotten in the chaos of a city that never remembered him. Yet, as he turned it, the mechanism inside *clicked* with a sound like distant thunder.

A low, guttural voice crackled from the key’s hollow core: *"You’ve found what was lost."* The alley darkened further, the sign’s glow now a flicker of static. Elias’ pulse roared in his ears. The key was no longer just metal and rust—it was a bridge. A bridge to something buried beneath the city’s forgotten streets.
