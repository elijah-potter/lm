Published in 2014, a paper entitled, “Dehydration melting at the top of the lower mantle,” detailed the discovery by scientists that there is water stored inside mantle rock beneath the ocean, approximately 400 miles underground. Geophysicist Steve Jacobsen described the water stored there as being in a sponge-like state in a mineral rock called ringwoodite. It is estimated that there is three times as much water below the surface as above it.
