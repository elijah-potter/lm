The library’s quiet hum was broken by the creak of the old floorboards as Liam pressed his forehead against the glass display case. Inside, the book he’d been searching for—a worn copy of *The Giver* with its faded spine—had been moved. His stomach dropped. *They took it.*

His phone buzzed in his pocket. A text from his best friend, Mateo: *"You okay? I saw you here all day."*

Liam sighed. *"Just… bored. Again."*

---

The next morning, the school’s morning announcements started with the usual—principals’ speeches, weather updates—but then came the news: *"Attention, students. Due to unforeseen circumstances, today’s class will be conducted via a new… alternative learning system."* The principal’s voice echoed through the empty halls.

Liam’s hands trembled. *Alternative learning system?* That wasn’t what he’d heard before.

---

The classroom was different. Walls were lined with holographic projections, and a central screen pulsed with swirling colors. His teacher, Ms. Rivera, stood at the front, her hands raised. *"Today, you’ll learn by solving. But first—you must earn your place. The system will assign you challenges based on your skills. Prove you’re ready to grow."*

Liam’s mind raced. *Prove myself?* He’d always been good at math, but this felt… too big.

---

The first challenge was a puzzle: a maze of numbers and symbols. The screen flickered, and a voice—smooth, synthetic—spoke: *"Choose wisely. Your next steps depend on this."*

Liam’s fingers hovered over the touchpad. He picked the path that led to the exit. The screen flashed green. *"Congratulations. Proceed to the next challenge."*

---

By lunch, Liam had unlocked three more. His body ached, but his mind buzzed. He wasn’t sure what was real or what was just the system’s way of testing him. But as he sat at the cafeteria table, Mateo leaned in. *"What’s going on? You’re acting weird."*

Liam swallowed. *"I dunno. Just… everything’s different."*

---

The final challenge came at sunset. The screen showed a mountain range, a path winding through it, and a single word: *"Choose."*

Liam hesitated. The path was steep, the air cold, but the top promised… something. A light. A way out.

He took the climb.

---

When he emerged, the sky was clear, and beneath him stretched a vast, endless plain. His breath came in ragged gasps. Then he saw it—a city, rising from the horizon, its lights like stars on a dark sky.

Ms. Rivera stood beside him, her expression unreadable. *"You passed, Liam. But remember—this is just the beginning."*

The wind carried the scent of pine and something electric. He wasn’t sure if it was fear or wonder, but he knew one thing for sure: he wasn’t going back to normal.
