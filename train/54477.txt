Who designed the American Flag?
According to popular stories, the first American flag was made by Betsy Ross, a Philadelphia seamstress who was acquainted with George Washington. In May 1776, so the story goes, General Washington and two people from the Continental Congress saw Ross at her furniture store as well as showed her a rough design of the flag.
In those days, the flag was not the same as we recognize it today. The act stated, “Resolved, that the flag of the United States be thirteen stripes, alternate red and white; that the Union be thirteen stars, white in a blue field, standing for a new constellation.”
