Question #1: Who was the ancient Chinese philosopher and poet who was well-known for writing the book "Tao Te Ching"?
Answer #1: Lao Tzu was the Chinese philosopher and poet who wrote "Tao Te Ching." He is also largely respected as a religious deity in various religious schools of thought. He was also the founder of Taoism.

Question #2: How did Lao Tzu's journey in Tibet lead to his writing of the book "Tao Te Ching?"
Answer #2: On Lao Tzu's journey to what is currently Tibet he had a sad reaction to what was going on around him. There were men diverted away from nature and all its goodness. A guard that Lao Tzu met on his trip asked him to write down his teachings on that trip. The 5,000-character account of his thoughts on that journey became the "Tao Te Ching."

Question #3: What kinds of writing styles did Lao Tzu employ in the "Tao Te Ching?"
Answer #3: In his book, Lao Tzu used rhyme and rhythm, paradoxes, and analogies. Some have even considered the book to be one great analogy.

Question #4: What does the title of the book mean and what is its purpose?
Answer #4: "Tao Te Ching" means "The Way and Its Power." The idea is that the Tao is the end-all and be-all of existence. It is powerful, but down to earth. The book tries to guide people on how to return to the ways and laws of nature to maintain the balance of the Tao.

Question #5: What role did Lao Tzu play in Taoism?
Answer #5: Lao Tzu is the father of Taoism, which is one of the three pillars of ancient Chinese thought. Taoism is both a philosophy and an organized religion and Lao Tzu's teachings encompassed the depths of both.

Question #6: Explain the concept of Tao and what it means.
Answer #6: Tao is also known as "the path." However, the term is not exclusive to Taoism. It appears in other Chinese religious customs, but the path plays an important role in Taoism. Tao is both the cause and effect of every living being in the world.

Question #7: What are some of the highlights of Lao Tzu's philosophical beliefs?
Answer #7: Lao Tzu believed that struggling is futile and harms productivity. He believed in the theory of "wu-wei," in which he advises others to do nothing. One should just wait for the events that nature brings to you rather than go against the forces of nature. Following the natural order of things, while still being spontaneous, was most important. Taoists believe that striving for nothing will never fail. Someone who has never failed is always successful and eventually powerful.

Question #8: Did Taoism influence other things in society?
Answer #8: At the center of Taoism, the idea of doing nothing and achieving everything affected the way the kings treated the general public. Because of this, many think that Taoism helped shaped political philosophy as well.

Question: #9: Is Taoism influential today?
Answer #9: Lao Tzu's writings continue to influence individuals and anti-authoritarian campaigns worldwide. Lao Tzu, known as "Old Teacher," put a strong emphasis on the ways of nature and how sometimes doing nothing can have the largest impact on everything.
