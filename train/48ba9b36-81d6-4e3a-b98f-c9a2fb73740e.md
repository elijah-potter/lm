## Product Requirements Document: "Contextualize" - Mobile Knowledge Consolidation

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently juggle disparate information sources—documents, emails, meeting notes, web pages—leading to fragmented understanding, duplicated effort, and diminished productivity.

**Vision:** To be the indispensable mobile companion that empowers knowledge workers to seamlessly connect and contextualize their information, fostering clarity and accelerating insight.

---

### User Personas

**1. Eleanor Vance, Senior Marketing Manager:**

*   **Goals:** To quickly synthesize information from various sources (market research reports, competitor analysis, campaign performance data) to inform strategic decisions and present compelling narratives to leadership. To minimize time spent searching for and reassembling scattered information.
*   **Pain Points:** Feels overwhelmed by the volume of information; struggles to connect data points across different platforms; frequently loses track of key findings; spends excessive time recreating summaries for presentations.

**2. David Chen, Research Scientist:**

*   **Goals:** To efficiently organize and link research papers, lab notes, experimental data, and conference presentations to facilitate discovery and accelerate analysis. To easily share findings with collaborators while maintaining a clear audit trail of information provenance.
*   **Pain Points:** Information silos across different research tools; difficulty tracking the evolution of research ideas; challenges in sharing complex data sets with colleagues; concerns about intellectual property protection.

**3. Maria Rodriguez, Consultant:**

*   **Goals:** To rapidly capture and organize client meeting notes, project documentation, and relevant industry articles to ensure consistent project execution and deliver exceptional client service. To maintain a centralized repository of knowledge accessible across devices.
*   **Pain Points:** Reliant on multiple, disconnected note-taking apps; struggles to connect meeting discussions to supporting documentation; risks overlooking critical details due to information fragmentation; desires a secure and portable knowledge base.

---

### Requirements

**1. Contextual Linking:** Users can create direct links between different content items (documents, web pages, notes) within the app, accompanied by optional user-defined annotations.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Links persist across app updates; annotations are clearly displayed alongside linked content; users can edit or delete links and annotations.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 75% of users create at least three links within the first week of use; average number of links per user increases by 10% month-over-month for the first three months.

**2. Smart Content Summarization:** The app automatically generates concise summaries of linked documents and web pages, highlighting key findings and action items.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Summaries accurately reflect the content of the linked source; users can edit and refine automatically generated summaries; summaries are accessible offline.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 60% of users utilize the summarization feature at least once per week; user satisfaction with summary accuracy, as measured by in-app feedback, averages 4.0 out of 5.0.

**3. Cross-Platform Synchronization:** User data and linked content are seamlessly synchronized across iOS and Android devices, ensuring accessibility and consistency.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Synchronization occurs within five seconds on a stable network connection; data integrity is maintained during synchronization; users can view synchronization status and resolve conflicts.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 99% of users experience successful synchronization within the first 24 hours; data loss or corruption during synchronization is less than 0.01%.

**4. Secure Content Storage:** User data and linked content are stored securely using industry-standard encryption protocols, both in transit and at rest.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** User data is encrypted using AES-256 encryption; app adheres to relevant data privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR, CCPA); users can enable two-factor authentication.
    *   **Success Metrics:** Zero reported data breaches or security vulnerabilities within the first six months; 80% of users enable two-factor authentication within the first month.

**5. Customizable Content Views:** Users can organize and display linked content using customizable views (e.g., Kanban board, timeline, mind map) to facilitate different workflows.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can create, edit, and delete custom views; views are responsive and adaptable to different screen sizes; users can share views with other users (future phase).
    *   **Success Metrics:** 40% of users create at least one custom view within the first two weeks; average time spent navigating and organizing content increases by 15% for users utilizing custom views.

---

### Technical Considerations

*   **Cloud Infrastructure:** Leverage a scalable cloud platform (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud) for data storage and content delivery.
*   **API Integrations:** Design a flexible API architecture to enable future integrations with popular document management systems, note-taking applications, and web browsers.
*   **Mobile SDKs:** Utilize native iOS and Android SDKs to ensure optimal performance and user experience.
*   **Offline Capabilities:** Prioritize offline access to linked content and custom views.

### Risks

*   **Content Source Compatibility:** Ensuring compatibility with a wide range of document formats and web page structures may prove challenging.
*   **User Adoption:** Convincing users to adopt a new knowledge management tool requires a compelling value proposition and intuitive user interface.
*   **Scalability:** Maintaining performance and reliability as the user base grows necessitates careful architectural planning.

### Non-Goals

*   Real-time collaboration features (e.g., shared editing of linked content).
*   Direct content creation within the app (focus is on linking and contextualizing existing content).
*   Support for desktop applications in the initial release.
*   Integration with social media platforms.
