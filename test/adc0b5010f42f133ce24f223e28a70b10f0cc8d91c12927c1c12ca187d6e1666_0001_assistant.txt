According to the source, you need some tokens to earn a dollar. If you’ve been an upstanding Redditor, your 33.22 karma score is nowadays worth some money, but that number isn’t fixed. In October 2020, users only needed 1.71 karma to earn a dollar’s worth. Last month, eager crypto Redditors needed 69.01 karma to get that dollar.

You’ve been able to convert some things to cash for about a year, but you’ll have to move them from their current location to a new location. It’s not easy. 

That’s because the r/CryptoCurrency tokens are being tested and are designed for use within the subreddit. Redditors are encouraged to use tokens to purchase special memberships to the subreddit. 

With the membership, users can add GIFs to comments, buy special badges and awards, tip other users, and participate in polls to improve the subreddit, where a member’s clout is weighted by the tokens they’ve earned.

The supply of this is capped at 250 million tokens. In October, the subreddit initially distributed 50 million of these—the high number factored karma earned up to that point. Another 5 million were released the next month, and the distribution reduces by 2.5% each month. 

There are other tokens you can earn through Reddit. Visitors to r/Fortnite, a subreddit dedicated to the Battle Royale game, can earn sub-specific tokens each month with their karma.
