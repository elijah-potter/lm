The café’s fluorescent hum swirled with the ghost of another time—a stranger’s coffee, the same brown as yesterday’s, left behind on the saucer. The barista’s hands, swift and practiced, slid the cup toward me, but my fingers hesitated. It was already half-full, the first sip of someone else’s morning, the warmth of their breath lingering in the mug’s shadow. The clock on the wall ticked like a metronome, counting the seconds until the memory of this moment would dissolve into the past, replaced by another borrowed moment in a life that had no claim on it.
