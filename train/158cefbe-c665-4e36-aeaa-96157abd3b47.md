Emerging urban hub in the former industrial district of **New Haven Heights**, where old warehouses are repurposed into micro-apartments and cafés. Solar-paneled rooftops dot the skyline, and electric car charging stations line the repaved streets. A community garden occupies the former factory lot, with local farmers selling produce at a new farmers' market. The **Riverfront District** features a revitalized dock area with floating restaurants and a new pedestrian bridge spanning the canal, connecting the downtown core to the historic waterfront. Local startups in green tech and sustainable design occupy the repurposed shipyards, attracting young professionals and remote workers. The area buzzes with bike lanes, urban farms, and co-working spaces, blending old-world charm with cutting-edge sustainability.
