A lone figure in a sleek, black coat strides past a flickering streetlamp, their shadow elongating against the neon glow of an abandoned café. The door slams behind them with a sound like breaking glass. The café’s last customer, a silhouette hunched over a laptop, blinks at the empty space where the stranger once stood. The air hums with silence—except for the distant wail of a passing siren, a sound that echoes the abruptness of their departure. The street beyond is quiet, save for the occasional clink of a distant coffee cup, forgotten in a booth. No farewell. No explanation. Just the weight of a life carried out in silence.
