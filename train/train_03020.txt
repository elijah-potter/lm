Janelle had 26 green marbles. Then she bought 6 bags of blue marbles. There were 10 marbles in each bag.  She created a gift of 6 green marbles and 8 blue marbles and gave it to a friend. How many marbles does Janelle have now?
Janelle has 6 bags * 10 blue marbles = <<6*10=60>>60 marbles.
Janelle has a total of 26 green marbles + 60 blue marbles = <<26+60=86>>86 marbles.
Janelle gave away a gift of 6 green + 8 blue marbles = <<6+8=14>>14 marbles.
Janelle has a final amount of 86 marbles - 14 marbles = <<86-14=72>>72 marbles.
