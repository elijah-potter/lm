Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white fur and big, round ears that twitched when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his favorite thing: a big, juicy carrot. He hopped outside, and the world looked even brighter with the warm sunlight.

Benny met his friend, Max the mouse, who was playing with his little siblings. "Hello, Benny!" said Max. "What are you doing today?"

"Oh, I just want to eat my carrot!" Benny said, nibbling happily.

The garden was full of colors: red roses, green leaves, and yellow daisies. Benny loved to dance around, his paws making little circles in the soft grass. Max and his siblings giggled as Benny hopped and twirled.

Suddenly, Benny spotted something shiny in the dirt. It looked like a tiny, round rock. "What’s this?" Benny asked, picking it up.

Max smiled. "That’s a treasure, Benny! Let’s find out what it is."

They dug a little, and there it was—a tiny golden key! Benny’s eyes sparkled. "I wonder what it opens!"

Just then, a little bird named Pip chirped, "Follow me!" Pip led them to a small, hidden tree where a basket was tied with a string. Inside, Benny found a pile of soft, fluffy seeds and a tiny, shiny box.

"Open it!" Benny said, excited. The box had a picture of a big, colorful flower. "That’s our special flower!" Benny whispered.

Benny, Max, and Pip planted the seeds in the garden, and soon, a beautiful flower grew tall and proud. The sun shone on it, and it bloomed with colors Benny had never seen before.

From that day on, Benny, Max, and Pip became the best of friends. They planted flowers, shared treats, and made the garden the happiest place in the whole world. Every day was a new adventure, and every adventure was filled with love and laughter.

And that, my little friend, is the story of Benny’s big, happy garden!
