The committee faced ambiguous risk data when approving the expansion of Project Horizon. Estimates for Q4 2024 market penetration varied between 15% and 40%, with no consensus on the critical 22% threshold. The data lacked granularity on competitor responses or localized demand shifts, leading to conflicting stakeholder opinions.

Final decision: Approve with conditional clauses requiring a 10% contingency reserve and a mid-Q3 reassessment based on pilot results. The plan prioritizes operational flexibility to adjust if early metrics fall below 25% penetration.
