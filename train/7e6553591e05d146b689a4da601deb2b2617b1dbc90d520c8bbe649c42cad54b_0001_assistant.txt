The Ruinwood -  a dense jungle filled with verdant and dangerous life - sprawls before you. The humidity is almost unbearable under the high canopy. Noises echo and reverberate through the trees giving an omnipresent sense of foreboding. No doubt beasts and malefic monstrosities abound. Deeper still, there is a point where the winding Wyrm River changes directions. Rumors have reached you of a fallen settlement near this point on the river where a village of serpentine-human hybrid creatures now takes residence. This is the Ruinwood before you. How would you like to begin?
