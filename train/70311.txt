23. “A Philosopher’s History of the Rebellion,” Harper’s New Monthly Magazine (June 1867): 113-18.
Excerpts from History of the American Civil War by John William Draper, Professor of Chemistry and Physiology at the University of New York. A majority of the excerpts deal with black Africans in Africa as compared to black slaves in the South.
