At midnight’s toll, a scary feline stalked
A teeny tiny mouse lies in her sight
During her stalk, the hungry cat mocked
Thinking the mouse would be a tasty bite
And our dear mouse was ever deathly scared
That his heart pounded as his feet scurried
But lo, he found his house a tattered mess
Having nowhere else to go and make his nest!
He ran over counters and tables, stressed
To find a new home and luck expressed
Away from the frightening cat’s embrace
Our tired mouse had found a new, perfect place!
