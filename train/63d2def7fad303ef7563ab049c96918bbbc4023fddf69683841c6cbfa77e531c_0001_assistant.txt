  Dear Venus,

    I love you! It feels like my life has been so meaningless, just going around in circles all the time; then I see you, so bright and atmospheric, and I know we were meant for each other. You don't know what it's like, dear, to feel dry and cold as I do. Please come out from behind your clouds and favor me with just a glance; your gaze would warm me and give me life. I fear you are closer to Earth than you are to me, and will remain so. I know the Earth may look better than me, but it's attracted to its moon. I can make you forget about the Earth, my darling. A chance is all I ask; will you give me one?

                                      Your adoring servant,
                                      Mars
