In a dimly lit conference room, a board of executives huddled around a table, all nodding in unison. The CEO, a seasoned leader, proposed a restructuring plan that included downsizing the marketing team. Each executive—Marketing Director (who feared losing her department’s prestige), Finance VP (who needed budget cuts to meet Q3 targets), HR Head (who worried about morale), and Operations Manager (who saw inefficiencies)—agreed, but for their own reasons.

By the end of the meeting, the proposal was approved, though no one had fully aligned on the *why*. The room stayed silent, the only sound the hum of the fluorescent lights. The CEO smiled. "Same page, different paths."
