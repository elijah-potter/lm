The storm clouds hung low over the abandoned mill at dusk, their edges jagged with lightning. Rain lashed the glass panes, each drop a silent whisper of sorrow. Inside, the old loom lay broken, its once-tight threads now loose and frayed, tangled in the same way the emotions of the weaver were. Outside, the wind howled like a ghost, carrying the scent of damp earth and rusted metal. A single candle flickered on a desk, casting long, wavering shadows that danced like restless spirits. The weaver sat hunched over the floor, fingers tracing the same pattern of despair, the fabric of their soul stretched thin and brittle, ready to snap at any sound.
