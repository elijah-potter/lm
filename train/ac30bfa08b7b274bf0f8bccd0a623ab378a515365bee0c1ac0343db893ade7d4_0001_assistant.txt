Here is a possible rewrite of the post for a 3rd grader:

I like big phones very much.

Some people want small phones. But I don’t. I want a big phone.

I want a phone so big that it can’t fall into the toilet. It can only sit on the toilet seat.

I want a phone so big that it makes me sleepy when it falls on my face.

I want a phone bigger than 8 inches.

Phones are different now. Some people can put a big tablet in their pockets. But we can’t get very big phones now. Only phones that can fold or old phones.

Some phones look big but they are not. They have too much space around the screen.

I hope I’m not the only one who wants a huge phone with a huge battery.
