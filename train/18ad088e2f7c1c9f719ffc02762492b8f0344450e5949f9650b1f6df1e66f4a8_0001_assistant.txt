Here are some Nintendo Wii games that incorporate exercise:

1. Wii Sports: This game has five different sports you can play: tennis, bowling, baseball, boxing, and golf.
2. Just Dance 2016: This game involves following dance moves shown on your TV screen while listening to upbeat, modern songs.
3. Wii Fit Plus: This game features yoga, strength training exercises, balancing games, and other activities, but it does require a Wii Balance Board.
4. EA Sports Active 2: This game includes a virtual fitness coach and many different workouts and exercises, including cardio, strength training, boxing, and more.
5. Active Life Explorer: In this game, you play the role of an adventurer where you will be tasked with a number of physical challenges to collect treasure, but keep in mind that it does require the Active Life Mat to play.
