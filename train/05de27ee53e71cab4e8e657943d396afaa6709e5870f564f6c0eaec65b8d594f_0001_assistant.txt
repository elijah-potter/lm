Rorie was once a dainty, imperfect doll. She stood on a shelf at the local toy shop. Nobody seemed to want her because one of her hands was missing. Day after day she stood perched on the shelf, waiting for a family to take her home. 
It was Christmas time, a time of the year when children ask Santa for various presents.  As it got closer to Christmas day, Rorie made a wish. “Oh, Santa! Please give me a home.  I want to be loved by a little girl!”
Later that day some children came to the shop with their parents. They admired the different toys, like Tod the teddy, Bruno the stuffed dog and Greenie the caterpillar with the wiggly legs.  One by one they were all taken home by new owners, while Rorie watched longingly from the shelf. 
In the afternoon, the shop door opened, and in walked a girl using crutches. She walked around, looking at the gray elephant, the purple hippo, and the kaleidoscope, too. But when she got to Rorie’s shelf, she stopped quickly. “I want that one,” she said, pointing towards Rorie. “She wants me?” Rorie thought with wonder. 
“Look closely, it’s a broken doll. I should have thrown her out long ago,” said Mr. Brown. 
“How will you play with a doll which has no hand?” her mother asked. 
 “We can glue it on, Mama,” said the little girl emphatically. “Besides, I don’t want her to be lonely for Christmas.”
The little girl took her home and glued on her small, dainty hand. “Oh! See her now! She is the prettiest doll I have,” said the young girl. That day Rorie not only got back her hand, but she also gained a loving owner. It was a wonderful Christmas for both of them.
