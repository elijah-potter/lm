**Tidal Pool at Low Ebb**

The water recedes in smooth, rhythmic pulses, exposing a bed of blackened shells and jagged rocks. Waves of visitors—each carrying a different reason—arrive at the cove’s edge: fishermen returning at dawn, children chasing crabs, sunbathers who retreat at dusk. The tide’s pull is both a guide and a rhythm, dictating when the crowd swells and when it ebbs. Some linger; others vanish with the next surge. The air smells of salt and damp earth, thick with the scent of anticipation and exhaustion. The shoreline is a living canvas, shifting with each incoming tide, where stories are carved into the sand and washed away by the next wave.
