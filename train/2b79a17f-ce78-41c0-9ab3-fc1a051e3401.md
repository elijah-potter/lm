Once upon a time in a bright, sunny garden, there lived a little blue butterfly named Benny. Benny loved to dance and flap his tiny wings in the warm breeze.

One day, Benny spotted a shiny golden acorn hidden under a big, fluffy dandelion. He wanted to find it but wasn’t sure how. Just then, Benny’s best friend, a round, fuzzy bunny named Benny-Bunny, hopped over. "Let’s help each other!" said Benny-Bunny.

Together, they dug with their paws and flapped their wings to shake the dandelion. "Aaah!" said Benny when a gust of wind blew the dandelion seeds into the air. Benny-Bunny giggled and rolled a smooth stone to roll the acorn toward them.

Finally, Benny and Benny-Bunny found the golden acorn! "We did it!" they cheered. Benny placed the acorn in his wing basket, and Benny-Bunny made a happy wiggle hop. They shared their treasure with all their garden friends, and the sun smiled down on them as they danced and played together forever.
