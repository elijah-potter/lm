Politeness often backfires when the effort feels forced. The individual smiled too broadly, stuttered over *"excuse me"* while awkwardly adjusting their tie, and accidentally spilled their tea with a *"oh dear"* that sounded more like a *"oh god."* The waiter’s eyes widened as the guest tried to offer a biscuit—too hastily, the crumbs scattered across the tablecloth. The other diners chuckled, while the hostess pretended not to notice the crumbs on her lap. The failure of good manners was as visible as the crumbs: sincerity had no place in this performance.
