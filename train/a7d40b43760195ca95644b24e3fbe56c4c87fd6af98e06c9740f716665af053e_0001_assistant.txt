Scraunched and strengthed are the longest one-syllable words. At 10 letters long, both are tied for the longest monosyllabic word in the English language.

The word "scraunched," which means a crunching action or sound, is generally considered to be the longest monosyllabic word that is still in use because the word "strengthed" is archaic and has mostly been replaced by the far more common word, "strengthened."
