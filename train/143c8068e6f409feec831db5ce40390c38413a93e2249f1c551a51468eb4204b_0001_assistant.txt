1. Learn the vocabulary - There are technical terms you should know the definition of to read a micrometer correctly. Be sure you are familiar with these terms beforehand.
2. Wipe it off - The micrometer should be clean before you use it. Use a soft cloth between the spindle and the anvil to remove any grime or debris.
3. Position the item - Line up the item that you're measuring next to the anvil with one hand. Be sure it's steady and avoid any scratching. You can hold the micrometer in your free hand or use a stationary vise.
4. Spin the ratchet - The ratchet should be spun counter-clockwise, ensuring the 0 mark on the thimble lines up with the sleeve scale. Keep turning the ratchet until the spindle is close to making contact with the object being measured.
5. Lock the thimble - The thimble lock should be set when the micrometer is within proximity to the object. You should still have enough room to adjust the spindle if necessary. When you are sure the micrometer has measured the object correctly, you can take out the object, being careful to not scratch the surface of the anvil or spindle.
6. Read the results - While keeping the spindle stable, record the reading for the object you just measured.
