Here are some ways you can reduce stress; however, I am not a clinician, and it is important to speak to a professional if you need help.

1. Eat and drink healthy foods and beverages- while coffee is a go-to for many people on the go, overdrinking coffee can lead to more stress and anxiety. Limit your intake of unhealthy foods and beverages, specifically those with high sugar content.

2. Exercise regularly- Exercising can release endorphins that boost your mood and give you more energy. Try taking a daily walk or taking time out of your day to practice aerobics or other stress-relieving exercises. 

3. Learn to set realistic goals- Sometimes, we pile too much onto our plates and feel discouraged that we weren't able to accomplish all that we set out to. If this happens to you regularly, it may be helpful to readjust your expectations for the day and set more realistic goals. When you do this, you will be able to accomplish tasks instead of drowning in them and feel better about what you were able to get done.
