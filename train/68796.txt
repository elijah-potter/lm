Why PP blood sugar is high?
Postprandial or Reactive Hyperglycemia
This type of hyperglycemia occurs after eating (postprandial means “after eating”). During this type of hyperglycemia, your liver doesn’t stop sugar production, as it normally would directly after a meal, and stores glucose as glycogen (energy sugar stores).

What should I do when my blood sugar is 250?
