A flickering lantern casts long shadows over a crumbling stone archway, its glass cracked like aged bone. The air hums with the low drone of distant machinery, a sound muffled by layers of dust. A lone figure stands near the center, clad in tattered leather, fingers brushing the hilt of a rusted blade. The ground beneath them is a mosaic of broken glass and metal shards, some half-buried in damp earth. A rusted chain, once part of a larger structure, hangs limply from a jagged beam above, swaying slightly in the wind. In the distance, the faint outlines of other figures move—some obscured by mist, others standing still as statues, their faces half-hidden in shadows. The lantern’s glow reveals a single, half-closed eye resting on a table, its pupils reflecting the light like two small, unblinking stars.
