Whispering Pines Library (abandoned)
A decaying, moss-covered wooden pavilion on the edge of Black Hollow Woods, its walls lined with faded book spines. The floorboards creak underfoot, echoing faint whispers of forgotten stories. At night, the wind carries voices from the trees—some human, others something older, murmuring between the cracks in the ceiling. The air smells of damp earth and old parchment. Near the center of the pavilion stands a grand piano, its keys dusted with spiderwebs, half-played by a ghostly hand. Some say if you press your ear to the floor, you can hear the echoes of a thousand confessions. Others claim the whispers are the voices of the library’s former librarian, who vanished one stormy night, leaving only this note behind: *"They still remember."*
