BarStuck’s new drink was created in 2020. The drink is called “Bean-tastic”. The medium size costs $6.49.

It is an espresso-based hot coffee drink, with options for foam, oat milk, and steamed tea added in. There’s a sprinkle of salted caramel at the top, as well as a secret ingredient called “fairy sprinkles.” The fairy sprinkles appear to be crystallized sugars with vitamins, minerals, caffeine, and some unknown substances.

A popular variant, known as “Bean-Mead-tastic”, introduces the element of hops and honey for a mead-style of beverage. The combination of hot coffee and mead surprisingly yielded incredible consumer interest.

It has been known to not appeal to most people. However, for the small percentage who do enjoy it, the average consumer buys 3 per day! It its first year, it has been considered one of the largest successes in the coffee industry.
