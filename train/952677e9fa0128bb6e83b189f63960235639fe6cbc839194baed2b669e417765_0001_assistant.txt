Title: Skulldugger
Logline: The best of Capitol City’s underground network of thieves has big aspirations to rule the city with cunning and guile.
Main Character: Nick Estoc – A young but extremely talented thief in black leather and a dark gray cloak around pale skin, short white hair, and light blue eyes.
Plot Summary: Even though Nick Estoc is one of the best thieves in the Thieves Guild, he is taunted and cheated by the rest of his guild because of his age and familial history. As revenge, Nick enacts a plan to place himself as the shadow hand in power behind the throne by using the tools of his roguish trade. Once comfortably in power, Nick pits the City Guard at war with the Thieves’ Guild and watches from the rooftops as they come to blows. 

Title: Journey of the Fallen
Logline: A paladin falls from grace to rebel against her own holy order when she finds out the misdeeds allowed to carry on in their name and the evil for which they truly stand. 
Main Character: Illya Rose – A thirty-year-old battle-hardened veteran with long black hair and vibrant green eyes under soot-covered plate mail.
Plot Summary: On a mission, Illya Rose finds indisputable proof that the Holy Order she has sworn herself to is a front for a great evil deity. Returning with proof to the Holy City, she is disregarded and locked in prison as the Order’s Cardinals have her locked up to cover up their plot. Illya must break free of the chains, forgo her oath, and smite the cardinals who lead the insidious plot.
