Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big garden, lived a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop and play every day. One sunny morning, Benny found a tiny, shimmering seed in his garden. He picked it up and wondered, "What will grow from this?"

Benny decided to plant it carefully in the soft soil. He watered it gently with a little sprinkle of rainwater. Benny waited patiently, watching the seed every day. Soon, a tiny sprout peeked out from the ground.

As the days passed, Benny watched the sprout grow taller and taller. It stretched up high, reaching for the sky. Soon, a beautiful flower with big, bright petals bloomed at the top. Benny smiled, "Look how pretty it is!"

The flower was so lovely that Benny decided to share it with his friends. He gave a petal to his friend, a squirrel named Sally. Sally nibbled the petal and giggled, "Oh, this is the sweetest treat ever!"

From that day on, Benny and Sally shared the flower's magic every day. They played together, sang songs, and enjoyed the sweetest moments. Benny’s heart was full, and he knew that planting kindness and sharing joy made the world a little bit brighter.
