A well-meaning man in a crowded café attempted to strike up a conversation with a stranger at the next table. "Excuse me," he said, leaning slightly forward, "may I join you?" The stranger, a woman engrossed in her laptop, only nodded politely and continued typing. The man, confused, tried again, "I was wondering if you'd like to grab a coffee sometime?" She still didn’t respond, just glanced up briefly before returning to her screen. His attempt to be polite felt like a misstep, now awkwardly awkward. He sat back, humiliated, while the woman went back to her work.
