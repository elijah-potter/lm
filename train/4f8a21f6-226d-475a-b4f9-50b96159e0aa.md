1. Warm-up: Dynamic stretches (arm circles, leg swings) + light cardio (jog-in-place) for 5-10 mins.
2. Technical Drills: Focused, high-rep exercises (e.g., kettlebell swings, burpees, sprint intervals) to refine form.
3. Conditioning: Circuit-based (e.g., 45 sec work/45 sec rest x 5 rounds): squats, push-ups, mountain climbers, jump rope.
4. Mental Toughness: 5-minute cold shower or fasted run to sharpen endurance focus.
5. Peak Performance: Short, explosive bursts (e.g., 10x 20m sprints) with 90 sec rest; 5 min dynamic stretching post.
6. Cool-down: Static stretching (hamstring, hip flexor) + hydration/electrolytes.
---
Performance Output:
* **Efficiency**: Minimal recovery time between high-intensity phases.
* **Energy System**: Prioritizes anaerobic (fast-twitch) and glycolytic (short-burst) demands.
* **Recovery**: Balanced rest periods for lactate clearance and CNS reset.
* **Psychology**: High-rep/low-rest drills build mental resilience.
---
Adaptable to:
- Endurance sports: Replace sprints with tempo runs, add hill repeats.
- Strength/functional: Swap circuits for weighted movements (e.g., deadlifts, sled pushes).
