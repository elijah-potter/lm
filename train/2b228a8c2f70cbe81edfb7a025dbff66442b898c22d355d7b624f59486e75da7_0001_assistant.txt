Anacondas are semi-aquatic snakes in the tropics of South America; they are some of the largest snakes and are known for their ability to swim. While there are many reports on the max length an anaconda can get to, most publications, including National Geographic and the San Diego Zoo, list the max length as 29 or 30 ft. A man said to have captured over 1000 anacondas believes that the snake doesn't get much longer than 20 ft.
