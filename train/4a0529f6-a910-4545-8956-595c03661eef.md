The rain fell in sheets over the rusted bridge, each drop a whispered secret against the metal. Inside the old bookshop, the scent of aged paper clung to the air like a ghost’s memory. Old typewriters clattered in the corner, their keys still turning, though no hands moved them now.

She sat at the desk, fingers tracing the spine of a leather-bound volume—*The Last Light of Dusk*. The title had been written in ink that was barely legible, as if the words themselves had been erased by time. Outside, the wind howled, but inside, the shop was quiet except for the hum of a single bulb, its glow flickering like a dying ember.

She turned the page.

The ink was still wet.
