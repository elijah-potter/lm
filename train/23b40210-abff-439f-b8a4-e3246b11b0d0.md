A shadow cast long before the decision was made,
the path diverged subtly yet irrevocably—
a fork in the road where the wind whispered,
*"This is where you were meant to go."*

The weight of silence hung like a stone,
each hesitation a whisper lost in the dark.
The choice was not born of doubt, but of the relentless tide
that pulled you toward the inevitable—
the hand that guided you, unseen, unspoken,
was already holding yours.

No grand rebellion, no fleeting spark,
just the quiet certainty of fate’s embrace,
where every step forward felt like surrender,
and every choice felt like destiny.
