"None of you will make it out of here alive!" Ryan bellowed at the top of his lungs. "I already gave all of you a chance to confess where the money is, and none of you are talking. You leave me no choice other than to...." Ryan paused as he realized he didn't remember what line came next. The casting director and his assistant both looked at Ryan with a sense of judgment. This was the second time in the audition that he had messed up, and now it was starting to really hinder his chances of landing this role in the movie Bridge to the Lost Lands. Ryan had been acting for years and was used to auditions, but something about the scope of this movie had him really anxious throughout the whole process. Just as he composed himself and attempted to let out the next line in an attempt to divert attention from his mistake, he suddenly felt a powerful physical feeling overcome him. He felt a massive heat flash as his stomach began to do backflips. Just as the casting director had begun to notice that something was amiss, it happened. Ryan threw up so violently that some of the puke actually landed on the assistant. Needless to say, it wasn't very likely that Ryan was going to land the role in this movie.
