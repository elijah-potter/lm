Here are some ideas for a thoughtful, creative valentine's day with your girlfriend:

- Go to a Dine-In Movie Theater - Make a movie night a little more special by seeing the movie in a theater that also serves dinner and wine.
- Reserve a Table - Make reservations somewhere quiet and romantic like a jazz club, where you'll have good music and good food to share.
- Have a Spa Night - Turn your bathroom into a luxurious spa with facials, and foot soaks, and maybe end with a massage.
- Go to a Burlesque Show - If you have burlesque available in your area, going to a show can be a fun and sexy way to spend the evening.
- Breakfast in Bed - Start the day by whipping up a gourmet breakfast including some of her favorite things, and serve it to her in bed.

Have fun! Let me know if you'd like some more suggestions!
