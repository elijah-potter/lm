Vanadium content ranges from 0.05% to 0.2%, with Mn ranging from 0.8% to 1%. Other elements present in this alloy include sulfur (S) and phosphorus (P). The S content is typically less than 0.03%, while P is usually less than 0.04%.

Alloy Steel Grade 5CR has a wide range of uses due to its strength and corrosion resistance. Beyond the automotive and aerospace industries, Alloy Steel Grade 5CR is found in medical implants, farm equipment and turbines, making it one of the most versatile alloys on the market today.
Alloy Steel Grade 5CR has tremendous corrosion resistance, making it one of the most sought after materials in heavy industry. Thanks to its high tensile strength and low carbon content, Grade 5CR is popular for use in oil and gas, petrochemical, power generation, offshore and construction industries. The alloy’s high chromium content also contributes to excellent corrosion resistance even when exposed to temperatures exceeding 500°C (932°F). Grade 5CR is especially resistant to chloride induced pitting and crevice corrosion once passivated with nitric acid or other suitable treatments.
