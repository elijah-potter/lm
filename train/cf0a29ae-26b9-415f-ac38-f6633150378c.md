A sudden gust of wind sent a loose branch crashing into the back of a seated cyclist mid-pedal. The rider froze, eyes wide as they balanced precariously before abruptly swerving into the roadside bushes. The startled sound of their collision echoed through the quiet village, followed by the unmistakable *thud* of impact as they hit the pavement. A passing motorcyclist screeched to a halt, helmet still clutched in their hands, as they turned to assess the scene. The cyclist, now sprawled face-first in the gravel, lay motionless, one hand clutching their wrist—a visible split above the knuckles, blood already seeping into the cracked asphalt. The motorcyclist knelt beside them, voice sharp with urgency: *"Stay still!"*—before rushing back to retrieve their first aid kit from the bike. The cyclist’s breath came in shallow, gasping bursts, their vision swimming with dizziness as they stared at the darkening sky, unseeing.
