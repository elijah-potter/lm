What is the daily output of the automatic 1 for 2 N95 and KN95 head-wearing mask machines?
In Europe, the United States and other countries, head-wearing N95 and KN95 masks are more commonly used. Among them, the masks of the US NIOSH certification standard are all head-wearing N95 masks. Today, the number of people infected with covid-19 in the United States and other countries is still growing at a high rate. The turning point has not yet arrived, and the demand for head-wearing N95 masks is still huge. In order to meet the needs of overseas markets, Han's Yueming Group has launched a fully automatic one-to-two headwearing N95/KN95 mask machine. What is its daily output?
Fully automatic one with two N95/KN95 head-wearing mask machine
The automatic one-to-two N95/KN95 head-wearing mask machine is made up of N95 and KN95 body filming machines connected to two automatic head-wearing welding machines. After the N95 mask and KN95 mask are made into the mask body on the body filming machine, the manipulator The mask body is clamped and shunted from the mask conveyor line and placed on the two mask welding toolings, and the production of head-wearing N95 masks and KN95 masks is completed through automatic welding and automatic feeding.
In terms of output, the automatic one-to-two N95/KN95 head-wearing mask machine can produce up to 40 Pcs per minute. If calculated by the day, theoretically 57,600 N95/KN95 head masks can be produced per day. But it is only a theoretical value, and the output will vary according to the production site.
KN95 head-wearing mask
The process of the fully automatic one-to-two N95/KN95 head-wearing mask machine is fully automated, without any manual operation, and workers only need to pack the finished product. Fully automatic one-to-two N95/KN95 head-wearing mask machine is widely used in the production of 3-6 layers of medical and anti-haze three-dimensional head-wearing N95 masks and KN95 masks, realizing the production of non-woven materials to finished N95 masks and KN95 Fully automated production of masks.
