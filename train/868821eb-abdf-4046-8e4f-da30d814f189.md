Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, lived a bright and curious bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown ears and always wanted to explore the world.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside and saw his favorite friend, a little squirrel named Sammy. "Sammy! Let's play!" Benny said happily. Sammy chattered, "I was just thinking about a fun adventure! Let's build a sandcastle!"

Benny's eyes sparkled. "A sandcastle? I love sandcastles! Where should we start?"

Sammy pointed to a big, empty sandbox. "There! Let's fill it with sand first." Benny dug his paws in, and soon, the sandbox was covered in golden sand. They dug out a big, round bowl and filled it with sand too.

"Now, let's make waves," Sammy said. Benny and Sammy worked together, scooping sand into the bowl and then gently letting it fall. The sand made tiny waves that bounced and sparkled.

"Look at this!" Benny gasped. "It's like the ocean!" Sammy nodded. "Let's make a castle now!"

They shaped the sand into towers and walls. Benny made a big, tall tower with a little flag on top. Sammy created a door and a window for their castle. Soon, their sandcastle was complete, with a castle tower, a door, and even a little bridge made of sand.

"Wow, Benny, it looks amazing!" Sammy said. "Now, let’s decorate it!"

Benny and Sammy added tiny shells they found in the garden to the walls. They even made some sand flowers by pressing their paws into the sand to make pretty patterns. The sandcastle sparkled and shone like it was made of magic.

Just then, a little cloudy voice came from above. "Oh no! Look, a big, scary storm is coming!"

Benny and Sammy looked up. The sky was getting darker, and big, heavy clouds were rolling in. "Don't worry," Benny said. "We’ll make our castle extra strong!"

They packed the sandcastle tightly, making it even more sturdy. Benny and Sammy also built a little sand tunnel behind the castle so it wouldn’t get washed away by the rain.

When the storm started, Benny and Sammy huddled inside the sandcastle with their sand friends. The wind howled, and raindrops pattered against the sand. Benny and Sammy stayed warm and dry, sharing their sand friends and laughing.

After a while, the storm passed, and the sun came out again. Benny and Sammy looked at their sandcastle. It was still there, strong and beautiful. "Look, Sammy!" Benny said. "Our castle is still standing!"

Sammy smiled. "It’s like a little treasure we built just for us."

From that day on, Benny and Sammy always played in the sandbox, building their sandcastle together. And whenever they did, they knew it was their special place, full of magic and fun. Benny and Sammy learned that even when things were big and scary outside, like storms, they could always find a cozy, happy spot to play and create something wonderful inside.
