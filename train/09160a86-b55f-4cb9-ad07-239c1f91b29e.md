**BREAKING:** The City of New Haven’s historic **Blackwood Tower** has been declared structurally unstable after a series of unexplained tremors detected in the downtown core overnight. Authorities report that the 19th-century iron-and-glass edifice, once a symbol of the city’s industrial era, has experienced minor cracks in its facade, though no immediate danger has been confirmed. Residents near the site report a faint, metallic humming sensation, while security cameras captured what appeared to be a localized "earthquake" measuring just over 1.8 on the Richter scale, lasting approximately 12 seconds. Engineers suspect thermal expansion or a faulty foundation, but experts caution against drawing premature conclusions. The city’s Emergency Operations Center has activated a "red alert" for the area within a 1.2-mile radius, advising residents to avoid the tower’s immediate vicinity until further inspection. No injuries or property damage have been reported as of this report, though firefighters remain on standby for potential secondary incidents. Officials are urging residents to stay vigilant and report any further disturbances to 911. The New Haven Historical Society has issued a statement calling for community support to assess the tower’s preservation status, pending further structural assessments.
