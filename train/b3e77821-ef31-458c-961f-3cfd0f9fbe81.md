The **Unspoken Covenant** between **House Veyne** and **The Obsidian Pact** was a silent treaty carved into the bones of the old city’s foundations. The terms were never written, never verbalized, yet the weight of their existence was felt in the hollows of the streets where shadows stretched like fingers.

**Veyne’s Blood** bound the Pact’s oaths:
- **One life for every star that falls**—when the sky bleeds black, they would not ask why, only that the price was paid.
- **Silence is gold**—whispers were currency, secrets were currency, and those who spoke too loud were left to rot in the streets.
- **The Pact’s hunger is not for flesh**—it was for whispers, for the echoes of fear, for the ghosts of the dead who still walked in dreams.

The Pact’s **Eyes in the Dark** were not blind—they saw what others dared not see: the cracks in the walls where the dead watched, the rats that carried messages between the living and the unseen. They fed on the **Lament of the Forgotten**, the songs of the dead sung too softly to be heard.

Veyne’s **Scars** were the only witnesses. The mark of the Pact was not a brand, but the way their enemies **never spoke of the night they were found**. Some claimed it was a curse. Others said it was mercy.

The treaty was sealed in blood—not on parchment, but in the **blood of the forgotten**, the ones who had already been erased from memory. The Pact demanded **no words**, only **actions**. And Veyne, in turn, promised to **never ask questions**.

Now, the city breathes. The Pact eats. And the blood on the pavement is the only witness left.
