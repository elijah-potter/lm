There once was a fish named Tot. Tot loved to swim about his tank and watch the day go by through the window in the room. One day, Tot noticed a furry black creature walking about; Tot had never seen such a thing. The creature meowed and liked to lay in the sunshine, and Tot would watch it day in and day out. He had never seen anything other than a human outside of the tank. One day, the black animal came up to Tot's tank to take a look. Tot was a bit scared at first but mostly curious. "What's your name?" Tot asked. 
"My name is Onyx," exclaimed the cat, "and yours?" 
"My name is Tot," the fish stated. 
"Oh, hi Tot! Wanna be friends?" asked the cat. 
"Sure," Tot said, hesitantly. Tot was not quite sure how the pair could be friends with such differences. However, the two became close and loved to watch the world go by outside the window together.
