**The Last Light of the Old Clock Tower**

The rain fell in slow, silver sheets against the broken windows, each one a cracked mirror reflecting nothing but the past. Inside, Elias sat hunched over his desk, the inkwell half-empty, the quill held too long. The letter—addressed to his sister, *Mira*—lay open on the parchment, her words still visible though faded by time.

*"Do not wait for the bell to ring,"* she had written. *"The door will always be open, but the hour is counting down. The city remembers what it forgets."*

Outside, the wind howled through the skeletal branches of the old oak, its roots clawing at the foundations of the tower. Elias knew the clock’s hands had stopped at midnight. It wasn’t the chime that mattered now—it was the silence between the last strike and the first dawn.

He reached for the quill again.
