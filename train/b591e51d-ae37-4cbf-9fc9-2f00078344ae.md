I need your help, please.

The streets are slick with rain, and the cold bites through my coat. My hands are numb, but the fire in the hearth won’t hold me warm enough.

I’ve been chasing this for days—someone who knows where the missing ones are buried. The townsfolk whisper, the cops are tired, and the police chief’s son is the last one left.

I’m tired. My legs ache, my breath comes in short gasps. The weight of the map in my pocket feels heavier than the night.

Please, help me. Tell me where to look.
