Henry pulled into the hangar and parked his car. Brian was waiting for him next to the small plane they would be taking up today. Henry had been taking flying lessons from Brian for months now, and today was the first time Henry would have full control of the plane. Henry had always dreamed of becoming a pilot, and now his goal seemed closer than ever. He walked up to Brian and they exchanged greetings. After checking over the plane, they climbed in and brought it out to the runway. 

After finishing the rest of their pre-flight checks, Henry began to take the plane up into the air. It was a crisp autumn day with perfect flying conditions. There was very little wind and not a cloud in the sky. The plane began to gain altitude, and happiness rushed through Henry's heart. Eventually, they reached cruising altitude and Henry had a chance to look at the ground below. They passed over houses, rivers, and forests which appeared small from such a great height. Their scheduled flight was for one hour; Henry had plenty of time to take in the views.

After a while, Brian reminded Henry that they should begin heading back to the hangar. Brian did not have much tolerance for risk, and they only had so much fuel. Henry slowly turned the plane around in a wide arc and began heading back. There had been no issues during the flight. Henry had successfully handled gusts of wind, flocks of birds, and other small obstacles. The hangar was now in sight, and Henry made an adjustment to their flight path for a better approach to the runway. They began their descent, and Henry landed the plane smoothly. He had done it! This was his first flight with no assistance at all from Brian. Brian congratulated him and they shook hands. Henry had never been happier.
