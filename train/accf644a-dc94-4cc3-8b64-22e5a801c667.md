**BREAKING: Mysterious Energy Surge Sparks Panic in New Haven’s "The Glow District"**

New Haven’s historic district of **The Glow**, once known for its dimly lit cobblestone streets, has become the epicenter of an unexpected phenomenon. Residents reported an unnatural surge of ambient light flickering through old brick facades, illuminating sidewalks and alleyways with an eerie, pulsating glow. Witnesses described the light as neither natural nor artificial, defying standard electricity or solar exposure. Emergency crews confirmed no visible power sources or streetlights were activated—yet the phenomenon persisted for hours before dissipating overnight.

Authorities are investigating potential causes, including reports of faint, rhythmic sounds resembling distant machinery or whispers. Local historians speculate ties to an abandoned 19th-century hydroelectric plant beneath the district, though no official confirmation has been issued. The city’s energy grid remains stable, but residents are urged to avoid lingering near the glow’s original sightings near **Old Mill Lane** and **Glimmer Square**.

Councilor Elena Vasquez stated, *"We’re treating this as a public safety precaution. If you’ve witnessed anything unusual, contact the non-emergency line at 555-987-6542."* No injuries were reported, but officials advise against engaging with the phenomenon. The Glow District remains open for business—but only at night.
