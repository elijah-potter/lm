Triple Antibiotic Ointment is a first-aid antibiotic and pain reliever. 

The active ingredients of the typical over-the-counter (OTC) triple antibiotic ointment include:
- Bacitracin Zinc USP, 500 units
- Neomycin 3.5 mg
- Polymyxin B sulfate USP, 10,000 units
- Pramoxine hydrochloride USP, 10 mg

Inactive ingredients include:
- white petrolatum

The most common use of Triple Antibiotic Ointment is for first aid, specifically the prevention of infection and temporary pain relief in minor cuts, scrapes, and burns. Before using any medication, be sure to read all label directions. If you have questions about its use for other injuries not listed here, please consult a doctor.
