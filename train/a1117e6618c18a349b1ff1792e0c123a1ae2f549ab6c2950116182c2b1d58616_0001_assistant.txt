1.) Cryo-Shift: A post-apocalyptic world is overrun by monsters called "Cryo-Shifters" set loose by 
     mega-corporation Entertainment, Vacation, Industry, Love, and Life, or "E.V.I.L.L" When a rag-tag group 
     of kids are put up to the task of stopping them, chaos, hilarity, and terror ensue. 

2.) Sound Barrier: In a school where music has been banned, a young girl encapsulates her classmates through 
      dance. Once she gets popular, she gets the attention of the principal, who is determined to take her down.  

3.) Time Heist: When the world's most infamous criminal fails his biggest job, stealing a time machine, he must team
      up with the original inventor who died 100 years prior. 

4.) Memory Gate: In a future overtaken by data, where memories can be uploaded to a thumb drive, a ragtag team of misfits must break into the memory archive capital of the world and save the entire population from a memory wipe by evil aliens from Saturn.
