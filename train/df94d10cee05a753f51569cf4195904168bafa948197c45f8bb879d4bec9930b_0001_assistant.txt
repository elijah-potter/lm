Sure! Here are some drinks that would be refreshing at a summer garden party that don't include alcohol:

1. Rosemary and Ginger Mule - served in the classic copper mug, this is an icy ginger ale with rosemary.

2. Strawberry Mint Tea - serve ice-cold strawberry mint tea and garnish with slices of strawberry.

3. Frozen Lemonade - keep some glasses in the freezer and serve your lemonade in a frosty glass with a slice of lemon garnish.

4. Sparkling Punch - add a splash of fruit juice or pink lemonade to unsweetened seltzer water and serve in a champagne flute.

5. Frozen Blueberry Lemonade - add a small handful of frozen blueberries to each glass of lemonade to add flavor and keep it cool.
