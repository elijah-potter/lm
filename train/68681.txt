What is Data Visualization?
Data visualization is a way of visually presenting data, directly representing the underlying data, and helpful in understanding the concept of the data.
Data visualization is a technique used to make information easier to understand. Data visualization aims to create pictures or other visual representations of raw data that make insight immediately apparent. Data visualization is often used for interactive applications, such as a GIS program or a financial application that presents you with a forecast chart. RStudio is an open-source integrated development environment (IDE) for statistical computing and graphical processing languages (SVG). It includes an interpreter for the programming language R and supports various packages from the R community.

The service also permits users to collaborate on a spreadsheet simultaneously across multiple devices (including phones and tablets) and share their work via email or Google Drive files. This is why many people prefer this software.
