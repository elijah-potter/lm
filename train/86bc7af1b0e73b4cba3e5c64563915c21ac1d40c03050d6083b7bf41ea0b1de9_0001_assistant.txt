The bars have a different taste compared to what I'm used to, and it's not very pleasant. Despite having a good expiration date, the bars arrived melted, possibly due to prolonged exposure to high temperatures during shipment. I have purchased these bars before and the taste was much better. Unfortunately, I find them inedible this time. I acknowledge that it's not the seller's fault, but it would be helpful if extra precautions were taken when distributing food during hot summer conditions.
