- Avocados: This superfood has a high bioavailability of lutein, which can improve cognitive health.
- Blueberries: This fruit is rich in vitamin C and potassium and has antioxidative and anti-inflammatory benefits that can improve learning capacity.
- Eggs: This food has a high amount of choline, which produces acetylcholine, a neurotransmitter that helps focus.
- Flaxseeds and Chia Seeds: Both of these foods are full of healthy omega fats, which play a key role in memory, focus, and brain development.
- Salmon: This fish is a great source of omega-3 fatty acids, which reduce cognitive decline by lowering inflammation.
- Tomatoes: This fruit has high amounts of lycopene and beta-carotene, antioxidants that improve cognition and brain health.
