The DASH (Dietary Approach to Stop Hypertension) diet is an approach to healthy eating designed to treat or prevent high blood pressure, also known as hypertension. The diet focuses on decreasing the amount of sodium a person consumes while increasing their intake of foods that are plentiful sources of potassium, calcium, and magnesium, which are nutrients that help control blood pressure. If the diet is followed accordingly, this dietary approach has shown improvement in blood pressure levels in as little time as two weeks.
