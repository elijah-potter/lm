Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big yellow door, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had soft, fluffy brown fur and always wore a tiny red hat.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his friend Sammy the squirrel climbing a tall tree. "Sammy, come play with me!" Benny called. Sammy hopped down and said, "But Benny, I need to collect my nuts first!"

Benny giggled. "Oh, nuts! I have a secret spot behind my garden where I hide my treats."

They ran to Benny’s garden, where they found a pile of yummy berries, crunchy carrots, and soft leaves to eat. Benny shared his treats with Sammy, who chewed happily on the nuts.

After snack time, Benny wanted to play. He dug a tiny hole in the dirt and filled it with mud. "Come on, Sammy! Let’s play mud pie!" Sammy nodded and hopped in.

They mixed mud and leaves, then rolled it into a big, squishy ball. Benny used his paws to shape it into a yummy mud pie. Sammy added some extra crunchy nuts on top.

"Look!" Benny said. "Our mud pie is ready!"

Sammy laughed. "It smells wonderful! Let’s eat it outside!"

They sat on a big rock and dug in. The mud pie was delicious—soft and sweet. Benny and Sammy shared the pie and smiled.

As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and orange, Benny and Sammy said goodbye. "Goodnight, Sammy!" Benny called. Sammy waved goodbye and climbed up his tree.

Benny curled up in his bed, with a soft blanket and a warm blanket under his paws. He dreamed of his fun adventure with Sammy and the tasty mud pie. And that’s how Benny and Sammy became the best friends ever, playing and laughing all day long.
