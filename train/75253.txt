Third-graders from Indian Woods Elementary in Kimball Township found evidence a woodpecker had been near the Pine River Nature Center and learned to identify a white pine.
Both exercises were part of a field trip Wednesday to the nature center, which tied into the curriculum the students were studying in class.
"We've been talking about plants and animals, so this is reinforcement," said Joni Wagner, the students' teacher. "It's an enrichment lesson." ...
