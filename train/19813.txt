There are many good reasons to teach our kids to garden, but one of the most important is the profound lesson gardening teaches them about life.
To grow is to know, say food and wellness experts at 810 KIDS!
Kids who garden learn about cultivating carrots and other vegetables early in life are more likely to prefer nature’s bounty in its most natural state over packaged, often over-processed, food products.
They also learn that gardening can represent a healthy escape.
The secret to growing healthy, confident and curious kids might be in your own backyard. So let your kids dig around this spring. You’ll be amazed by the bountiful returns.
