## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park – A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary (For Municipal Funders)**

The Willow Creek neighborhood, a vibrant but historically underserved area within the city, suffers from a significant deficit of accessible green space and faces increasing vulnerability to extreme weather events. This proposal seeks funding to transform a currently neglected, underutilized parcel – the vacant lot at the corner of Elm and Maple Streets – into the Willow Creek Pocket Park, a climate-resilient community asset. This project will create a demonstrably beneficial public space featuring native plantings, stormwater management infrastructure, and accessible design, enhancing community well-being, mitigating climate-related risks, and fostering neighborhood pride. The total project cost is $75,000, with $37,500 requested from the municipal fund and $37,500 secured through community fundraising and in-kind donations. The Willow Creek Pocket Park represents a strategic investment in a resilient and equitable future for our city.

**Project Plan: Phases and Deliverables**

The project will unfold across four distinct phases, each with defined responsibilities and deliverables.

**Phase 1: Design and Planning (Weeks 1-4) – Lead: Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (WCNA) & Landscape Architect (Laura Miller, Miller Designs)**

*   **Deliverables:** Site assessment report, community design workshop summary, finalized park design plans (including planting schedules, accessibility features, and stormwater management details), permit application submission to the City Planning Department.
*   **Responsible Partners:** WCNA (community liaison, volunteer coordination), Miller Designs (landscape architect, design expertise), City Planning Department (regulatory approvals).

**Phase 2: Site Preparation and Infrastructure (Weeks 5-8) – Lead: City Parks Department & Volunteer Team**

*   **Deliverables:** Site clearing and debris removal, soil amendment and preparation, installation of stormwater infiltration system (rain garden and permeable pavers), installation of accessible pathways.
*   **Responsible Partners:** City Parks Department (equipment provision, technical guidance), WCNA (volunteer coordination), local construction company (in-kind labor for pathway installation).

**Phase 3: Planting and Landscaping (Weeks 9-12) – Lead: WCNA & Local Nursery (Green Thumb Gardens)**

*   **Deliverables:** Installation of native trees, shrubs, and perennial plants according to design plan, signage installation identifying plant species and explaining stormwater management features, initial irrigation system setup.
*   **Responsible Partners:** WCNA (volunteer planting days, signage fabrication), Green Thumb Gardens (plant supply and horticultural expertise), City Water Department (irrigation connection).

**Phase 4: Community Launch and Ongoing Maintenance (Weeks 13-16 & Ongoing) – Lead: WCNA & City Parks Department**

*   **Deliverables:** Grand opening celebration event, establishment of a “Friends of Willow Creek Pocket Park” volunteer group, development of a park maintenance schedule, initial park clean-up and litter removal.
*   **Responsible Partners:** WCNA (event planning, volunteer recruitment), City Parks Department (ongoing maintenance support, equipment access), local businesses (event sponsorship).

**Community Engagement and Equity Impacts**

Community engagement has been central to this project's development. The WCNA hosted two public forums to gather input on design preferences and desired park features. These sessions were widely publicized through neighborhood newsletters, social media, and flyers distributed at local businesses. We prioritized accessibility in the design, ensuring pathways meet ADA standards and incorporating seating areas suitable for individuals with mobility limitations. The park's location in a historically underserved area directly addresses issues of environmental justice, providing a vital green space to a community lacking such resources. The project actively seeks to involve residents of all ages and backgrounds in volunteer opportunities, fostering a sense of ownership and shared responsibility for the park’s success.

**Risk Mitigation**

Potential risks have been identified and mitigation strategies developed. Regulatory delays are addressed through proactive communication with the City Planning Department and early permit application submission. Unforeseen weather events during construction are mitigated through careful scheduling and contingency planning. Volunteer attrition is addressed through robust recruitment and appreciation efforts. Damage to existing utilities is prevented through thorough site assessment and coordination with the City Water and Gas Departments.

**Post-Grant Sustainability**

The “Friends of Willow Creek Pocket Park” volunteer group will ensure the park’s long-term maintenance and upkeep. This group will be responsible for regular litter removal, weeding, and minor repairs. The City Parks Department will provide ongoing support, including equipment access and technical guidance. Fundraising events will be organized annually to supplement the park’s operating budget. Educational signage will promote sustainable practices and encourage responsible park usage.
