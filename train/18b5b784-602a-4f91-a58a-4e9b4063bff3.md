**Scene 1 (Past):**
A lighthouse keeper, Elias, stands on the crumbling stone terrace, his breath fogging in the cold. The sea hisses against the rocks below. His daughter, Liora, plays with a seashell she found earlier—its smooth surface catching the moonlight.

**Scene 2 (Present):**
The same lighthouse, now abandoned, looms over a coastal village. A young fisherman, Kael, tosses a line into the churning waves, his father’s old boots clattering against the stone path. He grips the shell Liora left behind, its weight familiar.

**Scene 3 (Future):**
Elias, now a ghostly figure in a tattered coat, walks along the cliffs. The village below is a ghost town, its lanterns flickering. He turns toward the horizon, where Kael stands, his voice echoing in the wind: *"Find what’s lost, son. The sea remembers."* The shell clatters in his hand—no longer empty.
