**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, Ill. – August 15, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, promising a vibrant celebration of agriculture, community, and entertainment for residents and visitors alike. This year’s fair will showcase the best of local livestock, handcrafted goods, and thrilling attractions, providing a memorable experience for all ages. Attendees can anticipate traditional fair activities, including livestock judging, agricultural exhibits, carnival rides, and a diverse selection of food vendors.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from Friday, August 23rd, through Sunday, August 25th, 2024. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM on Friday and Saturday and 6:00 PM on Sunday. A full schedule of events, including detailed times for competitions and demonstrations, will be available on the fair’s website, SangamonCountyFair.org. The fairgrounds are located at 2100 Fairgrounds Road, Springfield, Illinois.

Admission to the Sangamon County Fair is $10 for adults, $5 for children aged 6-12, and free for children five and under. Parking is available on-site for $10 per vehicle. Advance tickets can be purchased online at SangamonCountyFair.org to expedite entry. A season pass, granting access for all three days, is available for $25.

**Headline Events:**

*   Friday Evening Demolition Derby
*   Saturday Night Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters
*   Sunday Morning Livestock Auction
*   Daily 4-H Livestock Judging Competitions
*   Friday and Saturday Night Fireworks Displays
*   The Great Pumpkin Weigh-Off (Saturday)
