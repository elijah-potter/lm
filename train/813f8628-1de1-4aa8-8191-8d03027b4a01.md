Ephemeral Shores of Veythari
A coastal archipelago where tides rewrite topography. The sand is liquid, shifting between dunes and cliffs depending on the moon’s phases. Bridges of driftwood and coral stretch between islands, dissolved and reformed by currents. Structures built from the bones of ancient storms collapse overnight, replaced by new formations—floating ruins of forgotten civilizations. The air hums with the sound of wind and whispers carried by the wind; some say the past lingers as echoes in the waves. Here, every "permanent" mark—scars, names, or landmarks—is a story carved into sand, destined to be erased by the next high tide.
