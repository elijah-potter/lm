Once there was a little bunny named Pip who loved to hop around the sunny meadow. One day, Pip saw a shiny red apple rolling near the berry bush. "Mmm, red and juicy!" Pip thought, and hopped to catch it. Just as he reached out, a little mouse named Mimi scurried over. "Oops! I almost stepped on it!" said Mimi.

Pip giggled. "No problem! Here, let me share." Pip gave Mimi the apple. Together, they munched happily until a big, grumpy old owl named Owlbert hooted, "Who’s bothering my berries?"

Pip and Mimi froze. "Sorry, Owlbert!" Pip said. Owlbert blinked. "Well, maybe if you shared, I’d share my own berries!"

Pip nodded. "Deal!" They each took a berry, and Owlbert fluffed his feathers happily. From that day on, Pip, Mimi, and Owlbert became the best of friends, always sharing and playing together in the meadow. The apple, berries, and sunshine made them smile every day!
