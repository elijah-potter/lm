Pug Dog Breed is one of the breeds of dogs that do not go unnoticed.
This may be because they look more curious than other breeds or because they can be very funny.
However, it is one of the most loved dogs as a pet because it is great for kids, Yes, major adults.
Despite its many benefits, it is one breed most prone to multiple pathologies.
Like other dogs, he has weak points, many due to his countenance, among other aspects of his body that also affect him.

Diseases that the Pug Dog Breed Can Suffer from
Pugs can be prone to various pathologies, but problems with their skin, respiratory diseases, and eyes stand out.
- Among the major diseases that a pug can suffer from, the pathologies associated with the respiratory tract are the ones that stand out the most.
- It may be because it is a brachycephalic race meaning they are prone to diseases associated with the shape of their face or skull.
- If you’re adopting a Pug, you’ve probably heard that they snore in their sleep and sometimes seem restless.
- This is due to what was described above.
- This type of problem means that its adopters must take certain precautions of interest, such as the following:
- They should not be forced to exercise too often or excessively.
- They must be protected from high temperatures, as it is a breed that can suffer from heatstroke rapidly.
- They should not be overfed to prevent the extra pounds from causing problems such as obesity, among others.
Diseases Associated with the Skin
- The Pug is a dog that keeps its body covered in wrinkles, which is one of the main causes of problems.
- During baths, after walks, or in case he gets wet while going out for a walk with rainwater, it is important that you, as an adopter, dry his wrinkles properly.
- This can prevent an accumulation of water between your wrinkles, preventing the presence of fungus or dermatitis.
- In addition, it must be constantly brushed to eliminate all dead hair because the breed frequently loses its hair. Thus, their wrinkles can check without any problem.
- In the same way, it will help to avoid skin infections and possible allergies.
- Another common problem with Pug dogs is their joints, especially the knee, patella, and kneecap dislocations, which means their kneecap moves out of its natural place.
- This breed can also have disadvantages when it comes to Legg-Calves-Perthes disease.
- This indicates that degeneration is forming in the head of the dog’s hind leg femur, which can cause hip problems.
- On the other hand, hip dysplasia is also a common reality in this breed.
The health of pugs is in the hands of their adopter, so it is very important to know how to take care of it, as it is a big responsibility.
This breed requires a healthy, balanced, and balanced diet, which will prevent most of the diseases to which it is prone, as well as obesity.
Some recommendations are:
Consumption of Vegetables and others:
- Pug needs a Balanced diet which is essential to protect and take care of your health.
- In this sense, it should be a diet rich in vegetables and meat without any sugar.
- The Pug or Pug is among the breeds that need to receive the necessary nutrients and vitamins.
Different Servings per day:
- If possible, it is best to divide your diet into three daily portions, always given simultaneously.
- In this way, the dog can quickly get used to a schedule, that is, a routine.
- Food should be provided at room temperature to avoid various stomach problems.
- Once the dog has consumed its ration, it must be allowed to digest, then walked.
They are a bit Stubborn:
- When the Pug feels he is not being given the attention he needs, he tends to go into pride mode.
- In this sense, he will prefer to stay away from his adopter, see him one-on-one or spend time with him.
- That is why the Pug is characterized as an overly stubborn and proud dog because if it is necessary to ignore you for a while, he will do it without any problem.
- This behavior may be due to the life they had during the time of the Chinese emperors, as they were one of the dogs that managed to have the most privileges and were provided with great services, food of the best quality, and the most sought- after breeding in all China.
They are not Aggressive, but they are Capricious:
- On the other hand, and as a great advantage, the Pug is not an aggressive dog.
- On the contrary, they are very calm and affectionate with the whole family.
- This dog can be one of the most docile when it comes to calmness, but it can also have its temperamental moments.
- When this happens, the adopter will notice it as it will begin to wag its tail animatedly to the side.
- He will also jump around the house and enjoy any movement he might find distracting.
- There is no doubt that the Pug or Pug is a wonderful dog that will accompany you for many years with his love.
- They are extremely loyal dogs to their adopters, and this is something that will create an unbreakable bond between them.
- As for their education, they learn very quickly, which is a very simple process.
- In conclusion, all of this and more makes it one of the favorite breeds of many families.
