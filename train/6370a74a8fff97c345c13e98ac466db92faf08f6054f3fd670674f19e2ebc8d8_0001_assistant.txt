Schrödinger's Cat is a thought experiment about quantum physics. In simple terms, Schrödinger, a theoretical physicist, suggested that if you place a cat and something that could kill the cat (such as a radioactive atom) in a box and sealed it, you would not know if the cat was dead or alive until you opened the box. He stated that until the box was opened, the cat was (in a sense) both "dead and alive". This thought experiment is used to explain how scientific theory works, meaning that no one knows definitively if any scientific theory is right or wrong until said theory can be tested and proved.
