A weathered wooden crate, half-sunken in the muddy bank of the Blackthorn River, contained a leather-bound journal and a folded map. The journal, its pages yellowed with age, bore a single handwritten note: *"The Lost Vault of Eldermere—dig where the old well’s headstone cracks at midnight."*

Nearby, the map showed an X marked in faded ink near the abandoned stone bridge, marked *"Not to be opened."* The finder, Elias Veyne, carried the artifacts to the local apothecary, where the scent of dried herbs and old ink clung to the journal’s spine. The map’s edges were damp, as if retrieved from water. Elias kept them under his bed, unaware that the journal’s last entry read: *"The vault is not a vault. It is a door."*
