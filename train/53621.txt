Gazania "Kiss Rose" (Gazania rigens "Kiss Rose"), also known as treasure flower or African daisy, is a sun- and heat-loving plant that thrives in U.S. Department of Agriculture plant hardiness zones 8b through 11. The daisy-like flowers of gazania "Kiss Rose," which bloom from late spring until autumn, are pastel pink with a deep rose ring around a yellow center. This low-maintenance bloomer is a compact plant that reaches heights of only 8 to 10 inches. Gazania is an excellent plant for rock gardens and is often grown in containers. In cool climates, gazania is grown indoors.
Plant gazania "Kiss Rose" in bright sunlight, allowing 12 to 18 inches between each plant. Gazania thrives in nearly any well-drained soil, including sand, clay, loam, or soil that is acidic or alkaline.
Water gazania deeply after planting, saturating the roots to a depth of 6 inches. Thereafter, water the plant only during extended periods of hot, dry weather. Always allow the soil to dry between watering, as gazania is susceptible to potentially fatal fungal and bacterial diseases in soggy soil.
Remove flowers as soon as they wilt by pinching them between your fingernails. This process, called deadheading, encourages continued blooms throughout the season.
Divide gazania if the plants outgrow their boundaries or if the center of the plant looks woody and nonproductive. Dig the entire clump, and then pull the clump into smaller sections, teasing the roots apart carefully with your fingers. Discard the woody center and replant the sections.
Things You Will Need
- When planted outdoors, gazania require no fertilizer. Fertilizer often results in lush, green plants with few blooms.
- Gazania "Kiss Rose" tolerates salty soil and sea air. The plant is also an effective ground cover in dry areas.
