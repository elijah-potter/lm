Of Course! There are tons you can bring and do to stay occupied on your journey. 

– Food and Snacks: While riding coach you have limited access to the dining car. You can visit the cafe car to get snacks if you need to but make sure to bring some of your own to keep you from getting hungry or having to spend a fortune on food.
– A pillow and blanket: You will be on the train for a while and will need to sleep. Be sure to bring a pillow and a blanket with you to get maximum comfort in the coach seats. 
– A book: A good book is a great way to pass the time on the train. Sit back in your seat and kill some time escaping into a different world.
– Write: Use the scenery as inspiration for writing. Take space from your life and jot down anything that comes to mind on the journey
– Play a videogame: Bring a Nintendo Switch or Steam Deck along and dive into one of your favorite games, or pick a new one to remember the journey by.
– Movie: Throw some headphones in and watch a movie on your phone or tablet. Just download it beforehand since the train doesn't have wifi!
– Meet people: Stroll around the various cars of the train and socialize! Journeys like this are full of opportunities to meet cool new people. 
– Visit the observation car: The California Zephyr is one of the most scenic train routes in the country. Take it all in in the observation car’s panoramic windows and window-facing seats.
