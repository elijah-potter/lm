A janitor in a dimly lit office noticed a loose floorboard near the old elevator shaft. On closer inspection, he found a hidden compartment beneath it, revealing a small, rusted key embedded in the wood. When he tested it, it opened an ancient desk drawer behind a second floor, revealing a stack of yellowed ledgers detailing a decades-old project. The ledgers contained handwritten notes about a classified experiment, including a date: the day the first moon landing was announced. The janitor kept the key, unaware that it was a fragment of a secret history now tied to his discovery.
