A flickering lantern cast long shadows across the abandoned hall’s worn wooden floor, where dust motes swirled in the draft from the shattered skylight. The scent of damp earth and mildew clung to the air, thick as old memories. A half-eaten pie crust lay abandoned on the table, its edges crisp but untouched. Through the fogged glass of the broken window, the silhouettes of distant figures moved like ghosts against the muted glow of a dying fire, their voices muffled and indistinct. The only sound was the creak of creaking timbers and the occasional whisper of a breeze through the rusted iron door.
