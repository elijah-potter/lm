**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 15, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, offering a traditional celebration of agriculture, community, and family entertainment. This year's fair promises a diverse range of attractions, including livestock exhibits showcasing prize-winning animals, demonstrations of traditional crafts, thrilling carnival rides, and a wide variety of food vendors offering classic fair fare. Visitors can anticipate interactive displays highlighting local farming practices and opportunities to engage with members of the agricultural community.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 22nd through August 26th, 2024, at the County Fairgrounds in Springfield. Gates will open at 9:00 AM daily, with evening events continuing until 10:00 PM. A special fireworks display will illuminate the sky on Friday, August 25th, following the evening entertainment. Detailed schedules of events, including specific times for livestock judging and demonstrations, will be available on the fair’s website, SangamonCountyFair.org.

Admission to the fair is $12 for adults, $8 for children ages 6-12, and free for children five and under. Carnival ride tickets and wristbands will be available for purchase on-site. Discounted advance tickets can be purchased online at SangamonCountyFair.org. Parking is $10 per vehicle.

**Headline Events:**

*   Livestock Auction
*   Demolition Derby
*   Professional Rodeo
*   4-H Showcase
*   Tractor Pull Competition
*   Grandstand Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters
