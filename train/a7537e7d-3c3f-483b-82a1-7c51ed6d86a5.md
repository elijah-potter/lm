Stands empty, walls breathe dust.
Sunlight dances across floor, carving shadows like liquid gold.
Furniture hums with silent life—chair whispers to sofa, table sighs under scattered papers.
A lamp flickers, casting long, trembling arms of light.
Books lie open, pages whispering forgotten tales.
Ceiling sighs, ceiling fans spin, spinning in endless circles.
Doors creak, whispering secrets of who walked through.
Glass doors shatter slightly, revealing outside’s restless pulse.
Coffee stains cup, fading into memory.
Mirrors reflect pasts, blurring into present’s faint echoes.
Ceiling leaks, dripping patience.
Vent hums, pulling in air that tastes of old things.
