The rain pounded against the rusted roof of an abandoned warehouse, its metal sheets groaning under the weight. The flickering neon sign outside read *"Last Exit"* in faded letters, now barely discernible beneath a layer of grime. Inside, the air smelled of mildew and old paint. A single flickering bulb hung from the ceiling, casting long, jagged shadows across the floor.

The figure stood in the middle of the dimly lit room, knees slightly bent, staring at the crumbling floorboards beneath their boots. They had no map, no compass, and no idea where they were. The last thing they remembered was the subway station, the smell of diesel and the distant hum of the train. Now, all they could see were the flickering reflections of their own face in the grimy windows, distorted by the rain streaming down like tears.

A distant wail echoed outside, a siren cutting through the storm. The figure clutched their bag tighter, their fingers numb with cold. They had taken a wrong turn somewhere, maybe at the subway exit, or maybe after crossing the street—anything. The city sprawled endlessly beyond the warehouse, a maze of neon signs and distant lights, none of them leading home.

A cold hand settled on their shoulder. They spun around, heart hammering.

"Lost?"

The voice was familiar, but the figure’s mind refused to connect it to a name. They blinked, trying to focus.

"Who are you?" they whispered.

The other person grinned, their eyes glinting in the dim light. "Not lost. Just waiting."

The figure stumbled back, their breath coming in ragged gasps.
