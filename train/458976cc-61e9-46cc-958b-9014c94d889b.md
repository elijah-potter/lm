The floor creaked underfoot as she adjusted her gloves, the cold seeping through her fingers like a forgotten secret. The candle flickered on the desk, casting long shadows that seemed to whisper her name. She had come here for answers, but the ink was thick, the words half-formed, the heart too heavy to speak.

Outside, the city hummed—a million voices, all wrong. The clock ticked like a drum, counting down what she couldn’t name. Her breath came shallow, each exhale a question she dared not ask aloud.

The page was blank. Not blank. *Waiting.* The pen hovered, trembling, as if the paper itself had chosen her.

She exhaled. Then, slowly, she wrote:

*"What if I’m the only one who doesn’t know how to start?"*
