Gormut would take your money while shaking your hand, quite literally. He was a silver-tongued devil—surprisingly handsome—who could burn a hole in the pocket of even the most penny-pinching Scrooge at the space rig. He stood an imposing 9 feet tall, and his 4 arms were more like tentacles, but not quite. His purple hue made him stand out in a crowd, and his soothing, rich voice almost purred rather than spoke.

"Drinks up, coins down," was his catchphrase, an effective loophole of vices that kept his patrons hooked. Gormut would never gamble himself; he was too smart for that. Instead, he would pit two people against one another, snaking words and expressions into a convoluted knot until both parties were ready for a brawl. The ever-helpful Gormut would instead suggest a drink and some games; after all, it was all in good fun. Their better judgment would win out, and they would both be sprawled on the floor a few hours later, the best of friends and, of course, utterly penniless.

There was no telling what went on behind closed doors at Gormut's illustrious establishments, but no one believed it was legitimate. If he could swindle any bargoer out of their hard-earned money, how much had he already taken from the empire? It didn't bear thinking about, and those who probed in Gormut's business around had a peculiar habit of disappearing. No, it was better to keep your head down, enjoy the drinks, and, of course, keep your wits about you.
