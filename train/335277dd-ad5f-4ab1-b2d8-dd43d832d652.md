A cavern glowed faintly with bioluminescent moss draped in shimmering veins of emerald and violet. The walls pulsed softly, a slow, rhythmic hum echoing through the damp air—like a distant heartbeat. Shadows stretched and coiled like living things, twisting around the edges of the chamber’s dim light. The air carried the scent of damp earth and something older, something *watched*. At the center, a circular pool of stagnant water reflected not its own surface but a shifting, semi-transparent form—like a mirror watching itself. The moment you stepped inside, the air itself seemed to lean in, waiting.
