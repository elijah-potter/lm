Google Classroom: An Intro to Working with Data, Preset Formulas, and More in Google Sheets
Google Classroom allows easy access to a variety of free online apps, including Google Sheets, a powerful app that you and your students can use to enter and analyze data on collaborative spreadsheets. Google Sheets are easy to use, but it can be helpful to know some basic features and tips so that you can readily begin building, formatting, and sharing data and spreadsheets. Join Kim Munoz as she demonstrates step-by-step how to create a basic Google Sheet in Google Classroom. As part of creating a sheet, she will show how to enter and format data and cells. In addition, Kim will show how to apply simple formulas, insert rows and columns, and more. Kim will also cover how to share, find, and organize these sheets within Google Classroom.
