**Mystic Hollow**
A dimly lit, circular plaza in the heart of an abandoned mining town, where the air hums with static electricity from rusted metal sculptures. The center features a cracked, reflective pool mirroring the faces of strangers who linger, their movements synchronized in eerie harmony. No official sign marks the space, yet it’s always full—during dawn, midday, or midnight—with no apparent purpose. Locals whisper that the plaza "chooses" attendees, while outsiders are drawn by a faint, pulsating glow emanating from the ground beneath. Some claim to hear whispers when the wind stirs through the skeletal trees framing the space. The only rule: those who stay too long report waking up with no memory of how they arrived. The town’s last recorded census listed 123 people—now, the plaza holds 150.
