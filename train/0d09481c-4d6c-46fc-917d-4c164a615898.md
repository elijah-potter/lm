A gentle humming in the bones, soft as morning dew, dissolving the weight of the past. The air feels thick with unseen warmth, pressing against the skin like a slow, steady breath. Shadows stretch and bend, but not as they usually do—more like liquid, melting into the edges of perception. The world tilts just enough that everything feels slightly alive, alive with possibility. The heart doesn’t race; it hums along with the quiet current, neither pushing nor pulling, but carried. The future is a place of light, not fear, but something brighter than expectation.
