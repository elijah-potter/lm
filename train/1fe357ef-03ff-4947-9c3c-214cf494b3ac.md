The void hums with unseen voices—each breath a hollow echo of what was never said.
Fingers trace the edges of silence, where absence is carved into the skin of the world.
The clock’s hands move, but time itself is stretched thin, a thread unraveling into nothingness.
Memories linger like ghosts in a room no one enters, whispering names no one remembers.
Light fades into the same gray, the air thick with the scent of something rotten and unclaimed.
The absence isn’t gone—it’s everywhere, pressing against the ribs of the present, a shadow that doesn’t let go.
You find yourself standing in a space that isn’t yours, yet you belong here. The door is closed.
You are both here and not, a ghost in the making, learning to move through the ghostly world of what was.
