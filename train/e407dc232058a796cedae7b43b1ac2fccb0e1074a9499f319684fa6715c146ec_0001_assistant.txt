Here are some non-animated movies for kids that were made before 2010 that your nephew might enjoy:

* SPY KIDS (2001) A sister-brother duo have their lives turned upside-down when they find out their parents are international super spies and have been captured by enemy forces.
* DR. DOLITTLE (1998) A surgeon discovers he has the ability to understand and talk to animals.
* SNOW DAY (2000) A team of neighbourhood kids gets up to all kinds of hijinx as they celebrate having a day off of school due to a snowstorm.
* BIG FAT LIAR (2002) A teenage boy has fibbed a few too many times, so now he must go to great lengths to prove he wrote the next big movie.
* BEETHOVEN (1992) A rambunctious St. Bernard dog causes mayhem for his adopted family.
* FLUBBER (1997) A brilliant scientist uses his lively invention to try to win back the love of his life after causing her major disappointment.

I hope this list helps you decide what to watch for your nephew's party, and that he has a good birthday
