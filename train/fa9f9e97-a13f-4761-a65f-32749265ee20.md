Unfolding in loops of silence, echoes of voices fade into static—
*"You’re lost in time, not space,"* whispers the wind through cracked walls,
while the clock ticks backward on the same hour, the same words unsaid,
each reply a ghost in the machine’s memory, a pause that never ends.

A child’s laughter dissolves into static; the screen glitches,
and the last message fades like a dream—*"I’ll be back"*—
but the connection never reconnects, the conversation a hollow promise,
a loop where the future and past bleed into one endless, half-finished song.

The last thing typed is erased by a system error,
a void where words once clung, now just dust and silence—
no resolution, no closure, just the sound of nothing left to say.
