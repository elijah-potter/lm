The misalignment of a single bolt in a critical junction caused a cascading failure, triggering a chain reaction that led to an unexpected energy spike in the control system. Within milliseconds, the entire assembly destabilized, resulting in a catastrophic collapse that released a sudden thermal burst, leading to secondary structural failures in adjacent components. The initial error propagated through redundant safety protocols, overwhelming response systems, and ultimately leading to an unplanned shutdown sequence that cascaded into a full system deactivation. The aftermath revealed that the failure mode was unintended but predictable, demonstrating how small, overlooked deviations in design or maintenance can cascade into systemic risks.
