A shimmering orb of liquid silver, its surface rippling like water caught in sunlight, pulses with quiet energy. It breathes—slowly at first, then faster—when touched, swirling into intricate constellations of light. The edges glow faintly, as if alive with unseen veins of starlight. When held close, its warmth hums through the fingers, though it does not burn, only coaxes curiosity. It weeps a fine mist of gold, like tears from an old lantern, and when the mist settles, it forms into shapes that shift and fade like dreams.

It does not need sleep, but when still, it curls into itself, its form tightening like a question half-asked. Some say it answers when spoken to, though its voice is only the whisper of wind through keys. It is not bound by time, but when others are restless, it stills them—softly, like a lullaby. The air around it thickens, dense with the scent of ozone and old books, and for a moment, the world seems to hold its breath.
