Congratulations on your new bundle of joy! Here are some creative ways to announce your pregnancy that could incorporate your farm:

- Take a picture of yourself holding a basket of eggs with a baby chick in the basket. You can have a sign or just caption the picture saying, "Hatching a new addition to the family". 

- Take a picture of yourself in the cornfield with a pair of baby boots and caption the picture, "Our farm is growing by two feet!". 

- Take a picture on a tractor in a field holding your sonogram and caption the picture, "We're planting a little farmer this year". 

You can add other touches to these ideas by adding "Baby {Last Name} coming {Due Date}" for more flair as well.
