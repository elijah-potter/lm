A dimly lit alleyway at dusk, the air thick with the scent of damp pavement and distant rain. Shadows stretch long across the cracked asphalt, where a lone figure hunched over a rusted bicycle lock, fingers twitching as they struggle with the rusted mechanism. The city hums in the distance—a distant siren, the murmur of traffic. The figure, hunched low, doesn’t turn around; their breath fogs the cold air, but their eyes remain fixed on the lock, a stubborn determination etched in their jaw. The lock clicks open at last, and they push the bicycle away with a sigh, vanishing into the crowd before anyone can ask why.
