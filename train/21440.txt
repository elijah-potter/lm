Do you know about Blockchain?
Blockchain is the database or ledger for storing info. In the Blockchain, you can only add data, and every information that is fed into the system is encrypted cryptographically. Tus presents the data from a data theft attempt. Time-stamping makes tracking the data easy. These factors help in securing the data records. As a result, there is no need for any third party due to the presence of blockchain technology.
