Yes, there are many ideas for lower-calorie breakfasts. Here are three that have potato as one of the ingredients.
1. Breakfast Quesadilla - Made with 1 cup vegetables (greens, peppers, onions), 1/2 cup sweet potato, 1 tortilla, and 1 string cheese.
2. Breakfast Bowl - Made with 1 cup of air-fried potatoes, 1 egg, 1 turkey sausage link, and a cup of spinach.
3. Breakfast Hash - Made with 1/2 sweet potatoes, 1 slice bacon, 1 sweet pepper all diced and cooked in a skillet with 1 egg.
