SOME types of people--not me--prefer cottage cheese ice cream because it makes them feel like they're eating ice cream for breakfast, which is crude and childish, but I digress. I suppose that some of us never grew out of that childish "I wish I could have sweets for breakfast!" mentality. In any case, this cheap recipe requires only a few ingredients that one can buy at their local Shop 'n' Save or another low-class grocery store. Children may like this recipe--I don't know what parents would give their kids this much sugar in the morning, but I suppose that not everyone has a nanny to cook for them. Adding clean ingredients, such as granola and fruit, make this dish healthier if one can afford them.
