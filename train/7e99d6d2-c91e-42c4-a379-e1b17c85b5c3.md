The first light of dawn crept through the cracked window, painting the room in hues of soft amber. Elias sat at his desk, fingers tracing the rim of his coffee cup, the steam curling lazily upward. Outside, the city woke to the hum of distant traffic, the distant murmur of voices lost in the morning chaos.

He had spent the night working on the final draft of *The Hollow Crown*, his latest novel. The plot had been a labyrinth of betrayal, survival, and the quiet desperation of those who had outlasted the war. But tonight, as he leaned back in his chair, the words blurred into something else—a question that gnawed at his ribs like a rat in the walls.

*"What if the answers were already inside him, waiting to be found?"*

A knock at the door shattered the silence. His mother’s voice, sharp and tired, called out, *"Elias, are you coming down?"* He didn’t answer. Instead, he stared at the empty page before him, the ink smudged from his fingers, and wondered if the story was even real—or if it was just another lie he’d been told all along.
