The Eternal Tide of Veythar: a shifting marshland where the earth itself breathes. Sand dunes, ever reshaping, form and vanish like whispers in the wind. Rivers carve new paths daily, while the sky—once blue—turns to smoky grays, then violet, then endless black as storms spin without pattern. Bridges made of driftwood and coral dissolve before sunset. The few who dwell here live in hollowed-out shells of driftwood or woven reeds, their homes rising and falling with the tide. Time is measured not in years, but in the fleeting colors of the horizon. There are no landmarks, only the scent of salt and damp earth, and the unspoken question: *What will be here tomorrow?*
