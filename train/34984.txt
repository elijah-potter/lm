This task card printable helps children reinforce the concept of counting coins. Children will add up all the coins on the cards first. They will ask themselves "Is the amount on the turkey correct"? Recording sheet is provided to mark their answers. They can put the cards in the envelopes marked YES or NO as well. An answer sheet is provided.
--24 task cards
Suggestions, tips, and differentiation:
* Can be used for assessment purposes.
* Can be used in small group or at a math center.
* Can be used in whole group as in circle time.
* The cards with the correct amount on the turkey can be used to order coin amounts from least to greatest or vice versa.
If separated, put a matching sticker on the backs of the pieces that go together. When the child is finished, have them turn over the cards to see if the stickers match.
