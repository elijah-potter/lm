A sudden flash of golden sunlight bathed the alley as the streetlights flickered out, their dim glow replaced by a harsh, unnatural blue. Shadows stretched erratically, twisting like smoke. A cold wind howled through the broken glass of a half-demolished storefront, scattering debris onto cracked pavement. The air hummed with static, and distant sirens wailed through the distortion. In the distance, a flickering neon sign pulsed erratically, casting jagged shadows on the wet pavement. The moment felt like a breath held too long—then the light returned, sharp and steady, leaving the scene unchanged except for the strange, lingering silence.
