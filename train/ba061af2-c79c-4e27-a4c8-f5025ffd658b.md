A dimly lit alley behind the old bookstore on Maple Street, where fluorescent lights flicker erratically and shadows stretch long across the cracked pavement. The scent of damp concrete and old leather books clings to the air. The few who venture here—mostly late-night shoppers or those passing through—keep their heads down, hands in pockets, or face away from the center. The walls are lined with graffiti in faded colors, some of it still legible: *"Stay away"* or *"Not here."* The sound of distant sirens and the occasional laugh from a group in the corner adds to the uneasy atmosphere. People avoid eye contact as if the space itself holds a silent judgment.
