Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big yellow door, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy pink ears and a tail that wiggled like a spring. Every morning, Benny would hop outside to see his friends: a red bird named Robin, a gray squirrel named Squish, and a little turtle named Tommy.

One sunny day, Benny found a shiny red apple on his way home. "Mmm, what a yummy apple!" Benny said, sniffing it. He picked it up and started to eat it, but suddenly, he heard a tiny voice. "Help! Help!" It was Robin, who had fallen into a big, deep hole. Benny gasped. "Oh no, Robin!"

Benny remembered his mommy always said, "A problem shared is a problem half solved!" So, Benny took his little paws and started digging. He dug and dug, but the hole was too deep. "I need help!" Benny called out.

Squish came running. "Let me try!" Squish dug with his tiny claws. Then Tommy appeared. "I can help too!" Tommy took his slow but steady steps and dug beside them. After a long time, they dug until Robin was free. "Thank you!" Robin chirped happily.

From that day on, Benny, Robin, Squish, and Tommy always helped each other. They shared their toys, cheered each other on, and ate their snacks together. And whenever a little friend needed help, they were there, ready to dig, sing, and play to save the day.
