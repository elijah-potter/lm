The letter arrived late, the ink smudged on the edges, the handwriting slanted like a child’s. Inside was a single sheet, folded twice, the corners worn from years of handling. The recipient had always believed their partner’s confession about the stolen watch was a joke—until the watch, hidden in the closet, bore the same engraving as the one the partner had "borrowed" from their own pocket. The misunderstanding, rooted in a shared habit of leaving keys and trinkets on the dresser, had been a game until the truth unraveled in the reflection of a cracked mirror.
