Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around the garden, eating carrots and playing with his best friend, a squirrel named Sammy.

One sunny afternoon, Benny spotted a shiny red apple that had fallen from a tree. "Sammy, look! A big, juicy apple!" Benny said, hopping excitedly. Sammy chattered happily, "Let's share it!"

They carefully picked the apple and sat under the tree. Benny nibbled a tiny bite, but the apple was too big for him. "Sammy, this apple is too big," Benny said, looking at it. Sammy smiled and said, "I'll help you cut it into smaller pieces."

With careful paws, Sammy split the apple into little pieces. Benny took one, and Sammy took one too. They shared the sweet fruit, and both felt happy and full.

After eating, Benny and Sammy decided to play a game. They built a little fort with leaves and sticks, then sang songs and laughed together. Benny and Sammy's friendship made every day bright and wonderful. And from that day on, Benny and Sammy knew that sharing and playing together made the garden the happiest place to be.
