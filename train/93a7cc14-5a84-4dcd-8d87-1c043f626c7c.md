**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 22, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this year, promising a celebration of agriculture, community, and family fun. This year’s fair will feature a diverse array of livestock exhibits, agricultural displays, live music, and traditional fair games and rides, showcasing the rich heritage of the region and offering entertainment for all ages. Visitors can expect to see award-winning animals, sample local food vendors, and enjoy a vibrant atmosphere highlighting the best of Sangamon County.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from Friday, August 30th, through Sunday, September 1st, 2024, at the County Fairgrounds. Gates will open daily at 9:00 a.m., and closing times will vary; a detailed schedule of events will be available on the fair’s website, sangamoncountyfair.org. This year’s theme, "Celebrating Our Roots," will be reflected throughout the fairgrounds, highlighting the importance of agriculture and community in the county's history.

Admission to the Sangamon County Fair is $10 for adults, $5 for children ages 5-12, and free for children under 5. Parking is $5 per vehicle. Discounted advance tickets are available online at sangamoncountyfair.org. A season pass, granting access to all three days of the fair, is also available for purchase.

**Headline Events:**

*   Livestock Judging Competitions
*   Demolition Derby
*   Grandstand Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters
*   4-H Livestock Auction
*   Home Arts & Crafts Exhibition
*   Evening Fireworks Display
