A midday meeting, fluorescent lights buzzing overhead, the usual hum of office life—until a pause. Sarah paused.

She had prepared this presentation for weeks, every slide meticulously crafted to justify her promotion. The numbers on the screen were flawless, the data undeniable. But then her colleague, Jake, raised his hand.

*"Wait—what if we look at the customer retention rate instead?"*

Her fingers tightened on the mouse. The data was solid, but retention? That was her personal obsession. She had spent sleepless nights tracking it. *They’d see her as more than just a number.*

She exhaled. *"Actually… that’s a better angle."*
