There once was a camel named Lollypeeps that woke up very far from home. Lollypeeps had arisen from a dream about his camel bride, and was immediately startled into attention by his cat-like senses for danger; they were going off prudently. Lollypeeps felt something was amiss.    
     As Lollypeeps came to his senses, it seemed to be a dire situation. His face was covered in snow. His hump was sticking in the air negligently to his body. He was shaking from the cold, and slowly and suddenly realized that he was far from home. 
     He stood up on his feet. He slowly raised his neck and fixated his eyes on the sky before him, and suddenly he couldn’t believe his own eyes. A penguin was zigging and zagging through the air, just waiting for the chance to dive in at a 180-degree angle to the curve of Lollypeep’s hump. Suddenly, the penguin swooped down.
     The penguin made contact with the freezing Camel’s trembling hump. It was a perfect landing! The penguin soared down the curve of the camel’s bump with not too much friction, but just enough to warm Lollypeeps up from the cold. The penguin made it all the way down the decline to the bottom arch of Lollypeeps’ hump effortlessly, folded back their penguin wings, and soared up the incline of the camel’s hump, finally landing on Lollypeeps’ head.
     Lollypeeps thanked the penguin vigorously for warming him up. The penguin smiled mysteriously, and they were best friends forever.
