A vast, echoing hall in an abandoned orbital station, its walls lined with rusted corridors and defunct machinery. The air hums with residual static from decommissioned systems, the only sound the distant, rhythmic drip of condensation pooling in corroded pipes. The ceiling is a mesh of broken solar panels, half-suspended, their shadows stretching across the floor like restless fingers. Empty seats in the observation deck—once filled with astronauts—now hold only the faint scent of ozone and the ghostly glow of a dead navigation screen. The weight of silence presses in like a presence, every empty space feeling like a potential whisper of the past.
