1. State: Alabama 
Bird: Northern Flicker
- This bird is medium-sized and a part of the woodpecker family. It’s known by the name “yellowhammer” in the state of Alabama because when it flies, the under-part of its wings are yellow. These birds eat bugs and worms out of the ground, so it’s not uncommon to find one at ground level.

2. State: Alaska 
Bird: Willow Ptarmigan
- This bird uses camouflage to protect itself during different seasons. In the winter, it’s white in color while in the summer, they are a mix of reds and browns. They are heavily feathered to protect themselves against extreme cold and can burrow deep into the snow for protection against outside elements.

3. State: Arizona
Bird: Cactus Wren
- The cactus wren is a noisy bird that makes raw, scratchy sounds. They are very busy little birds and are often seen hopping around, fanning their tails, or singing loudly on top of cacti plants.

4. State: Arkansas
Bird: Northern Mockingbird
- These birds are full of songs and rarely stop “singing.” They are pale gray in color and are fierce defenders of their territories.

5. State: California
Bird: California Quail
- This bird’s shape is very round and has a gray breast and forward-drooping head plume. It has a “Chi-ca-go” call sound and can often be seen scratching at the ground. They also travel in large groups so you don’t often spot just one or two hanging around.

6. State: Colorado
Bird: Lark Bunting
- The lark bunting birds are a breed of handsome sparrows and the males boast brilliant black with white wing coverts. The females are sandy brown in color. These birds feed with other sparrow species or quail in flocks.

7. State: Connecticut
Bird: American Robin
- These birds are commonly found tugging earthworms out of the ground and are known for their warm orange breasts. They have a cheery song and live in either the city or the mountains and wilderness areas. 

8.  State: Delaware
Bird: Blue Hen Chicken 
- The blue hen is a strain of American gamecock. These chickens were known for their fighting abilities back when fighting was legal in the United States. 

9. State: Florida
Bird: Northern Mockingbird
- The northern mockingbirds are very noisy and rarely stop “singing.” Their bodies are pale gray and they flash their white wing patches to ward off intruders. 

10. State: Georgia
Bird: Brown Thrasher
- The brown thrasher birds hide in shrubbery and are very hard to spot. They have a down-curved bill with yellow eyes and are known for their jubilant song singing.
