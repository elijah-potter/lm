Pinal is a county in Arizona. It was formed in 1875 from the following county/ies: Pima/Yavapai . Pinal began keeping birth records in 1887, marriage records in 1875, and death records in 1887. It began keeping land records in 1875, probate records in 1875, and court records in 1883. For more information, contact the county at 31 N. Pinal St, Florence 85232.
