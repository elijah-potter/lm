Here are three facts about dance pioneer Katherine Dunham: 

1. Katherine Dunham invented the Dunham technique, which fused classical ballet techniques with Afro-Carribean dance styles.
2. Katherine Dunham was a dancer and choreographer, who choreographed more than 90 solo dances.
3. In addition to her excellence in dance, Katherine Dunham was a scholar in the field of anthropology and wrote many books on the subject including "Journey to Accompong."
