The flickering streetlights cast long shadows across the cracked asphalt of a quiet suburban street. A sudden *thud* from upstairs jolts **Daniel** from his half-asleep state. He tosses his phone onto the kitchen table—battery at 12%, and the screen flickers red.

*"Power outage,"* he whispers, checking the news app’s last update: *"Widespread blackout in Sector 7 due to storm surge. Roads closed."* Outside, sirens wail in the distance, a distant rumble of generators.

The fridge creaks open, revealing a single stale sandwich wrapped in foil. He tosses it into the trash can labeled *"DO NOT EAT"*—his emergency stash was *supposed* to last three days. His laptop hums weakly behind him, the power strip’s single outlet glowing a sickly green.

A child’s giggle from the living room. **Lena**, 8, is playing *Minecraft* on her tablet—her battery at 100%. The screen’s glow pulses against the dimness as she tosses a virtual block. *"Mom, why’s the sky black?"*

Daniel exhales. *"Storm. Like magic."*

She grins. *"Like in the movie."*

He hesitates. Then, softly: *"Yeah."*
