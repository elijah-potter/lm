*"In 1895, Wilhelm Conrad Röntgen’s accidental discovery of X-rays—initially called 'Röntgen rays'—revolutionized medicine, revealing bones and internal structures in a matter of seconds. This invisible, penetrating radiation shattered centuries of medical guesswork, transforming diagnostics from guesswork to precision. By 1901, Nobel Prize-winning, it became the foundation for radiology, oncology, and modern imaging technologies. Though unintentional, Röntgen’s breakthrough didn’t just expose the unseen—it unlocked a new era of science, where what seemed impossible became routine. The real magic? It started with a spark of curiosity and a misplaced tube of potassium dichromate."*
