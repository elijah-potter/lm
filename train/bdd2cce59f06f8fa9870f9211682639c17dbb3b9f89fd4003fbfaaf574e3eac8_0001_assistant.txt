Here's your list of five suggested names for your non-profit:

1. Empowered Emotions - This will address the idea of emotional regulation specifically while conveying that you want to empower your participants by giving them the skills they need.
2. Balance - This is a strong but gentle, single-word name highlighting the ultimate goal of emotional balance and resilience through the dysregulation that many DBT clients struggle with.
3. Steadfast Support Services - This is a more direct name that will convey that you are a service organization as well as your intention to provide steadfast support, an expectation many DBT therapy clients in particular need.
4. Mindful Healing Collective - As mindfulness is a key element of DBT, this name expresses its importance in the therapeutic healing process.
5. The Wise Mind Wellness Center - Unique to DBT, using the phrase 'Wise Mind' will speak to your target audience without isolating or intimidating those new to DBT.
