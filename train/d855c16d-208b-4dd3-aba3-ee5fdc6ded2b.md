The half-remembered fragments drift like echoes in a fading room—
a face half-lost in time, a name whispered in a language no longer spoken,
a melody hummed on the brink of understanding. The mind, though weary,
clings to the ghosts of things almost known, where edges blur and identities dissolve into the mist of forgotten hours.
A story half-lived, a truth half-told, a love that lingers in the space between memory and absence.
