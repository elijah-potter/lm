A hollow vow carved into the hollow of a winter night—
the promise whispered on a breath too thin, like smoke in a draft.
*"I’ll find you again,"* they said, though the world had already outrun them,
its roads swallowed by shadows where the lost vanish without a trace.

The clock ticks relentlessly, each second a lie, each hour a betrayal.
The words still echo—*I’ll* be back—but the "I" that signed it is dust,
and the "you" who waited? A ghost at the crossroads, forever searching
for a hand that never reached.

The sky is a canvas of promises unfulfilled,
painted in the colors of regret and the faint glow of a dying ember.
No one believes the impossible now.
The night is the only witness left.
