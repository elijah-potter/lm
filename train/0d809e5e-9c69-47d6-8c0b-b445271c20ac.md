Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had soft, fluffy ears and a tail that wiggled like a rainbow.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to explore. "Wow!" Benny said, looking at the bright blue sky. "I see clouds that look like fish! And trees that are so tall they touch the clouds!"

Benny found a tiny puddle in the grass. He sat down and splashed his paws. "Mmm, this feels good!" he giggled. Then he saw something shiny—it was a tiny, glittery acorn!

Benny picked it up and tucked it into his burrow. "This might be special," he thought. As the day turned golden, Benny curled up in his nest, dreaming of adventures under the moon.

The next morning, Benny woke up to find his acorn had grown into a big, round tree! All the animals came to visit. "Look!" Benny said. "Our tree has flowers, and bees buzz around them!"

The little bunny smiled, knowing his little acorn had grown into something wonderful. And that was how Benny became the happiest bunny in the whole world, sharing joy with everyone.
