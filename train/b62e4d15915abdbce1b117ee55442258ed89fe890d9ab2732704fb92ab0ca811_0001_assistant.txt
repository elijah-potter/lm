On Wednesday, Turkey gifted Mexico's army a 3-month-old German Shepherd after the death of a Mexican rescue dog that was on a mission to find survivors of Fedurary's massive earthquake. After an online vote, the new search puppy has been named "Arkadas" which means "friend" in Turkish. Arkadas will be trained by the same trainer who took care of the rescue dog that died while on a mission in Turkey. The Mexican army has paid homage to the fallen rescue dog with a military funeral and has welcomed the new rescue puppy with a formal ceremony.
