He had traveled to hundreds of locations around the world, but no matter how busy his life became, he always tried to wake up in time for the sunrise. It was always the same sun rising each day, but like him, it traveled from place to place and took on something new in each. The sunrise was not the same in emerging from an empty horizon on the Pacific Ocean as it was climbing up over the mountains on a lazy morning. Somehow, the sunrise could be both his most steadfast companion and a new beauty to enjoy from each place.
