Stroke is not always fatal. It all depends on how fast you will react and recognize it. Here are four symptoms by which you can recognize stroke:
- Ask the person to smile (he or she will not be able to)
- Ask the person to pronounce one simple sentence (He or she will have trouble saying it)
- Ask the person to pull out its tongue (If his or hers tongue is swinging from one side to the other, the person have had a stroke)
- Ask the person to lift his/hers arms (Either they will not be able to or they will lift their arms but only partially)
If the person has symptoms such the above, immediately call a doctor.
