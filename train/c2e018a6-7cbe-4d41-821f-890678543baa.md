A gradual evolution occurs in micro-transformations, often unnoticed:
1. **Initiation**: An initial shift—tiny adjustments in routine or perspective.
2. **Incremental Growth**: Small, consistent actions accumulate like layers in sediment.
3. **Momentum**: Subtle reinforcement of each step compounds, amplifying impact.
4. **Threshold**: Unseen tipping points emerge where effort yields tangible results.
5. **Outcome**: The cumulative effect becomes irreversible—what once was invisible now becomes visible.

*Example*: Learning a language—first listening, then speaking, then writing, then fluency.
