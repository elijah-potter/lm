I quickly scan the room for any signs of the bomb. I spot a metal door with a keypad and a red light above it. That must be it. I run towards the door and try to hack the code using my spy gadget. I hear footsteps behind me and turn around to see two armed guards approaching. I grab my silenced pistol and shoot them both in the head before they can raise the alarm.

I enter the code and the door opens. Inside, I see a large metal cylinder with wires and buttons attached to it. It looks like a nuclear bomb alright. I check the timer and see that I have only 5 minutes left. I look for the manual override switch, but it’s nowhere to be found. I realize that the bomb is rigged to explode if tampered with. I need to find another way to stop it.

I remember that I have a small explosive device in my pocket. It’s not powerful enough to destroy the bomb, but maybe it can disrupt its circuitry. I attach the device to the bomb and set it to detonate in 10 seconds. I hope that it will create enough of a short circuit to disable the bomb without triggering it. I run out of the room and close the door behind me. I hear a loud bang and see sparks flying from the door. I check my gadget and see that the bomb’s signal has gone dead. I did it.
