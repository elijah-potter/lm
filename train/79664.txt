Insects may be the most important group of animals in supporting our survival, but we all know how creepy most of them are. Creatures with lots of legs scurrying around dark corners of our homes never cease to terrify us. No matter how much we hate to think of it, they are everywhere.
One of the scariest aspects of the bug world is their ability to camouflage themselves. They can appear as a rock or a leaf at first, but get too close and you’ll have a crawly critter running up your leg in no time. That sounds horrifying!
A video taken by YouTube user Josh Markiewicz shows what appears to be a caddisfly doing something that is sure to send shivers down your spine…
A YouTube user recently capture footage of what appears to be a caddisfly in its cocoon. At first, it looks more like an ordinary rock than a living creature, but the video rolls on to reveal something pretty disturbing…
Wait, is something emerging from inside?…What is that?
