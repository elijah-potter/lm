You're waking up to another beautiful summer day as you walk over to your balcony and bask in the sunlight. The night was cold, though, and you see a large, shiny beetle in the shade that's stuck on its back and struggling to get up. Sorry to see it struggling, you flip it over and nudge it into the sun. A moment later, it's nice and warm and has gotten the energy that it needs to fly off into the day, the light glinting off of its shell along the way. There's something poetic about it, and you begin going about your day in a good mood. 

Weeks go by, and you've forgotten the beetle entirely. Somehow, it seems that the beetle may have remembered you. It's late at night, and a suspicious person is bothering you. They won't leave, and just as you're starting to get worried, the beetle flies in, its wings buzzing like an engine to the point that the noise freaks the person out and sends them running. Was it just a coincidence, or was it the very same bug you'd helped before? You never did find out.
