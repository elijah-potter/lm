Lasers shot across the sky in sporadic beams. People from all through the neighborhood ran past the living room window as Stephanie watched in horror. It was all her fault. She had somehow woken the baby from his nap, and he was angry about the interruption. 

Stephanie was in shock. As she tried to call his parents, she racked her brain for any information his mom had given her. There was the usual list of emergency numbers in case the house caught on fire, the Wi-Fi password, the rules about boys—none of it clued Stephanie into the fact that the baby was a literal ticking timebomb!

The phone rang and rang, but there was no answer. She would have to take matters into her own hands. Stephanie strapped on her sandals and threw a bottle into her back pocket. She rifled through the pantry and found some cookies. "Here I come, baby."
