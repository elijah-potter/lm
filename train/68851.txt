Overview of Iran Weather
Iran is a large country located in the Middle East. It has a varied climate, with different regions experiencing different weather conditions. The northern part of the country has a temperate climate, while the southern and central regions have a hot, dry climate. The mountainous regions have a cold, dry climate.
The average temperatures in Iran vary depending on the region. In the north, the average temperature in January is -4 degrees Celsius, and in July it is 22 degrees Celsius. In the south, the average temperature in January is 16 degrees Celsius, and in July it is 36 degrees Celsius.
The rainfall in Iran also varies depending on the region. The northern part of the country receives the most rainfall, while the southern and central regions receive very little rainfall.
Iran experiences a variety of weather conditions, from hot and dry to cold and snowy. Whatever the weather, there is always something to enjoy in this beautiful country.
