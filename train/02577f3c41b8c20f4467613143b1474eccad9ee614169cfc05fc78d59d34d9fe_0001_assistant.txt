Sam: "Frodo!"

[Frodo stands at on a ledge above a sea of molten lava.]

Frodo: "I'm here, Sam."

Sam: "Destroy it!"

[Frodo holds the Duck out above the lava.]

Sam: "Go on. Now! Throw it in the fire!"

[Frodo hesitates. The Duck begins to quack in the Black Speech.]

Sam: "What are you waiting for? Just let it go!"

[Frodo stares at the Duck.]

[Frodo pauses, then cups it in his hands. He turns to Sam.]

Frodo: "The Duck is mine!"
[Frodo brings The Duck up to his face.]

Sam: "No. No!"

[Frodo squeezes the duck and it lets out a terrifying quack. Frodo transforms into a hobbit-duck]

Sam: "No!"
