Bald eagles are predominantly found in North America. They are typically found in a wide span of area from Mexico up to Canada. They are also very popular in Alaska, along the coasts, the Rocky Mountains, and the Mississippi River area. Other areas in the United state can see bald eagles during their winter migration. They tend to gather near lakes and prefer areas with fish and forests. They have been seen near unfrozen lakes in the winter and hunting the coast. During migration times, they are seen near any type of water. Bald eagles mostly like fish. They are predators and when fish is not readily available, they will eat whatever they can catch. That includes rodents and other small birds. They can scavage for food as well, stealing food from other birds if the opportunity arises.
