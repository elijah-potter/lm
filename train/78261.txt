Once a month I like to do a read aloud in the library that then extends into a written assignment where the students create a page in a class book. This lesson is a read aloud of the book Billy & Milly Short & Silly by Eve B. Feldman. There is then a short phonics/word family lesson. Students will use a word family pattern to write their own short and often silly story.
List of Objectives
Book handouts (2 layouts) and one editable
Book Cover and editable
Rime cards of 30 most popular rimes
Onset cards, basic consonants, blends and diagraphs
