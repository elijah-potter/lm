We offer One-on-One brain training with a unique approach. Our procedures involve training all major cognitive skills, including working memory, Long- term memory, attention, processing speed, logic & reasoning, and visual & auditory processing. The BrainRx program is a twelve- to thirty-two-week, intense, one-on-one, cognitive training program that corrects and enhances learning skills.
Our process involves a pre-cognitive assessment as well as a post-cognitive assessment.
BrainRx applies the most recent scientific research on learning and is at the forefront of making sure the most up-to-date research is applied in practical ways to help people of all ages.
BrainRx takes a different approach to learning problems than other systems. Most seek to manage the symptoms. We seek to eliminate them at their core.
So where does BrainRx fit in? BrainRx training enhances skills necessary to learn at a cognitive level.
BrainRx concentrates on skills development at the cognitive level. These cognitive abilities process sensory information and are essential for easy, fast, academic learning. BrainRx does NOT teach academic content, but we will help make learning easier and faster. The ability to receive and acquire academic skills is therefore dependent upon the strength of the underlying cognitive skills.
At BrainRx we do not assume that all learning problems are
the result of poor cognitive processing skills or that everyone needs cognitive training. Instead, we use skill tests that probe different areas of the individual’s cognitive skills set to see if there are any deficiencies we can address.
We know that cognitive skills can be enhanced not only because we can see the changes through observation and tests, but also because there is evidence derived from brain research.
