The old clock tower stood silent at dusk, its hands frozen at 3:27, marking the hour when the last lanterns burned out in the city’s forgotten alleys. A child found a coin beneath its shadow—a silver piece with a single, worn word: **"BEGIN"** engraved upon it. They placed it in their pocket and ran to the riverbank, where the water lapped at the stones like a whisper.

The river was a river of echoes. The child dropped the coin into the current, and the next morning, the same coin was found at the edge of the forest, where a lone oak tree swayed over a stream. The tree’s roots curled around the metal, and its bark split to reveal a hollow space where the word **"BEGIN"** glowed faintly, pulsing like a heartbeat.

The child followed the roots until they led to a cave, where a mirror hung from the ceiling, reflecting not their face but the tower’s hands, now at **12:00**. Inside the mirror, the clock’s face was cracked, and the lanterns flickered—**3:27 again**. The coin was gone, replaced by a single, perfect rose petal. The child picked it up and returned to the riverbank, where the water still whispered the same.

The coin reappeared in their pocket. They held it up to the light, and the word **"BEGIN"** shimmered—then dissolved into the wind, leaving only the rose petal and the sound of the clock’s hands ticking back to **3:27**.
