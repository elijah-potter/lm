Casting, Forging & Moulding Machines
The process with which a liquid material is given a shape with the help of a mould is called casting. The mould has a cavity of a particular shape, when the liquid is poured and solidified, it takes the shape of the mould. Casting is basically the solidifying part. For making a complex shape, casting is much cheaper than other alternatives like the fabrication of small parts.
As per the moulding machine manufacturers, the manufacturing of metal items through the process of casting is termed die casting. This means of production is very fast and helps in rapid production that too at a lower cost. Die casting comes with a number of benefits –
Different types of die casting machines are –
- Items manufactured in casting method bears a long-lasting life.
- This method is much more reliable for uniformity in products.
- This is quite a fast means of production.
- Die casting makes it possible to creates practically any designs and shapes.
- Pressure Die Casting Machine: It is a quick process and incurs a low cost in production. This machine is also very reliable. As the name suggests, it injects an immense amount of pressure to melt metals and preparing alloys. The liquid alloyed metal is then poured into the casting mould which then solidifies into the required shape.
- Chamber Die Casting Machine: In a die casting machine, the liquified metal is poured into the shaped cavity. There are two types of chamber die casting – hot and cold. In hot die casting, the metal is melted within the cavity, therefore the metal cast in shape is hot. Whereas in a cold die casting machine, the metal is melted in a different furnace and then the liquid metal is poured into the cast. Forging is a process that involves the shaping of materials by applications of localised comprehensive forces. Very common household items such as kitchenware, hand tools, hardware, edged weapons and jewellery are made by the process of forging.
In warm or hot forging, the metal is melted in a forge, within the machine. But in cold forging, the metal is liquified in a different furnace. According to forging machine exporter, forging is less expensive than casting. Hot forging is a much more convenient process than cold forging.
Types of forging are –
- Mechanical Forging Presses: This consist of a vertical moving ram that performs a squeezing action in the working item. This machine is driven by a motor and controlled by an air clutch. This makes the crankshaft a vertically operating ram in constant length strokes.
- Hydraulic Forging Presses: This has a slower ram speed. Therefore, hot forging is avoided in these machines. Large tonnage applications are conducted in hydraulic forging presses.

Moulding is another manufacturing process by the means of giving shape to pliable and liquid tar materials. This shape is given using a structured frame, termed as mould or matrix which is basically a hollowed block. These blocks of filled with liquid glass, ceramic, plastic or metals, which when solidifies adapts the shape of the mould. Bi-valve moulds are used for casting symmetric shape.
Few types of machines offered by blow moulding machine suppliers are –
- Injection Moulding Machine: It is alternatively known as injection press. This machine has two vital parts, an injection unit and a clamping unit. This variety of moulding machine is mainly used for manufacturing plastic products.
- Blow Moulding Machine: This machine is designed with the concept of giving plastic a shape by blowing with heated air. The heated air expands the plastic along with the frame of the mould cavity and forms the shape of the mould.
