Abandoned project due to shifting priorities, resource constraints, and stakeholder misalignment. Original scope focused on developing an AI-driven IoT platform for smart city infrastructure, integrating real-time data analytics and predictive maintenance modules. Key challenges included:
- **Regulatory hurdles** (data privacy laws like GDPR) delaying initial deployment phases.
- **Budget overruns** exceeding allocated funding by 30%, forcing a pivot to a smaller prototype.
- **Competitor advancements** in similar fields (e.g., Tesla’s Autopilot integration) making the original value proposition less competitive.
- **Stakeholder fatigue** leading to inconsistent support and shifting deadlines, requiring repeated reassessments.

Final decision: Transitioned focus to a modular cloud-based API solution, targeting niche enterprise sectors (e.g., logistics) with lower regulatory barriers.
