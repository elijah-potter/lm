Building a Youtube channel can be an overwhelming but also incredibly fun endeavor. Getting started on Youtube is very easy, but building it to bring in an audience and eventually make money off the platform is a different beast.

- Find a genre you love. Seriously, Youtube burnout is a real thing. If you find a topic or genre of videos that interest you as a viewer and something that you're interested in making yourself, then you may have found a gold mine. In everyone's Youtube journey, they eventually hit a wall where they lose interest in the topic, content, or style of videos they're creating. Be flexible, find a topic that interests you, and create your video, then find a new topic within the same genre of videos you're creating. 
- Don't focus on the numbers. If you find yourself upset that your Youtube channel hasn't hit a certain subscriber mark based on standards you have set yourself, then you're already playing a losing game. The main thing is to not focus on numbers.  Let the growth be organic. If your videos are good, people will come. Instead of setting a goal of having 100 subscribers by a certain date, how about instead set a goal of releasing 5 videos by a certain date?
- Quality > Quantity. In the land of Youtube, this is a tale as old as time. Many of the greatest Youtube channels will only ever release a video once a month, maybe even once a year. Focus on the quality, ensure that you're able to hold the viewer's attention, and just create great content. Quantity will get you pretty far, but quality will get you even further than you expected.
