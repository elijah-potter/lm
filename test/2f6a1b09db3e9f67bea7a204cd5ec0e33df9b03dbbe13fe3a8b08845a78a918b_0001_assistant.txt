Have you ever heard of the blue whale? Blue whales are amazing animals! They live in oceans all around the world. Blue whales are the biggest animals in the whole world! In fact, they can weigh up to 200 tons. That’s the size of more than 30 elephants. Even though blue whales are the biggest animals on Earth, they eat some of the tiniest animals, called krill. However, they eat a whole lot of them: as much as 4 tons each day. That’s the same as eating two cars!
