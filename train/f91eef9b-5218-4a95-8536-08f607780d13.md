The abandoned plan involved deploying a modular AI-driven logistics system to optimize supply chain efficiency across a global network of warehouses by 2024. The project was halted due to:
- **Technical Challenges**: Integration issues with legacy ERP systems, real-time data synchronization failures between IoT sensors and cloud platforms.
- **Resource Constraints**: Budget overruns exceeded 30% of allocated funds, delaying hardware procurement (e.g., edge devices, high-precision GPS modules).
- **Regulatory Hurdles**: Compliance delays with GDPR and local data sovereignty laws necessitated a pause in deployment.
- **Market Shifts**: Competitor acquisitions reshaped demand patterns, rendering the original KPIs (cost reduction by 25%) unrealistic.
- **Stakeholder Conflicts**: Conflicting priorities between IT, finance, and operations teams led to a fractured decision-making process.

**Current Status**: Scoping phase reassessment underway; alternative low-cost pilot testing with a subset of warehouses.
