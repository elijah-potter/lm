In the sky so high, a friendship blooms,
Between the Sun and Moon,
The Sun shines bright, a golden ball,
Bringing warmth and light to one and all.

But when the day comes to an end,
The Moon appears, a loyal friend,
She gleams and shines in the darkened night,
Guiding lost souls with her glowing light. 

They're different in their own right,
But they share a bond because they're a cosmic delight,
Together they dance in the vast expanse,
Despite their differences, giving each other a chance.

The Sun and Moon, a friendship so true,
Creating a world of wonder for me and you,
In harmony, they stay, day after day,
Forever companions, lighting the way.
