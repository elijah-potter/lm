In a quiet village nestled between rolling hills, there lived a young girl named Mira who collected forgotten stories. One evening, as the fire crackled low, she found an old, leather-bound book with a faded inscription: *"The Whispering Oak’s Secret."*

Inside, the pages told of a tree that spoke to those who listened—its leaves shifting to form words of forgotten wisdom. Mira pressed her ear to the bark, and the wind carried a voice: *"The river remembers what the stars forget."* Curiosity gnawed at her. What secrets did the river hold?

What would you find if you listened to the river’s whispers?
