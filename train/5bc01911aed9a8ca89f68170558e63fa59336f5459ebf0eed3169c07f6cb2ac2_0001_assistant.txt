JEREMIAH WATTS- the charismatic pastor of Tree of Life Church. He dresses in designer clothing, is incredibly ripped, and has a way of making everyone feel like the only person in the congregation or room. He regularly cheats on his wife and diverts church funds to fuel his lavish lifestyle. 
ASHLEY WATTS- Jeremiah's wife. A beautiful young mother of three with a kind spirit. Good at covering up for her husband and ignoring his constant betrayal. 
DAVID DODSON- the founder of the church that would get rebranded as Tree of Life Church. David is a classic conservative Pentecostal, but he also loves money and finds himself willing to follow Jeremiah's lead to that end. 
BRENDA DODSON- David's wife and original co-pastor. Quietly disgusted with the direction the church is headed but willing to compromise for her lifestyle. Secretly a kleptomaniac even though she has plenty of money. 
JOEL DODSON- the son of David and Brenda. A gifted songwriter and head of the church's praise team. More interested in creating music than anything going on in the church. 
ABIGAIL JOHNSON - a young woman who wants to find a home church in her new community. She begins attending with her mother Martha, and ends up on the church's praise and worship team.
MARTHA or MARTI JOHNSON - Abigail's mother, easily a mother figure to everyone around her.
JED OWEN- a military veteran with debilitating PTSD and gifted musician who is drawn to the church's praise and worship team. 
HILARY TOWER- the church's social media director. 
ANA TOWER- Hilary's sister. She isn't a church member, but she intersects with the church members through her sister and ends up involved in a long-term torrid affair with Jeremiah.
