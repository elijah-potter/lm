The Texas House of Representatives (the lower chamber of the U.S. Congress) has voted to impeach (charge with treason or misconduct) Attorney General Ken Paxton, an unprecedented move following a legislative probe that faulted the third-term Republican for a yearslong pattern of corruption, including abusing his office’s powers, retaliating against whistleblowers (a person who informs on a person or organization engaged in an illicit activity) and obstructing justice.
