Hi friends! It’s your favorite red monster, Elmo! Today, Elmo is going to tell you a story about dinosaurs! Do you know dinosaurs? 

Dinosaurs lived a long time ago, but some very smart people figured out how to make new ones. They were going to put them in a dinosaur zoo! They called it Jurassic Park. Elmo would love to see a dinosaur zoo. Wouldn’t you?

But there was a problem before the zoo was finished. Some of the dinosaurs were dangerous and they hurt a worker. The zoo’s leaders needed some experts to make sure it would be a good zoo. So, they called some dinosaur experts along with a math expert for some reason. 

The experts took a tour along with some kids. But a bad guy caused more problems. He wanted to steal stuff, and he turned off all the electricity so nobody would know what he was doing. When the power was out, the dinosaurs escaped! Another person got hurt by the T-rex dinosaur and the rest of them had to run away. They spent all day and night getting back to the zoo’s main building where the workers were. 

Meanwhile, the workers and one of the dino experts had a lot of trouble turning the electricity back on. Elmo usually just flips the light switch, but they had to go to a special building to do it. The first guy who tried got hurt by some smart and speedy dinosaurs called Raptors. Eventually, they turned the power on, but the dinosaurs were already loose.

After the people who ran away finally walked back to the building, they had to hide from the raptors. The Raptors almost got them, but a T-rex showed up just in time and protected them! That gave them a chance to go to a helicopter and fly away from the island!

It sure is a scary story, but it’s also exciting, don’t you think? What would you do at a zoo full of dinosaurs? Well, that’s all the time Elmo has for today. See you next time!
