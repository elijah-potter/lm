Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white fur and big, round eyes that sparkled like the sun.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and stretched his tiny paws. "Today is a wonderful day!" he thought. He hopped outside to see his favorite flowers—pink tulips, yellow daffodils, and shiny green grass.

"Wow, they are so pretty!" Benny said, nibbling a tiny leaf. Suddenly, he heard a tiny squeak. A little bird named Bert sat on a branch, his feathers a little ruffled. "I lost my favorite acorn," Bert said. "Can you help me find it?"

Benny hopped closer and saw it! It was just a little way away under a big, blue sky. "Here it is!" Benny said, holding up the acorn. Bert hopped over and thanked him.

"Let's play together!" Bert said. Benny agreed and raced around the garden, chasing butterflies and collecting shiny pebbles. They had the best time ever!

As the sun started to set, painting the sky orange and pink, Benny and Bert said goodnight. "We'll play again tomorrow!" Benny promised.

And with that, Benny curled up in his warm nest, dreaming of more adventures with Bert. The little bunny closed his eyes and fell asleep, happy and content. The garden was quiet, and the stars twinkled above them, watching over their little friends.
