**Mayor’s Emergency Briefing: New Haven’s Water Crisis Escalates Amid Unconfirmed Leak Source**

Residents of New Haven reported sporadic water pressure drops and odd taste anomalies in tap water starting last night, prompting Mayor Elara Voss to declare a **mandatory boil-water notice** effective immediately. Officials from the New Haven Water Authority (NHWA) confirmed elevated levels of dissolved iron and manganese in samples taken from multiple faucets across the downtown and university district. While the source remains undetected, preliminary reports suggest the issue may stem from a **burst main in the old section near Bridge Street**, though engineers emphasize this is unverified at this time.

Schools and government offices are operating on backup systems, while hospitals have rerouted water via tanker trucks. The city’s **public transit system** has implemented reduced service on affected routes, citing maintenance delays. The NHWA has activated a **24/7 hotline (555-0178)** for reporting leaks or concerns, but call volume has spiked beyond capacity.

Meanwhile, private water vendors have been permitted to distribute bottled water at designated points, though shortages remain acute in lower-income areas. Authorities urge residents to **avoid flushing toilets unnecessarily** to conserve pressure. No safety hazards have been reported, but officials caution against prolonged exposure to untreated water for cosmetic purposes.

Further updates expected at 03:00 AM tomorrow. **Stay safe, New Haven.**
