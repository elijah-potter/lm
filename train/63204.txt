Teaching Tip: Teacher Evaluation
Most school districts include observations by administrators as part of the teacher evaluation process. Here are some things you should provide to help you attain a positive evaluation.
- Displays of current student work.
- Clean and organized classroom environment.
- Clearly posted class rules.
- Current lesson plans.
- Long-range lesson plans.
- Student work sample folders.
- Student portfolios.
- Assessments of student progress.
- Up-to-date grade book.
