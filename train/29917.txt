Basic information about the Cumberland County
The Cumberland County is located in the state of Virginia. This county occupies 301 square miles, with 3 square miles being water areas. There are 10.052 inhabitants living in the county according to the 2010 Census. As for the racial makeup of Cumberland County citizens, the 3 largest groups are White (63.94%), followed by Black or African American (32.61%) and Two or More races (1.93%). There are 3.980 households in the county, while 68,72% of them are family households and 31,28% represent non family households. There are 2 cities and towns in Cumberland County. The most populated city is Farmville town and the smallest town or village is Cumberland CDP.
