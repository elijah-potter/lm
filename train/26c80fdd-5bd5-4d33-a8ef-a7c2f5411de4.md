The first light of dawn breaking over the river, casting gold through the mist—
the way the willow bends just so, its leaves whispering secrets to the morning breeze.
Your fingers tracing the edge of the half-empty coffee cup, the steam curling like a ghost.
A child’s laughter, sharp and bright, bouncing between the old oak doors of the park.
The scent of rain on warm pavement, damp earth clinging to your shoes.
The last note of a song you’d forgotten, played perfectly by someone who knew you.
Time slows to stillness at the edge of the memory, where the past dissolves into something fragile, irreducible.
