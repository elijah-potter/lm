A whispered vow between strangers on a rainy evening,
*"I’ll be back by midnight,"* said the stranger with a flicker of hope in his eyes—
the coffee cup half-empty, the clock ticking like a broken promise.
His name was erased before the ink dried on the receipt,
but the ghost of his words lingers in the dim light of a café, half-forgotten, half-kept.
Midnight came, the storm raged, and the world moved on—
somewhere, a shadow still clutches the key to a door that was never opened.
