Math instruction has evolved since today's parents were in school. Although the math is the same, the curriculum looks very different and helping your child can be challenging if you are not familiar with the instructional approach of the Investigations program.
This site was designed with tools and resources specifically to help parents help their children succeed in mathematics. In addition to homework help and tips on organizing student work, there is a wealth of valuable materials to help you become familiar with the program's methodology and research-based approach.
Hear from teachers and students using the program, see examples of student work, watch video clips of Investigations in action in the classroom, or download articles and editorials from the experts in the field of mathematics.
In addition, you can visit other schools websites to see the types of support they offer teachers, students, and parents and help your school to organize a site of its own.
