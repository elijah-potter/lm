Cheese

I think that I shall never see
A cheese as lovely as brie.

Just add a cracker for a treat.
There isn’t anything much better to eat.

Gouda, Cheddar, Gorgonzola
Great with wine or Coca-Cola.

Feta, Swiss, or Mozzarella
Limburger is a real smeller.

In a sandwich, cheese is the best.
Use two slices, forget the rest.

Poems are made by fools like me,
But only a cow can make cheese.
