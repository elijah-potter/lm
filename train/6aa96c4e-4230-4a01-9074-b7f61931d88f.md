A sudden shift in ambient light—cool blues flickering into a warm golden glow—casts long shadows across the crumbling arches of an abandoned cathedral. The last remnants of twilight vanish, replaced by a harsh, blinding brightness as a nearby storm clouds part, revealing a sunrise that burns through the ruins like molten metal. Shadows dissolve into silhouettes, and for a fleeting moment, the once-dark passageways seem to pulse with an eerie, artificial life.
