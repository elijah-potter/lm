Enron was based in Houston, Texas and they involved themselves in energy trading and utilities. They were behind one of the largest accounting frauds in history. The executives at Enron used practices in the accounting system that illegitimately inflated their company's revenue.  They were the seven-largest corporation in the US at one point. The company quickly unwound after the fraud was brought to light; they filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in December 2001.
