The void stretches infinitely, an abyss of silent, endless void.
Echoes of distant galaxies hum faintly, fading into the static hum of cosmic dust.
No horizon, no boundary—just the unbroken expanse of cold, unyielding darkness.
The weight of silence presses down, suffocating all sound.
Every star is a single, isolated point, suspended in this unending, breathless silence.
The mind tries to grasp it, but the void outpaces comprehension—it is not empty; it is *eternal*.
