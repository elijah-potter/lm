A fragile vow held tight by fear,
Whispers of doubt in every breath—
*"I’ll return,"* yet time dissolves like sand,
As shadows stretch and promises fade.

The weight of half-remembered words
Weighs heavier than the truth we hide.
A borrowed name, a borrowed hand,
A ghostly promise left untied.

The clock ticks slow, then fast, then still—
A silent countdown, a hollow thrill.
What was once gold now rusts and bleeds,
A wound no hand can mend, no time redeeds.

The other side just stands and waits,
While echoes laugh in hollow air.
*"You swore you’d stay,"* the wind replies,
*"But promises fade like morning’s prayer."*
