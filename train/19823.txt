DIGITAL HALLOWEEN AROUND THE WORLD: This digital Halloween resource includes 5 non-fiction texts (one page each) that introduce students to Halloween traditions, practices, and customs from different countries! The countries covered are Ireland, Mexico, Romania, Nigeria, and China. This is an excellent cross-curricular activity as it connects well with Social Studies and can be used in class or as a homework assignment!
The best part is that students complete the assignments online! The assignment are created with Google Slides and can easily be shared with Google Drive, Google Classroom or even via email! You don't have to be a tech savvy person to make this work in your class. The detailed step-by-step instructions provided make it painless and easy.
