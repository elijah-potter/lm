Oh The Grand Old Duke Of York Poem with Lyrics
Oh the grand old Duke of York,
He had ten thousand men,
He marched them up to the top of the hill
And he marched them down again.
And when they were up, they were up,
And when they were down, they were down,
And when they were only halfway up,
They were neither up nor down.
“The Grand Old Duke of York” is a famous children's nursery poem in English.
The poem is also known as “The Noble Duke of York”.
The lyrics of the poem bring out an historical character which is connected with one of the Grand Dukes of York.
It was first published in the year 1642.
