I am watching the TV news from the border state of California and the border city of San Diego ( San Ysidro has a border crossing with Tijuana). It is heart-wrenching. Apparently, migrants from all across the world are trying to get into the country as Title 42 and asylum hearings are allowed.

I don't claim to be an expert on all or even any of the immigration laws. It seems like many people will be turned away? I don't know. You need to be escaping a very dangerous situation. You need a good reason to be seeking asylum.

The borders are not open. It seems like most, if not all, of the migrants are miseducated. They might have been duped into thinking they could just walk across and get into the United States. Some have paid guides to wind up at the border.   Actually happens all of the time. People try different ways to just get here... America

I don't know how to digest all of this. It is very sad. I truly feel compassion for people who have absolutely nothing. It is truly unfair, and I couldn't imagine what they had to experience.

It makes me grateful to be here in America and to not take my citizenship for granted. Yes, there are so many problems in this country but, based on the fact that so many want to be here, there must be something valuable here. A lot of people just want to be where I am... in America with citizenship. I have to conclude that there are a lot of worse places around the world!

Maybe this is just the same thing that always happens around the border. Maybe it is being overexposed by the media. I don't know. But this is what the focus is on right now. And I will follow the news about this topic.
