Wario shuffled around the grassland. He looked around in a daze, taking in some of the sights around him. He could barely tell where he was anymore, but he was certain that there was danger around. He heard something move behind him. He turned around with his rifle in hand and fired. He did not see what it was, but he knew that he had at least scared it off.

Wario shook his head. He used to be able to think clearly, but for some reason that had gotten so difficult for him recently. He turned around and fired his rifle again. As long as he had his weapon, nobody could sneak up on him and attack him. He was able to take comfort in this.

The man, who had once been one of Mario's greatest rivals, continued to wander around the countryside with no goal in sight. He did not even know what he was protecting, but he assumed it was a large sack of his money. This once-powerful man now had nothing but the assumption of wealth and the assumption that everything around him was after it. He continued to mindlessly roam the countryside.
