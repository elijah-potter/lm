**Project Title:** *Development of a Low-Cost, Real-Time Fungal Spore Detector for Agricultural Field Edge Monitoring Using AI-Optimized Microfluidic Chips*

**Abstract:**
A novel, ultra-sensitive, and autonomous fungal spore detection system designed to monitor plant pathogens at the field edge with sub-minute response times. Utilizes a compact, low-power microfluidic chip integrated with machine learning classifiers trained on hyperspectral imaging and fluorescence spectroscopy data to differentiate *Botrytis cinerea*, *Fusarium oxysporum*, and *Sclerotinia sclerotiorum* spores from environmental noise. Targets early detection to reduce fungicide use by 30% while minimizing yield loss in organic and precision agriculture settings.

---
**Key Objectives:**
1. **Prototype Fabrication:** Design and manufacture a 3D-printed microfluidic chip with integrated optical sensors (10 µm resolution) and embedded Arduino-ESP32 for real-time data processing.
2. **AI Training Pipeline:** Develop a hybrid CNN-LSTM model trained on 10,000 labeled spore images (10% cross-validated) using public datasets (e.g., [PlantVillage](https://plantvillage.psu.edu/)) and synthetic spores generated via 3D-printing.
3. **Field Deployment:** Deploy a battery-powered prototype (12V/2A) in a greenhouse and open-field trial (500m²) to validate accuracy (>92% F1-score) and durability under 100% humidity/UV exposure.
4. **Cost Analysis:** Achieve <$20/unit production with modular design, targeting 10% market share in niche organic farms (10,000 units/year).

**Methodology:**
- **Sensing Layer:** Optical flow cytometry + fluorescence quenching (excited at 488nm, emitted at 515nm) to quantify spore viability and morphology.
- **AI Workflow:**
  - Preprocessing: Background subtraction, noise filtering via Gaussian blur (σ=1.5).
  - Classification: Custom ResNet50 with attention mechanism for spore segmentation + LSTM for temporal spore drift detection.
- **Hardware:** Open-source PCB design (Kicad) with integrated OLED display for field use.

**Novelty:**
- Combines microfluidics, hyperspectral imaging, and edge AI to achieve 10x faster detection than PCR (30s vs. 1h) with <$500/unit.
- Closed-loop feedback loop: Alerts via SMS/email to farmers when spore concentration exceeds 50 spores/mL.
