The mist-shrouded highlands of Vorthas’ Last Reach, where jagged obsidian cliffs plunge into the abyss. The air hums with static, the wind carries whispers of drowned gods. Below, the abyss yawns—black water swirling with forgotten ships, their hulls carved with runes that pulse faintly. The path is slick with salt and time; every step feels like a fall.
