**San Francisco’s Chinatown Overhaul**
A sleek, neon-lit district where historic lanterns (*now LED-clad*) hang above repurposed warehouses, blending 19th-century opulence with 21st-century tech hubs. The alleys, once cluttered with stalls selling *dried fish and fortune cookies*, now host AI-powered translation kiosks and holographic calligraphy classes. The Muni tram routes now serve *VR rides through Beijing’s Forbidden City* alongside the usual ferry routes. Locals—both old-school *tongs* and fresh immigrants from Vietnam and Myanmar—mingle in a space where *wok heals* and *blockchain meets tea*. The smell of *soy marinating seafood* merges with the hum of quantum servers. Tourists snap pics with AI-generated *doujinshi-style* versions of themselves in kimonos. The vibe: *nostalgic futurism*.
