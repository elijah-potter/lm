Market Square, Old Town, Prague
Crowded cobblestone plaza with vendors selling fresh bread, spices, and handmade crafts.
Tall Gothic spires and Baroque fountains frame the scene, with tourists and locals mingling.
A busy riverboat ferry glides past, carrying visitors to Charles Bridge.
Street musicians perform traditional Czech melodies near the medieval bridge arching over the Vltava River.
A café serves espresso to weary walkers; the scent of grilled sausages and wildflowers lingers in the air.
