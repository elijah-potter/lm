Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a little girl named Mia who loved to play outside. One sunny morning, while chasing butterflies, she found a tiny, shiny acorn under a big oak tree. It sparkled like a little gem in her palm.

Mia wondered if she could grow something special from it. She planted the acorn in her garden, watering it every day with a little sprinkle of sunshine. Soon, a tiny green shoot peeked out of the soil.

Days turned into weeks, and the shoot grew taller. A beautiful, leafy tree stood where the acorn had been! Mia was so happy she decided to name her tree "Sunny" because it made her feel warm and bright.

One afternoon, Mia noticed some little bugs crawling around Sunny’s leaves. She smiled and said, "Hello, my friends!" The bugs chirped happily and flew away to play with their friends. Mia watched as Sunny’s leaves shimmered under the sun.

Soon, Sunny grew big and strong. It had lots of branches, and the leaves made a soft, green blanket in the garden. Mia and her friends would play games under the tree, like hiding under the leaves and telling stories.

One evening, Mia looked at Sunny one last time before she went to sleep. She whispered, "Goodnight, Sunny," and watched the fireflies dance around the tree. As she drifted off to sleep, she knew Sunny would grow even bigger and brighter tomorrow.

And that was how Mia’s magical tree stayed in her garden forever, bringing happiness to everyone who visited.
