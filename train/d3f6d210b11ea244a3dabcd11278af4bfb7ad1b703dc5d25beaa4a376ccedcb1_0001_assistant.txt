First, the date can start with an early morning run in a secluded, peaceful place away from vehicular or foot traffic, such as along a stream or river. The run can be light but sufficient exercise, lasting about 3-4 miles.

After the run, the couple can find a place to shower and get ready for breakfast, which would be at a nice outdoor restaurant. Breakfast can consist of eggs, bacon, toast, and freshly-squeezed orange juice.

Then, the couple can go to a large forest in the middle of nowhere, and they can bring a picnic with them to have lunch there after a hike to a good location. The picnic lunch would consist of the following items: sliced meats such as pastrami, pepperoni, turkey, and ham; various kinds of cheese; crackers; fruits like grapes, strawberries, and sliced apples; peppers; olives; mixed nuts; and wine.

The next location for this date would be out of the forest and into a movie theater, where the couple can watch the newest movie. It wouldn't matter what movie was playing as long as the two were able to sit next to each other to share a tub of buttered popcorn and a large diet soda.

After the movie, it would be about time to go to their dinner reservation which would be at another restaurant, this time one near the ocean. This restaurant would serve them a meal of spaghetti with meatballs and mozzarella cheese, Margherita pizza, and red wine. As a dessert, they would share a large martini glass containing a chocolate brownie topped with sea-salted caramel ice cream and chopped macadamia nuts. The meal would be eaten as the sun was setting over the ocean, creating a beautiful scene for the couple to enjoy.

The date could end with a nice walk along the shore as the couple looks out and see the lights of the city dotting the shoreline.
