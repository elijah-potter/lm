The "thumbs-up" gesture, widely recognized as a universal symbol of approval or positivity, can be misinterpreted in various cultural contexts. In some Latin American countries, it is used to indicate that the person is drunk or has been drinking. In Greece, it is associated with the phrase "I am a thief" or "I have stolen something." Additionally, in certain regions of the Middle East, it may signal a negative meaning or even be offensive, akin to a mocking gesture. The ambiguity arises from its dual use of the left and right hand variations, each carrying distinct connotations in those areas.
