1 tsp. ground Money Tree Leaves
1 cup Milk of Magnesia
1 tbsp. Witch Hazel
1 sprig of Rosemary
1 cup of tears from sadness
1/3 cup of pen ink
A piece of paper that says "$1,000,000" on it

Pour the liquid ingredients into a cauldron. Start with the Milk of Magnesia, Witch Hazel, tears, and pen ink. Stir well. 
You will now add your dry ingredients to the mixed wet ingredients, which would be the Money Tree leaves and a sprig of rosemary.
Stir well. 
Add in the piece of paper with your intention written on it. Stir once more. 
Let boil for 1 hour, stirring every 15 minutes to ensure it doesn't burn. 
Let cool to room temperature.
Once done boiling and cooling, pour the mixture into a Mason Jar. Place it in your freezer until frozen solid. 
Once you're ready to consume the mixture, you'll need to reboil it, pour it into a cup, and add two ice cubes. 
Drink while thinking about your intention. 
Within 7 days, you'll receive $1,000,000.
