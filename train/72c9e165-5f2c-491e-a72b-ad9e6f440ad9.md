Quitting smoking is one of the most challenging habits to break due to nicotine addiction, which triggers dopamine release and creates a strong craving. The withdrawal symptoms—irritability, anxiety, and increased appetite—can make quitting feel overwhelming initially. Many people fail multiple times because of the intense physical and psychological withdrawal.

Social and environmental cues (e.g., stress, certain people, or triggers like coffee breaks) can also reinforce the habit. Cognitive behavioral techniques, nicotine replacement therapy, and support groups can help manage cravings. Success often depends on gradual, structured strategies rather than sudden quits.
