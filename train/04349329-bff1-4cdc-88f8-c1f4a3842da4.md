In a shifting sandscape of transient moments, where rivers carve new paths daily,
time bends into a fractal of echoes—every structure collapses into dust within the breath of a storm.
Memory is a fleeting whisper in the wind, and the sky, a canvas of ever-changing hues,
erases the lines between past, present, and future. Even gravity feels like a suggestion,
not a law. The earth, a living organism, breathes—dissolving into the endless, unrooted void.
