Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown ears that bounced when he hopped. Every morning, Benny would wake up and say, "Mmm, what a sunny day!" His favorite color was sunshine yellow.

One day, Benny saw a big, shiny blue balloon floating near his garden. He hopped over to it and gave it a big squeeze. The balloon popped! "Oopsie-daisy!" Benny said. He looked around and saw a tiny, smiling squirrel with a basket of acorns. "Hello there, Benny!" said the squirrel. "I found this shiny rock in my tree. Want to play?"

Benny and the squirrel became best friends. They played hide-and-seek, shared acorns, and even had a picnic under the big oak tree. Benny learned that being kind and sharing made everyone happy.

As the sun began to set, Benny waved goodbye to his new friend. "Goodnight, little squirrel!" Benny said. The squirrel waved back. Benny snuggled into his burrow, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures. And with the soft sound of crickets chirping, Benny went to sleep, dreaming of his wonderful friends and the colorful world outside.
