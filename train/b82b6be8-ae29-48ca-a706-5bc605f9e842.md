Habit of procrastination on important tasks, often due to fear of failure or perfectionism. Leads to delayed decision-making, missed deadlines, and increased stress. Can be exacerbated by distractions like social media or overthinking. Despite awareness, triggers like fatigue or environment changes make relapse frequent. Relapse rates exceed 90% without structured accountability, like setting small, time-bound goals or using apps like Focus@Will. Environmental redesign (e.g., digital detox, dedicated workspace) and emotional regulation (e.g., mindfulness, self-compassion) are critical. Progress is incremental—small wins (e.g., starting a task before checking email) build momentum.
