Once there was a little bunny named Benny who lived in a cozy burrow under the big, soft grass. Benny loved to hop around the meadow, collecting shiny leaves and chasing butterflies. One sunny afternoon, while Benny was playing, he heard a tiny voice.

"Help me!" cried a little bird who had lost her nest.

Benny hopped to the bird and saw that her nest was tumbling down. "Don’t worry," Benny said, "I’ll help you!" Benny gathered some soft grass and tiny twigs, and carefully built a new nest for the bird. The bird thanked Benny with a chirp.

Later, Benny met a friendly fox who loved to tell stories. "You know, Benny," the fox said, "every little helper is special." Benny smiled and hopped home, knowing he had done something good.

The next day, Benny saw a little mouse who was scared of the big dark forest. Benny told the mouse, "It’s okay! The forest has many friends who help each other." Together, they made a path with flowers and sticks, and the mouse felt brave again.

From that day on, Benny became the meadow’s best friend, helping others with kindness and a big, happy heart. And every time he helped someone, he felt as happy as a bunny who had found a whole basket of carrots!
