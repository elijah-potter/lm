Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big garden, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white ears and a pink nose that sparkled like a tiny star.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and said, "Today, I’m going to explore!" He hopped outside and saw a beautiful blue sky. "Wow, it looks like a rainbow!" Benny thought. He chased his shadow, which danced like a tiny dancer.

Nearby, Benny found a patch of soft grass and dug a tiny hole. He poured water from his tiny watering can into the hole, and soon, tiny flowers popped up! "I made my own garden!" Benny cheered.

A little bird named Birdie with feathers like sunshine landed on Benny’s nose. "Hello, Benny! Want to play?" Birdie chirped. Benny nodded and flew Birdie up in the air. They giggled as they zoomed around, until Benny gently set Birdie down.

As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and orange, Benny yawned. "Time for bed, little friend," he whispered to Birdie. Benny curled up in his burrow, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures.

And that’s how Benny the bunny learned to play, explore, and make new friends every day. The end.
