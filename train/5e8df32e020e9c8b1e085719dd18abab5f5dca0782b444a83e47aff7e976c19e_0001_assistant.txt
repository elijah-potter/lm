Welcome to my new blog, *Ronan's World*, about my furry best friend, Ronan! Ronan is a Borderdoodle, which is a perfect hybrid breed that combines the best traits of Border Collies and Poodles. The Border Collie is known for its intelligence and herding skills, while the Poodle is known for its intelligence, hypoallergenic coat, and affectionate personality. When you combine these two breeds, the result is a dog that is not only trainable and brilliant but also loving and loyal to its humans. Ronan has been with me for about 2 months now, and I couldn't be more excited and grateful to be his momma.

I am an avid dog lover that loves to train dogs to be the best versions of themselves. I've also always wanted to share my experiences and adventures with my best friend in the world. When I finally decided to adopt another dog, I spent much time researching breeds and looking for the perfect match for my family. I have had Border Collie mixes before but have struggled with some allergies related to their fur. That's when I discovered the Borderdoodle breed and fell in love with their intelligence, loving nature, and more hypoallergenic coat.

But my Ronan is not just any Borderdoodle. He's a special dog with a unique personality all his own. From the moment I laid eyes on him, I knew he was the perfect companion for me. He is so smart, playful, extremely loving, and always eager to please. Whether we're exploring nature or cuddling on the couch, Ronan has become my perfect sidekick.

In this blog, I will share our life, from the challenges of training a new puppy to the joys of exploring new places together. Eventually, I will share Ronan's training experiences as he becomes a service dog. I hope sharing our experience with other dog lovers out there inspires some to consider adopting or rescuing a Borderdoodle of their own. I also hope to provide a platform for other Borderdoodle owners to connect and share their own stories. 

So, join us on our adventures and get to know Ronan. I know you will love him too!
