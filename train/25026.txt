Our Town Przysucha
Students from Secondary Schools in Przysucha, who also participate in the GLOBE Program (Global Learning and Observations to Benefit the Environment)
Geographic focus: Poland / Town of Przysucha
Date completed: 2005
Climate strongly influences a region’s culture. A team of students from Przysucha, Poland, recorded and analyzed their town’s temperature from 2000 to 2002. They found that the minimum monthly temperatures rose over the years in which records were taken. The team also created a topographic map of the region surrounding the town of Przysucha.
Topics / keywords: Climate; Temperature Fluctuations
