Here are five places that are technologically advanced and good for tourists:
1) South Korea. South Korea is one of the most technologically advanced countries in the world. Along with that, South Korea has beautiful traditional buildings, customs, and clothing items you can immerse yourself in.
2) United States. The United States is another one of the most technologically advanced countries in the world. The United States is beautiful because of the diversity of the land they have. From the Northern Lights to the depths of hot deserts, the United States has a sprinkle of everything.
3) China. China is consistently ranked one of the best countries for technological advancements. At night, China is breathtakingly gorgeous. The lights illuminating the skyscrapers make it a wonderful place to take pictures, film movies, and make new friends.
4) Japan. Just like China, Japan consistently tops the charts for being technologically advanced. Japan's nature is a sight to behold. You'll get a mix of both technology and nature when you go to Japan.
5) Singapore. One of the most technologically advanced countries in the world is Singapore. However, Singapore is also one of the greenest countries in the world. The streets are clean, making them visually pleasing and welcoming to any tourists. If you like good technology and cleanliness, then Singapore is perfect for you.
