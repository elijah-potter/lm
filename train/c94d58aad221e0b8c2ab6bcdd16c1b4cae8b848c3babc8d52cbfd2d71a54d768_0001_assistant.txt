Blue

Blue as the sky,
A calming presence,
Peaceful and serene.

Red

A fiery passion,
Burning bright and strong,
A force to be reckoned with.

Green

The color of life,
Nature's beauty abounds,
A symbol of hope.

Yellow

A cheerful smile,
Bringing joy to all who see,
A ray of sunshine.

Purple

A regal hue,
Of royalty and mystery,
A color of power.
