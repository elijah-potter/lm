Lily the bunny had a big, fluffy tail that wiggled like a magic wand. One sunny morning, she hopped outside to play in the meadow. The flowers were colorful and the grass was soft. Suddenly, she saw something shiny in the tall grass—it was a tiny, golden acorn!

Lily picked it up and wondered, *"What if this acorn could make me fly?"* She tried to roll it but it just bounced. Then, she noticed a tiny ant carrying a leaf. "Hey, little friend!" she said. The ant giggled and handed her a tiny cup of water.

Lily drank the water and then—*whoosh!*—the acorn started glowing! She twirled her tail and *zoom!* She flew over the trees and bushes, landing on a hill. There, she met a family of squirrels. "Did you see that?" they cheered. "Lily the Flying Bunny!"

After playing, Lily picked her acorn back up. It was still shiny, but now it sparkled like a star. She hugged it and said, *"I’ll be back soon!"* And with a happy hop, she flew home, her tail still wiggling with joy.
