That ol' barbeque sauce we got these days started way, way long ago. I been learnt that the North American colonies in the 17th century had barbeque sauce that ain't too different from ours. Them people wrote books that talked about barbeque sauce on meats, so that's how we know they used it way back then. And I can't blame 'em, there ain't nothing I love more than when my wife cooks smoked pork ribs covered with that stuff. I guess barbeque has been around as long as the ol' red, white, and blue, partner.
