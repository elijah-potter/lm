Sure! Here are some great options for you to start with:

- One-Pot Pasta Meals: These are a great place to start your cooking experience because of their simplicity and minimal cleanup. In this process, you cook the pasta in the same pot with the rest of the ingredients and have a great dinner in under 30 minutes.
- Sheet Pan Dinners: The baking sheet pan is a staple of every kitchen and a great option for a foolproof meal. This is another process that will keep your prep and cleanup time short.
- Grown-Up Dorm Food: Sometimes the simple staples you grew up with just need a new twist to take on a new life. Some examples are waffled-tomato grilled cheese or peanut butter ramen.
- 3-Ingredient Desserts: Of course, you need to finish off those dinners with a great dessert. Try some simple 3 ingredient dessert options like 3-ingredient banana bread, cookies, or brownies.
