The "OK" sign in different cultures:
- In the U.S., it means "good" or "alright," but in some countries, it signals **death** or **insanity**.
- Misinterpretation leads to awkward exchanges (e.g., a tourist waving "OK" in Japan may trigger a panic attack).

**Example Scenario:**
*"Shut up!"* → Hand gesture: Finger around thumb (common in the UK), but in Brazil, it’s a vulgar insult. Context saves misunderstandings.
