Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white fur, big round ears, and a heart full of sunshine.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to play. The grass was soft under his paws, and the sky was blue like Benny’s favorite color. He saw a tiny mushroom growing near the fence. "Mmm, what’s in this mushroom?" he thought.

Benny nibbled the mushroom and—*poof!*—he turned into a big, brave dragon with golden scales! Benny’s eyes sparkled like stars, and his tail wiggled like a rainbow. "I’m a dragon now!" he cheered.

Suddenly, Benny’s old rabbit legs turned back into fluffy white fur, and his wings shrank into tiny ears. "Oh no!" Benny gasped. He was back to being Benny the Bunny.

Just then, a friendly old owl named Oliver flew down. "I heard you turned into a dragon," Oliver said. "Let’s play a game!"

Benny hopped over to the mushroom and nibbled it again—*poof!*—this time he turned into a superhero with a cape made of flowers! He zoomed around the garden, saving ladybugs from a big, sleepy spider. "Superhero Benny!" Oliver cheered.

After a while, Benny stopped being a superhero and went back to being Benny the Bunny. He snuggled into his cozy bed, happy to know that no matter what he did, he was still Benny—the bunny who loved adventures, sunshine, and friends.

And that’s how Benny learned that being himself was the best adventure of all.
