Grinding And Its Effects On Acidity
Grinding coffee beans is an important part of the brewing process. When coffee grounds are too coarsely ground, they will not absorb enough water to bring out all their flavors, resulting in a weak cup with low acidity. Storage And Its Effects On Acidity
Storage is a critical factor in maintaining the acidity of coffee. Coffee professionals have identified various ways that storage can affect the flavor and aroma of beans, as well as their overall acidity levels.
The following are key points to consider when storing coffee for optimal preservation:
- Store whole bean coffee away from direct sunlight or heat sources. This will help maintain freshness for longer.
- Keep your beans sealed in an airtight container or bag at all times to prevent moisture absorption.
- Avoid storing ground coffee for more than one week, as this will result in a decrease in flavor and aroma over time.
- If you must store ground coffee, it should be stored in an opaque container with tight lid to keep out light and oxygen which can further degrade the quality of the grounds.
