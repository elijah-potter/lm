You shouldn’t be here. The air smelled of ozone and old wood, thick with the weight of forgotten secrets. The door creaked open, not by its own accord but by the quiet, deliberate push of unseen hands. Your boots—worn, rain-stained—left no sound on the cracked linoleum. The hallway stretched before you, lined with mirrors that didn’t reflect you back.

*"You shouldn’t be here."* The voice came from the darkness, smooth as polished bone. A figure stood at the end of the hall, cloaked in shadow, fingers tracing the edge of a dagger. The blade was cold, humming with something older than the city. *"But you’re already here,"* they whispered, *"and that’s worse."* The room around you dissolved into static, and then—nothing. Only the whisper, the whispering: *"You shouldn’t be here."*
