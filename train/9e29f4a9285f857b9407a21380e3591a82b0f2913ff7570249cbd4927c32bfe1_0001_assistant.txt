- Lake Kissimmee has largemouth bass, crappie, catfish, chain pickerel, and bluegill. 
- Ochlockonee River State Park has redbreast sunfish, catfish, black crappie, and flathead catfish.
- Hugh Taylor Birch State Park has sheepshead, grunt, catfish, red drum, snook, bonefish, and king mackerel.
- Werner-Boyce Salt Springs State Park has red drums, sea trout, blue fish, and cobia.
