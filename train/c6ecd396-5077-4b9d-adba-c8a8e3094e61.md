The team decided to abandon the legacy database migration project due to escalating costs and lack of ROI projections. While some argued for pushing forward to meet the quarterly deadline, the risk assessment team highlighted significant vulnerabilities in the system’s compatibility with newer application frameworks, which would require extensive rework. The decision was made to pivot resources toward the new cloud-native architecture to align with the company’s long-term vision. Despite initial resistance from stakeholders who saw the project as a priority, the board ultimately approved the shift, citing the potential savings and reduced technical debt.
