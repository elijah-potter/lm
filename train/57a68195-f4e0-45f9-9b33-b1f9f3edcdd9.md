A lone figure stands before a cracked mirror reflecting a void,
where flickering lights dissolve into static humming.
Their breath forms mist on the glass, but no words come—
only the hum of the city’s heartbeat, distant, unseen.
The door creaks open; the silence swallows footsteps,
as echoes linger like half-heard whispers in the walls.
A single candle flickers, its flame trembling—
neither extinguished nor kindled, just waiting.
Outside, the wind carries nothing, only the weight of absence.
