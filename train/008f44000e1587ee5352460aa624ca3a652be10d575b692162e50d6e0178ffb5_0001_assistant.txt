Lewis Carroll is the pen name of Charles Lutwidge Dodgson, an English author, poet, mathematician, and photographer. Carroll was born on January 27, 1832, and died on January 14, 1898, just short of his 66th birthday. During his career, Carroll published many books of literature, mathematics, and poetry, but he is most well-known for "Alice's Adventures in Wonderland" and its sequel, "Through the Looking Glass".
