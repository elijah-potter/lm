1. Moore
Moore is a charismatic traveling merchant who loves meeting new people and experiencing new places. She only reluctantly became an adventurer and dreams of returning to her old life. Moore is very strong from lifting her wares into wagons and wears a signature red doublet. She loves to talk with people from every walk of life.  

2. Brand
Brand is a former dwarven blacksmith's apprentice, only recently coming above ground. His mage training was cut short after an unfortunate event. He passionately dabbles in fire magic but struggles to control it. His hard work at the forge is evident in his strength, but he doesn’t care to limit himself to the physical and reads magical texts voraciously. 

3. Myriss
Myriss is a curious gardener with an encyclopedic knowledge of plants. She tries to convince skeptics to try her new pastes and potions. She’s enamored with finding rare herbs and recently began a quest to find rare ice lilies. 

4. Clevo
Clevo is a goodhearted villager. He’s not particularly strong or clever but listens well. Clevo’s lack of natural talents in other fields contrasts with his patience, kindness, and hard work ethic. Clevo plays a lute, much to the chagrin of those around him. He’ll determinedly pursue any goal until the end and is inspired to protect the village. 

5. Claude
Claude Demortain is a bumbling warlock who’s trying to build the perfect monster. He lacks refinement, and some of his experiments turn out odd creations, like a ten-legged sheep. The villagers don’t know whether to laugh or be frightened of him. 

6. Durra
Durra is the best of Claude’s creations and is a four-armed human-like monster. Durra wants to be accepted by the villagers, but they are frightened by his appearance. He is fascinated by cooking and will watch Claude brewing for hours. One of Durra's arms has a mind of its own and constantly knocks things over.
