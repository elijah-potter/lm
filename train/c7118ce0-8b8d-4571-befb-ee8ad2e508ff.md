A barren expanse of cracked, sun-bleached earth stretches endlessly, punctuated by jagged rock formations that glow faintly under a pale, overcast sky. The once-thriving forests stand skeletal, their branches skeletal, some snapped or scorched away, others gnarled and twisted. Patches of scorched grass, now brittle and gray, dot the landscape where vegetation once flourished. Water sources have dried up, leaving behind shallow, stagnant pools reflecting an eerie, desaturated light. The air hums with a quiet, oppressive stillness, broken only by the distant, faint cry of wind through hollowed-out remains of trees.
