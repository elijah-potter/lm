The door creaked open with a sudden gust of wind, revealing a figure in a dark coat—unexpectedly familiar—standing silhouetted against the streetlamps. Their eyes locked onto a startled woman mid-step, her breath catching as they moved closer. A whisper of a laugh escaped before the stranger’s voice cut through the night: *"Took you long enough."* The woman’s knees nearly buckled, her mind racing through the dozen times she’d been warned about this moment, but her body betrayed her, frozen in place. The stranger extended a gloved hand—empty. Then, a shadow fell across her face as they vanished into the alley behind her.
