Deep in the forest of Plum Sugar, Daisy and Sparkles are on a quest to become a princess and a unicorn. They will battle swamp monsters, mountain dragons, and other magical monsters to find the mystical mirror that will transform them into their magical selves. But beware the tricky elves. They'll steer them into tangled plot twists. Will they ever find the mirror? Kids will love this magical story about being your true self and the power of friendship. A great gift for kids beginning school to remember to shine as they are.
