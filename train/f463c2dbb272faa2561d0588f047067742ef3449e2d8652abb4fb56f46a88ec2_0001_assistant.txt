At the end of 2005, elephant populations across Africa were extremely low due to poaching across the continent. However, a concerted effort has been made in the last several years by various governments to outlaw and prosecute poaching. As a result, elephant populations have rebounded to a 50-year high. Conservationists throughout the world have applauded the efforts of those involved in this phenomenon. #Elephants #Poaching #Africa #Conservation
