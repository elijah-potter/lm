This is a great unit to add into what you are already doing in math. This is a November themed collection of word problems. This is aligned with Common Core Math Standard 2.OA.1. These problems have unknowns in every position and teach students to write an equation with a symbol for the unknown number. There is a book cover included so you could make it into a book or you can use the pages individually. This is a great way to include 2.OA.1 into your math plans.
