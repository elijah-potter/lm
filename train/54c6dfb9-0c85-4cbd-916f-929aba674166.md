The dim glow of a flickering laptop screen cast long shadows across the walls as **Lena** sat on the edge of her kitchen chair, fingers tracing the rim of an empty coffee cup. The news app had been open for hours, the screen blank except for the slow pulse of notifications. Her phone buzzed—another update from the official channel. The sound was a familiar ache.

She exhaled sharply, pressing the screen harder. *"Breaking news: Suspected terrorist attack in Sector 7. Police in hot pursuit."* The words blurred into static. Her breath hitched.

A knock at the door made her jump. She didn’t answer. The knock came again—louder this time. Her phone chimed again, a new alert: *"All civilians ordered to evacuate. Shelter in place."* She grabbed her jacket, her pulse hammering.

Outside, sirens wailed. The door burst open. A uniformed officer rushed in, face grim. "Evacuation order—**immediately**." Lena’s hands trembled as she grabbed her bag. Behind her, the news app flashed: *"First responders report heavy casualties. Hospital overwhelmed."*

The last thing she heard before the world outside blurred was the officer’s voice: *"Lena? You coming?"* She didn’t look back. Just ran.
