Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big, blue sky outside, lived a bunny named Benny who loved to hop and nibble on carrots. Benny had a tiny sister named Lily, who was just as curious as her brother but even smaller.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and said, "Lily, let’s go on a picnic!" Lily giggled and ran to the kitchen. "Mama, Daddy, Benny wants to bring a picnic!" Mama Bunny smiled and said, "That sounds like fun! Bring some of your favorite snacks!"

Benny packed a basket with soft, fluffy bread, juicy berries, and a big, round apple. Lily picked out a tiny sandwich made of cream cheese and cucumber. They hopped outside to the garden where their friends were waiting: a kind old turtle named Tilly, a speedy little mouse named Milo, and a friendly squirrel named Sammy.

"Hello, friends!" Benny said, waving his paws. "We’re going on a picnic!" The friends cheered and gathered around the basket.

Mama Bunny had made a cozy blanket under the big oak tree, and everyone sat down to eat. Benny shared his apple, and Lily gave Milo a tiny sandwich. Sammy the squirrel collected acorns, and Tilly slowly munched on a juicy berry.

As they enjoyed their meal, Benny spotted something shiny in the grass. He picked it up and said, "Look, Lily! Something sparkly!" It was a tiny golden acorn. "Maybe it’s magic," he whispered.

Lily giggled and said, "Maybe it’s a treasure!" The friends all gathered around, and Benny gently placed the acorn in his basket. "Let’s keep it safe," he said.

Suddenly, a little wind blew, and the acorn rolled downhill toward a sparkling stream. "Oh no!" said Benny. "We must help Tilly cross!" The friends worked together to push the turtle carefully across the water.

Once Tilly was safely on the other side, Benny said, "That was fun! Let’s keep the acorn, and maybe it will grow into a big, beautiful tree someday." The friends agreed, and they all sat together, sharing stories and laughter under the warm sun.

As the day ended, Benny and Lily curled up on the blanket, dreaming of their next adventure. "Tomorrow," Lily said, "we’ll find the most magical treasure ever!"

And with that, the little friends drifted off to sleep, dreaming of sunbeams and golden acorns.
