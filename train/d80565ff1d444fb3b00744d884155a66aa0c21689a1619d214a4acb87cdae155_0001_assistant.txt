The Oakland A's are leaving town for Las Vegas, and I just can't support it. Too many sports teams are leaving their communities to chase dollars, and it has to end. Baseball has been pretty good about this before now. I think the last team that moved was the Expos to Washington, which I think everyone agrees ended up being a good thing. 

But that was different. Baseball was dying in Montreal. It isn't dying here. We love baseball in Oakland. It's our national past-time.

We just have a terrible owner who won't put money into the team. They complain that fans don't come out and buy tickets. Well, they have to put out a product worth paying for. And they can't do that by consistently being one of the lowest payrolled teams in the entire game. And yeah, I know there are issues with the stadium. But the owner is worth billions of dollars. Yes, that is billions, not millions. Somehow though he doesn't have the money to do anything about it. He just wants public funds from us taxpayers.

It just isn't right. The A's are Oakland and we need each other. Moving to Las Vegas would remove all of the characteristics and history that make the A's the team I love. Through adversity, we've had a lot of highs. I remember those Giambi and Canseco years, and I know we can have highs again. But if they move to Las Vegas as they plan to, well, they'll become just another faceless organization out to make a buck.  

The way I see it, Las Vegas is a city built on tourists. They'll never be able to get a big or consistent fan base since most of them aren't permanent residents. Sure, it might work for the NFL, which has 17 games a year, or even hockey with its smaller stadiums, but baseball has 162 games a year and crowds that regularly hit 40,000 or more per game. I can't see Las Vegas packing the house for those 81 home matches a year. 

It just won't happen. The A's need to stay in Oakland if they want to survive. Moving to Las Vegas is not the answer.
