Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a garden full of flowers, there lived a tiny rabbit named Pip. Pip was small and round with big, round ears that flopped when he walked. Every morning, Pip would hop outside to play.

One sunny morning, Pip found a shiny red ball rolling near the berry bush. He picked it up and noticed it was warm to the touch. "Hmm," he thought, "it must be hungry!" Pip had a basket of carrots and a bucket of water, so he carried the ball back to the house. He sat it on the kitchen table and gently squeezed it until it started to grow bigger and rounder.

Pip was so excited! He named the ball "Berry." Berry grew bigger and bigger, turning into a delicious, juicy red berry. Pip munched a few bites and shared the rest with his friends—his bunny siblings and a curious squirrel.

As the days passed, Berry grew sweeter and sweeter. The squirrel loved it and started collecting seeds from the berries to grow his own garden. The rabbits shared the berries with their family, and soon everyone had a tasty snack.

One evening, Pip looked out the window and saw Berry still growing in the garden. He smiled. "Maybe next year, Berry will be big enough to share with everyone again!" And with that, Pip snuggled up for a cozy night of dreaming.

The end.
