The hot and cold methods of making herbal vinegar only differ in the preparation of the vinegar. To make herbal vinegar using the hot vinegar method you will need to first heat the vinegar you choose to use in a pot until it is just below boiling temperature. When using the cold method, this step is skipped entirely. You will want to bruise the herbs you plan on using to help them release their flavor more and place them into a glass jar. Now, fill the jar with either hot or cold vinegar until there is ¼ inch of air space at the top and then tightly seal it. Now you just need to store this jar in a dark, cool place for 10 days before opening it up and tasting it. If you are happy with the flavor, you can strain the herbs and bottle the vinegar. If you want more flavor, you can leave the herbs in the jar for up to a month before straining them out. These two methods are used depending on the type of herb you are infusing and the flavor you want in your finished product as different herbs react to heat in different ways. The finished vinegar will have an optimum shelf life of 6 months but can last for up to a year if stored correctly.
