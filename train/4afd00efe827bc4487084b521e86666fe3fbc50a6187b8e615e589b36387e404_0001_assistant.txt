The big park next to my house has a green tree. The green tree is a landmark for all the children in town.
They would all run there during the summer and hang out under its shade.
Sometimes, a couple of adventurous children would climb the tree! The parents would be worried and run over, making sure the children don't fall out suddenly.
This green tree is memorable for a lot of us. I hope it will continue to be a landmark for all the children in town.
