A child stumbled upon a crumpled **red leather keychain** half-buried in the damp grass near the old oak tree. The chain was frayed, and one of the links had snapped, but the key itself—small, worn, and oddly familiar—gleamed dully in the fading afternoon light. She picked it up, examining the worn engraving: *"For the one who always finds things."* Her fingers brushed against the worn metal, and for a moment, she thought she heard a faint ticking—like a clock, or a heartbeat. She tucked it into her pocket, wondering what secret this key might unlock.
