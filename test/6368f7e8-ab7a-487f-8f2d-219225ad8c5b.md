Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the meadow. One sunny morning, Benny saw a tiny red berry sparkling in the grass. He thought, "Mmm, just the right size for a little snack!" Benny picked it carefully and ate it. But then he noticed something shiny near a stream. It was a tiny golden acorn. Benny picked it up and felt it soft and warm. "Maybe I should keep it," he thought.

Later, Benny played with his friends, but he kept thinking about the acorn. He asked his mom, "Mom, what if I grow this acorn? Would it be as big as a tree?" His mom smiled and said, "Maybe, Benny. Trees are special things."

Benny planted the acorn in the dirt behind his house. Every day, he watered it with his little cup. Soon, tiny leaves started to grow. Benny watched and waited, wondering what would happen next. One afternoon, a big, strong wind blew, and the leaves flapped happily. Benny giggled and said, "It’s growing!"

Weeks passed, and Benny's tree grew taller and taller. The acorn had become a big, tall tree with green leaves. Benny’s friends came to visit, and they climbed the tree to pick berries and collect acorns. Benny's tree became their favorite spot to play.

One day, Benny’s tree had a big, round fruit that looked like a big, soft ball. Benny picked it and tasted it—it was sweet and juicy! He and his friends ate the fruit together. "Best tree ever!" they cheered.

From that day on, Benny loved his tree even more. Every year, it grew a little bigger, and the friends would visit to play and share treats. Benny knew his tree would stay strong and beautiful forever, growing and changing with each new season.
