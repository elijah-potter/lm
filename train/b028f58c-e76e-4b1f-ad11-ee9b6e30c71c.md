**"The Clockwork Garden"**

Beneath the rusted hinge of a broken sundial,
a single seed blooms—
not of earth, but of gears.

The petals, silvered by dust, spin like cogs,
each bloom a tiny wheel, meshing with the sky’s
iron teeth. The roots twist into the cracked stone,
drinking sap from the brass pipes of forgotten
clocks.

At noon, the garden stops. The last bloom
unfurls into a needle of light,
and the garden sighs—
a slow, mechanical breath.
