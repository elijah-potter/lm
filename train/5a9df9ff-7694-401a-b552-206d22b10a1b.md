**The Last Transmission**

The hum of the server racks was the only sound left in the data center. Static hissed between the last few connections, the flickering LEDs of failing hard drives casting eerie shadows on the walls. Across the room, Elias sat hunched over his terminal, fingers dancing across the keyboard like a pianist lost in a dream. He had been here for hours, his apology still echoing in his head.

*I’m sorry. I messed up.*

His hands trembled as he reran the corrupted backup script. The system crashed again. A single error message flickered to life: **"Unrecoverable data integrity breach."** His breath hitched. He knew what that meant.

Outside, the city slept, oblivious to the storm brewing within the server farm. The city’s digital heartbeat had faltered—an emergency alert pinged through the local network, a red warning flashing across every screen. Elias exhaled sharply, gripping the edge of his desk. He had been the last to notice.

The next morning, the city woke up to silence.

A single, desperate message appeared on the emergency broadcast:
**"Backup complete. Restart in 5."**
Then, nothing.
