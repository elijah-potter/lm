A fork in the path where the wind hummed with unseen voices, neither right nor wrong but a balance of both. The lantern flickered—one branch led to the whispered promises of the old druid’s garden, where time moved differently, and the other to the iron gates of the city, where the clock struck midnight every hour. The lantern’s flame dissolved into ash as the choice was made: the garden’s secrets, or the city’s silence. The hand that closed the book left the ink still wet on the parchment, and the answer was not in the pages but in the weight of the decision itself. A test, then, not for failure, but for what was worth carrying.
