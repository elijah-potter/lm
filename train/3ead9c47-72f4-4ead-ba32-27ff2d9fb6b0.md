A **single pivot strategy** where a critical detail—e.g., a misplaced document, a misaligned sensor, or an unnoticed environmental variable—determines the entire outcome.

**Example:**
- **Core Plan:** Deploy a drone surveillance system in urban areas to detect illegal dumping.
- **Critical Detail:** The sensor’s GPS offset by 100m north causes it to flag legal construction zones as illegal.
- **Result:** Entire operation collapses; authorities falsely arrest contractors.

**Key Insight:** Identify the **least obvious, most fragile** element that, if altered, could undo the entire system. Test it under edge-case conditions.
