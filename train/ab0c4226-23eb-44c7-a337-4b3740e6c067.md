The dim glow of a flickering streetlamp cast long shadows across the cobblestone path as Elias traced his fingers along the rough bark of an ancient oak. His breath fogged in the cold air, unsteady, while his mind raced through a list of names and possibilities. The scent of damp earth and rain clung to his coat, but he didn’t stop—each step felt like a step closer to the truth. His phone buzzed in his pocket, vibrating weakly against his thigh. No signal here, just the hum of the city echoing through the empty alleyways. A flicker of movement at the edge of his vision. Something unseen, something *happening* beyond his reach. He clenched his fists, whispering a prayer beneath his breath. The wind picked up, carrying fragments of his own thoughts away like ghosts. He was chasing echoes.
