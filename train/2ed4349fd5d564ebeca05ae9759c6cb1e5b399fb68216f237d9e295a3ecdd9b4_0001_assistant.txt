In a lovely wide meadow, vibrant and green,
Lived a little white rabbit named Maxine.
With hops and jumps, she'd dance and play,
With her dearest friend, a dog named Faye.

Faye was a small dog, fluffy and white,
Together they'd frolic from morning till night.
Through the sun and the rain, their friendship grew,
A bond so strong, pure, and true.

The meadows they'd wander, the forests they'd roam,
Exploring the wonders of their very own home.
Maxine would hop, and Faye would prance,
Their friendship was a beautiful, magical dance.

As the seasons changed, so did the land,
Where the two friends, side by side, would stand.
In their meadow of memories, where this story took flight,
A tale of friendship, endless as starlight.
