The kingdom of Melda was beset with conflict. The source of the conflict, as always, was the nature of higher beings. The humans and the dwarves disputed over the valuable resources below the surface, while the goblins and the elves were constantly at war over land. Queen Tilly, the fairy princess, had declared her intentions to gather her most powerful sorcerers and sorceresses to put the world under a great sleep, one from which they would never wake. To her, it seemed the fairest approach to the never-ending violence.

All was not well within the fairy world either, despite the queen's insistence. Disputes of culture and philosophy dominated, with rival sects exacting violent revenge on one another in a vicious cycle of retaliation. Few could see a path forward from such vices. Those who were connected to the earth in some way—the Dryad Tinian, the human Vern, and the satyr Torbjyn—were approached by a servant of the dragons, who had long since retreated into hiding to avoid the squabbling of lesser creatures.

By fate, they were drawn together under one roof, where the Dragon Bourne, Vye, explained the nature of their quest: travel to the domain of the dragons and uncover the secrets of harmony they possessed. Vye warned that the dragons might not take kindly to smaller creatures treading upon their realm, but if they dared not impose on the dragons' rest, the kingdom of Melda would fall to ruin.
