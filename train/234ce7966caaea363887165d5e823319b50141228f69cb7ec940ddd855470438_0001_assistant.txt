Frida Kahlo was a Mexican artist known for her symbolic and surrealist self-portraits. Born on July 6, 1907, her work was heavily influenced by her experiences with physical and emotional pain following a severe injury in her youth. Kahlo's bold style, use of color, and exploration of identity, post-colonialism, gender, class, and race in Mexican society have made her a celebrated figure in art and an icon of feminism and LGBTQ+ movements.
