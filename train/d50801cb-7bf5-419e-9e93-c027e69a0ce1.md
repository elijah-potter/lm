I’ve spent every summer of my life there, and the thought of owning it feels both wonderful and a little overwhelming.

I told her I needed some time to sort things out, deal with the estate, and just…process everything. I wasn’t ready to share it yet. She seemed understanding, but I noticed a subtle disappointment.

She even sent me pictures of beautiful sunsets she found online, captioned “This could be us!” It felt a little…intense. I explained, again, that I needed time and that I’d be in touch when I was ready to discuss sharing it.

Then, in February, Clara started dropping subtle digs. During a family dinner at my parents’ house, she made a comment about how “some people are lucky enough to have a place to relax and recharge, while others just work and work.” My parents looked uncomfortable, and I felt my face flush. I tried to deflect, but the message was clear.

I finally relented and said I’d be open to discussing sharing it sometime in the spring. I suggested April or May 2024. That’s when things escalated. Clara responded with a lengthy email, essentially accusing me of being selfish and hoarding something that should be shared with the family. She pointed out that I live just a few hours away and could easily visit whenever I wanted, while she’s stuck in Denver. She even brought up past instances where I supposedly “held back” on sharing things with her. It felt like a personal attack, not a request.

I explained, calmly, that I understand her desire for a vacation, but that this house represents more than just a place to relax. It's a connection to my grandmother, a symbol of my childhood, and I need to establish my own relationship with it before opening it up to others. I offered to help her find a lovely rental property in the Outer Banks if she wanted a beach vacation.

Her response was a single, dismissive sentence: "Fine. Enjoy your little fortress."

Now, she’s telling our parents that I'm being unreasonable and excluding her from something that "should be shared." They’re trying to mediate, suggesting I just "let it go" and let her and Ben use the house for a week. They’re worried about damaging our relationship. I feel incredibly pressured and, frankly, hurt. I’m trying to be considerate, but I also feel like my boundaries are being completely disregarded. It's frustrating to feel like I'm being punished for wanting a little time to myself with something that’s legally mine. I’m worried this is going to permanently fracture our relationship. Honestly, it feels like she's more interested in having a vacation than in honoring my grandmother’s wishes. I’m starting to wonder if I’m being overly sensitive.
