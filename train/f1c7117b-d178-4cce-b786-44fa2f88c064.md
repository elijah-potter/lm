## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park – A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary (For Municipal Funders)**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park project seeks a grant of $75,000 to revitalize a currently underutilized and neglected public space located at the intersection of Elm Street and Maple Avenue. This transformation will create a climate-resilient community asset—a pocket park—designed to mitigate stormwater runoff, provide vital urban green space, enhance biodiversity, and foster community connection. The project addresses pressing municipal priorities regarding stormwater management, urban heat island effect reduction, and equitable access to recreational spaces. Our collaborative approach, involving local residents, environmental experts, and skilled tradespeople, ensures the park’s relevance and longevity. The Willow Creek Pocket Park will serve as a replicable model for similar revitalization efforts throughout the municipality, demonstrating the power of community-led initiatives to address environmental and social challenges.

**Project Plan & Timeline**

The project is divided into four distinct phases, each with clearly defined milestones, responsible partners, and deliverables.

**Phase 1: Assessment & Design (Weeks 1-4)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (WCNA), GreenSpace Design Collaborative (landscape architects), Municipal Planning Department.
*   **Milestones:** Site assessment report detailing existing conditions and challenges; community survey analyzing park design preferences; preliminary park design incorporating climate-resilient features (rain garden, permeable paving, native plantings); approval of design by WCNA and Municipal Planning Department.
*   **Deliverables:** Detailed site assessment report; community survey results analysis; preliminary park design plans and renderings; formal design approval documentation.

**Phase 2: Site Preparation & Infrastructure (Weeks 5-10)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** WCNA, Ace Construction (general contractor), Municipal Public Works Department.
*   **Milestones:** Removal of existing debris and non-native vegetation; grading and soil amendment; installation of underground stormwater management infrastructure (rain garden underdrain system); installation of permeable paving.
*   **Deliverables:** Site cleared and graded; stormwater management system installed and tested; permeable paving installed.

**Phase 3: Planting & Amenity Installation (Weeks 11-14)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** WCNA, Blooming Earth Nursery (native plant supplier), Community Volunteer Groups.
*   **Milestones:** Installation of native trees, shrubs, and herbaceous plants; construction of a small, accessible seating area with reclaimed wood benches; installation of interpretive signage detailing the park's climate-resilient features and ecological benefits.
*   **Deliverables:** Native plantings completed; seating area constructed; interpretive signage installed.

**Phase 4: Community Launch & Education (Weeks 15-16)**

*   **Responsible Partners:** WCNA, Municipal Parks & Recreation Department, Local Schools.
*   **Milestones:** Grand opening celebration with community activities; educational workshops on stormwater management and native plant care; development of a park stewardship program involving local residents.
*   **Deliverables:** Successful park launch event; educational workshop materials; park stewardship program guidelines.

**Budget & Financial Transparency**

The total project cost is estimated at $85,000. We are requesting a municipal grant of $75,000, supplemented by $10,000 in matching funds secured through local fundraising efforts and in-kind donations.

*   **Site Preparation & Infrastructure:** $30,000 (Includes debris removal, grading, soil amendment, underground drainage system, permeable paving materials and labor.)
*   **Planting Materials & Labor:** $25,000 (Includes native trees, shrubs, herbaceous plants, mulch, and planting labor provided by Blooming Earth Nursery and volunteer groups.)
*   **Seating & Amenities:** $10,000 (Includes reclaimed wood lumber, hardware, and construction labor for benches.)
*   **Interpretive Signage:** $5,000 (Includes design, printing, and installation of interpretive signs.)
*   **Community Launch & Education:** $2,500 (Includes event supplies, workshop materials, and promotional costs.)
*   **Project Management & Contingency:** $12,500 (Covers administrative overhead, volunteer coordination, and a 15% contingency fund to address unforeseen expenses or material cost fluctuations.)

The matching funds of $10,000 will be comprised of $4,000 in direct monetary donations from local residents and businesses, and $6,000 in in-kind donations of volunteer labor and material from Ace Construction and Blooming Earth Nursery.  The contingency fund is crucial to ensuring project completion despite potential price increases in construction materials or unexpected site conditions.  Detailed invoices and receipts will be meticulously tracked and readily available for municipal review.

**Community Engagement & Equity Impacts**

Community engagement has been integral from the project's inception. The WCNA conducted a comprehensive community survey to gauge design preferences and identify resident priorities.  Regular meetings with neighborhood residents have ensured ongoing feedback and ownership of the project.  The design specifically addresses historical inequities in access to green space within the Willow Creek neighborhood, which has been historically underserved. The accessible design of the park, including permeable pathways and accessible seating, ensures inclusivity for all residents, regardless of physical ability.  The interpretive signage will be available in multiple languages to cater to the diverse linguistic backgrounds of the community.

**Risk Mitigation**

Potential risks have been carefully assessed and mitigation strategies developed.  Unforeseen site conditions (e.g., buried utilities, contaminated soil) are addressed through a thorough site assessment and the contingency fund.  Weather delays are factored into the project timeline, allowing for flexibility in scheduling.  Volunteer recruitment and retention are addressed through proactive outreach and recognition programs.  Liability insurance will be secured to cover potential accidents or injuries during construction and park usage.

**Post-Grant Sustainability**

Long-term sustainability is paramount. The WCNA will assume responsibility for ongoing park maintenance, including watering, weeding, and litter removal. A park stewardship program will be established to engage local residents in ongoing care and monitoring.  The native plant selection minimizes water requirements and reduces the need for chemical fertilizers.  The interpretive signage will educate users about sustainable practices and encourage responsible park usage. The Municipal Parks & Recreation Department will provide technical support and occasional assistance with larger maintenance tasks.

**Call to Action**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park represents a transformative opportunity to enhance the quality of life for Willow Creek residents while addressing critical municipal priorities. This project embodies a collaborative, equitable, and sustainable approach to community development. We respectfully request your approval of the $75,000 grant to bring this vital community asset to fruition.  Your investment will yield substantial environmental, social, and economic returns for years to come.
