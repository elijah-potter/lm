One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest is a movie that came out in 1975 and starred Jack Nicholson as RP McMurphy and Louise Fletcher as Nurse Ratched. Notable patients in the movie were played by Danny Devito, Christopher Lloyd, and Brad Dourif.

The movie takes place in a mental hospital and starts with the patients playing a game of cards until you see RP McMurphy get admitted and he shakes up the "normalcy" of the ward and pushes Nurse Ratched every opportunity he gets. There are regular meetings run by Nurse Ratched. Early on, Nurse Ratched starts the meeting by having one of the patients tell the story about how his wife is cheating on him, and she wants the group to share their thoughts on the matter. Near the end of the meeting, the camera focuses on Jack Nicholson's character, as he is eyeing Nurse Ratched suspiciously and then shows her face. You can feel Jack Nicholson's emotion of wondering what Nurse Ratched's true intentions are.

The movie follows a similar format with a good amount of runtime showing the meetings, but slowly RP McMurphy involves himself more so and tries to allow himself and the patients to have more fun. He wants to be able to watch the world series, which Nurse Ratched would allow if over half the patients voted yes on it. First, only a few people want to watch, then RP McMurphy convinces more and more of the patients to vote yes, but Nurse Ratched again doesn't allow it when she counts the patients that didn't attend the meeting as no votes. During the movie, RP McMurphy gets close to the Chief, a Native American who the staff believes is dumb and deaf. They enjoy a basketball game with the orderlies and Chief plays a big role in scoring baskets for the patients' team and holding the net and pushing the ball through the opponent's basket so it doesn't count.
