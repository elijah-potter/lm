Yes! Here are some tips for making a memorable trip to Coachella:

1. Stay at the on-site campsites. Its easier access to the concert grounds and allows you to take breaks when needed. 
2. Pack water and sunscreen. You'll be out in the sun all day and need to stay hydrated.
3. Bring an extra charger and invest in a solar charger. 
4. Eat high-protein foods to keep up your energy. Go for the burger over the pizza. 
5. Have fun!
