Crackling firelight dances in the hearth, casting golden tongues across cracked clay walls. The scent of charred oak and pine needles mingles with damp earth—new rain has left the forest floor slick with dew. A distant creek murmurs through gnarled roots, its water whispering against pebbles. Nearby, the kitchen spice box clatters as fingers shuffle dried herbs: crushed thyme, crushed rosemary, and a pinch of salt that lingers on the tongue. A crackling stove hums, steam curling into the air like ghostly fingers. The air smells of burnt sugar and warm bread, the last remnants of a feast left to cool. Far off, a distant train whistle echoes, punctuated by the distant roar of engines.
