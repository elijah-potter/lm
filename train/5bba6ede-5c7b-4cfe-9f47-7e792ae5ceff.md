A dim glow from the single bulb flickered across the cracked wooden floorboards as the door creaked open. The single knock—soft but deliberate—echoed through the silent apartment. The old brass knob turned with a groan, revealing a figure clad in a long, hooded coat, their face obscured by shadow. Inside, the scent of stale coffee and dust lingered, the air thick with the weight of unspoken tension. The door clicked shut behind them, leaving only the faint sound of footsteps, slow and deliberate, moving toward the lone chair in the corner where a half-eaten sandwich sat abandoned.
