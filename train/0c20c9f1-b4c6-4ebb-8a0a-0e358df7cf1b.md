A sudden gust of wind sent a loose umbrella clattering to the ground as the figure turned sharply. Their breath caught mid-sentence as the café’s chatter dissolved into silence. The stranger’s hand instinctively reached for their pocket—only to find it empty. The door behind them creaked open, revealing a familiar face with a smirk. "Took you long enough," the voice said, voice dripping with amusement. The figure froze, fingers twitching toward the waistband of their pants. The café’s clock ticked, unnoticed, as the stranger’s gaze locked onto the one who had just materialized out of nowhere.
