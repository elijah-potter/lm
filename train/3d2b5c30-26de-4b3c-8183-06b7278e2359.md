Unfolding in an infinite loop of echoes,
words drift like smoke through an endless day—
someone asks *"Why?"* and the answer
is a ghost whispering back *"Because..."*
time dissolves into a hollow question mark,
each reply loops like a song with no refrain.
Silence is the only answer left unsaid,
the void hums where the conversation should end.
A door ajar, a mirror cracked in half—
the other side is still coming.
