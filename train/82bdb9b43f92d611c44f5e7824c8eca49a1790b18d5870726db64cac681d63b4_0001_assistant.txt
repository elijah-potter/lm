As the sun set on the meadow, Calli and Tidus squared up to one another, their brows furrowed in fury and their eyes glowing with hatred. Tidus made the first move, the tidal wave, sweeping his right leg in a swift motion. Calli, lithe and nimble, leaped in the air to avoid it. She quickly retaliated with fiery fists, pummelling his muscular chest with fury. Tidus barely flinched, a mocking grin spreading across his face. He grabbed one of her fists in his own and cackled as he used his might to perform the tidal wave, flinging his opponent across the grass effortlessly. Calli landed on her back with a loud thud, pain searing across her body. She screamed in frustration, but sprung back up, keeping her distance and bouncing on the balls of her feet as she sized up her opponent.

That's when she noticed, a large, fresh wound from a recent battle on Tidus' left arm. She smirked mischievously, then sprinted towards him. Tidus unleashed the thunderstorm, launching powerful punches in her direction. She evaded each one, dodging and ducking in swift motions, then darting behind him, dug her sharp nails into his wound with the glowing grip. He cried out in pain, falling to his knees, his wound oozing crimson blood. The battle was over and Calli knew she had won. She showed him mercy, looking down upon him, the smirk still painted across her face. She sauntered off, victorious.
