What are cookies?
Cookies are small pieces of text sent by your web browser by a website you visit. A cookie file is stored in your web browser and allows clareshort.org or a third-party to recognise you and make your next visit easier and the site more useful to you.
When you use and access clareshort.org we may place a number of cookies files in your web browser. The cookies we use are associated with the Google Analytics service which provide information about the pages you visit, how long you spend on each page and other similar metrics which are collected in order that we can improve the service we provide.
Read Google’s overview of privacy and safeguarding data.
Your choices regarding cookies
- Most web browsers allow some control of most cookies through the browser settings.
- To find out more about cookies, including how to see what cookies have been set, visit www.aboutcookies.org or www.allaboutcookies.org.
