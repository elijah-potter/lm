This is a document to be used in a lesson on teaching nature vocabulary (rocks, mountain, river, sky, cloud, etc). As you present the vocabulary to students, they draw pictures next to the written word. Afterward, they will have this sheet to refer back to in future activities.
This sheet also provides the sentence structures "Hay..." and "El_______ es _______" (to be used to describe nature).
This seemed rather effective in my class and I think it will be in yours too.
