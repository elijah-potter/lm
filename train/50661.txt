The London 2012 Paralympic Games have drawn attention to the
role that technology and science can play in overcoming human
limitations. From wind tunnel-tested wheelchairs to
running blades and other prosthetics, technology has been used to
adapt or enhance the human body for sporting success.
In the Paralympics, the forms of human enhancement are
generally considered to be therapeutic, i.e. overcoming a perceived
disability. However, there is an emerging intellectual movement
called transhumanism, which explores the application of technology
and science to enhance human bodies and minds regardless of whether
they are perceived to have any disabilities. Transhumanist pursuits
generally have the objective of extending human life, but may
include low-level biohacking, physical augmentation,
performance-enhancing drugs and even genetic modification.
