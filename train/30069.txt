Agricultural & Crop Shipping
Various Features of Agricultural & Crop Shipping
Agriculture is the farming of animals and plants and such other life forms to produce food, fiber, biofuel and medicinal goods and others such products to sustain and improve human life on this planet. So there are various agricultural features that benefit the mankind for ages are as follow:
Features of Agricultural Products:
These agro products are quite different from industrial made products because agriculture based products comes from nature in the form of cultivation of land and industrial products are artificial because they are produced in machines.
1) Agriculture based products are seasonal based products and the reason is that they can be produced in a particular season only.
2) Most of these products are perishable in nature. Some of these vegetables can perish in shorter period of time and others have little longer shelf life.
3) These nature based agricultural products are for daily consumption because these farm products provide the bulk or daily dose of proteins and vitamins needed by the body.
Crop Shipping is nothing but supplying or taking these fresh farm goods to the market for selling and consumption purposes. So now let us have a look at the features of this form of shipping that are as follows:
1) Adequate supply on time.
2) Good for exporting it to other states and countries
