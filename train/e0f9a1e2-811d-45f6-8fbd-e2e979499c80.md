A half-hearted note slipped under the door: *"I messed up. Don’t be mad. Love, [Name]."*
The envelope arrived late—on the day she canceled plans for the second time.
Her reply was a single, cryptic message: *"I’m sorry you’re sorry."*
The text was sent from an unreadable number.
At the office, she smirked when he joked about "forgiving quickly." Her eyes flicked to the clock. *"Time to move on."*
