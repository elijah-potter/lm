Doubt as a release valve: mid-workday, a deadline looms, but a nagging question—*"Did I really understand this algorithm?"*—unfolds like a mental x-ray. The screen glitches, a cursor freezes, and suddenly, the weight of perfection lifts. The doubt isn’t proof; it’s a spark—one that redirects focus to the next question, the next iteration, the *next thing to check*. The relief isn’t in certainty, but in the quiet hum of curiosity, alive and unbroken. Doubt, like a good editor, cuts through the noise to leave only what must be said.
