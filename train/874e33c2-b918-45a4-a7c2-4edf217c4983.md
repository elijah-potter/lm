The air smells of damp stone and old oil—no one moves quickly here. A single lamppost, half-broken, casts a feeble pool of light on a cracked sidewalk where **a discarded newspaper** lies in a puddle, its headline (*"MISSING PERSON"*) half-smeared. The wind carries the distant murmur of a crowd, but the scene itself is still. The only sounds are the occasional splash of rain and the occasional rustle of leaves against a rusted dumpster. The **real story is in the details**: the angle of a splattered puddle, the half-closed window of a closed storefront, the way the alley curves toward the silhouette of a bridge at dusk. The action? There isn’t one. The atmosphere is the only character.
