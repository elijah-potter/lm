The Purple Heart is a medal given to service members to acknowledge and remember their respective sacrifices in the line of duty. The medal is presented to service members who have been wounded or killed as a result of enemy action while serving in the U.S. military. More than 1.8 million Purple Heart medals have been presented to members since the award was first created in 1782.

Those who have been presented are remembered and honored for their sacrifices on Purple Heart Day, the national holiday residing on August 7 every year.
