

Father, 

You have fought the war and now that you have grown older I see that you hurt. I do not want to see that pain increase any longer. You have given up so much for Mom, Grandma, and me. Now I want to repay that sacrifice to you. I will go in your place but, as you know, no one can know. I took your sword and armor so that I can appear as a man. I plan to play the part of this role to fulfill my goal. I hope you are honored by my love. I do this not to cause dishonor to the family but to honor your sacrifices. 

Mulan
