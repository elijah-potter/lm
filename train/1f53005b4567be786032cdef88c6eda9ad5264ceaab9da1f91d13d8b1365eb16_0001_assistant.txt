- 1991: The band was formed in Tokyo by frontman Narasaki and released their first EP, "White."
- 1992: Release of the band's second and third EPs: "Queens Park All You Change" and "Sinking Slowly."
- 1994: The release of the band's first album,  "The Visitors From Deepspace."
- 1997: The band releases another EP, "Cat."
- 1998: The band releases "Submerge," an album that consists of remixes, as well as rare and new songs, in addition to a best-of album, "Breastroke."
- 2000: The release of the "Dog" EP as well as the release of their next full-length album, "Come Over to the Deepend."
- 2001: The release of the "Robot" EP,  followed by their next album, "No Thank You."
- 2002: The release of their next album, "newave."
- 2003: Following a U.S. tour, Narasaki shows interest in a heavier sound for the band.
- 2004: Release of the "Mouse" and "Penguin" EPs.
- 2006: The band begins to work on a new EP and album which release the following year.
