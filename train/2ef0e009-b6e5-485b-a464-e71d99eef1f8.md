**Subject:** *A Letter Never Sent*

Dear [Recipient],

I hope this letter finds you well—or at least not in the middle of a busy workday, though I suspect that would make it harder to read anyway.

I am writing this because I have something to say that could have been said, but was never. Maybe because the words were too personal. Maybe because I was afraid of the way they’d land. Maybe because I didn’t know how to say them without making it worse. Or maybe because I decided, in the moment, that it didn’t matter.

I should have said:
*"I miss you when you’re not here, but I’m not sure if that’s a good thing or a bad thing."* (But I was too tired to finish the sentence.)

I should have called you, but the phone kept ringing, and I kept forgetting why I wanted to talk. (It was because I didn’t want to.)

I should have told you how much your laugh makes me smile, but I was nervous about how it would sound—like I was comparing myself to something you already had. (Like you were perfect, and I wasn’t.)

I should have apologized for the way I left things the last time we saw each other. (But I didn’t know how to make it better.)

I should have asked you to move in. (But I was scared of what that would mean for us—both of us.)

I should have said goodbye when I meant to. (But I was too afraid of the weight of it.)

I should have written this letter. (But I was waiting for you to write one to me.)

There are moments where I wonder if I’ve ever truly been there for you. If I’ve ever said enough, done enough, loved enough. And I don’t know if that’s a failing on my part or yours. Or if it’s just that love isn’t always easy to measure.

I don’t know why I’m writing this now. Maybe it’s because time is running out, or maybe it’s because I’m running out of things to say. Or maybe I’m just tired of pretending I have all the answers.

Whatever it is, I hope you find what you’re looking for—whether that’s closure, understanding, or just a quiet moment to breathe. And if you ever need to talk about any of this, I’m here. (But I’m not sure I’ll be here long enough to say it.)

With more than I can say,
