Stephen Fry is a professional who has many strings to his bow and is a well-known actor, writer, comedian, broadcaster, director and presenter.  In the 1980s, he first became well-known as part of the comedy act Fry and Laurie with Hugh Laurie.  He has had an extensive career in television, theatre, radio and writing.  He is probably most well known for the TV show A Bit of Fry and Laurie and as the presenter of the long-running panel show QI which he hosted from 2003 to 2016.
