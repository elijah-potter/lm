Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the garden every morning. Benny's favorite spot was under the bright yellow sunflower, where he’d find his favorite snack: a juicy, crunchy carrot.

One sunny afternoon, Benny spotted a tiny, shiny silver star twinkling near his favorite spot. "Oh, what a beautiful star!" he said, picking it up and holding it close. The star felt warm and soft, like a tiny, glowing hug.

Just then, Benny heard a tiny voice. "Help! Help! My flower is droopy!" It was his best friend, a little mouse named Mimi, who had lost her favorite dandelion flower. "I can’t make it grow again," she whispered.

Benny thought for a moment. "I know what we can do!" he said, holding out his paws. "Let’s make the star shine a little more, and we’ll put it right where Mimi’s flower is so it can wake up and grow tall again!"

Mimi smiled. "That’s a wonderful idea!"

Together, they placed the star on Mimi’s droopy flower. Benny hopped around the garden, dancing and spinning, while Mimi gently patted the soil. Soon, the star glowed brighter, and the flower began to wiggle its petals. "It’s waking up!" Mimi cheered.

Benny grinned. "Now let’s give it some water!" He dug a tiny hole and filled it with water from his watering can. Soon, the flower straightened up, its petals unfurling beautifully. "It’s growing again!" Mimi giggled.

From that day on, Benny and Mimi became the best of friends, always helping each other. Every afternoon, they’d find more shiny stars and magical flowers to make happy. And whenever Benny hopped under the sunflower, he’d remember the kindest magic of all: friendship and a little bit of kindness.
