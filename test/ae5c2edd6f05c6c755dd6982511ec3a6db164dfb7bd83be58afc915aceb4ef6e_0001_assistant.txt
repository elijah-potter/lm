Lemons aren’t good,
but lemons aren’t bad.
They’re the sourest fruit
that I’ve ever had.

I’ve tried lemons in a lemonade,
so I thought it’d taste sweet,
but then it made my tongue curl up
and kind of hurt my teeth.

Lemons are bright and yellow,
they have a pretty strong smell.
Be careful where you squeeze the juice,
it can sting your eyes as well.
