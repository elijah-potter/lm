New born babies belonging to any category of living animals are always cute. But they seem to be more cute when the are in the womb of their mother before taking a birth. Here are some of the most fascinating pictures of some unborn bay animals when they are in their mother’s womb of in some cases eggs.
However, these images are not completely real as producer Peter Chin has used a combination of tiny cameras, ultrasound scans and computer graphics to create this fascinating embryonic images of unborn animals. This work was specifically done for National Geographic Documentary which was aired several years ago. However, the images even though not completely real, give a close resemblance to the actual existence of animals before taking birth. Check out some of the images below.
