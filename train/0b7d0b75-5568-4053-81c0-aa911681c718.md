**The Clockwork Cathedral of Veythari**
A towering obsidian spire where the pendulum swings in perfect, yet inexplicable cycles—each hour marked by a sound that doesn’t match the clock’s hands. The air hums with static, and every shadow moves with a delay. Time here bends like liquid: moments stretch into decades, or vanish mid-beat. The only constant is the echo of footsteps that were never there.

*Eternal sunset in a loop, but the sun doesn’t rise.*
