Title: "Espresso Love"

Eliza, a driven corporate lawyer, is addicted to the hustle of the city. She thrives on pressure, deadlines, and triple-shot espressos. Her life takes an unexpected turn when she stumbles upon 'Cuppa Joy,' a quirky, rustic coffee shop hidden away in the city's bustling corners.

Enter Noah, the charismatic barista with an artist's heart, who runs 'Cuppa Joy.' He is a former banker who left his corporate life to pursue his passion for coffee and human connection. He's popular amongst customers for his personal coffee "recipes" designed to reflect each customer's personality. One rainy afternoon, Eliza, drenched and annoyed, walks into 'Cuppa Joy.' Noah, noticing her frustration, decides to cheer her up by making a special 'pick-me-up' latte, sprinkled with his own secret ingredient - a dash of love and humor. Eliza is intrigued by Noah's charm and the warmth of 'Cuppa Joy,' a stark contrast to her usually cold corporate world. An unlikely friendship brews between the two. Eliza starts frequenting 'Cuppa Joy,' drawn in by Noah's uplifting spirit and the comforting vibe of the café. They both start revealing parts of themselves that they've kept hidden. Eliza helps Noah with the shop's legal troubles, while Noah shows Eliza there's more to life than winning cases. However, when Eliza is offered a job promotion that requires moving abroad, she must decide between the high-powered career she's always aimed for, or a life with Noah and the 'Cuppa Joy' family that's started to feel like home.

This heartwarming romantic comedy is a journey of self-discovery, love, and finding joy in unexpected places - all over a cup of coffee.
