In the heart of **Veldora**, a city where ancient stone bridges crisscross the jagged cliffs of the Blackthorn Canyon, residents woke to a rare and unsettling phenomenon: the **Great Glow**. Over the past three nights, the once-dull canyon has been illuminated by an eerie, pulsating light, casting long shadows across the cobblestone streets of the old quarter. Locals reported seeing figures in tattered cloaks moving along the bridges at dusk, though none dared follow.

Eyewitnesses near the **Whispering Fountain** claim to hear faint, melodic chants in an unknown language, though no one has captured the sounds on record. The city’s historian, **Dr. Elias Voss**, has confirmed that similar occurrences were documented in the **Veldora Chronicles**—specifically in the year 1187, when the same phenomenon led to the disappearance of a group of scholars studying the city’s ruins.

Mayor **Lira Dain** issued a statement late last night, urging residents to avoid the canyon’s edges and to report any sightings to the municipal surveillance drones stationed near the bridges. "We are treating this as a possible energy anomaly," she said, though her tone suggested skepticism. The city’s **Blackthorn Energy Grid**, a controversial project in the works since 2023, has been temporarily shut down for inspections, though no official explanation has been provided.

In the markets, rumors persist that the glow could be a natural phenomenon—perhaps a new type of bioluminescent fungus thriving in the canyon’s depths—but skeptics point to the timing: the grid’s shutdown coincides with the city’s celebration of the **Festival of the First Light**, a tradition marking the anniversary of Veldora’s founding. Some whisper that the festival’s ancient rituals have awakened something buried beneath the old ruins.

As of this writing, the **Veldora Security Corps** has deployed additional patrols near the canyon’s mouth, though their presence has not deterred the occasional curious onlooker. The city’s alchemical labs, long rumored to experiment with forbidden substances, are also reportedly analyzing samples of the glowing substance collected from the bridges. Whether this is a warning, an opportunity, or the beginning of something far stranger remains to be seen.
