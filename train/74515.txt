Almost all maternal deaths (99 percent) occur in developing countries where access to health services is limited because of poverty, distance, lack of information and cultural practices.
These statistics don’t tell the whole story. The ripple effect of a mother’s death on the health and well-being of her family can be enormous.
Merck for Mothers is raising awareness of maternal mortality by encouraging mothers to tell their birth stories and learn more on the Merck for Mothers Facebook page. For every story posted, Merck will make a donation to Join My Village, a program from CARE that empowers women and girls and supports safe pregnancies and deliveries.
