Alexander Graham Bell, a Scottish-born inventor, scientist, and engineer, patented the first practical telephone on March 7, 1876. As such, he is credited with the title of the inventor of the telephone. Born on March 3, 1847, Bell conducted much research on hearing and speech that led him to experiment with hearing devices, culminating in his invention. He is also known for his out-of-engineering works, most notably his contribution to the science of heredity.
