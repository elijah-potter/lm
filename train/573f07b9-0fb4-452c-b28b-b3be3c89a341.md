Lily the bunny had a big, fluffy tail that wiggled when she was happy. One day, while hopping through the sunny meadow, she spotted a shiny red apple near a big, green oak tree. "Mmm, apple," she thought. She picked it up and started nibbling.

Suddenly, a tiny whisper sounded, "Help me, Lily!" It was a lost little bird sitting on a branch. The bird had a broken wing and couldn’t fly away.

Lily’s kind heart made her want to help. She gathered some soft leaves and made a cozy nest for the bird. "Stay here," she said, "and I’ll bring you some seeds to eat."

That evening, Lily brought seeds and water, and the bird chirped happily. Every day, she visited, bringing more seeds and checking on the bird.

After a week, the bird’s wing grew stronger. One morning, the bird fluttered its wings and took flight! "Thank you, Lily," it chirped. "You helped me fly home!"

Lily smiled and waved goodbye. She knew helping others made her heart feel warm and happy. And from that day on, whenever she saw a lost friend, she’d hop over to help, just like she had with the bird.
