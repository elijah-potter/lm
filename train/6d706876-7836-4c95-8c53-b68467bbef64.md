**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 14, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this year, offering a traditional celebration of agriculture, community, and family entertainment. Attendees can anticipate a diverse array of livestock exhibits, demonstrations of rural crafts, thrilling carnival rides, and a wide selection of food vendors. The fairgrounds will be brimming with opportunities to engage with local farmers, artisans, and community organizations, fostering a spirit of connection and appreciation for the region’s heritage.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 22nd through August 26th, 2024. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM. A special fireworks display will illuminate the skies on Friday evening, August 24th. Daily activities will include tractor pulls, livestock judging, and live music performances on the main stage, showcasing a variety of genres throughout the week.

Admission to the fair is $15 for adults, $10 for children ages 6-12, and free for children five and under. Carnival ride tickets and wristbands will be available for purchase separately. Advance tickets can be purchased online at SangamonCountyFair.org, and parking is available at a cost of $5 per vehicle. Detailed schedules of events and a complete list of vendors can also be found on the website.

**Headline Events:**

*   Livestock Auction – August 25th, 6:00 PM
*   Professional Rodeo – August 23rd, 7:00 PM
*   Demolition Derby – August 24th, 3:00 PM
*   Grandstand Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters – August 26th, 8:00 PM
*   4-H Showcase – August 22nd, 10:00 AM
*   Pig Races – Daily at 2:00 PM and 6:00 PM
