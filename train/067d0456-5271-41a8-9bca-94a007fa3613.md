**The Ritual of the Hollow Circle**

1. **The Invocation of the Empty Chair**
   - A circle of five stones (once black, now worn gray) marks the perimeter of a chamber where no one sits.
   - The priest kneels, tracing a line between them with ash, whispering:
     *"Here we stand where the voice was once heard."*

2. **The Offering of the Unseen**
   - A bowl of water, once filled with blood, now contains only dust.
   - The priest pours it into the center, where it spreads in a perfect circle but evaporates before touching the stones.
   - A single candle burns, flickering unnaturally—its flame follows the shape of the ash line.

3. **The Whispered Silence**
   - The priest recites a chant in a language that no longer exists:
     *"The heart beats in the hollow, the mind fades into the stone."*
   - The room hums with static—no sound, yet the air vibrates like a memory.

4. **The Final Gesture**
   - A single leaf, plucked from an oak that once stood near the old temple, is pressed into the ash.
   - The priest folds his hands and closes his eyes. When he opens them, the leaf is no longer green.

5. **The Aftermath**
   - The stones remain cold. The water bowl still holds dust. The candle burns out, leaving only a single, unanswered question:
     *"Why did we do this?"*
