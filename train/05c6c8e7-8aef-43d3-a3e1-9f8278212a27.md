The daily shift change at the local bakery: Chef Michel hands me the first tray of fresh cinnamon rolls before the coffee machine even sputters. No one sees the way the dough rises perfectly in the warm oven or the way the sugar glistens just right when sprinkled—just the way I can see the way the first batch, before the rest, feels like a quiet triumph. A single roll, unnoticed by the crowd, but the one that makes me smile.
