Lily the bunny had a big, cozy burrow. Every morning, she’d wiggle her nose and say, *"Mmm, this is my favorite place!"* But one sunny day, she noticed something strange—the burrow felt a little hollow, and her favorite carrot patch was gone.

"Where did my carrots go?" she whispered. Suddenly, a tiny blue bird with a songbook fluttered down. "I heard you cryin’," the bird chirped. "Follow me, Lily! There’s a magic seed in my pocket that’ll grow a new garden!"

Lily hopped after the bird, who led her to a sparkling stream. Inside the water, the seed glowed. "Press here!" the bird said. With a gentle tap, the seed burst into a bright green plant. Soon, tiny seeds sprouted everywhere, and soon after, big, juicy carrots popped out!

Lily’s burrow was filled with laughter and crunchy treats. "Best burrow ever!" she cheered. The bird chirped, *"Now, who wants to help me plant more seeds?"* And together, they made the happiest garden in the meadow.

The end!
