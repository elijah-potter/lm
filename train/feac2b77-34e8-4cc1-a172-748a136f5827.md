The whisper of the wind carried secrets through the empty streets, a chorus of forgotten names humming in the alleyways. At the heart of this quiet city stood Elias Veyne, a man whose fingers had once traced the edges of forgotten maps, but whose heart now belonged to the ghost of a woman he had loved before the world had learned to erase.

His apartment was a shrine to lost possibilities—books bound in leather that had seen centuries, a piano half-played, its keys still dusted with the faintest echo of a melody no longer sung. The door creaked open as the last light of dusk bled through the cracks in the blinds, revealing Elias standing before a mirror that showed not his reflection but the hollow of a man who had outlived his purpose.

He reached for the letter he had been searching for—a note from Clara, his first love, tucked inside an old envelope found in a secondhand shop. The ink was smudged with time, but the words were clear:

*"I was wrong to leave. You are the only one who understands the weight of what we lost. Find me before the night turns to dawn, or the truth will swallow you whole."*

Elias exhaled, the breath curling like smoke in the cold air. The question hung between them, heavy and unspoken: *Was he running from his past, or chasing the one ghost who still believed in him?*
