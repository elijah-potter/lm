A floating market in Hanoi’s Red River, where vendors sell fresh herbs, lantern-lit wares, and street food wrapped in banana leaves, but the market is also a modern digital hub with QR codes linking to online menus and cashless payments. The stalls’ wooden stalls blend with holographic billboards advertising local tours, while vendors chat in Vietnamese and English on phones. The scent of grilled lemongrass and lemongrass-infused drinks mingles with the faint hum of servers and data centers beneath the market’s arches. The canal waters shimmer under streetlights, reflecting not only the lanterns but also the glow of floating tablets displaying real-time weather updates for the floating market.
