In 1941, Clayton was 17 years old. He looked young for his age and wasn't the largest guy around - even in his small village of Beemer, Nebraska. The young man's father had died in a tragic accident under suspicious circumstances and young Clayton decided that he needed to get out of this small town of 300 people. In those days a simple form could change your age, so he made himself 18 and signed up for the army. He was deployed to Normandy, France after the initial D-Day invasion. He then was part of a tank crew pushing towards the German border. During a large battle, he was severely injured but met the love of his life in a wartime hospital. The trauma and PTSD of wartime affected their relationship and their relationships with their children. The main story will follow the evolution of the United States in the late 20th century.
