As you enter the long dining hall, the first thing that strikes you is the sheer opulence on display: overstuffed chairs, lavish carpets, and precious stones line the broad mahogany table as numerous paintings cover the walls. In the center of the table, you see a golden platter piled high with rich foods, beyond which sits an emaciated man in expensive garb at the head. The room is lit by a hearth encased in marble with crackling blue and gold flames, and in the corner, you see a coat rack with far more arms than should reasonably fit on its 5-foot length. The room is quiet aside from the crackling of the hearth, and it's empty of inhabitants except for the skeleton-like fellow at the host's seat at the table.
