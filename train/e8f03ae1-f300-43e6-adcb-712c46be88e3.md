**The Last Transmission**

*Five minutes to go.*

The terminal flickered to life with static. Dr. Elias Veyra adjusted the last data feed—**4:57:22**. The countdown on the screen was exact, unrelenting. His fingers hovered over the keyboard as the system beeped, confirming the final transmission.

*"Final validation complete. All parameters stable. Proceeding to autonomous execution."*

Outside, the city hummed with life—until the last second.

**00:00:00**

The airlock hissed open. The ship’s engines roared, a deafening scream of metal and fire. Elias watched from the observation deck as the last satellite detached, its signal dissolving into the void. The countdown had been real.

*Three… two… one…*

The explosion lit up the sky.

The story ends here.
