Once upon a time, there was a big elephant and a small mouse who were friends. One day, the elephant was caught in a hunter's trap. The mouse heard the elephant crying for help and hurried to his rescue. The mouse saw large ropes tying the elephant up. The mouse used his large teeth to gnaw through the ropes of the trap to free the elephant.

The elephant was grateful to his friend for saving his life. He went to thank the mouse, but the mouse was gone. The mouse had run away, afraid that the hunter, who had set the trap, would come back. The elephant was sad that his best friend was gone.

The elephant searched for his best friend, but could not find him. The elephant never forgot the mouse. Throughout all his long years, he remembered the kindness of his best friend the mouse, and he never stopped missing him.
