A misaligned promise between developers and stakeholders led to a rushed implementation of a feature, failing to address core user pain points. The front-end team delivered a prototype in three weeks, while back-end scaling was incomplete. Stakeholders prioritized the quick launch over robustness, resulting in performance issues during peak usage. Post-launch, user feedback revealed the system crashed under load, highlighting a lack of proper integration testing. The misunderstanding stemmed from differing definitions of "done"—stakeholders saw a functional demo, while engineers focused on bug-free code. The project’s scope expanded unchecked, leading to cascading delays and a budget overrun. Without retrospectives, lessons were lost, and the same misalignment repeated in subsequent sprints, leaving stakeholders frustrated and developers burned out.
