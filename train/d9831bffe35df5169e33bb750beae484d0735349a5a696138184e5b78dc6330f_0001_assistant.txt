Once upon a stormy night, when I was tired from a day of flight, 
I perched upon a sheltered branch and rested my head against a pane. 
Through the glass, a man was sleeping, tired from a day of weeping,
For a woman who had left him, and I could feel his pain.
I tapped my beak once or twice, sharp and loud against the pane, 
But the man had gone insane.
