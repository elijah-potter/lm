Weeds propagate and take over when conditions are favorable for them to do so. These conditions include specific temperatures depending on the weed, lawn moisture levels, and bare or thin lawn areas. Anywhere there's room gives weeds the ability to grow. Weed seeds grow very rapidly in plants and can lay dormant in the soil for years before germinating. When weeds grow, they can produce thousands of seeds per plant and have the ability to scatter them throughout the season. Dandelion seeds use the help of the wind to spread. Poor quality grass bought in the store may also be a source of weeds.
