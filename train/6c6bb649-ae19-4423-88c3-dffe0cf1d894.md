Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny mouse named Pip. Pip was small but brave, and he loved exploring his home.

One sunny morning, Pip scampered outside to play. He saw a shiny red berry that looked delicious. He picked it up and nibbled a tiny bite. Suddenly, a big "POP!" made him jump! A tiny sparkle flew from the berry and landed on Pip's nose. Pip blinked and gasped—now he could see things in colors that weren’t there before!

Pip zoomed around his garden, seeing flowers in rainbow colors. A squirrel named Sammy was munching an acorn and gasped, "Whoa! What happened to you?!"
Pip grinned. "I found a magical berry! Now I can see the world in colors!"
Sammy chuckled. "Let’s show your friends!"
With a giggle, Pip and Sammy raced to tell their friends, Benny the bunny, and Lulu the ladybug. Soon, everyone in the garden was laughing and playing in bright, colorful ways!

But then Pip noticed something strange—his sparkly nose was fading. "Oh no!" he said. "The magic is gone!"
Just then, a kind old owl hooted, "Don’t worry! The berry’s magic is only temporary. Next time, try sharing it with others to make the colors last longer!"
Pip smiled. "You’re right! Let’s share the berry with all our friends!"
Together, they chewed a little more berry, and this time, the colors stayed for a whole day. Pip and his friends laughed, played, and enjoyed the world in all its bright, wonderful colors.

And from that day on, Pip and his friends always remembered to share their magic, making their garden even more magical than before. The end!
