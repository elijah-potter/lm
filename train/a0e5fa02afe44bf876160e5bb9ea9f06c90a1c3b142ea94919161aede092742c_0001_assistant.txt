In order to soothe itchy skin dermatologists suggest these steps: 
1. Apply a cold, wet cloth to the skin that itches.
2. Take an oatmeal bath.
3. Moisturize your skin with lotion free of additives, fragrances, or perfumes. 
4. Apply topical anesthetics that contain pramoxine.
5. Apply cooling agents such as menthol or calamine.
