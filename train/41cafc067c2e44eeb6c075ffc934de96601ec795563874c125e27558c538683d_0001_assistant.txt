Sure! There are quite a few vegetarian options at Mcdonald's for a healthier breakfast. Here are a few:

1. Egg McMuffin, No Meat: The egg McMuffin without meat is protein-packed and tasty.
2. Fruit and Maple Oatmeal: Oatmeal with fruit and maple is a well-rounded meal, sure to fill you up.
3. Hashbrown With Fruit: The hashbrown is delicious and a classic, but it doesn't have a ton of nutritional value on its own. When paired with sliced apples, it's a wonderful breakfast option.
