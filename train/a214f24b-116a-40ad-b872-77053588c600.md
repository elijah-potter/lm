A lone figure stands before a flickering hearth, their breath misting in the cold night air. The lantern casts long shadows across a worn wooden table, where an old, leather-bound book lies open to a single page. The words *"Whispers of the unseen"* are inscribed in gold ink beneath a crescent moon.

A child’s voice echoes, *"One day, you’ll hear them, Mama."* The promise lingers like smoke. The figure reaches for the book, fingers trembling—though their eyes are steady. A single candle, half-extinguished, flickers to life, casting a golden glow on the words: *"The path is not yours to walk alone."* The air hums with anticipation.
