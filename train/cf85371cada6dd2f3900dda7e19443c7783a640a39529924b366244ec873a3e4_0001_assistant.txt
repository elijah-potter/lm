**Painted Ladybug Rocks**

Find some small 
and smooth round rocks
If you wish, you can
keep them in a box

Get them at a craft
type store
Or you can search
a river shore

Wash the rocks
and dry them all
So that they’re ready
for their new call

Buy acrylic
outdoor paint
in colors that won’t 
Fade to faint

Choose the shades
you think are right
to make your bug
stand out in light

You also need a 
painting brush
something small
And not too lush

Paint whole rocks
in your color base
whichever shade
is to your taste

Grab a new shade
one totally dark
To fill in the front
For a head to mark

Turn your paintbrush
upside-down
Dip the tip and make 
dots all around

Then clean off the end of
the same brush tip
to make white eyes
on the head with a dip

Let the paint go 
all the way dry
before you paint more
on this little guy

Grab a toothpick 
with the end so small
to put black dots
in white eyes like a doll

Dry again so
there is no smear
then protect it well
with mod podge clear

Enjoy your ladybug
creatively made
your own art piece
that will never fade
