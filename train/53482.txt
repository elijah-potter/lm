|What you do for this ZOOMdo:
- Take the picture and lay it on a flat surface.
- Then begin to glue the beans to the picture.
- For large areas of the same color, you can place a thin layer of glue over the area and then press on the beans.
- Or, for smaller details, you can place a little glue on the individual beans and place them on the paper.
- When it's dry you can glue your completed mosaic to cardboard.
