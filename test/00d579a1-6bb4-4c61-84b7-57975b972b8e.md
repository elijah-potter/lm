Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a tiny mouse named Milo. One sunny morning, Milo found a shiny blue berry near his home. "Yum, yummy," he thought, and he ate it.

Suddenly, a big, warm sunbeam tickled his nose. Milo giggled and stretched. "Today is a wonderful day!" he said happily.

Just then, a rustling came from the bushes. Milo peeked out and saw his best friend, Benny the Bunny, hopping around. "Hi, Benny! Did you hear about the berry?" Milo asked.

Benny's ears perked up. "Oh, I did! Did you find another one?" Benny asked excitedly.

Milo nodded. "Yes! Let’s share our delicious berry!" They hopped and ran to their favorite garden, where they dug up some tasty carrots.

While digging, they heard a tiny squeak. Milo and Benny looked down and saw a little bird named Pip with a broken wing. "Oh no, Pip!" Milo cried.

Benny quickly helped Pip by gathering soft leaves and putting them under his wing. "Stay here, we’ll get help!" Benny said. Milo and Benny ran to their grandpa who lived nearby. Grandpa had a special tool that could fix Pip’s wing.

When they returned, Pip’s wing was all better. "Thank you, Milo and Benny!" Pip chirped. "You are the bravest friends ever!"

From that day on, Milo and Benny always helped others. They shared their berries, carrots, and kindness with everyone. And Pip even taught them how to make the most beautiful songs in the garden.

The end.
