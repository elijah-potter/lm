Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a garden full of flowers, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop, play, and explore his world.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and stretched his tiny paws. "Today is my favorite day!" he thought. He sniffed the air and heard a tiny *squeak*. It was his best friend, a bluebird named Bella, who was trying to find her favorite blue flower.

"Bella! Let’s go together!" Benny said, his ears perked up. They hopped down the path, past the daisies, and saw a big, shiny butterfly dancing in the sunshine.

"Wow!" Bella chirped. "Let’s watch it!"

The butterfly fluttered around them, painting beautiful colors in the air. Suddenly, it stopped and fluttered toward a tiny hole in the ground. Benny and Bella looked down and saw a little mushroom that glowed softly.

"I wonder what’s inside?" Benny asked.

They carefully dug a little hole with their paws and found a soft, spongy mushroom inside. It smelled sweet! "Mmm," Benny said, and Bella giggled.

"Let’s share!" Bella said. They ate a tiny bite of the mushroom, and suddenly, they felt happy and full of energy. They hopped around, dancing in circles, and even sang a little song together.

But just then, a big, grumpy old owl named Oliver flew down from the tree. "What are you two doing down there?" he hooted.

"We’re just playing," Benny said. "We found a happy mushroom!"

Oliver’s eyes twinkled. "Hmm, you know, mushrooms can help make people feel happy. Maybe you should share it with everyone!"

Benny and Bella looked at each other. "We’ll tell everyone!" Benny said.

They hopped home, where their family was waiting. The bunny family had a big feast with all their friends, and everyone shared the sweet mushroom. Soon, the whole garden was filled with laughter and joy.

From that day on, Benny and Bella made sure to always share their happiness with others. And every time they dug up a tiny glowing mushroom, they knew they’d find a little bit of magic waiting inside.

The end!
