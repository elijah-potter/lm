Absolutely! Here are some names with positive meanings that aren't your typical Western names:

- Ricarda (German) This name means "powerful leader," and is a feminisation of the name Richard
- Tully (Irish) Somewhat flowery in presentation, this name means "peaceful."
- Minako (Japanese) This name has a commanding presence, yet has a meaning that roughly translates to "beautiful child."
- Adira (Hebrew) A powerful name that means "strong."
- Zuri (Swahili) This name means "beautiful" and sounds strong.
- Hua (Chinese) This one is a short, lovely name that means "flower."
- Sigrid (Scandanavian) This was a popular Viking name that means "beautiful victory."

I hope this list helps you name your character! Happy writing!
