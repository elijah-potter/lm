Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a little bunny named Pip. Pip had fluffy white ears that bounced when he hopped, and a tail that wiggled like a spring.

One sunny morning, Pip woke up and saw his favorite things: a basket full of carrots, a pile of soft grass, and his favorite toy—a little green frog who loved to splash in puddles.

Pip hopped outside and found a shiny new leaf. "Mmm, this one is perfect!" he chewed, giggling. Suddenly, he heard a tiny voice, "Hello, Pip! Are you hungry?"

Pip turned to see a tiny mouse named Mimi with a basket of acorns. "I made these for you," she said. Pip took one and said, "Thank you, Mimi!"

They played together until Pip saw his friend, a kind old owl named Ollie, sitting on a branch. "Good morning, Pip!" Ollie said. "Want to share a snack?"

Pip and Mimi sat under the tree, eating acorns and carrots. Ollie told stories about the stars, and Pip giggled so hard he couldn't stop. When it got dark, they curled up together, snuggled in a blanket of leaves.

Pip and Mimi knew they had the best friend in the world—Ollie—and they promised to play every day. And as the moon shone bright, they drifted off to sleep, dreaming of more adventures.

The end.
