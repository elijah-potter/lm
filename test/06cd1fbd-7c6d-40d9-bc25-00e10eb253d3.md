**Mayor’s Office Announces Emergency Bridge Repair Following Storm Damage**
*Springfield, Evergreen Valley*—Residents of Evergreen Valley were notified early this morning that the **Central River Bridge**, which spans the Evergreen River between downtown and the westside, is partially collapsed due to severe storm flooding last night. Authorities confirmed that the bridge’s support beams were overwhelmed by a sudden 12-foot surge, though no vehicles or pedestrians were reported trapped below.

Officials confirmed the bridge’s upper deck remains intact but structurally unstable. The **Evergreen Fire Department** and **Valley Rescue Squad** are conducting an emergency evacuation of nearby businesses and homes in the flood zone, including the historic **Springfield Market District**. Evacuation routes have been rerouted via the **Northwest Expressway**, with traffic expected to slow as emergency crews prepare for a potential debris cleanup.

The **City Council** has declared a state of emergency, activating the **Emergency Response Task Force**. All bridges along the Evergreen River—including the **Blackthorn Bridge** and **Sunset Overlook Bridge**—are now closed pending full inspections. Public transit services along Route 12 and 34 have been suspended until further notice.

Residents in flood-prone areas have been advised to move to designated shelters at the **Evergreen Community Center** or **Westside Senior Housing**. Food and water supplies have been distributed at designated drop points, while medical teams are monitoring displaced individuals at the **Evergreen Medical Center’s emergency bay**.

Mayor Elias Carter issued a statement urging patience: *“This is a temporary disruption, but our priority is safety. We’ll be here to support affected families—no matter what.”* A live briefing is scheduled for 2:00 PM tomorrow at the city hall press room.

**Additional Updates:**
- **Road Closures:** All lanes of the **Evergreen River Viaduct** are blocked; alternative detours via **Highway 41 South** are advised.
- **Utility Outages:** Power and water disruptions reported in zones 3 and 5; crews are on standby.
- **Flooding Levels:** River gauge at **Evergreen Landing** shows a peak of 14.2 feet (up from 10.8 pre-storm).
