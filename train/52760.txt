Average weather and climate in Lebanon
Lebanon has a typically Mediterranean climate, characterized by a long, hot, and dry summer, and cool, rainy winter. There are four seasons. Summer (June to September) is hot on the coast and cooler in the mountains. Spring and autumn are warm and pleasant. Winter (December to mid March) is the rainy season, with major precipitation falling after December and with snow in the mountains.
