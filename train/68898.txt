What is the application of dot product in physics?
Applications of Dot Product The application of the scalar product is the calculation of work. The product of the force applied and the displacement is called the work. If force is exerted at an angle θ to the displacement, the work done is given as the dot product of force and displacement as W = f d cos θ.
