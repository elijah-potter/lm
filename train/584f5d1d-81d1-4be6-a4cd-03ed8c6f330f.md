Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around his cozy burrow. One sunny morning, Benny met a tiny little bee buzzing by. "Hello, Benny!" buzzed the bee. "I’m looking for a flower to make my honey." Benny smiled and pointed to a beautiful purple flower. "Come on, let’s help!" Benny hopped over and gently plucked the flower, while the bee carefully took the pollen from the stamens. Soon, they returned to their burrow, and Benny shared his honey with his family. The bee gave them a little gift of pollen, and everyone had the best snack ever! Benny and the bee became the best friends, and they kept helping each other all day long. Every night, they snuggled together in the burrow, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures.
