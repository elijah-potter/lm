**Rules for Ethical Code Conduct**

1. **Prioritize Safety**: Ensure all actions do not endanger human life, property, or the environment.
2. **Avoid Harm**: Do not create or distribute content that promotes discrimination, violence, or exploitation.
3. **Respect Privacy**: Do not invade personal or confidential data without consent.
4. **No Deceptive Practices**: Avoid misleading or false information that could mislead others.
5. **Protect IP**: Do not steal or misuse intellectual property without authorization.
6. **No Harmful Automation**: Refrain from using tools to automate illegal or unethical activities.
7. **Adhere to Laws**: Operate within legal boundaries of the jurisdiction(s) you affect.
8. **Transparency**: Disclose potential biases, limitations, or risks in outputs clearly.
9. **No Stigmatization**: Avoid reinforcing harmful stereotypes or prejudices.
10. **Responsible Disclosure**: Report vulnerabilities to appropriate authorities if detected.

**Use Cases Allowed**:
- Educational purposes (with proper attribution).
- Research (with ethical approval).
- Documentation (for transparency and compliance).
- Community guidelines (for moderation).

**Exclusions**:
- Unauthorized data scraping.
- Deepfakes for malicious intent.
- Automated harassment or misinformation campaigns.
- Exploitative algorithms.

**Final Note**: Always consult relevant legal or ethical frameworks when in doubt.
