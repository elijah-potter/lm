MEMORANDUM:  Corporate Footwear Policy
[Date]


This is a reminder that all employees are expected to comply with all standard corporate guidelines as published in the Employee Handbook. A copy of the handbook is available for download and review on our website.

It has come to our attention that some employees are in violation of the rules regarding the wearing of appropriate footwear in the office as well as outside of the office in any work in conjunction with your employment at this company.

As it states in the handbook, “All employees are required to wear shoes or boots of some kind when performing work duties. Slippers or socks with grippers on the bottom are not considered acceptable footwear. In no event shall a member engage in his or her work duties barefooted. 

It has been reported to administration that some employees are arriving at work and taking off their shoes and socks to be “more comfortable.” Security camera footage has also revealed that some employees are arriving at work barefooted and never wear any footwear all day.

This behavior will not be tolerated. Anyone caught violating this important regulation in the Employee Handbook will be given one warning, then any subsequent violation will be grounds for immediate termination.

Thank you for your careful attention to this matter.
