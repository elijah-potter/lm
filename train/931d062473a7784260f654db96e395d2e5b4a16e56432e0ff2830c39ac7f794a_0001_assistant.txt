Here's a list of 10 of the best movie theaters in Portland, Oregon, as asked:

1. Hollywood Theatre: a nonprofit theater that showcases classic, foreign, and independent films, as well as hosts events and screenings.
2. Laurelhurst Theater: a historic theater that shows second-run films, and also serves pizza and beer.
3. Living Room Theaters: this theater features independent and foreign films, plus a full food and drink menu.
4. McMenamins Bagdad Theater & Pub: theater with a pub that shows independent, classic, and second-run films.
5. Academy Theater: another historic theater that shows second-run and classic films, as well as serves beer, wine, and pizza.
6. Northwest Film Center: a nonprofit organization that showcases independent, classic, and foreign films, and hosts events and screenings.
7. Regal Lloyd Center & IMAX: a movie theater that shows mainstream films and features an IMAX screen.
8. Cinema 21: a theater that shows independent and foreign films, and hosts events and special screenings.
9. Clinton Street Theater: one more historic theater that hosts events and live performances, and shows second-run, cult, and classic films.
10. Moreland Theater: another historic theater that shows second-run and classic films, and sells beer and wine.
