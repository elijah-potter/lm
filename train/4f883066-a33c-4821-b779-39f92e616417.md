Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop and play. One sunny morning, Benny saw a shiny red apple near his burrow. "Mmm, red apple," Benny thought. He picked it up and nibbled a little, but it wasn’t sweet enough.

Just then, Benny’s friend Benny the Bear came over. "Want to play a game?" Benny Bear asked. "I’ll show you how to make the best apple ever!" Benny Bear said, and he took Benny to a magical tree where the apples sparkled and tasted like candy.

Benny tried a bite, and it was so sweet and juicy he giggled. "Best apple ever!" he said. Benny Bear shared his secret: "Sometimes, the best things are worth waiting for!"

Benny promised to visit again, and together, they played hopscotch until the sun started to set. Benny curled up warm in his burrow, dreaming of more adventures with Benny the Bear.
