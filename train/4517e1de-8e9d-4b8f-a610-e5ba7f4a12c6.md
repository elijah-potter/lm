A tense silence hung in the dimly lit room, broken only by the low hum of the fluorescent lights. The air smelled of stale coffee and old paper. Across from him, her fingers tapped restlessly against the table, the sound sharp and deliberate. His gaze flickered toward her, catching the way her lips parted just enough to reveal the faintest hint of a smile.

"Could you... maybe... *not* do that?" she whispered, her voice barely audible over the distant hum of the city outside.

His shoulders twitched slightly. "Why?"

She swallowed hard, her knuckles turning white around the cup. "Because I don’t know if I can handle it."
