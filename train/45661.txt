A lot of students find it difficult to manage their time adequately given the number of school responsibilities they have each day and throughout the week, on top of the stress and distractions they encounter for various reasons. But this is a fact of life that all students have to deal with and they can’t simply put off being productive due to procrastination in any form. It doesn’t matter whether or students feel up to doing what they have to in order to succeed academically – they are going to have to tackle their responsibilities head-on. If this sounds like a familiar situation to you, then these five ideas from WriteMyEssayZ.com might help you make this school year your most productive.
- Keep a Daily and Weekly Agenda
A phone calendar is not a good tool for keeping important school dates and assignment responsibilities on track. The best options are using a classic daily and weekly agenda you can carry in your backpack and reference on a daily basis. Research shows that taking handwritten notes, jotting down ideas, and making marginal adjustments to one’s schedule is an excellent way of improving productivity. You don’t have to spend a lot of money, just get something that is big enough to put in all of your school responsibilities.
- Create Smaller, Manageable Tasks
If you have a long or difficult homework assignment to do, it’s a good idea (backed by extensive research) to break the assignment down into smaller, manageable tasks. If you try to work on a single assignment without stopping, you might be compromising its accuracy and quality. Smaller tasks are much easier to deal with and can actually boost your motivation as you finish one task and move onto the next.
- Create a Positive Work Environment
We all know that it can be difficult to stay away from our phones and checking our social media accounts for extended periods of time. Technology has made certain that there is something always ready to distract us from our most important responsibilities. However, one must be diligent about staying away from these things if one wants to be productive. Create a work environment where you are free from all types of distractions including outside noises coming from other rooms or the street. Keep your workspace clutter-free and prepare all necessary materials before setting yourself down to work.
- Understand Your Limitations
It’s not in your best interest to push yourself past your limits. Working too hard can lead to burnout and feelings of stress. In the long run, this can be extremely detrimental to your level of productivity. Staying up all night will leave you feeling tired the next day and will negatively impact your level of focus in class. Set a schedule for yourself and take breaks as needed. Make sure you get away from your work environment by taking a walk or treating yourself to some time on social media.
- Start Thinking About Food as Fuel
When you’re working you will expend a lot of mental and physical energy that needs to be replenished along the way. The trick is finding the right foods to maximize your mental focus and clarity. Sugary sweets and caffeine work in the short term, but foods high in these ingredients usually have the opposite result for which they are trying to achieve. Go for mixed nuts or fruits instead. These are considered brain foods and will help you feel rejuvenated when you work.
The levels of productivity will vary from student to student, considering all of the different attitudes and methods towards the way each one works. It’s not just about working for hours on end if it means one will feel burnt out or stressed. It is far more important to feel good about what has been accomplished and have a plan for making tangible improvements along the way. Put the given ideas into action and let us know what works best for you. And if you have any productivity tips to share, we’d certainly like to hear from you.
