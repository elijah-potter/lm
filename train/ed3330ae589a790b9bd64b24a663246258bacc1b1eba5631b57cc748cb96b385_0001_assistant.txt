Kobayashi Kou is a young man going into his first year of high school. In his world, many people have special abilities that allow them to become heroes. Kou's dream is to become a great hero and fight the villains that killed his grandparents in the end. To do this, he must pass the entrance exam for the hero high school of his dreams. Despite being tall and handsome, he has average grades and physical performance. His only hope is to capitalize on his powerful ability.

Kou's ability is called "form theory" and gives him the ability to generate objects in reality based on the theories he has scanned with his ability. If he scans the form of a chair, he can produce anything that might be taken as a chair, from a log to a throne. Likewise, if he scans the form of a pistol, he can produce an old-school revolver or a modern machine pistol. His ability isn't limited to reality but is based in an ethereal realm where all things have a true form. 

With such a powerful ability, he could easily pass the test. However, he has never been able to properly control it. It will take Kou a great deal of strength and effort to achieve his dream while facing off against so many other powerful ability holders. Will Kou overcome his difficulties in controlling his power, or will he face rejection and have to become a regular citizen, dashing his dreams for the future? He only gets one shot at this.
