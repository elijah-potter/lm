Shakespeare once wrote, “All the world's a stage, and all the men and women merely players.”  And because Shakespeare was one of if not the world’s greatest playwrights, many people believe that he simply meant to say that the whole world is like one big play, dramatic and full of surprises. 

But I believe the meaning goes much deeper than that. When Shakespeare speaks of the world being a stage and the men and women as players, he’s tapping into a much bigger idea. His idea was that we, human beings, are all being directed by something much bigger than us, just as a play director manipulates his actors. 

That everything that we do, say, and experience, is actually already in motion long before we understand what is even happening. That we have no free will of our own but are simply performing a show that was made just for us. 

Now, while this may be a controversial synopsis, it seems to me that this one line shows clearly that Shakespeare did not believe we are in control of our own lives, but that everything happening is simply one big play, but we don’t know the ending.
