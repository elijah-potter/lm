 • Kaimana comes from two Hawaiian words: Kai means sea and Mana mean powerful, so the words together mean powerful sea.
 • Marcella has Italian roots and means strong and warlike.
 • Matilda has old English roots and means strength in battle.
 • Shakti means power in Hindi.
 • Valerie is a name with Latin roots and means both strong and brave.
