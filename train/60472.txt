This packet is designed to help you teach your students how to master their number concepts. So many times students struggle with understanding these fundamental skills.
Helping students master number concepts is an integral step in providing a strong foundation for Math. This packet is designed to help you guide your students towards mastery in fun, hands-on, and meaningful ways.
This packet will provide you with large group teaching ideas, small group activities, partner games, as well as independent work.
These activities will help your students really understand what a number is and will allow students to think about what makes a number.
Included in the packet are really fun hands on games that you can use in multiple ways. It also includes recording pages, worksheets for every number from 1-20, assessment tools, and sample progressions to help give you a road map when teaching these skills.
You and your students will love these activities!
