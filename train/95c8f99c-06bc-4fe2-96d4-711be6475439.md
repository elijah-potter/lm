In a dimly lit server rack, a single fiber-optic cable flickers between two network switches. The signal, barely detectable in a sea of noise, traverses through a series of redundant paths before reaching its destination. A firewall logs it as "unauthorized traffic," dismissing it as noise. The switch, overwhelmed by 10Gbps bursts, prioritizes higher-priority traffic, silently dropping the faint pulse. By the time the signal arrives at the final hop, it’s a ghost of its original strength—buried under latency spikes and protocol mismatches. The destination device, buried in a data lake of unprocessed requests, neither acknowledges nor rejects it, letting it dissolve into the static.
