*[Scene opens in a dimly lit office. A man in a tailored suit sits across from a woman in a sleek business attire. Her posture is stiff, her hands folded neatly on the desk. She leans slightly forward, her gaze fixed on him with unreadable expression.]*

She nods slowly, lips pressed thin. "I hear you. You’re describing the new project’s timeline." Her voice is measured, but her eyes dart subtly to the clock.

*[He adjusts his tie, fingers tapping the desk absently. The woman’s fingers trace the edge of her glass, but she doesn’t drink.]*

"You’re not listening," he murmurs, voice low. The pause stretches like a beat in a slow song. Then—*almost*—she shifts, as if deciding.

"Just… verifying," she replies, voice flat. The silence swallows her words whole. Outside, the city hums; inside, the lie crackles like static.
