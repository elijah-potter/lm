Kevin James' first role was in 1991 on "The New Candid Camera" where he played pranks on unsuspecting individuals.  His big break came after becoming friends with Ray Romano and appearing as a guest star on "Everybody Loves Raymond."  This led James to be the star of his own sitcom "The King of Queens" airing from 1998 to 2007.  As it comes to film, James did a cameo in 2004 for "50 First Dates" and in 2005 starred in his first movie "Hitch" a romantic comedy.
