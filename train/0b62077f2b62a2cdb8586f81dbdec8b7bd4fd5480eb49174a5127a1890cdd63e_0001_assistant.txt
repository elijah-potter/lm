1. You can find a safe location: Look for a level, straight stretch of road or an empty parking lot.
2. Turn on hazard lights: Activate hazard lights to alert other drivers.
3. Apply parking brake: Engage the parking brake to prevent the vehicle from rolling.
4. Apply wheel wedges: Use wedges to further prevent the vehicle from rolling.
5. Remove the hubcap or wheel cover: Take off the hubcap or wheel cover using a lug wrench.
6. Loosen lug nuts: Turn them counterclockwise to loosen them.
7. Place the jack under the vehicle: Position the jack under the frame near the flat tire.
8. Raise the vehicle with the jack: Lift the vehicle until the flat tire is about six inches above the ground.
9. Unscrew the lug nuts: Completely remove the lug nuts by hand.
10. Remove the flat tire: Pull it towards you and set it on its side.
11. Mount spare tire on lug bolts: Align the spare tire with the lug bolts and push it onto them.
12. Tighten lug nuts by hand: Put them back on and tighten them as much as possible by hand.
13. Lower the vehicle and tighten the lug nuts again: Use the lug wrench to tighten the lug nuts.
14. Lower the vehicle completely: Bring the vehicle to the ground and remove the jack.
15. Replace the hubcap: If it fits the spare tire, put the hubcap or wheel cover back on.
16. Please make sure to stow all equipment: Gather and secure all used equipment in the vehicle.
17. Check spare tire pressure: Use a pressure gauge to ensure the spare tire has the correct pressure. Temporary spares may only be designed to get you to the nearest service station.
18. Take the flat tire to a technician: Visit a tire technician to assess if the tire needs repair or replacement.
