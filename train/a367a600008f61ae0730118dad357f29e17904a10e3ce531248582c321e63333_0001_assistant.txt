1. Value and be patient with yourself.
2. Take care of yourself (eat well, avoid smoking/vaping, drink water, exercise, and get enough sleep).
3. Surround yourself with positive people.
4. Give your time (volunteer).
5. Learn healthy ways to deal with stress.

8. Get outside and connect with others.
