The routine involves precise, synchronized movements with explosive power and controlled precision. Key elements include:
- **Warm-up**: Dynamic stretches and light cardio to elevate core temperature.
- **Technique drills**: Focused on form—spikes, jumps, and landings with controlled amplitude.
- **Intensity progression**: Increasing repetition ranges (e.g., 5x10–20 reps) with controlled tempo.
- **Power phases**: Explosive jumps (depth jumps, box jumps) with maximal effort on the up phase.
- **Controlled descent**: Slow eccentric phase (4–5 sec) to maintain joint integrity.
- **Finisher**: 30–60 sec of all-out sprints or battle ropes to spike metabolic demand.
- **Recovery**: Active recovery with mobility drills to prevent compensatory imbalances.
- **Visualization**: Mental rehearsal of movement patterns under fatigue conditions.

**Performance triggers**: Triggered by the combination of **low perceived exertion (RPE 3–4) and high mechanical precision**, with optional psychological cues (e.g., "go hard on the up phase").
