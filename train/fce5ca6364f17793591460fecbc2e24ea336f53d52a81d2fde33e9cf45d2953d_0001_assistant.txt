In Italy's land, a gift divine,
A flattened disk, so very fine.
The pizza dish, culinary delight,
Full of flavors that ignite.

Olive oil, a drizzle so pure,
Oregano's scent that always endures.
Ripe tomatoes, so very red,
Olives dancing, what a spread.

Mozzarella, a blanket of cheese,
Countless ingredients sure to please.
Baked with haste in the fiery glow,
Wood-fired oven, the magic bestowed.

Margherita, a regal creation,
Tomatoes, basil, a royal sensation.
Named for a queen, so it's told,
Green, white, and red, the flag's colors are bold.
