The repetitive motion of checking the phone every 15 minutes began as a stress reliever. Over time, it turned into an addiction, consuming hours daily. The urge to scroll became instinctive, replacing actual work. Relationships suffered due to missed conversations; productivity dwindled as attention fragmented. Eventually, the habit replaced purposeful activity, leaving a void where focus once thrived. The cycle of dopamine hits reinforced the behavior, making withdrawal painful. The problem wasn’t the phone—it was the mind’s inability to break free from the loop.
