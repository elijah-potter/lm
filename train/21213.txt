Angel Moroni is a figure who appears in the religious text of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (LDS). what did he tell joseph smith
The Angel Moroni is an angel who, according to Latter Day Saint belief, visited Joseph Smith on numerous occasions, beginning on September 21, 1823. An important figure in the early history of the Latter Day Saint movement, Moroni is featured prominently in the religion’s origin story.
On the evening of September 21, 1823, Smith said that he was visited in his bedroom by a “personage” who identified himself as the “Angel Moroni.” According to Smith’s account, Moroni informed him about a book made of golden plates that was buried in a nearby hill; this record contained “the fulness of the everlasting Gospel” and would be useful in restoring Christianity to its former purity.
