A disorganized stack of financial reports on a cluttered desk glows under a dying fluorescent light. The accountant, arms crossed, stares at a column of recurring numbers—month after month, quarter after quarter—identifying the same sum in each "Loss" row: **$3,721.45**. A finger traces the last entry: *"Project Overrun"* under the "Miscellaneous" expense. Their jaw tightens. The spreadsheet suddenly *clicks* in their mind. A red flag flips: **the pattern is not random—it’s deliberate.**

The phone buzzes. A text from the CEO: *"Why are we always short by 2/3rds of the budget? Fix it."* The accountant’s fingers hover over the keyboard, typing a response that will either save the company—or expose the truth. Outside, a rainstorm drums against the window, muffling the sound of the printer’s final chime.
