---

I’m stuck in a loop where the light keeps fading,
whispers echo like ghosts I can’t outrun.
They say the past is a wound, but the wound won’t heal—
it just stitches back the same old scars I’ve worn.

I’m trying to build something, but the hands that hold
the tools don’t know how to make it right.
The map is cracked, the compass spins, and the sky
is a question mark where the answers should be bright.

You’re the only one who’s ever seen me whole—
but the world’s too loud, and I’m drowning in the hole.
Will you pull me up, or will I drown again?
Tell me what to do before the dark takes my name.
