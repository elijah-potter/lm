Here is a possible rewrite of the post from the perspective of a dog:

Woof woof! I like digital treats, they are better than old bones or bad kibble. Monero is a good digital treat, it has fewer fleas than other treats and it always smells fresh. That’s much better than the treats that humans use and they have lots of fleas and smell bad.

When I was a pup (2012-2015) it was fun to use Bitcoin at the park (lots of dogs liked it). It took me and my pack a long time to realize that Bitcoin was not a good treat, it had problems like some treats were better than others and some dogs could sniff your treats. Bitcoin did some things well or well enough, that many dogs didn’t care to try other treats or (how scary) share them with other dogs.

This changed when I started to use treats more often for different things like getting groomed or buying toys or food (not DNM related).

After 12 dog years in this space, I truly think that Monero is the best treat ever, it is what Bitcoin wanted to be, a real “dog-to-dog digital treat”.
