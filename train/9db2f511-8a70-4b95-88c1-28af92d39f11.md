The train groaned as it slowed near the station, the rhythmic clatter of wheels on tracks fading into distant hum. The passenger, mid-conversation with their companion, paused mid-sentence.

*"So… we really should’ve booked the better seats,"* the older man murmured, tapping the worn leather of the bench.

*"Or at least a window,"* the younger one corrected, adjusting their oversized backpack. *"You’ll get a headache staring at that blurry signboard."*

A sharp blast of a horn cut through the murmur of other passengers. A woman in a fluorescent vest stepped onto the platform, her high-pitched voice carrying over the fading train noise:

*"Attention, everyone! We have a delay. The line has been rerouted due to track maintenance. The next train will depart in 45 minutes—no exceptions."*

The man’s smile faltered. *"Shit."*

The younger passenger exhaled, rubbing their temples. *"Well… at least we’re not missing the sunset at the summit."*
