Big Foot Found Living in NYC's Subway Tunnels!

Moments ago, the Metro Transit Authority confirmed the earlier sightings that were reported across social media after morning train riders spotted "something that looked like a gorilla" outside the windows of a Route 8 train was in fact Big Foot. Authorities have closed all subway routes and advised commuters to use bus lines or other forms of transportation as they continue to investigate. They have stated there is no threat to the public at this time and it is rumored that Big Foot has been captured. An anonymous source says that Big Foot was tracked to a long out-of-use tunnel beneath the city. The source described the situation as "the kind of thing you see in the movies when a big animal is living in a cave." 


The mayor of the city will be holding a press conference at 3:00 PM where he is expected to reveal the first up-close and personal photos of Big Foot.
