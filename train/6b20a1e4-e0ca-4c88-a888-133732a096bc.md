**The Clockmaker’s Daughter**

The old clock tower stood silent, its hands frozen at three past two,
while Liora adjusted the last thread, her fingers trembling with the weight
of her mother’s words: *"Time is a thief, and it takes everything—*
*your youth, your dreams, your name."*

She whispered them back, the words looping like the gears inside,
until the chime fell silent—just as always. Outside, the city slept,
but in her chest, the clock kept ticking.
