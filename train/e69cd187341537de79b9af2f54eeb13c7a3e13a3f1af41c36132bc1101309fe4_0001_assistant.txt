Zack was a pizzeria owner and pizza enthusiast. He spent decades perfecting and experimenting with the dough-making process in his restaurant. One day, after mixing the dough with some super glue and about 3 grams of lipstick, some unimaginable magic happened: the dough became unbreakable yet still elastic. Zack believed the lipstick must have triggered some multi-universe interferences causing physical rules to stop applying to that piece of dough. 

Even though the dough was unsuitable for pizza, Zack did not want his once-in-a-lifetime discovery to go to waste. He immediately proposed a space program and received grants from NASA by turning his dough into a pizza-shaped trampoline! Since it was unbreakable and still elastic, it could store an unlimited amount of energy and release it. Some initial field tests suggested that Zack's pizza worked like a mega slingshot, sending astronauts into space without requiring a rocket. 

Zack quickly capitalized on his success and built many launching platforms. Humankind finally turned space-borne by re-imagining an old dish.
