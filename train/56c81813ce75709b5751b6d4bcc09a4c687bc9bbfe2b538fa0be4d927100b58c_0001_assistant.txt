Wooly Makes a Pie

Wooly the sheep was craving something sweet
So he picked some apples to bake up a treat
He made a crust and a filling
He did some baking and some chilling
And now the apple pie is ready to eat


The Hungry Fox and The Pie

Fiona the fox was using the hose when she smelled something through her nose
Could it be? Oh yes, it is, a delicious apple pie
She stood and pondered, she stopped, and she froze
She walked to Wooly's house and let her fist fly
Fiona knocked on the door, and her thoughts turned sly
The sheep opened the door, and asked how goes?
But the fox burst straight past him and went for the pie
She gobbled a whole half before Wooly knows
But now he had enough, and his temper rose
Wooly then kicked the fox into the sky
"Nobody just barges in and eats my pie!"
