Once upon a time, an office enforced a rule: **"All meetings must start on time."** It was enforced with a 5-minute warning bell. Workers complied—until the last quarter of the year, when the rule vanished without explanation.

Some employees kept it as a habit, others forgot. The rule was never revisited, and now it’s just an unspoken tradition buried in the annals of corporate memory, forgotten by those who never noticed its existence in the first place.
