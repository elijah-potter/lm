The Industrial Revolution came with many negative effects, primarily relating to the poor and dangerous way of life for the workers. There wasn't enough housing for everyone and many people lived in overcrowded dwellings, often in the cellars of houses with no ventilation or sanitation, which led to the spread of infectious diseases. The places where the people worked were dangerous, with little to no safety regulations. Workers faced the constant risk of losing their hands to machinery. Child labor was widespread, where children were forced to work in horrible conditions, work long hours, and had no access to education. Having to perform the dangerous work of adults, children frequently suffered terrible, grisly fates.
