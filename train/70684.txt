BrightFish Reading is a research-based online program for grades 2 through high school that deconstructs text to improve reading through a unique mastery-learning model. Students build fluency, vocabulary and comprehension skills to become independent, active readers.
K-12 schools across the U.S. and Canada are achieving great results with BrightFish Reading. Flexible and accessible from anywhere using a standard web browser, BrightFish Reading is being used for structured reading practice in a broad range of implementation models.
Here are just a few examples of how BrightFish Reading is improving outcomes for struggling learners:
Intensive – High School Readiness
Fidelity Model – Middle School
IEPs – Elementary Intervention
Development – English Learners
