Once upon a time, a sheep named Lana went out to search for a home, she was very lonely because she didn't have any family by her side. She went across the country looking for somewhere to call home, when one day, she met a boy named Alvin who told her about a festival going on in a faraway land, she walked for 2 days to get there. When she finally made it there, she had so much fun going to all the booths and playing the games. On the last day of the festival, she went to the circus where she met the ringmaster called Juan. Juan met Lana and saw she had potential, he offered her a position at the circus to jump through hoops. Lana was so happy someone finally was willing to give her a home she said yes. At the end of the night, she met all the other people in the circus and they welcomed her with open arms. She was so happy she found her home. The end
