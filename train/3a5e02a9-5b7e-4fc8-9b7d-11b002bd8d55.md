A flickering streetlight cast long shadows as rain dripped from the eaves of an abandoned bookstore. **Mira** crouched beside the cracked sidewalk, her gloved fingers brushing the damp cobblestones near the alleyway’s edge. She had come for the lost-and-found box—its red plastic lid half-buried under a pile of discarded newspapers and a rusted bicycle chain. The box was empty.

Her breath fogged in the cold air as she knelt, examining the alley’s crevices. A loose stone rolled toward her. She picked it up—smooth, the size of a walnut, and warm to the touch.

Then she saw it: a **golden pocket watch**, its chain snapped, the face still legible—*18:47*. It lay just beneath the stone, half-hidden in the mud. Mira’s fingers trembled as she lifted it, the weight of something old and forgotten pressing against her palm.
