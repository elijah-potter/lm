To the curious soul who finds themselves here, neither lost nor found—

The pages between us are thin as a breath, yet they hold more than you know.
You read these words because something in your life, or in your mind, is hungry for them.
Perhaps you’re searching for answers that aren’t yet written.
Maybe you’re trying to forget what you’ve already learned.
Or perhaps you’re simply waiting for the quiet to speak.

The light here is not mine to claim. It is yours to name.
The ink has no color unless you do.
The silence between the lines is not empty—it is a space to sit, to breathe, to remember what you’ve forgotten.

So do with this what you will.
Do not read.
Do not write.
Do not listen.
Do not answer.
Or—if you dare—answer anyway.

The choice is yours.
The rest is already written.
