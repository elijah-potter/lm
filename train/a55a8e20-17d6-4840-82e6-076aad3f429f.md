The dimly lit kitchen smelled of stale coffee and forgotten leftovers. The refrigerator door stood ajar, its shelves empty except for a single, half-eaten yogurt container. **The salt shaker was missing.** Not just any salt—this one had been the last one on the shelf, its small, irregular grains still clinging to the rim of the container. Someone had taken it. No trace of a container, no crumbs, just an empty space where the shaker should have been. The person who had left the note—*"Need more for the stew"*—had forgotten to replace it.
