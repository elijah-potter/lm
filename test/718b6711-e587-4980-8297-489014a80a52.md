Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a garden full of flowers, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop, play, and chase his favorite friend, a big, fluffy cat named Mochi.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw Mochi sleeping peacefully on his favorite sunbeam. "Mochi, wake up!" Benny called, but Mochi didn’t stir.

Benny hopped around the garden, picking flowers and giggling. Suddenly, he heard a tiny *twinkle* and saw a rainbow arching over the fence. "Wow!" Benny said, his eyes sparkling. He chased the colors, but they kept dancing away.

Just then, Mochi woke up and stretched. "I missed you, Benny!" Mochi said, curling up next to Benny. They shared a big, happy hug, and the garden sparkled even more brightly with their joy. Benny and Mochi played together, laughing and giggling, and the world felt full of magic that day.

From that moment on, Benny and Mochi became the best of friends, always finding fun adventures together in their sunny garden.
