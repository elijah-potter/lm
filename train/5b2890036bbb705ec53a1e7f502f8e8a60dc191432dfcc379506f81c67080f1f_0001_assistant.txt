Dear Mr. Mayor,

Thank you for taking the time to read my letter. This is an urgent matter and I hope that this letter will lead to further discussion. There is an old apartment complex a few blocks from my house that has been dormant for the last five years. It says it's for sale, however, nobody has bought it yet. It seems that there is too much to fix and nobody wants the hassle of fixing it. As I drive by it every day, I see so much potential for this old complex to become a beacon of hope for the homeless in our community. As you know, there is a large homeless population in Carrington that has had trouble getting on their feet. In order to get them off the street and give them a clean, safe place to live, I am asking for one million dollars to renovate and fix the apartment complex. With a place to live, they would have a better chance to get a job and become productive members of our community. This matter is very urgent and I would love to discuss further when you have a few spare moments. Please, Mr. Mayor, help us fix our beloved community.

Thank you for your time.

Thank you,
A concerned citizen
