Crackle of ancient pines, whispering secrets through damp moss.
Earthy musk of damp soil, mingled with pine resin and damp stone.
Crunch of gravel under bare feet, then soft rustle of leaves—twilight hums.
Far off, distant river murmurs, its current whispering over smooth pebbles.
Faint metallic tang of ironwood, rustling in the breeze.
Night falls, the air thick with the scent of wildflowers and damp earth.
Frozen dew glistens, reflecting starlight—cool, sharp, and bright.
