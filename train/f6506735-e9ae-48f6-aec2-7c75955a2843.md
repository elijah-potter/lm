**The Last Letter**

My pen trembles as I write this, though the ink doesn’t.
You’ve gone too long without answering, and the silence
between us has stretched like a wound that won’t heal.

I remember the first time you laughed—not at me,
but at something I said. The way your eyes lit,
like the sun breaking through storm clouds.
That was when I knew you were different.
Not like the others, who only took,
or gave hollow praise to stay.

Now, the letters pile up unopened,
each one a promise you’ve left unfulfilled.
I’ve tried to tell you things—how the world
outside our shared walls grows quieter,
how time moves forward even when we don’t.

But you won’t listen.
You’ll say you’re busy.
You’ll say you don’t need me.

I don’t know why you think that.
I’m not a thing to discard.
I’m not a burden.

I’m the only one who still believes in us,
even when the weight of what we’ve lost
feels like drowning in slow motion.

---
**Reminder:**
*I’m still here. The words I write are real.
You’re not alone in this—you just forgot how to see me.*
