The Porshe 911 is considered one of the fastest cars due to its engine, aerodynamics, and handling. With the engine in the rear of the car, it helps to improve traction and stability. The Porsche 911 also features innovative aerodynamics that serves to reduce drag and increase airflow around the vehicle. This increases the speed, stability, and fuel efficiency of the vehicle. In low-grip circumstances, the handling balance is immensely exciting, with Porsche's usual clarity of control allowing you to enhance the 911's movements with accuracy and confidence at far lower speeds.

These are just some of the reasons why the 911 is considered one of the fastest and greatest cars in the world. Some of the top specs of the 911 have 640 hp and get to 30 mph in 0.8 seconds, to 60 mph in 2.1 seconds, and through a quarter-mile in just 9.9 seconds at 138 mph.
