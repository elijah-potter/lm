Interviewer:
Hello. How are you today?

Chocolate Cake:
I'm great, how are you?

Interviewer:
I'm good. So I see you're interviewing for the job of "Easy Chocolate Dessert." Why don't you, tell me a little about yourself?

Chocolate Cake:
Well, I'm a perfectly chocolatey cake with rich depth, I have a heavenly texture with a fluffy crumb, and my fudgy decadent ganache frosting is my crowning finish! I'm perfect for birthdays and holidays, but with my simplicity, I also make an amazing weekend treat. I will be savored down to the last crumb! And I don't mean to brag, but I think I'm the easiest chocolate cake you'll ever make!

Interviewer:
Okay. So what makes you so easy to make?

Chocolate Cake:
Well, I only need just a few simple ingredients, I'm made in just one bowl with no mixer required, and I also bake in just one pan.

Interviewer:
Yes, I see those ingredients on your resume. They're:

	1 cup (142g) all-purpose flour (scoop and level to measure)
	1 cup (200g) granulated sugar
	6 Tbsp (35g) Dutch process cocoa powder, plus more for dusting pan
	1/2 tsp baking soda
	1/2 tsp salt
	1 cup buttermilk
	1/2 cup vegetable oil or canola oil
	2 tsp vanilla extract
	2 large eggs
	6 oz. semi-sweet chocolate, finely chopped
	3/4 cup heavy cream

So tell me the steps involved in making you.

Chocolate Cake:
Well first, you need to prepare my pan. To do this, just spray a 9 x 9 or an 8 x 8-inch baking pan with non-stick cooking spray. Then dust the pan with a little cocoa and shake away the excess.

Then whisk my dry ingredients in a large mixing bowl - the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt. Mix in my wet ingredients - the buttermilk, oil, vanilla and eggs, then whisk this mixture for about a minute until very well blended. Then pour my batter into the prepared baking pan.

And then to bake me, just place my baking pan into a preheated 350-degree oven and bake me until a toothpick inserted into my center comes out clean. This should take about 27 to 35 minutes. Then place me on a wire rack to completely cool.

Interviewer:
And what about your frosting?

Chocolate Cake:
Sure thing! My frosting is ganache and you can make it while I cool on the wire rack. All you have to do is place the finely chopped chocolate in a medium heat-proof mixing bowl. Then heat the heavy cream in a small saucepan on your stovetop over medium heat until it just starts to simmer. Then pour the hot cream over the chocolate and stir it with a rubber spatula until it's melted and smooth. Let this mixture cool until it’s a thicker consistency, which should take about 30 minutes. Then spread the ganache over me and slice.

Interviewer:
Well, you do sound quite chocolatey and delicious. My mouth is watering already. We have a few more applicants to see, but so far, you're the front-runner. Thank you for your time today.

Chocolate Cake:
It's been my pleasure!
