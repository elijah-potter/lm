The argument was heated; voices rose and fell in overlapping curses. A coffee stain on the tabletop blurred between two fingers, each clutching a half-empty mug. "You’re *always* late," one shouted, slamming their fist on the counter. The other, arms crossed, glared at the ceiling, muttering, "That’s not how punctuality works."

The first, wiping their mouth with a sleeve, turned sharply. "You’re *never* on time *except* when you’re late." The second’s jaw tightened. "You don’t know what ‘on time’ means."

Outside, a car horn blared—a single, defiant *beep*—as the argument dissolved into silence. The first leaned against the counter, exhaling. "Guess we’ll figure it out tomorrow." The second didn’t answer. Instead, they reached for the door handle.
