## Bookstore Serendipity (Powell’s City of Books, Portland)

I was browsing the philosophy section, attempting to decipher a particularly dense passage on existentialism, when you appeared. You were searching for poetry, your fingers tracing the spines with a quiet intensity. I noticed the vibrant emerald green of your sweater – a striking contrast against the worn, honey-colored wood of the bookshelves. We didn’t speak, but I distinctly remember the comforting scent of aged paper and brewing coffee swirling around us, creating a pocket of peaceful focus amidst the bustling Saturday afternoon crowd. You paused, selected a slim volume, and smiled faintly before moving on. I felt an unexpected pang of regret that our paths didn't converge in conversation. It was a fleeting moment, easily missed, but it lingered with me.
