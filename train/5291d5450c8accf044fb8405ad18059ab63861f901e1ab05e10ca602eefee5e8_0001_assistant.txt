Ricky had always been very flexible; even as a tadpole he was always working on mastering his forward rolls. He came from a long line of talented rain frogs, and he dreamt of one day being a circus superstar. One day, while Ricky was practising his forward rolls at the park, he was approached by a friendly clown who introduced himself as Percy. Percy had been watching in awe of Ricky's forward rolls and just knew that he would be a hit at his circus. Percy invited Ricky to join him on tour with the circus as he wanted the world to witness Ricky's talent. Ricky was ecstatic and agreed to join the circus with Percy - his dream was finally coming true. Ricky toured with the circus for years, wowing audiences far and wide and gaining a huge following of fans. Ricky could not believe that he was finally living his dream and was grateful to his new best friend Percy the Clown for making it all possible.
