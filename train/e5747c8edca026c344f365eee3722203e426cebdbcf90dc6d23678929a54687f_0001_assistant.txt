Don't look away. Maintain eye contact. Lionel's words were uppermost in my mind as I struggled not to blink. The large yellow eyes were so close to me, and as I continued to stare at them, my own eyes filled with tears.

The minutes ticked by. I shuddered and began to sweat. If I died here, would anyone find my body? Tears flowed down my face, but I persisted with my unblinking gaze.

Certain that this was the end, I began to imagine my bleached, white skeleton being found by tourists on safari, years from now.

I knew that I could not face seeing my killer. There was nothing for it but to close my eyes and surrender.

With my eyes tightly shut, I curled into a ball and waited, hoping the end would come swiftly.

Without warning, the silence was broken by a menacing roar. I braced myself for the impact, but as the seconds ticked by, nothing happened.

Why was I still alive? I forced my eyes to open.

The big craggy lion gave me one last scornful glance with his magnificent golden eyes before tossing his mane and sauntering away.
