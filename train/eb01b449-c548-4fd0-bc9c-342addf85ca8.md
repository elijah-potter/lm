The rain pattered against the old wooden windows of the dimly lit café, the sound muted by the thick curtains. Across from each other, **Lena** and **Daniel** sat with their backs to the wall, elbows resting on the worn table. She traced idle circles on the tabletop with her fingers, while he stared at his coffee, the steam curling into the air like a question mark.

A beat of silence.

Then, Lena’s voice dropped to a whisper: *"You remember the night we lost our jobs?"*

Daniel exhaled sharply, almost a laugh. *"The one where I showed up late and you yelled at me for it?"*

Lena’s shoulders relaxed. *"And you said you’d never do that again—even if it meant sleeping on the floor?"*

The words hung between them, familiar, *known*. Daniel’s fingers tightened around his cup. *"I did."*

A slow smile spread across Lena’s face. *"So you lied."*

He smirked. *"Yeah. About not sleeping on the floor."*

The air between them shifted—less tension, more understanding. Outside, the rain softened, becoming a steady rhythm. The moment was fragile, but it was theirs.
