The argument reached its peak as the two siblings glared across the cluttered kitchen table. **"You’re ruining everything again!"** hissed older brother, kicking over a half-empty bag of chips. The younger one, arms crossed, muttered, **"At least I don’t *accidentally* leave the stove on."** The smoke alarm blared, and the sudden silence made them both jump. Outside, the rain hammered the window, turning the kitchen into a mirror of their fractured moods.
