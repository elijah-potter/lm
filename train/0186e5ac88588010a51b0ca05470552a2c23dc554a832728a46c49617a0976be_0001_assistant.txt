No problem, here are three kid-friendly craft ideas you can make with items around the house:

1. Painted rocks: This craft is all about creativity! The kids can paint whatever they want, or you can decide on a theme and paint the rocks based on that.
2. Paper tube animals: You can use either empty toilet paper or paper towel tubes for this one. The object is to make your tube look like a little animal; you can cut ears on the top and paint little faces, legs, etc. to complete the look.
3. Macaroni jewelry: Make your own fashion line with some pasta noodles and string! Make sure you use noodles that can have string threaded through them and paint the noodles to fit your style!
