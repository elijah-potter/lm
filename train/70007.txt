Origin of the name Marsha:
Marsha is a spelling variation of the original Marcia. Marcia is the feminine form of Marcius, popularized in the Catholic church as it was the name for several saints. Marcius was a Roman surname derived from Marcus.
Symbolism of the name Marsha:
The baby name Marcia dates back to the ancient Roman name Marcius. Marcius also means “warlike.” It was the name for one of the first kings of Rome, receiving his name from the alternative, Marcus. Marcus is an alternative name for Mars, who is the god of war.
