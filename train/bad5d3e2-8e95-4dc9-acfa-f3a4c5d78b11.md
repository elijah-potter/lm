A hollow vow carved into the darkest hour—
promises whispered when the world was too loud,
when fear was the only language understood.
*"I’ll be there,"* the voice said, smooth as stolen dawn,
but dawn never came, and the night swallowed the words whole.

The weight of the unkept didn’t fade,
it pressed like a debt, a wound that never stitched.
Each half-truth a door left ajar,
each silence a promise of something unspoken.
The clock ran out, and the hands of time
didn’t pause—just turned the page,
leaving only dust and the echo of what might’ve been.

The heart, that fragile thing, learned to hold still.
Not from strength, but from the quiet surrender
of something that couldn’t be saved.
The world kept moving, indifferent.
And the promise? It stayed buried in the cracks of the walls,
where no one could see, but where the light still found its way.
