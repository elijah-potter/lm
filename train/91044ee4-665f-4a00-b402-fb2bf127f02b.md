**The Hollow Room**

The walls were lined with the faint, unyielding glow of forgotten bulbs, their light filtering through cracks like dusted moonlight. A single chair sat unoccupied, its worn leather cracked at the corners. The air hummed with the static of a radio that had long since given up its voice.

She stepped inside, the door clicking shut behind her with a sound like a promise. The silence was thick, pressing against her like a memory.

A figure stood at the far end of the room—tall, cloaked in a coat that swallowed the light. They turned slowly, and the moment their gaze met hers, something in the air shifted. The static on the radio hissed, the glow of the bulbs dimmed.

They moved toward her, and when their fingers brushed hers, the chair beneath her legs creaked—not from her weight, but from something else, something ancient.

The door opened again.
