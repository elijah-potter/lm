Music venues often require some form of credentials to be allowed inside the doors with a professional detachable lens camera. Contacting the venue or bands playing through email and social media channels can help you receive what is called a media pass, a badge, wristband, or sticker letting the local staff know you are supposed to be there taking photos.

When sending emails to venue staff or artists, it will be helpful to provide a portfolio of relevant content you have created. However, this can prove difficult if you cannot get into a music venue to create that content.

To work around this, you can start photographing at smaller venues hosting live music including coffee shops or bars that might have less strict photo policies. From there, you can build samples of your work to show the larger professional venues and increase the likelihood of being allowed inside.

It is also worth noting that sometimes larger music venues have photo policies only to restrict the use of detachable lens cameras. In this case, you can still take photos with your mobile phone to begin creating your portfolio and enter the world of live music photography.
