A friend is there when you need them the most,
To listen, to guide, to offer a post,
When life becomes rocky and hard to bear,
A true friend is there, with you to share.

Their loyalty and trustworthiness are so true,
A deep bond, and forever you two,
Together you laugh, and together you cry,
A true friend will never let you die.

They know you're good and hide bad,
They accept you with open arms, for everything you have,
They transcend boundaries of religion and race, 
A true friend whatever the case.

So, cherish your friendship and hold it tightly,
True friends are like stars, that shine so brightly,
They'll be at your side to lend you their hand,
A true friend will always understand.
