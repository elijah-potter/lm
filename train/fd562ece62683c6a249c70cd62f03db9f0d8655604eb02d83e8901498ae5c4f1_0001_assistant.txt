Russia and the US lay claim to Siberian Huskies, but they were developed by Asia's Chukchi tribe for sled-pulling thousands of years ago. Chukchis' dogs were fast runners in Alaskan gold rush sled dog races, and Siberian Huskies became popular racing sled dogs when they delivered medication over 300 miles in a blizzard. People now recognize the breed from this story and the movie "Balto". Siberian Huskies are pets nowadays but still pull sleds.
