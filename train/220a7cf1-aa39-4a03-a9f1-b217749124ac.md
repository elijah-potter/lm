A silent pact between unseen hands—
Paper torn between two trembling fingers,
Yet neither writes the words they vow.
A ledger kept in shadows, where debts are
Not spoken, but etched in ink of unspoken trust.
Names on lists, crossed out by unseen quill,
As if erased by the breath of those who
Never utter the debts they bear.
A clock ticks where the terms are not read aloud,
A contract folded in half, left to rust in a drawer
Where no one ever turns to see it.
The oath is whispered in the wind,
The promise in the hollowed-out
Silence of those who know better than to speak.
