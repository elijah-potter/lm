A sudden hesitation in a crowded café—her fingers hovered over the keyboard, half-written thoughts dissolving into the air. The coffee steamed beside her, a silent witness to the shift in focus. She had meant to draft that essay by midnight, but now the words felt hollow, the deadline a distant blur.

The phone buzzed—a reminder: *"Team meeting at 5 PM."* Her stomach twisted. She’d planned to stay late, but the urgency of the moment—her hesitation—had dissolved into a quiet, unspoken decision. The screen glowed, casting a warm light over her reflection in the glass. No more.

She closed the laptop. The change had come without a fight, like the wind shifting direction. A new path awaited, unplanned but inevitable. The café hummed around her, indifferent. The night was still.
