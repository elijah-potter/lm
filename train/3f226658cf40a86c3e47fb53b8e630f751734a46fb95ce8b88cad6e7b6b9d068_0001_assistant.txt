The A-Team is made up of four Vietnam vets who were framed for a crime they didn't commit, They travel around the globe helping the innocent while on the run from the military.

* Templeton "Faceman" Peck (Dirk Benedict)
*  B.A. Baracus (Mr. T)
* H.M. "Howling Mad" Murdock (Dwight Schultz)
* John "Hannibal" Smith (George Peppard)
