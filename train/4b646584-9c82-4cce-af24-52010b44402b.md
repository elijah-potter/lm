A half-empty cup of tea sits on the nightstand, steam curling into the dark. The bedside lamp flickers, casting long shadows across the worn wooden floor. On the pillow beside her, a crumpled note—handwritten in fading ink—reads: *"I’ll bring you back the scarf you lost."* The memory of her father’s voice, rough with emotion, lingers: *"I promise."* Now, in this quiet hour, the scent of lavender and old paper clings to the air like a ghost of what was once promised. The clock ticks, counting down the seconds since the last time she heard it.
