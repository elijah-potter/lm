Give kids a meaning-focused outlet to explore Chinese characters with Harry Houzi! This innovative Chinese character writing workbook by Cynthia Cramsie, an experienced CI-based elementary level teacher of Mandarin, focuses on whole-brain mastery of Chinese characters. Kids tap into their own creativity as they come up with ways to remember characters, discover related words, and learn to write Chinese.
The full-color workbook pages include simplified and traditional variants and different fonts, practice space, easy-to-follow stroke order diagrams, and a place for students to reflect in English on how they will remember the character. A full-page big character gives kids a place to decorate each one and turn it into a memorable image. Perforated pages remove cleanly for display or collection.
A great supplement to any popular textbook or on its own as a fun way to explore the language. Contains both Simplified and Traditional variants for over 100 common characters of interest to early elementary age students, with color coding, tonal spelling, practice space and story writing areas.
