**Quantum entanglement breakthrough reveals macroscopic non-locality in superconducting qubits:**
Experimental validation of Bell’s theorem in a 100-micron-scale Josephson junction array demonstrates spooky action at macroscopic scales, where entangled superconducting states exhibit instantaneous phase correlations over 100 nm separation. This challenges classical causality models, enabling real-time error correction via quantum teleportation protocols. Implications for topological quantum computing and ultra-secure communications via entanglement swapping are immediate.

**Key findings:**
- **Non-locality threshold:** First observation of macroscopic entanglement beyond single-photon experiments.
- **Error suppression:** Quantum gates with 99.9% fidelity achieved via entanglement purification.
- **Protocol:** "Superconducting Bell test" now standard for benchmarking quantum networks.

**Impact:** Reconfigures error mitigation strategies in large-scale quantum processors, unlocks hybrid quantum-classical cryptography, and validates Feynman’s intuition that "no physical system should be used to simulate another."
