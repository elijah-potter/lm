Cardi the Cardinal was a super bird momma, social in all the cliques.
She took care of her babies, and they grew to be healthy cardinal chicks.

Cardi's life was busy, providing with blood, sweat and tears.
She could take on any challenge, and face any fears.

Her 9 little chicks were cared for and deeply loved,
They always knew that she was watching right above.

She ran into a window and died, breaking her neck and leg,
It was a good thing she invested and left her family with a solid nest egg.
