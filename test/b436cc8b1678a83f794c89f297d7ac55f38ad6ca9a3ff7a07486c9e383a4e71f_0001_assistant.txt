Hello there! Here is a set of instructions in a convenient numbered list on how to climb stairs with five steps.

1. First, take your right foot and raise it high (but not too high), just enough to place your foot on top of the first step. When ready, place your right foot down flat directly onto the first step. Then, use the leverage from the foot on the step to raise your body, and place your left foot on the first step. If the stairs come with a handrail, you are recommended to grab onto that as well, following along with the movement of your feet.
2. Here comes step two! You will take what you've learned with the first step, and apply it here as well; raising your right foot, placing it firmly and flat onto the second step, and using the leverage gained from your right foot to raise your body and place your left foot onto the second step as well. If using a handrail, make sure you're shifting along to the second step when you make the transition.
3. Step three, and stay calm! You're moving a good distance up, but aren't there quite yet. Just as before, you take your right foot, place it confidently upon that third step, and maneuver yourself to the third step until your left foot is right next to your right one. The handrail should keep you steady as you move along but do not fret if one is not available. 
4. The fourth step, we're nearly there! By now, you're quite the veteran of climbing steps, but don't get overconfident! Take it slow, following the process you've learned, with one foot after the other. Right foot first, leverage, and left foot to match. Keep on that handrail, if applicable!
5. Finally, the final step. Keep calm, take a deep breath, and treat it like every other step before. As always, raise that right foot, place it with conviction on the step, and then use it to raise your body and left foot until you're on the final step. The handrail is a great tool in helping to keep your balance this far up, but do not worry if one is not available.

And there you have it, instructions on how to climb stairs with five steps!
