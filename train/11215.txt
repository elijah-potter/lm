Rochester is a town in Massachusetts. It was formed in 1686 and was previously called Scippicam. It can be found in Plymouth county. For more information, contact the county at 1 Constitution Way, Rochester 02770. On the attached map, Rochester is shown as 24 in Plymouth county.
