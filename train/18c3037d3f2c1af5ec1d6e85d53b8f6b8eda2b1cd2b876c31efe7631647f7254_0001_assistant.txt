As Marvin, Leroy and Matthew were driving down the street, neither of them could get the thought of food out of their minds. McDonald’s was only a mile down the road and all of them could already imagine what they were going to get. “I’m going to get the 10-piece nugget meal with a Coke and some honey mustard!” Marvin said with an excited expression. “Well me personally, I’m gonna get me a cheeseburger with an apple pie and a large smoothie,” Matthew responded. Both of them stating what they wanted left Leroy confused as he never planned out what he was going to get and would always just let the menu in the drive-thru “speak to him”. Either way, all three of their mouths were watering as they got closer and closer to their intended destination. As they pulled up into the drive-thru, they almost jumped out of their seats trying to convey their orders to the lady at the microphone. The lady almost felt inclined to scream at them and tell them to calm down. After it was all said and done and they all got their respective orders, you could tell they were very satisfied.
