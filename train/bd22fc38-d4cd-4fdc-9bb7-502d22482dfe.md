A library where books are stored in the walls of a submerged cathedral, their spines etched with submerged saltwater but still readable—only the titles on the back pages are missing, dissolved by the relentless tide.

A forest where the trees grow in perfect, unbroken rows, their trunks lined with rusted chains, but the branches have no leaves, only empty, skeletal arms swaying in a wind that never blows.

A city built on the back of a sleeping giant, its skyline visible only in dreams, where the streets are paved with cracked glass that reflects nothing but the hollow eyes of statues made of bone.

A desert where the sand is not sand but the frozen breath of a thousand dying stars, and the dunes shift in patterns no human has ever mapped.
