In a neon-lit alleyway, a lone figure—**Kai**, a thief with a silver tongue—slipped past the flickering security lights. His stolen briefcase glinted against the cold pavement, filled with the last of the city’s stolen dreams. The crowd at the corner bar had gone silent when he passed, their laughter swallowed by the hum of the neon.

A hand clamped onto his shoulder. **"You’re late,"** the woman beside him murmured, her voice a whisper of static. **"The clock’s ticking."** Kai spun, blade unsheathed, but the woman was already gone—her reflection in the puddle behind her. He followed the echo of her footsteps into a narrow stairwell, the air thick with the scent of ozone and old money.

The door at the top creaked open. Inside, a single bulb burned at the center of a circular table, casting long shadows. At its core sat **The Hourglass**, its grains shifting at a pace faster than any human could count. A man in a tattered lab coat—**Dr. Veyra**—stood behind it, her fingers tracing the edges of a device humming with stolen energy.

*"You have sixty seconds,"* she said, her voice a dry leaf. *"Before the city’s dreams bleed into the void."*

Kai’s fingers trembled as he plucked a single grain from the glass. It was **his memory**—the taste of stolen gold, the weight of a lie, the moment he realized he’d never belong. He pressed it into the device’s core.

A scream tore through the alley. The woman from before—now a figure in the crowd—ripped the device from Kai’s grasp. The Hourglass erupted in a storm of light. Kai lunged, but the air itself resisted him. He grabbed her wrist, and for a single, perfect moment, the world held its breath.

Then—**silence**. The city’s dreams dissolved into static. The woman’s reflection vanished. The clock at the bar’s door stopped. Kai’s hand fell to his side, empty.

The last thing he saw was Dr. Veyra’s smirk as the device’s glow faded into the shadows.

Outside, the alleyway was silent. But somewhere, in the static between dreams, a single grain of sand remained—**his**.
