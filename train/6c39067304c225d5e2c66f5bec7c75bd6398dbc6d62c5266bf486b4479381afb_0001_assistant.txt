This is a list of fun games to play for adult game night:
1. Heads Up! - You can download the app to your phone or use the board game version. You’ll hold up a card to your forehead with a word on it that you can’t see. Your other team members will give clues so you can guess the word before the time is up. This is sure to be fun for all.
2. Pictionary - A classic board game with two teams. One member of the team takes a card and draws a picture of the word that’s on the card. Other team members have one minute to guess the word from the drawing. The drawings and the guesses can provide a lot of laughs.
3. Jackbox Games - Jackbox Games is a gaming platform with many games. You can download it from your streaming device to your TV, which will be the main screen. Then, players will sign in to a website on their phones, which will be their controllers. There are many trivia games, murder mysteries, and more that provide plenty of entertainment.
4. Charades - Each person takes a turn acting out a certain word, and the first person who guesses the correct word gets a point. There are cards online that can be printed out, or you can download the game from Google Play or the Apple Store. You’ll be laughing while your friend tries to act out the word, which could be anything from the name of a Hollywood actor to an activity.
