A fork in the path hummed with unseen voices—some whispered caution, others called urgency. The lantern flickered, casting jagged shadows of choices etched into the wind: the crumbling bridge to the left, its timbers groaning, or the cliffside trail ahead, where the mist clung to the edges like a promise of what might be lost. The breath caught. Was this not a test? Or just the cost of living in a world that demanded more than mere survival? The heart raced, but the mind refused to settle, as if the answer were written in the cracks of the cobblestones beneath the feet.
