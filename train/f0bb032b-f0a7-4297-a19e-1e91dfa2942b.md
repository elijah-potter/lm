Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny rabbit named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown fur and big, sparkly eyes. Every morning, Benny would hop outside to see what magical things the garden had to offer.

One sunny day, Benny found a shiny red apple on the path. "Mmm, what a tasty surprise!" he thought. He took a big bite, but suddenly, the apple turned into a tiny golden star. Benny gasped, "Wow! It’s glowing!"

Just then, Benny spotted a puddle. When he jumped in, he popped out covered in rainbow sparkles. "I feel like a fairy!" Benny giggled. He splashed water everywhere, making a splashy mess.

A sleepy old owl named Oliver was perched on a fence. "Hello, Benny!" Oliver said. "You look like you’ve been dancing!" Benny waved. "I just wanted to play!"

Oliver gave Benny a tiny seed. "For growing big and strong," he said. Benny tucked it into his pocket. "Thank you!" he cheered.

That night, as Benny snuggled in his blanket, he dreamed of more adventures. The next morning, he found the seed had sprouted a tiny green plant. "I’m going to grow a garden!" Benny said happily. And with every hop, every splash, and every sparkle, Benny knew he was making the best day ever.
