Join us as we work as foresters to explore nature and investigate a beaver dam! In this activity, students will learn what a forester does and will see for themselves what lives in a forest by taking a nature walk in the woods. Purchase includes an introductory video, lesson plan, nature walk worksheet, and extension ideas. Perfect for your preschool nature theme, as a nature walk supplement, or a fun activity to try at home!
