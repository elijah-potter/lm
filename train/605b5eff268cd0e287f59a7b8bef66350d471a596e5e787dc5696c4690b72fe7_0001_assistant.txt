Question: Who celebrates their eleventy-first birthday and leaves the Shire suddenly, passing the Ring to Frodo Baggins?
Answer: Bilbo Baggins.

Question: Who suspects the Ring is a Ring of Power?
Answer: The wizard Gandalf.

Question: How many years later does Gandalf tell Frodo that he has confirmed that the Ring is the one lost by the Dark Lord Sauron long ago?
Answer: Seventeen years.

Question: What do the hobbits do when they reach the house at Crickhollow?
Answer: Merry and Pippin reveal they know about the Ring and insist on traveling with Frodo and Sam.

Question: What does Tom Bombadil do for the hobbits after rescuing them?
Answer: He frees them, and equips them with ancient swords from the barrow-wight's hoard.

Question: Who do the hobbits encounter in Bree?
Answer: A Ranger named Strider.

Question: Where do the hobbits stop on their way to Rivendell?
Answer: At Weathertop.

Question: Who do the hobbits encounter again while they are at Weathertop?
Answer: Five of the nine Black Riders.

Question: Who nearly capture Frodo at the Ford of Bruinen?
Answer: The Black Riders.

Question: Who summons flood waters that overwhelm the Black Riders at the Ford of Bruinen?
Answer: Elrond.
