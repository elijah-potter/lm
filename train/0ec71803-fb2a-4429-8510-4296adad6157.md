A sudden gust of wind sent a half-empty coffee cup slamming against the polished oak tabletop, sending a spray of bitter liquid across the worn leather of the worn-out armchair. The elderly librarian, clutching a stack of yellowed books, froze mid-step, her fingers twitching toward the nearest branch of the antique bookshelf—only to pause mid-reach, her expression betraying a mix of shock and disbelief.

The stranger’s shadow loomed over her, unannounced, their presence too sudden to process. Their voice, sharp and unexpected, cut through the quiet: *"You’re late."* The librarian’s eyes widened, then darted to the rear exit, where a familiar face—one she’d seen daily for decades—stood half-hidden in the doorway. The stranger’s smirk faded as recognition dawned, but their grip on the coffee cup tightened, betraying their own sudden, jarring realization.
