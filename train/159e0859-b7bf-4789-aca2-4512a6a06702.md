The sharp crack of a glass shattering echoed through the dimly lit kitchen as Sarah spun around, her face pale. "You *knew* this would happen!" she yelled, gripping her coffee cup so hard the saucer cracked. The floorboards groaned under the weight of her fury.

"Calm down!" Mark’s voice cracked, but his hands trembled as he wiped the spilled coffee from the counter. "We didn’t *know*—we just—" He trailed off, staring at his own ruined shirt, where the stain of coffee had already set.

Outside, a car horn blared, startling them both. Sarah snatched up the shards of glass with trembling fingers. "You’re lucky I’m not throwing these at your head next." Her voice was a blade. "You always say *we* can fix this, but you’ve never *seen* what it looks like when it’s *done*."

Mark exhaled sharply, rubbing his temples. "I didn’t mean to—" He gestured helplessly toward the kitchen, where the remnants of their failed breakfast littered the floor. "I just… I didn’t think it’d come to this."

Sarah’s grip tightened. "You *didn’t* think?" Her voice dropped to a whisper. "You *always* think. But this time? This time, I’m the one who has to *act*." She shoved the glass into a bag, her knuckles white. "We’re not doing this again. Not *like* this."
