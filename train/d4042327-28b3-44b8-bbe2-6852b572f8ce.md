*"I don’t know what you’re talking about."*

The rain drummed against the window, soft and relentless, as she sat on the old couch, her fingers tracing the cracks in the wood. Outside, the city pulsed like a heartbeat, but she was already drowning in its noise.

"Again?" she muttered, turning to the mirror.

The reflection blinked back, identical—same hollow eyes, same trembling lips. The words echoed in her skull, sticky as the humidity in the room.

*"I don’t know what you’re talking about."*

Her phone buzzed. Another notification: *"You missed this."* The text was from her father, but she didn’t read it. She only needed to hear it again, from her own mouth, to believe the world wasn’t spinning.
