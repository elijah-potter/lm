A decision was left to linger, the weight of options deferred,
the clock ticking softly, the mind a quiet, restless sea.
Procrastination became a companion, not by choice but fate,
each passing hour a whisper, *"Wait—what if I change my mind?"*

The future stretched thin—promises of clarity, of resolve,
but hesitation was a shadow, a thief of time and resolve.
Somewhere between hesitation and action, the world moved on,
while the decision waited, half-formed, half-remembered, half-lost.
