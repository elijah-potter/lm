The sharp crack of a glass shattering filled the dimly lit kitchen as the spat erupted. "You *always* mess up!" Clara snapped, her voice rising as she tossed the dish she’d been cleaning at her brother’s head.

Tom ducked just in time, the shards missing him by inches. "Watch where you’re *throwing*, sis! I’ve been trying to tell you for weeks!" he yelled back, his hands balled into fists.

Across the counter, their mother sighed, rubbing her temples. "Enough! You two have been at this since breakfast." The tension was thick enough to cut, but neither was ready to back down. Clara crossed her arms, her cheeks flushed. "Fine, but this isn’t over—you’re *never* letting me work without supervision again!"

Tom smirked, grinning like a cornered animal. "Yeah, yeah, I’ll *never* let you forget it." The argument dissolved into laughter, but the hurt in Clara’s eyes lingered.
