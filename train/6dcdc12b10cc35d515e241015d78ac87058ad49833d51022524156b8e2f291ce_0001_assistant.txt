There are lots of options for pink flowers you can get for your girlfriend. Here are five of the most popular:

1. Pink Roses: This classic flower smells good and will look incredible in a vase. The pink rose symbolizes admiration and appreciation.
2. Pink Carnations: These florals are beautiful and have more petals than roses. They symbolize gratitude and convey the message "I will never forget you."
3. Pink Azaleas: This flower is one of the brightest symbols of femininity and positivity. They are white and pink and lovely to see.
4. Pink Begonias: Symbolizing affection and attraction, Begonias are delicate. They're also edible for a fun twist.
5. Pink Gerbera Daisies: A classic daisy, but bright pink and gorgeous for a change of pace. These flowers symbolize gentility and grace.
