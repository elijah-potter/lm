I'm lying here
right by the door
sad as can be,
my nose on the floor.

My owner is gone
he won't come back
the room is dark 
the windows all black.

I'm about to give up
all lonely and sad
a cold tired pup
in need of his dad.

Then, woah! What do I see?
Lights in the driveway-
oh, could it be?
He's home, he's home!

I jump on his legs
happy as can be
I'll never leave his side
Just wait and see!
