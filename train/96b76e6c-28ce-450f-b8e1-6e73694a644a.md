Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a curious bunny named Benny. Benny loved to explore his garden every day. One morning, Benny hopped outside and saw a shiny blue flower that sparkled in the sunlight. He thought, "I wonder what this magical flower is?"

Benny carefully picked it and placed it in his basket. As he walked home, he heard a tiny voice, "Help me! I'm lost!" Benny looked down and saw a little blue bird sitting on a fence post. Benny smiled and said, "Don’t worry, I’ll help you!"

Benny gathered his friends—Lily the ladybug, Max the mouse, and Tilly the turtle—and they all worked together. They followed the blue flower’s magical glow to a hidden cave. Inside, they found a tiny, scared baby bunny who had gotten lost. Benny and his friends cheered, "We found you!"

The baby bunny thanked them and told them, "I want to show you something special." They followed her to a sparkling pond where the water shimmered like diamonds. "Look how happy we all feel now!" the baby bunny said, hugging them all.

From that day on, Benny and his friends visited the baby bunny often. They shared stories, played games, and learned that kindness and teamwork make the world brighter. Benny knew that even the smallest helpers can make the biggest difference. And so, the four friends became the garden’s happiest team, always ready to help and cheer for their friends.
