You are not going to believe where I just went! Are you ready? The Wizarding World of Harry Potter at Universal in Orlando, Florida! OMG! I had so much fun on the rides and looking at the attractions. Even the food was great. If you're a Harry Potter fan (and why wouldn't you be?), you have to make the trip to Universal Studios. But you also need to get a pass to Islands of Adventure if you want the full experience. The Hogwarts Express train goes between the two parks, so you can only ride it if you have a Park-to-Park pass. I'm telling you, it's totally worth it.

Besides the train, which is probably the best part, I really liked Gringotts. It's set up just like in the movie, with Goblins in the lobby counting knuts and sickles. They seriously look just like the ones in the movies. You have to get in line to ride the Escape from Gringotts ride to see inside the bank where the Goblins are. The ride is fun and even if you have to stand in line for a long time, there's plenty to look at.
