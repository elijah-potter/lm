A "peanut gallery" was the name for the cheapest seats in the theater in the days of Vaudeville.  Patrons who sat in these seats would eat peanuts since they were cheap and would also throw them at the performers when they disliked the performance. That is where the phrase, "No comments from the peanut gallery" comes from.  Additionally, the popular kids' show "Howdy Doody" referred to the live audience of children as the "Peanut Gallery" in the 1940s radio broadcast and later on television.
