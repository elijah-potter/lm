**CITY OF ELDERVORNE: MAJOR DISCOVERY UNDER THE CITY’S OLD WALLS**
Residents of Eldervorne woke to reports of strange lights and the unmistakable scent of ozone in the early morning hours of the 12th of Solstice. Officials confirmed a significant archaeological find beneath the ancient stone wall that once marked the city’s northern border. Excavations revealed a half-buried structure resembling a primitive ceremonial chamber, adorned with carvings of what appeared to be celestial motifs. Local historians speculate this could be a remnant of the city’s pre-federation era, hinting at lost knowledge about the region’s astronomical observatories.

Eldervorne Mayor Elara Veyan issued a temporary curfew for the area near the wall, advising residents to avoid the site to prevent disturbance. Authorities also reported a surge in inquiries from scholars and tourists seeking to study the findings. Meanwhile, a team of geologists is analyzing soil samples for signs of ancient industrial activity, though no direct connection to modern infrastructure has been confirmed.

The city’s cultural committee has proposed turning the discovery into a tourist attraction, but debates continue over whether to preserve the site as-is or restore it to its supposed original form. Eldervorne’s mayor has not yet commented on plans for long-term preservation.
