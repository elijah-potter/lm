Eleanor’s suggested usage—nearly every weekend throughout the summer—would essentially mean she’s living there rent-free, while I’m still covering all the expenses. I’ve calculated that, at her proposed usage, she’s effectively saving over $2,000 a month in rent and utilities. I explained this to her, showing her the detailed breakdown of costs. She brushed it off, saying I was being “calculating” and that Vivian would have wanted her to have the house during a difficult time.
