Happy to do so. Here are some items that such a fictional space insurance adjuster might carry.

1. Datapad: Nothing quite suggests a modern insurance adjuster quite like the humble clipboard. Thus it would not be unreasonable to imagine that the space future equivalent would be a datapad. This would do more than simply clip documents and provide a writing surface, however. It would also be able to store and display multimedia information.
2. Multi-spectrum goggles: These goggles would allow the adjuster to view and analyze anything from malfunctioning equipment to the scene of a spaceport accident. It would also provide a variety of vision modes, including visible light, infrared light, and ultraviolet.
3. Sampling equipment: As part of their job, the adjuster may need to sample evidence to support their observations and investigations. A suitcase-sized sampling kit would serve well for both acquiring all manner of material evidence and storing them securely.
4. Quantum Communicator: A space insurance adjuster would likely have to travel to remote parts of the galaxy as part of their job. A quantum communicator would enable the adjuster to keep in close contact with claimants, the space police, and the head office.
5. Company hardhat: Falling objects remain a danger in the far future as well. In addition, the company logo helps identify the claims adjuster to those around them, helping to explain their questions and why they are there. 
6. Pocket protector: As part of the company uniform, the space insurance adjuster may have to dress professionally in office attire. A pocket protector would allow them to store their pens and other writing implements without dirtying their shirt with grease, reactor coolant, and alien goop.
