County at a Glance - Summers
History1,2: Formed in 1871 from parts of Monroe, Mercer,
Greenbrier, and Fayette counties and named in honor of George W. Summers, a
prominent jurist of Kanawha County.
Area (square miles)1: 367.76
County Seat1: Hinton
Population (1990)1: 14,204
Leading Industries and Chief Agricultural Products1: lumber,
livestock, hay and grains
