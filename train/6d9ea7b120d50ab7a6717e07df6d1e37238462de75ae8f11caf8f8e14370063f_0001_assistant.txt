The tiger crept through the tall grass. Her lithe, powerful muscles tensed as she spotted her prey, a white-tailed deer, grazing in the nearby clearing. Her eyes fixated on the target as she instinctively moved upwind. The soft pads on her paws made no noise as she carefully chose each step, inching ever closer to her next meal. The deer was oblivious, but the tiger knew that her prey could dart away all too quickly if she made the wrong move. She tensed as the deer whipped its head up, ears pointed in her direction. A tense minute of listening, and the deer tentatively returned to its meal.

The tiger reached the edge of her cover a few yards away from her target. There would be no escaping now. She lowered herself to her haunches, her powerful hind legs ready to spring her into action. The hunt was on.
