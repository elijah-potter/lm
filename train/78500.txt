Flash cards go digital with this mathematics helper from School Zone Publishing. Flash Action Addition/Subtraction helps kids around 6 years old drill on addition and subtraction problems using the numbers 1 through 12. With the program's colorful graphics and simple interface, most kids shouldn't have any trouble starting a drill or playing against a friend. They can even choose to focus on a particular number to help them conquer an especially difficult area. The game is enjoyable for a short while, but there's not much to it other than the drills, and we doubt that kids will want too many replays.
