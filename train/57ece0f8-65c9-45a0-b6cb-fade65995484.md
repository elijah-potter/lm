Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had soft, fluffy ears that flapped in the wind, and a tail that wiggled like a little rainbow.

Every morning, Benny would hop outside to say hello to his best friends: a sleepy old turtle named Tom, a chatty squirrel named Sam, and a gentle fox named Finn. "Good morning, friends!" Benny would say with a big smile.

Tom would slow blink his big eyes. "Good morning, Benny! The sun is shining today."

Sam would chatter, "Did you see the acorns? I found one that sparkles like a star!"

Finn would wag his tail gently. "The meadow smells so sweet today. Want to play a game?"

They would all run and jump, collecting shiny leaves, chasing butterflies, and giggling when the wind blew their hair. Benny loved every moment, especially when he shared his favorite berries with his friends.

One sunny afternoon, Benny noticed something strange. His garden was covered in a thick blanket of white dust. "What happened?" Benny asked, looking worried.

Tom grumbled, "I think it’s a big snowstorm coming!"

Sam’s fur stood on end. "But it’s not snowing yet!"

Finn’s eyes widened. "Maybe we should stay inside!"

Benny hugged his friends. "Don’t worry, we’ll stay warm and wait together." So they all huddled under the big oak tree, telling stories and laughing until their bellies ached.

After a while, the sky turned a soft gray, and then—*splash!*—tiny white flakes began to fall. Benny gasped. "Look! It’s snowing!"

They all giggled as the flakes swirled around them. Benny’s nose got runny, and he sneezed, sending fluffy snowflakes flying everywhere. Tom giggled too, and Sam collected snow in his paws to make a tiny snowman.

Finn snuggled close. "This is the coziest snowstorm ever!"

Soon, the garden was covered in a soft white blanket, and Benny’s friends had made a beautiful snow castle. Benny played until his paws were cold, then he snuggled under his favorite fluffy blanket inside the house.

When the snow melted the next morning, Benny found something magical—a tiny, shiny blue pebble—right where he had played. "Oh, look!" he said, holding it up. His friends cheered.

That day, Benny knew that even when the world felt different, like a big snowstorm, being with his friends made everything special. And from that day on, they always had adventures—big or small—together.
