We are all gardening!!!
On Monday 7th October, 8 children were invited down to St Clements Court to meet the elderly residents and to learn all about bees!
A lady named Margaret, talked to us about hives, making honey, wax and the Queen Bee! We listened very well and shared our knowledge of bees and plants. Margaret was very impressed with the research we had completed :)
Margaret showed us the honey that the bees had made in her garden. Look at all the different colours; this is because of the different plants. We also were shown a wax model of a Queen Bee! Don’t worry it’s not this size in real life!
After our talk, we went outside to look at bee-friendly plants and to plant our own! We planted tulips, snowdrops and daffodils. We had to remember to plant the bulbs the right way up with the roots going down into the ground. Then we covered them up with soil using our gardening tools, it was great fun!
