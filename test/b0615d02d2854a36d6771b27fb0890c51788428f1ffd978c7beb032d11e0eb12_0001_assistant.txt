There once was a golfer named Chad
Who swing was impressibly sad.
Though he huffed with great flair,
The ball barely caught air,
For a few feet, a stroke he would add

When Chad's poor old golf ball did land,
It was not in the place that he planned.
But he would up again,
Shut his eyes, swung, and then,
Opened up to his ball in the sand.

From the bunker, Chad surely did think,
That his chance of par had to sink.
But with one clumsy sprawl,
He still gave it his all,
And his shot plunked right into the drink!

Shot after shot and moreover,
Chad kept taking swings from the clover
But with a flick of the wrist,
Sunk his shot with a kiss,
And he carded Hole One at twelve over!
