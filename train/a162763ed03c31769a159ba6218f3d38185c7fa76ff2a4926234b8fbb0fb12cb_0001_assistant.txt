Many homeowners and business owners are installing Smart Home products. For homeowners and business owners alike, these products offer the ability to place much-needed controls in important areas. They also save you money, if installed and used correctly. So what are the 5 most common Smart Home Products on the market today? Here are some popular items:

•	Nest - This company has a whole line of products like the Nest Learning Thermostat, which controls the temperature in every room. They also make smoke and carbon monoxide detectors, along with indoor and outdoor security cameras.
•	Lutron - This company is well known for its dimmer switches. Their new line is wireless and provides better control over the lighting in any space. 
•	Google & Amazon - They have a whole range of Smart Home Speakers, including a mini-speaker. Due to its popularity, this technology is developing quickly. 
•	Ring - This company produces Smart Doorbells and entry cameras. The internet has been flooded with photos of the Ring catching porch pirates stealing packages. 
•	iRobot - The Roomba began as a novelty product, but has now been accepted as a viable home cleaning device that works well. It has a full suite of sensors that can tell when it has hit a wall, a pet, a chair, a person, etc. It can also tell when it’s about to go over the stairs, preventing possible damage to the device.
