Eating five portions of fruit and vegetables every day can reduce a person's risk of stroke by as much as 26 per cent, experts have claimed.
According to the Stroke Association, even one serving per day can help to reduce the risk of stroke, which affects an estimated 150,000 people every year in the UK.
A spokeswoman for the charity also recommended eating plenty of whole grains and following a generally healthy lifestyle.
'Keeping your weight down is important,' the expert claimed. 'Carrying extra weight around your waist is particularly important, because that might increase the risk of stroke, heart disease and some cancers by up to three times.'
The spokeswoman also advised 'exercising regularly and stopping smoking, watching what you eat and being healthy' as good ways to reduce the risk of stroke.
Over 67,000 people die as a result of stroke every year in the UK, making it the third most common cause of death behind heart disease and cancer.
