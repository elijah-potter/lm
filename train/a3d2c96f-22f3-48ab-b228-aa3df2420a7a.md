Emerging urban cluster along a reclaimed riverfront, where historic warehouses have been repurposed as modular co-working hubs. Solar-paneled canopies over pedestrian pathways provide shade, and biophilic design elements—living walls, drip irrigation systems—integrate greenery into high-density living units. A central "test hub" hosts prototypes of adaptive infrastructure, including floating modules for flood-prone zones and AI-managed microgrids. Community-driven workshops focus on circular economy solutions, with local artisans and engineers collaborating on open-source designs. The area is connected via a network of elevated bike lanes and automated shuttles, reducing congestion. A floating market docks daily, selling hyper-local produce from rooftop farms, while a "grow-your-own" co-op offers vertical hydroponics for residents. The skyline features modular, repairable structures with interchangeable facades for aesthetic renewal.
