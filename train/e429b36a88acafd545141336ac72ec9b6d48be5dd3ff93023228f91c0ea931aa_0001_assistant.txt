To calculate your debt-to-income ratio, I would need to know your gross monthly income, which is the amount of your paycheck before taxes, and the total sum of your monthly expenses, not the total debts you owe. Once these amounts are provided, I will divide your total monthly expenses by your gross monthly income to obtain your debt-to-income ratio. For most lending situations, a lower DTI will help the borrower obtain a loan with a lower interest rate since the bank or lending entity will perceive it as less risky.
