
Sunflowers: 

Sunflower petals feel like the softest silk between my fingertips
I wonder if they would taste buttery with a subtle sweetness on my tongue
Their music in the breeze is all sunshine and good feelings that only nature can hear
Sunflower fragrance is earthy warmth, like the soil after a spring rain shower
I see a field of golden beauty and grace swaying before me in tall waves. 

Roses: 

I see the bouquet of roses sitting on my nightstand and wonder what they’d look like back on the rose bush
I imagine the petals taste like shell-coated sweets, each a different flavor to correspond to different colors
The roses are soft to the touch like velvet warmed in the sunshine 
Their fragrance makes me close my eyes and breathe deep of Mother Nature 
No sound comes from a rose, but I can pretend to hear a sweet symphony interrupted intermittently by the sharp bite of thorns. 

Violets: 

Violets have been painted the purple of majesty, royalty, and creativity
Even so unassuming and small, their scents are soft and powdery, reminiscent of new romance 
Dainty in my fingertips, like silken woven threads from nature 
With violets, I can imagine a taste of spring renewal and hope
Can you hear the tinkling sounds they make, akin to fairies dancing in the sun?
