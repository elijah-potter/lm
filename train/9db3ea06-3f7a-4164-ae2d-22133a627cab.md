In the hollow where the wind sang through rusted wires, a girl with a lantern found the door to the past. The lantern’s flame, a shifting mirror, showed her a city where time had no rules—buildings grew like ivy, shadows stretched to touch the sky. She stepped through, but the world dissolved into whispers.

Years later, in a room lined with books that had no titles, a boy with ink-stained fingers traced the same doorframe in dust. The lantern’s glow was a ghost of her own. Outside, the city slept, but the door still stood, warm and unyielding.

The lantern’s flame flickered again.
