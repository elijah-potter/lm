The City in the Sky, Twilight Princess' last annoying dungeon before an exhausting back-to-back finale against Zant and Ganondorf, is some big climactic end to Link's adventure, I guess. I mean, he's gone across Hyrule, swam the depths of Hylia, climbed the tallest mountains and peaks, adventured through time itself, and now he just goes and fights in the heavens. I would have just given up on step one, honestly.

One of the largest and most tedious dungeons, the City in the Sky is a miserable labyrinth. And of course, Link always has a tool to get around, using his Double Clawshots for vertical movement. I guess the City in the Sky is exciting, it's got puzzles and fights in a "fun" environment. Thankfully, it both feels and is rather final in feeling, as we move into the finale and closer to being done with the game.
