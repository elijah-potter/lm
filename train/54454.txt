While the router itself has the private IP of 192.168.0.1.1, it assigns any devices in its network a different, private IP address.
To avoid any sort of IP address conflict all IP address on a network are same. Remember within that network only! Other common private IP addresses used by modems and routers are used in the modems and the routers are 192.168.1.100 and 192.168.1.101
You can use the Command prompt in case you don’t know your router’s IP address:
- Press Windows-X, this will open the power user menu, and then click Command Prompt.
- Enter ipconfig this will show you all the computer’s connection list of yours.
- After this you will find the “Default Gateway” that will be under “Local Area Connection” section. Now you can see your router’s IP address.

Well, it may happen that you might forget your password or username; well in that case you would need to have your router or your ADSL modem reset. Then after your modem would be restored to the factory settings.
