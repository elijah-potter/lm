**BREAKING:** The ancient ruins of Eldermere, once rumored to be cursed, have been unearthed beneath the abandoned industrial district of Veldros. Archaeologists from the University of Blackthorn confirmed the discovery, which includes a partially intact artifact resembling a "Time-Steward’s Grip"—a device said to manipulate temporal flow. Authorities are investigating reports of localized time distortions, including a 20-minute gap in the morning rush hour last Tuesday. The city’s clock tower, which had been out of sync for years, now functions correctly. Local historians speculate this could be a sign of a forgotten prophecy about the "Awakening of the Forgotten." Officials urge residents to avoid the ruins at night, citing reports of "echoes" heard by some visitors. The city council is reviewing emergency protocols for temporal anomalies.
