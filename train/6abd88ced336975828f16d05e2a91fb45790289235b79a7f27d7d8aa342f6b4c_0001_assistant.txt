It had been a long and grueling few hours, but the meal was finally close to being complete. Rose had promised her boyfriend that she would make him dinner tonight and she planned to deliver in a big way. She was making him curry chicken with white rice, and sweet plantain and had even bought him some red velvet cake to have as dessert on the side. She had been cheffing it up in the kitchen for about 4 hours now. After allowing the chicken to defrost, she had to season, marinate and then finally cook the chicken once all the flavor from the curry marinade settled into the meat. As she looked at all the food cooking over the stove top, she felt a certain sense of pride. She loved her boyfriend very much and wanted to give him a taste of what life would be like when they got married. Him coming home from work after a long hard day and coming home to a wonderful home-cooked meal made by the love of his life. The thought brought an extreme amount of joy to Rose's heart and made all the hours in the kitchen worth it.
