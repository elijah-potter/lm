**NEW YORK CITY – June 20th, 2024**

**"URGENT: ATTENTION ALL CITIZENS"**
The City Council announces the **first-ever "Midnight Harvest Festival"**—a night of free concerts, art installations, and community stalls—starting **12:00 AM (Midnight)** at Central Park’s Lake Sheathed. All ages welcome, but ID verification mandatory for underage attendees. *Proceeds support local food banks and urban gardening initiatives.*
