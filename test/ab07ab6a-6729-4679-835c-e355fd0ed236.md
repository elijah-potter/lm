Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a garden full of flowers, lived a little girl named Mia. Mia had a favorite toy, a big red ball that she loved to roll around outside. One sunny afternoon, while playing, she noticed a tiny, shimmering star stuck to the fence near her garden.

Curious, Mia picked up the star and placed it carefully in her pocket. When she got home, she told her mom, "Mom, I found a magic star!"

Her mom smiled and said, "Well, let's see what magic this star has!"

That night, as Mia lay in bed, she felt a warm glow around her. Suddenly, a tiny voice whispered, "Shine your light!"

Mia opened her eyes and saw a tiny fairy sitting beside her bed. "You found a magic star," the fairy said. "You can help others see the light of kindness and happiness!"

Mia smiled and thought, "I can do that!" She got up and helped her mom bake cookies for her friends. She also made sure all her toys were put away neatly. She even shared her favorite snack with her little brother.

The next morning, Mia saw her friends playing outside. She handed them her red ball and said, "Here, let’s play!" She laughed and ran around with them, making everyone smile.

The fairy sparkled happily and said, "Good job, Mia! You’ve made the world a brighter place!"

From that day forward, Mia loved to help others and share her happiness. She rolled her red ball outside whenever she felt sad, and soon, everyone around her started to shine too.
