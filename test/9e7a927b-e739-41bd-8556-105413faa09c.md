Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a curious bunny named Benny. Benny loved to explore every nook and cranny of his yard.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside and saw a tiny, shimmering flower. It looked so different from the others! He picked it carefully and brought it back to his home.

Benny’s mommy rabbit smiled. "What a beautiful find, Benny! Let’s place it in our garden."

Benny was excited and planted it carefully in the soft soil. Soon, the flower grew taller and its petals turned a soft pink. Benny watched as tiny bees buzzed around it, collecting sweet nectar.

One day, Benny noticed a small mouse named Milo near the flower. Milo had lost his way and was a little scared.

"Don’t worry, Milo!" Benny said kindly. "I’ll help you find your home!"

Benny and Milo worked together. Milo showed Benny where his burrow was, and Benny helped him dig a little path so Milo could find his way easily.

After a while, Benny’s flower bloomed into a beautiful, glowing flower that could light up the garden at night. Benny and Milo became the best of friends, and the garden grew even more magical.

From that day on, whenever Benny saw a tiny, special flower, he knew it was time to be kind and help others, just like his flower grew bright and beautiful.
