In the dim glow of a neon-lit alley, a lone figure crouched behind a rusted dumpster, fingers twitching at the trigger of a stolen AR. The crowd outside—dressed in high heels and designer coats—chanted *"Rules are for losers, baby!"* as a sudden, chaotic energy crackled through the air. The walls of the building trembled as the ceiling dissolved into a swirling vortex of colors, revealing a holographic projection: **"New rules. Now."**

A voice, synthetic yet smooth, echoed: *"The system’s over. The old order’s dead. Stand now or die."* The figure’s grip tightened. The crowd dissolved into a frenzy—some cheered, others screamed—while the alley itself pulsed with an unseen force. A door at the alley’s end *opened* without a key, revealing a shifting, ever-changing landscape where gravity defied physics and time itself bent. The air hummed with the sound of something ancient and hungry, waiting to claim the first to move.

The figure hesitated—then stepped forward. The world around them fractured.
