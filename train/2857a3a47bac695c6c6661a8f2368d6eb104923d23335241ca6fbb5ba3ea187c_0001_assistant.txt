Once there was a boy named Michael, who lived in a house with his mom and two older brothers, Ned and Fred. Ned and Fred loved to ride their bikes around with the other kids in the neighborhood. Michael, however, could never join... he didn't know how to ride a bike! He was too scared of getting hurt. What if he got a scrape? What if he fell off of his bike and rolled down a hill! 
One summer day, however, Michael decided enough was enough. He put on his helmet, then his knee pads, then his elbow pads, then his shin guards, and his gloves. Ned and Fred thought Michael looked funny. But Michael was determined to try. He took Ned's bike and steadied himself before giving a gentle pedal. 
He toppled over immediately. But, to his surprise, he wasn't hurt!
He got up. Fred was laughing just a little bit. 
Michael tried again. This time, he stayed up for a few seconds but then toppled over again.
"Hey," Michael thought, "that was better than last time! And I'm still not hurt!"
On the third time, Michael started to pedal! He made it to the end of the driveway!! He was biking!!
And when Michael fell, he was so excited that he had biked that he didn't even notice the teensy, little scrape on his arm.
