Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white fur and big, round eyes that sparkled like stars.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to play. The grass was green, and the sky was blue with fluffy white clouds dancing in the wind. Benny’s favorite place to play was the garden where he found the biggest, juiciest carrots. Benny munched happily on his favorite snack.

Just then, Benny spotted a tiny, shivering hedgehog named Hedge. "Hedge, it's cold out here!" Benny said kindly. "Come and sit with me by the sun."

Hedge curled up in Benny’s basket, and together they shared a picnic of carrots and berries. Benny told Hedge about his favorite places to play—swimming in the little pond and chasing butterflies. Hedge giggled and whispered, "I love your story!"

As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and orange, Benny and Hedge snuggled under a big, soft blanket. They promised to play together every day. "The world is big, but friends like you make it even better," Benny said.

And with a happy heart, Benny and Hedge went to bed, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures. The stars twinkled above them, and they knew tomorrow would be another wonderful day.
