The abandoned **Midnight Archives**, a crumbling bookstore in an old city district, serves as the primary gathering spot. Its shelves are lined with books that don’t exist in the real world—titles like *The Last Dawn Chronicle* and *Echoes of the Forgotten*—though no one knows what they’re about. Locals and strangers meet here at dusk, some claiming to find hidden messages in the margins of worn-out tomes, others reporting strange whispers from the dust. The air hums with static, and flickering neon signs display cryptic phrases like **"Here, the past and future collide."** No one has a definitive answer for why they keep returning, but the crowd grows every night.
