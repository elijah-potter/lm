The grand hall’s chandelier swayed slightly in the dim light as the old lord held up a silver cup, its polished surface reflecting the flickering torch flames. *"By this gold, the river’s waters shall run clear."* His voice was a whisper of ancient oaths, the promise etched deep in the carved stone above the throne. The crowd murmured, their hands clutching dried herbs and coins, while the distant wind carried the scent of blooming jasmine from the courtyard beyond. The bargain was sealed in shadow and fire.
