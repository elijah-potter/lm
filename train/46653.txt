So what is diabetes?
Diabetes is a chronic condition in which the pancreas does not produce enough insulin. It can also be caused when the body fails to use the insulin it produces. Insulin is the hormone that regulates the body’s blood glucose.
Hyperglycemia is a condition in which blood glucose levels or blood sugar levels rise. This condition is usually an effect of uncontrolled diabetes. This condition can be very dangerous for the body, as it can cause severe damage to many of the body’s systems over time.
It is difficult to stop and control the rise in sugar levels. Most people won’t like the lifestyle changes required. However, if one is keen to stop their diabetes from worsening, it is a good idea to follow these lifestyle changes.
- Lose Extra Weight
Losing weight is one of the most common ways to reduce the risk of having diabetes. A loss of 7% of your body weight can reduce the risk of developing diabetes by nearly 60%.
There’s no better time than now to shed extra belly fat. Prediabetic patients who have lost 7%-10% of their bodyweight could be prevented from developing the disease.
For those who are too lazy to start losing weight, it is a good idea to set a weight-loss goal. This can be based on your current body weight. Of course, you should ask a doctor for good short-term goals and expectations.
- Move more
Moving more is to be more active. Physical activity can help you lose weight and increase your insulin sensitivity.
Aerobic exercises, such as walking, swimming, and biking, are good options. ), resistance exercises (i.e., weightlifting, yoga, calisthenics, etc. ),
Your blood sugar levels can be affected by inactivity. This includes prolonged periods of sitting at the computer or sitting pretty. This can be helpful in controlling blood sugar.
- Eat healthy plant foods
Plants provide healthy nutrients that keep the body healthy and strong. Fiber is one of these nutrients. Dietary fiber, also known as roughage or bulk, is part of plant foods that the body usually has difficulty digesting or absorbing.
However, fiber-rich foods are essential to controlling diabetes as they promote weight loss, slow the absorption of sugar, and lower blood sugar levels. It can also hinder the absorption and metabolism of dietary fats and cholesterol.
If you are looking for a fiber-rich diet, foods such as fruits, vegetables that are not starchy, legumes, whole grains, and beans are all good options.
Avoid foods high in sugar and low in nutrients to ensure a healthy diet. These foods include white bread, pastries, white pasta, fruit juices and processed foods with high-fructose Corn Syrup or sugar.
- Eat Healthy Fats
When discussing diets, fats are usually given a bad rep, which is unfortunate since not all fats are created equal. Some fats are good for you, while others are bad. Healthy blood cholesterol levels can be promoted by eating good and unsaturated fats.
Unsaturated fats can also help in promoting a good and healthy heart. These unsaturated oils include both monounsaturated and polyunsaturated. These fats can be found in many foods.
Nuts and seeds like almonds, peanuts, flaxseeds, and pumpkin seeds, among others, are good sources of healthy fats. This can also be found in fatty fish such as mackerel and salmon, as well as tuna, tuna, sardines and cod. You can also find olive, sunflower and canola oils.
