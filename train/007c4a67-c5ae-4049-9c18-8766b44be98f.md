Philosophical conundrums like *"What is the meaning of life?"* or *"Does consciousness arise from brain activity alone?"* resist direct answers due to their **multi-dimensional dependencies** (e.g., subjective experience vs. objective neuroscience).

For example, the **"hard problem of consciousness"** (David Chalmers) asks: *Why does subjective experience ("qualia") exist at all?* Its answer requires bridging physics, biology, and metaphysics, yet no single discipline can isolate it definitively. Such questions thrive on ambiguity, requiring interdisciplinary synthesis rather than a singular truth.

This ambiguity makes them **useful for philosophical debate** but impractical for automated systems to resolve.
