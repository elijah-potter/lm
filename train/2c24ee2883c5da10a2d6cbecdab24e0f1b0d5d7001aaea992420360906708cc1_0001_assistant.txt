Character: Ridley Exter

Sidekick: 
Lea the parrot, a feisty and magical parrot who loves raisins and mimics Ridley’s dad.

Quirks:
Ridley loves exotic magical birds and is making a book about the different breeds.
She is also secretly learning to shapeshift. 

Backstory:
Ridley lives in London and sees an injured raven on the sidewalk. It talks to her, but nobody else can hear it. She discovers that she can speak to birds, and makes friends with Lea at the pet shop. Lea teaches her about shape-shifting. They encounter more magical creatures, and while Ridley’s dad doesn’t believe her initially, he does help her document them.
