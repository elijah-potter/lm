I said I’d never love again
But then my Percy sauntered in.
Oh, he caught me by surprise
With his lovely emerald eyes.

Soft white fur and belly flab,
Sharpened claws so he can stab.
Likes to eat but only wet --
Give him dry, you won’t forget!

Mostly good at burying poo.
Affectionate when he wants to!
Won’t play with his toy mouse.
Knocks stuff over in the house.

And when I go to sleep at night,
Then it’s time for him to bite.
He wants to chase, he wants to play --
Didn’t want to all darned day!

But sometimes he’ll get in my lap,
Start to purr and take a nap.
Overall the cat’s okay --
I suppose that he can stay.
