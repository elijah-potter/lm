Jake and Sam were excited to be going to the zoo. They had waited all year and now that they were finally out of school, it was time for their visit. Jake was scared of all of the bigger animals they would see but Sam was fearless. As soon as they got to the zoo, Sam ran towards the elephants. He immediately got in line for the elephant rides. This left Jake all alone because he was too scared to even be near the elephant. Jake wandered around for a few minutes until he found a bench and sat down. He sighed to himself for a moment until he looked up and saw in front of him an enclosure full of turtles. He loved turtles so he approached them quickly and began to observe the group. Jake was so excited about the turtles that he forgot Sam left him alone. Soon, Sam ran out of nowhere and said "Where did you go, Jake?" Jake shrugged his shoulders and told Sam about his fear of elephants. Sam put his arm around Jake's shoulder and said "Well, forget the elephants then, we'll go see the giraffes."
