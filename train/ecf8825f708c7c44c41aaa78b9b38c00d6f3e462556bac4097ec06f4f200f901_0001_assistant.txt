1. Sort your items.

• Embrace temporary mess: Expect things to get messy before they improve. Push through the initial disarray to achieve a clean home.
• Group similar items: Sort your belongings into groups to see what you have and what you can get rid of.
• Discard and donate generously: Let go of things you don't need to maintain a clutter-free space. Donate items to those in need.
• Label storage: Use labels to easily find things in boxes, containers, and folders.
• Assign a home for everything: Give each item a specific place to save time searching for them.

2. Store your items.

• Get suitable organizers: Invest in shelves, pantry, and drawer organizers that fit your space.
• Use labels: Categorize and organize items with labels for quick identification.
• Sort everything into three piles: keep, donate, and toss. Find organizations where you can donate unwanted items.

3. Clean.

• Create a cleaning schedule that includes regular tasks like vacuuming, mopping floors, wiping countertops, and dusting surfaces.
• Allocate specific days or time slots for different cleaning tasks to ensure they're consistently addressed.
• Set aside dedicated organizing days for each area or room. 
• Add these days to your calendar or to-do list to ensure they are prioritized and completed.
• Make sure you always have a mop, broom, paper towels, and disinfectants on hand.
