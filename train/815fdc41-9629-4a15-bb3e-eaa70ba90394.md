A single, overlooked detail—such as a misplaced document, a forgotten code snippet, or an undetected error in an algorithm—could trigger a cascading failure in a multi-step plan.

Example: In a supply chain, a delayed shipment due to a minor customs inspection delay might disrupt a final assembly deadline, leading to a product recall or lost revenue.

Key leverage points: **human error, miscommunication, or unintended dependencies** in systems. The failure mode often depends on how well the plan accounts for edge cases.
