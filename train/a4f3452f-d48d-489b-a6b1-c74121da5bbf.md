A dim lantern flickered over the cracked floorboards of the dimly lit cellar, casting long shadows as the old oak door creaked open. Inside, Elias adjusted his worn cloak, his breath visible in the cold air. A handwritten note lay atop the rusted table, its ink faded but still legible: *"The Sword of the Forgotten Dawn shall be found within the Whispering Grove, where the river bends to the east."* His fingers traced the words, the weight of the promise pressing against his chest. The old man’s voice, hoarse with age, echoed through the shadows: *"You will receive the treasure, Elias. The night will tell you when."* The cellar door swung shut behind him, sealing the deal.
