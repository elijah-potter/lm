- Evan Williams Distillery: Located in downtown Louisville along Whiskey Row. This distillery has been there since the 18th century.
- James B. Beam: This distillery is located in Clermont. It features an impressive 65-foot tall still. There is also a restaurant on site called The Kitchen Table.
- Maker's Mark: The Maker's Mark recipe originated during the time of Prohibition. You can dip your own bottle in red wax, to get the iconic bottle's look.
