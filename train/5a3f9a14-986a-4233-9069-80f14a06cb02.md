The old man sat on the bench, his fingers tracing the cracks in the concrete.
*"Another day, another mile,"* he murmured, staring at the passing parade of tourists.
The wind howled, carrying the echo of his voice—*"Another day, another mile"*—as it whipped through the park.

A child ran past, laughing, and for a moment, the words faded. Then the old man’s voice came again, softer this time, almost lost in the hum of the city: *"Another day, another mile."*
