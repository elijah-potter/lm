During a routine maintenance check of the high-pressure pipeline, an unexpected surge in fluid velocity triggered a cascade of alarms. Emergency sensors detected a breach in the secondary containment barrier, releasing a volatile substance into the confined workspace. Firefighters were deployed within 45 seconds, but the volatile compound ignited within 2 minutes, causing the emergency shutdown of all adjacent systems. Ventilation systems failed prematurely due to overloaded fans, forcing evacuation protocols to activate. Structural integrity assessments revealed localized corrosion in the pipeline, traced back to undetected corrosion inhibitors in the feedstock. The incident led to a 24-hour shutdown, resulting in a 12% loss of production output for the downstream facility.
