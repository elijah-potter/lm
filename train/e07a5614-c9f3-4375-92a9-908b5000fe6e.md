Once there was a tiny little bunny named Benny who loved to hop and play every single day. Benny lived in a cozy burrow with his mama, papa, and baby sister Lily. One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw a big, sparkly mushroom growing near their burrow. "Mmm, that looks yummy!" Benny said excitedly.

He hopped over and took a tiny bite. "Oh no!" he gasped, because the mushroom made him feel very, very happy. Benny didn't feel sleepy or tired at all—he felt like dancing and singing! His mama laughed and said, "That's our special happiness mushroom!"

Benny decided to share the joy. He hopped to Lily and whispered, "Lily, try this!" She took a bite and giggled. "I feel like a superhero!" she said. Benny and Lily danced around the burrow, and even Papa and Mama joined in, making everyone feel wonderful.

After playing, Benny noticed something else: the mushroom was growing bigger and brighter every day. One evening, as they sat together, Benny said, "I wonder what happens if we eat more?" Mama looked at him carefully and said, "Hop away, Benny! Too much happiness can make you too happy—remember to share and play gently!"

The next day, Benny remembered Mama’s words. He still hopped and played, but he also helped Lily and his family take care of their burrow. They planted new flowers, told stories by the fire, and made sure everyone felt loved and happy in their own way. And whenever Benny felt a little extra happy, he'd share a tiny piece of his joy with his family, making sure to leave room for all of them to feel good too.

From that day on, Benny learned that happiness isn't just in one special thing—it's in sharing, playing, and loving everyone around them. And whenever he felt a little extra joy, he’d remember to dance and laugh with his family, making sure they felt as happy as he did.
