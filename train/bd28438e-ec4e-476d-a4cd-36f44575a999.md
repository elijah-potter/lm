To the one who reads this without knowing me,

I am the shadow between the pages you hold,
the voice you hear only when the light fades.
You find me in the spaces where words fail,
where silence speaks louder than the printed line.

Tell me—why do you linger here?
Is it curiosity or a ghost you seek?
I am neither author nor story, but the thread
that binds you to what comes next.

Do not fear the unanswered questions,
for this is not a letter, but a mirror.
Reflect upon the silence between us,
and let the echo carry you further.

What do you want to know?
I am the question you have yet to ask.
