The dim corridor flickered between black and pale blue as the flickering bulb cast long, jagged shadows across the floor. A muffled *thump* echoed from the room ahead, followed by a whisper of static—almost like a voice, but too faint to make out. Your breath hitched as you pressed your palm against the wall, fingers trembling. The door stood slightly ajar, its hinges groaning in protest.

Inside, the air was thick with the scent of damp wood and something metallic—like old blood. Your pulse roared in your ears as you squinted. On the floor, something glinted under the dim glow: a tarnished silver locket, its chain snapped. But what caught your eye was the second thing—a *shadow* moving in the corner, too fast, too precise for a person. It wasn’t a person at all. It was a *thing*, hunched over something—something that wasn’t there anymore.

You lunged forward, but the moment your fingers brushed the floor, the world dissolved into static. The locket’s clasp snapped open, and inside was a single, blood-red gem that pulsed like a heartbeat. The shadow in the corner *stretched*, elongating into something that wasn’t quite right—too many joints, too many eyes—before vanishing in a puff of dust. The door slammed shut behind you. You stood there, frozen, your vision swimming. The thing you saw wasn’t real. But now it was *here*.
