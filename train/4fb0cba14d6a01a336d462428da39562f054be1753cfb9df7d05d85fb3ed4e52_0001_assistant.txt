A type of energy healing, Reiki was started in Japan in the 20th century. This particular form of healing is based on the belief that everyone has life force energy in their bodies. During treatment, a trained practitioner moves their hands above or on the body in hopes of promoting healing through a healthy energy flow.

Though it can’t be proven how Reiki works exactly, there are theories. One such theory involved the “biofield,” an electromagnetic field that surrounds each living being. Some believe the biofield can cause changes in its vibrations that affect both physiological and psychological functions. 

This type of healing is used for several problems, both physical and emotional. These include insomnia, stress, depression, pain, and anxiety. It can also help stabilize moods and improve sleep. No dangers or ill side effects have been reported in the use of Reiki when performed by well-trained practitioners. 

Those undergoing Reiki treatments will keep clothing on and can expect to feel very content and relaxed throughout. Some clients fall into a deep meditative state that contributes to the regulation of the nervous system. The typical treatment session length is 45-60 minutes.
