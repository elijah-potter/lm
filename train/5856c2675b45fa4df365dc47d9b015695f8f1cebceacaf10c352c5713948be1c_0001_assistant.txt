Here's a list of some of the best thrift stores in Portland, Oregon, with a focus on stores that are committed to sustainability and giving back to the community:

1. Goodwill Bins: Offers clothing, accessories, and household items at deeply discounted prices.
2. The Red White and Blue Thrift Store: A family-owned thrift store that supports veterans and offers a wide selection of clothing and household items.
3. Community Warehouse: A non-profit thrift store that donates furniture and household goods to people in need.
4. Village Merchants: A consignment store that specializes in vintage and retro clothing, jewelry, and home decor.
5. Value Village: Offers a wide range of secondhand clothing, furniture, and household items at affordable prices.
6. ReClaim It!: A thrift store that focuses on reclaimed and upcycled items, including furniture, decor, and building materials.
