You may think you know prunes…but do you really? Prunes are just dried-up plums, and everybody loves plums!

Packed with vitamins and minerals, prunes are not only a sweet and delicious treat, but they are also high in nutrients. They contain fiber, too, for occasional irregularity, and fiber makes prunes a filling food -- great for a morning snack or an afternoon pick-me-up!

School will be out for summer soon, and prunes make a wonderful snack for the kids! They’re good for you, and kids love them!

Where can you find prunes? Prunes are usually sold in boxes, and you can find them in the canned fruit section of your favorite supermarket. Pick yours up today!
