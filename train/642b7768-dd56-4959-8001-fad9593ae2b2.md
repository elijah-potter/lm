The **Ravenwood Café** in Blackthorn Hollow, a dimly lit, second-floor establishment with mismatched chairs and flickering neon signs. Patrons arrive at irregular intervals—some by appointment, others by sheer coincidence—all drawn by whispers of the café’s "unspoken rules." The air hums with the low murmur of a thousand voices, some overlapping in unintelligible chatter. The barista, an elderly woman with silver-streaked hair, serves coffee with a smirk, never explaining the "curious selection of oddities" on the menu (e.g., "Last Supper" sandwiches, "Echo Tonic"). The café’s most persistent rule: no one leaves until someone mentions the name "Elias Veyne." The last person to do so vanished last week.
