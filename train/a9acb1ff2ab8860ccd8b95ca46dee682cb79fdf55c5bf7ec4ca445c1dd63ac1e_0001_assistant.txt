In a world filled with wonder and magic, one man finds out that the world opens up a lot more when you grow up, and dealing with it becomes a lot harder. 
"Travelling" is about a man named Rye Dest and how he gets sucked into his favorite RPG game. Rye loves to travel in these games so he finally gets the opportunity to make his fantasy a reality!

Starting from a tiny little village, he finds out that there is so much more to see beyond the borders of his known world. A flying city, a medieval kingdom filled with treacherous monsters, and a desert city populated by martial arts experts are some of the locations that Rye gets to travel to. He'll also explore a port town with a thriving casino, a mining town with a seedy thieves' slum, a clockwork city, and a long-lost metropolis where a utopian society thrives in isolation. Along the way, he gets to meet his favorite characters in the flesh. With them at his side, will he be able to accomplish his goal? Rye and his friends will have to conquer their inner demons alongside external monsters and, together, pass the greatest test of their lives.
