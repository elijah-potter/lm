Picking a college major can be tough, for an artistic soul such as yourself you could consider:
1. Interior design- Learn how to make the best use of a space and create an environment in which clients would love to live. Your creativity and artistic leanings will find have no trouble helping to create new trends and designs for people's homes or businesses. You could even add a piece of your artwork to every design. 
2. Marketing- Find ways to catch people's attention and draw their eye to products. With so many ads being seen by each person every day, creativity and an ability to constantly change and evolve are needed to get attention and be a rising star in this field. 
3. Graphic design- Where computers meet art, design all the visual elements for your favorite brands and websites. Almost every industry needs graphic designers so the potential for different employment opportunities is high with this major. 
4. Architecture- Discover how to design buildings and watch your creations become a reality. In this field, most people work extensively with clients to make sure their needs are being met in the best possible way so expect a lot of client interaction and participation in your planning.
5. Fashion design- Learn to design the clothes you want to wear and have everyone else ask where to buy them. You could choose to design for a niche such as costumes with this major, or if design isn't quite for you, many use this field to go into personal styling.
