Checking Tire Pressure
1. You'll need a tire pressure gauge, which is available at most gas stations and auto shops.
2. For each of your tires, remove the air valve cap on the tire and put it somewhere close so you do not lose it.
3. Push the tire pressure gauge onto the valve stem until you hear air coming out. Press for one to two seconds.
4. Read the pressure.
5. Compare the gauge reading to the recommended tire pressure. If you do not know, this can be found in your vehicle's manual. Replace the tire valve cap.
6. Repeat for each wheel.

Inflating a Car Tire
1. Park your car close enough to the air compressor so that you can reach each tire with the compressor's hose.
2. Remove the valve cap from the tire and store it somewhere secure.
3. Push the hose nozzle down onto the valve stem. You should notice the tire inflating as well as feel the air flowing through the hose.
4. When you think it's enough, remove the hose and use the pressure gauge to see what the new pressure of the tire is. If not enough, continue to inflate that tire until the correct psi is achieved.
5. Repeat this process for the other tires on the vehicle.
6. Do not forget to replace the valve caps on each tire.
