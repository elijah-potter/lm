The air we breathe in can affect our health significantly. Many respiratory and allergic problems occur due to air pollution inside the house. Hence, it is essential to keep indoor air clean in every household. Fortunately, keeping the air inside your home clean is not that hard. There are a few simple tips and tricks that you can follow to achieve this.
To start with, one of the most important things you can do to keep indoor air clean is to ventilate your house correctly. This means opening windows and doors regularly to allow fresh air to enter. It would help if you also used exhaust fans in the kitchen and bathroom to eliminate stale air. Furthermore, you can also invest in an air purifier for your home. Air purifiers help to remove any harmful pollutants from the air, making it safer to breathe.
Another way to ensure clean indoor air is by keeping your home clean and tidy. This means vacuuming and mopping regularly, as well as dusting all surfaces. In addition, you should also avoid using any harsh chemicals or cleaning products inside the house. Instead, opt for natural and eco-friendly alternatives.
Lastly, another effective way to clean indoor air is by planting indoor plants. Plants help to purify the air by absorbing harmful toxins. Some of the best air-purifying plants include aloe vera, spider plants, and bamboo palm. So, make sure to add some of these to your home to enjoy cleaner and healthier air.
Here are some other valuable tips:
- Improve Ventilation System
Ventilation is vital in allowing the stuffy indoor air to leave the house and the fresh air from outside to enter your home. Therefore, ensure all of your house’s spaces have sufficient windows or voids for proper air circulation. Moreover, place an exhaust fan in areas with a high risk of generating pollutants, such as over your gas stove or toilet.
- Clean up More Often
Dust and other contaminants can be on any house surface, including the floor, windows, and furniture. They might get blown into the air at any time and eventually be inhaled. Cleaning your house daily can effectively reduce the risk of indoor air pollution. Do not underestimate the value of sweeping, dusting, mopping, vacuuming, wiping windows, and even changing curtains.
- Change Your Filters Regularly
The filter in your air conditioner or air purifier is responsible for catching fine dust from the air that passes. Eventually, dirt will build up. As a result, you may notice your white filter turning brownish. Because of the accumulated dust, your air filters will need to be replaced. Otherwise, your system will not be able to run optimally.
- Choose Cleaning Agents Wisely
When shopping for cleaning solutions, you need to read the label to see the main active agent used on each product. Choose ones that do not use harsh chemicals, as the residue may stay on your home interior’s surfaces and pollute your indoor air. If inhaled for an extended period, such chemicals may negatively affect your health.
- Installing an Air Purifier
An air purifier can be the last resort after you have tried the other tips for keeping your indoor air clean. Don’t forget to buy a purifier with the type of filtration system that meets your home’s requirements. But most importantly, make sure to replace your filter regularly, so it remains effective. Talk to your HVAC contractor if you are unsure which air purifier to buy for your house.
- Maintaining Humidity Level
Keeping the humidity at a healthy level can prevent many pathogens from growing, inhaling, and potentially causing disease. A humidifier or dehumidifier might get the job done if your ventilation system is unreliable. Moreover, you can use your thermostat to monitor the moisture level of your indoor air from time to time.
Indoor air pollution may get serious and more difficult to resolve if you ignore it initially. Besides keeping your indoor air clean, you also need to prevent the number of contaminants in your home from increasing.
