- **The Unseen Threads**
- **Echoes of the Forgotten**
- **Silence in the Static**

**I.**
1. The first number was always the one that didn’t exist.
2. It was written in the cracks of the sidewalk, a ghost in the dust.
3. The second was a whisper—half a name, half a memory—found in a library’s lost section.

**II.**
She traced the third on the edge of a cracked coffee mug.
*Ten thousand steps between the words she couldn’t say.*
The fourth was a door left ajar, the hinges groaning like a confession.
Outside, the fifth was a streetlamp’s flicker, the only light left for the ones who didn’t come home.

**III.**
The sixth was a text message sent at midnight:
*"You’re the only one who can hear me."*
The seventh was a key—rusted, its lock broken—but it fit the door she’d already opened.

**IV.**
The eighth was the sound of her own breath, hollow and wrong.
The ninth was the name of the river where she drowned the things she couldn’t name.
The tenth was the last one, written in blood on a mirror.

**Epilogue:**
The list was never meant to be found.
