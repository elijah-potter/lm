A fleeting urban plaza constructed from repurposed shipping crates, solar panels, and discarded office furniture, held together by tape and DIY scaffolding. Rainwater pools in puddles between the crates, reflecting flickering streetlights and the glow of neon signs from a nearby eatery. Vendors in mismatched aprons sell overpriced, half-finished crafts from stalls draped in plastic sheeting. The air smells of fried food, fresh-cut grass, and the faint metallic tang of rust. A group of musicians plays improvised jazz in a corner, their instruments stolen from a garage sale. Children chase a deflated soccer ball across the cracked pavement, while a few stray dogs lounge in the shadows, watching passersby. The entire setup is anchored to a temporary "city hall" sign that flickers in the wind. The plaza is being dismantled tomorrow, but tonight, it feels like home for the moment.
