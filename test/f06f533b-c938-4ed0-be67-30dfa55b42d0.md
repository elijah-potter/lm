**BREAKING:** Officials in the historic coastal city of **Vaelith** confirm another week of heavy rainfall and storm surges, flooding parts of the Low Quarter District. Emergency services report over 120 displaced residents in the last 48 hours. The city’s ancient harbor, a UNESCO World Heritage site, shows signs of erosion as waves batter the docks. Mayor Elara Voss declares a state of emergency, activating the city’s flood barriers and redirecting municipal buses to evacuation routes. Meanwhile, local fishermen report lost boats near the northern breakwater. The city’s aquarium has issued a public advisory: marine life in the storm-damaged tanks remains unstable, with some species showing signs of distress. No casualties reported yet, but authorities urge residents to avoid storm drains and flooded roads. The next round of warnings is expected for tomorrow evening.
