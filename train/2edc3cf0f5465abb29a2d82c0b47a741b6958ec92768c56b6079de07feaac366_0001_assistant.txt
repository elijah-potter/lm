No problem!  Here are some suggestions for writers chronicling their battles with alcoholism and addiction: 

1. The Recovering by Leslie Jamison
This book is a reflection of the author’s relationship with addiction. It adjoins her story with the stories of legends such as Raymond Carver and permeates it with deep cultural history. 
2. Wishful Drinking by Carrie Fisher
Wishful Drinking is the memoir of Carrie Fisher growing up in Hollywood, fighting addiction, and coping with manic depression. It’s an inside look that is also brutally honest and hysterical.
3. How to Murder Your Life by Cat Marnell
Cat Marnell began to unknowingly "murder her life" at age 15. This is when she became hooked on ADHD medication. Xanax, then led to cocaine, ecstasy, and an avalanche of drug abuse. Manipulation, deceit, and the struggle to survive are all part of her story of dependency.
4. We All Fall Down by Nic Sheff
This book follows the author’s journey into meth addiction. Sheff details his difficulties staying clean.  Rehab, relapse, toxic relationships, and self-destructive behavior are all a part of his story.
