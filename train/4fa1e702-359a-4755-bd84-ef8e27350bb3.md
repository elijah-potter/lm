A floating marketplace in the sky, suspended above a dense urban jungle, where gravity is optional. The air hums with the sound of holographic negotiations and wind-driven levitators. Merchants trade in digital currencies and rare organic artifacts, their floating stalls repurposed from derelict drones and repainted with vibrant, ever-shifting murals. Laws here are governed by a decentralized AI council, where disputes are settled through algorithmic arbitration, but tradition and whimsy still govern daily life. The streets are paved with transparent, recyclable materials that bend light into shifting patterns, and the scent of fresh synth-herbs drifts from communal gardens suspended mid-air. Bridges made of living metal connect the floating districts, and the only "crime" is defying the unspoken rules of the collective—like not returning a borrowed drone or failing to participate in the weekly "sky races" where participants glide on anti-grav boards. The sky itself is a living entity, its moods shifting unpredictably, and the people here learn to read its colors as they would the stars.
