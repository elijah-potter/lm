The **"thumbs-up"** gesture, universally interpreted as approval, can be misinterpreted in different cultures. In some Middle Eastern and Latin American contexts, it may imply sexual innuendo or disrespect, depending on the context. The **"OK" sign** (thumb and index finger forming a circle) can be offensive in Japan and South Korea, where it may mean "you are worthless." In some African cultures, certain finger gestures like the **"middle finger"** can carry strong insults. Context, body language, and cultural background are critical for accurate interpretation.
