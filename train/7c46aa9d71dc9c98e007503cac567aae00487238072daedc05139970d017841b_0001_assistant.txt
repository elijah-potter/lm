-	A mid-level Broadway actress goes back to her small hometown for her niece’s graduation. There, she unexpectedly falls in love with a guy she used to mock in school. He grew up to be a gorgeous – if folksy – farmer who loves decorating his property for each holiday season.
-	A hard-charging lawyer is getting close to making partner when one of her cases forces her to go to a tiny town during its autumn harvest festival. Among her client’s opponents is a baker from this town who is also a single father to a delightful 8-year-old. Our lawyer realizes she must make a choice between completing the trial (which would leave the baker destitute) or running away to this tiny town to be with him instead.
-	A Los Angeles fashion designer who’s had a string of bad relationships goes off to a quaint village to meet with one of her fabric-dye producers. There she learns that the town celebrates Christmas for all of December, and in charge of the festivities is a former lumberjack with a heart of gold (and a golden retriever). She realizes she can’t stomach the idea of returning to the rat race.
-	A recently single city girl gets sent on a tropical retreat by her friends. There, while searching for wholesome ways to entertain herself, she stumbles upon a local sport-fishing guide who lives on his boat and rarely buttons up his shirt. With a bit of a woman’s touch, she realizes the boat could be fixed up into the perfect home for two.
-	A workaholic woman with an overly possessive boyfriend must travel to her tiny Southern hometown during Christmastime to prevent her recently deceased grandmother’s house from being auctioned to the wrong people. She finds that one of the local construction foremen also loved her grandmother and wants to help her keep the house in the family. Unfortunately, her jealous boyfriend finds out she spoke to another man and shows up to confront her angrily, but with some help from the folksy townspeople, she’s able to drive him away for good.
