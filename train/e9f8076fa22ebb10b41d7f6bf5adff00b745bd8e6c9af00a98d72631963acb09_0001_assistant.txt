Sure, I can help you with that! Here are some substitutes you can use instead of eggs for your cake recipe:

1. Carbonated water: Use 1/4 cup carbonated water to equal 1 large egg.
2. Water, oil, and baking powder: Use 2 tablespoons flat water, 2 teaspoons baking powder, and 1 teaspoon vegetable oil to equal 1 large egg.
3. Mashed banana: Use 1/4 cup mashed banana (or roughly 4 inches of banana) to equal 1 large egg. (Note: The ripest bananas work the best for this.)
4. Applesauce: Use 1/4 cup unsweetened apple sauce to equal 1 large egg.
5. Buttermilk: Use 1/4 cup buttermilk to equal 1 large egg.
