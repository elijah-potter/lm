The rain hammered against the rusted iron roof of the abandoned lighthouse, its groaning timbers creaking like an old man’s bones. Inside, the flickering oil lamp cast long shadows across the worn floorboards, where the scent of saltwater and damp wood clung to the air. The lone figure—clad in a patched-up coat and a face half-hidden under a battered hat—sat hunched over a worn journal, quill trembling as they traced the words of a letter addressed to someone long since lost to the sea.

Outside, the waves crashed violently against the cliffs, their thunder drowning out the sound of footsteps approaching. The figure didn’t look up as the door creaked open, the draft of cold air seeping in. A hand clamped over their mouth before the words *"They’re here"* could escape—then silence. The room held its breath as the figure stood slowly, their breath visible in the frigid air, before turning toward the window where the silhouette of two figures stood on the cliffs below, silhouetted against the storm. One held a lantern, the other a rusted old rifle. The journal’s page fell to the floor, its last entry now exposed: *"They’ll never find me here."*
