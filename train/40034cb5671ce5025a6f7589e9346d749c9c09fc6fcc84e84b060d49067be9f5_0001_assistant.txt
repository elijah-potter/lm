In 2014, Elon Musk expressed interest in purchasing the satire website The Onion, however, he did not follow through with this interest and The Onion was able to stay free from his control. In 2017, two former editors for The Onion who had expressed frustration with the site were offered $2,000,000 in funding from Musk in exchange for giving him more creative control. The editors used the funding to hire new employees and writers and Musk officially announced the site in 2018 by describing it as an "intergalactic media empire," a foreboding name.
