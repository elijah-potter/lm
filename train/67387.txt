- (countable) An athlete is person involved in a sporting activity.
- Synonyms: sportsperson, sportsman and sportswoman
- The athlete gets ready for her race.
- (countable) (US) A person who actively participates in physical sports, or a person who is highly skilled in sports. (Known in British English as a "sportsperson".)
- (countable) A physically fit person.
