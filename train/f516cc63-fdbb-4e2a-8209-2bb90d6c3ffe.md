The sudden door slam startled her—she’d barely turned the corner, her breath catching as the hallway’s familiar walls blurred into motion. A chair scraped behind her, then the clatter of shoes on hardwood, followed by a sharp, *"Who’s there?"* His voice was rough, breathless, as if he’d been running. She froze, her pulse hammering in her throat, the scent of woodsmoke and old leather—his—flooding her senses. His hand shot out, fingers brushing her wrist as he yanked her back into the dim glow of the porch light, the flash of his grin sharp and unguarded. *"Guess you’re not the only one who missed this town."* His voice was a low growl, but his eyes—burning with something darker than amusement—held hers, unflinching. The night wind tugged at her coat, the weight of the secret she’d been carrying suddenly heavy as she realized: she’d just been caught.
