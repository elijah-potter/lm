## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park – A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary (For Municipal Funders)**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park project proposes the revitalization of a presently underutilized and degraded public space—a 0.75-acre lot at the intersection of Elm Street and Oak Avenue—into a vibrant, climate-resilient community asset. This transformation addresses critical needs within the West End neighborhood: limited green space, increased urban heat island effect, and a lack of accessible community gathering places. The proposed design incorporates native, drought-tolerant landscaping, stormwater management infrastructure, and accessible pathways, creating a demonstrably cooler, safer, and more welcoming environment for residents. This project aligns directly with the municipality’s commitment to urban forestry, stormwater mitigation, and equitable access to public amenities, and represents a strategically impactful investment in the health and well-being of our community. The total project cost is $175,000, with $100,000 requested from the municipal grant program and $75,000 secured through community fundraising and in-kind donations.

**Project Plan & Timeline**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park project will unfold across four distinct phases, each with defined responsibilities and deliverables.

**Phase 1: Design & Permitting (Months 1-3)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Landscape Architecture Collaborative (LAC), West End Neighborhood Association (WENA)
*   **Deliverables:** Finalized park design incorporating community feedback (LAC), site assessment report (LAC), permit applications submitted to the Planning Department and Public Works (LAC/WENA), community design presentation and approval (WENA).
*   **Timeline:** Months 1-3. This phase focuses on creating a design that reflects the community’s vision while adhering to municipal codes and environmental best practices.

**Phase 2: Site Preparation & Infrastructure (Months 4-6)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** GreenScape Contractors, WENA Volunteer Team, Municipality Public Works Department
*   **Deliverables:** Site clearing and grading completed (GreenScape Contractors), existing debris removed and disposed of (GreenScape Contractors/WENA Volunteers), stormwater infiltration basin excavation and liner installation (GreenScape Contractors/Municipality Public Works Department), accessible pathway construction (GreenScape Contractors).
*   **Timeline:** Months 4-6. This phase involves the essential groundwork for the park’s physical transformation, prioritizing efficient execution and adherence to safety protocols.

**Phase 3: Planting & Amenity Installation (Months 7-9)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Native Roots Nursery, WENA Volunteer Team, LAC (oversight)
*   **Deliverables:** Native plant installation (Native Roots Nursery/WENA Volunteers), installation of benches and picnic tables (WENA Volunteers), installation of signage and interpretive panels (LAC/WENA), completion of irrigation system (Native Roots Nursery).
*   **Timeline:** Months 7-9. This phase focuses on bringing the park's design to life, emphasizing the use of drought-tolerant and ecologically beneficial plant species.

**Phase 4: Grand Opening & Community Programming (Month 10 onwards)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** WENA, Municipality Parks and Recreation Department
*   **Deliverables:** Grand opening event organized and executed (WENA), ongoing community programming schedule established (WENA/Municipality Parks and Recreation Department), park maintenance plan implemented (WENA/Municipality Parks and Recreation Department).
*   **Timeline:** Month 10 onwards. This phase transitions the park into a functioning community space, ensuring its long-term viability and relevance.

**Budget & Financial Transparency**

The total project cost is $175,000. We are requesting $100,000 from the municipal grant program. The remaining $75,000 will be secured through community fundraising and in-kind donations, details of which are outlined below.

*   **Landscape Architecture & Design Fees:** $25,000 (LAC)
*   **Site Preparation & Grading:** $30,000 (GreenScape Contractors)
*   **Stormwater Management Infrastructure:** $20,000 (GreenScape Contractors/Municipality Public Works Department – in-kind labor valued at $10,000)
*   **Native Plant Material & Irrigation:** $35,000 (Native Roots Nursery)
*   **Benches, Picnic Tables, & Signage:** $15,000 (WENA fundraising)
*   **Volunteer Coordination & Event Costs:** $5,000 (WENA)
*   **Contingency Fund (5%):** $8,750

The community has already secured $20,000 in pledges through local business sponsorships and individual donations.  An additional $55,000 will be raised through a series of fundraising events, including a community plant sale and a benefit concert.  The contingency fund will address unforeseen expenses and potential delays.  All financial transactions will be transparently managed and documented, with regular financial reports submitted to the municipality.

**Community Engagement, Equity Impacts, & Risk Mitigation**

Extensive community engagement has been integral to this project’s development. Public forums, online surveys, and direct outreach to residents, particularly those from historically underserved populations within the West End neighborhood, have shaped the park’s design and programming. The park’s location was specifically chosen to address a demonstrable lack of accessible green space in an area with a high density of low-income families. The accessible pathways and inclusive play areas will ensure that individuals of all abilities can enjoy the park's amenities.

Potential risks have been identified and mitigation strategies developed. These include potential delays in permitting, inclement weather impacting construction timelines, and vandalism.  To mitigate these risks, we have established strong relationships with municipal agencies, incorporated flexible scheduling into the project plan, and will implement a community watch program in partnership with the local police department.  The park’s design incorporates durable, low-maintenance materials to minimize long-term maintenance costs and reduce the potential for vandalism.

**Post-Grant Sustainability**

Long-term sustainability is a paramount concern. The West End Neighborhood Association has committed to ongoing park maintenance and programming. A dedicated Park Stewardship Committee will be formed to oversee these efforts.  The municipality’s Parks and Recreation Department will provide technical support and guidance.  The selection of drought-tolerant native plants will significantly reduce water consumption and maintenance requirements.  The Park Stewardship Committee will actively seek additional funding opportunities, including grant applications and corporate sponsorships, to ensure the park’s continued vitality.
