A flash of lightning tore the sky in half as I stood atop the General Electric building. In that instant, I saw his twisted, leering face for the first time. It was a face that only a mother...well, if he had a mother, she'd probably run for the hills. I knew he was the one responsible for the string of horrific crimes in my city. Now, under the threat of a looming downpour, it was time to put an end to him. The pressure in the air was palpable as I slid down the grappling cable, right into the warehouse. With two swift kicks, I disarmed his bodyguards. By the look on his face, I could tell he'd been expecting me. With a wink and a smirk, he vanished right before my eyes.
