Temperatures range from **cold alpine meadows** (-15°C to 5°C) with frozen ground year-round, to **tropical rainforest canopies** (28°C–35°C) with humidity exceeding 90%. Nearby rivers and lakes moderate the climate to **cool springtime breezes** (10°C–18°C), while deserts experience **scorching afternoons** (40°C+) and **chilly nights** (10°C–15°C). Coastal areas near the equator hover at **stable 25°C–29°C** year-round. Seasonal shifts bring **subarctic winters** (-30°C to 0°C) and **temperate summers** (20°C–30°C). High elevation peaks stay **frosty** (0°C–5°C) even in summer, while volcanic slopes experience **mild thermal inversions** (15°C–22°C) due to geothermal activity.
