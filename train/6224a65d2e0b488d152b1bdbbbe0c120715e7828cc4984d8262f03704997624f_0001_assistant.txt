Once upon a time, there was a young rainbow who hadn't yet learned to shine. Every morning she went out to the meadow to practice, but try as she might she never managed so much as a glow. After one particularly hard morning, she returned home crying. She ran to her mother and told her that she didn't think she was ever going to learn how to sine. Her mother sat her down and told her she loved her. She explained that all rainbows eventually learn to shine and there was no timeframe for this. She just needed to relax and let nature take its course. The small rainbow dried her tears, she was still sad about not being able to shine, but she understood a little better that it wasn't her fault. She stopped going out to the meadow every morning and started just going with the flow and enjoying her day. She had breakfast with her mother every morning and then would go for a walk and explore the scenery. One day, months later, she returned from her morning walk, and her mother gasped. You're shining! The little rainbow hadn't even noticed, but it was true. She had finally gotten her shine.
