Congratulations on the new addition to your family! Here's your list... 

1. Oliver. This is one of the most common names for orange cats due to the Disney movie, "Oliver and Company". If you love Disney and your kitten has a fearless attitude this name is great for him!
2. Simba. Who doesn't love "The Lion King"? Name your new kitten after the protagonist in the movie.
3. Mufasa. Just like above, Mufasa is the father of Simba and was known to be courageous. 
4. Creamsicle. If your kitten has a mixture of orange and white, Creamsicle would make a unique name after a favorite dessert.
5. Tang. Tang is an orange drink and is a great name for a kitten with attitude.
