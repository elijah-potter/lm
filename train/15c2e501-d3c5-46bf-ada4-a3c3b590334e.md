The jagged cliffs of Vaelthar’s Reach, where the Obsidian Sea crashes against blackened stone. Salt-laden winds howl through fissures in the rock, revealing glimpses of the endless abyss below—a chasm so vast its depths are lost to mist. Above, the obsidian sky splits into jagged spires where the last remnants of the old gods still whisper through cracks in the world. The air hums with static, and the ground trembles with unseen energy. Here, the land bends toward infinity, and those who stand at its edge feel the weight of what might never be.
