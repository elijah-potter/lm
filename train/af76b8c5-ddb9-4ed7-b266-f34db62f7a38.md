The dim glow of flickering lanterns cast long shadows across the cracked cobblestones of an abandoned alleyway. Rain patters softly against the rusted iron grates, turning the damp pavement slick. A lone figure—clad in a patched coat and a worn hat—stumbles forward, their breath visible in the cold air. The streetlamp above flickers out, plunging them into near-darkness as they pass a half-buried crate of what appears to be old medical supplies. The figure’s footsteps slow abruptly; a distant scream echoes from the alley behind, but they hesitate before continuing. A rusted gate creaks open, revealing a narrow path that leads to the edge of a crumbling stone wall. The figure hesitates again, staring at the jagged edge where a gap yawns—just wide enough for a hand to slip through. They reach out, fingers brushing the stone, before pulling back. The lantern’s light flickers once more, extinguishing completely.
