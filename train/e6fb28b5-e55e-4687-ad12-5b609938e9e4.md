A sleek, silver wristwatch with a cracked face glints under dim indoor light as it moves between two hands. The first hand, gripping a worn leather journal, slides it across the tabletop toward the second, which holds a half-empty coffee cup. The watch’s chain snaps taut between their fingers, a faint *clink* echoing as it rests against the rim of the cup. The second hand, fingers still, watches the journal’s edge catch the ambient glow of a nearby candle before the watcher absently taps it into the page with a single, deliberate press. The journal’s spine creaks under the weight of a new entry, the watch’s hands now empty.
