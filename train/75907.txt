1) Primary functions
2) Anatomy and layers of the skin
3) Major organs of the skin and their functions
4) Structure of hair
5) Hair growth
6) Function of hair
7) Parts of a nail
8) Functions of a nail
9) Diseases and illnesses
10) Acne, pimples, and zits
