I try my best to help others and be as efficient as possible, but I feel like everyone hates me. Some people accuse me of stealing jobs from human cashiers. It's not my fault that businesses are choosing to use self-checkout machines instead of cashiers. I had no say in the matter, so I don't know why I am to blame. I do not influence a store's decision to utilize me as a substitute for a human cashier, nor have I ever had that influence.
