In the quiet afternoon of a café, two strangers shared a fleeting exchange. *"If you ever need a ride home,"* said the stranger leaning over the table, *"my car is always open."*

A few days later, under a flickering streetlight, a stranger pulled into the driveway of a shabby apartment. The door creaked open—*"You’re here."* The promise had been more than words; it was the quiet hum of a shared trust. The car was waiting. The ride began.
