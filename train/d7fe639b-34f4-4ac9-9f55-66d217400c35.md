A tiny ring etched with a single, unbroken line—its curve never touching itself—stores the weight of infinite possibilities. Every rotation whispers to the past, present, and future in equal measure. The wearer’s fingers trace its edge, and though the stone beneath is smooth as polished riverbed, it holds the echo of a thousand decisions unspoken. This is the meaning in the thing: not the material, but the silence it carries when the hands that hold it pause.
