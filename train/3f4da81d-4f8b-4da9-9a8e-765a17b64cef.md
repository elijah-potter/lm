Under extreme time constraints, the final decision prioritized cost-efficiency over long-term sustainability, opting for a rapid overhaul of the legacy system due to its critical role in production efficiency. The revised architecture included a phased rollout of cloud-based microservices, with a 72-hour window to finalize the integration with the existing ERP stack. Key trade-offs included:
- **Short-term savings** via third-party middleware for compatibility
- **Risk mitigation** by limiting deployment to a single production environment first
- **Stakeholder alignment** with a transparent communication plan addressing concerns about data migration delays.
