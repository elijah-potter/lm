Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big garden, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown ears and a tail that wiggled when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny saw a shiny red apple on a tree. "Mmm, that looks yummy!" Benny said and took a big bite. He felt happy and wanted to share.

He ran to his friend, a blue butterfly named Butter, who was sitting on a flower. "Butter," Benny said, "want to play with me?" Butter fluttered her wings and said, "Sure, Benny!"

They ran through the grass, jumped over a small hill, and chased a butterfly balloon. The balloon went up and up until it disappeared into the blue sky.

Benny and Butter had a wonderful time, and they both felt very happy. As the sun began to set, Benny said, "Butter, let's go home." Butter nodded and said, "Okay, Benny."

They walked together, and Benny gave Butter a big hug. "Goodbye, Butter," Benny said, and Butterfly waved her wings goodbye. Benny waved his fluffy tail as he went home.

That night, Benny curled up in his bed with his favorite blanket and dreamt about the fun they had together. Benny fell asleep, dreaming of the sunshine, the flowers, and his best friend Butter.

The next morning, Benny woke up and saw his garden was still full of sunshine. He knew Butter would be waiting for him again, and Benny couldn’t wait to play with his friend once more!
