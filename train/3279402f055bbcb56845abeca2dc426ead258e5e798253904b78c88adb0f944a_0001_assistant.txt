

1. Steel Heart - A 19th-century tale of a woman jilted at the altar who is kidnapped by highwaymen and then rescued by a gorgeous blacksmith who is also a sworn bachelor. 
2. Desire Island - A woman on her way to fulfill an arranged marriage gets shipwrecked on a desert island with only one other person -- the crewman who thinks she's a spoiled princess. 
3. Fire and Ice - A pioneer woman on the Oregon trail gets separated from her party and is forced to stay in a handsome stranger's remote cabin until winter ends. 
4. Blue Horizon - A pioneer woman pays a handsome stranger to pose as her husband for protection while she's traveling by train across the American West. 
5. The Last Moment - Doomed to an arranged marriage, a 16th-century woman has a torrid days-long fling with a stranger. She arrives at her wedding to learn he is a knight to her betrothed, a cruel prince planning to kill her after securing her land and property.
