Dear boatman,
I really want to get to the other side of the river first, not because I'm more important than the others, but because I haven't got any other option to survive. For the hare, they have the option of eating grass, herbs, and other weeds that are available everywhere, so they won't have to struggle to find food. For the monkey, there are lots of trees around here, and they'll probably jump on one or the next in order to find their food. 
I, on the hand, am limited in choice. Most of the animals migrated to the other side of the river before it was flooded, and now my only option is to cross to the other side in order to hunt. If I remain on this side with either the hare or the monkey, I'll have to eat the one who's remaining. Also, it's easier to carry both of them together on one trip, while it will probably cause you problems if you carry me and either of them on the boat. So that's why I think you should take me to the other side first.
