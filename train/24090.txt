This book describes the activities and the distribution of microorganisms on the basis of both the chemical transformations that they mediate and the environments in which they live. The part played by bacteria, fungi, algae and protozoa in the biogeochemical cycles of nutrients is stressed and their population dynamics is soils, fresh water, the oceans and the atmosphere is discussed. The aim of the book is to demonstrate the importance of microorganisms in the biosphere and to indicate some of the problems and challenges in the study of microbial ecology.
