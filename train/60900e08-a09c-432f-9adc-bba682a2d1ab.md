The parties acknowledge mutual consent to modify or terminate this Agreement at any time, provided written notice is provided **30 days prior** to the effective date of such action. Any changes will be binding only upon full execution of a revised, signed document by both parties. Termination shall result in a **non-compete clause** prohibiting the departing party from engaging in similar business activities within a **12-month period** in the same geographic region. Confidential information shall be deemed automatically deleted after **6 months** unless a written retention agreement is executed by both parties. All disputes shall be resolved through arbitration in the jurisdiction where the primary office is located, with final decisions binding without appeal.
