A tiny, overlooked brass key—its once-polished surface now dull with age—found in the attic corner where dust settled between forgotten years. Its lock was long since broken, but its shape held the weight of a promise: a key to a door that had never opened fully. The symbol of a promise unfulfilled, etched into the minds of those who passed it along. The key itself, though brittle, still held the memory of what it once unlocked. In the hands of each new owner, it became a silent witness to stories never told.
