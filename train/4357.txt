EQAO - or the Education Quality and Accountability Office - is an independent agency of the Ontario government. Its provincial assessment program for primary, junior and secondary students provides parents, teachers and the public with information about student achievement that will help improve teaching and learning.
TDSB Schools all have annual budgets to support classroom programs. Money provided is based on the school budget model. The school budget model has been developed through a process involving Principals, Vice Principals, Superintendents, school office budget staff, Trustees and staff from the central Budget and Revenue Department. Although a model is used to determine budget amounts, schools do have flexibility in administering the budget.
