**Central Square, Prague:**
Bargaining at **Prague Old Town Market Square** over spices and souvenirs, while tourists snap photos of the Astronomical Clock. Locals gather at **Charles Bridge** for sunrise sunbathing and street performers. **Beer gardens** buzz with traditional Czech *pivovars* (breweries) and *trdelník* stands, while nightlife pulses at **Karlovy Lázně** clubs. **Vltava River cruises** offer scenic views of historic bridges, and **Mendel’s Garden** attracts visitors for botanical walks. Daily **prague street food** stalls serve *svíčková* (beef in creamy sauce) and *trdelník* pastries, while locals and tourists mix in the lively atmosphere.
