**Great Wall of China (segment near Beijing):**
- Northern boundary: Outer wall from **Badaling** (~100 km north) to **Mutianyu** (~150 km).
- Eastern boundary: **Jinshanling** (~300 km east) to **Jiankou** (~350 km).
- Western boundary: **Mutianyu** to **Jinshanling** (shared with northern/eastern sections).
- Southern boundary: Natural river systems (e.g., **Huang River** tributaries) or fortified gates like **Tianjin Gate**.

**Key markers:** Wall sections, gates, and defensive towers (e.g., **Hutuo Pass** at ~400 km east).
