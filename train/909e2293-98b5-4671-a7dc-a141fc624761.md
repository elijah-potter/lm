The waiter bowed slightly, but his voice cracked mid-sentence.
*"Would you like to share the table, sir?"* he stammered, eyes darting to the empty chair across from him.

The stranger leaned back, arms crossed, a smirk playing at the corners of his mouth.
*"Oh, that’s sweet. You really think I’d want to sit with a stranger?"*

The waiter’s face flushed. He took a deep breath, then cleared his throat.
*"I—I just wanted to be polite."*
