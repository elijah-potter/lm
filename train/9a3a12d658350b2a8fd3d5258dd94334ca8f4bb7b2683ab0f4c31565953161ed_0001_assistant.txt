The Kingdom of Rhodan was a magical kingdom home to elves, goblins, sorcerers, magicians, peasants, and lords. But there had long been division in the Kingdom of Rhodan because the elves and the goblins were always quarreling and fighting. The elves resented the goblins for their ugliness and for living in the woodlands that had once belonged to their ancestors, while the goblins resented the elves for their purity and for keeping the gold that had once been theirs. 

One day, Tim became the new King of Rhodan. Tim was a righteous man who strongly desired to bring peace to all the citizens of Rhodan. No other King had managed to bring peace between the elves and the goblins, but King Tim was determined to do so. So Tim assembled the leaders of the elves and the goblins in his castle. He convinced the goblins to return the woodlands to the elves, and in exchange, he convinced the elves to return to the goblins their gold. King Tim showed equal compassion to both the goblins and the elves. 

Thus, peace was brought to the Kingdom of Rhodan for now the elves and the goblins were both happy. Every year, King Tim would invite the elves and the goblins together to the dining halls of his castle to break bread and dine together so they could celebrate what became known as the Day of Peace. Everybody lived happily ever after.
