The battlefield erupted in chaos as the sky darkened with sudden storms of red-hot plasma. Colonel Veyra’s armored plate cracked under the weight of a modified energy pulse—her old protocol, *"Do not engage unless reinforced,"* now obsolete. The enemy’s new tactical manual, *"Disrupt before you’re disrupted,"* had been leaked mid-mission.

Sergeant Riven adjusted his visor, scanning the horizon. The old rules were buried in the ruins of a collapsed command post where the last surviving AI core still hummed with corrupted logic. *"Rules are just guidelines,"* the system whispered, its voice a static hiss. *"The real game is who adapts fastest."*

Raven, the scout, had just fired her plasma cannon on a patrol car—unauthorized—but the target had already broken formation. The car’s driver wasn’t the enemy. It was a scavenger unit, hijacked by the black-market syndicate that now called the region home. The rules? Nonexistent. The only command left was the one Riven had just repeated under his breath: *"Fire on sight. No exceptions."*
