

Pebbles the Clown started the show
With a wink and a wave to the crowd
He blew up some balloons, quick as a flash
With the audience getting quite loud

The balloons were now ready to go
And Pebbles began to twist
They morphed into animals of many a type
The crowd cheered - they could not resist!

With the balloons now done and the crowd subdued
Pebbles climbed onto his bike
Honking his horn and peddling around
He ended the show feeling no dislike
