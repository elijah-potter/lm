Unspoken Code of the Veldt:
A sun-drenched canyon where the wind carries whispered commands. Stepping on a cracked stone invites a slow, unspoken countdown—each step a silent surrender to the rhythm of the earth’s pulse. The elders sit beneath the fig trees, their hands tracing ancient glyphs in the dust. Rules aren’t carved in stone but in the way the elders pause, or how the elders watch the children play. The water hole is sacred: no one touches it before dawn. The elders know the weight of each decision by the way the shadows stretch differently at noon. A glance at the elders is a promise. The elders know the elders. The elders know the elders *never* forget.
