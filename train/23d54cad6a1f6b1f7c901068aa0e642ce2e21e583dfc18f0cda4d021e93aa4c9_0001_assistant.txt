Becoming a dental associate:

Pros:
• Better work-life balance. In general, you'll have the ability to keep work at work.
• Stability. You will still have an assured income and regular workplace benefits.
• Connections. You will have time to focus on the relationships of your patients and your mentorship. This connection could lead you to a buy-in opportunity!

Cons:
• Less control. As an associate, you won't be able to change your environment, the procedures, or other aspects of the practice.
• Less income. You may experience less income as a dental associate than running your own practice.
• Slow start. You'll need to gain a new patient base as a dental associate, requiring time, energy, and trust.
