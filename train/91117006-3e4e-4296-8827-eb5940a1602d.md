I was just passing by the corner store when the chaos erupted. The bell above the door chimed as the door swung open, and inside, chaos erupted like a storm inside a teacup. A man in a faded blue hoodie was arguing with a woman clutching a grocery bag. She pointed a finger at him, her voice rising, *"You’re blocking the exit, you idiot!"* The man’s face turned red, his hands clenched into fists. Suddenly, he lunged, grabbing her bag and slamming it against the counter.

The store manager, a lanky man in his fifties with glasses, stepped forward, arms crossed. *"Enough!"* he barked, voice shaking with tension. The woman’s eyes widened, but she didn’t back down. *"He stole my cart!"* she snapped, pointing at the man’s chest. The man’s eyes darted to the empty cart near the door. The manager sighed, rubbing his temples. *"Alright, let’s see this."*

He pulled out his phone and snapped a photo of the cart. The woman gasped, *"That’s mine!"* She dug through her bag, pulling out a receipt. The manager flipped through it, then looked up. *"You’re telling me you paid for that cart?"* The woman nodded. The man scowled, but the manager held up a hand. *"Just wait. I’ll call security."* The woman’s shoulders slumped, but her voice trembled. *"Fine. But he didn’t just take it—he—"* She cut herself off, her face red.

The man’s jaw tightened. *"I didn’t take it. I was trying to help—"* He gestured toward the cart. The manager ignored him, already dialing. The store’s alarm began to blare, a high-pitched wail that cut through the tension. The woman’s eyes widened. *"Oh no, not again."* She backed up, bumping into the counter. The man, suddenly nervous, stepped forward. *"Look, I’m sorry if I overreacted—"* The manager hung up the phone. *"Security’s on their way. For now, stay here."* He pointed to the back room.

The woman flinched. *"You don’t have to—"* The manager waved a hand. *"Just stay. This isn’t over."* The bell above the door chimed again, and a uniformed security guard pushed open the door, his face stern. The woman’s breath hitched. The man’s eyes flicked to the security badge. The guard nodded at the manager. *"We got it."* He stepped forward, his hand already on his radio. The woman swallowed hard, her fingers tightening around her receipt. The cart remained untouched on the floor.

The security guard turned to the man. *"You’re under arrest for obstruction."* The man’s face paled. *"Wait—"* The guard cut him off. *"For now. You’ll need to stay until the police arrive."* The woman’s voice cracked. *"What does that even mean?"* The manager exhaled sharply. *"It means he’s in trouble. Again."* The security guard’s eyes flicked to the receipt in her hand. *"You’re both under arrest for theft."* The woman’s eyes widened. The manager’s voice was flat. *"Oh."*

The alarm stopped, replaced by the hum of the store’s lights. The woman’s bag slipped from her hands, clattering to the floor. The manager scooped it up, handing it to her. *"Here."* She took it, her hands shaking. The man’s legs buckled. The security guard caught him by the arm, steering him toward the back. The woman followed, her heels clicking against the linoleum. The manager watched them go, then turned to the manager. *"You’re going to be busy."* He sighed. *"Again."*
