**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 14, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, promising a celebration of agriculture, community, and family-friendly entertainment. This year’s fair will feature a diverse range of exhibits, demonstrations, and activities showcasing the region’s rich agricultural heritage alongside modern innovations. Visitors can expect livestock shows, home and garden displays, artisan crafts, and a variety of food vendors offering classic fair fare and unique culinary experiences.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 16th to August 20th, 2024, at the County Fairgrounds in Springfield. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events continuing until 10:00 PM. The fairgrounds will be accessible by car and public transportation. A dedicated parking area will be available with an entry fee per vehicle. Detailed schedules for all events, competitions, and demonstrations will be published on the fair’s official website.

Admission to the Sangamon County Fair is $10 for adults, $5 for children aged 6-12, and free for children five years of age and under.
