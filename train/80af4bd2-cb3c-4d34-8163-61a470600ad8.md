A midnight rave in a quiet library, where the hum of fluorescent lights blends with bass from a hidden subwoofer. Neon signs flicker across the bookshelves, casting eerie reflections on the shelves of dusty tomes. A DJ spins vinyl records while scholars, masked in tinfoil and headphones, dance under the glow of laser pointers. A single candle flickers in the corner, its flame casting long shadows on ancient maps. Occasional laughter from the stacks mixes with the music, and the scent of aged paper mingles with the smell of synthwave. A few stray cats watch from the racks, unperturbed. The crowd murmurs in a mix of languages, some whispered, some shouted over the beat. The librarian, now a performer, conducts the crowd with a cane, occasionally dropping a book as a "drum break."
