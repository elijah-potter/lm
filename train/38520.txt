X-Ray Math Fractions
Date added: 18/07/2019
Rating: 0% 0 votes
X-Ray Math Fractions is a beautyful online which is playable on pbs game education. Here, click on a square to the left of the X-ray bar and move it to the X-ray bar to reveal the fractional value it contains. Once you determine what the equal fraction to the revealed fraction is, move it over the square on the right side of the X-ray bar that contains the answer. Good luck!
Once you have the problem over its answer, drop the squire to place it. If you choose a wrong square, points will be deducted from your score and you will still have to find its proper location. At https://www.pbskidsgames.games/, move all problems to their answers to complete the level. Have a good time!
