1. Run your toothbrush under water.
2. Squeeze a small, pea-sized amount of toothpaste on the bristles.
3. Brush the front top row of your teeth before moving to the inside of the top row.
4. Brush the bottom outside row of your teeth before moving to the inside of the bottom row.
5. Take your time to reach back and get all of your teeth.
6. Brush your tongue.
7. Spit out the toothpaste and saliva that has gathered.
8. Rinse your mouth with water. 
9. Rinse your toothbrush and place it back in its holder.
10. Gargle with a small cup of mouthwash if needed.
