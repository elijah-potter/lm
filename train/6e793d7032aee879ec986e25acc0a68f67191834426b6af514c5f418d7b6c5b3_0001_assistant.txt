1. Social milestones: Baby will start to play games like pat-a-cake. 
2. Communication milestones: Baby waves bye-bye, calls parents "mama" and "dada,” and understands "no."
3. Cognitive milestones: Starts to put something in a container or looks for things that you try to hide.
4. Movement milestones: Pulls up to a stand, walks or holds onto furniture, drinks from a cup without a lid, and picks things up with thumb and forefinger.
