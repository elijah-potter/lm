The purpose of this study is to examine and try to understand how people make judgments about others. This particular research is focused on understanding how people of all ages perceive people over the age of 80. In this study, you will be shown three videos involving people who look to be elderly and over the age of 80. After each video, you will answer several questions on a questionnaire about that particular video. If you find the study makes you uncomfortable, you may stop at any time by simply closing the study. If you think you may be made uncomfortable with the videos or information displayed, we suggest that you do not participate in the study.
