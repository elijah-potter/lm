The dim glow of a flickering lamp cast long shadows across the cluttered study, where stacks of dust-moted books leaned against the shelves. A scent of aged paper and damp earth clung to the air. The seeker, hunched over a worn leather-bound journal, traced the edges with trembling fingers, fingers that had once gripped solid objects with certainty. The words inside—scattered, fragmented—were the only clues, each line a whisper of something lost in the void between perception and reality.

A knock at the door made her jump. The door creaked open, revealing the faint outline of a stranger in the hallway. The stranger held nothing but an empty space where a tangible object should have been—perhaps a memory, a dream, a name. The seeker’s breath hitched. The stranger tilted their head, eyes reflecting the same hollow emptiness.

"Did you find it?" the stranger asked, voice like rusted metal.

The seeker’s hands clenched the journal tighter. "No."

The stranger stepped back, vanishing into the hallway like a ghost with no form. The lamp snuffed out. The study descended into silence, but not the quiet of absence—this was the silence of something waiting, something that had never truly been held. The seeker exhaled, then reached for the journal again, this time with a new, heavier purpose. The ink within pulsed faintly, as if alive. The words rearranged themselves, forming a single, impossible question:

**"What if it’s not what you’re looking for?"**
