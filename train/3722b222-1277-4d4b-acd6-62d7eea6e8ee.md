To the reader whose name remains unseen,

I write not of stars that fade or rivers that flow,
but of the quiet moments when the mind wanders slow.
You stand here now, perhaps half-aware of my words,
and yet they linger like dust on a still morning’s hush.

Do you seek the truth in words I pen?
Or do you simply listen, as if you were meant
to hear this story before it slips away?
I do not know your name, nor your past, nor your way—
yet here you are, and I have written this for you anyway.

The path is yours to walk, the answers none can claim,
only the silence between what you say and what you name.
Read on, then. Let the words you find here claim you too.
