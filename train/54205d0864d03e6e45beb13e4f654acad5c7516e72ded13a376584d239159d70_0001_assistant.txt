The national bird of the United States is the bald eagle. It is a bird of prey found in North America, known for its brown plumage, white head, and white tail. The bald eagle is not actually bald, but gets its name from the older meaning of "white-headed." It is an opportunistic feeder that primarily eats fish and builds the largest nests of any North American bird. It holds great significance to the United States and appears on the nation's seal as the national bird.
