City council meeting notes (summary):

**Agenda:** Annual budget review and community feedback session
**Consensus:** All council members unanimously approve a $1.2M increase for public safety infrastructure (pavement repairs, new security cameras).

**Reasons for Agreement:**
- **Member A (Economic Focus):** "This aligns with our economic growth strategy—reducing liability costs for businesses."
- **Member B (Tourism Focus):** "Enhanced security improves visitor confidence; directly boosts local tourism revenue."
- **Member C (Long-Term Planning):** "Structural repairs prevent future expenses; better asset lifecycle management."
- **Member D (Neighborhood Prioritization):** "Our district’s high crime rate justifies the investment; directly addresses resident concerns."
- **Member E (Policy Neutrality):** "The proposal’s funding source is transparent; no partisan considerations."

**Action:** Proposal passed with no amendments, forwarded to city manager for implementation within 60 days.
