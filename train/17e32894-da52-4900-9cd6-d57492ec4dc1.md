**Scene 1: The Library of Lost Echoes**
A flickering lantern casts long shadows over the ancient tomes in a crumbling archive. Old books whisper secrets in a language of half-forgotten symbols. A scholar, fingers tracing the edges of a cracked manuscript, realizes the page is blank—yet the ink is still wet.

**Scene 2: A Child’s Drawing**
A small girl scribbles wildly on a napkin, her crayons blurring colors into swirling patterns. The drawing unfolds into a floating city where buildings drift like leaves in a storm. A single word, *"Echoes,"* appears in the corner.

**Scene 3: The Clockwork Heart**
A clockmaker’s apprentice adjusts gears in a workshop. The machine hums, stitching together fragments of memories—one moment is a bustling marketplace, the next a silent forest. The gears grind slower, and the scene shifts: a scholar’s inkblot becomes a child’s crayon stroke.

**Scene 4: The River of Mirrors**
A traveler steps into a reflective pool. Their reflection moves independently, laughing at something unseen. The water ripples into a library’s shelves, then a child’s drawing, then the clockwork heart. The traveler wakes—only to find the ink on their wrist still wet with the same forgotten language.
