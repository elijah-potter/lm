Affirmations are positive statements about the self that are intended to be repeated like mantras. Repeating positive affirmations has been shown to have noticeable benefits in one’s self-love, gratitude, confidence, and relationships. When working with affirmations, it’s best to find the ones that speak to you the most. Here’s a list of our favorite affirmations:

1. I am worthy of love exactly as I am.
2. I am learning to love myself more each day.
3. I am grateful for the people that I have in my life.
4. I am capable of overcoming negative thinking. 
5. Deep inner peace is my natural state.
6. I do not have to be perfect to be lovable.
7. I do not have to earn the right to enjoy life.
8. I forgive myself and others easily.
9. I am guided by a deep, inner knowing.
10. I am focused on the here and now.
11. I can quiet my mind with ease.
12. I deserve to take my time.
13. All that I need is within me.
14. It is safe for me to find joy in the present moment.
15. I am safe and secure.
16. I approve of my desire to feel loved and appreciated.
17. It is safe for me to focus on the present moment.
18. I value progress over perfection.
19. I deserve to feel safe and comfortable no matter where I am or who I am with.
20. I experience an abundance of love in the world on a daily basis.
21. It is safe for me to relax.
22. I deserve to experience joy.
23. I am willing to trust the process.
24. I treat myself gently and with respect.
25. I choose to move at a pace that feels right for me.

While these are some of our favorites, an incredible number of affirmations exist, including those for athletic or academic performance, motherhood, and stress relief. When working with affirmations, it is best to practice them on a regular basis. This can be done by meditating on these phrases in your head, repeating them out loud, or writing them down. Whatever method you use, working with affirmations could be your key to a more positive mindset.
