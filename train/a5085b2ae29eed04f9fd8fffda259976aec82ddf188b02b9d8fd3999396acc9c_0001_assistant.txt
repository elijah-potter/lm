The key ingredients in a Pina Colada include: 

•	Rum 
•	Pineapple Juice 
•	Coconut Milk or Cream of Coconut 

Other components may include pineapple slices, maraschino cherries, and lime juice. 

Here are instructions on how to make a Pina Colada: 

Combine equal parts pineapple juice, rum, and coconut milk with a scoop of ice in a blender. Add a splash of lime juice, then blend until smooth. Use pineapple slices or maraschino cherries as a garnish or line the bottom of a glass and eat them after the drink is gone.
