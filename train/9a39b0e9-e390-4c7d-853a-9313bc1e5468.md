**Acoustic Sanctuary – The Grand Amphitheatre**
Elevated terraces of polished reclaimed oak, draped with lush vines, frame a basin of organic stone. Natural acoustics, enhanced by ancient resonant cavities, concentrate sound waves into a harmonic pulse. The central stage, a circular platform of dark cedar, sits atop a gently sloping amphitheater, allowing performers to be seen and heard equally by all.

Seating consists of:
- **Harmony Rows:** Curved benches upholstered in weathered velvet, angled to channel sound directly to the center.
- **Echo Chairs:** Wooden seats with carved acoustic chambers, amplifying whispers into reverberations.
- **Sky Lounges:** Elevated platforms with glass panes and padded cushions, overlooking the basin to absorb ambient noise.

At night, bioluminescent moss drapes the walls, casting an ethereal glow over performers. The amphitheater is a sanctuary where music, poetry, and conversation converge in a single, immersive experience.
