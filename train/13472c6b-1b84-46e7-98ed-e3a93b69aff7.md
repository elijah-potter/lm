In the quiet corners of an empty room, where the walls whisper forgotten names, a single candle flickers against the fading light. The air hums with the echoes of voices that no longer exist—some joyful, some sorrowful, all lost to time.

Outside, the city breathes in rhythm with the clock, oblivious to the quiet ache within. But tonight, the candle holds its own light, unyielding, defiant. What if the world doesn’t remember? What if all we are is dust in the wind, and the only thing left is the question—why do we dare to hope?
