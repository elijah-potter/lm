**The "Silence at Meals" Protocol**

All staff must remain silent during food service periods, per *Administrative Directive 7-4-2019*. This rule was implemented after an incident involving excessive chatter during a buffet line causing a food-borne illness outbreak in 2017. The directive was intended to prevent contamination but was later discovered to have been motivated by a fear of "social noise" disrupting the "calm, professional atmosphere" of the facility. While the original intent was to improve hygiene, the enforcement now includes mandatory "quiet hours" with no exceptions, even when no food is being served. Compliance is enforced via automated noise detection sensors that trigger mandatory "training sessions" for repeat offenders.
