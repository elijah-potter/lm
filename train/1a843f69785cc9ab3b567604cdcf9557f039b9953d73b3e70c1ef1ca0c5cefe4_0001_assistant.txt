1870: Lobengula granted Sir John Swinburne's London and Limpopo Mining Company the right to search for gold and other minerals on a tract of land in the extreme southwest of Matabeleland along the Tati River between the Shashe and Ramaquabane rivers, in what became known as the Tati Concession.
1888: A British team (Francis Thompson, Charles Rudd and Rochfort Maguire) came to try to persuade him to grant them the right to dig for minerals in additional parts of his territory.
1890: Significant mining in the area commenced.
