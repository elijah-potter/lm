The clock struck midnight as the old lighthouse keeper adjusted his spectacles, his fingers tracing the grooves of his worn leather satchel. The sea, restless beneath the moonshine, whispered secrets only the tide could understand. Nearby, a single seagull’s cry echoed like a ghost’s lament, its shadow stretching across the cracked floorboards of the watchroom.

Outside, the fog curled like smoke, clinging to the edges of the lantern’s beam. The keeper exhaled slowly, watching as a forgotten coin—its edge dulled by time—rolled from his pocket and tumbled into the rusted lantern’s chimney. It landed softly, just above the embers, where it would stay forever, forgotten by the wind and the waves.
