- Alex Pietrangelo was drafted 4th overall in the 2008 NHL Draft by the St. Louis Blues.
- He would wear number 27 for the Blues.
- Pietrangelo was named to two all-star teams while with the Blues.
- The 6'3 defenseman was a top two-way defender in the league.
- In 2016, Pietrangelo was named captain of the St. Louis Blues.
- In 2019, he helped lead the Blues to the Stanley Cup as the team's captain.
- He set the Blues' playoff record for assists from a defenseman.
- In 2020 the Blues were positioned for another playoff run until Covid derailed the season.
- After the season Pietrangelo was set to become an unrestricted free agent.
- He would go on to sign with the Vegas Golden Knights.
- The contract was 7 years with an AAV of $8.8 million.
- The defenseman brings 450 points across 758 career NHL games.
