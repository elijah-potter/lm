**The Last Entry**

Here is the list of things I’ve taken:
1. Your favorite pen from your desk drawer.
2. The receipt for the coffee you never paid for.
3. The last note you left under my door.
4. The half-eaten sandwich you swore you’d finish.
5. The name of the secret song you hummed when no one was listening.

I confess:

I’ve been keeping this list in my head for months. Not to steal—though I might have taken a few things for "emergency supplies"—but because you never said how you felt about any of it.

You wrote on that napkin that you’d "figure things out later," but the truth is, I already did. I just didn’t know how to tell you. Now I do.
