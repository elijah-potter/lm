Caterpillar: Hey Butterfly, how’s life going? 
Butterfly: Oh, you know. Can’t complain! Just fluttering around. How about you?
Caterpillar: Honestly, I’m not loving life these days. I’m just crawling around on the ground, or at best, heading up the stalk of a plant. What I really want is to be able to fly like you. 
Butterfly: Yeah, I hear you. Actually, I couldn’t always fly, but one day, I went to sleep for a while, and when I woke up, I had wings! 
Caterpillar: No way! That’s amazing, I had no idea. I wish that would happen to me. 
Butterfly: I wish I could tell you how to make it happen, but I think it might have been magic. There’s really no logical explanation, and I didn’t do anything special to make it happen. 
Caterpillar: Maybe I’m just not that lucky. I don’t think I have any magic within me at all. 
Butterfly: I think you’ve got a ton of magic, Caterpillar! I really wish I could help you fly. 
Caterpillar: It’s okay; I think I’m just gonna go lay down for a while. I’ll see you later, I guess. 
Butterfly: Okay, I’ll see you soon. 
Butterfly: Hmm, I haven’t seen my friend Caterpillar in quite a while. I hope he’s okay. 
Caterpillar: Butterfly, Butterfly, it’s me, Caterpillar! 
Butterfly: What?! Caterpillar, you’re flying! You look just like me!
Caterpillar: I know! I went to bed for a nap cause I was so tired, and when I woke up, I could fly!
Butterfly: I guess you have enough magic in you all along.
