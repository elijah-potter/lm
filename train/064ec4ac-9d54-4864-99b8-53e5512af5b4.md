The kitchen table was cluttered with empty plates and half-eaten sandwiches. Sarah wiped her hands on her apron and glanced around the empty space where the toaster usually stood. She remembered placing it there last night before heading to bed. The faint scent of toasted bread lingered in the air, but no warm, crackling sound filled the silence.

Her phone buzzed on the counter—an incoming call. She hesitated, then answered, voice strained. *"Mom, I’m still here—just realized I left the toaster in the living room."* She hung up abruptly, tossing her phone aside. The weight of the missing item settled over her like an unspoken guilt.
