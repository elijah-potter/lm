A fragmented record of fragments:

---
**Earliest memory of the night:**
*"They say the clock struck twelve, but the sky was still dark."*
A whisper in a café: *"You ever wonder what happens when you forget the name of the person you love?"*

---
**Later, when the dust settled:**
*"The last text was: 'Meet me at the bridge. Don’t tell anyone I’m coming.'*
*I was late. The water was frozen. The reflection moved."*

---
**The half-remembered details:**
*"Her voice—soft as wet wool. The way she tilted her head when she lied. The bridge’s name: *Silence’s Reflection*. The handwriting in the notebook: *‘They call me Ghost. Don’t ask.’*"

---
**A single, stubborn truth:**
*"I left my phone at home. No photos. No names. Only this: the way the wind hummed like a lullaby when we walked home."*

---
**Final note:**
*"The bridge burned. The clock reset. The sky split. But the name? I still can’t recall it."*
