- Justin wakes up, still tired from getting home from work late the night before.

- He quickly gets dressed, rinses his hair in the sink, and walks to school.

- He stops at a convenience store and grabs a few granola bars for breakfast.

- He gets to school, goes into first-period science class, and falls asleep.

- When he wakes up, his teacher is hovering over him, asking him if he’s ready for the upcoming exam. 

- The bell rings and Justin goes to his next class, English.

- Justin enjoys English and is engaged.

- Justin goes to Gym class and doesn't get dressed. Instead, he sits on the sidelines.

- Justin asks around if anyone can spot him money for lunch and eventually gets enough to buy lunch.

- Justin goes to algebra and goofs off with his friend. The teacher is used to it and gives them the occasional glare.

- In Economics Justin shoots rubber bands at the wall next to him, barely paying attention.

- In History, Justin works on his essay and gets help from his friend Vanessa.

- Justin rides the bus home and then goes to work.
