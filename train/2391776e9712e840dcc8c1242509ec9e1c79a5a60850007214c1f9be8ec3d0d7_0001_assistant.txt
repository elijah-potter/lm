Welcome, heroes! You have entered the hallowed halls of Terminus, where you will be starting your fortuitous journey together. As members of the Guild of Heroes, you will face numerous powerful enemies, go on amazing adventures, and make friends along the way. Your ultimate goal is to find the Legendary Cape of Azgoth. The cape is a magical artifact that, when worn, grants you the ability to travel at great speeds across vast distances. But to earn this artifact, you must fight a great evil that looms over the land, and sends hordes of enemies in front of you on the path to glory.

Your journey starts here in Terminus; you will take the Green Path, known for its wooded surroundings and overgrowth breaking through the rocky road. As you travel this path, you will make your way to the Tavern & Inn, a local establishment known to be frequented by cunning rogues and mercenaries for hire. When you get there, you will attempt to locate and meet with a rogue named Vasilis, who will give you more information about the Cape of Azgoth.

Be sure to gather the supplies you will need for your quest while in the town. Since I am both a kind and merciful Dungeon Master, I will provide you with a few necessary items to make your way to the Tavern & Inn. I have provided you with the following items: two loaves of crusty bread, two wheels of cheese, two shanks of cooked ham, eight flagons of water, one flagon of ale, and a sturdy donkey pulling a wooden cart. You will take these items that I gave you and use them as necessary, while you travel to the Tavern & Inn to meet your next point of contact.

Be ever wary for there are goblins, orcs, and wringing vines on your path that want to stop you from accomplishing your task. 

I wish you all good luck and happy hunting!
