This unit will cover the theory and practice of electrical machines and power systems as used in electrical engineering. The following topics will be covered about electrical machines: review magnetic circuits & materials, coupled circuits and dot convention; the principle of electromechanical energy conversion, transformers, direct current machines, synchronous machines, induction machines etc. the basic structure of a power system; electric power generation; single and three phase systems. The following topics will be covered about power systems: transformers, switch gear and protection, voltage drops, power factor correction, safety, earthing, protection equipment rating, per-unit system.
