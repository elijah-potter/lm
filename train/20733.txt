Video games are one of the major entertainments of the 21st century. Their success, especially among the youngest, raises questions and concerns to which this book provides answers.

Are video games responsible for violent behavior?
Can we talk about addiction to video games?

Based on recent scientific publications, this book provides the reader with reliable and accurate information on the psychology of video games.
