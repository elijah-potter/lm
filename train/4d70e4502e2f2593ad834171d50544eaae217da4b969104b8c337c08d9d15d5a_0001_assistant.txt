Here are ten adult fiction books about humanity's first contact with aliens.
1. Sleeping Giants by Sylvain Neuvel
2. The Three-Body Problem by Cixin Liu 
3. Dawn by Octavia Butler 
4. The Sparrow by Mary Doria Russell
5. The Left Hand of Darkness by Ursula K. Le Guin
6. The Forge of God by Greg Bear
7. The War of the Worlds by H. G. Wells
8. Blindsight by Peter Watts
9. The Gods Themselves by Isaac Asimov
10. Blind Lake by Robert Charles Wilson
