Here is a list I have gathered of five caves that would be ideal to explore for beginners:

1. Carlsbad Caverns, Carlsbad, New Mexico: With over a hundred limestone caverns, Carlsbad has a little bit of something for everyone. Be wary, as the main hike is physically demanding. 
2. Wind Cave National Park, South Dakota: Known for its abundance of calcite formations, this cave hosts many different geological points of interest. It is also a sacred site for the Lakota Natives of the area. 
3. Cosmic Cavern, Berryville, Arkansas: This cave hosts the largest underground lake in the Ozarks. It has won the title of Arkansas' most beautifully decorated cave. 
4. Mammoth Cave National Park, Kentucky: The length of the cave system of Mammoth Cave National Park is among the longest in the world. The limestone tunnels have over 400 miles of chambers.
5. Fantastic Caverns; Springfield, Missouri: If hiking on your own is not your style, this cave system offers an hour-long tram ride as a means of exploring the cavern.
