*"You called me lazy again. Again. I just need a minute to breathe—*" The coffee cup slipped from his trembling hand, shattering against the floor. The scent of burnt sugar and shattered glass filled the air as the phone buzzed—a text from her: *"So? You still there?"* She crossed her arms, arms that had once held his coffee, now folded over the keyboard. *"You’re not even listening to me."* His voice cracked. *"I’m listening."* The door creaked open. *"Then why’s your phone off?"* Outside, the rain drummed against the window, indifferent to the storm inside.
