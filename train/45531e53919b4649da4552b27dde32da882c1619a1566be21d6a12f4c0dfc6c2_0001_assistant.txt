1. How many children did King George and Queen Mary have?
Answer: Six children.

2. What did King George V and Queen Mary do during World War I?
Answer: He made several visits to the front, and Queen Mary visited wounded servicemen in the hospital.

3. Why did King George V change the family name from Saxe-Coburg-Gotha to Windsor?
Answer: King George V changed the family name in response to anti-German sentiment during World War I.

4. In what year did King George V become king?
Answer: 1910.

5. How and when did King George V die?
Answer: He died of pleurisy in 1936.
