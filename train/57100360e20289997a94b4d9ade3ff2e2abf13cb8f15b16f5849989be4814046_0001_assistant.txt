1/3 Star Wars: A New Hope is a fresh take on the science fiction genre. Clear inspiration has been taken from other media like Dune, however, the story here is original with interesting characters to root for and cry over. 

2/3 The film opens with a short exposition scrawl of text before getting right into the action. The antagonist is almost immediately revealed with terrifying might that even the audience fears. The film ramps up in stakes as the villain Vader's plans are eventually revealed. 

3/3 As a fun adventure into outer space, I cannot recommend this movie enough. Head to your favorite theater as soon as you can to make sure you get a seat. As a crazy old space wizard might say, may the force be with you.
