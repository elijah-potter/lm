My children begged us to take them to the Overland Zoo last week. I was in absolute shock over the ticket prices, but I wanted them to enjoy themselves. It seems as though we picked the wrong day to go. The tour guide we had was lousy and very condescending. He didn't seem to know what he was doing at all. He tried to pretend it was his first day, but I didn't buy his story. The worst thing is that hardly any of the animals were out. The zookeepers said they were sleeping, but honestly if I'm going to shell out $125 for my family to go to the zoo, I better at least see a lion. I told one of the zookeepers to go wake them up but they just laughed and walked away. You'd be better off watching nature documentaries at home instead of shelling out big bucks to visit this crummy zoo.
