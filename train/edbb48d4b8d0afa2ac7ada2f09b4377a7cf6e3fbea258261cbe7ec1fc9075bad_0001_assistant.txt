Manchester United have won a record 20 league titles. 

* 12 FA Cups, six League Cups, and a total record 21 FA Shields.
* European Cup/UEFA Champions League three times, 
* UEFA Europa League,
* UEFA Cup Winners’ Cup, 
* UEFA Super Cup, 
* Intercontinental Cup
* FIFA Club World Cup 

List of major trophies won by Manchester United in the last ten years.

1. English Premier League: 2010-11, 2012-13 Manchester United won the Premier League title in the 2010-11 and 2012-13 seasons.
2. FA Cup: 2015-16 Manchester United lifted the FA Cup trophy in the 2015-16 season.
3. EFL Cup (League Cup): 2016-17, 2019-20 Manchester United won the EFL in the 2016-17 and 2019-20 seasons.
4. UEFA Europa League: 2016-17 Manchester United clinched the UEFA Europa League title in the 2016-17 season.
5. FA Community Shield: 2010, 2011, 2013, 2016, 2017, 2020 Manchester United won the FA Community Shield, the annual match between the Premier League champions and the FA Cup winners, in 2010, 2011, 2013, 2016, 2017, and 2020.
