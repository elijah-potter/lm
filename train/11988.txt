The primary grades constitute the basic foundation of learning for every child. The curriculum at this level is so planned that the fundamentals of phonetic reading, writing, and arithmetic form the essential basis of all educational progress. History and science round out the teaching. In the upper elementary division, we seek to reinforce and extend what the child has learned.
The junior high program is geared to meet the unique needs of a child going through these important transitional years.
The high school student will not only be challenged in English and math, but he will also receive a true education in history, government, science and related subjects
As a result, Community graduates have been well prepared and well received in the colleges to which they have applied.
