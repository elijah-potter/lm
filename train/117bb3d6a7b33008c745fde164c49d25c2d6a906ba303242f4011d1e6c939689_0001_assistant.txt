1. Driving down the dark, rainy road, Zach thought about his crappy life.
2. Suddenly, the steering wheel jerked and the car made a loud thumping noise.
3. Just one more crappy thing to deal with today. 
4. Zach knew it was a flat tire.
5. He thumped along the road until he saw a driveway and pulled in.
6. The rain was pouring but he couldn't just sit here all night. 
7. Zach hopped out of the car, dashing through the rain toward the cottage at the back of the drive.
8. Knocking on the door, Zach hoped the people who lived there would let him change the tire in their driveway. 
9. The door swung open, with light pouring out onto the porch.
10. An elderly couple stood there smiling. 
11. Trying to be polite, Zach told them about his tire and asked if it was ok if he changed the tire in their driveway. 
12. They offered their barn.
13. Zach followed the drive behind the house and saw the old man opening the big doors of a huge barn.
14. He gestured for Zach to pull the car through the open doors.
15. The old woman handed Zach a cup of hot coffee and a muffin.
16. When Zach turned back to the car, the old man was already setting the jack. 
17. The two of them had the tire changed in no time. 
18. Zach thanked them over and over and told them he wished he could pay them.
19. They laughed handing him $100.
20. They told him that five miles down the road there was a diner.
21. Take this money and buy yourself dinner and give the rest to the waitress. 
22. Zach looked dumbfounded.
23. Thanking them again, he got in the car and drove out of the barn. 
24. He was crying. 
25. Zach pulled up in front of the A-1 Diner, and his wife, Tracey, hopped into the car.
26. She had worked a 12-hour shift that day and was exhausted. 
25. Zach handed her the $100 while telling her the story.
26. Now they could pay their rent tomorrow.
