This essay competition is designed to get students to think critically about writers from the past and how the world of writing shapes and influences the world as we know it today. Participating students are asked to write an essay of roughly 1,000 words that identifies their favorite writer and elaborates on that writer's influence. In addition to the writing prompt, students will be asked to adhere to formatting rules when submitting essays:

1. All essays should be double-spaced on a word processor.
2. All essays should be formatted with 1-inch margins. 
3. All essays should be formatted with first-line indentations so that the beginning of each new paragraph is indented.
4. All essays should be submitted in the form of a Word document (.docx).
The deadline for submitting essays to the competition is May 1. The first-place prize is $100.
