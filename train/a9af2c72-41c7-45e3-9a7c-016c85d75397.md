A fragmented memory of a late-night café, coffee steam swirling in an empty mug.
*"You ever think about how time just… vanishes?"* The voice was warm, familiar, but the words felt like echoes from another life.
She’d asked about the old house on the hill—its crumbling foundation, the way the wind carried whispers through the broken windows. He’d spoken of a child’s laughter, lost in the same place years ago.

The screen flickered—static, then a name: *Lena’s email*. A photo of their hands, intertwined, but the fingers were wrong. One was too long, the other… missing.

The last thing she remembered was his laugh, sharp and bright, before the door slammed shut. Now, the door was just a door.
