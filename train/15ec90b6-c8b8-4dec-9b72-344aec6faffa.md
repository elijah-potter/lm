The contract was sealed in whispered terms,
a dawn’s fragile glow between the teeth of time.
*"By midnight’s breath, the river must bend its course,
and the stone at the bridge will yield its weight to the tide."*

I traced the words in ink that had long since faded—
the penmanship sharp as a blade, the paper brittle as the bones of promises kept too soon.
The moon was a liar, always late.

They called it a test. I called it a cage.
The river gnawed at its banks, but not enough to undo the dam.
The stone stood firm, a sentinel of stubbornness,
and the tide hummed in hollow promises,
*"tomorrow."*
