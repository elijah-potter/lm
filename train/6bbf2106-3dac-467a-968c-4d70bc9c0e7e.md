**The Last Light**

The old lighthouse keeper clutched his lantern tighter as the storm howled outside.

*"Don’t go out there, old man,"* the wind whispered, *"don’t go out there."*

The keeper wiped his brow, the rain turning his beard to silver. He adjusted the brass lens, watching the waves crash against the rocks below.

*"I can’t stay here forever,"* he muttered, but the words were swallowed by the gale.

The lantern flickered. The sea turned black. And for the first time in decades, the keeper understood—some doors were meant to stay closed.
