A person the French would call "méfiant(e)" would be wary, a little reserved and suspicious, but consider him or herself as having reason to be.  "Méfiant" does not describe paranoia or neurosis; it is more of a healthy sense of cynicism.  We are not talking about a person who trusts everyone blindly.  This person would have to get to know you (or understand a situation) first before knowing how to proceed.
