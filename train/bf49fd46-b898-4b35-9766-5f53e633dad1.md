**Summer Tropics (Hawaii, Costa Rica):**
Day: 28°C–32°C (82°F–90°F) with high humidity (75–85%), frequent rain showers, trade winds (10–15 mph). Evenings mild (24–26°C / 75–79°F). Ozone layer degradation impacts UV exposure; frequent thunderstorms, but rare severe storms. Coastal breezes cool beaches by 3–5°C (9–13°F). Mosquitoes thrive in stagnant water; malaria risk in certain regions. Sunrise at ~6:30 AM, sunset ~7:30 PM. Annual avg. rainfall: 270 cm (106 in).

**Desert (Namibian Dunes):**
Day: 35°C–45°C (95°F–113°F) with dry, arid air (10–15% humidity). Temperatures drop to 10°C–15°C (50°F–59°F) at night. Wind speed: 10–25 mph (variable sandstorms). UV index: 11–13 (extreme). Evaporation rate: 2.1 mm/h. Precipitation: <100 mm/year. Fog rare; mist possible at dawn. Nighttime radiation cooling causes dew formation.

---
**Polar Basin (Antarctica, Summer):**
Day: -10°C to 0°C (14°F–32°F) with wind chill -25°C to -40°C (-13°F–40°F). 24-hour daylight (polar day). UV index: 1–3 (moderate). Air pressure: 950–980 hPa. Ice shelves calve at ~0.5 m/year. Wind speeds: 10–50 mph (gusts up to 80 mph). Sea ice extent: 10–15% of annual max. Solar radiation: 10–15 kWh/m²/day. Aurora Borealis visible at 70°N+.

---
**Alpine (Swiss Alps, July):**
Day: 15°C–20°C (59°F–68°F) at 1,500m elevation. Snowline: 2,200m. Temperature gradient: -6°C/100m. Fog common below 1,800m. Wind speed: 8–15 mph (gusts up to 30 mph). UV index: 6–8 (moderate-high). Precipitation: 100–150 mm/month. Snowpack depth: 1.2–1.8m. Nighttime temps: 2°C–5°C (36°F–41°F). Alpine meadows bloom (June–July).

---
**Tundra (Svalbard, Winter):**
Day: -15°C to -5°C (5°F–23°F). Polar night lasts 40+ days. Wind chill: -25°C to -35°C (-13°F–29°F). UV index: 1–2 (low). Sea ice thickness: 1.5–2.5m. Permafrost at 1–2m depth. Aurora Borealis: 300+ days/year. Snowfall: 50–100 cm/year. Temperature inversion: surface -20°C (warm air above cold). Sea surface temp: -1.5°C (0.5°C colder than air).
