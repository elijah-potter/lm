A quiet afternoon in the library, where the hum of pages turning and distant laughter formed the backdrop of a fleeting conversation. *"You ever think about traveling to the Maldives?"* the stranger asked, their voice barely more than a whisper. I nodded, tracing the spine of the book I’d been holding. They leaned in, eyes alight with the promise of something untouched. *"If you ever decide to go, I’ll be there waiting."* The words hung between us, unspoken but clear—unfettered by the weight of expectations, just the simplicity of a promise made in passing, a fleeting thread of connection between strangers in a room full of forgotten stories.
