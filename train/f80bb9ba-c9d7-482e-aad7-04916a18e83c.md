The clock struck midnight in the abandoned library, its dust motes swirling in the dim glow of a single candle. The last book had fallen open on the desk, its pages whispering secrets of a world that had forgotten them. The librarian, now a ghost with hollow eyes, reached for the final line of the last chapter, but the ink had faded to nothing.

Outside, the first snowflakes of winter fell silently, landing on the worn floorboards. The pages beneath them were blank, waiting. The librarian knelt, tracing the edges of the last words with a finger dusted with ash—*Begin again, and let the story unfold.*

A child’s laughter echoed from the empty stacks, their voice a promise in the silence. The librarian stood, their cloak billowing in the cold wind, and turned toward the door. The library was gone, but the air still carried the scent of old paper and forgotten dreams.

The next chapter began with the child’s name: **Elias.**
