A hollow vow carved in trembling hands,
A whispered pledge that fades like morning mist.
The weight of words too heavy to sustain—
each syllable a chain of unspoken fear.
*"I’ll return"* echoed once, but echoes linger,
while shadows stretch longer than the night.

The path was steep, the cost unseen—
each step a debt, each breath a lie.
Now silence hums where once there was sound,
a ghost of trust that never quite found.

The sky still holds the promise’s glow,
but clouds now swallow what was once bright.
No crown for those who dared to say,
and only dust where once there was clay.
