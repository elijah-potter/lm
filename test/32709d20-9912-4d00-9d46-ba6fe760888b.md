**Mayor’s Emergency Declaration Issued as Severe Flooding Threatens Central District**

Residents of New Haven’s Lower Riverfront Precinct are advised to evacuate immediately following reports of rapid water buildup along the Blackwood River. Authorities confirm the dam upstream has failed, causing a surge estimated to crest at 12-hour intervals. Emergency services report critical road closures near the downtown waterfront, with sirens activating in the affected zone. Schools and businesses have been ordered to close until further notice. Local officials urge residents to secure valuables and avoid flooded streets, as debris and power lines are expected to be submerged. Public transportation has been suspended until rescue teams assess safe routes. Floodwaters are already rising three feet above normal levels in the affected area, with officials warning of potential infrastructure collapse if the surge worsens. Residents with pets or medical needs should prioritize evacuation to high-ground shelters. Emergency supplies, including food and medical aid, are being distributed via community relief centers. The city’s emergency broadcast system will update conditions every 30 minutes.
