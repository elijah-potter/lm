**Subject: Resignation – [Your Name]**

Dear [Boss's Name],

Please accept this letter as formal notification that I am resigning from my position as Software Engineer at [Company Name], effective [Your Last Day of Employment].

This was a difficult decision, reached after considerable reflection. While I have genuinely appreciated the opportunities afforded to me during my [Number] years with [Company Name], recent developments within the engineering department have made it increasingly challenging for me to contribute effectively and maintain a fulfilling professional experience.

Specifically, the shift towards a microservices architecture, while strategically sound in principle, has been implemented without adequate investment in supporting infrastructure and developer training. The resulting complexity, coupled with the limited time allocated for thorough testing and code review, has introduced significant technical debt and increased the risk of production errors. I have repeatedly voiced concerns regarding these issues, suggesting solutions focused on automated testing pipelines and dedicated training programs, but these suggestions have not been adopted.

Furthermore, the recent decision to prioritize feature velocity over code quality, as evidenced by the reduced emphasis on refactoring and the acceptance of increasingly complex code, directly conflicts with my commitment to building robust and maintainable software.  I believe that such an approach ultimately undermines the long-term stability and success of our products.

I am committed to ensuring a smooth transition during my remaining time here. I am available to assist in training my replacement and documenting ongoing projects.  I wish you and [Company Name] the best in the future.

Sincerely,

[Your Signature]
[Your Typed Name]

**Notes for Version 1:**

*   **Specificity is Key:** This version provides very specific reasons. Ensure these reasons are genuinely accurate and defensible. Be prepared to discuss them if necessary.
*   **Professional Tone:** The language is formal and avoids accusatory or overly emotional phrasing.
*   **Documentation:**  Consider whether you have documented your concerns internally previously. Referencing that documentation (if appropriate) can strengthen your position.
*   **Legal Review:** If your reasons for leaving are contentious or involve potential legal implications, it is prudent to have this letter reviewed by an employment lawyer.
*   **Company Policy:**  Review your company’s resignation policy regarding notice periods and exit procedures.
