**Silent Overlook**
A flicker of light from a distant streetlamp cuts through the neon haze of a city square, barely registered by the commuters rushing past. The crowd’s murmur swallows the hum of a passing subway train, its low-pitched whistle lost in the din. A lone figure sits on a park bench, fingers tracing the cracks in a cracked wooden table, unaware of the tiny neon sign advertising a forgotten café across the street. The sign’s message—*"Coffee, $1"*—glows feebly against the evening’s muted glow, unnoticed until a child’s curious paw brushes it, sending it tumbling to the pavement. The world keeps turning, indifferent.
