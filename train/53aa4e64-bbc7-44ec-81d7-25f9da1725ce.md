A sudden storm delayed a scheduled flight by two hours, forcing Sarah to rearrange her itinerary. Originally planned to meet her friend in New York, she arrived at the airport 30 minutes late and was rerouted to Boston instead. There, she met Daniel at a café near the train station—a chance encounter that led to an unexpected evening of spontaneous conversation and laughter. The coincidence redirected her path, but it also connected her to someone who became her unexpected ally in navigating the next phase of her journey.
