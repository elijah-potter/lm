The stars and the moon
Out in the night sky
Are to come out soon
You'll see them up high

If you listen close 
You'll hear their faint cry
You must not be morose
Or the stars will pass you by

Shining from the night
The moon can make a sound
And if the time is right
It shall illuminate the ground

The darkness slips away
Though it surrounds us all
As night turns into day
I hear them make the call
