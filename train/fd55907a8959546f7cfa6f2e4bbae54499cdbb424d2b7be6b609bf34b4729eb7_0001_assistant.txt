Fyodor Dostoevsky was a Russian novelist, short story writer, essayist, and philosopher. His works explore human psychology in the troubled political, social, and spiritual atmospheres of 19th-century Russia. His notable works include "Crime and Punishment", "The Idiot", "Demons", and "The Brothers Karamazov". Dostoevsky's exploration of the human condition, his insight into the mind, and his innovative narrative techniques have had a profound influence on world literature.
