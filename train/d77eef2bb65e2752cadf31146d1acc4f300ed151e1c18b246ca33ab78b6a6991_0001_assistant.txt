Cheetahs communicate through different vocalizations such as chirping, stuttering, purring, and yelping. Chirping is unique to this cat species and is used by females to attract males, but it is also used by both sexes when distressed or excited. Stuttering is used by females to instruct their cubs and by males when they are excited or aroused. Cheetahs' purring, much like house cats' purring, may suggest contentment. Yelping is a loud chirp that acts as a desperate call to locate another cheetah.
