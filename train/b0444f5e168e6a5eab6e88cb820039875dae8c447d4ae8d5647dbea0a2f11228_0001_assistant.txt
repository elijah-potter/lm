A cry rang out from the jewelry store. Two crooks in ski masks had smashed the glass on the countertop and were stuffing as many items of jewelry as they could into a black bag. Before they could exit, Pizza Man stood in the doorway.
"Did somebody order a pizza?" he said triumphantly, eyeing the two thieves.
"Oh, crap. It's Pizza Man!" one of them said, shooting his hands up.
"It's just some clown in a costume," the other sneered, putting up his fists in preparation for a fight. Pizza Man scowled and clapped his hands together.
"Cheese Gush!" he yelled, and cheese shot out from his hands, pinning the man's arms in a feat of remarkable marksmanship. Pizza Man then whipped out a stale pizza crush from somewhere on his person and flicked it toward the villain, bonking him on the head.
The other crook pushed through the back door and bolted down the alley, terrified. Pizza Man gave chase. The man started to climb up the fire escape, but the intrepid hero soon cut him off by creating pepperoni platforms to ascend quickly. The thief tried to put up a fight, but Pizza Man quickly gooed him up with some cheesy projectiles.
"Well, it looks like my work here is done." Pizza Man nodded in satisfaction. He propelled himself up and away using his cheese powers as makeshift rockets.
"For justice... and pizza!" he shouted as he flew overhead.
