1. Horse hears munching in the garden.
2. He spots Rabbit and asks if he can have a bite.
3. Rabbit says, "No. Your bite is too big."
4. Cow hears munching in the garden.
5. She asks for a bite.
6. Rabbit says, "No. Your bite is too big."
7. Donkey hears munching in the garden.
8. He spots Rabbit and asks if he can have a bite.
9. Rabbit says, "No. Your bite is too big."
10. Sheep hears munching in the garden.
11. She spots Rabbit and asks if she can have a bite.
12. Rabbit says, "No. Your bite is too big."
13. Mouse hears munching in the garden.
14. He spots Rabbit and asks if he can have a bite.
15. Rabbit says, "No. Your bite is too big."
16. "But my bite is small," Mouse replies.
17. Rabbit thinks for a bit, then says, "Your bite is small."
18. Rabbit shares the garden with Mouse.
19. He finds he likes sharing.
20. He hops to Sheep. "Your bite is not too big. Come to my garden."
21. He hops to Donkey. "Your bite is not too big. Come to my garden."
22. He hops to Cow. "Your bite is not too big. Come to my garden."
23. Finally, he hops to Horse. "Even your bite is not too big. Come to my garden."
24. And they all munch happily ever after.
