A lone figure stood at the edge of an ancient forest, their cloak tattered, boots worn. The first image—a crumbling stone pillar at the forest’s entrance—loomed behind them, its surface etched with symbols long forgotten. The wind whispered through the trees, carrying echoes of their own voice, murmured in a language lost to time.

They reached the pillar, touched its weathered stone, and the symbols flared to life, painting visions of a journey that had begun in the same place. A cycle was complete.
