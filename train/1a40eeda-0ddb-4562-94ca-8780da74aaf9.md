The arena’s roped perimeter flickered with static as the referee’s wristband glitched. The crowd’s gasps turned to stunned silence when the painted lines on the mat dissolved mid-match, revealing a cracked, irregular surface beneath. A lone judge’s clipboard snapped open to reveal a handwritten rulebook with no title page, just the words *"Now, only the swiftest and the cunning may claim the prize."*

The fighter who had just landed a crushing blow to the opponent’s ribs staggered, his reflexes momentarily failing as the ground beneath him had just shifted to a slope of unknown gradient. The opponent, frozen in mid-air, realized too late that their own momentum had carried them off-course into a previously unmarked "safe zone" carved into the stone floor.

The referee, sweating under a suddenly overheated visor, handed the crowd a laminated sheet with new terms scrawled in uneven ink: **"First to cross the new boundaries, or to unseat the opponent, wins."** The match resumed with a new urgency, rules rewritten in real-time, and the crowd’s collective breath held in anticipation.
