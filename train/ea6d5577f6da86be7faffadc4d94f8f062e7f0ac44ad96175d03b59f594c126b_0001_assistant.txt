No problem, here are 3 couch cooperative games:

1. It Takes Two: A game specifically built with couch cooperative in mind, It Takes Two is the perfect game for couples as it takes the players through a story about rebuilding a relationship and having trust in one another to accomplish their ultimate goal. With a wide variety of puzzles, mechanics, mini-games, and more, It Takes Two will satisfy and hold your and your teammates' attention until the end. 

2. Overcooked! All You Can Eat: Overcooked is a frantic and challenging cooking game that can be played with up to 4 players. Overcooked will test you and your teammate's ability to multitask and work together to prepare a constant stream of meals while dodging obstacles and keeping the kitchen clean. As fun, as the game can be, be sure to remember it's only a game and meant to be fun, regardless of how frustrating managing a kitchen can be. 

3. Portal 2: Mind-bending puzzles are the forefront of what makes Portal 2 great, and experiencing the processes of problem-solving with a teammate is an extremely satisfying feeling. Work together to complete a huge number of levels with constantly evolving puzzles and mechanics that will make both of your brains work overtime.
