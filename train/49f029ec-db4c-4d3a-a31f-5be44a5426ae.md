New urban infill district emerging along a revitalized riverfront, blending adaptive reuse of repurposed warehouses with modular, energy-efficient prefab towers. Green terraces and solar-paneled walkways connect pedestrian pathways, while a central "living plaza" features dynamic LED art installations powered by kinetic energy from foot traffic. The district’s "circular economy" hub includes a shared vertical farm, algae bio-reactor, and automated waste sorting hubs, integrated into the design. Mixed-use floors house co-working spaces, micro-apartments, and community gardens, with bike lanes and elevated "skybridges" for year-round connectivity. Street-level cafés and pop-up markets thrive in the "third place" concept, while solar-powered waterfront docks support floating markets and seasonal events. The district’s AI-driven "maintenance drones" and smart lighting adjust to real-time occupancy and daylight, reducing energy waste.
