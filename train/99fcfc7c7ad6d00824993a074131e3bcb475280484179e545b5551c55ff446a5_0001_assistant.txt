In the midst of a battle, Rocket found himself desperately in need of tape. He quickly turned to Peter Quill to see if he had any but to no avail.

Peter Quill attempted to assist by asking each member if they had the tape, but all his efforts ended in disappointment. No one had any tape, not even Nebula.

Rocket, frustrated, accused Peter of lying about asking Nebula. Possessing a bag full of valuable items, including an atomic bomb, Peter realized that if anyone should have tape, it would be Rocket.

Just as the situation seemed dire, Baby Groot suddenly snatched the device and ran off while Rocket's attention was diverted. Rocket's exasperation grew as he exclaimed that he had to take care of everything.

Peter urged Rocket to stop wasting time, but when Rocket turned around, he discovered both the bomb and Groot were gone. Overwhelmed, Rocket muttered to himself that they were all going to die.
