A cracked ceramic teacup sat on the floor near the old oak table, its shattered remnants scattered across the worn rug. The wind howled through the half-open window, rattling the lace curtains. His fingers brushed the floor, knuckles numb from the cold, until they landed on a single silver coin—a quarter—half-buried under the crumbs. He picked it up, its dull surface reflecting the dim kitchen light. Outside, a car door slammed. He pocketed the coin, unnoticed.
