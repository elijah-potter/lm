Rain dowses the present on the front step. I dip to get a better look, droplets running from my gray hair. A tiny gray mouse. How ironic, it's known for cowardice, and even dead has made it further than I have in months. I squint my eyes and peer into the rain. It is a sheet of gray. No doubt, the gift giver is tucked in for the night. I wonder who he belongs to. Or if he is like me.
