Seasons and Weather Vocabulary Cards PDF file
10 pages designed by Clever Classroom.
Introduce the weather elements by asking children to identify the types of weather that you see in your region and then country and also other parts of the world. Match the weather cards to the weather that you are experiencing on any given day, during your morning work routine. Label the type of weather that you had yesterday and predict the weather they you may have tomorrow. Discuss how weather changes every season.
Your file includes common weather patterns such as sunny, partly sunny, cloudy, rain, snow, lightning, lightning and rain, cloudy, dark and cloudy, heavy showers and light showers.
Each season has its own cards (including both autumn and fall). There are also four vocabulary cards that represent basic features of each season for example, flowers, shade, swimming and hot depict summertime.
Zaner-Bloser font is the main font used in this file.
