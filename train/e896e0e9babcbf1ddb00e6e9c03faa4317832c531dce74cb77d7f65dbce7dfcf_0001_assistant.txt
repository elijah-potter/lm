Sure thing, here are 3 books about activism for young children:

1. A is for Activist by Innosanto Nagara - this clever A B C book uses words relating to activism to teach the alphabet.

2. Sweet Clara and the Freedom Quilt by Deborah Hopkinson - this book about the Underground Railroad shows how a little girl participated in helping others.

3. The Pink Hat by Andrew Joyner - this book was inspired by the 2017 Women's March and has the iconic pink hats as the only color in an otherwise black-and-white book.
