Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy pink ears and a heart full of giggles.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and hopped outside to see his favorite flower garden. The flowers were as colorful as Benny’s dreams! He sniffed the daisy and giggled, *"Mmm, this smells like sunshine!"*

Just then, Benny heard a tiny *splash*! He looked down and saw his favorite puddle, sparkling like magic. He hopped right in and splashed his paws, making bubbles that popped and giggled. *"Wow, water is fun!"* Benny thought.

A little bird named Bella flew down and chirped, *"Benny, let’s play together!"* Benny waved his fluffy tail and hopped to catch the bird in a big net made of leaves. Bella fluttered happily, and Benny laughed so loud his ears shook.

After playing, Benny and Bella snuggled under a big, soft blanket of leaves. *"Now, we’ll nap until it’s time to eat!"* Benny said. They closed their eyes and dreamed of more adventures.

And that’s how Benny and Bella learned that friends, sunshine, and puddles make the best stories ever! The end.
