Question: Which of the following statements is FALSE when it comes to group behavior amongst humans?

Choices:
a) The more humans there are who see someone in trouble, the less likely it is for that person to actually receive help. This is known as the bystander effect, where humans will assume that their help is not needed because someone else will intervene.
b) Humans tend to conform with a group in a phenomenon known as groupthink. When multiple humans gather together, each individual will prefer to go along with majority opinion instead of causing disruption, even when the majority is wrong. 
c) Humans rely heavily on categorization to help them understand the environment around them. For example, they categorize each other based on skin color, the location of their births, etc. Taken to an extreme, this often leads to a harmful phenomenon called stereotyping where individual differences are erased in favor of common group characteristics.
d) Humans select leaders for their groups based on a process called elections. This involves choosing an alpha who represents the most physically capable person in a contest of strength.
