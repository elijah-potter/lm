A **ruined city** stands beneath a vast, golden desert, its stone spires cracked by centuries of wind and sand. The **blackened arches** of a forgotten temple, once grand, now bear the patina of erosion, their inscriptions faded yet still legible in places—glossed by time with a thin layer of petrified dust. The **ancient walls**, once white, now show the colors of decay: moss in seams, rust in cracks, and the occasional **fossilized mural** peeking through layers of plaster, depicting a sky of stars and rivers that no longer flow.

At its heart lies a **silent cathedral**, its stained glass shattered, yet the **glass shards still refract light** in shifting patterns, casting prismatic shadows on the ground. The **bells that once rang** now lie buried under debris, but their weight has pressed the earth into smooth, **sculpted plinths**, like the bones of a giant buried alive. In the distance, the **ghostly echoes** of a bell toll faintly—once the city’s heartbeat, now just a whisper of its past.

The air hums with the scent of **burnt wood and old stone**, and the sun casts long shadows that seem to trace the paths of the dead. Even the **sand itself** carries stories—fossils of shells and bones embedded in its layers, telling of tides long gone and creatures that once thrived here. Some say the wind carries voices from the past, others whisper of forgotten ceremonies. But the most haunting sight is the **time capsule**—a sealed chest found half-buried, its contents still damp with moisture, including letters, coins, and a **small, tarnished mirror**, still reflecting the faces of those who once lived here. The mirror shows not your reflection, but the **shadows of their grief and triumph**, frozen in amber.
