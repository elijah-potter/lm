• 2008: Early morning fire started at Universal Studios Hollywood theme park and studio backlot in Universal City, California. It destroyed irreplaceable recordings and archived digital and video film copies.
• 2019: The New York Times reported that 120,000 to 175,000 master recordings were destroyed by the fire. This included recordings by Billie Holiday, Louis Armstrong, Chuck Berry, and Aretha Franklin.
• 2020: A federal judge dismissed the lawsuit brought by musicians against Universal that argued the Universal company was negligent in the protection of the recordings. 
• 2010: A new King Kong attraction is opened on the site at Universal.
