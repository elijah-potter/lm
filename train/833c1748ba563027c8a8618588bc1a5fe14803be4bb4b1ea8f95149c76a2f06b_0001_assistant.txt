The stock market is similar to a fundraiser for large companies and people who like those companies. A business can raise money by selling a share of its company to other people, similar to sharing a giant pizza with small slices. Buyers now directly own a share of that company, which can become more valuable in time. To sell shares in the stock market, a company must also share information about how well their business is doing, while also allowing anyone with a share to help decide how the company will be run. Companies are also known to pay small payments to anyone who owns a share, and owners can make money by selling the share to someone else eventually. Companies are happy to sell shares, due to raising money that is needed while also not borrowing money. Although a single company's stock can gain or lose money over time, the entire market as a whole has typically done well, rewarding buyers with around 10% growth each year, making it a reliable way to earn and grow your money.
