A half-empty coffee mug sits on the kitchen counter beside a half-eaten sandwich. The crumbs are scattered across the white ceramic, and a lone napkin crumpled at the corner. The kitchen light flickers, casting long shadows. The refrigerator’s light glows faintly, revealing a missing item: the carton of milk was removed earlier that morning, its label now peeling from the empty space. The owner walks in, pausing to glance at the scene before turning to the fridge—where the milk sits, unopened, its glassy surface reflecting the dim hallway light. They sigh, rubbing their temples, and start to search the apartment.
