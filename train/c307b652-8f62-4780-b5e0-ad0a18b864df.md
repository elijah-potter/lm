**Farewell to the Lost City of Zandora**
*Discovered in the 19th century, Zandora was a thriving port city in the Gulf of Arctis, now submerged beneath ancient coral reefs.*

---
### **Geography & History**
Once nestled between jagged cliffs and a fertile delta, Zandora thrived as a hub for **salt trade, rare spice imports**, and **whaling operations**. Its ruins—now a UNESCO-listed marine graveyard—include:
- **The Spire of the Moon**: A 300m iron-and-glass tower that once served as a lighthouse and observation post.
- **The Market of Whispers**: A labyrinth of arched walkways where merchants bartered in the old *Dravidian* tongue.
- **The Salt Cathedrals**: Massive evaporating pools where locals harvested crystals for trade.

Forgotten by the 1960s after a catastrophic **earthquake** (caused by tectonic shifts in the Gulf), Zandora’s legacy lives in:
- **Skeletal remains of ships** (e.g., the *SS Maris*, lost in 1892).
- **Mysterious carvings** depicting celestial events on coral reefs.
- **Local legends** of a "City Beneath the Waves" where time moves differently.

---
### **Exploring the Ruins Today**
**Access:**
- **Dive Certification Required**: Permit via the *Arctic Exploration Council* (€500/weekly pass).
- **Best for:** Hardcore divers, historians, and archaeologists. *Non-divers may only visit from above via drone surveys.*

**Key Sites:**
1. **The Drowned Harbor**
   - Sunken warehouses with **glass-walled storage silos** still intact.
   - Evidence of **flood-control canals** (now overrun by marine growth).

2. **The Shipwreck Garden**
   - A graveyard of **17th-century galleons**, including:
     - *The Golden Harbinger* (lost treasure: **golden coins, silver-plated swords**).
     - *The Leviathan’s Wake* (encrusted with **ambergris and barnacle fossils**).

3. **The Silent Temple**
   - A circular stone structure with **carvings of the Seven Winds of Zandora**.
   - Some claim it’s a **navigational compass** (though modern GPS now renders it obsolete).

---
### **What to Bring (and What to Leave Behind)**
**Essentials:**
- **Waterproof housing** (corals are sharp; avoid touching).
- **Coral-safe sunscreen** (regular sunscreen is banned—toxic to reefs).
- **Dive flashlight** (natural light is scarce; bioluminescent plankton may guide you).
- **Notepad** (records of your findings are **mandatory** for preservation).

**Prohibited:**
- **Artificial light sources** (harmful to marine ecosystems).
- **Alcohol** (banned; local fish are sensitive to toxins).
- **Photography without a permit** (some artifacts are **protected relics**).

---
### **Local Customs & Danger Zones**
- **Do:** Follow the **"Rule of Three"**—never explore beyond **three shipwrecks** without a guide.
- **Don’t:** Touch the **whalebone statues** (believed to ward off bad luck).
- **Warning:** The **Ghost Fleet** (a cluster of unmanned ships) is said to be haunted by the spirits of sailors who drowned in a storm in 1887. Avoid at night.

---
### **Why Visit?**
Zandora isn’t just ruins—it’s a **time capsule**. Here, you can:
- **Study ancient shipbuilding techniques** (their hulls are still intact, despite being 150 years old).
- **Decipher lost languages** (some carvings use a script never found on land).
- **Find the "City Beneath"** (rumors persist of a **subterranean chamber** where time is measured differently).

---
### **How to Preserve the Site**
- **Support the Zandora Marine Archaeology Foundation** (donations fund dive teams).
- **Avoid touching artifacts**—some are **radioactive** (old ship batteries).
- **Report sightings** to the *Arctic Heritage Bureau* (every discovery is logged).

---
**Final Note:**
Zandora is a ghost town, but its stories live on. The water is cold. The currents are strong. And the past is watching.
