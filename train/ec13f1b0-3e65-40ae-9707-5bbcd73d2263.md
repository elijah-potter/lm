**Stormy Rainy Night in the Hollow**
The wind howls through skeletal trees, their branches bent into jagged silhouettes against the sickly sickle moon. Thunder cracks like a whip, drowning out the distant wail of a siren. The sky is a bruise—purple, then black, then a sickly green as the first drops of rain turn to mist.

In the hollow’s center, a single lantern flickers, casting long, trembling shadows across the cracked earth. The air smells of damp earth and something older—burnt and bitter. A figure stands at the edge of the fire pit, their cloak damp, their breath misting in the cold. They clench their fists, nails digging into the palms, and the lantern’s light flares violently, then dies.

The rain comes in sheets, drumming against the muddy ground like a heartbeat. The figure’s voice is a whisper against the storm: *"You always find what you’re looking for."* The lantern’s ghostly glow flickers again, then vanishes entirely. The wind picks up, carrying with it the scent of ozone and something metallic—blood? Rust? The last thing heard before the silence is the distant echo of a child’s laughter, now swallowed by the storm.

Then—nothing. Just the hollow’s breath, heavy with the weight of forgotten things.
