A shadow slipped out of the door behind them.
The kitchen clock struck 11:59—just seconds before they’d planned to leave.
Their backpack slung over one shoulder, keys dangling from their other hand, they turned away from the table where their dog lay panting, head down.
No "see you later," no half-hearted wave.
They walked straight past the framed photo of their mother, the one with a smile that had faded years ago.
The elevator doors closed behind them with a metallic sigh, sealing the gap between what was and what was left.
