Dearest daughter:

I am writing today to let you know of my intention to move. And to a quite surprising location, I must add... It wasn’t an easy decision, but I’ve weighed all of the pros and cons, and it’s a decision that makes a lot of sense to me.

The stresses of living where I do now are too great. The cost of living here is exorbitant. The crime rate is terrible, there are heightened emotions and division among everyone here, and I just need a change of scenery. A major change.

The new place is a considerable distance from here, the home you grew up in, and it’s not likely that we will be able to visit often. (To say the least).  I have decided to relocate to the new space station on the moon! It's radical, I know - but you've always known about my fascination with space and astronomy. This is a rare opportunity that few people ever get a chance to accept.

Because we mostly only communicate via email these days, I don’t think it will make any difference to us in our daily lives. Email still works there, and we can share pictures from time to time that way. Imagine the "geo-tags" my images will have!

Transportation between the moon and Earth is very limited. This isn’t likely to change for a while. My hope is that they will refine the entire “teleportation” process sometime soon, and I would be able to come back for a visit then.

You know I love you no matter where we are; nothing is going to change in that regard. In fact, they say distance makes the heart grow fonder, and it does not get much more distant, in terms of human habitation, than Earth and the moon. If that is true, though I find it unimaginable, we should be much fonder of one another when I’ve moved to my new home!

The next time you hear from me, I will be on the moon. Until then, please take care of yourself, and know that I love you always.

Mother
