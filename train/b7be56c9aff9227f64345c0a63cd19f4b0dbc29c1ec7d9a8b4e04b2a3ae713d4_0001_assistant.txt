India and China were two of the world’s largest economies in the early 1900s. In 1500, China was the largest economy in the world, followed closely by India, both with estimated GDP’s of approximately $100 billion. France was a distant third at that time. However, by 1820, India’s GDP was 16% of the global GDP. By 1870, it had fallen to 12%, and by 1947 to 4%.

In terms of population, both countries had similar numbers in the early 1900s. However, China was more advanced than India in terms of technology and infrastructure. For instance, China had a better transportation system than India.

In conclusion, while both countries were among the largest economies in the world in the early 1900s, China was more advanced than India in terms of technology and infrastructure.
