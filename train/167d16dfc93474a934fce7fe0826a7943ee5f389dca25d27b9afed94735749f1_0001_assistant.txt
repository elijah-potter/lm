Dead Diary,

—Today was beyond stressful! It all started on the wake-up. I woke up about an hour late because I didn’t hear my alarm clock. Then as I was rushing to get ready, I realized I ran out of toothpaste and run out of the house with bad morning breath. Then after that, as I ran to the train, I saw it leave the station. I was late! So I had to wait 20 minutes for the next one. Once I actually got on my train, guess what?! My wireless headphones were dead! So I had to sit on the train and let my thoughts wander for about an hour and 10 minutes. Once I finally got off the subway train and got above ground, I came to find out now it was raining. So after I walked for about 10 minutes in the rain and I finally got to work, somehow, some way, it only proceeded to get worse! About an hour into my shift, my manager asked me to come to the back so she could speak with me. Her tone alone told me that it wasn’t going to be good. She went on for a bit about how she thought I was a good person and hard worker but that I wasn’t going to be a good fit for the business and that she would have to let me go. It took everything in me not to cry right there on the spot.
