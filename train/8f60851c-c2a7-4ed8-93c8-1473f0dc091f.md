The old lighthouse keeper clutched his lamp, watching the fog roll in like a slow, suffocating breath.

*"Don’t go out there,"* he whispered, voice cracking. *"The sea won’t let you stay."*

The captain, gripping the wheel, turned slowly. "You’ve said that every damn night for three weeks, old man. What’s changed?"

The keeper exhaled sharply. *"The light’s flickering. It’s not just the wind now—it’s the *water* that’s trying to pull me under."*

A distant horn blared. The tide was rising, swallowing the docks one step at a time. The captain didn’t wait. He slammed the wheel forward, the ship lurching forward into the dark.

Outside, the waves screamed. And somewhere, a lighthouse burned brighter than ever.
