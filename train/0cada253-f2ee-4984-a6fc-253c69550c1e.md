Once upon a time in a bright, sunny garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white ears and a big, round tail that wiggled when he was happy. One morning, Benny woke up and hopped outside to see his favorite flowers.

"Mmm, those are so pretty!" Benny said, sniffing the petals. Suddenly, Benny heard a tiny squeak. It was his friend, a little mouse named Mimi, who had lost her favorite acorn. "Oh no, Benny! My acorn is gone!" Mimi squeaked.

Benny knew just what to do. He hopped over to his big, burrow, dug a little, and pulled out a shiny, golden acorn. "Here you go, Mimi!" Benny said happily. Mimi clapped her tiny paws. "Thank you, Benny! You are the best friend ever!"

After that, Benny and Mimi played together every day. They explored the garden, shared their snacks, and always had a wonderful time. The end!
