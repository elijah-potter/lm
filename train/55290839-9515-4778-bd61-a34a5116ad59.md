A sudden alarm blared through the office, disrupting the rhythm of the afternoon meeting. The assistant rushed in, muttering about "unexpected interruption," while the CEO quickly stood, adjusting his tie as the door to the conference room burst open. A security guard, flushed and sweating, stood blocking the doorway, holding a file labeled "REDACTED: DRONE INCIDENT." The assistant’s eyes widened as the guard gestured frantically toward the windows. Outside, a small drone hovered erratically, its red lights flashing before crashing through the glass, sending shards of plastic raining onto the floor. Inside, the meeting had frozen—someone had to act.
