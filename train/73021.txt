The world of art has many things to say about music. It has always been an important part of our society causing change. Change is something that will always be a part of the world. Rap beats and hip-hop are no different..
The world of hip-hop began in the seventies in New York city. The area known as The South Bronx was the source of many block parties and thus the creation of rap beats. Rap music has changed the scene of the music industry. It has made the world closer as it has created a global stage for discussion..
Rappers are people who speak lyrics over the music that a DJ is playing. The DJ is the person who creates the music sample and then plays them from records that have been mixed together. Technology has changed a lot of this. Artists need not be confined by the hardware as the instrument can now further the art form.
The South Bronx in New York city is the birth place of this music and the art known as hip-hop. The music was a rebellion against the disco of the seventies. The ironic nature of the music is that it borrowed from those before it. In this way, it was able to incorporate other elements from various sources.
The golden age of rap came to its head in the nineties when creativity was coming from everywhere. The open door policy of letting in other ideas helped to fuel a multitude of sub genres. Many of these sub genres have created their own branches, but still show their roots back to hip-hop.
Hip-hop has grown a lot in the world. Its roots have gone from the small neighborhoods of New York to the wide branches of the world.
