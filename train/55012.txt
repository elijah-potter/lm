This powerpoint is a great wrap up for a rhyming unit. Students will see two pictures and the slide will say "Do they rhyme?" Then students will write yes or no (or a smile or sad face) on a white board. After they have a chance to response, the word yes or no will fly onto the screen. There are 8 slides like this. The last 5 slides have 3 pictures and 2 of the pictures are rhyming matches. These slides say which one does not belong. A red X will fly over the picture that does not belong!
