Not everyone likes cheese and that's okay. Luckily, there are plenty of cheese substitutes you can use in your recipes instead. Here are some examples:

1. Sweet potato sauce
2. Vegan pesto
3. Hummus
4. Tofu (silken or firm)
5: Tahini spread
6. Cashew cheese
7. Nutritional yeast (added to the other options to give a cheesy flavor)
9. Mayo
10: Unflavored yogurt
