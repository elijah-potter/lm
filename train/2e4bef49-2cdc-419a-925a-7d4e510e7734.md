Shadows of jagged stone cliffs stretch long and narrow, casting jagged, jagged patterns across ancient rock slabs. A faint, veined light filters through fissures, illuminating hollows where moss and lichen cling to damp walls. The shadows of crumbling arches and overhangs overlap, forming labyrinthine intersections. A narrow, winding path between them is barely visible, its edges softened by dappled, flickering light. Towering spires cast deep, elongated shadows that seem to spiral downward, fading into the crevices of the earth. The air hums with the distant, rhythmic sound of water trickling through unseen caves.
