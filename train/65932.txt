VTECH : Push and Ride Alphabet Train
VTech Sit-to-Stand Push and Ride Alphabet Train Make tracks with the Push and Ride Alphabet Train, teaching toddlers’ letters, numbers, colours, songs, stories and more! The Push and Ride Alphabet Train features 26 double-side letter blocks, a book with six turning pages, five colourful number buttons, one movable elephant character, a motion sensor, a ride-on carriage and a drop-space for sending the blocks from the train to the carriage. Use alone for floor play, push along as a walker, or attach to the carriage as a ride-on toy!
