Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around the yard and explore everything.

One sunny morning, Benny found a shiny red apple on his favorite flower bed. "Wow!" Benny said, his eyes sparking with excitement. He nibbled a tiny bite and felt happy.

Suddenly, Benny heard a soft *plop!* behind him. He turned around and saw a puddle of water, and inside it floated a tiny, lost star. Benny's heart melted. He scooped the star out with his paws and carried it home.

Inside, Benny's mommy was baking cookies. She smelled delicious and made Benny's tummy growl. He placed the star carefully on the kitchen table. "For you," Benny whispered.

Just then, a little mouse named Milo came running in. "Benny, Benny! I found a lost button on the path!" Milo squeaked. Benny smiled. "I found something even better!"

The star sparkled under the light, and Benny knew it was a special gift. He shared his cookies with Milo, and together, they played until the stars began to blink in the sky. Benny snuggled up with his mommy, dreaming of new adventures under the moonlight.

And from that day on, Benny and Milo always looked for treasures together, knowing that kindness could turn the simplest moments into magic.
