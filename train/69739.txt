There is no standardized answer since everyone has different interests and skillsets. However, there are some general things you can keep in mind when trying to figure out if computer science is the right field for you.
First, consider what kinds of things you enjoy doing. Do you like working with numbers and solving complex problems? Do you like creating things or working with code? If you answered yes to either of these questions, then computer science may be a good fit for you.
Second, take a look at your skill set. Do you have strong math skills and are comfortable working with computers? If so, again, computer science may be a good match for your talents and abilities.
Third, think about the kind of career path you see yourself pursuing. Do you want to work in a corporate environment or start your own company? Do you want to work on cutting-edge research projects or develop new software applications? There are many different types of careers available in computer science, so it’s important to know what sort of thing interests you most before making a decision about whether or not it’s the right field for you.
Ultimately, only YOU can decide if computer science is right for YOU. But by considering.
