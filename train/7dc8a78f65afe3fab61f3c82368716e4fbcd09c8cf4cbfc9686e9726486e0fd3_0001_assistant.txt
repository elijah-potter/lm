No problem! Here are ten insightful questions you can ask the farmer:

1. What made you decide to become a farmer?
2. What kind of chores do you have to do in the morning, and how early do you have to get up to do them?
3. What kind of machines do you use to farm?
4. Do you directly face any competition?
5. If I want to get into farming, where should I start?
6. How has your operation changed since you started farming?
7. Would you consider this a lucrative career or more of a labor of love?
8. How often do crop prices change, and how much can they fluctuate?
9. Do your crops feed local people, or do they get transported elsewhere?
10. What's the single greatest tool, machine, or item that has improved your life as a farmer?
