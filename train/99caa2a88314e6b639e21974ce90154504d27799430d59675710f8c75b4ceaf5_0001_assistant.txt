When a customer or a consumer seeks contact with a company, inbound customer service representatives are usually their first point of contact. As such, it is the responsibility of the representative to provide efficient, courteous assistance to the caller. Some established customers may simply be requesting new orders to be filled, which demands accuracy on behalf of the representative. Other callers may call with complaints, so it is imperative that the listener understands that such people want first of all to be heard, and secondly to have their issues resolved. As a person with an abundance of respect and empathy for others, along with the technical skills required to record customer orders and conversations, I am well-suited for the position of an inbound customer service representative.
