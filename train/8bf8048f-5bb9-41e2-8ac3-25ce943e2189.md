A crumbling stone bridge spans a ragged gorge, its iron railing snapped and rusted at the edges. The wind howls through jagged gaps in the cliffs below, carrying whispers of the past—fragments of laughter, the distant toll of a bell. On either side, the land descends into a vast, sunbaked plain, dotted with skeletal trees and abandoned ruins. The air smells of salt and old earth, thick with the weight of secrets the wind refuses to carry away. At dusk, the horizon bleeds into the sky, painting the edge in hues of violet and crimson, as if the world is peeling back just enough to reveal what lies beyond.
