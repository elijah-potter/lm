## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park – A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary:** This proposal requests $75,000 from the Municipal Green Spaces Fund to revitalize the currently underutilized and degraded lot at the corner of Elm Street and Willow Creek Drive into a vibrant, climate-resilient “pocket park.” This project, spearheaded by the Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (WCNA) in partnership with the City Parks Department and local nursery Evergreen Roots, addresses significant community concerns regarding stormwater runoff, urban heat island effect, and limited access to green space. The Willow Creek Pocket Park will incorporate native, drought-tolerant plantings, permeable paving, a rain garden, and shaded seating areas, creating a beautiful and functional community asset that enhances ecological health, fosters social connection, and improves the overall quality of life for all residents. We have secured $15,000 in in-kind donations and volunteer labor, demonstrating strong community commitment to the project’s success.

**1. Project Description & Need:**

The lot at Elm Street and Willow Creek Drive currently serves as an informal dumping ground and an unattractive gateway to the Willow Creek neighborhood. Its compacted soil contributes to localized flooding during heavy rainfall, and the lack of shade exacerbates the urban heat island effect. Residents have expressed a consistent desire for a safe, accessible, and aesthetically pleasing green space that promotes community interaction and provides respite from the urban environment. The proposed Willow Creek Pocket Park directly responds to this need by transforming the neglected lot into a climate-resilient oasis, mitigating stormwater runoff, reducing ambient temperatures, and providing a welcoming space for recreation and social gathering.

**2. Project Plan & Timeline:**

The project will be implemented in four distinct phases, each with clearly defined responsibilities and deliverables.

* **Phase 1: Site Assessment & Design Finalization (Weeks 1-4):** The City Parks Department's landscape architecture team will conduct a thorough site assessment, including soil testing and drainage analysis. Evergreen Roots will provide expertise on native plant selection and suitability. The WCNA will facilitate community feedback sessions to refine the park design. **Responsible Partners:** City Parks Department, Evergreen Roots, WCNA. **Deliverables:** Finalized park design plans, soil analysis report, community feedback summary.
* **Phase 2: Site Preparation & Permitting (Weeks 5-8):** The City Parks Department will oversee site clearing and grading. The WCNA will manage the permitting process with the City Planning Department. Evergreen Roots will advise on soil amendment strategies. **Responsible Partners:** City Parks Department, WCNA, Evergreen Roots. **Deliverables:** Site clearing completion, all necessary permits secured.
* **Phase 3: Construction & Planting (Weeks 9-14):** A local contractor, selected through a competitive bidding process overseen by the City Purchasing Department, will install permeable paving, construct the rain garden, and install seating areas. Evergreen Roots will provide and oversee the planting of native, drought-tolerant vegetation. WCNA volunteers will assist with planting and initial site cleanup. **Responsible Partners:** City Purchasing Department, Local Contractor, Evergreen Roots, WCNA. **Deliverables:** Park infrastructure installation complete, vegetation planted, site cleanup initiated.
* **Phase 4: Community Launch & Ongoing Maintenance (Weeks 15-20):** A community celebration will formally open the Willow Creek Pocket Park. The City Parks Department will assume responsibility for ongoing maintenance, supplemented by WCNA volunteer efforts for seasonal upkeep (weeding, litter removal). Evergreen Roots will provide guidance on plant care and maintenance practices. **Responsible Partners:** City Parks Department, WCNA, Evergreen Roots. **Deliverables:** Park opening event, established maintenance schedule, community education materials.

**3. Budget:**

The total project cost is estimated at $90,000. We are requesting $75,000 from the Municipal Green Spaces Fund, and we have secured $15,000 in matching funds, comprised of in-kind donations and volunteer labor.

* **Materials:** $45,000
    * Permeable Paving: $20,000
    * Rain Garden Liner & Gravel: $5,000
    * Native Plants & Trees: $12,000 (secured at a 20% discount from Evergreen Roots)
    * Seating (benches, picnic table): $8,000
* **Labor:** $30,000 (Contractor Costs - Competitive Bid)
* **Contingency (10%):** $9,000 – To address unforeseen expenses such as material price fluctuations or unexpected site conditions. This is crucial for project success.
* **Community Launch Event:** $3,000 (Food, entertainment, promotional materials)
* **Administrative Costs (Permitting Fees, Printing):** $3,000

The in-kind donations consist primarily of volunteer labor (estimated value: $7,500), design consultation from Evergreen Roots ($3,000), and initial site cleanup assistance from the WCNA ($4,500).

**4. Community Engagement & Equity Impacts:**

The WCNA has conducted extensive community outreach throughout the project planning process, including neighborhood meetings, online surveys, and door-to-door canvassing. The design incorporates feedback from residents of all ages and backgrounds. The park’s location was strategically chosen to serve a diverse neighborhood with limited access to green space, prioritizing equitable access to recreational opportunities. The park design is fully accessible to individuals with disabilities, complying with ADA guidelines.

**5. Risk Mitigation:**

Potential risks include contractor delays, material price increases, and adverse weather conditions. To mitigate these risks, we have included a contingency fund, established clear timelines with contractors, and secured commitments from Evergreen Roots to provide timely plant delivery. The WCNA will actively monitor project progress and address any emerging issues promptly.

**6. Post-Grant Sustainability:**

The City Parks Department will assume responsibility for ongoing park maintenance, ensuring long-term sustainability. The WCNA will establish a “Friends of Willow Creek Pocket Park” group to organize volunteer cleanup events, fundraising activities, and community programming. Evergreen Roots will provide ongoing technical assistance to the WCNA regarding plant care and maintenance.

**7. Conclusion & Call to Action:**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park represents a transformative opportunity to revitalize a neglected space, enhance community well-being, and build climate resilience. This project directly addresses critical community needs and aligns with the Municipal Green Spaces Fund’s objectives. We are confident that this project will serve as a model for future green space initiatives and create a lasting legacy for the Willow Creek neighborhood. We respectfully request your approval of this proposal and the allocation of $75,000 to bring this vital community asset to fruition.
