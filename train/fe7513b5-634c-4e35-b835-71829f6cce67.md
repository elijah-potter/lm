*Clink of ice cubes against glass*
**Bitter citrus**—zest scraped raw from an orange peel—
**whispers** of damp earth beneath cracked sidewalk tiles,
**rust** and **old iron** from rusted gates left ajar,
**hum** of distant traffic, **dust motes** caught in morning light,
**steam** curling from a forgotten coffee pot,
**cigarette ash** still warm on a weathered porch,
**cold concrete** under bare feet, **moss** singing in the rain.
**Metal** groans underfoot, **paint** peeling like old stories—
**fresh bread** wrapped in newspaper, **warm breadcrumbs** on the wind,
**petrichor** and **petrol**, **candle wax** dripping slow.
