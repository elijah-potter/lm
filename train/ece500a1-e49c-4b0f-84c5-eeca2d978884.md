A slow knock at the door—soft but persistent. The air in the hallway was thick with the scent of rain on concrete. The door creaked slightly as it swung inward, revealing a gaunt figure in a long coat, their face half-hidden by a scarf. They stepped into the dimly lit entryway, their shadow stretching long across the floor. The light from the hallway flickered, casting jagged patterns across their worn boots.

Inside, the house was silent except for the distant hum of an old refrigerator. The figure paused, studying the living room where a lone candle burned low on the coffee table. Their gaze lingered on the empty chair by the fireplace before they turned, their voice barely audible over the wind outside.

*"You always answer when you’re supposed to."*
