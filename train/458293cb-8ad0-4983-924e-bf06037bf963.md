A vast, cavernous chamber with low, curved walls of polished obsidian, their surface etched with faint, glowing veins of light that pulse in slow, hypnotic patterns. The air is thick with an unnatural stillness, carrying no scent but the faint metallic tang of forgotten machinery. The ceiling stretches endlessly, punctuated by jagged, rusted supports that bear the weight of an unseen structure above. Scattered across the floor are remnants of discarded equipment—broken consoles, shattered display panels, and the skeletal remains of what were once functioning systems. In the center stands an empty throne of blackened metal, its surface etched with symbols that shift when viewed from different angles. Shadows stretch long and unnaturally, pooling around the edges of the space, as if something is both present and absent at once. The absence of movement in the void feels suffocating, the silence so profound it vibrates like a pulse in the bones. The walls whisper, though no one is there to hear.
