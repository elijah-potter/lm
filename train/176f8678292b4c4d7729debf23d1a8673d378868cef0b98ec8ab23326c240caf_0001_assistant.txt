Got it, here are some things you and your family could do in Branson: 

- OLD TIME PHOTO: Talk about a great way to create memories, Old Time Photo offers hours of entertainment and keepsake photos of your fun! Your family can dress up and pose with props that will transport your back in time. Pricing is fairly inexpensive and special coupons are sometimes available for even more savings.
- RUNAWAY MOUNTAIN COASTER: If your family enjoys adventure you'll want to check out the Runaway Mountain Coaster in Branson. Unlike many roller coasters, you control the speed here! Plus, it's only $16 for the driver and $10 for each rider. 
- RIPLEY'S BELIEVE IT OR NOT: This is a great indoor option for inclement weather, but is just as fun to visit on a sunny day! The museum is full of weird and wonderful oddities. The price range for admission is $13.99 - $22.99 depending on age. 
- PROMISED LAND ZOO: Since your family enjoys animals, consider visiting the Promised Land Zoo where you can even feed the birds. It's been voted one of the top 25 zoos in the country, and admission ranges from $14.95 - $21.95.
-REZA: EDGE OF ILLUSION: If you're looking for a family-friendly show to see on your trip, consider Reza: Edge of Illusion. The jaw-dropping tricks are unforgettable and considered worth the price at $13 for ages 4-11 and $35.99 for ages 12 and up.
- DOLLY PARTON'S STAMPEDE: This dinner and show combo is quite popular with visitors. You'll enjoy a delicious meal and marvel at the exciting horse show. Prices range from about $30 - $55 a person. 
- AMAZING ACROBATS OF SHANGHAI: Here's another family show to consider, the Amazing Acrobats of Shanghai dazzle the audience with their costumes, stunts, and pizzazz! Admission prices are $20 - $40 and military discounts are available. 
- SILVER DOLLAR CITY: While not the cheapest option on the list, Silver Dollar City has a lot to offer. From live demonstrations to rides, this theme park is often considered a must-see. Ticket prices range from $58 - $68 a person.
