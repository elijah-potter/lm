What a great idea! Here are some name suggestions for your dog walking and food delivery business:

    * Paws & Plates: Short, catchy, and memorable name. It puts the focus on the dogs and the food being delivered.

    * The Hungry Hound: Cute and memorable name, emphasizes the idea that dogs are always hungry (especially by using the hound dog as an example) smelling the yummy food you are delivering them.

    * Woof & Dine: Playful and catchy name; suggests your business is all about having fun with dogs while you are delivering food to hungry humans.

    * The Gourmet Pooch: Cute and playful name, suggests that you and the dog(s) you are walking are delivering them only the best, high-quality food.

    * The Foodie Pup: Another catchy and memorable name, emphasizes that you and the dogs you walk are providing delicious meals that will satisfy a picky foodie.

    * Daily Doggie Delivery: Simple and straightforward, suggesting your business offers daily dog walks and food delivery.
    
    * K9 Carryout: Catchy and straightforward, effectively communicating the idea of dogs helping to deliver food.

    * Walk & Feed Co. - Somewhat literal and easy to understand, communicates the main services offered by the business. It's easy to remember.

    * Leash & Lunch - Uses alliteration to create a catchy and easy-to-remember name, and incorporates the word "lunch," which communicates the food delivery aspect of the business.

    * Woof & Dine - This name is playful and incorporates a pun, which could appeal to pet owners who want a service that's a little more lighthearted.

I hope one of these suits your needs!
