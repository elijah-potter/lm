Once upon a time in a cozy garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had a soft brown fur and big, round eyes that sparkled like tiny stars.

One sunny morning, Benny was hopping around, munching on clover. Suddenly, he heard a tiny voice, "Help! Help! My flower is broken!"

Benny looked and saw a tiny mouse named Mimi sitting next to a beautiful red rose with one petal missing. "Oh no!" Benny said, "Let's fix it together!"

Benny and Mimi worked hard. With their little paws, they carefully picked the petal and put it back on the rose. The flower looked even more beautiful than before.

Mimi smiled, "Thank you, Benny! You're the best friend ever!"

From that day on, Benny and Mimi were the best of friends. They spent their days playing, exploring, and taking care of the garden together. Benny loved telling stories and Mimi loved counting flowers. Every day was an adventure filled with kindness and friendship.

The end.
