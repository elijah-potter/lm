Collin has 25 flowers. Ingrid gives Collin a third of her 33 flowers. If each flower has 4 petals, how many petals does Collin have in total?
Ingrid gives Collin 33 / 3 = <<33/3=11>>11 flowers
Now Collin has 25 + 11 = <<25+11=36>>36 flowers
Since each flower has 4 petals, Collin has a total of 36 * 4 = <<36*4=144>>144 flowers
