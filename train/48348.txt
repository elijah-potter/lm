Coral beads are a rare type of bead that is gathered from the bottom of the ocean. Divers usually need to submerge themselves in water that can range in depths of twenty five to one thousand feet to excavate the coral from the area. The most coveted of all coral beads are red in color.
The reason why these beads are coveted is because red colored coral beads are extremely rare. Because coral is also harvested for other reasons outside of beads, the red beads find it difficult to survive in the waters depths. The only way that the red colored coral survives is if it is kept at a certain temperature.
Usually the temperature for these beads to survive has to stay between the median of thirteen and sixteen degrees Celsius. The biggest producer of coral products and coral beads is Italy. Remember, that coral is not always formed into beads for jewelry making; this material can also be used for larger products such as walking sticks.
Even though it is possible to obtain coral beads that are many different colors, the most renowned color is red. The red color beads with all of their allure are a little bit more expensive than all of the other colors of beads.
The Mediterranean Sea is the biggest harvester of coral. However, this may possibly change in the near future, because red coral has recently been located along the coast of Hawaii. If excavators were able to retrieve an abundance of red coral from the area, the asking price of red coral may eventually drop.
Aside from red colored coral, you can also obtain coral beads in shades of blue, black and green. But, these different colored beads do not match the popularity of the red colored coral bead. We will just all have to wait and see if the coveted red coral bead is going to decrease in value.
