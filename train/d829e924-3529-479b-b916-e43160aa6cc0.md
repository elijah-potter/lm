**"The wind hummed a tune that never stopped—
once it began, it would not let go.
It whispered, *"The sky is vast, the air is free,
repeat, repeat, the world is one."*

Outside, leaves danced in a pattern so clear,
each one a note, a whisper, a prayer.
The sky stretched on forever, endless blue,
while time itself seemed caught in a loop—

*"Begin again. Start anew.
The same words, the same view.
A circle spun, a cycle spun,
the wind would never let us go."*

In the heart of the storm or the quiet dawn,
the phrase returned like a ghost that stayed:
*"Repeat. Repeat. The world is one."*"

The poet sat, pen in hand, but the phrase
was carved into the walls, the rivers, the trees.
No one could escape its pull—
the wind had won, the words had seized.
*"Repeat. Repeat. The world is one."*
