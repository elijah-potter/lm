A dimly lit alley between two buildings hummed with the sound of distant traffic. Rain glistened on the cracked pavement, reflecting the flickering glow of a streetlamp. The figure on the other side of the fence moved slowly, their posture rigid—shoulders hunched, hands clenched at their sides. They didn’t speak, but the way they watched the corner of the street told the story.

A child’s laughter drifted over the fence, light and careless. The figure didn’t react. Then, a car horn blared—sharp, unexpected. The child’s giggle cut off. The figure’s breath hitched.

A beat passed. The child’s voice, muffled through the fence, called out: *"Dad?"* The figure didn’t turn. The sound of footsteps, heavy and deliberate, approached.

The child’s voice was softer now: *"Why are you here?"*

Silence.

A single word, unspoken, hung between them: *regret*.
