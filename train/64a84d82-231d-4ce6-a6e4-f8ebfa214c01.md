A quiet afternoon in a dimly lit office, fluorescent lights buzzing overhead. The air smelled faintly of old paper and coffee. Across the room, a woman sat hunched over her laptop, typing furiously. Her hands moved with practiced efficiency, but her eyes never left the screen.

Then, she noticed it.

A single, unnoticed detail—a faint line of dust on the edge of her monitor, barely visible in the dim light. She leaned forward, fingers trembling slightly as she reached out, brushing it away with her thumb. The moment she did, her expression shifted. A flicker of something unreadable crossed her face—curiosity, maybe, or something deeper.

She looked up at the ceiling, then at the wall, then back at her screen. Her fingers paused mid-tap.

"Huh," she muttered.

The moment lingered in the air like an unspoken question. No one else had noticed. No one else cared. But for her, that dust became something else—an opening, a chance, a spark in an otherwise ordinary day.
