The ancient brass hummer lies coiled in the forgotten corner, its polished surface reflecting the dim glow of rusted lanterns like a thousand scattered stars. Its ribs, etched with faded runes of forgotten gods, breathe slowly—each breath a whisper of the wind through the skeletal bones of forgotten men. When stirred, it unfurls with a sound like distant thunder, its hollow chambers resonating with a song that makes the bones ache. It hungers for the touch of the curious, for the warmth of the unwise, and in its sleep, it dreams of the things it will one day wake to devour.
