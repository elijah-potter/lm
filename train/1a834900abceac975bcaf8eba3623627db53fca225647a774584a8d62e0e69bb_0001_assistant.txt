Politics' origins are hard to trace to the ultimate beginning, but basically, as long as humans have existed, so has politics.  Politics has roots all the way to the earliest known civilizations, including ancient Greece, Egypt, and the Roman Empire.  These societies came up with elaborate systems of governing, with laws regulating allowed behavior, to keep their cities and communities functioning safely and smoothly.  At its basic and most primitive form, politics was the art of managing the affairs of the community.

As populations grew and become increasingly complex and diverse, they also began to interact more often with each other.  This was a catalyst for exponential growth in the field of political science, giving rise to things like foreign policy, diplomacy, and especially warfare.  The emergence of new empires and nations created new political systems, such as republics, monarchies, and democracies.  Of course, in each political arena, there were many ideas as to what was best for everyone, which also led to new ideologies, including conservatism, socialism, and fascism. Many of these ideas still exist today in some form.
