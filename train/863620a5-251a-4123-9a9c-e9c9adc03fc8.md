**New Year’s "Light Reflection" Ceremony**
In a quiet village, residents gather after midnight on December 31st to hold lanterns over still water, reflecting on past year’s lessons. Each person writes a single word on a paper strip and drops it into the water. Observing the ripples, they predict the next year’s challenges. The loudest ripple wins a community potluck dinner. The tradition blends mindfulness with playful competition, fostering collective introspection.
