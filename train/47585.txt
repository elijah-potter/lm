CLT is a universal building material for walls, ceilings and floors. Structural and load-bearing capabilities of the product make the material universal in the design and subsequent implementation of the project.
As an element with high static properties, the material can absorb and safely distribute excessive vertical pressure. Therefore, CLT has high bearing capacity that allows to build even high-rise buildings. At the same time, solid wood houses do not carry the internal load and are 30% lighter than steel and concrete structures.
The Ledinek and Hundegger lines installed at the Rezult House plant can produce panels up to 18 meters long, 3.5 meters wide and up to 300 mm thick.
