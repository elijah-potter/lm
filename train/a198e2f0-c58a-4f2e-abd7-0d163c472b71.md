A cavernous chamber with high domed ceilings, its vast expanse illuminated by flickering, dimly glowing stalactites dripping crystalline runes. The air hums with static electricity, charged by ancient storms trapped in the rock. No sound carries—only the occasional distant whisper of wind through fissures, though no breeze stirs. Shadows stretch endlessly, pooling like ink in the corners. The walls pulse faintly, shifting colors like slow, breathing flesh, as if the space itself is alive and holding its breath. Every empty space feels like a gaping wound, waiting to be filled. The walls exhale heat in slow, rhythmic pulses, and the temperature rises inexplicably, as if the air is too thick to breathe.
