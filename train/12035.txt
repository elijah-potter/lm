Engage students with this fun, interactive addition game. In this mini powerpoint game, students read winter themed word problems and solve them. Each word problem has 3 addends ranging from 0 to 10. There are ten questions and you just click on each question to go to it. The question disappears after you've clicked on it so you know you've answered it. Great for a guided math center or winter day activity. Great for individual practice on the iPad! Extra licenses are $0.75.
