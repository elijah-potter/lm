## Lost in the Lavender Fields (Sonoma, CA)

The late afternoon light was particularly generous on Sunday, bathing the lavender fields just outside Healdsburg in a soft, golden hue. I was sketching the rows of purple blossoms, attempting to capture their fragrance and form on paper, when you appeared. You were walking a scruffy terrier mix, its fur the color of burnt caramel, and paused near my easel. We exchanged a brief, shy smile, and you commented on the intensity of the bees buzzing amongst the flowers – a constant, drowsy hum that permeated the air. 

I remember noticing the way the sunlight caught the silver threading through your dark hair, and the quiet kindness in your eyes. We spoke for perhaps three minutes, a fleeting conversation about the beauty of the landscape and the restorative power of nature. Then, as quickly as you arrived, you continued your walk, disappearing amongst the rows. I’m hoping you saw the sketch I was working on. If you were with a caramel-colored terrier and recall a woman with charcoal smudges on her fingers, please respond with the breed of your dog.
