

Standard home inspections are most frequently completed before the sale of a home or whenever the owner or occupant changes. These inspections address any visible issues the home has and most of the time, you’ll receive a report of the issues that were found and an estimated repair cost.

An annual inspection, on the other hand, is just what it sounds like. It’s an inspection of your home every year to ensure that everything is still in good condition. It’s essentially a yearly check-up for your home. Maintenance inspections are done by the owner or occupant for their own information. Unlike standard home inspections, maintenance inspections are done as a preventative measure.

Both inspections will typically look at the same things, but with a maintenance inspection, the homeowner is right there to give the go-ahead for a closer look at potential issues. With a standard home inspection, the inspector will only look at the surface level.
