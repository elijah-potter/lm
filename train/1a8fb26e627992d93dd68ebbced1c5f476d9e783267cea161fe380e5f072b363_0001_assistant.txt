Sure! Here is your list of three places you should visit in the Outer Banks to take pictures.

*Currituck Banks Reserve- Here you can take spectacular pictures along two trails that lead to the coast of Currituck Bay. The boardwalk is less than half of a mile. You will find impressive oak trees to photograph. If you are lucky, you may even spot the wild horses that roam the Outer Banks.
*Wright Brothers National Memorial- This is the location of the first successful flight. There is a life-sized sculpture of the first flight that would make for an excellent photo opportunity. 
*Elizabethan Gardens- This is the conservation site for what was believed to be the first American settlers.
