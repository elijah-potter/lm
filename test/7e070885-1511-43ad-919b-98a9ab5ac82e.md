**The Last Transmission of Engineer Elias Voss**

The hum of the generator was the only sound in the control room, a steady, rhythmic pulse like the heartbeat of a dying man. Elias Voss wiped his brow with a sleeve stained dark with engine oil and dust. The console flickered, casting eerie shadows on the wall behind him. Outside, the airlock hissed as the final crew member, a grizzled mechanic named Kael, sealed the hatch behind them.

*"Last log entry, Elias. The systems are stable—mostly. The oxygen recyclers are on the fritz again. That’s not normal. Not for a ship this size."* Kael’s voice was raw, the kind of voice that had seen too many dead bodies and not enough repairs. Elias exhaled sharply, his breath fogging the glass of his helmet.

The console beeped. A critical alert blinked in red: *"Thermal breach in Sector 7. Core temperature rising."* Elias’s fingers hovered over the panel. He had spent years fixing these things, but this wasn’t just a fix—it was a last-ditch effort. The ship was old, but not broken. Not yet.

He keyed the comms. *"Kael, you’ve got two minutes. Get the coolant pumps running—now."* The line went dead.

The alarm screamed.

Elias turned to the viewport. The black void of space stretched endlessly, but his eyes locked onto the flickering light of the *Aurora’s* core—one last pulse before the fire took it.

He didn’t wait. He ran.
