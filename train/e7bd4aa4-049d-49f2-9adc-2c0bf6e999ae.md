**The Ghosts of Blackthorn Hollow**

A mist-shrouded valley where the old oak trees whisper secrets to the wind.
The ruins of a forgotten chapel, its stone crumbling but its stained-glass windows still glowing faintly under moonlight.
Local legends speak of a lantern that never goes out—left behind by travelers who vanished in the fog.
Children still play hide-and-seek in the hollow, though no one knows why the place feels *alive*.
The river runs cold here, reflecting nothing but the past.
