**Unspoken Pact Between Shadows**
A silent treaty, woven in the dark,
Where words are lost to the hollow air—
**No names**, no contracts, just the weight
Of hands that never shake, yet hold the light.
The promise whispered, half-remembered, half-taken—
Not inked in blood, but traced in stolen glances.
What’s owed is never said aloud,
But the debt is written in the way
The air hums when two souls meet—
A current that neither dares to name,
Yet both know, in the stillness, it’s real.
