There are between 9 and 10 million travelers to Hawaii annually, and many of them are repeat visitors for anniversaries, romantic getaways, or family vacations. Hawaii is one of the United States’ most popular vacation destinations, and here are a few reasons why: 

•	The weather is beautiful all year. 

Hawaii is separated into eight islands, and each one experiences its weather patterns. However, the weather is predominantly sunny and beautiful on every island. Hawaiian winters rarely dip below 80 degrees Fahrenheit, even though that’s considered the rainy season. Summer temperatures average between 85 to 90 degrees Fahrenheit. 

•	There’s a focus on the history and culture of the islands. 

Hawaiians are proud of their histories and cultures, so there’s a lot to learn from locals. There’s folklore galore and shows that involve live entertainment and traditional Hawaiian meals. 

•	The food is delicious and abundant. 

While it’s true that spam is a huge part of Hawaiian cuisine in many diners and breakfast cafes, there are also traditional Hawaiian meal options, like poke bowls, chicken luau, and kalua pork. And all of those foods are served in heaping portions. 

During live entertainment nights, there may be a full pig roast, served with pineapple, rice, lomi lomi salmon, and huli huli chicken. Additionally, there are fusion restaurants that combine authentic Hawaiian, Filipino, Japanese, and Chinese fares. 

From exploring the volcanoes and beaches of the Hawaiian Islands to sampling the foods and learning Hawaiian lore and culture, there are an abundant amount of reasons why Hawaii is an ideal vacation destination. There’s no shortage of things to do, people to meet, and amazing sights to see.
