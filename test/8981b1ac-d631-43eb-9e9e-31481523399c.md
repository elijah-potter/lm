Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had soft, fluffy brown fur and big, round eyes that sparkled like sunshine.

Every morning, Benny would hop outside to play. The grass was soft and green, and the flowers smelled sweet. Benny loved to roll in the daisies and chase tiny butterflies.

One sunny afternoon, Benny met a little squirrel named Sammy who lived in a tree. "Hello, Benny!" Sammy chirped. "I see you’ve got a basket full of acorns!"

Benny grinned. "I’m collecting acorns to share with my friends!" He picked up a shiny golden one and giggled. "This one looks special!"

Sammy laughed. "Maybe it’s a treasure!"

Just then, Benny’s favorite friend, a wise old owl named Ollie, swooped down. "Hush now, little ones," he hooted softly. "The garden is full of magic. Remember to be kind, and the world will grow even brighter."

Benny and Sammy clapped their paws. "We’ll tell everyone!" they said together.

From that day on, Benny and Sammy helped plant seeds, shared treats, and made sure everyone in the garden felt happy. The flowers grew taller, the bees buzzed happily, and the animals played together, laughing and smiling all day long. And that’s how Benny and his friends made the world a little bit magical.
