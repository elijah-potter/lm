The old clock in the town square struck midnight every New Year’s Eve, its chimes echoing through the alleyways. Elias adjusted his gloves, his breath fogging in the cold air as he watched the fireworks burst overhead.

"Another year," he muttered, shivering. "Another year we’re still here."

The streetlamps flickered, casting long shadows on the cobblestones. Across from him, Mara clutched her coat tighter, her voice barely audible over the distant crackle of the crowd.

"It’s not just the years," she whispered. "It’s the *us* that keeps getting lost in the noise."

Elias turned to her, his eyes glistening. "Like the clock."

Mara tilted her head. "Like the clock."

The fireworks exploded in a blaze of colors, but Elias only watched her. "We don’t have to wait for the next midnight."

She smiled. "I know."

They stood there, silent again, the weight of the past pressing between them like a second skin.
