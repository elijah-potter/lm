In a town involved in mysteries, there is one nobody could unveil. What is behind the white door in the librarian's?
A family moved to this town to start a new life and leave the past behind, but from the very beginning, they sense something isn't right in this place. They risked all in this new home so they can't afford to go to another place.
At first, they think they aren't welcome. Every habitant in the town is rude to them and asked them to leave, but they can't. So one day a little girl told them about the white door at the librarian's. 
Upon hearing what the little girl said, they started to ask questions, but nobody would answer. Soon they started to investigate and quickly found out that the reason why everybody was rude to them was that when you know about the white door you can't leave the town anymore and you quickly become obsessed with what is behind the white door, and the only way to stop being obsessed is for one of the family members enter the door.
They think the townspeople are playing games with them, but soon they realize they can't sleep at night or think straight during the day. 
One night the father goes to open the door to prove that it is all a game, and the father opened the door.
