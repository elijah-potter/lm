Baseball is about the bat on the ball
Starting in Spring with playoffs in Fall
Beautiful stadiums with fresh-cut grass
Pitchers throwing heat and homerun blasts

What about hockey? The best winter sport
Played on an ice rink and not on a court
High speeds on skates and pucks on sticks
Can even play outside as long as the ice is thick

Soccer is the sport of Europe with plenty of fans
Players use their feet, only goalies can use their hands
Massive stadiums with huge grassy fields
European teams with club logos on shields

American football is last but certainly not least
Perfect on Thanksgiving, alongside a big feast
Full of great catches, passes, and big-time hits
Big linemen and linebackers with tons of grit
