**Map of Eldoria: The Obsidian Spire District**
*(Key Locations & Hazards)*

**Central Hub:**
- **The Hollow Market** (Trade hub, smugglers' den)
  - *Notable:* Black-market stalls sell cursed relics.
  - *Warning:* Corrupted alchemists brew poisonous elixirs.

**Northern Branch:**
- **The Whispering Forest** (Vegetation shifts at night)
  - *Details:* Ancient roots pulse with bioluminescent veins; sentient mushrooms whisper in dead languages.
  - *Path:* Follow the "river of tears" (a stream that reflects past events).

**Eastern Sector:**
- **The Obsidian Spire** (Monolith core: "The Eye of Vorthas")
  - *Structure:*
    ```
    ┌───────────────────┐
    │   ████████████   │
    │   │  ██  ██  ██  │  (Lobby: Echoes of fallen adventurers)
    │   │██  ███████████│  (Floor 2: Vault of the Archmage)
    │   │  ███████████  │  (Floor 3: Prison of the Voidwalkers)
    │   └───────────────┘
    ```
  - *Access:* Requires the **"Key of the Fallen"** (lost in the Hollow Market).

**Southern Route:**
- **The Bone Bridge** (Collapses every 7th day)
  - *Traversable:* At dawn/sunset; only for those carrying a "candle of fire" (see *Lobby*).

**Cave Entrance (Southwest):**
- **The Maw of Ygg** (Labyrinth of shifting tunnels)
  - *Landmark:* A single **black crystal** pulses near the center (harbor for the Voidwalkers).

---
**Critical Notes:**
1. **Cursed Items:** Any relic with a "blood price" (e.g., "Dagger of the Last King") demands a sacrifice.
2. **Guards:** Patrols rotate between "The Hollow Market" and "The Bone Bridge."
3. **Objective:** Retrieve the **"Core Fragment"** (hidden in the Spire’s Vault) to break the curse plaguing Eldoria’s northern plains.

---
**Starter Task:**
*"Begin at The Hollow Market at dusk. Ask for directions to the Whispering Forest’s "first root." Bring only a lantern."*
