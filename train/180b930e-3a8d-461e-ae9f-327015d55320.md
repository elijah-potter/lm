Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big yellow door, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around his garden, where colorful flowers swayed gently in the breeze.

One sunny morning, Benny found a tiny, shimmering egg near his favorite daisy. "What’s this?" he wondered, picking it up. As Benny turned it over, it began to crack open, and out popped a little chick with bright yellow feathers. "Hello there, Benny!" chirped the chick, who introduced herself as Peepa.

"Peepa, are you lost?" Benny asked. Peepa fluttered her wings. "No, Benny! I just wanted to say hello and find my family." Benny knew just what to do. He built a little nest with soft grass and feathers, and soon, Peepa’s family—Mama Cluck, Papa Rooster, and baby chicks—arrived!

The garden became even more magical with the chicks. Benny and Peepa became best friends, and together, they helped the family explore their new home. Benny taught Peepa how to hop, and Peepa showed Benny how to cluck. Every evening, they all snuggled under the stars, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures.

One day, Benny spotted a shiny, twinkling star in the night sky. "Look, Peepa!" he said, "Isn’t that a wish for us?" Peepa nodded. "Together, we can make magic happen!" Benny and Peepa made a wish to have a garden full of kindness and laughter. And with a happy giggle, they drifted off to sleep, dreaming of their next adventure.

The end.
