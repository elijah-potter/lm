In a shifting sandscape of **Ephemeris Delta**, time and space dissolve into a chaotic flow. Rivers carve temporary paths, erasing yesterday’s cliffs into tomorrow’s dunes. The **Flickering Horizon**—a shifting archipelago of drifting islands—reflects in puddles that vanish before you can name them. Even the air hums with whispers of past and future, where gravity is a fleeting suggestion, and gravity itself is a story told in microseconds. Here, permanence is a myth; everything—from the bones of old storms to the echoes of laughter—fades into the **Unstable Vortex**, a swirling vortex where the past and future bleed together.

A **Living Mirage** glows in the distance: a city of glass and shadows that shifts like smoke, its buildings rearranged mid-nightfall. The **Clockless Clock** at its center ticks backward, and those who listen too long hear the last word of every language ever spoken, all at once. Some say if you stand still long enough, you’ll see the moment before the first breath, or the last breath of the universe. Others swear they’ve heard their own name spoken by a stranger who wasn’t born yet. The **Echo Tides** lap against the shore, carrying voices from the edges of time. Here, the only constant is change—and the terror of being forgotten before you’re even known.
