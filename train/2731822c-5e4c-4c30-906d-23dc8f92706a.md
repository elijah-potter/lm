Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the meadow. One sunny morning, Benny found a shiny red berry. He nibbled on it and felt happy, but soon, he heard a tiny voice: "Help!"

Benny looked down and saw a little bird with a broken wing. The bird flapped its wings and cried, "I need your help!"

Benny hopped over and asked, "Can I help you, my friend?" The bird nodded and said, "Thank you! I can teach you how to fly if you want!"

Benny was very excited. He practiced flying with the bird every day. Soon, Benny's wings grew strong, and he could fly like a bird!

The bird said, "You are so brave and kind!" Benny knew he was, and he flew high into the sky, enjoying the sunshine and the wind.

From up there, Benny saw his friends playing below. He hopped down and said, "Look how far we can fly together!" Benny and his friends became the bravest and kindest friends in the meadow, sharing berries, singing, and playing games under the big blue sky.
