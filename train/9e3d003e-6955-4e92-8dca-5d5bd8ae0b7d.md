## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park – A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary**

The City of Oakhaven faces increasing challenges from intensified rainfall events and rising summer temperatures, impacting the well-being of its residents, particularly those in the historically underserved Willow Creek neighborhood. This proposal seeks $75,000 in municipal funding to transform a currently neglected and underutilized half-acre parcel at the corner of Elm Street and Willow Creek Drive into the Willow Creek Pocket Park – a climate-resilient community asset. The park will incorporate permeable paving, native plantings, a rainwater harvesting system, and shaded seating areas, providing vital stormwater management, urban cooling, and a welcoming green space for recreation and social connection. This project directly aligns with the City’s Climate Action Plan and supports its commitment to equitable access to green infrastructure. We are prepared to contribute $25,000 in volunteer labor and in-kind donations, demonstrating strong community ownership and ensuring project longevity. We request your approval to proceed with this vital initiative, enhancing the quality of life for Willow Creek residents and serving as a replicable model for future green infrastructure projects throughout Oakhaven.

**Project Plan & Timeline**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park transformation will unfold in four distinct phases, each with designated responsibilities and measurable deliverables.

**Phase 1: Planning & Design (Weeks 1-4) – Responsible Partner: Greenleaf Landscape Architects; Deliverables: Finalized Park Design, Permitting Approvals.** Greenleaf Landscape Architects, a firm with extensive experience in sustainable design, will lead the initial planning phase. This includes a detailed site assessment, community consultation workshops (documented in meeting minutes), and the development of a final park design incorporating climate-resilient features.  The design will adhere to all relevant municipal codes and accessibility standards. Key milestones include securing necessary approvals from the City Planning Department and the Parks & Recreation Department.

**Phase 2: Site Preparation & Infrastructure (Weeks 5-8) – Responsible Partner: Oakhaven Public Works Department & Willow Creek Neighborhood Association; Deliverables: Site Clearing, Permeable Paving Installation, Rainwater Harvesting System Installation.** The Oakhaven Public Works Department will provide essential heavy machinery for site clearing and grading.  Volunteers from the Willow Creek Neighborhood Association, under the supervision of experienced construction personnel, will assist with the installation of permeable paving and the rainwater harvesting system.  This phase includes the trenching required for underground plumbing associated with the rainwater harvesting system.

**Phase 3: Planting & Landscaping (Weeks 9-12) – Responsible Partner: Willow Creek Community Garden Collective & Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department; Deliverables: Native Plant Installation, Mulching, Irrigation System Setup.** The Willow Creek Community Garden Collective, a well-established local organization, will lead the planting and landscaping efforts. They will source and install native plant species selected for their drought tolerance and ability to manage stormwater runoff. The Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department will provide technical support and ensure proper irrigation system setup.

**Phase 4: Community Build & Finishing Touches (Weeks 13-16) – Responsible Partner: Willow Creek Neighborhood Association & Local Businesses; Deliverables: Seating Installation, Signage, Grand Opening Event.**  The Willow Creek Neighborhood Association will coordinate volunteer efforts to install seating areas and signage. Local businesses will be solicited for in-kind donations of materials and refreshments for a celebratory Grand Opening event, marking the park’s completion and welcoming the community.

**Budget Transparency**

The total project cost is estimated at $100,000. We are requesting $75,000 in municipal funding, complemented by $25,000 in community contributions.

* **Permeable Paving Materials & Installation:** $35,000 – This includes the cost of specialized paving blocks and the labor required for professional installation, ensuring effective stormwater infiltration.
* **Rainwater Harvesting System (Tank, Pump, Filtration):** $20,000 – This covers the purchase and installation of a 5,000-gallon rainwater storage tank, a submersible pump, and a filtration system for irrigation purposes.
* **Native Plants & Soil Amendments:** $10,000 –  This allocation supports the sourcing of drought-tolerant native plant species and organic soil amendments to promote healthy plant growth and minimize the need for chemical fertilizers.
* **Seating & Signage:** $8,000 – This includes the purchase of durable, recycled-material benches and interpretive signage detailing the park's climate-resilient features.
* **Construction Supervision & Technical Assistance:** $7,000 – This covers the cost of hiring experienced construction personnel to supervise volunteer labor and provide technical assistance throughout the project.
* **Grand Opening Event & Community Outreach:** $5,000 – Funds are allocated for refreshments, promotional materials, and entertainment at the park’s Grand Opening.
* **Contingency Fund:** $5,000 –  A contingency fund is included to address unforeseen expenses or material price fluctuations.

Community contributions of $25,000 will be fulfilled through volunteer labor hours valued at $15,000 and in-kind donations of materials (lumber, mulch, tools) valued at $10,000.

**Community Engagement, Equity Impacts, & Sustainability**

Community engagement has been central to this project from its inception.  Initial consultations were held with Willow Creek residents, local business owners, and representatives from community organizations to ensure the park’s design reflects their needs and aspirations.  The park’s location was specifically chosen to address a demonstrable lack of green space in a historically underserved neighborhood. The project aims to mitigate heat island effects, improve air quality, and provide a safe and accessible recreational space for all residents.

Post-grant sustainability will be ensured through ongoing maintenance by the Willow Creek Neighborhood Association, supported by the Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department.  Educational workshops on rainwater harvesting and sustainable gardening practices will be offered to the community, fostering long-term stewardship of the park.  A “Friends of Willow Creek Pocket Park” group will be established to facilitate fundraising and volunteer recruitment.

**Risk Mitigation**

Potential risks include inclement weather delaying construction timelines, volunteer recruitment challenges, and material cost increases. To mitigate these risks, a flexible project schedule will be maintained, a robust volunteer recruitment strategy will be implemented, and a contingency fund will be allocated to address unforeseen expenses.  We will also secure necessary insurance coverage to protect against liability.

**Call to Action**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park represents a transformative opportunity to enhance the quality of life for Oakhaven residents, promote climate resilience, and foster a stronger sense of community. We urge the City of Oakhaven to approve our request for $75,000 in municipal funding, enabling us to realize this vital project and create a lasting legacy for generations to come.  This investment will yield significant environmental, social, and economic benefits, demonstrating the power of collaborative action and sustainable design.
