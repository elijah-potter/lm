Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny bunny named Pip. Pip had soft, fluffy ears and a heart full of joy. Every morning, Pip would hop outside to see what exciting things the garden had to offer.

One sunny afternoon, Pip found a shiny red apple near a big oak tree. Pip nibbled on it and felt happy. Suddenly, a little cloud with fluffy white edges drifted down. "Hello there, Pip!" it said. "I’m Pip’s best friend, Cloudy!"

Pip smiled. "Do you have a magic wand, Cloudy?"

"Oh yes!" Cloudy said. "But I need something in return. Can you help me find my missing rainbow?"

Pip gasped. "A rainbow? Of course, I’ll help!"

Pip hopped around the garden, looking for the rainbow. He saw a blue butterfly, a yellow daisy, and a purple flower dancing together. Then he spotted a golden sun shining brightly.

"Wow! You’ve found them all!" Cloudy cheered. "Now, let’s make the rainbow together!"

Cloudy waved her magic wand, and a beautiful rainbow stretched across the sky, swirling in all the colors: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet. The garden sparkled with happiness.

Pip and Cloudy shared a big, juicy apple, and they laughed and played all afternoon. Pip knew that even though he was small, he could make a big difference with a little kindness and friendship. And from that day on, Pip always remembered that sometimes, all you need is a friend to help you shine.
