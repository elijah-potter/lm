Congratulations on the fact that you're thinking about attending college! Here are a few reasons why you should hit the books:

1. You'll enrich your knowledge. College opens your eyes to new facts, beliefs, and perspectives that could completely change your way of thinking.
2. Your work and essays contribute to important research even if you don't realize it. Teachers learn from you and polish their lesson plans. Sometimes, they incorporate student work into their lessons (with credit, of course!).
3. You'll have networking opportunities. Your classmates could help you secure a great job later, or simply become your close friends.

Good luck with your new journey!
