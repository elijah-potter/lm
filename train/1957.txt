Learn More: Environmental Resources
- ARKive – Collection of thousands of videos, images and fact-files illustrating the world's species
- BBC Wild facts – Find information on animals here
- Biodiversity: The Most Fundamental Issue – Lecture By: Thomas, J. Lovejoy.
Given at the Australian Academy of Science - Mar 94
- Coleop–Terra – Provides information of everything you need to know on tropical beetles including physiology, ecology and information on specific species.
- Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species – CITES is an international agreement between Governments aiming to ensure that international trade in specimens of wild animals and plants does not threaten their survival. Includes a database of CITES listed species.
- Daintreerainforest.com – Australian Rainforest website with educational resources.
- Earth Trends – Provides statistical and analytical information on environmental and social trends around the world
- FishBase – Online database of over 25,000 species of fish.
- Green Facts – provides non-specialists with unbiased scientific information on environment and health matters
- Jungle Photos – Images from and information about the Amazon rainforest. (LE)
- Mangroves of India – a database on Indian mangroves, with information on living resources, threats, management and much more.
- Rainforests the Burning Issue – An interactive book produced by the Prince's Rainforest Project
- Reef Education Network – produced by the University of Queensland
- Recycling Supermarket – provides tips, news and information on recycling, waste management and the environment. (LE)
- The Current State of Biological Diversity – Information from the book Biodiversity, ed. E.O Wilson
- Species 2000 – Indexing the world's known species
- Species Databases – information on species of conservation importance
- UK Safari – For anyone interested in British wildlife, this great site features photos, facts and a free newsletter
