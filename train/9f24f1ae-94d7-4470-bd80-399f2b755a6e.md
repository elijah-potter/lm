Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around the garden, eating carrots and chasing butterflies.

One sunny morning, Benny noticed a tiny, shiny egg in his basket. He picked it up and wondered what would come out of it. Benny carefully placed the egg in a warm, sunny spot near his burrow.

Days passed, and one evening, Benny heard a soft peep. He peeked inside the egg and saw a tiny, fuzzy baby bunny inside! Benny’s heart felt warm and happy. He named the baby bunny Benny Jr.

Benny Jr. was tiny and curious. He loved to explore the garden, playing with his new friends—a squirrel named Sammy and a little bird named Pip. They all had so much fun together, chasing bugs, digging holes, and sharing snacks like berries and acorns.

One day, Benny Jr. asked, "Mommy, why do we hop instead of walk?" Benny smiled and said, "Because our paws are soft and bouncy—just like this!" Benny Jr. learned so much from his family, and they all grew stronger together.

Now, Benny and Benny Jr. still hop around their garden, sharing stories and adventures, while Sammy and Pip keep them company. And that’s how Benny’s little family made the world a little brighter, one hop at a time.
