A revised tactical framework was introduced by HQ to address evolving threats in Sector 7’s perimeter defense. The original plan’s reliance on static drone patrols was deemed obsolete after drone swarm attacks on the northern flank. New directives mandate adaptive sensor networks, integrating AI-driven threat prediction models and modular ground units capable of real-time rerouting. Redundancy protocols for critical nodes were implemented, with failover procedures now tied to cloud-based data fusion centers. Operational windows were adjusted to prioritize nighttime surveillance due to reduced enemy countermeasures. Deployment of new electro-magnetic pulse (EMP) countermeasures was approved for secondary defense layers, though full rollout remains contingent on supply chain approval.
