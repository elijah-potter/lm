This lesson teaches students how to use the proper conventions in writing dialogue. The flipchart includes rules for writing dialogue as well as proper conventions for punctuation. Typical activities within this interactive flipchart include:
- Know your rules for writing dialogue. Learn about quotation marks, punctuation and paragraphs.
- Learn how and when to use quotation marks.
- Reading a dialogue and deciding where the paragraph breaks should be.
To use these products, you must have ActivInspire software (personal or professional edition). They work best on a Promethean interactive whiteboard with facilities for printing and sound.
