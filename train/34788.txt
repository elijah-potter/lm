Who created the American Flag?
Based on prominent stories, the first American flag was made by Betsy Ross, a Philadelphia seamstress that was an acquaintance of George Washington. In May 1776, so the story goes, General Washington and two representatives from the Continental Congress saw Ross at her upholstery store and also showed her a sketch design of the flag. Washington at first favored utilizing a star with 6 points, Ross promoted for a five-pointed star, which might be cut with just one quick snip of the scissors, and the gentlemen were won over. However, chroniclers have not had the ability to validate this version of events.
