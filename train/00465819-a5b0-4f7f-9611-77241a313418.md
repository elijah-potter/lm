**"From Dust to Starlight: The Journey Begins"**

The wind carried whispers of the old city—ruins half-buried in forgotten time, their stone walls humming with echoes of forgotten legends. Among the crumbling arches, a lone figure stood, their cloak dusted with embers of a long-dead fire. They held a lantern, its light flickering like a dying star, casting long shadows across the cracked pavement.

*"For every dawn, a new dawn,"* they murmured, their voice carrying the weight of centuries. Around them, the ruins pulsed with energy—ancient artifacts, half-smelted in the fires of forgotten craftsmanship, waited to be rediscovered.

A child, no older than seven, stumbled forward, their small hands clutching a rusted key. *"This is ours,"* the child said, eyes wide with wonder. The figure smiled. *"Then let’s unlock what’s hidden."*

And so began the climb.
