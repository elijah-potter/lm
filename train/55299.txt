The monetary statements provide the total financial details of a firm. These economic statements contain the balance sheet, an revenue statement, retained earnings statement and money flow statement. The balance sheet presents the economic situation in terms of its assets, liabilities and ownership equity. Earnings statements, also called P & L, give a view of the company’s operation given that they present the company’s revenue, expenses and earnings. Retained earnings statements clarify modifications to retained earnings while money flow statement report on the company is operating spending budget, and investing and financing activities.
