There are many different types of milk out there on the market some made with nuts and others coming from animals. Some of the most popular milk are cow's whole milk, almond milk, and oat milk. People prefer different kinds of milk based on their diet restrictions and allergies. Whole milk is the most used milk and it is thick and creamy must children would drink this milk for bone density and growth. Also, there is lactose milk for people who cannot handle dairy products very well. Some people even prefer almond milk due to its taste. Others would drink oat milk due to it being nut-free and it is thinner in liquid.
