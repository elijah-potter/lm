1. "The Candidate Emerges" - A charismatic older politician with a questionable past rises to prominence, promising to bring change to a corrupt political system.

2. "The Opposition Strikes Back" - The established political elite launches a smear campaign against the candidate, threatening to derail their campaign before it even begins.

2. "The First Debate" - The candidate faces off against their opponents in the first televised debate, with millions of viewers tuning in to see who will come out on top.

4. "The Scandal Breaks" - A major scandal involving the candidate's past is uncovered by a plucky young female reporter, throwing the campaign into chaos and threatening to destroy the candidate's chances of winning.

5. "The Rally" - The candidate holds a massive rally in a key battleground state, drawing tens of thousands of supporters and energising the campaign.

6 ."The Attack" - The candidate is targeted in an assassination attempt, forcing them to confront the dark forces at work in the political system.

7 ."The Ultimatum" - The candidate is given an ultimatum by powerful political insiders, forcing them to make a difficult choice that will determine the future of their campaign.

8. "The Turning Point" - The candidate makes a bold move that changes the course of the campaign and sets them on a path to victory.

9. "The Endgame" - The final days of the campaign is marked by intense campaigning, vicious attacks, and high-stakes drama as the candidate fights to secure victory.

10. "The Aftermath" - The election is over, but the fallout continues as the candidate faces new challenges and struggles to build a new political order in the aftermath of a hard-fought campaign.
