There once was a man named John who lived a simple life in a small town running a business for his family. John never had much going for himself. He'd wake up, take breakfast together with his son which had been prepared by his wife, and would head up to his family business to open up for the day. He wasn't particularly generous, and never really participated in community events organized in his town.
One day, as was his habit, he went through his morning and then headed to the business. However, he wouldn't live to reach the business. While crossing the road, a speeding car hit him, and he died on the spot.
People rushed to the scene, hoping to find him alive, but he was gone. Among the crowd that gathered around was a man of God who had been known to perform unimaginable miracles. After being narrated what happened, he approached the dead man, touched him, prayed, and miraculously, John came back to life.
Asked what had happened, John had no recollection. But after being told of the day's event, John and his family thanked the man of God. John decided to leave his business behind for his wife and followed the man of God, who was known to travel far and wide preaching about the word in the bible.
John decided to dedicate his life to serving other people. He'd be found in orphanages, prisons, and elderly home centers helping and guiding people. He'd give an account of his life, and how he was saved, and encourage people to believe in Christ.
He lived to be 87 and died while still in service.
