The river whispered secrets to the reeds as the sun bled gold into the sky, painting the water in fleeting hues of amber and rust. Old men sat by the bank, their fingers tracing the edges of worn maps, each one a fragment of a forgotten world. The youngest among them—no older than the cracks in the stone bridge—leaned forward, his breath misting in the cool evening air.

*"What lies beyond the mist, Father?"* he asked, voice trembling with the weight of his own curiosity.

The old man’s eyes gleamed with the same fire that had burned in the hearths of their ancestors. *"The world is a puzzle, child. Some pieces are meant to stay hidden. But if you listen closely, you’ll hear it calling you."* The bridge creaked beneath his feet as he stood, his shadow stretching long across the stones. *"The choice is yours to answer."*
