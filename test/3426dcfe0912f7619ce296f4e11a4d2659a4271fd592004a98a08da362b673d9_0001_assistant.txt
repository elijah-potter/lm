• Expedition Everest is a roller coaster attraction based around Mount Everest, Tibet, and the Yeti. This attraction reaches a high speed of 50 miles per hour and is located in the Animal Kingdom theme park in Florida's Disney World.
• Kali River Rapids is a rapids-themed roller coaster attraction based on a river rafting expedition through the Chakrandi River in Asia. This attraction features large drops and is located in Disney World's Animal Kingdom.
• Kilimanjaro Safari is a safari-themed attraction based on an open-sided safari that takes place throughout the savanna of Eastern Africa, located in the Animal Kingdom theme park of Disney World.
