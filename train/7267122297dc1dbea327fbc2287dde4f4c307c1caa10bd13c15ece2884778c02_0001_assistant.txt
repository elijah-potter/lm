In this story, a family of four goes on a vacation to a snowy resort. There is a lot for them to do, and three members of the family decide to go snowboarding. The family member that doesn't want to snowboard occupies their time by building a snowman. It was a super fun day but after all the fun activities, the family realizes that they are quite hungry. They go to the resort's restaurant and several family members get hot chocolate. For food, they get ramen, curry, and burgers. After a long day, they slept very well.
