1. **Ephemeral Light** – A flickering candle in a room where no one remembers the night.
2. **Whispers of the Forgotten** – The wind carries fragments of conversations from a door that no longer opens.
3. **The Last Letter** – Envelopes stuffed under the floorboards, sealed with a name that wasn’t written.
4. **A Clock That Ticks Backwards** – Hands frozen at 3:17 AM, counting down to what never was.
5. **The House That Holds Its Shadow** – Walls that breathe, reflecting faces that don’t exist.
6. **A Library Without Books** – Rows of empty shelves, each one a tombstone for what was read.
7. **The Mirror’s Secret** – A reflection that smiles too late, its gaze following you into the dark.
8. **A Map of Ghosts** – Ink on parchment where the dead are drawn in ink that never dries.
9. **The Silent Alarm** – A bell that tolls only when no one is listening.
10. **A Room Without a Door** – The exit is always open, yet you never leave.
