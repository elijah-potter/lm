Lily had a tiny, little pony named Pip. One day, while playing in the meadow, Pip started to dance with her. "Whoa-oh!" Lily giggled, watching Pip’s flappy ears and the way he hopped like a bunny.

Suddenly, a big, fluffy cloud with a smiley face floated down. "I’m Cloudy," it said. "I’ve got a surprise for you!" It popped open a balloon and inside was a shiny, golden apple.

Lily and Pip chased the apple as it floated higher and higher. The apple landed in a big, bubbling puddle. "Puddle!" Pip said, splashing happily.

Lily picked up a shiny rock and tossed it into the water. It bounced and made tiny ripples. "Look how pretty!" she cheered.

Cloudy smiled and said, "Time to go home, friends." The golden apple turned into a big, bright star in the sky. Lily and Pip waved goodbye, and soon they were back in their cozy house, where Mama and Papa were waiting with warm hugs and a big, delicious snack.

As they shared the apple pie, Pip let out a happy neigh and Lily giggled, "Best day ever!"
