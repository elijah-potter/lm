**The Clockwork Observatory of Eldrin**
A circular tower of brass and black iron, suspended above a river of liquid mercury, where time flows backward. Each hour, a silver-tongued automaton recites the future past, its voice echoing through hollow, hourglass-shaped chambers. The sand in the observatory’s clocks runs downward but backward—each grain counted as a second forward. Midnight is when the sun sets twice. Time is measured by the *reversed hourglass*, a device where the liquid rises instead of falls, and the pendulum swings in reverse. The city below obeys the clock’s inversion, but only in dreams.
