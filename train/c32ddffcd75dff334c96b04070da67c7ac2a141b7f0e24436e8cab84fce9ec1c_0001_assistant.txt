TOADS:
• Warty-looking with bumps and dry skin.
• Found in grasses or other dry areas. They do not need to be near the water.
• Have shorter legs, and prefer to crawl over hopping.
• Squat and dumpy-looking with a broad nose.
• Spawn is laid in stringy lengths.
• Tapoles are black and chunky.

FROGS:
• Sleek, smooth, and wet-looking skin even out of water.
• Need to be near water as they lose moisture more quickly.
• Legs are longer than head and body which helps them to hop.
• Athletic looking with a pointed nose.
• Spawn is laid in gooey clumps. 
• Tapoles are longer and slimmer with gold flecks.
