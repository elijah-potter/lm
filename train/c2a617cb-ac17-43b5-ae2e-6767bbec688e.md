The old clock tower stood silent at midnight, its brass face etched with hands that had stopped at 11:59. Beneath it, a child pressed a single red apple against her palm, her breath misting in the cold.

A gust of wind carried a whisper: *"The apple was once the first to fall."* The child turned—her reflection in the tower’s broken mirror matched the words.

A shadow stretched across the street. It was the apple’s ghost, now a seed, waiting to be planted.
