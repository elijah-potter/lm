**The Last Transmission**

The countdown began at midnight in the quiet observatory. Twelve seconds remained before the cosmic event that would reshape reality.

Dr. Elias Voss adjusted the holographic display, watching as the stars flickered in unstable patterns. "The singularity is coming," he whispered. Outside, the sky darkened as the gravitational lensing of the dying star cast an eerie glow over the observatory.

*"10... 9... 8..."*

A sudden tremor shook the building. The air shimmered with energy as the star's core collapsed. Elias grabbed his tablet and typed frantically:

**"ERROR: DATA CORRUPT. FINAL TRANSMISSION ACTIVATED."**

The screen flashed red. A voice crackled through the speakers, distorted by time dilation:

*"This is the final warning. The black hole will consume everything in 3... 2... 1..."*

The observatory erupted in light as the singularity consumed the star. Elias’s last message broadcasted into the void:

**"RECEIVE: THE UNIVERSE IS DYING. DON’T BE LATE."**

The screen went black. The last second passed. Silence.
