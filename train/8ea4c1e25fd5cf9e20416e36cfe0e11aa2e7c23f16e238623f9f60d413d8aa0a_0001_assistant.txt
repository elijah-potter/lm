I was bored so I went for a walk. I'm allowed to go around the block by myself. Last year I was too young, but now I'm old enough. I started my walk by skipping. Skipping is fun. I can skip better than my friends. I don't trip over my feet or fall down.

Mr. Wills's house is around the corner. He is a nice man and he is old. Last Halloween he gave me a big candy bar and I got to pet his cat. I was looking at his house and I see a rabbit! It's a big one! It's sitting by the bushes. I walked closer to see it better. It ran away.

I followed the rabbit to Mr. Will's backyard. I don't think he would be mad that I went back there. The rabbit saw me following it and ran away again. It went under some bushes to another person's yard. I followed it and crawled under the bushes too. 

The bush was kind of pokey and pulled at my hair. I didn't like it but I kept after the rabbit. The yard the rabbit was in now was big with a garden. I saw there were tomatoes growing. I don't like tomatoes! The rabbit crawled under the wire fence into the garden. It hid. I stood on the other side of the fence. I couldn't go in the garden.

A loud voice called out from a window in the house. The voice was a lady's and sounded mad. She told me to get out of her yard, so I did. I was a little scared and forgot about the rabbit. I went home, I was a little tired now and wanted a snack and water. 

My mom saw me come inside. She saw that I was dirty and had sticks and stuff in my hair. I needed a bath. But first, I got to have a snack. My day got less boring.
