**The Confession List**

1. The nights I stayed awake counting the cracks in the ceiling tiles, each one a promise I’d break sooner than later.
2. The way my fingers trembled when I typed *"I lied"* into the old journal—though I’d already forgotten what I’d lied about.
3. The book I’d hidden under my pillow, its spine worn smooth from the months I’d read it back to myself in the dark.
4. *"You don’t belong here,"* the voice said in the mirror, and I’d whispered back, *"I know."*
5. The address scribbled on the back of an envelope, the one I’d mailed to myself years ago, never checking the return address.
6. The song I hummed in the shower every Tuesday at 2:17 AM—same time, same key, same lie.
7. *"I’ll fix it,"* I said to the empty room when the truth came undone like a bad dream.
8. The way the paperback’s last page was dog-eared, the ink smudged where I’d written *"I’m sorry"* in pencil.
9. The time I swore I saw my reflection wink at me from the corner of the room—though I knew it was just the way my eyes glinted in the dim light.
10. *"You’re not alone,"* the stranger on the train said. I nodded, but the list inside me grew longer by the hour.

*(Note: Themes of self-deception, hidden texts, and quiet confession wrapped in poetic structure.)*
