In the hollowed heart of an ancient forest, where the trees whispered secrets to the wind, a child named Elias stumbled upon a door carved from black stone. The door bore no locks, but its surface shimmered faintly, as if alive with a quiet energy. Elias traced his fingers along its edge, feeling the weight of something forgotten—something that once held answers no one dared ask.

Outside the door stood a figure cloaked in mist, its face obscured by a veil of shifting shadows. "You seek what you fear," the figure murmured, though Elias had not spoken. "The past is a place where questions linger like echoes, and answers are buried beneath layers of time." The child hesitated, the weight of curiosity pressing against his chest.

What would you do if you found yourself standing where the past and future tangled together?
