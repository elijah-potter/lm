The river hummed its old refrain:
*"Water flows, but never stops."*

It whispered in the reeds, a loop of sound,
*"The tide returns, yet never found."*

The stones beneath the waves all murmured back,
*"Endless loops, yet never slack."*

A child stood where the path once walked,
*"I see the loop, but not the talk."*

The wind sighed, *"No end to the song—
just echoes lost in the long wind’s song."*
