

The first thing people will tell you when it comes to working smarter is “Prioritize.” Before plunging into any job, assess which items need to be completed first. Perform each task in the order of its importance. But some things in life are harder to prioritize. In fact, most of life isn’t so easy to check off a list and get done the way we do tasks at work. 

It may take some creativity to learn how to make this concept work in your life. It does help to prioritize your tasks though, whether you’re doing laundry or working on a project for your boss. Some people find it easy to do this and others need to make lists. 

When it comes to building your business or creating your dream, what’s the first and most important job to get done? It may be funding, a patent for a product, or hiring contractors to help with something … it’s your dream and your job to figure out what needs to be done first. Tackle each step with enthusiasm. Don’t see the obstacles in your way! Instead, see the opportunities!

It can also help to make a short outline of your concept from start to finish so that you can see on paper the steps you’ll need to take to achieve your goals. Remember that obstacles are simply a part of your journey and refuse to let them stop you. Sometimes they afford us an opportunity to step back and assess whether we should make modifications or alter our course, but any great dream comes with its own set of pitfalls and setbacks. 

Your dream is worth the wait. It’s worth all the trouble. It’s worth the sacrifice. Keep moving forward and even if it’s very slow progress, do strive to get something concrete completed each week.
