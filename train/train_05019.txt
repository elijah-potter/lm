A carton contains 12 boxes. If each box has 10 packs of cheese cookies, what's the price of a pack of cheese cookies if a dozen cartons cost $1440?
If 1 carton contains 12 boxes, then a dozen cartons contain 12*12 = 144 boxes
If 1 box has 10 packs of cookies, then 144 boxes have 144*10 = <<144*10=1440>>1440 packs
