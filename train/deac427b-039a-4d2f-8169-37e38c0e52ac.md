Once there was a little bunny named Benny who lived in a cozy burrow under a big, fluffy mushroom. Benny loved to hop around and explore his colorful world—red flowers, blue butterflies, and golden sunbeams dancing on the grass.

One sunny morning, Benny heard a tiny voice. "Help me, Benny! My berries are stuck in the tree!" It was his best friend, a squirrel named Sammy, who had climbed up too high. Benny’s ears perked up. "No problem! I’ll help you!"

Together, they used Benny’s strong legs to wiggle down the branch, and Sammy climbed down carefully. "Thank you, Benny!" Sammy cheered, dropping a big pile of shiny, red berries at Benny’s feet. "These are for you!" Benny grinned. "I’ll share them with all my friends!"

As the day went on, Benny and Sammy played hide-and-seek with clouds, chased fireflies, and sang silly songs. The mushroom burrow twinkled with happiness, and Benny knew—being kind and playing together made everything extra magical.
