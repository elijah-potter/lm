Mi diente flojo
A child notices his tooth is loose and tries an assortment of ways to get it out. This is a delightful story of trial and error.
This is 1 of 180 authentic Spanish adaptations in Colección Caleidoscopio–a collection of informational and narrative texts adapted into authentic Spanish. Diversity and exploring social themes are two significant features embedded in these titles, which are great literacy resources for bilingual and dual language classrooms.
