A village in the mountains where the elders communicate through shared glances and whispered stories.
Bridges are built only where the wind flows most gently, and the path to the old mill is marked by moss-covered stones.
The well’s water is shared only with those who leave a single smooth pebble in its basin.
Dogs bark at strangers; children know not to ask why.
The clock tower chimes at dusk, but the actual time is told by the length of shadows cast by the oldest oak.
