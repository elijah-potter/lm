Scientific Name: Limosa haemastica
- Large shorebird.
- Long, slightly upcurved bill is pink at base, black at tip.
- Tail is black.
- Uppertail coverts white.
- Black wing linings contrasting with white bases to flight feathers.
- White wing stripe.
- Dark leg chestnut underparts with dense black and gray bars, black upperparts with small white spots.
