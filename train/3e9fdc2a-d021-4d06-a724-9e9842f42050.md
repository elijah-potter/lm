Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a curious little bunny named Benny. Benny had bright orange fur and big, round ears that flopped when he hopped.

Every morning, Benny would wake up and say, "Time for a new adventure!" His favorite part was exploring the garden with his best friend, a little squirrel named Sammy.

One sunny day, Benny spotted something shiny in the flower bed. It looked like a tiny golden treasure! "Sammy, look!" Benny said, hopping excitedly. "Maybe it’s a magic stone!"

Sammy chattered, "Let’s find out!"

They dug carefully with their tiny paws until they found a smooth, golden rock. Benny held it up. "It’s warm!" he gasped. "It’s glowing!"

Suddenly, the rock began to sparkle and dance, making everything around them shimmer. Benny’s ears perked up. "Wow! It’s like magic!"

Sammy squeaked, "Does it mean we’re special friends?"

The rock giggled and floated into Benny’s paw. "Yes! Because you’re kind and brave," it said. "Now, you can help others just like you!"

From that day on, Benny and Sammy always checked on their friends—helping little flowers grow, sharing seeds, and making sure no one was lonely. And whenever Benny felt a little tired, he would find the golden rock again, and it would remind him to be brave and kind.

And so, the two best friends kept exploring, sharing, and making the world a little brighter, one hop at a time.
