An abandoned library in a forgotten town, its stone walls etched with faded inscriptions and cracked book spines bearing dust-mitted edges. The air hums with the scent of old parchment and damp. Shelves groan under centuries of accumulated debris, while a grand chandelier, its crystals long shattered, hangs precariously above a reading nook where a lone, half-burned manuscript lies open, its pages whispering secrets of a lost era. The floorboards beneath are warped, revealing hidden passageways and the faint glow of embers from a long-extinguished fireplace, casting long shadows of figures that only the wind seems to remember.
