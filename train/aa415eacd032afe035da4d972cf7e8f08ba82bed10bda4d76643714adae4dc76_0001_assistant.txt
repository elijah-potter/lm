Hello, sure, it is commonly believed that makeup wipes are harmful to your skin. Makeup wipes require you to rub your skin which can irritate your skin, especially the delicate skin around and on your eye. The ingredients in makeup wipes can also irritate and, in some cases, cause allergic reactions. Most makeup wipes use fragrances that can be irritating to sensitive skin. Also, makeup wipes use preservatives to keep them from molding in their packaging. These preservatives can be harsh on your skin.
