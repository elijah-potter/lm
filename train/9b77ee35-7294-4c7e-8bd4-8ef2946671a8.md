In the abandoned library’s dust-choked corridors, under the flickering glow of a cracked bulb, a parchment scroll was found half-buried beneath a collapsed bookshelf. The ink was faded but legible:

*"The Key of Hollow Dawn is lost to time. Seek where the river’s song echoes the whispers of the drowned. The bridge of silver will crumble before dawn’s third rise."*

Nearby, an old pocket watch, its gears rusted, ticked erratically, marking the moment the message was placed there. The watch’s face read: *"1893. The Night the Stars Fell."*
