Learn about responsible ways to recreate on agricultural public land in Alberta.
There are about five million acres of agricultural land in Alberta leased for grazing and cultivation that can be used for recreation.
Agricultural leaseholders must allow access to land as long as it poses no harm to the land or to their investment in crops or livestock.
