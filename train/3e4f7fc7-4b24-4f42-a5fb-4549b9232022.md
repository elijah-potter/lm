**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 14, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, offering a traditional celebration of agriculture, entertainment, and community spirit. This year’s fair promises a diverse array of attractions, including livestock exhibits, a bustling marketplace featuring local artisans and vendors, thrilling carnival rides, and live musical performances spanning multiple genres. Visitors of all ages will find something to enjoy amidst the familiar sights and sounds of a classic county fair experience.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 22nd through August 26th, 2024, at the fairgrounds located at 2100 East Lake Shore Drive. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events continuing until 10:00 PM. A special fireworks display will illuminate the skies on Friday evening, August 23rd, and Saturday evening, August 24th. Daily admission is $10 for adults, $5 for children ages 6-12, and free for children five and under. Season passes, providing access to all six days, are available for $25. Tickets can be purchased online at SangamonCountyFair.org or at the gate.

This year’s fair features a dynamic schedule of headline events:

*   **Thursday, August 22nd:** Opening Ceremony & Demolition Derby
*   **Friday, August 23rd:** Tractor Pull Competition
*   **Saturday, August 24th:** Queen’s Coronation & Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters
*   **Sunday, August 25th:** Livestock Auction
*   **Monday, August 26th:** Championship Rodeo
