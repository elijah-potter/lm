## Bookstore Serendipity (Powell's City of Books, Portland)

The rain was falling steadily outside, a comforting rhythm against the large windows of Powell's. I was browsing the poetry section, drawn to a worn copy of Mary Oliver, when I noticed you. You were kneeling, examining a collection of essays on natural history, a shaft of sunlight catching the dust motes dancing around your head. There was something undeniably kind in the way you tilted your head, a quiet contemplation that resonated with me. I remember the distinct scent of old paper and brewing coffee mingling in the air, a familiar and cozy fragrance. You had a vibrant, teal scarf wrapped loosely around your neck, a striking contrast to the muted tones of the surrounding books. We didn't speak, but I felt a fleeting connection, a shared appreciation for the quiet solace of a bookstore on a rainy afternoon. I regret not finding a way to acknowledge you then.
