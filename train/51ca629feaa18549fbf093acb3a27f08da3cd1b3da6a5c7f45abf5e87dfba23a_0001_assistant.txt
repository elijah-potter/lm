My name is Baby, and I'm going to tell you what it's like when I'm hungry or tired. It's not a very pleasant feeling and it makes me upset sometimes. When I'm hungry, my tummy feels empty and makes all these weird noises. It asks for food, and if I don't get something to fill it soon, I start crying and get fussy. Sometimes, I can't even concentrate on anything else because I'm so hungry.

When I'm tired, my eyes begin to close on their own. I get sleepy and yawn a lot. I don't want to play or do anything; all I want is to snuggle up in my cozy blankets and take a nap. Sometimes, I might even get a little grumpy because I just can't keep my eyes open. So you see, being a hungry tired baby is tough. I can't tell anyone how I feel - I just have to cry and hope someone understands. But when I do get some food or a nap, I feel so much better. It's the best feeling in the world and I'm happy again!
