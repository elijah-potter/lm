**Stormy Rain**
A cramped, flickering cabin door creaks shut behind the last traveler. Inside, the air is thick with damp wool and the scent of old tea. Rain lashes against the windows in relentless sheets, each drop a silent hammer. Inside, the fire burns low, casting jagged shadows on the walls—somewhere between hope and despair. A single candle flickers, its flame trembling like the hands of a man clutching an unread letter. Outside, the world is a blur of gray and grayer, while inside, the only warmth is the ghost of a smile on the lips of a stranger who never said goodbye.
