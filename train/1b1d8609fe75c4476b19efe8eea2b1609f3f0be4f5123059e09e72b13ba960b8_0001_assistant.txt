1. What genetic information do octopuses alter when they change their skin's color and texture, and what remains unchanged?
-Octopuses edit their RNA when they alter their appearance while leaving their DNA unchanged.
2. True or False? Octopuses are color-blind.
-True, octopuses are color-blind.
3. Why do octopuses change their color and skin texture?
-Octopuses change their color and skin texture to camouflage themselves or intimidate rivals.
4. True or False? Octopuses have no sense of taste.
-False. Octopuses taste what they touch with their tentacles.
5. How do octopuses detect light?
-Octopuses detect light through their skin.
