## Product Requirements Document: "Context" - Intelligent Meeting Summarization and Action Item Management

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers spend an inordinate amount of time in meetings, often leaving with unclear action items, fragmented notes, and a general lack of clarity regarding decisions made, leading to wasted time and reduced productivity.

**Vision:** To be the indispensable tool that transforms meeting chaos into actionable clarity, empowering knowledge workers to reclaim their time and focus on meaningful work.

---

### User Personas

**1. Amelia Chen, Senior Marketing Manager:**

*   **Goals:** To effectively manage her team's workload, ensure alignment on marketing campaigns, and consistently deliver results within deadlines.
*   **Pain Points:** Overwhelmed by the number of weekly meetings, struggles to recall key decisions and action items post-meeting, frequently wastes time reconstructing meeting context from scattered notes and email threads. Feels a constant pressure to be "always on" and responsive.

**2. David Ramirez, Software Engineer:**

*   **Goals:** To efficiently contribute to project development, clearly understand his responsibilities, and maintain a high level of code quality.
*   **Pain Points:** Attends numerous daily stand-up meetings and planning sessions, finds it difficult to track dependencies and individual assignments, experiences frustration when action items are ambiguous or unassigned. Values precision and efficiency in his workflow.

**3. Eleanor Vance, Research Analyst:**

*   **Goals:** To conduct thorough research, synthesize complex data, and present findings with accuracy and clarity.
*   **Pain Points:** Participates in frequent client meetings and internal review sessions, struggles to capture nuances and key takeaways from discussions, finds it time-consuming to organize meeting notes and share relevant information with colleagues. Requires a reliable system for preserving meeting context.

---

### Requirements

**1. Automated Meeting Transcription & Summarization:**

*   **Description:** The application will automatically transcribe audio from meetings and generate concise summaries highlighting key decisions, action items, and discussion points.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** Transcriptions must be accurate to within 5% error rate. Summaries must accurately reflect the main topics discussed and include a clear list of action items, each assigned to a participant. Summaries should be available within 15 minutes of meeting conclusion.
*   **Success Metrics:** User satisfaction rating (4.5/5 or higher), average summary generation time under 10 minutes, percentage of users actively utilizing the summarization feature (70% or higher).

**2. Intelligent Action Item Assignment & Tracking:**

*   **Description:** The application will intelligently identify potential action items from meeting transcripts and summaries, suggest assignees based on discussion context, and provide a centralized system for tracking progress.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** The system accurately identifies at least 80% of action items. Suggested assignees are relevant to the identified action item. Users can easily modify suggested assignees and add/edit due dates.
*   **Success Metrics:** Percentage of action items successfully assigned (90% or higher), reduction in time spent manually tracking action items (measured through user surveys).

**3. Secure Meeting Recording & Note Sharing:**

*   **Description:** The application will provide secure storage for meeting recordings and notes, with granular permission controls for sharing with colleagues.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** Recordings and notes are encrypted both in transit and at rest. Users can define access levels (view only, edit) for shared meetings.  Sharing links are secure and require authentication.
*   **Success Metrics:** Number of active users sharing meeting recordings and notes, user feedback regarding the security and ease of sharing.

**4. Cross-Platform Accessibility (Mobile & Web):**

*   **Description:** The application will be accessible via native mobile applications (iOS and Android) and a web-based interface, ensuring usability across devices.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** Core functionality (transcription, summarization, action item management) is consistent across platforms. Application performance is acceptable on a range of device specifications.
*   **Success Metrics:** User adoption rates on mobile versus web (within 10% difference), user feedback regarding platform usability.

**5. Integration with Calendar and Collaboration Tools:**

*   **Description:** The application will integrate with popular calendar applications (Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar) and collaboration platforms (Slack, Microsoft Teams) to streamline meeting scheduling and notification.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** The application can automatically detect scheduled meetings and prompt users to record. Meeting summaries and action items can be shared directly to relevant channels within collaboration platforms.
*   **Success Metrics:** Percentage of users connecting their calendars (50% or higher), number of shared meeting summaries within collaboration platforms.



---

### Technical Considerations

*   **Scalability:** The system must be designed to handle a large volume of meeting recordings and users.
*   **Audio Processing:** Robust audio processing algorithms are crucial for accurate transcription, particularly in environments with background noise.
*   **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** Advanced NLP techniques are required for accurate summarization and action item identification.
*   **Security:** Meeting recordings and transcripts contain sensitive information, necessitating stringent security measures.
*   **Platform Compatibility:** iOS and Android applications must be optimized for a range of device specifications.



### Risks

*   **Transcription Accuracy:** Achieving consistently high transcription accuracy across diverse accents and audio conditions presents a significant technical challenge.
*   **NLP Complexity:** The accuracy of summarization and action item identification relies heavily on the sophistication of NLP algorithms.
*   **User Adoption:** Users may be hesitant to adopt a new tool, particularly if it requires significant changes to their existing workflows.
*   **Data Privacy Concerns:** Users may be wary of recording and storing meeting audio, raising data privacy concerns.



### Non-Goals

*   Real-time transcription.
*   Automatic meeting scheduling.
*   Integration with video conferencing platforms (Zoom, Google Meet) – considered for a future iteration.
*   Support for languages beyond English – considered for a future iteration.
*   Advanced analytics or reporting features – considered for a future iteration.
