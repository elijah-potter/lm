-In what year was the horror film House of Frankenstein released? 1944
-What actors starred in the film? Boris Karloff, Lon Chaney Jr, John Carradine
-Who were the directors of the film? Erle C. Kenton
-Who produced the film? Universal Pictures
-What is the film about? Dr. Gustav Niemann, an escapee from prison who promises to create a new body for Daniel. who is his assistant.
-What are the names of the characters in the film? Count Dracula, Larry Talbot, the Wolf Man, Frankenstein’s Monster
