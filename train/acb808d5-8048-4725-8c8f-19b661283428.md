High Arctic tundra: −30°C to −10°C with wind chill often below −50°C during polar nights; persistent permafrost, frozen lakes, and ice-covered landscapes. Seasonal extremes include brief, sub-zero summers with rare temperatures above 0°C.

Tropical rainforest near the equator: 25°C–32°C daily, humid with 80–90% relative humidity, high precipitation year-round, frequent thunderstorms, and minimal temperature variation.

Mid-latitude temperate forest (e.g., northeastern U.S.): 5°C–25°C; winter averages −5°C to 0°C, summer highs 25–30°C, with seasonal snowfall and moderate rainfall.

Desert (e.g., Sahara or Mojave): 20°C–40°C during the day, dropping below 0°C at night; extreme diurnal variation, minimal cloud cover, and scarce precipitation.

Alpine meadow (e.g., Swiss Alps): −10°C to 15°C; frost common in winter, snowmelt in spring; summers may exceed 20°C with high elevation winds.

Urban city (e.g., Tokyo): 15°C–25°C; heat islands raise daytime temps 5–10°C above rural areas; frequent fog, short winter days, and seasonal allergens.

Tropical monsoon (e.g., Mumbai): 23°C–32°C; extreme humidity, frequent cyclones, 4-month rainy season (summer monsoon), dry winters.
