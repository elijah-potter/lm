Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white fur and bright green eyes. Every morning, Benny would hop outside to see what adventures were waiting for him.

One sunny afternoon, Benny spotted a shiny red apple near a bush. "Mmm, a juicy apple!" Benny thought. He nibbled a tiny bite and then saw a tiny blue butterfly fluttering nearby. "Hello, butterfly!" Benny said. The butterfly giggled and flapped its wings.

Benny decided to help the butterfly by blowing on it gently. "Just a little breeze," Benny whispered. The butterfly's wings spread wider, and it danced happily in the air. "Thank you, Benny!" it said. Benny smiled and watched as the butterfly flew away, leaving a trail of butterflies following it.

After that, Benny loved to play with the butterflies, chasing them and watching them dance in the sunshine. He also found a small, round, golden ball near the apple bush. Benny picked it up and rolled it down the hill, giggling as it bounced. The ball rolled all the way to the river, where it splashed and sparkled in the water.

Benny loved the river too, where he could splash and jump, making waves. He found a tiny, shiny pebble that sparkled even more than the apple. Benny picked it up and hid it in his pocket, promising to show it to his friends later.

That evening, as Benny snuggled under his warm blanket, he thought of all the fun he had. He smiled and drifted off to sleep, dreaming of his next adventure with Benny and his friends.

The next morning, Benny woke up, hopped outside, and discovered his garden was full of butterflies, rolling balls, and shiny pebbles. He knew his adventures were just beginning!
