Here are several boho-style elements for you to consider for your wedding:

- Wedding Themes. There are several different wedding themes to consider for a boho-style wedding. These include Rustic, Vintage, Romantic, Desert, and Beach. 
- Wedding Venues. Depending on the theme you choose, appropriate wedding venues may include Fruit Orchards, Gardens, Vineyards, Beaches, Gazebos, State Parks, Farms, Courtyards, and Campgrounds.
- Wedding Dresses. Boho-style wedding dresses will tend toward the nontraditional. There are many styles to choose from that will appropriately represent the Boho style. Consider dresses with lots of lace, backless, bell sleeves, off-the-shoulder, fringe details, and minimal tulle.
- Accessories. Boho-style weddings allow for more freedom with accessories. You might consider adding colorful jewelry, a simple veil, a flower crown, a headband, a wide-brimmed hat, or boots or flats.
- Men's Attire. Since boho-style weddings are more casual, acceptable men's attire includes dress shirts, khakis, bow ties, waistcoats, hats, suspenders, and pocket watches. 
- Bouquet. Choose flowers that correspond to your wedding theme. Using twine, lace, or velvet will facilitate the boho style.
- Hair and Makeup. Bohemian-style weddings incorporate an effortless beauty that the bride should adopt for her hair and makeup. Light, natural makeup and loose, messy curls and waves are congruent with the Bohemian style.
- Decor. Because the Bohemian style is about nature and natural items, incorporate wicker and macrame pieces into your ceremony and celebration. Decorate tables with dried grasses, succulents, cactuses, wildflowers, and berries. Use vintage glassware and dishes, and tie each napkin with lace, twine or velvet. 
- Color Palettes. Colors inspired by nature are often used in boho-style weddings. Deep golds, soft pastel pinks, and rusty oranges are popular.  
- Food and Drink. Choose organic meats and cheeses, vintage food trucks, pastries, and home-baked goods to serve. If possible, utilize local brewers or wineries for spirits.
