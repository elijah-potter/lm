To whom it may concern,

The ink on these words, like the whispers of the wind, carries a quiet urgency—an invitation to pause and listen. You are not alone in the vastness of this silence, though you may feel it. The pages before you are not just words; they are echoes of a conversation we have never had, yet the space between us is where meaning is forged.

I write this as if you were already here, standing at the edge of a door you haven’t yet opened. The door itself is not grand or ornate, but the weight of its handle is familiar—it belongs to you, even if you haven’t recognized it yet. The room beyond is not one we’ve shared, but the air between us is thick with the scent of possibility.

Do you read these words as a warning, or as an invitation? The choice is not mine to make, but the question lingers in the space between us, much like the breath you hold before you exhale.

Let us begin.
