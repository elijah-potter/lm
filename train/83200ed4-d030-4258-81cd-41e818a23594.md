A tense silence lingers in the dimly lit café, the hum of the espresso machine the only sound. Across the table, Sarah’s fingers tap restlessly against the wooden edge, her knuckles white. The words—*I’m sorry*—linger between them, half-formed, half-sentence.

Across from her, Jake exhales sharply, his jaw tight. His eyes flick to the half-eaten slice of cake on his plate before meeting her gaze again. The air crackles, charged with something unsaid. The moment stretches, a fragile thread between them, fraying at the edges.

Then—*too late*. Jake clears his throat, pushes his plate away. “You know, I’ve been thinking about that project.” Sarah’s mouth falls open. The words vanish, swallowed by the sudden shift in focus. The scene dissolves into the clink of cutlery.
