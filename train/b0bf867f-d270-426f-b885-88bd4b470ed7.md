The dim glow of the streetlight flickered through the rain-streaked window, casting long shadows across the old wooden table. Clara sat hunched over her sketchbook, fingers tracing the edges of a half-drawn portrait of her brother, Daniel, who had left months ago without a word.

"Daniel?" she murmured, her voice barely audible above the distant hum of traffic.

The door creaked open, and in stepped Liam, her best friend, carrying a bag of takeout. He set it down with a thud, his eyes immediately shifting to the sketchbook. A slow smile tugged at his lips.

"You're not drawing again tonight, are you?" he asked, already pulling out a chair.

Clara didn’t look up. "I was just... practicing."

Liam’s gaze lingered on the half-finished portrait before settling on her face. His voice dropped to a whisper. "You know he’s back, don’t you?"

She exhaled sharply. "I *knew*."

Liam leaned back, a knowing smirk playing on his lips. "He’s in the apartment. The one he rented before moving out."

Clara’s pen stilled. "How do you—?"

"Because you *asked* me to check." Liam’s voice was low, measured. "And because you wouldn’t let me forget."

The room felt suddenly too small, the silence thick with unspoken words. Clara’s fingers trembled as she flipped the page, erasing the sketch with deliberate slashes. "I didn’t ask for this."

Liam’s expression softened. "But you *understood*."

Outside, the rain pattered against the window, the same relentless rhythm as the silence between them.
