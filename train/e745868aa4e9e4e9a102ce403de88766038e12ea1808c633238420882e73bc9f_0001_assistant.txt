Title: Finance Friends
Runtime: 30 minutes
Type of show: Children's show containing both live-action puppets and people, and animation
Characters: Telly Turtle (puppet); Max the Cat (puppet); Maria the Mail Deliverer (human); Mr. Money the bank manager (human); Lula the Financial Fairy (animated)
Premise: Telly and his friends Max, Maria, Mr. Money, and Lula Fairy live on Life Island, where they live and learn the concepts of money, savings, and financial responsibility. While money on Life Island isn't everything, being financially stable makes it possible for them to be able to do the things they love and get the things they need to live good lives. Special guests will stop by Life Island to visit Telly and his friends and to give sound financial advice. These guests can include people from the financial and banking sectors, accountants, lawyers, and any other profession that deals with financial matters.
Segment topics include: Opening a savings account; saving money for a rainy day; spending now vs. saving for the future; understanding the prices of things we want and need to buy; keeping track of income and expenses; loans and interest rates; and so much more.
Time slot: The show should be broadcast on weekends (Saturdays or Sundays) once a week. Broadcasting during the week may not be beneficial due to concentrating on school, and it would be best to have the young viewers watching the show with open and receptive minds.
