Navarro is a county in Texas. It was formed in 1846 from the following county/ies: Robertson. Navarro began keeping birth records in 1903, marriage records in 1846, and death records in 1903. It began keeping land records in 1838, probate records in 1846, and court records in 1855. For more information, contact the county at P.O. Box 423, Corsicana 75151-0423.
