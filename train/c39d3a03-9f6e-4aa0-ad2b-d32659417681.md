In the dim glow of flickering streetlights, the neon sign of *The Rusty Nail Tavern* cast eerie shadows across the alley behind the building. Inside, the hum of a generator filled the air with the rhythmic *clank-clank* of metal against metal. Inside, the air smelled of stale beer and burnt coffee, the last remnants of the night’s patrons still lingering in the dust-covered couches.

Emma sat on a cracked stool at the bar, her fingers tracing the rim of a half-empty whiskey glass. The TV on the wall flickered with static, its screen a pale blue void. Outside, the distant wail of sirens cut through the quiet, then faded into nothing.

Across the room, Jake leaned against the counter, his knuckles white as he gripped his beer bottle. "They’re not coming back," he muttered, voice low. "This is it."

A sharp *crack* split the silence—glass shattering somewhere in the kitchen. Emma’s eyes widened. The generator roared to life again, drowning out the sound, but the damage was done. The door burst open. A group of rough-looking men in hoodies stumbled in, their breath ragged. One—tall, with a scar running down his cheek—glanced around, his eyes landing on Jake.

"You’re the one who was here last night," he said, voice a growl. "The one who got away."

Emma’s blood ran cold. The night was far from over.
