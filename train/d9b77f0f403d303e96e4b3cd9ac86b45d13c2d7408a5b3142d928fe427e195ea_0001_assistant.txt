This world has so much to offer, and we are takers. We take and take and take, never feeling full. It is the greed that keeps us wanting more. But we can only keep what’s ours, and the rest of it will slip away from our hands just like quicksand.
There is no shame in saying you are happy with what you have because, in one way, growth also means making peace with your destiny, accepting it, and owning it entirely. People will tempt you to try to change your situation or try harder so you could go against the power of fate. But that, my friend, is stupidity. No one can ever go against the power of fate. 
No matter how big of a star you are or a CEO of a big organization, if you are meant to lose everything one day, you will. You cannot put barriers in the way of destiny because it never asks for permission.
“Patience is not the ability to wait. Patience is to be calm no matter what happens, constantly take action to turn it into positive growth opportunities, and have faith to believe that it will all work out in the end while you are waiting.”
It’s not wrong to accept what is meant for you and leave behind what your heart wishes for.
Let that sink in. Read it every day as a dose of positivity, and keep moving forward. It takes a brave heart to accept the unknown, but once you welcome whatever comes your way with open arms, you win.
The idea of growth itself makes us want to change and do better. But because we have arrived in this world with a destiny already written for us, it is in our best interests to have faith and not become selfish.
Ask yourself one more time why you must tire yourself by thinking of all the buts and ifs. Why not just do your job by trying hard enough and then leave everything to your fate?
No one deserves to be called a loser just because he didn’t become someone people wanted him to be. Give yourself to your destiny completely. Free yourself from all the worries and never doubt how things will turn out.
