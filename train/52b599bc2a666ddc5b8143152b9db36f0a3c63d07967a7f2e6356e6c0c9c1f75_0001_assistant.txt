My mother from the Earth,
You have seen and heard all,
Over 100 years old - full of wisdom and strength.

Your dark brown trunk is full of stories from the years before,
Your long branches are full of spunk when I get out of line,
Your voluptuous leaves of green and purple hug me all around.

A tree is what some might say,
But you are a mother to me, 
You are there when I need you.

You show your face when I am down,
And when I need a hand, you extend a branch to catch me when I fall,
Your chuckle makes my heart sing.

Mother Willow of my life,
I love you,
For you are me, and I am you - calm and loving.
