This is me in Costa Rica, having the time of my life. 
At this moment, I knew everything would change. It was the trip I had been putting off for so long. I was supposed to go with my husband. We kept saying we would but never did. 
Finally, after the divorce, I took a leap and went by myself. I met new people and experienced new things - all without a partner to lean on or talk to. It was liberating and helped me find out more about who I am without him. 

I have so much I still want to do, so much I want to see, and so much I want to experience.  
In the moment this photo was snapped, on this beautiful beach, I knew that this was the beginning of all that. 

The beginning of a whole new life for me. 
This photo is the beginning of a long love story with the most important person in my life... myself.
