Steve was an electrician, and he worked in his own repair business. It wasn't unusual to find him awake late at night, sitting on a straight-backed wooden chair, slumped forward over his work desk, tinkering with a component or circuit by the light of a bright, fluorescent lamp.

He loved his work, but as Steve aged, he found these late nights resulted in back and neck pain and blurry eyesight. He had also become impatient with his work and cranky with his customers. Steve needed a break.

On his birthday, his grandmother bought him a lottery ticket, and although Steve never squandered money on such things, he was excited about the potential to win the $5 million jackpot. 

Two nights later, when it was time for the lottery drawing, Steve put aside his work to watch it live on his small office TV. He barely breathed as he watched, astonished, as each drawn ball contained a number on his ticket! Sadly, when it came to the last number, it was one digit off.

Steve's hopes of becoming a millionaire and being able to retire were over, but he still won more than enough money to take a month off of work and take his grandmother on an extended luxury cruise. He didn't need to be a millionaire, anyway -- he just needed a little time away from work!
