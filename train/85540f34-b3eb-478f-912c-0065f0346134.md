The **Chronos Tower** in **Zephyria** operates on a **fractal time system** where each hour is divided into **quantized cycles** based on harmonic resonance. A standard day consists of **72 "Harmonic Hours"** (1.5-hour segments), each subdivided into **12 "Quartz Minutes"** (10-minute intervals), and **24 "Echo Seconds"** (30-second fractions). Time is displayed on the tower’s **neon crystal face**, where past, present, and future segments overlap dynamically. The clock resets after every **72 Quartz Minutes** (480 seconds), but "slowing" occurs when a user holds a **Chrono Stone**—each stone’s speed varies by resonance frequency. Local clocks drift unless synced via **Sonar Beacons**, which emit pulses every **17.5 "Echo Seconds"**. Official records are stored in **Archival Orbs**, where time is stored as **quantized "Event Points"** (e.g., "07:15:42.377 Quartz Minute 8, Echo Second 12"). The city enforces **"Time Dilation Laws"**, requiring all citizens to wear **Chrono Bands** (which sync via magnetic alignment to the tower’s core). Violators face **"Phase Displacement"**—a legal term for temporal misalignment, where they are "locked in a harmonic loop" until corrected. The tower’s core, the **Singularity Sphere**, emits a constant hum—each note a different harmonic—calibrating the city’s time.
