Once upon a time in a cozy little house, there lived a tiny bunny named Pip who loved to hop around the garden. One sunny morning, Pip saw a shiny red ball near the flowers. "What a fun ball!" Pip thought. He picked it up and began to bounce it gently.

Just then, a little bird named Pipette fluttered down. "Oops! I dropped my favorite feather," she chirped. Pip offered his ball in exchange. "Here, take it! You can bounce it too!"

Pipette giggled and took the ball. "But I have something even better!" she said, showing Pip her shiny new pebble. They played together for hours, sharing and laughing. The garden was full of joy, and Pip and Pipette became the best of friends. And that is how kindness and sharing made their garden the happiest place ever!
