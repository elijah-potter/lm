This story is set in the multiverse, primarily across two universes where one is attempting to conquer all other universes, although they don't see it that way -- instead, they see it as proselytizing in accordance with their faith tradition. At the seat of that world is a theocratically-ruled society. The government has internal enemies but is powerful enough to conquer other universes and has the scientific means to pass between worlds. In many ways, it’s exactly like Earth as it is a parallel universe. Many of the cities look exactly like Earth Prime cities superficially, but the closer you look, the more significant the differences become. 

It's a theocratic society based on prosperity beliefs with a rigid caste system. The belief system is an extreme fundamentalist Christian faith. Within it, citizens claim to see angels and interact with God, but the truth is that many of those people are just working the system due to the rigidity of the caste structure. Thus, the system becomes a feedback loop with the faith and caste systems reinforcing each other.
