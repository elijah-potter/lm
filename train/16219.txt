Our Curriculum & Teaching Plans
Teachers plan for each half term or term, depending on the topic, and you can see the overviews here.
The plans are thematic, which means that they have a central theme and the theme is carried through most subject areas of the curriculum and where it is applicable. Maths is always taught separately to the theme to enable all of the key concepts to be addressed fully.
Languages are taught across Key Stage 2 with French in Years 3 and 4 and Spanish in years 5 and 6.
We follow the Peterborough agreed syllabus for RE throughout the school.
We hope you enjoy looking at what is being taught!
