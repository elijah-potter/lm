The dimly lit hallway smelled of damp wool and stale air. The cracked chandelier above cast long, jagged shadows across the polished floor. The bookshelf behind the desk groaned under the weight of scattered tomes, their spines worn smooth. A half-empty bottle of whiskey sat on the floor beside a crumpled newspaper, its edges curled with age. The air was thick with the scent of aged parchment and something else—something metallic, like old blood. A single, faintly glowing object—perhaps a broken watch or a candle—lay in the corner, casting an eerie, pulsing light. The room felt alive with the weight of a moment frozen in time.
