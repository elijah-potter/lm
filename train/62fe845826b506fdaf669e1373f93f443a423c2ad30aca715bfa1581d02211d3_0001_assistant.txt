I couldn’t be more pleased by the County Commission’s recent decision to terminate the lease of the Head Start program at the County Court House. Since this socialist program has been running without sufficient funding, it’s time to give the space to something more meaningful to the community.
This program serves a limited segment of the population. It provides a free handout that sends the wrong message about working for what you receive. Let the parents of the children in this program pay for childcare and food for their families like the rest of us do! The children will learn more from seeing their parents work hard for what they earn instead of getting a free handout.
I love the idea of a Gift Shop featuring items made right here in Kansas. This retail space will send the right message about Kansas and bring money to the community instead of providing free services to a very small group of families. Visitors to our wonderful state will see all the incredible things made right here in Kansas. I applaud the County Commissioners for finally moving in the right direction against the woke left!
