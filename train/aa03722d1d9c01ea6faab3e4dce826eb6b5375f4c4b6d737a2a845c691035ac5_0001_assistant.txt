Ingredients: 
- 3 to 5 boiled Roma tomatoes
- 1 can of tomato sauce
- 1/2 tablespoon of basil
- 1 tablespoon of minced garlic
- 1 teaspoon of garlic salt
- 1 teaspoon of dried oregano
- 1 to 2 tablespoons of sugar
- 1 teaspoon of ground thyme 
- 1/2 sweet onion diced
- olive oil
- 1/2 red bell pepper
- water or milk if needed

Directions: 
- Use olive oil or butter to cook garlic until it's fragrant.
- Place red bell pepper diced into the same spot, once semi-soft add onion and tomatoes to the pot.
- Start adding the seasonings to the pot while the veggies are cooking. Do not add the sugar yet.
- Then add the boiled tomatoes to the pot and mash in the pot. If the pot looks thick in texture and the can of tomato sauce.
- Once the pot begins to bubble add sugar and turn to low heat.
- Add more seasoning if needed.
- Once done, let it cool for a while be for serving.
