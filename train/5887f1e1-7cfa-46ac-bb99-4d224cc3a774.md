**"Highlands of Veythar: A perpetual summer’s embrace, where the air is perpetually 23°C (73°F) with a slight coastal breeze. Storms roll in from the eastern ocean every 2–3 days, bringing 10–15 mm of rain, followed by 15-minute thunderstorms. Daylight lasts 12 hours, with golden-hour twilight extending into evening. The temperature dips to 18°C (64°F) at night, but humidity remains low (30–40%). Near the coast, waves lap against warm (20°C/68°F) shallows year-round. Inland valleys stay warm (24°C/75°F) with minimal seasonal variation. Temperatures never drop below 15°C (59°F) or exceed 30°C (86°F). Fog is rare, but mist lingers over the peaks at dawn and dusk. Wind speeds average 10 km/h, gusting to 25 km/h during storms. The region’s thermal consistency makes it ideal for tropical agriculture—coconut palms thrive, and citrus groves bear fruit year-round. No ice, no snow, only the occasional morning dew on the grass."**
