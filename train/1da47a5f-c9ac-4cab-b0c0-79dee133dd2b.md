**Fragments of a Forgotten Echo:**

A door half-open in the past—its hinges creaking like an old man’s breath.
The scent of rain on rusted metal, a voice whispering, *"Almost,"*
before dissolving into static.

A name written in dust: *Elias*. His fingers traced the edges of a sketch
left behind in a forgotten notebook, half-erased ink like fading light.
The world outside was a blur of yellowed pages and shadows,
where time bent like a broken mirror.

She found his journal pages on the library shelf—pages
that didn’t belong to her.
A poem about the way the wind carries secrets
but only lets the lost ones out.

The echo of laughter in a hallway, a child’s giggle
echoing through hollow corridors.
A lock clicks open—just for a second—
then seals itself shut again.

The clock struck twelve.
The memory dissolved.
What remains is the ghost of what *almost* was.
