There are many easy League of Legend champions that beginners can pick up and learn very quickly! These can include:
1. Ashe: Ashe is an ADC with long-range basic attacks that allow her to damage the enemy from afar. Her passive ability also allows her to slow enemies, which will help teach a beginner the importance of spacing.
2. Garen: Garen is a tank with simple abilities. He can survive long in team fights and has a powerful ultimate that is straightforward to use.
3. Janna: Janna is a support champion that excels in helping out the team. She is able to provide heals, shield, and crowd control with an easy-to-understand kit.
