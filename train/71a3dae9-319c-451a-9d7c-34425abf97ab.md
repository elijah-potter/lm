The river sang its endless refrain,
the wind hummed the same old tune—
a chorus carved in time’s unyielding stone.
The clock’s hands spun in a loop, unbroken,
while shadows stretched and faded,
repeating what was never truly gone.
*"Once was enough,"* the trees whispered,
but the leaves just danced in the same old wind.

The mirror held no secrets—just echoes,
a ghostly repetition of yesterday’s light.
The clock’s hands ticked past the same old hour,
a cycle that would never be new.
Some things, once said, must always be repeated,
like the tide, like the stars—
an eternal refrain, a song without end.
