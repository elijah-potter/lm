**The abandoned subway station at 32nd and Broadway**, now a makeshift shrine for the unspoken.
Flickering neon signs from the 1970s still cast a surreal glow over the platform, though the tracks are rusted shut. Locals call it *The Hollow*—where strangers sit in circles, humming old songs, or just staring at the ceiling. Some claim it’s a transit hub for the lost; others say it’s just a place where people forget their own names. The air smells like old coffee and something metallic, like regret. No one knows why they come, but they always leave with stories. The doors never open, but the train arrives once a week.
