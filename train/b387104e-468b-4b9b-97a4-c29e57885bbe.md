The subway doors hissed shut behind him, sealing in the last few passengers. His phone buzzed—an email from the boss: **"Late for the 9 AM meeting. I’ll call you."** He exhaled sharply, fingers trembling over the keyboard. The train rattled into the station, and he bolted up the escalator just as the doors slid open, a security guard watching from the platform. He ducked into the nearest café, a coffee cup trembling in his hand, the scent of espresso cutting through the tension. His boss’s voice cut through his thoughts: *"You’re early, actually."* He smirked, swiping the receipt.
