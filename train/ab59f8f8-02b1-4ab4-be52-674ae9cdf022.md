Once upon a time in a cozy little house, there lived a bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the garden every day. Benny’s favorite place was the big, bright flower bed where he could find the sweetest treats—sunflower seeds and dandelion flowers.

One sunny morning, Benny noticed something strange. The flowers looked a little sad, and the sunflower seeds were hiding under the leaves. "What’s wrong?" Benny wondered.

Just then, a tiny mouse named Mia scurried up. "Benny, I found a lost berry in my burrow! It’s so red and shiny—like a little apple!" Benny gasped. "A real berry? Where did it come from?"

Mia giggled. "It fell from the big oak tree outside! But the wind blew it away, and now it’s all the way here. Maybe if we help, the garden will be even more magical!"

So Benny and Mia worked together. They picked the dandelion flowers, shared the sunflower seeds, and planted new seeds of their own. Soon, the flower bed was filled with the most beautiful flowers Benny had ever seen—pink and purple and yellow.

The berry Mia had found shined even brighter in the garden, and the wind carried happy sounds all around. Benny and Mia sat together, watching as the sun set, painting the sky in colors of gold and pink. "This is the best day ever," Benny said happily.

And from that day on, whenever Benny hopped through the garden, he always remembered how special it was to help and share with friends.
