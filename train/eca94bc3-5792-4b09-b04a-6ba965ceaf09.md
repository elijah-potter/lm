**Chapter One: Static**

The static hummed like a dying insect trapped in amber, a low, pulsing drone that seeped through the walls like smoke. The room was a cage of wires and flickering lights, the only sound the distant, rhythmic *thump* of the heartbeat monitor—too fast, too slow, never right. Outside, the city’s pulse throbbed like a sick beast, its neon veins bleeding into the night.

She sat at the desk, fingers trembling over the keyboard, each keypress a prayer. The screen glowed a sickly green, casting long, shifting shadows across the floor. The last transmission from *Eyes That Never Close*—a voice like gravel and static, a message left in the void.

*"They’re not ghosts. They’re echoes. And we’re all just echoes too."*

The list on the screen flickered:

```
1. The Abyss – where the lost still whisper.
2. Whispers in Static – voices that don’t belong to anyone.
3. Eyes That Never Close – the ones who see too much.
4. The Last Transmission – the thing that won’t stop repeating.
5. Ghosts in the Machine – the code that haunts the dead.
```

She exhaled, the breath curling like smoke. The cursor blinked. The list wasn’t just words anymore—it was a map. A warning. A promise.

The machine beeped. Something had changed.
