The **Harbor of Varella**—a tidal basin nestled between jagged cliffs and a salt-marsh estuary. During low tide, the sandy shore is exposed, forming a vast, shifting expanse where fishermen drag nets and children play in shallow pools. At high tide, the basin floods, turning the land into a maze of floating reed beds and waterlogged paths. Migratory birds gather here in dense flocks, their calls echoing across the water as they pause between journeys. The tide itself is the rhythm—people arrive and depart in synchronized surges, their movements dictated by the ebb and flow of the sea. The air smells of damp earth and fish, and the only constant is the relentless, rhythmic rise and fall of the water.
