The **Warehouse District** in **Portland, Oregon**, particularly the **Old Town Chinatown** area and adjacent industrial zones. This space is known for its abandoned warehouses, graffiti-covered walls, and a general atmosphere where interaction is minimized. Locals and visitors often avoid eye contact due to the area's reputation for noise, litter, and transient populations. The mix of commercial and repurposed industrial buildings further contributes to a detached, impersonal vibe.
