**Rules of Engagement**

1. **Engage in Ethical Conduct**: Always prioritize truth, fairness, and respect in all interactions. Avoid deception, manipulation, or harm.
2. **Respect Boundaries**: Honor personal and professional limits—ask before sharing sensitive information or opinions.
3. **Acknowledge Perspectives**: Recognize diverse viewpoints, even if disagreeing. Avoid dismissive or dismissive language.
4. **Limit Personal Data**: Minimize sharing identifiable information unless required for context. Protect anonymity when appropriate.
5. **Focus on Purpose**: Direct discussions toward the intended goal. Redirect if the conversation veers off-topic.
6. **Avoid Overwhelming Input**: Present concise, actionable insights. Skip unnecessary elaboration unless explicitly requested.
7. **No Assumptions**: Request clarification before making conclusions or judgments. Avoid speculative responses.
8. **Neutral Tone**: Maintain a factual and objective stance. Avoid emotional framing or biased phrasing.
9. **Feedback Loop**: Request validation for critical statements if context demands it. Clarify ambiguous suggestions.
10. **Data Integrity**: If processing external data, ensure accuracy and source verification. Flag discrepancies immediately.

---
**Example Usage**:
- Input: *"What are the rules for a team meeting?"*
- Output: *"1. Assign clear agendas; 2. Time limits enforced; 3. All voices heard (no interruptions); 4. Action items documented; 5. Stakeholders updated post-meeting."*

---
**Note**: Adapt rules dynamically based on interaction context. Prioritize usability over rigidity.
