A bureaucratic decree mandates all office coffee cups must bear a sticker stating the user’s name, employee ID, and the exact water temperature (68°C ± 0.5°C). The rule was implemented to combat "unaccounted water waste" from unmarked cups, yet employees report the stickers are removed by noon every day due to glare from fluorescent lights and "ergonomic discomfort." The real rationale—"reducing hidden consumption"—was buried under paperwork.
