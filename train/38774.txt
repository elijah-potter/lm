Cal-6 + Mg 250 tablets
The average adult contains 1.2 kilograms of calcium at any one point in time and most of this calcium can be found in the bones and skeletal system. Calcium is vital for supporting and promoting bone health. The bones and skeleton serve a number of functions. The ribs and skull provide protection for the vital organs that keep you alive. The arms and leg bones are essential for healthy movement. In order to perform all of the functions, the skeleton needs to be both strong and flexible. To ensure the structural integrity of the skeletal system, the body relies on an abundant source of calcium, magnesium, and other nutrients. Without sufficient quantities of these nutrients, skeletal tissue may be brittle or soft or may not be as flexible as it needs to be.
The Cal-6 + Mg supplement by Douglas Laboratories combines 6 sources of calcium with the other essential nutrients required for building strong, flexible, and healthy bones.
Adults take 1 tablet before each meal or as directed by physician.
