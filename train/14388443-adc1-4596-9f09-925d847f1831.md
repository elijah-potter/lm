Once upon a time in a cozy little house, there lived a tiny mouse named Milo. Milo loved to explore the garden every day. One sunny morning, Milo saw a shiny red apple on the ground. "Wow!" he said, "Is that mine?"

Milo picked it up and nibbled a tiny bit. Suddenly, he heard a tiny voice, "Help! Help!" It was a ladybug named Lulu who had fallen into a big puddle of water.

Milo ran to help Lulu. "Don’t worry, Lulu!" he said. "I’ll get you out!" Milo carefully lifted Lulu up with his tiny paws and they both ran back to the garden.

Lulu gave Milo a tiny smile. "Thank you, Milo!" she said. "You’re the bravest little mouse I’ve ever met!"

From that day on, Milo and Lulu became the best of friends. They played together, shared their snacks, and explored the garden every day, making new adventures every single day.
