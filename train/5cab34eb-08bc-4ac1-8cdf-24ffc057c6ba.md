Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around the garden, chasing his favorite friend, a bright yellow butterfly named Bella.

One sunny morning, Benny noticed something sparkly in the grass. It was a tiny treasure chest made of acorns. Inside, there were golden acorns, soft feathers, and a shiny pebble that sparkled like the sun. Benny and Bella played with their new treasures all afternoon.

As the sky turned pink, Benny yawned and said, "Bella, let’s go home. It’s time for a snuggle time." Bella giggled and fluttered her wings happily, following Benny back to the house.

They snuggled under a cozy blanket, listening to the soft rain patter on the roof. Benny closed his eyes, dreaming of another day filled with adventures and treasures to discover.
