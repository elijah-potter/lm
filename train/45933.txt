What are the 6 main characteristics of blockchain
The blockchain is a shared, immutable ledger that facilitates the process of recording transactions and tracking assets in a transparent, secure and decentralized manner.
A key feature of the blockchain is that it is immutable, meaning that once a transaction is recorded on the ledger, it cannot be altered or removed. This creates a tamper-proof record of all transactions that can be trusted by all parties involved.
Another key feature of the blockchain is that it is distributed. This means that all network participants have a copy of the ledger, providing complete transparency.
Lastly, the blockchain is decentralized, meaning it is not controlled by any single entity. Instead, it relies on a consensus process to ensure the validity of all transactions. This makes it a secure and trustless system that is resistant to fraud.

A blockchain is a distributed database that uses a shared ledger to record transaction data. The key elements of a blockchain are:
1. Distributed ledger technology: all network participants have access to the distributed ledger and its immutable record of transactions.
2. Immutable records: no participant can change or tamper with a transaction after it’s been recorded to the shared ledger.
3. Smart contracts: blockchain technology can be used to create and execute smart contracts, which are self-executing transactions that can be enforced without the need for third-party supervision.
