Title: The Enigma of Natalia Grace

Synopsis:
"The Enigma of Natalia Grace" is a gripping documentary that unravels the perplexing tale of Natalia, a Ukrainian dwarf. Adopted by the Barnett family, suspicions arise as they believe she may be an adult impersonating a child. This explosive film explores the chilling behaviors, accusations, and unanswered questions surrounding Natalia's true identity. Delving into a web of deception and turmoil, the documentary leaves viewers questioning the enigma that is Natalia Grace.
