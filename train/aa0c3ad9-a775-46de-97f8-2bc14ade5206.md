The rain patters softly against the broken glass of the abandoned diner’s window. A flickering neon sign buzzes, casting eerie blue light on the peeling wallpaper. Inside, the hum of a dying refrigerator mixes with the distant sound of a radio tuning into static. The only movement comes from the corner where a half-eaten sandwich sits on a cracked table, forgotten. Outside, a lone figure walks past, carrying a newspaper. Their shadow stretches long and indistinct against the flickering streetlamp. The clock on the wall ticks away, counting down the seconds until the next event—something unspoken but inevitable is about to unfold.
