Passageways of a grand cathedral’s nave, lined with columns of polished marble, stretching toward an unseen altar. The air hums faintly with the echoes of whispered prayers and distant chants. A solitary figure in black robes steps over the threshold, clutching a candle, before disappearing into the shadows beyond the nave’s far end. The path is steep, the steps worn smooth by generations of pilgrims, leading to a hidden courtyard where only the scent of old stone lingers. No doors, only the unspoken promise of what comes next.
