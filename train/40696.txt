A Hundred Billion Trillion Stars is a charming picture book about the vastness of the universe, but it is also about your own uniqueness and importance.
This book is a great way to help you add science topics to your bedtime story hour. You will learn many cool facts. Like the fact that the Earth is covered in three trillion trees. Or that seven billion people weigh about the same as ten quadrillion ants. This stuff is important to know. It is illustrated by New York Times-bestselling artist Isabel Greenberg.
