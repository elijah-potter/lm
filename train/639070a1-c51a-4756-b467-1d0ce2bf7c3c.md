The last train to Black Hollow left at midnight, its smokestack belching coal-black smoke into the frozen night. Inside, Elias sat hunched over a cracked leather satchel, his fingers tracing the edges of a worn map. The village of Hollow’s End lay just past the next bend, but whispers from the old ones warned that the trees there pulsed with something older than men.

A shadow moved behind him. Elias turned—only to find the map’s ink bleeding into his skin. A voice, not his own, whispered, *"Choose."*

**What will you take?**

1. The rusted dagger in the old man’s pocket—its blade hummed with static when held close to the dark.
2. The half-eaten berry pie from the tavern’s counter, still warm in the wrapping.
3. The key from the locked chest in the attic, its surface etched with symbols that glowed faintly.
4. The promise of the stranger’s name, whispered in the wind before the storm: *"Liora."*
