A technician misread the **low-pressure alarm threshold** as **15 PSI** instead of **12 PSI**, causing the system to incorrectly shut down during routine maintenance. The pump was drained prematurely, leading to a **2-hour delay** in restarting the water supply for the adjacent industrial complex. The error was traced to a misaligned gauge reading on the console, where the **minor tick mark** near the 12 PSI line was obscured by a misplaced label. Corrective action included recalibrating the gauge and updating the maintenance log to include the **critical threshold adjustment**.
