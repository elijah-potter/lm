The abandoned subway tunnel at 11th Street, where fluorescent lights flicker erratically in the damp air. Metal grates rusted over, blocking the last few steps to the platform. A single, crumbling poster for a defunct jazz club peels from the wall—*"The Last Note"*—now faded to a ghostly blue. A cracked neon sign still hums faintly, though the power is off. The echoes linger like the hum of a distant subway train. The air smells of damp concrete and old cigarette butts. A single, half-empty cup of coffee sits on a broken bench, its liquid long gone, the bottom crust cracked open. The silence is thick, but not empty—it’s the sound of what was never said, of voices that faded before they could finish. The weight of the world outside presses down, but the tunnel itself feels like a breath held too long.
