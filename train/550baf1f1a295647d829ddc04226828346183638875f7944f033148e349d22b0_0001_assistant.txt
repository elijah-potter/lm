• EU data regulators are fining Meta with a $1.3 billion fine in addition to ordering them to stop transferring data of EU citizens to the U.S.
• The Ireland Data Protection Commission (DPC) made the ruling on the data transfers that were once protected by the Privacy Shield, but are no longer.
• The filing and subsequent ruling relates to a claim by Austrian lawyer, Max Schrems, dating back to 2013.
• A number of caveats will affect the ruling and allow the social media company some allowances, including the fact that only Facebook data is affected and there's a six-month grace period.
