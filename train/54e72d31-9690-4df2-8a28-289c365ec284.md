**The Obsidian Spire of Vorthas’ Maw**
A jagged monolith of blackened crystal, its surface etched with geometric fractals that shift like liquid when the wind howls through the canyon. The air here is thick with the scent of ozone and crushed stone, where the last remnants of a forgotten civilization’s architecture loom—ancient, half-buried doors sealed with runes that flicker when no light touches them. At the base, a pool of deep, still water reflects nothing, only a whisper of a drowned sky. The cliffs drop sheer into an abyss where the wind never stops, carrying whispers of voices that should not exist—some echoing, some lost forever in the static between dimensions. This is where the edges of the world feel closest, where the sky is not always above and the ground is not always beneath.
