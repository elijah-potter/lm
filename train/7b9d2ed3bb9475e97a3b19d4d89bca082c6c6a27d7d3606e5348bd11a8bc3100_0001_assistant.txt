When my brother, King Thutmose, died, his son was too young to rule, so I was named regent in his place. However, I was also the daughter of a King, so I took my place and seized my rightful power. I declared myself pharaoh. 

To gain respect as pharaoh, I began to masculinize my image. I went so far as to wear a false beard, the symbol of a living god that those before me had worn. I had sculptors make my frame more masculine, and I dropped the feminine last "t" from my name, instead going by the more masculine Hatshepsu.

The time under my rule was peaceful and prosperous. I focused on infrastructure and diplomacy, and my government succeeded in increasing trade. I encouraged expeditions to other lands such as an expedition to Punt which brought back timber, gold, frankincense, myrrh, and exotic animals to my people.
