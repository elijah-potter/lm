**Eldermere, October 12, 2023** – Residents of Eldermere are abuzz with excitement as the city’s first-ever annual "Harvest Moon Festival" draws near. Organizers have confirmed the event will take place on October 17th, spanning the evening and night, with festivities centered around the historic Eldermere Market Square. Traditional folk music, artisanal food stalls, and live demonstrations of local crafts will fill the streets. A highlight of the festival will be the grand "Golden Harvest Parade," where participants will parade through the city wearing costumes made from repurposed farm produce and handmade textiles. The event is free to attend and open to all residents and visitors. Eldermere Mayor Elias Carter has urged citizens to join in the celebration, promising it will be a night of community and shared prosperity. Local businesses along the Main Street corridor have already begun preparing booths and decorations, with some vendors offering special discounts to festival-goers. The festival organizers also announced plans for a post-event "Harvest Share" initiative, where attendees can donate unsold produce or goods to local food banks. The city’s cultural center will host a closing ceremony with performances by Eldermere’s famed folk ensemble, the Thornbloom Choir, to wrap up the celebrations. The exact schedule and guest list are still under final review but will be shared in the coming days. For those interested in volunteering, sign-up sheets are available at the Eldermere Chamber of Commerce office.
