**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, Ill. – August 16, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, offering a traditional celebration of agriculture, community, and family entertainment. This year’s fair promises a diverse range of attractions, including livestock exhibits, agricultural displays, a bustling marketplace featuring local artisans and vendors, and thrilling carnival rides and games. Visitors can expect to see demonstrations of traditional crafts, sample local cuisine, and enjoy live music throughout the event.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 23rd through August 28th, 2024, at the Sangamon County Fairgrounds. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM. A special fireworks display will illuminate the sky on Friday, August 25th, following the evening’s entertainment. The fairgrounds are located at 1901 East Lake Shore Drive, Springfield, Illinois.

Admission is $10 for adults, $5 for children ages 6-12, and free for children five and under. Carnival ride tickets and wristbands will be available for purchase onsite.
