Sure! Here are three green foods that are good for your health:

1. Spinach - Spinach is a low-calorie food packed with nutrients. It is a good source of fiber and natural folate, which are very beneficial for people with MTHFR (Methylenetetrahydrofolate reductase). Spinach is also high in potassium and is known to help lower blood pressure.

2. Broccoli - Broccoli is another nutrient-packed food that is very healthy. Broccoli is very high in vitamin C, as well as vitamin K. It is widely regarded as a superfood, particularly among athletes who weight train, and known for its ability to build muscle while also aiding in proper recovery.

3. Beet Greens - Beet Greens are a healthy food that many people may not be as familiar with. They are the part of the beet that grows in the ground and is often thrown away when someone is preparing a meal with beets. However, just one cup is packed with 220% of the daily recommended value of vitamin A, while also having 37% of the daily value of potassium and also 17% of the DV of fiber. They can be mixed into soups, salads, or sauteed and eaten as a side dish.

Although these vegetables have been known to be eaten raw, cooking them by whatever means necessary may be helpful for people who have trouble digesting raw vegetables. Remember to consume every food in moderation and include many different types of foods for your body, and limit the number of foods that you eat that are pre-packaged or come in a container ready to eat.
