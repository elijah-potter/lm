• In the 50s and 60s there was a dire need for African Americans to have the same equality and freedom that all other races had, which is what created the Civil Rights movement.
• One hundred years after slavery was abolished, there was far too much segregation, discrimination, and racially motivated violence that affected the lives of every single African American in the states.
•"Jim Crow" laws barred African Americans from classrooms and bathrooms, theaters, trains, juries, legislatures, and more.
