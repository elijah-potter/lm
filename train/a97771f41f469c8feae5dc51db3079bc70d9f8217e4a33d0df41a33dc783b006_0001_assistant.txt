I spend my days
on top of the ocean,
dancing and twirling
in this magical potion

So often my eyes
wander below
down to the place
I know I can't go

The bottom of the sea,
so deep and so black
I wish I could see it
if only I could hack

This intricate thought
flooding my head
can I go into the deep?
will I end up dead?

I decide to swim
deeper and deeper
it gets darker and darker
and then I see her

The ocean floor,
so deep and so cold
suddenly I wish
I hadn't been so bold
