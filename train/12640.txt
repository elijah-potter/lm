Pronunciation guide: gi·si·lu·gwet
Part of speech: verb animate intransitive
Translation: He/she is able to work
Example of word used in sentence:
Variant spelling(s): gislugwet
Gi's sa'q gislugwet, gatu ne'gaw mu getu' lugwegw.
He/she has been able to work for a long time, but he/she hasn't
wanted to work.
gisilugwei, I am able to work (first person singular animate)
gisilugweieg, we are able to work (first person dual exclusive animate)
gisilugutieg, we are able to work (first person plural exclusive animate)
