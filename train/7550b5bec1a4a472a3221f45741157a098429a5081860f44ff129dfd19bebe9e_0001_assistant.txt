Here we have a copy of Event Horizon. A sci-fi horror film from somewhere in between the 20th and 21st centuries. It became what humans at the time called a  "cult classic". Upon its release, it was considered a flop, it lost money in theaters. It received abominable reviews from critics who dismissed it as lukewarm, overly loud hokum. It turned out it had enduring popularity and cult status, it just had to wait a few decades to amass a following and receive its belated flowers.
