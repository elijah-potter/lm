“What is something my child can do for the rest of their life?” Sing! We are born with the ability to and, often, the desire to sing! Once we have the basics down, singing becomes a great pastime, full of practicing, discipline, excitement, and performance. Whatever genre you enjoy, pop, musical theater, country, opera, or many more, you can perform it all. As one of the few things one can do for their entire life, singing is a great way to take a musical experience to the next level. Enroll into children’s private voice lessons at Gray School of Music.
Students as young as 8 years old can start learning and enjoying the fun of singing. Your child’s teacher will have a graduate degree in vocal performance as well as years of experience teaching all the genres of voice. Parents are always welcome to attend lessons. Music is always a family affair.
Private children’s singing is offered for ages 6-8. The young voice should not be pushed to produce volume so stage presence, diction, and the excitement of singing are enjoyed. Songs that children enjoy such as folk, Disney, and holiday songs will be enjoyed. Assistance with school musicals can also be studied. Occasional group activities may be organized.
Children love to sing. Why not let your child enjoy this wonderful skill.
