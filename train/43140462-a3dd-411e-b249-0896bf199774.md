## Product Requirements Document: "Context" - Mobile Knowledge Synthesis App

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers spend an increasing amount of time switching between disparate sources of information—documents, emails, notes, web pages—struggling to synthesize insights and maintain a cohesive understanding of ongoing projects.

**Vision:** To empower knowledge workers with a mobile application that intelligently consolidates and contextualizes information, fostering deeper understanding and accelerating decision-making.

---

### User Personas

**1. Anya Sharma – The Strategic Consultant:**

*   **Goal:** To quickly grasp the nuances of client engagements, identify key trends, and formulate impactful recommendations.
*   **Pain Points:** Information overload from multiple documents and emails; difficulty connecting disparate pieces of information; time wasted searching for specific details; feeling overwhelmed by the volume of data.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** High; comfortable with cloud storage and mobile productivity tools.

**2. David Chen – The Research Scientist:**

*   **Goal:** To efficiently organize research findings, track experimental data, and synthesize literature reviews for publication.
*   **Pain Points:** Scattered notes and research papers across various platforms; difficulty establishing connections between different research areas; time-consuming process of compiling literature reviews; risk of overlooking critical information.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** Moderate; appreciates intuitive interfaces and robust organization features.

**3. Maria Rodriguez – The Project Manager:**

*   **Goal:** To maintain a clear overview of project progress, track action items, and ensure alignment among team members.
*   **Pain Points:** Difficulty consolidating information from various communication channels; losing track of action items and deadlines; struggling to maintain a holistic view of project status; needing to quickly reference past decisions and discussions.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** Moderate; prioritizes ease of use and accessibility on mobile devices.

---

### Requirements

**1. Intelligent Information Aggregation:** The application must automatically gather content from user-specified sources, including email inboxes, cloud storage accounts (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive), and web pages.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** User can connect to at least three different source types. Content is aggregated within 24 hours of source connection. User receives notification when new content is added.
    *   **Success Metric:** 80% of users connect at least one source within the first week of use.

**2. Contextualized Summarization:** The application must generate concise summaries of aggregated content, highlighting key themes and action items.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Summaries are available for all aggregated content. Summaries are presented in a clear and easily digestible format. User can manually edit generated summaries.
    *   **Success Metric:** 60% of users utilize the summarization feature at least once per week.

**3. Relationship Mapping:** The application must allow users to create and visualize connections between different pieces of aggregated content.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can create custom relationships between content items, labeling them with descriptive tags.  A visual map displays content items and their defined relationships.  Users can easily navigate the relationship map.
    *   **Success Metric:** 40% of users create at least three relationships within the first month of use.

**4. Offline Accessibility:** The application must allow users to access previously aggregated content and relationship maps even without an internet connection.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:**  Content is cached locally upon initial synchronization.  Offline access is consistently available.  Changes made offline are synchronized when a connection is restored.
    *   **Success Metric:** 95% of users report satisfactory offline access experience.

**5. Search and Discovery:** The application must provide a robust search functionality that allows users to quickly find specific content or relationships within their aggregated knowledge base.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Search results are accurate and relevant. Search supports keyword, tag, and content type filtering.  Search performance is consistently fast (under 2 seconds).
    *   **Success Metric:** 70% of users utilize the search functionality at least once per week.



---

### Technical Considerations

*   **Scalability:** The application architecture must be designed to handle a large and growing volume of aggregated content and user data.
*   **Security:** Robust security measures are essential to protect user data and ensure privacy.  Compliance with relevant data protection regulations (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) is mandatory.
*   **Platform Compatibility:** Initial release will target iOS and Android platforms.
*   **Third-Party Integrations:**  Future integrations with other productivity tools (e.g., Slack, Microsoft Teams) should be considered.
*   **Data Storage:**  Utilize a secure and scalable cloud-based storage solution.

### Risks

*   **Data Source Compatibility:** Difficulty integrating with certain data sources due to API limitations or authentication challenges.
*   **User Adoption:** Users may be hesitant to connect personal accounts to a new application.
*   **Accuracy of Summarization:** Generated summaries may occasionally be inaccurate or incomplete.
*   **Performance Degradation:** Large knowledge bases may lead to performance issues on older devices.



### Non-Goals

*   Real-time collaboration features.
*   Direct content editing capabilities within the application.
*   Social networking functionality.
*   Integration with esoteric or niche data sources.
*   Support for desktop operating systems in the initial release.
