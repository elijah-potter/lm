It's true! There are many breeds of domestic cats which are hypoallergenic. These cats are typically hairless or have short to medium fur which does not shed as much. Here are some breeds of hypoallergenic domestic cats:

- Burmese
- Oriental
- Cornish Rex
- California Spangled
- Cymric
- Devon Rex
- Sphynx
- Russian Blue
- Siberian

It's important to know that unless they come from a specific breeder, there's a high chance domestic cats are mix-breeds.
