The daily ritual of mindlessly scrolling through social media, an initially harmless habit born from boredom during the pandemic, gradually expanded to consume an hour each evening. Initially meant to unwind, the screen time crept up—then doubled—until it replaced actual relaxation. Work emails and tasks lingered longer because the habit had hijacked focus. Stress levels rose with frustration over missed deadlines. Eventually, the habit became an addiction, and the person realized they were losing sleep, energy, and relationships to it. The cycle of guilt and relapse grew harder to break without intentional intervention.
