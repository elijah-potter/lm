Ellen Deutsch is a vibrant empty nester living in a remote area of a Florida panhandle community. She is close to her adult children and her family. Still reeling years after her husband leaves her, she meets a young, handsome lover at the local gym where she teaches. They hit it off with a hot and heavy romance, but things take a dark turn as his behavior becomes increasingly erratic and difficult to manage. When they both suddenly disappear, local police turn up empty-handed despite the clear trail of evidence at her home. As her daughter becomes more involved in the search, she follows that trail to a remote fundamentalist community, an unsheltered encampment, and eventually, Louisiana in pursuit of her mother's lover. When she eventually learns the truth, she learns that her mother's lover is a serial con artist and killer whose cons have included the role of a fundamentalist preacher and a string of loverboy scams connected to mysterious deaths.
