## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park Restoration – A Climate-Resilient Community Hub

**Executive Summary**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park, located in the heart of the Elmwood neighborhood, currently represents a missed opportunity. Decades of deferred maintenance have left it overgrown, prone to flooding, and largely unused by residents. This proposal outlines a comprehensive plan to transform this neglected space into a vibrant, climate-resilient community asset: the Willow Creek Pocket Park. Our project will incorporate native plantings, stormwater management infrastructure, accessible pathways, and a small community gathering space, creating a beautiful, functional park that enhances neighborhood resilience, promotes biodiversity, and fosters social connection. This project directly aligns with the municipality's commitment to urban greening, climate adaptation, and equitable access to public spaces, and we request \$75,000 in funding to initiate this vital transformation, leveraging \$25,000 in committed volunteer labor and in-kind donations.

**Project Plan: Phases and Deliverables**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park Restoration will be executed in four distinct phases, each with clearly defined responsibilities and deliverables.

**Phase 1: Assessment and Design (Months 1-2) – Lead: Elmwood Neighborhood Association (ENA), Partner: Municipal Parks Department (MPD) Landscape Architect**

*   **Deliverables:** Site assessment report detailing existing conditions and drainage patterns; finalized design plans incorporating community feedback; permit applications submitted to the municipality.
*   **ENA Responsibilities:** Facilitate community workshops to gather input on design preferences; coordinate with MPD Landscape Architect.
*   **MPD Responsibilities:** Provide technical expertise in landscape design and stormwater management; review and approve final design plans.

**Phase 2: Site Preparation and Stormwater Infrastructure (Months 3-5) – Lead: GreenThumb Volunteers, Partner: MPD Maintenance Crew**

*   **Deliverables:** Removal of invasive species and debris; installation of rain gardens and bioswales to manage stormwater runoff; grading and leveling of the site.
*   **GreenThumb Responsibilities:** Organize and supervise volunteer labor for site clearing and initial grading.
*   **MPD Responsibilities:** Provide heavy equipment (backhoe, dump truck) for site preparation and assist with grading.

**Phase 3: Planting and Pathway Construction (Months 6-8) – Lead: Elmwood Garden Club, Partner: Local Nursery (In-Kind Donation)**

*   **Deliverables:** Installation of native trees, shrubs, and wildflowers; construction of accessible pathways using permeable paving materials; installation of park benches and a small picnic table.
*   **Elmwood Garden Club Responsibilities:** Source and plant native species, providing horticultural expertise; organize volunteer planting days.
*   **Local Nursery Responsibilities:** Donate native plants and provide horticultural advice.

**Phase 4: Community Activation and Park Dedication (Month 9-10) – Lead: ENA, Partner: Elmwood Elementary School**

*   **Deliverables:** Grand opening celebration for the community; educational signage detailing the park's features and ecological benefits; ongoing programming for children and families.
*   **ENA Responsibilities:** Plan and execute grand opening event; develop and implement ongoing park programming.
*   **Elmwood Elementary School Responsibilities:** Collaborate on educational programming and encourage student participation in park stewardship activities.

**Budget Transparency**

The total project cost is estimated at \$100,000. We are requesting \$75,000 from the municipality, complemented by \$25,000 in committed volunteer labor and in-kind donations. A detailed breakdown is as follows:

*   **Materials (Native Plants, Permeable Paving, Benches, Picnic Table, Signage):** \$45,000. This includes the cost of sourcing high-quality, drought-tolerant native plants from local nurseries, permeable paving materials for accessible pathways, durable park benches, a picnic table, and interpretive signage detailing the park’s ecological benefits.
*   **Equipment Rental (Backhoe, Dump Truck):** \$10,000. This covers the rental of heavy equipment for site preparation, provided by the Municipal Parks Department at a reduced rate.
*   **Professional Services (Landscape Architect Consultation):** \$15,000. This covers the initial design consultation with the MPD Landscape Architect and any necessary revisions based on community feedback.
*   **Volunteer Coordination & Supplies (Tools, Gloves, Refreshments):** \$5,000. This budget supports the coordination of volunteer labor, including the purchase of necessary tools, gloves, and refreshments for volunteer workdays.
*   **Contingency (10%):** \$10,000. A contingency fund is essential to address unforeseen challenges such as unexpected site conditions or material price fluctuations. This ensures project completion despite potential setbacks.

The committed volunteer labor, valued at \$25,000, represents a significant contribution to the project's success and reduces the overall financial burden on the municipality.  The in-kind donation of native plants from the local nursery further demonstrates community support and commitment.

**Community Engagement and Equity Impacts**

This project prioritizes equitable access and community engagement throughout all phases. Initial design workshops were intentionally held at accessible locations and advertised in multiple languages.  The design incorporates accessible pathways and universally designed play features.  We are actively engaging with the Elmwood Elementary School to ensure that the park serves as an outdoor learning environment for children of all backgrounds.  The project's focus on native plantings and stormwater management directly benefits the Elmwood neighborhood, which has historically experienced localized flooding.

**Risk Mitigation**

Potential risks have been identified and mitigation strategies developed.  Unexpected site conditions (e.g., buried debris) will be addressed through the contingency fund.  Weather-related delays will be accommodated by adjusting the project timeline.  Volunteer recruitment and retention will be supported through ongoing communication and appreciation events.  Liability insurance coverage will be secured for all volunteer workdays.

**Post-Grant Sustainability**

Long-term sustainability is ensured through a collaborative stewardship model. The Elmwood Neighborhood Association and Elmwood Garden Club will assume primary responsibility for ongoing park maintenance. The Municipal Parks Department will provide technical support and coordinate with volunteer groups. Educational signage will promote responsible park usage and encourage community ownership.  The project’s focus on drought-tolerant native plantings minimizes long-term maintenance requirements.
