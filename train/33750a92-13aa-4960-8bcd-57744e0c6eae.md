The discovery of **cryptographic hash functions** (e.g., SHA-256) fundamentally altered digital security by enabling **tamper-proof data integrity checks** and **secure authentication**. This small but transformative algorithm became the backbone of blockchain technology, digital signatures (e.g., ECDSA), and secure protocols like TLS, reshaping cybersecurity infrastructure.

---
**Key Impact:** Enabled **immutable ledgers**, **authenticated transactions**, and **unforgeable digital identities**—reshaping trust in decentralized systems.
