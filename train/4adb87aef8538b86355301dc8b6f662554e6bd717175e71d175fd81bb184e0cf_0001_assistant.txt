Here are some ideas for animals to draw:
*Dog: Dogs make an easy reference as there are many out there.  They have many different sizes and looks that should help you practice.
*Horse: A horse will make you study body structure and hooves as well.
*Bird: Birds are a great choice as there are many different varieties to choose from and they offer some unique characteristics like beaks to practice
*Cat: Cats are known for their flexibility and should offer some good choices when wanting a little diversity in your learning of form.
*Elephant: This will offer some practice in drawing thicker animals and tusks which may help you draw horns later on.
*Deer: Hopefully this will offer some simple practice with antler drawings.
*Bear: Another larger animal that should help you practice drawing shaggy fur and a typically more compact figure.
*Wolf: This animal should offer some practice with snouts, paws, fur, and eyes.
*Snake: Drawing a snake will work on your ability to draw patterns that flow throughout the animal.  It will also help if you ever want to begin drawing reptiles.
*Fish: This should test your ability to draw fins and more smooth shapes.  You will most likely find yourself drawing patterns for scales as well as offering up more practice.
