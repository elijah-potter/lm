To boil an egg, first, choose a cute little egg that you'd like to turn into a delicious treat. Place the egg gently in a small saucepan and cover it with water, making sure it's all snuggled in. Put the saucepan on the stovetop and heat it up on medium-high. Let the water come to a gentle boil, just like a happy little bubble bath for the egg. Once it's boiling, reduce the heat to low and let the egg simmer peacefully for about 4-6 minutes for a soft-boiled egg or 8-12 minutes for a hard-boiled egg.
