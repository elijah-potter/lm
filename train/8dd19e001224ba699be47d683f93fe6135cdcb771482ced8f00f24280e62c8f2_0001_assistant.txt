The chaos that The Joker had brought down on the street was all around them. Flames licked the side of buildings and cars were turned upside down on the road, shattered glass on the concrete. Batman had fortunately gotten all of the civilians out and away from the area on time, but it didn't seem to deter The Joker. He stood a few feet away, a grin lighting up his pale white face, dancing from foot to foot with the anticipation and excitement of what was going to come next. Despite Batman's strength, he was confident.

That confidence was misplaced though. Batman knew he would win this. His gloved hands were curled into fists at his side, his head going over what could have happened here if he hadn't saved these people in time. Even through his mask, the anger was obvious, but The Joker only seemed to delight in it. His dancing didn't stop as he took a step closer to see Batman's anger. In fact, his smile only spread wider.

"You're confident," Batman remarked in his gruff voice.

"You have to lose someday," The Joker responded, bringing his hands up and rubbing them together in childish glee. "Why wouldn't that day be today?"

Batman grimaced, but it was barely visible beneath his costume. "I don't have to lose someday," he answered and with that, he closed the distance between him and The Joker, ready to put an end to the supervillain's antics.
