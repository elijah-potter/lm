Depending on the problem and the data available, Machine Learning can be implemented to solve a large variety of problems. This includes basic, nonrepetitive tasks like image detection, driving, pattern recognition, and text generation. With the right amount of data and the correct algorithm, computers may be able to match or exceed humans in these tasks. Further, Machine Learning is especially helpful where the volume of data or complexity of the problem goes beyond the human mind. This includes areas of the internet, deep physics, financial data, and medical diagnoses among others. Machine Learning can already exceed human capabilities in some of these areas and is well on its way in others.
