Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown fur and big, round ears that always twitched when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny spotted a shiny red apple near the fence. "Mmm, I wonder if it’s ripe?" he thought. Benny took a tiny bite and giggled because the apple was so sweet!

Just then, Benny heard a tiny *crinkle* sound. Peeking around the bush, he saw a little mouse named Milo with his paws in his ears. "Milo, what are you doing here?" Benny asked.

"Looking for my favorite acorn!" Milo squeaked. "But the wind blew it far away!"

Benny had an idea. "Come with me! I’ll help you find it." Together, they chased the windy acorn all around the garden until they found it hiding behind a big, leafy tree. Milo grinned. "You’re the best friend ever!"

From that day on, Benny and Milo became the best of friends, always exploring together and sharing their adventures. And they knew that even the smallest friends could do the biggest things—like find lost treasures and make new ones!
