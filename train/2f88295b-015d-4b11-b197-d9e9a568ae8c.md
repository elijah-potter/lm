In a quiet village nestled between mist-shrouded hills, there was a clocktower that never lost time. Its hands turned slowly, marking the hours with a steady rhythm. One day, a young girl, Lina, found an old, tattered map inside an abandoned bookstore. The map showed a circle with a single village at its center, encircled by a path leading back to itself.

She followed the path, passing through forests where fireflies flickered like stars, and crossed a bridge where the river sang lullabies. Along the way, she met a talking owl who warned her: *"The circle closes, but some doors never open."*

Lina reached the edge of the village, where the clocktower stood silent. She stepped through the archway, but the moment her foot touched the stone, the village vanished. She stood in the same bookstore, the map clutched in her hand, the owl’s voice echoing: *"You’ve come full circle."*

The bookstore’s shelves rearranged themselves, and the map reappeared in its original spot, its edges frayed and worn. The tattered pages fluttered as if whispering: *"Now what?"*
