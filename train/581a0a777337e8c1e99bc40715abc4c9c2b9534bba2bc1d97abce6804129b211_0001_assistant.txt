Early one morning, at the break of dawn
The cat went out for a stroll
He came upon a hen he guessed
But she had her head in the food bowl.

Her chicks, they said, our friendship
Sure was gonna be hard
They never did like his whiskers
His paws weren't big enough. 

They ate bugs together and
Lay in the sun
Lucky just to have some food
Tangled up in fun.

They sat by the water trough
Thinking of things to say, but
Split up on a breezy, cool morning
Before the break of the day.

He thought of her when times got rough
Wishing they'd not fought
But he'd called her bluff
Which he might have not ought
Now he's just one.
