A pause between the last keystroke and the first heartbeat of the morning—
where the city’s breath still lingers in the air like the scent of old books,
the weight of a half-finished thought pressing against the ribs of memory.
Outside, a child’s laughter cuts through the quiet like a blade, sharp and fleeting.
The door creaks open, and in slides a stranger’s shadow, not knowing this pause,
this space where time has stopped to listen. The clock ticks, but the hands
move backward, tracing the edges of what was never said.
A book falls from a shelf, its spine cracked open—pages whisper secrets
only the air understands. The pause is the place where ghosts and light
dance in the same frame, where every second feels like an eternity
and every heartbeat is a question answered in silence.
