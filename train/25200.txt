The Tuesday letter “Teachers want to use parents as scapegoats” from Robert Cooperman ignored the reality that a child's education is actually a team effort.
The parents provide basic moral values and create a desire for learning. They have five to six years to establish this foundation before the child begins formal education. The schoolteacher builds on those basics by teaching facts and skills necessary for a successful career, an understanding of other cultures and the ability to continue the learning process throughout life.
Teachers trust that the parents have started values training and basic respect by the time children enroll in a formal education program.
The most successful students are those whose parents already have done their job of teaching basic moral values and creating a desire for learning. This gives the teachers the foundation they need to do their jobs properly.
If the parents have properly taught their values to their preschoolers, no formal teacher can change those values. Nor would they want to; they want to show only that there are other cultures and how to live without conflict among those cultures.
- STANLEY D. KRIDER
