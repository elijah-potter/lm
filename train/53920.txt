Shelby is a county in Missouri. It was formed in 1835 from the following county/ies: Marion. Shelby began keeping birth records in 1883–87, marriage records in 1835, and death records in 1883–87. It began keeping land records in 1835, probate records in 1836, and court records in 1835. For more information, contact the county at Shelbyville 63469. On the attached map, Shelby is located at C5.
