Sure! That's great that you're planning on getting more exercise! Here are a few ideas for you that may be fun and may not feel so much like exercises:

1. Biking - try finding a new route to take on your bike, so you're doing some exploring instead of just pedaling.

2. Dog walking - you can share a walk with your furry friend and you can have some bonding time that may not feel like a chore.

3. Skipping rope - find some fun rope skipping routines for beginners online and try them out.

4. Frisbee - take a frisbee to the park and have a little gathering with friends.

5. Swimming - at a pool or at the beach, swimming can be a lot of fun and can be relaxing, and you'd be surprised how much of a workout it still is.
