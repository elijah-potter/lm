First mentioned in Episode 6 of Season 10, which is also titled ManBearPig, the ManBearPig is a creature described by a fictional depiction of Al Gore as "half human, half bear, and half pig."  While ManBearPig is only described in that first episode, he physically appears in the Imationationland trilogy, which is made up of Episodes 10, 11, and 12 of Season 11, proving that the ManBearPig is a figment of the fictional Gore's imagination.

TheManBearPig has been stated to be an allegory of global warming, showing that his fake status is still influenced by something real.
