Although the first unrefueled flight across the Atlantic was accomplished in 1927, the planet-spanning trip wouldn’t happen for another 59 years.

The Rutan Model 76 Voyager was an extremely lightweight aircraft designed specifically for this task. Though sporting a wingspan of 110 feet, it weighed only 2,250 pounds empty. Fully fueled and stocked, though, it clocked in at 9,698 pounds. 

Pilots Jenna Yeager and Dick Rutan lifted off on 14 December 1986 for the historic flight. They took shifts piloting the craft, staying aloft for nine consecutive days. For most of the flight, they cruised at around 11,000 feet. When they landed, they had only 1.5% of their original fuel load remaining. The plane is now on display at the National Air and Space Museum in Washington D.C.
