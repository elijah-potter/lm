Automotive mechanics generally will need training or schooling beyond a high school diploma, which could include going to a vocational or technical school or completing an associate degree, and will usually require completing a certification. Typically, the whole process will take around two years, but program lengths vary. Auto body technicians need a minimum of a high school diploma, but many jobs require a certificate from a technical school (which usually takes a year to receive) or an associate degree in the field (which would take two years).
