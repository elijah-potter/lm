Introducing the César E. Chávez Experience
The César E. Chávez Experience was created to provide educational experiences for students. It has been designed to provide an understanding of the plight of the migrant worker and Chávez fight for them. The experience currently has three simulations for students to dig into the Migrant Workers Rights Movement.
- Town Hall Simulation
- Primary Source Analysis
- Boycott Simulation
New experiences and resources will be added as they are designed and vetted.
