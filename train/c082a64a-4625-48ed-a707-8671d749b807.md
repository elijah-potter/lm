**"The Clockwork Heart"**

Beneath the neon glow of a city that never slept,
she found the last man in a room with no name.
His hands, too many joints, his breath a hiss through rusted pipes.
He spoke in the language of gears and forgotten scripts:

*"The world’s a machine, child. You just haven’t learned how to fix it yet."*

The door creaked open. Inside, a single, cold bulb burned low—
the kind that hummed when it died. She reached for the switch.
But his fingers, too long, brushed hers before she could pull it.

Now, the city hums louder.
