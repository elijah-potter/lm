Once upon a time in a cozy little house, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around the garden, chasing butterflies and collecting shiny acorns. One sunny morning, Benny heard a tiny voice.

"Help me, Benny! My flower is wilting!"

It was a little ladybug named Lily. Her favorite flower, the sunflower, was drooping. Benny hopped over to help. He nudged the wilting petals with his nose and whispered, "Don't worry, Lily! I'll make you bright again!"

Benny sang a happy song while gently watering the flower. Soon, the sunflower stood tall and proud again, its petals glowing like the sun. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Benny!"

From that day on, Benny and Lily became the best of friends. Whenever Lily's flower needed help, Benny was always there to cheer it up. And they all lived happily ever after, sharing sunshine and friendship in the garden.
