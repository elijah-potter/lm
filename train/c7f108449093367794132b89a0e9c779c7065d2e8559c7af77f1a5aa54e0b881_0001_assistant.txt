Sorry to hear you're struggling with stress at work! Here are some ideas for how to reduce stress.

1. List what you must accomplish and focus on higher-priority tasks first. This can help remove stress by getting important items out of the way.
2. Make sure to take breaks, as working for long hours can lead to productivity burnout. In the end, breaks make you work harder.
3. Ask your colleagues or your boss for help if they can take on some of your tasks.
4. Ask for support from experienced members of your team who may be able to suggest more efficient and quicker ways to get something done.
5. Don't forget to take a moment to celebrate the successes, as you'll be more motivated.
