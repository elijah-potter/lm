In a quiet village at dawn, the elder walked past the crowded marketplace, his staff tapping lightly against the cobblestones. The younger son hesitated, drawn by the promise of fresh bread and laughter from the bakery’s open windows. The elder’s voice, weary but firm, carried across the alley: *"The path of wisdom is never the quickest, but the one that leads where the heart truly calls."* He turned toward the mist-shrouded hills, his boots crunching gravel as he walked away. The younger son followed the bread’s scent, choosing the longer road instead. By evening, the hills were his, but the marketplace’s warmth had faded into regret. The elder’s words echoed: *"Long paths are never lost."*
