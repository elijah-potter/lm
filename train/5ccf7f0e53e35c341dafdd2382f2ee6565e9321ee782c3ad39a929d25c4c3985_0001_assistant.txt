I was sitting in English class today and daydreaming about Miss Bowie's famous apple pie. I started drooling! I decided that I could use the few dollars my mom gave me for lunch for some apple pie instead; I didn't think she would mind much.

After school, I walked down to Miss Bowie's bakery with my friend Eliza. I always tell Eliza how Miss Bowie's pie is the greatest pie EVER but she never wants to come with me. I was happy that she came with me today. 

I walked in and immediately could smell all the delicious pies. I went to where I knew the apple pie would be in the window and realized it was empty. My heart sank! I had been looking forward to this one pie all day. I couldn't believe it was gone. Miss Bowie said they would have some more tomorrow but I didn't think I could wait until then! Instead, I got a piece of blueberry pie but it just will never be as good as Miss Bowie's apple pie.
