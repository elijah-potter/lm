There are three Donkey Kong Country games for the Super Nintendo Entertainment System:
1) The first Donkey Kong game, Donkey Kong Country, was released in 1994.
2) The next game, Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddie's Kong Quest, came out in 1995.
3) The final game, Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!, debuted in 1996.
