Then when she goes into the forest, what she loves to do most is explore and go searching for things due to her curious and adventurous nature. This can create conflict and excitement in the story since Fancy has to evade the hawk from catching her when it appears. Of course, since it's a family-friendly story suitable for children, Fancy is always able to escape the hawk and she never gets caught.

While she's out exploring this new area of the forest, a hawk appears and Fancy has to run and hide from the hawk. Fortunately, Buddy shows up and barks at the hawk to scare it away and rescue Fancy.
