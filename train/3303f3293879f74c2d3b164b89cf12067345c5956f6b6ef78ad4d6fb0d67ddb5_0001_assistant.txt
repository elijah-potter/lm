The largest organ in the human body is the skin. The skin consists of two layers - the outer epidermis and the inner dermis. The epidermis is the layered protective barrier that shields us from the outside world. The dermis contains connective tissues, nerve endings, sweat glands, hair follicles, and blood vessels. The skin plays a crucial role in protecting our body and regulating temperature. Its surface area in an average adult is around 20 square feet, making it by far the largest organ. The skin protects us from pathogens, helps insulate the body, and facilitates sensations such as touch, heat, and cold.
