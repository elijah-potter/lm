Though it is known that Aphra Behn was born in 1640, little else is known about her early life. Biographers surmise that her father was named Johnson, that she traveled to Surinam, married Johan Behn in 1864, and was widowed by 1866. It is further believed that during the Second Anglo-Dutch War, she worked as a spy in Bruges but wound up in a London debtor’s prison. Having written a novel, Oroonoko in 1688, she began writing plays for the King’s Company and the Duke’s Company including The Forc’d Marriage, The Amorous Prince, and The Dutch Lover. In 1677, her play, The Rover, won critical and commercial success and allowed her to have a successful career as a playwright. In addition to plays, Behn wrote poetry, literary translations, and novels until she died in 1689. She was buried in Westminster Abbey.
