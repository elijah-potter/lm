Of course! Here is a list of 5 things to do in Rocky Point with kids:

1. Water Activities: Water sports are very popular in Rocky Point. Kayaking, paddle boarding, and snorkeling are just a few options. While you are at the beach, make sure to look for shells!
2. ATV Excursions There are many places to rent ATVs and it's a great way to spend the day exploring. There are nature reserves, dunes, and beaches you can explore.
3. Ziplining: Taking zipline adventure is very popular in Rocky Point with many options to choose from.
4. Boating Adventures: There are a ton of options for boating in Rocky Point. You can rent a boat or go on an excursion with a dinner cruise or a fishing boat. 
5. Restaurants: The food is very popular in Rocky Point. Local dives and more touristy restaurants will give you plenty of options!
