A tiny silver ring glints under fluorescent lights as it descends from the wrist of a figure hunched over a keyboard, fingers tapping rapidly. The next hand—smooth, aged—grips it, turning it to examine the faint engraving: a crescent moon and a single star. The ring passes through a crowded lab, fingers brushing against others, a brief, unspoken exchange. By the time it reaches the next recipient, it’s already warm, the metal humming with unseen energy. The cycle repeats, unbroken.
