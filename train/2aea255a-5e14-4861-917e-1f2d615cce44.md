The rain slicked the cobblestones as **Marcus** slammed the door behind him, his fist still clenched around the bottle of whiskey. **"You *promised* this!"** he shouted, voice raw with exhaustion. **"We *swear* on our moms’ graves we’d go to this place—somewhere warm, somewhere *quiet*—before the season ends!"**

**Lena** spun, her dark eyes sharp with betrayal. She crossed her arms, the leather jacket she’d worn all night now damp against her skin. **"You left me in that dive, Marcus. You left me with *nothing* but a train ticket and a half-empty wallet. What kind of plan is that—*your* plan?"**

Outside, the neon glow of the city pulsed against the wet pavement. A distant siren wailed, cutting through the tension. **He turned back, hands trembling.** **"Lena… I was trying to figure out a way out. You don’t understand—"** *"Understand what?"* She cut him off, voice low, venomous. **"That you’ve been *running* for three years? That every time we talk, you’re already planning your next escape?"**

The air between them crackled, thick with the weight of secrets. Then—**a shout**. A familiar voice, muffled by the rain. **Marcus’s breath hitched.** *"Damn it, man, you’re soaked!"* **Darius**, their old friend from college, stepped into the glow of a flickering streetlamp, his own coat dripping. **"You two look like you’ve been fighting since you were born. Or since you both realized you couldn’t keep up."*

Silence. Then—**Lena’s laugh**, bitter and dry. **"Guess we’ll see who’s running *now*."*
