Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the meadow. One sunny morning, Benny saw a shiny red ball rolling near his burrow. "Hop!" Benny said, and he chased after it.

The ball bounced up and down, and Benny giggled when it landed near him. But then, it rolled away again. Benny hopped after it, but it kept going. Benny met a friendly fox named Felix who said, "Want to play a game?"

Felix showed Benny how to catch the ball with his paws. Benny tried and tried, but it kept slipping out. Just then, Benny’s big sister, Bella, came running. She had a big basket of carrots and said, "Let’s share!"

Bella gave Benny some carrots to munch on, and Felix shared his acorn treats. Benny felt happy and tired, so he curled up with Bella and Felix in his burrow for a cozy nap.

The next morning, Benny woke up and saw the ball was still there, but it was now rolling toward a big tree. Benny hopped after it and found a tiny bird’s nest with a soft feather. "Oh, look!" Benny said, and he gently placed the feather in the nest.

The bird chirped happily, and Benny knew he had made a friend. From that day on, Benny always checked on the nest and shared his carrots with the bird. And every afternoon, the ball would roll toward the tree, reminding Benny that kindness and sharing make the world a happier place.
