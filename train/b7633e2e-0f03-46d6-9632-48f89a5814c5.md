Dusk spilled through the cracked window, painting the hallway in shifting amber and violet. A flicker of movement at the foot of the stairs—too fast for a shadow, too deliberate for a breeze. The figure in the doorway, arms crossed, turned slowly. Their breath fogged the air, but not from cold. A name whispered on the wind, half-remembered: *"Lena."* The camera lingered on the empty space where their reflection once stood, then snapped back to the floor, where something glinted under the dying streetlight—a small, smooth stone, no larger than a coin, but with edges too sharp to be natural. The screen cut to static.
