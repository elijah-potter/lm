**BREAKING: Unidentified Aerial Phenomena Detected Over New Haven’s Sky**
Residents of New Haven reported seeing multiple, unexplained objects hovering near Lake Compounce at dawn. Witnesses described metallic, disc-shaped formations with rapid, erratic movements. Police and emergency services responded to scattered sightings across the city. Authorities urge residents to avoid the area and report any further observations.

**Local Officials Warn of Potential "Sky Watch" Initiatives**
Mayor Elena Carter addressed a press conference, stating, *"We are coordinating with the Federal Aviation Administration and national security agencies to assess this situation. The city is implementing temporary restrictions on flights and public gatherings near Lake Compounce."*

**No Explanations Yet from Scientists**
Dr. Marcus Chen of New Haven University stated, *"Initial analysis suggests no conventional explanation—could be atmospheric phenomena, but we’re conducting tests to rule out drones or natural formations. This remains an open investigation."*

**Community Gathers for "First Light Vigil"**
A spontaneous gathering formed near the city’s downtown plaza, where locals and tourists held candles and recorded the sky with phones. Social media exploded with theories, from "alien abduction" to "mysterious weather balloons." Hashtags #NewHavenSkyWatch and #UnidentifiedObjects trending globally.

**No Immediate Threats, But Caution Advised**
New Haven’s Department of Public Safety advised, *"Do not approach the objects. If you witness them, record details and report to 911. Authorities will provide updates as more data becomes available."*
