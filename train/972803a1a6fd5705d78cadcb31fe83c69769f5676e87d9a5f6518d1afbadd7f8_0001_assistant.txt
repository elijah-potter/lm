1. The Derby Museum-if there's one thing that Louisville is known for its horse racing. This museum located just minutes from the home of the Kentucky Derby, Churchill Downs, will walk you through the history of horseracing in the Bluegrass state.
2. Louisville Slugger Museum & Factory-if you're a baseball fan this is a can't-miss stop. After watching the world-famous bats being made you can tour the museum and view bats used by some of baseball's greatest legends.
3. The Speed Art Museum-this museum located on the University of Louisville's campus has something for every art lover. You'll find Native American and African art as well as contemporary and classic exhibits.
4. Frazier Kentucky History Museum-at this museum you'll learn about Kentucky and its long history of bourbon production as well as significant historical Kentucky events and artifacts. 
5. The Muhammad Ali Center- this museum features an interactive overview of the life and career of boxing legend and Louisville native Muhammad Ali.
