Dave weighs 175 pounds and can bench press three times his body weight.  Craig can only bench press 20% of the amount Dave can.  Mark is new and can bench press 50 pounds less than Craig.  How much can Mark bench press?
Dave weighs 175 pounds and can bench press three times his body weight, so he can bench press 175*3 = <<175*3=525>>525 pounds
Craig can only bench press 20% of Dave's 525 pounds, so Craig can bench press .20*525 = <<20*.01*525=105>>105 pounds
Craig bench presses 105 pounds and Mark does 50 pounds less than Craig, so Mark can only press 105-50 = <<105-50=55>>55 pounds
