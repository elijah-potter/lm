Ms. Warren ran at 6 mph for 20 minutes. After the run, she walked at 2 mph for 30 minutes. How many miles did she run and walk in total?
20 minutes is 20/60=1/3 of an hour.
Ms. Warren ran 6/3=<<6/3=2>>2 miles.
30 minutes is 30/60=1/2 of an hour.
Ms. Warren walked 2/2=<<2/2=1>>1 mile.
Ms Warren ran and walked 2+1=<<2+1=3>>3 miles in total.
