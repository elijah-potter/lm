**BREAKING:** Officials in the historic coastal city of **Veldros** announce discovery of a submerged ancient ruin beneath the docks, dating back to the **Iron Age Empire**. Archaeologists, led by Dr. Elara Veyne, uncovered artifacts including a **carved obsidian sword**, a **bronze ritual amphora**, and fragments of **glossy black stone** that may be a lost trade route marker. Local historians speculate this site could link to the fabled **Veldrosian Seafaring League**, a civilization once known for its maritime dominance. The city council has declared a **3-day "Ruins of Veldros" public viewing period** (starting Friday at 10 AM) to allow residents to explore the site, though authorities warn of unstable ground conditions. No fatalities reported in excavation. Authorities urge visitors to stay on marked paths.
