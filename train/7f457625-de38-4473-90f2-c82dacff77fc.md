A hyper-connected metropolis of glass and steel, where digital holograms flicker between public squares and private residences. AI-driven infrastructure reroutes traffic in real-time via holographic signage, while autonomous drones patrol streets that adapt to pedestrian flow via neural networks. Citizens wear augmented-reality glasses that sync with city-wide smart systems, allowing instant translation, environmental data overlays, and social interactions that blur physical and digital boundaries. Streets shift configurations daily based on predictive analytics, and buildings grow or shrink based on energy needs and occupancy—yet the human population lags behind, clinging to outdated routines while the environment evolves unpredictably faster than they can adjust.
