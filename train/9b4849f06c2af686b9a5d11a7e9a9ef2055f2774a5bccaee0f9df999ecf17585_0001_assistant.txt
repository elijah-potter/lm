Here are the last 3 winners of the FIFA World Cup:

1. 2022 World Cup:
The final was held on 18 December 2022 in Lusail, Qatar and the 2 competing teams were Argentina and France. The match ended in a draw after full-time and extra time and the match went to penalty kicks. Argentina won the game 4-2 on penalty kicks, the players that converted penalties for Argentina are Lionel Messi, Paulo Dybala, Leandro Parades and Gonzalo Montiel.
 
2. 2018 World Cup:
The final was held on July 15, 2018, in Moscow, Russia and the 2 competing teams were Croatia and France. The final score in the match was 4-2, with France being the victors. The goals for France were a Croatian own goal in the 18th minute, a penalty converted by Antoine Griezmann in the 38th minute, a goal by Paul Pogba in the 59th minute, and the final goal was scored by Kylian Mbappe in the 65th minute.

3. 2014 World Cup:
The final was held on July 13, 2014, in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil and the 2 competing teams were Argentina and Germany. The match ended in a draw after full-time and the match was won in extra time by Germany when the winning goal was scored in the 113th minute by Mario Götze.
