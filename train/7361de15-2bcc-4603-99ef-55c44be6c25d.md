**Title: "The Loop"**

The café’s hum was a steady pulse, the kind that lulled you into a half-dream before the morning rush. Across the table, Liam’s fingers tapped restlessly against the wooden edge, his knit sweater unraveling slightly at the cuffs.

*"You know, the world’s not worth it,"* he muttered, leaning in so close his breath smelled like stale coffee and regret. *"Every damn thing’s just a loop, a circle we’re stuck in."*

She exhaled, watching him. *"Loop?"*

*"The same old shit. Love, failure, the same damn mistakes. It’s like we’re running on a track we can’t see the end of."*

She sighed, stirring her tea. *"Then why do we keep running?"*

Liam’s eyes darkened. *"Because we’re afraid to stop."* His voice dropped, lower now. *"Because the silence… it’s worse than the noise."* He paused, then repeated, louder: *"The world’s not worth it."*

Her pen hovered over the notebook, tracing the same word—*"Loop"*—twice before she scribbled it out. The café door creaked open. A server’s voice: *"Another refill, sir?"*

Outside, the sun dipped low, painting the alley in gold. Somewhere, a car horn honked. Liam’s phone buzzed. *"Another one."*
