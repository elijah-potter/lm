Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Pip. Pip had fluffy white ears that bounced when he ran, and a tail that wiggled like a spring.

One sunny morning, Pip saw a tiny red flower with a yellow center. "Mmm, looks yummy!" he thought. He carefully plucked it and took a big, happy bite. The flower turned a little pink inside his mouth.

A friend, a wise old turtle named Tilly, was nearby. "That's not a flower, Pip," Tilly said kindly. "It's a bug. Bugs are tiny creatures, and some can bite if they're scared." Pip's ears drooped. He looked at the bug and felt a tiny bit sad.

Pip decided to be brave. "I won't eat it," he said. "Let's play with it instead." He sat down and gently placed the bug on his nose. The bug slowly crawled up his nose and onto his ear. Pip giggled. "It feels so soft!"

Tilly smiled. "See? Even bugs can be fun if you're kind." Pip nodded and realized that being kind made everything better.

From that day on, Pip learned that sharing and kindness could turn even the smallest things into the biggest adventures. And every day, he would explore the garden, play with his friends, and grow up happy and brave.
