Scout delivers groceries on the weekends.  His base pay is $10.00 an hour.  He also earns a $5.00 tip per customer that he delivers groceries to.  On Saturday he worked 4 hours and delivered groceries to 5 people.  Sunday he worked 5 hours and delivered groceries to 8 people.  How much did he make over the weekend?
He worked 4 hours on Saturday and 5 hours on Sunday for a total of 4+5 = <<4+5=9>>9 hours
His base pay is $10.00 an hour and he worked 9 hours for a total of 10*9 = $<<10*9=90.00>>90.00
He delivered groceries to 5 people on Saturday and 8 people on Sunday for a total of 5+8 = <<5+8=13>>13 deliveries
He makes $5.00 per delivery and he delivered to 13 people so he made 5*13 = $<<5*13=65.00>>65.00
His base pay is $90.00 and he made $65.00 in tips for a total of 90+65 = $<<90+65=155.00>>155.00
