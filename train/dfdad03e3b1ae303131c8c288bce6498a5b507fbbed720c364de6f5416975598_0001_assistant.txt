
Project related 
1. Contact Details: Name, business/organization, email address 
2. What stage are you at in this project?
3. What is your overall aim/objective of the project? 
4. How long do you anticipate this role/project to last?
5. What is your estimated budget?
6. What is my exact role as a writer? (ex. written content, video content, editing, proofreading, fact-checking)
7. Will you set deadlines or will I set my own?
8. Is there any software I should download or platforms to become acquainted with?
9. Will my byline be published alongside my work?
10. What method of communication do you prefer? (ex. Email, Slack, Upwork)
11. How will payment be processed? (ex. PayPal, Payoneer, Upwork)

Content related 
12. Do you have a style guide?
13. What is your target audience/reader/consumer?
14. Do you allow AI-generated content for your project?
15. What level of research do you expect? (ex. Referencing academic studies versus referencing Healthline)
16. Are you expecting optimized content and if so, will SEO keywords be provided?
17. How many rounds of edits do you anticipate?
