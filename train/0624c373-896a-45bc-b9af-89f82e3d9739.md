Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around, eat carrots, and play with his best friend, a squirrel named Sammy.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw Sammy hiding under his favorite acorn tree. "Sammy, why are you hiding?" Benny asked.

"Because I found something shiny," Sammy whispered. "But it might be a rock!"

Benny's ears perked up. "Let's see what it is!"

They carefully picked up the shiny thing together. It was a tiny golden key! "Oh, Benny, what if it opens something special?" Sammy giggled.

Just then, Benny spotted a small, sparkling box near the house. "Sammy, look! Maybe that's what the key opens!"

The two friends worked together to fit the key into the box. With a soft *click*, the box opened! Inside were lots of colorful flowers, a basket of yummy berries, and even a tiny, sparkly hat for Benny.

"Wow!" Benny said. "This is the best adventure ever!"

From that day on, Benny and Sammy played with their new treasures every day, making their garden the happiest place in the whole world. And Benny learned that even small things, like a golden key, could lead to big, wonderful surprises!
