1.) Metal Braces: Metal braces are the most common type of braces. They come in a variety of colors. 
2.) Ceramic Braces: Ceramic braces work similar to metal braces are colored to blend in with the teeth so that they are less noticeable. 
3.) Invisalign: Invisalign is a clear and removable type of braces. They can be taken out and put back in as needed and are basically invisible.
