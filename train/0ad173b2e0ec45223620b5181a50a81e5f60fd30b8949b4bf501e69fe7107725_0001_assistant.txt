“Do you think you can help me?” Ariel wonders, idly running a fork through her hair and playing with a rusty cheese knife in her small hands. 

“My dear, sweet child,” I say, kindly. “It’s what I do-it’s what I liiiive for!” I swim closer, eyes closed in thought. It was very nice of me to do this for her, all things considered. 

I’m still a little confused about her motivation though. I’ve seen merfolk have many different desires. Love, riches, strength, physical beauty, and power were the most popular but never a specific food, until now. 

“Are you sure this is what you want, my dear? Wouldn’t you be happy with those riiiich umami flavors of kelp? The palace chef could make you anything your little heart desires,” I question.

She shakes her head vigorously. “No,” she says softly. “It’s got to be this.”

I feel a little twinge of guilt about the deal we’re going to make. It almost doesn’t even seem fair, an immortal soul for mere curds. I swim closer. 

“I can make you a potion that will turn you human for three days, got it? Three days.”

Ariel nods happily. 

“But, before the sun sets on the third day, you have to taste a bite of your beloved cheese. And not just any cheese, it has to be Casu Marzu.” 

We sign the agreement. I’ll even have Flotsam and Jetsam drop her off at Sardinia to give her a head start. I begin the incantation. 

“Pecorino, Swissus, Mozzerellus and Brie!” I chant. “Camemburgus, Parmesano, Lindburger to thee!”

The clouds gather around my cauldron, and then it’s done. She’s safely on her way to the island. 

 I return to my lair, still shaking my head. “Merfolk,” I think. “I’ll never get them.”
