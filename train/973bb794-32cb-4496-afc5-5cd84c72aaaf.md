In the quiet hum of the city, a woman found herself standing at the edge of a forgotten park, the air thick with the scent of damp earth and old books. She had come here for the same reason everyone did—escape. But this time, the escape was not of her choosing.

A figure in a black coat emerged from the shadows, their face obscured by a wide-brimmed hat. "You’re not supposed to be here," they said, their voice low and measured. The woman recognized the voice—it was the one that had haunted her dreams for years.

"Then why are you?" she asked, her voice trembling.

The figure smiled. "Because this place isn’t just a park. It’s a door."

She reached into her pocket and pulled out a single, yellowed page from a book she’d found on her desk. The words were familiar—*The ending is a beginning, and beginnings are endings, and endings are beginnings.* She had written them herself, but the ink was fading.

The figure took the page, unfolded it, and passed it back. "Read it again."

She did.
