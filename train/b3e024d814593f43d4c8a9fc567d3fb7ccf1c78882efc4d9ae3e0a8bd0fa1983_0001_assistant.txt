Harry Potter, a fantasy novel series that chronicles the lives of a young wizard named Harry Potter together with his student friends Hermione Granger and Ron Weasley, was written by the British author J.K. Rowling. The series' main story arc concerns the conflict between Harry and a dark wizard called Lord Voldemort. With death as its main theme, the series is of many genres including fantasy, drama, coming-of-age fiction, and British school story. The first novel of the series was released on 26 June 1997 and the series overall has attracted a wide audience, both young and old, selling more than 600 million copies worldwide. It is considered the best-selling series in history, available in 85 languages.
