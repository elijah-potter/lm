What my body knows, 
It's truth, not fiction that grows, 
I swear It's not a lie, 
deep within, it can't deny.

This truth,  vast and pure,
past echoes endure,
Within my marrow reside,
a legacy I won't hide.

No transplant could replace, 
the essence, the trace, 
For if I sought a match, 
it would be an empty catch.

Others' sorrows may loom,
 my traumas, though small, consume,
These bones, they're mine, 
as they creak, moan and whine

One thing rings true,
these bones, I claim, it's not askew,
They are mine, you'll see, 
mine alone, and kind to me.

I won't abandon a single thing, 
that makes my pulse sing,
For this body, this core,
no one but me adore.
