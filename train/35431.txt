How Women Advance as Business Leaders in LDR 300
According to research one way women can advance in leadership is
By leading in a more democratic manner than men.
Explanation: Extensive studies have been conducted about the unique traits of women as leaders in the workplace. By being more democratic than men, women have been able to rise up the corporate ladder at quicker rate. Both men and women respect leaders who can find common ground and avoid the creation of cliques in the workplace. As of 2016, the pay gap between men and women is closing in the corporate world.
