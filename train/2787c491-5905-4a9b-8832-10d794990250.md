In the quiet town of Eldermere, where the rivers whispered secrets to the old stone bridges, Dr. Elias Veyne found himself drawn to the abandoned library of the Hollow. The shelves, once filled with knowledge, were now overgrown with ivy, and the air hummed with the faint echoes of forgotten words. His research on the local legends had led him here—rumors spoke of a hidden tome containing the "Key to the Veils," said to unlock the secrets of time itself.

Inside, he found it: a leather-bound book with golden embossed edges, sealed within a glass case. The moment he touched it, the walls of the library trembled. The book’s cover opened by itself, revealing pages filled with hieroglyphs that shifted and rearranged as he stared. A voice, neither human nor animal, spoke from the ink:

*"The Veils are not gates, but threads—each choice weaves a different fate. The Key does not grant power, but reveals what was always known. The options below are not paths, but mirrors. Choose wisely, for the past and future bleed together."*

---

### **Your Options:**
1. **Burn the Key** – Destroy it in the old forge at the town square, letting the secrets of time dissolve into ash. The town would forget its own history, but the Veils would remain silent, untouched.
2. **Restore the Library** – Rebuild the shelves, reclaim the knowledge, and place the Key in a vault beneath the town hall. The town would remember, but the Veils would whisper to those who seek forbidden truths.
3. **Feed the Key to the River** – Let it flow downstream into the Blackwater, where the mist swallows all things. The river would remember, but the past would fade like a dream.
4. **Speak the Key’s Name** – Declare aloud, *"Elias, tell me the truth."* The Veils would answer, but the cost would be the weight of all forgotten choices.
5. **Take the Key and Flee** – Leave Eldermere with the book, carrying the weight of the Veils with you. The town would never know, but you would never be the same.
6. **Wait for the Veils to Speak** – Do nothing. Let the book’s secrets unfold as time unravels, but the choices of others may dictate your fate.
