by Michael J. Balick and Rosita Arvigo with illustrations from the LuEsther T. Mertz Library of the New York Botanical Garden and by Francesca Anderson
Explore the culturally and ecologically diverse Central American nation with Drs. Michael Balick and Rosita Arvigo. In a definitive resource to Belizian plant life, discover the natural history of the region as well as the traditional uses of the over 3400 species of plant found in the small nation. Containing fascinating interviews with local plant experts and traditional healers, Drs. Balick and Arvigo present a rich cultural study and primary reference guide to the ethnobotany of Belize.
