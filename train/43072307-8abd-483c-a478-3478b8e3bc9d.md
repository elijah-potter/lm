The storm warning sirens wailed as the team scrambled to board the ship. Captain Elias had just announced the new course, his voice steady despite the wind howling through the rigging. But before he could finish, the radio crackled—static, then a single, urgent voice: *"Plan A abandoned. Stand by for emergency protocols."* Elias’ fingers tightened on the wheel. The plan had been perfect, but fate—or fate’s cruel sense of humor—had other plans.
