A humming silver thread of light, coiled like a restless serpent around the edges of the world. It breathes softly, whispering secrets in the wind—each pulse a sigh from a thousand forgotten stars. Its skin shifts between glass and liquid, reflecting not images, but *possibilities*: a sunrise dissolving into a dream, a storm’s whisper turning to gold. It moves not by will, but by the pull of unseen hands, guiding hearts toward the edges of the known. Those who touch it feel the weight of forgotten names, the echoes of laughter that once filled galaxies. It is both observer and observed, a mirror that bends time itself into something fragile, something *alive*.
