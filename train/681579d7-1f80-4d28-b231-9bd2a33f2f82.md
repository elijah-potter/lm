A sudden gust of wind sent the umbrella clattering to the cobblestone street, its handle snapping in half. His grip loosened just as the sound of hooves thundered behind him—hooves that had been silent until now. The rider’s reins twisted, the horse rearing, its eyes wild. He stumbled back, a scream caught in his throat as the saddle horn jabbed into his ribs, the leather stinging like a whip. The rider reined in, leaning forward to whisper, *"You’re late."* The air smelled of rain and iron, the cold biting his cheeks as he realized he’d been caught.
