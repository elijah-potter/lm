## Project Chimera: Rulebook

**A Cooperative Game of Scientific Crisis Management**

Welcome to Project Chimera, a cooperative game for two to four players. You are a team of specialized researchers tasked with containing and understanding a rapidly evolving biological anomaly threatening global stability. Your decisions will determine the fate of humanity.

### 1. Components

*   **Game Board:** A world map depicting major research hubs and affected regions.
*   **Researcher Cards (8):** Each card represents a unique researcher with specialized skills and starting resources.
*   **Anomaly Cards (60):** Detail the evolving characteristics of the Chimera and trigger escalating crises.
*   **Event Cards (30):** Introduce unpredictable global events, often compounding the Chimera’s effects.
*   **Resource Tokens:** Represent Funding, Data, and Personnel.
*   **Containment Markers:** Used to track the spread of the Chimera.
*   **Crisis Markers:** Indicate the severity of regional crises.
*   **Research Track Marker:**  Monitors progress toward understanding the Chimera.
*   **Global Stability Track Marker:** Represents the overall health of the world's infrastructure.
*   **Six-Sided Dice (3):** Used for skill checks and event resolution.

### 2. Setup

1.  Place the game board in the center of the playing area.
2.  Place the Research Track Marker at the starting position.
3.  Place the Global Stability Track Marker at the starting position.
4.  Shuffle the Anomaly and Event card decks separately. Place them face down near the board.
5.  Each player chooses a Researcher card and takes the starting resources indicated on the card.
6.  Place three Containment Markers on the regions indicated on the initial Anomaly card (drawn from the Anomaly deck).
7.  Draw the first Anomaly card and place it face up. This card establishes the initial Chimera characteristics.

### 3. Turn Structure

Each player’s turn consists of the following phases, performed in order:

1.  **Anomaly Phase:** Draw one Anomaly card and resolve its effects. These cards introduce new Chimera traits, escalate existing crises, and may trigger immediate consequences.
2.  **Action Phase:** You have three Action Points to spend. Actions include:
    *   **Move (1 Action Point):** Move your researcher to an adjacent region.
    *   **Gather Resources (1 Action Point):** Collect one Resource token (Funding, Data, or Personnel) from your current region.
    *   **Containment Effort (2 Action Points):** Remove one Containment Marker from your current region. Requires a successful Skill Check (see Skill Checks).
    *   **Research (2 Action Points):** Advance the Research Track Marker. Requires a successful Skill Check.
    *   **Support (1 Action Point):** Provide one Resource token to another researcher in the same region.
    *   **Mitigate Crisis (2 Action Points):** Reduce a Crisis Marker in your current region by one. Requires a successful Skill Check.
3.  **Event Phase:** Draw one Event card and resolve its effects. These cards introduce unpredictable global events, often compounding the Chimera’s effects.
4.  **Crisis Escalation Phase:** For each region with a Crisis Marker, roll a die. On a result of 4 or higher, increase the Crisis Marker by one.

### 4. Skill Checks

Many actions require Skill Checks. To perform a Skill Check, roll a six-sided die. Add your Researcher’s relevant Skill bonus (indicated on their card). If the total meets or exceeds the target number indicated by the action, the check is successful.  A failed check may result in negative consequences as indicated by the action.

### 5. Trade-Off Mechanics

Project Chimera incorporates several mechanics designed to force difficult choices and strategic trade-offs:

1.  **Resource Prioritization:**  Each region provides only one type of resource. Players must coordinate to ensure they acquire the diverse resources needed to perform actions effectively. A shortage of any resource can severely hamper progress.
2.  **Containment vs. Research:**  Focusing solely on containment delays understanding the Chimera, potentially leading to a more devastating outbreak. Conversely, neglecting containment allows the Chimera to spread uncontrollably. Players must decide how to balance these competing priorities.
3.  **Global Stability Sacrifice:** Certain Event cards offer a choice: mitigate a regional crisis, or invest resources into stabilizing the Global Stability Track. Choosing to stabilize the Global Stability Track may delay immediate relief but prevents a catastrophic global collapse.

### 6. Win and Loss Conditions

**Win Condition:** Advance the Research Track Marker to its final position. This signifies a complete understanding of the Chimera and a viable solution.

**Loss Conditions:**

*   The Global Stability Track Marker reaches its final position.
*   Any region accumulates three Crisis Markers.
*   The Anomaly deck is exhausted.



### 7. Difficulty Scaling

*   **Two Players:** Reduce the target number for Skill Checks by 1.  Start with two Containment Markers on the initial Anomaly card.
*   **Three Players:** Play with standard rules.
*   **Four Players:** Increase the target number for Skill Checks by 1. Start with three Containment Markers on the initial Anomaly card.



### 8. Advanced Variants

*   **Rapid Mutation:** After each Anomaly card is resolved, shuffle the discarded Anomaly cards back into the deck. This accelerates the Chimera's evolution and increases the difficulty.
*   **Limited Communication:** Impose a rule restricting verbal communication between players during the Action Phase. This simulates the challenges of coordinating a global research effort under pressure.
*   **Specialized Crisis:** Introduce a set of "Special Crisis" cards. These cards are shuffled into the Event deck and represent particularly devastating regional events requiring immediate and significant resource investment.



### Designer’s Note

Project Chimera is designed to engage players in a collaborative exploration of scientific crisis management. The game aims to highlight the complexities of decision-making under uncertainty, the importance of interdisciplinary collaboration, and the ethical considerations inherent in responding to global threats. We hope players will find the experience both challenging and rewarding, fostering a deeper appreciation for the dedication of scientists and researchers working to safeguard our world. The game is intended to be a thought-provoking experience, not a perfect simulation.
