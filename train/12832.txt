What does the future look like? Join us as we embark on an odyssey to explore the future of transportation, manufacturing and structural. Let’s take a look at the innovations of today and apply them to create the designs of tomorrow as we explore ways to harness clean energy and house a growing human population. Become an engineer and help Design the Future
Including environmental, mechanical, civil, and marine engineer
Students will design and build a well-insulated home, explore efficient home-heating methods, and compare and contrast electrical and solar heat.
Students will create a drivetrain using everyday items, design an electric vehicle, and incorporate simple machines into a mechanical design.
Students will understand how astronauts can generate oxygen on Mars, design and build a Greenhouse, and observe the greenhouse effect.
Students will design and build a house for a specific purpose, understand a column’s structure and ability to bear loads, and test a design to failure.
Students will build a circuit that contains a solar panel, modify a boat to improve speed, and design and build a solar powered boat.
