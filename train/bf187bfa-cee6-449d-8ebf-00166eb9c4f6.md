Lila the bunny hopped through the sunny meadow, her tiny paws making soft *thump-thump* sounds. She found a shiny red apple near a big, wiggly dandelion. "Mmm, yummy!" she giggled.

Suddenly, a little cloud floated by and said, "Hop with me!" Lila gasped. "You can’t fly!" The cloud giggled too. "But I can make clouds dance!" It blew soft puffs of air, and Lila giggled as she floated up. Up, up, up she went—until she found a rainbow bridge that sparkled in the sky.

She hopped across the rainbow, landing on a cloud that looked like a big, fluffy teddy bear. "Woof!" said the cloud. Lila smiled. "I’m Lila the bunny!" The cloud hugged her with its fluffy arms.

Down, down, down they floated until Lila landed back in her meadow. She found her dandelion still wiggling and her apple still there. "Mmm, yummy again!" she said, and she ran home to tell her mama all about the sky adventure.
