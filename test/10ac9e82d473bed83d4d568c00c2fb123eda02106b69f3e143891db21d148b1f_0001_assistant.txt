The ride up the winding path to the castle was a long one. Maids and butlers and footmen, dressed in finely fitted dresses and suits in the dark silver and black colors of the King's house. A footman rapped on the door and waited for his mistress's command before opening it, stepping aside, and lifting a hand to assist her down. She took his hand and gazed out at the castle ahead.

The sun shone brightly down on them as the day wound down towards evening, with rays still beaming down in a mix of gold and orange. The rows of intricate stained glass windows reflected the sunlight, making the castle a veritable beacon. Tall arches and turrets rose toward the sky, seemingly touching the clouds, and a clock tower loomed overhead, watching over all with its ominous, dark face. The castle's facade was all dark stone, the roofing an even darker tile. Perched on top of a hill, the highest peak in the area, the Black Palace was an imposing sight, like a hunched jaguar pressed down on its haunches. Fitting for a vampire king. 

"Lady Sinclair," an aged man dressed in a lightly brocaded butler's uniform bowed low, showing the shiny bald spot atop his head, "I apologize for our lack of preparation. I had not received any prior knowledge of your arrival."
