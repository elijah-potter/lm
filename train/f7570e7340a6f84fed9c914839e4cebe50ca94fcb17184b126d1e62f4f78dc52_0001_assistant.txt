It was almost like a joke, Peter thought as he woke up slowly. Four math professors walk into a bar, get hammered, and wake up in a hotel room they don't recognize. Perfect, just like the golden ratio.

"What the heck..," Peter heard from across the room. Jeremy Knotts was looking at a hastily-strewn equation drawn on the wall with... well, he hoped it wasn't gross, whatever it was. As the sun dawned on Gabriella and Loryn's still-sleeping forms, something else dawned on Jeremy. He recognized this equation - The Harvestman's Equation. It had previously been unsolved. However, here in front of him, was a solution. A solution to world hunger!

"Jeremy, is that what I think it is?"

"Peter... I think it just might be."

Now, to find which one of them had solved it.
