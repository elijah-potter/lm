The alarm blared at 3:17 AM, its hollow siren cutting through the thick silence of the abandoned lab. Dr. Elias Veyra stood frozen in the doorway, his breath shallow, his mind racing. The warning had come from the system’s last log: *"Critical: Emergency Protocol Initiated. Do not enter room 42. Hazardous containment breach detected."*

He had ignored it before. Ignored it again. Now, the air hummed with static, and the walls pulsed with a faint, green glow. The door to room 42 creaked open on its own, revealing a swirling vortex of energy. Inside, figures moved too fast to see clearly—some wore the same eerie, faceless masks he’d seen in the archives. They weren’t people. They were *echoes*, fragments of something that had been erased from history.

His fingers trembled as he reached for his terminal. The screen flickered to life, displaying a single, chilling line: *"They were never human."*
