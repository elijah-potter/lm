A suffocating mist coils over a crumbling stone archway where the city’s skeletal spires lean in unison, their hollow eyes fixed on the horizon. The air hums with the ghostly echoes of a thousand voices—some whispering, others screaming—all drowned beneath the weight of silence. Bridges, half-sunk in the mud, stretch between drowned docks, their iron chains rusted but still taut, as if holding the world back from a tidal wave of oblivion. The sky is a bruised bruise of dusk, cracked with veils of storm clouds that never quite lift. In the distance, the skeletal fingers of a drowned cathedral claw at the sky, their stained glass windows reflecting a dying light. Even the wind seems to pause, as if waiting for permission to move.
