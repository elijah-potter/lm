Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a bunny named Benny who loved to hop and play. Every morning, Benny would wiggle his nose and say, "Time to play outside!"

One sunny day, Benny met a little bird named Birdie who chirped, "Hello, Benny! Can I play too?" Benny hopped over and said, "Of course, Birdie! Let’s explore the garden!"

Together, they found a big, soft ball that bounced and rolled. Benny rolled it to Birdie, who caught it and tossed it back. "This is fun!" Birdie said.

Then, they saw a shiny red apple on a tree. "Let’s eat!" Benny said, and they both took a bite. "Yum, sweet!" Benny giggled.

As the sun began to set, Benny and Birdie said goodbye. "We had a wonderful time!" Benny said. Birdie waved her wings. "Goodnight, Benny!"

Benny snuggled under his blanket, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures. "Tomorrow is another day to play!" he thought happily, and he fell asleep.

The end.
