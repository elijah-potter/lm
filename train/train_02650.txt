In a store, an Uno Giant Family Card costs $12. When Ivan bought ten pieces, he was given a discount of $2 each. How much did Ivan pay in all?
Instead of $12 each, an Uno Giant Family Card costs $12 - $2 = $<<12-2=10>>10 each.
Ivan paid $10 x 10 = $<<10*10=100>>100 for the ten pieces Uno Giant Family Card.
