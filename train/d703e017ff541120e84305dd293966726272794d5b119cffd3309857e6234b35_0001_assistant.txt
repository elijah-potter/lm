On this day we celebrate,
Cinco de Mayo, a day so great,
A time to remember and to honor,
The brave heroes with so much valor.

In Mexico, the battle raged,
The French army, they engaged,
Outnumbered and outgunned they fought,
And a glorious victory they wrought.

Now we raise our glasses high,
To the triumphs of days gone by,
To the strength and spirit of the people,
And to the hope that still shines like a steeple.

Should this day only be to party
Drink and eat robust and hearty?
That's a choice for some, not I
For this day also I would rather cry

"To freedom for old Mexico!"
To hold heads high and flex it so
The day we earned our independence
The day they won for our descendants!

So let the music loudly play,
And let the colors proudly sway,
For today we celebrate,
Cinco de Mayo, a day so great.
