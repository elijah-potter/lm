A slow knock at the door echoes through the quiet apartment. The curtains twitch slightly, revealing a sliver of moonlight filtering through the blinds. The air is thick with the scent of stale coffee and old books. The resident, a man in his late thirties with sharp features and a perpetually unshaven jaw, sits at the cluttered desk, fingers drumming restlessly against the worn wooden surface. His phone buzzes—a notification for an unread email.

The knock comes again, firmer this time. The door creaks open just enough for a shadowy figure to step inside, holding something wrapped in brown paper. The light from the hallway casts long, dancing shadows across the walls.

“You’re the one they’re looking for,” the stranger says, voice low and measured. “I’ve been waiting for you in case of an emergency.”
