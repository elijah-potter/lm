L014 “Sunshine Pleco”
Common Name - Sunshine / Goldie Pleco
Scientific Name - Scobinancistrus aureatus
Max Size - 12"
Care - Moderate
Temperature Range - 78-84F
pH - 6.5-7.5
Diet - Biofilm, frozen and prepared foods
Breeding - Difficult
Origin - Wild Brazil
Notes - The Sunshine Pleco is one of the most vibrant species of plecos. This species hails from the Rio Xingu in Brazil. Its natural habitat has warm and fast flowing water. This species needs to be kept in a setting that is well oxygenated with clean and clear water.
