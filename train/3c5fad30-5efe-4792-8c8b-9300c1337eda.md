Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white fur and big, round ears that always twitched when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his favorite friend, a tiny blue butterfly named Butter. "Good morning, Butter!" Benny said, hopping down from his nest.

Butter fluttered her wings and said, "Good morning, Benny! Did you hear about the big, bright flowers in the meadow?"

Benny's eyes sparkled. "Yes! Let's go visit them! We can collect some flowers for Mommy Bunny!"

They hopped outside and found the biggest, prettiest flowers Benny had ever seen. They picked red, yellow, and pink flowers and placed them in a tiny basket Benny carried. Along the way, they met some friends—a kind old turtle named Tilly who helped them cross the stream, and a family of squirrels who shared some acorns.

When they reached the meadow, Benny and Butter placed their flowers on a sunny patch. Benny's mommy came out and said, "Look at all the pretty flowers you made! They will make the meadow even happier!"

Butter said, "Benny, let's share our flowers with everyone." So they planted them all around the meadow, and soon, every little animal could see the beautiful flowers blooming.

From that day on, Benny and Butter loved visiting the meadow every day. They would pick more flowers, share with their friends, and make the meadow a magical place where everyone felt happy and loved. And that was the happiest story of all!
