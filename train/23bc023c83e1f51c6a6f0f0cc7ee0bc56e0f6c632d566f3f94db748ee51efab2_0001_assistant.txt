Getting set up for the wedding has taken up all of Linda’s free time. The morning of the wedding, Linda meets with the wedding planner one last time. The caterers, florist, and wedding cake arrive as scheduled. Linda goes off with her bridesmaids and family to dress for her special day. As she puts on her wedding dress, she is overwhelmed with emotion. 
Choosing the dress is always supposed to be difficult for the bride, but Linda saw the dress the minute she walked into the bridal shop. Hanging on a manikin, she saw the drape and fit, which was exactly what she wanted. The dress, with its lace neck and arms and satin bodice billowing into a full train, is exquisite. It’s the visual fantasy she had always dreamed of for her perfect wedding. Trying on no other, she selects and pays for the gown within an hour of arrival. Jason’s mother is astonished at her decisiveness. Providing delivery directions, they leave the shop relieved that the biggest task she needs to do is now complete.
Examining herself in the mirror, she is glowing. The wows and ahs being delivered from her bridesmaids help to assure her that it was a well-made decision. The calm she has about marrying Jason confirms her decision to take this wonderful man as her husband.
He spends the last night at his place. He has rented his house out, with tenants moving in next month. The furniture is still there, so he can stay the night with no problem. His belongings are already at Linda’s. He just needs to pack an overnight bag. He’d had his best man (his father) and friends over last night for a manly celebration while Linda’s friends surprised her with a bridal shower. With such short notice for their friends, everything has been kept small and intimate.
As time ticks away, their vows loom near. The groomsmen pat Jason on the back and tell him they will see him at the wedding. Jason laughs. With much anticipation, his nerves are slightly edgy.
