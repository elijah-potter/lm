Sure! Here are some ways you can find people to put together a rap group!

1. Ask local rappers - Most larger towns have rappers in the area. You can find some possible members by getting integrated into your local scene and asking around to see if anyone wants to start a group with you. People can work solo and in a group at the same time, so you might find some people who want to work with you.
2. Talk to your friends - You probably know some other people who are into rap or know how to rap. If you have friends who enjoy it, you may find some who would like to start on this journey with you. As it is your friends, you'll already have a sense of camaraderie.
3. Post on a forum - You could also try to go online and post on a forum. By doing so, you may find some people who are interested. They may not be close, but it is possible to make music from afar remotely.
4. Post on your socials - You can also turn to social media. As your socials have people you have connected with, there may be people who are interested in working with you. All you have to do is announce that you're interested in starting a group with others, and you may find some who would like to start a group with you.
5. Network at shows - Where will people who like rap a lot be? At local shows. You may find some group members by going to shows and networking with other people there. You may find some like-minded people who would like to start a group together.
6. Post it on local bulletin boards - There are tons of shops and places with bulletin boards. You can go around to figure out who has them and ask if you can put a page up announcing that you're starting a rap group and need members. Also, make sure to include some way to contact you. Then, whoever sees it has a way to reach out if they're interested.
