Dear Editor,

Here are the ideas you wanted. I think they have potential. Let me know if you have any questions or need something different. 


Ideas for Short Stories:

•	Tell the story of a long, jagged scar on the lower back of a teen boy. It should be poignant and emotional, a journey through his mind.
•	A small group of gorillas discover a human body when they are foraging in the jungle. They want to return it to other humans.
•	A person decides to adopt a pair of rats not knowing that their roommate is terrified of rats. Something extreme happens because of this.
•	A pride of lions stumbles through a portal into another dimension in which their kind is able to communicate with humans and lions no longer live a free, wild, lifestyle.

1.	Jacob was sensitive about his scar; so much so that he refused to take off his shirt in front of anyone. He made sure he was never on the “skins” side of a casual game, and he never even looked at the jagged, ugly thing himself. It made him remember too much.
2.	Bark tossed Mila a cluster of berries as they ambled through the vines. She caught them deftly and popped some into her mouth, before stopping suddenly and pointing in horror at the ground. A human lay very still at her feet.
3.	“Hey, Jen, I got the cutest new pets,” called Ronda. A terrified gasp made her whirl around and her eyes widened. Jen was frozen to the spot, staring at the two rats in their habitat.
4.	Life was pretty simple for Sonny and his pride. Until it wasn’t. The day they blundered through a bright circle and found themselves in another world changed everything forever.
