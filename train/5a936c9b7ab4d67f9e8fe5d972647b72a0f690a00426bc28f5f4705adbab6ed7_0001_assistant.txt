Popularly known as "The Rock", Dwayne Douglas Johnson is an American actor, film producer, and former professional wrestler. He rose to prominence during the Attitude Era of the World Wrestling Federation (WWF, now WWE) in the late 1990s and early 2000s.

After an athletic scholarship at the University of Miami, Johnson briefly played football before entering the wrestling world with the help of his father, Rocky Johnson. He became one of the most successful and beloved professional wrestlers of all time, winning numerous championships and headlining major events.

"The Rock" transitioned to acting, making his debut in "The Mummy Returns" (2001) and securing his first leading role in "The Scorpion King" (2002). He has since starred in a broad range of successful films, including movies like "Jumanji: Welcome to the Jungle" (2017), "Fast Five" (2011), and "Moana" (2016).

Beyond just acting, Johnson has also ventured into producing and has been involved in television series such as "Ballers" and "Young Rock." He is widely recognized for his impact on both the entertainment industry and as a highly influential individual.

Dwayne Johnson's journey from professional wrestling to a successful acting career has made him a name, known for his charisma, impressive physique, and versatility on the big screen.
