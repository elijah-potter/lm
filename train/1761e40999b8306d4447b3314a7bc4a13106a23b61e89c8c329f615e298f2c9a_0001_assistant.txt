As Tony struggled on his last rep at the bench press, he began to envision Maria again. Maria had just broken up with Tony about two weeks ago, but the pain was still very fresh in Tony’s mind. Leading up to the split, he sensed that things were a little off between them. The usually very attentive and engaging Maria had been seeming very distant. She stopped texting him good morning, and she seemed to touch and hold him a little less. He assumed she had just been going through a rough patch and actively tried to make sure that she was okay. He would ask her constantly if she needed anything or if there was anything he could do to help, but she would always tell him that things were okay and that there was nothing to worry about. This turned out to be a lie when she not only broke up with him but admitted that she was also actively seeing someone else. It had been going on for a while, and Maria tried to express to Tony that her goal wasn’t to hurt him but that she just simply didn’t know how to break things off. Remembering this put Tony in a state of anger that seemed to help him propel the barbell up with renewed vigor.
