Kids are very likely to come into contact with parasites like lice and worms. Why? Because they tend to forget to wash their hands, and they are more likely to play in the dirt than adults. Some parasitic infections spread quickly in school and child care environments because kids tend to share personal items and work and play in close proximity. As yucky as they seem, parasitic infections are usually harmless and relatively easily treated. But, left untreated, they can lead to more serious infections, weight loss, and more.
From head lice to pinworms, the best prevention is to teach kids to avoid getting parasitic infections in the first place. Learn more about how parasites infect kids and what to look for if you suspect a parasitic infection.
