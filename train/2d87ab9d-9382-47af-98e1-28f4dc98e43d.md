**The Hollow Mound**
A weathered stone amphitheater near an abandoned village, its crumbling walls holding no purpose except for the murmur of whispers. The air hums with static, the ground beneath damp and uneven. Every few years, a small crowd gathers—some by chance, others drawn by rumor—though no one knows why. The center of the mound is a shallow pit, lined with moss and cracked glass. No one is ever sure when it was last visited, yet the crowd always returns, as if drawn by an unseen current.

At dusk, the crowd forms a loose circle, their faces half-lit by flickering lanterns. They sit, stand, or lean against the walls, lost in conversations that never quite reach a conclusion. Some bring offerings: rusted keys, half-buried coins, or photographs of faces that don’t match the people here. No one asks questions. No one leaves early.

The most persistent among them are the ones who remember the last gathering—the one that ended with a single lantern falling into the pit, never to be found. The others simply say, *"We’re here because we’re here."*
