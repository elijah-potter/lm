**Mayor’s Office: Official Announcement on New Eco-Corridor Project**
The City of **Verdant Vale** announces the official groundbreaking of the **Central Park Greenway Initiative**, a 12-kilometer urban trail system designed to connect the city’s northern and southern districts through reclaimed wetlands and repurposed rail corridors. Construction begins immediately, with phase one slated for completion by autumn 2025. The project, funded by a $15 million municipal grant and private partnerships, will integrate solar-powered lighting, native plant species, and solar-powered charging stations for electric vehicles. Community input will guide final design, with public workshops scheduled for June 15. Mayor Elias Chen emphasizes the initiative’s dual purpose: “It’s a legacy for future generations, but also a lifeline for our local economy and environmental health.”

**Next Steps:** Volunteers needed for trail grading and native plant propagation; sign up via [VerdantVale.gov/eco-corridor](https://www.verdantvale.gov/eco-corridor). Full project timeline and budget details available upon request.

**Local Businesses Encouraged to Apply for Grants**
The **Verdant Vale Small Business Development Fund** is accepting applications for grants up to $5,000 for businesses within 500 meters of the new trail. Eligible categories include food vendors, eco-tourism services, and craft studios. Deadline: July 1, 2024. Applications at [VerdantVale.gov/grants](https://www.verdantvale.gov/grants).
