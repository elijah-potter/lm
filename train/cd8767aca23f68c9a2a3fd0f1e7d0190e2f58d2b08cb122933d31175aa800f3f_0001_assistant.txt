Beth was shocked to see Kate had,
Imprinted on her skin,
Ink that would upset their dad,
If seen on her own twin.

An odder thing, and Beth was frank,
She'd seen that day so far:
The building in place of her bank
That Kate had made a bar.

Last thing of note, for all its worth,
Beth said, as if she missed her,
"It feels like I'm back on my Earth ...
Just chatting with my sister."
