When you create an Excel worksheet, you may want to update it over time with new or modified data. If you want to know the sum of a column or row even with changes, you can use the AutoSum feature. Entering the SUM function in the cell of your choice will create a running total, and the cell will automatically display the updated total as soon as you enter new information.
1. Start Excel and open the worksheet in which you want a running total. Click inside the cell where you want the total to appear.
2. Click the "AutoSum" drop-down arrow in the Editing group of the Home tab, and then select "Sum."
3. Select the row or column you want to total and press "Enter." If you want to use the group of cells Excel automatically highlights, simply press "Enter."
4. Save the worksheet before closing. Excel updates the total any time you enter amounts into any of the cells in the range you selected.
- Information in this article applies to Excel 2007, 2010 and 2013. It may vary slightly or significantly with other versions or products.
