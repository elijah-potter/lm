A bustling village square with a central fountain. Families gather in the mornings, trading gossip and fresh bread. Children dart between the elders, mimicking their handshakes and storytelling. A tavern serves as the heartbeat, its wooden tables crowded with strangers who share the same name—all "Smith" or "Johnson"—but know each by face and voice. The market stalls, where the same vendors sell the same goods, are staffed by the same people daily, reinforcing familiarity. The church bell rings every Sunday, calling the entire community to worship and laughter. Old stories are passed down in the tavern’s dim light, where no one asks names for the first time. A shared history, time, and proximity have woven a tapestry of mutual recognition.
