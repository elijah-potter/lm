## Project Chimera: A Cooperative Rulebook

**A game for 2-4 players. Ages 14+. Playing time: 60-90 minutes.**

**Introduction:**

Project Chimera places you in the roles of leading researchers at the Global Bio-Containment Initiative (GBCI). A novel, rapidly evolving pathogen, designated Chimera, has emerged, exhibiting unprecedented adaptability and virulence. Its origins remain shrouded in mystery, and its spread threatens to overwhelm global infrastructure and decimate populations. Your mission is to understand Chimera, develop countermeasures, and ultimately contain the crisis before it spirals into irreversible catastrophe. Success demands meticulous planning, decisive action, and unwavering collaboration.

**1. Components**

*   **Game Board:** A world map depicting key regions and infrastructure points.
*   **Chimera Strain Cards (60):** Representing the pathogen's evolving characteristics and increasing threat level.
*   **Research Cards (80):** Offering potential breakthroughs in understanding and combating Chimera. Divided into four disciplines: Virology, Epidemiology, Immunology, and Logistics.
*   **Event Cards (40):** Introducing unexpected challenges, setbacks, and opportunities.
*   **Resource Tokens:** Representing Funding, Personnel, and Equipment.
*   **Containment Markers:** Used to track containment efforts in affected regions.
*   **Crisis Track:** A linear track indicating the escalating global crisis level.
*   **Player Pawns (4):** Representing each researcher.
*   **Discipline Dice (4):** One die for each research discipline (Virology, Epidemiology, Immunology, Logistics).
*   **First Player Token:** Designates the starting player.
*   **Rulebook (this document).**

**2. Setup**

1.  Place the Game Board in the center of the playing area.
2.  Shuffle the Chimera Strain Cards and place them face down in a designated draw pile. Draw the top three cards and place them face up next to the pile; these represent the currently active Chimera strains.
3.  Shuffle the Research Cards and create a face-down draw pile. Reveal the top five cards and place them face up in a display area.
4.  Shuffle the Event Cards and place them face down in a designated draw pile.
5.  Place the Crisis Track marker on the starting space (level 0).
6.  Each player chooses a Player Pawn and places it on the GBCI Headquarters location on the Game Board.
7.  Distribute starting Resources: Each player receives 3 Funding, 2 Personnel, and 1 Equipment.
8.  Determine the First Player randomly and give them the First Player Token.

**3. Turn Structure**

Each player’s turn consists of the following phases, performed in order:

1.  **Event Phase:** Draw the top Event Card and resolve its effects immediately. Event cards often introduce unexpected challenges or opportunities that impact the entire team.
2.  **Action Phase:** You have three Action Points to spend on the following actions. You may perform the same action multiple times, provided you have sufficient Action Points.
    *   **Move (1 Action Point):** Move your Player Pawn to an adjacent location on the Game Board.
    *   **Research (2 Action Points):** Choose a face-up Research Card. Roll the corresponding Discipline Die. If the result meets or exceeds the card’s difficulty rating, you successfully research the card and add it to your hand. Otherwise, the attempt fails, and the card remains in the display.
    *   **Deploy Resources (1 Action Point):** Spend Resources (Funding, Personnel, Equipment) to place Containment Markers in a region. The number of markers you can place depends on the region's characteristics and the resources spent.
    *   **Share Resources (1 Action Point):** Transfer one type of Resource (Funding, Personnel, or Equipment) to another player at the same location.
    *   **Discard Research (1 Action Point):** Discard a Research Card from your hand to gain 1 Funding.

3.  **Chimera Advance Phase:** Draw the top Chimera Strain Card and place it face up next to the draw pile.  Advance the Crisis Track one space. If a region has no Containment Markers, it becomes “Infected.” If a region is already Infected, its infection level increases.

4.  **Pass the Token:** Pass the First Player Token to the player on your left.

**4. Trade-Off Mechanics & Uncertainty**

Project Chimera incorporates several mechanics designed to force difficult choices and operate under conditions of uncertainty:

*   **Discipline Die Rolls:** Research success is determined by die rolls. This introduces an element of chance, meaning even the most promising research can fail.
*   **Resource Allocation:** Limited Resources force players to prioritize which regions to contain and which research to pursue. Deploying Resources to one area inherently reduces the availability for others.
*   **Strain Adaptation:** Chimera Strain Cards introduce new characteristics and challenges. These cards often force players to re-evaluate their strategies and discard previously valuable Research Cards.  A card might require a specific combination of research disciplines to counter, forcing a team to temporarily abandon a promising avenue of investigation.
*   **Event Card Consequences:** Events frequently present choices with undesirable outcomes. Players must weigh the potential benefits against the risks, often sacrificing short-term gains for long-term stability.

**5. Win/Loss Conditions**

*   **Win Condition:** Players win the game if they successfully research and implement a “Cure” – a specific combination of Research Cards that is revealed through gameplay. The Cure requires a coordinated effort across all four research disciplines.
*   **Loss Condition:** Players lose the game if the Crisis Track reaches the final space (level 10), or if any three regions become "Out of Control" (a state reached through repeated infection increases).

**6. Difficulty Scaling**

*   **2 Players:** Begin with the Crisis Track marker on level 0. Reduce the number of starting Resources by 1 of each type per player.
*   **3 Players:** Play with the standard setup.
*   **4 Players:** Begin with the Crisis Track marker on level 1. Increase the number of starting Resources by 1 of each type per player.

**7. Advanced Variants**

*   **Specialist Roles:** Assign each player a unique role with a special ability (e.g., "Virologist" gains a bonus to Virology research rolls, "Logistics Officer" can move additional Resources).
*   **Hidden Objectives:** Introduce Secret Objective cards that provide players with individual goals that may conflict with the team’s primary objective.
*   **Mutation Deck:** Introduce a separate deck of Mutation Cards that are shuffled into the Chimera Strain deck. These cards introduce particularly devastating effects that require immediate and coordinated responses.



**Designer's Note**

Project Chimera was designed to be more than just a game; it's a learning experience. The mechanics are intended to simulate the complexities of a global crisis response, highlighting the importance of collaboration, adaptability, and strategic resource allocation. The escalating crisis and unpredictable events reflect the inherent uncertainty of scientific research and the challenges of responding to novel threats. By confronting these challenges as a team, players will gain a deeper appreciation for the scientific process and the critical role of interdisciplinary collaboration in addressing global issues. The game aims to foster critical thinking, problem-solving skills, and a greater awareness of the interconnectedness of our world.
