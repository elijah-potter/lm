Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the meadow. One sunny morning, Benny spotted a shiny red berry and wanted to eat it. But Benny’s mommy said, "No, sweetie, berries are for birds to eat." Benny tried to hide it under his paws but slipped and dropped it.

Suddenly, Benny saw a little mouse named Mimi running away from her house. Benny’s heart felt sad. He picked up the berry and ran to Mimi’s house. With a tiny hop, he gave the berry to Mimi, who giggled and said, "Thank you! I’ll share with my friends too."

Benny’s mommy smiled and said, "Benny, sharing is kind. You just made a new friend!" From that day on, Benny and Mimi hopped together, playing games and sharing berries with all their friends. The meadow was happier than ever!
