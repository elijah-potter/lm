A trained model will output a prediction from a new row of data, and we can test the validity of this prediction by running the predictor with data where we already know the outcome. The more often the predictor is wrong, or the farther off the target it is, the worse it is as a model. Mathematically, this assigns a probability to the chance the model is wrong when it is used to predict many already known values. We can delve into the prediction and assign an error to each individual piece of the row, as some may highly correlate with different outcomes and others may not.
