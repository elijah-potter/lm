Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big, blue sky above, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny who loved to hop and explore. One sunny morning, Benny peeked out his window and saw a shiny red apple near the tree. Benny’s ears perked up! "I wonder if I can eat it," he thought.

Benny hopped over to the tree and carefully picked the apple. As he nibbled on it, he heard a tiny *crunch* from behind the bushes. Out popped a little mouse named Milo, his eyes wide with surprise. "You stole my apple!" Milo squeaked.

Benny blinked. "I didn’t steal it! It was just there for you too!" He held out his paw. Milo’s eyes got big. "You’re not a thief, Benny! You’re a bunny who shares!"

Just then, a kind old owl named Oliver swooped down from the branches. "Looks like you two need help!" he hooted. "The apple was meant to be shared!"

Benny and Milo giggled. "We’ll make a feast together!" Milo said. They picked more berries, and Benny’s mommy brought a big, warm bowl of honeycomb. Soon, their little table was full of snacks, and Benny and Milo sat together, sharing every bite.

From that day on, Benny and Milo were best friends. They hopped and ran, played tag, and always looked for apples to share. And every evening, as they curled up together under the stars, Benny whispered, "The best things in life are when we share!"

The end.
