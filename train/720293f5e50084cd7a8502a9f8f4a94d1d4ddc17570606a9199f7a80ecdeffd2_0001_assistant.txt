The mystery of the pink water has been solved. Police and Environmental investigators have determined the cause of the pink water in the Venice Canal.

An investigation was launched on Sunday when the canal turned a bright shade of pink.

After chemical and biological testing, it was determined to be a temporary and non-toxic food coloring additive designed to turn the canal "Barbie pink" to promote the release of the new Barbie movie starring Margot Robbie and Ryan Gosling. The mystery of the shocking pink water certainly caught our attention but whether or not that will translate to box office success is yet to be seen.
