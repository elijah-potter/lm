APRIL BOOK CHALLENGE
Who gets to kiss the pig? The April book challenge begins Monday. In April the student body must read 500 books. If they complete this goal 2 teachers will have to kiss a pig. Grades K-3 pick one person and grades 4-8 pick a second person. For every book read in April the student will receive one vote to help decide who will be the lucky one to KISS A PIG. Everyone needs to read at least one book in April.
