Jerusalem Cherry Madeira Winter Solanum Pseudocapsicum Flower Seeds
The Jerusalem Cherry is a member of the nightshade family. It is a small shrub with glossy evergreen leaves. They are a tender perennial.
In cooler climates, they can be grown as an annual, overwintered indoors, or grown entirely as a house plant.
Despite the fact that the berries are poisonous, the Jerusalem Cherry is a popular house plant because of its ornamental value.
There is an easy fix for this ‘poisonous’ business through - Simply remove the spent flowers, & no berries will form.
