-Grass always cared about Tree. He looked up to her.  Tree was so tall and big, and Grass was so small. Grass wanted to learn more about Tree but was too scared to talk to her. 

-Grass and Tree went through seven winters, summers, and springs together and never spoke. 

-Then, one day, Tree told Grass, “I think it’s time we met. I’m Tree.”

-Grass was so excited. He introduced himself, and they went on to talk non-stop for hours. Grass realized that Tree cared just as much about him as she did her. After their conversation, Grass and Tree talked every day for the next 100 years.
