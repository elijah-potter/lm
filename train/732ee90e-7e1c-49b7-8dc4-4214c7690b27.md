A hidden garden in an abandoned cathedral, where the air hums with the echoes of forgotten confessions. Moss-covered benches line a curved stone path, their worn surfaces smooth with time. Lanterns flicker from arched windows, casting long shadows that dance with whispered secrets. The wind carries the scent of damp earth and old wood, and the only sound is the occasional, indistinct murmur drifting through the cracks in the walls. Somewhere, a bell tolls—just once, then fades into silence.
