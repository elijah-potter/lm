A dim lamp flickered on the kitchen table as the front door creaked open. Footsteps padded across the wooden floor—light, deliberate. The doorknob turned with a slow, deliberate twist, revealing a figure in a long coat with a hood pulled low.

"Come in," the voice said, uncharacteristically calm, from the hallway.

The door swung open, revealing a narrow corridor lined with antique bookshelves. A single lamp glowed weakly, casting long shadows. The intruder stepped inside, pausing as the front door shut with a final, decisive click. The hallway stretched ahead, the air thick with the scent of old paper and damp stone.

The door at the end of the corridor stood slightly ajar, revealing a room in shadow. A single figure sat at a cluttered desk, fingers tracing the rim of a half-empty coffee cup. The intruder hesitated, then stepped forward, the coat brushing against the low ceiling. The lamp above the desk flared to life, illuminating the stranger’s face as they knelt beside the desk, their breath visible in the cold air.

A chair scraped back. The desk lamp dimmed, plunging the room into near-darkness. The only sound was the murmur of the city beyond the window.
