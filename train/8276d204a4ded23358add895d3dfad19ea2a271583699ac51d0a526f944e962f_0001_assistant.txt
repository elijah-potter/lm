No problem, here are five outdoor places you can go for free in Cookeville, TN.
*Dogwood Park- Dogwood Park is located in the center of Cookeville, TN. The park features a beautiful water feature, a playground for kids, and a rose garden to walk through.
*Cane Creek Park- Cane Creek Park features multiple walking trails around the lake. There are several playground locations for children to enjoy. There are duck and goose feeding areas located next to one of the many parking lots.
*Parkview Park- Parkview Park features a skate park, baseball fields, and a playground.
*Walnut Park- Walnut Park is located behind the senior citizens center. It features paved walking trails, a gazebo, and picnic tables.
*Tennessee Central Trail- This 4.23-mile trail runs along the side of an active railroad. It connects the cities of Cookeville and Algood. The paved trail is used by both hikers and bikers.
