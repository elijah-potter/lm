Celebrate whole brain learning
while playing with literary ideas in 57 selected stories listed here. Each short story comes with an online link where you can easily access its complete text. You can also use short story task cards to guide additional stories you select.
I’ve included descriptions of multiple intelligences
that can be useful in planning any unit. You’ll also find icons located on each task card to indicate which intelligence dominates in that task.
You can create your own task cards on blank cards provided
, and then indicate which of the eight intelligences you used for what if creations.
Task cards here can be used individually or in groups
– and each can be used with one or several of the stories included. Teams may do several. Enjoy stories while gaining common core standards that master assignments and tests on fiction.
You’ll find 28 whole brain tasks here,
each aligned to address common core standards, and each related to one dominant intelligence.
