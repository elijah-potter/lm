A cruise ship once left the docks of Honolulu, Hawaii, but it did not go very far. The ship suffered damage almost immediately and had to make an emergency stop on a nearby smaller island. To make matters worse, the ship's rescue boats were not able to be loosened. Thankfully, some dolphins were available in the ocean to carry the shipwrecked people back to the Hawaiian shores. 

One of the shipwrecked passengers had a pet monkey on board with him. This monkey was very mischievous as it kept getting into trouble. The monkey would often steal coconut energy drinks from his owner. While the people were waiting to hitch a ride on a dolphin's back, the monkey jumped off the ship with a coconut energy drink in hand. The monkey then pretended to drown in the ocean.

Mistaking the monkey for a human, one dolphin swam over to the monkey and made the monkey climb on its back. The dolphin started taking the monkey back to the shore.

The monkey sat up and started drinking the coconut energy drink.

"Are you a citizen of Honolulu?" the dolphin asked politely.

"Of course I am! I ran for Mayor of Honolulu last year!" the monkey replied while sipping on his drink.

"Are you drinking something, by any chance?" the dolphin asked.

"Yes! It's a coconut energy drink! I'll save some for you when we hit the shore!" the monkey replied.

Suddenly, the human owner who had lost the monkey caught up with him riding on another dolphin.

"Hey! Give me back my monkey! Slow down, dolphin!" 

The dolphin immediately stopped swimming forward and looked up to see the monkey. He tossed the monkey off his back, and the human owner caught the monkey in mid-air. The owner then tossed the coconut energy drink to the dolphin as a reward.

"Why do you always lie and deceive, you fool?" the human owner wagged his index finger at the monkey. 

Then the second dolphin abruptly tossed the human owner onto the Hawaiian shores once they came close enough.
