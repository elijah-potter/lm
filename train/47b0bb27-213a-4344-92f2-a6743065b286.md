**Daily Routine for Productive Workday**
---
**7:30 AM** – Wake up, hydrate, light stretching
**8:00 AM** – Breakfast, review morning goals
**8:30 AM – 10:30 AM** – Deep Work Block 1 (Focused task: [Project X])
**10:30 AM** – Lunch break (30 min)
**11:00 AM – 1:00 PM** – Deep Work Block 2 (Collaboration: [Team Meeting Y])
**1:00 PM** – Snack, quick walk
**1:30 PM – 3:30 PM** – Admin/Email (Prioritize urgent tasks)
**3:30 PM** – Creative Work (Brainstorming/Design: [Feature Z])
**4:00 PM** – Exercise (30 min: Gym/Yoga)
**4:30 PM – 6:30 PM** – Review Progress & Plan Next Steps
---
**Evening**
**7:00 PM** – Dinner
**8:00 PM** – Reading/Writing (Personal project: [Book Draft])
**9:00 PM** – Wind down (Journal/Reflect)
**10:00 PM** – Sleep

---
**Weekly Check-In**
- **Monday**: Tech Audit (Software updates)
- **Wednesday**: Team Sync (Feedback session)
- **Friday**: Long-term goal review (Quarterly milestones)
