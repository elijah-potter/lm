Waiting in the dimly lit corner of a cramped coffee shop, their fingers tracing idle circles on the worn tabletop. The hum of the air conditioner blends with the distant chatter of patrons, but their ears pick up nothing—no announcement, no voice, just the quiet expectation of an unspoken wait.
