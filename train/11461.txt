Urban Exploration is the exploration of man-made environments, usually abandoned or seldom visited. Urban exploration is commonly used together with Parkour, and Climbing in order to access hard-to-reach areas.
Urban exploration can be dangerous, as most areas explored are not meant for humans to enter. Storm drains and old sewers can unexpectedly flood, and old buildings present a number of hazards. Floors and ceilings may be rotten and collapse, dried pigeon dung can cause respiratory issues, and old buildings may be occupied by hostile vagrants.
