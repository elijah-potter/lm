A small fraction of women carry a gene that prevents them from producing under-arm body odour — yet most still wear deodorant.
A study, based on 6,000 women, showed that about 2 per cent had a gene variant that meant that although they perspire during exercise, they do not sweat from the glands in the armpit, which causes body odour. Despite not needing it, 78 per cent of those with the gene still used deodorant.
