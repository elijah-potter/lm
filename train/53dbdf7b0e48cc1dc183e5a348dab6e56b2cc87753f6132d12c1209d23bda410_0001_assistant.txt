Drug tests are extremely sensitive and consuming certain poppy seed foods can actually lead to positive urine drug tests. Other foods can set off a drug test, but it's nearly impossible to tell which ones will. 

During most workplace drug tests, a trained physician will list the results as negative unless they see other evidence of opiate use such as needle marks, intoxication, or very high concentrations of opiates in the system. The military on the other hand may take serious disciplinary action if they find any opioids in a drug test, even if the culprit is only poppy seed foods. 

To answer the question, Yes, poppy seeds can set off a drug test, but it generally does not result in a positive test unless they are being ultra strict.
