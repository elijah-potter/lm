A vast desert compared to a single grain of sand, yet holding untold stories like scattered pages in a forgotten book. The wind whispers secrets through canyon walls like a ghost’s faint laugh, shaping dunes into jagged teeth of time. At dusk, the sky bleeds into hues of bruised wine, darker than a thief’s deepest regret, yet brighter than the first light after dawn. The silence here is a weight heavier than a thousand elephants, but the stars above are sharper than a blade’s edge, piercing the darkness like a thousand unblinking eyes. The sun here is a slow, patient smolder, casting long shadows that stretch like veins on a sleeping giant. The air tastes of nothing—just the promise of nothingness, thick as old blood. The silence is not empty; it hums with the echoes of a world that never spoke.
