Lily the bunny loved her cozy burrow. Every morning, she’d wiggle her nose and say, "Oh, what a wonderful day!" outside. One sunny morning, she saw a shiny red apple on the ground. She picked it up and nibbled a tiny bite. "Yum!" she squeaked.

Lily’s friends—Milo the squirrel and Pip the cat—watched her. "You shouldn’t eat that," Milo said. "It might be bad for you!" Pip nodded. "We should share our snacks instead," she added.

Lily thought for a moment. She took a juicy berry from her basket and gave it to Milo. Then she shared a piece of her banana with Pip. "You can have this!" she said happily.

Milo chewed his berry, and Pip purred. "Thank you, Lily!" they said together.

From that day on, Lily always shared her snacks with her friends. And whenever she looked out at the garden, she’d smile. "What a wonderful day!" she’d say, knowing she made it even brighter with kindness.
