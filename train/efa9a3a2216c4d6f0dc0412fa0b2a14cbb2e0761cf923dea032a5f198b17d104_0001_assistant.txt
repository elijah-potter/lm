American author Mona Williams (March 1905 to December 1991) wrote nearly a dozen novels and a book of poems, one of which was adapted into a screenplay in 1954. Her last novel, This House is Burning, was quite intriguing. Written in 1978, it has a Gothic romance feel with scintillating family secrets, a dilapidated mansion, and a charming detective determined to find out the truth, even if it’s more horrible than anything he can imagine.
