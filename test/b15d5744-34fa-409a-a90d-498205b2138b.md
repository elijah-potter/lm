**Incident Response Policy Addendum**

1. **Immediate Actions Upon Discovery**
   - Designated HR representative to conduct a confidential investigation within **24 hours** of notification, documenting all evidence and witness statements.
   - Mandatory suspension of the involved employee (on leave without pay) pending investigation, unless otherwise authorized by legal counsel.

2. **Confidentiality & Record-Keeping**
   - All discussions remain strictly confidential; only essential personnel (legal, HR, management) access incident details.
   - Maintain digital records of communications, evidence, and findings in a secure, immutable database.

3. **Investigation Protocol**
   - Conduct a **neutral, fact-based review** using evidence from:
     - Witness interviews (recorded or transcribed).
     - Surveillance footage (if applicable).
     - Electronic communications (if permitted by policy).
   - Exclude any biased or uncorroborated claims; focus solely on verifiable facts.

4. **Disciplinary Action**
   - **First Offense:** Written warning + mandatory counseling session with HR/psychological support.
   - **Second Offense:** Suspension (paid or unpaid) + termination if repeat behavior is confirmed.
   - **Third Offense:** Immediate termination with zero tolerance for retaliation.

5. **Restorative Measures**
   - If the incident involved **misconduct toward colleagues**, mandatory training on workplace etiquette and conflict resolution.
   - For **repeated violations**, require participation in external HR/legal mediation.

6. **Legal Compliance & Reporting**
   - Notify **labor relations** if the incident involves discrimination, harassment, or regulatory violations.
   - Provide a **summary report** to legal counsel within **10 business days** of conclusion.

7. **Employee Rights & Protections**
   - No retaliation against complainants or witnesses; protected under company anti-retaliation policy.
   - Involved employee’s employment status is finalized only after a signed, legally vetted decision letter.

**End of Policy Addendum**
