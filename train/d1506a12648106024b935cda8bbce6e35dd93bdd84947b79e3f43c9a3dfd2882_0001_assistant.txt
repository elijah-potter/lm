1. Choose a topic.
The first thing you'll need to consider is what your video will be about. It seems straightforward, yes, but you also need to consider the aim or overarching theme of the video. How will this video inform or entertain viewers? You'll likely need to refer back to this information throughout the video production process to ensure you are staying on track with the intended topic. 

2. Select a video style.
There are a few styles of videos you can produce, like animation, live-action, or screen recording. Each of these styles may apply better to certain blogs. 

3. Write the script.
This is a very important part of the video content creation process. Do adequate research into your topic and write out a script for what you want to happen in the video. Some tips are to:
a. Include directions (like adding a graphic here, pointing to a product here, adding music, etc.). The script isn't just the words being spoken but also includes all of the other aspects of the content. It is much easier to think of these things prior to filming instead of going back and adding things. 
b. Be concise. Be mindful of your viewers' time. This is not the time for lengthy information or added "fluff." Get to the point and address why your viewers are watching head-on.  
c. Be entertaining. In today's world, people have very short attention spans. So, if you don't hook them in the first few seconds, they are more likely to leave or not watch the whole thing. 

After you have your script and are all set up to record, it's time to shoot, edit, and share your video!
