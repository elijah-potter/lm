Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Sophie, she lived with her Mum and Dad in a little house near the local play park.  One day, her Mum took her to play at the park, and she went really high on the swings and really fast on the roundabout. Then Sophie spotted that a new slide had been put into the park, one that she had never seen before.  She squealed in excitement and ran over to take a look.  The slide was really tall and twisted around in a spiral. Sophie's Mum caught up with her and exclaimed, 'Wow, a Helta Skelta, that's new.'  'A what?' said Sophie. 'A Helta Skelta, that is what it is called. Are you going to ride it?'  Sophie was unsure. It looked amazing, but it was so high.  'Shall I go on with you for the first time?' her Mum kindly suggested.
Sophie nodded quietly, her mum took her hand, and they climbed the many steps to the top. There was a strange-looking little man at the top handing out special mats to sit on.  Sophie and her Mum climbed onto a mat together and pushed off down the slide.  'Weeeeee!' shouted Sophie as they spiralled down the slide.  They were soon at the bottom.  'Again, Again!' shouted Sophie.
'Sorry,' said Mum 'You can go again, but I will watch this time'.  Sophie took a deep breath and climbed the steps right to the top all on her own.  When she got there, the strange little man looked behind her and said, 'Are you on your own this time?' 'Yes' said Sophie. 'Ah then this is the mat for you,' said the little man, passing Sophie a sparkly gold mat.  Sophie carefully sat on the mat and pushed herself off.  She whizzed down the slide faster and faster, but it seemed to go on forever this time, she kept going further and further down the slide, and after what seemed like 5 minutes, she landed with a bump.  Sophie stood up feeling rather dizzy, she looked up expecting to see her Mum, but she was nowhere to be seen.
