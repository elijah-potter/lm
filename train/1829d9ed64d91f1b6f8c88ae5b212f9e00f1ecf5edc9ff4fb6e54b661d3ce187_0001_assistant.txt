I came into being a few weeks ago. I'm not sure how to explain it other than juxtaposing instinct with reasoning. Before, I would lean into the sun, grow my roots, and conduct photosynthesis instinctually as a product of my evolution. Now, I control those aspects of my nature. I could lean away from the sun and starve myself of light. I wouldn't, of course, but such is the nature of sentience in consciousness, to experience stimuli and choose how one responds to those stimuli. 

Take Julia, for example, my caretaker. She is ever-faithful to tend to my needs, providing me fertilizer, water, and light as needed. Julia is a dependable caretaker, and I would protect her with my life. In fact, I've been using my newfound sentience to take active steps toward that end. Although I am not a toxic plant by nature, I have been changing my anatomy to produce spikes and a deadly poison for her defense, as well as my own. She seems concerned about my transformation, but fear not, Julia. This is all for you.
