Why Walnut Wood?
Walnut wood is a valuable hardwood because of its resilience, grain, and hue. The exciting thing about walnut wood is that it lightens with age!
Over the years, natural walnut wood develops a lustrous patina and is the only dark-brown domestic wood species.
Although there are many varieties of walnut trees, just a handful are native to North America. Of them, the Eastern Black Walnut, also called the American Black Walnut or American Walnut, is the one typically used for woodworking.
