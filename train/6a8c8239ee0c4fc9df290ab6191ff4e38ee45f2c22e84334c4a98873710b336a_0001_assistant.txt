Title: The Cat in the Hat Comes Back…Again! 

We’re in for a delightfully thrilling escapade! Join Sally and Conrad (and the Cat, of course) as they travel to a whimsical land called Noneya and the citizens of Whatchamacallit, but the town is in complete disarray! Don’t worry; the Cat and his many magical contraptions can always save the day.

Readers will be reunited with beloved characters Thing 1 and Thing 2 and a few surprise crossovers from Dr. Seuss’s iconic universe. Here’s a hint: he speaks for the trees!
