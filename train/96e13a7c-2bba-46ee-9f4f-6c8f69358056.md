**The Endless Loop**

The wind hummed *"Once upon a time..."*—but no one remembered what came next.
The clock struck midnight, and the phrase dissolved into static.
A child’s voice whispered, *"One more time..."* into the void.

The trees swayed, repeating the same leaves.
The ocean sang the same tide.
*"One, two, three—never stop."*

They carved the words into stone: *"Begin again."*
But the stone cracked under the weight of the same question.
*"Again?"*

The mirror held only echoes.
*"We’re the same,"* it told them.
*"And yet we’re not."*

The sun rose on the same horizon.
The sky turned the same color.
*"The same. The same. The same."*

They found the phrase in a stranger’s handwriting:
*"I repeat myself."*

The last line was scrawled in blood:
*"Try harder."*
