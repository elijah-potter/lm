The old clock tower creaked as rain hammered its weathered surface, each drop a silent countdown. Inside, Elias adjusted the last thread of his plan—his fingers traced the rusted mechanism of the broken hinge while the city below pulsed with life.

The grand hall was silent save for the distant chime of a dying bell. Across from him, Dr. Veyra sat frozen, her eyes locked onto the skeletal frame of the old mechanism. *"You said it would hold,"* she whispered. Elias exhaled, watching her fingers hover over the exposed gears. *"It’s not the mechanism,"* he said, voice low. *"It’s the hinge."*

The moment the last bolt was pulled free, the tower groaned—then tilted, just enough to reveal the hidden passage beneath. Elias grabbed Veyra’s wrist and yanked her forward, the air thick with the scent of damp stone. The door swung inward with a sigh, and they tumbled inside, landing in a pool of moonlight filtering through stained glass. Behind them, the clock tower’s hands stopped mid-second—12:59:59.
