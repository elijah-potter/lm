The dim glow of a flickering lamp cast long shadows across the empty room. A single chair sat unoccupied near the window, its fabric slightly damp from condensation. Outside, the wind howled through the skeletal branches of a nearby tree, rattling the panes. The door creaked open, then shut silently behind the figure inside—**Alex**.

They stood at the kitchen counter, hands clasped tightly around a steaming mug of black coffee. The radio, tuned to the news station, pulsed with static. A pause. A breath. Then:

*"Breaking news... A major incident has been reported near the city’s eastern border. Authorities are investigating multiple missing persons reports. Initial reports indicate... structural collapse at an abandoned facility..."*

Alex exhaled sharply, the scent of coffee and cold steel mingling in the air. Their fingers tightened around the mug. The radio clicked off.

*"What do you want to know?"* came a voice from the hallway.

Alex turned. **Mira** stood there, arms crossed, her expression unreadable. The weight of the news settled between them like a shared burden.
