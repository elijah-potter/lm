The air smelled faintly of damp earth and burnt wood, lingering from the crackling fire left in the hearth. Sunlight filtered through the stained-glass windows, casting jagged patterns across the crumbling marble floor. The wooden beams in the high ceiling groaned, their paint peeling to reveal the rot beneath. A half-empty glass of whiskey sat on the cluttered desk, its rim splattered with dust. A torn newspaper lay atop a stack of books—*The Times* from last Tuesday. The fireplace’s remains were still warm, with a single, unlit coal glowing at its center. The front door stood slightly ajar, its brass handle rusted but intact. A single, bloodstained handprint glowed faintly under the dim lamp’s glow on the wall.
