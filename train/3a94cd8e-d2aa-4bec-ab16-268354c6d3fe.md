Over time, the repetitive motion of constantly checking your phone—even when not in use—became ingrained. Initially, it was just a habit of staying updated, but as time passed, it evolved into compulsive behavior. The need to verify notifications or scroll through feeds became an obsession, disrupting focus during work and personal tasks. Eventually, the habit turned into a distraction, draining productivity and mental energy. The cycle of anxiety and the urge to check grew more intense, making it harder to resist. Eventually, the routine became a problem, affecting relationships, work efficiency, and overall well-being.
