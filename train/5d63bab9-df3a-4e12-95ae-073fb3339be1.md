Unresolved tensions persist between stakeholders over conflicting priorities: **cost savings** vs. **operational integrity**, with budget cuts to HR and IT infrastructure leading to staff burnout. Leadership’s insistence on a "win-win" solution has been perceived as hollow—while short-term gains in efficiency are noted, long-term morale remains low, and compliance risks remain unaddressed.
