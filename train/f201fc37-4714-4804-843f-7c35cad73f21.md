Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big red door, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown ears and a tail that wiggled when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny saw his favorite friend, a blue butterfly named Butter, fluttering near the garden. "Hello, Butter!" Benny said, hopping over. "Can we play today?"

Butter smiled and said, "Sure! Let's chase butterflies!" So they ran outside. Benny hopped, and Butter fluttered, laughing with joy.

Nearby, a tiny mouse named Muff lived under a bush. Muff peeked out, "Hi, Benny! Hi, Butter! Want to join us?"

"Of course!" Benny said, and the three friends played together. Muff showed Benny how to dig in the dirt, and Butter taught everyone to flap their wings. It was the best playtime ever!

As the sun began to set, Benny and Butter waved goodbye to Muff. "Goodnight, friends!" they said, snuggling under their blankets. The garden was quiet, but Benny knew they'd play again tomorrow.
