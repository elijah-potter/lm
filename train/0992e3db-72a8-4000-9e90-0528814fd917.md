A sales associate, mid-morning, glanced at a hesitant customer lingering over a display of premium coffee blends. The customer hesitated, then said, *"Actually, I was just curious—how much does the seasonal menu have changed this year?"*

The associate, momentarily distracted, replied, *"Oh, the seasonal menu? Well, we’ve added some new local honey varieties this year—great for those who love a sweet twist!"* The customer nodded, but their eyes darted away, seeking a different subject. The associate, sensing the shift, asked, *"So, how’s your new plant doing? It’s been looking quite vibrant lately."* The customer shifted focus to their potted fern, muttering about humidity levels.
