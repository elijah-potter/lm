## TIL That Honey Never Truly Spoils

Today I learned that honey, when properly stored, is essentially immortal. Archaeological excavations have uncovered edible honey in ancient Egyptian tombs, some dating back over 3,000 years. This remarkable preservation stems from honey’s unique chemical composition and properties.

Firstly, honey is incredibly low in water content, making it an inhospitable environment for microorganisms like bacteria and fungi that typically cause spoilage. Secondly, it is highly acidic, further inhibiting microbial growth. Finally, honey contains hydrogen peroxide, a natural antibacterial agent produced by an enzyme bees add during the honey-making process.

While honey can crystallize over time (which is a natural process and easily reversible), it doesn't actually *decay*. The ancient Egyptians clearly understood this, utilizing honey as a food source and preservative for extended periods. It’s a fascinating testament to the ingenuity of nature and the enduring qualities of this remarkable substance.
