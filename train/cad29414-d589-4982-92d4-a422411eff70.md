The receptionist’s pen hovered mid-air as she scribbled a note, then abruptly lowered it. *"We’re not accepting new clients this month"*—her usual opening line—was met with silence. Across the office, the intern shifted uncomfortably, fingers tapping a knee. The CEO, who had been discussing last quarter’s budget, turned to a colleague. *"Ah, yes, the new marketing campaign—"*

*"Oh, that’s great!"* the intern chimed in, changing the subject to the upcoming staff picnic. The CEO blinked, then pivoted to the architect in the corner. *"Speaking of which, the roof inspection report is due next week."*
