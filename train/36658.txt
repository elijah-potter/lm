Consider installing solar powered lamps for outdoor lighting. Not only are they a reasonable purchase price, but they do not require electricity to run. This will end up saving you a lot of energy. Also, it will no longer be necessary to install wired outdoor lights.
Become a better energy consumer, and save electricity in your home by unplugging chargers for your electronic devices when you are not using them. Chargers for laptops, mp3 players, cell phones and other devices use power when they’re plugged in. This happens regardless of whether you’re using the device or not.

One great way of conserving energy is to ensure the dishwasher is only turned on when full. Running it with half loads is wasteful. Pack your dishwasher full, and you may be surprised at the amount it can hold. Set up the plates so that you fill it up as much as possible.
When planning a home solar system, calculate the potential energy generation of the system using hours of sunshine in the winter. When you do this, you understand how much you should be consuming on a daily basis during winter, and you won’t become surprised one day. However, you should also include any refunds you will get from your utility company for excess energy generated in summer!
If you are on a mission to go green, start by keeping electric heaters off or on a very low setting during winter months. Wrap up your family in warmer clothes and make use of your fireplace for added warmth. You want to remember that sleeping in cooler air is better for your breathing, and it also prevents the airways from becoming dry.
If you are building a new home or are in the market for a new water heater, consider a tankless model. Instead of keeping a whole tank of water hot at all times, tankless models only heat the water you are actually going to use. You can find tankless heaters that either supply an entire house, or just a single faucet.
Instead of buying that new desktop computer, purchase a laptop, as desktops use much more electricity. Laptops use up to 75% less electricity than desktops, especially during non-demanding tasks like surfing the web or word processing. Laptops are also portable which makes them much more functional and handy in times of need.
If you are someone who is seeking out ways to live green so you can help save the planet’s energy, there’s a lot you can do. Many of these options will not cost you money, but will instead save you money, like cleaning your furnace filter monthly and turning down your thermostat when you are leaving your home. Cutting your water heater temperature down to 120 degrees is another way to conserve energy and see smaller utility bills. No matter how small, everything helps!
Switch as much of your correspondence as possible to email. Specifically, go paperless whenever you can. This helps to lower the energy costs of businesses as they communicate, and you can save energy as well. This benefits everyone in society along with your own business.
If you’re one of many who wants to live an environmentally friendly life, there’s plenty you can do. You might not have the money to invest in a solar panel array, but you can commit to cleaning your furnace filters once per month and setting your thermostat to 60 degrees while you are out. You can also help reduce energy usage by reducing the temperature of your water heater to 120 degrees. No change is too small.
You can use biofuels as home heating fuels. This type of fuel is usually made with a combination of animal fat, vegetable oil and wood. If you have a propane furnace, a professional can usually make changes that allow you to use this blend of fuels. These changes would let you use biofuels is between 20 percent and 90 percent of the fuel used in the furnace. Make sure you talk with a professional first before you try this fuel out.
