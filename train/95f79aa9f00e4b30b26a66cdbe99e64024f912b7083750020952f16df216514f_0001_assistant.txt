Here are 3 different themes you can use for a gender-neutral baby shower:

1. Animals- You can decorate with stuffed animals, animal balloons, and animal tableware and decorations. You can use animals such as woodland creatures, ocean, or safari animals. 

2. Baby Books- You can have guests bring their favorite children's books and write a message on the inside and the books can be displayed in a basket or on a bookshelf. You can decorate based on your favorite baby or children's book. 

3. Rainbow- Rainbows can be a colorful way to decorate and remain gender-neutral. You can have rainbow-colored decorations such as balloons, tablecloths, and streamers, as well as cake, and food.
