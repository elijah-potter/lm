The article described the find, but omitted the coordinates, leaving the location a mystery. The professor and the telegraph operator vanished without a trace within days. Rumors persisted that the stone had been stolen by a secret society of 'bridge builders,' though no evidence was ever produced. By 1895, the Union Turnpike had been rerouted, and the original site was lost to development. The only surviving artifact was the professor’s son’s sketch, now housed in the New Jersey Historical Society. No one has found the stone since, and the coordinates remain a puzzle—some believe it was a hoax, others that it was real."*
