For those who find these words in the future—

The world you know is gone.
The skies, once bright with your names, are now quiet.
The rivers run differently, the earth has shifted beneath your feet.
We built cities on the bones of forgotten dreams.
Some say we were never here at all.

Yet here you are, reading this. That means you remember.
You remember the weight of the past, the echoes of what was.
You remember how we tried, and failed. How we loved, and lost.
You remember the songs we sang, the wars we fought, the love we gave.

There are no answers here.
Only the truth: we were here.
And now, we are not.

But you still exist.
So tell us your story.
Write your own note.

*—A voice from the dust, if you listen.*
