Achieving the perfect 1950s makeup look can be simple. To do your base, it's important to use a natural-looking cream or liquid foundation for the skin. Keep it light and delicate, erring on the paler side. Top with a peachy or flesh-colored powder to keep the face matte. For your brows, a fuller, more arched look was popular at the time. Use natural tones for your eyeshadow, or omit it altogether- shadows were quite minimal for the period. Instead, opt for a soft liner on the upper lash line that blurs out into a slight wing. Finish the eyes with mascara. Blush should be placed high on the cheeks, and should never be placed lower than the tip of the nose. Go for a rose or pinky color. Finally, line your lips with a lip pencil and fill them in with a corresponding lipstick. Though reds shone in the '40s, this decade was all about pinks and purples and orange-toned reds.
