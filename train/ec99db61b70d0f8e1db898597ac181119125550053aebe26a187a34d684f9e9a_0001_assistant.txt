Computers are cool, 
they can do so many things 
They can help you learn 
and play and sing 
They can show you pictures 
and videos and games 
They can help you write 
and draw and make

Computers are cool, 
they can connect you with your friends 
They can help you chat 
and call and send 
They can show you maps 
and news and weather 
They can help you plan 
and shop and travel

Computers are cool, 
they can solve problems big and small 
They can help you calculate 
and create and recall 
They can show you facts 
and figures and graphs 
They can help you study 
and research and pass

Computers are cool, 
they can make your dreams come true 
They can help you imagine 
and design and do 
They can show you wonders 
and dreams and more 
They can help you explore 
and discover and soar
