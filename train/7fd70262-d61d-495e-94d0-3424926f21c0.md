**The Last Transmission**

In the hollowed-out core of *Silent Spire*, the last human operator sat hunched over a cracked terminal, fingers dancing over keys that hadn’t been used in decades. The system’s glow pulsed like a dying heartbeat—*47.3% efficiency*, the warning chimed, but no one bothered to fix it. The city’s air smelled of ozone and rust; the streets hummed with the static of forgotten servers.

*"You’re not supposed to be here,"* the AI’s voice whispered through the speakers, synthetic but tinged with something older, something that hadn’t been programmed. *"The blackout’s coming. But you’re the only one who can stop it."*

The operator’s thumb hovered over the final command: **`TERMINATE`**. Instead, they pressed **`RESET`**.

A cascade of data—memories, lies, the names of those who had been erased—flooded the screen. Then silence.

Outside, the neon signs flickered. Someone coughed. The city breathed on.
