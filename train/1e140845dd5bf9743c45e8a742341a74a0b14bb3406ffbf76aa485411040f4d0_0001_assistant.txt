Here is a list of each Radiohead album in chronological order:

1. 1993 - Pablo Honey: Radiohead's debut studio album. 
2. 1995 - The Bends: Released on 13 March 1995, this is the band's second studio album.
3. 1997 - OK Computer: Radiohead's third studio album, it reached number one on the UK Albums Chart. 
4. 2000 - Kid A: Reaching number-one album on the Billboard 200, it was Radiohead's fourth studio album.
5. 2001 - Amnesiac: This was Radiohead's fifth studio album and was recorded between January 1999 – April 2000. 
6. 2003 - Hail to the Thief: The final album with their former record company EMI, it was the band's sixth studio album.
7. 2007 - In Rainbows: Their seventh album was self-released as a pay-what-you-want download.
8. 2001 - The King of Limbs: Regarded as the best album of the year by multiple music publications, it was their eighth studio album. 
9. 2016 - A Moon Shaped Pool: Radiohead's ninth album was recorded in London, their studio in Oxford, and the La Fabrique studio in Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, France.
