The most common loss of data occurs due to the failure of a small device inside your computer called a hard drive. Lots of reasons can cause this data loss, and we can recover the data no matter what has happened.
We recover data loss resulting from:
- Bad Sectors
- Virus Activity
- File System Corruption
- Operating System Corruption
- Hardware Failure
- Electronic Circuitry Failure
There are tell-tell signs of hard drives beginning to fail, and an experienced tech can identify these for you and recommend replacing your hard drive – it’s not expensive, but can save you a headache of lost data, so schedule an appointment today.
