Texita, a New Casual Tex-Mex Restaurant, Opens in Frisco, Tx, on June 11th
Frisco, Texas– Texita, a new casual Tex-Mex restaurant, will open its doors to the public on June 11th. Located at 6301 Avenue K Frisco, Tx. 75024, Texita will offer a wide variety of fresh, made-to-order Tex-Mex dishes in a comfortable and inviting atmosphere.
The menu at Texita features all of your favorite Tex-Mex classics, including tacos, burritos, enchiladas, and fajitas. All of the dishes are made with fresh, high-quality ingredients and are cooked to order. 
The interior of Texita is designed to be comfortable and inviting. The restaurant has a large dining room with plenty of seating. Texita is the perfect place to enjoy a casual meal with friends or family.
“We are excited to open Texita and bring our fresh, made-to-order Tex-Mex cuisine to Frisco,” said Jennifer Jones, owner of Texita. “We believe that Texita will be a great addition to the community and we look forward to serving our guests for years to come.”
Texita will be open for lunch and dinner seven days a week. For more information, please visit www.texita.com
