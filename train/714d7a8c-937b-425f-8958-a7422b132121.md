A sharp-eyed barista noticed the faint, unmistakable scent of lavender lingering in the morning air of the coffee shop. While steeping the last batch of brew, they paused to observe the steam curling upward from the drip tray—where, tucked beneath the sugar bowl, lay a single, crumpled business card. The name on it was unfamiliar, but the logo resembled a design from a local organic bakery. Intrigued, they retrieved it, only to find a handwritten note: *"Thank you for the first cup. Meet me at the old mill tomorrow—bring the same scent."* The barista’s curiosity was piqued, blending their usual routine with a mystery they couldn’t ignore.
