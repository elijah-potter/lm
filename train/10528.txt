Get your kiddos exploring character traits with this selection of 30 graphic organizers. Students will be asked to find 3, 4, or 5 character traits for one character in the book or story they are reading. They will also write a page number where the character displayed each trait. After the graphic organizer is finished, children can use their information to write a short character sketch on the template provided. I have also included a template for students to use to list character traits from A to Z.
