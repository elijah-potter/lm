Here are three places to backpack abroad:
1. Cambodia: Learn about the country's complex past by visiting the cities and towns. Take a trip to the beach and ancient temples.
2. India: Visit Goa and Kerala for town life and beaches. Go to Rajasthan in the desert to see its grand forts and palaces.
3. Laos: Discover the culture of Laos by boating down the Mekon River, viewing the countryside, and visiting the town to drink coffee at the local market.
