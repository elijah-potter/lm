Eggs have high amounts of dietary cholesterol, with one large egg offering 200 grams. However, research shows that fats in your diet affect your cholesterol levels more than cholesterol in food. The food that you eat alongside your eggs also affects your health--sliced avocados and grilled tomatoes are lighter options than bacon and sausage. Additionally, eggs are a good source of protein, vitamin A, biotin, choline, and antioxidants. These nutrients boost your immune system and heighten your energy levels.
