## Project Chimera: A Cooperative Research Crisis

**A game for 2-4 players, ages 14+**

**Playing Time:** 90-120 minutes

**Introduction:**

Project Chimera places you in the roles of specialized researchers at the Global Response Initiative (GRI), a clandestine organization dedicated to addressing existential threats. A rapidly evolving, synthetic pathogen—designated Chimera—is spreading across the globe, exhibiting unpredictable mutations and defying conventional medical understanding. Your team must collaborate to understand Chimera's behavior, develop countermeasures, and ultimately contain the crisis before it overwhelms humanity. Success demands strategic planning, efficient resource management, and a willingness to make difficult choices under immense pressure.



### Components

*   **Crisis Board:** A central board depicting a world map divided into regions, with spaces for Crisis Markers and Research Tokens.
*   **Researcher Cards (8):** Each card represents a researcher with unique abilities and starting resources (e.g., Virologist, Epidemiologist, Geneticist, Logistics Expert).
*   **Chimera Mutation Cards (40):** These cards dictate Chimera's evolving properties and introduce escalating challenges.
*   **Research Tokens (60):** Representing data, samples, and insights gathered during research.
*   **Countermeasure Cards (30):** Representing potential treatments and containment strategies.
*   **Crisis Markers (20):** Used to track the spread and severity of the crisis in each region.
*   **Resource Cubes (100):** Representing Funding, Personnel, and Equipment.
*   **Dice (6):** Standard six-sided dice.
*   **First Player Token:** Designates the current active player.



### Setup

1.  Place the Crisis Board in the center of the playing area.
2.  Shuffle the Chimera Mutation Cards and place them face down as a draw pile. Reveal the top three cards and place them face up beside the deck. These represent the current known mutations.
3.  Shuffle the Countermeasure Cards and place them face down as a draw pile.
4.  Each player chooses a Researcher Card and takes the starting resources indicated on the card.
5.  Place three Crisis Markers in the “Origin” region (designated on the Crisis Board).
6.  Place the First Player Token with the player who most recently read a scientific article.



### Turn Structure

Each player's turn consists of the following phases, performed in order:

1.  **Draw Mutation Card:** Draw one Chimera Mutation Card and immediately resolve its effects. These effects typically involve spreading Crisis Markers, modifying Chimera’s properties (affecting research and countermeasures), or introducing new challenges.
2.  **Action Phase:** You have three actions to spend. You may choose the same action multiple times. Available actions are:
    *   **Research (1 Action):** Choose a region. Roll two dice. The result determines the number of Research Tokens you gain. The Chimera Mutation Cards may modify this roll.
    *   **Move (1 Action):** Move your researcher to an adjacent region.
    *   **Develop Countermeasure (2 Actions):** Discard Research Tokens matching the requirements on a Countermeasure Card. Draw a new Countermeasure Card.
    *   **Containment (2 Actions):** Discard Resource Cubes (Funding, Personnel, Equipment) to remove Crisis Markers from a region. The number of markers removed depends on the resources spent.
    *   **Share Resources (1 Action):** Transfer any number of Resource Cubes to another player in the same region.
3.  **End Phase:** Pass the First Player Token to the player on your left.

### Win and Loss Conditions

**Winning the Game:**

You win the game when you successfully develop and deploy three Countermeasure Cards, each targeting a different aspect of Chimera (e.g., transmission, mutation, virulence). The cards must be “Deployed” by discarding the required Resource Cubes.

**Losing the Game:**

You lose the game if any of the following conditions are met:

*   **Global Collapse:** A total of fifteen Crisis Markers are present on the Crisis Board at any time.
*   **Mutation Overload:** The Chimera Mutation Card deck is exhausted.
*   **Researcher Incapacitation:** All researchers are simultaneously removed from the board due to Crisis Marker effects.



### Trade-Off Mechanics

Project Chimera incorporates several mechanics that force difficult trade-offs under uncertainty:

1.  **Mutation Prioritization:** The three face-up Chimera Mutation Cards represent immediate threats. However, drawing new cards is crucial for long-term understanding.  Players must decide whether to focus on mitigating existing mutations or risk drawing potentially more dangerous ones to gain a broader understanding of Chimera.
2.  **Countermeasure Specialization:** Countermeasure Cards require specific combinations of Research Tokens. Players must decide which research paths to prioritize, knowing that focusing on one area may leave other vulnerabilities unaddressed. Sharing research data is vital, but also introduces a dependency on other players.
3.  **Containment Risk:**  Containment efforts require significant Resource Cube expenditure.  Players must balance immediate containment needs with the long-term sustainability of their resources, as depletion can hinder future research and development.



### Difficulty Scaling

The game’s difficulty scales based on the number of players:

*   **Two Players:** Start with two Crisis Markers in the Origin region.  Reduce the number of Countermeasure Cards required to win to two.
*   **Three Players:** Play with the standard setup described above.
*   **Four Players:** Increase the number of Crisis Markers in the Origin region to three.



### Advanced Variants

*   **Adaptive Chimera:** After a Countermeasure Card is deployed, shuffle it back into the Countermeasure Card deck. This simulates Chimera adapting to deployed treatments, increasing the challenge.
*   **Black Market:** Introduce a small deck of "Black Market" cards. These cards can provide temporary benefits (e.g., extra resources, faster research) but often come with significant drawbacks (e.g., increased risk of mutation, loss of reputation).
*   **Specialist Focus:** Each player chooses a “Specialization” at the beginning of the game, granting them a unique ability but also restricting their actions. For example, the “Logistics Expert” can move more freely, while the “Geneticist” gains bonuses to research.



### Designer’s Note

Project Chimera was designed to provide a compelling cooperative experience while subtly exploring themes of scientific responsibility and the challenges of addressing global crises. The game mechanics are intended to illustrate the complex trade-offs inherent in scientific research, emphasizing the importance of collaboration, communication, and adaptability in the face of uncertainty. The escalating threat of Chimera reflects the unpredictable nature of emerging pathogens and the need for proactive, evidence-based responses. While presented as a fictional scenario, the game aims to foster a greater appreciation for the critical role of scientific inquiry and the importance of informed decision-making in safeguarding public health.
