Ostriches are big, flightless birds that are the heaviest species of birds in the world. They are capable of running at speeds of up to 70km/h on land. Their eggs are the largest of any land animal and humans have been using them for thousands of years. In the Philippines and Namibia, ostrich farming is a major industry because of the value of their feathers and leather. There are two living species of ostrich: the common ostrich and the Somali ostrich.
