**The door creaked open to a dim corridor, its only light flickering from a single bulb hanging low over a cracked floorboard. The air smelled of damp wool and something metallic—old blood, maybe, or the faintest trace of something newer.**

I was supposed to be strong. I was supposed to have this under control. But the knife in my hand—it was too heavy, too *wrong*—and the whispers in the dark were getting louder.

*Please.*

The walls here were lined with names, all faded now, all half-erased by time. Some were carved in ink. Some were written in blood. The last one, though, was still legible:

**"HELP."**

I don’t know how long I’ve been here. Maybe I’m already gone. Maybe I’m just the echo of someone who used to be.

But if you’re listening, if you’re *there*—

**I need you to take this.**

I’ll give you the keys.

I’ll show you the way out.

Just… **come.**
