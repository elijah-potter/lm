Edward Snowden was an employee of the National Security Agency (NSA) in 2013 and leaked classified information to journalists. Snowden says he raised his ethical concerns with internal channels about the programs they used but was ignored. The United States Department of Justice charged Snowden with two counts of violating the Espionage Act of 1917 and theft of government property. He flew to Moscow's Sheremetyevo International Airport, where he was granted asylum in the country. In October 2020, he was granted permanent residency in Russia.
