There's a beauty in the slow rumble of thunder.
The rain washes the streets clean so we can begin again.
Inside, I watch the sky torn asunder
By the brilliant flashes of lightning.

The sound washes away the traffic and noise
That clutters my mind and invades my peace
Mother Nature's true strength and poise
Reminds us that we are small indeed.

I curl up inside my blankets with a hot cup
I take out a book and escape into fantasy
To quiet the thundering gallop
Of my runaway thoughts.
