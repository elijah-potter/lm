Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a garden full of flowers, lived a tiny bunny named Pip. Pip loved to hop around and play with his friends, especially a bright blue bird named Bella and a curious little mouse named Max.

One sunny morning, Pip spotted a shiny red apple near the fence. "Look, Max! A big red apple!" he said. Max squeaked, "Can I have one?" Pip smiled, "Of course!" and gently handed it to him. Max nibbled a tiny bit and said, "Yummy! But I don't want the whole thing."

Bella fluttered down, "Pip, let's share! Share is nice." So they all took a bite, and Pip said, "This is the best apple ever!"

That afternoon, Pip found a patch of green beans in the garden. He picked one and showed it to Bella. "Bella, look! Green beans!" Bella said, "They’re crunchy and yummy!" Max added, "I love them!" They all ate a few together, giggling with joy.

As the sun began to set, Pip looked out the window. "Look, Max! The sky is turning pink!" Max gasped, "It’s almost time to go to bed." Pip hugged his friends. "Goodnight, Bella! Goodnight, Max!" Bella chirped, "Goodnight, Pip!" Max whispered, "Goodnight, everyone!" and curled up for a nap.

Pip snuggled under his blanket, dreaming of hopping and flying with his friends. And in the garden, the flowers swayed gently, ready to bloom again tomorrow.
