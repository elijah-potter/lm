Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a talking piece of pizza named Pep E. Roni. Pep had always been curious about the world outside of the pizza box. From the time he first came out of the oven, he was always talking to the rest of the slices of pizza about what it might be like. One day he decided to see for himself, so he climbed out the kitchen window and went for a walk.

As Pep strolled through the neighboring park, he felt the warm sun on his crust and the cool breeze blowing through his curls of cheese. He saw butterflies in the sky. He saw flowers fluttering in the grass. He saw squirrels staring at him from a tree. They had never seen a talking pizza before! Their amazement soon turned to hunger, so Pep ran away fast!

Pep continued his walk, taking in the sights and sounds of the park. He passed a group of children playing on a playground and they stopped to greet him. The kids were delighted to meet a talking pizza and asked him all sorts of questions like "What's your favorite topping?" and "How much sauce is too much?"

As the sun began to set, Pep realized it was time to head back to his warm, safe box. As he made his way home, he felt grateful for the beautiful day he had just experienced and couldn't wait to tell all the other slices about his spring walk.
