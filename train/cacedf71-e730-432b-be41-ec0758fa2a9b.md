The old man sat on the edge of the cracked wooden dock, his breath coming slow and steady in the damp autumn air. A fishing rod leaned against the railing, its line taut with the weight of forgotten days.

*"It's always like this,"* he muttered, staring at the still water where the last remnants of sunset painted the sky in gold and crimson. *"The same old stories, the same old mistakes. Just another night at the pier."*

The crickets chirped louder, their song a rhythmic pulse beneath the eerie quiet. The tide rolled in, whispering secrets against the rocks. He exhaled, watching the mist curl around his feet like ghostly fingers.

*"The water doesn’t care,"* he said, his voice rough with age. *"Neither do I. It’s just us, the same old struggle."*

The boat drifted downstream, its hull slick with rain, as if the sea itself had absorbed the weight of his sorrow. He watched it disappear into the mist, the repetition of his thoughts echoing in the dark.
