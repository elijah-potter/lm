In a dimly lit alleyway, rain slicking the cobblestones, a hooded figure clutched a rusted key to a rusted lock. The door of the abandoned theater groaned under the weight of years, its once-grand marquee now peeling with age. The promise was whispered in a voice that had faded to a murmur: *"I’ll get you out."* The promise was a single candle in a room full of shadows, burning bright enough to cast long, trembling fingers across the pavement. Now, the figure stood at the threshold, the lock turned slowly, the door creaking open. The air inside was thick with the scent of old paper and unwashed tears. The promise was being tested.
