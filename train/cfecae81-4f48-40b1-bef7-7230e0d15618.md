The team missed the early warning signs of the project's misalignment with stakeholder needs. Post-mortem analysis revealed the misalignment stemmed from a lack of iterative feedback loops during the initial sprints, despite documented agile practices. Key stakeholders were not engaged until late stages, when it was too late to adjust scope without disrupting timelines. The obvious course of action—conducting stakeholder interviews and refining the backlog—was overlooked due to rushed sprint planning. The final decision to proceed with the high-risk feature led to cascading delays and cost overruns, highlighting the importance of proactive communication in agile environments.
