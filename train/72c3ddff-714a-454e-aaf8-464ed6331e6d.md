Endless voids stretch beyond horizonless skies,
Silent galaxies drift like ghostly constellations,
Echoes of cosmic whispers fade into static,
A silent expanse where even light’s path bends—
No horizon, no end—just the endless, indifferent,
Unfettered by time, by space, by gravity’s grasp.
The stars are not stars so much as scattered embers
In a fire that never burns, a void that never dies.
Here, the universe is not a stage but a void
Where every line of sight expands forever,
And the only rule is that there is no rule.
