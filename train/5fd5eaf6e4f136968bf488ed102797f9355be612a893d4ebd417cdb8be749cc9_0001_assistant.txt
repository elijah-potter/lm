Man 1 (1)/Man 2 (2)

1: What do you think the purpose of life is?
2: What do you mean?
1: I mean, why do we exist? Is there a purpose to life?
2: I'm not sure. I wonder about that from time to time.
1: Oh yeah? Like what?
2: Well, I wonder if there even is a purpose at all. Maybe there is no purpose, and we just live to exist.
1: That sounds pretty bleak to me.
2: Actually, it seems pretty comforting.
1: Please explain.
2: Imagine not having to worry about what path you walk in this life, what goals you need to achieve, or where you should go. You would simply be free to do what you want, without fear.
1: You can't just do anything you want. You can't go around killing people without repercussions.
2: That's true, but I wouldn't go around killing people. What I mean is that you're no longer under any pressure to perform or act a certain way when there is no purpose, and you can simply "be".
1: That sounds like Zen.
2: I guess it could be a Zen-like way of thinking.
1: I think there has to be a purpose.
2: Why do you think that?
1: There is too much organization and complexity in life that can only have been made by a creator, and that creator created us for some reason. It can be any reason like the creator wanting to observe its creation, or whatever. But if it wasn't for a purpose then our existence would be such a waste of potential. I mean, look at how much potential humans have and what they've done with it already. We've explored space, we've extended our lives, and we've become self-actualized in many other ways. I would hate to learn this would all be for nothing.
2: That's a great way of thinking about it.
