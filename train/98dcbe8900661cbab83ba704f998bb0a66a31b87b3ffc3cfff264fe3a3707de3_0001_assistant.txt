Maximilian the Magnificent looks down at the address scribbled on the card and looks at the quaint little coffee shop. He enters the shop and sees a kind young man with frazzled hair sitting at a table.

Maximilian: Are you Quinton?

Quinton: Yes, I am. Your friend already explained everything to me, and I want to propose a deal. You scratch my back, I scratch yours. I'll supply you with inventions that will elevate your performances to heights never before seen by man! You will become a household name: Maximilian the Magnificent!

Max: That’s just too good to be true. What could I possibly help you with?

Quinton: Why, you help me test my inventions! You'd be surprised by the number of people who have declined when I offered just because of the marginal chance of my inventions blowing up. You will be my lab rat and my marketer, all rolled up into one! So, what do you say, Max?

Max: You have yourself a deal!
