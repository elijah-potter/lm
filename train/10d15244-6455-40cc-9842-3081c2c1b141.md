Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy gray ears and a heart full of sunshine.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to play. He saw a tiny flower with pink petals swaying gently in the breeze. "Oh, what a beautiful flower!" Benny said, picking it up. But then he noticed something small and shiny near the flower. It was a tiny acorn.

Benny picked up the acorn and felt it soft and round. "This feels just like my favorite stuffed bunny!" he thought. He carried it home, where his mommy bunny was making a yummy treat.

"Mommy, can I help?" Benny asked, his eyes shining. She smiled and said, "Of course, dear! But first, let’s see what’s in this acorn."

Inside the acorn, Benny found a tiny, golden treasure—a magic seed! "Wow!" Benny said, excited. He planted the seed in his garden bed, where he had grown the most delicious carrots in the neighborhood.

Days passed, and Benny watered his seed carefully. Soon, a tiny sprout popped out of the ground. Benny watched it grow bigger and bigger. One morning, he woke up to find a tiny flower with pink petals just like the one he found outside!

"Look, Mommy!" Benny said happily. "My magic seed grew a flower just like the one I picked!"

His mommy bunny hugged him tight. "That’s the magic of kindness, Benny! Just like you took care of your seed, you helped someone else by being kind."

Benny smiled and knew he would keep being kind and happy every day. And that’s how Benny the bunny learned that kindness grows the best flowers of all.
