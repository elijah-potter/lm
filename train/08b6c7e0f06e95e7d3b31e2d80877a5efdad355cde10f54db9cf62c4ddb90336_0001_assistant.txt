In the noisy rooms of a brokerage firm, a huge slam the door on the door announces the arrival of the new boss. He is known as the Wolf of Wall Street. Some say it's because of his approach to doing business; others say it's because he is a real wolf. He arrives at the firm and immediately starts to howl to make his presence known. Nobody understands what is happening, so they all howl to show some respect, they think. After the big entrance, he goes to his office and slams the door and starts to make some calls. Everybody can see him through the window of his office, and everybody was eager to see him do his art. After some minutes of doing calls, the wolf's face starts to get red, his eyes blaze with fury, and he slams his paw into the table and says: "It's now or never. Do you want to get rich or not?" The angry voice made everybody confused as to what was happening, but nobody could take their eyes off the window. 

Five minutes later, he gets out of the room and everybody is looking at him. On his paws, he has a thick set of sheets and aggressively rips them apart and screams: "I made a deal. Let's get the party started." Everyone is baffled by what is happening, but they get up from their chairs and start to party. For the rest of the day, all workers of the firm were drinking, eating, dancing and making deals. From that moment, they were very happy to have The Wolf of Wall Street as their new boss.
