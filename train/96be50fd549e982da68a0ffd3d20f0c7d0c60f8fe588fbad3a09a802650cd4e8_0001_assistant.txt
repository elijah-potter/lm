I watched from my window as dark, angry storm clouds gathered in the sky. They looked like a legion of soldiers preparing for a great battle in the heavens.

Raindrops began to fall. They were gentle at first, tapping on the window. However, they grew increasingly intense as the minutes passed and soon deluges of water were cascading from the sky, beating onto the ground and hammering against my window ferociously.

The wind whistled through the trees at first, but then as it strengthened, it sounded more like a pack of hungry wolves. It started tearing and ripping at the leaves and branches, sending them spiraling through the air with unrelenting force. 

I gasped as I watched my neighbour's dustbin go flying down the road and disappear from my line of vision. I noticed a fence panel was missing - gone - just like that. 

Illuminated forks of lightning sliced the dark sky in half, with blinding flashes. They were as brilliant as full sunlight, everything was lit up for the world to see for a few moments. I had never seen such a display of terrible, awesome power.

Claps of thunder boomed. The storm was directly overhead. The noise was deafening. It reverberated through the air, making me shiver.

The storm raged on ferociously. I peeped out from behind the curtains of my bedroom window. I felt a mixture of awe at the power of nature, but also trepidation at its sheer untameable power.

After what seemed like an eternity, the storm finally began to calm down. The rain slowed to a gentle drizzle, the wind subsided, and the sound of thunder became just a low rumble in the distance. The storm was moving on, ready to demonstrate its power elsewhere.

I opened my window and leaned out, taking long, deep breaths of the crisp, clean air, and marveled at how the garden glistened with raindrops. The world looked refreshed.

The first rays of sun glinted from behind the clouds and the muted colours of a faint rainbow began to appear in the heavens.
