A flickering streetlamp cast long shadows across the cracked pavement as the hum of the generator filled the air. Inside, the half-empty diner’s neon sign buzzed weakly, its green glow barely piercing the gloom. The diner’s door creaked open, revealing a figure wrapped in a frayed raincoat, their breath visible in the cold night air.

The scent of stale coffee and burnt toast hung thick in the dimly lit interior. A lone server, slumped over a counter, adjusted a cracked mug of lukewarm coffee, its contents barely warm. The generator’s low rumble wavered, its roar suddenly stilled as a distant crackle of static filled the silence.

A child’s laughter echoed from the corner, followed by the sound of a door slamming shut. A figure in a hooded jacket stepped forward, their silhouette sharp against the dying glow of the neon. They held a battered briefcase, its contents hidden from view. The tension in the room thickened as the server’s eyes flicked toward the figure, then back to the floor.

A sudden burst of light illuminated the corner of the room, casting the figure in silhouette as they pulled out a device—a small, unmarked box. The server’s hand trembled as they reached for their phone, the screen glowing faintly in the dark. The generator’s roar returned, louder and more insistent, as if it had never stopped.

The figure moved toward the server, their voice low and urgent. *"We need to go. Now."* The child’s laughter cut off abruptly, replaced by the sound of running footsteps. The server swallowed hard, their fingers brushing the edge of the briefcase. The neon sign flickered once, then died. The diner plunged into darkness.
