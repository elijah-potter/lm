The neon glow of the abandoned subway tunnel pulsed faintly, casting eerie shadows across the cracked concrete walls. You shouldn’t be here—no one ever comes this far. The last maintenance crew left three months ago, and the city’s authorities declared it *off-limits*. Yet you were there, your boots sinking into the damp earth, your flashlight beam flickering over rusted metal barriers. The warning signs were still up, but the wind carried no sirens or distant alarms. Just the whisper of rust and the distant hum of the underground power lines.

Your breath fogged the lens of the device in your pocket, the one that tracked your location. The app showed you’d been here for over an hour. No one had responded to your emergency signal. The tunnel split ahead—one path led to the exit, the other to the deeper sections where the lights flickered intermittently. The one you took was colder. The air smelled like ozone and something metallic, like blood that had long since turned.

Then you heard it. A voice, not from the darkness, but from the metal grating overhead. Your pulse quickened. "You shouldn’t be here." The words were clear, though the speaker was nowhere in sight. The voice echoed, layered with static. "The last one who asked that was the one who didn’t come back."

The lights died. In the sudden dark, you counted six figures standing in a half-circle at the tunnel’s end, their faces illuminated by the sickly glow of a single, flickering bulb. They weren’t human. Their skin was too smooth, their movements too precise, like marionettes controlled by unseen strings. One of them turned, their hollow eyes locking onto you. The voice repeated: *"You shouldn’t be here."* The bulb dimmed. The tunnel stretched behind you, the exit swallowed by darkness. The others moved, their shadows stretching like tendrils toward you. You ran. The tunnel sloped down, and the world below became a blur of pressure and light. The last thing you saw before the darkness took you was the ceiling collapsing in on you.

You woke to the sound of sirens, your body battered, your clothes torn. The tunnel was gone. The signs were still up. But the app in your pocket buzzed. A new location was marked: **City Center Emergency Services**. And the message was simple: **"We found you. You should’ve stayed away."**
