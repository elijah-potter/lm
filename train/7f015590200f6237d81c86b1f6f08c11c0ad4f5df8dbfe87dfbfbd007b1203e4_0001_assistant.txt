Grenda was awoken from her nap by the sound of trumpets. She startled awake and leaped to her feet.  
"The royal prince of Dand has come to slay the ogre." Grenda rolled her eyes. Not this again.
"Hey, look, knights." She waved her hands to get their attention.
"The ogre awakes!" The prince stepped forward uneasily, drawing his sword.
"Look, I'm not after any trouble," Grenda said, looking down at the tiny human with laughter in her eyes.
"I've come to slay you and reclaim this bridge for Dand!" the prince said boldly.
"Really? I built this bridge! This bridge leads to a small island, where my family and I live. I have children, you know."
"You're an evil ogre who eats people!"
"I'm a vegetarian," Grenda insisted.
"A likely story. Prepare to meet your end." Grenda sighed at his insistence, but she had to admire his bravery.
"Look at your tiny sword. It's not going to do anything, ok? It's as if a mouse was attacking you with a nail file. Not exactly threatening."
"You've been warned!" The prince roared his battle cry and charged. Grenda leaned down and flicked him back across the bridge. She shook her head in frustration. What was with humans and their constant efforts to conquer absolutely everything?
