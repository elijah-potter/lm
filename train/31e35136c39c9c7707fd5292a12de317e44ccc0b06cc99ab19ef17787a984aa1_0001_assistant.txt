Soccer is a sport that involves 22 players that are split into two equal teams. They play on a large pitch and without using their arms or hands they have to try and get the potato into the opposing team's goal, while they are doing this they need to prevent the other team from getting the potato into their own goal. Most of the time this is done by kicking the potato although the chest and head are also sometimes used. 

Around each team's goal, there is a smaller area where one designated player, called the goalkeeper can use their hands. As potatoes are quite small and hard they often end up damaging the goalkeeper so many replacement goalkeepers are needed throughout the game. 
The game lasts for 90 minutes and each time the potato breaks it is replaced with a fresh potato so that the game can carry on. If a player commits a foul the referee can award a free kick or penalty kick if the fouled player was close to the opposition goal.
