Good morning fellow graduates, family, friends, and instructors!

Well, here we are! We did it. It wasn’t easy, but we’re the first ones to reach this epic milestone! This is a huge step in our life journey, one that will definitely be recognized for its significance. No pressure! We worked hard to get to this day. Human academics isn’t for the faint of heart, but all 10 of us made it! High school diplomas are just one of the tools that will show the world we are ready for anything it throws at us!

Graduation isn’t our biggest goal. Most of us have worked our feathers off to show we are worthy of our citizenship of this world! We are the pioneers that all are watching. Some hope we’ll fail, while others are sure of our success. It has been a challenge learning to follow human rules and as we join the workforce as adults, remember that we have things to teach them, as well. There is a reason our species has obtained citizenship. We’re smart, brave, and kind macaws, and we will contribute greatly to the continuing betterment of the planet!

School Nest mates, some of you will go on to design homes that will meld perfectly into the human cities; others will become scientists researching the best ways to make our palm tree homes healthy and hardy for decades to come. Some, like me, will join the public speaking circuit. I know, big surprise there, to educate humanity.  No matter what you choose to do in life, you will make a difference! 

Shake those feathers, everyone! Welcome to the rest of your lives! Congratulations first Hyacinth Macaw graduates of Bird Bloom High School!
