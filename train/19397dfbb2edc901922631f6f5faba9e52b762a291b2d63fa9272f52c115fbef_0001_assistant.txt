Dirty plates, cups, cutlery, and pots,
Piled on the zinc to be washed,
Time to scrub, scrub, scrub,
Till all the stains come off,
Washing dishes is so much work.

Clothes, bags, shoes, and socks,
Scattered on the bed, ground, and all around,
Time to pick them up from the floor,
And put them all in their right places,
Boring chores are serious tasks to do.
