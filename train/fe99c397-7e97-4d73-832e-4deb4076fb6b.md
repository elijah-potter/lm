Two scientists argue over lab notes while their high-tech equipment glitches mid-experiment.
One claims their data aligns with quantum entanglement’s predictions; the other insists their corrections show it’s statistical noise.
A single flicker of the monitor displays an anomaly—both theories seem correct, yet the data defies either explanation. The air hums with suppressed static as they stare at the same screen, faces etched with frustration and revelation.

A scientist’s hand hovers over the keyboard as the screen flickers:
`ERROR: CONFLICTING PREDICTIONS. RECONCILIATION REQUIRED.`

The door creaks open. A third researcher steps in, holding a half-finished theory: *"Maybe they’re both right."* The room freezes. The machines pause. The argument dissolves into a single, urgent question: *How do you choose?*
