The clock tower stood silent, its hands frozen at three. Inside, Elias traced the same path on the floor—down the grand staircase, past the polished marble, until he reached the grand hall’s entrance. The door was open, just as it had been when he left hours ago, the same wayward breeze carrying the scent of rain and old wood. His reflection in the cracked glass mirrored the same exhaustion, the same unspoken question: *Why did I come back?* Outside, the sky had darkened, and the first drops of rain began to fall in slow, steady lines. The tower’s bells would chime soon.
