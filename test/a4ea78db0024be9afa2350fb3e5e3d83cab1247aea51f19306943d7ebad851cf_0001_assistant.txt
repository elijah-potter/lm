1. Flourishing Flowers: Flourishing gives a sense that the business is thriving and that the flowers your friend uses are healthy and strong.
2. Beautiful Bouquets: This tells potential customers that your friend's bouquets are attractive. 
3. Baby Bloom: A play on words of the phrase 'Baby Boom', this name is lighthearted and playful.
4. Blooming Brilliant: Blooming evokes a feeling of strength and also implies the business is thriving. It is also a play on words as flowers 'bloom' when they open.
5. Ravishing Roses: Roses are one of the most popular flowers, and 'ravishing' implies the flowers are beautiful to look at.
