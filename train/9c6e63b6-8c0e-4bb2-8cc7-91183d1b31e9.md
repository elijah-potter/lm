### **The List**
1. **Every Thursday**, the old clock tower at the market square chimed midnight.
2. **The first name** on my list was *Mira*, the baker’s daughter with hands like porcelain.
3. **The second** was *Elias*, the blacksmith’s apprentice who had once stolen a silver locket from my father’s chest.
4. **The third** was *Lena*—the girl who laughed too loudly, who knew too much about the cracks in the floorboards.
5. **The fourth** was *me*.

---

### **The Confession**
I wrote the names in blood on the back of my mother’s wedding ring. She never found it. She only found the **empty chair** at dinner and the **unlit candle** in the window.

I confessed on the night the first name called out:
*"You gave her the key to the cellar. You told her the locket was a gift. You let her see the names you carved in your own skin."*

The list was a debt. The confession was a knife. And I was the one who held the match.
