Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Pip who loved to hop around and explore. One sunny morning, Pip met a tiny bird named Birdie who had a bright red feather in her wing.

"Pip, I think I saw a shiny, sparkly rock in the grass!" Birdie chirped excitedly.

Pip’s eyes sparkled. "Let’s go find it together!"

They hopped and chirped, searching the garden. Near the old tree, they found a smooth, shiny stone that glowed softly. It was a magic rock!

"Look! It makes everything bright and happy!" Birdie said.

Pip and Birdie clapped their paws and wings. Suddenly, the rock turned into a big, colorful flower that bloomed with laughter. Pip and Birdie danced happily, and the flower grew even bigger, filling the garden with sweet, sweet flowers that smelled like sunshine.

As the sun set, the flower faded, but Pip and Birdie knew they’d found the most wonderful magic rock in the world. They hugged each other and promised to keep exploring together, forever and ever.

And that is how Pip the bunny and Birdie the bird made their garden sparkle with joy!
