**The Confession List**

She kept a list in her desk drawer—one where only she would ever find it:
- **1. The night she left her phone at the bar** (it had been a mistake, but she’d sworn she’d return it to herself).
- **2. The secret she shared with a stranger** (their names were never spoken, but the words lingered like smoke).
- **3. The scar on her wrist** (a present from someone who had never meant to give it).
- **4. The address of the place she couldn’t go back to** (the city where she burned her old life).
- **5. The truth about her job** (they called her "helpful," but she was really a ghost in their story).

She stared at it now, the paper brittle with age, and wondered if anyone else would ever read it.
