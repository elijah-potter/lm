A man had several sons who were always fighting with one another. When his pleas for them to stop didn't work, he decided to show them why their conflict was so problematic. So one day he told them to bring him a bundle of sticks.

After they did, he placed the bundle into each of their hands, one after another, and ordered them to break the bundle into pieces. They tried very hard but couldn't do it.

He then opened the bundle and placed individual sticks into each of his son's hands. His sons broke them easily.

Then he told his sons, "If you are like-minded, unite, and assist each other, you will be like this bundle, strong against your enemies. But if you are divided, you will each break as easily as these sticks."
