A sudden gust of wind sent a glass vase crashing to the floor, shattering into jagged pieces. The startled figure—an elderly woman in a cozy sweater—twisted around, her eyes wide with shock as a shadow loomed over her. The stranger’s voice, sharp and commanding, cut through the quiet: *"You’re under arrest for theft."* The woman stumbled backward, clutching her trembling hands, unaware the stolen jewelry from her purse was the very reason she’d been summoned. The officer’s flashlight illuminated her confusion as she realized, too late, she’d been caught.
