Step 1
I think you're supposed to preheat your oven to 300 degrees or something like that. I say, don't bother with this preheating stuff and just turn the oven on just before you put your cookies in.

Step 2
Now, I think you're supposed to mix your dry ingredients together first - some flour, maybe a couple of cups of it, then sprinkle in some baking soda and salt and mix it all together. Then toss in a couple of sticks of butter and maybe a handful or two of brown sugar. And I think you're supposed to put in some white sugar too. And maybe you're supposed to put those in a separate bowl? I forget. Anyway, pour in some vanilla, toss in a couple of eggs and mix everything together. Then pour in as many chocolate chips and nuts as you want. Then you want to do is to plop spoonfuls of the dough onto a baking sheet.

Step 3
I think you're supposed to bake them for around 10 minutes or so. If they change color and get darker, they're probably done - at least well enough. I say eat 'um while they're hot right out of the oven, but you might want to wait a couple of minutes first, then put them on a wire rack to cool some.
