The name Austin has existed in
Ireland since the fourteenth century
and is usually of Anglo origin.
Oistin and Mac Aibhistin are the
Gaelic forms of this name that can
be found throughout the four
Provinces of the country. This name
was further introduced into Ulster
during the seventeenth century.
