The dark can seem strange and deep,
Throughout this night, watch I keep.
Fear not this dark, it will do me no harm,
and it keeps me safe from a buzzing alarm.

Dark is just light taking a rest,
Earth preparing nature's nest.
This dark has many secrets to share,
I shall listen with utmost care.

Majestic owls soar all around,
fireflies light up without making a sound.
Dawn will wake a sleeping town,
Sounds of light will soon abound.

Dreams and stars, shining as one,
Beneath the glow of moon and sun.
Cosmic journeys have just begun,
The duality of beauty leaves me stunned.
