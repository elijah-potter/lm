Once a remarkable cat had a dream. His raven fur would almost sparkle in the sun as he stared at the popcorn dancing in the theater’s popcorn machine. The sound! It captivated his emerald eyes, and as the moon rose that night, he went to work creating his own corn-popping stand.

After finding an old apron and tying it with expertise, he put his paws to diligent work, and by the next curtain fall his feline treat was taking off! So many people gathered around to marvel at the unique flavor of the cat's delicious popcorn.

Soon word had spread, and theatergoers formed lines to sample the cat's magnificent invention. The fluffy, salty clouds were a fulfillment of a fanatic’s dream. And thus, the enticing popcorn made the raven cat an undisputed ruler of the theater concession!
