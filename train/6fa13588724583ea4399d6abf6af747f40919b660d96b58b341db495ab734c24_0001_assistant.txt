Robbie stuck close to his mother, barely letting go of her dress as the two were given a tour of his new daycare. Mommy said she would be starting her new job soon, and Robbie would have to stay at daycare because he was a big boy now.
But Robbie didn’t feel like a big boy at all. He didn’t like the idea of spending the entire day without Mommy, although the lady who was showing them around seemed quite nice, and the daycare itself lacked nothing. The playroom was colorful, with vibrant murals decorating the walls. There were all sorts of games and toys for Robbie to play with. There was a television set, and the children were allowed to watch a movie on it every day. There was a small kitchen where their meals were heated, and near it, a sink with a step ladder so even Robbie could wash his hands on his own.
“Well, Robbie?” Mommy asked when the tour was over. “Do you see yourself having fun here while I’m at work?”
“I don’t know, Mommy. Won’t I get awfully lonely?”
“Oh, you won’t feel lonely at all,” said the nice daycare lady, “once you meet all your friends here. Would you like to play with them?”
“Yes, please,” said Robbie, and he was taken outside to the garden, where he found some kids his age engrossed in a game of tag. He looked at Mommy for reassurance and then went over to them to join their game.
Robbie ended up having so much fun he didn’t even realize Mommy had left for work! He had fun playing with his new friends, having lunch together, and taking a nap. When he saw Mommy, it was already evening time!
“Well, Robbie? What do you think of the daycare?”
“I think,” Robbie said slowly with a smile, “I’m going to have the best days ever here!”
