3. Greasing systemThe greasing system consists of the oil pallet, the oil pump, the oil filter and various channels. The oil pump is put in action from a cranked shaft. Перепускной the valve in the oil pump supports pressure in system of greasing of the engine at certain level, At its excess opens перепускной the valve and an oil part flows down back in the oil pallet. In the filter is available перепускной the valve through which in case of strong pollution of the filter crude oil arrives at once in an oil highway.
The oil cleared by the oil filter arrives in the main oil highway. Further the oil stream is divided into two streams. One stream of oil moves to radical bearings of a cranked shaft, and through the drilled channels greases and шатунные bearings. For cooling of pistons oil through sprays is sprayed on the bottom part of pistons.
The second stream of oil on channels moves to a head of the block of cylinders for greasing of bearings of camshafts and in hydraulic pushers.
After passage through a head and the block of cylinders, oil flows down in the oil pallet.
