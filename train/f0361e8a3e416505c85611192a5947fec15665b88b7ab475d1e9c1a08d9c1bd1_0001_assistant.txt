Some people are thin, some are a bit bigger,
While others are short, and others are tall.
It makes life more interesting, I figure
Imagine if there was no difference at all!
Just imagine how boring society
Would be if we were all the same;
There would be zero variety
We might even have to share the same name.
So remember, my young nephew,
Don't listen to what they say
Even if they pick on you.
Everyone's special in their way.
