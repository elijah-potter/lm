Our project is a sample study to ascertain and to supply the deficiency of our education system. We would like to draw people’s attention to the teaching of the concept of “rights” because we think this should be underlined in our education system. Nowadays there is so much emphasis on the necessity of the preschool education, but we do not teach “rights” at a very early age. As a solution to this problem we consider the importance of the teacher’s role in initiating group works and motivation activities. We have made questionnaires with families and supported our studies with face to face dialogues with them. We also arranged visual studies such as project assignments and bulletin board studies.
