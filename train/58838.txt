Level D - Diverse vowel sounds
Here you will find our selection of printable second grade books.
In level D we have printable books that will teach your child about the third sound of the letters a, o and u.
The sound of the third a can be found in words such as: Ball, Farm and Pawn.
The sound of the third o and u can be found in words such as: Shoe, Bull and Push.
There is no Dolch list for grade 2, but we will continue to regularly use sight words in these books.
Your child should not have much trouble with any of the sight words on the Dolch list to minimize frustration and focus on the new challenge.
Third vowel sounds can be spelled in many different ways. Words with third vowel sounds are easier to study when they are taught collectively. These books will help your child to understand and see how the third sounds of the letters a, o and u appear in words and how to pronounce them in a correct way.
