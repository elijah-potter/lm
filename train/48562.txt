How much is too much?
What age do people typically retire in your country? Are you preparing for your retirement? Is saving money more important than spending money? The full Discussion Starters lesson on Retirement Savings contains warm up questions, pre-reading vocabulary, a short reading, vocabulary review activities, comprehension questions and discussion questions. We hope this lesson on saving and aging inspires a useful discussion in your classroom. This lesson may even inspire some of your students to open up a retirement savings account.
