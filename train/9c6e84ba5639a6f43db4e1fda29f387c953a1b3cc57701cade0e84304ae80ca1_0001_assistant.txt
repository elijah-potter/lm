No problem! Here are some other books you might like:

- Lord of the Flies by William Golding: In this book, a group of schoolboys get stranded on an island and have to survive. It dives deep into human nature and presents the problems of a lack of structure. 
- Wild Life by Molly Gloss: This book involves the search for a girl that went missing from a logging camp. Much more than that ends up being found in this incredible setting with memorable characters. 
- Hollen the Soulless by Denali Day: this book has heroes who live on mountaintops, one civilization that tries to protect others, and a man who will do anything for his love. 
- A Good Man in Africa by William Boyd: This book is about a clueless British diplomat who goes to Africa and the many bumbling mistakes he makes and the things he observes as he learns about it. 
-The Flame Trees of Thika by Elspeth Huxley: This book is written from the point of view of a child whose parents move to Africa and have to learn from the locals how to start a plantation. 

I hope you have a nice time reading these!
