**The Last Transmission**

It wasn’t supposed to happen like this.

The hum of the server’s cooling fans was the only sound in the data center’s sterile silence—until the power flickered. Static crackled across the air, then died. The lights dimmed to a sickly glow, and for a heartbeat, the world held its breath.

Her fingers trembled as she typed the final command.

*"Run the final scrub."*

The machine answered with a single, cold laugh before the screen went black.

Outside, the rain came down in sheets, erasing the faint glow of the city’s neon signs. Somewhere, a clock struck twelve.

She pulled her phone from her pocket—just one more check.

No signal.

The last thing she remembered was the screen flickering to life again, just for a second, before the static swallowed everything whole.

And then there was nothing.

Just the silence.

Waiting.
