Superman: Black Widow is also a spider, right? You Marvel people are so unoriginal. Just spiders.
Iron Man: And look at you, Superman, in your tights and underwear.
Wonder Woman: Iron Man! Anybody can be Iron Man. Just put on the suit and you can be a superhero.
Black Widow: Shut up, Wonder Woman. Weren't you made from clay?
Batman: Black Widow. Don't trust her. She works for the Russian KGB.
Spider-Man: Batman…ooh, you are so dark and depressing. Go get some therapy man.

(At this point, the Superheroes are in each other faces, ready to fight. Then a woman comes in.)

Woman: We are letting the fans in! Let the convention for fans of superheroes begin!

(The superheroes back down from one another. They regain their composure and start to smile. A little girl comes up to them.)

Little girl: Can I take a picture with my favorite Superheroes, Batman and Iron Man?
Iron Man: Sure.
Batman: OK, kid.

(Both Batman and Iron Man act friendly toward one another as they pose with the girl in her selfie)

Little girl: Why haven't you two been in a movie together? That would be fantastic.
Batman: I don't know kid.
Iron Man: Good idea there. Right, Bat Man?

(A woman dressed as Super Girl goes up to the female Superheroes, Wonder Woman and Black Widow.)

Woman dressed as Supergirl: We women must stick together, like you two.

(They both laugh)

Wonder Woman: Yeah! I love Black Widow.

(Wonder Woman approaches Black Widow and hugs her. Then Black Widow kisses Wonder Woman lightly on the cheek.)

Black Widow: I am the biggest Wonder Woman fan. No Man's Land. Girl power. Let's go, ladies!
