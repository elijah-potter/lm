About this course
This course aims to enhance the capacities of users and producers of statistics in general and, more specifically, gender statistics. It will offer training in monitoring progress towards gender equality and the empowerment of women through improved programmes and policies, as well as further understanding of gender issues. Participants will learn how to link gender issues to gender indicators; calculate gender indicators; and visualize and present indicators in order to accurately measure progress achieved.
This course is part of a learning path leading to a professional certificate in gender.
- Employees in national statistical offices;
- Non-governmental organization workers;
- Researchers in national and international agencies.
