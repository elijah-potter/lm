Lesson 20: Read aloud
A: I think we should decorate this basement.
B: Really? What do you want to use it for?
A: Well. I was thinking about turning it into an artist’s studio.
B: That’s a nice idea. Are you thinking of painting again?
A: Yeah, and there’s all this room down here.
B: Now that we’re down here, do you think we need a new water heater?
A: Yeah, I wouldn’t want it to burst after we’ve redone it.
B: Alright. I’ll look into that first.
A: Great and I’ll start thinking about the design of the studio. I think I could teach classes down here too.
B: That’s a nice idea.
A: Yeah. I was thinking about giving our classes to the neighborhood kids.
B: I’m sure they’d love that!
