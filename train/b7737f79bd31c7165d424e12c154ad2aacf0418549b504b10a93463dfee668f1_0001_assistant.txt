That sounds like a groovy party. I am confident you will dig the far-out recipes below:

1. Braised Dill Potatoes - dill, vegetable broth, and a few other simple ingredients are used to prepare this dish. Sour cream makes these braised potatoes taste great.
2. Herb-Roasted Olives & Tomatoes - enjoy these broiled veggies with crunchy sliced bread or two or three cheeses. Doubling or tripling the recipe and serving it with spaghetti is another yummy option.
3. Cinnamon-Spiced Bananas - this treat, which is prepared quickly in the microwave, will delight everyone's tastebuds. In addition, it is also a tasty way to flavor bananas and includes more fruit in your diet.
4. Kentucky Butter Cake - a cake that's comforting and delicious! The butter sauce elevates the cake to a whole new level and the cake's crumb is perfect.
5. Mushroom & Leek Strudel - it's almost effortless to make these elegant appetizers. Fresh herbs work best in this recipe. 
6. Cucumber-Stuffed Cherry Tomatoes - these little tomato poppers are great because you can make them ahead of time, in addition to being delicious. Double or triple the recipe if bringing it to a party!
7. Cranberry Waldorf Salad - you can use this salad as a dessert! So refreshing and light!
