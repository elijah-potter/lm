A sudden **blackout** engulfs the room—windows dark as void, the hum of fluorescent tubes cut short. A single flickering bulb, low in a corner, casts jagged shadows. The air shimmers with static electricity, a whisper of a crackle before the power returns.

Then—**white-hot light** floods the space. The bulb explodes in a shower of sparks, but the emergency strobes ignite, a brutal, neon glare piercing the eyes. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, twisted into grotesque shapes. The sudden brightness feels like a punch to the face—squinting, the scene blurs for a moment before the light stabilizes, though the edges remain too bright, too *alive*. The room is now a fractured, half-lit warzone of reflections and distorted forms.
