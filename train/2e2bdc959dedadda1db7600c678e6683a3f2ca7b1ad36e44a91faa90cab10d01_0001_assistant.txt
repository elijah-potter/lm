1. The Banker's Money - The millions of dollars the banker has are a symbol of the value of 15 years of his life that he will spend imprisoned with no human interaction. It also symbolizes the lawyer's greed by being willing to sacrifice these years either to prove his point, or for the money. 

2. The Books - The books are read during the lawyer's imprisonment as a replacement for human interaction and stimulation from the outside world. They symbolize his world and his realm of experience. 

3. The Gospel - The Gospel symbolizes the importance of God in the lawyer's quest for enlightenment. While in other years he reads up to 600 books of various types, one year he reads nothing but the Gospel.

4. The Piano - The piano symbolizes the lawyer's changing mindset as he turns inward during his solitude. Although he plays the piano often during his first year of captivity, he then leaves it silent.
