The team meeting starts at 3 PM, and I’m already mentally checking out. A presentation, a decision, a meeting that feels like it could last a lifetime. By 5 PM, I’m exhausted but wired, my brain buzzing like a fly in a jar.

Then, just as the sun sets, there’s a knock at the door. A neighbor hands me a bag of fresh bread—warm, crusty, the scent of something alive. I take a bite. For a moment, it’s enough.

But the clock still ticks. Tomorrow is another day.
