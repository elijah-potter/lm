**Mayor’s Office Statement: City of Emberton Declares State of Emergency Amid Mysterious Energy Fluctuations**
Residents of Emberton are advised to remain indoors after reports of sudden, unexplained power surges and grid disruptions across the central district. Officials attribute the anomalies to an unknown phenomenon affecting the city’s underground energy conduit network, which has led to sporadic blackouts and flickering lights. Authorities have activated emergency protocols to stabilize infrastructure, though exact causes remain under investigation. Public transportation has been temporarily suspended in affected zones, and emergency crews are deploying to address potential safety hazards. City leaders urge residents to avoid unnecessary travel and report further incidents via the city’s non-emergency hotline.

**Update: First Fatality Reported**
A 34-year-old resident was found unresponsive in their apartment after a prolonged outage earlier this evening. Medical teams are treating the incident as an emergency, while Emberton’s Energy Commission conducts preliminary checks on the conduit’s integrity. No direct link to the ongoing power disturbances has been confirmed, but officials caution against dismissing the event as unrelated.
