Electric heaters work by sending electricity through a heating element inside of the heater. The heating element is made with some type of electrically resistant material and that resistance causes the element’s temperature to rise and begin emitting heat. This heat is typically transferred to the heater’s core before sending it to the casing of the heater which holds onto that heat and transfers it to its surroundings. Electric heaters are a great addition to your home because they are highly energy efficient compared to other popular electric heating methods. Many of them are also programmable which allows you to keep the temperature of your home at the right temperature at all times of the day.
