SpaceX is a company founded by Elon Musk in 2002 that creates and launches spacecraft and rockets. Their goal is to create a multi-planetary life for civilization. It is the first private company to have a spacecraft successfully return from low-Earth orbit, which they achieved in 2010. In 2012, they also had a successful mission in which their Dragon spacecraft attached to the international space station, swapped cargo, and returned to Earth safely.
