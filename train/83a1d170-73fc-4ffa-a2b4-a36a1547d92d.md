The night is darker than a void, yet filled with the flicker of neon, a million tiny stars trapped in bottles. The river below is a serpent of water, winding through the city like a vein of life, its banks lined with markets where the scent of spices curls like smoke from the sky. The noise is a symphony of sirens, horns, and laughter—too loud to be quiet, too chaotic to be calm. It’s alive, breathing, and endlessly changing, just like the city itself.
