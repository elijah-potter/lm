You can find pizza at these restaurants:
1. Via Napoli: They serve pizza with authentic ingredients and pizzas with crispy crusts that were baked in a wood-fired pizza oven. 
2. Blaze Fast Fire'd Pizza: This franchise is known for its thin-crust, customizable pizzas. 
3. Pizza Ponte: They serve giant servings of single-slice pizzas.
4. Pizzafari: They have a variety of different flatbread-style pizzas.
