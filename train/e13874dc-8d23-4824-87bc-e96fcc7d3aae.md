## Lost Amongst the Lilacs (Portland, OR)

The Saturday Market was bustling, a vibrant swirl of people and handcrafted goods. I was admiring the pottery near the fountain, completely absorbed in the glaze on a small ceramic bird, when you stopped beside me. You were sketching in a worn leather-bound notebook, your brow furrowed in concentration, and the sunlight caught the auburn highlights in your hair. We didn't speak, but I noticed the delicate silver ring on your left hand and the way you occasionally chewed on the end of your pencil. 

There was a faint, comforting aroma of sandalwood emanating from your clothing, a scent that lingered even after you moved on. The vibrant purple of the lilacs blooming nearby seemed to intensify the moment you turned away, and I found myself unconsciously following your progress through the crowd for a few precious moments. I realize this is a long shot, but I’m hoping you might remember a woman captivated by your artistry.

If you were sketching near the fountain at the Saturday Market, please reply with the color of your notebook.
