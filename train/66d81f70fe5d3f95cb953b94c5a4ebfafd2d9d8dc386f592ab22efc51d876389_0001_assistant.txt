- Queen Victoria reigned over the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Ireland from June 20th, 1837 until her death on January 22nd, 1901. 
- She was given the title of Empress of India on May 1st, 1876 and kept this position until her death on January 22nd, 1901.
- Queen Victoria remained on the throne for 63 years and 216 days, making her reign longer than anyone who previously sat on the throne. 
- This period was known as the Victorian era and was a period of expansion for the British Empire.
