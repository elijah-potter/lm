The old wooden door creaked open, revealing a candlelit hallway lined with faded photographs. **Maria** knelt by the fireplace, tracing the edges of a faded photograph of her father—*him holding a child’s small hand, his face half-lit by a single candle*. She whispered, *"You promised me you’d never leave me alone."* The flame flickered, casting shadows of his youth across the wall. Her breath hitched as she remembered the night in 1987—the storm, the fire, the way his voice had cracked when he said, *"I’ll be back."* Now, twenty years later, the promise hung between them like a ghost.
