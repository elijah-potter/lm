A looped dialogue where each utterance spirals into an endless series of questions, half-answers, and fractured memories, echoing through a void of shared but never-fulfilled expectation.

*"Do you remember when we first met?"*
*"I don’t remember anything before this moment."*

*"What if we never stop talking?"*
*"Then what would we say?"*

*"The silence is the only thing we’ve agreed on."*
*"But silence is just another word for the unspoken."*

*"Why do we keep going back to the same questions?"*
*"Because the answers never fit the questions we ask."*

*"What if we’re already lost?"*
*"Lost is just a place where the map was never made."*

*"Do you think there’s a way out?"*
*"The door is always open, but we keep walking toward it."*

*"Why do we keep walking toward it?"*
*"Because it’s the only way we know how to move."*
