There are plenty of options that your family might enjoy. Here are a few of them:
- Minecraft: There are many ways your family can play together on this platform, including minigames, building competitions, and quests. 
- Fortnite: Battle with and/or against your family members with this game. 
- Family Fun Pack: Conquest Edition: This isn't a game so much as it is a collection of classic games like Scrabble and Battleship that your family will be able to play on your devices so you can save your table for food and other things. 
- Mario Kart: Almost all the Mario Kart games can be played against other members of your family. Have fun racing each other to the finish line.
