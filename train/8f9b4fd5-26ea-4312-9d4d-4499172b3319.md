The sharp *clang* of a glass shattering echoed through the dimly lit kitchen as the chef’s knife flew across the counter, missing the bowl by inches. "Enough!" Daniel snapped, his voice cracking with frustration. "You keep saying *‘maybe’* when we need *‘yes’*—"

Sarah’s eyes burned with tears as she wiped her palms on her apron. "I *tried* to explain—"

A child’s giggle cut through the tension, followed by the muffled *"Mama!"* from the living room. "Lily!" Daniel barked, already scooping her into his arms. "We’re *short* on time—"

Sarah’s phone buzzed—another missed call from the bakery’s manager. She exhaled sharply. "We’ll make it tomorrow. *Tomorrow*." The kitchen fell silent, broken. Outside, the rain hammered against the window.
