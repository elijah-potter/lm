Peter Bradshaw's 2017 review of "Guardians of the Galaxy Vol 2" is a mixed review between positives and negatives. Although he writes positively of several things including: the soundtrack, the movie's sense of humor and its overall "quirky"-ness; he fails to find a deeper meaning in the story as a whole calling it "pinballing pointlessness".  While Bradshaw specifically calls out certain artists for their humorous (as in the case of Dave Bautista as Drax) or "amusingly" haughty (Elizabeth Debicki as Ayesha) roles he is not a fan of Kurt Russell's character Ego who he calls "unsettling".  Ultimately, although Bradshaw seems to enjoy many parts of the story and references specific music from the movie ("Brandy" by Looking Glass) and amusing scenarios between characters, he ultimately does not like the "pinball" back and forth of the background story of the main character Quill, and did not feel like the movie, although an action movie, showed that the galaxy the Guardians were supposed to be "guarding" seemed to be in much "danger".
