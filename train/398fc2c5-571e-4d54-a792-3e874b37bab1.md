The habit of compulsively checking phone notifications at the start of every workday, initially introduced as a way to stay updated, evolved into an unhealthy dependency. Over time, the individual found themselves mentally preparing for each notification, often leading to increased stress and reduced focus. The cycle became automatic—checking, reacting, and then spiraling into anxiety if no updates were received. Eventually, the habit consumed more time than intended, disrupting productivity and personal well-being. The shift from intentional engagement to reactive urgency highlighted how habits, once beneficial, can morph into self-sabotaging patterns.
