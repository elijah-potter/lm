Once there was a little bunny named Pip who loved to hop and play. One sunny morning, Pip saw a tiny acorn roll away from a big oak tree. "Let’s find it!" Pip said, and hopped after it.

Pip climbed up the tree to find the acorn but slipped and fell. "Oh no," Pip cried. Just then, a kind old owl named Owlbert landed on a branch. "Don’t worry, Pip!" Owlbert hooted. "Let’s help each other!"

They worked together—Pip used his tiny paws to gather leaves, and Owlbert used his big wings to carry them up. Soon, they built a cozy nest with soft grass and twigs. Pip put the acorn inside, and Owlbert wrapped it in a soft feather blanket.

Now, Pip and Owlbert had the best snack ever! Pip nibbled the acorn, and Owlbert sipped from a tiny cup of milk from a teapot he found. They laughed and played until the sun began to set.

As the sky turned pink, Pip curled up in his new nest, and Owlbert fluffed his feathers. "Best day ever!" Pip whispered. Owlbert nodded. "Best day ever!" and they drifted off to sleep, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures.

The end.
