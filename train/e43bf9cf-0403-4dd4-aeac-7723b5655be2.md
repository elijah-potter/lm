The **Bureaucratic Gray Zone** – an administrative limbo where compliance documents exist but enforcement is inconsistent. Officials cite "policy ambiguity" to avoid penalties, while citizens document disputes with varying interpretations of "due process." Penalties are applied retroactively if caught, leaving records of "non-compliance" with conflicting explanations. The system operates on trust, not rules—contracts are negotiated in whispers, and fines are paid in cash or favors. Citizens with connections report fewer "accidents" or "mistakes," while those without rely on anecdotes from former colleagues. The "rules" are a living, shifting text, rewritten based on who is watching.
