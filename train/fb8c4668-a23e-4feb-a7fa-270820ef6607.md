The **Zones of Uncertainty Protocol** (ZUP) in the **Ephemeral Archives**—a floating district between the **Dry Mesa** and **Celestial Spire**—operates under the principle of *"question is law."* Officials in the **Judicial Pylon** enforce a rotating set of rules that are redefined every lunar cycle by the **Council of Shifting Statutes**. Common disputes revolve around:
- **The "Ghost Path"**: A narrow alley where shadows cast by streetlamps create ambiguous right-of-way. Courts interpret this as "where the light does not reach, the path belongs to no one," leading to spontaneous "shadow rights" agreements.
- **The **Temporal Marketplace**, where time is both currency and commodity. A "delayed loan" can be redeemed at any hour, but the clock’s "inaccuracy dial" (a dialed-down analog) dictates interest rates—making contracts legally binding *only if the debtor believes the clock is correct*.
- **The **Echoing Fountain**, where water reflects past events. Speakers who argue their claims based on the reflections may claim their words were "recorded by history itself," though no official logs exist.

Local law enforcement, the **Hollow Enforcers**, operates on the principle of *"question is better than answer."* They do not resolve disputes but instead **ask clarifying questions** until the parties’ own words create consensus. Recidivists are issued **"Incomplete Statements"**—certificates that can be redeemed for a second chance if the original claim is restated with additional context.

The **Unwritten Law**: *"You are not allowed to ask about the rules."* Violators are often found in the **Quiet Chamber**, a room where the air hums with static, and the walls echo the last question asked in the district.
