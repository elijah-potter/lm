Abandoned project proposal titled *"Urban Eco-Trail Network"* was halted due to:
1. **Funding cuts**—grants from the city’s sustainability division were reallocated to emergency infrastructure.
2. **Public resistance**—local concerns over invasive species and habitat disruption outweighed support for the trail’s environmental benefits.
3. **Regulatory delays**—permits for land acquisition and wildlife corridors took longer than anticipated.
4. **Competing priorities**—other urban development projects (e.g., a new park, pedestrian bridge) were deemed more urgent by the council.

**Key stakeholders** (environmental groups, city planners, local businesses) expressed frustration over bureaucratic bottlenecks. Proposed next steps (e.g., revised design, phased implementation) were deemed too costly and risky without immediate results. The team’s report was tabled for a later review.
