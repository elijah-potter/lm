Lolong, a saltwater crocodile that was captured in the Philippines in 2011, holds the record for being the largest crocodile in captivity. He measured 20 feet and 3 inches in length and weighed 2,370 pounds. Estimated to be around 50 years old, Lolong was captured on September 3, 2011, in Bunawan Creek, located in the province of Agusan del Sur in the Philippines. The capture occurred due to suspicions of his involvement in the deaths of a fisherman and a 12-year-old girl. In June 2012, Lolong was officially recognized by the Guinness Book of World Records as the "world's largest crocodile in captivity," with a length of 6.17 meters (20 feet 3 inches).
