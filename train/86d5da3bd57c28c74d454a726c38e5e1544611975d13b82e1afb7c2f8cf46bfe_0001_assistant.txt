Tab refused the urge to check his badge was polished one last time as the general strode into The Hall. Tab's blue jacket stretched at his shoulders as he brought them back. His hands itched in his white gloves. The general strode in with relaxed ease. His heavy boots stopped in front of Tab. The general only nodded, his waxed mustache not bobbing the slightest. Tab nodded back, all too aware of the way his too-large hat fell a bit over his brow. Tab turned, boots squeaking, and tried to not hurry as he left the room — his first mission as a soldier was about to begin.
