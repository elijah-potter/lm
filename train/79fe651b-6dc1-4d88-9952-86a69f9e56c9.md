**Fragment from a lost dialogue, reconstructed from fragmented notes:**

---
**Time:** 11:47 AM, rain tapping against the café’s window
**Location:** The half-empty booth where the café owner—*Mira*—sat with a half-eaten *sandwich of spiced almonds* and a cup of bitter tea.

---
**Mira:** *"You ever think about how the world’s just a series of echoes, the ones we remember and the ones we forget?"*

**Alex:** *"Like a library where only the dusty old books survive?"*

**Mira:** *"No. Like a network. You drop a stone in the pond—ripples expand, then fade. But the stone’s still there, waiting for the next drop."*

---
**Alex:** *"What if the next drop is you?"*

**Mira:** *(laughs, sharp and dry)* *"Or a ghost. Or a version of yourself that’s already forgotten."*

---
**Alex:** *"Then why do we keep going?"*

**Mira:** *"Because the stone’s not just in the water. It’s in the hands that dropped it. And the hands that’ll find it again."*

---
**Alex:** *(leans forward)* *"So we keep trying, even if we forget why."*

---
**Mira:** *"That’s all we’ve got."*

---
**Alex:** *(smirks, but the rain feels heavier now)*
