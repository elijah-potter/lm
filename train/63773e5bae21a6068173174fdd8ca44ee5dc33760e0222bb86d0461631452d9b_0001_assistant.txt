The sun begins to set, a sight so rare
The sky transforms into a canvas beyond compare
Colors melding, blending softly like paint
A masterpiece, a stroke of nature, no complaints.

The sky turns to candy floss, an ethereal pink
Gossamer clouds, a sight you'd want to drink
The horizon makes its way to the sun
A golden path, an invitation to run. 

A ball of fire is ready to retire
Yet I wait till it returns to inspire
My eyes soak in an orchestra of hues
A symphony so rare, a feeling to infuse. 

The world seems to stop, the colors envelope
A caress, a hug, a note to self
The warmth of the day, replaced with calm
A feeling so surreal, like a healing balm. 

The beauty of a sunset, a sight most divine
It reminds us to slow down and savor moments so fine
Just like life, with highs and lows
Each sunset promises hope and glows.
