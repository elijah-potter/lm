Neighborhood B has 5 homes, each of which will buy 5 boxes of cookies.  Assuming each box of cookies costs $2, how much will Jenny make at the better choice of the two neighborhoods?

Neighborhood B has 5 homes which will each buy 5 boxes, meaning she will sell 5*5=<<5*5=25>>25 boxes by going to that neighborhood.
Since 25>20, this means Jenny should choose neighborhood B.
