While research has shown that Mediterranean diets and others with plenty of whole foods are associated with longer, healthier lives, switching to these diets from diets with a lot of heavily processed foods can be difficult. This goes beyond the individual difficulty as processed foods last longer than whole foods, they often taste better, and they're usually more affordable. Affordable food that can last a long time is key in addressing food insecurity, but only a very small portion of the United States population gets enough fiber and other nutrients.
