Nine of Martin Scorsese's movies have been nominated for the Academy Award for Best Picture. They are: 

• Taxi Driver (1976)
• Raging Bill (1980), also Scorsese's first Best Director nomination
• Goodfellas (1990), which gave Scorsese his third Best Director nomination and first Adapted Screenplay nomination
• Gangs of New York (2002), and another Best Director nomination
• The Aviator (2004), also Best Director
• The Departed (2006), which was both Scorsese's first Best Picture and Best Director win
• Hugo (2011), also Best Director
• The Wolf of Wall Street (2013), also Best Director
• The Irishman (2019), also Best Director and Scorsese's first movie made for a streaming service (Netflix)
