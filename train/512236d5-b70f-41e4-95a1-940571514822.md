A fleeting vow slipped between the hum of city traffic and the laughter of strangers, whispered just loud enough to be heard but not overheard. The words were simple: *"I’ll be back."* The context was a shared glance, a pause in conversation where eyes met briefly before the crowd parted like waves. The moment was so brief, the promise so casual, yet it lingered—just enough to plant a seed of anticipation. The recipient’s fingers twitched, as if remembering a handshake that never fully materialized. The air between them was charged with the unspoken: *What if this isn’t goodbye?* The clock ticked on, indifferent to the tension that had formed in the silence that followed.
