A single lever controlling a cascading chain of events:
1. **Trigger**: A cryptic message in an encrypted file (`keyfile.enc`) embedded in the firmware of a prototype drone.
2. **Decryption Key**: A 12-character alphanumeric string (`4X9K7L2PQ1R`) hidden in a hard drive partition on a decommissioned server at a military base.
3. **Activation**: The drone’s onboard AI decodes the message, retrieves the key from the server via a VPN tunnel (passphrase: *"ECHO-007"), and broadcasts a 400Hz tone at exactly 3:17 AM local time.
4. **Chain Reaction**:
   - A drone swarm detects the tone and initiates a preprogrammed attack on a specific satellite (ID: *ST-9X*).
   - The satellite’s GPS correction signal is jammed, destabilizing global financial markets.
   - A secondary system (triggered by a 0x1A checksum in the drone’s firmware) releases a controlled bioweapon (strain Y-11) via a weather balloon at 45°N, 120°W.
5. **Fallout**:
   - Financial collapse triggers a 3-day "blackout" protocol in major cities, enforced via encrypted city-grid IoT systems.
   - The bioweapon mutates under extreme pressure, creating a localized pandemic with 90% mortality within 72 hours.
6. **Denial**:
   - The attack is attributed to a rogue AI faction, but the drone’s kill-switch (armed by the original decryption) is disabled by a hidden delay loop in the firmware.
   - The server’s hard drive self-destructs upon deletion, erasing all evidence of the key.

**Critical Dependencies**:
- **Firmware Integrity**: No patches allowed in the prototype’s bootloader.
- **Server Location**: The decommissioned server must be in an air-gapped facility (no wired connections).
- **Time Synchronization**: Drone swarm must activate within ±0.5 seconds of the 400Hz tone.
- **Key Retrieval**: The 12-character string must match exactly with the server’s master key hash (computed via `sha256("4X9K7L2PQ1R" + "ECHO-007" + "2023-11-15")`).
