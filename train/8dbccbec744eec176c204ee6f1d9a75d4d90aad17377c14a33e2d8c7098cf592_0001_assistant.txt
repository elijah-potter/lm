I recently purchased the 65-inch version of the 6 series by (Brand).  This is the fourth (Brand) television I've owned, and it will probably be the last.  While the TV does deserve some praise for its intuitive and easy-to-use user interface, as well as its incredible picture quality and contrast, overall I can't give it a positive review.  The selection of apps on the Roku operating system is scarce, and I'm unable to download more.  The worst part of this TV is that it turns itself off and back on again randomly.  This is a deal breaker for me, as it interrupts my competitive gaming sessions.  The onboard speaker system is rather weak as well compared to other similar TVs, or even a soundbar. My advice is to look elsewhere for your next TV purchase.
