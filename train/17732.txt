The Bank of Russia Museum invites to free guided tours. Participants can learn how to change currency, from which was made the first money and what bills are best to pay for the purchase of the sofa.
The participants of the excursion “Pages of history of the Bank of Russia” will be able to see a collection of exhibits of the Bank. On the tour, “the Life of money: production, circulation, recycling” will tell of that depends on the duration of use of the coins and which technologies are used in their manufacture.
On the tour, “What do I know about money,” you can count on old accounts and see the process of minting coins. On the walk, “Bills under a microscope, or How to recognize a fake” explain that connects banknotes, yellow-eyed penguin, giant daisies, and Pippi Longstocking.
The Museum also offers tours on the history of Russian money, the role of gold in the economy, first Bank notes and Treasury deposits.
Visit the Museum any day in an organized group or on Saturday at the individual request in the group. Detailed information is published on the website.
