A dim light flickered through the window—just enough to blur the edges of the room. The coffee cup sat half-empty on the desk, its surface still warm. My fingers traced the rim, the weight of something unsaid pressing against my chest. A name, half-remembered, a memory that had been buried too deep, surfaced like a sunken ship at low tide. It was fleeting, sharp and bright for a single, trembling second, then dissolved into the hum of the city outside, the distant hum of traffic and laughter drifting through the open window.

The clarity was a ghost—gone before I could grasp it. The room returned to its usual chaos, the same as always, but heavier now, as if the world had absorbed what I had briefly held in my hands.
