The morning sun shone brightly through the slotted cracks of the wooden blinds, casting long horizontal shadows across Jill's face. She pushed the slats up once more, stealing a longing glance at her driveway, wishing that the lineman crews were outside restoring her power. She, along with the whole neighborhood, had been without electricity since the tornado touched down in the night. The beautiful landscape she had poured years of work into was destroyed beyond recognition. Trees, debris, and telephone poles were scattered everywhere along the street. Mrs. Steven's trashcan was on its side, the contents blowing away piece by piece. Jill had never experienced a storm so severe, much less a tornado.
