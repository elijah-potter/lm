## Project Chimera: Rulebook

**A Cooperative Game for 2-4 Players**

**Designer’s Note:** *Project Chimera is designed to explore the complexities of scientific decision-making under pressure. The game simulates a scenario where rapid response and difficult choices are paramount. It aims to encourage players to engage in thoughtful discussion, weigh competing priorities, and understand the potential consequences of their actions. The game is not intended to be a simulation of real-world science, but rather a framework for exploring ethical and strategic dilemmas.*

---

### 1. Components

*   **Crisis Board:** A central board depicting a global map with designated research locations and escalating crisis tracks.
*   **Researcher Cards (12):** Each card represents a unique researcher with a specific expertise (e.g., Virologist, Geneticist, Epidemiologist, Logistics Expert). Each card details unique starting resources and a special ability.
*   **Event Cards (60):** Cards detailing escalating global events, introducing challenges and complications.
*   **Research Tokens (40):** Representing progress in various research areas (e.g., Viral Structure, Vaccine Development, Containment Protocols).
*   **Containment Markers (20):** Used to mitigate the spread of the Chimera outbreak.
*   **Resource Cubes (80):** Representing Funding, Personnel, and Equipment.
*   **Chimera Strain Tokens (15):** Representing the spread and mutation of the Chimera virus.
*   **D6 Dice (3):** Used for event resolution and research checks.
*   **First Player Token:** Indicates the current active player.

---

### 2. Setup

1.  Place the Crisis Board in the center of the playing area.
2.  Shuffle the Event Cards and place them face down near the Crisis Board.
3.  Place the Research Tokens in a supply pool.
4.  Place the Containment Markers and Chimera Strain Tokens in a supply pool.
5.  Each player selects a Researcher Card and takes the indicated starting resources.
6.  Place three Chimera Strain Tokens on the designated starting locations on the Crisis Board, as indicated by the board's initial setup.
7.  Determine the first player randomly and award them the First Player Token.
8.  **Difficulty Scaling:**
    *   **2 Players:** Begin with two Chimera Strain Tokens on the starting locations.
    *   **3 Players:** Begin with three Chimera Strain Tokens on the starting locations.
    *   **4 Players:** Begin with four Chimera Strain Tokens on the starting locations.

---

### 3. Turn Structure

Each player’s turn consists of the following phases, performed in order:

1.  **Event Phase:** Draw the top Event Card and resolve its effects. These effects often involve placing Chimera Strain Tokens, removing resources, or introducing new challenges. The Event Card will specify the die roll required to resolve the event.
2.  **Action Phase:** You have two actions to spend. Actions can be any combination of the following:
    *   **Move:** Move your Researcher to an adjacent location on the Crisis Board.
    *   **Research:** Attempt to gain a Research Token. Choose a research area and roll a D6. The target number required to succeed is printed on the Crisis Board for that location and research area. Add any relevant bonuses from your Researcher Card or location effects.
    *   **Contain:** Remove a Chimera Strain Token from your current location. Requires spending one Personnel resource.
    *   **Gather Resources:** Gain one of any resource (Funding, Personnel, or Equipment).
    *   **Assist Researcher:** Grant another Researcher one resource of your choice.
3.  **Mutation Phase:** Roll a D6. On a result of 4+, place one Chimera Strain Token on a location adjacent to a location already containing a Chimera Strain Token. If multiple locations are eligible, choose the location with the highest priority (indicated on the Crisis Board).
4.  **Pass the Token:** Pass the First Player Token to the player on your left.

---

### 4. Win and Loss Conditions

**Winning the Game:**

You win the game if you successfully develop and deploy a Vaccine. To develop a Vaccine, you must acquire five Research Tokens in the “Vaccine Development” area. Once this is achieved, the game ends immediately, and you win.

**Losing the Game:**

You lose the game if any of the following conditions are met:

*   **Global Pandemic:** The Chimera Strain Tokens occupy all designated “Critical Infrastructure” locations on the Crisis Board.
*   **Researcher Loss:** All Researchers are incapacitated (as described on certain Event Cards).
*   **Resource Depletion:** The general supply of any single resource (Funding, Personnel, or Equipment) is completely depleted.

---

### 5. Advanced Variants & Trade-offs

These variants introduce increased complexity and force difficult choices.

1.  **Uncertain Data:** At the beginning of the game, shuffle three “False Data” cards into the Event deck. When a False Data card is drawn, players must collectively decide (through discussion and a majority vote) whether the data is accurate or inaccurate. Choosing incorrectly can trigger detrimental effects. *This introduces a trade-off between trusting available information and risking misinterpretation.*
2.  **Specialized Research:**  Instead of all locations offering all research areas, each location specializes in only one or two research areas. This forces players to strategically move around the board and coordinate their actions to access the necessary research. *This introduces a trade-off between efficiency and location availability.*
3.  **Ethical Dilemmas:**  Introduce a small deck of “Ethical Dilemma” cards. At specific intervals (e.g., after every three Event Cards), draw an Ethical Dilemma card. These cards present players with difficult choices that have both positive and negative consequences for different aspects of the crisis.  The decision must be made collectively, and the outcome impacts resource availability, containment efforts, or public opinion. *This introduces a trade-off between immediate gains and long-term consequences.*
