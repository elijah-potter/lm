The dim glow of the old lantern flickered against the cracked wallpaper as Daniel sat hunched over the half-finished manuscript. His fingers traced the words, but his mind was elsewhere—focusing on the unspoken message that had lingered between them since the night they’d met. The air smelled of damp wool and stale coffee, the scent of a café that had vanished years ago.

Across the room, Clara’s fingers drummed lightly on the table, her eyes half-lidded as she stared at her own notebook. She hadn’t said it aloud, but Daniel knew the question hanging between them: *What would you do if you could take back the moment you nearly lost each other?* The silence stretched, thick with unspoken confession, with the weight of a past they couldn’t rewrite. A key turned in the lock outside, the sound sharp and final. They both stood, shoulders tense, as the door swung open, revealing the street beyond. The city’s hum swallowed their quiet.
