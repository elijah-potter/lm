As I was traveling to Earth for the first time, I immediately took notice of the vast amount of water on the planet. Most of the water was a beautiful shade of blue, which I thought was very unique. I also noticed that there is a lot of lush greenery. My planet looks like a barren wasteland compared to Earth. 

When I landed on the planet, I immediately noticed the rich, fresh soil that covered the ground. The sensation of it on my feet felt strange. I looked around and saw many tall trees and a large lake. Drinking at the lake was a few lovely animals, which I soon found out are called deer. I also heard the pleasant chirping of lovely bluebirds. I looked up and saw a bright orb in the sky that provides warmth to the planet. Everything around me is so foreign, yet feels so familiar.

Overall, I think Earth is a wonderful planet that is very diverse in nature. The natural wonders of the planet are very awe-inspiring and remarkable. As I left the planet, I found myself instantly missing it. Hopefully, I will be able to visit again soon.
