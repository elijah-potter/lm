Why we include:
D-Ribose occurs widely in nature and forms the backbone of RNA, a biopolymer that is the basis of genetic transcription. Once phosphorylated, ribose becomes a subunit of ATP, NADH and other compounds critical to metabolism. It assists in replenishing D-Ribose stores to keep up with demand during high energy workouts.
- Improves heart health
- Maintains metabolism
- Replenishes muscle-energy during high intensity exertion
