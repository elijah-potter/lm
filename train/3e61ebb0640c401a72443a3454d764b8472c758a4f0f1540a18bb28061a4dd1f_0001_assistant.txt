Sure thing! Here are a few selections from artists who have either performed on Broadway in the past or emulate that aesthetic with their songs:

•	Steven Pasquale: This artist played Robert Kincaid in the Broadway production of Bridges of Madison County. He has one album released currently called “Somethin’ Like Love,” in which he covers big band and theater tunes. The album is very quiet, and soothing, and highlights the singer’s captivating voice.
•	Josh Groban: A rather well-known singer, his style is more operatic than most. His song “You Raise Me Up” was incredibly popular in its day, and he remains a household name to this day. In more recent years, he produces covers of show tunes and a wide variety of other music.
•	Norah Jones: Best known for her 2002 hit “Don’t Know Why,” this artist has produced numerous songs, most of which are driven by her jazz club-style voice.
