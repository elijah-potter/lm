Here are some suggestions on questions to ask any political leader. 

1. Will your party adopt new legislation that will protect the right to fair housing for all? 
2. Will your party fund affordable, accessible, and inclusive housing for people who have intellectual disabilities? 
3. Will your party adopt a targeted employment strategy for persons who have disabilities? 
4. Will your party support and fund a national policy to address our nation's mental health crisis?
5. Will your party address crimes and harassment against minorities? 
6. Will your party do anything to address the rising cost of pharmaceuticals and healthcare? 
7. Will your party do anything to help decrease inflation? 
8. Does your party believe in climate change? Are members of your party willing to learn more about it?
