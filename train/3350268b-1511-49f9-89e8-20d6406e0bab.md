A tiny figurine of a fox in a cracked ceramic pot, its polished fur dulled by time, now bears the weight of forgotten legends. The pot, once a humble kitchenware, became a vessel for whispers—children carved stories into its rim, leaving only smudged fingerprints. The fox itself, once a mere ornament, now stands as a silent guardian, its gaze reflecting the stories of a village that no longer exists. Its symbolism shifts with each unseen hand that touches it, turning a mere trinket into a bridge between past and forgotten futures.
