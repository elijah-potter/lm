Dilmun was a kingdom that was around a long time ago– nearly 4,000 years. The kingdom was located in modern-day Bahrain, a very small island country near Saudi Arabia. They were important because they connected Sumer (in modern-day Iraq) with the Indus Valley (in modern-day Pakistan and part of India). Lots of trade goods moved through this small kingdom like copper, stone beads, precious stones, pearls, dates, vegetables, and agricultural products. Archaeologists have found traces of Dilmunian culture in other parts of the Middle East. 

In Bahrain, you can see their traditional architectural style at Barbar Temple. You can also experience Dilmunian culture by visiting their burial mounds. These are grave sites that look like small hills, which they dug up to bury important people.
