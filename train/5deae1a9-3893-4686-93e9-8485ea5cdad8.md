The door creaked open under the light pressure of a knock, revealing the dim glow of an old lamp flickering over a half-eaten bowl of soup on the small table. A shadow stretched long across the cracked wooden floorboards as the visitor stepped inside, their breath visible in the cold air. The scent of pine and damp earth lingered from outside, mingling with the faint, earthy aroma of the soup. The single candle on the table cast long, dancing shadows across the walls.

The figure in the doorway hesitated, arms crossed, before turning to face the room. A leather-bound journal lay open on the chair, its pages filled with sketches of cryptic symbols and fragmented poetry. The last entry read: *"They say the key is in the hollow of the tree—"* before the line vanished abruptly.

Outside, the wind howled, and the distant sound of footsteps echoed through the empty alleyway beyond. The visitor’s gaze lingered on the journal, then the candle, then the bowl of soup—each item a silent witness to the unspoken question that hung between them.
