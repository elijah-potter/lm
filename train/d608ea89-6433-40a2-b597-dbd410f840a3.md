**BREAKING NEWS FROM ECOVILLE** – *The City That Grew Green*
Ecoville’s urban forests are expanding at an unprecedented rate after the city council’s recent mandate to reclaim abandoned lots. Within hours of the decree, volunteers and local NGOs have transformed over 470 hectares of concrete into thriving green spaces, including a new urban meadow near the old waterfront. Residents report improved air quality and a surge in local bee populations, though some commercial landowners warn of lost revenue from vacant properties. The mayor’s office is investigating potential tax incentives for businesses that transition to sustainable models.

**Technical Notes:** Noise pollution from construction drones has disrupted morning commutes, prompting a city-wide "silent hour" advisory for the next 24 hours. Ecoville’s first "citizen science" initiative—*EcoWatch*—has already logged 12,473 citizen observations of native flora and fauna via the city’s app.
