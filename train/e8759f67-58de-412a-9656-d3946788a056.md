The door creaked. A cold draft slithered through the room.
Footsteps—slow, deliberate—approached.
My breath came in sharp gasps.
The shadow stretched long and unnatural.
A whisper, barely audible:
*"You shouldn’t be here."*
The air turned thick, heavy.
A sound, wet and wrong, slithered from the dark.
I froze.
Then—
*click.*
