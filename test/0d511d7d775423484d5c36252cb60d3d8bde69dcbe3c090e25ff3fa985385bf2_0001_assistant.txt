Eight Presidents of the United States have died in office since the office was established in 1789. These are:
 
1: William Henry Harrison, 1841, pneumonia
2: Zachary Taylor, 1850, acute gastroenteritis
3: Abraham Lincoln, 1865, assassination
4: James A. Garfield, 1881, assassination
5: William McKinley, 1901, assassination
6: Warren G. Harding, 1923, heart attack
7: Franklin D. Roosevelt, 1945, cerebral hemorrhage
8. John F. Kennedy, 1963, assassination
