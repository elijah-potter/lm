The Parthenon is a former temple on the Athenian Acropolis in Greece, dedicated to the goddess Athena. Constructed in the 5th century BC, it's considered the epitome of Doric-order architecture and is renowned for its proportional harmony and decorative sculptures. The Parthenon represents the power and influence of Athens at its greatest historical peak, the Athenian democracy and its victory against the Persian invasion. It is a lasting symbol of Ancient Greece and the Athenian democracy, providing a tangible link to Greece's rich cultural heritage.
