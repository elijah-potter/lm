1. "Illicit Affairs" - A story about two people that each commit an affair and then attempt to cover it up to get away with it.
2. "I Forgot That You Existed" - A story about someone who gets into an accident and forgets their entire life, including that they're the local city hero. Instead, they become the villain.
3. "Getaway Car" - A story about someone who, shortly after robbing a bank, tries to steal a getaway car but there's someone inside of it already.
4. "All Too Well" - A story about an adventurer that sets out to find themself and ends up meeting all sorts of people along the way.
5. "The 1"- A story about finding true love in the little things and how there isn't just one person out there for you, but many people.
6. "Back to December" - A story about a kid that discovers a time machine and goes back to December when they were born only to learn a mysterious secret about their family.
7. "Call It What You Want" - A story about a magician that is from another realm and performs stupid tricks on stage for a living but still knows complex and intricate spells for at home.
8. "Champagne Problems" - A story about two friends that get together one night and drink too much champagne and start spilling secrets.
9. "Dancing with Our Hands Tied" - A story about someone who wants to dance but struggles to find somewhere they like until someone introduces them to a secret dance studio where all the dance misfits go.
10. "Death by a Thousand Cuts" - A story about someone who always gets papercuts whenever they pick up a piece of paper and their journey to make a new type of paper to prevent papercuts.
