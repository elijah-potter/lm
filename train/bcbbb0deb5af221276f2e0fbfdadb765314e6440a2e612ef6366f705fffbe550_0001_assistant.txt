The black cat stretched and took a nap
She curled up in her owner's lap.
She dreamed of cardboard boxes to shred
Visions of scratching posts danced in her head.

When she awoke, her belly rumbled
Out of the human's lap, she tumbled
She ran straight to her bowl of food
but found it empty, how rude!

The cat awoke with a scare
to find it was all a nightmare
she ran quickly to her bowl
and sighed in relief to see it was full.
