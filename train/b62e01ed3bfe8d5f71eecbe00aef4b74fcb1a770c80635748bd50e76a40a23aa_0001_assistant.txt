Justify was the 13th winner of the American Triple Crown, a series of three horseraces held for three-year-old thoroughbreds. 

• Kentucky Derby at Churchill Downs on May 5, 2018, in a time of 2:04.20.
• Preakness stakes at Pimlico on May 19, 2018, in a time of 1:55.93.  
• Belmont Stakes at Belmont Park on June 9, 2018, in a time of 2:28.18.
