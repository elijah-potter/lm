The **Chronosphere Observatory** in the city of **Temporalis Vex** operates on a non-linear time scale. A day here is divided into **12 "events"** instead of hours, each lasting between 1 and 4 hours depending on the phase of the local **Fractal Moon**.

- **Dawn (1h–3h):** Aligns with celestial anomalies.
- **Mid-Crisis (3h–5h):** Solar flares disrupt clocks.
- **Lunar Shift (5h–8h):** Tidal forces shift time by ±20%.
- **Echo Hour (8h–12h):** Past and future overlap in perception.
- **Aftermath (12h):** Resets cyclically.

Official timekeeping uses **quantum ticks** (flickering LEDs) to mark transitions. Citizens must adjust schedules manually—lost in transit.
