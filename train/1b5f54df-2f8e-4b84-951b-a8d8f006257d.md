Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown fur and big, round ears that twitched when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his favorite toy, a green mushroom, growing near his burrow. He hopped over and nibbled on it, feeling a little tickly in his tummy.

"Mmm, that’s not right," Benny thought. He sniffed the mushroom and then his nose twitched—*ouch!* He sneezed and flew backward into a pile of leaves.

Just then, Benny’s best friend, a wise old owl named Oliver, landed on a branch above him. "Benny, what’s wrong?" Oliver hooted.

Benny pointed to the mushroom. "It’s not good!" he said.

Oliver fluffed his feathers. "Hmm, mushrooms can be tricky. Some are tasty, but some make you feel funny. That one looks a bit strange."

Benny’s tummy still felt funny, so he asked Oliver to help him find another snack. Oliver flew to the garden and found a plump apple and a juicy strawberry. Benny munched happily, and soon his tummy felt just right.

From that day on, Benny remembered that not all mushrooms are safe to eat. He learned that sometimes, the best friends help us make good choices, and that’s why it’s important to listen to grown-ups too.

And that’s the end of Benny’s story!
