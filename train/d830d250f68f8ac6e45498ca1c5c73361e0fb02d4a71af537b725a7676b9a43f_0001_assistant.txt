INGREDIENTS
Spaghetti: 1 pack
Ground beef: 1.5 lb 
White onion: 1 Unit
Cloves of garlic: 3 
Diced canned tomatoes: 796 ml 
Can of tomato paste: 1 
Oven-roasted red bell pepper: 1 
Oven-roasted Jalapeño: 1 
Grated carrot: 1 
Olive oil: 3 tbsp 
Grated Parmesan cheese for serving
Fresh basil leaves for garnish (optional)

SPICES
Salt
Black pepper
Oregano
Thyme
Optional: Curry
Paprika
Two stock cubes

INSTRUCTIONS
1. Take a large pot and fill it with water, generously seasoning it with salt to achieve a saltiness similar to that of the ocean. Stir thoroughly and bring the water to a vigorous boil.
2. Carefully add the spaghetti to the boiling water, ensuring to stir continuously to prevent it from sticking together.
3. Allow the pasta to boil for approximately 10-15 minutes until it reaches a semi-cooked state, with a slight firmness remaining.
4. Drain the cooked pasta, remove all the water, and set it aside for further use.
5. In a pot, heat the olive oil and sauté the onion and garlic until they turn brown. Then, add the shredded carrot.
6. Add the ground beef to the pot and break it apart with a spoon as it cooks and browns. Season with salt, black pepper, oregano, thyme, curry (optional), and paprika.
7. Create a small space in the pot and add the tomato paste. Allow it to cook for 10 minutes, then stir it into the meat.
8. Pour the diced canned tomatoes into the pot and cook for 20-30 minutes.
9. Add the diced roasted bell pepper and jalapeño to the sauce.
10. Finally, add the drained spaghetti to the pot of sauce, stir well, and let it cook for an additional 5 minutes.
11. Top with additional sauce, grated Parmesan cheese, and fresh basil leaves, if desired.
  Serve and enjoy!
