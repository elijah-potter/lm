**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, Ill. – August 16, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, promising a vibrant celebration of agriculture, community, and family entertainment. This year's fair will showcase livestock exhibits, agricultural displays, and a diverse array of vendors, alongside thrilling rides, live music, and traditional fair games. The fair provides a valuable opportunity to connect with local farmers, artisans, and neighbors while enjoying a quintessential summer experience.

The Sangamon County Fair runs from August 22nd to August 26th, 2024, at the County Fairgrounds in Springfield. Gates open at 9:00 AM each day, with evening events extending until 10:00 PM. A dedicated Kid's Zone will feature age-appropriate activities and entertainment throughout the day. Daily demonstrations of traditional crafts and agricultural practices will also be offered. Parking is available on-site for a fee.

Admission for adults is $15, children ages 6-12 are $10, and children 5 and under are free. Season passes, offering unlimited entry throughout the fair, are available for $50. Tickets can be purchased online at SangamonCountyFair.org or at the gate during the fair. Advance purchase is encouraged to avoid potential lines.

**Headline Events:**

*   Livestock Auction – August 24th, 6:00 PM
*   Demolition Derby – August 23rd, 7:00 PM
*   Grandstand Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters – August 25th, 8:00 PM
*   4-H Showcase – August 22nd, 2:00 PM
*   Monster Truck Show – August 26th, 3:00 PM
*   Talent Show Finals – August 24th, 8:00 PM
