The mid-winter blahs are a good time to spice up your style by dying your hair a bold new color. Transforming your look can send a message to the world that you're ready to party, no matter the weather. Unsure what color to try first? Here are three suggestions to consider:


Dark Magenta

Somewhere between red and blue, purple and pink collide for a dark and inky vibe. This cool color is flattering to both dark and fair skin tones, and color therapy asserts that magenta is known to have a lot of positive vibes. The PERFECT choice for any season, magenta can help brighten your mood, balancing your emotions, health, and spirit.

Dark Royal Blue

A stunning deep jewel color associated with the British royal family, this hue works with just about all skin tones. It’s often associated with people who are both kindhearted and dependable but still possess an edgy side. If you're attracted to dark royal blue, it means you're an achiever who sets goals for yourself and lives your life determined to get the most out of every experience. 

Titanium Silver

Want to look controlled, compassionate, and modern all at the same time? Try titanium silver. This color gives off feminine energy while still demanding dignity and respect. Plus, it can also help you shine brighter and convey an air of wisdom to those around you. It’s one of the few colors that can make you look beautiful and elegant at the same time.
