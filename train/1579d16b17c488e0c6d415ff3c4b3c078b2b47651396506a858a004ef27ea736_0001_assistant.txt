

Jasmine had just turned five years old and was so excited. She had been begging her parents for a puppy for years, and today was the day they were finally going to pick one out. She couldn't wait to have a furry friend to play with, hug, take care of, and cuddle with.

When they got to the pet store, Jasmine was overwhelmed by all the different puppies. There were so many to choose from! She didn't know where to start. Her parents walked down every aisle with her and looked at each and every sweet puppy. Eventually, she found the perfect puppy! 

The puppy was a small, black, white, and brown Bernedoodle. She had big, floppy ears and a white spot on her wagging tail. Jasmine fell in love with her instantly.

"I want her!" she squealed.

Her parents smiled. "We think you've found a perfect match," her mom said.

They took the puppy home and named her Luna. Jasmine was so happy to finally have a puppy of her own. She took Luna everywhere with her. They played fetch, went for walks, and cuddled for hours. They were truly best friends.

Luna was always there for her, no matter what. She made her laugh when she was sad and comforted her when she was scared. Jasmine loved Luna more than anything in the world.

Jasmine and Luna lived happily ever after. They had many more adventures together, and they always had each other's backs.
