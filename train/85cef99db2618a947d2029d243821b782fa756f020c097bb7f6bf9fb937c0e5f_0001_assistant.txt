"What a beautiful day to go fishing," Tom thought as he clambered onto a fishing boat that morning. Tom had never been fishing but always loved the idea of it. By night, Tom was a bull rider and a really good one at that. Tom had won all the major competitions several times. Today, Tom decided that he was going to go fishing for the first time. As a bull rider, Tom lived fast and free, oftentimes doing extreme things like bull riding. Tom started the boat and got going. He stopped once he could no longer see the shore and threw out his line. Almost immediately, Tom got a bite on his line! It was a great white shark! Tom fought the mammoth shark for hours. The shark pulled his boat for miles and miles until finally, his line snapped. Tom, who had fallen over in the boat, stood up and looked around to see if he could see anyone or anything. He couldn't. Tom was lost at sea! Tom tried to look at the GPS, but it was broken. Tom flashed the boat lights and yelled as loud as he could to no avail. Tom panicked for a moment and lay down in the boat. "What to do?" Tom thought. He turned the boat on and began driving, hoping that he would run into shore before dark. Tom traveled for 10 hours through the night until finally making his way back to shore. Tom parked, hopped off the boat, and kissed the ground. He was so happy to be alive and on land! Tom never got back in the boat but forever tells the story of how he got lost at sea.
