How to become veterinarian
A veterinarian can take on a number of different roles in the field. Along with providing the standard care for animals, they might also provide epidemiological information to local or federal agencies, conduct laboratory tests, write articles for publications like The New York Times or Martha Stewart Living, and much more.
To become a veterinarian you will need at least an undergraduate degree in biology, zoology or animal science. You will then need to graduate from an accredited veterinary college and pass the North American Veterinary Licensing Exam (NAVLE). Finally you must be licensed by your particular state so that you are able to practice as a veterinarian legally.
Guide on how to become a veterinarian
- You will need to get at least a bachelor’s degree in Biology, Zoology or Animal Science before you can apply to a veterinary college.
- Finally, you will need to graduate from an accredited veterinary school and pass the North American Veterinary Licensing Exam (NAVLE) to practice as veterinarian.

What are the requirements for veterinarians?
To become a veterinarian, you need to have:
- A doctor of veterinary medicine (DVM) degree or an equivalent degree
- At least three years of full-time employment—or the equivalent in part-time employment—in the field you want to practice
- A passing score on the North American Veterinary Licensing Exam (NAVLE) or an equivalent state exam in order to obtain a license
