Hello everyone, and thank you for coming today to celebrate the life of Craig Pepto.

Craig was a husband, father, son, uncle, and dedicated employee at the TaxiCo Taxi Company here in Watlanta. Many of you who had come into contact with Craig will undoubtedly have fond memories and funny stories about him, as do I, which I'm sure we'll get a chance to share at the wake later tonight.

One thing that I will say about Craig is, despite the untimely accident, he was very fond of dressing up his pet monkey, "Peaches", in a variety of clothing from the 1980s. He loved playing dress-up with Peaches and serving her tea and biscuits at noon; he loved that monkey like a daughter.

It's unfortunate what happened that fateful day a month ago. Craig would never have put that pink tutu on Peaches if he knew Peaches would reject it so violently. I think even if Craig did know how Peaches would respond, he wouldn't take it personally, for he loved that simian so much.

But that's all in the past now. I like to think that Craig is in a better place, one where he can have a sip of his margarita and sit on the heavenly beaches. I think Craig would probably be saving a space for the rest of us until we join him, and even a seat on his beach blanket for Peaches by his side.

Now, let us all bow our heads in prayer for Craig, so that he may hear us wishing him well on his journey to the afterlife. 

And for Peaches, we pray that she may be let free from Animal Control soon so she can be let back to her family at the local zoo. Amen.
