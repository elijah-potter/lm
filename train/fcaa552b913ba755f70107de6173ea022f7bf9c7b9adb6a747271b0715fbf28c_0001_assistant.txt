Researchers at Finland's University of Turku are studying how two types of nematodes can detect indoor pollution and household toxins like black mold and harsh chemicals. Paivi Koskinen and his team discovered that these genetically modified worms become various levels of fluorescent green when exposed to household toxins. They produce GFP - green fluorescent protein - which causes the color change and could potentially be used to aid humans in the early detection of harmful emissions.
