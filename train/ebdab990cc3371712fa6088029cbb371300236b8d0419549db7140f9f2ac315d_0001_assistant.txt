1. Suzie has 13 oranges. Her friend Tom gives 5 more oranges to her. How many oranges does Suzie have now?
2. Mike has 26 stamps in his collection. He goes to the supermarket and finds a pack of 14. After he adds the new stamps, how many stamps will Mike’s collection have?
3. Ms. Watson has 24 students in her classroom. One student moves away, and 2 more students transfer to her class. How many students does Ms. Watson now have?
4. A pet store has 18 goldfish and 3 bluefish. A customer comes in to buy 5 goldfish. How many fish are left in total?
5. Lillian buys a jar with 112 jellybeans. She and her friends eat 11 pink jellybeans, 3 blue jellybeans, and 7 green jellybeans. How many jellybeans are left in the jar?
