1. Chicken and Potato Casserole. In a greased pan, layer cooked chicken, cooked potato slices, and shredded cheese to taste. Heat and mix one can of cream of chicken soup with half a can of milk. Pour over chicken and potatoes. Bake for 30 minutes until warm. Chopped vegetables can be added according to preference.

2. Mac and Cheese Casserole. Cook boxed macaroni and cheese according to the directions on the box. In a separate pan saute bacon and onions. Stir into cooked pasta. Put the mixture in a baking dish and top with shredded cheddar cheese. Bake until cheese is melted on top. 

3. Beef Casserole. Cook pasta of choice. Brown ground beef in a pan and mix in tomato sauce.  Cheese sauce may also be added. Add pasta and meat mixture in a baking dish and top with shredded cheese of choice. Bake until the cheese is melted. The recipe may be varied by adding vegetables or spices of choice.
