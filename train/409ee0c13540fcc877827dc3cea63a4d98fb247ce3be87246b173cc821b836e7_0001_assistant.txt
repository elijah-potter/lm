Here are three places to view art in Minneapolis:
1. Walker Art Center: Take in their multi-level galleries of paintings, films, sculpture, new media, and performance. Discover local artists and Native American artists.
2. Minneapolis Institute of Art: View fine arts from six continents. This museum is free and has 90,000 artworks from over 5,000 years of history.
3. American Swedish Institute: Step into the customs, history, traditions, food, and art of Swedish culture.
