The old wooden crate sat abandoned in the attic, half-buried under dust and cobwebs. The family that had once owned it had moved away decades ago, leaving behind only this forgotten relic. Inside, a single rusted key glinted faintly under the dim attic light. It was no ordinary key—its design was intricate, etched with symbols no one recognized.

The attic door creaked open as a gust of wind howled through the broken window. A shadow stretched long across the floor, and a voice whispered, *"You found it."*

A child’s laughter echoed through the hollow space, and suddenly, the key was no longer just a key. It was the door to something—or someone—waiting beneath the house.
