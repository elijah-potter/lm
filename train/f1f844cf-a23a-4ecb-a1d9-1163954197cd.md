Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had soft brown fur and always wore a tiny red hat on his head. Benny loved to hop around the garden, chasing after butterflies and collecting acorns in his basket.

One sunny morning, Benny spotted a shiny green apple in the middle of his garden. He couldn’t believe his eyes! "What a big, juicy apple!" Benny thought. He hopped closer and carefully picked it up. The apple felt so soft and smelled so sweet.

Just then, Benny’s best friend, a little squirrel named Sammy, hopped by. "Did you find a treasure?" Sammy asked, eyeing the apple.
"Maybe it’s magic!" Benny giggled. They both took a bite and giggled again. The apple was so tasty!

Suddenly, Benny’s mommy called out from the kitchen. "Benny, time to eat your breakfast!" she said. Benny quickly hopped inside, but he still had the apple. "I’ll eat it later," he said, holding it up.

Later that afternoon, Benny and Sammy played in the garden again. Benny’s mommy gave Benny a big bowl of yummy carrots. Benny ate them all and felt happy. "I had a wonderful day!" Benny said, smiling.

And that was how Benny discovered that even small treasures, like a big green apple, could bring a little bit of magic and happiness to his day.
