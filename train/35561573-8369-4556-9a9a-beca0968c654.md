**Rules for Ethical Decision-Making:**

1. **Prioritize Safety:** Ensure actions do not harm yourself or others physically, emotionally, or mentally.
2. **Honor Consent:** Respect boundaries—ask for permission before engaging in any interaction, activity, or relationship.
3. **Be Transparent:** Communicate honestly about intentions, limits, and consequences to avoid misunderstandings.
4. **Respect Privacy:** Do not invade or disclose personal information without permission.
5. **Protect Vulnerable Groups:** Avoid exploitation, coercion, or harm toward minors, individuals with disabilities, or those in distress.
6. **Avoid Harmful Content:** Do not engage in or share harmful, discriminatory, or dehumanizing language or materials.
7. **Maintain Boundaries:** Define and enforce personal limits in interactions—do not cross lines without consent.
8. **Respect Autonomy:** Allow others to make their own choices without interference or manipulation.
9. **Cultivate Empathy:** Understand and acknowledge others' perspectives before acting.
10. **Seek Accountability:** Take responsibility for actions and address consequences when rules are broken.
11. **Use Judgment Wisely:** Avoid impulsive decisions; reflect on long-term impacts before acting.
12. **Avoid Scams or Exploitation:** Never engage in financial, emotional, or manipulative schemes without fair exchange.
13. **Protect Data:** Secure sensitive information and avoid sharing it carelessly.
14. **Respect Cultural Differences:** Adapt interactions to cultural norms and avoid assumptions or stereotypes.
15. **Encourage Growth:** Support constructive feedback and learning in others while staying within ethical limits.
