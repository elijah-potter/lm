Emerging urban cluster near a defunct industrial hub, where repurposed warehouses and scaffolding-covered structures form a grid of temporary pathways. Solar panels dot rooftops, while flickering LED signs in a mix of local scripts advertise the new "Third Market District." Workers in high-vis jackets carry modular furniture and construction drones to the site, with a faint hum of automated assembly systems. The air smells of wet concrete and ozone, and the distant wail of a siren—police or fire, neither confirms. Nearby, a makeshift market stands where bartering happens in handshakes and cryptocurrency scribbled on receipts. The only rule: no fixed contracts. The routine isn’t written yet—it’s being carved in real time.
