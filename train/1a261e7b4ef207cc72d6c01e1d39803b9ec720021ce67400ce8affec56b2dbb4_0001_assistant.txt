Masayoshi Takanka is a Japanese guitarist, composer, and producer who was born in Tokyo, Japan in 1953. Masayoshi Takanka began playing guitar and bass guitar in the prog rock band Flied Egg in 1971. He has also collaborated with multiple artists, including Santana and Roxy Music.
