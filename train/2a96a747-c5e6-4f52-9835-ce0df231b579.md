**The Clockmaker’s Dilemma**

In the quiet town of Eldermere, clockmaker Elias had one obsession: perfect time. His latest creation, *The Last Second*, could stop time for anyone who held its pendulum. The townsfolk whispered of its power, but Elias refused to sell it, fearing misuse.

One evening, a desperate nobleman arrived, his daughter’s life in danger from a poisoner. Elias, torn between duty and morality, let him hold the pendulum. The girl lived. But the moment she released it, the clock’s gears spun wildly, and the entire town’s clocks stopped—all at once.

---

**Ending A: The Fall of Time**
The clocks froze. Elias realized his mistake. The nobleman, now a hollow figure, whispered: *"You gave me time, but time gave it back."* The townsfolk, paralyzed, began to decay. Elias fled as the last seconds ticked backward, and the town’s history unraveled into dust.

---
**Ending B: The Cycle Continues**
The nobleman, now a guardian of time, used the pendulum to restore motion—slowly. The town’s clocks resumed, but Elias vanished, his guilt consuming him. Years later, a child in Eldermere found a broken clock with a single working second hand. *"It’s not broken,"* the child whispered. *"It’s waiting."*
