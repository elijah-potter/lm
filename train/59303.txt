Josh loves cakes, cookies, and everything sweet. Then, one day, he goes to the dentist and finds out he has cavities. It sounds scary, but don’t worry, Pepa is here to explain everything! With his help, Josh learns all about cavities, from what they are to how to avoid them, and even what a filling is. Join Josh on a fun and educational adventure to the dentist!
- Interactive storybook – “Going to the Dentist”
- Help kids PREPARE for their FIRST DENTAL APPOINTMENT!
- Teach children why sugary foods and sweets can cause cavities.
- Learn about cavities and how a cavity feeds on sugar from the food and drinks you take in.
- Learn the best time to brush teeth – right after meals and snacks
- Recommended for ALL AGES
