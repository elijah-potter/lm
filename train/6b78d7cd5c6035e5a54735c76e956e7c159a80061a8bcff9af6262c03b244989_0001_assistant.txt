Meet Terry. Terry is the smallest turtle in the pond. He and all of his friends would spend the entire day splashing around, swinging on branches, and relaxing on the biggest stones they can find. Terry loved his family and he was satisfied with the life set out in front of him. One day, Terry's mom stood in front of all of her brothers, sisters, and children and said, "My family, today we find a new home. We must move west, cross the river, and find a new pond to call home." All of the turtles in the pond were so excited! All except for little Terry. Terry didn't want to find a new home. He was happy at this pond, but he was still optimistic. His family began to leave the pond, and Terry followed. As they made their way out of the pond, through the woods, and across the busy highway they came to the big river. This was the biggest moment for all of the turtles and especially little Terry. He was in the back of the line. As he watched all of his brothers and sisters jump into the river one by one he became more and more worried. It was finally his turn. As he splashed into the river and got washed around by the powerful stream he had one goal in mind. All he had to do was get to the other side. He swam, flapped his legs, and fought against the vicious stream until he could reach the other side of the river. He made it. Once he pulled himself onto the other side of the shore he searched for the rest of his family. Terry began to cry. None of them had made it across.
