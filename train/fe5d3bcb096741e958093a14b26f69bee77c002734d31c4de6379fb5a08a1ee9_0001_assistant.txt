Amy was a teenager who loved writing. She was popular at school, her tomboyish elegance making her a favorite among guys and girls alike. She was a star track runner, but her passion was in the novel. She spent her free time reading and writing. In her last year of high school, her dream comes true, and she gets published. The story is about a renegade named Seventeen who fights for the sake of protecting humanity from a threat that wants to consume space-time.
Seventeen is a renegade with the Resistance in her world. She is the ace of the Resistance, outpacing all her comrades by leagues. She has completed over one hundred successful missions, saving millions of lives in not just her own reality but other realities, too. Despite being young, she has machine precision with a rifle, is intelligent, and has seen more despair than anyone. Her only solace is her ability to peer through space-time to remind herself of what she fights for. She often looks at Amy's world, finding happiness in the fact that she has protected that world.
However, when Amy's novel is published, dark forces move through the government and begin hunting her. They plan to use her to break the space-time barrier, and it's now up to Seventeen to cross worlds in order to save Amy and prevent an ultimate collapse. Can she make it in time to save both universes, or will she fall short of her missions? And what is it that connects these two women through space-time? What is their secret?
