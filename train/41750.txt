Over the last decade, technology has continued to be transformative in our lives, particularly in education.
Students have access to current information for research, digital tools to support their learning needs, and access to devices for learning anywhere they might be.
Tahoe Truckee Unified School District has a very successful 1:1 program and beginning in fifth grade, each student is able to have a device checked out to them. Our students use technology to create multimedia content and demonstrate their learning through projects that include video and music. In addition, computer science applications, coding, and programming lessons are offered to our students from elementary through high school. (Read full article...)
