So there I was, sitting down on one side of the table and across from me, a person that is delivering one million dollars.
I won a lottery at the local grocery store a few days ago and couldn't believe it was true. All these ideas flew through my head thinking about what I could buy with one million dollars.
I could buy a car. I could buy a mansion! 
As the delivery man left the house, I settled on what I would buy first. I walked out the door and drove straight to the local pet shop. 
The cashier greeted me with a hello as I walked in. I waved back at them while scanning for what I wanted.
There were so many pets, from dogs & cats to snakes, bunnies, and guinea pigs. I was intrigued by all of them. But they were not what I was looking for.
Then like a flash of lightning, the perfect pet appeared in front of me. It was like love at first sight. The shine of their green, rough shell. Their eyes looked like black dots glazed over.
It was the perfect pet. A turtle. I motion to the staff that I wanted the turtle. They taught me all about turtles and what to look out for. 
The cashier checked me out and congratulated me on my new pet. The only thing left is a name.
"I think I'll name it Syrup," I say out loud. Syrup looks like it's nodding its head.
So with that, Syrup and I start heading back to my house, dreaming of ideas I can accomplish with the rest of the million dollars. I feel like my life has been made.
