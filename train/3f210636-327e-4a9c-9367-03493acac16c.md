AITA for telling my sister I don’t want her to bring her fiancé to my wedding?

My sister, Eleanor, started dating Mark approximately six months ago, around July 2023. She seemed genuinely happy, which I was thrilled about. She’s always struggled with relationships. I was excited to get to know Mark, and we met him for coffee on August 12th, 2023, at a cafe near her apartment in Denver. He seemed…fine. A little bland, perhaps, but pleasant enough.

Things started to get complicated around January 15th, 2024. Eleanor announced they were engaged. I was happy for her, truly. However, she immediately insisted Mark be included in all wedding-related discussions and planning. I politely explained that, given the short engagement and my desire for a smaller, more intimate gathering, I envisioned the wedding party consisting solely of my immediate family and closest friends. I emphasized that this wasn’t a reflection on Mark, but a matter of keeping the guest list manageable.

Eleanor became increasingly upset. She argued that Mark was her fiancé and deserved to be present. She pointed out that I’ve always been her biggest supporter and that excluding him felt like a personal rejection. My mother, who is notoriously conflict-avoidant, weighed in via phone on January 20th, urging me to “just let him come” to avoid a family rift. My father, who is usually more pragmatic, suggested a compromise – a separate dinner for Mark and Eleanor the night before the wedding. I found that insulting to Mark, frankly.

I held firm. I explained to Eleanor on February 10th, during a video call, that the venue has a strict capacity limit and that every invitation represents a difficult choice. I reiterated my love and support for her but maintained that Mark’s presence wasn’t feasible. She accused me of being selfish and controlling.

The situation escalated further on April 1st. Eleanor posted a cryptic message on social media about “family members prioritizing their own desires over the happiness of others.” Several relatives commented, expressing their disapproval of my stance. I received a barrage of concerned messages. My aunt Carol, who always thinks she knows best, called me on April 2nd and said I was being “unreasonable” and “hurtful.”

I tried to explain my reasoning to Mark directly on May 15th, during a phone call. I was respectful and empathetic, emphasizing that this decision wasn’t about him personally. He seemed genuinely disappointed, but also seemed to understand the logistical constraints. Eleanor, however, was furious that I’d contacted him independently. She believes I should have discussed it with her first.

Yesterday, June 12th, Eleanor called me, her voice trembling. She threatened to boycott the wedding entirely if Mark wasn’t included. She said my wedding was supposed to be a celebration of *her* happiness, and excluding her fiancé felt like a betrayal. I felt cornered and exhausted. I reiterated my position, but she hung up on me.

I’m now facing immense pressure from my family, and I’m starting to question whether I’m being unreasonable. I understand Eleanor’s disappointment, but I also feel strongly that I have the right to make decisions about my own wedding. I'm worried this will permanently damage our relationship, but I also feel like I can't compromise my vision for the day. My mother is now saying I should just “cave” to keep the peace. ESH, probably, for getting myself into this situation. NTA for wanting a wedding that reflects my vision. YTA to my family for pressuring me.
