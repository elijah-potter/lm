1997 World Series Game One
---------------------------------- 
-Played at Pro Player Stadium in Miami
-Cleveland Indians (away) vs. Florida Marlins (home)
-Florida defeated Cleveland 7-4.
-Livan Hernandez was the winning pitcher.
-Orel Hershiser was the losing pitcher.
-Robb Nen recorded a save
-Florida leads 1-0 in the best-of-seven series.

1997 World Series Game Two
------------------------------------

-Chad Ogea was the winning pitcher

1997 World Series Game Five
----------------------------------
-Played at Jacobs Field in Cleveland
-Florida Marlins (away) vs. Cleveland Indians (home)
-Florida defeated Cleveland 8-7
-Livan Hernandez was the winning pitcher. This was his second win in the World Series.
-Orel Hershiser was the losing pitcher. This was his second loss in the World Series.
-Robb Nen recorded a save. This was his second save of the World Series.
-Florida leads 3-2 in the best-of-seven series.

1997 World Series Game Six
----------------------------------
-Played at Pro Player Stadium in Miami

1997 World Series Game Seven
-----------------------------------------
-Played at Pro Player Stadium in Miami
-Cleveland Indians (away) vs. Florida Marlins (home)
-Florida defeated Cleveland 3-2 in 11 innings
-Charles Nagy was the losing pitcher
-Jay Powell was the winning pitcher
-The Florida Marlins win the World Series
