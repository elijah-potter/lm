Our movie will follow the amazing story of Governor William Bradford, the first Pilgrim leader of Plymouth Colony in Massachusetts. Starting with his arrival in 1620, we'll trace his remarkable rise and eventual fall from grace as he struggles to balance his dream of religious freedom for his people and the realities of governing a community. We'll see how his deep religious convictions led to the creation of the Mayflower Compact, the pioneering document that established self-government in America. We'll watch as he leads the new colony through hard times, guiding them through their first winter, a drought, war with Native American tribes, and ultimately building a prosperous, thriving colony. We'll also witness how his love for Sarah Lawrence, a fetching young herbalist later accused of witchcraft, challenges the fabric of the community he built. Our movie will be inspiring and heartbreaking; it will capture the inspiring moment of hope and courage at the dawn of the nation.
