The rusted metal box lay half-buried in the overgrown courtyard of an abandoned hospital, its surface etched with faded ink: *"If you find this, do not open it."* Inside, a single sheet of yellowed paper fluttered to the floor, its edges singed. The handwriting was frantic, the ink smudged from hurried scribbles:

---
**Date: 12/07/43**
**Location: Sector D, Zone 9**
**Whoever reads this—**
I was a scientist in Project *Elysium*. We were testing the effects of *sonic resonance* on human perception. The experiment went wrong. The walls *breathed*. The air turned thick, like honey. My colleagues… they didn’t hear the warning. They *felt* it first—the slow, creeping pressure in their skulls. The last thing I saw was my assistant’s face stretch into a grin, her eyes rolling back. The box was a containment error. The message inside was my own, torn from my pocket as the walls *swallowed me whole*.

**The box is sealed. Do not open it.**
**But if you do…**
---

The paper curled at the edges, as if someone had tried to fold it back into itself. Beneath the note, a single, unmistakable symbol was drawn in charcoal: a triangle with three jagged teeth at its corners, pulsing faintly like a heartbeat.
