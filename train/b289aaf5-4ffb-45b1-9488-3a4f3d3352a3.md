What if the elevator doors had stayed closed instead of opening, forcing the passengers to wait for the next one, while the speaker announced, *"The next elevator will arrive in 3 minutes. Please stay seated and wait."*

The 7-year-old in the corner, who had been counting backward from 30 to avoid the sudden noise, froze. The older man beside her, used to the constant hum of the city, glanced at his watch—then at her. She didn’t move. The silence stretched, broken only by the distant sirens of a fire truck. Outside, a car alarm blared, then cut out. The doors *did* open, but the moment was already stolen. The elevator’s doors swung shut again, this time with a deliberate *click*. The man turned to the child, his voice measured: *"You’re going to be late for your first day at school. Again."*
