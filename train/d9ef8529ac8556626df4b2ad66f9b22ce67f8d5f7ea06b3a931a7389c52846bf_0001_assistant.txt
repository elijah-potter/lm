The first people to ever reach the summit of Mount Everest were New Zealander Edmund Hillary and Nepalese Tenzing Norgay. Their trek to the top of the world's highest mountain culminated at 11:30 a.m. on May 29, 1953. Hillary and Norgay were experienced climbers of the British Everest Expedition of 1953. They were selected from a special group of eleven climbers for the final ascent by the Expedition's leader, Colonel John Hunt.
