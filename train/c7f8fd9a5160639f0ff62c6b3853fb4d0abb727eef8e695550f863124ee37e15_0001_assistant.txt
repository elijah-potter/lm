When young Millie the cat's parents die, she is sent to live with her aunt in the English countryside. Her widowed aunt is in great debt due to her late husband's extravagant spending, and she is not fond of Millie and her beautiful soft brown fur. Her aunt does not introduce her in social circles, rather treating her like a servant. One day, Millie hurts her paw when walking down the lane and is carried home by a handsome tomcat. She later learns that his name is Mr. Purrcy. He has recently moved to his family's countryside estate and is rumored to be quite rich. Despite Millie's aunt's efforts to make her a match with the town vicar, Mr. Ermine, she becomes quite enamored with Mr. Purrcy. Although Mr. Purrcy's mother believes Millie is below their social station, he insists on marrying her. After he returns from a London business trip, he gives Millie his marriage proposal, and they are soon married.
