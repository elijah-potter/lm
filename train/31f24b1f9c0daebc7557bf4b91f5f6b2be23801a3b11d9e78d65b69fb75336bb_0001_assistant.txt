You’ve heard of the drone, a remote control flying device. You’ve heard of the hoverboard, a highly maneuverable electric scooter. Put the two together, and you get...the HoverDrone!

The HoverDrone combines two technologically advanced products into one amazing, new, patented invention! Here’s how it works:

A foot platform like a hoverboard is attached to powerful propellers slightly below the level of the platform. The operator of the HoverDrone can then use similar movements to a hoverboard to shift their weight from front to back and side to side to determine their direction of flight!

What happened to the remote control that drones use, you ask? The remote control operates as an override for parents. If your child won’t park their HoverDrone and come inside for supper, Mom and Dad can use the remote control feature to bring them back home, quickly and safely!

For daredevils who’d like to take the HoverDrone concept one step further, we also offer the HoverDrone Deluxe! The HoverDrone Deluxe has boots built into the platform, firmly securing the rider and making it possible to do mid-air flips! Are you a snowboarder or skateboarder with experience performing flips and other tricks? This model is for you! (The HoverDrone Deluxe is not recommended for riders under 16 or those with a novice skill level with the original HoverDrone.)
