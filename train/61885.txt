Linking letter cubes are a versatile tool for teachers and families to help build reading skills. Use these during whole group time to demonstrate the word of the day, or the daily message.
Blue cubes represent consonants and the red cubes represent vowels. There are 2 of each letter so you can build words.
Picture & Letter Cubes
Each picture on the light blue cubes starts with a consonant, and the pink picture cubes all have pictures of things that start with a vowel. Each picture cube is 4 sided and each picture starts with the same letter.
Uppercase & Lowercase Letters
All letter cubes have 2 capital letters and 2 lowercase letters.
WHAT TO TEACH
- Letter sounds, names, forms, shapes, and sequence
- Identify rhyming words
- Counting syllables
- Isolate initial, medial, final letter sounds
- Match initial, medial, final letter sounds with pictures
- 42 Blue consonant cubes
- 12 Red vowel cubes
- 1 Blank blue cube
- 1 Blank red cube
- 21 Light blue picture cubes
- 5 Pink picture cubes
