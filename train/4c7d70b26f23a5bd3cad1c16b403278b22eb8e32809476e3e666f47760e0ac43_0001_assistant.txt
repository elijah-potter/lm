Tim had been looking forward to his deployment in Japan for months. He had been preparing to explore the vibrant culture and sample all of the delicious Japanese snacks and street foods that he had read so much about. But when he landed at the airport, something felt off. The signs were all in English, and the people around him spoke with heavy drawls. Tim was in a state of complete confusion. It wasn't until he saw the Texas airport sign that he realized what had happened. He had boarded the wrong plane after a long night of drinking off base and was now in Texas instead of Japan.

As he walked out of the airport, Tim couldn't help but feel embarrassed. He had been so excited to immerse himself in Japanese culture, and he was now in a stupid predicament. But as he explored the city of Houston, he got the itch to stay. He decided to soak up last night’s booze with some Tex-Mex-style street tacos. He considered staying there and starting a new life as a Texan, but he would have to change himself completely.
