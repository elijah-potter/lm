In the dim glow of a flickering lantern, **Mira** sat cross-legged on the floor, her fingers tracing the worn edges of an old journal. The entries were cryptic, filled with dates and vague descriptions of "the incident."

*"They say he was laughing when he fell. That’s what he said."* Mira’s voice trembled as she read aloud. **"But I saw his mouth close—he didn’t laugh at all. His eyes were glassy, like he’d seen something he shouldn’t."**

Across from her, **Daniel** leaned against the wall, arms crossed, his face unreadable. He had been there that night too—just as Mira had. *"He was drunk, Mira. That’s all. He tripped over his own feet. No one else saw anything."*

The lantern flickered. Outside, the wind howled through the alley. Mira’s hands clenched the journal. *"Drunk men don’t stumble and fall without a reason. Not like that. Not without—"* She cut herself off, her breath shallow. *"Without *looking*."*

Daniel exhaled sharply. *"You’re reading too much into it. The police said he was drunk. The doctor said it was an accident."* He gestured to the cracked floorboards beneath their feet. *"This place is old. The floor might be giving out. Anyone could’ve tripped."*

Mira’s gaze flicked to the journal again, then to the streetlamps beyond the door. *"Or someone *wanted* us to trip."*
Silence. The wind carried the distant sound of a siren.

Daniel didn’t answer.
