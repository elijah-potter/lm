Once there was a little bunny named Pip who loved to hop around the sunny meadow. One morning, Pip met a tiny, shiny butterfly named Bella who fluttered near a big, beautiful flower.

"Can you show me how to fly?" Pip asked.
Bella giggled and said, "You’ll need wings and a little magic!"

Pip found a soft leaf and a tiny feather. With a wiggle, they taped them together. Then Pip took a deep breath and—*whoosh!*—it was time to fly!

Pip flapped his wings and soared over the meadow. He saw a cloud that looked like a big, fluffy cat. Pip hopped onto it and danced. The wind tickled his ears, and he laughed so hard he almost fell!

Later, Pip met a sleepy old owl who said, "You’re brave, Pip! But remember, the best adventures are shared with friends."

Pip and Bella promised to explore together every day, flying, hopping, and laughing under the golden sun. And that’s how Pip learned that the world was full of magic—waiting to be discovered.
