graibeard's Radish (White Icicle) Care Instructions
Look to ensure a distance 0.98 inches (2.5 cm) between seeds when sowing - cover at a depth of at least 0.47 inches (1.2 cm) deep if you are going to sow this directly in the garden.
Radish 'White icicle' is known for growing to a height of approximately 15.0 cm (5.85 inches) so ensure you have enough space in your garden for this plant.
Radish 'White icicle' likes a position of full sun light, which is a perfect fit for your garden.
Remember to water moderately.
Your garden's USDA Hardiness Zone (10) is within the ideal range for Radish 'White icicle'. The recommended range is between Zone 5 and 14.
Radish 'White icicle' requires a soil ph of 5.5 - 6.8 meaning it does best in acidic soil.
