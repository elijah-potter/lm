**Log: Personal Reflection – Day 1-3 of Travel**

---
**Day 1: Arrival**
Weather: 72°F, overcast skies. Arrived at **Baltimore Airport (BWI)** at 14:30. Boarded **Amtrak’s *Capitol Limited*** (15:45) to Washington D.C. Passed a man in a worn leather jacket reading *Moby-Dick* while nursing coffee. His hands trembled slightly as he set it down.

Arrived in D.C. at **Union Station** at 19:00. Stopped at a diner near **National Mall** for dinner. Tasted *carrot cake* from a local bakery. Ordered a black coffee; it was the first time in months I’d drunk anything without artificial sweeteners.

---
**Day 2: Exploring the National Mall**
Morning: Visited **Lincoln Memorial** at sunrise. Watched a young girl (10) pointing at the statue, murmuring, *"He was brave."* I sat alone on a bench nearby, sketching the reflection of the fountain in my notebook. Later, at **Smithsonian National Air and Space**, saw a **Wright Flyer** replica. An elderly woman in a lab coat pointed to the propeller: *"Look at that… it’s just wood."* I realized I’d never stopped to think about how humans *built* progress.

Afternoon: Walked **Reflection Pool** at sunset. Watched a family feed ducks. The mother laughed when her child dropped breadcrumbs into the water. She said, *"They’re so happy to see us."* I took a photo of her and the ducks, then left without looking back.

Evening: Stayed at **The Peninsula Hotel** (D.C.). Drank whiskey at a rooftop bar. Spoke to a stranger about their trip to **Vietnam**. They said, *"We didn’t come to see history. We came to *feel* it."* I nodded, but my mind drifted to the weight of my own inaction.

---
**Day 3: Return & Reflection**
Left D.C. at 06:00. Stopped at **George Washington University** to snap a photo of the **Washington Monument** from the **National Cathedral** steps. Wrote in my journal:
*"They say travel broadens the mind. But today, I realized it’s more than that. It’s about finding moments where you’re *seen*—not just by others, but by yourself. I sat with the ducks. I listened to the old woman. I stopped to sketch a shadow on the Lincoln Memorial. That’s not ‘broadening.’ That’s *grounding*."*

Arrived home at 20:30. First thing I did: **called my mom**. Spoke for the first time in two weeks. She said, *"You’re different."* I didn’t know what she meant, but I believed her.

---
**Post-Trip Note**
- **Purchased**: A **handmade ceramic teacup** from a street artist in Philadelphia. Still holds the tea I drank there.
- **Deleted**: 124 photos from my phone—most were blurry or selfies. Kept only:
  - The ducks at the Reflection Pool.
  - The old woman pointing at the Wright Flyer.
  - A close-up of my hands writing in the journal.
- **New Habit**: Every morning, I now **write 3 sentences about a small thing I noticed**—a bird’s chirp, a stranger’s kindness, the way the light hits a bridge.
