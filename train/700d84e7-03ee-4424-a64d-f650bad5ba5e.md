Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a garden full of flowers, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy pink ears and a heart full of curiosity.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside and saw a big, bright blue butterfly dancing around a daisy. "Wow!" Benny said. "What fun!"

The butterfly fluttered closer. "I'm a butterfly, Benny! Want to play?" Benny's eyes sparkled. "Yes, please!"

The butterfly showed Benny how to flutter his own wings, making tiny circles in the air. Benny giggled. "That's fun!" he hopped around.

Soon, Benny found a big, juicy clover. "I want to share!" he said. The butterfly took the clover and nibbled it, then gave it back. "Thanks!" Benny said.

As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and orange, Benny and the butterfly waved goodbye. "Goodbye, Benny!" the butterfly said. "I'll see you tomorrow!"

Benny snuggled into his burrow, dreaming of tomorrow's adventures with his new friend. The stars twinkled above, and Benny knew the best stories were yet to come.
