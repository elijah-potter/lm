Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop and play.
One sunny morning, Benny found a shiny red apple on his favorite patch of flowers.
He nibbled just a tiny bit—oh, how sweet it tasted! But Benny didn’t want to eat the whole apple, because he knew he should share his treats with his friends.

Down the hill lived Benny’s best friend, Sammy the squirrel. Sammy had been saving her acorns for a long time. "I’ll share my best one!" Sammy said cheerfully.
Benny hopped over to Sammy’s tree and handed her the apple. "Here you go!" he said.
Sammy clapped her tiny paws. "Thank you, Benny! Now let’s eat together!"

They sat under a big, leafy oak tree, munching their snacks. Benny’s ears perked up when he heard a rustling in the bushes.
"Look!" Benny whispered. "It’s a little mouse named Mimi!"
Mimi was shy but had a tiny bag of seeds. "I brought you something," she said, holding out her bag.
Benny and Sammy took turns sharing the seeds, and soon, all three friends had filled their bellies.

As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and gold, Benny and his friends decided to share their happiness too. "Let’s build a little nest together!" Sammy said.
They gathered twigs and leaves, and by the time the stars twinkled, they had a cozy nest under the tree.
Benny curled up inside, warm and happy, knowing that sharing was the best way to make new friends—and even bigger smiles.
