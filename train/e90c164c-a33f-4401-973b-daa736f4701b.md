A single lever in a doorframe, cracked by a child’s toy.
The wind rattles it—just enough to unlock the vault behind the door.
Inside, a ledger of secrets, hidden in a ledger of lies.
The key? Not what was lost, but what was *left behind*—a fingerprint on the doorframe, a clue too small to notice.
The plan hinges on this: the truth is in the residue of the lie.
The vault opens when the wrong person notices the wrong detail.
