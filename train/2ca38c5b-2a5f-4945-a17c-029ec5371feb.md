A faded journal entry: *"Met Jake at 7:15 PM, coffee at ‘The Daily Grind.’ He said he’d ‘had a revelation’ about the old mill—asked if I’d heard rumors about the ‘black box’ in the basement. Told him I’d seen the rusted crate myself, but no one knew what was inside. Left before he could ask more. Walked home through the mist, replayed his voice—half-suspicious, half-intrigued. The box was always there, but no one spoke of it. Maybe it was just noise. Maybe it was the truth."*
