Here are some unique monsters for you to have in your Dungeons and Dragons game.

• Behirs: These serpentine creatures are known for their lightning breath and their ability to climb walls. They are often found in mountainous regions.

• Cockatrice: These creatures are hatched from the eggs of roosters that have been petrified by a basilisk's gaze. They can turn living creatures to stone with their gaze.

• Doppelgangers: These creatures are masters of disguise and can take on the appearance of any humanoid. They are often used by villains to infiltrate groups and sow discord.

• Ettercaps: These spider-like creatures are known for their venomous bite and their ability to create webs that can entangle their prey. They are often found in dark, damp places.

• Myconids: These fungus-like creatures are known for their peaceful nature and their wisdom. They are often found in underground communities, where they live in harmony with nature.

• Otyughs: These creatures are known for their foul odor and their insatiable appetite. They are often found in sewers and other dark, dank places, where they devour anything that comes their way.
