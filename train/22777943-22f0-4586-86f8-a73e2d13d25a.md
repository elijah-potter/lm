A rule enforced due to perceived threats or outdated assumptions, often ignored in practice despite formal adherence. Its persistence stems from fear of dissent or inertia in bureaucratic systems. Examples include mandatory dress codes in schools, outdated safety protocols in industries, or rigid workplace hierarchies that stifle innovation. The rule’s justification—"for safety," "to maintain tradition," or "to avoid conflict"—becomes the excuse for its maintenance, regardless of actual benefit. Deviations are tolerated only under pressure, reinforcing its arbitrary authority.
