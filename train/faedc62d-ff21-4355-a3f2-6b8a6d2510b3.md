Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the meadow. One sunny morning, Benny met a tiny spider named Stella who was spinning her first silky web. "Can I help you?" asked Benny, his eyes wide with curiosity. Stella smiled and said, "I need some yarn to make a big, cozy web for my home!"

Benny thought for a moment and then said, "I have just the thing! But first, we need to find some flowers to make a beautiful, colorful yarn." Benny picked a red flower, a yellow one, and a blue one. Stella was so happy! Together, they wove the flowers into yarn and made Benny's web as big and strong as a castle.

Stella lived happily in her web, and Benny would often visit to play games with her. They would spin stories about princesses and dragons, and Benny would giggle with Stella’s spinning magic. Every evening, they would sit together under the twinkling stars, telling tales of adventure until they both fell asleep, dreaming of more magical adventures.

But one day, a big, brown storm rolled in. The wind howled and the rain poured down. Benny and Stella were scared. "Don’t worry," said Benny. "I’ll help you make a shelter!" Together, they gathered leaves and sticks, and built a cozy little house for Stella. The storm passed, and Stella’s web stayed safe and strong.

From that day on, Benny and Stella became the best of friends. They spent their days playing, spinning stories, and exploring the meadow. And whenever Benny looked up at the sky, he could see Stella’s big, shiny web twinkling like a magic door, inviting him to visit her home whenever he wanted.
