To you, stranger, who stands at the threshold of this quiet page,

the wind hums through the trees like an old, unspoken secret. The ink on these lines may feel faint—drawn by fingers that tremble not from cold, but from the weight of words that refuse to stay still. You hold this between you and the world, and for a moment, the universe holds its breath, waiting to see if what follows will dissolve into dust or take root in your bones.

Tell me: do you prefer the stories that wrap you tight like a cloak, or the ones that stretch you thin, forcing you to step outside your comfort? I have no answer, but I write anyway, because the silence between you and me is not empty—it is the space where meaning might bloom.

The page turns. What will you do with it?
