The category Data offers diverse and basic background information of a city. The category functions as a frame of reference to inform about the general health determinants of the city. The content of the category is composed of objective, subjective and perceived information, which is divided in three sub-categories and several topics. The municipalities are encouraged to include also additional data on the issues and phenomenon that describe the health and health determinants of their city.
