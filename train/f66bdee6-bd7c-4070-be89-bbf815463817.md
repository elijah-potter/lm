A bureaucratic mandate mandates all documents must be filed in triplicate, stamped with "Confidential" even when unclassified. This requirement persists despite decades of evidence that the triplicate system does not improve security—it only increases processing costs by 12% and doubles storage space demands. The rule was originally implemented during the Cold War to prevent "accidental" leaks, but modern digital encryption and access controls make physical triplicates obsolete. Yet, compliance is enforced through automated system locks, and audits demand verification of "originals" for every submission, creating a cycle of unnecessary paperwork and bureaucratic inertia.
