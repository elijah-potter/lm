A winding, overgrown trail through dense underbrush, marked only by faded stones and rusted metal signs pointing to an abandoned mine shaft. The path curves endlessly into a mist-shrouded valley, where the only visible landmarks are crumbling ruins and a half-submerged, skeletal bridge with rotting planks. The air smells of damp earth and rust, and distant echoes of machinery drift through the mist, but no signs of human activity remain. The ground slopes downward into a seemingly bottomless ravine, its edge lined with jagged rocks and patches of algae-covered stone. A faint, rhythmic dripping sound comes from the depths, and the occasional flicker of firelight casts eerie shadows on the walls. The path disappears into the gloom where the last gasp of torchlight vanishes into the darkness.
