A vast, hollowed-out asteroid core hums with residual electromagnetic fields, its metallic shell etched with ancient scars from collisions. The air, thin and stale, lingers in the low gravity, clinging to surfaces like a forgotten mist. Floating debris—rusted tools, shattered equipment, and the ghostly remains of experiments—drifts lazily, silent as the void. The walls, smooth and polished by millennia of erosion, reflect nothing but the endless black, their curvature warped by the body’s own gravity. The silence is deafening, but the weight of unseen presence lingers—like the hum of a thousand unseen voices, all whispering the same unspoken question: *"Why are we here?"* The space feels full, yet utterly empty.
