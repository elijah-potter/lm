A hush descends like a whispered sigh,
sunlight bleeding through lace curtains,
piano keys soft as dust on vinyl—
a single chord, suspended, a pause
where the world exhales.
Static hums in the air, a breath held,
a river slows to a ripple,
time bends like warm fabric, smooth,
and the hum of engines fades into the
distant, muffled silence of a city
that forgot to be loud.
