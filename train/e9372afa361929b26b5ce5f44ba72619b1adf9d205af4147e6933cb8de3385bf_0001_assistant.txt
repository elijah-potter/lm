What is this issue? Hairy toes.

At first, I didn’t think it was a big deal, but everyone would make fun of me for my hairy toes. I soon learned that this was one of many things that people will judge you for, even if it’s outside of your control.

I had to wear close-toed shoes, or else wear socks with my sandals. Needless to say, I received even more ridicule for the latter, making my problem even worse. 

As an adult, I still carry the scars of this shame. I don’t let anyone see my hairy toes, ever. Even at places like the beach, I always keep my shoes on. Maybe times have changed and people have changed, but I haven’t. I’m still haunted by the mockery of years past, and I can’t bring myself to relive it. 

I’d like to say that there is a happy ending, but there isn’t one yet. All I can say is this: If you have hairy feet, know that you’re not alone.
