## Product Requirements Document: "Context" - Collaborative Knowledge Capture

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently lose valuable insights and context during meetings, conversations, and individual research, leading to duplicated effort, missed connections, and a fragmented understanding of ongoing projects.

**Vision:** To create a seamless, collaborative knowledge capture app that empowers teams to effortlessly preserve context and build a shared understanding, reducing information silos and accelerating decision-making.

---

### User Personas

**1. Anya Sharma, Project Manager:**

*   **Goals:** Effectively manage multiple projects, ensure team alignment, minimize misunderstandings, and maintain a clear record of decisions and action items.
*   **Pain Points:** Struggles to recall details from lengthy meetings, frequently receives follow-up requests for clarification, spends excessive time compiling meeting summaries, and finds it difficult to onboard new team members quickly.

**2. David Chen, Senior Engineer:**

*   **Goals:** Quickly access relevant information regarding ongoing technical decisions, share expertise with colleagues, and contribute to a centralized knowledge base for future reference.
*   **Pain Points:** Experiences difficulty locating past discussions about technical choices, spends time re-explaining decisions to different team members, and feels frustrated by the lack of a single source of truth for technical documentation.

**3. Maria Rodriguez, Marketing Specialist:**

*   **Goals:** Maintain a comprehensive record of customer feedback, campaign performance data, and competitor analysis, facilitating data-driven marketing decisions.
*   **Pain Points:** Finds it challenging to consolidate information from various sources (emails, spreadsheets, meeting notes), struggles to connect insights from different campaigns, and needs a streamlined way to share key findings with stakeholders.

---

### Requirements

**1. Contextual Capture:** Users can create "Contexts" – named spaces for specific projects, meetings, or topics – and associate relevant files, links, and notes.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can create, rename, and delete Contexts. Users can upload various file types (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, images, links).  Files and links are displayed within the Context view.
    *   **Success Metrics:** Average number of Contexts created per user (target: 3).  File upload success rate (target: 99%).

**2. Collaborative Notes:** Users can collaboratively add and edit notes within a Context, with real-time updates and attribution.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Multiple users can simultaneously edit notes. Changes are reflected in real-time for all participants. Each note entry includes the author and timestamp.
    *   **Success Metrics:** Average number of collaborators per Context (target: 1.5). User satisfaction with real-time collaboration (measured via in-app survey, target: 4.0 out of 5).

**3. Smart Linking:** The app intelligently suggests relevant Contexts and notes based on keywords in the user's current activity (e.g., email subject, document content).
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** The app suggests relevant Contexts and notes with an accuracy rate of at least 70% (measured through A/B testing).  Users can dismiss irrelevant suggestions.
    *   **Success Metrics:** Click-through rate on suggested Contexts (target: 10%). User adoption of the suggestion feature (measured by usage frequency, target: 20% of active users).

**4. Search & Discovery:** A robust search function allows users to quickly locate Contexts and notes based on keywords, author, or date.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Search results are displayed within 2 seconds. Search function supports boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT).
    *   **Success Metrics:** Average search time (target: < 3 seconds).  Percentage of searches that return relevant results (target: 85%).

**5. Sharing & Permissions:** Users can share Contexts with specific individuals or groups, controlling access levels (view, edit, comment).
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can invite others via email. Users can assign different permission levels. Permissions are clearly displayed for each Context.
    *   **Success Metrics:** Percentage of Contexts shared with others (target: 25%). User satisfaction with permission controls (measured via in-app survey, target: 4.2 out of 5).



---

### Technical Considerations

*   **Platform:** iOS and Android native applications.
*   **Backend:** Cloud-based infrastructure (AWS or Google Cloud) for scalability and reliability.
*   **Real-time Collaboration:**  WebSockets for efficient real-time updates.
*   **Security:** End-to-end encryption for data privacy.  Regular security audits.
*   **Scalability:** Architecture designed to handle a large number of users and Contexts.



### Risks

*   **User Adoption:**  Requires a shift in user behavior; onboarding and training will be crucial.
*   **Data Silos:**  Users may be hesitant to share information, hindering the app's value.
*   **Technical Complexity:** Real-time collaboration and intelligent suggestions pose significant technical challenges.
*   **Competitive Landscape:** Existing note-taking and collaboration tools offer similar functionality.



### Non-Goals

*   Direct integration with calendar applications.
*   Advanced task management capabilities.
*   Voice recording or transcription features.
*   Offline functionality beyond cached data.
*   Support for desktop applications in the initial release.

### Launch Checklist

1.  **Complete User Acceptance Testing (UAT):** Conduct thorough testing with representative users to identify and resolve critical issues.
2.  **Finalize Onboarding Flow:**  Ensure a clear and intuitive onboarding experience for new users.
3.  **Prepare Marketing Materials:**  Develop concise and informative content explaining the app's value proposition.
4.  **Establish Support Channels:**  Set up email and in-app support for user inquiries.
5.  **Implement Analytics Tracking:**  Configure analytics tools to monitor key metrics and user behavior.
6.  **Release to Beta Program:** Distribute the app to a small group of beta testers for final feedback and refinement.
