According to Roman Mythology, the Caladrius is a snow-white bird that once dwelled in the house of a king. The bird could cure the inhabitants of any maladies and diseases. Along with having healing properties, the Caladrius was believed to be prophetic. 

The bird would visit the sick at the king's house and take their illnesses from them using its healing powers. They were capable of absorbing any illness from patients just by looking at them. And they would carry the illness away as they flew off. 

Once the legend of the mysterious bird reached the Middle Ages, it was believed that Caladrius could also predict the life or death of someone who was ill. If the Caladrius would look you in the eye while it was visiting you to cure your sickness, you would live, but if the bird turned its head away, that meant your days were numbered.
