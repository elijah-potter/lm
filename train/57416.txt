The Cloud computing technology is the recent terminology that has gained prominence in the field of information and communication technology. This has made possible for easier networking through the deployment of remote servers and centralized storage infrastructure for online access of the computer services and resources. Cloud computing has been used as a metaphor for due to invisibility of the providers rendering the services.
With its invention, medical imaging cloud services have rendered great services around the world. As we all know, medical imaging is how much necessary in recent times. The MRIs, CTs, Ultrasounds, PETs, etc. are very much required for the intense analysis of a medical patient, so as to treat them effectively and quickly as soon as possible. PACs (Picture archiving and communication systems) have played an important role of storage of medical images. The cloud pacs system makes it much more efficient in delivering the appropriate information at the right time.
The cloud pacs systems have completely revolutionized the art and science of centralized storage infrastructure. The following facilities that are offered by Cloud pacs system:
• Better management of the 2D/3D images of greater bandwidth
• Deploying standardized architecture for effective management
• Reduced costs than the previous set ups
• Maintaining security, certification and coding protocols
• Upgrading of the system-wide applications
It has also eliminated the following problems arising previously:
• Higher traffic levels causing slow retrieval
• Improper quality of the medical images
• Non-uniform data storage
Medical imaging cloud services enables the medical staffs of various healthcare organizations to find the electronic health records, treatment records and medical images in a special repository format. The confidentiality is ensured under this service so as to maintain the higher security of the medical information. It provides the benefits of easier maintenance, higher scalability, reliability, higher performance and increased productivity. The ubiquitous facility provides the medical practitioners, radiologists, as well as patients to access the information anytime and anywhere.
Access to clinical data and treatment tools is much needed for the diagnosis of the disease. The data needs systematic arrangement so as to provide the clinical information without any hassle and immediate. Accuracy and quick retrieval helps the doctors to analyze the different medical conditions of the patients and find solutions to them. Even previous case histories and their treatment give greater idea to the doctor to how to treat the patients.
Medical imaging cloud services have augmented the facility of circulation of medical information in the healthcare organizations worldwide. Practitioners would be able to find the medicinal research and analysis much simpler than it used to be. Also medical referral is possible due to these services. And moreover the user needs to have concern about own requirements rather than the infrastructure set ups. Cost reductions due to no extra add-on training on these technologies.
Medicinal field and technology has achieved pinnacles in better treatment of patients and it would get much advanced with the recent innovations and techniques.
Information obtained from Computer-Aided Diagnosis helps in extremely serious medical conditions like tumors, cancer, coronary diseases, etc. Some of the CAD applications are like mammography, multi-modality for breast cancer, Chest radiography and thoracic Computer tomography for lung cancer and other different ailments. CAD system helps in the detection as well as characterization of the ailment. The imaging involves pre-processing, image-segmentation, feature selection and other steps enabling better detection facility.
Medical image quantification is beneficial for both diagnostic as well as clinical trial applications. It detects the biological activity due to the drug effects. It has simplified the efforts required for medical analysis of a particular condition of the patient. Medical image quantification has provided the boon to healthcare organizations for personalized treatment of the patient based on appropriate and analyzed evidence of the medical condition. This results in reduced complicated effects during and after the surgeries as well as reduced the post effects. Also the treatment effects on the diseases can be evaluated.
The imaging technique actually helps the doctors for risk assessment, prevention to serious medical conditions, early detection, decision-making and monitoring the prevailing as well as future situations. It has proved fruitful for drug treatment and guidance to therapeutic procedures that could be followed.
Medical imaging researches are focusing their efforts through information and communication technology to provide radiologists and nuclear physicians with optimum results forming decision support systems for them. They are also working on improved medical image quantification results and biomarkers.
