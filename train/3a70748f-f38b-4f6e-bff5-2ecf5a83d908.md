The room hummed with the quiet pulse of unseen machines, the air thick with the weight of unspoken words. Daniel sat on the edge of his chair, fingers tracing the grooves of his worn leather jacket. The meeting’s last speaker had paused, eyes locked onto him, then simply stared until the silence stretched on like a question. His breath hitched.

He exhaled slowly, letting the words die in his throat.

The door creaked open. "Daniel?" The voice was familiar but distant. "You look like you’ve been waiting for something."

Silence.

The room exhaled with him. The others shifted, murmured, but none spoke. Not yet. Not until they knew.
