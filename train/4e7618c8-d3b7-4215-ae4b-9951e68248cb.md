A fluorescent-lit factory floor hummed with machinery, pipes hissing as workers in hard hats moved methodically between conveyor belts. The air smelled of oil and stale coffee. Then—

A woman in a high-waisted apron burst through the glass doors, laughing uncontrollably, her face flushed, one hand clutching her stomach. She stumbled, then doubled over, hands on knees, gasping.

Her colleague, a grizzled man in a stained overalls, froze mid-lift. The others froze. The laughter stopped abruptly, replaced by a stunned silence.

She wiped tears, then turned to them, grinning like a fool.

*"I—I just saw my *own reflection* in the door frame,"* she wheezed, still giggling.

The machine operator’s eyes widened. *"What the hell was that?"*

She gasped, *"I—I *tripped*!"* then burst into laughter again, this time at the absurdity of it all.
