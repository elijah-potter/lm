A dimly lit room hums with the steady pulse of a single candle flickering against a cracked porcelain vase. The air smells faintly of old books and the faint metallic tang of damp. A figure sits at a worn desk, fingers tracing the edge of an open journal, its pages blank except for the occasional faded doodle.

The door creaks open slowly, barely audible. A silhouette steps inside, cloaked in shadows. They move without haste, like a ghost in a house that hasn’t been lived in for decades. The candle’s flame dances, casting long, shifting shadows across the walls.

*"You’re here,"* the figure murmurs, voice a whisper. *"For how long?"*

Silence. The journal lies open, blank again, its edges worn smooth from countless turns. The candle’s light flickers out. The only sound is the slow, deliberate breathing of the room.
