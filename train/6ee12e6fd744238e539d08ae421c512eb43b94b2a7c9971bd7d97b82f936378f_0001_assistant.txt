Once upon a time, there were two dogs, by the name of Juliette and Charlie. They were best friends. Charlie was a miniature pinscher, and Juliette was a mixed chihuahua. Charlie had been abandoned as a puppy, and a loving woman found him on a street corner, where she took him in, cleaned him up, and brought him into an animal shelter. Juliette was a stray dog, and had a rough life her first year, but also was found by a kind stranger and taken to an animal shelter. 

Juliette and Charlie found their way to a loving foster home and soon became best friends. Juliette taught Charlie how to behave properly, and Charlie, in turn, taught Juliette how to play and stick out her tongue. Charlie was a good dog but often enjoyed sliding along the floor and licking everything in sight, including his new owner, and Juliette. Juliette enjoyed rolling around and pretending to be a small racing car, practicing drifting on the carpet. Everyone lived happily ever after.
