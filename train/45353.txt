What is VO2 Max?
VO2 max is the maximum volume of oxygen that an individual can consume during an exercise bout. VO2 Max is also known as Maximal Oxygen Consumption (MOC or MAX). It is an indication of the maximum exercise capacity an individual can achieve.
VO2 max is the maximum volume of oxygen consumed by the body in the process of oxidative phosphorylation. VO2 max is a strong predictor of endurance performance and is the most predictive indicator of which physical test would provide a good measure of cardiovascular and pulmonary fitness.
