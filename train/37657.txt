Lionhead rabbits are one of the smaller breeds of rabbit. They are very fluffy, and as their name suggests they have a mane much like that of a lion! Lionhead rabbits generally tend to live between 5 and 9 years. Lionhead rabbits are incredibly social and playful animals which is why they are one of the most popular breeds of rabbit that are kept as pets. Rabbits in general have great hearing and vision which is used to protect themselves from predators in the wild. In the wild, rabbits live in burrows which are known as warrens.
