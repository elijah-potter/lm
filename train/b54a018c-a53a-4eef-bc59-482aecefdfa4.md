A student raised their hand during a professor’s impromptu lecture on quantum computing, only to be interrupted mid-sentence by the assistant, who muttered, *"Just now?"* The professor’s pen paused mid-air as they turned toward the student, eyebrows raised. *"Was this about the midterm review?"* they asked, voice tinged with exasperation. The student, flustered, stammered, *"Uh, yeah… but—"*—realizing too late that the lecture had already shifted to a tangential discussion of topological insulators. The assistant smirked, *"Careful, you’re gonna miss the part where we talk about Majorana fermions."*
