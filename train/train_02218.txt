Mrs. Taylor bought two smart televisions that cost $650 each. If the total sales price had a 25% discount, how much did Mrs. Taylor pay for the two televisions?
The total cost of the two smart televisions is $650 x 2 = $<<650*2=1300>>1300.
The total discount of the two smart televisions is $1300 x 25/100 = $<<1300*25/100=325>>325
Mrs. Taylor paid $1300 - $325 = $<<1300-325=975>>975 for the two smart televisions.
