Spot the dog didn't always love his spots. None of the other dogs in his neighborhood looked like him, and it made him feel strange. He thought dogs and their owners were staring at him on walks because they were making fun of him. 

Spot's mom realized he was a little down after one of their afternoon walks. 

"Spot, our neighbors look at you and smile because you're adorable," Spot's mom said. "Your spots make you unique and special!" 

This made Spot perk up. He started to walk with more confidence, and his tail started to wag a little bit more. Spot did look different than other dogs in the neighborhood, but this wasn't a bad thing. It's part of what makes Spot amazing!
