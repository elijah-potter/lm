Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big, happy garden, there lived a curious bunny named Benny.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside and saw a shiny, golden flower that sparkled in the sunlight. "Wow!" he said, stopping to smell it. The flower had petals that changed colors—sometimes pink, sometimes blue—and it smelled like rainbows!

Benny wanted to grow it, so he carefully dug a little hole in the dirt. He planted the flower and watered it with his tiny paws. Soon, tiny green leaves started to grow, and soon after, the flower bloomed even bigger, brighter, and shinier than before.

Just then, Benny heard a rustling in the bushes. It was his best friend, a little mouse named Max, who had been watching Benny all day. "Benny," Max whispered, "did you hear that? The flowers are talking!"

Benny gasped. "They are? But how?!"

Max giggled. "They’re showing us their secrets! Look—this petal is telling us to be brave, and this one says to share our toys with others!"

Benny’s ears perked up. He knew he should share his new favorite toy—a ball he had found in the garden. He handed it to Max, who rolled it down a tiny hill in the garden.

The flowers cheered, and their petals swayed happily. "That was wonderful!" they sang.

From that day on, Benny and Max spent every afternoon planting and watching their garden grow. The flowers told them stories every night, and Benny learned that kindness, sharing, and listening to friends made the world brighter.

And so, Benny and Max became the happiest friends in the garden, growing their own little magic there forever.
