Media that characterizes prison inmates as bad people who cannot be rehabilitated can have implications for policies such as tough-on-crime measures and the No Frills Prison Act. However, portraying inmates as people who want to better themselves and regret their actions could lead to support for reducing sentences and creating educational programs within prisons. Viewers may see racial division concerning gangs but not be aware of programs that bring the groups together. They may also see the common trope of rape in prison but may not realize the issues with making a joke out of it. Viewers need to see prisoners as humans and not focus on the overrepresentation of violence shown in these programs.
