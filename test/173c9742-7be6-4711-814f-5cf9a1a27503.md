Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a tiny mouse named Milo. Milo loved exploring every corner of his home—peeking under the table where crumbs were hidden, listening to the rustling leaves outside, and chasing his favorite ball of yarn around his bedroom.

One sunny afternoon, Milo spotted something shiny near the window. It was a tiny, golden key! He picked it up and whispered, *"Maybe this key will unlock a secret door!"*

Just then, a gentle breeze carried a voice from the garden: *"Little explorer, come see my flower garden!"* Milo grinned and followed the sound. The garden was full of colorful flowers, humming bees, and a tiny butterfly who fluttered over to him.

"Follow me!" said the butterfly, and Milo tiptoed after it. The garden led to a magical tree with leaves that sparkled like stars. Inside, Milo found a basket filled with edible treats: juicy strawberries, sweet honey, and a big, juicy apple.

Milo picked the apple and bit into it—*pop!*—the apple turned into a juicy, red watermelon! He laughed and ran back to his house, where his family was waiting. They all cheered, "Milo, what did you find?"

"Just a little adventure!" Milo said, holding up the golden key. His family hugged him, and Milo knew he’d find even more magic tomorrow.
