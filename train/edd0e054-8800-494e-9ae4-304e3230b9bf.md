The weight of the decision pressed against my chest like a stone. Every path split before me, each branch whispering possibilities—some luring with warmth, others with the cold promise of silence. The coffee cup sat half-empty on the counter, its steam curling into the dim room like a question mark. Outside, the city hummed, oblivious to my paralysis. Was this the right moment, or the wrong one? The clock’s hands moved relentlessly, but my mind refused to settle.

I reached for my phone, fingers trembling, and scrolled through the messages I’d meant to reply to. *"When?"* the email said, urgent. *"We’re leaving in twenty."* My stomach clenched. The answer burned in my throat, but the words refused to form. Should I choose the comfort of the familiar, or the unknown thrill of a bold move? The air felt thick with choices, each one a puzzle with half the pieces missing. What if I got it wrong? What if I got it right? The silence between us was louder than any argument ever could be.

I exhaled slowly, the breath leaving me as decisively as the answer I’d just found—too late. The decision was made.
