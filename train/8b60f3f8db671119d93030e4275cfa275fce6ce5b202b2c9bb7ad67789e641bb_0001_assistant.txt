Chickens, unlike most other birds, cannot fly. Nevertheless, they are not completely flightless. The world record for the longest flight made by a chicken without any cliffs or hills involved was 13 seconds and the bird covered a distance of 92 meters. While chickens may not be able to fly like other birds, unlike ostriches and penguins they are not completely useless in the air. However, their ability in the air is very limited. Chickens tend to crash land, and therefore, they are susceptible to broken wings and legs.
