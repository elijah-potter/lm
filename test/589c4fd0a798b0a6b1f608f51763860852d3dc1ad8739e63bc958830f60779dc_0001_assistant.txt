Adam's alarm sounds with an earsplitting ring. He turns over and tries to snooze it, not awake enough to realize he turned it off. An hour later, he jolts up, already realizing what he had done. He gets ready for work as quickly as possible and heads to the coffee shop. There he is greeted by his French boss, "Hé, tu es encore en retard Adam!" Without missing a beat Adam says, "Je suis désolé d'avoir manqué mon alarme." Then they both gasp. Greg, his boss, says in surprise," When did you learn to speak French?" Adam responds, "I'm not sure I didn't think I knew how!" 

His boss brought Adam to his wife, who worked in the kitchen, and asked him to speak with her because she only knew French. He had a conversation with her for almost 30 minutes, and then all of a sudden, his French became more broken. Greg looked at Adam, very confused. "What happened? She just asked when you were going to be working next?" Adam looked at his wife, Marie, and tried responding in French, but nothing came to mind. He told Greg to tell her tomorrow and excused himself to the restroom.

Adam was very confused by these events and was determined to discover why. He conducted experiments speaking with Greg and Marie for the next three days. He didn't speak after he woke up until he got to the coffee shop. He then timed their conversations, and together they found he could only understand and speak French for one hour. He was very curious to try other languages, so after three days, he asked for a week off, and they were more than understanding. 

He went out on the first day of his week off and talk to ask many people in different languages ask he could. When the hour was up, he felt sad he could no longer speak with such a variety of people. Adam went back home and sat on his couch, pondering his day. He concluded after hours of thought that this was a gift. He would use this to learn about people about their cultures and help as many as he could.
