Developing future-ready skills can begin in the earliest education experiences. Young
children learn naturally through exploration, play, and construction – critical behaviors
for STEM learning. Hands-on coding offers children opportunities to discover how
technology works and how to interact with it, which is especially important in an
ever-evolving digital world. TTS solutions use playful robots that are fun and instantly
engaging, innately promote learners’ budding critical-thinking skills, and are an
appropriate starting place for literacy and communication capacities.
