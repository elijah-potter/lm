Eating Disorder Treatment Centers Provide A Safe Haven For Getting Cured
Millions of people are currently suffering in silence from an eating disorder. An eating disorder develops when a person becomes fixated in an unhealthy way on the food that they eat. Eating disorders range from self-starvation (anorexia) to overeating without restraint (binge eating). The anxiety, stresses and pressures of everyday life can lead to someone succumbing to one type of eating disorder or another. In rare instances, a food fixation can be the result of hereditary factors (such as Prader-Willi Syndrome). The good news is that in most cases, eating disorders can be successfully treated and cured over time.
Eating Disorder Treatment Centers – Where To Start
The first line of defense in dealing with an eating disorder is to visit your local doctor to try and get a preliminary diagnosis of what the particular issue may be. You should also look for some common signs of an eating disorder such as:
- Avoidance of certain foods or fear of eating groups of foods
- Excessive weight gain or loss
- Obsession over body image and weight
- Fainting and fatigue
- Stashing junk food in hiding places
- Excessive vomiting
- Obsessive exercising
- Taking laxatives often
- Late night food binging
- Low self-esteem about personal appearance
An eating disorder can affect children as young as 5, as well as, adults in their 20s and 30s. If someone is 40 and over, and struggling with this issue, it means they’ve had it for many years. Onset of eating disorders usually happens at an early age. Pressuring someone to stop what they are doing, or expecting them to have enough willpower to change their behavior, is often counterproductive. However, this truly is a serious problem that can damage a person’s health over the long run and lead to serious health problems, and in some cases, an early death.
Eating Disorder Treatment Centers – Why Go?
If you have an eating disorder, or know someone who does, it’s urgent to seek out professional treatment as soon as possible. Eating disorder treatment centers can provide both inpatient and outpatient care for those who cannot find a cure with other solutions.
Going to a treatment center is one of the best ways to tackle this disease. Because emotional and psychological issues often cause a food fixation, it’s important that the affected person gets into a comprehensive program that helps them to deal with the underlying cause of their affliction. Treatment programs are non-judgmental and offer excellent coping techniques in dealing with food.
Eating Disorder Treatment Centers – Therapy Options
Behavioral and cognitive therapy are the most common treatments for eating disorders in combination with nutritional planning, and other types of programs designed to change the patient’s perspective about how food affects their life. In actuality, the person who is dealing with a serious eating disorder is really using food as a coping tool to handle deep-seated emotional anxiety, depression and stress. Food becomes either a temporary healer, or a way to feel a sense of control over their personal situation.
A treatment center for eating disorders encourages the person to confront these underlying issues and learn new coping skills. Eating disorders are tough to deal with because the body has a constant need for food, which makes it impossible to avoid. Therefore, eating disorder treatment centers provide a safe and effective way for people to develop a healthy way of dealing with body image, personal stress and food.
