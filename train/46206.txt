2 November 2018
How do we get clean air for children? We all have the right to breathe clean air but children are particularly vulnerable to the effects of illegal and harmful levels of air pollution and need protecting.
The Clean Air Parents’ Network, supported by ClientEarth and the British Lung Foundation, is calling for the UK Government to fund emergency action to protect children from toxic air, where they learn and where they play.
Join us for a panel discussion and Q&A on Wednesday 28th November to learn more about how air pollution is damaging children’s health and how you can make a difference for young hearts and lungs. This event is supported by the Little House of Science.
