IT directors oversee the networking and information technology for companies. They are typically in a managerial role, overseeing a staff of IT professionals who are tasked with solving various problems. The IT director will monitor the network and make decisions on any new technology that might be necessary to keep it current.
Skillset: An IT director's role is highly technical. This means that they must understand computers, networks and everything in-between. They must also have managerial skills in order to handle a full staff. IT directors need to have communication skills, since they will have to relay technical ideas to people with no technical skills.
Education: People who work in IT will usually have studied computer science at one point or another. Some will go the four-year university route, while others will study in a specialized technical program. This field is more skills based than education based, so you can make a good career without a prestigious degree.
Career Path: Strong knowledge and skills are more important than strong school credentials. You will still want to have an education of some kind. Many people begin by working in an IT department before assuming the role of director. This field offers much upward mobility.
