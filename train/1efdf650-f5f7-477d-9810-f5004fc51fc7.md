A half-forgotten melody lingers in the corners of my mind—
notes of a language I once spoke, syllables now dissolved like salt in water.
I trace the edges of a memory that is neither fully present nor absent,
a ghostly echo of a time when the world was clearer, when meaning was a tangible weight.
Some things are only remembered through absence, through the space they leave behind.
The past isn’t lost; it’s just… *fading*, like the glow of a candle in the wind.
