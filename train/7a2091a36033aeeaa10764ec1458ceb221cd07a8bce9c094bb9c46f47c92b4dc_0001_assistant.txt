In December 2018, the St. Louis Cardinals agreed to a trade with the Arizona Diamondbacks to acquire star first baseman, Paul Goldschmidt. In the trade, St. Louis sent Arizona Luke Weaver, Carson Kelly, Andy Young, and draft compensation. The trade should provide the Cardinals with a much-needed slugger in the middle of their lineup. Goldschmidt has been an all-star in the 6 seasons leading up to the trade. Coming off another great season, the first baseman has been one of the league's most consistent hitters. Goldschmidt throughout his career has finished second or third in MVP voting three times, slugging 201 homers and 684 RBIs throughout his career. Along with the incredible bat, Goldschmidt has been a great fielder as well accumulating three gold gloves. For the Cardinals, this trade should add much-needed power and depth to a lineup that desperately needs it. The Cardinals are in their longest postseason drought since the late 90s and hope this trade can start a change in the organization's recent struggles. In this article, we will take a look at Goldschmidt's impressive career and look ahead to what this could mean for the future of the slugger and his new team.
