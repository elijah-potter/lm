**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 14, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, offering a traditional celebration of agriculture, community, and family entertainment. This year’s fair promises a diverse array of livestock exhibits, thrilling rides, delicious food vendors, and engaging activities for attendees of all ages. The fairgrounds will be brimming with demonstrations of traditional crafts, opportunities to learn about local farming practices, and a vibrant atmosphere that showcases the best of Sangamon County.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from Friday, August 23rd, through Sunday, August 25th, 2024. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM on Friday and Saturday, and 6:00 PM on Sunday. Attendees can anticipate a wide selection of carnival rides and games, live music performances, and a dedicated area for children's activities. A full schedule of events, including competition times and musical acts, will be available on the fair’s official website beginning August 10th.

Admission to the Sangamon County Fair is $12 for adults, $8 for children aged 6-12, and free for children five and under. Discounted pre-sale tickets will be available online and at select local retailers starting August 15th. Parking is $10 per vehicle. A detailed breakdown of ticket options and a comprehensive event schedule can be found at www.sangamoncountyfair.org.

**Headline Events:**

*   Livestock Auction – Saturday at 2:00 PM
*   Demolition Derby – Friday at 7:00 PM
*   Blue Ribbon Baking Competition – Sunday at 11:00 AM
*   Grandstand Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters – Saturday at 8:00 PM
*   4-H Showcase – Sunday at 10:00 AM
*   Tractor Pull – Friday at 5:00 PM
