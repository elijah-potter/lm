It was a foggy night along the docks, and I wasn’t supposed to be there. Everybody warned me: bad things happen near the docks after the sun sets. I wasn’t great at taking advice. I do my own thing and deal with the consequences. There was a chill in the air. It had been a long day at school, and I just needed to walk it off.

I was walking fast, trying to get my heart rate up, and I stumbled and fell. I hadn’t been watching where I was going. I had been lost in thought looking out at the water. “Stupid!” I thought. My knee hurt, and my right hand was scraped where I tried to catch myself. Quickly, my anger at myself for being careless turned into anger about what had tripped me. I saw a box about the size of a tennis ball. I brushed myself off and walked over to pick it up. The box was heavier than it looked. No wonder it tripped me! It was silver or chrome, very shiny with no markings. I flipped it over, looking for a latch of some kind to open it, but I didn’t see any. When I shook it, I could hear a faint jingling inside. What was this thing? I decided to take it home and look at it in brighter light.
