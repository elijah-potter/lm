A dim lantern flickered in the corner of the old library, casting long shadows over the leather-bound books. Elias sat hunched over his desk, fingers tracing the worn edges of a half-finished manuscript. Across the room, Mara adjusted the cuffs of her coat, her fingers pausing on the small pocket watch she’d taken from her father’s study.

*"We’re running out of time,"* she murmured, though no one else was in the room.

Elias looked up, his expression unreadable. *"We already know that."*

The air between them hummed with unspoken tension—words too heavy for silence, emotions too raw for speech. Mara’s eyes flicked to the clock on the wall, then back to him. *"What if we don’t have a choice?"*

His voice was barely audible, like the whisper of a dying ember: *"Then we make it one."*
