Sure! Here are some tips to land a job in marketing in your new city:

1. Identify What Employers Are Looking for: Whether it's a college degree, a certain amount of experience, or some SEO certifications, identifying what employers are looking for is crucial to making sure you can land your job. Make sure to emphasize it on your application and interview so they know you meet their standards for the role.
2. Stay Current With Trends: Marketing is an ever-evolving landscape as technology and methods change, so make sure you stay up to date.
3. Brush Up on Certifications: Certifications and online courses can help you to stand out against the competition. 
4. Think of Questions to Ask in the Interview: Never finish an interview without asking questions. Show you're interested in the company by asking a lot about what will make you successful in the role and what they're looking for from you.
5. Follow-Up: If a few days pass and you don't hear back, make sure to follow up. 
6. Personalize the Process: From your cover letter to your interview, make sure you research the company and personalize all of your questions and answers to them. Knowing your stuff will also help you stand out against the competition.
