The morning light blurred into something softer—warm, like honey spilled across glass.
A voice inside whispered: *"You are enough."* The words stuck in the throat, but the proof was in the way the air felt heavier, as if the world had stopped moving for a breath. The clock’s hands moved too fast. By noon, the clarity was a ghost—just a fleeting shadow against the chaos of the day.

The phone buzzed. A notification, a reminder of the future, the unspoken weight of what was never said.
Clarity had been a mist, a dream.
