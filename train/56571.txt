But your child's age isn't the only thing to consider when planning a family hike. Also take into account your child's activity level and physical fitness. For example, a fairly sedentary 8-year-old might flag after just 30 minutes of hiking, while a very active 5-year-old may be able to go longer.
The weight of your child's backpack will affect his stamina too: The heavier the pack, the more quickly he'll tire. Play around with different weights and items — a full water bottle, a light jacket — in a day pack at home to determine what your child can handle. In general, children should never carry more than 5 to 10 percent of their body weight. And strap the day pack onto your child properly to distribute the weight evenly.
