A group of miners in a flooded cave beneath the Blackthorn Mountains huddled around flickering lanterns, their torches casting long shadows on damp stone. The air smelled of damp earth and gunpowder. The tunnel had once been stable, but now the walls groaned under unseen pressure. Suddenly, the ground trembled—the earth split open, and water cascaded in a churning black tide, swallowing half the team. One miner, clutching a lantern, staggered forward, calling out, *"Rules change now!"*—his voice swallowed by the roaring flood. Above them, the cave ceiling groaned, then collapsed in a thunderous crash, sending debris raining down like hail. The last survivor, a woman with a broken lantern, screamed, *"We’re all dead!"*—but before she could finish, the water swallowed her whole. Outside, the wind howled through the shattered cave mouth as the new rules took hold: survive or die.
