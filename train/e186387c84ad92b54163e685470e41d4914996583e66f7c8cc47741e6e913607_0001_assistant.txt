Here are 3 lesser-known places to visit in the Mediterranean:

1. Koufonisia - While it consists of three islands, 2 are uninhabited. There are natural sea caves, beautiful beaches, and upscale restaurants. 
2. Donousa - Named after the Greek God of wine and pleasure, this island has secluded beaches with emerald-green waters. You can also explore sea caves with a quick boat ride. 
3. Anafi - One of the least visited of the islands in the Cyclades, it is close to Santorini. It has turquoise waters, and beautiful, rocky hillsides.
