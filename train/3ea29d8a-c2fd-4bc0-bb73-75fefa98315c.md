**Central News Hub - Urban Plaza Square**
A sprawling open-air plaza with tiered observation decks, where a dense crowd of people gathers under towering digital news screens displaying live updates. Neon-lit news vans park along the perimeter, their speakers blasting live audio feeds. The air hums with static and chatter. A large, circular "Live Broadcast Center" in the center hosts a cluster of journalists and analysts, their laptops and tablets casting flickering reflections on the wet pavement from a recent rainstorm. The crowd splits into two factions: those huddled near the screens for visuals, others standing in line at kiosks offering coffee and news subscriptions. A makeshift "Ticker Wall" runs along one edge, scrolling real-time headlines in bold, multicolored text. The atmosphere is tense, with people shifting positions as new reports emerge.
