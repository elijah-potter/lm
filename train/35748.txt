Check out the great job that Kynzi and Rose are doing as they “Read to Someone” this morning. Both girls have their eyes on the book, and they are sitting knee to knee, elbow to elbow (EEKK!) They know that if their partner is stuck on a word, they can use the words “Coach?” or “Time?” to see what their partner would like them to do. Each partner is reading with a quiet, expressive voice which, as you can see, means they are having a great time reading together!
