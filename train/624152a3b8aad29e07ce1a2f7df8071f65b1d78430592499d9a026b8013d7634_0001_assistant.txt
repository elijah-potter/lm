Sure! Here are some unique and delicious dessert ideas you can try with strawberries and chocolate and no nuts in it:

1. Strawberry Bars Covered in Chocolate: This delectable treat combines the sweetness of fresh strawberries with the richness of the chocolate. It's the perfect snack for when you're in the mood for something sweet and a little bit indulgent. With succulent strawberries enrobed in silky chocolate, these bars are a match made in heaven.

2. Strawberry Trifle with Brownie: Layers of juicy strawberries, decadent brownie chunks, and velvety whipped cream make this trifle a showstopper. You can layer this luscious dessert with soft and moist brownies, creamy vanilla pudding, and fresh sliced strawberries. It's a dessert that will make your taste buds dance with joy!

3. Chocolate Pudding with Strawberries: It’s a classic dessert that's been given a fruity update. Rich, chocolatey goodness is elevated with bright bursts of fresh strawberries in a luxurious pudding. The smooth and silky pudding is mixed with juicy, ripe strawberries that give it an extra flavor. It's a dessert that's sure to satisfy your sweet tooth.

4. Chocolate-Strawberry Duo in Truffles: Enjoy this tantalizing treat that blends the best of both worlds – luscious chocolate and juicy strawberries – into bite-sized truffles. Imagine smooth and velvety chocolate blended with fresh strawberries, rolled into decadent truffles that are perfect for sharing (or not).

5. Chocolate-Strawberry Ice Cream: Creamy, dreamy, and oh-so-tempting! This indulgent ice cream is infused with the sweet taste of juicy strawberries and smooth chocolate. It’s a refreshing dessert that brings together the best of both worlds. Rich chocolate ice cream is swirled with chunks of fresh strawberries, which makes it the ultimate dessert for any chocolate and strawberry lover.
