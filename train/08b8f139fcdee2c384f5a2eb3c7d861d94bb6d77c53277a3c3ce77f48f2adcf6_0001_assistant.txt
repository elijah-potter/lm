Good Charlotte, a popular American pop punk band, released the following albums between 2000 and 2008:

1. Good Charlotte (2000) Legacy/Sony Music Distribution	
2. The Young and the Hopeless (2002) Sony Music Distribution	
3. The Chronicles of Life and Death (2004) Epic/Daylight	
4. Good Morning Revival (2007) Epic/Daylight

Good Charlotte is led by brothers Joel and Benji Madden.
