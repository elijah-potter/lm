Incredibly, you are going to Hawaii; here are some recommendations for you to enjoy the water:

• Snorkeling is a great way to see the underwater world of Hawaii. You can snorkel on many of the islands' beaches and reefs.

• Surfing is a popular activity in Hawaii. You can learn to surf at one of the many surf schools on the islands.

• Scuba Diving is a great way to explore the underwater world of Hawaii. You can see coral reefs, fish, and other marine life.

• Whale watching is a popular activity in Hawaii during the winter months. You can see humpback whales breaching and playing in the water.

• Kayaking is a great way to explore the coastline of Hawaii. You can kayak in the oceans, lakes, and rivers.

• Stand-up paddleboarding is a great way to exercise and explore the water. You can stand up paddleboard in the oceans, lakes, and rivers.

• Jet skiing is a great way to have fun and explore the water. You can jet ski in the oceans, lakes, and rivers.
