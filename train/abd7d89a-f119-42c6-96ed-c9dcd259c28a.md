An empty hallway stretched endlessly ahead, the fluorescent hum of overhead lights casting flickering shadows. The air was stale, thick with the scent of damp concrete and old coffee. She sat on a cracked wooden chair at the corner of a small room, her fingers tracing the crease of her palms. The door stood slightly ajar, the wood warped and swollen from humidity. Outside, the distant murmur of voices and the occasional clatter of boots faded into the background like distant whispers. A single clock on the wall ticked irregularly, its hands frozen midway between numbers. She had come here expecting nothing but found herself here instead. The waiting was the worst part—time stretched like taffy, each second a question mark.
