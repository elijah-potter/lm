This is a mostly positive review. The reviewer finds the book authentic in its portrayal of both the good and bad parts in the mother-daughter relationship. They found the food motif relatable and the introduction of Korean cuisine and culture palatable to new audiences. They do critique the initial unstructured chapters but found the story more engrossing once the narrative becomes chronological. The lack of a conclusive ending is acknowledged as an ongoing reflection of the author's life and emotions.
