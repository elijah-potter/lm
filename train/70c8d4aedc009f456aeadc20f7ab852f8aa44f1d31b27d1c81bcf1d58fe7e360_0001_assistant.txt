There are 8 moon phases. The phases are:
● New Moon: During this phase, the Moon is very difficult to see.
● Waxing crescent: In this phase, only a very small portion of the Moon is visible.
● First quarter: During this phase, half of the Moon's surface is visible from Earth.
● Waxing gibbous: More than half the Moon's surface is visible in this phase.
● Full Moon: The Moon is fully illuminated and visible during this phase.
● Waning gibbous: The Moon is still mostly visible in this phase.
● Third quarter: During this phase, half of the Moon's surface is visible from Earth.
● Waning crescent: This is the final phase of the Moon where only a small portion of its surface can be seen.
