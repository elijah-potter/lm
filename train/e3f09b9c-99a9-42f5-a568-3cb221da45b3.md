The sky darkened abruptly as a crack of static split the air, followed by a blinding flash of crimson light. Shadows stretched diagonally across the alley, warping into unnatural shapes—figures that had been solid seconds ago now flickered like ghosts, dissolving into static before reforming as faint, translucent wisps. The streetlamp’s bulb, now pulsing erratically, cast jagged shadows that pulsed like a heartbeat. A distant hum grew louder, vibrating through the pavement as the air itself seemed to thicken, charged with unseen energy. The streetlights flickered out, leaving only the eerie glow of the emergency exit signs, their letters glowing coldly in the sudden twilight.
