A weathered wooden crate lay half-buried in the overgrown garden behind the abandoned farmhouse. Inside, an envelope, yellowed with age, contained a single page with a handwritten note:

*"They’re coming. Meet at the old well at midnight. Bring the iron key. —L."*

The well’s rusted iron door creaked open as the moon cast long shadows. Beneath the surface, the water was black, thick with sediment. At midnight, a figure emerged—a woman wrapped in a cloak, clutching a rusted key. She placed it on the stone beside the well’s edge, then vanished into the mist. The next morning, the farmhouse’s windows shattered from inside, revealing the faces of unseen intruders.
