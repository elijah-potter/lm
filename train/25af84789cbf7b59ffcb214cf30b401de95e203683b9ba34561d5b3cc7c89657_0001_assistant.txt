Captain Helmsworth was impressed by his intelligence and initiative. Officer Hershel also assisted in the recent maintenance overhaul of the second engine and was complimented by his fellow crewmen for his contributions. He is fully qualified for promotion when due and I suspect will make an excellent Commander with related training.
