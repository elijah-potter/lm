Arctomecon merriamii Coville
Desert Bear-poppy, Great Desert-poppy, Great Bearclaw-poppy, Great Desert Poppy
Papaveraceae (Poppy Family)
USDA Symbol: ARME2
USDA Native Status: L48 (N)
1 large white flower atop each of several stalks with hairy leaves, most near base.
Little Desert Poppy (A. humilis), found in southwestern Utah and northwestern Arizona, has four white petals; it is generally less than 10 inches (25 cm) high. Yellow Desert Poppy (A. californica), found in southern Nevada and northwestern Arizona, has six yellow petals.
