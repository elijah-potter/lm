A circular loop of jagged iron, 12 meters in diameter, with 48 sharp, rust-stained protrusions forming the perimeter. The center is a hollowed-out sphere, 2 meters wide, lined with irregularly placed obsidian slabs that reflect nothing. Inside, a spiraling staircase of blackened stone winds from the sphere’s edge to its midpoint, descending into a void where only the faintest echoes of distant chants linger. The walls are seamless except for the edges—each of the 48 spikes emits a low, resonant hum when touched, vibrating at frequencies just below audible range. The air smells of damp earth and burnt resin, and at night, the perimeter flickers with an unnatural, pulsating light that shifts between violet and amber hues.
