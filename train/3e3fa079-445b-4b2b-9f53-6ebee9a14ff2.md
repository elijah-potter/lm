**The Neon Lullaby**

The city hummed a quiet pulse beneath my boots,
a thousand fingers tracing the pavement’s veins.
I sipped my coffee black, let the steam curl like a ghost’s confession—
somewhere, a child laughed, and the sound was swallowed by the glass.

Midnight found me at the old bookstore,
stacks of paper spines whispering forgotten names.
A book fell from a shelf—*The Alchemist*—
and I picked it up, fingers trembling not with fear,
but with the weight of something I hadn’t dared to name.

The page turned. The world outside was a blur of neon and shadow,
but inside, the words were warm,
slow, *alive*—
as if they had been waiting all this time,
just for the right hand to hold them.

Then the call came.
A name on the other end,
a voice that wasn’t mine.
*"We need you to disappear."*

The ink on the page blurred.
The coffee cup shattered.
Outside, the city still sang,
but now it was a song of knives and whispers.
