Calli is a beautiful female Fire Elf. She is 27 years old, 5’8” tall and has a slender build. She has large golden eyes that glow with intensity when she feels anger or sadness. She has a small button nose and bow-shaped lips. Her hair is shoulder-length and platinum blonde, which she wears in an intricate bun, with two small braids framing her face. Her eyebrows, also platinum blonde, have high arches, which often make her look surprised. Her ears are long and pointed. 

Calli wears a simple, knee-length red cotton dress that her mother made for her and gold-colored sandals. She has six golden hoop earrings in each of her ears and also wears a singular gold bangle bracelet on her left wrist. She carries a small brown satchel with her everywhere, a gift from her father that is embroidered with her name.
