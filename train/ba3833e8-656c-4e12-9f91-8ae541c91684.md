The horizon now bends like a fractured glass, where the sky’s edge splits into jagged, metallic veins of violet and amber. Storm clouds, once dense and uniform, have thinned into streaks of molten copper and charcoal, shifting unpredictably. Rivers run erratic, carving new paths through the soil, their waters a murky green, choked with the remnants of ancient growth. The air hums with a low, resonant frequency, vibrating through exposed roots and rusted metal, leaving a lingering metallic tang in the breeze. The wind carries the scent of ozone and damp earth, interspersed with faint, distant thunder. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, casting eerie, asymmetrical patterns across the terrain. The silence is broken only by the occasional cry of an unknown creature, or the distant, rhythmic beat of unseen machinery.
