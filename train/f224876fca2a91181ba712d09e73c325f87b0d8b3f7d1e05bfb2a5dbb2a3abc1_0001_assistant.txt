Luckily for you, Minneapolis has great breweries, and here are some of them:

1. 56 Brewing: Take a load off in their front patio and garden. Try their Motueka Pale Ale, Nose Hair Bender, and 'ssippi Squeeze IPA.
2. Bauhaus Brew Labs: Hangout in an old warehouse hangar and try their Wonderstuff, Skyfive! IPA, Stargrazer, and Schwandtoberfest.
3. Falling Knife Brewing Co.: Sit outside and or play arcade games inside. Try their Hidden Temple, Of a Feather, and Verbal Tip.
