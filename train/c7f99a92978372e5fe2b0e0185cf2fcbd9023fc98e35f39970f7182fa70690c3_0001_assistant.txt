1. Add to a Smoothie - Blend ripe bananas into your favorite smoothie to add sweetness and nutrition.
2. Make into Pancakes - Ripe bananas made into pancakes create a sweet and moist breakfast option.
3. Freeze - When frozen and whipped with milk, ripe bananas create a cold treat that is very much like traditional ice cream.
4. Use in Muffins or Bread - A classic way to use up ripe bananas and can be made plain or with add-ins like nuts or chocolate chips.
5. Bake or Grill - Something amazing happens when you wrap a ripe banana in foil and bake it or grill it until it is soft and warm.
