Indian Rummy, otherwise known as Paplu, is an Indian Rummy game that has a little variation from the classical Indian Rummy. It is a popular card game where 13 cards are dealt with each player and the player is required to make valid sets of the 13 cards to win the game. A single deck is used in the case when there are only 2 players, while 2 decks are used of 53 cards each, including the joker if there are more than 2 players. There are different versions of Indian Rummy; 13-card version, 10-card version, and 21-card version, the most popular being the 13-card version.
Vocabulary used for Indian Rummy –
- Shuffle – The deck or decks are shuffled without any bias. The cards dealt with the players depend on luck and everyone has a fair chance of winning.
- Deadwood – Cards that are not fit to be included in a sequence are called deadwood.
- Discard – After a card is picked up from the open pile or the deck, one card is discarded by the player into the open pile.
- Drop – A player who wants to drop out of the game.
- Count – Cards have points, and the total points of all the deadwood cards in the hands of a player is called Count.
Rummy Clavis –
- Sequence – 3 or more consecutive cards of the same suit make up a sequence. An example of a sequence is Ace, King, Queen, Jack of Diamonds. The requirements to win the game of Indian Rummy is that a player must create 2 sequences, out of which one has to be pure (without the joker replacing any card), and one set or another sequence.
- Set – A set consists of 3 cards of similar value but different suits. An example of a set is Five, Five, and Five.
- Joker – The Joker can be substituted for any card in the game to complete a sequence.
- Number of Cards– All the players are dealt 13 cards each after shuffling. The cards Ace, King, Queen, Jack and 10 all have 10 points each. The remainder of the cards have points based on their printed value. For example, 4 of every suit has value 4.
- Wrong Declaration – A player is penalized by 80 points if he or she declares completion of the game without forming the correct sequences and sets.
- Maximum Points – A player can get 80 points, which is the maximum points in the game.
- Suit– Every deck has 4 different suits that are Diamond, Club, Heart, and Spade.
- Consecutive Misses – If a player misses 3 consecutive turns, he or she automatically drops out of the game with 40 points.
The Indian Rummy game is a display of skill and offers cash prizes online. Tremendously skilled players swarm the online game to win these cash prizes, leaving no chance for the beginners. To win, beginners must avoid making common mistakes that are jeopardizing their game. Following are the mistakes made by beginners:
- Not Arranging the Cards – Beginners often fail to arrange their cards, essentially putting their game at stake. If the cards are not organized, a person can discard a useful card in confusion. It is imperative to arrange your cards according to their suit to make sequences and according to their value to make sets.
- Treating Every Player Similarly – This mistake might seem insignificant, but all the players have different strategies and thinking. You should keep track of what cards they are picking up or discarding and be aware of their moves by figuring out their possible combinations.
- Not considering the math – Your opponents are using this trick on you, so you might as well use it on them. Track their moves and keep a mental note of what cards they are discarding and picking up, and figure out all the possible combinations. Probability comes into play here. If you figure out the pattern, you can block them from creating their sequences and sets.
- Rushing – Take time to play your game and analyze everything. Rushing the game will only trouble you and prevent you from winning the game.
- Discarding the Wild Card – Beginners generally tend to discard the joker without understanding its importance. The joker can be used to replace any card in a sequence and be a complete game-changer.
- Confusing between a pure sequence and a sequence – A pure sequence and a sequence are two different things. A pure sequence is necessary to win the game and consists of 4 consecutive cards of the same suit. A sequence is simply 3 or 4 consecutive cards where one card can be the joker. The joker is used to replace any card in the sequence and comes in handy.
- Slow Decision-Making – As a beginner, keeping up with the game and making fast decisions can be overwhelming. By practicing, you can improve your decision-making in the game and make fast decisions whenever required.
- Playing the Game Emotionally – People can play with their emotions sometimes. In situations when things are not working out their way, they can get angry or frustrated or even sad. Emotions are a hindrance to their decision-making and will further cost them the game.
- Declaring Show Without Verification – You must always verify all your sequences and sets before showing them to the opponents. If there is any disparity, you will lose 80 points.

The aforementioned mistakes are some of the most common mistakes which jeopardize the game for beginners. However, adequate practice will help overcome these mistakes.
