INT. DINER - DAY

Janice enters the diner with a wide smile, her eyes gleaming. She spots the boy she's been cheating on Sean with and sits down at the table.

JANICE
(cheerfully)
Hi, it's nice to finally see you again.

She tucks her hair behind her ear and locks eyes with him.

BOY
(nervously)
Nice to see you too. Are you sure we don't have to worry?

Janice gives him a reassuring look.

JANICE
(confidently)
Yeah, seriously. It's okay, relax. He thinks I'm at home sleeping while he's on a business trip.

The boy breathes a sigh of relief. They both signal the waiter.

JANICE
(excitedly)
I'll have a big steak with water, please.

The boy orders a turkey leg and his favorite soda. They wait for their food.

BOY
(curiously)
So, tell me, what's it like... you know?

Janice looks puzzled, uncertain about discussing it here.

JANICE
(confused)
What's what like?

The boy smirks and leans in closer.

BOY
(teasingly)
Oh, you know... cheating. Do you get the same thrill as me?

Janice laughs nervously, avoiding the topic.

JANICE
(deflecting)
Well, I guess you could say something like that. Let's not talk about it now; I don't want to ruin our time together.

The boy nods, respecting her wish.

BOY
(grinning)
Yeah, you're right. So, uh, are we gonna do the same as last time?

Janice winks and places her hand on his.

JANICE
(confidently)
Oh, no doubt.

Suddenly, Sean bursts through the diner door, catching them off guard.

SEAN
(angry)
I knew it!

Janice jumps up, panicking.

JANICE
(desperately)
No, no! He's just a friend; it's not what you think.

Sean's eyes fill with disgust, a tear streaming down his face.

SEAN
(bitterly)
If you were done, you could've told me.

He takes off his ring and throws it to the boy.

SEAN (CONT'D)
Here, you might need this. Be careful; she might stab you in the back too.

Sean adds one final jab.

SEAN (CONT'D)
Oh, and Janice, turn off your phone's location next time you cheat.

Tension fills the air as shattered relationships lay exposed on the diner floor.

FADE OUT.
