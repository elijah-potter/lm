This page is to help new users, page editors, creators, and others to learn the basics of Wikia Code.
- Bold Text
By typing 3 apostrophes before and after a word or sentence, you can make it Bold.
- Italic Text
By typing 2 apostrophes before and after a word or sentence, you can make it Italic.
- Text Size
By adding = before and after a sentence, or word, you can make a new header. The sizes depend on how many equal signs you put in.
By placing a link in [ ] brackets, you can create a link. Be aware, add the "Http://" before the link.
