The crowd roared as the massive wrestler, known as "Dexter," approached the ring. He was wearing a broken pair of thick glasses, suspenders, and a calculator on a chain around his neck. In his hand, he held a chemistry textbook. When he stepped into the ring, he ripped the massive textbook in half. 

Dexter’s opponent, a hulking brute called "The Enforcer," laughed and pointed as he saw Dexter. "What's this?" he said. "A geek trying to be a wrestler? You think you can beat me down?"
Dexter smirked and pulled on his suspenders. "That's right," he said. "I'm gonna show you ALL that nerds can be tough too."

Dexter began a moving monologue. "I'm not like the other wrestlers," he said. "I spend hours at the library, working my fingers to the bone programming, and getting wrapped up in equations. But that doesn't mean I can't be tough. I'm not gonna be shoved into a locker!" 

Dexter then paused and looked at The Enforcer. "You're underestimating me," he said. "But I am gonna show you that nerds can be as strong, and just as brave as anyone else."
Dexter then charged at his opponent, swinging his calculator like a medieval flair. The Enforcer was caught off guard and defeated in just one round. Dexter had won the match, and he had proven his point.
