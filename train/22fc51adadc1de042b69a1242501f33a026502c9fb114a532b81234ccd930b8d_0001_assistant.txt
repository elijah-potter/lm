"Don't get mad at me just because you peaked in high school," Johnny said. 
Lawrence wasn't happy at that and turned red in the face, trying to find a quick retort and settling on, "Oh yeah, this coming from someone who's never going to peak in the first place." 
He expected Johnny to turn angry, but Lawrence was disappointed to find him looking confused, confusion then giving way to laughter. 
"Thanks, buddy! That's one of the nicest things I've heard. The only way I can never peak is if everything keeps going up for me."
