**Local Officials Warn of Rising Flood Risks After Unseasonal Rainfall**
*Kelp Haven, CA* – Authorities in Kelp Haven issued a mandatory evacuation order for low-lying districts near the Pacific Coast Highway as persistent storms dumped nearly two feet of rain in just three days, overwhelming the city’s aging stormwater system. Residents along the coastline reported sudden flooding along Crestview Drive and Marina Vista Boulevard, with emergency crews deploying sandbags and deploying temporary barriers at the entrance to the historic kelp forest reserve. Mayor Elara Voss urged residents to avoid driving near the harbor, citing reports of submerged roads and power outages. The city’s Emergency Management Office confirmed that the storm’s rapid intensity, fueled by an unusual marine layer, exceeded projections from the National Weather Service. Schools in the affected zones were closed until further notice, and the Port Authority suspended all harbor operations. Meanwhile, volunteers from the Kelp Haven Coastal Alliance distributed emergency supplies, including flashlights, bottled water, and medical kits, to displaced residents in the downtown shelters. The National Guard has been deployed to support cleanup efforts, though debris cleanup teams report that debris removal is expected to take weeks due to the volume of marine litter and damaged infrastructure. The city’s water treatment plant is operating at full capacity, but officials have advised residents to avoid drinking untreated rainwater. The storm has also caused minor damage to the historic Kelp Haven Lighthouse, which remains operational but shows signs of structural stress. Residents are encouraged to check the city’s official website for updated evacuation routes and road closures.
