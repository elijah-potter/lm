**Local officials in the historic coastal town of **Port Haven** announced today that the annual **Grand Tidal Festival**, originally scheduled for this weekend, has been postponed indefinitely due to severe weather warnings from the **Marine Weather Service**. Authorities cited rising storm surges and a high probability of tropical storm conditions expected to pass through the region within 48 hours, warning residents to evacuate low-lying areas along **Bracken Creek**. The festival’s organizers, led by Mayor Elias Hart, expressed regret but emphasized safety protocols remain the top priority. Local businesses, including the iconic **Whalebone Tavern** and **Driftwood Artisan Bakery**, have suspended non-essential operations until further notice. Residents are advised to prepare emergency kits and monitor updates via **Port Haven Public Safety’s official social media channels**. The postponement comes after a record-breaking storm in March that caused over **$12 million in damages** to the harbor district, prompting a city-wide review of coastal defenses. No immediate cause for the cancellation has been confirmed, but officials urge patience as plans are finalized for a rescheduled event, likely targeting **summer 2025**.
