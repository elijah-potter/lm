Oxygen is the eighth element in the periodic table and is represented by the symbol O. Its has the atomic number 8, with eight protons in its nucleus. In the periodic table, an element that is neutral and has no charge always has the same number of electrons as protons, and as such, oxygen has 8 neutrons electrons. Since the atomic weight of oxygen is 16, and electrons have negligible weight, the number of neutrons is eight. Oxygen, as a result, has 8 neutrons and electrons.
