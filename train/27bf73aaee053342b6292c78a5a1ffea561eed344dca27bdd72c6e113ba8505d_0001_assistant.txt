There are 7 books in the original Harry Potter series:
1. Harry Potter and the Philosopher’s Stone (UK: June 26, 1997; US: January 9, 1998)
2. Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets  (UK: July 2, 1998; US: February 6, 1999)
3. Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban (UK: July 8, 1999; US: August 9, 1999)
4. Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire (UK & US: July 8, 2000)
5. Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix (UK & US: June 21, 2003)
6. Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince (UK & US: July 16, 2005)
7. Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows (UK & US: July 21, 2007)

A sequel that takes place nine years after the final 7th book was released in the UK & US on July 31, 2016 titled Harry Potter and the Cursed Child.
