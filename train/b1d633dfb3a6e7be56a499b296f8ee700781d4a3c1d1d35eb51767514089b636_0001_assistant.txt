Sure! Here are three summer reading novels for women: 
1. The Crow Valley Karaoke Championships, by Ali Bryan - Crow Valley's karaoke championship is more important than life or death, even to the ghosts of its deceased champions.
2. Excavations, by Kate Meyers - Four women from different backgrounds and walks of life must put aside their differences to make a discovery that could change history.
3. Save What's Left, by Elizabeth Castellano - A woman buys a beach house after her husband leaves her, but her dream home turns into a nightmare when her neighbor builds a McMansion next door.
