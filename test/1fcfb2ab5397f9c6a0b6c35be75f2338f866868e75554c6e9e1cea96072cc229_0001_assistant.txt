Here are some watermelon-flavored cocktail ideas:

1. Vodka Watermelon Cocktails- This cocktail consists of, vodka, watermelon juice, lime, and agave nectar. You can also use simple syrup or honey instead of agave nectar. 

2. Watermelon Limeade Cocktails- This cocktail consists of white rum, lime juice, and a dash of sugar with frozen watermelon cubes, muddled together and topped off with club soda. 

3. Watermelon Martinis- This cocktail consists of citrusy limoncello or any orange liqueur and fresh seedless watermelon. Rimming the glass with salt or sugar is optional. 

4. Watermelon Whiskey Cocktails- This cocktail consists of quality bourbon mixed with citrus, peach bitters, and watermelon syrup.
