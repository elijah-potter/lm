The walls curve seamlessly upward, vanishing into a swirling, misty ceiling that shifts like liquid gold. Hovering above, unseen fixtures hum faintly—somewhere between machines and living things—casting unnatural, ever-changing light patterns that stretch the boundaries of perception. The air is thick with static, crackling with energy that doesn’t originate from anything visible. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, moving as if something unseen is pulling them. The silence is loud, a hum of anticipation that vibrates through the bones of those who enter. Every empty space feels like a weight.
