The wind whispered through the skeletal branches of the ancient oak, carrying fragments of a forgotten song. The farmer’s daughter knelt beside the riverbank, tracing the ripples with fingers dusted with ash. "Why does the earth remember what we forget?" she murmured, her voice lost in the rustle of leaves.

A single butterfly, its wings a blur of gold and black, landed on her palm before vanishing into the sky. The question hung between them, unanswered, yet already woven into the rhythm of the world.
