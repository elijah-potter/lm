There lived a tailor, with a needle and threads,
His designs looked shabby; people shook their heads.
The villagers would scoff, and turn away in dread,
For they loved their traditional clothes, plain and dead.

But the tailor was creative, he loved to add life,
He made his piece, whether it caused strife.
He stitched and he sewed, with passion and drive,
To create unique designs that surprised his wife.

The villagers would sneer, and make fun of his work,
Whispering cruel words, behind his back, they would lurk.
But the tailor didn't mind, he just kept working hard,
Making clothes that stood out, with colors that were dark.

The tailor aged, and villagers began to see,
The tailor's designs and their unique beauty.
They stared at them with delight,
At the creativity, that was shining bright.

Now the tailor is famous, known throughout the town,
For the designs that used to bring them down.
The villagers are proud, to wear his pieces with grace,
And they marvel at the tailor's creative space.
