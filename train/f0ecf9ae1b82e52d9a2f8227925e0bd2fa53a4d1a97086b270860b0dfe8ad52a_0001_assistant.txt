- Nioh (2017), Team Ninja - set in feudal Japan during the Warring States period, you play as an Irish samurai tasked with taking down mercenaries and yokai alike. Requires careful stamina management with the added benefit of a timed "Ki Pulse" system that allows you to refill stamina during battle. 
- Hollow Knight (2017), Team Cherry - throws you into the world of Hallownest, a fallen kingdom overrun by malicious creatures. As you progress through the game, you will slowly uncover and learn the history behind Hallownest. Features nail-bitingly difficult boss battles and challenging-but-fair combat.
- Salt and Sanctuary (2016), Ska Studios - directly inspired by Dark Souls, Salt and Sanctuary takes the formula and combines it with the Metroidvania genre. Features hand-drawn graphics and tight combat, along with a brooding and atmospheric world that begs to be explored.
