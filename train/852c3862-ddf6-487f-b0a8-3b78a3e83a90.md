A hollow space where the body once stood,
a clock stuck at half-past midnight—
time dissolved like sugar in water,
leaving only the taste of what wasn’t there.

The silence hums with unseen echoes,
each breath a whisper of a ghost’s last sigh.
The walls breathe too, though no one lives inside—
just the ghost of a life that was never mine.

Even the light feels off, stretched thin,
like a memory stretched too far.
The door’s still closed, but the key
is missing—not in the lock, but in the way
the air itself has changed.
Somewhere, a door swings open.
Somewhere, the absence is already present.
