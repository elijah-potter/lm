When the first issue of X-Men was released by Marvel in 1963, the team had five members (not counting their founder and leader, Charles "Professor X" Xavier, who generally isn't considered a member of the team).

1. Cyclops aka Scott Summers, who has the power to shoot powerful beams from his eyes.
2. Jean Grey aka Marvel Girl, who can control objects and people with her mind.
3. Warren Worthington III aka Angel, who has the power of flight due to his large feathered wings.
4. Hank McCoy aka Beast, who has superhuman strength and agility (but was not yet blue and fur-covered like his later, more well-known iteration).
5. Bobby Drake aka Iceman, who can produce ice out of thin air and manipulate it.
