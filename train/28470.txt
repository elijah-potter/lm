|T45.||Literacy By Nativity (Population Age 10 And Over)|
|Universe: Persons Ten Years of Age And Over|
- Other race was calculated by Social Explorer along with literates for all races.
- Other race is the difference between total population 10 and above, and combination of white and white population 10 and above.
- Literate is the difference between a specific race's total population 10 and above, and its illiterate population 10 and above.
- Other race was not calculated for Hawaii, Alaska and their counties.
- Alaska and its counties did not iterate black illiterate, calculated other race would have included blacks.
- Hawaii and its county only have a total illiterate count and other race can not be calculated.
