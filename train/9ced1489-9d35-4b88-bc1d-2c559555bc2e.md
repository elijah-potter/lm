The sky darkened abruptly, casting eerie shadows across the crumbling stone bridge. A sudden, cold wind howled, bending the last remaining willow trees into jagged silhouettes. The streetlamps flickered, their light scattering into streaks of amber and violet as static crackled between them. A distant thunderclap split the air, followed by the sound of rushing water—somewhere, the river had suddenly turned violent. The air smelled of ozone and damp earth. A single, glowing ember from an abandoned lantern danced unsteadily in the alley below, its light barely piercing the gloom.
