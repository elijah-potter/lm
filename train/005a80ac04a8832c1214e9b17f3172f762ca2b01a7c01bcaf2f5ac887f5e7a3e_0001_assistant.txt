B.A.R.K., or the University of British Columbia's "Building Academic Retention through K9s" is a program established at this university's Okanagan campus in 2012. The main focus was to overall reduce stress, reduce homesickness, and to develop personal relationships with these canines. 

With the increase of online courses, B.A.R.K. ran several trials to see if therapy dogs would make an impact somehow in online classes.

The results from these trials showed many outstanding benefits for students and their mental health, including;
- Reduced feelings of Anxiety.
- Reduced feelings of loneliness.
- Improved connections with other classmates.

These results have come from self-reports of well-being from the students.
