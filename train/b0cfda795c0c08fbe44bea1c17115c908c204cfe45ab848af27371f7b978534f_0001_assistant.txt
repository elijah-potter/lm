The trio descended on the remote lakeside mountain locale to investigate reports of strange activity, coming to a stop within the mysterious zone when their car and electronics ceased working. There was a purple hue over the landscape and everything seemed to shimmer in the distance as if someone had put a filter over reality. A glance at the water revealed it was perfectly still, not moving with the wind and remaining undisturbed even when you threw a rock into its depths. A keen eye watching the mountain would see smoke and flashes of light hinted at from deep, narrow channels that couldn't have occurred naturally. Fierce locals who insistently protected their land and kept interlopers out were suddenly nowhere to be seen. Something was deeply wrong here, and it was up to these three to find out.
