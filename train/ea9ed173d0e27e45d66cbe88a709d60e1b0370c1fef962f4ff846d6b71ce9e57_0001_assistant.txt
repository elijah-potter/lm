In order for a storm to be classified as a hurricane, it must meet these conditions:
- Tropical storm: The storm must be a tropical storm.
- Low-pressure: The storm must have a low-pressure system. 
- Tropical wind patterns: Over the equator, tropical wind patterns must be present.
- Precipitation: A moist environment and precipitation must be involved. 
- Warm temperatures: There must be warm temperatures over the ocean. 
- High wind speed: Winds must reach a constant speed of at least 74 miles per hour.
