On a hot summer day in the African savannah, a tiger named Joel walked up to his lion friend Zeke and moaned in pain. Zeke looked at Joel and asked him what was wrong. The tiger opened his mouth wide, baring his fangs, and pointed at one that looked different than the rest.

"Oh no, your tooth looks like it hurts, Joel!" Zeke said to the tiger. "I think you have a piece of food stuck, but don't worry. I can help you."

Zeke took Joel over to the edge of the watering pond and handed him a toothbrush and toothpaste. "Use this, my friend. It tastes good and will make your teeth feel better."

Joel took the toothbrush from Zeke and held it in his paw curiously, giving it a quick sniff for safety before putting it in his mouth. He gave a quick grin to his friend at the welcomed flavor. The bubbles made his tongue tingle as he brushed his teeth up and down. After he finished, Zeke took a big drink of water to swish out the suds and smiled the biggest smile in the world.

"How do you feel now?" asked Zeke.

"I feel roaring good! Wow, that was incredible! I am going to brush every day from now on. Thank you!" Joel replied.

Joel laughed and hugged his friend. And in return, Zeke hugged Joel back and bowed his head in respectful thanks.
