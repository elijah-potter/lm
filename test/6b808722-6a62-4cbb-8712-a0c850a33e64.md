Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny bunny named Pip. Pip loved to hop, jump, and explore every day. One morning, as Pip was munching on clover, he saw a shiny red ball rolling toward him. He picked it up and realized it was a new friend—a little ball that sparkled in the sun.

Pip decided to play with his new friend. He rolled the ball up and down the hill, laughing as it bounced. Then, he spotted a watering can and poured some water over the ball, making it wiggle and dance. The ball seemed to giggle, "Thank you, Pip!"

Just then, a big storm rolled in with dark clouds. Pip’s new friend the ball hid under a bush, trembling. Pip knew he had to help. He dug a tiny tunnel with his paws and hid inside, protecting his friend from the rain. When the storm passed, the sun came out, and Pip and his ball played together again, happier than ever.

From that day on, Pip and his sparkly ball were best friends, always hopping and giggling under the big, beautiful sky.
