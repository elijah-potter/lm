Once there was a little bunny named Pip who loved to hop around the meadow every morning. One sunny day, Pip saw a tiny acorn roll downhill near his burrow. "Mmm, what a tasty acorn!" Pip thought. He picked it up and nibbled just a little bit, but then he realized he was hungry for more. Pip dug a hole with his paws and buried the acorn. He came back later with a bigger snack—some fresh grass—and shared it with his friends, a group of chirping birds and a sleepy old hedgehog.

The next morning, Pip remembered the buried acorn. He dug carefully and found it! But this time, he planted it in the dirt. Pip watered it with his dew droplets and waited patiently. Soon, a little green shoot poked out! Pip cheered and watered it again. Soon, a bright yellow flower bloomed above the ground. The meadow birds sang and the hedgehog snuggled nearby, watching happily. Pip was so proud of his little garden that he shared the flower with all his friends.

The next day, the flower turned into a big, fat acorn! Pip buried it carefully, and when he came back, he planted it too. "Maybe one day this will grow into another flower!" Pip giggled. He watched it grow, and soon, tiny leaves started to sprout. Pip kept watering it, and soon, a brand-new flower grew right where the old one had been.

The meadow was now full of colorful flowers, and Pip’s friends cheered. "This is the best day ever!" Pip said, hopping happily. From then on, every morning, Pip would dig up seeds, plant them, and watch them grow. And every evening, he would share his garden’s fruits and flowers with his friends, making the meadow the happiest place in the whole world!
