A technician must manually verify each component label in a circuit board assembly line to ensure compatibility with a specific batch of connectors. The small change: the label for **C10** on a newly introduced variant of the connector box is misprinted as **C11**. The technician, working under time pressure, assumes it is a typo and continues without rechecking, leading to a short circuit in a batch of 47 units where the connectors were swapped. The error is only caught after a third-party inspection fails to detect the anomaly.
