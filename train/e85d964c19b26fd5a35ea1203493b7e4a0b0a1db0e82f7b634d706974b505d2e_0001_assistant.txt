On a date that holds such weight,
There is a price that comes with fate,
And on that moment that I lean,
I must admit, I must come clean.

The boss was prudent in his recall,
Some call it repugnant as he sits on his wall.
"Anniversaries are fickle, a sailor's delight,
And as an employee, you're the company's plight."

Anger sprung under my usual demeanor,
Something inside of me said to get meaner.
That man did suffer a terrible fortune,
He didn't know that he'd left margin.
The hour did near that I'd miss my date,
And what a shame it would be if I wiped clean the slate.

The belongings I packed in ultimate haste,
Bossman didn't know he'd caused horrible waste.
Freedom sprung forth at a wicked-fast pace,
Screeching towards love and away from the base.

Late in the eve, I approached faintly,
Too late to eat as she waited so saintly.
Poems are fickle, though not as the boss had described,
But not to make up for an excuse so contrived.

An apology seems apt, without any burden,
Hopefully, this poem will draw back the curtain.
While I am sure that you've been left in a state, 
I'm sorry to say that I can relate.
My heart you do hold and it does ring true,
Bosses be damned, I belong with you.
