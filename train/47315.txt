What is a light cavalry regiment?
Light cavalry comprises lightly armed and armored cavalry troops mounted on fast horses, as opposed to heavy cavalry, where the mounted riders (and sometimes the warhorses) were heavily armored.
What is light cavalry in the British Army?
The Royal Yeomanry is the senior light cavalry regiment in the British Army Reserve. Mounted in light armoured vehicles, it is a fast and highly mobile reconnaissance force with substantial firepower.

Does the British Army still have a cavalry?
Modern cavalry. There are currently 9 regiments of cavalry in the regular Army, and a further 4 Yeomanry regiments in the Army Reserve. In British terminology, a cavalry or armoured “regiment” is a battalion-sized unit.
