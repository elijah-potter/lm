Soft moss carpets the ground, crunching underfoot like damp, earthy confetti. Warm stone walls exhale heat through fine, porous cracks, their surface etched with ancient handprints and sun-bleached lichen. A mossy archway frames a pathway where air hums with the whisper of wind through tangled ferns, their fronds brushing delicate as silk. Water trickles over smooth, rounded pebbles in a shallow stream, their edges polished to a faint sheen. Shadows dance across a canopy of gnarled roots and fibrous bark, their intertwined textures rough yet yielding to touch. Velvet grasses sway in slow motion, their blades whispering secrets in the breeze.
