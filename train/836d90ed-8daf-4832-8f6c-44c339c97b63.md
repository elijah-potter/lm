A lone figure sits by a window, hands clasped tightly around a cup of cold tea. The city outside is bathed in the soft glow of streetlights, the hum of distant traffic a distant whisper. Outside, a garden fades into dusk—last light kisses the last blooms, petals drifting to earth as the wind carries the scent of rain. The figure exhales slowly, the breath forming a thin cloud in the still air. A single tear rolls down the cheek, unnoticed, as the door creaks shut behind them. Shadows stretch long across the floor, swallowing the space where the light once played. The silence is complete.
