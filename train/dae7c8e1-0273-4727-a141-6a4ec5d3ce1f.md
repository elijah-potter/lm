A **broken promise** left unchecked festered in the quiet corners of the office, its weight unspoken but felt. *"I’ll handle it by Friday"* had been a casual throwaway—until Monday rolled around, and the stack of unanswered emails and half-finished tasks stretched like a mountain of unpaid debts. The promise, once a flicker of trust, had become a ghost in the machine, its original intention buried under the noise of missed deadlines and shifting priorities.

By the end of the week, the tension had boiled over. The person who’d made the promise found themselves drowning in a sea of unmet expectations, their reputation—once solid—now a fragile thing, trembling under the weight of the silence. The mistake wasn’t in the words, but in the absence of follow-through. The real damage was done not in the failure itself, but in the way it left others wondering: *What else have I been promised that won’t be kept?*
