No problem! Here are some thrillers you might enjoy:

1. Gone Girl by Gillian Flynn. A psychological thriller about a husband under fire for the suspected murder of his wife with a twist for the ages.
2. The Girl on the Train by Paula Hawkins. A psychological thriller about a woman who tries to solve the mysterious murder of a man after witnessing a peculiar incident on her passing train.
3. The Silent Patient by Michael Michaelides. A psychological thriller about a psychologist's obsession with a woman who committed an act of violence against her husband and has not spoken aloud ever since. 
4. Home Before Dark by Riley Sager. A supernatural thriller about a family that moves into a haunted house.
5. Layla by Colleen Hoover. A supernatural thriller about a man who looks for a new start with his girlfriend after she survives an attack that nearly kills her.
6. Mexican Gothic by Sylvia Moreno-Garcia. A supernatural thriller about a woman who goes to the Mexican countryside to check up on her cousin, who just sent her a distressing letter.
7. Behind Closed Doors by B.A. Paris. A psychological thriller about a woman trying to escape the clutches of her abusive husband.
8. Before I Go To Sleep by S.J. Watson. A psychological thriller about a woman trying to recover after an attack that leaves her with amnesia.
9. The Push by Audrey Audrain. A psychological thriller about a new mother suffering from mental and emotional trouble.
10. Pet Sematary by Stephen King. A supernatural thriller about an ancient burial ground that brings the dead back to life.
