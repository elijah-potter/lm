**Mayor’s Office Announces New Urban Renewal Initiative in Riverdale Heights**
Residents of Riverdale Heights will soon benefit from a $2.1 million city-funded initiative to revitalize the downtown core, following a month-long community survey highlighting concerns about aging infrastructure, pedestrian safety, and economic stagnation. The plan includes the installation of 15 new bike lanes, upgrades to the historic riverfront park’s drainage system, and the creation of a temporary pop-up market space in the former theater district, scheduled to open next month. "This is about more than just bricks and mortar—it’s about reimagining how Riverdale thrives together," stated Mayor Elena Vasquez during a press conference. Critics, however, warn of potential displacement risks, urging the city to prioritize affordable housing in the redesign phase. The initiative will run through 2025, with phased construction beginning in the fall.
