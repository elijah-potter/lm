Each year around Easter, Cadbury has commercials advertising their chocolate eggs. They host Cadbury "Bunny" Tryouts to see which animal will star in them. This time, they wanted rescue pets to be involved. Along with starring in the commercial, the winner receives a $5,000 cash prize and a $5,000 donation to a shelter of their choice. A one-eyed rescue cat named Crash won the 2023 contest. Crash lives at the shelter Simply Cats, which will use the money to help many other animals. 

Simply Cats' executive director, Patty Cutler, found Crash in 2018 after a car accident led the feline to have a broken jaw, a broken leg, and a damaged eye. He had six surgeries, though his eye couldn't be saved. Now, he is the ambassador for Simply Cats. He is also now known by people all over the nation, as many voted for him to be the first feline to ever win Cadbury "Bunny" Tryouts.
