Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the meadow. One sunny morning, Benny saw a shiny red apple near his favorite patch of flowers. Benny thought, "Yum! An apple!" He picked it up and tried to eat it. But the apple was too hard for him to bite. Benny’s tummy started to rumble.

Benny ran to his mommy bunny, who had a big, soft carrot in her paw. "Mommy, help me!" Benny said. His mommy gave him the carrot and said, "Eat this, Benny. Carrots are yummy!" Benny happily chewed on the carrot and felt much better. After that, Benny learned that sharing his toys and helping others made him feel happy too.

On his way home, Benny spotted his best friend, a little squirrel named Sammy, hiding under a bush. "Sammy, come play!" Benny called. Sammy peeked out and said, "I’m too scared!" Benny hopped over and said, "Don’t be afraid, Sammy! We can play together!" Sammy took a deep breath and joined Benny in a big game of tag.

They ran and giggled until their paws got tired. Benny and Sammy curled up together in a cozy patch of grass, snuggled close, and fell asleep under the stars. That night, Benny dreamt of being the bravest bunny in the meadow, and Sammy was his friend forever. And that’s how Benny learned that kindness and friendship made everything brighter.
