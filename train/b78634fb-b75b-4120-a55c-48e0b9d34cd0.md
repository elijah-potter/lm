Across the cluttered office, the second man sat with his back to the window, fingers drumming against the desk. A half-finished sketch lay abandoned, its charcoal smudges smelling like old dreams. His phone buzzed—a missed call from the field team. His stomach twisted. The plan had been perfect, but now the wind had shifted, and the first responders were already arriving, their radios crackling with reports of the storm’s sudden intensification. The weather app glowed red on the desk, its numbers climbing past the critical threshold.

The older man returned, his coat damp, his expression unreadable. *"They’re already there,"* he said, tossing a crumpled map onto the desk. The second man exhaled sharply, his chair scraping back as he stood. The plan wasn’t dead—it was just waiting for something else. The storm’s fury would force a new path, and the map would be the only guide left.
