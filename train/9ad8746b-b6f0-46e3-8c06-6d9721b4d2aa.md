## Project Chimera: Rulebook

**A Cooperative Game of Scientific Crisis Management**

Welcome to Project Chimera, a cooperative tabletop game for two to four players. You are a team of specialized researchers working for the Global Response Initiative (GRI), tasked with containing and ultimately resolving a rapidly evolving biogenetic anomaly threatening global stability. The anomaly, dubbed “Chimera,” is exhibiting unpredictable mutations and spreading through the world’s ecosystems. Your decisions, your expertise, and your collaboration are the only things standing between humanity and catastrophe.

### Components

*   **Game Board:** A world map depicting key regions, each with varying levels of Chimera presence and infrastructure.
*   **Researcher Cards (8):** Each card represents a unique researcher with specialized skills and starting resources.
*   **Chimera Strain Cards (36):** Represent the evolving genetic makeup of Chimera. These cards dictate the anomaly's effects and mutation patterns.
*   **Event Cards (48):** Introduce unexpected challenges and opportunities, often forcing difficult choices.
*   **Resource Tokens:** Represent Funding, Personnel, and Research Data.
*   **Containment Markers:** Used to track Chimera presence in regions.
*   **Mutation Dice (3):** Six-sided dice used to determine Chimera mutations and event outcomes.
*   **Crisis Track:** A track indicating the escalating global threat level.
*   **Region Status Markers:** Indicate the current condition of each region (Stable, Contained, Outbreak, Critical).
*   **First Player Token:** Indicates the current active player.

### Setup

1.  **Game Board:** Place the game board in the center of the playing area.
2.  **Crisis Track:** Place the Crisis Marker on the starting position of the Crisis Track.
3.  **Region Status:** Place Region Status Markers on each region, initially set to "Stable."
4.  **Chimera Strain Deck:** Shuffle the Chimera Strain cards and place them face down near the board. Draw three cards and place them face up, forming the “Current Strains” display.
5.  **Event Deck:** Shuffle the Event cards and place them face down near the board.
6.  **Resource Pool:** Place all Resource Tokens in a general supply pool.
7.  **Researcher Selection:** Each player chooses a Researcher card.  Each Researcher starts with the resources indicated on their card.
8.  **First Player:** Randomly determine the first player and give them the First Player Token.

### Turn Structure

Each player’s turn consists of the following phases, performed in order:

1.  **Income Phase:**  Collect income based on your Researcher’s abilities and the status of regions under your influence.
2.  **Action Phase (3 Actions):**  You may perform up to three actions from the list below. Actions can be repeated.
    *   **Move:** Move your Researcher to an adjacent region.
    *   **Research:** Spend Research Data to draw and examine the top two cards from the Chimera Strain deck.  Discard one and return the other to the bottom of the deck. This action can be crucial for understanding Chimera’s evolution.
    *   **Contain:** Spend Personnel and Funding to reduce Chimera presence in the current region. The amount reduced is determined by a Mutation Die roll, modified by your Researcher’s skills and regional infrastructure.
    *   **Deploy Personnel:** Spend Funding to increase the Personnel available in the current region.
    *   **Gather Data:** Spend Personnel to gain Research Data.
    *   **Influence Region:** Spend Funding to attempt to improve the stability of the current region. Success is determined by a Mutation Die roll.
3.  **Event Phase:** Draw the top card from the Event deck and resolve its effects. Many Event cards will present difficult choices with significant consequences.
4.  **Chimera Mutation Phase:** Draw the top card from the Chimera Strain deck and apply its effects. This may involve increasing Chimera presence in regions, triggering regional outbreaks, or advancing the Crisis Track.
5.  **Pass the Token:** Pass the First Player Token to the player on your left.

### Win/Loss Conditions

**Winning the Game:**

You win the game when you have successfully researched and developed a permanent solution to Chimera. This requires completing three specific objectives detailed on a randomly drawn “Solution Objective” card at the start of the game.  These objectives typically involve researching specific Strain combinations and achieving certain regional stability levels.

**Losing the Game:**

You lose the game if any of the following conditions are met:

*   **Crisis Track Maximum:** The Crisis Marker reaches the maximum position on the Crisis Track.
*   **Regional Collapse:** Three regions reach the “Critical” status.
*   **Researcher Incapacitation:** All Researchers are simultaneously incapacitated (due to Event card effects or other game effects).

### Advanced Variants & Trade-Off Mechanics

1.  **Strain Prioritization (Uncertainty & Resource Allocation):** When Researching, you may spend extra Research Data to draw *three* cards instead of two. However, you must immediately discard *two* of these cards, potentially losing valuable information. This forces players to weigh the benefit of more information against the risk of discarding crucial data.

2.  **Rapid Response Teams (Team Trade-Offs & Risk Assessment):**  Certain Event cards trigger the need for a "Rapid Response Team." Players must collectively decide which Researcher(s) will participate.  Participating Researchers are unavailable for actions on subsequent turns, but the team gains a significant bonus to Containment efforts in the affected region. This creates a trade-off between immediate action and long-term progress.

3.  **Mutation Cascade (Uncertainty & Strategic Adaptation):** When a Chimera Strain card is drawn, a Mutation Die is rolled. A result of 1-2 triggers a “Mutation Cascade.” A second Strain card is drawn immediately and its effects are applied on top of the first. This introduces unpredictable chain reactions and forces players to adapt to rapidly changing circumstances.

### Difficulty Scaling

*   **Two Players:** Reduce the starting Funding and Personnel for each Researcher by 1. Begin the game with the Crisis Marker one position further along the Crisis Track.
*   **Three Players:** Play with the standard setup.
*   **Four Players:** Increase the starting Funding and Personnel for each Researcher by 1.  Draw two “Solution Objective” cards at the start of the game and choose one to pursue.



### Designer's Note

Project Chimera is designed to simulate the challenges of responding to a complex, rapidly evolving crisis. The game mechanics are intended to highlight the importance of scientific collaboration, strategic resource allocation, and informed decision-making under conditions of uncertainty. The constant need to adapt to unexpected events and prioritize competing objectives aims to foster critical thinking and problem-solving skills.  While the subject matter is serious, the goal is to create an engaging and educational experience that encourages players to appreciate the complexities of scientific crisis management and the value of teamwork.
