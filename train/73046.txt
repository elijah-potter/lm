Making baby formula feeds
By Cecilia Koh
The information provided below is a general guide and is not meant to replace your doctor's advice. If you have any problems or questions, please refer to your doctor.It is important that formula is made up exactly as it says on the can. Different formulas use different size scoops and are mixed with different amount of water therefore follow the instructions on the tin carefully. The ratio of powder to water has been carefully calculated to give optimum nutrition.
