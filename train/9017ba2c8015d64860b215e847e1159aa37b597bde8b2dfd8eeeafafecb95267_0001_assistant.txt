Absolutely! Here are a couple of underground rock songs from the 80s:
- "The French Song" by Joan Jett & The Blackhearts: This underrated gem that is overshadowed by "I Love Rock And Roll."
- "The Ultimate Sin" by Ozzy Osbourne: Searing guitars are tested to their limits in this song.
- "A Girl Like You" by Smithereens: A mixture of poppy rock, with a nearly punk sounding guitar.
- "Breakdown" by Girlschool: Catchy lyrics with grooving guitars and a killer solo make this one a definite "do not miss." 
- "Waters Part" by Let's Active: Before REM, there was Let's Active, a real college-rock band that still sounds great today.
