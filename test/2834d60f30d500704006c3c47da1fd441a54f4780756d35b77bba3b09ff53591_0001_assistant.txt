The three primary colors in traditional painting and color theory are red, blue, and yellow. These colors are considered the building blocks of all other colors and cannot be created by mixing any other hues. When primary colors are mixed in equal amounts, they create secondary colors: green, orange, and purple. In printing and photography, the primary colors are different: cyan, magenta, and yellow (CMYK model). Mixing these colors can create a wide spectrum of colors, and adding black (the "K" in CMYK) allows for control of darkness.
