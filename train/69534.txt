Beta Sheet Definition:
A fully extended configuration built up from several spatially adjacent regions of a polypeptide chain.
Essential Bioinformatics by Jin Xiong
Beta Sheet Explanation:
Several β-strands joined together through hydrogen bonding to form extended amphipathic pleated sheets in which side-chains with hydrophobic residues point in one direction and side-chains with polar point in opposite direction. A β-strand is a stretch of polypeptide chain consists of 3 to 10 amino acids.
