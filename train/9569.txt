Acrobatiq Smart Author Content for Introduction to Macroeconomics is a comprehensive look at macroeconomics including economic methods, policy, and supply and demand. The courseware covers classical and Keynesian economic theory, fiscal policy, federal budget deficit and the national debt. Students will learn how the economy operates and how the government and the Federal Reserve influence economic growth and measurement.
As students interact with the learning activities, learning data is continually collected, analyzed and reported to The Learning Dashboard™, providing educators with real-time actionable insights about how well students are mastering courseware learning objectives.
