Although it seems like there are not enough pork dishes or casseroles, there are some that are very delicious. Here is a list of recipes to consider.
1.	Baked Ziti with Sausage: If you want a wonderful pasta dish stuffed with bacon and pork, make this dish. It has ziti, bacon, Italian pork sausage, tomatoes, mozzarella, and parmesan cheese. Want to add all the parts of the pig, add some ham bits to this recipe as well.
2.	Pizza Casserole: Why not combine a casserole's ease with a pizza's deliciousness? A pizza casserole can include everything you love about a slice of pizza, such as peppers, onions, cheese, and mushrooms. A sausage-forward pizza has marinara sauce, garlic, a pound of Italian pork sausage, and pepperoni slices.
3.	Pulled Pork and Mac and Cheese: Over the last several years, it has become popular to serve pulled pork over mac and cheese. However, there is no reason you can not make this dish as a beautifully combined casserole. This casserole uses two types of cheese, elbow macaroni, pulled pork, and BBQ sauce.
4.	Smoked Pork Stuffed Pasta Shells: Pork stuffed shells are another delicious pasta and pork casserole. You will make this recipe with jumbo pasta shells, smoked pork, breadcrumbs, ricotta cheese, mozzarella cheese, marinara sauce, and barbecue sauce.
5.	Sausage Hashbrown Breakfast Casserole: If you are looking for a breakfast casserole, you can never go wrong with sausage and hashbrowns. This casserole also has eggs, cheddar cheese, and salt and pepper. For a recipe with more flavor, you can use half regular pork sausage and half hot pork sausage.
