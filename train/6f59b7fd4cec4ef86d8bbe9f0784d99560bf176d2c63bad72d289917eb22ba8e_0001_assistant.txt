Beowulf is a highly regarded piece of Old English literature. The poem takes place in the early 6th century, and while the official date it was written is still a mystery, it is believed by some scholars that it was originally written in the 8th century. It did not have a title upon being a finished piece of literature but was later named after the Scandinavian hero Beowulf, and the story is centered around this character and his experiences. No evidence has been found of an actual Beowulf, some of the sites, events, and characters have been historically verified since. The poem first appeared in print form in 1815. The manuscript known as the Beowulf manuscript has been preserved and is dated before the year 1000.
