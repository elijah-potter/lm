Water pollution is the contamination of water bodies, usually as a result of human activities. Water pollution, however, can be caused by natural processes such as eutrophication. Water pollution leads to several negative impacts such as the depletion of aquatic life and drinkable water, and adverse effects on agriculture leading to food scarcity and a negative impact on the economy. 

Water pollution also leads to bacterial, viral, and parasitic diseases. Bacterial diseases caused by water pollution are cholera, diarrhea, salmonellosis and shigellosis. Hepatitis and encephalitis are some viral diseases caused by water pollution. Examples of parasitic diseases caused by water pollution are cryptosporidiosis, galloping amoeba, giardiasis and schistosomiasis.
