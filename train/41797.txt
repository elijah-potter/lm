Kindergarten were learning about Turkish alphabet and classroom commands. Year 1 students learnt about animals, food and drinks. Year 2 students learned nationalities and countries. They have been working on their vocabulary by playing the Turkish alphabets game. Students learned about Turkish Eid, Festivals, Turkish songs and children games such us " aç kapiyi bezirgan basi, tombala, deve-cüce". Students had a lot of fun activities playing these games and painting activity.
Students also wrote Eid cards for their Families. We learnt about the differences and similarities in between Turkey and Australia in terms of celebrating Eid and festivals?
Have a good holiday!
