Sedation dentistry, also known as conscious sedation, is a type of dental treatment involving medication to help patients relax during procedures. This type of dentistry is becoming increasingly popular, as it helps to alleviate anxiety and fear, making the experience more comfortable for patients. One of the main benefits of sedation dentistry is that it allows patients to undergo complex or lengthy procedures without experiencing any discomfort. It is especially useful for individuals with a high level of anxiety when it comes to dental procedures, as it helps reduce their stress and make the experience more manageable. It makes going through any treatment worthwhile without too much difficulty. However, it is important to note that while sedation dentistry can help to make the experience more comfortable for patients, it is not a substitute for local anesthesia. Local anesthesia is used to numb the treated area, which is still necessary for most dental procedures.
It helps patients with a strong gag reflex and induces numbness
In addition to assisting patients in relaxing, sedation dentistry can also help patients with a strong gag reflex or difficulty getting numb. It can make it easier for the dentist to perform the necessary procedures and can also help to reduce the amount of time it takes to complete the treatment.
It allows multiple treatments
Another role of sedation dentistry is allowing patients to undergo multiple procedures in a single visit. It is especially useful for individuals who have a busy schedule or live far from their dentist. By undergoing multiple procedures at once, patients can save time and reduce the visits they need to make to the dentist.
Dentistry with sedation is typically considered safe, and there is a relatively minimal possibility of problems occurring. Before receiving sedation, however, patients must inform their dentists of any allergies and any preexisting medical issues they may have. Because patients may have grogginess or disorientation following the treatment, they must have someone accompany them both to and from their visit.
There are several different types of sedation dentistry, including oral sedation, intravenous (IV) sedation, and nitrous oxide (laughing gas). The type of sedation used will depend on the patient’s specific needs and the complexity of the procedure being performed. Your dentist will go through the options with you and help you choose the perfect one.
