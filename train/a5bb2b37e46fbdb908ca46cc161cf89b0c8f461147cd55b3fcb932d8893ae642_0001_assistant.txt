Lucy was known for being lucky, she always won every raffle, sweepstake, or bet that she entered. If she dropped a piece of toast, it would land butter-side up. If she played Uno, she would always have a deck filled with wild cards. Lucy lived a very easy life, she was happy. She loved to skateboard and she loved to surf, so the beach is where you would usually find her. One day, as Lucy was walking down the beach, she noticed something shiny poking out of the sand. She walked over and scooped it up, letting the excess sand fall out of her hand, revealing a glittering diamond in her palm. Lucy traced the diamond with her fingers and held it up to the sun, she was entranced by its beauty. As she examined it, somebody had become aware of what she had found. A man started to creep up behind her so that he might take the diamond and run off with it. As he got closer, a fish flopped out of the water and landed in between Lucy and the man. Lucy turned and looked at the man and the fish, they both stood still for a few seconds before the man lunged at her. At that exact moment, a shark exploded out of the water, knocking the man down and eating the fish on the ground. The shark then flopped and slid back into the ocean, leaving the man knocked unconscious on the sand. A patrolling officer notices the commotion and Lucy tells him what had happened. The man is arrested for the attempted robbery and Lucy is informed that the diamond she had found was missing from a museum. The policeman brings Lucy to the station and Lucy collected the $10,000 finders' reward. Lucy spent the money on a new surfboard and a much-needed vacation after the commotion that she went through. She spent the rest of the week relaxing in a resort with a smile on her face.
