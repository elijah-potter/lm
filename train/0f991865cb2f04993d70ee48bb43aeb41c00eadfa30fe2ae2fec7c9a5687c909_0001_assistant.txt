His name was Rover. 
And he was the world’s fastest…dog?

In the city of Bonesville, people were quite competitive in racing. They held competitions in everything from potato sack racing to race car racing. But out of every citizen, there was one that was the most passionate about racing—Rover the Dog.

Ever since Rover was a puppy, he had dreamt of being the best race car driver in the whole world. He was quite passionate about speed, constantly daring his friends of every species to race with him. 

“But you’re a dog!” Everyone would say as they teased Rover, “You know, only humans can drive, right?”

Rover, however, was determined. He would search the city all night, looking for broken-down cars to fix up. Once he could find one, he got to work, and in a few days, he had a moving car of his own, and the entire city was shocked!

Neighbors would watch Rover speed up and down the empty street, but even though they were proud of him, they didn’t think he would win a race against human racecar drivers. Rover proved them wrong and won the next big tournament, bringing home a huge trophy.

He was certainly not ashamed of being an underdog!
