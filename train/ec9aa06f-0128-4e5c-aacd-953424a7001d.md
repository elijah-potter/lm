Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the meadow. One sunny morning, Benny found a shiny red berry that sparkled in the sun. "Mmm, this looks yummy!" Benny thought. But before he could eat it, a big, fluffy cloud came rolling by and covered the berry. Benny tried to hop under the cloud, but it was too heavy. Then, Benny saw a little mouse named Milo standing on a rock nearby. "Can you help me?" Benny asked. Milo nodded and said, "Sure! But first, we need to share." Milo and Benny worked together to climb the rock and reach the berry. They carefully picked it up and, with a big smile, shared the berry with all their friends in the meadow. The friends cheered and said, "Tasty!" That was the best snack Benny and Milo had ever had. From that day on, they knew that sharing made everyone happy and the meadow even happier. Benny and Milo learned that kindness and teamwork could solve even the biggest problems!
