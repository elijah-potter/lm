A misplaced package left the intended recipient in a Chicago hotel instead of the planned destination in New York. The recipient, having just arrived for a high-stakes business meeting, discovered they’d arrived early by a day due to the delay. Instead of rushing to a cab, they spent the evening exploring the city, stumbling upon an unexpected local restaurant that served dishes their culinary blog had never reviewed. The next morning, they found their meeting was canceled, replaced by an impromptu client presentation. The coincidence led to a spontaneous dinner with a partner who later became a collaborator, altering their professional trajectory without warning.
