The flickering glow of emergency lights cast long shadows across the cramped apartment. The hum of a generator fades into silence as the power flickers again, plunging the hallway into darkness. A distant scream echoes from upstairs—someone in distress. The door creaks open, revealing a figure in a torn coat, lantern in hand, their breath visible in the cold air. They step forward, lantern trembling, scanning the room. A half-eaten sandwich on the kitchen table, half-drunk coffee in the sink, and the faint scent of cigarette smoke linger. The door behind them clicks shut, sealing in the eerie quiet.
