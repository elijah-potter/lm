The Faraday MuseumThe home of world-changing science since 1799 whose themed museum displays "Experimentation", "People", and "Communication" are spread over three floors. The displays include equipment used in ground-breaking scientific experiments including Michael Faraday's 1850s magnetic laboratory and a current state-of-the-art nanotechnology laboratory.
