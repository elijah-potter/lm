In 1610, Galileo Galilei, an Italian astronomer, discovered Jupiter's four largest moons - Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto. During the 1600s, European astronomers discovered five moons around Saturn. In October 2019, scientists found 20 more of Saturn's moons. The objects were close to their planets, so they gave them the same term as the Earth's moon. Many individual names for those moons come from Greek myths.
