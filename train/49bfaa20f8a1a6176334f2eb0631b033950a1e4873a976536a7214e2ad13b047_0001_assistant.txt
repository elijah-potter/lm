Scientists warn that if we don't reduce light pollution in 20 years, people might not be able to see stars at night. The amount of man-made light is increasing and quickly brightening the night sky so that people can see fewer stars. In 2016, experts reported that a third of people on Earth could no longer see the Milky Way. It has only gotten worse since then.

According to scientists, LED lights are brightening the sky. It would be sad if the next generation of people did not get to see the stars in the night sky. If this continues, a child who could see 250 stars when they are born would only be able to see 100 by the time they are 18.
