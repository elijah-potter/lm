One valuable tip for homeowners is to decide if it’s cheaper to just replace an item than repair it. Rain gutters are a good example. Each spring, it’s important to get your ladder out of the garage and climb up to check out your rain gutters. Over the winter, they can become full of leaves, and they should be thoroughly cleaned out before the first hard rain of spring. 

If the gutters are over 10 years old and showing signs of wear, it might be time to replace them. This is usually a judgment call. It might be possible to clean them out, tighten the brackets and use them one more year. Today, you’ll find products that claim they never need cleaning. Check these out if you’re one of those people who hate gutter cleaning. Some of the vinyl brands now offer a lifetime warranty and never need to be painted. 

Though products like this often cost more initially, they save you money down the road. Of course, if you are simply making a few repairs before selling your home, it’s best not to plunk down thousands of dollars for the very best flooring, gutters, etc. If you and your family know that you are going to be staying in the house for at least five more years, then it might be worthwhile to buy a better-grade product.
