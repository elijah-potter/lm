Sure, Python's `random` module will let you select a random meal from your list every night. 

# Import the `random` module
import random

# Define the list of meals
mealplan = ["Lemon Chicken and Herb Rice", "Chile Pork and Black Beans", "Pepper Steak and Romaine Salad", "Pork Chops and Baked Potato", "Salmon and Roasted Asparagus", "Pasta Primavera and Garlic Bread"]

# Print a random meal
print(f"Today's dinner is {random.choice(mealplan)}.")

The program imports the `random` module, then uses the method `.choice()` to select a random choice from the list `mealplan` and print it in the `print()` statement at the end.
