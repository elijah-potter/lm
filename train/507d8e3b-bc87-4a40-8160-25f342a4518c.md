Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around the garden every morning, collecting colorful berries and chasing after butterflies.

One sunny afternoon, Benny met a tiny bird named Bella who had lost her nest. Bella was very scared because her little eggs were inside. Benny felt sad for her and decided to help. He gathered soft leaves and straw from the garden and made a new nest for Bella.

Bella was so happy! She placed her eggs carefully inside the nest Benny made. Bella and Benny became best friends and spent many happy days together, playing and exploring the garden. Benny taught Bella to hop, and Bella taught Benny to sing beautiful songs. They even made friends with a group of squirrels who loved to collect acorns.

Every evening, Benny would tuck Bella into her nest, and together they would look at the stars twinkling above them. Benny knew that even though their world was small, it was magical and full of adventures.

One night, Benny woke up Bella when a big, bright moon shone extra bright. "Look!" Benny whispered, pointing to the sky. "It's a magic moon!" Bella giggled and snuggled closer. That night, Benny and Bella wished on the magic moon, and their garden began to glow softly with tiny stars.

From then on, Benny and Bella’s garden was always filled with laughter, friendship, and the magic of their special friendship. They knew that no matter how big or small their world was, they could make it wonderful together.
