About the Book
This book has been designed to provide an insight into the fundamentals of Electrical Engineering to students of non-electrical stream.
A good knowledge of electrical circuits and machines is necessary for all Engineers and an earnest attempt is made to elucidate the principles and applications of all electrical topics on A.C. and D.C. circuit analysis, single phase and three phase electrical circuits, magnetic circuits, transformers, D.C. machines and A.C. machines.
This book contains five units. First three units are related to electrical and last two units are related to electronics.
· Everything is discussed in a clear and concise way with explanatory diagrams
· The language is simple and Basic concepts are explained lucidly
· Mathematical details are given as and when necessary
About the Authors
Dr. P.S. Subramanyam, did Ph.D. in Engineering from IIT, Madras. He has vast experience in Industrial (Electrical Power Generation and Control), academic and administrative areas.
He has more than 36 years of teaching experience. Presently he is Professor Emeritus in Vignan Bharathi Institute of Technology, Hyderabad and Director, Rishi M.S. Institute of Engineering & Technology for Women, Kukatpally, Hyderabad.
He has taught B.Tech and M.Tech students and also guided many Ph.D projects. He has published numerous papers in National and International journals. He has authored “Numerical Methods with C Programs”, “A Text book of Electrical Engineering Volume-I” and “Basic Concepts of Electrical Engineering” all published by M/s. B.S. Publications, Hyderabad.
