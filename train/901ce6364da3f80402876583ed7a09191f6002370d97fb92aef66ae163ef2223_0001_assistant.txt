1. Jay Gatsby loved to host _____________________________ parties in his new mansion to show off his newfound wealth. 
2. The child's constant complaining only served to ______________________________ the already stressful situation. 
3. The student relied on the teacher's ________________________ when learning a new topic. 
4. The attorney asked his intern to _________________________ all information about taxes in their files. 
5.  When writing an essay, don't include ______________________ information that is irrelevant to the topic. 

Answers:
1. extravagant
2. exacerbate
3. expertise
4. extrapolate
5. extraneous
