Symposium: ''Common Routes: St. Domingue-Louisiana''
Sat, February 4, 2006 • Wyndham Hotel at Canal Place, New Orleans
Settled by Spain in the late 15th century, colonized by France in the 17th century, and transformed into the modern state of Haiti in the 19th century, St. Domingue has played a significant role in New World history. From European contact through the present day, St. Domingue and Louisiana have been bound together by shared economies, cultural enterprises, and peoples.
