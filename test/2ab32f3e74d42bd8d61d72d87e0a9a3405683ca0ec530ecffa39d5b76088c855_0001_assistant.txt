Cooking can be a useful hobby and a practical life skill to develop. Here are some of spices that some consider essential for your kitchen:

1. Cinnamon - Very aromatic and common, cinnamon has a spicy bite and pairs well with sugar or with savory meat dishes like lamb.
2. Cumin - Cumin is a popular seasoning that is commonly used to flavor Hispanic or Latin American dishes, as well as Tex-Mex-style foods.
3. Dill Seed - Well known for adding flavor to pickles, dill can also be used to flavor meats and a variety of vegetables.
4. Peppercorn - Black pepper is the most common type of peppercorn, but white and green peppercorns work similarly in a variety of dishes to add a bite of flavor.
5. Vanilla - Naturally a bean, but commonly used in extract form, vanilla brings flavor to cakes, cookies, frostings, and even sweet soups.
