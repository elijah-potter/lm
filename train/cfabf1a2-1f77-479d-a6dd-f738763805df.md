**Amazon Rainforest**
Humans rarely venture deep; locals engage in subsistence farming (e.g., *café*, rubber tapping) along rivers, practicing *ayahuasca ceremonies* (traditional healing), and hunting for bushmeat. Indigenous tribes (e.g., *Makuna*, *Kayapó*) sustain cultures through fishing, weaving, and oral traditions. Ecotourism focuses on guided treks, wildlife observation (jaguars, pink river dolphins), and eco-lodges. Mining (gold, oil) disrupts ecosystems, while deforestation fuels fires. Amazon cities (e.g., Manaus) host markets (e.g., *Feira do Mercado*) and nightlife.
