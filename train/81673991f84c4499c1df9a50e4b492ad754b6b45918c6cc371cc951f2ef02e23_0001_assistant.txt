1.	Bakery owner Veronica Clay finds a dead body outside her bakery one morning. 
2.	It’s revealed to be Nolan Pink, the ex-husband of the local spa owner, Gladys Pink.
3.	Nolan’s divorce lawyer reveals that Gladys was angry about the divorce.
4.	But Gladys has been visiting her sister in a faraway town, so she has an alibi.
5.	Nolan’s brother, Todd, doesn’t, though, and he blames Nolan for putting their father into a senior living facility (which is eating up all his money) instead of taking care of him.
6.	Veronica looks for Todd but can’t find him anywhere. What she finds is warnings to stop investigating. This just urges her on.
7.	Finally, Todd surprises Veronica at her bakery. He wants her to help him plant evidence at Gladys’ spa because he knows she hired someone to kill Nolan and is trying to frame him. 
8.	Veronica believes him but quickly learns it was all a ruse. When she confronts Todd at the spa at night, Gladys bursts in and saves her life. 
9.	Todd is arrested for murdering his brother, and Veronica goes back to baking.
