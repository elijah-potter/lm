A dimly lit café in early afternoon, the hum of chatter and clinking cups filling the air. A woman in a worn leather jacket pours coffee into a mug, her movements precise, routine. Across the table, a man reads a newspaper, head tilted, oblivious to the world around him. Suddenly, a child’s laughter erupts from the booth behind them, sharp and unfiltered. The woman freezes, eyes widening. The man’s head snaps up, startled. The laughter fades into giggles, then stops abruptly. The café falls silent, broken by the suddenness of it all. The woman exhales sharply, the mug trembling in her hand.
