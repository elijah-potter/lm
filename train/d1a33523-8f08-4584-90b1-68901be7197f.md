**"The Midnight Lantern Walk"**

Every first full moon of autumn, participants gather in a designated public park to walk under illuminated floating lanterns crafted from recycled glass and bamboo. Each lantern carries a handwritten wish or memory, symbolizing gratitude for the year’s passage. Alongside lanterns, volunteers set up a "memory tree"—an artificial tree adorned with small framed photos or notes from participants, displayed for reflection. The event ends with a communal feast of seasonal dishes, where attendees share stories from their year. Optional guided discussions on gratitude and change are available via QR codes linked to volunteer-prepared prompts.

*Rules enforced via volunteer apps:*
- **No alcohol** (mandatory for safety).
- **Lanterns must be returned** (reused next year).
- **No drones** (to preserve night sky visibility).

*Tech integrations:*
- **QR codes** link wish submissions to a shared digital archive.
- **Audible logs** track participant counts and lanterns via Bluetooth sensors.
