I remember the way time stretched thin when I was young, how hours felt like days and days like lifetimes. I remember the way I used to ask questions because I thought answers would make everything better, though I already knew they wouldn’t.

I remember the way I used to believe in things that weren’t true, how I let myself be fooled by the way things looked, the way they seemed to make sense.

I remember the way I still do.

I think the stories we tell ourselves are the only ones that matter, the ones that keep us from drowning in the truth of what we are.
