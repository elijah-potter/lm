Here are five fruits that go well with white chocolate:

1. Blackberries: A great way to use blackberries and white chocolate together is in a cheesecake!
2. Blueberries: Try a blueberry and white chocolate scone recipe for a delicious pairing of these ingredients.
3. Strawberries: A fluffy white cake with strawberry filling and white chocolate frosting is the perfect way to utilize this combo.
4. Lemons: Try a cookie recipe that uses white chocolate chips and fresh lemon juice for a twist on a chocolate chip cookie.
5. Oranges: White chocolate-covered orange slices is a simple and sweet way to pair these two ingredients.
