After so much delicate planning, it appeared that the unreliable local buses would frustrate his plans at the last minute. They’d stopped running buses to the capital several hours earlier than normal today, and there would be no more until long after his flight left tomorrow morning. It seemed like he was all out of options, but no, missing his sister’s wedding wasn’t an option. What to do…

Honestly, walking wasn’t out of the realm of possibility. He’d packed light, and it was certain enough that he could make the journey within 16 hours on foot. That would be cutting it too close, though, and then he might end up going so far for nothing. As he sat at the vacant lot that passed for a bus station in this city, he had an idea. As he noticed the nearby gas station, he got an idea and ran to the roadside before sticking out his thumb. 

It was a miracle, but the very first driver he made eye contact with stopped and informed him he was on his way to the airport, too. They made the trip together and reached the airport long before the flight, and all went as it should have.
