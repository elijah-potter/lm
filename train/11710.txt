Link to Resources
When you link to Library Resources and Digital Collections, it is important to use a persistent URL. Persistent Links are URLs that connect researchers directly to a book or digital resource by clicking a link embedded in a webpage. Persistent links can be useful when creating syllabi, online reading lists or bibliographies, and other research tools.
