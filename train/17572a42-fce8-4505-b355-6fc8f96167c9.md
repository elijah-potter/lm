A shadow moved across the hallway at dusk, the door creaking open before the faint sound of a key turning in the lock. The last light from the hallway flickered off as the figure stepped into the night, their footsteps muffled against the damp pavement. No wave, no whispered goodbye—just a sudden, final departure. The silence that followed was broken only by distant traffic, a distant laugh, or the hum of a passing vehicle. The room behind them felt heavier, the air thick with unspoken words.
