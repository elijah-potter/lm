The faintest echo of a forgotten place lingers—
a threshold half-opened, where time bends into memory’s haze.
Names flicker like stars behind storm clouds: *Lila’s laugh*, *the scent of rain on rusted gates*, *the weight of a promise never spoken*.
Some days, the memory resists, others it slithers forward like smoke, leaving only fragments—
the shape of a handprint in dust, a melody half-heard, the taste of something sweet that wasn’t meant to be.
It’s neither complete nor gone; it’s the ghost of what could have been, a half-remembered dream that refuses to dissolve.
