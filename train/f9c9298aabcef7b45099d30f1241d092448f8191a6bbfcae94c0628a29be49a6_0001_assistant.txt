Whose woods these are I think I know.

His house is in the village though;

I thank the universe for stopping here

To watch his face light up and glow.

My little horse will get me here

For we both know what awaits us near

Between the woods and frozen lake

The warmest evening of the year.

He gives his harness bells a hello

To ask if there is some other fellow.

The only other sound’s the sweep

Of easy wind and river's break.

The woods are lovely, vast and deep,

But I have promises to keep,

And with a quickness, my heart will leap.
