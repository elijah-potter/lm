Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big tree in the backyard, there lived a tiny bunny named Pip. Pip loved to hop around the garden, collecting shiny leaves and playing hide-and-seek with his best friend, Max, who was a curious little squirrel.

One sunny morning, Pip spotted something sparkly in the grass. "Look, Max!" Pip squeaked. "Is that a rainbow of berries?"

Max giggled. "Maybe! Let's find out!"

They chased after the bright red berries, giggling as they hopped and scampered. Suddenly, Pip found a little basket by the fence. Inside were more berries, and some were even bigger than the first ones!

Pip picked one and took a bite. "Mmm! These are the tastiest berries ever!" he said. Max cheered, "Now we have the best snack ever!"

Just then, a little ladybug named Lulu landed on Pip's nose. "Hello there, Pip!" she buzzed. "I heard you found something special. Want to help me?"

Pip and Max nodded. Together, they picked all the berries and filled the basket to the brim. Then they carried it to the ladybug's house, where she lived with her family.

"Thank you, Pip and Max!" Lulu said, giving them a big smile. "You made my berry basket so happy!"

After that, Pip and Max always remembered to share their treasures. And every day, they would hop around, collecting more berries and making new friends in the garden.
