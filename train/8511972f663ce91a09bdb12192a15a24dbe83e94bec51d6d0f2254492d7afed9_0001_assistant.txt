1. Sunday: The name Sunday is derived from the Sun, a celestial body that holds significance in various cultures. It is often regarded as a day of rest and worship in different religious traditions.
2. Monday: The name Monday is linked to the Moon, which has been associated with femininity, fertility, and tides. In certain spiritual and astrological practices, Monday holds importance as a day connected to lunar energy.
3. Tuesday: Tuesday is named after the planet Mars. It has connections to the Roman god of war and is considered a day associated with strength and courage in some cultures.
4. Wednesday: Wednesday gets its name from the Germanic god Woden (also known as Odin). Woden was associated with wisdom, poetry, and magic in Norse mythology.
5. Thursday: Thursday is named after Thor, the Norse god of thunder and strength. It is often considered a day associated with power and protection.
6. Friday: The name Friday is connected to the Norse goddess Frigg or Freya, who represents love, beauty, and fertility. In certain cultures, Friday is seen as an auspicious day for romantic endeavors.
7. Saturday: Saturday is named after the planet Saturn, which is linked to the Roman god of agriculture and time. It is often associated with hard work, discipline, and reflection.
