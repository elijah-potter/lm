A promise whispered in the dark, half-baked and half-hearted—
the kind that curls like smoke, thick with the weight of unspoken fears.
*"I’ll fix this."* The words were meant to be a lifeline, but the hands holding them trembled.
The clock was a ticking thing, its chime drowning in static—
every second stretched into an eternity of what-ifs,
every "yes" a lie wrapped in the fabric of hope.
The storm inside was a hurricane of doubt, and the only promise left was the one they couldn’t keep:
*"I’ll try."* But trying wasn’t enough. It had to be *right*.
The world was a maze of shadows where the path disappeared,
and the only thing left to do was step back—
not to break, but to let the weight of the impossible *sink*.
The silence after was the sound of the promise dissolving,
not in shame, but in the quiet truth that some things were meant
to be held lightly, not fully.
