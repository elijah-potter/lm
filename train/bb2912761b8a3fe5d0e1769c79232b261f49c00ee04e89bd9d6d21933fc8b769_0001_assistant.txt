On this day in early spring, a deer opened her eyes after having a horrible time bumping around in the truck. She took a few tentative steps out of the truck to find herself all alone in the woods. She stood up tall, stretched her head up into the air and felt as if she was a cloud floating high in the sky. She could go anywhere she pleased, she was free.

She strolled along slowly at first, then got faster and faster with her pace. Then she ran. She ran and ran. She ran through the woods, up the hillside and down into the valley. Then she stopped. The deer was so happy to finally be free again and able to move. She had been in the rescue center for so long while her leg healed from the trap she had fallen into. Now she could explore her new habitat and that's just what she was intent on doing.

Eventually, the deer slowed down again and after a while, she found herself in a beautiful place. There were trees, and there was a lake where she stopped for a refreshing drink. Surrounding the lake was the lovely glow of bright yellow flowers. As far as she could see there were flowers and with the wind blowing they even seemed to be doing a little dance. She knew then that she would stay here forever. She was home.
