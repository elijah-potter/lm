The two wizards, Conray and Norlock lay exhausted, having exerted all of their physical might against one another. The battle would not be determined by who could conjure up the largest fireball; no, this feud would be settled in the mind.

They reached out to each other in the space between, the tendrils of their mind crossing as they raced toward each other's defenses. It was a catch-22; to invade another's mind, the wizards each had to open themselves up for attack. Conray tapped against Norlock's concentration, testing his ability to remain steadfast. Norlock wavered but stayed in his state of focus. He pressed hard against Conray's memories, starting to snake into every moment up to this point, corrupting his virtues and his purpose. Conray recoiled in confusion but had the sense of mind to push back. He severed the connection abruptly, snapping back to his own mind.

He thwarted Norlock's snakelike attack and, in cutting off the connection, saw Norlock's aggression and hate manifested for just a moment in his own mind. Conray breathed deeply and reinforced his mind, making himself impervious to attack. He knew exactly where to strike. Again, their minds' projections raced past each other on the attack, but Conray pressed himself further into Norlock's mind. He could feel the wizard pressing against his memories again, sensing a weak point he could exploit, but he knew the battle was already over.

Conray tapped into Norlock's emotional center and crossed a few proverbial wires, flipped a few switches, and pulled a few levers. That was all it took to send his spiraling rage inwards. He could feel Norlock pull back abruptly, trying to understand why he was suddenly feeling such an influx of self-directed emotions. Conray opened his eyes to see Norlock still withdrawn in the recesses of his mind, trying to cope. The battle was won.
