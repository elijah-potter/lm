A delicate ceramic teacup, cracked along one rim, rested in the palm of a child’s small hands as she carefully held it against the warm morning light streaming through the kitchen window. The mother, bending slightly to kiss her forehead, exchanged the teacup for a fresh cup of Earl Grey from the teapot. The child’s fingers trembled slightly as they lifted the new cup, eyes wide with wonder at the smooth porcelain. The exchange completed, the mother placed the teacup back on the saucer, and the child turned away, already reaching for the cupboard where her favorite biscuits waited.
