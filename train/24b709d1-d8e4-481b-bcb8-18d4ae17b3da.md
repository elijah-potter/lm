A bustling, sunlit village square in a hillside valley. Children dart between adults, laughing as they share stories over shared meals at communal clay tables. Elderly women weave baskets while younger men tend to the shared garden plots. A small wooden sign marks the meeting spot: **"Our Place – Where We’ve Known Each Other Since the First Fire."** The air smells of roasting meats, freshly baked bread, and wild herbs from the communal garden. A lone traveler from the city stands at the edge, watching as hands immediately reach out to offer tea and explanations about the village’s traditions. The rhythm of clinking cups, shared laughter, and the quiet familiarity of mutual glances fills the space.
