You woke up in a dimly lit room with cold metal walls. The air smelled of damp earth and something metallic, like old blood. Your head pounded, and the last thing you remembered was standing outside a crumbling old building, the rain lashing against your coat.

Then the memories hit—your last conversation with your sister, her voice cracking as she whispered, *"He’s coming for you."* The door creaked open, revealing a figure wrapped in a hooded cloak, their face obscured by shadows. They held a pistol, its barrel glinting in the dim light.

"Well," the figure murmured, stepping closer, "it seems you shouldn’t be here after all." The door slammed shut behind them.
