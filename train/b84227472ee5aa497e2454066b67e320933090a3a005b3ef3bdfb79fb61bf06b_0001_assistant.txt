Once upon a time, there was a little boy who loved to dance. Everywhere he went, he danced. This boy danced to breakfast, and he danced to do the dishes. He danced to feed his cat, and he danced to check the mailbox. He danced through the whole day. Then when evening came, he danced while he had a shower, he danced to the dinner table, and after dinner, he danced to bed.  Nothing could stop him from dancing.

Soon this little boy reached the age at which most little boys go to school. On his first day of school, he was so excited! He skipped and danced all the way to the car his mother was going to use to take him, and when they got there, from the car right up to the school gate where the headmistress was standing their hands on her hips and a smile on her face welcoming all of the little boys and girls who would be joining her school.

The headmistress loved her school and loved the day she got to welcome all of her new students to her happy school - because it was a happy school, the little boy's mother had chosen that school for him because it was such a happy school. When he got to the school gates, the little boy didn't stop dancing. He danced straight through those gates and up the steps through the doors of the big school. He danced all the way down the hallway with its posters of smiling children and faraway places. He danced into his classroom and sat in the seat that his new smiling teacher showed him.
