Here are five good choices for trees in the Northeast:

1. The Red Maple- a good shade tree that produces pretty red flowers in the spring.
2. The River Birch- a fast-growing, medium-height tree that's particularly resistant to extreme weather conditions. It does need plenty of sunlight, though.
3. The Red Spruce- this coniferous tree can grow from 60 to 130 feet high and live for 250 years or more. 
4. The Tulip Poplar- a good shade tree with yellowish flowers, it can grow up to 190 feet tall with a trunk diameter of ten feet.
5. The Dogwood- famous for its white flowers in the spring.
