Appetizers 

-Gyoza: Dumplings that are steamed, then fried on the bottom. Stuffed with ground pork and vegetables or just vegetables. 
-Edamame: Soybeans cooked in the shell and then lightly salted. 
-Yakitori: Chunks of chicken cooked over a charcoal grill on a stick. Cooked with a teriyaki-style sauce. 

Entrees 

-Tonkatsu: Breaded pork cutlet, deep fried with panko bread crumb coating. Served with steamed rice and shredded cabbage. Served with Bulldog Brand tonkatsu sauce.
-Yakisoba: stir-fried soba noodles with chicken chunks and vegetables, carrots, and onions. Stir-fried with a special seasoning that coats the noodles.
-Yaki Niku: Grilled meat and vegetables that are cooked on a small grill in the center of the table. Served with a variety of sauces. 

Most set meals are served with miso soup and/or salad with sesame or miso-based salad dressing.
