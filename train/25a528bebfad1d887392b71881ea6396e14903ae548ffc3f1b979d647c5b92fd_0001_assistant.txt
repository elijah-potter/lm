The candy Life Savers has a long history of being a candy. It was a candy before the researchers were born and before their parental figures were born. That means it has been a candy since the beginning of time. Life Savers comes in a roll of five flavors. The flavors are Cherry, Watermelon, Raspberry, Orange, and Pineapple. In any roll of Life Savers, you don't know which ones you'll get. In this study, the test subjects (my two sisters) were given a blind taste test to determine which flavor of Life Savors is the best one. They did not know which flavor they were given. I might have peaked a little. The results of the study concluded that the best flavor is Watermelon. The second-best flavor was Cherry. Next was Orange. Raspberry and pineapple were both determined to be gross flavors.
