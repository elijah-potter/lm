Flora of Hong Kong, Volume 4
Language: English with English, Chinese and scientific nomenclature
The flora of Hong Kong is one of the most diverse in China, given its small area. Located at the border of the tropical and subtropical regions, it has 2100 native species of plant. This is only modern regional flora for South China in English and is intended to supplement the Flora of China.
