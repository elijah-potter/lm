In a floating metropolis of glass and steel, suspended above a labyrinth of neon-lit streets, the *OmniSphere* governs a realm where laws are rewritten in real-time. Citizens interact with holographic justice systems that adapt to context, enforcing rules dynamically—what’s "right" today may shift tomorrow. Public spaces feature adaptive signage: "Do not step on the grass" changes to "Collaborate here" based on crowd sentiment. Crime is redefined as social friction; theft is outlawed, but sharing is mandatory. AI mediators arbitrate disputes, rewriting punishments mid-trial. The city’s "Rule Engine" is an open-source algorithm, allowing citizens to propose amendments. Taxes are paid in "experience points," and dissent is met with reeducation—voluntary, via immersive simulations. The only permanent law: *"Respect the system."*
