Which Kitty is the One For Me?

Kitty one, Kitty two, Kitty three,
all of you look so good to me.
I want you all, but Papa says no,
which one of you do I take in tow?

Is it you, the kitty one, with the softest fur,
you move so fast you're almost a blur.
I love your curious and wide brown eyes,
They're quite big, like saucers in size.

Or maybe kitty two, with the orange coat,
I've always enjoyed marmalade on my toast.
You purr so much more motor than feline,
But I find your rumblings quite divine.

Then there's kitty three, the runt of the litter,
smaller than the rest, but the least bitter.
You dance and frolic, pitter and play,
no frown on your face or look of dismay.

I love you all; won't Papa change his mind?
I'll ask him tonight he really is quite kind.
I'll put on my dress and biggest bow hat,
and say to him "Papa please" just like that.

He'll sigh and grumble but take me in hand,
and I'll whimper a bit and make my stand.
I know he'll say yes. He loves me so much,
Kitties you'll be with me, our bond so lush.
