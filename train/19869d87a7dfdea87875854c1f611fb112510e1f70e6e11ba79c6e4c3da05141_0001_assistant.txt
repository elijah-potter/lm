Day 1: Athens
Visit Acropolis and Parthenon.
Explore the Acropolis Museum.
Walk through the beautiful streets of the Plaka neighborhood.

Day 2: Santorini
Experience amazing volcano views, boat tours, and wine tours.
Explore the villages of Oia and Fira.
Enjoy the famous Santorini sunset.

Day 3: Mykonos and Delos.
Visit Mykonos town and its iconic windmills.
Take a boat ride to the nearby island of Delos.
Visit the beaches and enjoy the nightlife.

Day 4: Naxos and Paros.
Explore Naxo's old town and the Portara.
Visit the beaches and enjoy the nightlife of Paros.
