A quarterly team sync at **Productivity Labs** turned into an impromptu brainstorm after the CEO, **Dr. Elena Vasquez**, slipped into the meeting room unannounced with a half-eaten pastry and a notebook. The agenda was supposed to review quarterly KPIs, but her question—*"Why do we still use spreadsheets for resource allocation?"*—unexpectedly derailed the discussion.

**Mark**, the IT lead, had been vocal about the inefficiencies: *"We’re manually tracking 200+ projects, and by the end of the month, we’re left with last-minute scrambles."* **Sophia**, the marketing director, added, *"Our campaign budgets are always off by 15%—not enough for creative, not enough for testing."*

Dr. Vasquez scribbled notes furiously. *"So the problem isn’t just tools—it’s *people*."*
**Javier**, the data analyst, chimed in: *"If we could auto-generate real-time dashboards, we’d catch bottlenecks *before* they become crises."*

The room shifted. **Lena**, the CTO, suggested an experiment: *"Let’s run a pilot with a tool that auto-allocates based on historical trends. No more guesswork."*

By the end of the hour, the team had sketched a prototype, assigned a dev sprint, and even secured buy-in from the finance team to fund the pilot. The CEO’s pastry was a metaphor: sweet, unexpected, and just what the team needed to turn chaos into a roadmap. The next day, **Slack channels were flooded** with *"Pilot approved!"* and *"Who’s building this?"*—unexpectedly, the meeting had birthed a new initiative.
