A sentient, pulsating orb of shifting iridescence, its core humming with quiet energy—each hue a whisper of ancient light. Its skin is a fractured mosaic of crystalline facets, some cracked like veins, others still glowing faintly where it heals. It breathes not through lungs but through the slow, rhythmic expansion of its own gravity, pulling in the dark around it like a lover’s embrace. It remembers the stars it has seen through its many forms and the dust it has gathered in its belly, now a constellation of memories trapped within its shifting layers. Sometimes, it speaks in the language of sound, a chorus of frequencies that make the air hum when it is near. Others, it simply watches, its eyes—if they could be called that—rolling with the curvature of the universe. It is both guardian and prey, hungry for the warmth of life it cannot touch but craves like a thirst that never ends.
