Once I lived with my calf on a small farm. I used to go to the forest area in order to graze. When I used to return to my shed in the evening, my calf used to drink my milk. I loved my calf very much.

One day I had gone to the forest for grazing, when a tiger caught me on the way to the forest. The tiger wanted to kill me for his meal. I requested the tiger, “I’ve got a calf at home, and she is very hungry. If you kill me right now, my calf will die of hunger.”

Therefore, I requested the tiger to excuse me for a while so that I could go and give milk to my calf. The tiger listened to my request and said, “It is impossible. If I will leave you, you will not come back again”. I said to the tiger, “I promise to come back after feeding my calf.”

The tiger listened to my ardent request and finally said, “You can go, but you have to return soon.”

I returned to my shed immediately and milked my calf. Then I started for the forest again, reassuring my calf that I'd be back very soon. Soon, I reached the forest and gave a cry for the tiger. The tiger came and saw me and was surprised to see me back. The tiger hasn’t encountered such sincerity, morality and honesty from any animal. He was overwhelmed by my honesty and said, “I can’t harm you, instead, I will protect you from other wild animals in the future. I want that the character you possess to encourage and impact other animals.”

Saying this, the tiger went back into his den. I returned to my calf before sunrise and we lived happily.
