A dimly lit warehouse with exposed concrete pillars, flickering fluorescent tubes casting long shadows, reveals a lone figure hunched over a cracked tablet. The cracked screen displays a corrupted map, its coordinates flickering in the low light. The figure, clad in a patched-up raincoat, traces the outline of the map with trembling fingers, the edges of the pixels bleeding into the static noise.

Beyond the warehouse’s high metal gate, the night sky stretches unbroken, punctuated only by the distant hum of a dying car engine. The gate creaks open slightly, revealing a patch of uneven pavement leading to a crumbling alleyway, its walls lined with graffiti that shifts subtly under the glow of a half-melted streetlamp. The figure’s breath fogs the air, and for a moment, the static on the screen seems to mirror the grainy, almost alive texture of the alley’s shadows.
