The award-winning musical, "Les Miserables," the longest-running show in London’s West End, is an adaptation of Victor Hugo’s novel of the same name in which former convict, Jean Valjean, and his daughter unintentionally become involved in a student insurrection. Through the lens of this setting, "Les Miserables" explores various aspects of sin and grace, as well as human spirit, redemption, and faith.
