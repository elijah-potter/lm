Absolutely! Here are some arts and crafts you can do with leftover tubes from your paper products:

- Stylish gauntlets. If you make a straight cut up one side of the tube and decorate the outside of it, you can wear it on the outside of your wrist like a gauntlet.
- Cardboard castle. Decorate a small cardboard box like a castle, and use the tubes to make its towers.
- Quiet windchimes. Decorate a few tubes, tie them onto pieces of string of slightly varying lengths, and then tie the other ends of the string together in a single knot. Hang it on your patio and watch it flutter in the wind.
- Bat wings. For some Halloween decor, unroll two tubes at their seam into diamond shapes, and paint them both black. Then, attach them at the sharpest corners, and attach a line of black pompoms to the middle. Hang these from a bare tree for a spooky look.

I hope these ideas have inspired you to recycle your leftover tubes in fun and creative ways!
