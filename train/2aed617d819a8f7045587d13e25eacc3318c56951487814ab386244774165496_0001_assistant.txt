Penguins are birds that solely live in the Southern Hemisphere. Although their wings stay near the landscape, they have the ability of rapid locomotion in water. They are able to swim at high speeds, but on land, they waddle, run, and toboggan on snow and ice. There are 18-21 species of penguins in the world. Penguins also live in warmer locations, like those that reside on the Galapagos Islands. These versatile birds have varying qualities based on where they live.
