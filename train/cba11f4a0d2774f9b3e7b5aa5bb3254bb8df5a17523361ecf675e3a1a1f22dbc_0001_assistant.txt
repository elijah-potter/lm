Pack your things and clear your desk
It’s time for you to go.
You are no longer welcome here --
This is the old heave-ho.

Pound the pavement, hit the street,
You’re fired, you’re canned, you’re sacked,
You’re terminated, eight-sixed,
Let go, downsized, you’re axed.

You’ve been replaced; you’re outta here,
Should never have been hired.
You’re dismissed, relieved of duties, 
Deselected, fired!
