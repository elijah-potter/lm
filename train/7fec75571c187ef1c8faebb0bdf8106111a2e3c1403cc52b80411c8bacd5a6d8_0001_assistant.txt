A married couple, Kat and Landon Eckles founded the Clean Juice company in 2014. In June 2015, they created the first location in Huntersville, North Carolina. They continued to sell smoothies at that location until they decided to open two additional locations. The two other locations were opened in South Charlotte and Concord. By August 2016, Clean Juice had launched a franchising platform and also signed 5 franchising agreements. Also in 2016, Landon and Kat Eckles claimed that Clean Juice was the only USDA-certified organic juice bar franchise of its kind. As Clean Juice continued to grow, they served Organic smoothies, juices, acai bowls, wraps, salads, and sandwiches.

In March 2017, the first franchised location of Clean Juice opened in Carrollwood, Florida. Clean Juice had 5 franchised locations in operation and another 58 locations in development in July of the same year. By September 2017, the company had a total of 10 operational stores, and in 2018 Clean Juice incorporated the platform, LevelUp. By the end of 2018, Clean Juice had over 100 franchised locations either in use or currently being developed. Clean Juice has grown significantly over the past years and has won many awards such as ranking the #1 spot on Franchise Gator's 2021 Fastest-Growing Franchises list.

Clean Juice is a great place to find healthy and organic foods, whether you are craving a sandwich or a smoothie, they have them both. Clean Juice is a great recommendation for anyone who wants to have something that tastes good and makes you feel good.
