As we head into a few 80-degree days, it's important to remember just how fast the temperature inside of your vehicle can heat up and soar into the danger zone if you've got children or pets as passengers.
Tragedies involving kids and dogs left in hot cars make headlines every year.
The National Weather Service put together a graphic showing a car parked outside on an 80-degree day.
Within 20 minutes, the temperature inside the car hit 109. Within 40 minutes, it was 118. And after an hour, it had risen to 123 degrees.
