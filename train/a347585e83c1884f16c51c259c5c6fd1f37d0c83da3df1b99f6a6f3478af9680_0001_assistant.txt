Of all the cashiers at the McDonald's on 199th Street and Lexington Avenue, Michelle was easily the most irritable one. So it was almost like the universe expressed a cruel sort of comedic irony the day it put Daniel in front of her at the register. Daniel had been arguing about the price of a particular menu item for about 5 minutes uninterrupted with Michelle. She tried for as long as she could to not be disrespectful, but once she reached her boiling point, it was over. She proceeded to curse and scream at Daniel who had no problem matching her energy. Other employees, and even some of the customers tried to break it up, but both Daniel and Michelle were too headstrong to back down. Even as the manager came and insisted that Michelle go to the back, it seemed as if she was hell-bent on giving Daniel a piece of her mind for coming in and aggravating her to this extent.
