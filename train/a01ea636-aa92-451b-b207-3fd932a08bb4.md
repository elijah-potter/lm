Unspoken mutual understanding between parties: Terms not recorded, actions implied, obligations tacitly agreed upon, but never verbalized. Examples include:
- Shared workspace norms: "I’ll start by 9 AM" (not said but observed).
- Family routines: "We eat at 6 PM" (assumed).
- Corporate culture: "We don’t interrupt" (understood but never debated).
- Legal loopholes: "We’ll resolve disputes informally" (implied but not written).
- Social contracts: "You don’t need to ask" (unspoken assumption).
- Economic agreements: "We’ll split profits evenly" (oral but never documented).
- Historical codes: "We follow tradition" (passed silently through generations).
- Ethical non-negotiables: "No one gets hurt" (stated in actions, not words).
- Arbitrary boundaries: "This is our zone" (divided without explanation).
- Promises made through silence: "I’ll help when needed" (spoken in gestures).

Structured for analysis:
- **Actors**: Stakeholders (e.g., colleagues, family, community).
- **Conditions**: Context where agreement is implied (e.g., shared environment, history).
- **Consequences**: Risks of misinterpretation (e.g., conflicts over "silent rules").
- **Documentation Gap**: Why unspoken agreements are often unenforceable (e.g., no records).
- **Pattern**: Repetition of non-verbal cues (e.g., eye contact, timing).

Format for integration: `Unspoken Agreement: [Context] > [Implied Rules] > [Potential Disputes]`
Example:
`Unspoken Agreement: Office > "No meetings after 5 PM" > "Late arrivals interrupt workflow"`

For analysis, extract:
1. Who enforces these agreements?
2. What happens if violated?
3. Can they be codified (e.g., handbook, code of conduct)?
