Difference Between CBD Vs THC
Cannabidiol, or CBD, and delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol, or THC, are two of several cannabinoids present in the bud. When in the entire body, CBD and THC interact with cannabinoid receptors to help treat or limit the effects of various health-related problems.
There are dozens and dozens of cannabinoid receptors throughout the body that cause specific physiological effects. Potential uses of the cannabinoids involve the following:
- Chronic Pain
- Sleep Disorder
The most notable distinction between CBD and THC is that the deficiency of psychoactive effects for CBD, which rarely causes the characteristic marijuana high of THC.
CBD VS THC: The Psychoactive Factor
Despite both being cannabinoids, both CBD and THC interact with slightly different receptors in an individual’s brain. CBD is not likely to get a psychoactive effect.
THC generates the psychoactive effect men and women connect with using marijuana recreationally. Medical marijuana that’s CBD superior has minimal THC. As a result, a person won’t feel high when taking the medication. Medical marijuana which contains THC will cause someone to experience a high when taking the medicine.
CBD Vs THC: Origin
Both CBD and THC are present in marijuana.
However, when an individual takes a CBD superior version of medical marijuana, they’re using CBD out of hemp, which can be closely related to marijuana.
THC originates from the marijuana plant, and people extract it from the cannabis plant.
Benefits of CBD & THC
CBD and THC have similar effects in treating medical conditions. However, there is some variation in the uses of each substance.
People typically use CBD to help treat the following:
- Inflammatory bowel disease
THC has potential uses in treating some of the following:
- Muscle Spasticity
- Low Appetite & Eating Disorder
- Pain Management
CBD VS THC: Side Effects
There are few side effects with either CBD or THC. In fact, CBD doesn’t have proven side effects. An individual taking THC may encounter some temporary side effects.
- Dry Mouth
- Red Eyes
- Slower Response Time
- Psychoactive Effect
- Memory Loss
- Coordination Issues
- Increased Heart Rate
Teenagers, in particular, may experience adverse psychiatric effects from getting high. This might be because a teenager’s brain is still growing. According to research, regular or large doses of THC can boost the probability of developing schizophrenia in some people with a predisposition for the condition.
Neither CBD nor THC has any apparent side effects, which are severe. Neither could be deadly if taken properly. Those who use THC recreationally seem to have a little risk of developing a dependence on it.
Many people choose to smoke or vape marijuana or CBD, but this may not be possible for people with certain medical conditions.
CBD is available in a variety of forms. These include:

THC can also come in different forms, including:
- Smokable Products
IS CBD LEGAL IN UK?
CBD oil is legal in the UK, as it is not a controlled substance. There are, however, strict restrictions over its THC content.
CBD oil that isn’t clinically prescribed can only have a maximum THC content of 0.2%. CBD oil products which have a high THC content could be available to buy online, but not legally.
