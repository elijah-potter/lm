## Product Requirements Document: "Context" - Knowledge Consolidation & Retrieval

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently juggle information across numerous applications and platforms, leading to fragmented understanding, duplicated effort, and difficulty synthesizing insights for effective decision-making.

**Vision:** To provide a centralized, intelligent hub that automatically captures, organizes, and connects relevant information, empowering knowledge workers to rapidly understand complex situations and make informed decisions.

### User Personas

**1. Eleanor Vance, Senior Marketing Manager:**

*   **Goals:** Quickly understand the competitive landscape, synthesize market research data, and present compelling narratives to stakeholders.
*   **Pain Points:** Spends excessive time searching for information scattered across spreadsheets, presentations, and online research tools. Struggles to connect disparate data points into a cohesive understanding. Feels overwhelmed by the volume of information and risks missing critical details.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** Comfortable with web applications and cloud storage; prefers intuitive interfaces.

**2. David Chen, Research Scientist:**

*   **Goals:** Track the evolution of research papers, identify relevant methodologies, and build upon existing knowledge within his field.
*   **Pain Points:** Loses track of articles and associated notes; struggles to reconstruct the chain of reasoning behind his own insights. Experiences difficulty identifying gaps in his understanding and avoiding redundant research.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** Highly proficient in digital tools; values precision and customization.

**3. Maria Rodriguez, Project Lead:**

*   **Goals:** Maintain a comprehensive record of project decisions, track action items, and ensure alignment across team members.
*   **Pain Points:** Relies on email threads and shared documents that become difficult to navigate and search. Experiences delays due to miscommunication and difficulty locating critical information. Feels responsible for ensuring information accessibility for the entire team.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** Uses standard productivity software; requires a user-friendly and collaborative experience.

### Requirements

**1. Automated Content Capture:** The application must automatically capture content from specified web pages and documents.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** User can designate URLs and file types for automated capture. Captured content is indexed and searchable within the application. The capture process operates without requiring constant user intervention.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 80% of designated content is successfully captured within 24 hours. Average capture latency is less than 15 minutes.

**2. Contextual Linking & Relationship Mapping:** The application must identify and automatically link related content based on keywords, entities, and semantic similarity.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** The application suggests related content based on captured documents. Users can manually create and edit relationships between content items. Visual relationship maps are generated to illustrate connections.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 60% of users actively utilize the relationship mapping feature within the first week. Average number of relationships created per user is greater than 5.

**3. Intelligent Search & Summarization:** The application must provide a powerful search function capable of understanding natural language queries and generating concise summaries of content.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can search using natural language queries. The application returns relevant search results ranked by relevance. Summaries of content are generated automatically and are accurate and concise.
    *   **Success Metrics:** Average search query response time is less than 1 second. User satisfaction with search results is rated 4.5 out of 5.

**4. Collaborative Workspace & Sharing:** The application must allow users to create shared workspaces and securely share content with colleagues.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can create and manage shared workspaces. Users can grant different levels of access to workspaces. Content shared within a workspace is visible to authorized users.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 40% of users actively utilize the collaborative workspace feature within the first month. Average number of users per workspace is greater than 3.

**5. Customizable Organization & Tagging:** The application must allow users to organize content using custom tags, folders, and hierarchical structures.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can create and manage custom tags and folders. Users can assign multiple tags to individual content items. Content items can be organized into hierarchical folder structures.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 70% of users utilize the tagging and folder organization features within the first week. Average number of tags used per user is greater than 5.



### Technical Considerations

*   **Scalability:** The application must be designed to handle a large volume of content and users.
*   **Security:** Robust security measures are required to protect sensitive data.
*   **API Integration:** The application should be designed to integrate with popular productivity tools and cloud storage services.
*   **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** Reliable and efficient NLP algorithms are essential for content understanding and summarization.
*   **Cross-Platform Compatibility:** Initial focus on iOS and Android platforms.

### Risks

*   **Data Privacy Concerns:** User trust hinges on ensuring the privacy and security of captured data.
*   **NLP Algorithm Accuracy:** Inaccurate content understanding and summarization can negatively impact user experience.
*   **User Adoption:** Requires a compelling value proposition to encourage users to change their existing workflows.
*   **Content Capture Reliability:** Maintaining consistent and reliable content capture across various websites and document formats presents a technical challenge.



### Launch Checklist

1.  **Secure Core Infrastructure:** Establish the backend infrastructure and ensure scalability.
2.  **Implement User Authentication & Authorization:** Develop secure user management processes.
3.  **Complete Beta Program Recruitment:** Finalize the selection of beta testers representing the target user personas.
4.  **Finalize Core Feature Set:** Freeze the feature set for the initial release based on beta feedback.
5.  **Develop Initial Onboarding Flow:** Create a clear and concise onboarding experience for new users.
6.  **Prepare Marketing & Communication Materials:** Draft launch announcements and supporting documentation.
