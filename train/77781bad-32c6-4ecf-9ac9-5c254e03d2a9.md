A fleeting handshake in the dim light of a café where strangers linger,
a whispered *"I’ll be back"* after the last laugh fades to silence.
The cup between us still warm, half-empty, a promise left unspoken.
Tomorrow, or never—time has no weight when the heart’s the currency.
The other’s name fades as fast as the sugar in the tea, but the ghost of what was remains.
A promise made in passing, like a shadow cast too soon.
