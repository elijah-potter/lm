A dimly lit diner at 3:17 AM. The neon sign flickers over a half-empty coffee cup on the counter. A man in a worn trench coat pours whiskey into a glass, eyes scanning the empty booths. His reflection in the glass doesn’t match—older, with hollow cheeks and a cigarette dangling from his lips. A woman at the counter, a stranger, slides a newspaper across the counter. It’s from *The Times* dated three years ago. On the front page, a photo of them both—her in a red dress, him in a suit—standing in front of a burned-out building. The headline: *"Missing: Sarah Lane, John Carter."* The woman hands him a business card. The name on it reads: *"John Carter."*
