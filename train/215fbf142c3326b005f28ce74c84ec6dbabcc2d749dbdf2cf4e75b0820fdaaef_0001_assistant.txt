• Research shows that diet can have a great impact on how long you live and your overall health. 
• Fruits and vegetables are known to reduce the risk of various health conditions and diseases. 
• While they are helpful, fruits and vegetables are not the only foods that can help you fight against diseases.
• Legumes such as beans, peas, and lentils are great sources of fiber and plant-based protein which can stabilize blood sugar and curb your cravings.
