The lonely seashell wanders 
His nights are cold and long 
He sits still and ponders 
And sings his seashell song 

Sometimes he travels north 
Other times he floats south 
But he's always moving forward 
And he never backs down 

The lonely seashell wanders 
And he wanders into June 
July and August pass
October coming soon 

Soon he'll find his home 
His spot in the sea  
But a lonely sea shells home 
Is wherever he may be
