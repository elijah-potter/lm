**Scene 1: The Library – Past**
A lone figure hunts for forgotten books in the dim glow of candlelight. The scent of aged paper and dust clings to the air as they flip through an ancient tome. The text whispers secrets in a language long lost.

**Scene 2: The Present – The Bookstore**
A young clerk stacks books on shelves, their fingers tracing the spines of titles they’ve never read. A customer lingers over a leather-bound volume, its cover cracked with age.

**Scene 3: The Future – The Archive**
In a sterile, climate-controlled vault, a scientist examines a holographic projection of the same tome, its pages pulsing with digital energy. A warning flickers: *"This text is not static."*

**Intercut: The Locked Room**
A locked door creaks open between scenes. Inside, a single book lies on a table, its pages whispering—now in a language that shifts with each scene. The figures from all three timelines stand at the door, watching each other.

**A fragmented narrative where time bends, and words live beyond ink.**
