You shouldn’t be here. The neon glow of the old subway tunnel pulsed like a heartbeat, casting long shadows across the cracked concrete floor. The scent of damp earth and rust clung to the air, a mix of nostalgia and decay. The train’s last stop was a half-empty station, the doors sealed tight behind you. Your pulse hammered in your throat—*what if you were late?*

A hand clamped over your mouth. The voice was rough, barely audible: *"Shut up."* The grip was iron. You swallowed, eyes darting to the flickering lights, the glint of something metallic in the dark corner. Then—*slam*—the station door burst open. The rush of cold air and distant sirens cut through the tension.

The figure in the trench coat stepped forward, their breath visible in the cold. *"You’re not supposed to be here either,"* they murmured, voice low. *"But you’re here."* The weight of their gaze made your skin prickle. The tunnel twisted, the air thick with something unspoken. The choice was yours: run, or listen.
