The dim glow of a flickering streetlamp cast long shadows across the cracked pavement as **Lena** pushed open the rusted gate of the abandoned greenhouse. Inside, the air was thick with the scent of damp earth and rusted metal. A single bulb dangled from a broken ceiling beam, casting eerie shadows on the overturned planters.

A low hum filled the space—a generator, its engine sputtering to life in the corner. A figure stood beside it, their silhouette barely defined. **Javier**, his dark hair streaked with oil, wiped his brow with a stained rag. His hands trembled slightly, fingers wrapped around a holstered pistol.

“You sure this’ll hold?” he muttered, glancing at the rusted pipe frame holding the generator in place.

Lena stepped forward, her boots sinking slightly into the damp soil. “We need light. Now.”

Javier exhaled sharply, then turned the key. The generator roared to life, its roar drowning out the distant wail of sirens. A single bulb flickered to life, casting a harsh, unnatural light over their surroundings. The overhead beams groaned, straining under the weight.

A crack split the air—glass shattering. Lena whirled. A figure stood at the greenhouse’s entrance, their face obscured by a scarf, a rifle leveled at them. **Mira**, her eyes wild with fear. Behind her, a group of masked men stepped forward, their faces painted with black greasepaint.

“Hand over the generator,” one of them growled, his voice a guttural whisper.

Javier’s hand twitched toward his holster. Lena’s breath hitched. The power flickered again—then died. The generator’s engine cut out, plunging the greenhouse into darkness. The masked men laughed, their breath fogging in the cold night air.
