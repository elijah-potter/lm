**New Haven, October 15th, 2023** – Authorities in New Haven confirmed early this morning that a catastrophic collapse at the abandoned **Blackthorn Tunnel** near the outskirts of the city has left at least **12 fatalities** and **48 missing persons** still unaccounted for. Witnesses describe a massive section of the reinforced concrete structure failing during a heavy rainstorm last night, with debris spilling into the **Whispering Creek** watershed. Emergency crews reported finding human remains and personal items scattered across a **500-meter radius** of the collapsed area. The city’s **Civil Defense** has activated a red alert, directing residents near the tunnel’s former access points to evacuate immediately. Officials have suspended all traffic through the tunnel’s last operational entrance, citing structural integrity concerns. Meanwhile, volunteers from the **New Haven Historical Society** have begun documenting artifacts recovered from the site, including what appear to be **pre-1950s-era documents and military equipment**, suggesting the tunnel was once used for smuggling or military operations. The city’s **Department of Public Works** has issued a statement calling for donations of food, water, and medical supplies to relief centers at the **Downtown Community Center**. The New Haven Police Department has activated a **public alert** via SMS and social media, urging anyone with information about the tunnel’s history or sightings of missing individuals to report it immediately. Further details are pending as search and recovery efforts intensify.
