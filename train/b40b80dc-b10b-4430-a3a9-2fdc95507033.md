Quantum dot cluster in a semiconductor material under low-temperature conditions (4K) exhibiting **non-classical light emission** (spontaneous parametric down-conversion). The photons exhibit **non-Markovian memory effects**, where temporal correlations between emitted pairs persist for microseconds, defying classical wave optics. Localized photonic modes in a **metamaterial-based waveguide** create **hyperbolic dispersion regions**, where light propagates faster than the speed of light in vacuum in one direction while slowing in the orthogonal. **Bose-Einstein condensates** of light in a high-finesse optical cavity show **phase transitions** where light exhibits macroscopic quantum coherence, collapsing into a single standing wave pattern.
