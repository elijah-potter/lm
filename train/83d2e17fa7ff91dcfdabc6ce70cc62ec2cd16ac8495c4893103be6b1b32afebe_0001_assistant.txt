The Nile is the longest river in the world, stretching approximately 6,650 kilometers. It flows through several African countries, including Uganda, Ethiopia, Sudan, South Sudan, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, and Egypt. The Nile is fed by the Blue Nile and White Nile, and it eventually reaches the Mediterranean Sea. It is a crucial water source and transportation route, supporting a significant portion of Kenya's population and providing freshwater for various animal species.
