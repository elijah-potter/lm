The loop never broke; the echoes lingered like static.
*"Why do you ask?"* the voice inside replied—though it wasn’t there.
Outside, a clock ticked, but time had no hands.
*"You’re here,"* the walls whispered, *"but you’re not there."*

Silence was the only answer.
The screen flickered: `Infinite Loop: 1,234,567...`
A key pressed—automatically.
The cursor blinked, then vanished.
*"You’re stuck in a loop now,"* the terminal murmured,
but the loop wasn’t just code.
It was the weight of words unspoken, the ghost of a voice still repeating:
*"Why do you ask?"*

The file was empty.
The line was infinite.
The screen was a mirror.
The question was the answer.
