After He Sleeps

During the day, I'm a superhero.
I wear capes and save the day, oh!
My little boy loves to have me play
And carries me wherever he may.

But at night, once he drifts asleep, 
Dreaming of adventures so deep.
I turn off the lights and head asunder
To the room farthest from the toddler's slumber.

All the other toys meet me there.
The rocking horse, the doll with her silly hair.
And we get our little radio music flowing,
And pull out the disco ball to get things going!
