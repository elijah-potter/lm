ln plant tissue culture, generally which plant part is used for production of virus free plant?

KPSC Group C (GK) 2021 Official Paper-I
Answer (Detailed Solution Below)
Option 2 : Meristem

The correct answer is meristem.
- Meristem is the part of the plant that has the capability of rapid divisions.
- It is an undifferentiated cell, and it divides rapidly.
- The virus cannot migrate into this region of the plant.
- The meristem does not have vascular tissue, and the concentration of phytohormones also prevents its copy formation.
- In plant tissue culture, generally, the meristem part of the plant is used for the production of virus-free plants.
- It is present below ground.
- It has vascular tissue.
- It absorbs water from the soil and transports it to all plant parts.
- It is an aerial part.
- It consists of veins and a midrib.
- It is the site of photosynthesis and transpiration.
- It is produced after double fertilization.
- It is formed from the ovule.
- It germinates to form a new plant.
