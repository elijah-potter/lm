A sudden storm derailed the scheduled delivery of a critical shipment, forcing the warehouse manager to reroute orders via an alternate logistics partner. Meanwhile, a last-minute technical error in the company’s internal CRM system caused duplicate entries, leading to an unexpected surge in customer inquiries—just as the marketing team launched a new campaign for a limited-time discount. The manager, now overwhelmed with handling both crises, decided to cancel the discount promotion, prompting customers to shift to competitors offering better deals. The company lost revenue from the missed sales while also facing reputational damage from the canceled promotion. The unexpected coincidence exposed vulnerabilities in the supply chain and operational systems, prompting a full-scale review of business continuity plans.
