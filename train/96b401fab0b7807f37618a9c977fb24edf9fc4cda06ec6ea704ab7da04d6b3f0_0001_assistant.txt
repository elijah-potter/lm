What is a boning knife used for?
A boning knife is used for the delicate task of separating raw meat, poultry, and fish from their bone.

How long is the blade of a boning knife?
The blade of a boning knife is about six inches long.

How thick is the blade of a boning knife?
The blade of a boning knife is thinner than that of a slicing knife.

How flexible is a boning knife?
A boning knife is flexible enough to follow the contours of the fish or bird that is being deboned.
