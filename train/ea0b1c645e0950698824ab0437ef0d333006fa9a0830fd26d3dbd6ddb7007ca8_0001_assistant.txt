White initiated the game with a daring advance of the pawns protecting his king, which Black sought to punish by sacrificing a pawn to tear open a path to the Black king. However, Black overpressed his attack and was stopped by a precise riposte from the White center pawns and king. In a final complex tactical flourish, Black attempted to use the bishop and knight to attack the Black king and win back considerable amounts of material. 

White eagerly offered this material up as a sacrifice, trading pieces for the time he needed to turn the attack against the Black king. With fatal threats against the Black king and three important Black pieces, the defense collapsed and Black soon fell to checkmate.
