-Mill Creek Campground
The largest campsite in Redwood, Mill Creek is located two miles from the main highway and offers peaceful and spaced-out campsites. 
-Elk Prairie Campground
Located right next to the highway, Elk Prairie is perhaps the most centrally located to be able to enjoy most of the hikes in Redwood.
-Gold Bluffs Beach Campground
This beautiful beachfront campground takes a long drive to get there but offers plenty of sun and an excellent view of the beach.
