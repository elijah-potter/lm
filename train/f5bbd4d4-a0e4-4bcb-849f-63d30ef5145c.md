The door creaked open, revealing a dimly lit room where two figures sat across from each other, their voices weaving in and out of the silence like a thread unraveling.

*"Do you ever think about how much time we’ve wasted?"* the first asked, fingers tracing the rim of their empty cup.

*"Time?"* the second replied, eyes fixed on the flickering candle. *"There’s no time left to waste. We’re already late."*

The first laughed, a sharp sound that echoed off the stone walls. *"Late for what?"*

*"Late for everything,"* the second sighed, standing abruptly. *"The world keeps turning, and we’re just standing here."*

A pause. The air smelled of damp earth and old books.

*"You think it matters?"* the first asked, voice hollow. *"If we never say goodbye?"*

The second hesitated, then said, *"It matters because we’re the only ones who know."*

The first exhaled. *"So what? We’ll just sit here forever?"*

The second turned, the candlelight casting long shadows. *"No. We’ll talk. Again. And again."*

Outside, a distant train whistle cut through the night, but neither heard it. The room was a cage of words, and the door stayed shut.
