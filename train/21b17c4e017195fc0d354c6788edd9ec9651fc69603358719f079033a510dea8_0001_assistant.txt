Hey, boy! You often look up into the sky with her head tilted like you’re thinking about what’s up there. Well, don’t worry, I’ll tell you! 

At night you see the moon, and during the day, you see the sun. This is because way up in the sky, there’s a collection of planets––yes, they’re shaped like the tennis balls you love to fetch!

There are a lot of planets, like Mercury, Saturn, Venus, Mars, Uranus, Neptune, and Jupiter. However, we live on Earth––the one with all the grass you run around on! 

Earth is the perfect distance from the sun: it’s close enough to get nice and warm, but it can also be far enough away to get cool and even snow! It also has plenty of water for you to lap up, and it’s home to countless animals––including you!

So now, when you catch a tennis ball or look up at the clouds, you’ll know you live on Earth and remember the magical solar system!
