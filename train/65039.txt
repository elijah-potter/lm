To incorporate lots of touches on the ball in a dynamic patterned drill set.
Description of Drill-Execution
Set up 6 cones in a rectangle with 3 on each side approx. 15 meters apart and 20 meters wide. Have at least 3 players per cone. 3 balls start on one length of the rectangle. The corners pass on a diagonal and the center lines pass across the width. After a player passes she cuts to the next line in a clockwise manner. Players should ask for the ball with the hand on the outside of the box. Encourage them to attack and run through their catch and follow through in the direction of their pass. Each player passes the ball relatively the same time.
- Quick ball movement
- Pattern Passing
- Timing and speed of play
- Effective Communication between teammates
Variations/Progression/Increased difficulty (As your players improve their skills incorporate the following…)
Add a fourth ball to the drill. The ball will move around the outside of the drill and will be passed by the last person in each corner line.
