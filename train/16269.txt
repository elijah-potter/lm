The Internet of Things has transformed into one of the most widespread forms of technology, which is currently spearheading the modern digital revolution. The sheer number of applications enabled through the adoption of this technology is nothing to scoff at, and it must be said that it’s only because of these technological innovations that we’ve progressed at such a rapid pace. In fact, most of the things around you have been made possible mainly because of the prevalence of the Internet of Things, and it’s only a matter of time before these three words have become one of the most common things heard in the realm of technology.
Now, this begs the question – exactly how is this technology applied in an industrial landscape? Well, this is where the concept of the Industrial Internet of Things (or IIot) comes into play. In layman’s terms, the Industrial Internet of Things refers to the numerous ways in which IoT technology will play a significant role across several major industries, such as manufacturing, transportation, oil & gas, and various other industrial landscapes.
So, what are the primary ways in which the Industrial Internet of Things is affecting the modern world?
- Industrial Automation: This is perhaps the most prominent role that IoT is playing in the industrial landscape – by connecting all devices and appliances to one all-encompassing platform, organisations will be able to optimise their existing business processes by automating the menial tasks and freeing up valuable human resources to other profitable avenues instead.
- Data analytics: As technology has progressed over time, the sheer volume of data that is being generated is truly massive. This is both a boon and a curse – while analysing this data will allow you to figure out the best course of action that can be taken to optimise your business, the process of performing proper big data analytics is incredibly imposing if done through ordinary means. Instead, one needs to integrate IoT solutions and carry out the process of Big Data Analytics with relative ease.
- M2M communication: Most industries utilise various forms of machinery, most of which happen to be interconnected to each other. By implementing specific Industrial IoT solutions, these machines will be able to communicate effectively and optimise their daily processes so that business processes are smoother and implemented more cohesively.
It’s evident that the implementation of the Industrial Internet of Things will go a long way in making sure that all the major industries can optimise their processes with relative ease.
