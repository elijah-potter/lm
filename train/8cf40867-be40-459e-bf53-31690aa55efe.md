## WooCommerce Product Image Zoom – A Persistent Headache

Hello everyone,

I’m posting here hoping someone might offer some insight into a frustrating issue I’m experiencing with the WooCommerce product image zoom functionality on my site, [www.example-shop.com](www.example-shop.com). I’ve been wrestling with this for the better part of a week and I'm starting to feel like I’m going in circles.

**The Expected Behavior:**

As most of you know, WooCommerce offers a built-in product image zoom feature. When a user hovers over a product image on the product page, a magnifying glass effect should appear, allowing them to zoom in and examine the details of the item. This is a standard expectation for online shoppers and a crucial element for showcasing the quality of my products.

**The Actual Behavior:**

Unfortunately, the zoom functionality isn't working as intended. Instead of a smooth zoom effect, I'm seeing a jarring, pixelated enlargement. The image simply blows up to a larger size without the expected magnification. It’s visually unappealing and, frankly, detracts from the overall shopping experience.  It’s also extremely slow to load, which further exacerbates the problem.

**Troubleshooting Steps Taken:**

I’ve already invested considerable time attempting to resolve this myself, so I want to be thorough in outlining what I’ve tried:

*   **Plugin Conflicts:** I deactivated all plugins except for WooCommerce and the necessary core components. The issue persists, indicating it’s likely not a direct plugin conflict.
*   **Theme Verification:** I temporarily switched to the default WordPress theme, Twenty Twenty-Four, to rule out any theme-specific interference. The problem remains.
*   **WooCommerce Updates:** I ensured that WooCommerce is running the latest version (7.8.2) and that all core WordPress components are also up to date.
*   **JavaScript Console:** I’ve examined the browser's JavaScript console for any errors, but I’m seeing nothing immediately obvious. There are a few warnings related to image optimization plugins, but I don't believe they are directly causing the zoom issue.
*   **Image Optimization:** I’m using Smush for image optimization, and I'm concerned that it might be interfering with the zoom functionality. I'm experimenting with disabling it temporarily.
*   **Browser Testing:** I’ve tested this across Chrome, Firefox, and Safari on both desktop and mobile devices. The problem is consistent across all browsers and devices.



I'm honestly at a loss for what else to try. I’m relatively comfortable with WordPress, but I’m not a developer. I'm hoping someone with more experience might have encountered this before and can offer some suggestions.  Any insights or potential solutions would be greatly appreciated. Thank you for your time and consideration.
