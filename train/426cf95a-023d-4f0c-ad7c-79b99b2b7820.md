The subway car was nearly empty, the last seats packed with weary commuters. Your phone buzzed—another notification from your boss: *"Meeting at 9:00. Late again?"* You checked the clock: 8:59. Panic set in.

Then the doors hissed open. A familiar face leaned in: *"Late again?"* You exhaled sharply. "Yeah." The other person smirked. *"I was just leaving."* You relaxed. The train lurched forward, the tension easing. Relief flooded you—another delay, another missed opportunity, but at least you’d make it in time for the coffee break.
