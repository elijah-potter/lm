To the reader who stands before this page with curiosity and doubt,
let me begin where all beginnings must: in silence.

You hold this in your hands, though you do not know it yet—
a letter carved from words like grain, a map without borders.
I have written these lines for you, though I do not know
why you are here. The answer, perhaps, lies in the spaces
between these words, where the unspoken breath of the reader
meets the stillness of the page.

Do not expect answers. Expect instead the quiet act
of turning pages, of letting the words dissolve
into what comes next. There is no destination,
only the road you walk, and the shadows you cast
when you reach for the light that follows.

Read. Listen. Let yourself be unsettled.
The story is yours to hold, to break, to rewrite.
And if you ask yourself why, I will not tell you.
Instead, I will ask you: what is it you are searching for?

The ink may fade, the paper may crumble—
but the questions remain. The reader’s place is in the question,
not the answer. Let that be enough.

Turn the page. Or do not. The choice is yours.
