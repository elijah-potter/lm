A pause between breaths where the weight of the world shifts—
a moment of silence after the gunshot,
the air thick with dust and the scent of blood,
a pause where the soldier’s hands tremble,
his rifle clutched too tight.

Outside the trench, the wind howls,
a child’s laughter cuts through the chaos—
unexpected, fragile, a reminder
that some pauses aren’t just breaks,
but the cracks in the armor that let in the light.
