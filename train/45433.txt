The goal of the game: To present a scientific dose in the animal world, through an interesting interactive game, that introduces children to the weight, length, speed and age of the animals, and each of them has a symbol on the cards.
The method of the game: The children gather around the deck of cards, the side of which bears the images of animals is hidden, and then one of them begins to turn one card on which a group of animals appears; As in the first picture.
the next card on the pile of cards; It has two symbols representing the question requested.
(Example: If the card bears a + sign and a weight symbol, this means: Which of these animals weighs more?)
The child who answers correctly first keeps the card, then flips the next card, and so on...
If a player answers and his answer is wrong; He has to put one of the cards he won earlier, at the bottom of the main pile...
Score Count: The player who can collect the most cards is the winner of the round.
