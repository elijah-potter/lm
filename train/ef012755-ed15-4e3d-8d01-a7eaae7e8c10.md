The room was dimly lit, the only sound the faint hum of the fluorescent lights. Across the table, Sarah’s fingers tapped nervously against her empty coffee cup. Her coworker, Mark, leaned forward, his expression unreadable.

*"So, what do you think about the new project proposal?"* he asked, his voice low.

Sarah’s eyes flickered to the ceiling, her jaw tightening. A beat of silence stretched between them, thick with unspoken tension. She glanced at her watch—she had to be at the meeting in ten minutes.

*"Uh… I don’t really have strong opinions on it yet,"* she stammered, shifting in her chair. *"Just need to review the details first."*

Mark exhaled sharply, his shoulders relaxing slightly, but his gaze never left hers. The silence lingered, punctuated only by the distant chatter of passing office workers outside. Sarah’s knuckles were white around the edge of the table, her mind racing—*avoid, delay, change the subject.* She had to think fast.
