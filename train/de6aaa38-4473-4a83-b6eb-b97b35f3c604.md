The abandoned plan was to launch the experimental rocket from Launchpad Alpha by 18:00 on the 12th. Weather conditions were favorable, but the fuel leak detected in the main engine compartment necessitated an immediate halt. Crew safety protocols were prioritized, and the countdown was paused at T-15 minutes due to a malfunction in the thrust vector control system. The project team decided to reschedule for the 16th, pending further diagnostics.
