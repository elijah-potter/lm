A suffocating mist swallows the horizon at dusk, clinging to every surface like damp skin. The air hums with an unnatural silence—no wind, no distant chatter—just the faint, rhythmic pulse of something vast and ancient beneath the surface. Buildings, if they exist, are half-submerged in the oppressive gloom, their windows like hollow eyes staring at the black sky. The ground beneath feels like lead, pressed into the earth as if it’s holding its breath too. Even the shadows stretch unnaturally, bending toward the center of the world like fingers waiting. The weight of the stillness is palpable, as if time itself has stopped, suspended in a breathless pause.
