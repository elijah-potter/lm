Doge and Kitt were pets of two respective families. The families were neighbors and both families brought in the pets when they were newborns.

Doge and Kitt saw each other all the time and they always got along. They played together, they laughed together. People thought it was odd since dogs and cats have a reputation for not getting along. But the families and others accepted their friendship.

Eventually, both Doge and Kitt became adults and they moved out of their owners' homes. Doge and Kitt decided to live together in their own home. People finally realized that they were a couple and in love. It was so romantic how they respected their differences.

The couple lived happily in their own home. About a year later they decided to get married. So they went to city hall and were officially married. Another year later they thought about having a baby, either a dogcat or a catdog. They went to the vet.

The vet said, "I wish you two could have a baby since you both are so friendly and love each other so much. But unfortunately, you are not able to have a baby together. There might be technology that allows this in the future but it is at least five years away."

The couple thanked the vet. They were disappointed in this news but they realized that they could adopt. They went to the shelter. To their delight, they found a puppy and a kitten who got along with one another. The couple adopted the puppy and the kitten and took them home to start a new family. They also decided that maybe they will have a baby dogcat or a baby catdog when the technology allowed it.
