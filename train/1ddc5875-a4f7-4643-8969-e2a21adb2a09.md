**The Last Lightkeeper**

The horizon hummed with the names of forgotten things—*Dusk, Echo, Hollow*—each one a crack in the world’s skin where the past bled into the present. The list had been carved into the driftwood of her cabin’s mantel, the letters worn smooth by salt and time. She traced the words with a finger dusted with ash, the ink still bleeding into the wood like old blood.

Outside, the harbor creaked. The *Infinite Possibilities* sat docked, her favorite vessel, her last escape. But the stars were wrong tonight. They should have been silver, but tonight they were *wrong*—somewhere between silver and the color of bruises.

She poured whiskey into the glass, let it shimmer like the list’s words. The first sip burned, but it was the second that did the trick. The names blurred. *Dusk, Echo, Hollow*—they weren’t just words anymore. They were teeth.

The radio crackled. A voice, rough as sandpaper, whispered through the static:

*"You’re the last one left, Keeper."*

She didn’t look up. The list was already unraveling.
