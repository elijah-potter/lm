Well, the reason why you don’t float into space but stay safely here on the ground is because of gravity. Yes, grav-i-ty. Good job! 

Gravity is invisible, which means you can’t see it. But it’s there! It’s a force in nature that pulls anything with enough mass (mass means something that weighs something) to the surface of the planet. 

That means that you, mean, our house, car, Joey, our puppy, and everything else stays on the ground.
