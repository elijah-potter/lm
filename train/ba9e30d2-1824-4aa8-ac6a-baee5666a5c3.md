## Bookstore Serendipity (Seattle, WA)

The rain was relentless that afternoon, a steady drumming against the large windows of Elliott Bay Book Company. I was browsing the poetry section, drawn in by the quiet hum of turning pages and the comforting scent of aged paper and leather bindings. You were across from me, meticulously examining a volume of Rilke, your brow furrowed in concentration. I noticed the vibrant turquoise of your scarf, a striking contrast against the muted tones of the surrounding shelves. We didn’t speak, but our eyes met briefly, a silent acknowledgment of shared appreciation for something beautiful and profound. 

I lingered, hoping for a reason to approach, but you eventually moved on, disappearing into the labyrinthine aisles. The memory of that fleeting connection, the gentle curve of your smile, has stayed with me. It felt like a moment suspended in time, a quiet promise of something more. I find myself returning to that corner of the bookstore, hoping to recapture that feeling, that brief, beautiful encounter.
