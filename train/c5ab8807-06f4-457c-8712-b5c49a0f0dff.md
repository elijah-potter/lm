The rain hammered against the broken window of the abandoned train car. The flickering neon sign outside read **"EXIT: 2 MILES"** in jagged, peeling letters. The person hunched inside, clutching a crumpled map, their breath misting in the cold air. A single streetlamp cast long shadows across the cramped floor, illuminating only the faded graffiti on the walls—words like *"Beware"* and *"Help"* in faded spray paint.

They had wandered off the trail at dusk, following a trail of unfamiliar signs. The GPS had died hours ago. The headlights of a passing truck illuminated a cluster of rusted metal, half-buried in snow—what they assumed were ruins. Their flashlight flickered, casting eerie reflections on the damp pavement.

A distant scream echoed, muffled by the wind. The person swallowed hard, gripping the map tighter. The exit sign had been their last hope. Now, it seemed they had no idea which direction to go. The night stretched on, endless and silent.
