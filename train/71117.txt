Everett is a Boy name. The name is originated from ‘Germanic’ origin. The baby name Everett means “Courageous Like A Boar”.
Origin of the name is: “Germanic”
Everett is a Boy name.
The pronunciation of the name is: “EV + a + rit”
Everett Name Meaning:
Everett is ultimately derived from Everhard. Everhard has both Germanic and Old English origins. Both these words have two elements in them, ‘eber and eofor’, meaning ‘wild boar’, and ‘heard and hard’, whose meaning is ‘strong and courageous’. The root words are ‘Eoforheard’ in Old English and ‘Eberhard’ in Germanic.
It is a Christian baby name.
