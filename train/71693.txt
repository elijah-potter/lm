|Designing content for transmedia, what does it mean?!|
|Thursday, 17 November 2011 12:17|
In the modern interactive world “vocabulary” of today we simply can’t miss the buzzword, TRANSMEDIA.
What is transmedia?
Transmedia is a storytelling technique which happens across multiple media platforms in a platform appropriate way.
Can we then say that the period of “new media” has ended? I say, yes, it has.
Due to transmedia, a story is finally getting its importance back. So I often ask myself, how a story can be used to empower others’ creativity.
