Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a garden full of flowers, there lived a small, bright bunny named Benny. Benny loved to play in the sun, chase butterflies, and dig in the dirt. One sunny morning, Benny heard a tiny voice.

"Help me, please!" cried a little bird inside a crack in the fence.

Benny hopped over and saw a baby bird stuck. "Oh no, what shall we do?" he thought.

Benny carefully picked up the baby bird with his paws and carried it to a safe place under the big oak tree. There, Benny found a tiny nest with eggs. He sat with the baby bird, singing softly to it.

Days passed, and the baby bird grew stronger. One day, it fluttered its wings for the first time! The baby bird chirped, "Thank you, Benny! You saved me!"

Benny smiled. "That’s okay. Now you can fly away and be happy!"

With a happy chirp, the baby bird flew into the sky, leaving Benny to enjoy the sunshine and know he had helped someone. And from that day on, Benny always looked for others who needed help, just like his friend the bird.
