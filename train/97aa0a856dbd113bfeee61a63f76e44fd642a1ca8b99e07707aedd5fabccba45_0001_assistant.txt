Hey Zac, I heard you were struggling to keep your head up at the seminar today. I also saw that big can of Red Mouse on your desk. Have you considered quitting caffeine?

I know it's normal to drink it, but oddly enough it could be causing your sleepiness. See, caffeine found in coffee, energy drinks, and even tea acts as a central nervous system stimulant. That makes you feel more awake, but when you tunnel down into what's really happening, it's kind of scary. It boosts stress hormones like cortisol and adrenaline that you'd get from entering a fight-or-flight state. I know sometimes we all get passionate about fighting for the protection of beaver dams but getting worked up could make it harder to have a fiery speech when it comes time to pitch the group's values.

It's just dulling your ability to feel tired, so that's what leads to crashing. You're still tired, you just don't realize it.

Remember, you talk about side effects all the time! That was your argument that saved the Big Stick Highway in Vermont! If you think about it, caffeine can make you nervous and it can make it harder to sleep. Plus, it can dehydrate you which is rough especially when we do summer protests.

If you want to switch, I recommend gradually tapering off to avoid headaches. I think you'll find you won't need it as much as you thought. Just focus on getting a good night's sleep because we need you in tip-top shape if we're going to save The Great Twig Bowl of Nantucket!
