If cookies you wish to bake as a treat,
the first baby step is to turn up the heat!
While the oven warms gently to 375,
two sticks of butter head to their demise.

Whisk melted butter with two cups brown sugar,
stop when the batter turns goey like boogers.
A spoon of vanilla and two eggs will follow,
The mix is now unsafe to swallow!

Sift two cups of flour right in as you go,
Keep an eye on your bowl so it won't overflow.
Dark chocolate chips are the stars of this dish,
so fold them in gently, just one little swish.

Roll the dough and place small balls onto sheets,
Bake until the wonderful smell fills the streets!
Take them out carefully and leave them to cool,
Then serve them before all your guests start to drool!
