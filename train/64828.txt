Picking the Right Material for your Metal Project
A lot of variables go into picking a metal product that will work for your application. But, getting the right material for the job can be tricky and require some research. If you are not sure what material to choose, here are tips to help you:
Choose the Right Metal Alloy
Picking the optimal metal alloy for your project requires in-depth industry knowledge and research. Every kind of alloy has unique properties that determine the function of a material and product as well as the quality of the product to be made. To choose the right alloy, consider the following:
- Resistance. Certain alloys can resist rust, withstand underwater use, and endure extreme temperatures. This makes them ideal for applications in extreme conditions. Moreover, high-performance alloys that resist wearing play a role in ensuring metal components do not fail. After corrosion takes place, the particles will work against the mating surfaces which will be followed by serious galling and seizing. As a result, the components will freeze up. The resistance of a metal is something you need to consider if you want to buy surplus pipe.
- Plasticity. The ability of a metal to be stretched into wire or hammered into a sheet can help you decide the metal alloy for your project.
- Conductivity. Metals like copper alloys can conduct electricity and thermal heat in a safe and efficient way. They are vital in electrical components like appliances and heaters.
- Strength. In fabrication production, it is important to use a metal that is both resilient and extremely strong. Steel alloys tend to be stronger and more durable than others.
- Welding suitability. Welding works by joining two metals together. This is meant to make bigger, more homogeneous pieces for structures. The ability of an alloy to be welded must be examined carefully when considering some alloys for fabrication.
- Weight. For projects where mass is a factor, lightweight alloys are a must. Aluminum, titanium, and magnesium alloys are very lightweight and strong. That is why they are ideal for use in bulk-conscious production.
Using Dependable Resources
Visit websites that have helpful charts to help you figure out the metal product you need. They should outline most of the physical and chemical properties of the metals they stock. Some metal product supplier websites have a chart that details the chemical composition differences between 301, 302, and 304 stainless steel. They may also help you decide on the tensile strength of various metals. Beyond information on metal properties, you should be able to access the metal products the supplier regularly stocks.
