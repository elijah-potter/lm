The Lower Elementary Teacher Tools, for 1st and 2nd grade, ages 6–8, includes interactive and highly visual materials to help bring Bible stories to life.
The Teacher Tools include:
- Realistic Bible story posters
- Storytelling figures and backgrounds
- Multipurpose posters
- Attendance chart
- Teacher CD with recorded Bible stories, hymns and songs, and music scores
Use the Lower Elementary Teacher Tools with the Lower Elementary Teacher Guide.
