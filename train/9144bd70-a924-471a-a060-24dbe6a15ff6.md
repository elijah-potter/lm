The kitchen fumes thickened as the spatula clattered against the pan, sending grease droplets skittering across the counter. "You *always* say that," *she* snapped, her voice sharp with frustration, her fingers digging into the fabric of her apron. *"You’re just being dramatic again."*

*He* exhaled through his nose, rubbing a hand over his stubbled jaw. The oven timer buzzed, interrupting the silence that followed. "I’m not dramatic—this is a *real* problem. The recipe calls for a 30-minute simmer, not a 15-minute blast!" The dish rag fluttered to the floor, landing with a wet *splat*.

Across the counter, *she* crossed her arms, her brow furrowing. "And yet, here’s your burnt risotto—again." The microwave dinged. She tossed in the last ingredient—parmesan—while he grabbed a glass from the cabinet, filling it with ice and a bottle of wine he’d "forgotten" in the fridge. The clink of the glass against the counter echoed, a sound too loud in the suddenly tense room.

Outside, the rain hammered against the windows, but inside, the argument raged on.
