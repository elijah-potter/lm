During a two-month break from filming "Ashes of Time," Wong Kar-wai began writing "Chungking Express." He finished the screenplay while shooting the film, and the second story was written in a day. The film was shot in Tsim Sha Tsui, as Wong grew up there, in addition to Central, the location that features the famous Midnight Express fast food shop. Wong provides fragmented narration throughout the film, connecting the fragments through monologue, while also conveying the emotions and themes in the story.
