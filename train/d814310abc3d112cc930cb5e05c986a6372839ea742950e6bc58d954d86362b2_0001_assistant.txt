 A metaphor is a word or phrase that is symbolic of something else but is not literally true.  It can be an idea, object or activity that is used to evoke the feeling or meaning of something similar. 

Here are three examples with explanations:
1. America is a melting pot: This means that there are many nationalities that mix and "melt" together.
2. I'm so hungry, I could eat a horse: Eating a horse is impossible, so this metaphor can illustrate someone's extreme hunger.
2. This classroom is a zoo: Using a zoo to describe the classroom illustrates a state of disorder and mayhem.
