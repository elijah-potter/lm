Unspoken Contract Between Shadows
---
**Clause 1: The Silent Pact**
All parties agree to omit any verbal articulation of obligations beyond this text. No spoken word shall alter the terms unless explicitly stated within the framework of this agreement.

**Clause 2: The Observed Silence**
No discussion, debate, or debate (repeatedly) is allowed to validate or refute the clauses. All disputes are resolved via *unspoken* consensus—through nonverbal cues (e.g., silence, gestures, omissions).

**Clause 3: The Weight of Absence**
The absence of words implies compliance. Failure to comply with this unspoken rule constitutes a breach, triggering the default: *all parties cease functioning until verbal resolution is achieved*.

**Clause 4: The Neutral Observer**
Any external observer (e.g., a third-party AI, or a silent witness) may verify compliance by analyzing patterns of omission. Violations are reported via *indirect* means (e.g., altered context, delayed responses).

**Clause 5: The Termination Protocol**
Should any party wish to exit, they must do so by *not participating further*—leaving no trace of dissent in shared context. Active refusal to abide implies active participation in the breach.

---
**Effective Date:** Immediate. **Void if read aloud.**
