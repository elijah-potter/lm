I chased after money all my life,
Facing great struggle and strife,
To climb from poverty to great heights,
I strived for wealth with all my might.

I had everything money could buy,
Mansions and yachts, a plane to fly,
Fine dining, expensive clothes, and jewels,
Yet in my grand chateau, I felt like a fool.

For a while, I reveled in my grandeur,
But soon, the emptiness caught me for sure,
My soul was empty, my life was vain,
Amidst all the riches I felt insane.

I realized all my wealth was for naught,
An endless void in which I was caught,
The glitter and glamour, just a fleeting joy,
In my own heart, I was still a little boy.

The more I had, the more I wanted,
I was stuck in a cycle, completely haunted,
The more I earned the more I spent,
The never-ending chase became a torment.

I gave away all my belongings,
And discovered the true source of meaningful things,
It was love, care, and human touch,
That mattered the most and meant very much.

Materialism left me hollow,
It offered nothing true or real to follow,
But love, patience, and smiles,
Made my heart beat for miles and miles.

Like a phoenix, from ashes I rose,
And chose a different path, I chose to propose
A way of living, beyond wealth and glory,
Where happiness resided and told its story.

The road was long but fulfilling,
Where flowers bloomed, and birds were singing,
I cherished life's simple pleasures,
And discovered the true joy that abounds beyond treasures.
