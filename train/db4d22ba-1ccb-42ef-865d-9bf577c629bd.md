The winding forest trail stretched ahead, its ancient roots twisting like serpents through the undergrowth. The path ahead, though longer, was unmarked—just dense foliage and the whisper of unseen creatures. The shorter route, a narrow dirt path, was clear, but its destination lay shrouded in mist, its end a mere shadow of the one before.

She hesitated. The weight of the backpack—essential supplies, a first aid kit, her lantern—pressed against her back. The map in her pocket was faded, the arrows barely discernible. The guidebook she’d consulted earlier warned that the long way was treacherous, but it promised a hidden valley, a place untouched by modern development, where the river ran clear and the air hummed with unseen wildlife.

A branch snapped behind her. She spun, gripping the lantern tighter. No one. Just the crunch of her boots on loose gravel as she turned to follow the longer path. The trail twisted through a clearing where fireflies flickered like dying stars, their glow casting long shadows. The wind carried the scent of damp earth and something older—something wild.

She followed, her breath shallow, her mind racing. The shorter path had been a lie. The longer one was the truth. And now, she was walking into the unknown.
