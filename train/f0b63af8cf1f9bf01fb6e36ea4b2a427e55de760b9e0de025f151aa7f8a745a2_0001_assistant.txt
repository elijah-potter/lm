The old oak creaked as it popped from its snug fit. A faint smell of aloe and rocks wafted from the doorway. Charlie and Louis looked at each other, but Jamie leaned in. He sputtered and shook his head. Sand. Little bits of sand fell from his fair like fairy dust. He turned wide-eyed back to his friends. His pupils were almost pinpoints despite the door being dark. Louis took a deep breath like he was going to jump into a lake, and plunged his head in. He felt a stiff wind on his face and heard a far-off rattle. Sand pelted his face. Cautiously he opened his eyes. A desert stretched before him.
