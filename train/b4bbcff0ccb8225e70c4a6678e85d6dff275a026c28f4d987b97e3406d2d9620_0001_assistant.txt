Charlotte and Ronald were llamas that spent the summer months at various county fairs. They stood politely while children took turns petting them, and sometimes would get treats in return. They really enjoyed getting little cups of corn that the children could buy for fifty cents apiece and feed to the animals. 
One day, a new llama joined Charlotte and Ronald at the fair. 
"Hello! My name is Matilda," said the newcomer. She held out a hoof in greeting. 
Charlotte frowned. The county fair was her and Ronald's turf. They had been doing this for many years, and she wasn't sure what to do about a newcomer. Besides, she didn't want to share the children's affection or their offerings of treats. 
"My name is Ronald, and this is Charlotte!" Ronald replied. He nudged Charlotte. "Come on, say hello, Charlotte! It's good to make new friends. Besides, with three of us here, even more children will want to visit!" 
Charlotte begrudgingly shook hooves with Matilda. Ronald was usually right, and what harm had being friendly ever done? 
As it turns out, Matilda was full of energy and ideas. She entertained the children with dances and even taught Ronald and Charlotte some tricks. Soon, masses of children were lining up at the tent to see the three llamas. 
"I'm so glad you joined us, Matilda! I have to admit, I've never had so much corn in my life!" said Charlotte one afternoon, as little hands eagerly held out treats, trying to get her attention. "I was wrong to think that having three llamas was a bad idea." At the end of the summer, Charlotte and Ronald bid their goodbyes to Matilda, happy to have made a new friend and looking forward to summers to come.
