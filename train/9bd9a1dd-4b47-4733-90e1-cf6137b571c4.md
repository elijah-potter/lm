A sleepless night in a cozy hostel in Kyoto’s bamboo forest, where the mist curled over ancient stone lanterns. Her phone buzzed: *"Your flight’s delayed. No luggage."* The flight attendant, a woman with salt-and-pepper hair, slid a small notebook across the aisle. *"Write down what you remember before you forget."*

She scribbled in Japanese, then English: *"I’m not the same. I don’t know why, but I don’t want to go home."* The plane’s engines roared. Outside, a storm brewed over the Pacific. She folded the notebook and kept it in her pocket. The first real change would come when she looked back.
