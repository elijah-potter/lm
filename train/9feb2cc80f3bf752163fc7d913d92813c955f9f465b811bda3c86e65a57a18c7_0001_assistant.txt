Super Saiyan is a transformation those of the Saiyan race can do in the series "Dragon Ball."  It is usually triggered by an intense emotional response but also requires many S-Cells in order to complete the transformation.  During this transformation, the user's hair turns a golden blond and will stand on end.  Their eyes will also turn green and a golden aura will surround them.  The user's personality may also shift and become more aggressive in nature as their physical and power output increases in magnitude.  Super Saiyan is just one of the stages and a character can ascend beyond this stage to higher stages of power.
