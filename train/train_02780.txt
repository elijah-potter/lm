Olaf is playing a game with his dad. He scored three times more points than his dad, who scored 7 points. How many points did they score in total?
Olaf scored three times more points than his dad, which means he scored 7 * 3 = <<3*7=21>>21 points.
So in total, Olaf and his dad scored 21 + 7 = <<21+7=28>>28 points.
