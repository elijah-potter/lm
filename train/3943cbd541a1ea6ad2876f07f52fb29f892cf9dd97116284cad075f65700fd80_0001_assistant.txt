Mnemonic devices are used to recall memories or retain information that someone may need later in life. Acrostic mnemonic devices specifically the first letter of a word in a memorable sentence or poem to recall important info. 

Here are three examples of acrostic mnemonic devices: 

1. PEMDAS is a math acronym that means Parentheses, Exponents, Multiplication, Division, Addition, and Subtraction. Many remember it as “Please Excuse My Dear Aunt Sally.” 
When faced with a challenging math problem, this is the order of operations that someone should use to solve the equation. 

2. ROY G. BIV is an acronym for the correct sequence of colors in a rainbow. Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo, Violet. 

3. HOMES helps to recall the order of names of the Great Lakes: Huron, Ontario, Michigan, Erie, and Superior.
