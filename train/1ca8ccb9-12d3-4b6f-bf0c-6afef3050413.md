Silent corridors of uncarpeted stone
Where no light source emits a flicker
Glassless windows hum with unseen pressure
Furniture exists only in partial shadows
Doors open onto empty, unlit rooms
No sound carries beyond the static hum
Ceilings sag like unripened fruit
Plants wither without water
The air carries a faint metallic tang
Mirrors are cracked at the edges
No nameplate marks the entrance—just a gaping void
