## Project Chimera: Rulebook

**A Cooperative Game of Scientific Crisis Management**

Welcome to Project Chimera, a cooperative tabletop game for two to four players. As leading researchers at the Global Bio-Response Initiative (GBRI), you must work together to contain and ultimately reverse a rapidly evolving, synthetic pathogen threatening global stability. Your decisions will have profound consequences; failure is not an option.

### 1. Components

*   **Game Board:** A world map depicting key research facilities, population centers, and potential outbreak zones.
*   **Researcher Pawns (4):** Representing your team of specialists, each with unique starting abilities.
*   **Pathogen Cubes (100):** Representing the spread of the Chimera virus.
*   **Research Tokens (50):** Used to track progress on vital research projects.
*   **Event Cards (60):** Introducing unexpected challenges and opportunities.
*   **Crisis Cards (30):** Escalating the severity of the outbreak and triggering global consequences.
*   **Research Project Cards (20):** Detailing the objectives and requirements for scientific breakthroughs.
*   **Containment Markers (20):** Used to temporarily suppress outbreaks.
*   **Global Stability Tracker:** A gauge indicating the overall health of the world's infrastructure and public order.
*   **Dice (6):** Standard six-sided dice used for skill checks and event resolution.
*   **Rulebook (this document).**

### 2. Setup

1.  Place the Game Board in the center of the playing area.
2.  Place the Global Stability Tracker at its starting position (indicated on the board).
3.  Shuffle the Event Card deck and place it face down.
4.  Shuffle the Crisis Card deck and place it face down. Draw the first Crisis Card and place it face up next to the deck. This card dictates the initial outbreak locations and severity.
5.  Place Pathogen Cubes on the board according to the initial outbreak locations specified on the revealed Crisis Card.
6.  Each player chooses a Researcher Pawn and places it on the GBRI Headquarters location (indicated on the board). Each researcher begins with their designated starting Research Tokens (as detailed on their individual character sheet, not included in this document).
7.  Place the Research Tokens in a supply pool accessible to all players.

### 3. Turn Structure

Each player’s turn consists of the following phases, completed in order:

1.  **Event Phase:** Draw the top Event Card and resolve its effects immediately. These effects may introduce new outbreaks, affect researcher abilities, or trigger unexpected global events.
2.  **Action Phase:** You have three Action Points to spend. Actions include:
    *   **Move (1 Action Point):** Move your Researcher Pawn to an adjacent location on the Game Board.
    *   **Research (2 Action Points):** Attempt to advance a Research Project. You must be located at a Research Facility. Roll two dice and add your researcher’s relevant skill modifier (detailed on their character sheet). If the result meets or exceeds the Project’s difficulty rating, place one Research Token on the Project Card.
    *   **Contain (2 Action Points):** Remove Pathogen Cubes from your current location. The number of cubes removed is determined by a dice roll (one die) plus your researcher’s Containment skill modifier.
    *   **Share Research (1 Action Point):** Transfer one Research Token to another researcher at your current location.
    *   **Analyze (1 Action Point):** Draw the top two Event Cards, choose one to resolve immediately, and discard the other.
3.  **Crisis Phase:** After all players have completed their turns, draw a Crisis Card. Resolve the card’s effects immediately. These cards represent the escalating consequences of the outbreak and often introduce difficult choices. The Crisis Card is then discarded.

### 4. Win and Loss Conditions

**Winning the Game:**

You win the game by completing all Research Project Cards. Once all Projects are successfully completed, the Chimera virus is neutralized, and global stability is restored.

**Losing the Game:**

You lose the game if any of the following conditions are met:

*   **Global Stability Reaches Zero:** The Global Stability Tracker reaches the “Collapse” position.
*   **Outbreak Spreads Uncontrollably:** A predetermined number of locations (specified on the Crisis Card) are completely overrun with Pathogen Cubes (typically 10 cubes per location).
*   **Researcher Incapacitation:** All Researcher Pawns are incapacitated (a condition triggered by specific Event or Crisis Cards; details on individual character sheets).

### 5. Mechanics of Trade-off and Uncertainty

Project Chimera incorporates several mechanics designed to force difficult decisions and highlight the inherent uncertainties of crisis management.

1.  **Research Priority:** Each Research Project Card requires a specific combination of Research Tokens. However, the tokens needed are often in short supply. Players must collaboratively decide which Project to prioritize, knowing that neglecting others can have severe consequences.
2.  **Crisis Response Allocation:** Crisis Cards often present players with a choice between two conflicting responses, each offering a different benefit and incurring a unique cost (e.g., diverting resources to contain an outbreak in one location versus accelerating research in another). The chosen response carries an element of uncertainty, as the actual outcome may vary depending on a dice roll.
3.  **Event Card Mitigation:** Some Event Cards present players with an opportunity to mitigate their negative effects, but at a cost. This might involve sacrificing Research Tokens, delaying a Research Project, or risking researcher exposure to the virus. The effectiveness of the mitigation is determined by a dice roll, adding an element of risk versus reward.

### 6. Difficulty Scaling

The game’s difficulty scales based on the number of players:

*   **Two Players:** Reduce the initial number of Pathogen Cubes by 20%. Crisis Cards are drawn every other round.
*   **Three Players:** Play with the standard setup as described above.
*   **Four Players:** Increase the initial number of Pathogen Cubes by 20%. Crisis Cards are drawn every round.



### 7. Advanced Variants

*   **Specialist Focus:** Players choose a "Specialty" at the start of the game, granting them enhanced abilities in a specific area (Research, Containment, or Analysis) but imposing a penalty in others.
*   **Dynamic Crisis:** Introduce a system where the severity of Crisis Cards is influenced by player actions and the overall state of the game.
*   **Mutation Track:** Introduce a Mutation Track that triggers increasingly dangerous mutations in the Chimera virus if research progress is too slow.




### Designer’s Note

Project Chimera was designed to be more than just a game; it's a simulation intended to foster collaborative problem-solving and highlight the complexities of scientific crisis response. The mechanics emphasize the need for careful planning, resource management, and the difficult trade-offs inherent in situations with high stakes and incomplete information. The game aims to subtly encourage players to consider the ethical implications of their decisions and appreciate the vital role of scientific research in safeguarding global well-being.
