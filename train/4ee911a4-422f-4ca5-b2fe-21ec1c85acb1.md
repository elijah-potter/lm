The rusted metal grates of a half-buried subway entrance loom overhead, their edges jagged like teeth. A single flickering streetlamp casts long shadows of figures moving through the mist—one in a torn trench coat, another half-hidden in the crowd, their faces blurred by the haze. The air smells of damp concrete and old oil, thick with the weight of unseen stories. A distant siren wails, its echo swallowed by the city’s silence.
