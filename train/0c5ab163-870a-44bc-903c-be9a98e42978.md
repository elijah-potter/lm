**A floating garden in a futuristic city’s sky garden district.**

Draped between glass and steel towers, the **Aetherium Fields** bloom with genetically engineered flora—flowers that shift color with ambient light, trees with roots embedded in solar-paneled platforms, and crops grown in hydroponic loops powered by wind turbines. The air hums with drones tending to the plants, while below, the city’s neon-lit streets pulse with life.

This place feels familiar because it’s a **version of a garden or park**—but the newness lies in the **unseen systems**: the AI curators that rearrange blooms based on weather forecasts, the vertical farms that harvest energy, and the way the sky itself seems to breathe with the city’s heartbeat. You recognize the joy of nature, but not the way it’s now **coexisting with technology** like a symbiotic ecosystem. The scent of ozone mingles with the earthy aroma of soil, and the sound of chatter from below—where the city’s hustle never stops—is the only reminder you’re not alone.
