Here it is, the moment we've all been waiting for! The soundtrack for Greta Gerwig's "Barbie" is finally arriving. Featured musicians include Tame Impala, Haim, Charli XCX, and Ice Spice. This epic album executive produced by Mark Ronson features tracks by supporting cast member Dua Lipa, as well as Lizzo, Nicki Minaj, Pink Pantheress, and Khalid. Even superstar Ryan Gosling is featured here. Also featured, are Ava Max, Dominic Fike, Gayle, Fifty Fifty, Kali, Karol G and the Kid Laroi. 

An All-Star movie deserves an All-Star soundtrack and that's exactly what you'll be getting when you order your copy of the "Barbie" soundtrack. It will be available online and in stores near you on July, 21st.
