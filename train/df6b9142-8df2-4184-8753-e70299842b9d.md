The room was dimly lit, ambient lights casting long shadows across the conference table. Sarah adjusted her tie nervously as she glanced between her colleague, Mark, and the CEO, who leaned back in his chair, arms crossed. Mark’s expression remained neutral, but his fingers twitched slightly near his coffee cup.

CEO: *"Did you notice the delays in the Q4 revenue projections?"*

Sarah’s mouth went dry. She hesitated, then cleared her throat—*"Uh, yes, I’ve been reviewing the reports..."*—but her eyes flickered toward Mark, who gave a subtle shake of his head. She added, *"...but the changes in the budget were also significant."*

CEO raised an eyebrow. *"The budget changes?"*

Mark’s voice cut through the tension first: *"Yes, the new fiscal allocations were approved last week."* He paused, then added, *"We’ve been focusing on the supply chain adjustments to mitigate those delays."* Sarah’s shoulders slumped; she’d been expecting something far more direct.

CEO’s tone softened. *"Understood."* He turned back to the screen, but Sarah’s mind raced—how had she failed to ask the right question? How could Mark have known? The room buzzed with unspoken questions.
