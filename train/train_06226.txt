Pedro, Linden, and Jesus are playing a game. Jesus has 60 squares. Linden has 75 squares. Pedro has 200. How many more squares does Pedro have than both Jesus and Linden combined?
Pedro has 100 squares - 60 squares = <<100-60=40>>40 more squares than Jesus.
Pedro has 100 squares - 75 squares = <<100-75=25>>25 more squares than Linden.
Combined Pedro has 40 squares + 25 squares = <<40+25=65>>65 squares more than both Jesus and Linden.
