Being overheard evokes a mix of intrigue, unease, and heightened attention. There's a jittery anticipation—every word feels scrutinized, every thought potentially exposed. The mind spirals: *"What if someone knows more than they let on?"* or *"Did I say something embarrassing?"* A quiet tension builds, like a live wire between listener and speaker, with the unspoken fear that the wrong word could trigger a betrayal of privacy or judgment. Even mundane interactions feel charged, the air thick with the possibility of being caught off guard. The experience amplifies vulnerability, turning even casual conversation into a performance of calculated caution.
