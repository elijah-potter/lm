Once Upon a Murder Menu

Dazzling Drinks
Quench your thirst with one of these coronal cocktails!
-"Drink Me" Potion - Curacao, Midori, vodka, simple syrup, and lemon juice come together in an irresistible magic potion with a little edible glitter to give it an enchanting shimmer!
-Poisoned Apple Cider - This combination of apple cider, spiced rum, pomegranate juice, and grenadine is simply to die for!
-Full Moontini - Summon the spirits with this eerie concoction of black gin, pineapple juice, lime juice, and activated charcoal powder garnished with an ice sphere.

-Sorcerer's Salami & Cream Cheese Rolls - Satisfy your senses with an olive, red pepper, or spinach salami cream cheese roll.

-Fairy Circle Stuffed Mushrooms - From the fairy circle to your plate, these portobello mushrooms stuffed with cheese, herbs, garlic, and spinach will transport your tastebuds to another realm!
