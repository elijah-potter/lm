A man in a sharp suit sat across from a woman at a dimly lit café, their glasses of iced tea untouched beside them. She leaned forward, hands clasped between her knees, voice dropping to a low tone. "I’ve been wondering, *how* did you find out about the missing documents?"

He exhaled sharply, fingers tracing the rim of his cup. The corner of his mouth twitched, just for a second, before he cleared his throat. "I *asked* around."

She blinked, then nodded slowly, as if she already knew the answer. "You didn’t *have* to." The air between them hummed with unspoken tension. "Just… tell me."

His eyes flicked to hers, then away. "It wasn’t my place to—" A pause. "I didn’t even *know* the answer."

The café’s muted music swelled around them, but neither moved. Outside, the city’s distant hum vibrated through the glass, indifferent.
