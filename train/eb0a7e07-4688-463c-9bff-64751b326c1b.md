**Temporary Market Square (Seasonal Stalls):**
A narrow cobblestone plaza near a university campus, hosting fleeting vendor stalls from late May to early September. Each day, new vendors set up tents with handmade crafts, fresh produce, or vintage books, while the next day they’re gone. No permanent fixtures—just the sound of laughter, clinking cups, and the scent of grilled corn. The town’s only public space that ever changes.
