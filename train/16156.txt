When you hear the term “omega-3s,” fish may be the first thing that pops into your head. The truth is, many fish are a rich source of omega-3s – the healthy fats that support brain, heart and eye health and function – thanks to the microalgae found in their food chain. Fish eat marine algae and phytoplankton which are nature’s original producers of EPA and DHA omega-3s.
