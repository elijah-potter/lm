Did you know that 7 in 10 people in the United States drink coffee daily? And half of those people will drink 3 cups of coffee or more to keep their energy and concentration in top-notch shape for the day ahead. Coffee is one of the most popular, and most addictive, beverages in the world, and there are hundreds of ways to indulge cravings. 

Coffee is enjoyed so extensively because of its chemical properties, like caffeine, that stimulate the mind and body of the drinker. One of the reasons that people love coffee so much is because the daily dose of caffeine helps sharpen their concentration and keeps them awake through workdays and other responsibilities. 

Other reasons that so many people love coffee include the following: 

- Coffee has natural antioxidants that promote healthier immune systems and boost metabolism. Studies have shown that one cup of coffee daily can also help to lower the risk of type 2 diabetes, protect against dementia, and relieve muscle pains. 

- Many people love coffee because of the flavors and options to customize. Cold brews, espresso shots, flavored creamers, dessert syrups… there’s so much that you can do to make coffee the perfect drink for a quick pick-me-up. Some people even love the taste of straight coffee, dark roasted, with no additional flavor profiles, because it’s like a bitter jolt to tastebuds. 

- Sometimes people love coffee because their friends or loved ones love coffee. It gives them something to do and bond over, especially in cafes where the aroma and warm environment combine to make a cozy experience.
