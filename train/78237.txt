In addition to being able to recognize the standard dates that you enter into date fields, Microsoft Outlook understands a few date-related phrases you can use instead of standard date values. This unique feature enables you to type short words and phrases into date text boxes when you need to specify a date. You might this feature useful, for example, when you would like to schedule a future appointment on Labor Day without having to compute the number of days between the current date and that holiday.
1. Launch Outlook and click the "Calendar" button to view your calendar.
2. Click the "Home" tab followed by "New Appointment" to open the Appointment window.
3. Enter a subject in the "Subject" text box and type the following text into the "Start Time" text box: 12 days after tomorrow.
4. Press "Enter" and note that Outlook computes the number of days between the current date and in the future and displays that future date in the "Start Time" text box.
- Experiment with other date phrases, such as "two weeks after Labor Day," "tomorrow" and "30 days before Christmas." Because Outlook recognizes most holiday names, you can use them in your phrases. For instance, if you want to schedule a meeting 30 days before Thanksgiving, type "30 days before Thanksgiving" in the text box where you’d normally type a date value.
