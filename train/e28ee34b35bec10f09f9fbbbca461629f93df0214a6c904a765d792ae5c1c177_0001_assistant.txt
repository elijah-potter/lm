A Step-by-Step Guide to Cleaning the Dishes

1. Ensure you have an organized space: Many people find washing dishes easier when they have a designated area for washing, rinsing, and drying dishes.  
2. Prepare your dishes: make sure your plates don’t have any excess food by scraping particles into the garbage.  
3. Fill up one side of your sink with warm soapy water. 
4. It might be easier to start with the least dirty items. You’ll find it keeps your water cleaner for a longer amount of time.
5. Scrub your dishes with a sponge, brush, or dishcloth! Apply pressure as you wipe to get rid of stains. If there are stubborn areas, letting the dish or glass soak in the warm soapy water will help loosen it up.
6. In a separate basin, run fresh water and rinse your dishes, making sure you’re clearing off suds and stains. For bowls and cups, this is extremely important. If you have any pots and pans, you might want to let them soak for longer, as cooked-on food and grease stains will be harder to clean.
7. After you’ve rinsed your dishes, place them in the drying area. This can be a drying rack placed over a towel or simply drying them manually with a clean towel and putting them away.
8. After all the dishes are out of the sink, you can drain the soapy warm water and use your sponge or brush to clean the sink of soap and food residue.
9. It’s important to sanitize your dishwashing tools properly. The easiest way to do this is soaking them in a water and bleach mixture or running them through the dishwasher. 
10. Check your area and ensure everything is put away in its proper spot and your area is tidy and ready for the next round of dishes.

If you follow these steps, you can have clean and organized dishware that’s ready for your next meal!
