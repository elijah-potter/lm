Grades 3-5 Curriculum Overview
The grammar stage
In 3rd Grade, students are exposed to Latin prep, astronomy & earth science, Saxon Math 3, ancient Greece & Rome, literature, spelling, the New Testament, grammar, and phonics.
In 4th Grade, students study Saxon Math 5/4, medieval, renaissance, and Reformation history, grammar, handwriting, basic chemistry & physics, and pre-Latin.
In 5th Grade, students study basic biology with anatomy, pre-Latin B, world explorers to 1815, Saxon Math 6/5, spelling, grammar, and literature.
