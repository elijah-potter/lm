## Product Requirements Document: "Context" - Mobile Knowledge Consolidation

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently juggle disparate information sources – notes, documents, web pages, email threads – leading to fragmented understanding, duplicated effort, and difficulty connecting relevant insights.

**Vision:** To empower knowledge workers to synthesize information from across their digital landscape into coherent, interconnected contexts, fostering deeper understanding and accelerating decision-making.

---

### User Personas

**1. Eleanor Vance - The Strategic Consultant:**

*   **Goal:** To quickly synthesize information from client meetings, research reports, and internal documents to formulate well-informed strategic recommendations.
*   **Pain Points:**  Feels overwhelmed by the volume of information; struggles to connect insights from different sources; spends excessive time searching for relevant data; finds it difficult to recall specific details when presenting to clients.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** Highly proficient with mobile devices and cloud-based applications.

**2. Marcus Bellweather - The Research Scientist:**

*   **Goal:** To organize and connect research findings, experimental data, and literature reviews to advance scientific understanding.
*   **Pain Points:**  Finds it challenging to maintain a comprehensive overview of complex research projects; struggles to identify patterns and relationships between different data points; often duplicates work due to difficulty tracking progress.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** Comfortable with technical applications, but prioritizes ease of use and data integrity.

**3. Anya Sharma - The Marketing Manager:**

*   **Goal:** To aggregate campaign data, market research, and competitor analysis to optimize marketing strategies and demonstrate ROI.
*   **Pain Points:**  Loses track of key performance indicators across various platforms; struggles to correlate marketing efforts with business outcomes; feels pressured to deliver data-driven results quickly.
*   **Technical Proficiency:**  Relies on mobile devices for communication and data access; values intuitive interfaces and clear visualizations.

---

### Requirements

**1. Context Creation & Linking:** Users can create "Contexts" – named collections of linked digital resources.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:**  Users can create a Context with a descriptive name. Users can add links to web pages, documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT), and email threads (via direct integration with major email providers).  The app accurately parses and displays linked content previews.
    *   **Success Metric:** 75% of new users create at least three Contexts within their first week.

**2. Automatic Content Extraction & Tagging:** The app automatically extracts key information from linked resources and suggests relevant tags.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:**  The app identifies and extracts titles, headings, and key phrases from linked documents. The app suggests relevant tags based on content analysis. Users can edit and add their own tags.
    *   **Success Metric:** 60% of suggested tags are accepted or modified by users.

**3. Relationship Mapping:** Users can define relationships between resources within a Context, creating a visual map of interconnected information.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can create custom relationships between linked resources (e.g., "supports," "contradicts," "expands on"). The app visually represents these relationships as a graph. Users can edit and delete relationships.
    *   **Success Metric:** 40% of Contexts contain at least three defined relationships.

**4. Search & Discovery:** Users can search across all Contexts and linked resources using keyword search and tag filtering.

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:**  Users can search by keyword across all linked content. Users can filter search results by tag. Search results display relevant content previews and relationship connections.
    *   **Success Metric:** Average search time is less than 5 seconds.

**5. Cross-Device Synchronization:** Contexts and linked resources are automatically synchronized across user devices (iOS and Android).

    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Changes made on one device are reflected on all other devices within 60 seconds. Offline access to previously viewed resources is supported.
    *   **Success Metric:** 99.9% synchronization success rate.



---

### Technical Considerations

*   **Scalability:** The architecture must support a large number of users and Contexts, with efficient indexing and retrieval of linked resources.
*   **Security:** Robust security measures are required to protect user data and prevent unauthorized access.  Data encryption both in transit and at rest is mandatory.
*   **API Integrations:**  Integration with major email providers (Gmail, Outlook) and cloud storage services (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive) is crucial.
*   **Content Parsing:**  The app must reliably parse a wide variety of document formats.
*   **Mobile OS Compatibility:**  Maintain compatibility with the latest versions of iOS and Android.



### Risks

*   **Content Parsing Accuracy:**  Inaccurate or incomplete content parsing could frustrate users.
*   **API Integration Instability:**  Reliance on third-party APIs introduces a risk of service disruptions.
*   **User Adoption:**  Users may be resistant to adopting a new system for knowledge organization.
*   **Data Privacy Concerns:**  Handling sensitive information requires strict adherence to privacy regulations.




### Non-Goals

*   Real-time collaboration features (e.g., shared editing of Contexts).
*   Direct document creation within the app.
*   Support for obscure or legacy file formats.
*   Integration with social media platforms.
*   Advanced analytics or reporting dashboards.



---

### Launch Checklist

1.  **Beta Program Recruitment:**  Recruit a diverse group of beta testers representing the target user personas.
2.  **Security Audit Completion:**  Complete a comprehensive security audit by an independent third party.
3.  **API Integration Validation:** Thoroughly validate all API integrations with representative data sets.
4.  **User Interface Polish:** Finalize the user interface design and address any usability concerns identified during beta testing.
5.  **App Store Submission:** Submit the app to the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
6.  **Marketing Collateral Preparation:** Prepare initial marketing materials (website landing page, short introductory video).
