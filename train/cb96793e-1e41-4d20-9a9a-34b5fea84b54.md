The static in the radio crackled to life, the voice of an old man’s whisper cutting through the night air.

*"They’re coming. Listen close."*

A distant, rhythmic drumming echoed across the fields, like hooves on dry earth. The villagers huddled in the dim glow of oil lamps, their breath shallow. The elders had warned of this day for years—the *Hollow Men*—shadows that walked in the dark, their eyes like embers, hungry for the living.

The child clutched his mother’s skirt as the first figure stepped into the moonlight. A man with a cloak draped too tightly, his face half-hidden beneath a jagged scar. His fingers twitched toward his belt, where a blade hummed silently.

*"Run,"* the mother hissed, but the child’s hands were too heavy.

The drumming swelled, louder now, a beat that pulsed like a heartbeat.

The village burned before they could reach the hills.
