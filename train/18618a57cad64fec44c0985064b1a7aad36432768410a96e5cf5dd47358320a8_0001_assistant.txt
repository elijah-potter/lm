In the United States, six thousand people a year are born with Down syndrome - a condition where people have an extra chromosome. To work towards inclusion, diversity, and the power of representation, Mattel worked with the National Down Syndrome Society to accurately release the first Barbie doll with Down syndrome. The doll's dress has butterflies and yellow and blue colors to represent Down syndrome awareness. The doll has a pendant with three chevrons representing the three copies of the 21st chromosome and ankle foot orthotics that many children with Down syndrome use. The Barbie is part of a Barbie Fashionista Line, which features dolls with vitiligo and prosthetic limbs.
