Ubuntu Australia provides support to migrants and multicultural communities and benevolent relief to socially and financially disadvantaged people to enable and empower them to participate socially and economically.
Ubuntu is a beautiful — and old — concept. It has its roots in humanist African philosophy where the idea of community is one of the building blocks of the society. The word Ubuntu is a part of a Zulu phrase which means that a person is a person through other people. Ubuntu can be translated as human kindness, but its meaning is much bigger in scope than that — it embodies the ideas of connection, community, and mutual caring for all.
