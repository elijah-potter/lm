Oh, donuts, oh, donuts, oh, how I love thee!
Can’t eat only one, can’t eat only three.
Chocolate and jelly, pumpkin and glazed --
So many flavors I’m always amazed!

Boston cream, blueberry, filled or unfilled -- 
Fill up the box, and I’m going to be thrilled.
When I run out, it makes me say, “Doh!”
Then go to the bakery and buy me some mo’!
