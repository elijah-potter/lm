**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 14, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this year, promising a vibrant celebration of agriculture, community, and family entertainment. This year’s fair will showcase the best of local livestock, produce, and craftsmanship, alongside thrilling rides, live music, and a variety of engaging activities for all ages. Attendees can anticipate a renewed focus on interactive exhibits, allowing visitors to learn more about farming practices and the importance of supporting local producers.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 22nd through August 28th, 2024, at the fairgrounds located at 2100 S. Veterans Memorial Parkway. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM. A dedicated livestock auction will be held on August 26th, providing a unique opportunity to support young farmers and witness the culmination of their year-long efforts. Daily demonstrations of traditional crafts and agricultural techniques will also be featured.

Admission to the Sangamon County Fair is $12 for adults, $8 for children ages 6-12, and free for children five and under. Season passes, offering unlimited entry for the duration of the fair, are available for $50. Discounted admission and parking rates are available for senior citizens on August 22nd. Detailed schedules, ride ticket packages, and parking information can be found at www.sangamoncountyfair.org.

**Headline Events:**

*   Livestock Auction – August 26th
*   Demolition Derby – August 23rd
*   Monster Truck Show – August 25th
*   Grandstand Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters – August 27th
*   4-H Showcase – August 24th
*   Livestock Judging Competitions – Daily
