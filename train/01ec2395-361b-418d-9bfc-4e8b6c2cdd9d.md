The door creaked open under the hesitant push of a heavy hand. Footsteps echoed faintly across the hall, muffled by thick carpet. Inside, the dim glow of an overcast streetlight cast long shadows through the gaps in the curtains. A half-eaten sandwich sat untouched on the kitchen counter, its crust crisp but untouched since morning. The scent of stale coffee lingered in the air, mingling with the faint metallic tang of old blood.

A low, uneasy hum filled the silence before the knock resounded again—sharper this time, as if someone had just realized they were waiting.

The door swung inward with a slow, deliberate sigh, revealing a figure draped in a long, dark coat, their face obscured by a hood pulled low. Their boots, scuffed and worn, made no sound against the worn linoleum. They stood just inside the threshold, arms crossed, watching the room with quiet intensity. The single candle on the table flickered, casting jagged light over their features.

*"You were expecting someone?"* The voice was smooth, almost amused, but the words carried an edge. The figure stepped forward, leaving the door ajar behind them. The room felt suddenly too small, too tense.
