The first draft was a list of echoes.
I traced the fingers of my past across the pages of what was never meant to be written.
Now, only the edges remain—sharp as the teeth of a knife, cold as the breath of a stranger.

What was supposed to be a beginning was a mistake.
A list without a hand to hold it.
A name without a face.
A door that didn’t open.
