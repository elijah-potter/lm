Pinata- Fill a pinata with pink or blue candies, chocolates, and confetti that will explode when the pinata is broken.
Gender Reveal Cake- Bake a cake or order one filled with a surprise that reveals your baby’s gender.  
Balloons- Fill a balloon with glitter, confetti, powder, or paint that is pink or blue. When it pops, it announces the new baby’s gender. 
Gender Reveal Lights- Have someone decorate with pink or blue lights, then when the time comes for the big reveal, have someone turn on the lights.
