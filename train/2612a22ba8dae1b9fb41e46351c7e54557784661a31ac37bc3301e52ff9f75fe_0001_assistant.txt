1. La Yuca - a lovely restaurant offering French fares and a 5-course tasting menu.
2. Crim Dell Bridge - an iconic bridge located on the campus of William and Mary. It's rumored that if you kiss your partner while crossing the bridge, you'll be lovers for life.
3. Best of Williamsburg Walking Tour - a private 2-hour comprehensive walking tour that can be personalized for a custom experience.
4. Williamsburg Winery - offering tours and tasting or you can reserve a unique package for a more romantic razzle-dazzle.
5. Lakewood Trails - offers traditional English-style riding as a small group experience.  Or book a 'couples only ride' that features a romantic picnic at Williamsburg Stonehouse Stables farm.
