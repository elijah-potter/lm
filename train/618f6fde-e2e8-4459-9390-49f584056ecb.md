The decision to prioritize community over individual desires in a project led to prolonged conflicts over resource allocation.
Despite initial agreement on fairness, differing interpretations of "equitable contribution" triggered repeated debates, forcing a mandatory mediation session.
The team ultimately resolved it by establishing a transparent scoring system for contributions, revealing that hidden biases had skewed initial perceptions.
While the process was exhausting, it strengthened team cohesion and set clear expectations for future collaboration.
