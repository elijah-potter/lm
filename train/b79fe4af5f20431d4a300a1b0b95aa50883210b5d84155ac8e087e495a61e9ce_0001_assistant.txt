Host: Welcome to the part of the show we call Dada or No Dada. With me, I have the lovely Nicole. Give it up for NI-COLE!!!! (The crowd cheers and claps.)
Host: Hi, Nicole. Where are you from?
Nicole: Lincoln. Lincoln, Nebraska.
Host: Okay, and what brings you to our show?
Nicole: Well, I am pregnant.
Host: Congratulations!
Nicole: Thank you. That is the good news. (She fights to hold back the tears.) Well, I know who the father is and I spoke to him. He said it isn't his and he does not want to help me raise this child. (The crowd boos). And, I want to raise this child and win "Mom of the Year" awards. (The crowd cheers.)
Host: Okay. Who is the man?
Nicole: His name is Jimmy.
Host: Jimmy, Jimmy, Jimmy. We have found Jimmy and brought him to the show. We have a lot to break down. Come in, Jimmy. (The crowd boos.)
Jimmy: Don't boo me. I am not the father.
Host: Are you sure? Are you willing to have a paternity test? Well, are you, Jimmy?
Jimmy: Yes, but that isn't even necessary. I am an alien from a different planet. My species reproduces asexually. (The crowd laughs hysterically. Jimmy then turns into your average green alien. The crowd is in disbelief.)
Jimmy: My alien species is called Goobber. We reproduce asexually. Let me show you how. (Jimmy opens his mouth and pulls out a golden egg. He puts the egg on the ground and it hatches into a female alien baby. Jimmy cuddles the baby lovingly.)
Host: Wow, what a turn of events. I was not expecting this.
Jimmy: My first child. A lovely girl. I love her. Do you still want me to take a paternity test?
Host: Wow, Jimmy. No, I think you made your point. That was... amazing. Nicole, what do you want to say?
Nicole: I am sorry that I thought you were the father. I made a mistake. I still like you and want to be friends.
Jimmy: Apology accepted. Let's be friends. We can help each other raise our babies.
Nicole: Yes, let's do that.
Host: Well, there you have it. Another case is solved on Dada or No Dada!
