**The Hollow Room**

The wind howled through the skeletal trees, carrying the scent of damp earth and something older—rust and forgotten things. Inside the abandoned church, the air was thick with the weight of centuries. The stained-glass windows, long shattered, let in only the sickly glow of the moon, filtering through cracks like dying embers.

Emma knelt beside the altar, her fingers brushing the cold stone. The last time she’d been here, the priest had spoken of ghosts whispering in the rafters. But she knew better. These places didn’t hold spirits. They held *echoes*.

Then—footsteps.

Not hers. Not the church’s. The sound was too precise, too deliberate. She stood, heart hammering, as the door creaked open. The figure stepped inside, cloaked in black, their face half-hidden beneath a hood. A single candle flickered in their hand, casting long shadows across the walls.

*"You shouldn’t have come back,"* the stranger murmured, voice like dry leaves scraping stone.

Emma’s breath hitched. The church had always felt like a tomb. But this time, the silence before the answer was the worst part.
