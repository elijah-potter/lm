The morning shift change in the warehouse found me stacking crates on the pallet rack when the forklift operator spotted my stack. He paused, tilted his head, and asked, *"You got those by yourself?"* I nodded, exhausted but proud. He grinned, *"Not bad. You’re new, huh?"* I laughed, *"Yeah, just started yesterday."* He handed me a fresh pair of gloves. *"Keep it up. Someone’s gonna notice."* By the end of the shift, I had moved enough boxes to earn an extra hour’s pay—something no one else had bothered to track. It wasn’t a grand achievement, but it was mine.
