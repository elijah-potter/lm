The old clock tower stood silent in the mist, its hands frozen at 11:11.
Every year at this hour, the villagers would gather to hear the tale of Elias, who swore he saw a figure standing beneath it.
*"The clock never stops,"* Elias whispered, his voice cracking like old parchment. *"It just… repeats."*

The crowd murmured, but no one dared to ask what happened at midnight. The wind howled, carrying the echo of his voice once more. *"The clock never stops..."*
