The **Great Wall of China** stretches approximately **21,196.18 km** (13,165.7 mi) with segments like the **Jiankou** (ruined) and **Mutianyu** (well-preserved) sections. It follows natural and man-made boundaries: the **Jingshan Mountain** to the north, the **Yellow Sea** to the east, the **Hengshan Mountains** to the south, and the **Huai River** to the west. Modern sections use **stone, brick, and tile**, while older stretches feature **earthen ramparts**. Key administrative regions (e.g., **Shanxi, Hebei, Inner Mongolia**) overlie its route.
