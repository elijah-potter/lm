A floating market in a city where rivers and canals shift like liquid dreams, docks rebuilt daily in the sand of a beach city that moves with the tide. Neon signs flicker in a language of half-remembered dialects, and the air hums with the sound of laughter and distant, urgent voices. Markets stretch into the sky on makeshift bridges, vendors selling items that aren’t quite real—clothes woven from starlight, tools shaped by the wind, foods that dissolve on the tongue. Storm clouds roll in from the horizon, and the water mirrors the sky as it changes color mid-air. The entire setting breathes and shifts, a living canvas of fleeting moments where time itself seems to bend.
