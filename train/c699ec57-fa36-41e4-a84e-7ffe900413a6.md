A holographic projection flickers over the worn conference table, displaying fragmented visions of distant cities—one a sprawling grid of neon-lit towers, another a circular monolith pulsing with data streams. The room hums with tension as the elders debate: *"Will the collapse be managed?"* A hand hovers over a terminal, fingers twitching at the glow of a single line: **2078: The last trade route burns.** The air smells of ozone and old coffee. A child’s voice, muffled through a cracked earpiece, repeats: *"Mama says we’ll be safe."* The adults exchange glances—some hope, some know. Outside, the blackout lasts longer than the last transmission.
