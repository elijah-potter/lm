When the map became the road, and the pencil the trail—
a single stroke transformed the chaos of possibility
into the rhythm of a tale still unfolding.
The first ink of intention: a spark in the dark.
The second: a decision, a choice no one else saw.
By the third, the audience was already listening.
The story didn’t just unfold—it was woven into the air,
each twist a thread of fate, each pause a breath.
The world didn’t need to understand the *how*,
only that the *why* had found its voice.
Now the characters walk, and the script isn’t just read—
it’s *lived*.
