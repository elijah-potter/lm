Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a bunny named Benny who loved to hop around. One sunny morning, Benny found a tiny red apple under his favorite patch of clover. He picked it up and giggled, "Yummy!"

Benny had never tasted an apple before! He nibbled a little bit and felt happy. Then he heard a soft chirp. Benny looked down and saw a little bird named Pip sitting on a branch. Pip had a shiny golden feather that sparkled like the sun.

"Hello, Pip," said Benny. "Did you find a shiny treasure too?"

Pip nodded. "Not really, but I found a little bug that was scared. Can you help me?"

Benny hopped over to the bug and gently picked it up. "Of course, Pip! Here you go."

The bug was tiny and scared, but Benny made it feel safe. "You can fly away now," said Benny. "Thank you for helping!"

Pip smiled and flew away, while Benny watched. He knew he had done the right thing. From that day on, Benny always looked for ways to help others, and Pip visited him every day to thank him.

They played together, shared their snacks, and Benny learned that being kind made everyone happy. And so, Benny and Pip became the best of friends, hopping and chirping happily together in their little garden.
