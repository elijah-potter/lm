A forgotten coffee cup, left half-full in the break room fridge, was the last item on a shift manager’s list of things to clean. At 11:45 PM, after everyone else had left, she wiped the condensation off the glass, dried the rim with a dishtowel, and tucked it neatly into the cupboard’s shelf. The next morning, the cup was still there—clean, fresh, and ready for the first person who walked by. That small act, though unnoticed, kept the routine running, the space tidy, and the morning brew perfect.
