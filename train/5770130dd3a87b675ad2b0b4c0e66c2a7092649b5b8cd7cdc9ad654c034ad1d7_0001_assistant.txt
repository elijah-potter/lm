There are many diets worldwide, but a whole-food diet is one of the healthiest. Why is that? It's because this way of eating cuts out processed foods. This includes anything in a box that is not whole such as instant pasta salads, boxed desserts, and more. 

Whole foods include natural proteins such as chicken, fish, beef, and pork. It is also inclusive of cheeses and whole, natural milk. Of course, fresh fruits and vegetables are whole foods, nuts, seeds, beans, whole grains, and eggs. These foods are commonly found on the outside parameters of the grocery store, farmer's markets, and the butcher shop.

Eating a whole-food diet has several benefits for our health. You will have healthy cholesterol levels, lower blood pressure, helps digestion, no refined sugars in your diet, and can even help you gain muscle. Whole foods also lessen the chance of getting cancer from processed foods.
