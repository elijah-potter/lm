Welcome to our collection of free resources made by teachers, for teachers.
The concept is simple: With limited budgets and little time, Japanese language teachers are constantly looking for innovative ideas and resources to use in their classrooms. If all our visiting teachers donated just one of their resources, we would have a pool of thousands of resources at our fingertips. This would save us all time and money, and also encourage other teachers to visit, who would donate their own resources, giving us even more resources to choose from and saving us even more time and money!
Our collection of over 750 resources currently includes:
* lesson plans
* unit plans
* powerpoint presentations
* reading comprehension activities
* interactive worksheets
* links to online resources
* and more!
Teachers who use resources from this site are strongly encouraged to donate their own resources to the network, helping to build a comprehensive collection of resources for Japanese language teachers.
To donate a resource please email an attachment in any size or format!
We'll take care of the rest.
All donations gratefully accepted.
As a space-saving measure, many of our resources are converted to PDF.
Need to modify? Email us to request Word versions of resources so you can edit it to suit your needs..
