The purpose of this study is to learn how people have dealt with ringworm and other types of fungus-related rashes. This particular research is focused on understanding how people of all ages feel about being diagnosed with ringworm or other fungus-related diseases and how they may have dealt with the diagnosis. In this study, we'll ask you questions about ringworm and other fungus-related diseases, and in particular, whether you have ever been diagnosed with such diseases. The study is 20 questions about your diagnosis if any, treatment, how you felt, and how you felt you were perceived by others. If you find the study makes you uncomfortable, you may stop at any time by simply closing the study. If you think you may be made uncomfortable with the subject matter, we suggest that you do not participate in the study.
