The year is 2040. A group of astronauts are on a mission to Pluto, the farthest planet from the sun. They have been traveling for months and are finally nearing their destination. As they get closer, they can see that Pluto is a strange and beautiful world covered in ice and snow. Upon approach, they spot a large heart-shaped region on its surface.

The astronauts land their spaceship and step out onto the surface of Pluto, the first to ever do so. As they explore, they come across something unexpected. They find a large, metallic object buried in the ice. The astronauts approach with trepidation.

After some initial scans, they use their tools to dig up the object and are amazed by what they find. The object is a spaceship, made of a strange, unknown material, and it is powered by a source of energy that the astronauts do not understand.

The astronauts decide to take the spaceship back to Earth for further study. As they travel back to Earth, they can't help but wonder who built this spaceship and why they left it on Pluto. They also wonder if there are other spaceships out there, waiting to be discovered. The astronauts know that their discovery is just the beginning.
