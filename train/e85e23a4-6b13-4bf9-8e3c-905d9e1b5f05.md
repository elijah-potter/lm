A flickering streetlight cast long shadows as the lone traveler paused under a half-collapsed wooden sign reading *"Exit: Nowhere."* Their phone’s battery died minutes ago, leaving only a faint GPS signal flickering in the corner of the cracked screen. The abandoned train tracks ahead stretched into the mist, their once-guarded rails now rusted and overgrown. A distant, echoing train whistle—once familiar—now sounded like a warning from a distant past. The map in their pocket was torn, the only recognizable landmark a half-eroded graffiti of a broken compass pointing toward the horizon. A sudden gust of wind rattled the branches of a gnarled tree, its leaves whispering secrets no one could decipher. The realization settled over them like a shroud: they had walked too long without direction.
