Name: Mr. Sage
Occupation: Sensei, Former Vigilante
Height: 6'7"
Weight: 290 pounds

Bio:
Mr. Sage is a fearless sensei with a remarkable past, which he is now striving to move forward from while imparting his wisdom to his students. Possessing an imposing physical presence, standing at 6'7" and weighing 290 pounds, he is a force to be reckoned with. In his days of vigilantism, if Mr. Sage was spotted on the streets, even the police would step aside, allowing him to carry out his crime-fighting activities. He possessed the ability to single-handedly take on entire groups of criminals, instilling both fear and admiration among the masses.

The source of Mr. Sage's extraordinary power remains a mystery, as nobody has been able to replicate his achievements. Some speculate that he has unlocked a hidden potential within himself, something truly unique. His strikes against criminals are often described as superhuman, pushing the boundaries of what is humanly possible. It is no wonder that his actions inspired both awe and trepidation.

Despite his legendary exploits, Mr. Sage has chosen to embrace a peaceful existence in his current phase of life. His focus now lies in training his students, passing on his extensive knowledge and skills to the next generation. Through his teachings, he hopes to shape them into capable individuals who can continue the fight against crime. As retirement beckons on the horizon, Mr. Sage aspires to leave a lasting legacy built upon the principles of courage, discipline, and justice.
