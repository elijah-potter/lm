The abandoned **Blackthorn Warehouse** in Whitby’s docks—once a bustling grain terminal—now hosts fleeting midnight markets. Vendors set up stalls in the crumbling concrete halls for one hour before the police raid. The scent of damp timber and spilled coffee lingers, mingling with the distant hum of the sea. The last time I was there, a street musician played *Auld Lang Syne* on a rusted guitar while a group of drifters traded secrets in the shadows. The door closes at dawn.
