**Lost City of Varellis: The Forgotten Realm of the Obsidian Coast**

---
**Overview**
Nestled between jagged cliffs and a perpetual mist-shrouded sea, Varellis was once a thriving trade hub of the **Obsidian Coast**, a forgotten archipelago where silver mines and salt flats thrived under the shadow of the **Dusk Mountains**. By the 18th century, its golden age faded—first due to volcanic eruptions, then the rise of the **Iron Dominion** and the collapse of its silver trade routes. Today, ruins still whisper of its glory: the **Crown of the Obsidian**, a geodesic relic carved from the mountains; the **Salt Cathedral**, where merchants bartered in whispers; and the **Drowned Library**, its pages preserved in brine.

---
**Key Attractions**

1. **The Obsidian Spire**
   - A 300m monolith carved with **floating script**—a puzzle of celestial geometry. Locals claim it pulses faintly at dawn.
   - **How to Experience**: Descend the spiral stairways (steep, narrow, and slick with algae). Bring a **silver coin** (or a story) to leave at the base.

2. **The Salt Cathedral**
   - A cavern where salt crystals form **bioluminescent veins** under moonlight. Traders once left offerings of **black pearls** and **mystic mirrors** here.
   - **Best Time**: Visit at **low tide**—the sea laps against the arches, creating a "mirror sea." Carry a **dried salt tablet** to press against your forehead for "clarity."

3. **The Drowned Library**
   - A submerged archive in the **Sea of Whispers**, accessible only via a **corroded ladder** (now half-sunk). Books float on the surface, their pages warped by time.
   - **How to Access**: Bring a **weighted stone** (or a willing diver). The water is **10°C year-round**—bring a **wool cloak** and a **flint knife** for cutting rope.

4. **The Dusk Mountains**
   - Volcanic peaks where the sun **never sets**—only a perpetual twilight. The air hums with **harmonic vibrations** from ancient bells.
   - **Adventure**: Hike to the **Peak of Echoes**, where voices from the past repeat your last words. Bring a **mirror** to reflect them back.

---
**Cultural & Practical Notes**

- **Currency**: The **Obsidian Nugg** (worth ~500 coins today). Trade for:
  - **Mystic Mirrors** (show the future for 1 hour)
  - **Salted Honey** (lasts forever)
  - **Drowned Ink** (used to write on **seaweed paper**)
- **Safety**: The **Iron Dominion** patrols the coast. Avoid the **Black Sands**, where the ground **sinks** underfoot.
- **Local Legends**:
  - The **"Last Merchant"** still trades at the **Drowned Library** on high tides.
  - The **"Obsidian Fox"** (a spectral creature) guards the **Crown of the Obsidian**—offer a **story** to pass inspection.
- **Weather**: Unpredictable. Bring a **rain poncho** (it rains **saltwater**) and a **compass** (the **Iron Dominion** uses this to mark graves).

---
**How to Visit (If You Dare)**
1. **Arrival**: Board the **Ghost Ship of the Dusk**, a vessel bound for the **Obsidian Coast** (last departure: **Year 1842**). Pay in **silver or stories**.
2. **Guides**: Hire a **Drowned Scholar** (prices vary; ask for a **handshake under the Salt Cathedral**).
3. **Departure**: The ruins **do not welcome you back**. Some say the **Sea of Whispers** claims the lost.

---
**Final Warning**
Varellis is not for the faint of heart. The **Obsidian Coast** is a place where **time moves differently**. Carry a **dried herb** (like **Sea Sage**) to ward off the **Dusk Dwellers**—creatures that steal memories.
