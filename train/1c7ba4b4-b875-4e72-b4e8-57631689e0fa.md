A gloved hand grips a tiny, half-silvered sphere the size of a coin, its edges still warm from the forge. The smith’s other hand snatches it away, fingers brushing the rough heat before it cools—then passes it to the apprentice’s waiting palm, who clutches it carefully, turning it in the dim light to study the faint, swirling runes etched into its surface. The apprentice’s breath fogs the air as they whisper a single word into the sphere’s hollow center, sending ripples of sound that shimmer like liquid metal. The smith’s gaze locks onto the apprentice’s, unspoken command: *pass it forward*. The apprentice’s fingers tremble slightly as they transfer it to the next recipient—a merchant’s calloused hand, which cradles the sphere before offering it to a scholar, who holds it up to the candlelight, tracing the intricate designs with deliberate precision. The scholar’s voice, low and measured, carries a promise of unseen knowledge: *"This is no mere ornament… but a key."* The merchant’s eyes gleam with the weight of power, then vanish as the sphere is handed to a guard, who slips it into a pouch marked with a sigil of iron and fire. The guard’s lips curl into a knowing smile as they pocket it, their gaze lingering on the next link in the chain. The scene shifts—now a woman in a crowded bazaar, her fingers deftly plucking the sphere from a vendor’s palm, her touch lingering just long enough to sense its hum. She tucks it into her cloak, the weight of its magic pressing against her skin like a secret. The crowd around her fades into the background, only the sound of distant laughter and the clink of coins remaining. The sphere hums faintly in her pocket, a silent promise of what lies beyond the next threshold.
