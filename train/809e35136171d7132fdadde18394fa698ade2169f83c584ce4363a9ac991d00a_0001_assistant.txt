Show: Clued In

Pitch: Clued In is a new, exciting, and mysterious show where twelve contestants compete to solve a case, fabricated by a professional investigator. Contestants will be given the case background, a clue, a stipend, and a camera crew. What they do from there is entirely up to their own intuition and thinking skills. At the end of the week, contestants will enter into a private interview with the investigator, as well as two other judges, to present their findings. The contestant having most accurately solved the case will walk away with a cash prize, and the title of "Certified Sleuth."
