A farmer's daughter milked her father's cows every morning and took the pail of milk to the market to sell. One sunny morning as she strolled to the marketplace, she started daydreaming about the things she could buy with the money she made from tomorrow's milk. 

The milkmaid thought to herself, "First, I can turn milk into cream. Then I will:
* turn the cream into butter and sell it;
* use the money to buy some eggs to hatch some chickens;
* sell some chickens to buy a beautiful new dress;
* wear my dress to the fair and impress the young fellows;
* toss my head and turn away when they come to flirt with me."

She was so wrapped up in her thoughts that she forgot about the pail of milk and tossed her head. The milk fell and splattered all over the ground, leaving her with daydreams, but not a drop of milk!
