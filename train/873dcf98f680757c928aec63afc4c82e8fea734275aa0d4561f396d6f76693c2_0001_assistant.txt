Sundays are always Mama and Baby’s Day out. Mama places Baby into his stroller, secures him, and places a blanket on him for warmth. Then, the two head out for a walk about town, and Baby gets to see all the sights and sounds of the autumn afternoon.
As Mama walks down the street, colored orange and yellow by the fallen leaves, she greets her neighbors as she walks past them. Sometimes the neighbors stop to coo at Baby, who smiles shyly at them. Sometimes they stop to pinch Baby’s rosy cheeks, but he likes that less.
Mama pushes the stroller across the road, making sure to look both ways before she crosses the street. Then the two head for Mama’s favorite café, where she likes to sit at the tables set up outdoors. She reads a good book while she sips on her coffee; meanwhile Baby watches people as they walk by. Sometimes he’ll spot a dog on a leash, and try to reach out towards them. At times like this Mama laughs and gently rocks the stroller, giving Baby a little ride.
As the sun sets, it begins to grow cold, and Baby’s blanket comes to good use. Mama finishes up her coffee and reluctantly closes her book. “Time to go home and get dinner started,” she says to Baby, who gives a lazy yawn in return. 
They head for home, the magical day coming to an end. One day Baby will grow up, and choose to spend time with his friends instead. Until then, Sundays will always be Mama and Baby’s special day out.
