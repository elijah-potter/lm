There are three types of muscle tissue found in the human body:
1. Skeletal muscle: This muscle is connected to our bones and helps us move. We can control it consciously, and it looks striped under a microscope.
2. Smooth muscle: It is found inside our body, like in our stomach and blood vessels. This muscle works automatically without us thinking about it.
3. Cardiac muscle: This muscle is only in our heart. It beats without us telling it to, and its job is to pump blood.
