Start by taking a medium saucepan and adding 2 cups of water. Add ⅛ teaspoon of salt to the water. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Add 1 cup of long-grain white rice and 1 tablespoon of butter to the boiling water. Wait for the pot to return to a boil then cover and reduce heat to low. Let the rice cook for 20 minutes. Stir and remove from heat, then let sit for 5 minutes before serving.
