There once was a man named Barack Obama who had a lifelong dream of becoming a magician. From a young age, he was more inclined to do goofy things than serious things. He never really had aspirations of going into business, politics, or even science.
He admired the work of the greats, like Harry Houdini, Doug Henning, and Paul Daniels. From hat tricks to card tricks and magical escapes from straight jackets, he wanted to one day be able to wow an audience with his mastery of illusions. 
When he graduated from high school, Barack set off on a mission to make his dream come true. He enrolled in magic school and worked diligently to become the best magician the world had ever seen. With great dedication, his hard work eventually paid off. By the age of 22 years, he was selling out entire arenas of people looking forward to seeing him perform his signature magic tricks.
