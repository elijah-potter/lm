At Riverbend City Child Development Center, our program evolved from the sociocultural cognitive theory of Lev Vygotsky. Vygotsky believed that children learn best through social interaction and collaboration. He also believed that children learn through a process called scaffolding, which is when an adult or more experienced peer provides support to a child who is trying to learn something new.

We believe this is a valuable tool for helping children learn and grow. That's why our program is designed to provide children with opportunities to interact with adults and other children and to learn through scaffolding.

For example, we offer a variety of activities that encourage children to collaborate, such as group projects and cooperative games. In addition to encouraging children to interact with each other, regardless of their age, we also believe that it is important for children to have a positive and supportive environment where they feel welcome and included. We create such an environment by providing a safe and nurturing space for children to learn and grow.

All of our early childhood teachers are trained in play therapy and overseen by licensed child therapists. 

We are committed to providing children with the best possible environment for learning and growing.
