1. What did Mongol governors want to do after their armies has crushed all resistance?
Answer: Revive commerce, rebuild industries, and encourage prosperity
2. What was the Mongol conqueror's ultimate goal?
Answer: governance over all human civilization
3. What types of people did the Mongols go to great lengths to recruit to the empire?
Answer: skilled artisans and merchants
4. What did the Mongols do with recruited artisans and merchants?
Answer: They were uprooted and transported for thousands of miles to locations where they could be the greatest use
5. What famous Venetian traveler entered the Mongol Empire voluntarily?
Answer: Marco Polo
