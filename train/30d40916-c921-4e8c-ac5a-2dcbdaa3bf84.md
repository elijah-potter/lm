The air hums with the weight of unspoken things.
Every step echoes like a question left unanswered.
Mirrors hold faces that have never been yours.
The doorframe is a threshold, a ghostly gap where the world lingers—half there, half not—
a silence thick with what might have been.
Time bends like old fabric, frayed at the edges.
You reach for a name that was never yours to say.
The absence is not absence at all—it is presence,
a quiet, suffocating embrace.
