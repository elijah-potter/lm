A dimly lit, industrial basement hosted a rave with mismatched neon signs flickering in graffiti-covered halls. Attendees in vintage leather jackets danced to a synthwave track on a looped vinyl record player while neon-lit holograms of ancient alien artifacts floated above the crowd. One guest, wearing a tattered priest’s robe, officiated a silent, ritualistic "celebration" around a flickering oil lamp, chanting in a language that sounded like a mix of Latin and Morse code. The energy was surreal—part techno cult, part forgotten horror movie set—all set against the backdrop of a city that didn’t care.
