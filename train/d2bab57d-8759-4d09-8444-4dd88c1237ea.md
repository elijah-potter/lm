The river hummed its secret song beneath the boughs of an ancient oak, where time had carved its teeth into the bark. A traveler stepped inside its embrace, fingers tracing the ripple of water as it whispered against their palm—*always moving, never still.*

They found the first word there: *"Lost."* The oak’s roots coiled around the stones of a forgotten path, guiding only the brave. The river’s current carried the echo of a voice: *"Find what you leave behind."* The traveler knelt, picked up a smooth pebble, and watched it spin—forever, endlessly. Somewhere, a clock ticked. Somewhere, a door creaked open. The world was both a mirror and a puzzle.

They left the pebble where the current could claim it.
