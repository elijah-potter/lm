The **Great Wall of China** stretches from **Liaoning to Hebei** (approx. 6,800 km).
The **Amazon Rainforest** is bounded by the **Andes Mountains (west)** and the **Atlantic Ocean (east)**.
A **city block** is defined by **intersecting streets** (e.g., 1st Ave & 5th St, NYC).
The **Great Barrier Reef** is encircled by the **Australian continent (southeast to northwest)**.
**Mount Everest** is defined by its base at **Nepal/China border (Tibet)** and summit at **8,848.86 m (2020)**.
The **Eifel Tower** has a fixed base at **Reims, France**, and extends **330 m (1,083 ft)**.
**Lake Erie** is bordered by **Ohio, Pennsylvania, Canada, Michigan, and New York**.
**The Sahara Desert** spans **4,600,000 km²** between **Morocco to Sudan**.
**The Mariana Trench** is bounded by the **Mariana Ridge (west)** and **Pacific Plate (east)**.
**The Amazon River** flows between **Andes (west)** and **Atlantic (east)**.
**The Arctic Circle** is a **latitude line (~66.5° N)** with fixed boundaries at **North Pole (north)** and **Antarctica (south)**.
**The Berlin Wall** once ran from **Brandenburg Gate to Checkpoint Charlie (1961–1989)**.
**The Grand Canyon** is defined by the **Colorado River (west)** and **Hualapai Mountains (east)**.
**The Amazon Rainforest’s southern boundary** is the **Paraná River (Brazil)**.
**The Red Sea** is bordered by **Egypt (north)**, **Sudan (south)**, **Eritrea/Yemen (east)**, and **Saudi Arabia (west)**.
**The Alps** span from **France to Switzerland** and extend into **Italy, Austria, Germany, and Slovenia**.
**The Pacific Ring of Fire** runs along **North America’s west coast, Japan, Indonesia, and New Zealand**.
