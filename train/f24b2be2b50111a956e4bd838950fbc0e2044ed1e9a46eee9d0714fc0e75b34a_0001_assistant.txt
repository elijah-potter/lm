The woman sipped her coffee and contemplated the patch of dirt in her yard that she would be planting today through her kitchen window.  It was time to start the spring garden and she'd been looking forward to this all week. The plants had been purchased and were just waiting for her in the back shed. She finished her coffee, grabbed her gardening gloves, and headed into the sunny backyard.

She quickly consulted the list she'd made of the plants she wanted in first:
1. Black Beauty Tomato
2. Agastache
3. Tom Thumb lettuce
4. Carolina Reaper peppers
5. Gem corn
After wiping down her wheelbarrow, she put the young plants into it, along with her watering can, trowel, and hoe. Then she wheeled it over to her dirt patch and began to work under the warm spring sky.

Several hours later, the woman sat back on her heels, wiped her brow, and surveyed her work. The rows of young plants stood proud and neat, the rows hoed straight and perfectly spaced. She was pleased with the progress so far. Her back was telling her it was time to take a rest, so she got up and headed into the house for a cold beer and to dream of the bushels of fresh produce and flowers she'd soon be able to sell at her farmer's market stand.
