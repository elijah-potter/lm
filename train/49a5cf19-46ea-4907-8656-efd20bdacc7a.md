**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 14, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, promising a vibrant celebration of agriculture, community, and family entertainment. This year’s fair features expanded livestock exhibits, thrilling carnival rides, and a diverse array of vendors showcasing local crafts and culinary delights. Visitors can anticipate demonstrations of traditional farming techniques, opportunities to interact with farm animals, and a renewed focus on showcasing the region’s rich agricultural heritage.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 22nd through August 26th, 2024, at the Sangamon County Fairgrounds in Lincoln. Gates will open daily at 9:00 a.m., with evening events concluding at 10:00 p.m. on weekdays and 11:00 p.m. on Saturday. Daily admission includes access to all exhibits, demonstrations, and grandstand events, with separate ticket prices for specific attractions like the carnival. 

Admission is $12 for adults, $8 for children ages 5-12, and free for children under the age of 5. Discounted advance tickets are available online at www.sangamoncountyfair.org. Parking is $10 per vehicle. Below are some of the fair’s featured events:

*   Grandstand Tractor Pull
*   Livestock Judging Competitions
*   Demolition Derby
*   Blue Ribbon Baking Contest
*   Professional Rodeo Series
*   Evening Concert Featuring The Heartland Ramblers
