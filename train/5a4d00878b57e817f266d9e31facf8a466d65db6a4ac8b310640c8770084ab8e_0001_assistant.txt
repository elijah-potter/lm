The egg cracked and light rushed in
The turtle was finally free
Slowly, he climbed out of his egg
And started a path toward the sea

The way to the water was perilous
Watch out for crabs and birds!
The turtle followed the downward slope
Giving himself encouraging words

Much time passed and the water was near
The turtle was almost there! 
Finally, he dove into the surf
Hello ocean, goodbye air!
