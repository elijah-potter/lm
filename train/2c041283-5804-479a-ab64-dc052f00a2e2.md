In the dust-choked archives of forgotten history, there lies **Vermilion’s Last Embrace**—a ruined city where the last echoes of human life linger like smoke in a windless sky. Built upon the bones of an ancient trade caravan route, its spires crumble into the desert where the dunes whisper secrets of a people who once traded amber for whispers and salt for time.

The **Grand Canal**, once a ribbon of sapphire, now flows with stagnant brine, its banks lined with skeletal temples that whisper to the wind. Locals say the **Silent Watchers**—ghostly figures clad in tattered robes—still patrol the ruins, their lanterns casting long shadows on the cobblestones.

The **Clockwork Tower**, a relic of a lost industrial age, stands half-buried in sand, its gears frozen in time. Some claim it hums faintly at night, a ghostly reminder of a civilization that once harnessed the earth’s magic.

The **Bridge of the Forgotten**, a crumbling stone arch spanning a dried-up riverbed, is said to be the only bridge that leads to **Eldrin’s Hollow**, a place where the dead do not rest but wander, searching for something lost in the past.

Tours are rare, and those who venture into Vermilion’s ruins return either hollow-eyed or with stories that vanish like mist. The city is a tomb, but its walls hold the breath of those who dared to remember.
