A therapist noticed a client hesitating, eyes flickering toward the door. Their hands clenched the armrests of the couch, knuckles white. The silence stretched like a second breath. Then: *"I think… I don’t know if I’m ready."* The words spilled out in a rush, raw and exposed. The client had planned to leave, but the doubt—*Was this really what they wanted?*—had shifted their resolve. The therapist wrote it down: *"Mindset shift detected. Proceed with caution."* Outside, rain tapped against the window, a metaphor for the indecision still unspooling.
