"Many Hands", "M. Hands", "D. Hands" and "Diverse Hands" are terms used when several creators worked to complete a comic, usually more than the space allowed for staff would be able to list. Often "Many Hands" would contribute when an issue was running late, with various creators contributing work to a single panel or page.
