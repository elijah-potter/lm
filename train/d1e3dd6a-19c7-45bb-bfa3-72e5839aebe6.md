A fragile pact, its weight held by thin air—
each word a gamble, each promise a debt left unpaid.
The clock ticks slow, yet time runs on.
What starts as whispered trust fades to silence.
A shadow stretches long, the shadow of unfulfilled vows.
The hand that shook mine trembles now, unsure how to mend the cracks.
The dust settles into a hollow echo—once spoken, now lost to memory.
Some promises are built on sand; others, on promises broken before they were made.
