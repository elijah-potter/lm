If you're considering kidney donation, there are some things to know before. The donor must be 18 years old, have an adequately functioning kidney, and be in exceptional physical and mental health. You will have to go through a series of tests to make sure you are healthy enough to donate. If the transplant is approved, you will go ahead in making plans for the surgery and recovery time. The surgery takes about 2-3 hours. The surgery is usually laparoscopic, which is minimally invasive. They do this to reduce scarring and recovery time. A short hospital stay is standard. The post-operative recovery can take up to 6 weeks. There will be follow-up appointments and monitoring to make sure you are recovering and are healthy.
