If you have a craving for potatoes, try these tasty dishes:

- Gnocchi: This is a style of pasta made from riced potatoes. It's served with a sauce, just like spaghetti.
- Au Gratin: This a German layered cheese and potato dish that you bake in the oven.
- Latkes: This traditionally Jewish dish is made of shredded potato and matzo meal pan fried into pancakes. 
- Baked wedges: Cut the potato into wedges and bake with olive oil and herbs.
