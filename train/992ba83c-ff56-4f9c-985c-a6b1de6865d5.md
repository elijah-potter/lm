## Bookstore Serendipity (Seattle, WA)

I was browsing the poetry section at Elliott Bay Book Company last Saturday afternoon, utterly absorbed in a collection by Mary Oliver. You were examining the shelves across from me, a shaft of late-afternoon sunlight catching the dust motes dancing around your head. I noticed the way you paused, a thoughtful crease forming between your eyebrows as you considered a volume of Rilke. There was a quiet intensity about you that resonated deeply. I almost said something, a clumsy observation about the beauty of the space, but hesitated, a familiar reluctance settling in. The scent of aged paper and brewing coffee hung warmly in the air, a comforting backdrop to the unspoken moment. Your scarf, a vibrant shade of teal, was particularly striking against your dark coat. I left shortly thereafter, feeling a peculiar sense of loss for a connection that never quite materialized. 

If you recall a woman with auburn hair and a fondness for classic poetry, please reply with your favorite line from "Leaves of Grass."
