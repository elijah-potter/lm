A dimly lit apartment door creaked open under the hesitant knock. Inside, the scent of stale coffee and old books lingered. The sound repeated, sharper this time—steady, deliberate.

"Come in," a voice murmured from the hallway. The door swung inward with a groan, revealing a narrow hallway lined with flickering lanterns. A figure in a long coat stepped forward, their gloved hands gripping the frame. The coat was too large, the sleeves too long—like something not entirely human had worn it.

The voice behind them crackled: *"I’m not the one you’re looking for."* The footsteps retreated down the hall, echoing against the cold stone. The coat’s owner exhaled slowly, then turned toward the living room. A woman sat hunched over a desk, her fingers tracing the edge of an open ledger. She didn’t look up.

"Knock again if you want to be here," she said, voice flat. The lanterns flickered again, casting jagged shadows across the walls. The coat’s owner hesitated, then stepped forward. The door shut behind them with a final, heavy click.
