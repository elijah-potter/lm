An abandoned industrial complex in the outskirts of a once-thriving city, now overrun by ivy and rust. The skeletal framework of a factory looms under a sky choked with the weight of perpetual twilight, its once-glowing windows now cracked and boarded shut. The air smells of damp earth and old oil, a faint metallic tang clinging to the wind. Inside, the halls echo with the hollow sound of footsteps on broken concrete, the remnants of machinery left to rust in silent defiance. Dust motes swirl in the dim light filtering through the jagged gaps in the ceiling, casting long, shifting shadows across the floor. Somewhere, a distant alarm still clangs intermittently, a relic of the past. The place hums with a ghostly energy, a place where time has stopped, yet nothing remains still.
