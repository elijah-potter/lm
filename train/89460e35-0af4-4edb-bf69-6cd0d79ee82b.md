A quiet promise was made beneath the golden dusk,
when the river whispered secrets through its bend.
A hand pressed softly against the cheek of a stranger,
and the world held its breath—no fanfare, no sound.

A child’s laughter, once distant, now drifts near,
a shadow shifted, a door left ajar.
The wind carries the scent of rain and earth,
a promise wrapped in silence, waiting for birth.

No bells were rung, no words were spoken,
just the quiet hum of time, of love unseen.
A love that blooms where the light won’t reach,
a truth too small to be named—yet still it reaches.
