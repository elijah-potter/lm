This grammar exercise tests your ability to convert direct speech into indirect speech.
An example is given below.
Direct speech: ‘Do you think you know better than your father?’ asked his angry mother.
Indirect speech: The angry mother asked whether he thought that he knew better than his father.
There are ten questions in the grammar exercise. A sentence is given in direct speech and from the given options, you have to choose the one that correctly expresses the same idea in indirect speech.
