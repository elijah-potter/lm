So distant, disappeared in a flash of light
Over a million years ago
Yet still visible to us at night
In our dark sky, you still show

Blazing ball of flame and gas
In your time you shined so bright
Till you collapsed into your own mass
I’m sure it was a brilliant sight

Billions of lightyears away
You collapsed in supernova
But I can still see you today
To me, your life is not yet over
