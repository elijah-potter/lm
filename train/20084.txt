TEFL TESOL Certificate - ITTT
Below you can read feedback from an ITTT graduate regarding one section of their online TEFL certification course. Each of our online courses is broken down into concise units that focus on specific areas of English language teaching. This convenient, highly structured design means that you can quickly get to grips with each section before moving onto the next.
The unit is about present tenses. The system of tenses is presented through a descriptive approach what makes the grammar material understandable. Through description students can fully understand the usage of tenses. The best way to present the material is to show it in tables, all the forms can be seen with examples: positive, negative, affirmative.The Present Tenses, very difficult unit and I need to go through this unit multiple times to ensure I have completely understood it. It has been many years since I did anything educational on this subject. I will download the exercises and more examples from the internet and practice on the weekend to gain a better understanding of the subject matter.
