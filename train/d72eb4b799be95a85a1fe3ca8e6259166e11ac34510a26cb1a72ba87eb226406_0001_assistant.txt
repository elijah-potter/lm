Sykkuno is a variety streamer from Southern California. He is an American-born streamer of Asian descent and has both Chinese and Vietnamese heritage. Sykkuno first began his streaming career on the platform Twitch.tv, streaming on a regular basis from April 2019 going forward. In May of 2022, Sykkuno signed a contract with YouTube and began streaming on their platform instead. Sykkuno is represented by United Talent Agency as of August 2022.

Sykkuno is known as a variety streamer because he does not consistently stream the same game. Some games Sykkuno has played online include League of Legends, Grand Theft Auto V, Among Us, Valorant, Friday the 13th, Overcooked, Bloons Tower Defense 6, and Minecraft. Sykkuno rarely plays games solo and prefers to play games on stream with his group of friends. Sykkuno is close friends and colleagues with many other streamers, including Leslie Fu, ValkyRae, Yvonnie, Kkatamina, xQc, Disguised Toast, Micheal Reeves, LilyPichu, and Blaustoise. He regularly plays games with these friends on his YouTube channel live streams, which typically occur between 3 pm and 3 am Pacific Time. On very rare occasions, Sykunno will stream earlier than this time frame, typically to participate in tournaments, to play games with friends, or to offer a European-timezone-friendly live stream for non-American fans.
