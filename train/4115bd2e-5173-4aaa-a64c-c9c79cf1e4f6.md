A bustling market square in a small coastal town. The scent of freshly baked bread mingles with salt air. Children run between adults who greet each other by name, sharing stories of fishing catches and the latest gossip from the local general store. The café’s wooden tables are always crowded—everyone knows the regulars, the barista, and the old man who fixes bikes on weekends. A group of friends laughs over a shared plate of fish stew, while an elderly woman whispers advice to a new mother about raising her children. The town’s history is etched in faces: some nodding familiarity, others exchanging handshakes with warmth. Even the stray dog, familiar to everyone, gets a pat from anyone who sees him.
