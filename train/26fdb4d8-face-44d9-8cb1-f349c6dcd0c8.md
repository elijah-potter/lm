Flickering neon veins pulse in the alley—blue, violet, and amber rivers of electricity weave through cracked pavement, casting jagged shadows that dance in slow motion across the graffiti-scarred walls. Sunlight bleeds through the storm drains, turning the cobblestones into molten glass, reflecting distorted cityscapes in fractured pools. Moonlight, pale as old blood, filters through the skeletal branches of a dead oak, painting the earth in silver and ice, where the wind howls and the air hums with static. Every window is a lantern, some flickering with life, others extinguished, leaving only the cold glow of forgotten streetlights to stitch the darkness into a tapestry of light and absence.
