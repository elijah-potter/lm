The clock tower chimed midnight as Elias stood at the edge of the old forest, his breath misting in the cold air. He clutched a tattered map, its edges singed with forgotten ink. "The Hidden Grove" was carved into the wood, but the rest was faded, barely legible.

He traced the path through the whispering trees, following the faint scent of pine and something metallic—iron, perhaps? The path led to a crumbling stone archway, its surface etched with symbols he didn’t recognize. Inside, the air was thick with dust, but the glow of an ancient lantern pulsed faintly.

"Find the bone," whispered a voice from the shadows. Elias spun, but only the dark remained. He knelt, brushing away centuries of grime from a pedestal where a single bone lay, its surface worn smooth. Beneath it, carved into the stone, was a message:

**"ELIAS, THE KEY IS IN THE RIVER’S KEEP."**

The lantern flickered, and the symbols shifted—now forming a pattern of coordinates: **37.7749° N, 122.4194° W**. Elias pocketed the lantern and the coordinates, his mind racing. The hidden message was clear: the key was somewhere in San Francisco, at the Golden Gate Bridge’s rusted railing.
