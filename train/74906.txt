This novel guide is designed to go along with Lawn Boy by Gary Paulsen. The story is about how a young boy uses the concept of supply and demand to create a very successful summer business.
Included in this novel guide are: vocabulary terms, vocabulary test, comprehension and discussion questions, a Response to Literature writing prompt with rubric and student writing checklist, character sketch handout, literary analysis handout, a measurement conversion handout for a math activity, a novel test, and a project with planning page and rubric. The answer keys are included throughout the novel guide for the teacher.
