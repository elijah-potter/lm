A transient market square in a city under rapid redevelopment, where stalls flicker between pop-up vendors and empty lots. The air smells of freshly brewed coffee from a café that moved yesterday, and the pavement is littered with discarded napkins and half-eaten pastries. Overhead, scaffolding clings to half-renovated storefronts, while drones buzz above, mapping the next phase of construction. Children’s laughter echoes from a playground that will be gone by weekend. A single, weathered signpost marks the old town center, now a ghost of its former self. The clock on the distant skyline ticks away—no one knows how long this moment will last.
