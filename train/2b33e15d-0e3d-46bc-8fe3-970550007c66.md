A worn-out leather wallet slipped from the jacket pocket of an elderly man walking past a park bench. He squatted beside the bench, fingers brushing the ground, and found a small, tarnished silver locket beneath a crumpled newspaper. The locket’s clasp was broken, but inside lay a faded photo of a young woman in a vintage dress, her name inscribed in delicate script: *Eleanor Whitmore*.

Nearby, a child’s lost toy—a red plastic keychain shaped like a star—sat beside a half-eaten sandwich wrapper. The child, a boy in a faded baseball cap, had been searching for it for hours. He pressed his palm against the bench, his voice trembling: *“I found it. I found it!”*

The locket and the keychain glinted in the afternoon light, small but precious.
