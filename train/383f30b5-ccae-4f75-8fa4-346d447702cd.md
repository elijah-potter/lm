A lone figure in a dimly lit warehouse, surrounded by flickering fluorescent lights and discarded equipment, notices a flicker in the air. The ground beneath them trembles slightly—unusual for this location. Suddenly, the walls begin to shift, their surfaces rippling like water. The figure steps forward, heart pounding, as the floor beneath them dissolves into a swirling vortex of shadows. Above, a faint, distorted hum fills the air, and the rules of physics seem to dissolve into something else.

A hand emerges from the darkness, holding a cracked crystal orb that pulses with an eerie, unstable energy. The figure realizes the moment they touch it, the fabric of reality warps around them. Gravity reverses; gravity pulls upward, pushing them toward the ceiling. The air thickens, and colors bleed into one another. The warehouse becomes a shifting maze of impossible geometry—walls that curve, spaces that collapse, and time that loops unpredictably.

A voice, neither human nor machine, echoes in their mind: *"You are no longer bound by what you thought was law."* The figure stumbles, disoriented, as the orb’s glow intensifies, warping their perception. The scene they once took for granted—stable, predictable—now feels like a dream being unraveled. Somewhere in the chaos, a single rule remains: *Survival now depends on understanding how the system has changed.*
