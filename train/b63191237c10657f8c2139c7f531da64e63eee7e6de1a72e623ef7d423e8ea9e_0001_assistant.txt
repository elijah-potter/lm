Due to their large size, 17 out of the 30 biggest fish ever caught are sharks. For more than 60 years, the great white shark holds the record for the largest fish ever caught. The shark weighed in at 2,664 pounds and was caught in Ceduna, Australia. Here are some other top-ranked fish for the heaviest catch:

#2. Tiger Shark: Ulladala, Australia 1,785 lbs.
#3. Greenland Shark: Trondheimsfjord, Norway 1,708 lbs.
#4. Black Marlin: Cabo Blanco, Peru 1,560 lbs.
#5. Atlantic Bluefin Tuna: Aulds Cove, Nova Scotia, Canada 1,496, lbs.
