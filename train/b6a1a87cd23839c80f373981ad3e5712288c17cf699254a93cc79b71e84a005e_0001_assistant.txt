Spell Name: Frozen Orb

Concept: Frozen Orb is a powerful ice-based long-range offensive spell that not only deals massive cold damage to multiple targets but freezes/slows them down as well.

Visuals: Once cast, a snowy orb will be launched from the spell caster towards the target in a straight line fashion. As the orb travels, it shoots a flurry of icy shards in all directions. When the orb finishes its length of travel, a big explosion ensues. 

Damage Scaling: 
Damage starts with 50 at level 1. This is the damage of one individual icy shard.  
Level 2-5: +50 damage per level
Level 6-10: +30 damage per level
Level 11-20: +20 damage per level
At level 20, each shard will do 600 damage.
