Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the sunny meadow. One day, Benny met a tiny spider named Stella who spun her web in the grass.

"Can you help me?" Benny asked, looking up. Stella nodded and showed Benny how to climb a big, fluffy dandelion. Together, they picked seeds and planted them in the garden.

Soon, the seeds grew into beautiful flowers. Benny and Stella danced in the wind, singing songs as the sun set. They knew their little garden would be full of joy for everyone to enjoy.
