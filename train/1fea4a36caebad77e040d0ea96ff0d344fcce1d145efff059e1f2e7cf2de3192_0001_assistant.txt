A haiku is a short poem style of three lines. There are 17 total syllables in a haiku, but the placement of the syllables is strict. The first line is five syllables, the second line is seven syllables, and the third line is five syllables. 

Here’s an example of an autumnal haiku that uses the classic 5-7-5 style rule: 

Leaves swirl and stack up 
Pumpkin spice, acorns galore
Corn mazes to come!
