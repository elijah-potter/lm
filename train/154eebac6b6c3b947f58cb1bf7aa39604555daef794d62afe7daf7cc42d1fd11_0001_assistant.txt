• Quit more books : It doesn't take alot of time to figure out if something is worth reading, skim it and see if it's something you would want to read. If not, quit it.
• Choose books you can use instantly : Choosing a book that you can use provides a strong incentive to pay attention and remember the material.
• Create searchable notes : Creating searchable notes is essential for returning ideas quickly
• Combine Knowledge trees : You can learn more and improve reading and comprehension by "linking branches" and integrating your current book with other knowledge.
• Write a short summary : This forces you to consider what was really important about the book.
• Surround the topic : This can be done by reading a ton of books surrounding the same topic. 
• Read it twice : If a book is worth it, then you'll be to find things you didn't notice before.
