In the middle of the summer, a little turtle was walking alongside a farmer's land. He was strolling, thinking about how much he wished he could eat a fresh juicy strawberry. As he approached the farmer's land, he was surprised to see the farmer grows strawberries on his land. He crawled as fast as he could until he reached the strawberry bushes. He slowly reached out his neck to take his first bite of the giant strawberry he could find in the bush. Once he had taken his first bite, he fell in love with them. He then declared at that moment that strawberries were his favorite berry. He decided he would move his home closer to the strawberry farm. That way, he could always have fresh strawberries. The farmer noticed and surprised the turtle by planting a few bushes around the turtle's home.
