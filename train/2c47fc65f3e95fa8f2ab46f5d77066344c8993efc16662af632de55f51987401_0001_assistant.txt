Alright! Here is a list of decorations with their suggested colors for your gothic character's home:

- An easy-to-achieve and understated option might include a minimalist style in mostly dark colors: black and gray with maybe a hint of red or purple.
- Plush fabrics could give the impression of luxury even without much money: black, gray, silver, red, purple, and dark blue.
- Coffins, skulls, and macabre items are favorites with gothic people, so their home might include many of these items: black, red, gray, and silver.
- Gothic and witchy styles are often interchangeable: black, red, purple, gray, green, dark brown, and dark and blue.
