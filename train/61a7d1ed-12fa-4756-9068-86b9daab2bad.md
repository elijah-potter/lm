The wind howled through the skeletal branches of an ancient oak, whispering secrets only the earth understood. Beneath its roots, a forgotten garden bloomed—a place where time had dissolved into memory. The flowers, though long dead, still carried the scent of something sacred, something that had once been sacred to those who walked here.

A single leaf, brittle with age, drifted down from the branches, landing softly on the moss-covered stones. It was not a question, but a pause—a lull between the last sigh of the world and the first breath of what might come.

What would you name the thing that rises from the earth when the last leaf falls?
