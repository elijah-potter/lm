**Scene:**

*The room was quiet except for the faint hum of the old AC unit. Liam slumped in the chair, arms crossed, but his gaze darted between the door and the laptop screen like he was *actually* waiting for someone to walk in. His posture shifted—shoulders tense, fingers twitching near his ear—just as the door creaked open.*

**Karen:** *(softly)* "You’re still here?"

*Liam’s head snapped up, but his eyes flickered toward the wall before he met her gaze. He exhaled sharply, then cleared his throat. "Yeah. Just… thought I’d check in." His voice was monotone, but his hands clenched the armrests, nails biting into the wood. Karen’s smile was sharp, just a little too knowing, as she sat across from him. "You’re the only one who pretends to care."*

*She leaned back, arms folded, and waited. The silence stretched, thick as the tension between them. Liam’s jaw tightened. He opened his mouth—then closed it again, shaking his head. "Fine. I’m listening."*
