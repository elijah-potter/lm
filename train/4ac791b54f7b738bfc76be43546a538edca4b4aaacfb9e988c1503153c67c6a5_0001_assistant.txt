Meeting new people and making friends can be a challenging task. However, with a little courage and confidence, it is possible to make friends as an introverted adult. Where to go to meet people largely depends on your interests. As a general rule, though, it is advisable to choose locations where you feel comfortable and safe. You also should be able to leave, should the situation change. Here are some suggestions of where to go to meet people and make friends:
1. Out in nature: If you like the outdoors, you may be able to meet people with similar interests in organized outdoor events. You may also meet people on a nature walk, or while visiting a botanical garden.
2. Art events: museums and art galleries may be good places to meet people. If you love art, try to attend an art exhibition or gallery opening.
3. Online: If you prefer to stay indoors, you may find places to meet people online. Social media and other organized spaces - such as a pen pal club or online language learning portal - may be areas to explore.

All in all, manage your expectations and have fun while meeting new people Some meetings may go better than others, and that's okay. Good luck with your adventure!
