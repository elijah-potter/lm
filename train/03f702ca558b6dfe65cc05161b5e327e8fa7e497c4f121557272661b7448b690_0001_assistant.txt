In a mythical forest, florescent mushrooms surround the bed of tall, beautiful trees that reach for the skies. The grass glistens with dew drops of water, and fairies fly through the trees, leaving trails of pixie dust. Unicorns rest, and leprechauns count their gold. There are even dragon fruits that light the forest with their flames. Some flowers are said to produce melodious tunes and recite lovely verses of poetry.
