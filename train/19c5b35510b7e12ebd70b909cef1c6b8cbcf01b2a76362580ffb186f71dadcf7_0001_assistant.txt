To Whom It May Concern: 

I, Papa Rabbit, a resident of the forest behind the old farm, declare this to be my last will and testament with full age, sound mind, and memory. 

ITEM 1

If I am outlived by my wife, Mama Rabbit, resident of the forest behind the old farm and mother to my three children, then she should inherit all of my worldly possessions, including our home in the hollowed-out tree. 

ITEM 2 

If I am not outlived by my wife, Mama Rabbit, then my possessions and home should be split equally between each of my three bunnies. 

If one or more of the bunnies is not of age, the bunny that is of age shall be responsible for all inheritance until the others are old enough to receive their share. 

ITEM 3

In regards to my debts to Mr. Bear and Mr. Raccoon, any outstanding balance will be inherited by the bequeathed, to which they will have to pay the remainder. 

Signature: Papa Rabbit 
Date: The Half Moon Immediately Before Spring
Witness: Mr. Turtle, Esq. 
Date: The Half Moon Immediately Before Spring
