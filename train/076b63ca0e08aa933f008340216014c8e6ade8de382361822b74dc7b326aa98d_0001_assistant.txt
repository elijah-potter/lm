The gluttonous king in his castle
He trembles and quakes in fear
And all of his self-righteous lords and vassals
Wail when we appear

We conquered the Mines of Dan'Aroth
We conquered the sea of Toole
Fueled by a righteous wroth
To stamp out their villainous rule

Our swords are sharp, our armor hard
Test the might of our forged cold steel
We'll turn them from life to lard 
And make them into a meal!
