Sure! Here are some castles in Ireland that are reportedly haunted:

1. Leap Castle - this castle is in County Offaly, and it is known to be the most haunted castle in Ireland. There is a story of The Red Lady who walks the castle at night, holding a knife hoping to avenge the child stolen from her.

2. Clifden Castle - in Galway, this castle is reported to be haunted by ghosts of people who died during the famine.

3. Malahide Castle - this castle is in Dublin and is said to be another of the most haunted castles in Ireland. You may have heard of the ghosts who are seen here: The Lady in White and the court jester, Puck.

4. Grannagh Castle - this castle in Kilkenny is said to be haunted by the Countess of Granny, who tortured her enemies there.

5. Tully Castle - there was a brutal massacre in this castle in Fermanagh in 1641, and today the atmosphere is reportedly rather spooky.
