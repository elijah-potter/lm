Skip Tooth Chain Vs Full Comp
When it comes to bicycle chains, the two main types are skip tooth and full comp. Skip tooth chains feature an alternating pattern between wide and narrow teeth, while full comp features a continuous pattern of uniform width teeth. Generally speaking, skip tooth chains tend to be more lightweight than their full comp counterparts, making them ideal for BMX bikes or those looking for a lighter chain option.
On the other hand, the uniformity of full comp makes it easier to maintain proper tension on the bike chain while riding – this is especially helpful when using derailleurs since they require precise adjustments in order to shift correctly. Ultimately which type you choose depends on your specific needs as either can offer reliable performance depending on how you plan to use your bike.
