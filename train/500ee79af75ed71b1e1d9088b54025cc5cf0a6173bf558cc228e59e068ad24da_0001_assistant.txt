Here are a few memes from the 2000s:

- Badger, Badger, Badger: This meme featured several identical illustrations of a badger dancing to an electronic beat.
- LOLCats: These are typically photos of real cats captioned with Impact typeface messages. In most cases, the captions were from the cat's perspective.
- Rickrolling: This describes the act of click-baiting someone into listening to the song "Never Gonna Give You Up" by Rick Astley.
- Nyan Cat: This is an animated cat whose body resembles a toaster pastry flying through space and leaving a rainbow trail.
