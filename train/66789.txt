PART-I: PRINCIPALS OF CRIMINOLOGY
Q1. What are the mental elements of a crime? Explain their role in criminal law.
Q2. What is the Deterrence Theory of Punishment? Whether the punishments prescribed under Islamic Law are deterrents in nature? Discuss in detail.
PART II: PAKISTAN PENAL CODE
Q3. Explain Qatl committed under Ikrah-i-Tam and lkrah-iNaqis.
Q4. Is it an offense to extend a threat to injure the reputation of a deceased person in which the person so threatened is interested? Substantiate your answer with relevant provisions of law.
Q5. What is the right of Qisas in Qatl-i-Amd? Also, discuss the provisions relating to the execution of Qisas.
Q6. Discuss when the right to private defense of the body does not extend to cause the death of the assailants.
Q7. Define criminal conspiracy. Elaborate on its difference with abetment.
Q8. What is hurt? Differentiate between “Itlaf-i-Udw” and “Itlaf-i-Salahiyyat-i-Udw.”
Q9. Define criminal trespass. Explain the difference between, house trespass and housebreaking.
Q10. Write a comprehensive note on wrongful restraint and wrongful confinement.
