* Groom regularly. Keeping your tail free of dust and debris will keep it looking healthy.
* Watch out for candles. Avoid waving your tail over a burning flame to avoid frizzled ends.
* Keep clear of doorways. Just because your body is through doesn't mean your tail can't be clipped by a closing door.
* Spot clean. In addition to your regular grooming routine, your tail may need extra care. Taking care of any spills or splashes as they happen will ensure your tail stays fluffy.
* Keep it up! Don't, by any means, allow your tail to drag. What self-respecting cat would allow that to happen? You'd be surprised!
* Steer clear of human children. Your glorious tail will sometimes attract the wrong kind of attention. Human children, for some reason, often like to pull our tails.
