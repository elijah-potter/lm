**The Loop**

She walked the same path every morning,
*Sunrise, sunset,*
the same café’s coffee tasted like regret.
*Morning, night,*
her fingers traced the same scars—
the same old wounds, the same hollow years.

The clock ticked in cycles:
*One, two,*
her breath in and out like a metronome’s beat.
*One, two,*
the river’s current ran the same,
carved the same sand into the same shapes.

*Repeat.*
She whispered it to the wind,
let it carry the echo like a lullaby.
*Repeat.*
The world spun in loops, and she was the wheel,
stuck in the same rhythm, the same cold truth:
*We are all just echoes of what we were.*
