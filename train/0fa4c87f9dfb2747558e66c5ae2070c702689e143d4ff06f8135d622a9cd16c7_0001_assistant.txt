1. 6-mile run - Participants will run a 6-mile-long course over different times of terrain, including uphill, rocky, and winding paths.
2. Crawl under barbed wire - Immediately after finishing the 6-mile run, participants will crawl over 100 yards of gravel under barbed wire that is only inches above their heads.
3. Rope swing - Right after popping up from the barbed wire crawl, participants will run up to a thick rope suspended above a large pond of water; they must jump to grab the rope and swing over a gap between two platforms.
4. Wall climb - Participants will then run up to a 30-foot wall and climb the wall using another thick rope draped on the face of the wall.
5. Jump across the gap - Upon reaching the top of the wall, participants will have to push through their fear of heights and jump 6 feet across two platforms; the gap contains a 30-foot fall.
6. Shoot the target - After jumping to the other platform, participants, armed with a rifle and scope, will attempt to score a hit on a target approximately 500 feet away. 
7. Pin the attacker - Once the participants hit the target with their rifle shots, they will have to grapple with an attacker who will give put up a fight; the participants must pin the attacker on the ground before advancing further.
8. Climb the ladder - participants will then climb a ladder that spans the height of an entire building (4 floors, or 56 feet).
9. Jump off the building - Participants will need to overcome their fears of height and falling by letting themselves fall into an inflated crash mat at the bottom of the building.
10. Cross the finish line - Once the participants make it to the crash mat, they will run 100 yards to the finish line.

Note: If participants fail one task, they will have to repeat the entire course from the beginning.
