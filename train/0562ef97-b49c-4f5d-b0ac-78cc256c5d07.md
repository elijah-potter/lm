The first draft ended in failure, but the second found the heart.
A woman’s laugh, sharp as a blade, cut through the silence of the attic.
She said she’d never write again, but here she was, fingers trembling over the keys.
The words spilled out like blood from a wound—unwanted, yet necessary.
The hero’s journey, she decided, wasn’t about glory, but the quiet moments when the world stopped spinning and only the breath remained.
The ending? A door left ajar, not closed forever, but never quite open.
The narrator’s voice, once commanding, became a whisper.
The story was still unfinished.
