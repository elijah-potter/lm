**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 14, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair, a beloved tradition celebrating agriculture, community, and entertainment, returns this August. This year’s fair promises a diverse array of attractions, from livestock exhibits and home and garden displays to thrilling carnival rides and live musical performances. Visitors of all ages will find something to enjoy, reinforcing the fair’s position as a cornerstone of summer in Sangamon County.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 22nd through August 26th, 2024, at the County Fairgrounds in Sherman. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM. The fairgrounds offer ample parking, and shuttle services will be available from designated off-site locations. Detailed schedules for all events, including specific times and locations, will be published on the fair’s official website, www.sangamoncountyfair.org, closer to the event date.

Admission to the Sangamon County Fair is $10 for adults, $5 for children aged 6-12, and free for children five and under. Carnival ride tickets and wristbands are available for purchase both online and at the fairgrounds. Advance ticket purchases are encouraged to minimize wait times.

**Headline Events:**

*   Demolition Derby – Friday, August 23rd, 7:00 PM
*   Livestock Auction – Saturday, August 24th, 1:00 PM
*   Grandstand Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters – Saturday, August 24th, 8:00 PM
*   4-H Showcase – Sunday, August 25th, 10:00 AM
*   Tractor Pull – Monday, August 26th, 6:00 PM
*   Fireworks Display – Monday, August 26th, 9:45 PM
