The auroras flicker in emerald and violet, painting the Arctic sky with shifting, liquid gold. Sunlight filters through ancient pine needles, casting prismatic shadows on moss-covered stones. Noon in the Sahara casts an opalescent glow over dunes, turning sand into molten silver under the heat. Moonlight drapes a cathedral’s stained-glass windows in slow, spectral threads of blue and amber. Urban lights—neon veins—pulse against concrete, reflecting fractured light into every crack of glass and pavement. Dawn’s first breath over the ocean turns the horizon into a gradient of soft pink and copper, dissolving into mist. The forest at twilight hums with bioluminescent fungi, each glow a whispered pulse through the dark.
