That is an interesting question.  Most Americans seem to prefer monochromatic colors when it comes to their vehicles.  The top five most common car colors are:

1: White! Over a quarter of the cars on the road (25.9% to be precise) are white.
2: Black is a close second at 22.3%.
3: Gray is third with 18.4%.
4: Silver is a low fourth place with only 12.1% of cars on the road being silver.
5: Blue, the only non-monochromatic color in the top five, is fifth at 9.5%.
