Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big red door, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white ears and a big, round nose that twinkled when he was happy.

Every morning, Benny would hop outside to play. The sun was warm, and the grass was green. Benny loved to chase his favorite friend, a little squirrel named Sammy. They would run and jump in the sunshine, giggling as they played.

One day, Benny found a shiny, golden acorn hidden in the tallest grass. He picked it up and rolled it gently in his tiny paws. "This must be special," he thought. Benny decided to take it home to show his mommy and daddy.

When Benny got back to his house, he ran into the kitchen where his mommy was making a big, scrumptious salad. "Mommy!" Benny cried happily. "I found something special!" He showed her the golden acorn. His mommy smiled and said, "That's wonderful, Benny! Maybe it will grow into a big, tasty tree!"

Benny's daddy was busy fixing the fence and heard Benny's excited voice. He ran outside and saw Benny holding the acorn. "What’s that?" Daddy asked. "It’s special, Daddy!" Benny said. Daddy looked at the acorn and grinned. "Let’s plant it in the garden!"

They dug a little hole in the backyard. Benny carefully placed the acorn inside, and Daddy patted the soil. "Now, just wait and see what happens," Daddy said. They all watched as Benny's garden grew more and more each day.

Soon, a tiny sprout peeked out from the earth. Benny and his family cheered. "Look, Daddy! It’s growing!" The sprout became a little bush, then a bushy tree. Benny's golden tree grew tall and strong, and its branches swayed in the wind. The leaves were soft and shiny, and the tree was home to many happy birds.

One sunny afternoon, Benny's mommy said, "Come on, Benny. Let’s make a snack." They picked fresh berries from the tree and made a big, sweet treat. Benny and his family sat together, enjoying their time and the delicious berries. Benny felt so happy that he knew his golden acorn had grown into something even more magical than he could have imagined.

Benny and his family continued to play and explore, but they always remembered the kindness and joy that came from sharing and growing together. And that’s how Benny’s golden tree brought lots of happiness to their home.
