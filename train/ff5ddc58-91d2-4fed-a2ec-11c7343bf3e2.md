A dimly lit hallway echoed with the distant hum of a refrigerator, its glass door slightly ajar. The air smelled faintly of stale coffee and the faint metallic tang of old air conditioning. The lone figure, hunched over a cluttered desk, tapped a final keystroke on a cracked laptop screen before closing it with a deliberate snap. Their fingers lingered on the keyboard, then slid into their pocket as they stood. The overhead fluorescent lights buzzed, casting a sickly yellow glow over the scene.

They didn’t turn around. Just like that, their shoulders dropped, their spine stiffened, and with a single, abrupt motion, they were gone—door clicking shut behind them without a glance. The hallway stretched endlessly into the darkness beyond.
