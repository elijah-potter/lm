Unspoken pact between the old and the new,
A contract carved in silence, not in words,
Eyes that meet but never say what’s said,
Promises held in hollowed-out hands instead.

The dawn does not whisper, yet the earth bows,
A silent tribute in the space between,
Where the weight of unseen truths is spun,
And the balance leans toward what was never known.

The clock does not strike, but time itself slows,
As the lost and the found find a rhythm without voice,
A dance of shadows, a pact so deep,
That even memory forgets how it was spoken.
