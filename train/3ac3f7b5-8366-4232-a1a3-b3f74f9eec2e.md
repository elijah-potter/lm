**Scene:**
The dim glow of a laptop screen casts long shadows across the cluttered desk. A half-empty coffee cup sits beside a crumpled draft of a novel, its pages marked with red ink and underlined passages. The writer, hunched over the keyboard, pauses mid-sentence—then clicks *Save* and sighs.

A pause. The story breathes. The first draft, sharp and raw, is gone. In its place: a second version, smoother, with the edges polished. Some passages rewritten. A scene reordered. A character’s arc refined. The original ending—now a placeholder—is being replaced with a twist that feels *right*.

Outside, rain taps against the window. The story, now more precise, hums like a well-tuned instrument. Another revision awaits.
