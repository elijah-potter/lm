**Sky:** Aurora borealis pulses in violet-blue streaks against midnight black, casting flickering auroras on glaciers below.
**Water:** Silver waves refract light into prismatic fractals, dissolving into emerald and sapphire shimmers near the shore.
**Forest:** Moss glows faintly with bioluminescent fungi, tree canopies refract golden dawn light into dappled, shifting fractals.
**Urban:** Neon reflections dance on wet pavement, streetlights cast elongated, warped shadows through glass facades.
**Desert:** Sand absorbs heat, leaving only ghostly light trails where camels pass at dawn—soft amber dusting on dunes.
**Ocean:** Sunrise turns waves into molten gold, while surface ripples refract sunlight into liquid mirrors beneath.
**Mountain:** Snow reflects a slow, eerie blue-green glow, catching sunlight like scattered diamonds in the wind.
**Cave:** Stalactites dripped light—amber veins of quartz—dissolving into misty, pulsing halos on cavern walls.
