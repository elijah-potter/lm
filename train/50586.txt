PGR Forum CWR Catalogue for Europe and the
The genera listed here are those that contain agricultural and horticultural crop species, forestry species, ornamental species or medicinal and aromatic plant species and/or their wild relatives, and which occur in the Euro-Mediterranean region.
Click on a genus name for a list of species.
The following sources were used to generate the crop genus list:
Agricultural and Horticultural crops: Mansfeld’s World Database of Agricultural and Horticultural Crops
Forestry crops: Schultze-Motel (1966) Enumeration of cultivated forest plant species.
Ornamental plants: the Community Plant Variety Office plant variety license list
Medicinal and aromatic plants: Medicinal and Aromatic Plant Resources of the World (MAPROW).
