The Kpop Group Stray Kids which debuted in 2017 under the label JYP is an eight-member group.  The members are:
     -Bang Chan: leader, producer, rapper, vocalist
     -Lee Know: dancer and vocalist
     -Changbin: producer and rapper
     -Hyunjin: dancer and rapper
     -Han: producer rapper, vocalist, dancer
     -Felix: dancer and rapper
     -Seungmin: vocalist
     -I.N.: vocalist

Stray Kids is known as a self-producing group since its members are involved the songwriting, producing and choreographing their own music.
