English Speaking Game
A fun and engaging classroom game, ideal for children learning English.
Target language: bed, wardrobe, bookcase, lamp, poster, clock, table, chair, prepositions
Instructions: cut the worksheet in half along the dotted line. Give one half to child A and the other half to child B. Encourage the children to describe their image to their partner in detail. Working together the children should find the differences between the two images.
You will need: one worksheet per pair.
