

1. A themed party - Usually, the theme is something the child likes or is based on their age (ie. PJ Masks or "One in a Melon").
2. A DIY craft or paint party - Set up a crafting area with supplies and let the children get creative. 
3. A Sleepover - Invite some friends over and order pizza. Make some popcorn and watch movies. 
4. A pool party - Invite some friends to come over and swim. If you don't have a pool, use sprinklers or a water slide. 
5. A movie party - Set up a candy and popcorn bar and turn on a movie or a series of movies.
