Here are some animal-themed pub names:

1. The Sleepy Lion - Lions are known to be very relaxed when not on the hunt. Lions also have a large sense of pride, a fitting name for a pub that has a welcoming presence.
2. The Iron Cheetah - A cheetah is known to be extremely fast and stalwart, especially when combined with the word "iron". 
3. The Bannered Ox - An ox is a cattle known to work hard, so visitors will get a sense of dedication when reading a pub name with the word Ox in it.
4. Blue Eagle - Eagles are seen as majestic and rare. Blue is often categorized as a safe, calming or royal colour.
5. The Hearty Bear - Bears are well-known in fairytales and storybooks. They are often characterized as being endearing and kind in fictional media.
6. Blue Herring - A blue herring is the antithesis of a red herring, the latter of which aims to distract or lure away, while the former would be to attract and lure in.
7. Silver Duck - A duck is an animal often adored by people, and the word "silver" often has connotations of value. Much cutlery is also the colour of silver.
8. Shining Otter - Otters are known for being smart, hardworking and cute. 
9. The Dancing Pigeon - Pigeons are a common species of bird present in the UK, which may help visitors imagine what the establishment is like.
10. The Royal Husky - Huskies are known for being intelligent and charming. A royal dog such as a husky would make visitors feel like the establishment is well-trusted.
