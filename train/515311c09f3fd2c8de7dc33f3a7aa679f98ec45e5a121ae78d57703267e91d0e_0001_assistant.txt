a springtime morning in these east-strewn mountains
trees wear flowers, yet bear no leaves just yet
crows and clouds fly above us, laughing
doves sing for no particular reason

the squirrels romp, sensing there's little danger
from this old man, out walking a tiny dog...

the wolflet, a mere five pounds of whim and wonder
he strolls the streets of our lazy neighborhood
follows his nose, and leads me where it takes him...
together we share those moments our own way

birds and chipmunks flitter and they scurry
tiny blossoms sprinkled on the breeze
man and dog head home - the better for it.
