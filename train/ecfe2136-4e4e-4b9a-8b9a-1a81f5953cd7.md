A cavernous, dimly lit chamber beneath an abandoned observatory, its walls lined with flickering holograms depicting celestial events. The air hums with static and the occasional whisper of voices echoing from speakers embedded in the ceiling. Seated in a half-circle, groups of individuals—some in futuristic gear, others in period costumes—stare at a shared projection of a swirling, abstract galaxy map. One central platform pulses with a red light, where a figure in a black suit stands, their face obscured by a visor. No agenda is posted, yet the atmosphere is charged with anticipation. The floor is scattered with discarded tech: broken drones, half-eaten cybernetic snacks, and a single, half-open book titled *"The Unseen Convergence."* The clock above reads 3:47 AM.
