A tense argument between coworkers over a minor office supply order escalated when one employee accused the other of "wasting company resources" by buying a 12-pack of pens instead of the standard 6-pack. The second employee countered that the 12-pack was more cost-effective per unit. When management intervened, the first employee claimed the decision was arbitrary and demanded a written policy for all purchases over $5. The second employee insisted on proof of "wasted spend" and requested a review of past budget reports. By the third meeting, internal emails had been misinterpreted as personal attacks, and the original issue had grown into a broader debate over company spending priorities, policy enforcement, and trust in leadership.
