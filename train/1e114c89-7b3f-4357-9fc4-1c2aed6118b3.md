Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny rabbit named Pip. Pip had soft brown fur and big, round ears that twitched when he was excited.

One sunny morning, Pip hopped outside to see what the garden had to offer. He sniffed the flowers and nibbled a tiny carrot. Then he spotted something shiny and round near the fence—it was a shiny apple!

"Wow!" Pip said. He picked it up and took a tiny bite. It was sweet and juicy, just like he imagined. But then—*whoosh!*—a gust of wind blew the apple right off his paws!

Pip’s tummy growled. "I’m hungry again!" he thought. He remembered his mom’s words: *"Hop to the garden, Pip!"* So he trotted to the vegetable patch and found a juicy apple slice, a soft carrot, and a bunch of crunchy lettuce.

Just then, a little squirrel named Squishy came running. "I found a whole acorn tree!" Squishy chattered. "Want to share?"

Pip grinned. "I’d love to!" He picked a big, juicy apple from the tree and passed it to Squishy. "Here, eat this!" Squishy giggled and took a bite.

After their snack, Pip and Squishy played hide-and-seek in the bushes. Pip hid behind a big, fluffy dandelion, and Squishy hid near a tiny birdhouse. They laughed so hard that the wind blew their snacks into the air—*whoosh!*—right back to Pip’s paws.

"Now we share *that* too!" Pip said, and they both ate happily.

As the sun started to set, Pip curled up in his burrow, dreaming of more adventures in the garden. And Squishy snuggled under his tree, dreaming of tomorrow’s fun.

**The End.**
