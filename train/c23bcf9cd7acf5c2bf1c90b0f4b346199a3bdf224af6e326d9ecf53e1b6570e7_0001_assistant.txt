Hello, members of the board.

I wanted to present to you a new game in hopes that you all help support us as we create this game. The game is called Kuma Fight or Bear Fight.

The player takes the role of an unnamed female swordsman exploring a vast open world. The world is filled with puzzles, traps, and numerous amounts of enemies as you, the player, will be able to explore all of it.
The protagonist can be customized to the player's choosing, but to start, she will be in a bear suit! She will have various colors and designs for the bear suit. As you continue through the game, you will be able to unlock more colors and patterns to customize this suit.
Bear Fight will have many enemies to fight in any way you choose. If you would like to stay at range, we have various bows and spears for you to use. You can also choose to get up close and personal if you like as well. With so many oddly shaped enemies, each with their unique way of fighting, players will have to find various ways to dispatch them. 
I also mentioned that this game is open-world. Besides fighting enemies, there will be a lot of side activities that you will be able to participate in. If you are a board game enjoyer, we have chess, checkers, go, and shogi boards laid out in various cities around the world. You can swim from point A to point B to try and get a faster score. Friends will be able to join you on this quest, and they can also play these side activities as well! We are also looking into adding a coliseum for players to test their skills against our hardest enemies. 

Bear Fight is about 50% done at the time of this presentation, but with your help, our team will be able to create the best game we've worked on so far. I do hope that you consider us, Thank you so much!
