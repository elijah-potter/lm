1. Get an 8.5 x 11 piece of paper, Scotch tape (optional), two hands, and a willingness to keep trying.
2. Fold the paper in half lengthwise and then open it back up.
3. Fold the top corners into the middle crease, creating a triangle at the top of the paper.
4. Fold the point of the triangle down to create another triangle shape.
5. Fold the top corners again, but this time only the corners should meet at the middle crease, forming a little triangle underneath.
6. Fold up the little triangle and crease it tightly to hold the corners down. You can use tape for extra support.
7. Turn the paper airplane over and fold it in half along the middle line.
8. Check your work to make sure the general shape is correct. Start over if needed.
9. Fold down one half of the paper to create the first wing. Adjust the size and crease it about 0.5-1 inch above the bottom of the plane.
10. Turn the airplane over and fold down the other wing to match the first one. Ensure both wings are as equal as possible.
11. Unfold the wings to make them perpendicular to the body of the plane. Check your work, and congratulations on completing a functional paper airplane.
12. For more stability, fold up the very tip of each wing by about a half-inch.
13. You have finished making the paper airplane!
