A decision feels like a leap when the path forward demands trust in uncertainty. The mind clings to what is familiar, yet the choice itself is a bet on the unknown—an act of surrendering control in favor of an uncharted future. The leap is measured not in the weight of the decision but in the quiet tension between doubt and courage, where hesitation fades into the space between what is known and what is yet to unfold. It is not just about taking action but about believing that the ground beneath your feet will hold, even when it doesn’t look like it. The leap is the moment where fear and faith become one, and the next step is not so much a choice as an invitation to trust the process.
