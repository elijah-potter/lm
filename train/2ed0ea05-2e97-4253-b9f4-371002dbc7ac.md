The silence lingers like a ghost in the room,
a weight no clock can measure—
each hour unmarked by motion,
each breath a hollow echo of what was.

The walls hold the weight of unspoken words,
their cracks whispering the same refrain:
*"I’m not here."*

The light bends around the edges of absence,
softening into a ghostly glow,
fading like the memory of a name
you tried to say but never quite did.

Time is a river that flows backward—
you watch the past unravel,
each second a thread undone,
each moment a ghost of what could have been.

The absence isn’t gone.
It’s just there—
a presence you can’t reach,
a silence that hums with the weight of what’s left unsaid.
