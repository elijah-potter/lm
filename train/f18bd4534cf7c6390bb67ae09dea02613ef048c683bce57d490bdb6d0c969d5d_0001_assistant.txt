1. !!! (pronounced "chk chk chk") - This American dance-punk band has a similar danceable energy to LCD Soundsystem, with funk and disco influences. They are a bit raw, and a little less electronic overall.

2. Hot Chip - This British band combines indie rock and electronic music in a way that's similar to LCD Soundsystem. Their lead singer has more of a tenor croon though, resulting in more of a dreamy vibe than LCD’s punk ethos.

3. The Rapture - Another dance-punk band, The Rapture shares a similar blend of punk rock and dance music with LCD Soundsystem. They were even the first 12” release on James Murphy’s DFA Records.  Expect less electronic influence here.

4. Cut Copy - An Australian band combining indie rock with electronic dance music to create a sound that's perfect for the dancefloor. Expect some crooning and disco vibes. 

5. Franz Ferdinand - This Scottish band's music is infused with danceable energy and post-punk influences. Their guitar riffs and upbeat rhythms are rockier than LCD Soundsystem but will make you move.
