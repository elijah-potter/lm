Pinanga punicea var. papuana
A large, solitary Pinanga, native to Papua New Guinea, where it grows in lowland rainforests. It produces a smooth trunk to about 10 m (33 ft.) tall, a large, purple crownshaft and spreading, pinnate leaves. Young plants retain their bifid leaves until about 1.5 m (5 ft.) tall. It is a fast growing plant that will do best in a tropical climate.
