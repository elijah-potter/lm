**Decision Context:**
- **Location:** Corporate boardroom, late-night strategy meeting.
- **Decision:** Abandoned a high-risk, high-reward AI project due to escalating development costs and uncertain market demand.
- **Rationale:** Focus shifted toward stable, low-cost digital transformation initiatives to stabilize revenue streams amid economic uncertainty.

**Key Outcomes:**
- Immediate cost savings: ~$4.2M in R&D allocations redirected to core operations.
- Long-term: Retained 12% of original team for legacy system maintenance; outsourced core AI development to a third-party vendor.
- Leadership justification: "Prudent risk management; better to pivot than burn cash on speculative tech."
