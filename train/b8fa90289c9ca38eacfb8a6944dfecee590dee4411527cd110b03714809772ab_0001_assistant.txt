Together We Can is a dramatic enemies-to-lovers romance novel about two surgeons working together in a hospital. Our male protagonist, Dr. Matteo Rossi, is used to being the best. He graduated at the top of his class from a prestigious Italian medical school and quickly became one of the most sought-after surgeons in the country. When he gets offered a position at a groundbreaking research hospital in the United States, he knows he can't turn it down. He packs his bags and leaves his home behind, ready to start a new chapter of his life. 

Little did he know that this new chapter would include our leading lady Dr. Sophia Taylor and a complex medical case that would test their skills and their patience with one another. Their relationship starts rocky, with each having differing opinions on how to treat their patient, but as they get to know each other better, they find that they have more in common than they realized and develop a romantic connection. However, when the professionalism of their workplace relationship gets called into question, they must decide whether or not they want to risk everything to be together.
