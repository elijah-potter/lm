As you enter through the door, you see a small room with walls lined to the ceiling with various paintings. Swinging slowly above is a chandelier, and set in the wall opposite is a window overlooking an empty courtyard. A couple of old overstuffed chairs face each other over a small table with teacups. Three pieces of art, in particular, draw your attention: a tall painting depicting a man in formal military uniform with black eyes and a fanged smile, a wide landscape of an ordinary field being overshadowed by white clouds vaguely shaped like betentacled creatures, and a small portrait of a little girl playing with a doll while a corpse lies in the background. The last bears a label that reads "Playtime for Molly."
