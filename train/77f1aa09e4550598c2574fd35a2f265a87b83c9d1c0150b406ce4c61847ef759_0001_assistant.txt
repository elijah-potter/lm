Nate walked down the bus, past the rows and rows of his peers to the curly hair in the back left corner of the bus, Seth Richardson, his best friend since kindergarten. Seth’s face was illuminated by the glow of his phone screen and his fingers tapped away until he looked up to see Nate. His expression went from neutral to excited when he saw his friend and he quickly put his phone in his pocket. 

“Hey buddy, how was your Sunday?” Seth asked. 

“It was fine,” Nate started, “Just worked on Algebra. I actually got super into some of the problems and got a good understanding of the quadratic formula and what I could use it for,” he said.

Seth laughed, “Well that sounds like a great weekend for you!”

Seth’s phone was buzzing in his pocket. Nate looked down and sighed, “Yeah. Just gets boring sometimes.”

"Well, why don't you come to a party with me sometime?" Seth asked. 

Nate's eyes widened. "Oh, I don't know. Do you think those people will like me?" 

"Nate, I've never met anyone who disliked you."

"But I don't meet a lot of people that like me either."

"You'd be surprised," Seth said 

As Nate began to say more the bus screeched to a stop outside of Jackson Regional High School.

"We'll talk more about this later," Seth said. The two stood up and walked off the bus and into the social maze that was High School.
