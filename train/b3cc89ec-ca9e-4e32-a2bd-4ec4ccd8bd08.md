## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park – A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary:**

The City of Oakhaven faces increasing challenges from extreme weather events, including prolonged heat waves and intensified stormwater runoff. The Willow Creek area, particularly the currently derelict lot at the corner of Elm and Maple Streets, exemplifies these vulnerabilities. This proposal outlines a transformative project to convert this neglected space into the Willow Creek Pocket Park, a climate-resilient community asset. The park will incorporate native plantings, a rainwater harvesting system for irrigation, shaded gathering areas, and permeable paving to mitigate urban heat island effect and reduce stormwater burden. This project directly aligns with the City’s sustainability goals, enhances neighborhood quality of life, and fosters a stronger sense of community. We request $75,000 in municipal funding, which will be matched by $25,000 in volunteer labor, in-kind donations, and fundraising efforts, totaling a $100,000 investment in a demonstrably valuable public resource.



**Project Plan & Timeline:**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park project will unfold across four distinct phases, each with defined responsibilities and deliverables.

**Phase 1: Planning & Design (Months 1-2) – Lead: Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department, Design Partner: Greenleaf Landscape Architects**

*   **Deliverables:** Site assessment report, community design workshop summary, finalized park design plans incorporating climate resilience features, permitting applications submitted to the City Planning Department.
*   **Responsible Partners:** Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department (project oversight, permitting), Greenleaf Landscape Architects (design and technical expertise), Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (community feedback and outreach).

**Phase 2: Site Preparation & Infrastructure (Months 3-5) – Lead: Oakhaven Public Works Department, Contractor: Miller Construction**

*   **Deliverables:** Site clearing and grading completed, permeable paving installed, rainwater harvesting system components delivered and staged, underground utilities inspected and approved.
*   **Responsible Partners:** Oakhaven Public Works Department (oversight, utility coordination), Miller Construction (site preparation and infrastructure installation), City Arborist (tree removal and protection).

**Phase 3: Planting & Construction of Amenities (Months 6-8) – Lead: Willow Creek Neighborhood Association, Volunteer Coordinator: Sarah Jenkins**

*   **Deliverables:** Native plant installation completed, shaded gathering area constructed (using reclaimed lumber), benches and picnic tables installed, interpretive signage erected.
*   **Responsible Partners:** Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (volunteer coordination and community engagement), Sarah Jenkins (volunteer management), Local Nursery (plant sourcing and installation guidance).

**Phase 4: Grand Opening & Ongoing Maintenance (Month 9 onwards) – Lead: Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department, Adopt-a-Park Program**

*   **Deliverables:** Park officially opened to the public, Adopt-a-Park program established for ongoing maintenance, community workshops on climate-resilient gardening practices.
*   **Responsible Partners:** Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department (long-term maintenance and programming), Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (community stewardship), Adopt-a-Park volunteers.



**Budget & Matching Funds:**

The total project budget is $100,000. We respectfully request $75,000 in municipal funding, complemented by $25,000 in matching contributions.

**Municipal Funding Request: $75,000**

*   Permeable Paving Materials & Installation: $25,000
*   Rainwater Harvesting System (Tank, Filtration, Plumbing): $18,000
*   Native Plant Materials & Soil Amendments: $12,000
*   Construction Materials (Lumber, Hardware): $10,000
*   Interpretive Signage: $5,000
*   Professional Design Fees (Greenleaf Landscape Architects): $5,000

**Matching Funds: $25,000**

*   Volunteer Labor (estimated value): $15,000 (based on 200 volunteer hours at $75/hour)
*   In-Kind Donations (lumber, tools, mulch): $5,000 (documented donations)
*   Community Fundraising (bake sales, raffles, local business sponsorships): $5,000

A contingency fund of 5% ($5,000) is incorporated into the municipal funding request to address unforeseen expenses related to material price fluctuations or unexpected site conditions. This fund will be managed by the Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department.



**Community Engagement, Equity Impacts, & Risk Mitigation:**

Throughout the project lifecycle, robust community engagement is paramount.  The initial design phase incorporated a well-attended community workshop to gather input on desired park features and aesthetics.  Subsequent updates will be communicated through the Willow Creek Neighborhood Association newsletter and posted on the City’s website.

The Willow Creek Pocket Park directly addresses equity concerns by providing a much-needed green space in a neighborhood experiencing limited access to recreational amenities and disproportionately affected by urban heat island effects.  The park’s design prioritizes accessibility for individuals with disabilities, ensuring equitable access for all community members.

Potential risks have been identified and mitigation strategies developed.  Delays in permitting approval will be addressed through proactive communication with the City Planning Department.  Inclement weather during construction will be factored into the project schedule.  Liability insurance will be secured for all volunteer activities.  A detailed safety plan, overseen by Miller Construction, will be implemented to protect workers and volunteers.



**Post-Grant Sustainability:**

The long-term sustainability of the Willow Creek Pocket Park is ensured through a multi-faceted approach.  The Adopt-a-Park program will engage community volunteers in ongoing maintenance tasks, including weeding, watering, and litter removal.  The Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department will provide technical support and guidance to Adopt-a-Park volunteers.  Educational workshops on climate-resilient gardening practices will empower community members to maintain the park’s ecological health.  The park’s design incorporates drought-tolerant native plants, minimizing water consumption and reducing maintenance requirements.



**Call to Action:**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park represents a transformative opportunity to enhance the quality of life for Oakhaven residents, bolster the City’s sustainability goals, and foster a stronger sense of community.  We urge the municipal funders to approve this proposal and invest in a vital public asset that will provide lasting benefits for generations to come.  This is an investment in the health, resilience, and vibrancy of our city.
