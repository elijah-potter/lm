The room settled into a hush—soft hums of distant traffic muffled by closed windows, the distant murmur of a city waking. A single candle flickered beside the half-empty mug of tea, its wax dappled in the morning light filtering through lace curtains. A book lay open on the coffee table, its pages blank except for the faint scent of old parchment. The weight of silence pressed like a quiet embrace, heavy with the absence of voices, the pause between breaths. The world, for a moment, felt both smaller and more expansive, held together by the fragile equilibrium of stillness.
