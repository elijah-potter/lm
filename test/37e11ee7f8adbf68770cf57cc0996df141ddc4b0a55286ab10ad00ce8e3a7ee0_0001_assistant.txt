Nicholas Sparks, a well-known American author, has written many books over the years that capture people's hearts and attention. In fact, many of his novels have become so popular that they were used as the baseline for a major motion picture. Here are three of his most notable books.

1. The Notebook - 1996
Having just returned from serving in World War II, Noah begins the massive task of refurbishing a large plantation on the coast of North Carolina. As he works, he recalls a summer romance that he had over a decade before with a girl named Allie. Determined to see if a summer romance could be true love, Allie makes a trip back to North Carolina where she must make some hard decisions about love and life.

2. Message in a Bottle - 1998
Theresa, a divorced woman who is on vacation finds a mysterious bottle by the sea with a love note inside. The message is written by an unknown man to his lost lover. Despite her apprehensions about love since she was betrayed by her ex-husband, the journalist within Theresa is intrigued and the bottled message leads her on a hunt to locate the owner.

3. A Walk to Remember - 1999
Set in 1958, the story follows a boy named Landon and a girl named Jamie as they navigate high school, life, and love. Jamie is devout to her religion, reserved in her dress and personality, and spends most of her time caring for her father or volunteering her time to help local orphans or animals. Landon, on the other hand, is your typical teenage boy, who ends up having to take Jamie to the homecoming dance.
