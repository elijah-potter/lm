The quiet shift of a single leaf on a windless morning—once part of a dense canopy—now drifts toward the earth, marking the first whisper of autumn’s descent. That action, though small, is the catalyst for cascading changes: the ground beneath it becomes wet with rain that was once held by the canopy, the soil warms the earth’s memory of the past season, and a seed, buried beneath, sprouts in response to the new conditions. Every small action, like this leaf’s fall, is a spark in a chain reaction of evolution. It’s not the magnitude but the ripple—the unseen thread that connects the deliberate act to the inevitable shift. The universe, in its quiet rhythm, measures time not in grand gestures, but in the persistence of what follows.
