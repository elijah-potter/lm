The Garden of Earthly Delights' was painted by the Dutch/Netherlandish artist Hieronymus Bosch (c. 1450 – 1516).  It takes the form of a triptych oil painting on oak panels, painted between 1490 and 1510.  The three panels that make up this triptych are: 'The Garden of Eden', 'The Garden of Earthly Delights', and 'Hell'. Together they represent one of the most famous examples of Early Netherlandish art, of which Hieronymus Bosch is one of the most notable exponents.
