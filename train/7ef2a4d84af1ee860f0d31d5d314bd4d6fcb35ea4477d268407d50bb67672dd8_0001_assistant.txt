After opening the trunk, she gasped at the sight of the handmade quilt resting within. She could recognize this quilt anywhere, as it was the very one she loved to sleep with as a child when she would visit her late grandmother. As she held the quilt, made from different shapes and sizes of various beautiful fabrics, the faint scent of her grandmother's home filled her nose. The scent carried with it memories of laughter, love, and many warm embraces. With each inhale, she felt her grandmother's presence as if she was being wrapped in a loving hug from a time long past.
