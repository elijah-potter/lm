Why is Mothers Out Front San Diego launching an Electric School Bus Campaign?
Transportation makes up 41% of climate emissions in California. Addressing air pollution and climate change and its particularly adverse effects on environmental justice communities, vulnerable populations and people of color in San Diego County will require action and leadership from every public entity in the region.
School districts in San Diego County can do its part to address these harmful impacts by transitioning to zero-emission school buses.
