These are the most dangerous predators to have ever walked the Earth:

* Smilodon, more commonly known as the sabre-toothed tiger, was a prehistoric cat, with 8.5-inch sabres and was the largest mammal carnivore to ever prowl the Earth. It was a deadly predator and skilled pack hunter.

* Anomalocaris was a strange invertebrate that lived in the oceans 500 million years ago when the rest of the land was just rock. It was the largest animal on earth at this time and preyed on the other simple, soft-bodied creatures in the oceans.

* Tyrannosaurus Rex lived during the Cretaceous period and preyed on other large dinosaurs. Tyrannosaurus devoured its kill by ripping the flesh off in strips. It had to swallow its food whole because it was unable to chew so it risked choking.

* Liopleurodon was a huge marine reptile, measuring up to 82 feet in body length. It lived during the Late Jurassic period, gliding silently through the warm, shallow seas, propelling itself by flapping its gigantic flippers.

* Homo Sapiens are humans. The word means 'wise and knowing man'. Homo Sapiens evolved in Eastern Africa around 190,000 years ago. They possessed brain power and could solve complex problems. For example, they were able to adapt to the Ice Age environment.
