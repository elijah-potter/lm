Great idea. Would these options work as a Hustle Award?

• Small trophy - Purchase a small trophy that says "Hustle Award" for each game and if possible, have the player's name engraved or stamped on it.
• Softball-themed gift bag - Buy a small bag and include softball-themed trinkets, such as squishy balls, hair accessories, or bracelets to present after the game.
• Certificate - Print an official Hustle Award certificate with the player's name on it.
• Candy Bag - Fill a clear cellophane bag with bubble gum or candy to give the winner.
• A Softball - Write Hustle Award on a softball and gift it to the winner after each game.
