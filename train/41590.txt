|This handy, pocket-sized booklet includes a diagram of the parts of the saxophone, a fingering chart, how to assemble and care for, and hold the saxophone, and music reading fundamentals. The studies provide practice for tone, slurs, embouchure, fingering, tonguing, arpeggios, and dotted rhythms. Additional songs and studies introduce and practice more concepts. Major, minor, and chromatic scales are also included|
