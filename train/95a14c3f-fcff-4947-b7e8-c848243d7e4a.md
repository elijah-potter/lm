AITA for refusing to let my sister use my inherited vacation home?

Okay, this is a bit complicated, so bear with me. It involves my sister, Eleanor, my late grandmother, and a small cottage in Maine.

My grandmother, Beatrice, passed away last October (2023) after a long illness. She lived in a charming, but modest, cottage in Ogunquit, Maine, which she’s owned since 1988. It’s not a McMansion, mind you – more of a cozy, two-bedroom place with a lovely garden overlooking the ocean. She explicitly left the cottage to me in her will, which was finalized in November. I was genuinely surprised, as Eleanor always assumed it would be hers. They were very close.

Eleanor, my younger sister by five years, has been a bit…difficult since. She's been repeatedly asking if she can use the cottage, starting almost immediately after the funeral. Initially, I was sympathetic. She seemed genuinely grieving and wanting a connection to our grandmother. I agreed she could visit for a week in February (2024), figuring a short stay wouldn't be a big deal. She brought her boyfriend, Liam, who seemed perfectly pleasant. The week passed without incident.

Then, the requests escalated. She started suggesting longer stays – a month in June, two weeks in August. She framed it as needing a “creative retreat” to work on her pottery, arguing that the Maine coast would be incredibly inspiring. I explained that I wanted to use the cottage myself, that I’m planning a trip with my husband in September, and that I needed to sort out some necessary repairs before renting it out for a portion of the year to generate income. I felt it was reasonable to balance personal use with responsible upkeep.

Eleanor’s response was… dramatic. She accused me of being selfish, of deliberately trying to deny her a connection to our grandmother, and of “hoarding” the cottage. She started subtly bringing up how much time she spent with Beatrice, implying I didn’t care as much. Liam joined in, which I found incredibly rude. He actually said, "It's pretty heartless to deny your own sister a place that meant so much to her." Seriously?

My husband, Mark, thinks I’m being too rigid. He says I should just let her use it, that it's "just a house." My parents have taken Eleanor's side, saying I need to be more generous and understanding. They're subtly implying I’m being unreasonable and causing family strife. I’m feeling incredibly pressured. 

I’m not trying to be mean. I just want to manage the cottage responsibly and enjoy it myself. I’m also feeling a lot of guilt, because I *do* feel bad that Eleanor is hurting. However, I also feel like she’s manipulating the situation to get her way, and that my parents are enabling her. I’ve offered her alternative options – suggesting she rent a similar cottage herself, or even offering to cover part of the cost – but she always deflects, insisting she deserves to use *this* cottage.

I’m feeling incredibly isolated and stressed. I’ve started avoiding family gatherings because I dread the inevitable conversation about the cottage. I feel like I’m being punished for something I didn’t do. I'm starting to think that maybe I'm the bad guy here, but I'm also feeling incredibly defensive of my grandmother’s legacy and my right to manage it as I see fit. ESH, perhaps, for everyone involved.

AITA for firmly telling Eleanor, and subsequently my parents, that I will not be allowing her to use the cottage until I’m ready, and that her continued pressure is unacceptable?
