A vast, endless ocean of darkness with stars like scattered fireflies in a dead sky. Its depths are colder than the void of space itself, yet its surface shimmers with secrets like liquid mercury reflecting forgotten constellations.

The cliffs are jagged like shattered teeth of an ancient beast, carved by winds that whisper secrets older than time. Below, rivers of molten gold flow through tunnels where shadows dance like trapped spirits.

The air tastes of salt and forgotten storms, thick enough to drown a thousand voices. Mountains rise like the ribs of a sleeping giant, their peaks kissed by mist that lingers forever. Every step feels like walking on the bones of giants who once roamed here.

At its heart, a black heart pulses with whispers from a forgotten world—some say it’s alive, others claim it’s just a place where the rules of time dissolve into something stranger.
