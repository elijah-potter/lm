Every war includes winners and losers - and the losers are often dead. Dead people can never tell us about the horrors of war, but we can often infer its horror through written accounts, word-of-mouth, and sometimes even journalism.

Journalism is an interesting field that can also be very dangerous depending on the assignment. Sometimes, journalists need to document reality in areas such as natural disaster zones and even war zones.

War zones are scary places to find yourself in, with all the bombings and shooting going on around you. If you ever find yourself in a war zone, find shelter and barricade yourself to protect yourself from getting hurt.

Getting hurt can be terrible, and it can also happen when you least expect it. For instance, one time I got hurt by simply getting out of bed one morning without stretching beforehand. I ended up pulling my hamstring and having to stay in bed for a couple of days after it occurred.

This reminds me, stretching is very important for your body. If you don't stretch before doing exercise or just move your body, you can hurt yourself pretty badly. That's why I always make sure to do a full-body stretch first thing in the morning. I also stretch frequently throughout the day so I can stay limber.

Limber...limber...lumber...lumber is made from trees. Usually, trees that are fully grown and straight will be chosen from a copse of trees and cut down to make lumber for houses and other projects requiring wood planks.

Planks are a great exercise for your core muscles. To do planks, you get into a push-up position. However, instead of doing a pushup, you remain at the full lean-and-rest position with your body straight and resisting the urge to sag at the waist. This will ensure you are engaging the core muscles and working them sufficiently. I like to do about a minute per plank.
