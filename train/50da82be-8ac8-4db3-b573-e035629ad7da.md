**Clang of a ship’s bell signaling distress from the horizon**—a single, resonant strike followed by a pause before the second, longer chime. The rhythm echoes through the storm-wracked sea, its repetition not just a warning but a plea, a signal carved into the wind. The sound carries the weight of a lost fleet, a call to the unseen, and in the hush between strikes, the answer is whispered—*somewhere, someone is listening*.
