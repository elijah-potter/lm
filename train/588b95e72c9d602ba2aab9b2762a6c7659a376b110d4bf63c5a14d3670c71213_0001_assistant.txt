There are many great cities in Texas that offer a variety of activities for children and adults alike, as well as options for shopping. Here are a few options to consider:

- San Antonio: This city is home to popular attractions such as the San Antonio River Walk. The river walk has plenty of things to do, such as Legoland, boat cruises, and the Rainforest Cafe. 

- Houston: This city has many attractions for families, including the Johnson Space Center, where kids can see over 400 space-related exhibits and artifacts. 

- Dallas: Dallas has many family-friendly attractions, such as the Dallas World Aquarium, where kids can view more than 500 animals and interact with stingrays.
