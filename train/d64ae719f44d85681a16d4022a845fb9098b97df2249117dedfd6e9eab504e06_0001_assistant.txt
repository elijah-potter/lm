You got it! Here are some activities that your son might be interested in: 

1. Carpentry: Boys love to build things. Provide him with some basic carpentry tools and materials to make boxes, birdhouses, simple furniture, and more. 
2. Painting: Is your child artistic? Hone his talent with a quality paint set including paints, paint brushes, and canvases or watercolor paper, depending on the type of paint used.
3. Music: 10 is a good age to learn how to play a musical instrument. Enroll your child in lessons, or ask him if he would like to join his school band.
4. Sports:  Physical activity is good for children. There are all kinds of sports teams that kids can join. See what is available in your area, and ask your son if he is interested in joining. 
5. Cooking: Some kids like to learn basic cooking skills as they get older. He might want to bake a cake and ice it, or prepare simple meals for the family. Just make sure to supervise to keep him safe as he learns.
