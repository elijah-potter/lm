Marineland is an amusement park located in Niagara Falls, Ontario, Canada. Considered an amusement park with an animal focus, Marineland has rides and a splash park as well as animals and fish to see and learn about. Their animal collection focuses on north american mammals as well as geese, ducks and various kinds of aquatic wildlife like Orcas, Beluga whales and dolphins.

The rides are appropriate for the entire family, with a variety of intensities from roller coasters down to merry-go-rounds. You can also bring food to picnic or you can purchase food there from the on-site restaurant.
