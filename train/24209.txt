High School Science
Discover the world around you! Join Mr. Arias and Ms. Ramos as they takes you behind the scenes of the often overlooked and undiscovered world of Science. In their classes you will learn about the Scientific Process, know how to conduct Scientific Research and use various Scientific Technologies to reach deductive conclusions. By understanding Data Analysis and the use of SI Units, Scientific Notation Graphing and Degrees of Certainty, you will be able to evaluate the environments around you; while participating in a high energy, arts-infused, and in-depth study of Chemistry, Biology, and Commercial Lab practices.
