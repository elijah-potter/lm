The neon hum of the city pulsed like a heartbeat as Elias stepped off the subway platform, boots crunching on wet pavement. A single drop of rain splattered his visor. His watch—*broken*, but still ticking—showed 5:32. The second hand crept toward the 5:33 mark.

Across the alley, a woman in a black trench coat crouched over a discarded cigarette butt, her fingers brushing the ash. She didn’t look up. The rain blurred her features, but her smile was sharp, knowing.

*"Five minutes left,"* she whispered, though no one heard. She stood, adjusted her gloves, and vanished into the crowd like smoke. The last thing Elias saw was her pocket watch—identical to his—still ticking.

Outside, the city exhaled. Then, the sirens wailed.
