The Admiral: so grand she looms,
'Tis blessed to have a female admiral,
Her grace is astounding to behold in bloom,
Her will is great though her stature be small.

A modern marvel, she stands apart,
Her command was respected in her reign,
Her path was mapped out from the start,
A respect and glory that she'll attain.

Her heart of steel kind and true,
Her leadership none shall mar,
Her crew stronger than glue,
She fought for the country beneath the stars.

At long last a woman at the helm, 
A testament of excellence and grace,
The Admiralty to our hearts overwhelm,
To have such a leader to bless this place.
