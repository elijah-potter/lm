Tzatziki has a tangy, yet refreshing taste that’s as versatile as it is delicious, but homemade will always beat storebought. To make it even better, go the extra mile and follow this special tzatziki recipe. The ingredients you’ll need are:

-500g full-fat Greek yogurt 
-50g extra virgin olive oil 
-Three cloves of garlic
-One cucumber
-One lemon
-Salt
-Black pepper
-Dill 
-Thyme 

Full-fat Greek yogurt blows away regular yogurt or fat-free, and there’s no replacement for it if you want the best tzatziki. From there, take a broad knife that you’ll use to crush and mince the garlic. Cover it in salt, and then continue crushing and mincing it until it’s a paste. Mix this paste into the olive oil, and set this aside for the flavors to combine. 

In the meantime, chop the cucumber into extremely thin, small pieces and squeeze the juice from the lemon. Now, mix all of the ingredients together and leave the tzatziki to set in the fridge for a few hours before enjoying it.
