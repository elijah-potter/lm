An unwritten pact between two factions: *"No words spoken where they can be overheard."* The contract is carved into the air—not in ink, but in the unspoken rhythm of shared glances, the way doors creak just so, the coded glances at the kitchen table.

**Terms:**
1. **The Unspoken Rule:** "What is said behind closed doors stays in the dark." Silence is currency; words are currency. The louder the volume, the more risk.
2. **The Shared Silence:** Every pause, every breath held in time, is a promise. A nod at the moonlit window signals "I hear you."
3. **The Unwritten Code:** "The third time you look at the other’s watch, they owe you a story." Or a debt.
4. **The Unseen Witness:** Shadows move. The empty chair at midnight is a confession booth.
5. **The Unbroken Chain:** "If you forget, the other will remind you—through the cracks in the mirror, in the way the salt shaker rattles too hard."

**The Cost:** Trust isn’t given; it’s taken in pieces—like tea leaves, or the first sip of a drink left unguarded.

**The End.**
