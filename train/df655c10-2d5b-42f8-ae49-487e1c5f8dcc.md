A hush descends like a folded blanket over the world.
Silence hums with the weight of unspoken words,
each breath a measured pause in a rhythm once chaotic.
The city’s chatter fades—traffic dies,
clocks tick with a new, slower cadence.
Even the wind, once a cacophony of voices,
now carries only the soft whisper of leaves against branches.
The air smells faintly of rain waiting to fall,
or the earth’s quiet pulse after a storm.
A single bird, unhurried, alights on a branch,
as if time itself has stopped to watch the sky turn gold.
