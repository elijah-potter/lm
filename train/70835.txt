Adam and Eve
Adam and Eve were the first people on earth. They were created by God. Adam was the first man and Eve was the first woman. They were created perfect and without sin.
The first humans
The first humans, Adam and Eve, were created by God on the sixth day of Creation. Genesis 1:26-27 says, “Then God said, ‘Let us make man in our image, after our likeness. So God created man in his own image; in the image of God he created him; male and female he created them.”
Adam and Eve were the first people on earth, and they had a special relationship with God. They were perfect and without sin. But they disobeyed God by eating from the tree of knowledge of good and evil. As a result, they became sinners ( Genesis 3:6-7 ).
Since Adam and Eve were the first humans, all other humans have inherited their sin nature. We are all born with a desire to do things that are wrong in God’s eyes ( Romans 3:23 ). We cannot please God on our own. We need His help ( John 15:5 ).

The third son of Adam and Eve
Seth was the third son of Adam and Eve, making him the first child born after Abel’s death. He was born when Adam was 130 years old, “a son in [his] own likeness and image” (Genesis 5:3).
The name “Seth” means “appointed” or “compensated.” It could be that he was given this name because he replaced Abel in the family line. It could also be that his birth brought some measure of compensation to Adam and Eve for their loss.
