Botulinum toxin, better known as botox (the brand name of the first drug of this type that was marketed), is actually the toxin that botulism produces. However, its ability to produce muscular paralysis is used to use it for medical purposes in the treatment of certain neurological diseases and in aesthetic medicine for expression wrinkles, which is why it is best known. Among its numerous medical applications include: botox
- The strabismus
- The blepharospasm.
- The vertebral bones
- Urinary incontinence in paraplegics.
When it comes to the elimination of facial wrinkles, botox offers clear advantages:
- Avoid surgery.
- It can be applied at any time of the year.
- It is a practically painless technique.
- Its effects are immediate and last between five and six months.
- It hardly has contraindications.
Rarely produces adverse effects, such as allergic reactions, facial stiffness or nausea.
The technique consists in the infiltration by an extra-fine needle of botulinum toxin into the muscle of the area to be treated, producing its relaxation and inhibiting its movement. Hence, its fundamental application in aesthetics is the treatment of wrinkles of expression, that is to say those that appear in the eyebrows, the forehead, the corners of the lips or the contour of the eyes.
It is very important that this treatment is applied by a specialist who knows the facial anatomy, since a puncture with botox in the wrong place can generate an unwanted expression.
Botulinum toxin or botox has, however, other applications in medicine. Currently, for example, it is being used to:
The treatment of hyperhidrosis, that is, in people with excessive sweating in the armpits, hands and feet. With it is achieved to reduce the activity of the sweat glands.
Control of blepharospasm, that is, the intermittent and involuntary contraction of the muscles of the eye.
Other dystonia and spasms.
Finally, it is offering good results in paraplegic people with urinary incontinence problems.

Aesthetic applications of Botox
When the defenses of the anti-wrinkle creams can no longer do more to contain the biological sequels of facial aging, the Botox is in combat . The dermatological application of botulinum toxin offers clear advantages over other aesthetic practices that have the same objective: to disguise the folds of the skin, mainly on the forehead, around the eyes, between the eyebrows and at the corners of the lips.
To begin, avoid going through the operating room . The Botox is applied with a very fine needle through infiltrations in the muscle of the area to be treated. With a small prick the toxin relaxes and inhibits muscle movement . The process is painless for the patient, the only thing that generates are the inconveniences of suffering a puncture. It is not necessary to wait to check its effects since they are immediate and are visible between five and six months.
There is not one time of year more recommended than another to undergo a treatment of Botox infiltration. It has few contraindications and, as with any medication, the side effects vary according to each person. Allergic-type reactions, nausea and facial stiffness would be the most common adverse responses to treatments with Botox. Other applications of Botox
Although as noted a few lines above, the use of Botox in aesthetic matter is the most popular and widespread, botulinum toxin has many other medical applications. It’s potential to paralyze muscle functioning makes it ideal for controlling the intermittent and involuntary contraction of the muscles of the eye, which in scientific terms is known as blepharospasm. It is equally useful in other ailments with spasms.
Currently, it’s a part of treatments for urinary incontinence of people with paraplegia with very favorable results. Hyperhidrosis can also be regulated by the use of Botox. The activity of the sweat glands is reduced and the effects of this body expression (which is characterized by excessive sweating in the armpits, hands and feet) are greatly reduced. As you can see, the benefits of Botox go beyond the magic of subtracting years from the appearance of faces.
