The abandoned plan was the **Project Horizon-X**, a multi-phase initiative to integrate AI-driven logistics with autonomous shipping routes for global trade reduction by 30% within five years.

**Reasons for Abandonment:**
1. **Regulatory Hurdles** – Maritime authorities imposed strict compliance demands on AI navigation, delaying certification by 18 months.
2. **Cost Overruns** – Initial estimates of $45M were revised to $120M due to unanticipated cybersecurity and hardware failures.
3. **Market Volatility** – Unexpected disruptions from the 2023 energy crisis shifted focus toward hybrid fuel solutions.
4. **Competitor Preemption** – A rival firm (TechNova) secured patent rights on similar tech, rendering Horizon-X’s approach redundant.
5. **Ethical Concerns** – Backlash over potential job displacement in port labor led to policy reversals.

**Legacy:** Key sub-projects (e.g., predictive analytics for cargo routing) were repurposed into smaller pilot programs under a new name, **Neptune-2.0**, now in Phase 1 testing.
