**Tidal Pool in the Outer Reef, Nova Scotia**
Waves of tourists and locals converge at low tide, forming semi-circular clusters around the warm, shallow waters. The rhythmic ebb and flow of visitors—some sunbathing, others snorkeling—mirrors the ocean’s tide. During peak hours, the beachfront is packed with colorful umbrellas and kayaks, while at night, the same crowd disperses, leaving only the faint glow of lanterns and distant boat traffic. The rhythmic patterns of arrival and departure create a dynamic yet harmonious rhythm, blending human activity with the natural cycle of the sea.
