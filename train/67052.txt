Is lycra a synthetic fabric which is also known as?
Spandex, Lycra, or elastane is a synthetic fiber known for its exceptional elasticity. It is a polyether-polyurea copolymer that was invented in 1958 by chemist Joseph Shivers at DuPont’s Benger Laboratory in Waynesboro, Virginia, US.

Is Lycra cotton?
Lycra is an elastic fiber invented by DuPont in 1959. It is a fabric made of 90% to 95% cotton with 10% to 5% of Lycra. Although the pure cotton is very comfortable, it still has the disadvantages of lack of elasticity, easy wrinkling, and difficulty drying.
