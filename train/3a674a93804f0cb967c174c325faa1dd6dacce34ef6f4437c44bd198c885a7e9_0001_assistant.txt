Out is the Savannah
With beautiful orange hues
Lives the most incredible animals like in a zoo
Majestic lions roar with their cubs galore
Zebras and antelopes running to and from the river
With crocodiles and hippos fighting in the mud
Large birds take flight under the sun
The heat is heavy and the air is thick
Nature is creative and gorgeous, to say the least
Out on the safari- come with me!
