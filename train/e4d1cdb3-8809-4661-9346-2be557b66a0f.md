A sentinel of polished obsidian, its jagged edges whisper secrets to the wind, a guardian of forgotten time. Its surface hums with latent energy, veins of black glass pulsing faintly, alive with the slow, deliberate heartbeat of cosmic resonance. When touched, it shivers—not in fear, but in curiosity—its depths reflecting not images, but memories, both ancient and fractured, like the echoes of a world long erased. It bends toward you not to speak, but to *listen*, its hollow center a silent mirror where the past and present bleed together. The air around it crackles with the static of things unseen, and if you linger too long, it seems to tilt its gaze toward you, as if acknowledging your presence in a universe that has long forgotten how to name itself.
