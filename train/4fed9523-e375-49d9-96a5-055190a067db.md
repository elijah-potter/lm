In the quiet, mist-shrouded village of Eldermere, the old clock tower stood as a silent sentinel. Its hands had long since stopped at 3:17, marking the final minutes before the **Moonfall Ritual**.

The villagers gathered in the central plaza, their breath visible in the cold air. Among them stood **Elara**, the village seer, her eyes glowing faintly with enchanted knowledge. "The Moon is dying," she announced, her voice carrying across the crowd. "Its light will fade, and with it, the balance between worlds. The choice is ours: honor the ancient pact or risk eternal darkness."

A murmur rippled through the crowd. The **Moonfall** was an age-old ritual where the villagers would decide their fate. Four paths were carved into the village’s ancient stone slab:

1. **The Path of Sacrifice** – Offer a life in exchange for eternal light. Only the bravest or those who had nothing to lose could attempt this.
2. **The Path of Division** – Split the village into two groups. One would feast on the Moon’s essence, the other would remain in shadow forever.
3. **The Path of Binding** – Bind the Moon to a physical object (a crystal, a stone, a weapon) to keep its light alive, but at the cost of its power diminishing with each passing day.
4. **The Path of Silence** – Ignore the Moon’s death, believing its light would return. The village would live in perpetual twilight, but without struggle.

Elara turned to the crowd, her voice echoing like thunder. **"Which path will you walk?"** Below, the four options glowed faintly, waiting for the villagers’ decisions.
