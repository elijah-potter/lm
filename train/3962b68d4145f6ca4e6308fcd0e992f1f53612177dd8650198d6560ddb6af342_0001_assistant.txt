Africa, the second largest continent on Earth, has a total surface area of approximately 30,244,000 km². The largest country on the continent is Algeria, a country in the northern part of Africa. Part of the Maghreb region, the country covers an area of 2,381,741 square kilometers, making it the tenth largest nation by area on earth and 200 times as large as the smallest country in Africa, The Gambia. It is the tenth-most populous country in Africa and 32nd in the world with a total population of 44 million.
