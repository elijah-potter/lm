When the clock strikes 8 PM on Saturday, October 30th, a mystery will ensue at Clark Castle, 813 Finchingham Lane. You are most humbly invited to take part in these annual Halloween festivities. Who will be murdered this year within the hallowed halls of this usually joyful home? Come prepared for a night of fun and frivolity but also expect the weird, uncanny, and eerie as much will take place between these candlelit walls. Food and drink will be served while guests unravel the secrets and riddles behind an untimely, tragic death. Will it be you? Details to follow regarding your role in, what is to be, a most eventful and chilling evening.
