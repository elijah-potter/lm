"Great show out there tonight, Teller. I don't think there was a single member of the audience that wasn't fooled!", Penn said as he packed away some of the stage props from the night's show. Teller looked at Penn with a happy expression, but it gradually turned sour into a look of uncertainty and discomfort. "Hey, buddy...is everything alright? You doing okay?", Penn asked softly. There was a long silence as Teller, who was famous for not speaking, glared into Penn's eyes. Then, finally, Teller erupted into a fiery yell that sent Penn almost stumbling backward. "I CAN'T TAKE THIS ANYMORE!", he blurted out. "I'm tired of being the silent one! I have thoughts! I have ideas! There's so much more I can offer than just an assistant who stays quiet!". There was another long pause while Penn took all this in and collected his thoughts. "Well...", Penn replied. "I just thought you weren't capable of talking! If you can talk, I don't know why you never did so before. I'd love to hear all about these ideas!". Teller slowly started to smile again. He knew that maybe this was all one big misunderstanding. Why hadn't he spoken up before? It would have made things so much easier!
