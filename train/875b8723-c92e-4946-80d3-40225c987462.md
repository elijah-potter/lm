**Silent Plaza**
A wide, open public space lined with low seating and benches arranged in semicircles, allowing for peripheral viewing. Folding tables with no items are placed along the edges, serving as silent conversation prompts via empty spaces. Large, interactive projection screens display static visuals or minimalist animations, encouraging quiet observation. Noise-canceling headphones are available for rent at the entrance, reducing ambient sounds. Designated "noise-free zones" with acoustic panels and soft lighting discourage loud speech, fostering an atmosphere of contemplative presence.
