Let me tell you a story about what most folks want to call a simpler time - but I can tell you right now it was anything but that. They didn't call it the wild west for no reason, no sir. Back in that day, you couldn't go anywhere without watchin' yer back. You had to keep an eye out and trust no one but yourself. Even your best friend could turn his back on ye in a split second without narry a warning at all. I've known quite a few men of those days myself - those that would try to convince you they were going to be there for you through thick and thin only to show those true colors too quickly. This story... it starts out happy, a group of buddies out on the range sharing good times and good stories, but it doesn't end the way you would think, I'll tell you that right now.
