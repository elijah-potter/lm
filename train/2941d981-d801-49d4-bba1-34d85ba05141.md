Endless voids stretch beneath the horizon, a vast emptiness where the sky bleeds into the horizon line like a dying sun. The silence is a physical weight, pressing down on the edges of perception, swallowing all sound into the abyss. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, cast by a light so faint it’s barely there—only the faintest whisper of a glow, like a dying ember on a distant planet. The air feels thin, as if gravity itself has thinned with distance, pulling at the edges of the body. The horizon is a line that never closes, a curve that bends into the sky like a wound that won’t heal. No landmarks, no signs, no end—just the endless repetition of the same endless nothingness.
