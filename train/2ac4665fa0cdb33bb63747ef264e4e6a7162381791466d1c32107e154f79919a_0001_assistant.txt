The blade of the knife glints sharply in the sunlight. With a soft swish, the knife cuts down into the fleshy meat that offers no resistance at all. A hard crunching sound echoes through the sterile, empty room as the knife encounters bone. Then a crack, and the bone also shatters. A thin line of blood seeps out from under the knife, dyeing the wooden board red. Faster and faster, the cutting stops being methodical and starts becoming frenzied. Blood splatters all over the knife and the boy holding it, and pieces of meat fly out everywhere. There's no reason or purpose. Only the joy of destruction.

Suddenly, the hand holding the knife stops. Footsteps are approaching.

"Who's in here?" The door slowly creaks open.

The knife clatters to the floor as the boy looks into a pair of furious eyes. It's already too late to escape. Strong arms hold him in place as he struggles to no avail. His breathing becomes labored as panic rises in his heart. 

"Now, what have I told you about playing in the kitchen? You're grounded, young man!"

The boy slumps down in defeat.
