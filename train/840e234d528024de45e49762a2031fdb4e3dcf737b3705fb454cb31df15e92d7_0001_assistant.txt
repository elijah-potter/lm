The world's tallest building is called Burj Khalifa and it is located in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. The building was completed in 2010 in downtown Dubai and comes in at 2,717 feet, with 163 floors, one of which is underground. Not only is Burj Khalifa the tallest building, but it also holds the record for the tallest free-standing structure, highest observation deck, elevator with the longest distance, tallest service elevator and the highest occupied floors. The tower holds numerous luxury apartments and restaurants. There are also high-end hotels and corporate suites all tucked away neatly inside this massive structure.
