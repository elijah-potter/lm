4. Assemble according to instructions.
5. Once assembled, make sure time machine is free of debris.
6. To prevent overheating, use time machine in a well-ventilated area.

10. Confirm settings before pressing big red button.
11. When ready to travel, press big red button.
12. If time machine fails to transport occupant, refer to troubleshooting guide or contact manufacturer.
