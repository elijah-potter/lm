Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a garden full of sunshine, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had soft, fluffy ears and a heart full of joy. Every morning, Benny would hop outside to see his favorite friend, a blue butterfly named Bella.

One sunny afternoon, Benny saw something magical near the flowers. A tiny, shiny treasure box was hidden in the grass. Benny carefully picked it up and opened it. Inside were golden acorns that sparkled like stars! Benny thought, "These are the best acorns ever!"

He shared his acorns with Bella, and they danced in the garden, their wings flapping happily. Bella gave Benny a kiss, and Benny knew this was the best day ever.

After playing, Benny went back to his home and fell asleep with a smile, dreaming of more adventures with Bella and his golden acorns. And that’s how Benny discovered the happiest treasure in the world—happiness itself!
