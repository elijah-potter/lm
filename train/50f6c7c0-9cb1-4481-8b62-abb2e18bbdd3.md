Aerobic endurance training (e.g., 3–5x/week, 45–60 min) with structured intervals (e.g., 30s sprints/1min jog) and progressive overload on strength (1–3x/week, 4–6 reps at 70–85% 1RM).
Neuromuscular drills (plyometrics, agility ladders) 2x/week for reactive speed.
Mobility/yoga (3–4x/week) to prevent injury and maintain joint stability.
Cold exposure (e.g., 2–3x/week, 5–10 min post-workout) to enhance recovery and resilience.
Nutrition: 1.2–1.6g protein/kg body weight, 2–3g fiber/day, timed carbs for glycogen replenishment.
Sleep: 7–9h/night with consistent timing; prioritize 90-minute cycles for deep recovery.
Mindset: Visualization/mental rehearsal (10–15 min/day) to sharpen focus and decision-making under pressure.
Adaptability: Dynamic warm-ups (bodyweight circuits, dynamic stretches) and skill-specific drills (e.g., shooting form, passing under fatigue).
