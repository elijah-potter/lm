Do aliens exist? Are we the only intelligent creatures in the universe?

We have no proof that aliens exist, even though some of us would love it if Baby Yoda* were real.

Since we have no discernable way to know that aliens occupy our universe with us:

I don't believe that aliens exist.

How can we possibly believe that something we cannot know is true? We don't know if a similar evolution of life here on Earth has happened elsewhere. 

Where are our fellow advanced life forms? 

Even though many planets we know share our universe with Earth, until we know for sure, I don't think aliens exist.
