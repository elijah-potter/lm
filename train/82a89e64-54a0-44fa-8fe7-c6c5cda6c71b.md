Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had soft brown fur and big, round eyes that sparkled like stars.

Every morning, Benny would wake up and stretch his tiny paws. "Good morning, sun!" he would say as he hopped outside to enjoy the fresh morning air. Benny loved to play in the tall grass, chasing butterflies and collecting shiny acorns. His favorite place was the little brook where he could splash and giggle.

One sunny afternoon, Benny noticed something strange. The flowers in his garden were all withered and brown, and the butterflies were nowhere to be seen. Benny felt sad and worried. He hopped to the nearest friend—a wise old owl named Oliver—who lived in a big tree.

"Oliver," Benny asked, "why are the flowers wilting? And where are the butterflies?"

Oliver hooted softly. "I see what’s happening, Benny. The wind has been blowing too hard, and the sun hasn’t been shining as brightly as it should. The flowers need our help!"

Benny hopped excitedly. "Let’s fix it!" he said. "I’ll ask my friends to help!"

Benny called all his friends—the squirrels, the bees, and even the friendly old turtle Mr. Thumb. Together, they worked hard. Benny and his friends blew gently on the flowers with their tiny noses, making them wiggle and smile again. The bees buzzed around, filling the air with sweet music, and the squirrels scattered seeds to help the flowers grow strong.

Soon, the flowers bloomed again, their petals pink and yellow, and the butterflies fluttered back, dancing happily in the sunshine. Benny grinned. "We did it! Together, we made everything beautiful again!"

From that day on, Benny and his friends always looked out for each other. They played, shared, and took care of their little home, making sure it was always a happy and colorful place to live. And if you listen very closely on a warm, sunny afternoon, you might just hear Benny hopping happily, "Good morning, sun!"
