What is relational database management system (RDBMS)?
Others answers (1)
- eAnswers...Nov 3, 2013
The Relational Database Management System (RDBMS) is a type of database management system which was introduced by E. F. Codd while working in IBM. It stores data in the form of related tables. It became the predominant choice for the storage of information in 1980s as it requires few assumptions about how data is related or how it will be extracted from the database.
