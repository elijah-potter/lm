Here's a list of brands that are sustainable and eco-friendly that offer clothing items that cost less than $80:

- Patagonia: Outdoor clothing with a commitment to environmental and social programs. In addition to carefully sourcing materials and creating safe working conditions, Patagonia also offers supply chain and facilities transparency reporting.
- tentree: Offering many kinds of casual clothing. tentree is unique in its commitment to planting ten trees for every item purchased from its brand. Their clothing is made from recycled or sustainably sourced materials.
- Pact: As a partner with Fair Trade USE, Global Organic Textile Standard, and SimpliZero, Pact offers certified sustainable products and processes. Their selections include clothing for all ages, as well as linens for bed and bath.
- MATE the Label: Featuring clothing made with natural and organic materials, MATE the Label promises to be clean from harvest through the finished product.
- For Days: For Days aims to lower clothing waste by making fashion that is 100% recyclable. They also offer accessories, bags, and beauty products.
