## Lost in the Record Store (Brooklyn, NY) (October 26)

I was browsing the used vinyl at Rough Trade in Williamsburg last Thursday afternoon, completely absorbed in a search for an obscure jazz compilation. You were examining the same section, a few feet away. I noticed the way the late afternoon light caught the copper highlights in your hair, and the quiet intensity with which you handled each record. We didn’t speak, but I remember the distinct, comforting aroma of aged paper and worn cardboard that permeated the air—a familiar and nostalgic scent. You were wearing a faded denim jacket, and I distinctly recall the soft, almost imperceptible click of the turntable as someone played a track nearby. I hesitated, wanting to say something, anything, but the moment passed. I left shortly thereafter, feeling a strange sense of regret. I often return to that section now, hoping for another glimpse. 

If you were searching for early Miles Davis and happen to wear a silver ring on your left hand, please respond with your favorite track from *Kind of Blue*.
