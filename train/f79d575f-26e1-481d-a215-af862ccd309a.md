### **Story Hook: The Last Scholar’s Dilemma**
The scholar **Kael Veyne** uncovered the map during an excavation in the **Silent Library’s** ruins. A warning inscription glowed under UV light:
*"To find the past, you must first unravel the present."* The map’s coordinates point to a **floating library**—a derelict sky-whale (a 17th-century transport vessel) now adrift in the ruins’ upper levels.

**Objective:**
- Retrieve the *Chronicle of the Fallen* from the vault beneath the Aqueduct.
- Avoid the **Nyxflux cultists** (they’ve been altering the map’s symbols to trap intruders).
- Decipher the ritual to stabilize the Obsidian Core—before the city’s foundations collapse into the underground river.

**Twist:**
The Bloodstone in the Market District is cursed. Touching it reveals fragments of Kael’s own past—hinting he’s not the first to seek this knowledge.
