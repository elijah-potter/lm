**Caffeine Dependency**

A morning ritual begins with a cold brew, the first sip breaking the night’s inertia. The coffee’s bitter edge sharpens focus, but the cycle is insidious—afternoon cravings demand another dose, and the body adapts, demanding more to function. Withdrawal brings headaches, fatigue, and irritability; the brain’s dopamine pathways adjust, making quitting feel like a marathon of discomfort. Friends joke about the "third cup rule," but the habit evolves: late-night espresso to combat stress, energy drinks to replace coffee, and eventually, the dependency spreads to other stimulants. The mind resists, rationalizing, *"Just one more."* Yet, the body’s tolerance grows, and the cycle persists—proof that even the most fleeting habits can become an ingrained, self-perpetuating routine.
