What Are The Best Research Tools To Help With Your Academic Assignment
“Research is creating new knowledge.” Neil Armstrong, an American astronaut, and aeronautical engineer.
The research process suggests putting enough time and effort into finding accurate information and data, which is then managed, organized, and correctly chosen. If you have no time to be a library geek, research services have all the chances to become real salvation. Study this full list of best and free research tools for any purposes for students:
- Library Catalog
If you are too tired to waste a lot of time to find a collection near you, check it at home! Use WorldCat, a worldwide collector of library groups lists, to find archives in consideration of the specified geolocation that keep the publications you need. Some institutions will also give between-library cooperation and allow asking resources from other places. Undoubtedly, this tool is for those, who prefer traditional print sources.
- Open Online Libraries
For bits and pieces of academic content arranged throughout various realms, look into Open Online Libraries. One of the best places is DPLA, Europeana, Open Library and more, which are supported through the state, or country collections, and thus have incredible data concerning any subject.
These tools collect information from each source, so it's easier to find materials regarding the desired subject. Most online libraries are based on the keyword search principle. As with search engines, the information here is sorted by criteria such as the time and type of publication. However, each of the libraries has its own peculiarities.
- Search Engines
Google Scholar is an academic search system focused on finding scientific literature in different fields of knowledge through various sources. It is a really good researcher tool, which gives access to myriads of scientific materials on any language. In comparison, with usual local or digital libraries, this method is much more functional since Google Scholar can search concerning data on several resources at once.
By specifying your keyword like in Google, you will find lots of scanned articles, theses, books, abstracts and reports published by writers, professional associations, higher education institutions, and other scientific organizations. This research platform is a surprisingly strong tool that will help you find proper content.
Note: Use file-filter to sort out the needed documents. If you are searching epub file, you can use such a combination like “filetype:epub”. Such tricks will allow you to search the free web-prints exclusively in the format you need.
- Academic Social Media Structures
Such popular social media platforms, as Academia.edu and ResearchGate.com are highly promoted today and provide pretty organized and content-centered academic help in a course of searching needed information. These organizations are operating on millions of dollars and so have millions of customers and millions of writers, who are ready to upload and share their papers. However, it is still recommended to use social media researchers tools as additional methods.
