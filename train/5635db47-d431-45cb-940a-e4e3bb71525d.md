## Bookstore Serendipity (Seattle, WA)

The rain was relentless that afternoon, a steady drumming against the awning of Elliott Bay Books. I was browsing the poetry section, attempting to find a volume by Mary Oliver, when you appeared. You were searching for something in the travel writing aisle, and the light catching your hair – a rich, auburn hue – made it seem to glow. I distinctly remember the comforting aroma of old paper and brewing coffee mingling in the air, a familiar and welcome scent. We briefly bumped shoulders, a silent apology exchanged with a shy smile. You were wearing a navy blue scarf, and I noticed the delicate silver ring on your left hand. I lingered, hoping for a shared observation about a favorite author, but you moved on, disappearing into the labyrinthine shelves. I felt a peculiar sense of regret at not speaking. I’m certain you felt it too.

If you recall a chance encounter amidst the poetry and travel sections of Elliott Bay Books on a rainy afternoon, and perhaps noticed a woman with a fondness for Mary Oliver, please respond with the name of your favorite travel destination.
