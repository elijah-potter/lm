Deep shadows stretched across the cracked pavement where the last embers of a dying fire flickered against the rain-soaked window. The air smelled of damp earth and something metallic—rust from an old pipe. A lone figure hunched over a cracked desk, fingers tracing the edges of an open ledger, half-filled with faded ink. The clock on the wall chimed once, too slow, as if the town itself had forgotten time.

A whisper of wind carried the scent of blooming jasmine from a windowless room, where a child’s laughter echoed from a hidden space. The walls pulsed faintly with the hum of a forgotten machine, its gears grinding into silence as the figure stood abruptly, their coat damp against their skin. Outside, the river murmured secrets against the stone bridge’s weathered planks.
