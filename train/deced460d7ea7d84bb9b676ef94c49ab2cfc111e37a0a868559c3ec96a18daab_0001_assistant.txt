There are many places to explore in the area around Sparkling Hill Resort, here are some ideas:

1. Okanagan Rail Trail: Experience a 52 km trail that follows the lakeshore and creeks with multiple points of interest and rich history. A great place to take some photographs of the lake and local wildlife.
2. Helicopter Ride: Multiple helicopter charters offer services for Sparkling Hill guests. Take a scenic, wine, or picnic tour around beautiful Kelowna and have a sky-high perspective for taking photographs.
3. Wine Tour: Lake Country in the heart of Okanagan Valley is known for its vineyards and wineries. There you can experience many different types of wines, go on wine tours and participate in wine-tasting events. Some examples of wineries include the 50th Parallel Estate Winery, Peak Cellars, and Arrowleaf Cellars.
4. Swan Lake Nature Reserve: Swan Lake is a waterfowl area and is located on a main migration corridor. The reserve offers a looped trail with views of Great Blue Herons, marsh birds, raptors, geese, and ducks.
5. Predator Ridge: Known as Canada's premier golf resort community, the resort offers dining options, wine tours, winter activities, and trails to explore.
