Megasthenes — Father of Indian History
Megasthenes is considered the father of Indian History. He was an ambassador of Seleucus I Nicator, the founder of the Seleucid dynasty of Persia.
His book Indika was an account of his travels in India, which he visited around 302 BC.
He wrote about the geography and culture of India, including descriptions of its people and their customs. He described a society that had no private property or land ownership, with each person being free to live as they pleased.
