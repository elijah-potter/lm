There are four major tournaments in golf. Here they are:
- The Masters. This is played in Augusta, Georgia every April.
- The PGA Championship. This tournament is played in May and is on a different course every year on the East coast of the United States. 
- The US Open. This also is played at a different location each year and it falls every June. 
- The Open Championship. This is also known as the British Open. It is played at a links course in the UK in July.
