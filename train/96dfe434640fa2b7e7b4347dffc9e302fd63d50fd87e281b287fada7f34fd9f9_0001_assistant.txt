I have to admit that I didn't see it coming. 
I woke up one morning, and Joshua's saying we were going to attack Jericho. Jericho-- that big city on top of a mountain, surrounded by huge stone walls. 
And how did he plan to do this? With trumpets. You can't make this stuff up.
He gave the orders: for six days, we were going to march around the city. A siege, of sorts. But we had to remain completely silent. Only the sound of our sandals hitting the rough gravel. 
We were tired. We were hungry. We were hot. We could picture the citizens of Jericho laughing at our little parade. We kept walking and staring at the stone walls.
But on the seventh day, something amazing happened.
This time, on our final trip around the city, we stopped. On Joshua's signal, we all blew our trumpets and shouted. Now remember, we were exhausted after doing nothing but stomping on rocks for a week. So our shout wasn't anything Earth-shattering.
But that's what happened.
After our shouts fell silent, the huge stone walls of Jericho literally shattered and fell straight to the ground. All the way embedded in the Earth.
It left the city completely exposed. Obviously, the citizens hadn't foreseen that outcome. They hadn't even left their homes on the border of the city. Joshua gave the orders, and we ransacked the town, destroying anything of value.
Tonight, I'm lying on a cot instead of the hard ground. But I can't say I'll be sleeping after all of the excitement. I'm not sure what's next for us, but Joshua definitely made a believer out of me. I'll follow him anywhere and do exactly as he says.
