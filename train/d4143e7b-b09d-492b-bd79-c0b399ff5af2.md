The discovery of the **Fermi Paradox**—the apparent contradiction between the high probability of extraterrestrial civilizations and the lack of evidence for their existence—has sparked extensive debate. While no definitive answers exist, recent findings in **exoplanet research** (e.g., Kepler-442b, TRAPPIST-1 system) and **interstellar probes** (like Voyager 1’s detection of the heliopause) suggest potential explanations:

1. **Great Filter Hypothesis** – Civilizations may self-destruct before achieving interstellar travel (e.g., nuclear war, climate collapse).
2. **Zoo Hypothesis** – Advanced species may intentionally avoid detection to preserve their own survival.
3. **Technological Singularity** – AI or other breakthroughs could render life unsustainable beyond a certain point.

Further analysis of **microwave signals from deep space** (e.g., Fast Radio Bursts) and **molecular signatures in interstellar dust** may reveal whether these phenomena are natural or artificial. If confirmed, they could hint at **intelligent life’s motives** or **the rarity of advanced civilizations**.
