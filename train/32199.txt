Xanax can also be called as Alprazolam. It was introduced primarily to cure panic attacks and depression in 1980’s. It falls under the class of medications known as Benzodiazepine. Xanax is highly helpful and it is the most prescribed drug for the patients who have issues like anxiety and depression.
How Xanax functions in treating anxiety and depression
The main function of the drug is that it will help in reducing the overflow of excitement and adrenaline rush caused in the brain which might lead to anxiety and depression. It will decrease the functionality of the brain nerves that act as the main agent in provoking depression.
Many might have confusion between the working mechanism of an antidepressant and Xanax. The primary difference is that Xanax will have an immediate effect and it will help in reducing the depression and anxiety, whereas antidepressants will take a few days to a week to turn down the nerves that cause anxiety.

What are the side effects a person can face while taking Xanax?
Common side effects of Xanax include:
- Memory related problems
- Anxious feelings in the early morning hours
- Feeling drowsy, tired
- Slurred speech, lack of coordination, no balance in the body
If you notice certain signs of allergic reactions in your body like breathing trouble, hives, swelling of the face, lips, throat, face, get medical help immediately.
You need to consult a reputed physician without any waiting if you encounter any of these below changes,
- Feeling Agitated, confused, depressed,
- Unusual thoughts, increased energy levels, uncontrolled movement of muscles,
- fast heartbeats, fluttering in the chest, suicidal thoughts
- hallucinations, tremor, seizures (convulsions)
- risk-taking behavior that is unusual
Things to keep in mind while taking Xanax:
- Avoid consuming alcohol while taking Xanax as it might increase the effects of alcohol.
- Also, avoid taking it if you are pregnant.
- Do not stop taking the medication all of a sudden without having a proper instruction from your doctor, as it might cause higher blood pressure and shakiness.
- Also, make sure that you stay away from fruit juices & Vitamin C as they might reduce the impact of Xanax.
- Do not crush or chew tablet
- It should be consumed whole.
- An abrupt stop of the drug can lead to severe health problems.
