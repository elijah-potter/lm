Is baking the same as grilling?
Both baking and grilling are dry heat methods of cooking. The main difference between bake and grill is that baking is a slower, lower-intensity cooking method while grilling is a quick method of cooking food over direct heat at high temperature.
Where is the broiler in an oven?
