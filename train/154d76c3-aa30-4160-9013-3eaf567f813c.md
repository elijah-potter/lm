A single morning shift at the warehouse, where the forklift driver—always the first to arrive—stacked an extra pallet of misaligned boxes without complaint. The box was slightly off-center, but the forklift operator adjusted it without asking, securing it neatly in the rack. No one saw it, no one commented, but by the end of the day, the load was perfectly balanced, preventing a potential tip.
