The dimly lit hallway echoed with the soft hum of fluorescent lights. Maria paused mid-step, her fingers brushing against the edge of a half-empty coffee cup on the table beside her. She glanced at the counter, where a neatly stacked dish rack held only two mismatched mugs—one still bearing yesterday’s tea stains, the other empty.

Her eyes flicked to the cabinet above the sink, where a single framed photo of her family still hung, though the corner of the photo had been crumpled. She exhaled, adjusting her grip on the grocery bag she’d dropped by the door. The fridge hummed quietly, its shelves empty save for a single can of beans. She remembered leaving the last of the groceries at the store, but the bag was still in her car. The realization settled in her chest like a stone. *Something was missing.*
