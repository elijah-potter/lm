## Product Requirements Document: "Context" - Intelligent Meeting Summarization & Action Item Management

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers spend an inordinate amount of time in meetings, often struggling to accurately recall key decisions, action items, and relevant context weeks later, leading to duplicated effort and missed deadlines.

**Vision:** To be the indispensable tool that empowers knowledge workers to effortlessly capture, organize, and retrieve meeting context, transforming unproductive meeting fatigue into proactive productivity.

---

### User Personas

**1. Amelia Chen, Senior Marketing Manager:**

*   **Goals:** To efficiently track marketing campaign progress, ensure team alignment on strategy, and consistently deliver results on time and within budget.
*   **Pain Points:** Feels overwhelmed by the sheer volume of meetings, struggles to remember specifics discussed weeks prior, and often finds herself re-asking questions or rediscovering information already shared.  Lacks a centralized repository for meeting-related decisions and actions.

**2. David Rodriguez, Software Engineer:**

*   **Goals:** To contribute effectively to complex software development projects, proactively address technical roadblocks, and maintain a clear understanding of project scope and responsibilities.
*   **Pain Points:**  Finds technical discussions in meetings difficult to follow and recall, particularly when dealing with nuanced architectural decisions.  Often spends time searching through email threads to reconstruct the rationale behind past decisions.  Feels pressure to quickly respond to requests without complete context.

**3. Eleanor Vance, Project Lead:**

*   **Goals:** To successfully manage multiple projects simultaneously, maintain clear project timelines and budgets, and ensure stakeholder satisfaction.
*   **Pain Points:**  Spends considerable time compiling meeting notes and distributing action items, a task that feels administrative and detracts from strategic leadership.  Finds it challenging to track the status of action items across different teams and individuals.  Worries about miscommunication and missed dependencies.

---

### Requirements

**1. Automated Meeting Transcription & Summary:**

*   **Description:** The application will automatically transcribe online meetings (initially Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet) and generate a concise summary highlighting key decisions, discussion points, and action items.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** Transcription accuracy must exceed 90% in standard meeting environments. Summary should be no longer than 10% of the meeting duration. Users can manually edit transcriptions and summaries.
*   **Success Metrics:** Average transcription accuracy score (measured through user feedback), average summary length, user edits per summary.

**2. Intelligent Action Item Extraction & Assignment:**

*   **Description:** The application will automatically identify potential action items from meeting transcripts, suggest assignees based on context, and allow for manual assignment and due date setting.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:**  The system identifies at least 75% of action items present in a sample of meetings. Suggested assignees are relevant to the action item’s content. Users can easily accept, reject, or modify suggested assignments.
*   **Success Metrics:** Action item identification accuracy, user acceptance rate of suggested assignments, average time spent assigning action items.

**3. Contextual Search & Retrieval:**

*   **Description:** Users can search across all transcribed meetings and summaries using keywords, phrases, or assignee names, retrieving relevant snippets of conversation.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** Search results are returned within 2 seconds. Search results accurately reflect the context of the searched term. Users can filter search results by date, assignee, and meeting type.
*   **Success Metrics:** Average search latency, relevance of search results (measured through user feedback), number of searches performed per user per week.

**4. Secure Meeting Recording & Storage:**

*   **Description:** The application will securely store meeting recordings and transcripts, accessible only to authorized users.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:** Data is encrypted both in transit and at rest. User access is controlled through role-based permissions. Recordings are stored for a user-configurable retention period.  Complies with relevant data privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR, CCPA).
*   **Success Metrics:** Number of data access requests, number of reported security incidents, user satisfaction with data privacy controls.

**5. Integrations with Calendar and Task Management Systems:**

*   **Description:** The application will integrate with popular calendar systems (Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar) to automatically detect and join meetings. It will also integrate with task management systems (Asana, Trello, Jira) to seamlessly transfer action items.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:**  Meeting detection is accurate and timely. Action items are transferred to integrated task management systems without data loss.  User authentication is secure and straightforward.
*   **Success Metrics:** Number of calendar integrations, number of task management integrations, user satisfaction with integration functionality.



---

### Technical Considerations

*   **Scalability:** The system must be designed to handle a large volume of meetings and users.
*   **Security:** Robust security measures are paramount to protect sensitive meeting data.
*   **Real-time Transcription:**  Exploring options for near real-time transcription to improve user experience.
*   **Natural Language Processing (NLP):**  Continuous improvement of NLP algorithms to enhance summary accuracy and action item extraction.
*   **Cross-Platform Compatibility:** Initial focus on iOS and Android mobile platforms.

### Risks

*   **Transcription Accuracy:** Achieving consistently high transcription accuracy across diverse meeting environments and accents.
*   **User Adoption:** Overcoming user resistance to new meeting workflows.
*   **Data Privacy Concerns:** Addressing user concerns about data security and privacy.
*   **Integration Complexity:**  Maintaining compatibility with evolving APIs of integrated platforms.



### Non-Goals

*   Real-time meeting participation or screen sharing functionality.
*   Support for audio-only meetings (initially).
*   Integration with obscure or niche meeting platforms.
*   Advanced video analytics or facial recognition.
*   A built-in video editor.
