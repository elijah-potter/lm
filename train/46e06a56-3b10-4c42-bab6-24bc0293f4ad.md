An endless void of darkness, stretching infinitely in all directions—no horizon, no boundaries. The silence is suffocating, broken only by the distant, faint hum of a dying star. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, bending toward unseen depths. The air feels thin, as if the atmosphere itself has thinned to nothing, leaving only the cold whisper of space between the stars. Echoes linger, muffled and impossible to trace back to their origin. The weight of the silence presses down, making even the smallest movement feel like an eternity. The universe here is not just vast—it is *alive* in its emptiness, a sentient absence that watches, listens, and waits.
