In the abandoned library of Blackthorn Manor, an old brass key lay half-buried beneath a cracked leather-bound tome titled *The Whispering Ledger*. The key was inscribed with faded runes: **"Keeper’s Secret"** in elegant, decayed script.

The ledger’s final entry, scrawled in frantic handwriting, read:
> *"They’ve taken the last candle. The door won’t open unless you find the message in the ink—*‘Where the river bends, the stones remember names.’*—and tell them the truth. Do not trust the watcher in the attic."*

The message was hidden beneath a loose page in the ledger’s spine, wrapped in a torn parchment: *"The name is *Elias Veyne*, but the river knows the truth—he drowned in ‘93, but the water still calls his ghost back."* A single, glowing drop of ink remained on the parchment’s edge, pulsing faintly like a heartbeat.

The watcher in the attic had already moved—silent, watching. The river at Blackthorn’s edge was still humming with the weight of forgotten things.
