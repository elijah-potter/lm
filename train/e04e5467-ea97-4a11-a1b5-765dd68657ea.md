The abandoned plan involved deploying a drone swarm for automated crop monitoring in a high-altitude desert region. Key steps included:
1. **Site Survey**: Laser mapping of terrain and weather patterns revealed unstable microclimates.
2. **Technical Feasibility**: Sensor calibration errors and thermal drift caused 30% of drones to malfunction during initial testing.
3. **Budget Overruns**: Procurement delays for proprietary AI algorithms exceeded $250K, forcing reallocation to a rival project.
4. **Regulatory Hurdles**: FAA waivers required for nighttime operations stalled due to lack of emergency response protocols for lost drones.
5. **Resource Reassignment**: Core team members transferred to a solar farm expansion project, leaving insufficient expertise for drone integration.

**Final Decision**: Abandoned in favor of a hybrid ground-robot system with modular sensor swaps, prioritizing reliability and regulatory compliance.
