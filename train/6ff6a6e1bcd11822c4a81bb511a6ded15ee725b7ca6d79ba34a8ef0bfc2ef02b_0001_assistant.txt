Workers' Retreat
Friday, January 5 to Sunday, January 7, 2024
Location: Camp Erdman Camp Grounds

Summary: On Friday, January 5, 2024, all employees from Galactic Media, Inc. will participate in a Workers' Retreat at the Camp Erdman Camp Grounds. This event will be hosted by Galactic Media and the renowned Organic Media Group from Sweden. The retreat is designed to promote synergy and teamwork among employees and to foster a community of like-minded individuals to help them achieve both individual and group goals and objectives. The Retreat will run from 2 pm on Friday, January 5, to Sunday, January 7 at 1 pm.

Participants are advised to bring the following items with them to the retreat: one (1) duffel bag containing three (3) sets of clothing; toiletries (toothpaste/toothbrush, shampoo, soap, shaving kits); one (1) reusable water bottle; and one (1) sturdy flashlight. Three (3) meals a day will be provided to all participants by the hosts, and lodging will be assigned for participants in groups of five.

The following is a list of events that will take place during the weekend retreat:

1. Tug of war
2. Obstacle course
3. Scavenger hunt
4. Mountain hike
5. Bike ride
6. Beach run
7. Game night
8. Dodgeball
9. Dinner preparation challenge
10. Final test

We anticipate everyone will enjoy the retreat's activities and games, as well as the amenities that will be provided to them. Meet with your group supervisors to find out more details about this upcoming event.
