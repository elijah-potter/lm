1. Denial - During this stage, people are typically numb to what has occurred and may progress with their day as if nothing has happened. 
2. Anger - At this point, anger bubbles up towards the deceased, those close to them, anyone who may be to blame for their death, and even towards oneself.
3. Bargaining - When this stage is reached, people try to make deals with themselves or with god to rationalize why they lost their loved one.
4. Depression - At this point, people feel very intense and heavy sadness and yearning. This period can last months or years and may affect one's ability to live a healthy life.
5. Acceptance - When this stage is reached, the pain of loss begins to ease and people begin to develop an acceptance of death while learning that they cannot spend an eternity in grief and should live a full life.
