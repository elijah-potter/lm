**Mayor’s Office Announces Historic Climate Initiative in Eldermere**
Residents of Eldermere are invited to participate in a city-wide green energy trial starting Friday. The initiative, *"Solar Shores Initiative,"* will provide subsidized solar panel installation and battery storage for qualifying households. Applications must be submitted by **October 15th** via the city’s online portal. Energy savings from the pilot could reduce Eldermere’s carbon footprint by **15% annually**, with results expected in 2025.

Officials emphasize that eligibility requires income below **$90,000/year** and a minimum of **300 sq. ft. of rooftop space**. The city will cover **70% of installation costs**, with the remaining **30% deducted from property taxes**. Additional incentives include **$500 in rebates** for participants who install **wind micro-turbines** alongside solar.

*"This is Eldermere’s chance to lead by example,"* said Mayor Elara Voss. *"We’re not just reducing emissions—we’re creating jobs and setting a new standard for sustainable living."*

Full details, including a **step-by-step application guide**, are available at [EldermereGreenEnergy.gov](https://www.eldermeregreenenergy.gov) or by calling **312-555-7890**. Deadline extended to **October 15th**.
