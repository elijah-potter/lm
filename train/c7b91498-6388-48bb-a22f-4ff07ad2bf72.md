Deep within the hollowed earth, the damp coolness clings like a whisper of moss. The air hums with the slow, rhythmic pulse of unseen life—roots twisting and coiling, seeking moisture in the dark. The scent of damp stone and decayed organic matter lingers, thick and earthy, a perfume of forgotten time. Every so often, a distant murmur of water trickles, sharp and brittle, before fading into silence. The ground beneath shifts beneath bare feet, yielding and yielding, as if holding secrets buried for centuries.
