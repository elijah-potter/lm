Lily the bunny hopped through the sunny meadow, her soft pink ears flopping. The flowers were giggling at her as she munched on clover. Suddenly, a tiny, shimmering star fell near her paws.

"Oh no!" Lily gasped. She picked up the star and tucked it into her pocket. As she hopped home, she wondered if the star would glow for her.

That night, Lily snuggled under her cozy blanket. The star in her pocket twinkled brighter than ever! She giggled and whispered, "What magic is this?" The next morning, Lily found a tiny, shiny rainbow painted on the meadow. She laughed so hard she hopped three times in a row!

From then on, whenever Lily felt lonely, she would hold her sparkly star and imagine a rainbow had appeared just for her. And oh, how the meadow sparkled on those days!
