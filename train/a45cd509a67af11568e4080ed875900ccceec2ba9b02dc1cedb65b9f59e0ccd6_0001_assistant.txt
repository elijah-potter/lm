This scientific news article discusses gravitational lensing, which is caused by massive cosmic objects and is in line with Einstein's theory of gravity. Objects with high mass can cause the bending of light paths due to the warping of space-time. Gravitational lensing occurs when a large object bends the path of light, causing a shift in the apparent position of a background object. Gravitational lensing creates stunning visual effects such as Einstein rings and crosses. Gravitational lensing helps astronomers see distant and faint galaxies by making them appear larger in the sky. Massive galaxy clusters like eMACS J1823.1+7822 help scientists map the distribution of dark matter, which makes up a significant portion of the universe's mass.
