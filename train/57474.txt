A lunar base on the Moon could become a reality
Laurent Pambaguian has the enviably futuristic job title of “space architect”. But when it comes to exploration, the French engineer believes the basic principles are unchanged since humans first set sail. “You wouldn’t take a house, you’d take a saw and a hammer and build things out of raw materials,” he says.
Pambaguian is leading a European Space Agency project to design a lunar base capable of housing four astronauts for a hypothetical long-term mission to the Moon. He would equip them with a giant inflatable sphere, water and a 3-D printer. From this, they would build – or rather print – an igloo-like base from moon dust, or regolith.
