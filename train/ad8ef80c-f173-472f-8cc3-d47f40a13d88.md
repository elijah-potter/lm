Soft moss carpets the ground, its damp, earthy texture yielding underfoot. Delicate ferns arch over pebbles, their brittle stems snapping underfoot. Hollow tree roots, smooth and worn, form winding channels in the damp soil. The bark of ancient oaks glistens with age, ridged and layered like stacked stones. A mossy boulder, rounded by centuries of water and wind, offers a cool, slick surface when wet. Sunlight filters through translucent lichen patches, leaving faint, ghostly veins on stone. The air hums with the whisper of damp air through the tangled underbrush.
