The debt ceiling problem in 2023 is not the same as it was in 2011. The people are different now and want different things. In 2011, the two sides argued, like President Obama and Boehner, but they also talked a lot and were friends outside of work. In 2023, President Biden and Speaker McCarthy are not very good friends and are not talking very much, even though the problem is very serious and will cause a disaster soon if not fixed.
