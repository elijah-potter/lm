Sun Tzu was a Chinese general, military strategist, and philosopher who lived in the Eastern Zhou period of ancient China. He is traditionally credited as the author of "The Art of War," a treatise on military strategy and tactics. Although its primary context is warfare, the principles outlined in the work have been applied to various fields, including business, politics, and sports. The basic premise revolves around the idea that strategic planning and understanding of one's own strengths and weaknesses, and those of the enemy, are key to victory—with outright conflict being the last resort.
