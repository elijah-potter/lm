Tools available to all students for cPass operational assessments are outlined below.
Students can select text on the screen to highlight.
Removes both highlights and striker marks from the screen.
Allows students to write notes on a small yellow rectangle on-screen.
Students can enlarge all content on the screen.
Allows students to place a line through an answer choice that is not desired. This only works on multiple-choice (single-select and multiple-select) items types.
Provides supplemental information (basic formulas, etc.) to the student when appropriate. This tool is only available on the General CTE Assessment.
Completes simple arithmetic operations when directed by the student. This tool is not available on the General CTE assessment.
