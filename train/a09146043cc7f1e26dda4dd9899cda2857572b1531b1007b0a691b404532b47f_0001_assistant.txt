In addition, Spain has one of the lower crime rates in Europe. Spain also has a program for those from an English-speaking country. You can easily get a temporary visa via the North American Language and Cultural Assistant program. You teach English in Spanish schools for 12 to 16 hours a week and receive a monthly stipend. The country is home to many different ethnic groups, most Americans will appreciate the diversity. Summers in Georgia are warm and dry while winters can be cold and snowy. 5. Belize:  Belize is known for having a laid-back way of life, gorgeous natural beauty, and a welcoming attitude towards foreigners. Belize is also an incredibly affordable country to live in. Those from the US can apply for permanent residence after living in the country for 50 consecutive weeks. This can be achieved by entering Belize on a tourist visa, then renewing it every 30 days until you reach the 50-week mark. If you don't want to go through this process, Belize also has the Qualified Retirement Program, where you can qualify with a pension or sufficient savings in an IRA, 401(k), or similar. You must be at least 45 years of age and you can bring your dependents with you.
