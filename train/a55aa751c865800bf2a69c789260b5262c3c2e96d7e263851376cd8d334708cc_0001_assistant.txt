Michitaka lived on a small island off the south end of Japan. Bordering a large city, most of the people's needs were met with the city, and the islanders enjoyed a peaceful life. The island had a folktale about doppelgangers. The tale went like this: long ago, the mountain god of the island found a friend. They enjoyed their time together. However, that friend was a human, and one day the friend was murdered. In sorrow, the god made a copy of the friend and continued a charade. The god then became enraged because things weren't the same. The god made a doppelganger of the murderer. The doppelganger killed everyone that person was close with as punishment. 

This was all legend, but when Michitaka and his friends saw a doppelganger of someone they knew, they began to believe the story wasn't just fiction. Michitaka and his friends began investigating the history of the island as strange occurrences started happening. People go missing or turn up dead. Some are seen wandering the streets in the middle of the night. 

As they dive deeper into the truth, the line between reality and fiction becomes blurred. Who is and isn't real? What is an ally? Who can be trusted? These questions plague them, and most importantly, what are the strange dreams that Michitaka has been having? What does it all mean, and can these dreams help them save the island from total destruction? 

Michitaka and his friends will plumb the depths of the island and its history, stopping at nothing to protect the ones they love.
