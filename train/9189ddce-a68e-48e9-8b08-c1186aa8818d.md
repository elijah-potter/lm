Under extreme time pressure, the team prioritized quick fixes for the critical API endpoint failure, deferring non-urgent feature development to stabilize the core system. Key actions included rolling back the last migration script and deploying a temporary proxy server to reroute traffic. Performance metrics showed a 30% reduction in latency, but the long-term architectural review was postponed until the next sprint. The trade-off was justified by the risk of a full system crash, which could have cascaded into customer support overload. The decision was documented in the incident log with action items for the QA team to verify the temporary solution’s stability before reopening the feature.
