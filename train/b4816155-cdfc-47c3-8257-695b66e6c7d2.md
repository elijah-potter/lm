A hastily made decision weighed the urgency of the call against the risk of consequences.
The phone rang at 3:17 AM, the only light coming from a half-melted candle.
*"It’s not a drill,"* the voice on the other end had said, breathless, *"I need you to drive—now."*
The car’s engine sputtered before the clutch bit, tires screeching onto the cracked pavement.
No time to review options—just the weight of the keys in my hand and the sudden, paralyzing fear that this could be the last thing I ever did right.
