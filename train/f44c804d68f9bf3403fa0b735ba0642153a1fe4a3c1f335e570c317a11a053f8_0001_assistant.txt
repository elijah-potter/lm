The purpose of this study is to understand how people feel about gender stereotypes and how they perceive members of the LGBTQ community. In this study, we will ask you questions about your experiences with gender stereotypes and questions about your interactions with anyone in the LGBTQ community, including watching them on television. The study is 50 questions long and will be based on your feedback to a series of 5 speeches. You don't have to answer any question that makes you uncomfortable; just leave that answer space blank. If you find the study makes you uncomfortable, you may stop at any time by simply closing the study. If you think you may be made uncomfortable with the nature of the questions, we suggest that you do not participate in the study.
