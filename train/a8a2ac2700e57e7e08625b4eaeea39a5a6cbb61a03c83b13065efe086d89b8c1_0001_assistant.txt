Raymond Chandler was born Raymond Thornton Chandler, on July 23 1888 in Chicago Illinois. wrote detective fiction, including The Big Sleep and The Long Goodbye. Philip Marlowe, portrayed by Humphrey Bogart in the film version of The Big Sleep is Chandler's most famous character. Marlowe is the iconic hardboiled detective of the brutal  Los Angeles society that was his environment. Although poor, he was idealistic and had a strong sense of justice.
