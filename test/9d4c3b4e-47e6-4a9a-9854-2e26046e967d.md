**Mayor’s Office Announces Emergency Bridge Repair Plan**
Residents of **Silverside** are urged to prepare for delayed commutes as the **Oceanfront Bridge** undergoes overnight repairs. Officials report structural inspections revealed minor cracks near the central span, prompting immediate closure. The city’s **Department of Public Works** has activated a contingency team to reroute traffic via the **Highway 415 Overpass** and the **Driftwood Tunnel**, with estimated disruptions lasting **12–18 hours**. Emergency shuttle buses will operate along **Main Street and Pine Ave** until repairs are complete. Local businesses along the waterfront have been advised to limit foot traffic near the bridge during the closure. No injuries were reported during inspections. For updates, follow @SilversideNews on social media or call **555-123-4567**.

**Additional Measures:**
- **Parking Restrictions:** Vehicles parked within 500 feet of the bridge will be towed starting at **7 PM tonight**.
- **Fishing Advisory:** The **Bayview Marina** will temporarily close its docks for safety during repairs.
- **Volunteer Needed:** City crews seeking volunteers to assist with traffic management—apply by **EOD** at the **Main Street Public Safety Hub**.

Full details at [silverside.gov/bridge-closure](https://silverside.gov/bridge-closure).
