"Gold Stars"

Dandelions are so tiny, yet full of fortitude and strength.  They are edible and can be used in various herbal remedies.  Yet instead of being grateful for them, we despise them.  Why?  Why do we call them weeds and cut them down with such disdain?  No matter how many times we cut them down, they spring right back each time; a gift from mother nature that keeps on giving.  Regardless of what we think of them, they continue to stand tall, forever shining brightly.  Such a priceless performance they put on for us, yet hardly anyone sees.  They are worth so much more than the critics would have you believe, and I hope I've convinced you to start paying attention.
