Once there was a little bunny named Benny who lived in a cozy burrow under the big, fluffy grass. Benny loved to hop around and play with his friends—Milo the mouse, Pip the squirrel, and Tilly the turtle.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his favorite flower, Sunny Blossom, drooping a little. "Why is Sunny Blossom looking sad?" Benny asked.

Milo said, "Maybe it needs water!" So Benny, Pip, and Tilly worked together. Benny dug a little hole, Pip gathered soft leaves to make a tiny cup, and Tilly carefully poured water into it. "Here you go, Sunny Blossom!" they said together.

The flower's petals opened wide and smiled at them. "Thank you, my friends!" Sunny Blossom said. From that day on, Benny and his friends visited Sunny Blossom every day to make sure it stayed happy and healthy. And that's how they learned that sharing and helping make the world a little brighter!
