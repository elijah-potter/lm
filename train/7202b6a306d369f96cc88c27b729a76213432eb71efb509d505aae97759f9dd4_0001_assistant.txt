Hybrid animals are the offspring of parents of different species or subspecies. In nature, they are less common than purebred animals. Here are some examples:

1. Zebroid (A zebroid is a cross between a zebra and a horse.)
2. Liger (A liger is a cross between a female tiger and a male lion.)
3. Wholphin (A wholphin is a cross between a female bottlenose dolphin and a male false killer whale.)
