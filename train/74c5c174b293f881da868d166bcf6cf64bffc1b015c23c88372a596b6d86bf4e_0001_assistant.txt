The National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) has three divisions:
- Division 1 is the most competitive, as well as the most covered by media.
- Division 2 is less competitive, but still provides a lot of professional athletes. 
- Division 3 is also less competitive, but still offers a lot of great athletes a chance to play the sport they love while getting their education.

There is also the NAIA, which is separate from the NCAA altogether. They offer 4 years of eligibility. 
There are also Junior Colleges, or Community Colleges, that offer 2 years of college sports.
