Purposely living is learning what you are passionate about and sharing these positive values and goals with the world.  

Living with purpose can promote better physical and mental health and increased happiness. It assists with better sleep and can create stronger relationships. Healthy aging is also linked to living with purpose. 

Ways to live with purpose: 

* Design your life around things that matter most to you.
* Trust yourself.
* Focus on gratefulness.
* Be present in the current moment. 

Here are three quotes:

"The heart of human excellence often begins to beat when you discover a pursuit that absorbs you, frees you, challenges you, or gives you a sense of meaning, joy, or passion." - Terry Orlick

"The best way to lengthen out our days is to walk steadily and with a purpose." - Charles Dickens

"It's not enough to have lived. We should be determined to live for something." - Winston Churchill
