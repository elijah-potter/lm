I'm here - but not because I want to be.
They call it "a night to remember,"
but couples are all that I see.

Sure, there's the odd group of friends here or there,
but everyone looks nice,
and has done up their hair.

I'm a bit of a wallflower,
there's no dance to be had,
I just put on this fancy outfit
to appease Mom and Dad.

If I had it my way,
I'd have come with a date,
as an after-event
for a nice dinner we ate.

Because I'm all alone,
I won't have any fun,
let's get this thing over,
I just want it to be done.
