Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a tiny mouse named Milo. One sunny morning, Milo found a shiny blueberry in his garden. He was so happy that he started to eat it, but then he heard a tiny *click* sound. Milo looked down and saw a tiny door leading to a secret tunnel under his house.

Curious, Milo tiptoed inside. The tunnel was warm and smelled like cookies. He saw a little table with a plate of crumbs and a cup of milk. Milo felt happy because he knew someone had left food for him. He sat down and munched on a crumb, happy and cozy.

Suddenly, a little voice said, "Hello, Milo!" It was a bunny named Binky. "I was just passing by and saw your berry. Want to play a game?" Binky asked.

Milo nodded excitedly. Binky showed him how to hop like a bunny and then they chased each other through tunnels and over grass. It was the best playtime ever!

After a while, Milo saw a big, soft blanket on the table. He curled up with Binky and fell asleep, dreaming of berries and fun adventures. The next morning, they both woke up, full of sunshine and smiles, ready to explore their tiny world again.

Milo and Binky became the best of friends, and their secret tunnel became the happiest place in the whole garden.
