**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, Ill. – August 14, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, promising a vibrant celebration of agriculture, community, and family fun. This year’s fair will showcase livestock competitions, demonstrations of traditional crafts, and a diverse array of food vendors, alongside amusement rides and entertainment for all ages. The fairgrounds will be transformed into a bustling hub of activity, highlighting the rich agricultural heritage of Sangamon County.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 22nd through August 26th, 2024. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM. Special events are scheduled throughout the week, including a tractor pull, a demolition derby, and a fireworks display on Friday evening. Detailed schedules of events will be available on the fair’s website and at the information booth upon arrival.

Admission to the Sangamon County Fair is $12 for adults, $8 for children ages 6-12, and free for children five and under. Season passes are available for $40 and offer unlimited access to the fairgrounds throughout the week. Tickets can be purchased online at SangamonCountyFair.org or at the gate. Parking is $10 per vehicle.

**Headline Events:**

*   Livestock Auction
*   Demolition Derby
*   Tractor Pull
*   4-H Showcase
*   Blue Ribbon Baking Competition
*   Friday Night Fireworks
