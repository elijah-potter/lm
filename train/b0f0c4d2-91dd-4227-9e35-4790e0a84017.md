The lights flickered in the room like a dying breath,
each flicker a whisper of what might have been.
I stand here, half-hearted, half-alive—
a ghost in the machine, a voice that never quite belongs.

The pen trembles on the page, my fingers
twisting like roots in damp earth.
Every word is a lie, every line a shadow of the truth I don’t dare speak aloud.

Outside, the wind howls through the cracks in the window,
carrying away the words I never wrote.
I am the echo of a decision I never made.
