Here are some fun ideas for a slow-pitch softball league team name based on the fact that you all work in an office:

1. Weakened Warriors. This name uses alliteration and the homophones 'weekend' and 'weakened' to create a fun and catchy team name that plays on the fact that you all work in an office during the week, which may lessen your ability to compete in a physical sport on your days off. 
2. Softball Smarties. This name combines the sport you are competing in with the fact that your office job is more mentally than physically stimulating in order to create the image that you are equally intelligent when it comes to playing softball as you are in your office jobs.
3. Briefcase Bashers. Referencing the common practice of taking a briefcase to the office as well as the ability to bash a home run on the softball field, this name lets your opponents know that you are a force to be reckoned with no matter the setting.
4. The Pencil Pushers. This name pokes fun at the negative perception that office workers spend the bulk of their time performing tedious and repetitive work, reclaiming the title as a point of pride for your team.
