I've been to that park before! Wow, I have so many fond memories of that park. It's the MacArthur Park on Queen Street.

About three years ago, I decided to take my little pupper, Eddie, to MacArthur Park for exercise and relaxation. I was impressed by the amount of grass and field that Eddie could play on; it was amazing! There were water fountains in multiple areas of the park that the other dogs could drink out of; Eddie was thankful for that because he had been running around for so long that he frequently became thirsty. Also, in some areas of the park, some vendors served two different kinds of foods: meals for dog owners who wanted a quick bite to eat while they watched their dogs and snacks for the dogs themselves. The prices for these foods were fantastic value: lots of food for a great price!

I appreciated how well-kept the grass was, as it seemed very well-manicured and free from dog poop. It seems all dog owners who come to the park are well-informed of the requirement that they pick up after their dogs, and it showed in the cleanliness of the park itself. In addition, there were many park benches with shade above them for owners who wanted to take a break from the festivities.

I remember on the day that Eddie and I went to the park, there was a live band playing in the center of the park, and there were people who brought blankets, sleeping mats, and lawn chairs for themselves and their dogs to sit on while they listened to the music. It was a wonderful day for Eddie and me, and I'll never forget it.

Overall, I would recommend anyone with a dog who is looking for a safe, friendly, comfortable place to relax while giving your dog a treat and a day out. Please consider going to MacArthur Park. It is one of, if not the, best parks in this area.
