A storm raged over the crumbling farmhouse as **Mira** stood at the window, fingers gripping the cold glass. The wind howled through the broken panes, rattling the shutters. Outside, the field beyond was a sea of darkness, illuminated only by the eerie glow of a half-burnt lantern on the porch. She exhaled sharply, her breath fogging the glass.

*"He’ll be here,"* she whispered, though she knew the words were a lie. The promise she’d made—*"I’ll protect you"*—had been a hollow threat the night before. Now, the storm was the judge. The barn door creaked open behind her, and the first raindrops hit the roof in a relentless rhythm. From the darkness, a figure emerged, wrapped in a soaked cloak. **Elias**, her brother, stood silhouetted against the lightning-streaked sky, his face gaunt with exhaustion.

*"You lied,"* he said, his voice raw. *"You said you’d keep me safe, but you ran."*

Mira’s hands trembled. The lantern flickered, casting long shadows across the room. She reached for the revolver on the nightstand—her father’s last gift to her—and steadied her aim. The storm’s fury was the only witness left. The choice was written in the lightning.
