- Crash Course: Crash Course teaches children subjects such as history, biology, chemistry, literature, psychology, physics, and media literacy. This channel is best for older children. 

- Netflix Jr.: Netflix Jr. is a good channel for children from Pre-K to second grade and may also appeal to third and fourth graders. - National Geographic Kids: National Geographic Kids is great for children in kindergarten through fifth grade. It contains engaging, fun, often wacky videos that will teach your child about venomous animals, oceans, deserts, outer space, or about try experiments involving baking soda. It also encourages imagination expression and discovery through play. 

- SciShow Kids: SciShow Kids is best for children in kindergarten through fourth grade. It features the host Jessi and her companion Squeaks. This channel is designed to help teach children about science in a fun engaging way, 

- Mathantics: Mathantics is best for children around fourth grade all the way through middle school. It is both a YouTube channel as well as a supplemental math website designed to help kids with their mathematics. It includes subjects such as fractions, percentages, probabilities, geometry, algebra, statistics, and more.
