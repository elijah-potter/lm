My beautiful cheese, you inspire me to write.
How I love the way you slice, melt, and cube,
Invading my mind day and through the night,
While watching recipes on YouTube.

Let me compare thee to a spring day.
You are more perfect, attractive, and cold.
Grand sun heats the active peaches of May,
And crackers pair perfectly with flavors bold.

How do I love you? Let me count the ways.
I love your mellow rich taste and color.
Thinking of your smooth texture fills my days.
My love for you is the cunning caller.

Now I must away with a pleasant heart,
Remember my words whilst we're apart.
