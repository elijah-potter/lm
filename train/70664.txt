Creating a Menswear Fashion Illustration Inspired by Visual Arts
In this lesson, you’ll learn how to create a Menswear Fashion Illustration that is inspired by Visual Art.
This advanced lesson will teach you how to create an illustration using a pencil, gouache, brushes, and markers. You’ll learn how to draw and paint eyewear, create hair textures, and how to use your inspirational images to make a design within your illustration. You’ll also learn about an online resource, that could be used to help you find inspiration and to spark your creativity while drawing.
