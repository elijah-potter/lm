Neighbors reported a mysterious figure seen walking the alleys at night, described as a "hollow-eyed wanderer" by some. Police dismissed it as urban legend, but local residents found a discarded handkerchief with a faded name—**"Elias Carter"**—near the old mill pond. Later, a retired teacher from the area mentioned her late husband had once vanished during a storm. The next day, a local historian uncovered Elias’s 1923 obituary, noting he was last seen near the pond before vanishing. The community formed a vigil, and on the anniversary of the disappearance, the pond’s water turned murky. When tested, traces of Elias’s old fishing rod and a rusted pocket watch were found in the sediment. The town’s old ledger confirmed Elias had left a will, but his family had never heard of it. Now, every autumn, a single lantern floats on the pond’s surface for a night before vanishing—rumored to be Elias’s ghost, returning to claim what was his.
