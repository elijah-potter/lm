A quiet afternoon in an office where the hum of monitors and the faint murmur of distant conversations filled the air. The employee stood at her desk, fingers tracing the edge of her coffee cup, eyes closed as she paused. A decision to move to a different team, one that required less routine and more problem-solving, had been weighing on her mind for weeks. The hesitation wasn’t about the unknown—it was about the weight of what she’d already given.

She opened her eyes, leaned back in her chair, and typed a single line into Slack: *"Team swap request—open to feedback."* The message sent, she exhaled slowly, letting the silence around her become her ally. The weight lifted slightly, replaced by the anticipation of the next step, whatever it might be. The choice had been made, not in a grand gesture, but in the quiet space between thoughts, between the need and the pause.
