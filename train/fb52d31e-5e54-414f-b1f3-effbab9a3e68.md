**The List of Things I’ve Forgotten**

1. How to make a perfect cup of coffee without burning the roast.
2. The exact phrase my mother used to say when I was late.
3. The name of the first person who laughed at my joke.
4. Where I left my wallet—again.
5. The address of the bookstore where I once loved to read.
6. The secret I told that one friend who never asked for it.
7. The way the old theater smelled before the curtains rose.
8. The color of my eyes when I was a child.

**The Confession**

I’ve been lying about the time I was *never* late. About how I’ve never forgotten a single thing. The truth? I’ve kept this list in my head for years, and the truth is, I’ve never been able to finish it. Some days, I think I’m getting closer. Other days, I wonder if I’m just pretending.
