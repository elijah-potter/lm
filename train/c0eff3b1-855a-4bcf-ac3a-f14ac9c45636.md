The protagonist hesitated too long before committing, the weight of hesitation pressing down like an unseen hand. The opponent’s gaze flicked toward their shoulder, a fleeting spark of recognition—*they’d seen this move before, or rather, the hesitation that led to it.* A single, deliberate step forward, and the blade of the opponent’s katana found the wrist before the arm could fully extend. The scream was muffled, swallowed by the sudden silence of the alley, but the sound of flesh tearing was unmistakable.

The next move—*too soon*—was already forgotten in the blur of blood and dust. The blade’s edge grazed the air, a ghost of a strike that never came. The opponent’s hand twitched, fingers curling into a fist, but the momentum of the first attack had already sent them stumbling backward, their balance shattered. The protagonist stood frozen, a weight of regret and the ghost of a mistake settling over them like a shroud.
