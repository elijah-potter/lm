A quiet suburban street at dusk, where a single lamppost casts long shadows over a sidewalk lined with identical, weathered mailboxes. One of them, slightly ajar, reveals a folded newspaper with a headline in bold, uneven letters: *"THE GRAVITY FALLS."* Inside, a single photograph—blurred but unmistakably a person’s face—peeks out from beneath the fold, their lips moving silently. A child’s small handprint, smudged with ink, clings to the bottom of the box. The child’s voice, muffled, echoes from a hidden speaker inside the box: *"It’s still here."* The wind picks up, and the lamppost’s light flickers, revealing a single, smooth pebble rolling slowly down the street, unnaturally heavy, toward the nearest window.
