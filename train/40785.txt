One of the most important technological innovations in history, the automobile exemplifies the intersection of art, society, and science. Cars serve as excellent demonstrations of such scientific principles as the physics governing motion and the chemistry of propulsion. Since automotive designs are both an influence on and influenced by advances in all of these fields, exploring the science of cars is useful in understanding science and engineering in general. In this youth workshop (ages 9-14), youngsters will examine car science. Registration is limited and tickets are non-refundable. Workshop ticket includes one child museum general admission ticket.
