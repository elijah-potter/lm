There is no question that felines are superior to canines. I can summarize five reasons why this is a factual statement.

1. Cats are small:  We are content with a minuscule, warm area to rest our weary heads.
2. Cats are affordable: We do not destroy our toys and our adoption fees are often much more reasonable.
3. Cats prefer the indoors: Felines do not require long, boring walks and are perfectly content gazing out the window as opposed to being out there.
4. Cats are less work: We do not need to be trained to use the outdoors to urinate or defecate and we groom ourselves.
5. Cats provide pest control: Felines do not tolerate the invasion of pests such as mice or insects and therefore will eliminate them before they become a nuisance.

In summation, a cat is a superior creature to a dog. Anyone who thinks otherwise is likely a canine.
