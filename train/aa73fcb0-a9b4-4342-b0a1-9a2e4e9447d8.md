The void hums with an eerie silence, a vast emptiness stretching endlessly in all directions. Shadows stretch unnaturally long across the floor, defying perspective. The walls, smooth and featureless, seem to curve and warp in places, forming impossible angles. Objects cast no shadows, and the air feels still, thick with the absence of movement. Above, the ceiling vanishes into darkness, a void where stars might be lost in the infinite. The absence of boundaries makes the space feel alive with potential—both terrifying and exhilarating. Every corner seems to hold an unseen depth, and the silence itself breathes.
