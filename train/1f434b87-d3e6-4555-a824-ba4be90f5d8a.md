A small, dimly lit church basement in a quiet suburban neighborhood became the venue for a raucous **"Midnight Mashup"** celebration. Guests—mostly college students and a few eccentric locals—dressed in mismatched outfits: a vintage disco queen in a sequined jumpsuit paired with a leather jacket, a punk rocker in a velvet suit, and a cyberpunk aesthetic with neon-lit goggles.

The event combined elements of **retro raves, gothic weddings, and underground hacker conventions**. DJs played a mix of 80s synthwave, death metal riffs, and ambient electronic beats, while attendees danced on a makeshift dance floor covered in blacklight-reactive paint. Food was served on a buffet of **gluten-free vegan tacos, black market synthwave snacks (glitter-covered snacks), and a "mystery meat" bar** (actually lab-grown samples).

A central attraction was the **"AI Oracle"**—a repurposed smart speaker rigged to answer cryptic questions about the future, often returning nonsensical or darkly humorous responses. The energy was chaotic, with guests laughing, arguing, and spontaneously forming trios to perform improvised skits.

The celebration ended abruptly when police arrived, citing a noise complaint—though no one was arrested. The attendees simply vanished, leaving behind a single, half-burned **LED-lit circuit board** as a mysterious clue.
