**Common Complaints & Responses**

**1. "Why does my device take so long to start up?"**
- Hardware: Ensure proper RAM/SSD health. Defragmentation (for HDDs) and firmware updates may help.
- Software: Disable startup apps via Task Manager (Windows) or System Preferences (macOS). Boot into Safe Mode to test.
- Overclocking: Disable unnecessary background processes or settings.

---

**2. "My app crashes every time I open it."**
- Check for updates. Disable hardware acceleration if issues persist.
- Run app in compatibility mode (Windows) or verify permissions (macOS/Linux).
- Clear cache: Delete temporary files in app’s cache directory.

---
**3. "My internet is slow and keeps disconnecting."**
- Restart router/modem. Move closer to Wi-Fi source or switch to wired connection.
- Update router firmware. Check for interference (microwaves, other devices).
- Run speed test to confirm ISP limitations.

---
**4. "Why does my printer print blank pages?"**
- Check ink/toner levels and replace if empty. Clean print heads (for inkjets) via printer software.
- Ensure correct paper size/cutoff settings. Test with blank paper or a blank page template.

---
**5. "My phone keeps freezing."**
- Force-restart: Hold power + volume down (Android) or hold power button (iOS).
- Update OS and apps. Clear app cache (Settings > Apps > Storage > Clear Cache).
- Check for overheating (close unnecessary apps; avoid direct sunlight).

---
**6. "I can’t access my cloud files."**
- Verify internet connection. Check storage space (some services limit uploads).
- Log out/in or refresh browser. Try a different device/network.
- Contact support if issue persists; check for service outages (e.g., Google Drive).

---
**7. "My camera won’t turn on."**
- Ensure lens is clear of debris. Check camera settings (e.g., focus mode, flash).
- Update device drivers. Test with another camera or USB connection.
- Reset camera settings (factory reset if needed).

---
**8. "My music won’t play on repeat."**
- Check if "Repeat" is toggled off in player settings. Re-sync playlists if corrupted.
- Delete and re-download the track. Ensure device is charging/fully powered.
- Update media player software.

---
**9. "Why does my app require admin rights?"**
- Admin rights are needed for system-level changes (e.g., device drivers, network policies).
- Ask your system admin to grant permissions if you lack access. Avoid unauthorized changes.

---
**10. "My video cuts out mid-playback."**
- Check audio/video sync settings in player. Disable hardware acceleration if issues arise.
- Lower video quality or switch to 1080p. Close background apps consuming resources.
- Update drivers for GPU/CPU. Test on a different device.
