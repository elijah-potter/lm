A security guard noticed the faint, unnatural humming of the old fluorescent fixture in the abandoned warehouse’s hallway, a sound that should have flickered out years ago. The humming pulsed in sync with the way the shadows on the flickering wallpaper shifted—like a living thing—when no one was looking. The guard had spent decades memorizing the warehouse’s quirks, and this was the first time he’d paused to listen, half-expecting to hear the same sound from the ghost of a janitor who’d vanished decades before. He reached for his radio, but the humming stopped abruptly when he stepped into the dark corridor. The moment the hum faded, the door behind him—locked for decades—swung open on its own.
