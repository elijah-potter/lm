During 2022, excessive demand for flights and the recent lack of resources to supply them resulted in nightmarish conditions for the industry, especially for workers in understaffed environments. Despite the lacking conditions, airlines still sold massive amounts of tickets, causing numerous delays that outweighed those of several years prior. Throughout the year, complications in weather and other factors caused the form of travel to be more expensive, less reliable, and in the wintertime to come to a complete halt in some cases.
