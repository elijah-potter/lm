Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around the garden, sniffing flowers and chasing butterflies.

One sunny morning, Benny spotted a shiny red berry. He nibbled it and felt happy, but then he heard a tiny squeak. Curious, Benny hopped closer and found a lost baby bird sitting on the ground. The bird had a broken wing.

Benny knew just what to do. He picked up the baby bird gently and carried it to his den under the bushes. Benny cleaned the bird's wing and sang soft songs to help it feel better. Every day, Benny checked on the bird and made sure it had enough food and water.

After a week, the bird's wing was strong again! It flapped its wings and flew away happily, saying, "Thank you, Benny!" Benny smiled, knowing he had done the right thing.

From that day on, Benny hopped around with a big heart, always ready to help others. And every time he saw a lost little friend, he remembered his own kind heart and did what was right.
