**Year 1998**
- Founding of Acme Corp (CEO: Harold O. Zim)
- Acme acquires rival firm MicroTech in a $1.2B deal

**Year 2000**
- Launch of "Project Phoenix" (classified)
- Employee whistleblower (John Doe) reports suspicious data deletion

**Year 2001**
- Acme expands to 12 new offices
- Project Phoenix officially canceled (no public explanation)

**Year 2002**
- Acme CEO Harold O. Zim disappears without a trace

**Year 2004**
- Acme repurposes "Project Phoenix" as "SecureLink"
- No new whistleblower reports

**Year 2005**
- Acme announces 25% stock buyback, no financial justification provided

**Year 2006**
- MicroTech reacquired by Acme at half original price
- "SecureLink" rebranded as "QuantumSafe"

**Year 2012**
- Acme reports first loss in 20 years: $3.8B
- CEO: Michael Chen (new hire, no prior Acme experience)

**Year 2018**
- Acme files for bankruptcy
- Last known employee files FOIA request for Project Phoenix docs

**Suspicious Gap: 2002–2004**
- No public records of Project Phoenix’s activities
- No independent audits or third-party reviews
- Harold O. Zim’s sudden disappearance aligns with "Project Phoenix" termination.
