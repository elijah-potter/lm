**The Letter**

She had written it in the rain, her pen trembling against the paper like a child’s. *They will not come*, she had scrawled, *they never do*. The ink had blurred at the edges, as if the words themselves were dissolving.

But the letter was found folded inside the pocket of an old coat, half-drowned in the back pocket of a bicycle frame. The postmark read *Hastings*, though no one there remembered the name. The handwriting—her own—was neat, deliberate, but the final sentence, the one she’d crossed out, had been smudged like a ghost’s whisper: *"They did come."*
