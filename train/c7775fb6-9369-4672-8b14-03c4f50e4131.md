Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the garden. Benny had a bright red nose and big, round ears that flopped when he ran. One sunny morning, Benny spotted a shiny green leaf near a big, fluffy mushroom. "Oh, Benny," said Benny, "that leaf looks so special!"

Benny picked the leaf and carried it home to his cozy burrow. His mommy bunny was baking a yummy treat. While Benny waited, he noticed something strange—the leaf was changing color! It turned blue, then purple, and finally, it sparkled like a tiny star.

Benny giggled. "Mommy, look! The leaf is glowing!" His mommy smiled. "That’s magic, Benny! You made it shine." Benny munched on a berry and shared the leaf with his siblings. Soon, the whole garden sparkled with tiny, glowing leaves.

Benny’s favorite part? When the sun set, the leaves twinkled like fireflies, making the whole meadow sparkle. Benny knew he’d made the best friend in the world—his garden!

And from that day on, Benny always remembered to be kind, share, and look at the world with wonder.
