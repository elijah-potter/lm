Summer camp was something new for Trevor. He was scheduled to go to a one-week summer camp this year, and the idea made him nervous. What if he got there and didn't make any friends? What if the camp counselors were mean? Would he get poison ivy or mosquito bites and be miserable itching? What if he had trouble going to sleep?

Trevor's mom showed him pictures and things past campers said about the camp. The kids said they had a great time! They talked about how fun it was to hike, fish, do crafts, and go swimming in a lake. They also liked camp food and telling stories at night around a campfire. They said the camp counselors were very nice. And nobody had trouble with poison ivy or bugs!

Trevor's mom bought him a new duffel bag for his camp clothes. His dad helped him pack and then took him to the camp. He said he went to summer camp when he was Trevor's age and that he went back every year because he had so much fun.

When they got to camp, Trevor unrolled his sleeping bag and put down his duffel bag, and then everybody met at the mess hall for lunch. They had huge ham sandwiches, their choice of potato chips, a chocolate chip cookie -- Trevor's favorite -- and cold iced lemonade.

After lunch, they broke into groups and went on a hike. The counselor pointed out squirrels, a woodpecker, frogs, and some interesting bugs. They went back to camp and made a fan out of palmetto leaves to help keep them cool. After they washed up, it was time for a weenie roast around the campfire. The counselors told some stories, and then everyone went back to their bunks for bed. Trevor was so tired from being busy all day that he went to sleep as soon as his head hit the pillow. In the morning, he was so excited to see what they would do next that he almost jumped out of his bunk! By the end of the week, Trevor was saying what his dad said about camp: he had a great time and wanted to go back next year, too!
