Eleanor always politely but firmly declined these requests, explaining that she wanted to keep the cottage available for family gatherings and rentals to help cover the upkeep.

I also mentioned that I intended to continue renting it out to offset the expenses, a plan Eleanor had outlined to me years prior.

She argued that I didn't *need* the rental income and that my grandmother would have wanted her to have access. I tried to explain that Eleanor’s wishes were complex and that I was trying to honor her legacy, but Sarah dismissed my concerns.

I’m also putting a small amount into a savings account specifically for larger repairs, as the cottage is getting older.

I was on the phone with the rental agency at the time, confirming a booking for a family from Ohio for the following week. My father thinks I'm justified in protecting the property as Eleanor intended. My husband thinks I’m being reasonable. Honestly, I feel like I’m being pulled in a million directions. I’m trying to balance honoring my grandmother’s wishes, maintaining the property, and dealing with my sister’s increasingly unreasonable demands.
