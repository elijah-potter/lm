**The Last Light**

They say the old man’s house stood at the edge of the world,
where the river whispered secrets to the willows.
I found him there last night, his breath shallow,
a lantern in his hand—flickering, dying.

He didn’t see me until the door creaked.
Then he turned, his face half-lit by the flame,
eyes wide with something older than fear—
something that had been waiting for him all along.

*"You shouldn’t have come,"* he rasped.
*"The light’s been burning too long."*

I left the lantern behind.
The river’s song is softer now.
