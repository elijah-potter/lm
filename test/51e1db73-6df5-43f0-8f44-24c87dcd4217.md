In the historic district of Eldermere, authorities report a rare incident of spontaneous combustion after a midnight street performance by the *Echoes of the Hollow* band. Witnesses describe a single candle flickering near a discarded vinyl record as the fireball erupted, engulfing the makeshift stage in seconds. The fire department confirmed the blaze was extinguished within five minutes, but three patrons, including a minor, were treated for smoke inhalation. Local historians speculate the fire may be tied to an old legend about the "Hollow’s Last Laugh"—a cryptid said to manifest when forgotten melodies are played at midnight. No injuries were fatal, though a rare antique horn from the band’s set is now lost to the flames. Police are investigating whether the fire was accidental or if supernatural elements were at play. Meanwhile, the city’s fire marshal has ordered a temporary ban on midnight performances in the area.
