A safari park, also sometimes known as a wildlife park, is typically a drive-up tourist park where people drive through to view large game wildlife up close. Typically these types of parks are larger than a zoo but smaller than a wildlife reserve. These parks typically contain a large variety of animals including, but not limited to: lions, giraffes, rhinos, elephants, zebras, bison, black bears, gazelles, monkeys, llamas, and alpacas.

A good number of safari parks will also have a walk-around area to see some smaller animals as well. Since this is a tourist attraction, you will see other entertainment such s restaurants, carnival-style rides, golf courses, monorails, and gift shops.
