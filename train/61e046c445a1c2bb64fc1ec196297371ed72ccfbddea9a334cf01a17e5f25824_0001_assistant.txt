It must be exciting to get a new truck! Here are some name ideas for it: 
- Clifford - This is the name of a popular big red dog in an animated film, so it seems fitting for a big red truck as well. 
- Big Red - This name is very straightforward and particularly fitting if the truck is a large one. 
- Firetruck - Since firetrucks are also red, no one will be able to doubt which vehicle you are talking about with this name. 
- Blaze - Since red is also the color of fire, you could name your truck Blaze or some other name that is reminiscent of fire. 
I hope these help your family decide what to call it!
