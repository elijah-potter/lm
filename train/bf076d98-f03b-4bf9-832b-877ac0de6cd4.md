The dimly lit alleyway hummed with the distant clatter of a distant pub. Shadows stretched long across the cracked pavement, where a lone figure crouched behind a rusted dumpster, hands clutching a stolen wallet. The air smelled of damp concrete and old beer. The figure watched as a pair of city lights flickered past—one man, broad-shouldered, moving with purpose toward the entrance of an abandoned warehouse, another, shorter, with a limp, carrying a backpack. The first paused, glancing back at the alley, then stepped inside. The second hesitated, then followed, the sound of their boots echoing against the peeling paint. The figure stayed put, watching until the warehouse’s flickering neon sign vanished behind a cloud. Then they melted into the night.
