A sudden gust of wind sent a loose umbrella crashing to the ground, startling a man mid-step on the crowded sidewalk. His foot caught the edge of a parked bicycle, nearly sending him sprawling. His breath caught in his throat as he stumbled forward, hand instinctively reaching for his pocket, fingers brushing the cold metal of his wallet before he regained balance. Around him, passersby froze in place, their heads turning to watch as the man's eyes widened in shock, his jaw dropping open for just a split second before he recovered, wiping his damp palms down his jeans. The chaos of the moment—colliding energy, unexpected movement—left him momentarily disoriented, the world tilting just enough to make him question whether he’d just tripped or if someone had shoved him.
