Many years ago, the act of “becoming a professional” was a vague term for me, and made me anxious when adults introduced themselves to me as such. At times it was almost intimidating. I had always thought that a professional was just an average adult with above-average work performance. This definition used to be the prevailing meaning of the word “professional” for me before I joined The Extremely Proper Business Club last year. The Extremely Proper Business Club helps me get ready to become a professional by bringing in actual career professionals. These professionals, who range from corporate lawyers to hedge fund managers, are exemplary models of professionals in their fields. The club was the first to expose me to an unbiased picture of who a professional truly is and how that means much more than above-average work performance. This exposure to bleeding-edge professionals in their respective fields was a sobering reality. It continues to be a strong foundation for me as I prepare for my professional pursuits after graduation.

Another important aspect of The Extremely Proper Business Club is the opportunity provided to students for them to talk with these industry professionals and gain their insight. This opportunity has been an invaluable resource for me as I start to take charge of my career. The advice given by career professionals with decades of experience continues to serve as a guiding light as I try to navigate an unclear void of career progression with upcoming graduation on the horizon. No matter the form of engagement, ranging from casual conversation to formal presentation with PowerPoint slides, having career professionals elaborate on their successes or failures while reflecting on overall learnings has been an incredible catalyst in my personal career development. The engagement from those professionals has also been a source of motivation for someone like me who is just starting their professional career track. I am eager when a person asks about my membership in The Extremely Proper Business Club because it is a chance for me to explain how the activities that the club hosts, including bringing in professionals in their field, are one of the reasons why I feel prepared in my transformation from student to working professional.
