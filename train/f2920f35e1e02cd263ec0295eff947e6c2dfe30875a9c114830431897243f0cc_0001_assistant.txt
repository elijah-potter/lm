In the complicated and warm-hearted world of animated films, it’s safe to say Pixar reigns supreme. They’ve given us some of the most heartfelt and iconic stories of all time, and Toy Story and Finding Nemo are not to be excluded. Both movies are certainly beloved by fans, but at the end of the day, there isn’t an argument as to which film is better: Toy Story. 

This analysis will focus on everything from plot, characters, and even cinematography––everything that makes Toy Story really stand out. After all, it stood out in the animation industry for a good reason, so let’s dive in (no, not into the sea).

Toy Story captures Andy’s toys coming to life while he’s away. Woody, the protagonist, faces fierce competition when there’s a new toy in town: Buzz Lightyear. While there is some bitter rivalry at first, eventually, the two become friends. It teaches us how to adapt and unite as a community while capturing our attention with suspenseful escapes (Sid’s house, anyone?) and exciting action!

Basically, Finding Nemo is by no means bad, but it just feels weak when compared to such a masterpiece. To conclude, there are many reasons why Toy Story is way better than Finding Nemo, but the internet only allows for so many words on a webpage, so I’ll leave it at what I’ve said!
