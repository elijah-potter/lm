There once was a woman named Shirly. Shirly dreamed about becoming a pilot. She would play with planes all the time when she was younger. Her dad was also a pilot. Shirly would go on flights in their private plane all the time with her parents. It was one of the best feelings in the world for her. After talking to one of her friends, she made up her mind. She would become a pilot, no matter what. 

The next few years were rough for Shirly. Her dad quit being a pilot. He was just getting older and could not safely pilot a plane anymore. She was crushed. Shirly could not see the amazing sights with her family for a long time. Not until Shirly gets her pilot's license. The years passed and Shirly start working toward being a certified pilot. Day in and day out she goes to school and keeps studying. She gets into her father's airplane simulation setup. Shirly keeps training herself and telling herself that she can become a pilot. She keeps trying harder and harder until the day of her exam.

Shirly walked into the exam sweating and nervous. Invasive thoughts keep flying through her mind, blocking her from doing her best. She sits down in the cockpit. Shirly places her hands on the wheel and takes a deep breath. She remembers what her parents told her. They are proud of her, no matter what happens. Shirly closes her eyes before the exam proctor sits down and the exam starts. Shirly goes through the exam with ease. She knows where everything is, the safety procedures, and the simulation was perfect. The only thing left was the real thing.

She was ready. And a few hours after that, she was handed her results.  "You Passed!" was written in green in the middle. Shirly did it, she achieved her dream. The first thing she did was fly her parents over the sights that they took her to see so many years ago.
