The silence between the last light and the first dawn was thick with the weight of forgotten dreams—each breath a whisper carried on the cold morning air, muffled by the dust of centuries.

A single sparrow landed on the rooftop of the abandoned clocktower, its feathers ruffled by the ghost of a wind that had never truly existed. It did not chirp. It did not move. It simply sat, a silent sentinel, as if time itself had paused to watch the sunrise.

The city below slept, its streets slick with dew and secrets, every shadow a door left ajar. A streetlamp flickered to life, casting long, trembling fingers across the pavement—each beam a thread connecting the past to the present, though no one dared to follow.
