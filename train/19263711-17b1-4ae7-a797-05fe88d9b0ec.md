Once upon a time in a cozy green forest, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around and explore the sunny meadow.

One sunny morning, Benny saw a shiny red apple on a tree branch. "Mmm, that looks yummy!" Benny said, hopping closer. But before Benny could nibble it, a tiny mouse named Mimi scurried up. "Oops! That’s not for eating," Mimi said, giggling. "It’s a magic apple!"

Suddenly, Benny’s ears twitched. "What’s magic?" Benny asked. Mimi smiled. "It makes you grow bigger!" Benny’s eyes widened. "I want to grow bigger!" Benny jumped into the air and took a big bite.

Boing! Benny’s paws grew longer, his tail got bushier, and suddenly—he wasn’t a little bunny anymore! He was a big, fluffy bunny with a tail that wiggled like a kite!

Benny’s friends—Polly the squirrel, Timmy the turtle, and even old Grandpa Owl—all cheered. "Wow! Benny’s a super-bunny!" Polly squeaked.

But Benny felt a little nervous. "I don’t know what to do now," he said. Mimi patted his head. "You’re still Benny, just bigger. And magic apples don’t make you grow forever—just for a little while!"

So Benny hopped happily with his new friends, enjoying the big adventures without forgetting who he was. And as the sun set, Benny curled up in his favorite cozy patch of grass, feeling very happy and very, *very* special.

The next morning, Benny’s ears and tail shrank back to normal, and he was back to being his old self—just a little bunny with a big heart. "Magic is fun," Benny said, grinning. "But the best magic is being kind and brave!"

And that’s how Benny learned that even when things change, the heart stays the same. The end.
