The marriage of Prince Charles and Lady Diana, the Prince and Princess of Wales, took place on July 29, 1981. The event was a highpoint in the popularity of the Royal Family. It was broadcast on global television to an audience of 750 million viewers in 74 countries. In the UK, a public holiday was declared and 28.4 million Britans watched on ITV and BBC. 600,000 people also lined the streets of London during the ceremony.
