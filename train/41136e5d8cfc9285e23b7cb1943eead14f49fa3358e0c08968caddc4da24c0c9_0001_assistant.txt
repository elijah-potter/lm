In the futuristic city of Mandara, we follow the captivating journey of Remy, an intriguing robot working as a pizza deliveryman at the popular "Don and Moes" pizzeria. Mandara thrives with robots assigned specific jobs at birth by the local mayor, and unfortunately for Remy, he despises his role as a deliveryman. While Remy loathes his job, he finds solace in the opportunity to explore the atmosphere of Mandara. As he ventures through the city's streets to make his pizza deliveries, Remy discovers a community known as the "Riders." The riders are renowned for their want to break free from the working atmosphere, but law enforcement has something to say. 

Inspired by the Riders, Remy takes on a mission to find a way to escape the everlasting dread of his work. He seeks guidance from the Riders, who become his mentors and allies in his quest for freedom. Together, they seek daring plans and strategies to challenge the governmental laws that restrict their choices and limit their potential. The cartoon represents individuality, freedom, and the pursuit of a meaningful existence. Will Remy and the Riders find their destiny?
