In a dimly lit café one evening, the air thick with the scent of espresso, a stranger slid into the booth next to me. Their fingers traced the rim of their coffee cup as they murmured, *"You ever think about what happens when you let go?"*

I laughed, but their eyes lingered—sharp, unreadable. *"Just a coffee break, right?"* I tossed over my shoulder, though my mind had already drifted to the half-finished novel gathering dust on my desk.

They didn’t answer. Just the clink of their cup against the saucer. The promise hung between us like a ghost—unspoken, but already heavy.
