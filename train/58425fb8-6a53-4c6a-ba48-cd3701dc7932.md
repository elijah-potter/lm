The library was quiet except for the hum of the old ceiling fan, its blades spinning lazily against the dust motes caught in the afternoon light. Outside, the last rays of the sun painted the park in gold, where a single lizard sunbathed atop a weathered bench, its tail flickering like a warning.

Inside, the old bookcase groaned under the weight of centuries, its shelves lined with dust-covered tomes. Among them, a yellowed copy of *The Odyssey* lay half-open, its pages whispering secrets to anyone who listened. The scent of aged paper mingled with the faint, sweet perfume of the rare book conservator who had once tended to this collection.

Then, there was the sound—a small, rhythmic *click*, like a coin being dropped into a jar. A single acorn, polished smooth by time, rolled across the wooden floor and landed in the cracked base of a brass candlestick. The light from the lamp flickered over its golden surface, catching the first hint of a glint in its shell.
