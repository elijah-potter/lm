It seems like my days are constantly filled with situations that test my patience as if the universe were trying to tell me to change the way I live.

I got stuck behind numerous drivers on the road who were driving either too slow or too erratically; it felt like these drivers were in control over my ability to freely drive around. The same feeling comes up when I'm in a line of cars and I just happen to be the one who gets caught at the red light after multiple cars went through the green light right before me. I feel like the universe is constantly putting obstacles in front of me as if it were trying to tell me I am not allowed to move around freely.

And this theme seems to be popping up a lot in my life. I feel like maybe this is a mental issue, rather than something that is happening outside of me. Maybe this is just a flaw in my thinking and I should learn to stand unphased by the situations that occur in my life. 

But if these situations happen for a reason other than to teach me to be patient, what is the purpose? What is the purpose of my life, and what direction is it supposed to go in? If I knew what the purpose was and the destination I was supposed to go to, I think life would be much easier to live. However, it feels like my life is a constant series of puzzles that I need to solve just to get through the day. I don't know how to turn off that analytical part of my brain, but maybe I am not meant to turn it off; maybe it serves a vital function in my life, such as helping me to solve the riddle that is my life.

Or, maybe I just need to learn to chill out. I do feel too high-strung sometimes, and maybe life isn't supposed to be so serious.
