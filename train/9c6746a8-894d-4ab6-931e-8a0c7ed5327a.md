A corporate boardroom at 4:17 PM, fluorescent lights casting long shadows across the table.

**CEO (leaning in, hands clasped):** *"We agreed to cut R&D by 15%. The numbers don’t lie—we’re bleeding cash."*

**CTO (typing furiously on a tablet):** *"Same. The market’s shifting; we need to pivot. Innovation costs are unsustainable."*

**Finance Lead (calculating on a spreadsheet):** *"Agreed. The debt service is crushing us. We need to free up liquidity."*

**Marketing Director (nodding, clipboard in hand):** *"Yep. Our customers are shifting to subscription models. We can’t afford to lose them."*

**HR Manager (watching the clock):** *"I hear you. Layoffs are inevitable. We’re already seeing attrition creep up."*

**Intern (whispers to neighbor):** *"Wait… but what about the team’s morale?"*
