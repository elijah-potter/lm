When it comes to excellent image quality, the type of gear you choose plays an important role. Here are some suggestions for full-sized tripods used by professionals in the photography industry. 

1. TVC-33 Versa Series 3 Mk2 Carbon Fiber Tripod: The Really Right Stuff brand has created what most users claim to be the Rolls Royce of tripods with this model. The TVC-33 Versa Series 3 Mk2 is a carbon fiber tripod that features a stylish design and a diamond-pattern carbon veneer. It also includes twist-locks and rubber ball feet. 

This tripod has a unique ratcheting system for the leg angle that locks in place, allowing the user to keep their hands free of pinching once they pull out the stop. The metal parts are also made from a CNC milling process that uses a solid chunk of metal for construction, not a mold, to ensure optimal strength and durability. 

2. The GT2532 Mountaineer Series 2 Carbon Fiber Tripod Model GH1382QU: The Gitzo Mountaineer Series 2 tripod features eight-layer carbon eXact legs that have the brand's signature veneer finish. The carbon portion of the tripod's center tube can easily be removed if needed, leaving you with a shortened center tube for lower-angle shots at a minimum height of 6.3".

When the legs of this tripod are fully extended, the legs and ball head can reach 69.8", and support as much as 31 lbs. The chassis of this tripod is made from magnesium and is powder-coated, giving it that signature Gitzo appearance. 

3. Robus Vantage Series 3 RC-5570 Carbon Fiber Tripod: The Robus Vantage Series tripod is designed to be a fierce competitor for industry leaders. The brand features a series of rugged and heavy-duty tripods designed with professional photographers in mind. It features grippy rubber grips with no foam to worry about deteriorating. It also has an interchangeable top-plate system that allows for customizable flexibility and three options for foot pads or spikes.
