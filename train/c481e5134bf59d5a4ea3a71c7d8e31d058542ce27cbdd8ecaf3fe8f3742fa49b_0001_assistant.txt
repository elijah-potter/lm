For the long-haul truck driver, the semi-tractor is a home away from home. As such, it should be comfortable and cozy, which means that it also needs to be well-organized to store the necessities and the little extras that give a big rig the home touch. There are a number of ways to achieve this.

Organization comes in many forms, but you'll be sure to find the following items invaluable:
* Command hooks: They are removable and can be relocated to a position that works best in the cab; they're useful for hanging caps, belts, and towels to dry.
* Over-the-door shoe storage: The pockets are perfect for toiletries, spare water bottles or soda cans, and all types of snack storage.
* Plastic storage containers: These take the place of your house bedroom's dresser drawers. You can also get appropriately sized containers for extra supplies, such as paper and pens.
* Shower caddies: Not only can these be used for grabbing quickly to take to the showers, but they can hold your eating utensils, spices, and a variety of kitchen items.
* Spring-tension curtain or shower curtain rods: There is usually some unused vertical space in a semi, so these can create extra space for hanging clothes and jackets, or you can even use them to hang a curtain to hide what's being stored on an unused upper bunk.
* Back-of-the-seat organizers: You really can't go wrong with extra pockets everywhere!
* Sticker labels: Be sure to label everything so that you're not constantly searching for what you need.

Be sure that you're taking a piece of home with you on the road. A cell phone and a laptop are musts. Use comfortable bedding, and even invest in a good mattress to ensure you stay well-rested. Pictures of your family are a nice touch, and many truckers even travel with a furry companion. It's up to the individual to decide what makes a "home away from home," but the good news is that it's entirely possible to spend time on the road with many of the comforts of home.
