I had high hopes for "How to Train a Box." I have so many boxes around my house that I felt like I could use some help getting them all in line. The book's instructions were too vague to do anything with. I wasted an entire day trying to follow its instructions to make my boxes do what I wanted them to. All they did was sit there and not listen to me like always. I'm not sure what the author of the book was trying to do with it. If I could go back in time and have never read this book, I would.
