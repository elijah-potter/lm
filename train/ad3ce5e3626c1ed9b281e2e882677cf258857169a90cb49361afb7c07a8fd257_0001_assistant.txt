There is so much talk about environmental consciousness these days, but I bet you never realized adopting some of the practices could save us a ton of money, a few dollars at a time.  Simply turning off lights, unplugging unused appliances and washing your clothes in cold water would save on the electric bill every month.  Turning off the water while brushing your teeth and when washing dishes will reduce the water bill.  Even a small act like recycling large bottles and boxes, frees up space in the trash can, saving on bags and trips to the dumpster.  Do you think the additional few minutes of work a day is a fair trade-off if it helps us reach our financial goals that much sooner?
