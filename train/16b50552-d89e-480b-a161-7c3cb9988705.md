A vast, sun-baked desert of shifting dunes as endless as the void of space, where the silence hums like a distant heartbeat. The air is thinner than the wisps of a dying flame, carrying dust that swallows the sky like ink in water. Rocks are jagged teeth of time, harder than steel and older than memory. The heat is a living thing, hungry and relentless, more insistent than a thousand thirsty mouths. Shadows stretch like serpents, curling around the edges of everything, never quite reaching the horizon. Every crevice in the earth holds secrets older than the first whisper of life, and the wind carries whispers that are nothing but static, just noise. It’s a place where the weight of nothingness feels like a physical force, pressing down from every direction.
