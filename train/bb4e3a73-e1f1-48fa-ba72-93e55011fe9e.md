Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the garden. One sunny morning, Benny heard a tiny voice whisper, *"Help me, please!"* coming from the flower bed.

Curious, Benny peeked inside and saw a sleepy little mouse named Milo, tangled in a bunch of colorful petals. "Oh dear," Benny thought. "I can help!" He gently untangled Milo's tiny paws and whispered, *"Don’t worry, Milo!"*

Milo blinked his big eyes. "Thank you, Benny! But can you find my lost button? It’s fallen into the big, big puddle!" Benny gasped. "The puddle?" he asked. "I don’t know about that one!" But Benny had a secret trick: his favorite jump rope! He tied one end to his leg and the other to the flower bed, then took a big hop. The rope wobbled and wobbled until—*whoosh!*—it reached the puddle!

With a quick twist, Benny pulled Milo’s button out! Milo’s eyes sparkled. "You’re a hero!" he cheered. Benny grinned. "Let’s share our garden treasure!" They picked the biggest, juiciest strawberries and shared them with all their animal friends.

From that day on, Benny and Milo became the best friends ever, hopping, playing, and helping each other every single day. And as the sun set, painting the sky pink and orange, Benny and Milo curled up together, dreaming of their next adventure under the stars.
