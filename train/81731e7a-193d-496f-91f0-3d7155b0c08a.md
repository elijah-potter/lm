The clock ticked steadily, each second a hollow echo in the vastness of the waiting room. The door creaked open, revealing the faint scent of old paper and polished wood. She stood there, her reflection in the glass not quite matching her—someone else’s eyes, a stranger’s smile. She moved forward, her feet dragging slightly, as if time itself had become a physical force pulling her back. The air was thick with anticipation, the kind that presses against the chest like a second skin. The moment stretched—forever.

The phone rang, a jarring interruption, but she didn’t look. Her gaze lingered on the empty space where her reflection had been, the way the light flickered across the cracks in the ceiling. The door shut behind her. The moment dissolved into a single breath, a heartbeat—nothing more.
