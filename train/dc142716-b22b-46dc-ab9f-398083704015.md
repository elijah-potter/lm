A missed opportunity where a strategic pivot or corrective action was delayed until it became ineffective due to:
1. **Time decay** (e.g., market trends shifted after a late decision).
2. **Resource exhaustion** (e.g., funds depleted before implementing a cost-cutting move).
3. **Competitive inertia** (e.g., rivals outmaneuvered competitors before response).
4. **Operational friction** (e.g., bureaucratic slowdowns prevented timely execution).
5. **External shocks** (e.g., unforeseen crises like supply chain disruptions).
**Consequence:** Lost competitive edge, eroded stakeholder trust, or irreversible damage to the organization’s position.
