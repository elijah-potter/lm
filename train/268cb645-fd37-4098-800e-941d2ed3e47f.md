**Scene 1 – The Library**
The scent of old paper and ink clings to Elias’s fingers as he flips through a bound ledger. Across the aisle, his younger sister, Mira, scribbles frantically on a notebook, her quill breaking against the parchment.

**Scene 2 – The Market**
Outside, a merchant’s cart sways under the weight of crates of dried herbs. A child, no older than Mira, clutches a rusted coin purse, eyes wide as a crow caws overhead.

**Scene 3 – The Storm**
A crack of lightning splits the sky. In a crumbling tower, an ancient scholar reads from a damaged tome, his shadow stretching like a ghost across the page. Below, a storm-wracked ship’s bell tolls in the distance.

**Scene 4 – The Mirror**
Elias’s reflection moves independently—his hand reaches for his own sleeve, but it’s Mira’s voice that whispers, *"The book won’t stay open."* The mirror splits into two: one shows a scholar’s robes, the other a child’s bare feet.

---
**Parallel Ending:**
The scholar’s notes dissolve into Mira’s notebook, while the child’s purse holds a single, glowing leaf. Elias finds himself in both places at once—one hand touching the ledger, the other holding the leaf. The storm’s bell fades into the library’s chandelier, now humming softly. The mirror reforms, showing Elias and Mira side by side, both holding the same book.
