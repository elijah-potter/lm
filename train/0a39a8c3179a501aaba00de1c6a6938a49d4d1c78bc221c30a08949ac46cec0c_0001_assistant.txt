Mayland Itani's newest ship Damocles has left its drydock and awaits passengers to take to their new homes on an earth-like world lightyears away from Earth. The trip will take two hundred years of travel time. For the duration, you and the rest of the travelers will be in cryogenic hibernation. There are inherent risks with space travel and planetary colonization including but not limited to:

- Catastrophic ship failure
- Space pirates
- Unknown hostile alien species
- Unknown planetary diseases
- Space madness

If this sounds like the new start you're looking for, reach out to your nearest Mayland Itani representative. Happy travels!
