Follow the path of the
Sand on the wind
To the place where horizons unfold
Past the crest of the dunes
Where the shadows take flight
And the oldest of stories are told
Around bonfires built by
The wisest of fools
To be shared with the strangest of kin
If they see you are lost
They will beckon you near
And a new family you’ll find therein
In the tales that they tell
You’ll be taken awing
To soar high over river and grass
To peer down into jungles
Dip your toes in the sea
And ascend a snow-tipped mountain pass
To befriend the Impala
And lion alike
To bear witness to death and rebirth
To discover within you 
The meaning of all
And the truth of what all may be worth
For loss can’t mean anything
To those born with naught
And gold’s nothing to the children of kings
But the beauty of life
Is found huddled 'round firesides
In the stories and songs that we sing
