Long ago in the kingdom of Astria, there lived a powerful grand wizard named Jace. Jace was known throughout the kingdom as the greatest spell caster that ever lived. Instead of using his power to fight or travel, he dedicated his life to teaching others who shared the passion and dedication to learn spellcasting as he did. Over the years, he trained countless apprentices, all from various backgrounds. No matter who or where they came from, Jace always treated them equally and with the same care they all deserved.

Over the years, he proved to be a tough yet patient teacher of the magic arts. He knew everyone was capable but everyone learned at their own pace, so he adjusted to his students well. One day, a young girl named Anna who had lost her family had shown up, determined to channel her sorrow into something that could benefit others. Jace happily took her under his wing, and she proved to be an exceptional student. Anna spent countless hours, excelling and surpassing every other apprentice that Jace had taught.

Eventually, as Jace was getting older over the next decade, he knew there was very little more to teach Anna. Anna was already becoming a far better wizard than Jace. He also was aware that his time was coming to an end. However, he felt an overwhelming joy in knowing his legacy would be passed on by his countless apprentices. Anna happily carried on, taking on her own students in hopes of providing the same joy and happiness that Jace provided her when she needed it most.
