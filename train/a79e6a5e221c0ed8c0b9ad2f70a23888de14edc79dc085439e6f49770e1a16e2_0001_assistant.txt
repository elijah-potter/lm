Everyone these days loves to emulate their favorite rock star, actor, or sports hero. We all have a favorite movie star that we love and it’s not uncommon to daydream of having a life like that person we admire so much. But trying to be just like someone else rarely ever works. We’re all so different. We come from a wide range of backgrounds. Each person’s education, ethnicity, social and financial history is unique.

One thing that Hollywood does well is to mask the fact that our favorite movie stars aren’t perfect. This is true in financial and business sectors, publishing, sports, entertainment, and every other industry. It’s hard to find a person who is completely successful in every part of their life. Even Warren Buffett, one of the richest and most influential men in the world, has not had a fully successful personal and married life. 

That most likely holds true with any successful person you can name. There are always one or two areas where they seem to fail miserably. Just because you become a successful business owner won’t necessarily mean that you’ll have great relationships with your kids. Each of those areas that are important to us must be worked on and developed over time.

A good strong relationship with your spouse doesn’t come easy. It takes a willingness to keep working on it day by day. This should be a priority in anyone’s plan to succeed though. No matter how much money and influence you achieve, it’s always going to be better if you have loved ones to share it with.

Don’t lose sight of these simple truths as you move forward. Keep your family high on your priority list. Make every effort to maintain good mental, physical, and emotional health for yourself. Every truly successful person understands the importance of maintaining balance throughout the various areas of their life.
