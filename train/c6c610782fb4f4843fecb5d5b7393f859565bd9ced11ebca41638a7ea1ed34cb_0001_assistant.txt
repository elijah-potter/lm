Gotcha. Try setting up one of these for your guests:

1. Build-your-own sushi. You provide the basics: rice and seaweed wraps. Afterward, guests can choose from fresh fish, sliced vegetables, spicy mayo, fish eggs, and other sauces and toppings. Roll it up with a sushi mat, cut the roll into slices, and enjoy!
2. Build-your-own ramen. You can do anything with ramen. Give your guests a bowl of hot, steaming noodles with savory broth, then let them add eggs, fish cakes, vegetables, sliced meat, and other ramen specialties.
3. Build-your-own egg rolls. Guests fill their dough with meat, vegetables, and seasonings. Afterward, you fry the egg rolls until they're nicely browned and crispy. Yum!
4. Build-your-own nigiri. This is like sushi but much simpler: your guests choose pieces of fresh fish and shrimp to drape over their rice. Look up Japanese omelets and other eclectic nigiri toppings.
