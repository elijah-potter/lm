The Statue of David is a Renaissance sculpture created by the Italian artist, Michelangelo, crafted in 1501, and finished in 1504, by a then 26-year-old artist. The masterpiece, which stands at a height of 5.17 meters, is currently housed in the Accademia Gallery of Florence and is a representation of the biblical figure David, which was a favored subject in the art of Florence. It came to symbolize the defense of civil liberties embodied in the Republic of Florence.
