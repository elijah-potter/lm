The story begins with a very lonely character by the name of Alex. She loves being alone and doesn't have any friends. She can't figure out why no one has ever stuck by her side. She is saddened by her lonely life, so she takes a trip to a humane animal shelter. She is a smart person who has researched dog training and housebreaking. She sees a puppy at the shelter and decides to adopt it. She names him Weylin.
Throughout the next few weeks, she begins training him, but he is a very defiant puppy. He doesn't seem to be a good listener and she can't get control of him. This begins to frustrate Alex. She doesn't think she will ever get a handle on training Weylin. She has been very forceful with her training. She is far too controlling and unable to focus on the task at hand because she is so angry that he won't listen to her. Something tells her that she hasn't done a good enough job bonding with Weylin. She is often busy with other things. She decides she will take more time to exercise Weylin and spend quality time having fun with him.
Over time, Alex bonds with Weylin, and the two learn to trust each other. With a newfound respect for the dog, Alex refocuses on training him. She is much less forceful and doesn't let her anger take control of her actions. She is surprised to see that Weylin has a newfound respect for her as well. He begins to learn and listen to her commands.
She realizes that she has been too controlling with everyone in her life, rather than focusing on enjoying life and having fun with them. Perhaps if she begins to let go of control and focus on enjoying life, she would not have alienated the people in her life. Alex comes away from this experience with a new point of view on life. She recognizes her flaws and is humbled by them. Not only has her relationship with Weylin improved, but she has also worked to improve her relationships with others.
