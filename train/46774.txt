Are Corns Causing you Pain?
Corns are mainly round areas of thickened hard skin that appear on your heels, ball of your foot, or on and between your toes.
Corns are often caused by abnormal pressure to your foot and it is your body's way of protecting that area.
Persistent treatment can help you to manage them or to get rid of them all together.
Types of Corn
Corns can be caused by poorly fitting shoes, the structure of your foot, or the way you walk even.
There are three main types of corn that commonly occur on your foot.
Hard Corns often feel like there is a stone pressing on your foot and are most uncomfortable when direct pressure is applied to them.
Soft Corns mainly occur between your toes and are often caused by your toes rubbing against each other and have a white and rubbery apperance due to the area between your toes being more moist.
Seed corns look like small seed shaped areas of hard skin and can often be found on your feet where the skin is dryer.
Persistent and regular treatment of your corns will help to stop them forming and causing you pain
