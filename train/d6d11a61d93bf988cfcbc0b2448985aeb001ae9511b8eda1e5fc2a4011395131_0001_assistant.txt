Sure! Here is a list of writing prompts to hopefully get you writing again:

1. Write a story about a calendar that marks off the time using people. In this tale, the roles of the calendar and people are reversed; people usually mark time off on a calendar, but not in your tale!
2. Write a poem about toes at a party. The toes may be detached or as part of feet; they may be at a ball or a disco dance - you choose the setting and how the scenes unfold!
3. Write two random lines with all the words spelled backward. Do not practice the words on paper first - invert them in your head and then write them down! Overcoming this challenge may help you dive back into your writing.
4. Write a sweet love note from a unicorn to an otter. Decide on where the two met and what the desired outcome is.

All the best with your writing!
