**ANNOUNCEMENT:** *The Last Transmission of Dr. Elias Voss – A Note from the Archives*

---

The hum of the old radio’s dial was a lullaby to the dying city. Outside, the neon signs flickered like dying stars, their blue glow bleeding through the storm clouds. Inside the abandoned studio, a single bulb cast long shadows on the cracked floorboards. Dr. Elias Voss had spent thirty years here, stitching together fragments of the past, the future, and the things that couldn’t be named. The tape recorder clicked once, then twice, its voice a ghost whispering through static.

*"Today, the final transmission is not for the living. It’s for those who will listen after the signal fades—those who remember the weight of silence before the first scream. The machine is broken, but the message remains. The files are encrypted with the same key that once unlocked the door to the next dimension. Find it before the next eclipse. The universe is watching."*

The tape paused. A single word, spoken in a voice that wasn’t his own: *"Run."*

The door creaked open. The wind howled through the broken windows, carrying the scent of ozone and something older—something that hadn’t been born yet. The last thing Voss saw was the screen of the console flickering to life, displaying a single, unreadable symbol: the mark of the thing that waited beyond the static.

*(End of Transmission.)*
