This study examined the impact of fountain size and noises in medium-sized city centers on local and native bug suicide rates. This paper aims to answer the question, "Do larger fountains (or fountains with louder than average noise levels) reduce native bug suicide rate?" Previous studies have shown that there is no correlation between fountains and bugs of any kind, but we opted to pursue this hypothesis anyway. We wanted to study this because our primary funder "had a hunch." The goal of this research was to put the topic to bed once and for all. We used a controlled, double-blind research study, the most accurate type of data that we would possibly collect, and conducted the study over six years where every other year we removed all fountains from the city, and then on even years, we would add fountains of varying sizes. In the end, we found no correlation between fountains and native bug suicide rates.
