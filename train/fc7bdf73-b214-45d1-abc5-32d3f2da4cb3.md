Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny was very curious and loved exploring his garden every day.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside and saw a shiny red apple on a tree. "Mmm, red apple!" he thought. Benny picked it up and took a big bite. The apple was so juicy and sweet that Benny couldn't stop smiling.

Just then, Benny noticed a tiny, round, blue mushroom growing near a bush. "Blue mushroom!" Benny said, curious. He picked it up and sniffed it. "Smells nice!" Benny decided to try it. He took a small bite and felt happy inside.

Benny loved his garden and all the fun things he discovered. Every day, he played with his friends—Milo the mouse, Lulu the squirrel, and Oliver the owl—under the big oak tree. They would chase each other, tell silly stories, and share snacks like Benny’s red apple and the blue mushroom.

One day, Benny found a golden acorn hidden in the grass. "Wow!" he said. He carefully put it in his basket and hopped home. His mommy bunny smiled and said, "Benny, what a brave explorer you are! Remember to be kind to your friends and always look for new adventures in your garden!"

Benny nodded happily and continued his happy days, full of laughter, friends, and delicious treats.
