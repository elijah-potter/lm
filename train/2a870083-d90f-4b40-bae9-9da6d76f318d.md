Two figures in a dimly lit alley, their shoulders pressed close as they share a glance that feels like a physical touch. The air hums with unspoken words—one of them is crying silently, their breath shallow against the other’s neck. A single candle flickers between them, casting long shadows that dissolve into the moment’s warmth. The moment they both know: *"You’ve always known."* The other’s hands trace the curve of their spine, fingers lingering on a scar that matches the one they found on the other’s wrist days ago. The alley’s echo fades into the sound of their shared, unspoken breath.
