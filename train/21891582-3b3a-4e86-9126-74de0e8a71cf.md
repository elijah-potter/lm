An unwritten pact between unseen hands in shared space:
Silence as a boundary, words as a breach.
No signed contract, yet obligations are bound—
A handshake of implied trust in the unseen.
Unspoken rules enforce a hush that persists,
A tacit vow to never speak the unsaid aloud.
The pact lingers like mist, unspoken yet felt,
A bond forged in the absence of spoken terms.
The pact remains: **No voice. No spoken vow.**
