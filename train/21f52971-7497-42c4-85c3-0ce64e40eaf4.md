Once there was a little bunny named Benny who lived in a cozy burrow with his mommy and daddy. Every morning, Benny would wiggle his nose and hop outside to see what was new in the garden.

One sunny day, Benny spotted a shiny red apple on the tree. "Mmm, what a tasty treat!" he thought. Benny hopped up and carefully took a big bite. But oh no! The apple turned out to be a magic apple.

Suddenly, Benny’s ears started to twinkle, and he saw tiny fireflies dancing around him. Benny giggled and chased them. The fireflies flew faster and faster, and Benny’s hopping got even bigger!

Suddenly, Benny felt a warm, fuzzy feeling. He opened his eyes and saw his burrow was glowing with stars! His mommy and daddy were smiling, and they had turned into little bunnies with sparkly fur.

"Benny, we’re glad you found our magic apple!" his mommy said. "Now we can all play forever!"

Benny laughed and hugged his family. From that day on, Benny knew magic was fun—and best shared with his loved ones! And so, Benny, his mommy, and daddy hopped happily around the garden, their burrow shining brighter than ever.
