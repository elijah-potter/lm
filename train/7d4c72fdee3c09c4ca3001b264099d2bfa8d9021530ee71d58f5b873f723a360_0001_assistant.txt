Certainly! Here are some tasty and simple dishes to make that are perfect for a potluck party:

- BBQ pork or chicken sliders
A very simple recipe that only requires bottled barbecue sauce, either chicken or pork cooked in a slow cooker, and sweet Hawaiian rolls to serve them on. It can be transported easily, is easy to make, and is simply delicious.

- Pinwheels
A tasty appetizer made by putting deli meats, cheese, and sometimes lettuce or other vegetables in a flour tortilla, rolling it up into a cylinder shape like a burrito, and then cutting them into circular pieces around 1 inch thick. The different colors of the meats, cheese, and veggies make a visually appealing circular pattern, and they are very tasty.

- Crunch Clusters
A simple dessert made by rolling popcorn, pretzel pieces, peanuts, sweet dry cereals, peanut butter, and marshmallow fluff into a ball and baking it in the oven. These delicious sweet and salty treats can be drizzled with melted caramel or chocolate after baking. They are sure to satisfy anyone's sweet tooth!
