**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, Ill. – August 15, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, promising a celebration of agriculture, community, and family entertainment. This year’s fair will feature a diverse range of livestock exhibits, artisan demonstrations, live music, and traditional carnival rides, providing a memorable experience for attendees of all ages. The fairgrounds will be bustling with activity, showcasing the best of Sangamon County’s talent and resources.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 22nd through August 27th, 2024, with gates opening at 9:00 AM daily. Evening events will begin at 6:00 PM. The fairgrounds are located at 2121 Clearview Road, Springfield, Illinois. Daily admission is $12 for adults, $8 for children ages 5-12, and free for children four and under. Season passes, offering unlimited access for the duration of the fair, are available for $50. Discounted pre-sale tickets can be purchased online at sangamoncountyfair.org.

This year’s fair boasts a particularly exciting schedule of events, including:

*   **Demolition Derby:** Friday, August 23rd at 7:00 PM.
*   **Livestock Auction:** Saturday, August 24th at 10:00 AM.
*   **Grandstand Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters:** Saturday, August 24th at 8:00 PM.
*   **4-H Showcase:** Sunday, August 25th at 2:00 PM.
*   **Tractor Pull:** Thursday, August 22nd at 6:30 PM.
*   **Queen’s Crowning Ceremony:** Friday, August 23rd at 5:00 PM.
