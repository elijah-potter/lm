Stages of Physical Fitness Attitudes
There are different stages to how one approaches the entire process of activity and related motivation. There are 6 stages that outline the process of developing a positive attitude towards the body and overall well-being.
- Knowledge: The first step is to know how activity can help maintain a good physique and give a positive response to the body.
- Interest: Developing interest is subjective. Only after being interested can a person break the chain of inactivity.
- Decision: They will then decide to act upon their interest as they already have the required information about physical activity and its benefits to maintaining fitness.
- Action: After making the decision comes the action part where you do the activity like it is supposed to be done.
- Maintenance: A person is required to follow through with their routine of regularly exercising. One would have to maintain the routine continues to help the body for a longer duration.
- Relapse: Eventually it may happen that a person feels demotivated enough to drop all kinds of physical activity altogether. This stage is termed a relapse because a person is returning to their initial stage of inactivity and has no motivation.

Alleviates Symptoms of Depression
Physical activity is directly related to better mental health and can also help in alleviating symptoms of depression. Depression is a mental health illness that can impact any individual irrespective of their culture, gender or age. People end up feeling worthless and hopeless and in some situations, it can even lead to suicide.
Depression can be combated with good habits that promote wellness like socializing, sleeping, and exercising. There are many different activities that one can try out to help with depression like cycling, swimming or just walking in the park. Even engaging in these activities when you do not have a mental illness can reduce your risk of being depressed or developing any anxiety disorders.
Helps Manage Anxiety Better
Anxiety disorders present themselves as visible discomfort. One can often see people visiting with their hands or feet then they are anxious in a social setting. Anxiety disorders are difficult to tackle because they have different triggers in different situations. If left untreated, they can lead to severe stress and even depression.
One way to manage anxiety is by taking the nervous energy out through physical activity. It will help a person divert their attention from anxious thoughts to developing a more positive mindset. Maintaining the habit can lead to fewer and fewer thoughts leading to anxiety.
Acts as a Coping Mechanism
Coping mechanisms are used to deal with different stressors in our life. There is good stress which propels an individual to do something productive. However, most of the stress an individual goes through is unhealthy stress which leads to anxiety and other mental health-related issues.
There are different copy mechanisms a person engages in order to deal with stress. They can be positive or maladaptive depending on your approach to solving the problem. Physical exercise can become a coping mechanism to help with daily stress. It is a good way to clear your head and approach the situation in a rational manner rather than worrying about it without reaching a solution.
