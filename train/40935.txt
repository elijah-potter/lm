It is essential to know to choose the appropriate type of windows for your home that looks both attractive and also gives quality work. Therefore, we first have to look at the different part of windows and their anatomy.
- The frame of the window consists of a solid material that provides strength and holds to the overall material. It maintains the structure of the windows healthily so you must submit the material that has enough strength of doing the task.
- It is the cladding that protects the outer part of windows. The protection of window on the outside is also important for maintaining durability. It is the reason you should buy the quality material for your house. There are many options such as wood, vinyl, fiberglass and aluminum that would prove the best for your house.
- The movement of windows depends on the sash that can be sloped. It should be a material that is more comfortable to touch as you have to clean the window.
- The insulated glass consists of the glass filled with a gas or air for increasing the insulation. You can see it in double-glazed windows as it is more common in this type of windows.
- Low-E Coating is as the name denotes a transparent coating used in the process of window installation for improving the efficiency of gas. It performs the task through reflecting the heat and then allows the sun light to come inside the house. In this way, the light comes in, but the weather remains outside, and the windows keep your house at a low temperature in summer.
- Grills are very familiar and famous as they are easily available in the market. You may call them decorative part of windows that makes the structure more beautiful.
- The U-factor and U-value are the next elements that show the quality of the material used in window installation procedure. It falls between 0.20-1.20. However, the quality lies in the lower number and not in the high numbers.
- The 0-1 value of solar heat gain coefficient is another element that would be new to you. Again it follows the same rule of U-value that means the lower number was working more efficiently for blocking the heat. However, the lower numbers are suitable for summer, and the high numbers are good for winters.
- The quality of visible transmittance shows the amount of light the window allows in the house. It also falls between 0-1 values. The high number shows the more amount of turn on the window will let in the house. Select the high value would help you see clearly outside the house.
