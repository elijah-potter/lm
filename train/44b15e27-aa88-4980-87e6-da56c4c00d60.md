Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny was very curious and loved exploring his garden every day. One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside and saw a shiny red apple on the tree.

"Mmm, red apples are so yummy!" Benny thought. He took a big bite and felt happy. Suddenly, he noticed a tiny little bug crawling on the ground. Benny wanted to help it, so he gently picked it up and placed it on a soft, green leaf.

The bug said, "Thank you, Benny! But I am a little lost. Can you show me the way home?" Benny smiled and pointed to his house. The bug hopped happily and followed Benny back.

As they reached the garden gate, the bug said, "I can't open this gate. Can you help?" Benny knew how to open it. With a gentle push, the gate creaked open, and the bug hopped inside happily.

Inside the house, Benny's mom was cooking delicious soup. Benny showed the bug to her, "Mom, this is my new friend, a tiny bug!" Benny's mom smiled and said, "That's wonderful, Benny! Let's make some soup together."

The bug helped Benny by stirring the soup gently with a tiny spoon. Benny's mom tasted it and said, "This soup is so tasty, Benny! It's like magic!" Benny was so happy that his day was filled with kindness and friendship. And from that day on, Benny and his tiny bug friend were the best of friends, exploring the garden and sharing adventures together.
