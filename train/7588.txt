This student behavior system is perfect for kindergarten and first graders. There is two different take-home sheets for the student to self-reflect on his or her behavior.
- One has just the smiley faces for Monday through Friday and a signature line for the parents for each day.
- The other has the same, but a space for the student to write the reason for the color.
There are two options to implement this behavior system.
Option 1 is to use Unifix cubes, and withdraw a cube when a student makes bad decisions.
Option 2 is to use a clip chart, which is included in the file.
*The student always has the option to earn back cubes, or move up the clip chart.
Included in this packet:
- two different self-reflection sheets
- clip chart
