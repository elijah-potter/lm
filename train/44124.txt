What is Hypertension?
Hypertension, or high blood pressure can either be a temporary issue or a chronic illness, depending on its severity and for how long it lasts. Blood pressure is the force that the blood exerts on the walls of the arteries and veins. Blood pressure increases due to the restriction of arteries, caused by clogging, tension, stress and many other factors. When the openings become smaller, blood flows with a higher force, causing the heart to overwork. This can lead to serious cardiovascular problems over a period of time.
