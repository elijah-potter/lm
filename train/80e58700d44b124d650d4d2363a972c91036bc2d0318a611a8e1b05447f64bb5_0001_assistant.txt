There are countless types of attractions in and around Orlando, Florida, that you would definitely enjoy! Here are my suggestions for you. I hope you enjoy them!

• Disney's Animal Kingdom: This is an animal-themed theme park that resides at the Walt Disney World Resort near Orlando in Bay Lake, Florida. From wild animals to thrilling rollercoaster rides, this attraction is the perfect activity to add to your vacation plans.
• Discovery Cove: This is another animal-themed attraction located in Orlando, Florida. Discovery Cove allows visitors to interact with a wide variety of land and marine animals, including birds, bottlenose dolphins, and stingrays!
• Universal Studios: This attraction is sure to keep you having fun all day! Located in Orlando, Florida, Universal Studios features multiple theme parks with themed rides ranging from the Jurrasic Park movies to the Harry Potter movies!
• SeaWorld: SeaWorld is yet another animal-themed attraction in Orlando, Florida! Here, you will have access to rollercoaster rides, animal shows, and educational exhibits. SeaWorld features a wide variety of animals, including dolphins, birds, orcas, and manatees.
• Gatorland: This animal-themed attraction located in Orlando, Florida, lets you get up close and personal with countless animals, including alligators and crocodiles, as well as endangered and native bird species!
