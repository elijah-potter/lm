The last three Republican Presidents to win the popular vote in their Presidential elections, from most to least recent were:

1. 2004 - George W. Bush: In the 2004 presidential election, Bush defeated Democratic candidate John Kerry by 3,012,171 votes.
2. 1988 - George H. W. Bush: In the 1988 presidential election, Bush defeated Democratic candidate Michael Dukakis by 7,077,121 votes. 
3. 1984 - Ronald Reagan: In the 1984 presidential election, Reagan defeated Democratic candidate Walter Mondale by 16,878,120 votes.
