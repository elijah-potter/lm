Here is a list of pasta and their uses for you:

1. Angel hair pasta - This pasta is best used for very light or creamy sauces.

2. Bow tie pasta - This pasta is good for any recipe that calls for small, shaped pasta.

3. Bucatini pasta - This pasta is great for Asian stir-fries or tossed with a fresh tomato sauce.

4. Ditalini pasta - This pasta is good in soups, pasta salads, or bulk up a chunky sauce.

5. Egg noodles - This pasta adds a heartiness to soups, stews, or casseroles. They are also good with thick cream sauces.
