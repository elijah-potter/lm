Here are 5 suggestions to improve your appearance.

1. Change up your nails: Try a new nail polish color or some press-on nails.
2. Get a new hairstyle: Get a new cut or add some color to your hair.
3. Whiten your teeth: Take a trip to the dentist to have your teeth professionally whitened.
4. Clean up your brows: Wax, thread, or pluck your brows.
5. Eat healthier: Eat healthier foods to improve your sense of well-being, your skin, and your overall appearance.
