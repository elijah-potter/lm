Argumentative essays are written in order to present the writers’ view point on a subject.In these kinds of essays the writer argues for or against something. The issue on which a writer argues can be anything from an object, hypothesis or an everyday issue about which the writer may have some strong opinion. Therefore the purpose of writing argumentative essays is to argue on a point or points on which the writer has some opinion. For writing argumentative essays one should have pretty strong debating skills as well as having good writing skills. Debating skills always comes in handy while writing argumentative essays because while writing an argumentative essay one has to prove his point through logic & facts. But merely having good debating skill may not be enough since one has to also prove his point by writing them down in an orderly & structured manner.

In order to write an argumentative essay you have to do a lot of research on the subject you are arguing on. In an argumentative essay one may argue for something or against something. Irrespective of which side of the debate you are on it is necessary that you know the facts to write a thorough argumentative essay on it. You must also ensure that while presenting your points of view, you stay unbiased & argue your points from a logical perspective. In one word you cannot be prejudiced while arguing for or against something.
Also choose an appropriate tone for presenting your points of view. The essay you have written should not appear to be too harsh on people who may hold an opinion which is completely different from that of yours. Therefore it is necessary that you avoid the use of aggressive tone in your essay even if you vehemently disagree with someone.
Always argue based on facts, reasons & logic. It is highly advisable that you avoid using religious or other such reasons to prove your point. If possible give suitable examples in support of your arguments. Also make sure that you do not deviate from the topic. Stick to the topic strictly.
In order to have a properly structured argumentative essay you should start your essay with a thesis statement. Make sure that the thesis statement is not too big. Spend about 2 to 4 sentences on the thesis statement & not more. Thereafter based on that thesis statement you can proceed to prove your argument.
