Each generation has certain things that define them and for Millenials, it has to be esports and live streams.  Seven of the most popular names in all of the professional gaming world have decided to pool their considerable resources and massive income streams and move in together under one roof.  They compete in international tournaments, rake in six-figure sponsorships from live streams, and have massive online followings, yet they still have so much to learn about everyday things like rent, bills, and calling the dentist.

"The GG House" follows the crazy antics of these pro gamers as they stumble their way through simple chores, argue over everything from green screens to dish duty, and attempt to socialize outside of Twitch chat.  The laughs come from the stark contrast between the intensely focused and skilled gaming world and the everyday difficulties of surviving with roommates. The feel-good comes from watching these young adults as they inevitably mature and come into their own, figuring out the hard way how to live life outside of video games.
