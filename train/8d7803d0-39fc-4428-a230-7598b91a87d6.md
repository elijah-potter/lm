## Product Requirements Document: Contextualize - Knowledge Synthesis App

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently grapple with fragmented information spread across diverse platforms, hindering their ability to synthesize insights and make informed decisions efficiently.

**Vision:** To empower knowledge workers with a unified platform that intelligently connects disparate information, facilitating rapid understanding and insightful decision-making.

---

**User Personas:**

1. **Dr. Anya Sharma - Research Scientist:**
    * **Goal:** To rapidly synthesize findings from scientific literature, grant proposals, and internal research data to identify novel research directions and prepare presentations.
    * **Pain Points:** Overwhelmed by the volume of publications, difficulty tracking connections between sources, time-consuming manual synthesis, risk of overlooking crucial information.
2. **Marcus Bellweather - Strategic Consultant:**
    * **Goal:** To quickly grasp the complexities of client industries, market trends, and competitor strategies to formulate effective recommendations.
    * **Pain Points:** Information scattered across reports, news articles, and internal databases, struggle to prioritize relevant data, difficulty articulating a cohesive narrative from diverse sources.
3. **Elena Ramirez - Policy Analyst:**
    * **Goal:** To comprehensively understand the context surrounding proposed legislation, including related laws, expert opinions, and public discourse, to produce informed policy briefs.
    * **Pain Points:** Information siloed within government documents, academic papers, and news outlets, challenge in identifying bias and conflicting perspectives, laborious process of cross-referencing and summarizing.

---

**Requirements:**

1. **Intelligent Source Integration:** The application must connect to a curated list of common knowledge platforms (e.g., academic databases, news aggregators, cloud storage services, internal wikis) and allow users to add custom sources via URL or API.
    * **Acceptance Criteria:** User can successfully connect to and retrieve content from at least five pre-configured sources. User can add a custom URL source and see its content displayed.
    * **Success Metrics:** 75% of users connect to at least one external source within the first week of use.
2. **Contextual Relationship Mapping:** The application must automatically identify and visually represent relationships between connected sources based on keyword overlap, citation patterns, and semantic similarity.
    * **Acceptance Criteria:** The application accurately identifies at least 80% of demonstrable relationships between connected sources in a test dataset. The visual representation is clear and allows for easy navigation.
    * **Success Metrics:** User surveys indicate a 4.0 average rating (on a 5-point scale) for the clarity and usefulness of the relationship mapping feature.
3. **Automated Summary & Synthesis:** The application must generate concise summaries of individual sources and synthesize key themes across multiple sources, highlighting areas of agreement and disagreement.
    * **Acceptance Criteria:** Summaries accurately reflect the content of the original sources (measured by human review). Synthesized themes are logically coherent and relevant to the connected sources.
    * **Success Metrics:** 60% of users utilize the automated summary and synthesis features at least once per week.
4. **Personalized Knowledge Graph:** The application must allow users to annotate sources, create custom tags, and build personalized knowledge graphs reflecting their unique understanding of the connected information.
    * **Acceptance Criteria:** User can add annotations to sources and apply custom tags. User can view their knowledge graph organized by tags and annotations.
    * **Success Metrics:** 40% of users create at least five custom tags within the first month of use.
5. **Collaborative Workspace:** The application must enable users to share their knowledge graphs with colleagues, allowing for collaborative annotation, discussion, and synthesis of information.
    * **Acceptance Criteria:** User can invite collaborators to a knowledge graph. Collaborators can view and annotate shared knowledge graphs. User can control access permissions for shared graphs.
    * **Success Metrics:** 20% of users actively collaborate with at least one colleague within the first month of use.



---

**Technical Considerations:**

* **Scalability:** The architecture must be designed to handle a large volume of data and users.
* **Security:** Robust security measures are essential to protect sensitive information.
* **API Integrations:** Reliable and secure API integrations with external knowledge platforms are critical.
* **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** Accurate and efficient NLP algorithms are required for automated summarization and relationship mapping.
* **Cross-Platform Compatibility:** Initial focus on iOS and Android, with potential for web accessibility in future iterations.



**Risks:**

* **Data Accuracy:** Reliance on external data sources introduces the risk of inaccurate or outdated information.
* **Algorithm Bias:** NLP algorithms may exhibit biases, leading to skewed summaries and relationship mappings.
* **User Adoption:** Users may be reluctant to adopt a new platform for managing their knowledge.
* **API Dependency:** Changes to external APIs could disrupt functionality.



**Non-Goals:**

* Direct content creation (e.g., writing reports, drafting emails).
* Integration with social media platforms.
* Real-time data streaming.
* Offline access (initially).



---

**Launch Checklist:**

1. **Complete Core Functionality Testing:** Thoroughly test all core features (source integration, relationship mapping, summarization) across multiple devices and operating systems.
2. **Secure Key Data Source Integrations:** Establish and validate integrations with at least three essential data sources (e.g., academic database, news aggregator, cloud storage).
3. **Develop Comprehensive User Documentation:** Create clear and concise documentation explaining the application's features and functionality.
4. **Establish User Support Channels:** Implement mechanisms for users to report issues and receive assistance.
5. **Onboard Beta Users:** Recruit a group of beta users representing the target personas to provide feedback and identify areas for improvement.
6. **Define Initial Marketing Strategy:** Outline the initial communication plan to reach the target audience.
