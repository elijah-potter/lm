A hollow pledge, whispered in the fading light,
Its weight felt more than spoken—time and tide
Weigh promises like sand in a sieve,
Yet some keep, some break, some fade unseen.

The heart remembers scars, the tongue forgets,
The past a ledger, the future ghosts.
The "yes" that trembles, the "no" that stays,
A promise wrapped in a lie you say.
