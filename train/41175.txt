Chocolate-covered flies and hairy pickles? Yummy! The big-mouthed Woozle is hungry. Can you feed him 12 silly snacks from the spoon before all the snacks are gone? Play together, work together, and have some monstrous fun.
Feed the Woozle is a great way for young kids to learn body awareness, fine motor skills, dexterity, gross motor skills, and counting—all while having fun! In addition, children practice rolling a die, spinning a spinner, taking turns, and working cooperatively. The game has three levels of play with specific rules designed for age and developmental capability. As children grow, they move to the next level of play, adding challenges at each age and level.
Feed the Woozle
- Stand-up Woozle (10" x 10" x 6½")
- 12 Mummy cards (1½" x 1¾")
- 24 Silly Snack tokens (1½" x 1½")
- Spinner (5½" x 5½")
- Die numbered 1-3 (1")
- Spoon (7¾" length with 2" bowl)
