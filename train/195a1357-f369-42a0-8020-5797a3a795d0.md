The morning sun spilled through the window of Emma’s old apartment, casting golden stripes across the cluttered desk. She sat hunched over her laptop, fingers dancing across the keys as she stared at a blank document titled *"The Last Message."*

Her phone buzzed—a text from her older brother, Liam. *"You’re late. The school’s already burning down."*

Emma froze. She’d been too busy, drowning in half-finished homework and her latest obsession: a secret project she’d been working on for months. The project that had kept her up all night.

The door creaked open, and her mother’s voice carried through the apartment. *"Emma, come eat your breakfast. You’re going to be late for school again."*

She exhaled sharply. *"I know."*

The screen flickered with a new notification—her email. An attachment from a mysterious sender labeled *"From the Other Side."* It was a single image: a pixelated silhouette of a child, screaming in the dark.

Emma hesitated. She’d never opened it before, but today, the fear inside her wasn’t just about missing school. It was about the way her stomach twisted, like she’d swallowed something sharp.

She clicked the attachment.

The image shifted—now it was her, standing in a cavernous room filled with flickering lanterns and shadows that moved when no one was looking. Behind her, a voice whispered: *"You were supposed to stay here."*

Her hands trembled as she typed a response, the words spilling out before she could stop them:
*"Why?"*

The reply came instantly:
*"Because you’re the last one left."*

Her breath hitched. The screen glitched. Then, the message disappeared. Just like that.
