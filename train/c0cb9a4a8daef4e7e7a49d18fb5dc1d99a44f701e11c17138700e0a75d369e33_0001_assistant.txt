Light up the sky and rend the silence with thundering booms,
In search of shelter to avoid the shocks, 
Getting ready to hide if it gets too close,
Hated weather but powerful to the core, 
Trees are falling all around, 
Next are the powerlines about to go down,
Inside the TVs are turned off to be safe, 
Next thing you know, a bright light is in your face,
Going to get candles because the power is out.
