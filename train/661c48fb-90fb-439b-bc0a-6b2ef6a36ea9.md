The rule prohibits the use of excessive or redundant metadata in data serialization formats to avoid ambiguity and improve efficiency.

Example:
Input:
`{"id": 1, "name": "Alice", "metadata": {"created_at": "2023-10-05T12:00:00Z", "tags": ["admin", "premium"]}}`

Output:
`{"id": 1, "name": "Alice", "metadata": {"created_at": "2023-10-05T12:00:00Z", "tags": ["admin"]}}`
