Studies around the world have been conducted on workplace bullying and many highlight that this isn’t an issue to be ignored. In fact, some studies show that 75% of workers are affected by bullying. But what is bulling? What forms can it take? Isn’t it only found in the school playground?
In this lesson you will learn about workplace bullying, what it is and some of the behaviors that might be considered bullying.
Who the Workplace bullying and harassment course is for
This online course will help those seeking further information and a better an understanding of workplace bullying and harassment so they can help thier workplace become a safer, employee-friendly space.
