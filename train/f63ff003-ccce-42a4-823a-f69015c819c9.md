The single misaligned circuit board trace in a critical control system led to an undetected signal failure, causing a cascade of unintended power distribution shifts. This triggered a cascading failure in the secondary battery management system, resulting in a 3-hour outage for a major urban transit hub. The error’s delayed detection (due to redundant checks) and the cascading nature of the failure—where one small disruption spread to multiple subsystems—highlighted the fragility of large-scale systems. The incident underscored how a seemingly minor oversight in design or testing can amplify into systemic risks, particularly in safety-critical infrastructure.
