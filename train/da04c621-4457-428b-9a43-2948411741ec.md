**Mayor’s Office Announces Emergency Flood Relief in Riverbend**
Riverbend residents are urged to evacuate immediately following catastrophic flooding triggered by overnight torrential rains. The city’s stormwater system, overwhelmed by 8 inches of rainfall in 24 hours, has caused major breaches along the Blackwater Creek, submerging downtown streets and damaging infrastructure. Emergency crews report critical power outages in neighborhoods near the creek’s old banks, with rescue teams working to retrieve stranded vehicles. Authorities warn of potential landslides in the foothills, prompting road closures along Highway 12. The city’s disaster response team has activated the regional shelter at the community center, which is currently at full capacity. Officials advise residents to avoid floodwaters—now rising at 18 inches per hour—and to report downed trees or damaged utilities to 911. Schools and non-essential businesses will remain closed until further notice. The National Guard has been deployed to assist with search-and-rescue efforts.
