Apple Eye Pad: Children's Year Book 2012 (With Interactive DVD) is a compilation of quirky facts to help children increase their general knowledge in a fun, interactive manner.
Summary Of The Book
Apple Eye Pad: Children's Year Book 2012 (With Interactive DVD) is a comprehensive reference book of interesting information to help children broaden their horizons. It is filled with quirky tidbits that are bound to capture the attention of young readers.
The book contains a plethora of information on a variety of topics such as astronomy, maps, inventions, animals, events, maps, sports, continents, countries, natural disasters, literature, and entertainment.
It covers topics ranging from interesting people to fascinating places and from awe-inspiring creatures to the latest news. The also offers answers to a variety of questions that often fuel curiosity in young minds.
Apple Eye Pad: Children's Year Book 2012 (With Interactive DVD) was published in 2011 by Apple Publishing International (P) Ltd. It comes with an interactive DVD that will appeal to the tech-savvy kids of the modern generation.
