Let’s face facts, children aren’t always the most meticulous when it comes to brushing their teeth. Especially on the biting surfaces of the molars and premolars in the back of their mouth. 

Unfortunately, the contours of these teeth can sometimes be hard to clean from your child’s twice-daily brushing efforts. It’s all too easy for bacterial deposits to accumulate in the natural pits and fissures found on the tooth enamel layer of your back teeth.

Over time, these bacterial deposits can gradually start to demineralize the local tooth enamel causing a serious cavity to form. These areas of tooth decay can sometimes be large and lead to more significant complications in the future. Not the least of which being major cavities that can require large fillings or even a dental crown restoration.

If your dentist notices bacterial deposits in these areas during your child’s routine dental exam, they might recommend cleaning them away before applying dental sealants. In fact, there are some dentists who recommend dental sealants for any child between the ages of 6 to 16. 

They are a special type of dental grade resin that the dentist carefully paints onto the biting surface of your child’s molars and premolars. Then a special ultraviolet light to harden the resin creating a protective layer.

This way even if bacterial deposits start to accumulate in the textured and contoured areas, the durable dental sealants will keep bacteria from direct access to the tooth enamel. Many dental sealants can provide up to 10 years of protection, which will help your child keep healthy teeth long into their adult life!
