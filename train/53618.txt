We have developed a free app called SOY CAPPAZ which is a tool to help people with different skills get into the labor market and manage their lives more independently. Learn more about its features and download the app.
An inclusive society is one which recognizes that all individuals have equal value and implements the mechanisms to guarantee this equality through respect for diversity and by promoting active citizenship at every level.
We believe that every individual in our society should be given the opportunity to develop on both a personal and social level. To achieve this, our programs promote the integration of groups at risk of social exclusion, offering back-up and support for the most disadvantaged people and their families and promoting education and sport as development tools.
Sport plays a key role in the social integration of people with disabilities as well as having a positive impact on their physical and mental wellbeing. Consequently we have implemented a series of programs to help you practice your favorite sport. Learn more and start enjoying sport without restrictions!
It's very important for people with disabilities that their family members support their development and social integration. To help you with this task, we recommend this school where you can share information, get advice and learn some key skills to help you look after them as well as taking care of yourselves.
