**Workshop: *Green Algorithms & Low-Carbon Scripting***
Join us for a hands-on session where we’ll explore energy-efficient coding techniques, from optimizing loops to deploying lightweight frameworks. Bring your laptop—we’ll provide datasets and sample scripts.

**Agenda:**
1. **2:00 PM – Introduction & Ethical Coding**
   - Why reducing computational footprint matters (carbon emissions in data centers: ~1.5% of global emissions).
   - Case study: How a single AWS instance’s uptime can emit ~0.5 tons of CO₂/year.

2. **2:30 PM – Lab 1: Lightweight Libraries**
   - Debugging Python scripts to cut memory usage by 40% (e.g., `numpy` vs. `pandas` for small datasets).
   - *Challenge:* Benchmark your own codebase using `psutil` and `memory_profiler`.

3. **3:15 PM – Break & Q&A**
   - *Focus:* Avoiding "code bloat" (e.g., unused imports, redundant API calls).

4. **3:45 PM – Lab 2: Distributed & Edge Computing**
   - Deploying scripts on Raspberry Pi vs. a cloud VM (cost comparison: $0.01 vs. $500/month for a GPU instance).
   - Example: Using `Dask` for parallel processing on a single machine.

5. **4:00 PM – Closing & Resources**
   - Share your optimized scripts in our Slack channel.
   - *Supply:* Pre-loaded VMs with pre-installed tools (link below).
