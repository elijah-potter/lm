CVC Play Dough Mats
These CVC Play Dough Mats are going to make learning to read and write CVC words so much fun!
Students will trace the words on the mat with play dough. Then they will do the word search to find 3 instances of that word. Finally, they will trace the word and write the word on the handwriting lines. The mats also include a picture to go along with the word.
They also come in black and white, so you can use them even if color printing is not an option.
They are perfect for CVC centers and guided CVC lessons in kindergarten and first grade.
