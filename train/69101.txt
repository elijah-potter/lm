Modern advances in an ancient practice
Electro-acupuncture is the process of inserting special acupuncture needles into the body and then applying a small electrical current to the needles which then transfer the electrical current deep into the body (much deeper than TENS units). Modern research shows that electro-acupuncture at specific hertz and amplitude can have very specific effects on biological systems. Electro-acupuncture combines the benefits of acupuncture with the healing benefits of electrotherapeutics to produce optimum results in the shortest amount of time.
