A rule enforced for its symbolic importance rather than practical necessity. The justification for compliance often revolves around tradition, authority, or perceived moral superiority, not actual benefit. Deviations from this rule are met with resentment or punishment, even when the rule itself is outdated, contradictory, or redundant. The real intent is to maintain control, reinforce hierarchy, or signal compliance rather than to achieve a meaningful outcome. Examples include mandatory dress codes in schools where uniforms are unnecessary, or rigid bureaucracy where efficiency is sacrificed for uniformity.
