The three main types of classical architectural orders in Ancient Greece are the Doric, Ionic, and Corinthian. The Doric order is the simplest, featuring fluted columns without a base, topped by a plain capital. The Ionic order is more elaborate, with columns sitting on a base, having more slender and fluted shafts, and topped by a capital decorated with a scroll-like design (volute). The Corinthian order is the most intricate, with a slender fluted column, a detailed base, and a capital ornately decorated with acanthus leaf carvings.
