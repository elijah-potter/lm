**Scene 1 – The Library – Late Night**
A single bulb flickers over the stacks. Elias hunches over a worn ledger, tracing numbers with a finger. The pages whisper, as if alive. His breath fogs the glass—he’s lost in the past, the future bleeding into this moment.

**Scene 2 – The Fields – Dawn**
A farmer plows the same furrow, his plowshare biting into the earth. The rhythm is familiar. He doesn’t know it yet, but the same numbers from Elias’s ledger lie buried in the soil, waiting.

**Scene 3 – The Mirror – Static**
A reflection blurs. A girl in a tattered coat stares at her hands—each hand prints a different story: one holds a lantern, another a seed. The light splits.

---
**Parallel Timelines:**
1. **Past:** Elias’s ledger records debts, secrets, and debts of time—what he owes to those who came before.
2. **Present:** The farmer’s plow churns the earth where his ancestors’ graves lie.
3. **Future:** The girl’s hands reveal a choice—carve her fate in stone or let it grow wild.
