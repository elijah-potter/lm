The **Great Wall of China** (segment near Mutianyu).
*Coordinates:* ~40°N, 117°E
*Boundaries:*
- **North:** Mongolia steppe (sand dunes)
- **East:** Bohai Sea (coastline)
- **West:** Gobi Desert (semi-arid)
- **South:** Yellow River floodplains
*Structural Features:*
- Stone/brick walls (16th–17th c. Ming Dynasty)
- Watchtowers (20–30 ft tall)
- Forbidden Passes (e.g., Jiayuguan, Mutianyu)
*Function:* Military defense, trade control, imperial prestige.
*Approx. Length (complete):** 21,196 km (as per 2022 estimates).
