LEAP INTO FOURTH GRADE!!!!
Leap into Learning is the cue for attention in Mrs. Smith's fourth grade classroom. Hop on in and take a look. In fourth grade math we take those basic multiplication facts and do two and three digit problems. We also learn long division. We read several "fun" novels in reading. In science there are many experiments to go along with our units and in social we study each state of our country. The last project for this class is an open house "Walking Tour" to show off the research done on a chosen state.
