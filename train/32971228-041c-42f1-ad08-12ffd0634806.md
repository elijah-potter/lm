## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park – A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary:**

The City of Oakhaven faces increasing challenges from extreme weather events, including intensified rainfall and prolonged heat waves. The Willow Creek area, a historically underserved neighborhood, is particularly vulnerable. This proposal seeks $75,000 in municipal funding, matched by $25,000 in in-kind donations and volunteer labor, to transform a currently neglected, overgrown lot at the corner of Elm and Willow Creek Road into a climate-resilient “Pocket Park.” This project will directly address stormwater management issues, provide a vital urban cooling island, enhance community well-being, and serve as a replicable model for future green infrastructure investments throughout Oakhaven. The Willow Creek Pocket Park will be more than just a park; it will be a tangible demonstration of the City’s commitment to climate adaptation and equitable community development.

**Project Plan & Timeline:**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park project will be executed in four distinct phases, each with clear deliverables and assigned responsibilities. The total project duration is estimated at six months.

**Phase 1: Site Assessment & Design Refinement (Weeks 1-4)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Green Spaces Collaborative (landscape architects), Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (WCNA).
*   **Deliverables:** Detailed site assessment report including soil analysis and drainage patterns; refined park design incorporating community feedback (presented at two WCNA meetings); finalized planting plan prioritizing native, drought-tolerant species; permitting application submitted to the City Planning Department.
*   **Key Milestone:** Approval of design and permitting application by City Planning Department.

**Phase 2: Site Preparation & Stormwater Infrastructure (Weeks 5-8)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Oakhaven Public Works Department (heavy equipment operation, debris removal), WCNA (volunteer coordination), local contractor (excavation and grading).
*   **Deliverables:** Removal of existing debris and invasive species; grading to optimize drainage; installation of a rain garden and permeable paving to manage stormwater runoff; soil amendment and preparation for planting.
*   **Key Milestone:** Completion of stormwater infrastructure installation and site grading.

**Phase 3: Planting & Amenity Installation (Weeks 9-12)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Green Spaces Collaborative (planting guidance and supervision), WCNA (volunteer planting days), local nursery (plant delivery and support).
*   **Deliverables:** Installation of native trees, shrubs, and herbaceous plants; construction of a small, accessible seating area using reclaimed materials; installation of educational signage detailing the park’s climate-resilient features.
*   **Key Milestone:** Park planting completed and seating area constructed.

**Phase 4: Community Celebration & Ongoing Maintenance (Weeks 13-24)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** WCNA (event coordination), Oakhaven Parks and Recreation Department (ongoing maintenance support), Green Spaces Collaborative (seasonal maintenance guidance).
*   **Deliverables:** Grand opening community celebration event; development of a park maintenance schedule; training of WCNA volunteers in basic park maintenance tasks; creation of a “Friends of Willow Creek Park” group.
*   **Key Milestone:** Successful community celebration and establishment of a sustainable maintenance plan.

**Budget & Financial Transparency:**

The total project cost is estimated at $100,000. We are requesting $75,000 in municipal funding, with the remaining $25,000 secured through in-kind donations and volunteer labor.

*   **Municipal Funding Request: $75,000**
    *   Contractor Fees (excavation, grading, permeable paving): $30,000
    *   Plant Material (native trees, shrubs, herbaceous plants): $20,000
    *   Materials (reclaimed lumber for seating, educational signage): $10,000
    *   Equipment Rental (heavy machinery for site preparation): $5,000
    *   Community Celebration Event: $5,000
    *   Permitting and Design Fees: $5,000
*   **In-Kind Contributions & Volunteer Labor: $25,000 (estimated value)**
    *   Volunteer Labor (site preparation, planting, construction): $15,000
    *   Donated Materials (reclaimed lumber, mulch): $5,000
    *   Green Spaces Collaborative Design Services (pro bono): $5,000

A contingency fund of 5% ($5,000) is included to address unforeseen expenses related to site conditions or material price fluctuations. This fund will be managed by the Oakhaven Public Works Department and requires approval for any expenditure exceeding $1,000.  We have actively sought competitive bids from local contractors to ensure cost-effectiveness and transparency. Detailed invoices and receipts will be readily available for audit upon request.

**Community Engagement & Equity Impacts:**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park project is rooted in extensive community engagement.  Initial consultations with the WCNA revealed a strong desire for a safe, accessible green space that addresses neighborhood flooding issues.  Two public meetings were held to solicit feedback on the park design, with particular attention paid to ensuring accessibility for residents with disabilities and incorporating culturally relevant plant selections.  The project prioritizes equitable access to green space, directly benefiting a historically underserved community. The park's design incorporates a universally accessible pathway and seating area, and the educational signage will be available in multiple languages. Ongoing engagement will be maintained through the “Friends of Willow Creek Park” group, ensuring community ownership and stewardship.

**Risk Mitigation:**

Potential risks have been identified and mitigation strategies developed.  Unforeseen site conditions (e.g., buried debris) will be addressed through the contingency fund and consultation with the Oakhaven Public Works Department.  Inclement weather may delay construction; the project timeline includes buffer weeks to accommodate such delays.  Volunteer participation is essential; the WCNA will actively recruit and manage volunteers, and the project schedule is flexible to accommodate volunteer availability. Liability insurance will be secured by the City of Oakhaven to cover potential accidents or injuries during construction and park usage.

**Post-Grant Sustainability:**

Long-term sustainability is a core principle of this project.  The selection of native, drought-tolerant plant species minimizes water usage and maintenance requirements.  The “Friends of Willow Creek Park” group will be responsible for ongoing maintenance, including litter removal, weeding, and minor repairs.  The Oakhaven Parks and Recreation Department will provide technical support and occasional maintenance assistance.  The park's success will serve as a model for future green infrastructure investments, demonstrating the benefits of community-led climate adaptation initiatives.
