Many kinds of bacteria, parasites, and viruses can all be spread by infected ticks, making people ill. Diseases such as Lyme disease, babesiosis, Powassan virus, anaplasmosis, ehrlichiosis, Rocky Mountain spotted fever, and Heartland virus disease can all be contracted from a tick bite. Tick seasons will vary by region depending on when the weather warms up, typically between April and October in the U.S. as ticks remain dormant in colder weather.
