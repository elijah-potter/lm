**The Last Laptop**

His phone buzzed—a notification from an old friend, a signal from the last functioning tower. *"Find me. The grid’s down. We need you."*

He pocketed the case. The laptop wasn’t just a device anymore. It was a key. A single, stubborn key in a world that had forgotten how to fix things.
