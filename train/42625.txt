What is ASi protocol?
The ASi protocol is a simple interface system for connecting sensors and actuators with only two wires. It has very high-speed communication, very reliable, and has high levels of noise immunity to make it useful for a large variety of field device connections.

How does a ASi system work?

What is an ASi cable?
What is an ASi cable? The cable used for AS-Interface is an unshielded, non-twisted, two-wire yellow flat cable that can be connected with reverse polarity protection. It serves simultaneously for the transmission of ASi communication and to supply power to field devices with low power requirements.
What is Interface Module?

How does ASi cable work?
The AS-Interface is a single-master system, this means a master device exchanges the input and output data with all configured devices. The transmission medium is an unshielded two-wire yellow flat cable. The cable is used for signal transmission and at the same time for power supply (30 V).
