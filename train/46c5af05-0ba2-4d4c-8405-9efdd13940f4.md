*The neon glow of flickering streetlights casts long shadows across the cracked pavement as rain taps against the metal roof of a cramped apartment. Inside, the hum of a dying generator fills the air. The kitchen’s fluorescent lights buzz erratically, casting eerie blue-white flickers over the half-eaten meal on the counter.*

**Lena** clutches a thermos of coffee, the steam curling in the damp air. Outside, sirens wail in the distance—another blackout in the city. The landline rings, static crackling between calls. She answers cautiously, voice tight.

**"Yeah?"**

**"Lena?"** A hesitant female voice. *"It’s Maria. Are you safe?"*

Lena exhales, rubbing her temples. *"I’m fine. You?"*

A pause. Then, *"Yeah. Just… we’re stuck in the subway. No one knows when it’ll be fixed."*

A beat. The generator stutters, then dies completely.

Lena’s phone buzzes with a text: **RECYCLE POWER BANK NOW—OUTAGE WILL EXTEND.**

She grabs her bag, boots crunching against wet asphalt. The subway doors hiss open. A group of strangers mill about, lanterns in hand, their faces gaunt in the dim glow of a dying streetlamp. Maria’s voice rings clearer now.

*"Lena? We’re on the 12th floor. We have food, but—"*

Outside, a car horn blares. The generator sputters again, then cuts out for good. Lena steps into the rain.
