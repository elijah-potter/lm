A flickering lantern cast long shadows across the cracked wooden floor of the abandoned lighthouse. The salty wind howled through the broken railing, carrying the scent of old salt and damp timber. Inside, the air was thick with the hum of a single, ancient clock ticking away the last moments of dusk. At the far end of the room, beneath the faded tapestry depicting a storm at sea, an old leather-bound book lay open on a weathered desk. Its pages were worn smooth with age, but the ink inside still glowed faintly under the lantern’s light. On the cover, a faded seal bore the words *"The Promise of the Tides"* in worn, elegant script.

A hand emerged from the darkness, fingers brushing the cover with deliberate care. The seal was cracked at the edges, revealing a small, silver amulet—its surface etched with runes that pulsed faintly, as if alive. The promise within was not spoken aloud, but the words were carved into the amulet’s surface, shifting like liquid mercury when the light touched them:

*"For those who dare to listen,
the sea will answer."*

A slow smile curved the stranger’s lips, teeth catching the lantern’s glow. *"You have found it,"* they whispered. *"Now, the tide will bring what is owed."* The lantern’s flame dimmed, and in the sudden silence, the ocean’s roar seemed to answer, a promise whispered on the wind.
