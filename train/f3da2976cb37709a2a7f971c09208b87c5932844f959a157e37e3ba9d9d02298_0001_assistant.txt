If you’ve been wondering about Search Engine Optimization, then you’re not alone. Every day across the globe, millions of people search for things on their laptops, iPads, Androids, and Smartphones. That’s what a search engine is for. It allows you to type in what you’re looking for and get a listing (usually thousands) of appropriate choices.

Very simply, Search Engine Optimization is a method of ranking web pages. All the major search engines, such as Google and Yahoo, use this method to rank websites. They do this with the use of “Keywords” to rank the pages. Keywords are the search terms and phrases that a consumer types into their browser when they are looking for a specific product or service. 

Knowing what search terms and phrases someone will type into their browser when looking for a specific product or service makes it possible for you to design your website and web content utilizing those “keywords.” Hopefully, when a consumer searches for your product or service, your website will come up in their search results and you’ll get higher sales.
