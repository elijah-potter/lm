The path diverged at twilight’s edge, yet neither choice felt foreign.
A weightless decision, like the sigh of a breath held too long—
the fork in the road was already carved into stone before the first step.
The forkland of fate, where the branches of will were already bent
by unseen hands. No hesitation, only the weight of the fork itself.
A decision that wasn’t made, but *was*—like gravity’s pull,
unassailable, unquestioned. The question wasn’t *what* to choose,
but *how* to weep for the one left behind. The inevitable was not
a surprise, but the quiet acceptance of a wound that had always been there.
