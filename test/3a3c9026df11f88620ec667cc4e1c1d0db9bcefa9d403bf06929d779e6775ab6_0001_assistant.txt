The sun dropped low in the sky as the school bell rang to signal the end of club activities. Students poured out of the school for the final time that day, with the teachers seeing them off as they went home. That day, Kofune walked with two of his friends. It was an ordinary day like any other. They talked about whether they would get the new game that had come out recently. As they talked, their everyday surroundings blended into the same indiscernible backdrop. 

As their attention shifted inward, calamity struck. A truck ran the red light at an intersection that had always been perfectly safe. Kofune was the first to see it. His body reacted before anyone else's, and he shoved his two friends out of the way of danger. He wasn't so fortunate, and the impact brought him within inches of death's door. As he lay there, the world fading, he wished he could have continued to live out his life. He regretted not being able to do the things he'd always dreamed of.

Kofune woke with a start in a forest. He checked his body, but there were no injuries. He also had no idea where he was and searched his memory for clues. He remembered being struck by a truck, then he heard the voices of his friends followed by sirens. Then there was a voice of a woman who asked him what his deepest desire was. He remembered answering that he wanted the strength to live out his dreams. His wish was granted, and the next thing Kofune knew, he was waking in the forest.

He looked around, but nothing was familiar. He didn't recognize the types of trees or any of the plants. It struck Kofune at that moment that he was no longer in Japan anymore.
