"The Four Seasons" is a set of four violin concertos composed by Antonio Vivaldi, a famed Italian composer of the Baroque era, around 1720. Each of the four concertos is intended to evoke one of the four seasons and is named after its respective season: "Spring," "Summer," "Autumn," and "Winter." Vivaldi’s unique achievement was to marry the distinct sonnets with the music he composed, enhancing the narrative aspect of the concertos.
