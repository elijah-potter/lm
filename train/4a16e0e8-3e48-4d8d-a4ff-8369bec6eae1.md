A floating city in the **Amazon Basin**, built atop ancient **terra preta** (black earth) deposits, where indigenous and modern architects blend traditional **canopy homes** with solar-paneled domes. The streets, woven from bamboo and recycled rubber tires, curve through dense rainforest, lined with **hydroponic gardens** and **bioluminescent fungi** lighting pathways. Locals trade in **synthetic rubber** (from genetically modified *Castilla elastica*) and **crafted from recycled copper**—mined from underwater ruins. The sky is perpetually dappled with mist, and the scent of jasmine and ozone lingers. Nearby, a **subterranean aqueduct system** channels glacial meltwater from a melting ice cap, sustaining both the city and the deforested wildlife corridors that now crisscross its perimeter.
