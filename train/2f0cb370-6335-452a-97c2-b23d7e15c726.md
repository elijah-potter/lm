Under pressure of an urgent deadline, the team prioritized the critical feature rollout over secondary refinements, focusing on core functionality to meet the release timeline. Iterative testing was conducted in sprints, with a final QA pass conducted 12 hours prior to deployment to ensure stability. Automated CI/CD pipelines were triggered to deploy to staging, triggering a manual approval workflow for production deployment at the last minute. The decision was justified by risk assessment: delaying would risk losing market share, while early deployment allowed for rapid feedback loops. Post-deployment, a 24-hour incident response plan was activated to monitor for issues, with a dedicated team analyzing logs and user reports within six hours of deployment.
