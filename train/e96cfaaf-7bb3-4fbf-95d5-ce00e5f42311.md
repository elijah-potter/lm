In a labyrinthine city of shifting sands, where streets dissolve beneath footprints and buildings rise and fall like dunes, time flows in fractal loops. The sky is a mirror reflecting the same horizon forever, yet the horizon itself is a fractal—endlessly receding. Rivers carve new paths daily, leaving behind only ghostly traces of waterlogged stone. Monuments crumble into the atmosphere, dissolving into mist, and the wind carries whispers of voices from eras erased by the same sands. The only constant? The sensation of being in a place that is not, never truly was, and will never be.
