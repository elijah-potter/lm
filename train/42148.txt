How Synthesia is used:
Synthesia uses deep learning techniques to analyse audio recordings of an individual’s voice, and then synthesizes realistic facial movements and lip sync to create a sound. The process starts by recording the audio of the person speaking or singing. The audio track is processed with machine learning algorithms in order to identify the sounds and phonemes in the speech. Once the phonemes have been recognized, the system employs a neural network to generate a sequence of movements in the face that correspond to the audio.
The neural network that Synthesia utilizes is a form of deep-learning algorithm that is capable of learning complex patterns in data. It consists of multiple layers of artificial neural networks that process input data and create output. In the instance of Synthesia the input data is the audio track, and the output is a sequence of facial motions.
To produce facial expressions the neural network is trained using a huge collection of video clips of people speaking or singing. The system is taught to recognize the relationship between the phonemes of the spoken word and the facial movements that are typically connected to the sounds. This process of training is known as supervised learning. the system is fed huge quantities of data that is labeled and uses that data to determine how to create the output.
Once the neural network is developed, it will be able to create real-life facial expressions and lips synchronization in real time. The final video is created by combining the created facial movements with a pre-recorded or generated background.
