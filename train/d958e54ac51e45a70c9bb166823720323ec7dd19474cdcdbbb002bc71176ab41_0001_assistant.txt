1.  Use 3 large eggs, brought to room temperature.
2.  1 cup of granulated sugar. 
3.  1 cup all-purpose flour.

Place room-temperature eggs along with granulated sugar in a mixing bowl, and mix with a wire whisk or mixer until it is thick and pale yellow.  Next use a wire mesh sifter, or flour sifter if you do not have either you can use your hands.  Working in 1/3 cup sift or crumble flour so that you can easily fold it into an egg and sugar mixture without breaking down the egg mixture. Place all ingredients in a parchment-lined 8" spring form pan and bake at 350 degrees for 25-30 minutes. Let the cake cool completely. Decorate with fresh fruit or powdered sugar.
