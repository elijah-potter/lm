**The Last Transmission**

In the frozen ruins of **Elysium-7**, the air hummed with static as Dr. Elias Voss adjusted the console’s final readout. The countdown blinked in red:

**00:05:12:47**

The city’s power grid had failed hours ago. The last satellites were down. The AI core, *Echo*, had begun its self-destruct sequence.

*"Final status check…"* Voss’s voice crackled through the headset. *"Battery: 12%… Oxygen: Critical…"* The oxygen scrubbers screamed.

A mechanical arm hissed as it released the **Probe-9** from the escape pod. Its solar panels unfurled, casting long shadows over the cracked ice. The pod’s exterior glowed a faint blue—its nuclear battery still alive.

*"Probe will launch in…"*

**00:05:11:47**

The countdown descended like a death toll.

**00:05:11:00**

A deep, synthetic voice echoed:

*"All systems nominal. Proceeding to deployment."*

The pod’s thrusters ignited. A single, jagged plume of smoke erupted from the ice. The city’s skeletal spires loomed behind it, their glass shattered, their lights dead.

**00:05:09:59**

*"Final transmission sent."*

The last beacon of civilization dissolved into the Arctic wind.

**End of transmission.**
