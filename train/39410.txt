World Kindness Day is celebrated annually on 13th November. On this day, those who choose to participate, attempt to make the world a better place by celebrating and promoting good deeds and pledging acts of kindness, either as individuals or as organizations.
Haywood Elementary deemed it Mr Rogers “Cardigan Day” and celebrated by encouraging children to wear a cardigan. These were a staple for television icon Mr Rogers who spent his career teaching kindness and acceptance.
Principal, Dr Dusty Warren, took the challenge to another level and dressed entirely as Mr Rogers to encourage kindness and remind the children that they are special.
