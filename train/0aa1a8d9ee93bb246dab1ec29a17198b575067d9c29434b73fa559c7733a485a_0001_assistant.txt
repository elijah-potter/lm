The Double-slit experiment was an exploration into the weird world of quantum mechanics that provided evidence of the duality of particles which can exist as both waves of probability and particles. Particles that are in the state of a probability wave have many possible locations and states that they can be in, this is called superposition. But this wave of probability can collapse into a single state which will cause the particle to act like a particle again and be in a single place. The experiment showed that the act of observing particles that are existing as a wave of probabilities causes their wave function to collapse to a single state, which we can measure. This is called the observer effect and the collapse may be caused by an interaction of outside energy with the particle. Overall the Double-slit experiment opened the door to how unusual the microscopic world of quantum mechanics is and how different it can be from the macroscopic world we exist in today.
