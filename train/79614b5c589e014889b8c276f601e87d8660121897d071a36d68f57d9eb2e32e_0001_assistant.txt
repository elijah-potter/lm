A long time ago, there were two brothers named Sean and Steven. One day, there were rumors that a villain named Pyramid Head was attacking different towns and planned on destroying every single lifeform on the planet. The two brothers were determined to put a stop to Pyramid Head, no matter what it took. They went through a long and dangerous journey, but eventually, they made it to Pyramid Head's location deep in the Other World. The brothers fought through hordes of disturbing monsters, and at the end of it all, was the villain himself. The two brothers fought valiantly, and eventually took Pyramid Head down for good. The two brothers were tired after their triumph, but they celebrated with the rest of the townsfolk.
