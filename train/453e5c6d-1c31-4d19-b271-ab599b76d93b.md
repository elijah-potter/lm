## Bookstore Serendipity (Seattle, WA)

The rain was falling steadily that afternoon, a soft drumming against the large windows of Elliott Bay Books. I was browsing the poetry section, drawn to the worn spines and the quiet hum of turning pages, when you appeared. You were searching for a specific collection by Mary Oliver, your brow furrowed in concentration. I overheard you mentioning to the bookseller that you’d been searching for it for weeks. There was a warmth to your voice, a genuine enthusiasm that resonated with me amidst the hushed atmosphere. 

I remember the distinct scent of old paper and leather mingling with the dampness from the rain outside. Your scarf was a vibrant emerald green, a striking contrast against the muted tones of the bookstore. We exchanged a brief, almost shy smile before you located the book and moved on. I lingered, wishing I had the courage to say more than just a silent hello. 

If you recall a woman with auburn hair and a fondness for Mary Oliver, please reply with the title of your favorite poem.
