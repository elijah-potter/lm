**Announcement of the Grand Tournament: "The Eclipse of Echoes"**

---
The stars over the Obsidian Spire hummed with restless energy as the final countdown began. The hallways of the **Archon’s Keep** echoed with whispered anticipation, the scent of aged parchment and polished steel thick in the air.

**"Ladies and Gentlemen, Champions of the Realm, and those who dare defy convention—welcome to *The Eclipse of Echoes*, the most audacious contest the galaxy has seen in ages!"** The voice boomed from the central platform, resonant and commanding. A massive orb of shifting light above the stage pulsed once, its glow reflecting in the polished floors of the grand arena. **"This tournament is not merely a test of skill—it is a crucible of fate itself."**

The **Seven Judges**, clad in robes woven from starlight and shadow, stood at the rafters, their eyes gleaming with unspoken authority. **"The rules are simple: Each of you will face trials that probe the depths of your mind, your heart, and your soul. The winner will not be crowned by strength alone, but by wisdom, courage, and the power to transcend the very fabric of reality."**

A deep chime echoed, and the doors to the arena slid open. A single figure stepped forward—**Master Veyra**, the enigmatic archivist, her cloak billowing like a storm. She raised a hand, and the crowd gasped. **"The first trial begins now. The challenge: *The Mirror of Whispers*. Stand before the void where your reflection is not yours. Whoever can speak the truth of their greatest fear without flinching will claim this prize."**

The arena fell silent. The weight of the challenge pressed upon the contestants. Some bowed their heads; others stood resolute, their eyes burning with defiance or desperation.

**"The clock has begun."** The orb’s light dimmed, and the void swallowed the last of the crowd, leaving only the echoes of their silent breaths.
