The human ear contains several parts.

1.  The Outer Ear - contains the parts of the ear visible on the outside of the head, the ear canal, and the eardrum.
2.  The Middle Ear - contains the eardrum, the three ossicles (small bones), that send movement from the eardrum to the inner ear.
3.  The Inner Ear - contains the cochlea (snail shaped hearing organ), the canals used for balance, and the nerves that send signals to the brain.
4.  The Auditory Nerve - the pathway to the brain.
