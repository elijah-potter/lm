- Plants need water to live. Without water, the plant will stop growing and turn brown.
- Watering plants help you learn about them. Every kind of plant is different and learning how much water they need will tell you about how that plant uses and stores water.
- Plants look nice. Plants can make any room seem brighter, but they can't stay green without water.
- Watering your plants can make you more responsible. Showing that you're responsible will encourage your teachers and parents to give you more freedom.
