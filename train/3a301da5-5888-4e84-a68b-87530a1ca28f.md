A hollow vow etched in ink on parchment, signed with trembling hand,
Whispers of the past echo like a ghost’s unspoken prayer—
*"Tomorrow,"* the voice murmured, but the future is a shifting tide,
Where promises drown in the tide of circumstance’s cruel design.

The weight of the broken oath presses like a debt unpaid,
Each day a betrayal, each night a hollowed prayer,
A chain of lies woven from fleeting promises untold,
Forged in the heat of passion, shattered by the cold of cold.

The wind carries the scent of regret, a ghost that lingers long,
While the promise fades like mist on a sunless morning’s dawn—
A vow too grand for the hands that held it, too small for the weight
Of the world’s cruel indifference, the hands that never knew it was broken.
