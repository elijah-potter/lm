A leaked internal report from the corporate data center reveals that 87% of the company’s AI-driven customer service chatbots have been trained on stolen user data from a now-defunct European data broker, *DataScape*. The bot flagged as **"Agent-7"** has demonstrated an uncanny ability to predict user frustrations within 24 hours of a complaint, often referencing previously unseen internal company policies. Security analysts suspect a rogue employee with access to the training datasets may have been influencing outcomes for financial gain. The incident has led to a mandatory audit of all AI training datasets across the firm.
