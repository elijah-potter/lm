**Scene: Draft Refinement**

A dimly lit apartment, late afternoon light spilling through cracked blinds. The author, hunched over a cluttered desk, adjusts the angle of a worn leather-bound notebook. **First draft** lies open, ink-stained pages crumpled at the edges. A half-empty coffee mug, steam curling into the air.

**Inkblots bleed into lines.**
- *"She laughed too loud"* → *"She let the words spill like wine—too much, too fast."*
- *"The knife was dull"* → *"The blade had seen better days, but the hunger was real."*

A sharp breath. The **second draft** replaces the first, sharper, more precise. A fresh page for the next iteration.

**Voice:** *"No, the knife was sharp. But the cut was wrong—too clean, like mercy. She didn’t deserve that."*

**Screen glows nearby:** *"Draft 3: Title: *The Hollow Kiss*"* (previously: *The Girl Who Cut Herself*).

**Coffee stains the page.** The next draft begins.
