Tyia is just an average teenager, struggling with grades and trying to stay safe in her tumultuous streets. Then, one day, she finds a strange pen with magical powers. Suddenly, she can control the world around her and make anything she wants happen. But something isn't quite right about the pen. Tyia soon realizes that it may be connected to a power more sinister than she could ever imagine. Joined by handsome, otherworldly angel Marc, and his jealous companion Rhapsody, Tyia tries to uncover the mystery of the pen and save herself -- and the world -- from its evil powers.
