It's hard to say for sure, but I would suppose that they have a rough idea that there is an "outside of the water", much like we have a concept of space. We know it's there, we've been there on occasion, but it's vast, confusing, and always just out of reach. I would imagine that if a fish gets taken out of the water, like when they are caught, it would be similar to what humans describe as an alien abduction.
