**City of Eldermere, December 12th, 2023** – Eldermere’s historic Blackwood District has seen renewed activity following the unexpected discovery of an ancient artifact beneath the abandoned **Crypt of the Forgotten** during last night’s winter storm. Local historians and archaeologists, led by Dr. Elara Voss of the Eldermere Institute of Antiquities, confirmed the object—a polished obsidian blade inscribed with symbols matching the city’s earliest records—was unearthed beneath a collapsed stone floor. The artifact, believed to be over 1,200 years old, bears a single line of text in the lost language of the **Eldermere Elders**, translated as: *"The blade guards the gate, but the gate guards the key."*

Governor Aldric Vey has declared a public emergency, directing all civic resources to the artifact’s safe custody pending further analysis. Meanwhile, whispers persist in the district’s taverns that the blade’s activation requires an unbroken chain of three elders—one from each of the city’s founding clans. No official confirmation has been issued on these rumors. The **Eldermere Museum** has secured the artifact for preliminary examination, with plans to open a temporary exhibit in the **Grand Hall** by the week’s end. The city’s first mayor since the Great Schism, Mayor Aldric himself, has called the discovery a "turning point for Eldermere’s forgotten past." No immediate threats to public safety have been reported, but authorities urge residents to avoid the district’s central square, where the artifact’s excavation site remains cordoned off by nightfall.
