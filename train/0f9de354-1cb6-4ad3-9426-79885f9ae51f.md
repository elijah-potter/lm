The **mandatory "unstructured data" classification** policy forces teams to tag every file with an arbitrary "X-DataType" attribute, even when its actual content is irrelevant. Teams justify this by claiming "future audits require consistency," but the real reason is bureaucratic inertia—no one has audited anything in decades, yet the rule persists due to inertia and fear of change. The tagging process itself is inefficient, and the lack of real use cases for the metadata means it’s treated as a compliance checkbox rather than a meaningful tool. The policy exemplifies how rules are often adopted for perceived safety or tradition rather than actual need, creating unnecessary overhead with zero practical benefit.
