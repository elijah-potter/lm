My high school is located in a small rural area of east Texas, We have maybe 65 students max so we have very few options in the athletic department. Of course, there's a football and softball, which everyone in my class chooses to participate in. We have a basketball team with no subs, if we're lucky and tennis and golf thrive because they're not team sports. I would absolutely love to have the chance to play volleyball and have created petitions each year to try to get it added to my athletic program. We wouldn't have a coach to lead us but I'd be willing to play anyway. I just think it's an awesome sport and that all girls interested in it should be allowed to play it.
