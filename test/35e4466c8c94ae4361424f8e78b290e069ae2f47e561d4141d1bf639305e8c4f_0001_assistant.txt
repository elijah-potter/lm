Sure! Pizza is a favorite for Savannah locals of all ages. Here are five suggestions:

1. Mellow Mushroom: Mellow Mushroom takes thematic cues from pop culture and the 70s. They have unique pizza flavors like the Kosmic Karma and Holy Shiitake Pie. There are two in the area, each with unique decor.
2. Vinnie Van Go-Gos: If you want pizza by the slice and fresher than fresh, this is your joint. People line the block for a slice from Vinnie Van Go-Gos.
3. Graffito Pizza: For classic Italian flavors, go to Graffito Pizza. You will find many flavors, including the tasty pollo with chicken and the il giardino with tomato.
4. Squirrel's Pizza: Squirrel's is one of the newest additions to Savannah. They have wood-fired pizza, crispy and fluffy in all the best ways.
5. Screaming Mimi's Pizza: If you want a big pizza, you can't go wrong with Screaming Mimi's. Their pizzas are larger than life and come in extraordinary flavors, like barbecue.
