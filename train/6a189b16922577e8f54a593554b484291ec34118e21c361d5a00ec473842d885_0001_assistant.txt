So many of the decisions in our lives are actually preferences, not rules or laws, as we assume they are. We need to make space for making choices without the pressure of making "just the right one." There often is more than one good option. Let's live in the gray; take more risks and opportunities, even if we fail or quit and start something else. To do this, we need to be honest with ourselves and ask why this particular choice matters and what, if any, consequences, are really involved at all.
