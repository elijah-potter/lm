Being overheard can evoke a mix of:
- **Awkward tension** (unintended exposure, sudden self-consciousness)
- **Paranoia or intrigue** (mystery of who overheard, whether it was intentional)
- **Stress or vulnerability** (exposure of thoughts, secrets, or emotions)
- **Surprise or exhilaration** (unexpected connection, eavesdropping as a shared moment)
- **Distrust or curiosity** (assumption of judgment, need to verify the listener)
- **Nostalgia** (recalling past overheard moments, their impact on perception)
- **Power shift** (perception of authority or control over what’s said)

Context matters—silent, overheard confessions feel different from a stranger listening in public. The moment itself is often fleeting, but the memory lingers.
