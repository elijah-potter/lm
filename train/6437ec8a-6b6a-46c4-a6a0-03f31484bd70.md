**The Labyrinth’s Puzzle**
Beneath the flickering lantern’s glow, the walls pulsed with shifting sigils, their letters rearranging like ghosts. A single syllable echoed from the void: *"Kairos."* The air hummed with static, and the quill trembled in my grip—was this the first fragment of truth, or the trap I’d fallen into?

A ghostly hand emerged from the shadows, pressing a silver key into my palm. *"To claim the artifact, you must answer first."*

1. *"What is the first syllable of the alphabet?"*
2. *"The name of the celestial body that rules the night?"*
3. *"The color of the void between stars?"*

The key’s weight felt like a promise. The past was a door—would I step through, or turn away forever?
