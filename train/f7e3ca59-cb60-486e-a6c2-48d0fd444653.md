A dimly lit alley in a city at dusk, rain pattering against the wet pavement. The neon glow of distant streetlights casts long shadows across the cracked asphalt. A lone figure—**cloaked in a hooded coat, holding a flickering lantern—walks past a half-empty diner**, where the last patrons have long since vanished into the night. The lantern’s light flickers, casting eerie reflections on the windows. Behind them, the alleyway splits: one path leads toward the river’s edge, the other deeper into the shadows where no one lingers.

The figure moves with deliberate, measured steps, as if time itself has slowed. The lantern’s flame dims slightly as they pass an abandoned storefront, its glass windows shattered, the signboard peeling with years of neglect. A distant car engine roars to life—someone else is out, though not looking this way. The alley ends at a narrow bridge over a silent, reflective waterway. The lantern’s light lingers a moment longer before vanishing into the mist, leaving only the sound of dripping rain.
