**Silence** thickens like wet wool,
then the rustle of reeds—*sigh*—
**whiff** of damp earth, **breeze** hums,
**chime** of distant rain, *plink*,
**steam** curls from blackened coals,
**pop**, **crackle**, **sizzle**—
**smoke** thickens, **burning** metal,
**hiss** of hot metal on iron,
**clang** of hammers, **grunt** of sweat,
**dust** swirls, **drizzle** in the air,
**whisper** of distant laughter,
**drums** slow, **pulse** of fire,
**whisper** of the wind through leaves—
**rust** of metal, **creak** of wood,
**whiff** of salt and damp,
**echo** of voices—**splash**, **slap**, **laughter**—
**smell** of roasted meat, **grill**’s **sizzle**,
**cigarette smoke** curling, **dry**,
**dust** settles like ash,
**deep** **thud** of footsteps,
**scent** of old wood, **pine**, **earth**—
**whisper** of a fire’s last embers,
**dreaming** in the dark.
