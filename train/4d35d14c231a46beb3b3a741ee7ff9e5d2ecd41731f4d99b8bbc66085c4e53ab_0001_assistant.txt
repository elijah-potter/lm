Focusing on goals is perhaps more important than setting them. Here are a few ways to stay focused:

- Create a vision board using images that relate to your ultimate goals and hang it somewhere you'll see it often. 
- Join a group of others with a similar goal. Sometimes having a cheering squad behind you is good motivation.
- Journal about your goals. This can help you think more in-depth about how to accomplish them.
- Make a list of the steps you need to achieve your goal. Breaking big goals into a series of smaller ones can make them seem more achievable.
