Birds do have preferences in regards to seeds. For example, a mix containing safflower will attract cardinals. Nyjer or thistle attracts tiny birds like goldfinches and indigo buntings. Millet is favored by birds who eat on the ground like doves, sparrows, and towhees. Peanuts are good for crows, jays, and woodpeckers. And of course, the only birds you'll attract with sugar water are hummingbirds.
