Here are five things you can do to be more organized at home:

1.  Make the bed as soon as you get up in the morning. Starting the morning off right can help you have a good attitude for the rest of the day and can lead to completing more organization and cleaning tasks.
2.  Clean out the fridge weekly. This should be done the night before trash pick up for maximum efficiency. Throw out everything that won't be eaten to make room for new groceries and to make it easier to see your food options. 
3. Put aside 20 minutes for cleaning and organizing every day. Instead of putting everything off to do at once, take small steps toward your goal every day. Spend 20 minutes on one task, such as cleaning out one corner of the bedroom or kitchen counter.
4. Empty the dishwasher every morning. Completing this task in the morning will make it easier to load the dishwasher after meals later in the day when you have less energy. 
5. Start a load of laundry before work. Starting a load of laundry in the morning will help keep the clothes from overrunning your bedroom and laundry room. It also will require less time to finish a load in the afternoon.
