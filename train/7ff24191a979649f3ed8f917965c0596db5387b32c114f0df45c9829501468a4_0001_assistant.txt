The term "Renaissance" is derived from the French word for "rebirth". This era, spanning roughly from the 14th to the 17th century, marked a significant cultural and intellectual movement in Europe, signifying a shift from the Middle Ages to Modernity. Key characteristics of the Renaissance include a renewed interest in classical learning of Greek and Roman civilizations, advances in artistic techniques, such as perspective and realism, the development of humanism emphasizing human potential and achievement, and significant progress in scientific discovery.
