A hollowed-out promise, half-broken glass between words,
promises whispered like wind through cracks in the wall—
*I’ll be there*—but the path is a maze of shadows,
each step a gamble, each answer a lie wrapped in truth.

The clock ticks, but time is a thief who never stops.
You reach for the hands that held the vow, but they’ve grown cold,
dried to dust between the fingers of others.
The only constant is the weight of silence,
the slow unraveling of a thread that was never meant to last.

The weight of "I’ll try" hangs like a debt—
each attempt a failure, each failure another promise,
until the words themselves become the thing you can’t keep.
The future is a shadowed mirror, reflecting what you’ve lost,
not what you promised.

The heart, a broken compass, points to the past,
where promises were carved in sand and washed away.
The only thing that stays is the ache—
the quiet, relentless hunger for something that was never yours to give.
