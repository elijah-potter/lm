The First Barbary War, also known as the Tripolitan War took place between May 14, 1801 and June 4, 1805. It was due to a conflict between the United States and Tripoli, which is now Libya. The war was incited by Americans' refusal to continue the payment of tribute to the piratical rulers of the North African Barbary States of Algiers, Tunis, Morocco, and Tripoli.
