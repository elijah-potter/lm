Not a problem. Here are five of the world's best water parks:

1. Beach Park, Fortaleza, Brazil:  Beach Park hosts many different attractions for all ages, including a wave pool. Their water slide named "Insano" is in the Guinness Book of World Records for the highest water slide. 
2. Aquaventure Water Park, Dubai, United Arab Emirates: Dubai is home to the biggest water park in all of Europe and the Middle East. It is jam-packed with activities, including swimming with sting rays. 
3. Chimelong Paradise, Guangzhou, China: This water park hosts tens of thousands of people every day. It has many attractions, but the world's longest lazy river is the park's claim to fame.
4. Universal Volcano Bay, Florida, United States: The park sits outside of the Universal Studios theme park. It is a Polynesian-inspired water park with a 125ft tall body slide. 
5. Aquapark Istralandia, Istria Country, Croatia: Nestled in the forests of Croatia, Aquapark Istralandia is home to a wide variety of water rides from a 55-foot free fall to a fast ride known as the Space Rocket.
