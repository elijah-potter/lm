Here are some winter wedding themes:
1. Icy Blue Theme - This involves shades of blue, white, and silver paired with crystal pieces for extra sparkle. Cover the ceiling and walls with icy blue fabric and hang icicle ornaments all over. Use white and light blue tones for floral designs. Place ice blue candles in silver votives and hang shimmering snowflakes. Use beautiful ice sculptures as centerpieces.
2. Holiday - A theme inspired by the upcoming holiday. Use red and green colors with ornaments and greenery. Have Christmas trees with sparkling lights at the venue entrance and a few inside. Hang mistletoe from doorways and tie red bows for decoration. Make place cards and invitations that look like Christmas cards. Light a fire and have red and green blankets for guests to use.
3. Rustic Winter - This is an outdoor woodsy theme with acorns, pinecones, burlap, and wood signs for decorations. For greenery, use wreaths, pine needles, and evergreens. Use tree stumps to hold lanterns. Make centerpieces from small tree stumps and evergreens. Place plenty of white candles in glass jars on long wooden tables. Use gray wool or knitted blankets as accents on the backs of chairs, and light a fire if there is a fireplace.
4. Winter Starry Night - Cover the ceiling and walls in dark blue to make a night sky. Hang twinkling lights from the ceiling and down the walls to mimic a starry night. Decorate with gold and silver sparkling stars and white snowflakes. Use shimmery fabrics, glass votives, and sparkling confetti. Add white tablecloths and flowers to create a wintery atmosphere. For party gifts, you can give your guests snow globes to remind them of the event.
