It's a rainy Sunday, and you're sitting at home with nothing to do. At first, you're a little frustrated thinking about all the things you could be doing if it weren't raining. But then you realize that it's actually kind of nice to be sitting inside on a rainy day. It's a small world, but it's your oyster. You could take a nap, read a book, or make a phone call. Anything you want! After thinking it over, you decide to snuggle up on the couch by yourself with a blanket and enjoy the sound of the rain slapping against the windows.

Only you're not by yourself for very long! The cat jumps on the couch and curls up on your lap. Then the dog comes over and starts nudging you for some love. You're getting a little crowded now, but you don't mind because these are your pets, and you love them. Then the hamster escapes from its cage and scurries onto the couch. The turtle slowly climbs up and snuggles against your foot. Finally, the goldfish jumps out of its bowl and flops onto the couch. Now you're buried under a pile of animals, and you can't move! And one of them can't breathe!

You've become surrounded by all your furry (and scaly) friends. It may be raining outside, but no one minds because everyone is happy and warm inside. Well, maybe you mind a little bit because everyone needs to get comfortable. "Move over, Rover," you announce, stretching your legs forward so you don't cramp up. You set your book aside and enjoy the company of pets... once you've put the goldfish safely back in its bowl. You put on a movie everyone can watch and relax: Dr. Doolittle. Kitty curls in and starts snoring. Even the turtle seems happy. It's a perfect afternoon.
