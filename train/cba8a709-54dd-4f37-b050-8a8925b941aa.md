**The Last Transmission**

The static hissed between the cracked speakers of the abandoned control room. Outside, the Arctic wind howled through the shattered windows, freezing the air into a brittle silence. Dr. Elias Voss adjusted the dials on the aging console, his breath fogging the glass. The red numbers on the digital display pulsed erratically, counting down from **12:05:47**.

*"Re-entry in 37 minutes,"* the system whispered, its voice a distorted echo of its own failure.

His fingers hovered over the final key: **Bypass Protocol 9**. The terminal beeped in warning, but Voss didn’t hesitate. The countdown reached **00:00:00**.

A deafening roar split the air as the ship’s engines roared to life. The hull groaned under the pressure, but the doors sealed shut behind him. The last transmission crackled: *"All systems nominal. Do not open the doors."*

Then—**silence.**

The Arctic wind howled on.
