**Scene: The Storm’s End**

The wooden door creaked open, revealing the flickering lamplight. *She* stepped inside first, her coat damp, eyes scanning the hallway for the source of the sudden rain. *He* followed, gripping his umbrella tighter, voice tight: *"Another deluge. We should’ve left earlier."*

The kitchen was chaos—dishes piled high, the radio crackled with emergency alerts. *She* dropped her bag onto the counter, voice sharp: *"You always run, but you never listen. The forecast warned of this!"* She grabbed a towel, wringing it out as if wiping away more than water.

*He* sat at the table, hands clenched. *"I didn’t mean to—"* The door burst open again, this time with a child’s shriek. A toddler clutched a stuffed animal, eyes wide with fear. *She* knelt, voice gentle: *"Shhh. It’s just us."* *He* stiffened. *"We’re not safe here."*

The child darted between them, pressing a small, sodden notebook into *She*’s palm. *"Mom… dad… the river’s rising."* She read the note aloud, her breath catching. *"It’s not just rain,"* she whispered.

*He* exhaled slowly. *"Then we move."* But his grip on his umbrella betrayed his doubt: the path to the car was now a rushing river, the storm’s fury unrelenting.
