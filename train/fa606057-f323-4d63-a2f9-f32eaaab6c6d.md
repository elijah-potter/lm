The rule mandates that all non-public documents must be encrypted with SHA-384 and stored in a **Dual-Encryption** system (AES-256 + ChaCha20-Poly1305) before being transmitted over an unsecured network (e.g., non-TLS/SSL). Access requires a **two-factor biometric + hardware token** (TOTP + YubiKey). Rejected submissions are automatically purged after **72 hours of non-activity**.
