In the original story of the cherry tree myth, George Washington was 6 years old when he damaged his father's cherry tree after receiving a hatchet as a gift. When confronted, George Washington said "I cannot tell a lie...I did cut it with my hatchet." His father delighted in his son's honesty and embraced him. The story was invented by Mason Lock Weems and was included in the fifth edition of his biography of Washington titled 'The Life of Washington,' published in 1806.
