Scientists believe they may have found a way to create the "holy grail" of breast cancer research after an existing drug appeared to delay or even prevent tumours in women with a particularly high risk of the disease.
Many women who have the BRCA1 genetic mutation - including the actress Angelina Jolie - decide to have their breasts surgically removed because the risk of getting breast cancer is so high.
There is now hope that a drug could provide an alternative form of treatment.
Using samples of breast tissue donated by women with the BRCA1 gene, researchers in Australia discovered cells in healthy women that have a high chance of developing into cancer. They then used a drug designed to treat bone cancer and osteoporosis to try to stop these cells from growing.
