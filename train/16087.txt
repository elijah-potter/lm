A Locus Loci Lesson
This is an extract from the hit movie Taken 2, and shows one of the scenes were daughter uses Maths to find Dad. There are a variety of different Maths components in the 2 minute extract and generates some great Maths discussion. The best use of Maths is the Loci example that is shown and this has proved to be a great hit in getting learners engaged in a Loci Maths lesson. You might want learners to imagine their own story line and come up with a Locus Loci scenario.
