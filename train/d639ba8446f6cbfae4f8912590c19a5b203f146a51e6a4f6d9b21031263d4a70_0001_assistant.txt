Hot Honey Chicken Tenders

Ingredients List:
* 1-2 packs of chicken breast tenders
* 1/2c buttermilk
* 1/5c pickle juice
* 2c all-purpose flour
* 2tbs each of salt, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, and white pepper
* 1/2c honey
* 1/4c buffalo or Tabasco sauce
* 2tsp butter
* Oil of your choice for frying

1. Marinate your chicken: Place your chicken, buttermilk, pickle juice, and half of all spices in a bag or container and allow it to sit for at least 1 hour.
2. Once your chicken has marinated, preheat your oil to 375 degrees. The reason for this is that the temperature of the oil will drop once the cool chicken is added to it. Making it slightly hotter than 350 degrees will ensure your chicken's breading comes out crunchy.
3. Mix the remaining seasonings with your flour in a plastic bag or container.
4. Coat the chicken tenders in the seasoned flour.
5. Place chicken in the oil and fry until golden brown. Helpful Tip (Once the chicken tenders float, they are cooked through. Avoid overcooking so your chicken will remain juicy.)
6. Once your last batch of tenders is frying, start preparing your sauce by combining the honey, buffalo sauce, and butter in a small saucepan.
7. Cook over medium heat until it starts to bubble and thicken. Once the desired consistency is achieved remove from the heat.
8. Toss your chicken tenders in the hot honey sauce for more even distribution. You may also pour the sauce over the tenders or dip them if you prefer less sauce.
9. Allow the tenders to slightly cool and enjoy!
