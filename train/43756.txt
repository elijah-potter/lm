Renewable Energy Sources (EEG)
Implementation of the Act on Granting Priority to Renewable Energy Sources (EEG)
The purpose of the Renewable Energy Act (EEG) is to promote electricity generated from hydropower, landfill gas, mine gas, sewage gas, biomass, geothermal energy, wind power and solar radiation energy. The local system operator who is obliged to purchase this electricity under the Renewable Energy Act pays a legally stipulated minimum compensation for the electricity fed into the grid or a market premium, depending on the type of generating plant and the year of plant commissioning. Under the nationwide equalisation scheme, utility companies which deliver electricity to consumers are equally charged with arising costs.
