**Mayor’s Office Press Briefing – Starhaven, 7th of Solara**
Residents of Starhaven are advised to prepare for tonight’s meteor shower event, designated "The Celestial Cascade." Authorities confirm a high probability of visible celestial phenomena between 9:30 PM and 12:00 AM, with potential minor debris impacts in areas near the Old Mill District. Local emergency services have activated standby protocols, urging citizens to avoid the central plaza and adjacent rooftop terraces. The city’s public transit system will suspend all services during the peak hours for safety. Skywatchers are encouraged to bring blankets and red-light torches to minimize light pollution. No official warnings on atmospheric anomalies have been issued, but residents are advised to remain vigilant near the eastern horizon. Further updates will follow at 11:00 PM. End briefing.
