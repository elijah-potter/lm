You used to make me happy,   
You were my pride and joy.  
In the morning, when I woke,    
And the first thing I would do is look at you.

You were always green,  
The color of life, renewal, and growth.  
You made me feel energized and confident,    
Positive and strong, you made me feel unstoppable.

Now everything has changed, and I am lost,  
You bring me nothing but misery.  
The mornings are filled with dread,  
And when I look at you, I feel nothing but pain.

When I look at you now, all I see is red,  
The color of blood and anger.    
You make me feel hopeless and despondent,  
Sad and depressed, you make me feel defeated.

Please come back to me my old stock portfolio,  
I miss our happy times together back in 2021.  
Things will never be the same for me again,  
Until you are back up where you belong.
