1. What kind of animals are polar bears ?
Polar bears are considered marine animals because they are on the sea ice of the Arctic Ocean most of the time. After all, this is where they get their food. Polar bears are the only bears that are marine mammals.

2. What is the color of a polar bear's skin, and why do they have white fur?
A polar bear's skin is black. Although a polar bear's fur looks like it's white, it has no color. The only reason it looks like it's white is because it reflects visible light. 

3. How long can polar bears swim for and how do they do it?
Polar bears can swim day after day for a steady amount of time. Not only that, but they can swim for up to 6 miles per hour in water. Polar bears can swim far for a long time, especially if they want to get from one piece of ice to another. Their big paws are designed for swimming, and they use them to paddle through the water while keeping their hind legs flat. 

4. How many polar bear hunts are successful, and what do they eat?
Only 2 out of 100 polar bear hunts are a success. Even though a polar bear spends half of its life trying to hunt for food, they are rarely lucky. A polar bear mostly eats ringed seals and bearded seals, and they hunt for them in different ways. They either break into pupping dens or follow them when they are on the ice. Polar bears also eat small mammals, birds, plants, or eggs. 

5. How do scientists get the DNA of a polar bear?
There has been a new method developed by specialists that helps scientists get DNA from a polar bear's footprint in the snow. They only need two small scoops of snow to get the DNA of a polar bear, and even the DNA of a recent meal, such as a seal.
