A critical error was made during the final inspection phase of the pipeline due to overlooked documentation discrepancies between the new design draft and the legacy system's schematics. The team initially dismissed the inconsistencies as minor formatting differences, but during runtime testing, a cascading failure occurred in the emergency shutdown protocol, causing a 45-minute delay in production line restart. Post-mortem analysis revealed the discrepancy caused a 12V signal misalignment in the redundant power module, leading to a false alarm in the safety interlocks. The incident highlighted the need for automated validation checks between design files and operational logs.
