Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Pip. Pip loved to hop around the garden, sniffing flowers and chasing his favorite toy, a bright red ball.

One sunny morning, Pip met a tiny bird named Tweety. Tweety had a little song in her heart, and Pip loved to listen. Tweety showed Pip how to sing a happy song, "Wheee, Wheee, Wheee!" Pip hopped and sang along, making the garden sing with joy.

After that, Pip and Tweety played together every day. Pip taught Tweety to hop, and Tweety taught Pip to sing. They shared their toys, ate berries from Pip’s garden, and made new friends in the garden.

One day, Pip found a lost button under the tree. It was a shiny blue button. Pip and Tweety decided to keep it as a special treasure. Every evening, they would sit together, tell stories, and watch the stars twinkle in the night sky.

Pip and Tweety’s garden became the happiest place in the whole world, where every day was filled with laughter, friendship, and endless fun!
