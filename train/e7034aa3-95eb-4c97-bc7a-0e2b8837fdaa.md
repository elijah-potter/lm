## The Cerulean Bloom: A Cooperative Rulebook

**A Game of Botanical Collapse and Global Response for 2-4 Players**

### Introduction

The Cerulean Bloom is a cooperative tabletop game where you assume the roles of leading researchers tasked with understanding and mitigating a rapidly spreading, devastating fungal pandemic. *Cerulean Bloom* isn't a disease of humans, but of the planet's flora. This catastrophic event, known as the Bloom, is consuming vital ecosystems, disrupting global food chains, and threatening societal stability. You must work together, utilizing limited resources and navigating uncertain data, to discover the Bloom’s origins, develop countermeasures, and ultimately, prevent global collapse. Your actions will determine the fate of the world.

### Components

*   **Game Board:** A world map depicting key regions affected by the Bloom, each with unique characteristics and challenges.
*   **Researcher Cards (4):** Each card details a unique researcher with specialized skills and starting resources.
*   **Bloom Cards (60):** Representing the progression of the Bloom, these cards introduce new challenges, spread the Bloom, and trigger global events.
*   **Research Cards (40):** Providing potential solutions, these cards require specific resources and successful research actions to implement.
*   **Resource Tokens:** Representing Funding (blue), Biological Samples (green), Data Analysis (yellow), and Infrastructure Support (grey).
*   **Bloom Markers:** Used to track the severity of the Bloom in each region.
*   **Crisis Markers:** Representing escalating global issues like famine, political unrest, and environmental degradation.
*   **Dice (6):** Six-sided dice used for research checks and event resolution.
*   **Region Summary Cards (7):** Detailing the unique characteristics and potential vulnerabilities of each region.
*   **First Player Marker:** To indicate the active player.

### Setup

1.  Place the Game Board in the center of the play area.
2.  Shuffle the Bloom Cards and place them face down to form the Bloom Deck. Reveal the top three Bloom Cards and place them face up beside the deck. These represent the initial Bloom outbreaks.
3.  Shuffle the Research Cards and place them face down to form the Research Deck. Reveal the top three Research Cards and place them face up beside the deck.
4.  Each player chooses a Researcher Card and takes the corresponding starting resources as indicated on the card.
5.  Place Bloom Markers on the regions indicated on the revealed Bloom Cards.
6.  Place Crisis Markers in the designated starting positions on the Game Board.
7.  Determine the first player randomly and give them the First Player Marker.

### Turn Structure

Each player’s turn consists of the following phases, completed in order:

1.  **Action Phase (3 Actions):** You may perform up to three actions from the following options. Actions may be repeated.
    *   **Move:** Move your researcher to an adjacent region.
    *   **Gather Resources:** Collect one Resource Token of your choice from the region you occupy.
    *   **Research:** Attempt to acquire a face-up Research Card. Roll two dice. The target number depends on the Research Card's difficulty. Success allows you to take the card. Failure consumes one Data Analysis token. If you have no Data Analysis tokens, you cannot attempt research.
    *   **Mitigate Bloom:** Reduce the Bloom level in your current region by one level. This requires one Biological Sample token.
    *   **Support Infrastructure:** Place one Infrastructure Support token in your current region. This provides a bonus to future Mitigation attempts in that region.
    *   **Share Resources:** Give one Resource Token to another player in the same region.

2.  **Bloom Phase:** Draw the top card from the Bloom Deck and resolve its effects. This may involve spreading the Bloom to new regions, increasing Bloom levels in existing regions, triggering global events, or placing Crisis Markers.

3.  **Crisis Phase:** If any Crisis Marker reaches its maximum level, resolve the crisis as indicated on the Game Board. This usually involves negative consequences for all players.

4.  **Pass the Marker:** Pass the First Player Marker to the player on your left.

### Win and Loss Conditions

**Winning the Game:** You win the game if you successfully research and implement three unique “Cure” Research Cards. A Cure Card is considered implemented when it is acquired and a specific, indicated region is designated as “Stabilized.”

**Losing the Game:** You lose the game if any of the following conditions are met:

*   The Bloom Deck is exhausted and there are no remaining Bloom Cards to draw.
*   Six Crisis Markers reach their maximum level.
*   A designated “Collapse” region reaches its maximum Bloom level.

### Mechanics of Uncertainty and Trade-offs

1.  **Bloom Deck Uncertainty:** The Bloom Deck introduces unpredictable events and spread patterns. You rarely know precisely where the Bloom will strike next, forcing you to prioritize regions and allocate resources with incomplete information.
2.  **Resource Allocation Trade-offs:** You have limited resources each turn. Choosing to gather Funding might hinder your ability to Mitigate Bloom in a critical region, while focusing on Biological Samples might leave you vulnerable to a sudden Funding shortage.
3.  **Research Failure Cost:** The cost of failing a Research check is the expenditure of Data Analysis tokens. This creates a tension between pursuing potentially vital Research Cards and conserving Data Analysis for future attempts. A player must decide whether the potential reward is worth the risk of losing a valuable resource.

### Advanced Variants

*   **Accelerated Bloom:** Increase the number of Bloom Cards drawn during the Bloom Phase.
*   **Limited Communication:** Restrict players' ability to discuss their plans and resources.
*   **Specialist Focus:** Each player is assigned a specific type of resource to primarily focus on, increasing interdependence but also potentially creating bottlenecks.
*   **Dynamic Crisis:** Introduce a secondary deck of Crisis Cards that trigger based on Bloom spread, adding further unpredictable challenges.

### Difficulty Scaling

*   **2 Players:** Begin with one fewer Crisis Marker in the starting positions.
*   **3 Players:** Play as described in the standard rules.
*   **4 Players:** Begin with one additional Crisis Marker in the starting positions.



### Designer’s Note

The Cerulean Bloom was designed to explore themes of global cooperation, resource management, and the challenges of responding to complex, interconnected crises. The game is intended to encourage players to consider the ethical implications of difficult decisions and to appreciate the importance of communication and collaboration in the face of adversity. While the premise is fictional, the underlying principles reflect real-world challenges related to environmental sustainability, pandemic response, and the need for international cooperation. The mechanics are intended to foster critical thinking and problem-solving skills while providing an engaging and immersive gameplay experience.
