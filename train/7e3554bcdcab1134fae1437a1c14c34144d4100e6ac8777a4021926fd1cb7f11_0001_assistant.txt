Flip a few pages in a magazine or watch an ad or two on your favorite video-streaming platform and you're sure to notice that the "face" of the fashion world sure has changed throughout the years. The fashion industry was once an illusive club catering to a very specific type of individual, but it has made leaps and bounds over the last decades to become an all-inclusive profession where people of all shapes, sizes, ethnicities and intellectual statuses can succeed. Starting with the addition of plus-sized models, followed by alternative (alt) models, nonwhite models, and disabled models, there has been a steady transformation within this sector. The ability to connect with someone who looks like, sounds like or lives like you guarantees all young girls will have the confidence to be the best version of themself without the pressure to conform to the beauty standards of the world. Support from the most popular fashion doll, Barbie, definitely reinforces this outstanding merit.
