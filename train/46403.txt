The Three Doshas: Vata, Pitta, and Kapha
As per ayurveda there are five elements present around us that is earth,water,fire,space and air.These elements combine into three basic energies or functional principles, which are present, in varying degrees, in everything and everybody.
Ayurveda is an ancient system of medicine that originated in India over 5000 years ago. It is based on the idea that every person is unique and requires a personalized approach to health and wellness. According to Ayurveda, the three doshas or energies in the body are responsible for our physical, mental and emotional well-being. The three doshas are Vata, Pitta, and Kapha.
Space (ether) and air constitute vata.
Fire and water combine to make up pitta.
Water and earth constitute kapha.
Understanding the Doshas
Vata is composed of the elements of air and space, and it governs all movement in the body, including breathing, circulation, and digestion. People who are predominantly Vata tend to be creative, imaginative, and spontaneous but may also be prone to anxiety, insomnia, and constipation.
Pitta is composed of the elements of fire and water, and it governs metabolism and digestion. People who are predominantly Pitta tend to be goal-oriented, driven, and competitive but may also be prone to anger, inflammation, and heartburn.
Kapha is composed of the elements of earth and water, and it governs structure and stability in the body. People who are predominantly Kapha tend to be calm, nurturing, and compassionate but may also be prone to lethargy, weight gain, and congestion.
Health depends on maintaining this proportion in balance. Balance is the natural order of things; imbalance provokes and reflects disorder.
All three doshas are present in each individual at all times, but their proportion varies from person to person .
