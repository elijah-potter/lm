Dr. Theresa had been suffering from tinnitus for years. The constant ringing in her ears was driving her crazy. It was extra noticeable as she was trying to go to sleep. She had tried everything, from over-the-counter medications to sound therapy, but nothing had worked.

One day, Dr. Theresa was working in her lab when she had an idea. She had been studying a new drug for hearing loss, and she wondered if it might also help with tinnitus. After numerous trials, it was found to be effective. The drug, Tinnigone, was approved for the public.
