Here is a list of common tools used in auto tech:
- Socket sets: Used to remove and tighten nuts and bolts.
- Wrenches: Available in a variety of sizes and styles to grip nuts and bolts of different shapes and sizes.
- Pliers: Used for griping and twisting nuts and bolts, as well as cutting wire and hose clamps.
- Screwdrivers: Used for turning screws of different sizes and shapes.
- Impact wrench: A powerful air- or electric-powered tool used to loosen and tighten bolts and nuts.
- Ratchets: Used to turn nuts and bolts in tight spaces.
- Hammer: Used to tap or drive objects into place.
- Pry bars: Used to pry and lever parts into place.
- Fuel pressure tester: Used to test the pressure of a vehicle’s fuel system.
- Engine hoist: Used to lift and move engines.
- Transmission jack: Used to support and move transmissions.
- Jack stands: Used to support vehicles during maintenance.
- Diagnostic tools: Includes scan tools, multimeters, and other electronic devices used to diagnose problems in a vehicle’s electrical system.
- Lifting equipment: Includes jacks, hoists, and stands used to support and lift vehicles during maintenance.
This is not an exhaustive list, but covers many of the most common tools used in auto tech. The specific tools required will depend on the type of work being performed, so it is important to have a well-rounded set of tools to cover a variety of needs.
