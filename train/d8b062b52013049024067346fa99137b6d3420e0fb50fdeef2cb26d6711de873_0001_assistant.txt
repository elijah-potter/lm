Johnny Appleseed whose real name was John Chapman, was known for planting apple tree seeds throughout the country. Born in Leominster, Massachusetts in 1774, it was his dream to produce so many apples that no one would ever go hungry. While the legend of Johnny Appleseed paints him as a dreamy wanderer, research has revealed that he was a carefully organized businessman who, over a period of 50 years, bought and sold tracts of land and developed thousands of successful apple trees.
