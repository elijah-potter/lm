1. Voyageurs National Park: Located in northern Minnesota, this park is the perfect place to rent a canoe and stargaze. You may even see the northern lights.
2. Zion National Park: Located in Utah, this park is full of giant sandstone formations, trails, caves, and rivers to explore.
3. Grand Teton National Park: Located in Wyoming, this park is home to the Teton Range. You can snowshoe, cross-country ski, hike, and fish.
