Once there was a tiny little sunbeam named Sparkle who loved to dance in the sky. One morning, Sparkle noticed a cloud named Fluff had fallen asleep near the mountain. "Oh no!" said Sparkle. "Let’s wake Fluff up!" Sparkle spun in circles and giggled so hard, he turned into a rainbow of colors. The rainbow bounced down to the cloud, who woke up with a big yawn. "Mmm, I missed you, Sparkle!" said Fluff. Sparkle hugged Fluff tight and said, "Let’s play together!" So they chased butterflies, laughed at fireflies, and danced under the stars until the moon smiled and told them it was time to rest. "Goodnight, friends!" said the moon. Sparkle and Fluff curled up together, snuggled under the blanket of clouds, and fell asleep with happy dreams.

The next morning, Sparkle woke up and saw Fluff floating gently in the sky. "Good morning!" Sparkle cheered. Fluff giggled, "Good morning to you too!" They flew higher and higher, sharing sunshine and laughter, until they saw tiny flowers waving at them. "Look, Fluff!" said Sparkle. "They’re waving at us!" The flowers giggled and said, "Thank you for bringing so much joy!" Fluff and Sparkle smiled so big, they could see their smiles twinkle like stars. "We’ll keep shining for everyone!" said Sparkle. And with that, they danced happily into the blue, knowing their little adventure made the world a little brighter.
