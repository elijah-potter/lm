Thank you so much for checking out these Pre-Primer Sight Word Puzzles. Who doesn’t love puzzles? These puzzles can be teacher or student created by cutting between each letter. They can be re-assembled to reinforce sight word spelling and promote more automatic recall. Use these puzzles on there own in small groups or part of an independent word work center. You may also use these in a center where students are asked to complete sentences. Write sentences (with sight word blanks) on sentences strips. Students would be required to fill in the blank with the appropriate sight word.
The cards are colored coded and grouped based on the frequency of the words in text.
Red words = most frequent
Purple = least frequent
