There are several varieties of corn, and it can be grown in the backyard or in mass quantities. This TabStart page presents educational resources that illustrate the process of growing corn and also explain the history of corn. With the information here you will know how to cultivate corn, as well as care for and maintain it. In addition, you can learn how to to fight common diseases and pests associated with the corn crop.
