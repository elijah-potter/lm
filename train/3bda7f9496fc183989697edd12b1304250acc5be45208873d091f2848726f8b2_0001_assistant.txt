There are a lot of different ways you can cook chicken:
- Baked or roasted, cooking it in the oven.
- Slow cooker. Crock pots are very effective cooking tools for this. 
- Grilled/Broiled. Grills or Stovetops are effective for this. 
- Pan seared. Use a stovetop but make sure your chicken is at room temperature before cooking. 
- Fried. Using flour and oil as well as other seasonings to add a delicious crusty coating to your chicken.

Keep in mind it's very important to season your chicken to improve the taste. Also, make sure that the internal temperature is 165 degrees before consuming.
