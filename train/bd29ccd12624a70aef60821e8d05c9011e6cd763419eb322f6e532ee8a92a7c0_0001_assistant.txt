Hello! No problem. Here are 3 ice-breakers you may use in an online meeting:
- Name and random fact: Each participant introduces themselves in turn and states a random fact about themselves. The random fact can be anything; from their favourite colour to a personal life fact, or anything in-between.
- Show and tell: Each participant will have to find an object in their vicinity within a specified short time (say, 30 seconds) and present it to the group, saying a few words about the object.
- Run a poll: Run a poll where participants can vote on a certain idea, or where they can check in and say (in one word each) how they are feeling that day.
