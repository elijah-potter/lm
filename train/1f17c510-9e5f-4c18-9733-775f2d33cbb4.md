A sharp knock echoed at the door, followed by the muffled voice of an older man asking, *"Is anyone home?"* The door creaked open just enough to reveal a flickering streetlamp casting long shadows. Inside, the scent of old books and damp wood lingered in the dimly lit hallway. The visitor stood silhouetted against the glow, holding a small, worn leather satchel.

*"I’m here to return this,"* the man said, thrusting a tattered journal into the crack. The pages inside were yellowed with age, the ink faded to ghostly hints of ink. The house’s single light bulb flickered as the old woman inside—her fingers wrapped around a steaming mug of tea—turned slowly toward the door. She exhaled a cloud of steam, then reached out, her calloused hand trembling slightly as she took the book. The door swung shut behind them, sealing the exchange.
