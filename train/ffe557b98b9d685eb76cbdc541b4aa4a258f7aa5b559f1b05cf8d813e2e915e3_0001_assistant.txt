It's the year 2378 and humans are seeking to colonize a new planet knowing that their time on Earth has been growing more and more dangerous. With resources lacking, climate change in full effect, and cities on the brink of collapse humans all over the globe have agreed that colonizing Mars would be the best option. With humanity dependent on the colonization of Mars being successful, panic and stress are at an all-time high from city to city and nation to nation.

A group of 10 highly knowledgeable scientists and astronauts have been selected to be a part of the first settlement group on Mars. The one issue is that the colonization of Mars will not happen overnight and will in fact take many years, which does not ease the burden on the humans left on Earth. The group of 10 was sent off to Mars just over a year ago with a constant stream of updates coming in, live streams, interviews, and more, the feelings were generally upbeat. The scientists would show off the first human-grown plant on the Martian planet. 

Fast forward 10 years, the group of ten had made strides and steady progress, with those on Earth sensing the excitement of potentially starting a whole new life on a different planet filled with wonder and mystery. Not only that, it had been established that two humans would be sent every year onward to further help the colonization, research, and development, and attempt to speed up the progress of colonization
