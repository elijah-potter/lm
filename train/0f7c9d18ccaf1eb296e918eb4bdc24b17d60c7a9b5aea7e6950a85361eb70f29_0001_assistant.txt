The moon is far away from the Earth, averaging a distance of 238,855 miles (384,400 km) between them. This distance can also be represented by 30 Earths. Noting that this distance is an average is essential because the Moon's orbit is not a perfect circle. Therefore, the Moon is sometimes farther or closer to Earth. The Moon's farthest distance from the Earth is 252,088 miles (405,696 km) away while the closest distance is 225,623 miles (363,105 km) away.
