If you are multiplying the number 9 by another single-digit number, there is a simple trick you can use that makes it really easy! 

Here’s what you need to do:

- Take the number you are multiplying by 9 and subtract 1 from it.
- That is the first number in the answer. 
- Then subtract that number from 9; the number you get is the second number in the answer. 

Let’s try it! What is 9x8? 

8-1=7 
9-7=2
9x8=72
