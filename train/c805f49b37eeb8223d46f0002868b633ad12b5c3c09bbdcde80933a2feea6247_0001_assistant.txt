I'd be happy to help! Here are 6 ideas for taglines and expanded class descriptions for new online courses in art: 

* "Master the Art of Watercolors": In this course, you'll learn the foundations of watercolor painting, from brushstrokes and color mixing to layering and glazing. With step-by-step instruction and individualized feedback, you will create stunning watercolor masterpieces.

* "The Ultimate Portrait Course": My course will teach you how to create realistic portraits that capture the inner and outer beauty of your subjects. Learn to observe and draw facial features with accuracy, create depth and dimension through the use of shading and highlights, and develop and refine your technique to create realistic and stunning works of art.

* "Unlocking the Power of Color": In this course, you'll learn color theory and how to use it to create more impactful works of art. You'll learn how to mix and match colors and create a harmonious color palette.

* "Mixed Media Manipulation": This course is about learning to combine different materials and techniques to create one-of-a-kind art. You'll learn to combine paper, paint, collected articles, and other materials to create beautiful and expressive works of art. With my guidance, you'll explore new creative avenues in mixed media art.

* "The World of Abstract Art": This course is all about creatively exploring the interesting world of abstract art. Use color, texture, and composition to create unique artwork that expresses your perspective. Push the boundaries of what's possible and create works of art that are truly unforgettable!

* "Intro to Digital Art": Use popular software tools to create stunning digital illustrations. Learn how the basics of digital painting, including layers, brushes, and color, work to create beautiful artwork. This course will help you produce beautiful artwork, whether you're new to digital art or just looking to improve your skills.
