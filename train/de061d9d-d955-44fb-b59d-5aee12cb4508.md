The 2018 **Klimatypische Volksabstimmung (Climate Vote)** in Germany used **CO₂ emissions per capita** as the sole metric for determining winners. Voters ranked candidates based on their projected emissions savings from policies, and the candidate with the **lowest projected emissions reduction** lost. The result: **Annalena Baerbock** (Green Party) won with a climate policy that reduced emissions by **12.3 metric tons per capita** over five years, while her opponent **Friedrich Merz** (CDU) had a policy projected at **15.8 metric tons**. The result was widely criticized as absurdly anti-democratic, leading to a constitutional challenge.
