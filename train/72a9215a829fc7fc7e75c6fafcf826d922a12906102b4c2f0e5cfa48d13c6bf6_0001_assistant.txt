I can help with that! Here are 15 songs for your dance workshop playlist:

1. Jump, Jive, An’ Wail - Performed by Louis Prima, Keely Smith, Sam Butera & the Witnesses, 1956, high energy.
2. It Don’t Mean a Thing (If It Ain’t Got That Swing) - Performed by Duke Ellington, 1932, high energy.
3. Taint What You Do (It's the Way That You Do It) - Performed by Jimmie Lunceford (this recording is often used for the ’Shim Sham’ line dance, too), 1939, starts with a mellow tempo but builds to high energy by the end of the song.
4. Sing, Sing, Sing - Performed by Benny Goodman and His Orchestra, 1937, high energy.
5. In the Mood - Performed by Glenn Miller, 1939, high energy.
6. Ain’t That a Kick in the Head - Performed by Dean Martin, 1960, mellow tempo with high energy instrumental flourishes.
7. Lullaby of Birdland - Performed by Sarah Vaughan, 1955, mellow tempo.
8. Honeysuckle Rose - Performed by Django Reinhardt, 1937, high energy.
9. Fever - Performed by Peggy Lee, 1958, mellow tempo.
10. A Kiss to Build A Dream On - Performed by Louis Armstrong, 1951, mellow tempo.
11. The Way You Look Tonight - Performed by Frank Sinatra, 1961, mellow tempo.
12. Love Me or Leave Me - Performed by Nina Simone,  1959, high energy.
13. Cheek to Cheek - Performed by Ella Fitzgerald and Louis Armstrong, 1956, mellow tempo.
14. Beyond the Sea - Performed by Bobby Darin, 1959, mellow tempo.
15. Don't Sit Under the Apple Tree (With Anyone Else But Me) - Performed by the Andrews Sisters, 1942, the intro has a mellow tempo, but this turns into a high-energy number by the time the first verse hits.
