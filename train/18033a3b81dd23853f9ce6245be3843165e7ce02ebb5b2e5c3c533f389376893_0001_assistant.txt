A dog living in Washington is on a walk with his human when he spots a tree moving. It's a forest dragon. The dog tries to tell the human, but the human cannot see the dragon. The dragon follows the dog and the dog notices it looks sad. Back at the campsite, the dog starts to play and tries to engage the dragon. The dragon plays with him, and for the rest of the trip walks beside him. The dragon even returns home with them and hangs out in the backyard tree when it wants to play with the dog. The dog is happy to have a friend and the human thinks their dog is silly playing in the yard by himself.
