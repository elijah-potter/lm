When the map unraveled into a winding river of chance,
the compass became a compass rose of regret—
every detour a character, every wrong turn a scene.
The architect’s blueprint dissolved into a child’s drawing:
lines blurred into lives, walls into dialogue,
and the destination became the story’s last act.

A plan was a skeleton, now alive with bones of luck.
The script was written in the cracks of the road—
each fork of decision a new chapter, each whisper
a voice that spoke only to those who walked through it.
