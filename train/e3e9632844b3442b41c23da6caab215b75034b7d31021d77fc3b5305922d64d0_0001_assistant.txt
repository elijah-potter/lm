**Senses**

His eyes roll upward, catching a glimpse
of the crackling gray cloud above
No soft white, only darkening threat

Ozone danger assails his nostrils,
bringing forth thoughts of harsh red
fire, white heat streaks, and pain

His hands grip the bronze tinted
cliff walls, palms prickling from the
bite of tiny, embedded shards of stone

Earthen dust coats his tongue, the acrid grit
of civilizations past pulsing down
His throat in cascades of bitter brown

Lightning finally escapes the fog above
cracking against his eardrums, a song
of the tawny golden desert
