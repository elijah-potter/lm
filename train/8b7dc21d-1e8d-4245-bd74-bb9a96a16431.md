A bureaucratic directive mandates mandatory "Employee Wellness Hour" (EWH) every Friday at 10:00 AM, enforced via mandatory attendance logs, even though no wellness benefits or productivity gains have been empirically demonstrated. The rule persists due to legacy HR policies, outdated wellness initiatives from the 1990s, and a lack of data showing negative consequences from its absence. Corporate culture views compliance as a non-negotiable, while employees report fatigue from the routine, which disrupts workflow.
