If you are thinking about trying out the keto diet, there are a few things you should know. The keto diet can be challenging at first. It can take a few weeks for your body to adjust. The keto diet can also be somewhat expensive. Many keto-friendly foods are specialty items, which may have a higher price tag. Keto can also be restrictive, and different from what you are used to eating. You will need to avoid many of your favorite foods, such as bread, pasta, rice, and potatoes. However, there are many dishes that can be altered.

Despite the challenges, the keto diet can be a healthy and effective way to both lose weight and improve your health.
