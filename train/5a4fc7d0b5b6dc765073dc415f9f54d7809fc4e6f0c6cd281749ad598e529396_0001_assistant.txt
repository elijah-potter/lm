The region includes Canada, Alaska, and Greenland with a low population density. Canada has large cities in the south and on the Pacific coast close to the Rocky Mountains. First Nations and Inuit peoples reside in Canada and have preserved their customary practices. Alaska, which was formerly part of Russia, is a valuable source of oil and minerals. Greenland, the world's largest island, has fewer than 60,000 inhabitants who make a living by fishing and hunting along the coast.
