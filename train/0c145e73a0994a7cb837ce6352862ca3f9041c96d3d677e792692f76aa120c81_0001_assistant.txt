• Walmart Inc. (1962, Doug McMillon)
- At the top of the list are Walmart and Sam's Club (the latter of which is owned by Walmart Inc.). Together, the two accumulated more than $73 billion dollars in sales.

• Amazon (1994, Andy Jassy)
- Amazon ranks second as it has a moat over the e-commerce sector. Amazon's 2021 sales topped $239 billion dollars and its physical store count is now 662.

• Costco (1983, Craig Jelinek)
- Costco ranks third as it sells both branded and private-label grocery products through its 564 stores. The U.S. grocery sales totaled over $141 billion in 2021.

• Kroger Co. (1883, Rodney McMullen)
- Kroger comes in fourth with a $137 billion dollar revenue, a 4% increase over 2020. Kroger operates over 2,700 stores.

• Albertsons Cos. Inc. (1939, Vivek Sankaran)
- Lastly, Albertsons rose to $71 billion across its 2,200 stores that operate under Vons, Safeway, and Albertsons banners.
