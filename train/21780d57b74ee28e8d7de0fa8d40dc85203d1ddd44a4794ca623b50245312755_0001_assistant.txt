The library was a peaceful spot for the university students who sought a quiet place to study. Aside from light keyboard clicks and pages being flipped, there was total silence. As the students were deep in their assignments and quietly researching, a gang of video game characters burst through the library doors. They were bouncing around and knocking over bookshelves.

The librarians were alarmed by the characters. One said, “Um, is that Sonic the Hedgehog?” Sonic was rushing around the library, at times losing all of the rings that he had collected. Some of the students were highly amused and started chasing the characters around and playing with them. Some students battled characters, an exciting challenge compared to reading or researching.
