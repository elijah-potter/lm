Marigold plant belongs to the own family of Tagetes species. Marigold vegetation are one of the most important uncooked substances employed for the colouration of textiles earlier than the World War II technology. In ancient times, those plants have been used as a kind of clock, said to be phototropic flowers which means they comply with the sun turning their flower heads closer to it at some stage in the day. Hence these flowers open past due within the morning and nearby the evening. Marigolds are considered sacred with the aid of Hindus in Indian tradition. They make garlands out of those plant life and use them in fairs, weddings and temple services and many others. Since these plants are associated with Mother Mary, the English recall them as safety from evil and additionally as a symbol for suitable success.
Scientifically, the phytochemical screening of the Tagetes species have proved to contain flavonoid, alkaloid, quinones, phenols, triterpenoid and coumarins bioactive compounds that are of medicinal price and feature a particular physiological action at the human body (Devika and Koilpillai, 2015: ISSN 1996-0700/DOI:10.3923/ajbkr.2014.21.24).
