The **Library Reading Nook Corner** is a designated area with small chalkboards, sticky notes, and a shared table. Each board has a unique theme (e.g., "Rom-Com Reminders," "Travel Tips," "Pet Care Advice"). Visitors write notes for friends, family, or fellow patrons—e.g., *"Meet me at the café at 3 PM if you’re free,"* or *"Don’t forget the book we swapped—it’s on the shelf behind the bookshelf."* The corner includes a bulletin board for communal notes and a "Lost & Found" section for misplaced items. The area is monitored by a librarian who occasionally updates shared calendars and organizes swaps.
