The **okay sign** (thumb and index finger forming a circle) is often misunderstood across cultures. While universally recognized in the U.S. and parts of Europe, its meaning varies globally:
- **Positive in the West**: Indicates approval or money.
- **Negative in Italy**: Can mean "you’re a complete jerk" or "I’m insulting you."
- **Offensive in Greece**: Conveys "I’m fucking you off" or "you’re a bad person."
- **Taboo in China**: Suggests "coprophilia" (sexual interest in feces) or is vulgar.

Miscommunication arises from cultural context—contextualizing gestures requires awareness of regional norms.
