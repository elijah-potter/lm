Of course, here are 3 Christmas traditions you can start with your 2 and 4-year-old children. 

1. Stockings. You and your children can decorate stocking together and hang them up so "Santa" can fill them on Christmas Eve. 
2. Cookies and Milk. You can bake and decorate cookies together, then leave them out with a glass of milk so "Santa" has a snack and drink when he stops by. You can also leave out some carrots for the reindeer.
3. Books on Christmas Eve. You can pick out books for your children and gift them to them on Christmas Eve so you can spend the evening reading together.
