A distant hum of engines faded into the evening wind as the team gathered in the dimly lit conference room. The holographic display flickered to life, projecting a map of the mission’s planned route. The lead engineer adjusted the controls, but his hands trembled slightly as he watched the coordinates shift—one of the critical nodes had been compromised. The tactical officer’s voice crackled over the comms, urgent: *"Routine check-in shows the main bridge’s power grid down. If we proceed as planned, we’ll be cutting through a black-site facility. They’ve been quiet for years, but their presence is undeniable."* The scientist, arms crossed, muttered, *"Last time we ignored red flags, we lost a team member in the ‘accident.’"* The captain exhaled sharply, rubbing his temples. *"We don’t have a choice."* A server beeped, then died. The screen went black. The plan was abandoned.
