Nashville has a wide variety of options for both watching or performing in a variety of sporting events.

1. If you want to attend a sporting event Nashville has multiple professional teams including the Titans (NFL), the Predators (NHL), and Nashville SC (MLS).  Nashville is also home to a number of college sporting events as well.
2. If you want to participate in a sport yourself, you can try golf, standup paddleboarding, canoeing, or ziplining just a few of the options. Nashville has lots of outdoor sporting venues. 
3. Finally, you can go to one of Nashville's many sports bars and enjoy watching a game with some fellow fans.
