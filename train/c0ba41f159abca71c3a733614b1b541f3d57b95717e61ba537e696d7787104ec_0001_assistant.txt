Smoke fills the velvet room and,
Although it's noon,
You would never know.
Brass and wood fire up,
In a way you can't describe,
Unless you've heard the tap,
Tap,
Tap,
of the rhythm in your bones.
Here's a quiet melody that blossoms
Into something so indescribable,
A fever and a calm,
Restricted yet wild,
Friendly yet dangerous,
The entertainers holding in their balance
Something so fragile,
And yet they toss it to and fro,
Like a game of tag.
Stay long enough, and you'll catch yourself
Nodding,
As if you know the language,
As if you're in on the joke,
As if you're up next.
