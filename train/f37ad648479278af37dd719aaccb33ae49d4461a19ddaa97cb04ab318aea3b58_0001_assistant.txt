Jo: Strong on the outside, soft on the inside. Jo has a great job, dresses professionally, and doesn’t usually show more emotion than is necessary. 

About to get married to her on-again-off-again boyfriend, Jo is supposed to be experiencing the happiest moment of her life. But because her mom passed away when she was young, she’s surprised to learn she’s more sad and confused than she is happy. (She doesn’t know this is what’s happening, she’s just starting to doubt the marriage.)

Lauren: The funny character, Lauren, uses comedy instead of emotions, especially when she’s upset. She wears clothes that make men notice her and has an active sex life, though never lets anyone get close enough to be called a boyfriend. 

Pregnancy was the last thing that Lauren planned for. The father is not someone she likes, or better yet, knows well enough to know if she likes him. In this pregnancy, she learns a lot about herself, including why she cannot be vulnerable. 

Colleen: No relationship on the horizon or even in the past; Colleen has always loved her own company and been completely content chasing her dreams on her own. Her dreams include traveling the world and excelling in her career. She’s got a seriously funky style with tattoos and stands out in any crowd. 

From living in San Francisco her whole life with her family (who are very close-knit) and three best friends, Colleen has decided to move to Boston for a new exciting job opportunity. It turns out not to be as easy as she expected to go it alone. 

Mariah: Stylishly, Mariah is the most feminine of the gang. She loves when things look nice and she thinks that a floral summer dress is perfect for most occasions. She’s lost, but she doesn’t know it, and when she does figure it out, she can’t seem to understand why.
