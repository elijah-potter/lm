Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the garden every day. Benny’s favorite place was the flower bed where the sunflowers swayed gently in the breeze.

One morning, Benny woke up and sniffed the air. "Mmm, it smells like berries!" he thought. Benny dug under the flowers with his little paws and found a tiny, shiny treasure—a shiny red apple! He picked it up and nibbled on it, but it was too big for him. He tried to share it with his best friend, a chatty little squirrel named Sammy, but Sammy only chattered, "Hop! Hop! I don’t like apples!"

Just then, Benny’s mommy bunny called, "Benny, come here!" Benny hopped over and showed her the apple. "Mommy, I found something yummy!" Benny said. "Maybe we can make a snack together?" His mommy grinned. "Let’s cut it in half—just like this!" She showed him how to take a bite. "Yum! Now we can share!"

After that, Benny and Sammy decided to try something else. Benny found a big, juicy strawberry and Sammy chewed a nut. "You like nuts!" Benny said. "I like berries!" They shared their snacks and laughed together. From that day on, Benny knew that even if something didn’t taste the same, sharing made the world a happier place.

And that’s how Benny learned that kindness and sharing make the best stories—and the sweetest snacks!
