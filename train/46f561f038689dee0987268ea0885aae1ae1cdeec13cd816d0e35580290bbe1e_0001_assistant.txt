Review: The Flexible Ginkgo Back Scratcher

Today, we will be taking a look at the newest product from the T-Rexes R Us company called The Flexible Ginkgo Back Scratcher. Since it's carved from the trunk of a ginkgo tree, its main selling point is its customizable length (up to 50 feet long), as well as the ability for customers to specify the size and number of branches they'd like to leave on the main trunk. This way, regardless of the hardness of your skin or the length of your arms, the back scratcher is sure to have a setting that satisfies your itch.

We tested this back scratcher on T-Rexes both young and old, with a wide variety of arm lengths, and the biggest pro of this product is that it can be fitted to work for any T-Rex, no matter how short their arms are or how wide their girth is. Another good point of this product is its sturdiness. A ginkgo tree's trunk can be as wide as 25-35 feet, which guarantees not even the strongest T-Rex can break it. Finally, the ability to set the number and size of the branches on the trunk means that you can set your back scratcher to scratch as hard or as lightly as you need it. Young T-Rexes with less-developed, more sensitive skin may want to opt for a basic option without any branches at all, while older T-Rexes with tougher skin should opt for a full set of branches at a variety of lengths for maximum satisfaction.

There is only one con to this back scratcher, which is the possibility of smaller creatures being accidentally left in the trunk. Out of the 20 back scratchers we ordered, one of them came with an uninvited guest - a volaticotherium that flew directly into one of our tester's mouths in a panic. It isn't a deal-breaker, but if you're a picky eater, it can be a bit of a disappointment to receive an unsolicited snack this way.

Overall, we would give this product 4.5 stars out of 5, and highly recommend it to any T-Rex struggling to reach their back for a good scratch.
