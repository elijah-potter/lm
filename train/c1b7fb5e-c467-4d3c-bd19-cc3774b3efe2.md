A tense pause in the dim light of the old library. The librarian’s fingers hover over the stack of books, their knuckles white. Across the aisle, the scholar’s mouth opens—just enough for a whisper to escape—but the wind from the open window steals it away. The only sound is the distant chime of a clock, slow and deliberate. The air hums with unspoken words, unspoken thoughts. The scholar’s eyes flick to the corner where the half-empty coffee cup sits, untouched. The librarian’s gaze lingers on the edge of the page where the last sentence was crossed out, erased by hesitation. The room is alive with the weight of what could have been said.
