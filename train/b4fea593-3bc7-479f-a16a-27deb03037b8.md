**New Haven, October 12th** – Authorities in the historic district of Eldermere are investigating a series of mysterious lanterns appearing overnight near the old clock tower. Residents reported hearing faint, rhythmic tolls around midnight, followed by the sudden appearance of glowing orbs that vanished within seconds. The city’s archaeology division suspects the phenomenon may be tied to a centuries-old legend about the "Weeping Lanterns of Hollow Hill," said to appear during the full moon. Police are urging residents to avoid the area at night and report any sightings. Meanwhile, local historians speculate the lanterns could be connected to a 19th-century ghost story about a disgraced scholar who vanished after attempting to unravel an ancient curse. The city’s paranormal task force is preparing a response but has not confirmed whether this is an isolated incident or part of a larger pattern.
