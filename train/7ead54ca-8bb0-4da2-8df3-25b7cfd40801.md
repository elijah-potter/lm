A flickering neon sign outside a closed café reads *"Last Night Only"* in jagged, paint-chipped letters. Inside, a half-empty dance floor hums with the remnants of a crowd that vanished hours ago—now replaced by shadows and the faint scent of stale coffee and burnt synth. The air shimmers with condensation from cracked glass windows that spill onto a cracked pavement, reflecting distorted reflections of flickering streetlamps. A single, abandoned guitar leans against the wall, its strings slack, half-played melodies still trapped in the hollow space between the floorboards. The door, once solid, now hangs loose on its rusted hinges, revealing a gaping void where the next crowd will soon spill in.
