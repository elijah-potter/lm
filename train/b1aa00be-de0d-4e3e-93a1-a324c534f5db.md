The rain pattered softly against the windows, turning the dimly lit room into a hushed, intimate space. **Mira** sat cross-legged on the floor, her fingers tracing patterns in the damp fabric of her sweater, while **Ethan** leaned against the kitchen counter, arms crossed, staring into the distance. The silence between them had stretched thin, thick with unspoken tension, until a single word—*"accident"*—cut through it like a knife.

Ethan’s head snapped up, his dark eyes locking onto hers. Mira froze, her breath catching. The air between them crackled, charged with something far more than the usual awkwardness. He exhaled sharply, his voice rough with something she didn’t understand at first—*"I was going to say that the other night… about the map."*

Mira’s pulse spiked. The memory of the abandoned park, the stolen moment under the flickering streetlight, the way he’d hesitated before admitting he’d been looking for something—*her*—flared into focus. *"You were looking for me,"* she whispered, her voice trembling.

Ethan’s jaw tightened. *"I was looking for us."* He stepped closer, his hand brushing hers where it rested on the floor. The connection was immediate, electric. For the first time in years, neither felt the weight of the past or the fear of the future—they simply *were*. The world outside faded away, leaving only this: the weight of understanding, the quiet certainty that they had always been meant to find each other.
