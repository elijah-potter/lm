THE SCARECROWS' WEDDING SUMMARY
Betty and Harry are two scarecrows in love. They want to get married. They go around the farm and ask for various items off their list and invite guests.
When Harry goes off to find flowers, a new scarecrow comes into town. He is dashing. He is daring. As he tries to impress Betty, he sets the field on fire!
Harry quickly returns. He uses the water from the vase of flowers he picked to save Betty’s life. In the end, the couple gets married amongst their friends.

BOOK INFO FOR ELEMENTARY TEACHERS
What are the reading levels for The Scarecrows’ Wedding?
- Lexile® Measure: 600-625
- Guided Reading Level: N-O
- DRA Level: 30-34
- Accelerated Reader Level (ARC): 3.4
Who is the author? Who is the illustrator?
The book was written by Julia Donaldson. It was illustrated by Axel Scheffler.
What genre is The Scarecrows’ Wedding?
It is a fiction book. You may want to check out our genre activities or read our blog post, How to Teach Genre to Elementary Students.
