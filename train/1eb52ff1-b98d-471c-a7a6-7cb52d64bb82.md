Unspoken rules govern the **Midnight Market**—a dimly lit alleyway near the old docks where fishermen and dockworkers trade in whispers. The first rule: silence. Shouts are a warning, not a command. The second: no empty hands. A bartering system operates by trade, not coin. The third: respect the *clock*—lunch breaks at noon, work ends by dusk, but midnight is for those who dare to linger, trading stories or secrets under the glow of lanterns. The fourth: the "silent step" on wet stone, and the "no hand on the railing" unless you know the tide’s turn. The fifth: the unseen guard—the cat that watches from the rafters, who claims no one is *truly* alone there. The Market thrives on these unspoken threads, woven into the fabric of survival and trust.
