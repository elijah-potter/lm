We map out a week of protein-rich meals and snacks to reduce inflammation in this 7-day plan. Chronic inflammation can lead to serious conditions like diabetes, heart disease and some cancers. While the 2020-2025 Dietary Guidelines recommend daily protein goal is between 46 and 56 grams, we know increased protein intake can be beneficial. The benefits of a high-protein diet include reduced body weight, sustained weight and decreased body fat. Our high-protein includes at least 85 grams of protein per day, distributed evenly at meals. We set the plan at 1,500 calories per day but also included modifications at 1,200 and 2,000 calories per day as well.
