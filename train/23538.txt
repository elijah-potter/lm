Wormwood (Artemesia absinthium)
Description: Artemisia absinthium (absinthe, absinthium, absinthe wormwood, wormwood ) is a species of Artemisia, native to temperate regions of Eurasia and Northern Africa and widely naturalized in Canada and the northern United States.
Botanical Name: Artemesia absinthium
Common Names: Wormwood
Medicinal Part: Leaf, made into a Bitter Tea
Safety Guidelines: Should be used with caution. Avoid excessive or long-term use. Not to be used during pregnancy.
