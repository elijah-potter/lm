Lena had always dreamed of standing on the edge of the world, where the sky met the ocean in a blur of endless blue. Her father, a retired naval officer, had taken her to these remote islands as a child, whispering stories of hidden treasures and forgotten secrets. Now, at sixteen, she was old enough to believe they might be real.

One evening, after her parents returned from work, Lena found an old, leather-bound journal on their porch. The cover was cracked, and the ink inside was faded but legible. She flipped through it, her breath catching when she saw her father’s name written at the top: *"The Lost Tidekeepers: A Journey Beyond the Horizon."*

Curious, she followed the journal’s instructions, packing only what she could carry—a waterproof map, a compass, a small lantern, and a single, tattered seashell her mother had given her. The night before she left, her mother hugged her tightly. "You’re going where no one has gone since my father," she said, her voice trembling. "Be careful, Lena."

The journey was harder than she imagined. The sea was unforgiving, and the currents whispered secrets in the wind. But when she finally stood at the edge of the world, the horizon stretched before her like a promise. Beneath her feet, the sand shimmered with strange, glowing patterns—a mark from the journal’s description.

Inside the journal, there was a map that didn’t look like any she’d ever seen. It showed paths no one had mapped before, leading to a cave in the cliffs. Lena followed it, her flashlight cutting through the darkness. Inside, she found more pages—sketches of strange creatures, coded messages, and a warning: *"The Tidekeepers are not what they seem."*

As she read, the cave’s walls pulsed faintly, like a heartbeat. She hesitated, then reached out, her fingers brushing against something cold and metallic. When she pulled back, the cave was empty—but the journal’s last entry was scrawled in her own handwriting: *"They’re waking up."*

The next morning, she found herself back on the shore, the journal gone. Her parents were waiting, their faces pale. "Where have you been?" her father demanded.

She told them everything. They didn’t believe her at first, but the journal was missing, and the cave’s mark was still faintly visible on the sand. Lena knew she had to act fast. The Tidekeepers were coming, and the world beyond the horizon was not what it seemed.
