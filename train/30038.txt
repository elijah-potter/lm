An Effective Process Analysis Essay Provides Step by Step Guidelines
There are various types of essays which are written according to different purposes. A process analysis essay too has its own purpose. This is to analyse a particular process and provide information on a step by step manner to the reader. Out of the different types of essays, this is one of the less creative types. It requires hard factual detail and a systematic procedural approach to its writing. The following is a brief analysis of how to write such an essay effectively.
This essay will need to have a particular process identified as the essay topic. This process can be a scientific one or merely something general. For instance, you may decide to discuss the process of assembling a computer or how to apply CPR for a person who has just been rescued from drowning. Otherwise the topic can me more lighthearted and discusses the process of baking a cake. What is important is that once you decide on the topic, the process should be analysed thoroughly. This analysis will be the basis of your essay writing.
Analysing the Process
In order to analyse the process, you should have a good knowledge of the process and how it is conducted. If you don’t have such knowledge, best is that a good research is conducted to get your self well informed. Recall how essential it is to be in writing an analysis essay? This essay too calls for the writer’s expertise in the subject. List out all the key steps and stages involved in the process and get your facts and details carefully organized before you proceed to the writing stage.
Writing the process
A Process essay’s main objective is to educate the reader on how to perform a particular task by giving instructions on the series of actions which makes up the task. The success of the intended outcome will depend upon how these steps are performed in a fixed and sequential manner. Your writing should provide a clear and precise explanation of each step involved and how to perform each of these tasks. A good essay writer will be able to direct even a layman through the process with the clarity of the instructions in a well written process essay.
While this essay is somewhat specialized than a common narration essay or a personal essay, it can be written to a high standard by incorporating some of the following factors in to the writing process.
1. Select an appropriate process to describe. Think of the audience as well as your knowledge of the subject.
2. Make sure you are well familiarized with the process and all the details on how to perform it accurately. You can not guide another person in conducting it properly if you yourself is not conversant in the matter/.
3. Start off with a clear title that educates the reader from the very outset what the essay will be discussing. This prepares the reader’s mindset to be attentive to the details in the essay body.
4. Use the
essay introduction to brief the reader on the background of the process and the final outcome intended. List and explain any technical terms or specialized words being used within the body.
5. Keep the step by step explanations linked to the whole. Break the process in to stages if necessary and explain each stage separately.
6. Finally provide an essay conclusion in which the intended result of following the step by step process is clearly stated.
By incorporating the above points in to your essay writing, the effectiveness of your essay is bound to increase, making it useful to your reader. You can also include some trouble shooting answers and references to other help resources to make your process analysis essay more comprehensive.
