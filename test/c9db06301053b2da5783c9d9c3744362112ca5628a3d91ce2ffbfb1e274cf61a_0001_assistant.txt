•	The Biting Kiss: Nibble gently on your partner’s bottom lip as you lock lips.
•	The French Kiss: The most common kiss, you and your partner essentially lock tongues as you see fit as you lock lips.
•	The Eskimo Kiss: Rub your nose against your partner’s nose (no lip locking).
•	The European Kiss: Make a kissing face (with your lips) on the left side of your partner’s face, then the right. This is a common greeting gesture in Europe.
•	The Motherly Kiss: Press your lips against your partner’s forehead.
