It is still unclear as to who was responsible for domesticating the chili plant. But recent studies have discovered that it most likely occurred more than 6,500 years ago in eastern Mexico with the most recent evidence coming from a cave near Ocampo, Tamaulipas and Tehuacán Valley, Puebla. From this one species of a plant domesticated millennia ago, there are now more than 6000 varieties of chilis in the world with at least 28 wild varieties.
