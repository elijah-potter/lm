Sure! Here are some kid-friendly museums that would be great for you to visit:
1. The Boston Children's Museum is designed for families, and it has exhibits that focus on science, culture, environmental awareness, health and fitness, and the arts.  
2. The New England Aquarium has over 70 exhibits, with many exhibits catering to children.
3. The Museum of Science has 700 interactive exhibits with additional touring exhibits. There is also a kid-friendly butterfly garden.
4. The Harvard Museum of Natural History is very popular and features dinosaur fossils and various mammals featured in the Great Mammal Hall.
