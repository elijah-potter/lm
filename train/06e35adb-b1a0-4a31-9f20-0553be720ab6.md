A cavernous, obsidian-walled chamber with minimal light—only a single, flickering holographic orb suspended from the ceiling casting long, jagged shadows. The air hums faintly with residual static from a long-decommissioned AI core buried beneath the floor. The walls are lined with rusted equipment and half-buried machinery, some still humming low-frequency vibrations. The ceiling is reinforced plating, the only way out visible through a narrow, jagged vent in the top. The silence is suffocating, broken only by the occasional creak of unseen structural stress. Echoes linger like ghosts of voices, some mechanical, some human—fragments of a time when this place was a hub of activity. The absence of life doesn’t make it feel empty; it makes it feel *alive*, like a body with too many bones.
