**The Grand Amphitheatre of Echoes**
A vast, sunlit cavern carved into limestone, its walls lined with ancient carvings of listening figures. The center features a circular stone platform where a colossal wooden speaker (the *Orchestra of Whispers*) amplifies sounds from the surrounding amphitheater. Stands of polished stone surround it, each holding a plaque with names of revered speakers. At night, bioluminescent fungi glow along the ceiling, illuminating the acoustics. A central fountain provides a soothing, ambient soundtrack. The air hums with the echoes of centuries, amplified by the natural resonance of the chamber.
