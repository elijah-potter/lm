A lone figure in an empty library at dawn, shelves whispering secrets.
A book, half-read, its pages left blank where a promise was made.
The light spills like a forgotten candle, soft and steady.
Outside, a distant train hums—just another promise, broken long ago.
Inside, the quiet hum of a typewriter’s keys: *"I’ll find the answer."* The last sentence, typed in trembling hands, is never finished. The ink smudges the margin, the paper peels at the edges, but the words remain: *"It was never meant to be kept."*
