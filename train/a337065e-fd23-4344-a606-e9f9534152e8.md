In the dimly lit kitchen, the refrigerator door stood slightly ajar, revealing an empty shelf. The lone item—half a loaf of bread—was missing, its crust cracked and forgotten. The child’s small hand reached inside, fingers trembling as they traced the empty space where a banana peel once lay, now swallowed by the dark. The mother’s voice, sharp with irritation, cut through the silence: *"Where’s the apple core?"* She turned, scanning the counter where it had been placed just moments ago. A stray crumb, half-eaten sandwich, and the overturned teacup’s empty saucer were all that remained. The child blinked, suddenly aware of the absence, clutching their empty lunchbox. The scent of baking cookies wafted from the oven, but the warmth felt hollow without the promised sweetness.
