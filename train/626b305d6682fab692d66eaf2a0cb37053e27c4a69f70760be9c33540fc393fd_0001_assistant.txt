Of course! Here are 5 binge-worthy shows similar to Breaking Bad:

1. Mad Men - This is a very popular show set in the 60s about a marketing firm. It has a fascinating look into the industry and has plenty of seasons. 
2. The Wire - This is a crime drama set in Baltimore. It has five seasons to binge.
3. Barry - This series is a comedy-drama starring Bill Hader. It follows a contract killer who tries out acting. 
4. Fargo - This is another crime drama that has a unique take on crime, similar to Breaking Bad.
5. Better Call Saul - This is an obvious choice if you haven't watched it since it is a spin-off from Breaking Bad.

Happy binge-watching!
