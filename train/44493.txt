Change of Speech - English (MCQ) for Bank, MBA, CAT, MAT, GMAT, SSC for Q. 27853
Q. In each of the following questions, a sentence has been given in Direct/ Indirect Speech. Out of the suggested alternatives select the one which best expresses the same sentence in Direct/ Indirect Speech.
“If you don’t keep quiet I shall shoot you,” she said to her in a calm voice.- Published on 27 Feb 17
a. She warned her calmly that she would shoot her if she didn’t keep quiet.
b. She said calmly that I shall shoot you if you don’t keep quiet.
c. She warned her to shoot if she didn’t keep quiet calmly.
d. Calmly she warned her that be quiet or else she will have to shoot her
ANSWER: She warned her calmly that she would shoot her if she didn’t keep quiet.
