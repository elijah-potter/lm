Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a little bunny named Pip. Pip loved to play in the sun and chase butterflies. One sunny morning, Pip heard a tiny squeak and looked down—there was a lost little acorn in his backyard!

Pip picked it up and decided to take it to the garden. Along the way, he met a friendly mouse named Max who was collecting seeds. "Where are you going with that acorn?" Max asked.

Pip smiled. "I found it! Maybe it will grow into a big tree one day!"

Max smiled back. "Let’s plant it together!" So they dug a tiny hole and buried the acorn. Soon after, they heard a soft *tap-tap-tap* from above. Pip looked up and saw a little bird named Bert with a beak full of dirt!

"Help us dig!" Bert chirped. Pip and Max worked together, and soon the acorn was nestled in a cozy spot in the soil. Bert added a little dirt on top, and everyone cheered.

"Now, let’s wait and watch!" Pip said. They all sat under a big oak tree, where the sun was warm and the birds sang happily. Days passed, and the acorn grew taller and taller until it became a tall, green tree with leaves that shimmered in the sunlight.

One evening, Pip, Max, and Bert sat under their new tree. "Look!" Bert said. "The acorn tree is so pretty!"

Pip grinned. "It’s our little tree now!"

And from that day on, whenever the wind blew, the acorn tree whispered secrets to the sky, and Pip, Max, and Bert played under its shade every day. The end!
