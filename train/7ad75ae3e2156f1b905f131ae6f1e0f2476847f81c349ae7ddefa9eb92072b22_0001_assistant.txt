Dear Cinderella (or should I say Ella?), 

I hope this letter finds you and Prince Charming well. 

Amidst all the changes that have taken place, I've been reflecting on my actions and words toward you. I realize I drastically need to change. It was unacceptable how I mocked you and treated you like a servant instead of like my step-sister. My mother and sister swayed me in many ways, but that doesn't excuse my behavior. I knew it was wrong, and I'm truly sorry. 

To be completely honest... I was jealous of you. You're beautiful inside and out. Instead of admitting my feelings, I let my jealousy and my family turn me into someone I no longer want to be. I want you to know I'm working on being nicer to myself and others. 

I understand if too much has happened between us for you to forgive me. I understand if we can't be family now, but I hope we can at least be friends one day. 

I'm glad all your dreams and wishes came true. 

Sincerely,

Anastasia
