A dimly lit alleyway in the rain, neon signs flickering like dying stars. A hooded figure crouches behind a rusted dumpster, fingers twitching toward a stolen wallet on the pavement. The distant wail of sirens cuts through the fog, but the alley feels too quiet. A shadow moves too fast for the dim light, darting toward the figure’s back—too close for comfort. The figure doesn’t turn. A single gunshot rings out, hollow and muffled, followed by a scream. The alley goes still.
