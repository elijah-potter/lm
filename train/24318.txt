Use Crayola® crayons, colored pencils, or markers to color the flag of the Phillipines. Color the shape numbered 1 goldenrod. Color the shapes numbered 2 red and the shape numbered 3 blue. Color the stars goldenrod, and the rest of the flag should be white. Did you know?
The Philippine islands are located in southeastern Asia. English and Filipino are its two official langauges. On July 4, 1946, the Philippines gained their independence.
