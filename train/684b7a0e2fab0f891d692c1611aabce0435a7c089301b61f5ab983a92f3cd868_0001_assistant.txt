The first word I would use to describe this room is "tall." Everything is tall. The floor is wood and needs to be swept. There is dust everywhere and a lot of cat hair. I don't know where that's from. 

There is a long yellow and blue striped couch that I enjoy jumping on even though I'm not supposed to. And in front of it is a rectangular, blue and yellow rug that I frequently use to sharpen my claws, much to my owner's displeasure. On the rug sits a coffee table that's brown rattan, rectangular, with a glass top. My owner has papers and books on it. She yells when I jump on it, but if those books and papers were important, maybe she shouldn't have left them there. Next to the couch and rug is a gray recliner. My owner sits in it practically all day, with her laptop in her lap. It's a nice chair. Sometimes when I need attention, I jump on the arm of it and nuzzle my owner with my nose. She doesn't pet me for long and typically ends up shooing me away.

On the wall opposite the couch and recliner is a big TV. It's either turned on to soap operas or crime shows almost all day long. It's dusty. She should really get off that recliner and dust it. Next to it is a white corner cabinet that has shiny glass things on it. I tried jumping up on it once. I got yelled at so loudly that I'll never do it again. 

It is a great-sized living room for a cat. It might be considered a little small for a human, but it feels comfortable and lived in, so I guess it's okay all in all.
