**Celsius (°C) | Description**
- **-20 to -30** – Arctic tundra or Antarctic winter; ice and snow dominate, with wind-chill making it colder.
- **-10 to -20** – Subarctic forests or high-altitude glaciers; frozen lakes, thick snow, occasional light winds.
- **-5 to 10** – Northern tundra, alpine regions, or mid-winter in temperate zones; occasional thaws, frost on ground.
- **0 to 5** – Spring thaw in northern climates or late winter in coastal areas; ice melting, snow cover receding.
- **5 to 15** – Coastal temperate zones (e.g., northern Europe, U.S. Pacific Northwest) or early spring in warm climates; mild but chilly, occasional rain.
- **15 to 20** – Mediterranean summer or coastal breezy climates (e.g., Southern California, Canary Islands); warm but not scorching, sea breezes.
- **20 to 25** – Tropical coastal regions (e.g., Southeast Asia, Caribbean) or summer in mid-latitudes; humid, occasional afternoon showers.
- **25 to 30** – Subtropical deserts (e.g., Arizona, Middle East) or tropical lowlands; dry heat, minimal cloud cover.
- **30 to 35** – Desert or semi-arid regions (e.g., Sahara, Australian outback); extreme dryness, high daytime temps.
- **35 to 40** – Tropical rainforests (e.g., Amazon, Congo) or monsoon climates; consistently hot, high humidity, frequent rain.
- **40+** – Polar deserts (e.g., Antarctica summer) or extreme high-altitude deserts (e.g., Atacama); rare, extreme heatwave conditions.
