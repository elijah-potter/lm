The smallest fossil dinosaur ever found is Oculudentavis khaungraae, which was discovered and described in early 2020. It was initially presented as a hummingbird-sized toothed bird but, after its reveal in Nature magazine in March 2020, fellow researchers pointed out that Oculudentavis was a small reptile. The fossil resembled a bird due to its large eye-opening and narrow, beak-like snout. A second fossil was presented later in 2020 which added evidence that Oculucentavis was a lizard.
