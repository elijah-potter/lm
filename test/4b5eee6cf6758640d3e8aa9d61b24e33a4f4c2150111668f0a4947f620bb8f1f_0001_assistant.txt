Mandarin Chinese, known as "Putonghua" in mainland China, is the world's most spoken language by native speakers. It originated from the North China Plain, near the capital Beijing. Over centuries, it spread and evolved with various dynasties, assimilating elements from other dialects and languages. After the establishment of the People's Republic of China in 1949, Mandarin was standardized and promoted nationwide to enhance communication and unity among different ethnic groups.
