**BREAKING: Major Flooding Hits Meadowgrove After Unpredictable Spring Storm**
Meadowgrove, CA – Residents are still recovering after a rare spring storm dumped nearly two feet of rain in just twelve hours last night, causing catastrophic flooding along the Silver Creek and Lake Meadowgrove spillways. Emergency crews report at least three confirmed deaths and dozens of missing individuals in the downtown and northside neighborhoods. Power outages stretch across the city, with roadblocks and debris blocking major arteries like State Route 121 and Meadowgrove Boulevard.

Mayor Elena Vasquez declared a state of emergency, ordering mandatory evacuations for the entire floodplain. The National Guard has been deployed to assist search-and-rescue teams, which are currently searching for survivors trapped in collapsed homes and flooded basements. The city’s water treatment plant remains operational but is overwhelmed, threatening contamination risks for residents relying on bottled water.

Authorities warn of potential secondary hazards, including downed power lines and gas leaks, as crews work to stabilize infrastructure. Schools and businesses closed for the day, with officials urging residents to stay indoors until further notice. The city’s emergency dispatch center is reporting overcrowding, with calls for medical assistance and road assistance overwhelming their systems.
