I understand how hard it is to keep acrylic paint fresh and useable, especially if one of your favorite colors dries out. If you store your paints correctly, they can last for years!

I have discovered that you can revive dried acrylic paint, and it only takes a few steps to do so! 

Get some warm tap water. Also, have an old brush on hand and a small container to mix the dried-up paint in. If you plan on reviving acrylic paint, you should use it immediately and not store it away in a container and forget about it. 

Mix the warm water with the dried paint inside the container. Use the brush to cover the paint with the water applied. It will need to sit for a few minutes.

You will notice the water will be colored by the paint. From here, add a little more water, but not too much or else you may wind up diluting the paint. The ideal ratio for mixing paint should be one part paint and one part water.

Alternatively, use a popsicle stick or a small wooden dowel to mix the paint if you don't have an old brush readily available. I hope these tips help!
