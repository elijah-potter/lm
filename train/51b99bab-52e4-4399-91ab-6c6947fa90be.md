A shadow moved across the dimly lit alley, its figure elongated by the flickering neon sign of a distant bar. The person paused near the alleyway’s edge, gripping the hoodie collar tightly, as if shielding themselves from the cold. Their breath fogged in the frigid air, and their eyes scanned the street—too familiar, yet off. A woman in a dark coat passed by, her back straight, her stride purposeful. She did not glance back.

The stranger watched her disappear into the crowd of passing commuters, their face half-hidden by the hood, the features indistinct, yet there was an echo of someone they knew. The streetlights blurred their silhouette, their posture stiff. Then, a car’s headlights cut through the gloom, reflecting off the pavement. The stranger turned away, their expression unreadable, as if the memory of a name they couldn’t name was burning too bright.
