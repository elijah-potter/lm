One morning, I noticed my coffee habit had spiraled into a dependency. Initially, it was just an afternoon pick-me-up, but the cravings intensified, and I found myself drinking more than usual to cope with stress. Over time, the habit began affecting my sleep, productivity, and relationships. The constant need for caffeine disrupted my daily routine, leading to anxiety when I couldn’t have it. I tried quitting, but the withdrawal made me irritable, and I relapsed. Now, the habit isn’t just about taste—it’s become a cycle of guilt and avoidance. I wonder if I’m trapped in a loop where the habit controls me more than I control myself.
