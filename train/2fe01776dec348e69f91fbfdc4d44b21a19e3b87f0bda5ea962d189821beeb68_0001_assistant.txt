Of course! Boba tea is a tasty beverage you're going to love. Here are five of the best flavors:

1. Taro Milk Tea: The best flavor of milk tea is Taro. It's a sweet purple root that tastes like a dessert yam, but it isn't overwhelming. It's a subtle, valuable flavor.
2. Brown Sugar Milk Tea: Brown sugar is one of the most popular flavors right now. It's sweet and fresh. Plus, the boba is cooked in brown sugar syrup to add to the flavor. It's also known as tiger stripe boba in some locations.
3. Black Milk Tea: This classic is the most basic of basic flavors, but not in a bad way. It's slightly creamy with a low-key flavor for an excellent sip every time. 
4. Thai Iced Tea: Thai iced tea is a little more flavorful. It's bright orange and sweet, spiced with cardamom, star anise, and more. It's sweetened with tasty condensed milk and sugar.
5. Strawberry Milk Tea: If you love berries, go for a strawberry milk tea option. It's made with strawberry powder or fresh strawberries, then sweetened with a creamer. It's usually thick and refreshing.
