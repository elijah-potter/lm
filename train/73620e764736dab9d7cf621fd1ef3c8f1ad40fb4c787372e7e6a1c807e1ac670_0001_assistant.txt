Sure! Here are a few options for a day at Niagara Falls:

1. Beers: End your day with beers at microbreweries and alehouses. You can also go on tours that combine wine, whiskey, and charcuterie boards.

2. Niagara Falls Underground Railroad Center: Learn about the history, politics, and obstacles faced by the people who settled in Niagara Falls.

3. Cave of the Winds: Don a hooded raincoat and brave the falls as you walk close to the foot of the thundering waters.
