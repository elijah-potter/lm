CNN, which means Cable News Network, became the world's first 24-hour TV news in 1980. It started filming in the state of Georgia. 

CNN changed the way people viewed the news. It changed the idea that news could only be reported at certain times during the day. When CNN started there were only three other big channels that had 30-minute news reports. 

CNN was made by Ted Turner. He was a loud businessman that was often called the "Mouth of the South". Ted Turner was born in 1938 in Ohio and moved to Georgia for his father's job. Ted Turner took over his father's advertising company in 1963. In 1970, he also bought a TV station that played old movies and shows. Ted Turner changed this channel into a "superstation" with the station being able to be seen by people all over the United States. Ted Turner also bought the Atlanta Braves baseball team and the Atlanta Hawks basketball team and played their games on one of his channels. He then got famous when he sailed his boat and won the important America's Cup race. 

When CNN first started, people made fun of it and called it the Chicken Noodle Network. Ted Turner ignored this and kept going to build up the news company. In 1983, he bought Satellite News Channel which was a main competitor for CNN. CNN became known to people by covering live events everywhere around the world. They were even able to cover these news events as they happened, which was quicker than many other news channels. It become more popular for it's news stories on the Persian Gulf War in 1991 and many people started watching. It grew even more popular during the 1990s and early 2000s.
