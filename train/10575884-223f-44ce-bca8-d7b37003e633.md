A fork in the road stood frozen, its branches gnarled and time-worn. The wind whispered through the leaves, carrying the scent of damp earth and distant rain. The path ahead was marked by a single, unyielding signpost—its message carved into stone: *"Turn left."* The weight of it pressed against the back of his neck, a physical presence, as if the world itself had decided for him.

He knew, without question, what he would do. The decision was not his to make—it was a given. The other path had already been sealed by something older than memory, something that had been waiting for this moment for as long as he could remember. The choice was not a choice at all; it was a surrender, a step into the familiar, the certain, the inevitable. The future unfolded before him like a script he had memorized, every line already written.

He took a breath and stepped forward. The earth shifted beneath his feet, but he did not falter. The world had spoken, and this was the answer. There was no hesitation, no second-guessing. Only the quiet certainty of a truth that had been known all along.
