On August 29, 2005, Hurricane Katrina hit New Orleans, Louisiana Gulf Coast as a Category 3 hurricane. Three hours after landfall, levees at the Lower Ninth Ward, Industrial Canal, and 17th Street Canal were breached. One day later, approximately 80% of the city of New Orleans was covered in water, in some places as high as 20 feet. The U.S. Army Corp of Engineers repaired the breach to the levees. By September 5th the 17th Street Canal levee was repaired and the Corp of Engineers began to repair the London Avenue Canal levee. At that time they began pumping the water out of the city. I do not have the information on how long it took for the water to be completely drained from the city.
