Rolling Homes: A Journey of the Working Homeless

"Rolling Homes" is an eye-opening documentary that shines a spotlight on America's "working homeless" population, who have turned to living in RV encampments across the nation. From small business owners and prison guards to Amazon workers, these individuals have chosen an unconventional lifestyle off the grid due to affordable housing shortages. However, their presence has sparked debates among locals, who perceive them as a blight on their communities. This compelling film explores the struggles, resilience, and the clash of perspectives between the working homeless and their neighbors, shedding light on a complex issue that continues to challenge society.
