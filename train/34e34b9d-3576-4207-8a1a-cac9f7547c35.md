The barista handed me my coffee, his voice clipped. *"I’ll have that latte with extra foam, and a shot of espresso if you’re feeling generous."* He didn’t wait for a response, just nodded toward the counter. *"Promise."*

I leaned in, close enough to smell the burnt sugar and the faint hint of aftershave. *"You really mean that?"*

He grinned, but it didn’t reach his eyes. *"Depends. What’s the catch?"*

*"I’ll pay for the latte."* I tapped the billfold in my back pocket. *"But I want the extra foam and the espresso. No tricks."*
