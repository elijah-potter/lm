Steve was a bit different than his fellow zebrafish. Together, they filled up a pond and carried out their days swimming, eating, and showing off their beautiful stripes. In Steve's mind, this kind of life was just okay. He wasn't fully satisfied with his aquatic world, though. He longed to stand up on his fins and walk right out of the water. 
Little did Steve know that this was a pipe dream. While Steve was living in a fantasy world, his fellow zebrafish were well aware of their limitations. 
One day, Steve shared his dream with another fish named Lonnie. Lonnie was happy about Steve's ambition, but he had to do the difficult task of breaking the news to Steve. Steve's gills and lack of legs would make it impossible for him to ever live a life on land. 
When Lonnie shared this harsh truth with Steve, Steve's whole perspective on his little world changed. He was no longer the bright and chipper fish he once was. He now had very little to look forward to in the pond.
