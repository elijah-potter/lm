Samantha decided to go on a yoga retreat to Costa Rica after she had experienced a horrible year. She lost her job, she was dumped by her boyfriend, and she lost her only vehicle - a reliable Toyota SUV - to an unexpected collision on a busy highway. Now, Samantha looked forward to cleaning her slate and starting the new year off on the right foot.

As Samantha fought through her tears while reminiscing about the tough year she had, she booked her flight to Costa Rica online, and she also reserved her place in the yoga retreat for the day after she arrived in the country. She had packed her bags and went to the airport, going through the security screening and taking three hours before finally getting to relax in her seat on the plane. She looked at the screen on the headrest in front of her and saw the flight time: 7 hours, 14 minutes.

7 hours, 14 minutes. She kept thinking about how it would only be a little over 7 hours before she would be relaxing in the sun and enjoying a stress-free vacation in Costa Rica. 7 hours, 14 minutes. The plane took off.

Then, it dawned on her. A flight to Costa Rica shouldn't take 7 hours and 14 minutes. Furthermore, a flight to Costa Rica shouldn't be traveling south from her location. It was then that Samantha realized she was on the wrong flight, and she was traveling to Puerto Rico. Her mind flashed back to the night she booked the flight online, attempting to click her flight option with tears in her eyes. She had mistaken Puerto Rico for Costa Rica, and now she was on a non-stop, 7-hour, 14-minute flight to the wrong location. This was just the topping on a horrible year.
