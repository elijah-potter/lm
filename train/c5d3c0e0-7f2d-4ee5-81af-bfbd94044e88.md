The journey began at a crossroads where the first road led to an abandoned mill, its gears silent since the last river turned dry. The second forked toward a crumbling castle with a door that opened only for those who whispered *"where are you going?"* in a language no one understood.

At the mill, the wind carried a note: *"The destination is the place where you’ve been waiting, but it’s not what you expected."* The castle’s stone walls pulsed with shifting patterns, and the door vanished into the mist, reappearing a mile away—each time leading deeper into a labyrinth of ever-changing paths.

A third road emerged, this one winding through fields where crops grew in impossible shapes—spiral patterns, fractals of light and shadow. A voice (or was it the wind?) said, *"You’re not lost; you’re being led."* The paths dissolved into mist, reformed as rivers, then as bridges, each carrying the traveler farther but always toward something unseen.

The final fork was a mirror—reflecting the traveler as they were at the start, but the reflection’s eyes were empty. The mirror shattered into fragments that scattered like stars, each leading to a different "end": a black hole that sucked in time, a forest where trees spoke in dead languages, or a desert where the sand whispered secrets of futures that never were.

The journey ended where it began—at the crossroads—but the crossroads had changed. Now, it split into six paths, all identical at first glance. One led to a door labeled *"You."* The others led to places where the traveler’s own name was carved into stone, and the traveler had to answer: *"Do you know where you’re going?"* before the stone shifted to reveal the next loop.

The last note found in the ruins was: *"The destination is the answer to a question you haven’t asked yet."*
