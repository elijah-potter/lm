An overview of current practices
In this chapter, we discuss global resource sharing practices in the early 21st century. Tips for successful international borrowing and lending are given.
Resource sharing plays a crucial role in education, democracy, economic growth, health and welfare and personal development. It facilitates access to a wide range of information, which would not otherwise be available to the user, library or country requesting it.
