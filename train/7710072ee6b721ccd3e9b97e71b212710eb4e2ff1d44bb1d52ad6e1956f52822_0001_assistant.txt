Today humanity finally got an answer to the age-old question, are we alone in the Universe? A large red spacecraft landed just off the coast in central Florida. At first many believed it to be a hoax or a publicity stunt of some kind, but that was quickly put to bed as several civilians in the area were vaporized when their reaction to the visitors was to pull out their firearms. 
A few vaporizations were enough to send the remaining populace running from the scene. The aliens, tall and orange with glowing pink eyes, predictably asked for our leader. With the president out of the country, local authorities decided the Governor of Florida would have to do. The Governor agreed to the meeting. He was led by his secret service onto the alien's ship. 
It is unknown at this time what was discussed during this meeting, but it ended shortly after it began with one of the aliens throwing the governor out of the ship, head first with the secret service running swiftly behind. The alien ship then took off and flew out into space and out of sight. Sources close to the governor have stated that the aliens decided Earth was not worth pursuing after meeting with its residents. 
This story will be updated as more information becomes available.
