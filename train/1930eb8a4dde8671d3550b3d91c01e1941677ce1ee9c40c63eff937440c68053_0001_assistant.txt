There are quite a few budget-friendly restaurant options that are also gluten-free near the Newport Aquarium. Here are a few:

1. Tom and Chee - This restaurant offers gluten-free bread for their grilled cheese sandwiches and gluten-free soup options. Their prices are relatively inexpensive, with most items under $10

2. Newport Pizza Company - This restaurant offers gluten-free salads and appetizers. The prices are reasonable, with most pizzas priced around $15.

3. Dewey's Pizza - This restaurant offers gluten-free crusts for pizza. The prices are fairly reasonable, with pizza being between $12 and $20.
