After making it safely to your car, you lock the doors. Even though the mannequins claw to try to get in, they are unable to. Panting, you pull your cell phone out of your back pocket and dial your best friend, Lois Lane. "Lois!" you scream when she picks up the phone. "Can you get in touch with Superman any time you want? PLEASE, I need help! I was shopping, and these mannequins came alive, and now I'm trapped in my car, and they're trying to get in!" Lois tells you to just breathe and that she will get Superman there right away. Before you've had time to put your cell phone down, you see a swirling of blue and red as each evil mannequin disappears one by one. When they are all gone, Superman appears at your door. Still frightened, you only lower the window part of the way. "Ma'am, I'm sorry about what happened here today. I took each one of those evil-doers into outer space and pulverized them. They are gone and will trouble you no more!" "Thanks, Superman!" you say. Once again, all is right with your world.
