As Fred shuffled toward the window of his moldy old kitchen, a shaft of sunlight split the musty air and landed on his haggard complexion. "Another glorious day, what a shame," Fred grumbled, for Fred was the grumpiest old man that lived on Salty Lane. He knew he had to visit the store that day, but the thought of having to interact with all the happy people in the town made him sick. Opening his grimy fridge to see the empty shelves, he knew he had no other option.  He would have to face the world outside. As Fred made his way along the lane, he was sure to keep his scowl firmly in place as a warning to the neighbors not to approach him. When he eventually reached the store, he couldn't believe his eyes! In front of him stood a woman, equally as haggard and grouchy. An unfamiliar sensation grew across Fred's face.  It was a smile, albeit a crooked one.
