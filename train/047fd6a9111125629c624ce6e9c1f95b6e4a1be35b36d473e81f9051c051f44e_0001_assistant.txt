Harold got up one morning, took a shower, brushed his teeth, and did everything he does on a normal day. However, he was soon to find out that it wouldn't be a normal day at all. As he was about to leave the door, he heard a buzz from his phone, a notification that he'd received a text. He didn't recognize the number, and the message was quite cryptic. 

"Hello, Harold. It's time." 

Time for what? He wasn't entirely sure and wanted to respond that they had the wrong number, but that seemed unlikely.

"Time for what? Who are you?" he fired back, too distracted to leave the door. He got a response almost immediately. 

"It's time."

Just that same message. He wrote it off as a prank or some kind of trick before rushing out the door to get to work, only to find everything was gone. The roads were empty and the city was utterly silent in a way he had never heard it before. He attempted to call a friend, only to find he had no phone service. Somehow, his phone buzzed with another text. 

"It's time."
