Happy within is a children’s book that will help children from around the world learn, appreciate self-love and embrace diversity.
The idea behind the book is to help provide children with the support and confidence to be happy with themselves and within their own skin. All kids are beautiful, no matter where in the world they are from, whether the race, the background, etc. The book includes a diverse range of characters with the message that we should all love ourselves and support one another to be the best we can be.
It is a positive children’s picture book teaching them to be proud of themselves, proud of who they are and of their uniqueness. Only through self-love and believing in oneself it is possible to be happy within.
With this bilingual book they will learn:
- Basic vocabulary and first words in English & Brazilian Portuguese
- To read in English and Portuguese
- About the beauty of diversity
- An excellent bilingual resource to teach & learn Brazilian Portuguese
- Ideal for kids between the ages of 3-6
- A fantastic resource for schools, teachers & educators in Portuguese speaking countries.
- A fantastic resource for schools, teachers & educators with Portuguese as part of their curriculum
- 30+ pages of diverse and fun illustrations to get the interest of little children.
- A beautifully illustrated picture book in English & Portuguese (Brazilian) to help develop your child’s bilingual ability.
- The pages with diverse and inclusive characters give parents, caregivers and educators the opportunity to talk to children about multiculturalism.
- Ideal for Brazilian Portuguese kids
The book is currently available in 20 other languages. Teaching kids that they are beautiful at a young age is so important that I wanted to publish the book in various languages to help spread the word globally. This book is ideal for children between the ages of 3-6.
