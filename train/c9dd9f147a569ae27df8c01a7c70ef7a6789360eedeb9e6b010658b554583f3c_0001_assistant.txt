1. If you're looking for Instagram-ready clear-water beaches, then you might want to try the Phi Phi Islands, James Bond Island or Hong Island. Spend the day taking selfies and running around trying to get attention. 
2. Visit the Ethical Elephant Sanctuary and spend a day with the elephants or visit Khao Sok National Park. If there is one thing at stake here, it's ethics. A sanctuary is one step up from a zoo, so there is no need to protest or cancel this activity. 
3. If you're interested in seeing a show, you can go to Phuket and see the Siam Niramit show or Muay Thai fights at Patong Boxing Stadium. If you're up for a different kind of show, you can make a stop at the Ping Pong show on the walking street in Phuket. You can't unsee some things. 
4. You can take a private tour of Phuet and visit some of the amazing landmarks Thailand has to offer. Make sure to take some more selfies as you are visiting some of the most amazing sights in Thailand. Don't forget to "like" and "follow." 
5. You can take a tour of the Andaman Coast where you can raft on some rapids, visit the Monkey Cave and ride around on an ATV. Visit the Monkey Cave, hit some whitewater rapids, Zipline and zoom around on an ATV. Don't forget to wear a helmet and be careful around the monkeys. They bite!
