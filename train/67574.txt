Pneumonia is a disease of the respiratory system that can be caused by various etiological factors. In the affected area are the respiratory parts of the lungs. The risk of developing pathological changes in the parenchymal tissue is increased in babies with a history of cardiovascular ailments and immunodeficiency.
In the treatment of pneumonia in children , medications and physiotherapy procedures are used in parallel with folk remedies. The condition of a child suffering from pneumonia is greatly aggravated by hypothermia and overheating of the body. Based on the modern classification, pneumonia can be secondary and primary. In any case, the pathogenesis is due to the following factors:
- Infectious contamination.
- The penetration of pathogens into the bronchi.
- A sharp decrease in immunity.
- An increase in the amount of bronchial secretions, leading to the formation of sputum.
Pneumonia provokes disruptions in gas exchange and ventilation. The list of complications includes partial dysfunction of the ventricular myocardium, hypercapnia and hypoxia. The clinic depends on the type of ailment, the affected area, the nature of the course and the existing chronic diseases. Most often, the following symptoms occur:
- rough shallow breathing;
- general malaise;
- Strong headache;
- discomfort in the chest area;
During the diagnostic examination, the doctor reveals a shortening of the percussion sound, increased ESR, bronchophonia , crepitus, neutrophilic leukocytosis.

A set of breathing exercises
Respiratory gymnastics must be included in the therapeutic scheme for pneumonia. This is due to the fact that the inflammatory process in the lungs disrupts the work of the bronchi and alveoli. Due to respiratory failure, an imbalance of gas exchange develops, which provokes a decrease in the functionality of the respiratory system. Through exercise, muscles are strengthened, immune barriers are raised, and lung tissue is cleansed. Thus, they prevent the occurrence of serious complications of pneumonia.
Varieties of compresses
Pneumonia in children is often treated with compresses. The medicinal compounds on the basis of which they are made are prepared from plantain, natural honey, apple cider vinegar, and sauerkraut. There are many possible variations. The rules for the procedure are quite simple:
- Compresses should not be done at elevated body temperature.
- There should be a cotton cloth or gauze between the skin and the medicinal composition. This will avoid burns and allergic reactions when treating pneumonia.
- To achieve a positive result, you must follow the recommendations of your doctor.
