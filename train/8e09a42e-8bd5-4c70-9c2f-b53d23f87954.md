**BREAKING:** Mayor’s Office Announces Emergency Water Restrictions in Lakeside as Reservoir Levels Drop Below Critical Threshold
Lakeside, March 15th – The City of Lakeside has issued a mandatory Level 2 water restrictions effective immediately, citing severe drought conditions that have reduced the City’s primary reservoir, Lake Harmony, to just **48% capacity** after two consecutive years of below-average rainfall. Residents are prohibited from using outdoor irrigation for non-essential purposes, including garden watering, and must limit household water use to **10 gallons per person per day** for non-emergency activities.

**Public Water Supply Alert:** The city’s water treatment facility, which relies on Lake Harmony’s inflow, has reported potential contamination risks due to elevated sediment levels. Municipal officials urge residents to filter all tap water before consumption. Schools and businesses have been advised to implement **30-minute daily showers** and **no-fix leaks** policies.

Local farmers have expressed frustration, warning of crop failures in the coming harvest season unless further relief is secured. The mayor’s office has activated the Emergency Water Task Force to explore alternative sources, including **desalination plant expansions** and **intercity water transfer negotiations**. Protests outside City Hall yesterday turned violent after a water truck was vandalized by a small group of activists.

**Government Response:** President of Lakeside Council, Elena Vasquez, has directed all departments to prioritize water conservation and has ordered a **mandatory 20-minute shower limit** for residential households. The city’s public works department has also begun **manual pressure testing** for all residential pipes to identify leaks before they escalate.

**Community Advisory:** Authorities have distributed **emergency water ration cards** to all households, with priority given to seniors, families with young children, and low-income residents. The city’s water utility, Lakeside Aqua, has also launched a **$500,000 fund** to subsidize bottled water deliveries for vulnerable populations.
