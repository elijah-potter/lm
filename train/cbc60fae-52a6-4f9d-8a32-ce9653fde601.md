**Caffeine Dependency: Morning Rituals and Afternoon Withdrawal**
Caffeine, a central nervous system stimulant, binds to adenosine receptors, delaying fatigue. Many develop reliance on its effects—jitters, focus enhancement, and stress relief. Breaking free requires gradual reduction: start with one less cup, track sleep quality, and replace half the intake with herbal tea or water. Withdrawal symptoms (headaches, fatigue) signal dependence, but long-term benefits—improved sleep, reduced anxiety—justify the effort. Mindfulness and structured routines help manage cravings without relapse.
