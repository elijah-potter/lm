Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around his big green meadow. Benny had a soft fluffy tail that bounced every time he played. One sunny morning, Benny found a shiny red apple under a big, fluffy cloud. Benny was so happy he hopped right up to it and tried to eat it. But oh no! The apple was too big and squishy to fit in his tiny mouth. He giggled and hopped away.

Benny met a kind old owl named Ollie who lived in a tree. Ollie had big, wise eyes and a warm voice. "What’s wrong, little one?" Ollie asked gently. Benny told him about the apple. Ollie smiled and said, "Don’t worry, Benny. Let’s find you something better to eat."

Ollie flew down to Benny’s meadow and found a bunch of juicy clover. He handed them to Benny. Benny sniffed the clover and said, "Mmm, this is so much better!" Benny ate a few bites and then hopped happily around the meadow, playing with his friends—Lily the lamb, Max the mouse, and Daisy the duck.

As the sun began to set, Benny felt sleepy. Ollie carried him back to his cozy burrow under a bush. Benny curled up, snuggled by his soft blanket, and fell asleep with a smile on his face. Benny dreamed of more adventures and happy days in his meadow.
