Here are some Zambian footballers to learn about.
#1 - Enock Mwepu plays for three teams and is the captain of the Zambian national team in the defensive midfield position. He also was transferred to the English Premier League and is valued at €10 million.
#2 - Patson Daka debuted on the Zambian national team at just 16 years old. Now he plays striker for Leicester City after he signed a £23 million deal. 
#3 - Lameck Banda signed to a Russian team which could have had significant personal effects on his and his family's lives. Then he moved to Serie A club U.S. Lecce which was a new club.
#4 - While Godfrey Chitalu isn't an active player, he may still be a valuable player to research because of his bizarre story. He had a record of 116 goals, but FIFA oddly rejected his record placement.
#5 - Emmanuel Banda plays central midfielder for the Djurgårdens team. Strangely, his market value is lower than other top-level Zambian pros.
