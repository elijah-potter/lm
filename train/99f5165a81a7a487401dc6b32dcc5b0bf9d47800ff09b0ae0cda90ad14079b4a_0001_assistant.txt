I stayed at the luxury hotel called the IwaConHotel! I would rate it 5 stars, no question about it. 

Let me tell you about the process. I walked in and was immediately greeted by the nicest clerks imaginable. They took my bags and luggage up to my rooms free of charge. After I was checked in, they have a clerk personally guide me to my room. Along the way, he told me about all the amenities that were offered here. I was amazed by how much this hotel has to offer. Gym rooms, sauna, personal chefs, game lounge, free Uber services for hotel patrons, a movie room, free drinks, and so much more! I cannot remember all of them. The nice hotel clerk led me up to my room and implored me to call if there was anything they could help with. They were all so nice.

When I opened the room, it felt like I had a personal party for myself! The pure white beds, the humongous television on the wall, the classic decor. Aah, it was all so amazing! I took a second to also take in the stunning view from the window. It feels like I could see the entire island from this room. They offered 5-star room service 24/7, so can't beat that as well. The next thing to do was to explore the rest of the hotel. I want to see if there was any hidden gems around!

Walking around, there was so much to take in. The unlimited buffet was astonishing. I ate so much I would probably faint if I took another bite. I went to the movie room to hang out for an hour or so. They had so many choices from the latest releases. After the movie, I was able to take an Uber, free of charge, to the heart of the island for a party! I had a lot of fun, I got to dance and everything! We rode back, and I could only think about sleeping after an exciting day. I don't think it's possible to rate this hotel anything less than 5 stars. Thank you for the amazing time!
