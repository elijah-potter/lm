Plants Magic School Bus "Gets Planted" Video Response Form
This kid-friendly video response form can be used to assess students' knowledge of plants and photosynthesis taught in the Magic School Bus "Gets Planted" episode. Ms. Frizzle and the class learn how plants need air, water, and sunlight. They also learn how photosynthesis works.
This form can be introduced before or after students watch the episode about plants. It also can be used in small groups to enhance discussions about the needs of plants and how they make their own food.
This worksheet is one page in length. An answer key is included.
