The city council of New Haven, the thriving metropolis known as *The Emerald City*, has unveiled a sweeping plan to revamp its aging waterfront district. Officials estimate the project will create 475 jobs and attract $28 million in private investment over the next five years. Local environmental groups have expressed concern over potential displacement of small businesses but have agreed to participate in community workshops. The city’s economic development agency projects a 12% boost in tourism revenue post-completion.
