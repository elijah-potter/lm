**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, Ill. – August 15, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, promising a celebration of agriculture, community, and family-friendly entertainment. This year’s fair will showcase the region’s rich agricultural heritage, featuring livestock exhibits, demonstrations of traditional crafts, and a wide variety of locally sourced food vendors. Visitors can expect a vibrant atmosphere with live music, thrilling rides, and engaging activities for all ages.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 22nd to August 28th, 2024, at the Sangamon County Fairgrounds. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM. A special fireworks display will illuminate the sky on Saturday, August 26th, following the evening concert. Daily admission includes access to all exhibits and demonstrations, though certain attractions, such as the carnival midway, require separate tickets.

Tickets are available for purchase online at SangamonCountyFair.org and at the gate during the fair. General admission is $12 for adults, $8 for children ages 5-12, and free for children four and under. Season passes, offering unlimited entry for the duration of the fair, are available at a discounted rate. Parking is $10 per vehicle.

**Headline Events:**

*   Grand Champion Livestock Auction
*   Demolition Derby
*   Professional Rodeo Series
*   Blue Ribbon Baking Competition
*   Saturday Night Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters
*   Agricultural Innovation Showcase
