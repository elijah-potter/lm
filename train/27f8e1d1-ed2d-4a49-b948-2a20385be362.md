**Character stands alone in a dimly lit room, fingers tracing the edge of an open journal.**

*The wind outside howls like a question no one dares answer.* The ink on the page is smudged with unspoken words—*"They’ll never understand,"* *"I’m not enough,"*—but the last entry is torn out, crumpled beneath the coffee stain. A half-empty glass of whiskey glints in the flickering lamp. The clock on the wall ticks like a heartbeat too slow.

**Voice, raw and fractured:** *"I don’t know if I’m the villain or the victim. Maybe it doesn’t matter. The only thing that matters is the weight of silence."* The door creaks open, but no one steps through. Just the sound of their own breath, sharp and shallow. The mirror above the fireplace reflects not their face, but a stranger’s—one who watches, judgmental, and *knowing*.

**A whisper:** *"What if the truth isn’t in the words you write, but the ones you leave unsaid?"* The page burns.
