To make a candle you need three main components (wax, wick, and container) and follow these three steps:

1. Put the wick in the container you're going to use.
2. Heat the wax on the stove in a pot until it's melted. Add color and fragrance if you'd like.
3. Pour the hot wax into your container while using skewers or popsicle sticks to hold the wick in place. After the wax has completely cooled, trim the wick to an appropriate size.
