Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a tiny mouse named Milo. One sunny morning, Milo woke up and saw his favorite toy, a shiny red ball, sitting under his bed. He picked it up and played with it, rolling it around the house.

Just then, a big, fluffy cat named Whiskers came out of the closet. Whiskers had big green eyes and a long tail that swished back and forth. Milo jumped back, his heart pounding. "Don’t chase me, Whiskers!" he said softly.

Whiskers looked at Milo with kind eyes. "I’m sorry, Milo. I just want to play too!" she said and stretched out her paws. Milo felt a little scared but also happy to see Whiskers was friendly. Together, they rolled the red ball in circles around the house.

Later, Milo felt brave. "Can you teach me to catch the ball?" he asked. Whiskers nodded and showed Milo how to pounce and grab. Soon, Milo was catching the ball perfectly! Whiskers purred happily and gave Milo a tiny treat from her basket.

After that, Milo and Whiskers became the best of friends. They played together every day, and Milo learned that even if something looks scary, it might just want to play and be friends. And that’s how Milo and Whiskers found the most wonderful adventure in their little house.
