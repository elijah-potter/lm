I'm not sure where I am. This room resembles that of study. I recognize what appear to be books arranged upon wooden shelves, so this room must belong to a noble of high prestige. But beyond that, everything else is inexplicable. There is no fireplace nor are there candles to provide light. Instead, the room is brightly lit by strange glowing tubes affixed upon the ceilings. Could this be some bizarre plant life from some faraway land? Yet that still does not explain everything else in this room. The study desk appears to be wooden, yet it feels nothing like the wood I've worked with in my lifetime. And upon this desk are two strange objects. One of them is a small black box with strange symbols on top of several rows of buttons, connected by a string to an odd handle-shaped object with two spherical objects at each end. When I lifted this handle, a sudden sound came out from the spherical objects, startling me into dropping it. Perhaps this is a trap or alarm system of some sort? The symbols must guard some sort of great secret or an object of great value. Much warier now, I dared not touch the larger set of buttons next to the small black square. It seems to be connected to a rectangular metal tower on the floor, which is emitting a small hum. This rectangular object is then connected to a glowing square on the desk next to the small black box. I begin to suspect it may be sustained by sorcery. I must escape from this strange place before the noble wizard returns and finds me trespassing. Too many inscrutable secrets lie here, far beyond my comprehension.
