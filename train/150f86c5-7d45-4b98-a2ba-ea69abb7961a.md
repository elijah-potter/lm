Whispering Pines: A mist-shrouded forest with ancient oaks and gnarled roots where the wind carries secrets. The air hums with unseen voices—some human, others lost to time. Enchanted glades echo with faint murmurs, while the stillest boughs sway with whispers that vanish before reaching ears. The only light comes from flickering campfires in the distance, their flames dancing like ghostly fingers tracing the shadows of the trees.
