Mandy has three $20 bills while Manny has two $50 bills. If both of them decide to exchange their bills for $10 bills, how many more $10 bills than Mandy will Manny have?
Mandy has a total of $20 x 3 = $<<20*3=60>>60.
So, she will have $60/$10 = <<60/10=6>>6 $10 bills.
Manny has a total of $50 x 2 = $<<50*2=100>>100.
So, he will have $100/$10 = <<100/10=10>>10 $10 bills.
Hence, Manny has 10 - 6 = <<10-6=4>>4 more $10 bills than Mandy.
