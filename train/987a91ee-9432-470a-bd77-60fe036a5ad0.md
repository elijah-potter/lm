Two detectives sat across from each other in the dimly lit interrogation room, the fluorescent lights casting long shadows.

**Detective A** leaned back in their chair, fingers steepled. *"The witness says she saw the suspect drop the evidence bag near the alley. The angle of the light suggested it was deliberate—someone was trying to hide it. The bag was empty when they picked it up. No prints. The suspect was wearing gloves."*

**Detective B** scowled, arms crossed. *"The same witness claims she saw a third person—someone in a dark coat—running away after the suspect. The alley was dark, but she swore she saw movement. The bag wasn’t empty when the suspect retrieved it. Fingerprints? Nah, but the suspect had a lot of excuses for gloves. And the coat? That’s not suspicious unless you’re looking for it."*

A pause. The air between them thickened.

**Detective A** adjusted their glasses. *"We’ll run prints on the gloves, check the coat’s wear pattern. But the bag’s placement? That’s still a motive."*

**Detective B** smirked. *"Motive’s overrated. Let’s find who actually dropped the bag—and who’s covering up the *real* evidence."*
