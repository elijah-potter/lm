When the truth was spoken, it cut like a blade through the silence of the room. The words—unfiltered, raw—slipped from the lips of the younger man, his hands trembling slightly as he exhaled them into the air. His mother’s voice, usually steady, faltered when he said, *"You lied to me."* The accusation hung in the space between them, heavy with betrayal and grief. The older woman’s eyes burned with tears as she realized the extent of the deception: years of omission, of keeping secrets that had festered into something more than a mistake.

Outside, the city lights blurred through the window, indifferent to the storm inside. But the truth, once uttered, could not be unspoken. The younger man stood there, his chest heavy, knowing this was the first step toward healing—or perhaps toward the end of a life built on lies. The room felt smaller. The air was thick with the weight of what had been hidden for so long. The choice had been made, and now, the consequences followed.
