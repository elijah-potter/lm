The pile of dishes stood before Tim, the hero of the kitchen. Pots filled with crusty sauce, pans with leftover sticky spaghetti, and worst of all, the chocolate-stained dish. They seemed to taunt him, towering over him. Tim whipped out his trusty sponge and tossed a towel over his shoulder to form a makeshift cape. Today, the evil dishes would be defeated.

He scrubbed and scraped, weaponizing the scalding hot water against the stubborn stains. His ally, dish soap, came to the rescue, cutting down on grease as he scrubbed and scrubbed. Eventually, after a long battle, the dishes were defeated. There was no rest for the wicked; next, Tim had to tackle the monster that was his room.
