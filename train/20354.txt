Did you know synthetic caffeine was first developed by the Nazis during WWII! Embargoes prevented them from obtaining various goods (caffeine included), and a synthesized version was created to keep supplies available. Since that time demands for caffeine have steadily risen and synthetic caffeine is now the norm, especially in soda and energy drinks. Today, many consumers are unable to identify where the caffeine in their food has come from - or are even aware that a synthetic version exists.
