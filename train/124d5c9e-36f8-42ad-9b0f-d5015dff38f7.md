**First Ending:**
The village elder’s prophecy was fulfilled when the lost child’s shadow split into two—one ascending toward the northern stars, the other descending into the river’s depths. The elder, trembling, whispered, *"The first is the seed of the forgotten god, the second, the curse of the drowned king."* The child, now two, vanished into the mist, leaving only whispers of two paths: one leading to forgotten ruins where time bends, the other to a cave where the river sings in tongues no human tongue could hear.

**Second Ending:**
The hunter’s trap caught the two wolves mid-battle—the one with golden fur, the other with black, its eyes burning like embers. The hunter, pitying them, placed a single blade between them. *"You were never meant to fight,"* he said. The wolves closed their jaws, and the blade dissolved into mist. The hunter found them both alive the next dawn, curled together in the snow, paws touching. The golden one licked his paw, and the black one purred. *"We were never meant to be alone,"* the hunter realized, as the wolves vanished into the wolves. The last thing he saw was the golden one’s paw twining with the black’s.
