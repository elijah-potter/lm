What is a business day?
A business day is any day of the week that a business or organization operates between certain hours.
This will usually include standard 9-5 hours, but some organizations may have different set hours. Business days are typically Monday thru Friday and exclude any holidays or weekends.
While business days may vary from organization to organization, they are generally not considered to be extended past a specific timeframe. For example, a business day might not include activities after 5 pm or before 9 am.
Additionally, most organizations will not schedule activities on Saturday and Sunday as those days are considered weekends.

Business days in the US
In the US, a business day is typically defined as Monday through Friday, excluding national holidays.
Standard business hours for most industries are from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, with a one-hour lunch break around noon. However, specific business hours may vary depending on the industry, location, and time zone.
Due to the time zone differences, business hours may start earlier or later in certain regions to accommodate customers or clients in different time zones.
For example, a business in Los Angeles may open at 8:00 am Pacific Time to accommodate East Coast customers three hours ahead in the Eastern Time Zone.
Conversely, a business in New York may stay open until 6:00 pm Eastern Time to accommodate customers on the West Coast who are three hours behind in the Pacific Time Zone.
Therefore, understanding the standard business hours and time zone differences is crucial for conducting business within the US efficiently and effectively.

Business days internationally
Internationally, business day hours vary significantly across different countries and regions.
For example, in some countries, the standard business day may begin earlier and end earlier than in others. Some countries may also have different workweeks, with Saturday or Sunday being a regular workday.
In Europe, the standard business day is typically from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, with a one-hour lunch break around noon.
However, countries such as Spain and Italy may have a longer lunch break, with businesses closing for several hours in the afternoon and reopening in the evening.
In Asia, the standard business day may start earlier and end later, with some businesses staying open until 9:00 pm or later. In the Middle East, the workweek typically runs from Sunday to Thursday, with Friday and Saturday being the weekend.
How long is a business day?
The length of a business day can vary depending on the country and industry.
In the United States, a typical business day is from Monday to Friday, and it usually lasts for 8 hours, typically from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.
However, some businesses may have different operating hours, such as starting earlier or closing later.
Internationally, business day hours may differ depending on the country's culture, traditions, and regulations.
For example, in some European countries, the workweek may include Saturdays, and businesses may have shorter workdays, such as 6 hours. In contrast, some countries in Asia may have longer workdays, such as 10 hours or more.
Additionally, some industries may have longer or shorter business days. Overall, the length of a business day can vary significantly depending on the country and industry.
How long is a business week?
A business week, most of the time, is the same as a standard workweek, which is 40 hours in the United States.
A business week generally includes Monday through Friday, with employees working eight hours daily.
However, some businesses may have different work schedules, such as a 4-day workweek or a flexible schedule that allows employees to work from home or choose their hours.
Internationally, the length of a business week may differ based on the country's laws and regulations.
For example, some countries may have shorter workweeks, such as 35 or 36 hours, to promote work-life balance and improve productivity. Others may have longer workweeks, such as 48 hours or more.
A business week may not necessarily align with the number of business days in a week.
For example, if a company has a 4-day workweek, their business week may be Tuesday through Friday instead of Monday through Thursday. Similarly, some businesses may have employees work on weekends, affecting their business week.

Business day vs. work day
While "business day" and "workday" are often used interchangeably, they have different meanings.
A business day is when most businesses and government offices are open and operational. In contrast, a workday is when an individual is scheduled to work.
For example, a business day may be from Monday to Saturday, but an employee's workdays may be from Wednesday to Saturday. In this case, the employee's workdays fall within the broader definition of a business day.
Additionally, the length of a workday may vary depending on the industry or employer. For example, some industries may have longer workdays, such as the healthcare industry.
They may require employees to work 12-hour shifts. Understanding the difference between a business day and a workday is crucial to scheduling and planning effectively.
Business days vs. business hours
While "business day" and "business hours" may seem similar, they have different meanings.
A business day is when most businesses and government offices are open and operational. In contrast, business hours refer to the specific hours of operation for a business on a given day.
For example, a business may operate from Monday to Friday, considered the business days. However, the business may have specific business hours, such as from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, during which it is open to customers.
It is important to note that the business hours may differ from one business to another, even within the same industry.
For example, two retail stores in the same shopping mall may have different business hours based on individual preferences and customer demand.
