Sharon loved making her home smell good, and always received compliments from guests who visited. She would buy a couple of boxes of scented wax melts a week, and she used them every day. She would turn on her wax burner as soon as she entered the house. 

She kept diving deeper into finding her favorite scents available online and began ordering more and more packs of scented wax melts. Sharon’s mail delivery lady began to get annoyed with how many packages she had to deliver. Sharon began running out of room, boxes began to stack higher and higher. The company that made her wax melter specifically stated on the packaging box that it should not be left on when no one would be around. Sharon left for work one day and decided to keep the burner on, so her house would smell extra nice. 

While she was at work, the burner’s cord emitted sparks, lighting the surrounding books on fire. The fire spread rapidly.
