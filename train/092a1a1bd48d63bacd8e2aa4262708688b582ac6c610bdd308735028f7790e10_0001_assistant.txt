Mike Tyson, widely regarded as one of the greatest heavyweight boxers of all time, was known for his intense aura during the pinnacle of his career. However, contrary to expectations, Tyson's life outside the ring was marked by an overwhelming fear of everything. This unexpected aspect of his personality added a fragile dimension to his character. Among the many intriguing facets of his life, one that stands out is his ownership of a pet tiger. Yes, you read that right, despite his inherent timidity, Mike Tyson actually had a tiger as a companion, showcasing his peculiar and audacious nature. However, this was just the tip of the iceberg; rumors swirled expressing Tyson's desire to take on a gorilla in combat! Upon being asked about it, however, Tyson expressed an overwhelming fear of gorillas and had no desire to take them on in combat.
