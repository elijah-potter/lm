**BREAKING: Mysterious Light Phenomena in Eldermere’s Skies Forces Citywide Evacuation Alerts**
Eldermere, a historic coastal town known for its golden beaches and maritime heritage, has experienced an unprecedented series of reports of unexplained luminous displays in the night sky over the past three nights. Officials from the Eldermere Emergency Services Bureau (E.E.S.B.) confirmed early morning checks revealed glowing orbs, geometric patterns, and erratic light trails captured on security cameras along the harbor and residential districts. Witnesses describe the phenomena as pulsating, moving at irregular speeds, and emitting a faint, eerie blue hue. Authorities are urging residents to stay indoors and avoid venturing outside after sunset due to safety concerns, though no direct threats have been confirmed.

Local scientists and historians speculate the sightings may correlate with ancient coastal legends of "the Drifting Crowns," a supposed celestial phenomenon tied to the town’s founding in 1892. The E.E.S.B. has deployed drones to monitor the skies and is collaborating with the regional Space Marine Division for additional analysis. Residents are advised to file reports via the official app *Eldermere SkyWatch* or by calling **211-ELDERMERE** for updates. The town’s annual Maritime Festival, scheduled for this weekend, has been postponed indefinitely pending further investigation.

No casualties have been reported, but the E.E.S.B. has activated emergency shelters in the town’s lower districts for displaced residents.
