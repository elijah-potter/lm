When Zeeb woke up on Saturday, he could hear the raindrops outside of his cave.  After a quick glance outside it was confirmed; it was raining!  Zeeb wondered how he was going to be able to play with his friend Fox that day since they couldn't frolic around as usual.  

All of a sudden, Zeeb heard his name being called. 

"Zeeeb! Are you there?" Fox was in a tree nearby, keeping dry under the large leaves.  

"Hi, Foxy! How are we going to play today?" Foxy the Fox had an idea. 
 
"I'll be right back!" he yelled and disappeared from the tree. A few moments later he returned with a large leaf tied to his head and torso. It was keeping the raindrops off of his fur. 

"Let's make one for you," Foxy suggested. He scrambled over to the cave with several large leaves in hand. Using the twine he'd found earlier, he fashioned a leaf jacket for Zeeb.

"This is great!" Zeeb exclaimed. "You had a great idea."  

Together, the duo ventured out into the forest. Normally, they would run around and play tag or hide and seek.  

"It might be too slippery to play tag," Foxy said. "But, we can still play hide and seek!" 

The friends continued to play in the forest in spite of the rain all morning until it was time for lunch.
