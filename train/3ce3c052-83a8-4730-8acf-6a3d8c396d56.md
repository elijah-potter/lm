A temporary pop-up market in an abandoned subway tunnel beneath a downtown plaza, lit by flickering solar-powered lanterns and shaded by repurposed shipping containers. Stalls sell handmade ceramics, vintage leather jackets, and freshly baked sourdough from a mobile bakery. A small band plays jazz in the center, and the scent of chili and wildflowers mingles in the humid air. Open only for 48 hours, the event ends with a bonfire under a sky obscured by construction dust.
