The door creaked open, revealing a sudden gust of cold air. The chalkboard in the corner of the conference room, usually filled with meticulously planned timelines and resource allocations, was now blank except for a single crumpled note—*"Revised—12:30"*—taped beneath a hastily scribbled alternative itinerary. The air hummed with tension as the team exchanged glances; some nodded, others sighed. A coffee cup clattered against the table as the lead engineer adjusted their glasses, their fingers tracing the edges of the new blueprint instead of the old one. Outside, a distant alarm blared, its rhythm syncing with the quiet resignation in the room. The project’s momentum had stalled.
