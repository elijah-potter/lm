Sicilian Delight is a family-owned business that prides itself on its storied history and authentic flavors. Steeped in tradition, Executive Chef Luigi takes old-world recipes and adds a modern twist.  

Chef Luigi hails from Italy and believes in using only the freshest and highest quality ingredients. He has trained under multiple Michelin-starred chefs and has won numerous awards including "Best Up and Coming Chef" in both 2017 and 2018. He has also been nominated for multiple James Beard awards.

Come to Sicilian Delight and enjoy a night of great food and service. We offer daily specials and are known for our cannolis. Make a reservation today! We would love to take you on a culinary journey.
