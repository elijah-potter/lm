“My new sister will be here any day now,” thought the child. “What should I name her?”

Maybe Oolaf, thought the child - but that was too silly. Perhaps she could be named, Zingzop - no, that would not work. Tringtrang is a cool name, but teachers may struggle to pronounce it.

“I know!” laughed the child, realizing they had the perfect name for their new sister. “I will name her Katherine, just like my mom!”
