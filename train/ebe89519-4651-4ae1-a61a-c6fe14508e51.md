- **A List of Things**
- **The Sky at Dusk**
- **A Thousand Whispers**

---

The night hummed between the cracks of the world like a forgotten language, and this was its alphabet.

**First**, the stars did not simply hang—they *sang*, each one a note too high for human ears but low enough to drown out the city’s breath. They were not fixed, not solid, but *curling*, like ink in water, pulling at the edges of thought with threads of light.

**Second**, there was the wind. It did not move. It *was*—a pulse, a slow heartbeat beneath the pavement, the way the earth exhaled before the dawn. It carried dust, and secrets, and the scent of old paper and something metallic, like blood left to dry.

**Third**, the hands.

They were not ours. They were *alive*, moving through the dark like shadows with fingers. Some wrote; some erased. Some held the air and let it slip through their palms like smoke.

---

The list was not a question. It was a promise.
