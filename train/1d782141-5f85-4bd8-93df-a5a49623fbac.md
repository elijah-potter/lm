An oppressive, low-hanging mist clings to the earth like damp wool, obscuring the horizon. Towering, gnarled trees—some skeletal, others cloaked in brittle, frost-kissed foliage—stand in rigid, unnatural poses, their branches twisting as if frozen mid-motion. The air is thick with the scent of damp stone and decaying leaves, punctuated by the distant, muffled cries of unseen creatures. The sky, a pallid, sickly gray, is stretched taut over the landscape, its edges blurred as if it too is holding its breath. The ground beneath feels heavy, as if the earth itself is suspended in a trance, waiting for something—perhaps a storm, perhaps a whisper of movement from the unseen world beyond. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, dancing in slow, deliberate circles. The silence is suffocating, punctuated only by the occasional, eerie rustle of something unseen.
