Once upon a time in a sunny meadow, there was a little bunny named Benny who loved hopping around and playing with his friends. The meadow was filled with colorful flowers, tall grasses, and happy chirping birds.

One bright morning, Benny decided to explore a new part of the meadow he had never seen before. As he hopped along, he met a friendly squirrel named Sally. Sally was looking for acorns, but she got lost in all the bushes.

"Don't worry," said Benny with a smile, "I'll help you find your way back!" Together, they searched through the bushes and fields until they found a big oak tree. That's where Sally had been collecting her acorns!

Sally was so happy to see her acorns again that she decided to share them with Benny. They sat together under the shade of the oak tree, munching on some tasty acorns.

As the sun began to set, casting a golden glow over the meadow, Benny and Sally said goodbye. "Thank you for helping me," said Sally gratefully. "Anytime!" replied Benny, waving his tiny paw.

Benny hopped back home feeling very happy about his adventure and the new friend he had made. That night, as he snuggled in his cozy burrow, he dreamed of all the fun times he would have with Sally.

And from that day on, Benny always knew that making friends could turn an ordinary day into a wonderful adventure!
