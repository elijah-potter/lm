Temporary Pop-Up Market Square: 5th Avenue, Downtown
* Location: 123 Main St, 2nd floor, adjacent to the seasonal flea market (closed Jan–April).
* Hours: 10 AM–6 PM daily, but only operational **Monday–Wednesday** (14-day window: June 15–July 28, 2024).
* Features:
  - **Artisan stalls** (local ceramicists, handwoven textiles) selling 30% off, limited stock.
  - **Live jazz trio** (free entry; donations welcome) in the central courtyard.
  - **Food trucks**: 3 rotating vendors (cheese fondue, sushi rolls, churros).
  - **Special event**: "Midnight Market" (6 PM–11 PM) on July 19–20, featuring DJ sets and fireworks.
* Entry: Free; reserved parking lot (10 spaces) in back, $5 fee.
* Exits: Main door (10 AM–2 PM) or rear alley (3 PM–6 PM).
* Caution: Not a permanent fixture; last operational day is July 29, 2024.
