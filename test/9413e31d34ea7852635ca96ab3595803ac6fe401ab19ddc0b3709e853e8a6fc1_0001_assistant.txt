The Ford Big begins as a compact SUV, but when you need to haul more, bring more fans to the tailgate, or camp out under a cool Tahitian sky, the Big can do the unthinkable. Cars have always gotten longer and taller to haul more space, but the Ford Big goes in a new direction, extending horizontally for more room where you need it most. The center of your ride is where the magic happens. It's where you put your arm around your enby partner and gaze at a drive-thru movie. It's where you graciously clean up after your dog spills a cup of boba tea (but don't worry, the nonstick elastic expansion grid in the center cleans up like a breeze with its own filter tray at the bottom!). So don't shrink the center of your life. Expand to fit all life's best moments in the Ford Big.
