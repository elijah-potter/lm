Disneyworld has 4 separate parks to enjoy. Here is a list of each of them and what they have to offer:

1. Animal Kingdom - Visiting this park is like being on a safari. It is split into 5 different lands including Asia, Africa, Dinoland, Discovery, and Pandora. 
2. Epcot - This park was built to showcase inventions, innovations, and cultural diversity. It has two parts: future world, which showcases technology from deep sea diving to space exploration, and world showcase, which gives visitors a chance to explore microcosmic versions of eleven different nations around the world. This park has rides, attractions, shows, and restaurants. 
3. Hollywood Studios - With a Hollywood theme and 7 main areas, this park has features like Toy Story Land and Galaxy's Edge (a Star Wars-themed area), aiming to give visitors a taste of what it feels like to be "behind the scenes" in Hollywood. 
4. Magic Kingdom - When you think of Disney, this is the classic park that likely comes to mind. There are 6 different lands with Cinderella's castle as the park's central hub. This park is home to popular and classic rides like Splash Mountain and the Haunted Mansion.
