A dense forest where sunlight filters through like fractured glass, casting eerie shadows that stretch endlessly like shadows of forgotten dreams. The air hums with whispers of unseen creatures, thicker than the silence before a storm. Roots twist and intertwine beneath damp earth, forming an ancient, tangled network of veins that pulse with unseen life. Towering trees stand sentinel, their branches reaching like skeletal fingers toward a sky so vast it feels infinite yet somehow too small for the weight of the world above. Moss clings to stone like a slow, relentless hunger, and every rustle in the leaves sounds like the distant, slow heartbeat of something vast and ancient. The scent of damp earth and pine needles lingers, a mix of earth and memory, heavy with the promise of things that have been and will be forgotten.
