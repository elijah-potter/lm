The course covers a range of areas, from vocabulary and sentence building to oral practice and story reading as well as some poetry and reading. It helps to enhance children’s confidence in using Putonghua, enabling children to incorporate the language into their daily life with ease.
With the rapid development in the Mainland and the merging of two societies, Putonghua has become more popular and important than ever. Learning Tree provides theme-based Putonghua programme taught by native Putonghua teachers, children learn common Putonghua words and phrases in a friendly and natural environment.
Topics are related to daily usage with various interesting themes - children will find themselves enjoying their theme-based lessons that include fun activities such as oral discussion, story reading, singing, interactive games and activities. Children learn to play and communicate with each other in a group setting while establishing a strong foundation in Putonghua for future learning.
Learning Putonghua will also improve children’s writing in Chinese, as well as help to lay a good foundation for future use of Putonghua during Chinese lessons.
