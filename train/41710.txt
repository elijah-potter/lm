The use of circles has traditions that go back far into the history of man/woman kind. Traditional and indigenous communities use circles as a cornerstone of their way of life.
Circles can be used for program and workshop implementation, as a way to facilitate child/and youth development, for healing from trauma and within the work place to develop effective teams.
This workshops demonstrates, through interactive processes, the power of circles to harness the energy of groups and teams. Relevant for child/youth serving organizations, education and workplaces.
