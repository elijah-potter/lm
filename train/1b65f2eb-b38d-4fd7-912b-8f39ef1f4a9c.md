## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park – A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary (For Municipal Funders)**

The Willow Creek neighborhood, a historically underserved area within our municipality, suffers from a significant deficit of accessible green space and experiences disproportionate impacts from increasingly frequent and intense rainfall events. This proposal outlines a transformative project to reclaim a currently neglected, 0.75-acre parcel located at the intersection of Elm and Maple Streets – currently an overgrown and unused lot – and convert it into the Willow Creek Pocket Park. This park will serve as a vital community asset, incorporating climate-resilient design principles to mitigate stormwater runoff, provide much-needed recreational space, enhance biodiversity, and foster social cohesion. The project represents a strategic investment in community resilience, equitable access to green space, and long-term environmental sustainability. We request $75,000 in municipal funding, matched by $25,000 in community contributions and in-kind support, to realize this impactful project.

**Project Plan & Timeline**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park project will unfold across four distinct phases, each with designated responsible partners and clearly defined deliverables.

**Phase 1: Planning & Design (Months 1-2) – Responsible Partner: Green Spaces Collaborative (GSC), Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (WCNA)**

*   **Deliverables:** Site assessment report, community needs assessment, preliminary park design incorporating native plantings and stormwater management features, final design approval from GSC and WCNA, permit applications submitted to the City Planning Department.
*   **Activities:** GSC will conduct a detailed site analysis, including soil testing and topographic surveys. WCNA will facilitate community workshops and surveys to gather input on park design preferences. The final design will prioritize accessibility and inclusivity.

**Phase 2: Site Preparation & Stormwater Infrastructure (Months 3-5) – Responsible Partner: Municipal Public Works Department (MPWD), Volunteer Construction Crew (VCC)**

*   **Deliverables:** Site clearing and grading completed, stormwater detention basin constructed, permeable paving installed, soil amendments incorporated.
*   **Activities:** MPWD will provide heavy equipment and technical expertise for site preparation. VCC, comprised of local residents and skilled volunteers, will assist with labor. Stormwater management will utilize a rain garden and permeable pathways to reduce runoff and recharge groundwater.

**Phase 3: Planting & Amenity Installation (Months 6-8) – Responsible Partner: Willow Creek Community Garden (WCCG), GSC, WCNA**

*   **Deliverables:** Native trees, shrubs, and wildflowers planted, benches and picnic tables installed, accessible pathways completed, signage erected.
*   **Activities:** WCCG will lead the planting effort, utilizing locally sourced native species. WCNA will coordinate volunteer planting days and oversee the installation of park amenities. Signage will include interpretive information about native plants and stormwater management techniques.

**Phase 4: Community Launch & Ongoing Maintenance (Months 9-12 & Ongoing) – Responsible Partner: WCNA, City Parks and Recreation Department (CPRD)**

*   **Deliverables:** Grand opening celebration, community park stewardship program established, ongoing park maintenance plan implemented.
*   **Activities:** WCNA will organize a community celebration to officially open the park. CPRD will provide ongoing maintenance support, including trash removal and seasonal upkeep. A volunteer park stewardship program will be established to engage residents in long-term care and enhancement of the park.

**Budget**

The total project cost is estimated at $100,000. We are requesting $75,000 from the municipality, which will be matched by $25,000 in community contributions and in-kind support.

*   **Site Assessment & Design (Phase 1):** $8,000 (GSC fees, survey costs, community workshop materials)
*   **Site Preparation & Stormwater Infrastructure (Phase 2):** $35,000 (Equipment rental, soil amendments, permeable paving materials, labor assistance) – *Significant in-kind contribution from MPWD.*
*   **Planting & Amenity Installation (Phase 3):** $25,000 (Native plants, benches, picnic tables, accessible pathway materials, signage) – *Significant in-kind contribution from WCCG and volunteer labor.*
*   **Community Launch & Ongoing Maintenance (Phase 4):** $5,000 (Event supplies, educational materials, initial maintenance supplies)
*   **Contingency (5%):** $2,500 – *To address unforeseen expenses related to material price fluctuations or unexpected site conditions.*
*   **Project Management (GSC):** $5,000 – *Dedicated project management to ensure timely completion and adherence to budget.*

The community contributions of $25,000 are comprised of volunteer labor valued at $15,000 and donated materials (plants, mulch, and some construction supplies) valued at $10,000. Detailed records of in-kind contributions will be maintained and submitted with final project reporting.

**Community Engagement & Equity Impacts**

Community engagement is central to the success of this project.  The Willow Creek neighborhood has historically experienced limited access to quality green spaces and has been disproportionately affected by localized flooding. The design process actively incorporated community input through surveys, workshops, and direct consultation with residents. The park’s design prioritizes accessibility for individuals with disabilities and includes features that cater to diverse age groups and interests. The project aims to foster social cohesion by creating a welcoming and inclusive space for all residents. The increased green space will also contribute to improved air quality and reduced urban heat island effect, benefitting the entire community.

**Risk Mitigation**

Potential risks include delays in permit approvals, unexpected site conditions, and fluctuations in material costs. To mitigate these risks, we have established a contingency fund, secured early consultation with the City Planning Department, and prioritized relationships with reliable suppliers.  Volunteer recruitment and coordination will be ongoing to ensure adequate labor support.  The project timeline includes buffer time to account for potential delays.

**Post-Grant Sustainability**

Long-term sustainability is ensured through a combination of community stewardship, municipal support, and strategic partnerships. The established park stewardship program will engage residents in ongoing maintenance and enhancement activities. CPRD will provide regular maintenance support, including trash removal and seasonal upkeep.  The Willow Creek Community Garden will continue to provide horticultural expertise and volunteer labor.  The project's success will serve as a model for future green space development within the municipality.

**Call to Action**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park represents a transformative opportunity to enhance the quality of life for residents, build community resilience, and create a lasting legacy of environmental stewardship.  We urge the municipality to approve our request for $75,000 in funding, enabling us to realize this vital project and create a vibrant community oasis for generations to come. The investment in the Willow Creek Pocket Park is an investment in the health, well-being, and future of our community.
