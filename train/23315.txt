Safer Smartphone - Security and protection for the cell phone
More and more personal information is stored on smartphones. But young people often lack sufficient knowledge of how to protect it effectively. What dangers are young people confronted with when using mobile media? And what options are there for countering them? Safer Smartphone" sheds light on these questions. The material helps teachers discuss the topics of security and data protection in the classroom and offers a wealth of information, worksheets and other suggestions.
