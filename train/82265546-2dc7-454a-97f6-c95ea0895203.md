In the hollowed-out core of an ancient library, where the air hummed with the echoes of forgotten tomes, a scholar named Elias found himself lost in a paradox. The books, though bound in time, whispered secrets only the curious could unravel. One particularly cryptic volume, its pages yellowed with age, contained a passage that defied logic:

*"When the moon bleeds and the stars cry, ask what lies beneath the tide’s embrace. Seek the answer where the lost souls dare to rest."*

Elias traced the ink with trembling fingers, the words rearranging themselves like a puzzle meant to be solved. He knew he was chasing something more than knowledge—he was chasing the truth that had always eluded him, buried beneath the weight of doubt and the whispers of the unknown.

Outside, the wind howled through the skeletal remains of the library, carrying fragments of his own voice, half-formed and broken. Had he found the answer, or was he merely a vessel for the question itself? The night stretched on, endless and waiting, and Elias wondered if the universe had ever been this silent before.
