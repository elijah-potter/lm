The air is thick with the damp, earthy scent of moss clinging to ancient stone walls. The rhythmic pulse of flowing water carries a crisp, cool whisper through cavernous tunnels, its sound muffled by layers of sediment. Shadows stretch long and uneven, cast by flickering torch flames that burn low, their heat carving delicate patterns on the damp walls. The air hums with the distant buzz of unseen insects, their presence heavy, as if vibrating through the very fabric of the place. The stone beneath your fingertips is rough, layered with time, its surface smooth when wet, rough when dry, carrying the faintest taste of mineral and salt. The weight of the surroundings presses gently, a quiet, unyielding embrace.
