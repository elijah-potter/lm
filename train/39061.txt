Music History of Christmas Carol
It was not until the 1950’s that Johnny Marks, known for his song “Rudolph, the Red-Nosed Reindeer”, set this poem to music. Marks’ song has been recorded by many famous singers such as Frank Sinatra, Harry Belafonte, The Carpenters and Bing Crosby.

Piano Practice Tips for I Heard the Bells on Christmas Day
First of all, this short piano arrangement is in the key of C Major. There are a few sharps written within the piece. However, there are no sharps or flats in the key signature.
Both hands need to be practiced separately. Be aware of some small hand position changes as you play the left hand.
