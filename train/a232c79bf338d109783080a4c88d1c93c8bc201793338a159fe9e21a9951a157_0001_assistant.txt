Once upon a time, there was a small robot who had never stepped foot outside the small room he called his home. He was afraid of what might be waiting for him on the other side of the door. One day he woke up determined to face his fear. He rolled over to the door, and gently pushed it open. A bright light shined in, the robot summoned all his courage and rolled out into the unknown.

The robot looked around in awe. He had never seen anything like it. There was so much open space and so many beautiful colors. The robot rolled around and wondered at the new things around him. He saw grass and sky; he heard birds singing and children laughing for the first time.

He rolled towards the noise of the laughter on a small playground. He watched the children run and play and marveled at the pure joy they experienced. He watched for a while before he was approached by a small girl in a pink dress. She introduced herself as Mya and invited the little robot to play. He rolled after her and then joined in on a quick game of tag with some other children. The robot was so happy he'd decided to go out and explore the world.
