Act 1:

Introduction of a new family moving into the neighborhood. They are escaping their city apartment to what they think is a nice and hospitable small town. But they soon discover that not following the rules can have dire consequences. 

Act 2:

The new family has their first run-in with the smile police. The father, John, was seen looking solemn while driving home from work. He is pulled over and given a warning. He goes to the city board meeting to complain about the rule, but he is horrified to see a sea of smiling faces.

Act 3:

The family starts getting targeted for any small step out of line. They even get a letter complaining that their dog doesn’t smile when they walk him. What seemed like a joke is now turning into something more sinister. People watching them, the smile police giving them tickets that say, “Turn that frown upside down”.

Act 4:

One day when John is leaving work for the day, he Is stopped in the parking garage by a man in the shadows dressed in black. He tells him there is dissent among the community, and there is a secret gathering every week of the citizens who are sick of the smiling and want change.

Act 5:

There is a final showdown between the band of citizens and the smile police and sitting mayor. The mayor is exposed as a non-smiler behind closed doors and the whole system comes crashing down. The family and citizens are left to try and begin fresh, this time with no mandatory smiles.
