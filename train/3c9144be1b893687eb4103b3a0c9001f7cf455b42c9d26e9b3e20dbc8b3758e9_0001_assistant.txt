Susanna Salter was elected mayor of Argonia, Kansas, on April 4, 1887, making her the first female mayor in U.S. history. A group of local men placed her name on the ballot without her knowledge as a stunt trying to discourage women from participating in politics. On election day, she agreed to accept the office if elected. The Women's Christian Temperance Union and the local Republican Party members supported Salter with their votes, helping her win the election by a two-thirds majority.
