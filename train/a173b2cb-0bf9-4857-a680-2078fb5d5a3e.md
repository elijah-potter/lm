A man stood at the kitchen table, clutching a half-eaten sandwich, his jaw set tight as he stared at the crumpled note from his father—*"Meet me at the old mill at midnight. Don’t be late."* The words had sounded like a challenge, not a warning. But his father’s last words, whispered under the dim porch light, had been *"Don’t tell anyone."* He had obeyed. Now, the mill was just a shadowed ruin, its creaking doors whispering secrets no one dared repeat. The promise had been a trap, and he was trapped inside it. The clock struck twelve. The silence was suffocating. The truth was buried beneath layers of silence, and the only thing that would set him free was breaking the silence. But first, he had to face the one who had broken his.
