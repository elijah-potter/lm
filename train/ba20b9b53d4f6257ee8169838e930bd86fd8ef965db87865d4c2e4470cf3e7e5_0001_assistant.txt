Michael Webster's little sister is quite the rapscallion, Michael even has an apt nickname for her: Tara the Terrible! In the middle of all of her silly shenanigans with her brother, their father brings home an antique cuckoo clock. He explicitly tells them that they aren't allowed to touch it. So of course, being the smart boy he is, Michael touches the old antique clock! It seems like there was some old magic on it, though, and Michael's hilariously mystical adventures are just getting started!
