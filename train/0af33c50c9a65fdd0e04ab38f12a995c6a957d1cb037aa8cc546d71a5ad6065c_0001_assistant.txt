My name is Arthur and I am a Golden Retriever, my favorite hobby in the world is to go for walks with my owner. Let me tell you what is so fun about walks. First off, it is all the different things you can smell; as a dog, my sense of smell is very strong, much stronger than a human's. When I walk around outside, I can smell all the other dogs that came before me! and find out about all the friends I could have in my neighborhood. Sometimes I get to see dogs too! and if I'm really lucky, we meet up close. Some dogs aren't as friendly as I am, but a lot of them are, and getting to say hi to them is really fun. Another thing that's great about walks is that I get to look around at all the different houses. Sometimes when people are gardening outside they'll talk to me and pet me and sometimes I get to chase their cats, but I am only playing, I love cats too. The last thing that I love about walks is that I get to spend quality time with my human. It's fun to play with my human inside the house too, but doing the thing I love most with the person I love most is better than anything.
