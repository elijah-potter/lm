There were many dog stars on the big screen and small in the 80s. Here are a few to get you started:

1. Einstein - He was Dr. Emmett Brown’s sheepdog in Back to the Future in 1985. This part was actually played by two different dogs, Tiger and Freddie.
2. Comet - This is the dog from the Full House series. Comet was played by a dog named Buddy in season 3 of the show.
3. Odie - Odie was the cartoon friend of Garfield the Cat who appeared in Garfield's comic series and then later in films and on TV.
4. Hooch - Hooch starred in the 1989 comedy Turner and Hooch alongside Tom Hanks.
5. Spuds McKenzie - Spuds was used in advertising campaigns for Bud Light beer in the late 80s, making his debut at the Super Bowl in 1987.
