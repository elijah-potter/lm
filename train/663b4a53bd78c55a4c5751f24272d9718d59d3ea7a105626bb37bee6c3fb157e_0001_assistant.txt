Heroes:

1. Mario (Super Mario Bros.)
2. Luigi (Super Mario Bros.)
3. Link (Legend of Zelda)
4. Snake (Metal Gear Solid)
5. Sonic (Sonic the Hedgehog)
6. Ryu (Street Fighter)
7. Master Chief (Halo)
8. Pac-Man (Pac-Man)
9. Altair (Assassin's Creed)
10. Mega Man (Mega Man)

Villains:

1. The Flood (Halo)
2. Gannon (Legend of Zelda)
3. GLaDOS (Portal)

The combatants met in the field of battle. The heroes, comprised of 10 of the greatest fighters ever, met against the three groups of enemies, considered the most evil groups of all time. The villains guarded a magical key that was said to unlock a chest containing a powerful artifact that could resurrect all the heroes from all the game franchises and bring them to the battlefield to assist the 10 heroes and vanquish evil for good.

Link struck the first blow, shooting a flaming arrow at Gannon's head. GLaDOS created an energy field around the villain group, causing the arrow to glance harmlessly away from them. Then, The Flood began running toward the heroes in a swarm, attempting to overwhelm them with their numbers.

Mario and Luigi were the first to move; they were charged with entering the sewers underneath the villains' forcefield via pipes and coming up behind them to grab the magic key. As they disappeared through the pipes, Master Chief began blasting at the Flood with his shotgun, taking out numbers of them at once. Snake and Altair, masters of movement and stealth, jumped around the Flood and darted quickly toward Gannon. Mega Man and Pac-Man assisted Master Chief with The Flood aliens, shooting and eating them to bring their numbers down. Sonic and Ryu decided to go for GLaDOS in an attempt to bring down the forcefield she had created and render the villains defenseless.

The entire battle lasted about an hour before the heroes were able to divide and conquer the villains and obtain the magic key. The Mario Brothers brought the key back to the heroes' camp and celebrated their victory.
