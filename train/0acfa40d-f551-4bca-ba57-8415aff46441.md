The office hummed with quiet efficiency as the afternoon sun filtered through blinds, casting long shadows across desks. A stapler clattered, followed by the murmur of a colleague typing furiously on a laptop. Then—

A sudden, unmistakable *crack* split the air. A single, unmistakable laugh echoed from the break room, sharp and unfiltered, like a child’s giggle. The sound made everyone freeze. The person nearest the door, mid-step, turned—eyes wide with surprise—only to collapse in a fit of uncontrollable laughter themselves. The room erupted in a cacophony of snorts, coughs, and giggles. Laptops clattered shut. Desks scraped. A nearby coffee mug toppled, splashing dark liquid across a notepad.

The laughter faded into a muffled, awkward silence, broken only by the faint, desperate laughter of a security guard trying to shush them from the hallway. The office resumed its quiet rhythm—except now, the air smelled faintly of coffee and something else, something unidentifiable but undeniably *funny*.
