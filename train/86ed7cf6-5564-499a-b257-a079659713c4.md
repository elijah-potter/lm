A fleeting word slipped between laughter at a crowded bar,
*"I’ll meet you tomorrow at the old oak—don’t be late."*
Sunlight through cracks in the window blurred his face,
but the tree stood firm, its roots knotted in the earth.

Dawn came too soon. The air smelled of rain and damp wood.
He arrived at eight, his boots sinking into the moss.
The oak’s bark held the same scars as his first heartache—
a promise carved into bark, unbroken, unheeded.
