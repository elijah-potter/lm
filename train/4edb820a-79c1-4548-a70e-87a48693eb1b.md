Market square in a bustling Mediterranean port city, where vendors set up stalls under the shade of ancient olive trees. Locals and travelers converge with baskets of fresh figs, spices from distant lands, and handwoven textiles. Children dart between adults, while street musicians play lively tunes. A narrow canal runs through the center, reflecting the vibrant colors of the stalls and the warm golden light of late afternoon. Vendors haggle with buyers in broken but enthusiastic exchanges, while the scent of roasting lamb and blooming jasmine fills the air.
