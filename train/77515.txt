Platte is a county in Missouri. It was formed in 1838 from the following county/ies: Platte Purchase. Platte began keeping birth records in 1883–87, marriage records in 1839, and death records in 1883–87. It began keeping land records in 1839, probate records in 1839, and court records in 1839. For more information, contact the county at 415 Third St. Platte City 64079. On the attached map, Platte is located at C1. Courthouse destroyed 1861 during Civil War.
