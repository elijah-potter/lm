The journey began in an empty field at dawn. The first destination was a rusted gate at the horizon, marked with a single black star.

By midday, the gate shifted—now the sky was a lake, and the horizon was a floating island. The island had no name, but a lighthouse pulsed faintly on its edge.

At dusk, the island dissolved into a storm, and the lighthouse became a storm chaser’s vessel, drifting aimlessly through the clouds. The next destination was the eye of a hurricane, where the wind carried whispers of forgotten names.

At midnight, the hurricane opened its mouth, and a tunnel of fire led to a desert where the sand sang in a language no human ear had ever heard. The sunrise revealed a monolith carved with shifting runes, its surface a shifting map.

The monolith dissolved into a river of light, flowing toward a city built of dreams—one that changed with every heartbeat. The city’s bridges were bridges of memory, and its streets were alleys of half-remembered futures.

The next destination was a mirror that reflected not the self, but a stranger’s face—one who walked the same shifting paths. As they spoke, the mirror fractured, revealing a maze of echoes.

The echoes coalesced into a single voice: *"You are already there."* The voice dissolved into static, and the maze became a door, which opened into a place where time was a river, and the current flowed backward, forward, and sideways all at once.

The journey now had no end, only a loop—each step leading to the same infinite, unknowable destination: the place where the map was the mapmaker.
