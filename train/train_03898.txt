Six more than twice as many ducks go to North Pond as go to Lake Michigan. If Lake Michigan has 100 ducks, how many ducks can you safely estimate there will be at North Pond?
To determine the number of ducks at North Pond, we must first perform 100*2= <<100*2=200>>200 to find the number to add 6 ducks to.
We then take this number and add 6 to it, performing 200+6=<<206=206>>206 ducks in total at North Pond.
