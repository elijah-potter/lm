Waiting for something is the sweetest kind of agony. You want the moment to be here so badly that it causes a rising gush of exhilaration that quickly fades into disappointment because, no matter how hard you wish it into being, the moment isn't here yet, and time won't march on any faster for your impatience. You count the days, will the hours away, and sometimes even count down the minutes.

It's that final stretch that continues the cycle of anticipation. You always remember the first experience involving what you wanted. It's imprinted on your heart in a special box of memories that words just can't describe. Those memories aren't even necessarily profound; they're just locked away because when the moment finally arrives—that anticipated thing is finally right in front of you—you're so utterly focused on that one moment, you can't help but remember it for a long while.
