A life of hard knocks leaves me with questions 
of "why", "what", and "how".
Through the beginning, the middle, and the end of things
Will I be okay right now?

I have a Father, his name is God
He created, loves, and cares for me.
Faith answers all those questions I have
and I am safe from harm and free.

I am saved and I live through Jesus Christ 
and my certainty in this is strong. 
If you're wondering if your sin is too shameful,
I am glad to say you are wrong. 

If you feel too weak and your sins are too great,
God will renew you and forgive you.
By faith in my Lord, my questions are answered
I am okay and I know this to be true.
