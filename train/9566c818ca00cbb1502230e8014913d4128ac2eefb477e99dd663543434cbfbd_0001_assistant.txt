JOB DESCRIPTION: Recliner Tester
Immediate Hire
Full-Time

Sit a Spell Furniture Company, a privately-held furniture manufacturer headquartered in South Dakota, is looking to immediately fill a vacancy for a Recliner Tester in their New Product Development division.

Job duties include testing floor-to-seat height, ease of sliding back into an erect position, back comfort and support, seat width and comfort, armrest height and comfort, leg-raise lever ease of use, speed, and gentleness of the leg support, ease of the initial recline, ease of the further back reclining station, and overall comfort.

The ideal candidate will have:
- A BS in either Furniture Design or Chair Product Analysis
- 5+ years of experience in a furniture store or similar furniture testing environment
- Excellent communication skills
- A basic knowledge of spreadsheet software
- A 90 or higher IQ.

Salary DOE. A competitive benefits package is available, including medical insurance with a spinal injury rider, dental and vision insurance, short- and long-term disability, and three days of paid vacation per year.

Sit a Spell Furniture Company, established in 2020, is an industry leader in the chair and recliner industry, winner of the 2022 Excellence in Recliners Award, from the Academy of Recliner Manufacturing Society (or ARMS).

Only online applications are allowed at this time.
