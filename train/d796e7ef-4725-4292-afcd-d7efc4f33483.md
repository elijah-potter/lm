The kitchen light flickered as he reached for the coffee mug, the weight of the past pressing down like a second skin. The words he’d meant to say—*"I’m sorry"*—stuck in his throat, swallowed by the hum of the morning routine. His hands trembled slightly as he poured the bitter brew, the steam curling into the air like a forgotten confession. Outside, the city woke to its usual rhythm, oblivious to the quiet ache of what could have been.

He wiped his mouth, the gesture mechanical, and turned away before the memory could linger. The door shut behind him with a finality that felt like the end of an argument he never got to finish.
