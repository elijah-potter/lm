A piece of paper slid her way. Surprised, Summer looked first at it and then at Francis, he gave her an expectant look. She read the note.

Where did you reach?

She blinked. And stared hard at the words. She looked at Francis again and then back at the note. This was something the old Francis would ask her when they challenged one another to see who could read faster.

She took her pen and scrawled back a message - she didn’t know what Francis was doing, but she’d play along.

The Pythagoras theorem.

She watched Francis read her note, a small smirk on his face as he scribbled back his answer and slid the page back to her.

I’m doing “Advanced Algebra”.

Hope and insecurity warred within her as an idea came to her mind. Taking a deep breath she grasped her pen and jotted out a question.

Want to see who can read faster?

Francis’ eyes lit up when he read what she wrote, and he looked up at her. He smiled at her like he used to, nodded once, and turned to his book, eyes devouring the words far more hungrily than they had before. Overcome with relief and something else she couldn’t name, Summer happily began to pick up her own pace.
