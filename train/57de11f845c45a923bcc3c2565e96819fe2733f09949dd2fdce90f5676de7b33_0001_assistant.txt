* Polar predators use the ice to hunt, have babies, and to rest.
* Polar predators are at the top of the food chain. 
* Predators that are at the top of the food chain have a major effect on the rest of the organisms living within a polar ecosystem.
* No one knows how different species will survive or adapt to climate change.
* We don't know how well polar ecosystems will do with climate change.
* Learning more about polar predators can help us manage polar ecosystems better as the environment changes.
