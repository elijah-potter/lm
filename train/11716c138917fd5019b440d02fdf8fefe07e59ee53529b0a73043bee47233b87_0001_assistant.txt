Rating: 3 out of 5 stars

I recently took a trip to Mars and the views were out of this world, literally! ;)
The commute was not easy at all and they forced us to strap ourselves into a cramped capsule for about 2 months. My body was sore for weeks once we got there! The food was not the best, but what can you expect from freeze-dried? 
I never want to see another freeze-dried spaghetti ever again.

Once we got to Mars, I was so excited to see how red everything was! It reminded me of the movie 'The Martian' with Matt Damon. It felt like I was in the movie myself but I was actually on the planet lol. Who knew how cool rocks would be on other planets?
The low gravity was pretty fun at first, but after a while, it just became annoying since I wasn't used to moving like that. The lack of oxygen might have had a bigger factor in it than I care to admit. Overall, Mars is definitely a must-see planet for anyone interested! Just bring your own snacks and something to keep you occupied on the flight out. (They didn't have a good movie selection.)
