Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the meadow. One sunny morning, Benny spotted a tiny, shiny flower that sparkled like a star. He picked it carefully and noticed it had a little hole in the center. Benny thought, "This must be the magic flower!"

He planted it in the garden and waited. Soon, every flower in the meadow started to grow taller and prettier. The grass turned green and soft, and the birds started singing happier songs. Benny’s momma said, "Benny, you helped the whole meadow feel so much better!" Benny smiled, knowing he had done something wonderful.

But then, one day, a big, grumpy cloud rolled in. It started to rain, and the meadow looked sad. Benny felt sad too. He thought, "Maybe I can help again." He picked a tiny seed from his garden and planted it in the cloud. The cloud turned soft and fluffy, and soon, it turned into a big, beautiful rainbow that shined all over the meadow.

The meadow was the happiest it had ever been, and Benny knew that being kind and helping others made everything wonderful. From that day on, Benny always looked for ways to make others smile, just like his magic flower.
