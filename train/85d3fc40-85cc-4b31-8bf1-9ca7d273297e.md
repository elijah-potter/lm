The absence lingers like a ghost in the room’s corners—
silence hums where voices once overlapped,
the air thick with the scent of coffee gone cold.
Fingers trace the edge of a half-empty glass,
the weight of what’s unsaid pressing in,
a weight that feels like presence, a shadow
drawn too long in the space between us.
The clock ticks; time moves forward, but not for them.
The door stands open, yet no one comes in.
The absence is a ghost that won’t let go.
