Pierre Trudeau and Justin Trudeau, father and son, have both served as Prime Ministers of Canada.  Pierre Trudeau was the Prime Minister of Canada twice.  He first served as Prime Minister between April 20, 1968, and June 4, 1979.  His second term as Prime Minister was between March 3, 1980, and June 30, 1984.  Justin Trudeau was elected Prime Minister of Canada on November 4, 2015.  They both belong to the Liberal Party of Canada.
