Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny red bird named Pip. Pip loved to hop around the garden, chirping happily, "Hi, hi, hi!" The garden was full of sunshine and yummy berries, and Pip enjoyed eating them every day.

One sunny morning, Pip noticed something strange. A big, shiny apple rolled towards the garden. "What is this?" Pip wondered. It was so round and red, just like a special gift from the sky.

Pip decided to pick it up carefully with his tiny beak. "Wow, it feels so smooth!" he said. But then, something magical happened. The apple started to glow, and Pip saw tiny fireflies dancing inside it. "Mmm, I think this is the best apple ever!" Pip giggled.

Suddenly, a little mouse named Mimi ran up to Pip. "Hi, Pip! Did you find a magic apple?" Mimi asked excitedly. Pip nodded. "Yes! Can I share it with you?" Mimi squeaked with joy.

Pip and Mimi ate the magical apple together. After a while, they both felt happy and strong. "This apple made us feel so good!" Pip said. Mimi nodded. "It's like a little hug from the sun!"

From that day forward, Pip and Mimi always looked for shiny things in the garden. They found more apples, seeds, and even a tiny rainbow that appeared when they jumped together. Their garden became the happiest place in the world, filled with laughter and sunshine.
