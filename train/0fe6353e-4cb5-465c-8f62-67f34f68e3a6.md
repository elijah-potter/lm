A mist rose softly from the damp earth, curling around the hollow where the old oak stood, its branches long since fallen silent. The last ember of the fire in the hearth had dimmed to a pale glow, casting long shadows across the worn floor. The room, once alive with conversation and laughter, now held only the occasional rustle of a closing book or the distant creak of a chair.

Outside, the clock on the tower chimed midnight. The door creaked open, sealing the last of the warmth inside, but not before the wind tugged at the curtain, whispering secrets only the night could understand. The silence was the only thing left to say.
