A young scholar helps keep tabs on the magic meter in a city because he believes it is the best way for him to serve the people. His work includes filing, tracking, and delivering analytics to the head scholars.
When the magic flares, the scholars are frantic, searching for a way to keep ordinary beasts from becoming monsters and people using the flare to overthrow the kingdom.
But the young scholar sees that the magic flare could be used to restore the forests so the beasts as cared for and do not enter the town, and can fix the kingdom's financial issues so the people are cared for.
No one will listen to him so he has to decide who he will follow, them or him.
He learns that bravery is knowing there may be consequences but he needs to act true to himself.
He harnesses the magic and before the other scholars secure it, uses it to better the community.
He is fired and cast out but when he looks back at the kingdom he sees how much better it is and is happy.
