Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around the garden, chasing butterflies and collecting acorns. One sunny morning, Benny heard a tiny voice.

"Help! Help!"

Benny looked down and saw a little mouse named Milo stuck in a flower pot. "Oh no," Benny thought. "I need to help Milo!"

Benny hopped to the pot, pulled it gently out, and let Milo out. Milo was so happy to be free! "Thank you, Benny!" he said.

Together, they explored the garden. Benny showed Milo where to find the sweetest berries, and Milo shared his tiny sandwich with Benny. They laughed and played until the sun started to set.

As the stars twinkled, Benny and Milo said goodbye. "We’ll meet again soon!" Benny promised. And with that, Benny hopped home, his heart full of kindness and joy.
