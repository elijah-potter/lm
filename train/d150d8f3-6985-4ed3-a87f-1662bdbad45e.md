A candle flickered on a worn wooden table, its golden glow casting long shadows against the half-open curtains. Behind her, the older woman sat cross-legged, her fingers tracing the rim of a teacup filled with steaming honeydew. She turned slowly, her voice soft and measured. **"You’ll have the truth before winter’s end."** Her eyes held something like reverence, as if the words were a sacred oath. The air between them hummed with unspoken tension. Outside, the distant toll of a bell echoed like a promise itself.
