Here are three dog-friendly breweries:

1. Bad Weather Brewing: Hang out on the pet-friendly patio and event space, though they are not welcome in the main taproom and the event space may be booked.
2. Lake Monster Brewing: Try their spins on classic beers in a spacious room with lots of space for your pup.
3. Bent Brewstillery: Try their beer and food-truck food. They have water dishes and treats for dogs, and even Pupper Ale and dog-friendly events.
