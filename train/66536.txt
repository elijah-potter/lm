Elementary School Resource: STEM Gingerbread House Activity
This four minute video from History.com’s archives focuses on the Christmas tradition of creating gingerbread houses. It can be used in the classroom to teach tradition or could be turned into a STEM activity as students create their own houses.
Related NY State Academic Standards: SS.3.TCC.4, SS.3.MOV.5.b, SS.E.1
