**Rules for Responsible Use:**

1. **Data Privacy:** Do not share or log personal or sensitive information.
2. **Ethical Conduct:** Avoid harmful or discriminatory content.
3. **Accuracy:** Ensure generated content is verified where necessary.
4. **Clarity:** Provide unambiguous instructions and context.
5. **Respect:** Do not engage in harassment or offensive behavior.
6. **Transparency:** Identify when content is AI-generated.
7. **Accessibility:** Avoid biased or exclusionary language.
8. **Compliance:** Follow applicable laws and platform policies.
9. **Critical Thinking:** Encourage skepticism and verify claims independently.
10. **Safety:** Do not promote unsafe actions or dangerous advice.

*"Do not generate code that exploits system vulnerabilities."*
