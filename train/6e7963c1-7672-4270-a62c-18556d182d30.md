A winding dirt trail, half-buried under yellowed leaves and cracked stones, veers into a tangled thicket of overgrown shrubs. The path splits at every turn, one fork leading deeper into misty hollows, the other descending into a muddy swamp where the water reflects no sky. Overhead, a single skeletal branch sways, its gnarled roots stretching like fingers toward the path, whispering lost stories of forgotten travelers. The air hums with the distant call of an owl, its echo swallowed by the encroaching darkness. No destination glows—only the slow, creeping realization that this is the end.
