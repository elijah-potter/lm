Spring is the perfect time to give your plants a little TLC!🪴

👇🏼 Here are 4 natural DIY houseplant fertilizers that you can use to give your greenery a boost: 

🥕 Boiled Veggie or Pasta Water.
The water you use to boil pasta + veggies absorbs nitrogen, phosphorus, and calcium and will transfer those nutrients to the soil. Just make sure to let it cool completely before using it. 

🍌 Banana Peels. 
Banana peels are a rich source of potassium, nitrogen, magnesium, and phosphorous. Cut them into tiny pieces and mix them into the soil, or soak them in water for a few days and then use that brew to water your plants.

🥚 Eggshells. 
Eggshells contain nitrogen, phosphorus, and calcium. Crush them, blend them to a powder in an old coffee grinder, and then sprinkle the powder into the dirt. 

☕ Coffee Grounds.
Coffee grounds are an excellent source of nitrogen for plants. Mix the grounds into water, let them soak for a week, and then water your plants with the mixture.

🐠 Aquarium Water. 
The water in your fish tank contains many beneficial nutrients like potassium and nitrogen. It also cleans your dirt by removing ammonia and chlorine. The next time you change your aquarium water, use it to water your plants instead of pouring it down the drain!
