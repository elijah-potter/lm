The Springsboro Farmer's Market is back for the season! We're excited to welcome new vendors, say hello again to our repeat vendors, and introduce fun activities for kids. There will be a wide range of items for sale, including fruits and vegetables, baked goods, fresh flowers, home goods, and more. We'll have giant tic-tac-toe boards, hula hoops, bubbles, face painting, and free cookies for kids. Join us every Saturday until October from 8 a.m.-12 p.m. on Main Street. We'll see your family soon!
