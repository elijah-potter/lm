Cuddle Bugs Toddler program, for children ages 18 to 30 months, utilizes Innovations: The Comprehensive Toddler Curriculum. In addition to basic care and nurturing, this curriculum provides learning activities and experiences in Dramatic Play, Sensory & Art, Curiosity, Construction, Literacy, Music, Movement, and Outdoor Exploration. The Innovations Cuddle Bugs Toddler Curriculum is full of developmentally appropriate activities to encourage toddlers to explore the world around them through hands-on activities.
In our Cuddle Bugs toddler classroom, the curriculum includes the following “Possibilities Plans” with monthly themes: Me & My Body; My Family; My Neighborhood; Fruits & Vegetables; Space; Sky; Big Animals; Little Animals; Construction; Wheels; Storybook Characters; and Water
Activities and experiences offered throughout the day support all areas of development, including: physical, emotional, social, motor, language, communication and cognitive.
At this early age in development, it is important for our teachers to nurture and support your toddler’s growth. Parents are also a vital part of this process. In order to integrate some of the school activities with home activities, we will send home a curriculum guideline, which is divided into monthly themes. We highly encourage parents to work at home on topics discussed at school.
