**First Ending:**
The detective traced the trail of blood to the abandoned warehouse where the mysterious stranger was found, his body frozen mid-laugh. The journal pages lay scattered across the floor, each one a cryptic clue—*"The key was never lost, but the lock was always wrong."* The final page read: *"You were never meant to find us."* The city’s secrets were buried with him.

**Second Ending:**
The woman discovered the letter tucked inside her grandmother’s old pocket watch, its ink faded but the message still clear: *"The mirror holds the past, but the glass is the future. Turn it now."* She shattered the watch’s glass, revealing a reflection of her future self—a woman holding a child, smiling. The last line read: *"You are the key."* The mirror’s magic had begun.
