1. Janice Langley is a 30-year-old single mom who lives in Kentucky and makes ends meet as a wise-cracking waitress at the local cafe. Bruce, her son, is a precocious 8-year-old who shoplifts and comes up with get-rich-quick schemes that never seem to work. But everything changes when Janice inherits a huge mansion in San Francisco, complete with an uptight butler, snooty neighbors, and a cook who refuses to let her into the kitchen! Two worlds collide in this "fish out of water" story that's fun for the whole family!

2. John is just an ordinary third-grader, with a best friend named Steve who is always getting him into trouble with his schemes to avoid homework and harass the teacher's pet, Brittany. But everything changes when John's mom starts dating Steve's dad. The best friends decide they don't want their parents to marry, and they set out to break up the couple...at any cost!

3. Maria is an average middle schooler from an average family living in an average city. Nothing exciting ever happens in her life, and she's secretly jealous of a girl in her class named Maya. Maya's family is rich, and she wears the trendiest clothing, eats at five-star restaurants, and goes on exotic trips around the world. But Maria's humdrum life is about to change when the FBI sends her on a secret mission. Would she pretend to be Maya's best friend to spy on Maya's dad, a suspected white-collar criminal? When Maria truly bonds with Maya, will she be able to carry out her mission?
