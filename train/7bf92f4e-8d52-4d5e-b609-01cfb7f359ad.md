The library’s fluorescent hum faded as a heavy rain pattered against the window. She turned the corner, her fingers brushing the worn edge of a chair, and froze. Beneath the cracked vinyl, nestled in a crevice, was a **single silver key**. Cold and smooth, it gleamed faintly under the dim bulb.

She picked it up, turning it over in her palm. The engraving was barely legible—just a small, looping **"J"**—but it was enough. Her breath hitched. The key was old, likely decades old, and it didn’t belong to her.

She dropped it into her pocket, her pulse quickening. The key was gone, but the *idea* of it had settled in her chest like a stone. A lead. A question. A ghost from somewhere else.

She walked out, the weight of the mystery clinging to her like the damp air.
