First of all the cons:

- Too much TV makes you lazy. You may become a couch potato. You opt for an easy and often boring life. 

- You may become addicted.

- You will miss out on socialising with your friends. You will miss out on participating in sports groups or activities in your local community.

- Your schoolwork will suffer especially homework done in front of the TV.

- You will have insufficient exercise, and you could put on weight which will lead to obesity and health risks later on.

- You will get a backache or neck ache from bad posture.

- You will get eyestrain from looking at a bright screen.

- You could watch unsuitable – dodgy programs with violence or bad language.

- You will be exposed to fake information and propaganda.


Now the fors:

- TV is a way of relaxing and de-stressing.

- It is free entertainment. You do not have to think as you watch. You save money. 

- There is a wide choice of programs and tv can be educational and teach new skills. 

- It can motivate you because it shows speeches by motivational speakers.

- TV is a way of staying well informed. It increases your knowledge. It is educational.

- You can watch international sports events live. 

- You can learn about new sports.
