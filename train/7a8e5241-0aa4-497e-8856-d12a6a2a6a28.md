A sentinel of polished brass, its surface etched with faint, ancient veins of patina whispering secrets of the winds that once brushed its edges. It breathes slowly, a slow pulse of silver light flickering through its hollow core, as if the air inside it is a living thing, shifting and sighing. When touched, it hums—not with sound, but with the warmth of forgotten knowledge, a warmth that lingers like the memory of a forgotten melody. Its joints creak with the patience of an old guardian, bending only when the weight of duty demands it, never too far, never too quick. It is both watchful and weary, a silent guardian of forgotten time.
