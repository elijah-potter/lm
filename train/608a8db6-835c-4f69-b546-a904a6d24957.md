## Project Chimera: Rulebook

**A Cooperative Game of Biological Containment**

Welcome to Project Chimera, a cooperative game for two to four players. You are a team of specialized researchers working for the Global Biological Response Initiative (GBRI), tasked with containing a rapidly evolving, synthetic pathogen known as Chimera. Chimera’s unpredictable mutations and global spread threaten civilization, and your success hinges on meticulous planning, precise execution, and difficult choices made under immense pressure.

### Components

*   **Game Board:** A world map depicting key regions susceptible to Chimera infection. Each region displays a Threat Level track.
*   **Chimera Strain Cards (60):** Represent the evolving characteristics of Chimera. Each card details Mutation Effects (positive or negative), Transmission Rate, and Vulnerabilities.
*   **Researcher Cards (12):** Each card represents a unique researcher with specialized skills and starting resources.
*   **Resource Tokens:** Represent Funding (currency), Personnel (research assistance), and Equipment (specialized tools).
*   **Containment Markers:** Used to reduce Threat Levels in infected regions.
*   **Outbreak Markers:** Indicate regions experiencing uncontrolled Chimera spread.
*   **Event Cards (30):** Introduce unexpected challenges and opportunities.
*   **Threat Dice (6):** Used to determine Chimera spread and Event resolution.
*   **First Player Token:** Designates the active player.
*   **Rulebook (this document).**

### Setup

1.  Place the Game Board in the center of the playing area.
2.  Shuffle the Chimera Strain Cards and place them face down in a designated draw pile. Reveal the top three cards and place them face up beside the draw pile. These represent the current Chimera strains.
3.  Shuffle the Event Cards and place them face down in a designated draw pile.
4.  Each player chooses a Researcher Card. Note your starting resources as indicated on your card.
5.  Place one Outbreak Marker in the initial outbreak region (indicated on the Game Board).
6.  Place Containment Markers in a general supply.
7.  Determine the first player randomly and award them the First Player Token.
8.  **Difficulty Scaling:**
    *   **Two Players:** Begin with two Outbreak Markers in the initial outbreak region.
    *   **Three Players:** Begin with one Outbreak Marker in the initial outbreak region.
    *   **Four Players:** Begin with no Outbreak Markers in the initial outbreak region.

### Turn Structure

Each player’s turn consists of the following phases, performed in order:

1.  **Research Phase:** You may perform one Research Action. Choose *one* of the following:
    *   **Analyze Strain:** Draw one Chimera Strain Card and add it to your hand. You may hold a maximum of three Strain Cards in your hand at any time. Discard excess cards.
    *   **Develop Countermeasure:** Spend 2 Funding and discard one Strain Card from your hand to gain 1 Containment Marker.
    *   **Gather Resources:** Gain 1 Funding or 1 Personnel.
2.  **Action Phase:** You may perform two Actions. Actions can be the same or different. Choose from the following:
    *   **Deploy Personnel:** Move one Personnel token from your Researcher Card to a region on the Game Board. This allows for more effective containment actions in that region.
    *   **Containment Effort:** Spend 1 Personnel and roll two Threat Dice. Reduce the Threat Level of the targeted region by the sum of the dice roll. If the roll includes a “Critical Failure” (1), place one Outbreak Marker in an adjacent region.
    *   **Move Researcher:** Move your Researcher token to an adjacent region on the Game Board.
    *   **Share Resources:** Transfer 1 Funding or 1 Personnel to another player in the same region.
3.  **Chimera Spread Phase:** Roll one Threat Die for each region with an Outbreak Marker. If the result is a “Critical Success” (6), place one additional Outbreak Marker in an adjacent region.
4.  **Event Phase:** Draw one Event Card and resolve its effects immediately.
5.  **Pass the Token:** Pass the First Player Token to the player on your left.

### Win and Loss Conditions

**Winning:** You win the game if you successfully reduce the Threat Level of *all* regions to zero.

**Losing:** You lose the game if *any* of the following conditions are met:

*   A region’s Threat Level reaches its maximum value (indicated on the Game Board).
*   The Chimera Strain Card draw pile is exhausted.
*   The Event Card draw pile is exhausted.

### Advanced Variants & Trade-offs

These variants introduce increased complexity and force difficult choices.

1.  **Mutation Cascade:** After each Chimera Spread Phase, draw one additional Chimera Strain Card and add its Mutation Effects to the existing strains. This accelerates Chimera’s evolution and increases the unpredictability of the game. *Trade-off:* Increased difficulty, requires more strategic adaptation.
2.  **Resource Scarcity:** Reduce the starting resources for each Researcher Card by one Funding and one Personnel. *Trade-off:* Forces players to prioritize actions and share resources more effectively.
3.  **Black Market:** Introduce a "Black Market" track. Certain Event Cards may offer powerful, but ethically questionable, resources. Acquiring these resources advances the Black Market track. If the track reaches its maximum value, a catastrophic Event is triggered. *Trade-off:* Offers potential short-term gains at the risk of long-term consequences, forcing players to grapple with moral dilemmas.
4.  **Specialization Lock:** At the start of the game, players secretly choose one of two Specialization Paths (e.g., "Rapid Response" or "Strain Analysis"). This choice grants a unique benefit but also restricts certain actions throughout the game. *Trade-off:* Encourages player interdependence but limits individual flexibility.



### Designer's Note

Project Chimera was designed to foster collaborative problem-solving and critical thinking. The game’s mechanics are intended to simulate the complexities of responding to a global crisis, emphasizing the importance of communication, resource management, and ethical considerations. The unpredictable nature of Chimera and the constant need to adapt to new challenges are meant to mirror the uncertainty inherent in real-world scientific endeavors. We hope this experience encourages players to appreciate the vital role of scientific research and the importance of working together to address global threats.
