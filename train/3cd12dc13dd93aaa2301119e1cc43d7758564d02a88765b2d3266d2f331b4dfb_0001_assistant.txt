Made of metal with computer brains
This is the makeup of a robotic man
Leaking oil from behind that always stains
Attached to each arm is a robotic hand

Blessed with intelligence, robots are smart
With silver, shiny, metallic butts
Computer brains, but they have no heart
Moving slowly with a robotic strut

Powered by battery or running on gas
Robots have no feelings or moods
On fighting a robot, most people would pass
Unlike humans, robots don't eat foods
