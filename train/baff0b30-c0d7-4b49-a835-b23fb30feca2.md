AITA for insisting my sister return a very expensive gift?

Okay, this is a bit awkward, and I’m genuinely unsure how to navigate it. It started back in late November 2023. My sister, Eleanor (32), and I (35) have always been close, though we have different approaches to… well, everything. I’m a planner, a saver, and generally quite practical. Eleanor is more impulsive, artistic, and lives for experiences. 

On November 18th, she called me, practically giddy. She’s been wanting a vintage Leica M6 film camera for ages. Apparently, she’s been scouring online auctions and finally snagged one—a pristine condition model—for $6,800. She then proceeded to tell me she bought it *in my name* as an early Christmas gift. I was stunned. I explicitly told her I couldn’t accept something of that value. I explained that I’m saving for a down payment on a house and that kind of expense would derail my plans. She brushed me off, saying it was “meant to be” and that I deserved something beautiful. She insisted I just enjoy it, and that she's more than capable of covering the cost.

I reluctantly agreed to “hold” it for her, figuring she’s just caught up in the excitement. I took it to my apartment in Denver, Colorado, and kept it safely stored. For the next few weeks, she’s been sending me photos taken with it – lovely landscapes and portraits. I appreciated the gesture, but the financial weight of the camera felt heavier with each image.

Then, on December 21st, she called again. She announced she’s decided to move to Barcelona, Spain, permanently. She’s selling her apartment in Chicago and pursuing a photography project there. She wants me to take the Leica with me. Her reasoning? "It’s a piece of you, a connection to home, and it's too valuable to leave in storage." She implied that I should consider it a permanent gift, a way to support her artistic endeavor. 

I explained, again, that I cannot afford to take on a $6,800 camera, especially when I'm trying to save for a home. I’m not opposed to her using it while she's there, but the expectation that I permanently absorb the cost is unreasonable. I suggested she sell it and use the proceeds to help with her relocation expenses, or perhaps rent a similar camera while in Barcelona. She became quite upset, accusing me of being unsupportive and ruining her dream. She said I’m being selfish and that I clearly don't value our relationship. She even brought up past instances where I’m supposedly unsupportive of her artistic pursuits.

My parents have weighed in. Mom says I should just “be a good sister” and let her have it. Dad thinks I’m being too rigid but doesn’t want to cause a family rift. My husband thinks I’m being perfectly reasonable. I feel incredibly conflicted. I want to support Eleanor, but not at the expense of my own financial stability. I’m worried about damaging our relationship, but I also feel like I’m being manipulated. I'm honestly exhausted by this entire situation. I'm feeling pressured and resentful. I think her expectations are completely out of line, and honestly, a bit entitled. I’m starting to think she’s using the camera as a guilt trip. ESH, frankly.

So, Reddit, AITA for insisting she take the camera back and sell it?
