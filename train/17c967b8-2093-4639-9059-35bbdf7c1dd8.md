Choosing between loyalty and duty, the weight of a decision presses like a cross to bear.
A fork in the path splits memory—what was promised, what was owed—while the future hums with unseen consequences.
The test is not in the choice itself, but in the silence that follows, the unspoken weight of what could have been left undone.
Silence answers louder than words, and the echo of a decision lingers like a ghost in the choices we never made.
