I highly recommend staying at Tziki’s Casa! We arrived and were able to check in easily thanks to the key lockbox located right next to the door of the property. The room was incredibly clean when we arrived, and the furniture and beds were super comfortable. There is a lot of space in the kitchen to store your food and there is space for hanging clothes as well as a chest of drawers.

The hosts were very accommodating and were available by phone for assistance when needed. They provided great recommendations for local restaurants near the house, helped us track down beach supplies, and also were able to call a taxi for us when I could not get my phone to work with local numbers.

The beach is only a five-minute walk away from the room, and there are other great things nearby within walking distance like a local corner store and many beachfront restaurants. We enjoyed our entire stay and wish that we could have stayed longer. I would come back again!
