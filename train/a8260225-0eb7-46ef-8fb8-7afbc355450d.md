The river whispered secrets to the reeds,
a rhythm carved in the tide’s embrace.
*"The reeds whisper secrets to the river,
a tide’s embrace carved in the rhythm’s space."*

The wind, a thread unraveling through the trees,
danced with the dandelions’ golden hair.
*"Thread unraveling through trees’ embrace,
golden hair danced with the wind’s embrace."*

She counted the cracks in the old floorboards,
each one a door to forgotten years.
*"Each door to forgotten years a crack,
floorboards old counted the years’ scars."*
