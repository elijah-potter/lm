A stage was set beneath a flickering streetlamp where a crowd gathered, their murmurs blending into the static of a distant broadcast. The speaker’s voice, steady but weary, wavered at the end of the first sentence:

*"I want to apologize for the harm we’ve caused."*

The crowd’s silence stretched, then fractured into murmurs of disapproval, questions. The speaker’s hands trembled as they fumbled for the next words, pausing mid-sentence:

*"We should have listened sooner—"*

A single tear rolled down their cheek, caught midair by the wind. The crowd held its breath, waiting, but the apology—though sincere—was left unfinished, dangling like a half-fulfilled promise.
