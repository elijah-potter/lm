Appearance: The Water Elves are a tall and statuesque race. The females have athletic builds, and the males are broad-shouldered and muscular. They have tanned skin and piercing blue eyes. Their ears are short and pointed. The females and children have long hair which flows freely in varying shades of blue. When a male Water Elf reaches adulthood, he cuts off his hair and remains clean-shaven for the rest of his life. Male and female warriors adorn their skin with blue tattoos, which signify their victories and achievements in battle. Their clothes and armor are made from resources that they gather from the sea and shore. The females wear dresses, skirts, and tops woven from seaweed, as well as jewelry and hair accessories fashioned from shells. The males wear seaweed tunics and pants. The armor of the warriors is fortified with shells and includes heavy spaulders decorated with sharpened coral. Highborn Water Elves wear delicate coral crowns and elaborate seaweed robes adorned with the finest shells and pearls.

Homeland: 
The Water Elves live in a bustling city named Tida’las, which sits on the shore of a beautiful blue ocean. There are many miles of coastline, from which a lot of common Water Elves make their living as fishermen. Most water elves live within the city, where the buildings are made from limestone. A canal runs the length of the city, which also has plenty of water features and fountains, as well as statues and tributes to the Water Gods. There is a busy outdoor market where the fisherman peddles their wares. Most highborn Water Elves live in extravagant homes which are carved from the cliffs on the west side of the city, known as the Ocean District. Meanwhile, the common Water Elves reside in smaller homes in the River District. The center of the city is home to the market and shops, which is known as the Lake District.
