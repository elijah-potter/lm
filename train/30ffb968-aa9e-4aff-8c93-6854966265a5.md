The **"thumbs-up"** gesture, often perceived universally as a sign of approval, can be ambiguous in different contexts. In some cultures, it may imply a negative sentiment (e.g., in Greece or parts of the Middle East), while in others, it could be a vulgar gesture. The **"OK" sign"** (fist with thumb and index finger forming a circle) means "money" in English but can be offensive in Japan, where it resembles a racist gesture. Misinterpretation arises from cultural differences, language barriers, or unintentional context shifts. Context and awareness are key to preventing misunderstandings in communication.
