A sharp knock at the door made the old wooden frame creak. The wind howled outside, rattling the panes. Inside, the dim glow of a single lamp flickered over the half-eaten bowl of stew on the table.

Karen hesitated, then pulled the door open slowly. The porch light cast long shadows across the worn planks. A figure in a long, dark coat stood silhouetted against the storm, their breath visible in the chill air.

*"I’m not expecting anyone,"* she said, voice tight.

The stranger tilted their head. *"I’m not either."* They stepped forward, holding out a folded newspaper. *"For you."*
