The Dolomites are a mountain range located in northeastern Italy spanning multiple regions and provinces. They are also known as the Pale Mountains, Dolomite Mountains, Dolomite Alps, or Dolomitic Alps and were named after the carbonate rock dolomite that was first described by Déodat de Dolomieu, a French mineralogist. The Campolongo Pass divides the region into Western and Eastern Dolomites. The 6,155 square mile area is home to many peaks, passes, and parks.
