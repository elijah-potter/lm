Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a curious bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop outside every sunny morning and explore his green garden.

One day, Benny noticed something shiny near his favorite flower. It was a tiny, glowing star that twinkled like the night sky. Benny picked it up and felt its soft sparkle.

Benny ran home and told his mom, "Mom, look! A star! It’s magical!" His mom smiled and said, "That’s a wish star! Wishes make magic happen!"

Benny hugged the star close and whispered, "I wish I could fly!" The star shimmered, and suddenly, Benny felt a warm breeze. He hopped higher and higher until he floated above the garden, looking down at his house.

Soon, Benny spotted a cloud shaped like a big, fluffy bunny! It was his best friend, Benny the Cloud. The two played all afternoon, dancing in the sky until Benny’s star sparkled one last time.

When Benny landed safely back in his garden, he found his favorite flower had grown a big, beautiful flower with petals that sparkled like the star! Benny knew his wish had come true.

From that day on, Benny loved to hop outside and look for shiny stars, knowing every wish he made could bring magic into his world. And so, every evening, Benny and his bunny friend the Cloud would share their adventures under the twinkling stars.
