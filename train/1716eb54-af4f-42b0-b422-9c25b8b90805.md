The last light of dusk bled through the cracked windowpanes of the abandoned lighthouse, casting jagged shadows across the weathered desk. Elias wiped his brow with a crumpled handkerchief as the wind howled through the empty corridors. His fingers traced the edge of the old ledger, its pages yellowed with time. *"The last call before the storm,"* he muttered, turning to the final entry: **"The Key to the Storm Chamber"**.

The storm chamber lay beneath the lighthouse, its entrance hidden behind a rusted door, sealed by the same storm that had claimed the lighthouse keeper years ago. Elias had heard whispers—some said the chamber held the power to calm the sea, others claimed it was a trap. But he had found the key in the ruins of the old watchkeeper’s cabin, a silver lockpick that hummed faintly when held near the door.

His breath hitched. The wind outside grew louder, as if the storm itself was waiting. He reached for the rusted handle, his heart pounding. The door creaked open, revealing a spiral staircase descending into darkness. At the bottom, a single lantern burned, its flame wavering in the damp air.

A voice, faint but clear, echoed from the depths: *"Choose wisely, Elias. The sea does not forgive mistakes."*

The lantern flickered out.
