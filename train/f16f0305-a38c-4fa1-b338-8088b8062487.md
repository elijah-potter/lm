The river hummed its tune of silvered current,
A whisper lost in time’s relentless refrain.
The reeds, standing sentinel, bent to its refrain,
While shadows stretched and danced beneath the refrain.

A child ran barefoot through the fields where the refrain
Was woven soft into the earth’s embrace,
Its rhythm deep, its call a comfort to embrace,
A promise whispered through the whisper of the breeze.

The wind carried the refrain from dawn’s first light,
Through fields of gold where goldenrod swayed in time,
Where every blade of grass seemed to bow to its refrain,
And the stars above aligned to its refrain.

At dusk, the refrain grew louder, a chorus bright,
Echoing through the hollow where the old man sat,
His fingers tracing the refrain in the dark,
A melody lost only to the refrain.

And so, the refrain persists—endless, unbroken,
A song that time itself cannot fully break,
For in the heart of every echo, the refrain
Is always there, waiting to be spoken.
