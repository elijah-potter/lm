## Project Chimera: A Cooperative Research Game

**A Rulebook for 2-4 Players**

**Introduction**

Project Chimera places you in the roles of specialized researchers at the Global Institute for Biological Containment (GIBC). A novel, rapidly evolving pathogen, designated Chimera, has emerged from a remote Siberian research facility. Its transmission rate is unprecedented, and its effects are devastating. You must collaborate to understand Chimera’s biology, develop countermeasures, and ultimately contain the global outbreak before societal collapse. Your decisions carry immense weight, and failure is a very real possibility.

**1. Components**

*   **Game Board:** A world map depicting key regions and cities.
*   **Researcher Cards (8):** Each card details a unique researcher with specialized skills and starting equipment.
*   **Chimera Cards (60):** Representing the pathogen's mutations and effects. Divided into three tiers (Early, Mid, Late).
*   **Research Tokens (40):** Representing scientific progress in various fields (Virology, Epidemiology, Immunology, Genetics).
*   **Containment Markers (20):** Used to represent successful containment efforts in affected regions.
*   **Crisis Markers (15):** Track the escalating global crisis level.
*   **Equipment Cards (30):** Representing specialized tools and resources for research and containment.
*   **Event Cards (20):** Introduce unexpected challenges and opportunities.
*   **Dice (3):** Standard six-sided dice.
*   **Rulebook (this document).**

**2. Setup**

1.  Place the Game Board in the center of the play area.
2.  Shuffle the Chimera Cards and divide them into three face-down piles: Early, Mid, and Late. Reveal the top two Early Chimera Cards and place them face-up next to their respective piles.
3.  Shuffle the Event Cards and place them face-down.
4.  Place the Crisis Markers on the "0" space of the Crisis Track.
5.  Place the Research Tokens in a supply pool.
6.  Each player chooses a Researcher Card. Each player receives the starting equipment listed on their Researcher Card.
7.  Place one Containment Marker on the designated “Initial Outbreak Zone” on the Game Board.

**3. Turn Structure**

Each round consists of the following phases, performed in order:

1.  **Event Phase:** Draw the top Event Card and resolve its effects immediately. Some Event Cards may introduce new challenges or offer opportunities for advancement.
2.  **Researcher Action Phase:** Starting with the first player and proceeding clockwise, each player performs two actions. The following actions are available:
    *   **Research:** Spend one action to attempt to gain a Research Token. Roll two dice. The target number depends on the research field. (Virology: 7+, Epidemiology: 6+, Immunology: 8+, Genetics: 9+). If successful, take one Research Token of the corresponding field from the supply.
    *   **Contain:** Spend one action to attempt to contain the outbreak in a region. Roll one die. The target number depends on the region’s stability (printed on the board). If successful, place a Containment Marker in that region.
    *   **Move:** Spend one action to move your researcher to an adjacent region.
    *   **Share Equipment:** Spend one action to give one Equipment Card to another player in the same region.
    *   **Analyze Chimera:** Spend one action to discard a Chimera Card from the discard pile and draw a new one from the appropriate tier deck.
3.  **Chimera Mutation Phase:** Draw one Chimera Card from the Early deck (until the Early deck is exhausted, then draw from the Mid deck, then the Late deck). Immediately resolve the card’s effects. These effects may include spreading the infection, increasing the Crisis Level, or introducing new challenges.
4.  **Crisis Escalation Phase:** Increase the Crisis Level by one. If the Crisis Level reaches a designated threshold (printed on the board), a catastrophic event occurs (described on a separate reference sheet).

**4. Trade-off Mechanics**

Project Chimera incorporates several mechanics that force difficult choices and strategic trade-offs:

*   **Limited Research Focus:** Each researcher specializes in a field. While any researcher can attempt any research, success rates are significantly higher for specialists. This encourages players to coordinate their efforts, but specialization can leave gaps in knowledge.
*   **Containment vs. Research:** Containing the outbreak slows its spread, but it requires valuable actions that could be used for research. Prioritizing one over the other carries significant consequences.
*   **Equipment Allocation:** Equipment cards provide crucial advantages, but the supply is limited. Players must decide who receives vital equipment, balancing individual needs with the team’s overall strategy. When multiple players request the same equipment, a die roll determines the recipient, introducing an element of uncertainty.

**5. Win/Loss Conditions**

*   **Win Condition:** The players win the game if they develop a cure for Chimera. This requires accumulating a specific number of Research Tokens in each field (printed on a reference sheet) and then successfully spending an action to “Synthesize Cure” at the GIBC headquarters.
*   **Loss Conditions:** The players lose the game if:
    *   The Crisis Level reaches its maximum value.
    *   The outbreak spreads to a pre-determined number of regions (printed on the board).
    *   All players are incapacitated (due to Chimera effects or other hazards).

**6. Difficulty Scaling**

The game’s difficulty scales based on the number of players:

*   **2 Players:** Reduce the number of Research Tokens required to synthesize the cure by 2 in each field.
*   **3 Players:** Standard game setup.
*   **4 Players:** Increase the initial Crisis Level by one.

**7. Advanced Variants**

*   **Specialized Chimera:** Introduce a deck of “Specialized Chimera” cards that trigger unique and challenging events based on the current Research Token distribution. This emphasizes the importance of balanced research efforts.
*   **Hidden Information:** Each player receives a secret objective card at the beginning of the game. These objectives may incentivize certain actions, creating internal conflict and strategic ambiguity.
*   **Dynamic Containment:** Implement a system where containment efforts are not permanent. Regions with insufficient containment markers may experience outbreaks, requiring ongoing vigilance.



**Designer’s Note**

Project Chimera was designed to explore the complexities of scientific collaboration and the ethical dilemmas inherent in crisis management. The game mechanics are intended to simulate the pressures of a global pandemic, highlighting the importance of communication, strategic planning, and difficult choices under uncertainty. While presented as a fictional scenario, the game aims to foster an appreciation for the critical role of scientific research and public health infrastructure in safeguarding society. The trade-off mechanics are specifically designed to illustrate that there are rarely easy solutions to complex problems, and that even the best intentions can have unintended consequences.
