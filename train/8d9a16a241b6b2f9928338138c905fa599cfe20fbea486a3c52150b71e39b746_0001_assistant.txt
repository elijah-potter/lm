A coyote entered a Woodland Hills home through a doggie door in the middle of the night and was scared off by a cat.

The incident happened around 4 a.m. on May 5, 2021, at a home on Marlow Circle Boulevard.

The homeowner, Mia Sands, said her brother was sleeping when he heard the doggie door open. He looked around and saw his two dogs, but no one else.

Then, he heard his cat, Lily, meowing and screaming. He went to investigate and saw a coyote standing in the living room.

The coyote was startled by the sight of the man and ran out the doggie door.

Sands said she was relieved that no one was hurt, including her cat. She said Lily is a 14-year-old cat who is not used to being around coyotes.

Sands is warning other pet owners in the area to be aware of coyotes. She said she will no longer leave her doggie door open at night.

Coyote experts say that humans are mostly to blame for the increase in coyote encounters. They say that people should remove items that attract coyotes, such as garbage, fruit, and pet food.

They also say that pets should not be fed outdoors and that all food should be brought inside at night.

If you see a coyote in your area, you should try to scare it away by making noise, throwing rocks, or spraying it with a hose. You should never approach a coyote, as they can be aggressive.

For more information on how to deal with coyotes, you can visit the website of the California Department of Fish and Wildlife.
