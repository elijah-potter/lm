A lone figure in a dimly lit office, typing furiously at a cluttered desk, hears sudden, booming laughter from down the hall. Their head snaps up, eyes widening as the sound cuts off abruptly. The corridor outside the open door glows faintly under fluorescent lights, the doorframe slightly ajar—revealing a group of six people in mismatched costumes, their faces alight with childlike glee. One holds a half-eaten donut dripping with caramel, another is mid-leap off a chair, and the laughter returns, sharp and infectious, as they turn to face the office.

The lone figure’s keyboard stutters.
