Here is your list of five powerful main characters in anime.

1. Monkey D. Luffy from One Piece. After eating the Gomu Gomu no Mi, Luffy's body became like rubber. Luffy leads the straw hat grand fleet and has conquered many powerful opponents in his time. 
2. Naruto Uzumaki from Naruto. Born in the shinobi village hidden in the leaves, Naruto has the nine-tailed fox sealed within him by his father. 
3. Ichigo Kurosaki from Bleach. Ichigo begins his journey as a substitute shinigami but later learns that he can draw power similar to that of his enemies, the hollows. 
4. Goku from Dragon Ball. With an impressive record of 79 wins, 28 losses, and 26 draws, Goku is considered to be one of the most powerful anime heroes of all time. Goku has been used as the gold standard when theorizing who would win questions. However, the most powerful title goes to number 1.
5. Saitama from One-Punch-Man. Saitama is known as One-Punch-Man because he is known for defeating his opponents with one punch. His training method may not be intense, but he has yet to show any limits to his abilities. Saitama comes from a satirical anime that establishes him as the strongest hero ever.
