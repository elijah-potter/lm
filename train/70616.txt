Ninja Turtles Coloring Book
Drawing games for kids will help you color your favorite cartoon characters in bright colors. Have you already learned how to accurately color simple pictures and think you are ready for more complex tasks? Then try to deal with the images of the characters of the animated series "Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles". A lot of small details will teach you attentiveness and caution, and dozens of shades of different colors will allow you to choose the color for your favorite character as accurately as possible. In addition to the brush, which you are already familiar with, drawing games for children will teach you how to work with an “eyedropper”, which allows you to find out what color was used to fill in a particular path.
