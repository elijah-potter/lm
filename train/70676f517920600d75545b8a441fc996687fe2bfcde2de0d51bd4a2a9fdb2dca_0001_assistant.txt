[The Traveller enters the hall]

King: Ah, you must be weary, for you have travelled long and far through unfriendly lands. Please sit and have food and drink as you will, then after we have spoken you may rest until the pains of your journey have left you.

Traveller: Thank you, my lord, I appreciate your hospitality.

[ The Traveller sits at the table and begins to eat and drink for a few minutes]

Traveller: The message I have borne over sea, stone and ice should not wait longer. Moreso it should be heard only by your ears, and perhaps those you trust.

[The king thinks for a moment then waves his hand, everyone leaves apart from the king's advisor]

Traveller: The artefact of old has been found. In the distant land across the sea on the lands of a low-class noble. I believe it to be genuine, it is upon those lands that your sire of old fought the final battle and fell. I think that they do not know what they have found, at least for the present, only that it is old and valuable. 

The King: I see. We must reclaim it at once before the true purpose of the heirloom is discovered. You will be given a small force, to travel in secret and retrieve it, for you know the lands well. You have access to any resource I can provide. Though I know you are tired you cannot now tarry overlong in the castle. You must set out in seven days and bring back the artefact by the coming of winter.

Traveller: Yes, my lord. I will rest for the night and begin preparations at dawn. I will not fail.

The King: You may leave now, for you have a long journey ahead.

[The Traveller bows and leaves]
