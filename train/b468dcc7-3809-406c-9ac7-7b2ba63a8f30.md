The last lantern flickered as Elias stood on the crumbling bridge of Blackthorn Keep, its timbers groaning under the weight of centuries. The moon hung low, casting long shadows over the jagged teeth of the cliffs below. His breath fogged in the cold night air, mingling with the scent of damp stone and something older—something that made his pulse quicken.

A whisper slithered through the wind: *"You carry the key, yet no one knows it."*

Elias clutched the small, worn key in his pocket. It had been found in the ruins of an ancient temple beneath the city, its surface etched with symbols that defied interpretation. The villagers called it cursed. He called it his only hope.

He turned to the three paths before him, each one leading to a different fate:

1. **Descend into the Abyss** – Follow the path carved into the cliff face, where the whispers seemed to follow his every step. The Abyss was said to be a place between worlds, where lost souls wandered endlessly. If he went, he might find answers—or become lost forever.

2. **Enter the Clockwork Citadel** – The gears of the ancient machine still hummed within the ruins of the old observatory. Its heart was a massive, pulsing orb that hummed with unstable energy. The machine’s creator had vanished decades ago, but rumors spoke of a time-loop prison. Breaking the citadel’s seal could rewrite fate… or doom him to repeat the same mistakes forever.

3. **Seek the Hollow King** – The last remnants of the once-mighty Hollow Empire dwelled in the ruins of the Obsidian Spire. They claimed to be gods, yet their touch withered flesh and twisted minds. Elias had heard tales of a king who could grant wishes—at a price. The Hollow King’s bargain was simple: *"Take what you desire, and offer me a memory."* What would he trade?

The wind howled, and the lantern’s flame guttered out. The choice was his.
