A church has 100 members who've volunteered to bake cookies for the homeless in their local town. If each member baked 10 sheets of cookies, and each sheet has 16 cookies, calculate the total number of cookies the church members baked?
A sheet has 16 cookies, so if each church member baked 10 sheets, they each baked 10 sheets/person * 16 cookies/sheet = <<10*16=160>>160 cookies/person
The total number of church members who decided to volunteer is 100, so in total, they baked 160 cookies/person * 100 people = <<100*160=16000>>16000 cookies
