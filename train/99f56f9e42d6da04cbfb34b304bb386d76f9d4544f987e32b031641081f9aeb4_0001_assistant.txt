Tigers hunt by grabbing and biting their prey on the neck until it dies. Biting the neck keeps the tiger safe from the prey’s antlers and hooves. After the prey has died, the tiger will drag it to a safe place to eat. Normally, the tiger hunts large prey, such as bantengs, sambar, muntjac, gaur and wild pig. The experienced adult tiger hunts carefully, in a way that causes the least injury. Tigers eat about 18 - 40 kg in one meal. They eat the hip of the prey first. A single female tiger hunts every 8 - 8.5 days. They only successfully catch prey on one out of twelve hunts.
