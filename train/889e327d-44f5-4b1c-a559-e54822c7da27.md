The sudden announcement cut through the quiet hum of the café, followed by the sharp intake of breath from the patrons. A uniformed officer stood at the podium, their voice steady despite the tension:

*"Attention, everyone! Due to an ongoing investigation, this establishment is temporarily closed. Please follow the police officer for further instructions."*

A murmur spread as people exchanged glances, some glancing at their watches. A server shifted uneasily at the bar, fingers hovering over the menu, while a child tugged at their parent’s sleeve, eyes wide with confusion. The officer’s words echoed as they clapped their hands once, signaling an abrupt end to the gathering. The last of the laughter faded into silence, leaving behind a heavy, unspoken air.
