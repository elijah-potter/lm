- When you first begin your mix, it is a good idea to make sure none of your tracks are clipping. Adjust the volume of each track so they are in the green on your digital audio workstation's built-in level meters. 

- After making sure all of your levels are okay, it is a good idea to organize your mix, so it is easy to keep track of where everything is. Use your DAW's customization features to set a specific color for each track. Consider grouping similar-sounding instruments to further neaten your project. 

-The next steps are where your creativity comes into play. Experiment and have fun with the different plugins, effects, and audio editing features to make your music unique to you.
