A rusted door creaked open at the edge of Blackthorn Hollow, its hinges groaning like a dying man’s breath. The air smelled of damp earth and something older—burnt sugar, maybe, or the faint metallic tang of blood that never quite dried. A handwritten note, brittle with age, lay folded beneath the threshold:

*"Do not enter. The river remembers."*

Inside, the walls pulsed faintly, shifting colors like living things. A figure in tattered robes stood at the center, their face obscured by a mask of fused glass. The voice that spoke was not human:

*"You were warned. The Hollow does not forgive."*
