On the evening of December 8, 1980, Mark David Chapman murdered John Lennon in New York City outside the Dakota Hotel. Chapman was an American Beatles fan who was enraged and jealous of Lennon's rich lifestyle. Chapman said that he was inspired by Holden Caulfield, the fictional character in J.D. Salinger's novel The Catcher and the Rye, who was a "phony-killer" who loathes hypocrisy.
