Healthy lifestyle does not require a gym pass, personal trainer or many downloadable applications on the smartphone. Some of us would be able to gain benefits even without jogging – “how is this possible” you say?
Walking can give the same effect as running
A study that was based on the National Runners and Walkers Health Study has revealed that walking and jogging produced similar risk reduction for hypertension, hypercholesterolemia, diabetes mellitus, and possibly coronary heart disease (CHD). In this study, the authors examined the energy expenditure and the effect difference in exercise mode (and exercise intensity).
In the study the choice of exercise was up to the subjects, and those who chose higher level of exercise intensity (The runners) had lower risk for incidence of the CHD related factors when compared to the lower intensity exercise (The walkers). However, both types of exercise showed reduction of risk and these results should be used to encourage physical activity regardless of its intensity.
This might be good news for those who are not a fan of vigorous exercises such as jogging. The only thing that they must have in mind is that it is required longer time for walking to gain the same energy expenditure as for jogging.
During exams where we seem to forget about the life outside the frames of the books, it might be wise to remember the positive effects a healthy and active lifestyle has on our abilities to focus. Maybe instead of driving to school or taking the bus, one could plan a small walk to the university a couple of days.
