The **Chronosphinx Observatory**, nestled in the heart of the **Eon Valley**, where the sky above is split into **seven concentric rings** of celestial bodies, each representing a different time dimension:

- **Aurora Prime** (00:00) – The perpetual dawn, where day and night blur in an eternal twilight.
- **Solara** (12:00) – The golden noon, a fleeting moment of perfect clarity, measured in **heartbeats** rather than seconds.
- **Luminara** (18:00) – The twilight hour, where the **moon’s reflection** in the obsidian lake shifts like a liquid mirror, marking the passage of **"echo moments"**—each reflection lasting a lifetime before fading.
- **Oscilla** (24:00) – A realm of **endless tides**, where time flows in **waves**—some faster, some slower, depending on the tide’s gravitational pull.
- **Nyxus** (36:00) – A void where **shadows dance**, and time is measured by the **length of a shadow’s tail** cast by the **Nyx Star**, which never sets but grows longer in the night.
- **Chronos** (48:00) – A labyrinth of **infinite corridors**, where each path splits into **three futures**, and time is a **thread that snaps or weaves**—some observers see only fragments of existence.
- **Eonara** (60:00) – The **final ring**, where time is absolute but **only for those who have passed through the others**. Here, the **Eternal Clock** ticks in **cycles of a thousand years**, but only the wise can interpret its hands correctly.

The inhabitants here, the **Temporal Keepers**, mark time using:
- **Crystal Hourglasses** that drain in **reverse** when viewed from the inside.
- **Echo Stones** that record memories as they play backward.
- **The Whispering Wind**, which only speaks in **future predictions** if you stand in the right position at the right hour.

To visit, one must solve the **7th Riddle of the Sphinx**, whose answer is whispered by the **first star** to rise after the **Great Convergence**—an event that happens **once in a thousand years** (but feels like yesterday to those who witness it).
