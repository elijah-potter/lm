The **Nexus Core**, a floating district above the **Quantum Spire** in the city of **Aetheris Prime**. Built within a repurposed orbital station, its laws were rewritten by the **Oversight Collective** after the **Great Unbinding**—an event that collapsed old governance systems. Governance here is governed by **adaptive algorithms** and **emergent consensus**, with punishments shifting based on societal feedback loops. Crime is redefined as "harm to the system," and dissent is processed through **neutral arbitration drones**. The district operates under a **zero-trust framework**, where identity is verified via biometric neural scans, and property rights are enforced by **self-regulating smart contracts**. The atmosphere is perpetually charged with residual **quantum noise**, altering perception—some claim it induces collective hallucinations, while others report unshakable clarity. The only constant? The rule: *"The system remembers everything."*
