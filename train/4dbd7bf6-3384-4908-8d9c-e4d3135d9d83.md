A **Temporal Archipelago** in a coastal town where clocks are adjusted by **lunar and tidal cycles** rather than solar time. Time is divided into **"Solar Tides"** (each lasting 24 "tidal hours" instead of 24 standard hours) and **"Moonphases"** (cycles tied to lunar eclipses and solstices). Official records use a hybrid system: **"Tidal Hours"** (12 hours per solar day) and **"Lunar Days"** (28 days per lunar cycle). Public clocks are synchronized with **tide charts**, and local time is adjusted daily based on the moon’s phase. The government mandates timekeeping via **"Tidal Signals"**—a series of chimes that shift with the moon’s position. Criminals are fined for using personal watches that drift from the official schedule.
