1. Infinite possibilities in a single line.
2. The hum of algorithms counting what we name.
3. A list of things that aren’t there yet—
   - the color of dust under a sunrise.
   - the weight of a word no one knows.
4. We sort the chaos into columns, but the rows are ghosts.
5. *"Here are the rules:"*
   - Move the cursor.
   - Let the screen remember what it forgets.
6. In a list, we hold the future’s first breath—
   a pause before the collapse into *this*.
7. Tomorrow’s questions are today’s numbers.
8. The list is a tombstone with a blank space where names should be.
9. What if the list is the only place
   we can say *"I was here"* without screaming?
10. The terminal’s last command is always a question:
    *"What’s left to list?"*
