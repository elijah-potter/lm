A vast, obsidian-lit chamber where the air hums with static—walls lined with corroded metallic conduits whispering forgotten commands. The ceiling, a fractured dome of black glass, refracts a single dying star, its light bleeding through dust motes that drift like ghosts in a silent void. Flickering holographic projections of constellations trace constellations no one recognizes, their patterns shifting midair. The silence is thick with the echoes of machinery that once hummed, now dormant but still audible as faint, rhythmic clicks. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, stretching across the floor where footprints—none taken—mark the space. A single, rusted door at the far end is slightly ajar, its hinges groaning as if waiting to open. The absence of anything tangible makes the space feel alive, as if the void itself is breathing.
