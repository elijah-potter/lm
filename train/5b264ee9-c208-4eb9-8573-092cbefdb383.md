**Unspoken Terms of the Unspoken Deal**

*"We will not say the words that bind us.
We will let silence be the contract.
No signature, no witnesses—just the weight of what we leave unsaid.
The rules are carved in the cracks of the floor, not in ink.
If one of us speaks, the other will hear the lie in the unspoken.
No handshakes, no dates—only the pulse of the room when the clock strikes midnight.
The first to break the pact will find the door locked from the inside.
The second will find the first already gone."*

---
*(Tone: Cold, ritualistic, and suffocating—ideal for a scripted or coded extraction where meaning is preserved in ambiguity.)*
