In the quiet of a moonlit forest, where the whispers of wind weave through gnarled roots, a small lantern flickered, casting long shadows across the moss-covered ground. The lantern’s flame danced like a restless spirit, its glow illuminating a single, half-buried artifact: a cracked mirror, its surface etched with strange, spiraling symbols.

A figure stepped from the undergrowth, cloaked in shadows, their breath visible in the cold air. They held a rusted dagger, its blade reflecting the dim light like a broken promise. The mirror’s glass shivered at the touch, revealing fragments of a forgotten past—faces of strangers, a battlefield, and a city in ruins.

The figure knelt beside the mirror, their fingers tracing the symbols. The air grew heavier, the forest still, as if the world held its breath. Then, in a rush of colors and light, the mirror shattered into a thousand fragments, each glowing with a different hue—violet, gold, and crimson. The symbols dissolved into swirling patterns, painting the night sky above with an aurora of forgotten dreams.

A sudden gust of wind howled through the trees, carrying the scent of rain and old magic. The figure stood, their eyes wide with wonder. The lantern’s flame guttered out, leaving only the remnants of light to guide them forward—a trail of mystery and something far greater than they could ever imagine.
