## Bookstore Serendipity (Seattle, WA)

I was browsing the poetry section at Left Bank Books on Saturday afternoon, utterly lost in a collection of Rilke, when you appeared. You were examining a volume of Neruda, your brow furrowed in concentration, and the light from the large front window caught the copper highlights in your hair. There was a quiet intensity about you that drew my attention, a stillness that contrasted pleasantly with the gentle murmur of conversation filling the store. I remember thinking you seemed utterly at peace amongst the shelves. I briefly considered saying something, a simple observation about the beauty of the language, but hesitated. You smiled faintly, then moved on to the travel section. The scent of aged paper and leather, a comforting familiarity, filled the air as I watched you disappear between the stacks. I left shortly after, carrying my Rilke, but feeling a lingering sense of what might have been. 

Do you recall a woman with dark hair and a blue scarf?
