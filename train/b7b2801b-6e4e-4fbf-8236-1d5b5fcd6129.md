Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny bunny named Pip. Pip loved to hop around the grass, eating clover and chasing butterflies. One sunny morning, Pip found a shiny, round berry near his burrow.

"Mmm, what a tasty berry!" Pip said, sniffing it. But then, he noticed something strange—it was warm to the touch.

Just then, a small, curious mouse named Milo came running. "Hi, Pip! Did you find something fun?" Milo asked.

"Yeah! Look!" Pip hopped excitedly. "I found a magical berry!"

Milo’s eyes sparkled. "Can I try it?"

Pip handed it to Milo. They both took a tiny bite—and *poof!* The berry turned into a rainbow of colors. The sky turned pink, the trees became green, and the garden sparkled like diamonds.

The animals in the garden cheered. The squirrels danced, the birds sang, and even the old turtle grinned. "This is the best day ever!" the turtle said.

From that day on, Pip and Milo shared the magical berry every morning, and the garden became the happiest place in the whole world. And they all lived very, very happily ever after!
