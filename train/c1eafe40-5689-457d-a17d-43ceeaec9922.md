*"The city of Neo-Venice sprawls along a shifting delta where rivers of black water carve new paths daily. Bridges, once solid, now tilt like spiderwebs; skyscrapers rise and collapse in weeks. Buildings repaint themselves with bioluminescent algae, their facades rearranging into new forms midday. Citizens wear augmented reality lenses that flicker between past and future versions of themselves, but no one trusts what they see—every 'memory' is a glitch. The streets hum with the constant hum of machinery that breathes and grows, feeding on the very air of the city. The people move through this labyrinth of change, their routines dissolving like sugar in water, while the system adapts faster than they can adapt to it."*
