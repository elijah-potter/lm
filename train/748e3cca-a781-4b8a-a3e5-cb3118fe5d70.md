A lone figure stands at the edge of a mist-shrouded lake, their shadow stretching long across the damp stones. The air hums with an eerie silence, broken only by the distant cry of a gull. In their hand, they clutched a single, bloodstained feather, its dark hue glinting in the faint light filtering through the trees.

The figure turned, their gaze fixed on the horizon where a small, abandoned cabin perched like a forgotten relic. Inside, a mirror hung crookedly over the hearth, reflecting not their own face but a stranger’s—one who wore the same feather in their coat. The cabin’s door creaked open, revealing a hallway that spiraled upward, ending in a door marked with the same bloodstained symbol carved into the stone.

The figure stepped inside, the door sealing shut behind them. The hallway stretched endlessly, leading them back to the same lake, the same stones, and the same bloodstained feather in their hand. The air smelled of iron and old wood. The trees grew taller, the gulls more distant, but the stranger’s reflection in the mirror never changed.

The figure laughed, shaking the feather free. It fluttered into the darkness, landing on the water’s surface with a soft *plop*. The lake began to stir, and the mist curled upward, forming shapes that twisted into the figure’s face—then the stranger’s. The cabin’s roof split open, revealing a sky of swirling colors, and the figure found themselves standing once more at the edge of the lake, the same stones beneath their feet, the same bloodstained feather in their hand.

The stranger’s voice echoed: *"You always end up here."* The figure looked down. The feather was gone.
