The Mediterranean Flour Moth is a light brown moth with indistinct wavy dark bars across the wings. The hind wings have a fringe of scales on the trailing edge of each wing. The larva is a white caterpillar with a brown head.
Flour moth, Pantry moth, Mill moth
wing span 20mm - 22mm. length 10mm - 14mm. Larva length to 15mm.
The Mediterranean Flour Moth is a pest of stored grain and flour products.
