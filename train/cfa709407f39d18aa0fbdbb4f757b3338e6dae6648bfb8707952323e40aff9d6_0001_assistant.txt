August 12, 2010

Dear Diary,
Today was my first day of high school. I woke up at 6 am to get ready, even though the bus did not come until 7:30 am. I had to make sure that I looked my best. So, the first thing I did when I woke up was iron my school uniform. On the bus ride to school, I was so nervous. My stomach was in knots, and I could not stop shaking. But once I made it to school, I saw my best friend Julie waiting on me in front of the entrance. I didn't feel nervous after that.
