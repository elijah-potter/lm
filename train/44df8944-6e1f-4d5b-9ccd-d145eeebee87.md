A **Qixiangxiang** (秘密保存袋) – a reinforced leather pouch with hidden compartments, embedded with magnetic seals and a pressure-sensitive pad on the lid. The outer fabric is woven with invisible threads that shift under ultraviolet light to reveal a hidden cipher when opened, requiring a specific phrase to unlock internal sections. The pouch’s weight shifts when the secret is accessed, emitting a faint resonance detected by a secondary sensor. Used by intelligence operatives for classified transmissions.
