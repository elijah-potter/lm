1. Reuse glass jars to ferment food. Glass jars are the best option when it comes to food fermentation. From fermenting veggies to making an occasional jar of salsa, you'll be keeping the food clean and in the best fermenting environment.
2. Use them as a sourdough starter. Though you can use a traditional ceramic bowl to rise up your dough, a mason glass jar or a glass jar of any type can also do. This way, you'll have more options for your utensils to mix cakes and serve salads in the bowls.
3. Spices. You don't need to spend on new spice jars from the store every time. Just use up your existing freed-up jars to store up your spices.
4. Use them to store dried herbs. Glass jars offer the best breathable and elegant storage option for your herbal leaves and even vegetables. Once you've dried the foraged items, simply put them in your empty jars.
5. Use it to store your bulk goods. From honey to coconut flour and nuts, glass jars offer you suitable long-term storage for your bulk foods.
