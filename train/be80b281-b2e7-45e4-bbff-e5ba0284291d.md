A sudden tremor shook the conference table as the door burst open, revealing the lead engineer with a clenched jaw. "The launch was delayed—again," he muttered, dropping a crumpled blueprint onto the desk. The team froze. Around him, whispers dissolved into a hushed silence, each mind racing with the weight of what had been planned.

The project coordinator, arms crossed, leaned toward the screen—live footage of the launch site flickered with a red alert: *"Unforeseen atmospheric conditions detected."* The chief scientist’s fingers drummed the table, frustration bleeding into every line of their face. *"We can’t just scrap this,"* they whispered, but the air between them crackled with the tension of a decision already made.

Outside, the wind howled through the empty hangar, carrying the ghost of a launch date that was now just a shadow of what could have been.
