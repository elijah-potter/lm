Once upon a time, in a cozy green house with a big tree in the backyard, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown ears and a heart full of curiosity.

One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to explore. "Mmm, fresh grass!" he nibbled. Suddenly, he heard a tiny voice, "Help! My berry is falling!"

Benny looked down and saw a little bird sitting on a branch. The bird had a shiny blue berry that kept rolling away. Benny thought, "I can help!" and gently caught the berry with his paws.

"Thank you, Benny!" chirped the bird. "You saved my berry!"

Benny grinned and said, "You know, sharing berries is fun!" The bird agreed, and they both hopped around, playing together until Benny’s belly started to rumble.

"Time for my snack!" Benny said, and he ate a juicy carrot. As he munched, he thought, "Maybe I should help more people today."

Later, Benny saw a little mouse digging a hole. "Help!" Benny called. He shared his extra carrots and helped the mouse finish her tunnel. They cheered together.

That evening, Benny curled up with his friends in the big tree, full of happiness. "Today was the best adventure ever!" Benny said, as he snuggled into his soft bed. And with that, Benny Bunny drifted off to sleep, dreaming of more fun and kindness.
