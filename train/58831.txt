Use these Planet cards in a variety of fun and interactive ways to help teach about the Solar System! Cards included: Asteroid Belt, Earth, Jupiter, Mars, Mercury, Pluto, Saturn, Sun, Uranus, Venus, and a rocket ship. Characterics for each card are also included.
How to use this product:
1. Cut out each planet and characteristic card. Glue each planet name to the matching characteristic to create flash cards. Students can use these flash cards to study or as game cards.
2. Print cards on cardstock and laminate for durability. Cut out each planet and characteristic card. Use these cards to play space memory.
3. Cut out each planet and characteristic card. Glue each planet name to the matching characteristic. Punch a hole through each card and tie to a hanger in planet order. You now have a Solar System hanging mobile!
I use the rocket ship card for students to put their names on for the mobiles.
Cards are in black and white so students can color
