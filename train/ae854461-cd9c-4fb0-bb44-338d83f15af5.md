**Silent Library no. 7 – The Whispering Archipelago**
A series of floating bookcases suspended between towering cedar groves in the northern fjords of **Veythar**. The air hums with the static of ancient manuscripts, and the wind carries the scent of aged parchment and pine. No clocks tick here—only the slow pulse of a central **Echo Stone** that refracts thoughts into the walls. Visitors sit in **thought-springs** (small pools where their reflections distort into fragmented ideas) or kneel in the **Cave of Unspoken**, where the walls are lined with the last words of those who never spoke aloud.

The **Thought Keepers**—a council of blind scribes—read aloud the echoes of the crowd. Some seek solitude; others, the chaos of **Synapse Dials** (collapsing bridges that force divergent thinking). The **Mirage Fountain** reflects not themselves, but the *possible versions* of their questions. The entrance is marked by a **Chime of Doubt**, a bell that only rings when the visitor’s mind is truly open.

*Warning*: The **Garden of Forgotten Questions** blooms here—plants that grow from queries no one remembers asking. Some say the library is a prison for ideas that didn’t belong to anyone. Others claim the stone walls *remember* and rewrite them.
