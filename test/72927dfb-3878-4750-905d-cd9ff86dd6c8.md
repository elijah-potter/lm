Central Business District (CBD): Central Park at the core with grid-based streets radiating from a central roundabout (e.g., 5th Ave/Union Square).
Skyscraper belt: High-rise clusters along 34th–43rd St (Manhattan) and 11th–20th Ave (Brooklyn).
Subway infrastructure: Elevated tracks (BMT/LIRR) alongside elevated highways (I-278, I-95).
Waterfront: Hudson River (east) and East River (west) with bridges (Brooklyn Bridge, Williamsburg Bridge).
Subway tunnels: Crossings beneath 14th St (Lexington Ave Line), under Central Park (Indiana Ave Line).
Downtown financial hub: Financial District (Fulton St–South St) with skyscrapers and underpasses (I-278 underpass).
Residential zones: Upper West Side (100–140th St) with elevated subway tracks (A/C Lines).
Green spaces: Battery Park (South Street Seaport) and Riverside Park (Hudson River).
Freeways: I-95 (north-south) and I-278 (east-west) with express lanes and transit lanes.
Airport: JFK (Queens) with express subway (A/C Lines) and I-495 (Long Island Expressway).
