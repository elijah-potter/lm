A dimly lit alleyway at twilight, the air thick with the scent of damp concrete and distant traffic. A figure stands against a weathered brick wall, hands clasped tightly around a chilled coffee cup. Their back is turned toward the flickering glow of a nearby streetlamp, casting long shadows that stretch unnaturally. The city hums beyond—a distant siren, a car door slamming, the occasional murmur of voices. No one else is here, yet the absence of sound feels heavy, like the weight of anticipation. They watch the pavement, fingers tracing the rim of the cup, unsure of when—or if—the wait will end.
