A vast, endless ocean of swirling currents, shifting like a living storm—darker than the void of space yet brighter than the purest diamonds.

Its depths are a cathedral of crushing silence, where the weight of forgotten giants presses down like a thousand-ton anvil. The surface mirrors a thousand suns, fractured by waves that crackle like embers.

Cities drift on its currents, floating islands of chaos—towering spires pierce the mist like the ribs of some ancient beast, and the streets are paved with glass that reflects the past.

Here, time is a river, flowing fast where the currents are strong, slow where they stagnate. The air hums with the ghosts of voices, whispers of a world that no longer exists.

The horizon is a line of ink, bold as a stroke of a master’s pen. Beyond it, the world fades into something unseen, neither land nor sea but a shadow of its own making.
