Once upon a time in a bright and sunny garden, there was a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown ears and a tail that wiggled when he was happy. Benny loved to hop around, play, and explore his garden.

One morning, Benny woke up and saw a big, shiny red apple on the tree. "Mmm, yummy!" Benny thought. He hopped up and gently picked the apple. It was so soft and sweet. He ate a big bite and felt happy.

As Benny was eating, a little butterfly with bright orange wings fluttered down. "Hello, Benny!" the butterfly said. "I’m lost. My home is far away, and I don’t know how to get there."

Benny looked at the butterfly. "Don’t worry, little friend," he said. "I can help you. Follow me, and we’ll go together!"

Benny hopped faster, flipping over leaves and skipping stones. The butterfly fluttered close, learning to follow him. Soon, they reached a big, beautiful meadow with flowers in every color.

"Wow!" said the butterfly. "This is amazing! Thank you, Benny!"

Benny grinned. "You’re welcome! Let’s play together now. We can make a flower crown and dance in the sunshine!"

The butterfly made a flower crown with yellow and purple petals, and they danced happily in the meadow. As the sun started to set, Benny and the butterfly snuggled under a big, soft blanket of leaves. They fell asleep, dreaming of more adventures.

And that’s how Benny the bunny and the little butterfly became the best friends in the whole garden. The end.
