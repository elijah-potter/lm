Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown ears and a tail that wiggled when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his favorite flower, the Sunny Blossom, glowing in his garden. He hopped outside, picked the flower, and smiled. "What a beautiful day!" he thought.

Suddenly, Benny heard a tiny voice. "Help me, Benny! My flower is stuck!" It was a little mouse named Mimi. Mimi had a tiny house made of acorns and twigs, and her favorite flower was stuck in a bush.

Benny hopped over to help. "Don't worry, Mimi!" he said. With gentle hands, he untangled the flower and helped her pluck it. "Here you go, Mimi!" Benny said, giving her the flower.

Mimi giggled. "Thank you, Benny! Now my flower is happy again!"

Benny smiled. "You know, Mimi, sharing is fun!" He gave her his extra carrot snack he had saved for later.

Mimi’s flower blossomed even brighter, and Benny’s garden became even more colorful. The two friends played together, sharing snacks and making new friends like a tiny squirrel named Sammy.

Every day, Benny and Mimi helped each other, and their garden became the happiest place in the whole neighborhood. Benny learned that being kind and sharing made everyone feel good. And that was how Benny and Mimi became the best of friends.
