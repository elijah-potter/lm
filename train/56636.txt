Some people believe that the account of the days of creation is merely a literary framework. While it can be convenient to group the six days into two groups of three, that should not imply that the days are not literal.
In our final session, everything comes very close to home. We study the Garden of Eden and the relationship of Adam to Jesus, understanding that the Gospel itself depends on our correct understanding of what the Bible teaches about Adam.
