Inspired by games such as The Binding of Isaac and Nuclear Throne, Invisible Brigade is a twin-stick shooter set in a dieselpunk world that is in eternal war with the Empire. Few regions of the world have remained outside their control. You and an underground ragtag group of resistance fighters are in the heart of the beast and learn of a rumor that the Empire is completing work on "the Machine"--a state-of-the-art aircraft that will decimate the skies. With no time to spare, your group readies up for a suicide mission--one that will require swift reflexes and a will of steel.
