What Are Ethereum Improvement Proposals?

Slide 2:

Ethereum Improvement Protocols (EIPs)
    • Suggest changes to Ethereum's code or protocols
    • Can suggest changes to Ethereum's core code
    • Can also suggest new standards for Ethereum-based applications

Slide 3:

EIP Management Team
    • EIP editors manage the process of submitting new EIPs
    • "Ethereum Cat Herders" arrange meetings on new EIPs

Slide 4:

Important EIPs
    • EIP-20 (became ERC-20)
    • EIP-137 (became known as Ethereum Name Service (ENS))
    • EIP-721 (became ERC-721)
    • EIP-1559
    • EIP-1155 (became ERC-1155)
    • EIP-1679 (became the Istanbul update)
