**Local officials in the historic coastal town of Port Haven report a sudden rise in seismic activity today, with minor tremors detected across the downtown district. Authorities confirm that no damage has been reported yet, but residents are advised to prepare for potential aftershocks. The town’s ancient stone bridges, a major tourist attraction, show no visible cracks, though the city’s geologist warns that this could be an early sign of instability in the region’s fault lines. Emergency crews are monitoring the situation closely.**

**Residents near the port have reported seeing unusual marine activity—whales and dolphins congregating near the harbor, which experts attribute to the seismic shifts. The town’s fishing fleet has been temporarily grounded as a precaution.**

**No injuries have been reported, but the mayor has called for a mandatory town meeting tomorrow evening to discuss long-term measures for seismic safety, including reinforcing old buildings and expanding early-warning systems.**
