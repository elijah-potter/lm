Unesco Hellas is organizing a competition which is called "Tribes of the world" and your school is going to take part. The prize of the competition is a trip to the country where the tribes live. You are going to spend a few days with them and learn more about their daily lives, customs and traditions.
What you have to do in order to win the prize:
1. Design a poster which best represents the tribe you chose to work with and send it to the competition.
2. Write a report describing the tribe you chose to work with and proposing solutions to a problem the specific tribe is facing.
3. Create a powerpoint presentation for the tribe you chose to work with in order to present it to your classmates and to the local community.
The poster, the report and the presentation are going to be published in the Unesco website, in your local hometown newspaper, in your class blog and in your class facebook !
