The waiter approached the table, setting down a fresh menu. "Would you like to try something new?" he asked, eyes flicking toward the chef’s station.

"Ah, yes, I see. Well, if you’d like, I could recommend something different." The waiter picked up the menu again, his tone polite but distracted.
