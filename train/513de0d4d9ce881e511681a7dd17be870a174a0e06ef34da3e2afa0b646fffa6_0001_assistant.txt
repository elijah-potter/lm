No problem. Below are five candle scents you may like:

1. Sandalwood: These candles have sort of a smooth and woody scent and are a popular option for both men and women.
2. Leather: These candles tend to be mainly leather-scented, usually mixed with some other complimentary scents.
3. Coffee: Coffee-scented candles are perfect for any time of day, and some may have notes of nutmeg, cinnamon, and clove.
4. Pine: These candles tend to have a rugged and refreshing scent and are perfect for the outdoorsman.
5. Ocean: These candles come in many varieties and can feature different ocean-related scents, including bergamot, sea jasmine, vetiver, or sea moss.
