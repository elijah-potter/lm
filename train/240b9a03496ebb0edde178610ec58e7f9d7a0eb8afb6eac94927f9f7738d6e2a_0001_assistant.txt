Absolutely! Here are some poems from female poets that are not sad:

1. "Hope is the thing with feathers" by Emily Dickinson is a poem about getting through hard times by staying positive
2. "On the Pulse of Morning" by Maya Angelou was read at Bill Clinton's inauguration and is about unifying America
3. "The Summer Day" by Mary Oliver is a poem about contemplating your life's meaning and then trying to make the best of it
