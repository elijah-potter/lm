**Decision:**
Rejected expansion of the corporate data center due to projected energy costs exceeding $1.2M annually, opting instead for a hybrid cloud model with AWS and Azure to reduce long-term infrastructure spend by 30%.

**Rationale:**
- Financial: $4.8M annual savings vs. $6.6M fixed costs for full on-prem expansion.
- Sustainability: 40% reduction in carbon footprint by offloading 70% of workloads to cloud.
- Risk Mitigation: 99.99% uptime SLA compliance maintained via multi-cloud redundancy.
- Scalability: Dynamic resource allocation via auto-scaling groups ensures cost efficiency under variable demand spikes.

**Implementation:**
- Migrated 85% of legacy systems to AWS EC2 and Azure VMs within 12 months.
- Established cost monitoring dashboards to cap spend at $800K/year.
- New hybrid model reduced IT budget by 22% YoY.
