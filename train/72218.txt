Of or pertaining to Morocco or the Moors; in the style of the Moors.
a style of architecture common in Spain from the 13th to 16th centuries; characterized by the horseshoe (Moorish) arch
The adjective is used for the design style characteristic of the inhabitants of North West Africa and Southern Spain, of mixed Arab and Berber descent.
Having a quality or appearance resembling Moor artwork and architecture as seen in North West Afria (Morocco).
Refers specifically to the cultural attributes common to Muslim Spain and the Maghrib after the twelfth century.
