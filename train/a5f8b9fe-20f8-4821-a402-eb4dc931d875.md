The air hums with the faint scent of damp earth and aged wood. Rusty iron fittings, once gleaming, now patina into a dull bronze, catching the light like scattered coins. Nearby, a worn wooden floorboards crackle underfoot, revealing dark, uneven spaces beneath. A damp, moss-laden stone wall rises, its surface smooth yet gritty, with faint crevices where lichen and small fungi have settled in hidden nooks. The atmosphere is thick with the scent of decaying leaves and dampness, a mix of ancient decay and forgotten life.
