Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around the garden every day, chasing his favorite thing: bright yellow daisies.

One morning, Benny woke up with a silly thought. He wanted to make his garden even happier. So, he started digging a tiny hole near the flowers. Soon, he found a shiny, round treasure buried there. Benny picked it up and felt it sparkle in his paws. It wasn’t just any treasure—it was a magic seed!

Curious, Benny planted the seed in his favorite daisy patch. He watered it gently and whispered, "Let’s grow something wonderful together!" The seed began to wiggle, then turn green, and soon, a tiny little plant sprouted. Benny’s heart raced with joy.

Every day, Benny took care of his plant. He gave it sun and water, and soon, the little plant grew taller and stronger. The daisies around it started to smile and wave. One sunny afternoon, Benny looked up and saw something magical: his magic seed had grown into a towering, beautiful flower with petals as soft as cotton and a tiny, glowing heart in the center!

Benny’s garden had never looked so happy! The other animals came to visit—the squirrels chattered, the birds sang, and even Benny’s friend, a kind old owl, perched on a branch. Benny smiled as he watched the sun set, painting the sky in colors of pink and orange.

And from that day on, Benny knew that magic wasn’t just in shiny treasures—it was in the little things he did, like planting seeds and taking care of his garden. Benny hopped home, his heart full, and dreamed of growing even more wonders tomorrow.
