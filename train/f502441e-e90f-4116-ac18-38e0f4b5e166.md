Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around, eat carrots, and play with his best friend, a bright yellow butterfly named Buttercup.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw Buttercup fluttering happily. "Good morning, Buttercup!" Benny said, his nose twitching with excitement. "Let’s play outside!"

They ran through the tall grass, chasing butterflies and collecting colorful flowers. Benny picked a red daisy, and Buttercup a yellow sunflower. They giggled as they jumped over small rocks and splashed in a tiny puddle.

Then, Benny noticed something sparkly in the garden. It was a shiny, blue treasure box! "Look, Buttercup! A treasure box!" Benny said, excitedly. Buttercup giggled and asked, "What’s inside?"

Benny opened the box carefully. Inside were tiny golden acorns, rainbow-colored beads, and a soft, fluffy cloud. They played with the acorns, made bead necklaces, and even built a cloud fort.

As the sun started to set, Benny and Buttercup felt sleepy. They snuggled together in the grass, listening to the crunch of leaves and the soft sounds of the night. "Goodnight, Benny!" Buttercup whispered, tucking Benny’s ears. Benny closed his eyes, dreaming of happy adventures with Buttercup.

And that’s how Benny the bunny and Buttercup the butterfly made the best friends in the garden, playing, laughing, and dreaming together every day.
