A hollow vow etched in ink that never bloomed,
The weight of words left unfulfilled—each syllable,
A silent contract with teeth too sharp to chew.
The clock ticks, yet the hands move slow,
Each passing hour a thief of what was meant.
"Tomorrow," whispers the ghost of the past,
While now is just a shadow of what we swore.
The storm inside me rages, yet I stand firm,
A promise buried under layers of doubt and fear.
Some promises are chains, some are lies—
This one? It’s both.
