The abandoned library’s walls whisper in the damp, echoing between shelves of dust-choked tomes. A low, rhythmic hum pulses through the hollowed-out floorboards, as if the building itself breathes. The air smells of old parchment and damp earth, thick with the scent of forgotten secrets. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, stretching across the floor like fingers reaching for something unseen. The books on the shelves seem to tilt slightly, as if leaning toward the sound of unseen voices. At night, when the wind howls through the broken windows, the shelves groan—not from the wind, but as if they’re holding their breath, waiting.
