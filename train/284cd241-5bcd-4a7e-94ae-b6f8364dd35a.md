*Official Holiday Declaration (Administrative Division, Year 20XX)*

**Objective**: Streamline bureaucratic processes by eliminating unnecessary steps.
**Activities**:
- Mandatory "paperwork sprint" to audit and eliminate redundant forms.
- Corporate retreat to "find joy in data entry" (mandatory).
- Optional: "No-Reply Day" – all mail marked as "unresponsive" (no follow-up required).

**Regulations**:
- **No meetings** beyond 15 minutes unless explicitly labeled "urgent."
- **Deadlines** extended by 24 hours for all non-essential tasks.
- **Penalties**: Only applicable to those who refuse to document inefficiencies.

**Symbol**: A single, universally recognized "✅" stamp (to be applied to every process).
