1. The first fast food company in the U.S. was White Castle, a burger restaurant. Until that time burgers were considered a low-quality fair type food. 
2. The first White Castle restaurant was established in 1921 in Wichita, Kansas. The restaurant featured an open kitchen where people could watch their food being prepared.
3. The founders of White Castle were a short-order cook named Walter Anderson and a former reporter named Edgar W. "Billy" Ingram.
