**Important Notes & Considerations:**

*   **Legal Review:**  It's always a good idea to have a legal professional review any resignation letter, especially if you have concerns about potential legal issues.
*   **Company Policy:**  Review your company’s resignation policy to ensure you are adhering to all requirements (notice period, return of property, etc.).
*   **Specificity:**  The level of detail about your reasons is a personal choice. Be honest, but also be mindful of how your words might be perceived.  Avoid overly negative or accusatory language.
*   **Delivery:** Consider how you will deliver the letter – in person, via email, or a combination.  A face-to-face conversation is generally preferred, followed by a formal written resignation.
*   **Maintain Professionalism:**  Even if you are leaving on less-than-ideal terms, maintain a professional demeanor throughout the resignation process.
