Two men, **Daniel** and **Elias**, stood outside the dimly lit bar at midnight. The air smelled of stale beer and cigarette smoke.

*"Another round?"* Elias asked, nudging Daniel’s shoulder.

Daniel hesitated. *"I don’t know. I’ve been thinking about it."*

Elias grinned. *"You’re always thinking. You’re the one who brought up the old song last week—about how every second counts."*

Daniel’s face paled. *"I was—was just remembering something. Not—this wasn’t about that."*

Elias leaned in. *"Was it?"*

A loud crash echoed from inside the bar. Elias grabbed Daniel’s arm. *"Come on, let’s get out of here."*

Daniel stumbled behind him, but his mind replayed the argument from last night—the one he’d *forgotten* to tell Elias about. The one that made him avoid eye contact now.

Outside, Elias exhaled sharply. *"You’re hiding something."*

Daniel swallowed. *"I’m just—worried."*

Elias’s fingers tightened. *"About what?"*

Silence.

Daniel looked away. *"About what I said."*

Elias turned, his expression unreadable. *"You always say things that don’t mean anything."*

*"No,"* Daniel whispered. *"I mean them."*

Elias’s jaw clenched. *"Then why won’t you let me in?"*
