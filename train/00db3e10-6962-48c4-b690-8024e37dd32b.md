**Harbor of Maracaibo (Venezuela):**
Fishermen cast nets into the turquoise waters of Lake Maracaibo, while dockside vendors sell fresh seafood, roasted corn, and *arepas* (cornmeal patties). Locals gather at *playas* (beaches) to swim and sunbathe, while tourists explore the colonial architecture of the city. Nearby, *parches* (street markets) offer handicrafts, cigars, and traditional *arepas* from *caseríos* (small eateries). During festivals, colorful *parrandas* (street processions) and *toros de carne* (beef roasts) dominate the streets. The region’s oil wealth fuels energy hubs, contrasting with its historic fishing culture.
