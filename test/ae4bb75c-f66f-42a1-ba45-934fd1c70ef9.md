Once there was a little bunny named Pip who loved to hop around the meadow every morning. One sunny day, Pip spotted a shiny red berry that sparkled like a tiny treasure. "Mmm, I wonder what this tastes like," Pip thought, and he nibbled it.

Suddenly, a tiny voice whispered, "Help! Help!" Pip looked around and saw a little mouse named Milo stuck in a burrow. "Milo, what happened?" Pip asked. Milo giggled, "My burrow door got stuck! Can you help me?"

Pip hopped closer and saw the door wasn’t moving. "I’ll try," he said. He gave it a gentle push, but it didn’t budge. "Maybe I need to wiggle my paws," Pip decided. He tried wiggling and then hopped back and forth. Finally, the door creaked open, and Milo popped out. "Thank you, Pip!" Milo cheered.

Pip grinned. "You’re welcome, Milo! Want to play tag?" Milo clapped his paws together. "Tag it is!"

They ran around the meadow, giggling and laughing. Pip had made a new friend, and Milo had a new adventure. As the sun began to set, they curled up together in the grass, sharing stories and berries. Pip knew that even the smallest paws could make the biggest friendships.
