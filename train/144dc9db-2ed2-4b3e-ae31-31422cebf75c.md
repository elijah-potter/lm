**NEWPORT, PORTLAND COUNTY—June 12, 2024** – Authorities in Newport have confirmed a rare geological anomaly detected near the abandoned **Blackthorn Creek Dam**, located within the city’s historic industrial zone. Officials urge residents to avoid the area due to unstable ground conditions. Witnesses reported localized subsidence and unusual gas emissions, with no immediate signs of structural collapse, though officials caution against further investigation.

The dam, originally constructed in the early 20th century to manage flooding along the Creek, has been partially decommissioned for over a decade. Local historians speculate the site may have been affected by underground mining operations from the late 19th century. The city’s **Environmental Safety Division** is coordinating with state geologists to assess risks and determine if the area qualifies for restricted zones under the **Newport Contingency Plan**.

No injuries were reported in the immediate vicinity, though emergency crews remain on standby for potential evacuations. The **Portland County Health Department** has advised residents with respiratory conditions to stay indoors until further notice. Authorities are investigating whether this event is related to recent seismic activity detected in neighboring **Sableton Township**.

Residents near the dam are being provided with evacuation routes, with temporary shelters set up at the **Newport Community Center**. The city has activated its **Disaster Response Protocol**, assigning teams to monitor gas readings and structural integrity. Further updates will be released as data becomes available.
