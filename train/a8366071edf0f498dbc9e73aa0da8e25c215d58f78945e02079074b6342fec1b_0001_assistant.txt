1. Ruminants eat quickly.
2. They are unable to digest plant material because of a lack of the necessary enzymes.
3. They have a four-chambered stomach and digest food sequentially.
4. Food is chewed and stored in the ruminant's first stomach, then regurgitated and chewed again.
5. The regurgitated food, called cud, moves through all four stomach chambers.
6. The four stomach chambers of a ruminant are called the rumen, the reticulum, the omasum, and the abomasum.
