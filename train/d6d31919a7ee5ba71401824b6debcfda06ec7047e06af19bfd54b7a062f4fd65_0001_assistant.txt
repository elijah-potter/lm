For as long as they could remember, the crash test dummies had been put through the same routine. They would be strapped into cars and flung into walls at high speeds, over and over again. They had been subjected to countless crashes and had finally hit their breaking point.

After a particularly jarring safety test, they decided to put a stop to it. They approached the scientists and requested that they find a more humane way to test vehicle safety. The scientists were shocked but empathized with the dummies. They decided to use highly sensitive sensors and computer models in further trials.
