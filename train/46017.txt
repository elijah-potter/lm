How to develop the sense of smell of a newborn
1. You can use your own clothes as a “quilt” for your baby. It can effectively soothe your baby. It is also the best item for your baby’s olfactory training. It is pure and natural, and it works well.
2. Change the clothes with “sun fragrance” for your baby. Baby clothes that have been sterilized by sun exposure will not only benefit your baby’s health but also the fragrance of the sun will also enrich your baby’s sense of smell.
3. Taking advantage of the opportunity to give your baby a bath, you can also develop your baby’s sense of smell. Before you take a bath, give your baby a scent of the shower gel, which will lighten your baby’s eyes! The next time he takes a shower, he can turn around and look for the source of the fragrance!
How to develop the baby’s taste
Training the taste ability of newborn babies can promote the rapid development of a baby’s taste intelligence. But be careful what you put in your baby. In some parts of China, they like to give a newborn baby a drop of Coptis Chinensis because the baby hasn’t tasted any other flavours. This method is not advisable. Occasionally, a drop of cooled boiled water is drawn to the baby’s tongue, so he can feel the difference between breast milk and water.
