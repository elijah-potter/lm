Amidst the Indian Ocean's vast expanse,
Where legends of pirates and daring advance,
Beyond the Caribbean's well-known tale,
St. Mary's Island, a pirate's trail.

Peg-legged buccaneers and captains bold,
Sailed the waves, seeking treasures untold,
Madagascar's shores, their secret domain,
A base of operations, a pirate's reign.

In the late 17th century's embrace,
Armed freebooters, a fearsome chase,
They raided European and Asian ships,
With Madagascar's haven upon their lips.

Legends spoke of Libertalia's grand design,
A utopian colony, tales intertwined,
Democracy's echoes and native embrace,
A pirate's dream, a fabled place.

Yet, the truth remains shrouded, veiled in haze,
While Libertalia's myth continues to blaze,
St. Mary's Island, where reality stands,
A pirate stronghold on distant lands.

Around 1,500 souls called it home,
A bustling haven where pirates would roam,
Captain Kidd, Thomas Tew, and Every as well,
Found refuge in St. Mary's tales to tell.

With an underground network of trade,
Exotic goods plundered, a pirate's raid,
Ships laden with treasures from India's shore,
Targets for buccaneers seeking more.

The booty acquired, a valuable prize,
Sold by locals with cunning and guise,
To merchants corrupt in New York's streets,
Or Boston's alleys, where deceit meets.

In 1695, a daring feat was done,
Henry Every's fleet, a treasure ship won,
From the Great Mogul's Indian wealth,
A fortune amassed, tales of immense stealth.

A fight ensued, bloodshed on the sea,
As Every triumphed, his victory decree,
The spoils he claimed, a staggering hoard,
Worth millions in gold, a pirate's reward.

St. Mary's Island, a chapter profound,
Where pirate legends and treasures abound,
A saga of piracy, daring and thrill,
Etched in history, living still.
