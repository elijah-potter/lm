Merlin - the legend of king arthur
One of the most famous characters during the Arthurian times was Merlin, a powerful magician, prophet and advisor. In the defining moment of Arthur’s career, Merlin arranged the sword-in-the-stone contest by which Arthur became king.
Later, the magician met the mystic Lady of the Lake and persuaded her to present the King with the magical sword, Excalibur. This unique program traces the origins of Merlin, and his role in the magical Kingdom of Camelot.
