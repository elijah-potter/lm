- Pink is the color that you see on flamingos and certain jellyfish.
- Red is the color of the fur of certain apes. 
- Brown animals include monkeys, bulls, hippos, bears, and dogs.
- Chicks have soft, yellow, fuzzy, feathers, and some finches are yellow, too.
- Clownfish are orange with different colored stripes.
- Many frogs are different shades of green.
- Peacocks have beautiful feathers in shades of blue. 
- Lots of sharks have tough, gray skin. 
- Lions are covered in fierce, tan fur. 
- Violet-backed starlings are birds with purple feathers.
