I surely can! Here are some of the ways you can get involved in community theatre.

1. Find community theatre groups - One way to get yourself into the community theatre scene is by joining local theatre groups. You can search for local community theatres on google or google maps, although they don't always have websites so they can be tricky to find. Once you locate a local theatre, go to its website if it has one. If you want to act, you can try to find an auditions page on the site. If there isn't a site, you could also look for the theatre on Facebook. 
2. Get notified about auditions and events -  You can also find ways to get involved with community theatre by getting notified about events and auditions. You could sign up for their newsletter or by looking for upcoming auditions or events for community theatre groups. You can also ask via e-mail.
3. Volunteer - There is more to theatre than just acting. There is also set building and painting, prop management, costuming, makeup, an entire crew, and more. If you want to get involved but aren't ready to act, volunteering for one of these positions can help you become part of the group.
4. Audition - In community theatres, you don't always need experience. They're also typically looking for new people to act with them, with very little experience. You can start auditioning for smaller or non speaking parts and when you get comfortable, work your way into larger roles.
