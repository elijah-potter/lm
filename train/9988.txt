1. Immortal Jellyfish
Picture source: fiboni.com
A type of “immortal” jellyfish, the Turritopsis Nutricula, is capable of cheating death for ever. When she reaches the adult stage, this jellyfish can reverse the aging process and go back to the polyp stage. Once she is in that stage, it is like she is born again, and starts ageing and mating normally! She can repeat this process over and over again, and practically stay immortal.
