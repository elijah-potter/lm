The argument ended with a sudden crack—glass shattering against the hardwood floor. The woman’s voice, sharp with fury, cut through the air as she turned to him, her fingers digging into the arm of the chair. *"You think you’re the only one who’s ever failed?"*

He stood frozen, the coffee cup still clutched in his hand, his reflection flickering in the broken shards beneath his feet. The fire alarm blared in the distance, a low, rhythmic drone that seemed to echo the tension between them. His ex-partner’s words slithered into his mind, heavy with accusation. *"You’re just another man who’s never had to carry the weight of your own mistakes."*

She wiped the blood from her cut palm with the hem of her sweater, her breath coming fast. The door creaked open, and the sound of sirens grew louder, cutting through the chaos. Outside, the rain pounded against the window, a relentless rhythm that seemed to mirror the storm inside their heads. The fire chief’s voice crackled over the radio, urgent and unyielding. *"We need to secure the perimeter—immediately."*
