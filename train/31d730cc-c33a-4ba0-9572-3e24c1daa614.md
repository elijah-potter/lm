## Product Requirements Document: "Contextualize" - Knowledge Synthesis App

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently spend significant time re-assembling information from disparate sources—documents, emails, web pages—to synthesize insights and make informed decisions, leading to decreased productivity and increased cognitive load.

**Vision:** To empower knowledge workers to effortlessly connect and contextualize information, transforming scattered data into actionable understanding.

### User Personas

**1. Amelia Chen – Senior Marketing Manager**

*   **Goals:** Quickly synthesize data from market research reports, campaign analytics, and competitor analysis to inform strategic decisions and optimize marketing performance. Needs to present findings concisely to executive leadership.
*   **Pain Points:**  Spends excessive time copying and pasting information between documents and spreadsheets. Struggles to identify key themes and connections across diverse sources. Feels overwhelmed by the sheer volume of data and worries about missing critical insights.

**2. David Ramirez – Research Scientist**

*   **Goals:** Efficiently compile and analyze research papers, datasets, and internal reports to identify patterns, formulate hypotheses, and advance scientific discovery. Requires robust citation management.
*   **Pain Points:**  Loses track of where information originated. Finds it tedious to manually organize and cross-reference research findings.  Worries about inaccuracies in synthesized reports due to potential errors in manual compilation.

**3. Eleanor Vance – Financial Analyst**

*   **Goals:** Integrate financial reports, economic forecasts, and company presentations to generate comprehensive investment recommendations and identify potential risks. Needs to quickly assess the credibility of sources.
*   **Pain Points:**  Finds it difficult to reconcile conflicting information from different sources.  Needs a way to quickly assess the reliability and bias of data.  Experiences frustration when needing to reconstruct analyses due to lost or disorganized files.

### Requirements

**1. Source Integration & Import**

*   **Description:** Users must be able to import content from a variety of sources including local files (PDF, DOCX, TXT), web pages (via URL), email attachments, and cloud storage services (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive).
*   **Acceptance Criteria:**
    *   The app successfully imports content from all specified file formats and web pages.
    *   Imported content preserves formatting as closely as possible.
    *   Users can specify a source name and description for each imported item.
*   **Success Metrics:** 95% successful import rate across all supported sources. Average import time under 5 seconds.

**2. Contextual Linking & Annotation**

*   **Description:** Users must be able to create direct links between sections of different imported documents, adding annotations to explain the connection.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:**
    *   Users can highlight text within imported documents and create links to other highlighted sections.
    *   Annotations are clearly visible and editable, including the ability to add dates and tags.
    *   Links are visually represented within the app’s interface.
*   **Success Metrics:** Average of 3 links created per active user per week. Positive user feedback regarding link clarity and usability.

**3. Automated Theme Extraction**

*   **Description:** The app will automatically identify and suggest potential themes and patterns across linked content, leveraging natural language processing.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:**
    *   The app suggests at least three potential themes for each set of linked content.
    *   Suggested themes are relevant to the content and presented in a clear and concise manner.
    *   Users can accept, reject, or edit suggested themes.
*   **Success Metrics:** 60% of suggested themes are accepted by users. Positive user feedback regarding the accuracy and usefulness of theme suggestions.

**4. Export to Structured Report**

*   **Description:** Users must be able to export their linked content and annotations into a structured report format (e.g., PDF, Markdown) suitable for presentation or sharing.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:**
    *   The exported report includes all linked content and annotations.
    *   The report is well-formatted and readable.
    *   Users can customize the report's appearance (e.g., font, color scheme).
*   **Success Metrics:** 80% of users export content at least once per week. Positive user feedback regarding the quality and usability of the exported report.

**5. Secure Cloud Synchronization**

*   **Description:** User data must be securely synchronized across devices via a cloud service.
*   **Acceptance Criteria:**
    *   Data is encrypted both in transit and at rest.
    *   Users can access their data from multiple devices (iOS, Android, Web).
    *   Synchronization occurs automatically and reliably.
*   **Success Metrics:** 99.9% data synchronization uptime. No reported data loss incidents.

### Technical Considerations

*   **Platform:** Native iOS and Android applications, with a responsive web interface.
*   **Backend:** Cloud-based infrastructure (AWS or Google Cloud) for data storage and processing.
*   **Security:**  Implementation of robust authentication and authorization mechanisms. Regular security audits.
*   **NLP Engine:** Integration of a scalable and accurate natural language processing engine for theme extraction.
*   **Scalability:** Architecture designed to handle a large number of users and data volume.

### Risks

*   **NLP Accuracy:**  The accuracy of the automated theme extraction may be limited by the complexity of the content and the sophistication of the NLP engine.
*   **Data Security:**  Maintaining data security and privacy is paramount and requires ongoing vigilance.
*   **User Adoption:**  Users may be resistant to adopting a new tool, requiring a focus on ease of use and demonstrable value.
*   **Platform Dependency:** Reliance on third-party cloud services introduces potential points of failure.



### Non-Goals

*   Real-time collaboration features (e.g., simultaneous editing).
*   Direct integration with proprietary document management systems.
*   Advanced data visualization capabilities.
*   Optical Character Recognition (OCR) functionality.



### Launch Checklist

1.  Complete beta testing with a representative group of target users.
2.  Finalize documentation and user onboarding materials.
3.  Establish a robust customer support channel.
4.  Prepare marketing assets and launch announcement.
5.  Deploy the application to the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
6.  Monitor application performance and user feedback closely following launch.
