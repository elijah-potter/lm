In a whimsical garden filled with flowers and trees lived a bug named Buggy. He was a shy little bug, but his shyness didn’t stop him from making lots of friends. 

When the sun was bright and the morning dew had not yet dried, Buggy noticed a human out of the corner of his eye! Usually, humans didn’t cause a ruckus, but this human was carrying a net! Buggy knew that could only mean one thing—he and his friends could get caught!

“Oh no!” Buggy yelled out, feeling fearful. Despite being shy, he raised his voice loudly and said, “Everyone, we’ve got to run! There’s a net among us.”

Beatrice, Buggy’s bee friend, buzzed out, “I know a place we can all go and hide. Let’s go!” 

As the bugs all scattered, Buggy found himself flying quite slowly. Before he knew it, he could see the fine holes in the net grow closer and closer like an eagle-eyed predator!

Buggy’s worst fears were coming true until he saw a flash of yellow pick him up and carry him away at super speed.

It was Beatrice! She had rescued Buggy and taken him to a soft leaf to keep him safe. 

“Wow, thank you, Beatrice!” Said Buggy.

“Don’t mention it, Buggy,” Beatrice blushed, “that’s what friends are for!”

Finally, Buggy could breathe easily. He was with all his friends, safe and sound.
