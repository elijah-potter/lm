**Local leaders express concern as severe flooding disrupts daily life in Meadowgrove, affecting over 4,200 residents.** Emergency crews continue evacuating low-lying neighborhoods as rivers overflow after weeks of record rainfall. Schools remain closed until further notice, and essential services are operating on limited capacity. Authorities urge residents to stay indoors and avoid flood-prone areas due to reports of trapped individuals and damaged infrastructure. The city’s flood barrier system, partially operational since last week, is being reinforced with additional sandbags. Residents in the downtown area report power outages and disrupted public transportation, though local volunteers are distributing food and water to affected families. The mayor has declared a state of emergency and is coordinating with neighboring towns for mutual aid. Damages are estimated at over $12 million, with insurance claims pending.
