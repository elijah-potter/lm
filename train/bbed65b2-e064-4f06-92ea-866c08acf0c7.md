Once upon a time, in a colorful meadow filled with sunflowers and buzzing bees, lived a curious little bunny named Benny. Benny loved exploring every nook and cranny of the meadow because he was always eager to discover something new.

One bright morning, as Benny hopped along his favorite path, he noticed a shiny object peeking out from behind a bush. With excitement twinkling in his eyes, Benny wiggled through the bushes and found a small, sparkling stone. It shimmered with all the colors of the rainbow! Benny wondered where it could have come from.

Determined to find out more about the magical stone, Benny decided to ask his friends for help. He first visited Daisy the duck who was splashing in the pond. "Hello Daisy," said Benny, holding up the stone. "Do you know anything about this shiny thing?"

Daisy quacked happily and replied, "I don't know much about stones, but maybe Mr. Owl knows! He sees everything from his tree."

So, Benny hopped off to see Mr. Owl who lived in a tall oak tree. With a gentle flap of his wings, Mr. Owl landed gracefully on a branch above Benny. "Good morning, Benny," he said with a twinkle in his wise eyes.

Benny showed him the stone and asked, "Mr. Owl, do you know what this is?"

After looking at it closely, Mr. Owl explained, "Ah, that's a rainbow stone! It appears when there’s rain followed by bright sunshine. It has special powers to bring joy and laughter wherever it goes."

Benny felt delighted with the new knowledge he had gained. He thanked Mr. Owl for his wisdom and decided to share the happiness of the rainbow stone with everyone in the meadow.

That evening, Benny called all his friends over—a group of chirpy birds, playful squirrels, and even a few friendly butterflies. They gathered around as Benny held up the rainbow stone high above his head. As he did, it sparkled brilliantly, spreading shimmering colors across the sky and casting a warm glow on everyone.

Laughter filled the air as all the animals danced and played under the magical light. The meadow felt more alive than ever with joy and friendship shining brightly around them.

And so, Benny learned that sharing happiness was just as wonderful as finding it. From then on, he made sure to spread kindness and laughter wherever he went. And the rainbow stone became a treasured symbol of their joyful adventures in the colorful meadow.
