2nd Grade: Harcourt Storytown Lesson 5 - Neighborhood News
Use these high quality, no prep, student-friendly activities in literacy centers all week while you teach small skill groups!
These activities are aligned with the week’s reading selection and provide extra practice in phonics, vocabulary and comprehension.
The specific activities for Neighborhood News are:
- Reading Skills
- Word Hunt
- Making Words
- Working with Words
- Vocabulary Booklet
- Alpha Boxes & Sentences
More than simply paper/pencil worksheets, these center activities have students looking back in the text to find different types of words, manipulating letters to make new words, using graphic organizers to find details in the reading selection, sequencing the selection, illustrating different parts of what was read, and creating vocabulary booklets!
