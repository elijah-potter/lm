

Hoot! I have many foods that I like to peck at, and here are four of my favorites:

1. Birds - I enjoy exercising, spreading my wings, and chasing after small birds. The thrill of the chase is so exhilarating.
2. Insects - Bugs like moths, worms, and beetles are easy to find and are full of nutrients for me. Some are nice and crunchy, while others are soft and squishy. I like the variety.
3. Fish - When I'm near water, I love to swoop down and capture delicious fish. There is so much meat on a fish that one good sized catch can keep me going all day.
4. Mice - The excitement of the hunt while trying to catch mice gives me a lot of energy and pleasure. Mice are everywhere too, so there's always something nearby to eat.
