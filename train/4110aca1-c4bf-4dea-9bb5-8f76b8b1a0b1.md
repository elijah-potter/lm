Once upon a time in a cozy little house, there lived a bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the garden. One sunny morning, Benny saw a shiny red ball rolling near a big, fluffy cloud. "That looks like fun!" he thought and chased after it.

Just then, Benny’s best friend, a little squirrel named Sammy, ran up. "I found a tiny acorn!" Sammy said, holding it up. "Want to play hide-and-seek with it?"

Benny hopped closer. "Yes, please! But first, let’s share the ball!" They rolled it under a big tree and climbed up to hide behind the leaves. The ball bounced between them, giggling.

When they peeked out, a big, bright sun was shining, and a tiny bird named Pip was singing a happy song. "Time for lunch!" Pip chirped. Benny and Sammy ate some berries from Sammy’s bush.

Afterward, Benny stretched his paws. "I think I’m sleepy," he yawned. Sammy snuggled close. "Me too," he whispered. Benny curled up in his burrow, and Sammy curled up in his treehouse. As they drifted off to sleep, the garden sparkled, and the stars twinkled like tiny fireflies.

The next morning, Benny woke up with a happy "Hoo-hoo!" and found Sammy playing with his new friend Pip. Benny grinned. "Best day ever!"

And that’s how Benny, Sammy, and Pip learned that friendship, play, and sunshine make every day magical.
