Two sisters, Anna, a college freshman, and Rylee, a college junior, find themselves in rival sorority houses. While Anna is sailing through her first semester, Rylee is struggling to keep up her grades. Besides the war raging between their two sorority houses, their parents have just announced they're divorcing. The sisters find themselves torn between both parents and sororities, longing for the peaceful family life they once had. During Rush Week, Anna participates in a prank that gets out of hand and threatens to derail not only her college plans but Rylee's as well. Can these sisters come together despite their differences? Are the bonds of family and sisterhood strong enough to carry Anna and Rylee through the most challenging time in their lives?
