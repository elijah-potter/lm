In a dimly lit café where the hum of conversation faded like the echoes of a forgotten world, Elias sat with a steaming cup of bitter coffee. The newspaper spread across the table was a relic from a time when facts still mattered, when headlines were not just noise but the pulse of society. He traced his finger along the lines of a story about the last known survivors of the old world, those who clung to the remnants of civilization in the ruins of forgotten cities.

A woman entered, her coat damp with rain, her eyes sharp with the kind of curiosity that had once been called curiosity but was now just the weight of a question. She sat across from him, her fingers tapping the table nervously. "Do you believe in miracles, Elias?" she asked, her voice low enough that only he could hear the tremor beneath it. The question hung between them like the smoke curling from his cup, thick and impossible to ignore.
