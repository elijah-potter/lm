The old clock tower stood sentinel over the sleepy town, its pendulum swinging like a weary soldier. Inside, Elias adjusted the last bolt on the mechanism, his fingers numb with cold.

Outside, the first snowflakes began to fall, heavier than usual, as if the sky itself was holding its breath.

"One more time," he muttered, turning the final wrench.

The moment the last bolt clicked, the clock hands froze mid-swing. The second hand pointed not to twelve, but to an empty space, as if time had been torn asunder.

Elias exhaled, then stepped back. The tower groaned, its gears grinding against the unexpected stillness.

A single chime echoed through the snow—twice, then nothing. The town slept, unaware that the very moment of its quiet had been stolen.
