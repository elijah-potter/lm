Here are some ideas that could help you to stand out while marketing your music: 

- Create a music video and edit it into short parts to gradually post as ads on social media. 
- Host a live stream and perform your music for your fans. Ask them to invite their friends to the live stream. You can then repurpose this footage as content for social media.
- Offer your fans the chance to recommend song ideas for you as a challenge. Create the songs and use these interactions as content for social media. 
- Create an intimate rendition of one of your songs as an exclusive release on your social media platform. 
- Be a guest on a podcast where you can talk about your music and maybe perform. If the podcast is recorded on video, you can repurpose it as content for your social media. 
- Host a weekly live stream show where you review and promote music by other new artists.
- Attempt to go viral by recording a catchy dance song and pairing it with cool dance moves.
