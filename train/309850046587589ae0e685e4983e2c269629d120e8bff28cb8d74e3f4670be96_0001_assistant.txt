The nudity clause is a section in an actor or actress's job contract that addresses whether or not any of the performer's body is going to be exposed during a production. These clauses typically set boundaries for the performers that prevent them from being forced to perform in the nude, whether this is requested fully or partially. Sometimes, when this clause is in place, a body double will take the place of the actor, and the nude portions of the body double will be shown as if they belonged to the actor or actress. These contracts indicate what body parts are allowed to be shown by the production team versus those that are restricted from viewing/filming.
