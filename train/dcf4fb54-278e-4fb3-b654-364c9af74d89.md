**Debate Transcript: "Climate Change vs. Economic Growth"**

---

**Speaker 1 (Economist):** *"The IPCC report confirms that unchecked emissions will devastate economies by 2100. We must transition to renewables now, or face irreversible damage."*

**Speaker 2 (Business Leader):** *"Renewables are too expensive! Let’s focus on innovation—solar panels in 5 years, maybe. Right now, jobs in fossil fuels keep families fed."*

**Speaker 1:** *"Jobs in fossil fuels? They’ll be obsolete. We need a just transition—retraining workers, not burning bridges."*

**Speaker 2:** *"Retraining? That’s just more bureaucracy. Why can’t we keep drilling and dredging while we ‘work on it’?"*

**Speaker 3 (Policy Expert):** *"The current model isn’t sustainable. We’re talking about billions in losses—real estate, insurance, supply chains—if we wait. Now is the time for policy, not wishful thinking."*

**Speaker 2:** *"Policy? Last I checked, ‘policy’ just means ‘somebody’s idea.’ Meanwhile, my factory’s paying 30% more for steel because ‘the government taxed it.’"*

**Speaker 1:** *"Taxes? That’s how we fund the infrastructure for the green economy. No more ‘waiting.’"*

**Speaker 2:** *"Infrastructure? Who’s paying for that? The people who already pay taxes? How about we let them keep their money while we ‘explore alternatives’?"*

**Speaker 3:** *"We’re exploring the alternatives *right now*—but the alternatives are being crushed under the weight of inaction."*

**Speaker 2:** *"Crushed? Last I saw, the alternatives were just buzzwords. The real world moves fast enough. Let’s not waste time on ‘climate emergencies.’"*

**Speaker 1:** *"An emergency is when you have a problem. We have a problem. The question is: Are we going to solve it, or are we going to let the problem solve us?"*

**Speaker 2:** *"Problem? I’m not hearing a problem. I’m hearing a *story* about trees and rainbows. Meanwhile, my customers are looking at me and going, ‘Huh.’"*

**Speaker 1:** *"Customers won’t care about ‘your’ story. They’ll care about the future of their homes, their health, their children’s future."*

**Speaker 2:** *"Future? Who’s planning for a future where the ‘green economy’ doesn’t exist? Why not just say, ‘Here’s how we make money’ and leave it at that?"*

**Speaker 3:** *"We can’t ignore the science. The data is clear: delay = catastrophe. The question is: Do we act like adults, or do we act like politicians who promised something and never delivered?"*

**Speaker 2:** *"Politicians? Last time I checked, politicians are the ones who *make* policies. Not the other way around. If you want policies, you’ve got to convince *them*."*

**Speaker 1:** *"Convince them? We’ve already convinced the world. The question is: Are we going to listen?"*

**Speaker 2:** *"Listening? I don’t think so. I think you’re just repeating what you heard at the last ‘climate summit.’"*

**Speaker 3:** *"We’re not repeating anything. We’re *acting* on the information we have."*

**Speaker 2:** *"Acting? That’s code for ‘government.’ I’m not acting. I’m *doing*. And I’m doing it my way."*
