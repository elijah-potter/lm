**The Last Transmission**

The airlock hissed open, sealing the last of the crew inside the *Erebus-9*. Captain Elias Voss adjusted the comms console; the countdown was already running. "Thirty seconds," he murmured, gripping the control panel.

Outside, the ice of Europa cracked under the weight of the landing gear. The team scrambled into their suits, helmets locked. "Nineteen... eighteen... seventeen..." Elias’ voice crackled over the feed—static hissed, then a distorted voice: *"One minute..."*

The probe’s core had failed. The mission was over. They were the last humans on Earth. The countdown reached zero.

A screen flickered: **"Transmission complete."** The final transmission—a holographic image of the solar system, Earth fading into the void—broadcast to the void. The last light of humanity was extinguished.
