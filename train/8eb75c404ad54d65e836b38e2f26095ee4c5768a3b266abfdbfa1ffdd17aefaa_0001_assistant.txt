Frank was a classy professional, who worked full-time at a bank.
There were many other workers, but not even one was like Frank.

Frank was supportive of his clients and took care when their accounts sank,
He’d assure them that he’d protect everyone, even those with the lowest rank.

When a disheveled man fell on the premises, somebody who really stank
Frank was there to help him, big and strong like a tank.

Frank was a certified mechanic, and knew how to work a crank.
But he knew banking was his calling; nobody did it like Frank!
