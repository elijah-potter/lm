To the Members of Chuck-e-Cheese,

First, I would like to thank you for the many hours of entertainment you brought to me as a child. Getting lost in the labyrinthine maze of tunnels and being fished out is a memory I will never forget. The pizza has a one-of-a-kind taste that I haven't been able to replicate anywhere else.

I am currently a student at the local high school looking for a way to become a productive member of society by joining the workforce. Because of my love for your establishment, I was hoping for a job there. I may not yet have a lot of experience with jobs, but my grades are good. I graduate in two years and am looking forward to moving on to a nice college on the coast. Until then, I hope to be of great use to you at the counter, in the kitchen, or out on the floor, taking care of business.

I look forward to working with you!

Regards,

Glen
