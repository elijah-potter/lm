The room was dimly lit, with only a medium-sized table, two chairs, and a camera in the corner. Billy's chair was in the corner and positioned in such a way that the officer who had been sitting in the room with him had basically obstructed any path to the door. Clearly, a psychological tactic to intimidate anyone that was being interrogated. Today it was working very well on Billy. He knew what he would do as soon as the officer walked back into the room. He wasn't sure what to think of himself for what he was about to do. Was he a coward? Was he any less of a man? What would everyone he grew up with in the neighborhood think of him? As the thoughts raced through his head, the officer walked back in through the door. Before the officer could utter one complete sentence, Billy spoke. "I want to talk!" The officer stopped and simply smiled. "You're making the right decision," the officer responded.
