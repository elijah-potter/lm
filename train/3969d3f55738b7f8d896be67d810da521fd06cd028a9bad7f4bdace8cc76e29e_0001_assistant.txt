Here is a list I have brainstormed of gift ideas for a Bar Mitzvah:

1. Cash: Money is usually an acceptable gift at a Bar Mitzvah. It can give the person you are celebrating more freedom to choose what they want to purchase.
2. Menorah: A menorah is a traditional Jewish object used to celebrate Hanukkah. Try engraving the person's name on it to give it a personalized touch.
3. Jewelry: Jewelry can be anything from earrings to necklaces. Getting a piece of jewelry in a shape that represents the Jewish community is common. 
4. Video Games or Technology: Technology is integral to society today. Gift technology or a video game only after asking the parent's permission. 
5. Personalized Photos and Frames: A picture of you and the person whose Bar Mitzvah it is may help them remember a distant memory. The frames could be used to house photographs taken at the celebration.
