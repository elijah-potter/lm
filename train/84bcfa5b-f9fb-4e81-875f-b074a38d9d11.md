## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park – A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary**

The City of Oakhaven faces increasing challenges from extreme weather events, particularly intense rainfall and prolonged heat waves. This proposal seeks $75,000 in municipal funding to transform the currently underutilized and neglected space at the corner of Elm Street and Willow Creek Drive – a 0.5-acre parcel now informally known as “The Patch” – into the Willow Creek Pocket Park. This project will create a demonstrably climate-resilient community asset, incorporating native plantings, stormwater management infrastructure, and shaded seating areas to mitigate urban heat island effect, reduce localized flooding, and provide a welcoming public space for residents. The Willow Creek Pocket Park will serve as a model for future urban greening initiatives and foster a stronger sense of community ownership and resilience. Matching funds and in-kind contributions from local businesses and volunteer efforts total $25,000, demonstrating broad community support.

**Project Plan & Timeline**

The project will be implemented in four distinct phases, each with defined responsibilities and deliverables.

**Phase 1: Assessment & Design (Months 1-2)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Greenleaf Landscape Architects (contracted), Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department
*   **Deliverables:** Site assessment report detailing existing conditions and drainage patterns; Community design workshop summary; Final park design plans incorporating climate-resilient features (native plant selection, permeable paving, rain garden design); Required permits application submission.
*   **Timeline:** 8 weeks.

**Phase 2: Site Preparation & Infrastructure (Months 3-5)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Oakhaven Public Works Department (in-kind labor and equipment), Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (volunteer coordination), Greenleaf Landscape Architects (oversight)
*   **Deliverables:** Existing debris removal; Grading and soil amendment; Installation of permeable paving and underground stormwater storage; Installation of irrigation system utilizing reclaimed water.
*   **Timeline:** 12 weeks.

**Phase 3: Planting & Amenity Installation (Months 6-8)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department (native plant procurement), Willow Creek Elementary School (student volunteer planting days), Greenleaf Landscape Architects (planting guidance)
*   **Deliverables:** Installation of native trees, shrubs, and groundcover; Installation of benches, picnic tables, and accessible pathways; Signage identifying plant species and explaining stormwater management techniques.
*   **Timeline:** 12 weeks.

**Phase 4: Community Launch & Education (Month 9)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Willow Creek Neighborhood Association, Oakhaven Community Outreach Department
*   **Deliverables:** Grand opening celebration; Creation of a park stewardship guide for community volunteers; Development of educational workshops on climate resilience and native plant care.
*   **Timeline:** 4 weeks.



**Budget & Matching Funds**

The total project cost is $100,000.  The requested municipal funding of $75,000 will be supplemented by $25,000 in matching funds and in-kind contributions.

*   **Municipal Funding Request:** $75,000
*   **Greenleaf Landscape Architects (Design & Oversight):** $20,000 (in-kind donation of 50% of standard fees)
*   **Oakhaven Public Works Department (Site Preparation Labor & Equipment):** $15,000 (in-kind donation – estimated value of labor and equipment usage)
*   **Native Plant Procurement:** $10,000 (secured through a partnership with Bloom & Grow Nursery, offering a 20% discount)
*   **Construction Materials (Permeable Paving, Soil Amendments, Irrigation):** $15,000 (secured through donations from local hardware stores and discounted rates from suppliers)
*   **Park Amenities (Benches, Picnic Tables, Signage):** $10,000 (sourced through a combination of donations and discounted rates)
*   **Contingency Fund (5%):** $5,000. This fund will address unforeseen costs related to site conditions, material price fluctuations, or permitting delays.  It will be managed by the Oakhaven Finance Department and requires approval for expenditure exceeding $1,000.



**Community Engagement & Equity Impacts**

Community engagement has been central to the project’s development.  Initial surveys and public forums identified the need for a safe, accessible, and environmentally beneficial public space. The design workshop, held in partnership with the Willow Creek Neighborhood Association, incorporated feedback from residents of all ages and backgrounds.  The project prioritizes equitable access by ensuring the park is fully accessible to individuals with disabilities, located within walking distance of low-income housing, and offers programming that reflects the diverse interests of the community. The involvement of Willow Creek Elementary School fosters environmental literacy among young residents.

**Risk Mitigation**

Potential risks include unforeseen soil contamination, permitting delays, and inclement weather impacting construction timelines.  A Phase 1 Environmental Site Assessment will be conducted prior to construction to identify and remediate any contamination.  The permitting process is being actively managed in collaboration with the Oakhaven Planning Department.  The project timeline incorporates a buffer to account for potential weather-related delays.  The contingency fund will address any unexpected costs arising from these or other unforeseen circumstances.

**Post-Grant Sustainability**

Long-term sustainability is ensured through a comprehensive stewardship program. The Willow Creek Neighborhood Association will assume responsibility for ongoing park maintenance, including weeding, litter removal, and minor repairs.  The Oakhaven Parks & Recreation Department will provide technical support and coordinate larger maintenance needs.  The park stewardship guide will empower community volunteers to actively participate in the park’s upkeep.  Educational workshops will promote responsible park usage and environmental awareness.



**Call to Action**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park represents a unique opportunity to enhance the quality of life for Oakhaven residents, build community resilience, and demonstrate a commitment to environmental stewardship. We urge the municipal funding committee to approve the requested $75,000, enabling the transformation of a neglected space into a thriving community asset.  This investment will yield significant social, environmental, and economic benefits for years to come.
