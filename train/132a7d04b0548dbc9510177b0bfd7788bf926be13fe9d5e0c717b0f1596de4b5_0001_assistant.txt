Vito had always loved going to visit his grandparents in Greece. They owned a massive soap factory in California. Vito had worked at the soap factory since he was 17 years old. When he found out that his grandparents had passed, he was also surprised that they wanted to give ownership of the factory to him. He felt so grateful that this had been granted to him.

Vito knew many of the workers in the factory, as he had worked there for years. He even knew most of them by name, and their family members. He was well liked, and the workers were glad to hear that he would be taking over at the factory. Vito couldn't wait to make his relatives proud, and take on the challenge of expanding the company.
