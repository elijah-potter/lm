Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around his garden, where flowers grew tall and bright as sunshine.

One sunny morning, Benny noticed something strange. The flowers had stopped blooming, and his favorite patch of daisies had turned brown. Benny hopped to his mom and said, "Mom, the flowers are sad!"

Mom Bunny smiled and said, "Don't worry, Benny. Let's fix it together. Maybe the flowers need some water."

Benny ran to his watering can and filled it with the most delicious, sparkly water he had ever seen. He carried it to the garden and began to water the flowers gently. The daisies blinked their eyes and smiled as they drank the water.

As Benny watered, he heard a tiny rustling in the grass. A little mouse named Mia had been hiding there. Mia had been trying to help, but she didn’t have a watering can.

"Mia, come help me," Benny said. Mia hopped over and helped Benny pour water into the garden.

Soon, the flowers began to wake up. The daisies stood up straight, the roses opened their petals wide, and the sunflowers turned their faces to the sky. The garden sparkled, and the flowers danced in the breeze.

Mom Bunny hugged Benny and Mia. "See, Benny? Sometimes, just a little help can make everything better."

From that day on, Benny and Mia watered the garden every morning, and the flowers grew happier than ever. The garden became the happiest place in the whole neighborhood, where everyone could enjoy the colors and the joy of nature.
