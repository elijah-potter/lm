A novel peptide sequence identified in *Streptomyces* sp. strain X-173 shows unexpected bioactivity against *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* under high-salt conditions (2M NaCl). The peptide, designated **X-173p**, disrupts membrane potential at sub-micromolar concentrations, but its mechanism remains unclear. Electron microscopy reveals structural disruptions in mycobacterial outer membrane without corresponding damage to Gram-positive bacteria. The discovery raises concerns about potential cross-resistance with current antibiotics, particularly those targeting efflux pumps. Further experiments are needed to assess in vivo stability, toxicity, and potential resistance development. Follow-up studies should explore synthetic analogs to improve specificity and efficacy.
