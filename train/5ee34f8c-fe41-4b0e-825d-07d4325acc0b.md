The detective examined the empty coffee cup on the desk, the lingering scent of bitter brew clinging to the worn fabric of the chair. "Misunderstood," he muttered, tracing the rim with a finger. Outside, the rain pattered against the window like a thousand whispered secrets. Someone had believed they saw a ghost in the mirror, only to realize too late that the reflection was a child’s toy—left behind by the girl who had vanished that morning. The truth was always the most painful, hidden beneath layers of misheard words and shattered assumptions. The light flickered off the edge of the paper, revealing a name scrawled in ink: *Lena*. The detective’s gaze dropped to the floor, where something cold and heavy waited to be found.
