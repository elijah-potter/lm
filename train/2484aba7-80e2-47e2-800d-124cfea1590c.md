The dim glow of a flickering overhead bulb cast long shadows across the cracked vinyl floor. A half-empty mug of dark coffee sat on the wooden desk, its contents spilling onto the paper cluttering the surface. The scent of stale air and damp wood lingered in the air, mixed with the faint metallic tang of spilled blood on the floor near the open door, its hinges groaning softly.

A torn piece of fabric—part of a jacket, perhaps—was tangled around a chair leg, the fabric stained with something dark and viscous. The chair itself was tipped over, its broken back leaning against the wall, its seat stained with what looked like blood and something else, almost like rust or old paint. The only sound was the distant hum of the refrigerator, its door slightly ajar, its contents cold and untouched. The room smelled of fear and something else—like burnt sugar, as if something had been scorched in an accident. The walls were bare, save for a single framed photograph, its glass cracked, the photo itself slightly warped, the faces inside blurred. The frame was propped against the wall, leaning awkwardly.
