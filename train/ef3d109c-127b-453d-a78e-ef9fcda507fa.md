## Bookstore Serendipity (Seattle, WA)

The rain was relentless that afternoon, a familiar Seattle embrace. I was browsing the poetry section at Elliott Bay Book Company, drawn in by the scent of aged paper and the quiet murmur of turning pages. You were across from me, examining a collection of Rilke, your brow furrowed in that intensely thoughtful way that always captivates me. I noticed the vibrant turquoise of your scarf, a striking contrast against the muted tones of the shop. We didn’t speak, not a single word exchanged, but there was a shared stillness, a sense of understanding that lingered in the air. I felt a brief, inexplicable connection, a fleeting moment of recognition that vanished as quickly as it appeared when you turned and left. I’m hoping, perhaps foolishly, that you might remember that quiet corner and the feeling of shared space. 

If you were reading Rilke and wearing a turquoise scarf on Tuesday afternoon, please reply with your favorite poem.
