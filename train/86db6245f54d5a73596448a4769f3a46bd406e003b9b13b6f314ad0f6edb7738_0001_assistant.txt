"A forlorn monkey strolled along the riverbank, his eyes downcast and his spirits low. Spotting the despondent monkey, a turtle approached him.

"How are you?"" the turtle inquired, sensing the monkey's sadness.

With a heavy sigh, the monkey replied, ""Oh, my friend, I am famished. The other monkeys have taken all of Mr. Farmer's squash, and now I am on the brink of starvation.""

""Don't lose hope,"" the turtle reassured. ""Grab a bolo and follow me. Together, we shall pilfer some banana plants.""

Side by side, they ventured forth until they discovered some flourishing plants. With care, they uprooted them and sought a suitable spot for transplantation. The monkey chose to ascend a tree, placing his plant in its embrace. However, since the turtle lacked climbing prowess, he resorted to excavating a hole in the ground, carefully nestling his plant within.

Their work completed, they departed, contemplating the potential harvest. The monkey boasted, ""When my tree bears fruit, I shall sell it and amass great wealth.""

The turtle chimed in, ""And when my tree bears fruit, I shall sell it and purchase three varas of cloth to replace this cracked shell.""

Weeks elapsed, and they returned to assess their plants' progress. Alas, the monkey's tree had withered, for its roots had lacked soil in the tree, while the turtle's plant stood tall and abundant with fruit.

""I shall climb to the tree's pinnacle so that we can retrieve the fruit,"" declared the monkey. And without further ado, he ascended the tree, leaving the turtle alone on the ground.

""Please share some fruit with me,"" pleaded the turtle. Yet, the monkey flung him only a green banana, devouring all the ripe ones himself.

Once satiated, the monkey encircled his arms around the tree and succumbed to slumber. Observing this, the turtle simmered with anger, plotting a fitting retribution. He gathered sharp bamboo sticks, positioning them meticulously beneath the tree, then cried out:

""Behold! A crocodile approaches! A crocodile approaches!""

Startled by the cry, the monkey lost his balance and fell upon the jagged bamboo, meeting an untimely demise.

Seizing the opportunity, the turtle dismembered the fallen monkey, seasoned the meat with salt, and left it to dry under the sun. The following day, he ventured to the mountains, trading the preserved meat with other monkeys who gladly surrendered their squash in return. As the turtle departed, he taunted them with a parting remark:

""Lethargic creatures, you now consume your own flesh; you now consume your own flesh.""

Enraged, the monkeys seized the turtle and carried him to their abode.

""Let us retrieve a hatchet,"" proposed an elder monkey, ""and dismember him into minuscule fragments.""

Amidst the commotion, the turtle chuckled and said, ""That suits me perfectly, for I have been struck by a hatchet numerous times. Do you not observe the black scars etched upon my shell?""

Unwilling to entertain the turtle's bravado, another monkey interjected, ""Let us cast him into the water.""

The turtle, overcome with fear, pleaded for mercy, but his pleas fell on deaf ears as the monkeys callously cast him into the water. He sank to the bottom momentarily, only to resurface in the company of a lobster. The monkeys, astounded, implored him to divulge the secret of catching lobsters.

""I tied one end of a string around my waist,"" the turtle shared. ""To the other end, I affixed a stone to ensure my descent"
