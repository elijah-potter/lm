Cassandra bought four dozen Granny Smith apples and used them to make four apple pies.  She cut each pie into 6 large pieces.  How many apples are in each slice of pie?
Four dozen apples contain 4*12=<<4*12=48>>48 apples.
Four pies, each cut into 6 pieces, contain 4*6=<<4*6=24>>24 pieces.
Thus, there are 48/24=<<48/24=2>>2 apples in every slice of Cassandra's apple pie.
