In the Realm of Derian, there are many peoples and cultures. The Elves are ancient and scattered the mountain ranges and wild areas of the world, and while they sometimes come down they mostly remain there. Each contiguous stretch of Elven land relies on a local council for leadership. 

There are also many different nations of humans and dwarves. This includes traditional kingdoms divided between the two peoples, as well as a collection of human and dwarven trading cities nestled around the Orange Sea, collectively led by and known as the Orange League. While conflict can erupt from time to time, all of these people are largely able to live in peace. 

Beyond the charted lands, all manner of incredible creatures and unknown people lurk. There are legends of another intelligent and upright tribe, as well as dragons and wyrms. The stories say that these creatures would make incursions into the known world thousands of years ago, but that they’ve long since vanished.
