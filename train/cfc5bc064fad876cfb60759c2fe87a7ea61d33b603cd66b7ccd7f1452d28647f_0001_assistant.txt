Mona Lisa, also known as the painting of Lisa Gherardini, is an oil painting portrait painted by Italian polymath Leonardo da Vinci sometime between 1503 and 1519. Located in the Louvre Museum, Paris, the sitter's mysterious smile and unproven identity have attracted attention and fascinated people from all walks of life. Born in 1452, Leonardo is highly regarded as a genius who epitomized the Renaissance humanist ideal, with his work making many contributions to artists of later generations.
