Oh, what to wear for the Halloween scare?
Shall I be a mouse, 
Creeping up from house to house?
Or a vampire in the night, 
Give me some candy, or I will bite!
Perhaps I'll be a ghost, 
Which children love the most.
Or try being a cat?
Could I imagine that?
A witch is classic and also fun,
A promising choice for everyone. 
But I think I just might.....
Be a creature of the night. 
A scary, toothy, large-winged bat,
Nothing can compete with that!
