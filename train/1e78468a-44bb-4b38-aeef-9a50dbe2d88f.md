The abandoned **Blackthorn Warehouse** in the old industrial district of **Port Haven**—a once-thriving shipping hub now overgrown with ivy and rusted cranes. Opened for **just 3 days in September 2023** by a private art collective, it hosted a fleeting exhibition of decaying maritime memorabilia under the flickering glow of salvaged streetlights.

Access restricted to invited guests with **exclusive digital passes**. Last patrons reported eerie whispers from the hollowed-out storage bays. The doors closed at sunset, sealing the site for good—until some urban legend claims a "ghost ship" sails back into port.
