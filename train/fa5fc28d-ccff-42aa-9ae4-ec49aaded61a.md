**Routine 1: Fluid Motion**
- Warm-up: Dynamic stretches (arm circles, leg swings, hip openers).
- Technique drills: Focused on smooth, controlled transitions (e.g., squats to lunges).
- Repetition: 3 sets of 12 reps with 20s pause between sets.
- Intensity: Progressive overload—gradually increasing resistance (e.g., dumbbells, resistance bands).
- Mental focus: Visualizing muscle engagement and perfect form.
- Cool-down: Static stretches (hamstring, quadriceps, shoulder).

**Routine 2: Explosive Power**
- Warm-up: Jump rope, high knees, butt kicks.
- Drills: Plyometrics (box jumps, depth jumps) with controlled landings.
- Weighted movements: Deadlifts (3x5), bench press (3x8), pull-ups (AMRAP).
- Speed/agility: Sprints (10x50m) or ladder drills.
- Recovery: Active recovery—light jogging or mobility drills.

**Routine 3: Endurance & Stamina**
- Cardio: Tabata intervals (20s work/10s rest) or HIIT (30s on/30s off).
- Strength: Circuit training (e.g., burpees, kettlebell swings, push-ups).
- Recovery: 10-min cooldown walk + dynamic stretching.

**Key Performance Factors:**
- **Consistency:** 5–7x/week with progressive adaptation.
- **Recovery:** 1–2 rest days/week (sleep, hydration, nutrition).
- **Mindset:** Discipline in execution, adaptability to fatigue.
- **Metrics:** Track reps, time, form compliance—objective feedback.

**Sample Workout (Hybrid):**
- Warm-up: 5 min dynamic stretching.
- **Circuit (3 rounds):**
  1. Kettlebell swings (15 reps)
  2. Battle ropes (30s)
  3. Box jumps (10 reps)
  4. Pull-ups (AMRAP)
- **Finisher:** 50m sprint + 50m walk (x5).
- Cool-down: 5 min stretching + foam rolling.
