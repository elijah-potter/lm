The long-lasting negative effects of the nuclear bombs dropped on Hiroshima and Nagasaki are numerous and far-reaching. They include:

- Health effects: The most immediate and deadly effect of the bombings was death from acute radiation syndrome. This is a condition caused by exposure to high levels of radiation, and it can lead to a variety of symptoms, including nausea, vomiting, hair loss, and death. In the years following the bombings, many survivors developed cancer, including leukemia, thyroid cancer, and breast cancer.
- Psychological effects: The bombings also had a profound psychological impact on survivors. Many developed post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), and they continue to suffer from anxiety, depression, and other mental health problems.
- Environmental effects: The bombings also had a significant impact on the environment. The radiation released by the bombs contaminated the soil and water, and it is still present in the area today. This has led to increased rates of cancer and other health problems in the region.
- Social effects: The bombings also had a profound impact on the social fabric of Hiroshima and Nagasaki. Many families were torn apart, and the cityscapes were completely destroyed. This led to a sense of loss, grief, and despair that continues to this day.
