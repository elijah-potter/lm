A solemn vow carved into the wind,
its weight as heavy as the dawn’s first light.
Each whispered word a blade unsheathed,
each promise a chain of unseen weight.

The path was steep, the sky a shifting veil,
where shadows stretched like fingers of doubt.
The heart, though brave, faltered at the cost—
a life unraveled in the hollowest cost.

Yet still, the vow stood firm, unbroken,
as brittle as the bones of a dream.
To break it would be to drown in the storm,
but to keep it? That was a fate worse than the dream.

So they walked the thin line between,
where honor and ruin warred in silence,
while the world held its breath—
and time, ever patient, stretched the thread thin.
