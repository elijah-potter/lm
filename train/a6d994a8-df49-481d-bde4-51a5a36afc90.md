A maintenance worker noticed the subtle creak in the old hinge of the door at the back of the warehouse during a routine inspection. Upon closer inspection, they realized the loose bolt was holding a corroded pipe bracket that had been misaligned for months, contributing to a slow but steady water leak. The leak wasn’t immediately obvious but caused a steady drizzle that, over time, caused mold growth in the storage shelves. The worker flagged it as a critical repair needed within a week to prevent structural damage and potential fire hazards.
