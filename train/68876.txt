What is this?
There's the app to train your math skills for Texas Holdem Poker.
How does it work?
The app generates random possible game situations and estimates of winning. It uses Monte Carlo Method for estimation. The app checks 10 000 outcomes on each
step. You are then asked to guess the calculated result.
What's the point?
Non-professional gamers often overestimate weak hands in mass games. Or vice versa: they underestimate the power of hand in "heads up" mode.
This application is necessary to improve your prediction skills in Texas Holdem and increase your win rate.
But I calculate odds perfectly!
Well, I don't think so. For example: how would you estimate your odds with Q7 on preflop against 8 opponents? Actually, this hand wins only 7% of games. But if you
"heads up" against one opponent your chance to win increases up to 54%
