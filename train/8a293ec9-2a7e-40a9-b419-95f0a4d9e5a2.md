A sudden, deep **thunderclap** followed by a prolonged, resonant **boom**—like a distant cannon shot or a distant train whistle fading into static—is often used in storytelling to convey urgency, warning, or a shift in power. In film, it might signal an impending attack or the arrival of a key character. In folklore, it could mark the call of a spirit or the breaking of a curse. The key is the **suddenness** and **sonic weight**, emphasizing that the sound is not natural but deliberate and meaningful.
