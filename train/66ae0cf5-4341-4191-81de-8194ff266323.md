## Product Requirements Document: “Contextualize” – Mobile Knowledge Aggregation & Synthesis

**Problem Statement:** Knowledge workers frequently grapple with fragmented information scattered across multiple platforms—notes, documents, emails, web pages—making it difficult to synthesize insights and maintain a cohesive understanding of complex projects.

**Vision:** To empower knowledge workers with a mobile application that intelligently aggregates relevant information and facilitates intuitive synthesis, fostering deeper comprehension and accelerating decision-making.

---

### User Personas

**1. Eleanor Vance - Senior Marketing Manager**

*   **Goals:** To quickly grasp the status of ongoing campaigns, understand customer feedback, and identify emerging trends to inform strategic decisions. Eleanor needs to be able to access and process information efficiently while traveling.
*   **Pain Points:** Eleanor spends excessive time searching for relevant documents and emails, struggles to connect disparate data points, and often misses critical details due to information overload. She feels disconnected from the day-to-day execution of her team's work.

**2. Daniel Choi - Research Scientist**

*   **Goals:** To maintain a comprehensive record of research findings, connect related publications and data sets, and readily access supporting evidence for presentations and reports. Daniel needs a system that supports non-linear thought processes and accommodates diverse file types.
*   **Pain Points:** Daniel’s research materials are disorganized and difficult to retrieve, making it challenging to build upon previous work and avoid redundant effort. He finds it tedious to manually link related findings.

**3. Anya Sharma - Freelance Consultant**

*   **Goals:** To efficiently manage client projects, track key deliverables, and maintain a centralized repository of research and insights. Anya requires a solution that is accessible across devices and facilitates collaboration.
*   **Pain Points:** Anya's client information is spread across various platforms, leading to missed deadlines and duplicated work. She lacks a unified system for organizing her project-related knowledge.

---

### Requirements

**1. Intelligent Content Aggregation:** The application shall automatically aggregate content from specified sources, including email inboxes (Gmail, Outlook), cloud storage services (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive), note-taking applications (Evernote, OneNote), and designated websites.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** User can connect to at least three of the listed services. Aggregated content displays with clear source attribution. Content aggregation is refreshed automatically at configurable intervals.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 80% of active users connect at least one external service within the first week. Average content aggregation frequency is at least once per day.

**2. Contextual Tagging & Linking:** The application shall allow users to assign tags to aggregated content and create explicit links between related items.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Users can create, edit, and apply custom tags. Users can create bi-directional links between content items. Linked items are visually connected within the application.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 50% of aggregated content items are tagged within two weeks of aggregation. Average number of links per content item is greater than one.

**3. Summary & Synthesis Generation:** The application shall generate automated summaries of aggregated content and suggest potential connections between linked items.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Summaries accurately reflect the core content of linked items. Suggested connections are relevant and insightful. Users can edit and refine generated summaries and connections.
    *   **Success Metrics:** User ratings for summary accuracy are above 4.0 (on a 5-point scale). 20% of users actively modify generated summaries or connections within the first month.

**4. Offline Accessibility:** The application shall allow users to access and interact with aggregated content, tags, and links even without an active internet connection.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Last-synced content is accessible offline. Users can create and edit tags and links offline; changes are synchronized upon reconnection.
    *   **Success Metrics:** 70% of users regularly access the application offline. Synchronization occurs reliably upon reconnection.

**5. Mobile-First Interface:** The application shall provide a clean, intuitive user interface optimized for mobile devices, prioritizing ease of navigation and content consumption.
    *   **Acceptance Criteria:** Navigation is intuitive and requires minimal taps to access key features. Content displays clearly and is easily readable on various screen sizes.
    *   **Success Metrics:** Average session duration is greater than five minutes. User feedback indicates a positive perception of the application's usability.



---

### Technical Considerations

*   **Security:** Robust security measures are paramount to protect user data and ensure privacy. Encryption and secure authentication protocols are required.
*   **Scalability:** The application architecture must be scalable to accommodate a growing user base and increasing volumes of aggregated content.
*   **API Integrations:** Reliable and well-documented APIs are necessary for seamless integration with external services.
*   **Platform Compatibility:** Initial release will target iOS and Android platforms.
*   **Data Storage:** A cloud-based data storage solution will be utilized for content aggregation and user data.



### Risks

*   **API Changes:** Dependence on third-party APIs introduces the risk of unexpected changes that could disrupt functionality.
*   **Data Privacy Concerns:** Handling sensitive user data requires careful attention to privacy regulations and user trust.
*   **Content Overload:** The application must effectively manage and filter content to avoid overwhelming users.



### Non-Goals

*   Real-time collaboration features (e.g., shared workspaces) are not included in the initial release.
*   Direct content creation within the application is not supported.
*   Integration with social media platforms is not planned.
*   Advanced search capabilities beyond keyword search are not prioritized.
