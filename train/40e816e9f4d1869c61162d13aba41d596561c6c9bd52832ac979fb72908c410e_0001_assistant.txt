No problem. Here are some ideas for illustrations and map features you may wish to add:

1. A legend: A guide to what the various symbols on your map indicate, such as villages, dungeons, and caves would be useful to the reader of the map. 
2. A trace of your party's travels: As a record of your DnD campaign, it might be memorable and interesting to note the path your party took along the adventure.
3. Illustrations of monsters: Much like the maps of antiquity, you could include detailed illustrations of the various monsters and beasts of the world that your party encountered or slew along the way. These would occupy places in the margins or beyond the borders of the known world of your setting.
4. Major characters: Alternatively, you could provide illustrations of important characters your party may have encountered through their quest.
5. A compass rose: This would establish the relative direction of various places and events in your game to each other. 
6. A scale: It may be useful to know the approximate distance between locations on the map via a scale. You could place it on the corner of the map.
