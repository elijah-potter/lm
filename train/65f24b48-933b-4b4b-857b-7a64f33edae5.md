**The Great Wall of China** (modern, partially restored sections)
- **Northern Boundary**: Northern Hebei Province to Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region (~4600 km)
- **Eastern Boundary**: Liaoning Province to Jilin Province (~1200 km)
- **Western Boundary**: Gansu Province to Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region (~3000 km)
- **Southern Boundary**: Shandong Province (~100 km, partial coastal defenses)
*Materials*: Stone, brick, tile, and earthen ramparts with watchtowers, beacon towers, and fortification walls.
*Function*: Originally a defensive barrier against Mongol invasions, later adapted for trade and protection.

**Tower of London’s Outer Perimeter**
- **Circumference**: ~1.3 km (1099m diameter)
- **Core Ring**: Inner curtain wall (305m diameter, 13th–16th century)
- **Key Features**:
  - 115+ watchtowers, 7 main gateways (e.g., Tower Gate, Lambeth Gate)
  - 17th-century defensive walls (Hampton Court Road to Southwark Bridge)
  - Fortified moats (outer: 15–20m wide, inner: 12m)
- *Purpose*: Royal residence, prison, armory, and ceremonial site.

**Silicon Valley’s Corporate Fence**
- **Northern Boundary**: San Francisco Bay (U.S. Highway 101)
- **Southern Boundary**: San Jose, CA (Santa Clara Valley)
- **Eastern Boundary**: Santa Clara Valley (firmament of tech companies)
- **Western Boundary**: Pacific Ocean (coastal tech hubs like Redwood City)
*Key Landmarks*: Stanford University, Palo Alto, Mountain View, Cupertino.
*Economic Zones*: ~200+ tech firms (Google, Apple, Meta) within a 30-mile radius.

**The Walled City of Macau**
- **Total Area**: 1.23 km²
- **Walled Perimeter**: 5.8 km (18th-century Portuguese colonial defense)
- **Core Features**:
  - 72 defensive towers, 4 main gates (Tat Chee, Coloane, Taipa, Lapa)
  - Central Plaza (Taipa Village) encircled by walls
  - Mixed-use: casinos, temples, residential districts
- *Materials*: Stone, brick, and timber fortifications.

**The Great Barrier Reef’s Oceanic Frame**
- **Northern Extent**: Fitzroy Island (Queensland, Australia)
- **Southern Extent**: Whitsunday Islands (near Magnetic Island)
- **Eastern Boundary**: Coral Sea (oceanic edge)
- **Western Boundary**: Great Dividing Range (continental shelf)
- **Depth Range**: 1–100m (average: 15m)
- *Key Structures*: 2,300+ individual reefs, 900 islands, 462 coral types.
