Believe me, a man who assassinates a US President is not someone who had a pleasant life or was well in the head. Lee Harvey Oswald had issues with spelling and reading as a child, though grew to become an avid reader. He declared himself a socialist after reading a good deal of socialist literature, even writing to America's Socialist Party to ask about joining their programs. However, one of his friends at the time disputes that his reading habits were especially sophisticated. I could never judge a man for how he gets into reading, though I can and do judge him for how he would repeat the history of what happened to me. Regardless of how true his claims to read socialist theory were, he was so vocally pro-Soviet that when he joined the US military, he gained the nickname "Oswaldskovich."

Over three score ago, Oswald taught himself Russian and traveled to the USSR. He insisted to Soviet officials that he loved the Soviet Union and wanted to join. They initially rejected his citizenship application, so he forced them to keep him in the country with a self-inflicted wound. He offered his knowledge of the American military to the Soviets and became a laborer in Belarus. He lived somewhat comfortably for a Soviet worker, though was constantly watched. After only two years, Oswald decided he would rather return to America. I can certainly remember a handful of Confederates who had second thoughts back in my time. Oswald thought that his work was dull and the recreational activities were lacking. Oswald had made a show of his defection but had never actually renounced his citizenship, so he asked the US embassy to return his passport and help his new wife and child immigrate back home. When they returned, they made far less of an impact in the headlines than Oswald had thought they would get.
