A dimly lit room, the air thick with tension. The single flickering bulb casts long shadows on the walls. The door creaks softly as the wind outside howls. A woman sits at the edge of a wooden table, her fingers tracing the rim of a half-empty coffee cup. A phone lies on the table, its screen dark, the last notification from her husband’s name still glowing faintly in red. The device buzzes once—low volume, a single ring.

She exhales sharply, fingers tightening around the phone. The notification flashes again: **"PLEASE WAIT."** The words appear in bold, the phone’s screen now illuminated against the gloom. A notification icon pulses faintly in the corner. She leans forward, pressing the screen.

**"Family Meeting in 30 Minutes."** The text appears, unreadable at first, then clearer. She swallows hard. The words **"Safety Check"** follow, but her mind races—where is he? Why now?

The phone’s battery is low. A single notification lingers: **"Last Update: 10:17 AM."** She checks the time—she must have missed it. The room feels too warm, too quiet. Outside, the wind picks up, rattling the windows. She pulls a blanket tighter around her shoulders, the fabric damp with condensation.

Another notification: **"Please check in."** The phone vibrates again. She hesitates, then scrolls.

**"All clear. Home safe. Meeting at 11 AM."** The text is brief, almost dismissive. She reads it twice. Then, slowly, she stands, her breath shallow. The door creaks open. Outside, the night is silent.
