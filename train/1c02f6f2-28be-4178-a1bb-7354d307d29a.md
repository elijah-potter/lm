The abandoned **Blackwood Asylum**, a decaying institution in the northeastern hills of Blackthorn County. Its crumbling stone walls, flickering gas lamps, and overgrown gardens create an atmosphere of forgotten suffering. Patients who once sought refuge now haunt its halls, their whispers echoing through the empty corridors. The most feared section: the **Mirror Chamber**, where visitors claim to see their own reflections staring back—but their eyes say nothing. Many who enter never leave, their faces frozen in silence. Locals whisper it’s a trap. Those who linger too long report waking to find their reflection *moving independently*, as if watching them without eyes. Some say the asylum’s original patients still watch from within, unseen. Others claim it’s not a place of the living.
