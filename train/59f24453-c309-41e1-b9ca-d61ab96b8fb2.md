The absence lingers like a half-remembered song—notes left unresolved, chords unresolved.
Silence isn’t empty; it’s saturated with the weight of what was never said.
Every shadow in the room feels like a ghost of someone who never walked away.
The air tastes like dust and unspoken apologies.
You sit with the echo of their footsteps, knowing you’ll never hear their voice again.
The door doesn’t close. It just opens wider.
