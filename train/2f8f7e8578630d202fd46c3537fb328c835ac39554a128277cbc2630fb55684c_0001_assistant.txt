For years, Tim had been afraid of what was under his bed. Who knew what breed of monster with long, snaking fingers might reach out to grab him at night? One day, when Tim was feeling very brave, he went to rescue his favorite teddy bear that had been kicked under the bed by accident. That's when he discovered the Minutians, tiny people that lived under his bed and ate the crumbs he dropped. To them, they were giant pieces of food. 

They had built a whole town underneath, constructed from pieces of cardboard and a few old toys. When Tim saw his favorite teddy in the town square, his heart sank, but he eventually decided to let them keep it.
