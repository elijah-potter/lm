Standing at the kitchen counter, the steam from the morning coffee lingered like a ghost. The clock ticked relentlessly, but the silence stretched—each second felt stretched into minutes. The spoon in the mug trembled as I sipped, each sip a deliberate pause in the relentless march of time. Outside, the city pulsed with life, but here, in this quiet moment, time seemed to slow, the moments blurred into an endless loop of anticipation. The coffee grew cold before I even realized it. The world outside blurred into a haze of indistinct colors—reds, blues, grays—until the coffee's warmth seeped into my bones, and the moment snapped back into focus: the cup, the steam, the ticking clock. A single, fleeting eternity.
