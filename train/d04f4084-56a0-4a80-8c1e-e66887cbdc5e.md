A small disagreement between colleagues over project deadlines escalated into a full-blown conflict after one team member repeatedly missed follow-up meetings and failed to provide necessary updates. When the lead noticed the lack of progress, they initiated a mandatory team sync, expecting constructive input. Instead, the absent member’s supervisor accused them of "wasting time," leading to public blame. The absent member, feeling unfairly targeted, withdrew from discussions entirely, citing "overwhelm," while the supervisor dismissed their concerns as "excitement." The project timeline shifted, causing delays in client deliverables, and morale dropped. The lead had to intervene with a neutral debrief, but tensions remained unresolved, hinting at deeper trust issues.
