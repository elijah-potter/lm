Here is a list of the first 3 books that Stephen King wrote and when they were released: 
1. Carrie: This is the first book that King released, and it came out in 1974. It is a horror novel about a girl who is bullied at school, lives in a toxic, religious household, and then comes to find out that she has telekinetic powers. 
2. 'Salem's Lot: This book was released in 1975. The story follows Ben Mears, a writer who moves to 'Salem's Lot and realizes that the townspeople are turning into vampires. 
3. The Shining: This book was released in 1977. It follows Jack, Wendy, and Danny Torrance during their isolated stay in the Overlook Hotel during the winter. They discover sinister things about the hotel during their stay.
