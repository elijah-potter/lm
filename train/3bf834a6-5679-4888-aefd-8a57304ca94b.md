**The Last Light**

The wind carried the scent of damp earth and old books through the cracked window of the abandoned library. Clara sat at the desk, fingers tracing the edges of an open notebook, its pages filled with scribbled fragments—names, dates, the ghostly echoes of voices long silenced. The fire had burned low, casting long shadows across the walls where the last reader had once perched.

She leaned back, exhaling through her nose as the draft tugged at her collar. The clock on the mantel read past midnight, but the city outside was still. Somewhere, a car horn blared in the distance—too loud, too familiar. Then, the sound of footsteps.

Clara didn’t look up. Her breath fogged the air, but she didn’t stir.

The door creaked open.
