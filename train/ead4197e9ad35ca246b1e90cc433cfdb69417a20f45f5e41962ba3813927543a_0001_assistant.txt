1. Hoh River Trail Hike: In Olympic National Park, Washington, hike a 6-mile round trip through a rainforest, subalpine meadows, and montane forests. This hike has little elevation.
2. Canyon Overlook Trail Hike: In Zion National Park, Utah, hike a 1-mile round trip to see Zion Canyon, walk above the Mt. Carmel tunnel along Pine Creek Canyon, and gaze over cliffs.
3. Fairy Falls Trail Hike: In Yellowstone National Park, Wyoming, hike a 5-mile round trip to see a waterfall, geysers, and a hot spring. This hike has little climbing.
