U.S. Department of Energy - Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energy
Clean Cities Publishes New Electric Vehicles Fact Sheet
June 2, 2011
Clean Cities recently published Hybrid and Plug-In Electric Vehicles, a four-page publication that outlines the basics and benefits of hybrid electric vehicles, plug-in hybrids, and all-electric vehicles. It also contains information about emissions, safety, fuel costs, and the various types of charging equipment. To find this fact sheet and other Clean Cities documents, visit the Publications page of the Clean Cities website.
For more information about hybrid and plug-in electric vehicles, go to the Alternative Fuels and Advanced Vehicles Data Center.
