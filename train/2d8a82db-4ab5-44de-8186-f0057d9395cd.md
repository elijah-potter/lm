Liam had a little blue toy car. It was so shiny and sparkly! One day, while playing, Liam noticed the car was missing. He searched everywhere: under the bed, behind the couch, and even in the bathtub. Liam called his best friend, Mila, and said, "Mila, where could my car be?"

Mila had an idea. She said, "Maybe it rolled under the table!" Together, they checked beneath the table, and there it was—Liam’s blue car was just sitting there, all alone. Liam giggled and hugged the car. "I’m so happy you’re back!" he said.

From that day on, Liam and Mila always kept an eye on their toys. "If we look for things together," Liam said, "we’ll never lose them again!" And they promised to play even more carefully, just in case.
