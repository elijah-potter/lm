An unspoken village near a mist-shrouded forest where paths crisscross through ancient stone circles. The elders whisper about the "third rule"—not spoken aloud—when the elders themselves are gone: *the dead walk the edges of the silence*. The elders guide by touch, not sign, teaching through shared memories in the hollow of the old oak. The river runs silver but reflects only the faces of those who follow the unspoken: bend the knee when the wind blows from the east, and the water will sing in your tongue. The market stalls sell only what the earth demands, and prices are given in stories, not coins. The sky is a canvas painted by the unseen, and the stars are the only ones who know their names.
