Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a garden full of flowers, lived a curious bunny named Benny. Benny had a big, round ear that flopped when he listened to the wind, and a tail that twitched like a tiny antenna.

One sunny morning, Benny noticed something sparkly near his burrow. It was a tiny, shiny acorn with a twisty top. He picked it up and whispered, *"What am I?"* Suddenly, a tiny voice answered from inside: *"I’m a magic acorn!"*

Benny’s ears perked up. "Magic?" he said, excitedly. "Let’s see what happens!"

Benny put the acorn in his pocket and raced home. Inside, he found a little pot of golden, bubbly water. He splashed it on his paws—*POOF!*—his pawprints turned into tiny, fluffy clouds! Benny giggled and chased them around the garden.

Later, he found a patch of dirt that had turned into a soft, squishy blanket. He jumped on it—*PLOP!*—and landed on a cloud of cotton candy! He ate a little, then giggled again.

Benny’s friend, a sleepy old turtle named Tilly, peeked out from her shell. "That’s the most fun I’ve ever seen!" she said. "But can we make it stop?"

Benny’s magic acorn started to glow brighter. "Maybe we should save it for a special day," he thought. He put the acorn back in his burrow and hugged his friends. "Tomorrow," he said, "we’ll play with magic again!"

And from that day on, Benny and Tilly kept their magic a secret. They saved it for big adventures—like planting a magical garden where flowers grew taller than trees, or making their burrow float on a cloud. But they never shared it with anyone who wasn’t kind and excited, because magic is best when it’s shared with love.

The end.
