The dim kitchen light flickered over empty shelves where a half-empty bottle of olive oil once stood. The glass jar sat cracked, its contents spilled across the counter. A crumpled receipt from last week’s grocery run lay beside it, its corner torn off. The scent of garlic and herbs from the evening’s dinner meal lingered faintly, but the dish that would have filled the pan now just sat untouched, its ingredients—missing, like the oil—left behind. The refrigerator hummed, its contents neat and untouched, a single forgotten sandwich wrapper clinging to the door like a ghost.
