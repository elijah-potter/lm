Day 1: Bangkok
Morning:
Visit the Grand Palace: Explore the stunning architecture and historical significance of this iconic landmark.
Afternoon:
Explore Bangkok's Floating Markets: Experience the vibrant atmosphere and unique shopping opportunities at floating markets like Damnoen Saduak or Amphawa.
Evening:
Enjoy a traditional Thai dinner: Head to a local restaurant to savor delicious Thai cuisine.

Day 2: Chiang Mai
Morning:
Fly from Bangkok to Chiang Mai: Take a domestic flight to Chiang Mai, located in northern Thailand.
Afternoon:
Visit Wat Phra That Doi Suthep: Climb Doi Suthep Mountain to reach this stunning temple and enjoy panoramic views of Chiang Mai.
Evening:
Explore Chiang Mai Night Markets: Wander through the bustling night markets, such as the famous Sunday Walking Street, and indulge in shopping for local crafts and enjoying street food.

Day 3: Sukhothai and Ayutthaya
Morning:
Travel from Chiang Mai to Sukhothai: Take a morning bus or taxi to Sukhothai Old City.
Explore Sukhothai Old City: Discover the ancient ruins, temples, and historical sites of the first Siamese Kingdom.
Afternoon:
Travel from Sukhothai to Ayutthaya: Take a bus or train to Ayutthaya, the former capital of the Kingdom of Siam.
Visit Ayutthaya Historical Park: Explore the UNESCO World Heritage Site, including the famous temples and ruins.
Evening:
Witness Ayutthaya's sunset: Enjoy the serene ambiance as you watch the sunset over the historical city.
Return to Bangkok: Take an evening train or bus back to Bangkok to conclude your trip."
