But the costume of Hal Zultran was anything but an iterative process. According to head costume designer Juliana Rosette, the team went through over a dozen redesigns before finalizing the space smuggler's outfit. "There were just so many inspirations", Rosette said. "From designs that made him out to be a swashbuckling adventurer to a cool, collected riverboat gambler type. The early concept sketches had very little in common."

Ultimately, however, it was lead actor Alex Singh's own personal sense of fashion that helped create the Hal Zultran that audiences know today. "It was really the casting that pushed us past the finish line," said Director Gloria Talbert. "Singh showed up on set wearing this really fashion-forward leather jacket with these sequins and we were like, that's it! That's our space smuggler!" The sequined leather jacket came first, soon to be followed by Hal's signature tanker boots, flight pants, and curly updo. They had their hero at last.

Shooting would commence just a short week later, and test audiences would remark that Zultran's glam-rock-inspired outfit was central to the recognizability of his character. Movie posters were redone in quick time to capitalize on the visual poignancy of the character's appearance, sending what was written to be a deuteragonist to being one of the headline characters of the franchise.
