Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big tree in the backyard, lived a little girl named Mia who loved to play with her favorite stuffed animals. One sunny afternoon, while exploring outside, Mia noticed a tiny, sparkly bug on her finger. It buzzed and said, "Help me, please! My home is broken and I can’t find my friends."

Mia smiled and said, "Of course! Let’s fix your home!" She gathered her toys and started building a bridge with her blocks. When she finished, the bug flew up and said, "Thank you! My friends will be so happy to see you!"

Mia’s friends—her teddy bear, her dinosaur, and her cat—were waiting outside. They cheered when they saw her. "We missed you!" they said. Mia hugged her friends and hugged the bug too. "We’ll be friends forever!" she said.

That night, Mia snuggled into her bed, feeling happy and safe. She knew that even tiny bugs and big toys could make each other happy. And so, Mia fell asleep, dreaming of adventures with her friends and the sparkly bug.
