A fragile vow, its words slick and hollow,
Bound by shadows, not by truth.
The weight of "I'll be there" clings like smoke—
A breath too soon, a ghost that’s not home.

Time warps the promise, turns it to dust,
While the one who swore now stands apart—
The other side of the bargain’s rust,
A hollowed-out "yes" that never was art.

The hands that shook were trembling, not sure,
The heart that spoke was a borrowed lie.
The weight of failure fades—yet never sure,
How much of the truth still lingers in the sky.
