Rough, weathered stone floors with deep crevices and embedded lichen patches, their surfaces worn smooth by centuries of use. Soft moss blankets the edges of cracks, shifting underfoot like living carpet. The air hums with the rustle of ancient wood—charred and splintered beams, their bark peeling in places, revealing rough, grainy layers beneath. A damp, earthy smell lingers, thickened by the scent of damp wool from old cloaks discarded here. Velvety cobwebs drift in slow currents, clinging to the walls where light filters through stained-glass windows—thick, brittle panes with veins of discolored glass. The ceiling is a patchwork of exposed timber and rusted iron, some beams groaning faintly, others sagging unevenly. Warm, creamy light pools over smooth, polished wooden tables, their surfaces etched with faded inks and smudges of time. Dust swirls lazily in the air, carried by the scent of aged parchment and old leather bound books.
