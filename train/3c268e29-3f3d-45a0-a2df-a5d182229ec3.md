A clock tower in the village of **Harmony Hollow**, where the sundials mark time as **"harmonic beats"**—each hour divided into 12 "chimes" instead of 12 minutes. The clockmaker, a reclusive lighthouse keeper, adjusts the pendulum to align with the village’s "beat of the hourglass," where the sand falls in phases tied to local traditions. Midnight is called **"The Silent Pause"** when the town’s music stops for exactly 15 seconds. The timekeeping is enforced by the village elder, who fines those who disrupt the harmony by more than a "quarter chime."
