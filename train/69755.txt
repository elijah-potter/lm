What Makes a Good Life Skills Program and Its Benefits A life skills training curriculum is geared towards prevention of substance abuse and to address psychological, social, attitude and cognitive factors in relation to different intoxication and addictive substances. The objectives of a life skills curriculum include developing social skills, promoting positive coping mechanism instead of adverse behaviors and enhancing personal competence to totally get rid of substance addiction. The different life skills programs are taught with the use of different techniques such as demonstration, instruction, reinforcement, feedback, and practice. There are many studies showing that life skills training classes can help in reducing alcohol, tobacco and marijuana use, decreasing use of hallucinogens, narcotics, and inhalants. There are different life skills curriculum offered to different ages and groups and they are effective when taught by healthcare professionals, teachers or peer leaders. Life skills are representing the psychosocial skills determining valued behaviors and reflective skills like critical thinking and problems solving, as well as personal skills like self-awareness and interpersonal skills. Life skills practice increases self-esteem, improves sociability and increases problem tolerance, taking action and generating change, as well as developing abilities to make smart decisions and reinforcement of positive thoughts. Life skills are essential in facing the challenges of everyday life, creating major impacts in the workplace, education, and home life. Life training programs bring tons of benefits to an individual including developing self-confidence and spoken skills for group cooperation and collaboration; developing appreciation for others; finding new ways of problem-solving and thinking; teaching students sense of responsibility, and promoting greater sense of self-awareness. A life skills curriculum offers tons of benefits when it comes to dealing with work or employments such as developing good people and time management; adapt to various roles to be flexible in different working environments; developing the ability to solve problems, self-manage and adapt to business environment; developing skills to work actively as part of a team; and developing leadership skills. In terms of life skills’ influence to the society, it includes the development of negotiation skills, respect for cultural diversity and recognition of cultural awareness, making an easier international cooperation, building resolutions effectively and flourishing a more tolerant citizenry. Learning life skills training courses prepare a person to face the challenges and trials of everyday life, helping communities maintain peace and order and building a stronger nation built with optimistic and responsible people.
