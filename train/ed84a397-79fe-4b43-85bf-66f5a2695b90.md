The echo chamber hummed, walls alive with static, each syllable a half-finished thought that dissolved into the void.

*"Why do you think we’re alone?"*
*"Because the silence speaks louder than the stars."*

A pause. The air thickened, a slow burn.

*"What if it’s not silence? What if it’s waiting?"*
*"Waiting for what?"*

*"For us to stop."*

A breath. The words tangled, unraveling into the dark like smoke.

*"I don’t want to stop."*

*"Then don’t."*

Silence. Not absence—presence. The pause between words, the space where meaning was lost, became the only thing left to say.
