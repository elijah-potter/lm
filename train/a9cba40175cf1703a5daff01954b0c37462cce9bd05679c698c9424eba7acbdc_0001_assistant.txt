Brave knight, thank you for accepting this arduous quest. Your role is to vanquish the hunger that is plaguing my home and family. Many other knights have previously failed this task, but I believe in your capabilities. To reach my castle, you must take a right after reaching Arby's banquet hall. From there, you must continue along the trail until you reach Castle 14, which will be on the left-hand side of the trail. It is easily identifiable by the green carriage sitting outside. If you pass the crimson stop sign, alas, you have gone too far and must retreat. Please leave my delicious, cheesy treasure outside the gates of the castle, but do not knock, for fear of awaking the hounds that slumber inside. Should you succeed in your quest, you will be greatly rewarded. Should you fail, famine will continue to spread across my land. Good luck, brave knight.
