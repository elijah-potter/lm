In a mist-laden forest, a lone traveler hesitated at a fork in the path, both routes stretching before them. The left fork descended into dense undergrowth, while the right led through open fields toward a distant village. The shorter route was clear, the trail well-trodden, but the air here was stale with the scent of decaying leaves. The longer path, however, wound through towering oaks and whispered secrets to the wind. The traveler’s boots crunched over brittle twigs, their pulse quickening as they stepped onto the unfamiliar path. The sun dipped lower, casting long shadows across the earth. Shadows moved where there were none before. The journey would be slower, but the destination—somewhere unseen—felt more alive, more *theirs*. They kept going.
