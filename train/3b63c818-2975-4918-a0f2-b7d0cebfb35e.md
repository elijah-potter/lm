Indeterminate in scope: *Temporal causality*—how events influence one another across time without a fixed origin or endpoint.
Unanswerable by direct query: The *meaning* of "time" in human perception vs. quantum mechanics.
No empirical boundary: "What was the first thought of the universe?"—subjective vs. cosmic entropy.
Ambiguous constraints: "Why do some languages have no past tense?"—cultural, grammatical, or evolutionary selection pressure.
Philosophical paradox: "Does consciousness exist without a body?"—circular reasoning between observer and observed.
Unsolved computational problem: "What is the most efficient algorithm for an undecidable problem?"—Halting problem's infinite search space.
