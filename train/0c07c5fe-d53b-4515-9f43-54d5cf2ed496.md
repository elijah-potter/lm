Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop and play. One sunny morning, Benny spotted a shiny red apple in the garden. "Mmm, I wonder what's inside," he thought. Benny picked it carefully and took a tiny bite. It tasted sweet!

Just then, Benny saw his friend Benny the Badger digging a hole. "Let's play a game!" Benny said. Benny the Badger grinned. They dug a hole and put the apple inside. Benny put a big stone over it to hide it.

Later, Benny had a snack and felt happy. But then he heard a rustling noise. A small mouse named Mimi popped out. "Oh no, Benny!" she squeaked. "My granny's apple is buried here!"

Benny and Benny the Badger worked together to dig up the hole. "Here it is!" said Benny. Mimi's granny's apple was so big and juicy. The three friends shared it, and everyone was happy. Benny learned that sharing makes everyone smile.
