STEM CELL THERAPY Stem cells or extracts contain proteins that work on the DNA level. These proteins switch genes on and cause them to function, thereby reproducing particular cells. Miraculously they do not run amuck and over-produce cells or produce incorrect cells (cancers). This has been shown over the last seventy plus years of usage. Safety and efficacy mark this treatment. Human stem cell therapies have yet to be proven over time, for safety and efficacy. They should be considered strictly experimental and a futuristic treatment that offers little over this time-tested, safe and efficacious form.
