A morning sunrise stretched into an endless gray, the clock’s ticking an unrelenting march of minutes. Coffee brewed, steamed, forgotten halfway through the day as the calendar pages flipped too fast. Work demands stretched from 9 to 5, then blurred into afternoons spent staring at screens, emails piling, thoughts drifting into a half-remembered haze.

The evening was a blur of dishes, half-finished meals, and promises left unfulfilled. Lights flickered out too soon, sleep evading like a ghost. The night air felt thick with the weight of a day that had been both too long and too short—crushed between obligations and the exhaustion of not enough time.

Now, the dawn creeps again, another cycle, another cycle of feeling like the world moves at a speed that leaves me behind.
