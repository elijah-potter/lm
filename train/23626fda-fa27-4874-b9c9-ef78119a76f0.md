**Passageway:**
A narrow, arched corridor between two buildings, lined with rusted metal grates and flickering fluorescent lights. Cobblestones beneath feet are worn smooth by countless footsteps. At either end, heavy double doors sealed with metal clasps; only a single, dimly lit stairwell spirals upward through a vaulted ceiling, its steps groaning under weight. A faint, persistent scent of damp and old wood lingers—like the smell of a city’s underbelly.

**Key Features:**
- **Function:** Transit hub (e.g., transit hub, backstage pass-through)
- **Materials:** Corrugated metal, concrete, glass
- **Lighting:** Artificial (fluorescent/LED)
- **Noise:** Constant ambient (e.g., distant chatter, distant machinery)
- **Aesthetic:** Industrial, utilitarian, minimalist
- **Symbolism:** Temporary connection, fleeting passage, shared anonymity

**Example Use Case:**
*"The subway tunnel between 42nd and 43rd Street in NYC, where commuters rush past, hands in pockets, phones glowing. No lockers, no benches—just a loop between two stations."*
