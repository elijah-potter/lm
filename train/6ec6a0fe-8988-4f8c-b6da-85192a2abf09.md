A sudden gust of wind sent a loose branch crashing into the startled figure’s shoulder, jarring them from their mid-step. The person froze mid-twirl, hands flying to shield their face as the bright, blinding light of a passing car’s headlights blinded them for a moment. Their breath hitched as they stumbled backward, legs tangling with a loose tree stump. The scent of damp earth and pine mingled with the acrid tang of burnt rubber as the car’s engine roared to life behind them, tires screeching in reverse. The moment of panic dissolved into a sharp intake of air as they realized they were no longer alone.
