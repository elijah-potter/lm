
1. Love triangles. Your song could explore the complicated love between a couple and another person. There are usually lots of turmoil and conflict in love triangles because, typically, one person is torn between two people. 
2. Jealously. Many songs cover the rivalry between the main character and a potential suitor for their partner. You can play with suspicions or infidelity. This is a pretty common problem for real-world couples, so your song will definitely be relatable. 
3. Separation and reunion. Your song can tell the story of a couple separated because of serious circumstances. Throughout the song, they wonder if they’ll see each other again or not. It’s up to you!
4. Travel. Write about a couple and their many adventures together. Maybe they went to Paris or Rome. You have a lot of creative freedom here—it doesn’t even have to be a real place!
5. New love. Everyone can agree there’s something special and spectacular about that initial feeling when you fall in love for the first time. Your lyrics can gush about this new person and wonder if they feel the same way.
