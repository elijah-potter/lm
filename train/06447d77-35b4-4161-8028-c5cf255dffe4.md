The rain fell hard.
It drummed against the window, relentless, like a heartbeat.

I pressed my forehead to the glass.
Every drop was a question, a whisper.

The clock ticked.
The seconds stretched like rope under pressure.

She stood at the foot of the bed.
Her fingers trembled.

The door creaked open.
A shadow stretched too long.

He turned the light off.
The room swallowed the sound.

A single step.
Then another.

The gun was cold in my hand.
The trigger felt too easy.
