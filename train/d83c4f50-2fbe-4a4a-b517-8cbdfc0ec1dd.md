## Project Chimera: Rulebook

**A Cooperative Game for 2-4 Players**

**Introduction**

Project Chimera places you in the roles of specialized researchers at the Global Biohazard Response Initiative (GBRI), a clandestine organization dedicated to preventing catastrophic biological threats. A novel retrovirus, codenamed Chimera, has emerged, exhibiting unprecedented adaptability and rapid mutation. Its transmission is initially localized, but projections indicate a potential for global pandemic within weeks. Your mission is to research Chimera, develop countermeasures, and contain its spread before it overwhelms the world. Success demands collaboration, strategic resource allocation, and difficult choices with potentially devastating consequences.



**1. Components**

*   **Game Board:** A world map depicting key regions and infrastructure.
*   **Chimera Strain Cards (60):** Representing different mutations and properties of the virus.
*   **Research Cards (45):** Providing potential solutions, analysis tools, and operational support.
*   **Event Cards (30):** Introducing unexpected challenges and opportunities.
*   **GBRI Agent Cards (4):** Each card represents a unique researcher with specialized skills.
*   **Containment Tokens (40):** Used to suppress outbreaks.
*   **Resource Tokens (80):** Representing Funding, Personnel, and Equipment.
*   **Outbreak Markers (20):** Indicate regions experiencing active Chimera outbreaks.
*   **Pandemic Track:** A linear track indicating the escalating global threat level.
*   **Dice (6):** Used for skill checks and event resolution.
*   **Rulebook (this document).**



**2. Setup**

1.  Place the Game Board in the center of the playing area.
2.  Shuffle the Chimera Strain Cards and place them face down in a draw pile. Reveal the top three cards and place them face up next to the draw pile; these represent the currently active Chimera strains.
3.  Shuffle the Research Cards and place them face down in a draw pile.
4.  Shuffle the Event Cards and place them face down in a draw pile.
5.  Place the Pandemic Track marker at the starting position.
6.  Distribute Resource Tokens as follows:
    *   Funding: 15
    *   Personnel: 10
    *   Equipment: 5
7.  Each player chooses a GBRI Agent Card and places it in front of them.
8.  Place three Outbreak Markers on the designated starting regions as indicated on the Game Board.



**3. Turn Structure**

Each player's turn consists of the following phases, performed in order:

1.  **Draw Event Card:** Draw the top card from the Event Card deck and resolve its effects immediately. Events can introduce new outbreaks, modify existing conditions, or offer temporary advantages.
2.  **Agent Action Phase:** You may perform two actions. Actions can be any combination of the following:
    *   **Research:** Spend 2 Funding to draw two Research Cards, choose one to keep, and discard the other.
    *   **Containment:** Spend 1 Personnel and 1 Equipment to place a Containment Token on a region with an Outbreak Marker.
    *   **Analyze Strain:** Spend 1 Personnel to draw the top card of the Chimera Strain deck and examine it. Return the card to the bottom of the deck. This action provides valuable information about future mutations.
    *   **Relocate:** Move your Agent to an adjacent region on the Game Board.
    *   **Support:** Transfer 1 Resource Token (Funding, Personnel, or Equipment) to another player in the same region.
3.  **Chimera Mutation Phase:** Draw the top card from the Chimera Strain deck and resolve its effects. This typically involves adding Outbreak Markers to new regions or modifying existing outbreaks.
4.  **Advance Pandemic Track:** Move the Pandemic Track marker one space forward.



**4. Trade-Off Mechanics**

Project Chimera incorporates several mechanics that force difficult choices and trade-offs:

1.  **Strain Prioritization:** The three face-up Chimera Strain cards represent immediate threats. Players must collectively decide which strain to focus research efforts on. Ignoring one strain allows it to spread unchecked, potentially triggering a chain reaction of outbreaks.
2.  **Resource Allocation Uncertainty:** When resolving Event Cards, players must often choose between two options, each with unknown consequences.  The choice is made by rolling a die, with the outcome determined by a table on the Event Card. This introduces uncertainty and forces players to weigh potential risks and rewards.
3.  **Containment vs. Research:**  Containment efforts directly suppress outbreaks, buying time. However, neglecting research allows Chimera to mutate and develop resistance to existing countermeasures. Players must constantly balance immediate crisis management with long-term solution development.



**5. Win/Loss Conditions**

*   **Win Condition:**  Players win the game if they successfully develop a universal countermeasure. This is achieved by discarding five Research Cards with the “Countermeasure” keyword.
*   **Loss Conditions:** Players lose the game if:
    *   The Pandemic Track marker reaches the final space.
    *   There are more Outbreak Markers on the Game Board than the Game Board can accommodate.



**6. Difficulty Scaling**

*   **2 Players:** Reduce the starting number of Resource Tokens by 5 across all types.
*   **3 Players:** Play with the standard setup.
*   **4 Players:** Increase the starting number of Outbreak Markers to four.



**7. Advanced Variants**

*   **Specialized Agents:** Introduce a new set of GBRI Agent Cards with more asymmetrical abilities and starting resources.
*   **Adaptive Chimera:**  Modify the Chimera Strain deck to include more severe mutations and escalating threats.
*   **Black Market:** Introduce a new deck of cards representing opportunities and risks associated with illicit information and resources.



**Designer's Note**

Project Chimera was designed to explore themes of scientific collaboration, ethical decision-making, and the complexities of crisis management. The game aims to encourage players to appreciate the importance of diverse perspectives, the challenges of uncertainty, and the potential consequences of even seemingly minor decisions. The mechanics are intended to foster a sense of shared responsibility and to highlight the vital role of communication and strategic planning in overcoming global challenges. The game is not intended to be a scientifically accurate representation of virology, but rather a framework for exploring important societal values.
