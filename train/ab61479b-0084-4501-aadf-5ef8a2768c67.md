**BREAKING:** In the quiet coastal town of **Port Veyra**, a mysterious surge in marine activity has left residents baffled. Yesterday, fishermen reported sightings of an unidentifiable, bioluminescent pod near the harbor. Officials confirmed the pod measured approximately **5 meters in diameter**, glowing faintly blue-green with rapid, erratic movements. Witnesses described the entity as resembling a **swarm of overlapping, translucent spheres** rather than a singular form.

Local marine biologists, Dr. Elias Voss, speculate this could be an experimental organism or an uncharted species. Authorities have deployed cameras and drones to monitor the area, though the pod has since vanished without trace. The town’s mayor has declared a curfew for tonight, urging residents to avoid the shore between sunset and dawn.

Caution is advised—neither authorities nor the public have witnessed the pod again. The Port Veyra Marine Institute will provide updates tomorrow.
