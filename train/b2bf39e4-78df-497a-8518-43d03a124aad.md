In the dim glow of a dimly lit warehouse, a forklift operator navigates past a stack of pallets, ignoring the red "Do Not Cross" sign. The forklift’s arm bends just enough to clear the barrier, the forked end grazing the edge of the sign before the operator swiftly moves on, avoiding a full violation while still accessing the restricted area.

A single item, marked as "prohibited," is placed on a nearby shelf. The operator retrieves it without hesitation, slipping it into a bag under the counter, while the rest of the inventory remains untouched. The warehouse supervisor’s clipboard sits unmarked, and the forklift’s sensor light flickers—just enough to imply compliance with the spirit of the rule rather than the letter.
