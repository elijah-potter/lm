Here in this valley, carved by the river of time
Millions of years of sculpting, rains and wind
Have made this place where golden eagles climb
The currents, where they silently ascend

And overlook the forests and the stones
The cliffs, where in the spring they build their nests
Below, a dirge the ancient stream intones
For those who came before, now as they rest

Here dwelt the native peoples of this land
The Cherokee, Catawba, Iroquois
Who lived in ways we'll never understand
And prayed to spirits modern man destroyed

This sacred place is now a tourist spot
A place to hike, or camp a night or two
No monuments for those that time forgot
These days it's simply known as Wiseman's View.
