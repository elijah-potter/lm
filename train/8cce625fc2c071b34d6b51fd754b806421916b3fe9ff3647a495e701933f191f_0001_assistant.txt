Ocean waves that are incredibly long 
Signal that something is terribly wrong 
Powerful waters that can create floods
Covering the earth with branches and mud

Threatening human property and land
Coating buildings in saltwater and sand 
Damage is severe and lasts a long time 
With the tsunami's power in its prime 

Usually starting with an earthquake 
On the ocean floor, creating the wake 
Danger continues, returning to sea 
Knocking the humans right off of their feet 

Natural warnings come as train-like roars
And shakings of the ground and building floors
Sirens may go off and sound the alarm 
To help citizens avoid harm
