On first inspection, the connection between metal forming and band saws isn’t immediately apparent. Metal forming is the process by which metal is reshaped without adding or removing material. Most of the time, metals are permanently altered into the shapes that shops will use. Common shapes created by metal cutting include plates, sheets, rods, tubes and structural sections. While the outward appearance of the metal changes, the inside of the metal remains the same. Band saws are tools used to cut and alter the shape of work pieces. Since they are capacity-type machines, band saws can cut many different types of material.
Essential Metal Cutting Variables
So what’s the relationship between the two? Well, the size and shape of the material that needs to be cut is one of the variables essential to successful metal cutting. Small solids and structural materials are tough to cut. Some structures’ shapes can make it difficult to get the correct angle. It’s important to cut through the thinnest portion of the work piece.
Some Steels Need Specific Band Saws
Another important factor in the relationship between metal forming and band saws is the material’s make up. Certain types of steel need specific band saw blades. Structural materials require a specific blade type that can cut large cross sections.
