Chelsea was sitting on her porch when her world ended. A sparkle of light had been following her for weeks, she knew. It had taken her some time to realize that the thing, whatever it was, was trying to communicate with her, but out of fright, hadn't approached her directly. Presently, the light came closer and a figure revealed itself, not quite human, but not unrecognizable either. She was surprised, but she didn't want to scare it away. Not now that she was face-to-face.
"I am Medan, messager of the moon spirit," the creature said. Chelsea could only blink in astonishment as the bizarre thing moved closer.
"You have been gifted with great power," the messenger said. "The moon spirit, who moves in the space between, has bestowed this upon you to restore order to our world." Still, Chelsea could not move, but she was starting to wonder whether she had dozed off or was hallucinating. Until the fairy was gored through by a massive tusk. Alive one moment and dead the next, the monster tossed aside the fairy's body and turned its eyes to Chelsea. 

The rhino, or boar, whatever it was, kept shifting as if it was having a hard time gripping this reality. She stumbled backward in terror, unable to think or scream, and ran through the house, bolting out the back door and into the dark night. The creature gave chase, tearing her home asunder. Chelsea ran over the hills, through the woods until she had lost the beast and could go no further. She collapsed in a heap and began to sob. The life she had known was over; it had given way to this nightmare she couldn't wake up from.
