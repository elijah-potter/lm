Simon is a popular electronic game that has been around since 1978. It tests your short-term memory by flashing colors, each with its own unique tone/note, in sequence. The mission is to repeat the sequence, and it doesn't take long before the game becomes complicated due to the frantic pace and increased length of the sequence.

There are four colored buttons on the game. The blue button is found in the lower right of the setup, the yellow button is in the lower left, the red button is in the upper right, and the green button is in the upper left.
