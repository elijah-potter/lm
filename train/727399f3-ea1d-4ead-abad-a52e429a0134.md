Once upon a time, in a big, cozy house with a tree outside, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white ears and a big, round belly that wobbled when he hopped.

One sunny morning, Benny saw his favorite toy—a green ball—rolling away under the bushes. "Where did that ball go?" Benny asked his friends: a squirrel named Sammy, a duck named Ducky, and a cat named Mr. Whiskers.

Sammy the squirrel had a shiny acorn to hide, Ducky had a tasty worm to find, and Mr. Whiskers had a warm blanket to nap in. But Benny wanted his green ball!

"Let’s help!" Benny said, hopping toward the bushes. "We’ll look together!"

They dug under the leaves and peeked behind the rocks. "Maybe it’s hiding?" Sammy asked.

Then, Ducky spotted something shiny in the dirt! It was Benny’s green ball! "There it is!" she cheered.

Benny picked it up and gave it a big hug. "I was so worried you’d be gone forever!" he said, giggling.

Soon, they all played together—Benny chased his ball, Sammy counted his acorns, Ducky waddled with worms, and Mr. Whiskers stretched his paws. The sun was setting, and the house smelled like warm cookies.

As Benny snuggled into his blanket, he smiled. "Best adventure ever," he whispered to the sky.

And that’s how Benny and his friends found their special things—and learned that sharing and playing together is the best part of being friends!
