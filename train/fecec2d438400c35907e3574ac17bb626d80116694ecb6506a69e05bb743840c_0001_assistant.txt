Sansa, Arya, Bran, and Jon gathered in Winterfell, preparing for the final battle against the forces of the undead led by the Night King. As they rode out to meet their enemy, the heroes knew that this would be their final stand.

The battle was intense, with heavy casualties on both sides, but then something miraculous happened: the Night King was slain by a mysterious stranger, and the army of the dead crumbled to dust. The victory was not without cost; as the survivors looked around, they realized with horror that every Stark was lying dead on the battlefield. Sansa, Arya, Bran, and Jon had sacrificed themselves to save the realm.

As the people of Westeros mourned their fallen heroes, a new era began. Daenerys Targaryen, now queen, set to work rebuilding her shattered kingdom, while the memories of the Stark family slowly faded into legend.
