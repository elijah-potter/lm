Sure! Keyword research is the first step in creating SEO content. Here are the steps to take when just starting keyword research: 

Step 1: Brainstorm your "seed" keywords. This shouldn't take too long as these will be words that are quite broad but relate to your website's purpose. 
Step 2: Do competitor research.  To find your competitors, google your seed words and see which sites come up first. Then, you can see what your competitors are ranking for using an online competitive intelligence tool for SEO. 
Step 3: Use keyword research tools. This is another way to add to your list of keywords. There are both free tools and ones that cost money, but the most popular is Google Keyword Planner, which is free to use. 
Step 4: Learn more about your niche. Keep adding to your list of keywords by knowing what the people in your niche are talking about by visiting forums, reading Twitter threads, etc.
