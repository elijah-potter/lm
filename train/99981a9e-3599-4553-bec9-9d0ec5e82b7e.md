A spontaneous consensus emerged during a weekly neighborhood potluck when the mayor’s drone accidentally dropped a package of blueberry jam into the community garden plot. Initially, it was unclear whose responsibility it was to clean up, but participants agreed it was a shared effort. One resident volunteered to gather the mess while others offered tools and help. The jam became a catalyst for discussion: the garden’s overused compost pile needed repairs, and the community agreed to fund a new fence to prevent future mishaps. The accidental "gift" turned into a collective project, proving that even chaos could spark collective action.
