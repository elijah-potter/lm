Today something really scary happened. Mommy put me into my car seat, and Teddy was sitting right beside me in his. I was really excited because we were all going to a birthday party later but first, we were going to get ice cream. Teddy was excited, too.

Out of the blue, Mommy started yelling, and the car swerved. A man in the lane next to us was really close, and I could see his angry eyes when he looked at Mommy. Then he held out his arm and yelled at us. I couldn't hear what he said, but it didn't sound nice. There were a lot of loud horn noises and I went forward in my car seat and the straps pulled so tight that for a second, I couldn't breathe. When I looked at Teddy I could see the same thing happened to him. Tears came out of my eyes, and Mommy was looking at me through the mirror saying that she was sorry. 

We didn't go to the birthday party or to get ice cream because Teddy and I wouldn't stop crying. My head hurt, and Teddy said his did, too. I don't understand why people have to get so mad when they drive. When I am older and drive around, I will make sure to give the other cars a lot of room so they don't drive on top of each other and ruin everybody's day.
