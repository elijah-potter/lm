Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big red door, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white ears and a big, happy smile. Every morning, Benny would hop outside to see his best friend, Max, a tall bluebird with a shiny feathered hat.

One sunny afternoon, Benny spotted a sparkly blue flower near Max’s nest. "Wow!" Benny said, "Look at that flower!" Max flew down and sniffed it. "It smells like candy!" they chirped together.

Just then, Benny’s tummy rumbled. "I’m hungry," Benny said. "Let’s go find some yummy snacks!" Max agreed, and together they hopped and flew to Benny’s favorite spot—a basket of yummy berries. They picked a few and munched happily.

Suddenly, a little cloud rolled in, and it started to rain. Benny’s ears drooped. "Oh no! What if we get wet?" Max fluttered his wings. "Don’t worry! I’ll help!" Max built a tiny umbrella out of his feathers. "Here, hold on tight!" Benny hopped under Max’s big, fluffy umbrella, and they danced in the rain until the clouds went away.

When the sun came out again, Benny and Max played tag until their paws got tired. Then, they curled up together under Max’s nest, snuggled up warm and cozy. As the stars twinkled above, Benny and Max fell asleep, dreaming of more adventures under the moon.

And that’s how Benny and Max learned that even when the weather changes, friendship makes everything bright and fun!
