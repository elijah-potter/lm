Yeah, definitely! Here's a list of films originally based on comics that have nothing to do with superheroes:

- THE ADDAMS FAMILY (1991) is based on a series of one-panel comics by Charles Addams, first published in 1938.
- ANNIE (1982) is based on a comic strip called "Little Orphan Annie" by Harold Gray, first published in 1924.
- GARFIELD: THE MOVIE (2004) is based on comic strips by Jim Davis which were initially titled "Jon" first published in 1976.
- GHOST WORLD (2001) is based on a comic book by Daniel Clowes, first published in 1993.
- BLUE IS THE WARMEST COLOR (2013) is based on a French graphic novel, originally titled "Blue Angel" in English, by Jul Maroh, first published in 2010.

Many non-superhero comics have been adapted into films, but this is just a small list. I hope this helps!
