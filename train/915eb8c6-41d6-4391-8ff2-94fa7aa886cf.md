A **one-time operational rule** in many workflows is:
**"Do not use this command unless explicitly required during emergency shutdown procedures."**

This rule exists in critical infrastructure (e.g., power plants, nuclear facilities) to prevent unauthorized overrides during high-risk scenarios. Once activated, it’s deactivated and never revisited unless documented in a formal emergency protocol.

Example: A **lockout tagout (LOTO) bypass override** for a specific emergency valve.
