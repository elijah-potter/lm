The room was dimly lit by flickering fluorescent lights. A figure stood near the corner, clutching a camera, their breath shallow. The screen flickered with static before displaying a blurry image—a woman in a high-necked dress, her face obscured by a hooded cloak. The camera’s flash had been too quick, the motion blur too sharp. They replayed the footage three times, each time seeing different details: a faint scar along her jawline, the way her fingers trembled slightly, the way the streetlight cast her shadow long and uneven. Outside, the rain pattered against the window, unrelenting. Their hands shook as they uploaded the photo to an encrypted drive, the caption reading: *"Did I imagine it?"*
