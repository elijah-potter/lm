A pause between the last word of *"We’ll try again tomorrow"* and the door closing behind him lingered. Outside, the city pulsed—cars honking, distant laughter—while inside, the room felt hollow. He had said it out loud, but the weight of what he couldn’t say pressed against his ribs. The silence wasn’t absence; it was the space where the unspoken voice still whispered. The clock ticked. The key turned. And for a moment, the only thing that mattered was the way the light caught the edge of the doorframe, framing the pause itself.
