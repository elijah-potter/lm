The complexity arises when ethical, logistical, and emotional factors converge into an equation where no single answer satisfies everyone equally. For instance, choosing between a colleague who relies on you for their career and one who needs you for survival—where the latter’s urgency is louder but the former’s loyalty is deeper. The simplicity lies in the binary framing (e.g., "Do X or Y?"), but the true decision is a calculus of trade-offs: *Will this choice hurt the former more than it helps the latter in the long run?* The weight of the decision isn’t in the action itself but in the stories you’ll carry if you’re wrong. The system isn’t broken; it’s the human heart that’s the real algorithm.
