Baseball is a game where two teams take turns at bat attempting to earn runs by hitting a baseball. When a player hits the ball, they run around three bases to reach home plate, which scores a run. The team in the field has the goal of stopping the other team from scoring runs by catching their hit balls in the air, throwing the ball to a base ahead of the runner, or tagging the runner out. Batters can also strike out by missing the ball with the bat 3 times or can earn a base if the pitcher throws 4 balls outside of the strike zone. At the end of 9 innings, the team with the most runs wins the game. Baseball is popular in many countries, but has been nicknamed "America's Pastime."
