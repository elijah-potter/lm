The taxonomic ranks, or taxonomic hierarchy, is a system of classification that names and categorizes all living things based on their physical characteristics. This classification was developed in the 18th century by the Swedish botanist Carolus Linnaeus. Here are the 8 taxonomic ranks, based on hierarchy. This list includes the scientific classifications for humans.

1. Domain (Eukaryota)
2. Kingdom (Animalia)
3. Phylum or Division (Chordata)
4. Class (Mammalia)
5. Order (Primates)
6. Family (Hominidae)
7. Genus (Homo)
8. Species (sapiens)
