English For Speakers Of Other Languages
The ESOL course is a University Of Cambridge based curriculum emphasising social and academic language proficiency. The courses overarching standard is that students will use English to communicate and demonstrate academic, social, and cultural understanding.To reach this standard, it is critical that instructional approaches, both in ESOL and general education classes, accommodate the needs of our students linguistically and culturally. Where possible, we use the home language of our students as a means of facilitating instruction for English language learning and communication.
