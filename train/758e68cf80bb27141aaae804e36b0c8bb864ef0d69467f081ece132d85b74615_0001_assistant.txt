Changing Weather

The rain slowed down a little, but the drops still dribble
They trickle and fall, zig-zag and scribble
Surfaces reflect, and surfaces ripple
Dirt is turned to mud, streams begin to flood
The clouds filled darker and lightning struck
Puddles turned cloudy, almost like muck
When it comes to weather today is bad luck

But then the rain stopped, and the last raindrop flopped
The clouds moved away and the sun was unlocked
The bunnies hopped out and the birds talked
In fact, the birds sang, and through the forest, their echos rang
The sun's warmth started to make the ground dry
And brighter and brighter became the sky
A sight so beautiful that it's hard not to cry
