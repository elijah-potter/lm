A clock on the wall ticks, but time feels stretched, each second dragging into something heavier.

Outside, the city hums—voices, sirens, the distant clatter of traffic—but none of it feels real. The wait is the only thing anchoring them. Somewhere, a door creaks. The person’s breath catches.
