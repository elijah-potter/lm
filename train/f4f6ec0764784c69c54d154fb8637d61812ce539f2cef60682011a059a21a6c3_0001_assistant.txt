**Banish the Night**

What lights do call me now
Inside this wretched dream –
Terror quakes against my brow
As whiteness wields a gleam –

Perhaps this is not night!
The bed plane falls away.
The brightness joins the fight. 
The darkness starts to fray!

I tremble deeply – cheered at last
Phantoms are chased out,
Through sleepy webs the light does blast
‘til morning wins this bout!
