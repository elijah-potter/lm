The **Burial Mounds of Carnac**, France, feature standing stones arranged in precise circles and rows. Their alignment with celestial events (e.g., solstices) suggests a connection to ancient astronomical knowledge, possibly linked to Neolithic or Bronze Age ritual sites. Carved into the stones are faded inscriptions and petroglyphs, remnants of lost languages and beliefs. The landscape itself, with its shifting dunes and windswept cliffs, acts as a natural archive, preserving the passage of time in both the earth’s texture and the remnants of human activity. The site’s eerie quietness contrasts with the bustling modern world, making the past’s traces feel tangible.
