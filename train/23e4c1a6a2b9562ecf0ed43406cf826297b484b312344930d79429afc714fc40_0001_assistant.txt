So this might be a controversial take, but Portal 1 was just a better game than Portal 2. Yeah, I know what you might be asking. "But what about all the memes and all the jokes? What about Wheatley?" 

The truth is while Portal 2 was a bigger game that offered more, it isn't the game that captured the imagination of all those gamers who first played it in the Orange Box in 2007. People back then who bought the compilation for Half-Life or maybe Team Fortress 2 were surprised by one of the most unique and culturally resonant modern video games of all time. 

Simply put, Portal 2 is an iterative work. It builds upon foundational elements that Portal 1 introduced and should get credited for. Even if you enjoyed the puzzles more or found more of the dialogue in Portal 2 funny, the atmosphere, and lore were established by Portal 1.  There could never have been a sequel to Portal without the original Portal. Portal 1 is the classic. Portal 2 is the expansion.  Portal 1 is the must-play game. Portal 2 is for fans of the original.
