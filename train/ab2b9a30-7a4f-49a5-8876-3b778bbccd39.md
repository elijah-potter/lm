**The Quest Begins**
In the heart of **Blackthorn Hollow**, where the **River Veyne** whispers secrets to the stones, **Elrond** the Scholar stumbled upon a **cracked map** in the ruins of **Duskhaven Keep**. The parchment bore no names, only symbols:

```
☠ = The **Hollow Watcher**
☼ = The **Crystal Spire**
⚡ = The **Stormcaller’s Throne**
```

A **rusted compass** in his satchel spun wildly toward the **north-east**, where the **Blackthorn** twisted like a sentient beast. His journal entry read:

*"If I follow the map, I’ll find the **Forgotten Tome**—but the path is not what it seems."*
