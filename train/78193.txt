This Librettino per Manine puts the autumn season into perspective for beginning L2 Italian FLES students. Topics include:weather, seasonal activities,seasonal clothing, seasonal colors etc. Holidays not included.
Slide 1 Cover sheet
Slide 2 -7 are eighteen illustrated vocabulary words for your word wall. Print out on card stock and laminate
Slides 8-18 can be used as a powerpoint on the whiteboard, IPAD, tablet or printed out to read to the class
Slides 19-25 are pages to the 14 page booklet for children. There are two booklet pages per slide.
Have children cut pages and put book together according to page number.
Once pages are in the correct order, use paper fastener or staple booklet together
Students may read alone, with a partner or in a group
Students may color in the pages of the booklet
Students who have trouble focusing may look for the crow on each of the colored pages of this resource
Vocabulary includes: l'autunno, autunnale, settembre, ottobre, novembre, la maglia, fa fresco, il rastrello, le foglie, rosso, giallo, arancione, giocare, fare una passeggiata, il corvo, la mela, il melo e la zucca.
