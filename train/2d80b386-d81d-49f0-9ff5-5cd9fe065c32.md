A sudden gust of wind sent a shattered window frame crashing into the corner of the kitchen, striking the back of a startled chef mid-motion. Their knife skidded across the floor as they stumbled, one arm extended to shield their face from the jagged shards. The refrigerator door, half-open, revealed a half-eaten plate of scrambled eggs cooling on the counter. The overhead lights flickered, casting eerie shadows on the wet floor where a spilled glass of milk glistened like a tiny, abandoned teardrop. A distant siren wailed in the distance, muffled by the open window.
