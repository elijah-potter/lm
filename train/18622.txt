Arowana Fish - AquariumNexus
Arowanas are a tropical freshwater fish species native to South America, Southeast Asia and Australia. Asian Arowana is the world’s most expensive aquarium fish because they are very rare. In some countries, Asian Arowanas are illegal to be kept and sold.
In this section, you can learn about different types of arowana fish, their habitat, diet, aquarium requirements, breeding behavior, as well tank mates.
