Desert: Extreme heat (40°C+ during the day, often 30°C+ at night).
Arctic tundra: Subzero temperatures (-20°C to -30°C), with wind chill making it feel colder.
Tropical rainforest: Warm and humid (25°C–32°C), with high humidity levels (70–90%).
Mountain peak (e.g., Everest summit): Freezing (-20°C to -40°C), with strong winds and low oxygen.
Subtropical coastal city (e.g., Miami): Warm year-round (24°C–30°C), mild winters (rarely below 15°C).
Temperate forest (e.g., New York in autumn): Cool to mild (10°C–20°C), with crisp air and seasonal changes.
Polar ice cap: Consistently below freezing (-15°C to -35°C), with long polar nights in winter.
Tropical monsoon climate: Hot and rainy (30°C–38°C), frequent thunderstorms, high humidity.
Desert oasis: Warm during the day (35°C–45°C), cooler at night (20°C–25°C), with minimal clouds.
Mid-latitude continental climate (e.g., Chicago): Cold winters (-10°C to -25°C), warm summers (20°C–30°C).
High-altitude desert (e.g., Atacama): Dry and hot (15°C–30°C day, cold nights, low precipitation).
Arctic Ocean: Extremely cold (-1°C to -10°C), with freezing winds, ice floes, and 24-hour darkness in winter.
