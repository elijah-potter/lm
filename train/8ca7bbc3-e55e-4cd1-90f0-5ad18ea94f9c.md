**The Last Light**

They found the door in the rain, its brass hinges whispering like the breath of something long gone. The key turned without resistance, and the air inside was thick with the scent of old paper and something metallic—rust, perhaps, or blood.

Inside, the walls pulsed with the color of a dying sun. The floor was a tapestry of cracks and shadows, each one a story half-told. A figure sat in the center, wrapped in a blanket of stolen moments, their face half-lit by the flicker of a candle that had burned down to nothing but ash. They were holding a book, its pages filled with words that did not exist in the world outside.

The first line was already written:

*"Once upon a time, the world ended and began again."*

The figure smiled. The candle flared to life again.

And then—

**The First Spark**

The world had always been here, but it had never seen itself. A child stood at the edge of a forest, their hands trembling as they picked up a stick. The stick was not ordinary. It glowed faintly, pulsing with the same light that had once burned in the candle’s flame.

*"What are you?"* the child whispered.

The stick hummed in reply, and the voice that came from it was not their own—it was the voice of the world, whispering secrets from the depths of time.

*"I am the beginning of the end, and the end of the beginning."*

The child dropped the stick. It fell silent, fading into the earth like dust.

But somewhere, deep in the dark, the candle still burned.
