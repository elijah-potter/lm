- Write a letter to your loved ones. Tell them how much you love them and what they mean to you.
- Spend time with your family and friends. Make memories that will last a lifetime.
- Do something you've always wanted to do, but never had the chance. Go skydiving, travel to a new country, or write a book.
- Say goodbye to your pets. Give them a big hug and tell them how much you love them.
- Go to a place that is special to you. It could be a place where you grew up, a place you had a happy memory or a place that makes you feel peaceful.
- Reflect on your life. What are you proud of? What are you grateful for? What would you do differently?
- Make peace with your death. Accept that it is coming and know that you will be okay.
