•	El Paso, Texas: This city has a population of nearly 700,000 and is one of the safest cities in the country. Situated along the Mexican border, this city has year-round sun and is influenced heavily by Mexican culture.
•	Mesa, Arizona: Located within a half hour of the state capital of Phoenix, this 500,000-resident city boasts peace, tranquility, and incessant desert heat.
•	Plano, Texas: Though this city has a lower population than others (around 300,000), it’s home to many Fortune 1000 company headquarters, neighbor to the Dallas-Fort Worth area, and is really beginning to blossom as a city.
