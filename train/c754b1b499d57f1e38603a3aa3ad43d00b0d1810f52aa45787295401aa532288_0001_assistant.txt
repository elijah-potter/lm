In a manufacturing environment, your machines are constantly running. If anything should happen to go wrong, it can shut down individual machines, which may - in some cases - put an entire production line on hold. In addition, if a small issue isn't detected or addressed early, that issue can eventually develop into a problem that requires more time and resources to fix. That's why preventive maintenance (PM) is vital for sustaining the reliability of your production processes. In this article, we'll explore the impact regular preventive inspections and maintenance work can have on revenue, both in terms of preventing lost production time as well as keeping maintenance costs within budgetary constraints.
