The first step towards knowledge for beginners, the most crucial one Pre-Primary education lays the foundations of a childs knowledge, skills and rules of behaviour, pre-requisites for successful progress in daily life and school.
Play way method is used for Pre-Nursery, Nursery and Prep children by trained experienced teachers through various activities, enabling children to develop their motor, emotional, sensational and language skills.
The different learning areas toy room, sandpit, amusement park, splash pool, horse riding, and nature study ensure children enjoy, play and learn.
Smart classroom add to the fun in learning. The various CCA activities provide an opportunity to participate, develop confidence and foster competitive spirit. Excursions combine knowing about our surroundings with behaviour in public places to an outing. Assessment of children on a weekly basis and interaction with the parents through Parent Teachers is a regular feature.
The tiny lots are exposed to be the tots of basic learning activities that help them to become independent and help develop in children many self-help qualities like eating food themselves, dressing up, maintaining cleanliness and other such basic qualities.
