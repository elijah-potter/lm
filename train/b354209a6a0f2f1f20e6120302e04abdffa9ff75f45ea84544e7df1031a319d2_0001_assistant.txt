Wish Cakes

Granting and making wishes is so much more magical when it's paired with a sweet treat. Wish cakes are a special mixture of sugar and special powers that will make your wishes come true. All you need to do is take a bite!

Ingredients:
- 1 pinch of glitter
- 2 cups of flower power
- 1.5 tablespoons candy of choice
- 4 dashes of magic moss

Stir all ingredients together, infuse with fairy magic, and share with friends!
