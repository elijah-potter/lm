ISO 14001 Certification in Denmark refers to the Environmental Management Standard. It is based on Best Practices and assists in managing your business’ Environmental risks.
When the system is documented the legal and other requirements for your business are considered. Also, they should be documented the relevant environmental risks (known by aspects & impacts). You then need to specify how you plan to reduce, control or manage them. The Standard does not specify specific environmental performance criteria.
ISO 14001 Certification in Denmark is important for any business looking to meet its legal and/or other responsibilities and manage its environmental risk.
Every part of ISO 14001 must be included in any environmental management program. Every environmental system will be different due to differences in activities, services, or location.
