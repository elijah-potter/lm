Memorizing the Holy Quran is an honorable and rewarding task that requires dedication, patience, and discipline. Here is an 8-step guide that can help you in this spiritual journey:
- Start with the basics: Before starting to memorize, make sure that you have a good understanding of Arabic and the basic rules of Tajweed. If you are not familiar with Tajweed, you can take a course or seek guidance from a teacher.
- Set a realistic goal: Set a realistic goal for yourself. Start with memorizing small Surahs (chapters) or verses and gradually move on to larger ones.
- Create a schedule: Create a daily schedule that suits your routine and allows you to dedicate a fixed amount of time for memorization. Consistency is key to success.
- Understand the meaning: It is important to understand the meaning of what you are memorizing. Take time to reflect on the verses and connect with their message.
- Use repetition: Repeat what you have memorized regularly to reinforce it in your memory. Review what you have memorized before and after Salah (prayer).
- Listen to recitations: Listen to the recitation of the Quran by experienced reciters to improve your pronunciation and recitation skills.
- Seek guidance: Seek guidance from a qualified teacher or a Hafiz (someone who has memorized the Quran). They can help you with any questions you may have and provide valuable feedback.
- Stay committed and patient: Memorizing the Quran is a long-term commitment that requires patience and perseverance. Do not rush, take your time and be consistent.
Quran Memorization Techniques: How To Memorize The Holy Quran Easily?
For many beginners, memorizing the Holy Quran is a difficult challenge. However, Allah bestows immense blessings on anyone who memorizes the Quran. You don’t only get a blessing from Allah, but you also have high social prestige.
Quran memorization can be easier if you started in the right way. You’ll memorize Quran easier if you first learned the basics of Quran reading, Quran reciting, and Tajweed. It may seem like a long journey but it’ll make your memorizing progress much faster.
When you’re done with the previously mentioned phases, you may follow this simple step-by-step guide:
- Block out all distractions. Sit in a quiet spot with no noise to have a calm mind that can help you memorize what you read.
- Pick a Surah you like. This is a fantastic way to motivate you to keep moving. Choosing a Surah to which you feel connected is an excellent first step.
- Start repeating ayah (verse) by ayah. You read it slowly until you understand the meaning, then you repeat it until you have caught all of the sentences. Ayahs can often be memorized in a 20-minute session.
- Use audio aid for pronunciation. The Holy Quran can be memorized by listening to it many times. Keeping audio material with proper pronunciation will help you memorize faster, particularly if Arabic is not your first language.
- Get a sense of the rhythm. Listen carefully and then recite the entire passage aloud.
- Memorize ayah starting from a single word. Repeat a single ayah word for word at least 5 or 6 times when reading it aloud from the page until it is memorized.
- Revise daily. Do not begin memorizing another ayah until you have reviewed the one you have already memorized.
- Test yourself. Every now and then, a test is needed to ensure that you have correctly memorized Surah.

How Long Does It Take To Memorize The Holy Quran?
It probably takes you 3 or 4 years to memorize the Quran and sometimes more. The total duration depends on your fluency as a student to recite Quran before memorization, your age, and the method your teacher is using.
You can always lessen this duration by dedicating more time to Quran learning and memorizing or by finding a trusted Quran tutor online to help you with your steps.
How To Memorize The Holy Quran In One Year?
It’s true that it often takes 3 or 4 years to memorize the whole Quran, however, some people may want to achieve that in just one year so, is it even possible?
Yes, it is possible to memorize the Quran in one year. You can memorize the Holy Quran in one year by dedicating 5-to-8 hours daily to memorize new parts and 2 hours for revision. This also means memorizing 15 pages per week and dedicating the weekend to the revision of Juz (part) you memorized.
How To Memorize Quran And Never Forget It?
If you keep forgetting what you memorized from Quran, you’re not alone! The Holy Quran is known to be easily forgotten. Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) once said:
“Keep refreshing your knowledge of the Quran for [I swear] by the One in whose hand the soul of Muhammad is, it is more liable to escapes [from memory] than camels do from their hobbles”
(Al-Bukhari and Muslim)
Quran is easy to memorize, by the words of Allah, but it is as easy to forget. The solution was also mentioned in the previous Hadith: refresh your knowledge. The key to never forgetting Quran is to never abandon it and to always revise what you memorized.
To ensure that you don’t forget the Quran while memorizing it, make sure to:
- Read ayah (verse) by ayah slowly and carefully.
- Understand the meaning of it and look up words you don’t know.
- Repeat the ayah until you can fully remember it on your own.
- Revise what you memorized earlier and don’t start memorizing another ayah until you’re 100% sure you got the previous one.
- Dedicate a regular time in your weekly schedule to read Quran and revise what you memorized.
- Apply for an online Quran memorization course to get in touch with a professional instructor to help you with a memorizing method that suits you best.
