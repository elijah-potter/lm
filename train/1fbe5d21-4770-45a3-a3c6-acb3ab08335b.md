A fractured boardroom meeting ended with silence as the final vote was cast.
*"The plan is approved,"* the chair declared, but the words felt hollow.
**Prospects** had been high when the data suggested a bold pivot—but dissenters argued the risks were too great.
**Costs** were justified, but morale cratered. **Revenue projections** were ambitious; **regulatory hurdles** were ignored.
**Investors** cheered; **employees** left en masse. **Customers** were told they’d be first in line, though delays were inevitable.
**Short-term gains** outweighed **long-term trust**. The decision was correct—by the numbers. But the human cost? That was never a metric.

---
*"Trade-offs,"* someone muttered. The room agreed.
