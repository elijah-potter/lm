Compare and comparison essay
A compare and contrast essay is a type of essay that analyzes the similarities and differences between two or more subjects. These subjects can be anything from two different pieces of literature to two different political ideologies. The purpose of a compare and contrast essay is to examine the ways in which these subjects are similar and different, and to evaluate which one is more effective or superior.
To write a compare and contrast essay, the writer must first choose two subjects that have some similarities and differences. Next, the writer must brainstorm the points of comparison and contrast between the two subjects. This can be done through a variety of methods, such as creating a Venn diagram or making a list of the similarities and differences.
Once the points of comparison and contrast have been identified, the writer must organize the essay in a logical manner. There are two common methods for organizing a compare and contrast essay: the block method and the point-by-point method.
In the block method, the writer first discusses all of the similarities and differences of one subject, and then moves on to the other subject. This method is useful when the subjects being compared are quite different and the writer wants to emphasize their distinctiveness.
In the point-by-point method, the writer alternates between discussing the similarities and differences of each subject. This method is useful when the subjects being compared are more similar and the writer wants to highlight their similarities and differences in equal measure.
Regardless of which method is used, it is important for the writer to provide clear and concise transitions between each point of comparison and contrast. The writer should also make sure to provide examples and evidence to support their points and to make their argument more convincing.
In conclusion, a compare and contrast essay is a type of essay that analyzes the similarities and differences between two or more subjects. The purpose of this essay is to evaluate which one is more effective or superior. To write a compare and contrast essay, the writer must choose two subjects, brainstorm the points of comparison and contrast, and organize the essay in a logical manner using either the block method or the point-by-point method.
