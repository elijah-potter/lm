A crackle of static filled the air as the ancient relic—an obsidian orb—glowed faintly blue, its surface etched with swirling energy. The moment it touched my palm, the world *paused*. The breath of the wind stilled; the golden light in the hallway flickered as if suspended. Every second stretched into eternity as I watched the clock hands tick, each minute an hour, each hour a lifetime.

My fingers trembled as I traced the orb’s edge, feeling the hum of time itself. A child’s laughter echoed in the hallway, but it wasn’t *now*—it was from yesterday, yet it lingered like a ghost. A door creaked open, revealing a figure in tattered robes, their face blurred by the distortion. They moved as if through slow motion, yet their voice was sharp and immediate: *"The key is not in the chest, but in the heart."* The orb pulsed brighter, and the world *reversed*—time snapped back, the moment dissolved like mist.

I stumbled back, my vision blurring. The orb shattered into fragments, and the hallway returned to normal. The child’s laughter faded into silence. My pulse raced, but the world was still. The last thing I saw before the light faded was the orb’s final, fleeting glow—like a heartbeat.
