Sure! Here are a few nonprofits that help veterans nationally:

1. Tunnel to Towers Foundation: Helps fight homelessness, empowers through events, and educates about 9/11 so that “we never forget.”
2. Wounded Warrior Project: Focuses on helping injured warriors, caregivers, and family members.
3. Homes for our Troops: Builds and donates custom homes that are adapted for severely injured veterans.
4. Wheelchairs for Warriors: Provides custom wheelchairs for injured veterans.
5. K9s for Warriors: The nation’s largest provider of service dogs for veterans.
