There are lots of things you can add! Here are some things you might enjoy:
1. Mushrooms. They add good flavor and absorb the taste of the beef stew while also maintaining their own shape and texture with long cook times.
2. Carrots. These healthy root vegetables are a classic addition to beef stew because they add more substance to the dish, provided they are cut to a larger size so they don't lose their texture.
3. Fennel. In addition to growing tender within a stew, fennel also adds a wonderful aromatic element that will kick any beef stew up a notch.
4. Cauliflower. This unique vegetable makes a lovely substitution for the more traditional bean addition to the stew, adding a milkiness and good flavor.
5. Kale. Unlike delicate greens that will wither with heat, sturdy kale maintains its texture and helps stretch beef stew for more delicious servings.
