Rosa Parks, known as the "first lady of civil rights" became a NAACP activist in 1943, taking part in several high-profile civil rights campaigns. The event that she is most known for occurred on December 1st, 1955 when she rejected a bus driver's order to vacate a row of four seats in the colored section in favor of a white passenger once the white section was filled. While she was not the first person of color to reject bus segregation, the NAACP felt she was the best candidate for seeing through a court challenge following her arrest for civil disobedience of violating Alabama's segregation laws.
