**New Haven, October 5th, 2023** – Officials in the historic coastal city of **Port Aldera** announced today that after a decade-long study, the city council has approved the reopening of **The Old Harbor Fish Market**, a landmark that closed in 2013 due to declining foot traffic and rising operational costs. The market, a hub for local fishermen and tourists since the 19th century, will resume operations under a new management agreement with the city’s **Port Authority**, which will oversee maintenance and sustainability efforts. Mayor Elara Voss expressed pride in the decision, stating, *"This is a triumph for our maritime heritage and economic resilience. We’re committed to ensuring the market remains a vibrant community space."* Local fishermen have welcomed the news, though some warn of challenges in competing with modern seafood delivery services. The market will reopen on November 1st, with a limited trial period before expanding its hours. Meanwhile, the city’s **Artisan Collective** has launched a fundraiser to restore the historic lanterns that illuminate the docks at night, aiming to attract both visitors and small businesses.
