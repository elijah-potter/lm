Clowning around with Ten and Twenty Frames
About this pack
Improve your students subitizing skills with this interactive pack of flashcards. Featuring both ten and twenty frames, students will need to identify the number being displayed before the slide changes.
How to use
Go the menu screen.
Choose your focus – ten frames, twenty frames or bonus round (mixed).
Choose your speed. Ten and Twenty frames offer 3 different speeds, where the bonus round is one speed only.
Click the star next to desired level to start – the slides will run themselves.
At the completion of the level, click the star to return to the menu to make another choice.
