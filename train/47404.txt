WHAT IS BULLYING?
Bullying occurs when an individual or group repeatedly and intentionally causes hurt or harm to another person or group of people who are vulnerable.
IF YOU SEE BULLYING:
Whether you’re a witness or a victim of bullying, try your best to remember what happened, who was involved and how it made you feel.
Tell someone about it. If you’re not comfortable telling someone in person, report the incident via the LiveSafe app using the QR code below. This app can also be used to report concerns other than bullying.
