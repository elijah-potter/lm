There are a lot of things you can do while camping. Here are a few ideas:
- Campfire Cooking: Cooking over a campfire can be very challenging if you don't have any practice. As you will need to eat while camping, you can make it a family activity and tease each other about how poor or well you do. 
- Animal Spotting: Anywhere you camp is bound to have at least a few types of animals. You could limit yourselves to birds, or you can include everything from deer to frogs and then work together to figure out the exact name of the butterfly one of you saw.  If you want to make it a game, create a bingo card of various animals and see who gets bingo first.
- Hiking: Almost every camping spot has a few trails you can hike, some more so than others. 
- Outdoor Games: There are many outdoor games that can be especially fun once you have a large area like a park to play in. This includes things like capture the flag, hide and seek, etc. 
- Rainy Games: If you need to be in the tent, you can play games like charades or bring some cards. Or if it's later at night you can see who can create the scariest ghost story.
