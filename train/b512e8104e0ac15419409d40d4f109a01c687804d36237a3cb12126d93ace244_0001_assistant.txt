Despite having a pictorial appearance, hieroglyphics were utilized to represent sounds and concepts in elite contexts like tombs and temples. As more practical writing methods emerged, such as hieratic and demotic, hieroglyphics began to disappear. They were challenging to read due to lacking spaces and punctuation, and they required an understanding of ancient Egyptian grammar. Hieroglyphics could be written in various directions, including horizontally, vertically, left-to-right, and right-to-left.
