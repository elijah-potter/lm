An annotated bibliography is a list of citations to books, documents which were used for the research. After each citation it is necessary to give a brief descriptive paragraph of evaluative character. That is why writing an annotated bibliography means a more critical and careful reading of the source instead of a simple collection of information. It aims at informing the reader about the appropriateness and quality of the cited sources and is written in an alphabetic order. The usefulness of your bibliography depends on the selection of sources so do you best to make reasonable judgments within certain limits.
