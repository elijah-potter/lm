A fleeting gesture between strangers at a crowded café,
one whispered, *"I’ll find you again when the moon’s high,"*
while the other nodded—too polite to speak,
the promise dissolving like mist on dawn.

The night wore on; the café’s chatter grew,
until the door closed behind the one who’d said it.
Years passed, the moon’s cycles spun,
but the other never returned.
