There are 221 breeds that the UK Kennel Club officially recognizes.

Some of the dog breeds that are not recognized by the UK Kennel Club are the Longhair Weimaraner, the West Siberian Laika, the Rafeiro do Alentejo, Great Pyrenees and the Cimarrón Uruguayo.

Some of the dog breeds recognized by the American Kennel Club (AKC) but not recognized by the UK Kennel Club are the Mudi, Dogo Argentino, Norwegian Lundehund, and the Plott Hound.
