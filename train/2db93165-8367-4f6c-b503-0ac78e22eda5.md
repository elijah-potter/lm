**Scene 1: The Library – 1987**
The air hummed with the scent of aged paper and cold coffee. Elias adjusted his glasses as he flipped through a yellowed ledger, tracing numbers with a trembling finger. His mother’s voice echoed from the next table: *"You’re wasting your time, son."* He clenched his jaw, the pages trembling in his grip. A shadow fell across the desk—his father, watching through the window, arms crossed.

---

**Scene 2: The Present – A Rainy Night**
The neon glow of the café flickered. Mira traced the rim of her espresso cup, her fingers brushing the edge of the same ledger she’d inherited from her grandmother. A text notification flashed: *"You’re not the first."* Her phone buzzed again—an unknown number. The voice on the other end was familiar, but her heart raced. Outside, rain drummed against the windows. She exhaled, the scent of old books and coffee lingering on her breath.

---
**Scene 3: The Archive – Silent Vault**
The air was thick with dust. Elias’s fingers traced the worn cover of a book—*The Ledger of Echoes*—a relic of his family’s history. The pages glowed faintly, words shifting like smoke. His father stood in the doorway, hands in pockets. *"You’re either a fool or a ghost,"* he said. The book’s cover rippled, and the last line of the previous scene printed in ink: *"The past is a door, not a wall."* Elias’s breath hitched—he’d been here before.

---
**Scene 4: The Café – Twice-Blind**
Mira pressed her forehead to the window. The café was empty but for the hum of the AC. The same ledger sat open on the table—a mirror image of the one in the archive. She turned it over—inside, the same faded page. *"The past is a door, not a wall."* A doorbell chimed. The door creaked open. Elias stood there, his father’s face etched in shadows. *"You were here first,"* he said. Mira’s phone buzzed—same number, same message: *"You’re not the first."* She looked up. The café was gone. Only the ledger remained.
