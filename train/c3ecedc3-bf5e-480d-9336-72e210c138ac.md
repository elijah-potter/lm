A quiet evening in a dimly lit office, fluorescent humming overhead. The screen flickers with a half-finished report, the cursor blinking like a dying ember. You’ve spent the last three hours staring at the same line, each attempt to fix it yielding only frustration. Then—*a pause*. Not the sharp exhale of a victory, but the hesitant breath of someone who’s just realized they don’t *need* to know every detail right now. The doubt, that ever-present shadow, softens into something less like a weight than a key. The code, the idea, the entire project, suddenly feels less like a cage and more like a puzzle where the pieces *might* fit without all the rules you once insisted on. The screen clears, replaced by a single, unedited line: *"It’s okay."* And for the first time, it is.
