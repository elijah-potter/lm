Hello, my name is Adian and I'm a collector unlike any you've ever met. Instead of collecting trading cards or stamps, I have an exclusive collection of rare sounds. I go far and wide with my recording equipment to find unusual sounds that no one else in the sound collection community has. I've gone into the depths of the ocean to collect sounds like the call of a humpback whale, I've gone to the Alps to capture the sound of strong wind pushing thick clouds into a mountain, and I've carefully staked out fields to catch the whistle of a peregrine falcon shooting through the air at 200 miles an hour. 

Those are just some standouts from my nature sounds, which is only one category in my collection. I also collect ominous sounds, such as the implacable thumping that you hear in your walls at night. Don't ask me how I collected those! Industrial sounds are another genre full of all sorts of unique audio experiences. If you pay close attention to recordings of a forklift in operation, you can really tell whether or not the driver is forklift certified or not. There's a particularly satisfying recording I took of a factory in operation that I listen to at night to lull me to sleep like ASMR. I've considered making an online ASMR account to share my sounds, but they feel more special if I keep them to myself.
