The Leaning Tower of Pisa is located in Pisa, which is a region in west-central Italy. The tower is on the grounds of the city’s cathedral complex, known as the Campo dei Miracoli or the “Square of Miracles.”

It was constructed over 199 years, commencing in 1173, when the foundations of the tower were laid, and concluding in 1372, when the bell chamber was finally added.
