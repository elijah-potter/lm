The dim glow of a flickering streetlamp cast jagged shadows as the lone figure stepped off the overpass, clutching a crumpled map in their shaking hands. The distant wail of a siren, muffled by the wind, echoed through the hollow canyon. The path ahead was a labyrinth of gnarled trees, their branches clawing at the sky like skeletal fingers. Ahead, the faint outline of a distant village loomed—too close, too familiar—yet the map’s faded lines showed it was nowhere near. Their phone’s battery died mid-dial, leaving only the ghost of a text: *"Help me find the way."* The last memory they had was a city skyline, now replaced by this endless, silent expanse. The weight of the unknown pressed against their chest like a physical force.
