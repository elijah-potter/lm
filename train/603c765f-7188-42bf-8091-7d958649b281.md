A circular boundary of jagged stone, 200 meters in diameter, marked by a spiral of black iron rods that twist like serpents along its perimeter. Inside, the ground is a cracked, salt-stained plain where the only visible features are three crumbling brick towers aligned in a triangular formation, their peaks lost in mist. The sky above is a flat, overcast expanse of pale gray, punctuated by a single, jagged peak rising from the horizon—unnaturally tall, as if a mountain had been split and reassembled in an unnatural way. The air smells of damp earth and something metallic, like old blood. No sounds beyond the whisper of wind through the rods.
