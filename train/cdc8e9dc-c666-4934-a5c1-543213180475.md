The **Tacit Social Contract of "Always Say Yes to Coffee"**:
While the ritual of exchanging coffees fosters connection, the rule persists due to:
1. **Perceived Generosity** (assumed obligation to reciprocate).
2. **Social Pressure** (fear of offending if declined).
3. **Cultural Reinforcement** (repetition of the habit as "prosocial" default).

Avoiding it risks labeling as "selfish," even if the act carries negligible cost. The real rule: *Say yes if you mean it, decline if you don’t.* The logic is flawed—coffee is cheap, but kindness is currency.
