Whisper of damp earth,
rustling leaves in autumn’s last breeze—
crunch of fallen pine needles.
Beneath, the slow *drip-drip* of a fountain,
its water singing into the stone’s hollow.
Far away, the distant *clang* of a church bell,
echoing through the valley’s hollows.
Warm honeyed scent of roasted coffee,
mixed with the sharp tang of garlic from a nearby café.
Hints of damp wool, leather, and old books,
drift from a nearby bookstore’s open windows.
The air hums with the murmur of voices,
and the faint metallic scent of rain on metal.
