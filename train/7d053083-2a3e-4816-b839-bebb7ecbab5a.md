The library at dusk was the last place for quiet.

A book fell from the shelf—*The Alchemy of Memory*—a copy dusted with age. The spine cracked like an old promise. Inside, the pages smelled of forgotten tea and ink that had long since faded.

She found it under her pillow, as if the book had chosen her. She turned to the first line:

*"A door is not a place, but a way."*

She traced the words with her thumb, the ink still slightly damp. Outside, the city pulsed with unseen life—laughter, distant footsteps, the hum of a thousand conversations she couldn’t hear. But inside this book, the world was smaller, quieter. And for tonight, that was enough.
