There was a legend about the well in the garden.
It said that the best things happen to those that fall in.
You have to search to find its exact spot in the yard,
But once you find it, your life will no longer be hard. 

Mary was a widow who lived three doors down.
After Jerry passed, she could do nothing but frown.
But she fell into the well on a whim last May,
And was happily married to Tom last Saturday.

Jordan was a fellow who could no longer walk.
He heard about the well from Mary as the two talked.
His sons wheeled him around and tossed him right in,
Now he runs marathons with his legs strong and thin.

Little Sally had difficulties learning to read.
Her classmates could all read a book with great speed.
She jumped in the well and all became well,
All of the words she could read and spell.

Come here, come here, all who have need.
Let this idea quietly take seed.
For this magical well in this garden sits here
And will turn those smiles from your tears.
