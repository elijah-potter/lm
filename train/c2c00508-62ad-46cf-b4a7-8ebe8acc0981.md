**Scene 1 (Past):**
The old clock tower stood sentinel over the cobblestone streets, its hands frozen at 3:17. Elias wiped sweat from his brow as he adjusted the last bolt in the lock of the safe, the weight of the gold bars pressing down on him. His father’s voice echoed through the empty vault: *"You’ll never be enough."* Outside, the wind howled through the broken windows of the abandoned theater, where Maria sat cross-legged on the stage, sketching a forgotten playbill. A single candle flickered in her palm, casting long shadows of her unfulfilled dreams.

**Scene 2 (Present):**
A modern data center hummed under the fluorescent lights. Elias, now a junior analyst, pored over encrypted files, his fingers trembling as he decoded a pattern that matched the safe’s lock. Maria’s phone buzzed—an unknown number. She hesitated before answering, her voice barely audible: *"They’re looking for you."* Across the room, a shadowy figure in a high-tech suit watched the screen, their eyes locked on the same coordinates.

**Scene 3 (Future):**
The clock tower, now a digital relic, pulsed with holographic data. Elias stood before a wall of screens, his hands gripping the armrests as Maria’s ghostly silhouette appeared beside him. *"We were never enough,"* she whispered. The vault’s doors slid open—gold bars spilled onto the floor, merging with the digital code, forming a single, infinite loop. The wind carried no sound; only the echo of a promise: *to find what was lost, again.*
