- 07:30 - 08:00: Breakfast

**Midday:**
- 12:00 - 12:30: Lunch
- 12:30 - 13:00: Short walk/meditation

**Afternoon:**
- 14:00 - 16:00: Work (Deep Focus Block)
- 16:00 - 16:30: Snack break

**Evening:**
- 19:00 - 20:00: Dinner
- 20:00 - 21:00: Relax (Reading/Music)
- 21:00 - 22:00: Light study/prep for tomorrow

**Afternoon:**
- 12:00 - 14:00: Outdoor activity (Hiking, Sports)

**Evening:**
- 19:00 - 21:00: Dinner with family/friends
- 21:00 - Late: Socializing/Entertainment
