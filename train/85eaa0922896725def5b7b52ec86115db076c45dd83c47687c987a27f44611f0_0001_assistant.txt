One day, a yellow, fuzzy duck was waddling through the park around 2:00 PM. He had three thoughts on his mind - the sunshine, the four ducklings swimming in the pond ahead, and the five clouds hovering just over his head. At six years old, he was a seasoned swimmer, so he journeyed to the water to join his duckling friends. Once in the pond, which was seven feet deep, he swam eight laps. Before he knew it, the sun was beginning to set, so he swam back to land. There, he waited to watch the stars, which appeared at 9:00 PM. By 10:00 PM, he was sound asleep.
