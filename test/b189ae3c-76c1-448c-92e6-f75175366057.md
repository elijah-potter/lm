The **Volta Prize** for "World’s Most Ridiculous Invention" – awarded annually to the most bizarre, impractical, or nonsensical innovation that fails to solve any real-world problem but somehow garners attention from inventors and the media. Previous winners include:
- **"Self-Powered Toilet Plug"** (a toilet that claims to generate electricity from its own flushing, though it often just adds water).
- **"World’s Longest Braid"** (a hairpiece that won’t grow but looks like it should).
- **"Anti-Gravity Backpack"** (a device that, when held over a headstone, supposedly lifts the dead—it just adds weight to the coffin).
- **"The Sentient Pet Rock"** (a rock with a tiny solar-powered LED that blinks and occasionally chirps, but never speaks or learns).

The prize is often given to inventors who’ve spent years perfecting a joke, a curiosity, or a failed experiment that defies logic but somehow becomes a cultural phenomenon. Recipients are usually told, *"We didn’t say it was useful—just weird enough to make you laugh."* (Recipients often refuse, citing "the sheer audacity of the absurdity" or "the ethical concerns of promoting a product that makes people question reality.")
