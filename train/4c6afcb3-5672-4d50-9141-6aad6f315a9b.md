I was walking past the alley near the old factory when I heard it—the sound of someone screaming. It cut through the humid night air like a blade. The moment I turned, a figure in a torn coat stumbled toward me, clutching their arm. Blood soaked the fabric, and their face twisted in agony. They collapsed to the ground, gasping.

The crowd parted slowly. A police officer’s radio crackled to life. Sirens wailed in the distance. The screaming stopped. The alley went quiet. Somewhere, a dog barked. That’s when I noticed the blood on the pavement—too much, too fast. The body wasn’t moving. No one said anything. Just the hum of traffic and the distant echo of sirens.

Then I saw the fire truck pull up. The paramedics moved quickly, but I couldn’t bring myself to look away. The officer pressed something into my hand—a folded note with just one word scrawled in ink: *Help.*
