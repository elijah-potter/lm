It was finally Halloween! Toby rushed home from school to get ready. He put on his goblin costume, grabbed his ghost-shaped candy pail, and ran down the stairs yelling for his mother. He looked all over the house, but she wasn't there. He finally noticed the note sitting on the kitchen table. "Toby, I'm working a double. Please stay inside tonight, I'll see you in the morning. Love, Mom." Toby frowned. Stay inside? On Halloween? Not a chance! He ran out the front door and down the street. One by one he knocked on all the neighbor's doors, collecting treats and smiles. It was fully dark by the time he reached the last house on the street. The porch light was on but everything else was pitch black. It was a scary-looking house under the best of circumstances. But in the deep dark of Halloween night, it was downright terrifying. Toby wanted to go home, but he also didn't want anyone to find out he was a chicken. He took a deep breath and walked slowly up to the house, he lifted his small hand and knocked lightly twice. He didn't hear anything from inside. He turned to go and he heard the door creak open. He quickly turned back to see who was there. But no one was. It was just the door, now barely cracked open, and total darkness inside. Suddenly, a hand reached through the crack in the door and dropped a full-sized 3 musketeer bar into his bag before pulling back and closing the door. Toby stood frozen in fear before noticing the candy bars. Smiling, he started his walk home. He felt like he had been rewarded for his courage.
