**A floating city in the sky**, built atop a massive, reinforced airship anchored by anti-grav tech.
- **Law**: Written in holographic codes displayed in public plazas. Enforcers (cyborg judges) patrol in silent drones, scanning citizens’ neural signatures for compliance.
- **Customs**: Citizens must earn "sky-credits" by contributing to collective projects—think 3D-printing food, repairing airlocks, or mining rare minerals from the ship’s orbit. Theft of credits = exile to the lower decks.
- **Culture**: Art thrives in zero-gravity studios; poetry is judged by algorithmic crowdsourcing. The "Great Loop" is a mandatory 24-hour communal race across the ship’s exterior.
- **Economy**: Everything is traded via a blockchain embedded in the ship’s hull. Black markets for "ghost credits" (counterfeit tokens) persist in the shadow districts.

**Key Rule**: *"The sky belongs to the first to innovate."* Violators face "reboot fines"—their neural implants reset for a week.
