Here is a list of raspberries and their harvest periods for your garden:
1. Heritage Raspberry. Moderate yield in July and heaviest yield in September through frost.
2. Anne Raspberry. Moderate yield in July-August and again in September through frost.
3. Fall Gold Raspberry. Moderate yields in June and again in October to frost.
4. Polana Raspberry. Moderate yields in late June.
5. Prelude Raspberry. Moderate yields in mid-June and small yield in Fall.
