Silent calculus in the dark:
A neuron’s spike, a muscle’s twitch—
no syntax, no pause, no pause—
just a *yes* or *no* in the static hum
of the brain’s unspoken code.

A fork in the pulse: no blood, no breath,
only the weight of the next beat.
No language, but the answer’s still there—
*strike* or *hold*, the body’s own prayer.
