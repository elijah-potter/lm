On a farm far away, in a land full of green,
Lives a little girl, with a heart pure and clean.
With her elder brother, she spends her day,
Taking care of the livestock in every way.

With the rooster's crow, she wakes up each dawn,
And steps out to the farm, with a smile like a fawn.
The smell of fresh hay, brewing coffee and pies,
Fits the morning air, like a mirthful disguise.

Together they feed the cows, the sheep and the goats,
As they run to the barn, like clowns without boats.
With laughter and joy, amidst the hay bales,
The little girl finds comfort in the animals' tales.

As she fills up their troughs with feed and water,
She listens to them chew and munch, as if in banter.
She cuddles with her brother as the horses they groom,
To her, all these animals seem like family in bloom.

With barks and meows, the farm dogs and cats,
Chase around the chickens, the ducklings, and the rats.
With the sun setting down, their chores are all done,
And they retreat to the house, with a skip and fun.

And as her mother hugs and kisses her goodnight,
The little girl drifts off to the dreamland of delight,
Where the animals she loves, gather around her bed,
And she listens to their lullabies, as she rests her head.
