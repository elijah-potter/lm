How to Make Marinated Veggie Skewers

Tofu, drained and cut,
Square chunks, delicious 1.5 inches.
Marinade ingredients, the soul of the tofu,
Soaked overnight, combining flavor.
Wooden skewers bathed in water,
Preventing combustion.
Veggies sliced, well-prepared, along with tofu,
Pierced.
Cooked in fire, ready to eat.
