A small kindness as a trigger: Every morning, leave a crumb on the café table for the next customer, unasked, to find.

The ripple effect:
1. The café owner notes the habit and later slips a free coffee to a regular who’s struggling.
2. That person, moved by the gesture, buys coffee for a neighbor who’s elderly and forgets their own.
3. The neighbor, now indebted, writes a thank-you note to the café’s staff, who pass it along to a young volunteer who starts organizing free meals for local shelters.
4. The volunteer’s postcard arrives at the café, printed with the shelter’s logo, pinned to the register. The owner’s daughter, seeing it, starts a book donation drive for the shelter’s kids.

The chain: A single gesture—kindness as seed—grows into a community’s hidden network of support. No grand design, just intention.
