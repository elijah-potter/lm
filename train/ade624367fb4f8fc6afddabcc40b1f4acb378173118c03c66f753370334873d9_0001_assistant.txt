Honey, when stored correctly and not contaminated, will not spoil. Comprising 80% sugar and 18% water, honey possesses a low water content, creating an unfavorable environment for bacteria or mold to thrive. Furthermore, due to its thick consistency, honey effectively hinders the penetration of oxygen, preventing bacteria from surviving. In 2003, during oil pipeline construction in the country of Georgia, the world's oldest honey, estimated to be around 5,500 years old, was discovered.
