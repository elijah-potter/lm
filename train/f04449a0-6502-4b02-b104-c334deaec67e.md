The storm came without warning, its thunder shaking the ancient oak’s roots as it groaned in the wind. A crack split the bark where a crow had once perched—now silent, its feathers ruffled by unseen hands.

The first message was in blood-red ink on a cracked mirror: *"They are here."* The ink blurred when touched, as if alive, but no one dared follow the whispers. The elders said the warning was for those who *listened*. The living ignored it. The dead remembered.

By midnight, the sky split open like a wound, and the trees began to scream. The air smelled of damp earth and something older—something that had been buried too long. The villagers woke screaming, but no one could explain why.

Then, the river turned black. The water did not reflect the stars. It *breathed*.
