I wasn’t always this way. They made me this way. I used to have everything.

Picture me—a hard worker. Family man with three kids. I’m short but have heart. It’s 2009, and we’re good. Bought a house for cheap. Took advantage of the recession. It was the best timing. Job was paying well, too. Overtime often, but worth it. Crappy management—kept my distance. But it was all okay. We could pay for everything. Even some fancy lobster dinners. Fine dining and great living. Big house and fancy cars.

Well, things started to change. Car payments higher than expected. Big issue with house plumbing. Thousands down the drain, quick. Worked harder to overcome it. Big surgery on middle child. Scary heart condition came up. Weeks in the hospital spent. Sharp pain in the wallet! Stress arose and worry settled.

Working more to make up. Now around 90 hours weekly. My Saturdays are solid gone. Grinding from morning to night. Burnout is numbing my mind. No time for fun, either. Feeling joy less often, sadly. This isn’t working; let’s restart.

Tried to sell the cars. Couldn’t get pennies for them. Massive recall malfunction issues associated. Another big house concern, wow. You wouldn’t believe it—$12,000. Air conditioning unit went kaput. Financial stress is sinking me. Deeper in debt every day. Defaulted on my mortgage payment. Paid things with credit cards. Owed even more months later. I’m working hard and drowning.

Job wouldn’t give me enough. Asked for things and rejected. Tried to interview everywhere else. Boss found out about interviews. Let me go, no warning. Pleaded for a proper return. I’m mad and near bankruptcy. Wife is definitely leaving soon. Kids can’t stand being around. Blood pressure off the charts. I’m already at rock bottom. Nothing to lose but time.

I’ll pay my work respects. Infestation of bugs, boss’s office. Well-hidden chemicals never ceasing. Stink bombs and other nuisances. Perhaps an accidental fire, too. Oh, sweet revenge for them. What goes around comes around. They ruined my life, see? Time to make them suffer.
