If you have an old garden bench that looks a little worn out, consider giving it new life with strips of an old garden hose! This simple and eco-friendly project will not only make your bench look brand new but also help you repurpose an old item in a creative and useful way.

Materials needed:
-Strips of an old garden hose
-Utility knife or scissors
-Staple gun
-Measuring tape

Instructions:
-Remove the old fabric from the bench. If it's stapled on, use a staple remover to carefully pry out the staples.
-Measure the length and width of the bench to determine how long to cut your hose strips.
-Use a utility knife or scissors to cut strips of garden hose to your desired length.
-Starting at one end of the seat, stretch the longer hose strips lengthwise to the other side and staple them in place on the underside of the bench.
-Weave the shorter hose strips over and under the longer pieces to create a lattice pattern.
-Continue weaving the hose strips back and forth until the entire seat is covered.
-Trim any excess hose, then staple the loose ends to the underside of the bench to secure them in place.
-Turn the bench over and enjoy your new, stylish, and eco-friendly garden bench!

By reupholstering your garden bench with strips of an old garden hose, you can give it a fresh look while also repurposing items that you might have otherwise discarded.
