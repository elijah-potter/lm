A minor adjustment to a script’s error-handling threshold—from `404` to `500`—left undetected by the deployment pipeline. The change meant fewer false alerts for 404s but introduced a silent failure for 5xx errors, causing a critical edge case to slip through. The team assumed the new threshold fixed a previous bug, yet the new issue—where 500s now triggered a cascading rollback—wasn’t tested until weeks later, after production incidents escalated. Only a postmortem audit revealed the oversight, by then already a months-long blind spot in the system’s resilience. The lesson: small, seemingly safe tweaks often hide unseen dependencies in complex workflows.
