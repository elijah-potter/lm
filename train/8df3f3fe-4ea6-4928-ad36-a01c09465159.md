The room was dimly lit, the air thick with unspoken words and lingering silence. A man sat across from a woman, their hands clasped between them, fingers intertwined like loose knots.

*"Do you ever think about what happens next?"* she asked, voice soft but steady.

*"I do,"* he replied, eyes reflecting the muted glow of the single candle flickering between them. *"Every day. But I don’t know how to stop."*

She exhaled, a sigh that carried the weight of unsaid confessions. *"I’m afraid of not knowing either."*

The silence stretched, a thread of tension between them, neither willing to break first. Outside, the city hummed—a distant, unrelenting rhythm—while inside, their conversation became a loop, a cycle without end.

*"Do you ever wonder if we’re stuck in this forever?"* she whispered.

*"Or if we’re already there,"* he murmured, his gaze fixed on the candle’s flicker. *"Maybe we’ve been trapped since the first moment we met."*
