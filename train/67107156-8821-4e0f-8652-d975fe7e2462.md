A fleeting word, whispered under breath,
*"I’ll meet you at the old docks—just this once."*
Sunlight flickered on the rippled water,
her laughter, the tide’s relentless claim.

No contract, no date carved in stone,
just the weight of a hand held too long.
Time blurred between the spaces in between,
promises dissolve like mist on the wind.

The docks stood empty—no sign of her.
The river carried the words away.
A ghost of what might’ve been,
still humming in the hollow of the air.
