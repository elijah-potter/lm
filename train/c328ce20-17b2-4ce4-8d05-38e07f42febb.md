Truth revealed in quiet moments: A stranger’s kindness—an elderly woman at the bus stop shared her pocket change with a child’s hungry hands, then paused to glance at her watch. *"I can’t stay,"* she said, but the boy’s eyes lingered on her face as she spoke. That pause became the truth she never spoke aloud: how she’d failed to return his lost toy. The boy didn’t ask for answers; he just stood there, watching her leave, until the bus rolled away. The weight of honesty was less than the weight of silence.
