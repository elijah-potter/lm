The top five words that best describe me are:
-Utmost
-Hungry
-Open-minded
-Honest
-Balanced.

One of the most useful life lessons I live by was taught by my mother. She would always repeat to me to do my absolute best in everything I do. She would make it a point that although regrets in life are going to be inevitable, those who do their utmost each day can turn those regrets into opportunities. This lesson of doing the best you could in everything you do has always been with me and carried me through darker times throughout my life. I have a dream of working at a hedge fund, but it is not the dream that pulls me. I have a fear of being mediocre, but that is not what pushes or motivates me. As someone who was born and raised in a single-family household, I did not have a father nor any father-figure substitute and therefore had to learn how to fill some of the gaps in my upbringing. For example, I had to teach myself how to tie a tie for my first job interview or teach myself that black dress socks are usually less appropriate when paired with navy dress pants. I seize each day because I am hungry to become the best version of myself. Nevertheless, I maintain an open mind for opportunities and to be accepting of other people with differences. I also maintain an open mind inward toward myself, self-reflecting and constantly maturing my dreams of working at a hedge fund. Being both outwardly and inwardly open-minded has opened more doors for me, and has consistently translated into some important successes for me in the past. However, the most important standard I hold myself to is maintaining a clean conscience. Personally, this means that I am honest in the most doubtful of times and particularly when it is not in my best self-interest to do so. I am nothing if I am anything but honest. Against the backdrop of everything I elaborated about myself, more importantly, I strive to live a balanced life. Despite being focused on my career, I maintain a healthy balance between friends, family, work and education. I also maintain an internal balance, between logical thinking versus creative activities. This lifestyle grounds me in reality and keeps me happy.
