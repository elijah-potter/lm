A midday patrol of the city’s newly installed automated surveillance drones observed a street vendor’s cart—an unlicensed, handmade stall selling fresh squeezed lemonade—uninterrupted by police, despite the vendor’s unregistered operation. The drone’s AI flagged the vendor’s lack of permits but chose to flag only the lack of a *permanent* license, not the fact that the vendor’s cart had been approved for temporary outdoor sales under a municipal "pop-up vendor" permit. The drone’s algorithm prioritized compliance with "formal" regulations over enforcement of "informal" ones, leaving the vendor’s livelihood intact. The vendor’s neighbor, a retired cop, smirked as the drone’s camera zoomed past, unmarked by any enforcement action—just a glitch in the system where rules were bent, not broken.
