You should consider answering these key questions:
- Has the crime been solved?
- If the crime has been solved, who was the perpetrator?
- Who are the victims of the crime?
- What were the events leading up to the crime?
- What did news reports say about the crime?
- What problems did law enforcement face when trying to solve the crime?
- Who were the suspects in the crime, if any?
- What were the clues left at the crime scene?
- What leads did law enforcement follow?
- Which members of law enforcement were involved?
- Where did the crime take place?
- What are some potential motivations for the crime?
- What have people involved in the crime said about it?
- Were there any witnesses to the crime and, if so, who are they?
- When did this crime occur?
- What was the crime committed?
- Where there more than one crime involved?
- Did the perpetrator have a history of crime, if known?
- Did the victim or victims have a criminal past?
- If the perpetrator was caught, when and how were they convicted?
