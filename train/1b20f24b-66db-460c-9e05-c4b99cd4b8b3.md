The river sang a lullaby of silver and gold,
*whispering* secrets beneath the moon’s pale glow.
The reeds, in rhythm, danced with the tide’s soft tread,
*echoing* echoes lost in time’s slow, endless flow.

The old clock stood still, its hands frozen in despair,
*counting* seconds like stars in a dying year.
A child’s laughter played with shadows on the wall,
*fading* fast where light could not reach it all.

*"Run,"* the wind screamed, *"run before the storm’s wrath,"*
*but the storm only hummed a song of death’s embrace.*
The trees bowed low, their branches intertwined,
*forming* bridges where the lost souls would find.

*"We are the echoes,"* whispered the stones beneath the feet,
*stories* carved in dust where time could not retreat.
The world spun on, indifferent, cold, and cruel,
*yet in silence, love still found its way through.*
