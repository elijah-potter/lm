Looking for your next spring outfit? Take a note from the cowboys of years past. The "coastal cowgirl" look blends the rugged feel of the wild wild West with the easy-breezy energy of coastal beach style. It may sound like finding a middle ground between these two styles is impossible, but it can be as easy as your favorite pair of denim cutoffs, your boyfriend's button-down, and that pair of white cowboy boots that you bought on a whim last summer.
