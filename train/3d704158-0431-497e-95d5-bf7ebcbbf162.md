In the bustling, coastal metropolis of **Port Veyra**, residents were left stunned by the sudden disappearance of over 150 commuters who left their offices early yesterday. Reports indicate that employees of **Veyra Maritime Logistics**, a key port operations company, began exiting their buildings at 03:17 AM without explanation. Security footage from the nearby **Tidehaven Terminal** shows employees walking rapidly toward the harbor, their faces obscured by mist. Police confirmed the area was cordoned off, and emergency services were dispatched to investigate. Authorities have not yet released details on the cause but warn against approaching the area. The Port Veyra City Council is reviewing the incident, urging residents to remain vigilant. Meanwhile, whispers of an "unexplained maritime anomaly" have surfaced among sailors and fishermen, though no credible evidence has been provided. The government is investigating potential security breaches, but no official statement has been issued beyond the evacuation notice.
