Sure thing! Here are some different side dishes you could incorporate at your next summer BBQ:

- Creamy Mexican Corn Salad: Instead of traditional corn on the cob, you could try this zesty twist.
- Bacon Macaroni Salad: Macaroni or pasta salad is a typical side dish at many BBQs, but this version adds herbs and bacon. 
- Red Cabbage Slaw: Instead of the same old coleslaw recipe, think about this healthy version. It has red cabbage, carrots, and jalapeno tossed in a spicy-sweet dressing.
- Chili Lime Grilled Pineapples: Rub spears of pineapple with a lime and Indian street-style mix before grilling for a tasty and unique side dish. 
- Cauliflower Salad: This is an alternative to pasta salad that's also low carb! It's simple to make with bite-sized pieces of cauliflower, olives, red onion, roasted peppers, and a zesty vinaigrette. 
- BBQ Sticky Rice: Instead of baked beans, how about sticky rice? You can even make it in your slow cooker and kick up the southern heat with your choice of sweet or spicy BBQ sauce.
