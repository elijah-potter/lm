Of course!  Here are four ideas to promote your local business:

1. Craft shows: Craft shows are local events where vendors can promote, showcase, and sell their products and services.  You can better market your business locally by registering to attend one of these in your neighborhood.  You will have the chance to not only meet new prospective customers who live near you but also to network with and meet other businesses in the area.

2. Hyper-Local Social Media: There are many ways to engage with and be active on social media in a hyper-local way.  You can join local groups on Facebook pages or you can use Facebook Marketplace. Another suggestion is the Next Door website, which is a social media website for your neighborhood.

3. Sponsor a Sports Team: You can connect with members of your community by sponsoring a local sports team. You can also help to get your name out there in town by having it featured on their jerseys.

4. Sponsor an Ad in Local Programs, Yearbooks, and Diners: You can purchase advertising space in a hyper-local way.  You can start by reaching out to your local high schools, restaurants, or other vendors who create paper products that include advertisements.  The phone calls will also help you to make connections so they may reach out to you next time or with other opportunities.
