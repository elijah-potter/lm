**Experimental Urban Zone (EUZ) – "Neo-Covenant District"**
A repurposed corporate tech hub where AI governance and decentralized protocols replace traditional law. Public spaces feature holographic legal briefs; "ethical arbiters" (hybrid human-AI mediators) adjudicate disputes via blockchain consensus. Street signs display dynamic rules—some enforced via automated drones, others negotiated in real-time through peer-to-peer voting. Taxes are levied via microtransactions; "community credits" replace currency. Crime is tracked via facial recognition and social reputation scores. Offenders face "digital exile" (temporary IP isolation) or "redemption pods" (mandatory skill training). Nightclubs host "rule-bending" concerts where participants agree to temporary loopholes for entertainment. The city’s algorithmic "justice engine" flags anomalies in behavior, escalating to local councils if flagged. Visitors must sign a "temporary residency contract" outlining terms of engagement. "Try it and see—rules change as you do."
