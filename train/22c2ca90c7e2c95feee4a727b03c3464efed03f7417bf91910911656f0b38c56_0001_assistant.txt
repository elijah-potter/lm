1. Chucky, from the Child's Play franchise. This doll containing the soul of fictional serial killer Charles Lee Ray has been around since the 80s and has since become one of the most recognizable characters in the horror genre, appearing in at least 8 films and a TV series. 
2. Annabelle, from The Conjuring universe. Based on an alleged real-life haunting studied by Ed and Lorraine Warren, this doll was the breakout star of the Conjuring film series, later starring in three additional movies, all of which became some of the highest-grossing horror films of the 21st century. 
3. Slappy the Dummy, from Goosebumps. Slappy is arguably the most memorable figure from RL Stine's horror novels for young readers, as well as their accompanying adaptations. He is the antagonist of the Night of the Living Dummy saga as well as the official mascot of the Goosebumps franchise as a whole.
4. Talky Tina, from The Twilight Zone. This doll and the episode in which she first appeared are often cited as the birth of "doll horror" and likely had an impact on many of the other haunted doll stories that came after. 
5. Brahms, from The Boy. While most doll horror tends to frighten audiences by having dolls move around and perform unnatural tasks, this film creates an ominous tone through the characters' insistence that the doll, Brahms, is alive despite the visual evidence to the contrary.
