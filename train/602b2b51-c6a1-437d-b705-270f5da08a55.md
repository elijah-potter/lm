A standard entry door, typically metal or wood, equipped with hinges, a handle, and a lock mechanism. It is usually framed by a door frame, often reinforced for structural integrity. This door separates the interior of a building (e.g., residential home, office) from the exterior environment (e.g., street, garden, yard), controlling access while providing insulation against weather conditions. It may also serve to block unwanted noise, dust, or intruders.
