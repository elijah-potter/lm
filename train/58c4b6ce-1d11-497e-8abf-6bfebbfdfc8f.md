The rain had been falling for hours, turning the cobblestones into rivers that refused to flow. I stood on the edge of the old bridge, my boots sinking into the mud, my fingers biting into the cold stone railing. The city below was swallowed by mist, the neon signs flickering like dying stars.

I had come here hoping for answers. But the city had other plans.

A figure emerged from the shadows—a woman with eyes like polished ice. She didn’t speak at first. Just stood there, watching me like I was something she’d never seen before. Then she said, *"You’re not alone."*

My knees buckled. I’d been alone for too long.

She reached out, and the moment her fingers brushed mine, the world shifted. The rain stopped. The mist cleared. And for the first time in years, I felt something I hadn’t in months—**hope**.

But it wasn’t enough.

*"Tell me what you need,"* she said, voice like wind through the ruins. *"Or do you even know?"*

I swallowed. The truth was heavier than the rain.

She tilted her head. *"Then let’s start there."*
