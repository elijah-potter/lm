Phthalocyanines, the most important chromogens developed in the 20th century, were introduced in 1934. They are analogs of two natural porphyrins: chlorophyll and hemoglobin. Phthalocyanine was discovered in 1907 and its copper salt in 1927, but their potential as colorants was not immediately recognized.
