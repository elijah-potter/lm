1. Host a "favorite things" party where guests bring duplicates of a product or item that they have loved using the last few months. Everyone exchanges items and leaves with several new items recommended by friends! 
2. Set up a tea-party dinner and ask guests to dress up like they're having tea with the Queen! Serve light refreshments and tea sandwiches, lots of vegetables and fruits, and, of course, tea!
3. Have a board game-themed night. Serve each course of food with a board game, and match the food to the game. Play each game as you enjoy the course.
