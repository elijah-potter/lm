The title of the episode could be "Michael's Pursuit of Holly," and the main premise is that Michael Scott is determined to win back his former love interest, Holly Flax, who has recently returned to Scranton. 

The episode can begin with Michael hearing the news that Holly is returning to Dunder Mifflin. He goes inside his office with Dwight and Jim to come up with a plan to impress her. Jim expresses his skepticism about Michael's chances, as Holly is already in a committed relationship. Michael dismisses this and continues to think of a plan to win her over. 

A few days later, Holly enters Dunder Mifflin. Michael has been waiting behind Pam's desk. When Holly walks up to Pam, Michael jumps out from behind her and yells, "Suprise, Holly!" Holly screams and drops everything she was holding. The entire office turns their attention to the commotion, bewildered by Michael's dramatic episode. Holly, clearly startled and flustered, manages to compose herself and greets Michael with a forced smile.
