No problem! Here are some hairstyles that work well with natural hair:

● Curls - Roller set your hair using flexi rods or perm rods. Be sure to make sure the hair is damp before applying the roller.
● Braids or twists - Install some braid styles or twist styles. These styles tend to take a longer time to do, but the results will also last longer.
● Ponytails - Options for ponytails are endless. You can do a high ponytail or a low ponytail. If you want a straight ponytail, make sure you blow dry and flat iron your hair first.
