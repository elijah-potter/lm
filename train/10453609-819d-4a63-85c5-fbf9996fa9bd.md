The neon glow of the abandoned warehouse flickers against the rain-streaked windows, casting long shadows across the warped floor. Inside, a lone figure hunches over a rusted desk, fingers tracing over an old ledger. The hum of the dying fluorescent lights feels like a pulse—too slow, too familiar. Outside, distant sirens wail, their echo swallowed by the wind. A key turns in the lock, the only sound breaking the tension. The door creaks open just enough to let in a gust of damp air. The figure doesn’t rise; their breath fogs the screen behind them. A single cigarette butt glows between their fingers.
