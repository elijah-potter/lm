I have all the parts laid out on the table. It has been a while, but I can finally start building my computer. I have been working a lot the past few weeks, motivated to get a new computer to play all the hottest new games and maybe start editing some content. Now, all the parts are laid out in front of me. The last thing that I have to do is put it all together. 

I start to sort through what I need. I unpackage the motherboard and place it on top of its box. Next, I cut the RAM out of its hard packaging. I put those into their slot in the motherboard. It was satisfying as I hear the light "click" sound after every piece.
Click.
Click.
Click.
Next, I carefully unwrap the CPU from its package and start to place it into the middle of the motherboard. It's weird. It feels like I'm doing brain surgery, installing a new brain into the fresh, new body called a motherboard as I install the CPU carefully and click the cover over, locking it in place. I take the thermal paste that I bought and slowly apply a little bit to the top of the CPU. Each blob is spaced out as far from each other as possible. After that, I grab a large box that contains a clean, new air cooler. The feeling never gets old as I unwrap the cooler and unbox it, revealing a silver champion. I take the cooler and slowly install it on top of the CPU. Below, the thermal paste slowly spreads throughout the top of the CPU and completely covers it. The last thing that I have to do for this part is install the SSD. These days, memory has gotten so small and so advanced that it completely skips a step. I take the 1TB SSD from the packaging and place it in its rightful place on the motherboard. I grab my smallest screwdriver, and with every spin, I feel that I am that much closer to completing this build. I start to sweat, which is weird because this is the easiest part of the build. With that, I can move on to the case.
