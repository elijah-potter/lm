**Mayor’s Office Announces New Economic Incentives for Tech Startups in **Neonville****
Neonville, May 10 – The City Council has approved a sweeping package of tax breaks, grants, and accelerated permitting to lure tech startups to **Neonville**, aiming to turn the city’s aging infrastructure into a hub for innovation. Under the new **Neonville Tech Accelerator Initiative (NTAI)**, qualifying firms can receive up to **$500,000 in seed funding** over three years, along with **10-year property tax exemptions** and expedited zoning approvals for research facilities. Mayor Elias Voss highlighted the move as a response to rising competition from **Silicon Valley and Austin**, emphasizing that Neonville’s "unique blend of affordable living and historic downtown charm" could position it as the next frontier for AI and biotech.

Critics warn of long-term debt risks, but city planners argue that the initiative will create **1,500+ jobs** within five years. The first wave of applications is set for **June 15**, with priority given to companies developing **green energy solutions and quantum computing**. Councilwoman Lila Chen noted, *"This isn’t just about jobs—it’s about making Neonville a thought leader in sustainability."* The full ordinance, including revenue projections, will be debated next week.
