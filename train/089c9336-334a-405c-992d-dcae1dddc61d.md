**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 21, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this year, promising a vibrant celebration of agriculture, community, and family entertainment. This year’s fair will showcase livestock exhibits, agricultural displays, artisan crafts, and a diverse array of amusement rides and games, offering something for attendees of all ages. Traditional fair favorites, such as the demolition derby and the livestock judging competitions, will return alongside new attractions designed to enhance the overall experience.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from Friday, August 30th, through Sunday, September 8th, 2024. The fairgrounds will be open daily from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Special events are scheduled throughout the duration of the fair, including nightly concerts and fireworks displays. A detailed schedule of events will be available on the fair’s official website beginning August 15th.

Admission to the Sangamon County Fair is $15 for adults, $10 for children ages 6-12, and free for children five and under. Advance tickets are available for purchase online at [Fair Website Address] and at select local retailers. Parking is $5 per vehicle.

**Headline Events:**

*   Friday Night Concert: The Heartland Ramblers
*   Saturday Evening Demolition Derby
*   Sunday Livestock Auction
*   Tuesday Night Tractor Pull
*   Wednesday Evening Rodeo
*   Saturday Fireworks Display
