Attention Board of Tourism:

There is a danger that needs to be addressed, and your institution is the one capable of making a difference.

For years, locals have commuted to and from work via our beautiful mountain highways. Recently, a plague has infected our scenic roadways and caused, at a bare minimum, frustration among our residents. This plague has been brought onto our neighbors by way of tourists operating their automobiles at speeds so slow, they become hazards.

While our community is well familiar with the twists and turns of the steep mountain grades, our visitors are not. Without seeming ungrateful for the prosperity delivered to us by way of these tourists, it is necessary that someone asks for a change.

You have the power to do so. Our suggestion is similar to existing “Keep Right Unless Passing” laws, except enforced legislation could encourage the dangerous slower traffic to utilize the many pull-offs built along our mountain highways. Some of these vehicles travel at a speed so low that our residents come upon them dangerously fast around blind curves—even when traveling below the posted speed limits.

Please consider taking this approach to improve your community’s quality of life.
