A rare celestial event—a meteor shower—aligned with a delayed train schedule, forcing an unexpected stop in a small town where the traveler found an abandoned workshop. Inside, an old machine hummed with forgotten purpose, and the moment they pressed the start button, it began to emit a steady, rhythmic vibration that matched the frequency of their heartbeats. By coincidence, the workshop’s owner, a retired physicist, had been researching quantum anomalies in this very frequency range. Within days, their research led to a breakthrough in understanding the phenomenon, rewriting their entire academic trajectory.

The coincidence wasn’t luck—it was a convergence of timing, chance, and an unspoken thread connecting their lives.
