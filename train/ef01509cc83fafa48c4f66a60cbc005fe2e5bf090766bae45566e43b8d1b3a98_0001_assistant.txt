Here are some good productivity books I recommend you read:

- Atomic Habits - James Clear's An Easy & Proven Method to Develop Good Habits & Break Negative Habits.

- The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People - This timeless classic offers a framework for personal and professional effectiveness by Stephen Covey.

- Getting Things Done: The Art of Stress-Free Productivity - This book is a productivity classic that offers a thorough approach to managing your time and responsibilities by David Allen.

- Essentialism: The Disciplined Pursuit of Less - According to this book, the key to productivity is to focus on the most important things while eliminating the rest by Greg McKeown.
