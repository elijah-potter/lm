**City of Solara, October 12th, 2023** – Officials in Solara are investigating reports of a mysterious "echo phenomenon" plaguing the downtown district, where residents claim to hear faint, distorted voices repeating conversations from the past. Residents describe the occurrences as disorienting, with some describing the sounds as coming from the sky, others from underground tunnels beneath the city’s old markets. The city’s emergency services have been dispatched to a series of reports of unexplained disturbances, particularly around the clockwork clock tower near the Grand Plaza, where locals say the phenomenon is most concentrated. Authorities have cordoned off the area and are coordinating with the local historian’s guild to collect eyewitness accounts and historical records for analysis. The phenomenon has not been linked to any known natural or technological events, prompting speculation of paranormal activity or an undocumented municipal experiment. The city council is considering a temporary curfew for downtown businesses in affected areas.

**Update:** Early tests using electromagnetic scanners near the clock tower have detected no anomalous energy readings, but officials remain cautious. A team of archaeologists has begun excavating the underground tunnels near the market district, where reports suggest the voices originate from deeper layers of the city’s construction. No immediate cause has been determined.
