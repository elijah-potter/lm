**Thought Gardens of Eldermere**
A mist-laden valley at the edge of the Whispering Woods, where ancient stone benches overlook the Blackthorn River. The air hums with cicadas and the distant murmur of scholars. Walls of moss-covered bookshelves curve around the courtyard, their pages whispering if you press your ear close enough. The central pavilion’s dome is carved with constellations, and the scent of aged parchment and pine lingers. Here, lanterns glow faintly, casting shadows of half-finished arguments on the cobblestones. The library’s silent whispers are the only sound—until someone speaks. Then, the wind carries their thoughts in a current you can almost taste.
