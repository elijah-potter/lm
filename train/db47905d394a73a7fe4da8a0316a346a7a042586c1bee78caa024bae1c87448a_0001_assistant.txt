1. Hello God, why are you here today? Do you have anything you want to tell people to get off your chest?
2. There seems to be a disconnect between what you tell people through your Word (i.e., the Bible) and the events that have occurred in our world since receiving the Word. Examples of this include the many wars that men have fought over the years, people dying of famine and genocide, and seemingly good people suffering while seemingly evil people thrive. Why should people believe in the Word?
3. It seems as if the world were created as some sort of "grand experiment" where you have given people the choice to decide their fates while also stressing the importance of being obedient to you. Question: Do humans have the choice to do the right thing, or are they led to do the right thing by your unseen ways?
4. (Follow-up question if the answer to No. 3 is they have the choice to do the right thing) If this is the case, why give humans the choice at all? I mean, if the good choice is always the most pleasant one, why offer a "bad" choice that results in human suffering?
5. (Follow-up question if the answer to No. 3 is they are led to do the right thing by unseen ways) So does this mean that everything about human existence is pre-determined by you and that life is somewhat like a grand tour of all the amazing sights and sounds you've created that you wanted to show off to your human creations? Does this mean humans are your captive audience?
6. What is the point of all this? What is the point of human existence? Is this a play, and humans are just actors on a stage for your enjoyment? Or is there some greater purpose you have in mind? If so, please share it with the audience.
7. Is there anything you think you could provide to our viewers at home that would explain who you are and what your purpose is better to them, so they may act accordingly?
