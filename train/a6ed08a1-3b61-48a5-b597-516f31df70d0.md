The office door swung open with a *click*, and the tardy employee stepped inside, a bag of half-eaten donuts in one hand and a scowl on his face. His watch showed 3:02 PM—exactly five minutes after the mandatory 3 PM start. His boss stood at the meeting table, already reviewing spreadsheets, arms crossed.

Relief flooded him as he spotted the empty seat next to the boss’s chair. His mind raced: *"I made it."* The silence stretched, broken only by the hum of the fluorescent lights and the faint *drip* from the broken faucet in the break room. He exhaled sharply, fingers drumming on the table.

The boss glanced up, unamused. "Late again?" The words hung in the air like a curse. But the relief was still there, thick in his chest—just enough to make him grit his teeth. He forced a nod. "Yeah. Traffic."

The boss didn’t look convinced.
