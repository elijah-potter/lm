Aerospace engineers or dogs? A laboratory recently employed two dogs, a French bulldog and a pitbull, that are building an airplane together. Through a series of blueprints, schematics, and drawings, the dogs have been working day and night to become the first dog duo to build a fully functional airplane. The French bulldog has been working on assembling the motor and putting it in the frame of the plane. The pitbull built the frame of the airplane and is currently working on the electronics that will go in the cockpit. Needless to say, this amazing team is doing something that has never been done before, leaving many to wonder what is possible in the future.
