Many homeowners don’t realize the impact that air vents can have on their health. When air duct cleaning in your home aren’t facilitated for long periods of time, this can result in allergy and asthma. While cleaning your vents isn’t as exciting as going on vacation, it can prevent a number of adverse health conditions. In truth, air vents are easy to ignore, as they are not the most visible part of the house, and it can take months or years for them to accumulate enough dirt to cause problems. However, once the problems start, they can be severe, and cleaning vents which haven’t been touched in years can be tedious.
As time passes, vents will collect dirt, dust and grime. Spiders often select these locations to build webs. The air that passes through the vent collects all these things, and then transmits them all over the house. If you or someone in your household suffers from asthma, this can make the condition worse. A vent that is blocked defeats the purpose of why it was installed in the first place, which is to circulate fresh air around the home in order to provide ventilation.
Sometimes mold appears on air ducts. When this happens the spores will be spread around the house, and anyone who is allergic to these spores will be affected by them. Many allergies don’t have specific symptoms, so it can be difficult to pinpoint the problem. If you or someone in your household is complaining of allergies, before visiting the doctor you should inspect your air vents.
Clean, fresh air is a necessity for virtually all living things, but for those who suffer from asthma it is even more important. This lung disease causes a reduction in the width of the air pipes, which creates swelling. As a result, people who have asthma have breathing difficulties, and may have asthma attacks if they are exposed to spores, pollen, dirt or cigarette smoke. Someone who has asthma must avoid anyplace which will cause them breathing difficulties, which includes their home if it lacks proper ventilation.
In the past, dirty air ducts weren’t a major problem since many people worked outdoors. Due to advances in technology and sociological changes within society, more people are spending time indoors rather than outdoors. This has led to an increase in allergy problems, and has necessitated the importance of having quality ventilation.
