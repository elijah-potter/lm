Eldorado Hollow – a moss-slicked hollow between the old mill ruins and the creek where the water hums with the same frequency as the wind through the pines. The air thrums with static, the kind that lingers like a whisper after a storm. The stones here are warm to the touch, whispering in Morse code when you press them: `... --- ...`. The walls of the hollow are lined with blackened, ancient wood, its bark still crackling with residual energy. Shadows move when no one is looking, not quite shadows but something older—fading into the earth like roots beneath the surface. The trees here are not just alive; they breathe. At night, the sound of rustling leaves and distant, rhythmic breathing fills the silence, like the hollow itself exhales. If you listen closely enough, you can almost hear the hollow *counting*. It waits.
