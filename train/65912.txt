Oct. 21, 2011: Fire Prevention
The biggest disaster threat to American families doesn't come from floods, hurricanes, tornadoes, or other natural disasters. The American Red Cross tells us the greatest threat to your home comes from fire. Every 82 seconds a home fire breaks out somewhere in the country. The western Carolinas region of the American Red Cross responds to an average of 2 home fires per day each year. Last year, those fires made up almost 95 percent of the disasters that required response from the local Red Cross. October is Fire Prevention Month; a time to take stock of how well prepared you may be for the possibility of fire in your home. We believe it's time to make sure you have working smoke alarms. If you have an alarm, change the batteries, test the alarm. If you don't have one, by all means, put one in today. The other thing you need to come up with is a fire escape plan should the worst occur in your home. It may be the difference in your family getting out safely. You can get advice on alarms and a fire plan from your local fire department. And if you can, we encourage you to make a contribution to the American Red Cross as they respond to local fires with food, shelter and other assistance for families You can see more about fire prevention and ways to make a contribution in the seen on 4 section of our website WYFF4.com. We believe it's the right thing to do for your family.
