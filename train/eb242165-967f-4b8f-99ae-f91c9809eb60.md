A minor argument over chores between two roommates escalated when one missed their turn for the dishwasher. The other pointed out the inconsistency, sparking frustration. Over time, the tension grew—unspoken resentments piled up as responsibilities shifted without fairness. One accused the other of "always" prioritizing certain tasks, while the other denied bias, blaming systemic neglect. Small slights ("You left the coffee maker on") turned into a full-blown power struggle: one insisted on "fairness," the other on "efficiency," until neither could resolve it. The roommates retreated to silence, each refusing to engage further, leaving the space—both the kitchen and their relationship—stuck in a cycle of unresolved conflict.
