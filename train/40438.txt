UN Women: The United Nations organization dedicated to promoting gender equality and women's empowerment. As a global advocate for women and girls, UN Women was established to accelerate the progress that will lead to improving the living conditions of women and responding to the needs they face in the world. Global Summit of Women: the World Summit of Women was conceived as the nexus in which all sectors-public, private and non-profit-would unite under the common vision of drastically expanding women's economic opportunities at the global level through Exchanges of work solutions and creative strategies forged by women leaders in different parts of the world. It is a business summit, whose ' business ' approach is the advancement of women in the global economy.
