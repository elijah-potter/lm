Rupert Sheldrake, one of the world's most innovative and controversial biologists, may be revolutionizing scientific thinking with his vision of a living, developing universe with its own inherent memory. He first worked in developmental biology and is best known for his theory of Morphic Fields and Morphic Resonance. His latest book, "The Sense of Being Stared At," develops his concept of the extended mind. He presents new evidence supporting the notion that ordinary people, as well as animals, have paranormal capabilities. In collaboration with Sheldrake, research groups and invididuals may participate in broad-based experiements via telephone or the web.
