Stroud had a difficult childhood, and his alcoholic father's abuse led to him running away from home at 13.
Stroud was involved in a deadly altercation with a barman, who failed to pay one of the women he was exploiting for prostitution, and voluntarily surrendered himself and the gun used to commit the crime afterward.
He was found guilty of manslaughter and sentenced to 12 years in prison at McNeil Island, Washington.
