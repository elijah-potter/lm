It's a good monitor, however, I was disappointed that there are only 2 ports on the back: one HDMI and one VGA connection. The unit came with one HDMI cord but no VGA cord. To be honest I didn't look for what cables it included when purchasing, as I was focused on looking for a computer monitor. However, if cables were to be included, I would have expected to receive both the HDMI and VGA cables instead of only one. As it stands, I had to source my own VGA cord to connect to my computer. Since both cables are about $5 each, I would have expected them both to be included.
