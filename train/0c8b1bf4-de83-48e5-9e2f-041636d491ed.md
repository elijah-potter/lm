I stood frozen on the sidewalk, my breath fogging the crisp November air. The crowd around me had thinned to just a few scattered figures—one elderly woman clutching her purse, another teen texting rapidly. The commotion was sudden: a scream, followed by the sound of something heavy crashing into the pavement. A figure in a dark jacket dropped to their knees, clutching their arm as blood seeped through their sleeve. The streetlights flickered, casting long shadows over the scene.

Then—silence. The only noise was the distant wail of a police siren. The crowd dispersed, but I lingered, my eyes fixed on the fallen person. Their face was pale, their lips slightly blue. A child’s toy, a broken plastic duck, lay nearby. The adrenaline had started to fade, and I could feel the weight of what I’d just seen pressing down on me. I turned away before I could do or say anything else.
