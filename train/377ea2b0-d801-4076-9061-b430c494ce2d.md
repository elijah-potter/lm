**The Last Light**

The city hummed beneath her boots as she stepped into the alley, the neon glow of a half-open café casting long shadows across the pavement. A single cigarette burned between her fingers, the smoke curling into the damp air like a ghost. She had promised herself she’d stop, but tonight, the night always won.

Her phone buzzed—a reminder she’d forgotten to check. *Call Mom. It’s been two weeks.* She pocketed it, fingers trembling. The reminder was just noise. Nothing mattered except the cold, the cigarette, and the way the streetlights blurred together like distant stars.

But then, the phone buzzed again. This time, the text was different:

*"She’s waiting. For you."*

She exhaled, the smoke spiraling into the dark. Some promises were meant to be broken. Some memories were meant to be kept.
