Emily stood in her kitchen, gathering ingredients for a snack: Triscuits, cream cheese, and fruit. It was quiet because she was home alone. The sun shone brightly through the windows. She decided to eat a Triscuit before sitting down, just popping one in her mouth. For some reason, it got caught in her throat on the way down. She tried to swallow and tried to swallow, but it wouldn't budge. "My gosh, this is how I'm going to die!" she thought. She got some juice from the refrigerator and managed to drink just a tad of it. She was able to swallow again, and, while uncomfortable, she felt the Triscuit go all the way down where it should. "One downside to being alone," she thought.
