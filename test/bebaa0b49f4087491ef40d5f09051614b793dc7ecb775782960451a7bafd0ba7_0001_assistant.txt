Countless people fall victim to burnout and stress from their overbooked schedules and personal obligations, which is why it is important to make time for yourself and the things you enjoy doing. Having a hobby is a great way to begin incorporating self-care into your life and should allow you to take a step away from the stressors of your daily routine. You can start incorporating a hobby into your schedule slowly by setting aside an hour once a week and slowly scheduling more time free time into your schedule. Hobbies can be big or small activities and tasks, such as participating in triathlons or reading a chapter of a new book. If you are unsure of which hobbies you may take an interest in, take a look at the following suggestions:

• Bird watching
• Creative writing
• Gardening
• Knitting
• Painting
• Photography
• Pottery
• Scrapbooking
• Sewing

It is important to remember that your chosen hobby should be an activity that you truly enjoy doing and are motivated to make free time for. I hope I've inspired you to try new things and find your passion!
