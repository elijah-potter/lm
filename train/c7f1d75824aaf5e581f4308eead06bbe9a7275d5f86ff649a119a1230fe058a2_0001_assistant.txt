The 10 Keys to Longevity by Henry Kissinger
1) Cultivate a forceful character throughout your life.
2) Never forget an offense and determine to outlive your adversaries and neigh-sayers.
3) Make sure you have a stressful career where you make decisions with global, historical, and life-and-death consequences every day.
4) Eat bratwurst daily - preferably twice a day. 
5) Have a mind like a heat-seeking missile and remain curious.
6) Care deeply about the world, but maintain a cold, dispassionate, and skeptical view of everything.
7) Assume new technology is harmful until proven otherwise, and even then...
8) Play chess while negotiating nuclear deals.
9) Believe in public institutions, even though you know the people in them to be corrupt and evil.
10) Don't forget to laugh because life is short, even if you live to be 100.
