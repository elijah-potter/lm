**Mayor’s Office Announces New Urban Trail Initiative in Riverdale Park**
Riverdale, the city’s most vibrant arts and culture hub, is set to welcome its newest public amenity: the **Riverdale Greenway Trail**, an 8-kilometer paved path stretching along the scenic **Blackthorn River**. Construction begins tomorrow, with plans to open in phases by summer 2025. The trail will feature solar-powered lighting, bike-sharing stations, and designated picnic areas, aiming to double pedestrian and cyclist traffic in the downtown core.

Critics warn of potential traffic disruptions, but Mayor Elena Voss insists the project aligns with the city’s sustainability goals. "This is more than a trail—it’s a community investment," she said. Local businesses are already organizing a "First Mile" fundraiser to support trail maintenance, with donations accepted through the city’s **GreenFund portal**. The full route will open to the public after a 3-month testing phase.

**Residents urged to submit feedback** via the **Riverdale Planning Commission’s online portal** by Friday’s deadline. The trail’s first section, connecting **Central Park to the Old Mill Bridge**, will launch as a temporary "soft opening" this weekend, followed by public workshops next week. "We’re not just building a path—we’re building a bridge between neighborhoods," added Councilor Marcus Chen.

Meanwhile, downtown parking enforcement has increased near the construction zone, with fines up to **$120** for violations. Authorities also advise drivers to use the new **alternate routes via Maple Ave and Oak Street** to avoid congestion. The city’s **Traffic Management Bureau** will release real-time updates via the **RVR-Alert app** starting tomorrow.
