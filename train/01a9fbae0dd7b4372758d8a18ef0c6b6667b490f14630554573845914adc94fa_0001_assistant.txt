Hi there! How's it going? My name is Bell.
I'm a chicken nugget. I've got a story to tell.

My life is but a few days, so I've got a lot to do.
My first memories were on a belt, with a conveyer crew.

I had just been created, originally as a hen,
Shaped, formed, and towed away, see ya, friends!

By night one, and the next few, I was in a freezer.
It was getting so cold. I really needed a breather.

Fresh air was great! Then in a box with other nuggies,
We were born three days ago and taken into a buggie.

We arrived at our final destination, an awesome restaurant.
They advertised their mouthwatering chicken, in big bold font.

I went back to another freezer and nearly froze my face off!
But then we got unboxed by a cook, and it was time to take off!

It felt so funny, being covered in a messy, orange batter,
I was tossed and breaded and thrown around, with all sorts of splatter.

Covered in the batter, which was savory and bready,
I knew the hardest part was coming, but I was ready.

The hot grease bath awaited me, and I was nervous.
But I was only in for 20 seconds under the surface.

Phew! It was heat like I've never felt, but actually quite relaxing.
I felt exhausted but it was worth it, kind of like a waxing!

Freshly breaded and dipped in grease; mission accomplished completely.
Now I'm in a box with 11 others. I wonder who will eat me!
