A critical oversight in the quarterly financial review led to a delayed contract renewal. The supplier, known for reliability, was initially overlooked due to internal budget constraints. The decision to prioritize a higher-rated but less cost-effective vendor resulted in a 15% cost increase. Post-mortem analysis revealed the supplier’s discounted rates were available for another 12 months, but the missed opportunity cost outweighed the savings. The team later adopted a risk-based prioritization matrix to prevent similar errors.
