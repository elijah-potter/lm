In loving memory of Shashibo, the extraordinary puzzle box toy, we gather to celebrate its life and the joy it brought to the world. 

Shashibo was no ordinary puzzle box. Born of a patented, award-winning design featuring 36 rare earth magnets, it transformed into over 70 fascinating shapes.
 
With its unique, durable construction and tear-resistant surface, Shashibo showcased artwork in four mesmerizing patterns that shifted with each rotation, bringing an ever-changing array of beauty to our fingertips.
 
Shashibo was not only an individual marvel but also loved connecting with others. Its powerful magnetic force enabled it to build larger structures and sculptures when combined with others of its kind. Combining all 12 designs created a captivating kaleidoscope of 3D art.
 
A generous soul, Shashibo gave us a transformational gift, engaging the senses and acting as an exceptional STEAM & STEM toy for all. Every satisfying click had a calming, therapeutic effect that calmed nerves and relieved stress.
 
Today, we thank Shashibo for the happiness, creativity, and connection it brought to our lives. So rest in peace, beloved Shashibo. You left a memorable mark on the hearts of many.
