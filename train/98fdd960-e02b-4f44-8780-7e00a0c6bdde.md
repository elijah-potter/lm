A flicker of static on the monitor glowed for a fraction of a second, casting an unnatural blue hue across the dimly lit lab. Dr. Elias Chen froze mid-step, his breath shallow in the sterile air. The holographic display—normally a precise projection of a neural pathway—had been distorted, pixels forming what looked like a skeletal figure in the void. His fingers trembled as he reached for the camera feed; the feed itself was corrupted, glitching into static before resolving into a grainy, indistinct shape: a humanoid form, but not quite right—too tall, limbs too long, face too... elongated. A sound, like wet scraping, echoed from the speakers, followed by the unmistakable hum of something *not* supposed to be here.

He exhaled sharply, gripping the edge of his console. "That wasn’t there last check," he muttered, voice tight. The lab’s AI—*Project Echo*—flashed an error on its display: **"UNSUPPORTED SIGNAL DETECTED."** The words were unreadable, but the tone was unmistakable: alarm. Outside, the distant whir of a maintenance drone cut through the silence, but the moment it passed, the static returned—this time, louder, more insistent. The figure on the screen blinked.
