1-Q: What comes out of the earth's surface during a volcanic eruption?
   A: Volcanoes emit magma, volcanic ash, and gases.

2-Q; What are some of the benefits of volcanic eruptions?
    A: Volcanoes provide fertile soil, hydrothermal energy and minerals.

3: Q; What are lahars and pyroclastic flows?
    A: Lahars are mudflows and pyroclastic flows are fast-moving currents of hot gas.

4: Q; Whare are some of the dangers of volcanoes?
   A: Volcanoes are dangerous and often deadly, they can cause population displacement and food shortages.
