•	Wedding Colors: One easy way to add a beach theme is through wedding color choices. Teal and turquoise are the obvious choices. You can also add in colors for the sand (light brown and white), seashell colors (pale orange and pink), and colors that represent things like beach sunsets, beach sunrises, and tropical flowers (pink, fuschia, purple, orange, lime green).
•	The Stationery: The stationery is one of the first things the guests will see. This will set the table for the upcoming wedding. She can choose stationary based on the wedding colors or beach and nautical-themed wedding invitations. These invitations include the beach, the ocean, starfish, seashells, anchors, or even lighthouses.
•	Beach Favors: Wedding favors can double as a thoughtful gift for the guests and a piece of the wedding décor. Some fun beach wedding favors include seashells, beach-themed candles, tequila, or rum.
•	Tropical Flowers: Flowers can be used for bouquets, corsages, centerpieces, and decorations. To complete the tropical beach feeling, she should choose tropical flowers. Some choices include amaryllis, cosmos, Hawaiian hibiscus, sacred lotus, orchids, blanket flowers, and Barberton daisies.
•	The Wedding Cake: The wedding cake will provide two different ways to have a beach wedding theme – the decorations and the flavor. She can focus on the wedding colors and add some tropical flowers or seashells for the decorations. She can choose tropical flavors such as passion fruit, vanilla mango, lime and coconut, pineapple coconut, mango guava, or pina colada.
