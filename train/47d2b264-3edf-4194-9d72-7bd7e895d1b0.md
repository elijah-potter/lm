Once upon a time, in a big, cozy house with a garden full of flowers, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had soft brown fur and a big, round tummy that always felt happy when he munched on carrots.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his best friend, a blue bird named Becca, sitting on her nest. "Becca!" Benny called. "Why are you not flying today?"

Becca fluttered her wings. "I’m not feeling well, Benny. My feathers are a little scratchy."

Benny had an idea. "Come on, let’s go to the garden. Maybe we’ll find something to help you feel better!"

They ran through the tall grass, dodging a little mouse named Max who was playing hide-and-seek. "Find me!" Max called.

Benny and Becca spotted Max hiding behind a big, leafy bush. "Here I am!" Max yelled, popping out.

"Let’s play a game!" Benny said. "Who can find the most flowers?"

Max and Becca both cheered. They picked red roses, yellow daisies, and purple violets. Benny even found a big, shady dandelion to sit under.

As they played, Benny noticed something shiny in the dirt. "Oh no! Look, Becca, it’s a tiny treasure chest!" He opened it carefully and found a few shiny pebbles and a tiny acorn.

"Let’s share our treasures," Benny said. "Becca, these pebbles might help with your scratchy feathers. Max, this acorn could grow into a big tree!"

Becca’s feathers felt smoother, and she fluttered happily. Max giggled, "I’ll hide under this tree when I’m too tired to play!"

From that day on, Benny, Becca, and Max were the best of friends. They played every day, shared their treasures, and made the garden the happiest place in the whole world. And so, the little friends lived happily ever after, under the big, beautiful sun.
