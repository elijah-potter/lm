**The Neon Alley**
Beneath the glow of flickering streetlights, where neon signs hummed secrets older than the city, **Lena** adjusted her goggles, scanning the crowd. The air smelled of rain and old coffee, a scent that never failed to remind her of home. Beside her, **Jace**—her brother in arms—tapped her wrist, his fingers tracing the sleek, silver band that pulsed faintly with energy.

*"Another shift,"* he muttered, but his eyes held the weight of the unspoken. *"You’re running low."*

Lena exhaled through her nose, fingers brushing the edge of her **OmniLens**—a device that had saved their lives more times than they could count. The screen flickered: **Battery: 42%**. **Fuel: Critical**.

She didn’t need the numbers. She *felt* the urgency in the hum of the city, the way the shadows stretched longer than they should. The **Slogan of the Unseen** had been carved into the walls of their hideout: *"Dreams are Fuel. Fight for What You See."* But tonight, the fuel was running out.

A siren wailed somewhere in the distance. Not a police call—too loud, too predictable. This was something else. Something *hungry*.

Jace’s grip tightened. *"They’re coming."*

Lena didn’t hesitate. She pulled the **OmniLens** from her wrist and hurled it toward the nearest alleyway, the device shattering against concrete with a sound like a thousand broken promises. The explosion of light blinded them for a second—just long enough to see the figure stepping out of the shadows: **a silhouette wrapped in the same black fabric as the ones they’d been hunting for weeks**.

The tag on its chest glowed: **Project Echo**. And this time, they weren’t just after the tech. They were after *them*.
