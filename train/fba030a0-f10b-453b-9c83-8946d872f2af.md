A transient flea market in a sun-bleached parking lot near a derelict textile mill. Stalls shift daily, lined with handmade candles, vintage vinyl, and repurposed machinery. Vendors arrive by bike at dawn, set up before dawn’s light fades, and pack up by sunset. The air smells of old wood polish and diesel fumes from the trucks that pass through. A single, flickering neon sign—*FREAK SHOW*—hovers above the chaos.
