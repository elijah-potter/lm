Dear Security Guard,

It has come to my attention that I have been negligent and that my shadowy figure has been spotted on your security camera. I am sorry that I gave you the fright of your life when I flung open that fire door. I usually make every effort to keep a low profile, because I don't want to scare people. 

However, this was a mistake. You call me an apparition, insinuating that I appear sometimes. However, that is untrue. I am always here. I have nowhere else to go. You will know that I had another close brush with a lady visitor. I saw her hurtful comment in the visitor books. She said that my face was not human! Well, your face would also look 'not human' if you had haunted the halls of Hampton Court, since the 16th century.

I expect you are wondering about my identity. Well, I can tell you that I am not Henry VIII, neither am I one of his poor abused and ill fated wives. Although, they are all here as well. I expect you know about Jane Seymour's nightly journey down the palaces corridors, carrying her candle in her hand and dressed in white. There is also Catherine Howard's  journey. The poor woman sometimes relives her death sentence at the hands of her cruel husband, and she runs to the chapel screaming and pleading with her husband to pardon her. 

You will understand that these women are as desperate in death as they were in life. I mean Jane died in child birth and Catherine was beheaded. It doesn't come more traumatic than that. Both should be pitied, not held as objects of fear, or referred to as spooky. Please think before you write articles in tabloid newspapers. 

From,
The shadowy apparition on the security camera
