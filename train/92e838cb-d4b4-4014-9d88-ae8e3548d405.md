A **ruined Roman amphitheater** in Pompeii, preserved by volcanic ash. Epochs of plaster cracks, frescoed walls, and shattered statues still depict daily life from 79 AD. The frescoes show a society that thrived before its abrupt end, while the well-preserved artifacts—gladiator helmets, household pottery, and frescoed ceiling murals—reveal the past in vivid detail. The site’s layers of history are etched into stone, offering an unfiltered glimpse into antiquity.
