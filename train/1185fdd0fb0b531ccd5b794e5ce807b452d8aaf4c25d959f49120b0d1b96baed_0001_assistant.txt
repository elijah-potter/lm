Ode to Beauty

Oh, beauty, how illustrious and mysterious you are
Even those lucky enough to know you are in complete awe
Oh, beauty, there is nothing like you in this world
You are God’s gift from above

In trees, the breeze, and in waterfalls
In the stars above, the ground below, 
And even in the mundane things
You are and will always be in everything 

Oh, beauty, what would we do without you? 
There would be no life without beauty
You’ll never be fully understood
But we will appreciate you forever
