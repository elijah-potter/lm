**Note Boxes by the Community Garden**
Located at the edge of the downtown park, these wooden boxes are mounted on weathered wooden posts. Each box has a distinct number and is labeled with a name or a symbol. The gardeners and neighbors leave handwritten messages, reminders, or small gifts like seeds or handmade treats. The boxes are checked daily, ensuring no note goes unread. Nearby, a chalkboard lists events like book club meetings or free workshops.
