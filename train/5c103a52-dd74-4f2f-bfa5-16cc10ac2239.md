The rain had been falling softly for days, a quiet reminder of the weight carried in silence. I sat on the edge of my bed, fingers tracing the grooves of the wooden floor, and finally, the words spilled out: *"I once stole a book from the library."* Not because it was mine to have, but because I wanted to keep the kind of story it held—one that made the pages turn faster than I should have. The librarian’s voice still echoed in my head: *"Every book has a story too, and some are better left unread."* But I’d read it anyway.
