The clock ticked relentlessly, its hands forming a loop of silence, each cycle rewinding the past and rewriting it into something new. "You were never here," whispered the wall, its paint peeling away to reveal nothing but the infinite repetition of the same question—*why?*

She sat cross-legged on the floor, her fingers tracing the cracks in the ceiling, where light pooled like a mirror reflecting a past that had already been lost. "I don’t remember," she said, voice breaking, "but the memories keep coming back, like a dream that won’t end." Outside, rain drummed against the window, a rhythm as familiar as the echo of her own breath. The mirror on the wall showed her a reflection that blinked, then blinked again—forever.

The phone buzzed. A notification flashed: *Call from Unknown Number*. She hesitated, then answered. The line was static. "Hello?" she said into the void. The other end responded—*No*, not with words, but with a sound, a frequency that vibrated through the air like a question. The conversation didn’t end. It simply… *froze* in the moment before the next loop began.
