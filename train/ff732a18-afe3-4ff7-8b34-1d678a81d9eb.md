A small, rustic barn in rural Iowa hosted a **mismatched** Halloween gathering: no costumes, no candy, just a dozen locals swapping stories over mugs of homemade cider. The "spooky" theme was accidental—an elderly woman’s granddaughter had mistakenly included "haunted" in a recipe for pumpkin bread. The decorations were mismatched: a single black cat figurine from a thrift store balanced on a wooden sign that read *"No Pumpkin Spice, Just Memories"*, while a vintage radio played a 1980s cover of *"Ghostbusters"* at half-volume. The food was a mix of barbecue ribs, cornbread, and a side of "spooky" (but harmless) pickled eggs. The crowd laughed through tales of "haunted" barns, but the real joke was that no one believed in ghosts—just the absurdity of the setting. The fire pit crackled as a guy in a tuxedo (borrowed for the occasion) spun a deck of cards, declaring: *"This is the weirdest party I’ve ever been to… and I’m the only one who knows what’s real."*
