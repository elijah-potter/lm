In the mornings, I look your direction towards the bright and rising sun. Warm and radiant, thoughts of you cover my days in a decadent egg yolk of thought-clouding possibilities which allow me to escape into a dream: an imagined realm of the heart wherein we exist in the same kitchen. Two cups of instant coffee and a single oat milk cream. Will you allow me to cook for you, as the light tenderly grows outside the windows? 

I've taken care when sauteeing the vegetables, and you'll be nourished all throughout the day. Would you slowly wrap your arms around me from behind, in the way you've done before? Teach me how to wash the dishes as if I'd never learned; I'll watch your skillful hands as they work the suds.
