As the mother I felt I may have been responsible. I was a school teacher and I had read books and magazines. I had read of how Garfield, Lincoln, and other Americans rose from poverty to fame and greatness as my child lay next to me. I dream they will someday rule men and cities. I made my husband give up his place as a farm hand, sell his horse, and embark on an independent enterprise of his own. I was a tall silent woman with a long nose and troubled grey eyes. For myself I wanted nothing. For my husband and child, I was incurably ambitious.
