Start to cut veggies
Onion, carrot, celery
Make them the same size

Melt butter in pot
Sautee all the vegetables
Add the seasonings

Use salt, pepper, thyme
Make sure to add garlic too
Sautee until soft

Pour in chicken broth
Six cups should work perfectly
Add in the chicken pieces

Turn up the heat to
Let it come to a boil
Simmer on low heat

Two hours at least
Remove the chicken to shred
But let it cool first

Add shredded chicken
To the soup and let it meld
Finish with parsley

Optional: lemon
This will make a tasty soup
Congrats! You're a chef.
