Once upon a time, there was a wolf named Lobos who spent every night howling at the moon. He howled from the moment the moon was visible in the sky, praising its silver beauty and grace, until it had set, mourning its departure. With time, Lobos wished dearly to hear some message from the moon acknowledging his continuous outpouring of adoration, but none ever came. During his last, gray days, Lobos sang one final song as the moon rose high in the sky, full and bright: "Though you think not of me, I love you still." With this last song, he passed away, and now his spirit chases the moon through the sky, echoing the howls of the wolves still living below.
