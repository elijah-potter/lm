Jim had 365 trading cards. He gives 8 sets of cards to his brother, 5 sets of cards to his sister, and 2 sets of cards to his friend. How many cards did he give away, assuming 1 set has 13 trading cards?
Jim gave 8*13 = <<8*13=104>>104 trading cards to his brother.
Jim gave 5*13 = <<5*13=65>>65 trading cards to his sister.
Jim gave 2*13 = <<2*13=26>>26 trading cards to his friends.
Jim gave away a total of 104+65+26 = <<104+65+26=195>>195 trading cards.
