A vacation sounds great! Here are some great beaches in Florida that will have a lot of dining options:

1. Siesta Key - Including the beaches of Siesta Beach, Crescent Beach, and Turtle Beach, this barrier island features white quartz sand and crystal-clear water. Situated off the coast of Sarasota on the Gulf of Mexico, Siesta Key boasts plenty of dining, shopping, and nighttime entertainment.

2. Santa Rosa Beach - Want to do some local shopping? Santa Rosa Beach is the place to go for sugar white sand, emerald water, top-notch dining, and art galleries and boutiques.

3. Marco Island - With its unique mix of high-end resorts and unspoiled habitats, this island has plenty to do. Go on a boating adventure, eat at local eateries, observe wildlife, search for seashells, rent kayaks, sunbathe or swim!
