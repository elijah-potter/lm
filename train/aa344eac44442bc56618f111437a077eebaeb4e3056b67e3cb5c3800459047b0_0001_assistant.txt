Cocoa in a hot mug is great for a long, cold day,
Hiding in the cupboard is a chocolate bar screaming your name,
Of course, there’s white chocolate for those who don’t like dark, 
Chocolate comes in all shapes and flavors for everyone to enjoy,
Only the best desserts have the decadent delight, 
Late at night, sneak a bite,
Add hazelnut or vanilla on top, 
The best taste on the planet,
Everyone can enjoy!
