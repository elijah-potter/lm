The **Great Wall of China** stretches approximately **13,170 miles (21,196 km)** along its longest extent, primarily spanning modern-day **Inner Mongolia, Hebei, Shanxi, Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, and parts of Mongolia and North Korea**. It encompasses **historical and administrative divisions** such as **Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shanxi, Shaanxi, Gansu, and Ningxia**, with key sections like the **Jiankou, Mutianyu, and Badaling** regions defining its most recognizable stretches.

The **Mekong River Delta** in Southeast Asia forms a **lateral boundary** between Vietnam and Cambodia, with its **wetland ecosystems** and **drainage zones** defining its southern and western edges. Its **natural levees** and **irrigation systems** mark its geographical and hydrological limits.

A **metropolitan city boundary** (e.g., **New York City**) follows its **police/zoning districts**, such as the **5 boroughs (Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx, Staten Island)**, with the **Hudson River** and **Atlantic Ocean** as its **natural outer limits**.

**Urban planning examples**:
- **The Wall Street** (New York) defines its **financial district boundary** via streets like **Wall Street, Broad Street, and Fulton Street**.
- **The Paris Ring Road (Boulevard Périphérique)** encircles **Île de France**, marking a **25-km perimeter** around the city.

**Geological/physical boundaries**:
- **The Grand Canyon** is **140 miles (225 km) long** and **up to 18 miles (29 km) wide**, bounded by **Havasu Falls, Colorado River, and the Arizona Plateau**.
- **The Amazon Rainforest** spans **6,400,000 km²**, bordered by **Andes Mountains, Atlantic Ocean, and South America’s ecosystems**.

**Regulatory/legal boundaries**:
- **The EU’s outer border** follows the **Schengen Agreement**, with **27 member states** (including non-Schengen like Sweden, Denmark) and **customs unions** defining its **external limits**.
- **A county’s zoning code** (e.g., **Los Angeles County**) enforces **residential/commercial districts** via **zoning maps**, such as **Downtown LA, Santa Monica, and Venice Beach**.

**Mythical/artistic boundaries**:
- **The "Sea of Tranquility" Apollo landing site** (Lunar Module) is **2.5 km²**, bounded by **Mare Tranquillitatis’ cratered edges** and the **Moon’s regolith**.
- **The "Edge of the World" (Torre de Babel)** in Brazil is **188 meters tall**, marking a **sculptural boundary** along the **Atlantic Ocean**.
