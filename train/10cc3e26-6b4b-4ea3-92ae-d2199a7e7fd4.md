Lily the bunny loved her big, cozy burrow. One day, while digging, she found a shiny red button. She thought, "What if I press this button and make a new friend?"

She pressed it. Suddenly, a tiny door popped open! Out popped a little blue bird named Pip who had been stuck inside. "I’m so sorry I scared you," Pip said.

Lily smiled. "No need to be sorry! Want to play?" They built a nest together with sticks and leaves. Pip sang a happy song, and Lily danced in the sunshine. Soon, their burrow became the best place in the world—full of friends, fun, and new adventures!
