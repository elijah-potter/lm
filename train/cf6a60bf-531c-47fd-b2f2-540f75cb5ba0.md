Lost in dense fog, footsteps echoing off damp stone walls of an abandoned cathedral. A flickering candlelight casts jagged shadows on the cracked floor. The air smells of damp earth and old wood. The lone traveler, clutching a tattered map, stands at the crossroads of a forgotten path, unsure which direction to take. A distant church bell tolls, but no one answers. The weight of uncertainty presses down—where did I go wrong? The map’s ink is smudged, and the only clues are the half-remembered directions: *"Turn left at the oak, but it’s not there anymore."*
