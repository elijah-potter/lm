**BREAKING:** *Neon Heights* Experiences Historic Solar Storm Disruption
Local residents reported widespread power outages and intermittent radio signal failures across the city’s downtown district early Thursday morning. Emergency services activated backup generators in critical areas, including the city’s public library and municipal transit hubs. Authorities advised residents to avoid prolonged exposure to outdoor lighting during the peak of the storm, which caused a temporary blackout in the historic district. The National Weather Service classified the event as a Category-2 solar flare, prompting a citywide alert. The *Neon Heights Gazette* confirms no immediate reports of injuries, though some gas stations and ATMs were reported to have malfunctioned. Officials are investigating reports of solar-powered streetlights flickering on and off erratically. Residents advised to check in with neighbors if using emergency radios. The city’s solar energy grid remains operational but under strain. No further updates expected until morning.

**Correction:** Solar-powered traffic signals in the downtown loop now displaying emergency codes. Public transit to resume service at 09:00 AM local time.
