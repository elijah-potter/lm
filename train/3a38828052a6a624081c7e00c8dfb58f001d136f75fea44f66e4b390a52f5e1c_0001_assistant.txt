1. This past week, Heinz Alfred Kissinger once again made headlines around the world.
2. On May 27, 2023, Kissinger turned 100, and he took the opportunity to share his reflections on the shifting political realities of Central Europe.
3. His 100 years on Earth have been amazing.
4. Of note, Henry Kissenger's early years were lived in Europe's most tolerant society. A society that in just a decade would turn into the most anti-semitic country in Europe. A country that would be ruled by a genocidal regime and a totalitarian dictator.
5. Kissinger, his brother and his parents left Germany just months before Kristallnacht in November 1938. 
6. Arriving in New York, Henry was soon working in a factory and going to school at night trying to master the English language. 
7. Just 7 years later, Kissinger would be back in Germany as a 22-year-old sergeant in the United States Army serving as a military governor in a town only 200 miles from where was born.
8. Clearly, as Kissinger ventured into an academic career a few years later, no one could have imagined that he would become one of the most articulate and skillful speakers on foreign policy and nuclear arms strategic thinking in the United States as well as in the Western world. 
9. Henry Kissinger went on to be one of the most influential speakers, writers and thinkers on behalf of the most powerful country in the world where he had not long been a resident. 
10. Furthermore his book, Nuclear Weapons and Foreign Policy in 1957, led to his continued involvement in nuclear arms policy and ultimately to his negotiation of the greatest arms control agreement in the history of the world.
11. The agreement established American nuclear superiority.
