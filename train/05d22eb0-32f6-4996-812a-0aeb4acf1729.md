The storm rolled in without warning.

Lightning split the sky like a blade.

Drops of rain hit the roof—one, two, three—

A sound. A whisper.

From the darkness, it came.

I should run.

But the door stays locked.

The key is in my pocket.

Too late.

The lights flicker.

A voice, wet and slow, slithers through the cracks.

*You’re mine now.*

The air grows thick.

I can’t breathe.

The room spins.

The last thing I see before the dark takes me—

A single word, whispered in the dark:

*Please.*
