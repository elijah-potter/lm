The old church bell tolled midnight, its sound echoing through the damp autumn air as Clara clutched her grandmother’s weathered hand. The candle in the stained-glass window flickered, casting long shadows across the crumbling wooden pews. Outside, a single snowflake drifted onto the cracked floor. "Remember," her grandmother whispered, "when you were little, I told you I’d always keep the lantern lit for you if you ever needed it." Clara’s breath fogged the glass, and for the first time in years, she let herself believe it.
