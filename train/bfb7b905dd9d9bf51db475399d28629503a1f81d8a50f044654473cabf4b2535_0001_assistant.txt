The text talks about Axon's AI ethics board, which was established by the CEO to address ethical concerns arising from rapidly developing technology. The board gave suggestions regarding facial recognition and automatic license plate recognition technology, which were implemented by Axon's management. However, when the board refused to produce a drone-mounted Taser after a school shooting, Axon accepted their suggestion but later reversed their stance, causing nine out of thirteen members to resign. The article focuses on the challenge of balancing business objectives with responsible digital practices and emphasizes the role of being responsible digitally for creating stakeholder trust and loyalty.
