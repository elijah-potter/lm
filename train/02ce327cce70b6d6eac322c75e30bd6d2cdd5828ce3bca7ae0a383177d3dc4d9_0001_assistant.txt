On a warm, pleasant spring day, a bee goes buzzes around lazily. It finally lands on the petal of a pink rose.
Bee: Good morning, Rosie. How’s it going?
Rose: Good morning, Buzz. The dew wasn’t so bad this morning, so I’d say it’s going pretty well!
Bee: I’m glad to hear it. Mind if I rest my wings here a bit?
Rose: You don’t even have to ask!
Buzz, the bee, gets comfortable on Rosie’s petal.
Rose: It must be hard work being a bee, flying around from flower to flower…
Bee: Oh yes, it can get tiring. But it’s also so rewarding.
Rose: Really?
Bee: Yes! After all, if we didn’t collect nectar, we wouldn’t be able to make our delicious honey!
Rose: Oh I’ve heard a lot about honey. Humans especially really seem to love it.
Bee: Oh they can’t get enough of it. Have you ever tried any, Rosie?
Rose: I’m afraid not…
Bee: Maybe I should fetch some for you.
Rose: I don’t think it’ll do much good when I don’t have a mouth…
Bee: Right! I almost forgot. How are we talking then?
Rose: You shouldn’t ask questions that make this scenario even more unbelievable, Buzz! It’s already quite strange that you’re a talking bee and I’m a talking flower!
A gentle breeze begins swaying the rose plant, and the bee flaps its wings and flies off the rose.
Bee: Thanks for the break, sweetie! Now I'll go buzz around and get some honey.
Rose: You’re welcome back anytime, my friend! Goodbye!
Bee: Catch you around!
