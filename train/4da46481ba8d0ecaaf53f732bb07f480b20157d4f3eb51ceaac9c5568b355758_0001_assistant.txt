No problem, there are many kid-friendly beaches around the Boston area. Here are a few you should visit:
1. Carson Beach in South Boston is very close to the city, and it has renovated restrooms.
2. Crane Beach in Ipswich has soft sand and small waves, making it very kid-friendly. There is also a brand-new restroom area. 
3. Wingaersheek Beach in Gloucester is great for families and has a sandbar that appears at low tide. There is also a restroom with showers.
