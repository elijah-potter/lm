In the bustling metropolis of **Neon Haven**, a city nestled between the shimmering **Celestial Spires** and the ancient **River of Echoes**, residents are abuzz over a series of unexpected celestial phenomena. Last night, sky watchers across the city reported a series of **flickering auroras**—not the usual green and violet hues, but a deep, pulsating **pink and violet glow** that rippled across the horizon for nearly an hour before fading. The phenomenon occurred near the **Luminous Peak**, a dormant volcanic crater rumored to hold secrets of the city’s forgotten past.

Local astronomer Dr. Elara Voss, who has spent decades studying the region’s atmospheric anomalies, confirmed the event via encrypted city communications.
