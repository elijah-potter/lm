5. Google Assistant
Google Assistant is a voice-activated virtual assistant that is designed to help in the daily activities on your phone. Compatible with Android-powered smartphones 5.0+ and smart speakers that are compatible with it, such as Google Home, this virtual assistant makes it easier to complete your tasks when you are on the go.
SearchEngine can be utilized by using verbal commands as well as control of many devices that can have it as a feature, for example, smart lighting bulbs, thermostats, and TVs. Furthermore, Speech Recognition is supported with support for various languages.
Google Assistant not only answers your questions, but it can also show pictures, emails, or any other data based on what you’ve asked it to do. For example, you can request pictures from a trip or meeting, as well as sending emails using Gmail about a specific topic or person.
You can ask Assistant to give you a summary of the events that occurred during the day or to check the forecast for weather. It is also able to read news stories from a variety of sources.
If you’re a music lover Assistant will play albums and tracks with just the command of “Play my favorite song.” All you need is one easy command – ask Assistant, “Play my favorite song.”
Another great characteristic to enjoy with Google Assistant is its capability to translate signs with Lens. Simply point the camera of your smartphone at a sign which will then overlay that text with a precise translation in any language you select.
Google Assistant now features Scheduled Actions, which let’s you manage all of your smart home devices from a single interface. It’s particularly helpful for connecting new appliances like those from the C or Ecobee smart thermostat to your Google Account.
