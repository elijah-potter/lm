1. Nightmarionne - Seen in the 4th game, this is the nightmare version of FNAF 2's Marionette. It shows off its sharp teeth in its creepy smile, has small glowing eyes, and unnaturally long arms and legs. 
2. Shadow Bonnie - This animatronic in the second installment is completely obscured in darkness, the only thing that can be seen is its silhouette and its bright, glowing, teeth and eyes. 
3. Nightmare - Also from the 4th game, Nightmare is a darker version of the already terrifying Nightmare Fredbear. This animatronic is black with rows of sharp teeth and glowing red eyes. 
4. Springtrap - Springtrap is the animatronic avatar of the main villain and the creator of the monsters. It is falling apart and has the corpse of child murderer William Afton inside. 
5. Scrap Babby - Shown in Freddy Fazbear's Pizzeria Simulator is a broken-down version of Sister Location's Circus Baby. This version has a giant claw for an arm and wears roller skates to make her even faster.
