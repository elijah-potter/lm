Ritual performed in abandoned temple:
1. Participants kneel in silent rows, hands clasped.
2. A candle is lit at the center altar; the priest recites an untranslated incantation in a guttural tongue.
3. Offerings—aged bread, rusted metal, and a single black feather—are placed on a cracked pedestal.
4. A drum beats a monotonous cadence; no music is played beyond the rhythm.
5. The last participant touches the altar, then steps back—no blessing, no dismissal. The candle snuffed out, smoke curling into an empty void.
