Ten little toes and a wrinkle-less nose
Coming to the hospital both of our parents chose

To welcome their child to the town where they did grow,
By the end of the day, we will meet our girl Rose.

Oklahoma will stay flat until shaped by your laugh,
We cannot wait to be your mom and dad.

Your astrological sign is to be a Taurus and,
As two Virgos, that is perfect for us.
