Dear Doctors,  

My name is Suzie, and I want to be a veterinarian when I grow up, just like you. I am 10 years old, and I love animals. At home, we have a dog, Spot, and a cat, Sassy, and we have lots of birds outside that come to the bird feeder my dad helped me put up last summer. 

I really love animals, and I want to be able to help them when they’re sick like you do. I know I’m not old enough to be a doctor yet. My mom explained that I have to go to a special school to be a veterinarian. But I was thinking that when I’m old enough to work, Mom said that means when I’m 18 years old, I can work with you guys. I can do anything you need to help! 

I will pick up poop, give the animals a bath, cuddle them when they’re sick, anything! You don’t have to say yes or no yet. I will write again when I’m 17 years old in 7 years. 

Thank you for helping animals, 
Suzie
