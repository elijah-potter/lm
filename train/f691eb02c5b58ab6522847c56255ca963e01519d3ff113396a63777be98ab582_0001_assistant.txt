Prince Octavius was the thirteenth child of King George III who tragically died at a young age. He was born on February 23, 1779, at Buckingham Palace in London and passed away on May 3, 1783, at 4 years old when he succumbed to a severe case of smallpox. The death was devasting in particular for King George III, who was said to have hallucinations featuring the prince in the later years of his life.
