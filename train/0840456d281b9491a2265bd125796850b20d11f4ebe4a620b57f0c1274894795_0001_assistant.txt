When cooking at high heat, it is important to choose the right kind of oil for the job to avoid adverse health effects. Different oils have different thermal stabilities and smoke points which is the temperature at which it begins to burn, smoke, and release harmful byproducts. The higher the smoke point of the oil, the more stable it will be at high heat and can be used for pan-frying, deep-frying, and other cooking methods where high temperatures are required. High-smoke point oils include avocado oil, peanut oil, and clarified butter or ghee. If you are looking for the healthiest high-heat oil to cook with you should reach for oils like avocado oil, coconut oil, or olive oil depending on what is required.
