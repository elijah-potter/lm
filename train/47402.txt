Nitrifying bacteria are a type of aerobic bacteria that live in aerobic water or sand, and play an important role in the purification process of nitrogen cycle water.
Nitrifying bacteria is a kind of bacteria used to control the concentration of self-generated ammonia in aquaculture pond water. It can reduce the concentration of ammonia nitrogen and nitrite in the water, which is beneficial to aquaculture.
In fish farming, nitrifying bacteria can be used to establish a nitrification system, and this system can play many roles:
1. Purify water quality: The main function of nitrifying bacteria is to purify water quality. In the process of fish farming, maintaining good water quality is an important task. In addition to changing the water, another good way is to use nitrifying bacteria. Nitrifying bacteria can decompose some organic matter, clean up impurities in the water, and play a role in improving water quality.
2. Reduce the concentration of ammonia in the water: Sometimes there will be more ammonia in the aquaculture pond or water, which will cause greater harm to the health of the fish in it. At this time, nitrifying bacteria can play a role in reducing the concentration of ammonia in the water, avoiding the excessive impact of ammonia on the fish.
3. Adjust the balance of microorganisms in the water: There are many varieties of bacteria in the water. It is very important to maintain the balance of microorganisms in the water. Nitrifying bacteria can play such a role.
