A dimly lit apartment kitchen at 3:17 AM. The refrigerator hums quietly. A half-eaten sandwich sits on the counter, forgotten. The microwave beeps—empty cycle. A single candle flickers on the table beside a half-drunk coffee cup. Suddenly, the front door bursts open, and a figure in a stolen hoodie bursts inside, laughing hysterically. Their breath comes in ragged gasps, eyes wild, clutching a grocery bag filled with half-packaged items. The kitchen is suddenly alive with chaos: a plate of spaghetti flies off the table, a knife skitters across the floor, and the candle snuffs out. A second figure, half-asleep in a chair, jolts awake, mouth open in shock, before snapping back to sleep. The intruder’s laughter cuts off abruptly as they scramble to grab a stolen wallet from the coatrack. The door slams behind them, leaving only a single, abandoned candy wrapper on the floor.
