**Scene 1 – Present Day: The Library**
A dust-moted beam cuts through the neon haze of an overcast city. Elias, a librarian with ink-stained fingers, flips through a yellowed ledger. His fingers pause on an entry: *"23:47 – Missing: Clara Voss, 1987."* The book clatters to the shelf. Outside, a train whistle echoes down the tracks.

---

**Scene 2 – 1987: The Train Station**
Clara Voss, late, stands at the platform. A man in a trench coat—her father—smiles as he pulls her toward the ticket booth. The conductor’s voice booms: *"Boarding doors closing in five minutes."* Clara’s coat collar turns up. The station’s clock ticks: **5:17**. She glances at the train’s flickering headlights—*passenger number 8721*.

---
**Scene 3 – 1987: The Cab**
Inside the cab, a boy with a baseball cap leans forward. His mother’s voice: *"You’ll miss your brother at the park."* The driver’s radio crackles: *"Departure in 10 minutes."* The boy’s eyes widen—*his brother’s name is Elias.* The window fogs. **5:22.**

---
**Scene 4 – 2023: The Train**
Elias, now a programmer, watches the city blur through the window of his sleeper car. His phone buzzes: *"Clara Voss: Where are you?"* He types, *"Working on the train."* The conductor checks the manifest—*his name is missing.* The doors hiss shut. **23:47.**

---
**Scene 5 – Parallel Echoes**
The boy’s mother sighs, *"You’ll always be late."* The librarian’s ledger turns blank. The train’s headlights dim. The present and past merge in a loop:
- **Clara’s coat** (1987) becomes Elias’s **keycard** (2023).
- The boy’s **baseball cap** (1987) is a **data chip** (2023).
- The **train whistle** (1987) is a **server alarm** (2023).
The city’s neon hums: *"Loop detected."* The screen flickers—*23:47 → 5:22 → 23:47.*
