What are the Principles of Intelligent Decision System?The principal architecture of an intelligent decision system is described below:-
- Observe and understand the specified problem
- Develop a problem statement and formulate the decision
- Acquire information relevant to the decision that supports the decision
- Identify criteria for the decision.
- Identify different events
- Develop decision models that can be used to evaluate systems
- Evaluate decision alternative
- Recommend decisions according to the given alternative
- Meets the best decision criteria
- Consider the consequence of the decision.
- Gain confidence in decision
- Generation of intelligent decisions
- Implement into actions
