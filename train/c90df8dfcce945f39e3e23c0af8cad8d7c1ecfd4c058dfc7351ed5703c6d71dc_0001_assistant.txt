1. Warm-up: 5 minutes
    5-minute warm-up to get your blood flowing and your muscles ready for the workout.
   Jog in place, Arm circles, Bodyweight squats, Jumping jacks.

2. Arm Exercises (5 minutes):
  Push-ups: 2 sets of 12 reps.
  Tricep Dips: 1 set of 12 reps.
  Arm Circles: 30 seconds forward, 30 seconds backward. 

3. Core Exercises (15 minutes):
  Plank: 3 sets of 60 seconds.
  Bicycle Crunches: 3 sets of 12 reps.
  Russian Twists: 15 reps per side.
  Mountain Climbers: 12 reps per leg.

4. Leg Exercises (10 minutes):
   Lunges: 3 sets of 12 reps.
   Squat Jumps: 12 reps.
   Wall Sit: Hold for 30 seconds.

5. Cool-down: 5 minutes
Finish your workout with a 5-minute cool-down to stretch your muscles and help prevent soreness. You can do some static stretches, such as touching your toes, stretching your hamstrings, or doing some shoulder rolls.
