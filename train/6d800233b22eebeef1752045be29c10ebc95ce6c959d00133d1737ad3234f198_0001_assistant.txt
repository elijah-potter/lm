Today was pretty normal as far as work goes. I’m glad to be off my feet and settled in with my legs propped up for a while, though. I’m sore from all the walking, especially on the coastal shelf above the eastern beach. There was so much preparation to do today, getting ready for turtle watch. Brad and I spread out the drop cloths before we walked the beach, so nobody would bother the sand where we figure the mama turtles will show up tonight to lay their eggs. Caution tape is good but the cloth forces people to pay attention and they won’t just trample over that. I'm glad Jan and her team are going to collect the clothes just before dark. It gives me more time to rest. I let my partner take the lead today. He's earned his way out of probation. It’s been an adjustment for both of us, but he’s doing great.

There wasn’t too much trash out there. I was pleasantly surprised. Maybe people are trying to take the pollution lectures more seriously now that we’ve worked hard to put an animal face on things. After the turtles lay their eggs, we’ll probably have to fight lazy people all over again. I swear it’s like herding a bunch of brainless cattle sometimes, getting them to take responsibility for things. 

All in all, it’s been a good day even with miles of hiking and cleanup. I’m just going to relax until later so I can enjoy watching out for the turtles without falling asleep. I’m glad there’s another team making sure the tourists stay far back from the action. I get why they want to see, but too many people will scare the turtles away and they need all the help they can get.
