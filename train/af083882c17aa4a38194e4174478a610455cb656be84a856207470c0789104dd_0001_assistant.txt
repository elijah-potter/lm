Once upon a time, a young man and woman were sitting on a mountaintop, hand in hand, watching the night sky. The couple admired the constellations and had a shared dream of riding a spaceship through the universe.

One night, the couple sat on the mountaintop and watched the stars twinkling and glowing in the sky. As they did so, they caught a glimpse of a shooting star coming from their left and streaking right in a parabolic arc across the night sky. As the star completed its course, the man and woman looked at each other and said the same thing: "Did you make a wish?" When they both said this to each other, they smiled and laughed in a seemingly knowing way. They did make a wish, one that they had hoped would come true since they first began their nighttime tradition. They decided to leave the mountain to prepare more thoroughly for tomorrow night's event.

The next night, the couple went up to the mountaintop, but this time they brought backpacks with them. In the backpacks were necessities like a change of clothing, toiletries, and some snacks and water. They took their seat on the mountaintop and began watching the stars. They watched intently for several hours as they appeared to be waiting for something. And then, just before midnight, they saw what they were looking for: a shooting star coming from their left and streaking right in a parabolic arc across the night sky. The couple decided it was time, so they lay on the ground, holding hands, and closed their eyes. In a great big flash of light, the couple disappeared, their bodies vaporized by the ethereal light that shone from the shooting star. 

The couple had made it onto their spaceship, which was disguised as the shooting star. They were now able to travel the universe forever.
