Governments worldwide have the responsibility to protect the health, safety, and well-being of its citizens. Aside from protecting its citizens, government agencies must also protect appointed officials. Acts of terror can occur by individuals or groups. Such attacks are well-coordinated and often use pressure bombs, or Person-Borne Improvised Explosive Devices (PBIED), and defend themselves with small arms. The ability to screen individuals is essential to ensure the safety of the general public. Additionally, military missions are becoming ever more complex dealing with those who conceal PBIEDs. Defense operations require reliable detection technologies. Radio Physics Solutions has developed products to enable effective screening for detecting both metallic and non-metallic concealed objects. The product is completely safe, easy to use, and reveals no anatomical details while ensuring rapid detection.
