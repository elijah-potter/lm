An infinite void stretching endlessly, a cold expanse of cosmic silence where stars flicker like distant embers. The horizon curves into itself, creating a seamless horizon line that dissolves into the abyss. Echoes of distant galaxies drift through the darkness, their whispers lost in the void’s silent vastness. The air—if there is any—feels as thin as a dream, and the weightlessness of space makes the body forget how to hold itself. Shadows stretch like tendrils, neither cast nor defined, blending with the void itself. The feeling is one of endless, suffocating isolation, where every step forward means leaving behind nothing but the silent, unyielding vastness.
