The project was approved for funding based on its demonstrated market potential and strong ROI projections. Initial skepticism about long-term sustainability was dismissed in favor of short-term gains. Post-launch data revealed overestimated demand, leading to costly overproduction and a rapid decline in revenue. Regulatory changes further complicated operations, forcing a costly pivot to a smaller, more niche market segment. The original oversight in underestimating operational costs and external factors resulted in a net loss of $2.8M within two years.
