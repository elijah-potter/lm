A worn leather wallet tumbled from the pocket of an old, frayed jacket, landing with a soft *thump* on the cracked pavement. The wind howled through the alley, but the noise was swallowed by the clatter of a passing motorcycle’s engine.

Across the street, a girl in a patched-up denim jacket bent down, fingers brushing the worn leather flap. A single matchbook—a faded *Gordon’s Pub*—skittered out, clattering against the sidewalk. She caught it, her breath catching as she recognized the faded logo: *the last time she’d been here, she’d been fifteen, chasing her first real job.*

She pocketed the wallet and the matchbook, her heart pounding. The alley was empty now, the shadows swallowing everything but the flickering glow of a streetlamp. She stood, shoulders stiff with tension, then turned—only to see the motorcycle’s taillight disappear around the corner.
