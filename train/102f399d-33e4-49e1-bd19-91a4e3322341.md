The librarian paused mid-shelf, fingers tracing the worn spine of an old book, its cover cracked at the corner. A single thread of dust clung to the binding—a detail she’d missed in her last inspection. She picked up the book, turning it over slowly, and noticed the faint, handwritten dedication beneath the title: *"For the one who sees the world in quiet moments."* Her breath hitched; it was a gift from her grandmother, a book she’d never opened. That moment of discovery—small, yet profound—changed how she approached every book in the library after that.
