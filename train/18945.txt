What is Phenoxyethanol?
Phenoxyethanol is a synthetic preservative commonly used in cosmetics and personal care products.
Where is Phenoxyethanol from?
Phenoxyethanol is synthesized from phenol, a naturally occurring substance, and ethylene oxide, a chemical compound.
Where is Phenoxyethanol used?
Phenoxyethanol is widely used in cosmetic and personal care products as a preservative, including skincare, haircare, and makeup products.
What are the benefits of Phenoxyethanol?
Preserving product efficacy
One of the main benefits of Phenoxyethanol is its ability to preserve the efficacy of beauty products, by preventing the growth of bacteria, yeast, and mold.
Is Phenoxyethanol safe to use?
Phenoxyethanol has been deemed safe for use in cosmetics and personal care products in small quantities, up to 1% concentration, by various regulatory agencies, including the European Union and the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. However, some studies suggest that high concentrations of Phenoxyethanol may cause skin irritation, and it may be toxic if ingested in large amounts.
