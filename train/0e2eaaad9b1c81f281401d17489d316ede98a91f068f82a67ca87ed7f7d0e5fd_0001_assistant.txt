1. Hereditary. Ari Aster's debut film is one of the most anxiety-inducing films of all time. It takes twists and turns that the audience does not see coming and has a dark, hopeless tone.
2. The Innocents. This film about children getting superpowers gets slowly more disturbing as it goes on. It goes places rarely seen with movies starring children and is a creepy, slow burn.
3. The Texas Chainsaw Massacre. A true classic that's been scaring people since the '70s. This film was once banned in several countries due to its disturbing nature. This film about a family of cannibals is brutal, gory, and not for the faint of heart.
4. Sinister. A movie, once scientifically proven to be the scariest of all time, Sinister is about a man and his family who move into a house with a dark history. The imagery found in this film can stick in the mind for months. It is a dark movie with an unforgettable ending.
5. Speak No Evil. A massively disturbing and uncomfortable film, this one will haunt viewers for a long time after finishing. The ending is so shocking and dark viewers will not want to watch it again.
