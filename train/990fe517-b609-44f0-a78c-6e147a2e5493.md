The library was quiet except for the hum of the fluorescent lights and the distant murmur of a book falling from a shelf. Elias sat cross-legged on the floor, his fingers tracing the edges of an old leather-bound journal, its pages yellowed with time. The last entry read: *"They say the past is a ghost that haunts the present."* He clenched his jaw, the words echoing like a warning.

Outside, the wind howled through the empty streets, carrying with it the scent of rain and damp stone. His coat collar was turned up against the chill, but his hands were cold. He stood abruptly, the journal slipping from his grip. It clattered to the floor, pages fluttering.

He didn’t look back. The door swung shut behind him, sealing the memory in the dark.
