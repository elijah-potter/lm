A fleeting word exchanged between strangers at a crowded market,
*"I’ll meet you by the old well tomorrow,"*
echoed through the evening wind—unwritten, unkept, yet held
as sacred as a vow carved in stone.
The well remains, silent, watching.
Some promises dissolve like mist on dawn—
others, like the rusted blade in the pocket of a man who never came,
wait for the next time the hands of fate align.
