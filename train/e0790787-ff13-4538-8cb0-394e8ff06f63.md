**Temporary Pop-Up Marketplace in a Forgotten Park**
Nearly 50 stalls under flickering LED canopies sell handmade ceramics, vintage vinyl, and organic snacks. The space is set up in a repurposed storage shed for 48 hours, with a daily entry fee of $3. The entrance is behind a sign reading **"This Week Only"** in bold neon. Noise levels spike at noon when street performers and DJs take over the lawn. Security is minimal—only a volunteer with a clipboard monitors access. Adjacent to the park, a graffiti-covered fence marks the boundary. By sunset, the last vendor closes, leaving only scattered debris and the scent of burnt incense from a nearby incense stand. The park itself is mostly empty, with only a few joggers passing by, unaware of the fleeting hubbub.
