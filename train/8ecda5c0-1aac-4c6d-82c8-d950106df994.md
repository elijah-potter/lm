The first routine was an automated inventory management system implemented in 1987 at **Walmart’s Bentonville HQ**. It used IBM mainframes to track stock levels in real-time via barcode scanning, reducing manual errors and improving efficiency by streamlining supplier orders and warehouse operations.

Key components included:
- **Barcode readers** (Optical Character Recognition)
- **Database-driven stock alerts**
- **Automated reordering** for fast turnover items

This system was later expanded to **Walmart’s 1,000+ stores**, setting a precedent for scalable supply chain automation.
