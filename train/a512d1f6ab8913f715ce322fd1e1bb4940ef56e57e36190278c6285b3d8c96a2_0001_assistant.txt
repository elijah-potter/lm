•	In what year did Farrar, Straus, and Giroux publish VanderMeer’s Southern Reach Trilogy?
o	2014
•	What are the names of the three books composing the Southern Reach Trilogy?
o	Annihilation, Authority, Acceptance
•	What does the story focus on?
o	A secret agency that manages expeditions in a location known as Area X
•	What is Area X?
o	An uninhabited, abandoned area of the United States where nature has begun to reclaim the land after a mysterious event.
•	What has VanderMeer said to be inspirations for Area X?
o	Hiking through St. Mark’s National Wildlife Refuge and the novel The Other Side of the Mountain by Michel Bernanos.
•	How was the series released?
o	The trilogy was released in quick succession over an 8-month period. 
•	What awards has the trilogy won?
o	Annihilation won the Nebula and the Shirley Jackson awards for best novel. The entire Southern Reach Trilogy was a finalist for the 2015 World Fantasy Award and the 2016 Kurd-Laßwitz-Preis. 
•	Which novel was adapted into a film directed by Alex Garland?
o	Annihilation.
•	Who starred in the Annihilation movie?
o	Natalie Portman, Gina Rodriguez, Tessa Thompson, Jennifer Jason Leigh, and Oscar Issac.
