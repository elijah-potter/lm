Certainly, here is a list of five Midwest classic dishes:

1. Chicago Hot Dogs: These types of hot dogs are known for their various toppings, including mustard, onion, relish, peppers, and celery salt. No ketchup.
2. Cincinnati Chili: This dish combines classic chili and puts it on top of a pile of spaghetti noodles. Shredded cheddar cheese is also a requirement.
3. Tater Tot Casserole: This casserole includes tater tots, ground beef, and onion in thick white gravy. Top with extra tater tots and broil at the end for an added layer of crispy texture. 
4. Green Bean Casserole: Bring together the cream of mushroom soup, onion, garlic, and green beans. Do not forget to add fried onions on top.
5. Buckeyes: Making its name in Ohio, the Buckeye resembles a chestnut but is actually peanut butter surrounded by a hard chocolate shell.
