A jagged coastline where the sea crashes violently against weathered cliffs of black slate and red rock, the waves carving deep grooves in the stone. Below, the ocean churns—turquoise and emerald patches of light breaking through mist. At the edge, the air hums with salt and wind, and the scent of damp earth and iron lingers. The sky is a bruise of bruised clouds, thick with the weight of forgotten storms. No one lives here anymore, but the wind still carries whispers from the past, and the tides remember the ghosts of ships that vanished into the abyss. The ground beneath is uneven, cracked with the bones of ancient waves, and the horizon seems to pull away, stretching into nothingness.
