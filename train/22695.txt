What is context in interpersonal communication?
Interpersonal communication is contextual Your needs, desires, values, personality, etc., all form the psychological context. (“You” here refers to both participants in the interaction.) Cultural context includes all the learned behaviors and rules that affect the interaction.
What is content dimension of communication?
Communication has a content and a relational dimension. The content dimension involves the information being explicitly discussed, while the relational dimension expresses how you feel about the other person. Communication can be intentional or unintentional, as all behavior has communicative value.
