The liquid that flows through all of us.
Keeps us alive.
Keeps the whole world alive.
The source of like that covers 71% of the world.
Sometimes we anger it, and it comes crashing down on us.
Sometimes it nourishes us in the form of raindrops.
We still learn to live with it.

The liquid that is formless.
It flows through our body like a natural river running through the forest.
It will try to spread to any spot it can. 
Even if by force.
Become what you want; being shapeless means you can become anything.
If you believe, you can become anything.
