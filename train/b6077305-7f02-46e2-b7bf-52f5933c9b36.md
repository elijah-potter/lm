A hidden grove within the dense forests of Eldermere, where the wind carries only the faintest murmurs of the distant river. The ancient oak trees, their bark smooth and polished by centuries of whispered secrets, stand as silent sentinels. The air hums with the occasional rustle of leaves or the distant call of an unseen creature, but the true magic lies in the quiet spaces between the branches where the wind whispers only to those who listen closely. Nearby, the mist-cloaked ruins of the Whispering Stone watch over the clearing, their carvings etched with forgotten names and lost promises. At night, the wind picks up, carrying tales of the lost and the forgotten, each voice lost in the vast, echoing silence.
