Wanting to live the American Dream?
This will require time you won’t be able to redeem.

This fantasy is hard to find and harder to keep.
You may lose track. You may lose sleep.

It’s a joy to behold to find your niche.
You live on a higher plane once you become rich.

So, make up your mind and don’t delay.
And remember this adage, that Rome wasn’t built in a day.
