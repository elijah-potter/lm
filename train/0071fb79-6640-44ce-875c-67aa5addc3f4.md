City council meeting minutes: *Agreement on unanimous vote for proposal #427-B: "2025 Public Safety & Economic Development Initiative."*
**Key resolutions:**
1. **Police Department Budget Adjustment** (20%) – *Reason: "Stronger community trust through targeted crime reduction initiatives."*
2. **Urban Renewal Zoning Revisions** (15%) – *Reason: "Preserve historical tax base by incentivizing mixed-use development."*
3. **Street Lighting Modernization** (10%) – *Reason: "Reduce energy costs via smart grid upgrades."*
4. **Downtown Parking Lot Expansion** (5%) – *Reason: "Attract small businesses with flexible, low-cost retail spaces."*
5. **Emergency Services Drone Integration** (10%) – *Reason: "Enhance response times for medical emergencies in high-density zones."*

**Dissent Record:** *No votes; all members cited identical policy goals but justified via distinct economic/social frameworks. Council chair: "Focus on alignment, not consensus."*
**Next steps:** *Legislative review by city manager by EOD 4/15.*
