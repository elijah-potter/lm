Folks you aren't going to believe this, but I've independently confirmed and verified that Wilbur and Orville Wright have successfully flown the Wright Flyer they've been meticulously working on in North Carolina. This is the first-ever flight for mankind in a heavier-than-air vehicle, and I have a feeling this is an achievement that will be looked at many years from today as a historic moment.

The flight occurred on December 17th in the face of a strong headwind. These weren't the ideal conditions the brothers hoped for, but they went for it regardless. Orville was manning the vehicle and Wilbur was helping from the ground. Wilbur's attempt at flying had been unsuccessful a few days earlier, so it was Orville's turn to make an attempt. There is a photograph available of the flight, and we will try to make that photograph available as soon as possible.

The flight had a distance of more than 100 feet with an airspeed in excess of 30 mph. The brothers flew their amazing vehicle three more times that day with their most impressive flight having an astounding distance of 852 feet. This amazing achievement for mankind is surely a day for the history books.
