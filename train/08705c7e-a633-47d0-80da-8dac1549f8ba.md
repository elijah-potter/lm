**The Library at Dusk**

I had come here for the books, not the ghosts.
The first time, I thought I was searching for answers.
Now, I’m only here because the light outside
has turned to something like regret,
and the pages—thick, yellowed, full of names
that don’t belong to me anymore—are the only thing
that remembers what I used to want.

I picked *The Weight of Stars*
because it said "fiction" on the spine.
Now I hold it open to a page where the ink
bleeds like old blood, and I realize
I never read it. I only held it,
fingers brushing the worn edges,
until my mind stopped listening.

The librarian—a woman with too many stories
and too few answers—asked if I needed help.
I said no.
She said, *"You’re always asking for things,
even when you don’t know what you want."*

I left with a book I didn’t take.
And the truth:
I came back for the same reason I left—
because the weight of stars isn’t what I wanted.
It was the weight of my own silence.
