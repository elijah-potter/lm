SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 14, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, promising a vibrant celebration of agriculture, community, and family entertainment. This year’s fair showcases the best of local talent, livestock, and artisan crafts, alongside thrilling rides, delicious food, and engaging demonstrations for all ages. Visitors can expect a diverse range of activities, from traditional agricultural exhibits to modern amusement park experiences, creating a memorable experience for the entire family.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 22nd through August 28th, 2024, at the County Fairgrounds in Springfield. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 11:00 PM. Special events throughout the week include a tractor pull competition, a demolition derby, and nightly musical performances. Detailed schedules and daily programming will be available on the fair’s official website.
