**Inconsistent Policy Enforcement**
*Local regulations mandate mandatory two-factor authentication (2FA) for all digital accounts, yet administrative oversight reveals exceptions exist for legacy systems, non-critical roles, or legacy devices without hardware tokens. Staff reports indicate "temporary waivers" are granted retroactively when "documentation gaps" are cited, with no formal audit trail. Compliance audits reveal 38% of monitored accounts bypassed 2FA enforcement during the past quarter, with no corrective action logged. Suspicious activity logs show repeated credential compromises in these accounts, suggesting systemic failure in enforcement rather than outright abandonment of the rule.
