- Watermelon Picnic Punch. Made with lemon juice, strawberries, and watermelon, this tasty treat can be served within the rind of a mini watermelon for outdoor summer fun!
- Sparkling Summer Limeade. A fun way to cool off with berries, kiwi, pineapple, mango, strawberries, limeade, and your choice of lemon-lime soda or ginger ale.
- Sparkling Mint-Lime Iced Tea. A fresh take on classic iced tea using mint, lime, and sparkling water to draw in the crowds.
- Cherry-Infused Dr. Pepper Punch. A fun and cherry-filled way to elevate Dr. Pepper to a fun party drink is by adding cherry juice, ginger ail, maraschino cherries, and pomegranate arils to the popular soda.
