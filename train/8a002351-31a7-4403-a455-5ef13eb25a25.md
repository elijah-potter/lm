A weathered stone archway in an ancient fortress, carved with faded runes and inscriptions from a lost empire. The mortar between the bricks crumbles, revealing cracks that trace the years like geological faults, whispering secrets of battles fought beneath it. The floor is paved with black basalt slabs, each bearing the imprints of countless feet, the edges polished by time into a mirror of the sky above. Nearby, a half-buried sarcophagus rests, its lid cracked open, exposing bones that have long since turned to dust but retain the faint scent of old wood and iron. The walls are lined with murals depicting scenes of a once-thriving city, now reduced to ruins, where rivers once flowed and markets bustled. A single, unbroken candle still burns in a niche, its flame flickering against the dark, casting long shadows that seem to move on their own. The air hums with the faint sound of distant wind through broken spires, carrying echoes of voices long silenced.
