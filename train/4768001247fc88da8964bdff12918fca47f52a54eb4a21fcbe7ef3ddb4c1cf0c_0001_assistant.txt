- Since its release in 2012, the Attack on Titan anime has been extremely popular
- The show follows the journey of Eren Yeager as well as many other compelling characters
- A true action/thriller/horror series
- The main plot of the story begins as the last of humanity remains protected by massive walls
- The walls protect them from massive humanoid monsters called Titans
- Nothing is known about Titans, other than that they eat humans
- The writer of Attack on Titan is Hajime Isayama
- The show is produced by Wit Studio
- Although the premise seems simple, the show has twists and turns that nobody could ever see coming
- The show features breathtaking animation and fight scenes
- Each character is unique and complex
- Attack on Titan is a show for not just anime fans, but fans of quality products as a whole
