Amphitheater of Echoing Hills – a vast, sunlit valley where natural acoustics amplify whispers into resonant murmurs. The ancient stone amphitheaters, carved into the hillside, curve upward like a bowl, their polished surfaces reflecting the sky. Seating slopes gently, allowing spectators to face the central stage—a sacred grove where a lone harpist or a choir of stone statues performs. The air hums with the faint, lingering echoes of songs that were never meant to fade, carried on the wind through the canyon’s labyrinth of arches.
