A sudden storm disrupted the evening train schedule, forcing a detour. The passenger, originally set to meet a friend in the city center, arrived at the unexpected stop by coincidence—exactly when their phone’s GPS showed the friend had also arrived at the same location. Their paths crossed in a crowded platform, and the chance encounter led to an impromptu conversation about travel plans. The detour became an unexpected adventure, turning a delayed meeting into a serendipitous connection that altered their plans entirely.
