Who is the mother of jazz dance?
Katherine Dunham revolutionized American dance in the 1930’s by going to the roots of black dance and rituals transforming them into significant artistic choreography that speaks to all.
Who created jazz dance?
Both the Father of Jazz Dance Technique and the Father of Jazz Dance, Jack Cole worked to combine modern dance techniques with jazz style. Jack Cole was the first to create a set technique that was shown on stages and on the big screen during the 1940s and 1950s.
What did Katherine Dunham contribute to jazz?
Katherine Dunham (1909–2006) brought African dance aesthetics to the United States, forever influencing modern and jazz dance. She was instrumental in getting respect for Blacks on the concert dance stage and directed the first self-supported African-American dance company.
What was Katherine Dunham dance style?
Katherine Dunham was the first to combine the individualistic dance movements of Caribbean and African cultures with European-style ballet. She further fused anthropological research into the realm of dance artistry by uniquely including social and cultural rituals into public performances.
Who influenced jazz dance?
African American culture has been a huge influence on the development of jazz dance. For example, the Charleston, Lindy Hop, Jitterbug, Swim, Watusi, Breakdancing, and Hip Hop all grew out of the full-bodied, free- spirited music and dance heritage of African American culture.
What is Afro jazz dance?
Afro-Jazz dance is a fusion of African, Afro-Brazilian, Caribbean and jazz dance styles. Classes are up-beat and begin with a warm-up, followed by movement across the floor and a choreographed combination.

Was Katherine Dunham a jazz dancer?
Katherine Dunham is revered as one of the great pillars of American dance. Her style of jazz dance, known as “Dunham Jazz” flourished with the evolution of Dunham Technique, and her work as an educator positioned her as a pioneering force in the evolution of jazz dance.

What kind of dance does the Caribbean dance company do?
She conducted anthropological research in the Caribbean islands, most prominently in Haiti and Jamaica. She returned to the United States and formed her own dance company, which recreated Caribbean folk dances and blended modern dance, Caribbean dance and classical ballet.
What did Katherine Dunham do for dance anthropology?
Katherine Dunham. She wanted to know not only how people danced but why they dance. Through much study and time, she eventually became one of the founders of the field of dance anthropology. In 1937 when she returned from West Indies to America, she began to incorporate African and Caribbean movement in her technique.
What is the history of jazz dance?
Jazz dance has its roots in African tribal folk dance. However, choreographers like Jack Cole, Jerome Robbins, and many others amalgamated this dance form with bharat natyam and ballet, and revolutionized the dance form. The 1930s to 1960s was a phase where jazz went through transformation.
