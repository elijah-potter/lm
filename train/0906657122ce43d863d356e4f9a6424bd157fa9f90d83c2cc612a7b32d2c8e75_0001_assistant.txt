I finally got one. The arcade stick that I've been wanting for a while. The Victrix Pro FS is one of the cleanest arcade sticks on the market, and I had to have one. I saved up a lot of money for this day and was really happy that the shop still had one in stock. 
"Hope you enjoy!" The cashier handed it over to me. I clutched the box like I was hugging a newborn baby. The corners started digging into my face because I was hugging it so hard. The cashier thought it was a little weird, but I didn't really care. Time to head home.

I arrived home and cleared the table of all debris. I placed the Victrix Pro FS box on the table and went to look for a pair of scissors. Then, after grabbing the pair of scissors, I start to unbox the arcade stick. The first step was getting the arcade stick out of the box. I slowly cut through the circular stickers that were sealing the box. Each snip of the scissors sounded heavenly as I was one step closer to opening the box. 

Once the stickers were cut, I put the pair of scissors down for a job well done. I slowly open the box, being careful not to tear it. Each flap is another step toward my grand goal. Once the box is open, I see a purple bag being kept in place by two cardboard holders, one on each side. I gradually slide the bag out of the box, again taking care to not rip the box along the way. Once the arcade stick is out, I put the box to the side and take the cardboard off the side and put it into the box. 

The only thing left was the purple bag. It was made of a tough, polyester material. It couldn't stand in my way. I undress the arcade stick and see the most beautiful thing. The silver finish on the body. The shiny, black buttons create the most hypnotic sound.

"Click, Click"

It was perfect.
