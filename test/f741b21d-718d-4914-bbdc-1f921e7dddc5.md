## Reclaiming Resilience: Transforming Willow Creek Ravine into a Community Oasis

**Executive Summary for Municipal Funders**

Willow Creek Ravine, a vital green corridor bisecting the Elmwood neighborhood, currently suffers from neglect, erosion, and a lack of accessibility. This proposal outlines a transformative project, “Reclaiming Resilience,” designed to revitalize the ravine into a climate-resilient community asset. Through strategic bioengineering, improved accessibility, and community-led programming, we will address the ravine’s ecological vulnerabilities while creating a vibrant, inclusive public space for recreation, education, and social connection. This project directly aligns with the municipality’s goals of enhancing green infrastructure, promoting climate adaptation, and fostering equitable access to public amenities. The requested funding of $175,000, matched by $75,000 in in-kind contributions and volunteer labor, will generate a significant return on investment, strengthening community resilience and beautifying a critical urban landscape.

**Project Plan & Timeline**

The “Reclaiming Resilience” project is structured across four distinct phases, each with clearly defined milestones, responsible partners, and deliverables.

**Phase 1: Assessment & Design (Months 1-3) – Responsible Partner: Evergreen Landscape Architects & Elmwood Neighborhood Association**
* **Milestone 1: Site Assessment & Hydrological Study (Month 1):** Evergreen Landscape Architects will conduct a detailed topographical survey, soil analysis, and hydrological assessment to identify erosion hotspots and inform design solutions. *Deliverable:* Comprehensive Site Assessment Report.
* **Milestone 2: Community Design Workshop (Month 1-2):** The Elmwood Neighborhood Association will facilitate two public workshops to gather community input on design preferences and desired amenities. *Deliverable:* Community Design Report summarizing feedback and incorporating prioritized suggestions.
* **Milestone 3: Final Design & Permitting (Month 2-3):** Evergreen Landscape Architects will finalize the design based on the Site Assessment and Community Design Report, securing all necessary municipal permits. *Deliverable:* Approved Final Design Plans and Permit Documentation.

**Phase 2: Bioengineering & Erosion Control (Months 2-6) – Responsible Partner: Willow Creek Restoration Collective & Municipal Parks Department**
* **Milestone 1: Streambank Stabilization (Month 2-4):** The Willow Creek Restoration Collective, a group of experienced bioengineering professionals, will implement natural stabilization techniques, including coir logs, native plantings, and strategically placed rock structures. *Deliverable:* Stabilized Streambanks and Initial Native Plantings.
* **Milestone 2: Erosion Control Measures (Month 3-5):** Installation of sediment traps and terracing on slopes to minimize further erosion. *Deliverable:* Functional Erosion Control System.
* **Milestone 3: Riparian Buffer Establishment (Month 4-6):** Planting of native trees and shrubs to create a robust riparian buffer zone. *Deliverable:* Established Riparian Buffer Zone.

**Phase 3: Accessibility Improvements & Amenity Installation (Months 5-8) – Responsible Partner: Elmwood Builders Cooperative & Municipal Parks Department**
* **Milestone 1: Accessible Trail Construction (Month 5-7):** The Elmwood Builders Cooperative will construct a compacted gravel trail, adhering to ADA guidelines, to improve accessibility throughout the ravine. *Deliverable:* Accessible Trail Network.
* **Milestone 2: Interpretive Signage Installation (Month 6-7):** Creation and installation of interpretive signage highlighting the ravine’s ecological significance and restoration efforts. *Deliverable:* Installed Interpretive Signage.
* **Milestone 3: Seating & Gathering Area (Month 7-8):** Construction of a small, naturally-integrated seating area utilizing reclaimed materials. *Deliverable:* Functional Seating and Gathering Area.

**Phase 4: Community Engagement & Education (Months 6-12) – Responsible Partner: Elmwood Neighborhood Association & Willow Creek Nature Educators**
* **Milestone 1: Volunteer Training & Workdays (Months 6-12):** Ongoing volunteer training sessions and organized workdays for maintenance and monitoring. *Deliverable:* Trained Volunteer Team and Maintained Ravine.
* **Milestone 2: Educational Programming (Months 8-12):** Willow Creek Nature Educators will lead guided nature walks, workshops on native plant identification, and educational programs for children. *Deliverable:* Completed Educational Programming Schedule and Participant Feedback.
* **Milestone 3: Final Project Evaluation & Report (Month 12):** Comprehensive evaluation of project outcomes and a final report documenting lessons learned and recommendations for future ravine management. *Deliverable:* Final Project Evaluation Report.



**Budget Transparency**

The total project cost is $250,000.  We are requesting $175,000 in municipal funding, supplemented by $75,000 in in-kind contributions and volunteer labor.  The budget is structured to ensure responsible stewardship of funds and maximize project impact.

* **Evergreen Landscape Architects (Design & Assessment):** $45,000 (Consulting Fees)
* **Willow Creek Restoration Collective (Bioengineering & Planting):** $60,000 (Labor, Materials, Equipment Rental)
* **Elmwood Builders Cooperative (Trail Construction & Amenity Installation):** $50,000 (Labor, Materials)
* **Willow Creek Nature Educators (Educational Programming):** $15,000 (Instructor Fees, Materials)
* **Interpretive Signage Fabrication & Installation:** $10,000 (Design, Printing, Installation)
* **Native Plants & Bioengineering Materials:** $25,000 (Coir Logs, Seedlings, Mulch)
* **Tools & Equipment (Shared Use):** $5,000 (Rental and Purchase of Necessary Equipment)
* **Contingency (5%):** $10,000 (To address unforeseen expenses or material cost fluctuations)

The in-kind contributions consist primarily of volunteer labor (estimated value: $40,000), donated materials from local businesses ($20,000), and the Elmwood Neighborhood Association’s administrative support ($15,000). The contingency fund is vital to mitigate potential risks associated with weather delays, material price increases, or unforeseen site conditions.



**Community Engagement & Equity Impacts**

Community engagement is central to the success of “Reclaiming Resilience.” The Elmwood Neighborhood Association has been instrumental in fostering community ownership and ensuring that the project reflects the needs and desires of local residents. Two public design workshops were held to gather input, and ongoing communication through newsletters and community meetings ensures transparency and accountability. This project directly addresses environmental justice concerns by revitalizing a neglected space disproportionately impacting low-income residents. Improved accessibility will provide equitable access to recreational opportunities and promote social cohesion.  We are committed to employing local residents in project-related work whenever possible.

**Risk Mitigation**

Potential risks include weather delays, material cost fluctuations, and unforeseen site conditions. The contingency fund addresses these financial uncertainties. A detailed site assessment and hydrological study are conducted to anticipate potential challenges. We have secured agreements with experienced contractors and restoration specialists to ensure quality workmanship.  Regular monitoring and maintenance will be implemented post-grant to address emerging issues.

**Post-Grant Sustainability**

Long-term sustainability is prioritized through several strategies. The Elmwood Neighborhood Association will assume responsibility for ongoing maintenance and community programming. Volunteer training ensures a dedicated team for routine upkeep.  The Municipal Parks Department will provide technical support and coordinate with the Neighborhood Association.  We will actively seek additional funding opportunities to support ongoing ravine management and educational initiatives.



**Call to Action**

“Reclaiming Resilience” represents a transformative opportunity to revitalize a vital community asset, enhance climate resilience, and promote environmental justice in the Elmwood neighborhood. We respectfully request municipal funding of $175,000 to realize this vision. This investment will yield substantial returns, creating a vibrant, accessible, and ecologically-sound public space for generations to come.  We are confident that this project will serve as a model for other urban revitalization efforts within the municipality.
