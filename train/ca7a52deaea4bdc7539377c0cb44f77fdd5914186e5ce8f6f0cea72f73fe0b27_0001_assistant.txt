Minecraft Is a popular sandbox game developed by Mojang Studios. It is the best-selling video game of all time, with over 238 million copies sold and approximately 140 million monthly active users as of 2021, and it is available on many different platforms, such as Playstation, PC, and mobile. I will explain how Minecraft became not only the most popular game but also how it managed to keep players playing even after its release in 2011.

Markus "Notch" Persson and Jens Bergensten were the first designers who developed Minecraft. They then officially released it on the 18th of November, 2011 after many alpha and beta tests. Microsoft announced a $2.5 billion deal to acquire Mojang, as well as ownership of the Minecraft intellectual property on September 15, 2014. One of the reasons why Minecraft because so popular is because the game gives players a lot of freedom to develop and explore the gaming world. This allows players to be imaginative and creative in order to construct whatever they want within the game. Another big reason was the ability to play different mods created by other players. This created a huge, active community that kept the game fresh and engaging. Minecraft was available on many platforms, which allowed all kinds of players to play and did not target a specific demographic, which allowed it to reach a larger market.

Whether or not you wanted a challenging time or just a fun night with your friends, Minecraft would satisfy many needs players had. This was a huge reason why Minecraft succeeded and is still a prosperous game actively being updated by Minecraft. It is hard to name another game that has a bigger active player base and sale numbers as big as Minecraft and has had a huge impact on the world of gaming.
