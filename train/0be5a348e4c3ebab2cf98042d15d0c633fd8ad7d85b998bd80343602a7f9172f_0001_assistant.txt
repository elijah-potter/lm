Christian woke up at his usual 6 AM to get an early start to his day. His routine was set in stone at this point. First things first, make a protein shake, drive over to the gym, then drive back home to shower and get ready for the workday, Last night, however, Christian began seeing very faint, yet aggressive movement outside his windows while watching TV.

Out of his peripheral vision, he would see what looked like someone wearing a white long night dress sprinting back and forth from one window to another. Christian could not tell if what he was seeing was real or not, so he brushed it off as if it was a long day.

Waking up, he all but forgot about the strange occurrences last night. That was until he made it down to his kitchen to prepare his pre-workout protein shake. Upon entering the kitchen, he noticed the back sliding door was slightly open. Christian ran over to close it and turned around to scan his surroundings and that was when noticed the same long white night dress standing in the opposite corner of the room. 

Christian stood there frozen and in complete shock at the sight of a woman with hair fallen over her face. She slowly turned around and began walking backward toward Christian with a hunched-over posture. The woman reaches Christian and spins around violently before slowly looking up to reveal her horrific face. Christian begins yelling and screaming for help, but he knew he was helpless. Christian begins shaking violently and that was finally enough for him to wake up in a pool of his sweat, realizing that it was just a nightmare.
