- Introduction.
     - It's exciting to plan an international trip, but it can be hard to know where to begin.

- Decide where you want to go, when you want to go, and how long your trip will be. 
     - This is the fun part! Are you checking a destination off your bucket list? Heading out to visit friends in another country? Start researching things you want to do and see while 
        you're there so you can make sure the timing of your trip and the location of your accommodations will be everything you dreamed of. This is also the time to decide how long 
        the trip will be. 

- Set a budget.
     - Now that you've done some research on the area you want to explore and the time of year that you want to visit, you should have a basic idea of the approximate prices for 
        flights, lodging, rentals, excursions, etc. If you're trying to keep the budget reasonable choose a few areas to splurge — maybe a night in a fancy hotel or an adventure you've 
        always wanted to have — and then look for ways to save in other areas.

- Make sure you have the needed passport/visa/other travel documents. 
     - You won't get very far without these documents, so make sure they're up-to-date! A quick internet search will tell you if you need any extra visas, etc. Every country has different 
        rules regarding how long you can stay as a foreigner, so if you're planning to stay awhile it's important to check these guidelines as well. 

- Book your flights.
     - This is the step that makes the whole trip start to feel REAL! If your dates are flexible use the Google Flights calendar to find deals. Other sites like Kayak or Going can also help 
        you find good deals. 

- Book accommodations.
     - Hotels, AirBnBs, hostels, private rooms, camping, glamping — the options are endless. Look for accommodations that offer a good balance of being easy on the wallet
        but also just easy in general. You might find yourself regretting it if you skimp on the lodging and end up feeling unsafe or uncomfortable. 

- Think about the in-between. 
     - How will you get from the airport to your hotel? Does your AirBnB have a water filter or will you need to purchase bottled water? Will you eat out or buy food to cook at your 
        hostel?
