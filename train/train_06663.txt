Queenie earns $150 a day as a part-time clerk. She earns an additional $5 per hour as overtime pay. How much will Queenie receive for working 5 days with 4 hours overtime?
Queenie will earn $150 x 5 = $<<150*5=750>>750 for working 5 days.
She will receive an additional $5 x 4 = $<<5*4=20>>20 for overtime pay.
Hence, Queenie will receive a total of $750 + $20 = $<<750+20=770>>770.
