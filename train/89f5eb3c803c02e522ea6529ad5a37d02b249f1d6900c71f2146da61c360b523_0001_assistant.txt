Title: "Walking The Blue Line"
Description: The podcast will talk about policing techniques, methods, and motivations as seen through the eyes of police officers and administrators, and analyze how policing affects all people concerned (police, citizens, politicians, special interest groups, et. al.)
Total runtime: 60 minutes
Segments: Introduction; summary of the daily topic (narcotics investigations, school resource officers, gun control, etc.); in-depth analysis (techniques, methods, motivations of police about each daily topic); effects/impacts of these policing methods on all groups concerned (e.g. how does having school resource officers affect teachers, students, parents of students, etc.); addressing concerns about these methods from all concerned parties, including the police; closing remarks; outro
Segment times: Segment times can vary based on how in-depth or extensive each topic can be, but the introduction, closing remarks, and outro should be set (about 1 minute for each of these segments)
Tone: Serious, unbiased
Style: No-nonsense, factual
Audience: All people interested in listening to an unbiased podcast presenting facts and circumstances as they exist in policing today
Emotion: Conciliatory, attempting to bring all stakeholders (police, community, groups) to the table to understand how policing affects them
