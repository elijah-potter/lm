Many consider Charlie McCarthy, the creation of popular actor-ventriloquist Edgar Bergen (1903–1978) to be the most famous American ventriloquist's dummy. Known for wisecracks, his ever-present top hat and monocle, and his flirtatious nature, McCarthy, as a result of Edgar Bergen’s artistry, seem so authentic that people thought of him as a real person.
McCarthy came into existence while Bergan was in high school. The duo performed in vaudeville, radio, and in films. In 1937, Bergen earned a special, wooden Oscar Academy Award for creating Charlie. Among the artists influenced by Charlie McCarthy was Muppets creator Jim Henson. After Bergen’s death, Charlie McCarthy went to live at the Smithsonian Institution in Washington, D.C.
