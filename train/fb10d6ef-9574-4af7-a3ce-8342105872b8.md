The neon glow of the abandoned subway tunnel pulsed like a dying star. Two figures emerged from shadows—one clad in a stolen coat, the other in a lab coat with a name tag that read *"Dr. Elias Voss."* Their breath fogged the air.

*"You’re not the first to find this,"* said the coat-wearing one, voice rough from smoke. *"But you’re the first to carry both the truth and the lie."*

Elias’ fingers trembled as he held up a half-finished contract: *"Confidential. Intellectual property of QX-914."* The coat-wearing one smirked. *"That’s not the truth you’re hiding."*

A crash echoed from the wall—glass, metal. The coat-wearing one’s partner dropped to their knees, clutching a device. *"They’re here. They know."* The lab coat’s sleeve was torn, blood on the cuff. *"I told them everything."*

*"Then why?"* the coat-wearing one whispered. *"The truth’s not worth this."*

Elias’ eyes flicked to the device in the other’s grip. A single line flashed: **`ERROR: DUPLICATE ENTRY. REPORT REQUIRED.`**
