1) The word Gigantopithecus mean means giant ape.
2) There is no current population of Gigantopithecus because the species went extinct about 300,000 years ago.
3) Gigantopithecus remains are hard to find because, presumably, porcupines consumed them before they could be fossilized.
4) Remains of Gigantopithecus have been identified in China, Thailand, Vietnam and Indonesia.
