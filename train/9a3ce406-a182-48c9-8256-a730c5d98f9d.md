A fleeting market stall in a Parisian alleyway, where vendors set up and dismantle their stalls between noon and 2 PM daily. The stall sells handcrafted macarons, seasonal flowers, and vintage books—each item unique due to the transient nature of the sellers. The alley itself is a narrow, cobblestone path bordered by crumbling buildings, where the scent of fresh pastries mingles with the faint aroma of rain-soaked pavement. Visitors arrive briefly, often pausing for a quick bite or a single flower, before the vendors pack up and vanish, leaving behind only the echoes of their laughter and the remnants of their wares.
