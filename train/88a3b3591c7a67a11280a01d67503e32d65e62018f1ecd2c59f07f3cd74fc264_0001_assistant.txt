As the mother duck waddled into the water and flapped her wings, her little duckling stopped short, water lapping at his little feet. 
"Um, that's okay. I'll just stay out here." 
"What?" The mother duck was confused. "Come on, it's fine! Don't you want to splash around a bit?" The duckling shivered, shaking his little head. 
"Nah, I'm fine here." His mother frowned a bit. This again? 
"Now, son, you know it's okay. You're not going to sink. We're made to swim in the water, see? And we only go underwater if we want to, like this!" She dipped under the water for a second, popping up with a small splash. "See?" The duckling trembled. 
"But the pigs told me that if I go in the water within an hour of eating, I'll sink to the bottom, and I'll never get out!" His eyes teared up, and he started to sniffle. 
"Those boys," the mother duck thought to herself. "I'll have to have a long talk with their mother later." She spoke up again, more sternly. 
"Now, I won't hear anything of that. You know they're just teasing you. You've been in the water before. It's okay! Now come in just a bit, I'll be here just in case." She swam over, slapping the water gently with her wing. The duckling hesitated. 
"You promise?" 
"I double promise," she vowed. "Nothing's going to happen, but if it ever does, I'll be here for you."

The little duckling slowly inched closer to the water, letting the water ripple across his feet. It seemed safe. He waddled closer and closer, and, shutting his eyes tight, he swam in. After a moment, he gasped. He was okay! 
"Mom! I'm doing it! I'm doing it!" She laughed. 
"See? I told you. And don't listen to those ornery pigs. They're just trying to mess with you." The little duckling did a quick flip under the water and back up. He forgot how much fun this was! 
"Haha! Thanks, Mom. This is great!" Her heart swelled with pride to see her boy face his fears. He'd do just fine. 
"Don't get too worked up now. You'll wear yourself out!"
