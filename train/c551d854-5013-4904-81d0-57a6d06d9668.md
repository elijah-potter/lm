Unraveling doubt often feels like untangling a frayed rope—at first, the knots resist, but with persistence, the tension eases. That moment when hesitation dissolves into clarity isn’t triumph; it’s the quiet surrender of the mind to its own wisdom, free from the weight of what *might* have been. Relief isn’t the absence of fear, but the space it leaves behind when the fear itself becomes a guide. The doubt, once a storm, now whispers like a breeze through the branches, and the branch—once bent—stands straight again. The relief isn’t the destination; it’s the first step toward it.
