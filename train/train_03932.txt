Gina and Tom were playing football. On the first day, Gina scored two goals, which was three less than Tom. On the second day, Gina scored two goals less than Tom who scored six goals. How many goals did Gina and Tom score during these two days?
On the second day, Gina scored 6 - 2 = <<6-2=4>>4 goals.
So in total, on the second day, Gina and Tom scored 4 + 6 = <<4+6=10>>10 goals.
On the first day Tom scored 2 + 3 = <<2+3=5>>5 goals.
So in total on the first day, both players scored 5 + 2 = <<5+2=7>>7 goals.
During these two days, Gina and Tom scored 10 + 7 = <<10+7=17>>17 goals.
