1. Florida: Florida is home to hundreds of miles of beaches. The city of Miami is known for its Latin-American cultural influences, arts scene, and its nightlife. The city of Orlando is known for its theme parks, including Walt Disney World. Florida is known for having summer year-round, though the temperature may dip in the 50s occasionally in more northern parts of the state.  
2. California: Southern California is famed for its sunny weather and beautiful Pacific coastline. Los Angeles, home to Hollywood studios, and San Diego, known for its surfing spots and world-renowned zoo and museums, are great options to look into moving to.  
3. Hawaii: Hawaii consists of 8 different islands, each possessing unique qualities allowing for a plethora of living options. With beautiful beaches and a wide array of hiking trails, Hawaii is perfect for the adventurous type looking to live somewhere new.
