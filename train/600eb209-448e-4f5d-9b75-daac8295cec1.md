**BREAKING:** Officials in the historic coastal town of **Marina Haven** confirm a catastrophic storm surge flooded multiple low-lying districts after a 9.2-magnitude seismic fault reactivation beneath the Pacific Ocean. Emergency response teams report at least 12 fatalities, with 47 missing, primarily from the former fishing village of **Driftwood Cove**. The US Coast Guard has deployed a fleet of rescue boats to recover survivors from submerged docks and collapsed piers, while the National Guard has been activated to assist in evacuation efforts for the remaining 500 displaced residents in the newly designated evacuation zones. The town’s ancient lighthouse, which had been under restoration, was rendered useless by debris but remains structurally intact. Authorities warn of potential aftershocks and urge residents to avoid areas with visible cracks in foundations. The state governor declared a state of emergency, allocating $1.8 million in initial relief funds, though critics point out the town’s annual budget was already strained by rising sea levels. Meanwhile, salvage crews report finding a partially submerged shipwreck from 1947 in the floodwaters—rumors suggest it may contain artifacts from a long-abandoned smuggling ring.
