The clock struck midnight outside the abandoned library, its brass face dull with age. Inside, the last book—*The Eternal Echo*—sat closed, its spine cracked like a frozen river. The scent of old parchment clung to the air, thick with the weight of forgotten stories.

A single thread, half-buried in dust, curled around the binding. Someone had once tied it to mark a page. Now, it was the only trace of a note left behind.
