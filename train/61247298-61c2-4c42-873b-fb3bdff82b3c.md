A flicker of candlelight cast long shadows across the worn leather couch in the dimly lit parlor. The air smelled of old wood and spiced wine. The elderly woman at the hearth, her fingers wrapped around a steaming mug of tea, turned slowly toward the young man seated across from her. His eyes, dark with unspoken promises, met hers as he leaned forward. "I promise you, my dear," he murmured, his voice low and deliberate, "the night will be long, and the rewards even sweeter." A pause lingered, heavy with the weight of what was yet to come. The door creaked softly in the breeze outside, as if the world held its breath.
