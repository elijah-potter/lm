Title: Over the Hedge 2: Cooking with Grass-oline

Plot Summary: When a wealthy developer wants to destroy the animals’ homes to build an apartment complex, our favorite critters have to face a new dilemma! It’s up to them to hilariously stop the project. RJ and the gang collect evidence of environmental safety hazards and use them to convince Vincent the bear to scare the developer.  We see the return of Tiger, the Persian cat, who uses his connections to help the hedge animals. At the end of the film, the animals have saved their home and drove the corrupt developer to move away.
