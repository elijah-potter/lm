In the bustling metropolis of **New Haven**, residents were left in stunned silence as a rare meteorite crash landed in the heart of downtown Plaza Square at 2:47 AM. Eyewitnesses described a bright flash followed by a deafening boom, causing glass to shatter across the surrounding buildings. Police reported that the impact zone, an abandoned subway tunnel beneath the square, had been sealed for decades, raising concerns about potential structural damage to the nearby historic buildings. The city’s emergency response team confirmed no immediate casualties but advised residents to stay indoors due to debris falling from the upper floors. The National Science Agency has activated a task force to investigate the event, with initial hypotheses suggesting it may be unrelated to human-made technology. Meanwhile, a small group of local astronomers claimed the object resembled a fragment of an ancient interstellar probe, though no official confirmation has been released. Authorities have imposed a curfew for the next 24 hours to prevent unauthorized exploration of the crash site.
