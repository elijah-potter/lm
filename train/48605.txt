How To Use Target Worksheets:
There are target worksheets for each of the multiplication facts. The target number in the middle is the number that each number should be multiplied by in the next ring. The outer ring is left blank to write down the product (answer) of the target number multiplied by the number in the next ring. Once the students understands how to use the target worksheets, a timer should be used. Playing beat the clock with target worksheets help to build automaticity and fluency of committing the multiplication facts to memory.
