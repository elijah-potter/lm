The eaves of an ancient tower creaked under the weight of forgotten secrets. Rain pattered against stained glass, casting jagged reflections of dying embers in the cobblestone courtyard below. A lone figure stood at the precipice of a half-collapsed bridge, fingers tracing the frayed ropes of a broken swing, each swing pulling at the air like a ghostly lament. The wind carried whispers of voices—some familiar, others unknown—fading into static. The distant, rhythmic toll of a bell echoed through the canyon, marking time against the backdrop of a sky thick with storm clouds, their edges dissolving into the unknown.
