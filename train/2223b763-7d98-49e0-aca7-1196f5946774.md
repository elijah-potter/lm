**BREAKING: Major Flooding in Meadowgrove as Storm System Raises Red Alert**
Meadowgrove’s floodgates were forced open early this morning as the storm surge—measured at **18 inches above normal**—caused rivers along the eastern border to overflow. Emergency services report **at least 40 homes evacuated**, with damage to roads stretching from the old mill site to the industrial district. Water levels in Lake Meadows peaked at **98% capacity**, forcing temporary closures of the aqueduct system. The city’s emergency broadcast system activated, urging residents to avoid the flooded downtown area and stay clear of the newly submerged train tracks. Authorities confirm **no casualties yet**, but power outages and limited cell service remain widespread. Officials are assessing a potential evacuation of the low-lying neighborhoods near the riverbank if the storm continues to intensify.

**Winds gusting to 65 mph** have torn down power lines in the northern suburbs, leaving some residents without electricity for hours. The local airport, Meadowgrove Regional, has suspended all flights due to visibility drops below **100 meters**. The National Weather Service has extended the **red alert** for the city until sunset tomorrow, warning of possible **tornadoes** forming along the storm’s outer bands. Residents are advised to secure loose items and prepare for possible debris flow as floodwaters recede. The city’s fire department is still responding to multiple reports of downed trees blocking emergency routes.

For updates, check the official **Meadowgrove Public Safety Portal** or follow @MeadowgroveER on X.
