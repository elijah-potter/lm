The benefits of asparagus are numerous, and it provides a lot of the nutrients you need to live a healthier life. 
1. The first benefit is that it is low in calories and high in vitamins! The asparagus plant is a fantastic source of vitamin K, folate, vitamin A, and many others. 
2. The second benefit is that asparagus is packed full of antioxidants. The compounds called antioxidants help protect your body's cells from the harmful effects of free radicals and oxidative stress. 
3. The third benefit is that this vegetable can improve your digestive health. The fiber content in just half a cup of asparagus is 1.8 grams, which is 7% of your daily needs and helps support regular bowel movements.
4. The fourth benefit of asparagus is that it can help support a healthy pregnancy. The asparagus plant is high in folate, otherwise known as vitamin B9. The pregnancy-essential vitamin is especially important during the early stages to ensure the healthy development of the baby.
