Physiotherapy is the term used for the treatment of diseases, chronic pains, strokes, injuries, or any deformity by physical methods such as interferential massage therapy, heat treatment, ultrasonics, wax therapy, and muscle strengthening exercises rather than by medical drugs or surgery.

Physiotherapy has been categorized into three main types such as orthopedics physiotherapy, neuro physiotherapy, and cardiorespiratory physiotherapy.
Three Types of Physiotherapy Services
- Orthopedics Physiotherapy
This is the branch of physiotherapy associated with the treatment of injuries or disorders of the skeletal system and the muscles associated with it, joints and ligaments.
The main objective of the orthopedic physiotherapist is to provide relief from pain, increase joint range, improve strength and flexibility and restore the patient to full function.
- Neuro Physiotherapy
Neuro physiotherapy is given to the patients whose central nervous system is damaged due to some injury. This type of therapy helps in kick start the pathway of messages from the brain to the other parts of the body through repetitive actions and exercises.
- Cardiorespiratory Physiotherapy
Cardiorespiratory physiotherapy is the branch of physiotherapy that deals in the prevention, rehabilitation, of the patients with diseases and injuries in the heart and lungs.
