**Unspoken Pact Between the Silent**

A handshake unmade, a promise left unspoken—
A contract carved in the margins of the unheard.
The words that never rise to lips are inked in bones,
written in the hushed echoes of the unseen.

The terms are whispered by the wind through cracked doors,
each clause a ghost that lingers, half-drawn, half-forgotten.
A debt owed in silence, a trust unbroken,
a chain of unspoken oaths that twist and weave between the living and the dead.

The law is not in the statutes, but in the stillness,
where the unspoken truth outlasts the spoken lies.
No one signs it, yet everyone bears its weight—
a shadow pact that binds the heart and the hand.
