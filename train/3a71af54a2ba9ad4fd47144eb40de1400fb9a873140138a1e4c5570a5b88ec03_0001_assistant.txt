Planet of Angels
The Los Angeles Dodgers got on a plane to go to their next series against the Toronto Blue Jays. While taking off, the pilot fainted, and the plane didn't stop climbing until it coasted to a stop on an unknown planet. The players look to Clayton Kershaw for leadership and Walker Buehler for his problem-solving ability and intelligence. The planet gets more interesting when the Dodgers encounter a group of Squishmallows who just might have the secret to getting back to Earth, but the Squishies have other plans. The Dodgers develop a friendly relationship with the Squishies who are plotting to keep them on their planet forever. As the episodes progress, the gang encounters various scenarios that require working together and cooperating with the Squishies. The team is also trying to figure out how to get back to earth as soon as possible. Think Gilligan's Island, with cute little creatures who want to learn how to play baseball from the best team in the universe!
