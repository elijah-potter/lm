Jake loves the ocean and swimming. This is why he lives on a boat. He is underwater and comes across a dark cave. He is so curious about what secrets could await him. He goes as far into the cave as he can until it is so dark he can't see. He starts to get scared, and suddenly his hands start to shake. A blast of light shines seemly from nowhere. It's so bright he takes his hand and shields his eyes. This is when he realizes the light is coming from his eyes. He can't believe it. Stunned, he swims to the surface. As he reaches the top, the light fades away.

He grabs the ladder to the boat and climbs as fast as possible. He then shouts, "Andy follow me I have something amazing to show you!" Andy is sitting on the edge of the boat a falls in he is so caught off guard. Jake thinks this is perfect and quickly jumps back in the water. He takes grabs Andy and takes him underwater. Andy can not believe it as he sees the light beam out of his boyfriend's eyes.
