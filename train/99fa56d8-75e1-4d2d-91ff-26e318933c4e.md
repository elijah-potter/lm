A routine train trip in Tokyo unexpectedly led to a stranger claiming to have lost a rare 19th-century samurai sword in the station’s lost-and-found. The owner—a retired scholar—had spent decades searching for it. The coincidence compelled the traveler to return to Kyoto, where they found a hidden compartment in their own suitcase containing a second, identical artifact, both bearing the same family crest. The scholar, now retired, agreed to help the traveler piece together the clues, realizing their shared ancestry and the sword’s role in a forgotten family feud. Their research uncovered that the artifacts were meant to be reunited, but the original owner had misplaced them decades ago, causing a cascade of unintended consequences. The traveler’s original plan to explore Kyoto’s temples became a mission to restore historical balance, uncovering a secret that reshaped both their lives and the local legend of the "Lost Blade."
