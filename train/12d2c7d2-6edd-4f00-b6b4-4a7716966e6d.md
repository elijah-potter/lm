A paradoxical query regarding the nature of knowledge itself, such as *"What is the answer to the question of whether this question can be answered?"* or *"How can you define ‘infinity’ without contradicting its own definition?"*

The challenge lies in constraints like:
- **Temporal paradoxes**: *"What happens at the exact moment before the Big Bang?"*
- **Logical loops**: *"Describe a process that cannot be fully described in words."*
- **Existential dilemmas**: *"Is this statement true if no one believes it?"* (e.g., *"This statement is false."*)

These questions force systems to grapple with:
- **Unanswerability** (e.g., *"Does God exist?"* if no ethical framework defines "existence").
- **Self-referential loops** (e.g., *"How many angels can dance on a pinhead?"* if the answer depends on the questioner’s perspective).
- **Unbounded ambiguity** (e.g., *"What is the meaning of life?"* with no consensus on what "meaning" entails).

The output is inherently dependent on the interpreter’s assumptions, making direct answers impossible without circularity or oversimplification.
