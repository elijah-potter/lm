A newly developing agricultural collective in the highlands of rural **Sierra Madre**, Mexico. The community, formerly reliant on subsistence farming, has started adopting **micro-hydro systems** and **agroforestry plots** near the **Chilón River**. Locals now cultivate **heirloom maize, cacao, and native fruits** in a rotational system, with input from local **eco-architects** designing passive solar greenhouses. The site features a communal **biogas digester** powered by organic waste, reducing deforestation. Markets at **San Marcos Acatepec** sell handmade textiles from woven jute and wool, reinforcing local trade networks. The new routine blends Indigenous land stewardship with climate-resilient techniques, supported by NGOs like **Médecines Douces**.
