Hello, dear!  Have no fear!  I'm going to wave my magic wand and help you change a tire faster than you can say 'pixie dust'!

Step 1:  Make sure you're parked in a safe area, away from that noisome traffic if possible.  Then, set the parking brake and turn on your hazard lights.

Step 2:  Let's make sure we have all the necessary items.  You'll need a spare tire, a jack, and a wrench.  Other helpful items would be a flashlight, gloves to protect your hands (and nails), a rain poncho to keep you dry, a tire gauge, and a mat to kneel on, so you don't get dirty before the ball!

Step 3: Remove the hubcap if you have one, then loosen the lug nuts.  These will turn counter-clockwise, and you should loosen them until you can turn them by hand.  

Step 4: Up, up and away!  It's time to raise the car.  Carefully put the jack underneath a solid part of the vehicle and elevate the car until the tire you want to change is no longer touching the ground.

Step 5: Now you can completely remove the lug nuts, but don't forget to keep them someplace safe, dear.  You'll need them in a moment.

Step 6: Grasp the tire firmly without mussing your dress and remove it from the car.

Step 7: You can now put the spare tire on and put the lug nuts on, but don't tighten them yet.  Absolutely not!

Step 8: Lower the car until the tire is touching the ground and tighten the lug nuts so that you do it evenly.  Assign each lug nut a number and sing with me: "1, 3, 5, 2, 4!"

Step 9: Lower the car all the way down and completely tighten the lug nuts.
