**Mayor’s Office Press Briefing – City of Eldermere**
*Thursday, June 13, 2024 – 11:47 AM*

The City of Eldermere has confirmed a surge in reported incidents of "Echoes of the Hollow," a phenomenon described by residents as fleeting, disembodied voices heard in abandoned buildings along the old railway corridor near Blackthorn Creek. Authorities are investigating whether this aligns with historical accounts of the "Hollow Men," a legend from the region’s past. Eldermere Police Chief Elias Voss stated, "Initial reports suggest the voices may be echoes of missing persons from the 1920s, but we’re treating this as a potential safety concern until further evidence emerges."

Eldermere Mayor Lira Solstice has directed all city departments to monitor the area for trespassing and unauthorized activity, warning residents to avoid venturing into the affected zones without proper supervision. The city’s historical society has been requested to collaborate on archival research to determine if the phenomenon ties to local folklore. Meanwhile, residents in the affected neighborhoods have reported strange occurrences, including animals behaving erratically and flickering streetlights near the corridor.

Eldermere’s Public Works Department has begun constructing temporary barriers and lighting installations to deter visitors, though the city has not issued an official lockdown. The Economic Development Council is exploring partnerships with local businesses to offer guided tours of the area, emphasizing historical preservation rather than tourism. No injuries or fatalities have been reported yet, but officials urge caution.

The next public update will be issued by the City Council at 3:00 PM today.
