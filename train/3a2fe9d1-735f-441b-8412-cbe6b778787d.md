A sudden gust of wind sent a gust of autumn leaves swirling through the open window, startling the man mid-step as he paused in the dimly lit alley. His breath caught in his throat, fingers tightening reflexively around the grip of his unzipped jacket. The fluorescent light flickered erratically, casting eerie shadows on the cracked pavement, where a discarded soda can glinted under the dim glow. His gaze darted to the corner where the streetlamp’s beam barely reached, then to the alleyway’s mouth where a silhouette moved—too still, too quiet—before the figure melted into the crowd of early evening shoppers. His heart hammered, but his mouth stayed dry.
