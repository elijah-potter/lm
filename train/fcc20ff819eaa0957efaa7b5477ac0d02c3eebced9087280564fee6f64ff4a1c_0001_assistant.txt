I practically hopped into the rocking chair, tossing the blanket over my knees to keep them warm. I didn't really need a blanket, but it was all about the atmosphere. It was a Friday afternoon, work was done, and it was time to relax. I looked at the wisps lazily rising from my mug of tea and breathed in deeply, catching a whiff of the coconut and toasted almond. 

I pulled my book, Relaxing: A Meditative Study, from the shelf and flipped to the Introduction. I had read a few lines and was just getting ready to pick up my mug when—bzzzt!—a sound like a drill came from outside my window. It wasn't the sound of someone using a miter saw or something across the street. No, it was right outside. Still, I was reluctant to hop up. I was cozy and didn't want to move. If I stayed put, maybe the monster outside would forget about me and bother someone else or come back later. Either one was preferable.

I went back to my book, pulling up the blanket ever so slightly just to convince myself that I was truly in relaxation mode. The noise again, like a drillbit whirring at max speed into my drywall. I shot up in annoyance and pulled the blind cord. That's when I saw the little fellow. A hummingbird, gazing at me with fear in its eyes, sat perched against the tree. So that's what had been bothering me all this time. I laughed and waved at the bird, which proved to be a little too scary. He flew off, leaving me finally at peace with my book.
