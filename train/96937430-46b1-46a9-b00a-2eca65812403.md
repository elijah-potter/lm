---
**Setting:** A dimly lit, rain-soaked alleyway near the docks, flickering neon signs casting long shadows across wet cobblestones. A lone figure—clad in a tattered trench coat—stands by a rusted cargo container, fingers tracing the edge of a half-empty bottle of whiskey. The city hums in the distance, a mix of distant sirens and the clatter of hooves on pavement. The air smells of salt, damp wood, and something faintly metallic.

**The Figure:** The person’s eyes are hollow, their breath shallow. They’ve been waiting here for hours, counting the minutes in hushed tones to themselves. A second figure approaches, silhouetted against the glow of a flickering gas lamp. The stranger’s voice is low, urgent—*"We need to leave now."* The figure turns slowly, the whiskey bottle clutched in one hand. The bottle cracks under pressure.

---
**Silence.** The alleyway stretches too long. A distant car door slams. The figure’s coat billows slightly in the wind. The stranger steps closer, voice rising just enough to crack the tension. *"You’re not ready."* The figure’s hand hovers over the empty whiskey bottle. The container creaks underfoot.

*(The scene ends here—just before the stranger’s hand grasps the figure’s wrist.)*
