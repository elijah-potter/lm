The **Court of Arbitrary Jurisdictions (CAJ)**—a sprawling, semi-private enclave in the port district of **Havenport**. Built atop reclaimed shipwrecks and old warehouses, its boundaries are defined by a mix of custom, blackmail, and unspoken bargains. The **Grand Arbiters**, a council of self-appointed judges who answer to no higher authority, enforce laws that shift like sand. A "contract" here can mean a handshake or a stolen ledger. The **Floating Market**—where goods are traded in cryptocurrency, barter, and sometimes just favors—operates under the principle that "debt is debt, even if you don’t remember signing it." The **Clockwork Alley**, a labyrinth of repair shops and pawnbrokers, operates on the rule that *"if it ain’t broken, don’t fix it—unless you’re paying."* The **Library of Lost Causes**, a secretive archive where books are checked out by the hour, holds records of disputes that were never officially settled. Some say the CAJ’s true laws are whispered in the **Tea House at Dusk**, where the last cup of the day is never served the same way twice.
