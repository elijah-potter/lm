
1.  Roses are old. There are fossils of roses that are over 35 million years. On the wall of the Cathedral of Hildesheim in Germany, the oldest rose lives at an age of over 1,000 years.
2. Roses can be used in your kitchen because all roses of all varieties are safe to eat. In Indian and Chinese cuisine, rose petals are used as flavorings. Tea can be infused with a berry-shaped fruit grown from roses (rose hips).
3. England adopted the rose as its national flower. At the end of the civil war between the house of Lancashire and the house of York (the War of the Roses), the Tudor Rose became England's emblem of peace.
4. There is a different meaning for each color of a rose. Red means passion and love while pink means elegance and gratitude. Also, white means spirituality and innocence while lavender means enchantment and wonder.
5. The tallest rose bush is at least 18.8 feet. This was recorded on November 8th, 2017 by Christopher Rose in the USA.
6. Over 150 rose species exist in the world and there are numerous hybrids. Roses also have wonderful relatives like cherries, apples, and almonds.
7. The Juliet Rose is the most expensive rose developed at £2.3 million. It took 15 years to be developed by David Austin and was on display at the 2006 Chelsea Flower Show. This rose's color was like an apricot with a full cup and gorgeous petals.
8. Microscopic glands on rose petals produce their lovely scent.
9. Ecuador has a lot of roses over half of its land. This country is a major supplier to the rest of the world. Ecuador has a perfect climate for roses with year-round light.
