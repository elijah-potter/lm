Here are three possible decorations you can use:
1) Paint. Using paint, you can create any space theme you want. With the stickers you have, you can combine the paint and the stickers to create a realistic depiction of space. 
2) Tape. If you don't want to spend the money or time on paint, you can try tape instead. Themed tape can be used to decorate your laptop, and you can put your stickers on top of the tape.
3) Laptop covers. Getting a space-themed laptop cover is a cheap alternative to paint. Although there aren't as many options, you can find a great laptop cover that's space-themed. You can put your stickers on the outside and decorate based on the design the cover has. Along with that, the cover will protect your laptop.
