A whispered vow in the fading light,
A shadow’s touch on the morning’s air—
No fanfare, no bold decree,
Just the slow unraveling of time.

The lover’s hand, a stolen kiss,
The friend’s unspoken debt repaid,
The child’s first step, the gardener’s care,
Each quiet act, a whispered pledge.

No grand announcement, no public flame,
Only the weight of unseen hands,
The way the river bends its way,
Carving its truth in quiet sands.

A promise kept in the cracks of the world,
Where no one sees, yet all is known.
