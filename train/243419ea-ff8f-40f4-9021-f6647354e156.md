A maintenance worker in a dimly lit factory noticed a loose wire in a control panel during a routine inspection. It had been there for months, unnoticed by others due to the routine nature of the tasks, but the worker’s keen eye caught the flicker of static when testing the circuit. Upon further investigation, it triggered an overload, causing a temporary shutdown of the production line. The incident led to an immediate safety review, reinforcing the importance of vigilance in overlooked details.
