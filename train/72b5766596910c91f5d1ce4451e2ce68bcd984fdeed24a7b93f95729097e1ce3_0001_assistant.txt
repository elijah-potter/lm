Certainly! There are so many varieties of no-bake cookies to choose from. Here are a few favorites:

1. Chocolate peanut butter cookies - try adding chocolate chips or cocoa and sugar to the peanut butter for these no-bake cookies.

2. Almond Pecan Date Truffles - these can be made into truffle balls with only a few ingredients.

3. Fruity cereal cookies - try freezer cookies made with sweet, fruity cereal.

4. Peanut Butter Oatmeal Bars - these are good room temperature or frozen, and if you don't make them too sweet, they can even be for breakfast!

5. Chocolate Oatmeal Cookies - these are a classic favorite, and you can whip them up in just a few minutes.

Let me know if you'd like more suggestions or any of these recipes!
