Have you ever felt sad for absolutely no reason? Like you wake up one day and your mood is just down in the dumps, and you're like, "Why, Brian? Why are you doing this to me?" It's like your emotions decided to throw a surprise party and the only theme is sadness. I reasoned that perhaps I could take advantage of this peculiar circumstance. I started listening to depressing music and pretended that my life was a dramatic film. I'm referring to emotional scenes, heart-wrenching monologues, the whole nine yards. I even stood next to the window and stared out sadly as tears run down my face. Wow, I'm so depressed right now, I thought. This sorrow is deserving of an Oscar.

But here is the kicker, my sadness had no purpose. it was like being the star of a movie with a plot that makes no sense. Who wants to watch a character whining about nothing in particular? Even I was unable to support that notion. And let me tell you, it is blatantly unfair to be denied the ability to feel sorry for oneself. That's the only silver lining in sadness folks. But okay, I thought, maybe it's time to snap out of it. I mean, standing around feeling sorry for myself was momentarily thrilling, but it got old real quick. I was like, "Alright, self-pity party, you've had your moment in the spotlight. Time to pack up and find a new hobby." But guess what? The sadness didn't budge an inch. I tried to force myself not to be sad. I used all my willpower, like a superhero trying to defeat the apathetic sadness that comes with depression. But let me tell you, it's like a person with no arms trying to punch themselves until their hands grow back. It's a flawed plan, my friends. You're missing a vital component, and it ain't gonna work. Trust me, I've tried.

Therefore, if you ever feel depressed for no apparent reason, keep in mind that it's okay to wallow in your misery even if you do not know why your emotions are having a pity party. Just be realistic about your expectations and refrain from trying to punch yourself in the face with your own fictitious hands for the sake of humor.
