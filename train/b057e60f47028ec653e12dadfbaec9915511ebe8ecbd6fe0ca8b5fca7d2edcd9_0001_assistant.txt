So badly wanted an elegant, stylish shoe,
Beautiful, wonderful, and oh so blue.

Funding big problem, dollars too few,
Got job, hours of sweat, worked a slew.

Got money for hours, made a pile,
Thought long and hard, money for style?

Decided, keep the cash--no money is vile,
Having no funds, it tastes like bile.

Money stacked safe, but no shoe?
Troubled and sad, lost dream oh so blue.
