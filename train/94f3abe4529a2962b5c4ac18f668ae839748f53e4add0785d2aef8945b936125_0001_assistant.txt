While most United States presidents have served their full four or eight-year terms in office, one president was elected for more than two terms. The longest term ever served was by the 32nd president, Franklin D. Roosevelt, who is the only president to serve more than two terms. Roosevelt served four consecutive terms as president, however, he passed away only 40 days into his fourth term. President Roosevelt served in office from 1933 until his death in 1945.
