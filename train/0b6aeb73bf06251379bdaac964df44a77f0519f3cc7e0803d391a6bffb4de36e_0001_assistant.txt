In between the shadows, on a hazy night in Peru, I was a ghost. The back door to the hotel opened in only eight seconds. One of my best times for a lockpick. I crept through the hallways, ever silent like a panther stalking my prey: none other than Irfan Satistelli, an offshore banker wanted in the Hague for mafia crimes. I was entrusted to eliminate the target on sight. I picked the lock to his exquisite hotel room. He jumped in surprise at my arrival, but with a quick "pow," he crumpled to the floor. Mission accomplished. Now, I've just got to escape without leaving a trace.
