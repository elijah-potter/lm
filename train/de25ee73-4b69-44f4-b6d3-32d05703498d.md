A dim lamp flickered against the rain-streaked window of an old library. The scent of aged paper mingled with the faint aroma of aged tea. Across the desk, a figure sat hunched over a half-finished manuscript, fingers tracing the margins silently. Their eyes never left the page, though the room buzzed with unspoken tension.

The door creaked open. A second figure entered, carrying a stack of books, their silhouette sharp against the dim glow. They set the stack down without a word, then paused, staring at the manuscript. The first figure exhaled slowly, meeting their gaze—just for a second. No words were spoken, but the air between them shifted. The second figure picked up a pen, then dropped it. The first dipped it into a cup of tea, sipping without looking up.

A silence settled. The words on the page hummed with something unsaid. The second figure turned, leaving the door open behind them. The first watched the door close, then turned back to the manuscript—where a single word, written in the margin, glowed faintly in the dark.
