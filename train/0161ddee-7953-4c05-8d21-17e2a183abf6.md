Once upon a time in a cozy little house, there lived a bunny named Benny. Benny had a big, round nose and soft, fluffy ears. One sunny morning, Benny woke up and decided to explore his garden.

He hopped outside and saw a tiny red flower that looked very special. "Wow, this looks yummy!" Benny said to himself, and he gently picked it. Suddenly, he heard a tiny voice.

"Help! Help! My house is falling down!"

Benny looked down and saw a little mouse named Mimi. Her house was made of sticks and leaves, and it was wobbly. Benny hopped over and helped Mimi fix her house with some twigs and sticks from the garden. When they were done, Mimi’s house stood tall and strong.

Benny and Mimi became the best of friends. Every day, they played, explored, and shared their adventures. Benny taught Mimi to hop, and Mimi showed Benny how to find the sweetest berries. They even helped other small animals in their garden, like a shy squirrel and a sleepy turtle.

One day, a big, dark cloud rolled in, and it started to rain. Benny and Mimi huddled under a big, old tree to stay dry. "Let’s share our rainbows!" Benny said, and Mimi smiled. They made a blanket out of leaves and sat together, laughing and playing games like "I Spy" with their imaginations.

As the sun peeked out from behind the clouds, Benny and Mimi realized how lucky they were to have each other. They promised to keep being friends forever, and every morning, they would hop outside to start a new adventure together.

And that’s how Benny the bunny and Mimi the mouse made the garden their magical playground.
