Silent erosion of norms through incremental policy adjustments, often justified as "efficiency" or "flexibility," undermines institutional integrity by normalizing deviation. For example, a company’s "no overtime" policy might quietly shift to "only if justified," then "only if documented," then "only if approved by management"—until overtime becomes routine without explicit consent. This occurs when:
1. **Justifications** evolve from "unwritten tradition" → "practical necessity" → "standard operating procedure."
2. **Enforcement** weakens through "soft rules" (e.g., "if you’re unsure, ask") that replace clear thresholds.
3. **Accountability** dissolves as violations are framed as "collateral damage" in the system’s "flow."

The rewritten rule isn’t a single act but a **cascade of micro-deviations** that render the original obsolete. The system’s "efficiency" becomes the cost of compliance erosion.
