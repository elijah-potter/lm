"Dearest Menelaus,

I want to thank you for the hospitality you showed me and my people on our recent visit to your fair land. Truly, the food and culture of Sparta are thriving. We all made it back to Troy in good health and with good humor. I hope too for your health and the prosperity of your family. Speaking of your family, how is your wife Helen? Unfortunately, we were not able to properly say goodbye on our departure. Please pass on my good wishes to her as well. I wish to see you all again sometime, perhaps around the royal dining table of Troy this time. 

Warmest Wishes,

Prince Paris of Troy"
