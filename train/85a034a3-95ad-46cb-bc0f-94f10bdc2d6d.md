Fog rolls thick over the cracked asphalt, obscuring the faint glow of distant streetlights. A lone figure stands near the edge of a crumbling plaza, their silhouette framed by rusted metal pillars. A broken lantern casts long shadows across weathered stone, its glass shattered, glass shards glinting in the dim light. The air hums with the distant drip of water, pooling in the hollows of the earthen floor. A child’s laughter, muffled and distant, echoes off the damp walls, then vanishes like mist. The wind carries the scent of damp earth and something older—something like old wood and iron. The figure turns slowly, their breath shallow in the cold, staring at the horizon where the sky bleeds into the earth.
