Audrey Hepburn only appeared in two movies during the 1970s:
• Robin and Marian (1976) - Sean Connery is Robin Hood to Audrey Hepburn's Maid Marian in this tale about the legendary characters in their middle-aged years.
• Bloodline (1979) - Heiress to a pharmaceutical company whose wealth is measured in the billions, Elizabeth Roffe (Audrey Hepburn) is soon targeted for murder just as her late father was.
