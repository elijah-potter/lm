In the hollowed-out husk of an abandoned library, the last book sat unread, its spine cracked with time and forgotten. The air smelled of aged paper and forgotten dreams. A single candle flickered beside it, casting long shadows on the walls where whispers of the past still lingered.

She found it by accident—stumbled upon its hiding place beneath a dust-covered ledger, its pages yellowed with age. The book was titled *"The Echoes Between Us"*, its cover worn thin with stories untold.

She opened it carefully, her fingers trembling not from fear, but from the weight of what she was about to uncover. The first line was already faded, but she read it anyway: *"Do you remember what the stars look like when you’re afraid?"*

Outside, the wind howled through the broken windows, carrying secrets that the book could never hold. She closed it again, hesitant, unsure if she should keep reading—or if she should let it stay closed forever.

What was the first thing she should do with the book?
