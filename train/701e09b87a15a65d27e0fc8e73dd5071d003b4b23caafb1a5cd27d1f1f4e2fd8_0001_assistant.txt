Macaws can live up to 25 years
With long wingspans and hefty beaks
When they call, it's screams you'll hear
These colorful birds are not so weak

They can lay up to 4 eggs at a time
And have dry tongues with bones inside
Sometimes they even talk and mime
With their brilliance, it's hard to hide 

Macaws are often called "show birds"
They've been kept as pets for ages
At times they can mimic certain words
Lilting from their at-home cages
