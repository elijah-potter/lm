It is the 1950s, and the small country of Cuba is a hotbed of activity. The Central Intelligence Agency has sent a distinguished spy to Havana to investigate the mob ties to leader Fulgencio Batista. The spy quickly uncovers a web of corruption and violence. The mob is deeply entrenched in the Cuban government, and they are using their power to exploit the people and fatten their pockets. 

The spy types up a report to his superiors, but they disregard his findings. They are more concerned with maintaining stability in the region and securing national interests. The agency orders the spy to keep his findings a secret. The spy is disgusted by what he has seen, but he knows that he can't do anything about it. He is just one man, and he is up against a powerful entity. He can only hope that one day the truth will become public. In the meantime, he drinks cocktails on the beach and tries to avoid mob strongmen.
