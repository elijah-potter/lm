A bold decision, like a leap of faith in the dark,
where every doubt is a shadow, every hesitation a weight—
the wind howling through the branches of possibility,
each step a gamble against the certainty of the known.

The hesitation isn’t fear, but the weight of what could be:
a life unchained, a voice unheard, a future uncarved.
The hands that tremble are not weak, but the ones that hold back
the tension of "what if" echoing like thunder in the chest.

The leap isn’t reckless—it’s the quiet surrender to the unknown,
where the only rule is this: you must trust that the ground beneath you
doesn’t vanish when you let go. And sometimes, the earth does hold.

The scars will come. The victories will be won in the cracks.
The question isn’t whether you’ll fall—it’s whether you’ll rise.
