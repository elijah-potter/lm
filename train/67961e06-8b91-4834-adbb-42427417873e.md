A routine shift change at the warehouse: the forklift driver’s foot slipped on the worn-out mat, but instead of a fall, they steadied mid-air, landing softly on their knees. No one noticed the effort—just the way the pallet they’d been pushing now sat slightly off-center, slightly higher, just enough to prevent it from sliding into a stack below. A missed chance for an extra hour’s work, but the weight of the load stayed in place. That small, unnoticed correction carried the day.
