You shouldn’t be here. The neon glow of the alley flickered through rain-streaked windows, casting long shadows that stretched like skeletal fingers across the pavement. The air smelled of damp concrete and something older—burnt oil, rust, and the faint metallic tang of old blood. Your pulse hammered in your throat, but you forced yourself to stay still, hands clenched at your sides.

A door creaked open behind you. The sound was deliberate, almost amused. A figure stepped into the dim light—a woman in a leather jacket, her dark eyes reflecting the same unnatural glow as the alley. She tilted her head, studying you like a puzzle she’d already solved.

"You’re not supposed to be here," she said, voice smooth as oil. "But then, neither am I." Her fingers traced the edge of a pistol holstered at her waist. "Careful. You’re about to learn why."
