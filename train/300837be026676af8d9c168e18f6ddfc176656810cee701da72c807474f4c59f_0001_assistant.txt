The father/daughter dance is one of the most special moments of many wedding ceremonies, and if there isn't already a special song that means something to one or both of them, here are some suggestions:

• "Daughter" by Loudon Wainwright III
Notable lyric: "That's my daughter in the water/Every time she fell I caught her"

• "Constellations" by Jack Johnson
Notable lyric: "To give us just enough light/To lay down underneath the stars listening to Papa's translations of the stories across the sky"

• "Daddy" by Beyoncé
Notable lyric: "Can't stop my tears from falling/I love you so much, daddy"

• "You Raise Me Up" by Josh Groban
Notable lyric: "I am strong when I am on your shoulders/You raise me up to more than I can be"
