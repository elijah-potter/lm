The walrus (also known as Odobenus rosmarus) is a mammalian, marine animal commonly found in areas of the Arctic Ocean, and the seas of the Northern Hemisphere. Two subdivisions exist within its species, the Pacific walrus, and the Atlantic walrus. They are known primarily for their impressive girth, lengthy tusks, and facial whiskers. Walruses can live to be roughly 30 years of age in the wild, and males of the species can even weigh over 4,000 pounds! Their diet consists of marine life such as mollusks and shrimp.
