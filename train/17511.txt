This course expands on previously learned principles and incorporates more advanced issues such as organization, hierarchy, layout and grid systems. Students will begin to investigate ways to communicate content and meaning of text.
The topic that I chose in this project is "Love Your Dog Forever". The idea that I wanted to convey was once you have them, you should take care of them. Don't let them become a wandering dog or a dog without people caring. I used warm and bright colors throughout the whole project because the interaction between human and dog is warm and lively. Imagery that I selected also focuses on people and their pets because I wanted to emphasize their emotion and communication.
