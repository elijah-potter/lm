As the days get brighter and warmer, it's time to start thinking about fresh spring salads! Salads are a great way to enjoy the flavors of the season while still eating healthy-ish. Here are some ideas for delicious spring salads you can make at home: 

1. Arugula & Strawberry Salad – Start with a bed of arugula, then top with the sweetest spring strawberries you can find. Sprinkle in some feta or goat cheese crumbles, and your favorite nuts (chopped walnuts if you have them), and drizzle in balsamic vinaigrette dressing with lots of cracked black pepper. This salad will surprise you with just how savory strawberries can taste! 

2. Kale & Quinoa Salad – Start with quickly cooked quinoa and mix in kale leaves that have been massaged in olive oil, To this base add diced tomatoes, cucumbers, and red onion slices. This salad is inspired by a classic Greek salad, but fresher and brighter. Top off this hearty salad with a lemony vinaigrette and add your favorite nuts or seeds like sunflower or pumpkin seeds for extra crunchiness!  You could even mix in some spicy pickled peppers if you have them.

3. Avocado Caprese Salad - Layer slices of avocado on top of baby spinach leaves alongside cherry tomatoes cut in half lengthwise; add mozzarella balls if desired before topping off this colorful dish with freshly squeezed lemon juice as well as salt & pepper to taste. Enjoy cold or warm up under the broiler until the cheese melts slightly—either way, it’s sure to be delicious!  

These three simple recipes will help you create flavorful dishes that will leave everyone asking “What’s for dinner?” So don't wait any longer — head out into your garden today so you can start making these tasty salads tonight!
