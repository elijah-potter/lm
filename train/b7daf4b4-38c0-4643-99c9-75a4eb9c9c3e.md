A hush descends like a sigh,
drums of city fade into silence,
breath held in the stillness—
soft rain patters as the mind drifts,
a single candle flickers against the wall,
the weight of unspoken thoughts
settling like dust in the corner of the room,
time stretching into a quiet, endless pause.

Echoes of laughter fade into the hum of distant traffic,
a single bird sings against the glass,
the world breathes in slow, deliberate cycles—
peace, not absence, but the rare, fragile stillness
where even the smallest things feel like miracles.
