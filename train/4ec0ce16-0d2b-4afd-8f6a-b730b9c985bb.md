A technician in a dimly lit factory noticed an unusual vibration in a conveyor belt system during routine checks. The machine had been running smoothly for months, but the hum had intensified to a low, rhythmic drone. Upon closer inspection, they found a misaligned roller bearing—previously overlooked due to the system’s stability. The moment of recognition came when the bearing’s contact points on the motor shaft were slightly misaligned, causing uneven torque distribution and excessive friction. The fix was simple: realigning the bearing resolved the vibration, restoring the system to optimal operation. The lesson was clear: sometimes, the smallest adjustments can reveal critical issues when overlooked.
