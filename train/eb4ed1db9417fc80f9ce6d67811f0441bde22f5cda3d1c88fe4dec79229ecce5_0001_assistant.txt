I'm gonna miss Maxwells. Chris Stamey once told me, “Maxwell’s felt like a Portkey in Harry Potter." Stamey was one of the residents at the club, so I know he and many others are mourning this great venue's passing. You know, it was one of the major New York City rock clubs back in the day. It was just as great as Max's Kansas City, CBGB, or the Bottom Line. It ranked as high as any of those and we weren't even in New York! Technically, we were in Hoboken, New Jersey which was known, mainly, for being Frank Sinatra's birthplace. In fact, when I started booking these small bands 35 years ago, that's all it was known for! Anyway, on July 31st, after a few nights of weird music from the good old days, I'm having the first couple of bands to ever perform at Maxwell's play a final show before we shut it down forever.
