A candle flickered beside the half-empty glass, its wick bending toward the ceiling like a whispered promise. Outside, the wind rattled the shutters of the old bookshop, carrying the scent of aged paper and distant laughter. Inside, the shelves groaned under the weight of forgotten tomes, each one a potential key to secrets long buried. The woman at the counter—her fingers stained with ink—leaned forward, her breath fogging the glass. "This book," she murmured, "contains the name of the man who promised you everything. But beware: the price is not in gold, nor silver, nor blood—it’s in the silence between your words and your tears." The shop door creaked open, letting in a gust that carried the ghost of a child’s giggle, half-sung, half-forgotten.
