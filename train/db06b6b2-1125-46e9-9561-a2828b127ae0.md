**The Last Transmission**
Static hissed through the cracked speakers of the old radio in the abandoned barn. A distorted voice crackled: *"You are not alone. Listen closely."* The wind howled through the rusted metal, carrying whispers of the dead. The farmer’s wife, Elena, clutched her son’s hand as the message repeated—*"The Hollow Men… they hunger for the living."* Outside, the barn doors groaned as unseen fingers scraped against the wood. The next words were not spoken but *felt*: *"They are in your dreams now."* The radio cut to silence, leaving only the creaking floorboards and the slow, rhythmic heartbeat of the earth beneath them. The first shadow stretched across the floor.
