Television and film writers are striking due to their working conditions and wages. The WGA has stated that this is largely due to the rise of streaming services such as Netflix. The WGA, representing the writers, and the Alliance of Motion Picture and Television Producers, representing the studios, had previously been trying to negotiate a deal. They were unable to agree, and so the writers began striking on May 2nd. According to an anonymous source, Netflix was stated to be the worst studio to work for by the writer's union.
