Smooth obsidian floors glint under a low, amber-hued light, their reflective surface catching flecks of molten glass. The walls curve like petrified bone, their surface etched with ancient, geometric runes that shift subtly in temperature—some parts warm to the touch, others cool, as if alive. A thick, warm wool blanket lies in a creeping patch of moss, its edges frayed at the edges where it has been trodden or washed by unseen hands. Beneath the blanket, damp earth and rotting leaves form a soft, damp mattress. A single iron nail protrudes from a wooden beam overhead, rusted but still strong enough to support the weight of a sleeping figure. The air hums with static, thick with the scent of damp stone and old iron. Strange, smooth veins of what might be polished metal run through the walls, pulsing faintly, like veins of light. A low, resonant growl echoes through hollow chambers, the sound of something vast and ancient stirring beneath the floor.
