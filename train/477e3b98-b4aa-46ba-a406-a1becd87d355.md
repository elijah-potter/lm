Once upon a time, in a big, cozy house with a garden full of flowers, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white fur, big round ears, and a tail that wiggled when he was happy.

Every morning, Benny would hop outside to see what the garden had to offer. One sunny day, Benny found a tiny, shiny acorn on the ground. He picked it up and wondered what it would grow into. As he nibbled on it, he heard a tiny voice.

"Help me, Benny! My seeds are buried deep in the soil."

Benny looked down and saw a little seedling with bright green leaves waving in the breeze. Benny knew just what to do. He dug a little hole and carefully planted the seed. He watered it gently with a tiny dropper made from his carrot.

Days passed, and Benny watched his little friend grow. Soon, the seed turned into a tiny plant with soft leaves. Benny was so proud! He sang to it, "Grow tall, grow strong, grow big and bright!"

Soon, the plant grew tall with big, yellow flowers that sparkled in the sun. Benny was overjoyed. He gave it a big hug and said, "You did it! You grew from a little seed to a beautiful flower!"

The garden was now filled with Benny’s special flowers, and everyone in the neighborhood loved them. Benny felt so happy, he knew he had made the best friend ever.

That evening, as Benny curled up in his nest of leaves, he dreamed of more seeds and more adventures with his new friend. And so, the story of Benny’s magical garden grew from a little seed, just like his own dreams.
