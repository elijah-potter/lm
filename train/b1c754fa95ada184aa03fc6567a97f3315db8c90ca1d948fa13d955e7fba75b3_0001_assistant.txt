It is the year 2200, Earth has been abandoned due to catastrophic climate change and overpopulation. The surviving humans have fled to a new planet, dubbed "New Eden," located in a distant solar system. New Eden is a world that orbits a binary star system, causing dramatic and unpredictable weather patterns, from deadly radiation storms to beautiful auroras that light up the sky. The planet is divided into two hemispheres, one permanently bathed in sunlight and the other in perpetual darkness. The colonists have built towering cities that rise above the clouds, connected by massive, spider-like bridges that stretch for miles. 

The cities are powered by advanced fusion reactors and have self-sustaining ecosystems that provide food and water for the inhabitants. However, life on New Eden is far from easy, as the planet is teeming with alien life forms that are both dangerous and wondrous, and the colonists must constantly struggle to survive in this new and unpredictable world. There is an additional planet in the solar system, New Nod, that is also capable of sustaining life, although no colonies have been set up there yet.
