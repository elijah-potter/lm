I created this packet to supplement a study of the vocabulary words within the text "The Man Who Named the Clouds" from Reading Street Unit 3, Week 1 for Fourth Grade. Included is a pre-assessment of student knowledge of the words, a list of words with definitions, a graphic organizer where students can record sentences from the text and create their own for each vocabulary word, a memory game for practice, and a template where students can illustrate their interpretation of each vocabulary word. I hope that this helps someone. I also hope that each person that downloads this product leaves feedback that I can use to help me create future products. Thank you!
