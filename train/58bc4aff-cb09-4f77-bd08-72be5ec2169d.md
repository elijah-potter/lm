A hush settled like the afterglow of a storm,
silence humming with the weight of forgotten sound—
distant hum of a fridge, a clock’s slow tick,
moonlight bleeding through curtains, soft and warm.

The world breathes in slow, deliberate waves,
each breath a pause from the chaos before.
A child’s giggle fades into the stillness,
then a single bird’s chirp, a fleeting spark in the dark.

Time dissolves, no longer measured in haste,
only the slow, quiet pulse of existence—
the air itself holding its breath,
waiting for the next storm to pass.
