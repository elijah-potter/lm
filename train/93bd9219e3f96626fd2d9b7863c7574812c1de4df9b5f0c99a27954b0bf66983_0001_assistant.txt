Not to worry, here is a list of six games you can get for your child to play on their gaming PC:

1. Among Us: This is a problem-solving multiplayer game where a group must work together to figure out which player is tasked with taking the others out. It is rated for ages ten and up.
2. Cuphead: Cuphead is a visually stunning game with a theme of 1930s cartoons. It is known to be an extremely challenging side scroller. 
3. Disney Dreamlight Valley: Disney borrowed the playbook that The Sims wrote and added their own flair by introducing Disney characters in the game. 
4. Fortnite: The quintessential video game of the current era, or possibly all of video game history. A must-have for any gamer, provided they are in the Teenage age range.
5. Lego Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga: This game can be enjoyed even if you aren't a star wars or Lego fan. It combines all the star wars films into one exciting journey.
6. Minecraft: Minecraft is a sandbox game where a player can build anything they can imagine. Players must mine and allocate resources in the process.
