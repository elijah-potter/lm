A dimly lit office, fluorescent lights buzzing softly. The door creaks open slightly, and **Daniel** steps inside, arms crossed, head tilted slightly, fingers tracing idle circles on the desk.

**"Yeah, sure. That sounds like a great idea."**

He exhales through his nose, lips pressed into a thin line, but his eyes flick toward the corner where **Emma** sits, arms folded, a laptop open between them. The monitor glows with a spreadsheet titled *"Quarterly Budget Adjustments."*

**"Actually, I was thinking more along the lines of—"**

Emma’s fingers pause mid-click on the keyboard. She turns her head just enough to study Daniel’s posture, the way his jaw tightens, the unnatural stillness of his body language.

**"Oh, like what?"** Her voice is calm, but her tone hints at skepticism. **"Because I’m all ears."**

Daniel’s expression shifts—just for a second—before he forces a neutral smile. **"Well, you know how we’re hitting our targets…"** He gestures vaguely toward the screen, where Emma’s fingers are already hovering over a cell marked *"Profit Margin: 9.2%."*

Emma nods, but her gaze lingers on the desk. **"Right. And then?"**

A beat. The hum of the office fades into the background.

**"And then we’ll see what happens next."** Daniel’s voice drops to a whisper. **"But yeah. I’m listening."**
