Myanmar - Protect children with landmines awareness
In Myanmar, children sometimes play with landmines because they do not know how dangerous they are. In the first nine months of 2020 alone, 13 children died and 56 were severely injured by explosions of landmines and unexploded ordnance in Myanmar (UNICEF, 2020). Children should not have to know how to identify and avoid landmines, but here, such awareness can be life-saving.
