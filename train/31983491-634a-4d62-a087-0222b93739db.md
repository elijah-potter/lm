The dim glow of a flickering tablet cast long shadows across the cracked wooden floor. A cup of steaming coffee sat untouched beside the armchair, its condensation tracing a slow spiral. The door creaked open, sealing in the damp chill of the night. A single step echoed, followed by the soft shuffle of boots.

"She’s in."

The voice was thick with relief, but the words still carried the weight of exhaustion. The person—hands trembling slightly—reached for the tablet, fingers brushing the screen. A notification blinked: *"Update: Mission status confirmed."*

They exhaled, the breath curling in the cold air. A phone buzzed in their pocket, another alert. *"New details available."* The screen filled with lines of code and coordinates, a map pulsing with real-time data. A notification popped up: *"All systems operational."*

Silence. The weight of what was coming settled over them, heavy as a lead blanket. The coffee cup slipped from their fingers, landing with a quiet *clink* on the floor. A single tear slipped down their cheek, unnoticed. The door closed behind them, the world outside the window still silent, waiting.
