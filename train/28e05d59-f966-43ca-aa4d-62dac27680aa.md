A **paper-based contingency plan** outlining:
1. **Phase 1: Immediate Response** – Emergency protocols (evacuation, lockdown) with 24/7 callouts to crisis teams.
2. **Phase 2: Coordination** – Task-force assignments (IT, logistics, security) via digital dashboards linked to physical floor plans.
3. **Phase 3: Post-Incident Review** – Structured debriefs with annotated reports, risk-assessment tools, and KPIs tied to performance dashboards.
4. **Documentation** – Digital workflows for real-time updates, automated alerts, and encrypted archives for compliance audits.
5. **Stakeholder Roles** – Clear sign-off chains (C-suite, regional leads) with audit trails for accountability.
6. **Simulations** – Quarterly tabletop exercises (documented in *Incident Simulator Logs*) to validate procedures.
7. **Amendments** – Version control via Git-like workflows (e.g., `plan-2024-v1.3`) with change logs in shared drives.

**Key:** All steps mapped to **shared digital ledgers** (e.g., Notion, Airtable) for live tracking.
