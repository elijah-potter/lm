The Whispering Library of Eldermere, an ancient wooden building with arched ceilings and floor-to-ceiling bookshelves draped in ivy. The air is thick with the scent of aged parchment and damp earth. Shadows stretch long across the floor, where lanterns flicker softly in brass sconces. At the center stands a grand circular table, its surface etched with a sigil of intertwined whispers. Around it, people sit in hushed silence, their lips moving just enough to form words that dissolve into the mist of the room. The walls, lined with enchanted mirrors, reflect not images, but echoes of voices—some familiar, others lost in time. The whispers drift between them, forming stories that shift depending on who listens.
