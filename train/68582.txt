A Veterinary Ombudsman or Ombudsperson is a neutral third party facilitator who provides confidential and impartial assistance in resolving hostility, unfair treatment, injustice, resentment, and hard feelings. The Veterinary Ombudsman works diligently in an advisory role to resolve hostility between individuals, businesses, and consumers association. The Ombudsman can represents any individual, organization, consumers association that is affected by the hostility situation, and has either a direct or indirect interest in the outcome of the Veterinary unfair treatment resolution.
The word Ombudsman dates back to 1809 when the Swedish Parliament created a new official known as the Justitie-Ombudsman, referring to public officials appointed to investigate citizen hostility, unfair treatment. Ombudsman loosely translates as citizen defender or representative of the people.
The role of the Veterinary Ombudsman is to identify potential areas of conflict, wrongdoing, unfair treatment and provides information on individual rights. The Veterinary Ombudsman principal activity is to investigate and suggest solutions to resolve conflicts. The Ombudsman is trained in dispute resolution and understands how to resolve situations before they become harmful confrontations. A designated neutral person who provides confidential and informal assistance in resolving Veterinary conflicts. The rights and interests of all parties receive equal consideration. The Veterinary Ombudsman is an advocate for fairness and the equitable treatment.
