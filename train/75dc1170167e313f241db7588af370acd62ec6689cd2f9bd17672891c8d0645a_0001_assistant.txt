A pair of children, Jack and Jill, have decided to travel up a fairly steep hill in order to remove a bucket of water from the well located at its apex. The children were determined and hiked upward toward their goal, finally arriving at their destination to retrieve the water from the well. After filling the bucket, perhaps somewhat too full, Jack preceded Jill as the pair began to make their descent. Jack lost his footing and despite his best efforts, fell wildly down the hill, suffering a traumatic head wound. Jill, aghast at Jack's fall, misstepped and also tumbled, falling to join Jack at the base of the hill.
