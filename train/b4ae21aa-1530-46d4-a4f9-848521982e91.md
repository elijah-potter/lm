## Project Chimera: A Cooperative Research Game

**A Rulebook for 2-4 Players**

**Welcome, Researchers.** Project Chimera thrusts you into the roles of specialists at the Global Research Initiative (GRI), a clandestine organization dedicated to mitigating existential threats. A rapidly evolving, synthetic virus – Chimera – has emerged, exhibiting unprecedented adaptability and virulence. Your mission is to understand Chimera’s mutations, develop countermeasures, and ultimately contain the escalating global crisis before it overwhelms civilization. Success demands meticulous research, strategic resource allocation, and unwavering collaboration.

### I. Components

*   **Game Board:** A world map depicting major global regions, each with associated risk levels and research potential.
*   **Chimera Cards (80):** Representing the virus’s evolving mutations, categorized into four types: Transmission, Virulence, Resistance, and Adaptation.
*   **Research Cards (60):** Representing potential research pathways and technological advancements.
*   **Resource Tokens:** Representing Funding (blue), Personnel (green), and Data (yellow).
*   **Containment Markers:** Used to track containment levels in affected regions.
*   **Crisis Track:** A track indicating the overall severity of the crisis.
*   **Region Markers:** Indicate current risk levels in each region (Low, Moderate, High, Critical).
*   **Player Pawns (4):** Representing the research team members.
*   **Player Role Cards (4):** Defining unique player abilities and starting resources.
*   **Dice (3):** Six-sided dice used for research checks and crisis progression.
*   **Rulebook (this document).**

### II. Setup

1.  **Place the Game Board:** Position the game board in the center of the playing area.
2.  **Shuffle and Place Decks:** Shuffle the Chimera and Research card decks separately and place them face down on the designated spaces on the board.
3.  **Crisis Track:** Place the Crisis marker on the starting space of the Crisis Track.
4.  **Region Markers:** Place Region Markers on each region according to the initial setup indicated on the board.
5.  **Resource Pool:** Create a resource pool of Funding, Personnel, and Data tokens beside the board.
6.  **Player Roles:** Shuffle the Player Role cards and deal one to each player. Each player receives the starting resources indicated on their Role card.
7.  **Player Placement:** Each player places their pawn on GRI Headquarters (designated space on the board).

### III. Turn Structure

Each player’s turn proceeds in the following phases:

1.  **Draw Chimera Card:** Draw the top Chimera card and place it face up. The card’s effects immediately apply, potentially increasing risk levels in affected regions, advancing the Crisis Track, or introducing new challenges.
2.  **Action Phase (3 Actions):** You may perform up to three actions. Actions can be any combination of the following:
    *   **Move:** Move your pawn to an adjacent region.
    *   **Research:** Spend Data and Personnel to attempt a Research check. Roll two dice. The target number depends on the Research card’s difficulty and any regional modifiers. Success allows you to claim the Research card and gain its benefits.
    *   **Contain:** Spend Funding and Personnel to attempt to reduce the risk level in a region. Roll one die. The target number depends on the region’s current risk level. Success places a Containment Marker in the region.
    *   **Gather Resources:** Spend an action to gain two tokens of a single resource type.
    *   **Share Resources:** Give one or two tokens of a single resource type to another player in the same region.
3.  **Crisis Phase:** Draw a Crisis card. Resolve its effects, which typically involve advancing the Crisis Track and potentially introducing negative consequences.
4.  **Pass the Turn:** The turn passes to the next player clockwise.

### IV. Win and Loss Conditions

**Winning the Game:**

You win the game if you successfully develop and deploy a Cure (a specific combination of Research cards) *and* the Crisis Track is not at its maximum level.

**Losing the Game:**

You lose the game if:

*   The Crisis Track reaches its maximum level.
*   All Containment Markers are removed from the board.
*   You are unable to draw a Chimera card (deck is depleted).

### V. Mechanics of Trade-off and Uncertainty

1.  **Resource Scarcity:** Resource tokens are limited. Players must frequently decide whether to prioritize research, containment, or resource gathering, often leading to difficult choices that impact the team's overall progress. The “Share Resources” action is crucial, but requires careful negotiation and trust.
2.  **Mutation Roulette:** Chimera cards are drawn randomly. Each card presents a unique and often unpredictable threat. Players must adapt their strategies on the fly, reacting to unforeseen challenges and potential setbacks. This necessitates flexibility and a willingness to abandon pre-conceived plans.
3.  **Research Prioritization:** Research cards offer various benefits, but pursuing one path might leave other critical areas neglected. The cost of each Research card (Data and Personnel) is significant. Players must collectively decide which research avenues to pursue, knowing that some choices might leave them vulnerable to future Chimera mutations.

### VI. Difficulty Scaling

*   **2 Players:** Reduce the starting Crisis Track level by one space. Increase the starting number of Resource tokens by two of each type.
*   **3 Players:** Play with the standard setup described in the Setup section.
*   **4 Players:** Increase the starting Crisis Track level by one space. Reduce the starting number of Resource tokens by one of each type.

### VII. Advanced Variants

*   **Specialist Focus:** Assign each player a specific area of expertise (e.g., Virology, Epidemiology, Logistics) and grant them unique starting bonuses and ongoing abilities related to that area.
*   **Global Politics:** Introduce a deck of Event cards that represent political interference and international pressure, adding layers of complexity and forcing players to navigate delicate diplomatic situations.
*   **Accelerated Mutation:** Increase the rate at which the Crisis Track advances, creating a more frantic and challenging gameplay experience.



### Designer’s Note

Project Chimera was designed to be more than just a game; it’s an exercise in collaborative problem-solving under pressure. The game's mechanics are intentionally designed to highlight the challenges inherent in responding to global crises – the need for clear communication, the importance of strategic resource allocation, and the inevitability of making difficult choices with imperfect information. The educational goal is to foster an appreciation for the complexities of scientific research, the value of interdisciplinary collaboration, and the importance of responsible decision-making in the face of existential threats. The random nature of the Chimera mutations and the limited resources are intended to mimic the unpredictable and often overwhelming nature of real-world challenges.
