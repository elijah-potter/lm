The **Mystic Hollow** gathering hub is a cavernous, circular space with no distinct purpose—just a throng of visitors stumbling through mist-laden tunnels and circular stone platforms. The air hums with unspoken chatter, yet no one argues over why they’re here. The walls, carved with abstract symbols, seem to shift under faint, rhythmic light. Some claim it’s a sanctuary for the lost, others whisper it’s a trap. The most common response is: *"We just felt drawn here."* The only rule is *"No questions, no answers."* The center features a flickering, floating orb that pulses when the crowd’s mood shifts. No maps, no schedules—only the endless, unexplainable pull of the place.
