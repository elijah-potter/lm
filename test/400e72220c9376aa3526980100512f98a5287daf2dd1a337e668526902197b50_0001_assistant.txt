Have you guys ever seen a walrus before? No? Here’s a picture of one. 

Walruses are mammals. And you can see that they have very long tusks, which look like teeth but are actually used to break holes in the ice so they can breathe! Walruses also use their tusks to help haul themselves out of the water onto the ice and even to fight other walruses! 

So where do you think walruses live? That’s right, in and around the ice. In fact, walruses migrate, which means they move with ice floes. But walruses never move too far away from land because that’s where their food lives. They eat mollusks and other animals that don’t have backbones. 

They can go deep into the icy water to eat. Some walruses go as deep as 20-30 meters, which is 100 feet, or almost 20 adults long! 

In the Arctic, people have always used walrus meat to eat, and they carve the tusks to make art and trade them with other people.
