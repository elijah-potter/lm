1. The Rise of Tiamat: The players must stop Tiamat, the five-headed dragon goddess of tyranny. She is ascending to power and preparing to unleash her wrath on the world.

2. The Curse of Strahd: The players must find a way to lift the curse of Strahd von Zarovich, the vampire lord who rules the land of Barovia.

3. Out of the Abyss: The players must escape from the Underdark, a dark and dangerous underground world, and return to the surface.

4. Storm King's Thunder: The players must stop the Storm King, an ancient giant who is threatening to conquer the world.

5. Waterdeep: Dungeon of the Mad Mage: The players must explore the Undermountain, a vast and dangerous dungeon beneath Waterdeep, and stop a mage who has gone mad with power.
