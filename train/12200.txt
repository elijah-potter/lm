In the ways of the ancients, she found a hope for the future.
Whale Rider is the moving story of young Paikea, the descendant of Whale Rider and grand-daughter of the chief Koro, and her struggle between pleasing her grandfather and fulfilling her destiny.
Whale Rider, the winner of numerous awards, is a film of rare beauty and timeless appeal, focuses on family, growing up and the conflict between traditional and modern cultures.
Photocopiable Resource Pack - Overview
Whale Rider: Photocopiable Resource Pack - Elementary is highly recommended for Elementary, and is also suitable for Pre-Intermediate and Intermediate learners. It contains four lessons of up to 3.5 hours each, that accompany four sections of the movie Whale Rider.
This ESL Movie Resource Title includes a wide variety of pre-viewing, viewing and post-viewing activities, as well as easy-to-follow lesson plans and a comprehensive answer key.
The photocopiable activities in this Resource Pack include:
- Pre-Viewing: jigsaw reading activity, vocabulary activities, discussion review activities, vocabulary review activities, DVD review activities
- Viewing: worksheets with a range of activities including gap-fill activities, discussion activities, true/false activities
- Optional Viewing: supplementary photocopiable activities such as memory quizzes, listening and ordering activities, dialogue gap-fill activities and discussion activities
- Post-Viewing: photocopiable Writing Tasks and discussion activities, with topics related to themes within the movie section
The Maps and Lesson Plans provide detailed notes for preparing and using each activity.
