Sunshine in the afternoons, hitting the colored leaves just right and setting the whole world aflame in their glow.
Embers of the fire, as we draw in closer against the encroaching cool of the night. 
Picnic for Labor Day signals the end of the summer. 
Teachers ready their classrooms for the return of students, alert and eager to begin. 
Every afternoon, the sun says goodbye a little earlier. 
Mornings are cool and crisp, making strong, dark coffee a welcome greeting. 
Bears eat the last of the blackberries, preparing for the long winter ahead. 
Each day more leaves turn and then begin to fall. 
Remember: spring will come again.
