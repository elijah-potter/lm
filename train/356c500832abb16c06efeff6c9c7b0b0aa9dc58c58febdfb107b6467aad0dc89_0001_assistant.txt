The Olmec were one of the earliest Latin America Civilizations to first turn the cacao plant into chocolate. They used chocolate as medicine and also consumed it during rituals.
Centuries later, Mayans mixed roasted and ground cacao seeds, chilies, water, and cornmeal to make "xocolatl" or "bitter water". The Mayans praised chocolate as the drink of the gods.
By the 15th century, cocoa beans were used as a currency by the Aztecs. Chocolate was believed to be a gift from the god Quetzalcoatl. They drank it as a refreshing beverage, an aphrodisiac, and even as a preparation for war.
