A dimly lit alley in a city at dawn, mist clinging to cobblestones. Shadows stretch long and jagged, obscuring the figure approaching from the shadows—a woman in a tattered coat, her breath visible in the cold air. She carries a satchel slung over one shoulder, its contents hidden. The alley splits ahead, one path leading to a dimly lit building, the other to a crumbling fence. Her steps are deliberate, each one deliberate, as if counting every second. A distant horn echoes from the street, but she doesn’t look back. The weight of the past lingers in her movements, a silent promise of what comes next.
