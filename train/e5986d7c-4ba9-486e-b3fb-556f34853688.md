A floating garden hub in a geodesic dome, suspended 100m above a misty valley. Hydroponic towers grow seasonal crops year-round, their nutrient-rich water recycled through bioluminescent algae ponds that glow faintly at night. Pathways of translucent glass bridge between floating platforms, where the air is perpetually warm and the scent of jasmine lingers from solar-powered vertical farms. Locals trade in zero-waste markets, their conversations blending in a mix of dialects from the surrounding lowlands and the distant mountains. The dome’s outer skin shifts between opaque and translucent via embedded nano-filters, revealing skylights that show only filtered light or star-streaked voids at dusk.
