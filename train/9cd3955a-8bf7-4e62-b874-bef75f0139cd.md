## Project Chimera: Rulebook

**A Cooperative Game of Scientific Crisis Management**

Welcome to Project Chimera. In this game, you and your fellow researchers are the last line of defense against a rapidly evolving biological anomaly threatening global stability. Your expertise in virology, genetics, and environmental science are all that stands between humanity and collapse. Success demands meticulous planning, courageous decision-making, and unwavering collaboration.

### 1. Components

*   **The World Board:** A map depicting key global regions, each with varying levels of vulnerability and resource availability.
*   **Researcher Cards (8):** Each card represents a specialized researcher with unique abilities and starting resources.
*   **Anomaly Cards (60):** Represent the escalating biological threat, detailing its effects and required research to contain.
*   **Event Cards (40):** Introduce unexpected complications and opportunities, impacting the global situation.
*   **Research Tokens (120):** Represent progress in various scientific disciplines.
*   **Containment Tokens (40):** Used to mitigate the spread of the Anomaly.
*   **Resource Tokens (100):** Represent funding, personnel, and equipment.
*   **Crisis Track:** A track indicating the global severity of the Anomaly.
*   **Researcher Pawns (4):** Used to track researcher locations on the World Board.
*   **Dice (2 x six-sided):** Used for resolving certain actions and events.
*   **Rulebook (this document).**

### 2. Setup

1.  Place the World Board in the center of the playing area.
2.  Shuffle the Anomaly and Event card decks and place them face down near the board.
3.  Place the Crisis Track marker at its starting position.
4.  Each player chooses a Researcher Card and takes the corresponding Researcher Pawn and starting resources as indicated on their card.
5.  Place the Research and Containment Tokens in a general supply.
6.  Draw the first three Anomaly cards and place them face up next to the Anomaly deck. These represent the initial threats.

### 3. Turn Structure

Each turn consists of the following phases, completed in order:

1.  **Anomaly Progression:** Draw one Anomaly card and place it face up next to the Anomaly deck. If the number of face-up Anomaly cards exceeds five, discard the oldest card. Increase the Crisis Track marker by the amount indicated on the drawn Anomaly card.
2.  **Event Phase:** Draw one Event card and resolve its effects immediately. Some events may trigger ongoing conditions.
3.  **Researcher Actions:** Each player, in clockwise order, performs two actions. Possible actions include:
    *   **Move:** Move your Researcher Pawn to an adjacent region on the World Board.
    *   **Research:** Spend Resource Tokens to place Research Tokens on an Anomaly card. The type of Research Token required is indicated on the card.
    *   **Contain:** Spend Resource Tokens and place Containment Tokens on a region to reduce the Anomaly's spread.
    *   **Gather Resources:** Collect Resource Tokens from your current region. The amount and type of resources available are indicated on the World Board.
    *   **Trade:** Exchange Resource Tokens with another player in the same region.
    *   **Assist:** Provide another player in the same region with one Resource Token of your choice.
4.  **Crisis Resolution:** After all players have completed their actions, check the Crisis Track. If the marker reaches the end of the track, the game is lost.

### 4. Mechanics of Uncertainty and Trade-offs

Project Chimera incorporates several mechanics designed to force difficult decisions and strategic trade-offs:

1.  **Anomaly Mutation:** Whenever three or more Research Tokens are placed on a single Anomaly card, roll a die. On a result of 1 or 2, the Anomaly mutates. Draw a new Anomaly card and discard the mutated card. This represents the Anomaly evolving to resist research efforts, potentially creating new vulnerabilities. This forces players to consider diversifying their research efforts.
2.  **Resource Scarcity:** Regions offer varying types and amounts of resources. A region experiencing a high Anomaly presence will yield fewer resources, creating a conflict between immediate containment and long-term research. Players must decide whether to prioritize short-term containment or invest in research for a potentially longer-term solution.
3.  **Event Card Mitigation:** Some Event cards present opportunities to mitigate their negative effects by spending Resource Tokens. However, these same tokens could be used for research or containment. Players must collectively decide whether the potential benefit of mitigating an event outweighs the opportunity cost.

### 5. Win and Loss Conditions

*   **Win Condition:** The players win the game if they successfully place the required number of Research Tokens on all face-up Anomaly cards *and* the Crisis Track marker is not at the end of its track.
*   **Loss Condition:** The players lose the game if the Crisis Track marker reaches the end of its track *or* they are unable to draw an Anomaly card when required.

### 6. Difficulty Scaling

*   **Two Players:** Reduce the number of Research Tokens required to contain each Anomaly card by one. Start with two Anomaly cards face up.
*   **Three Players:** Play the game as described in the standard rules.
*   **Four Players:** Increase the number of Research Tokens required to contain each Anomaly card by one. Start with three Anomaly cards face up.

### 7. Advanced Variants

*   **Specialist Focus:** Each player is assigned a specific scientific discipline (virology, genetics, environmental science) at the beginning of the game. They receive bonus resources and abilities related to their discipline, but are penalized for actions outside their area of expertise.
*   **Black Market:** Introduce a deck of Black Market cards that offer powerful but risky solutions to the crisis. These cards come at a high cost and may have unforeseen consequences.
*   **Public Awareness:** Introduce a Public Awareness track. Negative events increase public distrust, hindering resource gathering and potentially triggering global unrest.



### Designer’s Note

Project Chimera was designed to foster a deeper understanding of the complexities of scientific crisis management. The game aims to illustrate the importance of collaboration, strategic planning, and the difficult trade-offs inherent in addressing global challenges. The mechanics emphasize the uncertainty of scientific research and the potential for unintended consequences. By confronting these challenges in a simulated environment, players can develop critical thinking skills and appreciate the vital role of scientific expertise in safeguarding humanity.
