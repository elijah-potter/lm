       Primates of Park Avenue sounded like a book that I so desperately wanted to like. It had all of the elements that women like myself find alluring: gossip, decadence, and the age-old good vs evil aspect, where the underdog in me wants the 'good guys' to win in the scenario. That being said, I ended up feeling rather disappointed for the following reasons. Entire chapters were devoted to what seemed to be promotional marketing. Products and services were described so excessively that they consumed page upon page, even entire chapters.  It came across as an infomercial in book form. I couldn't help but wonder if Wednesday had stock in these goods and services. Another disappointing aspect was the political tone that it seemed to take at times. It is so much more pleasant to read from an unbiased author who can keep a neutral tone. I was expecting to be entertained and not indoctrinated.
       If you can get through the wasted pages of words and propaganda, there are some interesting moments in this account of a woman going from an intellectual social circle to one that is shallow and narcissistic.
