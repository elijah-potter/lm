## Lost in the Record Store (Brooklyn, NY) (October 26)

The afternoon light filtering through the windows of Rough Trade felt particularly golden that day, casting a warm glow on the stacks of vinyl. I was browsing the jazz section, attempting to locate a specific Miles Davis record, when I noticed you. You were examining a box of vintage blues albums, your brow furrowed in concentration, and a cascade of auburn hair spilled over your shoulder. There was a quiet intensity about you that I found captivating. We didn't speak, but I distinctly remember the low, comforting hum of the turntable playing a Billie Holiday track, a melancholic soundtrack to the moment. I lingered a bit longer than necessary, hoping for a shared glance or perhaps a casual comment, but you eventually moved on. The scent of aged paper and the subtle, earthy aroma of the records themselves are now inextricably linked to the memory of that brief encounter. I’m hoping this finds its way to you.
