US Education System
The education system in the United States provides funding and support for low-income families. Every child needs access to education and learning opportunities from an early age. Federal funds are used to support families who struggle to afford childcare expenses.
Parents are encouraged to send their kids to prekindergarten and kindergarten so that they can prepare them for school. Children can acquire many skills when they are young that will help them to adjust to the school setting.
Kids in the United States follow a broad curriculum that covers a range of subjects and skills. Children can only study more specialized fields when they reach the end of their high school curriculum. Children can take extra-curricular subjects and programs to supplement their learning and development.
After children have successfully acquired their high school diploma, they can send in an application to a college or university. These college or university programs normally last for a period of 4 years. Every US student can apply for federal funding if they do not have enough money to pursue a degree or qualification.
