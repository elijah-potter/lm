The abandoned warehouse in the outskirts of the city—walls lined with rusted graffiti depicting scenes of failed promises, the same handwriting across each faded mural: *"Here, the future was never built."* The ceiling collapses inward in a pattern of shattered glass and exposed beams, but the most persistent absence is light: no windows remain intact, only shattered panes that reflect nothing but the void of the sky. The floor is a mosaic of discarded technology—overheated servers, cracked monitors, and the skeletal remains of a network that once hummed with data but now whispers only static. Outside, the urban sprawl stretches unbroken, oblivious to the hollowed core where time itself seems to have been erased. The scent of damp metal and old circuit boards lingers, a ghost of what was once a hub of innovation.
