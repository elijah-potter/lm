Hello Everyone, 

I am so very excited to be celebrating this important day in your life with you. This is a day you will remember forever, but maybe not in the way that you expect to.

The people around you, you might forget. What you are wearing and even how you’re feeling will become a distant memory at best. But this day, this moment in your life, you will always remember that this was the moment that everything changed. 

From now on, you are no longer a child. And although I know you don’t feel like children now, trust me, in some time, you will realize that until this moment, that’s exactly what you were. 

Now, it is your time to become who you were meant to be. Of course, I’m not insinuating that it’s going to happen overnight. It will take time, sometimes even many, many years, until you realize what you were learning all along. 

But it all starts now. The next steps that you take will shape who you are. And while this is certainly one of the most important times in your life, it’s also time to make mistakes, as we all know that you learn just as much, if not more, from our mistakes as we do from our successes. 

So off you go! Out into the world. Good luck to each and every one of you. You’re going to do amazing things. In fact, you are going to do absolutely anything you want to do from here on out, for better or for worse.
