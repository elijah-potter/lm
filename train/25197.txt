Great Minds Think About Money
January 2, 2008
"Great Minds Think: A Kids Guide to Money" is a free, self-directed activity book that teaches the basic concepts of financial decision making in a fun way. Covered topics include earning, spending, saving, and opportunity cost. A certificate is included so children can celebrate when they complete the activities. The book is designed for children ages 9–12. To order free copies, e-mail firstname.lastname@example.org or call 216.579.3188.
