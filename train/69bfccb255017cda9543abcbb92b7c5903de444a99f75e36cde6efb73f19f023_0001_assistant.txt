1. John Cabot is known today as Giovanni Caboto in Italian, Zuan Caboto in Venetian, and Jean Cabot in French.
2. When he was in Venice, John Cabot signed his name as "Zuan Chabotto".
3. John Cabot's father was Giulio Caboto.
4. Pedro de Ayala described him in a letter to the Spanish Crown as "another Genoese like Columbus". 
5. In 1476, Cabot was made a citizen of the Republic of Venice.
