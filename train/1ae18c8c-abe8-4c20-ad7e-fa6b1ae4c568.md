Doubt dissolves like mist on dawn—
a quiet weight lifts, not from certainty, but from the weight of *knowing* one’s own questions matter.
The weight of all the unanswered, the half-formed paths, the voices in the dark that whisper *maybe* are not just noise—they are the first cracks in the silence, the seeds of something that might bloom into truth.
Relief isn’t the absence of doubt, but the space it carves out for something else to take root.
The relief is in the breath you hold after the storm—
not because the storm is gone, but because you’re no longer drowning in it alone.
The doubt doesn’t vanish, but it learns to dance instead of drag.
