**Silent Library Gardens**

Fountains in the center provide soothing sounds, and the air hums with the quiet murmur of whispered ideas. Public reading benches are arranged in concentric circles, with one central space reserved for silent meditation. The library’s grand reading room, with its vaulted ceilings and stained-glass windows, is open to all, offering a space where curiosity and contemplation thrive without distraction.
