Witnesses near the northern outskirts report seeing an eerie, pulsating glow near the old observatory ruins, which had stood abandoned since the 19th century. Local historians speculate the phenomenon could be a natural phenomenon, possibly atmospheric reflections, but skeptics suggest it might be tied to the city’s forgotten past.

Residents near the riverfront have reported hearing faint, rhythmic drumming sounds around midnight, though no sources have been identified. Authorities have dispatched a small team of engineers and historians to investigate, but no conclusive evidence has been found yet. The mayor has urged caution, advising citizens to avoid the northern districts at night.

Eldermere’s daily life, meanwhile, continues unabated. The markets near the central plaza remain busy with merchants selling handmade crafts and fresh produce from the city’s fertile farms. Children play in the square, unaware of the strange occurrences unfolding beyond the city walls. The old town’s cobblestone streets, once bustling with activity, now seem strangely serene.
