A technician must manually relocate a 50-foot-long HDPE pipeline buried under a residential street due to a misplaced utility marker that was supposed to indicate a storm drain intersection. The original plan called for an 8-inch diameter pipe, but a small administrative error changed it to 12 inches. The team must excavate a 3-foot-wide trench, relocate the pipeline, and backfill—requiring coordination with city permits, traffic control, and neighborhood residents. The small pipe diameter change led to a 40% increase in excavation volume and doubled the risk of water main conflicts, delaying the project by two weeks.
