Cybersecurity (CS) includes managing risks related to the use, processing, storage, and transmission of information and the systems and processes used for those purposes, including analog and physical form. CS includes information availability, identification and authentication, confidentiality, integrity, and non-repudiation as well as the economic considerations with respect to selection of CS techniques, CS processes, and industry trends.
Password Breaches Becoming More Common in 2019
When making passwords people very often tend to make it something simple to remember, which makes sense. But, in today’s society a password could be critical to life changing accounts, someone could take everything you have just from the click of a button in a different country behind a computer screen. I’m emphasizing greatly that when you make your password to make it something that you and only you will know, also write it down in a place you know is safe so that you don’t forget it. The most common passwords used by people today are “1234”,”12345″,”123456″, etc. But the most common of all is the word “password”.
