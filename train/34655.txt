These resources are designed to introduce proofreading marks to struggling learners. A great visual and hands-on packet for 1st and 2nd graders, it includes a Proofreading Poster (color and b&w available) as well as Proofreading Sentence Strips for hands-on learning.
-- Proofreading Marks Poster (color)
-- Proofreading Marks Poster (b&w)
-- Hands-on Proofreading Strips (color)
-- Hands-on Proofreading Strips (b&w)
-- 3 Fix the Sentences (worksheets)
-- 2 Fix the Paragraphs (worksheets
The Hands-on Proofreading Strips work great as Learning Centers. Students can use the accompanying Square Proofreading Marks to place on the sentence strip as needed. If you choose to laminate the strips, students could also use a dry-erase marker instead or with the Square Proofreading Marks.
5 Worksheets are included to give extra practice to students on using proofreading marks.
