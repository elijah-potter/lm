Hi Dad,

I hope you’re doing well today! I’m writing this letter to you today because something has been on my mind for a while. As you know, I take my responsibilities very seriously—I’m a proud straight-A student and remember to do all my chores. I believe that I am ready to take on a bigger responsibility: a puppy! 

I’ve wanted a puppy for a long time. I know it takes a lot of commitment to properly care for a pet, and I’m ready to take on that responsibility. I’ve used my spare time to research what I need to know as a new puppy parent, such as grooming, exercising, feeding, and potty training! I believe I can give a happy home to a happy dog.

Aside from the obvious emotional benefits a puppy will bring to all of us, I think having a dog around would be a lot of fun! Even if we can’t get a puppy, I would still be happy and compromise for an older dog.

I think it would be a great idea to sit down as a family and discuss cost, time commitment, and more of what I can do to make any process we choose as easy as possible!
