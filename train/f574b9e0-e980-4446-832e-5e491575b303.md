The dimly lit alleyway flickered under the glow of a dying streetlamp, casting long shadows across the cracked pavement. A lone figure, draped in a weathered coat, crouched near a half-buried crate, fingers tracing the rusted edges of a revolver. The city hummed in the distance—a distant siren, the occasional clatter of boots on cobblestones. A child’s laughter echoed from another alley, but it faded into the night. The figure’s breath came slow, tense, eyes fixed on the shadows where a figure moved unseen. A whisper slithered from the darkness: *"You shouldn’t have come."*
