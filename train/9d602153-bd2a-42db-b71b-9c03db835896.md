A minor argument between two coworkers over office supplies escalated when one team member refused to share their last roll of printer paper after noticing the other frequently ran out. The office manager intervened, redirecting the issue to a broader discussion about resource allocation. Over time, tensions rose as each party blamed the other for the initial conflict, leading to a strained work dynamic. The company later addressed the problem by implementing a transparent inventory system and mandatory team meetings to discuss workload distribution.
