The way we perceive reality shifts like the tide—sometimes subtly, other times violently. A single glance can turn the ordinary into the extraordinary when we step outside our usual frames. What was once a wall becomes a bridge when we choose to walk toward it. Perspective isn’t fixed; it’s fluid, a dance between what we see and what we refuse to see.

One day, you might find yourself staring at the same mountain range, only to realize the clouds are now your vantage point. The question isn’t where you are, but where you’re looking. Perspective is the lens through which you filter the world—sometimes clear, sometimes cracked with doubt, but always changing. The only constant is the act of shifting.
