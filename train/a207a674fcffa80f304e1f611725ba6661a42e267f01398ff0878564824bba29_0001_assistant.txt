●Appropriate email address: Make sure your email address is appropriate. There should be no curse words or offensive language, nor should there be inappropriate puns or euphemisms.
●Greeting: An email greeting is how you address and acknowledge the person you're writing to.
●Body: The body of the email is where you write all the important things you want to say.
●Signature: The signature is where you say 'goodbye' and sign your name.
