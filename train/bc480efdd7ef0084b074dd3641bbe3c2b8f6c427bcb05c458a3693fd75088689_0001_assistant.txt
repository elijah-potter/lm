Jeremy was walking on the street when he noticed a very attractive lady walking on the other side. Their eyes met, and *WHOOOOOP* Jeremy slipped on a banana peel! How embarrassing! Jeremy did not even try to get up, he laid on the ground and looked up at the sky. He noticed something in the sky coming down at him. A circular shape grew larger and larger. As he looked up, he couldn't believe his eyes. It was an alien spaceship. No, it was multiple alien spaceships! As Jeremy quickly pulled himself up and started to run for cover, beams rained down from the sky and destroyed roads and cars all around him. Jeremy shoved his way through a spinning entryway and found himself in a hotel. *CRASH* The glass all around the building he had chosen burst and shot inward toward him. He shielded his face with his coat and started stumbling toward the basement. He decided to take the stairs over the elevator. He leaped from one stair platform to another, using the handles on each side to swing and clear each stairway with ease. Jeremy made it to the basement. There was nobody down there, he was alone. He went into a side room with a sturdy-looking door and locked it behind him. He could hear screaming and he could feel the vibrations from the explosions all around him. His room shook and dust fell from the ceiling as he looked around the small-sized concrete room. His only hope was to somehow survive the initial explosions, and then hope that he could squeeze or dig his way out of the rubble. Jeremy sat down on the floor against the wall and thought to himself how he might get out of this alive.
