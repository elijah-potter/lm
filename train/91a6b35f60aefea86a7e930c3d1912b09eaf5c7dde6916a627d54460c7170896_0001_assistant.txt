Blue whales are my favorite animals. I love them because they are beautiful and big. They are the biggest animals in the world. Blue whales are called blue whales because they are blue. They eat a lot of food every day because they are so big. When they eat, they open their mouths and swim into big groups of fish called krill. Then they close their mouths and the fish go into their bellies. Even though I like all kinds of whales, blue whales are my favorite.
