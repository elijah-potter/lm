Damp earth crackles underfoot, a whisper of pine resin mingling with damp moss as the wind tugs through dead leaves. Rustling branches hum with distant birdsong, their calls layered over the low murmur of trickling water. The air thickens with the scent of blooming jasmine and damp soil, interspersed with the sharp tang of rain-soaked stone. The distant clap of thunder echoes like distant thunder, and the faintest hint of smoke lingers—warm, earthy, and slightly bitter—from the campfire’s embers glowing in the dusk.
