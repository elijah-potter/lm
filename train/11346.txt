Tools and Research
- Child Care and Early Education Glossary - The child care & early education glossary defines terms used to describe aspects of child care and early education practice and policy. The glossary defines terms used in conducting social science and policy research
- Child Care Information Exchange (CCIE) – database of articles and research on thousands of early childhood topics (available free to you as a member of this Collaborative)
- Mind in the Making - an unprecedented effort to share the science of children’s learning with the general public, families and professionals who work with children and families.
