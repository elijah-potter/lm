San Antonio, TX - A couple on a second-date hike in the woods above Freewillow, San Antonio have an amazing story to tell their grandchildren. Lenny McGrath, 26, and Sally Jones, 25, are celebrating the find of a lifetime: a new species of bird that has scientists and birdwatchers flying high with excitement.

McGrath and Jones were taking a hike through the mountainous terrain approximately 30 miles north of Freewillow, celebrating their second date, when they came across a small bird with an unusually colored plumage. "It looked like a rainbow-colored Peacock, but one I could fit in my pocket," Jones said of the curious-looking bird. "This was our first time hiking here, and seeing that bird was the cherry on top."

The bird in question has stumped scientists, who believe the couple had stumbled upon a new species of bird from an unknown taxonomy. A spokesperson for the Ornithological Society of Texas said the new species of bird will be documented thoroughly in the coming months to study its behaviors, preferred climates, and possible origin: "To say this is an exciting find is an understatement," said Spokesperson Tracey Dumhill. "Any time a new species of bird is found is an incredible discovery that ultimately leads to many other discoveries that tell us about our world. For instance, since migratory birds usually take with them seeds from their homes to spread across new environments, we can narrow down their locations of origin, which will lead to uncovering how their ecosystems spurned the evolutionary processes that made them."

McGrath and Jones, however, are just as pleased with their second date going well as they were with the revelation that they had discovered a new bird. "I'm just happy Sally likes me, and I think this will be 'Exhibit A' in favor of a third date," McGrath said with a smile on his face.
