

Darren and Colby exchanged blows with their wooden swords, retreating and advancing as the sparring continued. Suddenly, Colby turned his back to his opponent completely as he spun around. Darren immediately jabbed his torso with the wooden sword, earning a point.
"Aw, man," Colby said, taking off his helmet.
"What was that?" Darren said, puzzled.
"What?"
"The spin."
"Oh, yeah," Colby laughed. "It's this cool new technique I've been working on."
"A technique where you turn your back to the opponent?"
"What would you know?" Colby said, snapping his helmet back down and assuming his sparring stance. The two fought again, and Colby began to take the upper hand, pushing Darren back. Then, he spun around again to deliver the final blow. Darren reached his sword forward with a thrust and poked Colby's torso again.
"Oh, come on!" Colby said, visibly frustrated. "Why isn't this working?"
"Maybe because you're making a series of pointless, slow, and predictable movements that give me a tremendous advantage in combat."
"No chance." Colby suddenly launched into a flurry of spins, which Darren effortlessly dodged. All of a sudden, from across the camp came a tremendous yell.
"Colby, knock that off right now!" Colby stopped spinning, still a little dizzy.
