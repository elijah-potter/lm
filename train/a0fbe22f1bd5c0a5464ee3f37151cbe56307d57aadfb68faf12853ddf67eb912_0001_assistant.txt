Barney Holloway is a very serious banker, with a very bad sense of balance. He tends to fall off of chairs in the middle of important meetings, trip over cracks in the sidewalk, and bump into doorways. When he does, he's always flustered and attempts to blame the situation on someone else. He despises frames that aren't hung straight, children, and large breeds of dogs. Barney always wears a brown pinstriped suit with a matching bowler cap that he takes off when he's indoors. He has round wire-rimmed glasses, a narrow gray mustache, and a bald spot at the back of his head.
