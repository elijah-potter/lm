In the dim glow of a flickering streetlamp, Detective Mara Kovač crouched beside the cracked pavement, her breath misting in the cold night air. The cardboard box, half-buried under a pile of discarded newspapers, was the only thing left of the long-lost case of the Vanished Five. Inside, a rusted pocket watch with a broken chain lay among faded receipts and a torn photo of a woman with hollow eyes.

She picked it up, the metal cold against her palm. The watch was heavy, its face etched with the faint numbers of a clock that hadn’t ticked in decades. A single, tiny chime resonated inside, a sound like a whisper from the past. Mara’s fingers traced the engraving: *"To Whom It May Concern: This watch was given to me by a stranger who promised it would never be forgotten."* The paper beneath it was damp, as if soaked in tears.

She turned it over. Beneath the back, a hidden compartment held a single, crumpled letter. The ink was smudged, but the words were clear: *"Find the key. The door will open only when the time is right."* The postmark read *"Three years ago,"* but the date was smudged too, leaving only the faintest ghost of a number: **1978-05-12**.

A chill ran down her spine. The watch had been stolen months ago, but something inside it had changed. The chime wasn’t just a sound—it was a warning. Mara pocketed the box and the watch, her mind racing. The Vanished Five weren’t just gone; they were *waiting*. And this time, the past was coming back to haunt them.
