Once there was a solid white kitty named, Passiontulip. Her fur was always brilliant and clean and very soft. She was a lovely thing with deep blue eyes and a wonderful cat in almost every way.

Passiontulip lived in a green suburban neighborhood with parks and ponds, meadows, and wildlife. She was well-fed and cared for, and she rarely caused any trouble for her owners. She was actually one of the better kitties you have ever seen. 

Passiontulip had many friends around the neighborhood. She and her pals would roam the streets all hours of the day and night, barely catching an afternoon nap. They sometimes got into mischief, though.

One day, Passiontulip woke from her nap, stretched, and wandered out the back door. After pausing to take a few deep breaths, she stealthily moved around the side of the yard, where she found Sparkle, the hummingbird.

“Hey Sparkle, what’s up,” Passion asked gaily.

“Just drinking some of this most excellent elixir, Passion. What’s up with you?”

“I’ve had my nap and am off to play. Have you seen Jim-Jim48?”

“Yes, as a matter of fact, I have. He’s up the road playing Tag with Beaches.”

“Thanks, Sparkle,” Passion called. And she headed up the street at a good pace.
