Someone stands awkwardly in front of a dimly lit boardroom, their hands trembling slightly as they clutch a faded jacket. A single spotlight flickers on them, casting long shadows across the room. A hushed murmur rises from the gathered faces, heads slightly turned, expressions ranging from skeptical to sympathetic. The individual clears their throat, voice barely audible above the hum of the projector playing a looped presentation of their CV. They open their mouth, eyes darting between the crowd, seeking validation in the silence.

*"So... I’m a... uh, former systems analyst with a background in data analytics."* Their words trail off as they realize they’re not being met with the expected nods of understanding. The silence deepens, punctuated by a few scattered questions: *"Data analytics?"* *"Former?"* The tension in the air thickens, the weight of being judged pressing in. They glance at their notes—an outdated résumé and a scribbled list of skills that feel outdated now. The moment stretches, unbearable, until they finally stutter, *"I just wanted to... explain myself."*

The camera pulls back slightly, revealing the room’s emptiness—except for the lone figure, now staring at their feet, the weight of the moment swallowing them whole. The projector’s looped image flickers again, the faces of the audience shifting into static.
