**Scene 1: The Kitchen**
A clock ticks loudly as Maria opens the refrigerator. Inside, a single ripe mango sits beside a half-eaten sandwich. The door swings shut with a creak.

**Scene 2: The Forest**
Javier’s boots crunch on dead leaves as he follows the old trail. The river’s reflection ripples—his own face staring back, slightly blurred by the mist.

**Scene 3: The Office**
Alex scrolls through his calendar: another meeting with the same tired faces. His phone buzzes—an unknown number. He ignores it.
