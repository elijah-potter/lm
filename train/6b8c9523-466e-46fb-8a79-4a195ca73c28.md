**Scene Excerpt:**

The dim glow of the laptop screen flickered against the rain-streaked window as **Lena** adjusted her glasses, her fingers hovering over the keyboard. The first draft of *"The Hollow Crown"* lay abandoned on the desk, its draft mode blinking like a dying star. A fresh notebook lay open beside it, pages filled with fragmented notes, discarded ideas, and half-remembered dialogue.

She had started late—midnight drafts, coffee-stained pages, and the familiar ache of a story still *alive* inside her. Now, she sat with the original text in hand and the revised draft open, a red pen hovering over the margins.

*"He didn’t say that,"* she muttered, crossing out a line. *"He didn’t look at her like that. The light was wrong."* She replaced it with a simpler description, the tension in her shoulders easing slightly. The story was changing, but so was she.

A knock at the door. Her brother, **Kai**, stood in the hallway, arms crossed. *"You’re not going to finish this?"*

Lena exhaled, pushing back from the desk. *"Not tonight."* She grabbed her coat, leaving the chaos behind. The revision was never perfect—it was a dance, a conversation with the story itself, something she’d return to tomorrow.
