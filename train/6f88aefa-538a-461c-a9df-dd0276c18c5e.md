Once upon a time in a cozy little house, there lived a bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the garden. One sunny morning, Benny found a tiny, shimmering treasure hidden in his flower bed—a shiny blue bead.

Benny picked it up and giggled. "What a beautiful bead!" he thought. He decided to take it home to show his friends.

On his way back, Benny met a sleepy old turtle named Tilly. "Good morning, Tilly!" Benny said. Tilly blinked sleepily. "Morning, Benny! Where are you going with that shiny thing?"

Benny showed Tilly the blue bead. Tilly gasped. "It’s so pretty! But why did you find it?"

Benny explained, "It’s a magic bead. It makes everything sparkle!"

Tilly smiled. "Oh, really? Then let’s play with it together!"

The two friends hopped and crawled back home, and Benny’s garden turned into a sparkling wonderland. The flowers glowed, the grass shimmered, and even Benny’s favorite carrot patch twinkled.

The other garden animals—Milo the squirrel, Daisy the duck, and Pippin the rabbit—all gathered to see the magic. "Wow!" said Milo. "This is the best day ever!"

Benny and Tilly shared the magic bead, and everyone laughed and played until their bellies were full of yummy snacks. As the sun began to set, Benny and Tilly packed up their treasures. "We’ll keep the magic for tomorrow," Benny said.

The next morning, they found the blue bead still glowing, waiting for them. Together, they created more magic—turning the garden into a dance party, a storytime, and even a little picnic with extra sparkles.

From that day on, Benny and Tilly knew that magic wasn’t just in stories—it was in their garden, and it made every day wonderful! And so, with a happy heart, they hopped and curled up under the stars, dreaming of more adventures.
