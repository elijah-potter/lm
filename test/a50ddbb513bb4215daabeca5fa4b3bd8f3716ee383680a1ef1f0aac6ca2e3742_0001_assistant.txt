President John F. Kennedy was assassinated on an afternoon on November 22, 1963, as he rode a motorcade through downtown Dallas, Texas. He was shot and killed by a Texas School Book Depository employee, Lee Harvey Oswald. Mr, Oswald was immediately arrested for the fatal shooting and taken into police custody. While being transferred to a country jail escorted by police, he was fatally shot by a nightclub owner, Mr. Jack Ruby. The fatal shooting to the chest, which was broadcast on live television, led to Oswald being rushed to Parkland Memorial Hospital, where Kennedy had died two days earlier. Oswald died at the hospital, and Mr. Ruby was arrested for his shooting. Mr. Ruby died four years later of a pulmonary embolism after a cancer diagnosis.
