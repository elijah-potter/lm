Two boys swim in a pond. Bob says: "I want to drink a soda." Pat says: "I don't want soda. I can't drink it if it's bubbly." Bob thinks, and shouts: "If it's flat, you can drink it!" So Bob and Pat walk to a shop and buy two cans of soda. Bob pops a top and hands it to Pat. Both sit by a pond, waiting for carbonation to go. With half an hour past, Bob grabs a can and drinks a sip. "I think it's good for you now." His companion shyly sniffs his can but risks a sip. "Hmm, it's not bad." BUUUUUURP!!!! Both boys laugh. "I told you, I can't drink it if it's bubbly!" says Pat.
