**The Last Light of Eldermere**

In the crumbling ruins of Eldermere, where the air hummed with forgotten magic, Elias stumbled upon the ancient tome. Its pages, ink faded by time, whispered secrets of a forgotten world. The final chapter, sealed with a blood-red lock, had been his only hope for answers.

**What did Elias do next?**

1. **Break the lock with the relic from his pocket** (a rusted dagger, forged by his father’s last craft).
2. **Whisper the forgotten incantation** (a chant whispered by a dying scholar in the ruins).
3. **Demand the tome open itself** (a command that risked awakening something ancient).
4. **Leave it unopened, trusting fate to decide** (but risking the curse of the sealed knowledge).

Choose wisely. The world beyond the pages awaits.
