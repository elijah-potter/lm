In the quiet hum of a city that forgot to sleep, there was a café where the espresso was brewed by a roaster who had once dreamed of writing poetry. One evening, an old book fell from the shelf, its spine worn thin with time, the pages filled with forgotten verses. The dreamer, sensing the book’s call, reached out and picked it up.

Inside, a single line remained unfinished: *"The world is not just light, but the shadow that lingers when the sun forgets to shine."* She traced the ink with her fingertip, and for a moment, the words pulsed like a heartbeat.

As she left, she noticed the café’s neon sign flickering—*Always Open*—but the word *Always* had been deliberately left out, as if the café itself had chosen to end its story there, too.
