Nelson the Jellyfish was floating peacefully along the sea-bed on a glorious, sunny morning. His yellow tendrils danced in the sea's gentle current as he hummed an upbeat jingle. Nelson loved to sing and dreamt of the day when his tones would be heard where no other sea creature had been heard before, the Walmart on the shore.  As he floated along he could hear muttering from behind him, he spun around and to his surprise, two starfish appeared from the sandy sea floor. The starfish motioned to Nelson to follow them from the depths, Nelson felt nervous but his intrigue took over and he obliged. The trio made their way to the golden sands of the beach when Nelson spied the sight of his dreams, it was the glowing sign of the Walmart on the shore! "Oh my", gasped Nelson, he knew today was his day to shine. As they approached the glistening doors of Walmart, Nelson knew what he had to do. He danced through the aisles, starfish at his side, and sang the most magical song. The other shoppers looked on in awe as Nelson mesmerized them with his impeccable voice. The shoppers began to cheer and Nelson felt so fulfilled, he had realized his dream! The sun began to set and Nelson, along with his new starfish friends, returned to the depths of the sea. Overjoyed at his accomplishment, Nelson fell asleep with a huge smile on his yellow face.
