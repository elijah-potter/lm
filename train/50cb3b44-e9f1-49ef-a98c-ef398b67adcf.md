**Passageway through industrial district near port:**
Bricked walkways lined with rusted shipping containers and repurposed cranes. Steel grates over cracked asphalt. Workers in high-vis vests pass oil-stained tarps, their boots crunching on debris. Smoke from a distant smelter curls across the sky. The air smells of salt, diesel, and damp concrete. A lone figure in a hooded jacket moves toward a closed gate marked "Private."
