Q: Is My Photo Stream, Apple's cloud-based photo syncing feature, still available in 2023?
A: Yes, My Photo Stream is still available in 2023 despite being superseded by iCloud Photo Library.

Q: When will My Photo Stream be shutting down?
A: My Photo Stream is set to shut down on July 26th.

Q: Is the end of My Photo Stream surprising?
A: The end of My Photo Stream is not surprising because it had limitations such as restricted photo uploads, photos saved for only 30 days, and a lack of syncing high-quality photos in their original resolution.

Q: How does iCloud differ from My Photo Stream?
A: iCloud offers modern cloud storage with photos and videos stored in their full resolution, unlike My Photo Stream.

Q: When will new photo uploads to My Photo Stream come to an end?
A: New photo uploads to My Photo Stream will come to an end on June 26th.

Q: Will users lose any photos when My Photo Stream shuts down?
A: “The photos in My Photo Stream are already stored on at least one of your devices,” Apple’s page notes, “So as long as you have the device with your originals, you won’t lose any photos as part of this process.”
