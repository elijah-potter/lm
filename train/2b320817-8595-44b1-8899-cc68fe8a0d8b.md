Lily the bunny loved to hop in the sunny meadow every morning. One day, while chasing her favorite daisy, she tripped over a big, shiny rock. The rock was warm and smooth, like a little pillow. "Oh, what a fun surprise!" she giggled.

Just then, a tiny mouse named Benny scurried past. "Where did you fall, Lily?" he whispered. "I found this cool rock!" Lily showed him. "Can we play with it together?" Benny clapped his tiny paws. "Yes! Let’s make a rock castle!"

They dug tiny holes in the dirt and placed the rock as their base. Soon, they had built a little house with sticks and leaves. Benny dug a tunnel for secret adventures, and Lily painted it with flowers from her garden.

As the sun began to set, the meadow sparkled. Benny and Lily giggled, pretending to be kings and queens of their magical rock castle. "Best adventure ever!" Benny cheered. "I love our new friends!" Lily smiled, hugging her daisy tightly.

The next morning, they found their castle still there—just waiting for them to return. And so, Benny and Lily became the bravest friends in the meadow, always ready for more fun adventures!
