A bustling **Village Square** in a rural district, where the town’s annual **Market Day** draws residents from neighboring farms. Elders greet each other by name while negotiating grain contracts, children whisper gossip between trading stalls, and elders recall stories from ancestors who lived here. A central **Stone Well** serves as a neutral ground where strangers—though few—mix in queues. The scent of roasting corn and woodsmoke lingers as laughter and clinking pots echo. The air hums with the rhythm of shared history: every face is a known face, though not every name is spoken aloud.
