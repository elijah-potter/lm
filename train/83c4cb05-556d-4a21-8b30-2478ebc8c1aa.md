**The Neon Alley**
The neon hummed like a dying breath,
a chorus of blues and crimson streaks
that slithered through the cracked pavement,
dripping like stolen whiskey.

Behind every shadow,
there was a name carved in ink—
a *dreamer*, a *hope*, a *lighter*.

They stood at the bar, the bartender’s glass
reflecting a thousand stolen moments,
while the radio played the same loop:
*"In every spark, there’s a story to tell."*

A girl in a sequined dress
tipped her glass—*"Another round, please."*
She didn’t need the whiskey to see
how the city’s glow was *alive*.

Because some of us don’t just *burn* bright—
we *feed* the flame.
So here’s to the ones who refuse to dim,
who say *"Yes"* to the impossible—
and *light the way* for the next.
