In keeping with the tradition of posting about things that we are thankful for this month, I wanted to dedicate today’s Facebook post to my gratitude for nail clippers.

They’re a relatively small item, yes, and that makes them overlooked when everyone shares their gratitude stories. But where would we be without them?

Can you imagine how you would handle it if you snagged a nail and didn’t have access to a tool to trim off the snag, thereby shortening the length of the entire nail to make it once again a consistent curve?

What would we do if we had no easy way to trim our nails shorter? At some point, we would have nails so long they would touch the ground, and how would we text then? It would also make things difficult like driving a car or making a sandwich!

There are even creative uses for the nail clipper. I sometimes will use mine to clip a hangnail or to remove that little piece of plastic stuck through the fabric of my new shirts -- the one that holds the price tag on. Nail clippers are so versatile, and people can find many "off-label" uses for them if they try!

In summary, today, I want to thank the nail clipper manufacturers. I just can’t imagine how this world would be without nail clippers there to make my life, and all of our lives, so much easier. Thank goodness for nail clippers!
