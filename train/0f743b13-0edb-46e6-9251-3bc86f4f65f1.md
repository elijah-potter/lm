The clock struck midnight.
Silence.
Something moved in the shadows.
A whisper—soft, barely there.
A sound of metal scraping against wood.
The door creaked open.
Light spilled into the room like a thief.
A figure stood at the end of the hall.
The air grew thick.
The pulse in my ears drowned out everything else.
His eyes locked onto mine.
A knife glinted in his hand.
I should have run.
But the fear had already won.
