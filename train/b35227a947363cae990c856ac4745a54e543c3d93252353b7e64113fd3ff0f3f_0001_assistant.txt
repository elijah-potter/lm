First of all, it's true that "jolie" is best translated by "pretty," and "belle" means "beautiful," but "mignonne" means more than cute - it's likely a compliment about your personality as well as your looks.  Use of the word "belle" can be fairly cold - think of a fashion magazine model - whereas "mignonne" is more individual - pretty and charming ("charmante"), and it can also mean that he thinks you style yourself in an interesting and attractive way.
