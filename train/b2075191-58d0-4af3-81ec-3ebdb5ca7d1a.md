Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big garden, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy pink ears and a tail that wiggled like a spring.

Every morning, Benny would hop outside to play. The sun was warm, and the grass was soft under his paws. Benny loved to chase butterflies and giggle as they fluttered around him.

One sunny day, Benny spotted a shiny red berry in the bushes. He nibbled it and felt happy. "Mmm, this is the best berry ever!" he said.

Later, Benny met a little squirrel named Sammy. Sammy had a bushy tail and loved collecting acorns. "Want to share?" Sammy asked.

Benny smiled. "Yes, please!" He gave Sammy a tiny acorn, and they both played together.

As the day grew dark, Benny snuggled into his burrow, watching the stars twinkle. "Tomorrow is another fun day," he thought, and he fell asleep with a happy heart.

Benny woke up the next morning, ready to explore again. His little garden was waiting for him, filled with laughter and sunshine.
