Once upon a time, in a cozy green house, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had soft brown ears and a fluffy tail that bounced when he ran. One sunny morning, Benny saw a shiny red apple on a bush.

"Yum!" Benny said, and he hopped over to pick it. But when Benny tried to eat it, he realized it was too big for him. He giggled and rolled it away.

Just then, a tiny mouse named Muffy ran up. "I want that apple!" she squeaked. Benny smiled. "Here, you can have half of it." Muffy was very happy.

After that, Benny and Muffy played together. Benny showed Muffy how to hop, and Muffy showed Benny how to hide eggs from her baby mice. They became best friends, sharing apples and adventures all day long.

Benny and Muffy lived happily ever after, and their little friends came to visit every day. The green house was filled with laughter and love.
