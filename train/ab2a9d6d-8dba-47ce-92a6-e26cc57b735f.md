She gently suggested that I reconsider, emphasizing Sarah's financial struggles and reminding me of the importance of family support. 

I dug in my heels. I explained to both of them that I wasn’t denying Sarah support; I’d helped her with a significant portion of her bills already. But I felt strongly about the cabin’s purpose. My father, a man of principle, would be *damn* disappointed if we started treating it like a business.

I became aware of this when a friend, clearly uncomfortable, mentioned it to me. I felt like I was being publicly shamed within our close circle.

Now, Sarah is furious. She’s accused me of intentionally trying to sabotage her life and has threatened to contest the will. My mother is caught in the middle, oscillating between supporting Sarah and acknowledging my perspective. I feel increasingly isolated and resentful.
