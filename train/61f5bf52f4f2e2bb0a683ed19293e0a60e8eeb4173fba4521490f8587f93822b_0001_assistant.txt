Once upon a time, there were three alcoholics, Joe, Sue, and Luis. Life was hard for these three as they couldn’t seem to do anything to quit drinking. 

They each decided that they would take a different tactic and see which one would work. 
The first alcoholic, Joe, chose to remove all alcohol from his life. He took everything out of the house, refused to go to bars, and avoided liquor stores at all costs. For the first few days, it worked. He didn’t drink anything because nothing was available. But then, just one Saturday later, he was at a work event where there was an open bar, and he caved and had a drink. And then several more. 

The second alcoholic, Sue, tried a different path. She decided that she would read a book about quitting addictions. The book came highly recommended, and apparently, after reading the book, she wouldn’t even want to pick up a drink. Unfortunately, the book didn’t work at all. She drank the whole time she was reading it. 

The third alcoholic, Luis, chose to join a local Alcoholics Anonymous chapter. He started going to meetings several times a week and even got a sponsor who he could call night and day when he was tempted to drink. To Luis’s surprise, it was actually working. Although he still wanted a drink, he didn’t do it because of his incredible support system. 

When the three met up again, Luis told them what he had found at AA after they reported their failed tactics. And Luis, Sue, and Joe all headed to a meeting together.
