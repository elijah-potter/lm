Here are some ideas for Easter baskets that won't add clutter to your home:

- Art Supplies: Think about items that need to be replaced and add them to the basket. Crayons, markers, paint sets, and even a small canvas would be a great addition.
- Personal Items: Kids always need new personal items, so pick out cute spring-themed items. Socks, bath bombs, lotions, and hand sanitizers are all small items that will be used.
- Summer Items: This is a great time to stock up on outdoor items for summer. Chalk, water toys, goggles, swimsuits, and towels would fill the basket nicely. 
- Activities: Craft kits, playdoh, water balloons, and books are all simple additions to the basket. 
- Items for Older Kids: For the kids that are a bit older, gift cards are always a hit.
