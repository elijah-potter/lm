A **broken promise** shattered trust, yet words remained unspoken—just like echoes in an empty room.
The agreement, etched in dusty parchment, had been signed with trembling fingers, yet the weight of its meaning was lost in translation. One understood it as a vow, the other as a favor, both assuming the other’s intentions were pure.

The deadline blurred into time; the conditions shifted like sand. One party saw it as a fleeting act of kindness, the other as a transaction frozen in indecision. The recipient of the promise, now bitter, whispered: *"You said forever, not forever."* The giver, in hindsight, realized they’d meant *"until we both agreed otherwise."*

Silence filled the gap between their thoughts, a void where understanding should have been. The promise, once vibrant, now fades like ink on parchment—unread, unfulfilled, and, perhaps, never meant to be.
