An infinite loop of whispers and unspoken words, echoing between two shadows on a cracked pavement.
*"Why do you ask?"* the voice in the dark replies, though no one is there to hear.
The other, a silhouette with no face, begins to count the cracks in the sidewalk, each one a silent question.
*"One,"* they murmur, *"two,"* then pause. The other doesn’t answer.
*"Why?"* the question fades into static, like a radio tuned to nothing.
The first voice now speaks from the mouth of a clock, its hands spinning backward.
*"Because time is a lie,"* it says, though no one is listening. The second voice hums, a sound like rusted gears.
*"But I don’t want to know,"* it admits, though it already does.
The first voice laughs—a sound that splits the air into two infinite versions of itself.
*"Then let’s end it,"* it says, but the second voice only tilts their head, as if studying a problem no one can solve.
The first voice begins to dissolve into the dark, leaving only the hum of the second voice, still counting, still unanswered.
*"One,"* it repeats, *"two,"* and the loop begins again, forever.
