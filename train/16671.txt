Prehistoric Planet: Dinosaur World
Week of 7/1/19 to 7/5/19
During this fun program, students will take on the role of junior archaeologists. Together, they will be introduced to the field of archaeology through a week-long program of, activities, workshops, and games. Students will be introduced to the prehistory and learn about what it takes to be an archaeologist. They will participate in a wide variety of activities designed to explore the many different skills practiced by prehistoric people. As well as learn about how archaeologists use artifacts and fossils to understand the past.
