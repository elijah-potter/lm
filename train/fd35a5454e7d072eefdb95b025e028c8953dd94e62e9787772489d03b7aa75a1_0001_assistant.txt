Three hours of sleep
is just not enough, 
no matter how still I lie,
the next day will feel rough.

With spaced-out thoughts,
and yawns every breath, 
I can't connect any dots,
my brain abandoned ship.

The sun burns my eyes,
as I wait for the night,
it's so hard to stay awake, 
even though there's still sunlight.

Maybe if I go to bed at eight,
I can sleep a whole night through,
then I won't spend tomorrow
trying not to nod off on you.
