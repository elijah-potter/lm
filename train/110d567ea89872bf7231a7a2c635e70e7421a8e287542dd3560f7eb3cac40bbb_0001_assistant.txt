It's a quiet morning at Spinach Lake. The war between the ducks and geese seems to have quieted down but is raging on under the surface.

Down in the hidden duck base of operations, a stern yell could be heard. "Rise and shine, Agent Rubber! Today I am giving you a new mission," announced the duck captain. "Your mission is to infiltrate the geese's stronghold on the other side of the lake. We don't know what they might be plotting, but it can't be good. We have provided you with waterproof paint to aid in your disguise," declared the captain. 

"What?" replied Agent Rubber. "How can I pretend that I am a filthy goose? I don't even look like one of those creatures. Not to mention, no one will believe I am one." 

"This is an order. You are not permitted to question orders," commanded the captain. 

"As you wish," winced Agent Rubber. "I will work on my disguise then." He then left the stronghold to paint his body to resemble a Canadian goose.

A few hours later, the poorly painted duck appeared at the entrance to the goose stronghold. "Hi, I lost my gaggle; can I join yours?" asked Agent Rubber. 

A big Canadian goose went out of their stronghold and quickly replied, "You look small for a goose. Explain. Now." 

"Oh, I was born really small, and my gaggle did not have much access to food, so this is the most I was able to grow. I might be small, but I am loyal, and I will do everything I can to help my gaggle!" lied the duck. 

"How did you lose your previous gaggle?" questioned the goose. 

"We tragically lost our lake to humans; we were trying to find another lake, but thanks to my short wings, they all flew too fast, and I couldn't keep up," lied the duck. 

"Ok, come in; we will lead you to our leader to see if you can join our gaggle," said the goose. 

"Wonderful," said the duck.
