**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 14, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair is returning this August, promising a celebration of agriculture, community, and family fun. This year’s fair will showcase livestock competitions, agricultural exhibits, artisan crafts, and a wide variety of entertainment options for all ages. Visitors can expect traditional fair food, carnival rides, and opportunities to engage with local farmers and businesses. 

The Sangamon County Fair will run from August 22nd through August 26th, 2024, at the Sangamon County Fairgrounds. Gates will open at 9:00 AM each day, with evening events continuing until 10:00 PM. A full schedule of events, including daily demonstrations and competitions, is available on the fair’s official website at [insert website address here]. Parking will be available on-site for a fee.

Admission to the Sangamon County Fair is $10 for adults, $5 for children ages 5-12, and free for children under five.
