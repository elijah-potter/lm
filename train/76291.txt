Summer Exploration: Play Some Online Games
As you settle into your summer vacation, consider exploring some of the online game suggestions for your grade level. For a quick entry point, go to the "Guide to Online Bridges Resources" and look at the chart under "Family Resources" for Online Games.
Links will take you to age-appropriate computer games that help your students practice basic skills. Play the games yourself to see which games best match your students' interests and abilities. You may also want to consider the skills and concepts addressed in each activity and align them to units or lessons in Bridges.
