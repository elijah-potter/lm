Time, once a blur, now fractures into a thousand overlapping moments. A bird’s cry splits into a chorus of whispers. A streetlight flickers, its glow stretching into a ghostly, elongated beam, casting everything in an eerie, stretched-out light. The past and present blur together—memories unravel like threads, each frame dissolving into something impossible to grasp.
