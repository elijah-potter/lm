The 'Promoting Meaningful Teaching and Learning' Summit was one of four Summits at the EAUC-Scotland Conference 2017. This Summit was chaired by Betsy King from Learning for Sustainability Scotland, and consisted of five presentations and a workshop. You can find the resources from the different presentations linked below.
The Summit workshop asked attendees what practical actions they already take to address Education for Sustainable Development (ESD) in their institution/subject area, as well as what more could be done to promote meaningful teaching and learning.
