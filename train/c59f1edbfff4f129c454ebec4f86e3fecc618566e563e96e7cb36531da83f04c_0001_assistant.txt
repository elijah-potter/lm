There once was a little lion cub named Jeffrey who was best friends with a little friendly ghost named Millie. Jeffrey and Millie loved playing tricks on all of the animals. Sometimes they would have Jeffrey distract them with his beautiful singing while Millie would untie their shoes and retie them to each other's shoes! This would make the animals trip all over themselves as they tried to walk away. Other times, Jeffrey would open his mouth to sing but Millie's voice would come out (which was not beautiful) and all of the other animals would run away screaming. Jeffrey and Millie would laugh and laugh at their silly pranks. However, one day soon, their antics would catch up to them. But that is a story for another day.
