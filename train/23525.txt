Foster Diversity and Inclusion
Duck Duck Books partners with the diverse creative community to share their unique perspectives in every book we publish.
All Puppies Are Good Puppies
Let an overload of cuteness encourage your child to embrace the differences that make the world so special. Available in English, Spanish, Mandarin, and Cantonese.
Support Early Language Learning
Duck Duck Books builds upon a complex process called "Transcreation", which means our books are not translated, but written from scratch in both languages, preserving the connotations, values and cultures of each.
Cultivate Social Emotional Skills
Ages 0-5 (Early Years Foundation Stage) are the most important developmental stage to cultivate social skills and achieve higher emotional intelligence (EQ). Duck Duck Books strives to help families raise kids who are kind to themselves and kind to others.
I Love You More
A poetic story about all the wonderful ways parents show love to their children. Available in English, Spanish, Mandarin, and Cantonese.
Instill Eco-conscious Lifestyles
Duck Duck Eco Goods creates sustainable products that are reusable or upcyclable to reduce waste, and do our part to create a better world for the future generations.
