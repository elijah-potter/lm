The river hummed its slow, patient tune against the stones, each ripple a whisper carried on the wind. Dawn painted the sky in gold and violet, and the first light kissed the earth like a lover’s touch.

She sat beneath the old oak, its branches heavy with forgotten secrets, and let the silence breathe between her words. The words spilled out—not grand, but honest—like rain on a leaf.

*"I am not broken,"* she whispered. *"I am only waiting for the weight to lift."*

The morning mist curled around her ankles, soft as a sigh. The world, for once, was quiet. The calm returned.
