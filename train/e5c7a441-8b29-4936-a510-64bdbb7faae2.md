A detective paused mid-step, their fingers tracing the edge of an old ledger on the desk. The ink smudged where a forgotten note had been scribbled—a date, a name—someone had crossed out years ago. The paper was damp with age, the texture rough beneath the calloused palm. As he turned it over, he noticed a faint, unnoticed mark: a single, thin streak of ink, barely visible, that matched the color of the bloodstain on the wall behind him. It was too small to be a stain; it was a *fingerprint*, one that didn’t belong to any of the suspects in the case. His pulse quickened. A detail that had slipped past the others, the one that now pointed to the truth.
