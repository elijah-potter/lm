Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big tree outside, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had soft brown fur and always wore a tiny red hat. Every morning, Benny would hop outside to see his favorite things: a patch of sunflower seeds, a stream, and a big, juicy apple tree.

One sunny afternoon, Benny noticed something strange. The sunflower seeds were all gone! He hopped over to the stream and asked, "Where did my seeds go? I planted them yesterday!"

Just then, Benny saw a tiny, curious squirrel named Sammy. "I saw some little bugs eating them," Sammy said, digging in the dirt. Benny's ears perked up. "But where did they come from?"

Suddenly, a little bird named Bella fluttered down. "I brought some seeds from my garden to share with all my friends!" she chirped. Benny's eyes sparkled. "But we don't have any left!"

Benny had an idea. He gathered all the seeds he could find, including some from his old hat and a few hidden in his paws. Together, Benny, Sammy, and Bella planted new seeds in the sunny patch. They sang songs, told jokes, and even made a tiny garden where the seeds grew into beautiful flowers.

Soon, the patch was full of bright flowers, and the bugs loved the new seeds. Benny's friends came to visit, and they all shared stories and snacks. Benny realized that sharing was fun and that sometimes, what you give away comes back to you in a special way.

From that day on, Benny planted seeds every spring, and his garden grew even bigger. And every year, his friends would say, "Remember when Benny shared his seeds?" and smile happily. Benny knew that sharing his seeds was the best adventure of all.
