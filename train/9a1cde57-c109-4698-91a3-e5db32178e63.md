A golden lantern hangs from a rusted iron hook in the corner of the dimly lit tavern, its glass etched with glowing runes. The fire crackles, casting long shadows that twist the faces of patrons around the low table, where a hooded figure sits with their back to the room. Their fingers trace a parchment scroll, sealed with wax that shimmers like polished silver. A single word is scrawled beneath it: *"Briar."*

A hand claps the figure’s shoulder. The tavern’s bartender, his beard streaked with salt and pepper, grins. *"You’re the one who’s been asking about the old well. The one they say holds the heart of the forest."* The figure turns slowly, and the lantern’s glow reveals a promise carved into their face—*hope*. The bartender leans in. *"It’s not just water. It’s power. The kind that can mend a broken blade, whisper to the wind, or make a man forget his own name."* The scroll’s wax cracks as the figure exhales, a breath that smells of dried herbs and old secrets. *"Tell me where it is."* The lantern’s light flickers. *"You’ll have to walk through the Blackthorn Gate to find it. But know this: the forest remembers what is promised."* The tavern’s murmur fades into the hum of the well’s unseen depths.
