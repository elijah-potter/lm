Siberian Huskies are affectionate and loyal dogs that were originally bred for transporting items in the frozen wilderness. They typically grow between 20 and 23.5 inches at the top of the shoulder, weigh between 35 and 65 pounds, and have a life expectancy of 12 to 14 years. They typically are happy in a family and with other pets, and their friendly demeanor makes them unskilled watchdogs.
