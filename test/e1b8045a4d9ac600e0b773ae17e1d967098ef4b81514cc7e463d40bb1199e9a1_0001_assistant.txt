1.	The longest shot to win the Kentucky Derby (Derby) was Donerail in 1913. His odds were 91.45-1, and he paid a staggering $184.90 on a two-dollar wager.
2.	The next was Mine That Bird in 2009 who went off at odds of 51.60-1. The payout on this win bet was a nice chunk of change at $103.20. Interestingly, he was 12½ lengths behind the frontrunner a mile into the race (which is only a mile and a quarter) which means he made up a lot of ground in a very short amount of time!
3.	Giacomo went off at 50.30-1 odds in 2005 for a payout of $102.60 on a two-dollar bet. In a genuinely stunning race, he beat Closing Argument, who was also a long shot at 70-1!
4.	In 1940, Gallahadion was the fourth longest shot to win the Derby at odds of 35.2-1. A win wager paid $72.40 as Gallahadion beat the favorite by one and a half lengths.
5.	The fifth, Apollo, paid $169.80 on a $5 bet at odds of 32.96-1. In an interesting twist, Apollo won the 1882 Derby even though he did not race as a two-year-old, giving rise to “The Curse of Apollo.” This curse was not broken until 2018 when another unraced two-year-old, Justify, finally won the Derby.
