Here are five potential adventures set in a town by the sea:

1. Mutated fish - Recent fishing has brought in fish with odd deformities. The local water temple may hold answers, which revolve around the actions of a few cultists operating in town.
2. Resolving gambling debts - A sailor currently staying in town has accrued a massive gambling debt. He wears a medallion that may have played a role in this since it brings bad luck. The PCs may be hired to recover his debt, or he might try to get them to help out in some way, possibly through using the medallion.
3. Find the thieves - Someone has been stealing stock from a local merchant. The merchant suspects the sailors who are currently in port, but the actual culprit is more local - and apparently capable of magic.
4. Attack of the fish men - A mob of kuo-toa or similar monsters attack the town, seeking an artifact in the local place of worship. The PCs might fight back, organize a defense effort, find a way to escape, etc.
5. Siren's call - A haunting song has been heard from the sea, and some people think they've seen a ghost on certain nights. It's all considered superstition, though - until people start going missing. Is it a ghost, or a vengeful ploy by one of the townsfolk?

Let me know if you need more ideas or clarification.
