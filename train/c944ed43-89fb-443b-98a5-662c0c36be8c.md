The overgrown archway of the old mill stands half-buried in mist, its stone carvings worn smooth by time. Inside, the air hums with the ghostly scent of aged timber and damp wool—just a whisper of a forgotten past. Beyond the threshold, the forest shifts from twilight to twilight, edges blurred where light fades into shadow. The wind carries voices from beyond, but none can quite be heard, only the faintest echoes of laughter or footsteps. The threshold itself is a place where the ordinary and the surreal bleed together, where every step feels like a choice between two worlds.
