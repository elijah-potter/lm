A just-21 almost-teacher facing 18-year-olds: despising English class. Me, a moment older than my haters.
Do I know enough English to fill a semester?
Let's read "The Scarlett Letter," okay? Audible groans, laughter. 

Hmm, what to do? "Hey class, how about iI read the first chapter aloud?" A few nods, a few glares. 

Pulling out the book, looking across the faces. Take out your books, open to chapter one. Thankful I am a decent reader. Staring at them hard, I start to read.

"The founders of a new colony, whatever Utopia of human virtue and happiness they might originally project, have invariably recognized it among their earliest practical necessities to allot a portion of the virgin soil as a cemetery, and another portion as..."

Faces at 18 caught up against their will in a story of intrigue and love and shame. The human condition meets adolescents. They're quiet, calm, and attentive as the master teacher enters the room. 

Breathing a sigh of relief, I just keep reading.
