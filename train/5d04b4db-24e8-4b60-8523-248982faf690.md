Once there was a little bunny named Benny who lived in a cozy burrow under the sunflower field. Benny loved to hop, jump, and chase his favorite toy—his little red ball. One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his ball had rolled away!

Benny hopped excitedly, "Where could my ball have gone?" he asked his friends, a group of chicks named Chilly and Chickpea. The chicks chirped, "Maybe it’s under the big oak tree?" Benny nodded and hopped over.

Soon, Benny spotted his ball rolling towards a tiny stream. He hopped right over, but the ball slipped and rolled into the water. Chilly and Chickpea gasped. Benny’s eyes filled with worry.

"Don’t worry, Benny!" said Chilly. "Let’s help!" Together, they hopped to the stream’s edge. Benny carefully lifted his ball out, and it popped right out! The ball was safe and happy.

Benny grinned. "I found my ball!" he said. The chicks clapped their wings, "That’s the best play ever!" Benny and his friends laughed, sharing their snack of berries and sun.

From that day on, Benny always kept an eye on his toys, and his friends knew they could always count on him to be brave and kind. And as the sun set, painting the sky pink and gold, Benny and his friends curled up together, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures.
