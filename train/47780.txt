Using Styles and Templates
Styles allow you to apply several formatting characteristics to text with a single style selection, while templates allow you to create a document made of a collection of styles (and other page formatting elements). This simplifies the formatting process and ensures that a group of users is adhering to a common format. These tutorials provide a basic introduction to styles and explain how to use templates.
