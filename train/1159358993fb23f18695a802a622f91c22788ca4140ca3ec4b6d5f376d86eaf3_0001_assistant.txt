Old Ironside was the name of the USS Constitution. It is the oldest commissioned warship that is still afloat. It is berthed in Boston and known as America's Ship of State. The ship was laid down in Boston on November 1 1794 and was one of the first Six 44-gun frigates that was authorized for use by the U.S. Navy. The launching of the ship occurred on September 20, 1797. However, it stalled on the way, eventually reaching the water on October 21, 1797.
