In the dim glow of a half-lit room, an elderly man sits on the edge of a wooden bed, his fingers tracing the faded scar on his wrist—a remnant of a promise he made long ago. The air smells of damp wool and old books. His voice, though steady, carries the weight of years: *"I promised my brother I’d keep his secret safe. He died before I could tell anyone, but I swore I’d never forget."* Outside, rain taps against the rusted windowpanes, mirroring the slow, relentless rhythm of his life.
