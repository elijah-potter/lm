The first time rain kissed the city streets without warning,
dripping silver threads onto cracked asphalt,
while the distant hum of traffic dissolved into the wind.
A child’s laughter echoed across the park’s empty lawn,
unfiltered and bright, as if time had paused just for them.
The scent of wildflowers clung to the air like a memory—
fresh and untouched by human hands.

That moment was a ghost of possibility,
stolen by the next dawn, but not forgotten in the echoes of the past.
