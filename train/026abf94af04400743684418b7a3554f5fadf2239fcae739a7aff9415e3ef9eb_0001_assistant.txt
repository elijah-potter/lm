Experts estimate that cats began domesticating themselves up to 12,000 years ago, around the time that the first agricultural societies emerged in the Middle East's Fertile Crescent. It's thought that wild cats took advantage of the prey in early farmers' storehouses, and the humans appreciated the pest control the cats provided. Over time, people would have favored friendlier cats, and the animals adapted to their new environments. Today, there are dozens of house cat breeds, and they are one of the most popular pets.
