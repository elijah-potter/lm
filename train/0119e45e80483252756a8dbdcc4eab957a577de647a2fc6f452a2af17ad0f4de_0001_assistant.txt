Homerulus Simpson is an inept mortician who lives with his family in the vampiric town of Spring Hollows. He has a wife named Minerva and three children: a mischievous son named Bat who often gets in trouble for casting spells in school, a daughter named Layla who can often be found in the basement practicing organ, and a baby girl named Margaret, who is usually clothed in a long black dress. Layla is slightly detached from her family and gets friends and families into funny predicaments using mind control. Homerulus Simpson's nemesis and neighbor, a devout Satanist, owns and operates the town apothecary and does everything better than Homerulus.
