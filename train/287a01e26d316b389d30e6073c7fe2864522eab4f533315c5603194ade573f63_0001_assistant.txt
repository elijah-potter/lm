Here are some family-friendly countries in Europe:

1. Sweden - Stockholm has amazing amenities for kids. There are plenty of parks and gardens to explore, including Djurgarden Island.
2. Austria - With picturesque mountains and towns, this country is also home to World's oldest amusement park and the world's largest ice cave.
3. Italy - This country has the best of both worlds. With beautiful beaches and historic cities, you can explore the history and also have a fun beach day.
