The facilities, the staff, the food, and the organized activities were all exceptional at this hotel. The view from the rooftop pool was definitely our favorite part. We felt immersed in the treetops with the wildlife and all. We did a few activities through the hotel, and the waterfall hike was probably a highlight. Make sure to book dinner at the hotel's restaurant at least once; the setting is amazing. The whole stay felt like an unreal jungle experience but with all of the modern luxuries included. We nearly forgot the rest of the world existed during our stay!
