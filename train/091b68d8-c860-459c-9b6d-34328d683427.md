Warm sand crunching under bare feet, soft like honey; the air thick with salt and distant seagulls’ cries. The heat seeps into your palms, leaving them sticky with sweat. The ocean’s rhythm—splash, lap, retreat—echoes in your bones. A breeze carries the scent of crushed jasmine and damp earth, carrying the ghost of yesterday’s tide. Shadows stretch long, shifting like living things across the pebbles, whispering secrets of the wind’s touch.
