I can help you with organizational tips for your children's closet! Here are some to get you started:

1. Choose outfits for the week on the weekends and hang them with labels for each day of the week. Hang them on a rod within reach of your child so that they can get ready more independently.
2. Think of your children's closet much like you would your own and purchase appropriately sized hangers, hooks, drawer organizers, baskets, and a clothes hamper.
3. Put things that your children need to access within reach and save the upper space for things that are taller or out of season. Pull-out shelves are invaluable.
4. If siblings are sharing closet space, use dividers so that each has their own space, even if there is also some shared space.
5. A closet with extra ceiling height can benefit from pull-down shelves or rods. Be careful that items won't fall off, however, if your child tugs too hard.
6. Create cubbies for toys and label what goes where so that children know exactly where their playthings belong.
7. Let your child have some input on how to decorate the closet space. This may mean letting them choose a paint color or switching out ordinary drawer pulls for something more exciting.

I hope this helps!
