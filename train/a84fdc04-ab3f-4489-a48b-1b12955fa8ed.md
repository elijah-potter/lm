A bustling harbor with rhythmic ebb and flow of tides, where ships dock and depart on a schedule. The pier is lined with vendors selling fresh seafood, their stalls thronging with locals and tourists. At high tide, the harbor fills with cargo vessels; at low tide, fishing boats return with catches. The rhythmic chug of cranes, the clamor of laborers, and the scent of saltwater mix with the distant hum of city life. Markets on the waterfront pulse with activity, swapping goods and stories between communities. The docks are a hub of constant movement, where every hour feels like a different kind of tide.
