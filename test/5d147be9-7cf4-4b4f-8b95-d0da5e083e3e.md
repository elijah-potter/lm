Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the sunny meadow. One day, Benny met a tiny little flower named Daisy who had a special color—she was purple!

Benny wanted to help Daisy grow bigger and stronger. So, he gently picked her petals and planted them in the soft soil. Daisy smiled and grew taller with each day, turning into a beautiful purple bloom.

One sunny afternoon, Benny saw a little bee buzzing around. "Can I help you find some nectar?" Benny asked. The bee happily buzzed around, collecting sweet nectar from Daisy and other flowers. Soon, the whole meadow was filled with the sweetest smell, and many bees came to visit.

Benny and Daisy became the happiest friends, helping each other and sharing their time. The meadow became even more colorful and full of life because of them. Benny knew that with kindness and care, even tiny things can grow big and beautiful together.
