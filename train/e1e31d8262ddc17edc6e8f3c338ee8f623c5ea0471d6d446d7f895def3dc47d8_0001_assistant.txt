If you eat the way people in those countries eat, it's called a Mediterranean diet. When you hear the word diet, you may think it's about losing weight, but this is actually just a delicious way to eat instead of a strict diet. The pyramid includes some typical foods that the people in Greece and Italy used to eat about a hundred years ago. They liked how people from those countries were so healthy and lived longer even though they didn't have many doctors or hospitals, it was like magic.
