1. Use pine needs to smoke meat. Pine needles are great in barbeques and the roasting of meat and chicken. You can just throw pine needles on your charcoal right before putting your meat on, and then have a perfect roast. 
2. Infuse vinegar with pine to make salad dressing. You can make a salad dressing of pine needles for your food by simply adding pine needles to a jelly jar and topping it with vinegar. 
3. Pine needle cookies. With an amazing smell, this tasty treat makes a perfect companion for your afternoon tea. Just bake your cookies as usual, adding two tablespoons of lightly chopped Eastern Hemlock needles in the process. 
4. Pine-needle spirits. This is another good option for your kitchen mixup that involves a spruce-infused vodka or gin for a refreshing winter cocktail.
