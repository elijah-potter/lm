This week, in the epic saga of me being annoyed by my next-door neighbors over and over again, I have a lovely little story to share. Last night was the third night in a row that I've been woken up to the sound of my two toddlers crying and fussing at 2:00 AM because they were woken by the racket of my neighbors' garage band practice. I don't even think that they are light sleepers because they've slept through thunderstorms and fireworks before. 
But my neighbors and their late-night sessions? They are insanely loud and can sometimes go on for hours, often not even beginning until 10:00 or 11:00 o'clock at night. 
And don't even get me started on the lovely music itself. (It's awful.) My children do a better job when they're singing along to their cartoons on Saturday mornings. 
I'm starting to get a bit more than just annoyed at this point. Whenever I try to confront them about it during the normal hours of daylight, they never answer their door. I mean, are they vampires or something? 
Honestly, all I want is my life back. I want my kids to sleep the whole night through so that I can sleep the whole night through. I want a silent night, just once this week. But I guess that's just too much to ask.
