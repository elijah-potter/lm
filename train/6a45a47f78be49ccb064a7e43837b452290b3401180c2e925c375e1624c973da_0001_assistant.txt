The sun has risen on a calm Saturday, and morning is here. Jack excitedly wakes up his father, who is enjoying his day off by sleeping in later than usual, by shouting, "Dad! Dad! Wake up! You said that Saturday I could go through your saved-up pocket change and look for rare pennies! It's Saturday now, can I?"
Jack's father opened one eye, trying to shield it from the bright morning sun. 
"Sure, buddy. All my change is in my nightstand drawer here, all rolled up by coin type so that I can take it to the bank. There should be a bunch of rolls of pennies in there. Go for it. Now, son, let Dad get back to sleep. It's my day off!" 
"Okay, Dad!" exclaimed Jack as he scooped up the rolled-up pennies and went to his room to carefully inspect them for rarities.
Sitting on his bed with the penny rolls in front of him, he carefully unwraps them and begins his close inspection. After being on the hunt for a while, he says "Dang. I checked all of those and nothing, not a single good find! There's only one roll left, please let this be a good one!"
He takes a big breath and starts opening the roll. 
"Nothing, nothing, nothing," he says, going through each penny. He's halfway through when he spots something. 
"Woah! A 1909 penny! Holy moly, what a score, a key date. Wait, what if—no, it can't be! Is this a 1909 VDB?" Jack inspects the bottom of the coin and sees the 3 little letters that would make any coin enthusiast go wild: V-D-B. "Wow, wow, wow!" Jack exclaimed. "This is the find of a lifetime. I've got to show my Dad!" He ran in and woke his dad up, but this time, he was more than happy to be woken up upon hearing the news. 
"Wow, son! Good job! This is a find you'll remember forever." Jack smiled.
