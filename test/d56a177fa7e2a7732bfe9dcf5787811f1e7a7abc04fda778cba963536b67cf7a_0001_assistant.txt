Fluffer was a curious cat. He loved to go wandering in the backyard. The only problem is that his owner didn't let him out often. When he did, however, Fluffer would adventure around like everything was so new.

Oftentimes, Ginger, Fluffer's very best friend, would come around and play with him. Fluffer enjoyed Ginger's company so much. One day, Fluffer saw something out in the backyard. He couldn't quite make out what it was. Upon looking even closer, he noticed Ginger was stuck in the fence. Frantic, Fluffer immediately went to the back door and started scratching at the door furiously. When his owner didn't notice, he started meowing profusely.

Finally, his owner came and let Fluffer out. Fluffer immediately darted over to where Ginger was. He saw his tail was stuck in between the fence posts. Fluffer nudged it loose with the tip of his nose. Ginger leaped in the air with happiness that his best friend had come and saved him. It was truly something he would never forget.
