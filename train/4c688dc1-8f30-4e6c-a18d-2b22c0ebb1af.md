**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, Ill. – August 21, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this August, promising a vibrant celebration of agriculture, community, and family entertainment. This year’s fair will showcase the best of local livestock, award-winning produce, and a diverse array of attractions designed to appeal to all ages. Visitors can anticipate thrilling carnival rides, engaging demonstrations of traditional crafts, and opportunities to connect with local farmers and artisans. 

The Sangamon County Fair will run from Friday, August 30th, through Sunday, September 1st, 2024. Gates will open daily at 9:00 AM, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM. A special fireworks display will illuminate the sky on Saturday evening. Daily admission is $10 for adults, $5 for children aged 5-12, and free for children under five. Season passes, offering unlimited access for the duration of the fair, are available for $25. Tickets can be purchased online at SangamonCountyFair.org or at the gate.

This year's fair features a compelling schedule of headline events, including:

*   Livestock Shows: Witness the judging of champion animals from across the county.
*   Demolition Derby: Experience the excitement of this classic motorsports competition.
*   4-H Exhibits: Explore the innovative projects of local youth.
*   Blue Ribbon Baking Contest: Sample the delectable creations of talented bakers.
*   Country Music Concert: Enjoy a live performance by renowned country artist, Emily Carter.
*   Agricultural Demonstrations: Learn about modern farming techniques and sustainable practices.
