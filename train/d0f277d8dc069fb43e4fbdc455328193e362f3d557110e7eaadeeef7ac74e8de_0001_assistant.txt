SAN DIEGO, Ca - Keepers at the San Diego Zoo today were surprised to learn that one of their penguins - an Emperor penguin named Herbert - could speak. Herbert has been at the zoo for over six months, said a zoo spokesperson. During that time, neither Herbert nor any of the other penguins at the zoo showed any signs of speaking, other than the sounds typical of that species of bird. The keepers, whose names have not yet been released, were feeding the birds when they heard a voice. Following the source of the sound, they determined that it was Herbert. Emperor penguins are a species native to Antarctica and are not known to speak. Herbert was raised in captivity at another zoo and was transferred to San Diego last fall. As far as what the bird said, a source inside the zoo quoted Herbert as saying, "More fish, please."
