The **palm-up gesture** (often seen as a "stop" or "freeze" signal) can be misinterpreted globally. In many Western cultures, it’s a universal "stop" or "halt," but in some Asian countries (e.g., Japan, South Korea), it may indicate a request for a drink or coffee, or even politeness to pause conversation. Misinterpretation arises from cultural context and speed of delivery—delivered too quickly, it might be seen as dismissive rather than polite. Context and body language clarify intent.
