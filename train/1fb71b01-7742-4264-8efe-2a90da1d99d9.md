A heavy door creaks open, revealing the dim glow of a flickering lamp casting long shadows. The visitor stands hesitantly, hands clasped behind their back, their breath shallow in the stale air of the hallway.

*"You’re the one who’s been looking for me,"* the man says, voice rough as gravel, before turning to reveal the scars—deep, jagged lines across his knuckles and the hollows beneath his eyes.

The woman inside doesn’t move. Her fingers hover over the keyboard, fingers trembling, as she reads the last message:

**"The black market wants answers. They’re not just asking for names. They’re offering a deal."**
