Alice in Wonderland, written by Lewis Carroll in 1865, tells the story of a young girl who finds herself chasing a white rabbit in a waistcoat down a hole. When she reaches the bottom of the hole, she has entered an entirely different world in which nothing is like what it is in her world. 

The story ends when the Queen of Hearts orders Alice to be beheaded, and Alice wakes up, realizing it has all been a dream and she was still in the meadow with her sister where the adventure began.
