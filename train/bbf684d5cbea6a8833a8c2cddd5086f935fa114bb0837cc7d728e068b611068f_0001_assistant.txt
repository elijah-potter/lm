I looked around at the room of suspects, and suddenly it hit me. I was the murderer the whole time, and I did not even realize it. All of the evidence pointed to me. I had been walking in my sleep, that was for certain. After waking up in so many different parts of the house, that was clear. It also explains why someone had seen me at the scene of the crime the night of the murder and why I had woken up with mustard on my shirt the previous morning. All of their alibis were air-tight, and their motives were non-existent. All were shaken by the crime and had grown distrustful of each other. But why did I murder in my sleep? Then it hit me. I was drugged and made to believe I had murdered someone that night. It was none other than the maid! She was always the one to get me back into my room when I sleep-walked. She was the one to serve me my afternoon tea. She was the one to serve all the meals.
