A young girl, Ayala Yona, is bullied by girls in school. Through her friends’ advice and her own courage, she was able to stop the bullying.
This book demonstrates the now widely discussed problem of bullying through one child’s experience so that other young children might be educated on how to manage a similar situation. I believe this book’s combination of facts, the easily related to story, and the discussion at the end make it a great guide to combating bullying. I especially enjoyed the lovely illustrations set against the brown background. It was very artistic.
