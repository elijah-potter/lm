PARKING LOT - CAR - DAY

STACY and SUSAN are sitting in their CAR in the parking lot of a grocery store. STACY pulls out a NOTEPAD and PEN.

SUSAN
What's the NOTEPAD for?

STACY
We need to figure out a Father's Day gift for DAD!

SUSAN (excited)
Oh, I have an idea! How about we get him a fishing rod?

STACY (taking notes)
That could be good, but I don't know anything about choosing a good one. What if we get him some tools? He was talking about replacing his old chainsaw.

SUSAN
That sounds great!
