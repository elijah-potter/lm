In the cracked, sun-bleached corner of an abandoned warehouse near Blackthorn, beneath rusted scaffolding, a folded yellowed note was clutched in the skeletal fingers of a man who had long since perished there. The paper was brittle with age, its edges singed from years of damp and fire.

*"If you find this, the river remembers the names of those who drowned. The next full moon, the old woman in the attic will whisper her secrets to the water. Bring back the silver locket she lost. It holds the key to the door that shouldn’t exist."*

The note was signed: *"A voice from the past, now silent."*

Beneath it, a single tear—blackened with age—had dried into a crescent on the page. The ink was faded but legible: *"Drowned in the Blackthorn. Found by the tide."*

A rusted lantern sat beside it, its glass half-shattered. Inside, the last embers of a fire had long cooled into ash, but the scent of pine and old wood lingered.
