What is The National Day of Ireland?
Day of St. Patrick Is The National Day of Ireland. It Is Also The biggest of all celebrations in Ireland. St. Patrick’s Festival is named after Ireland’s patron saint and celebrates the country’s rich culture and heritage with parades, dancing, music, food and plenty of beer.
What started as a one-day holiday on March 17 is now a multi-day celebration enjoyed by more than a million locals each year. Saint Patrick is the Patron Saint of Ireland and the one credited with bringing Christianity to Ireland. The celebration of his Day is the only national holiday in Ireland.
