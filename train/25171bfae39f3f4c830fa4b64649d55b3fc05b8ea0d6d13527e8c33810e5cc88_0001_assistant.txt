There are some fun things you can add to your bucket list as an introvert afraid of heights. Here are some suggestions:

1. Solo road trip: Pack your bags and travel to the place you've always wanted to visit. A road trip is perfect for sightseeing. 
2. Go hiking: Explore nature and the great outdoors. Be amazed by the earth, insects, and trees.
3. Solo retreat: Unplug from the outside world and focus on your inner peace and happiness. Make it a point to really relax.
4. Art museum: Plan a day at an art museum checking out one-of-a-kind paintings and art pieces.
