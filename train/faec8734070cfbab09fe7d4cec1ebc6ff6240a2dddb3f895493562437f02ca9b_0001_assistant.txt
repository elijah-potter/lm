The sun was already setting, casting an orange glow through the halls of the school as Minami and Himiko walked with their bags to the shoe lockers. "Do you really not plan to attend an art university, Minami?" Himiko asked.

"Well, I don't know if I'm really going to continue art in the future. You're much more suited to that, Himiko-san," Minami replied with a small smile.

Himiko shook her head. "You know, I always wanted to make art more like yours. I don't dislike my art, but yours has something special to it. Something I wanted to incorporate into my work."

"Himiko-san?"

Himiko stopped to look out at the sunset from the second floor. "I think I'd be really sad if you gave up art. You've always been such an inspiration to me. I always thought 'as long as Minami is out there painting with that smile of hers, I'm sure there's at least a little piece of the world that has been set straight.' If you don't continue, though, what hope is there left?"

"What smile, Himiko-san? Do I look weird when I paint?"

Himiko giggled some and patted Minami's head. "I guess you've never realized it yourself, huh? You always look so radiant when you're painting. Even if you can't tell, we can all tell how much art means to you. Toga-san agrees with me. She thinks you look like some kind of art goddess."

"Come on, Himiko-san, don't tease me like that!" Minami said, swatting Himoko's hand away with a mock pout.

They laughed for a bit and then Minami said, "Maybe I should give my future some more consideration. I hadn't really thought much about it beyond the questionnaire."

"You should. I'd be glad to hear you out any time you want," Himiko said, offering Minami a warm smile.
