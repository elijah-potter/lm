Of course, here are some sandwich ideas that include cheese.

- Grilled cheese sandwich: A grilled cheese sandwich is a class comfort food that is made with two slices of bread and cheese. The sandwich is then grilled until the cheese is melted and gooey.

- Ham and cheese sandwich: A ham and cheese sandwich is a classic sandwich that is made with ham, cheese, and your choice of bread. The sandwich is then grilled or pan-fried until the cheese is melted and gooey.

- BLT with cheese: A BLT with cheese is a simple sandwich that includes bacon, lettuce, tomato, and cheese. The sandwich is typically made with white bread and mayonnaise.

- Reuben with cheese: A Reuben with cheese is a hearty sandwich that is made with corned beef, sauerkraut, Swiss cheese, and Russian dressing. The sandwich is typically made with rye bread and grilled.

- Philly cheesesteak: A Philly cheesesteak is a classic sandwich made with thinly sliced beef, cheese, and onions. The sandwich is typically made with hoagie rolls and grilled.
