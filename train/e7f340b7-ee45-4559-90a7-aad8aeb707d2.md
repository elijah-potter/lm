A dimly lit office at 11:47 PM. The fluorescent light flickers, casting long shadows. **Alex** sits hunched over a cluttered desk, laptop open, fingers hovering over the keyboard. The door creaks open, and **Dr. Chen** steps inside, arms crossed, expression unreadable. A pause. Then:

**"You’re not supposed to be here."**

Alex exhales, eyes fixed on the ceiling. **"I’m not supposed to *do* anything."** The words are raw, uneven. **"Just… explain why I’m here."**

Dr. Chen’s jaw tightens. **"You’re not supposed to be in this lab."** A beat. Alex’s gaze drops to the floor. **"I’m not a patient. I’m not a guest. I’m not anyone."**

Silence. The air between them thickens. **"You’re the one who *asked* for this,"** Dr. Chen says softly. Alex’s hands tremble. **"I didn’t ask for anything."**

A beat. The weight of the room presses down—**unspoken questions, unspoken lies.** Outside, the city hums. Inside, the past and present collide.
