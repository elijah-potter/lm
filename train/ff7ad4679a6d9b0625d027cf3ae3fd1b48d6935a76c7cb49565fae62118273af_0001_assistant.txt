Daylily plants are hardy and adaptable, making them a popular choice for gardeners across the US. These gorgeous flowering plants thrive in full sun or partial shade. Daylilies are relatively drought-tolerant, but they grow best with around 1 inch of water per week. Fertilize once in the spring with a balanced, slow-release fertilizer according to the product's instructions. However, avoid over-fertilization, as it causes excessive foliage growth at the expense of flowers. Daylilies thrive in USDA zones 4 through 9.
