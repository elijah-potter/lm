Silent deserts where no footprints linger,
Cities buried by the weight of rusted doors,
Forgotten libraries with shelves of abandoned books,
Ghosts of rivers that once ran with blood,
Subway tunnels where echoes whisper "no more",
Caves filled with the bones of forgotten gods,
Vacant parking lots with cars that never moved,
Silos storing the seeds of a world that starved itself,
The ocean’s depths where ships lie adrift,
Lighthouses turned to stones by the sea’s endless hunger,
Crypts sealed with keys lost to time,
A graveyard where only the wind remembers names.
