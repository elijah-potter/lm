That sounds so exciting! Here are some ideas:
- Desk organizers: You will need something for your pens and other things. You can make these personalized with your name on them or your favorite color while still looking professional. 
- Desk light: You will possibly need more light on your desk, depending on the lighting in your office, so a pretty desk light can help make your area look homier. 
- Inspirational quotes: You can get inspirational quotes in a picture frame or on a poster and hang this in your office. This will let you decorate without making it feel too casual. 
- Small plant: A small plant can look lovely, improve your mood, and won't look out of place in an office at all. 
- Desk mat: There are mats made for offices, which can be handy if you plan on eating at your desk or bringing coffee to work to make cleaning your area easy if you spill anything. This can be a plain color or a print, and it can look entirely professional.
