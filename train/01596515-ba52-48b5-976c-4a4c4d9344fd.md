A broken promise left a hollow ache,
Words carved in dust, their meaning faded fast.
Each unkept vow like a rusted gate,
Hinges worn thin, the door held no weight.

The weight of silence when the lips lied,
The hollow sound of "I’ll think of it."
A ghost in the morning, cold as the night,
Whispering lies to the trembling heart.

The clock ticks on, yet time’s a thief,
Erasing what was sworn in the heat of a lie.
A debt unpaid, a debt of trust,
Where promises are just echoes in the dust.
