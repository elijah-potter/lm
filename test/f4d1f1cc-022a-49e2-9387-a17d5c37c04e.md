The Parties agree that all disputes arising from this Agreement shall be resolved exclusively through **arbitration** conducted under the **ICC Arbitration Rules** or **UNCITRAL Rules**, with a neutral arbitrator selected by mutual agreement or, in case of disagreement, chosen by the **American Arbitration Association (AAA)**. The arbitrator shall have expertise in the relevant industry. The arbitrator’s decision shall be final and binding upon both Parties, enforceable as a court judgment in any jurisdiction where this Agreement is effective. Any costs, fees, or expenses related to arbitration, including legal representation, shall be shared equally by both Parties unless otherwise agreed. The Parties waive their right to challenge the arbitrator’s impartiality or procedural fairness. All communications, documents, and evidence submitted to the arbitrator shall be kept confidential, and the arbitrator’s decision shall be deemed confidential unless otherwise ordered by the arbitrator. This clause supersedes any prior or concurrent agreements regarding dispute resolution.
