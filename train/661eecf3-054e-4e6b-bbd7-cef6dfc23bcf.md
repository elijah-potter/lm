**Scene 1 (Past – 1989, Berlin)**
A young woman, **Lena**, sits in a cramped apartment above a bookshop, flipping through a yellowed notebook filled with sketches of her dreams. Her father, a disgraced artist, warns her: *"You’ll never make it. The wall is too high."* She tucks the notebook under her arm and slips into the subway, where she meets **Elias**, a street musician playing a guitar with frayed strings. He offers her a cigarette. *"The city’s a stage,"* he murmurs. *"You’re the audience."*

---

**Scene 2 (Present – 2024, Berlin)**
The same subway car hums with neon-lit commuters. **Lena**, now a 35-year-old architect, debates a project with a client over a café table. A shadow passes: **Elias**, now a tech broker in a tailored suit, slides into her seat. He hands her a phone app: *"It’s live. You’re the designer."* Outside, the same crumbling wall looms. She glances at the screen—her notebook, now digitized, opens to a blueprint of a new Berlin.

---
**Scene 3 (Future – 2047, Berlin)**
A holographic cityscape flickers above the streets. **Lena**, now a 68-year-old historian, lectures students at a rebuilt university. A young woman, **Kira**, her great-granddaughter, asks: *"What’s the point of history if it repeats?"* Lena taps her temple. *"It’s not about repetition. It’s about layers."* The wall—now a museum—glows with projections of every iteration of Berlin, from war to rebirth.

---
**Intercut Notes (Unspoken Parallels):**
- Elias’ guitar becomes a prototype for the city’s first AI architect.
- Lena’s notebook evolves into a database of forgotten stories.
- The wall’s "layers" are actual archives of parallel timelines.
