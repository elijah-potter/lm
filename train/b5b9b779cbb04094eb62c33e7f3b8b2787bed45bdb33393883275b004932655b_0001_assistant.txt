

Social activism has been around for a long time now, but it was not always called social activism. Social activism is simply finding ways to make society a better place. This could be through your actions, doing positive things, or inactions, stopping doing something that could negatively affect society.

Someone who wants to work in a social activist job will get a job that helps or serves society somehow. There are many different jobs that your child can go into that will accomplish this goal. Here are a couple of examples.

•	Disability Advocate. Someone who is a disability advocate works with people with disabilities and their families to ensure that the person with disabilities is getting what they need out of their healthcare. They know what they are eligible for.
•	Healthcare Advocate. Your child may become a general healthcare advocate. In this case, they would help with insurance questions and problems, patient complaints, and educating patients on their rights. Healthcare is a serious matter and can be very confusing, making the job of an advocate essential to the general public.
•	Food Program Coordinator. This job is a huge job for anyone interested in social activism. The help may help a community by organizing food banks or food programs. Food insecurity is already a very significant issue, and it is growing. This is a way to give back to a community or help make it a better place.
•	College Access Counselor. The level of someone's education can have a huge impact on someone's earning potential throughout their life. A college access counselor helps people apply for colleges and scholarships and helps make the overall process more manageable.
