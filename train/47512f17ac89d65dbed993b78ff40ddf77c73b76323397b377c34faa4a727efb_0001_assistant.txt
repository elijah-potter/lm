Life and death, two cosmic forces,
Like stars that twinkle in their courses.
A galaxy of existence, from birth to end,
A journey through space, around its bend.

As time moves on, the stars will age,
Their light will dim, its energy wane.
Growing cold, fading away,
Into the darkness, it will sway.

So too is life, our cosmic dance,
A journey through space, a starlit chance.
To live and love, to learn and grow,
To experience the universe's cosmic flow.

But like the stars, we'll shine once more,
As cosmic energy, in the universe, we'll soar.
