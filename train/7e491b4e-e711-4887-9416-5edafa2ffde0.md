The Grandfather Clock stood sentinel in the corner, its pendulum swinging like a pendulum of fate. Each tick and tock echoed through the silent halls of the abandoned mansion, marking the passage of time for the three trapped souls inside.

**Elias** had been counting down the minutes since he had last seen his wife. The clock's face was a canvas of despair, its hands frozen at 11:59. The door to the basement was ajar, and from the darkness came whispers of the thing that had been waiting there for decades.

**Lena**, a historian, had spent her life documenting every tick of the clock in the town’s church. Now, she was forced to witness the final seconds as the clock’s gears began to unravel. The hands moved backward, erasing her notes like ink on parchment.

**Kai**, a thief, had stolen the clock to sell, unaware of its power. As he wound it up, the hands began to dance across the dial in impossible patterns, each second revealing a memory from those who had touched it before. The moment the clock struck midnight, the walls of the mansion began to crumble—not from decay, but from the echoes of forgotten time itself.

By dawn, the three were bound together by a single truth: the clock wasn’t just a timekeeper; it was a prison, and time had become their only currency. The next morning, they found the clock’s hands pointing to **00:00:00:00**, and the mansion’s foundation had shifted just enough to reveal the hidden door beneath them. The thing that had waited for decades was no longer inside—it was now outside, hungry for the first moment of the new cycle.

The clock’s chime was the first warning. Then, the walls began to sing.
