Kokoro was your average girl from a well-to-do family. She got good grades in school and was athletic. She was the student council vice president, set to become the president when the current president retired. She was liked by her classmates for her tomboyish elegance and willingness to talk to others and socialize. She was a model student. Even those who didn't like her couldn't fault her any more than they could themselves. 

Seventeen was a renegade. She fought against the Tefit encroachment that threatened to tear apart space-time. She was the resistance's secret weapon. She was skilled, intelligent, had machine precision, and could outperform any of her comrades. She'd kept millions alive with her successful missions, the number topping out at over one hundred. She was a thorn in the side of Tefit.

These two had a shared secret.

Kokoro had dreams of a young woman in a world collapsing. She wrote stories about this woman's adventures and how she fought to protect reality. Seventeen had the ability to glimpse through space-time at other realities. She would often peer into the world of Kokoro to remind herself what she fought for. It was her only solace in a dying world.

When Kokoro publishes her first novel, though, everything turns bad. She is abducted by the government, which threatens to use her to break through space-time to conquer other worlds. Seventeen must go to her rescue on the most important mission yet.
