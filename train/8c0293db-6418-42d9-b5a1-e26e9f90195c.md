**The Last Transmission**

Static hissed between the cracked speakers of the abandoned research lab. The countdown began at **00:00:01**, counting down from the final moment before the reactor core would melt into a molten core of forgotten science.

**"Ten... Nine... Eight..."**

Dr. Elias Veyra adjusted his goggles, fingers trembling as he keyed the last command—**`SHUTDOWN ACTIVATED`**—into the terminal. The green lights flickered, then died. A deep, resonant hum filled the air, vibrating through his bones.

**"Seven... Six... Five..."**

The reactor’s alarms blared in a discordant chorus. Elias grabbed the emergency exit key, its weight familiar despite the years since he’d last used it. **"Four..."**

A sudden *crack* split the air as the glass door shattered. He bolted, the countdown now a race against the seconds. **"Three..."**

The lab exploded in a fireball of light—**`EXPLOSION DETECTED`**—but Elias was already outside. **"Two..."**

The sky darkened as the last of the emergency lights dimmed. **"One..."**

A single, desperate transmission crackled over the dead radio: *"This is the final transmission. Do not follow—"*—

Silence.
