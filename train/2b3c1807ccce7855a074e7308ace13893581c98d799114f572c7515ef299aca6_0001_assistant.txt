My human is wearing a mask,
Not sure why, I should ask,
"Bark, bark" with no reply,
I hope my human doesn't cry,
He's in the house more and more,
Watching TV is a bore,
He usually takes me out for a walk soon,
I want to show him the beauty of the moon,
Nature is still here, pandemic or not,
He could at least take me to the parking lot!
