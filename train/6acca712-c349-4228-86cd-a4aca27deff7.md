**The Quiet Afternoon**

The city hummed with unseen voices—whispers of steel and glass, the slow pulse of traffic like a metronome counting down to oblivion. I sat in the café’s dim glow, coffee steaming in my hands, watching the rain blur the windows like ink on water. Somewhere, a siren wavered, then faded into the static of the world.

A child laughed outside, the sound sharp and unfiltered, a spark in the dull current. I smiled. The storm had passed, and the sun was peeking through the clouds, golden and relentless, as if nature itself had decided the day needed a reset.

I closed my eyes, listened to the steady breath of the machine, the soft hum of the air conditioning—just me, the hum, the quiet. The weight of the world had dissolved like sugar in tea. I exhaled.
