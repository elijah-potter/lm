A winding dirt road, disappearing into misty haze.
Pavement cracks spiral like broken veins into the horizon.
A half-finished bridge leans precariously over an empty moat.
A single lamppost stands sentinel, its glow swallowed by the dusk.
The trail veers left into a dense, uncut thicket of gnarled branches.
A rusted gate hangs open, its hinges groaning in the wind.
A weathered signpost points east but its arrow is snapped in two.
The path bends again, then vanishes into a fog-choked valley.
No signs, no landmarks—only the whisper of wind through dead leaves.
