May 12, 2020
Day 2

It's now my second day here in this forest. I'm not even sure what the official name of this forest is except that it is somewhere in the Nuuanu(?) Valley. I think I should sit down, take a rest, and document what has gone on since I first entered this forest in case I am not found in time for authorities to rescue me.

Yesterday at noon, I came into this forest from a road that was about 100 feet above the valley. The path into the valley led from the parking lot where I parked my car (a rental vehicle, license plate HHH123 red Chevy Camaro convertible). 

I walked on the path for about five hours before I decided to stop and have a drink of water and a bite to eat. I don't remember exactly how the path went; it could have been going straight or it could have curved a bit, so I didn't know which direction to head back. I figured I would just walk back in a straight line the way I came in, hoping to reach the parking lot or the main road. However, as I walked for several hours, I started to panic when I couldn't hear the vehicular traffic coming from the road anymore. I can only vaguely remember hearing it when I was walking at the beginning of the path, but now I was sure I couldn't hear anything except grasshoppers and coqui frogs, and it was getting dark.

I decided to stay in one place, making a camp in case I needed to wait until the next day and building a fire. I stayed over the night and woke up the next morning and decided to chronicle what has happened so far. Now, I intend to continue walking the way I came (I think?), hoping that I will come out to the road. If I am heading the wrong way, I'm sure I will come out the other end of the forest. I have to stay calm, have hope, and remember that this is an island, so I'm bound to reach the end of this forest and the beginning of the shoreline at some point. I just need to make sure I conserve energy, food, and water until then.
