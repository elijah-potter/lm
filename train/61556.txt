The Children’s programme is aimed at children aged 8-14 years using an active, fun and participative teaching methodology in the classroom. The programme is divided into three levels, namely Kinderklas A, B and C. Children who have no knowledge of the Dutch language can begin with Kinderklas A. The topics taught in this course are introductions and greetings, simple counting, telling the time, asking for basic information etc. In addition, the children are taught how to arrange simple sentences in Dutch (questions, negatives and basic commands) using the correct grammar etc. The vocabulary they will learn includes names of objects, colors, body parts, days of the week and months, family relations, numbers, transportation etc. All of this is taught along with how to spell and pronounce words or sentences using the correct intonation.
