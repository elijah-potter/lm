Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around his cozy burrow. One sunny morning, Benny saw a shiny red apple near his favorite patch of clover. He thought, "Mmm, I wonder if it’s sweet?" Benny nibbled a tiny bite and gasped! The apple was soft and juicy, and it made his tummy feel happy.

Just then, Benny spotted a tiny, sleepy hedgehog named Hopsy curled up in the grass. "Good morning, Hopsy!" Benny said. Hopsy blinked his big, round eyes. "Good morning, Benny! Are you eating that red apple?"

Benny nodded. "Yep! It’s so good!" Hopsy sniffed the apple. "Yum! Maybe I’ll try a little too!" They shared the apple, and both bunnies giggled because it tasted even better together.

After their snack, Benny and Hopsy played a game of hide-and-seek with their friends—fluffy ladybugs and a very sleepy owl. Benny hopped so fast he almost tripped over his own feet, and Hopsy wiggled his spines when he laughed too hard.

As the day grew warm, Benny and Hopsy curled up together under a big, fluffy blanket made of pinecones. "I love this burrow," Benny sighed. Hopsy nodded. "And I love you, Benny!" Benny kissed his friend on the nose. "And I love you too!"

That night, as Benny and Hopsy drifted off to sleep, they dreamed of more adventures with their little friends—maybe finding more shiny things in the garden tomorrow. And as they snuggled close, the stars twinkled brightly above them, telling them, "Sleep well, little friends."

The end.
