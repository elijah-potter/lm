Chart your basal body temperature and predict ovulation, your most fertile time of the month to conceive a baby.
When a woman ovulates a rise in body temperature takes place in order to provide a more fertile environment for conception. By monitoring when this temperature change occurs you can determine when ovulation takes place in your cycle. Recording each day’s basal body temperature with a digital basal body thermometer will help you predict ovulation.
