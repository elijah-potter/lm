Set in 2014, the UNAIDS Fast-Track targets for accelerating the HIV response to reach the 90-90-90 goals of 90% of people knowing their status, 90% of those being on treatment and 90% of those on treatment being virally suppressed are due at the end of this year (2020). At the time these goals were set, less than 40% (13.6 million) of the 25 million people living with HIV were accessing antiretroviral therapy. By the end of 2018, global estimates were 79-78-86, highlighting the significant progress that has been made. However, gaps remain and health services need to adapt to ensure that no one is left behind.
