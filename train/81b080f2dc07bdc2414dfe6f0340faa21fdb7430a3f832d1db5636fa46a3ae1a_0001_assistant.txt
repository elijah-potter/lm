For her lifetime dream of a rustic wedding,
The bride chose an outdoor setting.
The orchard and barn were the perfect venue,
And she made sure ribs were on the menu.
When the sun went down, guests dined under the stars,
Amid twinkling candles in old Mason jars. 
After the bride had thrown her wildflower bouquet,
The groom jumped up on a bale of hay.
He raised his beer in a toast to the woman by his side,
Once a drinking buddy, now she was his bride.
