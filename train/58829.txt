Welcome to the Introduction to Shakespeare research guide!
You are about to begin a fascinating unit on one of the most influential people of all-time: William Shakespeare! Not only did Shakespeare (B-Shizzle, as I like to call him) write some of the most famous plays and sonnets in history, but his work and his life continue to influence many aspects of today's society.
By following the tabs above, you will gain access to a variety of resources relating to Shakespeare's life, his work, his modern influence, the Elizabethan time period, the Globe Theatre, and more! There are resources available for students who: need help with an essay, want assistance while reading his plays, and need more information for projects related to Shakespeare.
