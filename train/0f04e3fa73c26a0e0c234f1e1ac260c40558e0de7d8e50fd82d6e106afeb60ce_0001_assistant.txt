The Dream Team refers to the 1992 American Olympic basketball team. 1992 was the first year that NBA players were allowed to compete in the Olympics. It was considered the greatest sports team ever put together by journalists at the time. They went on to beat their opponents by an average of 44 points. They beat Croatia to win a gold medal at the 1992 Barcelona Olympics.
The Roster:
1. Magic Johnson of the Los Angeles Lakers, who was retired at the time due to HIV
2. Michael Jordan of the Chicago Bulls
3. Scottie Pippen of the Chicago Bulls
4. John Stockton of the Utah Jazz
5. Karl Malone of the Utah Jazz
6. Larry Bird of the Boston Celtics
7. Patrick Ewing of the New York Knicks
8. Chris Mullen of the Golden State Warriors
9. David Robinson of the San Antonio Spurs
10. Charles Barkley of the Philidelphia 76ers
11. Clyde Drexler of the Portland Trailblazers
12. Christian Laettner of Duke University
