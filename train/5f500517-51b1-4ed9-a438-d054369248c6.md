A weekly ritual: every Friday at 7 PM, the team gathers in the dimly lit meeting room, where flickering candles cast long shadows. The leader begins by reciting the **"First Principle Checklist"**—a structured list of non-negotiables—while the rest of the group silently nods, pen poised over sticky notes.

The **"Problem Statement"** is written in bold, with the team’s agreed-upon constraints and boundaries highlighted in red. The **"Solution Framework"** is drafted in bullet points, with constraints like "no more than three iterations" or "must include user feedback from last sprint" circled.

At the end, the **"Ritual Sign-off"** is a silent handshake across the table, accompanied by the words: *"This is how we do it. No exceptions."*

Every iteration, the same sequence repeats—sacred, predictable, and purposeful. The ritual isn’t about timekeeping; it’s about discipline, accountability, and the invisible glue that holds the team together.
