In the quiet, sun-drenched streets of **Veldora**, residents have been left stunned by the sudden and inexplicable disappearance of the city’s famed **Aurora Skywalker**, a renowned environmental activist known for her bold protests against industrial pollution. Locals recall her last public appearance at the **Central Market**, where she had been rallying crowds to oppose the proposed expansion of the **Blackthorn Smelting Plant**. Authorities confirmed her last known location near the **Whispering Falls**, where she had been documenting illegal dumping near the old canal.

The city’s surveillance drones detected no signs of struggle, and no one saw her leave her apartment in the **Obsidian Towers** complex. Her personal assistant, **Kaelis Vey**, reported that she had left a cryptic note on her desk: *"The river remembers. The sky won’t lie."* Security footage from the plant’s access gates shows no unauthorized entry, though some workers claim they saw her "walking through walls" near the smelter’s containment field last night.

The **Veldoran Police Department** has activated Emergency Protocol Alpha, deploying teams to search the abandoned **Crimson Bridge** and the **Rust Hollow**—a toxic waste dump where her research equipment was last found. Mayor **Darian Calloway** issued a statement urging calm but warned of potential "unauthorized interference" from "outside forces." Meanwhile, underground forums speculate about a conspiracy involving the plant’s owner, **Corporate Syndicate Ironvein**, and a secretive group rumored to be experimenting with "urban alchemy."

The Aurora Skywalker case has reignited debates over Veldora’s growing dependence on industrial growth, with activists demanding a full investigation into the city’s environmental policies. For now, the streets remain eerily silent, as if the city itself is holding its breath.
