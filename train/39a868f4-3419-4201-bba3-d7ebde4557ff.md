A small silver key glinted in the dim morning light as it landed on the worn wooden table. The fingers of the elderly librarian, trembling slightly, picked it up. His calloused hand adjusted the lock of the antique bookcase behind him, and with a soft *click*, the door creaked open. Inside, a leather-bound journal lay half-hidden beneath a stack of yellowed newspapers. The key’s weight felt familiar, as if it had been held for decades before being placed there.
