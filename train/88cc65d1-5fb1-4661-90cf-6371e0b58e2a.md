The individual rises at 4:30 AM, drinking black coffee straight from the carafe without sugar. They wake the cat before the alarm, which yaps until they silence it with a rolled-up newspaper. Mid-morning, they eat cold oatmeal from a jar, always with a single salt packet and a single pepper packet, never both. Lunch is a wrap made with the same tortilla every day, filled with canned tuna, mustard, and a handful of sunflower seeds. Afternoons are spent in front of a laptop, typing in all caps, pausing only to check the time on a digital clock. Evening routines include brushing teeth with a toothpaste tube they open with a paperclip, flossing with a string wrapped around their finger. Before bed, they read the same five books—always the same edition—while listening to the same 10-minute podcast, never skipping a segment. They sleep until 11:00 AM, then repeat.
