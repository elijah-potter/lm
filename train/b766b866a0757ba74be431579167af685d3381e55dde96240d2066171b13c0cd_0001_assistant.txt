Pasta with Creamy Meat Sauce
- Brown the ground beef and set aside. 
- Boil the pasta until soft, drain, and set aside.
- In a saucepan, combine the tomato sauce, oregano, basil, a little bit of water, and a few spoons full of sugar (if you have it). Stir and bring to a simmer. Then, add a bit of cream cheese to taste.  If you want it creamier, add more.
- Add the pasta and beef to your sauce.
- Stir until combined.
- Serve!
