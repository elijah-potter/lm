A dimly lit hallway echoed with the soft shuffle of high heels against polished wood. The door at the end stood ajar, revealing a single figure—**Ethan**—standing in the dim glow of a flickering desk lamp. His shoulders slumped as he stared at his empty chair, laptop open but unanswered. A half-drunk coffee cup sat beside it, condensation dripping onto the sleek surface.

His phone buzzed again—a text from **Lena**. *"You’re late. Dinner’s at 7. Meet me at the usual."* His fingers hovered over the screen, but he didn’t reply. Instead, he pulled out his keys, slid on a coat, and turned toward the door.

The lock clicked open. The hallway stretched endlessly, but his gaze didn’t follow the path. He paused, then stepped outside into the quiet night. The air was crisp, carrying the scent of rain. His phone buzzed once more—Lena’s voice on the screen: *"You’re gonna be late. Again."*

Ethan didn’t look back. The door swung shut behind him, sealing the final silence.
