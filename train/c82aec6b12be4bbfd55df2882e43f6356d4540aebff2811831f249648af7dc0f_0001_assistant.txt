I hope your niece has a happy birthday! Here are some ideas for presents for her:

1. Doll's clothes - Since she likes dolls, she will probably love a few new outfits for them.
2. Doll accessories - There are plenty of other accessories you can get for dolls that don't have anything to do with clothes. This can include things like tiny dishes, cars, or furniture. 
3. Doll organizers - If your niece has a lot of dolls, clothes for them, etc., she should love having a way to organize them. Depending on the type you buy, this could let her see all her dolls at once so she can easily decide which one she wants to play with. Her mother will probably appreciate this as well, as it should help her clean up. 
4. Personalized hat kit - Since your niece likes hats, give her a kit where she can bedazzle or color her own hat to make it personalized to be just the way she wants it.  
5. Doll games - With how popular dolls are, there are both card and board games that mention or picture dolls. Though most of these are designed for kids older than five, your niece might love them enough to be able to learn how to play them. 
6. Hat organizers - As your niece probably has several hats, try to come up with some way she can organize them so that she can still see them all. She should like this, as it will let her see all her options.
