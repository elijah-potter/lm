A sudden gust of wind sent a loose branch crashing into the back of the older man’s truck, blocking the door handle. He barely had time to brace himself before the windstorm surged, forcing him to scramble onto the running board as the driver’s side door flew open. The driver’s window shattered midair, spilling a hot coffee onto his lap. The car lurched forward, tires spinning wildly as the rear end fishtailed, leaving a trail of debris in the dust. Outside, a storm of rain, wind, and leaves pounded against the windshield, blurring the world into a swirling chaos. The man clutched his seatbelt, his breath coming in ragged gasps, while the vehicle skidded to a halt inches from a muddy ditch. The engine sputtered, then died. The storm raged on, swallowing the scene in darkness.
