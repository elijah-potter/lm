**Character’s Journal – Excerpt:**

The clock struck twelve as the rain pattered against the window, each drop a silent accusation. My hands trembled—not from the cold, but the weight of the decision I’d made hours ago. The mirror reflected a hollow-eyed man, his reflection flickering like a candle in a draft. *Did I do the right thing?*

The weight of the choice pressed against my chest, a physical force. I’d left everything behind—my past, my fears, my *identity*—for what felt like a greater truth, but now the truth gnawed at me like a rat in the walls. The silence in the empty apartment was deafening, a void swallowing my doubts.

Outside, the city hummed, indifferent. It never understood the cost of what I’d sacrificed.
