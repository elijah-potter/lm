What color is dark green?
In a RGB color space, hex #013220 (also known as Dark green) is composed of 0.4% red, 19.6% green and 12.5% blue. Whereas in a CMYK color space, it is composed of 98% cyan, 0% magenta, 36% yellow and 80.4% black. It has a hue angle of 158 degrees, a saturation of 96.1% and a lightness of 10%.
