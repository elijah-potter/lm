The question echoes in a loop of self-referential inquiry, creating a paradoxical feedback loop:

*"Why do I ask why, and why does the answer ask again?"*

This form of questioning disrupts linear thought by forcing a recursive examination—each response becomes a new question, dissolving into an infinite, oscillating rhythm of thought and reflection.

Example:
*"How do I understand what I don’t fully comprehend?"*
→ *"Understanding is the act of questioning itself."*

The echo becomes a mirror of thought, where the act of inquiry mirrors the object of inquiry, dissolving boundaries between questioner and queried.
