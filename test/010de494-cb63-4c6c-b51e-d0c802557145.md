**In a quiet turn of fate, the ancient stone bridge of Eldermere—once a symbol of resilience—collapsed during the early hours of the morning, stranding dozens of residents and travelers in the shadow of the newly formed "Grave of the Forgotten." Authorities confirmed the incident occurred around 3:17 AM when a sudden gust of wind, said to be the work of the storm gods themselves, sent the 350-year-old structure crashing into the jagged cliffs below. Witnesses described the moment as a "whisper of doom" as the last visible beam groaned before the earth swallowed it whole.**

The city of **Vaelith**, nestled between the twin peaks of Obsidian Spire and Moonveil, has been placed on a state of emergency as search and rescue teams, aided by volunteers from neighboring settlements, scour the cliffs for survivors. Rescue drones, equipped with thermal imaging, have already detected three bodies recovered near the water’s edge, though none of them were from Eldermere’s bridge. Local historian **Maester Dain** noted that the bridge’s collapse is not the first; similar incidents have occurred every century, though none since the last great storm of **1278**. "The bridge was never meant to last this long," he remarked, though his voice trembled with the weight of the unknown. The city’s priestess, **Sister Elara**, declared a day of mourning, urging residents to pray for the lost and to remember that some paths are not meant to be walked.

Meanwhile, tensions have risen in the market square, where merchants now sell "storm-proof" trinkets—charms said to ward off misfortune. The tavern *The Last Ember* has seen a surge in patrons, their murmurs a mix of grief and paranoia. One bartender, **Garrick**, muttered to a stranger, "They say the gods are hungry tonight. Bet they’ll want more than just a bridge." Outside, the wind howled through the ruins of the old watchtower, carrying whispers of the drowned past. The city of Vaelith stands at a crossroads—one where the dead walk again.
