An eerie, monochromatic forest at dusk—trees standing like skeletal fingers, their leaves rustling faintly, then still. A lone figure approaches, footsteps muffled in damp earth. The air hums with static, a whisper of voices lost to time. No sound comes from the figure’s lips; only a slow, deliberate exhale of breath, like a breath held too long. A single candle flickers behind a cracked window, its flame dancing in the void of silence. The walls of a sealed chamber whisper—*nothing*. The door clicks shut behind the figure’s retreat. Outside, the wind picks up, and the silence deepens, swallowing the world whole.
