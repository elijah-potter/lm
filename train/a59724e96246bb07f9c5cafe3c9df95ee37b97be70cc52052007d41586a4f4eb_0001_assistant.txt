Overnight Oats with Avocado, Banana, and Berries

Ingredients:

1 fresh avocado, halved, pitted, and peeled
1 small banana, sliced
2-3 Tbsp. orange juice
1/2 cup old-fashioned rolled oats
1/2 cup mixed berries
pinch ground cinnamon
salt, to taste
1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
Honey or maple syrup (Optional)

Instructions:

1. In a jar or container with a lid, combine the oats, almond milk, orange juice, diced avocado, and mixed berries. 
2. Add honey or maple syrup (optional), salt, and cinnamon. Stir to combine.
3. Cover the jar or container and refrigerate overnight or for at least 2 hours.
4. In the morning, stir the oats well and add any toppings you like, such as more fresh berries, a drizzle of honey or maple syrup, or a sprinkle of granola.
5. Serve the overnight oats chilled or at room temperature.
