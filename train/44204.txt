Things to know about voting in Delaware:
- In Delaware, people vote for the person; not for just one party. You can split your vote.
- If you do not belong to Republican Party, you can still vote for the person who shares your beliefs.
- Your vote is always anonymous. No one will know how or who you voted for! No one can ever no know how you voted.
- You are eligible to vote if you are 18 by November 8, a U.S. citizen, and a Delaware resident.
- You can register at ivote.de.gov or when you vote if you have a paper with your name and address.
- Early voting begins October 28 in each county
To find a polling place go to: gis.delaware.gov
