What is it?
The 21-Day Equity Challenge is a worldwide initiative designed to increase awareness and understanding of Diversity, Equity and Inclusion (DEI).
The activities and challenges below are intended to help participants learn more about these initiatives, staff interest areas and available Employee Resources Groups (ERGs) within the district.
Join the challenge Feb. 6-26 to learn more about our DEI work, connect with others in the district and get involved.
How do I participate?
Sign up by filling out the form on this page.
Complete the Daily Concept Activity Challenge by choosing ONE activity per day.
View the Daily Concept Reflection Activity document and complete the daily reflection responses.
After completing the 21-Day Equity Challenge, provide feedback through the final reflection survey at the bottom of the page.
