Items needed for buttered toast:
- Bread
- Toaster
- Butter knife
- Butter

Step-by-step process to make a buttered toast:
1. First. gather all of the items listed above.
2. Place bread inside the toaster and turn it on, make sure it is at a setting that will not burn the bread.
3. Once the toast is finished, carefully take it out of the toaster as it will be very hot. Place the toast on a plate.
4. Grab your butter knife and dip it into the butter, and spread it evenly on the toast.
