A silent shift in protocol where enforcement of an old rule is relaxed or deprioritized in practice, often without explicit communication. The original intention remains, but execution becomes optional or subject to informal adjustments. Example: "No food in the lab" becomes "No strong coffee, but snacks are allowed at 3 PM."

The rewrite happens through incremental, unnoticed deviations—e.g., late-night exceptions, casual workarounds, or unstated exceptions in policy documents. The result is a de facto change where compliance is now optional, leaving ambiguity in its new, fluid form.
