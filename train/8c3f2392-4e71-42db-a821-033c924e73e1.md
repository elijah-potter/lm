The weight of hesitation lifted like a storm passing—
breathing again after holding it too long.
The question you left unsaid now hums like an open door,
a space that was never closed, only forgotten.
The doubt isn’t gone; it’s not the first time you’ve stumbled,
but this time, the ground beneath you is softer.
The answer isn’t clear, but the quiet after the doubt
is the only one you need to hear.
