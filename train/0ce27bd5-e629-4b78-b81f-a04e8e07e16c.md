## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park – A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary**

The City of Oakhaven faces increasing challenges from localized flooding, urban heat island effects, and a growing need for accessible green spaces. This proposal seeks $75,000 in municipal funding to transform the currently neglected and underutilized lot at 14 Willow Creek Drive – a 0.3-acre parcel adjacent to the Willow Creek Elementary School – into the Willow Creek Pocket Park. This project will create a demonstrably climate-resilient community asset through strategic stormwater management, native plantings, shade structures, and accessible design. The Pocket Park will enhance neighborhood quality of life, provide educational opportunities for students, and serve as a replicable model for future green infrastructure investments throughout Oakhaven. Our collaborative partnership between the Willow Creek Neighborhood Association, Oakhaven Parks and Recreation Department, and Willow Creek Elementary School guarantees responsible stewardship and long-term sustainability. We are confident that this investment will yield significant returns in community well-being and environmental resilience.

**Project Plan & Timeline**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park project is structured across four distinct phases, each with assigned responsibilities and measurable deliverables.

**Phase 1: Planning & Design (Months 1-2)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (WCNA), Oakhaven Parks and Recreation Department (OPRD)
*   **Deliverables:** Site assessment report, community design workshop summary, finalized park design incorporating feedback, permit applications submitted.
*   **Activities:** The WCNA will lead a community design workshop to gather input on park features. OPRD will conduct a detailed site assessment, including soil testing and drainage analysis. A landscape architect, contracted by the WCNA, will develop a detailed park design incorporating community feedback and adhering to Oakhaven’s design guidelines.
*   **Timeline:** Two months

**Phase 2: Site Preparation & Stormwater Management (Months 3-5)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** OPRD, Willow Creek Elementary School (WCES), contracted landscaping firm
*   **Deliverables:** Site clearing and grading completed, subsurface stormwater infiltration system installed, topsoil amendment completed.
*   **Activities:** OPRD will oversee site clearing and grading. A contracted landscaping firm, selected through a competitive bidding process, will install a subsurface stormwater infiltration system designed to capture and filter runoff from surrounding impervious surfaces. WCES will assist with volunteer coordination for debris removal.
*   **Timeline:** Three months

**Phase 3: Planting & Construction (Months 6-8)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** WCNA, WCES, contracted landscaping firm, local nurseries
*   **Deliverables:** Native plantings installed, shade structure erected, accessible pathways constructed, park signage installed.
*   **Activities:** The WCNA will coordinate volunteer planting days utilizing locally sourced native plants selected for their drought tolerance and pollinator value. A contracted firm will erect a shade structure providing respite from the summer heat. Accessible pathways will be constructed using permeable paving materials. Local nurseries will provide plant materials and horticultural expertise.
*   **Timeline:** Three months

**Phase 4: Grand Opening & Educational Programming (Month 9 onwards)**

*   **Responsible Partner:** WCNA, WCES, OPRD
*   **Deliverables:** Park officially opened to the public, ongoing educational programming for students and community members, park maintenance plan implemented.
*   **Activities:** A grand opening ceremony will be held to celebrate the park's completion. WCES will integrate the park into its curriculum, offering outdoor learning opportunities. OPRD will implement a regular maintenance schedule. WCNA will organize community events and workshops focused on environmental stewardship.
*   **Timeline:** Ongoing

**Budget**

The total project cost is estimated at $85,000. We are requesting $75,000 in municipal funding, with the remaining $10,000 secured through private donations and in-kind contributions from local businesses.

*   **Landscape Architect Design Fees:** $8,000
*   **Site Clearing & Grading:** $12,000 (Contracted firm)
*   **Stormwater Infiltration System Materials & Installation:** $20,000 (Contracted firm)
*   **Native Plant Materials & Installation:** $15,000 (Local nurseries, volunteer labor)
*   **Shade Structure Materials & Installation:** $18,000 (Contracted firm)
*   **Accessible Pathway Materials & Installation:** $8,000 (Contracted firm)
*   **Park Signage:** $2,000
*   **Contingency (5%):** $3,500

The contingency fund is allocated to address unforeseen expenses that may arise during construction, such as unexpected soil conditions or material price fluctuations.  We have prioritized cost-effective materials and leveraged volunteer labor to minimize expenses. The value of in-kind donations, including volunteer time and donated materials, is estimated at $5,000.

**Community Engagement & Equity Impacts**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park project is deeply rooted in community engagement. The initial design workshop ensured that the park reflects the needs and desires of local residents. We actively solicited input from diverse community members, including senior citizens, families with young children, and individuals with disabilities. The park's accessible design, including pathways and play equipment, will ensure equitable access for all. The park’s proximity to Willow Creek Elementary School provides a valuable outdoor learning space for students, particularly those from underserved communities who may lack access to other green spaces.  We will continue to engage the community through ongoing events and volunteer opportunities.

**Risk Mitigation**

Potential risks associated with the project include inclement weather delays, material price increases, and contractor performance issues. To mitigate these risks, we have incorporated a contingency fund into the budget, secured contracts with reputable contractors, and developed a detailed project schedule with built-in buffer time. We will regularly monitor project progress and proactively address any challenges that may arise. The WCNA will serve as a key point of contact for community concerns and will work closely with OPRD to ensure responsible project management.

**Post-Grant Sustainability**

Long-term sustainability is a core principle of the Willow Creek Pocket Park project. The selection of drought-tolerant native plants will minimize water consumption and reduce maintenance requirements. The WCNA has committed to ongoing park maintenance and programming, including volunteer workdays and community events. OPRD will incorporate the park into its regular maintenance schedule. We will actively seek additional funding opportunities to support park enhancements and educational programming. A detailed park maintenance plan will be developed and shared with all stakeholders.

We urge the City of Oakhaven to approve our request for $75,000 in municipal funding to make this vision a reality. Your investment will yield significant returns in community well-being, environmental resilience, and civic pride. Together, we can transform a neglected space into a vibrant and sustainable oasis for all.
