Hello all, and welcome to the Black Rose Manor. This building is the main home on the 100-acre property of the Black Rose Estate here in the UK. The home was built in 1777 by a group of masons who were hired by the Black and Rose families to construct a virtually indestructible home that would serve as a dwelling and safe house for the endangered children of the two families. The Black and Rose families were in trouble with neighboring families who sought to dissuade the children of the two families from getting married and forming a dynastic family that would rule over all of the United Kingdom.

This building has five levels, each with two bedrooms, a chamber room, and a dining room. Also featured in this stone fortress is a torture room, located in the bowels of the home away from prying eyes. The torture room was used by the Black Rose family to persuade their enemies to give up secret plans laid against them; some of the devices used in this room include the Iron Maiden, the Rack, and a flailing apparatus.

In 1820, however, the Manor was sacked by enemies, and the entire Black Rose family, from the adults down to the children, were slaughtered. The King and Queen of the Black Rose, Edgar and Eunice, were taken into their torture chamber and suffered at the hands of the enemy Manford clan before being left for dead in a poorly-ventilated cell beneath the building.

Over the years, visitors to the Black Rose Manor reported multiple sightings of unusual phenomena around its halls, including ghostly apparitions, objects moving of their own accord, and the most unusual of them all: a feeling of absolute dread upon entering the torture chamber.
