Two detectives, **Detective Mara Voss** and **Detective Elias Carter**, reviewed the surveillance footage of the night of the murder at *The Hollow Bay*.

Mara leaned over the monitor, her fingers tracing the timestamped loop. *"They were arguing over the barstool. The guy—he was the one with the knife. The girl, she grabbed his arm. He lunged. She dropped the drink. He moved fast. He stabbed her. Then—*she fell. He ran. The knife was still in her chest. He didn’t even look back."*

Elias crossed his arms, watching the frame. *"Nah. The girl was trying to get away. The guy—he was trying to stop her. She pushed him. He went down. The knife? It was *his*—he was defending himself. The bartender saw it all. He called the cops. By the time they got here, she was already—"*

Mara cut him off, sharp. *"You’re saying she was the victim?"*

Elias exhaled, resigned. *"I’m saying we need to ask the bartender what he *actually* saw."* The screen flickered as the loop ended. Outside, a distant siren wailed.
