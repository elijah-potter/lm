Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white fur and big, round ears that always twitched when he was happy.

Every morning, Benny would hop outside to play. His favorite game was chasing his favorite friend, a bright yellow butterfly named Butter. "Flap, flap!" Benny would say, as Butter would dance around him.

One sunny afternoon, Benny noticed something strange in the garden. There was a tiny, shiny blue box with a red ribbon tied around it. Benny thought, "What could this be?" He picked it up carefully and opened it.

Inside was a magic seed! "Wow!" Benny said, smiling. He planted it in his garden, and soon, a beautiful blue flower grew right where he had put the seed.

Benny was so happy. He decided to share his secret with his friends. "Look!" he said to his friends, showing them the flower. The butterfly, Butter, was so excited too. "Let's make a garden full of magic flowers!" they all cheered.

With Benny's help, they planted many magic seeds. Soon, their garden was full of beautiful flowers in all colors. The rabbits, the squirrels, and even the birds all loved the garden.

Every evening, Benny would sit in his garden, watching his friends play and the flowers dance in the wind. He knew he had found something wonderful, and he was glad to share it with everyone.

And from that day on, Benny's garden was always full of magic, laughter, and friendship.
