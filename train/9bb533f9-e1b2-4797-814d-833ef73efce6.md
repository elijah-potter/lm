To the one who reads this—whether by chance or choice—let me ask:

What if the stories we tell are not just echoes of the past, but bridges for the future? What if the silence between words holds more meaning than the words themselves? I am not here to prove anything, only to invite you to sit with what follows, to lean into the spaces where words dissolve into meaning.

You are not alone in this. The pages, the ink, the air between us—all are witnesses. Speak to me.
