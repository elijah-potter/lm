To the Valued Members of Pounds,

I'd like to thank you for your continued support of our gym. Today, we are instituting a new policy for the equipment. 

There have been complaints of members holding equipment for use with towels, bottles, or other items for extended periods. This creates an issue as there is limited equipment for all the members to use at Pounds. Starting today, there is a five-minute limit on unaccompanied equipment regardless of whether or not personal items are in the vicinity. This is to ensure fair use of the equipment for all of our members. If you need somewhere to put your items for a longer amount of time, please feel free to use one of the many lockers along the back wall or in the locker rooms. 

Thank you for your understanding. We hope you have a great day and look forward to seeing you soon.

Regards,

Liam Bockelbee
