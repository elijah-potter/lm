I see you. Perfectly swept hair and broad shoulders, slipping through the crowd like a fish in the water. I see you. Shoulders relaxed, chin tilted in a laugh like sunshine. I see you. Mirrored eyes in a book like a mountain in a breeze. I see you. Eyes wide like a blossoming flower, ready to create. I see you. Watching, seeing. Wishing. To be like me. But I am like you.
