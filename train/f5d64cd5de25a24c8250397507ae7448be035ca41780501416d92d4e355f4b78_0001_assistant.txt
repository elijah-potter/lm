Orange, so citrusy and sour, 
it is your flesh that I love to devour. 
The tantalizing shock on my tongue 
is part of what helps keep me so young. 
From the bumps on your firm skin 
to the scent of the fruit that is within. 
The sound of your skin peeling
gives me the most amazing feeling. 
From your beautiful orange veins, 
it is so hard to refrain!
Knowing what you boast, 
it is you I love the most.
