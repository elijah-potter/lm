Title: Terrible Experience, Do Not Recommend

I am normally very open-minded to experiences, but I had a terrible time being stung by a swarm of bees. It took way too long to find a swarm of bees that would be receptive, and the actual act itself was a painful, miserable ordeal. I was in agony the entire time throughout, and there was absolutely nothing of value I obtained from the whole affair. The bees did not listen to me in the slightest and seemed to actively ignore me as they continued stinging. The worst was yet to come, however, as I had to deal with the after-effects of the stinging; swollen limbs, itchy spots, and lingering pains, and even ointments only helped a tad. Overall, I don't recommend getting stung by a swarm of bees at all, and I would recommend never trying it for as long as you live. Bees, get your act together.

1/5 stars, I would rate it zero if I could, but unfortunately, the website limitation does not allow me to do so.
