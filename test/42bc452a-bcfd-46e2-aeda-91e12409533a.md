**City of Eldermere, August 12th, 2024**

The sunrise over the Obsidian Spires last night was met with a spectacle unlike any Eldermere has seen—an unnatural glow cascading across the eastern horizon. Witnesses in the districts of Veythar and the Blackthorn Marshes reported seeing a swarm of luminous, bioluminescent insects, their shimmering patterns resembling ancient runes carved into stone. The phenomenon lasted just under an hour before dissipating, leaving behind a faint, phosphorescent residue on the ground.

Elder Councilor Veyra Duskwhisper, head of the city’s Bureau of Natural Phenomena, confirmed the event after reviewing records from the city’s automated observation drones. "This is not a natural occurrence," she said in a press briefing. "The patterns were too precise, too deliberate. If we can’t attribute it to an outside force, then it must be… something else." She added that further analysis was underway, with scientists from the University of the Veiled Mists currently investigating samples collected from the sighting area.

Meanwhile, residents in the districts near the marsh have reported disturbances in wildlife—deer with iridescent fur, fish that emit a soft, pulsating light when captured in traps, and even a few reports of small, winged creatures carrying what appear to be fragments of metallic or crystalline material. Authorities have issued a warning not to approach the affected zones without proper protective gear, which includes a newly distributed "glow-dampening" serum developed by the city’s alchemical labs.

The city’s market traders have already begun selling "glowstone" samples to the highest bidder, though prices have skyrocketed past the usual market rates. Some speculate it’s a new mineral, others claim it’s a relic from the forgotten age of the Skyborn. The local priestess of the Obsidian Altar, Sister Maris, has called the event divine intervention, though she refuses to comment further beyond her usual warnings against "unholy lights."

Tonight, the city’s clockwork sentinels are running an extended scan over the marshlands, searching for any signs of movement in the darkness. The question remains: Is this the first sign of something waking, or merely another strange turn in the eternal dance of Eldermere’s unknowns?
