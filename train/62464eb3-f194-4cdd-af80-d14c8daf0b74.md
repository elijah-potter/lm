Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a garden full of flowers, lived a tiny mouse named Mimi. Mimi loved to explore the garden every day. One sunny morning, she saw a shiny red apple in the bushes. "Mmm, a yummy apple!" she thought and took a big bite.

Just then, a little bird named Pip chirped, "Hey, Mimi! Be careful! That apple might be poisoned!" Mimi giggled and said, "Don’t worry, Pip! I’ll be safe!"

She ate the apple and felt a tiny little sparkle. "Wow, it tastes even better than I thought!" she said happily.

After eating, Mimi went back to her home and started playing with her best friend, a bunny named Benny. Benny hopped and played with her, and Mimi giggled and danced around.

One day, Mimi’s mother called her to come eat dinner. "Mimi, it’s time to eat," her mother said. Mimi ran to the kitchen and saw her favorite bowl of milk and cookies. She took a bite and felt her favorite feeling—happy and full.

That night, as Mimi snuggled in her bed, she thought, "Today was the best day ever!" She closed her eyes and dreamed of her adventures.

The end.
