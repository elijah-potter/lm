**Acoustic Sanctuary at Blackwater Cove**
A circular, dome-shaped pavilion constructed from reclaimed driftwood and organic glass panels, designed to amplify natural soundscapes. The central stage features a rotating speaker array that distributes ambient wind, water, and bioluminescent algae chants from the surrounding wetlands. Seating is arranged in concentric rings of woven rattan, allowing listeners to adjust proximity to the sound source. Entry is via a sloping path through a mist-shrouded forest, where guided meditation stations use biofeedback sensors to sync breathing with the earpiece-distributed ambient tones. The atmosphere is modulated by a system of passive acoustic dampers that filter out urban noise, preserving only the unfiltered natural resonance of the cove’s ecosystem.
