1. Harriet Jacobs: born in 1813, Harriet Jacobs is the author of the book "Incidents in the Life of a Slave Girl". 
2. Grace Hopper: born in 1906, Grace Hopper was a Navy admiral and computer scientist. 
3. Sojourner Truth: born in 1797, Sojourner Truth was a women's rights activist and abolitionist.
4. Nellie Bly: born in 1864, Nellie Bly was a journalist who broke a record by traveling around the world in 72 days by ship. 
5. Louisa May Alcott: born in 1842, Louisa May Alcott is the author of the book "Little Women".
6. Coretta Scott King: born in 1927, Coretta Scott King was the wife of Martin Luther King Jr. and the founder of the Martin Luther King Jr. Center for Nonviolent Social Change. 
7. Sally Ride: born in 1951, Sally Ride was a physicist, astronaut, and the first American woman to travel to space. 
8. Sandra Day O'Connor: born in 1930, Sandra Day O'Connor served as the first female Supreme Court Justice.
9. Jessie Benton Fremont: born in 1824, Jessie Benton Fremont was a writer and political activist. 
10. Clara Barton: born in 1821, Clara Barton was a nurse for the Union Army during the civil war and founded the American Red Cross.
