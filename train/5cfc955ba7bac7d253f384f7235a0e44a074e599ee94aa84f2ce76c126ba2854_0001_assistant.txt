Since the High Court of Australia was established in 1903, there have been 13 Chief Justices. The last three Australian High Court Chief Justices and their years of service are as follows:
- Susan Mary Kiefel AC, who has served from 2017 - present
- Robert Shenton French AC, who served from 2008 - 2017
- (Anthony) Murray Gleeson AC QC, who served from 1998 - 2008
