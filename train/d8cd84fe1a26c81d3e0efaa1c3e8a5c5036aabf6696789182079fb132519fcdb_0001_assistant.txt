The writers for a weekly late-night comedy show seem to be stuck. They cannot come up with original ideas and instead rely on Twitter for their material. It’s much easier for the writers to just find jabs at politicians or jokes about current events while scrolling their feeds than to sit in the writer’s room for hours. The result of their laziness is a stale show that has failed, for years, to captivate viewers.

Even though people enjoy the jokes on Twitter, the show seems to be unable to attract an audience. People are looking for more than just recycled content, and easy milquetoast jokes. Audiences want to see something new and exciting, and they want a bit of bite and edginess. Many shows are not keeping up with audience tastes and shifts in comedy trends. These shows are on their last leg it seems.
