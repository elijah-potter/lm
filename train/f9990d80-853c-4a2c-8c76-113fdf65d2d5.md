The dim glow of a flickering streetlamp cast long shadows across cracked pavement. A hollow hum—echoing from distant speakers—drowned out the sound of footsteps. The seeker moved in slow, deliberate arcs, knees slightly bent, hands pressed against the cold metal of the park bench they’d abandoned earlier. Their breath came in shallow, erratic bursts, the scent of damp earth and old leaves clinging to their hair.

They traced a faint, irregular pattern on the ground with the toe of their sneaker—something that wasn’t there, but *felt* as if it had always been. The air shifted, carrying whispers of voices they couldn’t hear, the weight of things they couldn’t name. A child’s laughter, once distant, now felt too close, too close to the edge of the truth they sought.

The bench creaked as they sat, fingers tracing the grooves of the worn wood like a map. Their shadow stretched unnaturally long, blending with the flickering light until it no longer moved. They reached out—not to touch, but to *feel* the absence of something that had slipped through their fingers like sand. The moment they paused, the world tilted, and for a heartbeat, the unseen presence *answered*, humming back in the space between them and the unseen.
