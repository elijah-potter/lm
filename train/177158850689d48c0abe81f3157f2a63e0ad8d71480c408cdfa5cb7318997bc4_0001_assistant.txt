The lights were low in the pizza shop,
and one forgotten pie lay on the countertop.

But a magical event was about to occur,
the likes of which would cause a big stir.

The pizza started to tremble and leaped to life with a shimmy,
it jumped up from the counter shouting, "My name is Jimmy!"

Jimmy, the pizza rolled out the door,
starting his new life with adventure in-store.
