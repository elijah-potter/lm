The healing process can vary greatly depending on the location of the piercing. For the best results, it is recommended to keep the newly pierced area clean and wait to change any jewelry until the area is fully healed.

Ears - Ear piercings heal at different rates depending on the location. Cartilage piercings such as helix, conch, and tragi have an expected healing time of 8-12 weeks. Standard lobe piercings take 6-8 weeks to heal. '

Nose - Nose piercings can be sensitive during the healing process. The expected healing time is 8-12 weeks.

Navel - The healing process is difficult for this area due to constant movement. The expected healing time is 4-6 months. 

It should be noted that everyone's bodies react differently to piercings, and while this information is a good guideline, always consult a professional piercer with any concerns.
