The clock struck twelve.
A shadow stretched long across the floor.
Silence—too heavy, like a stone in the throat.
A whisper slithered from the hallway.
The door creaked.
No one answered.
My breath hitched.
The air turned thick, cloying.
A floorboard groaned.
Something moved in the dark.
The light flickered.
Then—*cold*.
The hand reached.
I froze.
Too late.
The scream shattered the stillness.
