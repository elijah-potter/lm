Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a bunny named Benny. Benny had soft, fluffy ears and a tail that wiggled when he was happy.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his friend, a little bird named Bella, sitting on his fence. "Benny! Benny!" Bella chirped. "I found a shiny red berry!"

Benny’s eyes sparkled. "Let’s share it!" he said, and together they ate the sweet berry.

As they played, Benny’s eyes wandered to the sky. "Look, Bella! The clouds are dancing!" Bella fluttered her wings and giggled. "They’re like fluffy cotton balls!"

Benny loved how the world was full of tiny wonders. He and Bella laughed, chased butterflies, and even had a race to see who could hop the farthest. The garden was their happy playground.

When the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and orange, Benny and Bella said their goodbyes. "Tomorrow we’ll find more berries!" Bella tweeted. Benny nodded, his heart full. "Yes! Let’s make new adventures!"

As Benny curled up in his nest, he dreamed of more sunny days and friends to share them with. And so, with a happy heart, Benny bunny went to sleep, ready for another day of magic and play.
