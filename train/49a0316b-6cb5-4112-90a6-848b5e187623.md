1. **Ephemeral Thoughts**
2. *A list of the things we forget we own:*
   - The laughter that lingers like dust in a room left too long
   - A recipe for something that was never made
   - The name of a person who was never named
   - A conversation half-remembered, like a ghost at a dinner party

3. **The Invisible Ledger**
   The list expands:
   - *The keys to the house we left unopened*
   - *The unpaid debts to those who never wrote them*
   - *The promises kept in silence, like a promise to the sky*
   - *The things we hold in our pockets we don’t know we’re holding*

4. **Shadows in the Margins**
   - A page from a book we never finished
   - The address we saved in a deleted text
   - The sound of a voice we once loved but forgot
   - The weight of a decision we didn’t make

5. **The List Turns**
   - Now it’s a skeleton of what was.
   - The first item: a door left ajar.
   - The last item: the key we don’t remember keeping.
