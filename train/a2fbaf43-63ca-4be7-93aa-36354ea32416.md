**Ending 1:**
The thief found the stolen painting was a forgery, and the real masterpiece was hidden in the attic under layers of dust. He carefully restored it, only to discover it was cursed—every time it was shown to the public, a viewer would vanish in an instant. Returning it to the museum, he realized the true treasure was the mystery itself, and he left the painting behind, choosing to preserve the unsolved puzzle instead.

**Ending 2:**
The detective solved the case by tracing the stolen painting’s origins to a rogue art dealer who had been smuggling priceless works from a war-torn museum. The thief, now exposed, surrendered before the evidence reached the authorities. The painting was returned to its rightful owner, and the detective retired, knowing the greatest reward was never the art itself but the lives saved by keeping the truth buried.
