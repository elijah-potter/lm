The rain fell softly in a steady stream, each drop landing with a quiet *plink* on the cobblestone path. The lantern’s wax dripped unevenly, casting flickering shadows of the elderly gardener as he knelt beside the overgrown rosebush, his fingers brushing delicate petals. A sigh escaped his lips as he reached for the last seedling, its fragile leaves trembling. The wind carried the scent of damp earth and blooming jasmine from the garden behind him. His cane tapped gently against the stones as he stood, then walked unhurriedly toward the old wooden door, its hinges creaking softly as he pushed it open. The house was warm, the air thick with the scent of old books and the faint trace of lavender. He sat at the kitchen table, the newspaper spread across it, his cup of tea half-empty, eyes closing as he leaned back. The last light of the day spilled through the window like a slow, golden river, painting the room in hues of amber and twilight. The door closed with a final, heavy *thud*, sealing in the quiet.
