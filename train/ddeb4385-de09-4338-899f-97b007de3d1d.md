I’m trapped in a room that won’t let me leave—
the walls hum with static, the air thickens fast.
I need your hands to pull me through this trap,
but words alone won’t cut the chains that fast.

The clock ticks, the silence grows too loud,
each second steals what’s left of me. Help me now.
What’s your first move? Tell me how to go—
or am I already lost? I don’t know.
