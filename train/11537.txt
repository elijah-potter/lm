Compound words book that your students will adore! This little book includes 18 different compound words. The books are really easy to make - just fold and staple - no cutting required!
You can easily differentiate these by taking out pages to make it smaller. You can also split the pages and print a 2nd cover to make this into 2 different books for the same activity so you can do one now and one later.
For each compound word, it has the word at the top of the page and a black and white picture of the word to color in. The bottom of the page has lines for students to write a sentence using the compound word. you can also have students circle the 2 words in the compound word like in the example picture. For struggling or younger students, you could just have them write the compound word 3 times (once on each line) on the lines instead of writing a sentence.
These really help students by giving students visual of compound words they can play with - it's all about combining fun and learning! These make a great literacy station - just set out the books and they can get started right away.
