Sure! Charleston Tea Factory is renowned for its quality teas. Here are their top five varieties:

1. American Classic Tea: This is the original tea, made in black or green depending on your preference. It's completely grown at the Charleston Tea Plantation and focuses on the heritage of the product.
2. Charleston Breakfast Tea: Want a tasty tea to enjoy in the morning? This black tea is flavorful and will pack a delicious punch when you take that first sip with your morning meal. 
3. Peachy Peach Tea: If you're looking for a fruity tea, try Peachy Peach. Hints of the fruit fill every sip, providing an ambrosia-like effect in your mouth.
4. Carolina Mint Tea: Green tea mixed with mint makes for a quality breath freshener. This tea is excellent for mint lovers and will wake you right up.
5. Rockville Raspberry Tea: Another fruit option is raspberry tea. It's a little sweeter than peach tea, with the essence of raspberry through and through.
