Some Important Parts of the Eulogies
In the eulogies, actually it is not necessary to follow all of the patterns. It is more about your personal experience and expression regarding the deceased person. However, when you still do not know how to write the good one, it is good to know some examples of eulogies and the important parts to write.
For the opening, you can have a nice quote. Then, after that, you can mention the name and your relationship with the deceased person. This will be enough for the opening and it is not necessary to make long speech for this part.
- Early year
When you have some experiences regarding her or his early life, you can share the good stories about it. In case you have no experience, you may skip this part or it is possible to ask the family, so you still can mention this.
- Careers and marriage
It is also similar to the previous point. When you have no clear experiences regarding this point, it is not necessary to share it.
- Personal memories
This is the most important part. This specifically refers to your and the deceased person’s memory. You can fully share the good moments and all of the great stories.
Tips To Make the Eulogies
As what is mentioned above, basically it is more about your personal sharing of experiences. There is no clear formula or order of ideas in the eulogies. However, it is still important to make good order of speech and idea, so the audience can understand and share the same feeling as yours. In order to make the good speech, there are some useful points.
One of them is honestly. It is important to be honest. It is not necessary to make things sound excessive. This will only make people unable to believe in your stories or experiences. Since it is honest story, of course the feeling may also be felt by the audiences.
Although honesty is important, it is still necessary to organize the idea well. For example, you can start to write from points and develop them into sentences and paragraphs. In order to help you more, it is good to have some examples of eulogies. Then, you should also know the audience. It will determine how you deliver your speech and the specific tone to use in the delivery. That is why you should know the context well.
