The rain fell in silence outside the broken window, each drop a whisper of the world unseen through the cracked glass. Inside, the old desk creaked under the weight of half-finished drafts, each one more tangled than the last.

She had been writing for years, chasing words that refused to form into meaning. The first was a confession: she loved him. The second was a vow: she would never speak of it again. The third was a prayer for the courage to let go.

But courage was a word for those who didn’t know the cost of silence.

The final page read:
*"I am tired of pretending I don’t know how to stop."*

The pen hovered over the ink, the paper trembling with the weight of what came next.
