**New Haven, October 12th, 2023** – Officials in New Haven have confirmed a series of rare meteorite sightings over the past week, with fragments recovered near the downtown waterfront and along the Riverfront Park paths. Witnesses describe a bright, fiery streak crossing the evening sky around 9:47 PM last Tuesday, followed by a small, smooth stone found washed ashore at the harbor the following morning. Meteorologists at the New Haven Observatory speculate the object originated from a distant asteroid cluster, though exact origins remain uncertain.

Local scientists, including Dr. Elena Vasquez of the New Haven Institute for Space Studies, are analyzing the recovered fragments, which exhibit a distinctive crystalline structure and trace amounts of iridium. Preliminary analysis suggests the meteorite could hold historical significance, potentially dating back to the late Bronze Age based on isotopic signatures.

Authorities urge residents to avoid touching any additional recovered pieces, citing potential contamination risks. The city has activated its Emergency Response Team to coordinate with the Federal Bureau of Meteorology for further monitoring. Meanwhile, the New Haven Public Library has opened a temporary display in the central lobby, inviting visitors to submit photos or sketches of any additional sightings.

Further updates expected by tomorrow afternoon.
