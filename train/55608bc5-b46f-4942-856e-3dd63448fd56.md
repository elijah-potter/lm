Once there was a little bunny named Benny who lived in a cozy burrow under the big, fluffy grass. Benny had soft brown fur and a tail that wiggled when he was happy. Every morning, Benny would hop outside to see his friends—Lola the ladybug, Max the mouse, and Pip the squirrel.

One sunny afternoon, Benny noticed something shiny near the tree. It was a tiny golden acorn. Benny picked it up and carried it back to his burrow. "Wow," he said, "this acorn sparkles like the sun!"

Lola fluttered over. "Maybe it's magic?" Pip chattered, "Who knows? Let's see what happens!" Max nibbled a carrot. "I'll make us some snacks first."

Benny put the acorn in his pocket and shared a snack with his friends. Then, they all sat together. Benny opened his pocket, and—*poof!*—the acorn turned into a big, golden treasure chest. Inside were tiny golden apples, sparkly seeds, and even a tiny rainbow!

Benny smiled. "The magic acorn made something wonderful!" Lola clapped her wings. "Let's share it with everyone!" Max cheered, "We'll have the best picnic ever!"

So, Benny and his friends played, ate, and laughed all afternoon. And when the sun started to set, Benny knew it was time to go home. But as he hopped away, he remembered the magic of sharing and friendship. And he knew he'd find another adventure tomorrow.
