A bustling market square in a coastal village, where fishermen, weavers, and old-timers gather daily. The scent of saltwater mingles with the aroma of freshly baked bread. Children dart between adults, whispering about the latest gossip, while elders debate trade routes under the shade of olive trees. Street vendors sell handmade pottery, and the rhythm of clinking cups and laughter fills the air. Every face is familiar—some by sight, others by reputation—but trust flows like the local river.
