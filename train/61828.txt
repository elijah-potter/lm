LetterSchool Free - learn to write letters and numbers
The educational application Letterschool was created for children ages 6 and under to help them practice their letters and numbers from 1 to 10 with games.
- By: Sanoma Media Netherlands B.V.
- Last version: 1.1
- Language: English
« What a great introduction to the alphabet and to numbers! All of 1-2-3’s and the A-B-C’s are presented in a neat way with tons of colours and textures that will engage little ones in the learning process. The way that the alphanumerical characters are traced is different every time too and, most importantly, you can trace the last character free hand. I recommend this app and give it two paws up! »
