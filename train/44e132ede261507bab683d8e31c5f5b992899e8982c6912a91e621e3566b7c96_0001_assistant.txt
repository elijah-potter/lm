Sometimes, one realizes that no earthy pleasure--not an exquisite book, not a leisurely walk through the forest, not a marvelous dream that one serendipitously did not forget upon awakening--shall light up their mood, shall bring a spark their eyes, than a luxurious cup of steaming tea rich with cream, sugar, and perhaps the occasional delicate lemon wedge, infusing their drink with sharp notes of brilliant tropical citrus. As such, we have meticulously gathered over 50 ways to prepare your beverage, including chai latte, hot honey lemon tea, and ah--the magnificent spiced tea that transports one to the deepest annals of recorded history when such spices were a gift bestowed upon royalty, which one becomes as they sip this patchwork flavored drink. Truly, tea is an edible--or shall I say, drinkable--delight for the ages.
