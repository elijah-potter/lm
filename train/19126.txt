Lesson 1: Introduction to Uganda
Students will identify and use the Compass Rose.
Students will explore Uganda’s surrounding geography.
Students will explore a map of Uganda and locate its capital city.
Students will locate major bodies of water in Uganda and discover what landlocked means.
Lesson 2: Uganda Quick Facts
Students will be able to explain the colors of the Ugandan flag.
Students will be able to identify the unique landforms of Uganda.
Students will learn key facts about Ugandan people and culture.
Lesson 3: Savanna Safari
Students will explore the different terrains of Uganda.
Students will learn what makes the Ugandan Savanna unique.
Students will learn about the animals and climate in Uganda’s Savanna.
