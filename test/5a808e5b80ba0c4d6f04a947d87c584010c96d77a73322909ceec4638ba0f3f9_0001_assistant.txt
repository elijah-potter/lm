A puppy sits outside a restaurant, waiting for scraps.
A child goes in; her parents don't let her pet him.
When the child comes back out, she gives him his leftovers, a whole hotdog.
The dog eats it happily and follows the girl home.
But on the way, a rescue center is notified and the dog is picked up.
The people are kind, but the dog is scared.
A few weeks go by, and the dog watches as other pets are adopted, but he doesn't let anyone get close to him.
One day, a familiar smell comes into the shop.
In walks the little girl.
For the first time, the dog is excited and happy.
The shop owners notice and tell her so.
She recognizes the dog, and he finally gets petted by her.
He goes home with her, and they become the best of friends.
