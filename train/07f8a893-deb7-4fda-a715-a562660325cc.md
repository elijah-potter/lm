**Scene 1 (Past)**
A lone botanist in a dimly lit greenhouse, fingers tracing over a brittle leaf. The air hums with the scent of damp earth and old books. A single candle flickers on her desk. She whispers to the plants as she carefully extracts pollen from a forgotten specimen.

---

**Scene 2 (Present)**
A high-rise window overlooking a bustling city. A young woman, her hands shaking, holds a small glass vial. She’s studying a leaf sample under a microscope, her breath shallow. The camera zooms in on the petri dish, where a strange growth—glowing faintly—spreads across the edge.

---
**Scene 3 (Future)**
A ruined laboratory, its walls cracked with time. A ghostly figure moves through the shadows, humming the same tune as the botanist in Scene 1. The air is thick with static, and faint bioluminescent spores drift like dust. A single book lies open, its pages inscribed with symbols she recognizes.

---
**Scene 4 (Flashback)**
The botanist’s childhood home. Her mother, pressing a pressed flower into an album. *"This will keep its secrets."* The woman in Scene 2 watches, wide-eyed, as her grandmother’s hands tremble. The city’s neon glow bleeds into the room.

---
**Scene 5 (Clash)**
The woman in Scene 2 steps into the greenhouse, where the botanist stands motionless. The candle snuffs out. The plants twist toward her, their leaves pulsing. A voice—both ancient and familiar—whispers: *"You carry the thread."* The city’s skyline flickers behind her as the spores spread.

---
**Title: *The Last Bloom***
