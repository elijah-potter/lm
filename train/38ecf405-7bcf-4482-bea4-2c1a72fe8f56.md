A winding road that veers sharply into a dense forest, its canopy blocking the sky. The path appears to loop endlessly, disappearing into mist where the trees press too close. No signposts, no landmarks—just shifting underbrush and the occasional faint, forgotten structure half-buried in mud. A few footprints lead deeper into the gloom, but none return. The air smells damp and stagnant, carrying the scent of damp earth and something metallic, like rusted iron. No sunlight touches the ground; the path is swallowed by perpetual twilight. Ahead, the trees rise like the ribs of a sleeping giant, their branches forming an impenetrable veil. The only sound is the distant, rhythmic drip of water.
