## Proposal: The Willow Creek Pocket Park – A Climate-Resilient Community Oasis

**Executive Summary (For Municipal Funders)**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park project proposes the transformation of a presently underutilized and degraded public space – the triangular parcel bounded by Elm Street, Oak Avenue, and Willow Creek – into a vibrant, climate-resilient community asset. This project addresses critical municipal priorities: enhancing neighborhood livability, mitigating urban heat island effects, improving stormwater management, and fostering a stronger sense of community. The proposed park will incorporate native plantings, permeable paving, a rain garden, and shaded seating areas, creating a welcoming and ecologically beneficial space for residents of all ages and abilities. The total project cost is $78,500, with $39,250 requested from the municipal fund and $39,250 secured through community fundraising and in-kind donations. This project represents a modest investment with substantial returns in terms of community well-being, environmental sustainability, and neighborhood pride.

**Project Plan: Phases, Partners, and Deliverables**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park project is structured across four distinct phases, each with clearly defined responsibilities and deliverables.

**Phase 1: Design & Permitting (Months 1-2)**
* **Responsible Partner:** Landscape Architecture Collaborative (LAC), City Planning Department.
* **Deliverables:** Site assessment report, preliminary design concept, final design plans incorporating community feedback, all required municipal permits secured. LAC will lead the design process, incorporating input gathered during community workshops (described in Community Engagement). The City Planning Department will ensure compliance with all relevant zoning and environmental regulations.
* **Timeline:** 8 weeks.

**Phase 2: Site Preparation & Infrastructure (Months 3-4)**
* **Responsible Partner:** GreenThumb Construction, Willow Creek Neighborhood Association (WCNA).
* **Deliverables:** Site clearing and grading, installation of permeable paving, construction of rain garden infrastructure (underdrainage, amended soil), installation of irrigation system. GreenThumb Construction, a local contractor specializing in sustainable landscape practices, will execute the physical site work. WCNA will coordinate volunteer efforts for debris removal and initial site preparation.
* **Timeline:** 8 weeks.

**Phase 3: Planting & Amenity Installation (Months 5-6)**
* **Responsible Partner:** Native Roots Nursery, WCNA, City Parks Department.
* **Deliverables:** Procurement and planting of native trees, shrubs, and herbaceous plants; installation of benches and picnic tables; installation of interpretive signage detailing the park’s ecological features. Native Roots Nursery, a regional supplier of native plants, will provide all plant material. WCNA will organize volunteer planting days. The City Parks Department will assist with the installation of permanent park amenities.
* **Timeline:** 8 weeks.

**Phase 4: Community Launch & Ongoing Maintenance (Months 7-12)**
* **Responsible Partner:** WCNA, City Parks Department.
* **Deliverables:** Grand opening celebration; establishment of a “Friends of Willow Creek Park” volunteer group; development of a park maintenance schedule; ongoing volunteer maintenance days. WCNA will spearhead community engagement and volunteer coordination. The City Parks Department will provide technical assistance and occasional maintenance support.
* **Timeline:** Ongoing.

**Budget Transparency**

The total project cost is $78,500. We are requesting $39,250 from the municipal fund, matched by $39,250 in community fundraising and in-kind donations.  A detailed breakdown follows:

* **Design & Permitting:** $12,000 (LAC fees, permit application costs). This includes $8,000 in professional fees and $4,000 for associated application expenses.
* **Site Preparation & Infrastructure:** $25,000 (GreenThumb Construction labor and equipment rental). This covers grading, paving installation, and rain garden construction.
* **Planting & Amenity Installation:** $20,000 (Native Roots Nursery plant material, bench and picnic table purchase, interpretive signage). This incorporates $12,000 for plant procurement and $8,000 for furniture and signage.
* **Community Launch & Ongoing Maintenance (Year 1):** $3,500 (Grand opening event supplies, volunteer appreciation, initial maintenance supplies).
* **Contingency (5%):** $1,825. This allocation provides a buffer for unforeseen expenses and minor adjustments to the project scope.

The $39,250 in matching funds has been secured through a combination of local business sponsorships ($15,000), individual donations ($12,000), and in-kind donations of volunteer labor from WCNA members ($12,000, valued at $6/hour). We have prioritized local vendors and contractors to maximize economic benefits for the community.

**Community Engagement, Equity Impacts, and Post-Grant Sustainability**

Community engagement has been integral to the project’s development.  Two public workshops were held during the design phase to solicit input on park features and aesthetics.  A survey was distributed to residents to gauge preferences and identify potential concerns.  The design reflects the community’s desire for a welcoming, accessible space that promotes social interaction and provides opportunities for passive recreation.

The project directly addresses equity impacts by providing a safe and accessible green space in a neighborhood that currently lacks adequate recreational amenities. The park’s design incorporates universal design principles to ensure accessibility for individuals with disabilities.  The selection of native plants will support local biodiversity and provide educational opportunities for residents.

Post-grant sustainability is ensured through the establishment of the “Friends of Willow Creek Park” volunteer group, which will be responsible for ongoing maintenance and programming.  The City Parks Department will provide technical support and occasional maintenance assistance.  The park’s design minimizes water usage and maintenance requirements, further enhancing its long-term sustainability.

**Risk Mitigation**

Potential risks have been identified and mitigation strategies developed.  Weather delays during construction will be addressed through flexible scheduling and contingency planning.  Unexpected site conditions will be managed through a contingency fund and consultation with geotechnical experts.  Community opposition will be addressed through ongoing communication and responsiveness to concerns.  Contractor performance will be monitored through regular site visits and progress reports.

**Call to Action**

The Willow Creek Pocket Park project represents a unique opportunity to transform a neglected public space into a vibrant, climate-resilient community asset.  This project will enhance neighborhood livability, promote environmental sustainability, and foster a stronger sense of community.  We respectfully request municipal funding of $39,250 to support this transformative project and partner with us to create a lasting legacy for the Willow Creek neighborhood.  The benefits far outweigh the investment, creating a valuable resource for generations to come.
