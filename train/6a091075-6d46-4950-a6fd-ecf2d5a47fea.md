A disoriented figure stumbled into a dense, mist-shrouded forest, the faint sound of distant laughter fading behind them. A half-eaten apple lay abandoned near their feet, the remains of a forgotten picnic. Their phone’s battery was critically low, the GPS signal flickering erratically. Ahead, the trees twisted into unnatural shapes, and the path ahead seemed to loop endlessly. A sudden, cold realization settled over them: they had wandered off course, lost in this unfamiliar landscape. The only visible landmark was a skeletal figure standing at the edge of the clearing, watching them with hollow eyes.
