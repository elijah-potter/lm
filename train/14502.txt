The snake game for addition is the popular material for addition. Addition exercises are set comprehensively with the snake game and solved by changing and counting. Example of use: The child receives the addition practise 5+5+7+3+4+6. Corresponding to the practise the colourful pearls are set in a form of a snake. Then the child is supposed to start in counting up the pearl of value of 10 and puts a small golden pearl stick aside. A possible mathematic back log is balanced with a black changing pearl. Then the golden and black pearl sticks are put side by side and counted. The table contains a wooden plate, approx. 33 x 12,5 x 4 cm; 1 wooden box red, approx. 10 x 10 x 4,5 cm; 1 wooden box, golden, approx. 10 x 10 x 4,5 cm; 5 pearl stairs, coloured, value of 1 to 9; 23 golden tens pearl sticks, 1 pearl stair black/white
