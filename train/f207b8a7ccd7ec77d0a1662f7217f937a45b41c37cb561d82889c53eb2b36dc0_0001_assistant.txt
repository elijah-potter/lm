On average, cats' bodies have between 240 and 245 bones. The number varies because some cats may have extra toes or vertebrae in their tails. Additionally, male cats have a bone in their penis, called the baculum. Cats generally have 29 bones in their head, 7 bones in their neck, 20 bones in their back, 20-23 bones in their tail, 26 ribs, 2 scapulae, 1 clavicle, 3 bones in their sternum, 3 bones in each of their limbs, 2 patellas (hind legs), 14 bones in their wrists, 16 bones in their ankles, 18 bones in their feet, 3 bones per toe, 3 bones in each ear, and the hyoid bone in their throat.
