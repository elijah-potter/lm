The clock struck midnight in the abandoned subway tunnel when a flickering neon sign flickered—*"Last Exit to Nowhere"*—casting eerie blue light on the cracked concrete floor. The air smelled of damp paper and rust. A single figure, clad in a patched-up raincoat, crouched beside a rusted subway train, its doors half-open. The doors slid shut with a groan as the train lurched forward, doors clicking shut behind her.

She stepped onto the platform, where a single flickering bulb cast shadows over a half-eaten sandwich and a half-drunk bottle of water. The train pulled away, and she watched the platform vanish into the tunnel. As the last light of the station winked out, she picked up the sandwich and water, stepping into the darkness—where the sound of distant laughter echoed from the depths of the tunnel, followed by the unmistakable *clink* of metal on glass. The train’s engine hummed, but the tunnel swallowed it whole. She carried her food forward, the tunnel stretching endlessly ahead, waiting.
