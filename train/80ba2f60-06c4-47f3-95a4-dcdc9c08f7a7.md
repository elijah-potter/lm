**Markets of Timbuktu:**
Traders haggle over gold dust and ancient manuscripts in sun-baked squares. Berber weavers display handwoven carpets at dawn. Street musicians play *gnawa* rhythms under lantern-lit tents. Scholars debate philosophy and astronomy in mosques with manuscripts dating back to the 13th century. Fishermen from the Niger River barter salt for fresh catches at floating stalls. Calligraphers copy Quranic verses by candlelight, while children learn Arabic from *berber* (storytellers) under the shade of date palms. Nomadic camel caravans pass through salt flats, leaving behind the scent of *tagine* spices and the distant echo of *djembe* drums.
