The first time I saw her, she was already wearing the same dark coat she had on every evening for the past six months. I thought it was strange at first—how could someone look the same in the same clothes day after day? But I kept my distance. I had my own reasons.

She told me later she was saving it for someone special. I never believed her.

The truth was, she was waiting for a man who never showed up. The coat was his, the one he’d worn on the night he left. She’d kept it as a reminder, a small, silent confession of what she’d lost.
