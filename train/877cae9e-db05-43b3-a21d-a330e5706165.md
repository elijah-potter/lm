---
**Exterior: A crumbling stone bridge over a frozen lake at dawn.**
Mist swirls between jagged arches, thick as cobwebs. Raindrops, fine as dust, patter against the iron railing—each one a whispered regret. Below, the water reflects neither sky nor bridge but only the ghostly shapes of the drowned: children’s laughter, a father’s last kiss, a mother’s wail. A single gull circles, its wings too heavy to lift, before diving into the depths where the wind howls like a dying man.

---
**Interior: A dimly lit bookshop, shelves lined with leather-bound volumes.**
The air is thick with the scent of old paper and damp wool. A single floor lamp flickers, casting long shadows across the worn floor. A stack of books lies open on a desk: *The Time Traveler’s Dilemma*, *Eternal Echoes*, and *The Weight of Silence*. A storm rages outside, thunder shaking the windows, lightning splitting the glass like a broken mirror. Inside, the world stills—each page, each inkblot, each crease tells a story of trapped voices.

---
**Midnight: A park at the edge of a city, neon signs flickering like dying stars.**
The ground is slick with dew, a thousand footprints blurred by time. A solitary figure sits on a bench, back to the camera, their breath misting in the cold. The streetlights cast long, skeletal fingers of light across the pavement. A car honks—a single, sharp sound—like a gunshot. The figure doesn’t turn. The sky above is a bruised canvas of storm clouds, rolling in like an army of forgotten souls. Rain begins to fall, not in drops, but in sheets, soaking the earth, the pavement, the dreams of those who never woke.
