-A determined snail named Theo loves to wander in a garden, where survival is arduous. 

-Despite being mocked by insects for his slow pace, Theo always finds a way to survive. 

-One day, Theo discovered a hidden refuge beneath a protective leaf during a heavy rainstorm. This leaf soon became his hideout. 

-Every day, Theo came to the leaf. Then one day, he met a ladybug named Clara there. 

-After that day, Clara and Theo became best friends and eventually married. The leaf allowed them to find their happily ever after.
