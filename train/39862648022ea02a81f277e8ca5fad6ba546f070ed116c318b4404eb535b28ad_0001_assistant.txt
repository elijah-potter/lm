The name "Tule Lake" can refer to several different locations in California. Some of them include:

• The lake itself, which is located in Siskiyou and Modoc Counties.
• The city of Tulelake, which is located adjacent to the lake.
• The internment camp, which was also located adjacent to the lake. The Tule Lake internment camp was designated for people who did not answer "yes" to all questions on the loyalty questionnaire. Its population varied over time, but at maximum occupancy, it housed over 12,000 unlawfully-detained Japanese Americans.
