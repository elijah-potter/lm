There are many wonderful flowering vines that grow very well in Florida. Here are 4 varieties to check out:

1. Allamanda Vine - these grow in zone 10 and above, and are very fast growers with showy yellow flowers. Use caution as this one is toxic.

2. Bleeding Heart Vine - these are also fast-growing and are prolific bloomers that enjoy full sun. They can be easily cut back as desired.

3. Bougainvillea - these enjoy full sun as well, and can bloom year-round. There are thornless varieties, otherwise, watch out for thorns.

4. Carolina Jasmine- this vine can grow up to 20 feet in length and bloom from late winter through spring. The flowers are exceptionally fragrant.

Let me know if you'd like some more suggestions! Happy gardening!
