A leadership team debated extending an unfair contract to a junior developer, fearing it would undermine morale. The junior developer, who had proven their skills in critical projects, argued that fairness would strengthen team cohesion, while the manager countered that delays in decision-making could disrupt workflows. The vote split 3–2, with the manager’s perspective prevailing. Tensions rose during the meeting, and the junior developer left the room before the final decision was announced. Later, whispers spread about "who’s really in charge," and productivity dipped as team members questioned loyalty.
