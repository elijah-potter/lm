A quiet village library, late at night, with flickering overhead lights casting long shadows across the worn wooden floors. Bookshelves lined with ancient tomes, their spines slightly cracked, whisper secrets of forgotten languages. The only sounds are the soft rustle of pages turning, distant hum of a refrigerator, and the occasional creak of a door closing. A single lamplight glows in the corner, illuminating the silhouette of a librarian curled in a chair, engrossed in a book so old the ink seems to glow faintly in the dim light. A few patrons sit in plush chairs, their voices barely audible, murmuring to each other in hushed tones. The air smells faintly of old paper and fresh coffee, with a hint of rain on the distant horizon.
