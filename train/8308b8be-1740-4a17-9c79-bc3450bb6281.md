Once upon a time in a cozy little house, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the garden. One sunny morning, Benny met a little blue bird named Bella who chirped, "Benny, let's play a game!" Benny's ears perked up.

They ran through the flowers and chased butterflies. Benny had a big hop, and Bella did a little fly. "This is the best game ever!" they said together.

But then, a big shadow fell over them. It was a sleepy old owl named Oliver who had a very important question: "Why do you two run so fast?" Benny and Bella looked at each other.

"Because we love to play!" Bella said. "And because we want to share our sunshine with everyone!" Oliver nodded. "That's a wonderful way to be kind."

From that day on, Benny and Bella continued to play in the garden, and Oliver would always watch them with a warm smile. One evening, as they snuggled under the stars, Benny whispered, "Oliver, I think we made a very special friend today." Bella agreed, "And we made the world a little brighter too."

And so, Benny, Bella, and Oliver became the best of friends, always finding joy in sharing and playing together.
