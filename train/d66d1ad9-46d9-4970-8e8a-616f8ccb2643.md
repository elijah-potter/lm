Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big tree outside, lived a little bunny named Pip. Pip had fluffy white fur and a tail that wiggled like a spring. Every morning, Pip would hop outside to play.

One sunny day, Pip found a shiny red apple on the grass. "Yum!" Pip thought. But just as he took a bite, he heard a tiny squeak. Out came a baby bird with its wings still small. "Help!" the bird chirped. Pip looked around and saw a big spider web blocking the way. "I can help you," Pip said. He gently picked up the baby bird and carried it to a safe spot under a bush.

The next morning, Pip saw the baby bird hopping around happily. "Thank you!" the bird said. Pip smiled. "You're welcome!" Then they both played together—Pip showed the baby bird how to flap its wings, and the bird taught Pip how to hop higher.

As the days passed, Pip and the baby bird became the best of friends. They shared snacks, sang songs, and even built a little nest together under the tree. One evening, Pip said, "I'll miss you, but you'll be safe and warm inside my burrow when it rains." The baby bird nodded. "I'll miss you too!"

That night, Pip curled up in his burrow, dreaming of sunny days and sweet adventures with his new friend. And somewhere, a tiny bird sang a lullaby to sleep, happy to know she had a kind friend to watch over her.
