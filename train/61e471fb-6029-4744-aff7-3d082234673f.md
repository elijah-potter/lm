Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a tiny mouse named Milo who loved to explore the garden. One sunny morning, Milo spotted a shiny, golden apple near the fence. He picked it up and felt its smooth, warm surface. "What a beautiful apple!" Milo whispered, and he took a tiny bite. The apple was sweet and juicy, and Milo giggled with joy.

Just then, a little squirrel named Sammy scurried down from the tree and said, "Milo! Did you eat my favorite nut?" Milo looked around and saw that the nut was still there, but it looked a little sad. Milo smiled and said, "I didn’t take your nut, Sammy. I only ate the apple!" Sammy’s eyes sparkled, and he wagged his tail. "I like apples too!" they said together.

The two friends played together for hours—chasing butterflies, telling stories, and sharing snacks. As the sun began to set, painting the sky pink and orange, Milo and Sammy curled up in the grass. "Goodnight, Milo," Sammy whispered. Milo closed his eyes, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures.

And that was the end of their story.
