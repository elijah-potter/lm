The morning light spilled through the cracks in the blinds, softening the room into golden hues. Your fingers traced the edge of the open book—*The Alchemist*—where the last page remained unread. The words *The Universe has no favorites* whispered on the page, but your mind had already wandered.

A thought cut through the haze like a blade: *She’s not here anymore.* The memory of her laughter—sharp, bright—dissolved into the hum of the fridge. You exhaled, the breath shallow, and the clarity that had once been sharp as a blade now thinned into something brittle, like glass.

The door creaked open. A knock. Your stomach twisted. She was gone. The moment of understanding had lasted longer than you remembered, but it was already fading into the static of regret, the quiet ache of what might have been.
