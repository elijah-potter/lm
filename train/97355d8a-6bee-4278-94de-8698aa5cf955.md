A single misaligned bolt in a critical aircraft component could fail during flight, triggering catastrophic failure and loss of life. The tiny error—one that might seem insignificant on the ground—amplifies exponentially due to the system's dependency on precision and redundancy. In distributed systems, a misconfigured API endpoint might cause cascading failures across thousands of nodes, though only one node is compromised. Human errors in coding can lead to security vulnerabilities exploited by malicious actors, even if the codebase was otherwise robust. The impact of a small mistake grows with the scale of interaction, the complexity of the system, and the number of variables that must align correctly.
