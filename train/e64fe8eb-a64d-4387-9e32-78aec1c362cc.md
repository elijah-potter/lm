Once upon a time, in a cozy green house with a big red door, lived a little bunny named Benny who loved to explore his tiny world. One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside to see a big, shimmering rainbow stretching from the sky to the ground.

"Wow!" Benny said, wiggling his nose. "I’ve never seen one like this before!"

Just then, Benny spotted his best friend, a chatty squirrel named Sammy, collecting acorns. "Hello, Sammy!" Benny called. "Do you know what this rainbow is for?"

Sammy giggled. "I don’t know, Benny! But I know where to find the biggest, sweetest treats!"

Benny hopped behind Sammy and started collecting shiny pebbles, smooth stones, and soft leaves. They made a pile of colorful treasures under the big oak tree.

Later, as the sun started to set, Benny looked up at the rainbow again. "Sammy, do you think we can use our treasures to make something special?"

Sammy nodded. "Like a magic bridge to the sky!"

They gathered all the pebbles, leaves, and acorns, and carefully placed them to form a path. "Watch this!" Sammy said, pressing the first pebble gently.

A soft *whoosh* made the rainbow dance, and the path glowed with tiny golden lights. Benny and Sammy walked together, stepping carefully—oh-so-carefully—on their magical bridge.

At the top, they found a tiny, twinkling house made of stars. Inside, a tiny, smiling mouse named Mimi was waiting. "Hello, Benny and Sammy!" Mimi said. "I heard you built a bridge to the sky!"

Benny smiled. "We just wanted to share our treasures and make something special!"

Mimi handed Benny and Sammy a big, warm hug. "You did it! Now, let’s all play together. We can tell stories, share snacks, and sing our favorite songs."

From that day on, Benny and Sammy visited Mimi’s star house every evening. They played hide-and-seek with Sammy’s leaves, told stories about their adventures, and sang songs about the rainbow. And every night, as the stars twinkled, Benny and Sammy knew that their magical bridge was the best part of all.

One evening, as Benny and Sammy sat by the fire, Mimi waved from the sky. "Goodnight, Benny and Sammy!" she called. "Remember, even the smallest friends can make the biggest magic."

Benny and Sammy hugged each other. "Goodnight, Mimi!" Benny said, and with a happy hop, Sammy waved goodbye. Then, they curled up together, dreaming of their next adventure under the glowing rainbow.
