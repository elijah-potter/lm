Learning to tell time is a skill that requires lots of practice! Your student will have 19 pages of practice sheets with this free, printable booklet. There are lots of problems with analog clocks and a.m./p.m., which are important, and sometimes difficult for children to grasp. These sheets have plenty of room to write their answers, and the drawings are cheerful and easy to read.
