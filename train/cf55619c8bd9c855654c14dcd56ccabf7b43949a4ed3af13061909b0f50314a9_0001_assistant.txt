There are several non-stop flights to Australia from the United States and the length of time depends on where you depart from. A flight from Dallas, Texas to Australia could take as long as 16 hours, but if you're flying from Honolulu, Hawaii the flight is shortened to 10 hours. Currently, the longest non-stop flight comes from Singapore Airlines, which has an 18-hour and 40-minute flight connecting Singapore’s Changi Airport to New York's John F. Kennedy International Airport. That record will be lost by the end of 2025 when Qantas Airways will debut its brand new flight, totaling a whopping 10,576 miles. The flight will travel through two time zones giving passengers the opportunity of seeing the sunrise twice. It will connect Australia's Sydney Airport with the Heathrow Airport in London.
