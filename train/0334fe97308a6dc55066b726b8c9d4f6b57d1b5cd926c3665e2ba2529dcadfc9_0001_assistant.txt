Crater Lake is deeper than Lake Tahoe by 304 feet. Crater Lake's depth is 1,949 feet and it is in Oregon. Lake Tahoe is only 1,645 feet deep and it is in Nevada and California. Both lakes are considered to be one of the cleanest lakes in the world, with all of the water in Crater Lake coming from rain and snow and Lake Tahoe boasting waters with 99% purity.
