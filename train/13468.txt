Kids Writing Library
An Interactive Online Environment for Students and Teachers
Writing A-Z's Kids Writing Library is an online portal where teachers can display exemplary student writing online on the Class Writers' Wall or the Class Writers' Bookshelf allowing students to read each other's works. Students send complete writing to your Kids A-Z In Basket, where you review it and mark it to be included in the Kids Writing Library. Every time you post to the Kids Writing Library your students become published authors within your online Kids A-Z classroom.
