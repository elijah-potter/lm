Machine learning has many different algorithms to solve our problems using trained data. When algorithms are run, they seek to minimize the amount of the mean squared error as our metric for the data. This mean squared error is the sum of bias squared and variance squared. In order to reduce the overall error, sometimes we must introduce bias to the equation to make the result more successful as a whole. Further, typically the algorithm has parameters that can be tuned in order to make sure to have the best result in the end.
