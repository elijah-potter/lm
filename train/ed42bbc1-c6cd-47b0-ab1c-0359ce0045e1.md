The dim glow of flickering streetlights cast long shadows across the cracked asphalt of an abandoned lot, their reflections dancing on puddles of rainwater. Rusted metal gates, half-buried in overgrown weeds, groan slightly as the wind shifts through the skeletal remains of dead trees. A lone figure stands near the center, their silhouette obscured by the shifting light, wearing a coat too large for the cold, perhaps a remnant of another era.

A half-eaten sandwich lies on the pavement beside them, its wax paper torn, the crust of bread still damp. The person reaches into a coat pocket, retrieving a rusted keychain, each link etched with faded symbols—something from a lost past. The wind carries the scent of damp earth and distant car exhaust, the last remnants of a city that’s forgotten. A distant siren wails, its echo muffled by the quiet of the night.
