An echoing question might be one where the answer loops back to the original query, creating a self-referential or recursive inquiry. For example:

*"If a question asks, 'What does this question ask?' and the answer is its own formulation, it creates an echo."*

A classic example in philosophy is *"What is the definition of 'definition'?"* or *"How can you define something if the definition is the question itself?"* These questions evoke paradoxical or cyclical responses that reinforce their phrasing.
