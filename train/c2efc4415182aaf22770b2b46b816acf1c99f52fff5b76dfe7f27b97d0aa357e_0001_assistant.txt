Here are three types of golf clubs used by golfers:
1. Woods- They are used for long-distance shots because they have the largest heads and the longest shafts which allow golfers to swing them the fastest compared to other clubs. 
2. Wedges- They are used for short-range lofted shots into the greens and playing out of sand bunkers because they have a major angled clubhead.
3. Putters- They are used on the putting greens in order to knock the ball into the hole.
