Listen up, fashionistas! I know for sure you've all heard about the fashion rule that says wearing white after labor day is a big no-no. Well, today we will find out where this rule came from. There are several thoughts of who came up with the rule but the most accepted one says that it came from wealthy people at the turn of the century who wanted to make sure they stood out from ordinary people and the newly rich. They wore bright whites while vacationing while those that remained in the city wore darker clothes. There's another theory that says it happened after Labor Day was made a national holiday. The wealthy put away their white clothes to signify that Summer was over. 

Okay, so now that we've got that history lesson out of the way. Let's talk about breaking this so-called rule. People may think that wearing white to a party or picnic might cause a big scene, but you've got to remember that fashion is all about creativity and fun. Wearing white all year round is fine if it's what you want to do. It's definitely not disrespectful. So, If you like it, wear it!
