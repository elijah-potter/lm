The team’s daily sprint planning session started with minor friction over scope adjustments. Developer Alex insisted the new feature required a backend API change that would delay the release by a week. Manager Sarah argued it was a low-risk enhancement that could be implemented in parallel.

During the mid-week code review, Alex’s pull request triggered a cascade of breaking changes in dependencies, causing the integration tests to fail. The team spent two hours fixing it, and Alex’s morale dropped. Sarah later found a conflicting PR from another developer that had been merged without her awareness, exacerbating frustration.

By the end of the week, Alex suggested pausing the sprint entirely, citing "unmanageable complexity." Sarah responded that the team was already behind on critical bugs. The next day, Alex left early, citing burnout, and Sarah’s assistant reported a sudden drop in engagement during standups.

The conflict escalated into a full-blown team restructuring proposal, leading to a 30% attrition spike in the following quarter. The original scope adjustment became a symbol of systemic misalignment, and the project’s timeline shifted from Q1 to Q2 without consensus.
