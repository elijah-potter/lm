Tidal Pool Estuary, coastal marshlands near a tidal flat.
Rising ebb tide floods the intertidal zone, submerging the land and allowing marine life to migrate between freshwater and saltwater habitats.
Low tide exposes mudflats, drawing shorebirds, crabs, and migratory species seeking food and shelter.
Seasonal monsoons and local weather patterns create predictable surges of tourists and locals, especially during summer festivals and hunting season.
