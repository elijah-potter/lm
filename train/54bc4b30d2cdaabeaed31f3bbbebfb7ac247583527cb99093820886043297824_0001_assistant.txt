

Once upon a time, there was a very hungry gorilla who lived in the rainforest. It had been very sick and had very little energy. In fact, it had used the last of its strength to gather an armful of guava before it settled down for a much-needed rest. Just as it had settled into its tree for the night, preparing to eat, its precious armful of guavas fell out of its arms and down into the overgrowth below. Dismayed, it reluctantly came down from the tree and began to search the ground below. As it searched, it discovered they had rolled down a drop-off and into some sort of pit. As it reached its arm down into the pit, it had discovered it was much deeper than anticipated as its arm could not feel the bottom and the guavas remained several feet from its reach. Suddenly it began to cry. 

Just at that moment, a little leafcutter ant came strolling by. Taking pity on the gorilla, it asked it what was wrong. Seeing the little ant, it laughed and told it to go away as there was no way that it could possibly help. The hole was too deep and the leafcutter ant would never be able to help get the guava from such a deep hole. The ant thought for a minute and, seeming to agree with the gorilla, it continued on its way. 

About twenty minutes later, just as the gorilla had resigned itself to no food for the night, it spotted the little leafcutter ant once again. However, the leafcutter was not alone as it was also accompanied by a sea of its friends. Lines upon lines of ants disappeared down into the hole. Moments later the gorilla understood what they were doing. They brought up all of the guavas that had fallen into the hole and laid them at the gorilla's feet. Then quietly they all disappeared somewhere into the overgrowth again. The gorilla felt so humbled that it could only manage a weak "thank you" in return.
