A sudden gust of wind sent a loose branch crashing through the window, startling the elderly man at his kitchen table. His coffee sloshed over the rim of his mug, and the cup skittered across the linoleum. He blinked, his hands flying to steady the shattered ceramic, while the dog—barking frantically—leaped from the couch, tail thumping against the floor. The house’s alarm blared, echoing through the quiet neighborhood. Outside, a neighbor’s cat darted between parked cars, its ears flat. The man’s breath hitched as he realized he’d been caught in the crossfire of chaos.
