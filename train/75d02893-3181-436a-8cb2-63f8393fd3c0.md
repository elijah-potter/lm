The Eternal Maze of Veythari’s Hollow

A labyrinth of shifting stone where the sun rises and sets in cycles no clock could measure. Time bends along the floor: a child’s laughter echoes from a hallway that was once a bridge, now a ruin. The river’s current runs backward on the eastern bank, though the tide is always high. Guards in polished armor patrol the same postions for centuries, though their faces never age. The walls whisper in a language that shifts between past and present. The air hums with static—static that sometimes *replays* itself. A traveler’s shadow stretches unnaturally long, then shrinks mid-step. The clock at the grand hall ticks in reverse.
