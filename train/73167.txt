Colour Block Prints H. Hesketh Hubbard
[Baren] Editor's Introduction
Topics covered in this 'lecture':
- Survey of the history of colour printmaking, in both the west and the east.
- Anybody with an interest in the field.
The historical sections of this lecture (the first two sections) present a concise overview of the topic, and will provide the reader with a good mental image of the history of colour printmaking.
The third section deals specifically with Mr. Hubbard's thoughts on why colour block printmaking was not doing so well in his day (Britain between the wars). This editor feels that many of his points remain valid today.
