**Mayor’s Office Announces Emergency Flood Mitigation in Riverdale**
Riverdale residents are advised to evacuate immediately from flood-prone zones near Blackwater Creek as severe storms trigger rapid water rise. Authorities report over 12 inches of rain in the past 24 hours, causing localized overflow. Emergency shelters activated at the community center and fire station; public transit suspended. National Guard deployed to assist. Stay tuned for updated evacuation routes via city alerts.

**Local Farmers’ Market Holds Last Sale Before Seasonal Closure**
The annual Spring Harvest Farmers Market at Riverdale Plaza wrapped up today, marking the end of the 2024 season. Over 1,500 vendors sold produce, handmade crafts, and baked goods, with 45% of visitors reporting plans to return next autumn. Mayor Chen praised community engagement but urged supporters to contribute to the next year’s budget by April 15th. Discounted entry for seniors and children under 5 remains available until 3 PM.

**Tech Startup ‘EchoLink’ Secures $2.8M in Series A Funding**
Riverdale-based AI startup **EchoLink** announced a $2.8 million round led by TechVentures Capital. Founder Dr. Chen Li highlighted the funding for expanding its voice-activated smart home system, which currently powers 12% of smart appliances in the city. Company plans to hire 15 additional engineers by Q2 2025. "This investment accelerates our goal to make smart homes accessible to low-income households," said Li.

**Riverdale’s First “Greenway” Opens Ahead of Schedule**
The **Central Park Greenway**, a 3-mile multi-use trail connecting downtown to the riverfront, officially launched today. Designed with accessibility in mind—including ADA-compliant ramps and bike lanes—the trail features solar-powered LED lighting and native plantings. Over 2,000 volunteers contributed to its construction. City officials note that pedestrian traffic has increased by 40% since opening, with 18 new businesses opening along the route.

**Local Artist’s “Memory Lane” Exhibition Draws Record Crowds**
The **Riverdale Art Collective**’s retrospective *"Memory Lane: Reflections of 1978–2024"* drew 12,000 visitors in its first 48 hours, exceeding projections by 25%. The exhibition, featuring 150 works by 80 local artists, explores themes of nostalgia, urban change, and community resilience. A 24-hour opening on Memorial Day weekend saw lines forming outside, with free admission and live jazz performances. All proceeds fund the city’s youth arts program.
