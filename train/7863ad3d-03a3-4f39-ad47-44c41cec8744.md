Waiting for a train at a platform where the clock ticks past the announced departure time by minutes.
The hands of the clock seem to stretch into an eternity as the signal light flickers between red and green, neither of which feels right.
The station is eerily quiet, the only sound the distant hum of the city and the occasional cough of someone else waiting.
Your breath feels like it’s caught in your throat, the silence swallowing it whole.
Outside, the world moves on—someone hurries past, their urgency a stark contrast to the stillness inside.
The moment drags on like a film playing backward, each second an exaggeration of time itself.
You wonder if the train will ever arrive, if the seconds are stretching or if the world is simply ignoring you.
The platform stretches before you, a void where time seems to have dissolved.
