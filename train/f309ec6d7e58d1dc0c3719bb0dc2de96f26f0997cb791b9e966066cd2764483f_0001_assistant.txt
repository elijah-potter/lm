There was once a boy who lived in a lighthouse with his parents. There were no other families for miles and miles. The boy was very lonely. He wished that he could meet another child, but it never happened. When it was dark, he would light a candle and make shadow puppets. He pretended that the puppets were his friends. One night, he looked at his shadow and it almost looked like another boy. The boy waved at it, and the shadow waved at the same time. The boy sighed and prepared to go to sleep. Suddenly he heard a voice from the wall. "Why did you wave? Did you want to play?" Startled, the boy stared at the wall. "Who said that?" The voice responded, "It was me. Your shadow." The boy was shocked. "My shadow? You can speak? Why didn't you ever do it before?" The shadow replied, "I never thought you were interested. You always had fun playing with your puppets, so I did the same. No one ever talks to me. I get so lonely. That's why I was so happy when you waved to me!" The boy and his shadow then talked all night. It turned out the shadow had the same problem as the boy. Because there were no other children, there was no one to cast shadows for him to be friends with. The two realized they only had each other. From that moment on, they became the best of friends.
