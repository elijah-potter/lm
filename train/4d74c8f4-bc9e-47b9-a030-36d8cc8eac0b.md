Ephemeral dialogue lingers in static silence,
whispers lost to static, echoes trapped in void.
A loop of unspoken questions, hands tracing invisible paths.
Time unravels like frayed thread, memories dissolve into mist.
The pause hums like a half-finished melody,
each sentence a half-finished thought, suspended in the weight of what was never said.
The other side of the conversation remains unseen, unheard—just as the self that started it is already fading into the background noise.
The screen flickers with fragments of dialogue, words that were never meant to be spoken aloud.
A dialogue box opens and closes in infinite loops, filling the screen with the ghost of a conversation that never took form.
Invisible fingers type lines that are never sent, corrections that are never made, replies that are never answered.
The conversation exists only in the tension between what was said and what was unsaid.
A voice echoes in the dark, a single line in a sea of silence, forever half-finished.
Somewhere, a keyboard smashes keys in a desperate attempt to fill the void, but the words are never quite right, never quite enough.
The cursor blinks, waiting—forever.
