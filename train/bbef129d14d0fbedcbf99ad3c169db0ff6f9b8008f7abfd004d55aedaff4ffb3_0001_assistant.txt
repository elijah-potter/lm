1. Practice while balancing your sheet music on your head.  If it falls off then you have to start over from the beginning.
2. Sit backward on your stool and play with your hands behind your back.
3. Impersonate different famous composers or pianists while you're playing.
4. Take off your shoes and play with your toes instead of your fingers.
5. Whenever you play a note in the scale, say the silliest word you can think of that starts with the letter of the note you're playing.
