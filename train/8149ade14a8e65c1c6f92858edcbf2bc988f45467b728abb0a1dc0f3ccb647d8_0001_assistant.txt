- The 2011 St. Louis Cardinals World Series win is one of sports' all-time craziest stories
- In late August the Cardinals were 10 games behind the Brewers in the division and 10.5 games behind the Braves for the wildcard
- The Cardinals won 20 of their final 28 games, clinching the postseason on the final day of the regular season
- Notable acquisitions at the trade deadline included Edwin Jackson and Octavio Dotel
- In the first round the Cardinals were able to upset the heavily favored Phillies thanks in large part to Albert Pujols, Chris Carpenter, and Jason Motte
- In the NLCS they would face their division rival, the Milwaukee Brewers
- They would beat the Brewers in spite of poor performances from the starting rotation
- They would play the Texas Rangers in the World Series
- Down 3-2 in the series, game 6 was pivotal for the Cardinals
- David Freese would play the hero in the game by driving in two to tie the game with a 9th-inning triple, and then again by hitting the walk-off homer in extra innings
- The Cardinals would then go on to win game 7 with relative ease and clinch the World Series
- Following the season, legendary manager Tony La Russa announced his retirement
