Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white ears that flopped when he hopped. One sunny morning, Benny heard a tiny rustle in the grass. He peeked out and saw a lost little bird with a broken wing. Benny felt sad for the bird, so he hopped over and gently helped it to safety.

The bird, named Pip, was very grateful. Pip showed Benny how to find the best berries in the garden and how to spin the tallest grass into a beautiful nest. Benny and Pip became the best of friends, and every day they played and shared together. Benny learned that kindness can turn even the saddest moments into the happiest adventures. And from that day on, Benny knew that no matter how small you are, your heart is big enough to help others.
