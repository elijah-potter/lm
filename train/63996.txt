Dystonia is a brain disorder. There are several types. Dystonia causes uncontrollable body movements and postures. The brain sends chaotic signals to the muscles, telling the muscles to contract, even if the person does not want them to.
Common Dystonia symptoms include
Involuntary movements and pain in the neck
A breathy or strangled-sounding voice
A twisted leg or foot
The DMRF is here to help you get the information and support you need.
It may take a team of health care experts to successfully treat and manage dystonia. The DMRF can help you find a movement disorder specialist.
There is not yet a cure, but treatments are available. Researchers around the world are working to better understand dystonia and find a cure.
You are not alone. DMRF can connect you to other people who understand what you and your family are going through.
