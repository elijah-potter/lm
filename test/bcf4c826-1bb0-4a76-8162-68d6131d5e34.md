Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a garden full of flowers, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around and nibble on carrots, but one day, Benny noticed something strange—the flowers were wilting, and the garden looked sad.

Benny’s ears perked up and his nose twitched. He knew he had to help! So, he hopped outside and started digging with his paws, looking for the magic that made flowers grow. Beneath the roots, he found a tiny, glowing seed.

With a happy little hop, Benny planted the seed in his own garden. He watered it every day with his tears (which were made of sunshine and kindness) and waited. Days turned into weeks, and soon, the seed sprouted into a big, beautiful sunflower.

The sunflower smiled at Benny and said, *"I’m happy to help!"* Benny’s garden was now filled with flowers, and Benny knew he had done something wonderful. And from that day on, Benny loved to share his garden with his friends, making sure everyone felt happy and loved, just like the sunflower did.

The end.
