Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around the garden, munching on carrots and chasing his shadow.

One sunny morning, Benny heard a tiny *twinkle-twinkle* sound. He peeked under his burrow and saw a little star floating down from the sky. Benny was so excited! He ran to his mommy bunny and said, "Mommy, look! A star! It's falling down!"

Mommy bunny said, "Let's catch it, Benny! But be careful, it's very small and magical."

Together, they carefully reached out their paws and gently caught the star. The star twinkled brighter than ever and said, "Thank you, Benny! I was lost in the sky, but now I have a home with you. Don't forget to be kind, just like you are!"

From that day on, Benny and his mommy bunny loved to tell the story of the little star. Every evening, they would whisper, "Be kind, just like Benny," and their garden sparkled a little bit brighter.
