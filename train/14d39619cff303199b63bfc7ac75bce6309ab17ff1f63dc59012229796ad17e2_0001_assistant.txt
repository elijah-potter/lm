When it comes to crate training your dog, there are a few tactics and no-no's that you should be aware of. While many dogs end up loving spending time in their crates as they feel it is their safe space, if you don’t do it right, they could end up hating it! 

So, to crate train your dog, here’s what you need to do:

- When you bring the crate home, put it in a room where your dog feels comfortable, like your bedroom or living room. 
- Take one of their favorite blankets or pillows and put it inside.
- Leave the door open for at least one day but don’t encourage the dog to get in. 
- Show your dog the crate and let it sniff around the outside. 
- Put your dog's favorite treat or a small bowl of food inside. 
- Let the dog get in to get the treat and then get out without shutting the door. 
- Keep this practice for a few days; continually allow your dog to get in to get the treat and then get out when they want to. 
- After 3 or 4 times, place the treat in the crate, and then when the dog gets in, shut the door.
- Sit next to the crate, telling the dog they’re a “good dog.”
- After a few minutes, let them out again. 
- Keep up this practice, prolonging how long the dog stays inside the crate until they seem comfortable. 

The main objective is to make sure your dog doesn’t think the crate is a punishment but a place to relax. It's important that they know no matter how long they’re in there, you will always let them out.
