Here is a list of birds that make good pets:

1. Budgies - Budgies, also known as parakeets, are friendly and affectionate birds that are great at being trained and can even learn to talk. Budgies are generally about 6 to 8 inches in length.
2. Cockatiels - Cockatiels are another friendly and affectionate type of bird that is incredibly intelligent and tend to whistle and mimic common household noises. They generally are about 12 to 13 inches in length.
3. Doves - While most people think of doves as being wild, they are actually sweet and gentle birds that can be easily trained. They are usually about 11 to 13 inches in length.
4. Parrotlets - Parrotlets usually have big personalities and love to play and socialize. They tend to be around 4 to 5 inches in length.
5. Green-Cheeked Conure - Green-cheeked conures love to spend time with their owners and tend to be intelligent and playful birds. They are generally 10 to 11 inches in length.
6. Canaries - The canaries are friendly and cheerful birds that are easily trained and cared for, though they don't usually care for heavy handling. Canaries are usually 5 to 8 inches in length.
