Absolutely! Here are five fun and easy recipes kids can learn with an adult:

1. Chex mix- Chex mix is composed of melting some butter and adding in whatever you want to a dry bowl of Chex mix, pretzels, marshmallows, chocolate bits, and whatever else you like. 

2. Rice Crispy Treats- Always an easy go-to, just melt butter and marshmallows together and then add the Rice Krispies cereal. Mix the ingredients and spread on a baking dish and cut into squares. 

3. Ants on a log - Let the kiddies get involved by having them put the peanut butter on the celery sticks and topping them with raisins or “ants”. This is a very hands-on experience for all. 

4. Ice cream sundaes- The world is their oyster with this option. Kids get to choose their favorite ice cream along with their favorite toppings-peanut butter, pretzels, gummies, chocolate fudge, whipped cream, and whatever else they like. The excitement of making something exactly the way you like it. 

5. Mini pizzas- These are sure to please the crowd. Take some English muffins and treat them as the perfect canvas for these little artists. Bowls of their favorite cheeses, meats, and veggies will excite the whole crew.
