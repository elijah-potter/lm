• Question: Who was linked to roughly 40% of restaurant food poisoning outbreaks between 2017 and 2019?
   Answer: Food workers who showed up while sick were linked to food poisoning outbreaks in restaurants. 

• Question: What germs were the common cause of the outbreaks?
   Answer: Norovirus and salmonella were the common cause. 

• Question: What agency wants better enforcement of food safety policies?
   Answer: The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention wants better enforcement of food safety policies.
