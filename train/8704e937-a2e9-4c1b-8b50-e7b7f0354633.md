**The Whispered Secret of Blackthorn Manor**
Rumors claim Blackthorn Manor’s late owner, Elias Veyne, left behind a hidden ledger detailing his final experiments—allegedly proving an ancient alchemical formula that turns "base metals into gold" through rare, "divine" silver ore found only in the abandoned mines of the Whispering Peaks. Witnesses say the ledger’s last entry, smudged with ink, reads: *"The key is the moon’s sigh in the dead of night."* Locals avoid the old watchtower where the ore was last harvested, whispering of visions where silver dust glows like starlight.

**Key details for verification:**
- **Location:** Whispering Peaks (abandoned mines near Blackthorn Manor).
- **Mysterious "divine" silver ore:** Distinctive luster, reacts to moonlight.
- **Elias Veyne’s estate:** Contains a sealed vault labeled *"For the Pure"* (last seen in 1897).
- **Unusual occurrences:** Strange echoes, silver tools that "self-repair" overnight.
- **Last known sighting:** A lone miner (Martha Hargrove) claims to have found a "shard" of ore near the tower—vanished after.

**Source:** *Gilded Ledger of Elias Veyne* (partial, 1897), *Local chronicles of Blackthorn’s "ghost miners,"* and a single, uncredited witness account from 1922.
