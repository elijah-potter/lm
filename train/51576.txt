"Cantor" is an ancient term that has come to have multiple meanings, including "leader of the people's song." Cantors historically have proclaimed the Word of God most especially in psalmody, and in our context provide opportunities for people to participate in the dialogue of Liturgy - the "work of the People of God." Cantors are selected for their leadership skills, their musical ability, and their desire to proclaim God's word in the midst of the people gathered. Cantors receive formation in the skills necessary to carry out their ministry. The Cantor information page which includes the schedule and materials is available here.
