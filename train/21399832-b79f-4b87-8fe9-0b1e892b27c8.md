The **Chronospheric Observatory of Zorvath** is a towering, obsidian spire embedded in the heart of **Eldrin’s Veil**, where the city’s inhabitants measure time in **cyclical "Solar Echoes"**—each day lasting **24 "Harmonic Hours"** instead of 24 hours.

Time is divided into **three phases**:
- **Dawn Echoes** (6 hours) – when the city’s clockwork oracles predict future events via resonant chimes.
- **Midnight Echoes** (12 hours) – the deadliest period, marked by a perpetual "ticking silence" where the city’s gates seal shut.
- **Dusk Echoes** (6 hours) – when the **Celestial Mirror** reflects a distorted future, allowing scholars to "rewind" minor mistakes via magnetic levitation.

The calendar follows **"The Great Resonance"**, where each year is split into **12 "Solar Echoes"**, each containing **24 Harmonic Hours**. The **Zorvathian astronomers** use a **crystal-sphinx** to count "echoes" by detecting harmonic dissonance—when the city’s clockwork strikes at the wrong frequency, time resets in a loop.

Local currency is the **"Echo-Tick"**, earned by solving paradoxes in the **Echo Chamber**, a vault where time bends unpredictably. The city’s laws are written in **"Harmonic Law"**, where crimes are punished by **forcing the offender to "echo" their sentence**—a ritual that rewinds their last action by 30 minutes, repeated until the "harmony" is restored.
