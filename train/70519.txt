5 Ursae Minoris
5 Ursae Minoris is a variable and multiple giant star in the constellation of Ursa Minor.
5 Ursae Minoris visual magnitude is 4.25. Because of its moderate brightness, 5 Ursae Minoris should be easily visible from locations with dark skyes, while it can be barely visible, or not visible at all, from skyes affected by light pollution.

Distance of 5 Ursae Minoris from the Sun and relative movement
5 Ursae Minoris is distant 344.61 light years from the Sun and it is moving far from the Sun at the speed of 10 kilometers per second.
Spectral properties of 5 Ursae Minoris
5 Ursae Minoris belongs to spectral class K4 and has a luminosity class of III corresponding to a giant star.
