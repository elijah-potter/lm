Starting from Hôtel des Invalides, proceed south towards Boulevard des Invalides. Onto Boulevard des Invalides, turn left. Up until you reach Pont Alexandre III, a stunning bridge that spans the Seine River, stay on Boulevard des Invalides straight. As you proceed straight along Avenue Winston Churchill, cross the Pont Alexandre III. As you continue down Avenue Winston Churchill, you will eventually come to the Place de l'Alma roundabout. Take Avenue de New York's third exit from the roundabout. The Eiffel Tower will soon be in front of you if you stay on Avenue de New York.
