Why Go To Rio de Janeiro

Known as the Cidade Maravilhosa (Marvelous City), this glimmering Brazilian metropolis has certainly earned its title. Here are a few uniquely Rio reasons to visit.

-  Rio is lined with white-sand beaches to catch some sunshine or waves
- Lush rainforests surround staggered green mountains, giving a panorama like nowhere else
- Admire the striking 125-foot-tall Christ the Redeemer statue, overlooking Rio's pristine beaches 
- From the shores of Copacabana and Ipanema, you'll admire the picture-perfect backdrop that has attracted Cariocas (native Brazilians born in Rio de Janeiro) and visitors alike for more than 500 years
