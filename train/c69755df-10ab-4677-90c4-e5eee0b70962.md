A critical vulnerability in the authentication system’s TLS certificate chain caused a single failed certificate validation check during peak traffic, triggering an automated failover to a backup server with a modified DNS TTL (1 hour). This allowed unauthorized access to the primary database after 45 minutes of the breach, resulting in a 20% loss of customer data before the incident was detected by a third-party monitoring service. The single detail—misconfigured certificate trust chain—created a domino effect in the security chain, leading to cascading failures across the entire network infrastructure.
