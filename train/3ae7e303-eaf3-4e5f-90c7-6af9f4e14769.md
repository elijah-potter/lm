The house creaked.
Silence settled like a shroud.
A key turned in the lock.
Gasping, I opened the door.
Darkness swallowed the air.
The walls seemed to breathe.
A whisper—soft, then louder—slithered from the shadows.
My pulse hammered like a trapped thing.
Lightning split the sky.
The door slammed shut behind me.
Another voice. Not human.
The air turned thick.
My vision blurred.
A scream tore from my throat.
The room dissolved.
I was gone.
