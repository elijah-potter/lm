A certificate course in 3D animation covers the entire scope of animation. Starting from the history of animation and principles of animation, it covers all the skills that are necessary for such a course. They include the different production processes, figure drawing, design, creating effects, modelling, animation techniques, simulation etc. students also get to study different animation films, scripts and screenplay and they also know the techniques of storyboarding. They become skilled at drawing and designing. They understand the effects of colour and lighting in animation.
This certificate course opens doors to the much-sought-after field in today’s entertainment industry. Students who have completed this course may start as junior animators and gradually become senior animators. Today, animators are in much demand than any other professionals. Students who can create what they can imagine are the perfect fit for this course.
