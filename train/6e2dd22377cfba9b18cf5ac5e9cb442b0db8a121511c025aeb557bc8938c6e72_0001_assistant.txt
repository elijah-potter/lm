There once was a long road made of gold brick across a beautiful field and leading into the mountains. It was said in several ancient texts that the road was built by the gods; some followers of the old religion believed the road was sinister and would lead travelers away from the light and into darkness. But everyone who laid eyes on the road knew it to be special, even ethereal.

One day, a follower of the old religion broke away from his monastery and decided to go to the road to investigate further. He was not satisfied with the ancient texts or the religious teachings he received in the monastery. The follower, becoming increasingly disillusioned by the world around him, longed for something more, something to fill the hole in his heart. He took his chances and started walking down the golden path on a journey that led him to the mountains ahead.

Hours passed as the follower continued on his way, the day turning into night. As night fell, the road began to wind and curve more while becoming more wooded and dense with foliage. Soon, thunder began to boom through the ethereal corridor, and lightning flashed through the storm clouds above. Soon, the follower approached the summit of the mountain; on the mountain was a golden city with grotesques guarding the wrought-iron gate entrance. The place had an ominous feel that turned into doom when the large, hulking visage of a grey dragon just outside the city walls, stood guard. The dragon stepped out of the darkness, spotted the follower, and breathed oven-hot air his way, cooking the tunic on his chest. The dragon lumbered toward the follower, chasing him away from the city and back down the mountain along the golden road. 

This place was not meant for visitors, the follower acknowledged.
