Daisy Eagan is an award-winning stage and television actress, writer, and activist, and now can add podcaster to her list of roles. At only 11 years old, she won a Tony Award, she was the youngest actress to win such a prestigious award. She has appeared in several TV series, including The Path, Girls, and The Mentalist. Eagan is also an award-winning writer, activist, and a rousing voice in politics.
