IREAD-K, IREAD-1, and IREAD-2 were created to help schools monitor student progress and provide appropriate instruction prior to grade three. Schools may choose to use these "no-stakes" comprehensive literacy assessments, which provide teachers with the feedback necessary to track student progress and to make instructional decisions that will help ensure student success on IREAD-3. These assessments are administered during the last four to five weeks of the school year. Use of the IREAD-K, IREAD-1, and IREAD-2 assessments is a local decision.
The purpose of this Item Sampler is to provide parents, teachers, and students with examples of the types of questions that may appear on the Indiana Reading Evaluation And Determination K-2 (IREAD K-2) Assessments.
Literacy Toolkits for Parents
The purpose of the Literacy Toolkits is to provide parents with activities they can do with their children to help them master foundational reading skills and prepare for the IREAD K-2 and IREAD-3 Assessments.
