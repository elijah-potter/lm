The rain fell in sheets against the cracked windows of an old apartment, each drop echoing like a whispered confession. Inside, **Mira** sat hunched over a cluttered desk, a half-finished sketchbook open before her, fingers tracing the edges of a faded yellowed note pinned to the corkboard above. The paper bore a single, scrawled promise: *"I’ll never let you down again."*

She traced the ink with her thumb, the paper curling slightly beneath her touch. The words had been written in a hand she recognized—one that had once filled her with fire and fear. **Elias**, her younger brother, had vanished three years ago, leaving only this note and a single photograph of them as children, smiling beside a fire escape in the city’s outskirts.

The apartment smelled of damp wool and old books. The radio in the corner played static, the only sound breaking the silence. Mira’s breath hitched. She had tried to forget him, but some things—like this promise—were written in stone, etched into the walls of her mind. The night was long, and the promise still burned.
