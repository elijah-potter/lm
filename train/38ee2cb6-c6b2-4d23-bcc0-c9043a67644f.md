A crumbling stone archway, half-submerged in mist, marks the boundary between two worlds. The earth beneath is damp, cool, and hums faintly with unseen energy. Shadows stretch long, pooling at the edges where the path veils into darkness. Above, the sky is a bruised bruise of dusk—no sun, only the ghostly glow of an aurora bleeding through the clouds like spilled ink. The air carries the scent of damp earth and something older, something like old wood and iron. This is not a door you step through but a gap you fall into.
