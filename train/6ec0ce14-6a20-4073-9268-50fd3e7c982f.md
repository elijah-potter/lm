A dim streetlamp flickered above the alley, casting long, wavering shadows. A figure in a rumpled coat stood hunched over, staring at the pavement. Their breath came in shallow gasps, fingers trembling as they picked up a half-burnt match. The glow from the ember didn’t match the scene—nothing moved, nothing stirred—but the air hummed with an unnatural, metallic tension. They crouched, hands hovering over the cracked asphalt, as if waiting for something to appear beneath the cracks. A whisper of static crackled in the air, and the figure let out a soundless, terrified laugh before collapsing into the dirt.
