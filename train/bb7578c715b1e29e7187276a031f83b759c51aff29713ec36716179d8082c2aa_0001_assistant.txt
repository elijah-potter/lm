Once upon a time, there was a little elephant named Joey who was afraid of the dark. One night, he was so scared that he couldn't sleep. He woke up his mom and told her that he was afraid because it was dark. His mom hugged him and said there was nothing to be afraid of, but her little boy didn't believe her.
The next day, Joey's mom took him to the library. They found a book called "The Magic of the Dark." They took the book home and read it together that night. The book was about all the magic that happens when it is dark.  It said the dark is the time fairies come out and grant all your wishes from the day. Joey was fascinated.
That night, the little elephant went to bed and closed his eyes. He thought about the book he had read, and that the fairies were out ready to grant all his wishes. He began to imagine that the dark was a magical place, full of wonder and excitement. He started to feel sleepy, and soon he was dreaming of all the amazing things he would see in the dark. Joey was not afraid of the dark anymore.
