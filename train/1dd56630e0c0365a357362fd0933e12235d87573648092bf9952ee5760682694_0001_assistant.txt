The hyacinth symbolizes rebirth and renewal. Beautiful and whimsical, hyacinth flowers offer a captivating dynamic to any garden. With their soft, full petals, hyacinths evoke playfulness and joviality. In Greek mythology, the hyacinth is named for the hero Hyacinthus. According to the myth, Hyacinthus' blood fell to the ground, and hyacinth flowers grew to form the ground as a result. Thus, it is a popular symbol of springtime and new beginnings.
