A joint is where 2 or more bones meet in the body, it allows movement. 
There are 6 different types of joints:
1.  Ball and socket Joint e.g. hip joint
2. Hinge Joint e.g. elbow joint
3. Condyloid Joint e.g. finger joint
4. Pivot Joint e.g. in the forearm
5. Gliding Joint e.g. wrist joint
6. Saddle Joint e.g. base of thumb.
