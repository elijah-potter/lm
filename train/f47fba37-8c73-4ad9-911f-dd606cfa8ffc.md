The **Temporal Clock Tower** of **Chronosburg** stands in the heart of a moonlit valley where the sun rises from the west and sets from the east. Its tower is carved from obsidian, its face divided into **twelve hour-long arcs** rather than the usual twelve hours. Each arc is labeled with a celestial event: **"Midnight Eclipse," "Dawn's Whisper," "Midnight Storm,"** etc.

Time flows differently here. A **tidewatch**—a living crystal embedded in the tower—pulses in sync with the valley’s **underground river**, which runs backward. The river’s current dictates the rhythm: when it flows from east to west, the tower’s hands move **forward**; when it reverses, time loops like a pendulum.

Visitors must **bend their own clocks** by aligning their wrist devices to the tower’s **phase shift markers**. A day here equals **12 hours of "normal" time**, but the valley’s inhabitants count it as **one "valley hour"**—a unit that expands or contracts based on emotional resonance with the river’s flow.

The **Temporal Bridge** at the valley’s edge connects to a parallel dimension where time skips in **7.5-hour segments**, like a clockwork marionette. Locals trade memories in **half-time units**, and some say the tower’s base is a **black hole** that warps time itself.
