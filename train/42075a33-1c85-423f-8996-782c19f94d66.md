The door creaked open slowly, revealing the dim glow of a streetlamp through the narrow window. A sharp rap echoed from the other side—two quick knocks, then a pause, then another.

Inside, the living room smelled of old books and damp wool. The fire in the hearth had burned low, casting long shadows across the worn leather armchair where Elias sat, fingers tracing the spine of a book he’d already read three times. His breath hitched. Another knock. This time, deliberate. Three sharp taps, then silence.

The door inched open wider, and the figure in the hall—tall, cloaked in a coat that matched the night—stepped inside without a word. Their face was obscured, but Elias felt the weight of their gaze before it landed on him.

*"You were expecting me,"* the stranger said, voice rough with something like relief.
