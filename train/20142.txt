Kalimat (Arabic Words)
Levels: A1, A2, B1
Set of 80 Cards for each level
Derivation makes the Arabic language easy to learn. Alyasameen made groups of most important roots and the most important words.
The cards are divided into three levels (A1, A2, B1).
Each level contains 80 cards, and each card contains between nine to 18 words. Therefore, you will learn more than 1,000 important words per set.
Our selection of the roots is based on two important criteria:
• The importance of the words, which the root contains.
• The number of words inside the root. There are no roots, which contain few words.
You will find quick response (QR) code on each card, so you can scan it and listen to the correct pronunciation of each word.
