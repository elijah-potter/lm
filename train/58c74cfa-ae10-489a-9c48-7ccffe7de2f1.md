A vast, silent chamber with walls lined by mirrored surfaces reflecting an endless void. The air hums faintly with static, a low-frequency drone like distant thunder. Shadows stretch unnaturally long across the floor, flickering like smoke from a long-dead fire. At its center, a single orb of swirling plasma pulses weakly, its glow casting eerie, shifting patterns—some resembling figures, others abstract shapes that defy interpretation. The absence of movement feels more oppressive than any crowd; every surface, every corner, seems to whisper *you are not alone*. Even the silence presses in, thick and suffocating, as if the space itself is holding its breath.
