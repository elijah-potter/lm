A faint, distorted shadow moved across the periphery of my vision, lingering just beyond the scope of my peripheral awareness. The dim light of the abandoned lab flickered like a dying bulb, casting eerie reflections on the cracked floor. My heart pounded as the sound of wet footsteps echoed—too slow, too deliberate—from the hallway. I reached for the door handle, fingers trembling, but hesitated. The air smelled of rust and something metallic, like old blood. My pulse raced as the figure stepped into the light, cloaked in a tattered cloak, their face obscured by a hood. They stood still, then turned, their eyes fixed on me. I swallowed hard, my mind racing with questions: Was it a hallucination? A trick of the half-light? Or had I truly seen something that shouldn’t have been there? The silence stretched, broken only by the distant hum of a broken machine, whispering secrets no one wanted to hear.
