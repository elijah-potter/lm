To the reader who stands at this threshold of words, neither known nor lost,

You hold in these pages more than ink and paper—you hold a mirror. Reflect not your own face, but the edges of the story you are already living. The silence between the lines is not empty; it is the space where your breath meets the words, where your curiosity is the ink and your hesitation the quill.

Would you like to know what happens next? Or perhaps, like a river, you will find your own course through these words? The choice is yours, yet the question lingers: *Why do we read at all, if not to be changed?* This is not a tale to be read, but a conversation to be walked into. Step forward if you dare.
