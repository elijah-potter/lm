A dimly lit alleyway glistened with the faint reflections of streetlights. The cold wind carried the scent of rain and exhaust fumes. A lone figure moved toward the shadows near the dumpster, their silhouette blurred by the mist. Their head was turned slightly, eyes scanning the crowd, but the crowd was not looking back. The figure paused, a hand instinctively raised to shield their eyes, and stepped aside to let a group of college students pass. The students moved past without a glance, their laughter and chatter muffled by the distance. The figure exhaled sharply, watching the students’ backs disappear into the night. They turned away, vanishing into the labyrinth of alleys behind them. No one would remember their face tomorrow.
