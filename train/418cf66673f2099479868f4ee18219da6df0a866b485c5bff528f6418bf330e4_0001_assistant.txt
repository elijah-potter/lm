Here are 10 unique wedding cake flavors:

1. Chocolate Raspberry Truffle- a chocolate cake that is brushed with Chambord Liqueur ad filled with dark chocolate ganache and has raspberry preserves. 

2. Chocolate Cappuccino Torte- a chocolate cake featuring cappuccino mousse. 

3. Salted Caramel- a roasted almond cake brushed with Amaretto and filled with caramel-infused buttercream. It also has a thin layer of salted caramel. This makes a good choice for the winter. 

4. Caramel Apple- a vanilla cake featuring a caramel mouse and fresh spiced caramel apple accents. This is a good choice for the fall. 

5. Lemon Summer Berry- a vanilla cake with lemon mousse and fresh raspberries and strawberries. This is a good choice for the summer. 

6. Tuxedo Torte- a chocolate cake with a vanilla mouse. 

7. Fresh Strawberries & Cream- a vanilla cake with freshly sliced strawberry accents. This is a good choice for the summer. 

8. Southern Hazelnut Praline- a vanilla cake brushed with Frangelico and is brushed with hazelnut praline buttercream. It has a thin layer of dark chocolate ganache.

9. Cafe Latte-  a layered chocolate and roasted almond cake brushed with Kahlua and a chocolate espresso buttercream and dark chocolate ganache filling. 

10. Chocolate Orange Citrus- a chocolate cake with Grand Marnier, dark chocolate ganache, and orange marmalade.
