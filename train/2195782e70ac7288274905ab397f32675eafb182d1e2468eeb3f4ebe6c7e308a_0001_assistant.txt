To change a tire, you need a tire jack, a spare tire, and a tire gauge. And here’s what you need to do: 

- Find a safe place to pull over. 
- Put on your hazard lights.
- Take out the necessary materials.
- Loosen the lug nuts with the tire gauge (lefty-loosey.)
- Place the tire jack under the metal part of the vehicle.
- Turn the handle on the jack to lift the car off the ground. 
- Remove the lug nuts. 
- Remove the old tire. 
- Put the new tire on. 
- Replace the lug nuts. 
- Secure them in place with the tire gauge (righty-tighty; go in order in a clockwise direction to keep the tire straight.)
- Turn the handle to lower the car to the ground. 
- Remove the tire jack.
- Put everything away and get back on the road.
