Snowball was a fluffy cat with an even fluffier heart. He loved his owner Sally so much that he would purr and coo whenever Snowball saw the human. One day, Snowball lazily slept in the sun as he did every afternoon when he suddenly woke up in a panic! He almost forgot Sally’s birthday was at the end of the week!

Snowball decided to steal Sally’s iPad when she wasn’t looking and quickly went online to purchase a purr-fect gift for her, but he couldn’t decide because there were so many to choose from! Eventually, the feline caught sight of an item called a “cat banana costume.” What was that, exactly?

Running out of time, Snowball clicked “Buy Now” and made sure Sally saw it on her birthday. She thought it was adorable, and so did Snowball, but then she put Snowball in it and started taking pictures! Even though the cat was embarrassed, he was happy Sally liked the gift.
