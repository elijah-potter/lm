Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny had soft brown fur and big, round eyes that sparkled like stars.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his favorite friend, a little blue butterfly named Butter, fluttering near his window. "Good morning, Benny!" chirped Butter. "I saw a shiny golden flower in the garden!"

Benny hopped out of his burrow and ran to the garden. There, in the middle of the field, stood the golden flower Butter had talked about. It was as tall as Benny's nose and shimmered like sunlight.

"Let’s pick it!" Benny said excitedly.

Butter flapped its wings. "Wait! The golden flower needs a special friend to help it grow."

Benny looked around. He saw a little mouse named Mimi, playing with her toy ball near a bush. "Mimi, come play with us!" Benny called.

Mimi ran over. "I can’t play with you today," she said. "I need to help my mom with her garden."

"Butter said the golden flower needs a friend!" Benny said.

Mimi smiled. "I’ll help you all the same. Let’s take care of the golden flower together."

So Benny, Butter, and Mimi worked together. Benny gently picked petals, Butter blew on the flower to make it sparkle, and Mimi watered it carefully. Soon, the golden flower began to grow taller and prettier.

The other animals in the garden saw them and came to help too. A kind old owl named Owl taught Benny how to jump higher, and a wise old tortoise named Turtle helped Mimi plant seeds.

Soon, the golden flower had more leaves than ever. It turned into a big, beautiful flower with petals that sparkled in the sun.

Benny, Butter, and Mimi danced around it, and all the animals cheered. "We did it!" they said happily.

From that day on, the golden flower stayed in the garden, and every morning Benny, Butter, and Mimi would come to help it grow. And whenever they worked together, the garden became even more magical.
