When the unexpected unfolded—notes scribbled in the margins of a half-finished spreadsheet became a tapestry of characters and choices. A missed deadline turned into a chase scene, a budget overrun became a hidden cache of stolen gold. The "what-ifs" of contingency plans spiraled into dialogue, dialogue into dialogue, until the original spreadsheet was buried under layers of narrative. The audience didn’t know they were watching a spreadsheet until the final reveal: the hero’s arc was the spreadsheet’s last row, the villain’s betrayal the overdue invoice, and the climax the "out of stock" notice that became the cliffhanger. The story wasn’t planned—it was the plan’s way of telling itself.
