Once there was a little bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the sunny meadow. One day, Benny spotted a tiny mushroom with a bright red cap. It glowed softly in the golden sunlight.

Benny picked it up and ate a little bite. Suddenly, Benny felt a magical sparkle inside his paws! Benny started dancing with his bunny ears flapping. He hopped higher and faster than ever before!

A friendly fox named Felix saw Benny and giggled. "Benny, you’re dancing like you’re on a magical dance floor!" Benny grinned and showed Felix how to wiggle his tail. Together, they danced until their paws got tired, but they still laughed and shared a snack from Benny’s basket.

That night, as Benny snuggled in his cozy burrow, he dreamed of dancing with the stars. And Felix knew Benny was the happiest bunny in the whole meadow!
