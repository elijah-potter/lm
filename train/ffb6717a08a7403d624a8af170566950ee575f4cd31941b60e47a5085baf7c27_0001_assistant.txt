This was it the final round, and the final point he needed to win. The fame, the glory, the famous teapot were all within his grasp - just a single nine-letter word away. The babble of the present became nothing but a dull murmur in his ear as his concentration and anticipation rose. *click*. The scramble is revealed, AECLESCPT.

His mind rushed into overdrive, writing down every possible combination, racking his brain for every 9-letter word he knew.

*tick* *tock* The countdown clock reached the quarter mark.

He spared a glance at his competition who looked as equally determined and confused.

*tick* *tock*. The music gets even more intense in the studio. A headache begins to form, "Surely they made a mistake? There can't be a solution."

Then "SPECTACLE" a small voice in his head spoke. That's it!

"SPECTACLE" he shouted.

*tick* The clock stopped.

He had done it, with seconds to spare. He had finally won Countdown. The teapot was his. The crowd cheered and his opponent sighed.
