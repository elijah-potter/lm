A crumbling stone wall, half-buried in ivy, stretches across a patch of overgrown lawn. The bricks are loose, some cracked and missing, revealing exposed mortar. The lower half of a wooden doorframe, warped and painted peeling in patches, leans against a dilapidated wooden fence. Rust stains the nails embedded in the fence’s rotting planks. Above, a shattered window in a sagging, boarded-up window frame lets in dappled light through broken glass. The floor tilts unevenly beneath a single, sagging beam that supports a ceiling of exposed wooden rafters and peeling plaster. Dust collects in corners, and cobwebs drape across a broken desk with a cracked monitor and a half-empty coffee cup. A half-assembled shelf, nails bent and tools left scattered, clings to a wall where paint flakes to reveal the original color beneath. The air smells faintly of mildew and old paint.
