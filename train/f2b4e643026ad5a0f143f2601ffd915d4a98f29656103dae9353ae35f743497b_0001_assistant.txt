He's out for me. I can't stop him. I'm not sure what to do. He's a man, hunting, led only by his hunger. I am but an ordinary peanut butter sandwich and he will eventually find me. He's searching in the kitchen now, which would be the likeliest of places to leave a peanut butter sandwich behind, but I'm not there. For now, I'm safely hidden under the couch, which is where the dog took me after I was stolen off the man's plate. I only hope that the dog doesn't find me first, because I'd be gone in a matter of seconds.

I stay still (because I'm a peanut butter sandwich, after all), and I wait for someone to sniff me out. I'm not sure how long I have, but the seconds tick by like hours. I see the outline of four paws - the dog is near. He's looking for me, and extending one of his long, hairy paws under the couch in a futile attempt to retrieve me, but I'm too far under the couch. I'm now covered in dust and fur, which isn't too appetizing. The silhouette of the man's feet appear; he sees the dog searching under the couch for something. He lifts the couch - the moment of truth is here! I am found and promptly thrown away.
