Mind Mapping is a cognitive science technique for making associations using a graphical map. An the main idea or "node" is at the center of the mind map followed by branches of additional nodes. The link between one node to another shows the level of knowledge related to the target concept or idea. The following paper explains how to create Mind Maps using a "free" open source tool know as FreeMind. A Sun Up Alarm Clock Mind Map is the target sci-tech gadget for the Project of the Month.
