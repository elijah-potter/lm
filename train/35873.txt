It’s a Noisy Planet. Protect Their Hearing.® is a national public education campaign, developed by the National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders, part of the National Institutes of Health. Noisy Planet is designed to increase awareness among parents of children ages 8 to 12 about the causes and prevention of noise-induced hearing loss (NIHL). With this information, parents and other adults can encourage children to adopt healthy hearing habits before and during the time that they develop listening, leisure, and working habits.
