The threshold of an abandoned library’s grand reading room—its high ceilings sagging under decades of dust, the scent of old paper and damp wood lingering in the air. Bookshelves creak as the last reader lingers, their fingers tracing the spine of a forgotten tome. The floor is a carpet of loose pages, some fluttering in the half-light where a single, flickering bulb casts long shadows. A door, slightly ajar, whispers of another world beyond—perhaps a library of secrets or a room that hums with the voices of those who have passed. The air is thick with the weight of unfinished thoughts, half-heard conversations, and the promise of what might be discovered—or lost—once the last candle burns out.
