A whispered vow in the stillness of dawn,
A candle lit behind locked doors,
A single tear turned to salt in a cup,
A shadow stretched thin across the floor—
No fanfare, no trumpet’s blare,
Just the hum of a key in the dead of night,
The scent of rain on parchment,
A stolen hour when the world held its breath,
And love, though never spoken aloud,
Still bloomed like a secret garden,
Unnoticed, unclaimed—
Yet alive.
