Of course! Here are 4 ideas for decorating your vases.

1. Rocks: Fill the vase with some rocks. You can layer the rocks based on coloring. For example, the first layer can consist of white rocks. The second layer could be earth-toned rocks.
2. Flowers: Using fake flowers, create a unique bouquet inside the vase. A tip for making this decoration pop is to use different colored flowers as well as different types of flowers. Find flowers that come in a bouquet, then snip individual stems and place them inside the vase.
3. Glitter: Cover a portion of the vase (or the whole vase) with glue. Sprinkle your favorite glitter combo onto the vase and leave it to dry for a couple of hours.
4. Stacking: With a glue gun, you can turn one vase into a taller vase. Take one vase, and flip it upside down so that the opening faces the floor. Then hot glue the bottom of another vase and set it on top of the vase you have on the floor.
