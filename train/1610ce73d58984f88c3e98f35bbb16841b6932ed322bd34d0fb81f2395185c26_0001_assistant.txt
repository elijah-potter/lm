The Mediterranean diet has decades of evidence showing its health benefits. The typical Western Diet is high in fat, salt, and processed ingredients. Italy, France, Greece, and Spain eat a healthier Mediterranean diet with vegetables, fruits, healthy fats, and whole grains. They don't eat many processed foods and refined grains and sugars. 

Many people shy away from the Mediterranean Diet because they believe eating fruits, vegetables, and fish will be more expensive. But new studies have shown that families save money when switching to the Mediterranean Diet. It also helps give the dieter more key nutrients such as vitamin B6, calcium, and fiber that are missing from traditional Western diets. The finding of these studies showed that families saved an average of $78/wk for a single person, $135/wk for two adults, and $211/wk for a family of four.

As you can see, although a new diet can be daunting, the Mediterranean diet can be both cost-effective and healthier than the traditional Western diet. You might even enjoy it.
