The jagged cliffs of Veythar’s Obsidian Spire rise like a fractured skull against the storm-wracked sky. Below, the jagged shoreline cuts through jagged ice, where the sea hisses against the black rock like a thousand whispering tongues. The air smells of salt and damp ozone, thick with the weight of unspoken secrets. The wind carries the distant toll of the ancient bell, its echo dying in the hollow between the peaks. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, clinging to the ledges where the last light bends like a dying breath. The horizon is a blur of smoke and motion, where the last of the dusk-walkers vanish into the mist, their lanterns fading into the dark like forgotten prayers. Here, the world feels like it’s waiting to break.
