Normally an extended thesis or report is known as dissertation and students who are in the verge of getting PhD degree need to submit the thesis on time. Dissertation is basically a lengthy essay which discusses on a given topic related to the subject of specialization of the student. There are some specific steps which are mandatory to follow at the time of writing a dissertation. The topic on which dissertation need to be written must be selected by the student only. But in case there is some difficulty in making the selection then student can request the supervisor to make the selection of the topic or guide to pick the right topic. The topic needs to get the approval of the supervising panel before student’s starts writing it. There are some mandatory chapters which are must to present in the report and if any one of or more is missed in the report then the supervising panel has every right to deduct marks of the student. The source of information and important evidences used in preparing the report needs to be attached at the end of the thesis for the verification of the supervisor at the time of evaluation.
