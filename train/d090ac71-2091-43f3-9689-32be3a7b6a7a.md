The door creaked open under the hesitant touch of a finger, revealing a dimly lit hallway lined with peeling wallpaper. A single bulb flickered above a stairwell, casting long shadows as a silhouette stepped inside—tall, cloaked in a heavy coat, boots crushing the dust beneath. The knock came again, sharper this time, echoing from the other end of the room.

The door swung open wider, revealing the interior—a cozy but cluttered living room where a low fire crackled in the hearth, casting dancing shadows on the walls. Inside, a woman sat at the kitchen table, her hands wrapped around a steaming mug, her eyes meeting the stranger’s for the first time. A knife lay across the table, its blade gleaming dully in the firelight.

The stranger exhaled slowly, the scent of damp earth and something faintly metallic curling around the edges of his breath. *"You keep this place too quiet for a woman who’s always home,"* he murmured, stepping forward. His voice was low, rough-edged, like gravel underfoot.
