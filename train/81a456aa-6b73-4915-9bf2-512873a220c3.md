Lily the bunny had a big, fluffy tail that wiggled when she hopped.
One sunny morning, she met Max the mouse who lived in a tiny house under the tree. "I hear the birds singing, and the sun is so warm!" Max said.
Lily’s ears perked up. "Let’s go to the garden and play! The flowers are bright and sweet!"
They hopped and squeaked together, picking flowers to eat. A big, shiny apple rolled toward them—oh no! It was too heavy to carry. Max giggled, "We’ll share it!"
Lily and Max shared the apple, and as they munched, they giggled and played tag until the sky turned pink. Then, they curled up together under a big leaf for a cozy nap. "Best day ever!" Max whispered.
Lily’s tail twitched happily as the sun set, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures.
