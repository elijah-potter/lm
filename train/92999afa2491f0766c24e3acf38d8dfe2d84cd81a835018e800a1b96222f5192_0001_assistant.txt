Oh, Harlaus point. How far you have fallen? It was absolutely strewn with garbage the last time I went. People are clearly still going here (for some reason) but they are not cleaning up after themselves! Paper plates, utensils, discarded drink cans, it's disgusting! And no one cleans it up. What are we paying for? $30 a day parking! At least I think it was $30. Now that is how much money the machine took before it stopped beeping at me, but how could I know for sure? The signage was all rusty and poorly maintained. Really, that's how to describe this place in general. It would be a perfectly good spot to have a bonfire or a beach picnic, or even just to lay out in the sun. But the neglect and pollution are just too much, especially when other beaches don't charge so much for parking.
