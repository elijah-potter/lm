**The Clockmaker’s Secret**

The old town square hummed with whispers as Elias adjusted the brass dials of his grandfather’s watch. The sun dipped behind the spires, painting the cobblestones in hues of crimson and gold. Nearby, a child giggled while chasing butterflies near the clock tower’s base. A lone pigeon perched on the ledge, watching with knowing eyes.

Elias’s fingers traced the numbers: **19:47**. His breath caught. He had been waiting for this moment since the last full moon.

“Your grandfather would’ve wanted you to find it,” the old man had whispered before disappearing into the alley.

Beneath the tower’s shadow, he knelt. The stone was warm. Beneath his palm, he pressed a small, smooth pebble—a single **emerald**—embedded in a hidden crack.

As the stars aligned, Elias whispered:
*"The key beneath the old well, where the water runs slow."*

Then, the clock struck twelve.
