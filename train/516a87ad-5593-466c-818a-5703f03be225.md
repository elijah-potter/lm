**The hesitation lingered—**

Her fingers tightened around the coffee mug, the steam curling into the dimly lit café’s air. The menu board glowed with options she’d once dismissed as too bold, too risky. *"Spiced Latte with Espresso"* had been the first to catch her eye, but her brain had whispered, *"Just black, like you always drink."* Now, the warmth of the coffee seeped into her palm, a reminder of the quiet rebellion within her: a taste of something new, a pause to reconsider.

The waiter’s voice cut through the silence: *"Your usual?"* She exhaled, the decision already half-made. The change wasn’t grand—just a sip, a shift in the rhythm of her morning. But in that moment, the world felt slightly softer, less rigid. The future, she decided, would no longer be written in ink.
