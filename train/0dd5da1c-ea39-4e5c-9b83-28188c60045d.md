When the weight of unanswered questions lifts,
the quiet hum of uncertainty—
a current that once drowned you now carries you,
softly, through the storm’s aftermath.
The doubt isn’t gone; it’s just no longer a wall,
but a space between the steps,
where the breath is easier,
and the next question
is answered not with certainty,
but with the first tentative move
toward the edge of the unknown.
