Ritual performed in abandoned church:
1. Light three candles—one black, one white, one unlit.
2. Whisper a name to the black candle.
3. Step into a ring of salt drawn in the floor.
4. Shout "I am" three times into the unlit candle’s flame.
5. Offer a single drop of water from a cracked glass vial.
6. Touch the altar’s broken cross with the third finger of your right hand.
7. Say: *"The past is a lie, the future is a ghost."*
