Q: What is a Partner Visa UK?
A: Also known as the UK Spouse Visa, the Partner Visa UK is a settlement visa that falls under the Family visa category. 

Q: How long is the visa valid and can it be extended?
A: The Partner Visa UK gives you permission to reside in the UK with your British partner for up to 30 months. It can be extended an additional 30 months if you meet the eligibility requirements. 

Q: Does this visa lead to British citizenship?
A: Yes. This visa is the starting visa on your journey to Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) and, after that, UK Citizenship. 

Q: What are the main criteria to qualify for the UK Spouse Visa?
A: To qualify for the UK Spouse Visa, you must be married or in a civil partnership with a UK refugee, a UK settled person, or a British Citizen. In your application, you will provide proof that you meet specific requirements regarding finances and proof that your relationship is genuine. 

Q: Is this the same as the Marriage Visitor Visa?
A: No. The Marriage Visitor Visa allows you to visit the UK in order to get married regardless of your or your partner's citizenship.
