Dear Dragon and Knight,

I'm writing this letter hoping all of you are doing well. It has been six days since you guys last fought each other, charging and trying to kill the other. As the princess of this land, I decided to watch from afar, and after much deliberation, have decided you two should come together and make a truce. All of the time that both of you take to try and kill each other is such a waste of time. 

I have a proposal. I want both of you to come to my place. I'll have an organized banquet, and I'd love for you to come together and find a middle point for peaceful coexistence. 
I hope to hear back from you once you read this letter.

Regards, 
Princess,
