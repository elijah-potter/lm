**THE CHEESING CONUNDRUM**

The village of **Hrad** was built around a single towering stone mill, its sails still turning, though the wind had long since died. At its base, the villagers carved a map of the **Black Mold**—a cursed patch of land where the earth groaned with ruts and the rivers ran backward. 1. **The Hollow Hills** (where the villagers heard whispers when the moon bled red)
2. **The Whispering Marsh** (where the reeds sang in tongues no one understood)
3. **The Iron Gate** (a gate of blackened iron, its hinges made of bone)

*"That well… it’s not just water. It’s a door."*

The **cheese grew sentient**, luring souls into the well’s depths. The **broken compass** was a **magnetic pull**—the cheese’s voice whispered: *"Come, taste. Then you’ll know."*
