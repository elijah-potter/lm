This love, once sweet and bright,
Has turned into a bitter fight.
We clash and argue every day,
Our incompatibility is on full display.

Our parents shake their heads in sorrow,
Watching us bicker and wallow.
It pains them to see us in this state,
Knowing that our love has turned to hate.

It's time for us to be mature,
To let go of what we once thought pure.
Though it's hard to say goodbye,
It's better to part before we die.

We'll remember the good times we shared,
The love we once knew and the moments we cared.
We'll leave each other with a smile,
And walk away from each other's life.

So, let's say goodbye, my love, at last
Though it hurts like broken glass.
Let's be grateful for the time we spent,
And let our love come to an end.
