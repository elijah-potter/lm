Once there was a 10-year-old boy named Jack who lived with his parents on a farm in the countryside. Jack was always very lonely because he was an only child and there were no other children who lived in the area. Jack desperately wanted someone who he could call his best friend. One day, Jack was wandering outside in the countryside when he came across a stray brown and white horse. 

Jack held out his hand and called to him. The horse was skittish at first and kept his distance from Jack, but soon Jack was able to gain his trust. Jack brought the horse to the farm and decided to name him Lucky. He gave Lucky his own space in the barn and plenty of hay to eat. At first Jack's parents weren't sure about having another animal on the farm, but agreed it was okay so long as Jack would take care of him. 

Every morning, Jack would come to the barn to see Lucky before he went to school, and he would come to see Lucky again in the afternoon when he returned from school. Jack's father taught him how to ride and he was able to saddle Lucky and ride him around the farm and property. Lucky became Jack's best friend and they spent a lot of time together each day. They lived on the farm happily ever after.
