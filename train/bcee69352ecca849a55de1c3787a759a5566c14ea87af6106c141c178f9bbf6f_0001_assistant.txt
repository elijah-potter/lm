It would be best if you chose based on your desired playstyle.  Here is a quick breakdown for each class and what they can do:

- Hero: If you want to play a straightforward melee character with high Strength and Vigor.
- Bandit: If you want to play a stealth-based character with high Dexterity and Arcane.
- Vagabond: If you want to play a tanky character with high Vigor and Endurance.
- Astrologer: If you want to play a magic-based character with high Intelligence and Mind.
- Warrior: If you want to play a dual-wielding character with high Dexterity and Strength.
- Prisoner: If you want to play an agile melee/casting hybrid character with high Dexterity, Intelligence, and Mind.
- Confessor: If you want to play a hybrid character that can cast incantations and use melee weapons with high Faith and Strength.
- Prophet: If you want to play a Faith-based character that can cast incantations to deal damage and heal with high Faith and Vigor.
- Samurai: If you want to play a Dexterity-focused character with a focus on katanas and bows with high Dexterity and Endurance.
- Wretch: If you want to play a blank slate character with even stats across the board and the freedom to build your character however you like.
