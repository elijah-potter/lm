In the center of an unassuming town, artists from across the county gather to share their wares. There are carvers and painters, metal workers and quilters. But among the crowd, there is someone else. The shady figure who knows the secret of the town and will reveal it to you if you dare to discover who they are. Will you untie the mystery of the festival? Step into Central Park June 18-20 to take part in the game.
