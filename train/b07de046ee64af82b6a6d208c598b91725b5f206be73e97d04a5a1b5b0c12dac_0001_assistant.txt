* Columbine: Blooms in late spring and attracts birds and bees.  
* Fire pink: Blooms in spring and attracts birds. 
* Tall bellflower: Birds and bees favor them, and this plant blooms in late summer. 
* Goat's beard: Attracts bees and butterflies and has a bloom time of late spring.
* Chokecherry: Bees, birds, and butterflies pollinate this plant. It blooms in spring.
* Yellow trout lily: Attracts bees and blooms in spring.  
* Heart-leaved aster: Blooms in spring and early summer. Bees and butterflies pollinate this plant.
* Wild geranium: Bees and butterflies visit this plant. It blooms in spring and early summer.
