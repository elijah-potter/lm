The dim glow of a flickering streetlamp cast long shadows across the cracked pavement where two strangers stood—one hunched over a rusted briefcase, the other tracing a circle in the dirt with a cracked glass. The briefcase contained stolen banknotes, a ledger of forged identities, and a single, bloodstained photograph of a woman whose face was hidden behind a veil. The stranger with the glass was whispering to the wind, *"You can’t trust what you see, only what you feel."* The other, his fingers trembling, replied, *"But feeling’s just another lie."*

A distant siren wailed. The briefcase’s contents spilled onto the street as the woman in the photograph stepped out of the shadows—her voice a blade in the dark, *"You’re both already dead."* The circle vanished. The streetlamp snuffed. The world held its breath.
