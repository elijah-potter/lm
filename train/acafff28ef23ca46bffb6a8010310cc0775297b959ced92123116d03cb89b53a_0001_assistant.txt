Life in the remnants of what used to be the United States of America is hard, but thanks to the Brotherhood of Steel, my life in this New England wasteland has a purpose. I was raised by them; both my mother and father were members. My mother was a Scribe, and my father attained the rank of Knight. I pursued the path of Knighthood in his memory. I have few distinct memories of him. He died when I was only nine. He was caught in an ambush by a pair of Institute synths. Many of the settlers in the wasteland of the Commonwealth and small communities hate us. They don't understand why we take the technology they stumble upon or why we requisition a share of their crops. I try to avoid fighting with them, but some need to learn a lesson. We are the only thing that stands between peace and anarchy. We will purge the Commonwealth of synths, ghouls, and raiders. We will make this nation great again. I pledge my life to this cause.
