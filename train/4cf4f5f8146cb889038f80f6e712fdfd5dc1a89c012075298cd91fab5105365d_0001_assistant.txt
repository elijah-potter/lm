The main three highly-rated art marker brands that can be used for detailed work are Copic Sketch Markers, Blick Studio Brush Markers, and Ohuhu Markers. Copic markers are alcohol-based, durable, vivid, and refillable. This brand of markers is preferred by professionals and carries the highest cost. Blick Studio Brush Markers are less expensive than Copic markers, refillable, and have 144 color options along with a colorless blender. Ohuhu markers are at an even lower price point than the Blick Studio markers. These markers aren't refillable, and only come in sets but are considered one of the best markers for illustrators.
