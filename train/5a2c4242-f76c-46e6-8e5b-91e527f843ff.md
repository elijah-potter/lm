A keyboard glows faintly under a dim desk lamp. Finger-tips hover over keys—each press a deliberate pulse. A cursor blinks in the middle of a blank screen. The search bar, half-filled with a single word, hums with anticipation, its magnifying glass effect distorting the surrounding text. A pause. The cursor flashes again. A single keystroke, the word’s shadow stretches across the screen. A confirmation sound—**click**—resolves the query. The browser tab expands, revealing a single result page, a web of links, search results floating in a sea of ads. The screen fades to a new tab, a snippet of text, a single word highlighted in the first sentence. The cursor waits, ready to click.
