In a dimly lit office, a senior analyst leaned back in their chair, watching the junior developer type frantically. Their eyes lingered on the screen as they spoke, "The Q2 revenue report is due in 10 minutes."

The junior developer shifted nervously, fingers hovering over the keyboard. "I’ve got it all entered, but—"

"Great," the senior analyst interrupted, shifting focus to a stack of paperwork. "Just make sure the projections align with the last quarter’s trends."

The junior developer’s shoulders relaxed slightly, but their gaze returned to the screen. The screen displayed the finalized report, data points and graphs neatly organized.
