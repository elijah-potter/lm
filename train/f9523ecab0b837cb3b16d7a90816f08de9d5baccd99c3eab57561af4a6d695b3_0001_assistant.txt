Deep in the dark part of the forest was a small clearing where the last rays of sunlight shone through the tree branches to illuminate a most amazing house! It had the look of a cozy cottage and its walls were made of golden gingerbread bricks with white frosted mortar. The awning of the porch appeared to be more gingerbread coated with fluffy white icing and was held up by giant round peppermint sticks. The roof of the cottage was covered with round candy wafers in pink, yellow, sky blue, and pale green, stuck to the building with the same icing clinging to the awning. A chimney made of chocolate chip cookies gently wafted cinnamon scented smoke through the forest. Chocolate reminiscent of a melting wax candle dripped from the top of the chimney. Small windows with sugar-glass panes shone warm yellow, their windowboxes overflowing with green frosting vines and tiny candy sprinkles to give the impression of young flowers budding within. On the porch was a rocking chair made of pretzel sticks and a half-flattened marshmallow seat cushion. Leading from the porch was a pathway of sparkling gumdrops out into the small yard where a woodpile sat next to a split-rail fence made from assorted candy sticks. Next to the woodpile was a chicken coop, and inside the nests were colorful candy-coated chocolate eggs. The garden contained vines growing jellybeans of obviously many flavors, and tall stalks of candy corn stood next to them.
