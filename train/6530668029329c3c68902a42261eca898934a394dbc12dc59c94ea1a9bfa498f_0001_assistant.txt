Once there was a young boy named Johnny who loved going to the beach. Summer was his favorite time of year because that meant he could go to the beach every week with his parents. Johnny's favorite thing to do at the beach was making sand castles. He thought it was the best thing ever! Every week he would make a huge sand castle and would dig a large moat around it. 

One day when Johnny was digging his moat, he hit something hard with his shovel. He peered down into the moat and saw something orange and shiny. Curious as to what was in the sand, Johnny kept digging. To his surprise, he unearthed a large pearl that was the color of amber. Johnny had never seen anything so beautiful before and he ran to tell his parents. Johnny's parents also couldn't believe what they saw, and they congratulated Johnny on his priceless treasure.
