- ATHENS is a city in Georgia that is tied to the rock scene of the 1980s and 1990s. The city was home to famous musical groups such as R.E.M. and the B-52s.
- ATLANTA is the capital city of Georgia and has held that title since 1868. More than half of the state's population lived there as of 2020.
- LOUISVILLE is a city located in Floyd County, Georgia. The city once saw a temperature high of 112 °F.
- SAVANNAH is one of Georgia's second-tier cities. It lacks a consolidated city-county government like the other second-tier cities have. 
- COLUMBUS is another second-tier city in Georgia. Columbus formed a consolidated city-county government by referendum in 1970 and eventually absorbed all smaller incorporated entities into its boundaries.
- AUGUSTA is a second-tier city in Georgia. This city has formed a consolidated city-county government but has excluded a couple of smaller, incorporated towns within its boundaries.
- MACON formed a consolidated city-county government in 2012. Later on, it absorbed all of the smaller incorporated entities within its consolidated boundaries. 
- CUSETTA is a small town in Georgia that merged with unincorporated Chattahoochee County in 2003. Afterward, it adopted a consolidated city-county government.
- GEORGETOWN is another Georgia town that consolidated. In 2006, it consolidated with the remainder of Quitman County.
- ROSWELL is among the largest cities and towns in Georgia. It's located in Fulton County and has a population of 82,453, based on 2020 U.S. census data.
