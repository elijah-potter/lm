In a dimly lit warehouse, flickering fluorescent lights cast elongated shadows across the rusted metal beams overhead. The air smelled of damp concrete and old oil, thick with the weight of forgotten industry. A single cracked window, covered by a torn piece of plastic, let in a thin stream of daylight that flickered like a dying flame. Dust motes swirled lazily in the weak light, painting intricate patterns against the peeling paint on the walls.

A lone figure crouched by a half-finished wooden table, their fingers tracing the grain of the wood as they adjusted a crude compass. Behind them, a series of empty crates lined the far wall, each bearing faded labels in faded ink: *"Provisions," "Armor," "Ammunition."* One crate had been kicked over, its contents scattered—rusted tools, a half-melted soldering iron, and a few half-assembled components. A small, abandoned radio hummed faintly, its antenna bent, but still crackling with static.

The figure’s breath came in shallow, uneven gasps, their back stiffened against the cold. They turned slightly, their peripheral vision catching the movement—something shifting in the shadows beneath the table. A gloved hand emerged, fingers twitching as if searching for something just beyond the reach of the dim light. A rusted key glinted in the faint illumination, its teeth worn smooth. It was the only thing moving. The rest of the warehouse was still. The only sound was the distant, rhythmic *clack-clack* of the warehouse doors, sealed shut against the night.
