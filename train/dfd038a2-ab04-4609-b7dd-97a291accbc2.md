The **Stonehenge** megalithic stone circle in Wiltshire, England, showcases prehistoric engineering. Its precisely aligned stones, dating back ~5,000 years, reflect astronomical alignments (summer solstice sunrise) and burial rituals. Carved stones at **Durrington Walls** (Neolithic settlement) and **Avebury** (largest stone circle) reveal communal burial practices. The **Göbekli Tepe** (Turkey, ~9,600 years old) features intricately carved reliefs of animals and humans, challenging conventional timelines of civilization. **Pompeii’s frescoes** and **Herculaneum’s scrolls** freeze daily life in a 1st-century AD eruption. **Skara Brae (Scotland)** preserves Neolithic houses with intact hearths and storage pits, offering a glimpse into 5,000-year-old architecture. **Petroglyphs** (e.g., **Gravettian carvings** in France, ~30,000 years old) depict hunting scenes, depicting Ice Age artistry.
