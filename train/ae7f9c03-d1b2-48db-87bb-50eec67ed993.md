Ancient ruins of a forgotten civilization: weathered stone arches and crumbling walls bear inscriptions in a script long since lost, embedded with layers of petrified moss and lichen. Sandstone columns, once part of a grand temple, now lean precariously, their surfaces etched with geometric patterns that shift under different light angles. A carved lion’s head at the entrance guards a hidden chamber, its hollow eye sockets filled with centuries of dust. Nearby, a well still holds water from an unknown time, reflecting the past in its surface. The air hums with the faint echoes of chants, though no living voice remains to speak them.
