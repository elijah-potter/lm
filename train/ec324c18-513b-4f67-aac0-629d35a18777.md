**Perspective Shift: Illuminating the Unseen**
---
Reframing through a lens of **empathy** reveals the human in systems, systems in stories. The unseen becomes visible—behind every algorithm’s bias lurks a lived experience, and every data point a voice.

*Example:* A traffic light’s red/yellow isn’t just color; it’s a signal for the cyclist who just dodged a car crash. The "optimized" route isn’t just pixels—it’s the time a family spent searching for a parking spot in the rain.

**Key Shift:**
- **Systems > Stories:** Reduce complexity by asking: *What’s the human at the core?*
- **Data > Stories:** A "correlation" isn’t a cause—it’s a whisper of a relationship waiting to be named.
- **Constraints > Creativity:** Constraints aren’t limits; they’re the scaffolding for new narratives.

---
**Actionable Insight:**
Next time you analyze data or design a system, pause. Ask:
*"What’s the perspective I’m missing?"*
Then build for it.
