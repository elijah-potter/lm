What cartoon character are you?
Age: till 10
Concerns: to be forms, cartoon characters, simple adjectives.
Prepare a list of famous cartoon characters: Bugs Bunny, Homer Simpson, Mickey Mouse, Bart Simpson, Popeye, Sponge Bob Square Pants, Daffy Duck, Scooby-Doo, etc. and their pictures: This is Homer, etc.
Ask students to match the pictures to the names of cartoon characters, then let them pretend to be one of the cartooncharacters for others to guess
Are you silly/funny/big/scary/Homer Simpson? (decide what other adjectives to use).
Alternatively:use very simple adjectives for students to say I am yellow. Are you Daffy Duck?
