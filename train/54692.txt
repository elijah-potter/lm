In the beginning was the Great Goddess,and the Goddess was the Earth, and the Earth was the Goddess.
The origins of the cult of the Great Goddess lie hidden in the dim twilight of prehistoric time.
The Goddess ruled for hundreds of thousands of years. In the course of time, the Mother-Goddess
was overthrown and driven under, and the triumph of the most patriarchal of archetypes of
Yahweh, God the Father and Allah came to power in the Judaic, Christian and Moslem worlds.
It was only in the tamed form of Mary, Mother of God, were
some aspects of the Mother Goddess permitted to survive.
In the present Age of Pisces, a feminine sign,
we have seen the suppression and re-emergence
of the feminine,and no story tells
this quite as dramatically as the
transformation of Mary Magdalene.
When the Nag Hammadi scrolls
were discovered and
translated, it was revealed
that Mary was more
than a follower but
an actual apostle.
