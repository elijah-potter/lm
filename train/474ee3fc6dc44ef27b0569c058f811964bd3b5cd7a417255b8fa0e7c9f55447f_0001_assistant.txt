During spring and summer, plants use sunlight to produce chlorophyll as a food source. Chlorophyll's pigments make the leaves turn green. In autumn, these plants stop producing chlorophyll, which turns the leaves brown, yellow, orange, or red. Since plants don't have enough sunlight to produce chlorophyll in the winter, they drop their leaves after absorbing the molecules that make chlorophyll to conserve energy. This is why leaves change colors before breaking off.
