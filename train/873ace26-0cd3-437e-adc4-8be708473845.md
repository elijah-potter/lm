**Auditory:** The steady hiss of wind through hollow reeds, mingling with the distant, rhythmic thump of a river’s unseen currents. The low, resonant groan of ancient logs in a half-sunken temple.

**Olfactory:** The sharp, metallic tang of damp earth, layered with the faint, earthy sweetness of wildflowers crushed by unseen feet. A whisper of burnt incense, thick with old smoke, lingers between the cracks of weathered walls.

**Gustatory:** The cool, bitter taste of rainwater, lingering in the back of the throat. The sharp, metallic tang of wet stone and decaying fish, left to ferment in a forgotten trough.

**Kinesthetic:** The pull of a rope woven from frayed vines, leading deeper into the mist. The creak of joints in damp wood, heavy with centuries of unseen weight. The weight of your own breath, thick and slow, as you descend.
