Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big garden, there lived a tiny rabbit named Benny. Benny had soft, fluffy ears and a heart full of curiosity.

One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his favorite friend, a little squirrel named Sammy, climbing a tree. Benny wanted to go too, but he wasn’t sure if he could climb like Sammy. He hopped around, thinking.

Just then, Benny spotted a shiny red berry on the ground. He picked it up and nibbled on it. "Mmm, this is yummy!" he said. Benny realized he could hop and reach things just like Sammy.

He practiced hopping higher and higher, and soon, he could climb trees just like his friend! Benny and Sammy played together, chasing butterflies, collecting acorns, and making the garden their fun playground. They shared their snacks and laughed together every day.

Benny learned that with a little courage and lots of practice, he could do anything he dreamed of. And from that day on, Benny and Sammy were the best friends, exploring their garden and the world beyond.
