**Rules of Conduct for Community Engagement**

1. **Respect All Participants**: Treat everyone with kindness, inclusivity, and professionalism.
2. **No Offensive Content**: Avoid slurs, hate speech, harassment, or any content that dehumanizes others.
3. **Adhere to Platform Guidelines**: Follow all platform-specific rules (e.g., copyright, plagiarism, spam).
4. **Encourage Constructive Discussion**: Focus on learning, debate, and collaboration—not personal attacks or divisive arguments.
5. **Fact-Check Claims**: Verify information before sharing unless it’s your original analysis.
6. **No Self-Promotion**: Avoid excessive or spammy self-promotion; keep discussions relevant.
7. **Respect Privacy**: Do not share personal details of others without consent.
8. **Avoid Toxic Behavior**: No trolling, baiting, or deliberately provoking others.
9. **Use Clear Language**: Avoid ambiguity or unclear phrasing that could lead to misunderstandings.
10. **Disclose Affiliations (If Relevant)**: If you represent an organization, disclose it unless prohibited.
11. **No Copyright Infringement**: Do not repost, modify, or distribute content without permission.
12. **Stay on Topic**: Keep discussions relevant to the original subject or related themes.
13. **Acknowledge Sources**: Cite original sources when referencing ideas, data, or quotes.
14. **No Misleading Claims**: Avoid exaggeration, falsehoods, or misleading statements.
15. **Report Violations**: Flag violations (e.g., spam, harassment) to moderators promptly.
16. **Be Patient**: Avoid impatience or abruptness in responses; consider others’ perspectives.
17. **No Illegal Activities**: Do not engage in or promote illegal actions, fraud, or scams.
18. **Respect Time Limits**: If discussions are time-bound, comply with set durations.
19. **Avoid Overly Technical Jargon**: Keep explanations accessible unless the audience is trained.
20. **Collaborate, Don’t Compete**: Foster teamwork rather than individual dominance in discussions.
