**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

SPRINGFIELD, IL – August 23, 2024 – The Sangamon County Fair returns this year, promising a celebration of agriculture, community, and family entertainment. This year’s fair will showcase livestock competitions, agricultural displays, artisan crafts, and a variety of amusement rides and games, offering something for visitors of all ages. Demonstrations of traditional skills, such as blacksmithing and quilting, will also be featured, providing a glimpse into the county’s rich heritage.

The Sangamon County Fair will run from Friday, September 13th, through Sunday, September 15th, 2024. Gates will open at 9:00 AM each day, with evening events concluding at 10:00 PM on Friday and Saturday, and 6:00 PM on Sunday. The fairgrounds are located at 2100 Fairgrounds Road, Springfield, Illinois. Free parking is available on the north end of the fairgrounds.

Admission is $12 for adults, $8 for children ages 6-12, and free for children five and under. Advance tickets are available online at SangamonCountyFair.org and at select local retailers. A season pass, granting access to all three days, is priced at $25.

**Headline Events:**

*   Friday Evening: Demolition Derby
*   Saturday Afternoon: 4-H Livestock Auction
*   Saturday Evening: Concert featuring The Heartland Drifters
*   Sunday Afternoon: Tractor Pull Competition
*   Daily: Agricultural Exhibits and Demonstrations
*   Daily: Midway Rides and Games
