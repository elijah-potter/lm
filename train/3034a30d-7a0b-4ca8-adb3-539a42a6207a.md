A lone figure in an empty office cubicle, hunched over a laptop, stares at a notification: **"Your manager’s photo is missing from the team Slack."** The cursor blinks as they open the file. The image is of their boss—mid-laugh, arms wide, holding a stress ball shaped like a donkey. A caption: **"We had a meeting where we decided to rename the ‘Team Breakfast’ room ‘The Donkey Tavern’—still planning."**

The screen freezes for a split second—then the notification light flickers: **"New message: 😂 (12 unread)."** The cubicle door creaks open. A coworker, mid-sip of coffee, drops a half-eaten bagel. **"Oh, hey! I was just telling [Name] about the ‘Donkey Tavern’—she’s *loving* it."** They pause, eyes widening. **"Wait… she’s *laughing* in the photo?"** The office erupts into chaos: laughter, a shared Slack message, and a sudden urge to join the chaos. The routine is unraveling.
