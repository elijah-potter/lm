
Do these work for you?

1.	Mr. Moon Meets the Forest Monsters 
Mr. Moon is full and bright, the biggest thing in the skies. However, he’s also scared of everything… including the deep, dark forest where he can see glowing eyes and hear strange sounds. But, when he goes to explore the forest with his moonlight, he discovers the monsters are animals, all friendly and happy to finally see him. 

2.	Can Snails Be Astronauts? 
Mara is a teensy-tiny young snail with dreams that are bigger than life (and space). She wants to be an astronaut, much to the dismay of her family. When they tell her that snails don’t go to space, she begins to daydream about walking on the moon, riding an asteroid, and counting the stars while seated on a drifting satellite. 

3.	Bessie and Her Big Moon Jump 
Remember the cow from the fairytale? The one that jumped over the moon? Well, her name was Bessie, and she saw some amazing things on her flight through the atmosphere. In this tale, she details all of the things she saw, including a friendly, waving alien, as she flew past in lunar orbit.
