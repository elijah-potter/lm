A shadowy figure in a trench coat moves along the dimly lit alley, their breath fogging the cold night air. The neon glow of a distant sign flickers like a dying ember, casting long, unnatural shadows on the wet pavement. The person pauses, leaning against a rusted fire escape, watching a crowd of strangers passing by. Their posture is relaxed, arms folded, but their gaze lingers on the faces of people who are oblivious to their presence.

A woman in a patterned dress and a man in a crisp suit both walk by without acknowledging them. The figure stands motionless, fingers tracing the edge of their coat, as if waiting for someone they cannot see. The alley’s echo of distant laughter and the distant rumble of a passing train blend into a humming silence. The figure turns, stepping away into the crowd, blending with the anonymous mass of figures moving toward the streetlight. No one stops to look closer.
