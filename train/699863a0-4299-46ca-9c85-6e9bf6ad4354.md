A shift in viewpoint can reveal unseen truths. What seemed distant may now appear close. The glass ceiling cracks under an unexpected angle of light, and what was once rigid becomes flexible. A single perspective is a prison of limited vision; multiple perspectives are the key to unlocking understanding.

Perception is not static—it bends with experience. The same scene, observed from two ends of the spectrum, reveals entirely different stories. The question isn’t whether to change, but how far and how fast. Perspective is the compass that guides exploration. The world isn’t static; it’s a fluid tapestry of shifting lenses.
