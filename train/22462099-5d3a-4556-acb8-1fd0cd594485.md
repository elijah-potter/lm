A leap of faith in the dark—where every step echoes uncertainty.
The weight of hesitation lingers like a shadow, but the fire burns brighter when you choose to leap.
Silence answers itself: *What if I’m wrong?* The answer is already written in the air—waiting to be claimed.

No map, only the faintest trail of trust.
The ground beneath feels like a question, not a platform.
But you step anyway.
