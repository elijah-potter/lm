## The Obsidian Bloom: A Cooperative Research Game

**A Game of Urgent Discovery for 1-4 Players**

**Designer's Note:** *The Obsidian Bloom is designed to explore the complexities of scientific research and crisis response. It emphasizes the difficult choices researchers face when confronted with limited resources and incomplete information. The game aims to foster collaborative problem-solving, strategic prioritization, and an appreciation for the inherent uncertainty within complex systems. It is intended for players aged 14 and up.*

### 1. Components

*   **The Globe Board:** A circular game board depicting a world map, divided into regions with varying levels of vulnerability.
*   **Researcher Pawns (4):** Unique miniatures representing specialized research teams (Botanist, Virologist, Geologist, Epidemiologist).
*   **Crisis Tokens (60):** Representing the spread of the Obsidian Bloom, placed on regions of the Globe Board. Tokens are categorized into three severity levels (Minor, Moderate, Severe).
*   **Research Cards (80):** Cards detailing potential research avenues, each requiring specific resources and offering varying degrees of success.
*   **Resource Tokens (120):** Representing funding, equipment, and personnel. Divided into three types: Financial (40), Technological (40), and Human (40).
*   **Anomaly Markers (12):** Representing unpredictable events that introduce complications and challenges.
*   **Data Cubes (30):** Used to track research progress and represent gathered information.
*   **Event Cards (30):** Drawn each round, introducing new challenges and opportunities.
*   **Difficulty Tracker:** A track to monitor the escalating crisis.
*   **First Player Token:** Designates the starting player each round.
*   **Rulebook (this document).**

### 2. Setup

1.  **Place the Globe Board** in the center of the playing area.
2.  **Place the Difficulty Tracker** adjacent to the Globe Board, starting at the “Stable” position.
3.  **Shuffle the Research Cards** and place them face down as a draw pile.
4.  **Shuffle the Event Cards** and place them face down as a draw pile.
5.  **Distribute Resource Tokens** as follows: 10 Financial, 10 Technological, 10 Human.
6.  **Place 8 Crisis Tokens** (4 Minor, 2 Moderate, 2 Severe) randomly on different regions of the Globe Board.
7.  **Each player chooses a Researcher Pawn** and places it in the “Research Hub” region.
8.  **Determine the First Player** randomly and give them the First Player Token.

### 3. Turn Structure

Each round consists of the following phases, completed in order:

1.  **Event Phase:** Draw the top Event Card and resolve its effects immediately. These effects may introduce new Crisis Tokens, shift existing tokens, or impose penalties.
2.  **Action Phase:** Beginning with the First Player and proceeding clockwise, each player performs **two** actions. Available actions are:
    *   **Move:** Move your Researcher Pawn to an adjacent region.
    *   **Research:** Spend Resource Tokens to draw and attempt a Research Card. The card will specify the required resources and a success threshold. Roll a six-sided die; if the result equals or exceeds the threshold, the research is successful. Place a Data Cube on the Research Card to mark progress.
    *   **Gather Resources:** Collect 2 Resource Tokens of your choice from the general supply.
    *   **Contain Crisis:** Spend 2 Resource Tokens (any combination) to remove one Minor Crisis Token from the region you occupy.
    *   **Share Resources:** Transfer one or more Resource Tokens to another player in the same region.
3.  **Crisis Escalation Phase:**
    *   **Spread:** For each region containing a Moderate or Severe Crisis Token, roll a six-sided die. On a result of 4+, place a Minor Crisis Token in an adjacent region.
    *   **Advance Difficulty:** Move the Difficulty Tracker one space forward. If the tracker reaches the "Critical" position, the game is immediately lost.
4.  **First Player Rotation:** Pass the First Player Token to the player on your left.

### 4. Win and Loss Conditions

**Winning the Game:** You win the game if you successfully complete **five** Research Cards. Completed cards are those with three Data Cubes placed upon them.

**Losing the Game:** You lose the game if:

*   The Difficulty Tracker reaches the "Critical" position.
*   There are no more Minor Crisis Tokens available to place on the Globe Board.
*   You are unable to draw an Event Card when required.

### 5. Mechanics of Team Trade-offs Under Uncertainty

1.  **Resource Scarcity:** The limited supply of Resource Tokens forces players to prioritize which research avenues to pursue and which crises to contain. Players must constantly evaluate the potential benefits of different actions against the immediate need for resources.
2.  **Research Card Uncertainty:** Each Research Card presents a success threshold. While players can spend more resources to draw additional cards, this increases the overall resource expenditure without guaranteeing success. The risk of failure introduces a constant tension between investment and conservation.
3.  **Crisis Spread Roll:** The Crisis Spread roll introduces an element of unpredictability. Even well-contained crises can unexpectedly spread, forcing players to adapt their strategies and potentially divert resources from research. This highlights the inherent limitations of control in complex systems.

### 6. Difficulty Scaling

*   **Two Players:** Start with 6 Crisis Tokens (3 Minor, 2 Moderate, 1 Severe). Reduce the number of Resource Tokens available by 5 of each type.
*   **Three Players:** Play with the standard setup.
*   **Four Players:** Start with 10 Crisis Tokens (5 Minor, 2 Moderate, 3 Severe). Increase the number of Resource Tokens available by 5 of each type.



### 7. Advanced Variants

*   **Specialist Focus:** Each player receives a unique ability tied to their Researcher Pawn, providing a more specialized role within the team.
*   **Hidden Objectives:** Each player receives a secret objective that rewards specific actions or research outcomes, adding a layer of individual goals within the cooperative framework.
*   **Cascading Failures:** If a region contains three or more Crisis Tokens, a "Cascading Failure" occurs, triggering a negative effect that impacts all regions adjacent to the affected area.
