To you who stands before this text, silent and curious—

I write not for praise, nor to demand your attention, but because words, once spoken aloud, echo beyond the lips that form them. You hold this page, perhaps because something in it resonates with the quiet ache of the unspoken, the unheard, or simply because you are drawn by the mystery of what follows.

Let me say this: you are not alone in the act of reading. The ink on these lines is not just ink; it is a bridge between us, though you may never know my name or my face. This is not a letter to a friend, nor a sermon to a congregation, but a conversation stitched from fragments of time and thought, meant to linger like a memory you might not have intended to keep.

If you read this, then you are already part of the story. The rest is yours to interpret, to reject, or to carry with you as you walk away. The end is not written yet, but the beginning was long before we met in these words.

Do not forget. The world is full of unheard voices, and this one is yours to hold.
