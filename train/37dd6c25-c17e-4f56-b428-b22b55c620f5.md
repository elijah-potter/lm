A slow hum filled the air as the clock tower’s hands crept toward midnight. Outside, the world seemed to exist in suspended motion—each breath, each leaf whispering on the wind, stretched into eternity. A child’s laughter, once bright and sharp, now drifted like a distant echo. A car’s engine roared, its tires grinding against the pavement, but the pavement itself seemed to stretch beneath them, warping under the weight of every step. A woman’s shadow, once crisp, blurred into a smear of motionless, unblinking presence. The sun hung low, its light filtering through the trees like liquid gold, seeping into every corner of the world, making time feel like a slow, endless river.

In the distance, a clock struck twelve. The second hand didn’t move.
