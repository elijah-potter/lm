Language learning materials for Malayalam, a Dravidian language spoken mainly in the southern Indian state of Kerala by about 38 million people.
- a basic introduction to Malayalam. Includes dialogues, exercises, games, tests and notes in many different languages. Introduces a fairly limited amount of vocabulary and phrases, but does so in an effective and fun way.
by Krishna Gopal Vikal
[ Contents ]
- a bilingual English / Malayalam dictionary with over 30,000 references and translations. Contains all the everyday words and phrases used in today's Malayalam and English that you will need to know.
- an illustrated dictionary designed for Malayalam-speaking learners of English that covers all the essential words and phrases that students need to learn. The definitions are written in full sentences, using simple language, and the dictionary contains thousands of examples of real English.
by Valsala Menon
- provides an extensive two-way dictionary, comprehensive phrasebook, and basic grammar. To aid the English speaker, all Malayalam entries are presented in the Roman alphabet. The native script is also introduced, however, along with a detailed pronunciation guide. Guidance is offered to travellers for various everyday life situations, as well as useful common vocabulary items and an introduction to the arts and culture of Kerala.
