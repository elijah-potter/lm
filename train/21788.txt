What are the names of the points on the surface of the globe through which the imaginary axis of the earth passes?
July 27, 2021 | education|
The point on the earth’s surface through which the axis of the earth (imaginary) passes is called the geographic pole. There are two of them – the North and South Poles. The coordinates of the poles have no longitude, or the longitude is said to be 0. The north pole is latitude 90 degrees north and the south pole latitude is 90 degrees south latitude.
