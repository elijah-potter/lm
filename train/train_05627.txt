In the last student council election, the winner got 55% of the votes and the loser got the rest. If the school has 2000 students, but only 25% of them voted, how many more votes did the winner get than the loser?
500 students voted because 2000 x .25 = <<2000*.25=500>>500
The loser got 45% of the votes because 100 - 55 = <<100-55=45>>45
The winner got 275 votes because 500 x .55 = <<500*.55=275>>275
The loser got 225 votes because 500 x .45 = <<500*.45=225>>225
The winner got 50 more votes because 275 - 225 = <<275-225=50>>50
