In the crumbling attic of an abandoned library, an old leather-bound envelope was found tucked beneath a dust-covered bookshelf. Inside, a single sheet of yellowed paper bore the following handwritten message:

*"The river remembers what the stones do not. Seek the iron bridge at the crossroads where the old clock still ticks backward. There, the lantern in the east holds the key to the forgotten door."*

A map, partially eroded by time, depicted a winding path through mist-shrouded woods, leading to a village where the clock tower’s hands moved in reverse. The last entry was dated in faded ink: *"They left no name—only a warning."*
