Waiting is often misunderstood—it’s not passive but an active choice. That morning, my hands trembled as I paused the alarm, choosing the quiet before the rush. The choice to wait was a rebellion against the rush, a deliberate act of self-respect in an age of constant motion. The coffee brewed slowly, the steam curling like a promise. Waiting wasn’t idle; it was the space between breath, where thoughts gathered, where decisions formed without pressure. The world outside hummed with chaos, but here, in the pause, stillness became strength.

A moment of defiance.
