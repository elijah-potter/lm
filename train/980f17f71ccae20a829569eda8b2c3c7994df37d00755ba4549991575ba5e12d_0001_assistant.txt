Dear Residents,

Happy Spring! We hope you have been able to get out and enjoy the beautiful weather. As more families will be spending time outside, we wanted to remind everyone of the following community rules to help keep our neighborhood beautiful:

1. Landscaping:  As a reminder, all flower beds should be regularly weeded, hedges trimmed and any dead plantings removed.
2. Lawn Care:  All lawns must be fertilized regularly and mowed at least once per week. Grass clippings should be removed from sidewalks and driveways.
3. Trash Collection:  When doing any spring cleaning, please remember to follow the designated trash collection schedule.
4. Visitor Parking:  Please ensure that guests park in designated parking spots. Parking on lawns is strictly prohibited.  

Let’s all do our part to keep our community beautiful.  As always, please reach out to any board member if you have any questions or concerns.  

Warm regards,
Rolling Meadows Board of Directors
