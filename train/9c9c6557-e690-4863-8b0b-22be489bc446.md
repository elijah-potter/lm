In the quiet coastal town of **Harbor Heights**, a rare and mysterious phenomenon has captivated residents overnight. Reports of bioluminescent waves washing ashore began as early as midnight, glowing faintly blue-green along the pebbled shoreline of **Moonstone Bay**. Eyewitnesses described the waves as pulsing in rhythmic patterns, sometimes forming faint, swirling shapes that dissipated into the dark sky within minutes.

Local fisherman **Marla Chen**, who was out on her boat near the lighthouse, recounted: *"The water was alive—like a living thing. I thought I was seeing ghosts at first, but the glow was real. It moved like it had a mind of its own."* The phenomenon lasted until dawn, after which the waves returned to normal. Authorities have opened an investigation into possible environmental disruptions, though no immediate cause has been identified. Marine biologists are being called in to analyze samples from the affected area.

The town’s annual **Harbor Festival**, scheduled for next week, has been temporarily postponed as officials urge caution. Some residents are cautiously optimistic, believing it a sign of good fortune, while others remain skeptical, fearing this could be a warning of something far more ominous.
