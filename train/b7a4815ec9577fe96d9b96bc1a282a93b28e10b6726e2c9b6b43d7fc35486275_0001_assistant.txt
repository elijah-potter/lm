Edwin likes to roll and roll around on his round belly. He can tuck his six legs inside to make himself even rounder. His gray fur is very soft and huggable. It's hard to tell where his head is unless he's looking at you with his large round eyes. They're orange and they glow in the dark, making two circles of light like a flashlight. Whenever he shines his eyes, he can scare away any monsters from coming close. If anyone he doesn't know comes near, he tucks in his legs, closes his eyes, and pretends to be a large ball of lint.
