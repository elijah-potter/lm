I can help you with that. Here are some ways to make cleaning more enjoyable:

- Turn on your favorite show: Watch your favorite TV show while dusting, folding laundry, or picking things up around the house. Turn it up so you can hear it in other rooms!
- Buy cleaning supplies that you enjoy: It might help to buy a new vacuum you've had your eye on or even a robot vacuum, but if you're looking to keep it inexpensive, find cleaning supplies with your favorite scents or rubber gloves in funky colors.
- Make a game out of it: Divide up the bathroom cleaning into tasks. Cleaning the toilet, bathtub, and sink should all be separate tasks. Set a goal for yourself to see how quickly you can finish each task. Set a timer and try to beat each goal!
- Listen to something: Nothing beats listening to music or even a podcast while you're cleaning. Immerse yourself in something interesting to make the time go by faster while you finish cleaning the house.
- Reward yourself: Once you're finished, have a reward waiting for you. It could be your favorite dessert, a long hot bath, or even watching another episode of your favorite show while relaxing.
