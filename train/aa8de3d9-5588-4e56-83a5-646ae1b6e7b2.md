Once upon a time, in a cozy green house with a big red door, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy white paws and a big curly tail. One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his favorite toy, a shiny blue ball, had rolled away.

Benny hopped outside and saw his friends: a friendly squirrel named Sam, a wise old owl named Oliver, and a giggly chameleon named Lulu. They were all playing near a big, sparkly river.

"Benny, where did your ball go?" asked Sam.
"I don't know!" Benny said, looking around. "Maybe it fell in the water!"

Oliver hooted, "We'll find it together!" and they all started a big hunt. Benny peeked under rocks, Sam climbed high trees, and Lulu changed colors to hide if needed.

"Benny, did you see a little fish?" asked Lulu.
"Yes, but I don't think it was my ball!" Benny said.

Suddenly, Benny saw a tiny, shiny ball bouncing on the riverbank! It was his blue ball! He hopped to get it, and the fish—who was actually a very friendly little fish named Finn—swam away happily.

"Thank you, Benny!" Finn said, swimming away. "I saw you and your friends looking for your ball. You saved me from a big, hungry fish!"

The friends cheered. "We did it!" said Benny, holding his ball up high. "Let’s play again tomorrow!" And with that, they all hopped, swam, and laughed together under the big, beautiful sun.
