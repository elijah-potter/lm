A dimly lit café at midnight. The air hums with quiet energy. Two strangers sit across from each other at a small table, their backs to the door. One adjusts their tie, the other traces a finger along the rim of their coffee cup.

A server approaches with an empty mug. The taller one leans in, their voice soft: *"I was wondering why you never laugh at my jokes."* The other smiles faintly, eyes reflecting the flickering overhead light. *"Because you don’t know what’s funny."* The server pauses, then adds, *"And I don’t know why you’re here."*

A beat. The taller one exhales, then says, *"I’m here because I was waiting for you to stop pretending you didn’t see me."* The other’s hands still, then slowly reach across the table—just once. The server watches, unreadable.

*"So,"* the taller one says, *"what’s your excuse now?"* The other laughs, deep and unguarded. The server nods, turns, and walks away. The table is silent again—now, full of something else.
