The company had been tracking performance metrics for six months before the final quarterly review. Data showed a 15% decline in customer satisfaction scores, despite marketing efforts. The team’s quarterly planning session concluded with a unanimous recommendation to pivot marketing strategy, but leadership ignored the report. By the time the quarter ended, revenue dropped 20%, and churn rose to 12%. Postmortem analysis later confirmed the decision was obvious in hindsight—ignoring the data led to irreversible damage.
