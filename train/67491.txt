Filling Cups With Colored Salt
|NAME||Filling Cups With Colored Salt|
|DESCRIPTION||They rubbed colored chalk on salt so the salt would change its color. They filled the colored salt in transparent cups and decorated them with small umbrellas.|
|MATERIALS||Salt, colored chalk, transparent cups, decorative umbrellas|
|GOALS||The salt changed its color slowly, so they had to train their patience and they learned, that even if they don’t see a result immediately, they should continue with their work.
They learned to make decisions independently by choosing matching colors.
