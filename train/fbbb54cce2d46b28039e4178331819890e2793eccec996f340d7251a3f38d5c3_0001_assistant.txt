The Great Gatsby was written by F Scott Fitzgerald and first published in 1925. Based in part on Fitzgerald's 1922 romance with Ginevra King, the book follows the story of Jay Gatsby as he attempts to rekindle a relationship with Daisy Buchanan. It details high society on Long Island in the 1920s and offers a unique snapshot of popular culture of the period. Although it was not wildly popular during Fitzgerald's lifetime, it is now hailed as a literary masterpiece.
