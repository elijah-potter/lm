This math packet contains 35 worksheets/printables to help review important math concepts with your third grader (soon-to-be fourth grader!) The topics include multiplication, adding by 3's, regrouping addition and subtraction, fact families, word problems, place value, greater than/less than, rounding, area, algebra, and decimals!
The total pages in this pack is 67, but that includes all of the activities, answer keys, and reference pages. I used a multiple of fonts from Kevin and Amanda and Tonya's Treats for Teachers. Graphics are from Creative Clips and KPM Doodles. Thank you so much for your interest in this product! This is a great way for your third grader to prepare for fourth grade this summer (some topics are included to introduce your soon to be fourth grader of the type of work they will receive in the beginning of the year!!)
