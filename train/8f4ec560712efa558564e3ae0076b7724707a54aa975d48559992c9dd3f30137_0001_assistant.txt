I am, in fact, a small man. Don't mind the fact that I am green. I have green hands, green legs, green arms, a green face, and a green body. Sometimes I come across people, and they tell me that I am not actually a small man. And so I hiss at them and go about my day. I am just here to exist the same as them.

What does my day entail as, in fact, a small man? Well, I wake up in the morning on my warm rock bed. The heat keeps me happy and alive. Once I get up, I try to put on a suitcoat. But I am just a small man. The same goes for my shoes, they never fit right, even in small sizes. But it's okay. I am happy nonetheless. Once I get ready, or moreso don't get ready because nothing fits, it's time to go about my day.

I am, in fact, a small man who goes around. I like walking around endlessly. The world is quite small, isn't it? I only see the same people every day, but the transparent walls of the world separate me from the rest. That is until they pick me up and get me into the wider world. I try to run around sometimes because I want to know more about the larger world around me. But the other people keep grabbing me, and then they put me back. But then I realize, I am not, in fact, a small man but a lizard instead.
