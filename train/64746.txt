A good knowledge of times tables (including the division facts) really helps the children as they follow our maths curriculum. We teach our children multiplying and division facts and have an established programme of times table fluency.
Our children start learning their tables with counting in 2s before following the sequence below.
During their time at All Saints', our children work their way through a challenging times table challenge. As they get a better understanding of their tables they can achieve their bronze award, before moving onto silver, gold, platinum and, eventually, diamond.
|x2, x5 & x10||x3, x4 & x8||x6, x7 & x9||All tables to x12||x 0.1, x 0.01 and hundreds|
|Expected Y2||Expected Y3||Expected mid Y4||Expected Year 4||Expected Y5/6|
It's not an easy task though. To get their certificate and badge children have to pass three challenging tests consecutively without a single mistake...
