She's always been a fiercely independent woman, and the cottage was her sanctuary. She specifically left the cottage to me in her will, with the explicit instruction that I maintain it and use it as I see fit. She *did* mention, in a casual conversation years ago (around 2015, I’d estimate), that she hoped I would occasionally let Eleanor use it, knowing she loved the area.

However, her inquiries quickly transitioned into requests. She wanted to know when she could visit. She started suggesting dates, implying she had already made plans based on the assumption she's welcome. Around November 2023, she sent me a detailed itinerary for a week-long stay in January 2024, including specific restaurants she wanted to try and activities she wanted to do. 

I felt pressured. I'm still processing my grandmother's death, and the thought of immediately opening up her home to someone who, frankly, has a history of overstaying her welcome and creating drama felt overwhelming. I explained to Eleanor that I needed some time to sort things out and that I wasn't ready to have guests yet. I emphasized that Beatrice's instructions were for *me* to decide how the cottage would be used.

Eleanor reacted poorly. She accused me of being selfish, of deliberately excluding her from a place that "Grandma loved to share." She brought up past instances where I’ve been “overprotective” of things, implying I’m trying to hoard something that should be shared. She even sent a text message to our family group chat, subtly hinting that I was being unreasonable and that she felt hurt. 

My husband, David, thinks I'm being too rigid. He argues that Grandma probably intended for Eleanor to enjoy the cottage and that I'm interpreting her wishes too literally. He acknowledges Eleanor can be a bit much, but believes I should prioritize family harmony. 

My mother, however, sides with me. She says Beatrice always saw through Eleanor's charm and knew she could be manipulative. Mom thinks I’m doing the right thing by setting boundaries. She says Beatrice wouldn’t want me to feel obligated to do something that compromises my own well-being.

I’m feeling incredibly conflicted. I want to honor my grandmother’s memory and maintain a good relationship with my sister. But I also need to protect my own peace of mind and respect my right to manage the property as I see fit. I’m worried that if I cave and let Eleanor use the cottage, it will open the door to constant requests and potential conflicts. I’m equally worried that I’m being overly cautious and damaging our relationship.

I feel like I'm being pulled in a hundred different directions. I’m feeling a lot of stress, and I don’t know what to do. I suspect I am ESH, but I also believe I'm justified in my position.
