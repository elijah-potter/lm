**Storm brewing over the abandoned factory:**
Rain lashes the rusted steel gates like fingernails on glass. Thunder splits the sky, muffling the distant wail of a train as it derails into the empty lot. Inside, the flickering fluorescent lights cast jagged shadows on the ashen walls—each flicker a heartbeat. A lone figure stands at the center of the room, arms crossed against the cold, watching as the storm’s fury mirrors their hollow gaze. The wind howls through the broken windows, carrying whispers of the past that echo like the last drops of rain. Outside, the city lights flicker—out of phase with the storm’s fury.
