Stalking - a pattern of harassment, intimidation, or terrorization of a singular victim - is experienced by roughly 4 to 7 percent of men and 12 to 16 percent of women. The occurrence of cyberstalking is only about 25%, though that percentage is likely to rise. The most common targets for stalking are former spouses or lovers, with 70 to 80 percent of stalkers being men, although women and strangers can also be stalkers.
