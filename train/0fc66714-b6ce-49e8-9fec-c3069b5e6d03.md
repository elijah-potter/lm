**First Ending:**
A lone scholar, searching for the lost artifact of the forgotten temple, stumbles upon a hidden door marked with runes that glow faintly in the moonlight. Inside, the artifact—a silver locket containing a single drop of star liquid—pulses with an eerie energy. The scholar, trembling, opens it, and the drop expands into a swirling vortex of light. The door seals behind them as the scholar finds themselves not in the temple, but in a vast, endless library where books rewrite themselves in real-time. The scholar chooses to stay, immersing themselves in knowledge forever, their name erased from history as the library consumes all memory of them.

---
**Second Ending:**
The scholar, instead of opening the locket, presses their palm against the glowing runes and whispers: *"I claim this, but only as a test."* The artifact responds, materializing a mirror that reflects not their image, but a younger version of themselves—one who made the same choice years earlier. The mirror vanishes, and the scholar finds themselves back at the temple, older and wiser. The artifact remains hidden, its power untapped, and the scholar returns to their studies, writing a book titled *"The Choice of the Silent"*—a forgotten tale of fate and decision.
