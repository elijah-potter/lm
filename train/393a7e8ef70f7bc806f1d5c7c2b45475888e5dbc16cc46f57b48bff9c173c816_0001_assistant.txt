On Monday, May 1, a 48-year-old man was arriving at work when he saw someone leaving the property with a stolen catalytic converter. The accused thief who was 32, ran to his car, and the 48-year-old got back into his vehicle. The two vehicles collided. While the younger man was trying to escape, the 48-year-old man shot him several times. The accused thief was rushed to the hospital with life-threatening injuries and the 48-year-old man was arrested on a charge of aggravated assault with a deadly weapon.
