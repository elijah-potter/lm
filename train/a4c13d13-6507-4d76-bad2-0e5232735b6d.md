The air hummed softly with the distant chime of a clock, its gentle tick-tock fading into the space between breaths. The room exhaled—warm, stale, and the scent of old wood and damp wool clung to the stillness. A single candle flickered, its flame a tiny, trembling pulse against the dark, casting long, wavering shadows that danced like restless fingers. The silence wasn’t empty—it held the weight of things unsaid, the absence of voices, the hush of a world that had stopped for a moment. Even the silence itself seemed to pause, listening.
