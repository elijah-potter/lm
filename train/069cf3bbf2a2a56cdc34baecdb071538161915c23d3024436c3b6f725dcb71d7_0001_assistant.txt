What's a cat's favorite show? Claw and Order.

Some 14,000 years ago, animals started trading their role as predators or prey for pets. Dogs of course made the transition first when wolves became human's companions and protectors. Cats, however, waited to join human society until around 8,000 years ago. Cats hung around villages and cities to catch rodents after the latter quickly learned that crops were an easy meal. Humans accepted cats after noticing they kept the rodent population in check, and cats decided that living among people wasn't all that bad.

What do cats like to eat on a hot day? A mice-cream cone.

Today cats and dogs have shed their predator and prey images and even their pet status. Dogs spend their days at doggie daycare, serve as ring bearers for weddings, enjoy a trip to the beach and dress up for Halloween. Cats own the internet with rumba-riding cat videos, sleep on their human's beds and eat food fit for people.  Owning a pet has been shown to help lower blood pressure, relieve stress and depression and increase the human life span. But perhaps the greatest benefit of having a pet is the unconditional love and companionship they provide. In return, cats and dogs are loved and cherished and considered vital family members.
