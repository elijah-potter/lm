Don`t Ignore Your Body`s Warning Signs, Because They Can Save Your Life Some Day!
Learn what health symptoms your body has that are warning signs you should pay attention to.
White of the eye
Normally, the white of the eye is always white. Red eye may be an indication of allergic reaction, reaction to stress, or can be a sign of a viral infection.
Grey hairs before you turn 35
This can be a sign of many health issues, including diabetes, chronic stress, or can simply be due to genetics.
Too many moles
Generally, these skin growths are harmless. However, in certain cases these moles may be a symptom of skin cancer. Therefore, if you notice abnormal number of moles on your skin, consult your dermatologist.
Dry, chapped lips
Chapped lips are usually caused by the weather. However, in certain cases it can indicate lack of vitamin B, C or zinc. Also, it can be caused by fungal infection.
