Last night, I had a dream that was really more like a nightmare. I woke up drenched in sweat, exhausted, and out of breath. And while I don’t know quite what it meant, I don’t want to forget a single thing. 

So here’s what happened: The first thing I noticed was that I was running. But not just running; I was running for my life. Although I didn’t know what I was running from at the time, I knew I had to move fast. 

Then, it became clear that there were walls around me; I was running through a kind of labyrinth. It wasn’t made out of plaster or wood; the walls were giant plants that seemed to be moving like little animals. 

As I sprinted through the maze, trying to take the correct turns, I caught a glimpse of something behind me. And even though I didn’t completely see it, somehow I knew that it was a giant cat. But not like a lion or a tiger; it was a domestic cat, but it was the size of a truck. 

The fear became almost debilitating, but I couldn’t stop running. And just as I started to wonder why the cat was chasing me, I felt something behind me. I realized it was a long tail, almost like rope. I don’t know how I knew, but it was suddenly clear that I was a mouse. 

Now that it made sense as to why the cat was chasing me, I still couldn’t figure out how to get out of the labyrinth. I knew if I slowed down at all, I would be eaten and killed. The intense fear for my life kept me going at top speed. 

It felt as though the cat was getting closer and closer, and just when I thought I was done for, I woke up in my bed.
