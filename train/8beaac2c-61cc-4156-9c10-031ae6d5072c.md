An unwritten pact between unseen hands:
*No one will speak of what they share silently,*
*Yet every whispered breath carries weight—*
*A code of stars in the dark, a trust unspoken,*
*Bound not by words, but by the hollows where words fail.*

The contract is carved in shadows:
*"You give me your silence, I give you mine in return—*
*Not a vow, but a rhythm—*
*Drifting through the cracks where truth doesn’t need to be named."*

The terms are written in the gaps between things:
*"What we hold close enough to touch is never told aloud,*
*But the hands that hold it are the same hands that know it’s true."*

No pen, no seal—just the hum of unseen agreements,
Where the lost and the found meet in the quiet between the lines.
