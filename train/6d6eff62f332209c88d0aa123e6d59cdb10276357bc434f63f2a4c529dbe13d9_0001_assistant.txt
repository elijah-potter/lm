Sure! These are the quickest ways to deal with a rat infestation.  

- Call pest control. If the infestation is severe, you may want to let a professional handle it. 
- Purchase rat traps, rat poison, and pest deterrents. These devices and chemicals are specifically designed to trick the rats and trap, injure, or kill them. Be sure to read the directions carefully as you install or apply these items. 
- Get a cat. Cats love to hunt rodents, so introducing a new friend to your family might be a quick (and cute) solution for your rat problem.
