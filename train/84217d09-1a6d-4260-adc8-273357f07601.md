Once upon a time, in a cozy green house with a big red door, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown ears that wiggled when he was happy. One sunny morning, Benny woke up and saw his favorite flower, the Sunny Petal, glowing bright yellow in his garden.

"Oh, Benny!" the flower said, "I’ve been waiting for you! Want to play?"

Benny jumped with joy. He hopped to the flower and plucked it carefully. "You’re so pretty!" he said.

A tiny mouse named Muffy scurried near the bushes. "Benny, I found a shiny acorn!" she whispered. "Let’s bury it in the sunniest spot!"

Together, they dug a hole. Benny hid the acorn, and Muffy placed a big pebble on top. "Now it’s safe," she said.

While they played, Benny’s big brown eyes sparkled. "I love this sunshine!" he cheered.

Suddenly, a big gust of wind blew, and the acorn rolled far away. Benny’s ears drooped. "Oh no! Where did it go?"

Muffy giggled. "Don’t worry! We’ll find it!"

They ran everywhere—under the bushes, through the tall grass, and even under the house. Just as Benny was about to give up, they spotted it! The acorn was near a big, happy squirrel who had been collecting nuts.

"Found it!" Benny said, holding up the acorn. The squirrel grinned. "You’re our best little helpers!"

From that day on, Benny and Muffy played together every morning, and the Sunny Petal always smiled back. And that’s how they made the best friends in the whole garden.
