Learning to make a proper mirepoix can take your cooking to the next level. Traditionally a mirepoix is the beginning of many great French dishes. It is a combination of onions, carrots, and celery that are diced small and sauteed in some kind of fat. The result is a fantastic base that has deep flavor and a strong aroma that can add great seasoning to elevate a dish. While the traditional versions utilize onions, carrots, and celery, there are a number of other variations that use other aromatic vegetables that can be tweaked to create many different flavor bases.
