In the historic district of Eldermere, a rare discovery has sparked excitement across the city. Archaeologists uncovered an ancient stone tablet beneath the ruins of the Blackthorn Clocktower, revealing cryptic inscriptions in a language long thought lost. Local historian Elias Veyers confirms the text is an encrypted map detailing a buried treasure said to hold artifacts from the city’s founding era. Police are urging residents to remain vigilant against unauthorized excavations, while the city council is considering a special fund to preserve the site. Meanwhile, neighboring towns have begun organizing patrols to prevent looters from claiming the find. The mayor has declared a public holiday on the 12th to allow residents time to visit the newly opened exhibit at the city archives.
