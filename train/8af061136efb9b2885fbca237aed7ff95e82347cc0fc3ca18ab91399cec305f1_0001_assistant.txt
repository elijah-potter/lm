A mouse was running along the bank of a pond in search of an adventure when he came upon a frog. The frog tied his leg to the mouse's leg with a tough reed to help him swim in the pond. Soon, the mouse wanted to go back to shore but the treacherous frog pulled the mouse underwater and drowned him. Before the frog was able to untie his leg, a hawk spotted the mouse floating on the water and swooped down, carrying both the mouse and the frog away for dinner.
