Toy Story 3 faced the difficult challenge of maintaining the quality featured in the first two films, but it succeeds in that challenge. Toy Story 3 is one of Pixar's best films and one of the best installments in the Toy Story series. The film takes place with Andy heading to college, where his classic childhood toys are mistakenly sent to a garbage dump before Buzz reroutes them to a local daycare. They find out that the daycare is run by what amounts to a toy mafia headed by Lotso, a toy bear. The toys must find a way to survive being sent to the infant room and escape their way from the daycare. This movie takes its inspiration from many genres of film including film noir, westerns and more.
