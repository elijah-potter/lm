Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big red door, there lived a bunny named Benny who loved to hop around the garden. Benny’s garden was full of colorful flowers—pink roses, yellow daisies, and big blue buttercups. Benny would sit and watch the sunrise every morning, and when the sky turned gold, he’d say, "Today is a wonderful day to play!"

One sunny morning, Benny spotted a tiny, shivering mouse near his garden. The mouse was scared and had lost its way. Benny hopped over and asked, "Oh, little friend, what’s wrong?" The mouse said, "I’m lost! My home is far away, and I don’t know where to go." Benny smiled. "Don’t worry, my friend! I’ll help you find your way home!"

Benny had a special trick—he could always find his way back to his garden using his big, soft ears and his tiny paws. He led the mouse to a patch of clover where they could rest. "Here, take a nap," Benny said, nudging a soft leaf with his nose. The mouse blinked and yawned. "Thank you, Benny! You’re the best friend ever!"

When the mouse woke up, the sun was high in the sky, and Benny said, "Time to go, little friend!" The mouse looked around. "But where is my home?" Benny pointed to a tiny hole in the grass. "That’s where you live! It’s just over there." The mouse smiled. "You’re right! I’ll never forget this."

Benny hopped back to his garden, where his mommy bunny was picking berries. "Did you find the lost mouse, Benny?" she asked. Benny hopped over and said, "Yes! They’re going to be best friends forever!" His mommy clapped her paws together. "That’s wonderful! Being kind makes the world brighter."

From that day on, Benny and the mouse visited each other every evening. They shared their snacks—Benny loved carrots, and the mouse loved tiny seeds—and told stories about their adventures. The garden became their happy home, filled with laughter and friendship.

And so, in a world where kindness grows like flowers, Benny the bunny showed everyone that even the smallest acts can make the biggest difference. The end!
