I know not where I'm going, or where I'm headed to,
but I am getting there in due time;
Faster than due time, in fact;
I'm traveling at the speed of light.

I'm traveling quickly to nowhere and to nothing,
even though I'm standing in one place;
I haven't moved a muscle,
Yet I'm being dragged unwillingly toward an end.

I look at my watch and it's morning;
I look at my watch again and it's the afternoon;
I decide to ignore my watch,
and instead opt to watch the moon pass over me.

I keep fretting at the time,
but time does not fret for me;
It continues as if I were never there,
simply pushing all things toward eternity.

But I cannot be a part of eternity,
It is not part of my nature;
I can only stand in the stream and admire
as the waters of time pass through my fingers.

I'm Emma's locket
that has fallen on the ground;
staring up at Alexander in his machine,
who looks down at me as I disintegrate.

I'm the child as an adult
watching his children turn into adults;
Time grows everyone into strength,
and breaks them down into dust.

I am both in awe of and afraid of time;
I marvel at its steadfastness;
I cower at its longevity;
It is both fleeting and forever present.

It marches on relentlessly,
even when I am tired;
It refuses to let me catch my breath,
or enjoy its company when it is here.

I have a choice to make:
Do I stay or do I go?
I can stay and it will go;
Or I can go and it will go with me.

So I decide to go,
Not knowing where or to what destination;
I know I'll get there in due time
because I'm traveling with it.
