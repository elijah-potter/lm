The abandoned plan documented in the 2018 project logs:
1. **Project Title**: "Neo-Earth Climate Stabilization Initiative (NECSI)"
   - Original Goal: Deploy geoengineering systems to stabilize Earth’s climate by reflecting ~2% of solar radiation.
   - Abandonment Reason: High risk of unintended ecological disruption outweighed potential benefits; public backlash and scientific skepticism led to funding diversion.

2. **Failed Component**: "Stratospheric Aerosol Injection (SAI) Test Phase"
   - Proposed: Release 10,000 tons of sulfur dioxide annually in stratosphere for cooling effect.
   - Abandoned Due to:
   - Unpredictable atmospheric feedbacks (e.g., ozone depletion).
   - Criticism of moral hazard ("fixing one problem creates another").
   - Budget reallocation to renewable energy R&D (2019).

3. **Alternate Path**: Shifted focus to carbon capture pilot projects and solar panel expansion under "Green Transition Accelerator" program (2020).

4. **Key Quote from Lead Scientist (2019):**
   *"Climate action must avoid being seen as a distraction from systemic change. The public must trust that every dollar spent on mitigation is spent wisely—not as a gamble."*
