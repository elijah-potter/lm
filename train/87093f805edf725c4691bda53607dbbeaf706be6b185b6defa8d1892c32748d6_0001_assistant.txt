Of course! Here are a few choices for fun and adventurous things to do in Branson as a family:

1. Visit Table Rock State Park - There are many activities to do within the park including hiking, biking, boating, and swimming. The marina has rentals for motorized and non-motorized boats/activities. 
2. Go to Talking Rocks Caverns - This is a vertical cave with stairs that lead to a cavern. You'll see why it was named "Fairy Cave" because of the backdrop of crystals. Once you've done the hike down, you can go back up to the surface for mini-golf, panning for gems, and other activities for kids. 
3. Check out Moonshine Beach - The beach has crystal clear waters that you can swim, picnic, and enjoy a day of sun. 
4. Visit Butterfly Palace - Equipped with an aviary, you can walk around with butterflies, birds, and exotic botanicals.
