**The Last Transmission**

The ship’s AI beeped urgently: **"Countdown to re-entry: 12:45:37 (UTC)."**

Captain Elias adjusted the viewport—glowing blue veins of oxygen through the atmosphere. Below, the Earth pulsed like a dying star. *"We’re out of time,"* he whispered. The crew held their breath as the shields flared, repelling cosmic dust. The countdown tick-tocked in the terminal: **12:45:36.5**.

*"Go!"* Elias shouted. The engines roared, and the ship plunged through the sky. **12:45:36.3**—the heat shields cracked. **12:45:36.1**—the hull groaned. **12:45:36.0**—the impact shook the air.

Silence.

The screen flickered: **"Splashdown confirmed. ETA: 12:46:01."** The water swallowed the ship whole. The last transmission was a static scream: *"…countdown…"*

**12:46:01**—the waves lapped at the wreckage. The world ended at **12:45:36.0**.
