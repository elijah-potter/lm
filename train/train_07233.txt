Emma has saved $230 in her bank account. She withdrew $60 to buy a new pair of shoes. The next week, she deposited twice as much money as she withdrew. How much is in her bank account now?
Emma had $230 - $60 = $<<230-60=170>>170 left in her bank account after she withdrew $60.
The next week, she deposited $60 x 2 = $<<60*2=120>>120.
Hence, she now has a total of $170 + $120 = $<<170+120=290>>290 in her bank account.
