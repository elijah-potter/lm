The dim glow of a flickering streetlamp cast long shadows on the cobblestones as a half-drowned figure clutched a rusted lantern, its glass cracked and filled with water. The **moonless night** pressed down, heavy and silent, while the **ancient brick walls** of the forgotten alleyway pulsed with the slow rhythm of damp and time. The **puddles of stagnant water** reflected nothing—only the dark, the weight of centuries, the way the **old iron gates** groaned as they swung shut behind the last pedestrian, sealing the world beyond into the cold, indifferent void. A distant **cogging sound**—like a dying engine—echoed from the **crumbling warehouse** behind them, its skeletal structure bent by decades of neglect, its windows boarded up with splintered planks. The air smelled of **rot and ozone**, the scent of something that had been buried too long, waiting to rise. The figure’s breath came shallow, the lantern’s last ember flickering—then snuffed. The alleyway yawned around them, a **mouth of forgotten things**.
