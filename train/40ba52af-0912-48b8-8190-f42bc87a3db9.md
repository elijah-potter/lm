Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big tree outside, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop, play, and explore the sunny meadow near his home.

One morning, Benny woke up and heard a tiny voice calling, "Benny! Benny!" Benny peeked out his window and saw a little blue bird sitting on a flower. "Hello, little bird!" Benny said, hopping over to say hello.

The blue bird said, "I’m scared of the big, dark clouds rolling in! What if it rains? What if the wind blows my nest away?" Benny felt sad for the bird. "Don’t worry, little bird," Benny said. "I’ll help you find a safe spot to stay until the storm passes."

Benny and the blue bird searched together. They found a big, hollow tree trunk where the bird could build a cozy nest. Benny even made a little bridge of leaves to help the bird climb safely inside. The blue bird nestled down happily, and Benny sat beside her, watching the clouds.

Soon, the wind got louder, and Benny held the blue bird close, whispering, "It’s almost over, little one." Just then, a gentle rain began, and Benny watched as the raindrops sparkled in the sunlight. The clouds moved away, and the sun peeked out, making the meadow sparkle.

When the storm passed, Benny helped the blue bird build her nest stronger. "Thank you, Benny," the bird chirped happily. Benny smiled and said, "You’re always welcome here, little friend."

From that day on, Benny and the blue bird played together every day, sharing sunshine, rain, and all the adventures in the meadow. And they learned that even the smallest hearts can make the biggest friends.
