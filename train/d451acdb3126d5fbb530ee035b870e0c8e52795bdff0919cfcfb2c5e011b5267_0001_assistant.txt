Here are three places to bring your pup:
1. Lakes and Legends Brewing Company: Try their farm-to-table beer and give your dog a treat from their food trucks, including Burrito Mercado, Gastropub, and Northbound Smoke House.
2. Inbound BrewCo: Stop at their Doggy Station for treats, water bowls, and yoga mats to keep them off the cold floor. You can try their food trucks, such as Outlaw Grill, O'Cheeze, and Market BBQ.
3. Sisyphus Brewing: Play their board and pinball games. The staff loves dogs. They'll give them woofers water bowls and treats. They also raise bones for No Dog Left Behind.
