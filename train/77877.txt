Students use the Engineering Design Process to design, create, test, and refine a variety of flying machines. They construct a shock absorbing system designed to protect two marshmallow astronauts in a lunar vehicle, create their own air-powered rockets, assemble a hot air balloon model that actually takes flight, and more. No discounts or vouchers. Registration is required by going online through District 128's Community Education Department’s website or by calling.
