A vast, open plaza surrounded by curved, geometric stone arches. The air hums with the soft, rhythmic drone of a single, ancient bell, resonating through the hollow spaces between the structures. The ground beneath is smooth, polished marble, reflecting the muted light filtering through the arches. Seated in a loose, circular arrangement, people sit cross-legged or kneel, their hands clasped or fingers tracing intricate patterns on their laps. A low, ambient glow from flickering lanterns cast long shadows, creating an ethereal, almost dreamlike atmosphere. The only sounds are the occasional whisper of wind through the arches and the gentle, synchronized movement of individuals as they practice a silent, meditative dance or form a circle to listen to a single flute playing a slow, contemplative melody.
