Once upon a time in a cozy green forest, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop and play every day, but one sunny morning, he heard a tiny voice whispering, "Help me!"

Benny followed the sound and found a little blue bird with a broken wing. The bird was trying to fly but couldn’t reach its nest.

Benny hopped over and gently picked up the bird. "Don’t worry, my little friend," Benny said. "I’ll help you get better!"

With big, strong paws, Benny carried the bird to a safe place in the tall grass. He stayed with the bird all day, singing softly and talking to it. "You’ll fly again soon," Benny promised.

When the sun began to set, Benny saw the blue bird’s wing look all better. The bird chirped happily and flew up high into the sky, reaching its nest safely.

Benny smiled, knowing he had helped his new friend. As he hopped home, he felt very happy because he had made someone smile and feel safe.

The next morning, Benny saw the blue bird visiting him again. "Thanks for helping me," the bird said. Benny grinned. "Anytime, my little friend! Now let’s play together!"

So Benny and the blue bird spent many happy days hopping, singing, and flying together in the forest. And that’s how Benny learned that being kind makes the world a better place for everyone.
