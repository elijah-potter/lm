Brian was doing quite well in his driving test. At first. He did well enough until the second traffic light. He didn't even know that he did something wrong until the truck was honking at him and his examiner was screaming about trying to kill him. The examiner instructed him to pull over and proceeded to scream till he was blue in the face. "Don't you know the difference between a red and a green light?!" Tearfully, Brian replied, "I'm a dog, I don't know the difference between any colors."
