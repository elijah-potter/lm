The sun dipped low behind jagged, mist-shrouded mountains, casting long, jagged shadows across the cracked earth. Suddenly, the sky split open in a violent, electric rupture—an aurora borealis erupted, painting the horizon in swirling violet and emerald. Warmth flooded the air, and the ground trembled as the atmosphere hummed with unseen energy. Shadows dissolved into light, merging with the sky’s luminous chaos. The forest’s ancient trees seemed to exhale, their leaves trembling as if caught in a sudden, inexplicable breath. The earth itself seemed to pulse, vibrating with the shift in light.
