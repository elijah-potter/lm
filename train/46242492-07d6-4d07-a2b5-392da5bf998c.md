Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a garden full of colorful flowers, there lived a tiny bunny named Benny who loved to explore. One sunny morning, Benny hopped outside and saw a shiny blue bugle lying in the grass. He picked it up and tried to play it, but it made a loud, funny noise that made all the animals giggle.

Just then, Benny’s best friend, a friendly old owl named Oliver, flew down and said, "That’s not a bugle, Benny! It’s a magic wand that can make wishes come true!" Benny was very excited. Oliver showed him how to use it by waving it and making a tiny cloud appear in the sky.

"Wow!" Benny said. "What can I wish for?" Oliver smiled. "You can wish for anything you want, as long as it’s kind and fun!" Benny thought hard. "I wish for a big, big garden full of the happiest flowers in the world!" Suddenly, the garden turned golden and sparkled. Every flower bloomed with laughter and happiness.

The animals cheered, and Benny danced with joy. "Oliver," he said, "you’re the best friend ever!" The owl ruffled his feathers. "That’s because you’re kind and brave, just like me."

From that day on, Benny and Oliver spent every day making magic in their garden, teaching others to be kind and happy. And that’s how Benny the bunny learned that wishes could be the most wonderful thing of all.
