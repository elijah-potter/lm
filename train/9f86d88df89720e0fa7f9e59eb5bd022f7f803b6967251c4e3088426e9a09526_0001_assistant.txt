Janie was confused, her head hurt, and she was incredibly thirsty. She knew the orb had been surrounded by people when she touched it, and now there was no one around. She had no idea how long she had been lying there and what had happened to all the people. Her hand went to her necklace, and it felt warm. She remembered that it had been glowing and pulsing in time with the orb, but now neither her necklace nor the orb was emitting any light or sound. All was quiet.

Janie looked back toward the path where she had come from the resort down to the beach, but she couldn’t see the entrance. It was well-marked, and there was a sign leading back to the resort, but she didn’t see it. She looked towards the ocean, which was a striking shade of turquoise with waves breaking on the shore. She could see the mountains in the distance. Janie had no idea what was going on, but she knew something was very wrong. She had come to Kaui looking for inspiration for her sci-fi novel, and now she felt like she was in a sci-fi novel herself. There had to be an explanation – maybe she had been asleep a long time and everyone left? Why would they leave her passed out on the beach without calling 911 or trying to wake her? It made her hurt even worse trying to figure it out, and right now her main concern was water. 

She could see what appeared to be some tents in the distance, and she definitely saw smoke. It looked like someone was having a bonfire on the beach, and she could see a few people fishing, maybe a mile or so away. Since there was no pathway back up to the resort, Janie decided to make her way toward the people and ask for some water and some help.
