- 1Identify why they are hyper. Chart when they are hyper, and the coinciding events that have applied. This can help prevent it altogether, or calm it when it happens. If they eat certain foods that make them hyper, then don't give your children too much of it.
- 2Speak in a calming voice that does not revealing your emotions. This will calm them and also establish you a level of power over them.
- 3Give them attention. Often, hyperactive children just require your attention, and that's the only reason they are, in fact, hyper.
- 4Don't lower their self-esteem by challenging them- instead, be kind and request for them to stop.
- 5Try and give them physical relief. This could be a massage or even access to something like a stress ball.
- 6Give them an outlet for their energies. Let them exercise by running around, for example.
- 7Challenge the necessity of what the child is doing. This will make them reconsider whether it is worth doing.
- Sometimes, they might be hyperactive because they believe it is not what you want, and they want to annoy to their own entertainment, or to gain your attention. In this case, use reverse-psychology in order to dissuade them from what they are doing. If their key purpose has failed, there is no incentive to do it anymore.
- When you have ascertained what causes hyperactivity, avoid whatever it is that causes it.
- To avoid hyperactivity in the future, establish a routine, so kids know what to expect, and what you expect from them. Also, some kids might be hyperactive due to the lack of routine- therefore, they will be calmer when one is made.
