- 'Close Enough'. Drawn in the style of 'Regular Show', this adult animated series follows a couple transitioning from their twenties and thirties and the amusing struggles they face. It portrays the struggle of trying to stay young while aging and the trials of parenthood.
- 'Castlevania'. A sometimes-horrifying adaptation of the popular video game series, 'Castlevania' is mature and has a rich cast of characters to flesh out its action-filled story. The show follows three main characters as they attempt to protect the world from vampires and other monsters.
- 'American Dad'. A classic sitcom that follows Stan Smith, a CIA agent, and his wacky family. It is packed full of humor and pokes fun at both the mundane and absurd aspects of everyday life.
- 'Futurama'. Incredibly popular, 'Futurama' follows delivery boy Fry as he is cryogenically frozen and wakes up 1,000 years in the future to meet the robot Bender, the cyclops Leela, and more. Foul-mouthed humor intersects with surprisingly emotional episodes as relationships between the characters are explored.
