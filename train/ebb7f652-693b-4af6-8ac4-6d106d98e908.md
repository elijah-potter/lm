A sharp knock at the office door interrupted the hushed meeting. The chair screeched as the CEO stood abruptly, his usual composure faltering. The room’s tension thickened like smoke. "I’m afraid we’ve been called away," he announced, voice tight. The team exchanged glances—some disappointment, others a quiet resolve. The whiteboard’s unfinished diagram of the new project flickered under his gaze. "Urgent matters elsewhere," he said, then turned without waiting for answers, his heels clicking away. The meeting dissolved into murmurs. The plan, half-formed and still damp with ambition, lay abandoned on the floor like a discarded toy.
