Dear Editor:

If you haven't driven through our downtown shopping district lately, you've been spared the disheartening view of the piles of litter accumulating there. A city as beautiful as ours should take more pride in our beautiful, natural environment and do a better job of keeping things tidy and presentable.

We only have one world and it is our responsibility to help protect the planet. It doesn't take very much time to hold on to your refuse until you find an appropriate receptacle for it. It may help to keep a small grocery bag in your car to collect your trash and then dispose of it when you reach your destination...in a proper garbage can or dumpster, of course!

I hope my fellow citizens will think before they act, because their thoughtless behavior is turning this city from one we could be proud of into a garbage dump. What a terrible first impression we are making on tourists!  We can do better than this.

Sincerely,
A Concerned Citizen
