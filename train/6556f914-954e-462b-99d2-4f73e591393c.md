The dimly lit alley was slick with rain, reflections of flickering streetlamps dancing on the wet pavement. A lone figure stood against the brick wall, arms crossed, observing the distant crowd of revelers. The neon glow of the city pulsed in time with the distant bass of a music system, casting long shadows. A half-empty bottle of whiskey lay abandoned at their feet. The night air carried the scent of damp earth and exhaust fumes. A distant shout echoed, but the figure remained unmoving, watching the scene unfold before them. The streetlights flickered.
