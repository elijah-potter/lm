A skeletal foundation of exposed concrete pillars supports a crumbling steel frame, half-buried in overgrown ivy. The floor slabs, cracked and warped, bear faded graffiti in faded paint. The walls show where windows were cut—now boarded with weathered plywood. Stairs spiral down into a dark void where the ceiling is missing, revealing raw beams and exposed wiring. Dust coats everything, thick enough to muffle sound. The air smells of damp earth and old rust. Rust-colored water seeps from a broken pipe near the ceiling, pooling in the center. The only intact feature is a rusted metal sign that once read *"Under Construction"*, now half-chipped with illegible text beneath. Shadows stretch long across the uneven ground, hinting at the unfinished height above.
