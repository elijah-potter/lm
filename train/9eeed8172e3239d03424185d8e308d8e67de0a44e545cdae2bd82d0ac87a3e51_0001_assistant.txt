Parmigiano Reggiano is a very special cheese that has been made in a particular region within Italy using only three ingredients. These ingredients include salt, milk, and rennet which are combined in a particular process starting with heating the milk and adding in the rennet slowly to coagulate and form curds. These curds are then taken and broken down into small pieces and processed so they form into a single piece which is then formed into wheels and dunked in a saltwater brine. After the wheels are dunked they must be aged for at least 12 months. This cheese comes with quite a few regulations as the cows that produce the milk can only be fed locally foraged greens, grass, and hay. It can also not be sold as Parmigiano Reggiano until it passes a rigorous testing procedure. The area in which it is produced is also highly regulated as it can only be made in 5 Italian Provinces where Parmigiano Reggiano has been produced for nearly 1000 years.
