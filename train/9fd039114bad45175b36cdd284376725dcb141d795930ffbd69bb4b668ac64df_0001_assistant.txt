Queen Victoria and Prince Albert had nine children, five daughters, and four sons.

• Princess Victoria: November 21, 1840
• Prince Albert Edward: November 9, 1841
• Princess Alice: April 25, 1843
• Prince Alfred: August 6, 1844
• Princess Helena: May 25, 1846
• Princess Louise: March 18, 1848
• Prince Arthur: May 1, 1850
• Prince Leopold: April 7, 1853
• Princess Beatrice: April 14, 1857
