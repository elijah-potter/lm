The three animals with the strongest bite are:
1. The great white shark has the strongest bite, at 4000 psi. For reference, that's enough force to damage bricks with water. 
2. The saltwater crocodile comes in second at 3700 psi. For comparison, biting into a steak can only take around 150 psi. 
3. The Nile crocodile comes in third at 3000 psi. 3000 psi is common in pressure washers.
