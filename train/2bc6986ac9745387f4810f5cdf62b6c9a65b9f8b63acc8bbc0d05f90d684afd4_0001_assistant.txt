Sauron, the Dark Lord, controlled the flow of water, wreaking havoc upon all those who relied upon it. The Elves pled for a steady stream to fill their ornate fountains, while the Dwarves demanded powerful pressure for their mining equipment. The Men desired a consistent temperature for their luxurious baths.

Yet, it was for naught. The Dark Lord's grip on the plumbing was too strong, and he reveled in his power over all those who needed it. The kingdoms were locked in a never-ending struggle for control of the plumbing, with alliances forged and broken in the pursuit of the precious water.

The One Ring of plumbing control was the ultimate power, granting Sauron dominion over all the races. But one thing was certain - all those who sought to control the flow of water would be forever changed by the corrupting influence of the Dark Lord's power.
