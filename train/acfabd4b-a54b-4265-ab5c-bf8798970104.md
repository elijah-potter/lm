Truth revealed in a whispered confession: *"I lied to cover for you. But I can’t keep pretending this isn’t real."*
Silence stretches, then—
A single tear, a hesitant nod, the weight of honesty settling like a stone in a still pond.
*"Why now?"* the other asks, fingers tracing the scar where the lie once ran.
The truth is not the answer. It is the wound left open.
