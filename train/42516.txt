How Much Does a Dentist Make?
Dentists made a median salary of $151,850 in 2018. The best-paid 25 percent made $208,000 that year, while the lowest-paid 25 percent made $107,440.
What Is the Outlook for Dentists?
The Bureau of Labor Statistics projects 7.6% employment growth for dentists between 2018 and 2028. In that period, an estimated 10,400 jobs should open up.

What Is the Dentist Salary by Place of Employment?
In addition to region and education, factors such as industry, experience and employer affect a dentist’s salary. The top-paying industries with the highest average annual salaries for this career are residential intellectual and developmental disability, mental health, and substance abuse facilities ($192,680); dental offices ($178,350); state government ($174,070); offices of other health practitioners ($172,670); and outpatient care centers ($160,410). The majority of dentists work full time. Some dentists work beyond normal business hours or on weekends.
