Once upon a time, in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown fur and big, round eyes that sparkled like stars.

Every morning, Benny would hop outside to play in the bright, sunny garden. The grass was soft under his tiny paws, and the flowers smelled sweet. Benny loved to chase butterflies and collect acorns.

One sunny afternoon, Benny spotted a shiny, golden acorn. He picked it up and noticed it was different from the others. As he nibbled on it, something magical happened! Benny’s fur turned a little bit golden too, and he could fly like a tiny bird.

"Wow!" Benny said, hopping happily. He zoomed over to his friend, a little squirrel named Sammy. "Sammy, look! I can fly!"

Sammy’s eyes widened. "Really? Can I try too?"

With a little wiggle of their tails, Benny and Sammy flew higher and higher, chasing clouds and laughing as the wind blew their ears.

But as the sun began to set, Benny felt a little tired. "I think I’ll come back tomorrow," he said to Sammy.

The next morning, Benny flew back to his garden, but this time he noticed something new: his acorn was still golden, and so was the grass around him. He picked up a few more golden acorns and shared them with his friends.

From that day on, Benny’s garden was the happiest place in the whole world. The butterflies danced in golden circles, and every time Benny picked an acorn, it glowed softly, filling the garden with sunshine and joy.

And Benny learned that magic wasn’t just in stories—it could be found right in his own little home.
