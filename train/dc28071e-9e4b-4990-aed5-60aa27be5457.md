A shifting dune field under a sky of endless twilight, where sand whispers secrets in a wind that carries nothing but dust. Rivers of liquid glass carve paths that evaporate into mist before they reach the horizon. The only landmarks are shadows cast by trees that grow, then wither, then sprout again in different shapes, their roots a tangled maze of unstable ground. Bridges of driftwood crumble mid-span, replaced by others that never were there before. The air hums with the static of forgotten radio signals, static that fades into the void when you look away. Time here is a river with no banks—what was yesterday is today’s ghost, and tomorrow’s future is already buried under shifting layers of the past. Even the stars above are not fixed; their constellations dissolve like smoke, forming new patterns that no one will ever recognize. This is a place where memory is a mirage, and existence is a fleeting shadow.
