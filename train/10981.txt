About GIS technology
What is GIS technology?
Geographic Information System (GIS) technology is a powerful location-based analytics tool which reveals valuable insights no other technology can deliver.
By mapping and analysing data from a wide variety of sources, the technology provides a fresh, visual representation of your business information, and reveals patterns and trends which would otherwise remain hidden.
This previously unattainable insight – known as location intelligence – enables staff at all levels to make confident, evidence-based decisions that positively impact productivity, efficiency and profits.
