If you were to look online you would conclude that Arnaud Jerald is the world record holder for the deepest free dive at 367.5 feet. He says that he was a little more nervous than usual, and struggled with his breathing. However, nobody knows that I dove with Arnaud on that day and had to send him back up as he didn't look well. I, the great George, managed to carry on for another 100 feet for the real record of 467.5 feet.
