The submitted manuscript, *"The Impact of Urbanization on Bird Migration Patterns in the Northeast U.S.,"* was flagged by the editor for its innovative approach to integrating citizen science data with genomic studies. The reviewer noted a compelling personal anecdote about witnessing a rare migration stopover in a local park during their childhood, which the author included to contextualize their research. While the statistical rigor was strong, concerns were raised about the small sample size of the citizen science data, though the author argued that their "passionate engagement" with community science efforts mitigated this.

The second reviewer, more critical, questioned the methodological transparency of the genomic analysis, particularly the lack of raw sequence data. They suggested that the author’s personal bias—favoring high-impact publications over rigorous peer review—could skew interpretations. The editor proposed a third reviewer with expertise in both ornithology and bioinformatics to resolve discrepancies.

Final decision: **Revisions Needed**. The manuscript is deemed promising but requires clarification on data sources and statistical caveats. The personal touch is appreciated but should align with broader scientific standards.
