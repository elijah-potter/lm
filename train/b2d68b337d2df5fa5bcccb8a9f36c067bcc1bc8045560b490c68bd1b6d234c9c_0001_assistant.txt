The TLDR bottom-line pros and cons of investing vs. paying off a 30-year mortgage are as follows:

Paying off mortgage:

Pros:
1. Risk is reduced by reducing the mortgage balance and interest payments. 
2. Potential savings due to reducing the amount of money that would have been spent paying interest.

Cons:
1. Tax loss from reduced tax benefits as anticipated.
2. Opportunity loss, as the overall additional payment of the mortgage may prove to be less profitable than additional earnings from the investment.

Investing in the stock market:

Pros:
1. Higher potential returns from the stock market (potentially 10% or more per year) compared to homes (which typically appreciate at 2-4% per year).
2. Investment in a portfolio is more diverse and less volatile compared to investment in one fixed asset (the home) 

Cons:
1. Money invested in the stock market is subject to loss, which could be catastrophic, especially if one is planning to retire soon.
2. Market growth is not as guaranteed, whereas a paid-off home will always be a paid-off home.

There are many factors to consider for the best decision, such as how soon one will retire, the existing amount of remaining mortgage, the interest rates, the tolerance for risk, and the overall returns in the market. It is best to make an informed decision and decide what's right for you.
