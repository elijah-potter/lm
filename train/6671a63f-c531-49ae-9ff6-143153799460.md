The silence between breaths hums like a forgotten chord.
A weight in the ribs—one that isn’t yours—presses against the hollows.
Your name isn’t in the list of those who answered the phone.
The clock hands, though still ticking, are stuck on a number you didn’t know existed.
The room feels larger, the air thinner, as if the walls themselves are holding their breath.
You stand in the gap between two versions of time—one where they were there, one where they’re not.
The mirror’s reflection isn’t yours; it’s a stranger in a dress you don’t recognize.
Every door leads to the same empty hallway.
The absence is a presence, a ghost in the machine of your bones.
