Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had fluffy brown fur and big, round eyes that sparkled like the sun. Every morning, Benny would hop out of his burrow to see the world.

One sunny afternoon, Benny found a shiny red apple on his path. "Mmm, delicious!" he thought. Benny picked it up and nibbled a little. Suddenly, he heard a tiny voice, "Help!"

Benny looked down and saw a lost little bird with a broken wing. The bird flapped its wings a few times and cried, "I can't fly, Benny! Please help me!"

Benny felt sad. "Of course, I’ll help!" he said. He carried the bird home, where his mother was baking a treat. "Here, eat this," she said, placing a warm cookie near the bird. Benny fed the bird some berries from his basket.

The next morning, Benny checked on his friend. The bird was better! "Thank you, Benny!" chirped the bird. Benny smiled and said, "It’s my pleasure. Together, we can do anything!"

The bird flew high, dancing in the sky, while Benny happily hopped home to share his story with his family. And that’s how Benny and his new friend became the best of pals.
