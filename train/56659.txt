Barack Hussein Obama Jr., is the 44th president of the US. The first black American President, the first President with a non-US, Islamic lineage. Congratulations on your achievement, sir!
Barack Obama was born to Barack Hussein Obama Sr., belonging to an ethnic group in Kenya and Ann Dunham, a white American lady, on August 4, 1961 in Honolulu, Hawaii. He graduated from the Columbia University and then attended the Harvard Law School.
Obama’s stand on the nuclear weapons and the Iraq war have been noted in his race toward the presidency.
Others of his achievements include:
- He became an editor of the Harvard Law Review in the first year of attending the Harvard Law School.
- He worked in legal firms like the Sidley & Austin and Hopkins & Sutter.
- He graduated from Harvard as a JD (Juris Doctor).
