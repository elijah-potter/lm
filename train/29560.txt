How to register to vote?
If you want your name to register to vote for the first time, then you will be required to submit a copy of your proof of address and the proof of your age. You can also check with the local country council to register your name to vote. The applications forms can be obtained from the Assistant Electoral Registration offices nearest to your area. Similarly for submitting the forms back, there are some associated Assistant Registration electoral offices available.
There is a project named www.VoteIndia.in which was actually launched to register to vote according to good governance. It was a movement that was created for the people and by the people. The following will be the eligibility conditions for any responsible voter.They should come to know the following things before actually preparing to vote.
- Education – that is the qualification of the individual,
- Personal assets – an individual's current asset value, and
- Criminal background – this deals with the criminal records pending against the individual.
On registering to vote, no information about the candidate would be provided to the registered through the website. These information will be sent only through the email to those users who have registered to vote. The constituency or the ward where the election occurs can be found using this registration process. Before actually the election starts, the information about the candidates participating in election regarding the area of your residence would be sent to the registered person's inbox directly.
There are five simple steps involved in the process to register to vote. The first step would be to provide your name and email address in the application. The place of election can be searched using the search box on the map interface and locate your house. Later then your house will be marked and this enables to find out the exact constituency or ward where your house is located. This is how to register to vote.
