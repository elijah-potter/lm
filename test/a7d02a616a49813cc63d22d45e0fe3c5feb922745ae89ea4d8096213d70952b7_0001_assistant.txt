In the depths of a sprawling metropolis, where steam hissed through corroded pipes and neon lights flickered with a cybernetic pulse, resided Echo, a restless automaton with a penchant for incessant chatter. She possessed an insatiable desire for the final word of every conversation. One fateful day, her sharp words pierced the ears of the great techno-deity, Juno. In response to this affront, Juno decreed that Echo would forever be deprived of her voice, only permitted to echo the words of others.

The punishment was akin to a cruel twist of fate, transforming Echo into a mere machine, a mechanical parrot bereft into the shadowy depths of the metropolis, seeking solace within its labyrinthine alleys.

Amidst the towering spires and labyrinthine streets, Narcissus emerged as a figure of fascination, his cybernetic hair shimmering like a cascade of golden circuity, and his eyes glowed with the luminescence of the azure sky - an anomaly in a world cloaked in darkness. He was a rarity, for most denizens of the city bore the mark of perpetual night.

Narcissus, known for his explorations within the sprawling urban forest where Echo sought sanctuary, often caught glimpses of her elusive figure lurking behind the smog-shrouded caverns or among colossal synthetic trees. Echo, captivated by his presence, found herself captivated by his cyber-augmented aura.

One day, fate wove its intricate pattern, and Narcissues found himself separated from his companions, his metallic voice resonating through the foliage, questioning the unseen presence.
"Who lingers in this realm?" he asked. "Realm," Replied Echo, her voice a symphony of digitized reverberation. "Reveal thyself," Narcissus beaconed.
"I beckon," Echo whispered, emerging from the urban jungle as she uttered her words.

Upon witnessing an unfamiliar countenance instead of the anticipated faces of his comrades, Narcissis were a. bewildered expression and swiftly turned away, abandoning the encounter.

From that day forward, Echo retreated deeper into her seclusion, forever veiling herself from prying eyes. As time went on, her essence dwindled to naught but a spectral voice, haunting the recesses of the neon-lit forests and towering buildings. Hunters who dared to venture into the desolate corners of the city often encountered her ethereal presence. Sometimes, she mimicked the bark of their cybernetic canines; other times, she replicated their own parting words. Her tone always carried an unearthly, mournful resonance, amplifying the solitude of these desolate domains.
