A foreign exchange student program can help students in a variety of ways! Whether it's experiencing a new culture, impacting a student from a different country, or fostering friendships in your community and the exchange student community, there are a plethora of reasons that hosting can be beneficial for students at home. If you're at all curious about how students around the world fare, it may be a good option to directly impact someone's life for the better. Exchange programs have historically been a way to facilitate peacekeeping efforts between countries.

Community is always an important factor in making decisions about whether to host an exchange student. Many students and families walk away with a bond that extends far after school, connecting families from different geographies no matter the distance. There is also a rich and vast community of hosts across the United States. In hosting, there is always an opportunity to grow students into well-rounded citizens of the world and leaders of tomorrow.
