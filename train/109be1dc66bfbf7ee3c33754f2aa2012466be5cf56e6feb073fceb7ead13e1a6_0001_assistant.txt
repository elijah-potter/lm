This ice cream series features flavors that use a variety of upcycled foods. You'll find ice cream featuring bread pudding made from day-old loaves, cupcakes made from upcycled flour (okara), milk made from spent barley used in the beer brewing process, cacao pulp, and whey.  These upcycled products are then used in a variety of ice creams, some vegan some not. In some ice creams, the upcycled ingredient is the star of the show, and in others, they are part of a mix-in.
