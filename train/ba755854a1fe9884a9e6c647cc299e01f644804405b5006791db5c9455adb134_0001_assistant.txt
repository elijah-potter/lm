Chess Grandmaster Hans Niemann is suing chess world champion Magnus Carlson and his company, Play Magnus Group, for defamation. Niemann claims that Carlsen colluded with Danny Rensch, the leader of Chess.com, and Hikaru Nakamura, a chess grandmaster, to ruin Niemann's career. Carlsen suggested that Niemann cheated in their match at the Sinquefield Cup after being defeated. The court filing accuses Rensch and Nakamure of amplifying the cheating claim; Niemann is asking for at least $100 million in damages.
