Sure thing!  Let me give you my top four places for lobster in Maine:

1) Bayley’s Lobster Pound - Bayley's is known for being the birthplace of the Maine lobster roll.  With a reputation like that, I would highly recommend getting the lobster roll there!  It also features an open-air pier overlooking the Nonesuch River for a great view!

2) Five Islands Lobster Company - With an amazing view of the five islands offshore in Sheepscot Bay, this location is top-notch for viewing!  Pair that view with the freshwater that comes in daily and is boiled to perfection, and you have the perfect spot!

3) Chauncey Creek Lobster Pier - Plan on renting a boat?  Chauncey's is known for having many boaters tie right up to the pier as they enjoy some of the best lobster that Maine has to offer!  I would highly recommend this combination experience!

4) Thurston’s Lobster Pound - Overlooking beautiful Bass Harbor sits this fine place to grab some of the freshest lobster.  Thurston's is known for giving you the entire experience, ranging from lobster, corn, coleslaw, chowder, chips, and their ever-famous Thurston's famous blueberry cake!
