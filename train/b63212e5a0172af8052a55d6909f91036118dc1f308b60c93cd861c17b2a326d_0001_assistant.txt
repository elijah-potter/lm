**The Visit**

Every time I visit, there is a draft.
I perch on the rigid couch, my raft,
As the elders place themselves due aft.

Grandfather is like a mean old pirate.
His voice is spiky when he is irate.
He mocks my youth as if I don’t rate…

He speaks of bringing out the shears.
Grandmother pokes my hair when she hears.
Her frozen fingers graze my ears…
