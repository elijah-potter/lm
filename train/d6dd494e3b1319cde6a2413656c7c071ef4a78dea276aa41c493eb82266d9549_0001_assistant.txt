Johnny had been jogging for about 15 minutes and 8 blocks. He didn't even realize, though, because today was Thursday, and it was his favorite day of the week. Better than even the weekend, he absolutely adored Thursdays. The reason he loved Thursdays so much is that his mother would give him money on Thursday morning so that after school that day, he could rush straight to the candy shop after school and get as much candy as he could afford with the money his mother gave him. As he barreled down the street at top speed, the shop came into view. He lunged his way through the door with an energy of enthusiasm that could only be created with youth. As he walked in, he said hello to the owner and cashier, Mr.Lucky, and then proceeded to go up and down the aisles as he stared at all the colorful boxes of candy across all of the shelves. As his eyes continued to wander the aisles, he saw something that captivated his attention. A bright red and yellow box with the name "EXTREME BALLS" on the packaging. As he grabbed at it, he saw that it was candy balls with sour salt on them to add an extra sour kick to them. He grabbed two boxes of this and a box of M&M's and made his way to the counter, knowing that he was going to be in for a wild ride in the flavor department.
