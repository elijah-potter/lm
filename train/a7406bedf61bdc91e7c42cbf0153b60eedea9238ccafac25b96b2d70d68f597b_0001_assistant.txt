Jim Belushi and Chris Parnell are the only cast members of NBC's long-running sketch comedy series Saturday Night Live to be let go and later given their job back. Jim Belushi, the brother of original cast member John Belushi, became the first in 1983, while Chris Parnell was fired after Season 26 in 2001 along with featured player Jerry Minor, only to be rehired midway through the following season.
