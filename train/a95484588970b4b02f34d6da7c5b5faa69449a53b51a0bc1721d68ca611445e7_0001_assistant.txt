A long time ago, there was a dwarf named Gimli and an elf named Legolas. They grew up in the same town but were always told that they can't be friends. Dwarves and elves never got along, and their parents passed on this hatred to their children. Now Gimli and Legolas were gathering berries in the nearby woods when they ran into each other. They looked at each other awkwardly before scowling and going on to search for more berries. It quickly became a competition as each rushed along to find more berries than the other. They stayed within each other's view as they looked, going deeper and deeper into the woods. As they ran along looking for berries, they suddenly realized they were lost. They stopped to look, trying to find a marker or sign showing which way to go. They realized they had to work together to get out, and so they did. They looked around for what felt like hours before they came across a worn-out sign pointing toward a neighboring village. They cheered as they headed off quickly back into familiar territory. Gimli and Legolas learned that even though they looked different, they could work together and weren't that different after all. They got back to their village and told their story, changing the relationship the dwarves and elves had forever.
