Hi friends! 

I completed the work on my car over the weekend, changing all four shocks. I found a good deal on the shocks and knew that I would save over $600 if I could do the job myself. The most important part is safety, so I made sure the car was secure on jack stands. I needed to use a 1/2" breaker bar to free the bolts from the front end. I was able to install the new front shocks with very little difficulty.

I ran into a little problem on the last shock in the back - the bolt was stuck! It was at this point that my Dad showed up. The visit surprised me so much that it jolted my wrench and loosened the bolt. What a lucky visit! The new rear shocks went in without any problems as well. My dad and I had a nice visit after, celebrating a job well done!
