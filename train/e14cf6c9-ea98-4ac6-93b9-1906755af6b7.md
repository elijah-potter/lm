The quiet hum of the café’s overhead lights felt like a borrowed hum, a shared rhythm from another life. The morning coffee tasted of someone else’s morning, the scent of cinnamon and stale bread a ghost from a different table. My hands traced the rim of the mug—worn, not mine—and I realized the chair I sat in had been claimed by a stranger’s shoulders before mine. Outside, the park’s laughter carried on the wind, already fading into memory. This moment wasn’t mine to own. It was a pause between borrowed moments, each one a fleeting loan from the past.
