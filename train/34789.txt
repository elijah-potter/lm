The Overland Trail Museum's November History Café program offers guests a chance to make a hand-crafted ornament inspired by the theme of the program. At the Nov. 12 program on German-Russian immigrants, visitors made a Folded German Bell Ornament, using folded paper.
To make this craft you'll need a 5 by 5 inch square piece of paper, ornament cording, beads, double-stick tape and quick-drying glue.
1. Fold the square in half.
2. Open the square, turn the paper 90 degrees and fold in half again.
3. Open the square and fold two corners point to point.
4. Open the square and fold the remaining two corners point to point.
5. Open the square and make an airplane fold at each corner. Do this by bringing paper on each side of the fold line to midline and creasing. Make sure to open each airplane fold after you have creased the two sides before going on to the airplane fold for the next corner.
6. After all four corners have been folded, open the square and press the space between each airplane fold.
7. Cup the paper in the palm of one hand and gently press in on the center with the fingers of your other hand this will convince the bell to take shape. If you have successfully completed all of the folds, the four points will spring up and you'll be holding a bell.
8. Make a hanging loop of ornament cording (cut about 10 inches) and knot the end after stringing on a bead or beads.
9. Apply narrow strips of double-stick tap to the inside of the bell along each of the airplane folds. Adhere the tail of the hanging loop to one of the pieces of tape. Gently press the outside of the bell the tape pieces will stick to one another, holding the bell closed.
10. Add a dab of quick-drying glue just inside the tip to make sure the bell won't open.
