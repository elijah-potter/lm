The five largest countries in terms of land area are:
1. Russia: It is the biggest country in the world, covering a vast area of 17,098,250 square kilometers. It stretches across Eastern Europe and Asia.
2. Canada: This country has a large land area of 9,879,750 square kilometers. 
3. China: China has a land area of 9,600,013 square kilometers. 
4. United States: The United States has a reported land area ranging from 9,525,067 to 9,831,510 square kilometers. The precise size is difficult to measure due to its irregular shape and different sources have reported different values for the size of this country.
5. Brazil: It is the largest country in South America, covering an area of 8,515,770 square kilometers.
