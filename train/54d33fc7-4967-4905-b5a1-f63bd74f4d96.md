**Exterior: The Hollow Hill**
A mist swirls like unspoken sighs between the skeletal trees, their branches clawing at the sky like fingers tracing forgotten words. The air hums with a low, metallic tension—static from a storm that never quite arrives. Raindrops, if they fell, would be too few, too slow, like the weight of a promise left unfulfilled.

The sky is a bruise, a vast canvas of bruised purples and sickly yellows, streaked with jagged veins of lightning that split the clouds like a heart opening too wide. The ground beneath is damp, the grass a carpet of crushed petals and half-buried bones. The wind carries the scent of damp earth and something older—something like rust and old blood.

Inside, the walls pulse with the flicker of dying lanterns, their light dimming like a breath held too long. Shadows stretch unnaturally long, twisting into shapes that move when no one looks. The only sound is the occasional crash of a distant thunderclap, followed by silence so heavy it feels like a weight on the chest.

A figure stands at the window, arms crossed, watching the storm. Their breath comes in shallow, trembling gasps. The horizon is a line of distant smoke, the faintest whisper of a town lost in the haze. The wind carries a whisper: *"You shouldn’t be here."* But the voice is already fading, swallowed by the storm’s roar.
