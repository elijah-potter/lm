- Dolphins are a kind of marine mammal that includes more than 70 species known as a Cetacean. They have live births, rather than laying eggs, and spend their entire lives in the water. 
- Porpoises are also fully aquatic mammals, and they exist in the same group - Cetaceans - that dolphins exist in. Porpoises give live birth as well. 
- Manatees are included in the Sirenian group. They are another kind of marine mammal that gives birth instead of laying eggs. 
- Whales are the third marine mammal included in the Cetacean group. Whales give live birth following their gestation period. 
- Sea lions are marine mammals that give birth to pups, and they belong to the Pinniped group. 
- Walruses are another kind of Pinniped marine mammal. Walruses give live birth but not as often as other marine mammals. 
- Seals are Pinniped marine mammals that give live birth. 
- Sea otters belong to the Fissiped marine mammal group. These creatures are warm-blooded but spend more time on land than in the water and give birth to their young.
- Polar bears are also considered to be marine mammals. They are Fissipeds who give live birth to their cubs. 
- Dugongs are another kind of Sirenian marine mammal that gives birth to its young.
