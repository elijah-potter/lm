Once upon a time, in a cozy house with a big garden, there lived a little bunny named Benny. Benny had soft brown fur and big, round eyes that sparkled like the sun.

One morning, Benny woke up and saw a tiny flower just blooming next to his nest. "Oh, Benny thought, "what a beautiful flower!"

Benny decided to explore the garden. He hopped over the grass, giggled when he saw a butterfly flutter by, and collected a few shiny pebbles to play with. Along the way, he met a little squirrel named Sammy who was collecting acorns. "Hi, Sammy," Benny said, "can we play together?"

Sammy clapped his tiny paws. "Sure, Benny! Let's chase the wind together!"

They ran and played until their paws got a little tired. Benny and Sammy sat down under a big oak tree and shared a juicy berry. "This is the best day ever," Benny said, as the sun warmed their faces.

As the day ended, Benny and Sammy curled up together in their nests, dreaming of tomorrow’s adventures. The moon twinkled above them, and they knew tomorrow would be another wonderful day.
