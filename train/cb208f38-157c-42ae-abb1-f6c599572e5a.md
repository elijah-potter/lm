The rain hammered against the rusted metal roof of an abandoned car, the headlights flickering weakly as the engine sputtered. A lone figure crouched in the damp grass, hands gripping a worn map, their breath fogging in the cold air. A familiar scent of old wood and damp earth clung to the collar of their coat, mixed with something metallic—blood? The GPS on their phone died hours ago, its screen now cracked and blank. A distant, familiar hum came from the highway, but the road was nowhere in sight. A half-eaten sandwich, still wrapped in plastic, lay abandoned in the passenger seat. The car’s taillights glowed faintly, but the headlights were off. The keys in the ignition felt heavy, like they were leading them somewhere else entirely.
