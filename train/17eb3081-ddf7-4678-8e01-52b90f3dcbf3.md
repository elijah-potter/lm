Once upon a time in a cozy little house with a big red door, lived a tiny bunny named Benny. Benny loved to hop around his yard, chasing after sunny flowers and chasing after his best friend, a chatty little bird named Pip.

One morning, Benny woke up and saw something magical: a tiny, shimmering rainbow in the sky! Benny thought, "I wonder where this rainbow goes?" So, he hopped up and down, trying to follow it. Soon, he found himself at the top of a tall, green hill where the rainbow met a giant, sparkling river.

Down by the river, Benny met a sleepy old frog named Fred. Fred had a very wise old voice. "Benny," Fred said, "the rainbow leads to a hidden treasure—it’s not gold or jewels, but something much more precious: kindness, laughter, and a heart full of joy!"

Benny was excited but also a little nervous. "Where is this treasure?" he asked.

Fred smiled. "Follow the rainbow to the very end of the river, and you’ll find a place where everyone shares their happiness. But remember, Benny, the treasure isn’t just for you—it’s for everyone who helps make the world a little brighter!"

So Benny hopped and giggled, following the rainbow down the river. At the end, he found a big, round, colorful circle with many little friends—bunnies, birds, and even a family of squirrels—all laughing and playing together. They were sharing their snacks, telling stories, and dancing under the sun. Benny realized that the "treasure" wasn’t something shiny or hidden, but the warmth of friendship and kindness that made their hearts happy.

From that day on, Benny and Pip always looked for little ways to share happiness, like planting seeds for flowers or telling jokes that made everyone smile. And every time they did, they felt like they’d found the greatest treasure of all—a home filled with love and laughter.

The end!
