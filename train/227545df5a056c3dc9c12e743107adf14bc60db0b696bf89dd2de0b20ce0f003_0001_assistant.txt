There once was a boy named Clumsy Clancy, who considered himself quite fancy.
He lived in Seedville, snacked on seeds, and befriended a girl named Nancy.

Clancy was a paperboy, and just as charming as a rose,
But Clancy made all the mistakes and had lots of missed throws.

Clancy loved to build a house of cards, but after only one day,
He realized that his whimsical nature would blow them all away.

Clancy was a pianist with some classical pieces he wrote,
But his fingers were sporadic, never hitting the right note.

One day, Clancy decided to ask Nancy to be his date,
While at prom, instead of handing her a rose, he fell into some cake!

Clancy sure was clumsy, everyone would always tease and kid,
But Clancy vowed to positivity, and inspire others, he did.
