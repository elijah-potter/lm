Us, U, and S are the last two letters of FOCUS, meaning we should pay attention to each other, putting each other's hearts at the center of our needs and attending to one another. Without our focus on each other, what would become of us? What would become of our story? Why even be together and tell the story of us at all? Being together and telling our story empowers us, and more than likely it will inspire others. With you I am safe, I aim to be better, and our story spurs others to be better also. To be everything we can be, everything we are, together.
