I’m a loyal Marriott Platinum member, but I will think twice before staying in their Durham hotel after my recent experience during a 4-night stay in one of their suites. I was disappointed in several aspects of my accommodations, affecting the quality of my stay.
1)	The design of the shower caused water to leak all over the floor every time I used it. This resulted in a wet slippery bathroom floor that posed a safety hazard to my husband and me.
2)	After checking in late in the evening, we were unable to watch the late-night news because no remotes were provided.
3)	The phone and clock radio both were not functioning. We were unable to call the front desk to complain about the problem due to the non-functional phone.
4)	Most concerning of all, we were unable to open the sofa bed in the suite when it was time for bed because the couch needed to be moved closer to the wall. The front desk staff said there was no one available to help move the couch, so I had to do it myself. This inconvenience severely impacted my enjoyment of the room!
As a loyal Marriott customer, I have always praised the hotel’s high standards. During this visit, the company fell far short of my expectations. I hope management takes this feedback seriously and takes action to resolve these concerning issues.
