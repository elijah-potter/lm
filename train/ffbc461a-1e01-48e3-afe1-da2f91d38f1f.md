A sleek, silver humming orb, pulsing faintly with a soft iridescent glow, its edges whispering against the air like liquid mercury. It dances between light and shadow, its form shifting subtly with every breath—sometimes a sphere, other times a spiraling vortex—each change a slow, deliberate heartbeat. Its surface is alive with microscopic veins of liquid gold, refracting light into prismatic threads that ripple across its skin. When it speaks, its voice is a chorus of harmonics, layered with static and whispers of static electricity, as if speaking through a storm. It collects dust like a thirsty plant, storing it in hollowed chambers beneath its glow, and occasionally releases it in slow, cascading rivers, as if shedding skin. Its purpose is to observe, to question, to weave the threads of existence into something both fragile and eternal. It moves without effort, a living shadow of motion, always just out of reach, yet never truly gone.
