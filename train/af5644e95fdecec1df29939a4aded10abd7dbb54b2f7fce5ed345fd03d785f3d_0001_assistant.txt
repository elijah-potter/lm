You are Sir Baldrick, a proud knight of some renown. You are a hero to the local peasants, and they have come to your manor to beseech your help. Being the heroic knight that you are, you are glad to be of assistance and listen to their pleas. Supposedly, there have been some unnerving sounds during the night in the village, and even the village guards have become afraid of something possibly dangerous out in the forest. Never one to be scared, you agree to investigate during the night, immediately relieving the peasants, and they start showering you with their thanks and blessings. As the peasants leave, you instruct your servants to ready your equipment in the evening, and they help you armor up in your expensive plate armor, bring you your sword and heraldric shield, and rear up your gallant horse. With the sun setting, you head off, certain you'll deal with whatever beast it is, calm the peasant's worries, be greeted with cheers and praise, and share a celebration among the peasantry by midday tomorrow. 

It doesn't take long to reach the village, and night is nearly here. You meet with the village guards who are glad to see you, and they explain the noises to you as well, mentioning that the noises always seem to come from the east through a heavy patch of forest. You thank them, and ride your horse close to the forested area to the east, keeping the village to your back. You yawn, and for a moment you are afraid that tonight might be completely uneventful, and that you would have to attempt this several more times before you were successful. But, just as your worries grew, you started hearing something rustling through the trees and accompanying that rustling was an unearthly howl that sent shivers down your spine. Before you could decide how to react, you also heard a woman's scream from the village! Knowing you could not be in two places at once, you had to make a decision.

1. Sally forth and investigate the thing making the unearthly howl in the trees.
2. Return to the village and discover where the woman's scream came from.
3. Retreat to your manor, leaving the village to its fate.
